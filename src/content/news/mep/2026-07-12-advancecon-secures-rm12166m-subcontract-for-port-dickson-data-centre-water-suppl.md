body_en: |
  Advancecon Holdings Bhd has secured a RM121.66 million subcontract to construct an off-river storage facility for a water supply scheme serving a data centre in Port Dickson, Negeri Sembilan, the company said in a Bursa Malaysia filing on July 10, 2026.

  Advancecon Infra Sdn Bhd, the group's wholly-owned subsidiary, accepted the letter of award on July 10 from an undisclosed main contractor. The scope covers earthworks and associated construction for the off-river storage (ORS) facility, which forms part of a proposed 65-million-litres-per-day (MLD) water supply scheme development for the data centre. Advancecon said the works are to be completed within 30 months from the date of written instruction to commence physical works and mobilisation.

  ## Undisclosed main contractor

  The party awarding the subcontract is a contractor with more than 30 years of experience in Malaysia's construction industry, Advancecon said in the filing, without identifying the company by name. Its previous projects include the PKNS Cyber Valley and a raw water pipeline project in Rasau, and it was involved in finishing works for the MRT (mass rapid transit) Line 2 in Cyberjaya. The identity of the data centre owner or developer was also not disclosed.

  Advancecon said the subcontract will be funded through a combination of internally generated funds and bank borrowings.

  ## Financial context

  The award follows a quarter of improved profitability for Advancecon. The group reported an 85.7% jump in net profit to RM1.81 million in the first quarter ended March 31, 2026 (1QFY2026), from RM973,000 in the year-earlier period, even as revenue declined 3.7% to RM97.45 million from RM101.21 million.

  Advancecon generates revenue from five segments. Construction and support services was the largest contributor at 51.3% of total revenue in 1QFY2026, followed by quarry operations at 47.3%. Property investment, green energy, and property development together accounted for the remaining 1.4%.

  Advancecon shares closed unchanged at 17.5 sen on July 10, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM102.3 million.

  ## Water infrastructure for data centres

  The 65-million-litres-per-day water supply scheme in Port Dickson is being constructed specifically to supply a data centre, according to Advancecon's bourse filing. The ORS facility that Advancecon Infra will build is a core component of that scheme. Physical works are set to begin upon written instruction to mobilise, with the 30-month completion period starting from that point.
body_zh: |
  Advancecon Holdings Bhd於2026年7月10日透過馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）申報公告，宣布公司已取得一份價值1.2166億令吉的分包合約，將在森美蘭州波德申興建一座河外蓄水設施，為當地一座資料中心的供水計畫提供服務。

  集團全資子公司Advancecon Infra Sdn Bhd於7月10日接獲來自一家未具名總承包商的得標通知書。工程範疇涵蓋河外蓄水（ORS）設施的土方工程及相關建設，該設施為擬議中每日供水6,500萬公升（MLD）計畫的一部分，專為上述資料中心所設。Advancecon表示，工程須自書面動員指示發出之日起30個月內竣工。

  ## 未具名總承包商

  Advancecon在交易所公告中表示，授予此分包合約的業者為一家在馬來西亞建築業深耕逾30年的承包商，但未披露其公司名稱。該承包商過去的工程案例包括PKNS網路谷（PKNS Cyber Valley）以及拉沙（Rasau）的原水管線工程，並曾參與賽城（Cyberjaya）大眾捷運（MRT）第2號線的收尾工程。此外，涉案資料中心的業主或開發商身分同樣未予揭露。

  Advancecon表示，本分包合約所需資金將以內部自有資金與銀行借貸共同籌應。

  ## 財務背景

  此次合約的取得，緊接於Advancecon一季盈利表現明顯好轉之後。集團在截至2026年3月31日止的2026財年第一季（1QFY2026），淨利大幅躍升85.7%至181萬令吉，相較前一年同期的97.3萬令吉成長顯著；惟同期營收則小幅下滑3.7%，從1.0121億令吉降至9,745萬令吉。

  Advancecon的營收來自五大業務板塊。其中，建設與支援服務為最大貢獻來源，佔1QFY2026總營收的51.3%；採石場營運以47.3%居次。物業投資、綠色能源及房地產開發三項業務合計僅佔餘下的1.4%。

  Advancecon股票於7月10日收盤時報17.5仙，股價持平，集團市值約為1.023億令吉。

  ## 資料中心的供水基礎建設

  根據Advancecon的交易所公告，波德申這套每日供水6,500萬公升的水源計畫，係專為服務一座資料中心而建置。Advancecon Infra將負責興建的河外蓄水設施，是整套供水計畫的核心組成部分。實體工程將於收到書面動員指示後正式啟動，30個月的完工期限亦自該時間點起算。
