  ## Bridge DC and Morong Electric launch containerized AI power module

  APAC data center operator Bridge Data Centers has launched PowerCore 5.0, a fully prefabricated and containerized power module designed for high-density AI data centers, developed in partnership with power solutions provider Morong Electric.

  Each unit is built within a standard 40-foot ISO container frame and integrates transformers, power distribution equipment, an uninterruptible power supply (UPS), cooling, fire suppression, and monitoring systems into a single assembly. All units are fully assembled and tested off-site before delivery.

  ## Delivery speed and maintenance

  Bridge DC says the modular approach allows on-site civil works and factory prefabrication to proceed simultaneously, which it says shortens total delivery timelines by more than 50 percent compared with conventional build methods. For large campus deployments, the company said units can be delivered and installed on a bi-weekly cadence.

  To simplify maintenance, core components including UPS units, transformers, and precision cooling systems are mounted on slide-out rails, allowing technicians to service or replace equipment without major disassembly.

  ## Executive rationale

  Bridge DC Chief Technology Officer Zhang Binghua attributed the product to the accelerating pace of AI infrastructure buildout. "The AI infrastructure sector is scaling at an unprecedented rate, and operators now require higher power density, faster hardware refreshes and maximum uptime," he said. "This shift requires us to rethink how power systems are designed and deployed."

  Zhang Kai, chairman of Morong Electric, framed the problem in terms of hardware refresh cycles. "AI hardware now evolves on a monthly cycle, and traditional on-site system integration can no longer keep pace with the operational needs of modern AI data centers," he said. "Prefabricated power modules clear the delivery bottlenecks holding back compute infrastructure expansion."

  ## Context

  Bridge DC describes its target market as high-density AI data centers and says the product is intended to support "standardized, low-carbon compute deployments across the globe," according to Zhang Binghua. The company also cited cross-climate operation and intelligent operations and maintenance as design objectives, though it did not provide specific performance figures for either capability.

  Morong Electric is described as a power solutions provider and developer and manufacturer of modular data center products. Zhang Kai characterized the PowerCore 5.0 launch as "a critical milestone" in the two companies' strategic partnership, adding that they plan to continue co-developing integrated power solutions meeting "global market standards and regional compliance requirements."

  ## Industry significance

  The launch reflects a broader shift in data center construction toward prefabricated and modular infrastructure as operators attempt to compress build timelines in response to surging demand for AI compute capacity. By moving assembly off-site, operators can reduce the time-sensitive on-site integration work that has historically constrained how quickly new capacity can come online.

  Bridge DC said the partnership with Morong Electric will continue, with further co-development of high-performance integrated power solutions planned for global markets.

  ## Bridge DC與Morong Electric聯手推出AI資料中心貨櫃化電力模組

  亞太資料中心業者Bridge Data Centers正式發表PowerCore 5.0，這是一款專為高密度AI資料中心設計的全預製貨櫃化電力模組，由Bridge DC與電力解決方案商Morong Electric共同研發。

  每套模組均建置於標準40呎ISO貨櫃架構之內，將變壓器、配電設備、不斷電系統（UPS）、冷卻系統、消防設備及監控系統整合為單一組件。所有模組在出廠前均已完成整機組裝與測試，再運送至現場部署。

  ## 交付速度與維護便利性

  Bridge DC表示，模組化設計可讓現場土建工程與工廠預製作業同步進行，據此將整體交付週期比傳統建造方式縮短逾50%。對於大型園區部署案，該公司指出模組可依每兩週一批次的節奏持續交付與安裝。

  為簡化日後維護作業，UPS、變壓器及精密冷卻系統等核心元件均採滑軌式安裝設計，技術人員無需大幅拆解即可進行維修或更換，大幅降低維運難度。

  ## 高層主管的產品定位說明

  Bridge DC技術長張冰華將PowerCore 5.0的誕生歸因於AI基礎設施建設的加速步伐。他表示：「AI基礎設施產業正以前所未有的速度擴張，業者如今需要更高的功率密度、更快的硬體更新週期，以及最大化的系統可用率。這樣的趨勢要求我們重新思考電力系統的設計與部署方式。」

  Morong Electric董事長張凱則從硬體更新週期的角度點出問題核心。他說：「AI硬體現在以月為單位不斷演進，傳統的現場系統整合方式已無法跟上現代AI資料中心的營運需求。預製電力模組正是為了突破制約算力基礎設施擴張的交付瓶頸而生。」

  ## 市場背景

  Bridge DC將目標市場定位為高密度AI資料中心，並表示PowerCore 5.0旨在支援「全球標準化、低碳算力部署」，此為張冰華對外說明的方向。該公司亦將跨氣候環境運作能力與智慧化運維列為設計目標，但並未就上述功能提供具體效能數據。

  Morong Electric定位為電力解決方案商，同時也是模組化資料中心產品的研發與製造廠商。張凱將PowerCore 5.0的發表形容為兩家公司策略夥伴關係的「重要里程碑」，並補充表示雙方計畫持續共同研發，推出符合「全球市場標準及各地合規要求」的整合電力解決方案。

  ## 產業意義

  此次發表反映出資料中心建設領域一個更大範圍的結構性轉變——隨著業者積極壓縮建設時程以應對AI算力需求的急速成長，預製化與模組化基礎設施正逐漸成為主流。將組裝作業移至工廠離場完成，可大幅減少現場整合工作的時間壓力，而這正是過去制約新增產能上線速度的主要因素。

  Bridge DC表示，與Morong Electric的合作夥伴關係將持續深化，雙方已規劃在全球市場進一步共同開發高效能整合電力解決方案。
