body_en: |
  The next constraint limiting AI data center expansion is not GPUs or memory — it is electricians. That headline conclusion is underscored by a visit to TeraWulf's Lake Mariner facility on the shore of Lake Ontario near Buffalo, New York, a former coal-powered power station now standing as one of the most concrete illustrations of the industrial scale at which AI infrastructure is being built.

  ## From Bitcoin Mining to a 750 MW AI Campus

  TeraWulf launched the Lake Mariner site roughly 18 months before mid-2022, when it announced it had reached 50 megawatts of Bitcoin mining capacity. A further 50 MW was planned for early 2023. Following a 2 MW AI and GPU pilot, the company had pivoted by 2025 to become an HPC and AI infrastructure provider, expanding the site's footprint to 157 acres and targeting 750 MW of total capacity at Lake Mariner. Bitcoin mining continues on an opportunistic basis; management and shareholders have determined that providing infrastructure for AI workloads represents the better commercial opportunity.

  Schneider Electric has supplied the majority of the electrical infrastructure at the site. Motivair, a Schneider subsidiary, has provided the liquid cooling technology — now a prerequisite for an AI-grade site.

  ## Tenants: Core42, Fluidstack, and a Possible Anthropic Connection

  TeraWulf's initial AI-grade build was CB-1, a 20 MW facility. Core42, a sovereign AI specialist and partner of both Cerebras Systems and AMD, is the company's first major client and has been running AMD-based systems in CB-1 for approximately ten months.

  CB-2, rated at 50 MW, was slated for 2025. CB-3 went into production just weeks before the visit, occupied by Fluidstack — an AI infrastructure firm backed by Google that is also helping Anthropic build and install its own TPU systems, and that is additionally a backer of TeraWulf. CB-3 was off-limits during the visit.

  ## CB-4: A 200 MW, 330,000 Square-Foot Flagship

  The focal point of the site's current construction phase is CB-4, a 330,000 square-foot, 200 MW building containing four data halls, each spanning 33,000 square feet. Construction began in January; the building was in its final preparation stages ahead of energization toward the end of summer. Once swept clean and powered up, customers will install their own racks and manage the space according to their requirements.

  ## Slab Floors Built for Racks Up to 10,000 Pounds

  CB-4 uses slab concrete floors rather than raised floors — easier to build and able to support loads that raised floors cannot. Chief operating officer Sean Farrell explained that high-density AI racks incorporating liquid cooling equipment and potentially 800-volt infrastructure are now too heavy for raised floors. TeraWulf designed CB-4 to a specification of 8,000 pounds per rack and could accommodate up to 10,000 pounds. The data halls also have space for fan walls should customers require them.

  ## Closed-Loop Cooling, No Water Consumed in Normal Operation

  The mechanical rooms supporting CB-4 are, in Farrell's words, "massive" — the data halls themselves account for less than half the building's total footprint. Cooling operates as a closed-loop system: chillers and coolers inside the data halls capture heat generated by GPU-dense racks, and heat exchangers exhaust it through rooftop cooling towers. No water is consumed in normal cooling operations. Once charged, the cooling fluid is designed to last ten to fifteen years.

  ## $290 Million Committed to Schneider Electric and Motivair

  TeraWulf has already spent $290 million with Schneider Electric and Motivair across the Lake Mariner buildout.

  AI資料中心擴張面臨的下一個制約因素既非GPU，也非記憶體，而是電工。這一結論來自《The Next Platform》對TeraWulf旗下Lake Mariner基地的實地參訪。該基地位於紐約州水牛城附近安大略湖畔，由前燃煤發電廠改建而來，已成為AI基礎設施以工業規模建設的最具代表性案例之一。

  ## 從比特幣挖礦到750 MW AI園區

  TeraWulf於2022年中宣布Lake Mariner基地達到50 MW的比特幣挖礦容量，距基地啟用約18個月。另外50 MW計畫於2023年初上線。完成2 MW的AI與GPU試行項目後，公司至2025年已全面轉型為HPC與AI基礎設施供應商，將園區面積擴展至157英畝，並以750 MW為Lake Mariner基地的總容量目標。比特幣挖礦業務仍以機會性方式持續進行；管理層與股東已判定，為AI工作負載提供基礎設施才是更具商業價值的機遇。

  施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）提供了基地大部分的電力基礎設施；其旗下子公司Motivair則提供液態冷卻技術——這已是AI級資料中心的必要條件。

  ## 進駐客戶：Core42、Fluidstack，以及與Anthropic的潛在關聯

  TeraWulf首個AI級設施為20 MW的CB-1。主權AI專家Core42是公司的首位重要客戶，同時也是Cerebras Systems與AMD的合作夥伴，目前已在CB-1運行AMD系統約十個月。

  50 MW的CB-2原定於2025年啟用。CB-3則在參訪前數週剛投入運行，由AI基礎設施商Fluidstack入駐——該公司獲Google投資，同時正協助Anthropic建置並安裝其自研TPU系統，亦是TeraWulf的資金支持方之一。CB-3在參訪期間未對外開放。

  ## CB-4：33萬平方英尺、200 MW旗艦設施

  目前建設的核心為CB-4，這是一棟33萬平方英尺、200 MW的大型建築，內含四個機房，每個機房面積達33,000平方英尺。工程於今年1月動工，大樓在參訪時正處於夏末通電前的最後準備階段。大樓通電後，客戶將依自身需求自行安裝機架並管理空間。

  ## 混凝土板地板：每機架承重規格高達10,000磅

  CB-4採用混凝土板地板而非高架地板——施工更為便捷，且能承受高架地板無法負荷的重量。營運長Sean Farrell說明，搭載液態冷卻設備、並可能採用800伏特基礎設施的高密度AI機架，重量已超出高架地板的承載極限。TeraWulf以每機架8,000磅的規格設計CB-4，最高可支撐10,000磅。機房內亦預留空間，供客戶視需求安裝風扇牆。

  ## 閉環冷卻系統：正常運行不消耗水資源

  Farrell形容支撐CB-4的機械室「規模龐大」——機房本身的面積不到整棟建築總面積的一半。冷卻採用閉環系統：機房內的冷水機與冷卻器吸收GPU密集機架產生的熱量，熱交換器再透過屋頂冷卻塔將熱量散出。正常冷卻運行不消耗水資源；冷卻液一旦注入，設計使用壽命為十至十五年。

  ## 已向施耐德電氣與Motivair投入2.9億美元

  TeraWulf在Lake Mariner整體建設過程中，已向施耐德電氣與Motivair合計支出2.9億美元。
