body_en: |
  A fire broke out on the morning of November 10 at a data center building in the Kabil area of Nongsa, Batam, Indonesia, at a facility believed to be developed through a joint collaboration between PT Telkom Data Ekosistem — the data center arm of state-owned PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk, operating under the NeutraDC brand — and Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd, according to local news reports cited by W.Media.

  The fire started at approximately 9 a.m. local time and was extinguished successfully. Police confirmed there were no casualties and no significant property damage.

  ## Cause and circumstances

  Police investigations and eyewitness accounts indicate the fire was caused by welding activities on the top floor of the building. Wind carried sparks that ignited a tarp or tent material at the site, according to those accounts.

  ## Broader context

  The incident occurred amid what W.Media described as broader local tensions over the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence (AI) and data center infrastructure on Batam Island. The island has recently seen public protests over water shortages, with residents linking those shortages to the pace of infrastructure development in the area.

  The fire adds to the scrutiny facing data center construction activity in Batam at a time when the region is already a focal point of community concern about the environmental and resource demands of the sector's growth.

  印尼廖內群島巴淡島（Batam）諾沙（Nongsa）卡比爾（Kabil）地區一棟資料中心建築，於11月10日上午發生火警。據W.Media引述當地媒體報導，該設施疑為印尼國營電信公司PT Telkom Indonesia（Persero）Tbk旗下資料中心事業部門PT Telkom Data Ekosistem（以NeutraDC品牌運營）與華為技術有限公司合作開發的資料中心。

  火勢於當地時間上午9時許起燃，隨後順利撲滅。警方確認無人傷亡，財產損失亦不顯著。

  ## 起火原因與事發經過

  警方調查結果及目擊者陳述均指出，火災起因為建築物頂樓進行的電焊作業。據相關陳述，風將焊接火花吹散，進而引燃工地現場的帆布或棚架材料。

  ## 更廣泛的背景

  此次事故發生之際，W.Media描述巴淡島正面臨更廣泛的在地緊張情緒，肇因於人工智慧（AI）與資料中心基礎設施在島上的快速擴張。近期，巴淡島已出現居民抗議缺水問題的示威行動，民眾將此一用水短缺現象，歸咎於當地基礎設施建設的快速推進。

  這起火警進一步加深了外界對巴淡島資料中心建設活動的審視，而該地區此刻已是各界高度關注的焦點——社區居民對於資料中心產業持續擴張所帶來的環境衝擊與資源耗用問題，早已憂慮日深。
