body_en: |
  Modular data center builder Ecoblox and Croatian electric vehicle technology firm Rimac Technology have signed a Joint Development Agreement to develop a modular data center that uses automotive-grade battery modules as its primary uninterruptible power and energy storage layer, with the first unit expected to be complete in November at Rimac's campus in Kerestinec, Croatia.

  The agreement combines Rimac Technology's UNI battery modules and battery management system with Ecoblox's modular data center (MDC) architecture, AI infrastructure design, and hardware/software integration. The companies said the pairing is intended to extend continuous backup power for critical computing hardware and give operators real-time monitoring and control over the modular unit via Rimac's battery management system.

  ## First data center application for the UNI module

  Rimac's UNI battery modules were developed for use across automotive, energy storage, marine, off-highway, commercial, and industrial applications. According to the companies, the Kerestinec deployment will mark the first time the modules have been used in data center infrastructure.

  Ecoblox, headquartered in Dubai, UAE, offers modular data centers ranging from rack-level units to 20-to-40-foot containerized modules, all designed for high-density workloads and compatible with Nvidia and AMD graphics processing units (GPUs), the chips most commonly used in AI training and inference. The company claims it can deploy its modular data centers in under eight months.

  ## Executives cite power density and grid constraints as drivers

  Doug Makishima, CEO of Ecoblox, said in a statement that integrating automotive-grade energy storage directly into the MDC architecture is intended to remove grid constraints and allow cloud providers and enterprise customers to deploy AI clusters more quickly. Nurdin Pitarevic, CEO of Rimac Technology, said the battery and power electronics capabilities his company developed to meet automotive requirements — covering power density, thermal management, safety, and reliability — are increasingly relevant to high-power AI infrastructure.

  ## What the deal signals

  The partnership represents an emerging pattern in which power and energy storage technologies developed for the electric vehicle industry are being adapted to data center applications, particularly for high-density AI workloads where reliable, dense power delivery is a primary constraint. By drawing on an automotive supply chain with established mass-production and safety certification processes, modular data center builders gain access to battery systems engineered for demanding duty cycles rather than adapting conventional stationary storage products.

  The first modular data center produced under the agreement is expected to be completed in November 2026 and installed at Rimac's campus in Kerestinec, Croatia.

  模組化資料中心建置商Ecoblox與克羅埃西亞電動車科技公司Rimac Technology已正式簽署聯合開發協議，合作開發一款以車規電池模組作為主要不斷電電源與儲能層的模組化資料中心。首部機組預計於今年11月在Rimac位於克羅埃西亞克雷斯蒂內茨（Kerestinec）的園區完工。

  此次合作將Rimac Technology的UNI電池模組與電池管理系統，結合Ecoblox的模組化資料中心（MDC）架構、AI基礎設施設計，以及軟硬體整合能力。兩家公司表示，此配對方案旨在延長關鍵運算硬體的持續備用電力，並透過Rimac的電池管理系統，讓營運商得以即時監控與管理模組化機組。

  ## UNI模組首度應用於資料中心

  Rimac的UNI電池模組原為汽車、儲能、海事、越野、商用及工業等多元場景所設計。根據兩家公司說明，克雷斯蒂內茨的部署案將是UNI模組首次被應用於資料中心基礎設施。

  Ecoblox總部位於阿拉伯聯合大公國杜拜，提供從機架級機組到20至40英尺貨櫃化模組的模組化資料中心產品，所有產品均針對高密度工作負載設計，並相容輝達（Nvidia）與超微（AMD）的繪圖處理器（GPU）——這也是AI訓練與推論最廣泛使用的晶片。該公司宣稱其模組化資料中心可在八個月內完成部署。

  ## 高層主管：電力密度與電網限制是主要驅動力

  Ecoblox執行長Doug Makishima在聲明中表示，將車規儲能系統直接整合進MDC架構，目的是消除電網限制，讓雲端業者與企業客戶能更迅速地部署AI叢集。Rimac Technology執行長Nurdin Pitarevic則指出，其公司為滿足汽車市場需求而研發的電池與電力電子能力——涵蓋電力密度、散熱管理、安全性與可靠性——對於高功耗AI基礎設施愈來愈具有直接應用價值。

  ## 這項合作透露的產業趨勢

  此次合作代表著一個正在成形的模式：原本為電動車產業所開發的電源與儲能技術，正被改造後導入資料中心應用，尤其是在可靠、高密度電力供應為首要瓶頸的高密度AI工作負載場景中更為明顯。藉由引入具備成熟量產流程與安全認證機制的汽車供應鏈，模組化資料中心建置商得以取得專為嚴苛運行週期所設計的電池系統，而非將傳統的靜態儲能產品加以改裝應用。

  依照協議，首部模組化資料中心預計於2026年11月完工，並安裝在Rimac位於克羅埃西亞克雷斯蒂內茨的園區。
