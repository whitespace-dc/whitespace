# AI's Real Bottleneck: Electricians, Not GPUs and RAM

While GPU and RAM scarcity dominates headlines about AI infrastructure constraints, the actual limiting factor for data center expansion is the shortage of skilled electricians. Building and upgrading electrical systems for large-scale AI data centers requires extensive specialized labor that is increasingly hard to find. Without addressing the electrician workforce shortage, hardware supply alone cannot enable the rapid data center growth AI deployment demands.

## 中文

# AI擴展的真正瓶頸：電工短缺

儘管GPU和記憶體的短缺主導了關於AI基礎設施瓶頸的討論，但資料中心擴展的真正限制因素其實是熟練電工的短缺。建造和升級大規模AI資料中心的電力系統需要大量專業人力，這些人才日益難尋。若無法解決電工勞動力短缺問題，再充足的硬體供應也無法支撑AI部署所需的快速資料中心擴展。
