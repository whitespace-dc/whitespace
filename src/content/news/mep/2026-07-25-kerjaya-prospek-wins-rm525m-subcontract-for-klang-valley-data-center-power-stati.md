body_en: |
  Kerjaya Prospek Group Bhd has secured a RM52.5 million subcontract to carry out civil, structural, and basic low-voltage mechanical and electrical (M&E) works for a new 275-kilovolt (kV) consumer landing station serving a data center in the Klang Valley, the Kuala Lumpur-listed construction group disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia filing on July 23.

  The award was accepted by Kerjaya Prospek (M) Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned subsidiary of the group. The client was not named in the filing. Work commenced on July 23 and is scheduled for completion by January 9, 2027, a construction window of roughly five and a half months.

  ## Contract and order book

  Kerjaya Prospek said the contract is expected to contribute positively to group earnings over the contract period. The award lifts the group's total outstanding order book, which stood at RM5 billion as of June 23, 2026.

  A consumer landing station at 275 kV is a high-voltage grid connection point where power from the national transmission network is received and stepped down for use by a large electricity consumer — in this case, a data center. Such infrastructure is a prerequisite for hyperscale and large colocation facilities, which require dedicated, stable grid connections at transmission-level voltages.

  ## Market context

  Kerjaya Prospek shares closed unchanged at RM2.41 on July 23, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM3.05 billion. The stock has declined 11.1% year to date.

  The company trades at 12.8 times trailing earnings, the lowest valuation multiple among its listed construction peers, according to AskEdge data. Gamuda Bhd trades at 24.6 times trailing earnings, Sunway Construction Group Bhd at 24.9 times, and Binastra Corp Bhd at 16.1 times.

  All nine analysts covering Kerjaya Prospek carry a buy recommendation on the stock, with Bloomberg-compiled target prices ranging from RM2.73 to RM3.46, implying upside of between 13% and 44% from the July 23 closing price.

  ## Significance

  The contract adds to a growing pipeline of data center-linked construction work in Malaysia, where the Klang Valley has emerged as a primary destination for regional data center investment. Power infrastructure — particularly high-voltage landing stations that connect large facilities directly to the transmission grid — has become a critical bottleneck and construction category as developers race to bring capacity online.

  The project is scheduled to reach completion on January 9, 2027.

  ## Kerjaya Prospek奪得5,250萬令吉分包合約　承建巴生谷資料中心電力站

  吉隆坡上市建設集團Kerjaya Prospek Group Bhd於7月23日透過馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）公告，宣布取得一份價值5,250萬令吉的分包合約，負責為巴生谷一座資料中心興建全新的275千伏（kV）用戶落地站，工程內容涵蓋土木、結構及基礎低壓機電（M&E）工程。

  此合約由集團全資子公司Kerjaya Prospek (M) Sdn Bhd承接。公告未披露業主名稱。工程已於7月23日正式動工，預計於2027年1月9日完工，施工期約為五個半月。

  ## 合約與訂單總額

  Kerjaya Prospek表示，此合約預計將在合約履行期間對集團整體盈利帶來正面貢獻。此次得標使集團未完成訂單總額進一步提升，截至2026年6月23日，集團手持訂單已達50億令吉。

  275千伏用戶落地站是一種高壓電網接入設施，負責接收來自國家輸電網絡的電力，並將電壓降壓後供大型用電戶使用——在本案中，即為一座資料中心。此類基礎設施是超大規模資料中心及大型共置（colocation）機房的必要前提條件，因為這些設施需要在輸電等級電壓下取得專用、穩定的電網連接。

  ## 市場背景

  Kerjaya Prospek股價於7月23日收盤報2.41令吉，與前一交易日持平，公司市值為30.5億令吉。該股今年迄今已累計下跌11.1%。

  根據AskEdge數據，Kerjaya Prospek目前本益比為12.8倍，為上市同業中估值最低。相比之下，Gamuda Bhd本益比為24.6倍，Sunway Construction Group Bhd為24.9倍，Binastra Corp Bhd則為16.1倍。

  目前追蹤Kerjaya Prospek的九位分析師，清一色給予「買進」評級。Bloomberg彙整的目標價介於2.73至3.46令吉之間，較7月23日收盤價分別隱含13%至44%的上漲空間。

  ## 產業意義

  此合約為馬來西亞持續擴大的資料中心相關建設案件再添一筆。巴生谷近年已成為區域資料中心投資的主要目的地，電力基礎設施——尤其是直接連接輸電網絡的高壓落地站——已成為開發商競相搶建產能過程中的關鍵瓶頸，也是建設市場中一個日益重要的工程類別。

  本工程預計於2027年1月9日完工。
