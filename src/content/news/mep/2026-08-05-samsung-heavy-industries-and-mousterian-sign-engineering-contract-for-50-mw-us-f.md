body_en: |
  Samsung Heavy Industries (SHI) and Texas-based infrastructure developer Mousterian Corporation (M3) signed an engineering contract on August 3 to design and build moored floating data center (FDC) units delivering 50 megawatts (MW) of IT capacity — the electrical power delivered to computing equipment — per unit, with initial deployments targeted at Texas and other US markets.

  The contract is the first project agreement to flow from a strategic framework the two companies announced in April 2026, converting a stated partnership into active engineering work. M3 described the project as the first moored floating data center deployment planned for the United States. The scope of work covers basic design, detailed engineering, and production design for purpose-built FDC units.

  "Signing this engineering contract transitions our partnership with Samsung Heavy Industries from intent to execution and gives us a clear path to scaling factory-built critical IT capacity for AI-class facilities," said Min Suh, chief executive officer of Mousterian. "We are jointly engineering the first mission-critical data center of its class to be fabricated off-site to shipyard standards."

  Sung-an Choi, vice chairman and chief executive officer of SHI, framed the move as an extension of the company's existing industrial base. "Floating data centers are a natural extension of the engineering, fabrication, and project delivery capabilities we have built over five decades in offshore and maritime construction," Choi said. "Together with Mousterian, we intend to deliver floating data centers with the same rigor, quality, and schedule certainty that define our maritime track record."

  ## Design and cooling approach

  The FDC units use bulk non-evaporative cooling that, according to the companies, consumes no potable water and returns no process discharge to the surrounding waterway. By eliminating air-cooled chillers and cooling fans, SHI and M3 say the design achieves a low power usage effectiveness (PUE) — a ratio measuring how much total facility energy is consumed relative to what IT equipment actually uses, where lower values indicate greater efficiency — and a low noise profile. The units are designed to support liquid-cooled AI computing systems.

  Each unit is moored, meaning it is anchored at a fixed location rather than self-propelled. The companies plan to site the units alongside existing baseload power generation infrastructure, which they say could bring capacity to power sources that lack adjacent land for conventional data center construction.

  Additional partners in the initiative include Capital Clean Energy Carriers, based in Greece, and Lloyd's Register, the maritime classification and standards body.

  ## Fabrication as a scheduling argument

  SHI and M3 argue that shipyard fabrication provides a delivery timeline advantage over conventional ground-up construction. Because a floating unit is built in a controlled shipyard environment, that work can advance at the same time as onshore site preparation rather than after it, Suh said. The companies say this parallel-path approach is intended to reduce deployment timelines and unlock AI-scale capacity faster than land-based methods allow.

  SHI first presented the floating data center concept publicly at the Posidonia 2026 maritime exhibition in June. Earlier in 2026, SHI had signed a memorandum of understanding with several international partners to develop an FDC concept using shipbuilding methods for scalable offshore digital infrastructure.

  ## Growing field of floating data center projects

  The SHI-M3 agreement is one of several floating data center initiatives that have emerged as AI infrastructure demand intensifies and constraints on available land, grid connections, and freshwater for cooling tighten at potential sites.

  Japanese shipping company Mitsui O.S.K. Lines (MOL) partnered with renewable energy firm Kinetics in 2025 to develop what the companies described as the world's first integrated floating data center platform. MOL announced a separate agreement with Hitachi in April 2026 to develop floating data centers using converted vessels, targeting commercial deployment from 2027.

  In China, authorities in Shanghai's Lin-gang Special Area announced the completion of a 24 MW wind-powered underwater data center, backed by an investment of 1.6 billion yuan (approximately $226 million).

  Market research firm QY Research has projected the global floating data center market could reach $593 million by 2031.

  ## Timeline

  SHI is targeting commercialization of the floating data center platform by the second quarter of 2028, according to the Seoul Economic Daily.
body_zh: |
  三星重工（Samsung Heavy Industries，SHI）與總部位於德州的基礎建設開發商Mousterian Corporation（M3）於8月3日正式簽署工程合約，雙方將共同設計並建造系泊式浮動資料中心（Floating Data Center，FDC）設備，每座IT容量——即輸送至運算設備的電力——達50百萬瓦（MW），初期部署目標鎖定德州及美國其他市場。

  此合約是兩家公司於2026年4月宣布戰略合作框架後所簽訂的第一份專案協議，標誌著雙方夥伴關係正式從意向邁向實際工程作業。M3將此專案定位為美國首座規劃中的系泊式浮動資料中心部署案，工作範疇涵蓋浮動資料中心專用設備的基礎設計、詳細工程設計及生產設計。

  Mousterian執行長Min Suh表示：「簽署這份工程合約，讓我們與三星重工的夥伴關係從意向轉為執行，並為擴大AI級設施的工廠化關鍵IT容量提供了清晰的推進路徑。我們正在共同打造同類型中首座在異地依船廠標準建造的關鍵任務資料中心。」

  三星重工副董事長兼執行長崔成安（Sung-an Choi）則將此舉定位為公司現有工業基礎的延伸。他表示：「浮動式資料中心是我們在海上及海事建設領域深耕逾五十年所累積的工程、製造及專案交付能力的自然延伸。我們與Mousterian攜手，將以我們在海事領域一貫的嚴謹態度、品質水準及進度確定性，交付浮動式資料中心。」

  ## 設計與冷卻方式

  FDC設備採用大規模非蒸發式冷卻技術，據兩家公司表示，該系統不消耗民生用水，且不向周圍水域排放任何製程廢水。藉由省去風冷式冷水機及冷卻風扇，SHI與M3表示其設計可實現低電源使用效率（PUE）值——PUE為衡量設施總用電量相對於IT設備實際耗電量的比值，數值越低代表效率越高——同時維持低噪音水準。這些設備專為支援液冷式AI運算系統而設計。

  每座設備均採系泊方式固定，意即錨定於固定位置，而非自行推進。兩家公司計畫將設備設置於現有基載發電基礎設施旁，此舉據稱可為鄰近土地不足、難以興建傳統資料中心的發電端導入容量。

  參與此計畫的合作夥伴還包括總部位於希臘的Capital Clean Energy Carriers，以及海事認證與標準機構勞氏船級社（Lloyd's Register）。

  ## 船廠製造作為縮短工期的論據

  SHI與M3主張，船廠製造在交付時程上較傳統地面建設具有優勢。Min Suh表示，由於浮動設備在受控的船廠環境中建造，工程進度可與岸上場址準備工作同步推進，無需等到場址完工後才開始。兩家公司表示，這種並行推進方式旨在縮短部署時程，讓AI規模的算力容量得以比陸地建設方法更快速地釋出。

  SHI首次公開提出浮動式資料中心概念，是在2026年6月的波賽頓國際海事展（Posidonia 2026）。2026年稍早，SHI已與多家國際夥伴簽署合作備忘錄，計畫以造船方法開發可擴展的海上數位基礎建設FDC概念。

  ## 浮動式資料中心的蓬勃發展

  隨著AI基礎建設需求急速升溫，加上潛在場址的可用土地、電網連接及冷卻用淡水日益緊俏，多項浮動式資料中心計畫相繼湧現，SHI與M3的協議便是其中之一。

  日本航運業者商船三井（Mitsui O.S.K. Lines，MOL）於2025年與再生能源公司Kinetics合作，開發雙方描述為全球首座整合式浮動資料中心平台的專案。MOL亦於2026年4月與日立（Hitachi）簽署另一份協議，計畫利用改裝船隻開發浮動式資料中心，目標在2027年起進行商業部署。

  中國方面，上海臨港新片區當局宣布完成一座24 MW風力發電驅動的水下資料中心，投資規模達16億人民幣（約2億2,600萬美元）。

  市調機構QY Research預測，全球浮動式資料中心市場規模至2031年可望達到5億9,300萬美元。

  ## 時程

  據《首爾經濟日報》報導，SHI的目標是在2028年第二季實現浮動式資料中心平台的商業化。
