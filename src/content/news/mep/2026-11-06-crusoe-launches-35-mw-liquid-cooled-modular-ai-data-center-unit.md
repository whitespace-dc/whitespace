body_en: |
  Crusoe has introduced Spark Liquid, its sixth-generation modular data center platform, designed to deliver up to 3.5 megawatts (MW) of compute capacity per unit and built specifically for Nvidia GB300 NVL72 and Vera Rubin NVL72 racks. The company said the new unit represents five times the compute density of earlier Spark generations and will enter production at its Brighton, Colorado factory in 2027.

  The launch extends a modular development strategy Crusoe says it has pursued for nearly a decade. Crusoe builds AI-optimized data centers and operates the Crusoe Cloud AI platform.

  ## Alignment with Nvidia's DSX POD framework

  Crusoe said Spark Liquid is aligned with Nvidia's DSX POD, a blueprint for modular, factory-integrated infrastructure designed around the Vera Rubin platform. Under the DSX POD model, Nvidia designs and validates the reference cluster architecture while partner companies manufacture, integrate, and service the physical units.

  Crusoe is one of eight container solution providers listed by Nvidia under the DSX POD program, alongside Eaton, Schneider Electric, and Vertiv, among others. Bryan Schramm, head of Spark at Crusoe, drew a distinction between the two offerings.

  "Spark is Crusoe's own design, built in our Colorado factory and operated as Crusoe Cloud capacity," Schramm told Data Center Knowledge. "Nvidia designs and validates the reference design, and partners manufacture, integrate, and service it."

  Nvidia requires customers to have a prepared site with power, water, and network connections in place before deployment.

  ## Closed-loop direct liquid cooling at high rack densities

  Spark Liquid uses a closed-loop direct liquid cooling (DLC) system. DLC removes heat at the point of generation rather than relying on air circulation, which Schramm said is a physical necessity at the power densities the platform targets.

  "DLC pulls heat out at the source far more efficiently than air, and it's necessary at the power density Spark Liquid is built for, because air can't remove that much heat," Schramm said. "The water recirculates in a closed loop, so the system needs minimal ongoing water and has no evaporative loss."

  Efficiency varies by deployment location. "Efficiency at any given site will vary with climate, because of physics, so we design each deployment with its local conditions in mind," Schramm said.

  The units are designed to operate in clusters. Crusoe acquired electrical manufacturer Easter-Owens Electric in 2022 to bring manufacturing capability in-house. The Brighton factory spans 352,000 square feet.

  ## Three converging constraints driving modular demand

  Schramm identified three factors accelerating interest in factory-built data center units: power availability, deployment predictability, and speed to revenue.

  "Three constraints are converging: power, predictability, and time to token," Schramm said. Large grid interconnects can take years to secure, while a modular unit can draw on whatever power is available at a site, including on-site generation, small modular reactors, or solar paired with batteries, in smaller increments closer to demand.

  Building units in a controlled factory environment also improves delivery schedule reliability, which Schramm said matters as organizations reserve capacity in advance. On deployment speed, Schramm said Crusoe can bring capacity online in as little as a few months from a ready site.

  "Demand is growing faster than conventional construction can keep up," he said.

  ## Factory model moves site variability upstream

  Schramm said Crusoe designed Spark Liquid to absorb deployment variability in the factory rather than on-site. "The hard part is variability: every geography and site brings different constraints," he said, adding that the factory model also accelerates the company's ability to adopt and deploy next-generation GPUs.

  He said future deployments will increasingly target sites closer to demand, including locations near robot fleets, sites within sovereign jurisdictions, and campuses in the tens-of-megawatts range. "Sites that size make it easier to find available power, and modular lets us right-size GPU clusters to them," Schramm said.

  ## Industry significance

  Nvidia's decision to standardize a modular reference architecture through DSX POD adds institutional weight to an approach that has until recently been a niche strategy. For Crusoe, it validates a manufacturing model the company has built over nearly a decade and positions its factory output within a broader partner ecosystem that Nvidia is actively promoting.

  As rack power densities continue to rise with successive GPU generations, Schramm said the integration of power delivery and cooling into a single factory-built design becomes increasingly necessary. "DLC becomes the default and rack power keeps climbing, so power delivery and cooling have to be designed together with the unit, which suits modular," he said.

  Spark Liquid production is scheduled to begin at the Brighton, Colorado facility in 2027, joining the existing Spark product line.
body_zh: |
  Crusoe 正式推出第六代模組化資料中心平台 Spark Liquid，每組運算容量最高可達 3.5 百萬瓦（MW），專為輝達 GB300 NVL72 及 Vera Rubin NVL72 機架量身設計。Crusoe 表示，新機組的運算密度是前幾代 Spark 產品的五倍，預計於 2027 年在科羅拉多州布萊頓工廠正式投入量產。

  此次發表延續了 Crusoe 自稱深耕近十年的模組化發展策略。Crusoe 以打造 AI 最佳化資料中心為核心業務，並同步營運 Crusoe Cloud AI 平台。

  ## 與輝達 DSX POD 架構接軌

  Crusoe 表示，Spark Liquid 符合輝達 DSX POD 規範。DSX POD 是一套圍繞 Vera Rubin 平台所設計、以模組化與工廠整合基礎設施為核心的藍圖。在此模式下，輝達負責設計與驗證參考叢集架構，合作夥伴則負責製造、整合及維護實體機組。

  Crusoe 是輝達 DSX POD 計畫旗下列名的八家貨櫃解決方案供應商之一，同列名單的還有伊頓（Eaton）、施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）及威圖（Vertiv）等業者。Crusoe 旗下 Spark 業務負責人 Bryan Schramm 特別說明兩者之間的差異。

  「Spark 是 Crusoe 自主設計的產品，在我們科羅拉多州的工廠生產，並以 Crusoe Cloud 容量的形式運營，」Schramm 向《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）表示。「輝達負責設計與驗證參考設計，合作夥伴則負責製造、整合與服務。」

  輝達要求客戶在部署前，必須先備妥電力、供水及網路連線等場地條件。

  ## 高機架密度下的封閉式直接液冷系統

  Spark Liquid 採用封閉式直接液冷（DLC）系統。直接液冷直接在熱源產生的當下進行散熱，而非仰賴空氣循環——Schramm 表示，在該平台所瞄準的高功率密度環境下，這是物理上的必要選擇。

  「直接液冷在源頭取熱的效率遠高於空氣冷卻，在 Spark Liquid 所設計的功率密度下，這是不可或缺的，因為空氣根本無法帶走如此大量的熱，」Schramm 說。「水在封閉迴路中持續循環，因此系統僅需極少量的補水，也不會有蒸發耗損。」

  效率表現因部署地點而有所差異。「由於物理特性，各場地的效率隨氣候不同而有所差異，因此我們在設計每一套部署方案時，都會將當地條件納入考量，」Schramm 說。

  這些機組設計以叢集方式運作。Crusoe 於 2022 年收購了電氣設備製造商 Easter-Owens Electric，將製造能力納入自有體系。布萊頓工廠廠房面積達 35.2 萬平方英尺（約 3.27 萬平方公尺）。

  ## 推動模組化需求的三大匯聚限制

  Schramm 點出三項加速工廠預建型資料中心機組需求的因素：電力可用性、部署可預期性，以及縮短收益實現的時間。

  「三大限制正在同時收緊：電力、可預期性，以及從部署到產生推論輸出的時間，」Schramm 說。大型電網互聯往往需要數年才能取得，而模組化機組可以利用場地現有的任何電力——包括現場自發電、小型模組化反應爐，或與電池搭配的太陽能系統——以更小的規模更靠近需求端逐步部署。

  在受控的工廠環境中建造機組，同樣有助於提高交貨時程的可靠性。Schramm 表示，隨著企業提前預約容量，這一點日益重要。在部署速度方面，他表示 Crusoe 從場地準備就緒到容量上線，最快只需幾個月。

  「需求成長的速度已超過傳統建設所能跟上的速度，」他說。

  ## 工廠模式將場地變數前移化解

  Schramm 表示，Crusoe 設計 Spark Liquid 時，著眼於在工廠階段就消化掉部署的不確定性，而非留到現場才應對。「難點在於變數：每個地區和場地都有不同的限制，」他說，並補充工廠模式同時也加快了公司導入與部署新世代 GPU 的能力。

  他表示，未來的部署將愈來愈瞄準靠近需求端的場地，包括機器人車隊附近的地點、位於主權管轄範圍內的場地，以及規模達數十百萬瓦的園區。「這種規模的場地更容易找到可用電力，而模組化讓我們能夠依場地量身配置 GPU 叢集，」Schramm 說。

  ## 產業意義

  輝達決定透過 DSX POD 推動模組化參考架構的標準化，為這套長期以來屬於利基策略的作法賦予了機構層面的分量。對 Crusoe 而言，這不僅驗證了公司近十年來所建立的製造模式，也將其工廠產能定位在輝達積極推廣的更廣泛合作夥伴生態系統之中。

  隨著歷代 GPU 世代更迭、機架功率密度持續攀升，Schramm 表示，將電力輸送與冷卻整合至單一工廠預建設計中的必要性也與日俱增。「直接液冷將成為預設標準，機架功率持續攀升，因此電力輸送與冷卻必須與機組一體化設計——這正是模組化的用武之地，」他說。

  Spark Liquid 的量產計畫預計於 2027 年在科羅拉多州布萊頓工廠正式啟動，加入現有的 Spark 產品線。
