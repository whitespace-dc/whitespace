body_en: |
  Basic building maintenance failures, including rodent infestations, overgrown brush, and faulty irrigation systems, pose a growing and underappreciated threat to the US data center sector, Kade Thomas of Emory Oak Partners argued in an analysis published November 1, 2026. Thomas contended that as investors accelerate construction to meet surging demand, the industry is overlooking facility upkeep risks that could compound into costly outages.

  More than 3,000 data center sites, ranging from fully operational facilities to construction-approved projects, exist across the US, according to Thomas, with the total growing week over week. Amazon, Meta, Alphabet, and Microsoft alone invested $400 billion in 2025, a figure Thomas projected would increase again in 2026. One hour of unexpected downtime can cost a business $540,000, with the total reaching into the millions for large enterprises, Thomas noted.

  ## Fire and weather exposure

  Data center fires account for more than 50 percent of the $800 million in losses and claims related to the artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure buildout across North America, Thomas wrote. Nearly 80 percent of all data centers globally face acute risk from extreme weather, including wildfires and floods, according to the analysis.

  Accumulations of dry leaves and unmanaged vegetation can obstruct HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) airways and increase fire risk, particularly in wildfire-prone states. Texas, which Thomas cited as having more than 300 active data centers, is among the states where such conditions can escalate quickly.

  In flood-prone areas such as Virginia, faulty irrigation systems can compound water intrusion risk, Thomas argued. If a site's electrical wiring is submerged, questions about grid connectivity become irrelevant, he wrote.

  ## Rodent damage

  Rodents seek out warm environments, making data centers attractive nesting sites. Infestations can result in damage to cable fiber trunks, wire insulation, and plastic conduits, causing outages and operational delays, Thomas wrote.

  ## Speed of buildout driving gaps

  Thomas attributed the maintenance shortfall to investor pressure to bring sites online as quickly as possible. That haste creates conditions in which facilities management is deprioritized, with problems such as mislaid irrigation lines resulting from rushed construction, he argued. Building and site managers also tend to have limited experience with data centers as a relatively new facility type, leaving teams underprepared for how maintenance problems can compound, according to the analysis.

  The industry's focus on power grid connectivity has further displaced attention from basic building upkeep, Thomas argued. Firms are concentrating on connecting to the grid to the point that other factors in a data center's operational success are falling aside, he wrote.

  ## Significance for the sector

  The scale of capital at stake makes operational reliability non-negotiable, Thomas argued. With more than 3,000 US sites in play and major technology companies committing hundreds of billions of dollars annually, maintenance failures carry systemic financial risk. As demand for AI grows, pressure on site performance increases further, he wrote.

  Thomas called on site managers, investors, and AI firms to evaluate all operational risks, including basic building upkeep, before failures occur. The number of active and planned US data center sites was continuing to grow as of the analysis's publication date, with no indication of a slowdown.

  ## 資料中心維護缺失恐危及美國4,000億美元建設大計，分析師示警

  Emory Oak Partners分析師凱德・湯瑪斯於2026年11月1日發布分析報告，指出鼠害侵擾、植被蔓生及灌溉系統失效等基礎建築維護問題，正對美國資料中心產業構成日益嚴峻且遭外界低估的威脅。湯瑪斯主張，隨著投資人加速擴建以因應激增的市場需求，業界正在忽視機房設施維護風險，而這些風險一旦累積，恐將釀成代價高昂的服務中斷事故。

  據湯瑪斯統計，美國目前已有逾3,000座資料中心站點，涵蓋已正式營運的設施及已獲建設核准的計畫案，且數量每週仍持續攀升。光是亞馬遜、Meta、Alphabet與微軟四家企業，2025年的投資總額便已達4,000億美元，湯瑪斯預估2026年這一數字將再度成長。他也指出，一旦發生非預期停機，每小時將使企業損失54萬美元，對大型企業而言，總損失更可能高達數百萬美元。

  ## 火災與極端天氣風險

  湯瑪斯在報告中寫道，資料中心火災所造成的損失與理賠，已占北美人工智慧基礎設施建設相關事故總金額8億美元的逾五成。報告並引用數據指出，全球近八成資料中心正面臨極端天氣的嚴峻威脅，包括野火與洪患。

  乾枯落葉堆積及未妥善管理的植被，可能阻塞空調（HVAC）進排氣通道，並提高火災風險，在野火好發州份尤為顯著。湯瑪斯特別點名德州，該州目前擁有逾300座活躍資料中心，在上述條件下，情況可能迅速惡化失控。

  在易受洪患侵襲的地區，例如維吉尼亞州，灌溉系統一旦故障，更可能加劇水患滲入的風險。湯瑪斯在報告中直言，若一座設施的電氣線路遭到浸水，討論電網連線能力便已毫無意義。

  ## 鼠害損害

  囓齒類動物偏好溫暖環境，使資料中心成為牠們理想的築巢場所。湯瑪斯指出，一旦發生鼠害，可能造成光纖幹線纜束、電線絕緣層及塑膠線管遭到啃咬破壞，進而引發服務中斷與營運延誤。

  ## 建設速度過快埋下隱患

  湯瑪斯將維護缺口的成因，歸咎於投資人急於讓站點盡快上線的壓力。他主張，這種躁進心態導致設施管理遭到降級處理，倉促施工更衍生出灌溉管線鋪設錯位等問題。報告也指出，由於資料中心作為建築類型相對新穎，建築與場地管理人員普遍缺乏相關經驗，致使團隊對維護問題如何連鎖惡化的認識明顯不足。

  湯瑪斯進一步主張，業界將注意力過度集中於電網連線，已使基礎建築維護工作受到排擠。他在報告中寫道，業者一心專注於接入電網，以致影響資料中心穩定運營的其他關鍵因素逐漸遭到忽視。

  ## 對整體產業的重要意義

  湯瑪斯強調，龐大的投入資本使營運可靠性成為不容妥協的底線。在全美逾3,000個站點持續運作，且各大科技企業每年承諾投入數千億美元的背景下，維護缺失將帶來系統性的財務風險。他並指出，隨著AI需求持續成長，各站點所承受的效能壓力也將同步攀升。

  湯瑪斯呼籲站點管理人員、投資人及AI企業，在問題發生之前，全面評估所有營運風險，包括基礎建築維護在內，不得有所遺漏。截至本份分析報告發布之日，美國活躍及規劃中的資料中心站點數量仍持續增加，未見任何放緩跡象。
