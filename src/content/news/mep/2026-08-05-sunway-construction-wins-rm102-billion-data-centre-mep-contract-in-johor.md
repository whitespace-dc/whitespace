body_en: |
  Sunway Construction Group Bhd has secured a RM1.02 billion (approximately USD 230 million) engineering contract for a data centre in Johor, Malaysia, awarded by a US-headquartered multinational technology company whose identity was not disclosed, the company said in an exchange filing on Aug 3.

  The contract covers mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) fit-out work — the installation of power, cooling, and utility systems that make a shell building operational as a data centre. The letter of award also served as a notice-to-proceed, meaning construction began upon signing. Sunway Construction said the project is expected to reach completion by the fourth quarter of 2027.

  ## Order book and financial position

  The award raises Sunway Construction's new order wins for the financial year ending March 31, 2027 (FY2027) to RM5.8 billion. The company's total outstanding order book — work contracted but not yet completed — now stands at RM10.4 billion.

  Shares of Sunway Construction closed 17 sen, or 2.15%, lower at RM7.75 on Monday following the announcement. Despite the session decline, the stock has risen 36.93% year-to-date, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM10.32 billion.

  ## What the contract covers

  The scope is limited to MEP fit-out, which is the trade-specific layer of data centre construction that encompasses electrical distribution, uninterruptible power supplies, cooling infrastructure, and plumbing systems. MEP fit-out contracts are typically awarded after a facility's civil and structural shell is underway or complete, and they represent a significant portion of total data centre construction cost given the density and redundancy of systems required.

  The exact location within Johor state and the name of the client were not disclosed in the filing.

  ## Significance for Malaysia's data centre market

  Johor has emerged as a primary destination for hyperscale and large-scale data centre investment in Southeast Asia, driven by land availability, power access, and proximity to Singapore. A RM1.02 billion MEP contract from a single US technology multinational reflects the scale of builds now entering the construction phase in the state. For Sunway Construction, the award demonstrates continued demand for its data centre MEP capabilities and contributes materially to an order book that now exceeds RM10 billion.

  Sunway Construction's FY2027 new order wins of RM5.8 billion, with this contract as the latest addition, represent the pipeline the company will execute through the period ending March 2027.

  ## 新威建設奪柔佛資料中心MEP合約，金額逾10億馬幣

  新威建設集團（Sunway Construction Group Bhd）於8月3日向交易所申報，宣布獲得一份價值10.2億馬幣（約2.3億美元）的馬來西亞柔佛州資料中心工程合約，業主為一家總部設於美國的跨國科技公司，惟對方身分未予披露。

  此合約涵蓋機械、電氣及水管（MEP）裝修工程，包括電力、冷卻及公用設施系統的安裝，旨在將一棟外殼建築轉化為可正式運作的資料中心。頒獎函同時作為開工通知，意即合約簽署後施工即刻展開。新威建設表示，本專案預計於2027年第四季竣工。

  ## 訂單簿與財務狀況

  此次得標使新威建設於截至2027年3月31日止財政年度（FY2027）的新接訂單累計達58億馬幣，公司未完成合約總額——即已簽約但尚未完工的工程量——亦隨之增至104億馬幣。

  消息公布後，新威建設股價週一收跌17仙，跌幅2.15%，報每股7.75馬幣。儘管單日收低，該股今年迄今仍累漲36.93%，公司市值達103.2億馬幣。

  ## 合約工程範疇

  本合約範疇僅限於MEP裝修工程，屬資料中心建設中專業分工的核心環節，涵蓋電力配電、不斷電系統（UPS）、冷卻基礎設施及水管系統。MEP裝修合約通常於廠房土建與結構外殼工程進行中或竣工後才會發包，鑒於資料中心系統的高密度與備援需求，此類合約往往佔整體建設成本相當大的比重。

  申報文件未披露業主名稱及廠區在柔佛州的確切位置。

  ## 對馬來西亞資料中心市場的意義

  柔佛州憑藉充裕的土地供給、穩定的電力取得以及毗鄰新加坡的地理優勢，已成為東南亞超大規模及大型資料中心投資的首選目的地。單一美國科技跨國企業即發出逾10億馬幣的MEP合約，充分反映出目前進入施工階段的建設規模之大。對新威建設而言，此案不僅印證了市場對其資料中心MEP能力的持續需求，亦對現已突破百億馬幣的未完成訂單簿做出實質貢獻。

  新威建設FY2027新接訂單達58億馬幣，本合約為其中最新一筆，代表公司將於截至2027年3月止的執行期間陸續推進的工程管線。
