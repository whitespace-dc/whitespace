# Electrician Shortage Is AI Infrastructure's Real Bottleneck

While GPU and RAM scarcity dominate discussions of AI capacity constraints, a shortage of skilled electricians and infrastructure specialists has emerged as the more critical limiting factor for data center expansion. Power distribution, cooling systems, and large-scale deployment require experienced professionals whose shortage is causing longer timelines than semiconductor supply chains alone. The underlying infrastructure expertise gap is now the primary constraint on AI infrastructure buildout.

## 中文

# 電工短缺成AI基礎設施真正瓶頸

儘管GPU與記憶體短缺主導了AI容量限制的業界討論，電工與基礎設施專家的人才短缺已成為資料中心擴展更關鍵的限制因素。電力配送、冷卻系統與大規模部署需要具備經驗的專業人員，其短缺造成的延遲比半導體供應鏈短缺更為嚴重。基礎設施人才缺口已成為AI資料中心擴建的首要制約因素。
