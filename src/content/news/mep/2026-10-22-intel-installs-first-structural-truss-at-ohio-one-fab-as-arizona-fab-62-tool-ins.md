body_en: |
  Intel has installed the first major structural truss at the first fab on its Ohio One campus in New Albany, Ohio, marking a visible construction milestone at a site first announced in January 2022. Separately, the company said tool installation has begun at Fab 62, its sixth high-volume manufacturing facility under construction in Arizona, though that facility remains incomplete.

  The Ohio One campus spans 1,000 acres east of Columbus. When Intel announced the project in 2022, it said production would begin in 2025. That schedule has since shifted materially. In a February 2025 report to the Ohio Department of Development, Intel said it now projects completing construction of the first Ohio fab in 2030, with operations beginning in 2030 or 2031. Construction of a second fab on the campus is projected to finish in 2031, with operations beginning in 2032.

  ## Federal funding and the CHIPS Act context

  Intel's US expansion has drawn substantial federal support under the CHIPS and Science Act, legislation designed to incentivize domestic semiconductor manufacturing. In March 2024, Intel and the White House announced a preliminary memorandum of terms for up to $8.5 billion in direct federal funding, alongside investment tax credits and low-interest loans. That support covers fab construction across four states: Arizona, New Mexico, Ohio, and Oregon. The announcement projected the investment would support approximately 10,000 Intel jobs and 20,000 construction-sector jobs.

  ## Arizona Fab 62 and the path to operations

  Fab 62, located near Chandler, Arizona, is still under construction, but Intel said tool installation — the process of placing and connecting semiconductor manufacturing equipment inside a fab shell — has begun. Tool installation is a significant step in bringing a fab online, though it precedes equipment calibration and the start of wafer production. Intel has indicated it plans to begin operations at Fab 62 by the end of 2028.

  ## Strategic shifts since the original announcements

  Intel's foundry strategy has evolved considerably since the Ohio One announcement. In September 2024, the company restructured its foundry operation into an independent subsidiary. Around the same time, Intel announced a multi-year agreement with Amazon Web Services (AWS) to produce custom chips using Intel's 18A process node — a leading-edge manufacturing technology the company is developing. Intel also paused planned fab projects in Poland and Germany by approximately two years, citing market demand conditions and what the source describes as a capital crunch, while reaffirming its commitment to US sites.

  ## US manufacturing footprint

  Beyond Ohio and Arizona, Intel's domestic activity includes packaging operations in New Mexico, which the company has described as a significant growth driver for its foundry business; research and development investment in Oregon; and, in Santa Clara, California, a recently broken-ground mask manufacturing facility. Photomasks are precision templates used to pattern circuits onto silicon wafers, and domestic mask production supports the broader fab supply chain.

  ## What the milestones mean

  The truss installation at Ohio One is an early structural step; a fab must still complete its building shell, receive and install equipment, calibrate that equipment, and begin running wafers before it can produce chips at volume. Tool installation at Fab 62 is further along that sequence but likewise precedes production. For the domestic semiconductor supply chain, the pace at which Intel can bring these facilities to volume production — and attract customers to fill that capacity — will determine whether the infrastructure investment translates into competitive foundry output.

  Intel projects Fab 62 in Arizona will begin operations by the end of 2028, with the first Ohio fab following in 2030 or 2031.
body_zh: |
  英特爾已在俄亥俄州新奧爾巴尼（New Albany）Ohio One園區的第一座晶圓廠完成首根主要結構桁架安裝，為這座2022年1月首度對外宣布的建廠計畫寫下肉眼可見的施工里程碑。與此同時，英特爾另行宣布，位於亞利桑那州的Fab 62——其在當地興建中的第六座高產量製造廠——已正式啟動設備進場作業，惟該廠目前仍在施工當中。

  Ohio One園區坐落於哥倫布市（Columbus）以東，占地約1,000英畝（約405公頃）。英特爾於2022年宣布這項計畫時，預計2025年即可投入生產。然而，時程此後出現重大延誤。英特爾在2025年2月向俄亥俄州發展部（Ohio Department of Development）提交的報告中指出，第一座俄亥俄廠的建設預計將於2030年完工，正式投產時間則落在2030年或2031年；園區內第二座晶圓廠的建設則預計2031年完成，2032年開始運作。

  ## 聯邦補助與《晶片與科學法》背景

  英特爾在美國的擴產計畫獲得聯邦政府大力支持，資金來源為旨在激勵國內半導體製造的《晶片與科學法》（CHIPS and Science Act）。2024年3月，英特爾與白宮共同宣布一份初步條款備忘錄，內容包含最高85億美元（約新台幣2,700億元）的聯邦直接補助，以及投資稅收抵免和低利率貸款。這批支持涵蓋英特爾在亞利桑那州、新墨西哥州、俄亥俄州及奧勒岡州四個州的建廠計畫。當時的聲明預估，相關投資將支撐英特爾約10,000個工作機會，並帶動建築業約20,000個就業職缺。

  ## 亞利桑那州Fab 62與投產時程

  Fab 62位於亞利桑那州錢德勒（Chandler）附近，目前仍在施工中，但英特爾表示設備進場作業已經展開。所謂設備進場，是指將半導體製造設備搬運至廠房結構內並完成配管配線的過程，是晶圓廠正式上線的重要環節，但仍早於設備校準與晶圓生產正式啟動。英特爾表示，Fab 62預計於2028年底前投入生產。

  ## 原始公告後的策略調整

  自Ohio One計畫公布以來，英特爾的晶圓代工策略已歷經相當大的轉變。2024年9月，英特爾將晶圓代工部門重組為獨立子公司。幾乎同一時期，英特爾宣布與亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）簽訂多年期協議，將以英特爾正在開發的前沿製程節點18A為AWS生產客製化晶片。此外，英特爾也以市場需求狀況及資金緊縮為由，將原訂在波蘭與德國的建廠計畫各延後約兩年，但同時重申對美國廠址的持續承諾。

  ## 美國製造版圖

  除俄亥俄州與亞利桑那州之外，英特爾在美國境內的布局還包括：新墨西哥州的先進封裝廠，英特爾將其定位為晶圓代工業務的重要成長引擎；奧勒岡州持續進行的研發投資；以及加州聖塔克拉拉（Santa Clara）一座甫破土動工的光罩製造廠。光罩是用於將電路圖案轉印至矽晶圓的精密模板，國內自製光罩的能力可為更完整的晶圓廠供應鏈提供支撐。

  ## 這些里程碑代表什麼意義

  Ohio One的桁架安裝屬於早期結構工程階段，晶圓廠後續還須完成廠房主體、設備進場與安裝、設備校準，並正式開始跑晶圓，才能實現大量生產。Fab 62的設備進場雖然在流程上更為超前，但同樣尚未達到量產階段。就國內半導體供應鏈而言，英特爾能以多快的速度讓這些廠房達到量產規模，並吸引客戶填滿產能，將決定這批基礎建設投資能否真正轉化為具競爭力的晶圓代工產出。

  英特爾預計Fab 62將於2028年底前在亞利桑那州投入量產，俄亥俄州第一座晶圓廠則緊接其後，預計於2030年或2031年正式上線。
