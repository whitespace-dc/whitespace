  ## US policy and Permian Basin volumes put produced water on data center agenda

  The US Environmental Protection Agency's Water Reuse Action Plan 2.0, launched in April 2026, lists accelerating water reuse for data center cooling among its priorities, bringing renewed attention to produced water — the water extracted alongside oil and gas during drilling — as an alternative to potable supplies. Jonathan Smith of tank manufacturer Balmoral Tanks, writing in Data Center Dynamics, argues that turning policy intent into working infrastructure requires storage systems designed around the specific conditions of each site, and that those decisions must be made before designs are fixed at tender.

  The scale of the available resource is substantial. According to forecasts from water intelligence provider B3 Insight, the Permian Basin — the prolific oil-producing region straddling West Texas and southeastern New Mexico — was expected to produce approximately 22.3 million barrels of water per day on average during 2025. Of that volume, roughly 16 million barrels per day went to disposal rather than reuse. Where produced water can be redirected toward data center cooling, Smith says, it reduces pressure on potable supplies while making use of an existing industrial byproduct.

  ## Produced water varies, and storage systems must reflect that

  Produced water is not a uniform resource. Depending on the formation it comes from, it may be brackish (moderately salty), highly saline, or laden with minerals and other substances that affect how tanks and their internal coatings must be specified, Smith writes. That variability means the storage system has to be designed around the water that will actually arrive at a given site rather than a generic industrial water standard.

  Data centers already operate complex water handling systems. A single facility may require separate storage for brine, pre-treated water, treated industrial water, clarified water, filtered water, clean water, and fire protection — each stream held under its own conditions and in its own dedicated capacity. Adding produced water introduces another duty to that system.

  Material selection determines whether a tank can withstand the chemical properties of produced water over its full service life. Smith identifies fusion-bonded epoxy as well suited to produced water and other industrial water duties, citing its corrosion resistance and durability during transport and installation. Glass-fused-to-steel — a process in which glass is bonded to steel panels at high temperature — is described as suitable in some applications. Galvanized steel and sectional glass-reinforced plastic (GRP) tanks serve other duties on data center sites.

  ## Early specification prevents costly redesigns

  Smith argues that the most consequential decisions are made — or missed — before a project reaches tender. Once a tank type and material are written into a specification, there is typically little room to change them, he writes, citing Balmoral's direct experience on data center projects dating to around 2010 across duties including clean water, fire protection, brine, effluent, and treated industrial water.

  Available footprint shapes the choice of tank type: a cylindrical tank may work in an external compound, while a sectional or shaped tank is more practical in a basement. Temperature is another variable specific to the Permian Basin context. Summer temperatures in the region can reach extremes, making the tank's location, exposure, and material selection relevant to both performance and longevity. The design must also preserve space for pipework and maintenance access — placing a required storage volume into whatever space remains after other site elements are fixed is not equivalent to a properly planned installation, Smith writes.

  ## Lifetime cost, not purchase price, is the right measure

  Data centers are designed for multi-decade operational lives, and Smith contends their water infrastructure should be evaluated over the same horizon. Industrial epoxy and glass-fused-to-steel tanks can be engineered for operating lives of approximately 35 to 45 years, depending on the application and maintenance regime. Some lined or plastic systems, he writes, may require substantial remediation substantially earlier, meaning an underspecified tank can force disruption and repair costs well before the data center itself reaches end of life.

  The specification should also accommodate changes in the produced water over time and allow for future expansion, rather than being sized and selected solely for opening-day conditions.

  ## Procurement chains can dilute the original specification

  Even a well-drafted specification faces execution risk. On data center projects, where programs are compressed and responsibility passes through multiple contractors and subcontractors before the finished system reaches the operator, the original material and quality requirements can be eroded, Smith writes. Data center operators typically demand detailed documentation confirming that materials and installation match what was approved — a level of scrutiny he compares to oil and gas sector standards, particularly for cooling and fire protection infrastructure.

  Tank manufacturers and installation partners must therefore carry both the technical capability to supply correctly specified tanks and the organizational and financial capacity to meet program requirements. Smith notes that local manufacturing can reduce transport complexity, a factor he identifies as particularly relevant in fast-growing US markets.

  ## What it means for the sector

  Produced water reuse for data center cooling remains in an early stage, with trial projects and contracts under way. The EPA's Water Reuse Action Plan 2.0 signals federal support for scaling the practice, and the volume of water currently going to disposal in the Permian Basin represents a resource that, with appropriate infrastructure, could reduce data center dependence on municipal and agricultural water supplies in water-stressed regions.

  The practical constraint, Smith concludes, is infrastructure design: the water chemistry must be characterized early, each storage duty defined clearly, and tank selection made against the actual site conditions rather than generic assumptions. The US EPA's Water Reuse Action Plan 2.0 identifies data center cooling reuse as a priority for acceleration.

  ## 美國政策與帕米安盆地水量使採出水進入資料中心議程

  美國環境保護署（EPA）於2026年4月推出《水資源再利用行動計畫2.0》，將加速資料中心冷卻用水的再利用列為重要政策目標，使採出水——即鑽探石油與天然氣時伴隨產出的地層水——重新獲得各界關注，被視為替代自來水的可行選項。儲水槽製造商Balmoral Tanks的Jonathan Smith在《Data Center Dynamics》撰文指出，要將政策意圖落實為可運作的基礎設施，必須根據各場址的實際條件設計儲存系統，且這些決策必須在設計定案送交招標之前便確立。

  可用的資源規模相當可觀。根據水資源情報供應商B3 Insight的預測，橫跨德克薩斯州西部與新墨西哥州東南部的帕米安盆地——美國產油量最豐沛的地區之一——2025年全年平均每日預計產出約2,230萬桶採出水。其中約1,600萬桶每日被直接注入地下處置，而非循環再利用。Smith表示，若能將採出水轉用於資料中心冷卻，不僅可減輕對自來水供應的壓力，更能善加利用既有的工業副產品。

  ## 採出水成分各異，儲存系統設計須因地制宜

  採出水並非均質的資源。Smith指出，依據來源地層的不同，採出水可能呈現微鹹（鹽度適中）、高鹽度，或是富含礦物質及其他物質的特性，這些都直接影響儲水槽本體及其內部塗層的規格選用。這種多變性意味著儲存系統必須依照特定場址實際引入的水質來設計，而非套用通用的工業用水標準。

  資料中心本身就運作著複雜的用水管理系統。單一設施可能需要分別儲存鹽水、前處理水、處理後工業用水、澄清水、過濾水、潔淨水以及消防用水，每一類水流都有其特定的儲存條件與專屬容量。引入採出水，等於為這套系統增添了另一項使用需求。

  材料選擇決定了儲水槽能否在整個使用年限內承受採出水的化學特性。Smith指出，熔接環氧樹脂（fusion-bonded epoxy）非常適合用於採出水及其他工業用水場合，原因在於其耐腐蝕性強，且在運輸與安裝過程中同樣具備良好的耐用性。玻璃熔接鋼（glass-fused-to-steel）——一種在高溫下將玻璃與鋼板結合的製程——在某些應用場合中亦是合適選項。鍍鋅鋼與分節式玻璃纖維強化塑膠（GRP）槽則適用於資料中心場址的其他用途。

  ## 前期規格確立可避免後期代價高昂的設計變更

  Smith主張，最關鍵的決策往往在專案進入招標程序之前便已確立——或已錯失時機。他寫道，一旦某種槽型與材料被寫入規格書，幾乎就沒有更動的餘地，並援引Balmoral自約2010年起參與資料中心專案的親身經驗，涵蓋潔淨水、消防、鹽水、污水及處理後工業用水等各類用途。

  可用地坪的形狀左右了儲水槽類型的選擇：圓柱形槽適合設置於戶外圍合區域，而分節式或異形槽則更適合地下室等受限空間。溫度是帕米安盆地場址的另一項特殊變數——該地區夏季氣溫可達極端高溫，使得儲水槽的位置、曝曬情況與材料選用直接關係到效能與使用壽命。設計方案也必須為管線與維修通道預留足夠空間——Smith指出，將所需儲存容量硬塞進其他場址元素排定後剩餘的空間，絕非等同於妥善規劃的配置。

  ## 衡量標準應是全生命週期成本，而非採購價格

  資料中心的設計年限往往長達數十年，Smith認為其用水基礎設施也應以相同的時間尺度來評估。工業用環氧樹脂槽與玻璃熔接鋼槽，依應用場合與維護方式不同，可設計為約35至45年的使用壽命。他寫道，某些內襯或塑膠系統可能在遠未達到這個年限之前便需要大規模修復，這意味著規格不足的儲水槽可能在資料中心本身退役之前，就已迫使業者承擔停工整修的高昂代價。

  規格設計也應預先考量採出水水質隨時間的潛在變化，並為未來擴充保留彈性，而非單純依照開幕當日的條件進行尺寸與規格選定。

  ## 採購鏈可能稀釋原始規格要求

  即便規格書撰寫得無懈可擊，執行過程仍潛藏風險。Smith指出，資料中心專案工期緊湊，責任在多層承包商與分包商之間輾轉交接，原始的材料與品質要求可能在此過程中逐漸遭到稀釋。資料中心業者通常要求提供詳盡的交付文件，確認材料與施工確實符合核准規格——他認為這種審查嚴謹度可與石油與天然氣產業的標準相比，在冷卻與消防基礎設施方面尤為如此。

  因此，儲水槽製造商與安裝合作夥伴不僅必須具備正確規格供貨的技術能力，還須擁有符合工程進度要求的組織能力與財務實力。Smith並指出，本地製造可降低運輸作業的複雜度，這在快速成長的美國市場中是特別值得關注的因素。

  ## 對產業的整體意涵

  採出水再利用於資料中心冷卻目前仍處於早期發展階段，部分試驗性專案與合約正在推進之中。EPA的《水資源再利用行動計畫2.0》傳達出聯邦政府支持擴大推廣這一做法的明確訊號，而帕米安盆地目前大量注入地下處置的採出水，若能建立起適當的基礎設施，將有潛力降低資料中心在缺水地區對市政供水與農業用水的依賴。

  Smith最終結論指出，實際的制約因素在於基礎設施設計：必須在早期就確立水質特性，清楚界定每一類儲存用途，並依據場址的實際條件選用儲水槽，而非依賴通用假設。美國EPA的《水資源再利用行動計畫2.0》已明確將資料中心冷卻再利用列為優先加速推動的項目。
