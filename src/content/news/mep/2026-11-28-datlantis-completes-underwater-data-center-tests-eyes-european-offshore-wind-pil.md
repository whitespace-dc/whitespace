body_en: |
  Swedish startup Datlantis has completed initial prototype tests of its underwater data center modules and is preparing for a commercial pilot across several European locations, the Stockholm-based company said November 27. Separately, Russian hosting provider RuVDS disclosed plans to deploy a floating data center aboard a vessel bound for Antarctica.

  ## Datlantis prototype tests

  Datlantis said it validated its high-density cooling system and what it described as "flexible maintenance operations" in a real underwater environment, including conditions with changing currents and tides. The company posted video footage of the trial on LinkedIn. It did not disclose the location of the test or confirm whether active compute hardware was on board during the exercise.

  Chief Executive Miguel Marques, a marine engineer with a background in offshore wind, said the tests also examined the environmental footprint of the modules, specifically their thermal effect on surrounding water and interactions with marine life.

  ## Design and power strategy

  According to its website, Datlantis intends to connect its underwater modules directly to offshore wind installations, drawing power from the turbines. The company says it plans to use robotic systems for maintenance — a practical requirement given the difficulty of human access to submerged equipment — and to exploit cold ocean temperatures as a passive cooling resource, reducing or eliminating the need for energy-intensive mechanical cooling.

  Marques said the company is now preparing for its first pilot, in which it plans to offer high-density IT infrastructure at several locations across Europe.

  ## Competitive landscape

  Datlantis is not the only company pursuing offshore or underwater data center concepts. Aikido and Exascale Labs have each proposed offshore wind-powered data center designs, according to the Data Center Dynamics report. HiCloud, meanwhile, claims to have an operational underwater unit already attached to a turbine off the coast of China.

  ## RuVDS Antarctic floating deployment

  In a separate announcement, RuVDS said it is testing a floating data center that will be deployed aboard a vessel taking part in a scientific expedition to Antarctica. According to Russian state news agency Tass, the platform will provide computing support for scientists attempting the first-ever ascent of Mount Siple, described as one of the last unclimbed peaks on the planet.

  RuVDS said the system, based on several servers, will support hundreds of simultaneous virtual machines to cover the scientists' workloads during the expedition. Chief Executive Nikita Tsaplin said the voyage would also function as a test of server performance under potentially hazardous maritime conditions. The party is scheduled to depart in January and return in March.

  ## Industry significance

  Both announcements reflect expanding efforts to move compute infrastructure offshore and underwater — configurations that offer potential advantages in cooling efficiency through access to cold seawater, and in power sourcing through proximity to offshore wind generation. Neither approach has been demonstrated at commercial scale. Datlantis's progression from prototype testing to a named pilot phase represents a concrete step toward resolving open technical questions around maintenance, reliability, and environmental impact in live undersea conditions. The RuVDS Antarctic deployment will generate operational data on floating server performance in an extreme maritime environment.

  Datlantis said its European pilot locations will offer high-density IT infrastructure, with specific sites not yet disclosed.

  瑞典新創公司Datlantis於11月27日表示，已完成水下資料中心模組的初期原型測試，並正積極籌備在歐洲多個地點展開商業試點。同日，俄羅斯主機服務商RuVDS亦宣布，計畫在一艘前往南極的船隻上部署浮動資料中心。

  ## Datlantis原型測試

  Datlantis表示，已在真實水下環境中驗證其高密度冷卻系統及所稱的「彈性維護作業」能力，測試過程涵蓋洋流與潮汐變化等多變海況條件。該公司在LinkedIn上發布了此次試驗的影片，但未透露測試地點，也未確認水下模組在試驗期間是否搭載實際運算硬體。

  執行長米格爾·馬爾克斯（Miguel Marques）為海洋工程師出身，具備離岸風電領域背景。他表示，此次測試亦針對模組對環境的影響進行評估，重點涵蓋模組對周圍水溫產生的熱效應，以及與海洋生物的互動情形。

  ## 設計理念與供電策略

  根據公司官網資訊，Datlantis計畫將水下模組直接與離岸風力發電設施相連，從渦輪機直接取電。由於潛水設備的人工維護作業難度極高，該公司表示將採用機器人系統執行維護工作——這是實際作業上不可或缺的配置。此外，Datlantis也計畫善用低溫海水作為被動式冷卻資源，藉此降低乃至完全免除耗能的機械冷卻需求。

  馬爾克斯表示，公司目前正積極籌備首個商業試點，計畫在歐洲多個地點提供高密度IT基礎架構服務。

  ## 市場競爭態勢

  Datlantis並非唯一投入海上或水下資料中心概念開發的業者。據《Data Center Dynamics》報導，Aikido與Exascale Labs各自提出了以離岸風電供電的資料中心設計方案。此外，HiCloud宣稱已在中國沿海一座風力渦輪機上，部署了一套實際運作中的水下裝置。

  ## RuVDS南極浮動資料中心部署計畫

  另一方面，RuVDS宣布正在測試一套浮動資料中心，該系統將搭載於一艘參與南極科學探險的船隻上。俄羅斯官方通訊社塔斯社（Tass）報導，此平台將為科學家提供運算支援，協助他們完成人類史上首次攀登西普爾山（Mount Siple）的壯舉——該山峰被描述為地球上最後幾座從未有人登頂的山峰之一。

  RuVDS表示，此系統以多台伺服器為基礎，可同時支援數百個虛擬機器，以因應科學家在探險期間的工作負載需求。執行長尼基塔·察普林（Nikita Tsaplin）表示，此次航行亦將作為一次實地測試，驗證伺服器在潛在惡劣海況下的運行表現。探險隊預計明年一月出發、三月返回。

  ## 產業意義

  上述兩項公告，均反映出業界將運算基礎架構遷移至海上與水下的趨勢持續升溫。此類配置在冷卻效率上具有潛在優勢，可藉由引入低溫海水降低能耗；在供電方面，也可透過鄰近離岸風力設施取得電力。然而，兩種方案目前均尚未在商業規模上得到驗證。Datlantis從原型測試推進至具名試點階段，代表在解決水下實際運作環境中維護性、可靠性與環境影響等未解技術問題上，邁出了具體的一步。RuVDS的南極部署計畫則將在極端海洋環境下，累積浮動伺服器實際運行表現的作業數據。

  Datlantis表示，其歐洲試點地點將提供高密度IT基礎架構，惟具體地點目前尚未對外公布。
