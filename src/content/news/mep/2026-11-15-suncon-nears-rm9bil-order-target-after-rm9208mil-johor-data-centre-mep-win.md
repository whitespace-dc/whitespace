body_en: |
  Sunway Construction Group Bhd (SunCon) has secured a RM920.8mil contract for mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) fit-out works at a data centre in Johor, pushing its year-to-date new order wins to RM8.06bil and placing it within reach of the upper end of its full-year target, according to CGS International Research.

  The contract, announced on Nov 4, was awarded by an existing US multinational client and covers additional MEP fit-out works at an ongoing data centre development in Johor. MEP work encompasses the mechanical systems, electrical infrastructure, and plumbing installations that make a building operational — in a data centre context, this typically includes power distribution, cooling, and related building services. Completion is targeted for the first quarter of 2028.

  ## Contract structure

  The award is an extension of an earlier RM570mil contract granted to SunCon in December 2025, which covered core and shell works for the same development. Under the terms of that original agreement, the client retained the option to award the MEP portion to SunCon — an option it has now exercised. The latest contract is therefore additive to, rather than a replacement of, the earlier scope.

  ## Order book and full-year target

  SunCon's outstanding order book stood at RM11.7bil as of November 2026, CGS International Research said. The company's full-year new order win target is set at RM7bil to RM9bil for the financial year ending Dec 31, 2026. With RM8.06bil already secured year-to-date, CGS International Research said the company is on track to achieve the higher end of that range.

  ## Significance

  The award underscores the continued role of data centre construction in driving new contract flow for Malaysian contractors. For SunCon, the client's decision to exercise its MEP option reflects the value of building in contractual pathways for follow-on scope when structuring large development awards. The Johor data centre project now carries a combined awarded value of at least RM1.49bil across the two contracts.

  SunCon's remaining full-year target — roughly RM940mil at the high end — is to be secured before Dec 31, 2026.
body_zh: |
  陽光建設集團有限公司（SunCon）取得一筆9.208億令吉的柔佛州資料中心機電及水電工程（MEP）裝修合約，使其年初至今的新增接單金額累計達80.6億令吉，距全年目標區間上緣僅一步之遙。CGS國際研究指出，以此進度，公司有望達成70億至90億令吉全年目標的高端水位。

  此合約於11月4日正式公告，由一名現有的美國跨國企業客戶頒發，涵蓋柔佛州同一資料中心開發案的額外MEP裝修工程。所謂機電及水電工程，係指使建築物正常運作所需的機械系統、電力基礎設施及管路安裝——以資料中心而言，通常包含電力配送、冷卻設備及相關建築服務設施。工程預計於2028年第一季完工。

  ## 合約結構

  本次合約是SunCon於2025年12月獲頒之5.7億令吉合約的延伸，原始合約涵蓋同一開發案的核心結構與外殼工程。依據當初協議條款，客戶保留了日後將MEP部分授予SunCon的選擇權，而客戶此次即行使了這項選擇權。因此，最新合約係原有工程範疇的追加，而非取代。

  ## 訂單簿與全年目標

  CGS國際研究表示，SunCon截至2026年11月的在手訂單金額達117億令吉。公司針對2026年12月31日結束之財政年度，設定的全年新增接單目標為70億至90億令吉。在年初至今已累計取得80.6億令吉的情況下，CGS國際研究認為，公司有望達成目標區間的高端水位。

  ## 重要意涵

  此次合約再度印證了資料中心建設在驅動馬來西亞承包商新增合約業務方面持續扮演的關鍵角色。就SunCon而言，客戶選擇行使MEP工程選擇權，彰顯了在大型開發案招標架構中預先納入後續工程合約途徑的策略價值。柔佛州這座資料中心開發案，兩筆合約的合計授予金額現已至少達14.9億令吉。

  SunCon今年剩餘的接單目標——以目標高端計，約為9.4億令吉——須於2026年12月31日前完成取得。
