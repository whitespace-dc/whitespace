body_en: |
  Taiwanese electrical manufacturer TECO Electric & Machinery debuted two modular data centre products at a Warsaw industry event on 17–18 November, targeting European AI infrastructure buyers with factory-prefabricated systems delivering up to 4.2 MW of IT load in as little as nine months.

  TECO presented the HULK 3.0 and HULK 6.0 at the TEEMA Science Park Industry Day in Warsaw, Poland. Both are modular data centres (MDCs) — self-contained, factory-built units that integrate power distribution, cooling, and monitoring into a deployable structure, reducing on-site construction work. The company said target customers include large cloud service providers, GPU computing suppliers, and colocation operators.

  ## Product specifications

  The two models are positioned at different scale points:

  **HULK 3.0**
  - 10-module configuration
  - IT load capacity: 2.7 MW
  - Supports 16 advanced computing systems and 12 IT cabinets
  - Cooling: liquid cooling distribution units (CDUs) combined with computer room air conditioning (CRAC)

  **HULK 6.0**
  - 22-module configuration
  - IT load capacity: 4.2 MW per data hall
  - Supports 16 advanced computing systems and 24 IT cabinets
  - Integrates power distribution, cooling, fire suppression, and monitoring systems

  TECO said both products are designed to a power usage effectiveness (PUE) target of 1.2. PUE measures how efficiently a data centre uses energy: a PUE of 1.2 means 1.2 watts are consumed for every watt delivered to IT equipment, with the remainder lost to cooling and other overhead. Industry averages typically run higher, making 1.2 a competitive design target.

  The company said the planning-to-delivery cycle for both models can be as short as nine months, and that the modular architecture allows customers to deploy in phases, beginning with an initial compute footprint and expanding to campus-scale installations.

  ## Market context

  TECO said the Warsaw appearance was intended to deepen technical and commercial ties with European industry partners. The company attributed growing demand for high-density power, cooling efficiency, and fast deployment to continued increases in generative AI and high-performance computing workloads.

  Alongside the MDC products, TECO also presented electric vehicle drivetrain offerings at the same event, including the OD180 flat-wire motor and an in-house electric powertrain platform covering 50 kW to 410 kW across 250 V to 800 V operating ranges. Those products target light commercial vehicles, medium and heavy trucks, electric buses, and non-road equipment such as port and mining machinery. The EV line is separate from the data centre business but forms part of the company's broader European market push.

  ## Significance

  The Warsaw showcase positions TECO as a supplier competing for European AI infrastructure spending at a time when demand for rapid data centre capacity is concentrated in the AI sector. The nine-month delivery claim and modular architecture directly address the deployment speed constraints that have emerged as a limiting factor for operators trying to bring GPU-dense facilities online quickly.

  TECO said it plans to continue combining its power equipment, drivetrain, and electromechanical integration capabilities to pursue AI infrastructure and electrification markets in Europe.

  台灣電機製造商東元電機於11月17至18日在波蘭華沙舉行的產業活動上，正式推出兩款模組化資料中心產品，以工廠預製系統為歐洲AI基礎建設買家提供最高4.2 MW IT負載的解決方案，最快可在九個月內完成交付。

  東元電機在波蘭華沙舉辦的「TEEMA科學園區產業日」活動上，發表了HULK 3.0與HULK 6.0兩款模組化資料中心。兩款產品均屬模組化資料中心（MDC）——即整合配電、冷卻與監控系統的自給自足工廠預製單元，可大幅減少現場施工作業量，以完整可部署的架構直接交付。東元電機表示，目標客群涵蓋大型雲端服務供應商、GPU運算業者及共置機房業者。

  ## 產品規格

  兩款機型分別鎖定不同規模的部署需求：

  **HULK 3.0**
  - 10模組配置
  - IT負載容量：2.7 MW
  - 支援16套先進運算系統及12個IT機櫃
  - 冷卻配置：液冷分配單元（CDU）搭配機房空調（CRAC）

  **HULK 6.0**
  - 22模組配置
  - 每座資料廳IT負載容量：4.2 MW
  - 支援16套先進運算系統及24個IT機櫃
  - 整合配電、冷卻、消防抑制及監控系統

  東元電機表示，兩款產品的電力使用效率（PUE）設計目標均為1.2。PUE是衡量資料中心能源使用效率的指標：PUE 1.2代表系統每向IT設備輸送1瓦電力，共需消耗1.2瓦，其餘電力則耗損於冷卻等附屬設備。業界平均值通常高於此水準，因此1.2是具市場競爭力的設計目標。

  東元電機表示，兩款產品從規劃到交付的周期最短可壓縮至九個月，且模組化架構讓客戶得以分階段部署——從初始運算規模起步，視需求逐步擴建至園區等級的大型設施。

  ## 市場背景

  東元電機表示，此次亮相華沙，旨在深化與歐洲業界夥伴的技術與商業合作關係。該公司指出，生成式AI與高效能運算工作負載的持續攀升，帶動了高密度電力、冷卻效率及快速部署能力的強勁需求。

  除模組化資料中心產品外，東元電機也在同一活動上展示了電動車傳動系統相關產品，包括OD180扁線馬達，以及自主開發的電動傳動平台——功率涵蓋50 kW至410 kW，支援250 V至800 V工作電壓，適用對象涵蓋輕型商用車、中重型卡車、電動巴士，以及港口機械與採礦設備等非公路用途車輛。電動車產品線與資料中心業務相互獨立，但同屬東元電機拓展歐洲市場布局的整體戰略一環。

  ## 市場意義

  此次華沙展示，使東元電機以歐洲AI基礎建設供應商的姿態正式進入市場競逐，時機正值快速資料中心產能需求高度集中於AI領域之際。「九個月交付」的承諾與模組化架構，直接回應了業者在快速部署GPU高密度設施時所面臨的建置速度瓶頸——這已成為阻礙業者及時上線的關鍵制約因素。

  東元電機表示，未來將持續整合旗下電力設備、傳動系統及機電整合能力，積極拓展歐洲的AI基礎建設與電氣化市場。
