body_en: |
  Delta Electronics made the global debut of a prefabricated AI modular data center at COMPUTEX 2026 in Taipei last week, with the company saying the factory-assembled design reduces deployment time by 60% compared with traditional construction methods.

  The launch came under Delta's show theme "Superior Efficiency, Shaping Sustainable AI," which the company framed around the urgent demand for high energy efficiency, high power density, and high computational density driven by rapid AI expansion. Delta also used the event to demonstrate advanced power, cooling, and microgrid technologies for next-generation AI data centers, and to introduce an 800-volt direct-current in-row power architecture it described as a critical foundation for future AI computing infrastructure.

  ## Prefabricated and modular design

  Yen-Wen Huang, vice president and general manager of Delta's ICT Infrastructure Business Group, said traditional data center construction can no longer meet the speed and density requirements of the AI era. The prefabricated approach uses a modular design in which units are pre-assembled and tested at the factory before delivery to the site, Huang said, enabling enterprises to bring computing capacity online in the shortest possible time.

  Huang said the design also significantly reduces PUE — Power Usage Effectiveness, a ratio measuring how efficiently a data center uses energy, where 1.0 represents perfect efficiency and lower is better. He described the prefabricated model as a computing foundation that supports on-demand capacity expansion while remaining efficient and sustainable.

  ## The shift from AC-DC to HVDC

  Ying-Yuan Chen, vice president and general manager of Delta's Power and Systems Business Group, said that within one to two years the current AC-DC — alternating current to direct current — power architecture used in data centers will face significant challenges as AI and high-density computing workloads grow. Chen said HVDC — high-voltage direct current — design can reduce distribution losses and improve energy efficiency, and is becoming a key solution.

  Delta has framed a "From Grid-to-Chip" approach as its complete solution spanning the full power delivery chain. The company's latest product in that lineup is an 800VDC in-row power solution, which deploys power and battery backup modules flexibly within server racks. Chen said the configuration optimizes both space and thermal management while improving system resilience and scalability.

  ## Microgrids and grid pressure

  Delta Chairman and CEO Ping Cheng said the ongoing expansion of AI data centers is placing substantial stress on traditional electricity grids, making improvements to energy efficiency and power supply stability an immediate priority. He said microgrids — localized power networks that can operate independently from or in coordination with the main grid — are becoming a key industry trend as a result.

  Cheng said Delta's accumulated expertise in power, cooling, and infrastructure technology allows it to address the varied requirements of large cloud data center operators and enterprise customers alike, providing solutions that combine high efficiency, energy resilience, and rapid deployment.

  ## Edge applications and NVIDIA Omniverse

  Alongside the data center infrastructure products, Delta demonstrated applications integrating its systems with the NVIDIA Omniverse library across smart manufacturing and smart building scenarios. Chief Brand Officer Shan-Shan Kuo said AI infrastructure deployment is no longer limited to any single industry, with AI entering factories, buildings, and transportation in multiple scenarios.

  ## What it means

  Delta's 60% build-time reduction claim for the prefabricated approach directly addresses what Huang characterized as an inability of traditional construction methods to meet the pace and density demands of AI deployment. If the claim holds at commercial scale, it would allow enterprises to accelerate AI computing capacity rollouts materially compared with conventional project timelines.

  Chen's one-to-two-year forecast for AC-DC architecture to face meaningful challenges signals that the power conversion equipment market is approaching a transition point, with HVDC positioned by Delta as the successor architecture for AI data centers. The company said it will continue to develop innovative energy-saving technologies in partnership with industry players as AI infrastructure demand grows. Kuo noted the COMPUTEX appearance falls in Delta's 55th anniversary year.
body_zh: |
  台達電（Delta Electronics）上週在台北COMPUTEX 2026展上全球首發預製式AI模組化資料中心，宣稱工廠預組設計相較傳統工法可將建置時間縮短60%。

  本次發表以「卓越效能，塑造永續AI」（Superior Efficiency, Shaping Sustainable AI）為展覽主題，台達電圍繞AI快速擴張所帶動的高能效、高功率密度與高運算密度迫切需求，完整呈現整體佈局。台達電亦於展會中示範次世代AI資料中心所需的先進電力、冷卻及微電網技術，並推出800伏特直流列間供電架構，稱其為未來AI運算基礎設施的關鍵基石。

  ## 預製化與模組化設計

  台達電ICT基礎設施業務群副總裁暨總經理黃彥文表示，傳統資料中心工法已無法滿足AI時代對速度與密度的需求。黃彥文指出，預製化方案採模組化設計，各單元在工廠完成預組與測試後再運抵現場，讓企業能以最短時間將運算產能上線。

  黃彥文表示，此設計亦可顯著降低PUE——即「電源使用效率」（Power Usage Effectiveness），這是衡量資料中心能源使用效率的比值，1.0代表完美效率，數值越低越好。他形容預製化模型是一套支援按需擴充產能的運算基礎，同時兼顧效率與永續性。

  ## 從交流直流到高壓直流的轉型

  台達電電力及系統業務群副總裁暨總經理陳英源表示，隨著AI與高密度運算工作負載持續成長，現行資料中心所使用的交流轉直流（AC-DC）供電架構將在一至兩年內面臨重大挑戰。陳英源指出，高壓直流（HVDC）設計可降低配電損耗、提升能源效率，正逐漸成為關鍵解決方案。

  台達電以「從電網到晶片」（From Grid-to-Chip）為完整解決方案的核心框架，涵蓋整條電力傳輸鏈。此系列最新產品為800VDC列間供電解決方案，可在伺服器機架內彈性部署電力與電池備援模組。陳英源表示，此配置可同步優化空間與散熱管理，並提升系統韌性與擴充彈性。

  ## 微電網與電網壓力

  台達電董事長暨執行長鄭平表示，AI資料中心的持續擴張對傳統電網帶來龐大壓力，提升能源效率與電力供應穩定性已成為當務之急。他指出，微電網——即能獨立運作或與主電網協同運作的區域化電力網路——正因此成為產業的重要趨勢。

  鄭平表示，台達電長期積累的電力、冷卻與基礎設施技術實力，使其得以因應大型雲端資料中心業者與企業客戶的多元需求，提供兼具高效能、能源韌性與快速部署能力的解決方案。

  ## 邊緣應用與輝達Omniverse

  在資料中心基礎設施產品之外，台達電亦展示將其系統與輝達（NVIDIA）Omniverse平台整合的應用，涵蓋智慧製造與智慧建築等場景。首席品牌官郭珊珊表示，AI基礎設施的建置已不再侷限於特定產業，AI正以多元場景深入工廠、建築物與交通領域。

  ## 意義與展望

  台達電聲稱預製化方案可縮短60%建置時間，直接回應了黃彥文所指出的傳統工法已難以因應AI建置速度與密度需求的問題。若此數據能在商業規模下獲得驗證，企業將能大幅加速AI運算產能的擴充進度，遠超傳統專案時程。

  陳英源關於AC-DC架構將在一至兩年內面臨重大挑戰的預測，意味著電力轉換設備市場即將迎來轉型節點，台達電已將HVDC定位為AI資料中心的下一代主流架構。台達電表示，將持續與產業夥伴合作開發創新節能技術，以因應AI基礎設施需求的持續成長。郭珊珊亦提到，此次COMPUTEX亮相恰逢台達電創立55週年。
