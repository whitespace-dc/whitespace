body_en: |
  Alibaba Cloud plans to more than double its global artificial intelligence data center (AIDC) capacity before the end of 2026, supported by a new modular construction architecture it says compresses delivery timelines from the industry-standard six to eighteen months to 100 days.

  ## Modular architecture compresses AIDC delivery to 100 days

  The new framework, called CUBE 5.0, raises the modular proportion of five core systems — power supply, cooling, security, intelligent management, and fire suppression — to 90 percent, according to Chinese financial outlet Wall Street Insights (华尔街见聞), as cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times. Modules are manufactured simultaneously at the factory and then assembled on-site in a process the company compared to stacking building blocks.

  The 100-day schedule divides into three sequential phases: 30 days of factory production, 50 days of on-site installation, and 20 days of testing. Conventional AIDC construction takes six to twelve months in China and twelve to eighteen months in the United States, the report said.

  Alibaba Cloud said the CUBE 5.0 approach also reduces overall data center construction costs by more than 10 percent compared with traditional methods.

  ## Revenue shift toward AI services

  The physical expansion accompanies a rapid reorientation of Alibaba Cloud's revenue mix. AI-related products surpassed 30 percent of the division's external commercial revenue in the fourth quarter of Alibaba's fiscal year 2026, according to the company's earnings report for that period.

  Alibaba Group Chief Executive Wu Yongming told analysts at a recent earnings call that annualized recurring revenue from AI model and application services — including the Bailing MaaS (Model as a Service) platform, which provides enterprise customers with on-demand access to AI models — is expected to exceed 30 billion yuan by the end of 2026.

  ## New model releases and analyst expectations

  Alibaba has recently launched Qwen3.8-Max, which the company described as comparable to leading closed-source AI models from the United States, and an enterprise AI agent software product called Qianwen Office (千问辦公).

  China International Capital Corporation (CICC) said in a research note that the new models are expected to drive a significant increase in model call volume. The bank added that Alibaba Cloud's combination of scale effects, proprietary chips, and proprietary models positions it to beat market expectations in the near to medium term, sustaining both accelerating revenue growth and margin improvement.

  ## Broader cloud buildout context

  Multiple cloud service providers have posted sharp revenue gains from the current surge in AI compute demand, and major global cloud operators are accelerating data center construction to meet it, Commercial Times reported. Alibaba Cloud said it developed CUBE 5.0 specifically to overcome the long construction timelines that have constrained how quickly providers can bring new capacity online.

  The 30 billion yuan annualized recurring revenue target for AI model and application services, which Wu disclosed at the earnings call, is the most specific near-term financial benchmark Alibaba Cloud has set for its AI transition.
body_zh: |
  阿里雲計畫在2026年底前將全球人工智慧資料中心（AIDC）產能提升逾一倍，並以一套名為CUBE 5.0的新型模組化建設架構為後盾，將交付工期從業界標準的六至十八個月壓縮至100天。

  ## 模組化架構將AI資料中心交付期壓縮至100天

  根據《工商時報》引述中國財經媒體《華爾街見聞》的報導，CUBE 5.0將供電、冷卻、安防、智慧管理及消防等五大核心系統的模組化比例提升至90%。各模組在工廠同步生產，再運至現場組裝，公司將這一過程比喻為積木堆疊。

  100天工期分為三個連續階段：工廠生產30天、現場安裝50天，以及測試驗收20天。報導指出，傳統AIDC的建設工期在中國為六至十二個月，在美國則需十二至十八個月。

  阿里雲表示，CUBE 5.0的做法也使整體資料中心建設成本較傳統方式降低逾10%。

  ## 營收結構快速向AI服務傾斜

  實體產能擴張的同時，阿里雲的營收結構也正快速轉型。根據阿里巴巴集團針對2026財年第四季的財報，AI相關產品在該季度的雲端事業部外部商業營收中佔比已突破30%。

  阿里巴巴集團執行長吳泳銘在近期的法說會上告訴分析師，涵蓋「百靈MaaS」（模型即服務）平台在內的AI模型與應用服務——該平台提供企業客戶隨需存取AI模型的能力——其年度經常性營收預計在2026年底前超過人民幣300億元。

  ## 新模型發布與分析師展望

  阿里巴巴近期推出了Qwen3.8-Max，公司描述其性能可媲美美國頂尖閉源AI模型，另外也推出名為「千問辦公」的企業AI智慧代理軟體產品。

  中國國際金融股份有限公司（中金公司）在一份研究報告中指出，新模型預計將帶動模型呼叫量大幅成長。中金公司補充表示，阿里雲憑藉規模效應、自研晶片及自研模型的組合優勢，有望在近至中期超越市場預期，同時維持營收加速成長與獲利率持續改善的雙重態勢。

  ## 更廣泛的雲端建設背景

  《工商時報》報導指出，多家雲端服務供應商已因當前AI運算需求激增而錄得強勁營收成長，主要全球雲端業者也紛紛加速資料中心建設以因應需求。阿里雲表示，CUBE 5.0的開發正是為了突破長期以來制約業者快速擴充新產能的漫長建設工期瓶頸。

  吳泳銘在法說會上所揭露的AI模型與應用服務年度經常性營收人民幣300億元目標，是阿里雲迄今為其AI轉型所設立的最具體近期財務里程碑。
