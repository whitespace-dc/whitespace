body_en: |
  KJTS Group Bhd has secured a RM48 million subcontract from Gamuda Engineering Sdn Bhd to carry out mechanical works at a hyperscale data centre under construction in Port Dickson, Malaysia, the Kuala Lumpur-listed engineering and building support company said in a bourse filing on Thursday.

  The contract was entered into by KJTS's wholly-owned subsidiary, KJ Technical Service Sdn Bhd, and covers the full mechanical package for the facility. Gamuda Engineering Sdn Bhd is the engineering and construction arm of Gamuda Bhd, a Kuala Lumpur-listed infrastructure group.

  The end client for the data centre has not been named, but KJTS identified it as a multinational technology company headquartered in the United States, according to the filing.

  ## Scope of work

  Under the subcontract, KJ Technical Service will be responsible for the provision of materials, plant, and labour required for the engineering, procurement, manufacture, delivery, installation, supervision, testing and commissioning, as well as ongoing maintenance and servicing of the mechanical systems.

  Mechanical systems in a hyperscale data centre — a large-scale facility designed to support the computing infrastructure of major cloud and technology platforms — typically include cooling, water treatment, and air management systems, which are critical to keeping servers operational and within safe temperature ranges.

  ## Project timeline

  The project is structured in two phases. Phase 1 is scheduled for completion by October 22, 2027, and Phase 2 by January 21, 2028, according to the company's disclosure.

  ## Market context

  The award adds to KJTS Group's order book and positions the company as a subcontractor in Malaysia's expanding hyperscale data centre construction pipeline. KJTS shares closed half a sen, or 0.51%, lower at 98 sen on Thursday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM676.9 million.

  ## 工程範疇

  依據分包合約，KJ Technical Service將負責提供機械系統所需的材料、設備及人力，業務範圍涵蓋工程設計、採購、製造、運送、安裝、監督、測試與試運轉，以及後續的維護與保養作業。

  超大規模資料中心是專為支援大型雲端及科技平台之運算基礎設施而設計的大型設施，其機械系統通常包括冷卻、水處理及氣流管理系統，這些系統對於維持伺服器正常運作及確保溫度處於安全範圍至關重要。

  ## 工程時程

  此次專案分兩階段推進。依據公司公告，第一階段預計於2027年10月22日完工，第二階段則預計於2028年1月21日完工。

  ## 市場背景
