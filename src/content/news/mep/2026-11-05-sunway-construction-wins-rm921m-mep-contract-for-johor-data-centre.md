body_en: |
  Sunway Construction Group Bhd has secured RM920.8 million in additional mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) fit-out works for an ongoing data centre development in Johor, the Malaysian contractor disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia filing on Wednesday.

  The contract was awarded by an unnamed US-headquartered multinational technology corporation to Sunway Construction's wholly-owned subsidiary, Sunway Construction Sdn Bhd. MEP fit-out refers to the installation of power, cooling, water, and related building services systems — the core infrastructure that makes a data centre operational. The letter of award also functions as an instruction to commence works immediately, with completion targeted by the first quarter of 2028.

  Sunway Construction said the additional works are expected to contribute positively to its earnings from the financial year ending December 31, 2026 onwards, barring unforeseen circumstances.

  ## Order book and year-to-date awards

  The Johor MEP contract is the latest in a series of data centre-related awards for the group. Including this contract, Sunway Construction has secured RM8.06 billion in new orders so far this year. Its total outstanding order book now stands at RM11.7 billion.

  ## Recent financial performance

  Sunway Construction reported a net profit of RM103.59 million for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026, up 23.5% from RM83.89 million in the same period a year earlier. The company attributed the improvement to stronger profit margins across its operating segments.

  Revenue for the quarter fell 31.1% to RM1.02 billion from RM1.48 billion, a decline the company said was driven primarily by lower construction revenue.

  Sunway Construction shares closed down four sen, or 0.52%, to RM7.68 on Wednesday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM10.2 billion.

  ## Significance

  The contract adds to a growing pipeline of data centre MEP work in Johor, which has emerged as a significant destination for large-scale digital infrastructure investment. The RM920.8 million award is an addition to an already-active project, indicating that the client — identified only as a US-headquartered multinational technology company — is expanding the scope of the development. Completion is scheduled for the first quarter of 2028.
body_zh: |
  雙威建設集團有限公司（Sunway Construction Group Bhd）宣布，旗下全資子公司雙威建設私人有限公司（Sunway Construction Sdn Bhd），獲得一家總部位於美國的跨國科技企業授予額外的機械、電氣及水管安裝（MEP）裝設工程合約，合約金額達9億2,080萬令吉。該集團於週三向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）發布公告，披露此一正在進行中的柔佛資料中心開發案相關合約詳情。

  此合約由上述美國跨國科技企業授予雙威建設的全資子公司。所謂MEP裝設工程，係指電力、冷卻、供水及相關建築機電設備系統的安裝作業，是資料中心投入正式營運所必備的核心基礎設施。工程授予函同時具有即時開工指令的效力，預計於2028年第一季竣工。

  雙威建設表示，上述額外工程預計自2026年12月31日止財政年度起，對公司盈利產生正面貢獻，惟須視乎是否出現不可預見的情況。

  ## 訂單簿與年初至今新接訂單

  此柔佛機電合約是雙威建設集團近期系列資料中心相關合約中的最新一筆。計入本項合約，雙威建設今年迄今累計新接訂單已達80億6,000萬令吉。集團未完成訂單總額目前為117億令吉。

  ## 近期財務表現

  雙威建設公布，截至2026年6月30日止第二季淨利為1億359萬令吉，較上年同期的8,389萬令吉增長23.5%，公司將此歸因於各營運部門利潤率的提升。

  本季營收則按年下滑31.1%，由14億8,000萬令吉降至10億2,000萬令吉，公司指出主要原因為建築工程收入減少所致。

  雙威建設股票週三收盤下跌4仙，跌幅0.52%，報每股7.68令吉，集團市值為102億令吉。

  ## 意義

  此合約進一步充實柔佛地區持續擴大的資料中心機電工程管線。柔佛近年已崛起為大型數位基礎設施投資的重要目的地。9億2,080萬令吉的合約金額係對現有進行中項目的追加授予，顯示該客戶——目前僅對外披露為一家總部位於美國的跨國科技公司——正持續擴大此開發案的規模。工程預計於2028年第一季竣工。
