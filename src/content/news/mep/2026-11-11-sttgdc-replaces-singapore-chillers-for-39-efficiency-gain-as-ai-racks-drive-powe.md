  ## Chiller overhaul at Tai Seng

  STTGDC has replaced 10 rooftop air-cooled chillers at its Tai Seng colocation data centre in Singapore with more efficient units, cutting energy consumption for that cooling plant by 39%, according to the company. The project required approximately three years of planning and was executed in phases over a year to avoid interrupting cooling to live customer equipment.

  The Tai Seng facility carries a total IT load of 13.7 megawatts (MW) — the ceiling on power available to customers' computing, storage, and networking hardware combined. The site spans six floors with a total floor area equivalent to two football pitches, the company said.

  STTGDC operates six data centres in Singapore and more than 100 globally, with locations in Malaysia, India, South Korea, Japan, the United Kingdom, and Germany.

  ## AI workloads multiply power density

  The efficiency upgrade coincides with a marked shift in what customers are asking data centres to support. AI server racks now draw roughly 20 kilowatts (kW) each — approximately seven to ten times the 2 kW to 3 kW load of a conventional server rack, according to the company. That increase in power density intensifies the thermal management challenge for site operations teams.

  STTGDC provides colocation services, meaning it rents space, power, and cooling infrastructure to customers who own and operate their own IT equipment. The transition from lower-intensity workloads such as email and web hosting, to cloud computing, and now to AI inference and training represents successive waves of demand growth the company describes as driving increased need for colocation, connectivity, and support services.

  Singapore imposes regulatory limits on how much electricity data centres can consume. Under those constraints, an individual facility's power allocation does not increase simply because a customer installs higher-density AI hardware, the company said.

  ## Cooling requirements and environmental controls

  Maintaining a stable thermal environment rather than achieving the lowest possible temperature is the operational objective, according to STTGDC. The company targets an air temperature range of 18 degrees Celsius to 27 degrees Celsius and a relative humidity band of 20% to 80% inside its data halls. Too much moisture risks condensation on electrical equipment; too little raises the risk of electrostatic discharge.

  The Tai Seng facility uses non-potable water in its cooling systems and recycles that water before it is released as sewage, the company said. STTGDC takes reference from Singapore's Tropical Data Centre standard, a local benchmark aimed at improving energy efficiency in the city-state's climate.

  For smoke detection, the facility deploys VESDA — very early smoke detection apparatus — a system that continuously draws air samples and can identify very small concentrations of smoke particles well before conventional detectors or unaided observation would register a hazard.

  Across its global portfolio, approximately 83% of STTGDC's electricity consumption is sourced from renewables, the company said. It also uses AI-driven analytics to monitor energy and water efficiency trends across its facilities.

  ## Site operations structure

  Day-to-day management of the physical infrastructure falls to a site operations team. At Tai Seng, that team numbers 20 people organised into four team lead positions working 12-hour shift cycles to maintain around-the-clock coverage. Responsibilities include monitoring critical power and cooling systems, coordinating maintenance, planning contingencies, and responding to alarms.

  The team also provides what the company describes as remote hands service — physical inspection, fault investigation, and troubleshooting carried out on behalf of customers who cannot be on-site.

  Backup power infrastructure, including uninterruptible power supplies and standby generators, is in place to prevent server downtime during grid outages. Site operations staff are also trained as emergency responders, with qualifications covering fire response, first aid, CPR, and use of automated external defibrillators.

  Data centres are classified as critical infrastructure in Singapore, alongside hospitals and traffic management systems. A sustained outage would disrupt the digital services — banking transactions, cloud applications, e-commerce platforms — that businesses and consumers depend on.

  ## Significance for regional operations

  The chiller replacement at Tai Seng illustrates the operational and capital complexity involved in upgrading active colocation facilities, where cooling cannot be interrupted while works proceed. The 39% efficiency improvement and the multi-year execution timeline serve as a reference point for the scale of retrofit investment Singapore's constrained data centre sector faces as AI-driven power density continues to rise within fixed power allocations.

  ## 大成資料中心冷凍系統翻新工程

  STTGDC已將新加坡大成共置資料中心的10台屋頂氣冷式冷凍主機，全數更換為效能更高的新型機組，使該冷卻設備的能耗下降39%。這項工程歷經約三年的規劃，並以分階段方式於一年內逐步完成，以避免中斷現有客戶設備的冷卻供應。

  大成機房的IT總負載為13.7百萬瓦（MW），這是客戶運算、儲存及網路設備可用電力的上限。該場址橫跨六層樓，總建築面積相當於兩座足球場，據公司表示。

  STTGDC在新加坡共操作六座資料中心，全球逾百座，據點遍及馬來西亞、印度、南韓、日本、英國及德國。

  ## AI工作負載推升機架用電密度

  此次效率升級，恰逢客戶對資料中心需求的顯著轉變。據公司表示，AI伺服器機架的單架功耗現已達約20千瓦（kW），約為傳統伺服器機架2至3千瓦的七到十倍。這種功率密度的激增，大幅加重了現場運維團隊在熱管理方面的挑戰。

  STTGDC提供共置服務，意即向擁有並自行操作IT設備的客戶出租空間、電力及冷卻基礎設施。從電子郵件、網站代管等低密度工作負載，到雲端運算，再到現在的AI推論與訓練，這些一波接一波的需求成長浪潮，據公司描述，正持續驅動著對共置、網路連接及支援服務的需求。

  新加坡對資料中心的用電量設有法規限制。在此限制下，據公司表示，個別機房的電力配額，不會因客戶安裝了高密度AI硬體而自動增加。

  ## 冷卻需求與環境控制

  據STTGDC表示，維持穩定的熱環境、而非追求最低溫度，才是運維作業的核心目標。公司在資料大廳內設定的目標溫度範圍為攝氏18度至27度，相對濕度則維持在20%至80%。濕度過高有導致電氣設備表面凝結水氣的風險；濕度過低則可能引發靜電放電問題。

  大成機房的冷卻系統使用非飲用水，並在排放至污水系統前進行回收再利用，據公司表示。STTGDC參照新加坡的「熱帶資料中心」標準，這是一項旨在提升新加坡氣候條件下資料中心能源效率的本地基準。

  在煙霧偵測方面，機房部署了VESDA（極早期煙霧偵測裝置），這套系統能持續抽取空氣樣本，在傳統偵測器或人眼察覺危險之前，即可識別極微量的煙霧粒子。

  STTGDC表示，全球組合中約83%的用電量來自再生能源。公司也運用AI驅動的數據分析，監控旗下各機房的能源及用水效率趨勢。

  ## 現場運維架構

  日常的實體基礎設施管理由現場運維團隊負責。大成機房的運維團隊共20人，設有四個班長職位，採12小時輪班制，全天候持續駐守。職責涵蓋關鍵電力與冷卻系統的監控、維護協調、應急規劃，以及警報處置。

  該團隊也提供公司所稱的「遠端代管」服務，代替無法親赴現場的客戶，執行實體檢查、故障排查及問題診斷。

  機房配備了不斷電系統（UPS）及備用發電機等備用電力基礎設施，以防止電網中斷期間伺服器宕機。現場運維人員亦受訓擔任緊急應變人員，具備消防應變、急救、CPR及自動體外心臟去顫器（AED）操作等資格認證。

  新加坡將資料中心列為關鍵基礎設施，與醫院、交通管理系統並列。一旦發生長時間停機，將影響企業與消費者所仰賴的數位服務，包括銀行交易、雲端應用及電子商務平台。

  ## 對區域業務的意義

  大成機房的冷凍主機汰換工程，充分展示了翻新現役共置機房所涉及的運維及資本複雜性——工程推進期間，冷卻系統不得中斷。39%的效率提升及歷時多年的執行時程，為新加坡受限的資料中心業提供了重要的參考座標，說明在AI驅動的功率密度持續攀升、而電力配額維持固定的前提下，業界在改造投資規模上所面臨的嚴峻挑戰。
