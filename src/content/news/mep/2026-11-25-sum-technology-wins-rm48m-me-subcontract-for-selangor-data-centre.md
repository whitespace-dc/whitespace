body_en: |
  Sum Technology Bhd has secured a RM48 million subcontract for mechanical, electrical, process utilities and firefighting works at a data centre project in Selangor, the Kuala Lumpur-listed company disclosed in a bourse filing on Tuesday.

  The contract was awarded to Sum Technic Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Sum Technology, on Nov 16. The company did not name the data centre owner or the main contractor, citing a non-disclosure agreement.

  ## Contract details

  The scope covers mechanical and electrical (M&E) works — the systems that keep a data centre operational, including power distribution, cooling, process utilities and fire suppression — alongside firefighting installations. The start and completion dates have not been finalised, the company said.

  ## Contracts since listing

  Sum Technology listed on Bursa Malaysia on June 18, 2026. The RM48 million award is its sixth contract since going public, bringing cumulative contract value since listing to RM144.26 million.

  Shares of Sum Technology closed one sen, or 2.25%, lower at 43.5 sen on Tuesday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM195.75 million.

  ## Significance

  The award adds to a run of contract wins that has more than tripled the company's post-listing order book in roughly five months, reflecting continued capital investment in data centre infrastructure in Selangor, one of Malaysia's primary data centre development corridors. The undisclosed identity of the data centre owner leaves open questions about the scale and end-user of the facility.

  The start and completion schedule for the project remains to be confirmed by the parties involved.

  ## Sum Technology奪得雪蘭莪資料中心4,800萬令吉機電分包合約

  馬來西亞統包解決方案供應商勝科技術公司（Sum Technology Bhd）旗下子公司已獲得一份價值4,800萬令吉的分包合約，負責承作雪蘭莪州一座資料中心的機電、工程公用設施及消防工程。這家掛牌於吉隆坡交易所的企業於週二在交易所公告中披露此消息。

  該合約於11月16日授予勝科技術的全資子公司Sum Technic Sdn Bhd。由於保密協議的限制，公司並未透露資料中心業主及總承包商的名稱。

  ## 合約細節

  此案承作範圍涵蓋機械與電氣（M&E）工程，即維持資料中心正常運作所需的核心系統，包括電力配送、冷卻系統、工程公用設施、消防抑制設備，以及消防設施安裝。公司表示，開工與完工日期尚未確定。

  ## 上市以來的合約紀錄

  勝科技術於2026年6月18日在馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）正式掛牌上市。此次4,800萬令吉的合約是其上市後拿下的第六份合約，使上市以來累計合約總額達到1億4,426萬令吉。

  勝科技術股票週二收盤下跌1仙，跌幅2.25%，報43.5仙，對應集團市值為1億9,575萬令吉。

  ## 意義

  此次得標為公司持續累積的合約勝利再添一筆——在約五個月內，其上市後訂單簿規模已翻逾三倍，折射出雪蘭莪州資料中心基礎設施建設的持續資本投入熱潮。雪蘭莪是馬來西亞主要的資料中心開發走廊之一。由於資料中心業主身分未予揭露，該設施的規模及最終用戶目前仍是未解之謎。

  此專案的開工與完工時程，有待各方當事人進一步確認。
