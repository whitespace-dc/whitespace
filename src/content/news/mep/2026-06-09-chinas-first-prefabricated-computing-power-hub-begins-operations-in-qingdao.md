body_en: |
  China's first prefabricated computing power hub has started operations in Qingdao, Shandong province, offering a faster and lower-cost alternative to conventional electricity infrastructure for data centres, according to a report by China Central Television (CCTV).

  The system reduces land use by more than 30% and cuts overall construction and supply costs by about 20% compared with traditional approaches, CCTV reported. Construction can be completed in as little as five months, the broadcaster added.

  ## What the system does

  The hub is a prefabricated power delivery system — meaning its components are manufactured off-site and assembled at the deployment location — designed specifically to supply electricity to data centres. Qingdao TGOOD Electric Co, which makes prefabricated substations and power transformers, built and operates the system.

  Each piece of equipment in the hub is fitted with three separate power sources, according to Zhou Jun, executive president of Qingdao TGOOD Electric Co, as quoted by CCTV. Zhou said the design is intended to handle both fluctuations in the external power grid and the variable, high-intensity electricity demands generated by graphics processing unit (GPU) workloads — the chips used for artificial intelligence and large-scale computing tasks.

  "This design not only withstands fluctuations in the external power grid and the demands of graphics processing unit computing loads, but also ensures safe and reliable operation in the event of equipment failure," Zhou said.

  ## Deployment status and next steps

  The prefabricated hub has already been connected to a data centre built by TGOOD, CCTV reported. The company expects the system to be deployed at national and regional computing hubs later in 2026, according to the broadcaster.

  ## Significance for data centre construction

  The claimed reductions in land use and cost, combined with the five-month build time, are directly relevant to the pace at which data centre capacity can be brought online. Power infrastructure procurement and construction is consistently among the longest lead-time elements in new data centre development. A prefabricated approach that compresses that timeline could allow operators to respond more quickly to demand for AI and cloud computing capacity. The scope of that impact will depend on how widely the system is adopted beyond TGOOD's own facilities and whether the claimed efficiency figures hold at scale across different sites.
body_zh: |
  中國首座預製式算力電力樞紐已在山東省青島市正式啟用，為資料中心電力基礎設施提供比傳統方案更快速、更低成本的替代選擇，中央電視台（CCTV）對此進行了報導。

  據中央電視台報導，該系統與傳統方案相比，土地使用量減少逾30%，整體建設與供應成本降低約20%。中央電視台並指出，整體工程最快五個月即可竣工。

  ## 系統功能

  該樞紐為預製式電力輸送系統——即各組件於廠房預先製造完畢，再運至部署地點進行組裝——專為資料中心供電所設計。建造並營運此系統的青島特銳德電氣股份有限公司，主要業務為預製式變電站及電力變壓器的製造。

  據中央電視台引述青島特銳德電氣股份有限公司執行總裁周軍表示，樞紐內每件設備均配備三組獨立電源。周軍指出，此設計旨在同時應對外部電網的波動，以及圖形處理器（GPU）工作負載所產生的高強度、高變動性用電需求——GPU正是用於人工智慧與大規模運算任務的核心晶片。

  「這一設計不僅能抵禦外部電網波動與圖形處理器運算負載的衝擊，更能確保在設備發生故障時，系統仍能安全穩定地持續運作，」周軍表示。

  ## 部署現狀與後續計畫

  中央電視台報導，該預製式樞紐已與特銳德自行建設的一座資料中心完成連接。據該廣播機構報導，公司預計於2026年稍後將此系統部署至國家及區域算力樞紐。

  ## 對資料中心建設的重要意義

  土地用量與成本的下降幅度，加上五個月的建設工期，與資料中心容量得以快速上線的速度直接相關。電力基礎設施的採購與建設，向來是新資料中心開發專案中前置時間最長的環節之一。預製式方案若能壓縮這段時程，將有助於業者更迅速地回應人工智慧與雲端運算容量日益增長的需求。然而，此一影響的實際範圍，最終仍取決於這套系統能否在特銳德自有設施以外獲得更廣泛的採用，以及所聲稱的效率數據能否在不同地點、大規模部署的情境下持續獲得驗證。
