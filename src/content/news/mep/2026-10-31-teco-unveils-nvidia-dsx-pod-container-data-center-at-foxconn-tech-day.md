body_en: |
  TECO Electric & Machinery (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 1504) demonstrated an all-in-one modular data center built on NVIDIA's DSX POD reference design at Foxconn Tech Day on October 30, marking the Taiwanese electrical equipment maker's second appearance at the annual event and its most detailed public display of a joint infrastructure product with Hon Hai Precision Industry (Foxconn, 2317).

  The exhibit centers on a 40-foot shipping container that integrates IT racks, alternating- and direct-current control panels, a cooling system, an energy management system (EMS), and fire suppression equipment into a single factory-assembled unit. TECO said more than 80 percent of the modular assembly work is completed at the factory before a unit ships to a customer site, an approach the company says shortens deployment timelines and reduces on-site labor and construction complexity.

  ## The NVIDIA DSX POD reference design

  NVIDIA's DSX POD is a reference architecture that allows manufacturing partners to build and verify a complete NVIDIA Vera Rubin AI factory — a data center configured for AI workloads — inside a modular container before it is delivered to the end customer. The design moves the integration of compute hardware, networking, cabling, rack-level power supplies, and liquid cooling from the construction site to the factory floor. TECO said this reduces the planning, skilled labor, and logistics costs typically associated with deploying an AI-optimized facility through conventional on-site construction.

  TECO's all-in-one MDC (modular data center) concept is built on the DSX POD reference design in collaboration with Foxconn, with which TECO established a strategic alliance through a share exchange in 2025.

  ## TECO's five-module infrastructure offering

  TECO Chairman Li Ming-xian said AI is reshaping global industry competition and that data centers have become critical infrastructure for computing capacity development. Li described TECO's strategy as connecting power, cooling, engineering, and energy management through modular solutions.

  Beyond the Foxconn-linked DSX POD concept, TECO's broader MDC offering covers five core modules: IT equipment, thermal management, power, generators, and EMS. The company said customers can configure and scale these modules to match their computing requirements, site conditions, and reliability specifications, allowing phased capacity additions as workloads grow.

  ## Malaysia acquisition and MEP capacity

  To expand its electromechanical engineering capacity for MDC projects, TECO acquired Malaysia-based Dynaciate, which it has since renamed TECO NXG Integrated Services Sdn. Bhd. Li said the acquisition is intended to accelerate the group's positioning as an AI data center solutions provider, extending its delivery scope from core electrical equipment through to full mechanical and electrical systems integration.

  The company said it aims to take on larger MDC engineering contracts by completing more than 80 percent of assembly work at the factory and then performing final installation and systems interconnection on site, a model it says improves construction quality and delivery efficiency versus fully site-built alternatives.

  ## Malaysia deployment and near-term milestone

  TECO is currently executing a megawatt-scale MDC project in Malaysia targeting large cloud service providers, which the company said require rapid deployment and flexible capacity expansion. A megawatt-scale facility can support substantial AI compute density; the exact capacity was not disclosed. TECO said it expects to complete the Malaysia project and open it for external demonstration by the end of 2026, representing the first large-scale application of the integrated modular approach it displayed at Foxconn Tech Day.

  ## 東元電機於鴻海科技日發表NVIDIA DSX POD貨櫃式模組資料中心

  東元電機（臺灣證券交易所：1504）於10月30日鴻海科技日展出以NVIDIA DSX POD參考設計為基礎的一體化模組資料中心，這是這家臺灣電機設備製造商連續第二年出席這項年度活動，也是其迄今對外最完整展示與鴻海精密工業（鴻海，股票代號：2317）聯合開發基礎設施產品的一次。

  此次展示的核心是一個40呎標準貨櫃，在單一出廠組裝單元中整合了IT機架、交流與直流控制盤、冷卻系統、能源管理系統（EMS）及消防設備。東元表示，模組超過八成的組裝工作在工廠即已完成，再行運抵客戶現場，此做法可縮短部署時程，並降低現場人力與施工複雜度。

  ## NVIDIA DSX POD參考設計

  NVIDIA DSX POD是一套參考架構，讓製造合作夥伴得以在模組化貨櫃內完整建置並驗證一座NVIDIA Vera Rubin AI工廠——即針對AI工作負載進行配置的資料中心——再交付給最終客戶。這套設計將運算硬體、網路設備、佈線、機架級電源供應器及液冷系統的整合作業，從施工現場移至工廠生產線。東元指出，相較於傳統現場施工方式，此舉可降低部署AI優化設施所需的規劃成本、專業人力及物流費用。

  東元的全整合模組資料中心（MDC）概念以DSX POD參考設計為基礎，與鴻海合作共同開發。東元與鴻海已於2025年透過股份交換建立策略聯盟。

  ## 東元的五大模組基礎設施方案

  東元董事長李明賢表示，AI正在重塑全球產業競爭格局，資料中心已成為運算力發展的關鍵基礎設施。他將東元的策略定位描述為透過模組化解決方案，串聯供電、冷卻、工程與能源管理。

  除了與鴻海合作的DSX POD概念之外，東元更廣泛的MDC方案涵蓋五大核心模組：IT設備、熱管理、電力、發電機組，以及EMS。東元表示，客戶可依據運算需求、場址條件及可靠度規格自由組合與擴充這些模組，隨工作負載成長分階段增加容量。

  ## 收購馬來西亞廠商與機電工程能量

  為擴大MDC專案的機電工程能量，東元收購了馬來西亞Dynaciate公司，並將其更名為TECO NXG Integrated Services Sdn. Bhd.。李明賢表示，此次收購旨在加速集團定位為AI資料中心解決方案提供商，將交付範疇從核心電機設備延伸至完整的機電系統整合。

  東元表示，透過在工廠完成逾八成的組裝作業、再赴現場執行最終安裝與系統整合，公司得以承接規模更大的MDC工程合約。東元認為，這套模式相較於全現場施工的替代方案，能提升施工品質與交付效率。

  ## 馬來西亞部署案與近期里程碑

  東元目前正在馬來西亞執行一個以大型雲端服務供應商為目標客群的百萬瓦級MDC專案，這類客戶需要快速部署與彈性容量擴充能力。百萬瓦級設施可支援相當高的AI運算密度，惟確切容量未予揭露。東元表示，預計於2026年底完成馬來西亞專案並對外開放參觀，這將是其在鴻海科技日所展示之整合模組化方案的首個大規模實際應用案例。
