body_en: |
  Data Center Dynamics (DCD) has published a whitepaper setting out a unified lifecycle framework for managing interdependencies across critical power, power generation, cooling, and Building Management Systems (BMS) in data centers, with particular focus on facilities serving AI workloads in the Middle East.

  The paper argues that facility resilience cannot be measured by the health of individual components in isolation. Hidden interdependencies across the four system domains can leave a facility exposed during grid disturbances even when each asset appears to be functioning normally, according to DCD.

  ## The case for integrated assurance

  DCD frames the problem as one of siloed maintenance practices failing to account for cross-system failure modes. The whitepaper calls for operators to move from managing each infrastructure domain separately to a unified assurance model that tracks system relationships across the full facility lifecycle.

  To structure that shift, the paper applies what it terms the AVAR lens — a framework organized around four properties: Availability, Value, Agility, and Risk. DCD presents AVAR as a way to engineer, prove, and sustain system relationships rather than individual asset ratings.

  ## Middle Eastern operating conditions

  The framework is explicitly scoped to the environmental and resource constraints common to Middle Eastern data center markets: high ambient heat, airborne dust, and limited freshwater availability. These conditions place particular pressure on cooling infrastructure and directly affect water usage effectiveness (WUE), a metric that tracks water consumption relative to IT load.

  DCD states that operators in this environment must balance power usage effectiveness (PUE) — a measure of total facility energy consumption versus IT energy consumption — and cooling efficiency against the need to preserve uptime margins. The whitepaper positions the two objectives as a tension to be managed rather than resolved by prioritizing one over the other.

  ## Commissioning and modernization

  Among the technical areas the framework addresses, DCD highlights validation of end-to-end utility-loss sequences — the chain of events that occurs when grid power fails and backup systems must take over. The paper describes this as testing a facility against its weakest link rather than against each system in turn.

  The whitepaper also covers resolving system interfaces, proving failure modes during commissioning, and procedures for modernizing live facilities without taking them offline. DCD presents these as a phased action plan rather than a one-time audit.

  ## Significance for the industry

  The publication reflects a broader shift in how data center operators are approaching infrastructure management as AI workloads impose higher and more variable power densities. By framing resilience as a property of the full operating chain, DCD's framework pushes operators to account for cross-system risks that standard equipment certification processes do not capture.

  The whitepaper is available for download from Data Centre Dynamics.

  ## DCD發布AI時代設施系統整合生命週期管理框架

  Data Center Dynamics（DCD）近日發布一份白皮書，針對資料中心的關鍵電力、發電、冷卻及樓宇管理系統（BMS）之間的相互依存關係，提出一套統一的生命週期管理框架，並特別聚焦於中東地區承載AI工作負載的設施。

  白皮書指出，設施韌性無法單憑個別元件的健康狀態來衡量。DCD表示，四大系統領域之間潛藏的相互依存關係，可能使設施在電網擾動時陷入風險，即便每項資產表面上運作正常，仍可能暴露在危險之中。

  ## 整合保障的必要性

  DCD將問題根源定義為：各自為政的維護作業無法涵蓋跨系統的故障模式。白皮書呼籲營運商放棄分別管理各基礎設施領域的做法，轉而採用統一的保障模型，在設施整個生命週期內追蹤系統間的關聯性。

  為推動這項轉變，白皮書引入所謂的「AVAR視角」——一套圍繞四項屬性建構的框架，分別為：可用性（Availability）、價值（Value）、敏捷性（Agility）與風險（Risk）。DCD將AVAR定位為一種方法論，用以建構、驗證並維繫系統關係，而非僅關注單一資產的評級。

  ## 中東的運營環境挑戰

  此框架明確針對中東資料中心市場普遍面臨的環境與資源限制：高溫環境、懸浮粉塵，以及淡水資源匱乏。這些條件對冷卻基礎設施形成特殊壓力，並直接影響用水效率（WUE）——該指標用於衡量相對於IT負載的用水量。

  DCD指出，在這樣的環境中，營運商必須在電源使用效率（PUE）——衡量設施總能源消耗與IT能源消耗之比的指標——、冷卻效率，以及維持正常運作餘裕之間取得平衡。白皮書將這兩項目標之間的張力定性為需要妥善管理的課題，而非透過偏重任何一方來加以解決。

  ## 試運行與現代化改造

  在框架所涵蓋的技術領域中，DCD特別強調對端對端電力中斷情境的驗證——即電網斷電後備援系統須接管時所觸發的一連串事件。白皮書將此描述為針對設施最薄弱環節進行測試，而非逐一測試各系統。

  白皮書亦涵蓋系統介面的整合、試運行期間故障模式的驗證，以及在不停機情況下對既有設施進行現代化改造的作業程序。DCD將上述內容呈現為一套分階段行動計畫，而非一次性的稽核作業。

  ## 對產業的意義

  此白皮書的發布，反映出資料中心營運商在面對AI工作負載帶來更高且更不穩定的功率密度之際，對基礎設施管理方式所出現的更廣泛轉變。DCD的框架將韌性定義為整個運營鏈的屬性，促使營運商正視標準設備認證程序所無法捕捉的跨系統風險。
