body_en: |
  Foxconn is showing its modular data center designed for artificial intelligence workloads and two electric vehicle platforms at the 2026 Japan-Taiwan Image Exhibition in Tokyo, the company announced July 14, marking the first public debut in Japan for all three products.

  The exhibition runs July 15 through 17 at the Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Triangle Square Smart City hall in Tokyo. Foxconn said the three exhibits represent its two strategic pillars: smart electric vehicles and AI computing infrastructure.

  ## Modular data center for AI factory workloads

  Foxconn is displaying what it describes as a Modular Data Center, or MDC — a prefabricated, standardized facility module intended to help enterprises deploy AI computing infrastructure rapidly. The MDC integrates IT equipment, power supply, cooling systems, and liquid cooling subsystems into a single modular unit, the company said. Liquid cooling is increasingly used in AI server deployments because the processors involved generate more heat per rack than conventional servers can shed with air alone.

  Foxconn said the MDC supports what it calls "AI Factory" deployments — high-density AI training and inference environments — as well as enterprise AI applications and high-performance computing. The company described its role as covering system design, integration of key subsystems, and rapid deployment. This is the first time Foxconn has exhibited the MDC in Japan.

  ## Electric vehicle lineup

  The MODEL A is a platform Foxconn said was designed with the Japanese market as a starting point. The platform is built for flexibility, the company said, and can be adapted into a multipurpose vehicle, taxi, or logistics vehicle depending on city and industry requirements. The version on display at the Tokyo show is a taxi variant configured for urban passenger service. Foxconn said it integrates AI-powered translation, a smart battery management system, and a wide cabin with luggage space.

  The MODEL B, displayed in its FOXTRON BRIA production configuration, targets younger urban consumers. Foxconn said the FOXTRON BRIA went on sale in Taiwan earlier this year. The vehicle is built on a battery-electric platform and includes driver-assistance and active safety systems, the company said.

  Foxconn deputy general manager and spokesperson Wu Junyi said Japan is a priority market for the company, which already serves Japanese Tier 1 information and communications technology clients. Wu said Foxconn has been expanding into Japan's EV and AI industries in recent years and that the exhibition is intended to demonstrate its Contract Design and Manufacturing Services model — known as CDMS — as well as its Build-Operate-Localize, or BOL, strategy. Under CDMS, Foxconn handles product design, engineering, and volume manufacturing on behalf of clients rather than selling finished vehicles or infrastructure under its own brand to end users.

  ## Japan market significance

  The simultaneous debut of an AI infrastructure product and two EV platforms in Japan signals that Foxconn is positioning the country as a market for both its data center business and its vehicle platform licensing and manufacturing model. The exhibition appearance is the first time the company has used a Japan venue to present either the MDC or the MODEL A and MODEL B vehicles to Japanese audiences, according to the company's announcement.

  Foxconn said the FOXTRON BRIA production version of the MODEL B is already in commercial sale in Taiwan as of this year, making it the furthest along toward market deployment of the three exhibits.
body_zh: |
  鴻海科技集團於7月14日宣布，正在東京舉辦的2026台日形象展（Japan-Taiwan Image Exhibition）中，展出專為人工智慧工作負載設計的模組化資料中心，以及旗下兩款電動車平台，三項產品均為首次在日本公開亮相。

  展覽於7月15日至17日在東京新宿住友大樓三角廣場（Shinjuku Sumitomo Building Triangle Square）智慧城市展廳盛大舉行。鴻海表示，此三項展品代表集團兩大策略支柱：智慧電動車與AI運算基礎設施。

  ## 專為AI工廠工作負載打造的模組化資料中心

  鴻海此次展出的產品稱為模組化資料中心（Modular Data Center，簡稱MDC），是一種預製、標準化的設施模組，旨在協助企業快速部署AI運算基礎設施。鴻海表示，MDC將IT設備、電源供應、冷卻系統及液冷子系統整合於單一模組單元之中。液冷技術在AI伺服器部署中的應用日益普遍，原因在於相關處理器每機架所產生的熱量，遠超傳統伺服器單靠氣冷所能散除的上限。

  鴻海表示，MDC支援其所稱的「AI工廠」部署——亦即高密度AI訓練與推論環境——以及企業AI應用與高效能運算。集團在其中扮演的角色涵蓋系統設計、關鍵子系統整合，以及快速部署。這也是鴻海首次在日本公開展出MDC。

  ## 電動車產品陣容

  MODEL A是鴻海表示以日本市場為設計起點所打造的平台。該平台強調高度靈活性，可依據城市與產業需求，衍生為多功能車輛、計程車或物流車等不同型態。本次東京展出的版本為計程車型，專為都市旅客運輸服務而設計。鴻海表示，該車型整合AI語音翻譯功能、智慧電池管理系統，並設有寬敞車廂及行李置放空間。

  MODEL B則以FOXTRON BRIA量產版本亮相，鎖定年輕都市消費族群。鴻海表示，FOXTRON BRIA已於今年稍早在台灣正式開賣。該車採純電平台打造，並配備駕駛輔助與主動安全系統。

  鴻海副總經理暨發言人吳俊億表示，日本是集團的優先市場，鴻海目前已服務多家日本第一線資通訊（ICT）客戶。吳俊億指出，鴻海近年來持續深耕日本電動車與AI產業，此次展覽旨在展示集團的「合約設計暨製造服務」（Contract Design and Manufacturing Services，CDMS）模式，以及「建置、營運、在地化」（Build-Operate-Localize，BOL）策略。在CDMS模式下，鴻海代表客戶負責產品設計、工程開發與量產製造，而非以自有品牌直接向終端用戶銷售成品車輛或基礎設施。

  ## 日本市場的戰略意義

  在日本同步首發AI基礎設施產品與兩款電動車平台，顯示鴻海正將日本定位為其資料中心業務及車輛平台授權暨製造模式的重要市場。根據鴻海公告，此次展覽是集團首次以日本場地，向日本觀眾公開展示MDC、MODEL A及MODEL B三項產品。

  鴻海表示，MODEL B的FOXTRON BRIA量產版本已於今年在台灣正式進入商業銷售，是三項展品中商業化進程最為成熟的產品。
