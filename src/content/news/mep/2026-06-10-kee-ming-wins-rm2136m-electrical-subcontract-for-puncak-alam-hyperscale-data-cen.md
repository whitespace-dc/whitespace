body_en: |
  Kee Ming Group Bhd has secured a RM21.36 million subcontract to perform electrical engineering works at a hyperscale data centre in Puncak Alam, Selangor, the Kuala Lumpur-listed mechanical and electrical engineering company disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia filing on Tuesday.

  The contract was awarded to Kee Ming Electrical Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the group, by an unnamed third-party contractor. Kee Ming described the awarding contractor as a firm involved in integrated mechanical and electrical, or M&E, solutions with expertise in data centre infrastructure, covering power systems, cooling, and engineering, procurement and construction — commonly abbreviated as EPC — services for mission-critical facilities.

  ## Contract structure and timeline

  The subcontract is divided into two work packages. The first package commences upon the subsidiary's acceptance of the letter of award; the second begins once the contractor issues a notice to proceed. Kee Ming said both packages are expected to be completed by December 30, 2026, followed by a 12-month warranty period.

  The group said the contract is expected to contribute positively to earnings, earnings per share, and net assets for its financial year ending March 31, 2027.

  ## Market reaction

  Shares of Kee Ming closed three sen, or 2.9%, higher at RM1.08 on Tuesday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM351 million. The stock has risen more than 180% since the company listed on Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market — the exchange's board for smaller and emerging companies — in early February at an initial public offering price of 38 sen.

  ## Significance

  The award adds to evidence of sustained subcontracting activity flowing from Malaysia's data centre construction pipeline, with M&E specialists such as Kee Ming positioned to capture work from larger EPC contractors building hyperscale facilities. Hyperscale data centres are large-scale facilities designed to support the infrastructure requirements of major cloud and technology platforms. The Puncak Alam project, whose principal developer was not identified in the filing, represents the type of mission-critical assignment the group cited as a growth area at listing. Kee Ming's next financial milestone is the completion of both work packages by December 30, 2026.

  ## 奇明電氣獲馬國霸型資料中心逾2,136萬令吉電氣分包合約

  奇明集團有限公司（Kee Ming Group Bhd）旗下全資子公司奇明電氣私人有限公司（Kee Ming Electrical Sdn Bhd）日前取得一份價值2,136萬令吉的分包合約，將負責馬來西亞莎蘭戈州邦卡阿蘭（Puncak Alam）一座超大規模資料中心的電氣工程作業。這家在吉隆坡掛牌的機電工程公司已於週二向馬來西亞證券交易所（Bursa Malaysia）申報揭露此一消息。

  本合約由一名未具名的第三方承包商發包予奇明電氣。奇明描述該發包商為一家提供機電整合解決方案的企業，在資料中心基礎設施領域擁有豐富專業，涵蓋電力系統、冷卻工程，以及俗稱EPC（工程、採購與施工）的統包服務，專門服務關鍵任務設施。

  ## 合約架構與工期

  本分包合約分為兩個工作包。第一工作包於子公司接受委任書後即告啟動；第二工作包則待承包商發出開工通知後方才啟動。奇明表示，兩個工作包均預計於2026年12月30日前完工，其後進入為期12個月的保固期。

  集團表示，本合約預期將對截至2027年3月31日止財政年度的盈餘、每股盈餘及淨資產帶來正面貢獻。

  ## 市場反應

  奇明集團股票週二收盤上漲3仙（約2.9%），收報1.08令吉，公司市值達3.51億令吉。自今年2月初以38仙的首次公開募股（IPO）價格在馬來西亞證交所ACE市場——即專為中小型及新興企業設立的交易板塊——掛牌上市以來，奇明股價已累計上漲逾180%。

  ## 意義

  此合約的取得，再度印證馬來西亞資料中心建設管線持續向下游承包商釋出訂單的趨勢，奇明等機電專業廠商得以從負責興建超大規模設施的大型EPC承包商手中承接工程。超大規模資料中心是為支撐大型雲端運算及科技平台基礎設施需求而設計的大型專業設施。邦卡阿蘭這項計畫的主要開發商並未在申報文件中揭露，但其所代表的正是奇明在掛牌時所點名的關鍵任務型工程增長領域。奇明下一個重要財務里程碑，即為於2026年12月30日前完成兩個工作包的全部施工作業。
