body_en: |
  Nefab has opened an upgraded packaging test laboratory in Taoyuan, Taiwan, designed to validate the packaging of heavy AI server racks and other large equipment, with the addition of on-site power and cooling infrastructure that allows customers to run powered functional checks without moving equipment to a separate facility.

  The 480-square-metre facility can accommodate packaged loads of up to 6,500 kg. Its testing procedures cover vibration, incline impact, drop, compression and climate simulation — all intended to replicate the stresses equipment experiences during transport and storage. Testing follows ISTA (International Safe Transit Association), ASTM, ETSI, ISO and Amazon protocols, as well as customer-specific requirements.

  ## Power-on testing at the same site

  The distinguishing addition to the upgraded lab is integrated power and cooling infrastructure, which allows compatible server racks to be powered on and functionally verified after packaging tests without being transported elsewhere. Nefab said the arrangement eliminates additional handling and transit between test stages, reducing the risk of further damage affecting the results.

  "With the new power and cooling infrastructure, customers can carry out power-on tests on their server racks at our lab after packaging testing," said Fred Hapiak, Executive Vice President for the Asia-Pacific region at Nefab Group. "This allows them to assess both how well the packaging protects the equipment and whether the equipment still works as expected, without moving it to another facility and exposing it to additional handling."

  Nefab's local engineering team in Taoyuan supports customers through pre-test consultation, material testing, results analysis and packaging design or redesign. In-house prototyping and sampling capabilities mean revised packaging designs can be produced and re-tested at the same site.

  ## AI server weight and size driving demand

  Nefab attributed the expansion to growing demand for packaging validation for larger and heavier equipment used in AI and cloud infrastructure. The company said hardware makers have increased focus on packaging tests as server systems have grown in size and weight and operators have sought to reduce damage during shipment.

  Beyond AI servers, the Taoyuan lab also serves customers in advanced cooling systems, lithium-ion batteries, e-mobility, telecoms and semiconductors.

  ## Taiwan's role in the supply chain

  Taiwan is a manufacturing and product development hub for electronics and data centre supply chains, including systems linked to AI deployments. Nefab framed the investment in Taoyuan as part of a broader effort to position engineering support closer to customers in those supply chains.

  "Taiwan is an important hub for our customers' product development and manufacturing," said Per Öhagen, President and CEO of Nefab Group. "We're strengthening our presence and performance by investing close to our customers, combining local engineering capabilities with expertise from across the Nefab Group."

  The upgraded facility is one of six ISTA-certified test labs Nefab operates across Asia, Europe and the United States. Geoffroy You, Managing Director for Taiwan, hosted the opening ceremony. Nefab said the lab operates in a controlled environment designed to protect customer products and confidential information.

  Nefab employs more than 6,000 people across 39 countries and reported annual turnover of SEK 10.3 billion — equivalent to approximately one billion US dollars — in its 2025 financial year. The group focuses on industrial packaging and logistics, serving sectors that include datacom and cloud, energy, medtech, mining and construction, semiconductors and telecoms.

  The Taoyuan facility's capacity to test packaged loads up to 6,500 kg positions it to accommodate the growing weight profile of AI server rack shipments, as rack-scale liquid cooling and high-density GPU systems continue to increase in mass across the industry.

  Nefab在台灣桃園啟用一座升級版包裝測試實驗室，專門用於驗證重型AI伺服器機架及其他大型設備的包裝品質。此次升級的一大亮點，是實驗室新增了現場電力與冷卻基礎設施，讓客戶無需將設備移送他處，即可在同一地點進行通電功能檢查。

  這座實驗室占地480平方公尺，可承載最重6,500公斤的包裝貨物。測試項目涵蓋振動、傾斜衝擊、跌落、壓縮及氣候模擬，全面模擬設備在運輸與倉儲過程中所承受的各種應力。測試依循ISTA（國際安全運輸協會）、ASTM、ETSI、ISO及亞馬遜的規範，同時亦可依客戶需求量身訂製。

  ## 同一地點完成通電測試

  此次實驗室升級最具特色的新增功能，是整合式電力與冷卻基礎設施。這套設施讓相容的伺服器機架完成包裝測試後，無需移送他處，即可直接在原地通電並進行功能驗證。Nefab表示，這樣的配置省去了各測試階段之間的額外搬運與運輸，降低了因再次移動而引發損壞、進而影響測試結果的風險。

  「透過新增的電力與冷卻基礎設施，客戶可以在完成包裝測試後，直接在我們的實驗室對伺服器機架進行通電測試，」Nefab集團亞太區執行副總裁Fred Hapiak表示。「如此一來，客戶不必將設備移往其他設施、承受額外搬運的風險，就能同時評估包裝的防護效果，以及設備是否仍能如預期正常運作。」

  Nefab駐桃園的在地工程團隊，可全程協助客戶進行測試前諮詢、材料測試、結果分析，以及包裝設計或重新設計。實驗室本身具備原型製作與打樣能力，修改後的包裝設計可在同一地點生產並重新測試。

  ## AI伺服器重量與體積推動需求成長

  Nefab將此次擴建歸因於AI與雲端基礎設施所使用的大型重型設備，其包裝驗證需求持續增長。該公司表示，隨著伺服器系統的體積與重量不斷增加，硬體製造商對包裝測試的重視程度大幅提升，業者也積極尋求降低出貨途中的損壞風險。

  除AI伺服器外，桃園實驗室也服務先進冷卻系統、鋰離子電池、電動移動載具、電信及半導體等領域的客戶。

  ## 台灣在供應鏈中的角色

  台灣是電子與資料中心供應鏈的製造與產品開發重鎮，涵蓋與AI部署相關的各類系統。Nefab將在桃園的投資定位為更貼近供應鏈客戶、強化在地工程支援的整體戰略之一。

  「台灣是我們客戶進行產品開發與製造的重要樞紐，」Nefab集團總裁暨執行長Per Öhagen表示。「我們透過在客戶附近持續投資，結合在地工程能力與Nefab集團的全球專業，不斷強化我們的在地佈局與服務品質。」

  此次升級後的設施，是Nefab在亞洲、歐洲及美國共六座ISTA認證測試實驗室之一。台灣區董事總經理Geoffroy You主持了開幕典禮。Nefab表示，實驗室採受控環境運作，以保護客戶的產品安全與機密資訊。

  Nefab在全球39個國家擁有逾6,000名員工，於2025財年的年營業額達103億瑞典克朗，約合10億美元。集團專注於工業包裝與物流，服務領域涵蓋資料通訊與雲端、能源、醫療科技、礦業與建築、半導體及電信等產業。

  桃園設施可測試最重達6,500公斤的包裝貨物，使其具備承接AI伺服器機架出貨日益增重趨勢的能力——隨著機架級液冷方案與高密度GPU系統在業界持續普及，整體重量也不斷攀升。
