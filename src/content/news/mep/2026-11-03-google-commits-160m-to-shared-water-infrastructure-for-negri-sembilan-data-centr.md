body_en: |
  Google is allocating US$160 million (RM651 million) to build shared water infrastructure in Malaysia's Negri Sembilan state, linking the investment to both community water supply needs and its planned data centre campus at Springhill Industrial Park in Port Dickson.

  The funding will cover construction of an off-river storage reservoir and a water treatment plant with a production capacity of 65 million litres of potable water per day once operational, the company said in a statement. Malaysian construction and engineering group Gamuda Bhd will carry out the project in partnership with the Negri Sembilan state government and state water utility Syarikat Air Negeri Sembilan Sdn Bhd (SAINS). Upon completion, ownership of the facilities will transfer to Menteri Besar Negri Sembilan (Incorporated) (MBINS), a statutory body responsible for managing state assets.

  ## How the system will work

  The reservoir will be sited along the Linggi River and is designed to capture excess river flow and rainfall during periods of high water yield. Storing that surplus serves two purposes: it reduces flood risk in surrounding flood-prone areas, and it provides a reserve that the treatment plant can draw from during lower-flow periods without disrupting the river's natural flow. Raw water channelled from the reservoir will be purified at the treatment plant before distribution for domestic and commercial use.

  ## Water replenishment rationale

  Google's Director and Head of Global Infrastructure and Energy for Asia Pacific, Drew Byrnes, said the commitment reflects the company's global target to replenish 120 per cent of the freshwater volume it consumes. Byrnes noted that Google's own water risk assessment found local water sources in Negri Sembilan are not at high risk of scarcity or depletion, but said the company is committed to growing sustainably through water replenishment regardless of baseline risk levels.

  ## Data centre campus and broader commitments

  The water infrastructure is one component of a broader development package in Negri Sembilan. Google said its planned data centre campus in Port Dickson is custom-designed to be among the most energy-efficient in the world and will support Google services including Search, Maps and Chrome for users across Malaysia. The company said it will bear the full cost of electricity consumed by the campus and the direct infrastructure costs required to connect the facility to the power grid, in compliance with local regulations. Google described its commitment to Negri Sembilan as covering both the construction phase and a multi-decade operational lifecycle.

  The company also said the overall development programme includes targeted education and workforce development initiatives, though no financial figures for those programmes were disclosed in the statement.

  ## State perspective

  Invest Negri Sembilan chief executive officer Datuk Najmuddin Sharif Sarimon said the planned data centre marks a significant milestone in the state's efforts to attract high-value digital infrastructure investment. He said Google's approach to site selection, water infrastructure, power upgrades and skills development reflected a responsible model, and that the project is expected to draw complementary investments, expand opportunities for local companies and develop a skilled workforce.

  ## Significance

  The structure of the deal is notable: a private technology company funding public water infrastructure that, upon completion, becomes state-owned and serves the broader population rather than being reserved for the data centre's exclusive use. That model positions water investment as a community benefit alongside a commercial build-out, and illustrates an approach some hyperscale operators are exploring in regions where resource constraints and community impact are scrutinised closely alongside large-scale power and water draws.

  Google said it looks forward to continuing close collaboration with the Negri Sembilan state government and local partners from construction through to long-term operations.

  Google宣布在馬來西亞森美蘭州斥資1.6億美元（約6.51億令吉），興建共用水利基礎設施，將此投資與當地社區供水需求，以及其規劃在波德申春日工業園區興建的資料中心園區相連結。

  根據該公司聲明，這筆資金將用於建造一座離河式儲水水庫，以及一座每日可生產6,500萬公升飲用水的淨水廠。馬來西亞建設工程集團Gamuda Bhd將與森美蘭州政府及州屬水務公司「森州水務有限公司」（Syarikat Air Negeri Sembilan Sdn Bhd，SAINS）合作執行此計畫。設施竣工後，所有權將移交予負責管理州有資產的法定機構「森美蘭大臣有限公司」（Menteri Besar Negeri Sembilan Incorporated，MBINS）。

  ## 水系統運作方式

  水庫將設於靈宜河（Linggi River）沿岸，設計用於在豐水期蓄積多餘的河水與雨水。儲存這些盈餘具有雙重效益：一方面可降低周邊易淹水地區的洪患風險；另一方面可在枯水期提供淨水廠所需的備用水源，同時不干擾河川的自然水流。從水庫引入的原水將在淨水廠經過淨化處理，再配送供家用與商業用途使用。

  ## 補水計畫的理念

  Google亞太區全球基礎設施暨能源事務總監兼主管Drew Byrnes表示，此承諾體現了公司達成「補充自身消耗淡水量120%」的全球目標。Byrnes指出，Google自行進行的水資源風險評估顯示，森美蘭州當地水源面臨短缺或枯竭的風險並不高，但他強調，無論基準風險高低，公司均致力透過水資源補充，以永續方式持續成長。

  ## 資料中心園區與更廣泛的承諾

  水利基礎設施只是森美蘭州更大規模開發計畫的一部分。Google表示，其在波德申規劃的資料中心園區採客製化設計，目標躋身全球能源效率最高之列，並將支援Google搜尋、Maps及Chrome等服務，為馬來西亞全國用戶提供服務。公司表示，將自行承擔園區全部電力消耗費用，以及將設施接入電網所需的直接基礎設施建設成本，且全程遵守當地法規。Google將對森美蘭州的承諾定位為涵蓋建設期及長達數十年的長期營運週期。

  此外，公司表示整體開發計畫還包括定向教育與人才培育措施，但聲明中並未披露相關計畫的具體金額。

  ## 州政府觀點

  「投資森美蘭」執行長拿督Najmuddin Sharif Sarimon表示，此資料中心計畫是該州吸引高價值數位基礎設施投資的重要里程碑。他指出，Google在選址、水利基礎設施、電力升級及技能培育方面的做法，體現了負責任的投資模式，並預期此計畫將吸引互補投資，為本地企業拓展商機，同時培育具備專業技能的在地人才。

  ## 本案意義

  此案的結構頗具看點：一家私人科技公司出資興建公共水利基礎設施，竣工後移交州政府所有，服務廣大民眾，而非專供資料中心獨佔使用。這種模式將水利投資定位為社區公益，而非僅作為商業開發的配套建設，也說明了部分超大型雲端業者（Hyperscaler）正在積極探索的路徑——尤其是在電力與水資源的大規模消耗備受外界審視、社區影響高度受關注的地區。

  Google表示，期待從建設期到長期營運各階段，持續與森美蘭州政府及當地合作夥伴保持緊密合作。
