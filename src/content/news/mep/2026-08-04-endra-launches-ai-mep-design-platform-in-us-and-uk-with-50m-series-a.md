body_en: |
  Stockholm-based startup Endra is entering the US and UK markets with an artificial intelligence-driven platform for mechanical, electrical and plumbing (MEP) design, backed by $50 million in Series A funding led by US venture capital firm Andreessen Horowitz.

  The company has established a North American headquarters in New York, a second US office in San Francisco, and a UK office in London. Co-founder and president Anton Juric will lead US operations, which Endra describes as the centrepiece of its global growth strategy.

  ## What the platform does

  MEP engineering covers the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems that make buildings function — heating, ventilation, power distribution, water supply, and related infrastructure. Designing those systems for large commercial buildings is a labour-intensive process that requires engineers to manually place devices, route conduits, coordinate systems across disciplines, and produce documentation that meets local building codes.

  Endra's platform is designed to automate that workflow. It ingests standard building model files and integrates with building information modelling (BIM) tools including Autodesk Revit, a widely used design application, then reconstructs each building in a detailed three-dimensional environment. From that model, it automates system design, device placement, conduit routing, and the generation of both 3D and 2D deliverables, as well as compliance-ready documentation, using artificial intelligence, simulation, and computational geometry.

  The company says a code-compliant electrical design for a 500,000-square-foot commercial building — roughly the footprint of a large office tower or hospital — that traditionally takes around two months can be completed in less than a day on its platform.

  ## Funding and market entry

  The US and UK launch follows Endra's $50 million Series A, which the company said closed earlier this year with Andreessen Horowitz leading the round. Endra was founded by Anton Juric, Niklas Lindgren, David Rydberg, and Gustav Hammarlund.

  Juric attributed the US focus to direct engagement with large design firms. "Having spent the past year in discussions with the largest design firms in the world, one thing has become clear: the shift to AI-native engineering is underway," he said. "The firms that move first will have more capacity, faster timelines, and a new way to scale their expertise. The US is the largest construction market in the world, and we are building there in person because enterprise trust is won in the room."

  ## UK role and hiring plans

  Endra's London office is intended to serve major engineering consultancies and act as a strategic hub for enterprise customers across Europe and the Middle East, the company said. Endra is actively hiring across engineering, sales, and customer success roles in New York, San Francisco, and London, targeting approximately 100 employees globally over the next twelve months.

  總部位於瑞典斯德哥爾摩的新創公司Endra，在美國創投機構Andreessen Horowitz領投的5,000萬美元A輪融資支持下，推出以人工智慧驅動的機械、電氣與給排水（MEP）設計平台，正式進軍美國與英國市場。

  Endra已在紐約設立北美總部，並於舊金山開設第二間美國辦公室，同時在倫敦設立英國辦事處。共同創辦人暨總裁Anton Juric將主導美國業務，Endra將此定位為全球成長策略的核心支柱。

  ## 平台功能

  MEP工程涵蓋維持建築物正常運作所需的機械、電氣與給排水系統，包括暖氣、通風、電力配送、供水及相關基礎設施。為大型商業建築設計上述系統，是一項高度勞力密集的工程作業——工程師必須手動佈設設備、規劃管線走向、協調跨專業系統，並製作符合當地建築法規的技術文件。

  Endra的平台旨在自動化整個工作流程。它能讀取標準建築模型檔案，並與建築資訊模型（BIM）工具整合，包括廣泛使用的設計應用程式Autodesk Revit，繼而在詳細的三維環境中重建完整建築結構。在此基礎上，平台運用人工智慧、模擬運算與計算幾何技術，自動完成系統設計、設備配置、管線路徑規劃，以及3D與2D交付文件的生成，並產出符合法規要求的技術文件。

  據該公司表示，一棟約4萬6,000平方公尺（50萬平方英尺）的大型商業建築——規模相當於大型辦公大樓或醫院——其符合法規的電氣設計，傳統上需耗費約兩個月時間；而在Endra的平台上，不到一天即可完成。

  ## 融資與市場進入

  此次進軍美英市場，緊隨Endra完成5,000萬美元A輪融資之後。該公司表示，本輪融資由Andreessen Horowitz領投，已於今年稍早正式完成。Endra由Anton Juric、Niklas Lindgren、David Rydberg與Gustav Hammarlund四人共同創辦。

  Juric將公司聚焦美國市場的決策，歸因於與頂尖設計公司的深度交流。他表示：「過去一年與全球最大型設計公司的對談，讓我們清楚洞察到一件事：轉向AI原生工程的浪潮已然啟動。率先行動的公司將擁有更充裕的產能、更快速的交付時程，以及一種可規模化複製專業知識的嶄新模式。美國是全球最大的建築市場，我們選擇親赴當地深耕，因為企業信任只能在第一線面對面建立。」

  ## 英國辦公室定位與人才招募計畫

  Endra表示，倫敦辦公室的設立，旨在服務大型工程顧問公司，並作為歐洲與中東企業客戶的策略樞紐。目前，Endra正積極在紐約、舊金山與倫敦，針對工程、業務及客戶成功等職位公開招募人才，預計在未來十二個月內，將全球員工人數擴充至約100人。
