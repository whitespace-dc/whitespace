body_en: |
  ## Launch

  Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Air-Conditioning Europe (MHIAE) is set to launch the KXZR3, a three-pipe variable refrigerant flow (VRF) system using the R32 refrigerant, the company announced. The system builds on MHIAE's existing two-pipe KXZ3 platform, extending pipe length capabilities, adding new branching controllers, and reducing the outdoor unit footprint by 28% compared to its predecessor.

  VRF systems are central plant air-conditioning architectures in which a single outdoor unit circulates refrigerant to multiple indoor units, allowing simultaneous heating and cooling across different zones. Three-pipe configurations add a dedicated pipe for simultaneous heat recovery — allowing one zone to cool while another heats — compared to simpler two-pipe systems.

  ## Capacity and performance

  The KXZR3 is available in 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 horsepower (hp) models. MHIAE rates the range at a Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (SEER) of up to 9.03 and a Seasonal Coefficient of Performance (SCOP) of up to 4.28. SEER and SCOP are standardised EU metrics measuring cooling and heating efficiency respectively over a full season; higher values indicate greater energy efficiency.

  Pipe runs extend to 1,000 metres total equivalent length, with a maximum height difference of 50 metres between the outdoor unit and connected indoor units. The system supports a 150% connection ratio — meaning the total rated capacity of connected indoor units can exceed the outdoor unit's rated capacity by up to half — and accommodates up to 50 indoor units per system.

  Indoor unit types supported include wall-mounted, ducted, cassette, ceiling-suspended, and floor-standing models.

  ## Noise and installation

  MHIAE states minimum sound pressure levels of 42.5 dB(A) for the KXZR3, with six selectable silent mode settings available to operators who need to reduce noise output at specific times.

  On installation, the company said single-branch power flow distributor (PFD) units require only a three-wire power connection between the indoor unit and branch controller, which the company said simplifies wiring. Multi-branch units connect via a Superlink II communication line and require a separate power supply.

  ## Branching controllers and refrigerant safety

  Alongside the KXZR3, MHIAE has developed a new range of refrigerant branching controllers — the components that distribute refrigerant from the main pipe to individual indoor units. The new range includes four-branch and eight-branch units, complementing existing single-branch models.

  All branching units include integrated gas and liquid shut-off valves, allowing a specific branch to be isolated if a refrigerant leak is detected, without shutting down the entire system.

  The initial factory refrigerant charge is either 7.7 kg or 8.7 kg across the single-unit outdoor platforms, depending on model. MHIAE said integrated leak-detection functionality will be available from 2027; a separate, standalone leak-detector solution is available in the interim for installations that require it before that date.

  ## 產品發表

  三菱重工工業歐洲空調事業（MHIAE）宣布即將推出KXZR3，這是一款採用R32冷媒的三管式變冷媒流量（VRF）系統。該系統以MHIAE現有的兩管式KXZ3平台為基礎，進一步延伸配管長度、新增分歧控制器，並將室外機佔地面積較前代縮減28%。

  VRF系統是一種集中式空調架構，由單一室外機將冷媒循環輸送至多台室內機，可在不同區域同時進行冷暖氣調節。相較於較簡單的兩管式系統，三管式配置新增一條專用管路用於同步熱回收——讓某一區域在製冷的同時，另一區域可進行暖氣供應。

  ## 容量與性能

  KXZR3提供8、10、12、14、16及18馬力（hp）共六種機型。MHIAE標示該系列的季節能效比（SEER）最高可達9.03，季節性能係數（SCOP）最高可達4.28。SEER與SCOP均為歐盟標準化指標，分別衡量整個季節的冷氣與暖氣能源效率，數值愈高代表能效愈佳。

  配管總等效長度最長可達1,000公尺，室外機與所連接室內機之間的最大高低落差為50公尺。系統支援150%的連接率——意即所連接室內機的額定容量總和，最多可超過室外機額定容量的一半——並可連接最多50台室內機。

  支援的室內機類型包括壁掛式、風管式、嵌入式卡匣型、天花板懸掛式及落地式等機型。

  ## 噪音與安裝

  MHIAE表示KXZR3的最低音壓等級為42.5 dB(A)，並提供六段可選靜音模式，供操作人員在特定時段有需要降低噪音時使用。

  在安裝方面，公司表示單分歧式電力流量分配器（PFD）機組只需在室內機與分歧控制器之間進行三線電力連接，該公司表示此設計可簡化配線作業。多分歧式機組則透過Superlink II通訊線路連接，並需要獨立的電源供應。

  ## 分歧控制器與冷媒安全

  MHIAE在推出KXZR3的同時，也開發了全新的冷媒分歧控制器系列——這些元件負責將冷媒從主管路分配至各台室內機。新系列包含四分歧及八分歧機組，與現有的單分歧機型形成完整配套。

  所有分歧機組均內建氣體與液體截止閥，當偵測到冷媒洩漏時，可針對特定分支進行隔離，無需關閉整套系統運作。

  單台室外機平台的出廠冷媒初始填充量依機型不同，分別為7.7公斤或8.7公斤。MHIAE表示整合式洩漏偵測功能預計於2027年推出；在此之前，若安裝場所有提前導入的需求，目前已有獨立的外掛式洩漏偵測器解決方案可供選用。
