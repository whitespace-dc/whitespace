body_en: |
  Nvidia is reportedly working to cultivate Taiwan-listed Yichan Precision Industry (stock code: 2486) as a second-source supplier of package-level thermal components for its next-generation Feynman GPU platform, according to supply chain sources cited by 經濟日報 on August 9. The development could end Jentech's expected sole grip on Micro-channel Lid production for that platform.

  Yichan had already entered the thermal supply chains of Google and Amazon when it submitted samples to Nvidia for a Micro-channel Lid — a heat spreader that integrates microfluidic channels at the chip-package level to shorten the heat-transfer path, centralize coolant-flow management, and reduce liquid-leakage risk. Industry sources had assumed Jentech, described in the report as the thermal sector's top-ranked stock, would capture that business alone; Yichan's candidacy has significantly raised the company's market profile, according to institutional analysts cited in the same report.

  ## Micro-channel Lid and the Feynman platform

  GPU power consumption is trending toward 2,000 watts per unit, a threshold at which traditional system-level cooling faces fundamental constraints, the report says. The Micro-channel Lid architecture addresses this by shortening the heat-transfer path through microscale channels embedded directly in the lid and concentrating flow management at the package level. Yichan completed early qualification work using precision metalworking and electroplating capabilities, according to supply chain sources.

  Institutional analysts noted that AI chip enlargement and Chiplet packaging — a design that assembles multiple smaller dies into a single module — have worsened warping, the structural deformation caused by mismatched thermal expansion during operation. The trend is raising both unit volumes and selling prices for Stiffeners, structural reinforcement frames that counteract warping, and high-end Lids. Gold-plating, which reduces thermal resistance, adds a further technical barrier the report says aligns with Yichan's established competencies.

  ## Current product mix and customers

  Yichan's thermal revenue is divided between two product families. Stiffeners account for roughly 40 percent of thermal revenue and ship primarily to Google, Amazon, and Broadcom; an AMD project is expected to ramp up in 2027. Lids are primarily built for systems using Nvidia's Grace CPU, with adoption into the Blackwell and Vera Rubin platforms expected progressively. The company is also shipping gold-plated high-end lids to Microsoft, according to the report.

  Chairman Zhou Wanshun declined to comment on specific orders or customers, a standard company position, but said that strong thermal-shipment momentum and new capacity coming online mean revenue is expected to grow month-over-month and quarter-over-quarter, with annual performance improving each year for the next three years.

  ## Capacity expansion and revenue targets

  Yichan is shifting its Taiwan factories away from low-margin LED lead frames to high-end lids and stiffeners. Taiwan operations account for roughly 70 percent of total company revenue. Thermal production capacity is targeted to double in 2026 and double again in 2027.

  Institutional analysts cited in the report project Yichan's thermal output at NT$3.6 billion for 2026 and total company revenue at NT$6 billion for 2027.

  A new plant in the Sanchong district of New Taipei City is planned to break ground in the second half of 2026 and be completed by end of 2027. The facility is designated as a research-and-development base for liquid-cooled Micro-channel Lid products.

  ## Industry significance

  Should Yichan win volume orders for Micro-channel Lid components on the Feynman platform, it would introduce a second-source dynamic into a segment that supply chains had viewed as a single-supplier arrangement. Institutional analysts said that successfully expanding its share of thermal orders would meaningfully enlarge Yichan's longer-term revenue potential.

  The completion of the Sanchong facility by end of 2027 is the nearest-term milestone tied to Yichan's ambitions in liquid-cooled package-level thermal products.
body_zh: |
  根據《經濟日報》8月9日引述供應鏈消息人士的報導，輝達（Nvidia）據悉正積極培育台灣上市公司億展精密工業（股票代號：2486），使其成為次世代Feynman GPU平台封裝級散熱元件的第二來源供應商。此一發展可能終結鋒泰預期在該平台微流道上蓋（Micro-channel Lid）生產業務上獨占的局面。

  億展先前已進入Google與亞馬遜的散熱供應鏈，並已向輝達送樣微流道上蓋。微流道上蓋是一種在晶片封裝層面整合微流道的均熱片，能縮短導熱路徑、集中管理冷卻液流向，並降低液體滲漏風險。業界原本普遍預期散熱族群股王鋒泰將獨吞這筆業務，但億展躋身候選名單一事，已大幅提升市場對該公司的關注度，同篇報導所引述的法人分析師如此指出。

  ## 微流道上蓋與Feynman平台

  報導指出，GPU功耗正朝每顆2,000瓦的門檻邁進，在此水準下，傳統系統級散熱面臨根本性的限制。微流道上蓋架構透過直接內嵌於上蓋的微尺度流道縮短導熱路徑，並在封裝層面集中管理冷卻液流量，藉此因應上述挑戰。供應鏈消息人士指出，億展已運用精密金屬加工與電鍍能力完成早期資格認證作業。

  法人分析師指出，AI晶片尺寸持續放大，加上小晶片（Chiplet）封裝——即將多顆較小的晶粒組合成單一模組的設計——已加劇翹曲問題，亦即運作時因熱膨脹係數不匹配所造成的結構變形。此一趨勢正同步推升補強框（Stiffener，用以抵抗翹曲的結構補強框架）及高階上蓋的出貨量與售價。鍍金工藝可降低熱阻，進一步墊高技術門檻，報導稱此與億展既有的核心能力高度吻合。

  ## 現有產品組合與客戶

  億展的散熱營收分為兩大產品線。補強框約占散熱營收的四成，主要出貨給Google、亞馬遜與博通（Broadcom）；AMD相關專案則預計於2027年開始放量。上蓋業務目前主要供應採用輝達Grace CPU的系統，並預計逐步導入Blackwell及Vera Rubin平台。報導並指出，該公司亦已向微軟出貨鍍金高階上蓋。

  董事長周萬順對特定訂單或客戶內容不予置評，此為公司一貫立場，但他表示，散熱出貨動能強勁，加上新產能陸續投產，預計營收將逐月成長、逐季成長，未來三年每一年的年度表現均將優於前一年。

  ## 產能擴充與營收目標

  億展正積極將台灣廠區的生產重心從低毛利的LED導線架轉向高階上蓋與補強框。台灣營運約占公司整體營收的七成。散熱產能目標為2026年倍增，並於2027年再度倍增。

  報導引述法人分析師預估，億展2026年散熱產值將達新台幣36億元，2027年公司總營收則上看新台幣60億元。

  新北市三重區的新廠預計於2026年下半年動土、2027年底完工，定位為液冷微流道上蓋產品的研發基地。

  ## 產業意義

  若億展成功取得Feynman平台微流道上蓋的量產訂單，將在供應鏈原本視為單一供應商格局的細分領域中引入第二來源競爭機制。法人分析師表示，若能順利擴大散熱訂單占比，將大幅提升億展的長期營收潛力。

  三重新廠預計於2027年底完工，是億展在液冷封裝級散熱產品布局上最近期的關鍵里程碑。
