body_en: |
  Daikin has introduced a coolant distribution unit (CDU) range targeting high-density AI data centre applications, with per-unit capacities spanning 400 kW to 2.5 MW and an architecture that allows up to ten units to be operated as a single integrated system.

  The announcement expands Daikin's data centre cooling portfolio at a time when AI workloads are driving demand for liquid cooling infrastructure capable of handling thermal loads that can shift rapidly and unpredictably.

  ## Capacity and system configuration

  Each CDU in the range is rated between 400 kW and 2.5 MW. For deployments requiring greater aggregate capacity, up to ten units can be coupled in parallel and managed as one system through Daikin's proprietary control logic, enabling centralised control of circuit temperature and pressure across the combined array.

  For larger-scale projects, Daikin also offers preassembled, engineered skid solutions — factory-integrated assemblies of multiple CDUs already configured for parallel operation — intended to reduce on-site commissioning time.

  ## Thermal control for variable AI loads

  A CDU in a liquid-cooled data centre sits between the facility water supply (FWS) and the technology cooling system (TCS) — the loop that carries coolant directly to servers or rack-level heat exchangers. Managing the boundary between those two circuits is critical when AI training or inference workloads cause rapid swings in heat output.

  Daikin said the new units combine variable-speed pumps with high-accuracy flow, pressure, and temperature sensors to allow dynamic adjustment of TCS operating parameters. A modulating valve dedicated to the FWS primary circuit, equipped with an equal-percentage control characteristic and a high-speed actuator, regulates thermal energy exchange between the FWS and TCS circuits and controls TCS outlet temperature according to configured parameters.

  ## Resilience and redundancy features

  The system architecture includes several features the company said are intended to support continuous operation:

  - Redundant pumps
  - Duplicated pressure and temperature sensors
  - Integrated leak detection
  - Advanced alarm management
  - Options supporting continuity of electrical supply

  The hydraulic circuit is manufactured entirely from AISI 316L stainless steel — a grade selected for corrosion resistance — and uses grooved mechanical couplings throughout, eliminating welded joints. Daikin said this approach is intended to simplify both installation and maintenance over the equipment's service life.

  ## Serviceability design

  All units in the range are designed for front-side access only, which reduces the clearance required around the equipment — a practical consideration in space-constrained data centre mechanical rooms. Pumps can be removed via dedicated sliding rails, a design intended to speed component replacement.

  ## Significance

  The introduction of a CDU range extending to 2.5 MW per unit, with multi-unit parallel scaling, reflects the increasing thermal density of AI-oriented data centres, where individual racks can generate tens of kilowatts and aggregate facility loads have grown substantially. The combination of stainless steel hydraulics, modular parallel control, and high-speed valve actuation positions the product line for operators who require both corrosion-resistant infrastructure and fine-grained thermal response.

  Daikin's inclusion of preassembled skid solutions indicates the company is also addressing the accelerated deployment timelines common in data centre buildouts, where factory integration can reduce the engineering and installation work required on site.

  ## 容量規格與系統配置

  該系列每台CDU的額定容量介於400 kW至2.5 MW之間。對於需要更大總容量的部署場合，最多可將十台機組並聯耦合，並透過大金專屬控制邏輯統一管理，實現對整合陣列中迴路溫度與壓力的集中控制。

  ## 針對AI可變負載的熱管理

  在液冷資料中心中，CDU位於設施供水（FWS）與技術冷卻系統（TCS）之間，TCS負責將冷卻液直接輸送至伺服器或機架級熱交換器。當AI訓練或推論工作負載導致散熱量急遽起伏時，管理這兩個迴路之間的邊界至關重要。

  大金表示，新款機組整合了變頻幫浦與高精度流量、壓力及溫度感測器，可對TCS運作參數進行動態調整。專為FWS主迴路配置的調節閥具備等百分比控制特性，並搭載高速致動器，負責調節FWS與TCS迴路之間的熱能交換，並依據設定參數控制TCS出口溫度。

  ## 韌性與備援功能

  大金表示，該系統架構包含多項旨在支援持續運作的設計：

  - 備援幫浦
  - 壓力與溫度感測器雙重冗餘配置
  - 整合式漏液偵測
  - 進階警報管理
  - 支援供電連續性的選配功能

  水力迴路全部採用AISI 316L不鏽鋼製造——該材質以耐腐蝕性著稱——並全面採用溝槽式機械接頭，消除焊接接點。大金表示，此設計理念旨在簡化設備整個使用年限期間的安裝與維護作業。

  ## 可維護性設計

  該系列所有機組均採單面前側存取設計，可降低設備周圍所需的淨空間距——對空間受限的資料中心機房而言具有實際意義。幫浦可透過專用滑軌拆卸，此設計旨在加速零組件更換作業。

  ## 意義

  推出單機容量最高達2.5 MW、支援多機並聯擴展的CDU系列，反映出以AI為導向的資料中心熱密度持續攀升的趨勢——單一機架的散熱量可達數十千瓦，整體設施負載亦大幅成長。不鏽鋼水力系統、模組化並聯控制與高速閥門致動的組合，使這條產品線得以定位於同時需要耐腐蝕基礎設施與精細熱響應能力的資料中心營運業者。

  大金將預組裝撬裝解決方案納入產品組合，顯示公司亦正積極因應資料中心建設中常見的加速部署時程——在這類場景中，工廠預整合可大幅減少現場所需的工程與安裝工作量。
