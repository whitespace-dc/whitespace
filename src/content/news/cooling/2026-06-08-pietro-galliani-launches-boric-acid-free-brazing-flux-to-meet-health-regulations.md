  ## New flux range targets regulatory and health compliance

  Italian brazing materials manufacturer Pietro Galliani has released Galflo Eco Green, a reformulated brazing flux range designed to eliminate reprotoxic hazard designations and comply with current chemical safety regulations, the company said.

  The product line is formulated to remove the H360 and H361 hazard statements — classifications under the European Union's Classification, Labelling and Packaging (CLP) regulation that indicate a substance may damage fertility or an unborn child. The range is also free of boric acid, a compound that carries those reprotoxic classifications and has faced increasing regulatory scrutiny under REACH, the EU framework governing the registration, evaluation, and authorisation of chemicals.

  ## Formulation and available forms

  Pietro Galliani said the Galflo Eco Green products retain the core functional characteristics of traditional brazing fluxes: oxide removal capability, process stability, and consistent brazing results. The company stated that performance is equivalent to conventional formulations despite the changed chemistry.

  The range is available in three forms: powder, paste, and coated rods, allowing use across different application methods and process configurations.

  ## Regulatory context

  The development directly responds to health and safety requirements that have tightened around substances classified as reprotoxic under European chemical law. REACH compliance is a baseline requirement for chemicals sold within the EU, and the removal of boric acid addresses a specific substance that regulators have increasingly restricted in professional and industrial applications.

  For manufacturers and contractors that use brazing processes — common in refrigeration, HVAC, and plumbing systems — sourcing flux materials that meet current hazard classification rules has become a procurement and compliance consideration alongside technical performance criteria.

  ## Industry significance

  The introduction of a flux that meets the H360/H361-free and boric acid-free requirements without a stated performance penalty addresses a gap between regulatory demands and process reliability that has been a practical obstacle for industrial users. If the performance equivalence claim holds in field conditions, the product gives operators a route to compliance without process changes.

  Pietro Galliani has not disclosed pricing, availability outside Europe, or a timeline for broader market rollout.
