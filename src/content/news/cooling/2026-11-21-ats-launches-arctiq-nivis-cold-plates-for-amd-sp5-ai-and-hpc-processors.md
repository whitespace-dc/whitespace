body_en: |
  Advanced Thermal Solutions, Inc. (ATS) has introduced the ArctiQ NIVIS Series of liquid-cooling cold plates designed for AMD's Socket Platform 5 (SP5) processors used in artificial intelligence, high-performance computing (HPC), and server-grade CPU applications.

  The series comprises two models with differing thermal performance. The AIC-1010, finished in purple, delivers 5.5 kilowatts (kW) of cooling capacity with a thermal resistance — a measure of how effectively heat moves from chip to coolant, where lower is better — of 0.007 degrees Celsius per watt (°C/W) at a coolant flow rate of 6 liters per minute (LPM). The AIC-1011, finished in blue, raises capacity to 6.5 kW and lowers thermal resistance to 0.006°C/W at the same 6 LPM flow rate.

  Both models share identical physical dimensions of 118 mm long by 94.4 mm wide by 30.85 mm high and are precision-machined from aluminum. Mounting holes measure 5.5 mm and are matched to the SP5 socket layout; mounting hardware is included. The plates use universal barb fittings compatible with tubing measuring 8.0 mm outer diameter and 5.0 mm inner diameter.

  ## Design and validation

  ATS said the NIVIS cold plates are designed for direct-contact cooling — meaning the plate sits in physical contact with the processor lid and transfers heat into a circulating liquid coolant loop rather than into moving air. The company said all plates were modeled using computational fluid dynamics (CFD), a simulation method used to predict fluid flow and heat transfer behavior, then validated in laboratory testing.

  The plates are sized for compact 1U and 2U server chassis. A 1U chassis is 1.75 inches tall in a standard equipment rack; 2U is 3.5 inches tall. These are among the densest server form factors in common use.

  ## Why liquid cooling for SP5

  ATS attributed the product's development to the increasing power density of AI processors, CPUs, and accelerators, which the company said makes air cooling progressively more difficult to implement in dense server and HPC environments. Direct liquid cooling cold plates, it said, reduce thermal resistance and support higher-density cooling architectures by moving heat off the chip and into a coolant loop rather than relying on airflow through a chassis.

  The AMD SP5 socket is used in server-class processors targeting AI inference, training, and HPC workloads — application categories where sustained high thermal output is a central design constraint.

  ATS said the NIVIS series is available now, with further information accessible through the company's product and inquiry channels.
body_zh: |
  Advanced Thermal Solutions, Inc.（ATS）推出ArctiQ NIVIS系列液冷冷板，專為採用AMD Socket Platform 5（SP5）的處理器設計，適用於人工智慧（AI）、高效能運算（HPC）及伺服器等級CPU等應用場景。

  該系列共有兩款型號，散熱效能各有差異。AIC-1010採紫色外觀，在冷卻液流量每分鐘6公升（LPM）的條件下，散熱能力達5.5千瓦（kW），熱阻——衡量熱量從晶片傳導至冷卻液效率的指標，數值愈低愈佳——為每瓦0.007攝氏度（°C/W）。AIC-1011則採藍色外觀，同樣在6 LPM流量下，散熱能力提升至6.5 kW，熱阻進一步降至0.006°C/W。

  兩款型號的外觀尺寸完全相同，長118公釐、寬94.4公釐、高30.85公釐，均由鋁料精密加工製成。安裝孔徑為5.5公釐，與SP5插槽佈局吻合，並隨附安裝硬體。冷板採用通用倒鉤接頭（barb fitting），相容外徑8.0公釐、內徑5.0公釐規格的水管。

  ## 設計與驗證

  ATS表示，NIVIS冷板採直接接觸式冷卻設計，即冷板與處理器頂蓋直接貼合，將熱量引入循環冷卻液迴路，而非依賴流動空氣散熱。該公司指出，所有冷板均已透過計算流體動力學（CFD）進行模擬——此為一種用以預測流體流動與熱傳行為的模擬技術——並在實驗室測試中完成實際驗證。

  冷板尺寸專為1U與2U伺服器機箱設計。在標準設備機架中，1U機箱高1.75英寸，2U機箱高3.5英寸，同屬目前業界最為緊湊的常見伺服器規格之列。

  ## SP5為何需要液冷

  ATS指出，本系列產品的開發背景，在於AI處理器、CPU及加速器的功率密度持續攀升，使氣冷方案在高密度伺服器與HPC環境中愈來愈難以有效實施。ATS表示，直接液冷冷板能有效降低熱阻，透過將晶片熱量導入冷卻液迴路、而非倚賴機箱氣流，進而支援更高密度的散熱架構。

  AMD SP5插槽廣泛應用於針對AI推論、模型訓練及HPC工作負載的伺服器級處理器，而如何長時間管理持續輸出的高熱量，正是這些應用場景的核心設計挑戰之一。

  ATS表示，NIVIS系列現已正式上市，詳細資訊可透過該公司的產品頁面及洽詢管道取得。
