body_en: |
  Modine has commenced commercial production of Airedale chillers at a purpose-built 682,000-square-foot (63,360-square-meter) facility in Grand Prairie, Texas, marking what the company said is a key step in its plan to expand U.S. manufacturing capacity for data center cooling equipment.

  The plant is dedicated to producing 2-megawatt and 3-megawatt chillers with integrated free cooling — a configuration that uses outside air to assist or replace mechanical refrigeration when ambient temperatures allow, reducing energy consumption — for the data center market.

  The facility is located within Grand Prairie's Wildlife Commerce Park, between Dallas and Fort Worth. The company said it expects the site to become the largest Airedale chiller manufacturing facility in its global footprint.

  ## Production ramp

  The first production line began operations this month, the company said. Modine is targeting four operational 2MW production lines by the end of its current fiscal year, subject to equipment commissioning, workforce readiness, and other operational considerations.

  The plant currently runs one shift and is planned to scale to a three-shift, fully operational model.

  ## Executive comment

  "The launch of our Grand Prairie facility is an important milestone in the plan we announced last year to expand US manufacturing capacity for Airedale cooling technologies," said Neil D. Brinker, Modine's president and chief executive officer.

  ## Significance

  The opening adds domestic U.S. production capacity for high-power data center chillers at a time when demand for cooling infrastructure has been rising alongside growth in hyperscale and AI-oriented data center construction. By targeting four production lines by fiscal year-end, Modine is positioning the Texas plant as the primary global manufacturing base for the Airedale chiller product line.

  The company's next stated milestone is completing commissioning of the remaining three 2MW production lines and transitioning the facility to three-shift operations.

  Modine已在德克薩斯州大草原市（Grand Prairie）一座專為此目的而建、占地682,000平方英尺（約63,360平方公尺）的廠房展開Airedale冷水機的商業量產。公司表示，這是其擴大美國資料中心冷卻設備製造產能計畫中的關鍵一步。

  該廠專門生產2百萬瓦（MW）及3百萬瓦等級、整合自由冷卻功能的冷水機，供應資料中心市場。所謂「自由冷卻」，是指在環境溫度許可的情況下，利用外部空氣輔助或取代機械製冷，以降低能源消耗的配置方式。

  廠區坐落於大草原市野生動物商業園區（Wildlife Commerce Park）內，位於達拉斯（Dallas）與沃斯堡（Fort Worth）之間。公司表示，預計該廠址將成為其全球版圖中規模最大的Airedale冷水機製造基地。

  ## 產能爬坡

  公司表示，第一條生產線本月已正式啟動運作。Modine的目標是在本財年結束前，讓四條2MW生產線全數投入運作，惟仍需視設備調試、人力整備及其他營運因素而定。

  目前廠區採單班制運作，計畫逐步擴展至三班全產能運作模式。

  ## 高層評論

  Modine總裁兼執行長Neil D. Brinker表示：「大草原廠的啟用，是我們去年宣布的美國Airedale冷卻技術製造產能擴張計畫中的重要里程碑。」

  ## 市場意義

  德州廠的開幕，在超大規模與人工智慧（AI）導向資料中心建設帶動冷卻基礎設施需求持續攀升之際，為高功率資料中心冷水機增添了美國本土的生產能量。藉由設定在本財年底前完成四條生產線的目標，Modine正將德州廠定位為Airedale冷水機產品線的全球主要製造基地。

  公司宣示的下一個里程碑，是完成其餘三條2MW生產線的調試作業，並推動廠區正式轉型為三班制運作。
