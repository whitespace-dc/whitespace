body_en: |
  Panasonic has supplied six 80kW ECOi-W Aqua-G Blue R290 chillers to Leeds Magistrates Court as part of a major cooling infrastructure upgrade, with the installation completed by Crawley-based mechanical services specialist Sub-Cool FM while the court remained fully operational throughout.

  The six units are Panasonic's ECOi-W Aqua-G Blue model, each rated at 80kW of cooling capacity. R290, also known as propane, is a natural refrigerant with a global warming potential (GWP) of 3, significantly lower than the hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants it displaces in many commercial cooling applications.

  ## Logistics and installation

  The city-centre location presented significant logistical constraints. The live-site installation required overnight crane operations, partial road closures, pedestrian management measures, and enhanced public safety controls, according to the project account. Despite those constraints, the crane lift was completed ahead of schedule, with the planned 11-hour operation finishing in six hours.

  ## Building management system

  The project also included enhancements to the building management system (BMS), the software and controls infrastructure that governs a building's mechanical and electrical services. Improved scheduling functionality was added, enabling the heating and cooling systems to run only when required rather than on fixed timetables.

  ## Significance

  The installation illustrates the application of low-GWP natural refrigerant technology in public-sector buildings subject to operational continuity requirements. The combination of R290 equipment with BMS scheduling improvements reflects a broader pattern in commercial building upgrades, where refrigerant transition and energy management controls are addressed together. The completion of a complex live-site crane lift in roughly half the planned time demonstrates that city-centre infrastructure replacements can be executed without disrupting the facilities they serve.

  ## 施工物流與安裝作業

  市中心的地理位置帶來了相當嚴峻的物流挑戰。根據專案報告，這項在運作中場址進行的安裝工程，需要安排夜間吊車作業、局部封閉道路、行人管制措施，以及加強公共安全管控。儘管面臨上述種種限制，吊車吊裝作業仍提前完工——原定十一小時的作業，最終僅花費六小時便順利結束。

  ## 樓宇管理系統

  此次工程亦包含對樓宇管理系統（BMS）的強化升級。BMS是負責統籌管理建築物機電設備的軟體與控制基礎架構。工程新增了更完善的排程功能，使冷暖氣系統得以依實際需求運行，而非按照固定時間表持續運轉，從而提升整體能源使用效率。

  ## 意義

  這項工程充分展示了低GWP天然冷媒技術在公部門建築中的實際應用潛力，尤其是在這類對業務連續性有嚴格要求的場所。R290設備與BMS排程功能的結合，反映出商業建築升級的一項更廣泛趨勢——冷媒轉型與能源管理控制往往同步推進、一併解決。而此次複雜的市中心在運作場址吊裝作業，以約莫原定時間一半的效率圓滿完成，也充分證明了城市核心區的基礎設施汰換工程，完全可以在不影響設施正常運作的前提下順利執行。
