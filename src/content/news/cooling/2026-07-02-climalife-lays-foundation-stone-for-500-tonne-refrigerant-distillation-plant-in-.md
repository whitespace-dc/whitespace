body_en: |
  Climalife has laid the foundation stone for a new refrigerant distillation plant at the Osiris industrial park in Péage-de-Roussillon, Isère, in southern France, marking a physical shift in the company's refrigerant regeneration operations and a significant expansion of its recovery capacity.

  The facility will be equipped with three latest-generation distillation columns and will have a processing capacity of more than 500 tonnes of refrigerant per year, according to the company. Climalife describes the site as a circular economy centre of excellence — meaning it is oriented toward recovering and reusing materials rather than disposing of them.

  ## What the plant will do

  The plant's core function is the treatment and purification of complex multi-component refrigerant blends. Modern refrigerants are often formulated as mixtures of multiple chemical components, and separating those components for reuse requires more sophisticated processing than handling single-substance refrigerants. Climalife says the new facility is designed specifically to handle that complexity and to maximise recovery efficiency.

  Using what the company describes as new patented technology, Climalife asserts that nearly 80% of the refrigerant waste currently destined for incineration can be recovered at the new plant. Incineration is a common end-of-life route for refrigerant blends that cannot be economically separated or purified with existing equipment; recovering that material instead reduces both waste and the demand for newly manufactured refrigerant.

  ## Relocation from Bry-sur-Marne

  Climalife's refrigerant regeneration business was previously based at Bry-sur-Marne, a commune in the eastern suburbs of Paris. The new Péage-de-Roussillon facility represents a relocation of that operation to the Osiris industrial park in the Isère department of the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region.

  ## Industry significance

  The plant's capacity and claimed recovery rate position it as a notable addition to Europe's refrigerant reclamation infrastructure at a time when regulatory pressure to reduce the use and disposal of fluorinated gases is intensifying. By diverting material from incineration and returning it to productive use, the facility is designed to support what the company calls circular use of refrigerants — a model in which recovered material re-enters the supply chain rather than being destroyed.

  ## Climalife在法國南部奠基500噸冷媒蒸餾廠

  Climalife已在法國南部伊澤爾省（Isère）Péage-de-Roussillon的Osiris工業園區，為一座新冷媒蒸餾廠舉行奠基典禮，標誌著該公司冷媒再生業務的實質性轉移，並大幅擴展其回收處理能量。

  據該公司說明，這座設施將配備三座最新一代蒸餾塔，年處理量逾500噸冷媒。Climalife將此廠址定位為循環經濟卓越中心，意即以回收再利用材料為核心導向，而非訴諸廢棄處置。

  ## 工廠的運作內容

  這座工廠的核心功能，在於處理和純化複雜的多成分冷媒混合物。現代冷媒通常由多種化學成分調配而成，要將這些成分分離以供再利用，所需的加工技術遠比單一冷媒更為複雜精細。Climalife表示，新設施正是專為應對這種複雜性而設計，目標是將回收效率最大化。

  借助該公司所描述的全新專利技術，Climalife宣稱，目前被送往焚化的冷媒廢料，有近80%可在新廠加以回收。焚化是冷媒混合物在現有設備無法經濟地分離或純化時，常見的廢棄處理途徑；改以回收代替焚化，不僅能減少廢棄物，也能降低對新製冷媒的需求。

  ## 從Bry-sur-Marne遷址

  Climalife的冷媒再生業務，此前設於巴黎東郊市鎮Bry-sur-Marne。新的Péage-de-Roussillon設施，代表該業務遷移至奧弗涅-隆河-阿爾卑斯大區（Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes）伊澤爾省的Osiris工業園區。

  ## 產業意義

  在監管機構加緊施壓、要求減少含氟氣體使用與廢棄之際，這座工廠的處理量及其所宣稱的回收率，使其成為歐洲冷媒回收基礎設施中一項值得關注的重要補充。藉由將冷媒材料從焚化線轉入再利用，這座設施的設計目的在於支持該公司所倡議的冷媒「循環使用」模式——即回收材料重新進入供應鏈，而非遭到銷毀。
