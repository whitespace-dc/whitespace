  ## Hotong Group Positions as Full-Stack AIDC Integrator with Liquid Cooling and Modular Data Centers

  Taiwan's Hotong Group is moving beyond liquid cooling components to offer end-to-end AI data center (AIDC) integration, bundling coolant distribution hardware, loop engineering, and prefabricated modular facility construction under a single vendor relationship, the company said. The pivot comes as high-density AI computing drives sustained growth in data center cooling demand, elevating liquid cooling from a point-solution into a core element of whole-facility planning.

  ## Strategic Agreement in Thailand

  Hotong Group chairman Chang Wei-cheng signed a strategic cooperation agreement with Mr. Surapon, project lead of Thailand's STP AI Edge Project, according to the company. The signing marks the company's regional expansion into Southeast Asia, where it said it is establishing sales and loop-manufacturing bases alongside existing Northeast Asia operations to support local project delivery and engineering.

  ## Liquid Cooling Product and Integration Scope

  On the hardware side, Hotong supplies fittings, seals, manifolds, and CDUs — coolant distribution units, the rack-level devices that circulate and regulate liquid coolant flow to servers. The company said it integrates products sourced from international brands with its own engineering resources, then extends the offering into loop design, on-site construction, and system integration.

  The company frames the value of that bundled scope in coordination terms: when fittings, component interfaces, and field installation are managed within a single planning framework from the start, it said, subsequent adjustments are reduced and deployment efficiency improves.

  ## Cold-Plate Reliability and Cleanliness Controls

  Hotong identified cleanliness and sealing as critical reliability factors specific to liquid cooling equipment. Cold plates — the heat exchangers that attach directly to processor chips — contain internal micro-channels with very small bore diameters; particulate contamination can block flow and impair system operation, the company said. To address this, Hotong applies pipe-cleaning procedures, precision filtration, and controlled welding techniques to reduce contamination and leakage risk.

  ## Prefabricated Modular Data Centers

  Beyond cooling, Hotong is developing PMDC — prefabricated modular data center — deployments that bring cooling, power, and facility construction into a single coordinated plan. The company said the modular architecture allows deployment scale and construction pace to be adjusted to project requirements, accommodating variables such as power supply constraints, site conditions, and shifting compute workloads, while preserving capacity for future equipment expansion.

  ## Regional Buildout and Strategic Direction

  Hotong said its Southeast and Northeast Asia manufacturing and sales network is intended to meet local construction requirements across different markets, providing regional supply chain support and project engineering assistance.

  The company described its strategic direction as developing into an AIDC system integrator — connecting liquid cooling, power, and engineering resources so customers can advance AI compute deployments through more fully coordinated planning. The Thailand STP AI Edge agreement represents the company's first publicly disclosed international project-level commitment under that positioning.

  ## 禾統集團定位全棧式AIDC整合商，整合液冷與模組化資料中心

  台灣禾統集團宣布，將業務範疇從液冷元件供應商擴展至端對端AI資料中心（AIDC）整合服務，把冷卻液配送硬體、迴路工程以及預製模組化機房建置，全數納入單一廠商服務框架之內。該公司表示，此一策略轉型的背景，在於高密度AI運算持續帶動資料中心散熱需求成長，促使液冷技術從單點解決方案，躍升為整體機房規劃的核心要素。

  ## 泰國策略合作協議

  據禾統集團表示，董事長張威成與泰國STP AI Edge專案負責人Surapon先生完成策略合作協議簽署。此次簽約標誌著禾統正式向東南亞展開區域性擴張，該公司表示，將在東南亞建立業務銷售與迴路製造基地，與既有的東北亞營運據點相輔相成，以支援當地專案交付與工程服務。

  ## 液冷產品範疇與整合服務

  在硬體端，禾統供應接頭、密封件、集管以及CDU（冷卻液配送單元）——CDU為機架層級裝置，負責循環並調節流向伺服器的液態冷卻劑。該公司表示，其將來自國際品牌的產品與自身工程資源整合，進一步延伸至迴路設計、現場施工與系統整合服務。

  禾統以協調效益來詮釋此種整合服務的價值：當接頭、元件介面與現場安裝從專案初期便納入統一規劃框架，後續調整需求得以減少，部署效率也隨之提升。

  ## 冷板可靠性與潔淨管控

  禾統指出，潔淨度與密封性是液冷設備特有的關鍵可靠性因素。冷板——直接貼附於處理器晶片的熱交換器——內部含有孔徑極小的微流道；禾統表示，微粒污染可能堵塞流道、影響系統正常運作。為此，禾統採用管路清潔程序、精密過濾以及受控焊接工法，以降低污染與洩漏風險。

  ## 預製模組化資料中心

  除散熱業務外，禾統也積極發展PMDC（預製模組化資料中心）部署方案，將冷卻、電力與機房建設整合進單一協調計畫之中。該公司表示，模組化架構可依據專案需求彈性調整部署規模與建置進度，能夠因應電力供應限制、場址條件以及運算工作負載變動等多項變數，同時保留未來設備擴充的空間。

  ## 區域布局與策略方向

  禾統表示，在東南亞與東北亞建立製造與銷售網絡，目的在於因應不同市場的在地化建置需求，提供區域性供應鏈支援及專案工程協助。

  該公司將自身策略方向定位為發展成AIDC系統整合商——整合液冷、電力與工程資源，讓客戶得以透過更完整的協調規劃，推進AI運算部署。泰國STP AI Edge合作協議，是禾統以此定位公開揭露的首項國際專案層級承諾。
