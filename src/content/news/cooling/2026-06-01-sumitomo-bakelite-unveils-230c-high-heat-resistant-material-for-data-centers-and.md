# Sumitomo Bakelite Unveils 230°C High Heat-Resistant Material for Data Centers and EVs

Sumitomo Bakelite has introduced a 230°C high heat-resistant material to address thermal challenges in AI data centers, 800V electric vehicle platforms, and HVDC power distribution systems. As electronic equipment power density continues to increase, heat management has become a critical bottleneck in these advanced applications. The new material is designed to overcome existing packaging temperature limitations.

## 中文

# 住友電木推出 230°C 高耐熱材料突破資料中心溫度瓶頸

住友電木推出 230°C 高耐熱材料，用於解決 AI 資料中心、800V 電動車平台及高壓直流配電（HVDC）系統中日益嚴峻的散熱挑戰。隨著電子設備功率密度持續提升，散熱管理已成為先進應用的關鍵瓶頸。此新材料旨在突破現有封裝溫度限制。
