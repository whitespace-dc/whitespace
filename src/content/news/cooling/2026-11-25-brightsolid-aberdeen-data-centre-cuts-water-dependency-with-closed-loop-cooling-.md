body_en: |
  ## Closed-loop design eliminates mains water feed

  Brightsolid's data centre in Aberdeen, Scotland, operates with no ongoing mains water consumption by using a closed-loop chilled-water system in which water and glycol are permanently sealed inside the facility's pipework, the company has said. The approach, developed in partnership with data centre lifecycle services provider Salute, is being cited as a template for reducing the water dependency that has become a flashpoint in the industry's sustainability debate as AI-driven compute demand pushes operators to build larger, denser facilities.

  The Aberdeen site is a Tier III facility — a classification indicating redundant components and no single point of failure — serving customers including Aberdeenshire Council, Dundee City Council, the University of Dundee, and West Yorkshire Combined Authority.

  ## How the system works

  Conventional data centre cooling often relies on open-loop or adiabatic systems, in which water is drawn from mains supply and evaporated or discharged to remove heat. Brightsolid's Aberdeen design avoids both approaches. Because the glycol-and-water mixture is sealed, nothing is consumed or discharged during normal operation.

  The facility uses free-cooling chillers — equipment that rejects heat to the outside air rather than through a refrigeration cycle — and operates at higher chilled-water temperatures than is typical, maximising the number of hours per year when the Scottish climate alone can dissipate heat without mechanical refrigeration. Brightsolid said the design delivers a power usage effectiveness (PUE) below 1.2. PUE is the ratio of total facility power to IT load; a figure of 1.0 would mean no overhead at all, and anything below 1.5 is generally considered efficient.

  ## Rack density and AI headroom

  The facility is divided into two halls with different density envelopes. Hall 1 supports up to 30 kilowatts per rack position. Hall 2 is rated to 75 kW per rack, with the ability to accommodate direct liquid cooling — a technique that runs coolant directly to processors rather than through the room air — at 135 kW per application position. Those figures are relevant because AI training servers, particularly those using high-end graphics processing units (GPUs), routinely exceed 20 kW per rack and increasingly require liquid cooling to operate at full capacity.

  Brightsolid said the closed-loop free-cooling architecture allows the site to serve those high-density workloads without adding supplementary water-based cooling infrastructure, avoiding the retrofits that operators who built for lower densities are now undertaking.

  ## Operational model and long-term support

  Salute's involvement began during the design and construction phase and has continued into ongoing operations. The company provides 24/7 monitoring, maintenance, and incident response under a four-hour call-out framework, drawing on a mix of in-house staff and subcontracted specialists covering transformers, power distribution units (PDUs), cooling, and fire suppression.

  Brightsolid said the longevity of the relationship with Salute was a material factor in the facility's operational performance over time.

  ## Why operators avoid adiabatic cooling

  Adiabatic cooling systems — which use water evaporation to pre-cool incoming air — consume substantial volumes of water and require active management of Legionella bacteria risk, standing-water maintenance, and chemical water treatment. Brightsolid said eliminating that equipment removes those operational and health-and-safety overheads, as well as the reputational exposure linked to water-intensive systems. It also reduces vulnerability to water-use regulation, which is tightening in multiple jurisdictions as authorities respond to drought and scarcity pressure.

  ## Trade-offs and capital cost

  Brightsolid and Salute acknowledged that closed-loop and free-cooling architectures carry higher upfront capital costs and require more complex design work than conventional systems, and that operators in warmer climates or with different site constraints will face different economics. They argued the higher initial outlay should be read as a lifecycle investment: water-dependent designs carry ongoing costs for supply, treatment, and standing-water management, face growing regulatory risk, and are likely to require expensive retrofits as AI rack densities rise above what the original build assumed.

  ## Industry significance

  The Aberdeen facility represents a documented example of a commercial multi-tenant data centre that has removed mains water consumption from its operational model while maintaining sub-1.2 PUE and density headroom sufficient for current AI workloads. As hyperscalers and colocation operators face scrutiny over water use from regulators, local governments, and corporate sustainability commitments, the closed-loop free-cooling model is likely to attract closer attention from facility planners weighing long-term operating risk against initial construction costs.

  Hall 2's 75 kW rack rating and 135 kW direct liquid cooling capability represent the facility's forward-looking capacity ceiling under its current design.

  ## 密閉迴路設計消除自來水需求

  Brightsolid位於蘇格蘭亞伯丁（Aberdeen）的資料中心，透過密閉迴路冷卻水系統實現零自來水消耗——水與乙二醇混合液永久封存於廠區管路之中，完全不對外排放或補充。該公司表示，這套方案由其與資料中心全生命週期服務供應商Salute共同研發，現正被業界視為降低用水依賴的參考範本。隨著AI算力需求推動業者興建規模更大、密度更高的設施，用水問題已成為資料中心永續爭論中高度敏感的焦點。

  亞伯丁站點屬於Tier III等級設施——此等級代表具備備援元件、不存在單點故障——服務客戶涵蓋亞伯丁郡議會（Aberdeenshire Council）、鄧迪市議會（Dundee City Council）、鄧迪大學（University of Dundee），以及西約克郡聯合管理局（West Yorkshire Combined Authority）。

  ## 系統運作原理

  傳統資料中心冷卻方式多採用開放迴路或絕熱系統，需從自來水管線汲水，透過蒸發或排放帶走熱量。Brightsolid亞伯丁廠區的設計則完全迴避上述兩種做法。由於乙二醇與水的混合液密封於管路內，正常運作期間不消耗、不排放任何液體。

  該設施使用自由冷卻冷水機（free-cooling chiller）——這類設備直接將熱量散逸至室外空氣，而非依賴冷凍循環——並以高於一般水準的冷卻水溫度運作，盡可能延長蘇格蘭氣候本身即可自然散熱、無需機械制冷的年度運作時數。Brightsolid表示，此設計可將電力使用效率（PUE）維持在1.2以下。PUE為設施總用電量與IT負載的比值，1.0代表完全無額外耗損，低於1.5則普遍被認為達到高效水準。

  ## 機架密度與AI運算空間

  設施分為兩個機房，各有不同的密度規格。第一機房（Hall 1）每個機架位置最高支援30千瓦（kW）。第二機房（Hall 2）每個機架額定最高75千瓦，並可支援直接液冷技術——將冷卻液直接導引至處理器，而非透過室內空氣散熱——每個應用位置最高可達135千瓦。這些數字至關重要，因為AI訓練伺服器——尤其是搭載高階繪圖處理器（GPU）的機型——每機架用電量經常超過20千瓦，且日益需要液冷才能以完整效能運作。

  Brightsolid表示，密閉迴路自由冷卻架構讓這個站點得以在不額外部署水基冷卻設施的情況下，支撐上述高密度工作負載，也因此避免了許多當初為低密度而設計、如今不得不進行升級改造的業者所面臨的困境。

  ## 營運模式與長期支援

  Salute從設計與施工階段便深度參與，並持續投入至日常營運。該公司在四小時到場機制下提供全天候監控、維護與事故應變服務，結合內部員工與外包專業人員，負責範疇涵蓋變壓器、電源分配單元（PDU）、冷卻系統及消防抑制系統。

  Brightsolid表示，與Salute長期合作關係的穩定延續，是設施長期營運表現的重要因素。

  ## 為何業者避免絕熱冷卻

  絕熱冷卻系統（adiabatic cooling）——利用水蒸發預冷進入空氣——耗水量龐大，且需主動管理退伍軍人症桿菌（Legionella）風險、積水維護與化學水處理等作業。Brightsolid表示，捨棄這類設備可同步消除上述營運與健康安全負擔，以及與高耗水系統相關的聲譽風險，同時也降低了各地當局為回應旱情與水資源短缺而持續收緊的用水法規所帶來的合規壓力。

  ## 取捨與資本支出

  Brightsolid與Salute坦承，密閉迴路與自由冷卻架構的前期資本支出高於傳統系統，設計工作也更為複雜；氣候較溫暖或廠址條件不同的業者所面對的經濟考量亦有所差異。但兩家公司主張，較高的初始投入應視為生命週期投資：依賴自來水的設計需持續承擔供水、水處理與積水管理的營運成本，面臨日益增加的法規風險，且當AI機架密度超出原始設計假設時，很可能需要進行代價高昂的升級改造。

  ## 產業意義

  亞伯丁站點代表一個有據可查的商業多租戶資料中心實例，在維持PUE低於1.2、並具備足以支撐當前AI工作負載之密度空間的前提下，徹底移除了自來水消耗。隨著超大型業者（hyperscaler）與主機代管業者在用水問題上受到監管機關、地方政府及企業永續承諾的日益檢視，密閉迴路自由冷卻模式預料將受到設施規劃者更多關注——他們正在衡量長期營運風險與初期建設成本之間的取捨。

  第二機房75千瓦的機架額定值與每個應用位置135千瓦的直接液冷能力，代表該設施在現行設計下的前瞻性容量上限。
