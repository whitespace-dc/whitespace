# LianDe Holdings to Mass Produce Liquid-Cooling Floating Fittings for US Chipmaker AI Servers

LianDe Holdings has entered the AI server liquid-cooling components market and secured a supply contract with a major US chipmaker. The company developed a liquid-cooling floating fitting designed to work with quick disconnects between coolant lines and servers. Mass production is slated to begin in the second half of 2026.

## 中文

# 聯德控股進軍美國晶片商液冷供應鏈

聯德控股已進軍AI伺服器液冷組件市場，並獲得美國主要晶片商的供應合約。公司開發的液冷浮動接頭，用於冷卻液管線與伺服器之間的快速接頭連接。量產預計於2026年下半年啟動。
