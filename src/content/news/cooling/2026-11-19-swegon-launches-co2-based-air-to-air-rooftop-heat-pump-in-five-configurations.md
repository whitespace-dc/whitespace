body_en: |
  Swegon has launched the Lambda Zero, a self-contained air-to-air rooftop heat pump built around carbon dioxide (CO2) as its refrigerant, the company announced. The product is available in five configurations spanning cooling and heating capacities from 20 kilowatts to 70 kilowatts and nominal air flow rates from 4,000 cubic metres per hour to 11,000 cubic metres per hour.

  ## Product design and technical approach

  Swegon said the Lambda Zero was developed through what it described as a completely new approach to rooftop heat pump technology. CO2, also designated R-744, operates at significantly higher pressures than conventional synthetic refrigerants, which presents engineering challenges for components, seals, and system controls. To address those challenges, the company said it re-engineered the refrigerant circuit and incorporated advanced pressure controls supported by artificial intelligence.

  Components were selected and validated through what Swegon described as extensive prototype development and testing activities. The company said the development involved multidisciplinary teams across engineering, product development, and testing functions, with multiple design iterations and validation phases before the product reached its current form.

  ## Configuration and capacity range

  The Lambda Zero is designed as a compact, self-contained unit — meaning refrigeration, heat exchange, and air-handling components are housed within a single rooftop-mounted enclosure rather than split across indoor and outdoor sections. The five available configurations cover:

  - Capacity range: 20kW to 70kW
  - Nominal air flow range: 4,000 m³/h to 11,000 m³/h

  ## Industry significance

  The use of CO2 as a refrigerant in commercial rooftop heat pumps reflects broader movement in the heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) industry away from hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants, which carry high global warming potential (GWP) ratings. CO2 has a GWP of 1, the baseline against which other refrigerants are measured. Its adoption in rooftop applications has historically lagged behind refrigeration and heat pump water heaters due to the pressure management complexity Swegon said it aimed to resolve with the Lambda Zero.

  Swegon said the launch marks the completion of a development journey, indicating the product is now commercially available rather than at a prototype or preview stage.

  ## 產品設計與技術方案

  Swegon表示，Lambda Zero的研發採用了該公司所稱的全新屋頂熱泵技術開發思路。CO2（又稱R-744）的工作壓力遠高於傳統合成冷媒，這對零組件、密封件及系統控制帶來相當的工程挑戰。為因應這些挑戰，該公司表示已重新設計冷媒迴路，並導入由人工智慧輔助的先進壓力控制系統。

  各項零組件均歷經Swegon所描述的大量原型機開發與測試驗證流程。該公司表示，開發過程涵蓋工程、產品開發與測試等多個跨領域團隊，並歷經多輪設計迭代與驗證階段，產品才達到目前的完成形態。

  ## 機型與容量範圍

  Lambda Zero設計為緊湊的一體式機組，意即冷凍、熱交換及空氣處理等元件均整合於單一屋頂安裝外殼之內，無需分離成室內機與室外機兩個部分。五款可供選擇的機型涵蓋：

  - 容量範圍：20千瓦至70千瓦
  - 額定風量範圍：每小時4,000至11,000立方公尺

  ## 產業意義

  在商用屋頂熱泵中採用CO2作為冷媒，反映出暖通空調（HVAC）產業正逐步遠離氫氟碳化物（HFC）冷媒的廣泛趨勢——HFC冷媒具有相當高的全球暖化潛勢（GWP）。CO2的GWP為1，是衡量其他冷媒的基準值。由於壓力管理技術較為複雜，CO2在屋頂空調應用的普及速度歷來落後於冷凍設備及熱泵熱水器，而Swegon表示Lambda Zero正是為了突破這一瓶頸而生。

  Swegon指出，此次發表標誌著一段開發歷程的完成，意味著該產品現已正式上市，而非仍處於原型機或預覽展示階段。
