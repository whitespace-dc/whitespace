body_en: |
  Chemours has confirmed that the court injunctions it served on four exhibitors at last week's Chillventa trade show in Germany concerned patents covering blends that contain the refrigerant R1234yf (2,3,3,3-tetrafluoropropene, a hydrofluoroolefin, or HFO, used as a lower-global-warming-potential alternative to older fluorocarbon refrigerants).

  A Chemours spokesperson told the Cooling Post that the affected patents relate specifically to R1234yf-containing blends. The company holds patents on five such blends: R454B, R452A, R454A, R454C, and R513A.

  ## Second manufacturer also acted at the show

  A separate refrigerant manufacturer, Solstice, announced that it had also issued injunctions at Chillventa — five in total. Solstice did not name the specific refrigerants involved, stating only that its patents covered the manufacturing process of a certain HFO and the composition of a certain proprietary HFO blend.

  ## Executive statement on intellectual property

  Joseph Martinko, Chemours' president of thermal and specialised solutions, framed the enforcement actions as necessary to sustain investment in refrigerant development. "Innovation does not happen by accident; it is built on years of research, development, and investment," Martinko said. "Protecting intellectual property is essential to preserving incentives for innovation, advancing refrigerant technology, and supporting fair competition."

  ## Background: the Chemours-Solstice relationship

  Chemours and Solstice have a history of joint development, patent cross-licensing, and shared market protection for next-generation HFO refrigerants, according to the Cooling Post.

  The enforcement activity follows a recent dispute in the adjacent R1234ze(E) segment. In August, Chemours announced the commercial launch of R1234ze(E) and a related blend, R515B, under its Opteon brand. Shortly afterward, Solstice issued a public statement saying it was aware of "a recent public announcement from another chemical company regarding the planned introduction of hydrofluoroolefin (HFO) 1234ze and 1234ze-containing blends," and warned that key elements of its own patent portfolio were relevant to processes for manufacturing HFO-1234ze. Solstice also stated it had not licensed any manufacturing-related intellectual property to others in that segment.

  ## Industry significance

  The injunctions signal that patent enforcement in the HFO refrigerant market is intensifying as adoption of lower-global-warming-potential blends accelerates. Manufacturers, equipment makers, and distributors exhibiting or sourcing refrigerant blends that include R1234yf now face legal exposure if their products or processes overlap with patents held by Chemours or Solstice. The Solstice warning over R1234ze(E) manufacturing IP suggests a parallel enforcement front may be developing in that refrigerant category as well.

  ## 另一製造商亦在展會採取行動

  另一家冷媒製造商Solstice宣布，其亦在Chillventa展會上發出禁制令，共計五份。Solstice並未具體說明涉及哪些冷媒，僅表示其專利涵蓋某種HFO的製造工藝，以及某種專有HFO混合配方的成分組成。

  ## 高層就智慧財產權發表聲明

  科慕熱能與特殊解決方案事業群總裁Joseph Martinko將此次執法行動定性為維持冷媒研發投資的必要之舉。「創新並非偶然，而是建立在多年研究、開發與投資的基礎之上，」Martinko表示，「保護智慧財產權，對於維護創新誘因、推動冷媒技術進步，以及支持公平競爭，都至關重要。」

  ## 背景：科慕與Solstice的關係

  據《Cooling Post》報導，科慕與Solstice在次世代HFO冷媒領域有著共同開發、專利交互授權及共同市場保護的合作歷史。

  此次執法行動發生於相鄰的R1234ze(E)市場近期出現爭議之後。今年八月，科慕宣布以旗下Opteon品牌正式商業化推出R1234ze(E)及相關混合冷媒R515B。此後不久，Solstice隨即發表公開聲明，表示已注意到「另一家化學公司近期關於計畫引進HFO 1234ze及含1234ze混合配方的公開聲明」，並警告其自身專利組合中的關鍵內容與HFO-1234ze的製造工藝高度相關。Solstice亦聲明，其從未就該領域的製造相關智慧財產權授權予其他業者。

  ## 產業意義

  此次禁制令事件顯示，隨著低全球暖化潛勢混合冷媒的採用持續加速，HFO冷媒市場的專利執法正日趨激烈。凡在展會上展示或採購含R1234yf混合冷媒的製造商、設備商及經銷商，若其產品或製程與科慕或Solstice持有的專利有所重疊，現已面臨法律風險。Solstice就R1234ze(E)製造智慧財產權發出的警告，更顯示該冷媒類別或將形成另一條並行的執法戰線。
