body_en: |
  Liquid cooling will carry most of the new computing capacity added to global data centers through 2030, when annual incremental IT load additions are projected to reach approximately 70 gigawatts (GW), according to a thermal management tracker report from research firm Omdia. The firm forecasts liquid cooling will become the dominant thermal management solution for next-generation AI infrastructure.

  Within liquid cooling, direct-to-chip single-phase (DtC/1P) technology — which circulates coolant through cold plates mounted directly on processor dies rather than relying on airflow across conventional heat sinks — will maintain market leadership throughout the forecast period, Omdia said. Other liquid cooling architectures, including immersion cooling, are also expected to grow, but Omdia said cold plate-based DtC/1P systems offer the most practical solution for retrofitting existing server racks and for meeting the ultra-high power densities required by modern graphics processing unit (GPU) clusters.

  ## Cold plates to capture a growing share of the value chain

  Cold plates are set to become the dominant revenue segment within the DtC/1P liquid cooling value chain. Omdia projects cold plate revenue share will rise from 40 percent of the DtC/1P market in 2024 to 61 percent by 2030.

  Omdia identifies three drivers behind this shift. First, cold plate adoption is expanding beyond central processing units (CPUs) and GPUs to cover memory modules, power delivery components, and other high-heat hardware, widening the total addressable market for the technology. Second, manufacturers are deploying advanced, high-conductivity materials in cold plate construction, increasing raw material costs and pushing up unit prices. Third, continuous engineering refinement of internal microchannel structures — the fine internal passages through which coolant flows inside a cold plate — is improving thermal performance and enabling premium pricing for optimized designs.

  ## CDU revenue share to compress despite volume growth

  Coolant distribution units (CDUs) — the systems that condition, circulate, and control the liquid coolant loop at the rack or row level — are forecast to see their share of the DtC/1P stack decline over the same period, even as total CDU market revenue continues to grow in absolute terms, according to Omdia.

  The firm attributes this to two pressures. As CDUs are engineered to handle higher rack power densities, the cost per watt of cooling capacity falls. At the same time, increasing production scale and greater supply chain maturity are expected to compress CDU per-watt costs further. The combined effect is that cold plates, not CDUs, will represent the largest and fastest-growing value segment in the DtC/1P ecosystem by 2030.

  ## Significance for AI infrastructure build-out

  Omdia's forecast positions DtC/1P liquid cooling — and cold plates specifically — as the central thermal management technology for next-generation AI infrastructure globally. The firm identifies cold plate-based systems as the most viable solution for both existing rack retrofits and the ultra-high power densities required by modern GPU clusters, and projects cold plates will be the primary growth engine within the liquid cooling market through the end of the decade.
body_zh: |
  根據研究機構Omdia發布的熱管理追蹤報告，液冷技術將承擔2030年以前全球資料中心所有新增運算容量的主要部分，屆時每年新增IT負載預計達到約70吉瓦（GW）。Omdia預測，液冷將成為下一代AI基礎設施的主流熱管理解決方案。

  在液冷技術中，直接接觸晶片單相（DtC/1P）技術——透過直接安裝於處理器晶片上的冷板循環冷卻液，取代仰賴氣流穿越傳統散熱片的傳統做法——預計在整個預測期間持續保持市場領先地位，Omdia表示。其他液冷架構（包括浸沒式冷卻）同樣預期將有所成長，但Omdia指出，冷板式DtC/1P系統在改造現有伺服器機架、以及滿足現代圖形處理器（GPU）叢集所需超高功率密度方面，提供了最具實用性的解決方案。

  ## 冷板在價值鏈中的佔比持續擴大

  冷板預計將成為DtC/1P液冷價值鏈中最大的營收區塊。Omdia預測，冷板的營收佔比將從2024年在DtC/1P市場的40%，攀升至2030年的61%。

  Omdia點出這一轉變背後的三大驅動因素。第一，冷板的應用範圍已從中央處理器（CPU）與GPU擴展至記憶體模組、電力傳輸元件及其他高發熱硬體，大幅拓寬了該技術的整體可服務市場。第二，製造商在冷板製造中採用更先進的高導熱材料，推升了原材料成本與單件售價。第三，冷板內部微通道結構——即冷卻液在冷板內部流動的細小通道——持續獲得工程改良，不僅提升熱管理性能，也使最佳化設計得以採取溢價定價策略。

  ## CDU營收佔比在出貨量成長的同時仍將承壓收縮

  冷卻液分配單元（CDU）——在機架或機列層級負責調節、循環與控制液冷迴路的系統——預計在同一期間內，其在DtC/1P技術堆疊中的佔比將下滑，即便CDU市場的整體營收絕對值仍持續成長，Omdia指出。

  Omdia將此歸因於兩大壓力。隨著CDU被工程設計以因應更高的機架功率密度，每瓦冷卻容量的成本隨之下降。與此同時，生產規模持續擴大、供應鏈成熟度不斷提升，預計將進一步壓縮CDU的每瓦成本。兩者交疊的結果是：至2030年，在DtC/1P生態系統中，冷板而非CDU，將代表規模最大、成長最快的價值區塊。

  ## 對AI基礎設施建設的重要意義

  Omdia的預測將DtC/1P液冷技術——尤其是冷板——定位為全球下一代AI基礎設施的核心熱管理技術。Omdia認定冷板式系統是現有機架改造以及現代GPU叢集超高功率密度需求的最可行解決方案，並預測冷板將在本十年結束前持續扮演液冷市場主要成長引擎的角色。
