body_en: |
  Sensata Technologies has launched the FL Series Ultrasonic Flow Sensors, a non-intrusive flow-monitoring product line designed for liquid cooling systems in data centers and industrial applications. The sensors support flow rates up to 400 liters per minute and are targeted at operators managing the cooling demands of graphics processing units (GPUs) and other high-performance computing hardware.

  The FL Series measures flow with accuracy of up to ±1% of full scale, according to the company. The sensors carry an IP65 ingress protection rating — meaning they are dust-tight and protected against low-pressure water jets — and use ultrasonic sensing technology that requires no moving parts and imposes low pressure drop on the system. Sensata said the non-intrusive design reduces maintenance requirements compared with mechanical flow meters.

  ## What the sensors measure and why it matters

  Accurate coolant flow monitoring is central to data center thermal management. Too little flow through a cooling loop can cause equipment to overheat and reduce performance; too much wastes energy and degrades cooling efficiency, Sensata said. The FL Series is designed for both primary and secondary cooling loops, and the company said the ultrasonic sensing method maintains stable readings even when bubbles are present in the coolant — a condition that can affect the accuracy of some other measurement technologies.

  The sensors are compatible with deionized water and glycol mixtures, which are the coolant fluids commonly used in liquid cooling systems. Buyers can select from multiple housing materials, connection types, and communication protocols. Integrated temperature sensing is available as an option.

  ## Integration and target applications

  The FL Series provides both digital and analog outputs, making it compatible with programmable logic controllers (PLCs), building management systems (BMS), and other control platforms operators already use. Sensata said intended applications include coolant distribution units (CDUs) — the rack-level hardware that circulates coolant to servers — as well as chillers, heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems, and broader industrial cooling equipment.

  "As AI and high-performance computing place greater demands on data center infrastructure, effective cooling is becoming increasingly critical," said Alice Martins, executive vice president and president of Industrials at Sensata Technologies. "The FL Series builds on that broader portfolio, giving customers reliable flow data to help them manage cooling performance and keep critical systems operating reliably."

  ## Industry context

  Sensata positioned the FL Series as an extension of its existing data center portfolio, which the company said spans thermal management, electrical protection, and power and peak management. The company described itself as a "grid-to-chip engineering partner" for AI and high-performance computing infrastructure — a claim that encompasses the range from utility power input to chip-level thermal control.

  The FL Series launch reflects the broader shift in data center cooling toward liquid-based systems, driven by the heat output of AI accelerators such as GPUs, which exceed what traditional air cooling can reliably manage at high rack densities. By providing continuous flow data, the sensors are intended to help operators identify potential cooling anomalies earlier, support proactive maintenance, and reduce the risk of unplanned downtime affecting compute availability.

  Sensata said the FL Series is available across a range of configurations, with customers able to specify housing materials, connection interfaces, and communication protocols to match their existing infrastructure.

  FL 系列的量測精度可達滿量程的 ±1%。感測器具備 IP65 防護等級——意即可完全防塵，並能防範低壓水柱的噴射——採用超音波感測技術，無任何活動部件，且對系統造成的壓降極低。Sensata 表示，與機械式流量計相比，這種非侵入式設計可大幅降低維護需求。

  ## 感測器的量測對象與重要性

  冷卻液流量的精確監控，是資料中心熱管理的核心所在。Sensata 指出，冷卻迴路中流量過低，可能導致設備過熱、效能下降；流量過高則會浪費能源、降低冷卻效率。FL 系列適用於一次側與二次側冷卻迴路，該公司表示，超音波感測方式在冷卻液中存在氣泡的情況下，仍可維持穩定的讀數——氣泡正是某些其他量測技術的準確性隱患。

  感測器與去離子水及乙二醇混合液相容，這兩種液體是液冷系統中最常見的冷卻液。買家可依需求選擇多種外殼材質、接頭型式及通訊協定，並可選配整合式溫度感測功能。

  ## 整合能力與目標應用

  FL 系列同時提供數位與類比輸出，可與業者現有的可程式邏輯控制器（PLC）、建築管理系統（BMS）及其他控制平台整合。Sensata 表示，預期應用場景包括冷卻液分配單元（CDU）——即機架層級負責讓冷卻液在伺服器間循環的硬體——以及冰水機、暖通空調（HVAC）系統，以及更廣泛的工業冷卻設備。

  「隨著 AI 與高效能運算對資料中心基礎設施的需求持續攀升，有效的散熱正變得愈來愈關鍵，」Sensata Technologies 工業事業群執行副總裁暨總裁 Alice Martins 表示。「FL 系列以更廣泛的產品組合為基礎，為客戶提供可靠的流量數據，協助他們管理散熱效能，確保關鍵系統穩定運行。」

  ## 產業背景

  Sensata 將 FL 系列定位為其現有資料中心產品組合的延伸，該組合涵蓋熱管理、電氣保護，以及電源與尖峰負載管理。該公司將自身定位為 AI 與高效能運算基礎設施的「從電網到晶片工程夥伴」，這一定位涵蓋了從公用事業電力輸入到晶片層級熱控制的完整範疇。

  FL 系列的推出，反映了資料中心散熱技術向液冷轉型的大趨勢，其背後的驅動力，是 GPU 等 AI 加速器所產生的熱量，已超出傳統氣冷在高密度機架環境中所能可靠應對的上限。透過持續提供流量數據，這些感測器旨在協助業者更早發現潛在的散熱異常、支援主動式維護，並降低因突發停機衝擊運算可用性的風險。

  Sensata 表示，FL 系列提供多種規格配置，客戶可依其現有基礎設施，自行指定外殼材質、接頭介面及通訊協定。
