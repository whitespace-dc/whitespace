body_en: |
  Three major Taiwanese thermal component makers — 奇鋐 (TWSE: 3017), 雙鴻 (TWSE: 3324), and 健策 (TWSE: 3653) — stand to benefit from an architectural shift in AI server liquid cooling that increases both the quantity and complexity of water cold plates per AI accelerator, institutional investors told Commercial Times.

  The transition is driven by two converging developments: increasingly uneven heat distribution across the surfaces of advanced AI chips, and the emergence of vertical power delivery, a design that relocates power-conversion components to the underside of the chip and creates a second heat source requiring its own cooling solution.

  ## How AI chip packaging changed the cold plate problem

  High-end AI GPUs and ASICs now use multi-chip and heterogeneous integrated packaging — where multiple types of silicon are combined in a single package — which causes heat output to vary increasingly across different regions of the chip surface, according to Commercial Times.

  Traditional micro-channel cold plates address cooling by shrinking the width of internal flow channels, increasing the surface area in contact with coolant and raising heat-exchange efficiency. However, the source notes that a cold plate with uniform channels across its entire surface cannot concentrate cooling where it is most needed: coolant may flow through low-heat regions, leaving hot spots in high-power computing areas.

  Next-generation cold plate designs map flow channels directly to chip heat-source positions, adjusting channel arrangement, density, and cooling capacity region by region. As one publicly disclosed example, Wiwynn's high-precision micro-channel technology customizes channels to match actual chip heat distribution and can handle heat flux exceeding 350 watts per square centimeter. The practical effect, the report says, is that the competitive threshold for high-end cold plates has expanded beyond machining precision to encompass thermal simulation, flow-channel design expertise, and high heat flux management capability.

  ## Vertical power delivery adds a second heat source

  Current high-end AI accelerators require more than 1,000 amperes of electrical current. At that level, long power-delivery paths generate substantial resistive losses and additional heat. To shorten those paths, next-generation AI platforms are introducing vertical power delivery — placing power-conversion components directly beneath the chip rather than at a distance on the circuit board.

  Moving power components to the chip's underside creates a new heat source in a location that top-mounted cold plates cannot reach. A dual-sided cold plate architecture has emerged as the solution, the report says. One plate on top manages high heat flux from AI computation; a second plate on the bottom handles the vertical power-delivery components. Because chip and power components generate heat at different intensities and in different locations, the two cold plates must be designed separately, each with its own flow-channel structure.

  ## Why the shift benefits established thermal vendors

  Institutional investors cited by Commercial Times say the architecture change represents more than a simple increase in cold plate volume per unit. Moving from single-sided to dual-sided cooling raises plate count per accelerator, while the distinct thermal profiles of chip and power components require two fully custom designs rather than a single replicated part. Both factors raise product complexity and per-unit value.

  Companies with both cold plate design capability and liquid cooling system integration expertise are those the investors identify as best positioned to capture the new business, with 奇鋐 (3017), 雙鴻 (3324), and 健策 (3653) named as the primary beneficiaries.

  The report indicates that competition among cold plate suppliers will increasingly turn on thermal simulation and high heat flux management rather than manufacturing precision alone.
body_zh: |
  台灣三大散熱元件廠奇鋐（上市代碼：3017）、雙鴻（上市代碼：3324）及健策（上市代碼：3653），有望因AI伺服器液冷架構轉型而受惠——此一轉型將使每顆AI加速器所需的水冷板數量增加、複雜程度提高，法人機構向《工商時報》透露。

  這波轉型由兩股力量交匯驅動：其一是高階AI晶片表面熱分佈日趨不均，其二是垂直供電設計的興起——此設計將電源轉換元件移至晶片背面，形成第二個需要獨立冷卻的熱源。

  ## AI晶片封裝如何改變冷板設計挑戰

  根據《工商時報》報導，高階AI GPU與ASIC如今普遍採用多晶片異質整合封裝——將多種類型的矽晶片整合於單一封裝體內——導致晶片表面各區域的散熱量差異日益顯著。

  傳統微流道冷板的做法是縮小內部流道寬度，藉此增大與冷卻液的接觸面積，進而提升熱交換效率。然而，報導指出，若冷板全面採用均一流道設計，便無法將冷卻力集中於最需要的位置：冷卻液可能流過低熱區域，卻讓高功耗運算區的熱點得不到充分冷卻。

  下一代冷板設計則直接將流道配置對應至晶片熱源位置，依區域調整流道排列方式、密度與冷卻能力。以一項已公開揭露的案例為例，緯穎科技（Wiwynn）的高精度微流道技術能依照晶片實際熱分佈客製化流道，可處理超過每平方公分350瓦的熱通量。報導指出，此一發展的實際意義在於：高階冷板的競爭門檻已從加工精度擴展至熱模擬能力、流道設計專業知識，以及高熱通量管理能力。

  ## 垂直供電帶來第二個熱源

  目前高階AI加速器所需的電流超過1,000安培。在如此高的電流下，過長的供電路徑會產生可觀的電阻損耗與額外熱量。為縮短供電路徑，下一代AI平台引入垂直供電設計——將電源轉換元件直接置於晶片正下方，而非遠置於電路板上。

  將電源元件移至晶片背面，便在頂部冷板無法觸及的位置形成了新的熱源。雙面冷板架構因此應運而生，成為解決方案，報導如此指出。頂部冷板負責處理AI運算所產生的高熱通量；底部冷板則負責冷卻垂直供電元件。由於晶片與電源元件的發熱強度不同、位置各異，兩塊冷板必須分別設計，各自配置獨立的流道結構。

  ## 為何架構轉型有利於成熟散熱廠商

  《工商時報》引述法人機構看法，認為此一架構轉變不只是單純的冷板用量增加。從單面冷卻轉為雙面冷卻，使每顆加速器所需的冷板數量提升；而晶片與電源元件截然不同的熱分佈特性，也要求兩塊冷板各自量身定製，而非沿用同一設計。這兩項因素都使產品複雜程度與單位產品價值雙雙提高。

  法人機構認為，同時具備冷板設計能力與液冷系統整合專業的廠商，最有條件掌握這波新商機，並點名奇鋐（3017）、雙鴻（3324）、健策（3653）為主要受益廠商。

  報導並指出，冷板供應商之間的競爭，將愈來愈取決於熱模擬與高熱通量管理能力，而非僅止於製造加工精度。
