body_en: |
  Taiwan-listed thermal management company Micro-Star (邁科, ticker 6831) began shipping new chip thermal modules to cloud service provider (CSP) customers in May 2026, with water cooling products expected to reach those same customers before year-end, the company said.

  ## May revenue and first-quarter earnings

  Micro-Star posted combined revenue of NT$330 million in May 2026, up 54.97% from the prior month, though the figure was 10.76% below the same month a year earlier. Cumulative revenue for January through April 2026 totaled NT$1.398 billion, up 14.69% year-over-year. First-quarter 2026 earnings per share were NT$0.66, a result the company attributed to recovering AI server orders and the start of new thermal module shipments to CSP customers.

  ## New modules and pull-through demand

  Micro-Star said the thermal modules it began shipping in May are designed for newer chips with higher power draws and deliver substantially improved heat dissipation efficiency compared to prior-generation products. Pull-through demand — the pace at which customers draw down orders — is expected to strengthen progressively as deployments ramp, the company said.

  ## Water cooling ramp and Vietnam factory

  Water cooling products, which remove heat through liquid circuits rather than air, are expected to begin shipping to CSP customers before the end of 2026, Micro-Star said. The company's new factory in Vietnam is expected to see rising capacity utilization in step with that ramp. Micro-Star described the Vietnam facility as highly automated, saying that characteristic reduces labor requirements and improves production yield rates. At full production scale, the company said the factory's capacity would be sufficient to meet growing CSP customer demand.

  ## Industry spending backdrop

  The four largest US-based CSPs — hyperscale cloud and data center operators — are projected to collectively spend US$725 billion on capital expenditures in 2026, a record high, according to the report. That level of spending is cited as a structural growth driver for the thermal management sector.

  Micro-Star said it expects operations to improve progressively as the new chip thermal module ramp broadens and the water cooling product launch proceeds, with the Vietnam factory's automation providing the production capacity needed to meet rising CSP order volumes.
body_zh: |
  台股上市散熱管理廠商邁科（股票代號6831）自2026年5月起開始向雲端服務業者（CSP）客戶出貨新型晶片散熱模組，並預計在年底前將水冷產品一併納入出貨陣容，公司表示。

  ## 五月營收與第一季獲利

  邁科公布，2026年5月合併營收為新台幣3億3,000萬元，較上月大幅成長54.97%，但仍較去年同期下滑10.76%。累計2026年1月至4月營收合計達新台幣13億9,800萬元，年增14.69%。2026年第一季每股盈餘為新台幣0.66元，公司將此成績歸因於AI伺服器訂單回溫，以及新型散熱模組開始向CSP客戶出貨所帶來的挹注。

  ## 新型模組與拉貨需求

  邁科表示，5月起出貨的散熱模組專為功耗更高的新世代晶片所設計，與前一代產品相比，散熱效率大幅提升。隨著客戶端部署持續推進，客戶的拉貨力道預計將逐步走強。

  ## 水冷產品量產與越南廠

  邁科表示，水冷產品預計在2026年底前開始向CSP客戶出貨。水冷技術採液態迴路移除熱能，有別於傳統氣冷方式。公司位於越南的新廠預計隨著水冷產品出貨升溫，產能利用率也將同步提升。邁科指出，越南廠高度自動化，此一特性有效降低人力需求並提升生產良率。在滿載產能規模下，公司表示該廠的產能將足以滿足CSP客戶持續成長的需求。

  ## 產業資本支出概況

  根據報告，美國四大CSP業者——即超大規模雲端與資料中心營運商——預計2026年合計資本支出將達7,250億美元，創歷史新高。此一支出規模被引述為散熱管理產業結構性成長的核心驅動力。

  邁科表示，隨著新型晶片散熱模組出貨規模持續擴大、水冷產品正式推出，以及越南廠自動化生產能力足以支應CSP訂單量攀升，公司整體營運預計將持續逐步改善。
