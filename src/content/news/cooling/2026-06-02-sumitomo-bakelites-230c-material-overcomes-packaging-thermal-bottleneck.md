# Sumitomo Bakelite's 230°C Material Overcomes Packaging Thermal Bottleneck

Sumitomo Bakelite has released a high heat-resistant material rated for 230°C operating temperatures to address thermal challenges in next-generation electronic packaging. The breakthrough targets rising thermal demands from AI 資料中心, 800V electric vehicle platforms, and high-voltage direct current (HVDC) distribution systems, which are driving higher power densities in electronic equipment. The material resolves a critical thermal management bottleneck for advanced packaging applications.

## 中文

# 住友電木推出230°C耐熱材料，突破封裝溫度瓶頸

住友電木推出新型230°C耐熱材料，解決新一代電子設備的封裝熱管理挑戰。此突破針對人工智慧資料中心、800V電動車平台以及高壓直流配電等技術帶來的功率密度提升，提供關鍵的熱管理方案。該材料克服了先進電子設備封裝的溫度瓶頸。
