body_en: |
  Canaan Inc. has contracted to provide up to 8 megawatts (MW) of waste heat from hydro-cooled Bitcoin mining equipment to an unnamed Nordic district heating provider, with an initial 2MW phase already supplying hot water to local residents and a follow-on order placed in March 2026 set to bring the project to its full scale.

  The Singapore-based mining hardware company said the deployment uses its Avalon A1566HA hydro-cooled units — application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) miners, which are chips designed exclusively to perform the cryptographic calculations that validate Bitcoin transactions. Those calculations generate large quantities of heat as a byproduct; liquid-cooled systems channel that heat into water loops that can be integrated into district heating infrastructure, which distributes centrally produced thermal energy through insulated pipe networks to residential and commercial buildings.

  ## Project scale and phasing

  The initial phase, comprising 228 A1566HA units, is already operational and producing approximately 2MW of heating capacity. Following that phase's performance, the customer placed a follow-on order in March 2026 for a further 692 units, adding another 6MW and bringing the total to roughly 8MW. Canaan said the completed project is expected to provide heating for around 2,800 homes.

  The company did not disclose the customer's name, the precise location of the deployment, financial terms, or a timeline for when the additional 6MW of equipment will be fully operational.

  ## How the system works

  Canaan said its A1566HA units produce hot water at approximately 80°C (176°F), a temperature high enough to feed directly into district heating infrastructure without additional thermal boosting. The units are deployed in parallel and support dynamic overclocking and underclocking, allowing operators to adjust compute load — and therefore heat output — in response to fluctuating heating demand. Canaan said this gives the system greater output flexibility than single-source heating systems such as boilers.

  ## Earlier waste-heat deployments

  The Nordic project is not Canaan's first effort to reuse mining heat for productive ends. Earlier in 2026, the company announced a two-year, 3MW pilot in Manitoba, Canada, carried out in partnership with Bitforest Investment. That project used 360 Avalon liquid-cooled computing servers connected to a commercial greenhouse's boiler loop, with recovered heat supporting year-round tomato cultivation.

  ## Significance

  The Nordic deployment illustrates a pattern in which crypto mining hardware manufacturers are engineering heat recovery as a primary design requirement rather than an afterthought. Canaan chairman and CEO Nangeng Zhang said the district heating application represents validation of the company's work in energy-integrated computing systems. "Heat reuse is no longer an ancillary byproduct of compute," Zhang said.

  Once the additional 692 units are operational, the 8MW system would represent one of the larger documented examples of Bitcoin mining heat feeding directly into a municipal residential heating network. Canaan has not provided a date for when that phase will reach full operation.

  這家總部位於新加坡的挖礦硬體公司表示，此次部署採用其Avalon A1566HA液冷機組——亦即應用特定積體電路（ASIC）礦機。ASIC是專為執行比特幣交易驗證所需密碼學運算而設計的晶片，這些運算過程會產生大量熱能作為副產品；液冷系統將這些熱能導入水循環迴路，可與區域供熱基礎設施整合——區域供熱透過隔熱管網，將集中生產的熱能輸送至住宅與商業建築。

  ## 專案規模與分期進度

  初期部署共228台A1566HA機組，目前已正式運作，供熱容量約達2MW。完成首期後，客戶於2026年3月追加訂購692台機組，新增6MW，總計約達8MW。嘉楠表示，整套系統完工後預計可為約2,800戶家庭提供暖氣。

  公司未揭露客戶名稱、部署確切地點及財務條款，亦未說明額外6MW設備何時將完全投入運作。

  ## 系統運作原理

  嘉楠表示，A1566HA機組產出的熱水溫度約攝氏80度（華氏176度），足以直接供入區域供熱基礎設施，無需額外的熱力增壓。各機組以並聯方式部署，支援動態超頻與降頻功能，讓營運商得以依據供熱需求的波動，靈活調整運算負載，進而控制熱量輸出。嘉楠表示，此設計使系統具備比鍋爐等單一熱源系統更大的輸出彈性。

  ## 先前的廢熱再利用部署

  北歐專案並非嘉楠首次將挖礦廢熱轉作有益用途。2026年稍早，該公司宣布與Bitforest Investment合作，在加拿大馬尼托巴省展開一項為期兩年、容量3MW的試驗計畫。該計畫使用360台Avalon液冷運算伺服器，接入一座商業溫室的鍋爐循環迴路，以回收熱能支持全年番茄種植。

  ## 意義

  北歐此次部署體現了加密貨幣挖礦硬體製造商的一種趨勢轉變：將熱能回收列為主要設計需求，而非事後追加的附加考量。嘉楠董事長兼執行長張楠賡表示，區域供熱應用驗證了公司在能源整合運算系統領域的工作成果。「廢熱再利用不再是運算的附帶副產品，」張楠賡說。

  當額外的692台機組全數上線後，這套8MW系統將成為有文獻記錄以來，比特幣挖礦廢熱直接供應市政住宅供熱網絡中規模較大的案例之一。嘉楠目前尚未提供該階段完整投入運作的時間表。
