body_en: |
  Electronics Cooling has opened the complete Data Center Cooling Days 2026 program for free on-demand streaming after the event's two live broadcast days, held July 15 and 22, 2026.

  The program, described by the organizers as the first Data Center Cooling Days event, drew engineers from across the data center thermal management field. Sessions spanned liquid cooling system design, AI infrastructure cooling, advanced cold plates, coolant care and testing, and open data center infrastructure.

  ## Day One and Day Two Presenters

  Day 1 featured presentations from Anton Paar, CPC, Ventiva, and Molten Dynamics. Day 2 included sessions from Texas Instruments, ThermAvant, the Open Compute Project (OCP), Phononic, Kelvin Thermal and Qnity, and Dow. Anton Paar served as master sponsor of the event.

  The OCP, an industry body that develops open hardware standards for data center infrastructure, participated in Day 2 alongside commercial vendors presenting on emerging cooling technologies.

  ## Topics Covered

  According to the organizers, the on-demand program addresses the following subject areas:

  - AI infrastructure cooling, control, and system design
  - Advanced cold plates and hotspot heat removal
  - Liquid cooling reliability, coolant care, and testing
  - Emerging cooling approaches and infrastructure optimization
  - Open data center infrastructure and OCP initiatives
  - Industry collaboration

  The breadth of topics reflects the expanding role of liquid cooling in data centers, driven in part by the heat loads generated by AI accelerator hardware, which can exceed what traditional air cooling can manage.

  ## Access and Upcoming Events

  Both days of the program are available to stream at no cost through Electronics Cooling. The organizers noted that attendees who missed live sessions can access the full recordings.

  Electronics Cooling said further events are planned, with Thermal Live Fall 2026 scheduled for October 14 and 15, 2026.

  ## 2026年資料中心冷卻技術大會完整議程開放隨選串流

  Electronics Cooling宣布，繼2026年7月15日及22日兩場實體直播活動結束後，「2026年資料中心冷卻技術大會」（Data Center Cooling Days 2026）完整兩日議程現已全面開放免費隨選串流。

  主辦單位表示，本次活動為資料中心冷卻技術大會首度舉辦，吸引了資料中心熱管理領域各地工程師踴躍參與。議程範圍廣泛，涵蓋液冷系統設計、AI基礎架構冷卻方案、高效能冷板技術、冷卻液維護與測試，以及開放式資料中心基礎架構等多項主題。

  ## 第一天與第二天講者陣容

  第一天議程由Anton Paar、CPC、Ventiva及Molten Dynamics等單位發表專題報告。第二天則有德州儀器（Texas Instruments）、ThermAvant、開放運算計畫（Open Compute Project，OCP）、Phononic、Kelvin Thermal與Qnity，以及陶氏化學（Dow）等單位登場。Anton Paar擔任本次活動主贊助商。

  OCP為一致力於推動資料中心基礎架構開放硬體標準的產業組織，與多家商業廠商共同參與第二天議程，分享新興冷卻技術的最新發展。

  ## 涵蓋主題

  根據主辦單位說明，本次隨選串流內容涵蓋以下主題領域：

  - AI基礎架構冷卻、控制與系統設計
  - 高效能冷板技術與熱點散熱解決方案
  - 液冷可靠性、冷卻液維護與測試
  - 新興冷卻技術與基礎架構效能最佳化
  - 開放式資料中心基礎架構與OCP倡議
  - 產業跨界合作

  議題涵蓋範圍之廣，反映出液冷技術在資料中心應用中日益擴大的重要性。這一趨勢部分源於AI加速器硬體所產生的龐大熱負荷——其散熱需求往往已超越傳統氣冷方案所能承擔的上限。

  ## 觀看方式與後續活動

  兩日完整議程均可透過Electronics Cooling平台免費串流觀看。主辦單位特別指出，未能參與現場直播的與會者，同樣可隨時取得完整錄影內容。

  Electronics Cooling表示，後續活動已在規劃當中，「2026年秋季Thermal Live」（Thermal Live Fall 2026）預計將於2026年10月14日至15日舉行。
