body_en: |
  NTT Docomo Business and three partner companies announced November 13 a pilot program to capture waste heat from a Tokyo-area data center and channel it into warming rearing water for Pacific white shrimp, targeting a commercial aquaculture model that could be deployed across other data center sites.

  The project addresses two simultaneous energy burdens. Data center electricity consumption has risen with the spread of generative artificial intelligence and other compute-intensive workloads, generating larger volumes of waste heat. That heat is typically low-temperature, which limits practical reuse options and means much of it is discharged unused. Land-based aquaculture, meanwhile, carries a heavy energy cost because operators must continuously heat rearing water to keep it within a narrow range suited to the target species. The four companies said routing low-grade data center heat to aquaculture tanks can reduce energy expenditure on both sides.

  ## Two-phase experiment structure

  The trial runs in two stages through March 2028. In fiscal year 2026, the partners will assess heat recovery from data center cooling equipment, measuring temperature, thermal volume, and other parameters to identify viable recovery methods. In fiscal year 2027, the consortium will move to actual shrimp rearing, evaluating the heat's effectiveness in maintaining water temperature, the resulting reduction in aquaculture heating energy, and any operational impact on the data center.

  The project was selected under the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's program to promote the use of otherwise wasted heat by startups.

  ## Partner roles

  Four companies divide the work. NTT Docomo Business provides one of its own data centers in the greater Tokyo area as the test site and evaluates the effect on facility operations. IMT Engineering, based in Iwata City, Shizuoka Prefecture, handles aquaculture system design and day-to-day rearing operations. Hibiya Comprehensive Equipment, headquartered in Minato, Tokyo, designs and installs the heat recovery and heat-supply infrastructure. NTT Green & Food, based in Chiyoda, Tokyo, monitors the rearing environment and assesses the commercial viability of the model.

  ## Why Pacific white shrimp

  The partners selected Pacific white shrimp (Litopenaeus vannamei, known in Japan as バナメイエビ), a commercially farmed species that reaches 15 to 20 centimeters at maturity and is widely used in Japanese cooking. An NTT Docomo Business representative told ITmedia NEWS that established land-based aquaculture techniques allow the species to reach harvest size in 16 to 18 weeks, making it well suited for a time-limited proof-of-concept.

  Whether shrimp raised during the trial reach consumers remains undecided. The representative said the companies would consider shipping the shrimp if they were in shippable condition at the experiment's end, but noted that no destination or timeline had been fixed and that achieving that outcome was an aspiration rather than a commitment.

  ## Path to commercial deployment

  The four companies said they intend to use results from the trial to establish a business model that other data center operators could adopt. Data on heat recovery efficiency, shrimp growth rates, and data center operational impacts gathered through March 2028 will form the basis for that broader rollout effort.
body_zh: |
  NTT Docomo Business 與三家合作夥伴於11月13日宣布，將展開試驗計畫，擷取東京都近郊一座資料中心的廢熱，導入養殖南美白蝦的用水加溫系統，目標是打造可在其他資料中心據點複製推廣的商業化養殖模式。

  此計畫同步解決兩項能源負擔。隨著生成式人工智慧及其他運算密集型工作負載日益普及，資料中心的用電量持續攀升，所產生的廢熱量也隨之擴大。這類廢熱溫度普遍偏低，實際可再利用的場合相當有限，大量熱能因此在未加利用的情況下直接排放。另一方面，陸上養殖業的能源成本極為沉重，業者必須持續為養殖用水加溫，使水溫維持在目標物種所需的狹窄範圍之內。四家公司表示，將低品位的資料中心廢熱引入養殖水槽，能同時降低雙方的能源消耗。

  ## 兩階段實驗架構

  試驗計畫分兩個階段進行，預計至2028年3月完成。在2026年度，合作夥伴將評估從資料中心冷卻設備回收廢熱的可行性，量測溫度、熱量及其他相關參數，以確立可行的回收方式。2027年度，聯合團隊將進入實際養蝦階段，評估廢熱維持水溫的效果、養殖加熱能耗的減少幅度，以及對資料中心日常運作的影響。

  本計畫已獲選為東京都政府推動新創業者有效利用廢熱資源的輔導計畫項目。

  ## 合作夥伴分工

  四家公司各司其職。NTT Docomo Business 提供位於大東京地區的一座自有資料中心作為測試場地，並負責評估試驗對設施營運的影響。位於靜岡縣磐田市的 IMT Engineering 負責養殖系統設計及日常養殖作業。總部設於東京港區的日比谷綜合設備（Hibiya Comprehensive Equipment）負責設計與建置廢熱回收及熱能供應基礎設施。總部位於東京千代田區的 NTT Green & Food 則負責監控養殖環境，並評估此商業模式的整體可行性。

  ## 選擇南美白蝦的原因

  合作夥伴選定南美白蝦（學名：Litopenaeus vannamei，日本稱バナメイエビ）作為養殖對象。這是一種商業養殖蝦種，成熟體長約15至20公分，廣泛應用於日本料理。NTT Docomo Business 的代表向 ITmedia NEWS 表示，目前已有成熟的陸上養殖技術可讓此物種在16至18週內達到收穫規格，非常適合作為有時限的概念驗證試驗對象。

  試驗期間養成的蝦子是否會流通至消費市場，目前尚未確定。該代表表示，若試驗結束時蝦子達到出貨標準，各公司將考慮出貨，但強調目的地與時程均尚未敲定，能否實現仍屬期望而非正式承諾。

  ## 邁向商業化部署的路徑

  四家公司表示，計畫運用試驗結果建立一套商業模式，供其他資料中心業者參考採用。預計在2028年3月前蒐集到的廢熱回收效率、蝦子生長速率及資料中心營運影響等相關數據，將成為日後更廣泛推廣計畫的重要基礎。
