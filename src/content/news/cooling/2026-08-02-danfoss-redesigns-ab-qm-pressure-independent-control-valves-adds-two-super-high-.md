body_en: |
  Danfoss has redesigned its AB-QM range of pressure independent control valves — devices that maintain a set flow rate regardless of pressure fluctuations in a piping network — in sizes DN 125 through DN 250, and expanded the family with two new Super High Flow models aimed at large-scale HVAC, data centre, and district cooling projects.

  The core change across the redesigned size L valves is a significant increase in nominal flow rate, the maximum rated water volume a valve can pass under specified conditions. Across the DN 125–250 range, that increase ranges from 5% to 60%, with an average gain of 30%, according to Danfoss.

  ## Actuator requirements and pressure ratings

  The redesign also reduces the actuator force required to operate the larger valves. Actuators are the motorised or electronic devices that open and close control valves in response to building management system signals. Danfoss said the DN 200 and DN 250 valves no longer require its digital NovoCon XL actuator; the smaller NovoCon L is now sufficient for both sizes. The company said this change can lower installation space requirements and reduce initial investment cost.

  The maximum allowable differential pressure — the pressure difference across the valve that the device can safely handle — has been raised from 600 kPa to 800 kPa. Danfoss said the higher rating provides a greater safety margin in demanding systems where pressure swings are more pronounced.

  ## New Super High Flow variants

  Alongside the redesigned existing sizes, Danfoss has introduced two new models under a Super High Flow designation, both in DN 250:

  - **DN 250 SHF**: nominal flow of 600 m³/h, starting differential pressure of 80 kPa
  - **DN 250 SHF+**: nominal flow of 670 m³/h, starting differential pressure of 100 kPa

  To put those figures in context, 600 m³/h equates to roughly 167 litres per second of chilled water — a flow rate associated with large central plant equipment serving substantial building clusters or district networks.

  ## Significance for data centre and district cooling design

  Pressure independent control valves are widely used in large chilled water systems because they simplify hydronic balancing — the process of ensuring each terminal unit in a distribution network receives its design flow — and maintain stable delivery as loads across a building or campus shift. For data centre operators and district cooling providers, where cooling loads can be large, variable, and geographically distributed, precise flow control at the valve level reduces the risk of hot spots and inefficient pump operation.

  Danfoss said the update gives HVAC design engineers more flexibility for demanding applications, specifically citing large chilled water systems and district cooling networks. The ability to use smaller actuators on the largest valve sizes addresses a practical constraint in dense plant rooms where physical space is limited.

  The two new Super High Flow models extend the AB-QM product line into flow regimes previously served by larger or less integrated solutions, according to the company.

  ## 致動器需求與壓力等級

  此次重新設計同時降低了操作大尺寸閥門所需的致動器出力。致動器是依據建築管理系統訊號開閉控制閥的電動或電子裝置。丹佛斯表示，DN 200與DN 250閥門不再需要搭配旗下的數位型NovoCon XL致動器，較小型的NovoCon L如今已足以應對這兩個尺寸的需求。該公司指出，這項改變有助於降低安裝空間需求，並減少初期投資成本。

  最大允許差壓——即閥門能夠安全承受的跨閥壓差——已從600 kPa提升至800 kPa。丹佛斯表示，更高的額定值在壓力波動較為劇烈的高需求系統中，提供了更充裕的安全餘裕。

  ## 全新超高流量型號

  除重新設計既有尺寸外，丹佛斯亦在DN 250規格下推出兩款全新超高流量型號：

  - **DN 250 SHF**：額定流量600 m³/h，起始差壓80 kPa
  - **DN 250 SHF+**：額定流量670 m³/h，起始差壓100 kPa

  就這些數字的實際意義而言，600 m³/h相當於每秒約167公升的冷凍水流量，此一流量等級通常與服務大型建築群或區域網路的大型中央設備相關聯。

  ## 對資料中心與區域供冷設計的意義

  壓力無關控制閥在大型冷凍水系統中被廣泛採用，原因在於它能簡化水力平衡作業——亦即確保配水網路中每個末端設備都能獲得設計流量的調配程序——並在整棟建築或整座園區的負載隨時變動時，仍能維持穩定供應。對於資料中心營運商與區域供冷業者而言，冷卻負載龐大、多變且地理分布廣泛，閥門層級的精確流量控制可有效降低熱點風險，並避免水泵運轉效率低落的問題。

  丹佛斯表示，此次更新賦予暖通空調設計工程師在高需求應用上更大的彈性，並特別點名大型冷凍水系統及區域供冷網路。在設備空間有限的密集機房中，最大尺寸閥門改用較小型致動器的設計，確實解決了實際安裝上的空間制約。
