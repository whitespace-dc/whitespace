body_en: |
  # One in Four Data Centers Sits in Water-Stressed Region, Competing With Residents

  One in four data centers worldwide is located in a water-crisis region, placing the facilities in direct competition with local residents for access to limited water supplies, Kompas.com reported.

  ## What the figures show

  The finding — that 25 percent of data centers operate in areas already under water stress — points to a structural tension between the industry's cooling requirements and the needs of surrounding communities. Data centers use water primarily to cool servers; the more computing load a facility carries, the greater its water draw.

  ## Significance

  The concentration of data center capacity in water-stressed areas raises supply questions for both operators and the residents who share the same sources. As demand for computing infrastructure continues to grow, the overlap between high-density facility siting and water-scarce geographies represents a material planning and regulatory risk for the industry.
