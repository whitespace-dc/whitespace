body_en: |
  Motivair, the liquid cooling subsidiary of Schneider Electric, has launched the MCDU-70, a cooling distribution unit (CDU) — the hardware that isolates facility water from IT-side coolant and precisely regulates flow, pressure, and temperature — with a single-unit capacity of 2.5 megawatts (MW), the company announced June 23. Multiple units managed through a centralized control system can extend total cooling capacity beyond 10MW, the company said.

  The MCDU-70 is Motivair's highest-capacity CDU to date and is aimed at next-generation GPU deployments, high-performance computing (HPC), and what the industry describes as gigawatt-scale AI factories — data centers whose aggregate power draw reaches into the gigawatt range.

  ## Specifications and redundancy configuration

  Motivair said the MCDU-70 is designed to sustain full coolant flow performance and maintain stable facility-side pressure within a compact physical footprint, accommodating rapid rack-level expansion as chip power draw rises.

  For large AI factory deployments, the company outlined a six-unit "4+2" redundancy arrangement: four CDUs handle a combined 10MW cooling load while the remaining two serve as standby units, maintaining system stability during maintenance or a single-unit failure and leaving headroom for future increases in GPU power consumption.

  With the MCDU-70 added, Motivair's CDU product line now spans 105 kilowatts (kW) to 2.5MW. All units in the lineup support modular expansion and integration with Schneider Electric's EcoStruxure software platform, which allows multiple CDUs to be managed as a unified system, giving data center operators real-time visibility into cooling status and the ability to add capacity as compute workloads grow, the company said.

  ## NVIDIA architecture alignment

  Motivair said the MCDU-70 is compatible with deployment architectures including NVIDIA's Omniverse DSX Blueprint. NVIDIA introduced both its Vera Rubin DSX AI factory reference design and the Omniverse DSX digital twin blueprint in 2026, frameworks that integrate compute, networking, power, and cooling systems into a coordinated design. Schneider Electric is among the infrastructure vendors participating in that architecture and software integration, according to the source.

  ## Shift from air to direct liquid cooling

  As AI chips have evolved from individual GPUs toward rack-level and cluster-level computing, data center cooling design has accelerated its shift from traditional air cooling toward direct liquid cooling, the source noted. The CDU has become a critical component in liquid cooling systems, and a single unit's capacity ceiling directly affects deployment efficiency and expansion flexibility.

  "AI infrastructure must keep pace with chip and silicon technology evolution," said Rich Whitmore, Motivair's chief executive and president, in a statement. "The key to successfully deploying next-generation AI factories lies in building scalable, reliable, and efficient infrastructure."

  ## Industry outlook

  As AI rack power density continues to rise and gigawatt-scale data centers begin coming online, liquid cooling equipment is expected to move beyond its role as a server-adjacent system and become core infrastructure that directly affects AI factory deployment timelines and compute uptime, according to the source.

  施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）旗下液冷子公司Motivair於6月23日宣布推出MCDU-70冷卻分配單元（CDU）——這是一種將機房設施側冷卻水與IT設備側冷媒相互隔離、並精確調控流量、壓力與溫度的硬體設備——單機容量達2.5百萬瓦（MW）。該公司表示，透過集中控制系統管理多台機組，總冷卻容量可擴充至逾10MW。

  MCDU-70是Motivair迄今容量最高的CDU產品，目標市場涵蓋新世代GPU部署、高效能運算（HPC），以及業界所稱的吉瓦級AI工廠——即總用電量達吉瓦（GW）量級的資料中心。

  ## 規格與冗餘配置

  Motiair表示，MCDU-70的設計目標是在精簡的實體佔地面積內，維持冷媒的全流量效能，並確保設施側壓力穩定，以因應晶片功耗持續攀升所帶來的機架層級快速擴充需求。

  針對大型AI工廠部署場景，該公司提出了一套六機組「4+2」冗餘配置方案：四台CDU共同承擔10MW的冷卻負載，其餘兩台則作為備援機組，在維護期間或單機故障時維持系統穩定，同時為未來GPU功耗的進一步提升預留擴充空間。

  隨著MCDU-70加入產品陣容，Motivair的CDU產品線現已涵蓋105千瓦（kW）至2.5MW的完整規格。產品線中的所有機組均支援模組化擴充，並可整合至施耐德電氣的EcoStruxure軟體平台，讓多台CDU以統一系統形式管理，使資料中心營運商能即時掌握冷卻狀態，並隨運算工作負載成長彈性擴增容量。

  ## 與輝達架構的相容性

  Motiair表示，MCDU-70相容於包含輝達（NVIDIA）Omniverse DSX藍圖在內的多種部署架構。輝達於2026年同步發表Vera Rubin DSX AI工廠參考設計與Omniverse DSX數位分身藍圖，這兩套框架將運算、網路、電力與冷卻系統整合為一套協調一致的設計體系。依據相關資料，施耐德電氣是參與該架構與軟體整合的基礎設施廠商之一。

  ## 從氣冷轉向直接液冷

  隨著AI晶片從單一GPU演進至機架層級與叢集層級的運算架構，資料中心冷卻設計已加速從傳統氣冷轉向直接液冷，相關資料指出。CDU已成為液冷系統中的關鍵元件，單機容量上限直接影響部署效率與擴充彈性。

  「AI基礎設施必須跟上晶片與矽技術的演進步伐，」Motivair執行長暨總裁Rich Whitmore在聲明中表示，「成功部署下世代AI工廠的關鍵，在於建構可擴充、高可靠、高效率的基礎設施。」

  ## 產業展望

  隨著AI機架功率密度持續上升、吉瓦級資料中心陸續上線，液冷設備預計將超越伺服器周邊系統的定位，成為直接影響AI工廠部署時程與運算正常運行時間的核心基礎設施，相關資料如此指出。
