  ## Keyter and Tecnalia sign R&D deal targeting heat pumps and data center cooling

  Córdoba-based HVAC and refrigeration equipment manufacturer Keyter has signed a collaboration agreement with Tecnalia, a Spanish research and technological development centre, to advance sustainable air conditioning and refrigeration technologies, the companies said.

  The agreement targets three primary areas: high-temperature heat pumps, cooling solutions for data processing centres (DPCs) — including chillers and terminal units — and advanced digitalisation applied to equipment and industrial processes.

  ## Scope of the collaboration

  The agreement outlines a broad set of joint activities. According to the companies, these include identifying technological trends and business opportunities in the heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and refrigeration (HVAC-R) sector; jointly defining and executing strategic research and development projects; generating and exploiting intellectual property; providing strategic innovation consulting; conducting laboratory validation and verification of equipment; offering technical advice on component design and selection; and developing specialised training programmes.

  Beyond the primary focus areas, both organisations said they will work on technologies related to advanced manufacturing, new materials suited for high-temperature heat exchangers and corrosive operating environments, hydrogen-based heating and cooling solutions, district heating and cooling systems, thermal energy storage, and applications in large-scale agricultural processes.

  ## Why it matters

  The stated goal of the partnership is to design and validate equipment with higher energy efficiency and lower operating costs to meet demand for sustainable solutions in both industrial and residential buildings, the companies said. The explicit inclusion of data centre cooling — a segment facing rapidly growing thermal management requirements as compute density rises — alongside industrial heat pump development positions the agreement at the intersection of two of the HVAC sector's most active growth areas.

  The partnership gives Keyter direct access to Tecnalia's research infrastructure, including laboratory facilities for equipment testing, while Tecnalia gains an industry partner for applied validation of emerging technologies such as hydrogen-based thermal solutions and new heat exchanger materials.

  The companies said the collaboration will also address intellectual property exploitation, meaning commercial products or licensed technologies could result from joint development work.

  ## Keyter與Tecnalia簽署研發協議，聚焦熱泵與資料中心冷卻

  總部位於科爾多瓦的暖通空調暨冷凍設備製造商Keyter，日前與西班牙研究暨技術發展機構Tecnalia簽署合作協議，攜手推進永續空調與冷凍技術，兩家公司對此共同發表聲明。

  此協議鎖定三大主要領域：高溫熱泵、資料處理中心（DPC）冷卻解決方案（包含冰水機及末端設備），以及應用於設備與工業流程的進階數位化技術。

  ## 合作範疇

  協議規劃了一系列廣泛的聯合活動。據兩家公司表示，內容涵蓋：識別暖通空調與冷凍（HVAC-R）產業的技術趨勢與商機；共同定義並執行策略性研發專案；開發並利用智慧財產；提供策略創新顧問服務；對設備進行實驗室驗證與確認；就元件設計與選用提供技術建議；以及開發專業培訓課程。

  除三大核心領域外，雙方機構表示亦將攜手研究以下相關技術：先進製造、適用於高溫熱交換器與腐蝕性操作環境的新材料、氫能供暖與冷卻解決方案、區域供熱供冷系統、熱能儲存，以及大型農業流程的應用。

  ## 合作意義

  兩家公司表示，此合作夥伴關係的既定目標，是設計並驗證具備更高能源效率、更低營運成本的設備，以滿足工業及住宅建築對永續解決方案日益增長的需求。隨著運算密度持續攀升，資料中心的熱管理需求急速成長，協議明確納入資料中心冷卻——這一需求快速擴大的細分市場——並與工業熱泵開發並列，使本協議恰好落在暖通空調產業兩大最活躍成長領域的交會點。

  此合作夥伴關係讓Keyter得以直接取用Tecnalia的研究基礎設施，包括設備測試實驗室；Tecnalia則獲得一位業界夥伴，可針對氫能熱力解決方案及新型熱交換器材料等新興技術進行應用驗證。

  兩家公司表示，此次合作亦將涉及智慧財產的商業化運用，意味著聯合開發成果有望轉化為商業產品或授權技術。
