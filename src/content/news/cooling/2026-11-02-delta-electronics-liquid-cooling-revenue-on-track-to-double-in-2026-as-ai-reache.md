body_en: |
  Delta Electronics (2308) said its liquid cooling revenue is expected to approximately double in 2026 compared with 2025, as AI data center-related products reached 50 percent of the company's total revenue, Chairman Zheng Ping said October 30.

  Zheng said all four of the company's AI data center product lines — cooling, power supplies, components, and data center infrastructure — had grown rapidly in 2026, driven by strong demand. Liquid cooling alone represented approximately 13 percent of total revenue in the third quarter of 2026.

  ## Liquid cooling revenue

  Delta Electronics reported liquid cooling revenue of approximately $1.6 billion for full-year 2025. In the third quarter of 2026, the segment reached NT$25 billion (New Taiwan dollars) for the single quarter. The company said that if liquid cooling maintains approximately 13 percent of annual revenue through the full year, 2026 liquid cooling revenue will approximately double the 2025 total.

  ## 2027 outlook

  Zheng said demand across AI data center power, components, infrastructure, and cooling products was "very strong" in 2026. The AI data center segment, now representing half of all company revenue, is expected to continue growing in 2027, he said.

  ## Solid oxide fuel cells

  Delta Electronics also said its solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) product line would begin mass production in 2027, with the first production line carrying capacity of approximately 100 megawatts. Zheng identified the verification and testing process as the most critical stage of SOFC development and said two or three overseas customers had already agreed to test the product ahead of the planned commercial launch.
body_zh: |
  台達電子（股票代號：2308）董事長鄭平於10月30日表示，2026年液冷業務營收預計較2025年翻倍成長，目前AI資料中心相關產品已佔公司總營收的五成。

  鄭平指出，公司旗下四大AI資料中心產品線——冷卻、電源供應器、元件，以及資料中心基礎設施——在強勁需求帶動下，2026年均呈現快速成長。其中，僅液冷一項，在2026年第三季即佔總營收的約13%。

  ## 液冷營收

  台達電子2025年全年液冷業務營收約達16億美元。進入2026年，單一季度的表現便已十分亮眼——第三季液冷營收達新台幣250億元。公司表示，若液冷業務全年維持約佔總營收13%的比重，則2026年全年液冷營收將較2025年翻倍。

  ## 2027年展望

  鄭平表示，2026年AI資料中心電源、元件、基礎設施及冷卻產品的需求「非常強勁」。AI資料中心業務目前已佔公司整體營收的一半，他預期這一態勢將持續延伸，2027年仍將維持成長動能。

  ## 固態氧化物燃料電池

  台達電子亦宣布，固態氧化物燃料電池（SOFC）產品線預計於2027年正式進入量產階段，首條產線的產能約為100百萬瓦（100 MW）。鄭平強調，驗證與測試流程是SOFC研發過程中最為關鍵的環節，目前已有兩至三家海外客戶同意在計畫商業化上市前，率先進行產品測試。
