body_en: |
  Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI) has developed an ultra-low temperature brine chiller that uses nitrogen as its refrigerant, the Japanese company said, with the unit capable of operating across a temperature range of -40°C to -100°C.

  The product, called N-Bleath, is built around what MHI describes as an air refrigerant cycle — a thermodynamic approach the company said it developed over many years of research. The system uses nitrogen, which is non-toxic and non-flammable, in place of the synthetic refrigerants commonly found in conventional chillers.

  ## How the unit works

  At the core of N-Bleath is a compact electric turbo unit that MHI said combines a high-performance impeller with an ultra-high speed motor, drawing on the company's aerodynamic and high-speed rotation technologies. The turbo operates on oil-free bearings, which MHI said eliminates the need for periodic lubricating oil changes and significantly reduces maintenance requirements compared to conventional systems.

  Advanced thermal insulation allows the turbo to sustain cooling below -100°C, MHI said — the lower end of the unit's stated operating range.

  ## Temperature range and application

  N-Bleath is designed to handle ultra-low temperatures (ULT) spanning -40°C to -100°C. That range covers applications that standard industrial chillers cannot reach without cascaded refrigeration stages, which typically add mechanical complexity and maintenance burden.

  ## Industry significance

  The use of nitrogen as a working fluid removes dependence on hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) or other synthetic refrigerants, which face tightening regulatory pressure in multiple jurisdictions due to their global warming potential. A chiller that achieves ULT performance using a natural refrigerant with no global warming potential addresses both the cooling performance and environmental compliance requirements that data center and industrial cold-chain operators increasingly face. The elimination of lubricating oil changes further reduces the total cost of maintenance over the unit's service life.
body_zh: |
  三菱重工（MHI）宣布研發出一款以氮氣為冷媒的超低溫鹽水冷水機，這家日本企業表示，該機組可在攝氏零下 40 度至零下 100 度的溫度範圍內穩定運作。

  這款產品名為 N-Bleath，其核心技術建立在三菱重工所稱的「空氣冷媒循環」之上——這是一種熱力學方法，該公司表示係歷經多年研究所開發。系統採用無毒、不可燃的氮氣，取代傳統冷水機普遍使用的合成冷媒。

  ## 機組運作原理

  N-Bleath 的核心是一個緊湊型電動渦輪機組。三菱重工表示，該機組結合了高性能葉輪與超高速馬達，充分運用公司在空氣動力學與高速旋轉技術方面長年累積的研發成果。渦輪機採用無油軸承運行，三菱重工指出，這省去了定期更換潤滑油的需求，與傳統系統相比大幅降低維護負擔。

  先進的熱絕緣技術使渦輪機得以在攝氏零下 100 度以下持續運作——亦即機組標稱運作範圍的低溫極限，三菱重工如此表示。

  ## 溫度範圍與應用

  N-Bleath 專為涵蓋攝氏零下 40 度至零下 100 度的超低溫（ULT）應用而設計。這一溫度區間，涵蓋了標準工業冷水機在不引入串聯式冷凍階段的情況下無法觸及的場景，而串聯式冷凍配置通常會增加機械複雜性，並帶來更高的維護負擔。

  ## 產業意義

  採用氮氣作為工作流體，意味著不再依賴氫氟碳化物（HFC）或其他合成冷媒。這類冷媒因具有顯著的全球暖化潛勢，已在多個國家與地區面臨日趨嚴格的法規限制。一款以零全球暖化潛勢的天然冷媒實現超低溫性能的冷水機，同時回應了資料中心及工業冷鏈業者日益迫切的雙重需求：冷卻效能與環保合規。此外，免除潤滑油定期更換作業，更進一步降低機組在整個使用壽命期間的總維護成本。
