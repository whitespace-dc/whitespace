body_en: |
  Amazon Web Services (AWS) has become the first tenant at the Greenland International Industrial Center (GIIC) in Cikarang, West Java, to use treated recycled water for data center cooling, as the estate's operator PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk expands a water reuse program launched in January 2025.

  The GIIC's water recycling facility can process up to 8,000 cubic meters of wastewater per day — equivalent to roughly three Olympic swimming pools — treating approximately 60 percent of effluent from the estate's existing wastewater treatment plant (known in Indonesian as IPAL, or Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah). The recycled water meets standards set by Indonesia's Ministry of Health Regulation No. 2 of 2023 and Government Regulation No. 66 of 2014, the company said.

  Data centers have drawn criticism for large-scale use of potable drinking water in server cooling systems, with environmental critics warning that such consumption can strain local water supplies for surrounding communities.

  ## AWS's water reuse strategy

  Saji PK, AWS's Director for Data Center Operations for the Asia Pacific, Japan, and China (APJC) region, said recycled water is "one of the main strategies" in the company's approach to water stewardship. "We believe that drinking water should be intended for communities and communities that need it most," Saji said, as quoted by Kompas.com. AWS has set a global target to return more water to communities than it consumes in operations by 2030; the company said it has currently reached 75 percent of that target.

  ## Industrial pressure to reduce freshwater withdrawals

  GIIC spans approximately 2,200 hectares and is managed by PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk, a publicly listed Indonesian industrial estate operator. Hongky J. Nantung, the company's President Director, said in a written statement dated August 20, 2026 that tenants have begun adopting the recycled water program since its January 2025 launch. "Sustainability is an inseparable part of GIIC's development strategy," Nantung said.

  The United Nations projects the world could face a gap of up to 40 percent between demand for and supply of clean water by 2030 if consumption patterns do not change, according to Kompas.com. The industrial sector accounts for approximately 19 percent of total global freshwater withdrawals, placing industrial operators under mounting pressure to reduce groundwater and potable water consumption.

  ## What it means for the sector

  The AWS partnership marks the first documented use of GIIC's recycled water supply by a data center operator at the estate, connecting one of the world's largest cloud providers to an industrial-scale water reuse program in Indonesia. The arrangement demonstrates that treated industrial wastewater can serve as a direct operational input for large-scale server cooling, a model with potential to reduce demand on municipal water systems as data center capacity grows.

  AWS said it has reached 75 percent of its 2030 global water-positive target.

  ## AWS成為GIIC工業園區首家使用再生水冷卻資料中心的租戶

  亞馬遜雲端服務（AWS）已成為位於西爪哇芝卡朗的綠地國際工業中心（GIIC）首家以處理後再生水用於資料中心冷卻的租戶。園區營運商PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk正擴大一項自2025年1月啟動的水資源再利用計畫。

  GIIC的水資源回收設施每日最多可處理八千立方公尺的廢水，相當於約三座奧運標準游泳池的水量，處理量約佔園區現有廢水處理廠（印尼語稱IPAL，即Instalasi Pengolahan Air Limbah）流出廢水的六成。該公司表示，回收水質符合印尼衛生部2023年第2號法規及2014年政府第66號法規所訂定的標準。

  資料中心大規模使用自來飲用水作為伺服器冷卻用水，長期以來飽受批評，環保人士警告此類用水需求可能對周邊社區的地方水資源造成嚴重壓力。

  ## AWS的水資源再利用策略

  AWS亞太、日本暨中國（APJC）地區資料中心營運總監Saji PK表示，再生水是該公司水資源管理策略中「最主要的方針之一」。Saji接受《Kompas.com》引述時表示：「我們相信，飲用水應當留給社區，留給最需要它的人。」AWS已設定全球目標，計畫在2030年前向各地社區回補的水量超過其營運所消耗的水量；目前該公司表示已達成此目標的75%。

  ## 工業界降低淡水取用量的壓力

  GIIC佔地約2,200公頃，由印尼上市工業園區營運商PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk管理。該公司董事長Hongky J. Nantung於2026年8月20日發表的書面聲明中表示，自2025年1月計畫啟動以來，已有租戶陸續加入再生水計畫。「永續發展是GIIC整體開發策略中不可分割的一環，」Nantung說道。

  根據《Kompas.com》引述聯合國數據，若消費模式不改變，全球到2030年可能面臨潔淨水需求與供給之間高達40%的缺口。工業部門約佔全球淡水總取用量的19%，使工業園區營運商承受著日益沉重的壓力，必須降低地下水及飲用水的消耗。

  ## 對產業的意義

  AWS與GIIC的合作，是該園區首個有據可查的資料中心業者使用再生水供水的案例，將全球最大雲端服務提供商之一與印尼工業規模水資源再利用計畫正式連結。這項安排證明，經處理的工業廢水可直接作為大型伺服器冷卻系統的運作投入，隨著資料中心容量持續擴張，此模式具有降低市政供水系統需求的潛力。

  AWS表示，其2030年全球正水效益目標目前已達成75%。
