body_en: |
  Refrigerant manufacturer Solstice has signed a licensing agreement allowing A-Gas, a lifecycle refrigerant management company, to recover and reclaim Solstice's patented lower global-warming-potential (GWP) refrigerant blends for resale in the UK and European markets, the companies announced.

  The agreement authorizes A-Gas to process Solstice-patented blends of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) and hydrofluoroolefins (HFOs) — two classes of synthetic refrigerants distinguished by their heat-trapping potency — to AHRI 700 standards, the industry benchmark for reclaimed refrigerant purity set by the Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute. Solstice will supply the HFO components to A-Gas for use in the reclaimed blends.

  The specific refrigerant blends covered by the agreement were not disclosed. According to the announcement, the blends are widely used in supermarket refrigeration systems, convenience-store display cases, cold-storage warehouses, and refrigerated-transport applications including temperature-controlled trucks and containers supporting the retail food cold chain.

  The Cooling Post reported that fellow manufacturer Chemours had previously approved A-Gas as a reclaimer of R448A, a Solstice-patented non-flammable retrofit refrigerant for legacy R404A and R507 systems. The publication said it is thought the new agreement covers Solstice-patented refrigerants including R455A and R454C, which are A2L-class mildly flammable alternatives to R404A; R454B, an alternative to R410A; and R515B, a replacement for R134a. Those identifications were not confirmed by the companies in the announcement.

  ## Reclamation model and supply rationale

  The agreement is designed to expand the supply of compliant refrigerants using reclaimed rather than virgin materials. Reclaimed refrigerant is recovered from existing equipment, reprocessed to purity standards, and returned to market, reducing the need to manufacture new refrigerant from raw inputs.

  A-Gas CEO Jack Govers said the company's lifecycle management model "supports and enables end-of-life gases to be recovered, reclaimed to AHRI 700 standards and returned to the market," adding that the process "helps preserve valuable resources, avoid or minimise emissions and support compliance as the industry adapts to accelerated HFC phase down requirements."

  HFC phase-down requirements refer to regulatory schedules in both the UK and European Union that mandate progressive reductions in the volume of high-GWP fluorinated gases placed on the market, under frameworks derived from the Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol on ozone-depleting substances.

  ## Industry significance

  The deal extends the reclaimed refrigerant supply chain into Solstice's patented lower-GWP blend portfolio at a time when end-users are transitioning away from older, higher-GWP refrigerants under tightening regulations. By licensing reclamation rights, Solstice allows reclaimed product to re-enter the market under its intellectual property framework, while A-Gas gains access to in-demand blends for its reclamation business.

  Solstice's commitment to supply HFO components to A-Gas for blending into reclaimed product indicates the arrangement is structured as a supply partnership, not solely a quality-certification license.

  ## Solstice授權A-Gas在英國及歐洲回收再生低暖化潛勢冷媒

  冷媒製造商Solstice已與冷媒全生命週期管理公司A-Gas簽署授權協議，允許A-Gas回收再生Solstice專利低全球暖化潛勢（GWP）冷媒混合物，並在英國及歐洲市場銷售，兩家公司日前聯合宣布。

  根據協議，A-Gas獲授權將Solstice專利的氫氟碳化物（HFC）與氫氟烯烴（HFO）混合冷媒——這兩類合成冷媒以其截留熱能的強度相互區別——處理至AHRI 700標準，即由空調、暖氣及冷凍協會（Air-Conditioning, Heating, and Refrigeration Institute）所制定的再生冷媒純度業界基準。Solstice並將供應HFO成分給A-Gas，用於再生混合冷媒的調配。

  協議所涵蓋的具體冷媒混合物未予披露。根據公告，這些混合物廣泛應用於超市冷凍系統、便利商店展示冷藏櫃、冷儲倉庫，以及冷凍運輸應用，包括支援零售食品冷鏈的溫控貨車與貨櫃。

  《Cooling Post》報導，同業製造商科慕（Chemours）先前已核准A-Gas為R448A的再生業者，R448A是Solstice專利研發、針對老舊R404A及R507系統的非可燃替代冷媒。該媒體表示，業界推測新協議所涵蓋的Solstice專利冷媒包括R455A與R454C（均為R404A的A2L級微可燃替代品）、R454B（R410A的替代品），以及R515B（R134a的替代品）。不過，上述認定未獲兩家公司在公告中證實。

  ## 回收再生模式與供應理由

  此協議旨在透過使用再生材料而非原生材料，擴大合規冷媒的供應。再生冷媒是從現有設備中回收、重新處理至純度標準後再度投入市場，從而減少以原料製造新冷媒的需求。

  A-Gas執行長傑克·高弗斯（Jack Govers）表示，公司的全生命週期管理模式「支持並推動廢棄冷媒的回收，使其再生至AHRI 700標準後重新進入市場」，並補充指出，這一流程「有助於保護寶貴資源、避免或減少排放，並在業界因應HFC加速削減要求調整之際，協助企業達到合規目標」。

  HFC削減要求是指英國與歐盟的法規時間表，依據《蒙特婁議定書》吉佳利修正案所衍生的框架，強制逐步減少高GWP氟化氣體的市場投放量。

  ## 產業意義

  此協議在終端用戶正因日趨嚴格的法規而轉離高GWP舊型冷媒之際，將再生冷媒供應鏈延伸至Solstice的專利低GWP混合冷媒組合。透過授予再生權利，Solstice得以讓再生產品在其智慧財產框架下重回市場；A-Gas則藉此取得市場需求殷切的混合冷媒，強化其再生業務。

  Solstice承諾向A-Gas供應HFO成分，用於再生產品的調配，顯示此安排不僅是單純的品質認證授權，而是以供應夥伴關係為架構。
