body_en: |
  Samsung Electronics is collaborating with the US Department of Energy's Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) to develop heat pump systems that can operate reliably in ambient temperatures below -30°C, the company said. The research will run through the first half of next year and culminate in field testing under some of the harshest winter conditions in North America.

  The program targets Fairbanks, Alaska, where average monthly lows reach approximately -40°C and historical extremes have dropped to -50°C or below, according to Samsung. Testing will be conducted at the field test lab of the National Laboratory of the Rockies (NLR), located in the city.

  ## Technology under development

  Samsung plans to employ rotary scroll compressors combined with flash injection — a technique in which a portion of refrigerant is injected into an intermediate stage of the compression cycle to boost heating capacity at low ambient temperatures — across a range of HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) product lines.

  The research team intends to verify heating performance, energy efficiency, long-term operational stability, and hot water supply performance during cold-season conditions to determine whether the technology is suitable for residential deployment, Samsung said.

  ## Scope of verification

  The program is designed to validate performance under real-world cryogenic field conditions before broader product rollout. Following field testing in Alaska, Samsung said it plans to apply the technology to various HVAC products, including combined heating and cooling air conditioners and heat pump boilers.

  "This collaboration will be an important opportunity to demonstrate Samsung's unrivaled high-efficiency technology and device reliability in actual cryogenic environments," said Lim Sung-taek, vice president of Samsung Electronics' DA Business Division. "Based on research data, we will introduce heat pump products to the global market that maximise performance and energy efficiency while reducing carbon emissions."

  ## Industry significance

  Cold-climate performance has been a persistent barrier to wider heat pump adoption in high-latitude and continental interior regions. Heat pumps extract heat from outdoor air to warm a building, and their efficiency and output degrade sharply as outdoor temperatures fall. Demonstrating reliable operation below -30°C would extend the viable geographic market for the technology into regions that currently depend on fossil-fuel heating systems.

  Samsung said the research data gathered through the ORNL partnership will inform the development of heat pump products intended for the global market. The company expects the verified technology to be expanded into multiple HVAC product categories.

  ## 三星與美國橡樹嶺國家實驗室攜手，研發零下30°C以下環境可運作的熱泵技術

  三星電子宣布，將與美國能源部旗下的橡樹嶺國家實驗室（ORNL）合作，共同研發能在攝氏零下30度以下低溫環境中穩定運作的熱泵系統。這項研究計畫預計持續到明年上半年，最終將在北美洲氣候條件最為嚴峻的地區進行實地驗證測試。

  根據三星的說明，此計畫的測試地點選定在阿拉斯加州費爾班克斯市——當地每月平均最低氣溫約達攝氏零下40度，歷史極端低溫紀錄更曾跌破零下50度。相關測試將在設於該市的洛磯山脈國家實驗室（NLR）實地測試場進行。

  ## 研發技術概覽

  三星計畫在旗下多個暖通空調（HVAC）產品線中，採用旋轉式渦卷壓縮機搭配「閃射注入」技術。所謂閃射注入，是指在壓縮循環的中間階段注入部分冷媒，藉此在低溫環境下大幅提升供暖能力。

  三星表示，研究團隊將針對寒冷季節條件，全面驗證熱泵的供暖性能、能源效率、長期運作穩定性，以及熱水供應效能，以評估這項技術是否適合作為住宅用途廣泛推廣。

  ## 驗證範疇

  這項計畫的核心目標，是在正式推出更廣泛的商業產品之前，先在真實的極低溫野外環境中驗證技術性能。完成阿拉斯加的實地測試後，三星表示將把這項技術應用於多款暖通空調產品，包括冷暖兩用空調及熱泵鍋爐。

  三星電子DA事業部副總裁林聖澤表示：「此次合作將是一個重要契機，讓我們得以在真實的極低溫環境中，展現三星無可匹敵的高效率技術與設備可靠性。我們將以研究數據為基礎，向全球市場推出能將性能與能源效率最大化、同時降低碳排放的熱泵產品。」

  ## 產業意義

  在高緯度及大陸型內陸地區，寒冷氣候中的性能表現一直是熱泵技術普及的主要障礙。熱泵的運作原理是從室外空氣中擷取熱能，用以加熱室內空間，但隨著室外氣溫下降，其效率與供熱能力也會顯著衰退。若能證明熱泵在攝氏零下30度以下仍可穩定運作，將大幅拓展這項技術的可行市場範疇，使其得以進入目前仍依賴化石燃料供暖系統的地區。

  三星表示，透過與ORNL合作所蒐集的研究數據，將用於開發面向全球市場的熱泵產品。該公司預期，經過驗證的技術將進一步擴展至多個暖通空調產品類別。
