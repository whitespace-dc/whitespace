body_en: |
  Samsung Electronics plans to invest approximately $158 million to establish a dedicated heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) production line in Gwangju, South Korea, targeting the fast-growing market for advanced cooling systems, including those used in artificial intelligence (AI) data centres.

  The facility will occupy 21,500 square metres at Samsung's existing Gwangju operations site and is scheduled to begin production by 2028. It will manufacture HVAC products developed by FläktGroup, a European HVAC manufacturer that Samsung Electronics acquired last year.

  ## Scale and rationale

  The investment represents Samsung's first move to localise FläktGroup production in South Korea following the acquisition. Cheolgi Kim, Samsung Electronics' executive vice president and head of its digital appliances business, described the acquisition as placing the company in "a unique position to expand the HVAC business and transform it into a new growth engine," with a stated goal of becoming a "global top-tier" HVAC company by 2030.

  Samsung did not specify the production capacity of the new line or the particular HVAC product categories it will initially prioritise beyond the reference to AI data centre cooling solutions.

  ## FläktGroup's existing manufacturing footprint

  FläktGroup currently operates a global network of 14 manufacturing facilities and supplies products across key markets including the United States, Europe, and India. Last week, the company inaugurated a new 13,800-square-metre manufacturing plant in India, bringing its recent capacity expansion activity to two continents within a short period.

  The Korean facility, at 21,500 square metres, would be larger than the newly opened Indian plant.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The investment signals that major consumer electronics and appliance manufacturers are moving to capture a share of the data centre infrastructure supply chain, where demand for high-capacity cooling equipment has grown alongside the deployment of power-dense AI computing hardware. By producing FläktGroup equipment domestically in Korea, Samsung positions itself to serve both regional and export markets from a single integrated base, combining its existing manufacturing scale with FläktGroup's established HVAC product lines.

  The Gwangju production line is scheduled to begin operations by 2028.

  ## 三星斥資逾158億美元在光州建HVAC產線，瞄準AI資料中心冷卻市場

  三星電子宣布，計畫投入約1億5,800萬美元，在南韓光州現有廠區內打造一條專屬的暖通空調（HVAC）生產線，搶攻包含人工智慧（AI）資料中心在內、快速成長的高階冷卻系統市場。

  新廠房佔地2萬1,500平方公尺，選址於三星光州現有廠區，預定於2028年以前啟動量產。生產線將製造FläktGroup旗下的HVAC產品——FläktGroup是一家歐洲HVAC製造商，三星電子於去年完成收購。

  ## 投資規模與戰略意圖

  這項投資是三星完成收購後，首度將FläktGroup的生產基地落地南韓本土的重要行動。三星電子執行副總裁暨數位家電事業部門負責人金哲基（Cheolgi Kim）表示，此次收購讓公司「站上獨特的有利位置，得以擴大HVAC事業版圖，並將其打造為新的成長引擎」，並明確揭示目標：在2030年以前躋身「全球頂尖」HVAC企業之列。

  三星方面尚未說明新產線的具體產能，也未進一步透露初期將優先生產哪些HVAC產品類別，僅提及將聚焦於AI資料中心冷卻解決方案。

  ## FläktGroup現有製造版圖

  FläktGroup目前在全球營運14座製造廠，產品供應美國、歐洲及印度等主要市場。就在上週，該公司甫於印度啟用一座面積1萬3,800平方公尺的全新製造廠，意味著其近期的產能擴張已接連遍及兩個大洲。

  相較之下，光州新廠的2萬1,500平方公尺規模，將超越甫落成的印度廠房。

  ## 對產業的意義

  這項投資傳遞出一個明確訊號：主要消費電子與家電製造商正積極布局，爭奪資料中心基礎設施供應鏈的市場份額。隨著高功率密度AI運算硬體持續大規模部署，對大容量冷卻設備的需求也同步攀升。三星選擇在南韓本土生產FläktGroup設備，等於是以單一整合基地同時服務區域市場與出口市場，將自身既有的製造規模與FläktGroup成熟的HVAC產品線緊密結合。

  光州生產線預計於2028年正式投入運營。
