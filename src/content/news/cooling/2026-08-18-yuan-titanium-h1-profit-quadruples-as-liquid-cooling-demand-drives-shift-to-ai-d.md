body_en: |
  Yuan Titanium (元鈦科, ticker 7892), a Taiwan-listed thermal-management and cooling equipment maker, reported first-half 2026 after-tax net profit of NT$261 million and earnings per share of NT$4.35, surpassing the year-earlier EPS by more than fourfold, according to Commercial Times. Cumulative first-half revenue reached NT$971 million, a 2.92-times increase year-over-year, driven by rising demand for liquid cooling systems in AI data centers.

  ## Margin expansion accompanies revenue growth

  Profitability improved alongside the revenue surge. Yuan Titanium's gross margin — the share of revenue left after direct production costs — reached 46.76% in the first half, up 2.67 percentage points from a year earlier. Its operating margin, which strips out overhead expenses, reached 32.18%, a 15.25-percentage-point improvement year-over-year. Institutional analysts cited by Commercial Times attributed the margin gains partly to the higher complexity of AI-related system integration projects compared with earlier PC server-room work, noting that more intricate projects carry better margins.

  ## Revenue mix tilts toward system integration

  The company's business model is shifting away from selling standalone liquid cooling hardware toward AI data center system integration — projects where Yuan Titanium designs, installs, and maintains the full cooling, electrical, and mechanical infrastructure of an AI computing facility. Institutional analysts expect full-year 2026 system integration revenue to exceed revenue from IT equipment for the first time, continuing a trend already visible in the first quarter, when system integration accounted for roughly 60% of total revenue.

  Revenue recognition from AI demonstration factory projects contributed to the acceleration, the report noted.

  ## Why AI data centers require higher-complexity integration

  High-density AI servers present integration challenges that conventional data center builds do not. According to Commercial Times, high-power AI servers require simultaneous planning of liquid cooling, power supply, and structural load-bearing capacity. Some high-density servers and CDUs — cooling distribution units, the devices that circulate and distribute liquid coolant to servers — exceed standard floor load limits, requiring custom steel frames to spread the weight. As a result, electrical, mechanical, and thermal systems must be engineered as a single coordinated system rather than separately, raising project complexity and, according to analysts, margins.

  On the equipment side, the company said it is enhancing CDU cooling capacity and long-duration reliability, including design improvements to pumps, heat-exchange components, and internal piping.

  ## Integration business feeds internal equipment demand

  Yuan Titanium said it intends to allocate more resources to system integration project planning rather than standalone CDU or water-to-air liquid cooling hardware sales. Because the system integration division procures equipment from the company's own IT equipment business unit when executing projects, growth in system integration work is expected to generate additional internal demand for liquid cooling hardware.

  Analysts also noted that AI sites in Taiwan using third-party or competing-brand equipment may still require independent parties for installation and ongoing maintenance, representing a potential additional revenue stream for Yuan Titanium.

  ## Significance for the cooling sector

  The results illustrate how liquid cooling demand from AI data centers is reshaping the economics of thermal-management suppliers: companies able to move up the value chain from component sales into full-facility integration capture higher margins and more durable project-based revenue. Yuan Titanium's institutional analysts project that system integration will remain the primary growth driver through the remainder of 2026, supported by continued expansion of AI demonstration factory projects.
body_zh: |
  元鈦科（元鈦科，股票代號7892）為台灣上市的散熱管理暨冷卻設備廠商。根據《工商時報》報導，該公司公告2026年上半年稅後淨利2.61億元，每股盈餘（EPS）4.35元，較去年同期成長逾四倍。上半年累計營收達9.71億元，年增幅高達2.92倍，主要受AI資料中心液冷系統需求持續升溫所帶動。

  ## 毛利率隨營收擴張同步提升

  在營收大幅成長的同時，元鈦科的獲利能力也同步改善。今年上半年，公司毛利率——即扣除直接生產成本後的營收占比——達46.76%，較去年同期提升2.67個百分點；營業利益率（進一步扣除管銷費用後）則達32.18%，年增幅高達15.25個百分點。《工商時報》引述法人分析師指出，毛利率提升的部分原因在於，AI相關系統整合專案的複雜度遠高於過去的PC伺服器機房工程，複雜度較高的專案通常可帶來更佳的利潤空間。

  ## 營收結構轉向系統整合

  元鈦科的商業模式正逐步從銷售單一液冷硬體，轉型為AI資料中心系統整合——亦即由元鈦科統籌規劃、建置並維護AI運算設施的完整冷卻、電力與機械基礎設施。法人分析師預期，2026年全年系統整合業務營收將首度超越IT設備業務，延續第一季已可觀察到的趨勢——第一季系統整合占整體營收比重已達約六成。

  報導也指出，AI示範工廠專案的收入認列，同樣為上半年業績的加速成長提供了貢獻。

  ## AI資料中心為何需要高複雜度整合

  高密度AI伺服器所帶來的整合挑戰，是傳統資料中心建置所不具備的。根據《工商時報》報導，高功率AI伺服器需要同步規劃液冷系統、電力供應與結構承重能力。部分高密度伺服器及CDU（冷卻分配單元，即負責循環分配液態冷媒至伺服器的裝置）的重量超過標準地板承載限制，必須訂製鋼架以分散重量。因此，電力、機械與散熱系統必須作為單一協調整合的系統進行工程規劃，而非各自獨立施工，進而提升了專案複雜度，也帶動了毛利率——此為分析師的共同看法。

  在設備端，元鈦科表示正持續提升CDU的冷卻能力與長效可靠性，具體措施包括針對幫浦、熱交換元件及內部管路進行設計改良。

  ## 整合業務帶動內部設備需求

  元鈦科表示，公司計畫將更多資源投入系統整合專案的規劃，而非單獨銷售CDU或水冷式液冷硬體。由於系統整合部門在執行專案時，會向公司旗下IT設備事業部採購所需設備，因此系統整合業務的成長，預計將為液冷硬體帶來額外的內部需求。

  法人分析師也指出，台灣採用第三方或競品設備的AI場域，仍可能需要獨立第三方負責安裝與日常維護，這對元鈦科而言代表潛在的額外營收來源。

  ## 對冷卻產業的意義

  元鈦科的業績充分說明，AI資料中心的液冷需求正在重塑散熱管理供應商的經營生態：能夠從零組件銷售向上攀升至整廠整合的業者，得以享有更高的毛利率，並取得更具持續性的專案型營收。元鈦科的法人分析師預測，在AI示範工廠專案持續擴張的支撐下，系統整合業務將持續作為2026年下半年的主要成長動能。
