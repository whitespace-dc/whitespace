body_en: |
  The thermal engineering community has recognized for decades that using the junction-to-air thermal resistance parameter θJA (theta JA) to calculate electronic component junction temperatures produces erroneous results in most real-world applications, yet component datasheets and industry standards continue to propagate the practice, according to Electronics Cooling.

  The widely taught formula — TJ = TA + Q̇·θJA, where TJ is junction temperature, TA is ambient temperature, and Q̇ is the component's heat dissipation — is presented to electrical and reliability engineers as applicable to any electronics packaging configuration. The thermal community has challenged that assumption through multiple bodies of research spanning decades, the publication states.

  ## How JEDEC Measures θJA

  The problem begins with the test conditions under which θJA is measured. JEDEC (Joint Electron Device Engineering Council), the semiconductor standards body, defines the measurement methodology in standard JESD51-2A: the test is performed in a sealed enclosure with interior dimensions of 305 mm x 305 mm x 305 mm, constructed from materials with thermal conductivity below 0.5 W/m/K and with high surface emissivity. The test board sits horizontally at the center of the enclosure under ambient temperatures between 15°C and 30°C, with cooling occurring solely through natural convection generated by the component itself. A companion standard, JESD51-6, covers the equivalent methodology under forced airflow.

  Because θJA reflects the behavior of a specific test setup rather than an intrinsic property of the component, it is not a constant. Printed circuit board design, chip and pad size, package configuration, and altitude all influence the measured value, according to Electronics Cooling.

  ## Substrate Mismatch

  Real circuit board designs differ from the JEDEC standard test board in size, surface area, and layer count. Since the test board used in a datasheet measurement is unlikely to match any given production PCB, the θJA value published in a component datasheet does not directly apply to that application.

  ## Heat Transfer Path

  The JESD51-2A measurement relies on free convection and radiation from the component substrate to surrounding air as the primary cooling mechanisms. In many electronics assemblies, the dominant heat path is conduction into the board and then into a primary heatsink, an arrangement referred to as conduction-cooled, with forced or free convection occurring only at the enclosure level. In sealed assemblies without ventilation or perforations, convective heat removal from the substrate surface is minimal, making it a secondary rather than primary mechanism.

  Electronics Cooling states that a well-designed conduction-cooled, unventilated assembly should not depend on convective and radiative cooling from the substrate to demonstrate adequate temperature margins. Top-cooling architectures, which draw heat away from the component and substrate directly, further undermine the applicability of θJA, the publication notes.

  ## Ambient Temperature Definition

  The TA term in the formula specifies the temperature of air in direct contact with the component and its circuit board, not the external environmental temperature. In enclosed assemblies without ventilation, internal air temperature exceeds external ambient because heat from dissipating components accumulates inside the enclosure. Substituting external ambient temperature for TA therefore underestimates junction temperature.

  Electronics Cooling presents two illustrative cases. In a desktop personal computer, the relevant TA is not room temperature (approximately 20°C) but the elevated internal air temperature produced by the system's own heat dissipation. In a sealed avionics unit with no cabin air entering the interior, TA is the temperature of stagnant internal air, which varies with location inside the enclosure and can be determined only through measurement or detailed thermal simulation.

  ## Industry Implications

  Determining an accurate TA value typically requires either a detailed thermal simulation or physical test measurement, adding the very complexity the simplified θJA formula was intended to avoid. For engineers working with conduction-cooled assemblies, Electronics Cooling states that sound engineering practice calls for designing mechanical conduction paths capable of keeping all components below their temperature limits — using manufacturer-rated values or derated thresholds depending on the application — without relying on substrate-level convection and radiation. Component datasheets and some industry standards continue to present θJA as a valid tool for junction temperature estimation, a practice the publication identifies as an ongoing source of erroneous thermal analysis in electronics design.

  ## JEDEC測試條件使θJA無法可靠預測接面溫度

  熱工程界數十年來已普遍認知：以接面至空氣熱阻參數θJA（theta JA）來計算電子元件接面溫度，在絕大多數實際應用中都會產生錯誤結果。然而，根據《Electronics Cooling》的報導，元件規格書與業界標準至今仍繼續推廣這種做法。

  這個廣泛流傳的公式——TJ = TA + Q̇·θJA，其中TJ為接面溫度、TA為環境溫度、Q̇為元件散熱量——長期以來被呈現給電氣工程師與可靠度工程師，視為適用於任何電子封裝配置的通用準則。該刊物指出，熱工程學界已透過數十年來的多項研究，對這項假設提出挑戰。

  ## JEDEC如何量測θJA

  問題的根源在於θJA的量測條件。半導體標準機構JEDEC（聯合電子元件工程委員會）在標準JESD51-2A中定義了量測方法：測試須在一個密封腔體內進行，內部尺寸為305毫米×305毫米×305毫米，腔體材料的熱導率須低於0.5 W/m/K，並具有高表面輻射率。測試板水平放置於腔體中央，環境溫度維持在攝氏15度至30度之間，冷卻方式僅靠元件本身產生的自然對流。另有配套標準JESD51-6，涵蓋強制氣流條件下的等效量測方法。

  由於θJA反映的是特定測試裝置的行為，而非元件本身的固有特性，因此它並非一個常數。《Electronics Cooling》指出，印刷電路板設計、晶片與焊墊尺寸、封裝形式，乃至海拔高度，都會影響量測結果。

  ## 基板不匹配問題

  實際電路板設計在尺寸、表面積與層數上，均與JEDEC標準測試板存在差異。由於規格書量測所用的測試板，幾乎不可能與任何量產PCB完全相同，因此元件規格書所公布的θJA值，並不能直接套用於實際應用。

  ## 熱傳路徑問題

  JESD51-2A的量測方式，以元件基板向周圍空氣的自由對流與輻射作為主要冷卻機制。然而在許多電子組件中，主要的散熱路徑是透過傳導進入板材，再傳導至主散熱器——這種方式稱為傳導冷卻——對流與輻射冷卻僅發生在機箱層級。在無通風口或穿孔的密封組件中，基板表面的對流散熱量極為有限，頂多只是輔助機制，而非主要手段。

  《Electronics Cooling》指出，一個設計良好的傳導冷卻密封組件，不應依賴基板的對流與輻射冷卻來達到足夠的溫度裕量。該刊物亦特別指出，頂部冷卻架構——直接從元件與基板頂部導走熱量——進一步削弱了θJA的適用性。

  ## 環境溫度定義問題

  公式中的TA項，指的是直接接觸元件及其電路板的空氣溫度，而非外部環境溫度。在無通風的密封組件中，由於內部散熱元件的熱量不斷積累，內部空氣溫度會高於外部環境溫度。因此，若以外部環境溫度代入TA，將會低估接面溫度。

  《Electronics Cooling》舉出兩個具體案例加以說明。在桌上型個人電腦中，相關的TA並非室溫（約攝氏20度），而是系統本身散熱所形成的較高內部空氣溫度。在無艙內空氣進入的密封航電裝置中，TA是靜止內部空氣的溫度，該溫度因機箱內部位置不同而有所差異，只能透過實際量測或詳細熱模擬才能確定。

  ## 業界影響

  準確取得TA值，通常需要進行詳細的熱模擬或實體測試量測，這反而引入了原本使用簡化θJA公式所欲避免的複雜度。對於從事傳導冷卻組件設計的工程師，《Electronics Cooling》指出，健全的工程實務應著眼於設計具有足夠能力的機械傳導路徑，使所有元件溫度維持在其溫度限制之下——依應用情境使用製造商額定值或降額後的門檻值——而不應依賴基板層級的對流與輻射冷卻。元件規格書與部分業界標準至今仍將θJA呈現為有效的接面溫度估算工具，該刊物將此定性為電子設計熱分析中持續錯誤的根源之一。
