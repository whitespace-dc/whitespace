body_en: |
  Amazon's data centers consumed 2.5 billion gallons of water in 2025, the company disclosed in a blog post, as a growing anti-data center movement has made water use by artificial intelligence infrastructure a focus of public concern.

  ## Withdrawal fell at owned sites despite expansion

  At the facilities Amazon Web Services (AWS) owns and operates directly, total water withdrawal decreased two percent from 2024 to 2025, even as the number of data centers increased. Amazon did not disclose whether water usage changed at the leased properties that form the other half of its real estate footprint.

  AWS owned approximately 28.37 million square feet (2.64 million square meters) of data center and office space in 2025 and leased a further 28.47 million square feet (2.64 million square meters), according to a previous 10-K filing.

  ## Efficiency figures and methodology limits

  Amazon said its global data center operations used 0.12 liters of water per kilowatt-hour (L/kWh) in 2025, which it claimed was seven times more efficient than an industry average of 0.84 L/kWh.

  The company published a graphic comparing its figure against rival hyperscalers — large-scale cloud infrastructure providers — but the chart measured Amazon's entire data center fleet against what it identified as Google's more power-intensive AI-specific facilities, not Google's full fleet.

  The 2.5 billion gallon figure also excludes indirect water consumption at the power plants that supply Amazon's facilities and does not account for water used during construction. Those omissions limit direct comparisons with figures from other operators.

  Amazon said its data centers rely on free air cooling — circulating ambient outdoor air rather than chilled water — approximately 90 percent of the time.

  ## Site-level disclosure lags rivals

  Google and Meta publish water usage data for individual facilities. Amazon provided no site-specific figures. The company said it operates 26 facilities using 100 percent reclaimed water and has contracted for 130 more globally, but did not report what share of the total 2.5 billion gallons came from reclaimed sources.

  ## Scale in context

  At 2.5 billion gallons, Amazon's reported consumption is substantially smaller than other large water-using sectors. California's almond orchards consume between 1.3 and 1.6 trillion gallons of water annually, and the US livestock and beef industry withdraws approximately 4.5 trillion gallons per year. A frequently cited distinction is that much of the data center industry draws on potable — drinking-quality — water rather than agricultural or recycled supplies, a factor that raises concern in water-stressed communities near data center sites.

  ## Progress toward 2030 target and industry context

  Amazon reiterated its goal to become water positive by 2030, meaning it aims to return more water to local watersheds than its operations consume. The company said it is approximately 75 percent of the way toward that commitment, without specifying the metrics used to calculate progress.

  The disclosure arrived as organized opposition to data center development has grown across multiple regions, with AI-related water use cited as a central concern. While the issue has drawn widespread attention, some public representations of data center water consumption have been characterized as inflated and unrealistic.

  Amazon's 130 contracted reclaimed-water facilities globally represent the stated mechanism for closing the remaining gap toward its 2030 water-positive target.
body_zh: |
  亞馬遜旗下資料中心2025年共消耗25億加侖水資源，公司透過一篇部落格文章公開揭露此數據。此時反資料中心運動持續壯大，人工智慧基礎設施的用水問題已成為社會大眾關注的焦點。

  ## 自有設施取水量下降，儘管規模持續擴張

  在亞馬遜雲端運算服務（AWS）直接擁有並自行營運的設施中，2025年的總取水量較2024年減少2%，儘管同期資料中心數量持續增加。亞馬遜並未揭露其租賃物業的用水是否有所變化，而租賃物業約占其不動產總面積的另一半。

  根據此前提交的10-K年報，AWS在2025年自有的資料中心與辦公空間面積約為2,837萬平方英尺（264萬平方公尺），另租賃2,847萬平方英尺（264萬平方公尺）的空間。

  ## 效率數據與方法論的局限性

  亞馬遜表示，其全球資料中心營運的用水效率為每千瓦時0.12公升（L/kWh），並宣稱其效率是業界平均值0.84 L/kWh的七倍。

  公司發布了一張圖表，將自身數據與其他超大規模雲端業者——即大型雲端基礎設施供應商——進行比較；然而，該圖表是以亞馬遜的整體資料中心機群，對比Google旗下耗電量較高的AI專用設施，而非Google的完整機群。

  25億加侖的數字亦未納入供電給亞馬遜設施之發電廠的間接耗水量，也未計入建設施工期間的用水量。這些遺漏限制了與其他業者數字之間的直接比較。

  亞馬遜表示，其資料中心約有90%的時間仰賴自然空氣冷卻——即透過循環環境外氣來散熱，而非使用冷卻水。

  ## 個別設施揭露程度落後同業

  Google與Meta均公布各個別設施的用水數據，亞馬遜則未提供任何具體設施的相關資訊。該公司表示目前有26座設施全數使用100%再生水，並已在全球簽約新增130座，但未說明25億加侖總用水量中，再生水究竟占多少比例。

  ## 數字的規模脈絡

  就25億加侖的用水量而言，亞馬遜公布的數字遠低於其他大型用水產業。加州杏仁果園每年耗水量介於1.3兆至1.6兆加侖之間，美國畜牧與牛肉產業每年取水量則約達4.5兆加侖。各界常提及的一項重要差異在於，資料中心業界大多取用符合飲用水標準的自來水，而非農業用水或再生水，此一因素使資料中心鄰近缺水社區的居民更加憂慮。

  ## 邁向2030年目標的進展與產業背景

  亞馬遜重申其目標：在2030年前達成「水資源正效益」，意即向當地集水區回補的水量，將超過其營運所消耗的水量。公司表示目前進度已達承諾目標的約75%，但未說明計算進度所使用的具體指標。

  此次資訊揭露之際，各地反對資料中心開發的組織化行動持續升溫，AI相關的用水問題被視為核心爭議之一。儘管此議題已廣受關注，部分對資料中心耗水量的公開描述，已被認定為誇大且不切實際。

  亞馬遜在全球已簽約的130座再生水設施，正是該公司宣示用以彌補2030年達成水資源正效益承諾缺口的主要機制。
