body_en: |
  Safety buffers added independently by chipmakers, server manufacturers, customers and colocation operators are compounding to keep data centres cooler than chip specifications require, constraining the potential to use ambient conditions for free cooling, Schneider Electric said.

  Tuan Hoang, head of cooling technology and product development at Schneider Electric, made the remarks during a visit to the company's Conselve cooling manufacturing facility in northern Italy. His comments came alongside an announcement of expanded liquid cooling production at the site.

  ## How margins accumulate

  Silicon makers specify that processors can be cooled with liquid supplied at temperatures up to 45°C. Server original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), which must guarantee system performance, design to a few degrees below that ceiling. Customers, concerned about voiding warranties, apply a further margin. Colocation operators, whose service-level agreements (SLAs) carry financial penalties for failures, add another layer still.

  "By the time the data centre operates, there may be three to five layers of buffer," Hoang said. Each additional buffer lowers the system's design set point — the target operating temperature — and with it the viability of free cooling.

  Free cooling is the ability to reject heat from a data centre using outdoor ambient air or water temperatures rather than mechanical refrigeration. The higher the permissible coolant supply temperature, the more hours per year a facility can rely on ambient conditions rather than energy-intensive cooling plant.

  ## The case for transparency

  Hoang said the solution is not to mandate higher operating temperatures but to improve transparency and education about risk. He said he wants the industry to recognise the margins already embedded in its specifications and make an informed decision about where it is comfortable operating. "The more comfortable people get, the more educated, the more optimised the solution might be," he said.

  ## Closed loop cooling and water use

  Hoang also pushed back on the perception that closed-loop cooling — in which coolant circulates in a sealed circuit without being consumed — is a niche choice, arguing that virtually all data centre cooling already operates this way.

  He used an automotive analogy: a traditional air-cooled data centre resembles a vintage Volkswagen Beetle with an air-cooled engine, while a modern liquid-cooled facility resembles a car with a conventional radiator that rejects heat to the atmosphere without consuming the coolant. Water is consumed only when operators choose evaporative cooling — the equivalent, in his analogy, of spraying water on the radiator to accelerate heat rejection.

  "You could if you wanted to, but you don't have to," Hoang said. Even facilities that use cooling towers operate closed primary loops, he said; water consumption arises from the deliberate choice of evaporation over moving more air.

  The distinction has become significant as public scrutiny of data centre water and power use grows. Hoang said the public is "no longer passive" about new developments and called for direct conversations between operators, developers and local communities about what a facility means locally. Schneider, which does not own or operate data centres, said it publishes white papers on water and power use to inform that debate.

  ## Conselve expansion

  The remarks came alongside Schneider's announcement that it will double coolant distribution unit (CDU) manufacturing capacity at Conselve from January 2027. A CDU is the hardware unit that circulates liquid coolant between a facility's central cooling plant and individual server racks. Conselve is Schneider's largest cooling manufacturing facility in Europe and is the site of its CDU production following the company's acquisition of Motivair.

  Large chiller manufacturing capacity at the site will increase by 50% by the end of 2026. A new test laboratory, due to open in December 2027, will triple the facility's testing capacity and will be equipped to validate CDUs rated up to 5 megawatts (MW) and chillers rated up to 4 MW.

  CDUs are now produced in floor space that previously housed air cooling equipment manufacturing. Air cooling has not been discontinued: Hoang said electrical infrastructure and the air-side components of servers continue to require it.

  Engineering expertise from Motivair's North American team is being extended to Conselve and to Schneider's other design centres. The company said regional manufacturing reduces shipping distances and supports business continuity.

  ## Significance

  The compounding-margin problem Hoang describes is structural: each actor in the supply chain is responding rationally to its own contractual exposure, but the cumulative effect is a system-wide reduction in the ceiling temperature at which data centres are willing to operate — and therefore in the share of hours each year when mechanical cooling can be bypassed entirely. Closing that gap does not require new technology; it requires coordination across parties that currently set their margins independently.

  The Conselve expansion, with CDU output doubling from January 2027 and the new test laboratory scheduled for December 2027, positions Schneider to scale liquid cooling supply as hyperscale and AI-driven deployments push more heat loads toward direct liquid cooling of processors.
body_zh: |
  施耐德電氣表示，晶片製造商、伺服器廠商、客戶及主機代管業者各自獨立加入的安全緩衝值，層層疊加後使資料中心的運作溫度持續低於晶片規格所允許的上限，進而制約了利用環境條件進行自由冷卻的潛力。

  施耐德電氣冷卻技術與產品開發主管 Tuan Hoang，在視察公司位於義大利北部康塞爾韋（Conselve）冷卻設備製造廠期間發表了上述看法，並伴隨著該廠液冷產能擴充的宣布一同揭露。

  ## 安全餘裕如何層層累積

  晶片製造商規範處理器可使用最高攝氏45度的液態冷卻液進行散熱。伺服器原始設備製造商（OEM）為確保系統效能，設計時會比該上限再低幾度。客戶為避免保固失效，再加入一層餘裕。主機代管業者的服務水準協議（SLA）一旦發生故障便需承擔財務懲罰，於是又疊上另一層緩衝。

  Hoang 表示：「等到資料中心正式運作時，可能已經有三到五層緩衝值疊加在其中。」每增加一層緩衝，就會壓低系統的設計設定點——即目標運作溫度——連帶降低自由冷卻的可行性。

  所謂自由冷卻，是指利用戶外環境空氣或水溫，取代機械製冷系統來排除資料中心熱量的能力。允許的冷卻液供應溫度越高，設施每年得以仰賴環境條件、無須啟動高耗能冷卻機組的時數就越多。

  ## 透明化的必要性

  Hoang 表示，解方並非強制提高運作溫度，而是提升透明度並加強教育。他希望業界能認識到各項規格中早已內嵌的安全餘裕，並就自身願意在哪個溫度範圍內運作做出知情決策。「大家越是熟悉、越是了解，解決方案就可能越優化，」他說。

  ## 密閉循環冷卻與用水

  Hoang 也反駁了外界認為密閉循環冷卻屬於小眾選擇的觀感——所謂密閉循環冷卻，是指冷卻液在封閉迴路中循環而不耗損。他主張，幾乎所有資料中心冷卻系統本來就已以這種方式運作。

  他以汽車做比喻：傳統氣冷式資料中心猶如採用氣冷引擎的老款福斯金龜車，而現代液冷設施則像裝有傳統水箱的汽車，將熱量散逸至大氣中，同時不消耗冷卻液。唯有當業者選擇蒸發式冷卻時，才會產生水資源消耗——以他的比喻來說，這就相當於在水箱外噴水以加速散熱。

  「你可以這麼做，但不是非這麼做不可，」Hoang 說。他表示，即使採用冷卻水塔的設施，主要冷卻迴路也是密閉循環；用水量的產生，源自業者主動選擇蒸發散熱而非加大風量的決定。

  隨著外界對資料中心用水與耗電的關注日益升溫，這項區別顯得格外重要。Hoang 表示，民眾對新建設施「不再是旁觀者」，並呼籲業者、開發商與在地社區就設施對當地的實際影響展開直接對話。施耐德電氣本身不擁有或經營資料中心，該公司表示會發布有關用水與耗電的白皮書，以充實此一公共討論的知識基礎。

  ## 康塞爾韋廠擴建

  上述言論伴隨著施耐德宣布，將於2027年1月起將康塞爾韋廠的冷卻液分配裝置（CDU）製造產能擴增一倍。CDU 是在設施中央冷卻機組與各伺服器機架之間循環液態冷卻液的硬體設備。康塞爾韋廠是施耐德電氣在歐洲規模最大的冷卻設備製造廠，也是該公司收購 Motivair 後 CDU 的生產基地。

  該廠大型冷水機組製造產能將於2026年底前提升50%。一座預計於2027年12月啟用的新測試實驗室，將使廠內測試產能擴增為三倍，並具備驗證額定功率達5百萬瓦（MW）CDU及額定4 MW冷水機組的能力。

  CDU 目前在原本用於生產氣冷設備的廠區空間內製造。氣冷業務並未因此停止：Hoang 表示，伺服器的電氣基礎設施與氣側元件仍需依賴氣冷技術。

  Motivar 北美團隊的工程專業正逐步延伸至康塞爾韋廠及施耐德電氣的其他設計中心。該公司表示，在地化製造可縮短運輸距離，並有助於強化營運連續性。

  ## 意義

  Hoang 所描述的層層疊加安全餘裕問題具有結構性：供應鏈中的每個行為者都在理性回應自身的合約義務，但累積效應卻導致全產業資料中心願意運作的系統設計溫度上限系統性地下移，進而壓縮了每年得以完全略過機械冷卻的時數比例。縮小這道差距不需要新技術，而需要各方跨越當前各自為政設定安全餘裕的格局，建立跨組織的協調機制。

  隨著超大規模與 AI 驅動的部署將更多熱負載推向處理器直接液冷，康塞爾韋廠此番擴建——CDU 產能將於2027年1月起倍增，新測試實驗室預計於2027年12月落成——為施耐德電氣擴大液冷供應規模奠定了基礎。
