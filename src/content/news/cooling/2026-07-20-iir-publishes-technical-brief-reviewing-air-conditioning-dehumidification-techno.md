body_en: |
  The International Institute of Refrigeration (IIR), a Paris-based intergovernmental organization focused on refrigeration science, has published a 22-page technical brief titled *Dehumidification in Air Conditioning*, reviewing the major technologies used to remove moisture from air in conditioning systems and evaluating how well each meets the demands of different applications.

  The document covers three broad categories of dehumidification technology: condensation-based systems, desiccant systems (which use moisture-absorbing materials to extract water vapor), and emerging approaches not yet widely deployed. For each category, the IIR brief assesses operational efficiency, current market penetration, practical applicability, and the ability to serve diverse sectoral needs.

  ## What the brief covers

  The IIR states that the report's purpose is to provide a reference for selecting the most appropriate dehumidification strategy across a range of scenarios. By comparing the operational characteristics and real-world applications of the three technology classes, the document is intended to help engineers, planners, and policymakers identify which approach best fits a given context.

  Dehumidification is a core function of air conditioning in many climates and building types, where controlling humidity is as operationally significant as controlling temperature. Condensation dehumidification — the process by which moisture in air condenses on a cooled surface and is drained away — is the mechanism underlying most conventional air conditioning equipment. Desiccant dehumidification uses hygroscopic (moisture-attracting) materials, either solid or liquid, to adsorb or absorb water vapor directly from an airstream, often without the need to cool air below its dew point.

  ## Significance for the industry

  The publication adds a structured comparative resource to a field where technology selection has significant implications for energy consumption and system design. The inclusion of emerging technologies alongside established condensation and desiccant methods signals IIR's intent to map the full current landscape rather than focus solely on dominant approaches.

  The brief is available for download from the IIR website.
body_zh: |
  ## 國際製冷學會發布技術簡報　全面檢視空調除濕技術

  總部設於巴黎、專注於製冷科學研究的政府間國際組織——國際製冷學會（International Institute of Refrigeration，IIR）——近日正式發布一份題為《空調除濕》（*Dehumidification in Air Conditioning*）的22頁技術簡報。這份文件系統性地梳理了空調系統中用於去除空氣水分的主要技術，並評估各項技術在不同應用情境下的適用程度。

  本文件涵蓋三大類除濕技術：冷凝式系統、乾燥劑系統（利用吸濕材料直接萃取空氣中的水蒸氣），以及尚未廣泛普及的新興技術。針對每一類技術，IIR 簡報均從運作效率、目前市場滲透率、實際適用性，以及服務不同產業需求的能力等面向進行評估。

  ## 簡報涵蓋內容

  IIR 表示，這份報告的目的在於提供一份參考資源，協助在各種情境下選擇最合適的除濕策略。透過比較三大技術類別的運作特性與實際應用案例，本文件旨在幫助工程師、規劃人員及政策制定者，判斷哪一種技術最符合特定情境的需求。

  在許多氣候環境與建築類型中，除濕是空調系統的核心功能，對濕度的控制與對溫度的控制同等重要。冷凝式除濕是最常見的傳統空調設備所採用的機制，其原理是讓空氣中的水分在冷卻表面上凝結，再將凝結水排出。乾燥劑除濕則是利用固態或液態的吸濕材料（即親水性材料），直接從氣流中吸附或吸收水蒸氣，通常無需將空氣冷卻至露點以下。

  ## 對產業的重要意義

  這份簡報的出版，為技術選擇對能耗與系統設計具有深遠影響的除濕領域，提供了一份結構完整的比較性資源。將新興技術與成熟的冷凝及乾燥劑技術並列討論，顯示 IIR 有意全面呈現當前的技術全貌，而非僅聚焦於主流技術。

  本技術簡報已可於 IIR 官方網站免費下載。
