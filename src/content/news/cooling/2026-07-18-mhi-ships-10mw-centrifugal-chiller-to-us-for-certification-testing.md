body_en: |
  Mitsubishi Heavy Industries (MHI) has dispatched a 10-megawatt (MW) centrifugal chiller test unit to the United States, the company said, in a move it described as a key commercialisation milestone for its artificial intelligence data centre cooling strategy.

  The unit is scheduled to arrive at the Port of Brunswick, Georgia this month. MHI said it plans to obtain US safety and regulatory certification — including certification from Underwriters Laboratories (UL), an independent product-safety organisation — potentially as early as 2026, with commercial availability targeted after that approval is secured.

  ## The chiller and its role in MHI's modular system

  The 10 MW chiller — a machine that removes heat from water or another fluid by compressing refrigerant through a centrifugal mechanism — serves as the core cooling component of MHI's Modular Chiller Plant (MCP). The MCP is a pre-engineered system that integrates the chiller together with pumps, heat exchangers, and controls into a factory-built modular architecture designed to be shipped and deployed as a unit.

  MHI said the MCP is designed to support NVIDIA DSX-aligned cooling architectures. NVIDIA DSX is NVIDIA's AI factory-scale platform that unifies design, simulation, operations, and ecosystem technologies. The MCP is also intended to support scalable liquid-cooled AI deployments while simplifying on-site installation and enabling operators to expand capacity incrementally.

  ## Efficiency and water use claims

  MHI said the MCP architecture enables effective use of free-cooling operation modes — a technique in which ambient outdoor conditions do some or all of the cooling work without mechanical refrigeration, reducing energy consumption — and improved power usage effectiveness (PUE). PUE is an industry metric that expresses how much of a facility's total energy consumption goes to IT equipment rather than support systems such as cooling; a lower PUE indicates greater efficiency.

  The system uses a closed-loop configuration, meaning water or coolant circulates within the system rather than being consumed or evaporated, which MHI said addresses growing concerns around water scarcity and water usage effectiveness (WUE), a related metric tracking how much water a data centre consumes per unit of IT energy.

  MHI noted that the MCP itself is currently undergoing US safety and regulatory certification, including UL certification, in parallel with the chiller unit's testing programme.

  ## Industry significance

  The shipment positions MHI to enter the US market for high-capacity data centre cooling at a time when AI workloads are driving demand for denser, more power-intensive server configurations that require liquid cooling rather than traditional air cooling. Obtaining UL certification is a prerequisite for commercial deployment in most US facilities, making the 2026 target date the critical near-term milestone for MHI's US market entry.

  該機組預計本月抵達喬治亞州布倫瑞克港。三菱重工表示，計劃在取得商業上市許可之前，先取得美國安全及法規認證，其中包括獨立產品安全機構「保險商實驗室」（Underwriters Laboratories，UL）的認證，最快可望於2026年完成，商業銷售則於認證通過後隨即展開。

  ## 冷凍機及其在三菱重工模組化系統中的角色

  這台10 MW離心式冷凍機——透過離心機制壓縮冷媒，從水或其他流體中移除熱能的裝置——是三菱重工「模組化冷凍廠」（Modular Chiller Plant，MCP）系統的核心冷卻元件。MCP是一套預先工程設計的系統，將冷凍機與泵浦、熱交換器及控制系統整合為工廠預組的模組化架構，設計上可作為整體單元進行運輸和現場部署。

  三菱重工表示，MCP的設計支援對齊輝達DSX的冷卻架構。輝達DSX是輝達的AI工廠規模平台，整合了設計、模擬、營運及生態系統等各項技術。MCP同時支援可擴展的液冷AI部署，能夠簡化現場安裝流程，並讓營運商得以視需求逐步擴增容量。

  ## 效能表現與用水主張

  三菱重工表示，MCP架構能夠有效運用「自然冷卻」操作模式——一種利用戶外自然環境條件部分或全部替代機械製冷、進而降低能耗的技術——並藉此提升電能使用效率（PUE）。PUE是業界衡量資料中心能源效率的標準指標，反映設施總能耗中有多少比例實際用於IT設備，而非用於冷卻等支援系統；PUE數值愈低，代表效率愈高。

  該系統採用封閉迴路設計，亦即水或冷卻液在系統內部循環，不會被消耗或蒸發散逸。三菱重工表示，此設計能有效回應外界對水資源短缺日益高漲的關切，同時改善「用水效率」（WUE）——這是另一項追蹤資料中心每單位IT能源消耗多少水量的相關指標。

  三菱重工指出，MCP本身目前正與冷凍機的測試計畫同步進行美國安全及法規認證，包括UL認證在內。

  ## 產業意義

  此次出貨讓三菱重工得以搶進美國高容量資料中心冷卻市場，時機正值AI工作負載推動伺服器配置朝更高密度、更高耗電方向發展，傳統氣冷方案已難以因應，液冷需求因此急速攀升。取得UL認證是在美國大多數設施進行商業部署的必要前提，因此2026年的目標認證日期，成為三菱重工進軍美國市場最關鍵的近期里程碑。
