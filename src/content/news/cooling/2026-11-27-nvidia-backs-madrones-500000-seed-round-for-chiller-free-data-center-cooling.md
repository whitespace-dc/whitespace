body_en: |
  Madrone, a Y Combinator startup developing a chiller-free cooling system for data centers, has raised $500,000 in seed funding from Nvidia, Y Combinator, and investment firm Tekedia Capital.

  The company was founded by Akshay Trikha and Erik Meike. Its modular system is designed to sit outside a data center and supply cool facility water to servers using a thermodynamic process that eliminates the mechanical chillers typically found in data center cooling installations. Madrone has not disclosed the precise thermodynamic mechanism.

  ## The claimed savings case

  Cooling consumes up to 30 percent of a data center's total power, Madrone says. By displacing that load, the company estimates operators could redirect the freed capacity toward revenue-generating hardware, yielding a potential $78 million in additional annual revenue per 100 megawatts (MW) of graphics processing units (GPUs).

  ## Target market: hot climates

  Madrone's commercial case rests on a gap in conventional free cooling. Free cooling uses ambient outside air to reduce the temperature of a cooling fluid, avoiding mechanical chillers altogether, and is already used by many data centers. However, the approach is not practical in hot environments. Madrone targets those markets specifically, citing Texas, which it describes as the second-largest data center market in the United States, as a primary deployment opportunity.

  ## Founders and development stage

  Meike previously worked as a thermal engineer at Apple; Trikha deployed machine learning systems at battery technology company QuantumScape. The two have constructed a 100-kilowatt (kW) demonstrator unit. The company's revenue model is built around 100 MW deployments, a scale that represents 1,000 times the demonstrator's current output.

  ## Significance

  Nvidia's participation as an investor is notable given its role as a supplier of the GPU hardware driving demand for data center power and cooling capacity. The investment links the chip company directly to an effort aimed at reducing the operational costs of running its products at scale.

  For the broader industry, Madrone's approach represents an attempt to extend chiller-free cooling to hot-climate markets where conventional free cooling is not workable. The company has built a 100 kW demonstrator unit and will need to scale up its technology significantly before the system can be deployed at data center scale.

  Madrone 由 Akshay Trikha 與 Erik Meike 共同創辦。該公司的模組化系統設計安裝於資料中心外部，透過一套熱力學程序向伺服器供應冷卻水，藉此取代傳統資料中心冷卻設備中常見的機械式冷水機。Madrone 目前尚未透露這套熱力學機制的具體原理。

  ## 節省成本的潛力

  Madrone 指出，冷卻系統最高可消耗資料中心總用電量的 30%。透過取代這部分負載，該公司估計業者得以將釋出的電力容量轉投入能創造營收的硬體設備，每 100 百萬瓦（MW）的圖形處理器（GPU）每年可帶來高達 7,800 萬美元的額外營收。

  ## 目標市場：炎熱氣候地區

  Madrone 的商業模式建立在傳統自然冷卻的一道缺口之上。自然冷卻是利用外部環境空氣降低冷卻液溫度、完全不需機械式冷水機的技術，許多資料中心已廣泛採用。然而，這種方式在高溫環境下並不可行。Madrone 專門鎖定此類市場，並以德州為主要部署目標——該公司將德州定位為美國第二大資料中心市場。

  ## 創辦團隊與開發現況

  Meike 曾任職蘋果公司（Apple）熱能工程師；Trikha 則曾在電池技術公司 QuantumScape 負責部署機器學習系統。兩人已建置一套 100 千瓦（kW）的示範機組。公司的營收模式以 100 MW 規模的部署為核心，相當於目前示範機組輸出功率的 1,000 倍。

  ## 市場意義

  輝達以投資方身分參與此輪融資，意義格外值得關注——輝達正是推動資料中心電力與冷卻需求的 GPU 硬體供應商，此次投資使這家晶片巨頭與降低旗下產品大規模運作成本的努力直接掛鉤。

  對整體產業而言，Madrone 的技術代表一項重要嘗試：將免冷水機冷卻延伸至傳統自然冷卻無法運作的炎熱氣候市場。該公司已建置 100 kW 示範機組，但在系統正式進入資料中心規模部署之前，仍需大幅提升技術規模。
