body_en: |
  Johnson Controls has released an absorption chiller design guide aimed at data center operators, showing how waste heat from on-site power generation can be redirected to drive cooling systems rather than being vented to the atmosphere, the company announced.

  The guide targets facilities using combined heat and power (CHP) — on-site generation that simultaneously produces electricity and heat — and pairs that recovered heat with absorption chillers, which use heat rather than electricity as their primary energy input. The result, according to Johnson Controls, is a reduction in cooling-related electrical demand of up to 44%.

  ## Capacity and efficiency claims

  At a 1 gigawatt (GW)-scale AI factory — a facility drawing roughly one billion watts of total power — Johnson Controls says the efficiency gains from the approach could free up to 97 megawatts (MW) of capacity for additional AI computing. The company frames this as a direct conversion of heat recovery into usable computing headroom.

  The reference design also claims a power usage effectiveness (PUE) as low as 1.23. PUE measures total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy; a PUE of 1.23 means 23% of total power goes to overhead such as cooling and lighting, with the remainder reaching servers. The design also claims zero on-site water use, a notable figure given that conventional evaporative cooling systems consume large volumes of water.

  Separately, the company states that operators can use the efficiency gains either to add computing capacity or to reduce cooling system carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions by up to 43%.

  ## How the system works

  Absorption chillers differ from conventional electric chillers in their energy source. Standard chillers use electric compressors to move refrigerant and produce cold water for cooling. Absorption chillers replace the electric compressor with a heat-driven thermochemical cycle, making them suited to facilities that generate significant quantities of waste heat.

  Johnson Controls president of global data centre solutions Austin Domenici said the company's CHP and absorption cooling solutions allow AI factories to scale more efficiently while reducing demand on the electricity grid and delivering value to surrounding communities.

  Katie McGinty, Johnson Controls vice president and chief sustainability and external relations officer, described the opportunity in terms of energy that currently goes to waste. "A vast amount of heat produced by on-site power generators is essentially thrown away — dissipated into the air," McGinty said. "We see a huge opportunity when that energy is put to work instead."

  ## Industry significance

  For data center planners and engineers, the guide addresses a constraint that has become more acute as AI workloads drive power densities higher: the share of available grid capacity consumed by cooling. By substituting recovered heat for grid electricity in the cooling cycle, operators can reallocate electrical capacity toward compute without increasing their total grid draw. At the scale Johnson Controls describes — gigawatt facilities serving AI infrastructure — even a modest percentage shift translates into tens of megawatts of additional server capacity.

  Johnson Controls says the absorption chiller guide is available alongside separate reference designs covering air-cooled and water-cooled chiller configurations.
body_zh: |
  江森自控近日發布一份專為資料中心業者設計的吸收式冷卻機設計指南，說明如何將現場發電所產生的廢熱重新導入冷卻系統加以利用，而非直接排散至大氣中，據該公司宣布。

  該指南以採用汽電共生（CHP）系統的設施為目標對象——此類現場發電設備可同時產生電力與熱能——並將回收熱能與吸收式冷卻機搭配使用。吸收式冷卻機以熱能而非電力作為主要能源輸入。據江森自控表示，此方案可將冷卻相關用電需求降低多達44%。

  ## 容量與效率聲明

  在1吉瓦（GW）等級的AI工廠——即總用電量約達10億瓦特的設施——江森自控指出，採用此方案所帶來的效率提升，最多可釋放97 MW的容量，供額外AI運算使用。該公司將此定位為將熱能回收直接轉化為可用運算空間的具體方案。

  此參考設計並宣稱電力使用效率（PUE）最低可達1.23。PUE為設施總用電量除以IT設備用電量的比值；PUE 1.23意味著總用電量中有23%用於冷卻、照明等輔助設備，其餘則直接供應伺服器使用。此設計亦宣稱現場零耗水，考量到傳統蒸發式冷卻系統需消耗大量水資源，此數字格外引人注目。

  此外，江森自控另指出，業者可選擇將效率提升所帶來的收益用於增加運算容量，或藉此將冷卻系統的二氧化碳（CO2）排放量降低多達43%，兩者可依需求擇一而行。

  ## 系統運作原理

  吸收式冷卻機與傳統電動冷卻機的根本差異在於能源來源。標準冷卻機採用電動壓縮機驅動冷媒循環，產生用於冷卻的冷水；吸收式冷卻機則以熱驅動的熱化學循環取代電動壓縮機，因此特別適合廢熱產出量龐大的設施。

  江森自控全球資料中心解決方案事業總裁奧斯汀·多梅尼奇表示，該公司的CHP與吸收式冷卻解決方案，使AI工廠得以更有效率地擴大規模，同時降低對電網的需求，並為周邊社區帶來實質效益。

  江森自控副總裁暨首席永續及對外關係長凱蒂·麥金蒂則以目前遭到浪費的能源角度闡述這項機會。「現場發電機組所產生的大量熱能，基本上是被白白丟棄的——直接散逸到空氣中，」麥金蒂說，「我們認為，若能讓這些能源轉而發揮作用，將是絕佳的機會。」

  ## 產業意義

  對資料中心規劃人員與工程師而言，此份指南正面回應了一項日益嚴峻的挑戰：隨著AI工作負載持續推升功率密度，冷卻系統所佔用的可用電網容量比例也不斷攀升。透過以回收廢熱取代電網電力來驅動冷卻循環，業者得以在不增加電網總用電量的前提下，將電力容量重新分配至運算設備。在江森自控所描述的規模下——服務AI基礎設施的吉瓦等級設施——即便只是小幅度的百分比調整，換算下來也能帶來數十MW的額外伺服器容量。

  江森自控表示，此份吸收式冷卻機指南可與另行發布的兩份參考設計一併取得，後者分別針對氣冷式及水冷式冷卻機配置。
