  ## Cooler Master launches 2.5 MW CDU at OCP 2026, expands liquid cooling portfolio

  Cooler Master introduced a 2.5-megawatt coolant distribution unit (CDU) at the OCP Global Summit 2026, anchoring an expanded data center cooling portfolio that spans facility-scale heat transfer, rack-level distribution, direct-to-chip cold plates, and the commissioning tools needed to deploy and maintain liquid cooling systems in the field.

  The new 2,500 kW liquid-to-liquid in-row CDU — a heat exchanger that isolates a building's facility water from the secondary coolant loop that circulates through IT equipment — sits at the top of a lineup that also includes smaller liquid-to-liquid configurations and a modular 300 kW air-assisted liquid cooling system targeting data centers without an existing water distribution network.

  Mass production of the 2.5 MW unit was targeted for the fourth quarter of 2026, according to the company.

  ## The 2.5 MW CDU: scale, architecture, and management

  The flagship CDU is rated for a 4 to 5 degree Celsius approach temperature difference — the gap between the temperature of the incoming facility water and the coolant leaving the heat exchanger — at its advertised 2,500 kW capacity. That thermal performance figure is a key specification for data center planners, because a smaller approach temperature difference requires either colder facility water or larger heat exchangers to transfer the same amount of heat.

  The system incorporates redundant pumping, fluid monitoring, and a centralized management architecture that the company said can coordinate as many as 32 interconnected CDUs. That capability allows operators to monitor and control coolant distribution across larger installations from a single interface. The unit also supports standard data center communications protocols for remote management.

  The significance of the scale is practical. As AI infrastructure moves toward increasingly dense rack configurations, CDU capacity has become a critical design variable. A single high-capacity unit can serve multiple racks or computing clusters, but doing so places greater demands on pumping, filtration, monitoring, redundancy, and facility piping — all of which the company said its broader portfolio is designed to address.

  Cooler Master CEO Thomas Chen emphasized deployment flexibility as a priority, particularly for customers balancing new construction against the need to expand AI capacity inside existing facilities, according to Data Center Frontier.

  ## Air-assisted system addresses existing data centers

  For facilities without dedicated liquid cooling infrastructure — those built around air cooling and not equipped with chilled-water or facility-water distribution — Cooler Master offers a 300 kW Air-Assisted Liquid Cooling (AALC) CDU. An AALC system circulates coolant through IT equipment but rejects the captured heat through fan-driven radiators rather than transferring it into a building's water loop, removing the need to extend new piping throughout a data hall.

  The 300 kW design separates pumping equipment from radiator assemblies. A company representative told Data Center Frontier that the system can be configured with a central pump rack paired with either one or two radiator racks, each rated at a nominal 150 kW, allowing cooling capacity to be matched more closely to a given installation rather than requiring every deployment to use the full 300 kW configuration.

  The system uses a redundant pump arrangement of 2+1: two pumps support normal operation and a third provides standby capacity. Pump modules are designed for replacement without interrupting cooling operations. Other field-replaceable components include fans, filters, expansion tanks, and power-related assemblies. The unit includes UPS support for up to 90 seconds — a short bridge during a power interruption or transfer, not a substitute for broader facility backup power.

  ## Portfolio scope: from cold plates to commissioning tools

  Beyond CDUs, Cooler Master's data center portfolio extends to direct-to-chip cold plates, manifolds, and the specialized equipment used to fill, flush, leak-test, and validate liquid cooling loops before and during service. Factory equipment for cold-plate drying and validation is also part of the lineup.

  The company said the portfolio extends liquid cooling coverage beyond central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs) to optical modules and networking hardware — components that generate heat but have historically been cooled by air in most deployments.

  Cooler Master said it began developing data center products for hyperscale customers in 2018, drawing on experience with liquid cooling in consumer and server markets. Its own branded data center infrastructure products have emerged more recently. Manufacturing operations are based in China, with production capacity expanding in Vietnam.

  ## Industry context

  The OCP Global Summit — an annual gathering organized around open compute and data center infrastructure standards — served as the venue for the announcement. The breadth of Cooler Master's current offering, from a 2.5 MW facility-level CDU to commissioning tools, reflects a broader industry shift in which thermal management has become a primary constraint in AI infrastructure deployment rather than a secondary consideration.

  Data center operators are currently navigating two parallel pressures: deploying liquid cooling for new high-density AI racks, and retrofitting or supplementing air-cooled facilities to handle denser compute without full facility conversions. The AALC system addresses the latter; the 2.5 MW CDU addresses the former.

  Mass production of the 2.5 MW CDU was targeted to begin in the fourth quarter of 2026.

  ## 酷碼科技於OCP 2026推出2.5 MW冷卻液分配單元，擴大液冷產品線

  酷碼科技（Cooler Master）在2026年OCP全球峰會上發表一款2,500千瓦（kW）冷卻液分配單元（CDU），成為其資料中心冷卻產品線的核心旗艦。這條產品線橫跨機房層級的熱傳輸、機架層級的分配系統、直接接觸晶片冷板，以及在現場部署與維護液冷系統所需的各類試運行工具。

  這款全新的2,500 kW液對液列間CDU，作用是將建築物設施端水路與在IT設備間循環的二次冷卻液迴路相互隔離，本質上是一種熱交換器。在整個產品陣容中，它位居頂端；陣容中同時涵蓋容量較小的液對液配置，以及一款300 kW模組化風冷輔助液冷系統，專為尚無現成水路分配基礎設施的資料中心所設計。

  據該公司表示，2.5 MW機型預計於2026年第四季進入量產。

  ## 2.5 MW CDU：規模、架構與管理

  這款旗艦CDU在2,500 kW額定容量下，接近溫差（approach temperature difference，即進入熱交換器的設施端水溫與離開熱交換器的冷卻液出口溫度之差）設計為攝氏4至5度。這項熱性能指標對資料中心規劃人員而言至關重要——接近溫差愈小，在傳輸相同熱量時，要麼需要溫度更低的設施端冷水，要麼需要面積更大的熱交換器。

  系統整合了冗餘幫浦、流體監控，以及一套集中管理架構。酷碼表示，這套架構最多可協調32台互聯CDU，讓操作人員能從單一介面對較大規模裝置的冷卻液分配進行監控與管理。此機型亦支援業界標準的資料中心通訊協定，可供遠端管理使用。

  這一規模的實際意義不容小覷。隨著AI基礎設施朝向密度日益提升的機架配置演進，CDU容量已成為資料中心設計的關鍵變數。單一高容量機型可同時服務多個機架或運算叢集，但這對幫浦、過濾、監控、冗餘設計及機房配管均提出更高要求——酷碼表示，其整體產品線正是為解決上述問題而設計。

  據《Data Center Frontier》報導，酷碼科技執行長 Thomas Chen 強調，部署彈性是首要優先考量，尤其是對於那些需要在新建工程與既有機房擴充AI容量之間取得平衡的客戶而言更為重要。

  ## 風冷輔助系統因應現有資料中心需求

  對於尚未建置專屬液冷基礎設施、以氣冷為主且未配備冷凍水或設施端水路分配系統的機房，酷碼提供一款300 kW風冷輔助液冷（AALC）CDU。AALC系統讓冷卻液在IT設備間循環，但透過風扇驅動的散熱排將所吸收的熱量排出，而非導入建築物的水迴路，從而免去在機房內重新鋪設新管路的需求。

  這款300 kW設計將幫浦設備與散熱排組件分開配置。有公司代表向《Data Center Frontier》說明，此系統可採用一組中央幫浦機架搭配一或兩組散熱排機架的配置，每組散熱排機架額定容量約150 kW，讓冷卻容量能更貼合特定裝置的實際需求，而無需每次部署都動用完整的300 kW配置。

  系統採用2+1冗餘幫浦配置：兩台幫浦負責正常運作，第三台作為備援。幫浦模組設計可在不中斷冷卻作業的情況下進行更換。其他可現場更換的零組件包括風扇、過濾器、膨脹水箱及電源相關組件。此機型配備不斷電系統（UPS）支援，可維持最長90秒——僅作為電源中斷或切換期間的短暫橋接，並非取代機房整體備用電源設施的方案。

  ## 產品線範疇：從冷板到試運行工具

  除CDU之外，酷碼科技的資料中心產品線還涵蓋直接接觸晶片冷板、歧管，以及液冷迴路投入服務前後所需的充液、沖洗、洩漏測試與驗證專用設備，另有用於冷板乾燥與驗證的工廠設備。

  酷碼表示，其產品線所涵蓋的液冷應用範圍，已從中央處理器（CPU）與繪圖處理器（GPU）延伸至光學模組與網路硬體——這些零組件雖然會產生熱量，但在過去大多數部署環境中仍以氣冷方式處理。

  酷碼表示，公司自2018年起開始為超大規模客戶開發資料中心產品，期間積累了在消費性與伺服器市場的液冷技術經驗，自有品牌的資料中心基礎設施產品則在近期才陸續推出。目前製造據點設於中國，並持續在越南擴充產能。

  ## 產業背景

  OCP全球峰會是一年一度圍繞開放運算與資料中心基礎設施標準所舉辦的業界盛會，此次成為酷碼科技發表上述產品的舞台。酷碼科技現有產品線涵蓋從2.5 MW機房級CDU到試運行工具的廣泛品項，反映出整個產業更深層的結構性轉變——熱管理已從AI基礎設施部署的次要考量，躍升為主要制約因素。

  資料中心業者目前同時面臨兩股平行壓力：一是為新型高密度AI機架部署液冷系統；二是在不進行整體機房改造的前提下，對氣冷機房進行改裝或補強，以因應更高密度的運算需求。AALC系統針對的正是後者，而2.5 MW CDU則應對前者。

  2.5 MW CDU的量產目標訂於2026年第四季啟動。
