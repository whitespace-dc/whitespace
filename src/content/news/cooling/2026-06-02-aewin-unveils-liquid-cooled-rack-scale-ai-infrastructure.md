# Aewin Unveils Liquid-Cooled Rack-Scale AI Infrastructure

Aewin has unveiled rack-scale AI infrastructure with liquid cooling capabilities. As chip heat and rack density increase, data center operators are adopting liquid cooling to reduce power consumption and support larger AI workloads. The solution addresses thermal and power challenges in modern AI-driven data centers.

## 中文

# Aewin推出機架級AI液冷基礎設施

Aewin推出配備液冷技術的機架級AI基礎設施。隨著晶片散熱和機架密度提升，資料中心營運商採用液冷技術以降低耗電量並支援更大規模的AI工作負載。此方案解決現代AI資料中心面臨的散熱和電力挑戰。
