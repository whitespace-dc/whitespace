body_en: |
  Carrier Transicold is donating two NaturaLine container refrigeration units to Technische Universität Berlin (TU Berlin) to support the university's research into transcritical CO2 refrigeration technology, the company said.

  The units will go to TU Berlin's Institute of Energy Engineering, where they will serve dual roles: as hands-on teaching equipment for graduate students and as test beds for prototype components.

  ## Student training on commercial hardware

  Fitted with basic instrumentation, the donated units will allow students to measure temperature profiles, system pressures, and power consumption directly on a real commercial system, according to Carrier Transicold. The institute said the setup is intended to give students first-hand insight into how transcritical CO2 differs from conventional refrigeration cycles.

  In a transcritical CO2 cycle, the refrigerant operates above its critical point on the high-pressure side of the system — a thermodynamic regime that distinguishes it from standard vapor-compression cycles using synthetic refrigerants.

  ## Research infrastructure

  Beyond classroom use, researchers at the institute plan to integrate and evaluate prototype components inside the units to develop and test future heating and cooling systems. Performance will be assessed through automated test sequences, the institute said. Researchers also plan to conduct long-term performance evaluations across a range of operating conditions to better understand how transcritical CO2 systems behave at commercial scale.

  ## Background on the NaturaLine

  The NaturaLine was introduced in 2010 as the first CO2 marine container refrigeration system. Sixteen years after its launch, improving the performance and efficiency of transcritical CO2 systems across a wide range of operating conditions remains a central focus of refrigeration research, according to the company.

  ## Industry significance

  The donation gives academic researchers direct access to commercial-grade transcritical CO2 hardware, bridging a gap between laboratory-scale studies and real-world system behavior. The arrangement positions TU Berlin's institute to generate performance data on a proven commercial platform while testing next-generation components under controlled conditions.
body_zh: |
  ## 開利冷凍捐贈CO₂冷凍機組　助柏林工大推進超臨界研究

  開利冷凍（Carrier Transicold）宣布，將捐贈兩台NaturaLine貨櫃冷凍機組予德國柏林工業大學（Technische Universität Berlin，以下簡稱TU Berlin），以支持該校對超臨界CO₂冷凍技術的研究。

  這兩台機組將交由TU Berlin能源工程研究所（Institute of Energy Engineering）使用，兼具兩項功能：一是作為研究生的實務教學設備，二是作為原型元件的測試平台。

  ## 以商用硬體培訓學生

  據開利冷凍表示，這批捐贈機組配備基本量測儀器，讓學生得以直接在真實商用系統上量測溫度分布、系統壓力及耗電量。該研究所指出，此設置旨在讓學生親身了解超臨界CO₂冷凍循環與傳統冷凍循環的差異。

  在超臨界CO₂循環中，冷媒在系統高壓側的運作狀態超越其臨界點，形成一種特殊的熱力學區間，有別於使用合成冷媒的標準蒸氣壓縮循環。

  ## 研究基礎設施

  除課堂教學用途外，研究所的研究人員計畫將原型元件整合至機組內部，以開發並測試下一代冷暖系統。研究所表示，性能評估將透過自動化測試程序進行。研究人員亦計畫在多種運轉條件下展開長期性能評估，以更深入了解超臨界CO₂系統在商用規模下的實際表現。

  ## NaturaLine機組背景

  NaturaLine於2010年問世，是全球首款CO₂海洋貨櫃冷凍系統。開利冷凍表示，自該系統推出至今已逾十六年，如何在廣泛的運轉條件下提升超臨界CO₂系統的性能與效率，至今仍是冷凍研究領域的核心課題。

  ## 產業意義

  此次捐贈讓學術研究人員得以直接使用商用等級的超臨界CO₂硬體，填補了實驗室小規模研究與真實系統表現之間的落差。這項合作安排使TU Berlin能源工程研究所得以在成熟的商用平台上取得性能數據，同時在受控條件下測試次世代元件。
