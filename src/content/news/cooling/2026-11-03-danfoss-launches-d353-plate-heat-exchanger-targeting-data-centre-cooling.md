body_en: |
  Danfoss has introduced the D353, a new gasketed plate heat exchanger designed for data centre cooling, as the Danish engineering company expands its heat exchanger portfolio into critical infrastructure applications.

  The D353 is aimed at deployments where uptime, heat transfer efficiency, and ease of maintenance are priorities, the company said. Danfoss said the unit's compact frame reduces both height and floor footprint, which it described as an advantage in facilities where space is constrained.

  ## Design and maintenance features

  The D353 incorporates several proprietary mechanical features Danfoss said are intended to simplify handling and servicing. These include the company's D-Lock gasket system, improved plate alignment, ArcCompact plate suspension, and an internal roller system. Together, the company said, these elements support easier handling, maintenance, and resealing — tasks that in a live data centre environment typically must be performed with minimal disruption to cooling uptime.

  A gasketed plate heat exchanger transfers heat between two fluid circuits through a stack of corrugated metal plates sealed with gaskets, allowing the unit to be opened and cleaned or resealed without replacing the entire assembly. The gasketed design contrasts with brazed or welded plate heat exchangers, which are permanent and cannot be serviced in the field.

  ## Target markets

  Danfoss said the D353 was developed with data centres specifically in mind but that its design also opens opportunities in other demanding applications. The company identified artificial intelligence infrastructure, offshore installations, and geothermal systems as additional target sectors. Support for higher operating pressures, Danfoss said, gives customers flexibility to adapt the unit to different system requirements across those environments.

  The extension into AI infrastructure is notable given that AI computing workloads generate substantially higher heat densities than conventional server deployments, increasing demand for efficient liquid cooling at the rack and facility level.

  The D353 adds to Danfoss's existing gasketed plate heat exchanger range and positions the company to address demand for compact, serviceable cooling hardware as data centre operators manage denser compute loads and tighter space constraints.
body_zh: |
  丹麥工程大廠丹佛斯（Danfoss）近日發表D353新型墊片式板式熱交換器，這款產品專為資料中心冷卻需求所設計，也標誌著丹佛斯正式將熱交換器產品線延伸至關鍵基礎設施應用領域。

  丹佛斯表示，D353的設計核心在於滿足那些將系統可用性、換熱效率與維護便利性列為首要考量的部署場景。該公司指出，D353採用緊湊型機架，有效降低了設備高度與佔地面積，對於空間受限的設施而言，這是一項相當實用的優勢。

  ## 設計特色與維護功能

  D353整合了多項丹佛斯專有的機械設計特點，旨在簡化操作與維修作業。這些特點包括：D-Lock墊片系統、改良式板片對位機構、ArcCompact板片懸掛設計，以及內建滾輪系統。丹佛斯表示，上述設計相輔相成，能讓設備在搬運、維護及重新密封等作業上更加便利——而這些工作在運行中的資料中心環境裡，通常必須在對冷卻系統正常運作影響降至最低的前提下完成。

  墊片式板式熱交換器的工作原理，是透過一疊附有波紋的金屬板片，在兩個流體迴路之間進行熱交換，板片之間以墊片密封。這種設計讓設備可以直接拆開清洗或更換墊片，無需汰換整組組件。相較之下，銅焊式或焊接式板式熱交換器屬於永久性結構，無法在現場進行維修，兩者設計理念截然不同。

  ## 目標市場

  丹佛斯表示，D353是以資料中心為主要開發對象，但其設計同時也為其他高要求應用場景開拓了商機。該公司點名人工智慧基礎設施、離岸設施及地熱系統，均為額外鎖定的目標市場。丹佛斯強調，D353支援更高的操作壓力，賦予客戶彈性，得以因應上述各類環境中不同的系統需求。

  值得關注的是，D353將觸角延伸至AI基礎設施領域。AI運算工作負載所產生的熱密度，遠高於傳統伺服器部署，這也大幅推升了機架與設施層級對高效液冷方案的需求。

  D353的推出進一步充實了丹佛斯現有的墊片式板式熱交換器產品線，也讓該公司得以在市場上搶佔有利位置——隨著資料中心營運商面對日益密集的運算負載與愈趨緊縮的空間限制，對於緊湊、可維修冷卻硬體的需求只會持續增長。
