  ## Citrus-peel emulsion stores cold to -85°C, Spanish researchers report

  Researchers at two Spanish universities have developed a water-free liquid emulsion made partly from citrus peel extract that can store and release cold across a temperature range of approximately -85°C to +5°C, and can hold up to 2.4 times more cold in the same volume as some conventional secondary refrigerants, according to the research team.

  The work is a collaboration between the Research Centre for Chemical Product and Process Technology (Pro2TecS) at the University of Huelva (UHU) and the Department of Chemical Engineering at the University of Seville (US). The team is targeting applications including industrial freezing, food and pharmaceutical transport, preservation of biological samples, and very low-temperature refrigeration systems.

  ## How the emulsion works

  The material combines three components. Polyethylene glycol PEG400 — a synthetic liquid commonly used in industrial and pharmaceutical applications — acts as the primary cold-storage medium, absorbing and releasing energy as it changes physical state. D-limonene, a compound extracted from citrus peels such as lemon, orange, and grapefruit, replaces water as the second liquid phase; because water freezes at 0°C, its absence allows the emulsion to remain functional at temperatures far below those achievable with conventional water-based secondary refrigerants such as glycol-water mixtures or brines.

  The two liquids do not naturally mix, so the team adds silica nanoparticles as a stabilising agent to keep PEG400 droplets suspended and prevent them from merging when the mixture freezes and thaws.

  The research team notes a formulation trade-off: adding more silica increases stability but also raises the liquid's viscosity, making it harder to pump through pipes. Optimising that balance is described as a key engineering challenge.

  ## Performance and comparison with conventional refrigerants

  Laboratory tests and mathematical modelling showed the emulsion operating across the -85°C to +5°C window and storing up to 2.4 times more cold per unit volume than some common refrigerants, the researchers said. The team said the material could circulate through pipes and heat exchangers in the same way existing liquid refrigerants do, or function as a static thermal accumulator analogous to a cold pack.

  ## Stage of development

  The team characterises the work as a first phase of research. So far, the researchers have formulated the material, characterised its thermal properties and flow behaviour, and benchmarked it against existing refrigerants through laboratory tests and calculations. The emulsion has not yet been validated under real-world conditions and is not a finished product.

  The next phase will involve testing the emulsion in systems that more closely replicate industrial refrigeration infrastructure, including compressors, valves, pipes, and heat exchangers. Those trials are intended to confirm whether laboratory performance holds at scale and whether the formulation requires adjustment.

  ## Significance

  If validated at scale, a secondary refrigerant with a 2.4-times improvement in cold-storage density and an operating floor near -85°C would address limitations of water-based coolants in applications requiring deep-freeze capability or compact thermal storage. The use of citrus peel — an agricultural byproduct — as a functional component adds a potential materials-sourcing angle for the food-processing and cold-chain sectors. Whether the performance gains survive the step from laboratory to industrial system will be determined in the team's next research phase.

  ## 柑橘皮乳液可儲冷至攝氏零下85度，西班牙研究人員發表成果

  西班牙兩所大學的研究人員，研製出一種以柑橘皮萃取物為部分原料的無水液態乳液，可在約攝氏零下85度至正5度的溫度範圍內儲存並釋放冷能，且相同體積的儲冷量最高可達部分傳統二次冷媒的2.4倍，研究團隊表示。

  此項研究由韋爾瓦大學（UHU）化學產品與製程技術研究中心（Pro2TecS）與塞維亞大學（US）化學工程系合作完成。研究團隊的目標應用領域包括工業冷凍、食品與醫藥運輸、生物樣品保存，以及超低溫冷凍系統。

  ## 乳液的作用原理

  此材料由三種成分組成。聚乙二醇PEG400——一種廣泛應用於工業與製藥領域的合成液體——作為主要儲冷介質，透過物態變化吸收與釋放能量。D-檸檬烯是從檸檬、柳橙、葡萄柚等柑橘類果皮中萃取的化合物，取代水作為第二液相；由於水在攝氏0度便會結冰，去除水分使乳液得以在遠低於傳統水基二次冷媒（如乙二醇水溶液或鹽水）的溫度下正常運作。

  這兩種液體本身不會自然相溶，因此研究團隊加入二氧化矽奈米顆粒作為穩定劑，使PEG400液滴保持懸浮狀態，並防止其在混合物凍結與解凍的過程中相互聚合。

  研究團隊指出，配方存在一項工程上的取捨：加入越多二氧化矽雖可提高穩定性，但同時也會增加液體的黏度，使其更難以在管路中泵送。如何在兩者之間取得最佳平衡，被認為是關鍵的工程挑戰。

  ## 效能表現與傳統冷媒的比較

  實驗室測試與數學建模結果顯示，該乳液可在攝氏零下85度至正5度的範圍內運作，每單位體積的儲冷量最高可達部分常見冷媒的2.4倍，研究人員表示。研究團隊指出，此材料可像現有液態冷媒一樣在管路與熱交換器中循環流動，也可作為靜態蓄冷裝置，功能類似保冷劑。

  ## 研發階段

  研究團隊將目前的工作定位為研究的第一階段。迄今為止，研究人員已完成材料配方的制定、熱力學性質與流動特性的表徵，並透過實驗室測試與計算，與現有冷媒進行了比較基準測試。該乳液尚未在實際條件下進行驗證，目前仍非成品。

  下一階段將在更接近工業冷凍基礎設施的系統中對乳液進行測試，包括壓縮機、閥門、管路與熱交換器。這些試驗的目的在於確認實驗室的效能表現能否在規模化後維持，以及配方是否需要進一步調整。

  ## 研究意義

  若能通過規模化驗證，一種每單位體積儲冷密度提升2.4倍、操作下限接近攝氏零下85度的二次冷媒，將有望解決水基冷卻劑在需要深度冷凍能力或緊湊型蓄冷設備等應用場合中的局限性。以柑橘皮——一種農業副產品——作為功能性原料，也為食品加工業與冷鏈物流業者提供了潛在的材料採購新方向。效能優勢能否在從實驗室轉換至工業系統的過程中得以保留，將在研究團隊的下一個研究階段中獲得驗證。
