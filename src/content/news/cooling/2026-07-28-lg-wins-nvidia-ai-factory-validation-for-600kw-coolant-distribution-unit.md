body_en: |
  LG Electronics has received NVIDIA AI Factory validation for its 600kW Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU), adding it to the pool of pre-approved liquid cooling systems available to AI data centre operators building out GPU-dense infrastructure.

  The approval followed evaluation against more than 100 technical criteria covering cooling performance, reliability and failover requirements under NVIDIA's AI Factory standardisation programme. LG said the CDU cleared all criteria, placing it among a relatively small number of suppliers to complete NVIDIA's technical verification process for this category of infrastructure.

  ## What the unit does and how it operates

  The validated CDU uses direct-to-chip liquid cooling, a method in which coolant is delivered directly to heat-generating components such as graphics processing units (GPUs) and central processing units (CPUs). LG said the design allows the unit to operate in a hybrid system alongside existing air-cooling infrastructure rather than requiring a standalone liquid-only setup.

  According to the company, the CDU delivers temperature control precision of plus or minus 0.25 degrees Celsius. It includes virtual sensor technology for real-time monitoring and leak detection. The unit can be paired with LG's Data Centre Cooling Control Manager, a software layer providing predictive control, centralised monitoring and connectivity with broader data centre management systems through standard communication protocols.

  LG evaluates its cooling systems at a dedicated validation site in Pyeongtaek, South Korea, where it tests transient system responses, temperature changes and low-load stability across the cooling chain. The company said the CDU was engineered to meet NVIDIA's failover requirements, keeping operations stable under demanding conditions.

  ## LG's broader cooling ambitions

  The 600kW validation is one part of a larger push by LG into AI data centre infrastructure. The company said it intends to seek the same NVIDIA approval for higher-capacity CDU models rated at 1 megawatt (MW), 2.5MW and 4MW.

  LG positions its data centre offering around what it calls a chip-to-chiller approach: chillers that generate chilled water, CDUs that distribute coolant, and cold plates that transfer heat directly from processors. The company produces key components in-house, including inverter systems and magnetic bearing compressors used across its chiller range.

  LG said it tests its cooling systems at its Pyeongtaek site by assessing the full cooling chain, including how systems behave during transient load changes and at low-load conditions — scenarios relevant to real-world data centre operations where workloads fluctuate.

  ## Market context

  Liquid cooling has become central to AI data centre design because newer processors generate more heat than traditional air-based systems can efficiently handle alone. CDUs form part of the cooling chain by circulating and controlling the liquid that carries heat away from computing components before it is rejected to the facility's broader thermal management system.

  Competition in AI data centre cooling has intensified as operators race to build capacity without compromising uptime. The market has attracted established heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) manufacturers, specialist liquid cooling companies and broader infrastructure suppliers seeking long-term contracts with hyperscalers — large cloud and AI computing companies — and colocation operators that house servers on behalf of tenants.

  NVIDIA AI Factory validation functions as a procurement filter in this environment. Operators building AI facilities must balance energy use, thermal performance, maintenance risks and deployment timelines, making pre-approved components attractive when projects are rolled out at scale.

  ## Commercial traction

  LG said it has recently secured commercial data centre cooling work, citing hyperscaler facilities in North America and the Sinar Mas data centre — SM+ Data Centre's AI-ready site in Jakarta, Indonesia — as project references. Those wins indicate the company has moved from pilot engagements into commercial deployments in regions where AI infrastructure investment is expanding.

  James Lee, President of the LG ES Company, framed the company's position in the market: "As AI data centres continue to scale, the need for highly reliable liquid cooling solutions has never been greater. Leveraging our advanced cooling technologies together with the LG Group's integrated infrastructure capabilities, we are expanding our AI infrastructure business with end-to-end solutions that help enable Physical AI, where AI creates value in real-world environments."

  LG's stated next steps in the NVIDIA validation process are to submit its 1MW, 2.5MW and 4MW CDU models for the same approval the 600kW unit has now passed.

  ## 設備功能與運作方式

  此次獲得認證的 CDU 採用直接對晶片液冷（direct-to-chip liquid cooling）技術，直接將冷卻液輸送至 GPU、CPU 等發熱元件。LG 表示，這套設計可讓 CDU 在混合架構下與現有氣冷基礎設施共同運作，無需單獨建置純液冷系統。

  據該公司說明，此 CDU 溫度控制精度達正負 0.25 攝氏度，並內建虛擬感測器技術，可執行即時監控與洩漏偵測。此外，CDU 可搭配 LG 的資料中心冷卻控制管理器（Data Centre Cooling Control Manager）使用——此為一套軟體層，提供預測性控制、集中監控功能，並透過標準通訊協定與更廣泛的資料中心管理系統整合。

  LG 在南韓平澤（Pyeongtaek）設有專屬驗證場地，針對冷卻系統進行各項測試，包括暫態系統響應、溫度變化，以及冷卻鏈在低負載條件下的穩定性。LG 表示，該 CDU 在工程設計上符合輝達的容錯切換要求，能在高需求條件下維持穩定運作。

  ## LG 更宏觀的冷卻佈局

  此次 600kW 認證，是 LG 大力進軍 AI 資料中心基礎設施行動的一環。LG 表示，未來計畫為容量更高的 1 百萬瓦（MW）、2.5MW 及 4MW CDU 機型申請同一輝達認證。

  LG 以「從晶片到冷卻機組」（chip-to-chiller）為核心理念，打造其資料中心產品線：由冷卻機組（chiller）產生冷卻水，再由 CDU 分配冷卻液，最後透過冷板（cold plate）直接將熱量從處理器導出。公司的關鍵元件均自行生產，包括應用於其冷卻機組系列的變頻器系統與磁懸浮壓縮機。

  LG 表示，在平澤測試基地，公司針對冷卻系統進行整條冷卻鏈的評估，包括系統在暫態負載變化及低負載條件下的運作表現——這些都是現實資料中心環境中工作負載波動時的實際應用情境。

  ## 市場背景

  液冷技術已成為 AI 資料中心設計的核心，原因在於新一代處理器產生的熱量，已超出傳統氣冷系統單獨處理的效率上限。CDU 在冷卻鏈中扮演關鍵角色，負責循環並調控液體，將熱量從運算元件帶走，再由設施的整體熱管理系統排放。

  隨著業者競相擴建 AI 基礎設施容量、同時兼顧不中斷運作，AI 資料中心冷卻領域的競爭也日趨激烈。市場吸引了暖通空調（HVAC）大廠、專業液冷企業，以及更廣泛的基礎設施供應商紛紛入局，爭取與超大規模業者（hyperscaler，即大型雲端與 AI 運算公司）及代客戶托管伺服器的共置資料中心業者簽訂長期合約。

  在此環境下，輝達 AI 工廠認證形同採購篩選機制。建置 AI 設施的業者，須在能源使用效率、散熱效能、維護風險與部署時程之間取得平衡；在大規模推動專案時，預核可元件自然更具吸引力。

  ## 商業進展

  LG 表示，近期已取得多項資料中心冷卻商業訂單，並以北美的超大規模業者設施，以及印尼雅加達的 SM+ Data Centre（Sinar Mas 資料中心）AI 就緒園區為案例佐證。這些訂單顯示，LG 已從試驗性專案邁入商業部署，並在 AI 基礎設施投資蓬勃的地區站穩腳跟。

  LG ES 公司總裁 James Lee 如此闡述公司在市場中的定位：「隨著 AI 資料中心持續擴大規模，對高可靠度液冷解決方案的需求從未如此迫切。我們結合先進冷卻技術與 LG 集團整合基礎設施能力，持續拓展 AI 基礎設施業務，提供端對端解決方案，協助實現實體 AI（Physical AI）——讓 AI 在真實世界環境中創造價值。」

  LG 表示，下一步計畫將 1MW、2.5MW 及 4MW CDU 機型送交輝達進行相同驗證，延續 600kW 機型已完成的認證路徑。
