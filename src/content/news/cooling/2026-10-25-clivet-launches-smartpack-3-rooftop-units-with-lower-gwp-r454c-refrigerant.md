  ## Clivet launches SmartPack 3 rooftop units with lower-GWP R454C refrigerant

  Italian HVAC manufacturer Clivet has introduced the SmartPack 3, a new generation of rooftop air-conditioning units using the A2L refrigerant R454C, which has a lower global warming potential (GWP) than many conventional refrigerants. The launch marks a refrigerant transition for Clivet's rooftop product line.

  R454C is classified as A2L under safety standards — meaning it is mildly flammable but has a lower GWP than widely used alternatives such as R410A. Clivet did not disclose specific GWP figures in its announcement.

  ## Performance and operating range

  The SmartPack 3 is rated with a Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio (SEER) of 4.60 and a Seasonal Coefficient of Performance (SCOP) of 3.72. SEER measures average cooling efficiency over a season; SCOP measures average heating efficiency. The units operate down to an outdoor temperature of -20°C, which Clivet said extends the range of the series.

  Available capacities run from 25.3 kW to 42.4 kW, with airflow rates between 3,200 m³/h and 12,000 m³/h. The series uses full inverter technology — meaning compressor speed varies with load rather than cycling on and off — and brushless electronically commutated (EC) plug-fan motors, which adjust fan speed electronically for efficiency. The units are configured as a monobloc, meaning all components are housed in a single self-contained package.

  ## REVO thermodynamic recovery system

  The SmartPack 3 incorporates Clivet's patented REVO (Recovery EVOlution) system, which the company said harnesses energy from exhaust air directly within the direct expansion (DX) refrigeration circuit. A direct expansion circuit cools or heats air by passing it over a coil through which refrigerant flows and changes phase. Clivet said REVO increases the unit's heating and cooling capacity without requiring a separate auxiliary refrigeration circuit.

  ## Multi-zone and unified control

  The SmartPack 3 shares standardised options and control logic with the broader Clivet rooftop range, allowing buildings that deploy units of different sizes or types to manage them through a common interface. Clivet said a single IntelliAir controller or the Clivet Eye app can handle multiple zones simultaneously.

  Product manager Lorenzo Cristiani described a practical application: "Let's consider the practical example of a shopping centre or a showroom with spaces serving different purposes: the shop will manage air renewal based on CO₂/VOC sensors for peak occupancy levels; the checkout area or kitchen will adopt the setup for forced extraction; and areas with high latent load will use free hot-gas post-heating for dehumidification. A single IntelliAir controller or the Clivet Eye app itself will manage all these requirements 24 hours a day without compelling the technician to learn different protocols or logic for each series."

  VOC refers to volatile organic compounds; sensors monitoring CO₂ and VOC concentrations are used to trigger ventilation in response to occupancy and air quality rather than on fixed schedules.

  ## Retrofit compatibility

  Clivet said the SmartPack 3 has been designed for retrofit installation, retaining the same ductwork configurations and external dimensions as the previous CKN-XHE2i series. That compatibility reduces the civil and mechanical work required when replacing existing units.

  ## Industry significance

  The shift to R454C reflects broader regulatory pressure across European and global markets to phase down high-GWP refrigerants in commercial HVAC equipment. For building owners and specifiers, the combination of A2L refrigerant compliance, extended cold-weather operation, and unified multi-zone control addresses several converging requirements: energy performance regulations, refrigerant transition timelines, and operational simplicity in multi-use buildings such as retail centres. The retrofit compatibility with the previous series lowers the barrier for existing Clivet customers to transition to the lower-GWP platform.

  ## Clivet推出SmartPack 3屋頂空調系列，改用低暖化潛勢R454C冷媒

  義大利暖通空調製造商Clivet正式發表SmartPack 3，這是其屋頂式空調產品線的全新世代機型，採用A2L等級冷媒R454C，其全球暖化潛勢（GWP）低於多種主流冷媒。此次發表標誌著Clivet屋頂產品線完成冷媒世代交替。

  R454C依安全標準被歸類為A2L等級——意即具有輕微可燃性，但其GWP低於R410A等廣泛使用的傳統冷媒。Clivet在此次發表中並未揭露具體的GWP數值。

  ## 性能規格與運轉範圍

  SmartPack 3的季節性能源效率比（SEER）達4.60，季節性性能係數（SCOP）為3.72。SEER衡量整季平均冷卻效率，SCOP則衡量整季平均加熱效率。機組可在室外氣溫低至攝氏零下20度的環境下正常運轉，Clivet表示此特性進一步拓展了本系列的適用範圍。

  機型冷暖容量涵蓋25.3 kW至42.4 kW，送風量介於每小時3,200立方公尺至12,000立方公尺之間。本系列採用全變頻技術，使壓縮機轉速可隨負載連續調節，無需頻繁啟停，並配備無刷電子換向（EC）直驅風扇馬達，能以電子方式精確調控風速以提升效率。機組採整體式單一機箱設計，所有元件整合於同一自給自足的殼體之中。

  ## REVO熱力回收系統

  SmartPack 3內建Clivet專利的REVO（Recovery EVOlution，回收進化）系統。Clivet表示，該系統能直接在直膨式（DX）冷媒迴路中回收排氣中的能量加以利用。直膨式迴路的運作方式，是讓空氣流經內有冷媒流動並發生相變的盤管，以達到冷卻或加熱的效果。Clivet指出，REVO系統可在無需額外附加獨立輔助冷媒迴路的前提下，有效提升機組的冷暖容量。

  ## 多區域控制與統一管理

  SmartPack 3與Clivet屋頂機系列採用標準化的選配方案與控制邏輯，讓同一棟建築中部署不同規格或類型機組的用戶，能夠透過共通的操作介面統一管理。Clivet表示，單一IntelliAir控制器或Clivet Eye應用程式即可同時管理多個空調區域。

  產品經理Lorenzo Cristiani以實際應用情境加以說明：「讓我們來看一個實際案例——購物中心或展示間內有不同用途的空間：店鋪區域依據CO₂／VOC感測器，在尖峰使用人數時段自動調節換氣；收銀台區域或廚房則採用強制排氣的設定；而高潛熱負荷區域則使用熱氣直接後加熱進行除濕。所有這些需求，都能由單一IntelliAir控制器或Clivet Eye應用程式全天候24小時管理，技術人員無需針對每個系列學習不同的通訊協定或控制邏輯。」

  VOC係指揮發性有機化合物；透過監測CO₂與VOC濃度的感測器，可依據實際使用人數與空氣品質觸發通風換氣，而非依據固定排程運作。

  ## 舊機汰換相容性

  Clivet表示，SmartPack 3在設計之初即考量到汰舊換新的安裝需求，保留與前代CKN-XHE2i系列相同的風管配置及外觀尺寸。這項相容性大幅降低了更換現有機組時所需的土建與機電工程量。

  ## 產業意義

  轉換採用R454C冷媒，反映出歐洲及全球市場日趨嚴格的法規壓力，要求商用暖通空調設備逐步淘汰高GWP冷媒。對建築業主與規劃設計人員而言，A2L冷媒法規合規、延伸的低溫運轉能力，以及統一的多區域控制，正好因應多項同步浮現的需求：能源性能法規要求、冷媒轉換時程，以及零售中心等多功能建築的操作簡便性。而與前代系列的汰換相容性，更進一步降低了現有Clivet用戶升級至低暖化潛勢平台的門檻。
