body_en: |
  Castrol's two direct-to-chip liquid cooling fluids, Castrol ON PG25 and Castrol ON PG25T, have been validated against NVIDIA requirements and included in the NVIDIA-Validated AI Factory Infrastructure program, the company announced. The designation is intended to give data centre operators, equipment manufacturers, and cooling-system providers greater confidence when specifying fluids for AI computing deployments.

  Both products use propylene glycol as their base fluid and are formulated for direct-to-chip cooling systems, in which fluid circulates through cold plates attached to processors such as central processing units (CPUs) and graphics processing units (GPUs). Castrol identified PG25 as an OAT (organic acid technology) formulation and PG25T as an IAT (inorganic acid technology) formulation, though the announcement did not detail the full commercial distinction between the two variants.

  ## What the validation covered

  NVIDIA's technical review examined corrosion resistance, thermal stability, dilution-water quality, propylene glycol base-fluid purity, and compatibility with wetted materials — the metals, plastics, seals, and other components that come into contact with coolant inside a liquid-cooling loop. The assessment also extended beyond fluid chemistry to evaluate Castrol's business operations and global supply-chain resilience, reflecting operator concerns about consistent sourcing as liquid cooling is scaled across multiple sites and regions.

  Sung A. Kim, Data Centre Global Technology Manager at Castrol, said both fluids had been tested internally before the NVIDIA review. "We evaluate every thermal management fluid under rigorous test conditions before it reaches the market. The validation from NVIDIA confirms that PG25 (OAT) and PG25T (IAT) hold up to that standard, even at the scale and intensity of today's AI infrastructure," Kim said.

  ## Why AI thermal loads are driving demand

  AI servers consume more power and generate more heat than conventional enterprise hardware, the source noted, pushing thermal output beyond what many air-cooling architectures can manage alone. Operators face pressure to increase compute density while controlling electricity consumption, water use, and equipment reliability. Direct-to-chip cooling addresses that by removing heat at the component level rather than relying primarily on air handling to cool an entire room.

  In a closed-loop direct-to-chip system, coolant circulates continuously without exposure to the atmosphere, which can reduce evaporative water loss and improve water usage effectiveness — a metric that has become more prominent as AI data centres draw scrutiny over resource consumption.

  ## Global reach and competitive context

  Castrol markets its Castrol ON thermal management brand across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, China, Australia, and Southeast Asia. Peter Huang, Global President of Thermal Management and Data Centres at Castrol, framed the validation as evidence of both product performance and the company's capacity to supply at scale. "The inclusion of Castrol ON PG25 and PG25T within NVIDIA-Validated AI Factory Infrastructure gives customers added confidence in both the fluids' performance and Castrol's ability to provide consistent global support across markets," Huang said.

  For fluid suppliers, inclusion in an ecosystem validation program can directly influence procurement decisions, particularly where customers require documented evidence of compatibility with sensitive hardware across long operating cycles. That dynamic has made third-party or manufacturer-backed validation a competitive consideration as the field of cooling-fluid providers seeking positions in the AI data centre build-out has grown.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The validation represents a broadening of the criteria buyers can apply when evaluating cooling fluids for AI infrastructure. By incorporating supply-chain and operational assessments alongside materials and chemistry tests, the NVIDIA program extends the scope of due diligence beyond laboratory performance to include questions of sourcing stability — a concern for operators planning deployments across multiple regions. As liquid cooling moves further into mainstream data centre design, validated product lists from major chip and systems vendors are expected to carry increasing weight in specification and procurement processes.
body_zh: |
  嘉實多（Castrol）宣布，旗下兩款直冷式液體冷卻液Castrol ON PG25與Castrol ON PG25T，已通過輝達（NVIDIA）技術審核，並正式納入「輝達驗證AI工廠基礎架構」計畫。此項認證旨在讓資料中心業者、設備製造商及冷卻系統供應商，在為AI運算部署選配冷卻液時能獲得更充分的保障與信心。

  兩款產品均以丙二醇（propylene glycol）為基礎液體，專為直冷式冷卻系統設計——其運作原理是讓冷卻液流經附著於處理器上的冷板，涵蓋中央處理器（CPU）與繪圖處理器（GPU）。嘉實多說明，PG25採用OAT（有機酸技術）配方，PG25T則採用IAT（無機酸技術）配方，但公告中未詳細說明兩款產品在商業定位上的完整差異。

  ## 驗證涵蓋範疇

  輝達的技術審查涵蓋多個面向，包括耐腐蝕性、熱穩定性、稀釋用水水質、丙二醇基礎液體純度，以及與液冷迴路中所有接觸材料的相容性——含括金屬、塑膠、密封件及其他與冷卻液直接接觸的零組件。此外，審查範圍還超出流體化學性質本身，進一步評估嘉實多的企業營運能力與全球供應鏈韌性，反映出業者在多個站點與地區規模化部署液冷時，對穩定供貨來源的高度關切。

  嘉實多資料中心全球技術經理金成雅（Sung A. Kim）表示，兩款冷卻液在送交輝達審核前，已先完成內部測試。「我們在每一款熱管理冷卻液上市之前，都會在嚴苛的測試條件下進行全面評估。輝達的認證進一步確認，PG25（OAT）與PG25T（IAT）確實達到該標準，即便面對當今AI基礎架構的規模與強度亦然，」金成雅說。

  ## AI熱負載推動需求成長

  報導指出，AI伺服器的耗電量與散熱量遠高於傳統企業級硬體，使熱能輸出超出許多純氣冷架構所能應對的上限。業者面臨在提升運算密度的同時，必須同步控制電力消耗、用水量及設備可靠性等多重壓力。直冷式冷卻的優勢在於直接在元件層級排除熱能，而非單純仰賴送風系統冷卻整個機房。

  在密閉循環的直冷式系統中，冷卻液持續循環且不與大氣接觸，有助於減少蒸發性水耗、提升用水效能（WUE）。隨著AI資料中心在資源消耗方面受到社會各界更多關注，用水效能已成為一項日益受重視的衡量指標。

  ## 全球佈局與競爭背景

  嘉實多旗下Castrol ON熱管理品牌的市場版圖涵蓋美國、歐洲、中東、中國、澳洲及東南亞。嘉實多熱管理暨資料中心全球總裁黃彼得（Peter Huang）將此次認證定位為產品性能與企業規模供貨能力的雙重印證。「將Castrol ON PG25與PG25T納入輝達驗證AI工廠基礎架構，讓客戶對這兩款冷卻液的性能表現，以及嘉實多在全球各市場提供一致支援的能力，都能更加安心，」黃彼得說。

  對於冷卻液供應商而言，獲納入生態系驗證計畫，可直接影響採購決策——尤其是在客戶需要具備文件佐證、以證明產品在長期運行週期中與精密硬體相容的情況下。隨著投身AI資料中心建設的冷卻液供應商持續增加，第三方或原廠背書的認證，已逐漸成為市場競爭的重要考量。

  ## 對產業的意義

  此次認證代表採購方在評估AI基礎架構冷卻液時，可援引的標準正在擴展。輝達計畫除材料與化學性質測試外，也將供應鏈與企業營運評估納入審查範疇，將盡職調查的範圍從實驗室性能延伸至供貨穩定性——這對於規劃跨多個地區部署的業者而言，是一項關鍵考量。隨著液冷技術在資料中心設計中逐步邁向主流，主要晶片與系統廠商所發布的驗證產品清單，預計將在規格制定與採購流程中發揮愈來愈舉足輕重的影響力。
