body_en: |
  Average refrigerant leak rates across German cooling and refrigeration systems fell to 1.0% in 2025, down from 3.2% in 2017, according to data published by VDKF, the German association of refrigeration and air conditioning companies (Verband Deutscher Kälte-Klima-Fachbetriebe).

  The figures are based on records from nearly 67,000 system operators covering approximately 307,000 systems logged in VDKF's own VDKF-LEC software platform, which the association uses to track leak performance across the sector.

  ## Breakdown by system type

  The 1.0% headline figure includes breakdown events and spans all system categories. Within that aggregate, performance varies considerably by application:

  - Commercial refrigeration (supermarket cases, cold stores, and similar): **1.7%** — the highest of any category
  - Industrial refrigeration: between the commercial and air conditioning bands (specific figure not disclosed in the data)
  - Special systems: similarly intermediate
  - Split air conditioning systems: **0.4%** — the lowest of any category

  Commercial refrigeration systems typically carry larger refrigerant charges and operate under greater thermal cycling stress than split air conditioning units, which likely accounts for the gap between the two extremes.

  ## Data scope and methodology

  The VDKF-LEC software primarily covers systems for which German law mandates regular leak checks — generally larger systems above defined charge thresholds. Smaller systems are only partially represented in the dataset, meaning the true sector-wide average could differ from the reported figure. VDKF did not quantify the degree of under-coverage for small-scale equipment.

  ## What the trend means for the industry

  The decline from 3.2% in 2017 to 1.0% in 2025 — a reduction of more than two-thirds over eight years — indicates that tightened leak-check regimes and improved service practices have measurably reduced refrigerant losses from the installed base covered by the data. Refrigerant losses matter both for operating cost, since lost charge must be replaced, and for environmental impact, because many refrigerants in use are potent greenhouse gases.

  The data covers systems already subject to statutory inspection requirements, meaning the reported improvement reflects outcomes in the regulated, professionally maintained segment of the market. Whether similar gains have occurred in the smaller systems outside the dataset's full scope remains uncharacterised by VDKF's current reporting.

  VDKF said the average leakage rate has continued to decline, suggesting the downward trend observed across the eight-year series was still moving in the same direction as of the 2025 measurement period.
body_zh: |
  根據德國冷凍空調業者協會（VDKF，即Verband Deutscher Kälte-Klima-Fachbetriebe）公布的數據，德國冷凍冷藏系統的平均冷媒洩漏率在2025年降至1.0%，較2017年的3.2%明顯下滑。

  上述數字來自近67,000名系統操作業者的紀錄，涵蓋約307,000套系統，這些資料皆登錄於VDKF自行開發的VDKF-LEC軟體平台，該協會以此追蹤整個產業的洩漏表現。

  ## 各系統類型分類

  1.0%的整體數字涵蓋故障事件，並橫跨所有系統類別。在這一整體數字之下，各應用場域的表現差異相當顯著：

  - 商業冷凍（超市冷藏展示櫃、冷藏庫及類似設備）：**1.7%**——各類別中最高
  - 工業冷凍：介於商業冷凍與空調之間（數據中未揭露具體數字）
  - 特殊系統：同樣居中
  - 分離式空調系統：**0.4%**——各類別中最低

  商業冷凍系統通常冷媒充填量較大，且承受比分離式空調更為劇烈的熱循環壓力，這可能是兩者之間差距的主要原因。

  ## 數據範圍與方法

  VDKF-LEC軟體主要涵蓋德國法規規定須定期進行洩漏檢測的系統，一般為冷媒充填量超過特定門檻的較大型設備。較小型系統在數據集中僅有部分代表性，這意味著整個產業的實際平均值可能與所報告的數字有所出入。VDKF並未量化小型設備資料覆蓋不足的程度。

  ## 趨勢對產業的意義

  從2017年的3.2%降至2025年的1.0%，八年間降幅超過三分之二，顯示更嚴格的洩漏檢測制度與改善的服務實務，已在數據所涵蓋的現有設備存量中，確實減少了冷媒損失。冷媒洩漏不僅影響營運成本——因為損失的冷媒必須補充——同時也對環境造成衝擊，因為目前仍在使用的許多冷媒都是效力強大的溫室氣體。

  這份數據所涵蓋的系統已受到法定檢查要求的規範，因此所呈現的改善，反映的是受管制、由專業人員維護的市場區塊的實際成果。至於數據集範圍之外的較小型系統是否也出現類似的進展，目前VDKF的報告並未加以說明。

  VDKF表示，平均洩漏率持續下降，顯示在這八年系列數據中觀察到的下降趨勢，截至2025年的測量期仍朝同一方向持續改善。
