  ## Cooling moves from afterthought to boardroom priority

  AI workloads and accelerating expansion into new European markets are forcing data centre operators to rethink how they approach cooling infrastructure, Carrier's European data centre director said at Datacloud Global Congress 2026 in Monaco.

  Bertrand Rotagnon, Executive Director for Data Centres in Europe at Carrier — a major supplier of heating, ventilation, and cooling systems — told Capacity Media that rapid market growth, AI-driven power density, and regional shifts are collectively reshaping how data centres are designed and built across the continent.

  ## Expansion beyond established markets

  Operators are moving beyond Europe's traditional data centre hubs and into markets including the Nordics and Southern Europe, Rotagnon said. That geographic expansion is introducing new site conditions and infrastructure requirements that place additional demands on thermal management systems.

  AI workloads, which pack significantly more computing power into each rack than conventional server configurations, generate substantially higher heat loads per unit of floor space. That density shift is a central driver of the design changes Rotagnon described.

  ## How thermal infrastructure is evolving

  Rotagnon outlined several areas where cooling approaches are changing in response to market pressures: modular deployment, which allows cooling capacity to be added in discrete increments as demand grows; faster commissioning timelines, which compress the period between construction and operation; integration with renewable energy sources; and heat reuse, which captures waste heat from data centre operations for other purposes.

  Taken together, Rotagnon said, these developments are making thermal infrastructure central to the next phase of European data centre growth — not a cost to be minimised but a strategic investment that shapes how and where capacity can be built.

  ## Industry significance

  The framing reflects a broader shift in how the data centre sector is treating cooling: as a constraint on AI deployment when inadequate, and as a competitive differentiator when well-executed. As operators extend into markets with different climate conditions and grid mixes, the choice and design of thermal systems increasingly determines whether a site can support AI-grade workloads at scale.

  Datacloud Global Congress 2026 runs in Monaco; Datacloud USA is scheduled for 1 September 2026.

  ## 冷卻從事後考量躍升為董事會議題

  AI工作負載與加速進軍歐洲新興市場的雙重壓力，正迫使資料中心業者從根本上重新審視冷卻基礎設施的規劃方式。Carrier歐洲資料中心總監日前在摩納哥舉行的2026年Datacloud全球大會上如此表示。

  Carrier是暖通空調系統的主要供應商。其歐洲資料中心業務執行總監Bertrand Rotagnon接受《Capacity Media》採訪時指出，市場的快速成長、AI驅動的功率密度提升，以及區域布局的結構性轉變，正共同重塑整個歐洲大陸的資料中心設計與建設方式。

  ## 擴張版圖延伸至傳統重鎮之外

  Rotagnon表示，業者正跳脫歐洲傳統的資料中心核心市場，積極進軍北歐與南歐等新興地區。這波地理版圖的擴張帶來了截然不同的場址條件與基礎設施需求，對熱管理系統提出了更嚴苛的要求。

  AI工作負載在每個機架內塞入的運算能力，遠超傳統伺服器配置，每單位樓地板面積所產生的熱負荷也大幅攀升。這種密度的質變，正是Rotagnon所描述的設計變革核心驅動力。

  ## 熱管理基礎設施的演進方向

  Rotagnon進一步點出，面對市場壓力，冷卻方案正在幾個面向上加速演進：其一是**模組化部署**，可隨需求成長分階段擴充冷卻容量；其二是**縮短調試時程**，壓縮從竣工到正式上線的等待期；其三是**整合再生能源**；其四是**廢熱再利用**，將資料中心運作過程中產生的廢熱轉作其他用途加以回收。

  Rotagnon表示，綜合來看，上述發展趨勢正使熱管理基礎設施成為歐洲資料中心下一階段成長的核心要素——它不再只是需要壓低的成本項目，而是決定容量能在哪裡、以何種方式落地的戰略投資。

  ## 產業意義

  這樣的論述框架，折射出資料中心業界對於冷卻議題更廣泛的認知轉變：冷卻能力不足時，它是AI部署的瓶頸；執行得宜時，它則成為差異化的競爭優勢。隨著業者延伸至氣候條件與電網結構各異的新市場，熱管理系統的選擇與設計，愈來愈決定了一個場址能否在規模化部署下支撐AI等級的工作負載。
