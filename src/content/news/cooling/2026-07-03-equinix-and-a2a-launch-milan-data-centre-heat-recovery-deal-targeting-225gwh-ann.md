body_en: |
  Equinix and Italian energy operator A2A have launched an industrial partnership to recover waste heat from data centre servers at Equinix's campus in Settimo Milanese and feed it into Milan's district heating network, in what the companies describe as one of the most significant circular economy initiatives applied to digital infrastructure in Italy.

  Under the agreement, Equinix will design and manage a system to export thermal energy from the Settimo Milanese campus, working with customers whose servers generate the heat. A2A will construct a new Energy Centre housing four large-scale heat pumps (which capture and concentrate waste heat for distribution) with a combined capacity of 72 megawatts (MW), along with two thermal storage systems totalling 6,000 cubic metres.

  ## Projected output and city impact

  Once fully operational, the project is expected to recover up to 225 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of thermal energy per year. That volume would increase heat delivered through A2A's Milan district heating network, which pipes hot water to buildings across the city for space heating, by around 20%, the companies said. The recovered heat would be sufficient to meet the heating needs of more than 21,000 homes and would avoid more than 345,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually.

  A2A will also expand its district heating infrastructure to reach a wider area of Milan, including landmarks such as the Duomo cathedral and Palazzo Reale.

  ## What executives said

  Adaire Fox-Martin, chief executive and president of Equinix, said the partnership demonstrates how "essential digital infrastructure and local sustainability goals can work in service of each other."

  Emanuela Grandi, managing director of Equinix Italy, said the project is expected to become one of the largest data centre heat-export initiatives in Europe outside the Nordic countries.

  Renato Mazzoncini, chief executive of A2A, described heat recovery as "a key lever for maximising the value of digital hubs and accelerating the decarbonisation of cities," and said the project aligns with investments outlined in A2A's 2035 Plan.

  ## Context

  A2A has previously launched heat recovery projects from data centres elsewhere in Lombardy, the company said. The Settimo Milanese initiative extends that approach to a larger scale and to the Milan city network specifically.

  A2A's planned expansion of the district heating network to include the Duomo and Palazzo Reale is the most concrete forward-looking development the companies have announced.

  ## Equinix與A2A攜手推動米蘭資料中心廢熱回收　年供熱量上看225GWh

  Equinix與義大利能源業者A2A宣布建立工業合作夥伴關係，計畫將Equinix塞蒂莫米蘭內塞（Settimo Milanese）園區內伺服器所產生的廢熱，導入米蘭市區域供熱網路循環利用。兩家公司表示，此舉是義大利迄今將循環經濟理念應用於數位基礎設施的最具代表性的專案之一。

  根據協議，Equinix將設計並管理一套廢熱輸出系統，協同在該園區運行伺服器的客戶共同產出熱源；A2A則將新建一座能源中心（Energy Centre），配置四台大型熱泵，合計容量達72百萬瓦（MW），並建置兩套總容量達6,000立方公尺的熱能儲存系統。熱泵的作用在於擷取並集中廢熱，以利後續配送至供熱網路。

  ## 預期產出與對城市的影響

  專案全面運轉後，預計每年可回收高達225十億瓦時（GWh）的熱能。兩家公司表示，這將使A2A米蘭區域供熱網路的供熱量提升約20%；該網路以熱水管道向全市各建築物輸送暖氣。回收的熱能足以滿足逾2萬1千戶家庭的供暖需求，並每年減少超過34萬5千噸的二氧化碳排放。

  A2A同時計畫擴建區域供熱基礎設施，將供熱範圍延伸至米蘭更廣泛的地區，包括大教堂（Duomo）及皇家宮殿（Palazzo Reale）等地標建築。

  ## 高層主管表示

  Equinix執行長暨總裁艾德爾·福克斯—馬汀（Adaire Fox-Martin）表示，這項合作充分說明「不可或缺的數位基礎設施與在地永續目標，可以彼此相輔相成、共同實現」。

  Equinix義大利董事總經理艾瑪努拉·格蘭迪（Emanuela Grandi）則指出，此專案有望成為北歐地區以外歐洲規模最大的資料中心廢熱輸出專案之一。

  A2A執行長雷納托·馬佐尼奇尼（Renato Mazzoncini）將廢熱回收定位為「最大化數位樞紐價值、加速城市去碳化的關鍵槓桿」，並表示本專案與A2A「2035計畫」中所規劃的投資方向高度契合。

  ## 背景脈絡

  A2A表示，此前已在倫巴底（Lombardy）大區其他地點推動過資料中心廢熱回收專案。塞蒂莫米蘭內塞的合作案，將此一模式擴展至更大規模，並首次直接接入米蘭市的供熱網路。

  將大教堂與皇家宮殿納入區域供熱網路延伸範圍的計畫，是兩家公司目前對外公布的最具體的前瞻性布局。
