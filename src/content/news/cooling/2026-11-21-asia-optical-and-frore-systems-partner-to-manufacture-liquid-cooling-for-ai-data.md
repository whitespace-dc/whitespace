  ## Partnership targets global data center operators

  Taiwan-listed Asia Optical (stock code: 3019) and U.S. chip cooling firm Frore Systems are partnering to manufacture advanced liquid cooling products for AI data centers, Asia Optical announced on November 20. The arrangement will draw on the precision machining expertise of Asia Optical's subsidiary Jingxi International and the group's operational management, with manufacturing to be developed at Asia Optical's factory in the Philippines.

  Asia Optical said the goal is to establish the partnership as a long-term supplier to large-scale data center operators worldwide.

  ## Frore Systems' direct-touch liquid cooling plates

  At the center of the partnership is Frore Systems' liquid cooling technology. Asia Optical described Frore Systems as setting new benchmarks in global thermal management with what it called a "revolutionary" liquid cooling architecture. The specific product targeted at data centers is a direct-touch advanced liquid cooling plate — a design in which the cooling surface contacts the heat source directly rather than through an intermediary spreader, which is intended to improve heat transfer efficiency.

  According to Asia Optical, the technology can deliver a substantial increase in a data center's computing power — the density of processors a facility can run — without increasing energy or water consumption compared to current levels. The company said the design would reduce capital construction costs for large data center operators and ease strain on local electrical grids, water supplies, and land use.

  ## Philippines manufacturing base, Jingxi International's role

  Asia Optical identified its Philippines factory as the production site for the liquid cooling line. The company's subsidiary Jingxi International, described as a precision machining specialist, will contribute manufacturing capability to the partnership. Asia Optical did not disclose production volumes, timelines, or the financial terms of the arrangement.

  ## Financial context: record margins through the first nine months of 2026

  Asia Optical reported third-quarter 2026 net profit attributable to shareholders of approximately NT$839 million, with earnings per share of NT$3.00. The company said all three profitability ratios — gross margin, operating margin, and net margin — reached their highest levels for any third quarter in the company's history.

  For the first nine months of 2026, net profit attributable to shareholders reached approximately NT$1.67 billion, with earnings per share of NT$5.98. Gross profit, operating profit, and net profit for the nine-month period each set new records for the comparable period. Asia Optical said the nine-month profit figure already represents 91 percent of its full-year 2025 earnings.

  ## Outlook includes cameras, AR/VR, and AI applications

  Looking to the fourth quarter, Asia Optical said digital camera sales remain strong and shipment volumes continue to grow at a high rate. The company noted that four of the five best-selling digital camera models in the Japanese market are manufactured by Asia Optical.

  Beyond cameras and liquid cooling, Asia Optical said it will continue to accelerate development of products in augmented reality, virtual reality, metalens optics — a class of flat optical components that use nanostructures to focus light — and AI applications, with the stated aim of expanding a product portfolio centered on AI-related uses.

  ## 合作目標鎖定全球資料中心業者

  上市公司亞光（股票代號：3019）與美國晶片散熱業者Frore Systems宣布合作，共同為AI資料中心製造先進液冷產品。亞光於11月20日公告，本次合作將整合旗下子公司晶璽國際的精密加工技術及集團的營運管理能量，製造端將建置於亞光位在菲律賓的工廠。

  亞光表示，目標是透過這項合作，成為全球大型資料中心業者的長期供應商。

  ## Frore Systems的直觸式液冷板技術

  此次合作的核心在於Frore Systems的液冷技術。亞光形容Frore Systems在全球熱管理領域樹立了新標竿，並以「革命性」液冷架構加以描述。針對資料中心市場推出的具體產品，是一款直觸式先進液冷板——這種設計讓冷卻面直接接觸熱源，不需透過中間導熱片，藉此提升導熱效率。

  根據亞光的說法，此技術能夠大幅提升資料中心的運算密度——亦即單一設施可運行的處理器數量——而無須增加目前的能耗或用水量。亞光表示，此設計可降低大型資料中心業者的資本建設成本，並減輕對當地電力網路、水資源及土地使用的壓力。

  ## 菲律賓廠負責生產，晶璽國際扮演要角

  亞光指出，液冷產品線將在菲律賓工廠進行生產。旗下子公司晶璽國際被定位為精密加工專家，將為合作提供製造能量。亞光未披露產能規模、時程規劃或合作財務條款等細節。

  ## 財務背景：2026年前三季利潤率創歷史新高

  亞光公告2026年第三季歸屬母公司股東淨利約為新臺幣8.39億元，每股盈餘為3.00元。公司表示，毛利率、營業利益率及淨利率三項獲利指標，均創下公司歷史上單一第三季的最高紀錄。

  2026年前三季，歸屬母公司股東淨利累計約為新臺幣16.7億元，每股盈餘為5.98元。前三季毛利、營業利益及淨利各自創下同期新高。亞光表示，前三季獲利金額已相當於2025年全年獲利的91%。

  ## 展望涵蓋相機、AR／VR及AI應用

  展望第四季，亞光表示數位相機銷售持續熱絡，出貨量持續高速成長。公司並指出，日本市場銷售排行前五名的數位相機中，有四款由亞光製造。

  相機與液冷業務之外，亞光表示將持續加速推動擴增實境、虛擬實境、超透鏡光學元件——一種利用奈米結構聚焦光線的平面光學元件——以及AI應用相關產品的開發，明確以擴大以AI應用為核心的產品組合為目標。
