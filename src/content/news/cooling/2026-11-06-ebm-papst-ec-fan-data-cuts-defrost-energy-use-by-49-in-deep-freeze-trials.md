body_en: |
  EBM-Papst has developed a technology that uses live operating data from EC (electronically commutated) fans to determine when refrigeration evaporators actually need defrosting, replacing fixed-interval defrost schedules with cycles triggered only by measured icing conditions.

  Laboratory results and initial field tests confirm the approach reduces energy consumption significantly, the German manufacturer said.

  ## What the technology does

  Conventional refrigeration systems defrost evaporators on a fixed timer — typically every several hours — regardless of whether ice has actually built up to a level that impairs performance. EBM-Papst's Defrost on Demand system reads operating data already generated by the EC fan to assess the icing state of the evaporator in real time and initiates a defrost cycle only when the data indicates one is needed.

  ## Energy and cycle results

  In studies conducted in a deep-freeze application running at -18°C, the technology reduced weekly energy consumption from 7.8 kWh to 3.4 kWh compared with time-controlled defrosting — a reduction of more than 49%, according to the company. Instead of a fixed defrost cycle every seven hours, the system required only three to six defrost cycles per week, depending on the actual icing condition of the evaporator.

  ## Integration approaches under development

  EBM-Papst is currently evaluating two paths for integrating the technology into existing refrigeration infrastructure. The first is based on an EVCO cooling unit controller, which manages the full refrigeration cycle and can incorporate the functions required for on-demand defrosting. The second uses the company's own NEXAIRA Gateway, a digital platform that evaluates fan operating data and can provide additional monitoring and analysis functions. Both approaches are undergoing further development and are being validated through pilot projects, the company said.

  ## Significance and next steps

  Unnecessary defrost cycles consume energy and subject refrigerated goods and mechanical components to repeated thermal cycles; eliminating them has direct implications for operating costs and system longevity in commercial refrigeration. The ability to derive icing state from fan operating data — without additional dedicated sensors — lowers the barrier to adoption for existing installations.

  EBM-Papst said further pilot projects are planned to confirm the results across different operating conditions beyond the -18°C deep-freeze environment tested to date.

  德國廠商EBM-Papst開發出一項技術，能夠運用EC（電子換向）風扇產生的即時運行資料，判斷冷凍系統蒸發器實際的結冰狀況，以此取代固定時間間隔的除霜排程，僅在量測結果顯示確實有結冰需求時才啟動除霜週期。

  根據該公司表示，實驗室測試結果與初步現場試驗均已證實，這套方法能顯著降低能源消耗。

  ## 技術運作原理

  傳統冷凍系統依固定計時器對蒸發器進行除霜，通常每隔數小時執行一次，無論是否真的積累了足以影響效能的冰層。EBM-Papst研發的「按需除霜（Defrost on Demand）」系統，直接讀取EC風扇本身既有的運行資料，即時評估蒸發器的結冰狀態，唯有當資料顯示確實需要時，才啟動除霜週期。

  ## 能耗與除霜週期結果

  在攝氏零下18度深冷凍應用環境中進行的研究顯示，與計時式除霜相比，這套技術將每週能耗從7.8度電（kWh）降至3.4度電，降幅逾49%。除霜週期亦從固定每七小時一次，縮減至每週僅需三至六次，實際次數取決於蒸發器的結冰程度。

  ## 整合方案持續開發中

  EBM-Papst目前正評估將這項技術導入現有冷凍基礎設施的兩種路徑。第一種以EVCO冷卻機組控制器為基礎，該控制器負責管理完整冷凍週期，並可整合按需除霜所需的相關功能。第二種則採用該公司自有的NEXAIRA閘道器——一套可評估風扇運行資料、並提供額外監控與分析功能的數位平台。上述兩種方案均在持續開發中，並透過先導專案進行驗證。

  ## 意義與後續計畫

  不必要的除霜週期不僅耗費能源，更會讓冷藏商品與機械元件反覆承受熱循環衝擊；消除多餘除霜週期，對商用冷凍設備的營運成本與系統壽命均有直接效益。這套方案能從風扇運行資料推算結冰狀態，無需加裝專屬感測器，大幅降低現有設備導入的門檻。

  EBM-Papst表示，後續計畫將展開更多先導專案，以驗證這套技術在目前已測試的攝氏零下18度深冷凍環境以外、不同運行條件下的實際表現。
