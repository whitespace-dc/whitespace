body_en: |
  Chillventa 2026, the refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat pump trade show held in Nuremberg, Germany, is on course to be the largest in the event's history, with 1,095 exhibitors booked as of early August — two months before the show opens on October 13.

  The current count exceeds the 1,010 exhibitors recorded at Chillventa 2024 and surpasses the previous all-time high of 1,019 exhibitors set in 2018, according to the Cooling Post, which cited the exhibition's own website.

  ## Expanded floor space drives growth

  The increase in exhibitor capacity follows a decision by the event's organiser to add Hall 1 at the NürnbergMesse venue to the existing halls used for the show, which previously ran across Halls 4 through 9. The addition brings total available exhibition space to 85,930 square metres.

  ## What the numbers mean for the industry

  With two months remaining before the show opens, the final exhibitor count is likely to climb further, meaning the 1,095 figure represents a floor rather than a ceiling for the record. The growth in participation reflects rising demand for floor space at one of the sector's principal international gatherings.

  Chillventa 2026 runs from October 13 to 15 at the NürnbergMesse in Nuremberg, Germany.

  ## Chillventa 2026參展商破紀錄達1,095家，紐倫堡展館擴增展廳

  在德國紐倫堡舉辦的冷凍、空調與熱泵專業展覽Chillventa 2026，正朝著史上規模最大的一屆穩步邁進。截至8月初——距離10月13日開幕尚有兩個月——已有1,095家參展商完成報名，締造歷屆之最。

  根據《Cooling Post》援引展覽官方網站的數據，此次報名人數不僅超越Chillventa 2024的1,010家，也打破了2018年所創下的歷史紀錄1,019家。

  ## 展廳擴增帶動容量成長

  參展商數量得以突破上限，源於主辦單位決定在原有展館基礎上新增1號展廳。此前，展覽使用的是紐倫堡展覽中心（NürnbergMesse）的4至9號展廳，加入1號展廳後，總可用展覽面積達到85,930平方公尺。

  ## 數字背後的產業意義

  由於距離展覽開幕仍有兩個月，最終參展商數字極有可能持續攀升，這意味著目前的1,095家僅是一個下限，而非天花板。參展規模的持續成長，反映出業界對這一國際重要展覽攤位需求的日益旺盛。

  Chillventa 2026將於10月13日至15日在德國紐倫堡展覽中心（NürnbergMesse）盛大舉行。
