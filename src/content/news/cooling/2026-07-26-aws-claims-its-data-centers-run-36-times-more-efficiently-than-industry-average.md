body_en: |
  Amazon Web Services said its global data centers operate 3.6 times more efficiently than the average conventional corporate data center, citing a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) score of 1.15 against an industry average of 1.67. AWS Indonesia Account Director Usman Niandinata disclosed the figures at the Lestari Summit 2026 in Jakarta on July 22, 2026.

  ## What PUE measures

  PUE is the ratio of a facility's total energy consumption to the energy consumed by its computing equipment alone. A score of 1.0 would mean every watt drawn from the grid reaches a server, with nothing lost to supporting systems. At a PUE of 1.15, AWS data centers use 1.15 watts for every 1 watt that reaches computing hardware, with the remaining 0.15 watts going to cooling, lighting, and other infrastructure. At the industry average of 1.67, conventional facilities lose significantly more power to those overhead systems — meaning 0.67 watts of overhead for every watt of compute.

  ## Technologies behind the efficiency

  Niandinata attributed AWS's efficiency to several design choices. The company uses its custom Graviton processors, which AWS says are engineered to deliver higher computing performance at lower energy consumption than general-purpose chips. AWS has also deployed advanced cooling systems designed to reduce water use as part of a company target to become water positive by 2030.

  ## Customer carbon tools and shared responsibility

  AWS offers a Customer Carbon Footprint Tool that allows customers to track estimated monthly carbon emissions from their cloud service usage, Niandinata said. The tool lets customers identify computing capacity that is no longer being used and adjust server allocations accordingly. Niandinata noted that many companies continue running servers that are not fully utilized, which consumes electricity without productive output.

  AWS frames its decarbonization approach as a shared responsibility model: the company commits to building efficient, low-emission infrastructure, while customers are responsible for optimizing their own application usage and computing capacity to control energy consumption.

  ## Sector context

  The information and communications technology sector accounts for an estimated 2 to 4 percent of global carbon emissions and consumes roughly 1.5 percent of the world's electricity, according to Niandinata. He said data center efficiency is therefore a meaningful lever for reducing the digital sector's emissions footprint.

  ## Targets and Indonesia operations

  AWS has committed to reaching net-zero carbon emissions by 2040 through The Climate Pledge, which the company says is 10 years ahead of the schedule set by the Paris Agreement. In Indonesia, AWS has partnered with state utility PT PLN (Persero) to supply renewable energy to its data centers, Niandinata said, as part of the company's global commitment to sourcing 100 percent of its energy from renewable sources.

  ## AWS宣稱旗下資料中心能源效率為業界平均的3.6倍

  亞馬遜雲端服務（AWS）表示，其全球資料中心的運作效率比一般企業傳統資料中心高出3.6倍，電源使用效率（PUE）評分為1.15，而業界平均值為1.67。AWS印尼區客戶總監烏斯曼·尼安迪納塔（Usman Niandinata）於2026年7月22日在雅加達舉辦的「Lestari高峰會2026」上公開了上述數據。

  ## PUE的衡量方式

  PUE是指一座設施的總用電量與其運算設備單獨耗電量之間的比值。若PUE為1.0，代表從電網引入的每一瓦特電力都直接送達伺服器，不存在任何損耗。在PUE為1.15的條件下，AWS資料中心每輸送1瓦特電力到運算硬體，總共需要消耗1.15瓦特，其餘的0.15瓦特則用於冷卻、照明及其他基礎設施。相較之下，業界平均PUE為1.67，傳統設施流失在這些附屬系統上的電力比例明顯偏高——也就是說，每1瓦特的運算用電，就有0.67瓦特耗費在額外開銷上。

  ## 效率背後的技術

  尼安迪納塔表示，AWS的高效率源自多項設計選擇。該公司採用自行研發的Graviton處理器，AWS宣稱這款處理器在相同運算效能下的能耗低於通用型晶片。AWS也部署了先進的冷卻系統，旨在減少用水量，作為公司目標在2030年實現「水資源正效益」的一環。

  ## 客戶碳排工具與共同責任

  尼安迪納塔表示，AWS提供「客戶碳排足跡工具」，讓客戶得以追蹤其雲端服務使用所產生的每月估算碳排放量。這套工具可協助客戶找出已不再使用的運算容量，並據此調整伺服器配置。尼安迪納塔指出，許多企業仍持續運行未被充分利用的伺服器，在沒有實質產出的情況下持續消耗電力。

  AWS將其去碳化策略定位為共同責任模式：公司承諾建置高效率、低排放的基礎設施，而客戶則有責任優化自身的應用程式使用方式與運算容量，以控制能源消耗。

  ## 產業背景

  尼安迪納塔表示，資訊與通訊科技產業約佔全球碳排放量的2%至4%，並消耗全球約1.5%的電力。他表示，提升資料中心效率因此是降低數位產業碳排足跡的重要槓桿之一。

  ## 減碳目標與印尼營運

  AWS已透過「氣候承諾」（The Climate Pledge）承諾於2040年達成淨零碳排，該公司表示這比《巴黎協定》所設定的期程提前了10年。在印尼，AWS已與國有電力公司PT PLN（Persero）合作，為其資料中心供應再生能源，尼安迪納塔說，這是公司全球承諾100%使用再生能源的一部分。
