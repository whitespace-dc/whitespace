body_en: |
  Rising temperatures and humidity levels are progressively cutting into the hours when data centers can use direct air free cooling, one of the cheapest and most energy-efficient cooling approaches available, according to research published in the journal *Scientific Reports* by atmospheric scientists at the University of Hawaiʻi (UH) at Mānoa.

  The study, led by Christina Karamperidou, a professor of atmospheric science at UH Mānoa, combined high-resolution hourly weather data, climate model simulations, and global data center location maps to measure how often outdoor air conditions exceed the thresholds required to run direct air free cooling. Direct air free cooling is a technique that draws naturally cold outdoor air directly into a facility to cool servers and equipment, eliminating or reducing the need for mechanical refrigeration.

  Over the 45-year historical record analyzed in the study, hours in which outdoor air was too hot and humid to support free cooling increased significantly, the researchers found, with tropical regions and the southeastern United States showing the most pronounced changes.

  ## Worst-case days deteriorating faster than averages

  A central finding of the study is that the sharpest deterioration does not always appear in daily average conditions. In several regions, conditions on the worst days of the year are worsening much faster than average-day conditions.

  "From an operational standpoint, it is the worst-weather days that typically drive emergency planning, system failover, backup capacity provisioning, and reliability decisions," Karamperidou said. "This means infrastructure planning cannot look only at average weather but must also account for how the most extreme days are changing over time."

  The researchers identified this as a pattern in which environmental stress accumulates on rare but high-impact events, rather than distributing evenly across a calendar year.

  ## Growing exposure across locations and markets

  Analysis at the location and market level showed that a growing number of data centers already face outdoor air that is too hot and humid to support free cooling for at least three months per year. Projections extending to mid-century indicate those constraints will continue to expand as global temperatures and humidity rise.

  "We found that periods in which temperature and humidity exceed the safe thresholds for natural cooling are now occurring more frequently and lasting longer in many regions," Karamperidou said. "This will reduce opportunities for free cooling for an increasing number of data centers worldwide."

  By analyzing datasets spanning the entire globe, the team said it was able to identify large-scale patterns and trends that would not be visible in studies focused on one or two locations.

  ## AI demand meets a narrowing cooling window

  The study identifies a compounding pressure: demand for data center capacity is rising sharply, driven in part by the expansion of artificial intelligence workloads, at the same time that climate change is narrowing the conditions under which the lowest-cost cooling method is viable.

  Karamperidou described the research as addressing a problem "at the intersection of climate, computer technology, energy, and water resources." Understanding how that intersection is shifting is essential for the long-term sustainability and resilience of data center infrastructure, the researchers said.

  The team said it hopes the findings can help determine where new technologies and advanced cooling methods could resolve the trade-offs between system reliability, energy consumption, and water use. Projections in the study indicate that constraints on free cooling will continue to widen through mid-century as temperatures and humidity increase globally.
body_zh: |
  根據夏威夷大學（UH）馬諾阿分校大氣科學家發表於《科學報告》期刊的最新研究，氣溫與濕度的持續攀升，正一點一滴地侵蝕資料中心能夠採用直接空氣自由冷卻的時數——而這正是目前最廉價、能源效益最高的冷卻方式之一。

  這項研究由 UH 馬諾阿分校大氣科學教授 Christina Karamperidou 主持，結合高解析度逐時氣象資料、氣候模型模擬，以及全球資料中心位置圖，量化戶外空氣條件超出直接空氣自由冷卻所需門檻的頻率。所謂直接空氣自由冷卻，是一種將戶外自然冷空氣直接引入機房，用以冷卻伺服器與設備的技術，可完全省去或大幅降低對機械製冷系統的依賴。

  研究人員分析長達 45 年的歷史氣象紀錄後發現，戶外空氣因溫度或濕度過高而無法支援自由冷卻的時數顯著增加，其中熱帶地區與美國東南部的變化最為明顯。

  ## 最惡劣天氣日的劣化速度遠超日均值

  這項研究的核心發現之一是：最劇烈的惡化並不總是體現在每日平均條件上。在若干地區，一年中狀況最差的幾天，其條件的惡化速度遠快於一般日的平均水準。

  Karamperidou 表示：「從營運角度來看，正是這些最惡劣的天氣日，通常驅動著緊急應變規劃、系統備援切換、備援容量配置，以及可靠性決策。這意味著基礎設施規劃不能只看平均氣候，還必須考量極端天氣日的變化趨勢。」

  研究人員將此現象定義為一種型態：環境壓力集中累積於罕見但衝擊重大的事件上，而非均勻分散於整個年度。

  ## 全球各地點與市場的曝險程度持續擴大

  針對各地點與市場的分析顯示，已有越來越多的資料中心，每年至少有三個月面臨戶外空氣溫度與濕度過高、無法支援自由冷卻的困境。延伸至本世紀中葉的預測更指出，隨著全球氣溫與濕度持續上升，這些限制將繼續擴大。

  Karamperidou 說：「我們發現，溫度與濕度超過自然冷卻安全門檻的時段，在許多地區已出現得更加頻繁，且持續時間更長。這將使全球越來越多的資料中心喪失使用自由冷卻的機會。」

  研究團隊表示，透過分析涵蓋全球的資料集，他們得以識別出大尺度的型態與趨勢，這是聚焦於單一或少數地點的研究所無法呈現的。

  ## AI 需求與日益收窄的冷卻時間窗口形成雙重壓力

  這項研究點出一個相互疊加的壓力：資料中心容量的需求正因人工智慧工作負載的擴張而急速攀升，而與此同時，氣候變遷卻正在壓縮成本最低的冷卻方式得以發揮效用的條件空間。

  Karamperidou 將這項研究描述為針對「氣候、電腦科技、能源與水資源交匯點」所衍生問題的回應。研究人員表示，理解這個交匯點如何持續演變，對於資料中心基礎設施的長期永續性與韌性至關重要。

  研究團隊表示，希望這些研究成果能有助於判斷新技術與先進冷卻方法，能在何處化解系統可靠性、能源消耗與用水量之間的取捨難題。研究中的預測顯示，隨著全球氣溫與濕度持續升高，自由冷卻所受到的限制將在本世紀中葉之前繼續擴大。
