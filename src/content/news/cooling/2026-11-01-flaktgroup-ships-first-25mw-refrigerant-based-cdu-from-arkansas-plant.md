body_en: |
  FläktGroup has produced its first 2.5MW direct-expansion (DX) cooling distribution unit (CDU) — a device that distributes cooled fluid to server racks — at its Arkansas manufacturing facility, delivering the unit to an undisclosed customer in the United States, the company announced.

  The CDU is refrigerant-based, meaning it uses a refrigerant fluid in its cooling loop rather than water or glycol as conventional liquid-to-liquid systems do. FläktGroup said the unit was engineered in close collaboration with the customer to meet specific project requirements.

  ## Scale of the deployment

  The first phase of the project is designed to serve a 25-megawatt (MW) data hall. A 25 MW data hall represents a substantial compute facility capable of powering thousands of high-density AI server racks. A second phase will double that capacity to 50 MW. The deployment is part of a larger campus with a total planned power capacity of 200 MW across eight development phases.

  ## What distinguishes this unit

  Conventional CDUs typically transfer heat between two liquid loops — for example, facility water on one side and server coolant on the other. FläktGroup's unit instead uses refrigerant-based cooling technology, which the company said was developed specifically to address the requirements of this project and modern data center applications more broadly.

  The unit was built at FläktGroup's Arkansas facility, which the announcement identifies as the production site.

  ## Company statement

  Marc Hayes, FläktGroup's executive vice-president for data centers in the Americas, said the project demonstrates the company's ability to develop solutions for emerging customer challenges. "As the industry increasingly adopts liquid cooling to support AI applications, this project demonstrates our ability to develop innovative solutions that address emerging customer challenges while strengthening our position within the rapidly evolving data centre cooling market," Hayes said.

  ## Industry context

  The announcement positions the unit as a response to growing demand for liquid cooling driven by AI workloads, which require higher power densities than traditional compute and generate more heat per rack than air cooling alone can efficiently manage. FläktGroup described the production of this CDU as a milestone in providing liquid cooling solutions for next-generation data centers.

  The 200 MW total campus capacity planned across eight phases, if fully built, would place it among the larger multi-phase data center developments in the U.S. market. The next near-term milestone is the second phase build-out, which would bring the served capacity to 50 MW.

  本次部署的第一期工程，設計用於服務一座25 MW的資料機廳。25 MW的資料機廳代表規模相當可觀的運算設施，能夠為數千台高密度AI伺服器機架提供電力。第二期工程將使容量倍增至50 MW。此次部署屬於一個更大型園區計畫的一部分，整體規劃電力容量達200 MW，分八個開發階段建設。

  傳統CDU通常在兩個液體迴路之間進行熱交換，例如一側為設施用水，另一側為伺服器冷卻液。FläktGroup的機組則採用冷媒式冷卻技術，該公司表示，這項技術是專為因應本次專案及現代資料中心的廣泛應用需求而開發。
