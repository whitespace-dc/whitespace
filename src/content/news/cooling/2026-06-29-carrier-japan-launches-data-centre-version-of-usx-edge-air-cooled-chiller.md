body_en: |
  Carrier Japan has started commercial sales of a data centre-specification version of its Universal Smart X (USX) Edge modular air-cooled chiller, the company announced, targeting the growing segment of facilities that use high-temperature chilled water for server cooling.

  The new series addresses two operational challenges the company says conventional chillers have not adequately solved: low energy efficiency when operating at chilled-water supply temperatures above 20°C, and slow recovery to full cooling capacity after a shutdown or power outage.

  ## Performance at high chilled-water temperatures

  Data centres increasingly use chilled water above 20°C — higher than the ranges for which most general-purpose chillers are optimised — because warmer supply temperatures reduce the energy penalty of refrigeration and are compatible with free cooling and liquid cooling infrastructure. Carrier says conventional chillers designed primarily for lower-temperature operation struggle to achieve strong energy-saving performance under these conditions.

  The USX Edge data centre variant expands the chiller's operating range in the low-pressure region of its refrigeration cycle, allowing it to maintain high efficiency specifically under high chilled-water temperature conditions. Carrier states that the coefficient of performance (COP) — a ratio measuring cooling output per unit of energy consumed — has been significantly improved in this temperature range compared with prior models. The company also says the efficiency gains are expected to benefit liquid cooling systems as that technology advances.

  ## Restart speed after shutdown

  Because data centres must maintain continuous chilled-water supply, the time between a chiller shutdown and its return to full capacity is a critical design and operational parameter. Carrier says the new model achieves 100% capacity recovery in 117 seconds for the 60 horsepower (hp) model and 150 seconds for the 70 hp model. Redesigned control logic optimises pre-startup processing and compressor stage sequencing to reach those figures.

  Faster restart times have a direct infrastructure benefit: they allow operators to reduce the size of chilled-water buffer storage tanks, which in turn lowers installation space requirements and construction costs, the company said.

  ## Harmonic suppression as standard

  The new series includes a pulse-width modulation (PWM) converter — a device that reduces electrical harmonic distortion, which can otherwise interfere with other equipment on the same power supply — fitted as standard equipment rather than as an option. Carrier states this brings current distortion below 5%, standardising harmonic suppression across the product line and contributing to overall electrical system efficiency in the facility.

  ## Product background

  The USX platform was first introduced in 2006 and uses a fully enclosed rotary compressor in a modular configuration. Carrier says cumulative shipments have exceeded 70,000 units across a range of applications, including data centres. The new data centre specification is a distinct variant within that platform rather than a replacement for existing USX models.

  ## Significance

  The product targets a documented shift in data centre cooling design toward higher chilled-water temperatures, which reduces refrigeration energy consumption and supports both air-side and liquid-side cooling architectures. By addressing efficiency and restart speed in a single product variant, Carrier Japan is positioning the USX Edge data centre series for facilities where cooling continuity and energy performance are simultaneous requirements rather than competing design priorities.
body_zh: |
  開利日本（Carrier Japan）宣布，已正式開始銷售針對資料中心規格所設計的 Universal Smart X（USX）Edge 模組化氣冷式冷水機組，鎖定運用高溫冷凍水進行伺服器冷卻的設施族群，主力訴求為在高冷凍水溫度下提升能效，並加快停電後恢復至滿載冷卻能力的速度。

  這款新系列所針對的，是開利表示傳統冷水機組迄今尚未完善解決的兩大運作痛點：一是冷凍水供水溫度高於 20°C 時能效低落的問題，二是機組停機或停電後恢復至滿載冷卻能力耗時過長的問題。

  ## 高冷凍水溫度下的性能表現

  資料中心越來越常採用 20°C 以上的冷凍水供水溫度——高於大多數通用型冷水機組的最佳運作區間——原因在於較高的供水溫度可降低冷凍循環所帶來的能耗懲罰，並與自然冷卻及液冷基礎設施相容。開利表示，傳統冷水機組主要針對較低溫度範圍設計，在高溫供水條件下難以達到良好的節能效果。

  USX Edge 資料中心版本拓展了冷凍循環低壓區的運作範圍，使機組得以在高冷凍水溫度的條件下維持高效能。開利指出，在此溫度區間，性能係數（COP，即每單位耗電所產生的冷卻輸出比值）相較前代機型已有顯著提升。公司也表示，隨著液冷技術持續進步，這項能效提升預計同樣將惠及液冷系統。

  ## 停機後的重啟速度

  由於資料中心必須持續供應冷凍水，冷水機組從停機到恢復滿載能力所需的時間，是設計與運作上的關鍵指標。開利表示，新型機組達到 100% 容量恢復所需時間，60 馬力（hp）機型為 117 秒，70 馬力機型則為 150 秒。重新設計的控制邏輯優化了啟動前的處理程序與壓縮機分段順序，從而達到上述數據。

  更快速的重啟能力帶來直接的基礎設施效益：允許機房業者縮減冷凍水緩衝儲水槽的容量，進而降低安裝空間需求與建設成本，開利如此說明。

  ## 諧波抑制列為標準配備

  新系列標準配備脈寬調變（PWM）轉換器——此裝置可降低電氣諧波失真，否則可能干擾同一電源上的其他設備——而非列為選購配件。開利表示，此設計將電流失真控制在 5% 以下，使諧波抑制功能在整個產品線上達到一致標準，並有助於提升設施整體電氣系統的效率。

  ## 產品背景

  USX 平台最早於 2006 年問世，採用全密封旋轉式壓縮機，以模組化架構設計。開利表示，該平台累計出貨量已超過 70,000 台，應用範疇涵蓋資料中心在內的多種場景。此次推出的資料中心規格版本，是 USX 平台旗下的獨立衍生型號，並非取代現有 USX 機型。

  ## 市場意義

  此產品瞄準資料中心冷卻設計向高冷凍水溫度轉型的明確趨勢——此趨勢可降低冷凍系統的能耗，並同時支援氣側與液側冷卻架構。透過在單一產品型號中同時解決能效與重啟速度兩大課題，開利日本正將 USX Edge 資料中心系列定位為同時追求冷卻連續性與能源效能的設施首選，而非在兩者之間各有取捨的折衷方案。
