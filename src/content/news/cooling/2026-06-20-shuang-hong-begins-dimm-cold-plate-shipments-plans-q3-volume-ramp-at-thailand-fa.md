body_en: |
  Taiwanese thermal management company Shuang Hong (3324) has begun small-volume trial shipments of a self-developed DIMM Cold Plate — a liquid cooling component designed to remove heat from memory modules in AI servers — and expects to scale output in the third quarter of 2026, the company said. Institutional analysts cited by Commercial Times said the product carries higher unit prices and gross margins than Shuang Hong's existing water-cooling plates, and could become a new growth driver for the company's AI liquid cooling business.

  The DIMM cold plate targets server platforms including NVIDIA HGX, DGX, and AMD SP series systems, according to Shuang Hong. Validation is nearly complete, the company said, with the product having moved from development into a small-volume trial production and shipment phase.

  ## A technically distinct product line

  Shuang Hong described the DIMM cold plate as a new product category, separate from its existing cold plates in both design and manufacturing process. The component must conform to the physical layout of server memory modules — the DIMM, or dual in-line memory module, slots that populate AI accelerator boards — and demands extremely tight dimensional tolerances and assembly precision. The company said the technical barrier is higher than that of conventional liquid cooling plates.

  The company attributed rising demand for memory-level cooling to increasing memory capacity and power draw in AI servers, which it said has made thermal management of memory subsystems a growing priority.

  ## Q3 demand picture

  Shuang Hong said enterprise customers and CSPs (cloud service providers — large-scale operators of data center infrastructure) have shown recovering demand recently, with urgent orders appearing. Even during a transition between server platform generations, the company said computing demand from customers has continued, lifting shipments of existing products.

  Pull demand related to GB300 server products has strengthened, the company said. An ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit) customer's water-cooling project is separately expected to enter mass production in the third quarter, providing additional support for second-half liquid cooling shipments.

  ## Quick-disconnect connector development

  Alongside the DIMM cold plate, Shuang Hong said it is accelerating development of proprietary QD (quick-disconnect) connectors — fittings that allow liquid cooling lines to be connected and separated without tools or fluid spillage. The company said it began integrating its own QD design starting with the GB300 platform and is extending that approach to ASIC customers, with the stated goals of improving supply chain independence, shortening lead times, optimizing cost structure, and reducing reliance on a single supply source.

  ## Thailand factory expansion

  Shuang Hong's Thailand factory currently produces water cooling plates and CDUs (cooling distribution units — rack-level systems that manage coolant flow to server components). In the second half of 2026, the company plans to add DIMM cold plate production capacity at the Thailand site, targeting 30,000 units per month. Shuang Hong said the Thailand factory will gradually become a key production hub for its liquid cooling thermal solutions.

  ## Industry significance

  The commercial introduction of DIMM-level liquid cooling represents an extension of active liquid cooling from processors and accelerators to memory subsystems — a broadening of the addressable market for cold plate manufacturers as AI server power densities rise. For Shuang Hong, institutional analysts said the combination of higher per-unit margins on the DIMM cold plate, applicability across major server platforms, recovering enterprise and CSP orders, and the ASIC water-cooling ramp positions the company for a stronger second half relative to the first.

  The Thailand factory's DIMM cold plate capacity is planned to reach 30,000 units per month in the second half of 2026.
body_zh: |
  台灣熱管理廠商雙鴻（3324）宣布，自主研發的DIMM冷板已進入小量試產出貨階段，預計2026年第三季擴大出量。所謂DIMM冷板，是一種液冷元件，專門用於帶走AI伺服器記憶體模組所產生的熱能。法人引述《工商時報》報導指出，該產品單價與毛利率均高於雙鴻現有水冷板，有望成為公司AI液冷業務的新成長動能。

  雙鴻表示，DIMM冷板鎖定的伺服器平台涵蓋輝達HGX、DGX以及超微SP系列系統。目前驗證工作已接近完成，產品已從開發階段推進至小量試產與試出貨階段。

  ## 技術定位獨特的新產品線

  雙鴻說明，DIMM冷板屬於全新產品類別，無論在設計或製程上，皆有別於現有冷板產品。該元件必須完全符合伺服器記憶體模組的實體配置——亦即AI加速卡上密布的DIMM（雙列直插式記憶體模組）插槽——對尺寸公差與組裝精度的要求極為嚴苛。雙鴻表示，其技術門檻明顯高於傳統液冷板。

  公司指出，AI伺服器記憶體容量持續擴增、功耗不斷攀升，使記憶體子系統的熱管理逐漸成為業界關注的重要課題，這正是DIMM層級冷卻需求持續升溫的根本原因。

  ## 第三季需求展望

  雙鴻表示，企業客戶與CSP（雲端服務提供商，即大規模資料中心基礎建設的運營業者）近期需求明顯回溫，緊急訂單也陸續浮現。即便在伺服器平台世代交替期間，客戶的運算需求依然持續，帶動現有產品出貨量走升。

  公司表示，與GB300伺服器產品相關的拉貨需求已顯著增強。另一方面，一家ASIC（特殊應用積體電路）客戶的水冷專案，預計將於第三季進入量產，可望為下半年液冷出貨提供額外支撐。

  ## 快速接頭自主研發提速

  除DIMM冷板外，雙鴻表示正加速開發自有QD（快速接頭）連接器——這是一種無需使用工具、且不會造成液體洩漏，即可完成液冷管路連接與分離的接頭元件。公司表示，自GB300平台起便開始導入自有QD設計，並將此做法延伸至ASIC客戶，目標在於提升供應鏈自主性、縮短交期、優化成本結構，並降低對單一供應來源的依賴。

  ## 泰國廠擴產計畫

  雙鴻泰國廠目前生產水冷板及CDU（冷卻分配單元，即管理冷卻液流向各伺服器元件的機架層級系統）。2026年下半年，公司計畫在泰國廠增設DIMM冷板產能，目標月產能達三萬片。雙鴻表示，泰國廠將逐步成為公司液冷散熱解決方案的重要生產基地。

  ## 產業意義

  DIMM層級液冷技術的商業化導入，代表主動式液冷的應用範疇正從處理器與加速器延伸至記憶體子系統——隨著AI伺服器功率密度持續提升，冷板廠商的潛在市場規模也隨之擴大。就雙鴻而言，法人分析師指出，DIMM冷板更高的單位毛利、廣泛適用於主流伺服器平台的特性、企業與CSP訂單回溫，以及ASIC水冷專案量產在即，多重利多匯聚，使公司下半年表現有望明顯優於上半年。

  泰國廠DIMM冷板產能規劃於2026年下半年達到月產三萬片的目標。
