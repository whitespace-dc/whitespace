body_en: |
  Trane Technologies reported record bookings of $7.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026, a 39% increase over the same period in 2025, with its Americas commercial HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) business identified as the primary driver.

  Organic bookings — which strip out the effects of currency movements and acquisitions — rose 37%, the company said. The Americas Commercial HVAC segment posted bookings growth of 50% and organic revenue growth in the low double digits, making it the standout performer across Trane's business lines.

  ## Backlog and revenue

  The record bookings pushed Trane's total backlog — orders received but not yet fulfilled — to $12.1 billion, also a company record. Reported revenues for the quarter reached $6.4 billion, up 11% year-on-year.

  ## Executive comment

  Chair and chief executive Dave Regnery described the quarter as "another outstanding quarter" and attributed the result primarily to the commercial HVAC business. "Our Americas Commercial HVAC business delivered a standout performance, with bookings growth of 50% and organic revenue growth in the low teens," Regnery said. He added that improving conditions in the residential and transport segments were also contributing to momentum.

  ## Significance

  The scale of the backlog — $12.1 billion — indicates Trane has secured a substantial pipeline of future work, providing visibility into revenue over coming quarters. With the Americas commercial HVAC segment leading growth at 50% bookings, demand for large-scale climate control systems in commercial and industrial settings, including data centers, appears to be accelerating. Regnery said the company is "carrying solid momentum into the second half of the year."

  創紀錄的訂單量，帶動特靈的總積壓訂單——即已接獲但尚未交付的訂單——攀升至121億美元，同樣寫下公司歷史新高。本季報告營收為64億美元，年增11%。
