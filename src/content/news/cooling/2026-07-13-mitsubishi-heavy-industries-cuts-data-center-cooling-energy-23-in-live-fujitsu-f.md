  ## MHI demonstrates whole-system cooling optimization at live Fujitsu data center

  Mitsubishi Heavy Industries has demonstrated a 2.3% reduction in total cooling system energy consumption at Fujitsu's Akashi data center in Hyogo Prefecture, Japan, by optimizing the entire cooling stack as a single system rather than tuning individual pieces of equipment. The company says the work was completed without interrupting data center operations.

  The project achieved a coefficient of performance (COP) improvement of more than 1.2 points on the facility's centrifugal chillers. COP is a ratio of useful cooling output to energy input; a higher COP means the same cooling is delivered with less electricity. The gains were realized in a single server room; MHI projects that extending the same approach across all server rooms in the facility would yield energy savings of 7.6% across the cooling system as a whole, producing a meaningful improvement in the facility's Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE), the standard ratio of total building power to IT equipment power.

  ## What was done differently

  Traditional data center cooling optimization typically targets individual units, such as a chiller or an air handling unit, in isolation. MHI's approach, developed at its research and innovation center, applied coordinated control across shared cooling infrastructure and the air conditioning units serving server rooms simultaneously.

  The team identified uneven temperature distribution in the server room as a primary constraint on wider system optimization. By rebalancing airflow through the room's air conditioning units, engineers improved return-air temperature distribution by 2 degrees Celsius. That improvement created headroom to fine-tune the operating points of the shared cooling infrastructure, including maintaining chilled water at an appropriate temperature based on simulation results.

  The demonstration used the existing mix of equipment from multiple manufacturers already installed at the facility, without replacing hardware.

  ## Vendor-agnostic deployment

  A stated differentiator of the project was that MHI's optimization technology is vendor-agnostic, meaning it can interface with and coordinate equipment regardless of manufacturer. Multi-vendor environments are common in operational data centers, where equipment is often procured across different generations and suppliers, complicating centralized control.

  "Operational data centres need to improve energy efficiency while utilising existing equipment," said Shoji Yamasaki, general manager of MHI's data center and energy management department. "This demonstration proves that system-level cooling optimisation, especially in multi-vendor environments, can deliver tangible results under real operating conditions."

  ## Industry significance and next steps

  The demonstration addresses a practical constraint facing data center operators: most efficiency gains in the industry have historically required capital investment in new, higher-efficiency equipment. An approach that extracts measurable savings from existing infrastructure, deployed without service interruption, reduces the barrier to improving energy performance at facilities that cannot afford downtime or large capital outlays.

  The 7.6% projected system-wide cooling savings figure, if validated at scale, would represent a substantial contribution to PUE reduction, given that cooling typically accounts for a significant share of a data center's total energy draw.

  MHI said it intends to expand the approach to integrate decarbonized energy, resilient power systems, high-efficiency cooling, and advanced digital solutions in support of data center operations worldwide.

  ## 三菱重工在富士通運營中資料中心展示全系統冷卻優化成果

  三菱重工日前宣布，在日本兵庫縣富士通明石資料中心完成一項實證，透過將整座冷卻系統作為單一整體進行優化——而非針對個別設備逐一調校——成功將冷卻系統總能耗降低2.3%。該公司表示，整個過程均在資料中心不中斷營運的情況下完成。

  此次專案令該設施離心式冷水機組的性能係數（COP，Coefficient of Performance）提升超過1.2點。COP是衡量有效冷卻輸出與能源投入的比值；COP越高，代表達到相同冷卻效果所消耗的電力越少。目前的改善成果僅在單一伺服器機房中實現；三菱重工預估，若將相同方案擴展至該設施所有伺服器機房，冷卻系統整體節能幅度可達7.6%，並將大幅提升設施的電能使用效率（PUE，Power Usage Effectiveness）——此指標為建築總用電量與IT設備用電量之比值，是業界的標準衡量基準。

  ## 有別於傳統的優化方式

  傳統資料中心冷卻優化通常著眼於個別設備，例如單獨針對冷水機組或空氣處理機組進行調整。三菱重工的方案源自其研究暨創新中心，採用的是跨共享冷卻基礎設施與機房空調機組的協調控制，同步進行整合調度。

  工程團隊發現，機房內溫度分布不均是阻礙全系統進一步優化的主要瓶頸。透過重新平衡機房空調機組的氣流配置，工程師成功將回風溫度分布改善了攝氏2度。這一改善為共享冷卻基礎設施的運行點精細調校創造了空間，其中包括根據模擬結果，將冷卻水維持在適當溫度。

  此次實證完全沿用設施內現有的多品牌混合設備，未替換任何硬體。

  ## 跨廠商部署能力

  本次專案的一大亮點在於，三菱重工的優化技術具備廠商中立性，能夠與不同製造商的設備介接並進行協調控制。多廠商混合環境在實際運營的資料中心中相當普遍——設備往往跨越不同世代、來自不同供應商，使得集中控制大為複雜。

  三菱重工資料中心暨能源管理部門總經理山崎昭二表示：「運營中的資料中心需要在善用現有設備的同時提升能源效率。此次實證證明，系統層級的冷卻優化——尤其是在多廠商環境中——能夠在真實運行條件下交出切實的成績。」

  ## 產業意義與後續規劃

  此次實證所回應的，正是資料中心業者長期面臨的現實困境：過去業界的效率提升多半仰賴投入資本購置新型高效設備。如今有一套方案能夠在不中斷服務、無需龐大資本支出的前提下，從現有基礎設施中挖掘出可量測的節能成效，大幅降低了那些無法承受停機或大規模投資的設施改善能源績效的門檻。

  若7.6%的全機房冷卻節能預估數字能在大規模部署中獲得驗證，將是對降低PUE的重大貢獻——畢竟冷卻系統通常佔資料中心總能耗相當大的比例。

  三菱重工表示，未來將進一步擴展此方案，整合去碳化能源、韌性電力系統、高效冷卻技術與先進數位解決方案，全面支援全球各地的資料中心營運。
