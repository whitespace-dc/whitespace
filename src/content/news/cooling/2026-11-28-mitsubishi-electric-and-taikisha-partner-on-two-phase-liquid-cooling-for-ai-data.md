body_en: |
  Mitsubishi Electric and Tokyo-based engineering firm Taikisha have signed a formal agreement to jointly develop two-phase liquid cooling solutions for artificial intelligence data centres, the companies announced. The deal brings together two Japanese companies that share an existing financial stake in ZutaCore, a US-based specialist in two-phase liquid cooling technology.

  Under the agreement, Mitsubishi Electric and Taikisha will conduct proof-of-concept demonstrations and produce reference designs — standardised, tested configurations that customers and integrators can adopt — using ZutaCore's technology.

  ## The parties and their roles

  Taikisha was established in 1913 and is headquartered in Tokyo. The company specialises in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems, cleanrooms, and paint finishing systems for commercial buildings and industrial plants, according to the announcement. That industrial and facilities-engineering background positions it as the systems-integration counterpart to Mitsubishi Electric's product and equipment capabilities.

  ZutaCore, the US company whose technology underpins the agreement, focuses on two-phase liquid cooling — a method in which a dielectric (electrically non-conductive) liquid absorbs heat directly from processors and evaporates in the process, carrying heat away without requiring chilled water or large air-handling infrastructure. Both Mitsubishi Electric and Taikisha hold equity stakes in ZutaCore.

  ## What the agreement covers

  The collaboration will move through proof-of-concept testing before advancing to reference design development and commercialisation. Reference designs allow prospective customers to evaluate a validated cooling architecture rather than engineering a bespoke solution from components, which can shorten deployment timelines for data centre operators.

  ## Significance for AI data centre cooling

  The agreement reflects growing demand from AI data centre operators for cooling approaches that can handle the high heat densities generated by graphics processing units and other AI accelerators, which conventional air cooling increasingly cannot address at scale. By combining Taikisha's HVAC and cleanroom engineering expertise with Mitsubishi Electric's equipment manufacturing and ZutaCore's two-phase technology, the partnership covers the full chain from cooling hardware through facilities integration.

  The next concrete step disclosed is the proof-of-concept demonstration phase, after which the partners plan to advance reference designs toward commercial availability.

  ## 三菱電機與大氣社攜手，為AI資料中心開發兩相液冷技術

  三菱電機與總部位於東京的工程公司大氣社（Taikisha）正式簽署協議，宣布將共同為人工智慧資料中心開發兩相液冷解決方案。此次合作匯聚了兩家在美國冷卻技術公司ZutaCore均持有股份的日本企業，共同推動相關技術的落地應用。

  根據協議內容，三菱電機與大氣社將以ZutaCore的技術為基礎，進行概念驗證示範，並研發參考設計——即一套經過標準化驗證的配置方案，供客戶與系統整合商直接採用。

  ## 各方角色與定位

  大氣社創立於1913年，總部設於東京，專精於商業建築與工業廠房的暖通空調系統、無塵室以及工業塗裝系統。這樣的工業設施工程背景，使其得以扮演系統整合的角色，與三菱電機在產品設備製造上的能力形成互補。

  本次合作的技術核心ZutaCore是一家美國公司，專注於兩相液冷技術。這項技術以介電液體（即不導電的絕緣液體）直接從處理器吸收熱量，液體在此過程中蒸發，藉由相變帶走熱能，無需依賴冷卻水系統或大型空調基礎設施。三菱電機與大氣社均已持有ZutaCore的股份。

  ## 合作協議的具體內容

  雙方合作將先進行概念驗證測試，再推進至參考設計開發與商業化階段。參考設計的優勢在於，資料中心業者無需從零開始自行整合各式零組件，而是能直接評估一套經過驗證的冷卻架構，有效縮短建置時程。

  ## 對AI資料中心冷卻的重要意義

  此次合作協議反映出AI資料中心業者對新型冷卻方案的迫切需求。繪圖處理器（GPU）及其他AI加速晶片所產生的熱密度極高，傳統氣冷方式已愈來愈難以大規模因應。透過整合大氣社在暖通空調與無塵室工程的專業、三菱電機在設備製造的實力，以及ZutaCore的兩相液冷技術，這項合作涵蓋了從冷卻硬體到設施整合的完整鏈路。

  目前雙方對外揭露的下一步，是推進概念驗證示範階段；完成後，合作夥伴計劃將參考設計進一步推向商業化，正式上市供應。
