body_en: |
  Schneider Electric has launched the Uniflair XCA series of chillers, targeting the growing demand for high-capacity cooling in AI-driven, high-density liquid-cooled data centres. The range comprises two sub-families: the air-cooled XCAC and the free-cooling XCAF, with six models available from 1,200 kilowatts (kW) to 2,500 kW of cooling capacity.

  All models use R1234ze refrigerant, a low global-warming-potential fluid, and incorporate oil-free centrifugal compressors with magnetic bearing technology. Magnetic bearings eliminate physical contact between rotating parts, reducing mechanical wear and energy losses. Each unit also includes built-in variable-speed drives, which adjust compressor and fan speed to match real-time cooling load rather than running at fixed maximum power.

  The series achieves an energy efficiency ratio (EER) — a measure of cooling output divided by electrical power input — of 4.66, meaning the chillers deliver 4.66 kW of cooling for every 1 kW of electricity consumed under rated conditions.

  ## Heat-exchange architecture

  Schneider Electric designed the XCA series around a heat-exchange architecture that combines spray evaporator technology with V-shape microchannel coils. The company said this configuration improves thermal performance while reducing refrigerant charge, material usage, and overall environmental footprint compared to conventional designs.

  The V-shape coil geometry, paired with a new generation of large-diameter electronically commutated (EC) fans — motors with integrated variable-speed control — is said to deliver higher heat exchange efficiency with increased airflow, lower acoustic output, and stable operation at high ambient temperatures.

  ## Free-cooling capability and operating range

  For the free-cooling XCAF models, the enhanced coil geometry and support for outlet water temperatures of up to 33 degrees Celsius (°C) extend the hours during which ambient air alone can meet cooling demand without engaging mechanical refrigeration. In moderate climates, Schneider Electric said the system can achieve up to 60 percent energy savings compared to mechanical cooling alone when free-cooling mode is available.

  The full series is rated to operate across an ambient temperature range of -20°C to +52°C, covering both cold northern European climates and hot arid regions where data centre construction is expanding.

  ## Customisation and resilience

  Schneider Electric said the XCA platform supports numerous electrical, hydraulic, noise control, and performance enhancement options, allowing operators to configure units to specific site requirements. The company positioned this flexibility as a means of improving operational efficiency and reducing management costs.

  For mission-critical applications, the series includes a quick-restart capability designed to restore full cooling capacity within three minutes of a power outage, a requirement for facilities where cooling interruption can cause server thermal shutdowns.

  ## Industry significance

  The XCA launch reflects the shift in data centre cooling architecture away from traditional air cooling toward liquid-based systems capable of handling the concentrated heat loads generated by artificial intelligence hardware. Chillers supplying chilled water or coolant to rear-door heat exchangers, direct liquid cooling loops, and immersion systems require higher-capacity, higher-efficiency units than previous generations of data centre equipment demanded. The Uniflair XCA series, with its upper capacity of 2,500 kW per unit, is positioned to serve these large-scale deployments. The inclusion of free-cooling models with high outlet water temperatures directly addresses the need to maximise economiser hours in liquid-cooled facilities, where even modest reductions in mechanical cooling runtime translate into significant annual energy savings at scale.

  施耐德電氣正式推出 Uniflair XCA 系列冷水機，瞄準 AI 驅動、高密度液冷資料中心對大容量冷卻設備與日俱增的需求。此系列分為兩個子系列：風冷型 XCAC 與自然冷卻型 XCAF，共推出六款機型，冷卻容量從 1,200 千瓦（kW）至 2,500 kW。

  所有機型均採用 R1234ze 冷媒——一種低全球暖化潛勢的流體——並配備無油磁浮軸承離心壓縮機。磁浮軸承消除了旋轉部件之間的物理接觸，有效降低機械磨損與能量損耗。每台機組亦內建變速驅動器，可依據即時冷卻負荷自動調整壓縮機與風扇轉速，無需以固定最高功率持續運行。

  此系列的能源效率比（EER）——即冷卻輸出除以電力輸入的效能指標——達到 4.66，意即在額定條件下，每消耗 1 kW 電力，冷水機即可提供 4.66 kW 的冷卻量。

  ## 熱交換架構

  施耐德電氣將 XCA 系列設計圍繞一套熱交換架構，結合噴霧蒸發器技術與 V 形微通道盤管。該公司表示，相較於傳統設計，此配置在提升熱性能的同時，可減少冷媒充填量、材料用量及整體環境足跡。

  V 形盤管幾何結構搭配新一代大直徑電子換向（EC）風扇——即整合變速控制的馬達——據稱能在提升熱交換效率的同時，增加氣流量、降低噪音輸出，並在高環境溫度下維持穩定運作。

  ## 自然冷卻能力與操作範圍

  針對自然冷卻型 XCAF 機型，強化的盤管幾何結構加上最高攝氏 33 度（°C）的出水溫度支援，延長了僅憑環境空氣便能滿足冷卻需求、無需啟動機械製冷的運行時數。在溫和氣候條件下，施耐德電氣表示，當自然冷卻模式可用時，系統最多可較純機械冷卻節省達 60% 的能源。

  整個系列的環境溫度操作範圍為 -20°C 至 +52°C，既涵蓋北歐寒冷氣候，也適用於資料中心建設持續擴張的炎熱乾旱地區。

  ## 客製化與韌性

  施耐德電氣表示，XCA 平台支援多種電氣、水力、噪音控制及性能強化選項，讓業者得以依據特定場址需求靈活配置機組。該公司將此彈性定位為提升營運效率、降低管理成本的重要手段。

  針對關鍵任務應用場景，此系列配備快速重啟功能，設計目標為在停電後三分鐘內恢復完整冷卻容量，以滿足一旦冷卻中斷即可能導致伺服器因過熱而關機之設施的需求。

  ## 產業意義

  XCA 系列的推出，反映出資料中心冷卻架構正從傳統氣冷走向液冷的轉型趨勢，液冷系統能夠應對人工智慧硬體所產生的集中式高熱負荷。為後門熱交換器、直接液冷迴路及浸沒式冷卻系統供應冷凍水或冷卻液的冷水機，所需的容量與效率均遠超前一世代資料中心設備的要求。Uniflair XCA 系列每台機組最高冷卻容量達 2,500 kW，正是為此類大規模部署量身打造。自然冷卻機型支援較高出水溫度，直接回應了液冷設施中最大化自然冷卻運行時數的迫切需求——在這類設施中，即便僅是小幅縮短機械冷卻的運行時間，在規模化之後也能轉化為相當可觀的年度節能效益。
