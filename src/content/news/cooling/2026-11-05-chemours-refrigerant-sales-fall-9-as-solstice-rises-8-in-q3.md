body_en: |
  Chemours and Solstice, two of the leading refrigerant manufacturers in the United States, posted sharply divergent third-quarter results, with Chemours reporting a 9% year-on-year revenue decline and Solstice recording 8% growth, reflecting uneven demand conditions across the refrigerant market.

  Chemours' refrigerant sales fell to $510 million in Q3, down from the same period in 2025, while Solstice's refrigerant revenue climbed to $433 million over the same period.

  ## Chemours: channel normalization weighs on Opteon

  Chemours attributed its decline to a 9% drop in volumes alongside a slight decrease in price. The company said the primary driver was lower North American aftermarket sales of its Opteon refrigerant blends for stationary air conditioning — meaning sales to service technicians and distributors rather than original equipment manufacturers.

  Opteon is Chemours' brand of lower global warming potential (GWP) refrigerants — newer fluid formulations designed to replace high-GWP predecessors under environmental regulations. GWP is a measure of how much heat a gas traps in the atmosphere relative to carbon dioxide.

  Opteon sales fell 22% to $287 million in the quarter. Chemours said this reflected a comparison against an elevated base in Q3 2025, when demand was boosted by the initial filling of aftermarket distribution channels ahead of requirements under the US AIM Act. The AIM Act — the American Innovation and Manufacturing Act — mandates a phased transition away from high-GWP refrigerants in stationary air conditioning systems, and that transition prompted an early surge in stocking activity that inflated prior-year figures.

  ## Solstice: automotive and data centres support growth

  Solstice said its 8% revenue increase was driven by strong demand in automotive and data centre applications. The company did not break out segment figures.

  ## Significance

  The contrasting results illustrate how the same regulatory transition — the AIM Act's stationary AC phase-down — can produce different outcomes for different companies depending on their product mix and channel exposure. For Chemours, the initial inventory fill that supported Q3 2025 results has now worked through the distribution chain, leaving a comparatively weaker demand environment in the most recent quarter. Solstice's growth, by contrast, was anchored in markets outside stationary AC aftermarket, where demand drivers are independent of the regulatory stocking cycle.

  The results indicate that Q3 2025 represented a high-water mark for Opteon aftermarket volumes tied to the AIM Act transition, and that Chemours' stationary AC business is now operating against that tougher prior-year comparison.

  Chemours第三季冷媒銷售額降至5.1億美元，較2025年同期下滑；Solstice同期冷媒營收則攀升至4.33億美元。

  ## Chemours：通路庫存正常化拖累Opteon

  Chemours將業績下滑歸因於銷售量下降9%，以及售價小幅走跌。公司表示，主要拖累因素為北美固定式空調售後市場的Opteon冷媒銷售萎縮——亦即銷售給維修技師及通路經銷商的部分，而非流向原廠設備製造商的部分。

  Opteon是Chemours旗下低全球暖化潛勢（GWP）冷媒品牌，採用新一代流體配方，旨在因應環保法規要求，取代高GWP的前代冷媒產品。全球暖化潛勢（GWP）是衡量某種氣體相較於二氧化碳，在大氣中吸收並滯留熱量程度的指標。

  本季Opteon銷售額大幅下滑22%至2.87億美元。Chemours指出，這主要反映出與2025年第三季高基期的比較效應——當時市場需求因美國《AIM法案》即將生效，售後流通通路提前進行初次大規模備貨，致使前一年同期數字顯著偏高。《AIM法案》（即《美國創新與製造法案》）強制規定固定式空調系統須分階段淘汰高GWP冷媒，此一轉型期引發業界提前囤貨潮，進而虛墊了2025年同期的銷售基數。

  ## Solstice：汽車與資料中心需求支撐成長

  Solstice表示，8%的營收成長主要由汽車及資料中心應用的強勁需求所帶動。該公司並未進一步揭露各分項數字。

  ## 意義解讀

  兩家公司的業績走勢形成強烈對比，說明同一法規轉型——《AIM法案》針對固定式空調的分階段淘汰——可能因各家公司的產品組合與通路結構差異，而產生截然不同的業績結果。就Chemours而言，曾支撐2025年第三季業績的初次通路庫存補充需求已逐步消化完畢，使最近一季面臨相對疲弱的需求環境。Solstice的成長則根植於固定式空調售後市場以外的領域，其需求驅動力不受法規備貨週期的左右。

  此次業績結果顯示，2025年第三季乃Opteon售後市場銷量受《AIM法案》轉型帶動的高峰時期，Chemours固定式空調業務目前正面臨更為嚴峻的前期高基期比較壓力。
