body_en: |
  HVAC controls company Airzone has joined the CoolDown project, a £5.5 million research initiative funded by Ofgem's Strategic Innovation Fund (SIF), to introduce automated air conditioning demand response for UK households. The company enters as a key technology partner alongside climate technology company Equiwatt, adding automated grid-balancing capability to a programme already running live trials.

  CoolDown is led by SP Electricity North West and National Grid, with University College London and Guidehouse Europe as project collaborators. Its central objective is to understand how rising air conditioning adoption could affect the UK electrical grid as temperatures increase and electrification of heating and transport advances.

  ## How the demand-response system works

  Demand response — the practice of voluntarily reducing electricity consumption during periods of peak network stress — sits at the core of CoolDown. Events are triggered when forecasts, drawing on weather conditions, renewable energy generation, and expected electricity consumption, indicate the network could come under pressure. Participating households are typically asked to reduce or pause cooling for one to two hours during weekday evenings.

  Equiwatt, a free UK mobile app, handles customer notifications and rewards users for cutting electricity use during those peak periods. Under the existing arrangement, customers receive an alert and manually switch off their equipment.

  Airzone's addition changes that dynamic. Through its Airzone Cloud platform, participating systems can respond to peak demand signals automatically, adjusting cooling settings without requiring a person to be present. The company said this makes it easier for households to participate in demand-response events even when no one is at home.

  ## Trial structure and schedule

  CoolDown is conducting two UK summer trials. The first, which initially focused on the Northwest region before expanding in size and scope, was completed in September. A second trial is planned for 2027 and will incorporate National Grid's licence areas, extending the project's geographic reach beyond the Northwest.

  ## Rising demand and the grid pressure case

  Fernando de la Cruz Quintanilla, Airzone's new markets EMEA director, said demand for air conditioning in the UK had been rising gradually until this year, when it "dramatically skyrocketed to the point where demand overtook supply" during what he described as the hottest summer on record. He said the company expects more UK households and businesses to install air conditioning ahead of next summer.

  ## Significance

  CoolDown is an early attempt to integrate automated HVAC control into grid-balancing infrastructure at the residential level. The manual notification model that Equiwatt already operates depends on users being available and willing to act; automating the response through Airzone Cloud removes that dependency and could increase effective participation rates. Whether the approach scales is the central question the 2027 trial, covering National Grid's broader licence areas, is designed to answer.

  CoolDown計畫由SP Electricity North West與National Grid共同主導，倫敦大學學院（UCL）及Guidehouse Europe擔任計畫協作方。計畫的核心目標，是在氣溫持續升高、暖氣與交通電氣化加速推進的背景下，釐清空調普及率攀升對英國電網可能帶來的衝擊。

  ## 需量反應系統運作方式

  需量反應——即在電網負載達到尖峰時自願降低用電量的做法——是CoolDown計畫的核心機制。當系統根據天氣狀況、再生能源發電量及預估用電量等數據預測電網可能承壓時，便會觸發需量反應事件。參與計畫的家庭通常被要求在平日傍晚時段將冷氣降溫或暫停運轉一至兩小時。

  Equiwatt是一款在英國免費下載的手機應用程式，負責向用戶發送通知，並在尖峰時段節電後給予獎勵回饋。在現有機制下，用戶收到提醒後需手動關閉設備。

  Airzone的加入改變了這一模式。透過Airzone Cloud平台，參與計畫的空調系統可自動回應尖峰需量訊號，在無需人員在場的情況下自動調整冷氣設定。Airzone表示，這讓家庭即便在無人在家的情況下，也能更輕鬆地參與需量反應事件。

  ## 試驗架構與時程

  CoolDown計畫在英國進行兩輪夏季試驗。第一輪試驗最初以英格蘭西北地區為主，之後擴大規模與範疇，已於九月完成。第二輪試驗預計於2027年進行，屆時將納入National Grid的授權區域，將計畫的地理覆蓋範圍延伸至西北地區以外。

  ## 需求成長與電網承壓的背景

  Airzone新市場EMEA事業處總監Fernando de la Cruz Quintanilla表示，英國的空調需求在今年以前一直是緩步成長，但今年卻「急劇飆升，需求甚至超過了供給」——他形容今年是有紀錄以來最炎熱的夏季。他表示，公司預期在明年夏季來臨前，將有更多英國家庭與企業安裝空調設備。

  ## 重要意義

  CoolDown是首批嘗試將自動化HVAC控制整合至住宅層級電網調度基礎設施的先驅計畫之一。Equiwatt現行的手動通知模式有賴用戶即時在場且願意配合行動；透過Airzone Cloud實現自動回應，可消除這一依賴，進而提升實際參與率。這套做法能否進一步擴大規模，正是2027年試驗——覆蓋National Grid更廣泛授權區域——所要回答的核心問題。
