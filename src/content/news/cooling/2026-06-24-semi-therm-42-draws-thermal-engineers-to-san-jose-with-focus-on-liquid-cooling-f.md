  The SEMI-THERM 42 Symposium convened March 9–12, 2026 at the DoubleTree Hotel in San Jose, California, with liquid cooling for data centers and high-performance computing emerging as the dominant technical theme across short courses, peer-reviewed papers, and the conference keynote.

  The annual symposium, organized under General Chair Dr. Navid Kazem of materials company Arieca, Program Chair Dr. Claire Wemp of Qnity, and Program Vice Chair Dr. Mohamed Abo-Ras of Nanotest, drew 44 vendor exhibitors and featured a 39-presentation technical conference, four short courses, 12 peer-reviewed papers, a newly launched student scholarship program, and recognition for two foundational figures in thermal science who died before the event.

  ## Short courses center on two-phase cooling

  Four short courses on March 10 set the tone for the week's technical emphasis. Two courses addressed two-phase cooling — systems that exploit the phase change of a refrigerant from liquid to vapor to carry heat away from components more efficiently than single-phase water alone. Villanova University Prof. Alfonso Ortega taught direct-to-chip liquid cooling in data centers using pumped two-phase refrigerants, covering fundamentals and design practices. Olivier de Laet and Hülya Geçim of thermal startup Calyos presented real-world applications of two-phase systems.

  The remaining two courses addressed simulation and materials. Prof. Tamara Bechtold of Jade University in Germany taught efficient thermo-mechanical simulation using compact models, and Ross Wilcoxon of Collins Aerospace covered thermal contact resistances and thermal interface materials — the layers placed between a chip and its heat-removal hardware to improve conduction.

  ## Technical program: 39 presentations across six tracks

  The technical conference ran March 11 through March 13 across six tracks: Liquid Cooling, Modeling, High Performance Computing, Testing and Measurement Methods, Thermal Interface Materials, and Assorted Topics, organized into nine sessions.

  Ortega organized a special honorarium session recognizing two late contributors to the field. Presentations honoring the late Prof. Robert J. Moffat included a talk by University of Maryland Associate Professor Damena Agonafer on measurement-driven thermal metrology and two-phase cooling mechanisms in high-power electronics, and one by Stanford Adjunct Professor Mehdi Asheghi on capillary-based two-phase cooling systems for data centers. A presentation by Binghamton University Assistant Professor Srikanth Rangarajan addressed image-based metrology as a pathway to digital twins — software models that replicate the behavior of physical systems — for diagnosing pool boiling regimes in cooling equipment. Ortega himself presented on thermal measurements in single and two-phase cooling systems for AI-scale facilities, framing the session as a tribute to Moffat.

  Presentations honoring Dr. Clemens Lasance were folded into the same session.

  ## Keynote: optical interconnects as a path to power-constrained scaling

  Sagi Mathai of Hewlett Packard Enterprise delivered the keynote, titled "Scaling Data Centers with Light," examining how optical and photonics technologies — which transmit data as pulses of light rather than electrical signals, reducing heat generation from interconnects — could address the challenges of high-power computing and data center design. The keynote was one of two invited presentations from industry practitioners.

  ## Awards recognize cooling research and a 50-year career

  The Thermal Hall of Fame inductee was Dr. Clemens J.M. Lasance, who died in 2025 after five decades in the thermal sciences community. The citation credited his work bridging theoretical modeling with real-world applications and described him as a leader in precision and intellectual honesty in the discipline.

  Guy Wagner of Electronic Cooling Solutions, Inc. received the SEMI-THERM 42 Thermi Award in recognition of a career spent designing fans and cooling systems for electronics.

  The Harvey Rosten Award, sponsored by Siemens Digital Industries Software, went to "Comparison of 3D Manifold Architectures for Cooling of Internal Heatsinks using External Airflow" by Gearóid Farrell, Rajesh Nimmagadda, and Tim Persoons of Trinity College Dublin's School of Engineering, together with Danny J. Lohan, Shailesh N. Joshi, and Ercan M. Dede of Toyota Research Institute of North America.

  The Best Paper award went to Bjorn Vermeersch of IMEC for a paper on fast multiscale thermal simulation of large system-on-chips — processors that integrate multiple computing functions on a single die — resolving detail down to sub-nanometer transistor channel features. An honorable mention went to Samaun Nili of Intel for work on active thermal management via direct-to-silicon submerged liquid jet impingement, a technique that directs pressurized coolant streams at a chip's silicon surface. The Best Poster award went to Brian Crumpston of Ventiva for a presentation on thermal challenges arising from AI-driven data growth.

  ## Peer-reviewed papers

  Twelve papers completed the conference's peer-review process. Topics included the effect of surface finish on copper corrosion in propylene glycol-based direct-to-chip coolants — a concern when glycol mixtures contact copper cold plates in server cooling loops — as well as reduced-order thermal modeling for power electronics modules, microfluidic heat-sink optimization via entropy generation minimization, and stability of flow in rack-level two-phase direct-to-chip systems.

  One paper presented a digital twin for a two-phase high-density computing data center, providing a system-level experimental dataset and component characterization for pumped two-phase rack cooling, co-authored by researchers including Ortega. A study on thermal interface materials used lock-in thermography — a technique combining periodic heating with infrared imaging to separate thermal resistance components — to simultaneously estimate interfacial and bulk resistances under controlled uniaxial pressure. Another paper applied computational fluid dynamics to assess high-power processor cooling using silicon carbide-diamond and chemical vapor deposition diamond microchannel spreaders under varied fin structures and flow paths.

  ## Vendor exhibits and student scholarships

  Forty-four companies exhibited products and services including heat sinks, thermal interface materials, heat pipes, vapor chambers, fluid connectors, air movers, advanced materials, simulation software, and thermal testing equipment. Several vendors conducted workshops alongside their exhibit booths.

  The SEMI-THERM Thermal Futures Initiative completed its first year, awarding scholarships to undergraduate and graduate engineering students who submitted accepted abstracts. Scholarships cover registration, lodging, meals, and limited travel to SEMI-THERM 43. The 2026 funding came from HP Enterprise, HPE Labs, Qualcomm Technologies, and Tim Shedd, among others. The program is structured to give early-career engineers experience presenting research and networking within the thermal management community.

  ## What the program signals for the industry

  The concentration of two-phase liquid cooling content across short courses, honorarium presentations, peer-reviewed papers, and the best-paper honorable mention reflects the field's accelerating move away from air cooling for racks supporting high-density AI computing loads. Direct-to-chip cooling, pumped two-phase refrigerant systems, and digital twins for rack-level thermal modeling each appeared across multiple program tracks, indicating broad research activity across academia and industry on the same set of problems.

  SEMI-THERM 43 is the next edition of the annual symposium; the Thermal Futures Initiative will accept scholarship applications from students with accepted abstracts, with program details available through the SEMI-THERM website.

  ## SEMI-THERM 42 聚焦AI工作負載液冷技術，熱工程師齊聚聖荷西

  第42屆SEMI-THERM研討會於2026年3月9日至12日在美國加州聖荷西DoubleTree飯店隆重登場，液冷技術在資料中心與高效能運算領域的應用，成為貫穿短期課程、同儕審查論文及大會主題演講的核心議題。

  本屆年度研討會由材料公司Arieca的Dr. Navid Kazem擔任大會主席，Qnity的Dr. Claire Wemp擔任議程主席，Nanotest的Dr. Mohamed Abo-Ras擔任議程副主席，共吸引44家廠商設展。活動內容涵蓋39場技術報告、四門短期課程、12篇同儕審查論文，以及新設立的學生獎學金計畫。大會亦特別表彰兩位在活動前已辭世的熱科學奠基人物。

  ## 短期課程聚焦兩相冷卻技術

  3月10日舉行的四門短期課程為本週技術重點定下基調。其中兩門課程深入探討兩相冷卻系統——此類系統利用冷媒從液態轉變為氣態的相變過程，將熱量從元件帶走，效率遠優於單純使用單相冷卻水。維拉諾瓦大學（Villanova University）教授Alfonso Ortega講授資料中心中以泵驅動兩相冷媒進行直接對晶片液冷的技術，涵蓋基礎原理與設計實務。熱管理新創公司Calyos的Olivier de Laet與Hülya Geçim則分享兩相系統的實際應用案例。

  另外兩門課程則分別涉及模擬與材料領域。德國玉德大學（Jade University）教授Tamara Bechtold講授以緊湊模型進行高效熱機械模擬的方法；柯林斯航太（Collins Aerospace）的Ross Wilcoxon則介紹熱接觸阻抗與導熱介面材料——即夾設於晶片與散熱硬體之間、用以改善熱傳導效率的材料層。

  ## 技術議程：六大主題39場報告

  技術會議於3月11日至13日進行，涵蓋六大主題軌道：液冷、建模、高效能運算、測試與量測方法、導熱介面材料，以及綜合議題，共分為九個場次。

  Ortega策劃了一場特別紀念場次，向已辭世的兩位領域先驅致敬。在緬懷已故教授Robert J. Moffat的系列報告中，馬里蘭大學副教授Damena Agonafer發表了關於量測驅動熱計量學與高功率電子元件兩相冷卻機制的研究；史丹佛大學兼任教授Mehdi Asheghi則介紹用於資料中心的毛細管兩相冷卻系統。賓漢頓大學助理教授Srikanth Rangarajan的報告探討以影像計量學作為建立數位孿生的途徑——數位孿生是能複現實體系統行為的軟體模型——應用於診斷冷卻設備中的池沸騰狀態。Ortega本人亦發表了針對AI規模設施中單相與兩相冷卻系統的熱量測研究，將整個場次定位為對Moffat的致敬。

  緬懷Dr. Clemens Lasance的報告亦納入同一場次。

  ## 主題演講：以光互連突破功耗限制的擴展之道

  惠普企業（Hewlett Packard Enterprise）的Sagi Mathai發表題為「以光擴展資料中心」的主題演講，探討光學與光子技術——以光脈衝而非電子訊號傳輸資料，藉此降低互連產生的熱量——如何應對高功率運算與資料中心設計的挑戰。本場演講是兩場業界專家受邀報告之一。

  ## 獎項表彰冷卻研究成就與五十年職涯貢獻

  本屆熱科學名人堂（Thermal Hall of Fame）得獎者為Dr. Clemens J.M. Lasance，他於2025年辭世，在熱科學領域深耕五十年。授獎詞肯定他架橋理論建模與實際應用之間的貢獻，稱其為推動學科精確性與學術誠信的領導人物。

  Electronic Cooling Solutions, Inc.的Guy Wagner榮獲SEMI-THERM 42 Thermi獎，表彰其畢生投入電子設備風扇與冷卻系統設計的職涯成就。

  由西門子數位工業軟體（Siemens Digital Industries Software）贊助的Harvey Rosten獎，頒給都柏林三一學院（Trinity College Dublin）工程學院的Gearóid Farrell、Rajesh Nimmagadda與Tim Persoons，以及豐田北美研究院（Toyota Research Institute of North America）的Danny J. Lohan、Shailesh N. Joshi與Ercan M. Dede，獲獎論文題目為「三維歧管架構用於外部氣流冷卻內部散熱片之比較」。

  最佳論文獎由IMEC的Bjorn Vermeersch獲得，其論文針對大型系統單晶片——即將多種運算功能整合於單一晶片的處理器——進行快速多尺度熱模擬，解析精度可達次奈米等級的電晶體通道特徵。英特爾（Intel）的Samaun Nili則獲得佳作獎，研究主題為透過直接對矽基浸沒式液體噴射衝擊進行主動熱管理，此技術係將加壓冷卻液流直接噴射至晶片矽基表面。最佳海報獎由Ventiva的Brian Crumpston獲得，報告主題為AI驅動資料成長所帶來的熱管理挑戰。

  ## 同儕審查論文

  本屆共有12篇論文通過同儕審查。議題涵蓋：表面粗糙度對丙二醇基直接對晶片冷卻液中銅腐蝕的影響——此為乙二醇混合液接觸伺服器冷卻迴路中銅製冷板時的重要考量——以及功率電子模組的降階熱模型、以熵生成最小化進行微流道散熱片優化、機架級兩相直接對晶片系統的流動穩定性等。

  其中一篇論文提出用於兩相高密度運算資料中心的數位孿生模型，並提供系統級實驗數據集與泵驅動兩相機架冷卻的元件特性描述，Ortega亦為共同作者之一。另有一篇關於導熱介面材料的研究採用鎖相熱像技術——結合週期性加熱與紅外線成像以分離熱阻成分——在受控單軸壓力下同步估算界面與體積熱阻。還有一篇論文應用計算流體力學，評估在不同翅片結構與流道配置下，以碳化矽-金剛石及化學氣相沉積金剛石微流道散熱片冷卻高功率處理器的效果。

  ## 廠商展覽與學生獎學金

  44家企業於展覽區展示散熱片、導熱介面材料、熱管、均熱板、流體接頭、氣流驅動元件、先進材料、模擬軟體及熱測試設備等產品與服務，多家廠商亦在攤位旁舉辦工作坊。

  SEMI-THERM熱科學未來倡議（Thermal Futures Initiative）完成第一年運作，向提交並獲接受摘要的大學部及研究所工程學生頒發獎學金。獎學金內容涵蓋SEMI-THERM 43的報名費、住宿、餐飲及有限度的交通補助。2026年度的資金來源包括惠普企業、HPE Labs、高通科技（Qualcomm Technologies）及Tim Shedd等。該計畫旨在讓初入職場的工程師有機會發表研究成果，並在熱管理領域建立人脈。

  ## 議程透露的產業走向

  兩相液冷內容密集出現於短期課程、紀念場次、同儕審查論文及最佳論文佳作，反映出業界正加速從風冷轉向液冷，以支撐高密度AI運算機架的時代趨勢。直接對晶片冷卻、泵驅動兩相冷媒系統，以及用於機架級熱管理的數位孿生技術，均跨多個議程軌道頻繁出現，顯示學術界與產業界正針對同一組核心問題展開廣泛研究。

  SEMI-THERM 43為本年度研討會的下一屆；熱科學未來倡議將持續接受已獲摘要接受之學生提出獎學金申請，詳情可參閱SEMI-THERM官方網站。
