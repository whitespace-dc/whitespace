body_en: |
  Researchers at Madrid's Institute of Micro and Nanotechnology (IMN-CNM, CSIC) have developed a nanomaterial capable of cooling surfaces exposed to direct sunlight without any electrical input, with outdoor tests on the institute's roof in Tres Cantos recording temperature reductions of up to 12.9°C against an uncoated reference surface.

  The material, still in the development phase, uses polyvinylidene fluoride (PVDF) — a polymer known for strong infrared heat emission — shaped into three-dimensional nanostructures by infiltrating the polymer into nanoporous templates made from anodised aluminium oxide. The researchers then applied cooling processes and ultraviolet radiation treatment to the resulting structures; the UV treatment bleaches the material, increasing how much solar radiation it reflects.

  ## How passive daytime radiative cooling works

  The technology is based on passive daytime radiative cooling (PDRC), a physical mechanism by which certain materials shed heat as infrared radiation without requiring an external energy source. Two conditions must be met simultaneously: the material must reflect the majority of incoming solar radiation to avoid absorbing heat, and it must efficiently emit thermal radiation within the atmospheric transparency window — the band of the electromagnetic spectrum between 8 and 13 micrometres through which the Earth's atmosphere allows heat to escape directly into space. When both conditions are met, the material's surface can reach temperatures below that of the surrounding air without consuming power.

  ## Why PVDF

  The choice of PVDF was deliberate. Cristina Vicente, a researcher at the institute, said the polymer combines several properties that suit outdoor deployment: high infrared emissivity, resistance to ultraviolet radiation, a hydrophobic surface that promotes self-cleaning and resists humidity, and general outdoor durability.

  The three-dimensional nanostructure fabricated using the alumina templates is intended to optimise those inherent material properties for the PDRC application.

  ## Outdoor test results

  Tests were conducted outdoors during the summer of 2025 on the rooftop of the institute in Tres Cantos, Madrid. Under what the researchers described as the most favourable conditions — warm, dry days with high solar radiation — the material achieved a surface temperature reduction of up to 12.9°C compared to an uncoated reference sample. The institute characterised the results as confirming the theoretical predictions behind the design.

  ## Significance for the cooling sector

  The research represents an early-stage but concrete demonstration that PVDF-based nanostructures can function as a passive cooling surface under real outdoor conditions in a warm European climate. If the approach advances from laboratory and rooftop trials toward commercial-scale manufacturing, it could offer a route to reducing the energy load of conventional refrigeration and air-conditioning systems in buildings and infrastructure — applications where surface coatings are feasible and where summer peak cooling demand places the greatest strain on electrical grids.

  The institute has not disclosed a timeline for further development phases or industrial partnerships.

  ## 西班牙研究團隊開發PVDF奈米材料　無需用電可降溫12.9°C

  馬德里微奈米技術研究所（IMN-CNM，隸屬西班牙國家研究委員會CSIC）的研究人員開發出一種奈米材料，能在直接暴露於陽光下的表面實現無電力降溫。研究團隊在位於特雷斯坎托斯（Tres Cantos）的研究所屋頂進行戶外實測，記錄到與未塗覆參考表面相比、最高達12.9°C的溫差。

  這項材料目前仍處於開發階段，採用聚偏二氟乙烯（PVDF）——一種以強紅外線熱輻射能力著稱的聚合物——並透過將其滲入以陽極氧化鋁製成的奈米多孔模板，塑造成三維奈米結構。研究人員隨後對成形結構施以冷卻處理及紫外線輻射處理；紫外線處理會使材料發生漂白效果，進而提升其對太陽輻射的反射率。

  ## 被動式日間輻射冷卻的原理

  這項技術的基礎是「被動式日間輻射冷卻」（PDRC）——一種特定材料無需外部能源即可以紅外線輻射形式散熱的物理機制。要達成這項效果，必須同時滿足兩個條件：材料必須反射大部分入射太陽輻射以避免吸熱，同時必須在「大氣透明窗口」波段內高效發射熱輻射。所謂大氣透明窗口，是指電磁波譜中8至13微米的波段範圍，地球大氣層在此波段允許熱量直接散逸至太空。當兩個條件同時滿足時，材料表面溫度即可在不消耗電力的情況下低於周遭空氣溫度。

  ## 為何選用PVDF

  選擇PVDF並非偶然。研究所研究員克里斯蒂娜．維森特（Cristina Vicente）表示，這種聚合物結合了多項適合戶外應用的特性：高紅外線發射率、耐紫外線輻射、具有疏水性表面（有助自潔並抵抗潮濕），以及整體的戶外耐久性。

  利用氧化鋁模板製備的三維奈米結構，目的正是為了針對PDRC應用，將上述材料本身的特性發揮至最佳。

  ## 戶外實測結果

  實測於2025年夏季在位於馬德里特雷斯坎托斯的研究所屋頂進行。在研究人員所描述的最有利條件下——即天氣溫暖乾燥、太陽輻射強烈的晴天——該材料與未塗覆參考樣品相比，表面溫度降低幅度最高達12.9°C。研究所表示，這項結果證實了設計背後的理論預測。

  ## 對冷卻產業的意義

  這項研究代表了一項早期但具體的驗證成果，證明以PVDF為基礎的奈米結構，能在歐洲溫暖氣候的真實戶外環境中作為被動式冷卻表面發揮功效。若這項技術能從實驗室與屋頂試驗階段推進至商業規模製造，將可望為建築物與基礎設施中的傳統冷凍空調系統提供一條降低能耗的途徑——在這些應用場景中，表面塗層的施作具有可行性，而夏季尖峰冷卻需求也對電網造成最沉重的負擔。

  研究所目前尚未公布後續開發階段的時程規劃，也未透露任何產業合作夥伴的相關訊息。
