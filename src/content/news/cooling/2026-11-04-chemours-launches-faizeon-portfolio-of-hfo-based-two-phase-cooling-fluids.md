body_en: |
  Chemours has launched Faizeon, a five-product portfolio of low-boiling-point dielectric fluids for two-phase direct-to-chip and immersion cooling in high-density data centres and AI servers.

  ## The portfolio

  The lineup comprises four direct-to-chip fluids (Faizeon 100, 110, 200, and 220) and one immersion cooling fluid, Faizeon 500. Two-phase, or phase-change, fluids absorb heat by transitioning from liquid to vapour, enabling heat removal from CPUs, GPUs, and high-wattage server racks. The direct-to-chip fluids are used in cold plates at the chip level; Faizeon 500 cools entire servers by transferring heat from every server component, Chemours said.

  ## Performance claims

  In independent third-party testing, a two-phase direct-to-chip system using Faizeon 200 maintained a GPU at up to 19°C lower than a single-phase, water-based cooling system while operating at a lower coolant flow rate, the company said. Chemours claims the Faizeon range delivers superior heat transfer, lower cooling-related energy demand, and minimal cooling-related water consumption. The fluids are engineered to support recovery, reprocessing, and reuse.

  ## Fluid composition

  Chemours did not publicly disclose the chemical identities of the Faizeon products at launch. After questioning, the company confirmed to the Cooling Post that the line includes HFOs (hydrofluoroolefins) and HFO blends. Based on published specification sheets, Faizeon 100 appears to be HFO-1336mzz(Z), Faizeon 110 is HFO-1336mzz(E), and Faizeon 200 and 220 are the non-flammable HFO/HFC (hydrofluorocarbon) blends 515B and 513A, respectively. Faizeon 500 appears to be HFO-153-10mczz-E, a compound for which Chemours holds a number of patents and which was previously announced under the Opteon 2P50 name.

  ## Qualifications and partnerships

  The fluid behind Faizeon 500, previously announced as Opteon 2P50, had accumulated qualifications from multiple partners before the Faizeon brand launch. Earlier this year, Chemours signed a joint development agreement with French server manufacturer 2CRSi, following several years of successful testing with 2CRSi's Atlantis and Octopus servers. The preceding year, Chemours disclosed that Samsung Electronics had qualified the same fluid for use with its fourth-generation solid state drive, following performance tests conducted in collaboration with US liquid cooling tank manufacturer Liquid Stack and PKI Corporation, a Korean semiconductor and data centre company.

  Chemours also has a manufacturing agreement with Indian fluorochemical company Navin Fluorine International to produce the HFO-based immersion fluid, and conducted a full-scale product trial with Japanese IT company NTT Data and engineering firm Hibiya Engineering.

  ## Industry context

  Denise Dignam, Chemours president and CEO, said the company is "applying more than 90 years of thermal-management expertise to help data centres manage higher heat loads while improving performance and dramatically reducing energy and water demand," and described the strategy as one of cross-ecosystem collaboration to enable deployment of two-phase liquid cooling at scale.

  The Faizeon brand launch brings together products that Chemours had been developing and qualifying under the Opteon name since at least July 2023, when it first announced entry into the immersion cooling market with an HFO-based two-phase fluid.

  ## 產品陣容

  Faizeon系列共包含四款直接對晶片冷卻液（Faizeon 100、110、200及220）以及一款浸沒式冷卻液Faizeon 500。兩相（相變）冷卻液透過液態轉氣態的相變過程吸收熱能，藉此帶走CPU、GPU及高瓦數伺服器機架所產生的熱量。其中，直接對晶片冷卻液應用於晶片層級的冷板；科慕表示，Faizeon 500則能將整台伺服器每個元件的熱量統一帶走，達到全機冷卻的效果。

  ## 效能表現

  據科慕表示，在獨立第三方測試中，採用Faizeon 200的兩相直接對晶片系統，在冷卻液流量更低的情況下，仍能使GPU溫度較單相水冷系統最多低攝氏19度。科慕宣稱，Faizeon系列具備優異的熱傳效率、更低的冷卻相關耗電需求，以及極少的冷卻用水消耗。這些冷卻液在設計上亦支援回收、再處理與重複使用。

  ## 流體成分

  科慕在發表之初並未公開揭露Faizeon各產品的化學成分。經媒體追問後，該公司向《Cooling Post》確認，本系列產品包含HFO（氫氟烯烴）及HFO混合物。根據已公開的規格表，Faizeon 100疑似為HFO-1336mzz(Z)，Faizeon 110為HFO-1336mzz(E)，Faizeon 200與220則分別為不可燃的HFO／HFC（氫氟碳化物）混合物515B與513A。Faizeon 500則疑似為HFO-153-10mczz-E——這是一種科慕持有多項專利的化合物，先前曾以Opteon 2P50之名對外宣布。

  ## 認證與合作夥伴

  Faizeon 500背後的冷卻液（前身為Opteon 2P50），早在Faizeon品牌正式發表前便已累積多項合作夥伴認證。今年稍早，科慕與法國伺服器製造商2CRSi簽署聯合開發協議，雙方此前已在2CRSi的Atlantis與Octopus伺服器上完成多年的成功測試。前一年，科慕宣布三星電子（Samsung Electronics）已針對其第四代固態硬碟（SSD）完成同款冷卻液的認證資格，該認證測試是與美國液冷槽製造商Liquid Stack及韓國半導體暨資料中心公司PKI Corporation合作進行。

  此外，科慕與印度氟化學企業Navin Fluorine International簽訂製造協議，負責生產這款HFO基浸沒式冷卻液；並與日本IT企業NTT Data及工程公司Hibiya Engineering共同完成全規模產品試驗。

  ## 產業背景

  科慕總裁兼執行長Denise Dignam表示，公司正「運用逾90年的熱管理專業，協助資料中心在提升效能的同時，因應更高的散熱負荷，並大幅降低能源與用水需求」，並將此策略定位為跨生態系統協作，以推動兩相液冷技術的大規模部署。

  Faizeon品牌的發表，整合了科慕至少自2023年7月起便以Opteon名義持續開發與認證的多款產品——當時科慕首度宣布以HFO基兩相冷卻液切入浸沒式冷卻市場。
