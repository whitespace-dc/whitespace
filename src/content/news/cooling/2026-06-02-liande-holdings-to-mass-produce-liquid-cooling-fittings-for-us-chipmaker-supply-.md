# LianDe Holdings to Mass Produce Liquid-Cooling Fittings for US Chipmaker Supply Chain

LianDe Holdings-KY has entered the AI server liquid-cooling components market and secured placement in a US chipmaker's supply chain. The company's product is a liquid-cooling floating fitting designed to pair with quick disconnects used between coolant lines and servers. Product validation is underway with volume production scheduled to begin in the second half of 2026.

## 中文

# 力銘液冷配件獲美晶片廠供應鏈認可

力銘控股進軍AI伺服器液冷配件領域，並獲得美國晶片廠商供應鏈認可。該公司開發的液冷浮動接頭設計用於伺服器冷卻液管線與快速接頭的連接。產品驗證正在進行中，預計於2026年下半年開始量產。
