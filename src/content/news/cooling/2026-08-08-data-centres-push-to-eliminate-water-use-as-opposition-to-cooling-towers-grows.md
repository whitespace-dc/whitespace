body_en: |
  Data centre operators are accelerating deployment of waterless cooling technologies, moving away from evaporative systems that can consume three to five million gallons of water per day at a single hyperscale facility, as community opposition intensifies in water-stressed regions including Central Texas and Arizona.

  The shift covers two main technology approaches: closed-loop air cooling, which rejects heat to ambient air through direct heat transfer rather than evaporation, eliminating the need for cooling towers and potable water draw; and direct-to-chip liquid cooling, in which a dielectric fluid — one that does not conduct electricity — boils directly at the chip surface to carry away heat without water.

  ## The scale of water consumption conventional cooling requires

  Most data centres cool servers using evaporative cooling, running water through cooling towers where it evaporates to draw heat from the building. Of the water consumed, around 30 to 40 percent is lost to evaporation rather than returned to its source, according to Capacity Media. In regions already under water stress, that draw has generated local opposition and regulatory scrutiny.

  ## Who is building waterless systems

  Microsoft is the most prominent hyperscaler pursuing the shift. The company's zero-water, closed-loop liquid cooling system for artificial intelligence data centres is designed to save more than 33 million gallons per facility each year. Pilot sites in Phoenix, Arizona and Mt. Pleasant, Wisconsin are coming online in 2026, with broader deployment planned from 2027. Microsoft has acknowledged a trade-off: its waterless design uses nominally more energy annually than its evaporative equivalents.

  On the direct-to-chip side, vendor ZutaCore raised $100 million in June 2026 to scale its two-phase liquid cooling technology, which operates without water and cuts cooling energy use roughly in half compared with conventional systems. The fundraise reflects rising demand as artificial intelligence chip density pushes past what air- and water-based systems were designed to handle.

  Edged US opened a 36-megawatt waterless facility in Mesa, Arizona in April 2026. Its second site, in Aurora, Illinois, is designed to save more than 277 million gallons of water annually and supports rack densities of up to 200 kilowatts per rack — a level that air cooling cannot practically serve. Novva Data Centers is deploying water-free cooling across colocation sites in five US states, projecting savings of over 300 million gallons a year. Vertiv, Evolution Data Centres, and Bridge Data Centres are also among operators moving toward closed-loop and immersion-based systems.

  ## What the technology does not resolve

  Nvidia's chief sustainability officer has said the water consumption challenge for data centres is "largely solved" following the company's warm-water cooling announcement. That framing has drawn pushback, according to Capacity Media.

  The savings produced by waterless cooling stop at the data centre's walls. Data centres that run on fossil fuels — a choice an increasing number of technology companies are making — still carry upstream water use tied to power generation, as well as associated emissions and energy intensity. Waterless cooling addresses one visible and locally contested form of resource use, but does not resolve the broader environmental footprint attached to the power those facilities consume.

  The distinction is consequential for how operators and regulators assess the industry's environmental claims. As deployments of waterless systems expand, the gap between on-site water savings and total lifecycle water and emissions impacts is likely to define the next round of scrutiny from communities and policymakers.
body_zh: |
  資料中心業者正在加速導入無水冷卻技術，逐步淘汰蒸發式冷卻系統。傳統超大規模資料中心每日用水量可高達三至五百萬加侖，隨著德州中部及亞利桑那州等水資源緊張地區的社區反彈聲浪日益升高，無水化已成為業界趨勢。

  目前主流技術分為兩大路線：一是密閉循環氣冷系統，透過直接熱交換將廢熱排入環境空氣，無需冷卻水塔，也不消耗自來水；二是直接液冷晶片技術，以不導電的介電液體直接在晶片表面沸騰帶走熱能，全程無需用水。

  ## 傳統冷卻技術的耗水規模

  現行大多數資料中心採用蒸發式冷卻：將水送入冷卻水塔，透過蒸發散熱。根據《Capacity Media》的資料，其中約三至四成的用水因蒸發而無法回收。在本已承受水資源壓力的地區，如此龐大的取水量已引發當地居民反對，並受到主管機關的監管審查。

  ## 哪些業者正在建置無水系統

  在積極推動無水化的超大規模業者中，微軟最為顯眼。該公司為人工智慧資料中心開發的零用水密閉循環液冷系統，設計目標是每座設施每年節省逾三千三百萬加侖用水。位於亞利桑那州鳳凰城與威斯康辛州普雷森特山的試點設施預計於二○二六年啟用，並規劃自二○二七年起全面推廣。微軟坦承其中存在取捨：相較於蒸發式冷卻方案，無水設計每年耗電量略高。

  在晶片直接液冷領域，供應商ZutaCore於二○二六年六月完成一億美元募資，用於擴大其兩相液冷技術的規模。該技術無需用水，冷卻能耗較傳統系統降低約一半。此次募資反映出市場需求的急速攀升——人工智慧晶片的熱流密度已遠超傳統氣冷與水冷系統的設計上限。

  Edged US於二○二六年四月在亞利桑那州梅薩啟用一座三十六百萬瓦的無水資料中心。其第二座設施位於伊利諾州奧羅拉，設計年節水量逾二億七千七百萬加侖，機架密度最高可達每架二百千瓦——此一密度已是氣冷技術難以勝任的水準。Novva Data Centers則在全美五州的共置機房全面導入無水冷卻，預計每年節水逾三億加侖。Vertiv、Evolution Data Centres及Bridge Data Centres等業者也正朝密閉循環與液浸式系統的方向推進。

  ## 技術尚未解決的問題

  輝達首席永續長曾表示，隨著該公司溫水冷卻方案的問世，資料中心的用水挑戰「已大致獲得解決」。然而，這樣的說法已遭到質疑，《Capacity Media》對此有所報導。

  無水冷卻技術的節水效益止於資料中心的圍牆之內。對於使用化石燃料的資料中心而言——而越來越多的科技公司正做出這樣的選擇——發電端的上游用水、相關排放以及能源強度問題依然存在。無水冷卻解決的，不過是一種可見、且在地方層面備受爭議的資源消耗形式，卻無法化解這些設施所消耗電力背後更廣泛的環境足跡。

  這項區別對於業者與主管機關評估產業環保聲明至關重要。隨著無水系統的部署範圍持續擴大，廠址節水成效與整體生命週期用水及碳排放之間的落差，很可能將成為社區與政策制定者下一波審視的焦點。
