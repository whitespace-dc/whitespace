  ## Johnson Controls pushes full thermal chain strategy for European data centre growth

  Johnson Controls is expanding its European data centre cooling operations through a series of acquisitions and strategic investments, positioning itself as a single-source provider across the full thermal chain as AI-driven infrastructure demand accelerates across the continent. Austin Domenici, president of global data centre solutions at Johnson Controls, outlined the company's capabilities and growth direction at Datacloud Global Congress.

  Domenici said European demand is creating an uptick in production for the company across the region. "EMEA is ramping up significantly, with real momentum in APAC too," he said, referring to Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia-Pacific. He described a "strong desire for in-region production" from customers, which he said Johnson Controls is positioned to serve.

  ## Acquisitions and investments building out the thermal chain

  The company's most recent acquisition is Alloy Enterprises, a thermal technology firm. Domenici said Alloy can produce "unique thermal transfer geometries and heat capture geometries that nobody else can make" due to its manufacturing processes. The acquisition adds heat capture capability to Johnson Controls' portfolio.

  An earlier acquisition, Silent-Aire in 2021, underpins the company's relationships with hyperscalers — large-scale cloud infrastructure operators — where Johnson Controls co-designs solutions and then scales production.

  Beyond acquisitions, the company has made an investment in Excelsior, a partner focused on two-phase direct-to-chip liquid cooling, a method that transfers heat directly from processor chips using a liquid coolant that changes phase from liquid to vapor. Johnson Controls has also invested in Armada, a modular data centre company. The two recently launched a product together: a modular data centre with a capacity of nearly two megawatts — enough to power a facility of significant compute density — which Domenici described as fast to deploy.

  ## Liquid and air cooling in parallel

  At Datacloud Global Congress, Johnson Controls showcased its CDU (coolant distribution unit) capability, which it described as designed and configurable, alongside air-side cooling solutions. Domenici said the market continues to run a mix of the two approaches. "Even though liquid cooling is increasing, we still have a mix of liquid and air," he said.

  The company also displayed an air-cooled modular data centre that was built in Europe, along with fire detection, fire suppression, and controls products. Domenici said Johnson Controls manufactures equipment in Europe, which he described as an advantage for customers with in-region sourcing requirements.

  On its chiller products — systems that remove heat from facilities using refrigeration cycles — Domenici said they are custom-designed to support liquid cooling deployments and can operate without oil, which he said enables additional capabilities he did not specify further.

  ## Single-source positioning as complexity grows

  Domenici said data centre operators are contending with growing complexity as facilities incorporate more components and more third-party vendors. He said customers are increasingly requesting broader product offerings from fewer partners. "The more customers can get from one partner, the easier their life becomes," he said.

  The company's stated strategic message is that it covers the full thermal chain — from heat capture to rejection — with integrated controls, modular build options, component ownership, and supply chain management. "We can optimise it in ways others can't and we own the supply chain, so we can help with sourcing too," Domenici said.

  He identified field expertise — including commissioning and ongoing service by locally hired technicians — as a competitive differentiator, particularly as deployments scale across regions with varying infrastructure and labour markets.

  ## Heat reuse and community engagement

  Domenici said the company is drawing on experience in district heating and cooling, as well as applications such as warming swimming pools and greenhouses, to address heat reuse opportunities in data centres. Data centres produce large volumes of waste heat as a byproduct of compute operations, and the potential to capture and redirect that heat to other uses is an area of growing interest across the industry.

  "These facilities generate enormous amounts of heat, and that heat is an asset," Domenici said. "If we can figure out how to reuse it, that's real value for the market."

  He added that Johnson Controls works with local legislators and governments in communities where it operates, describing early and transparent engagement as the approach the company uses to manage the community footprint of data centre facilities.

  ## Outlook

  Domenici said return on assets for Johnson Controls customers in the data centre segment remains strong and is showing no signs of slowing. He identified two market priorities: efficiency and power reduction gains, and modular or fast-to-deploy solutions that allow customers to begin generating revenue sooner. The company's stated focus for the year is ensuring its existing customer base — which Domenici said spans hyperscalers, neo-clouds, and colocation operators across all major regions — is aware of its full product portfolio.

  ## Johnson Controls推動全熱鏈策略　搶攻歐洲資料中心市場

  Johnson Controls正透過一系列收購與策略性投資，擴大其在歐洲的資料中心冷卻業務，在AI驅動的基礎設施需求加速席捲歐洲大陸之際，將自身定位為覆蓋完整熱鏈的單一來源供應商。Johnson Controls全球資料中心解決方案總裁Austin Domenici在Datacloud Global Congress大會上概述了公司的現有能力與成長方向。

  Domenici表示，歐洲需求正為公司在整個區域帶來產能提升。「EMEA正在大幅加速，APAC也出現強勁動能，」他說，EMEA指歐洲、中東與非洲，APAC則指亞太地區。他描述客戶有「強烈的在地生產需求」，並表示Johnson Controls已具備服務這一需求的能力。

  ## 收購與投資建構完整熱鏈

  公司最新的收購對象是熱技術公司Alloy Enterprises。Domenici表示，得益於其製造工藝，Alloy能夠生產「獨特的熱傳導幾何結構與熱捕獲幾何結構，這是其他廠商做不到的」。此次收購為Johnson Controls的產品組合新增了熱捕獲能力。

  較早的一筆收購是2021年併購的Silent-Aire，這奠定了公司與超大規模業者——即大型雲端基礎設施營運商——之間的合作關係；Johnson Controls在此模式下與客戶共同設計解決方案，再進行規模化生產。

  除收購之外，公司還投資了Excelsior，一家專注於兩相直接晶片液冷技術的合作夥伴。這種技術利用液態冷媒直接從處理器晶片傳導熱量，冷媒在過程中由液相轉變為氣相。Johnson Controls也投資了模組化資料中心公司Armada，兩者近期共同推出一款容量近兩百萬瓦（約兩兆瓦）的模組化資料中心——足以支撐高計算密度設施的電力需求——Domenici形容其可快速部署。

  ## 液冷與氣冷並行

  在Datacloud Global Congress上，Johnson Controls展示了其CDU（冷媒分配單元）能力，稱其為可設計、可客製化配置的方案，並同時展出氣側冷卻解決方案。Domenici表示，市場目前仍同時採用兩種冷卻方式。「即使液冷比例持續提升，市場仍維持液冷與氣冷並用的局面，」他說。

  公司也展出了一座在歐洲建造的氣冷式模組化資料中心，以及火災偵測、消防抑制與控制產品。Domenici指出，Johnson Controls在歐洲本地製造設備，他認為這對有在地採購需求的客戶而言是一大優勢。

  關於冷水機組產品——即利用冷凍循環排除設施熱量的系統——Domenici表示，這些產品均依液冷部署需求客製化設計，且能以無油方式運作，他表示這帶來了額外功能，但未進一步說明細節。

  ## 單一來源定位因應複雜性增長

  Domenici表示，隨著設施整合更多元件與第三方廠商，資料中心營運商正面臨日益增長的複雜性。他說，客戶越來越傾向從更少的合作夥伴處取得更廣泛的產品供給。「客戶能從單一夥伴獲得的愈多，他們的日子就愈好過，」他說。

  公司的核心策略訊息是：從熱捕獲到熱排放，覆蓋完整熱鏈，並整合控制系統、模組化建置選項、元件自主掌控與供應鏈管理。「我們能以其他廠商做不到的方式進行優化，而且我們掌控供應鏈，因此也能協助客戶採購，」Domenici說。

  他將現場專業能力——包括由當地招募技術人員負責的試運轉與持續維護服務——列為競爭差異化要素，尤其是在各區域基礎設施條件與勞動市場各異的情況下，隨著部署規模持續擴張，這一點顯得格外重要。

  ## 廢熱再利用與社區參與

  Domenici表示，公司正借鑒其在區域集中供熱供冷領域的豐富經驗，以及為游泳池加溫、溫室供暖等實際應用場景的積累，來開發資料中心的廢熱再利用機會。資料中心在運算過程中會產生大量廢熱，如何捕獲並將這些熱能轉作他用，正成為業界日益關注的課題。

  「這些設施產生了海量熱能，而這些熱能是一種資產，」Domenici說，「若能找到再利用的方法，對市場而言便是切實的價值。」

  他補充說，Johnson Controls會與其設施所在社區的地方立法機構及政府合作，並指出早期、透明的溝通是公司管理資料中心社區影響的一貫做法。

  ## 展望

  Domenici表示，Johnson Controls資料中心業務客戶的資產報酬率依然強勁，且未見放緩跡象。他點出兩大市場優先事項：效率提升與電力消耗降低，以及模組化或快速部署方案——後者能讓客戶更早開始創造營收。公司今年的既定重心，是確保現有客戶群——Domenici表示涵蓋跨所有主要地區的超大規模業者、新興雲端業者及主機代管業者——都能充分了解公司的完整產品組合。
