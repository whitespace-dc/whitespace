  ## Evaporative cooling persists as the dominant data center cooling method

  Data centers continue to depend on evaporative cooling to manage server heat despite the technology's heavy water demands, with multiple cost, energy, and scale barriers blocking a shift to alternatives that use less water or electricity — even as liquid cooling gains ground toward a projected 50% share of new installations within five years.

  Evaporative cooling works by pushing hot air through water-soaked membranes; as the hot air contacts the water, evaporation carries heat away from the facility. The method is the most common cooling approach in modern data centers, according to Data Center Knowledge, and its dominance persists in new builds as well as legacy facilities.

  A single data center relying on evaporative cooling can consume millions of gallons of water per day, equivalent to the water used by thousands of residences. Efficient evaporative systems use roughly 1,000 liters of water per megawatt-hour of IT load. At the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's estimated municipal water cost of $0.003 per gallon, that translates to approximately $0.80 per megawatt of IT capacity — a figure the source describes as markedly lower than electricity-based alternatives.

  ## Three barriers slow migration to alternatives

  The source identifies cost, electricity availability, and scale as the principal reasons facilities have not moved away from evaporative cooling.

  On cost: a small residential air conditioner — representative of mechanical refrigeration, which uses no water but requires substantial electricity — costs around $0.55 per hour to run, and thousands of such units would be needed to cool even a small data center. Immersion liquid cooling, which submerges IT equipment in non-conductive fluid and carries minimal ongoing electricity or water costs, can cost as much as $5 million per megawatt to install — more than double the cost of evaporative systems. Retrofitting an existing facility to replace evaporative cooling raises those figures further.

  On electricity: generation capacity has become a material constraint on data center expansion, with some facilities considering on-site generation to bypass grid limitations. Cooling methods that require more electricity than evaporative systems compound that pressure. The source notes the industry currently appears to prioritize reducing energy consumption over reducing water use.

  On scale: evaporative cooling can serve data centers of virtually any size. Mechanical refrigeration systems face practical limits in large-scale facilities because fitting sufficient refrigeration units is physically challenging. Liquid cooling does not share that ceiling but carries higher per-megawatt installation costs that grow with facility size.

  ## How the alternatives differ

  The source identifies three main alternatives to evaporative cooling:

  - **Mechanical refrigeration**: uses no water but is electricity-intensive; faces scale constraints in large facilities.
  - **Liquid cooling**: uses non-conductive fluid either circulated directly to chips (direct-to-chip) or surrounding fully submerged equipment (immersion); efficient in both energy and water terms but expensive to install and operate.
  - **Free cooling**: draws on outdoor air to dissipate heat without mechanical assistance; viable only in climates where ambient temperatures remain low enough.

  Evaporative cooling's relative advantages — low installation cost, low operating cost, minimal maintenance, and unlimited scalability — have made it the default even where water scarcity is a recognized concern. The source notes that water shortages, while worsening in many regions, have not yet reached a level acute enough to force operators toward waterless alternatives.

  ## Runoff and supply risks add to the water burden

  Beyond raw consumption volume, evaporative cooling carries a secondary environmental risk: runoff from the systems has the potential to contaminate local aquifers. That concern compounds the strain on municipal water supplies in regions already facing shortages, though neither factor has yet shifted industry-wide purchasing behavior.

  ## Liquid cooling projected to reach half of new installs by 2031

  Despite the barriers, liquid cooling's share is rising from what the source describes as single-digit adoption just a few years ago. Experts cited by Data Center Knowledge project liquid cooling could account for 50% of all cooling in new data center installations within the next five years — a trajectory driven by the increasing power density of modern servers and growing recognition of liquid cooling's efficiency and sustainability characteristics.

  That projection represents a significant potential rebalancing of the market, though the installed base of evaporative systems in existing facilities means the technology will remain a large part of the overall fleet well beyond any inflection point in new builds.

  ## 蒸發冷卻持續主導資料中心散熱市場

  儘管蒸發冷卻技術對水資源的需求極為龐大，資料中心仍持續倚賴這項技術來管理伺服器產生的熱量。成本、電力與規模等多重障礙，使業者難以轉向耗水量或耗電量更低的替代方案——即便液體冷卻正逐步攀升，預計五年內將佔新建設施散熱市場的50%。

  蒸發冷卻的原理是將熱空氣導入含水薄膜，熱空氣接觸水分後，蒸發作用便將熱量帶離設施。根據《Data Center Knowledge》報導，這是現代資料中心最普遍的冷卻方式，無論是新建還是既有設施，蒸發冷卻都持續居於主導地位。

  單一採用蒸發冷卻的資料中心，每日耗水量可達數百萬加侖，相當於數千戶家庭的用水總量。效能較佳的蒸發冷卻系統，每百萬瓦小時（MWh）的IT負載大約消耗1,000公升水。以美國環境保護署（EPA）估算的市政供水費用每加侖0.003美元換算，每百萬瓦（MW）IT容量的水費約為0.80美元——該資料來源指出，這個數字遠低於以電力驅動的替代方案。

  ## 阻礙業者轉型的三大障礙

  該資料來源指出，成本、電力供應與規模是業者遲遲未能拋棄蒸發冷卻的主要原因。

  **成本方面**：一台小型家用冷氣機——代表機械式製冷技術，不耗水但需要大量電力——每小時運行費用約0.55美元，而要冷卻一座規模較小的資料中心，就需要數千台這樣的機器。浸沒式液體冷卻技術將IT設備浸入非導電液體中，持續的電力與水費成本極低，但每百萬瓦的安裝費用高達500萬美元，是蒸發冷卻系統的兩倍以上。若要改造既有設施以取代蒸發冷卻，費用還會進一步攀升。

  **電力方面**：發電容量已成為制約資料中心擴張的實質瓶頸，部分設施甚至考慮自建發電設備以繞過電網限制。比蒸發冷卻更耗電的散熱方式，只會加劇這股壓力。該資料來源也指出，目前業界似乎將降低能耗列為比節水更優先的目標。

  **規模方面**：蒸發冷卻幾乎可以服務任何規模的資料中心。機械式製冷系統在大型設施中面臨實際限制，因為要容納足夠數量的製冷機組，在空間上相當困難。液體冷卻雖無此上限，但隨著設施規模擴大，每百萬瓦的安裝成本也跟著水漲船高。

  ## 替代技術各有特色

  該資料來源點出蒸發冷卻的三大主要替代技術：

  - **機械式製冷**：完全不耗水，但電力消耗極高；在大型設施中面臨規模限制。
  - **液體冷卻**：以非導電液體直接導入晶片（直接接觸式）或將設備完全浸沒（浸沒式）；在電力與用水效率上表現優異，但安裝與運營成本高昂。
  - **自由冷卻（Free cooling）**：直接利用戶外低溫空氣散熱，無需機械輔助；僅適用於環境氣溫長期維持足夠低的地區。

  蒸發冷卻的相對優勢——安裝成本低、運營成本低、維護需求少，且擴展規模不受限——使其即便在水資源匱乏已是公認問題的地區，仍是業者的預設選擇。該資料來源指出，儘管許多地區的缺水情況持續惡化，目前尚未嚴重到足以迫使業者全面轉向無水替代方案的程度。

  ## 逕流污染與供水風險加重水資源負擔

  除了龐大的直接耗水量外，蒸發冷卻還帶來另一項環境隱憂：系統排放的逕流可能污染當地含水層。這項疑慮，加重了原本就面臨水資源短缺地區的市政供水壓力，但目前這兩項因素都尚未能改變整個業界的採購行為。

  ## 液體冷卻預計2031年前佔新建設施一半

  儘管障礙重重，液體冷卻的市佔率仍在持續攀升，從幾年前該資料來源所描述的個位數採用率逐步成長。《Data Center Knowledge》所引用的專家預測，液體冷卻可能在未來五年內佔新建資料中心所有散熱裝置的50%——這一趨勢的主要驅動力，在於現代伺服器功率密度不斷提升，以及業界對液體冷卻效能與永續特性的認識日益深化。

  這項預測代表市場格局可能出現重大重組，但由於既有設施中蒸發冷卻系統的安裝基礎龐大，即便新建設施的採購趨勢出現轉折點，這項技術在整體設施存量中仍將長期佔有相當大的比重。
