body_en: |
  Midea Building Technologies launched a full suite of liquid and air cooling products and services for artificial intelligence data centers at a Kuala Lumpur event, targeting the thermal demands of high-density computing in tropical climates where server racks can exceed 100 kilowatts (kW) per cabinet.

  The company branded the showcase "Feel the Future" and framed its offering as a closed-loop, end-to-end solution covering consulting, design, delivery, and maintenance — from the cooling source through to terminal equipment. Midea said the integrated model addresses three industry pain points: slow deployment, energy efficiency concerns, and high total cost of ownership (TCO).

  ## Three core products for tropical conditions

  Midea presented three products built specifically for high-temperature, high-humidity environments:

  - **Midea maglev active CDU (coolant distribution unit):** Combines a magnetic-bearing cooling source and distribution unit in a single package, reducing physical footprint by more than 50 percent, the company said. Under tropical operating conditions, it achieves a system power usage effectiveness (PUE) below 1.2. PUE is a standard industry ratio measuring how much of a facility's total energy goes to computing versus overhead; a score of 1.0 would mean all power reaches the servers, while lower scores indicate less waste.
  - **Midea air-cooled magnetic-bearing chiller:** Designed for water-scarce, high-temperature sites, it maintains high energy efficiency with low performance degradation, according to Midea.
  - **Midea Industrial-grade CDU:** Supports cooling capacity up to 2,600 kW — enough to serve a substantial cluster of high-density racks — with a high-availability architecture targeting continuous, stable operation.

  Midea described its broader system design as a "wind-fluid synergy" architecture, which it said enables flexible deployment of both air cooling and liquid cooling across server racks within the same facility.

  ## RMB 1 billion manufacturing base

  In March, Midea broke ground on a liquid cooling smart manufacturing base in Shunde, in China's Guangdong province, with an investment of more than RMB 1 billion (Chinese yuan renminbi). The company said the facility is designed to support independent, large-scale production of core components including chillers and CDUs, and is expected to begin production in August 2027.

  ## NUS research collaboration

  Midea presented alongside researchers from the National University of Singapore (NUS) and said it is in active discussions with the university on collaboration for next-phase testbed projects.

  Assoc Prof Md Raisul Islam, a lecturer in NUS's College of Design and Engineering, described the Sustainable Tropical Data Centre Testbed (STDCT) — a 0.5 megawatt (MW) open laboratory in Singapore built to study energy, water, and carbon challenges specific to tropical data centers. Guo Shuai, a research fellow in NUS's mechanical engineering faculty, told the event that for racks exceeding 20 kW — a threshold at which conventional air cooling becomes increasingly strained — cold plates produced via additive manufacturing (AM) with topology-optimized fins are central to overcoming cooling bottlenecks.

  ## Shifting metrics and design philosophy

  In a panel discussion at the event, NUS researchers and industry participants agreed that thermal management is moving from a background support function to a core element of data center architecture, a shift they said is especially pronounced in tropical regions. Panelists called for efficiency metrics to move beyond PUE and incorporate "compute efficiency" alongside total cost of ownership. Integrated real-world testing and system engineering were identified as critical to reducing deployment risk and accelerating adoption of advanced cooling technologies.

  The Shunde manufacturing base, which Midea introduced to global customers at the Kuala Lumpur event, is scheduled to begin production in August 2027.

  ## 美的樓宇科技發布專為熱帶氣候打造的AI資料中心冷卻產品線

  美的樓宇科技在吉隆坡舉辦產品發表活動，正式推出一整套面向人工智慧資料中心的液冷與氣冷產品及服務，目標客群為高密度運算環境中的資料中心業者——尤其是熱帶氣候地區，當地伺服器機櫃的功耗可超過每台100千瓦（kW）。

  此次發表會以「Feel the Future」為主題，美的將旗下產品定位為一套閉環式、端對端的整合解決方案，涵蓋諮詢、設計、交付與維護等各個環節，從冷源一路延伸至末端設備。美的表示，這套整合模式能夠解決業界三大痛點：部署速度慢、能源效率不足，以及整體擁有成本（TCO）偏高。

  ## 三款專為熱帶環境打造的核心產品

  美的此次展示的三款產品，均針對高溫、高濕環境所設計：

  - **美的磁浮主動式冷卻液分配單元（CDU）：** 將磁懸浮冷源與分配單元整合為單一機組，據美的表示可使佔地面積縮小逾50%。在熱帶運作條件下，系統電能使用效率（PUE）可達1.2以下。PUE是業界衡量資料中心能源效率的標準指標，代表設施總用電量中用於運算的比例；理想值為1.0（即所有電力全數用於伺服器），數值越低代表損耗越小。
  - **美的風冷磁浮冷水機：** 專為缺水且氣溫偏高的場址設計，據美的表示在高溫環境下仍能維持高能效，且性能衰退幅度低。
  - **美的工業級CDU：** 支援最高2,600千瓦的冷卻容量，足以服務大規模的高密度機櫃叢集，並採用高可用性架構，目標是確保系統持續穩定運行。

  美的將其整體系統設計理念稱為「風液協同」架構，並表示這套架構能夠讓同一設施內的機架靈活混搭氣冷與液冷方案。

  ## 人民幣10億元製造基地

  今年3月，美的於中國廣東省順德區動工興建一座液冷智慧製造基地，投資金額逾人民幣10億元。美的表示，該基地設計目標是支援冷水機、CDU等核心元件的自主大規模生產，預計於2027年8月正式投產。

  ## 與新加坡國立大學的研究合作

  美的在此次活動中邀請了新加坡國立大學（NUS）的研究人員共同登台，並表示雙方正就下一階段的測試床計畫積極洽談合作。

  國立大學設計與工程學院副教授Md Raisul Islam介紹了「永續熱帶資料中心測試床」（Sustainable Tropical Data Centre Testbed，STDCT）——這是一座位於新加坡、裝置容量為0.5百萬瓦（MW）的開放式實驗室，專門研究熱帶地區資料中心所面臨的能源、用水與碳排挑戰。國立大學機械工程系研究員郭帥則在活動現場指出，對於功耗超過20千瓦的機架——超過這個門檻後，傳統氣冷方案的壓力將顯著增加——採用積層製造（AM）技術、搭配拓撲最佳化散熱鰭片所生產的冷板，是突破冷卻瓶頸的關鍵所在。

  ## 指標轉變與設計哲學

  在活動的座談環節中，國立大學研究人員與業界代表一致認為，熱管理正從資料中心的後勤支援功能，逐步躍升為核心架構要素，這樣的轉變在熱帶地區尤為明顯。與會人士呼籲，效率指標應跳脫PUE的框架，進一步將「運算效率」與整體擁有成本納入考量。他們同時強調，整合式實地測試與系統工程是降低部署風險、加速先進冷卻技術普及的關鍵途徑。

  順德製造基地在此次吉隆坡活動中正式向全球客戶曝光，預計將於2027年8月開始投產。
