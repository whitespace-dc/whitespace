body_en: |
  Mitsubishi Electric is establishing a US manufacturing subsidiary for IT cooling equipment in Mason, Ohio, investing approximately $30 million to convert part of an existing automotive production facility, the company announced.

  The new entity, MEHITS US Inc, will occupy around 14,000 square meters of manufacturing floor space inside a 60,000-square-meter facility that Mitsubishi Electric Automotive America has operated on the site since 1987. Production is scheduled to begin in April 2027.

  The $30 million investment will fund renovation and repurposing of the portion of that building, according to the announcement. Mitsubishi Electric US Holdings Inc is funding the project.

  ## Shift from Italian manufacturing

  Mitsubishi Electric's IT cooling systems are currently developed and manufactured by Mitsubishi Electric Hydronics & IT Cooling Systems SpA, a subsidiary headquartered in Italy. The Ohio facility would bring a portion of that production onto US soil for the first time.

  In the United States, the company's industrial infrastructure business is currently led by Mitsubishi Electric Power Products, which sells IT cooling equipment bundled with power supply systems, including uninterruptible power supplies — battery-backed devices that keep critical loads running through grid outages.

  ## Stated rationale

  Mitsubishi Electric said local production is expected to shorten lead times from manufacturing to delivery, ensure a more stable product supply, and allow the company to develop products tailored to local customer requirements. The company added that the Ohio operation will expand its product lineup to include equipment compatible with liquid cooling — a method of removing heat directly from servers and other hardware using water or other fluids, increasingly adopted in high-density data center environments.

  The company framed the move as part of a broader plan to grow its IT cooling systems business and strengthen what it described as one-stop service capabilities, meaning integrated support spanning system design, sales, and maintenance.

  ## Industry significance

  Establishing domestic manufacturing for IT cooling equipment positions Mitsubishi Electric to compete more directly in a US data center market where supply chain reliability and faster delivery have become purchasing priorities. The addition of liquid cooling to the product lineup signals an intent to address the higher-density compute installations associated with artificial intelligence workloads, which generate substantially more heat per rack than conventional server deployments.

  Production at the Mason, Ohio facility is scheduled to begin in April 2027.

  這筆3,000萬美元的投資將用於廠房翻修與功能再定位，資金由三菱電機美國控股公司（Mitsubishi Electric US Holdings Inc）提供。

  ## 從義大利製造移轉至美國本土

  目前，三菱電機的IT冷卻系統由旗下子公司——總部位於義大利的三菱電機水力暨IT冷卻系統股份公司（Mitsubishi Electric Hydronics & IT Cooling Systems SpA）負責研發與製造。俄亥俄州廠房的設立，將使部分產能首度落地美國本土。

  在美國市場，三菱電機的工業基礎設施業務目前由三菱電機電力產品公司（Mitsubishi Electric Power Products）主導，其銷售的IT冷卻設備通常與電源系統搭配販售，其中包括不斷電系統（UPS）——這類以電池為後備的裝置，能在電網中斷時維持關鍵負載的持續運作。

  ## 官方說明

  三菱電機表示，在地化生產預計能縮短從製造到交貨的前置時間，確保產品供應更為穩定，同時讓公司得以開發更符合當地客戶需求的產品。公司並指出，俄亥俄州廠房將擴充產品線，納入支援液冷技術的設備——液冷是一種利用水或其他液體，直接從伺服器及其他硬體帶走熱能的散熱方式，目前在高密度資料中心環境中的採用率持續攀升。

  三菱電機將此次布局定位為擴大IT冷卻系統業務、強化其所稱「一站式服務」能力的整體策略一環——所謂一站式服務，涵蓋系統設計、銷售乃至維護的全方位整合支援。

  ## 產業意義

  在美國本土建立IT冷卻設備製造基地，使三菱電機得以在供應鏈可靠性與快速交貨已成採購優先考量的美國資料中心市場上，更直接地與競爭對手抗衡。液冷產品線的加入，也顯示出三菱電機有意進軍與人工智慧（AI）工作負載相關的高密度運算部署市場——此類工作負載每機架所產生的熱量，遠超傳統伺服器的部署規模。
