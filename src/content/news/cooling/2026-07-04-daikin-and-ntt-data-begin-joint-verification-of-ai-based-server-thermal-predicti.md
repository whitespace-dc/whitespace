body_en: |
  Daikin and NTT Data have begun joint verification of an artificial intelligence system designed to predict the thermal state inside data centre servers and use those predictions to control cooling equipment, the two Japanese companies announced.

  The initiative, which started this month, combines NTT Data's data centre operations experience and its correlation data linking server behaviour to air conditioning performance with Daikin's air conditioning control technology and AI-based cooling management capabilities.

  ## How the system works

  A central technical challenge in data centre cooling is that operators often cannot obtain detailed temperature readings from inside individual servers directly. The joint system addresses this by inferring internal thermal conditions from indirect signals — specifically, a server's power consumption and external temperature readings — and feeding those inferences into an AI model.

  Based on the AI's thermal predictions, the system then performs integrated control across three types of equipment: air conditioning units, heat source machinery, and liquid cooling systems. The companies said this coordinated approach is intended to optimise cooling in response to actual server thermal conditions rather than relying solely on ambient or rack-level measurements.

  ## Verification and commercialisation timeline

  Verification will take place at an NTT Data data centre during fiscal year 2026, which runs through March 2027 under Japan's fiscal calendar. The companies are targeting commercialisation in fiscal year 2027.

  The programme draws on NTT Data's operational knowledge of how server workloads and behaviour affect air conditioning demand, paired with Daikin's advanced controls for both air conditioning and heat source equipment.

  ## Industry significance

  The approach — using power draw and surface temperature as proxies for internal server thermal state — offers a potential path to more precise cooling control in facilities where granular per-component telemetry is unavailable or impractical to deploy at scale. If the verification demonstrates that indirect data can reliably stand in for direct internal measurements, the method could apply broadly across existing data centre infrastructure without requiring hardware modifications to servers themselves.

  The companies plan to complete their effectiveness assessment at NTT Data's facility before the end of fiscal year 2026.

  ## 大金與NTT Data攜手驗證AI伺服器熱狀態預測冷卻系統

  大金工業與NTT Data宣布，雙方已啟動人工智慧系統的聯合驗證工作。該系統旨在預測資料中心伺服器內部的熱狀態，並依據預測結果控制冷卻設備。這兩家日本企業表示，相關驗證工作已於本月正式展開。

  此次合作結合了NTT Data在資料中心營運上的豐富經驗，以及其掌握的伺服器行為與空調效能之間的關聯數據，同時導入大金在空調控制技術及AI冷卻管理能力方面的專業積累，形成互補的技術架構。

  ## 系統運作原理

  資料中心冷卻作業的一大核心挑戰，在於業者往往無法直接取得個別伺服器內部的詳細溫度數據。此聯合系統針對這一痛點，改以間接訊號推算伺服器內部的熱狀態——具體而言，是透過伺服器的耗電量及外部溫度讀數，再將這些推算結果輸入AI模型進行分析。

  系統依據AI的熱狀態預測，對三類設備執行整合控制：空調機組、熱源設備，以及液冷系統。兩家公司表示，這種協調聯動的控制方式，旨在根據伺服器實際的熱狀態來優化冷卻效能，而非僅憑環境溫度或機架層級的量測數值來判斷。

  ## 驗證計畫與商業化時程

  驗證工作將於2026財年期間，在NTT Data的資料中心設施內進行。依照日本財年曆，2026財年截止至2027年3月。兩家公司的商業化目標訂在2027財年。

  本計畫充分運用NTT Data對伺服器工作負載與運行行為如何影響空調需求的實務知識，搭配大金針對空調及熱源設備所開發的先進控制技術，雙方優勢相輔相成。

  ## 產業意義

  這套以耗電量與表面溫度作為伺服器內部熱狀態替代指標的方法，為精準冷卻控制提供了一條潛在可行的新路徑，尤其適用於那些難以在所有元件層級大規模部署細粒度遙測設備的資料中心設施。若驗證結果能夠證明，間接數據可以可靠地取代直接內部量測，這套方法便可廣泛應用於現有資料中心基礎設施，且無需對伺服器本身進行任何硬體改裝。

  兩家公司計畫在2026財年結束前，於NTT Data的設施內完成效益評估。
