  ## ILK Dresden expands R290 compressor testing to 48 m³/h

  German refrigeration and energy research institute ILK Dresden is expanding its compressor test capabilities to accommodate R290 — propane — refrigerant at flow rates up to 48 cubic metres per hour, with the service set to be available from autumn 2026. The expansion responds to growing demand for high-performance testing capacity for heat pump and refrigeration equipment that uses climate-friendly refrigerants.

  R290 (propane) is a natural refrigerant with a very low global warming potential, increasingly used in place of synthetic refrigerants in heat pumps and refrigeration systems. Compressors in the performance class targeted by the new bench are used in heat pumps, commercial refrigeration systems, and other medium-sized refrigeration applications, according to ILK Dresden.

  ## What the new bench measures

  Beyond measuring cooling capacity to the DIN EN 13771:2016 standard — the European norm governing compressor performance testing — the new test bench also enables:

  - Differentiated stress and lifetime tests
  - Condition analyses
  - Investigations of dynamic oil behaviour

  The measurement data produced are intended to support evaluation of wear mechanisms, lubrication conditions, and other factors that affect compressor reliability, efficiency, and service life, ILK Dresden said.

  ## Industry context

  ILK Dresden managing director Prof Dr-Ing Uwe Franzke said realistic test conditions are a prerequisite for developing efficient heat pumps and refrigeration systems. "With the expansion of our compressor test bench, we are creating new opportunities to validate components under demanding operating conditions and to demonstrate their long-term reliability," Franzke said. "This helps our industry partners to bring innovations to market faster and more reliably."

  The expansion positions ILK Dresden to serve manufacturers developing and certifying R290-based equipment at a medium-to-large compressor scale, a segment the institute says is seeing increasing demand for rigorous independent testing.
body_zh: |
  ## ILK Dresden 擴充 R290 壓縮機測試能量，流量上限達每小時 48 立方公尺

  德國冷凍與能源研究機構 ILK 德勒斯登（ILK Dresden）正在擴充其壓縮機測試能力，以支援使用 R290（丙烷）冷媒、流量可達每小時 48 立方公尺的機組，相關服務預計於 2026 年秋季正式上線。此次擴建回應了業界對採用氣候友善冷媒之熱泵與冷凍設備的高效能測試產能日益殷切的需求。

  R290（丙烷）是一種全球暖化潛勢（GWP）極低的天然冷媒，日益廣泛地取代合成冷媒，應用於熱泵與冷凍系統之中。ILK 德勒斯登表示，新測試台所針對的性能等級壓縮機，被用於熱泵、商業冷凍系統及其他中型冷凍應用場合。

  ## 新測試台的量測項目

  除了依據 DIN EN 13771:2016 標準量測冷卻能力（此為規範壓縮機性能測試的歐洲標準）之外，新測試台還可執行以下項目：

  - 差異化的耐久與壽命測試
  - 狀態分析
  - 動態油品行為研究

  ILK 德勒斯登表示，所產出的量測資料旨在支援磨損機制、潤滑條件及其他影響壓縮機可靠性、效率與使用壽命之因素的評估。

  ## 產業背景

  ILK 德勒斯登執行董事 Prof Dr-Ing Uwe Franzke 指出，真實的測試條件是開發高效熱泵與冷凍系統的先決條件。Franzke 表示：「藉由擴充我們的壓縮機測試台，我們正為在嚴苛運作條件下驗證零組件、並展示其長期可靠性創造新的機會。這有助於我們的產業夥伴更快速、更穩健地將創新技術推向市場。」

  此次擴建使 ILK 德勒斯登得以服務正在開發及認證中大型 R290 設備的製造商。該研究所表示，這一市場區隔對於嚴格的第三方獨立測試需求，目前正持續增加。
