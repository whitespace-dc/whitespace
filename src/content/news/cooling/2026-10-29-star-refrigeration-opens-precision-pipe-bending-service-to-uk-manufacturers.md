body_en: |
  Star Refrigeration has made its industrial pipe bending capability available to third-party manufacturers across the UK, following the installation of an AMOB CNC tube bender at its Westway manufacturing facility in Renfrew, Scotland.

  The machine can bend carbon steel and stainless steel pipe up to 100NB — a nominal bore designation corresponding to 114.3 mm outside diameter — and handles wall thicknesses up to 4-inch Schedule 40, a standard classification for pipe wall thickness. Star says the machine is one of only a small number in Scotland capable of working at that scale.

  ## What the service covers

  Star is positioning the service for mechanical fabrication companies, mechanical subcontractors, pipe stockists, and industrial engineering businesses that need precision bends without owning the equipment. The company says it can accommodate single bespoke bends, overflow capacity work, and high-volume production runs.

  ## Cost and quality case for bending over welding

  Star states that precision pipe bending can reduce manufacturing costs and improve quality and consistency relative to traditional cut-and-weld fabrication. The company identifies several specific advantages: the process eliminates many hot works, reducing associated safety risks; it avoids contamination introduced by cutting and welding; and it lowers energy consumption by removing much of the welding process.

  "This latest investment strengthens our own manufacturing capability, but it also creates an opportunity to support other businesses that could benefit from specialist pipe bending without the capital investment," said Derek Barclay, Star Refrigeration's production director. "Whether a customer needs a single bespoke bend, support with overflow work or a high-volume production run, we have the capability to deliver precision pipework to the highest standards."

  ## Significance

  By opening its manufacturing facility to outside customers, Star is effectively monetising equipment it acquired for internal production, while addressing a gap in Scotland's industrial fabrication market. For UK manufacturers that currently rely on cut-and-weld methods or lack access to large-bore CNC bending, the service offers an outsourcing route that Star says delivers both cost and safety benefits.

  Barclay said the company intends to apply expertise developed in manufacturing its own refrigeration systems to help other manufacturers improve efficiency and achieve consistent results.

  ## Star Refrigeration開放精密管路彎管服務，供英國製造商使用

  Star Refrigeration宣布，將旗下工業管路彎曲設備開放給英國各地的第三方製造商使用。此舉緣起於該公司在蘇格蘭倫弗魯（Renfrew）Westway廠區新安裝了一台AMOB CNC管路彎曲機。

  這台設備可彎折碳鋼與不鏽鋼管路，管徑上限為100NB——即公稱口徑100，對應外徑114.3毫米——並可處理壁厚達4英吋Schedule 40（管壁厚度的標準分級）的管材。Star表示，這台機器是蘇格蘭境內少數能處理此規格的設備之一。

  ## 服務範圍

  Star將這項服務定位於機械製造公司、機械分包商、管材供應商，以及需要精密彎管但不具備自有設備的工業工程企業。公司表示，可承接單件客製化彎管、超載產能支援，以及大批量生產訂單。

  ## 彎管相較於焊接的成本與品質優勢

  Star指出，精密管路彎曲相較於傳統切割焊接製程，不僅可降低製造成本，更能提升品質與一致性。公司列舉了幾項具體優勢：此製程可省去大量熱工作業，從而降低相關安全風險；可避免切割與焊接所帶來的污染問題；並因大幅減少焊接工序而降低能源消耗。

  Star Refrigeration生產總監Derek Barclay表示：「這項最新投資強化了我們自身的製造能力，同時也為其他企業創造了契機——讓它們無需投入大筆資本，便能享有專業彎管服務。無論客戶需要的是單件客製化彎管、超載產能支援，還是大批量生產，我們都有能力以最高標準交付精密管路。」

  ## 市場意義

  透過向外部客戶開放製造廠區，Star實際上是在將原本為內部生產而購置的設備轉化為收益來源，同時填補了蘇格蘭工業製造市場的一項缺口。對於目前仍依賴切割焊接工法、或無法取得大口徑CNC彎管資源的英國製造商而言，這項服務提供了一條外包管道，Star表示此管道同時帶來成本與安全方面的雙重效益。

  Barclay表示，公司計畫將自身在冷凍設備製造過程中所累積的專業知識，應用於協助其他製造商提升效率、實現穩定一致的生產成果。
