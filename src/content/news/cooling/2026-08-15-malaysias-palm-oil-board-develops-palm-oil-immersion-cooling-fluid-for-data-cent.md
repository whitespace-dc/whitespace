body_en: |
  Malaysia's Palm Oil Board (MPOB), a government agency that supports the country's palm oil industry, is in the final stages of developing a palm oil-derived immersion cooling fluid for data centers called Sawit EcoTherm, the agency has said.

  MPOB said the fluid could reduce both water and energy consumption at Malaysian data centers. Dr. Noor Khairin Mohd, a senior research officer in MPOB's advanced oleochemical technology division, told Malaysian news agency Bernama: "If widely adopted, immersion cooling technology using Sawit EcoTherm has the potential to significantly reduce water demand in the data center industry." The board said it intends to work with data center operators to prove the fluid's efficacy.

  ## Malaysia's data center sector

  Malaysia has an active data center market in Kuala Lumpur and in Johor, a city that benefits from its proximity to Singapore's financial hub. Immersion cooling — a technique in which servers are submerged in a thermally conductive liquid rather than cooled by air — is not yet widely deployed in the country's facilities; AI data center operators have generally favored direct-to-chip liquid cooling. One planned exception is a 65-megawatt immersion-cooled facility in Cyberjaya, Malaysia, funded by state-backed developer Selangor Industrial Corporation.

  ## Palm oil's production and environmental record

  Palm oil is the world's most popular vegetable oil, derived from the pulp of the African oil palm and widely used in food manufacturing, beauty products, and biofuel production. Malaysia is the world's second-largest producer of the oil, after neighboring Indonesia.

  Palm oil production has been linked to deforestation, with plantations replacing diverse habitats for plants and wildlife. Environmental campaigners say the industry has directly harmed endangered species including orangutans and Sumatran elephants. MPOB's announcement did not address those concerns.

  ## Existing market alternatives

  Sawit EcoTherm would enter a segment that already includes vegetable oil-based products. Cargill markets an immersion cooling fluid composed of 90 percent vegetable oil. Other products on the market use fluorocarbons or mineral oils as their base.

  ## Significance

  MPOB's development effort represents a Malaysian government attempt to position the country's established palm oil industry as a supplier to its fast-growing data center sector. Broad adoption, however, would depend on validating the fluid's thermal performance in commercial deployments — work MPOB says it plans to undertake with data center operators.

  ## 馬來西亞資料中心產業

  馬來西亞擁有活躍的資料中心市場，主要集中於吉隆坡，以及因地處新加坡金融樞紐周邊而受惠的柔佛。浸沒式冷卻是一種將伺服器沉浸於導熱液體中、而非倚賴空氣冷卻的技術，目前在馬來西亞設施中尚未普及；AI 資料中心業者普遍較傾向採用直達晶片的液冷方式。其中一個計畫中的例外，是位於馬來西亞賽城（Cyberjaya）的一座 65 百萬瓦浸沒冷卻設施，由官方支持的開發商雪蘭莪工業公司籌建。

  ## 棕櫚油的生產現況與環境爭議

  棕櫚油是全球使用量最大的植物油，萃取自非洲油棕的果肉，廣泛應用於食品加工、美妝產品及生質燃料製造。馬來西亞是全球第二大棕櫚油生產國，僅次於鄰國印尼。

  棕櫚油的生產與森林砍伐問題密切相關，棕油種植園的擴張已大規模取代動植物賴以生存的多樣生態棲地。環保倡議人士指出，棕油產業已直接危害紅毛猩猩與蘇門答臘象等瀕危物種。MPOB 在此次聲明中，並未就上述疑慮作出任何回應。

  ## 現有市場競品

  Sawit EcoTherm 所進入的市場，已存在以植物油為基底的同類產品。嘉吉（Cargill）行銷一款由 90% 植物油組成的浸沒冷卻液。市場上的其他產品則分別以氟碳化合物或礦物油為基底。

  ## 市場意義

  MPOB 的研發行動，代表馬來西亞政府的一次策略性嘗試，意圖將該國成熟的棕油產業定位為快速成長的資料中心產業供應商。然而，能否廣泛推廣，最終仍取決於在商業部署中驗證 Sawit EcoTherm 的熱管理性能——這也正是 MPOB 表示計畫與資料中心業者攜手推進的下一步工作。
