body_en: |
  Carrier has added an R1234ze refrigerant variant to its AquaEdge 19XR two-stage centrifugal chiller range, extending the lineup's options for industrial facilities that require large-scale, efficient cooling.

  The upgraded unit is rated at a coefficient of performance (COP) — a measure of cooling output per unit of energy consumed — of up to 6.6, with an integrated part-load value (IPLV) of 11. IPLV reflects real-world efficiency across varying load conditions, making it a more practical benchmark than peak-load COP alone.

  ## Capacity and operating range

  The 19XR supports cooling capacity of up to 10 MW and operates across an ambient temperature range of -6°C to +45°C, which the company said positions it for demanding industrial process-cooling applications.

  Carrier said the chiller's two-stage compression technology delivers up to 15% higher efficiency in high-lift conditions compared with traditional single-stage designs. High-lift conditions arise when there is a large temperature difference between the chilled water supply and the heat rejection side — common in industrial process cooling.

  ## Design and maintenance

  The 19XR uses a semi-hermetic design, meaning the compressor and motor are enclosed in a shared housing but remain accessible for maintenance — a configuration suited to long-term operation in complex industrial environments, Carrier said.

  The chiller's architecture incorporates 54% fewer aerodynamic components than conventional single-stage chillers, according to the company, reducing maintenance complexity. Carrier also cited a compact footprint and low-noise operation as design outcomes of that configuration.

  ## Refrigerant options

  First introduced in 2014, the AquaEdge 19XR is now available in three refrigerant versions: R1234ze, R513A, and R515B. R1234ze is a low global warming potential (GWP) hydrofluoroolefin (HFO) refrigerant, meaning it contributes significantly less to greenhouse gas accumulation over a 100-year period than the high-GWP hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants it is designed to replace.

  ## Industry context

  The addition of an R1234ze option reflects broader movement across the chiller market toward lower-GWP refrigerants as regulatory pressure on high-GWP HFCs intensifies in the UK and European Union. For industrial operators facing both energy cost exposure and refrigerant compliance timelines, the combination of low-GWP refrigerant and high part-load efficiency in a single platform narrows the field of equipment choices available at the 10 MW capacity level.

  The 19XR R1234ze version is available now alongside the existing R513A and R515B variants.
body_zh: |
  ## 開利為AquaEdge 19XR離心式冷水機新增R1234ze冷媒選項

  開利已在其AquaEdge 19XR兩段式離心冷水機系列中，新增R1234ze冷媒版本，進一步擴充該產品線的選擇，以滿足需要大規模、高效率冷卻的工業設施需求。

  升級版機組的性能係數（COP）——即每單位能耗所產生的冷卻輸出量——最高可達6.6，整合部分負載值（IPLV）則為11。IPLV反映的是在不同負載條件下的實際運行效率，相較於單純的峰值負載COP，是更具實用參考價值的評估指標。

  ## 容量與運行範圍

  19XR的冷卻容量最高可達10 MW，可在攝氏零下6度至正45度的環境溫度範圍內運行。開利表示，這樣的規格使其能夠勝任要求嚴苛的工業製程冷卻應用場景。

  開利指出，該冷水機採用的兩段式壓縮技術，在高揚程條件下的效率比傳統單段式設計高出最多15%。所謂高揚程條件，是指冷凍水供水端與排熱端之間存在較大溫差的情況，這在工業製程冷卻中十分常見。

  ## 設計與維護

  19XR採用半密閉式設計，壓縮機與馬達封裝於共用殼體內，但仍可拆卸維修——開利表示，這種配置適合在複雜工業環境中長期穩定運行。

  根據開利的說法，該冷水機的架構比傳統單段式冷水機減少了54%的氣動元件，有效降低了維護的複雜性。開利同時強調，此一設計配置也帶來了緊湊的機組體積與低噪音運行的優勢。

  ## 冷媒選項

  AquaEdge 19XR於2014年首度推出，目前提供三種冷媒版本：R1234ze、R513A與R515B。R1234ze是一種低全球暖化潛勢（GWP）的氫氟烯烴（HFO）冷媒，意即在百年時間尺度內，其對溫室氣體累積的貢獻遠低於它所取代的高GWP氫氟碳化物（HFC）冷媒。

  ## 產業背景

  R1234ze選項的新增，反映出整體冷水機市場在英國與歐盟對高GWP氫氟碳化物的法規壓力持續升溫之際，普遍朝低GWP冷媒方向轉型的趨勢。對於同時面臨能源成本壓力與冷媒合規期限的工業用戶而言，能在單一平台上同時兼顧低GWP冷媒與高部分負載效率，且容量達10 MW規模的設備選擇，本已相當有限。

  19XR的R1234ze版本現已正式上市，與現有的R513A及R515B版本並行供應。
