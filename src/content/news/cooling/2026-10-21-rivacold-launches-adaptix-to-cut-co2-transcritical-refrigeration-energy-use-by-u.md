body_en: |
  Italian refrigeration manufacturer Rivacold has introduced ADAPTIX, a patented technology aimed at improving the year-round energy efficiency of CO2 transcritical refrigeration systems — the type increasingly used in supermarkets and food retail that operates above CO2's critical point (31.1°C) under warm ambient conditions, where efficiency losses are most pronounced.

  The company says ADAPTIX can deliver energy savings of up to 30% when deployed alone and up to 40% when combined with complementary efficiency technologies such as parallel compression, ejectors, or two-stage systems.

  ## How the technology works

  ADAPTIX continuously monitors ambient conditions and cooling demand, then adjusts system operating parameters in real time. According to Rivacold, the technology targets three interrelated performance levers: discharge pressure, evaporation temperature, and compressor power demand.

  On the high-pressure side of the system — where refrigerant exits the compressor and passes through the gas cooler — ADAPTIX reduces CO2 temperature at the gas-cooler outlet. Lower outlet temperature produces less flash gas (the portion of refrigerant that vaporises unproductively during expansion) and allows discharge pressure to be reduced. Lower discharge pressure directly reduces the work the compressors must perform.

  On the low-pressure side, ADAPTIX raises both the evaporation temperature and pressure. This narrows the compression ratio — the ratio between high-side and low-side pressures — which further reduces the energy each compressor must expend per kilogram of refrigerant circulated.

  The optimised operating point also enables very low superheat, the margin of temperature above the refrigerant's boiling point at which suction gas enters the compressor. Rivacold says this can reduce ice formation and the frequency of defrost cycles. Stable operation under peak cooling demand or high ambient temperatures is also cited as a benefit of the combined superheat and compression-ratio management.

  ## System components

  ADAPTIX integrates hardware and software into a single package. The hardware elements are:

  - A high-efficiency heat exchanger
  - A precision three-way regulating valve
  - Temperature and pressure sensors

  These connect to Rivacold's proprietary RIV-EVO electronic control board, which runs the company's smart algorithms to execute real-time parameter adjustments.

  ## Field installation

  Rivacold has installed ADAPTIX on one of its TX-series CO2 transcritical condensing units at a supermarket in central Italy. The unit is rated at 32 kW for medium-temperature (MT) refrigeration — a scale typical of small to mid-size food retail. The system has been running since June 2026.

  Testing methodology alternates 24-hour periods with ADAPTIX active and inactive, allowing direct comparison under similar conditions. Initial field data show net energy savings of 18–22%, the company said. Rivacold did not specify how many alternating cycles had been completed or over what total period the 18–22% figure was measured.

  ## Availability and retrofit

  ADAPTIX is offered as an optional feature on selected Rivacold R744 — the industry designation for CO2 used as a refrigerant — condensing units and compressor racks, subject to product configuration and installation requirements. Rivacold said retrofitting existing installations may be possible following an assessment of the complete system.

  ## Industry significance

  CO2 transcritical systems have gained wide adoption in food retail as operators phase out higher-global-warming-potential synthetic refrigerants. The technology's main commercial challenge has been its efficiency penalty in warmer climates and during summer peak conditions, where operating above CO2's critical point requires more compressor work than conventional refrigerants. Technologies that recover efficiency under those conditions directly address the primary barrier to broader deployment in southern European and warmer-climate markets — a segment relevant to Rivacold's Italian base.

  ADAPTIX is commercially available now, with field validation continuing at the central Italy supermarket site.
body_zh: |
  義大利冷凍設備製造商Rivacold推出ADAPTIX——一項專利技術，旨在全年提升CO2超臨界冷凍系統的能源效率。這類系統在超市與食品零售業中的應用日益普及，其運作條件為CO2臨界點（31.1°C）以上的高溫環境，而正是在這種條件下，系統效率損失最為顯著。

  Rivacold表示，ADAPTIX單獨部署時最高可節能30%，若搭配並聯壓縮、噴射器或兩段式系統等輔助節能技術，節能幅度可進一步提升至40%。

  ## 技術運作原理

  ADAPTIX持續監測環境條件與冷卻需求，並即時調整系統運作參數。據Rivacold說明，該技術針對三個相互關聯的效能指標進行優化：排氣壓力、蒸發溫度，以及壓縮機耗電量。

  在系統高壓側——即冷媒從壓縮機排出並流經氣體冷卻器的區段——ADAPTIX會降低CO2在氣體冷卻器出口的溫度。出口溫度降低，可減少閃發氣體的產生（閃發氣體是指冷媒在膨脹過程中無效汽化的部分），並使排氣壓力得以下調。排氣壓力降低，可直接減少壓縮機所需執行的壓縮功。

  在系統低壓側，ADAPTIX同時提升蒸發溫度與壓力，縮小高低壓側之間的壓縮比，進一步降低每公斤冷媒循環所需的壓縮機耗能。

  最佳化後的運作點亦可實現極低的過熱度——即吸氣進入壓縮機時，氣態冷媒溫度超過其沸點的餘量。Rivacold指出，此設計有助於減少結冰現象及除霜週期頻率。透過同時管控過熱度與壓縮比，系統在尖峰冷卻需求或高溫環境下的穩定運作能力亦獲得提升。

  ## 系統元件

  ADAPTIX將硬體與軟體整合為單一套件。硬體元件包括：

  - 高效率熱交換器
  - 精密三通調節閥
  - 溫度與壓力感測器

  上述元件與Rivacold自有的RIV-EVO電子控制板相連，由內建智慧演算法執行即時參數調整。

  ## 現場安裝

  Rivacold已在義大利中部一家超市，將ADAPTIX安裝於旗下一套TX系列CO2超臨界冷凝機組上。該機組額定中溫（MT）冷凍容量為32 kW，規格適合小型至中型食品零售業使用，自2026年6月起持續運轉。

  測試方法採交替循環方式，以24小時為單位分別比較ADAPTIX啟用與停用狀態下的能耗，藉此在相近條件下進行直接對照。初步實地數據顯示，淨節能幅度為18至22%。Rivacold並未說明已完成多少個交替週期，亦未透露該18至22%數據的完整測量期間。

  ## 供應與改裝

  ADAPTIX目前作為選配功能，適用於Rivacold部分R744冷凝機組及壓縮機組（R744為業界對CO2冷媒的標準代號），惟需視產品規格與安裝條件而定。Rivacold表示，現有安裝設備在完成整體系統評估後，亦有機會進行改裝升級。

  ## 產業意義

  隨著食品零售業者逐步淘汰高全球暖化潛勢的合成冷媒，CO2超臨界系統已在食品零售業獲得廣泛採用。然而，這類技術的主要商業挑戰在於溫暖氣候及夏季尖峰條件下的效率劣勢——在高於CO2臨界點的運作條件下，所需的壓縮機功耗高於傳統冷媒。能夠在上述條件下回復效率的技術，直接解決了這類系統在南歐及溫暖氣候市場更廣泛推廣的首要障礙，而這正是以義大利為根據地的Rivacold所高度關注的市場區隔。

  ADAPTIX現已正式上市，實地驗證工作仍持續在義大利中部超市現場進行中。
