body_en: |
  Nidec has developed a prototype in-rack coolant distribution unit (CDU) capable of handling up to 300kW of cooling load, the company said, as data center operators face mounting pressure to manage the heat generated by generative AI workloads.

  The CDU made its public debut at Interop Tokyo, described as Japan's premier internet and digital infrastructure technology exhibition.

  ## Design and monitoring features

  The unit is equipped with both internal and external leak sensors, the company said. It also includes real-time monitoring for temperature, pressure, flow rate, and liquid levels, with alarms designed to alert operators to anomalies.

  ## Standards compatibility

  Nidec said the CDU conforms to the OCP ORV3 standard rack specification — a rack form factor developed under the Open Compute Project and widely adopted in high-density data center deployments. The company said the unit is designed for high compatibility with industry-standard rack systems.

  ## Deployment flexibility

  According to Nidec, the CDU can be deployed across a range of environments, from incremental liquid cooling retrofits that make use of existing infrastructure to ground-up designs for next-generation AI data centers. That flexibility is intended to lower the barrier to liquid cooling adoption for operators who are not yet ready for a full infrastructure overhaul.

  ## Industry context

  Nidec cited the widespread adoption of generative AI as the primary driver behind rising thermal loads in data centers, framing the 300kW prototype as a direct response to that trend. CDUs sit between a facility's chilled water or cooling tower infrastructure and the in-rack liquid cooling components, managing the transfer of heat away from servers.

  The prototype's debut at Interop Tokyo positions Nidec to engage directly with the data center engineering and planning community as liquid cooling moves from niche deployments toward broader adoption in AI infrastructure.

  ## 日本電產開發300kW機架內CDU原型機，瞄準AI資料中心散熱需求

  日本電產（Nidec）宣布，已開發出一款機架內液冷分配裝置（CDU）原型機，可承載最高300kW的散熱負荷。隨著生成式AI工作負載持續推升資料中心的散熱壓力，各營運商正面臨愈來愈嚴峻的熱管理挑戰，這款原型機正是針對此一需求而生。

  該CDU於Interop Tokyo正式公開亮相。Interop Tokyo被視為日本規模最大、最具指標性的網路與數位基礎設施技術展覽會。

  ## 設計與監控功能

  日本電產表示，這款裝置同時配備內部與外部洩漏感測器，並提供溫度、壓力、流量及液位的即時監控功能，一旦偵測到異常狀況，系統將自動發出警報，通知維運人員即時處置。

  ## 標準相容性

  日本電產指出，該CDU符合OCP ORV3標準機架規格——這是由開放運算計畫（Open Compute Project）所制定的機架外形規格，已在高密度資料中心部署環境中獲得廣泛採用。該公司表示，此裝置在設計上充分考量與業界標準機架系統的高度相容性。

  ## 部署靈活性

  據日本電產說明，這款CDU可適用於多元的部署情境：既能整合進採用既有基礎設施的漸進式液冷改造方案，也能直接導入專為下一代AI資料中心所設計的全新建置環境。這樣的彈性旨在降低液冷技術的導入門檻，讓尚未準備好進行全面基礎設施升級的營運商，也能逐步跨入液冷領域。

  ## 產業背景

  日本電產將生成式AI的廣泛普及列為資料中心熱負載持續攀升的主要驅動力，並將這款300kW原型機定位為直接回應此一趨勢的解決方案。CDU在整體液冷架構中扮演關鍵角色，負責銜接機房的冷水或冷卻水塔基礎設施與機架內液冷元件，將伺服器產生的熱能有效導出。

  此次選擇在Interop Tokyo發布原型機，讓日本電產得以直接與資料中心工程與規劃社群進行交流互動。隨著液冷技術逐漸從利基應用邁向AI基礎設施的主流部署，日本電產正積極搶佔市場先機。
