body_en: |
  Danfoss and Engie Refrigeration are collaborating on the development of a high-performance chiller using R600a — isobutane, a natural refrigerant — paired with Danfoss's new oil-free Turbocor TNS385 compressor, with the Danish component manufacturer targeting a phased commercial introduction of the compressor from 2027.

  The TNS385 is an oil-free compressor specifically adapted for use with R600a in air-cooled chillers. Danfoss first previewed the unit at the Mostra Convegno trade exhibition in March. Initial development data indicates a nominal cooling capacity of approximately 385 kilowatts for comfort air conditioning under defined conditions — roughly the scale required to condition a large commercial building. Oil-free compressors use magnetic bearings rather than lubricated mechanical ones, which removes refrigerant-oil compatibility as a design constraint and reduces maintenance requirements.

  Following laboratory tests at Danfoss, the compressor is now being evaluated inside a purpose-built refrigeration prototype developed jointly with Engie Refrigeration, Danfoss said. The current development phase addresses compressor performance, system integration, and control, as well as safety requirements specific to R600a, which is classified as an A3 refrigerant — meaning it is flammable and subject to stricter handling and installation standards than most synthetic alternatives.

  ## Why the collaboration is structured as a joint system test

  By testing the compressor and the chiller platform together as a complete system before any market launch, the two companies can identify integration issues and safety edge cases that component-level testing alone would not surface, according to Danfoss. Engie Refrigeration contributes expertise in refrigeration system design, system integration, testing, and qualification; Danfoss contributes its oil-free compressor technology and its application in refrigeration systems.

  The partnership was publicly represented at the Chillventa trade fair earlier this month, where Kristian Strand, president of Danfoss Climate Solutions; Fabio Klein, divisional president of Danfoss Commercial Compressors; Jörn Stiegelmeier, CTO of Engie Refrigeration; and Jochen Hornung, CEO of Engie Refrigeration, appeared together with the TNS385.

  ## Phased introduction and remaining timelines

  Danfoss plans to introduce the TNS385 gradually and in a controlled manner throughout 2027, subject to the successful completion of remaining validation and qualification activities, the company said. The first phase will involve close collaboration with selected customers, intended to generate further insight into application, safety, and system integration requirements before broader commercial availability is assessed.

  Engie Refrigeration's chiller platform based on the TNS385 is also still under development, and no launch date for the complete chiller series has been announced.

  ## Industry significance

  The development is notable for the refrigerant involved. R600a carries a global warming potential near zero compared with the hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants it could displace, making it of interest to operators facing tightening refrigerant regulations. The practical challenge has been its A3 flammability classification, which complicates system design and site installation at the scale of large commercial chillers. A validated, market-ready chiller combining an oil-free compressor with R600a at approximately 385 kW capacity would address a portion of the comfort cooling market where low-GWP options remain limited.

  The next concrete milestone is the completion of validation and qualification activities that would allow Danfoss to begin the controlled 2027 rollout of the TNS385 to its initial customer cohort.
body_zh: |
  丹佛斯（Danfoss）與Engie Refrigeration正攜手開發一款採用R600a——即異丁烷，一種天然冷媒——的高效能冷水機組，搭配丹佛斯全新無油Turbocor TNS385壓縮機。這家丹麥零組件製造商計畫自2027年起分階段進行該壓縮機的商業化導入。

  TNS385是一款專為氣冷式冷水機搭配R600a而設計的無油壓縮機。丹佛斯在今年三月的Mostra Convegno展覽上首度對外展示這款機型。初步開發數據顯示，在特定條件下，TNS385用於舒適型空調的名義冷卻容量約為385千瓦，大致相當於為大型商業建築提供空調所需的規模。無油壓縮機採用磁懸浮軸承取代傳統潤滑機械軸承，因此在設計上毋需考量冷媒與冷凍油之間的相容性問題，同時也降低了維護需求。

  丹佛斯表示，在其實驗室完成測試後，該壓縮機目前已安裝於與Engie Refrigeration共同開發的專屬冷凍系統原型機中進行評估。現階段開發工作聚焦於壓縮機性能、系統整合與控制，以及R600a特有的安全規範——R600a被歸類為A3冷媒，意即其為易燃物質，在處理與安裝標準上受到比多數合成冷媒更為嚴格的規範。

  ## 為何採用聯合系統測試架構

  丹佛斯指出，在任何市場推出前，先將壓縮機與冷水機平台作為完整系統共同測試，兩家公司便能發現單一零組件層級測試所無法揭露的整合問題與安全邊緣案例。Engie Refrigeration在冷凍系統設計、系統整合、測試與認證方面提供專業知識；丹佛斯則貢獻其無油壓縮機技術及其在冷凍系統中的應用能力。

  這項合作關係於本月稍早在Chillventa展覽會上公開亮相，丹佛斯氣候解決方案總裁Kristian Strand、丹佛斯商用壓縮機事業部總裁Fabio Klein、Engie Refrigeration技術長Jörn Stiegelmeier，以及Engie Refrigeration執行長Jochen Hornung四人共同出席，並展示TNS385。

  ## 分階段導入與剩餘時程

  丹佛斯表示，公司計畫在完成剩餘驗證與認證活動後，於2027年全年以循序漸進、受控的方式推出TNS385。第一階段將與特定精選客戶緊密合作，旨在進一步累積應用、安全及系統整合需求等方面的實際洞察，再評估是否擴大商業供應範圍。

  Engie Refrigeration基於TNS385的冷水機組平台目前仍在開發中，完整冷水機系列的上市日期尚未宣布。

  ## 產業意義

  此次開發之所以備受產業關注，關鍵在於所採用的冷媒本身。與R600a可能取代的氫氟碳化物（HFC）冷媒相比，R600a的全球暖化潛勢（GWP）近乎於零，對於面臨日益嚴格冷媒法規的業者而言深具吸引力。實際挑戰在於其A3易燃分類——這使大型商業冷水機組在系統設計與現場安裝上的複雜度大幅提升。若能推出一款結合無油壓縮機與R600a、冷卻容量約達385千瓦的經驗證商用冷水機，將可填補舒適型冷卻市場中低GWP選項仍然有限的一塊缺口。

  下一個具體里程碑，是完成驗證與認證活動，讓丹佛斯得以在2027年開始將TNS385受控地推廣給首批客戶群。
