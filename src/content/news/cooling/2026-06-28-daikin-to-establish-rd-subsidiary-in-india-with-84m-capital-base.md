body_en: |
  Daikin has announced plans to establish a dedicated air conditioning research and development company in India, backed by approximately JPY13.6 billion (US$84 million) in capital, as the Japanese HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) manufacturer deepens its product development presence across the global south.

  The new entity, Daikin Research and Development India Ltd, will be registered in New Delhi and headquartered in Haryana state, according to the company. An accompanying R&D centre will be built in the same state, with completion targeted for June 2028.

  ## Strategic rationale

  Daikin said the subsidiary is aimed at strengthening product development capabilities for markets in Asia, Oceania, and Africa — regions the company groups under the label of the global south. The move signals an intent to localise research closer to the fast-growing demand centres those regions represent, rather than channelling all development through existing facilities.

  ## India operations background

  Daikin first entered India in 2000 through a joint venture, Daikin Airconditioning India Private Ltd, before converting it to a wholly-owned subsidiary in 2004. Since then, the company said, it has expanded India's role to serve both the domestic market and export destinations including Africa and South Asia, positioning the country as a central manufacturing and R&D hub.

  ## What comes next

  The physical R&D centre in Haryana is the most concrete near-term milestone, with the June 2028 completion target providing a defined timeline for when the new subsidiary's research infrastructure is expected to be operational.
