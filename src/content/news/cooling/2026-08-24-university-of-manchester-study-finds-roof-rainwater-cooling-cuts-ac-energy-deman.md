  ## Roof rainwater cooling system reduces AC demand and urban heat, Manchester study finds

  A system that harvests rainwater from rooftops and sprays it onto buildings during hot weather can reduce air conditioning (AC) energy demand and lower urban temperatures, according to research from The University of Manchester published in the journal Earth's Future.

  Researchers used process-based numerical simulations combined with artificial intelligence (AI) to model a system in which rainwater collected from rooftops is stored and automatically sprayed onto buildings during periods of high heat. The simulations were based on conditions in Tokyo.

  ## How the system works

  The cooling mechanism operates on two linked effects. Spraying water onto a roof surface increases evaporation, which cools the roof and reduces the amount of heat transferred into the building below. Lower heat transfer into buildings reduces the need for air conditioning. Less AC use in turn means less waste heat is released back into the surrounding urban environment — a feedback loop that compounds the cooling benefit across a city.

  Roof surface temperatures can be reduced by between 5.5°C and 19.5°C through sprinkling, according to the study.

  The research identifies water availability as the central constraint on roof sprinkling as a standalone cooling strategy. Coupling the sprinkler system with on-site rainwater harvesting — collecting precipitation directly from the roof — addresses that constraint by reducing reliance on mains water supplies and lowering the cost of operating the system.

  ## AI-assisted modelling

  Lead author Dr Zhonghua Zheng, co-lead for environmental data science and AI at the Manchester Environmental Research Institute (MERI) and senior lecturer in data science and environmental analytics at The University of Manchester, said the research demonstrates a practical pathway for cities facing escalating heat stress.

  "Cities around the world are facing growing challenges from extreme heat. Air conditioning can help keep people safe and comfortable, but it also consumes large amounts of energy and releases additional heat into the urban environment," Zheng said.

  "Our study shows that harvesting rainwater from roofs and using it strategically for cooling could provide a practical way to reduce both energy demand and urban temperatures."

  ## Performance scales with heat severity

  The study found that the cooling benefits of the system increase during hotter years — a finding the researchers describe as significant for long-term climate adaptation planning. Zheng said this characteristic suggests the approach "could become increasingly important as the climate continues to warm."

  The research positions roof-based rainwater cooling as a climate adaptation tool for urban areas, where concentrated building density and reduced vegetation amplify heat — a phenomenon known as the urban heat island effect. By reducing both building heat gain and the waste heat generated by air conditioning, the system addresses two reinforcing drivers of elevated urban temperatures simultaneously.

  The study was published in Earth's Future, a journal of the American Geophysical Union covering global change science.

  ## 曼徹斯特大學研究：屋頂雨水冷卻系統可降低空調需求、舒緩城市熱島效應

  英國曼徹斯特大學發表於《地球的未來》（*Earth's Future*）期刊的最新研究指出，一套從屋頂收集雨水、並在高溫期間自動將水噴灑於建築物表面的系統，能夠降低空調（AC）用電需求，同時有效壓低城市整體氣溫。

  研究團隊結合以流程為基礎的數值模擬與人工智慧（AI），針對屋頂收集的雨水在高熱時段自動噴灑於建築物的運作方式進行建模，模擬情境以日本東京的氣候條件為基礎。

  ## 系統運作原理

  這套冷卻機制透過兩項相互關聯的效應發揮作用。將水噴灑於屋頂表面後，蒸發量隨之提升，屋頂因此降溫，進而減少熱能向建築物內部傳導。建築物吸熱減少，自然降低了空調的使用需求；空調使用量下降，廢熱排放至周遭城市環境的量也隨之減少——這形成一個正向回饋循環，使冷卻效益在整座城市中不斷累積放大。

  根據研究數據，透過噴灑水霧，屋頂表面溫度可降低攝氏5.5度至19.5度。

  研究也點出，水源供應是屋頂噴灑系統作為獨立冷卻策略的核心限制。若將噴灑系統與現地雨水收集設施結合——直接從屋頂蒐集降雨——便可解決這項限制，減少對自來水的依賴，同時降低系統的運作成本。

  ## AI輔助建模

  本研究主要作者、曼徹斯特環境研究院（MERI）環境資料科學暨AI共同主持人、同時擔任曼徹斯特大學資料科學與環境分析學系資深講師的鄭中華（Zhonghua Zheng）博士表示，這項研究為飽受熱壓力困擾的城市提供了一條可行的出路。

  「全球各地的城市正面臨極端高溫帶來的日益嚴峻挑戰。空調雖能幫助人們保持安全與舒適，卻也消耗大量電力，並將額外廢熱排放至城市環境中，」鄭中華說。

  「我們的研究顯示，從屋頂收集雨水並策略性地用於冷卻，可能是同步降低用電需求與城市氣溫的可行途徑。」

  ## 降溫效益隨高溫程度提升

  研究發現，系統的冷卻效益在氣溫較高的年份會進一步增強——研究團隊認為這對長期氣候調適規劃具有重要意義。鄭中華表示，這項特性顯示，隨著氣候持續暖化，此一方案「可能將扮演愈來愈重要的角色」。

  這項研究將屋頂雨水冷卻系統定位為城市地區的氣候調適工具。在建築密度高、植被覆蓋稀少的城市中，熱能更容易集中累積，形成所謂的「城市熱島效應」。透過同時減少建築物吸熱量以及空調所產生的廢熱，這套系統能夠一併處理導致城市氣溫居高不下的兩大相互強化因素。

  本研究發表於《地球的未來》，該期刊為美國地球物理聯盟（American Geophysical Union）旗下涵蓋全球變遷科學的學術期刊。
