body_en: |
  QTS has acquired a "mega site" in Van Wert, Ohio and announced a $10 billion data center campus — a commitment that ranks among the largest single-campus investments declared in the United States this cycle and reinforces the Midwest's emergence as a preferred corridor for hyperscale-class compute infrastructure.

  ## Project Scale and Site

  The Van Wert development follows a land-banking model that Tier 1 operators have increasingly adopted: secure contiguous acreage well ahead of permitting and construction to hold optionality in a power-constrained market. QTS has not yet disclosed total site acreage, nameplate power capacity, or a phased build-out schedule, but the $10 billion investment figure signals a multi-building, multi-year campus program rather than a single facility.

  QTS, now part of Blackstone's real estate portfolio following its 2021 acquisition, has been scaling its hyperscale-oriented campus pipeline aggressively. Van Wert is consistent with that strategy.

  ## Economic Impact

  State and local projections for the campus include:

  - **$200 million** in tax revenue over the life of the development
  - **1,500 construction jobs** during the build phase

  These figures align with the economic multiplier effect typical of hyperscale campuses: multi-year construction programs drive sustained local employment and procurement spending before a single kilowatt goes live for a tenant.

  ## Why Van Wert?

  Van Wert County sits in northwestern Ohio, a corridor that checks several boxes for large-load data center siting:

  - **Power infrastructure**: Ohio's transmission grid and access to cost-competitive generation are primary draws for gigawatt-scale customers; the state sits within the PJM interconnection, the largest competitive wholesale electricity market in North America
  - **Land supply**: Agricultural land in northwestern Ohio provides the contiguous acreage required for multi-building campus layouts at a fraction of the per-acre cost found in established data center markets
  - **Fiber connectivity**: Proximity to major Midwest fiber routes linking Chicago and Cleveland backbone nodes
  - **Incentive environment**: Ohio's commercial activity tax structure, combined with county-level economic development programs, has proven competitive for capital-intensive industrial projects

  ## What This Means for Planning and Operations Teams

  A greenfield mega-site commitment at this scale carries several near-term planning implications:

  - **Transmission-level interconnection**: Developments of this size typically require direct negotiation with PJM for dedicated interconnection agreements, a process that can run two to four years and must be initiated early in the project timeline
  - **Phased capital deployment**: Campuses at this price point almost universally deliver in phases; initial buildings will represent a fraction of the total committed capital, with subsequent phases gated on customer pre-leasing or build-to-suit commitments
  - **Workforce pipeline**: Supporting 1,500 construction roles in a rural county requires proactive engagement with regional trade unions, apprenticeship programs, and community colleges — a coordination task that often begins in parallel with permitting
  - **Water and cooling rights**: Greenfield campus planning at hyperscale requires early-stage assessment of water availability and discharge rights, particularly as liquid cooling becomes standard for high-density AI workloads

  The Van Wert announcement adds another data point to a clear pattern: hyperscale operators and their capital partners are moving land acquisition upstream, treating site control as a strategic asset before demand is fully crystallized.

  ## 專案規模與基地概況

  范沃特開發案採用土地預儲模式——一線營運商近年日益倚重此策略，在取得許可及動工前數年即預先掌握連片土地，以在電力受限市場中保有彈性空間。QTS目前尚未公布園區總占地面積、額定電力容量或分期建設時程，但100億美元的投資承諾，顯示此案屬多棟建築、多年期的園區計畫，而非單一設施。

  QTS於2021年被黑石集團（Blackstone）收購後，持續積極擴張以超大規模為導向的園區組合。范沃特案正是此一戰略的最新體現。

  ## 經濟效益

  州府及地方政府對本案的預估效益如下：

  - **稅收貢獻**：開發期間累計產生 **2億美元** 稅收
  - **建設就業**：建設階段創造 **1,500個** 工作機會

  上述數字與超大規模資料中心園區典型的經濟乘數效應相符——多年期建設工程在設施正式對租戶供電前，即可持續帶動當地就業及採購支出。

  ## 范沃特的區位優勢

  范沃特縣位於俄亥俄州西北走廊，在大用電量資料中心選址上具備多項條件：

  - **電力基礎設施**：俄亥俄州輸電網路完善，加上具競爭力的發電成本，對吉瓦級大用電量客戶極具吸引力；該州隸屬PJM互聯系統，為北美最大的競爭性躉售電力市場
  - **土地供應**：俄亥俄州西北部農業腹地提供大面積連片土地，適合多棟建築園區規劃佈局，每英畝地價遠低於成熟資料中心市場
  - **光纖連通性**：毗鄰連結芝加哥及克里夫蘭主幹網的中西部主要光纖路由
  - **獎勵環境**：俄亥俄州商業活動稅結構結合縣級經濟發展方案，對資本密集型工業開發案具備競爭優勢

  ## 對規劃與營運團隊的啟示

  此規模的綠地超大型基地承諾，對近期規劃工作帶來多項重要影響：

  - **輸電級互聯**：此規模的開發案通常須直接與PJM談判專用互聯協議，過程可長達兩至四年，必須在專案時程早期即啟動
  - **分期資本部署**：此投資規模的園區幾乎均採分期交付模式；初期建築僅動用總承諾資本的一小部分，後續各期視客戶預租賃或客製化建設承諾而定
  - **勞動力管道**：在農村縣份支應1,500個建設職位，需積極與區域工會、學徒計畫及社區學院協調，此項工作通常與許可申請程序同步展開
  - **水資源與冷卻用水權**：超大規模綠地園區規劃須在早期階段評估供水可用性及排水權，尤其隨著液冷技術成為高密度AI工作負載的標準配置，此議題愈發重要

  范沃特宣布案為一個清晰趨勢再添佐證：超大規模營運商及其資本合作夥伴正將土地取得提前至需求完全成形之前，將基地控制權視為具有前瞻性的戰略資產。
