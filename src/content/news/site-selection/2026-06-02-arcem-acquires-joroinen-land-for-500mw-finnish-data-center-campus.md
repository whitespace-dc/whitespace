# Arcem acquires Joroinen land for 500MW Finnish data center campus

Arcem has acquired land in Joroinen, Finland to build a 500MW data center campus. The project is one of several data center developments the company is planning in the country. This expansion extends Arcem's European data center portfolio.

## 中文

# Arcem在芬蘭Joroinen收購土地興建500MW資料中心園區

Arcem在芬蘭Joroinen收購土地，計畫興建500MW資料中心園區。該專案為公司在芬蘭規劃的多項資料中心專案之一。此次擴展增強了Arcem在歐洲的資料中心布局。
