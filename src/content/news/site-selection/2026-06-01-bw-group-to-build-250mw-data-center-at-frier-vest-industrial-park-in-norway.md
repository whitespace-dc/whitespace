# BW Group to Build 250MW Data Center at Frier Vest Industrial Park in Norway

BW Group has signed a deal to construct a 250MW data center at the new Frier Vest industrial park in Telemark County, Norway. The facility will expand data center capacity in the region and support growing demand for data services in Scandinavia.

## 中文

# BW集團在挪威興建250MW資料中心

BW集團簽署協議，將在挪威特勒馬克郡新建的Frier Vest工業園區興建一座250MW資料中心。該設施將擴展該地區的資料中心容量，滿足北歐地區日益增長的資料中心服務需求。
