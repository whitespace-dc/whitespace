# 2,000-Acre Parcel in Mason County, Kentucky Rezoned for Data Center Amid Opposition

A 2,000-acre parcel in Mason County, Kentucky has been rezoned for data center use. The rezoning decision has faced mounting opposition from local residents concerned about the project's impact. The development represents significant infrastructure investment in the region.
