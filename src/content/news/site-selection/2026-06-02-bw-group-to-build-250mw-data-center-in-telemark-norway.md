# BW Group to Build 250MW Data Center in Telemark, Norway

BW Group has signed a partnership agreement to develop a 250-megawatt data center at the Frier Vest industrial park in Telemark County, Norway. The facility represents the company's continued investment in Nordic data center infrastructure.

## 中文

# BW集團在挪威特萊馬克郡興建250MW資料中心

BW集團簽署合作協議，將在挪威特萊馬克郡Frier Vest工業園區開發250百萬瓦資料中心。該設施代表該公司對北歐資料中心基礎設施的持續投資。
