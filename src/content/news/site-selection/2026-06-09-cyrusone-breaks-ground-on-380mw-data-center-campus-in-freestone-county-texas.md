body_en: |
  CyrusOne has broken ground on a new data center campus in Freestone County, Texas, located approximately 90 miles south of Dallas, the company announced. The development sits adjacent to the Calpine-operated Freestone Energy Center and is backed by power agreements totaling 760 megawatts.

  The KKR-owned colocation provider earlier this year signed a 380MW agreement with Constellation to connect and serve a data center development at the site. The companies subsequently signed a second agreement for an additional 380MW at the same location, bringing the total contracted capacity to 760MW. Full details of the planned development have not been disclosed.

  ## The power source

  The Freestone Energy Center is a 1-gigawatt natural gas-fired combined-cycle facility — a plant type that generates electricity by running fuel through both a gas turbine and a steam turbine, extracting more energy from each unit of fuel than a simple-cycle plant. The facility is located on approximately 506 acres near Fairfield, Texas, in Freestone County. Calpine secured regulatory approval to colocate a data center at the site around May 2026.

  Colocation of data centers directly adjacent to power generation facilities has become an emerging strategy for hyperscale and wholesale operators seeking to secure large, dedicated power supplies without competing for constrained grid capacity.

  ## CyrusOne's Texas footprint

  CyrusOne operates more than 50 data centers across U.S. and European markets. In Texas, the company has more than 14 data centers — both planned and operational — with more than half concentrated in the Dallas-Fort Worth area.

  The Freestone County campus is not CyrusOne's only Texas project tied to a co-located gas plant. The company is also developing a 400MW campus adjacent to Calpine's Thad Hill Energy Center in Bosque County. The first data center at that site is currently under construction and is expected to be operational by the fourth quarter of 2026.

  ## Significance

  The Freestone groundbreaking adds to a pattern of large-scale data center development in Texas tied directly to natural gas generation assets, reflecting the difficulty operators face in securing sufficient grid power for campuses measured in hundreds of megawatts. With 760MW contracted at Freestone and 400MW under development at Bosque County, CyrusOne is committing more than 1 gigawatt of new Texas capacity to the co-location-with-generation model.

  The next development milestone on record is the expected fourth-quarter 2026 operational date for the first building at CyrusOne's Bosque County campus.

  CyrusOne宣布，已在德州Freestone County破土動工，興建全新資料中心園區。該地點位於達拉斯以南約145公里處，緊鄰Calpine營運的Freestone Energy Center，背後並有總計760百萬瓦（MW）的供電協議提供支撐。

  KKR旗下的主機代管業者CyrusOne，今年稍早與Constellation簽訂一份380MW協議，取得在該廠址開發資料中心所需的電力連接與供電服務。雙方隨後再度簽署第二份協議，於同一地點追加380MW，合計簽約容量達760MW。目前該開發計畫的完整細節尚未對外公布。

  ## 電力來源

  Freestone Energy Center是一座裝置容量達1吉瓦（GW）的天然氣複循環電廠——此類電廠同時驅動燃氣渦輪機與蒸汽渦輪機發電，相較於單循環機組，能從每單位燃料中提取更多電能。該設施位於德州Freestone County費爾菲爾德（Fairfield）附近，佔地約506英畝（約205公頃）。Calpine約於2026年5月取得監管機構核准，獲准在廠址內共置資料中心。

  資料中心直接設於發電廠毗鄰地點的共置模式，已成為超大規模與批發型業者日益仰賴的策略——這些業者希望取得大量專屬電力，同時避開在已趨緊繃的電網容量中相互競爭的困境。

  ## CyrusOne在德州的佈局

  CyrusOne在美國與歐洲市場合計營運逾50座資料中心。在德州，公司旗下已有超過14座資料中心（涵蓋規劃中及已投入營運者），其中逾半數集中於達拉斯—沃斯堡都會區。

  Freestone County園區並非CyrusOne在德州唯一結合電廠共置模式的開發案。該公司同時在Bosque County的Calpine Thad Hill Energy Center旁開發一座400MW園區，首棟資料中心大樓目前正在施工中，預計於2026年第四季正式投入營運。

  ## 重要意義

  Freestone的破土動工，為德州一系列直接依託天然氣發電資產的大型資料中心開發案再添新頁，折射出業者在為動輒數百MW規模的園區確保充足電力時所面臨的現實困境。Freestone已簽訂760MW合約、Bosque County正在開發400MW，CyrusOne等於在德州為「電廠共置」模式投入逾1吉瓦的全新產能。

  目前有跡可循的下一個開發里程碑，是CyrusOne Bosque County園區首棟建築預計於2026年第四季完工啟用。
