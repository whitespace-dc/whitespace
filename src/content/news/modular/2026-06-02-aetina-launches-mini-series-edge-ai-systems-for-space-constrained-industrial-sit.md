# Aetina launches Mini Series edge AI systems for space-constrained industrial sites

Aetina has launched its Mini Series edge AI systems, compact platforms designed for industrial environments with limited space. The systems enable local vision AI processing at the edge, delivering advanced AI capabilities directly to industrial operations. The Mini Series has entered mass production.
