body_en: |
  Nvidia has signed leases worth up to $50 billion for Hut 8's 1-gigawatt Beacon Point data center campus in Texas, the Financial Times reported on July 28, citing five people familiar with the deal — naming the chipmaker as the undisclosed tenant behind a contract Hut 8 announced last week without identifying its counterparty.

  Hut 8 said last week the Beacon Point campus carried a base-term contract value of $19.6 billion over 15 years, rising to as much as $50.2 billion if renewal options are exercised. The company described the tenant only as an "existing investment-grade customer" that would install computing equipment at the site to support large-scale AI training and operations.

  Nvidia neither confirmed nor denied the Financial Times report. A company spokesperson told Reuters that "Nvidia is working with ecosystem partners to accelerate the deployment of efficient AI infrastructure through the DSX AI factory architecture." Hut 8 did not respond to a request for comment outside business hours. Reuters said it could not immediately verify the report.

  ## Facility scope and GPU deployment

  Nvidia would lease the entire 1-gigawatt (GW) facility — enough to house hundreds of thousands of Nvidia graphics processing units (GPUs, the chips used to run AI workloads), the Financial Times reported, citing sources. One person familiar with the deal told the FT: "They have the ability to use their balance sheet to get power, and use that to ensure their products get deployed."

  The commitment reflects a strategy by Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang to deploy the company's financial resources — Nvidia carries a market capitalization of nearly $5 trillion, according to Commercial Times — to secure a central role in the fast-growing AI compute market. Earlier moves included spending billions of dollars to support infrastructure providers such as CoreWeave, which buys and operates Nvidia GPUs. The Texas lease goes a step further, making Nvidia a direct financial backer of the facility housing its own chips, the FT reported.

  ## Power access

  The Beacon Point campus has secured a stable electricity supply, according to sources cited by the Financial Times — a material advantage at a time when data center developers are competing for grid capacity. Nvidia's financial commitment helped the company secure exclusive use of that power for its own hardware, the FT reported.

  ## Sublease structure and circular-financing concerns

  Once operational, Nvidia could sublease capacity to what the Financial Times described as "neocloud" partners: companies that buy Nvidia GPUs and resell AI cloud computing to end users. That structure has drawn scrutiny over what sources described as "circular financing" — the chipmaker using its balance sheet to underwrite demand for its own products, according to the FT and Commercial Times.

  ## DSX architecture

  Nvidia's spokesperson referenced the company's DSX platform in response to questions about the report. DSX is Nvidia's full-stack AI factory architecture, designed to help plan, build, and operate large-scale AI data centers by combining Nvidia technology with equipment from partner companies, according to Reuters.

  ## Ohio talks

  Separately, the Financial Times reported, citing sources, that Nvidia is also in talks to provide up to $250 billion in financial backing to help lease a 10-gigawatt data center that SoftBank is building in Ohio. The potential Ohio arrangement was first reported by The Information and The Wall Street Journal, according to Commercial Times.

  《金融時報》7月28日引述五位知情人士報導，輝達（Nvidia）已就Hut 8位於德州、容量達1 GW的Beacon Point資料中心園區簽署價值高達500億美元的租約，確認輝達即為Hut 8上週公告合約中未予披露的承租方。對此，輝達既未確認亦未否認相關報導。

  Hut 8上週宣布，Beacon Point園區基本合約期限為15年，基本合約總值為196億美元；若承租方行使續租選擇權，合約總值最高可達502億美元。Hut 8僅描述承租方為「現有投資等級客戶」，將在該場址安裝運算設備，以支援大規模人工智慧（AI）訓練與營運。

  對於《金融時報》的報導，輝達既未確認亦未否認。輝達發言人向路透社表示：「輝達正與生態系夥伴合作，透過DSX AI工廠架構加速高效AI基礎設施的部署。」Hut 8則未在非上班時間回應媒體的置評請求。路透社表示目前無法立即核實相關報導。

  ## 廠區規模與GPU部署

  《金融時報》引述知情人士報導，輝達將承租整座1 GW廠區——其容量足以容納數十萬片輝達繪圖處理器（GPU，即用於執行AI工作負載的晶片）。一位知情人士告訴《金融時報》：「他們有能力運用自身的資產負債表取得電力，並藉此確保旗下產品得以順利部署。」

  這項承諾反映了輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）運用公司財務資源的策略——根據《工商時報》，輝達市值目前接近5兆美元——以在快速成長的AI運算市場中確立核心地位。此前，輝達已斥資數十億美元支持CoreWeave等基礎設施供應商，後者負責購入並營運輝達GPU。德州租約更進一步，使輝達直接成為承載其自家晶片之廠房的財務後盾，《金融時報》如此報導。

  ## 電力供應

  《金融時報》引述知情人士指出，Beacon Point園區已取得穩定電力供應——在資料中心開發商競相搶奪電網容量之際，此乃一項重要優勢。輝達的財務承諾協助該公司取得這批電力的獨家使用權，專供自家硬體使用，《金融時報》如此報導。

  ## 轉租架構與循環融資疑慮

  一旦正式投入營運，輝達可能將部分容量轉租予《金融時報》所稱的「新興雲端」（neocloud）夥伴——這些公司購入輝達GPU，再向終端用戶轉售AI雲端運算服務。這項架構已引發外界關注，據《金融時報》及《工商時報》報導，知情人士將其形容為「循環融資」，亦即這家晶片製造商利用自身資產負債表為旗下產品的需求提供擔保。

  ## DSX架構

  輝達發言人在回應媒體就相關報導所提問詢時，提及該公司的DSX平台。據路透社，DSX是輝達的全棧式AI工廠架構，旨在結合輝達技術與夥伴企業的設備，協助規劃、建構及營運大規模AI資料中心。

  ## 俄亥俄州談判

  另據《金融時報》引述知情人士報導，輝達亦正洽談為軟銀（SoftBank）在俄亥俄州興建中的一座10 GW資料中心提供高達2,500億美元的財務支持，以協助租賃相關設施。根據《工商時報》，這項潛在的俄亥俄州安排最初由《The Information》及《華爾街日報》率先披露。
