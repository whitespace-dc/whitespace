body_en: |
  BDx Data Centers has secured a 1.2-gigawatt (GW) power supply commitment from PT Perusahaan Listrik Negara (PLN), Indonesia's state electricity utility, making it the largest power commitment ever awarded to a data center operator in the country, the company announced June 3, 2026.

  The agreement allocates capacity across three sites: 788 MVA (megavolt-amperes, a measure of electrical capacity) for CGK4 in Jatiluhur, West Java; approximately 60 MVA to expand the existing CGK3A facility in South Jakarta; and up to 385 MVA for the planned CGK5 development in Suryacipta, West Java. Together, those tranches sum to the 1.2 GW figure.

  ## Facility specifications

  Each of the three sites serves a distinct segment of the data center market. CGK4 in Jatiluhur is designed as an AI campus with a total planned capacity of up to 650 MW and already supports high-end graphics processing units (GPUs) used for AI model training. CGK3 in South Jakarta is equipped with liquid cooling technology to handle high-density computing workloads. CGK5 in Suryacipta is planned as a hyperscale data center — a large-scale facility built to the specifications of major cloud providers — with capacity of up to 300 MW.

  ## What the parties said

  BDx Indonesia CEO Agus Hartono Wijaya said securing grid power at this scale is a prerequisite for expanding to serve the largest cloud and technology customers. "Reliable and scalable power supply is the primary foundation for AI and digital infrastructure development in Indonesia. PLN's support enables us to expand capacity to meet hyperscaler needs and digital workloads," he said in a statement.

  PLN Director of Retail and Commerce Adi Priyanto framed the agreement as part of the utility's strategy to back Indonesia's digital economy. "PLN is committed to providing reliable and continuously scalable electrical infrastructure to support the growth of AI and the national digital economy," he said.

  ## Significance for Indonesia's data center sector

  The 1.2 GW commitment sets a new power-offtake benchmark for Indonesia's data center industry. Growing demand for AI computing and cloud workloads is driving requirements for large-capacity, purpose-built facilities, and grid power availability is a principal constraint on hyperscale development. By securing commitments across three sites simultaneously, BDx has locked in the electrical foundation for a combined potential capacity that spans both existing and greenfield campuses.

  The next concrete milestone in the portfolio is CGK5 in Suryacipta, for which BDx has secured up to 385 MVA of grid capacity and a planned buildout of up to 300 MW.

  BDx 資料中心於 2026 年 6 月 3 日宣布，已從印尼國營電力公司 PT Perusahaan Listrik Negara（PLN）取得高達 1.2 吉瓦（GW）的電力供應承諾，成為印尼迄今授予單一資料中心業者規模最大的電力承諾。

  此協議將電力容量分配至三處園區：西爪哇 Jatiluhur 的 CGK4 獲配 788 百萬伏安（MVA，電力容量計量單位）；南雅加達現有 CGK3A 設施的擴建工程獲配約 60 MVA；西爪哇 Suryacipta 計畫開發的 CGK5 則獲配最多 385 MVA。三筆電力額度合計達 1.2 GW。

  ## 設施規格

  三座園區各服務資料中心市場的不同細分領域。位於 Jatiluhur 的 CGK4 定位為 AI 園區，總規劃容量最高達 650 百萬瓦（MW），目前已支援用於 AI 模型訓練的高階繪圖處理器（GPU）。南雅加達的 CGK3 配備液態冷卻技術，可承載高密度運算工作負載。Suryacipta 的 CGK5 則規劃為超大規模資料中心——即依主要雲端服務供應商規格興建的大型設施——容量最高達 300 MW。

  ## 各方表態

  BDx 印尼執行長 Agus Hartono Wijaya 表示，在此規模下取得市電供應，是進一步服務頂尖雲端與科技客戶的先決條件。他在聲明中指出：「可靠且可擴展的電力供應，是印尼 AI 與數位基礎設施發展的首要基石。PLN 的支持使我們得以擴大容量，滿足超大規模業者的需求及各類數位工作負載。」

  PLN 零售暨商業事務董事 Adi Priyanto 將此協議定位為該電力公司支持印尼數位經濟策略的一環。他表示：「PLN 致力於提供可靠且持續可擴展的電力基礎設施，以支撐 AI 成長及國家數位經濟的發展。」

  ## 對印尼資料中心產業的意義

  1.2 GW 的電力承諾為印尼資料中心產業樹立了新的電力採購基準。AI 運算與雲端工作負載的需求持續成長，推動業界對大容量專用設施的迫切需要，而市電供應能力則是超大規模資料中心發展的主要制約因素。藉由同步鎖定三處園區的電力承諾，BDx 已為橫跨現有設施與全新開發園區的龐大潛在容量，奠定堅實的電力基礎。

  投資組合中下一個具體里程碑，是位於 Suryacipta 的 CGK5——BDx 已為該園區取得最多 385 MVA 的市電容量，並規劃最高 300 MW 的建置規模。
