body_en: |
  The National Unity Party (NUP) filed House Bill No. 11758, the proposed Advanced Industries Development Act, on Monday, seeking to carve out a dedicated advanced industries zone in New Clark City, Tarlac, with a projected minimum of 150,000 high-skilled jobs and a per-locator investment floor of P15 billion (excluding land costs).

  The bill was presented by NUP Chairperson and Antipolo City Representative Ronaldo V. Puno during a media briefing, where he described the investment threshold and job creation targets underpinning the proposal.

  ## Zone structure and investment requirements

  The proposed zone would cover 1,350 hectares and operate as a separate customs territory, meaning goods, capital equipment, and raw materials could be imported and stored duty-free and tax-free. Qualified enterprises would be required to invest at least P15 billion, excluding the value of land. Registered project expansions would require a minimum additional investment of P5 billion.

  Eligible industries listed in the bill include:

  - Semiconductor design and fabrication
  - Advanced packaging
  - Robotics and precision engineering
  - Artificial intelligence (AI) data centers
  - High-performance computing
  - Critical mineral processing
  - Scientific research

  ## Proposed governing authority

  The bill would create the Advanced Industries Development Authority (AIDA) as an independent government body with fiscal and administrative autonomy to oversee zone development and operations. AIDA would receive P50 billion in authorized capital stock. The National Treasury would provide an initial annual appropriation of P1 billion for five years to fund operations.

  ## Tax incentives

  Qualified enterprises would receive a seven-year income tax holiday. After that period, locators could choose between a 5% special corporate income tax on gross income earned or a 20% corporate income tax under an enhanced deductions regime.

  ## Jobs and utilities

  Projections cited in the bill estimate the zone would generate between 130,000 and 190,000 high-skilled jobs for engineers, technicians, and researchers, with Puno citing a minimum figure of 150,000. He emphasized the roles would be directed at skilled workers rather than lower-skilled occupations.

  The bill also stipulates that the zone would have its own dedicated power and water systems to support industrial operations without drawing on resources serving nearby communities.

  ## Significance

  If enacted, the legislation would concentrate semiconductor and AI infrastructure investment into a single government-designated zone with a purpose-built regulatory body, a model intended to reduce friction for large capital deployments. The P15 billion per-locator minimum is designed to filter for large-scale industrial investment rather than lighter commercial activity. The bill now advances through the Philippine House of Representatives for further deliberation.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓國家統一黨（NUP）週一向眾議院提交第11758號眾議院法案，即《先進產業發展法》草案，擬在塔拉克省新克拉克市劃設一座專屬先進產業特區，預計創造至少15萬個高技術就業機會，每家進駐企業（不含土地成本）的最低投資門檻設定為150億披索。

  法案由國家統一黨黨主席、安蒂波洛市眾議員羅納多．普諾（Ronaldo V. Puno）在媒體說明會上正式提出，他在會中詳述了投資門檻的設定依據與就業目標。

  ## 特區架構與投資要求

  擬議特區面積達1,350公頃，將作為獨立關稅領域運作，意即貨物、資本設備及原物料均可免關稅、免稅進口及儲存。合格企業須投入至少150億披索（不計土地價值）；已登記的專案擴充案則需額外最低投資50億披索。

  法案列出的合格產業包括：

  - 半導體設計與製造
  - 先進封裝
  - 機器人與精密工程
  - 人工智慧（AI）資料中心
  - 高效能運算
  - 關鍵礦物加工
  - 科學研究

  ## 擬設管理機構

  法案將設立「先進產業發展署」（Advanced Industries Development Authority，AIDA），作為具備財政與行政自主權的獨立政府機構，負責監管特區的開發與營運事務。AIDA核定股本為500億披索。國庫將在前五年每年撥付10億披索初始經費，以支應機構運作所需。

  ## 稅務優惠

  合格企業可享有七年所得稅假期。優惠期屆滿後，進駐業者可選擇按總收益課徵5%特別企業所得稅，或依加強扣除制度適用20%企業所得稅。

  ## 就業與公用設施

  法案引用的預估數據顯示，特區預計將為工程師、技術人員及研究人員創造13萬至19萬個高技術就業機會，普諾援引的最低數字為15萬人。他強調，相關職位是針對技術型人才，而非低技術勞工。

  法案亦明訂，特區將建置專屬獨立的電力與供水系統，以支援工業運作，同時避免排擠鄰近社區的公用資源。

  ## 重要意義

  若法案順利通過立法，此舉將把半導體與人工智慧基礎設施投資集中於單一政府指定特區，並設有專責監管機構，此一模式旨在降低大規模資本部署所面臨的行政摩擦。每家業者150億披索的最低投資門檻，其設計目的在於篩選大型工業投資案，而非一般輕型商業活動。法案目前已進入菲律賓眾議院的後續審議程序。
