body_en: |
  Saudi Arabia's National Infrastructure Fund (Infra) and HUMAIN signed two financing agreements on 8 November 2026 worth up to SAR 2.5 billion (US$667 million), marking the first formal commitments under Infra's AI Data Center Financing Program and setting up electrical infrastructure designed to support forty times the computing load being financed in the opening tranche.

  The deals were announced the following day. Infra is Saudi Arabia's national infrastructure financing body; HUMAIN is the kingdom's state-linked AI and cloud computing company.

  ## Two facilities, two purposes

  The first agreement, worth up to SAR 1.1 billion, will finance graphics processing units (GPUs) — the specialized chips used for AI training and inference — representing approximately 25 megawatts (MW) of information technology load. The second, larger facility of up to SAR 1.4 billion will fund substations, transmission lines, and grid connections. That electrical infrastructure is engineered to accommodate up to 1 gigawatt (GW) of IT capacity, roughly forty times the computing load covered by the GPU financing.

  The gap between the two figures is intentional. Esmail bin Mohammed Alsallom, CEO of Infra, said the program is designed to prepare a broader pipeline of projects for investment beyond the initial deployment. "Supporting HUMAIN's computing deployment alongside essential power infrastructure helps prepare a broader pipeline of data center projects for investment," he said. "Our role is to strengthen project readiness and bankability, with commercial viability and economic value guiding how we deploy our capital."

  He added that the agreements are intended to "unlock investment well beyond Infra's own commitment."

  ## Prior framework and HUMAIN ownership

  The 8 November agreements follow a non-binding financing framework announced by Infra and HUMAIN at the World Economic Forum in Davos in January 2026, which covered up to SAR 4.5 billion for up to 250MW of AI data centre capacity. The November deals represent the first binding commitments under that broader arrangement.

  The announcement also coincides with a change in HUMAIN's ownership structure. Aramco, Saudi Arabia's national oil company, acquired a 49% stake in HUMAIN in November 2026, contributing US$2.6 billion in cash and additional assets as part of the transaction. The Public Investment Fund (PIF), Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, retained a 51% majority stake.

  Research accompanying the announcement identifies a target of 3GW of AI data centre capacity by 2030 and more than 6GW by 2034. HUMAIN has also disclosed partnerships with Nvidia, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and xAI.

  ## Power infrastructure as a binding constraint

  The allocation of SAR 1.4 billion — the larger of the two facilities — to electrical infrastructure rather than computing hardware reflects the degree to which grid access is shaping the pace of AI data centre expansion in the kingdom.

  Saudi Arabia's electricity transmission network, managed by National Grid SA, the transmission arm of Saudi Energy, spans 104,633 circuit-kilometres and includes 1,315 substations serving 13,000 cities and villages, according to the organization's 2025 annual report. Saudi Energy has set a target of approximately 160,000 kilometres of transmission lines and 1,650 substations by 2030, according to its 2024 financial results.

  That expansion is driven partly by the kingdom's energy transition goals. Under Vision 2030, Saudi Arabia's national economic diversification program, the kingdom aims to generate 50% of its electricity from renewable sources by 2030. Building out renewable generation adds to the transmission capacity that AI data centres will also compete for.

  ## Vision 2030 and digital sovereignty

  The Infra-HUMAIN agreements sit within a wider Saudi strategy to build domestic technology capabilities and establish the kingdom as a regional hub for AI and cloud computing. Digital sovereignty — encompassing data localization, domestic computing infrastructure, and Saudi-controlled technology capabilities — is cited as a driving factor in that strategy.

  Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law and sector-specific regulations impose localization requirements on certain categories of sensitive information, supporting domestic demand for data centre capacity. International cloud providers including Google, Oracle, and Alibaba have established or announced cloud regions in the kingdom, while domestic operators are expanding their infrastructure.

  The Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA) oversees the National Strategy for Data and AI, which targets a position among the world's top 15 countries in AI by 2030. The kingdom's position between US and Chinese technology ecosystems presents opportunities for investment and cooperation, the source notes, but also potential exposure to export controls and technology restrictions.

  ## Financing program eligibility criteria

  Infra's AI Data Center Financing Program sets technical and commercial conditions that reflect the standard its first transactions are expected to meet.

  Facilities must generally be located within designated AI data centre hubs with neutral access to international subsea cables and Saudi Arabia's national fibre network. Projects must meet Tier III standards or higher — a classification indicating fault-tolerant infrastructure with no single point of failure — and target a power usage effectiveness (PUE) of 1.4 or below on a best-efforts basis. PUE measures total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy; a PUE of 1.4 means 40% of power consumed goes to overhead such as cooling.

  At least 50% of capacity must be contracted or in advanced negotiations at financial close, and projects generally must represent a minimum IT load of 10MW, though smaller developments may be considered individually.

  ## What the agreements do and do not establish

  The agreements provide a financing framework for expansion beyond HUMAIN's initial 25MW computing deployment but do not set timelines for when the full 1GW of electrical capacity will be matched by corresponding computing infrastructure. Converting substations and grid connections into operating data centres will require further GPU procurement, project delivery, and customer commitments.

  For Infra, the immediate test is whether the HUMAIN transactions can attract the private and institutional capital the program is designed to mobilize. HUMAIN's stated development targets call for 3GW of AI data centre capacity in Saudi Arabia by 2030 and more than 6GW by 2034.
body_zh: |
  沙烏地阿拉伯國家基礎設施基金（Infra）與HUMAIN於2026年11月8日簽署兩項融資協議，總金額高達25億沙烏地里亞爾（約6.67億美元），標誌著Infra人工智慧資料中心融資計畫的首批正式承諾，並建立了電力基礎設施框架，其規劃容量為首批融資涵蓋運算負載的40倍。

  協議於翌日對外公布。Infra是沙烏地阿拉伯的國家基礎設施融資機構；HUMAIN則是該王國旗下與國家關係密切的人工智慧與雲端運算公司。

  ## 兩項設施，兩種用途

  第一項協議金額最高達11億沙烏地里亞爾，用於融資圖形處理器（GPU）——即專門用於AI訓練與推論的特殊晶片——代表約25百萬瓦（MW）的資訊技術負載。第二項規模較大的設施最高達14億里亞爾，將用於資助變電站、輸電線路及電網連接工程。該電力基礎設施的工程設計可容納高達1吉瓦（GW）的IT容量，約為GPU融資所涵蓋運算負載的40倍。

  兩項數字之間的差距是刻意為之。Infra執行長Esmail bin Mohammed Alsallom表示，該計畫旨在為初期部署以外更廣泛的專案管線做好迎接投資的準備。他說：「支持HUMAIN的運算部署，同時配套建設必要的電力基礎設施，有助於為更多資料中心專案備好迎接投資的條件。我們的職責是強化專案的投資就緒度與可融資性，以商業可行性及經濟價值作為部署資本的指導原則。」

  他補充，這兩項協議旨在「撬動遠超Infra自身承諾規模的投資」。

  ## 前期框架與HUMAIN股權結構

  11月8日的協議，係依循Infra與HUMAIN於2026年1月在世界經濟論壇達沃斯年會上宣布的一份非約束性融資框架簽署——該框架涵蓋最高45億沙烏地里亞爾、高達250MW的AI資料中心容量。11月的協議代表在更廣泛安排下的首批具約束力承諾。

  此次公告亦適逢HUMAIN股權結構生變。沙烏地阿拉伯國家石油公司沙烏地阿美（Aramco）於2026年11月收購HUMAIN 49%的股份，以26億美元現金加計額外資產完成交易。主權財富基金公共投資基金（PIF）則保留51%的多數股份。

  伴隨公告發布的研究報告確立了兩項發展目標：2030年前達到3GW的AI資料中心容量，並於2034年前超過6GW。HUMAIN亦已披露與輝達（Nvidia）、亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）及xAI的合作夥伴關係。

  ## 電力基礎設施作為關鍵制約因素

  兩項設施中規模較大的14億里亞爾，分配給電力基礎設施而非運算硬體，折射出電網接入在多大程度上左右著該王國AI資料中心擴張的步伐。

  沙烏地阿拉伯的電力輸電網路由Saudi Energy輸電部門National Grid SA負責管理，根據該機構2025年年報，網路綿延104,633電路公里，涵蓋1,315座變電站，服務13,000個城市與村莊。依據Saudi Energy 2024年財務成果，該機構已設定2030年前建設約160,000公里輸電線路與1,650座變電站的目標。

  這波擴張，部分動力來自王國的能源轉型目標。在沙烏地阿拉伯國家經濟多元化計畫「2030願景」（Vision 2030）架構下，該王國目標於2030年前達成50%電力來自可再生能源。擴建再生能源發電設施，同時也意味著AI資料中心將與之競逐同一套輸電容量。

  ## 2030願景與數位主權

  Infra與HUMAIN的協議，座落於沙烏地阿拉伯建立本土科技實力、並以AI與雲端運算區域樞紐自居的更宏觀戰略之中。數位主權——涵蓋資料在地化、本土運算基礎設施及沙烏地掌控的技術能力——被列為推動該戰略的核心動因。

  沙烏地阿拉伯《個人資料保護法》及各行業法規，針對特定類別敏感資訊設有在地化要求，從而支撐本土資料中心容量的內需。谷歌（Google）、甲骨文（Oracle）及阿里巴巴（Alibaba）等國際雲端服務供應商已在該王國建立或宣布設立雲端區域，本土業者亦持續擴建基礎設施。

  沙烏地阿拉伯資料與人工智慧管理局（SDAIA）監管「國家資料與人工智慧戰略」，目標是於2030年前躋身全球AI實力前15名。報告指出，沙烏地阿拉伯夾處美中科技生態系之間的位置，既帶來投資與合作機遇，也存在面臨出口管制及技術限制的潛在風險。

  ## 融資計畫資格條件

  Infra人工智慧資料中心融資計畫設有技術與商業條件，反映首批交易所應達到的標準。

  設施一般須位於劃定的AI資料中心園區內，且能中立接入國際海底電纜及沙烏地阿拉伯國家光纖網路。專案須符合Tier III或更高等級標準——即代表具備容錯基礎設施、無單點故障的分類認證——並以盡力而為的原則，將電力使用效率（PUE）目標設定在1.4以下。PUE的計算方式為設施總耗能除以IT設備耗能；PUE為1.4意味著40%的電力消耗用於冷卻等輔助項目的額外開銷。

  融資完成時，至少50%的容量須已簽約或處於深度洽談階段，且專案一般須代表至少10MW的最低IT負載，惟較小規模的開發案可個別評估考量。

  ## 協議確立與未確立之事項

  這兩項協議為HUMAIN初期25MW運算部署以外的擴張提供了融資框架，但並未為1GW電力容量何時配備相應運算基礎設施設定時間表。要將變電站與電網連接轉化為實際運營的資料中心，仍需進一步的GPU採購、專案交付及客戶承諾。

  對Infra而言，眼前的考驗在於HUMAIN的交易能否吸引該計畫旨在動員的民間與機構資本。HUMAIN公布的發展目標是：2030年前在沙烏地阿拉伯建成3GW的AI資料中心容量，並於2034年前突破6GW。
