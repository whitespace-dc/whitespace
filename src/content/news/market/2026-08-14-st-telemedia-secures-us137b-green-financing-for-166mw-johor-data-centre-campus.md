body_en: |
  ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC) has secured a green financing facility of up to US$1.37 billion (RM5.6 billion) for Phase 1 of its 166-megawatt STT Johor data centre campus in Nusa Cemerlang Industrial Park, Iskandar Puteri, Johor, Malaysia.

  A four-bank consortium arranged the facility. United Overseas Bank (Malaysia) Bhd served as sole coordinator and mandated lead arranger. OCBC Bank (Malaysia) Berhad, Standard Chartered Bank Malaysia Berhad, and CIMB Bank Berhad participated as mandated lead arrangers.

  ## Financing structure

  The facility is structured as a Green Loan Facility, a financing instrument in which proceeds must be applied to projects meeting defined environmental criteria. STT GDC said the structure aligns long-term capital deployment with the development of sustainable, resilient, and energy-efficient digital infrastructure. Nelson Lim, group chief financial officer of STT GDC, said the facility "reflects strong confidence from our banking partners in STT Johor and the long-term fundamentals of the digital infrastructure sector."

  Lim said disciplined, long-term capital will be critical as demand for cloud, artificial intelligence (AI), and other digital workloads grows, and described the Green Loan Facility as reinforcing STT GDC's approach to integrating sustainability into how it finances, designs, builds, and operates its data centres.

  ## Campus scale and workloads

  The STT Johor campus is planned for a total development capacity of up to 166 megawatts (MW) of IT load, meaning the maximum sustained power available to run servers and computing equipment at the site. The campus is designed to serve cloud computing, AI, high-performance computing (HPC), and next-generation digital workloads.

  Darryll Sinnappa, STT GDC's country head for Malaysia, said the campus is "being developed to support the next generation of digital infrastructure requirements, including cloud, AI and high-performance computing workloads, while contributing to Malaysia's digital economy ambitions." STT GDC cited Johor's connectivity, access to power infrastructure, growing talent base, and proximity to Singapore as factors strengthening its position as a destination for hyperscale and large-scale digital infrastructure investment.

  ## Sustainability performance

  STT GDC said the green financing aligns with measurable environmental results it has already reported. In its latest environmental, social, and governance (ESG) report, the company said renewable energy accounted for 83.2% of its electricity consumption in 2025. Carbon intensity fell 70.5% against a 2021 baseline, surpassing a target the company had originally set for 2028 three years ahead of schedule.

  The company also reported an average power usage effectiveness (PUE) of 1.44, a ratio measuring data centre energy efficiency where 1.0 represents perfect efficiency with all power going directly to computing equipment, and a 41.2% improvement in water usage effectiveness (WUE) over the same period.

  ## JS-SEZ alignment and regional positioning

  The campus is positioned to support the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), a bilateral framework between Malaysia and Singapore aimed at deepening cross-border economic cooperation and strengthening Johor's role as a high-value investment destination. STT GDC said the financing positions it to advance subsequent phases of campus development in coordination with government agencies, utilities, and ecosystem partners.

  ## Talent and community development

  STT GDC said its investment in Johor extends beyond physical infrastructure. In April 2026, the company launched talent and community development initiatives in Johor, including a local talent development programme delivered in collaboration with the Johor Talent Development Council (JTDC), Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, and EPI Group. The initiative builds on a memorandum of understanding signed between STT GDC and the JTDC in 2025, aimed at developing a pipeline of skilled professionals to support the growth of Johor's data centre sector and the wider region, in line with the objectives of the JS-SEZ.

  ## What the financing enables

  Securing the green loan facility removes a key capital precondition for advancing one of Malaysia's largest planned data centre developments. The deal also signals that major regional bank consortiums are prepared to commit financing at scale to digital infrastructure projects structured around green criteria. With Phase 1 capital in place, STT GDC said it is positioned to move forward with subsequent phases of the 166MW campus in coordination with government agencies, utilities, and ecosystem partners.
body_zh: |
  **ST Telemedia Global Data Centres（STT GDC）**已就位於馬來西亞柔佛依斯干達布城（Iskandar Puteri）努沙斯米朗工業園（Nusa Cemerlang Industrial Park）、總容量達166百萬瓦特的**STT柔佛資料中心園區**第一期開發，取得高達**13.7億美元（馬幣56億令吉）**的綠色融資額度。

  本次融資由四家銀行組成的財團聯合安排：**大華銀行（馬來西亞）有限公司**（United Overseas Bank (Malaysia) Bhd）擔任唯一協調人及授權牽頭行；**馬來西亞華僑銀行**（OCBC Bank (Malaysia) Berhad）、**渣打銀行馬來西亞**（Standard Chartered Bank Malaysia Berhad）及**聯昌銀行**（CIMB Bank Berhad）則以授權牽頭行身份共同參與。

  ## 融資架構

  本次融資採**綠色貸款額度**（Green Loan Facility）形式——此為一種要求所有融資收益須專用於符合既定環境標準項目的金融工具。STT GDC表示，此架構有助於將長期資本部署與可持續、具韌性且高能源效率的數位基礎設施開發緊密結合。STT GDC集團財務長Nelson Lim表示，本次融資「充分彰顯銀行合作夥伴對STT柔佛及數位基礎設施產業長期基本面的高度信心」。

  Nelson Lim指出，隨著雲端、人工智慧（AI）及其他數位工作負載的需求持續攀升，紀律嚴謹、著眼長遠的資本配置將越來越關鍵，並表示本次綠色貸款額度進一步強化了STT GDC在融資、設計、建設與營運各環節中整合永續發展的一貫做法。

  ## 園區規模與應用場景

  STT柔佛園區規劃的IT負載總容量達**166MW**，即該場址可長期持續供應給伺服器及運算設備的最大電力上限。園區設計旨在承接雲端運算、AI、高效能運算（HPC）及次世代數位工作負載。

  STT GDC馬來西亞區負責人Darryll Sinnappa表示，該園區「正積極開發，以支援下一代數位基礎設施的多元需求，涵蓋雲端、AI與高效能運算等工作負載，同時為馬來西亞數位經濟的宏觀願景貢獻力量」。STT GDC並指出，柔佛的網路連接優勢、完善的電力基礎設施、持續壯大的在地人才庫，以及鄰近新加坡的地理位置，均使其躋身超大規模與大型數位基礎設施投資的重要目的地。

  ## 永續績效

  STT GDC表示，本次綠色融資與公司已公開的可衡量環境成果相互呼應。在最新的環境、社會及治理（ESG）報告中，公司指出2025年再生能源占其電力消耗的**83.2%**；碳排放強度較2021年基準年大幅下降**70.5%**，且提前三年完成了原訂於2028年達成的目標。

  公司亦披露平均電源使用效率（PUE）為**1.44**——此指標衡量資料中心能源效率，數值1.0代表所有電力完全用於運算設備的理想狀態——同期用水效率（WUE）較相同基準期間改善**41.2%**。

  ## 配合柔新特區與區域定位

  本園區定位與**柔佛—新加坡特別經濟區**（JS-SEZ，簡稱柔新特區）的發展方向高度契合。柔新特區是馬來西亞與新加坡共同建立的雙邊框架，旨在深化跨境經濟合作、強化柔佛作為高價值投資目的地的地位。STT GDC表示，融資到位後，公司將具備條件與政府機關、公用事業單位及生態系合作夥伴協同推進後續各期園區的開發。

  ## 人才與社區發展

  STT GDC強調，其在柔佛的投入遠不止於實體基礎設施的興建。2026年4月，公司在柔佛啟動人才與社區發展計畫，其中包括與**柔佛人才發展理事會**（Johor Talent Development Council，JTDC）、**馬來西亞理工大學**（Universiti Teknologi Malaysia）及**EPI集團**合作推行的在地人才培育計畫。此舉奠基於STT GDC與JTDC於2025年簽署的合作備忘錄，旨在建立一支具備專業技能的人才梯隊，以支撐柔佛資料中心產業及鄰近區域的持續發展，並與柔新特區的核心目標一脈相承。

  ## 融資的戰略意義

  本次綠色貸款額度的到位，掃除了馬來西亞目前規劃規模最大的資料中心開發計畫之一在推進過程中的關鍵資本障礙。這筆交易同時釋出一個重要訊號：區域主要銀行財團已準備好為採取綠色標準架構的大型數位基礎設施項目提供大規模融資承諾。第一期資本就緒後，STT GDC表示，公司已具備充分條件，與政府機關、公用事業單位及生態系合作夥伴協同推進這座166MW園區的後續各期開發。
