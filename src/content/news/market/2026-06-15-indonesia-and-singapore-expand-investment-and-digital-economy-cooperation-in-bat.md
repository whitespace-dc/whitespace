  ## Indonesia and Singapore Deepen Bilateral Economic Ties Across Investment, Digital, and Green Sectors

  Indonesia and Singapore have expanded their bilateral economic cooperation across investment, digital infrastructure, and green energy, with the Batam-Bintan-Karimun (BBK) special economic zone recording $5.7 billion in investment in 2025, up from the prior year, Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto said on June 9, 2026.

  Airlangga made the announcement following the 16th Ministerial Meeting of the Six Working Group Bilateral Economic Cooperation between Indonesia and Singapore. The BBK zone — comprising the islands of Batam, Bintan, and Karimun off the southern tip of Singapore — serves as the primary geographic anchor for the bilateral economic relationship.

  ## Free Trade Zone Expansion and Digital Park Development

  The Batam free trade zone (FTZ) — a designated area where goods can be imported, stored, and exported with reduced customs duties and regulations — is being expanded from eight to 22 islands, according to Airlangga. The Nongsa Digital Park, a technology-focused development within Batam, will continue to be developed to support digital economy expansion, he said.

  Several data center investments are underway in the zone. DayOne and PT Equator Gate System are investing in data center facilities in Batam, while DCI Indonesia is developing a facility in Bintan. The two governments also agreed to conduct a joint study on the technology sector in the BBK area to identify new opportunities and formulate concrete recommendations for regional growth.

  ## Kendal Industrial Park Marks Decade, Adds 1,000 Hectares

  The Kendal Industrial Park (KIP) in Central Java — a flagship Singapore-Indonesia industrial cooperation project — will mark its 10th anniversary in November 2026. The park is currently undergoing a second-phase expansion adding 1,000 hectares to support investment, job creation, and economic growth in Central Java, Airlangga said.

  ## Green Energy Projects Advance

  In the green economy sector, a utility-scale solar project by Sembcorp and PT Sumber Energi Surya Nusantara (SESNA) is under construction and targeted for completion in 2027. The project will have an installed solar capacity of 200 megawatts (MW) — roughly enough to power several hundred thousand Indonesian households — and an 80 megawatt-hour (MWh) battery energy storage system, which Airlangga described as one of the largest utility-scale solar projects in Indonesia.

  A separate biofuel project between CRecTech and Pertamina, Indonesia's state energy company, is developing what the two governments described as Indonesia's first biogas-to-bio-methanol pilot project in North Sumatra.

  ## Agriculture Technology Cooperation Launches

  A Young Farmer Development Program is set to launch for the first time in June 2026 as part of bilateral cooperation in agricultural technology.

  ## Significance

  The breadth of agreements — spanning free trade zone expansion, data center investment, large-scale renewables, biofuels, and agricultural technology — reflects the structured, multi-sector nature of the Indonesia-Singapore bilateral framework, which operates through six formal working groups at the ministerial level. The joint technology study for the BBK zone signals that both governments are actively seeking to identify additional investment opportunities beyond the projects already underway. The $5.7 billion in BBK investment recorded for 2025 and the ongoing expansion of Kendal Industrial Park represent the most concrete near-term indicators of the partnership's scale.

  The Sembcorp-SESNA solar project, targeted for completion in 2027, is the most time-bound forward commitment among the initiatives announced.

  ## 印尼與新加坡全面深化投資、數位及綠色能源雙邊經貿關係

  印尼與新加坡在投資、數位基礎建設及綠色能源領域擴大雙邊經濟合作，峇淡－民丹－卡里汶（BBK）經濟特區2025年錄得57億美元投資，較前一年有所成長。印尼統籌經濟事務部長艾爾朗加·哈爾塔托（Airlangga Hartarto）於2026年6月9日宣布上述成果。

  艾爾朗加是在印尼與新加坡六大工作小組雙邊經濟合作第16屆部長級會議後發表上述聲明。BBK特區涵蓋新加坡南端外海的峇淡島、民丹島及卡里汶島，是兩國雙邊經貿關係最主要的地理核心。

  ## 自由貿易區擴張與數位園區發展

  艾爾朗加表示，峇淡自由貿易區——一個讓貨物得以在關稅減免與法規鬆綁條件下進口、存放及出口的特定區域——將從原本涵蓋8座島嶼擴大至22座。園區內以科技為核心的農沙數位園區（Nongsa Digital Park）也將持續開發，以支撐數位經濟擴張。

  目前已有數筆資料中心投資在此落地。DayOne與PT Equator Gate System正在峇淡島投資興建資料中心設施，DCI Indonesia則在民丹島開發相關建設。兩國政府亦同意就BBK地區的科技產業展開聯合研究，以發掘新商機、提出具體的區域成長建議。

  ## 根達爾工業園區邁入十周年，擴增千公頃

  位於中爪哇的根達爾工業園區（Kendal Industrial Park，KIP）是新加坡與印尼合作的旗艦工業計畫，將於2026年11月迎來十周年紀念。艾爾朗加指出，園區目前正進行第二期擴建，新增1,000公頃土地，以支持中爪哇的投資、就業及經濟成長。

  ## 綠色能源計畫持續推進

  在綠色經濟領域，勝科工業（Sembcorp）與PT Sumber Energi Surya Nusantara（SESNA）合作的公用規模太陽能專案正在施工中，預計2027年竣工。該計畫裝置太陽能容量達200百萬瓦（MW）——大致相當於為印尼數十萬戶家庭供電——並配備80百萬瓦時（MWh）的電池儲能系統。艾爾朗加將此計畫形容為印尼規模最大的公用級太陽能專案之一。

  另有一項由CRecTech與印尼國家石油公司（Pertamina）合作推動的生質燃料計畫，兩國政府將其定位為印尼首座生質沼氣轉生質甲醇示範計畫，地點位於北蘇門答臘。

  ## 農業科技合作正式啟動

  「青年農民培育計畫」預計於2026年6月首度啟動，作為兩國農業科技雙邊合作的一環。

  ## 意義

  此次協議涵蓋自由貿易區擴張、資料中心投資、大規模再生能源、生質燃料及農業科技等多個面向，充分體現印尼－新加坡雙邊框架以六大部長級正式工作小組運作的結構性與多元性。BBK區的科技產業聯合研究，顯示兩國政府正積極在現有計畫之外，進一步發掘新的投資機會。BBK特區2025年錄得的57億美元投資，以及根達爾工業園區的持續擴建，是衡量這項夥伴關係規模最具體的近期指標。

  勝科與SESNA合作的太陽能計畫以2027年為完工目標，是此次發布的各項措施中，時程最為明確的前瞻承諾。
