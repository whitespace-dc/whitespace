body_en: |
  Data Vault, a New Zealand-owned colocation operator, has confirmed 11 connectivity and service partners for a planned 3-megawatt (MW) data centre on Auckland's North Shore, with the facility targeted to open in the second half of 2027.

  The announcement forms part of a wider expansion programme the company said would add approximately 6MW of capacity across Auckland, Hamilton and Wellington over the coming years. Data Vault said the building for the North Shore site has already been acquired, with fitout scheduled to begin in 2027 and capacity to be brought online in stages as customer demand grows.

  ## Expansion pipeline

  Data Vault has identified 5.3MW of additional capacity across four projects. The North Shore facility represents the largest single addition at 3MW. The company also plans a further 1MW at its existing Grafton facility in central Auckland, another 1MW at its Te Rapa site in Hamilton, and approximately 300kW in Wellington. Additional projects are under consideration to reach the broader 6MW target.

  At the Grafton facility, Data Hall 3 is fully contracted, according to the company. Data Hall 4, backed by an international anchor tenant, is scheduled to become operational in the first quarter of 2027, with Data Hall 5 following in the third quarter.

  ## Launch partners

  The 11 confirmed partners for the North Shore facility are Eon Fibre, Devoli, EdgeIX, Verge Infrastructure, WaveSquared, Hostpearl, Vetta Group, Mercury, Purelink, Vital and Megaport. Data Vault said it expects to announce additional partners before the facility opens. The partners will provide access to fibre networks, internet services, internet exchanges, cloud connectivity and other network solutions.

  The facility will operate on a carrier-neutral model, meaning customers can choose among competing providers rather than being tied to a single network operator.

  "We've already confirmed 11 launch partners, with more to come. Customers will have access to a wide range of services and providers from day one," said Peter White, general manager at Data Vault.

  Data Vault said its existing Grafton facility is approaching 70 autonomous system numbers (ASNs) — a count reflecting the number of distinct networks interconnected at the site — and offers access to internet exchanges including EdgeIX, NZIX and MegaIX. The company's website reports more than 50 network providers at its existing Auckland facility.

  ## Efficiency and design

  Data Vault said energy efficiency would be a priority across the expansion programme, with modern cooling systems, aisle containment and efficient power infrastructure intended to increase the proportion of available power that customer IT equipment can use. The company already operates rooftop solar installations at its Grafton and Hamilton facilities and plans to add solar at the North Shore site.

  Its existing operations carry a power usage effectiveness (PUE) rating of 1.14, according to the company. PUE measures total facility energy consumption against the energy consumed by IT equipment; a score of 1.0 would represent perfect efficiency, with lower figures indicating less energy lost to cooling and other overhead. Data Vault has not provided a target PUE for the North Shore facility.

  ## Company background and positioning

  Data Vault has operated in New Zealand since 2004, with facilities in Auckland and Hamilton. It expanded into Wellington in 2025 through an operations and management agreement with service provider Vital, extending its footprint across the country's three major data centre markets. That arrangement included planned modernisation to align the Wellington facility with Data Vault's existing standards.

  White said demand is coming from network operators, cloud providers and international technology companies seeking colocation in New Zealand. The company said it is targeting mid-density colocation — serving enterprises, managed service providers and cloud operators with flexible rack deployments — rather than the large-scale hyperscale or artificial intelligence infrastructure that has driven much of the recent global data centre investment.

  "There's a significant market for reliable, well-connected mid-density rack space, and that's where we see a real opportunity," White said.

  ## SOC 2 readiness

  Data Vault said it is nearing completion of a SOC 2 readiness programme. SOC 2 — Service Organization Control 2 — is an independent audit framework that assesses a service organisation's controls across security, availability and related criteria. The programme involves enhanced physical security, monitoring, operational procedures and governance controls, which the company plans to roll out across its facilities alongside the North Shore development, with the goal of progressing toward independent SOC 2 attestation.

  ## What it means

  The North Shore development represents Data Vault's first new-build location under its current expansion plan. Confirming a partner roster before the facility opens, rather than after, is a deliberate positioning move aimed at addressing the connectivity gap that can slow customer adoption at newly opened sites. For enterprises and network operators in New Zealand, a carrier-neutral colocation site in the North Shore adds a third Auckland address and greater geographic diversity to the country's existing mid-market colocation options.

  Data Vault said it expects to announce additional connectivity partners for the North Shore facility before it opens in the second half of 2027.
body_zh: |
  Data Vault已宣布，計畫在奧克蘭北岸興建一座3百萬瓦（MW）的主機代管資料中心，並確認了11家連線與服務合作夥伴，目標於2027年下半年正式開幕。

  此次公告是該公司更大規模擴張計畫的一部分。Data Vault表示，這項計畫將在未來數年內，於奧克蘭、漢密頓與威靈頓三座城市合計新增約6MW的機房容量。Data Vault指出，北岸廠址的建築已完成收購，裝修工程預計於2027年啟動，並將視客戶需求分階段投入服務。

  ## 擴張管線

  Data Vault已規劃四項專案，合計可新增5.3MW的額外容量。其中北岸廠址是單一規模最大的新增項目，達3MW。此外，公司計畫在奧克蘭市中心現有的格拉夫頓（Grafton）廠址再增加1MW，漢密頓的特拉帕（Te Rapa）廠址同樣新增1MW，威靈頓則約增300千瓦（kW）。另有其他專案正在評估中，以達成整體6MW的擴張目標。

  在格拉夫頓廠址方面，Data Vault表示第三機房（Data Hall 3）已全數簽約。以一家國際主力租戶為後盾的第四機房（Data Hall 4），預計於2027年第一季正式投入運營；第五機房（Data Hall 5）則預定於同年第三季跟進。

  ## 開幕合作夥伴

  北岸廠址已確認的11家合作夥伴分別為：Eon Fibre、Devoli、EdgeIX、Verge Infrastructure、WaveSquared、Hostpearl、Vetta Group、Mercury、Purelink、Vital與Megaport。Data Vault表示，預計在廠址正式開幕前還會陸續宣布更多合作夥伴。這些夥伴將提供光纖網路、網際網路服務、網際網路交換、雲端連線及其他網路解決方案的接取服務。

  該廠址將採取電信業者中立（carrier-neutral）的運營模式，讓客戶可自由選擇相互競爭的服務供應商，而無需綁定於單一網路業者。

  Data Vault總經理彼得．懷特（Peter White）表示：「我們已確認11家開幕合作夥伴，未來還會有更多加入。客戶從第一天起就能取用多元的服務與供應商。」

  Data Vault指出，現有格拉夫頓廠址的自治系統編號（ASN）數量已接近70個——此數字反映在該廠址互連的不同網路數量——並提供EdgeIX、NZIX及MegaIX等網際網路交換的接取服務。公司官網顯示，現有奧克蘭廠址已有逾50家網路供應商進駐。

  ## 能源效率與設計

  Data Vault表示，整個擴張計畫將以能源效率為優先考量，透過現代化冷卻系統、走道圍封（aisle containment）及高效電力基礎設施，提升客戶IT設備可實際使用的電力比例。該公司目前已在格拉夫頓與漢密頓廠址設有屋頂太陽能裝置，並計畫在北岸廠址同樣加裝太陽能設備。

  根據Data Vault提供的數據，其現有設施的電力使用效率（PUE）為1.14。PUE是衡量資料中心總耗電量相對於IT設備耗電量的指標；1.0代表完美效率，數值越低代表用於冷卻及其他開銷的能源損耗越少。Data Vault目前尚未公布北岸廠址的PUE目標值。

  ## 公司背景與市場定位

  Data Vault自2004年起在紐西蘭開始營運，設施分布於奧克蘭與漢密頓。2025年，該公司透過與服務業者Vital簽訂的營運管理協議進軍威靈頓，將業務版圖延伸至紐西蘭三大主要資料中心市場。該協議亦涵蓋對威靈頓廠址的現代化改造計畫，以使其達到Data Vault現有設施的水準。

  懷特表示，需求來源涵蓋網路業者、雲端業者，以及在紐西蘭尋求主機代管服務的國際科技公司。Data Vault指出，公司的目標市場是中密度主機代管——服務採用彈性機架部署的企業、託管服務業者與雲端業者——而非近年驅動全球資料中心投資熱潮的大規模超大規模（hyperscale）或人工智慧基礎設施。

  懷特說：「中密度機架空間有著相當可觀的市場需求，可提供可靠的設施與完善的連線，我們看到真正的機會就在那裡。」

  ## SOC 2 準備工作

  Data Vault表示，目前已接近完成SOC 2整備計畫。SOC 2（服務組織控制2，Service Organization Control 2）是一套獨立稽核框架，針對服務組織在資安、可用性及相關標準上的控制機制進行評估。此計畫涵蓋強化實體安全、監控、作業程序及治理控制，公司計畫在推進北岸開發案的同時，於旗下所有設施全面導入，最終目標是取得獨立的SOC 2認證。

  ## 意義解析

  北岸廠址是Data Vault在現行擴張計畫下首個全新建置的據點。在廠址開幕前（而非開幕後）就對外確認合作夥伴名單，是一種刻意為之的市場定位策略，旨在解決新廠址開幕初期常見的連線空窗問題，以免拖慢客戶的進駐腳步。對紐西蘭的企業與網路業者而言，北岸新增一處電信業者中立的主機代管廠址，代表奧克蘭多了第三個可選地點，也為紐西蘭現有中端市場主機代管選項帶來更高的地理多樣性。

  Data Vault表示，預計在北岸廠址於2027年下半年開幕前，將陸續宣布更多連線合作夥伴。
