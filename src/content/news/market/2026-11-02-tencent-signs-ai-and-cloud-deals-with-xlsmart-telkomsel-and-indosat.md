  ## Tencent Signs AI and Cloud Deals with XLSmart, Telkomsel, and Indosat

  Tencent announced partnerships with three Indonesian telecommunications operators — XLSmart, Telkomsel, and Indosat — spanning cloud migration, Super App platform services, and artificial intelligence integration, the Chinese technology company disclosed at its Global Digital Ecosystem Summit (GDES) in Shenzhen, Guangdong province, on October 29.

  Dowson Tong, Senior Executive Vice President of Tencent and CEO of its Cloud and Smart Industries Group (CSIG), said at the summit that the company has signed agreements and is actively driving "comprehensive collaboration" with the three operators through Tencent Cloud and its AI products.

  ## XLSmart core system migration

  The most operationally detailed disclosure concerned XLSmart, the merged entity of XL Axiata and Smartfren. Tencent said it helped XLSmart migrate its core systems to Tencent Cloud in five months, a process that involved more than 1,000 microservices and terabytes of data, orchestrated by multiple AI agents across discovery and deployment stages. Tencent said it also developed more than 200 functions customized to XLSmart's local requirements.

  Yessie D. Yosetya, Director and Chief Information Technology Officer of XLSmart, said the engagement began at the end of last year and originated from the need to evaluate and consolidate the technology infrastructure of two organizations — XL Axiata and Smartfren — that had operated differently before their merger. One entity had relied more heavily on cloud infrastructure while the other was predominantly on-premise.

  "So, we decided to move everything to the cloud," Yosetya told Bisnis.com on the sidelines of GDES 2026. The modernization also includes evaluating consolidation of AI and data platforms to serve an organization that had previously run on separate systems. XLSmart said the goal is improved system reliability and a smoother experience for both end customers and internal employees using the unified platform.

  ## Telkomsel Super App collaboration

  Tencent said it has worked with Telkomsel, the mobile unit of state-owned Telkom Indonesia, to build an ecosystem based on Super App as a Service — a model in which a single application hosts multiple services or mini-programs. The collaboration also covers the development of what Tencent described as Telkomsel Double Mini Program monthly active users (MAUs), though no figures for current or target MAU levels were provided.

  ## Sector focus and forward priorities

  Jimmy Chen, Vice President of Tencent Cloud and Head of APAC Business, said the company has been active across telecoms, financial services, digital platforms, and retail in Indonesia. He told Bisnis.com the company intends to deepen engagement in those sectors, particularly in customer service, identity verification and customer onboarding processes, and internal workflow automation.

  Chen said future priorities will be shaped by real demand from enterprises, strong local partnerships, and conditions that support AI adoption. "Tencent is committed to making AI an increasingly integrated part of local companies' operations, not just a technology used in isolation," he said.

  ## Significance

  The announcements position Tencent Cloud as a primary infrastructure partner for Indonesia's three largest mobile operators at a moment when at least one of them — XLSmart — is consolidating systems following a major merger. The deals span both infrastructure (core system migration) and application-layer services (Super App platforms), indicating a broad rather than point-solution engagement model in the market.

  Tencent said its forward priorities in Indonesia will be determined by demonstrated enterprise demand, local partnerships, and conditions conducive to AI adoption.

  ## 騰訊與XLSmart、Telkomsel及Indosat簽署AI與雲端合作協議

  騰訊宣布與印尼三家電信業者——XLSmart、Telkomsel及Indosat——達成合作協議，內容涵蓋雲端遷移、超級應用程式（Super App）平台服務及人工智慧整合。這家中國科技巨頭於10月29日在廣東省深圳市舉辦的全球數位生態峰會（Global Digital Ecosystem Summit，GDES）上正式揭露上述消息。

  騰訊高級執行副總裁暨雲與智慧產業事業群（CSIG）執行長湯道生（Dowson Tong）在峰會上表示，公司已與三家電信業者簽署協議，並正積極透過騰訊雲及旗下AI產品推動「全面合作」。

  ## XLSmart核心系統遷移

  此次揭露中，細節最為豐富的部分集中在XLSmart。XLSmart是XL Axiata與Smartfren合併後的新實體。騰訊表示，協助XLSmart在五個月內完成核心系統向騰訊雲的遷移，過程中涉及逾1,000項微服務及數TB的資料，並由多個AI代理（AI agent）負責統籌協調，橫跨探索與部署兩大階段。騰訊另表示，針對XLSmart的在地化需求，額外開發了逾200項客製化功能。

  XLSmart技術長暨董事Yessie D. Yosetya接受《Bisnis.com》訪問時表示，此次合作從去年底展開，起因在於XL Axiata與Smartfren合併之前各自採用截然不同的技術架構，合併後必須重新評估並整合。兩家公司中，一方原本較仰賴雲端基礎架構，另一方則以地端（on-premise）部署為主。

  「因此，我們決定將所有系統全面遷移至雲端，」Yosetya在GDES 2026峰會場邊接受《Bisnis.com》訪問時說道。這次現代化升級還包括評估整合AI與資料平台，以服務一個此前各自在獨立系統上運行的組織。XLSmart表示，目標是提升系統穩定性，並為終端客戶及使用統一平台的內部員工帶來更順暢的體驗。

  ## Telkomsel超級應用程式合作

  騰訊表示，已與印尼國營電信公司Telkom Indonesia旗下行動業務品牌Telkomsel合作，共同建立以「超級應用程式即服務」（Super App as a Service）為基礎的生態系——此模式係指單一應用程式內整合多項服務或小程式。雙方合作亦涵蓋騰訊所稱的「Telkomsel雙倍小程式月活躍用戶數（MAU）」計畫之推進，惟騰訊並未提供目前或目標月活躍用戶數的具體數字。

  ## 產業布局與未來重點

  騰訊雲副總裁暨亞太區業務負責人Jimmy Chen表示，公司已在印尼的電信、金融服務、數位平台及零售等領域積極布局。他向《Bisnis.com》表示，公司計畫深化在上述產業的投入，尤其聚焦於客戶服務、身分驗證與客戶引導流程，以及內部工作流程自動化等面向。

  Chen表示，未來工作重點將取決於企業的實際需求、在地合作夥伴關係，以及有助於AI導入的整體環境條件。「騰訊致力於讓AI成為在地企業營運中日益不可或缺的一環，而非一項孤立運用的技術，」他說。

  ## 意義

  上述宣布使騰訊雲確立了其作為印尼三大行動電信業者主要基礎架構合作夥伴的地位，時機恰逢其中至少一家業者——XLSmart——正因大型合併案而積極整合系統。本次合作橫跨基礎架構（核心系統遷移）與應用層服務（超級應用程式平台）兩個層次，顯示騰訊在該市場採取的是全面性合作模式，而非針對單點問題提供解決方案。

  騰訊表示，未來在印尼的工作重點，將由企業的實際需求、在地夥伴關係，以及有利於AI導入的條件共同決定。
