body_en: |
  DigitalBridge Group is exploring options for its controlling 70 percent stake in Malaysia's AIMS Data Centre Holding Sdn Bhd, including a potential sale, new investor participation, or a fresh capital raise, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be named because discussions with a financial advisor are ongoing and private.

  A transaction could value AIMS at approximately RM 8.23 billion, equivalent to about US$2 billion, Bloomberg reported. A financial advisor has been engaged to gauge investor interest, though the people familiar with the matter said a sale may not take place. Neither AIMS nor DigitalBridge had commented as of publication.

  ## How DigitalBridge built its stake

  DigitalBridge, a US-based digital infrastructure investment firm, entered AIMS in November 2022 by acquiring a 49 percent stake from TIME dotCom Berhad for RM 2 billion, when the overall AIMS business was valued at RM 3.2 billion. In 2024, DigitalBridge converted preference shares to acquire an additional 21 percent interest for RM 240 million, lifting its total ownership to 70 percent.

  At the RM 8.23 billion valuation cited by Bloomberg, that figure would represent more than a doubling of the RM 3.2 billion enterprise value stated at the time of the initial 2022 transaction.

  ## SoftBank acquisition pending

  The AIMS stake review is unfolding while DigitalBridge's own ownership is in transition. On December 29, 2025, DigitalBridge announced that it had agreed to be acquired by Japan's SoftBank Group for US$4 billion. SoftBank said the deal would advance its efforts to "realize Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI) for the advancement of humanity." That acquisition remains pending.

  ## AIMS's Cyberjaya expansion

  AIMS is a Tier III-certified, carrier-neutral data center operator — meaning its facilities are designed to be maintained without taking systems offline, and tenants can connect through multiple telecommunications providers. Earlier in 2026, AIMS acquired 10 acres of land in Cyberjaya, where it plans to develop a 200-megawatt (MW) artificial intelligence data center at a planned cost of RM 4 billion. The project is targeted for completion in 2027, though the source reporting on the stake review noted that timeline may change given recent developments.

  DigitalBridge 集團正就其持有的馬來西亞 AIMS 資料中心控股私人有限公司（AIMS Data Centre Holding Sdn Bhd）70% 控股股權評估多項選項，包括出售股權、引進新投資人或進行新一輪募資。據知情人士透露，由於目前與財務顧問的洽談仍在進行中且屬保密性質，相關人士要求匿名。

  彭博（Bloomberg）報導，此次潛在交易將使 AIMS 的估值達到約 82.3 億馬幣（約合 20 億美元）。目前已委任財務顧問探詢投資人意願，惟知情人士表示，出售案未必會付諸實現。截至本文發稿時，AIMS 與 DigitalBridge 均未就此事作出回應。

  ## DigitalBridge 如何建立持股

  DigitalBridge 是一家總部設於美國的數位基礎設施投資公司，於 2022 年 11 月首度進入 AIMS，以 20 億馬幣向 TIME dotCom Berhad 收購 49% 股權，當時 AIMS 整體企業估值為 32 億馬幣。2024 年，DigitalBridge 將優先股轉換為普通股，以 2.4 億馬幣再取得 21% 股權，使總持股比例提升至 70%。

  以彭博引述的 82.3 億馬幣估值計算，該數字較 2022 年初始交易時所揭露的 32 億馬幣企業價值已翻逾一倍。

  ## 軟銀收購案懸而未決

  AIMS 股權審查正值 DigitalBridge 自身股權結構處於過渡期之際。2025 年 12 月 29 日，DigitalBridge 宣布已同意以 40 億美元被日本軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）收購。軟銀表示，此交易將推進其「為全人類實現人工超級智慧（ASI）」的目標。該收購案目前仍在等待最終完成。

  ## AIMS 的賽城擴建計畫

  AIMS 是一家獲得 Tier III 認證的電信中立資料中心業者——意即其設施在設計上可在不中斷系統運作的情況下進行維護，且租戶可透過多家電信業者進行連線。2026 年稍早，AIMS 在賽城（Cyberjaya）購入 10 英畝土地，計畫斥資 40 億馬幣興建一座 200 百萬瓦（MW）的人工智慧資料中心。該專案預計於 2027 年竣工，惟報導此次股權審查的知情人士指出，鑑於近期情勢發展，完工時程或有所調整。
