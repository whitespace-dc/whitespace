body_en: |
  NTT Data Group, a unit of Japanese telecommunications conglomerate NTT, opened a 12-megawatt data center annex in Jakarta on Tuesday and said it is pursuing additional land acquisitions in Indonesia as part of a two-to-three-year expansion plan, declining to disclose the size of the planned investment.

  The new facility, designated Jakarta 2 Annex Data Center (JKT2A), is located within NTT Global Data Centers' existing Jakarta campus. Yasuhiro Kajiki, managing director for Japan and Asia Pacific at NTT Global Data Centers, told Reuters the company is being "very aggressive" in its Indonesia investment.

  ## $500 million committed since 2015

  NTT Data has invested $500 million in Indonesian data centers since 2015, the company said at a press conference in Jakarta. The opening of JKT2A is part of a broader capacity and connectivity expansion the company described as designed to support customers' growing infrastructure needs.

  Indonesia's data center market was valued at $2.81 billion in 2025 and is forecast to reach $6.08 billion by 2031, according to a Research and Markets report published in January. Research and Markets is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland.

  ## Photonic interconnect service launched in Indonesia

  Alongside the facility opening, NTT Data introduced APN Connect, a connectivity service the company says is the first of its kind in Indonesia. The service is built on All-Photonics Network (APN) technology, developed under NTT's Innovative Optical and Wireless Network (IOWN) framework.

  APN Connect provides Layer 1 connectivity (direct physical-layer data transmission) with high capacity and low latency, linking the JKT2 and JKT2A campus with the JKT3 facility approximately 35 kilometers away. The connection enables real-time data synchronization and replication between the two sites and is designed to support disaster recovery, allowing operations to transfer to a backup facility when a primary system is disrupted.

  Katsuhiko Kawazoe, president and chairperson of the IOWN Global Forum and chief executive fellow at NTT, Inc., said the technology can sustain system operations across data centers more than 70 kilometers apart with less than one second of downtime during a failover. NTT has previously deployed APN-based commercial inter-data center interconnection in Mumbai, India, serving financial technology platforms, Kawazoe said.

  Kawazoe also said IOWN supports a collective cybersecurity model called AI Constellation, in which multiple artificial intelligence systems operating over the photonic network can collaborate and share threat intelligence in real time. When an attack is detected at one location, those systems can develop countermeasures and distribute them across the network.

  ## JKT2A facility specifications

  JKT2A holds a Tier III design certification, an industry standard requiring fault-tolerant infrastructure with multiple independent power and cooling distribution paths. Key infrastructure elements include:

  - Dual power substations
  - 2N uninterruptible power supply (UPS) configuration (fully redundant parallel power backup)
  - N+1 backup generators
  - N+1 air-cooled chiller cooling systems
  - N+2 computer room air handlers (CRAH)
  - 24/7 multi-layer physical security

  The facility offers carrier-neutral access (no single network provider controls the connectivity) to more than 240 network service providers, the Jakarta Internet Exchange (JKT-IX), major cloud providers, and strategic submarine cable networks already present at the JKT2 campus. Customers expanding into JKT2A can use those established network connections and partnerships without building new ones.

  ## Community opposition

  The expansion comes as rapid data center growth, driven partly by demand for artificial intelligence and cloud computing, has triggered opposition in some countries, including Malaysia and Indonesia, over pressure on electricity grids and water supplies and competition for land, Reuters reported.

  Kajiki said engagement with local communities is important, noting that some projects have already faced opposition from residents.

  NTT Data Group ranks among the top 10 global IT service providers and is Japan's largest information systems integrator, according to its website. The company said it is actively seeking additional land acquisitions to support its expansion plan but did not specify target locations or capacity goals.

  ## 自2015年起已累計投入5億美元

  NTT Data在雅加達舉行的記者會上表示，自2015年至今已在印尼資料中心市場累計投入5億美元。JKT2A的正式啟用，是公司整體擴充容量與強化連網計畫的一環，旨在支援客戶日益成長的基礎設施需求。

  根據總部位於愛爾蘭都柏林的市調機構Research and Markets今年1月發布的報告，印尼資料中心市場規模在2025年估計達28.1億美元，預計至2031年將進一步成長至60.8億美元。

  ## 印尼首推全光子網路互連服務

  配合新廠開幕，NTT Data同步在印尼推出APN Connect連網服務，宣稱為全印尼首創。該服務以全光子網路（All-Photonics Network，APN）技術為基礎，源自NTT主導的「創新光無線網路」（Innovative Optical and Wireless Network，IOWN）架構。

  APN Connect提供第一層（Layer 1）實體層直接資料傳輸連線，具備高容量與低延遲特性，將JKT2、JKT2A園區與相距約35公里的JKT3設施串連起來。這條連線可實現兩地之間的即時資料同步與複製，並設計用於支援災難復原機制——一旦主系統發生中斷，業務可迅速切換至備援設施。

  NTT股份有限公司首席執行研究員暨IOWN全球論壇主席川添克彦（Katsuhiko Kawazoe）表示，這套技術能在相距超過70公里的資料中心之間維持系統運作，且在故障轉移期間的停機時間不到一秒。他指出，NTT此前已在印度孟買部署以APN技術為基礎的商業化資料中心間互連服務，主要服務對象為金融科技平台。

  川添克彦另表示，IOWN還支援一種名為「AI Constellation」的集體式網路安全模型，讓多套人工智慧系統透過光子網路協同運作，得以即時共享威脅情資。當某一節點偵測到攻擊時，這些AI系統便能協力研擬反制措施，並即時分發至整個網路。

  ## JKT2A設施規格

  JKT2A取得Tier III設計認證，這是業界標準規範，要求具備容錯基礎設施，並擁有多條獨立的電力與冷卻配送路徑。主要基礎設施包括：

  - 雙電力變電站
  - 2N不斷電電源供應（UPS）配置（全冗餘並聯備援）
  - N+1備援發電機
  - N+1氣冷式冰水機組冷卻系統
  - N+2電腦機房空氣處理機（CRAH）
  - 全天候24小時多層式實體安全防護

  該設施提供電信業者中立接入，可連接逾240家網路服務提供商、雅加達網際網路交換中心（Jakarta Internet Exchange，JKT-IX）、各大雲端服務供應商，以及JKT2園區內既有的重要海底電纜網路。客戶在擴展至JKT2A後，可直接沿用既有的網路連線與合作夥伴關係，無需另行建置新的連網基礎設施。

  ## 社區居民反對聲浪

  此波擴張熱潮恰逢全球資料中心快速增建，背後部分動能來自人工智慧與雲端運算的強勁需求。路透社此前報導指出，這股趨勢在馬來西亞、印尼等地已引發部分民眾強烈反對，擔憂電網與供水系統承壓，以及土地競用等問題。

  梶木表示，與在地社區的溝通協調至關重要，並坦言部分開發案已遭當地居民反對。
