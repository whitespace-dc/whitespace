  ## INET shares post 41% weekly gain, 50% monthly advance

  Shares of PT Sinergi Inti Andalan Prima Tbk (ticker: INET), listed on the Indonesian stock exchange, rose 41.03% over the most recent week of trading and have accumulated a 50% gain over the past month. On Friday, August 21, the stock closed up 5.1% at Rp 330 per share.

  The move has renewed a question that previously surfaced earlier in 2026: whether the price action reflects a genuine shift in the company's business fundamentals or is a repeat of speculative enthusiasm.

  ## Earlier surge was driven by shareholder count, not price

  A comparable wave of investor interest hit INET at the start of 2026, though it expressed itself differently. Rather than a sharp rise in the share price, the number of shareholders more than doubled in a single month, jumping from 84,142 at the end of December 2025 to 173,902 by the end of January 2026, according to Kontan.

  That episode raised similar questions about whether retail enthusiasm was outpacing any underlying change at the company.

  ## Fundamentals question remains open

  The current rally has revived the debate over whether INET's move is grounded in material business developments or driven by the same kind of speculative momentum that characterized the January shareholder surge. The sources available do not provide financial results, revenue figures, contract announcements, or other disclosures that would allow a conclusion on that question to be drawn.

  The pattern — a sharp price run-up following a period of unusually rapid retail accumulation — is the central fact investors and analysts are weighing as INET trades at its elevated level.
