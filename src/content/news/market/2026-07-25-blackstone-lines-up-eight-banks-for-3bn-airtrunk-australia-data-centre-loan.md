body_en: |
  Blackstone Inc is finalizing a syndicate of banks to underwrite an A$4.3 billion (US$3 billion) loan for AirTrunk Pty's SYD3 hyperscale data centre in Australia, according to people familiar with the matter, as the volume of debt raised globally for artificial intelligence infrastructure this year has already far exceeded last year's full-year total.

  The five-year loan will fund construction of the Sydney facility, the people said, asking not to be identified because the matter is private. Confirmed underwriters include Credit Agricole SA, DBS Group Holdings Ltd, Deutsche Bank AG, HSBC Holdings plc, ING Bank NV, Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group (MUFG), Morgan Stanley, and United Overseas Bank Ltd. The syndicate is expected to grow as additional banks seek to join, the people said. Representatives for Blackstone, AirTrunk, and all named lenders declined to comment.

  The Australian Financial Review first reported AirTrunk was assembling the debt package.

  ## Scale of AI infrastructure debt

  The deal lands against a backdrop of mounting concern over the sheer scale of borrowing being deployed to build out AI-linked data centre capacity. At least US$334.5 billion in bonds and loans have been issued so far in 2026 to finance AI infrastructure projects, according to data compiled by Bloomberg — already nearly double the US$185.5 billion raised across all of 2025.

  That acceleration is straining the capacity of the banking sector to absorb further exposure. In the Asia Pacific region, data centres have traditionally depended on bank financing rather than bond markets, but lenders are gradually approaching internal limits on how much they can commit to the sector after deploying billions of dollars across multiple projects, the people said. At the same time, strong demand for capital from data centre operators is pushing up borrowing costs.

  ## AirTrunk's broader debt activity

  Blackstone-owned AirTrunk is simultaneously wrapping up a separate loan of approximately US$2.3 billion for a data centre project in Johor, Malaysia, the people said. The concurrent fundraisings illustrate the pace at which the company is expanding across the Asia Pacific region.

  AirTrunk is not alone in tapping lenders for larger sums. Australian Securities Exchange-listed NextDC Ltd has increased the size of a new debt facility to A$2.3 billion from an initial target of A$1.8 billion, according to the people.

  ## What this means for regional data centre finance

  The SYD3 loan, if completed at its current size, would rank among the larger single-asset data centre financings in the Asia Pacific region. The participation of eight international and regional banks — spanning European lenders, Japanese megabanks, and Southeast Asian institutions — reflects both the scale of capital required for hyperscale construction and the continued appetite among lenders despite publicly acknowledged exposure concerns.

  The expected expansion of the underwriting group suggests demand to participate remains active even as the broader market approaches capacity limits. Whether lenders can sustain that appetite as issuance continues to accelerate is the central question facing data centre financing in the region. AirTrunk's Johor loan is expected to close in the near term, with the SYD3 facility following as the underwriting group is finalized.

  這筆五年期貸款將用於資助雪梨該設施的建設工程，知情人士表示，由於此事尚屬私密，他們要求匿名。已確認的承銷行包括：法國農業信貸銀行（Crédit Agricole SA）、星展集團（DBS Group Holdings Ltd）、德意志銀行（Deutsche Bank AG）、匯豐控股（HSBC Holdings plc）、荷蘭國際集團銀行（ING Bank NV）、三菱日聯金融集團（MUFG）、摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley），以及大華銀行（United Overseas Bank Ltd）。知情人士表示，預計還有其他銀行申請加入，聯合承銷團規模可望進一步擴大。黑石、AirTrunk及所有具名貸款行的發言人均婉拒置評。

  《澳洲金融評論》率先報導AirTrunk正在籌組這筆債務融資案。

  ## 人工智慧基礎建設債務規模

  這筆交易的背景，是外界對借貸規模之龐大日益憂慮——各方正大舉舉債，用以建設人工智慧相關資料中心產能。根據彭博匯編的資料，2026年迄今已有至少3,345億美元的債券與貸款發行，用於資助人工智慧基礎建設項目，幾乎是2025年全年1,855億美元籌資規模的兩倍。

  這波加速態勢正考驗著銀行業進一步承擔曝險的能力。知情人士表示，在亞太地區，資料中心傳統上依賴銀行融資而非債券市場，但各貸款行在對多個項目部署了數十億美元之後，已逐漸逼近其對該產業的內部授信上限。與此同時，資料中心業者旺盛的資金需求，也正推高借貸成本。

  ## AirTrunk 更廣泛的融資動態

  黑石旗下的AirTrunk同步正在為馬來西亞柔佛的一個資料中心項目敲定另一筆約23億美元的貸款，知情人士透露。兩筆融資同步推進，充分說明該公司在亞太地區的擴張步伐之快。

  向貸款行尋求更大規模資金的並非只有AirTrunk。在澳洲證券交易所掛牌的NextDC Ltd，已將新債務融資額度從原訂目標18億澳元擴大至23億澳元，知情人士表示。

  ## 區域資料中心融資的啟示

  SYD3這筆貸款若以目前規模完成，將躋身亞太地區單一資產資料中心融資案中規模較大者之列。八家國際及區域銀行的參與——涵蓋歐洲貸款行、日本大型銀行及東南亞金融機構——既反映超大規模建設所需的龐大資本規模，也顯示儘管各方公開承認曝險疑慮，放款意願仍持續存在。

  承銷銀行團預計繼續擴大，顯示即便整體市場逼近容量上限，參與意願仍然活絡。隨著發行規模持續加速，貸款行能否維持這股意願，正是整個區域資料中心融資所面臨的核心問題。AirTrunk的柔佛貸款預計近期即可完成，SYD3融資案則將待承銷銀行團確定後接續落地。
