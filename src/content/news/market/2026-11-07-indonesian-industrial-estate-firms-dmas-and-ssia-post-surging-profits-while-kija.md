body_en: |
  ## Nine-month results

  Three Indonesian industrial estate developers reported higher nine-month revenue, but profit performance diverged sharply: PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk (DMAS) and PT Surya Semesta Internusa Tbk (SSIA) posted surging earnings, while PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk (KIJA) was squeezed, according to Kontan.

  All three companies benefited from rising demand for industrial land, with buyers concentrated in the data center, electric vehicle manufacturing, electronics, and logistics sectors, the report said.

  ## Demand drivers

  Elandry Pratama, Branch Manager at Panin Sekuritas Pondok Indah, said the global supply-chain relocation trend and Indonesia's downstream industrial policy — a government push to process raw materials domestically rather than export them unprocessed — have become concrete positive catalysts for industrial estate developers, rather than speculative tailwinds.

  ## Outlook and execution risk

  Despite the promising near-term outlook, Pratama said the continuation of the growth trend will depend heavily on how effectively each company converts investor interest into actual land sales and brings planned investments to completion. The distinction between signed letters of intent and completed transactions remains a key variable, he said.

  ## 前九個月營運成果

  三家印尼工業區開發商前九個月營收均告成長，但獲利表現卻大相徑庭。根據《Kontan》報導，PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk（DMAS）與PT Surya Semesta Internusa Tbk（SSIA）獲利大幅攀升，PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk（KIJA）則遭受擠壓。

  三家公司均受惠於工業用地需求持續上升，買方主要集中在資料中心、電動車製造、電子及物流等產業。

  ## 需求動能

  Panin Sekuritas Pondok Indah分行經理Elandry Pratama表示，全球供應鏈遷移趨勢，以及印尼的下游工業化政策——即政府積極推動原物料在國內加工，而非直接出口未加工原料——已成為工業區開發商的實質正面催化劑，而非單純的投機性利多。

  ## 前景展望與執行風險

  儘管近期展望樂觀，Pratama指出，成長趨勢能否持續，將高度仰賴各公司能否有效將投資人興趣轉化為實際土地銷售，並順利完成規劃中的投資案。他強調，意向書的簽署與實際交易的完成之間，仍是左右後市的關鍵變數。
