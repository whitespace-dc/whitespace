  ## MSAB takes largest voting stake in Beijer Ref

  Swedish family-owned investment company Melker Schörling AB (MSAB) has agreed to acquire all outstanding A-shares in refrigeration wholesaler Beijer Ref from private equity firm EQT and former chairman Peter Jessen Jürgensen, making it the company's largest shareholder by voting rights, the parties said.

  The transaction gives MSAB 14.1% of voting rights in Beijer Ref, while representing 2.1% of the company's total share capital. The A-shares were acquired at an undisclosed premium; no financial terms were made public.

  ## Governance consequences

  With the largest block of voting rights, MSAB will be entitled to appoint the chair of Beijer Ref's nomination committee when that body is formed in September, according to the announcement. Nomination committees in Swedish-listed companies play a central role in proposing board members and the board chair to shareholders.

  Simultaneously with the share acquisition, MSAB and Per Bertland — Beijer Ref's former chief executive and current chairperson — have entered into a mutual right of first refusal agreement covering their respective holdings of A-shares in the company. Such an agreement requires each party to offer the other the opportunity to purchase their shares before selling to a third party.

  ## Long-term ownership shift

  MSAB described the transaction as the start of a long-term change of ownership, stating its intention to gradually increase its stake in Beijer Ref over time, with a focus on continued industrial development and long-term value creation.

  MSAB was founded in 1999 by the late Swedish billionaire businessman Melker Schörling. The firm holds significant interests in several major companies, including security group Assa Abloy, heat pump and energy systems manufacturer Nibe, and security services company Securitas.

  ## What comes next

  The transaction signals a transition in Beijer Ref's shareholder structure, with MSAB replacing EQT as a key influential owner. MSAB's stated intent to build its position incrementally suggests the ownership composition may continue to shift in the months ahead. The first formal consequence of the new structure will be MSAB's role in constituting the nomination committee in September.

  ## 公司治理層面的影響

  持有最大表決權股份後，根據公告，MSAB將有權於今年九月提名委員會成立時，指派一名代表出任該委員會主席。在瑞典上市公司中，提名委員會在向股東提名董事會成員及董事會主席方面扮演核心角色。

  MSAB將本次交易定位為長期股權更迭的起點，並表明將逐步增持Beijer Ref股份，聚焦於推動公司持續的產業發展與長遠價值創造。
