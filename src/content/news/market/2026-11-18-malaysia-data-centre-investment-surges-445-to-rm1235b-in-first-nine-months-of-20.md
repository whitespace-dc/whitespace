body_en: |
  Data centre and cloud computing investments in Malaysia rose 44.5% year-on-year to RM123.5 billion across 86 approved projects in the first nine months of 2026, the Malaysian Investment Development Authority (Mida) said on Tuesday, with the category now representing 35.2% of total approved investments, up from 29.4% a year earlier.

  The investments dominated the services sector, which attracted RM219.1 billion in total approved investments over the period, up 14.2% from RM191.7 billion a year earlier, according to Mida.

  Within services, the information and communications subsector, which covers data centre and cloud computing activity, led all categories with RM133.5 billion in approvals. Real estate ranked second at RM47.9 billion, followed by support services at RM19.3 billion, utilities at RM5.8 billion, and distributive trade at RM5.1 billion.

  To encourage local participation, Mida requires new data centres to meet a minimum 30% local content threshold.

  ## Foreign investment approvals jump 40.7%, led by the US

  Total approved investments across all sectors rose 20.7% year-on-year to RM350.4 billion over the nine-month period. Foreign investment approvals drove the gain, jumping 40.7% to RM212.3 billion and accounting for 60.6% of the total. Domestic investment approvals edged down 1% to RM138.1 billion.

  The United States was the largest single source of foreign investment, with approvals surging more than fivefold to RM60 billion from RM11.3 billion a year earlier. China ranked second at RM38.6 billion, Singapore third at RM38.5 billion, Japan fourth at RM24.4 billion, and Australia fifth at RM12.6 billion. The five countries collectively accounted for 82% of total foreign investment approvals.

  The 4,387 approved projects across all sectors are expected to generate more than 152,000 jobs, Mida said, with high-value roles concentrated in chip design, advanced electronics, and precision engineering. Mida chairman Tengku Datuk Seri Zafrul Abdul Aziz said the agency was working with industry and education partners to ensure talent development kept pace with investor requirements.

  ## Manufacturing rebounds on AI data centre component demand

  Approved manufacturing investments rose 13.3% year-on-year to RM106.3 billion across 1,353 projects, reversing a 25.1% contraction recorded in the first half of the year. Mida attributed the third-quarter recovery in part to approvals for the production of optical transceivers for artificial intelligence (AI) data centres and for liquefied natural gas processing.

  The electrical and electronics (E&E) industry led manufacturing approvals, rising 85.3% to RM40.8 billion. Petroleum products including petrochemicals ranked second at RM13.2 billion, followed by machinery and equipment at RM9.4 billion, basic metal products at RM6.7 billion, and food manufacturing at RM6.4 billion.

  Domestic manufacturing investments surged 82.7% to RM37.8 billion, while foreign manufacturing investments totalled RM68.5 billion. Manufacturing projects are expected to generate 92,489 jobs, up 27.3% from 72,672 a year earlier, with 78% of positions going to Malaysians. Of those positions, 16,607 are expected to carry monthly salaries of RM5,000 or above.

  ## Johor and Selangor lead by state

  Johor attracted the highest state-level approved investments at RM99.7 billion. Foreign investments accounted for 81.7%, or RM81.4 billion, of Johor's total, the highest foreign share among the five leading investment destinations. Selangor ranked second at RM87.9 billion, recording both the highest number of approved projects at 1,302 and the most expected jobs at 34,971. Penang drew RM41.1 billion and led in expected manufacturing employment with 26,643 jobs. Kuala Lumpur ranked fourth at RM37.3 billion and Sabah fifth at RM19.5 billion.

  ## From approval to production

  Of the 6,202 manufacturing projects approved between 2021 and September 2026, 87.4% had reached various stages of production, factory construction, or machinery installation as of the report period, Mida said. Another 9.5% remained in the planning stage and 3.1% had yet to be implemented.

  Mida chief executive Datuk Sikh Shamsul Ibrahim Sikh Abdul Majid said investment commitments needed to translate into operational outcomes. "Approval is an important milestone but implementation is what delivers factories, business activities and jobs," he said.

  As of October, Mida had 223 proposed projects worth RM41.2 billion in its pipeline, comprising 119 manufacturing projects worth RM18.6 billion and 104 services projects worth RM22.6 billion. The agency was separately in discussions over potential investments worth a further RM60.5 billion.

  ## 馬來西亞資料中心投資年增44.5%，前三季達1,235億馬幣

  馬來西亞投資發展局（Mida）週二公布，2026年前九個月，馬來西亞資料中心與雲端運算投資年增44.5%，達1,235億馬幣，涵蓋86件核准專案。此類別投資佔全國核准投資總額比重，從去年同期的29.4%攀升至35.2%。

  在服務業方面，同期核准投資總額為2,191億馬幣，較去年同期的1,917億馬幣成長14.2%，資料中心與雲端運算投資在其中獨占鰲頭。服務業各子類別中，涵蓋資料中心與雲端運算業務的資訊通訊子產業以1,335億馬幣的核准金額居首，房地產以479億馬幣排名第二，支援服務以193億馬幣居第三，公用事業以58億馬幣排第四，流通貿易以51億馬幣排第五。

  為鼓勵本地業者參與，Mida要求新建資料中心須達到最低30%的本地採購比例門檻。

  ## 外資核准額大增40.7%，美國居首

  前九個月各產業核准投資總額年增20.7%，達3,504億馬幣。外資核准額大幅躍升40.7%至2,123億馬幣，佔總額60.6%，是帶動整體成長的主力；國內投資核准額則微幅下滑1%至1,381億馬幣。

  美國是最大的單一外資來源國，核准投資額從去年同期的113億馬幣暴增逾五倍至600億馬幣。中國以386億馬幣排名第二，新加坡以385億馬幣居第三，日本以244億馬幣列第四，澳洲以126億馬幣排第五。上述五國合計佔外資核准總額的82%。

  各產業核准專案共4,387件，預計創造逾15萬2千個就業機會。Mida表示，高附加價值職缺主要集中於晶片設計、先進電子與精密工程領域。Mida主席東姑拿督斯里查弗魯阿都阿茲表示，該局正與產業界及教育機構合作，確保人才培育的速度能跟上投資者的需求。

  ## 製造業因AI資料中心零組件需求而回升

  製造業核准投資額年增13.3%至1,063億馬幣，涵蓋1,353件專案，扭轉了上半年25.1%的衰退走勢。Mida將第三季的復甦，部分歸因於AI資料中心用光收發模組生產及液化天然氣處理相關專案獲得核准。

  電機與電子（E&E）產業領跑製造業核准投資，大幅成長85.3%至408億馬幣。石油產品（含石化）以132億馬幣排名第二，機械與設備以94億馬幣居第三，基本金屬製品以67億馬幣列第四，食品製造以64億馬幣排第五。

  國內製造業投資大幅成長82.7%至378億馬幣；外資製造業投資總計685億馬幣。製造業專案預計創造92,489個就業機會，較去年同期的72,672個增加27.3%，其中78%的職位將由馬來西亞人出任。在這些職位中，有16,607個職位的月薪預計達5,000馬幣以上。

  ## 以州屬劃分：柔佛與雪蘭莪領先

  就州屬層級而言，柔佛以997億馬幣的核准投資額高居全國之冠。外資佔柔佛核准投資總額的81.7%，達814億馬幣，在五大主要投資目的地中，外資比重最高。雪蘭莪以879億馬幣排名第二，同時以1,302件核准專案數及34,971個預期就業機會，分居各州之首。檳城吸引411億馬幣投資，在製造業預期就業機會方面以26,643個職位領先各州。吉隆坡以373億馬幣排名第四，沙巴以195億馬幣居第五。

  ## 從核准到量產

  在2021年至2026年9月間獲核准的6,202件製造業專案中，截至報告期，已有87.4%進入生產、廠房建設或機械安裝等不同階段；另有9.5%仍處於規劃階段，3.1%尚未啟動實施。

  Mida執行長拿督錫克沙姆蘇依布拉欣錫克阿都馬吉德表示，投資承諾必須轉化為實際成果。他指出：「核准是重要的里程碑，但落地實施才是工廠、商業活動與就業機會真正誕生的關鍵。」

  截至10月，Mida手中有223件擬議中的專案，總值412億馬幣，其中包括119件製造業專案（總值186億馬幣）及104件服務業專案（總值226億馬幣）。此外，該局正就潛在投資進行洽談，相關金額另達605億馬幣。
