body_en: |
  Digital Realty launched operations in Malaysia on Monday with a multi-site data centre campus in Cyberjaya targeting approximately 32 megawatts (MW) of information technology (IT) capacity across three facilities, connecting the country to the company's global platform of more than 300 data centres across more than 30 countries. Malaysia's Minister of Digital, Gobind Singh Deo, officiated the launch.

  ## Three facilities, phased delivery

  The Cyberjaya campus centres on two existing sites separated by roughly 500 metres and a third parcel secured for future development.

  KUL10, formerly known as TelcoHub 1, is an operational carrier-dense data centre — meaning it hosts a high concentration of telecommunications network providers — currently providing 1.5MW of IT capacity. Digital Realty said it plans to upgrade KUL10 to its global operating standards and nearly double its capacity by the fourth quarter of 2027.

  KUL11, located approximately 500 metres from KUL10, provides 15MW of IT capacity. The facility was purpose-built to support artificial intelligence (AI) and high-performance computing (HPC) workloads and incorporates energy and water efficiency measures, the company said.

  Digital Realty has also secured a 1.6-acre land parcel approximately 200 metres from KUL10. A third facility on that site, carrying 14MW of capacity, is targeted for completion in mid-2028.

  The three facilities will be connected through dedicated fibre infrastructure and supported by more than 40 network service providers alongside cloud and connectivity partners, the company said.

  ## Global platform integration

  Digital Realty said it will integrate the Cyberjaya campus into PlatformDIGITAL, its global data centre network, and enable interconnection through the ServiceFabric suite of solutions. Customers in Malaysia will be able to access data centres in Singapore and Jakarta, and gradually connect to the broader global ecosystem, the company said.

  Serene Nah, Digital Realty's managing director and head of Asia-Pacific, said the Cyberjaya facilities will allow enterprises, cloud providers and digital businesses to deploy and scale infrastructure within the company's globally connected platform. She said the speed at which Digital Realty established a multi-site presence in Malaysia reflects strong customer demand and a long-term commitment to supporting the country's digital growth.

  Billy Lee, who previously led TelcoHub 1, said integration into Digital Realty's global platform gives existing customers access to a broader interconnection ecosystem and greater flexibility to scale infrastructure beyond Malaysia.

  ## Government response

  Gobind Singh Deo said the investment strengthens Malaysia's position as a digital infrastructure hub and supports the country's AI ambitions. He said high-quality, globally connected data centre infrastructure is critical to supporting innovation, attracting investment and enabling Malaysia's digital economy to grow with resilience and scale.

  Anuar Fariz Fadzil, chief executive of the Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC), the government agency overseeing the Malaysia Digital national strategic initiative, said the investment supports Malaysia's goal of becoming an AI Nation by 2030. He said investments of this kind create high-value opportunities and advance Malaysia's participation in regional and global digital value chains.

  ## Expansion outlook

  Digital Realty said it expects Malaysia to play an increasingly important role within its Southeast Asian operations as demand for AI workloads, cloud services and regional interconnection capacity increases. The company said it is also evaluating additional sites in Malaysia beyond the three Cyberjaya facilities, though no specific locations or timelines were disclosed.

  The third Cyberjaya facility, on the secured 1.6-acre parcel adjacent to KUL10, is targeted for completion in mid-2028.
body_zh: |
  Digital Realty 於本週一正式宣布進軍馬來西亞，在賽城（Cyberjaya）建立多址資料中心園區，三座設施合計目標提供約 32 百萬瓦（MW）的資訊科技（IT）容量，並將馬來西亞納入公司遍及逾 30 個國家、超過 300 座資料中心的全球平台。馬來西亞數位部長哥賓星（Gobind Singh Deo）出席主持啟用典禮。

  ## 三座設施，分階段交付

  賽城園區以相距約 500 公尺的兩座現有設施為核心，並另外取得第三塊土地，供未來開發之用。

  KUL10 前身為 TelcoHub 1，是一座電信商密集型資料中心——意即設施內進駐大量電信網路業者——目前提供 1.5MW 的 IT 容量。Digital Realty 表示，計畫將 KUL10 升級至公司全球營運標準，並於 2027 年第四季前將容量提升近一倍。

  KUL11 距 KUL10 約 500 公尺，提供 15MW IT 容量。該設施專為支援人工智慧（AI）與高效能運算（HPC）工作負載而建，並導入能源與用水效率措施。

  Digital Realty 另已取得一塊距 KUL10 約 200 公尺、面積 1.6 英畝的土地，計畫在該地塊興建第三座設施，容量 14MW，目標於 2028 年中完工。

  三座設施將透過專用光纖基礎設施互連，並獲逾 40 家網路服務商及雲端與連線夥伴支援。

  ## 整合全球平台

  Digital Realty 表示，將把賽城園區整合至旗下全球資料中心網路 PlatformDIGITAL，並透過 ServiceFabric 解決方案套件實現互連。馬來西亞客戶將可存取新加坡與雅加達的資料中心，並逐步接入更廣泛的全球生態系。

  Digital Realty 亞太區董事總經理暨負責人 Serene Nah 表示，賽城各設施將使企業、雲端供應商及數位業者得以在公司全球互連平台上部署並擴展基礎設施。她指出，Digital Realty 能如此迅速在馬來西亞建立多址據點，反映出強勁的客戶需求，以及對支持馬來西亞數位成長的長期承諾。

  前 TelcoHub 1 負責人 Billy Lee 表示，整合至 Digital Realty 全球平台後，現有客戶將可接入更廣泛的互連生態系，並獲得在馬來西亞以外擴展基礎設施的更大彈性。

  ## 政府回應

  哥賓星表示，此次投資強化了馬來西亞作為數位基礎設施樞紐的地位，並支持馬來西亞的 AI 發展抱負。他說，高品質、具全球連結能力的資料中心基礎設施，對支援創新、吸引投資，以及讓馬來西亞數位經濟得以具備韌性、規模化地成長，至關重要。

  馬來西亞數位經濟機構（MDEC）執行長 Anuar Fariz Fadzil——該機構負責監督「馬來西亞數位」（Malaysia Digital）國家策略倡議——表示，此投資有助於馬來西亞實現 2030 年成為「AI 國家」的目標。他說，此類投資將創造高附加價值機會，並推進馬來西亞參與區域及全球數位價值鏈。

  ## 擴張展望

  Digital Realty 表示，隨著 AI 工作負載、雲端服務及區域互連容量需求持續增長，馬來西亞在其東南亞業務中扮演的角色預計將日益重要。公司亦表示正評估賽城三座設施以外的馬來西亞其他選址，惟目前尚未披露具體地點或時間表。

  賽城第三座設施坐落於緊鄰 KUL10 的已取得 1.6 英畝地塊上，目標於 2028 年中完工。
