body_en: |
  Swedish refrigeration wholesaler Beijer Ref is reorganising its European operations across three countries effective January 1, merging its Dutch subsidiaries, splitting its German business units and consolidating Danish logistics into a new facility.

  The changes affect Beijer Ref's established wholesale and distribution network in the Netherlands, Germany and Denmark, restructuring legal entities and operational footprints without altering the group's ownership of the businesses involved.

  ## Netherlands merger

  Beijer Ref's three Dutch subsidiaries — Celsis BV, Coolmark and ECR Nederland — will legally merge into a single entity, Beijer Ref Nederland BV, from January 1. The company said the commercial transition, including the introduction of a new central business system, harmonisation of the product range and migration to a new central webshop, will proceed on a stepped basis through 2027.

  The three businesses were each acquired separately over a span of roughly two decades. Celsis BV is the former Uniechemie business, acquired by Beijer Ref in 2007. Coolmark came into the group in 2004 as part of Beijer Ref's purchase of the Danfoss-owned Elsmark Group. ECR Nederland, originally a Carrier business, joined Beijer Ref in 2009.

  ## Germany split

  In Germany, Beijer Ref is moving in the opposite direction, separating the two business units of Beijer Ref Deutschland GmbH into independent companies while both remain wholly owned subsidiaries of Beijer Ref AB.

  The Toshiba distribution business will continue to operate under the existing GmbH structure but will be renamed TClimateSystems GmbH, with Uwe Steinbach as chief executive. The German wholesale division, operating under the ARW name, will be transferred to Beijer Ref's sister company Coolair Klimasysteme GmbH under Mario Schemann and renamed Beijer Ref Germany GmbH.

  ## Denmark consolidation

  In Denmark, Beijer Ref will consolidate the Jutland operations of three entities — H Jessen Jürgensen A/S, Armadan A/S and Insulize A/S — at a new location in Horsens. The company described the investment as an important step in long-term development and said the new facility will serve as a central logistics hub, bringing together activities currently spread across several locations.

  Beijer Ref said the aim is to create a more integrated setup with increased capacity and greater flexibility. The company's location in Ballerup will be retained to provide technical advice, sales, a trade counter and a local collection warehouse.

  ## What it means

  The three reorganisations reflect distinct operational priorities by market. In the Netherlands, consolidating entities that were separately acquired over fifteen years standardises systems and customer-facing infrastructure, with the phased 2027 migration signalling the scale of integration work involved. In Germany, the separation of distribution and wholesale into independent legal structures suggests Beijer Ref sees those businesses as serving sufficiently different customer segments to warrant distinct identities and leadership. In Denmark, the Horsens hub centralises logistics while maintaining a sales and service presence in the Copenhagen-area Ballerup site.

  All restructured entities remain within the Beijer Ref AB group. The Dutch commercial transition, including system and webshop migration, is scheduled to complete during 2027.

  ## 荷蘭合併

  Beijer Ref在荷蘭的三家子公司——Celsis BV、Coolmark及ECR Nederland——將於一月一日起依法合併為單一法人Beijer Ref Nederland BV。公司表示，商業過渡工作，包括導入新的核心業務系統、統一產品線，以及遷移至新的中央網路商店，將分階段推進，預計於2027年完成。

  這三家公司係在約二十年間分別收購取得。Celsis BV原為Uniechemie，於2007年被Beijer Ref收購；Coolmark則於2004年隨Beijer Ref收購丹佛斯（Danfoss）旗下Elsmark集團時一同納入；ECR Nederland原屬開利（Carrier）旗下，於2009年加入Beijer Ref。

  ## 德國分拆

  在德國，Beijer Ref的作法截然相反，將Beijer Ref Deutschland GmbH旗下兩個業務單位分拆為獨立公司，兩者均繼續以全資子公司身分隸屬於Beijer Ref AB。

  東芝（Toshiba）配銷業務將沿用現有GmbH架構，並更名為TClimateSystems GmbH，由Uwe Steinbach出任執行長。以ARW品牌運營的德國批發部門，則將移轉至Beijer Ref的姐妹公司Coolair Klimasysteme GmbH，由Mario Schemann主導，並更名為Beijer Ref Germany GmbH。

  ## 丹麥物流整合

  在丹麥，Beijer Ref將整合H Jessen Jürgensen A/S、Armadan A/S及Insulize A/S三家企業在日德蘭半島的業務，統一遷入霍森斯（Horsens）的全新設施。公司將此項投資定位為長期發展的重要一步，表示新設施將作為中央物流中心，把目前分散於多處的業務集中整合。

  Beijer Ref表示，此舉旨在打造更整合的運作模式，同時擴大產能、提升靈活度。位於巴勒魯普（Ballerup）的據點將予保留，繼續提供技術諮詢、銷售服務、實體門市及在地取貨倉儲。

  ## 整體意義

  三項重組各自反映出不同市場的業務優先考量。在荷蘭，合併歷經約十五年間分別收購的法人實體，目的在於標準化系統及面向客戶的基礎架構；分階段推進至2027年的遷移計畫，也揭示了整合工程規模之龐大。在德國，將配銷與批發業務分拆為獨立法人，說明Beijer Ref認為兩者所服務的客群差異已足夠顯著，值得各自建立獨立品牌識別與領導架構。在丹麥，霍森斯中央物流中心將業務集中化，同時在大哥本哈根地區的巴勒魯普維持銷售與服務窗口。

  所有重組後的法人均繼續隸屬Beijer Ref AB集團。荷蘭的商業過渡工作，包括系統與網路商店的遷移，預計於2027年完成。
