body_en: |
  ABB has expanded its relationship with Electric Torque Machines (ETM), a partnership that began in 2025, to develop and commercialize motors built on ETM's patented transverse flux motor (TFM) technology. The motors will be sold under the ABB brand and are aimed at large cooling fans, pumps, and heat rejection towers used in high-density data centers.

  Transverse flux motors generate high torque at low rotational speeds — a characteristic that makes them suitable for the large, slow-moving fans and pumps central to data center heat rejection. ABB said the direct-drive architecture of TFM technology reduces mechanical complexity and conversion losses compared with conventional motor-and-gearbox arrangements, and also lowers noise output, maintenance requirements, and total cost of ownership over the equipment's lifetime.

  ## How the technology fits ABB's existing portfolio

  ABB described the ETM partnership as complementary to its existing lineup of high-efficiency motors, ultra-low harmonic variable speed drives, and liquid cooling products. The company did not provide specific performance figures for the TFM product or a commercial availability date.

  Justin McMahon, Executive Vice President of ABB's global business line for its NEMA Motors Division, said demand is being driven by the shift toward artificial-intelligence workloads, which are increasing heat loads inside data centers. "As AI reshapes data centre design, operators are looking for cooling solutions that deliver more with less — less energy, less complexity and less downtime," McMahon said in a statement. "Our expanded partnership with ETM brings a differentiated motor technology that addresses exactly these needs."

  ## Industry significance

  The deal signals a move by a major electrical equipment supplier to bring an alternative motor architecture into the data center cooling market as operators face rising thermal management demands. By commercializing TFM technology under its own brand, ABB is positioning the product within its broader data center infrastructure offering rather than as a standalone component. The motors are intended for the large cooling infrastructure — fans, pumps, and heat rejection towers — that sits at the perimeter of high-density facilities.

  ## ABB擴大與ETM合作，力推橫向磁通馬達進軍資料中心冷卻市場

  ABB宣布深化與Electric Torque Machines（ETM）的合作關係。這段夥伴關係最初於2025年建立，如今進一步擴展至開發並商業化以ETM專利橫向磁通馬達（Transverse Flux Motor，TFM）技術為核心的馬達產品。相關馬達將以ABB品牌名義銷售，主要針對高密度資料中心所使用的大型冷卻風扇、泵浦與散熱冷卻塔等設備。

  橫向磁通馬達的特性在於能在低轉速下產生高扭力，這使其特別適合資料中心散熱系統中常見的大型低速風扇與泵浦。ABB表示，TFM技術採用直接驅動架構，與傳統馬達搭配齒輪箱的組合相比，能有效降低機械複雜度與轉換損耗，同時也可減少噪音輸出、降低維護需求，並壓低設備整個生命週期的總持有成本。

  ## 與ABB既有產品線的整合定位

  ABB將此次ETM合作定位為對其現有產品組合的互補延伸，涵蓋高效率馬達、超低諧波變頻驅動器以及液冷產品等系列。ABB目前尚未公布TFM產品的具體性能數據，也未宣布正式上市日期。

  ABB全球NEMA馬達事業部執行副總裁Justin McMahon表示，此次需求動能源於人工智慧工作負載的快速增長，這類應用大幅拉高了資料中心內部的散熱負荷。McMahon在聲明中指出：「隨著AI重塑資料中心的設計思路，營運商正在尋求能以更少資源實現更高效能的冷卻解決方案——更低的能耗、更簡化的架構、更短的停機時間。我們與ETM深化合作，正是引入了一項能精準回應這些需求的差異化馬達技術。」

  ## 產業意義

  這項合作協議標誌著一家主要電氣設備供應商積極將替代性馬達架構引入資料中心冷卻市場，背景正是各地業者面臨日益嚴峻的熱管理挑戰。ABB選擇以自有品牌商業化TFM技術，意在將這項產品融入其更完整的資料中心基礎設施方案，而非以單一零組件的形式推出。這些馬達的主要應用場景，是部署於高密度設施外圍的大型冷卻基礎設施，包括風扇、泵浦與散熱冷卻塔等設備。
