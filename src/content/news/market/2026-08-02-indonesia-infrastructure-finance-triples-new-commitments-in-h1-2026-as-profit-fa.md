body_en: |
  PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance (IIF) booked Rp 2.5 trillion in new financing and investment commitments in the first half of 2026, more than three times the Rp 799 billion it deployed across all of 2025, according to results the company released July 31.

  The volume surge came alongside a 55% decline in net profit, to Rp 38.2 billion from Rp 85.3 billion in the same period a year earlier. Operating revenue fell to Rp 542.7 billion from Rp 653.9 billion, which IIF attributed to lower investment interest income following early loan repayments by several borrowers in the first quarter and a more conservative provisioning policy.

  ## Financing and advisory deal flow

  IIF closed six new financing and investment commitments in January through June, spread across healthcare, ports, telecommunications and data centers, and renewable energy.

  Advisory business expanded in parallel. The company secured nine new mandates in the half, with total contract value of Rp 19 billion — three times the level it reached across the whole of 2025. One of the principal mandates involves IIF's role in Indonesia's Waste-to-Energy program, known locally as PSEL (Pengolahan Sampah menjadi Energi Listrik, the conversion of municipal waste into electric power), supporting accelerated integrated solid-waste management in urban areas.

  ## Revenue composition

  Non-interest income rose 54.8% year on year to Rp 43 billion, driven by growth in financing, investment, advisory, and treasury activities. Treasury interest income increased 17.5% year on year. Those gains partially offset the fall in investment interest income caused by the early repayments.

  ## Balance sheet and capital

  Total assets reached Rp 15 trillion at June 30, up 4.6% year on year. Productive assets, comprising loans and securities, grew 4.9% year on year to Rp 13.4 trillion.

  IIF's Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR), the regulatory measure of capital held against risk-weighted exposures, stood at 56.79%, well above the regulatory minimum. The loan-loss coverage ratio exceeded 100%, and the net non-performing financing (NPF) ratio, which measures problem loans after provisions as a share of the total portfolio, improved to 3.49% from 3.61%.

  ## July transactions and funding

  Since the half-year close, IIF completed three transactions in July 2026 worth nearly Rp 833 billion covering logistics infrastructure, renewable energy, and water treatment facilities, according to President Director and Chief Executive Officer Rizki Pribadi Hasan.

  To support further expansion, the company raised more than Rp 1.3 trillion from domestic and international financial institutions. It also completed a first phase of capital-market issuance totaling Rp 652 billion, comprising Rp 432 billion in bonds and Rp 220 billion in perpetual securities.

  ## ESG and outlook

  Rizki said IIF is embedding Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) standards across its project portfolio, arguing that ESG compliance improves project quality and risk management while widening access to international financing.

  On the earnings decline, Rizki framed the first-half result as a function of the early repayments and provisioning caution rather than deteriorating business conditions. "Although first-half profit was lower, we are optimistic we can continue to expand our business and improve financial performance, supported by strong capital, improved services, lower cost of funds, and strong support and collaboration from shareholders and business partners," he said in the July 31 statement.

  The company said it expects to expand both its financing and advisory services in coming periods, citing its capital base and a project pipeline that continued to add transactions after the June reporting date.
body_zh: |
  PT印尼基礎建設金融（IIF）2026年上半年新融資與投資承諾額達2.5兆盧比，超過2025年全年7,990億盧比總額的三倍以上。該公司於7月31日公布上述業績數據。

  承諾額大幅攀升之際，淨利潤卻同步下滑55%，從去年同期的853億盧比跌至382億盧比。營業收入亦從6,539億盧比降至5,427億盧比，IIF將此歸因於第一季部分借款人提前償還貸款，導致投資利息收入縮減，加上公司採取較為保守的備抵呆帳提列政策，兩項因素共同壓縮了獲利空間。

  ## 融資與顧問業務成交動態

  IIF於1月至6月間完成六項新融資與投資承諾，涵蓋醫療照護、港口、電信與資料中心，以及再生能源等領域。

  顧問業務亦同步擴張。上半年共取得九項新委任，合計合約總金額達190億盧比，相當於2025年全年水準的三倍。其中一項重要委任，是IIF參與印尼的「廢棄物轉能源」計畫——印尼當地稱為PSEL（Pengolahan Sampah menjadi Energi Listrik，即將城市垃圾轉化為電力），協助加速推動都會地區的整合式固廢管理。

  ## 收入組成

  非利息收入年增54.8%至430億盧比，主要來自融資、投資、顧問及資金操作業務的成長。資金操作利息收入年增17.5%。上述收益部分抵銷了因借款人提前還款所造成的投資利息收入下滑。

  ## 資產負債表與資本狀況

  截至6月30日，總資產達15兆盧比，年增4.6%。生息資產（含貸款及有價證券）年增4.9%至13.4兆盧比。

  IIF的資本適足率（CAR，衡量銀行相對於風險加權曝險所持有資本的法定指標）為56.79%，遠高於主管機關規定的最低門檻。呆帳覆蓋率逾100%，淨不良融資比率（NPF，即扣除備抵呆帳後問題貸款佔總投資組合之比重）則從3.61%改善至3.49%。

  ## 7月交易與資金募集

  自半年度結算後，IIF於2026年7月完成三筆交易，總金額近8,330億盧比，涵蓋物流基礎建設、再生能源及水處理設施，此為總裁暨執行長里茲基·普里巴迪·哈桑（Rizki Pribadi Hasan）所透露。

  為支持後續擴張，公司從國內外金融機構籌得逾1.3兆盧比資金。此外，公司亦完成第一期資本市場發行，總額6,520億盧比，其中包含4,320億盧比的債券及2,200億盧比的永續債券。

  ## ESG與展望

  里茲基表示，IIF正將環境、社會與治理（ESG）標準全面融入整體投資組合，並強調ESG合規不僅有助提升專案品質與風險管理，更能拓展取得國際融資的管道。

  針對獲利下滑一事，里茲基將上半年業績定性為提前還款效應與謹慎備抵呆帳政策所致，而非業務基本面惡化的結果。他在7月31日的聲明中指出：「雖然上半年獲利有所下滑，但我們樂觀認為，在雄厚資本、服務提升、資金成本降低，以及股東與業務夥伴的大力支持與協作之下，我們有能力持續擴展業務、改善財務表現。」

  公司表示，預計在未來期間進一步擴大融資及顧問服務，並以現有資本實力為後盾，著眼於6月報告日後持續累積的專案管線，積極推動後續業務發展。
