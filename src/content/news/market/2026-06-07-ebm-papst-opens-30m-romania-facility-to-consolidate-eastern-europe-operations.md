body_en: |
  EBM-Papst, the German fan and motor manufacturer, has officially opened a €30 million facility in Oradea, Romania, bringing manufacturing, warehousing, office, and research functions together at a single campus in the city's Oradea II Industrial Park.

  The site spans approximately 5.5 hectares and totals roughly 13,600 square metres of built space across four functional areas: a 4,100m² production floor, a 5,300m² warehouse and logistics zone, 3,200m² of offices, and a 1,200m² research and development centre dedicated to designing and testing new generations of fans.

  ## Strategic rationale

  "With this new facility, we are consolidating production, development, and services in Eastern Europe, thereby significantly enhancing our responsiveness to European customers," said Chief Operating Officer Frank Mayer. The company described the plant as a "future-proof location" intended to provide current and future employees with conditions for collaboration, innovation, and sustainable growth.

  ## Construction and sustainability features

  Construction was completed within ten months. EBM-Papst said planning and implementation placed particular focus on energy efficiency and sustainable land use. The facility incorporates a smart building management system, photovoltaic modules for on-site electricity generation, and green spaces across the grounds.

  ## Significance

  The Oradea opening signals a push by European industrial manufacturers to concentrate Eastern European operations — spanning production, logistics, and product development — into purpose-built campuses rather than maintaining dispersed sites. The inclusion of an R&D centre alongside production capacity positions the facility as more than a manufacturing outpost, giving EBM-Papst the ability to develop and validate new fan designs closer to its European customer base.

  The 1,200m² R&D centre for fan design and testing represents the facility's most forward-looking element, indicating that new product development for the European market will be anchored at the Oradea site.
body_zh: |
  德國風扇與馬達製造商EBM-Papst，正式在羅馬尼亞奧拉迪亞（Oradea）啟用一座耗資三千萬歐元的新廠，將製造、倉儲、辦公及研究功能集中於該市「奧拉迪亞二號工業園區」的單一校園式園區。

  整座廠區占地約五點五公頃，建築面積合計約一萬三千六百平方公尺，分為四大功能區塊：四千一百平方公尺的生產車間、五千三百平方公尺的倉儲與物流區、三千兩百平方公尺的辦公室，以及一千兩百平方公尺的研究開發中心，專責設計與測試新世代風扇產品。

  ## 策略布局

  「透過這座新設施，我們正在整合東歐地區的生產、研發與服務功能，進而大幅提升對歐洲客戶的應變能力，」營運長法蘭克．邁爾（Frank Mayer）表示。公司將這座廠房定位為「面向未來的據點」，旨在為現有及未來員工提供有利於協作、創新與永續成長的工作環境。

  ## 施工歷程與永續特色

  整體工程在十個月內竣工。EBM-Papst表示，在規劃與執行階段特別著重能源效率與土地的永續利用。廠房導入智慧建築管理系統、供現場發電的光伏模組，以及遍布園區的綠化空間。

  ## 意義與展望

  奧拉迪亞新廠的啟用，標誌著歐洲工業製造商正積極將東歐業務——涵蓋生產、物流與產品研發——集中整合至專屬的園區式廠房，而非繼續維持多點分散的布局。廠內同步設置研發中心與生產設施，使這座廠房不僅是單純的製造基地，更賦予EBM-Papst在更貼近歐洲客戶市場的地點開發及驗證新型風扇設計的能力。

  占地一千兩百平方公尺、專注於風扇設計與測試的研發中心，是整座設施最具前瞻性的核心元素，顯示針對歐洲市場的新產品研發工作，將以奧拉迪亞廠區作為主要錨點。
