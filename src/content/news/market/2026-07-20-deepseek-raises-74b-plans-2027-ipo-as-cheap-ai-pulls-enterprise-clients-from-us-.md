body_en: |
  DeepSeek, the Chinese AI startup whose low-cost models upended the global AI industry earlier this year, has raised $7.4 billion in the largest private AI financing in Chinese history, valuing the Hangzhou-based lab at more than $50 billion, and is planning an initial public offering in 2027 that the company's backers expect will raise billions more.

  The fundraising, combined with pricing that undercuts US competitors by as much as 99%, is accelerating a structural shift in how corporations buy AI services. Chinese open-source models — led by DeepSeek — claimed more than 30% of traffic on OpenRouter, a major AI aggregation platform, during some weeks in 2025, up from less than 2% in 2024, according to The Edge Malaysia.

  ## The pricing gap

  The cost differential between DeepSeek and its closest US rivals has become the central fact driving adoption. Performing a standardized intelligence task using DeepSeek's V4 Flash model costs two cents, compared with $2.75 for the equivalent task on Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 — a ratio of roughly 138-to-1 — according to benchmarking site Artificial Analysis.

  For corporate buyers, the savings are material. Lindy AI, a San Francisco-based startup, switched from Anthropic's Claude Sonnet to DeepSeek after a six-week evaluation. "We're now paying about 10% of what we used to pay," Lindy CEO Flo Crivello said, adding the company is saving millions of dollars annually — more than the total cost of its 27-person workforce.

  TinyFish, a Palo Alto-based enterprise AI infrastructure company, previously spent six figures a month on AI coding tools. After shifting from frontier US models to alternatives including Chinese open-weight models, its inference costs fell 90%. "This kind of bill is actually a wake-up call for a lot of companies," said Keith Zhai, TinyFish's chief operating officer.

  ## Corporate adoption widens

  The switch is not confined to startups. Airbnb runs a mix of Chinese open-source models alongside Western vendors. Pinterest has touted their utility. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said he is considering incorporating DeepSeek models into the company's Copilot Cowork product — notable given Microsoft's position as one of OpenAI's primary backers.

  Hundreds of US firms, according to the report, are reserving premium frontier models for complex reasoning tasks while routing simpler coding, automation, and routine workloads to cheaper Chinese alternatives.

  The shift reflects a broader change in corporate AI spending priorities. Companies including Tesla and Meta, which had set up internal leaderboards rewarding employees for maximizing token consumption — a practice referred to as "tokenmaxxing" — are now focused on controlling expenses and generating measurable returns.

  ## The fundraising structure

  DeepSeek founder Liang Wenfeng conducted the fundraising through a limited partnership he controls entirely. Investors including Tencent Holdings and JD.com agreed to five-year lockup periods and received no voting rights.

  One investor received different terms: the National AI Industry Investment Fund, a primary Chinese government vehicle for directing AI development, received direct equity, voting rights, and the ability to sell its stake at any time. The asymmetry distinguishes the government's stake from those of all other investors.

  Liang presented the pitch at a four-hour video conference that limited attendance to two representatives per institution. "We're a group of very ordinary people," he told attendees, according to people present at the meeting.

  ## China's industrial strategy

  Analysts and investors describe DeepSeek's trajectory as part of a deliberate national policy framework, not a standalone commercial bet.

  "DeepSeek is not simply one company," said Yinliang Tan, a professor at China Europe International Business School in Shanghai. "It is better understood as part of China's broader effort to industrialise AI: models, chips, cloud infrastructure, applications, and deployment ecosystems moving together."

  DeepSeek's investor list maps to each foundational layer of that ecosystem. Contemporary Amperex Technology — known by the acronym CATL and one of the world's largest battery manufacturers — provides a link to energy infrastructure. Huawei, which produces Ascend chips that compete with Nvidia's AI accelerators, supplies domestic semiconductor capacity. JD.com and Tencent provide distribution channels and real-world application deployment.

  Robert Wu, CEO of BigOne Lab, a Chinese analytics company that tracks technology trends, said: "DeepSeek isn't just a technical breakthrough, it's an economic one."

  Beijing's stated policy goals align with DeepSeek's market approach. Chinese officials have said they want AI to be low-cost and widely adopted throughout the domestic economy, and have pushed back against proprietary AI model structures of the kind represented by Anthropic and OpenAI. Zhipu founder and Tsinghua University professor Tang Jie has argued that frontier AI should remain broadly accessible rather than concentrated among a small number of owners — a position echoed by the state-backed People's Daily newspaper.

  Bennett B Borden, founder and CEO of Clarion AI Partners, a Salt Lake City-based law and technology strategy firm, said: "Chinese open-source models are a remarkably effective competitive response to the billions of dollars US companies have invested in developing frontier AI models. By making high-performing models freely available and pricing them aggressively, Chinese developers are lowering the barrier to AI adoption around the world."

  ## Energy as competitive input

  Beyond chips and software, China's electricity generation capacity is emerging as a structural cost advantage. In 2025 alone, China built 543 gigawatts of generating capacity — more than the total installed capacity of any other country in the world — enabling it to run data centers at a scale and cost difficult to replicate elsewhere.

  "Maybe China doesn't have the best compute, but China has lots of electricity," Wu said. "At the end of the day, the cost of AI will just go down and down and down to the cost of energy. China has a clear advantage."

  ## What comes next

  DeepSeek's IPO, currently targeted for 2027, would represent a further escalation. The listing would give the company additional capital to expand its global distribution and deepen price competition with US rivals that are themselves preparing to go public — OpenAI and Anthropic are both expected to pursue listings in the near term at potential trillion-dollar valuations.

  Jaime Xiao, an AI analyst at Shenzhen-based Success Capital, said the structure of DeepSeek's investor group suggests the company's expansion will be coordinated across chips, cloud, and applications rather than through model development alone. The National AI Industry Investment Fund's preferential position in the capital structure indicates the Chinese government retains direct leverage over the company's direction as it moves into overseas markets.
body_zh: |
  DeepSeek，這家以低成本模型顛覆全球AI產業的中國新創公司，日前完成中國史上最大規模私募融資，籌得74億美元，使這家總部位於杭州的實驗室估值突破500億美元。公司投資人預期，DeepSeek計畫於2027年進行首次公開募股（IPO），屆時將再度籌得數十億美元資金。

  此次融資，加上DeepSeek定價最高比美國競爭對手低99%的優勢，正在加速企業採購AI服務的結構性轉變。根據《The Edge Malaysia》報導，以DeepSeek為首的中國開源模型，在2025年某幾週期間已佔據主要AI整合平台OpenRouter逾30%的流量，遠高於2024年不足2%的水準。

  ## 定價落差

  DeepSeek與其最接近的美國競爭對手之間的成本差距，已成為推動採用的核心因素。根據評測網站Artificial Analysis的數據，使用DeepSeek V4 Flash模型執行一項標準化智慧任務，費用僅需兩美分；而Anthropic旗下Claude Fable 5完成同等任務的費用則為2.75美元，比例約為138比1。

  對企業買家而言，這樣的節省相當可觀。舊金山新創公司Lindy AI在進行為期六週的評估後，從Anthropic的Claude Sonnet切換至DeepSeek。「我們現在的費用大約只有以前的10%，」Lindy執行長Flo Crivello表示，並指出公司每年節省了數百萬美元——超過其27名員工的全員薪資總成本。

  帕羅奧圖企業AI基礎設施公司TinyFish，過去每月在AI程式碼工具上的支出高達六位數美元。在從美國前沿模型轉向包含中國開放權重模型在內的替代方案後，其推論成本下降了90%。TinyFish營運長Keith Zhai表示：「這種帳單其實對很多公司來說都是一記警鐘。」

  ## 企業採用範圍擴大

  這股轉換風潮並不局限於新創公司。Airbnb同時混用中國開源模型與西方廠商的服務；Pinterest也公開肯定其實用性；微軟執行長薩帝亞·納德拉（Satya Nadella）表示，正考慮將DeepSeek模型整合進公司的Copilot Cowork產品——此舉格外引人注目，因為微軟正是OpenAI的主要投資人之一。

  據報導，數百家美國企業目前採取混合策略：將頂級前沿模型保留用於複雜推理任務，同時將較簡單的程式碼編寫、自動化及例行工作，導向定價更低廉的中國替代方案。

  這一轉變反映出企業AI支出優先順序的深層變化。Tesla與Meta等公司曾設立內部排行榜，獎勵員工盡量提高代幣消耗量——這種做法被稱為「代幣極大化」（tokenmaxxing）——如今則轉而專注於控制支出、創造可量測的實際回報。

  ## 募資結構

  DeepSeek創辦人梁文鋒透過其全資控制的有限合夥架構完成本輪募資。包括騰訊控股與京東在內的投資人同意接受五年鎖定期，且不具備任何投票權。

  其中一名投資人獲得了截然不同的條款：作為中國政府引導AI發展的主要工具，「國家人工智慧產業投資基金」獲得直接股權、投票權，以及隨時出售持股的權利。這種不對等安排，使政府持股條件與所有其他投資人明顯有別。

  梁文鋒在一場長達四小時的視訊會議上進行了募資路演，每家機構最多僅限兩名代表出席。據與會人士轉述，他在會中表示：「我們是一群非常普通的人。」

  ## 中國產業戰略

  分析師與投資人將DeepSeek的發展軌跡定位為中國蓄意推行的國家政策框架的一部分，而非單純的商業押注。

  上海中歐國際工商學院教授Yinliang Tan表示：「DeepSeek不只是一家公司，更應被理解為中國推動AI產業化這一更宏觀努力的組成部分：模型、晶片、雲端基礎設施、應用程式與部署生態系統正在協同推進。」

  DeepSeek的投資人名單，恰好對應該生態系統的每個基礎層次。全球最大電池製造商之一的寧德時代（CATL）提供與能源基礎設施的連結；生產昇騰晶片、與輝達AI加速器直接競爭的華為，提供國內半導體產能；京東與騰訊則提供發行渠道與實際應用部署能力。

  追蹤科技趨勢的中國數據分析公司BigOne Lab執行長Robert Wu表示：「DeepSeek不只是技術上的突破，更是經濟上的突破。」

  北京的既定政策目標與DeepSeek的市場策略高度契合。中國官員表示，希望AI能在國內經濟中廣泛普及且成本低廉，並對Anthropic和OpenAI所代表的專有AI模型結構持反對立場。智譜AI創辦人、清華大學教授唐傑主張，前沿AI應廣泛開放，而非集中在少數業者手中——此一立場亦獲官媒《人民日報》呼應。

  總部位於鹽湖城的法律與科技策略公司Clarion AI Partners創辦人暨執行長Bennett B Borden表示：「中國開源模型是對美國企業數十億美元前沿AI研發投資極為有效的競爭回應。透過免費提供高效能模型並積極壓低定價，中國開發者正在降低全球各地採用AI的門檻。」

  ## 能源作為競爭要素

  除晶片與軟體之外，中國的電力生產能力正逐漸成為一項結構性成本優勢。僅2025年一年，中國便新增了543吉瓦（GW）的發電裝置容量——超過世界上任何其他國家的總裝置容量——使其得以在全球任何地方都難以複製的規模與成本條件下運行資料中心。

  「中國或許沒有最頂尖的運算資源，但中國擁有大量電力，」Wu表示。「說到底，AI的成本最終將不斷向下收斂，趨近於能源成本。中國在這方面具有明顯優勢。」

  ## 後續發展

  DeepSeek目前預計於2027年進行的IPO，將代表競爭態勢的進一步升級。此次上市將為公司提供額外資本，用於擴展全球發行版圖、深化與美國競爭對手的價格競爭——而那些美國競爭對手本身也正準備上市：OpenAI與Anthropic均預計在近期以潛在兆美元等級的估值尋求掛牌。

  深圳盛世投資AI分析師Jaime Xiao表示，DeepSeek投資人組合的結構顯示，公司的擴張將跨越晶片、雲端與應用程式進行協同推進，而非僅靠模型開發單點突破。國家人工智慧產業投資基金在資本結構中的優先地位，表明中國政府在DeepSeek進軍海外市場的過程中，仍保有對公司發展方向的直接影響力。
