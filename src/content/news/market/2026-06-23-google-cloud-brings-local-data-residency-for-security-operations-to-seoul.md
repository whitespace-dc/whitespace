body_en: |
  Google Cloud has launched localized data residency for its Google Security Operations platform in the Seoul Region, allowing South Korean organizations to store and process security logs and analytical data entirely within the country. The move is directed at regulated industries where rules governing where sensitive data can reside have constrained adoption of cloud-based security tools.

  Google Security Operations is Google Cloud's cloud-based security operations platform, combining threat detection, investigation, and response tools with automation features and the company's Gemini artificial intelligence models. Before the Seoul launch, Korean customers using the service would have been subject to cross-border data handling — a barrier that financial institutions and public agencies in particular have been reluctant to accept.

  ## Regulatory backdrop

  The timing aligns with a shift in South Korea's financial regulatory environment. Google Cloud said the Financial Services Commission — the country's primary financial regulator — is easing network separation rules for security-focused AI applications. That policy change could give banks and insurers more scope to use cloud software for vulnerability detection and defensive security systems, which previously may have required on-premises infrastructure to comply with separation requirements.

  Security operations platforms function as centralized systems where analysts collect and examine data from across corporate networks, identify suspicious activity, and coordinate responses to incidents. In regulated markets, whether a provider can meet local residency, governance, and compliance requirements often determines whether the technology can be adopted at all, independent of its technical capabilities.

  ## Platform capabilities

  Google Cloud said its Seoul-region deployment gives Korean organizations access to near real-time threat detection and contextual information for investigation and response. The platform includes workflow tools for automated, playbook-based responses, with support for parallel actions and version control. The company also described drag-and-drop playbook tools intended to reduce manual work for security analysts.

  Jagdish Mahapatra, Managing Director of Google Cloud Security for the Asia-Pacific region, said the platform can reduce investigation times from 30 minutes to one minute, attributing that capability to the AI components built into the service.

  ## Partner channel

  Alongside the product rollout, Google Cloud said it plans to expand work with local partners Bespin Global, LG CNS, and Megazone Cloud — each of which has established customer relationships and cloud implementation operations in South Korea. In regulated sectors, local service providers typically handle integration work, migration planning, ongoing operational support, and assistance navigating internal risk reviews, which can be as consequential as the technology itself in determining deployment timelines.

  ## Industry significance

  The Seoul launch is part of a pattern across the cloud market in which global providers adapt infrastructure and product design to local data sovereignty demands rather than relying on cross-border service delivery. For cloud providers, security products have become a competitive differentiator for regulated workloads precisely because those customers require both technical performance and compliance assurances that vary by jurisdiction.

  For South Korean organizations in financial services and the public sector, the practical question is whether Google Cloud's in-country data handling arrangement is sufficient to allow adoption of cloud-based security operations without breaching internal policy or regulatory expectations — a determination that individual institutions will make in light of the Financial Services Commission's evolving rules on AI security applications.

  ## 法規背景

  此次上線時機與南韓金融法規環境的轉變相互呼應。Google Cloud表示，南韓主要金融監管機關「金融服務委員會」正在放寬針對資安導向AI應用程式的網路隔離規定。此一政策轉變可望讓銀行與保險業者在使用雲端軟體進行漏洞偵測及防禦性資安系統方面獲得更大發展空間——而這些應用過去可能須採用地端基礎架構，才能符合隔離規定。

  資安營運平台作為集中化系統，供分析師蒐集並檢查來自整個企業網路的資料、辨識可疑活動，並協調事件應變工作。在受法規規範的市場中，服務供應商能否滿足本地資料駐留、治理與合規要求，往往決定了技術能否被採用，而這與技術本身的效能高低無關。

  ## 平台功能

  Google Cloud表示，首爾區域的部署讓南韓企業能夠使用近即時的威脅偵測功能，以及調查與回應所需的情境資訊。平台內含工作流程工具，支援以劇本（playbook）為基礎的自動化回應，並提供平行動作執行與版本控管功能。該公司亦介紹了拖放式劇本工具，旨在減輕資安分析師的手動作業負擔。

  Google Cloud亞太區資安業務董事總經理Jagdish Mahapatra表示，該平台能將調查時間從30分鐘縮短至1分鐘，並將此能力歸功於服務中內建的AI元件。

  ## 合作夥伴通路

  在產品推出的同時，Google Cloud表示計畫擴大與當地合作夥伴Bespin Global、LG CNS及Megazone Cloud的合作——這三家公司在南韓均已建立深厚的客戶基礎及雲端導入業務。在受法規規範的產業中，本地服務供應商通常負責整合工作、遷移規劃、持續的維運支援，以及協助應對內部風控審查，而這些工作對部署時程的影響往往不亞於技術本身。

  ## 產業意義

  首爾此次的服務上線，是雲端市場一項普遍趨勢的縮影：全球雲端供應商正依照各地資料主權需求調整基礎架構與產品設計，而非仰賴跨境服務交付。對雲端供應商而言，資安產品已成為爭取受法規規範工作負載的核心競爭差異，正因為這類客戶既要求技術效能，也要求因地制宜的合規保證，而兩者標準因司法管轄區而異。

  對南韓金融服務業與公部門機構而言，實際問題在於：Google Cloud的境內資料處理安排是否足以讓其採用雲端資安營運方案，同時不違反內部政策或法規要求——而這一判斷，各機構將在金融服務委員會就AI資安應用持續演進的監管規則下，自行做出決定。
