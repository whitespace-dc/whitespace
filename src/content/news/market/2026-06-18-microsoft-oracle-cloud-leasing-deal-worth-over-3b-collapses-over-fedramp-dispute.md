body_en: |
  Microsoft's negotiations with Oracle to lease cloud infrastructure in an arrangement valued at more than $3 billion have collapsed over a dispute about U.S. government security certification, Business Insider reported, causing Oracle shares to fall in after-hours trading.

  ## Why the deal broke down

  Microsoft had planned to route some of its workloads onto Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), Oracle's public cloud platform, to free up capacity on its own Azure cloud computing service for enterprise customers, according to Business Insider, which cited people familiar with the matter. The leasing arrangement would have exceeded $3 billion in value, one of those people said.

  The central obstacle was FedRAMP — the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program, a U.S. government security framework that certifies cloud services for handling federal data. Oracle's public cloud does not hold FedRAMP authorization. Microsoft required that any leased infrastructure carry FedRAMP certification; Oracle concluded that obtaining it for its public cloud would require too much investment in personnel and cost, and declined to pursue it, according to Business Insider.

  ## Oracle contests the account

  Oracle disputed the report shortly after its publication. "The details mentioned in the article are inaccurate. Microsoft is both an OCI partner and a customer. We have a tremendously collaborative and fruitful partnership, where we often talk about ways we can expand upon our ongoing work together," an Oracle spokesperson said in an emailed statement. Oracle did not specify which details it considered wrong. Microsoft declined to comment. Reuters, which reported Oracle's response, said it could not independently verify the Business Insider account.

  ## Microsoft's alternatives

  Microsoft is in discussions with other cloud providers as it seeks alternative capacity arrangements, according to Business Insider. Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud — whose public cloud platforms already hold FedRAMP authorizations — remain among the potential candidates, according to Commercial Times, which reported on the same underlying account.

  ## Market reaction and context

  Oracle shares fell in after-hours trading following the report, dragging AI-related and semiconductor stocks lower, according to Commercial Times. The episode reflects how sensitive markets have grown to any indication that large AI infrastructure spending commitments may not proceed as anticipated. Commercial Times noted that the development is the second recent instance of an AI infrastructure announcement triggering market turbulence; earlier, AI infrastructure company Crusoe paused a 1.8-gigawatt data center project in Wyoming after a customer request, also prompting sector selling.

  ## Significance

  The dispute, if the Business Insider account holds, illustrates that U.S. government security compliance requirements can unwind commercial cloud arrangements of significant scale, and that Microsoft's strategy of supplementing Azure capacity with third-party cloud infrastructure depends on partners holding the necessary certifications. Microsoft is continuing to evaluate alternative cloud providers that already hold FedRAMP authorization, according to Business Insider.

  微軟與甲骨文就租用雲端基礎架構、總值逾30億美元的協議所進行的談判已宣告破裂，起因是雙方對美國政府安全認證的要求產生分歧，《商業內幕》對此進行了報導。消息傳出後，甲骨文股價在盤後交易中隨即走跌。甲骨文方面則表示報導內容不實，但未具體說明哪些細節有誤。

  ## 破局原因

  據《商業內幕》引述知情人士說法，微軟原計畫將部分工作負載移轉至甲骨文雲端基礎架構（OCI）——亦即甲骨文的公有雲平台——藉此釋出自家Azure雲端運算服務的產能，以服務更多企業客戶。其中一位知情人士透露，此次租賃協議的總值將超過30億美元。

  談判的核心障礙在於FedRAMP，即「聯邦風險與授權管理計畫」（Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program），這是美國政府認證雲端服務是否具備處理聯邦資料資格的安全框架。甲骨文的公有雲目前尚未取得FedRAMP授權。據《商業內幕》報導，微軟要求所有租用的基礎架構都必須持有FedRAMP認證；然而甲骨文評估後認為，為其公有雲取得該認證所需投入的人力與成本過高，因而決定放棄，不再繼續推進。

  ## 甲骨文反駁報導

  報導發布後不久，甲骨文隨即提出異議。甲骨文發言人透過電子郵件發表聲明：「文章中提及的細節並不準確。微軟既是我們的OCI合作夥伴，也是客戶。我們擁有極為緊密且富有成效的合作關係，雙方時常討論如何深化既有的合作。」甲骨文未具體指出哪些細節有誤，微軟方面亦拒絕置評。路透社在報導甲骨文的回應時表示，無法獨立核實《商業內幕》的說法。

  ## 微軟的替代方案

  據《商業內幕》報導，微軟目前正與其他雲端服務業者洽談，尋求替代的產能安排。亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）與Google雲端——兩者的公有雲平台均已取得FedRAMP授權——均列為潛在候選對象，《工商時報》在報導同一事件時亦提及了這一情況。

  ## 市場反應與背景

  據《工商時報》報導，報導發出後甲骨文股價在盤後交易中下挫，並拖累AI相關股及半導體類股跟著走低。此事件反映出市場對大型AI基礎架構支出承諾出現任何風吹草動，都已高度敏感。《工商時報》指出，這已是近期第二起因AI基礎架構消息引發市場震盪的案例；此前，AI基礎架構公司Crusoe應客戶要求，暫停了懷俄明州一座1.8吉瓦資料中心的建設計畫，同樣引發類股賣壓。

  ## 事件意義

  若《商業內幕》的報導內容屬實，此次爭議清楚表明：美國政府的安全合規要求，足以讓規模可觀的商業雲端協議功虧一簣；而微軟藉由向第三方雲端業者租用基礎架構來補充Azure產能的策略，也有賴合作夥伴持有必要的安全認證。據《商業內幕》報導，微軟仍在持續評估其他已取得FedRAMP授權的雲端業者。
