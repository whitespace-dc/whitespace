  ## Data centre segment drives record revenue

  YTL Power International Bhd's data centre business generated RM139.54 million in profit before tax (PBT) in the first quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (1QFY2027), up from RM1.05 million in the same period a year earlier — a near-133-fold increase — according to the Malaysian conglomerate's latest results filing. Segment revenue rose 491.35% to RM329.38 million from RM55.70 million, driven by the progressive completion and rising occupancy of additional data halls, the company said.

  The data centre division has become the group's third-largest earnings contributor, ranking behind power generation and water.

  ## Group net profit falls despite revenue gain

  At the consolidated level, YTL Power's first-quarter net profit fell 7.98% year-on-year to RM460.62 million from RM500.57 million. Group revenue, however, rose 40.70% to RM7.54 billion from RM5.36 billion. The company attributed the net profit decline to lower retail margins in its power generation business and to its water division, which was affected by higher maintenance costs and weaker earnings from the United Kingdom.

  ## Expansion plans and investment commitments

  YTL Power said it has invested RM13.5 billion in its data centre business to date and is planning to expand geographically beyond its existing Kulai Data Centre Park in Johor, Malaysia. The company said it plans to increase total capacity at the green data centre park to 1,200 megawatts (MW) — enough to power a large city district — of which 298MW has been contracted.

  To support power supply to the adjacent 600MW portion of that park, YTL Power is developing a solar facility on a substantial portion of the Kulai Young Estate with a generation capacity of up to 500MW. The first phase, a 215MWac (megawatt alternating current) solar power facility, has been implemented, the company said.

  YTL Power entered the data centre sector in 2021 through the acquisition of a 50% stake in Dodid Pte Ltd, which owns a 12.5MW Tier-3 data centre in Singapore. The group has since expanded into Johor, operating data centres equipped with graphics processing units (GPUs) — specialised chips used for artificial intelligence workloads — supplied by US chipmaker Nvidia Corp, with which YTL Power holds a cloud and data centre partnership.

  ## Nvidia controversy draws no comment from company

  YTL Power's quarterly report made no mention of the controversy surrounding its technology partner Nvidia. Former Nvidia chief executive officer Jensen Huang has admitted to having an extramarital affair, and media reports allege the woman involved was a spy who arranged for high-powered Nvidia chips to be diverted to China in violation of US export controls. The woman is alleged to be a former executive of Singapore-based cloud firm Megaspeed International, which is reported to be a major customer of YTL Power.

  There is no credible evidence implicating YTL Power in the alleged chip diversion. Queries from multiple media organisations, including The Edge, have gone unanswered by the company, and YTL Power's quarterly earnings report contained no statement on the matter.

  ## Telecommunications and AI investments

  In its telecommunications segment, YTL Power said subsidiary YTL Communications is working to grow its subscriber base through affordable data plans, 5G services, and partnerships. Its latest initiative offers ILMUChat Pro plans free to subscribers of its YES mobile network through a partnership with YTL AI Labs.

  YTL AI Labs, a 70%-owned subsidiary of YTL Power, owns and develops ILMU, described by the company as a Malaysian large language model (LLM) — a type of AI system trained to generate and process text.

  ## Market and outlook

  YTL Power shares closed unchanged at RM4 on Thursday, giving the group a market capitalisation of approximately RM35 billion. The data centre segment's rapid growth from a negligible base to the group's third-largest profit contributor within a single year underscores the pace at which GPU-intensive cloud infrastructure is reshaping the earnings profile of regional utilities that have positioned early in the space.

  The company's stated target of 1,200MW total data centre capacity — against a current contracted base of 298MW — indicates that the bulk of the planned buildout remains ahead, contingent on continued capital deployment and customer uptake.

  ## 資料中心部門創下營收紀錄

  YTL Power International Bhd的資料中心業務，在截至2026年9月30日的第一財季（1QFY2027）中，稅前盈利（PBT）達1億3,954萬令吉，較去年同期的105萬令吉大幅增長近133倍——此數據來自這家馬來西亞企業集團最新提交的業績報告。部門營收則年增491.35%，從5,570萬令吉攀升至3億2,938萬令吉。公司表示，增長動能來自陸續完工、並逐步提高進駐率的新增機房。

  資料中心部門已躍升為集團第三大獲利貢獻來源，僅次於電力發電及供水業務。

  ## 集團淨利下滑，儘管營收成長

  就合併報表而言，YTL Power第一季淨利年減7.98%，從5億0,057萬令吉降至4億6,062萬令吉。然而集團營收卻年增40.70%，從53億6,000萬令吉增至75億4,000萬令吉。公司將淨利下滑歸因於電力發電業務零售利潤收窄，以及供水部門因維護成本上升、加上英國業務獲利走弱所致。

  ## 擴張計畫與投資承諾

  YTL Power表示，迄今已在資料中心業務上投入135億令吉，並計劃在馬來西亞柔佛州現有的古來資料中心園區（Kulai Data Centre Park）之外，進一步拓展至其他地區。該公司計劃將這座綠色資料中心園區的總容量提升至1,200百萬瓦（MW）——足以供應一座大型城市區域的用電需求——其中已有298MW取得合約。

  為了支援毗鄰600MW園區的電力供應，YTL Power正在古來楊莊園（Kulai Young Estate）大部分土地上開發一座太陽能設施，發電容量最高可達500MW。公司表示，第一期215兆瓦交流電（MWac）的太陽能發電設施已完成建置。

  YTL Power於2021年透過收購新加坡Dodid Pte Ltd 50%的股份，正式跨入資料中心產業；Dodid擁有一座12.5MW的Tier-3資料中心。此後，集團持續在柔佛州擴張，營運配備繪圖處理器（GPU）的資料中心——GPU是一種專為人工智慧運算工作負載設計的特殊晶片——所使用的GPU由美國晶片大廠輝達（Nvidia）提供，YTL Power亦與輝達締結有雲端與資料中心的合作夥伴關係。

  ## 輝達爭議：公司未置一詞

  YTL Power的季報對其技術合作夥伴輝達所捲入的爭議隻字未提。輝達前執行長黃仁勳已公開承認有婚外情，且有媒體報導指稱，涉及的女性是一名間諜，據報曾安排將輝達高階晶片繞道輸往中國，違反美國出口管制規定。該名女性據報曾任職於新加坡雲端公司Megaspeed International，而Megaspeed International據報是YTL Power的主要客戶之一。

  目前並無可信證據顯示YTL Power涉及上述晶片轉移事件。包括《The Edge》在內的多家媒體機構曾向公司提出查詢，均未獲回應，YTL Power的季度業績報告亦未對此事發表任何聲明。

  ## 電信與人工智慧投資

  在電信部門方面，YTL Power旗下子公司YTL Communications表示，正透過平價資費方案、5G服務及合作夥伴關係，積極拓展用戶基礎。最新推出的方案，是透過與YTL AI Labs合作，向YES行動網路用戶免費提供ILMUChat Pro方案。

  YTL AI Labs為YTL Power持股70%的子公司，負責擁有並開發ILMU——公司將其定位為馬來西亞自主研發的大型語言模型（LLM），即一種經訓練後能生成及處理文字的人工智慧系統。

  ## 市場表現與未來展望

  YTL Power股價週四收盤報4令吉，與前日持平，集團市值約達350億令吉。資料中心部門從近乎微不足道的基礎出發，在短短一年內躍升為集團第三大獲利來源，充分體現出GPU密集型雲端基礎設施，正以多快的速度重塑那些提早卡位的區域公用事業集團的獲利結構。

  該公司所揭示的1,200MW資料中心總容量目標，對比目前已簽約的298MW，意味著計劃中的大部分建設工程仍在前方，能否如期實現，有賴於持續的資本投入以及客戶的實際採用情況。
