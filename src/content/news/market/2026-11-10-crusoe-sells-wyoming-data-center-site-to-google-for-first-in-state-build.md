body_en: |
  Crusoe, an artificial intelligence infrastructure and cloud computing company, has sold its pre-developed data center site in southeast Wyoming to Google, which last week announced a $17 billion investment commitment to the state. The transaction confirms Crusoe's departure from a project it originated and de-risked but did not bring to construction.

  Crusoe said it had completed pre-development work before the sale, including site selection, governmental approvals, and power strategy. The company originated the campus alongside Tallgrass Energy.

  Chase Lochmiller, Crusoe's co-founder and chief executive officer, said the company's preparatory work positioned the project for what follows. "Crusoe's pre-construction efforts — origination, entitlement, and de-risking — have set up the Wyoming project for construction and eventual operation," Lochmiller said.

  ## Background to the sale

  Reports that Google had pushed Crusoe out of the Wyoming development surfaced as early as June 2026, W.Media reported, noting it had previously covered the situation. Those reports attributed the displacement to Crusoe's failure to lock in customers for the data center campus.

  ## Google's Wyoming data center

  Google said last week it is working with Tallgrass Energy, Black Hills Energy, American Electric Power (AEP), and Related Digital on what will be its first data center in Wyoming. Google said it will pay for the power it uses and cover the infrastructure needs driven by its growth.

  The Wyoming project is contracted with AEP to include a commercial-scale, 702-megawatt (MW) fuel cell development with carbon capture and storage. Google described the arrangement as the first of its kind at commercial scale.

  ## Crusoe's remaining pipeline

  Despite losing the Wyoming project, Crusoe said it has more than 6 gigawatts (GW) of capacity under contract and more than 40 GW in its development pipeline. The company is also building a separate data center campus for Google in Armstrong County, outside Amarillo, Texas.

  That Texas campus connects to Serena's Goodnight 1 wind farm, which is operational with a capacity of 265.5 MW. The campus sits adjacent to the Goodnight 2 wind farm, which is under construction and will carry the same 265.5 MW capacity when complete.

  The Wyoming transaction marks Crusoe's exit from that project, with Google now positioned to develop a site Crusoe originated as part of its $17 billion commitment to Wyoming.

  ## 交易背景

  W.Media指出，早在2026年6月便有報導傳出Google已將Crusoe擠出懷俄明州的開發計畫，並表示此前已持續追蹤相關動態。報導將這一變動歸因於Crusoe未能為該資料中心園區鎖定客戶。

  ## Google的懷俄明州資料中心

  Google上週宣布，將與Tallgrass Energy、Black Hills Energy、美國電力公司（AEP）及Related Digital合作，在懷俄明州興建其首座當地資料中心。Google表示將自行承擔用電費用，並負擔其業務成長所帶動的基礎設施建設需求。

  懷俄明州專案已與AEP簽約，將興建一座商業規模、702百萬瓦（MW）的燃料電池設施，並配備碳捕獲與封存系統。Google將此方案定位為同類型設施中首個達到商業規模的案例。

  ## Crusoe的其餘開發案管線

  儘管失去懷俄明州專案，Crusoe表示目前已簽約的容量超過6GW，開發案管線中的總容量更逾40GW。該公司亦正為Google在德州阿馬里洛郊外的阿姆斯壯郡另行建設一座資料中心園區。

  德州園區與Serena的Goodnight 1風力發電場相連，該風場已投入運營，裝置容量為265.5MW。園區緊鄰正在興建中的Goodnight 2風力發電場，後者完工後將具備相同的265.5MW發電容量。

  此次懷俄明州交易標誌著Crusoe正式退出該專案，Google則將依其對懷俄明州170億美元的投資承諾，在這片由Crusoe起始開發的土地上推動後續建設。
