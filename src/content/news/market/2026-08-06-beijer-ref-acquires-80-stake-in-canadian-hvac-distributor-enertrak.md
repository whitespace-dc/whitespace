body_en: |
  Swedish wholesale distributor Beijer Ref has acquired 80% of the shares in Enertrak, an HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) distributor headquartered in Montreal, Quebec, with an option to acquire the remaining 20%. The deal is Beijer Ref's first acquisition in Canada and extends its existing North American presence into a new market.

  Enertrak was founded in 1982 in Laval, Quebec, and has since grown to distribute more than 40 product lines across seven warehouses serving Quebec, the Maritimes, and the Outaouais region. The company generates annual net sales of approximately €100 million and employs 120 staff.

  Beijer Ref said Enertrak will continue to operate under its own brand, consistent with the Swedish company's approach to previous acquisitions.

  ## Strategic rationale

  Beijer Ref chairperson Per Bertland said the deal advances the company's strategy to build out its North American platform. "This acquisition represents an important step in Beijer Ref's strategy to further expand the North American platform through an entry into the Canadian market," Bertland said.

  ## What the deal means for the market

  The acquisition gives Beijer Ref a distribution network spanning several Canadian provinces, including established warehouse infrastructure across Quebec and Atlantic Canada. For the Canadian HVAC distribution sector, the deal introduces a major European wholesale group as a new owner in a market it had not previously entered. The inclusion of an option to acquire the remaining 20% of shares signals a potential path to full ownership.

  ## 瑞典貝耶爾冷媒收購加拿大HVAC經銷商Enertrak八成股權

  瑞典暖通空調批發商貝耶爾冷媒（Beijer Ref）宣布收購總部位於魁北克省蒙特婁的HVAC（暖氣、通風及空調）經銷商Enertrak八成股份，並附帶收購其餘兩成股份的選擇權。此次交易是貝耶爾冷媒首度在加拿大完成的收購案，同時也將其既有的北美業務版圖延伸至一個全新市場。

  Enertrak創立於一九八二年，發跡地為魁北克省拉瓦爾市，歷經數十年發展，目前代理逾四十條產品線，透過七座倉儲中心服務魁北克省、大西洋省份及烏塔維地區（Outaouais）。該公司每年淨銷售額約一億歐元，現有員工一百二十名。

  貝耶爾冷媒表示，Enertrak將繼續沿用自有品牌獨立運營，此舉延續了這家瑞典企業在歷次收購中一貫採行的整合策略。

  ## 策略布局考量

  貝耶爾冷媒董事長佩爾·博特蘭（Per Bertland）表示，這項收購案推進了公司持續強化北美平台的既定策略。博特蘭指出：「此次收購代表貝耶爾冷媒策略上的重要一步，藉由進入加拿大市場，進一步擴大北美平台的規模。」

  ## 對市場的影響

  透過這項收購，貝耶爾冷媒取得了橫跨加拿大數個省份的配銷網絡，涵蓋魁北克省及大西洋加拿大地區已建置完善的倉儲基礎設施。就加拿大HVAC配銷產業而言，此交易意味著一家具規模的歐洲批發集團首次以業主身分進入這個原本未曾涉足的市場。至於附帶的兩成股份收購選擇權，則預示著未來全面持股的可能路徑。
