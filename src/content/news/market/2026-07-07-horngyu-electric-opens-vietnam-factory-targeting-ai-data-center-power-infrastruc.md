body_en: |
  Horngyu Electric (Taiwan stock code: 7869) formally opened Horngyu Electric Vietnam Co., Ltd. at Industrial Zone No. 5 in Hung Yen Province, Vietnam, on July 3, 2026. The inauguration ceremony drew representatives from Taiwan and Vietnamese government agencies, trade associations, business partners, and customers from both markets, the company said.

  The new facility is planned to house production lines for switchboard manufacturing and busbar and electrical conductor processing. Busbars are rigid metal bars used to distribute high-current electricity within switchgear and power distribution panels. The company said it will introduce modern production management systems across the operation.

  ## Vietnam plant role in supply chain

  Horngyu described the Vietnam facility as a manufacturing base to serve Southeast Asian and global customers, organized around what it called a "Taiwan R&D, Vietnam manufacturing, global service" model. The company said the structure is intended to improve product quality, delivery efficiency, and supply chain resilience, giving international customers faster and more flexible access to products and technical support.

  Chairman Zhan Jia-rong said the plant represents an important milestone in the company's international expansion. "Vietnam is not the endpoint of Horngyu's overseas expansion, but a new starting point toward global markets," he said in a statement.

  ## AI computing center demand as target market

  Horngyu cited accelerating global AI development as a primary driver of demand for the power infrastructure products it intends to manufacture in Vietnam. The company said construction of data centers and AI computing centers — facilities housing servers and processors dedicated to artificial intelligence workloads — has raised requirements for power supply reliability, power quality, and energy management.

  The company said it intends to expand its position in electrical infrastructure for AI computing centers, developing high and low voltage power distribution equipment, smart energy management systems, and integrated power solutions to help customers build what it described as "safe, stable, and high-efficiency critical power infrastructure."

  ## Company background

  Horngyu Electric was founded in 1987 and has operated in the power industry for nearly 40 years, according to the company. Its stated core areas include high and low voltage power distribution equipment, electrical conductors, and smart power system integration services. The company said its products are deployed in semiconductor manufacturing, electronics production, data centers, public works, energy projects, and large industrial facilities.

  In recent years, the company said it has been advancing development in smart power distribution, low-carbon conductors, energy systems, and an AI-driven low-carbon power infrastructure platform.

  ## Industry significance

  The Vietnam plant represents Horngyu's entry into overseas manufacturing, extending the Taiwan-based company's production footprint beyond its home market as customers in data center and AI infrastructure sectors have placed greater emphasis on supply chain diversification and geographic flexibility. The company said it aims to use the facility to strengthen its competitive position across Asian markets and to advance toward becoming a leading international supplier of power solutions for AI computing centers.

  ## 宏煜電氣越南廠正式啟用，鎖定AI資料中心電力基礎建設市場

  台灣開關設備製造商宏煜電氣（股票代號：7869）於2026年7月3日，在越南興安省第5工業區正式揭牌成立「宏煜電氣越南有限公司」。公司表示，啟用典禮吸引了台越兩地政府機關、工商團體、合作夥伴及客戶代表共同出席見證。

  新廠預計設置配電盤製造產線，以及匯流排與電氣導體的加工產線。匯流排是一種剛性金屬導電條，用於在開關設備與配電盤內部進行大電流分配。宏煜電氣表示，廠內將全面導入現代化生產管理系統。

  ## 越南廠在供應鏈中的定位

  宏煜電氣將越南廠定位為服務東南亞及全球客戶的製造基地，並以其所稱的「台灣研發、越南製造、全球服務」模式加以統籌運作。公司表示，此一架構旨在提升產品品質、交貨效率與供應鏈韌性，讓國際客戶能更快速、更彈性地取得產品與技術支援。

  董事長詹家榮表示，越南廠是公司國際化布局的重要里程碑。他在聲明中指出：「越南不是宏煜海外擴張的終點，而是邁向全球市場的新起點。」

  ## 以AI運算中心需求為目標市場

  宏煜電氣將全球AI發展的加速浪潮，列為在越南生產電力基礎建設產品的主要需求動力。公司表示，資料中心與AI運算中心——亦即專門承載人工智慧工作負載的伺服器與處理器設施——的大量興建，已大幅提升對供電可靠性、電能品質及能源管理的要求。

  公司表示，將積極拓展在AI運算中心電氣基礎建設領域的版圖，持續研發高低壓配電設備、智慧能源管理系統及整合式電力解決方案，協助客戶打造其所稱的「安全、穩定、高效率的關鍵電力基礎建設」。

  ## 公司背景

  宏煜電氣創立於1987年，深耕電力產業近40年。公司核心業務涵蓋高低壓配電設備、電氣導體，以及智慧電力系統整合服務。其產品廣泛應用於半導體製造、電子生產、資料中心、公共工程、能源專案及大型工業設施等場域。

  近年來，宏煜電氣表示已積極推進智慧配電、低碳導體、能源系統，以及AI驅動低碳電力基礎建設平台等領域的研發工作。

  ## 產業意義

  越南廠標誌著宏煜電氣正式跨足海外製造，將這家總部位於台灣的企業生產版圖延伸至本土市場以外。隨著資料中心與AI基礎建設客戶日益重視供應鏈多元化與地理彈性，此一布局具有重要的戰略意義。宏煜電氣表示，期望藉由這座新廠強化在亞洲市場的競爭地位，並朝成為AI運算中心電力解決方案領域的國際領導供應商目標持續邁進。
