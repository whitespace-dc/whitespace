body_en: |
  Edged US has topped out its second data centre at its Aurora, Illinois campus, completing the structural framework of a 72 megawatt (MW) facility that was fully pre-leased before reaching the milestone. The building, located at 2835 Bilter Road in Aurora, the second-largest city in Illinois, is scheduled to open in the second quarter of 2027.

  At 72 MW of critical capacity, the facility ranks among the larger single data centre projects in the Chicago area, a market where demand for AI computing infrastructure has driven accelerated investment. Critical capacity refers to the power available to run computing equipment, excluding overhead such as cooling and lighting.

  Edged opened its first Aurora facility in February 2025. Together, the two buildings form the company's Chicago campus. Edged's broader North American network includes sites in Atlanta, Columbus, Council Bluffs, Dallas, Des Moines, Kansas City, and Phoenix, which the company describes as expanding at gigawatt scale.

  ## Cooling and resource use

  The facility uses Edged's ThermalWorks closed-loop, waterless cooling system, which the company says will save more than 277 million gallons of water per year compared with conventional data centres. Water consumption has become a growing concern in the data centre sector, particularly in regions where local water resources face competing demands from municipalities and industry.

  Edged's portfolio-wide average design power usage effectiveness (PUE) is 1.15. PUE measures how efficiently a facility uses energy: a PUE of 1.0 would mean all power goes directly to computing equipment, with nothing lost to cooling or other overhead. The industry average is typically higher; a figure of 1.15 indicates relatively efficient operation.

  ## Pre-leasing and demand

  The Aurora building was fully pre-leased before the topping-out, meaning all available capacity had been committed by customers before the structural work was complete. Edged did not disclose the identity of the tenant or tenants.

  Pre-leasing has become more common across major US data centre markets as long lead times for power connections and specialist equipment push customers to secure capacity well ahead of delivery. Developers in turn use pre-lease agreements to reduce financial risk before and during construction.

  FCL Builders is the general contractor on the project. Edged did not disclose the total construction cost.

  ## AI infrastructure demand

  Edged characterises the Aurora facility as purpose-built for high-density AI training and inference workloads, which typically require greater power density than conventional enterprise computing. The company describes its customer base as hyperscale cloud providers, technology companies, and large enterprises.

  Mitch Fonseca, chief operating officer of Edged US, attributed the project's progress to demand from AI customers and said efficiency and sustainability were requirements alongside speed of delivery.

  "As demand for AI infrastructure accelerates, speed, efficiency, and sustainability must advance together," Fonseca said. "Reaching this milestone in Aurora reflects the strength of our partnerships, the dedication of our construction teams, and our commitment to delivering high-performance infrastructure designed for the future of compute."

  ## Local engagement

  Edged said it has supported local organisations in the Aurora area, including Hesed House and other non-profit programmes, without disclosing the scale of that support.

  Construction is continuing, with the facility on track to become operational in the second quarter of 2027.

  Edged US已完成旗下位於伊利諾伊州奧羅拉園區第二座資料中心的結構封頂，這棟72百萬瓦（MW）的設施在達成這一重要里程碑之前，便已全數完成預租。該建築位於奧羅拉市比爾特路2835號——奧羅拉是伊利諾伊州第二大城市——預計於2027年第二季正式啟用。

  這座設施擁有72 MW的關鍵容量，躋身芝加哥地區規模較大的單一資料中心開發案之列。在AI運算基礎建設需求的帶動下，芝加哥市場的投資步伐明顯加快。所謂關鍵容量，係指用於運行運算設備的電力，不含冷卻、照明等附屬耗電。

  Edged已於2025年2月開放其奧羅拉第一座設施，兩棟建築共同構成該公司的芝加哥園區。Edged在北美的整體網路涵蓋亞特蘭大、哥倫布、康瑟爾布拉夫斯、達拉斯、德梅因、堪薩斯城及鳳凰城等據點，公司表示正以十億瓦（吉瓦）規模持續擴張。

  ## 冷卻系統與資源使用

  這座設施採用Edged自有的ThermalWorks封閉循環無水冷卻系統，公司表示，相較於傳統資料中心，每年可節省逾2億7,700萬加侖的用水。在資料中心產業中，水資源消耗已成為日益受到關注的議題，尤其是在地方水資源面臨市政及產業需求競爭的地區，此問題更加突出。

  就整體投資組合而言，Edged的設計平均電力使用效率（PUE）為1.15。PUE是衡量設施用電效率的指標：PUE為1.0代表所有電力直接用於運算設備，沒有任何耗損於冷卻或其他附屬設備。業界平均值通常高於此水準，1.15的數值顯示其運營效率相對優異。

  ## 預租與市場需求

  奧羅拉這棟建築在結構封頂之前便已全數完成預租，意即所有可用容量在結構工程完工前即已由客戶全數承租。Edged並未透露承租方的身份。

  隨著電力接入和專業設備的交付前置時間拉長，在美國主要資料中心市場中，預租現象已愈來愈普遍，促使客戶必須提前鎖定容量。開發商則藉由預租協議，在施工前後有效降低財務風險。

  本案的統包商為FCL Builders，Edged並未揭露總建設成本。

  ## AI基礎建設需求

  Edged將奧羅拉設施定位為專為高密度AI訓練與推論工作負載打造，此類工作負載通常比一般企業運算需要更高的電力密度。公司表示，其客戶群涵蓋超大規模雲端服務商、科技公司及大型企業。

  Edged US營運長米奇．方塞卡（Mitch Fonseca）表示，這項專案的推進得益於AI客戶的強勁需求，並強調效率與永續性和交付速度同樣重要。

  方塞卡說：「隨著AI基礎建設需求持續加速，速度、效率與永續性必須同步提升。奧羅拉這一里程碑的達成，體現了我們夥伴關係的堅實、施工團隊的付出，以及我們致力於為運算未來打造高效能基礎建設的承諾。」

  ## 在地連結

  Edged表示已支持奧羅拉地區的在地機構，包括Hesed House及其他非營利計畫，但未透露贊助規模。

  目前建設工程仍在持續推進，這座設施預計將如期於2027年第二季正式投入營運。
