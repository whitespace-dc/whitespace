body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers Ltd, a Singapore-based hyperscale operator whose largest customer is ByteDance, the Chinese owner of TikTok, has paused plans for a US initial public offering that had targeted a raise of as much as $5 billion, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified because the information is not public.

  The company had intended to list as early as the fourth quarter, filing its IPO paperwork in October and retaining Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup, and BNP Paribas as underwriters. DayOne has since begun assessing options including a delay or a smaller listing, and may pursue a public offering in 2027 if market conditions improve, the people said. A DayOne representative declined to comment.

  ## Investor confidence in AI infrastructure cools

  The decision to pause reflects growing unease about valuations in the data center and artificial intelligence sector. The Nasdaq 100 — the technology-heavy US stock index — has fallen more than 6% this month, on course for its worst November performance since 2008. Investors have grown increasingly doubtful about whether the scale of capital flowing into AI-linked infrastructure will generate returns that justify current prices.

  DayOne is not the only operator to have reassessed its listing timeline. Last month, Australian data center company Firmus Grid abandoned what had been expected to be one of Australia's biggest-ever initial public offerings. Blackstone-backed AirTrunk has delayed a planned listing of a real estate investment trust — a vehicle that holds income-producing property and distributes rental income to shareholders — in Singapore until next year.

  By contrast, Anthropic staged a record-breaking $107.3 billion IPO and private placement, rising 17% on its trading debut and becoming the world's seventh-most valuable company, illustrating that investor appetite for AI-related listings remains uneven rather than uniformly subdued.

  ## US chip licensing rules hit DayOne's operating markets directly

  An additional complication emerged when the US government introduced interim rules this week restricting China's access to advanced AI chips and servers through data centers in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and Indonesia — the four countries where DayOne operates hyperscale facilities. Under the new rules, data center operators in those four markets will need a licence to purchase American technology hardware.

  The restrictions carry particular relevance for DayOne because its customer base is anchored by Chinese technology companies. Beyond ByteDance, the company serves demand driven by Alibaba Group Holding, according to the source report. The licensing requirement adds a layer of regulatory uncertainty for a business built on serving that demand.

  ## Company profile

  DayOne operates hyperscale data centers — large campuses housing tens of thousands of servers for cloud computing and AI workloads — across Southeast Asia, a region that has seen rapid data center development fuelled by Chinese technology company demand. The company's financial backers include investment firms Coatue, Hillhouse, GDS Holdings, SoftBank Vision Fund, and Citadel's Ken Griffin.

  ## What it means

  The pause illustrates a widening gap between the operational demand for AI computing infrastructure and capital markets' willingness to price that demand into public equity at current multiples. For Southeast Asia's data center sector specifically, the new US chip licensing regime introduces a structural regulatory variable that did not exist when DayOne first filed its IPO paperwork.

  Deliberations remain ongoing and no final decisions have been made, the people said. DayOne may still pursue a public listing in 2027.

  ## DayOne資料中心暫緩50億美元赴美IPO　AI市況動盪與晶片規定雙重夾擊

  據知情人士透露，新加坡超大規模資料中心業者DayOne Data Centers Ltd已暫停在美國首次公開募股（IPO）的計畫，該公司原定最高募資50億美元。由於相關資訊尚未公開，知情人士要求匿名。DayOne最大客戶為TikTok中國母公司字節跳動（ByteDance）。

  該公司原計畫最快於今年第四季掛牌上市，並於十月提交IPO申請文件，同時委任摩根士丹利、摩根大通、美國銀行、花旗集團及法國巴黎銀行擔任承銷商。知情人士表示，DayOne此後開始評估各種選項，包括延後或縮小上市規模，若市場條件改善，可能於2027年再行推動公開募股。DayOne發言人婉拒置評。

  ## 投資人對AI基礎設施信心降溫

  此次暫緩決定，反映出市場對資料中心與人工智慧（AI）領域估值的疑慮持續升高。以科技股為主的美國那斯達克100指數本月已下跌逾6%，有望創下2008年以來最差的十一月表現。投資人愈來愈懷疑，流入AI相關基礎設施的龐大資本，能否產生足以支撐現行股價的回報。

  DayOne並非唯一重新評估上市時程的業者。上個月，澳洲資料中心公司Firmus Grid放棄了原本預期將成為澳洲史上規模最大IPO之一的上市計畫。由黑石集團（Blackstone）支持的AirTrunk，也將原定於新加坡進行的不動產投資信託（REIT）——即持有可產生收益的物業、並將租金收入分配給受益人的投資載體——上市計畫，推遲至明年。

  相比之下，Anthropic完成了一筆創紀錄的1,073億美元IPO暨私募配股，掛牌首日股價上漲17%，躍升為全球市值第七高的企業，顯示投資人對AI相關上市案的胃口仍是冷熱不均，而非全面退潮。

  ## 美國晶片出口許可規定直擊DayOne營運市場

  另一項複雜因素隨之浮現：美國政府本週頒布臨時規定，限制中國透過新加坡、馬來西亞、泰國及印尼等地的資料中心取得先進AI晶片與伺服器——而這四個國家正是DayOne超大規模設施的所在地。依據新規，上述四個市場的資料中心業者，日後採購美國技術硬體時須事先取得許可證。

  這項限制對DayOne而言格外敏感，因為其客戶群以中國科技公司為核心。除字節跳動外，據報導DayOne亦服務由阿里巴巴集團帶來的需求。許可證要求為這家以服務上述需求為核心業務的公司，增添了一層法規不確定性。

  ## 公司簡介

  DayOne在東南亞各地營運超大規模資料中心——即容納數萬台伺服器、用於雲端運算及AI工作負載的大型園區。這一地區的資料中心建設近年在中國科技公司需求帶動下急速擴張。公司財務支持者包括投資機構Coatue、高瓴資本（Hillhouse）、GDS Holdings、軟銀願景基金（SoftBank Vision Fund），以及城堡投資（Citadel）創辦人肯·格里芬（Ken Griffin）。

  ## 市場意涵

  此次暫緩凸顯出AI運算基礎設施的實際營運需求，與資本市場在現行本益比水準下為這類需求定價的意願之間，正出現愈來愈大的落差。就東南亞資料中心產業而言，美國新實施的晶片出口許可制度，引入了一個在DayOne最初提交IPO文件時尚不存在的結構性法規變數。

  知情人士表示，相關討論仍在進行中，目前尚未作出最終決定。DayOne仍有可能於2027年推動公開上市。
