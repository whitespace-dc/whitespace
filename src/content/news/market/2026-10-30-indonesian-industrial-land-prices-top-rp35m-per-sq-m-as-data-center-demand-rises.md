body_en: |
  Data center investment is pushing industrial land prices sharply higher in Indonesia, with prices in the Cikarang industrial corridor of Bekasi Regency, West Java, exceeding Rp3.5 million (Indonesian rupiah) per square meter in the third quarter of 2026, up from around Rp3 million in 2025, according to property consultancy CBRE Indonesia.

  Anton Sitorus, CBRE Indonesia's Head of Research and Consulting, attributed the increase directly to data center demand. "This very sharp increase is not just because of ordinary industry, but indeed because of data centers too. With data centers present, they dare to pay high prices for land," Sitorus said at the Jakarta Property Market Update Q3 2026 event on October 28, 2026.

  Sitorus said the premium reflects market perception of data centers as a high-value industry, even as other sectors — including pharmaceuticals and consumer goods — remain active in the same industrial zones.

  Cikarang's price level stands well above the wider Jabodetabek average. CBRE Indonesia recorded mean industrial land prices across the greater Jakarta metropolitan area — encompassing Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi — at Rp2.6 million per square meter in Q3 2026. Total industrial land stock in the region stands at 15,600 hectares, with net absorption of 59 hectares in the quarter.

  ## Supply expanding away from Jakarta

  CBRE Indonesia projects 1,400 hectares of future industrial land supply in the Jabodetabek region, but Sitorus said available sites are increasingly distant from the capital. "We see the locations getting further away — starting toward Subang, toward Jatiluhur, toward Central Java," he said.

  ## National capacity targets and the Malaysia gap

  Indonesia's Communications and Digital Minister Nezar Patria said at a separate event on October 22, 2026, that the country's total data center capacity currently stands at approximately 580 megawatts (MW) — a measure of the total electrical power installed facilities can draw — and that the government is targeting an increase to 1.3 gigawatts (GW, equal to 1,300 MW) by 2027–2028. He noted that Indonesia's capacity trails Malaysia's roughly 3 GW, despite Indonesia having a larger population. "We need to arrange and also provide safeguards in the development of data centers so that they can grow well in the future," Patria said.

  ## Industrial estates racing to meet power and water needs

  Sanny Iskandar, Chairman of the Advisory Board of the Indonesian Industrial Estate Association (HKI, Himpunan Kawasan Industri), said rising investor inquiries in industrial zones confirm growing data center demand, translating into higher requirements for land, electricity, and water. He cited a Cushman & Wakefield report on Asia-Pacific data centers covering the first half of the year, which classified Greater Jakarta and Karawang as established data center markets in the region, alongside Shanghai, Kuala Lumpur, and Bangkok.

  Iskandar said estate operators are investing in infrastructure to capture the opportunity, particularly in cooling systems and alternative water sources, but acknowledged that land, power, and water availability represent the sector's primary constraints. "We continue to support technology development to help with data center system cooling and alternative water sources," he said.

  Iskandar also said competitiveness in attracting data center and artificial intelligence (AI) investment depends on government support — specifically consistent policy and regulation, ease of doing business, legal certainty, and security — as well as an adequately skilled workforce.

  ## Proposed rule to channel data centers into industrial estates

  The Ministry of Industry (Kemenperin) is moving to formalize where data centers can be built. Deputy Minister of Industry Faisol Riza said on October 13, 2026, that the ministry is proposing an amendment to a ministerial regulation that would require data centers to be located within designated industrial estates. Riza said the goal is to manage data centers' significant demands for electricity and water so they do not disrupt supply to the broader public. "Data centers are important for industry and a lever for economic growth. We also see and hear reports from many ministries, agencies, and state-owned enterprises that energy, electricity needs, and water needs are large," he said. The ministry is seeking input from state electricity company PT PLN (Persero) and other government bodies before finalizing the regulation.

  ## What it means for the market

  The combination of rising land premiums, a proposed regulatory requirement to site data centers within industrial estates, and a national capacity expansion target that would more than double installed power to 1.3 GW by 2027–2028 points to sustained pressure on industrial land in the greater Jakarta region. As prime sites in Cikarang and Karawang absorb demand and prices rise, supply is set to migrate progressively outward toward Subang, Jatiluhur, and Central Java.
body_zh: |
  資料中心投資正大幅推升印尼工業用地地價。根據房地產顧問公司世邦魏理仕印尼（CBRE Indonesia）的資料，印尼西爪哇省勿加西縣（Bekasi Regency）西卡朗（Cikarang）工業走廊的工業用地地價，2026年第三季已超過每平方公尺350萬印尼盧比，較2025年的約300萬盧比顯著攀升。

  世邦魏理仕印尼研究暨顧問部門主管安東・西托魯斯（Anton Sitorus）直接將此一漲勢歸因於資料中心需求。他在2026年10月28日舉行的「雅加達房地產市場2026年第三季更新」活動上表示：「這波非常急劇的漲幅，不單只是一般產業帶動，確實也因為資料中心的緣故。有了資料中心的進駐，業者敢於以高價購置土地。」

  西托魯斯指出，此一溢價反映了市場將資料中心視為高附加價值產業的認知，即便製藥、消費品等其他產業，同樣在相關工業區內持續活躍。

  就整體來看，西卡朗的地價水準遠高於大雅加達都會區（Jabodetabek）的平均值。世邦魏理仕印尼記錄的大雅加達都會區——涵蓋雅加達、茂物（Bogor）、德波（Depok）、丹格朗（Tangerang）及勿加西（Bekasi）——工業用地平均地價，2026年第三季為每平方公尺260萬盧比。該地區工業用地總存量為1萬5,600公頃，本季淨去化面積達59公頃。

  ## 供給向外遷移，遠離雅加達

  世邦魏理仕印尼預估，大雅加達地區未來工業用地供給量約為1,400公頃，但西托魯斯表示，可用地點距離首都愈來愈遠。他說：「我們看到地點持續向外移動——開始朝蘇邦（Subang）、加蒂盧胡爾（Jatiluhur）、中爪哇方向延伸。」

  ## 全國容量目標與馬來西亞的差距

  印尼通訊暨數位部長內扎爾・帕特里亞（Nezar Patria）在2026年10月22日另一場活動上表示，印尼目前資料中心總裝機容量約為580百萬瓦（MW，為已安裝設施可消耗的總電力量），政府的目標是在2027至2028年將此一容量提升至1.3十億瓦（GW，相當於1,300 MW）。他指出，儘管印尼人口多於馬來西亞，印尼的資料中心容量仍落後於馬來西亞的約3 GW。帕特里亞表示：「我們需要妥善規劃，並為資料中心的發展提供保障，使其未來能夠健全成長。」

  ## 工業園區競相滿足電力與用水需求

  印尼工業園區協會（HKI，Himpunan Kawasan Industri）顧問委員會主席桑尼・伊斯坎達爾（Sanny Iskandar）表示，工業區內投資詢問的增加，印證了資料中心需求的持續成長，並轉化為對土地、電力與水資源更高的需求。他引述戴德梁行（Cushman & Wakefield）今年上半年發布的亞太資料中心報告，該報告將大雅加達及卡拉旺（Karawang）列為區域內的成熟資料中心市場，與上海、吉隆坡及曼谷並列。

  伊斯坎達爾表示，園區業者正積極投資基礎設施以把握此一商機，尤其著力於冷卻系統與替代水源的建設，但他也坦承，土地、電力與水源的取得，是該產業面臨的主要瓶頸。他說：「我們持續支持技術發展，以協助資料中心系統冷卻及替代水源的問題。」

  伊斯坎達爾同時表示，在吸引資料中心及人工智慧（AI）投資的競爭中，勝出的關鍵在於政府的支持——具體而言，包括政策與法規的一致性、經商便利度、法律確定性與安全保障——以及具備足夠技能的勞動力。

  ## 擬議法規：資料中心須設於工業園區內

  印尼工業部（Kemenperin）正著手規範資料中心的建設地點。工業部副部長法索爾・里薩（Faisol Riza）於2026年10月13日表示，部會正提議修正一項部長規定，要求資料中心必須設置於指定的工業園區內。里薩表示，此舉旨在管控資料中心對電力與水資源的龐大需求，以免影響一般民眾的供應。他說：「資料中心對產業而言至關重要，也是經濟成長的引擎。我們同樣看到、也聽到來自眾多部會、機構及國營企業的報告，指出能源、電力需求與用水需求十分龐大。」工業部目前正向國家電力公司PT PLN（Persero）及其他政府單位徵詢意見，再行定案。

  ## 市場展望

  綜合地價溢價持續攀升、擬議法規要求資料中心落址於工業園區，以及到2027至2028年將裝機電力提升逾一倍至1.3 GW的全國擴容目標，預示大雅加達地區工業用地將面臨持續的需求壓力。隨著西卡朗和卡拉旺的優質地塊逐漸消化殆盡、地價節節攀升，供給將逐步向外延伸，朝蘇邦、加蒂盧胡爾及中爪哇方向遷移。
