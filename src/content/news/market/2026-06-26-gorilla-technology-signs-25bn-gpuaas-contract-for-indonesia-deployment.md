body_en: |
  Gorilla Technology Group has signed a $2.5 billion, five-year GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS — rented GPU compute capacity delivered as a managed service) contract to deploy AI infrastructure at a data center in Batam, Indonesia, the company announced.

  The customer has not been named. Gorilla described it only as a "high investment-grade global technology customer."

  ## Contract structure and deployment schedule

  The contract is structured in phases. The first phase will consist of 1,000 B300 GPU servers and is expected to generate $1.3 billion of the $2.5 billion total over the five-year term. Gorilla is targeting September 2026 for first-phase deployment, December 2026 for the second phase, and the first half of 2027 for the remaining capacity.

  Gorilla said it has secured debt financing covering approximately 70 percent of expected project costs.

  ## Site: NeutraDC's Batam facility

  The hardware will be deployed at NeutraDC's data center at Kabil Industrial Estate, a technology park on Batam island, which sits across the strait from Singapore. NeutraDC topped out the facility in October 2025; it is expected to provide more than 50 megawatts (MW) of total capacity. The company operates a second facility at the same park.

  Gorilla signed a capacity agreement with NeutraDC in May 2026 for 5.5MW and said it intends to scale that commitment to approximately 18MW by November 2026.

  ## Gorilla's strategic framing

  Chairman and CEO Jay Chandan positioned the deal as a transition from infrastructure buildout to contracted, revenue-generating operations. "This contract is a major validation of that strategy. This is not a pipeline story. This is not an aspiration. This is signed customer demand, secured data center capacity, executable deployment timing, and bankable economics," Chandan said.

  The company, which describes itself as an AI infrastructure solutions provider with offerings spanning smart cities, network infrastructure, video analytics, cybersecurity, and IoT, said it is expanding into what it calls a "global AI infrastructure execution platform" targeting South and East Asia, the Middle East, and India.

  ## Prior contracts and broader pipeline

  The Indonesia deal follows a $1.4 billion, three-year contract signed last year with Freyr, a Singaporean cloud provider, to develop a network of AI-powered data centers. In April 2026, Gorilla signed a separate agreement with India's Yotta to deploy more than 25,000 GPUs across that country. The company is also planning to develop a 200MW data center in Thailand.

  ## What it means

  The contract adds to a pattern of large GPUaaS commitments being directed toward Southeast Asian markets. For Gorilla, which originated as a security intelligence and analytics company, the $2.5 billion deal represents a significant step into contracted GPU compute at scale — and a test of whether it can execute multi-phase deployments across a compressed timeline ending in mid-2027.

  Gorilla said delivery of remaining capacity beyond the first two phases is scheduled for the first half of 2027.

  ## 合約架構與部署時程

  本合約採分階段結構推進。第一階段將部署1,000台B300 GPU伺服器，預計在五年合約期內創造25億美元總額中的13億美元。Gorilla目標在2026年9月完成第一階段部署，2026年12月完成第二階段，其餘容量則規劃於2027年上半年陸續到位。

  Gorilla表示，已取得涵蓋約70%預期專案成本的債務融資。

  ## 部署地點：NeutraDC巴淡島設施

  硬體設備將部署於NeutraDC位在巴淡島卡比爾工業園區（Kabil Industrial Estate）的資料中心。該工業園區是巴淡島上的科技園區，隔著海峽與新加坡遙遙相望。NeutraDC於2025年10月完成該設施的主體結構封頂，預計總容量將超過50百萬瓦（MW）。該公司在同一園區還另外營運第二座設施。

  Gorilla已於2026年5月與NeutraDC簽訂容量協議，取得5.5MW的使用權，並表示有意在2026年11月前將承諾容量擴大至約18MW。

  ## Gorilla的策略定位

  董事長兼執行長Jay Chandan將這筆交易定位為公司從基礎建設布局走向簽約、可創造營收的實際營運的重要轉折。他表示：「這份合約是我們策略的重大驗證。這不是一個管線故事，也不是一種願景展望。這是白紙黑字的客戶需求、落實確認的資料中心容量、可執行的部署時程，以及具備融資可行性的經濟結構。」

  這家公司自我定位為AI基礎設施解決方案提供商，業務涵蓋智慧城市、網路基礎設施、影像分析、資安與物聯網（IoT）等領域。Gorilla表示，正積極拓展其所稱的「全球AI基礎設施執行平台」，目標市場鎖定南亞、東亞、中東及印度。

  ## 既有合約與整體業務管線

  印尼這筆交易是繼去年之後的又一重大斬獲。Gorilla去年已與新加坡雲端服務商Freyr簽署一份為期三年、總額14億美元的合約，共同開發AI驅動的資料中心網路。2026年4月，Gorilla另與印度的Yotta簽署協議，計畫在印度全境部署超過25,000張GPU。此外，該公司亦規劃在泰國開發一座容量達200MW的資料中心。

  ## 市場意義

  這份合約進一步印證了大規模GPUaaS承諾持續流向東南亞市場的趨勢。對於起家於安全情報與分析業務的Gorilla而言，這筆25億美元的交易標誌著其大規模進軍簽約式GPU運算領域的關鍵一步，同時也是一場考驗——驗證該公司能否在2027年中期這個緊湊的時間節點前，順利完成跨越多個階段的部署任務。

  Gorilla表示，第一、二階段以外的其餘容量，預計交付時程安排在2027年上半年。
