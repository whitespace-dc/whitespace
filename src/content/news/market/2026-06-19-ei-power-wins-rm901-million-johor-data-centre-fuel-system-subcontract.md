body_en: |
  EI Power Bhd, a Malaysian power engineering solutions provider listed on Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market less than a month ago, has won a RM90.1 million subcontract to deliver the full fuel system package for a data centre in Johor, the company announced on June 18.

  The contract was accepted by EI Power's wholly owned subsidiary, EI Power Technologies Sdn Bhd, from a main contractor described as principally involved in engineering, construction and investment holding. The contractor's identity was not disclosed, with both sources noting confidentiality obligations under the agreement.

  ## Scope and timeline

  The subcontract covers the complete fuel system package for the data centre, including supply, delivery, installation, testing and commissioning. Work commenced upon acceptance of the works subcontract agreement (WSA), with completion scheduled for September 2027.

  ## Order book impact

  The award more than doubled EI Power's unbilled order book — the value of contracted work not yet recognised as revenue — to RM222.5 million from RM99.9 million as of March 24, according to the company. The company said the contract is expected to contribute positively to earnings over the project period.

  ## Context: a first major win after listing

  EI Power was listed on the ACE Market of Bursa Malaysia on May 21, raising RM62.2 million in its initial public offering to fund expansion that includes a new headquarters, a Johor branch and entry into the Thai market. The Johor data centre subcontract is the company's first publicly announced major contract since that listing.

  Executive director and chief executive officer Albert Chang Wan Siong said the award affirmed industry recognition of the group's expertise in mission-critical power engineering. "Coming just weeks after our listing, it reflects the growth trajectory we aim to deliver for our shareholders," Chang said, according to the company's statement. He added that EI Power intends to pursue further contracts in data centres, renewable energy and other infrastructure segments.

  The company serves data centres, commercial, industrial and residential developments, and solar farms.

  ## Market position

  Chang described data centres as "one of the most exciting and structurally supported growth segments in Malaysia," citing structural demand drivers without specifying them. EI Power's shares closed down three sen, or 4.29 percent, at 67 sen on June 18, valuing the group at RM469 million.

  With its unbilled order book now at RM222.5 million and completion of the Johor project targeted for September 2027, EI Power's near-term revenue pipeline extends well beyond the capital raised at listing.

  EI Power Bhd 是一家在馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）ACE 市場掛牌不到一個月的電力工程解決方案供應商。該公司於6月18日宣布，旗下已贏得一份9,010萬令吉的分包合約，將為柔佛一座資料中心提供完整燃油系統套件。

  此合約由 EI Power 全資子公司 EI Power Technologies Sdn Bhd 承接，主承包商被描述為主要從事工程、建設及投資控股業務的企業。由於合約訂有保密義務，主承包商身分未予披露。

  ## 工程範疇與時程

  此分包合約涵蓋該資料中心的完整燃油系統套件，包含供應、交付、安裝、測試及試運轉等項目。工程自接受工程分包協議（WSA）後即行展開，預計於2027年9月竣工。

  ## 訂單簿影響

  此合約的到手使 EI Power 的未入帳訂單簿規模逾倍增長——即已簽約但尚未認列為營收的工程金額——從截至3月24日的9,990萬令吉大幅躍升至2億2,250萬令吉。公司表示，此合約預計將在專案執行期間對獲利帶來正面貢獻。

  ## 上市後首件重大得標

  EI Power 於5月21日在 Bursa Malaysia ACE 市場掛牌，透過首次公開募股（IPO）籌得6,220萬令吉，所得資金將用於擴張計畫，包括興建新總部、設立柔佛分公司，以及進軍泰國市場。柔佛資料中心分包合約是該公司上市後首件公開宣布的重大合約。

  執行董事兼執行長 Albert Chang Wan Siong 表示，此次得標印證了業界對集團在關鍵任務電力工程領域專業能力的肯定。「在我們上市後僅數週內便取得此合約，充分體現了我們致力為股東實現的成長軌跡，」張氏依據公司聲明表示。他補充指出，EI Power 將積極爭取資料中心、再生能源及其他基礎建設領域的後續合約。

  該公司的服務範疇涵蓋資料中心、商業、工業及住宅開發案，以及太陽能農場。

  ## 市場定位

  張氏將資料中心形容為「馬來西亞最令人振奮、且具結構性支撐的成長板塊之一」，並援引結構性需求驅動因素，惟未具體說明細節。EI Power 股價於6月18日收跌3仙（跌幅4.29%），報每股67仙，使集團市值達4億6,900萬令吉。

  隨著未入帳訂單簿規模達到2億2,250萬令吉，且柔佛專案完工目標定於2027年9月，EI Power 的近期營收管線已遠超其上市時所籌得的資本規模。
