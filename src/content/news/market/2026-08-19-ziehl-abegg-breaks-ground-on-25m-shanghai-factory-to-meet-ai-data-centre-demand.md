body_en: |
  German motor and fan manufacturer Ziehl-Abegg has broken ground on a €25m factory in Yexie Town, Shanghai, targeting an October 2027 opening and citing growing demand for energy-efficient ventilation systems used in artificial intelligence data centres.

  The new facility will replace Ziehl-Abegg's existing plant in neighbouring Xinbang Town, which the company built in 2010. Manufacturing floor space will grow from 40,000 square metres to 63,000 square metres, a 57.5 percent increase. The China workforce is expected to expand from approximately 700 employees today to more than 1,000 once the site is operational.

  Chairman and Chief Executive Joachim Ley attended the groundbreaking ceremony alongside local authority representatives, business partners, and employees.

  ## Capacity expansion tied to data centre ventilation demand

  Ziehl-Abegg said the investment is a direct response to increasing global demand for its energy-efficient ventilation technology, specifically products supporting AI infrastructure in data centres. The company said the new site will also strengthen its local research and development capabilities, allowing it to align products more closely with requirements of Chinese customers.

  The company currently operates eleven sales offices spanning all major economic regions of China in addition to its manufacturing presence.

  ## What the move signals

  The decision to replace rather than expand the existing Xinbang Town site — and to add roughly 300 jobs in the process — indicates Ziehl-Abegg views China as a long-term growth market for data centre cooling and ventilation components rather than a facility requiring only incremental adjustment. Locating R&D alongside production at the new Yexie Town site suggests the company intends to develop China-specific product variants rather than simply manufacture designs originating elsewhere.

  The Yexie Town factory is scheduled to begin operations in October 2027.
body_zh: |
  德國馬達與風扇製造商齊爾-阿貝格（Ziehl-Abegg）日前在上海葉謝鎮舉行新廠動土典禮，這座耗資2500萬歐元的生產基地鎖定2027年10月正式投產，並以全球對資料中心人工智慧基礎設施所需節能通風系統的需求持續成長，作為投資的主要理由。

  新廠將取代該公司位於鄰近新浜鎮、建於2010年的現有廠區。生產樓地板面積將從4萬平方公尺擴增至6萬3,000平方公尺，增幅達57.5%。廠區正式啟用後，中國員工人數預計從目前約700人成長至逾1,000人。

  董事長兼執行長約阿希姆·萊伊（Joachim Ley）親赴現場出席動土典禮，與會者包括當地政府代表、業務夥伴及員工。

  ## 產能擴張與資料中心通風需求掛鉤

  齊爾-阿貝格表示，此次投資是針對全球節能通風技術需求持續增長所作出的直接回應，所指產品尤以支援資料中心人工智慧基礎設施者為主。公司指出，新廠也將強化在地研發能力，使產品得以更緊密契合中國客戶的需求。

  除現有製造據點外，該公司目前在中國各主要經濟區設有11間業務辦事處。

  ## 此舉釋放的市場訊號

  決定以新廠取代現有新浜鎮廠區——而非就地擴建——並在此過程中新增約300個工作機會，顯示齊爾-阿貝格將中國定位為資料中心冷卻與通風零組件的長期成長市場，而非一座僅需小幅增量調整的生產據點。將研發功能與生產線同置於葉謝鎮新廠，意味著公司打算開發中國市場專屬的產品系列，而非單純在當地製造源自其他地區的設計。

  葉謝鎮新廠預計於2027年10月正式啟用。
