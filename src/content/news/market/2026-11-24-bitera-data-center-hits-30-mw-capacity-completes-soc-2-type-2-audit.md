  ## Bitera Data Center hits 30 MW capacity, completes SOC 2 Type 2 audit

  Bitera Data Center, a carrier-neutral facility in Jakarta's Kuningan business district, has reached 30 megawatts of capacity and completed a SOC 2 Type 2 audit as the company targets corporate customers amid growing demand for cloud computing, data processing, and artificial intelligence infrastructure, the company said Monday.

  The 30 MW figure marks a capacity milestone for the facility, which has been operating since February 2024. Carrier-neutral means the data center does not favor any single network or telecommunications provider, allowing customers to connect to multiple carriers.

  ## SOC 2 Type 2 audit

  A SOC 2 Type 2 audit — a standard developed by the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants that evaluates how a service organization manages data security and operational controls — differs from a point-in-time assessment by examining the consistency and effectiveness of controls over an extended period. Bitera said the audit is part of an effort to strengthen its operational governance against international standards and adds to certifications the company already holds.

  Chief Operating Officer Gary Robinson said the value of such audits lies in giving customers an independent basis for evaluation. "Certifications and audits give customers an objective way to assess a data center. For us, these standards are not just labels, but the way our team works every day, from operational procedures to maintenance and monitoring," Robinson said in a written statement.

  ## Power availability and SLA performance

  Bitera reported 100 percent power availability since it began operations, exceeding its own service-level agreement (SLA) commitment of 99.999 percent uptime — a figure that translates to no more than roughly five minutes of downtime per year. SLAs are contractual guarantees a provider makes to customers about minimum service performance.

  Robinson said the record extends beyond power. "That discipline shows in the results: since we started operating, we have never once failed to meet our three main SLAs — not just the power SLA," he said, without specifying the other two metrics.

  To maintain those figures, the company said it uses documented standard operating procedures, scheduled preventive maintenance, and round-the-clock monitoring through a Network Operations Center (NOC) — a centralized facility where technical staff track infrastructure performance continuously.

  ## Market context

  Bitera said the push to formalize governance credentials is a direct response to conditions in the Indonesian market, where enterprise customers are placing greater weight on data center reliability as they expand their use of cloud services, data analytics platforms, and AI workloads. The company said it is specifically targeting the corporate segment.

  The SOC 2 Type 2 completion and the 30 MW capacity figure position Bitera's next phase of business development toward larger enterprise contracts that typically require independently verified compliance documentation before procurement decisions are made.

  ## Bitera資料中心達30MW容量里程碑，完成SOC 2 Type 2稽核

  Bitera Data Center是一座位於印尼雅加達Kuningan商業區的電信中立資料中心，該公司週一宣布，機房容量已達到30百萬瓦（MW），並完成SOC 2 Type 2稽核認證。在雲端運算、資料處理及人工智慧基礎設施需求持續升溫的背景下，Bitera正積極鎖定企業客戶市場。

  30MW的容量標誌著這座自2024年2月起正式營運的機房達到了一個重要的里程碑。所謂「電信中立」，意指該資料中心不偏袒任何單一網路或電信業者，讓客戶得以自由接入多家電信商。

  ## SOC 2 Type 2稽核

  SOC 2 Type 2稽核是由美國注冊會計師協會（AICPA）所制定的標準，用以評估服務機構在資料安全與營運管控方面的能力。與僅針對特定時間點進行評估的方式不同，SOC 2 Type 2著重檢驗管控措施在一段較長時間內的一致性與執行成效。Bitera表示，取得此項稽核認證，是公司致力於依循國際標準強化營運治理的具體行動之一，也為其既有的認證資歷再添一筆。

  首席營運長Gary Robinson表示，這類稽核的價值在於為客戶提供獨立客觀的評估依據。「各種認證與稽核，讓客戶能以客觀的方式評量一座資料中心的水準。對我們而言，這些標準不只是貼在牆上的標籤，而是我們團隊每天的工作方式——從作業程序到維護與監控，皆是如此，」Robinson在書面聲明中說道。

  ## 電力可用率與服務水準協議執行成果

  Bitera表示，自開業以來電力供應可用率達到100%，超越公司本身所承諾的服務水準協議（SLA）——99.999%的正常運作時間，換算後每年允許的停機時間不超過約五分鐘。SLA是服務供應商對客戶所作出的最低服務水準承諾，具有契約效力。

  Robinson表示，這份亮眼的紀錄不只展現在電力供應上。「這樣的紀律反映在成果之中：自營運以來，我們從未有過任何一次未能達成三項主要SLA的情形——不僅僅是電力SLA，」他說，但未進一步說明其餘兩項指標的具體內容。

  為維持上述成績，公司表示採用了完整的標準作業程序文件、定期預防性維護計畫，以及透過網路營運中心（NOC）進行全天候不間斷監控。NOC是一個集中式設施，由技術人員持續追蹤與監測基礎設施的運作狀況。

  ## 市場背景

  Bitera表示，積極強化治理資歷的動力，直接來自印尼市場的現實需求——隨著企業客戶對雲端服務、資料分析平台及AI工作負載的應用日益深化，對資料中心可靠性的要求也愈來愈高。公司表示，目前的業務發展重心明確鎖定在企業客戶市場。

  SOC 2 Type 2稽核的完成，加上30MW的容量規模，使Bitera在下一階段的業務拓展中，能夠有效爭取規模更大的企業合約。這類合約在採購決策作成之前，通常需要供應商提供經第三方獨立驗證的合規文件，方能取得資格。
