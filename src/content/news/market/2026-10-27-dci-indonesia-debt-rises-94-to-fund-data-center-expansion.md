body_en: |
  PT DCI Indonesia Tbk (ticker: DCII), Indonesia's publicly listed data center operator, reported a 94.45% rise in total liabilities to Rp 5.13 trillion (approximately $313 million) as of September 30, 2026, up from Rp 2.64 trillion at the end of December 2025, according to the company's published financial statements.

  The increase was driven primarily by additional drawdowns on long-term loans, the company said. Those funds are being used for two purposes: completing the fit-out of remaining capacity in its JK6 data center building, and constructing a new data center building.

  ## What is driving the debt increase

  DCI Indonesia said the liabilities growth reflects the capital-intensive nature of expanding data center capacity. Fit-out — the process of installing power, cooling, and IT infrastructure inside a completed shell building — requires significant upfront expenditure before a facility generates revenue. The company is financing both that final fit-out work at JK6 and the construction of an additional facility through long-term borrowing.

  ## Significance

  The near-doubling of DCII's debt load in nine months signals an accelerated pace of capital deployment as the company moves to add capacity. For an operator in a market where demand is tied to cloud adoption and artificial intelligence workloads, the expansion trajectory indicates management expects lease-up to absorb the new supply. The completion of JK6's remaining capacity and the new building under construction will determine when that additional debt begins converting to revenue.

  ## DCI Indonesia負債激增94%，全力擴建資料中心

  PT DCI Indonesia Tbk（股票代號：DCII）是印尼唯一掛牌上市的資料中心業者。根據該公司公布的財務報告，截至2026年9月30日，總負債達5.13兆印尼盾（約合3.13億美元），較2025年12月底的2.64兆印尼盾大幅上升94.45%。

  公司表示，負債增加的主要原因是進一步動用長期貸款。這筆資金用於兩大用途：一是完成JK6資料中心機房剩餘容量的設備安裝工程，二是興建一棟全新的資料中心建築。

  ## 負債增加的原因

  DCI Indonesia指出，負債規模的成長反映出擴建資料中心容量所需投入的高額資本。所謂「設備安裝工程」（fit-out），是指在已完工的建築外殼內部，安裝電力、冷卻及資訊科技基礎設施的過程，這類工程需要在設施開始產生營收之前就投入大量前期資本支出。目前公司正透過長期借款，同時為JK6的最後階段設備安裝，以及另一棟新建設施的建設工程提供資金。

  ## 市場意義

  在短短九個月內，DCII的負債規模幾乎翻倍，顯示該公司正在加速部署資本，積極擴充機房容量。在雲端服務普及和人工智慧推論工作負載帶動需求的市場環境下，如此迅速的擴張步伐，意味著管理層預期新增的供給量將能順利租出。JK6剩餘容量的完工時程，以及在建新機房的落成進度，將決定這批新增負債何時能夠轉化為實際營收。
