body_en: |
  PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk. will prioritize building data centers at or near power generation sites rather than extending grid infrastructure to existing urban locations, the company's network director said Tuesday, as the state-owned telecoms operator responds to what it describes as a sharp acceleration in demand for artificial intelligence (AI) compute capacity.

  Budi Satria Dharma Purba, Director of Network at Telkom, made the remarks at Bali Tech Week 2026 on October 20. He said demand for data center capacity in Indonesia had risen dramatically over the past six months, and attributed the acceleration primarily to electricity costs that are approximately one-third of those in Singapore.

  "Most data centers are already occupied. There is very large demand queuing," Budi said, according to remarks reported by Bisnis.com.

  ## From tenant uncertainty to full occupancy

  Budi said that when Indonesia's current generation of data centers was first being developed, operators were uncertain whether they could secure tenants. That picture has reversed, he said, as power costs have risen across the region and made Indonesia comparatively attractive.

  The country's existing data center capacity is concentrated in a handful of locations: Batam — specifically Nongsa Digital Park — Jakarta, Surabaya, Cikarang in West Java, and Semarang. Budi said electricity supply in those areas, particularly across Java, is constrained.

  ## Building toward surplus power, not toward demand centers

  To address the supply constraint, Telkom is shifting its siting strategy. Rather than attempting to bring more power to established data center corridors, the company intends to locate future facilities in regions outside Java where electricity supply exceeds local demand.

  Budi described the approach as more efficient than constructing new transmission infrastructure to serve urban data center sites. "We have to choose to build closer to the energy source," he said.

  Telkom plans to deploy supporting connectivity networks alongside the new facilities to ensure low-latency performance for end users running compute-intensive AI inference workloads — that is, the real-time processing stage in which a trained AI model generates responses or predictions.

  ## Significance for the regional market

  The strategy reflects a broader tension emerging across Southeast Asia's data center sector: AI workloads require large, reliable blocks of power that incumbent urban grid infrastructure often cannot easily supply. Telkom's stated approach — locating compute infrastructure at the power source and running fiber to users, rather than running power cables to compute clusters — represents one operational response to that constraint.

  Budi said Telkom's focus going forward will be on sites selected for energy availability and proximity to generation, with data center construction then organized around those locations to reduce cost and power consumption.
body_zh: |
  PT Telkom Indonesia（Persero）Tbk. 的網路事業群負責人週二表示，公司將優先在發電廠或電力來源附近興建資料中心，而非將電網基礎設施延伸至既有的城市地點。這位國營電信業者的高層指出，人工智慧（AI）運算需求正急速加速成長，公司必須積極因應。

  網路事業群總監布迪·薩特里亞·達爾瑪·普爾巴（Budi Satria Dharma Purba）於10月20日在「峇里科技週2026」上發表上述談話。他表示，印尼資料中心容量的需求在過去六個月間已大幅攀升，並將這股加速趨勢主要歸因於電費——印尼電費約為新加坡的三分之一。

  「現有資料中心幾乎已全數客滿，排隊候位的需求十分龐大，」布迪表示。上述言論由Bisnis.com引述報導。

  ## 從無人問津到全數滿租

  布迪表示，當印尼這一世代的資料中心首次開發之際，業者曾對能否順利招租心存疑慮。如今局面已全然翻轉——隨著區域各地電費持續攀升，印尼在電力成本上相對具備競爭優勢，市場吸引力大幅提升。

  印尼現有的資料中心容量集中於少數幾個地點：廖內群島的巴淡島（特別是Nongsa Digital Park）、雅加達、泗水、西爪哇的Cikarang，以及三寶壟。布迪指出，前述地區、尤其是整個爪哇島的電力供應已趨於緊繃。

  ## 朝電力充沛之地布局，而非追著需求跑

  為解決供給瓶頸，Telkom正調整選址策略。公司計畫不再試圖為既有的資料中心廊帶引進更多電力，而是轉而在爪哇島以外、電力供給超出當地需求的區域設置新設施。

  布迪將這項做法形容為比興建新輸電基礎設施更具效率——後者的用途原本是服務城市地點的資料中心。「我們必須選擇更靠近電力來源的地方興建，」他說。

  Telkom計畫在新設施旁同步部署配套的連線網路，以確保終端使用者在執行運算密集型AI推論工作負載時能獲得低延遲的效能表現。所謂AI推論，是指已訓練完成的AI模型生成回應或預測結果的即時處理階段。

  ## 對區域市場的意義

  這項策略反映出東南亞資料中心產業正浮現的一個深層矛盾：AI工作負載需要大量且穩定的電力供應，而城市既有的電網基礎設施往往難以輕易滿足此一需求。Telkom所揭示的做法——將運算基礎設施設置於電力來源旁，再鋪設光纖連接終端使用者，而非將電纜牽引至運算叢集——正是針對這項限制所提出的一種實際因應之道。

  布迪表示，Telkom未來的重心將放在以能源可用性及鄰近發電設施為優先考量的選址地點，再以此為基礎規劃資料中心的興建，從而達到降低成本與減少耗電量的目標。
