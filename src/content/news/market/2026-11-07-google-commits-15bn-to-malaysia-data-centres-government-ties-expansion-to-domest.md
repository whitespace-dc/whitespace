body_en: |
  Google has committed US$15 billion to data centre and cloud infrastructure in Malaysia over 2027 and 2028, drawing a response from the country's Minister of Digital, who said the government would push to ensure the capital generates domestic technology creation and high-value industries rather than simply expanding computing capacity.

  The commitment, announced November 4, builds on an earlier US$2 billion investment Google announced in May 2024 to establish its first Malaysian data centre and Google Cloud region at Elmina Business Park in Selangor. The data centre is now operational; the Cloud Region is still being developed, according to Digital News Asia.

  ## Scale of the commitment

  At US$15 billion over two years, the deal is among the largest cloud and AI infrastructure investment announcements in Asia Pacific since the AI era began in November 2023, Digital News Asia reported. The figure surpasses Amazon Web Services' (AWS) US$12.7 billion India commitment and AWS's approximately US$13 billion Australia plan, as well as Oracle's more than US$6.5 billion Malaysia commitment, though it falls short of Microsoft's US$17.5 billion India commitment announced in December 2025. Other hyperscaler commitments within Malaysia include Microsoft at US$2.2 billion and AWS at US$6.2 billion.

  ## Government's conditions

  Gobind Singh Deo, Malaysia's Minister of Digital, wrote that the government's priority is not to celebrate inbound investment but to ensure every ringgit translates into economic opportunity, resource security, and domestic technological capability. He said the government wants researchers, developers, and companies to become creators of AI solutions, including what he called "Made by Malaysia" products and services, as part of a broader goal of making Malaysia an AI Nation by 2030.

  ## Energy and water infrastructure

  Google plans to bring more than one gigawatt of new clean energy capacity to Malaysia's national grid as part of the investment and will cover its own electricity consumption and direct grid connection costs, the minister said.

  Google is also investing in an off-river storage reservoir and a water treatment plant in Negeri Sembilan, along with river restoration and water replenishment initiatives. The government said these projects support water resilience and create opportunities for Malaysia's renewable energy, engineering, and water technology industries.

  ## Jobs and economic output

  Google is already working with more than 80 Malaysian suppliers across construction, engineering, network infrastructure, power, and cooling systems. The new campuses are projected to support more than 37,000 local jobs annually during construction and contribute an average of US$1.1 billion annually to Malaysia's GDP over 2027 and 2028, the minister said.

  Once operational, the facilities are expected to sustain more than 9,000 local jobs annually, with average wages 45 percent above the national median.

  ## Talent development

  Google has committed four new programmes to upskill approximately 800,000 Malaysians between 2027 and 2031, covering educators and youth, microentrepreneurs, civil servants, and vocational jobseekers. The company has also proposed Data Center Institutes to strengthen Malaysia's technical workforce. The Ministry of Digital said it will work with Google and relevant agencies to support the implementation of those institutes.

  ## Proposed AI partnerships

  Gobind said he invited Google during their meeting to develop a deeper strategic partnership with the Ministry of Digital in two areas: AI Cities and AI Safety. He described AI Cities as living laboratories for solutions addressing traffic congestion, flooding, and public service delivery. He also welcomed collaboration between Google's experts and the Malaysian AI Safety Institute, particularly in AI safety research and evaluation.

  The Ministry of Digital said further details of the proposed strategic partnership would be announced in due course.

  ## Google斥資150億美元布局馬來西亞資料中心，政府要求投資轉化為本土AI產業

  Google承諾於2027至2028年間投入150億美元，在馬來西亞建設資料中心與雲端基礎設施，成為亞太地區規模最大的同類投資宣告之一。馬來西亞數位部長對此作出回應，表示政府將積極推動確保這批資本能夠催生本土技術創造與高附加價值產業，而非單純擴充算力。

  此項承諾於11月4日正式宣布，是在Google早先投資計畫的基礎上進一步加碼——Google曾於2024年5月宣布斥資20億美元，在雪蘭莪州Elmina商業園區設立馬來西亞首座資料中心及Google Cloud區域。該資料中心目前已正式投入營運，Cloud區域則仍在建置中，據《Digital News Asia》報導。

  ## 投資規模

  據《Digital News Asia》報導，這筆為期兩年、總額150億美元的協議，是自2023年11月AI時代揭幕以來，亞太地區規模最大的雲端與AI基礎設施投資宣告之一。此金額超越了亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）對印度承諾的127億美元、AWS對澳洲承諾的約130億美元，以及甲骨文（Oracle）對馬來西亞承諾的逾65億美元；但仍不及微軟（Microsoft）於2025年12月宣布的175億美元印度投資計畫。其他超大規模雲端業者在馬來西亞的承諾金額方面，微軟為22億美元，AWS為62億美元。

  ## 政府的條件

  馬來西亞數位部長哥賓星·德奧（Gobind Singh Deo）表示，政府的優先要務並非慶祝外資的湧入，而是確保每一令吉都能轉化為經濟機遇、資源安全與本土技術能力。他說，政府希望研究人員、開發者與企業能夠成為AI解決方案的創造者，包括他所稱的「馬來西亞製造」（Made by Malaysia）產品與服務，這也是馬來西亞在2030年前成為「AI國家」這一宏觀目標的組成部分。

  ## 能源與水資源基礎設施

  數位部長表示，Google計畫透過本次投資，為馬來西亞國家電網導入逾一吉瓦（GW）的新增潔淨能源裝置容量，並自行承擔電力消耗費用及直接併網的相關成本。

  Google亦將在森美蘭州投資建設一座離河儲水庫及一座水處理廠，同時推動河川復育與水資源補注計畫。政府表示，這些項目有助於強化馬來西亞的水資源韌性，並為再生能源、工程及水科技產業創造商機。

  ## 就業與經濟產出

  Google目前已與逾80家馬來西亞本土供應商展開合作，涵蓋建築、工程、網路基礎設施、電力及冷卻系統等領域。數位部長表示，新建園區預計在施工期間每年支撐逾3萬7千個在地工作機會，並於2027至2028年間平均每年為馬來西亞GDP貢獻11億美元。

  設施正式投入運營後，預計每年將維持逾9千個在地就業機會，且平均薪資高出全國中位數45%。

  ## 人才培育

  Google承諾推出四項新計畫，於2027至2031年間為約80萬名馬來西亞人提供技能提升培訓，涵蓋教育工作者與青少年、微型創業者、公務人員及職訓求職者。Google亦提出設立「資料中心學院」，以強化馬來西亞的技術人才體系。數位部表示，將與Google及相關機構攜手合作，支持這些學院的落實推動。

  ## 擬議中的AI夥伴關係

  哥賓星表示，他在雙方會面期間邀請Google與數位部在兩大領域建立更深層的策略夥伴關係：「AI城市」（AI Cities）與「AI安全」（AI Safety）。他將AI城市描述為解決交通壅塞、洪患與公共服務效能等課題的活體實驗室。他也歡迎Google專家與馬來西亞AI安全研究院（Malaysian AI Safety Institute）展開合作，尤其是在AI安全研究與評估領域。

  數位部表示，上述擬議策略夥伴關係的進一步細節，將於適當時機對外公布。
