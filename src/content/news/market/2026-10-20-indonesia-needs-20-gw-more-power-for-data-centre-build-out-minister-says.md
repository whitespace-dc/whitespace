  ## Indonesia needs 20 GW more power for data centre build-out, minister says

  Indonesia must add 20 gigawatts (GW) of electricity generation capacity to meet data centre demand, Coordinating Minister for Downstream Industries and Energy Transition Bahlil Lahadalia said on Monday, a figure he described as equivalent to one-fifth of the country's entire existing installed capacity.

  Bahlil made the statement at Bali Tech Week 2026 after discussions with the president director of state utility PLN (Perusahaan Listrik Negara). "That is equal to 20,000 megawatts. That is the same as 20 percent of total existing electricity today. This is not a small amount — it is quite large," he said, according to an official statement.

  ## AI workloads driving step-change in power demand

  Bahlil attributed the scale of the requirement to the shift from conventional cloud computing to artificial intelligence (AI)-oriented data centres. Cloud-era facilities typically consume between 10 and 30 megawatts (MW) each, he said. AI-focused data centres can require between 100 MW and 1 GW — a ten- to one-hundred-fold increase per facility.

  Bahlil, who also holds the position of Minister of Energy and Mineral Resources, said that without adequate energy supply, Indonesia's digitalization push would create new problems rather than solve existing ones. He framed clean energy readiness as the central condition for attracting regional-scale data centre investment.

  ## Batam, Karimun, and Bintan designated as new hubs

  The government is positioning the Riau Islands — specifically Batam, Karimun, and Bintan — as Indonesia's new data centre hub locations, intended to complement existing hubs in Malaysia and Singapore. Bahlil cited advantages including water availability, lower population density compared with West Java, Jakarta, or Bali, and potential for renewable energy development.

  Batam has already accumulated significant commitments. Data centre capacity that is operational, under contract, or in development in the city totals 1.47 GW, Bahlil said. PLN Batam and data centre operator DayOne have signed a power supply contract for 450 MW, which he described as the largest such contract for a data centre in Indonesia.

  ## PLN grid plan and solar buildout

  To meet the broader power requirement, PLN's RUPTL (Rencana Usaha Penyediaan Tenaga Listrik, or Electricity Supply Business Plan) 2025–2034 targets 69.5 GW of new generation capacity. Of that, 42.6 GW is earmarked for renewable energy sources, 10.3 GW for energy storage systems, and 16.6 GW for fossil fuel generation. The plan carries a potential investment value of Rp 2,967.4 trillion.

  Separately, the government is developing approximately 100 gigawatt-peak (GWp) of solar photovoltaic capacity across formats including utility-scale, floating, rooftop, and industrial-zone installations. Bahlil said he expects data centre investors to prefer renewable energy sources, and described that preference as a business opportunity for Indonesia.

  ## Investor requirements and local partnership rules

  The government intends to require prospective data centre investors to submit site location plans so that energy supply infrastructure can be prepared in advance, Bahlil said. It is also developing regulations that would oblige foreign investors to partner with domestic and regional businesses. "We must share with local entrepreneurs. Not everything should be done by them alone," he said.

  The combination of the 20 GW demand figure, the RUPTL capacity targets, and the Batam hub strategy indicates that Indonesia is positioning its energy and land infrastructure as a competitive differentiator for data centre investment across Southeast Asia. The government's next step, per Bahlil, is formalizing the investor location-reporting requirement and the local partnership rule.

  ## 印尼部長：資料中心擴建需額外增加20GW電力

  印尼下游產業與能源轉型協調部長巴赫利·拉哈達利亞（Bahlil Lahadalia）週一表示，印尼必須新增20吉瓦（GW）的發電裝置容量，才能滿足資料中心的用電需求。他形容這個數字相當於全國現有總裝置容量的五分之一。

  巴赫利在2026年峇里科技週（Bali Tech Week 2026）與國營電力公司PLN（Perusahaan Listrik Negara）總裁董事進行討論後，發表了上述聲明。根據官方聲明，他說：「這相當於20,000百萬瓦，也就是目前全國總電力的20%。這不是一個小數目——規模相當龐大。」

  ## 人工智慧工作負載推動電力需求大幅躍升

  巴赫利將如此龐大的需求歸因於從傳統雲端運算轉向以人工智慧（AI）為導向的資料中心這一結構性轉變。他表示，雲端運算時代的設施通常每座耗電10至30百萬瓦（MW）；而以AI為核心的資料中心，每座所需電力可達100 MW至1 GW，等同單一設施的用電量暴增十至百倍。

  巴赫利同時兼任能源與礦產資源部長，他指出，若無充足的能源供應，印尼的數位化推進非但無法解決既有問題，反而會衍生新的困境。他強調，潔淨能源的整備程度，是吸引區域規模資料中心投資的核心前提條件。

  ## 巴淡島、卡里蒙及賓坦島定位為新樞紐

  印尼政府正將廖內群島——具體而言是巴淡島（Batam）、卡里蒙（Karimun）及賓坦島（Bintan）——定位為印尼新的資料中心樞紐，目標是與馬來西亞及新加坡現有的樞紐形成互補。巴赫利列舉當地的優勢，包括水資源充沛、人口密度遠低於西爪哇、雅加達或峇里島，以及具備龐大的再生能源開發潛力。

  巴淡島方面已累積相當可觀的承諾投資。巴赫利表示，該城市目前已運營、簽約或在建的資料中心容量合計達1.47 GW。PLN巴淡與資料中心業者DayOne已簽署一份450 MW的電力供應合約，他形容這是迄今印尼針對資料中心簽署的規模最大的此類合約。

  ## PLN電網規劃與太陽能建設

  為滿足整體電力需求，PLN的RUPTL（Rencana Usaha Penyediaan Tenaga Listrik，即電力供應事業計畫）2025至2034年的目標是新增69.5 GW的裝置容量。其中42.6 GW將用於再生能源、10.3 GW用於儲能系統、16.6 GW用於化石燃料發電，全案潛在投資金額估達2,967.4兆印尼盾（Rp 2,967.4兆）。

  另一方面，政府正開發約100吉瓦峰值（GWp）的太陽能光伏裝置容量，涵蓋公用規模型、浮動型、屋頂型及工業園區型等多種形式。巴赫利表示，他預期資料中心投資者將傾向選用再生能源，並將此一偏好形容為印尼的商機所在。

  ## 投資者要求與本地合作規定

  巴赫利表示，政府擬要求有意進駐的資料中心投資者事先提交選址計畫，以便提前備妥能源供應基礎設施。政府同時也在研擬相關法規，要求外國投資者必須與本地及區域業者進行合作。他說：「我們必須與本地業者共享商機。不能什麼事都讓他們一手包辦。」

  綜合20 GW的電力需求規模、RUPTL裝置容量目標以及巴淡島樞紐策略來看，印尼正積極將自身的能源與土地基礎設施定位為東南亞資料中心投資的競爭利器。按巴赫利的說法，政府的下一步是將投資者選址申報要求及本地合作規定正式入法。
