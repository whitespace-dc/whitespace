body_en: |
  NEXTDC, the Australian data center operator, has signed new senior debt facilities totaling A$2.3 billion ($1.60 billion) to fund capital expenditure tied to recent customer contract wins and ongoing data center developments, the company announced Friday.

  The new facilities represent an A$500 million increase to the A$1.8 billion in commitments NEXTDC announced in May. Upon financial close, expected in mid-July, the company's total available senior debt facilities will rise from A$6.4 billion to A$8.7 billion.

  "The upsize reflects continued strong support from a broad syndicate of domestic and international banks," NEXTDC said.

  ## Contracted demand driving expansion

  The expanded borrowing capacity follows a sharp rise in contracted customer demand. NEXTDC said in April that its pro-forma contracted utilisation — the total power capacity formally signed up by customers — had jumped by roughly 60% to 667 megawatts as of March 31, compared with end-December.

  Financial close of the new facilities is expected to occur in mid-July, the company said.
body_zh: |
  澳洲資料中心業者NEXTDC於週五宣布，已簽署總額23億澳元（約16億美元）的新增優先債務融資，用以支應近期客戶合約簽署及持續推進中的資料中心建設所需資本支出。

  此次新增融資規模，較NEXTDC於五月宣布的18億澳元承諾額度再上調5億澳元。待預計於七月中旬完成的金融結算後，公司可用優先債務融資總額將從64億澳元提升至87億澳元。

  NEXTDC表示：「此次擴大融資規模，反映出國內外廣泛銀行財團持續給予的強力支持。」

  ## 合約需求驅動擴張

  此次借貸規模擴大，緣於合約客戶需求的急遽攀升。NEXTDC於四月指出，截至三月三十一日，公司的備考合約使用率——即客戶正式簽約的電力容量總和——相較去年十二月底已大幅躍升約60%，達到667百萬瓦。

  公司表示，新增融資的金融結算預計將於七月中旬完成。
