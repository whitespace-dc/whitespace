body_en: |
  Memory chipmaker CXMT overtook Tencent Holdings as China's most valuable company on August 13, surpassing half a trillion dollars in market capitalization in a milestone that analysts say reflects a structural rotation in Chinese equity markets away from consumer internet platforms and toward hardware companies aligned with Beijing's strategic priorities.

  CXMT, which supplies the fast memory chips required for artificial intelligence infrastructure, has risen more than 500 percent since its Shanghai stock market debut three weeks ago. On August 17, its shares climbed as much as 11.5 percent in a single session, far outpacing gains of roughly 2 percent for both Tencent and Alibaba in Hong Kong.

  ## Market weight shifts from software to hardware

  The shift is visible at the index level. China's chip-heavy Star 50 Index rose about 28 percent in 2026, while the Hang Seng Tech Index — which counts Alibaba and Tencent among its largest constituents — shed nearly 15 percent over the same period, according to the report.

  Data compiled by Bloomberg show that hardware's weighting in the MSCI China All Shares Net Total Return Index briefly overtook both retail and software sectors in 2026. Hardware climbed from under 3 percent of the index six years ago to more than 12 percent in August. Online retailers such as Alibaba and JD.com saw their combined weight fall from more than 15 percent in 2020 to roughly 7 percent.

  That divergence has come at the direct expense of China's once-dominant consumer internet companies. Alibaba, even with a pivot toward artificial intelligence, was down about 16 percent in 2026. Tencent had shed close to 27 percent of its market value. Xiaomi, which had benefited from a well-received electric-vehicle push, was down more than a third as of last week.

  ## The 2020 turning point

  For much of the previous decade, Tencent, Alibaba, and other mobile-era giants drew the bulk of investor attention at the expense of state-backed industrial companies. That began to change in 2020, when Beijing moved against an increasingly powerful private sector, a regulatory crackdown that analysts say set in motion the market-share shift that culminated in CXMT's ascent.

  Big tech names have again largely underperformed in 2026, hurt by prolonged economic and consumer weakness, according to the report.

  "Look at Tencent's cashflow — it's gone negative, and a lot of it is going to the hardware guys like CXMT," said Leonid Mironov, a Hong Kong-based fund manager at Gavekal Capital. "This is the dynamic in the US as well. At some point, the platforms will have to show that they can make money off this. For hardware guys, they are showing it right now."

  ## Beijing's industrial priorities as a valuation driver

  The government's role in directing capital toward specific sectors amplifies a dynamic already present in global AI markets, where demand for chips and memory has outpaced returns on the software side. Beijing has designated AI infrastructure and robotics as key industrial priorities, and President Xi Jinping has called publicly for game-changing breakthroughs in technology, against the backdrop of intensifying rivalry with the United States.

  "Valuations are propped up by the idea that self-sufficiency will create a larger total addressable market for those companies," said Jing Jie Yu, an analyst at Morningstar. "Regulatory uplift or support becomes a consistent valuation push factor for these companies."

  The report notes that China has narrowed the gap with the United States in AI model development and inference-processing chips, aided by companies including DeepSeek and Huawei Technologies. Beijing is seeking further advances in advanced chipmaking for data centers and cognitive AI for humanoid robots, areas it has identified as needing new domestic champions.

  ## IPO pipeline reflects hardware focus

  The AI-driven demand for hardware is also reshaping China's initial public offering market. Days before CXMT's market capitalization milestone, humanoid robotics firm Unitree Robotics completed an IPO that drew retail subscription demand more than 5,500 times the shares available in the tranche reserved for individual investors. Unitree was expected to begin trading in Shanghai as early as this week.

  "China's AI build-out will increasingly define equity and IPO leadership across Hong Kong and mainland Chinese markets," said Francis Chan, a senior analyst at Bloomberg Intelligence. "IPO pipelines are AI-led, featuring firms like Moonshot AI, Yangtze Memory, Shein and Unitree Robotics."

  ## Investor caution on volatility

  Analysts and fund managers caution that the current hardware premium is not guaranteed to hold. Tech stocks have proven volatile in the AI era, and sentiment can shift quickly. Alibaba alone has cycled through periods of optimism and skepticism over its high level of AI spending, which has alternately been viewed as a drag on near-term profits or as a long-term foundation.

  "If you look at the tech cycle, it is always the hardware set that goes first before the software set can come in," said Kelvin Tay, chief investment officer for Asia at Pictet Wealth Management. "I'm quite sure that at some point, this move into software will come back."

  For now, investor conviction is concentrated on hardware suppliers with clearer near-term revenue visibility. Unitree Robotics' trading debut in Shanghai this week will serve as a near-term test of how far that appetite extends.
body_zh: |
  記憶體晶片製造商長鑫存儲（CXMT）於8月13日超越騰訊控股，成為中國市值最高的企業，市值突破5,000億美元大關。分析師指出，此一里程碑反映出中國股市正從消費網路平台向配合北京戰略優先方向的硬體製造商進行結構性板塊輪動。

  長鑫存儲供應人工智慧基礎設施所需的高速記憶體晶片，自三週前在上海股市掛牌以來，股價已累計上漲逾500%。8月17日單日盤中最高漲幅達11.5%，遠超騰訊與阿里巴巴同日在香港市場約2%的漲幅。

  ## 軟體讓位硬體，指數結構性輪動

  這波板塊輪動在指數層面清晰可見。以半導體股為重心的科創50指數（Star 50 Index）2026年累計上漲約28%，而涵蓋阿里巴巴、騰訊等大型科技股的恆生科技指數，同期卻下跌近15%。

  彭博彙整的數據顯示，2026年硬體類股在MSCI中國全股票淨總報酬指數中的權重，一度短暫超越零售及軟體板塊。硬體類股在指數中的佔比從六年前不足3%，攀升至8月的逾12%。阿里巴巴、京東等網路零售商的合計權重，則從2020年的逾15%下滑至約7%。

  這一分歧正是以中國昔日稱霸一方的消費網路巨頭為代價換來的。阿里巴巴即便已轉型押注人工智慧，2026年至今仍下跌約16%；騰訊市值縮水近27%；小米曾因電動車業務廣獲市場肯定而一度受惠，但截至上週跌幅已逾三分之一。

  ## 2020年的轉折點

  過去十年的大部分時間裡，騰訊、阿里巴巴及其他行動網路時代的巨頭吸引了大量投資人目光，國家支持的工業型企業則相對受到冷落。這一格局從2020年開始改變——北京啟動了針對坐大民營科技業的整治行動，分析師認為正是這場監管風暴，觸發了此後持續至今、最終催生長鑫存儲崛起的市場份額轉移。

  報告指出，大型科技公司2026年再度普遍跑輸大市，主要受到持續低迷的經濟環境與消費疲弱拖累。

  嘉維爾資本（Gavekal Capital）駐香港基金經理Leonid Mironov表示：「看看騰訊的現金流——已經轉負了，大量資金正流向長鑫存儲這類硬體業者。美國市場也在上演同樣的劇本。平台業者終究得證明能從中獲利，而硬體業者現在正做到這一點。」

  ## 北京產業優先政策成為估值驅動力

  政府對特定產業定向引導資本的角色，進一步放大了全球人工智慧市場原本就存在的動態——晶片與記憶體的需求成長，已超越軟體端的投資報酬表現。北京已將人工智慧基礎設施與機器人列為重點產業優先項目，習近平主席也公開呼籲在科技領域實現顛覆性突破，此一背景正是中美科技競爭日趨激烈之際。

  晨星（Morningstar）分析師Jing Jie Yu表示：「估值之所以獲得支撐，在於市場預期自給自足政策將為這些企業創造更大的潛在市場規模。監管層面的扶持或政策支援，已成為推升此類企業估值的持續性因素。」

  報告指出，在DeepSeek、華為技術等企業的協助下，中國在人工智慧模型開發及推論處理晶片領域已大幅縮小與美國的差距。北京正進一步謀求資料中心先進製程晶片及人形機器人認知人工智慧的突破，並已點名這兩個領域亟需培育新一代本土龍頭企業。

  ## IPO管線反映硬體優先趨勢

  人工智慧驅動的硬體需求浪潮，也正在重塑中國的首次公開募股（IPO）市場。就在長鑫存儲市值創下里程碑的數日前，人形機器人廠商宇樹科技（Unitree Robotics）完成IPO，面向散戶投資人的公開申購倍數逾5,500倍。宇樹科技最快本週即可在上海掛牌交易。

  彭博行業研究（Bloomberg Intelligence）資深分析師Francis Chan表示：「中國的人工智慧建設熱潮，將越來越主導香港及中國大陸市場的股市走勢與IPO格局。IPO管線由人工智慧主導，代表企業包括月之暗面（Moonshot AI）、長江存儲、Shein及宇樹科技。」

  ## 投資人對波動性保持謹慎

  分析師與基金經理提醒，目前硬體類股享有的估值溢價並不保證持續。人工智慧時代的科技股波動性已有目共睹，市場情緒可能急速逆轉。以阿里巴巴為例，外界對其高額人工智慧支出的看法一再反覆，時而視之為壓縮近期獲利的包袱，時而視之為奠定長期競爭力的基石。

  百達財富管理（Pictet Wealth Management）亞洲區首席投資長Kelvin Tay表示：「回顧科技業的每一輪週期，始終是硬體先行，軟體再跟上。我相當確信，軟體這一波行情終究會回來。」

  就目前而言，投資人的信心集中在近期營收能見度更為明確的硬體供應商。宇樹科技本週在上海的掛牌首日表現，將成為市場檢驗這股買盤熱情能延伸多遠的近期試金石。
