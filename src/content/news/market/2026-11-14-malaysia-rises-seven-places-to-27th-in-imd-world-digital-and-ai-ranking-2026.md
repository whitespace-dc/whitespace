body_en: |
  Malaysia moved from 34th to 27th place in the IMD World Digital and AI Ranking 2026, a seven-position gain the Malaysian government described as reflecting collective efforts across government, industry, academia, and the public in building out digital and artificial intelligence capabilities.

  The IMD World Digital and AI Ranking is an annual index published by the International Institute for Management Development that measures countries' readiness to compete in the digital and AI economy. Malaysia's latest result places it 27th among the ranked countries.

  ## Drivers of the advance

  The Ministry of Digital credited three developments for underpinning the improvement: significant foreign and domestic investment in data centres and AI compute infrastructure; expanded digital and AI adoption programmes; and the establishment of AI Malaysia, a dedicated national institution created to coordinate the country's AI transformation agenda.

  The government said the result reinforces its confidence in two existing policy frameworks: the Malaysia Digital Action Plan 2030 and the National AI Action Plan 2026–2030.

  ## Four priorities for the next phase

  Alongside the ranking announcement, the Ministry of Digital set out four priorities it said would guide the next phase of transformation.

  The first is maximising the economic value of infrastructure investments already made — specifically, developing industries, technologies, and businesses that build on top of data centre and compute assets so that Malaysian companies, researchers, and developers can access AI tools and create what the ministry called "Made by Malaysia" products and services.

  The second is accelerating digital and AI adoption across the economy, with particular emphasis on micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises, known as MSMEs. The ministry said it would also advance digital government transformation to deliver more efficient public services.

  The third priority is strengthening talent and innovation. The ministry said its improved ranking in workforce development was encouraging and that it intends to expand the pipeline of AI engineers, researchers, developers, and entrepreneurs, while also upskilling and reskilling workers across industries.

  The fourth is building digital trust and governance. The Ministry of Digital said it is developing an AI Governance Bill intended to provide clarity and accountability in AI development and deployment. It is also working to establish a Malaysia AI Safety Institute under AI Malaysia, focused on AI safety evaluation, risk assessment, research, and standards development.

  ## Regulatory and governance work underway

  Beyond the AI Governance Bill, the ministry said it is strengthening data governance, cybersecurity, and regulatory coordination, with the stated aims of encouraging innovation, sustaining investor confidence, and protecting citizens' interests.

  ## What the ranking gain signals

  For the broader data centre and digital infrastructure sector, the seven-place advance confirms Malaysia's growing position as a destination for compute investment in Southeast Asia, while the government's four-priority framework signals continued policy support for infrastructure utilisation and AI-stack development. The establishment of a dedicated AI safety institute indicates Kuala Lumpur is moving toward a formal national AI governance structure alongside its build-out of physical infrastructure.

  The Ministry of Digital said it aims to realise its ambition of making Malaysia an inclusive and sustainable AI nation by 2030, with the AI Governance Bill and the Malaysia AI Safety Institute under AI Malaysia among the next concrete steps on that path.

  馬來西亞在IMD 2026年世界數位與AI排名中從第34名躍升至第27名，進步七個名次。馬來西亞政府表示，這一成果反映了政府、產業、學術界與民眾，在共同建構數位與人工智慧能力方面的集體努力。

  IMD世界數位與AI排名由國際管理發展學院每年發布，評估各國在數位與AI經濟領域的競爭準備程度。馬來西亞此次名列第27，位居所有受評國家之中。

  ## 推動排名進步的關鍵因素

  數位部將此次進步歸功於三項發展：外資與國內業者在資料中心及AI運算基礎設施的大規模投入；數位與AI應用推廣計畫的擴大；以及「AI馬來西亞」（AI Malaysia）的成立——這是一個專責國家機構，負責統籌全國AI轉型議程。

  政府表示，這項成果強化了對兩套現行政策框架的信心：《2030年馬來西亞數位行動計畫》及《2026至2030年國家AI行動計畫》。

  ## 下一階段的四大優先要務

  伴隨排名公布，數位部同步提出四大優先要務，作為引導下一階段轉型的施政方向。

  第一項是最大化現有基礎設施投資的經濟效益。具體而言，是在資料中心與運算資產之上，發展相關產業、技術與商業模式，讓馬來西亞企業、研究人員及開發者得以取用AI工具，並打造數位部所稱的「馬來西亞製造」（Made by Malaysia）產品與服務。

  第二項是加速全經濟體的數位與AI應用，尤其著重於微型企業及中小企業（MSMEs）。數位部表示，同時也將推動數位政府轉型，以提供更有效率的公共服務。

  第三項優先要務是強化人才培育與創新。數位部表示，勞動力發展排名的提升令人振奮，並計畫擴大AI工程師、研究人員、開發者及創業者的人才供給管道，同時協助各產業從業人員提升技能、進行轉型再培訓。

  第四項是建立數位信任與治理。數位部表示，正在研擬一部《AI治理法》，旨在為AI的開發與部署提供明確規範與課責機制；同時也在推動於「AI馬來西亞」架構下設立「馬來西亞AI安全研究院」，專注於AI安全評估、風險評估、研究與標準制定。

  ## 監管與治理工作持續推進

  除《AI治理法》之外，數位部表示正在強化資料治理、資安防護及法規協調工作，目標是鼓勵創新、維繫投資人信心，並保障公民權益。

  ## 排名躍升所傳遞的訊號

  就更廣泛的資料中心與數位基礎設施產業而言，七個名次的躍升確認了馬來西亞在東南亞地區作為運算投資目的地的日益重要性；政府提出的四大優先框架，也向外界發出持續支持基礎設施運用與AI技術堆疊發展的政策信號。專責AI安全研究院的設立，則顯示吉隆坡正在推動實體基礎設施建設的同時，逐步邁向建構正式的國家AI治理架構。

  數位部表示，致力於在2030年前實現讓馬來西亞成為具包容性與永續性AI國家的願景，《AI治理法》及「AI馬來西亞」架構下的「馬來西亞AI安全研究院」，將是推進此一路徑的下一個具體步驟。
