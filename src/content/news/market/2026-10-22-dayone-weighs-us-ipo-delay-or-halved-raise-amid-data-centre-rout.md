body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers is weighing whether to delay or sharply reduce the size of its planned US initial public offering as a broad deterioration in sentiment toward listed data centre companies undermines the market for new listings in the sector, according to people familiar with the matter.

  The Singapore-based company had originally targeted raising as much as US$5 billion from a US listing as soon as the fourth quarter, at a valuation of roughly US$20 billion, the people said. It is now considering a deal that would raise US$2.5 billion to US$3 billion — roughly half the original target — or deferring the sale until conditions improve. Deliberations are ongoing and no final decision has been made, the people said, asking not to be identified because the information is private. DayOne declined to comment.

  ## IPO timeline and structure

  DayOne filed for a US IPO earlier in October. The offering is led by Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America and Citigroup. IFR reported on Oct. 19 that DayOne was gathering additional investor feedback before launching the sale. The company is continuing to engage with potential investors and still intends to move forward as soon as the market allows, the people said; a launch in the coming weeks remains possible.

  The company's backers include Coatue, Hillhouse, GDS Holdings, the SoftBank Vision Fund and Citadel's Ken Griffin. DayOne has been raising billions of dollars in private funding rounds to finance its construction pipeline.

  ## A sector-wide listing slump

  The data centre sector drew strong global investor demand as companies raced to build capacity for artificial intelligence workloads, but a reversal in sentiment is now feeding directly into the IPO market. Firmus Grid, an Australian data centre operator, pulled its roughly US$5 billion IPO after investors pushed back on its valuation. AirTrunk, another data centre company, delayed a planned real estate investment trust (REIT) IPO — a structure that pools property assets and passes income to shareholders — in Singapore to 2027. South Korean digital infrastructure company Elice fell 32% on its Seoul trading debut on Oct. 20. Shares of Australian data centre operator Nextdc dropped sharply on Oct. 21 after the company cut earnings guidance. Cloud infrastructure provider CoreWeave's shares have more than halved since their May peak, and Nscale is facing pushback over its target IPO valuation, Bloomberg News has reported.

  ## Analyst: size down, lean on predictable cash flows

  Stephen Yin, an analyst at Bloomberg Intelligence, said DayOne should downsize its raise to US$2.5 billion to US$3 billion, adjusting its underlying valuation to offer a discount sufficient to ensure order book stability while keeping the company's multibillion-dollar construction pipeline funded. He said investors may be more receptive to DayOne than to so-called neoclouds — companies such as Firmus and Elice that rent out access to graphics processing unit (GPU) chip compute capacity — which carry higher execution risk. DayOne, by contrast, leases physical data centre space under long-term contracts, generating what Yin called "highly predictable cash flows." He described DayOne as offering a "vastly superior growth and scale profile" relative to those peers.

  ## What market participants are watching

  Some investors are hoping volatility will ease following the US midterm elections on Nov. 3. They are also monitoring the performance of artificial intelligence company Anthropic's planned listing as a gauge of broader sentiment toward the AI sector.

  DayOne operates or is developing data centres in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Hong Kong, Japan, Finland and Spain.

  ## IPO時程與架構

  DayOne已於10月初向主管機關申報美國IPO文件，此次發行由摩根士丹利、摩根大通、美國銀行及花旗集團擔任主承銷商。《國際融資評論》（IFR）於10月19日報導，DayOne正進一步蒐集投資人意見，再決定是否正式啟動發行程序。知情人士表示，該公司仍持續與潛在投資人接觸，並有意在市況允許時盡快推進；未來數週內啟動發行仍屬可能。

  DayOne的股東包括Coatue、高瓴（Hillhouse）、GDS控股、軟銀願景基金，以及城堡（Citadel）創辦人肯·葛里芬（Ken Griffin）。DayOne已透過多輪私募融資籌得數十億美元，用以支應旗下規模龐大的建設管線。

  ## 資料中心類股上市全面低迷

  資料中心產業先前受惠於企業爭相建置人工智慧（AI）運算基礎設施的熱潮，吸引全球投資人競相追捧；然而，市場情緒的逆轉如今已直接衝擊IPO市場。澳洲資料中心業者Firmus Grid在投資人對其估值強烈反彈後，撤回了約50億美元的IPO計畫。另一家資料中心業者AirTrunk也將原定在新加坡進行的不動產投資信託（REITs）上市計畫推遲至2027年——REITs是一種匯集不動產資產、並將收益分配給受益人的架構。南韓數位基礎設施公司Elice於10月20日在首爾掛牌，首日即重挫32%。澳洲資料中心業者Nextdc的股價於10月21日大幅下跌，起因是該公司下修盈餘預測。雲端基礎設施供應商CoreWeave的股價自5月高點以來已腰斬有餘；彭博新聞亦報導，Nscale正面臨投資人對其IPO目標估值的強烈質疑。

  ## 分析師建議：縮減規模、聚焦穩定現金流

  彭博資訊分析師Stephen Yin表示，DayOne應將募資規模縮減至25億至30億美元，同時調整基礎估值、提供足夠折讓，以確保認購簿穩定，並維繫公司數十億美元建設管線的資金需求。他指出，相較於所謂的「新雲端」業者——即以租用繪圖處理器（GPU）晶片運算資源為主要業務的公司，例如Firmus和Elice，此類業者的執行風險較高——投資人或許對DayOne更為青睞。DayOne的業務模式有所不同，採長期合約出租實體資料中心空間，因此產生Yin所稱的「高度可預期現金流」。他形容DayOne相較於上述同業，擁有「遠為優越的成長潛力與規模優勢」。

  ## 市場參與者的觀察焦點

  部分投資人期望11月3日美國期中選舉過後，市場波動能夠趨於緩和。他們同時也在密切觀察人工智慧公司Anthropic的上市計畫，將其視為衡量AI產業整體市場情緒的重要指標。
