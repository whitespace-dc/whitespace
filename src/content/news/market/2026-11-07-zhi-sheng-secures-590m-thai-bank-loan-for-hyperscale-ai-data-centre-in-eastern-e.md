body_en: |
  Zhi Sheng Group has secured a USD $590 million loan facility from a Thai bank to finance construction of Haoyang Data Centre 1 (HYDC1), a hyperscale artificial intelligence data centre in Thailand's Eastern Economic Corridor, the Hong Kong-listed company announced.

  The loan carries a term of more than seven years. Zhi Sheng said the land title deed for HYDC1 has been secured, core utility connections — water and power — are substantially complete, and the project has entered substantive construction. The facility spans approximately 140,302 square metres within the government-backed development zone.

  ## Facility scale and delivery schedule

  HYDC1 is planned in three phases. First-phase delivery is scheduled to begin in early 2027, with completion targeted within six months of that start date. That initial phase is expected to represent roughly one-third of HYDC1's total planned capacity. Zhi Sheng said phases two and three will advance in line with customer requirements and market conditions.

  ## Revenue anchor already in place

  Before the loan announcement, HYDC1 had already secured a 10-year colocation services contract signed in July with an international internet company, covering the project's first phase. Zhi Sheng said the contract is valued at approximately USD $1.6 billion, excluding electricity charges, and is expected to account for about one-third of total rental revenue once the full three-phase facility reaches completion.

  That long-term contracted revenue, combined with the utility readiness the company described, forms the foundation on which the Thai bank extended debt financing — a structure Zhi Sheng said gives the project a clearer capital base as construction advances.

  ## Local partnership through Chatchaval Jiaravanon

  A central figure in the Thailand project is Mr. Chatchaval Jiaravanon, who serves as Chairman of HYDC1 and is described by Zhi Sheng as one of its largest shareholders. Chatchaval is a member of the family that controls Charoen Pokphand Group, one of Asia's largest conglomerates, and also owns Fortune magazine. Zhi Sheng said his local business relationships and market knowledge have supported land acquisition, utility access, and meeting Thai market requirements.

  Chief Executive Officer Mr. Lai Ningning cited Chatchaval's "extensive business experience and established local network" as central to building a facility that meets local requirements, and said the company will draw on that expertise to execute phases two and three.

  ## A figure discrepancy in the announcement

  Chatchaval, in his own remarks at the announcement, described the loan as a "seven-year US$580 million facility" — $10 million less than the $590 million figure stated in Zhi Sheng's official deal summary. The announcement provided no explanation for the difference.

  ## What the deal signals for Southeast Asia AI infrastructure

  The financing tests whether long-term contracted revenue and completed utility infrastructure can support large-scale debt funding for AI-focused data centres in Southeast Asia — a market where operators have competed for sites with reliable access to land, power and water. Thailand's Eastern Economic Corridor, a government-designated zone intended to attract industrial and infrastructure investment, has drawn a series of regional and foreign developers on that basis.

  Zhi Sheng is listed in Hong Kong and operates AI data centre campuses in Inner Mongolia and Rayong, Thailand. The company's stated strategy spans mainland China and Southeast Asia.

  With first-phase construction under way and a contracted customer in place, Zhi Sheng said its delivery schedule remains unchanged, with first-phase completion targeted within six months of the loan announcement.
body_zh: |
  智昇集團（Zhi Sheng Group）宣布，已向泰國一家銀行取得一筆5.9億美元的貸款額度，用於資助在泰國東部經濟走廊興建「昊陽資料中心1號」（Haoyang Data Centre 1，HYDC1）——一座超大規模人工智慧資料中心。這家在香港掛牌上市的公司表示，本次貸款期限逾七年。

  智昇集團指出，HYDC1的土地所有權狀已取得，水電等核心公用事業接駁工程大致完成，專案已進入實質施工階段。該設施位於泰國政府設立的東部經濟走廊發展區內，占地面積約14萬302平方公尺。

  ## 設施規模與交付時程

  HYDC1規劃分三期建設。第一期預計於2027年初開始交付，目標在交付啟動後六個月內完工。第一期預估約占HYDC1全部規劃容量的三分之一。智昇集團表示，第二期及第三期將依客戶需求及市場狀況推進。

  ## 收益基礎已先行到位

  在本次貸款公告發布之前，HYDC1已於7月與一家國際網路公司簽署一份為期十年的主機代管服務合約，涵蓋第一期工程。智昇集團表示，該合約總金額約為16億美元（不含電費），預計在HYDC1三期全部完工後，約占總租賃收入的三分之一。

  這份長期合約收益，加上公司所描述的公用事業接駁就緒狀況，共同構成泰國銀行提供債務融資的基礎。智昇集團表示，這樣的架構為專案在施工推進過程中奠定了更清晰的資金基礎。

  ## 透過查察瓦·謝亞拉萬農建立在地夥伴關係

  此次泰國專案的核心人物，是擔任HYDC1董事長的查察瓦·謝亞拉萬農（Chatchaval Jiaravanon）先生。智昇集團表示，他是公司其中一位最大股東。查察瓦出身掌控正大集團（Charoen Pokphand Group）的家族——正大集團是亞洲規模最大的企業集團之一——本人同時持有《財富》（Fortune）雜誌。智昇集團表示，他的在地商業人脈與市場知識，在土地取得、公用事業接駁及滿足泰國市場規範等方面均發揮了重要作用。

  執行長賴寧寧（Lai Ningning）在公告中表示，查察瓦「豐富的商業經驗與深厚的在地人脈」是建設符合當地要求之設施的核心依托，並表示公司將善用這份專業資源，推動第二期及第三期工程的執行。

  ## 公告數字出現落差

  值得注意的是，查察瓦本人在公告場合的發言中，將這筆貸款描述為「七年期5.8億美元融資」——比智昇集團官方交易摘要中的5.9億美元少了1,000萬美元。公告對此差異未作任何說明。

  ## 此交易對東南亞AI基礎設施的意涵

  本次融資是一項實驗：長期合約收益加上完備的公用事業基礎設施，能否撐起東南亞地區以AI為核心的大型資料中心債務融資？在這個市場，業者競相搶進土地、電力與水源穩定可靠的選址。泰國東部經濟走廊是泰國政府指定的特殊發展區，旨在吸引工業與基礎設施投資，已陸續吸引多家區域及海外開發商相繼進駐。

  智昇集團在香港掛牌上市，目前在中國內蒙古及泰國羅勇（Rayong）均設有AI資料中心園區，公司明確的發展策略橫跨中國大陸與東南亞兩大市場。

  隨著第一期工程正式動工、合約客戶亦已確立，智昇集團表示交付時程維持不變，目標在本次貸款公告發布後六個月內完成第一期工程。
