body_en: |
  Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC Pte and Saudi Telecom Co are competing for a minority stake in Pure Data Centres Group, a UK-based developer and operator of data centre infrastructure, according to people with knowledge of the matter cited by Bloomberg News.

  GIC has partnered with Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP), a Danish infrastructure investment manager, for its joint offer. A deadline for binding bids is expected later this month, the people said.

  Pure DC's current owner, Oaktree Capital Management — the Los Angeles-based alternative asset manager — is seeking a new minority investor to fund an expansion of the business. Oaktree could target a valuation of approximately $8 billion for Pure DC in any stake sale, one of the people said. The people asked not to be identified because the information is not public.

  Deliberations are ongoing and there is no certainty that any of the suitors will submit a final bid, the people cautioned.

  ## The asset and its scale

  Pure DC develops and operates digital infrastructure used to support artificial intelligence workloads. The company has more than one gigawatt (GW) of data centre capacity either live or under development, according to its website. One gigawatt is enough to power roughly one million average homes and represents a substantial share of national-scale data centre capacity.

  This month, Pure DC secured up to $1.9 billion in debt financing to fund a build-out across Europe and the Middle East, underscoring the capital intensity of its expansion plans.

  "Pure is growing quickly and we're always looking for partners who can help us take advantage of the opportunities ahead of us," a Pure DC spokesperson said in an emailed statement, declining to comment on valuation. Representatives for CIP, GIC, Oaktree, and Saudi Telecom all declined to comment.

  Bloomberg News reported last year that Oaktree was seeking to raise funds for the company.

  ## Context: catching up on AI-driven demand

  Pure DC has positioned itself as a vehicle for the UK, Europe, and the Middle East to close the gap with the United States and China, where developers have already committed hundreds of billions of dollars to data centre construction driven by demand for artificial intelligence computing infrastructure, according to the company.

  ## What it means

  A completed stake sale at an $8 billion valuation would rank among the larger data centre transactions in Europe and signal continued appetite from sovereign and strategic investors for AI-linked infrastructure assets. The involvement of Saudi Telecom alongside a sovereign wealth fund-infrastructure manager pairing illustrates the breadth of capital competing for exposure to European data centre capacity at scale.

  The next near-term milestone is the binding bid deadline, expected before the end of November.

  新加坡主權財富基金GIC Pte與沙烏地電信公司（Saudi Telecom Co）正在競逐英國資料中心開發商Pure Data Centres Group的少數股權。彭博新聞引述知情人士指出，Pure DC現任大股東、洛杉磯另類資產管理公司橡樹資本（Oaktree Capital Management）正為這筆交易尋求約80億美元的估值。

  GIC已與丹麥基礎設施投資管理公司哥本哈根基礎設施夥伴（Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners，CIP）合作，共同提出聯合報價。知情人士表示，具約束力的出價截止期限預計在本月底前到來。

  目前持有Pure DC股權的橡樹資本希望引進新的少數股權投資人，以資助業務擴張。其中一名知情人士表示，橡樹資本在任何股權出售交易中，可能將Pure DC估值定在約80億美元。由於相關資訊尚未公開，上述人士均要求匿名。

  知情人士也提醒，相關磋商仍在進行中，目前無法確定任何潛在競標方最終是否會遞交正式出價。

  ## 資產概況與規模

  Pure DC主要開發並營運用於支撐人工智慧（AI）工作負載的數位基礎設施。根據其官方網站，該公司已上線或正在開發中的資料中心容量合計逾1吉瓦（GW）。1吉瓦的電力大約可供應百萬戶一般家庭使用，在全國規模的資料中心總容量中佔有相當可觀的比重。

  本月，Pure DC已獲得最高達19億美元的債務融資，用於資助其在歐洲與中東的建設擴張計畫，充分顯示其擴張策略所需的高度資本密集性質。

  Pure DC發言人透過電子郵件聲明表示：「Pure正在快速成長，我們一直在尋找能幫助我們把握前方機遇的合作夥伴。」該發言人婉拒就估值部分發表評論。哥本哈根基礎設施夥伴、GIC、橡樹資本及沙烏地電信的代表則均拒絕置評。

  彭博新聞去年曾報導，橡樹資本正積極為Pure DC尋求融資。

  ## 背景：追趕AI驅動的市場需求

  根據該公司的說法，Pure DC將自身定位為推動英國、歐洲及中東縮短與美國、中國差距的重要載體。美中兩國的資料中心開發商已承諾投入數千億美元興建相關設施，以滿足人工智慧運算基礎設施持續攀升的龐大需求，英國與歐洲正亟需迎頭趕上。

  ## 市場意義

  若此次股權出售以80億美元估值順利完成，將躋身歐洲規模較大的資料中心交易之列，同時也將印證主權及策略投資人對AI相關基礎設施資產的持續高度興趣。沙烏地電信與「主權財富基金加基礎設施管理公司」組合聯手競標，充分說明各路資本爭相佈局歐洲規模化資料中心容量的廣度與深度。
