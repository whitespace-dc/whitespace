body_en: |
  South Korea's major construction companies are simultaneously pursuing large-scale artificial intelligence (AI) data center projects at home and abroad, backed by a government plan to invest 550 trillion won to support 8.4 gigawatts (GW) of AI data center capacity by 2029. At least six builders — Hyundai Engineering & Construction (E&C), Daewoo E&C, Samsung C&T, GS E&C, DL E&C, and SK Ecoplant — have announced dedicated teams, new partnerships, or active project pipelines.

  Global real estate consultancy JLL estimates the data center market will grow at an average annual rate of 14% through 2030, with worldwide data center power consumption expected to nearly double from approximately 103 GW in 2025 to 200 GW by 2030. Total spending to meet that demand could reach up to US$3 trillion, JLL said.

  ## Rising construction costs

  Data center construction costs are climbing alongside demand. The average cost per megawatt (MW) — a measure of a facility's electrical capacity — rose 38% over five years, from US$7.7 million per MW in 2020 to US$10.7 million per MW in 2025, according to JLL. The consultancy expects that figure to rise further to US$11.3 million per MW in 2026.

  ## Company-by-company activity

  Hyundai E&C holds the largest completed data center portfolio among Korean builders, having finished four domestic projects. The company is participating in an AI data center project in Saemangeum, North Jeolla Province, and in June broke ground on the 40 MW Ansan Sihwa AI data center.

  Daewoo E&C established a dedicated data center task force in April to develop its design, technology, and construction capabilities. In July, the company signed a partnership with data center operator KINX, linking development, construction, and operations as part of a strategy to expand from construction into land acquisition, development, operations, and eventual asset sales. Daewoo completed the 40 MW Empyrion Digital AI Campus in Gangnam last year and is participating as both investor and constructor in the up-to-60 MW Pine data center in Jangseong, South Jeolla Province. It is also pursuing projects of 200 MW to 300 MW in the Jangseong and Gangjin areas of South Jeolla Province.

  Samsung C&T won a 40 MW contract — the Ansan Global Cloud Centre — worth 400 billion won (approximately US$283 million) in November 2024 and has participated in data center projects commissioned by Samsung affiliates. The company is pursuing orders from global technology companies and is expanding its target markets beyond Southeast Asia to include the Middle East and Australia. For a separate 2.4 MW project in Chuncheon, Gangwon Province, Samsung C&T said it would apply modular construction methods to shorten build timelines and ease future expansion.

  GS E&C is developing one of the largest projects in the domestic pipeline: an AI data center campus in Gangwon Province and the city of Donghae, with a total planned capacity of 2.4 GW. The first phase, at 1.2 GW, is estimated to cost approximately 10 trillion won. GS E&C is targeting a groundbreaking in early October.

  DL E&C has completed a 20 MW AI data center in Seoul and is developing an 80 MW facility in Gimpo. Its affiliated entity DL Construction secured a 126.8 billion won contract in May for a 9.8 MW AI computing-focused facility in Bucheon.

  SK Ecoplant is participating in construction of a 103 MW data center for SK and Amazon Web Services in Ulsan and recently established a dedicated AI data center team to manage rising demand.

  ## Why builders are moving in

  Data centers offer construction companies shorter project timelines and higher contract values than housing developments, with individual projects ranging from hundreds of billions to several trillion won. "Construction firms are paying close attention to [data centers] because they can generate sizeable orders within a relatively short construction period," a construction industry official told The Korea Herald.

  The business model extends beyond the construction contract itself. "Once builders expand into land development, equity investment and operations, they can continue generating rental and operating income long after construction is completed," the same official said. Daewoo E&C's partnership with KINX and its dual role as investor and constructor in the Pine project reflect that longer-term intent.

  ## Government backing

  The South Korean government has laid out plans to support 8.4 GW of AI data center construction by investing 550 trillion won through 2029 in cooperation with the private sector, providing an additional layer of demand certainty for builders.

  The Korea Research Institute for Construction Policy said in a recent report that AI data centers are increasingly being treated as strategic infrastructure assets rather than conventional buildings. Developers, the institute noted, are seeking contractors capable of coordinating an entire project lifecycle — from grid connections, substations, and backup power to cooling systems, telecommunications networks, and regulatory approvals.

  ## Technical capability as competitive differentiator

  Lee Gyu-eun, an associate research fellow at the Korea Research Institute for Construction Policy, said competitive success in the data center market will depend on integrating shorter power procurement timelines, modular prefabrication, parallel construction processes, and early commissioning.

  Over the longer term, Lee said, expertise accumulated in domestic data center work could position Korean builders for adjacent markets. "Accumulating expertise in modular construction, robotics, remote operations and thermal management could lay the groundwork for entering the emerging space infrastructure market," Lee said.

  The nearest-term milestone for the sector is GS E&C's planned groundbreaking on the 1.2 GW first phase of its Gangwon-Donghae AI data center campus, targeted for early October.
body_zh: |
  南韓主要建設公司正同步在國內外大舉推動人工智慧（AI）資料中心專案，背後有政府550兆韓元的投資計畫撐腰，目標是在2029年前建置8.4吉瓦（GW）的AI資料中心容量。至少六家建商——現代工程建設（E&C）、大宇E&C、三星物產、GS E&C、DL E&C，以及SK Ecoplant——已宣布成立專責團隊、簽署新合作協議或正式啟動專案管線。

  全球房地產顧問公司仲量聯行（JLL）估計，資料中心市場至2030年的年均成長率將達14%，全球資料中心電力需求預計從2025年的約103吉瓦近乎翻倍，升至2030年的200吉瓦。JLL表示，為滿足此一需求所需的總投資金額可能高達3兆美元。

  ## 建設成本節節攀升

  資料中心的建設成本也隨著需求同步上漲。根據JLL的數據，以衡量設施電力容量的每百萬瓦（MW）造價來看，過去五年間上漲了38%，從2020年的每百萬瓦770萬美元，升至2025年的1,070萬美元。JLL預計，這一數字在2026年將進一步攀升至每百萬瓦1,130萬美元。

  ## 各大建商動態

  現代E&C在南韓建商中擁有規模最大的已竣工資料中心投資組合，已完成四件國內專案。目前該公司參與全羅北道新萬金的AI資料中心開發案，並於今年六月正式動工興建40百萬瓦的安山始華AI資料中心。

  大宇E&C於四月成立資料中心專責特遣隊，深化設計、技術與施工能力。七月，該公司與資料中心業者KINX簽署合作協議，串聯開發、建設與營運三大環節，作為從純施工延伸至土地取得、開發、營運乃至最終資產出售的整體戰略布局。大宇E&C去年已完成首爾江南地區40百萬瓦的Empyrion Digital AI科技園區，並以投資方兼施工方的雙重身分參與全羅南道長城郡最多60百萬瓦的Pine資料中心專案；此外，該公司也積極推動全羅南道長城及康津地區合計200至300百萬瓦的開發案。

  三星物產於2024年11月取得40百萬瓦的安山全球雲端中心合約，金額達4,000億韓元（約2.83億美元），同時也參與了三星集團旗下關聯企業委託的資料中心工程。目前三星物產積極爭取全球科技大廠訂單，並將目標市場從東南亞擴展至中東與澳洲。在江原道春川的另一座2.4百萬瓦專案中，三星物產表示將採用模組化建設工法，縮短工期並便於日後擴建。

  GS E&C正在推動國內管線中規模最大的專案之一——位於江原道東海市的AI資料中心園區，總規劃容量達2.4吉瓦。其中首期1.2吉瓦的建設費用估計約為10兆韓元，GS E&C目標在十月初正式動工。

  DL E&C已完成首爾一處20百萬瓦的AI資料中心，目前正在金浦開發一座80百萬瓦的設施。其關聯公司DL建設則於五月取得1,268億韓元的合約，負責在富川興建一座9.8百萬瓦、以AI運算為核心用途的設施。

  SK Ecoplant正參與SK集團與亞馬遜雲端服務（AWS）在蔚山合作的103百萬瓦資料中心建設工程，並於近期成立專責AI資料中心團隊，以因應持續升溫的市場需求。

  ## 建商搶進的誘因

  對建設公司而言，資料中心的工期比住宅開發案短，合約金額卻更高，單一專案規模從數千億至數兆韓元不等。「建設公司之所以對資料中心高度關注，在於它能在相對較短的施工期內帶來可觀的訂單，」一位建設業界人士向《韓國先驅報》說道。

  這套商業模式的效益遠不止於承建合約本身。「一旦建商跨足土地開發、股權投資與營運管理，即便工程完工後，也能持續創造租金與營運收入，」這位業界人士表示。大宇E&C與KINX的合作關係，以及其在Pine專案中同時扮演投資方與施工方的雙重角色，正是這種長期布局意圖的具體體現。

  ## 政府全力支持

  南韓政府已制定計畫，擬聯合民間部門在2029年前投入550兆韓元，支持8.4吉瓦的AI資料中心建設，為建商提供了額外的需求保障。

  韓國建設政策研究院在近期報告中指出，AI資料中心愈來愈被視為戰略性基礎建設資產，而非一般建築物。該研究院表示，開發商正積極尋找能夠統籌整個專案生命週期的承包商，從電網連接、變電所、備用電力，到冷卻系統、電信網路及法規審批，無一不納入考量範圍。

  ## 技術能力成為決勝關鍵

  韓國建設政策研究院副研究員李圭銀表示，在資料中心市場脫穎而出的關鍵，在於整合縮短供電採購時程、模組預製、平行施工流程及提前試運轉等各項能力。

  從更長遠的角度來看，李圭銀指出，在國內資料中心工程中累積的專業知識，有望讓韓國建商順勢布局相鄰市場。「在模組化建設、機器人技術、遠端操作與熱管理方面所累積的專業能力，可能為進軍新興太空基礎建設市場奠定基礎，」李圭銀說。

  就近期而言，業界最受矚目的里程碑，是GS E&C計劃於十月初動工的江原東海AI資料中心園區首期1.2吉瓦工程。
