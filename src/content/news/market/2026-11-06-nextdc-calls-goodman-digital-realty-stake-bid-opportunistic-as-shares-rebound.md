body_en: |
  NEXTDC, the Australian data centre operator, on Thursday rejected a joint bid by Goodman Group and Digital Realty to acquire up to 9.9% of its shares, calling the approach "opportunistic" and stating that its board had received no formal takeover proposal. NEXTDC shares jumped as much as 45.8% to A$9.52 in early trade, on course for their strongest intraday gain on record.

  The move by Goodman and Digital Realty, reported by Reuters on Wednesday, involved an aftermarket book build — a share sale process open only to institutional investors conducted outside regular market hours. The two companies offered to pay approximately A$715 million (US$501 million) for the stake, which would give each of the two rival data centre operators a combined holding of just under 10% in NEXTDC.

  ## Timing follows October sell-off

  The bid came two weeks after NEXTDC disclosed on 21 October 2026 that its M3 Melbourne data centre site had suffered a power delay. Following that announcement, the company cut its financial year 2027 guidance and shelved all new development projects and land acquisitions for the foreseeable future. NEXTDC's stock lost 45.8% in October, the same percentage by which it rebounded on Thursday morning.

  In its Thursday statement, NEXTDC drew attention to the timing. "The share raid comes two weeks after NEXTDC's 21 October 2026 announcement of the M3 Melbourne power delay and revised FY27 guidance, and was made through an aftermarket book build open only to institutional investors," the company said.

  ## 'No intention' — with a reservation

  NEXTDC noted that Goodman and Digital Realty told investors during the book build that they had "no intention of submitting a control proposal or making a takeover bid for the company at this time, but reserve their right to do so." NEXTDC's board said it would consider any bona fide takeover approach on its merits. Neither Goodman Group nor Digital Realty responded to Reuters' requests for comment.

  ## Capital position under review

  Separate from the rejected bid, NEXTDC said in October it would evaluate capital recycling opportunities, including potential sales of selected development sites, as part of the strategic reset that followed the M3 Melbourne delay.

  ## What it means

  The episode shows how a large share price decline can create an opening for rivals to build a material stake through institutional channels, before any formal takeover process begins. By reserving the right to make a future bid while explicitly declining to do so now, Goodman and Digital Realty retain strategic optionality in NEXTDC. The company's board has signalled it will assess any formal approach on its merits if one is submitted.

  澳洲資料中心業者NEXTDC於週四正式拒絕了古德曼集團（Goodman Group）與數位房地產（Digital Realty）聯合提出、欲取得最多9.9%股份的入股行動，將此舉定性為「投機性操作」，並聲明董事會迄今未收到任何正式收購提議。NEXTDC股價在週四早盤最多暴漲45.8%，升至每股9.52澳幣，有望締造歷史單日最大盤中漲幅紀錄。

  古德曼集團與數位房地產的聯合入股計畫，於週三由路透社率先披露，兩家公司採用的方式是「盤後詢價圈購」——一種僅對法人投資人開放、在正常交易時段以外進行的股票出售程序。兩家公司合計願意支付約7億1,500萬澳幣（約5億100萬美元）以取得相關股份，屆時這兩家相互競爭的資料中心業者合計持股將略低於NEXTDC的10%。

  ## 時機恰在十月股價重挫之後

  此次收購行動，距NEXTDC於2026年10月21日揭露旗下M3墨爾本資料中心遭遇供電延誤一事，僅相隔兩週。該消息公布後，NEXTDC隨即下修2027財年財測，並宣布在可預見的未來暫緩所有新開發專案及土地收購計畫。NEXTDC股價在十月份單月跌幅達45.8%，而週四早盤的反彈幅度恰好與此跌幅相同。

  NEXTDC在週四聲明中特別指出時機的敏感性。「此次突襲式收購，發生在NEXTDC於2026年10月21日公告M3墨爾本供電延誤及修正FY27財測後僅兩週，且係透過僅對法人投資人開放的盤後詢價圈購方式進行，」公司如此表示。

  ## 「無意出價」——但保留權利

  NEXTDC指出，古德曼集團與數位房地產在詢價圈購過程中向投資人表示，兩家公司「目前無意提交控制權提案或對本公司發出收購要約，但保留日後為之的權利。」NEXTDC董事會表示，將就實質內容審慎評估任何善意的正式收購提案。截至本文發稿，古德曼集團與數位房地產均未回應路透社的採訪請求。

  ## 資本配置策略重新檢視

  有別於上述遭拒的入股行動，NEXTDC早在十月即表示，將在M3墨爾本延誤事件引發的策略重整架構下，評估資本回收機會，包括可能出售部分精選開發基地。

  ## 事件意涵

  此事件清楚說明，當一家公司股價大幅下挫，競爭對手便可能趁機透過法人投資管道累積實質持股，搶在任何正式收購程序啟動之前取得佈局優勢。古德曼集團與數位房地產以明確表示目前不出價、但保留日後行動權利的方式，對NEXTDC保留了高度的策略選擇空間。NEXTDC董事會亦已明確表態：一旦有正式收購提案送達，將依其實質內容加以評估。
