body_en: |
  PT Wiraraja Geseip and two American investors — Tynergy Technology Group and Essence Global — broke ground October 28, 2026 on a $500 million (approximately Rp 9.07 trillion) industrial complex in Batam, Indonesia, advancing the first phase of a joint venture aimed at building a high-technology supply chain from silica sand processing through to semiconductor production and sodium-ion battery assembly.

  The phase-one site covers 3 hectares at the Wiraraja Industrial Zone I in Kabil, Batam, Kepulauan Riau, and is expected to absorb 1,000 workers, according to Akhmad Makruf Maulana, president director of PT Wiraraja Geseip. The project has been designated a national strategic project (Proyek Strategis Nasional, or PSN) by the Indonesian government.

  ## What the complex will produce

  The first phase encompasses four industrial activities: downstream processing of silica sand (a raw material used in semiconductor and solar panel manufacturing), an AI-enabled smart factory, semiconductor industry operations, and assembly of sodium-ion batteries. Sodium-ion batteries are an emerging alternative to lithium-ion technology, using sodium — a more abundant element — as the charge carrier.

  The integrated "end-to-end" design is intended to link raw material processing with finished technology products within a single industrial zone, Makruf said.

  ## Origins of the deal

  The joint venture agreement (JVA) between Wiraraja Geseip, Tynergy Technology Group, and Essence Global was signed February 18, 2026 in Washington, D.C., in the presence of Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto. The October 28 groundbreaking, attended by approximately 10 partners from the United States and Japan, marks the start of physical execution of that agreement, Makruf said.

  "This is the initial phase of the realization of the US foreign direct investment that has long been committed together," Makruf said in a press statement dated October 27, 2026. "We are starting in the industrial zone that is already available, namely Wiraraja I Kabil."

  ## Planned expansion

  Following the Kabil phase, the parties plan to expand into the Wiraraja Geseip Industrial Zone on Pulau Galang, a separate island within the Batam administrative area. No timeline or investment figure for that expansion was provided.

  The parties will continue coordinating development in line with the scope of their agreement, project implementation stages, and applicable regulations, Makruf said.

  ## Significance for Batam

  The investment adds momentum to Batam's positioning as an industrial hub, with the project representing a formal commitment by US capital to a nationally designated strategic zone. Makruf said the initiative is intended to accelerate industrial downstreaming, strengthen the national industrial ecosystem, attract further strategic investment, and generate value-added manufacturing.

  The next milestone is expansion coordination and project phasing under the terms of the existing joint venture agreement, with Galang Island identified as the site for subsequent development.
body_zh: |
  PT Wiraraja Geseip與兩家美國投資方——Tynergy Technology Group及Essence Global——於2026年10月28日在印尼峇淡島舉行動土典禮，正式啟動一座斥資五億美元（約合印尼盾9.07兆）的工業複合園區，推動從矽砂下游加工到半導體生產及鈉離子電池組裝的一條龍高科技供應鏈，此為三方合資事業第一期的重要里程碑。

  第一期廠區位於廖內群島省峇淡市卡比爾的Wiraraja工業園區一期，占地3公頃，預計吸納1,000名員工就業。PT Wiraraja Geseip董事長Akhmad Makruf Maulana表示，該計畫已獲印尼政府指定為「國家戰略計畫」（Proyek Strategis Nasional，簡稱PSN）。

  ## 複合園區的生產內容

  第一期涵蓋四大工業活動：矽砂下游加工（矽砂為半導體及太陽能板製造的原料）、導入人工智慧的智慧工廠、半導體產業相關業務，以及鈉離子電池組裝。鈉離子電池是鋰離子電池技術的新興替代方案，以自然界蘊藏量更為豐富的鈉元素作為電荷載體。

  Makruf表示，此「端對端」一體化設計旨在於單一工業園區內，將原料加工與成品科技製造串聯為完整產業鏈。

  ## 協議的起源

  Wiraraja Geseip、Tynergy Technology Group與Essence Global三方的合資協議（JVA），於2026年2月18日在美國華盛頓特區正式簽署，印尼總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托亦親臨見證。Makruf表示，10月28日的動土典禮有來自美國與日本的約十名合作夥伴出席，標誌著上述協議正式進入實體施工階段。

  Makruf於2026年10月27日發布的新聞稿中表示：「這是長期共同承諾的美國外國直接投資實現的第一期，我們從現有的工業園區——即卡比爾Wiraraja一期——開始啟動。」

  ## 擴張計畫

  完成卡比爾第一期後，各方計畫進一步擴張至峇淡市行政管轄範圍內另一座島嶼——加蘭島（Pulau Galang）上的Wiraraja Geseip工業園區。目前各方尚未公布該擴張計畫的時程或投資金額。

  Makruf表示，各方將依據協議範疇、計畫實施階段及相關法規，持續協調推動後續開發事宜。

  ## 對峇淡島的意義

  此項投資為峇淡島的工業樞紐定位注入新動能，代表美國資本正式進駐一個國家指定戰略園區的具體承諾。Makruf表示，此計畫旨在加速工業下游化、強化國家工業生態系、吸引更多策略性投資，並創造高附加價值製造業。

  下一個里程碑，是依據現行合資協議的條款，協調推進擴張工作與計畫分期事宜，加蘭島已被確定為後續開發基地。
