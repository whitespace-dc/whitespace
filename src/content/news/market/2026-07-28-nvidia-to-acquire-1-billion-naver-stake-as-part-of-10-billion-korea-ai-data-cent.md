body_en: |
  Nvidia will invest approximately $1 billion in South Korean internet and cloud company Naver through a new share placement, becoming one of Naver's largest shareholders and anchoring a $10 billion expansion of an AI data center in South Korea, the companies announced on July 27.

  Naver said it will place 7.2 million new shares with Nvidia at 204,500 won per share, a discount of roughly 1% to Naver's closing price on July 24. Naver shares surged more than 10% in Seoul trading on the news.

  Investment group Brookfield will provide up to $9 billion in financing as the capital partner for the broader project, with the combined Nvidia equity investment and Brookfield debt bringing the total funding package to as much as $10 billion, Naver said.

  ## Ownership and shareholder context

  Following the investment, Nvidia will hold approximately 4.5% of Naver. As of the end of 2025, the National Pension Service of Korea, one of the world's largest pension funds, was Naver's biggest shareholder at 9.25%. BlackRock Fund Advisors, a subsidiary of asset manager BlackRock, held 6.12%.

  ## What is being built

  The expansion will be constructed at Naver's GAK data center in Sejong, a city in central South Korea. GAK Sejong is described by Naver as South Korea's largest hyperscale data center. The expanded facility will use Nvidia's Vera Rubin and Blackwell chip platforms, which are among Nvidia's most advanced processor families for AI workloads. The project is expected to reach a capacity of 200 megawatts by 2028. For context, 200 megawatts is roughly equivalent to the power consumption of a mid-sized city district and is at the upper end of current large-scale AI data center deployments.

  ## Background: sovereign AI strategy

  The Nvidia-Naver equity arrangement follows a June announcement in which the two companies said they would jointly pursue the development of gigawatt-scale global AI infrastructure, targeting demand for sovereign AI — a term referring to AI systems that nations build and control domestically rather than relying on foreign-owned infrastructure. That agreement covered South Korea and overseas markets including the Asia-Pacific region, Europe, and the Middle East.

  Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang has argued publicly that nations should build and control their own AI infrastructure. Huang said at the time of the June announcement that Naver would be ten times larger once the vision was realized, according to Reuters. At one gigawatt of capacity, the eventual target would be approximately four times the current maximum capacity of GAK Sejong, and would be capable of housing hundreds of thousands of Nvidia's latest graphics processing units simultaneously, Naver said.

  ## Nvidia's broader investment activity

  The Naver stake is part of a pattern of direct equity investments by Nvidia in AI and cloud companies. Nvidia has also made a $30 billion investment in OpenAI and a $10 billion investment in Anthropic, according to Reuters.

  ## What this means for the industry

  The transaction illustrates how Nvidia is using equity stakes to deepen commercial relationships with large non-US customers, securing both GPU sales commitments and a foothold in sovereign AI markets outside the United States. For Naver, the deal provides both capital and a technology partnership with its primary chip supplier to scale infrastructure at a pace that would be difficult to finance independently.

  The Sejong data center expansion is scheduled to reach 200 megawatts of capacity by 2028, with the longer-term gigawatt-scale buildout representing a multi-year construction program.

  輝達（Nvidia）與南韓網路暨雲端服務公司NAVER於7月27日聯合宣布，輝達將透過認購新股的方式，投資約10億美元入股NAVER，成為NAVER主要大股東之一，並主導一項規模高達100億美元的南韓AI資料中心擴建計畫。

  NAVER表示，此次將以每股20萬4,500韓元的價格，向輝達發行720萬股新股，較NAVER於7月24日的收盤價約打九九折。消息一出，NAVER股價在首爾股市盤中隨即飆漲逾10%。

  在整體計畫的資金架構上，投資集團布魯克菲爾德（Brookfield）將以資本合夥人身分，提供最高90億美元的融資。加計輝達此次的股權投資，總融資規模上看100億美元，NAVER指出。

  ## 持股結構與股東背景

  完成入股後，輝達將持有NAVER約4.5%的股份。截至2025年底，全球規模最大退休基金之一的南韓國民年金公團（National Pension Service of Korea）為NAVER第一大股東，持股比率為9.25%；資產管理巨擘貝萊德（BlackRock）旗下子公司貝萊德基金顧問（BlackRock Fund Advisors）則持有6.12%。

  ## 建設內容

  此次擴建工程將在NAVER位於南韓中部城市世宗市的GAK資料中心園區內進行。GAK世宗資料中心被NAVER定位為南韓規模最大的超大規模（hyperscale）資料中心。擴建後的設施將採用輝達旗下Vera Rubin與Blackwell兩大晶片平台，此兩款均屬輝達針對AI運算工作負載所推出的頂尖處理器系列。整體計畫預計於2028年前達到200 MW（百萬瓦）的容量規模。作為參考，200 MW的電力需求大致相當於一座中型城市行政區的用電量，亦屬目前大型AI資料中心部署的上限水準。

  ## 背景：主權AI戰略

  輝達與NAVER此次的股權安排，源自今年6月兩公司宣布的合作框架——當時雙方表示將共同推動吉瓦（GW）等級全球AI基礎設施的建設，目標指向「主權AI」（sovereign AI）的需求。所謂主權AI，係指各國自行建設並掌控本國AI系統，而非仰賴外國擁有的基礎設施。該協議涵蓋南韓及亞太地區、歐洲、中東等海外市場。

  輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）曾公開倡議，各國應自行建設並掌握本國AI基礎設施。據路透社報導，黃仁勳在6月宣布合作時表示，願景一旦實現，NAVER的規模將擴大為現在的十倍。以最終目標的1 GW容量計算，約為GAK世宗資料中心目前最高容量的四倍；NAVER並表示，屆時將可同時容納數十萬顆輝達最新款繪圖處理器（GPU）。

  ## 輝達更廣泛的投資動向

  此次入股NAVER，是輝達近期一系列直接股權投資AI與雲端公司的一環。據路透社報導，輝達另外亦對OpenAI投資了300億美元，對Anthropic則投入100億美元。

  ## 產業意涵

  此次交易清楚呈現輝達如何藉由股權入股，深化與大型非美國客戶的商業關係——一方面確保GPU採購承諾，另一方面在美國以外的主權AI市場取得立足點。對NAVER而言，這筆交易不僅帶來資金挹注，更取得與其主要晶片供應商的技術夥伴關係，得以用難以單靠自身融資所能達到的速度，大規模擴展基礎設施。

  世宗資料中心擴建計畫預計於2028年達到200 MW的容量，而更長期的GW等級建設目標，則代表一項橫跨數年的龐大工程計畫。
