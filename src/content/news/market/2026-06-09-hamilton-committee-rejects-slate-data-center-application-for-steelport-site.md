body_en: |
  Hamilton's committee of adjustment has rejected a proposal from Slate Asset Management to allow data center development at Steelport, a planned 800-acre industrial district on a former steelworks site in the Greater Toronto area. The application drew more than 1,600 written submissions, more than any other project the committee has considered according to local press, and a public hearing that lasted more than eight hours.

  The committee last week refused Slate's application to amend the existing land-use plan to add data centers as a permitted use on a portion of the Steelport site.

  ## The Steelport project

  Steelport occupies an 800-acre parcel on the shore of Lake Ontario in Hamilton, Ontario, on the site of Stelco's former Hamilton plant (later known as Hilton Works), which closed around 2013. Slate acquired the property in 2022 and has described the redevelopment as an AI, advanced manufacturing, and clean infrastructure district.

  The site reportedly has access to 200 megawatts (MW) of grid capacity, with potential for on-site power generation and energy storage. On its dedicated Steelport website, Slate said it is exploring data center uses for a portion of the development alongside other industrial uses, while noting that no data center tenants have been confirmed and no final decisions have been made.

  Slate Asset Management, which manages approximately $12.8 billion in assets and focuses on essential real estate and infrastructure, has called the project a "once in a generation national building opportunity" and said the site is "uniquely positioned to be Canada's most scalable sovereign cloud and compute district and the new epicenter of the nation's advanced manufacturing industry."

  ## Federal sovereign compute program

  Slate told CBC Hamilton it is participating in a proposal by the Digital Research Alliance of Canada (DRAC) for the federal government's AI Sovereign Compute Infrastructure Program, which is calling for applications to build a large-scale sovereign public AI supercomputer.

  ## Community opposition and next steps

  The committee of adjustment received more than 1,600 letters regarding the land severance proposal, more than for any other project it has considered, according to local press.

  CBC Hamilton asked Slate whether it plans to appeal the committee's decision to the province of Ontario. The company had not responded prior to publication.

  漢密爾頓土地調整委員會已正式駁回Slate資產管理公司提出的申請，拒絕在大多倫多地區一座佔地約800英畝的前鋼鐵廠舊址Steelport開發資料中心。此案引發逾1,600份書面陳情，據當地媒體報導，創下該委員會審查過的所有案件之最，公聽會更歷時逾八小時。

  委員會上週正式拒絕Slate的申請——該申請要求修改現行土地使用計畫，將資料中心列為Steelport部分用地的許可用途。

  ## Steelport開發計畫

  Steelport位於安大略省漢密爾頓安大略湖湖畔，佔地約800英畝，原為Stelco漢密爾頓廠（後稱希爾頓廠，Hilton Works）所在地，該廠約於2013年停工。Slate於2022年取得此處產業，並將再開發計畫定位為人工智慧、先進製造與潔淨基礎設施園區。

  據報導，該廠址可接入200百萬瓦（MW）的電網容量，並具備在地發電與儲能的潛力。Slate在Steelport專屬網站上表示，正探討在部分開發用地引入資料中心用途，同時兼顧其他工業用途，並指出目前尚未確認任何資料中心租戶，也尚未做出最終決策。

  管理資產規模約128億美元、專注於基礎不動產與基礎設施的Slate資產管理公司，將此計畫稱為「千載難逢的國家建設機遇」，並表示該廠址「具備獨一無二的條件，足以成為加拿大規模最大的主權雲與運算園區，以及全國先進製造業的新核心」。

  ## 聯邦主權運算計畫

  Slate告訴CBC漢密爾頓，公司正參與加拿大數位研究聯盟（DRAC）就聯邦政府「人工智慧主權運算基礎設施計畫」所提出的提案，該計畫目前正徵求建置大規模主權公共AI超級電腦的申請。

  ## 社區反對聲浪與後續發展

  調整委員會就本次土地分割申請共收到逾1,600封陳情信，據當地媒體報導，為該委員會歷來審理案件中陳情數量最多的一次。

  CBC漢密爾頓詢問Slate是否計畫就委員會決定向安大略省提出上訴，截至發稿前，該公司尚未作出回應。
