body_en: |
  Shares of CoreWeave and Super Micro Computer rose to their highest levels since June on August 12, after upbeat earnings forecasts from the two AI infrastructure companies signaled that demand for computing capacity continues to outpace supply.

  CoreWeave, an AI cloud provider that runs Nvidia-powered data center infrastructure, saw its stock climb more than 19%, putting the company on track to add approximately $8 billion to its market value if gains held. Super Micro Computer, a maker of AI servers, rose more than 13%, adding more than $2 billion in market value. More than seven brokerages raised their price targets on both stocks following the reports.

  Other companies tied to data center buildout also gained ground. Peer cloud provider Nebius Group rose 23% after beating quarterly revenue estimates. Data center operators Applied Digital and IREN Ltd advanced 4% and 8%, respectively. Shares of Dell were up 5%.

  ## CoreWeave: sold-out capacity and a growing backlog

  CoreWeave raised its guidance for annual revenue, adjusted operating profit, and capital spending, citing strong demand for its AI infrastructure. CEO Michael Intrator said near-term capacity is effectively sold out, allowing the company to secure new compute agreements on increasingly favorable terms.

  CoreWeave's revenue backlog — the value of contracted future business — rose to $104.2 billion in the second quarter from $99.4 billion in the first quarter. That figure excludes more than $25 billion in new commitments the company secured early in the current quarter.

  Analysts at MoffettNathanson acknowledged that higher capital expenditure guidance could unsettle some investors, but said the pricing environment suggests project returns are strengthening even as unit costs rise.

  ## Super Micro: margins beat forecasts

  Super Micro issued a revenue forecast for 2027 that exceeded Wall Street estimates, pointing to sustained customer investment in AI servers. The company's gross margin for its fourth quarter came in at 17.5%, above both its preliminary guidance range of 15% to 17% and its initial forecast of 8.2% to 8.4%.

  ## Financial institutions move to capture infrastructure spending

  Major U.S. financial institutions are positioning to participate in the data center buildout. Bank of America pledged $250 billion for U.S. infrastructure financing by July 2027. Morgan Stanley made a similar announcement days earlier, according to the report.

  The broader context is one of heavy capital deployment by technology companies to feed AI demand, accompanied by rising debt levels. That dynamic has prompted increased hedging against the default risk of heavily indebted hyperscalers — large cloud operators that require enormous capital to build and run data centers at scale.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The simultaneous move in CoreWeave, Super Micro, Nebius, Applied Digital, IREN, and Dell on the same trading session reflects broad market confidence that AI infrastructure spending is not slowing. CoreWeave's sold-out near-term capacity and its expanding backlog, now above $104 billion, indicate that demand is being locked in at the contract level well ahead of delivery.

  CoreWeave said it expects to add more than $25 billion in new commitments in the current quarter alone, a figure it said is not yet reflected in the reported backlog.

  8月12日，AI雲端服務商CoreWeave與超微電腦（Super Micro Computer）股價雙雙攀至6月以來高點，兩家AI基礎建設公司發布的樂觀財測顯示，運算產能需求持續超越供給。

  CoreWeave以輝達（NVIDIA）驅動的資料中心基礎建設為核心，當日股價大漲逾19%，若漲幅持穩，預計將為公司市值增添約80億美元。AI伺服器製造商超微電腦則大漲逾13%，市值增加逾20億美元。兩家公司財報發布後，超過七家券商上調了對兩檔股票的目標價。

  資料中心建設相關個股也普遍受惠。同業雲端服務商Nebius Group在季營收優於預期後大漲23%。資料中心業者Applied Digital與IREN Ltd分別上漲4%與8%。戴爾（Dell）股價亦上漲5%。

  ## CoreWeave：產能全數售罄，積壓訂單持續成長

  CoreWeave上調了全年營收、調整後營業利益及資本支出的展望，理由是AI基礎建設需求強勁。執行長麥可．英特雷托（Michael Intrator）表示，近期產能已全數售罄，使公司得以在愈發有利的條件下簽訂新的運算協議。

  CoreWeave的營收積壓訂單——即未來已簽約業務的總價值——從第一季的994億美元增至第二季的1,042億美元。此數字尚未計入公司在本季初期所確保的逾250億美元新承諾訂單。

  MoffettNathanson的分析師坦承，較高的資本支出指引或許會令部分投資人感到不安，但表示當前的定價環境顯示，儘管單位成本上揚，專案報酬率仍持續走強。

  ## 超微電腦：毛利率優於市場預期

  超微電腦發布了2027年的營收展望，超越華爾街分析師的預估，顯示客戶對AI伺服器的投入力道依然強勁。公司第四季毛利率達17.5%，不僅超越原先15%至17%的初步指引區間，更遠優於最初預估的8.2%至8.4%。

  ## 金融機構積極搶進基礎建設融資

  美國主要金融機構正積極布局，爭相參與資料中心建設商機。美國銀行（Bank of America）承諾於2027年7月前提供2,500億美元的美國基礎建設融資。摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）則在數日前發布了類似聲明。

  在更宏觀的背景下，科技公司正大規模投入資本以滿足AI需求，伴隨而來的是整體債務水位的攀升。這一動態促使市場加大對高負債超大規模雲端業者（hyperscaler）違約風險的避險操作——這些大型雲端服務商需要龐大資本，才能大規模興建並營運資料中心。

  ## 對產業的意義

  CoreWeave、超微電腦、Nebius、Applied Digital、IREN與戴爾在同一個交易日齊步大漲，反映出市場廣泛相信AI基礎建設支出不會放緩。CoreWeave近期產能全數售罄、積壓訂單持續擴大——目前已突破1,042億美元——顯示需求正在合約層面大幅超前實際交付進度被鎖定。

  CoreWeave表示，僅本季便預計新增逾250億美元的新承諾訂單，而該數字目前尚未反映在已公布的積壓訂單之中。
