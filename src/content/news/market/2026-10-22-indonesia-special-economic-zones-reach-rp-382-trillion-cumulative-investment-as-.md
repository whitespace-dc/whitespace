body_en: |
  Cumulative investment in Indonesia's 25 special economic zones — known as KEK, or kawasan ekonomi khusus — has reached Rp 382.1 trillion since the program began, the government announced Wednesday, while Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto identified licensing backlogs and land disputes as the primary obstacles preventing three zones from reaching their stated potential.

  Airlangga made the remarks at the KEK National Working Meeting for the third quarter of 2026, held in Jakarta on October 21.

  ## Three zones flagged for intervention

  The minister singled out KEK Kendal, KEK Galang Batang, and KEK Gresik as zones requiring accelerated permitting support and territorial expansion approval.

  KEK Kendal carries the largest prospective investment of the three, with an estimated potential of Rp 370 trillion and capacity to absorb 250,000 workers. KEK Gresik, which Airlangga described as already seeking to expand, has an estimated potential of Rp 264 trillion and 200,000 jobs. KEK Galang Batang, the smallest of the three by these measures, has a stated potential of Rp 114 trillion and 94,000 jobs.

  "KEK Gresik also [has] Rp 264 trillion, 200,000 workers, and is also in expansion status. Therefore, support for permits and the expansion of these three KEK is certainly needed," Airlangga said.

  ## Recent and cumulative performance

  Investment in the KEK system from January through September 2026 reached Rp 46 trillion, adding 53,000 jobs over the period, according to the minister. On a cumulative basis, the zones have absorbed approximately 302,000 workers and host 509 registered business entities.

  ## Zone composition

  Indonesia's 25 KEK are split geographically between seven zones on the island of Java and 18 in other parts of the archipelago. By sector, the network comprises:

  - 13 industrial parks
  - 6 tourism zones
  - 2 health zones
  - 2 technology zones
  - 1 digital zone
  - 1 other services zone

  ## Land and permitting obstacles

  Beyond licensing delays, Airlangga said unresolved land issues must be addressed to enable zone expansion. He cited the land management rights process as a specific complication, involving state plantation company PTPN and an industrial area in Batang.

  ## Strategic context and next steps

  The government has designated the KEK program as a strategic instrument for driving national economic growth to 8 percent, with development directed toward health, tourism, digital, and technology sectors alongside manufacturing.

  To address structural barriers across the program, Airlangga said he welcomed plans to form an alliance of business entities responsible for KEK development and management. The alliance is intended to help resolve operational bottlenecks that limit investment uptake and job creation across the zone network.
body_zh: |
  印尼25個「經濟特區」（kawasan ekonomi khusus，簡稱KEK）自計畫啟動以來，累計投資額已達382.1兆印尼盧比，政府週三正式宣布此一數據。統籌經濟事務協調部長艾爾朗加·哈爾塔托（Airlangga Hartarto）同日指出，許可審核積壓與土地糾紛問題懸而未決，是阻礙三個最具發展潛力的特區充分釋放效能的主要障礙。

  艾爾朗加於10月21日在雅加達舉行的2026年第三季經濟特區全國工作會議上發表上述談話。

  ## 三個重點特區須優先介入

  部長點名肯達爾（KEK Kendal）、加朗巴當（KEK Galang Batang）及格雷西克（KEK Gresik）三個特區，認為這三處需要加速取得許可支援，並獲批准推動區域擴張。

  三個特區中，肯達爾潛在投資規模最大，估計可吸引370兆盧比投資，並可容納25萬名勞工就業。格雷西克潛在投資額估達264兆盧比、可創造20萬個就業機會，艾爾朗加表示該特區目前已積極尋求擴張。加朗巴當則是三者中規模最小的，潛在投資額為114兆盧比，估計可提供9萬4千個就業機會。

  「格雷西克經濟特區同樣擁有264兆盧比的投資潛力與20萬名勞工的就業容量，目前亦處於擴張階段。因此，支援這三個特區取得許可並推動擴張，確實刻不容緩，」艾爾朗加說道。

  ## 近期及累計表現

  根據部長公布的數據，2026年1月至9月間，整個經濟特區體系共吸引46兆盧比投資，同期新增5萬3千個工作機會。累計至今，各特區共吸納約30萬2千名勞工，並有509家企業登記進駐。

  ## 特區組成概況

  印尼25個經濟特區依地理分布，分為爪哇島上的7個特區及其他群島地區的18個特區。依產業別區分，整體網絡涵蓋：

  - 13個工業園區
  - 6個觀光特區
  - 2個醫療健康特區
  - 2個科技特區
  - 1個數位特區
  - 1個其他服務業特區

  ## 土地與許可障礙

  除許可審核延遲外，艾爾朗加指出，土地問題懸而未決同樣是妨礙特區擴張的關鍵因素。他特別點出土地管理權申請程序的複雜性，涉及國營種植公司PTPN與巴當（Batang）工業區的相關糾紛。

  ## 策略定位與後續步驟

  政府已將經濟特區計畫列為推動全國經濟成長——目標達成8%增長率——的策略工具，重點發展方向涵蓋醫療健康、觀光、數位及科技等領域，同時兼顧製造業的持續發展。

  為從根本化解計畫中的結構性障礙，艾爾朗加表示，他樂見相關業者計劃籌組一個由負責特區開發與管理的企業體所組成的聯盟。該聯盟旨在協助排除阻礙投資引進與就業創造的營運瓶頸，使整個特區網絡得以充分發揮效能。
