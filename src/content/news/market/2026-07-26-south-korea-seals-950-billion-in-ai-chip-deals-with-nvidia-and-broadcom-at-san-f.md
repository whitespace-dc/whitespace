body_en: |
  South Korea's SK Group and Samsung Electronics signed $950 billion in AI partnership agreements with Nvidia and Broadcom at a summit in San Francisco on July 24, as U.S. technology companies move to lock in long-term supply of advanced semiconductor components from South Korean manufacturers.

  SK Group committed to deals worth $750 billion, anchored by a partnership between SK Hynix and Nvidia valued at more than $500 billion, South Korea's presidential Blue House said. Samsung Electronics separately signed a memorandum of understanding with Broadcom covering up to $200 billion across memory chips, foundry services and advanced packaging. President Lee Jae Myung hosted the summit.

  ## The SK Hynix-Nvidia partnership

  The SK Hynix-Nvidia deal spans large-scale AI data centers and next-generation memory. Under the agreement, Nvidia secures long-term supply of next-generation memory from SK Hynix, and the two companies will jointly develop high-bandwidth memory (HBM) chips for use in AI training, AI agents and physical AI applications.

  As part of the broader SK Group agreement, SK Telecom plans to build a two-gigawatt data center in South Korea powered by Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips and SK Hynix HBM4. The facility is scheduled to come online in 2027.

  ## The Samsung-Broadcom agreement

  Samsung said it will collaborate with Broadcom on the development of Broadcom's next-generation AI accelerator using Samsung's high-bandwidth memory technology. Samsung will also provide Broadcom with a sub-2-nanometer foundry process and advanced packaging solutions, the company said.

  ## Memory demand outlook

  SK Group chairman Chey Tae-won told the summit that AI customers are seeking far more memory than previously anticipated, citing recent discussions with Nvidia, Anthropic, OpenAI and Broadcom. Chey said Nvidia's projected memory requirements over the next five years could prove higher than current estimates. "I wouldn't be surprised if, the next time I see [Jensen Huang], he'll tell me those figures were too low and say, 'I want more,'" Chey said of the Nvidia chief executive.

  Broadcom CEO Hock Tan told Chey the company's memory demand was likely to surpass available supply, Chey added. Chey said Anthropic was regularly checking on future chip availability as it moves beyond developing AI models to building computing capacity, and that OpenAI would require enormous computing power and had urged SK to supply chips directly.

  Chey also said Anthropic was making its own "computing power," without elaborating, a remark that potentially refers to a plan reported by Reuters in which the AI laboratory is exploring the possibility of designing its own chips. An Anthropic spokesperson said the startup was always exploring options and drawing on a range of chips for a diversified approach to computing.

  Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, who attended the summit, made no comment on the reported chip-design plan, saying only that the company had signed supply deals with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix without providing further details. In May, Anthropic raised funding at a post-money valuation of $965 billion, naming Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix and Micron as partners whose technologies play a critical role in the supply of memory, storage and logic chips.

  ## South Korea's AI strategy

  Lee unveiled a "San Francisco AI Declaration" at the summit, outlining South Korea's goals for technological cooperation with the United States. He said South Korea would "lead the way to a new era of artificial intelligence in partnership with global technology companies," offering what he described as its "dynamic AI ecosystem" to expand the global market for industrial and personal AI use.

  Lee separately met Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Amodei and Broadcom CEO Hock Tan before joining the summit. About 150 executives, investors, researchers and startup founders attended the event. Samsung Electronics executive chairman Jay Y. Lee, SK Group chairman Chey, Hyundai Motor Group executive chair Euisun Chung and Naver founder Lee Hae-jin were also present.

  The July 24 agreements extend a broader South Korean industrial strategy Lee announced in June, comprising three state-driven projects anchored by Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix to build semiconductor production clusters, physical AI ecosystems and AI data centers, backed by more than $576 billion in investment.

  The deals reflect a pattern of U.S. AI companies moving into closer long-term partnerships with South Korean chipmakers as demand for chips and computing infrastructure outpaces supply, with senior executives at the summit describing memory requirements that could exceed companies' current projections.

  Lee is scheduled to attend a July 25 Silicon Valley meeting between South Korea's National Pension Service — described as the world's third-largest pension fund — and U.S.-based venture-capital firms, aimed at encouraging investment in South Korean startups.
body_zh: |
  南韓SK集團與三星電子於7月24日在舊金山峰會上，與輝達（Nvidia）及博通（Broadcom）簽署總額高達9,500億美元的人工智慧（AI）合作協議。此舉顯示美國科技公司正積極鎖定南韓半導體製造商的長期先進零組件供應。

  SK集團承諾的協議金額達7,500億美元，其中以SK海力士（SK Hynix）與輝達之間、價值逾5,000億美元的合作案為核心主軸，南韓總統府對此加以確認。三星電子則另行與博通簽署一份備忘錄，涵蓋記憶體晶片、晶圓代工服務及先進封裝，規模最高達2,000億美元。本次峰會由南韓總統李在明親自主持。

  ## SK海力士與輝達合作案

  SK海力士與輝達的合作協議橫跨大規模AI資料中心及下一代記憶體領域。根據協議，輝達將取得SK海力士下一代記憶體的長期供應保障，雙方亦將共同開發用於AI訓練、AI代理及實體AI應用的高頻寬記憶體（HBM）晶片。

  作為SK集團整體協議的一環，SK電信（SK Telecom）計畫在南韓興建一座總裝置容量達2 GW的資料中心，採用輝達Vera Rubin晶片及SK海力士HBM4驅動運作，預定於2027年正式上線。

  ## 三星與博通協議

  三星表示，將與博通攜手合作，以三星的高頻寬記憶體技術共同開發博通的下一代AI加速器。三星亦將為博通提供2奈米以下的晶圓代工製程及先進封裝解決方案。

  ## 記憶體需求前景

  SK集團董事長崔泰源在峰會上表示，AI客戶對記憶體的需求遠超出先前預估，此一判斷源自近期與輝達、Anthropic、OpenAI及博通的會談。崔泰源指出，輝達未來五年的記憶體需求預測，可能高於目前的估算。「下次見到黃仁勳時，他若告訴我那些數字太保守、說『我要更多』，我一點都不會感到意外，」崔泰源談及輝達執行長時如此說道。

  崔泰源並表示，博通執行長陳福陽（Hock Tan）曾告知他，博通的記憶體需求極可能超過現有的供應量。Anthropic方面則持續關注未來的晶片供應情況，該公司正從開發AI模型邁向建立自有運算能力；OpenAI則表示將需要龐大的運算資源，並敦促SK直接供應晶片。

  崔泰源亦提及，Anthropic正在自行打造「運算能力」，但未進一步說明。此番言論可能指向路透社此前報導的一項計畫——即該AI實驗室正探索自主設計晶片的可能性。對此，Anthropic發言人表示，公司一向積極探索各種選項，並採用多元化的晶片策略以建立多元運算架構。

  曾出席本次峰會的Anthropic執行長達里奧·阿莫迪（Dario Amodei）對外界傳聞的晶片自研計畫未置可否，僅表示公司已與三星電子及SK海力士簽署供應協議，但未透露進一步細節。今年5月，Anthropic完成新一輪融資，交割後估值達9,650億美元，並點名三星電子、SK海力士及美光（Micron）為關鍵合作夥伴，其技術在記憶體、儲存與邏輯晶片的供應上扮演核心角色。

  ## 南韓AI戰略

  李在明在峰會上發表「舊金山AI宣言」，勾勒出南韓與美國在科技合作上的目標藍圖。他表示，南韓將「與全球科技企業攜手，引領人工智慧新時代的來臨」，並以南韓「充滿活力的AI生態系統」為基礎，共同拓展工業及個人AI的全球市場。

  峰會開幕前，李在明分別會見了輝達執行長黃仁勳、OpenAI執行長山姆·奧特曼（Sam Altman）、Anthropic執行長阿莫迪及博通執行長陳福陽，隨後出席峰會。本次活動共吸引約150名企業高層、投資人、研究人員及新創公司創辦人與會。三星電子副會長李在鎔（Jay Y. Lee）、SK集團董事長崔泰源、現代汽車集團會長鄭義宣（Euisun Chung）及NAVER創辦人李海珍亦悉數出席。

  7月24日簽署的各項協議，是李在明今年6月宣布的更宏觀南韓產業戰略的延伸。該戰略以三星電子及SK海力士為核心，推動三項由政府主導的重點計畫，涵蓋半導體生產聚落、實體AI生態系統及AI資料中心的建設，總投資規模逾5,760億美元。

  這些協議折射出一個清晰的趨勢：隨著晶片與運算基礎設施的需求持續超越供給，美國AI公司正加速與南韓晶片製造商建立更深度的長期夥伴關係。出席峰會的多位資深高管均指出，記憶體需求可能超出各公司目前的預測。

  李在明預計於7月25日出席矽谷的一場會議，南韓國民年金公團——被稱為全球第三大退休基金——屆時將與美國創投公司進行會談，目的在於鼓勵外資挹注南韓新創企業。
