body_en: |
  Singapore-based technology company Sea Ltd. has launched a dedicated cloud business unit, Sea Cloud, which will sell AI cloud services to enterprise customers worldwide, according to an SEC filing that made public a staff email from founder and CEO Forrest Li.

  The announcement triggered a sell-off in Sea's stock. Shares fell from $88.67 to $82.06 following the filing's publication, and had declined further to approximately $77.81 at the time of reporting, according to Data Center Dynamics.

  ## What Sea Cloud is and how it was built

  Sea Cloud is not a new infrastructure build. Li said in his email to staff that Sea has operated its own private cloud platform for more than ten years, describing it as "the backbone powering Garena, Shopee, and Monee's scale every single day." Garena is Sea's digital entertainment arm; Shopee is its e-commerce platform; Monee is its digital financial services unit.

  Over the past two years, approximately 500 employees — referred to internally as "Sailors" — rebuilt that platform to support AI workloads, Li wrote. The result, he said, is that moving an AI application "from a whiteboard to production inside our company is now dramatically faster than it was two years ago." Sea Cloud is the externalization of that capability.

  ## Sea's scale and growth trajectory

  Li used the filing to frame the launch against Sea's growth since its initial public offering on October 20, 2017. Quarterly active users across Sea's platforms have grown from 69 million to 666 million — roughly a tenfold increase. Shopee's quarterly gross merchandise value, a measure of total transaction volume, rose 35 times, from $1.1 billion to $38.3 billion. Quarterly revenue grew more than 80 times, from $94 million to $7.8 billion.

  ## Infrastructure basis

  Sea's most recent annual report states that the company hosts its servers in leased data centers across its operating markets, supplemented by third-party cloud services. The data centers are owned and maintained by "major domestic and international data center providers" under leasing and hosting agreements with renewal terms, though the company does not publicly name the providers or disclose specific locations.

  The filing describes an architecture designed for large-scale content delivery: "We operate at a scale that routinely delivers massive amounts of content to tens of millions of users across our platforms," and notes that infrastructure will continue to expand as the user base and engagement levels grow.

  ## Significance

  Sea Cloud positions Sea among a growing category of technology companies — sometimes called neoclouds — that have built substantial internal cloud and AI infrastructure to support consumer-scale platforms and are now seeking to monetize that investment by offering capacity and services to outside enterprises. The move represents a strategic extension from Sea's existing consumer and financial services businesses into the enterprise technology market.

  Separately, Sea's financial services unit Monee may also pursue an independent public listing, according to the Data Center Dynamics report.

  消息公布後，冬海股價隨即遭遇拋售。根據Data Center Dynamics的報導，文件公開後股價從88.67美元跌至82.06美元，截至報導時更進一步下探至約77.81美元。

  ## Sea Cloud的定位與建構歷程

  Sea Cloud並非全新基礎建設的產物。李小冬在員工信中表示，冬海集團經營自有私有雲端平台已逾十年，形容其為「每天支撐Garena、蝦皮購物（Shopee）與Monee規模運作的核心骨幹」。Garena是冬海集團旗下的數位娛樂部門；蝦皮購物是其電子商務平台；Monee則是數位金融服務部門。

  過去兩年間，約500名員工——集團內部稱為「Sailors」（水手）——對這套平台進行了全面重建，使其得以支援AI工作負載。李小冬在信中指出，如今在冬海內部，將一項AI應用「從白板構想推進到正式上線，已比兩年前快得多」。Sea Cloud的成立，正是將這套內部能力對外開放的具體體現。

  ## 冬海集團的規模與成長軌跡

  李小冬在這份申報文件中，以冬海集團自2017年10月20日首次公開募股（IPO）以來的成長歷程，為此次發布奠定背景。冬海旗下各平台的季度活躍用戶從6,900萬成長至6億6,600萬，約達十倍之多。蝦皮購物的季度商品交易總額（GMV）——衡量總交易量的指標——從11億美元攀升至383億美元，成長達35倍。季度營收則從9,400萬美元躍升至78億美元，成長逾80倍。

  ## 基礎建設架構

  冬海集團最新年報指出，公司在各營運市場租用資料中心託管伺服器，並輔以第三方雲端服務。這些資料中心由「主要的國內外資料中心業者」依租賃與代管協議持有並維護，合約設有續約條款，惟冬海並未公開點名業者身份，亦未揭露具體地點。

  申報文件描述了一套專為大規模內容傳遞而設計的架構：「我們的營運規模，足以在各平台上向數以千萬計的用戶即時傳遞海量內容」，並指出隨著用戶基礎與參與度持續擴大，基礎建設亦將持續擴充。

  ## 市場意涵

  Sea Cloud的成立，使冬海集團躋身一個日益壯大的科技公司新類別——有時被稱為「新世代雲端業者」（neoclouds）。這類企業憑藉支撐消費者規模平台的需求，建立了相當規模的內部雲端與AI基礎建設，並積極尋求透過向外部企業提供運算容量與服務來變現這些投資。此舉標誌著冬海集團從現有的消費者與金融服務業務，策略性地延伸至企業科技市場。

  此外，根據Data Center Dynamics的報導，冬海集團旗下金融服務部門Monee或許也將尋求獨立掛牌上市。
