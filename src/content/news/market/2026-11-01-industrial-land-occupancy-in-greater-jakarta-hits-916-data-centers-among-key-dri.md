body_en: |
  Industrial and logistics properties in Greater Jakarta — the Jabodetabek region covering Jakarta, Bogor, Depok, Tangerang, and Bekasi — posted the strongest performance of any property sector through the third quarter of 2026, with industrial land occupancy reaching 91.6%, according to consulting firm CBRE Indonesia.

  The figures were presented by Anton Sitorus, Head of Research and Consulting at CBRE Indonesia, at the firm's Jakarta Property Market Update Q3 2026 briefing on October 28. He said industrial land absorption reached approximately 59 hectares during the third quarter alone, pushing the occupancy rate to its current level and outpacing other segments including office and retail.

  ## Data centers and tech industries lift demand

  Sitorus identified data center operators and technology-based industries as a significant factor behind the occupancy gains, alongside more established industrial tenants. He said reliable access to electricity, water, and telecommunications networks is increasingly influencing occupancy decisions, particularly for data center operators who require consistent, high-quality utility infrastructure.

  Occupancy remains concentrated in established industrial corridors with mature infrastructure and industrial ecosystems, principally Cikarang in Bekasi Regency and Karawang, both in West Java province.

  ## Logistics sector even tighter

  The modern logistics segment posted an even higher occupancy rate of approximately 97% in the same period, supported by sustained demand from e-commerce marketplaces, manufacturers, third-party logistics providers, and cold-chain operators — companies that manage temperature-controlled storage and transport for perishable goods.

  Absorption in the modern logistics sector reached approximately 26,800 square meters during Q3 2026. CBRE Indonesia estimated total logistics stock at 3.6 million square meters, with future supply of 1 million square meters in the pipeline. Sitorus said new supply in this segment has remained relatively constrained, a condition that has kept market fundamentals healthy and supported rental rate growth along Jabodetabek logistics corridors.

  ## Infrastructure quality shapes location decisions

  Sitorus described a broader pattern in which businesses that depend heavily on infrastructure — including data center operators — gravitate toward Greater Jakarta and its surroundings, while labor-intensive industries tend to relocate to lower-cost sites elsewhere on the island of Java.

  "Business players prefer established industrial locations that offer a mature ecosystem, complete supporting services, and better operational certainty," Sitorus said.

  The preference for established corridors over greenfield alternatives has also contributed to land price growth at primary industrial locations, CBRE Indonesia noted.

  ## What this means for the market

  With industrial occupancy at 91.6% and logistics at approximately 97%, the available land pool in Jabodetabek's main corridors is narrowing. The combination of limited new supply in logistics and concentrated demand from infrastructure-dependent sectors including data centers points toward continued upward pressure on land prices and rents in the established Cikarang and Karawang corridors. CBRE Indonesia estimated 1 million square meters of future logistics supply is expected to come to market, though the timing of that supply will determine how much relief it provides to occupancy-constrained tenants.
body_zh: |
  根據顧問機構世邦魏理仕印尼（CBRE Indonesia）最新報告，大雅加達地區——即涵蓋雅加達、茂物、德博克、坦格朗與勿加泗的Jabodetabek區域——工業與物流房地產在2026年前三季表現強勁，在所有房地產類別中領先群倫，工業用地入駐率達91.6%。

  上述數據由世邦魏理仕印尼研究暨諮詢部門主管安東·西托魯斯（Anton Sitorus）於10月28日舉辦的「雅加達2026年第三季房地產市場更新」說明會上正式公布。他表示，僅第三季單季工業用地吸納量即達約59公頃，推動入駐率攀升至現行水準，超越辦公室與零售等其他市場區塊。

  ## 資料中心與科技產業拉抬需求

  西托魯斯指出，資料中心業者與科技型產業是帶動入駐率成長的重要因素，與傳統工業租戶並駕齊驅。他表示，穩定的電力、水源及電信網路供應正日益左右選址決策，尤其對需要持續且高品質基礎設施的資料中心業者而言更為關鍵。

  入駐活動持續集中於基礎設施完善、工業生態系成熟的既有廊道，主要為西爪哇省勿加泗縣的芝卡朗（Cikarang）與卡拉旺（Karawang）兩地。

  ## 物流市場供應更為緊俏

  現代物流市場在同期錄得約97%的入駐率，表現甚至優於工業用地，需求來源包括電商平台、製造商、第三方物流業者，以及專門管理易腐商品溫控倉儲與運輸的冷鏈業者。

  2026年第三季現代物流市場吸納量達約26,800平方公尺。世邦魏理仕印尼估計物流總存量為360萬平方公尺，另有100萬平方公尺新供給正在規劃中。西托魯斯表示，此一市場新供給長期維持相對有限的狀態，此條件有助維持市場基本面健康，並支撐Jabodetabek物流廊道租金持續成長。

  ## 基礎設施品質左右選址決策

  西托魯斯描述了一個更廣泛的市場趨勢：高度依賴基礎設施的企業——包括資料中心業者——傾向選址於大雅加達及其周邊地區，而勞力密集型產業則趨向遷往爪哇島其他地點以降低成本。

  「業者偏好具備成熟生態系、完善配套服務與較高營運確定性的既有工業園區，」西托魯斯說。

  對既有廊道的偏好勝過未開發新址，也連帶推升主要工業園區的地價成長，世邦魏理仕印尼指出。

  ## 市場展望

  在工業用地入駐率91.6%、物流入駐率約97%的雙重壓力下，Jabodetabek主要廊道的可用土地正逐漸收窄。物流市場新供給有限，加上資料中心等基礎設施依賴型需求高度集中，預示芝卡朗與卡拉旺等既有廊道的地價與租金將持續承壓上行。世邦魏理仕印尼估計，100萬平方公尺的未來物流供給預計將陸續推出，但實際供給時程將決定此一新供給能為入駐率吃緊的租戶帶來多大程度的緩解。
