body_en: |
  Oracle's 2026 capital expenditure came in at $55.66 billion, exceeding the company's own $50 billion target for the year, the cloud computing company said. To sustain its artificial intelligence infrastructure build-out, Oracle added that it plans to raise nearly $40 billion through a combination of debt and equity in 2027. Shares fell 5.8% in extended trading on the disclosure.

  ## 2027 financing plan

  The nearly $40 billion Oracle plans to raise in 2027 includes a previously announced $20 billion at-the-market equity issuance, the company said. An at-the-market program allows a company to sell shares gradually into the open market rather than in a single large offering. Oracle had stated in February that it aimed to raise as much as $50 billion in 2026 through a combination of debt and equity sales; actual capital spending for the year ran past that figure.

  ## Margin pressure ahead

  Oracle Chief Financial Officer Hilary Maxson told analysts on a conference call that the company's gross margins — the share of revenue remaining after direct costs — will "step down" during fiscal year 2027, which has just begun, as the company ramps up data center projects.

  ## Contracted backlog exceeds analyst estimates

  Oracle's remaining performance obligations — a measure of contracted future revenue not yet recognized on the income statement — stood at $638 billion, well above the analyst consensus estimate of $592.52 billion, according to data from Visible Alpha. For the first time, Maxson gave a timeline for when Oracle expects that backlog to convert into recognized revenue: 12%, or $76.56 billion, within the next 12 months, and a further 34%, roughly $216.92 billion, in the two years after that.

  ## Quarterly results beat consensus

  For its fourth quarter, Oracle reported revenue of $19.18 billion, ahead of the average analyst estimate of $19.10 billion compiled by the London Stock Exchange Group. Adjusted earnings came in at $2.03 per share, above the $1.96 consensus expectation.

  ## Funding gap draws investor scrutiny

  Despite the strong backlog figure and the quarterly beat, how Oracle will finance the capital spending required to earn that contracted revenue remains a central concern for investors. Jacob Bourne, an analyst at market research firm eMarketer, said the demand signal is credible, with cloud infrastructure revenue and the backlog both growing. "But the funding question is getting harder, not easier, with capital expenditure coming in well above estimates and free cash flow still negative," Bourne said.

  ## Broader software sector pressures

  Oracle also contends with a concern affecting the wider software industry: the possibility that AI tools could draw enterprise customers away from traditional software products by automating tasks those products once handled, the company acknowledged.

  ## What comes next

  Oracle plans to raise nearly $40 billion through debt and equity financing in 2027, and expects $76.56 billion of its $638 billion contracted backlog to convert to revenue within the next 12 months.
body_zh: |
  甲骨文（Oracle）2026年資本支出達556.6億美元，超越公司自訂的500億美元目標。這家雲端運算大廠表示，為持續推進人工智慧基礎設施的擴建計畫，公司計畫於2027年透過舉債與發行股票的組合方式，籌資近400億美元。消息公布後，甲骨文股價在盤後交易下跌5.8%。

  ## 2027年融資計畫

  甲骨文計畫於2027年籌集的近400億美元中，涵蓋此前已宣布的200億美元按市值持續發行股票計畫。所謂按市值發行計畫，係指公司將股票逐步賣入公開市場，而非一次性大規模集中發行。甲骨文曾於今年2月宣稱，計畫透過舉債與股票銷售，在2026年籌集多達500億美元；然而，全年實際資本支出最終超過了這一數字。

  ## 毛利率面臨壓力

  甲骨文財務長Hilary Maxson在法說會電話會議中向分析師表示，公司的毛利率——即扣除直接成本後剩餘的營收比例——將在甫告展開的2027財年出現「下移」，原因是公司正加速推進資料中心建設案。

  ## 合約待履行義務超越分析師預估

  甲骨文的剩餘履約義務——衡量已簽約但尚未認列於損益表上的未來營收——達6,380億美元，遠高於Visible Alpha數據顯示的分析師共識預估5,925.2億美元。Maxson首度就這批合約積壓量提供轉換時間表：其中12%、即765.6億美元，預計在未來12個月內轉為已認列營收；另有34%、約2,169.2億美元，則將在其後兩年間陸續轉換認列。

  ## 季度財報優於市場預期

  甲骨文第四季營收為191.8億美元，高於倫敦證券交易所集團匯整的分析師平均預估191億美元。調整後每股盈餘為2.03美元，同樣高於市場共識預期的1.96美元。

  ## 資金缺口引發投資人關切

  儘管合約待履行義務數字亮眼、季報表現也優於預期，投資人最核心的疑慮仍在於：甲骨文將如何籌措資金，以承擔履行這批合約所需的龐大資本支出。市場研究機構eMarketer分析師Jacob Bourne表示，雲端基礎設施營收與合約積壓量均持續成長，需求訊號頗具可信度。「但資金問題只會越來越棘手，資本支出遠超預估，自由現金流仍為負值，」Bourne說。

  ## 軟體業整體面臨更廣泛壓力

  甲骨文亦坦承，整個軟體業正面臨一項共同隱憂：AI工具可能透過自動化，承接企業原本仰賴傳統軟體產品處理的工作，進而吸引企業客戶轉向，侵蝕傳統軟體的市場需求。

  ## 後續展望

  甲骨文計畫於2027年透過舉債與股票融資方式籌集近400億美元，並預計在未來12個月內，將6,380億美元合約積壓量中的765.6億美元轉為已認列營收。
