body_en: |
  Allbirds, the eco-footwear company that announced a pivot into AI computing infrastructure in April, has rebranded as Smartbird and named Nadia Carlsten as chief executive, with shares rising to around $6.58 on significant intraday trading following the dual announcement.

  The company simultaneously said it had doubled its convertible financing facility, a form of debt that can be converted into equity, from $50 million to $100 million.

  ## Background: from shoes to AI compute

  Allbirds was once valued at $4 billion as an eco-focused footwear brand before losing 99 percent of its share price after 2021. In April, the company announced plans to sell its footwear brand for $39 million and pivot to AI compute, operating temporarily under the name NewBird AI. That April announcement produced a 582 percent share price jump, pushing the market capitalization to $148 million at $17 a share. The valuation then declined steadily to $3.84 a share before the latest rebrand and CEO appointment.

  ## New chief executive

  Carlsten most recently served as chief executive of the Danish Centre for AI Innovation, where she led the deployment of Gefion, a national Nvidia supercomputer. Prior to that, she spent three years at Amazon Web Services (AWS), the cloud computing division of Amazon, as head of product for the AWS Center for Quantum Computing, a role she left in early 2022. She has also held positions at the US Department of Homeland Security, SandboxAQ, the US Department of Energy, and UC Berkeley.

  "AI is rapidly becoming mission-critical for organizations across every industry, yet many organizations lack a practical path to deploy and operate the dedicated infrastructure these workloads require," Carlsten said in a statement accompanying the announcement. She described the company's target as "enterprise-grade AI infrastructure that delivers control and performance without the capital and operational burden of hardware ownership."

  ## Financing and positioning

  The expansion of the convertible financing facility to $100 million, up from the $50 million announced alongside the April pivot, gives the company a larger capital base as it moves to build out its stated infrastructure strategy. Carlsten characterized the market opportunity as "one of the most significant infrastructure opportunities of the next decade."

  ## What it means

  The Smartbird rebrand and CEO appointment represent the second major public move in the company's repositioning from consumer goods to AI infrastructure, following the April brand sale. Each announcement has produced a sharp share price reaction, though the valuation remains well below the $148 million market cap reached immediately after April's disclosure. The $100 million financing facility is the primary disclosed resource available to the company as it moves to execute on its enterprise AI infrastructure strategy under Carlsten's leadership.

  Carlsten在加入Smartbird之前，擔任丹麥AI創新中心（Danish Centre for AI Innovation）執行長，主導部署了丹麥國家輝達超級電腦「Gefion」。在此之前，她曾在亞馬遜旗下雲端運算部門亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）任職三年，負責主持AWS量子運算中心（AWS Center for Quantum Computing）的產品部門，並於2022年初離職。她的資歷還涵蓋美國國土安全部、SandboxAQ、美國能源部及加州大學柏克萊分校等機構。

  可轉換融資額度從四月轉型時宣布的5,000萬美元，擴大至1億美元，為公司打造更充裕的資本基礎，以推進其既定的基礎設施策略。Carlsten將這一市場機遇形容為「未來十年最重要的基礎設施機會之一」。
