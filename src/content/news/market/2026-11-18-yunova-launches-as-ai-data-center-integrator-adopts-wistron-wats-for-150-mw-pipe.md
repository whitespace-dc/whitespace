body_en: |
  Taiwan-based YUNOVA (雲鑠股份有限公司) formally launched on November 17, 2026, announcing a partnership with AI server manufacturer Wistron (緯創資通) to deploy WATS — Wistron's AIDC Total Solution — across a pipeline of AI data center (AIDC) sites the company said will exceed 150 megawatts (MW) of total capacity within three years.

  YUNOVA was established by Fengchi Electric Energy (風馳電能), a developer active in renewable energy projects and land acquisition. The company said that background directly addresses what it identified as the two critical constraints on AI data center development: securing adequate electrical power and obtaining suitable land.

  ## Context: power demand driving the market

  The International Energy Agency (IEA), according to YUNOVA's launch statement, projects global data center electricity consumption will roughly double from 460 terawatt-hours (TWh) in 2022 to 1,000 TWh by 2026 — a volume the company compared to Japan's total annual electricity use. YUNOVA said generative AI is the primary driver of that acceleration, and that traditional data center architectures cannot handle the peak power loads and thermal density of modern AI server clusters.

  YUNOVA is positioning itself as an "AI computing power and green energy data center integration expert," offering what it described as a one-stop solution covering computing infrastructure, facility design, and around-the-clock operations.

  ## Wistron WATS partnership

  The WATS framework — Wistron AIDC Total Solution — is the hardware and systems architecture YUNOVA will adopt across its sites. The company said the arrangement is intended to ensure software and hardware stability at international quality standards. Wistron is described in the announcement as a global AI GPU server manufacturer.

  ## Three-pillar service model

  YUNOVA said its offering is built around three service categories:

  - **AI computing**: GPU cluster resources configurable for model training or real-time inference
  - **AIDC design and consulting**: engineering services covering power distribution, high-density cooling, and facility layout
  - **AIDC operations**: 24-hour monitoring and maintenance targeting minimized downtime and power risk

  ## Target industries

  At launch, YUNOVA identified four initial industry verticals: advanced semiconductor and manufacturing firms requiring computing for chip design simulation and defect detection; technology software companies and AI application developers; financial technology and quantitative trading institutions; and biomedical and smart healthcare organizations running workloads such as genomic sequencing, drug development simulation, and medical imaging analysis.

  ## Capacity and timeline

  YUNOVA said it plans to open multiple AIDC sites over the next three years, with cumulative capacity exceeding 150 MW. The company did not disclose specific site locations, construction timelines, or capital commitments in the launch announcement.

  ## Significance

  The launch adds a vertically integrated operator to Taiwan's emerging AI data center market, one combining upstream power and land assets — through its parent — with a downstream infrastructure and managed services business. By anchoring its technical stack to Wistron's WATS architecture from inception, YUNOVA is making a supply-chain bet on a single integrated vendor framework rather than assembling components from multiple sources. Whether that approach speeds deployment or introduces concentration risk will become clearer as individual sites come online.

  YUNOVA said it intends to continue expanding partnerships with both international ecosystem partners and domestic industry players as it develops its site portfolio.

  台灣AI資料中心整合商雲鑠股份有限公司（YUNOVA）於2026年11月17日正式成立，同步宣布與AI伺服器製造商緯創資通（Wistron）合作，計畫在旗下AI資料中心（AIDC）站點全面導入WATS——緯創AIDC整合解決方案（Wistron AIDC Total Solution）。雲鑠表示，預計在未來三年內，各站點累計總容量將突破150百萬瓦（MW）。

  雲鑠由再生能源與土地開發商風馳電能（Fengchi Electric Energy）所創立。公司指出，母公司的業務背景恰好能直接回應AI資料中心開發最關鍵的兩大瓶頸：穩定電力供應與合適土地取得。

  ## 電力需求推動市場成長

  雲鑠在成立聲明中援引國際能源署（IEA）的預測數據，指出全球資料中心耗電量將從2022年的460兆瓦時（TWh）翻倍成長至2026年的約1,000兆瓦時——相當於日本全年的總用電量。雲鑠表示，生成式AI是推動此波加速成長的主要驅力，而傳統資料中心架構已無法應對現代AI伺服器叢集的尖峰功耗需求與高熱密度挑戰。

  雲鑠將自身定位為「AI算力與綠能資料中心整合專家」，提供涵蓋運算基礎設施、設施設計及全天候營運的一站式解決方案。

  ## 與緯創WATS的合作

  WATS框架——即緯創AIDC整合解決方案——是雲鑠將在旗下各站點全面採用的硬體與系統架構。公司表示，此合作安排旨在確保軟硬體穩定性達到國際品質標準。緯創資通在聲明中被描述為全球AI GPU伺服器製造商。

  ## 三大服務支柱

  雲鑠表示，其服務體系建立在三大服務類別之上：

  - **AI算力**：GPU叢集資源，可依需求配置為模型訓練或即時推論用途
  - **AIDC設計與顧問**：涵蓋電力配置、高密度冷卻及設施規劃的工程服務
  - **AIDC營運**：全天24小時監控與維護，以將停機時間及供電風險降至最低為目標

  ## 目標產業

  雲鑠在成立之初鎖定四大初始產業垂直市場：需要算力進行晶片設計模擬與瑕疵檢測的先進半導體及製造業者；科技軟體公司與AI應用開發商；金融科技與量化交易機構；以及執行基因定序、新藥開發模擬及醫學影像分析等工作負載的生醫與智慧醫療機構。

  ## 容量規劃與時程

  雲鑠表示，計畫在未來三年內陸續開設多個AIDC站點，累計容量將超過150 MW。公司在成立聲明中並未揭露具體站點位置、施工時程或資本投入規模。

  ## 意義與展望

  此次成立，為台灣新興AI資料中心市場增添了一家垂直整合型業者——透過母公司的業務佈局，將上游電力與土地資產和下游基礎設施及託管服務事業串連起來。雲鑠自創立之初便將技術堆疊錨定於緯創WATS架構，意味著公司在供應鏈策略上押注單一整合供應商框架，而非從多個來源拼湊各項元件。這套做法究竟能加速部署，抑或帶來供應集中的風險，將隨著個別站點陸續上線而逐漸明朗。

  雲鑠表示，未來將持續擴展與國際生態系夥伴及國內產業業者的合作關係，以充實其站點組合。
