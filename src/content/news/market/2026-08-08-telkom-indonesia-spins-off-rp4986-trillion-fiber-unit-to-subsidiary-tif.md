body_en: |
  PT Telkom Indonesia (ticker: TLKM) plans to spin off its Wholesale Fiber Connectivity business, valued at Rp49.86 trillion (Indonesian rupiah), into controlled subsidiary PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia (TIF), with shareholder approval targeted for September 30, 2026, the company disclosed on August 7, 2026.

  The transaction, outlined in a Separation Plan and Information Disclosure published Friday, is the second phase of Telkom's fiber infrastructure reorganization. The spun-off segment's value equals approximately 33% of Telkom's total equity based on audited financial statements as of December 31, 2025, the company said.

  ## Transaction structure

  Under the plan, TIF will issue 498.58 million new shares at a conversion value of Rp100,000 per share, all of which Telkom will absorb. No cash will change hands; Telkom's contribution will be made entirely through the transfer of assets and liabilities from the fiber segment. After the transaction closes, Telkom's ownership of TIF will rise from 99.9999997% to 99.9999999%, while PT Multimedia Nusantara will hold the remaining 0.0000001%.

  Assets and liabilities will be transferred through what Telkom termed an impure separation mechanism (pemisahan tidak murni), with newly issued TIF shares serving as compensation to Telkom. The company stated that existing Telkom shareholders' ownership stakes will not be affected.

  ## Segment financials

  The business being transferred carried total assets of Rp29.15 trillion at year-end 2025, against total liabilities of Rp316 billion and equity of Rp28.84 trillion. Revenue from the segment fell to Rp22.72 trillion in 2025 from Rp25.19 trillion in 2024, and net profit dropped to Rp11.79 trillion from Rp13.9 trillion over the same period. On a consolidated basis, the transfer of assets and liabilities will not change the Group's financial summary or key ratios, Telkom said.

  ## Strategic rationale

  Telkom said the spin-off is part of its roadmap toward becoming a strategic holding company. TIF is intended to serve as the growth engine for external wholesale revenue monetization and as the wholesale fiber network connectivity provider for the broader Telkom Group. Concentrating asset management, customers, partners, and related functions under a dedicated infrastructure entity is expected to improve operational efficiency and long-term valuation potential, the company said.

  Telkom added that the restructuring is designed to open external monetization opportunities and enable strategic partnerships with global infrastructure and technology players, while deepening broadband penetration, accelerating fiber network construction, and strengthening the national connectivity ecosystem.

  ## Regulatory timeline

  Telkom notified OJK (Otoritas Jasa Keuangan), Indonesia's financial services regulator, of the extraordinary general shareholders' meeting on July 31, 2026. Creditors have until August 21, 2026 to file objections. The company will issue a formal meeting summons on September 8, 2026, ahead of the shareholder vote on September 30, 2026. If approved, the separation deed will be signed the same day, with official meeting results due by October 2, 2026.

  PT Telkom Indonesia（股票代碼：TLKM）於2026年8月7日揭露，計畫將旗下批發光纖連接業務（估值達49.86兆印尼盧比）分拆至受控子公司PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia（TIF），目標於2026年9月30日取得股東批准。

  此次交易細節已載於公司週五發布的《分拆計畫暨資訊揭露》文件中，為Telkom光纖基礎設施重組的第二階段。以截至2025年12月31日的經審計財務報表為基準，本次分拆業務的估值約占Telkom總股東權益的33%。

  ## 交易架構

  依據計畫，TIF將以每股盧比10萬元的換算價值，新發行4億9,858萬股，全數由Telkom承接。本次交易不涉及現金移轉，Telkom的出資方式完全透過將光纖業務相關資產與負債移交TIF來實現。交易完成後，Telkom對TIF的持股比例將由99.9999997%微幅上升至99.9999999%，其餘0.0000001%則由PT Multimedia Nusantara持有。

  資產與負債的移轉採Telkom所稱的「不純分拆機制」（pemisahan tidak murni）進行，以TIF新發行股份作為對Telkom的補償。公司表示，現有Telkom股東的持股比例不受此次交易影響。

  ## 業務財務狀況

  本次移轉業務至2025年底的總資產為29.15兆盧比，總負債為3,160億盧比，股東權益為28.84兆盧比。該業務部門2025年營收為22.72兆盧比，低於2024年的25.19兆盧比；同期間淨利則從13.9兆盧比下滑至11.79兆盧比。Telkom表示，就合併報表而言，此次資產與負債的移轉不會改變集團整體財務摘要或各項關鍵財務比率。

  ## 策略布局

  Telkom表示，此次分拆是公司轉型為策略控股公司路線圖的一部分。TIF定位為對外批發營收貨幣化的成長引擎，同時作為Telkom集團旗下批發光纖網路連接的專責供應商。將資產管理、客戶、合作夥伴及相關職能集中於一個專屬基礎設施實體，預計將提升營運效率及長期估值潛力。

  Telkom進一步指出，此次重組旨在開拓對外貨幣化機會，並促成與全球基礎設施及科技業者的策略合作，同時深化寬頻普及、加速光纖網路建設，並強化國家整體連網生態系。

  ## 監管時程

  Telkom已於2026年7月31日向印尼金融服務監管機構OJK（Otoritas Jasa Keuangan）通報臨時股東大會事宜。債權人可於2026年8月21日前提出異議。公司將於2026年9月8日正式發出會議召集通知，為9月30日的股東表決做準備。若獲股東批准，分拆契約將於當日簽署，正式會議結果預計於2026年10月2日前公告。
