body_en: |
  Taiwan Mobile (TWSE: 3045) reported second-quarter operating income and net profit after tax at their highest levels in nearly 20 years and sharply upgraded its full-year 2026 profit outlook, the company said on August 15, driven by accelerating growth in its enterprise and new technology segments and by cost controls that amplified margin gains well beyond revenue growth.

  The company raised its full-year 2026 consolidated operating income growth forecast to 7%-9% from a prior estimate of 1%-2%. Within that figure, the telecom business operating income growth forecast was raised to 13%-15% from 4%-6%. Both revised figures exclude any impact from a pending acquisition of enterprise IT company Systex Corporation (精誠資訊).

  ## Second-Quarter and First-Half Financial Results

  Taiwan Mobile's second-quarter consolidated revenue reached NT$49.43 billion, with operating income of NT$6.12 billion, up 18% year-on-year. EBITDA — earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, a standard measure of operating cash generation — rose 9% to NT$11.5 billion. Net profit after tax climbed 38% to NT$4.54 billion. Earnings per share (EPS) reached NT$1.49, up 37%.

  For the first half of 2026, consolidated revenue totaled NT$99.21 billion. Operating income was NT$12.05 billion, up 17% year-on-year. Net profit after tax was NT$8.69 billion, up 25%, with EPS of NT$2.86, up 24%.

  ## Three Business Segments

  Taiwan Mobile attributed the results to three segments it describes as its growth engines: mobile services, enterprise services, and new technology businesses. In the second quarter, mobile services revenue grew 3% year-on-year, enterprise services revenue grew 19%, and new technology business revenue — excluding the momo e-commerce platform — grew 18%.

  Cost discipline widened margins substantially. Telecom segment operating income rose 25% in the quarter, a rate the company said was six times the 3% growth recorded in telecom revenue over the same period.

  ## Systex Acquisition

  Taiwan Mobile recently announced a tender offer for Systex Corporation ordinary shares, conducted through its wholly-owned subsidiary Taiwan Telecom (台信電訊). The offer targets between 39% and 58% of Systex's outstanding shares. The consideration offered is NT$92.5 in cash plus 0.84 shares of Taiwan Mobile ordinary stock for each Systex share tendered, implying an acquisition price of approximately NT$184.5 per share.

  President Lin Chih-chen (林之晨) said the two groups' combined share of Taiwan's enterprise information and communications technology (ICT) market is approximately 7%. Taiwan Mobile's stated goal is to double that market share within five to six years through what it described as three synergy strategies: cross-selling of combined product and service portfolios, AI integration, and regional expansion. The acquisition is also intended to accelerate the company's AI data center deployment.

  ## AI Data Center Capacity

  Management said Systex does not currently hold AI-grade data center assets. Taiwan Mobile's combined capacity across conventional data centers and AI data centers — referred to internally as AIDC — stands at slightly above 30 megawatts (MW). In data center terms, MW measures total power draw and is the standard unit for comparing facility scale.

  The company said it is evaluating partnerships with developers at multiple locations in Taiwan and overseas, with plans to actively expand capacity over the next 12 to 24 months. It noted that the typical lead time from contract signing to operational startup is 6 to 18 months.

  ## Capital Expenditure

  Taiwan Mobile said its large-scale 5G network build-out is largely complete. It plans to use AI tools to improve capital allocation efficiency on future network spending, which it said should not generate significant additional capital expenditure (capex) requirements. In the first half of 2026, capex was concentrated in the telecom business; the momo unit accounted for approximately 10% or less of the total.

  ## What It Means

  The results and guidance revision indicate that Taiwan Mobile is moving from a phase of heavy network infrastructure spending — required for 5G rollout — toward a period focused on margin expansion and services-led revenue growth. The Systex tender offer, if completed at the targeted ownership level, would consolidate Taiwan Mobile's position in the enterprise ICT market and provide a platform for the AI data center expansion the company has outlined for the coming two years.
body_zh: |
  台灣大哥大（台灣證券交易所代號：3045）8月15日公布財報，第二季合併營業利益及稅後純益均創近二十年來新高，並大幅上調2026年全年獲利展望。公司指出，成長動能主要來自企業服務與新科技業務加速擴張，以及成本管控措施發揮顯著成效，使獲利增幅遠超同期營收成長。

  台灣大哥大將2026年全年合併營業利益成長率預測，從原先的1%至2%大幅上修至7%至9%；其中電信業務營業利益成長率預測亦從4%至6%上修至13%至15%。上述修正後數字均不包含正在進行中的企業IT公司精誠資訊收購案之影響。

  ## 第二季及上半年財務成果

  台灣大哥大第二季合併營收達新臺幣494.3億元，營業利益為61.2億元，年增18%。EBITDA（稅前息前折舊攤銷前利潤，為衡量企業營運現金產生能力的標準指標）成長9%至新臺幣115億元。稅後純益年增38%至45.4億元，每股盈餘（EPS）達新臺幣1.49元，年增37%。

  2026年上半年，合併營收合計新臺幣992.1億元，營業利益120.5億元，年增17%；稅後純益86.9億元，年增25%，每股盈餘2.86元，年增24%。

  ## 三大業務板塊

  台灣大哥大將此次亮眼成績歸因於公司定位的三大成長引擎：行動服務、企業服務及新科技業務。第二季期間，行動服務營收年增3%，企業服務營收年增19%，新科技業務營收（不含momo購物平台）年增18%。

  嚴格的成本紀律大幅擴展了利潤空間。電信業務板塊第二季營業利益年增25%，公司指出，此一增幅相當於同期電信營收3%成長率的六倍。

  ## 收購精誠資訊

  台灣大哥大近日宣布，透過全資子公司台信電訊，對精誠資訊普通股發起公開收購，目標收購精誠資訊39%至58%的流通在外股份。每股精誠資訊股份的收購對價為現金新臺幣92.5元，加計0.84股台灣大哥大普通股，換算每股收購價約為新臺幣184.5元。

  總經理林之晨表示，兩大集團合計約占台灣企業資訊通訊技術（ICT）市場7%的份額，台灣大哥大的目標是在五至六年內，透過三大綜效策略將市占率翻倍：整合後產品與服務組合的交叉銷售、AI整合，以及區域擴張。此次收購亦旨在加速公司AI資料中心的建置部署。

  ## AI資料中心容量

  管理層表示，精誠資訊目前並不持有AI等級的資料中心資產。台灣大哥大現有傳統資料中心與AI資料中心（公司內部稱為AIDC）的合計容量略高於30百萬瓦（MW）。在資料中心產業中，MW代表設施的總用電功率需求，是比較設施規模的標準單位。

  公司表示，正在評估與台灣及海外多個地點的開發商建立合作夥伴關係，並計畫在未來12至24個月內積極擴充容量。公司並指出，從合約簽署到正式投入營運，典型前置作業期為6至18個月。

  ## 資本支出

  台灣大哥大表示，大規模5G網路建設已大致完成，並計畫運用AI工具提升未來網路支出的資本配置效率，預計不會產生大幅額外的資本支出（capex）需求。2026年上半年，資本支出主要集中於電信業務；momo部門占整體資本支出比例約10%或以下。

  ## 意涵分析

  此次財報表現與展望上修顯示，台灣大哥大正從5G網路大規模基礎建設投資階段，逐步轉型至以利潤擴張與服務驅動的營收成長為核心的新階段。若精誠資訊公開收購案以目標持股比例順利完成，將鞏固台灣大哥大在企業ICT市場的競爭地位，並為公司在未來兩年所規劃的AI資料中心擴張提供有力平台。
