body_en: |
  Nvidia has halved the financial guarantee it plans to offer for a 10-gigawatt (GW) data center campus in Ohio, cutting its commitment from $250 billion to less than $120 billion in response to investor concerns about the company's risk exposure, people familiar with the matter told the Wall Street Journal.

  The revised structure would limit Nvidia's initial guarantee to the first phase of the campus — approximately 5 GW of power capacity, or half the total planned build — with a decision on whether to finance the remaining 5 GW to follow. A gigawatt is a unit of electrical power capacity; the 10 GW campus, if completed, would be the largest announced data center project in the world, according to the report.

  The two companies could sign an agreement as early as this weekend, the Journal reported. When the newspaper first disclosed negotiations over a $250 billion guarantee, Nvidia's share price fell as much as 5 percent.

  ## The campus and its developers

  The 10 GW campus is being developed by SB Energy, a subsidiary of Japan's SoftBank Group. OpenAI is still in talks with SB Energy and is expected to sign a binding lease for the full campus within days, two people familiar with the matter told the Journal.

  Nvidia's guarantee was designed to cover the data center lease and construction debt, giving lenders greater confidence in the project's financing and reducing borrowing costs, the Journal reported. Goldman Sachs is serving as SB Energy's financial adviser; Morgan Stanley is advising Nvidia.

  ## Chip financing and broader deal structure

  Separate from the real-estate guarantee, Nvidia is negotiating a chip financing arrangement with OpenAI to cover the cost of purchasing chips for the campus. Total chip procurement for the project could reach $350 billion, according to the report. The power required for the campus is controlled by the U.S. government; Japan is providing additional funding under a recent trade agreement, the Journal reported.

  The guarantee revision came in the same week that Nvidia announced plans to form "compute financing platforms" with six financial institutions: Apollo Global Management, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield Asset Management, Goldman Sachs, and KKR. The initiative targets more than $500 billion in external capital over the coming years to finance chip purchases at "attractive rates."

  ## Background

  In September 2025, Nvidia and OpenAI signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) — a non-binding agreement of intent — under which Nvidia committed to building at least 10 GW of computing capacity for OpenAI and agreed to invest up to $100 billion toward associated costs. The plan subsequently stalled because some people within Nvidia raised concerns about the deal, the Journal reported.

  The halved guarantee reflects investor unease about Nvidia committing its balance sheet to underwrite AI infrastructure at the scale the original agreement envisioned. By limiting its initial guarantee to the first 5 GW phase, Nvidia reduces its near-term financial exposure while keeping the broader campus project on track. Whether Nvidia extends financing to the remaining 5 GW will be determined once the first phase is under way, according to the Journal's sources.

  輝達（Nvidia）計劃為俄亥俄州一座10吉瓦（GW）資料中心園區所提供的財務擔保，已從2,500億美元大幅削減至不到1,200億美元。知情人士向《華爾街日報》透露，此舉係為回應投資人對該公司風險曝險的疑慮。

  依照修訂後的架構，輝達的初步擔保將僅涵蓋園區第一期——約5吉瓦的電力容量，相當於整體計畫規模的一半；至於是否為剩餘5吉瓦提供融資，將於日後再行決定。吉瓦為電力容量單位；據報導，若這座10吉瓦園區順利完工，將成為全球迄今宣布規模最大的資料中心建設案。

  《華爾街日報》報導，兩家公司最快可望於本週末簽署協議。此前，該報首度披露雙方正就2,500億美元擔保進行談判，消息一出，輝達股價盤中一度重挫逾5%。

  ## 園區及其開發商

  這座10吉瓦園區由日本軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）旗下子公司SB Energy負責開發。OpenAI目前仍在與SB Energy進行談判，據兩名知情人士告訴《華爾街日報》，預計將在數日內就整座園區簽署具有約束力的租約。

  據《華爾街日報》報導，輝達提供擔保的目的，在於涵蓋資料中心租約及建設債務，藉此提升放款機構對該專案融資的信心，同時降低借貸成本。高盛（Goldman Sachs）擔任SB Energy的財務顧問；摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）則擔任輝達的財務顧問。

  ## 晶片融資與整體交易架構

  除不動產擔保之外，輝達也正與OpenAI洽談晶片融資安排，以支應為園區採購晶片的費用。據報導，該專案的整體晶片採購金額可能高達3,500億美元。園區所需電力由美國政府掌控；日本則依據近期簽署的貿易協定，提供額外資金挹注。

  擔保金額縮減的消息，恰與輝達同週宣布的另一項計畫同步曝光——輝達宣布將與六家金融機構共同組建「運算融資平台」，合作夥伴包括阿波羅全球管理（Apollo Global Management）、黑石集團（Blackstone）、貝萊德（BlackRock）、布魯克菲爾德資產管理（Brookfield Asset Management）、高盛及KKR。該計畫目標是在未來數年內，從外部籌集逾5,000億美元資本，以「具吸引力的利率」為晶片採購提供融資。

  ## 背景

  2025年9月，輝達與OpenAI簽署了一份意向備忘錄（MOU）——即一份不具法律約束力的意向協議——輝達在備忘錄中承諾為OpenAI建置至少10吉瓦的運算容量，並同意為相關費用投入最多1,000億美元。然而，由於輝達內部有人對該協議提出疑慮，計畫隨後陷入停滯，《華爾街日報》對此有所報導。

  擔保金額縮減至原先的一半，反映出投資人對輝達以自身資產負債表為如此龐大規模的AI基礎建設背書的不安。藉由將初步擔保限縮在第一期5吉瓦階段，輝達得以降低近期的財務風險曝險，同時讓規模更龐大的整體園區計畫得以繼續推進。至於輝達是否延伸融資至剩餘5吉瓦，則需待第一期工程啟動後方能確定，《華爾街日報》消息人士如此表示。
