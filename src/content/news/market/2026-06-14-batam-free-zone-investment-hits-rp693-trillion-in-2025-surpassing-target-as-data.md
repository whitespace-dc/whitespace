body_en: |
  Batam's free trade and free port zone — formally known in Indonesian as the Kawasan Perdagangan Bebas dan Pelabuhan Bebas, or KPBPB — recorded Rp69.30 trillion in realized investment in 2025, exceeding its annual target of Rp60 trillion and supporting economic growth of 6.76% for the zone, BP Batam said. The governing authority attributed the outperformance in large part to accelerating investor interest in digital infrastructure, particularly data centers.

  Amsakar Achmad, head of BP Batam, said the result reflected broader confidence in the ecosystem the authority is constructing. "This is not just a number. It reflects investor trust in the ecosystem we are building in Batam," Amsakar said.

  ## Sector breakdown

  The services sector — which BP Batam said encompasses a range of digital-based activities — contributed the largest single slice of investment at Rp9.99 trillion. The digital sector specifically registered Rp8.557 trillion across 2025. Industrial machinery, electronics, and electrical equipment accounted for Rp6.08 trillion, while electricity, gas, and water infrastructure drew Rp5.80 trillion.

  ## Nongsa Digital Park as the hub

  Much of the digital investment is concentrated in Nongsa Digital Park, or NDP, which sits within Batam's Nongsa Special Economic Zone — a designated KEK, short for Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus, the Indonesian term for a special economic zone offering targeted fiscal and regulatory incentives. BP Batam described NDP as the primary location for data center development and broader digital economy activity in the zone.

  Amsakar said NDP functions as an early-stage indicator of the zone's digital trajectory. "Nongsa Digital Park is a positive early indicator because it shows investment interest, cross-border partnerships, and growing activity in data centers, technology development, and the digital economy," he said.

  In the fourth quarter of 2025, the Nongsa KEK employed 836 workers, according to BP Batam data. The authority said the zone also generates indirect economic activity through supply chain linkages and supporting services.

  ## Investor origins

  Singapore remains one of the largest source countries for investment in Batam, BP Batam said. Inflows also came from Hong Kong, the United States, and Malaysia, particularly directed at the digital sector.

  Amsakar pointed to geography and connectivity as central to Batam's pitch to foreign investors. "The stronger factor is the combination of strategic elements: geographic proximity to Singapore, fast connectivity, and the opportunity to build cross-border operating models to support regional needs," he said.

  ## Green energy infrastructure

  The expansion of power-intensive data centers has prompted BP Batam to accelerate investment in energy infrastructure, including solar power plants — known in Indonesian as PLTS, or Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Surya. The Rp5.80 trillion in electricity, gas, and water investment recorded in 2025 reflects that demand. Amsakar said renewable energy development is now part of the authority's strategy to meet the sustainability requirements increasingly imposed by international investors. "Our digital transformation is real. We are committed to making Batam a competitive, measurable, and investor-friendly investment destination, especially for the sustainable digital sector," he said.

  ## Positioning against regional competition

  BP Batam acknowledged the emergence of the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone — known as JS-SEZ — as a regional development but framed it as an opportunity for complementary positioning rather than direct competition. "We see this not merely as competition, but as an opportunity to build complementarity. Singapore is strong as a headquarters and global services hub; Johor offers capacity expansion; and Batam has unique characteristics such as its FTZ/SEZ status, industrial ecosystem, maritime-logistics capability, and operational proximity to Singapore," Amsakar said. FTZ refers to Batam's free trade zone designation.

  ## Workforce development

  BP Batam has paired infrastructure investment with human capital programs hosted at NDP. Active programs include the Apple Academy, IBM Hybrid Cloud Academy, RMIT University, and Epic Games training initiatives, according to the authority.

  In December 2025, BP Batam launched a platform called MANTAB, a digital workforce-matching system designed to connect industry labor demand with available workers in Batam.

  ## 巴淡島自由貿易區2025年投資額達693兆盾　超標逾15%　資料中心成長主引擎

  根據巴淡島區域管理機構BP Batam公布的數據，巴淡島自由貿易暨自由港區（印尼語正式名稱為Kawasan Perdagangan Bebas dan Pelabuhan Bebas，簡稱KPBPB）2025年全年實現投資額693兆印尼盾，超越年度目標600兆盾，超出幅度達15.5%，並支撐該區域經濟成長率達到6.76%。BP Batam表示，此次亮眼成績在很大程度上歸功於數位基礎建設——尤其是資料中心——持續加速吸引投資人目光。

  BP Batam主席阿姆薩卡爾·阿赫馬德（Amsakar Achmad）表示，這項成果反映出外界對該機構正在巴淡島建構的整體生態系的信心。他說：「這不只是一個數字，它反映的是投資人對我們在巴淡島所打造的生態系的信賴。」

  ## 各產業投資分布

  從產業別來看，BP Batam所定義涵蓋各類數位相關活動的服務業，以99.9兆盾居單一產業投資之冠。純數位產業在2025年全年累計投資額達85.57兆盾。工業機械、電子及電氣設備產業吸引投資60.8兆盾，電力、瓦斯與供水基礎建設則達58兆盾。

  ## 農沙數位園區作為核心樞紐

  巴淡島數位投資的重心多集中於農沙數位園區（Nongsa Digital Park，簡稱NDP）。NDP坐落於巴淡島農沙特別經濟區（KEK，即Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus，為印尼給予特定財稅與法規優惠的特別經濟區）之內。BP Batam將NDP定位為全區資料中心發展及數位經濟活動的首要據點。

  阿姆薩卡爾表示，NDP是觀察該區數位發展走向的早期正向指標。他說：「農沙數位園區是一個正面的早期訊號，因為它展現出投資興趣、跨境合作夥伴關係，以及資料中心、技術開發與數位經濟活動日益蓬勃的態勢。」

  據BP Batam統計，2025年第四季農沙特別經濟區共有836名員工在此就業。該機構亦指出，此一園區透過供應鏈連結與周邊支援服務，帶動了可觀的間接經濟活動。

  ## 投資來源國分布

  BP Batam表示，新加坡依然是巴淡島最大的境外投資來源國之一，此外亦有來自香港、美國及馬來西亞的資金流入，尤其集中於數位產業。

  阿姆薩卡爾指出，地理位置與網路連結能力是巴淡島向外資招商的核心賣點。他說：「更關鍵的因素在於多項戰略優勢的結合：與新加坡的地理鄰近性、快速的網路連結，以及建立跨境營運模式、支應區域需求的機會。」

  ## 綠色能源基礎建設

  隨著高耗電量資料中心持續擴張，BP Batam也加快推動能源基礎建設投資，包括太陽能發電廠（印尼語稱PLTS，即Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Surya）。2025年電力、瓦斯與供水領域58兆盾的投資額，正反映出此一需求。阿姆薩卡爾表示，再生能源發展現已納入BP Batam的整體策略，以因應國際投資人日益重視的永續性要求。他說：「我們的數位轉型是真實存在的。我們致力於讓巴淡島成為一個具競爭力、可量化、對投資人友善的投資目的地，特別是針對永續數位產業。」

  ## 因應區域競爭的定位策略

  BP Batam也正視柔佛—新加坡特別經濟區（JS-SEZ）的崛起，但將其定位為互補合作的機遇，而非正面競爭的威脅。阿姆薩卡爾說：「我們將此視為建立互補關係的機會，而不僅僅是競爭。新加坡擅長作為企業總部及全球服務中心；柔佛提供容量擴充的空間；而巴淡島則有其獨特之處，包括自由貿易區暨特別經濟區（FTZ/SEZ）地位、完整的工業生態系、海事物流能力，以及緊鄰新加坡的營運優勢。」其中FTZ係指巴淡島的自由貿易區定位。

  ## 人才培育與勞動力發展

  BP Batam在推動基礎建設投資的同時，也搭配在NDP內舉辦一系列人才培育計畫。據該機構表示，目前進駐的培訓項目包括Apple學院、IBM混合雲學院、澳洲皇家墨爾本理工大學（RMIT University）及Epic Games培訓課程。

  2025年12月，BP Batam正式上線一套名為MANTAB的平臺——這是一套數位勞動力媒合系統，旨在將產業端的人力需求與巴淡島現有的求職人才進行精準媒合。
