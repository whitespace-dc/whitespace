body_en: |
  Southern Score Builders Bhd (SSBB) has secured two sub-contract agreements worth a combined RM146.8 million (approximately US$33 million) to construct, complete, test, and commission Electrical Package 3 and associated works at two data centre projects in Port Dickson, Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia.

  The contracts were entered into with a local construction company, SSBB disclosed in a filing with Bursa Malaysia, the country's main stock exchange. The filing did not identify the main contractor or the data centre owner.

  ## Contract scope and financial impact

  The two agreements cover the same scope — Electrical Package 3 and other associated works — across both Port Dickson sites. SSBB said the contracts will have no effect on the company's share capital or shareholding structure, and no immediate effect on net assets per share or the group's gearing ratio, which measures debt relative to equity.

  The company said the projects are nevertheless expected to contribute positively to net assets and earnings per share from the financial year ending June 30, 2027, continuing until the projects reach completion. No completion date was stated in the filing.

  ## Board view

  SSBB's board described the contracts as being in the ordinary course of business and in the best interests of the group.

  ## Significance

  The awards add to the pipeline of electrical and mechanical subcontracting work flowing from Malaysia's expanding data centre construction market, particularly in Negeri Sembilan, a state that has attracted significant hyperscale and co-location investment in recent years. For SSBB, the RM146.8 million combined value represents committed revenue that the company projects will begin registering in its accounts from the mid-2027 financial year onward.

  ## 合約範疇與財務影響

  兩份合約的工程範疇相同，均為第三電氣套組及相關配套工程，分別對應波德申的兩個工地。SSBB表示，合約不會影響公司的股本或股權結構，亦不會立即影響每股淨資產，或集團的負債比率（即衡量債務相對股東權益之指標）。

  公司表示，儘管如此，上述項目預計將自截至2027年6月30日止的財政年度起，對每股淨資產及每股盈利產生正面貢獻，並持續至項目全數竣工為止。文件中並未提及具體完工日期。

  SSBB董事會認為，上述合約屬公司普通業務範疇，符合集團最佳利益。

  此次合約的取得，進一步豐富了SSBB在電氣與機械分包工程領域的業務管線，受惠於馬來西亞資料中心建設市場的持續擴張，尤其是森美蘭州——該州近年來已吸引大量超大規模（hyperscale）及聯合選址（co-location）資料中心投資。對SSBB而言，合計1億4,680萬令吉的合約金額代表已確認的收入來源，公司預計相關收益將自2027年年中財政年度起開始入帳。
