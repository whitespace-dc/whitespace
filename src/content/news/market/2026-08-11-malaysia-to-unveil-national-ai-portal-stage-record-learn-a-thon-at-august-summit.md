body_en: |
  Malaysia's government and private sector are hosting AI Malaysia Takeover 2026 (AIMTO_26) on 11 and 12 August at The Campus in Ampang, a two-day event intended to shift the country's artificial intelligence agenda from regional policy dialogue to mass public participation, with Minister of Digital Gobind Singh Deo scheduled to officially launch a new national digital skills platform at the event.

  Co-organised by Ludic Asia and 500 Global, a venture capital firm, and supported by the Ministry of Digital and MyDIGITAL Corp as strategic partner, AIMTO_26 follows the inaugural ASEAN AI Malaysia Summit 2025, which drew more than 10,000 participants and brought together digital ministers from six ASEAN member states. The 2026 edition narrows its focus to domestic execution, skills acquisition, and citizen-level digital inclusion rather than cross-border policy coordination, according to the organisers.

  ## Rakyat Digital portal launch

  Gobind Singh Deo will use the event to officially unveil the Rakyat Digital portal, a national platform led by the Ministry of Digital and implemented by MyDIGITAL Corp. The portal is designed to consolidate digital learning resources, skills training, and AI tools into a single access point for Malaysians across education and income levels. The organisers describe the launch as a direct contribution to the Malaysia Digital 2030 Action Plan (MD2030), the government's framework for establishing Malaysia as an inclusive AI nation by 2030.

  ## Record-setting learn-a-thon

  The centrepiece of AIMTO_26 is what organisers are calling Malaysia's Largest AI Learn-A-Thon, a simultaneous mass upskilling session targeting thousands of participants at once. Co-organised by 500 Global, Ludic Asia, Malaysia AI Residency, and KrackedDevs, the session covers prompt engineering — the practice of structuring instructions to AI systems — workflow automation, and practical AI deployment. YTL AI Labs will supply AI compute tokens through its Ilmu Chat platform to support the training.

  The Learn-A-Thon doubles as an official Malaysia Book of Records attempt, with the scale of simultaneous AI instruction framed as a national milestone. The organisers said the session is aligned with the Ministry of Digital's Rakyat Digital upskilling framework, which targets students, working professionals, business owners, and local developers.

  ## Speakers and programme

  Confirmed speakers include Gobind Singh Deo; Khairy Jamaluddin and Shahril Hamdan, co-founders of Kelas Sekejap; Dr. Rainer Deutschmann, Group Chief Strategy and Transformation Officer at Axiata; Paul Copplestone, CEO and founder of Supabase; Khailee Ng, Managing Partner at 500 Global; and Foong Chee Mun, CEO of YTL AI Labs.

  Alongside the Learn-A-Thon, the two-day programme includes a podcast stage for live on-site recordings with founders and policymakers, the Pasar AI Exhibition — an interactive showcase of government and private-sector digital public infrastructure — and developer and creator demonstrations covering fintech, education, healthcare, and enterprise efficiency use cases.

  ## Significance for Malaysia's AI agenda

  AIMTO_26 marks a deliberate shift in how Malaysia is staging its national AI programme. The 2025 summit operated at a regional, ministerial level during Malaysia's ASEAN Chairmanship; the 2026 edition is structured around direct public capability-building and a government portal launch that ties the event to the binding MD2030 policy timeline. That institutional anchor distinguishes AIMTO_26 from a standalone industry conference and makes the Rakyat Digital portal launch its most consequential output.

  The event is open to the public, developers, business leaders, and academic institutions, with registration available at aimto.my.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞政府與民間企業將於八月十一日至十二日，在安邦（Ampang）The Campus舉辦「AI Malaysia Takeover 2026」（AIMTO_26）。這場為期兩天的活動旨在推動該國人工智慧議程，從區域政策對話轉型為大規模全民參與。數位部長哥賓星（Gobind Singh Deo）預計出席，並將正式發布全新的全國數位技能平台。

  AIMTO_26由Ludic Asia與創投機構500 Global共同主辦，並獲數位部及MyDIGITAL Corp擔任策略夥伴支持。本屆峰會延續首屆「東協AI馬來西亞峰會2025」的基礎——該活動吸引逾一萬名參與者，並匯聚六個東協成員國的數位部長。主辦單位表示，二○二六年版將重心聚焦於國內執行、技能培育與公民層級的數位包容，而非跨國政策協調。

  ## Rakyat Digital入口網站正式上線

  數位部長哥賓星將藉此活動正式揭幕「Rakyat Digital」入口網站——這是一個由數位部主導、MyDIGITAL Corp負責實施的全國性平台。該平台旨在將數位學習資源、技能培訓及AI工具整合為單一入口，供各教育程度與收入階層的馬來西亞民眾使用。主辦單位將此次發布定位為直接呼應「馬來西亞數位2030行動計畫」（MD2030）的具體行動——這是政府在二○三○年前將馬來西亞打造成具包容性AI強國的政策框架。

  ## 破紀錄大規模學習松

  AIMTO_26的重頭戲，是主辦單位所稱的「馬來西亞最大規模AI學習松」——一場同步面向數千名參與者的大規模增能培訓。本場學習松由500 Global、Ludic Asia、Malaysia AI Residency及KrackedDevs共同主辦，課程涵蓋提示工程（prompt engineering，即向AI系統下達結構化指令的技術）、工作流程自動化，以及實際AI部署應用。YTL AI Labs將透過旗下「Ilmu Chat」平台提供AI運算點數，支援整場培訓活動。

  學習松同時作為官方「馬來西亞紀錄大全」的挑戰項目，意在以同步AI授課的規模締造全國里程碑。主辦單位表示，本場活動與數位部「Rakyat Digital增能框架」高度接軌，目標對象涵蓋學生、在職專業人士、企業主及本地開發者。

  ## 講者陣容與活動議程

  已確認出席的講者包括：數位部長哥賓星、Kelas Sekejap共同創辦人Khairy Jamaluddin與Shahril Hamdan、Axiata集團首席策略暨轉型長Rainer Deutschmann博士、Supabase執行長暨創辦人Paul Copplestone、500 Global執行合夥人Khailee Ng，以及YTL AI Labs執行長馮志文（Foong Chee Mun）。

  除學習松外，兩日活動議程還包括：邀請創業者與政策制定者進行現場錄製的Podcast舞台、「Pasar AI展覽」（互動展示政府與民間數位公共基礎設施成果），以及涵蓋金融科技、教育、醫療保健與企業效率等應用情境的開發者與創作者示範展演。

  ## 對馬來西亞AI議程的深遠意義

  AIMTO_26標誌著馬來西亞推動全國AI計畫方式的刻意轉型。二○二五年峰會是在馬來西亞擔任東協輪值主席期間舉辦，定位在區域部長級層次；二○二六年版則以直接建構全民能力為核心，並藉政府入口網站的發布，將整場活動與具有約束力的MD2030政策時間表緊密綁定。這種制度性錨定使AIMTO_26有別於一般產業研討會，也讓「Rakyat Digital」入口網站的發布成為本次活動最具深遠影響的成果。
