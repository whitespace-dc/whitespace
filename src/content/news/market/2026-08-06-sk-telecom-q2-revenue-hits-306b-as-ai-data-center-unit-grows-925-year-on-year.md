body_en: |
  SK Telecom reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of KRW 4.36 trillion (US$3.06 billion), with its artificial intelligence data center (AIDC) business — facilities purpose-built to run compute-intensive AI workloads — recording the fastest growth of any segment in the company's portfolio.

  Revenue from AIDCs reached KRW 136.2 billion (US$95.7 million) for the quarter, up 92.5 percent compared with the same period a year earlier, according to the company's earnings release.

  ## Financial results

  SK Telecom attributed the overall revenue performance to stable profitability in its core telecommunications business alongside rapid expansion in AI infrastructure. Park Jong-seok, the company's chief financial officer, said in a statement that the first half of 2026 was focused on "strengthening fundamentals" in telecoms while "laying the foundation for the rapid expansion" of the AIDC business.

  ## SK Hyper subsidiary and investment plan

  To accelerate that expansion, SK Telecom has approved the creation of SK Hyper, a dedicated subsidiary that will develop the company's AIDC business. SK Hyper is backed by an investment of KRW 750 billion (US$500 million) through 2030, according to the company. The subsidiary will be responsible for securing land and power capacity for new facilities and for attracting international customers, the company said.

  SK Telecom said it aims to bring AI data centers online in phases starting in 2029, targeting a combined capacity of 5 gigawatts (GW). For context, 5 GW is roughly equivalent to the continuous output of five large nuclear power reactors, illustrating the scale of power infrastructure the company is planning to support.

  ## Market context

  SK Telecom framed the push in terms of surging global demand. "As generative AI adoption spreads across industries and demand for high-performance computing infrastructure surges, timely delivery of AI infrastructure for customers such as global Big Tech companies has never been more critical," the company said in its earnings release.

  Market research firm Mordor Intelligence forecasts the South Korean AIDC market will reach US$1.89 billion in value by 2030, growth the firm expects to be driven by increased public-sector funding, hyperscale cloud expansion, and nationwide 5G network adoption, according to the earnings release.

  ## What it means

  SK Telecom's 92.5% AIDC revenue growth in a single quarter signals a meaningful shift in how the carrier is positioning itself — from a traditional telecoms operator toward an AI infrastructure provider competing for hyperscale and enterprise cloud customers. Park said the company intends to "play a leading role in enabling Korea to emerge as an AI infrastructure hub in Asia."

  The company's next concrete milestone is the phased delivery of AI data center capacity beginning in 2029, underpinned by the KRW 750 billion committed to SK Hyper through the end of the decade.

  ## SK電信Q2營收逾3,060億元，AI資料中心部門年增92.5%

  SK電信公布2026年第二季營收達4.36兆韓元（約30.6億美元），其人工智慧資料中心（AIDC）業務——專為執行高運算密度AI工作負載所打造的設施——成為集團旗下各事業部門中成長最快的一塊。

  根據公司發布的財報，該季AI資料中心部門營收達1,362億韓元（約9,570萬美元），較去年同期大幅成長92.5%。

  ## 財務表現

  SK電信將整體營收表現歸功於核心電信業務的穩健獲利，以及AI基礎設施的快速擴張。公司財務長朴鍾錫（Park Jong-seok）在聲明中表示，2026年上半年的工作重心在於「強化電信業務基本面」，同時「為AIDC業務的快速擴張奠定基礎」。

  ## SK Hyper子公司與投資計畫

  為加速業務擴張，SK電信已批准成立專責子公司SK Hyper，負責推動集團的AI資料中心事業。根據公司說明，SK Hyper將獲得總計7,500億韓元（約5億美元）的投資挹注，資金承諾期限至2030年。該子公司將負責為新建設施取得土地與電力容量，並積極爭取國際客戶。

  SK電信表示，計劃自2029年起分階段讓AI資料中心陸續上線，目標總容量達5吉瓦（GW）。作為參考，5GW大致相當於五座大型核能電廠的持續發電輸出，充分說明該公司為支撐這一規模所需規劃的龐大電力基礎設施。

  ## 市場背景

  SK電信以全球需求急速攀升為此次積極布局定調。公司在財報中指出：「隨著生成式AI在各產業的應用持續擴散，高效能運算基礎設施的需求急劇升溫，能否及時為全球大型科技公司等客戶提供AI基礎設施，其重要性已前所未有地凸顯。」

  市場調查機構Mordor Intelligence預測，南韓AI資料中心市場規模將於2030年達到18.9億美元。財報引述該機構分析，預期推動成長的主要動力包括公共部門投資增加、超大規模雲端擴張，以及全國性5G網路的普及。

  ## 意義解讀

  SK電信單季AIDC營收年增92.5%，清楚顯示這家電信業者正在進行一場意義深遠的戰略轉型——從傳統電信營運商蛻變為爭食超大規模與企業雲端客戶的AI基礎設施供應商。財務長朴鍾錫表示，公司打算「在推動韓國成為亞洲AI基礎設施樞紐的進程中，扮演領銜角色」。

  公司下一個具體里程碑，是自2029年起分階段交付AI資料中心容量，背後由承諾至本世紀末的7,500億韓元SK Hyper投資計畫作為後盾。
