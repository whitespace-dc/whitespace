  ## Wistron chief says AI server demand firm after Meta compute-rental report stirs market

  Wistron Chairman Lin Hsien-ming said AI server demand remains stable and unchanged, moving to counter market anxiety triggered by reports that Meta plans to rent out surplus artificial intelligence compute capacity. Lin spoke on July 2 at an AI Talent Forum hosted by Wistron subsidiary Wistron Education and Technology, making him the first chairman among the world's three largest AI server contract manufacturers to publicly address the Meta news.

  Reports that Meta intends to sell compute capacity had prompted concern among investors that large technology companies may be pulling back on AI infrastructure spending. Lin said those concerns are misplaced. "Everyone is still chasing capacity," he told reporters. "Demand is quite stable — there is no sign of loosening."

  ## Component shortages seen as manageable

  Lin acknowledged that some component shortages have recently affected both PC and AI server supply chains, but said he is confident the issues will be resolved. He said Wistron will fulfill its customer commitments.

  ## Compute donation program demand doubles

  Lin pointed to Wistron's own GPU compute donation program as evidence of strong underlying demand. Launched in 2025, the program donates one million GPU compute-hours annually to startups and academic institutions. In the first half of 2026 alone, applications already reached 1.2 million hours — exceeding the original full-year target. Wistron plans to provide a further 1.2 million hours in the second half of the year, bringing the 2026 total to approximately 2.4 million hours. Even at that level, Lin said, supply may fall short of applicant demand.

  Lin said the program's goal is to give users access to sufficient compute so they can generate new applications, create new technology, or develop new business models.

  ## Taiwan's manufacturing-to-application shift

  Lin used the occasion to argue that Taiwan must shift from hardware manufacturing toward becoming a nation that applies AI at scale. Competing globally using legacy models and labor-intensive approaches is no longer viable, he said; the country must change its thinking to build next-generation competitiveness.

  Internally, Wistron has increased its use of AI tokens — the units of input and output processed by large language models — and is examining how to improve efficiency and potentially convert self-generated tokens into commercial value, Lin said. The company is also pursuing partnerships with NeoCloud providers, a term for newer cloud service companies building large-scale AI infrastructure, to develop additional business opportunities.

  ## Industry significance

  Lin's intervention carries weight because Wistron is among the largest AI server contract manufacturers globally, and his public rebuttal of the slowdown narrative is the first from a peer-group executive since the Meta compute-rental reports surfaced. Whether other major contract manufacturers follow with similar statements will be a signal of how broadly the industry shares his assessment of demand durability.

  Wistron's GPU compute donation program, which will distribute up to 2.4 million compute-hours in 2026, is expected to remain oversubscribed through the end of the year, according to Lin.

  ## 緯創董事長：AI伺服器需求穩健，Meta算力出租傳聞引發市場疑慮

  緯創董事長林憲銘表示，AI伺服器需求維持穩定、並無改變，此番發言旨在化解市場因Meta擬出租閒置人工智慧算力而引發的焦慮情緒。林憲銘於7月2日出席緯創子公司緯育舉辦的「AI人才論壇」時向媒體發表談話，成為全球三大AI伺服器代工廠中，首位公開回應Meta相關消息的董事長。

  有報導指出Meta計畫對外銷售算力，此消息令投資人憂心大型科技公司可能縮減AI基礎設施支出。林憲銘認為這樣的擔憂並不成立。他向媒體表示：「大家都還在搶產能，需求相當穩定，完全沒有鬆動的跡象。」

  ## 零組件短缺情況可控

  林憲銘坦承，近期部分零組件短缺問題已波及PC與AI伺服器供應鏈，但他對問題的解決抱持信心，並強調緯創將履行對客戶的承諾。

  ## 算力捐贈計畫需求倍增

  林憲銘以緯創自身的GPU算力捐贈計畫為例，佐證市場對AI算力的強勁需求。這項計畫於2025年啟動，每年捐贈100萬GPU算力小時給新創公司與學術機構。然而，僅2026年上半年，申請量便已達到120萬小時，超越原訂全年目標。為此，緯創計畫在下半年再提供120萬小時，使2026年全年合計約達240萬小時。即便如此，林憲銘表示，供給量恐怕仍難以滿足所有申請者的需求。

  林憲銘說明，這項計畫的目標是讓使用者取得充足算力，進而催生新應用、創造新技術，或發展新商業模式。

  ## 台灣須從製造走向應用

  林憲銘也藉此場合呼籲，台灣必須從硬體製造轉型，成為大規模應用AI的國家。他指出，沿用舊有模式與勞力密集做法在全球競爭中已難以為繼，台灣必須轉換思維，建立下一世代的競爭力。

  就緯創內部而言，林憲銘表示公司已擴大使用AI tokens——即大型語言模型處理輸入與輸出的計量單位——並積極研究如何提升效率，乃至將自行產生的tokens轉化為商業價值。此外，緯創也正與NeoCloud業者洽談合作，NeoCloud係指正在大規模建置AI基礎設施的新興雲端服務業者，期望藉此開拓更多商機。

  ## 產業意義

  林憲銘此番表態具有一定份量，因為緯創是全球最大的AI伺服器代工廠之一，而他的公開駁斥也是自Meta算力出租消息曝光以來，同業高層首度出面反駁需求放緩的說法。其他主要代工廠是否跟進發表類似聲明，將是觀察產業界對需求韌性看法是否一致的重要指標。

  據林憲銘表示，緯創GPU算力捐贈計畫2026年預計發放最多240萬算力小時，預期全年申請量將持續超出供給。
