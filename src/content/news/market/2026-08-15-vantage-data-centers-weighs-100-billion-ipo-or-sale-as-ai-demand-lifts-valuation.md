body_en: |
  Vantage Data Centers, a major hyperscale data center developer and operator, is in early-stage discussions about a public listing or sale that could value the company at approximately $100 billion, according to people familiar with the matter who spoke to Reuters on condition of anonymity because the talks are confidential.

  A stock market listing could raise around $10 billion and, at the cited valuation, would be the largest initial public offering (IPO) in data center history, two of the people said. The company is also considering a full or partial sale, a third person said.

  Vantage is backed by private equity firm Silver Lake and infrastructure investor DigitalBridge Group. Silver Lake declined to comment; DigitalBridge and Vantage did not immediately respond to requests for comment, Reuters reported.

  ## State of discussions

  The company has held preliminary conversations in recent weeks about possible exit paths and met informally with financial advisers, the sources said. No formal process has been launched, the deliberations remain at an early stage, and any plans — including timing, structure, and transaction size — remain subject to change. Vantage could ultimately decide not to pursue any transaction.

  ## Vantage's capital base and partnerships

  Vantage has raised roughly $11 billion since late 2023, including a $9.2 billion equity investment led by DigitalBridge and Silver Lake. The valuation implied by those fundraising rounds was not disclosed.

  The company recently partnered with Oracle and OpenAI on a data center campus in Wisconsin connected to Stargate, a joint venture among SoftBank, OpenAI, and Oracle to build AI data center infrastructure of up to $500 billion in investment and 10 gigawatts (GW) of capacity. A gigawatt of data center power is enough to support large-scale cloud and AI computing operations roughly equivalent to several major hyperscale campuses.

  ## Why data center valuations are rising

  Data center operators have attracted billions of dollars from investors seeking exposure to the rapid growth in computing capacity required to support artificial intelligence workloads, the sources said. Technology companies and other strategic buyers have also shown increasing interest in acquiring data center assets to secure AI capacity, adding a potential buyer pool beyond public market investors.

  ## Broader IPO revival in the sector

  Vantage's deliberations coincide with a wider reopening of public markets to data center companies. Reuters reported last month that data center operator Switch had hired banks for an IPO that could raise up to $10 billion and value the company at about $80 billion. CyrusOne is separately preparing for a potential IPO as early as 2027, Reuters reported.

  If Vantage proceeds at the cited $100 billion figure, it would surpass Switch's reported valuation and set a new benchmark for the sector's public market ambitions. A final decision on whether, when, and how to pursue a transaction has not been made.
body_zh: |
  據知情人士透露，超大規模資料中心開發商暨營運商Vantage Data Centers，目前正處於評估公開上市或出售的初步階段，公司估值約達1,000億美元。消息人士以談判內容屬機密為由，向路透社匿名表示，此舉規模若成真，將改寫資料中心IPO史上的最大紀錄。

  其中兩位知情人士指出，若成功掛牌上市，本次IPO有望募集約100億美元資金；以前述估值計算，這將成為資料中心史上規模最大的首次公開募股。第三位知情人士則表示，公司也正評估全數或部分出售的可能性。

  Vantage的股東包括私募股權公司銀湖資本（Silver Lake）及基礎設施投資機構DigitalBridge集團。銀湖資本婉拒置評；DigitalBridge與Vantage均未立即回應路透社的採訪請求，路透社報導。

  ## 討論現況

  消息人士表示，Vantage近幾週已就可能的退場路徑進行初步磋商，並與財務顧問展開非正式接觸。目前尚未啟動正式程序，相關討論仍處於早期階段，包括時間點、交易架構及規模在內的一切計畫均可能隨時調整。Vantage最終也可能決定不推進任何交易。

  ## Vantage的資本基礎與合作夥伴

  Vantage自2023年底以來已累計募資約110億美元，其中包括由DigitalBridge與銀湖資本共同主導的92億美元股權投資。該等募資輪次所隱含的公司估值並未對外揭露。

  Vantage近期與甲骨文（Oracle）及OpenAI攜手合作，在美國威斯康辛州共同開發一座資料中心園區，並連結至「星際之門」（Stargate）計畫。「星際之門」為軟銀、OpenAI與甲骨文共同組成的合資企業，目標是建設總投資規模上看5,000億美元、總容量達10吉瓦（GW）的人工智慧資料中心基礎設施。一吉瓦的資料中心電力容量，足以支撐相當於數座大型超大規模園區的大規模雲端運算及人工智慧運算業務。

  ## 資料中心估值為何持續攀升

  消息人士表示，資料中心營運商持續吸引大量投資人資金湧入，原因在於支撐人工智慧工作負載所需的運算容量需求快速成長。科技公司及其他策略買家對收購資料中心資產的興趣也與日俱增，希望藉此鎖定人工智慧所需產能，使潛在買方不再局限於公開市場投資人。

  ## 產業IPO市場全面復甦

  Vantage的評估動向，恰與資料中心業者重返公開市場的大趨勢相互呼應。路透社上月報導，資料中心營運商Switch已委託多家銀行安排IPO事宜，預計募資規模最高達100億美元，公司估值約800億美元。CyrusOne亦正分別籌備潛在IPO，最快可能於2027年掛牌上市，路透社報導。

  若Vantage最終以前述1,000億美元估值推進交易，將超越Switch的報導估值，為整體產業在公開市場的企圖心樹立全新標竿。目前，是否推進交易、何時推進以及採取何種交易形式，均尚未作出最終決定。
