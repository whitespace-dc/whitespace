  ## Indonesian telcos grow revenue through Q3 2026, though profit results diverge

  Several Indonesian listed telecommunications companies posted positive revenue growth in the first nine months of 2026, with data services underpinning results across the sector, even as net profit performance varied among operators, according to Kontan.

  PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk, the state-controlled operator listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange under the ticker TLKM, reported revenue of Rp 113.5 trillion (Indonesian rupiah) through the third quarter of 2026, a 3.5% increase year-on-year. Despite the revenue gain, Telkom's net profit fell 3.89% year-on-year to Rp 14.4 trillion over the same period, Kontan reported.

  ## Revenue up, profits under pressure

  The divergence between Telkom's rising revenue and declining net profit illustrates a pattern Kontan described as common across the sector: companies are growing their top lines but facing pressure on the bottom line. The report noted that net profit realizations varied among telco issuers even as revenues broadly improved.

  Data services were identified as the continuing anchor of telecom revenue in Indonesia, with Kontan's headline characterizing the sector's reliance on the data business as ongoing rather than transitional.

  The results cover the January–September 2026 period, representing the first three quarters of the fiscal year.
body_zh: |
  ## 印尼電信業者2026年前三季營收成長，惟獲利表現出現分化

  根據印尼財經媒體《Kontan》報導，印尼多家上市電信業者在2026年前三季皆繳出正向營收成長，數據服務全面支撐各業者的營運表現，然而各電信業者的稅後淨利表現卻大相徑庭。

  印尼國營電信業者、在印尼證券交易所以代號「TLKM」掛牌上市的PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk，2026年前三季合併營收達新台幣折合人民幣計113.5兆印尼盾（印尼盾），年增3.5%。然而，儘管營收呈現成長態勢，Telkom同期稅後淨利卻年減3.89%，降至14.4兆印尼盾，《Kontan》對此有所報導。

  ## 營收上揚，獲利承壓

  Telkom營收成長卻淨利下滑的現象，正反映出《Kontan》所描述的產業共同困境：各業者雖能持續拉抬頂線（營收），卻普遍面臨底線（淨利）受壓的窘境。報導指出，即便各電信業上市公司的營收整體改善，稅後淨利的實現程度仍因業者而異，呈現明顯差異。

  數據服務持續被認定為印尼電信業營收的核心支柱。《Kontan》以「持續依賴數據業務」而非「過渡性倚重」來描述整體產業的現況，意味著數據業務已是印尼電信業長期且穩定的收入來源，而非短期現象。

  上述財務數據涵蓋2026年1月至9月期間，即本會計年度前三季。
