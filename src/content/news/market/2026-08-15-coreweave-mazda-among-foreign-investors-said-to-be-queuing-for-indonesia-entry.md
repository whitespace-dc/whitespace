  ## Foreign investment commitments mount

  Four foreign companies — CoreWeave, Mazda, Pure Battery Technologies, and Takeda — are preparing to invest in Indonesia, Indonesia's Ministry of Investment and Downstreaming, also known as BKPM (Badan Koordinasi Penanaman Modal, the Investment Coordinating Board), said in data covering the first half of 2026, according to Indonesian financial outlet Kontan.

  Foreign direct investment (FDI) into Indonesia reached Rp 507.6 trillion in the first six months of 2026, representing 50.2% of the government's full-year target, BKPM data showed. That figure marks a 17.3% increase from the Rp 432.6 trillion recorded in the same period of 2025.

  ## Industrial estates watch for inflows

  Kontan framed the incoming investment wave in terms of its potential impact on Indonesian publicly listed industrial estate operators, singling out three companies by their stock tickers: BEST (PT Bekasi Fajar Industrial Estate Tbk), DMAS (PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk), and KIJA (PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk). DMAS develops the Deltamas industrial and property area in Cikarang, West Java.

  ## 外資承諾接連湧入

  據印尼財經媒體《Kontan》報導，印尼投資與下游化部（即投資協調委員會，印尼文縮寫BKPM）公布的2026年上半年資料顯示，CoreWeave、馬自達、Pure Battery Technologies及武田藥品等四家外資企業正積極籌備赴印尼投資。

  BKPM數據顯示，2026年前六個月，印尼外國直接投資（FDI）金額達到5,076億印尼盾，相當於政府全年目標的50.2%。這一數字較2025年同期的4,326億印尼盾，大幅成長17.3%。

  ## 工業園區靜待資金挹注

  《Kontan》從印尼上市工業園區業者的角度切入，分析這波外資浪潮可能帶來的影響，並點名三家以股票代碼標示的公司：BEST（PT Bekasi Fajar Industrial Estate Tbk）、DMAS（PT Puradelta Lestari Tbk）以及KIJA（PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk）。其中，DMAS負責開發位於西爪哇省芝卡朗的Deltamas工業暨不動產園區。
