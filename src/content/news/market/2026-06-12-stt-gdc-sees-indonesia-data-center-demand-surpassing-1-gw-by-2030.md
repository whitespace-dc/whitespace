  ## STT GDC projects Indonesia data center demand above 1 GW by 2030

  ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC) projects that demand for data center capacity in Indonesia will surpass 1 gigawatt (GW) — enough to power roughly one million homes — by 2030, as artificial intelligence (AI) adoption and wider digitalization push requirements higher across multiple sectors.

  Hendrikus Hendra Gozali, Country Head of STT GDC Indonesia, made the projection on Wednesday at the company's Campus Expansion Ceremony in Cikarang. "I see the need for more than 1 GW now and going forward it could be even larger," Gozali said, according to Kontan. "So the projection is for 2030."

  ## Expansion backdrop

  The remarks came at an event marking a campus expansion by STT GDC, a data center operator with facilities in Cikarang. The company tied its demand outlook directly to the intersection of mass-market digitalization and rising AI workloads, which typically require substantially more power and cooling per rack than conventional enterprise computing.

  ## What it means

  A national demand figure above 1 GW would place Indonesia among the larger data center markets in Southeast Asia by installed capacity. The projection signals that operators are planning infrastructure at a scale commensurate with AI-era workloads, where a single high-density cluster can consume tens of megawatts.

  Gozali said current demand already exceeds 1 GW and that the figure could grow further beyond the 2030 horizon.

  ## STT GDC預測印尼資料中心需求2030年前將突破1吉瓦

  ST Telemedia Global Data Centres（STT GDC）預測，隨著人工智慧（AI）應用加速普及、多個產業數位化需求持續擴張，印尼對資料中心容量的需求將於2030年前突破1吉瓦（GW）——相當於大約百萬戶家庭的用電規模。

  STT GDC印尼區負責人恆德里庫斯・亨德拉・戈扎利（Hendrikus Hendra Gozali）週三在芝卡朗（Cikarang）的園區擴建典禮上提出上述預測。據《Kontan》報導，戈扎利表示：「我認為現在的需求就已超過1吉瓦，往後只會更大。這個預測是針對2030年所提出的。」

  ## 擴建背景

  前述言論是在STT GDC舉行園區擴建典禮的場合上發表的。該公司在芝卡朗設有資料中心設施，並將其需求展望直接與大眾市場數位化浪潮，以及AI工作負載的持續成長相互連結。相較於傳統企業運算，AI工作負載在每個機架所需的電力與冷卻量通常高出許多。

  ## 市場意涵

  若印尼全國需求突破1吉瓦，按裝置容量計算，該國將躋身東南亞規模較大的資料中心市場之列。這項預測顯示，業者正依照AI時代工作負載的量級規劃基礎設施——在這個時代，單一高密度叢集的耗電量便可能高達數十百萬瓦（MW）。

  戈扎利表示，目前需求已超過1吉瓦，而這個數字在2030年以後可能還會進一步攀升。
