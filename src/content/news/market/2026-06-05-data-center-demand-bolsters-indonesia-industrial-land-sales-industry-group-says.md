  ## Indonesia industrial land sector posts growth through April 2026

  Indonesia's industrial zone sector recorded strong land sales growth in the first months of 2026, with data center demand providing meaningful support, according to Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia (HKI), the country's industrial estate association.

  HKI Chairman Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana said industrial land sales through April 2026 showed solid year-on-year growth, outpacing the same period in 2025, even as the broader global environment remains marked by caution.

  HKI described the sector's performance as reflecting a positive growth trend despite global uncertainty. The association's assessment covers industrial estates across Indonesia, which are designated zones that aggregate manufacturing, logistics, and technology tenants on shared infrastructure.

  ## Data center activity as a demand driver

  The HKI headline finding — that data center business is supporting industrial land sales — points to technology infrastructure as a contributor to estate absorption. Data center operators typically require large, serviced land parcels with reliable power and connectivity, making industrial estates a natural fit. HKI's comments suggest this tenant category has been an active buyer or lessor of industrial land in the period under review.

  The image accompanying HKI's statement featured Subang Smartpolitan, a development by PT Surya Semesta Internusa Tbk (ticker: SSIA), one of Indonesia's publicly listed industrial estate developers.

  ## Significance

  Strong early-2026 land sales, if sustained, would indicate that Indonesia's industrial property sector has so far absorbed global trade and economic headwinds without a material slowdown. Data center demand contributing to that performance aligns with broader regional trends of technology companies seeking to place compute infrastructure closer to Southeast Asian end-users. HKI said the positive trend extended through April 2026.

  ## 印尼工業用地產業2026年前四個月錄得成長

  根據印尼工業區協會（Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia，HKI）的資料，印尼工業區產業在2026年初的土地銷售錄得強勁年增幅，資料中心需求提供了相當程度的支撐。

  HKI主席Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana表示，截至2026年4月的工業用地銷售呈現穩健的年增成長，表現優於2025年同期，即便整體全球環境仍籠罩於謹慎氛圍之中。

  HKI將此產業表現定性為在全球不確定性下依然呈現正向成長趨勢。協會的評估範圍涵蓋印尼各地工業園區，這些園區是將製造業、物流與科技租戶集中於共用基礎設施的指定區域。

  ## 資料中心活動成為需求驅動力

  HKI的核心發現——資料中心業務正在支撐工業用地銷售——凸顯科技基礎設施已成為園區吸收率的重要貢獻來源。資料中心業者通常需要面積廣大、配套完善的土地，並要求穩定的電力供應與網路連線，使工業園區成為天然的首選場域。HKI的說明顯示，在評估期間，此類租戶已積極買入或承租工業用地。

  HKI聲明所附的圖片以Subang Smartpolitan園區為背景，該開發案由印尼上市工業地產商PT Surya Semesta Internusa Tbk（股票代號：SSIA）所開發。

  ## 重要性

  若2026年初強勁的工業用地銷售態勢能夠延續，將意味著印尼工業地產產業迄今尚未因全球貿易與經濟逆風而出現明顯放緩。資料中心需求對此表現的貢獻，也與科技企業積極在東南亞終端用戶周邊部署運算基礎設施的區域整體趨勢相吻合。HKI表示，此一正向趨勢已延伸至2026年4月。
