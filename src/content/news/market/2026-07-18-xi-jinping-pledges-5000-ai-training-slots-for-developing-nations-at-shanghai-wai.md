body_en: |
  Chinese President Xi Jinping called for global open-source AI cooperation and announced concrete capacity-building commitments for developing nations at the opening of the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) in Shanghai on Thursday, positioning China as the centre of a new international AI governance order as tensions with the United States over technology leadership continue.

  Xi told delegates at the WAIC opening ceremony that countries should seize what he called a "historic opportunity" presented by open-source AI. He announced that China will provide 5,000 AI training opportunities to developing countries over the next five years and will establish international AI application cooperation centres with six regional blocs: the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), the League of Arab States, the African Union, the Community of Latin American and Caribbean States, the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO), and BRICS.

  China will also enable 30 countries to access MAZU, an AI-powered meteorological warning system, to provide weather-related early warnings.

  ## Governance and security principles

  Xi called for a "people-centred approach" to AI and urged nations to jointly build what he described as a "fair and equitable system for global AI governance" that would "bridge digital divides" and "promote sustainable development." He called for laws and regulations covering monitoring, early warning mechanisms, and emergency response systems to prevent malicious abuse of the technology and to ensure "AI is always under human control."

  He also warned against "overstretching the national security concept in the field of AI or placing one country's security over that of others" — language that did not name Washington but was delivered against the backdrop of US export restrictions on advanced semiconductors to China.

  "AI is an invaluable asset that encapsulates humanity's collective wisdom," Xi said. "We should enhance alignment and coordination on AI development strategies, governance rules, and technical standards to form a consensus-based global governance framework at an early date."

  ## US-China context

  The US has restricted sales of some advanced processors to China at a time when semiconductor supply chains are already strained by ongoing conflict in the Middle East and tariffs imposed by US President Donald Trump. Chinese state media has framed Beijing's AI strategy as a response to what it calls a US-led "AI Iron Curtain," according to Reuters. The WAIC conference reflects how China is moving to advance open AI models to compete with US companies such as OpenAI and Anthropic, Reuters added.

  Although Xi did not address Washington directly, he said the world should focus on "openness" to drive AI innovation and economic development. "We should seize this rare historic opportunity to encourage open source, openness, collaboration and sharing," he said.

  ## Five-Year Plan and domestic infrastructure

  Xi used the speech to highlight China's 15th Five-Year Plan, which commences this year and sets out the country's economic and social development agenda through 2030. He said the plan creates significant opportunities for the international community.

  The address comes as China prepares to spend approximately two trillion yuan over the next five years building data centres across the country. Government agencies including the National Development and Reform Commission are drafting a blueprint to create a network of interconnected computing hubs. State firms China Mobile and China Telecom are set to operate the bulk of those facilities and ensure they are connected, according to Capacity Media.

  ## What the commitments mean for the industry

  Taken together, the WAIC pledges tie China's domestic infrastructure build-out directly to an outward-facing diplomacy of AI capacity sharing, particularly with developing-nation blocs in Asia, Africa, Latin America, and the Middle East. The six regional cooperation centre announcements and the 5,000 training commitment give concrete form to what has until now been a broadly stated Chinese preference for multilateral AI governance over bilateral or US-led frameworks.

  China's planned two-trillion-yuan data centre investment, to be executed over the same five-year horizon as the new diplomatic commitments, represents the infrastructure base against which those international pledges will be measured.

  ## 習近平在上海世界人工智慧大會承諾提供發展中國家五千個AI培訓名額

  中國國家主席習近平週四出席在上海舉行的世界人工智慧大會（WAIC）開幕式，呼籲全球推動開源AI合作，並宣布一系列針對發展中國家的具體能力建設承諾。在中美兩國科技領導權角力持續之際，此舉意在將中國塑造為新國際AI治理秩序的核心。

  習近平在大會開幕典禮上告訴與會代表，各國應把握他所稱的開源AI帶來的「歷史性機遇」。他宣布，中國將在未來五年為發展中國家提供五千個AI培訓機會，並將與六大地區組織設立國際AI應用合作中心，包括：東南亞國家協會（東協）、阿拉伯國家聯盟、非洲聯盟、拉丁美洲和加勒比海國家共同體、上海合作組織（上合組織），以及金磚國家集團（BRICS）。

  此外，中國還將讓三十個國家接入「媽祖」（MAZU）AI氣象預警系統，協助這些國家取得天氣相關的早期預警資訊。

  ## 治理與安全原則

  習近平呼籲以「以人為本」的方式發展AI，並敦促各國共同建立他所描述的「公平公正的全球AI治理體系」，以「彌合數位落差」並「促進永續發展」。他主張應建立涵蓋監測、早期預警機制與緊急應對系統的法律法規，防止技術遭惡意濫用，確保「AI始終在人類的掌控之下」。

  他同時警告，不應「將國家安全概念過度延伸至AI領域，或將一國的安全凌駕於他國之上」。這番措辭雖未點名華盛頓，卻是在美國對中國實施先進半導體出口管制的背景下發表的。

  「人工智慧是凝聚人類集體智慧的寶貴財富，」習近平表示，「我們應加強在AI發展策略、治理規則與技術標準上的協調與對接，儘早形成基於共識的全球治理框架。」

  ## 中美角力背景

  美國已限制部分先進處理器對中國的銷售，而半導體供應鏈當前亦因中東持續衝突及美國總統川普所課徵的關稅而承受壓力。據路透社報導，中國官方媒體將北京的AI戰略定性為回應美國主導的「AI鐵幕」。世界人工智慧大會的召開，反映出中國正積極推動開放AI模型，以與OpenAI、Anthropic等美國企業競爭。路透社亦指出了這一趨勢。

  儘管習近平並未直接點名華盛頓，他仍表示全球應聚焦「開放」，以驅動AI創新與經濟發展。「我們應把握這一難得的歷史機遇，鼓勵開源、開放、合作與共享，」他說。

  ## 第十五個五年計畫與國內基礎建設

  習近平在演講中特別強調中國今年啟動的第十五個五年計畫，該計畫勾勒出中國至二○三○年的經濟與社會發展藍圖。他表示，這份計畫為國際社會創造了重大機遇。

  此次演講之際，中國正準備在未來五年投入約兩兆人民幣，於全國各地興建資料中心。國家發展和改革委員會等政府機構正在擬定藍圖，規劃建立一套互聯互通的運算樞紐網路。據Capacity Media報導，中國移動與中國電信兩家國有企業將承擔這批設施的主要營運工作，並負責確保設施之間的互聯互通。

  ## 這些承諾對產業意味著什麼

  綜合來看，世界人工智慧大會上的各項承諾，將中國的國內基礎建設擴張與面向外部的AI能力共享外交緊密相連，尤其聚焦於亞洲、非洲、拉丁美洲及中東的發展中國家集團。六個地區合作中心的宣布，以及五千個培訓名額的承諾，為中國長期以來模糊倡導的「多邊AI治理優先於雙邊或美國主導框架」立場，提供了具體的落實形式。

  中國計畫投入的兩兆人民幣資料中心建設，與上述外交承諾同樣以五年為期，這筆基礎建設投資將成為衡量中國能否兌現國際承諾的重要基準。
