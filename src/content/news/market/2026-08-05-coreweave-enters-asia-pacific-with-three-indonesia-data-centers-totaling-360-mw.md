body_en: |
  CoreWeave announced Tuesday it will build and operate three data centers in Indonesia, marking the AI cloud computing company's first physical presence in the Asia-Pacific region and adding 360 megawatts of contracted power capacity to its global network.

  The company said it will own and operate all three sites and will hire and train a local Indonesian team to staff them. The expansion adds to CoreWeave's existing footprint of 49 data centers worldwide, a figure reported as of March.

  ## Rationale: latency and data locality

  CoreWeave pointed to the particular demands of AI workloads in the region as the driver for the move. "Across Asia, enterprises, AI-native companies, and governments increasingly need AI compute located close to their data and users, driven by both latency-sensitive workloads and data locality requirements," the company said. Latency-sensitive workloads are applications where processing delays degrade output — such as real-time AI inference — while data locality requirements refer to legal or operational rules that restrict where data can be stored or processed.

  The company said the expansion also supports Indonesia's national AI ambitions, though it did not specify any government agreements.

  ## Capital spending and demand backdrop

  CoreWeave has been scaling infrastructure in response to surging global demand for AI cloud capacity, as technology companies compete to secure the hardware and cloud services needed to develop and run AI systems.

  In May, CoreWeave raised the lower bound of its 2026 capital expenditure forecast, citing higher component prices amid a shortage of memory chips. The company now plans to spend between $31 billion and $35 billion this year on infrastructure.

  The company has also secured several large cloud contracts in 2026. It expanded a deal with Meta to $21 billion and signed a multi-year agreement with Anthropic, the creator of the Claude AI assistant.

  ## Significance for the region

  The Indonesia announcement represents the first extension of CoreWeave's owned-and-operated data-center model into Asia-Pacific, a region where demand for locally hosted AI compute is growing alongside regulatory pressure to keep data within national borders. By committing to own all three facilities and build a local workforce, CoreWeave is establishing a long-term operational presence rather than a partnership or co-location arrangement.

  The three facilities are expected to reach a combined contracted power capacity of 360 megawatts upon completion, though CoreWeave did not disclose a timeline or phased opening schedule for the sites.

  ## 布局動機：延遲與資料在地化

  CoreWeave指出，推動此次擴張的關鍵因素，在於亞太地區AI工作負載的特殊需求。該公司表示：「在整個亞洲，企業、AI原生公司及各國政府越來越需要將AI算力部署在靠近自身資料與使用者的地方，這背後的驅動力來自對延遲敏感的工作負載，以及資料在地化的要求。」延遲敏感型工作負載，是指處理延遲會影響輸出品質的應用，例如即時AI推論；資料在地化要求，則是指法律或業務規範對資料儲存與處理地點所設下的限制。

  該公司亦表示，此次擴張有助於支持印尼的國家AI發展目標，但未透露任何具體的政府協議。

  ## 資本支出與需求背景

  CoreWeave持續擴大基礎設施建設，以因應全球AI雲端運算需求急速攀升的態勢，科技公司紛紛爭相取得開發和運行AI系統所需的硬體與雲端服務。

  今年五月，CoreWeave上調2026年資本支出預測的下限，原因是記憶體晶片短缺導致零組件價格上漲。該公司目前預計今年將在基礎設施上投入310億至350億美元。

  CoreWeave今年也已陸續簽下數筆大型雲端合約，包括將與Meta的合作規模擴大至210億美元，以及與Claude AI助理開發商Anthropic簽署多年期協議。

  ## 對區域的戰略意義

  此次印尼布局，是CoreWeave自有並自營的資料中心模式首度延伸至亞太地區。在監管壓力日益升高、各國要求資料留存境內的背景下，當地對在地AI算力的需求也正快速成長。透過承諾自行持有三座設施並建立在地工作團隊，CoreWeave確立了長期的在地營運布局，而非僅以合作夥伴關係或主機代管方式進場。

  三座設施完工後，預計合約用電容量合計將達360百萬瓦，但CoreWeave並未揭露完工時程或分階段啟用的計畫。
