body_en: |
  Telecom Italia's (TIM) board of directors has approved a takeover offer from Poste Italiane, Italy's state-controlled postal service, in a deal that would return the country's largest telecoms carrier to state hands after it was privatized in 1997.

  The board deemed the offer fair over the weekend, according to TIM. Based on Poste Italiane's closing share price last week, the transaction would value TIM at €13 billion ($14.8 billion), up from the initial €10.8 billion ($12.3 billion) bid that Poste submitted in March.

  ## Offer terms

  The board voted in favor of an offer structured as €1.67 in cash plus 0.218 newly issued Poste ordinary shares for each Telecom Italia ordinary share. The deal remains subject to shareholder approval.

  ## How Poste built its position

  Poste Italiane's path to a full takeover bid began in 2025, when it acquired a nine percent stake in TIM from Cassa Depositi e Prestiti (CDP), Italy's state-backed investment institution. Poste subsequently increased its shareholding through two separate stock purchases from Vivendi, the French media and telecoms conglomerate that had been TIM's largest shareholder. Vivendi had publicly opposed TIM's earlier sale of its fixed-line network unit, known as NetCo.

  ## The NetCo backdrop

  TIM sold NetCo, its fixed network spin-off covering Italy's landline grid infrastructure, to U.S. investment firm KKR in 2024 in a transaction valued at €22 billion ($25.6 billion). That sale separated TIM's physical network assets from its retail and services operations, leaving the rump company as the entity now subject to Poste's bid.

  ## Return to state ownership

  If TIM's shareholders accept Poste's offer, the carrier would revert to effective state control for the first time since its privatization in 1997. Poste Italiane is itself state-controlled, making the acquisition a consolidation of two major Italian communications utilities under public ownership. The deal would represent one of the more significant renationalizations in European telecoms in recent years.

  義大利電信（TIM）董事會已批准義大利國家控股郵政服務機構義大利郵政（Poste Italiane）所提出的收購要約。這項交易一旦完成，義大利最大電信業者將在1997年私有化後重回國家之手。

  根據TIM說法，董事會於週末認定此要約價格合理。以義大利郵政上週收盤股價計算，本次交易對TIM的估值為130億歐元（約合148億美元），較義大利郵政今年3月提出的初始要約108億歐元（約合123億美元）大幅提升。

  ## 收購條件

  董事會批准的收購要約結構為：TIM每股普通股可獲得1.67歐元現金，另加0.218股新發行的義大利郵政普通股。本次交易仍須取得股東大會批准，方能正式生效。

  ## 義大利郵政如何逐步建立持股地位

  義大利郵政邁向全面收購的進程始於2025年，當時它從義大利國家支持的投資機構「存款與貸款基金」（Cassa Depositi e Prestiti，CDP）手中收購了TIM 9%的股權。隨後，義大利郵政又透過兩筆分開的股權交易，陸續從法國媒體與電信集團威望迪（Vivendi）手中購入更多TIM股份，進一步提高持股比例。威望迪此前是TIM最大股東，曾公開反對TIM出售旗下固定線路網路部門「NetCo」的計畫。

  ## NetCo出售案的來龍去脈

  TIM於2024年將旗下固定網路分拆事業NetCo出售給美國投資公司KKR，交易金額達220億歐元（約合256億美元）。NetCo負責管理義大利全國固定電話網路基礎設施。這筆交易將TIM的實體網路資產與其零售及服務業務徹底切割，使得剩餘的TIM主體成為義大利郵政此次收購的標的。

  ## 重回國家所有

  若TIM股東接受義大利郵政的收購要約，這家電信業者將在1997年私有化以來首度重回國家實質管控。由於義大利郵政本身即為國家控股企業，這項收購案實際上意味著義大利兩大通訊公用事業將整合於公共所有制之下。這也將成為近年來歐洲電信業最具代表性的再國有化案例之一。
