  Zhongji Innolight Co., the Shenzhen-listed Chinese maker of optical transceiver modules and a supplier to Nvidia, has raised its Hong Kong initial public offering target to approximately $7 billion from an initial goal of roughly $5 billion, after international investors showed strong interest in the company's AI data center products during its roadshow, Commercial Times reported on June 18, citing Reuters and people familiar with the matter. The company is expected to launch the offering as early as July.

  The upward revision from the original $5 billion target followed investor demand generated during the roadshow, according to sources cited by Commercial Times. The increase reflects what the publication described as a hot environment of global AI computing expansion and cloud capital expenditure, which has driven attention to suppliers of components used in AI infrastructure.

  ## Products and market position

  Zhongji Innolight's core products are optical transceiver modules, components used in cloud computing and data center networks as well as mobile and fixed-line networks. The company is a supplier to Nvidia and, since 2023, has also entered the automotive intelligent systems sector, Commercial Times said. Its shares closed at RMB 1,276.11 on June 17, giving it a total market capitalization of RMB 1.423 trillion. The stock has risen nearly tenfold over the past year.

  ## Regulatory steps and listing structure

  The Hong Kong offering would be a secondary listing, as Zhongji Innolight already trades on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. Mainland Chinese regulations require domestic companies to obtain approval from Chinese regulators before listing overseas. The company is seeking that filing approval by the end of June, according to Commercial Times.

  Zhongji Innolight has confidentially submitted its Hong Kong listing application, though the company had not publicly responded to related inquiries at the time of reporting, Commercial Times said. Reports of a potential Hong Kong secondary listing first emerged in October of last year.

  ## Concurrent listings signal broader Hong Kong tech pipeline

  Luxshare Precision Industry Co., a major Apple supplier and electronics manufacturing group, is also preparing for a Hong Kong IPO, Commercial Times reported. Luxshare Precision has been conducting investor education activities ahead of a potential listing, with a launch expected as early as July and a fundraising target of approximately $3 billion.

  Analysts cited by Commercial Times expect Hong Kong technology IPO supply to increase noticeably in the second half of the year. The two proposed deals represent distinct segments of China's technology manufacturing sector: Zhongji Innolight in AI optical communications components, and Luxshare Precision in consumer electronics and precision manufacturing. Commercial Times said the concurrent timing would jointly test international capital's capacity to absorb Chinese hard-technology assets.

  ## What comes next

  Zhongji Innolight's receipt of mainland regulatory approval for the overseas listing filing, which the company is seeking by the end of June, is the next formal milestone before the Hong Kong offering can proceed. If the $7 billion target is met, the transaction would rank as one of the most prominent technology IPOs on the Hong Kong exchange this year, according to Commercial Times.

  ## 中際旭創赴港上市目標上調至70億美元　AI需求帶動投資人搶進

  據《工商時報》6月18日引述路透社及知情人士報導，在深圳掛牌上市的中國光模組大廠中際旭創，在路演期間獲得國際投資人對其AI資料中心產品的強烈興趣，香港首次公開募股（IPO）目標已從最初約50億美元上調至約70億美元，最快預計7月正式啟動。

  《工商時報》引述消息人士指出，此次上調係路演期間投資人需求所驅動。報導形容，目前全球AI算力擴張與雲端資本支出持續升溫，市場對AI基礎建設相關零組件供應商的關注度大幅提升，帶動此波估值修正。

  ## 產品線與市場定位

  中際旭創的核心產品為光收發模組，廣泛應用於雲端運算、資料中心網路及行動與固網通訊。該公司為輝達（NVIDIA）供應商，並自2023年起跨入車用智慧系統領域，《工商時報》指出。截至6月17日收盤，中際旭創股價為人民幣1,276.11元，總市值達人民幣1.423兆元，過去一年股價累計漲幅近十倍。

  ## 監管程序與上市架構

  由於中際旭創已在深圳證券交易所掛牌，此次香港上市將以第二上市形式進行。依據中國大陸相關法規，境內企業赴海外上市前須取得主管機關核准。《工商時報》報導，公司目標在6月底前完成境外上市備案申請。

  中際旭創已秘密遞交香港上市申請，惟截至報導截稿時，公司尚未公開回應相關詢問，《工商時報》表示。有關香港第二上市的傳聞最早於去年10月浮現。

  ## 多家科技企業同步叩關　香港IPO管線漸成規模

  《工商時報》同時報導，蘋果重要供應商暨電子製造集團立訊精密亦正籌備香港IPO。立訊精密已展開投資人教育活動，預計最快7月啟動，募資目標約30億美元。

  《工商時報》引述分析師預期，香港科技類IPO供給量將在下半年顯著增加。這兩宗擬議交易分別代表中國科技製造業的不同面向：中際旭創聚焦AI光通訊元件，立訊精密則深耕消費性電子與精密製造。《工商時報》指出，兩案同步推進，將共同檢驗國際資本市場對中國硬科技資產的承接能力。

  ## 後續關鍵里程碑

  中際旭創取得中國大陸主管機關的境外上市備案核准，是香港IPO得以推進的下一個正式關卡，公司目標在6月底前完成此程序。《工商時報》指出，若70億美元的募資目標順利達成，此案將躋身今年香港交易所規模最大的科技類IPO之列。
