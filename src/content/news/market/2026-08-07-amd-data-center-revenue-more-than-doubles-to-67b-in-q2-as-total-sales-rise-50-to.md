body_en: |
  AMD reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $11.54 billion, up 50 percent from the same period a year earlier, as its data center business — which sells server processors and AI accelerator chips — more than doubled year-over-year to account for 58 percent of total company revenue. Despite beating analyst estimates across revenue, profit, and third-quarter guidance, AMD shares fell nearly 9 percent in extended trading.

  ## Q2 financial results

  Data center revenue reached $6.72 billion for the quarter, a 107 percent increase from Q2 2025 and a 16.3 percent sequential gain from the $5.78 billion recorded in the first quarter. Analysts had projected $6.48 billion for the segment, according to data compiled by LSEG.

  Total quarterly revenue of $11.54 billion beat the analyst estimate of $11.28 billion. Adjusted profit was $1.66 per share, ahead of the $1.62 estimate. Net income was $2.3 billion.

  The remainder of AMD's revenue came from its client and gaming segment, which contributed $3.8 billion, and the embedded segment — chips used in industrial, automotive, and communications equipment — which added $977 million.

  Capital expenditure for the quarter reached $808 million, up from $282 million in the same period a year earlier, nearly a threefold increase that signals the company is expanding its manufacturing and infrastructure investments ahead of anticipated demand growth.

  ## What drove data center growth

  Data center revenue was driven by demand for AMD's EPYC server central processing units (CPUs) and Instinct graphics processing units (GPUs), which are used in AI training and inference workloads, the company said. Inference is the compute that occurs when a user queries an AI model such as a chatbot.

  AMD has also moved beyond selling individual chips to offering integrated AI systems that combine processors, networking gear, and related hardware — a shift the company said allows it to better compete with rack-scale products from Nvidia, which AMD is regarded as the closest rival to in the market for AI accelerator chips. Nvidia unveiled its next-generation "Vera" CPU in March, designed to pair with its "Rubin" line of graphics processors.

  AMD's second-generation Helios AI servers, featuring the MI455X AI accelerator and the "Venice" CPU manufactured by TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company), were in full production as of July and will begin shipping in the coming months, the company said.

  "We delivered an excellent quarter, with record revenue and profitability as Data Center revenue more than doubled year-over-year," Chief Executive Officer Lisa Su said. "We enter the second half with strong momentum as EPYC demand accelerates, Instinct deployments scale and Helios begins to ramp."

  ## Third-quarter guidance and longer-term outlook

  For the third quarter of 2026, AMD forecast revenue of approximately $13 billion, plus or minus $300 million. Analysts had estimated $12.52 billion, according to LSEG. Adjusted gross margin — the share of revenue retained after production costs — is projected at approximately 56 percent, in line with analyst estimates.

  Su said on the post-earnings conference call that AMD expects its data center sales to more than double in 2027, total revenue growth to exceed its 35 percent annual target, and annual earnings to surpass its $20-per-share goal set at the company's 2025 analyst day.

  AMD also revised its market size projections upward, citing an expansion faster than it had anticipated six months earlier. It now expects the global AI accelerator market to grow at a 45 percent compound annual rate to reach $1.4 trillion by 2030, and the server CPU market to grow at 50 percent annually to approximately $220 billion by the same year.

  Data center AI revenue growth in the third quarter is projected in the double digits, with further acceleration expected in the fourth quarter as Helios shipments scale. AMD said Helios sales will begin ramping in Q4, with initial shipments starting toward the end of Q3.

  ## Investor reaction

  AMD's stock fell nearly 9 percent in extended trading after the results, despite revenue, adjusted earnings per share, and third-quarter guidance all exceeding analyst expectations. The stock had more than doubled in 2026 before the results were released.

  "AMD is now in a similar position to Nvidia and the hyperscalers, where investors are looking for evidence that AI infrastructure investments will continue translating into accelerating returns," said Jacob Bourne, an analyst at market research firm Emarketer. Hyperscalers refers to large cloud computing providers that operate at massive scale.

  ## Recent deals and capacity commitments

  AMD has secured several significant customer and infrastructure agreements in recent months that underpin its forward growth projections.

  In July, the company agreed to sell AI research company Anthropic — maker of the Claude AI system — tens of billions of dollars worth of AI servers powered by up to 2 gigawatts (GW) of MI450 chips, with deliveries beginning in early 2027. AMD also agreed to invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic, contingent on deployment milestones. Anthropic is described as an IPO-bound company.

  Separately, AMD locked in up to 2.5 GW of data-center capacity through a deal with Core Scientific announced approximately one week before the earnings report, and received warrants to purchase Core Scientific stock as part of the agreement.

  The company is also benefiting from growing demand for CPUs used alongside GPUs in AI server configurations for inference workloads, a trend that has helped it gain server CPU market share from Intel, which has raised its own spending plans for the next two years to address the same demand environment, according to the sources.

  Looking further ahead, AMD said its 2027 rack-scale AI platform will integrate MI500 GPUs, Verano CPUs, and Pensando networking hardware in a system designed to support both copper and optical connectivity.
body_zh: |
  AMD公布2026年第二季營收達115.4億美元，較去年同期成長50%，旗下銷售伺服器處理器與AI加速晶片的資料中心業務年增逾倍，占全公司總營收的58%。儘管在營收、獲利及第三季展望三項指標均超越分析師預期，AMD盤後股價仍下跌近9%。

  ## 第二季財報結果

  資料中心業務本季營收達67.2億美元，較2025年第二季成長107%，並較第一季的57.8億美元環比增加16.3%。根據LSEG彙整的數據，分析師原本預估該業務部門營收為64.8億美元。

  公司整體季度營收115.4億美元超越分析師預估的112.8億美元。調整後每股盈餘為1.66美元，高於預期的1.62美元。淨利達23億美元。

  AMD其餘營收來源包括：客戶端與遊戲業務部門貢獻38億美元；嵌入式業務部門——主要銷售工業、汽車及通訊設備用晶片——則貢獻9.77億美元。

  本季資本支出達8.08億美元，高於去年同期的2.82億美元，近乎三倍的增幅顯示公司正積極擴大製造與基礎設施投資，以因應預期中的需求成長。

  ## 資料中心成長動能

  AMD表示，資料中心業務的成長動力來自EPYC伺服器中央處理器（CPU）與Instinct繪圖處理器（GPU）的強勁需求，這兩類產品廣泛用於AI訓練與推論工作負載。推論係指用戶向聊天機器人等AI模型提出查詢時所產生的運算過程。

  AMD也從單純銷售個別晶片，轉型為提供整合處理器、網路設備及相關硬體的完整AI系統——公司表示，這項策略轉型使其更能與輝達（Nvidia）的機架規模產品競爭；輝達目前被視為AI加速晶片市場中與AMD競爭最激烈的對手。輝達於今年3月發表了新一代「Vera」CPU，設計上可與旗下「Rubin」系列繪圖處理器搭配使用。

  AMD的第二代Helios AI伺服器搭載MI455X AI加速器，以及由台積電（台灣積體電路製造公司）製造的「Venice」CPU，截至7月已進入全面量產，將於未來數月陸續出貨，公司表示。

  「我們繳出了一個出色的季度，締造了營收與獲利的歷史新高，資料中心營收年增逾倍，」執行長蘇姿丰（Lisa Su）表示，「展望下半年，我們帶著強勁動能邁入——EPYC需求持續加速、Instinct部署規模持續擴大、Helios也開始起量。」

  ## 第三季展望與長期預測

  AMD預測2026年第三季營收約為130億美元，上下誤差幅度為3億美元。根據LSEG數據，分析師原先預估為125.2億美元。調整後毛利率——即扣除生產成本後保留的營收占比——預計約為56%，與分析師預期相符。

  蘇姿丰在財報法說會上表示，AMD預計2027年資料中心銷售額將再度倍增，總營收成長將超越公司在2025年分析師日所設定的35%年成長目標，年度每股盈餘亦將突破20美元的目標。

  AMD同時上修了對市場規模的預測，指出市場擴張速度比六個月前的預期更快。公司現在預期全球AI加速器市場將以45%的年複合成長率擴張，至2030年達到1.4兆美元；伺服器CPU市場則預計以每年50%的速度成長，至2030年達到約2,200億美元。

  第三季資料中心AI業務的營收成長預計將達兩位數，隨著Helios出貨規模擴大，第四季有望進一步加速。AMD表示，Helios銷售將於第四季正式放量，初始出貨預計於第三季末開始。

  ## 投資人反應

  儘管AMD在營收、調整後每股盈餘及第三季展望等指標均超越分析師預期，盤後股價仍下跌近9%。在財報公布前，AMD股價今年（2026年）已累計漲逾一倍。

  「AMD現在的處境與輝達及超大規模雲端業者相似——投資人正在尋找AI基礎設施投資將持續轉化為加速回報的具體證據，」市場研究公司Emarketer分析師雅各·伯恩（Jacob Bourne）表示。所謂超大規模業者，係指以龐大規模營運雲端運算服務的大型供應商。

  ## 近期合約與產能承諾

  AMD近幾個月陸續簽下數項重要客戶與基礎設施協議，為公司的前景成長預測提供有力支撐。

  7月間，AMD同意向AI研究公司Anthropic——Claude AI系統的開發者——出售價值數百億美元、搭載最高2 GW（吉瓦）MI450晶片的AI伺服器，首批交貨預計於2027年初開始。AMD並同意投資Anthropic最多50億美元，惟須視部署里程碑達成情況而定。Anthropic目前被描述為一家正在籌備股票上市的公司。

  此外，AMD在財報公布前約一週，透過與Core Scientific簽署協議，鎖定了高達2.5 GW的資料中心產能，並依協議取得可購入Core Scientific股票的認股權證。

  公司同時受惠於AI伺服器配置中、搭配GPU以因應推論工作負載而使用CPU的需求成長趨勢。這一趨勢協助AMD在伺服器CPU市場從英特爾（Intel）手中取得更多市占，而英特爾也已提高其未來兩年的支出計畫，以因應相同的需求環境，消息人士透露。

  展望更長遠的未來，AMD表示，其2027年機架規模AI平台將整合MI500 GPU、Verano CPU及Pensando網路硬體，系統設計同時支援銅纜與光纖兩種連接方式。
