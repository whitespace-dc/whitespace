  ## Philippines pushes capital market depth to draw AI infrastructure investment

  Philippines Finance Secretary Frederick D. Go told the ASEAN Business and Investment Summit on Sunday that the region must deepen its capital markets and pursue tighter economic integration to draw private investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure, saying no ASEAN government treasury could or should try to match the scale of spending now coming from the private sector.

  "The world will invest around one trillion dollars in AI this year. Four American companies alone will spend more than $700 billion on infrastructure," Go said in a keynote address. "I doubt that an ASEAN Member's treasury will want to match that. Our job, then, is to make this region the place where that capital will land, on terms that will serve our people and benefit our economies."

  ## Capital markets as the lever for data center finance

  Go framed capital market development — the broadening and deepening of domestic bond, equity, and financial instruments markets — as the mechanism that would allow ASEAN companies to finance data centers without full dependence on foreign balance sheets. Deeper markets, he said, would let domestic savings flow into infrastructure projects and reduce companies' reliance on external funding sources.

  "Capital-market depth is how ASEAN companies can fund data centers without depending entirely on foreign balance sheets," Go said.

  He said attracting that private capital would require ASEAN to function as a more integrated market, with common regulatory rules, connected payment systems, and linked power grids.

  ## DEFA signed, semiconductor roadmap finalized

  The summit coincided with two concrete ASEAN-level deliverables. The Digital Economy Framework Agreement, or DEFA — which sets common rules on digital trade, cross-border data flows, digital payments, and consumer protection — was signed on November 15, according to the Department of Finance. The agreement is intended to allow businesses and consumers to transact across borders more easily within the bloc.

  Separately, the ASEAN Semiconductor Roadmap was finalized on Saturday and will be distributed to member states, Go said. The roadmap provides a regional strategic framework for semiconductor supply chains and is intended to expand the Philippines' role beyond its current focus on assembly, testing, and packaging into integrated circuit design, advanced packaging, and high-performance computing, according to the Department of Finance.

  The Philippines, which held the ASEAN chair this year, identified 19 priority economic deliverables, with DEFA and the semiconductor roadmap among them.

  ## Regional growth and mineral endowment

  Go cited Southeast Asia's projected economic trajectory as the basis for the opportunity. ASEAN's digital economy currently stands at roughly $300 billion and is projected to reach nearly $1 trillion by 2030, according to figures he cited. The broader regional economy is growing at close to 5%, which he attributed in part to the global AI investment cycle.

  The Philippines also holds a potential supply-chain role through its reserves of critical minerals. Go noted the country has nickel, copper, gold, and chromite — materials directly tied to AI and energy infrastructure. "Data centres run on copper. Batteries that store clean power run on nickel," he said.

  ## What it means

  Go's remarks signal that the Philippines intends to use its final months as ASEAN chair — and the frameworks agreed at the summit — to position the region as a destination for private AI capital rather than a passive recipient of foreign-built infrastructure. The combination of DEFA's digital trade rules, the semiconductor roadmap, and the push for capital market development represents the policy architecture the Philippines is advancing to that end.

  The semiconductor roadmap, now finalized, will next be distributed to ASEAN member states for implementation.

  ## 菲律賓力推資本市場深化，吸引AI基礎建設投資

  菲律賓財政部長Frederick D. Go週日在東協商業投資高峰會上表示，本區域必須深化資本市場、推動更緊密的經濟整合，方能吸引私部門投入人工智慧（AI）基礎建設，並強調任何東協成員國的國庫都無力、也不應試圖比肩私部門如今的投資規模。

  「全球今年將投入約1兆美元於AI領域。光是四家美國企業，就將在基礎建設上砸下逾7,000億美元，」Go在主題演講中表示。「我不認為東協任何成員國的財政部會想要跟進這個規模。我們的任務，是讓本區域成為這些資金的落腳之處，同時確保相關條件能服務我們的人民、裨益我們的經濟。」

  ## 資本市場深化：資料中心融資的關鍵槓桿

  Go將資本市場發展——包括拓寬與深化國內債券、股票及金融工具市場——定位為讓東協企業得以在不完全仰賴境外資產負債表的情況下，自行籌措資料中心所需資金的核心機制。他表示，更深化的資本市場可將國內儲蓄引導至基礎建設項目，降低企業對外部融資來源的依賴。

  「資本市場的深度，正是東協企業籌措資料中心資金、而不必完全仰賴境外資產負債表的關鍵，」Go說。

  他表示，要吸引這些私部門資本進駐，東協必須以更整合的市場形態運作，具備共通的法規規則、互聯的支付系統，以及連結的電力網路。

  ## DEFA完成簽署，半導體路線圖正式定案

  此次高峰會恰逢兩項具體的東協層級成果相繼落地。根據菲律賓財政部表示，《數位經濟框架協議》（DEFA）——涵蓋數位貿易、跨境資料流通、數位支付及消費者保護等共同規範——已於11月15日完成簽署。該協議旨在讓企業與消費者能更便利地在東協各成員國之間進行跨境交易。

  另外，東協半導體路線圖亦已於週六正式定案，Go表示將分發給各成員國。該路線圖為半導體供應鏈提供區域性策略框架，根據菲律賓財政部表示，目標是協助菲律賓從目前以組裝、測試與封裝為主的定位，進一步擴展至積體電路設計、先進封裝以及高效能運算領域。

  菲律賓今年擔任東協輪值主席國，確立了19項優先經濟成果，DEFA與半導體路線圖均列其中。

  ## 區域成長潛力與礦產資源優勢

  Go援引東南亞的預估經濟發展軌跡，作為此一機遇的基礎。他引用的數字顯示，東協數位經濟目前規模約3,000億美元，預計至2030年將逼近1兆美元。整體區域經濟成長率約5%，他將此部分歸功於全球AI投資週期的帶動。

  菲律賓亦透過其關鍵礦產儲量，在供應鏈中扮演潛在要角。Go指出，菲律賓擁有鎳、銅、黃金及鉻鐵礦等資源，這些材料與AI及能源基礎建設直接相關。「資料中心靠銅運作，儲存潔淨電力的電池靠鎳運作，」他說。

  ## 政策意涵

  Go此番發言顯示，菲律賓有意利用擔任東協主席國的最後幾個月，以及此次高峰會所達成的各項框架，將本區域定位為私部門AI資本的目的地，而非被動接受境外建設基礎設施的角色。DEFA的數位貿易規則、半導體路線圖，以及推動資本市場深化的努力，共同構成菲律賓為此所推動的政策架構。

  現已定案的半導體路線圖，下一步將分發至東協各成員國，進入實施階段。
