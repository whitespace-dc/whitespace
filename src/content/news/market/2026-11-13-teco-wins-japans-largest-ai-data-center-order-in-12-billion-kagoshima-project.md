body_en: |
  Taiwan's Teco Electric & Machinery (TWSE: 1504) has won its first order in Japan's AI data center (AIDC) market, contracting to design and build a 6.4-megawatt (MW) modular data center in Satsumasendai City, Kagoshima Prefecture, as the opening phase of what developers describe as Japan's largest AI data center project.

  The order comes from AI cloud service provider GMI Cloud and KSDI (凱信數基株式会社), whose Kagoshima campus carries a total planned investment of $12 billion and targets a final computing capacity of 1 gigawatt (GW) — a scale comparable to the largest hyperscale campuses announced globally. The first phase targets 350 MW of operating capacity; Teco, GMI Cloud, and Wistron are named as partners in the project.

  Teco's role is as modular data center contractor, responsible for design, electromechanical system integration, modular equipment supply, and technical guidance, the company said. The 6.4 MW facility is due for delivery in the first half of 2027, within a nine-month construction period, integrating power, cooling, communications, and server equipment to serve as a demonstration base for subsequent expansion.

  ## Modular construction model

  The modular data center (MDC) approach integrates design, prefabricated equipment, and system integration to shorten on-site construction time compared with traditional data center builds, Teco said. The method also reduces civil construction costs and allows flexible capacity expansion. As the company's self-manufacturing share of components — including transformers, busbars, and cooling equipment — increases, President Gao Feiyuan said gross margins are expected to improve over time.

  To support rising order volumes, Teco operates an existing MDC production facility in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, and is expanding a second facility in Penang by more than 70,000 square meters, with completion expected in August 2027.

  ## Financial results and order targets

  Teco reported nine-month revenue of NT$47.785 billion for 2026, up 9.2 percent year-on-year, with earnings per share of NT$2.38 compared with NT$1.98 in the same period of 2025. Growth was driven by the electromechanical systems segment in Asian markets and by data center and offshore wind engineering revenue in the Power and Energy Business Group.

  The company set a target of more than NT$10 billion in AIDC-related engineering orders for the full year 2026 and said it had surpassed 80 percent of that figure by the end of October, with Malaysia and Thailand orders each recording double growth in the first ten months. Data center work accounts for approximately 15 to 20 percent of Teco's Power and Energy Business Group revenue.

  Some data center project deliveries have been delayed, the company said, shifting a portion of orders signed in 2026 into revenue recognition in 2027. With the Penang capacity expansion and the Japan project beginning, Teco said AIDC revenue contribution is expected to grow further next year.

  ## Southeast Asia and North America

  Southeast Asia remains Teco's fastest-growing AIDC market. Malaysia and Thailand both recorded multi-fold order growth in the first ten months of 2026, and Gao said Thailand's market scale could eventually match Malaysia's. Printed circuit board (PCB) industry plant expansions were cited as an additional driver of electromechanical engineering demand in the region.

  In the United States, Gao said large data centers may face stricter power supply and regulatory approval requirements, slowing construction timelines, while simultaneously driving demand for natural gas generation, microgrids, and transformers. North American large motor orders rose more than 20 percent year-on-year in the first ten months of 2026; third-quarter North American large motor and transformer orders were up nearly 50 percent year-on-year. Companies developing small modular reactors (SMRs) in the United States have begun inquiring about Teco-Westinghouse motor products, with potential extension into transformers, Gao added.

  ## Industry significance

  The Japan contract is Teco's first in that market and gives the company a foothold in a project that, at full buildout, would rank among the largest AI data center campuses by planned capacity in the country. Chairman Li Mingxian said the company has transitioned from equipment supplier to a provider of modular AI data center equipment and system integration services, and described the Japan win as evidence of Taiwanese companies' capacity to play a central role in global AI infrastructure supply chains through cross-sector collaboration.

  For the fourth quarter of 2026, Teco said it expects both electromechanical systems and power and energy revenue to grow year-on-year and sequentially, with overall gross margins roughly flat. The Penang production facility expansion is scheduled for completion in August 2027 — the same window in which the Kagoshima demonstration facility is due for delivery, after which Teco said it intends to pursue the roughly 350 MW expansion contracts that follow.
body_zh: |
  台灣東元電機（上市代號：1504）首度打入日本AI資料中心（AIDC）市場，取得在鹿兒島縣薩摩川內市設計興建一座6.4百萬瓦（MW）模組化資料中心的合約，作為開發商所稱「日本最大AI資料中心計畫」的起始階段。

  這筆訂單來自AI雲端服務商GMI Cloud與KSDI（凱信數基株式会社）。兩家公司規劃在鹿兒島興建的園區，總投資額高達120億美元，目標最終運算容量為1吉瓦（GW），規模可媲美全球已宣布的最大型超大規模資料中心園區。計畫第一期目標為350 MW運算容量，東元電機、GMI Cloud與緯創均列為本計畫合作夥伴。

  東元電機擔任模組化資料中心承包商，負責設計、機電系統整合、模組化設備供應及技術指導。這座6.4 MW設施預計於2027年上半年交付，建設期約九個月，將整合電力、冷卻、通訊及伺服器設備，作為後續擴建的示範基地。

  ## 模組化建設模式

  東元電機表示，模組化資料中心（MDC）整合設計、預製設備及系統整合，相較傳統資料中心建設可縮短現場施工時間，同時降低土建成本，並可彈性擴充容量。隨著變壓器、匯流排及冷卻設備等自製元件比重持續提升，總經理高飛遠表示，毛利率預期將逐步改善。

  為因應訂單成長，東元電機在馬來西亞柔佛巴魯設有現有MDC生產基地，並正在檳城擴建第二座廠房，擴增面積逾7萬平方公尺，預計於2027年8月完工。

  ## 財務表現與接單目標

  東元電機公布2026年前九個月合併營收為新台幣477億8,500萬元，年增9.2%，每股盈餘2.38元，優於2025年同期的1.98元。成長動能來自亞洲市場機電系統業務，以及電力與能源事業群的資料中心與離岸風電工程營收。

  公司訂下2026年全年AIDC相關工程接單逾新台幣100億元的目標，並表示截至10月底已超越目標八成，馬來西亞與泰國訂單在前十個月均呈倍數成長。資料中心業務約占東元電機電力與能源事業群營收的15%至20%。

  部分資料中心專案交付時程延後，使2026年簽約的部分訂單收入認列移至2027年。東元電機表示，隨著檳城產能擴增到位及日本計畫陸續展開，AIDC營收貢獻明年預計將進一步提升。

  ## 東南亞與北美市場

  東南亞仍是東元電機成長最快速的AIDC市場。馬來西亞與泰國在2026年前十個月訂單均呈多倍成長，高飛遠表示，泰國市場規模最終有望比肩馬來西亞。印刷電路板（PCB）產業廠房擴建，也被列為帶動區域機電工程需求的額外驅動因素。

  在美國市場，高飛遠表示大型資料中心可能面臨更嚴格的電力供應與法規審核要求，拖慢建設時程，但同時也推動天然氣發電、微電網及變壓器的需求。北美大型馬達訂單在2026年前十個月年增逾20%；第三季北美大型馬達與變壓器訂單年增近50%。高飛遠補充，美國小型模組化反應爐（SMR）開發商已開始洽詢東元西屋馬達產品，未來有望延伸至變壓器業務。

  ## 產業意義

  日本這筆合約是東元電機在該市場的首張訂單，使公司在這座完全建成後預計將躋身日本規模最大AI資料中心園區之列的計畫中取得立足點。董事長李明憲表示，公司已從設備供應商轉型為模組化AI資料中心設備與系統整合服務提供商，並將日本得標視為台灣企業透過跨領域合作、在全球AI基礎設施供應鏈中扮演核心角色的具體印證。

  展望2026年第四季，東元電機預期機電系統及電力與能源業務營收均將年增且環比成長，整體毛利率大致持平。檳城生產基地擴建預計於2027年8月完工，與鹿兒島示範設施預計交付時間相近，東元電機表示，屆時將積極爭取後續約350 MW的擴建工程合約。
