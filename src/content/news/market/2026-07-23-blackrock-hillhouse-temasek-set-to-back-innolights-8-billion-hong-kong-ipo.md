body_en: |
  BlackRock, Hillhouse Investment, and Temasek Holdings are set to anchor the Hong Kong listing of Zhongji Innolight Co, the world's largest maker of optical transceivers, in a deal that could raise up to US$8 billion and rank as Hong Kong's biggest initial public offering since 2019, according to people familiar with the matter.

  Aspex Management, JPMorgan Asset Management, Wellington Management, and Yunfeng Capital — the investment firm backed by Jack Ma — are also planning to participate as cornerstone investors, the people said, asking not to be identified because the information is private. Cornerstone investors receive a guaranteed allocation of shares in exchange for committing to hold them for a fixed period, typically six months. The group is expected to take up nearly half of the total deal, the people said.

  ## Deal size and structure

  The base deal is sized at approximately US$7 billion, with an over-allotment option — a mechanism allowing underwriters to sell additional shares if demand is strong — that could push the total to US$8 billion, the people said. Innolight started gauging investor demand on Monday and is planning to begin taking formal investor orders as soon as Wednesday, according to the people.

  At US$8 billion, the offering would surpass every Hong Kong listing since Alibaba Group Holding's US$12.9 billion share sale in 2019. If completed, Innolight's deal could also push Hong Kong's total listing proceeds for 2026 past the nearly US$37 billion raised in all of 2025, according to data compiled by Bloomberg.

  Goldman Sachs, China International Capital Corp, Morgan Stanley, and GF Securities are leading the listing. Haitong International Securities, Citigroup, HSBC, and China Galaxy Securities are also working on the transaction, according to a filing.

  All parties named as prospective cornerstone investors either declined to comment or did not respond to queries. Innolight also did not respond.

  ## Innolight's market position and recent share performance

  Innolight is described by people familiar with the matter as the world's largest producer of optical transceivers — the components that convert electrical signals to light pulses for high-speed data transmission inside data centers. Demand for those components has grown sharply alongside investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure.

  The company's shares on the Shenzhen stock exchange have risen more than 500% over the past year, driven by investor enthusiasm for AI supply-chain companies, lifting its market capitalization to approximately US$187 billion. That appreciation has materially increased the size of the Hong Kong offering from earlier estimates.

  However, shares have retreated about 18% from a peak reached in June, as a broader sell-off in chipmakers has gathered pace amid concerns that AI capital spending is becoming harder to justify.

  ## Hong Kong's IPO market and the AI supply-chain wave

  Innolight's listing is part of a wider wave of offerings by Chinese companies tied to the AI supply chain that have driven a deals boom in Hong Kong this year. A comparable transaction is in preparation from Eoptolink Technology, a peer of Innolight in optical components, which is planning to raise as much as US$5 billion, according to separate people familiar with that deal.

  Deliberations on Innolight's offering are ongoing and details, including the final cornerstone investor lineup, may change, the people said.

  If the transaction closes at its maximum size, it will be the clearest signal yet that institutional capital remains committed to AI infrastructure buildout despite near-term volatility in semiconductor equities. Eoptolink's parallel fundraising process will be the next test of whether investor appetite holds at that scale.
body_zh: |
  貝萊德（BlackRock）、高瓴投資（Hillhouse Investment）及淡馬錫控股（Temasek Holdings），將為全球最大光收發模組製造商中際旭創（Zhongji Innolight）的香港掛牌上市提供支持。據知情人士透露，此次交易募資規模上看80億美元，有望成為香港自2019年以來規模最大的首次公開募股（IPO）。

  安佰深資產管理（Aspex Management）、摩根資產管理（JPMorgan Asset Management）、惠靈頓管理（Wellington Management），以及由馬雲支持的投資機構雲鋒基金（Yunfeng Capital），也計劃以基石投資人身分參與此次發行。上述消息人士表示，因相關資訊屬私密性質，不願具名。基石投資人可獲得保證配股，但須承諾持股一段固定期間，通常為六個月。知情人士指出，上述基石投資人預計將合計認購近半數的整體交易規模。

  ## 交易規模與結構

  知情人士表示，此次交易基礎規模約為70億美元，另設有超額配售選擇權——即當市場需求強勁時，允許承銷商額外出售股份的機制——若悉數行使，整體規模可達80億美元。中際旭創已於本週一啟動投資人需求探詢，最快可能於週三開始接受正式投資人認購委託。

  若以80億美元計算，此次發行規模將超越阿里巴巴集團（Alibaba Group Holding）2019年籌得129億美元以來香港所有的掛牌案例。根據彭博彙整的資料，一旦交易完成，中際旭創此次集資將可能使香港2026年全年IPO總募資額，超越2025年全年的近370億美元。

  高盛（Goldman Sachs）、中國國際資本（China International Capital Corp，CICC）、摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）及廣發證券（GF Securities）擔任此次掛牌的主要承銷商。根據申報文件，海通國際證券（Haitong International Securities）、花旗集團（Citigroup）、匯豐銀行（HSBC）及中國銀河證券（China Galaxy Securities）亦參與此次交易。

  所有被點名為潛在基石投資人的機構，均拒絕置評或未回應詢問。中際旭創方面亦未予回應。

  ## 中際旭創的市場地位與近期股價表現

  據知情人士介紹，中際旭創是全球最大的光收發模組製造商——此類零組件負責將電訊號轉換為光脈衝，以實現資料中心內部的高速資料傳輸。隨著人工智慧（AI）基礎設施投資急速擴張，相關零組件的需求亦大幅成長。

  中際旭創在深圳證券交易所掛牌的股價，過去一年已累計上漲逾500%，主要受惠於投資人對AI供應鏈概念股的高度熱情，使其市值攀升至約1,870億美元。此一漲幅也使中際旭創此次香港上市的募資規模，較早先的市場預估大幅擴大。

  然而，由於半導體類股跌勢加速，市場對AI資本支出是否愈來愈難以合理化的疑慮持續升溫，中際旭創股價自六月份高峰已回落約18%。

  ## 香港IPO市場與AI供應鏈熱潮

  中際旭創此次掛牌，是今年以來一波由中國AI供應鏈相關企業帶動的香港上市熱潮的縮影，這股熱潮推動了本港今年的交易市況蓬勃發展。據另一批知情人士透露，中際旭創的光模組同業新易盛（Eoptolink Technology）亦正籌備規模相當的上市案，計劃在香港募資最多50億美元。

  知情人士表示，中際旭創此次發行的相關磋商仍在持續進行，包括基石投資人最終名單在內的各項細節均可能有所調整。

  若此次交易以最大規模完成，將是迄今為止最明確的訊號，表明儘管半導體股近期出現震盪，機構資本對AI基礎設施建設的承諾依然堅定。而新易盛同步進行的籌資過程，將成為下一個考驗投資人是否仍願意以此規模入場的試金石。
