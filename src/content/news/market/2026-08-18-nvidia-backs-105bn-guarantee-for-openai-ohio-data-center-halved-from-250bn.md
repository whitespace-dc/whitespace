body_en: |
  Nvidia has agreed to guarantee up to $105 billion in lease and power payments supporting OpenAI's planned data center campus in Pike County, Ohio, the companies said Monday, while separately committing $1.5 billion in equity to SB Energy, the SoftBank Group-backed developer building the site. The figure is roughly half the $250 billion backstop Nvidia had previously discussed, a reduction the Wall Street Journal reported was driven by investor concern over the chipmaker's growing off-balance-sheet exposure.

  ## Scale and structure

  The facility, known as the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus, is planned for a total of 10 gigawatts (GW) of computing capacity — equivalent to the electricity demand of about 7.5 million average U.S. homes, based on the industry benchmark the companies cited of 750,000 homes per gigawatt. If completed in full, it would be the largest data center project announced to date, according to Capacity Media. Nvidia's guarantee covers up to 8 GW of that total, representing the first phase of the buildout. An initial deployment of 4.25 GW is planned, with the first 800 megawatts (MW) — enough to supply roughly 600,000 homes — expected to come online by 2028.

  OpenAI has entered an agreement to secure up to 8 GW from the campus under a 20-year lease. The ChatGPT maker said it will begin paying only as capacity becomes available for lease. Nvidia confirmed it will be the exclusive chip supplier for the facility.

  Nvidia's guarantee does not cover OpenAI's full lease obligations. The chipmaker said its commitment covers a defined portion of lease and power payments, along with a pledge to maintain a minimum asset value for the site. If OpenAI were to default, Nvidia would cover the gap between that guaranteed floor and whatever SB Energy could recoup by re-leasing or selling the facility.

  ## Why the guarantee shrank

  The $250 billion figure reflected earlier-stage negotiations, which Bloomberg News had reported. Investor concern over Nvidia's expanding off-balance-sheet commitments prompted the reduction, the Journal reported. The revision came days after Nvidia announced a partnership with six major financial institutions, including BlackRock, to launch compute financing platforms aimed at raising more than $500 billion in third-party capital for AI infrastructure — a move that signals an intent to shift more of the funding burden onto outside investors rather than carry it on the chipmaker's own balance sheet.

  OpenAI's financial position amplifies the significance of the backstop. Despite an $852 billion valuation, the company remains unprofitable, meaning Nvidia's guarantee has effectively substituted for OpenAI's own creditworthiness on a commitment of this scale.

  ## Power supply

  Land and power availability have become binding constraints on large-scale data center construction, given the aging U.S. electricity grid and rising community opposition to new development driven by concerns over higher electricity prices and water use. To supply the Ohio campus, SoftBank and SB Energy plan to build at least 10 GW of new power generation capacity and invest $4.2 billion in regional grid infrastructure through a partnership with AEP Ohio.

  ## Revenue projections

  Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang said the Ohio site's initial 4.25 GW phase could contribute as much as $200 billion to Nvidia's revenue. Across all sites, Huang projected that selling OpenAI 16 GW of computing capacity by 2030 — including a 3.75 GW Ohio expansion beyond the initial phase — could yield $600 billion in total Nvidia revenue. Huang said the company plans to selectively secure prime sites where its chips can run across multiple hardware generations.

  ## Circular financing scrutiny

  The Ohio arrangement is the latest instance of Nvidia financing the ecosystem that consumes its chips — a strategy that has fueled demand for its products while drawing regulatory and market scrutiny over the circularity of the funding flows. The chipmaker has previously provided a financial backstop to CoreWeave, a cloud computing company whose total debt reached $35 billion at the end of the second quarter, and has taken direct equity stakes in both OpenAI and fellow AI developer Anthropic. The Ohio guarantee is the largest such arrangement Nvidia has entered.

  Huang rejected the circular financing characterization. "We are securing long-lived infrastructure for Nvidia compute so OpenAI can deploy the most productive AI factories that can be upgraded repeatedly with each new generation delivering more intelligence and better economics," he said. The company added separately: "OpenAI will pay the lease."

  Market analysts are less categorical. "Investors are right to be worried about what seems to be a never-ending loop of AI deals but realistically the field of players isn't all that vast and there was always going to be a degree of circular financing," said Danni Hewson, head of financial analysis at AJ Bell. "The biggest test is whether these investments ultimately generate decent returns for all those laying out cash and that's something that can only be figured out further down the line."

  The project is also a centrepiece for SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son and the Trump administration, which has promoted it as a major domestic investment win.

  ## Employment

  The Ohio campus is projected to generate approximately 35,000 construction jobs through 2032 and about 2,500 long-term operating roles, OpenAI said.

  ## What remains unresolved

  OpenAI's binding lease for the full 10 GW campus remains under negotiation. The $105 billion Nvidia guarantee backstops up to 8 GW — the first phase of the planned buildout — leaving the financing structure for the remaining capacity, and who backstops it, still to be determined.
body_zh: |
  輝達週一宣布，同意為OpenAI在俄亥俄州派克郡（Pike County）規劃中的資料中心園區，提供最高1050億美元的租約與電力費用擔保，並另行斥資15億美元，以股權形式投入由軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）支持、負責建設該園區的開發商SB Energy。這筆擔保金額約為輝達此前討論之2500億美元的一半，據《華爾街日報》報導，此次縮減的主因是投資人對這家晶片廠商不斷擴大的表外曝險感到憂慮，也進一步加劇了外界對AI基礎建設循環融資模式的審視。

  ## 規模與架構

  這座名為「PORTS-派克科技園區」（PORTS-Pike Technology Campus）的設施，總計畫算力容量為10吉瓦（GW）——依業界常用的每吉瓦可供電約75萬戶美國家庭的基準換算，相當於約750萬戶美國普通家庭的用電需求。根據Capacity Media的說法，若全數完工，將是迄今公布規模最大的資料中心建設案。輝達的擔保涵蓋其中最高8吉瓦，即第一期工程。首批計畫部署容量為4.25吉瓦，其中最初的800百萬瓦——足以供應約60萬戶家庭用電——預計於2028年前上線。

  OpenAI已簽訂協議，在20年租約期內向該園區取得最高8吉瓦的算力。ChatGPT的開發商表示，僅在容量正式可供租用時才開始付款。輝達亦確認，將擔任該設施的獨家晶片供應商。

  輝達的擔保並不涵蓋OpenAI的全部租約義務。這家晶片廠商表示，其承諾範圍涵蓋租約與電力費用的特定部分，並承諾維持該園區的最低資產價值。若OpenAI違約，輝達將補足擔保底價與SB Energy透過重新出租或出售園區所能回收金額之間的差額。

  ## 擔保金額為何縮水

  2500億美元的數字反映的是較早期的談判內容，彭博新聞（Bloomberg News）曾對此有所報導。據《華爾街日報》報導，由於投資人對輝達不斷擴大的表外承諾深感憂慮，促使公司做出削減。此次調整距輝達宣布與貝萊德（BlackRock）等六家主要金融機構合作、共同推出算力融資平台、為AI基礎建設募集逾5000億美元第三方資本的計畫，僅相隔數日——此舉顯示輝達有意將更多融資責任轉移給外部投資人，而非由晶片廠商自身的資產負債表承擔。

  OpenAI的財務狀況更凸顯了此次擔保的重要性。儘管公司估值高達8520億美元，OpenAI至今仍未獲利，這意味著在這筆龐大規模的承諾上，輝達的擔保實際上取代了OpenAI本身的信用。

  ## 電力供應

  土地與電力的取得，已成為大型資料中心建設的關鍵限制條件，原因在於美國電網日益老化，加上社區居民因擔憂電費上漲與耗水問題而對新建設的反對聲浪持續高漲。為供應俄亥俄園區的電力需求，軟銀與SB Energy計畫興建至少10吉瓦的新發電容量，並透過與AEP Ohio的合作，投入42億美元強化區域電網基礎設施。

  ## 營收預測

  輝達執行長黃仁勳表示，俄亥俄園區首期4.25吉瓦的建設，可為輝達貢獻最高2000億美元的營收。放眼所有場址，黃仁勳預估，若能在2030年前向OpenAI供應共16吉瓦的算力——包含首期之外俄亥俄額外擴建的3.75吉瓦——輝達的總營收可望達到6000億美元。黃仁勳表示，公司計畫有選擇性地取得優質場址，讓旗下晶片得以跨越多個硬體世代持續運行。

  ## 循環融資的疑慮

  俄亥俄案是輝達資助其晶片消費生態系的最新案例——此一策略雖帶動了產品需求，卻也引發監管機關與市場對資金循環流動的質疑。輝達此前已為雲端運算公司CoreWeave提供財務擔保，CoreWeave於第二季末的總負債達350億美元；輝達亦直接持有OpenAI及同為AI開發商的Anthropic的股權。俄亥俄擔保案是輝達迄今規模最大的此類安排。

  黃仁勳駁斥了循環融資的說法。他表示：「我們正在為輝達的算力確保長期基礎設施，讓OpenAI能夠部署最具生產力的AI工廠，並隨著每一代新硬體的推出反覆升級，以持續提升的智能效益與更優化的經濟效益回饋用戶。」輝達另行補充：「OpenAI將支付租約費用。」

  市場分析師的看法則較為保留。AJ Bell財務分析主管達妮・修森（Danni Hewson）表示：「投資人對這個看似永無止盡的AI交易循環感到憂慮，這種擔心是合理的；但現實是，這個領域的玩家本就不多，某種程度的循環融資本就在所難免。最大的考驗在於，這些投資最終能否為所有出資方帶來像樣的回報，而這個問題只有到了未來才能見分曉。」

  這項計畫同時也是軟銀創辦人孫正義（Masayoshi Son）與川普政府的重點旗艦案——川普政府將其定位為美國重大國內投資的重要成果，並大力對外推廣。

  ## 就業效益

  OpenAI表示，俄亥俄園區預計在2032年前創造約3萬5000個建設工作機會，以及約2500個長期營運職缺。

  ## 尚待解決的問題

  OpenAI針對完整10吉瓦園區所簽訂的具有約束力的租約目前仍在談判之中。輝達的1050億美元擔保涵蓋最高8吉瓦——即計畫第一期工程——其餘容量的融資架構，以及由誰提供擔保，目前仍懸而未決。
