body_en: |
  Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, Ooredoo Group, Nokia, and NVIDIA have launched a joint venture called Zankore by Indosat to build what they describe as one of Asia Pacific's largest AI infrastructure platforms, targeting 1 gigawatt (GW) of NVIDIA DSX AI Factory capacity in Indonesia.

  The partnership targets AI Factory capacity equivalent to 1 GW — a scale that would place the deployment among the largest dedicated AI compute buildouts in the region. As a reference point, a single gigawatt is enough electrical power to run roughly one million average server racks; applied to AI accelerators, which draw far more power per unit, the figure represents an exceptionally dense concentration of computing resources.

  Zankore is expected to deliver an initial 200 megawatts (MW) of AI capacity in the first half of 2027, according to the companies. That first phase will be powered by NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72, a high-density GPU compute module. The platform will subsequently adopt NVIDIA DSX MaxLPS, a system the company says can recover stranded power — electricity that would otherwise be wasted in a data center's power delivery chain — enabling up to 40 percent more compute within the same power budget.

  ## Partner roles and capital structure

  Each partner brings a defined function to the venture. Ooredoo Group, the Qatari telecommunications conglomerate, will serve as lead investor and platform sponsor, providing long-term capital and regional scale. Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, the Indonesian mobile operator, contributes market position and digital infrastructure capabilities. NVIDIA supplies accelerated computing hardware, AI software, access to next-generation GPUs, and its broader AI ecosystem. Nokia is responsible for what the companies call AI-native networking — network infrastructure designed from the outset to support AI workloads rather than adapted from general-purpose telecommunications gear.

  Citi acted as financial advisor to Indosat; FTI Capital Advisors advised Ooredoo Group on the transaction.

  Zankore will establish a Board of Directors drawn from its principal shareholders to provide strategic oversight and governance as the platform scales.

  ## AI stack and intended use cases

  Indosat's chief executive, Vikram Sinha, said the platform is designed to move enterprises beyond AI experimentation toward mission-critical deployment. He described Zankore as providing "the full AI stack," citing Indosat's own Sahabat-AI large language model, AI Grid network management, and AI-RAN — a radio access network architecture that integrates AI processing directly into mobile network infrastructure — as components of the offering.

  Nokia's president and CEO, Justin Hotard, framed the networking layer as a requirement for what he called sovereign AI — nationally controlled AI infrastructure that is secure and scalable. Hotard said achieving that requires orchestration of compute, connectivity, and control across AI factories, data centers, and the edge.

  Ronnie Vasishta, NVIDIA's senior vice president for telecom, said the platform would be built on NVIDIA accelerated computing and what he described as open, secure models.

  ## Indonesian government support

  Meutya Hafid, Indonesia's Minister of Communication and Digital Affairs, expressed government support for the initiative, citing Indonesia's resources and talent base and describing the project as advancing sovereign AI infrastructure. Hafid said the government supports initiatives that strengthen Indonesia's digital competitiveness and aims to distribute AI benefits equitably across the country.

  ## Significance for Southeast Asia

  The Zankore venture represents one of the largest announced commitments to AI infrastructure in Southeast Asia to date, combining a telco-led distribution model with hyperscale GPU capacity and a stated goal of expanding access across the region. The involvement of a government minister at launch and the explicit framing around digital sovereignty signal that the project is positioned as national infrastructure as much as a commercial platform.

  The first 200 MW phase is scheduled for delivery in the first half of 2027, with the full 1 GW target to follow as the platform scales across Southeast Asia, according to the companies.
body_zh: |
  ## Indosat、Ooredoo、Nokia與輝達合資成立Zankore，矢志在印尼打造1GW AI工廠

  Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison、Ooredoo集團、Nokia與輝達四方宣布成立合資公司Zankore by Indosat，計畫在印尼建設他們口中亞太地區規模最大的AI基礎設施平台之一，目標為1吉瓦（GW）的輝達DSX AI工廠運算容量。

  此次合作所設定的AI工廠容量達1GW，一旦實現，將使本案躋身該地區規模最大的專屬AI運算建置之列。作為參考，1吉瓦的電力大約可供應百萬個標準伺服器機架同時運作；若換算為功耗遠高於一般伺服器的AI加速器，這個數字所代表的運算資源密度更是極為可觀。

  根據各方公司的說法，Zankore預計於2027年上半年率先交付200百萬瓦（MW）的AI運算容量，完成第一期建置。這個階段將採用輝達的GB300 NVL72——一款高密度GPU運算模組——作為核心動力。後續平台將導入輝達DSX MaxLPS系統，該系統號稱能回收「被浪費的電力」，也就是資料中心電力傳輸鏈中原本白白耗散的電能，從而在相同的用電預算內提升最多40%的運算效能。

  ## 合作夥伴分工與資本架構

  各合作夥伴在合資公司中各司其職，分工明確。卡達電信集團Ooredoo將擔任主要投資方與平台主導者，提供長期資本及區域規模優勢；印尼行動電信業者Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison則貢獻其市場地位與數位基礎建設能力；輝達負責提供加速運算硬體、AI軟體、新世代GPU的使用權限，以及其完整的AI生態系；Nokia則肩負合作方所稱「AI原生網路」的建置責任——這類網路基礎設施從設計之初便以支援AI工作負載為前提，而非從通用電信設備改造而來。

  財務顧問方面，花旗擔任Indosat的財務顧問，FTI Capital Advisors則為Ooredoo集團提供本次交易的顧問服務。

  Zankore將從主要股東中選任成員，組成董事會，隨著平台規模持續擴張，負責提供策略監督與公司治理。

  ## AI技術堆疊與預期應用場景

  Indosat執行長Vikram Sinha表示，這個平台的設計目的，是協助企業客戶跨越AI實驗階段，邁向關鍵任務等級的正式部署。他將Zankore定位為提供「完整AI技術堆疊」的平台，並列舉Indosat自家的Sahabat-AI大型語言模型、AI Grid網路管理系統，以及AI-RAN——一種將AI運算直接整合進行動網路無線接取網（Radio Access Network）架構的技術——作為平台服務的組成要素。

  Nokia總裁暨執行長Justin Hotard則將網路層定位為實現他所稱「主權AI」的必要條件——所謂主權AI，指的是由各國自主掌控、具備安全性與可擴展性的AI基礎設施。Hotard表示，要達成這個目標，必須跨AI工廠、資料中心與邊緣端，對運算、連接與控制三個層面進行統籌協調。

  輝達電信業務資深副總裁Ronnie Vasishta則表示，這個平台將建立在輝達加速運算架構之上，並採用他所描述的開放、安全模型。

  ## 印尼政府的支持

  印尼通訊與數位事務部長Meutya Hafid對此計畫表達政府支持，援引印尼的資源條件與人才基礎，並將本案定位為推進主權AI基礎建設的重要里程碑。Hafid表示，政府支持所有能強化印尼數位競爭力的計畫，並致力讓AI的發展紅利能公平地遍及全國各地。

  ## 對東南亞的戰略意義

  Zankore合資案代表迄今東南亞地區規模最大的AI基礎建設承諾之一，將電信業者主導的分銷模式、超大規模GPU運算容量，以及擴大區域普及率的明確目標融為一體。政府部長親自出席發布活動，加上各方對「數位主權」的明確論述，均顯示這個計畫被定位為國家基礎建設，而非單純的商業平台。

  根據各方公司的說法，第一期200MW的建置預計於2027年上半年完成交付，完整的1GW目標則將隨著平台在東南亞持續擴張而逐步實現。
