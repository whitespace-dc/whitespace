body_en: |
  Malaysia's state digital economy agency MDEC has formally committed to leading execution of the government's Malaysia Digital 2030 (MD2030) plan, positioning itself as the primary implementation body for a roadmap that aims to raise the digital sector's share of gross domestic product from 25% to 30% and establish the country as a globally competitive artificial intelligence nation by 2030.

  Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim launched MD2030 as Malaysia's national digital action plan covering 2026 to 2030. The roadmap frames the country's ambition as a transition from being primarily a technology consumer to a producer of home-grown digital innovation, according to statements made at an event marking the Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation's 30th anniversary.

  ## Targets and deliverables under MD2030

  MD2030 sets four headline targets:

  - Raise the digital economy's contribution to GDP to 30% by 2030, up from 25%
  - Create 500,000 high-value digital jobs
  - Generate RM4.5 billion in public-sector savings through digitalisation
  - Deliver 95% of government services fully online, end to end

  The plan is organized around seven strategic pillars — government, economy, infrastructure, talent, society, trust and security, and innovation — under a whole-of-government approach led by the Ministry of Digital.

  ## MDEC's role and institutional position

  MDEC is one of several agencies under the Ministry of Digital tasked with implementing MD2030. The others include the National AI Office, GovTech Malaysia, CyberSecurity Malaysia, MyDigital Corporation, and the Malaysia Centre for the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

  MDEC chairman Ganesh Kumar Bangah, recently appointed to the role, said the agency's mandate is to translate the national agenda into execution. "The Government has set a mandate to grow the digital economy from 25% to 30% of the economy, and MDEC must lead again in driving AI adoption, digital economy acceleration and industry transformation," he said, according to statements cited in Digital News Asia.

  Ganesh said Malaysia already has foundational assets in place. "Malaysia has the infrastructure, we have the talent and we have thousands of companies in the ecosystem. It is now time for MDEC to put all these strengths together and lead the charge," he said.

  ## Agency history and mandate evolution

  MDEC was established 30 years ago in connection with the Multimedia Super Corridor (MSC), a government-designated zone created to attract technology investment and development. The MSC was Malaysia's early framework for drawing multinational technology companies and developing a domestic technology sector.

  CEO Anuar Fariz Fadzil said the agency has continuously redefined its remit across successive technology cycles — from the MSC era through digital platforms, content, investment promotion, export development, digital infrastructure, and now AI. "For 30 years, MDEC has evolved together with the nation through every wave of change. Technology has changed many times, but our purpose has remained clear: to strengthen Malaysia's digital economy and ensure technology improves the lives of Malaysians," he said.

  Anuar framed MD2030 as an expansion of institutional responsibility. "As Malaysia enters this next chapter, our responsibility grows with it. MDEC has a role to play in turning this national mission into reality by empowering Malaysians, strengthening industry capabilities and equipping talent with the skills needed to participate in the AI economy," he said.

  ## Significance for the regional AI and data center landscape

  MD2030's explicit framing of data as a strategic national asset and its integration of AI across government, industry, and society signals continued policy-level demand for digital infrastructure investment in Malaysia. The plan's 2030 deadline sets a five-year window in which MDEC and partner agencies are expected to coordinate industry mobilization, talent development, and infrastructure deployment under centralized national direction.

  MDEC is among six agencies under the Ministry of Digital responsible for MD2030 implementation, with the National AI Office serving as a dedicated institutional counterpart for AI-specific policy execution.

  ## MDEC承諾領航馬來西亞數位經濟　目標2030年佔GDP達30%

  馬來西亞國家數位經濟機構MDEC正式宣示，將主導政府「馬來西亞數位2030」（MD2030）計畫的執行工作，以主要推動機構之姿，帶領這份旨在將數位產業佔國內生產毛額比重從25%提升至30%、並在2030年前將馬來西亞打造為全球具競爭力人工智慧（AI）國家的路線圖落地實現。

  總理安華·依布拉欣（Anwar Ibrahim）正式發布MD2030，作為馬來西亞涵蓋2026年至2030年的國家數位行動計畫。根據在MDEC成立30週年慶典上發表的聲明，這份路線圖標誌著馬來西亞的發展抱負——從以技術消費者為主，轉型為本土數位創新的生產者。

  ## MD2030的目標與具體交付成果

  MD2030設定四大核心目標：

  - 至2030年，數位經濟對GDP的貢獻率由25%提升至30%
  - 創造50萬個高附加價值數位就業機會
  - 透過數位化為公部門節省45億馬幣
  - 實現95%政府服務全面線上化、端對端完成

  此計畫圍繞七大策略支柱構建，分別為：政府、經濟、基礎建設、人才、社會、信任與安全，以及創新，並在數位部的統籌領導下採取全政府協同推進的方式落實。

  ## MDEC的角色與機構定位

  MDEC是數位部旗下負責執行MD2030的多個機構之一，其餘包括國家AI辦公室、GovTech Malaysia、馬來西亞網路安全機構（CyberSecurity Malaysia）、MyDigital Corporation，以及馬來西亞第四次工業革命中心。

  甫獲任命為MDEC主席的卡內許·古瑪·班加（Ganesh Kumar Bangah）表示，該機構的使命在於將國家政策轉化為具體執行。他說：「政府已設定任務，要將數位經濟從佔整體經濟的25%提升至30%，MDEC必須再度領頭，推動AI應用、加速數位經濟發展，並帶動產業轉型。」上述引述來自《Digital News Asia》的報導。

  卡內許表示，馬來西亞已具備扎實的基礎條件。「馬來西亞擁有基礎建設、我們有人才，生態系中也有數以千計的企業。現在是MDEC整合這些優勢、領頭衝鋒的時刻了。」他說。

  ## 機構沿革與職能演進

  MDEC成立至今已屆30年，其創設背景與「多媒體超級走廊」（MSC）密切相關。MSC是馬來西亞政府劃設的特定區域，旨在吸引科技投資與發展；作為馬來西亞早期招引跨國科技企業、培育本土科技產業的政策框架，MSC奠定了馬來西亞數位發展的基石。

  執行長阿努爾·法里茲·法德希（Anuar Fariz Fadzil）表示，MDEC歷經多個技術週期，持續重新定義自身職責——從MSC時代，到數位平台、內容、投資促進、出口發展、數位基礎建設，直至如今的AI浪潮。「三十年來，MDEC與國家一同在每一波變革浪潮中共同演進。技術雖已數度更迭，但我們的使命始終明確：強化馬來西亞的數位經濟，確保科技改善馬來西亞人的生活。」他說。

  阿努爾將MD2030定位為機構責任的進一步擴展。「隨著馬來西亞邁入這個新篇章，我們的責任也與日俱增。MDEC在將這項國家任務化為現實上責無旁貸，我們將賦能馬來西亞人、強化產業能力，並為人才配備參與AI經濟所需的技能。」他說。

  ## 對區域AI及資料中心發展格局的意義

  MD2030明確將資料定位為國家戰略資產，並將AI全面整合至政府治理、產業發展與社會生活之中，這顯示馬來西亞在政策層面對數位基礎建設投資的需求將持續強勁。該計畫所設定的2030年期限，為MDEC及其夥伴機構提供了五年窗口，在國家層級的集中統籌下，協調推動產業動員、人才培育與基礎建設部署。

  MDEC是數位部旗下六個負責落實MD2030的機構之一，國家AI辦公室則作為AI相關政策執行的專責對口機構，並行推動。
