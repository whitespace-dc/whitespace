body_en: |
  Asia-Pacific data center operators are approaching US buyers with surplus Nvidia B300 graphics processing unit (GPU) capacity after U.S. export controls barred their Chinese customers from accessing the advanced AI accelerators, according to Ronit Jain, chief executive of GPU marketplace startup Liquid Compute.

  The price to rent a single Nvidia B300 GPU for one hour fell 7% over roughly a month, dropping from $7.72 at the end of October to $7.19 as of late November, according to a Liquid Compute index that the company says tracks more than 200 data center companies. A year ago, the same capacity cost $4.55 per GPU-hour and had risen steadily before the recent decline, Jain said.

  ## Why capacity is being redirected

  The Trump administration recently restricted advanced AI chip exports to four unspecified Southeast Asian countries, requiring sellers to obtain Washington's approval for each shipment. The administration also announced a forthcoming global rule that would bar data centers from granting Chinese firms remote access to restricted AI hardware — closing a channel some providers had used to work around direct sales bans.

  Jain, whose New York-based startup describes itself as a regulated exchange for AI computing capacity, said the displacement is now hitting providers that had signed long-term agreements to supply Chinese technology companies. "All the neoclouds based in Asia-Pacific selling to Tencent and other companies, or in Singapore or Taiwan with Chinese companies as offtakers, have excess capacity," Jain said in an interview. "They're trying to broker it into the US." Neoclouds are cloud computing providers that specialize in GPU-based computing for artificial intelligence workloads, distinct from large generalist cloud operators such as Amazon Web Services or Microsoft Azure.

  Jain declined to identify the sellers or the prospective US buyers. His post on X on November 19 — in which he said "a staggering number of neoclouds" had approached Liquid Compute seeking to remarket long-term B300 capacity "originally committed to Chinese-affiliated offtakers" — drew widespread attention, he said.

  ## Nvidia leadership turbulence

  The restrictions arrive amid upheaval at Nvidia. Co-founder Jensen Huang, who had been among the most vocal advocates for Chinese access to Nvidia chips, resigned as chief executive before being reinstated this week, 11 days after his departure. His resignation followed public disclosures of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman the U.S. government has investigated for allegedly spying on behalf of China. That woman's Singapore-based company, Megaspeed International, is alleged to have been smuggling Nvidia AI chips into China.

  Despite Huang's reinstatement, Jain said market participants are operating on the assumption that Chinese firms will no longer be able to obtain Nvidia's advanced chips through AI cloud providers.

  ## Significance for the market

  The combination of the new export rules and the displacement of capacity originally committed to Chinese buyers represents a structural shift in how GPU compute capacity is priced and distributed across Asia-Pacific. Supply that had been absorbed by Chinese technology firms is now competing for a different, smaller pool of buyers in the US and Western Europe, putting downward pressure on rental rates.

  Liquid Compute, founded in 2024, raised $15 million in a seed round in September co-led by FirstMark Capital and Chemistry Ventures; the company is also backed by startup accelerator Y Combinator. "Just based on basic economics, you would expect the price to decrease with a temporary influx of supply," Jain said. He said he expects B300 GPU-hour prices to continue declining.
body_zh: |
  亞太地區資料中心業者正積極向美國買家推銷多餘的輝達B300繪圖處理器（GPU）產能，起因是美國出口管制禁止其中國客戶取得這些先進AI加速晶片。以上資訊來自GPU市集新創公司Liquid Compute執行長羅尼特・傑恩。

  根據Liquid Compute的指數，租用單顆輝達B300 GPU一小時的價格在約一個月內下跌7%，從10月底的每GPU小時7.72美元，降至11月下旬的7.19美元。傑恩指出，該指數追蹤逾200家資料中心業者的報價。他表示，一年前同等產能的價格為每GPU小時4.55美元，在近期下跌之前持續穩步攀升。

  ## 產能為何重新導流

  川普政府近期限制先進AI晶片出口至四個未具名的東南亞國家，規定出口商每筆貨物均須取得華府核准。該政府也宣布即將推出一項全球性規定，禁止資料中心允許中國企業遠端存取受管制的AI硬體，藉此封堵部分業者用以規避直接銷售禁令的變通管道。

  總部位於紐約的Liquid Compute自我定位為AI運算產能的合規交易所，傑恩表示，這波衝擊已蔓延至與中國科技公司簽有長期供應合約的業者。傑恩在受訪時說道：「所有在亞太地區向騰訊等公司銷售服務的新興雲端業者，或是在新加坡、台灣以中國企業為主要客戶的業者，現在都出現了多餘產能。他們正設法將這些產能轉介給美國客戶。」所謂新興雲端業者（neocloud），是指專門提供以GPU為基礎之AI運算服務的雲端供應商，有別於亞馬遜AWS或微軟Azure等大型綜合雲端業者。

  傑恩拒絕透露賣方或潛在美國買家的身份。他在11月19日於X平台發文指出，「數量驚人的新興雲端業者」主動找上Liquid Compute，希望轉售原本承諾供給「與中國相關承接方」的長期B300產能。他表示，這篇貼文引發了廣泛關注。

  ## 輝達領導層動盪

  上述管制措施正值輝達內部陷入動盪之際。共同創辦人黃仁勳原是倡導中國取得輝達晶片的最力主張者之一，在離職11天後，本週宣布重返執行長職位。他的請辭肇因於多段婚外情遭公開揭露，其中一名對象是美國政府調查其疑似替中國從事間諜活動的女性。該女性在新加坡成立的公司Megaspeed International，據稱涉嫌將輝達AI晶片走私至中國。

  儘管黃仁勳已復職，傑恩表示，市場參與者普遍認定，中國企業將無法再透過AI雲端供應商取得輝達的先進晶片。

  ## 市場影響

  新出口規定，加上原本承諾供給中國買家之產能遭到排擠，兩者交疊之下，亞太地區GPU運算產能的定價與分配方式正出現結構性轉變。原本由中國科技公司消化的供給，如今轉而競逐美國與西歐數量更為有限的買家群體，對租賃費率形成持續的下行壓力。

  Liquid Compute成立於2024年，今年9月完成由FirstMark Capital與Chemistry Ventures共同領投的1,500萬美元種子輪融資，同時也獲得新創加速器Y Combinator的支持。傑恩說：「僅就基本經濟學原理而言，供給短暫增加，預期價格自然會下降。」他表示，預計B300 GPU每小時租賃價格將持續走低。
