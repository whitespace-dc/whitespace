body_en: |
  Lite-On Technology (2301.TW), the Taiwan-listed power supply and electronics manufacturer, has agreed to invest $34.3 million for a 21% equity stake in DenseLight Photonics, a Singapore-based integrated device manufacturer (IDM) — a company that both designs and fabricates its own components — specializing in indium phosphide (InP) photonic devices, according to president Qiu Senbin.

  The investment advances what Qiu described as a three-piece optical communications strategy encompassing light sources, packaging and testing assembly, and optical alignment technology. Lite-On already operates as a major optical coupler manufacturer and holds its own Micro LED light source and packaging and testing assembly capabilities, Qiu said. The company said it will continue seeking strategic partners and has not ruled out further alliances or investments to accelerate the build-out. Lite-On expects its optical communications module business, focused on networking and switch applications, to begin generating visible revenue in the second half of 2027, Qiu said.

  ## Transformation from components to systems

  The DenseLight deal sits within a broader strategic shift that Qiu described as moving Lite-On from a single-component supplier into a system-level solutions provider. "Lite-On is genuinely different now," Qiu said at a first-half earnings briefing. The company reported record first-half revenue and profit, with single-quarter gross margin reaching 27%.

  Traditional power supply manufacturing has faced intense price competition. Beginning in 2021, Lite-On pivoted to co-developing products with North American cloud service providers (CSPs) — the large-scale operators running cloud infrastructure — combining power supply units with battery backup units (BBUs), which supply emergency power to servers during outages. Qiu said the product validation process alone took nearly three years; he described the wait as so long that "tears almost fell." Annual BBU production capacity has since scaled from approximately 600,000 units last year to 900,000 units this year, he said. Analysts estimate year-on-year growth of at least 70% for the segment this year.

  ## Key accounts and revenue targets

  Lite-On currently has one CSP customer generating more than $1 billion in annual revenue for the company, and one customer in its IT and consumer electronics business approaching that threshold, Qiu said. He defined $1 billion — equivalent to roughly NT$32 billion — as the threshold for a "key account" relationship, and said the company's long-term target is seven to eight such relationships.

  Two additional CSPs, for which Lite-On is now developing next-generation products, are each expected to reach the $1 billion revenue mark within two years, Qiu said. Products under development for these customers include a 1.65-megawatt power rack — a high-density, cabinet-level power distribution unit — weighing 3.5 metric tons with a correspondingly high unit price, he said. Moving into cabinet-level integration rather than standalone component supply is central to the strategy, Qiu added.

  ## DC/DC converter acquisition and solid-state transformer timeline

  In June, Lite-On acquired Nanjing Nenglixin Technology, a Chinese startup focused on DC/DC conversion — the process of transforming one direct-current voltage level to another — for 800 million yuan. Qiu said projects using that technology are already under way and revenue from the Nanjing unit is expected to be visible in the first half of 2027. The Nanjing team has close ties with a U.S. CSP, and Lite-On plans to have both parties jointly develop products for one of the two newly targeted CSP customers, he said.

  Lite-On is also developing solid-state transformers (SSTs) — power conversion devices that replace traditional magnetic-core transformers with semiconductor switching — and plans to send samples to customers in early 2027. Because server qualification cycles run three to five years, Qiu projected mass production would begin between 2028 and 2030.

  ## Capital and R&D investment

  Research and development spending stands at 5 to 5.5 percent of revenue, and Qiu said that proportion will continue to rise. Of a NT$18 billion increase in capital expenditure, 70 percent is designated for R&D and test equipment, he said.

  The DenseLight stake gives Lite-On a foothold in InP photonic component manufacturing, filling the optical alignment and light-source gap in a module strategy the company expects to begin contributing revenue from networking and switch customers in the second half of 2027.
body_zh: |
  光寶科技（2301.TW）係一家在台灣上市的電源供應器與電子製造商，已同意斥資3,430萬美元，取得新加坡整合元件製造商（IDM）DenseLight Photonics 21%的股權。DenseLight專精磷化銦（InP）光子元件，具備自行設計並製造零組件的垂直整合能力。此消息由光寶總裁邱森彬宣布確認。

  此次投資推進了邱森彬所描述的光通訊「三大策略」，涵蓋光源、封裝測試組裝及光學對準技術三個面向。邱森彬表示，光寶本已是光耦合器的主要製造商，並自有Micro LED光源與封裝測試組裝能力。他也指出，公司將持續尋求策略合作夥伴，不排除進一步結盟或投資以加速相關布局。邱森彬說，光寶聚焦網路與交換器應用的光通訊模組業務，預計將於2027年下半年開始貢獻明顯營收。

  ## 從元件到系統的轉型

  DenseLight這筆交易，是邱森彬所描述更宏觀策略轉型的一環——光寶正從單一元件供應商，蛻變為系統級解決方案提供商。邱森彬在上半年業績說明會上表示：「光寶現在真的不一樣了。」公司上半年營收與獲利雙創新高，單季毛利率達27%。

  傳統電源供應器製造面臨激烈的價格競爭壓力。自2021年起，光寶轉型與北美雲端服務供應商（CSP）——即大規模營運雲端基礎設施的業者——共同開發產品，將電源供應器與電池備援單元（BBU）結合；BBU能在停電時為伺服器提供緊急電力。邱森彬表示，光是產品驗證流程就耗費了將近三年，等待之漫長讓他形容「眼淚差點都要掉下來」。他指出，年度BBU產能已從去年約60萬台，擴大至今年的90萬台；分析師估計該業務今年的年增幅至少達70%。

  ## 重點客戶與營收目標

  邱森彬表示，光寶目前已有一家CSP客戶，每年為公司帶來逾10億美元的營收；資訊科技與消費電子業務也有一家客戶正逼近此門檻。他將10億美元（約相當於新台幣320億元）定義為「重點客戶」關係的門檻，並表示公司的長期目標是建立七至八個此類關係。

  邱森彬指出，光寶目前正為另外兩家CSP開發次世代產品，預計這兩家客戶各自在兩年內達到10億美元的營收規模。為這些客戶開發中的產品，包括一款1.65百萬瓦電源機架——屬高密度機櫃級配電單元——重達3.5公噸，單價也相對偏高。邱森彬補充，從供應獨立元件轉向機櫃級整合，正是整體策略的核心所在。

  ## DC/DC轉換器收購案與固態變壓器時程

  今年6月，光寶以8億元人民幣收購南京能力信科技，這是一家專注於DC/DC轉換技術的中國新創公司，核心技術在於將直流電壓從一個等級轉換至另一個等級。邱森彬表示，採用該技術的專案已開始推進，南京部門的相關營收預計將於2027年上半年出現。他說，南京團隊與一家美國CSP關係密切，光寶計畫讓雙方合作，共同為新鎖定的其中一家CSP客戶開發產品。

  光寶也正積極開發固態變壓器（SST）——這種電力轉換裝置以半導體開關取代傳統的磁芯變壓器——並計畫於2027年初送樣至客戶端。由於伺服器認證週期需要三至五年，邱森彬預估量產將於2028年至2030年間啟動。

  ## 資本支出與研發投入

  目前研發支出佔營收的5%至5.5%，邱森彬表示這一比例將持續提升。在新台幣180億元的資本支出增量中，70%將用於研發及測試設備。

  此次取得DenseLight股權，讓光寶在InP光子元件製造領域取得立足點，填補其模組策略中光學對準與光源的缺口。光寶預期此業務將於2027年下半年，開始為網路與交換器客戶貢獻實質營收。
