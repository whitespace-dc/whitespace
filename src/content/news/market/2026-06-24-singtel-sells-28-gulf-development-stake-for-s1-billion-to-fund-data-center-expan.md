body_en: |
  Singtel, Southeast Asia's largest telecommunications operator, has sold a 2.8% stake in Gulf Development, Thailand's largest energy company, for approximately S$1 billion (US$772.9 million), the company disclosed on Tuesday, June 24, 2026. The sale, conducted through a private placement to institutional investors, is part of a broader effort to optimize the company's portfolio and fund expansion into data centers and AI — artificial intelligence.

  The transaction is expected to generate an equity gain of around S$140 million for Singtel. Following the divestment, Singtel retains a 4.95% stake in Gulf Development, valued at approximately S$1.8 billion.

  ## Capital reallocation toward data centers and AI

  Singtel's Group Chief Financial Officer Arthur Lang framed the sale as a disciplined capital management move. "This divestment underscores Singtel's concerted effort to optimize our portfolio as we continue our disciplined approach to capital management," Lang said, as reported by CNBC on June 23, 2026.

  Lang also stated that Singtel's business relationship with Gulf Development remains intact and that Thailand continues to be a strategic market for the company.

  ## Transaction structure

  The stake was sold to institutional investors via private placement — a process in which shares are sold directly to a select group of qualified buyers rather than on the open market. Singtel received approximately S$1 billion in proceeds, equivalent to about US$772.9 million.

  ## Retained position and ongoing Thailand presence

  Despite the partial exit, Singtel remains a significant minority shareholder in Gulf Development, holding a 4.95% stake worth roughly S$1.8 billion after the transaction closes. The company did not indicate plans to fully exit its Gulf Development position, and Lang's comments suggest Thailand remains within Singtel's strategic footprint.

  The divestment positions Singtel to direct additional capital toward data center infrastructure and AI-related growth, sectors the company has identified as high-growth priorities.
body_zh: |
  東南亞最大電信業者新加坡電信（Singtel）於2026年6月24日（週二）正式公告，已透過私募方式向機構投資人出售泰國最大能源企業海灣能源開發公司（Gulf Development）2.8%的股份，交易金額約10億新加坡幣（約7億7,290萬美元）。此次出售為優化投資組合、挹注資料中心與人工智慧（AI）擴張計畫的整體策略部署之一環。

  此次交易完成後，新加坡電信預計認列的股權收益約達1億4,000萬新加坡幣。出售後，新加坡電信在海灣能源開發的持股降至4.95%，市值約18億新加坡幣。

  ## 資本重新配置：聚焦資料中心與AI

  新加坡電信集團財務長Arthur Lang將此次出售定位為審慎的資本管理舉措。「此次出售充分展現新加坡電信積極優化投資組合的決心，也是我們持續落實紀律性資本管理方針的具體實踐，」Lang表示；相關言論由CNBC於2026年6月23日報導。

  Lang並指出，新加坡電信與海灣能源開發之間的業務合作關係維持不變，泰國市場對新加坡電信而言依然是具有策略重要性的市場。

  ## 交易結構

  此次股份出售採私募方式進行——即直接將股份售予特定合格買家群體，而非透過公開市場掛牌交易。新加坡電信共取得約10億新加坡幣（約等值7億7,290萬美元）的交易所得。

  ## 持續持股與泰國市場布局

  儘管進行部分出售，新加坡電信仍以重要少數股東身分持有海灣能源開發4.95%的股份，交易完成後市值約達18億新加坡幣。新加坡電信並未表示有意全面退出其在海灣能源開發的持股部位，而Lang的言論亦顯示，泰國市場依然在新加坡電信的策略版圖之內。

  此次出售使新加坡電信得以將更多資本導向資料中心基礎設施及AI相關成長業務，而這兩大領域均已被新加坡電信列為高成長優先投資方向。
