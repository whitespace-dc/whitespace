body_en: |
  The Philippines' Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) is drafting a masterplan aimed at fast-tracking the infrastructure needed to support artificial intelligence development in the country, Secretary Henry Rhoel R. Aguda said.

  Aguda made the disclosure on the sidelines of the National AI and Skills Summit, telling BusinessWorld that a policy meeting focused on regulation is scheduled for this month. "We are really pushing for infrastructure because we really need that," he said.

  ## AI hub at New Clark City

  A physical anchor for that infrastructure push is already in planning. The Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), a government agency that manages former military bases redeveloped for civilian use, said in April that approximately 1,618 hectares within New Clark City — a planned urban development north of Manila — has been designated for an AI-native industrial hub.

  The BCDA described the site as a Pax Silica Coordination Office, a convergence point where technology companies, research institutions, and government agencies would work together to build an AI ecosystem. The BCDA said groundbreaking is targeted within two years.

  ## Policy and regulation in parallel

  Alongside the infrastructure drive, the DICT is working on an AI policy framework. Aguda indicated the near-term focus of the policy meeting this month will be regulation, suggesting the government is pursuing physical infrastructure and governance frameworks on parallel tracks.

  The DICT has previously stated that the Philippines must accelerate AI adoption by both upgrading infrastructure and strengthening regulation, warning that slow uptake could prevent the country from fully capturing productivity gains.

  ## What comes next

  The DICT's masterplan and the BCDA's New Clark City hub represent the primary declared vehicles for the Philippines' AI infrastructure ambitions. With the policy meeting on regulation set for this month and groundbreaking on the industrial hub targeted within two years, both tracks are expected to advance concurrently in the near term.

  ## 菲律賓資通部起草AI基礎建設總體規劃，新克拉克城樞紐成形

  菲律賓資訊與通訊科技部（DICT）正在草擬一份總體規劃，目標是加快推動支撐人工智慧發展所需的基礎建設。部長亨利·魯埃爾·R·阿古達（Henry Rhoel R. Aguda）對此作出說明。

  阿古達是在「全國AI與技能高峰會」場邊向《商業世界》透露這項消息的。他表示，本月將召開一場以法規為主題的政策會議。他說：「我們確實大力推動基礎建設，因為這是我們真正需要的。」

  ## 新克拉克城AI樞紐

  支撐這波基礎建設推進的實體據點已進入規劃階段。負責管理前軍事基地並將其轉型為民用用途的政府機構——基地轉換暨發展局（BCDA）——於今年四月宣布，位於新克拉克城境內約1,618公頃的土地，已被劃定為以AI為核心的產業園區。新克拉克城是一座座落於馬尼拉北方的計畫型都市開發區。

  BCDA將該基地定位為「帕克斯矽谷協調辦公室」（Pax Silica Coordination Office），作為科技企業、研究機構與政府機關共同協作、建構AI生態系的整合平台。BCDA表示，預計在兩年內舉行動土典禮。

  ## 政策與法規並行推進

  在推動基礎建設的同時，資通部也正積極研擬AI政策框架。阿古達指出，本月政策會議的近期重點將聚焦於法規面，顯示政府正同步推進實體基礎建設與治理框架兩條軌道。

  資通部先前已明確表示，菲律賓必須從升級基礎建設與強化法規兩方面雙管齊下，加速導入AI，並警告若推動速度遲緩，將使該國無法充分掌握生產力提升所帶來的紅利。

  ## 後續展望

  資通部的總體規劃與BCDA在新克拉克城推動的產業園區，是菲律賓AI基礎建設雄心的兩大主要既定推進載體。隨著以法規為主題的政策會議預定本月召開，產業園區的動土典禮也鎖定在兩年內啟動，兩條軌道預計將在近期同步向前推進。
