body_en: |
  Indonesian venture capital firm Init-6 has announced an investment in Netra Runtime, an Indonesian AI inference infrastructure startup that claims its engine delivers four times the token throughput of leading open-source inference alternatives on the same GPU hardware, potentially cutting processing times from one hour to 15 minutes.

  The announcement was made at a media briefing in Jakarta on Thursday. Init-6 was co-founded by Achmad Zaky and Nugroho Herucahyono, who also co-founded Indonesian e-commerce platform Bukalapak; Nugroho serves as Founding Partner.

  ## What Netra Runtime does

  Netra Runtime helps enterprises run AI models on their own GPU servers or through managed cloud services. Its core product is an inference engine — the software layer that executes a trained AI model to generate outputs — positioned as a high-performance alternative to open-source engines already on the market.

  The startup's own benchmarks, as presented by Init-6, show Netra producing four times more tokens per second — tokens being the text units AI models process and generate — than the leading open-source inference engine on identical GPU hardware. Nugroho described the relationship in plain terms: "GPU is like a kitchen, inference engine is like the chef. So with the same kitchen, Netra can produce four times more dishes compared to other open-source inference engines."

  Init-6 said that efficiency gain translates to compressing a one-hour processing job to roughly 15 minutes. For companies that already own GPU hardware, higher utilization of existing equipment would also accelerate return on that capital investment.

  ## Service model and compatibility

  Netra offers two delivery options:

  - **Private deployment**: runs entirely on the customer's own GPU infrastructure
  - **Netra Cloud**: a managed API service billed on a pay-per-token basis

  The platform supports both AMD and NVIDIA GPUs. Its API is compatible with OpenAI's interface, meaning developers can integrate Netra without rewriting existing code built against that standard.

  ## The gap Netra targets

  Nugroho framed the investment around a specific infrastructure gap. Companies can run open-weight models — AI models whose parameters are publicly released, allowing self-hosting — on their own servers to maintain control over infrastructure and costs. However, achieving high performance from those models has been difficult because open-source inference engines have generally lagged behind proprietary technology developed by large AI companies.

  The cost sensitivity of inference has also grown as AI workloads have expanded beyond simple question-answering into code generation, large-document analysis, and continuous agentic tasks — operations that run AI models repeatedly and at scale. "The question for companies is no longer just which model is the smartest and fastest, but how much it costs to produce a piece of work," Nugroho said.

  ## Init-6's strategic rationale

  For Init-6, the investment carries a broader intent beyond the single company. The firm said it wants to push Indonesian technology companies to build the infrastructure that determines AI processing performance and cost, rather than relying entirely on foreign-developed models and API-based applications.

  Init-6 said its near-term goal is for Netra to demonstrate its performance claims serving enterprise customers. Its longer-term target is for Netra to become the preferred AI inference infrastructure for companies in Indonesia and in global markets. "We want Netra to be proof that Indonesian youth can produce AI technology for the entire world," Nugroho said.

  ## Netra Runtime 的產品定位

  Netra Runtime 協助企業在自有 GPU 伺服器上，或透過代管雲端服務執行 AI 模型。其核心產品為推論引擎——即負責執行已訓練 AI 模型以產生輸出結果的軟體層——定位為市場現有開源引擎的高效能替代方案。

  根據 Init-6 呈現的 Netra 自家基準測試數據，在相同 GPU 硬體上，Netra 每秒產生的 token 數量是市面上領先開源推論引擎的四倍。token 是 AI 模型處理與生成文字時所使用的基本單位。Nugroho 以淺顯易懂的方式比喻兩者關係：「GPU 就像廚房，推論引擎就像廚師。同樣的廚房，Netra 能做出比其他開源推論引擎多四倍的料理。」

  Init-6 表示，這項效能提升意味著原本需要一小時的處理作業，可壓縮至大約 15 分鐘。對於已自有 GPU 硬體的企業而言，提升現有設備的使用率，也將加速資本投資的回收速度。

  ## 服務模式與相容性

  Netra 提供兩種交付選項：

  - **私有部署**：完全在客戶自有的 GPU 基礎設施上運行
  - **Netra Cloud**：以按 token 計費的代管 API 服務

  該平台同時支援 AMD 與輝達（NVIDIA）GPU。其 API 相容於 OpenAI 的介面規格，意味著開發者無需改寫現有依循該標準建立的程式碼，即可直接整合 Netra。

  ## Netra 瞄準的市場缺口

  Nugroho 以一個特定的基礎設施缺口，說明此次投資的背景脈絡。企業可在自有伺服器上執行開放權重模型——即參數已公開釋出、允許自行架設的 AI 模型——以掌控基礎設施與成本。然而，要從這些模型中實現高效能向來並不容易，原因在於開源推論引擎的技術水準，普遍落後於大型 AI 公司自行開發的專有技術。

  隨著 AI 工作負載從簡單的問答應用，擴展至程式碼生成、大型文件分析，以及需要持續、大規模重複執行 AI 模型的代理任務，推論端的成本敏感度也日益升高。「企業面臨的問題，不再只是哪個模型最聰明、最快速，而是產出一件成果需要付出多少成本，」Nugroho 說。

  ## Init-6 的策略考量

  對 Init-6 而言，這筆投資所承載的意義，不僅止於單一公司。該公司表示，希望推動印尼科技企業主動投入建構決定 AI 處理效能與成本的基礎設施，而非完全仰賴外國開發的模型與以 API 為基礎的應用程式。

  Init-6 表示，短期目標是讓 Netra 透過服務企業客戶，驗證其效能主張；長期目標則是讓 Netra 成為印尼及全球市場企業首選的 AI 推論基礎設施。「我們希望 Netra 能證明，印尼的年輕人有能力為全世界打造 AI 技術，」Nugroho 說。
