body_en: |
  YTL Power International Bhd has announced plans to build a gigawatt-scale data centre campus at Sedenak Tech Park West in Johor, partnering with JLand Group, the real estate arm of the state-linked conglomerate Johor Corporation, to secure the land.

  The development will be carried out through SIPP Power Sdn Bhd, a unit in which YTL Power holds a 70% stake, according to a joint statement released on Aug 19. SIPP Power will acquire an initial 145 acres at Sedenak Tech Park West from JLand Group, with an option to purchase up to a further 400 acres — bringing the potential campus footprint to 545 acres. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

  ## The Sedenak site and its partners

  Sedenak Tech Park West sits within Ibrahim Technopolis, a development for which JLand Group serves as master developer. JLand Group manages assets valued at more than RM25 billion on behalf of the Johor state government. The remaining 30% of SIPP Power is held by SIPP Energy Sdn Bhd, controlled by Datuk Daing A Malek Daing A Rahman, a former Umno Kota Tinggi division chief.

  "Together with our expanding Kulai campus, Sedenak will further strengthen Johor's position as a leading regional centre for artificial intelligence, cloud computing and digital innovation," YTL Power managing director Datuk Seri Yeoh Seok Hong said in the joint statement. JLand Group managing director Datuk Akmal Ahmad said YTL Power's presence at the site "brings significant digital infrastructure investment and strengthens the wider ecosystem across energy, utilities, technology and supporting industries."

  ## Scale in context: what a gigawatt means

  A gigawatt (GW) is 1,000 megawatts (MW). For comparison, a large coal-fired power station typically produces around 1,000 MW. Data centres are rated by the electrical power they draw to run servers and cooling systems; a gigawatt campus would rank among the largest single data centre developments in Southeast Asia if fully built out.

  YTL Power's existing YTL Green Data Center Park in Kulai, also in Johor, had 298 MW of capacity under contract as of March 2026. The company said it planned to expand that park's total capacity to 1,000 MW from a previously announced target of 600 MW. The Sedenak campus would represent an additional, separate development on top of Kulai.

  ## Nvidia partnership and AI infrastructure

  The Kulai campus also houses YTL Power's AI infrastructure, developed in partnership with US chipmaker Nvidia Corp. YTL Power and Nvidia first announced that collaboration in December 2023, with plans to build AI infrastructure in Malaysia using Nvidia H100 Tensor Core GPUs and Nvidia AI Enterprise software.

  In March 2024 YTL Power announced the formation of YTL AI Cloud, described as a specialised provider of large-scale GPU-based accelerated computing. GPUs, or graphics processing units, are the chips used to train and run artificial intelligence models. The company said it was among the first to adopt Nvidia's GB200 NVL72, a liquid-cooled, rack-scale system that integrates 72 Blackwell-generation GPUs and 36 Grace CPUs — Nvidia's own processor design — connected via fifth-generation NVLink, a high-speed chip interconnect.

  ## IPO prospects and valuation

  The Sedenak announcement comes as YTL Power is weighing a potential public listing of its data centre business. RHB Investment Bank said in July that the group was exploring an initial public offering of the data centre unit in 2027 to raise funds and unlock value. RHB estimated the non-AI data centre business alone could be valued at approximately RM28 billion, equivalent to about RM3.06 per YTL Power share, based on its own assumptions.

  YTL Power shares closed at RM4.88 on Aug 19, down two sen or 0.4% on the day, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM42.57 billion. The stock has risen more than 46% year to date.

  ## Regional significance

  Johor has attracted a concentration of large-scale data centre investments from international technology companies, drawn in part by land availability, proximity to Singapore, and improving power infrastructure. The Sedenak campus, if developed to its full 545-acre option, would substantially expand YTL Power's footprint in the state and add to the region's position as a data centre hub.

  YTL Power has not disclosed a timeline for construction at Sedenak or the phasing of its planned gigawatt capacity target. The company's next disclosed milestone remains the expansion of the Kulai park to 1,000 MW, alongside the exploration of a data centre IPO in 2027.

  ## 實納達基地及合作夥伴

  實納達科技園西區坐落於易卜拉欣科技城（Ibrahim Technopolis）範圍之內，JLand集團擔任該綜合開發案的主要開發商，代表柔佛州政府管理逾250億令吉資產。SIPP Power剩餘30%股權由SIPP能源私人有限公司（SIPP Energy Sdn Bhd）持有，該公司由前巫統哥打丁宜區部長拿督Daing A Malek Daing A Rahman掌控。

  YTL Power執行董事拿督斯里葉昭鴻（Datuk Seri Yeoh Seok Hong）在聯合聲明中表示：「配合我們不斷擴展的古來園區，實納達將進一步鞏固柔佛作為人工智慧、雲端運算與數位創新領域領先區域中心的地位。」JLand集團執行董事拿督阿克馬·艾哈邁德（Datuk Akmal Ahmad）則表示，YTL Power進駐此基地「將帶來重大數位基礎設施投資，並強化能源、公用事業、科技及相關產業的整體生態系。」

  ## 規模解析：十億瓦意味著什麼

  1吉瓦（GW）等於1,000百萬瓦（MW）。相較之下，一座大型燃煤電廠的發電量通常約為1,000 MW。資料中心的規模以驅動伺服器及冷卻系統所需的電力容量來衡量；若十億瓦級園區全面建成，將躋身東南亞規模最大的單一資料中心開發案之列。

  YTL Power在柔佛古來現有的「YTL綠色資料中心園區」（YTL Green Data Center Park），截至2026年3月已簽約容量達298 MW。該公司表示，計畫將古來園區的總容量從先前宣布的600 MW目標提升至1,000 MW。實納達園區則屬於獨立的額外開發案，並非古來園區的延伸。

  ## 輝達合作夥伴關係與人工智慧基礎設施

  古來園區同時也是YTL Power與美國晶片製造商輝達（Nvidia Corp）攜手打造人工智慧基礎設施的所在地。YTL Power與輝達於2023年12月首度公布合作計畫，預計在馬來西亞以輝達H100 Tensor Core繪圖處理器（GPU）及輝達AI Enterprise軟體建置人工智慧基礎設施。

  2024年3月，YTL Power宣布成立YTL AI Cloud，定位為提供大規模GPU加速運算的專業業者。GPU（繪圖處理器）是用於訓練與執行人工智慧模型的核心晶片。該公司表示，是全球最早採用輝達GB200 NVL72的業者之一——這是一套液冷式、機架級系統，整合72顆Blackwell世代GPU與36顆Grace CPU（輝達自有處理器設計），透過第五代NVLink高速晶片互連技術相互串接。

  ## 上市前景與估值

  實納達園區的宣布，適逢YTL Power評估旗下資料中心業務潛在掛牌時機之際。RHB投資銀行7月指出，集團正研究於2027年將資料中心部門進行首次公開募股（IPO），以籌集資金並釋放價值。RHB依其自身假設估計，單是非AI資料中心業務的估值即可達約280億令吉，相當於每股YTL Power約3.06令吉。

  YTL Power股票8月19日收盤報4.88令吉，下跌2分或0.4%，公司市值為425.7億令吉。該股年初至今已累計上漲逾46%。

  ## 區域戰略意義

  柔佛州近年吸引大批國際科技公司投入大規模資料中心建設，主要原因在於土地供應充裕、毗鄰新加坡，以及電力基礎設施持續改善。若實納達園區按照最多545英畝的選購選項全面開發，將大幅擴展YTL Power在柔佛的佈局版圖，並進一步鞏固該地區作為資料中心樞紐的地位。

  YTL Power尚未公布實納達的建設時程，或其計畫中十億瓦容量目標的分期推進方式。目前該公司已披露的最近里程碑，仍是將古來園區擴充至1,000 MW，以及推進2027年資料中心IPO的評估工作。
