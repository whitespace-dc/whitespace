body_en: |
  PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk. (ticker: TLKM) has shortlisted final candidates for a 70% stake sale in its data center subsidiary PT Telkom Data Ekosistem, known as NeutraDC, with management targeting deal completion before the end of 2026, according to a research note published July 16 by analysts at BRI Danareksa Sekuritas.

  The analysts, Kafi Ananta and Erindra Krisnawan, put the implied transaction value at between $1 billion and $1.5 billion. Telkom currently holds a 99.99% direct stake in NeutraDC.

  ## Strategic rationale

  Telkom's management described the divestiture as a means of securing a global data center operator as a strategic partner to strengthen operational capabilities, specifically in serving hyperscaler customers — the large cloud providers such as those running global-scale cloud infrastructure that require specialist data center management and connectivity.

  NeutraDC remains a small contributor to the parent company at present. The unit accounted for 1.1% of Telkom's consolidated revenue in the first quarter of 2026 and across all of 2025. Management has set a medium-term target to raise that contribution to between 3% and 5% of consolidated revenue.

  ## Market reaction and equity valuation

  Telkom's shares rose 5.14% on Friday, July 17, 2026, closing at Rp2,660, a move the BRI Danareksa Sekuritas analysts attributed in part to market approval of the divestiture plan. Despite the single-session gain, the stock remains down 23.56% year-to-date.

  The analysts carry a buy recommendation on TLKM with a base price target of Rp3,750. They said a successful transaction at the upper end of the valuation range could push the implied target higher. "The entry of a global data center operator at a valuation of $1.0–$1.5 billion has the potential to unlock value for TLKM, indicating upside of around 4% to our price target, to Rp3,900 per share, assuming NeutraDC is valued using TLKM's blended EV/EBITDA of 5.4 times," they wrote. EV/EBITDA, or enterprise value divided by earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, is a standard measure used to compare company valuations.

  ## Deal timeline

  Telkom management stated the candidate shortlist is at its final stage and that the company expects to close the transaction within 2026, according to the BRI Danareksa Sekuritas note. No acquirer names were disclosed in the source material.

  ## Significance

  The transaction, if completed at the indicated range, would represent one of the larger data center asset sales in Southeast Asia's emerging market for digital infrastructure. For Telkom, bringing in an established global operator could accelerate NeutraDC's ability to compete for hyperscaler contracts — a segment that typically demands operational track records and technical standards that regional carriers are still building. The deal would also give Telkom a cash event at a time when the stock has underperformed significantly on a year-to-date basis.

  Telkom management has said it expects the transaction to be finalized before the end of 2026.

  印尼電信（PT Telkom Indonesia Persero Tbk，股票代號：TLKM）已就旗下資料中心子公司PT Telkom Data Ekosistem（NeutraDC）70%股權出售案完成最終候選名單遴選，管理層目標在2026年底前完成交易。上述訊息來自BRI Danareksa Sekuritas分析師於7月16日發布的研究報告。

  負責撰寫這份報告的分析師Kafi Ananta與Erindra Krisnawan，將此次交易的隱含估值定在10億至15億美元之間。目前，印尼電信直接持有NeutraDC 99.99%的股權。

  ## 策略考量

  印尼電信管理層表示，此次股權出售旨在引進全球資料中心業者擔任策略夥伴，藉此強化公司的營運能力，尤其是在服務超大規模雲端客戶方面——這類大型雲端服務供應商所經營的是全球規模的雲端基礎設施，對資料中心管理與網路連接均有專業層級的高度要求。

  就目前而言，NeutraDC對母公司的業績貢獻仍相當有限。2026年第一季及整個2025年，NeutraDC均僅佔印尼電信合併營收的1.1%。管理層設定的中期目標，是將這一比重提升至合併營收的3%至5%。

  ## 市場反應與股票估值

  印尼電信股價於2026年7月17日（星期五）收盤上漲5.14%，收在盧比2,660元，BRI Danareksa Sekuritas的分析師認為，此次漲勢部分反映了市場對股權出售計畫的正面看法。儘管單日表現亮眼，該股今年以來累計仍下跌23.56%。

  分析師對TLKM維持「買進」評等，基本目標價訂在盧比3,750元。他們指出，若交易以估值區間上緣完成，目標價仍有進一步上修的空間。報告中寫道：「全球資料中心業者以10億至15億美元估值入股，有潛力為TLKM釋放價值，假設NeutraDC採用TLKM混合EV／EBITDA倍數5.4倍進行估值，則目標價上調幅度約4%，至每股盧比3,900元。」所謂EV／EBITDA，即企業價值除以息稅折舊攤銷前利潤，是業界比較公司估值時普遍採用的標準指標。

  ## 交易時程

  根據BRI Danareksa Sekuritas的研究報告，印尼電信管理層表示，候選名單目前已進入最後階段，公司預計在2026年內完成交易。原始資料未披露任何潛在買家的名稱。

  ## 交易意義

  若此次交易以前述估值區間完成，將是東南亞新興數位基礎設施市場迄今規模較大的資料中心資產交易之一。對印尼電信而言，引進一家具備成熟營運實力的全球業者，有助於加速NeutraDC在爭取超大規模雲端合約方面的競爭力——這類合約通常要求供應商擁有一定的營運紀錄與技術規格，而這正是區域電信業者目前仍在持續建立的能力。此外，在公司股價今年以來明顯落後的背景下，這筆交易也將為印尼電信帶來一次重要的現金入帳機會。

  印尼電信管理層已明確表示，預計在2026年底前完成這項交易。
