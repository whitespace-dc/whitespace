body_en: |
  AirTrunk, the Blackstone-owned hyperscale data centre operator, has secured a US$2.325 billion green loan for its JHB2 data centre campus in Johor Bahru, Malaysia, the company said July 29. The deal is the largest single-asset financing in AirTrunk's history and the largest green project finance transaction for a single data centre in Malaysia.

  The loan was arranged through a syndicate of 30 local and international financial institutions, led by 10 global coordinators: BNP Paribas, Crédit Agricole Corporate and Investment Bank (CIB), DBS, ING, Mizuho, MUFG, Société Générale, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, HSBC and United Overseas Bank. Crédit Agricole CIB, DBS, ING and HSBC also served as green loan coordinators. Other mandated lead arrangers, underwriters and bookrunners included CIMB, Clifford Capital Credit Solutions, the International Finance Corporation (IFC), National Bank of Kuwait and Standard Chartered Bank Singapore.

  ## First IFC involvement and Blue Finance alignment

  The transaction marks AirTrunk's first financing from the IFC, the private-sector lending arm of the World Bank Group. The IFC's participation also designates JHB2 as the first data centre financing in Asia-Pacific aligned with IFC's Blue Finance Guidelines 2.0, a framework that links loan structures to site-level water efficiency measures, the company said.

  ## Energy and water efficiency targets

  JHB2 is designed to achieve a power usage effectiveness (PUE) ratio of 1.37. PUE measures how efficiently a data centre uses electricity: a ratio of 1.0 would mean every unit of power drawn from the grid reaches computing equipment, with none consumed by cooling, lighting or other building overhead. The company said the global industry average is approximately 1.5 to 1.6, placing JHB2's target below that norm. The campus will also use advanced water-efficient cooling technology.

  The loan sits under AirTrunk's Green Financing Framework and is structured around environmental key performance indicators. Margin savings generated by the green designation will fund community programmes in Malaysia, including recycled-water schemes in schools through Gravity Water and Water Watch Penang, disaster relief through MERCY Malaysia, and STEM (science, technology, engineering and mathematics) education with Universiti Teknologi Malaysia, the company said.

  ## Market context

  Data centre investment across Asia-Pacific has accelerated on the back of the artificial intelligence build-out, even as the broader regional syndicated loan market is in its deepest slump in 16 years. Johor has attracted particular investment because of its proximity to Singapore and its access to land and power.

  The size of the JHB2 syndicate reflects broad lender appetite for established data centre operators with large assets tied to sustainability benchmarks, though some banks are managing sector concentration. Crédit Agricole CIB, a global coordinator on the JHB2 deal, is separately seeking to sell approximately HK$150 million of a loan it and other banks provided to property and logistics platform ESR Group after reaching its internal lending cap for data centres, according to people familiar with the matter.

  ## AirTrunk's broader financing activity

  AirTrunk is also in talks with banks for an A$4.3 billion loan for a new data centre in Australia and has been working on asset-backed bonds to refinance existing bank debt. The company is said to be targeting an initial public offering of a Singapore real estate investment trust (REIT) that could raise approximately US$1.5 billion.

  黑石集團（Blackstone）旗下超大規模資料中心營運商AirTrunk於7月29日宣布，已為旗下位於馬來西亞柔佛新山的JHB2資料中心園區取得23.25億美元綠色貸款。此筆交易是AirTrunk史上最大單一資產融資案，同時也是馬來西亞單一資料中心規模最大的綠色專案融資交易。

  ## 首度獲IFC參與及藍色金融準則認定

  此次交易標誌著AirTrunk首度獲得IFC的融資支持。IFC是世界銀行集團旗下負責私部門放款的機構。AirTrunk表示，IFC的參與使JHB2成為亞太地區首個符合IFC《藍色金融準則2.0》（Blue Finance Guidelines 2.0）的資料中心融資案，該準則將貸款結構與場址層級的用水效率措施相互連結。

  ## 能源與用水效率目標

  JHB2的設計目標為達成電源使用效率（PUE）1.37的比率。PUE衡量資料中心的用電效率：若比率為1.0，表示從電網取用的每一單位電力均直接送達運算設備，冷卻、照明或其他建築設施的耗電為零。AirTrunk表示，全球業界平均值約為1.5至1.6，JHB2的目標低於此一水準。此外，園區將採用先進的節水冷卻技術。

  本次貸款隸屬於AirTrunk的綠色融資框架，並以環境關鍵績效指標為結構基礎。AirTrunk表示，綠色認定所帶來的利差節省將挹注馬來西亞的社區計畫，包括透過Gravity Water及Water Watch Penang推動學校再生水方案、透過MERCY Malaysia支援災難救援，以及與馬來西亞理工大學（Universiti Teknologi Malaysia）合作推動STEM（科學、技術、工程與數學）教育。

  ## 市場背景

  儘管亞太區整體聯貸市場正陷入16年來最深的低迷，亞太地區的資料中心投資卻在人工智慧建設浪潮的帶動下持續加速。柔佛州因毗鄰新加坡，且具備充裕的土地與電力資源，格外受到投資者青睞。

  JHB2銀團規模之大，反映出市場貸款機構對於擁有大型資產、且與永續指標掛鉤的成熟資料中心業者的廣泛興趣，儘管部分銀行正在管控產業集中度的問題。據知情人士透露，JHB2交易的全球協調行之一——農業信貸企業與投資銀行在達到資料中心內部授信上限後，目前正另尋買家承接其與其他銀行向不動產暨物流平台ESR集團所提供貸款中約1.5億港元的部位。

  ## AirTrunk更廣泛的融資動態

  AirTrunk目前亦正與銀行商討一筆43億澳元的貸款，用於在澳洲新建資料中心，同時也在推動資產擔保債券以為現有銀行債務進行再融資。據悉，該公司正計畫推動新加坡不動產投資信託（REIT）首次公開募股，目標籌資約15億美元。
