body_en: |
  Applied Digital has signed a lease with an unnamed investment-grade hyperscaler covering 150 megawatts (MW) of critical IT capacity — the amount of power dedicated to running servers and networking equipment — in a deal the company said will generate $5.4 billion in contracted revenue over an 18-year base term.

  The tenant was not disclosed. The lease can be extended to a total of 33 years, with the full value rising to approximately $12.7 billion if all renewal options are exercised, the company said.

  ## Deal terms and pricing

  Applied Digital said the new agreement carries improved commercial terms over its prior leases, including higher per-megawatt pricing, without specifying figures. The company did not identify the facility at which the 150 MW will be deployed.

  Capacity delivery is expected to begin in the first half of 2028, the company said, subject to development milestones, utility arrangements, and regulatory approvals.

  ## Financing structure

  CFO Saidal Mohmand said in a statement that the company plans to finance the build through its established structure: a $5 billion equity partnership with Macquarie Asset Management, followed by project-level debt financing.

  ## Context: prior leases and contracted revenue portfolio

  The deal follows a 15-year, 210 MW lease worth approximately $5.2 billion that Applied Digital signed in June at its Delta Forge 2 site, also with an unnamed tenant.

  With the new agreement, Applied Digital said its total contracted base-term lease revenue now exceeds $41 billion, rising to approximately $100 billion when renewal options across its portfolio are included. The company added that approximately 75 percent of its contracted base-term lease revenue is now tied to investment-grade customers — entities with credit ratings indicating low default risk — up from prior disclosures.

  ## Industry significance

  The scale of the deal — $5.4 billion in base-term contracted revenue from a single 150 MW lease — reflects the pricing power data center operators have gained as hyperscale cloud and AI infrastructure demand has outpaced available capacity. The 18-year base term, longer than many conventional colocation agreements, signals the hyperscaler's intent to secure capacity on a multi-decade horizon rather than shorter build-to-suit cycles.

  Applied Digital's reliance on a Macquarie equity partnership and project financing, rather than corporate-level debt, is consistent with how infrastructure developers have structured large power-intensive builds to avoid concentrating balance-sheet risk.

  Delivery of the 150 MW is contingent on development milestones, utility arrangements, and regulatory approvals, with the first capacity expected online no earlier than the first half of 2028.

  ## Applied Digital簽下54億美元、150MW資料中心租約，租客為匿名超大規模業者

  Applied Digital宣布與一家未具名的投資等級超大規模業者簽署租約，提供150百萬瓦（MW）關鍵IT容量——即專門用於運行伺服器與網路設備的電力規模——該公司表示，這筆交易將在18年基本租期內創造54億美元的合約營收。

  承租方身分未予披露。若所有續約選項均獲行使，租約可延長至最多33年，總合約價值將上升至約127億美元。

  ## 交易條款與定價

  Applied Digital表示，此次新協議的商業條款優於先前租約，包含更高的每百萬瓦計價，但未具體說明數字。該公司亦未透露這150MW將部署於哪座設施。

  容量交付預計於2028年上半年啟動，但仍須視開發里程碑達成情況、電力配置安排及法規審批進度而定。

  ## 融資架構

  財務長Saidal Mohmand在聲明中表示，公司計畫沿用既有架構為本次建設融資：先透過與麥格理資產管理（Macquarie Asset Management）的50億美元股權合夥關係籌資，再搭配專案層級的債務融資。

  ## 背景：先前租約與合約營收組合

  此次交易是繼Applied Digital於今年6月在Delta Forge 2園區簽署的一份15年、210MW、約52億美元租約之後的又一大案，同樣未揭露承租方。

  加計此次新協議，Applied Digital表示其合約基本租期總營收現已超過410億美元；若將整個投資組合的所有續約選項納入計算，金額更上看約1,000億美元。該公司並指出，目前約75%的合約基本租期營收來自投資等級客戶——即信用評等顯示違約風險低的企業——較先前揭露的比例已大幅提升。

  ## 產業意義

  此筆交易的規模——單一150MW租約即創造54億美元基本期合約營收——充分反映出超大規模雲端與AI基礎建設需求持續超越可用容量，資料中心業者因此取得強勁的定價議價能力。18年的基本租期長於多數傳統共置機房協議，顯示該超大規模業者意圖以數十年為視野鎖定容量，而非採取週期較短的客製化建置模式。

  Applied Digital仰賴麥格理股權合夥與專案融資、而非企業層級債務的作法，與其他大型高耗電基礎建設開發商集中管控資產負債表風險的業界慣例一致。

  這150MW容量的交付，仍取決於開發里程碑的達成、電力配置安排及法規審批，預計最快於2028年上半年完成首批上線。
