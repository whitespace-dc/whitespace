body_en: |
  Taiwan's industrial real estate market recorded more than NT$100 billion (New Taiwan dollars) in transactions in the first half of 2026, already surpassing the full-year 2025 total of NT$90 billion-plus, as demand from artificial intelligence (AI)-driven technology companies, contract manufacturers, and logistics operators simultaneously drives activity while each sector seeks fundamentally different site characteristics, Chen Yi-sheng, general manager of Taiwanese property advisory firm Hongda International Assets, said on June 26.

  Chen said the market's strength reflects two overlapping drivers: a large-scale return of Taiwanese manufacturers from overseas that accelerated from 2018, and a more recent surge tied to the AI and big-data electronics boom. A new category of demand, data center facilities, has also emerged alongside the three traditional types: technology office-factories, production and assembly plants, and logistics and warehousing operations.

  Despite the volume of transaction activity, finding suitable sites remains the primary obstacle for expanding companies, according to Chen. He identified the main barriers as poor location, land-use zoning mismatches, inadequate transportation access, and insufficient infrastructure, particularly power supply and wastewater treatment capacity, with high prices an additional deterrent. Decades of industrial land conversion to residential use, combined with quickly saturated new industrial parks, have tightened supply across the market, he said.

  Chen uses the shorthand of "people, goods, and vehicles" to distinguish the three main demand categories and their respective site priorities.

  ## Tech office-factories: talent and transit access

  Facilities operating as technology office-factories resemble corporate headquarters more than traditional manufacturing plants. Employee density is high, and key site criteria include commuting infrastructure, dining options, and access to financial services such as banks and post offices, Chen said. Improvements in public transit, particularly metro rail systems, have driven a wave of redevelopment in older industrial zones into tech campus-style buildings.

  Chen cited Nvidia's selection of Beitou-Shilin Technology Park in Taipei as an example of this category. He said at the time of the decision that Nvidia would choose Taipei over other cities despite bearing the highest costs, because Jensen Huang, Nvidia's chief executive, aimed to establish a global overseas headquarters with the specific purpose of attracting top-tier talent. Chen described that goal as distinct from the conventional thinking of most companies when selecting industrial sites.

  ## Production and assembly: power supply and cost control

  For production-oriented facilities, reliable industrial infrastructure is the overriding priority, with power supply the most critical single factor, Chen said. In the electronics sector, he said, inadequate local grid capacity renders a site non-viable regardless of any other merits. Outbound logistics access is the secondary priority, followed by land prices that reflect market rates.

  Foxconn, whose revenues derive primarily from contract manufacturing at relatively low margins, typifies this category, Chen said. The guiding principle for production-type site selection is cost reduction, he said. Production-use factories represent the most scarce category of industrial real estate in the current market, according to Chen.

  ## Logistics and warehousing: road width and interchange proximity

  Logistics operators require large tracts of land at low cost, and their defining site requirement is vehicle access, Chen said. He named Hsinchu Logistics and Yonglian Logistics as representative examples. Facilities handle dozens to hundreds of heavy-truck movements per day, making proximity to freeway interchanges and sufficient road width the primary selection criteria. Worker amenities are not a major factor, so logistics operators typically locate in suburban areas, with smaller transfer stations in urban centers for regional distribution, he said.

  Logistics operators have also been incorporating AI-driven automated systems for managing inbound and outbound freight, which has increased their power consumption requirements relative to previous years, Chen added.

  ## Supply mismatch and government incentives

  The divergence in site requirements means that surplus in one category does not resolve shortfalls in another. Chen said that while transactions in which traditional manufacturers sell older factories to technology companies have become a recurring pattern, the technical and regulatory requirements of each category often make such transfers impractical, leaving companies across all three sectors competing for a limited pool of appropriate sites.

  Companies returning to Taiwan that qualify for the government's Invest in Taiwan Three Major Programs 2.0 initiative, extended through 2027, are eligible for integrated support covering land, labor, financing, utilities, and tax benefits, Chen noted. The program is projected to attract NT$1.2 trillion in corporate investment between 2025 and 2027 and to generate 80,000 domestic jobs, while also encouraging participating companies to integrate AI into their business operations.

  ## AI與物流需求分歧　台灣工業不動產上半年交易額破千億

  台灣工業不動產市場2026年上半年交易金額已逾新台幣1,000億元，不僅超越2025年全年逾900億元的總量，更反映出人工智慧（AI）科技業、代工製造商與物流業者三路並進、各覓其地的市場格局。台灣本土不動產顧問業者宏大國際資產總經理陳一聲於6月26日指出，這三類需求者對廠址條件的要求南轅北轍，形成市場上截然不同的供需態勢。

  陳一聲表示，市場動能源自兩股相互疊加的驅動力：其一是從2018年起加速的台商回流潮，製造業大規模將產線遷回台灣；其二是近年AI與大數據電子產業的爆發性成長。在既有的科技辦公廠房、生產組裝廠與物流倉儲三大傳統需求類型之外，資料中心也已成為一股全新的需求力道。

  儘管交易量能旺盛，對有意擴張的企業而言，覓得合適廠地仍是首要難題。陳一聲點出幾項主要障礙：地點偏遠、土地使用分區不符、對外交通欠佳，以及基礎設施不足——尤其是供電能力與廢水處理容量——加上高昂的地價，種種因素相互疊加。他說，數十年來工業用地持續被轉用為住宅用途，加上新設工業區一開放便迅速飽和，使得整體市場供給日益吃緊。

  陳一聲以「人、貨、車」三個字概括三類主要需求，並以此區分各類廠址的核心選址條件。

  ## 科技辦公廠房：人才與交通可及性

  科技辦公廠房的型態更接近企業總部，而非傳統製造廠。此類設施員工密度高，選址時的關鍵條件包括：通勤基礎設施是否完善、周邊餐飲選擇是否充足，以及銀行、郵局等金融服務的便利性，陳一聲說。隨著大眾捷運系統等公共運輸不斷改善，老舊工業區也掀起一波向科技園區型建築轉型的再開發浪潮。

  陳一聲以輝達（NVIDIA）選擇台北市北投士林科技園區作為此類需求的典型案例。他表示，輝達當年在做出決策時，雖然台北的進駐成本高於其他城市，執行長黃仁勳仍選定台北，原因在於他希望在此設立一個全球性的海外總部，目標明確指向延攬頂尖人才。陳一聲指出，這樣的思維與大多數企業在工業選址時的慣常邏輯截然不同。

  ## 生產組裝廠：穩定供電與成本控制

  對以生產為導向的廠房而言，可靠的工業基礎設施是壓倒一切的首要條件，其中又以電力供應最為關鍵，陳一聲說。他表示，在電子業，當地電網容量不足便足以讓一塊地喪失投資價值，無論其他條件再優越都無濟於事。其次是出貨物流的便利性，再其次才是符合市場行情的地價。

  陳一聲以鴻海為例說明此類需求。鴻海營收主要來自毛利率相對偏低的代工製造業務，成本壓縮是其生產型廠址選擇的核心原則。他指出，生產用廠房目前是工業不動產市場中供給最為稀缺的一個類別。

  ## 物流倉儲：路寬與交流道距離

  物流業者需要大面積、低地價的土地，而決定選址與否的關鍵在於車輛進出的便利性，陳一聲說。他舉新竹物流與永聯物流為代表性案例。物流設施每日進出的重型貨車動輒數十乃至數百輛次，因此與高速公路交流道的距離及道路寬度是首要選址標準。由於員工生活機能並非重點考量，物流業者通常將主要設施設在郊區，僅於市區設置較小規模的轉運站，服務區域配送需求，陳一聲說。

  他補充，物流業者近年來也積極導入AI驅動的自動化系統來管理貨物進出，相較於過去，這使得物流設施的用電需求明顯提升。

  ## 供需錯配與政府投資誘因

  各類廠址需求條件如此分歧，意味著某一類型的供給過剩，並無法填補另一類型的缺口。陳一聲表示，傳統製造業者將老舊廠房出售給科技公司的交易模式雖已屢見不鮮，但各類廠房在技術規格與法規要求上的差異，往往使這類移轉窒礙難行，結果是三類業者持續爭奪那一池數量有限的合適廠地。

  陳一聲也提到，符合資格的回台投資企業可申請政府推出並延長至2027年的「歡迎台商回台投資行動方案2.0」，享有土地、勞工、融資、水電及租稅等一條龍整合支援。該方案預估在2025年至2027年間可帶動新台幣1.2兆元的企業投資，並創造8萬個國內就業機會，同時鼓勵參與企業將AI融入其業務營運。
