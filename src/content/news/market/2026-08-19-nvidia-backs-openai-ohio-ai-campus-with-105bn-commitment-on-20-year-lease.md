body_en: |
  OpenAI will lease the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Portsmouth, Ohio, built on the site of a former Cold War-era uranium enrichment plant, for 20 years under a financing arrangement backed by a $105 billion commitment from Nvidia, according to a regulatory filing published on August 17. The campus will initially carry 4.25 gigawatts (GW) of computing capacity and could expand to 8 GW, a scale that would make it the largest single AI data centre facility in the world.

  Nvidia's $105 billion guarantee is its largest financing commitment to date. It covers parts of the lease and power obligations for the initial approximately 4 GW phase, but does not cover the full cost of the campus or all of OpenAI's obligations. Capacity is scheduled to come online in phases between 2028 and 2030, with Nvidia's remaining exposure declining as data centres enter service and lease payments begin.

  The campus will run exclusively on Nvidia graphics processing units (GPUs), central processing units (CPUs), networking equipment, and infrastructure software, with Nvidia reserving the site for its compute platform.

  ## Financing structure

  SoftBank and its subsidiary SB Energy, which owns the campus, will invest more than $4 billion in local energy infrastructure and have committed to building at least 10 GW of new energy generation to power the site. Nvidia will separately invest $1.5 billion in SB Energy. OpenAI has pledged $40 million to "support local priorities."

  Nvidia directly rebutted concerns about circular financing, a term for arrangements in which chip suppliers back the infrastructure spending of their own customers, raising questions about whether AI demand is self-reinforcing rather than organic. "OpenAI will pay the lease," Nvidia wrote in a blog post on August 17, framing the deal as an exercise in the same discipline it applies to semiconductor supply-chain management. OpenAI carries the lease obligation; Nvidia's role is to backstop certain commitments to secure a site it expects to keep active across multiple generations of hardware.

  Nvidia also argued the site would retain value even if OpenAI stepped away. Capacity at PORTS-Pike could be reassigned to cloud providers, enterprises, AI labs, or start-ups from its customer base because its hardware and software are standardized across users, the company said.

  ## Site economics

  Nvidia described PORTS-Pike as a long-life asset designed for repeated hardware upgrades as new generations of compute equipment are released. Each generation deployed at the site could involve roughly 1.5 million Nvidia GPUs and generate between $150 billion and $200 billion in revenue for Nvidia. Over two decades, the company expects the campus to support several such cycles.

  OpenAI's existing and planned Nvidia compute commitments, including PORTS-Pike, total approximately 12 GW through 2030. If the Ohio deal expands to the full 8 GW, that figure could rise to 16 GW, representing roughly $600 billion in compute spending through 2030, Nvidia said.

  ## Scale

  A nuclear power plant generates approximately 1 GW of electricity. At its initial 4.25 GW, PORTS-Pike would equal roughly four such plants running simultaneously. The entire city of San Francisco consumed just over 5 GW in 2024, according to state data; the full 8 GW build-out would exceed that figure by more than half.

  ## Jobs and community

  OpenAI said the project will create 35,000 temporary construction jobs over six years and 2,500 long-term operating positions. OpenAI and SB Energy stated they will cover energy and infrastructure costs without passing them on to local residents.

  Those commitments address growing public hostility toward large data centre projects. More than 70 percent of Americans oppose building data centres near their homes, according to a Gallup poll from March, and rising electricity bills attributed in part to data centre power loads are driving debate in the midterm elections. Sam Altman, OpenAI's chief executive officer, said in a statement it would be "a huge site, with enough computing power to help millions of people use AI to do things we can only start to imagine today."

  ## Nvidia's expanding role

  The Ohio deal marks a further shift in Nvidia's position in the AI sector, from chip supplier to a company actively involved in financing and securing the physical inputs large-scale AI systems require, including land, power access, and data centre shell. Nvidia said it will remain selective, focusing on what it described as exceptional sites where sustained demand can support multiple hardware generations.

  Most Nvidia customers, including cloud service providers, enterprises, and state-backed AI projects, have the balance sheets and financing structures to sign long-term infrastructure contracts independently, the company said. Its backing is aimed at frontier AI labs, which Nvidia characterized as having fast-rising revenue and customer demand but lacking the credit profile to commit to large, long-dated site agreements without support.

  ## Context

  Nvidia had previously been reported to be considering a financing backstop of as much as $250 billion, according to the Wall Street Journal and Bloomberg in July. Nvidia's shares fell 5 percent after those reports and have gained more than 19 percent in 2026.

  The Ohio campus is separate from the $500 billion Stargate initiative announced in 2025 by OpenAI, Nvidia, SoftBank, and partners including Oracle. Stargate targets 10 GW of data centre capacity across multiple sites, with half of that capacity under construction in the United Arab Emirates.

  Data centres at PORTS-Pike are scheduled to come online in phases between 2028 and 2030. If the Ohio arrangement expands to the full 8 GW, OpenAI's total planned Nvidia compute commitments through 2030 would reach 16 GW, valued by Nvidia at approximately $600 billion.
body_zh: |
  根據2026年8月17日發布的監管文件，OpenAI將租用位於俄亥俄州朴茨茅斯（Portsmouth）的PORTS-Pike科技園區，租期20年，背後有輝達（Nvidia）1,050億美元的融資承諾作為保障。該園區建於冷戰時期一座舊鈾濃縮廠舊址之上，初期運算容量為4.25吉瓦（GW），最終可擴展至8吉瓦，屆時將成為全球規模最大的單一AI資料中心設施。

  輝達這筆1,050億美元的擔保，是其迄今規模最大的融資承諾。此承諾涵蓋初期約4吉瓦階段部分的租約與電力義務，但不包括整個園區的全部費用，亦不涵蓋OpenAI的所有義務。容量預計在2028年至2030年間分階段上線，隨著資料中心陸續投入服務並開始支付租金，輝達的剩餘風險敞口將逐步降低。

  整個園區將完全採用輝達的繪圖處理器（GPU）、中央處理器（CPU）、網路設備及基礎架構軟體，輝達亦保留該場址作為其運算平台的專屬用地。

  ## 融資結構

  軟銀（SoftBank）及其子公司SB Energy擁有該園區，雙方將在當地能源基礎設施上共同投入逾40億美元，並承諾至少新建10吉瓦的發電設施為園區供電。輝達將另行向SB Energy投資15億美元。OpenAI則承諾撥出4,000萬美元「支持當地優先要務」。

  輝達直接反駁了外界對「循環融資」的疑慮——所謂循環融資，是指晶片供應商為自家客戶的基礎設施支出提供擔保，令外界質疑AI需求究竟是自我強化還是有機成長。輝達在8月17日的部落格文章中寫道：「OpenAI將負責繳付租金」，並將此交易定性為應用於半導體供應鏈管理的同一套管控紀律。OpenAI承擔租約義務；輝達的角色則是為特定承諾提供後盾，以確保一個預期能跨越多個硬體世代持續運作的場址。

  輝達亦主張，即使OpenAI退出，該場址仍能保值。輝達表示，由於PORTS-Pike的硬體與軟體均已標準化、可跨用戶通用，園區產能可轉讓給其客戶群中的雲端服務供應商、企業客戶、AI實驗室或新創公司。

  ## 場址經濟效益

  輝達將PORTS-Pike定位為設計用於反覆硬體升級的長壽命資產，每當新世代運算設備問世便可更新部署。每一個部署在該場址的世代，大約需要150萬顆輝達GPU，並可為輝達帶來1,500億至2,000億美元的營收。輝達預計，在20年的租期內，園區將支撐數個如此的週期。

  OpenAI現有及規劃中的輝達運算承諾，包括PORTS-Pike在內，合計至2030年約達12吉瓦。若俄亥俄州的交易擴展至完整的8吉瓦，這一數字可能上升至16吉瓦，輝達估計屆時至2030年前的運算支出約達6,000億美元。

  ## 規模

  一座核電廠約可發電1吉瓦。PORTS-Pike初期4.25吉瓦的容量，相當於約四座核電廠同時運轉。根據加州州政府資料，整個舊金山市在2024年的電力消耗僅略超過5吉瓦；完整的8吉瓦建設規模將比這一數字高出逾一半。

  ## 就業與社區

  OpenAI表示，這項計畫將在六年內創造3萬5,000個臨時建設工作崗位，以及2,500個長期操作職位。OpenAI與SB Energy均表示，能源與基礎設施費用將由業者自行承擔，不轉嫁給當地居民。

  這些承諾針對的是社會大眾對大型資料中心計畫日益高漲的反感情緒。根據蓋洛普（Gallup）今年3月發布的民調，逾七成美國人反對在住家附近興建資料中心；部分歸因於資料中心電力負載而攀升的電費，也正在中期選舉中引發廣泛爭議。OpenAI執行長山姆·奧特曼（Sam Altman）在聲明中表示，這將是「一個巨大的場址，擁有足夠的運算能力，能幫助數百萬人用AI做到我們今天只能開始想像的事情。」

  ## 輝達角色的轉變

  俄亥俄州這項交易，標誌著輝達在AI產業中的角色正進一步轉變——從晶片供應商，轉型為積極介入大規模AI系統所需實體要素融資與確保的企業，涉及範圍涵蓋土地取得、電力接取及資料中心廠房。輝達表示，將維持選擇性策略，專注於其所稱的「優質場址」——即持續需求足以支撐多個硬體世代的地點。

  輝達表示，絕大多數客戶，包括雲端服務供應商、企業用戶及國家支持的AI計畫，本身均具備足夠的資產負債表規模與融資結構，能夠獨立簽署長期基礎設施合約。輝達的背書對象，是其所描述的「前沿AI實驗室」——這類公司營收與客戶需求快速攀升，但信用狀況尚不足以在缺乏外部支持的情況下承擔大型、長期的場址協議。

  ## 背景

  根據《華爾街日報》與彭博社今年7月的報導，輝達此前曾被報導考慮提供高達2,500億美元的融資後盾。相關報導出爐後，輝達股價一度下跌5%，但2026年迄今已累計上漲逾19%。

  俄亥俄州園區與OpenAI、輝達、軟銀及甲骨文（Oracle）等合作夥伴於2025年宣布的5,000億美元「星際之門」（Stargate）計畫是兩個獨立的項目。星際之門目標是在多個地點合計建置10吉瓦的資料中心容量，其中半數已在阿拉伯聯合大公國動工興建。

  PORTS-Pike的資料中心預計在2028年至2030年間分階段上線。若俄亥俄州的計畫擴展至完整的8吉瓦，OpenAI至2030年的輝達運算總承諾將達16吉瓦，輝達估計其價值約為6,000億美元。
