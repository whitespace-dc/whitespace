body_en: |
  Malaysia's National Semiconductor Strategy (NSS) has attracted more than RM85 billion in approved investments through December 2025, Deputy Minister of Investment, Trade and Industry Sim Tze Tzin told parliament on Thursday, as nearly 70% of all manufacturing projects under the country's broader industrial overhaul had already been realized.

  Sim delivered the figures during the question-and-answer session in the Dewan Rakyat — Malaysia's lower house of parliament — in response to questions about the New Industrial Master Plan 2030 (NIMP 2030), the overarching industrial policy framework launched in September 2023 under which the NSS operates.

  ## Investment realization under NIMP 2030

  Across the full scope of NIMP 2030, authorities approved 3,847 manufacturing investment projects worth RM427.9 billion between September 2023 and March 2026, with the potential to create 302,058 new jobs. Of those, 2,688 projects — 69.9% by count — representing RM318.5 billion in value, had been realized between 2023 and December 2025, Sim said.

  A further 1,076 projects worth RM101.1 billion, representing 28% of approved investments, were in early stages of implementation, with activity spanning site planning, submission of building plans, business registration, and early-phase facility construction.

  Combined, realized and under-implementation projects account for 97.9% of all approved investment commitments. Only 83 projects, or 2.2%, remain unimplemented, which Sim attributed mainly to changes in investors' global business strategies.

  The approved manufacturing investments span the electrical and electronics (E&E), machinery and equipment, transport equipment, chemicals, and metal products sectors — all designated strategic industries under NIMP 2030.

  ## Talent development

  The NSS has developed 18,062 highly skilled workers toward a national target of 60,000 workers needed to support the semiconductor and artificial intelligence (AI) industries, Sim said.

  ## Smart factory expansion

  Seventy-four companies had received smart factory recognition as of May 31, 2026, spread across two programmes: 32 identified under the Smart Tech Up programme and 42 recognized under the Smart Factory Recognition Programme. The designation is awarded to manufacturing companies — including those in the automotive sector — that have integrated Industry 4.0 technologies and automation into their operations. A further 60 companies are expected to achieve the status by the end of 2026, which would bring the total to 134 recognized smart factories by December 2026.

  ## SME and mid-tier company support

  Thirty-five small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and mid-tier companies (MTCs) had received capital through the NIMP Strategic Co-Investment Fund (NIMP CoSIF) as of April 30, 2026, with total injections of RM63.2 million. The participating companies operate across the E&E, chemicals, pharmaceuticals, food processing, and information and communications technology (ICT) sectors.

  ## Implementation pace and outlook

  The figures indicate that Malaysia's industrial investment pipeline has moved substantially beyond the approval stage: within roughly two and a half years of NIMP 2030's launch, close to 98% of committed projects are either completed or in active implementation. The NSS semiconductor and AI workforce development target of 60,000 workers — against which 18,062 have been trained to date — remains the principal long-term benchmark against which the strategy will continue to be measured.

  The government expects to reach 134 recognized smart factories by December 2026.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞國家半導體策略（NSS）截至2025年12月已吸引逾850億令吉核准投資，投資、貿易暨工業部副部長沈志勤週四在國會表示，該國更廣泛工業改革框架下的整體製造業計畫，近七成已落地實現。

  沈志勤在眾議院（Dewan Rakyat）質詢時段就2030年新工業總藍圖（NIMP 2030）相關提問作出答覆。NIMP 2030為2023年9月啟動的整體工業政策框架，國家半導體策略即在此框架下運作。

  ## NIMP 2030投資實現進度

  就NIMP 2030全貌而言，當局自2023年9月至2026年3月共核准3,847項製造業投資計畫，總值4,279億令吉，預計可創造302,058個就業機會。其中2,688項計畫——以項目數計佔69.9%——總值3,185億令吉，已於2023年至2025年12月間付諸實現，沈志勤說。

  另有1,076項計畫，總值1,011億令吉，佔核准投資的28%，正處於實施初期階段，涵蓋廠址規劃、建築圖則送審、工商登記及設施初期興建等工作。

  已實現與實施中的計畫合計，佔所有核准投資承諾的97.9%。僅剩83項計畫（佔2.2%）尚未啟動，沈志勤將此主要歸因於投資者全球商業策略的調整。

  核准的製造業投資涵蓋電機電子（E&E）、機械與設備、運輸設備、化學品及金屬製品等領域，均為NIMP 2030指定的策略性產業。

  ## 人才培育

  沈志勤表示，國家半導體策略已培育18,062名高技術人才，朝向支援半導體與人工智慧（AI）產業所需60,000名人才的全國目標邁進。

  ## 智慧工廠擴展

  截至2026年5月31日，共有74家企業獲得智慧工廠認證，分布於兩項計畫之下：32家通過「智慧升級計畫」認定，42家獲「智慧工廠認證計畫」表揚。此認證頒予已將工業4.0技術與自動化整合入其營運的製造業者，包括汽車業者在內。預計另有60家企業可於2026年底前取得資格，屆時獲認證的智慧工廠總數將於2026年12月達到134家。

  ## 中小企業與中堅企業支援

  截至2026年4月30日，已有35家中小企業（SME）及中堅企業（MTC）透過「NIMP策略共同投資基金」（NIMP CoSIF）獲得資本挹注，總計注資6,320萬令吉。參與企業遍及電機電子、化學品、製藥、食品加工及資訊通訊技術（ICT）等領域。

  ## 推進速度與前景展望

  上述數據顯示，馬來西亞工業投資管線已大幅超越核准階段：NIMP 2030啟動約兩年半內，近98%的承諾計畫已完成或正積極推進。國家半導體策略在半導體與AI人力發展方面設定的60,000名人才目標——迄今已培訓18,062人——仍是衡量策略成效的主要長期指標。

  政府預期於2026年12月前達成134家認證智慧工廠的目標。
