body_en: |
  Four of Taiwan's major electromechanical contractors posted record profits and near-record revenues through 2026, with order backlogs stretching at least to 2029 as AI data center construction and Taiwan's national grid hardening program drove demand for high-voltage equipment and electrical installation work, Commercial Times reported.

  The four companies — Shihlin Electric (TSE: 1503), Chung-Hsing Electric (TSE: 1513), Alyac (TSE: 1514), and Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing (TSE: 1519) — recorded first-nine-month earnings-per-share and first-ten-month revenue gains across the board. Institutional investors cited by Commercial Times projected full-year revenue and profit records for all four.

  ## Order Backlogs

  All four contractors have begun booking orders for delivery in 2029 and 2030, with existing backlogs already running past 2029, the newspaper reported. Figures in New Taiwan dollars (NT$):

  - **Shihlin Electric:** backlog exceeds NT$60 billion (approximately US$1.9 billion)
  - **Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing:** market estimates place backlog at NT$60 billion to NT$70 billion (US$1.9 billion to US$2.2 billion)
  - **Chung-Hsing Electric:** combined backlog of gas-insulated switchgear (GIS — high-voltage switching equipment used in substations) orders and turnkey engineering contracts totaling NT$41.3 billion (US$1.3 billion)
  - **Alyac:** backlog exceeds NT$13 billion (US$406 million), with 2026 AI data center orders up roughly 80% year-on-year

  ## Financial Results

  Shihlin Electric's first-nine-month earnings per share reached NT$6.74 on a third-quarter figure of NT$1.70, setting a new annual earnings record ahead of year-end. Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing's first-nine-month EPS came to NT$10.48 — a same-period record — on a third-quarter figure of NT$3.96. Chung-Hsing Electric reported first-nine-month EPS of NT$7.11, with NT$2.61 in the third quarter. Alyac posted first-nine-month EPS of NT$2.44, a same-period record, on a third-quarter figure of NT$0.83.

  On revenue, Shihlin Electric's first-ten-month consolidated sales reached NT$33.16 billion (approximately US$1.04 billion), up 10.02% year-on-year. Chung-Hsing Electric's first-ten-month revenue was NT$23.98 billion, up 6.27%. Alyac posted NT$8.96 billion, up 8.35% and a same-period record. Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing's first-ten-month revenue came to NT$18.10 billion, down 2.49% year-on-year — the company attributed the shortfall to customer delivery scheduling — but still the second-highest figure for any equivalent period.

  ## Demand Drivers

  Three categories of orders are filling backlogs: Taiwan Power Company's (Taipower) resilient grid hardening plan, construction and capacity expansion at semiconductor fabrication plants, and AI data center (AIDC) projects in Taiwan and the United States.

  Institutional investors cited by Commercial Times described the global AIDC construction wave as opening a "golden decade" for the four contractors.

  ## US Political Risk

  A potential headwind emerged following the U.S. midterm elections. Several states that had encouraged AIDC construction — including Texas and Ohio — saw governorships shift to Democrats. The incoming governors signaled intentions to pause new AIDC development, prompting market concern about the contractors' exposure to U.S. projects.

  Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing, Alyac, and Chung-Hsing Electric said the political shift would have limited impact on their businesses.

  Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing General Manager Hsu Yi-teh said the company began receiving transformer orders from the Stargate AI infrastructure project in Texas in the fourth quarter of 2024, with that program alone generating NT$2 billion (approximately US$62.5 million) in orders. The company's cumulative AIDC orders total at least NT$25 billion (approximately US$781 million), and all projects have obtained local government building permits, he said.

  Hsu added that the seven major U.S. AIDC operators are constructing their own dedicated power infrastructure, that AIDC construction lead times are long, and that none of the recent political changes would affect Hua Cheng Electric Manufacturing's production and delivery schedule over the next three to four years. The company said it is continuing to book orders for 2029 and 2030 delivery.
body_zh: |
  《工商時報》報導，台灣四大機電承包商在2026年前三季繳出亮麗成績，獲利全數創下歷史新高，前十月營收亦幾乎全面改寫紀錄，受惠於AI資料中心建設熱潮及台灣國家電網韌性強化計畫強勁帶動，訂單積壓已延伸至2029年以後。法人機構並預期四家公司今年全年營收與獲利均可望雙創歷史新高。

  四家公司分別為士林電機（證交所代碼：1503）、中興電工（1513）、亞力電機（1514）及華城電機（1519），前三季每股盈餘（EPS）與前十月營收均呈現全面成長。《工商時報》引述法人預估，四家公司今年全年營收與獲利均可望刷新歷史紀錄。

  ## 訂單積壓

  四大承包商目前已開始承接2029年及2030年交貨的訂單，現有積壓能見度已超過2029年。各公司積壓訂單規模如下（單位：新台幣）：

  - **士林電機：** 積壓訂單逾600億元（約19億美元）
  - **華城電機：** 市場估計積壓訂單介於600億至700億元（約19億至22億美元）
  - **中興電工：** 氣體絕緣開關設備（GIS，即變電站使用之高壓開關設備）訂單與交鑰匙統包工程合約合計積壓413億元（約13億美元）
  - **亞力電機：** 積壓訂單逾130億元（約4.06億美元），2026年AI資料中心訂單年增約80%

  ## 財務表現

  士林電機前三季每股盈餘達6.74元，第三季單季貢獻1.70元，已在年底前提前創下年度EPS歷史新高。華城電機前三季EPS為10.48元，為同期歷史新高，第三季單季貢獻3.96元。中興電工前三季EPS為7.11元，第三季單季貢獻2.61元。亞力電機前三季EPS為2.44元，同創同期歷史新高，第三季單季貢獻0.83元。

  在營收方面，士林電機前十月合併營收達331.6億元（約10.4億美元），年增10.02%。中興電工前十月營收為239.8億元，年增6.27%。亞力電機為89.6億元，年增8.35%，並創同期歷史新高。華城電機前十月營收為181.0億元，年減2.49%，公司表示係因客戶端交貨排程調整所致，但仍為同期歷史次高。

  ## 需求驅動來源

  推動訂單積壓的需求來自三大領域：台灣電力公司（台電）電網韌性強化計畫、半導體晶圓廠的新建與擴產工程，以及台灣與美國的AI資料中心（AIDC）建設項目。

  《工商時報》引述法人表示，全球AI資料中心建設浪潮為四大承包商開啟了「黃金十年」。

  ## 美國政治風險

  美國期中選舉結果帶來一項潛在逆風。德州、俄亥俄州等原本積極推動AI資料中心建設的多個州，州長職位轉由民主黨人接掌，新任州長發出訊號，有意暫停新建AI資料中心的開發計畫，市場隨之憂慮相關承包商在美國項目上的曝險程度。

  對此，華城電機、亞力電機及中興電工均表示，此一政治轉向對其業務影響有限。

  華城電機總經理徐義德表示，公司自2024年第四季起開始承接德州Stargate AI基礎建設計畫的變壓器訂單，光是該案即已貢獻約20億元（約6,250萬美元）的訂單量，公司累計AI資料中心訂單至少達250億元（約7.81億美元），且所有項目均已取得地方政府核發的建照。

  徐義德進一步表示，美國七大AI資料中心業者均在自行興建專屬電力基礎設施，AI資料中心建設工期本就漫長，近期的政治變化對華城電機未來三至四年的生產及交貨排程不會造成任何影響。公司亦表示，目前持續承接2029年及2030年交貨的訂單。
