body_en: |
  Envision Energy has announced the commissioning of the Galaxy Campus in Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, a facility designed for 2 gigawatts (GW) of AI computing capacity that the company positions as one of the largest AI data center projects in China.

  The 120,000-square-meter campus is built to deliver one million petaFLOPS (PFLOPS, a standard measure of computing throughput equal to one quadrillion floating-point operations per second) and to support one million AI accelerators at full buildout, according to a company press release. Envision said the facility uses its AI Power System, which integrates renewable energy generation, transmission infrastructure, and energy storage into a unified platform.

  ## Mission Gobi and the 5 GW target

  The Galaxy Campus is the first flagship project under Envision's Mission Gobi initiative, which the company announced at the VivaTech conference in Paris in June 2026. Mission Gobi targets 5 GW of green AI computing capacity in desert and arid regions worldwide by 2030.

  Wind and solar resources supply the campus's power; wind is cited as the primary energy source and is supplemented by AI-focused energy management capabilities, Envision said.

  Ricky Zheng, General Manager of Envision's AI Data Center (AIDC) division, said the project addresses constraints becoming central to AI scaling. "The next frontier of AI is infrastructure, as AI models become larger and more compute-intensive, the limiting factors are increasing power availability, network performance and energy efficiency," Zheng said. "Envision Galaxy Campus combines energy, networking and physical infrastructure at unprecedented scale, enabling hyperscalers, technology companies and AI innovators to deploy and scale the next generation of AI workloads."

  ## China's AI infrastructure zones

  Inner Mongolia is one of three regions where China has established AI-specialized zones, alongside Beijing and Hebei, according to DC market insights cited by W.Media. Those regions attract AI infrastructure investment partly through tax benefits, favorable regulations, and access to renewable energy resources. Regional governments are coordinating expansion with local innovation ecosystems to enable faster deployment and greater scalability, that report said.

  The competitive push to build AI-supporting data center capacity in China forms the broader context for the Galaxy Campus commissioning, according to Kontan. Envision has framed the project as part of a strategy to strengthen sustainable AI infrastructure.

  ## What comes next

  The Mission Gobi initiative, with the Galaxy Campus as its first project to reach commissioning, targets 5 GW of green AI computing capacity in desert and arid regions worldwide by 2030.

  遠景能源宣布，位於內蒙古烏蘭察布的「銀河園區」（Galaxy Campus）正式投入運營。這座以2 GW AI算力為設計目標的設施，被該公司定位為中國規模最大的AI資料中心專案之一。

  根據公司發布的新聞稿，這座佔地12萬平方公尺的園區，完全建成後算力可達100萬 PFLOPS（petaFLOPS，即每秒千兆次浮點運算，為衡量運算吞吐量的標準單位），並可支援100萬顆AI加速器。遠景能源表示，該設施採用其自主研發的「AI電力系統」，將再生能源發電、輸電基礎設施與儲能整合為一體化平台。

  ## 「戈壁任務」與5 GW目標

  銀河園區是遠景能源「戈壁任務」（Mission Gobi）計畫的第一個旗艦專案。「戈壁任務」於2026年6月在巴黎VivaTech科技大會上正式發布，目標是在2030年前，於全球沙漠與乾旱地區部署5 GW的綠色AI算力。

  園區的電力來源為風能與太陽能，遠景能源指出風能為主要供電來源，並輔以專為AI設計的能源管理能力。

  遠景能源AI資料中心（AIDC）事業部總經理鄭重表示，此專案正面對的是AI擴展中日趨核心的制約因素。「AI的下一個前沿在於基礎設施。隨著AI模型規模持續擴大、對算力的需求愈來愈高，電力供應、網路效能與能源效率將成為關鍵瓶頸，」鄭重說道。「遠景銀河園區以前所未有的規模整合能源、網路與實體基礎設施，使超大規模雲端業者、科技公司與AI創新者能夠部署並擴展下一代AI工作負載。」

  ## 中國AI基礎設施專區

  根據W.Media引述的資料中心市場資訊，內蒙古是中國設立AI專業化園區的三大區域之一，另外兩處為北京與河北。這些地區憑藉稅收優惠、有利的法規環境以及豐富的再生能源資源，吸引大量AI基礎設施投資。報告指出，各地方政府正協調推動與在地創新生態系統的融合，以加快部署速度並提升可擴展性。

  據Kontan報導，中國積極構建支撐AI的資料中心容量，形成了銀河園區啟用的更宏觀背景。遠景能源將此專案定位為強化永續AI基礎設施戰略的一環。

  ## 後續展望

  「戈壁任務」計畫以銀河園區作為首個正式啟用的專案，目標是在2030年前，於全球沙漠與乾旱地區建成5 GW的綠色AI算力。
