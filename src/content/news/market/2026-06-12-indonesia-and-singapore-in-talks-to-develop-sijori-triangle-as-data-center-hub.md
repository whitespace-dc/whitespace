body_en: |
  Indonesia and Singapore are in discussions to develop the Sijori corridor — the cross-border zone encompassing Singapore, Johor in Malaysia, and Indonesia's Riau Islands province — into an international data center hub, Indonesia's Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto said after the 16th Ministerial Meeting of the two countries' bilateral economic working groups on June 9, 2026.

  The meeting, held in Jakarta, brought together Airlangga and Singapore's Deputy Prime Minister Gan Kim Yong to review progress across six bilateral working groups: the Batam-Bintan-Karimun (BBK) free trade zone, investment, labor, transportation, agriculture, and tourism. The Sijori data center proposal emerged alongside agreements on BBK zone expansion, clean energy development, and talent mobility.

  ## Sijori as a data center hub

  Airlangga said both countries are examining how to "synergize" Singapore, Johor, and Riau as a data center hub, describing the initiative as strategic because the global community regards the digital sector as the next driver of economic growth. He said data center investment carries strong prospects specifically because it is not disrupted by conflict, and that investment trends in data centers are rising across Europe and Southeast Asia.

  As part of the initiative, Indonesia is pushing to expand Nongsa Digital Park — a technology-focused Special Economic Zone (KEK, or Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus) in Batam, Riau Islands — to 1,000 hectares. Airlangga linked this expansion directly to the Sijori hub proposal, saying the park would continue to develop as the broader concept advances.

  Singapore's Deputy PM Gan separately stated that both countries would work together to develop the BBK zone as a digital hub.

  ## BBK investment growth

  Investment in the BBK free trade zone rose to US$5.71 billion in 2025, up from US$4.61 billion in 2024, Airlangga said — an increase of more than US$1.1 billion in a single year. The Batam FTZ has also expanded its geographic footprint from 8 to 22 islands.

  Kendal Industrial Park (KEK Kendal), a joint Indonesia-Singapore special economic zone in Central Java, will also be expanded to 1,000 hectares.

  ## Clean energy projects

  Both governments will promote non-fossil energy investment within the BBK zone. A 200-megawatt (MW) solar power plant — enough to power a sizeable industrial zone — paired with an 80 megawatt-hour (MWh) energy storage facility is under development in Central Sulawesi as a joint venture between Singapore's Sembcorp and Indonesian firm PT Sumber Energi Surya Nusantara (SESNA). Gan said the project is currently under construction and targeted for completion in 2027.

  Singapore and Indonesia are also developing a pilot project to convert biogas into bioethanol in North Sumatra, through a biofuel collaboration between CRecTech and state energy company Pertamina.

  ## Broader bilateral agenda

  On digital talent, both countries are running the Tech:X and Youth Mobility Programme to strengthen connectivity between their digital workforces. In June 2026, 13 young Indonesian farmers also began agricultural technology training in Singapore as part of the agriculture working group.

  On aviation, Scoot launched new direct routes connecting Singapore to Belitung and Pontianak in Indonesia. Both governments are additionally pursuing a service cooperation arrangement between state carrier Garuda Indonesia and Singapore Airlines.

  On tourism, 2.44 million Indonesians visit Singapore each year, while 1.5 million Singaporeans travel to Indonesia annually, according to figures cited at the meeting.

  ## Significance

  The Sijori hub proposal links Indonesia's expanding BBK free trade zone — whose investment base has grown sharply in the past year — with Singapore through a shared digital infrastructure ambition. Airlangga framed the initiative as part of a global trend in which the digital sector, including data centers, is increasingly seen as a primary engine of future growth.

  Gan reaffirmed Singapore's commitment to being "a valuable and reliable partner" to Indonesia and said both governments were working to ensure supply chain resilience so that trade flows can continue under current global conditions.

  The 200 MW Sembcorp-SESNA solar facility in Central Sulawesi is targeted for completion in 2027.
body_zh: |
  印尼與新加坡正就將「Sijori走廊」——涵蓋新加坡、馬來西亞柔佛州及印尼廖內群島省的跨境地帶——打造為國際資料中心樞紐一事展開討論。印尼統籌經濟事務部長艾爾朗加·哈爾塔托（Airlangga Hartarto）於2026年6月9日兩國雙邊經濟工作組第16屆部長級會議後發表上述聲明。

  此次會議在雅加達召開，艾爾朗加與新加坡副總理顏金勇（Gan Kim Yong）共同出席，就六個雙邊工作組的進展進行檢視，範疇涵蓋峇淡—賓坦—卡里蒙（BBK）自由貿易區、投資、勞工、交通、農業及觀光。Sijori資料中心提案與BBK區擴張、潔淨能源開發及人才流動等協議一同浮出檯面。

  ## Sijori 作為資料中心樞紐

  艾爾朗加表示，兩國正研究如何「協同整合」新加坡、柔佛與廖內，共同打造資料中心樞紐，並將此倡議定位為具有戰略意義的舉措，原因在於國際社會普遍將數位產業視為下一波經濟成長的主要驅動力。他指出，資料中心投資具備強勁前景，特別是因為此類投資不受地緣衝突干擾，而歐洲與東南亞的資料中心投資趨勢均持續上揚。

  作為此倡議的一部分，印尼積極推動將農薩數位園區（Nongsa Digital Park）擴大至1,000公頃。農薩數位園區位於廖內群島峇淡島，是以科技為主軸的經濟特區（KEK，即Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus）。艾爾朗加將此擴張計畫直接與Sijori樞紐提案相連結，表示隨著整體構想持續推進，園區將同步發展。

  新加坡副總理顏金勇另行表示，兩國將攜手將BBK區建設為數位樞紐。

  ## BBK 投資成長

  艾爾朗加表示，BBK自由貿易區的投資額在2025年攀升至57.1億美元，高於2024年的46.1億美元，單年增幅逾11億美元。峇淡自貿區的地理覆蓋範圍也從8座島嶼擴展至22座。

  印尼與新加坡在爪哇中部共同設立的經濟特區肯達爾工業園區（KEK Kendal）亦將擴張至1,000公頃。

  ## 潔淨能源計畫

  兩國政府將積極推動BBK區內的非化石能源投資。一座裝置容量達200百萬瓦（MW）的太陽能電廠——足以供應一座相當規模工業區所需的電力——搭配80百萬瓦時（MWh）儲能設施，目前正由新加坡勝科工業（Sembcorp）與印尼企業PT Sumber Energi Surya Nusantara（SESNA）在蘇拉威西中部以合資方式推進開發。顏金勇表示，該計畫目前已進入施工階段，預計於2027年竣工。

  新加坡與印尼也正在北蘇門答臘推動一項試驗計畫，由CRecTech與國營能源公司Pertamina合作，將沼氣轉化為生物乙醇，共同推進生質燃料開發。

  ## 更廣泛的雙邊議程

  在數位人才方面，兩國共同推動「Tech:X」及「青年流動計畫」（Youth Mobility Programme），致力強化雙方數位人才的連結。2026年6月，13名印尼青年農民也赴新加坡接受農業科技培訓，此為農業工作組的合作成果。

  在航空方面，酷航（Scoot）新開闢了連接新加坡與印尼勿里洞（Belitung）及坤甸（Pontianak）的直飛航線。兩國政府另正積極推動國營航空嘉魯達印尼航空（Garuda Indonesia）與新加坡航空之間的服務合作安排。

  在觀光方面，根據會議引用的數據，每年有244萬名印尼人赴新加坡旅遊，而每年赴印尼旅遊的新加坡人則達150萬人次。

  ## 重要意義

  Sijori樞紐提案將印尼持續擴張的BBK自由貿易區——其投資規模在過去一年大幅成長——與新加坡透過共同的數位基礎建設願景緊密相連。艾爾朗加將此倡議定位為全球趨勢的一環，在此趨勢下，數位產業（包含資料中心）正日益被視為未來成長的主要引擎。

  顏金勇重申新加坡致力成為印尼「重要且可靠夥伴」的承諾，並表示兩國政府正積極確保供應鏈韌性，使貿易流通在當前全球情勢下得以持續運作。
