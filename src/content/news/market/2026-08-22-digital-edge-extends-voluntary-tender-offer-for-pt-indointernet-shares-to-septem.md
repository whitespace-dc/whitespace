body_en: |
  Digital Edge (Hong Kong) Ltd has extended the third voluntary tender offer period for shares of PT Indointernet Tbk, the Indonesian internet infrastructure company listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange under the ticker EDGE, as part of a plan to take the company private.

  According to a disclosure filed by the company on Wednesday, August 19, the extended tender period will run from August 24, 2026 at 08:30 Western Indonesia Time (WIB) through September 22, 2026 at 16:00 WIB.

  ## Extension details

  The disclosure identified the offer as the third extension of the voluntary tender period, indicating that previous acceptance windows have elapsed without the transaction being completed. Digital Edge (Hong Kong) Ltd is described as the controlling shareholder — the party initiating the tender.

  ## Go-private context

  The voluntary tender offer is connected to a plan to delist EDGE from the Indonesia Stock Exchange, a process commonly referred to as going private. PT Indointernet Tbk previously obtained approval from its General Meeting of Shareholders — known in Indonesia as a Rapat Umum Pemegang Saham, or RUPS — for the delisting, according to a related disclosure referenced in the source reporting.

  The extension to September 22 gives remaining public shareholders additional time to tender their shares under the offer's terms. The go-private process remains ongoing as of the new deadline.
