body_en: |
  Japan's largest power generator, JERA, has entered talks with Anthropic, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, and Google to build data centres near its power plants in the Tokyo area, the Nikkei newspaper reported on November 27.

  JERA Global chief executive and chairman Yukio Kani disclosed the discussions in a recent interview cited by Nikkei. The five companies named span the leading US cloud platforms and two of the most prominent AI model developers, suggesting JERA is marketing its generation assets to a broad range of potential anchor tenants.

  ## The October MOU with Dell and RHAELM

  The talks follow a partnership JERA announced in October with Dell Technologies and RHAELM, a UK-based AI infrastructure developer. The three parties signed a memorandum of understanding — a non-binding agreement to work toward a formal deal — to develop a standardised model for building AI infrastructure on a national scale in Japan.

  The partnership's initial focus is a US$15 billion hyperscale data centre in Chiba, a prefecture that borders Tokyo to the east. Hyperscale data centres are large-scale facilities, typically operated by or built for major cloud and technology companies, that house tens of thousands of servers and require substantial, reliable power supply.

  ## RHAELM's role and hyperscaler talks

  A JERA spokesperson told Reuters that RHAELM is responsible for marketing the project, and that JERA had been informed RHAELM is currently in talks with multiple hyperscalers. RHAELM was not immediately available to comment when contacted by Reuters.

  None of the five US technology companies — Anthropic, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, or Google — responded to Reuters' requests for comment.

  ## What the talks signal

  The proximity to power plants is central to the proposition. Data centres are among the most energy-intensive facilities in the modern economy, and siting them adjacent to generation capacity can reduce transmission costs and improve supply reliability. For JERA, potential long-term offtake from large technology tenants represents a stable demand base for its generation assets.

  The breadth of the discussions — spanning cloud infrastructure giants and AI model developers — indicates the scale of interest in Japan as a location for AI-linked data centre investment. The Chiba project, at US$15 billion, would rank among the largest single data centre commitments announced in the Asia-Pacific region.

  According to the JERA spokesperson, RHAELM is continuing to hold talks with multiple hyperscalers as it seeks to fill the planned facility.
body_zh: |
  日本最大發電商JERA正與Anthropic、Meta、亞馬遜、微軟及Google洽談，計劃在東京周邊的發電廠附近興建資料中心，日經新聞11月27日報導。

  JERA Global執行長暨董事長可尼幸雄（Yukio Kani）在日經引述的一篇近期專訪中，親自披露了上述洽談情況。此番點名的五家業者，橫跨美國頂尖雲端平台與兩家最具代表性的AI模型開發商，顯示JERA正積極向廣泛的潛在主力租戶行銷其發電資產。

  ## 10月與戴爾及RHAELM簽訂的合作備忘錄

  此次洽談，源於JERA今年10月宣布與戴爾科技（Dell Technologies）及英國AI基礎設施開發商RHAELM建立的合作關係。三方簽署了一份備忘錄——一份旨在推動正式協議的非約束性合作意向書——以期在日本全國建立標準化的AI基礎設施建設模式。

  此合作案的初期重心，是在毗鄰東京東側的千葉縣興建一座造價150億美元的超大規模資料中心。所謂超大規模資料中心，係指通常由主要雲端及科技公司自行營運或為其量身興建的大型設施，可容納數萬台伺服器，且需要充裕而穩定的電力供應。

  ## RHAELM的角色與超大規模業者洽談進展

  JERA發言人告訴路透社，RHAELM負責主導此案的市場推廣工作，且JERA已獲告知RHAELM目前正與多家超大規模業者進行洽談。路透社聯繫RHAELM時，對方未即時回應置評請求。

  Anthropics、Meta、亞馬遜、微軟及Google等五家美國科技公司，均未回應路透社的置評請求。

  ## 此輪洽談釋出的訊號

  毗鄰發電廠，正是此案的核心賣點。資料中心是現代經濟中能源消耗最密集的設施之一，將其選址於發電設施旁，可降低輸電成本、提升供電穩定性。對JERA而言，來自大型科技租戶的潛在長期購電協議，可為其發電資產帶來穩定的需求基礎。

  此輪洽談涵蓋雲端基礎設施巨頭與AI模型開發商，規模之廣充分顯示業界對日本作為AI關聯資料中心投資地點的高度興趣。千葉縣的這座超大規模資料中心投資規模達150億美元，將躋身亞太地區迄今宣布的單一資料中心最大投資案之列。

  據JERA發言人表示，RHAELM仍持續與多家超大規模業者進行洽談，致力填滿規劃中設施的容量需求。
