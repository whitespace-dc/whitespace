body_en: |
  Indonesia's Danantara Investment Management (DIM) will announce a package of strategic investments in data centers and artificial intelligence at the New Asia Forum (NAF) in Bali on December 3–4, 2026, Managing Director of Investment Stefanus Ade Hadiwidjaja said at a media briefing in Jakarta on Thursday, October 22.

  Stefanus described the announcements as intended to demonstrate that large-scale investment in Indonesia's digital infrastructure is achievable. "We have several projects, including in data centers, that will be announced at the New Asia Forum. We hope this will become one example of large-scale investment entering Indonesia," he said, speaking at the Coffee Morning session held at Wisma Danantara Indonesia.

  ## Investment criteria and national benefit

  DIM, the investment management arm of the state holding vehicle Danantara, frames its data center and AI commitments around a dual mandate: financial return and long-term national economic benefit. Stefanus said qualifying investments must deliver technology transfer, improve domestic industry capability, and develop Indonesian human resources — not simply generate yield for the fund.

  "Globally, AI is developing rapidly. In every investment we are always prudent and careful. But on the other hand, we also cannot fall behind on the very large opportunities in AI," he said.

  ## Energy and water requirements

  DIM is applying a "Bring Your Own Energy" (BYOE) requirement for large-scale data center projects it backs. Under that standard, a project developer must secure adequate independent energy supply rather than drawing on the national grid that serves households and other industries. Stefanus said large data centers require meticulous planning around both energy and water availability to avoid competing with public and industrial needs.

  On water, DIM is evaluating closed-loop cooling technology that recirculates water internally, reducing net consumption, as well as water treatment and desalination systems as potential supply options.

  Additional factors in DIM's investment assessment include carbon emissions, electrical grid readiness, data security, and broader environmental impact, Stefanus said.

  ## Scope of DIM's broader portfolio

  Beyond digital infrastructure and AI, Stefanus outlined active DIM investments in waste management, hajj and umrah (Islamic pilgrimage) services, and the protein industry, indicating the fund is deploying capital across multiple strategic sectors simultaneously.

  ## Significance

  The planned December disclosures will be the first public detailing of DIM's data center commitments and will set a baseline for how Indonesia's largest state investment vehicle intends to engage with the global build-out of AI infrastructure. The BYOE requirement signals that DIM intends to condition large digital-infrastructure deals on energy self-sufficiency, a condition that could shape how prospective partners structure proposals for the Indonesian market.

  Full deal terms and project names are scheduled to be disclosed at the New Asia Forum in Bali on December 3–4, 2026.
body_zh: |
  印尼達南塔拉投資管理公司（Danantara Investment Management，以下簡稱DIM）將於2026年12月3至4日在峇里島舉行的新亞洲論壇（New Asia Forum，NAF）上，宣布一批涵蓋資料中心與人工智慧領域的策略性投資案。該公司投資業務常務董事史蒂法努斯・阿德・哈迪維加亞（Stefanus Ade Hadiwidjaja）於10月22日（星期四）在雅加達舉行的媒體說明會上透露了上述消息。

  史蒂法努斯在達南塔拉印尼大廈（Wisma Danantara Indonesia）舉辦的「咖啡早晨」（Coffee Morning）座談活動中表示，此次宣布旨在展示外界對印尼數位基礎設施進行大規模投資的可行性。「我們有幾個項目，包括資料中心方面的項目，將於新亞洲論壇上正式公布。我們希望這能成為大規模投資進入印尼的一個示範案例，」他說。

  ## 投資標準與國家利益

  DIM是印尼國家控股平台達南塔拉旗下的投資管理部門，其資料中心與AI投資方針兼顧雙重使命：財務回報，以及國家的長遠經濟利益。史蒂法努斯指出，符合條件的投資案必須帶動技術移轉、提升國內產業能力，並培育印尼本地人力資源，而非僅為基金創造收益。

  「全球AI發展迅速。在每一筆投資上，我們始終審慎以待。但另一方面，我們也不能在AI領域的龐大商機面前裹足不前，」他說。

  ## 能源與用水需求

  DIM對其所支持的大規模資料中心項目採行「自備能源」（Bring Your Own Energy，BYOE）要求。依此標準，項目開發商必須自行確保充足的獨立能源供應，而非仰賴服務一般家庭與其他產業的國家電網。史蒂法努斯表示，大型資料中心須對能源與水資源的可用性進行周詳規劃，以避免與公共及工業需求形成競爭。

  在用水方面，DIM正在評估閉迴路冷卻技術——該技術在系統內部循環利用冷卻水，可降低整體耗水量——以及水處理與海水淡化系統，作為潛在的供水解決方案。

  此外，DIM在評估投資案時，亦將碳排放、電網整備程度、資料安全，以及更廣泛的環境影響納入考量，史蒂法努斯說。

  ## DIM整體投資組合規模

  除數位基礎設施與AI之外，史蒂法努斯還概述了DIM目前在廢棄物管理、哈吉與烏姆拉（伊斯蘭朝聖）服務，以及蛋白質產業等領域的積極投資布局，顯示該基金正同步在多個策略性產業部署資本。

  ## 重要意義

  預計於十二月舉行的此次公開宣布，將是DIM首次完整對外揭露其資料中心投資承諾，也將為印尼規模最大的國家投資機構如何參與全球AI基礎設施建設浪潮，立下最初的基準。BYOE要求則釋出明確訊號：DIM將以能源自給作為大型數位基礎設施交易的先決條件，此一條件可能進一步左右潛在合作夥伴在擬定進入印尼市場方案時的架構安排。

  完整交易條款與項目名稱，預計將於2026年12月3至4日在峇里島新亞洲論壇上正式公布。
