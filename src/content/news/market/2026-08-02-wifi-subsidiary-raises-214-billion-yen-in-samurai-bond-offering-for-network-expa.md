body_en: |
  PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem (IJE), a subsidiary of Indonesian listed telecommunications company PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (ticker: WIFI), issued 21.4 billion Japanese yen — approximately equivalent to the yen face value — in Samurai Bonds on July 24, 2026, tapping Japan's capital markets to finance fiber broadband infrastructure expansion and refinance existing debt.

  Samurai Bonds are yen-denominated bonds issued in Japan by non-Japanese entities, giving foreign borrowers access to Japan's institutional investor base while raising funds in yen.

  The offering was structured in two tranches. The first, and larger, series totals 19.4 billion yen with a three-year tenor and a coupon of 3.40 percent per annum. The second series totals 2 billion yen with a five-year tenor and carries a coupon of 3.92 percent per annum. Both tranches received a BBB+ credit rating from Japan Credit Rating Agency (JCR).

  IJE disclosed the transaction to Indonesia's Financial Services Authority (OJK) and the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX).

  ## Use of Proceeds

  The company said proceeds will be allocated across several purposes:

  - Capital expenditure for the development, expansion, and maintenance of Fiber to the Home (FTTH) networks and supporting telecommunications infrastructure
  - Working capital requirements
  - Refinancing or repayment of existing debt
  - General corporate purposes in accordance with applicable regulations

  FTTH refers to fiber-optic connections that run directly to individual homes or buildings, the dominant technology for delivering high-speed fixed broadband.

  ## Funding Structure and Strategic Rationale

  IJE's management said the bond issuance is expected to produce a positive effect on the financial condition and business development of the WIFI group by providing a competitive long-term funding source and diversifying the company's access to international capital markets, according to the regulatory disclosure.

  The company added that proceeds will support the acceleration of telecommunications infrastructure and digital network construction, which it said would strengthen operational capacity and support the achievement of the group's long-term growth strategy.

  IJE confirmed the transaction complied with all regulatory requirements in the relevant jurisdiction and said the corporate action would not cause any material adverse impact on the company's operations, legal standing, financial condition, or business continuity.

  ## Significance

  The Samurai Bond issuance represents IJE's entry into international debt capital markets, shifting part of the company's funding base away from domestic Indonesian sources. For a telecommunications infrastructure operator focused on fixed broadband rollout, accessing yen-denominated financing at investment-grade terms diversifies refinancing risk and lengthens the maturity profile of its liabilities. The BBB+ rating from JCR, the highest rating tier below the AA categories, reflects the agency's assessment of the company as investment grade.

  The five-year tranche extends IJE's debt maturity out to 2031, while the three-year tranche matures in 2029, providing staggered repayment obligations as the company deploys capital into network infrastructure.

  ## WIFI子公司發行214億日圓武士債券　籌資擴建光纖網路

  PT Integrasi Jaringan Ekosistem（IJE）為印尼上市電信公司PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk（股票代碼：WIFI）旗下子公司，於2026年7月24日在日本資本市場發行總計214億日圓的武士債券，以此進軍日本資本市場，籌措資金用於光纖寬頻基礎設施擴建及現有債務再融資。

  武士債券是指由非日本機構在日本境內發行的日圓計價債券，讓外國借款人得以進入日本機構投資人市場，並以日圓形式籌集資金。

  本次發行分為兩個系列。第一系列規模較大，總額為194億日圓，期限三年，票面利率為年息3.40％。第二系列總額為20億日圓，期限五年，票面利率為年息3.92％。兩個系列均獲得日本信用評等機構（JCR）授予BBB+信用評等。

  IJE已就本次交易向印尼金融服務監管局（OJK）及印尼證券交易所（IDX）進行資訊揭露。

  ## 資金用途

  該公司表示，所得款項將分配用於以下幾項目的：

  - 用於光纖到府（FTTH）網路及相關電信基礎設施之開發、擴建與維護的資本支出
  - 日常營運資金需求
  - 現有債務的再融資或償還
  - 符合相關法規規定的一般企業用途

  光纖到府（FTTH）是指將光纖線路直接接入個別住宅或建築物的技術，是目前提供高速固網寬頻的主流技術。

  ## 融資架構與策略考量

  IJE管理層在法規揭露文件中表示，此次債券發行預計將為WIFI集團的財務狀況與業務發展帶來正面效益，原因在於此舉可提供具競爭力的長期融資來源，並使公司進入國際資本市場的管道更加多元。

  公司並補充說明，所得款項將支持電信基礎設施及數位網路建設的加速推進，有助於強化營運能力，並支撐集團長期成長策略目標的實現。

  IJE確認本次交易符合相關司法管轄區的所有監管要求，並表示此次企業行動不會對公司的營運、法律地位、財務狀況或業務持續性造成任何重大不利影響。

  ## 市場意義

  本次武士債券發行標誌著IJE正式踏入國際債券資本市場，將公司部分融資來源從印尼國內轉移至海外。對於一家專注固網寬頻建設的電信基礎設施業者而言，以投資等級條件取得日圓計價融資，不僅有助於分散再融資風險，也拉長了負債的到期日結構。JCR授予的BBB+評等為AA級以下的最高評等區間，反映出評等機構對該公司投資等級信用水準的認可。

  五年期系列債券將使IJE的債務到期日延伸至2031年，三年期系列則於2029年到期，形成錯開的還款時程，為公司持續投入網路基礎設施建設提供充裕的資金部署空間。
