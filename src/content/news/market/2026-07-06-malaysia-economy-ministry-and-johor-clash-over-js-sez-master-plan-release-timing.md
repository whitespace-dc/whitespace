body_en: |
  Malaysia's Economy Ministry and the Johor state government publicly split on July 2 over when to release the master plan for the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ), a cross-border development corridor that both governments say is central to the region's economic future.

  Johor Menteri Besar (Chief Minister) Datuk Onn Hafiz Ghazi called on the federal government in Putrajaya to release the plan immediately, saying the document had been postponed multiple times and that the uncertainty was weighing on investor confidence. He said the master plan is needed to give investors, developers and planners clarity on incentives, investment rules and land-use decisions. Onn Hafiz also argued the plan does not require Singapore's formal approval because input from both countries has already been incorporated into the document.

  The Economy Ministry rejected the framing. In a statement, the ministry said the master plan is being finalized to ensure policy alignment, implementation readiness and effective Malaysia-Singapore cooperation. It said the JS-SEZ has not been delayed and described implementation as already ongoing across investment facilitation, infrastructure development, talent development and cross-border connectivity.

  ## Investment figures cited to counter delay narrative

  Economy Minister Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir cited a series of investment metrics to support the ministry's position. Approved investments in the JS-SEZ reached RM76.98 billion in 2025. In the first quarter of 2026 alone, RM5.49 billion in investments were approved. From January to May 2026, RM74.12 billion in potential investments were identified.

  The ministry also pointed to ongoing preparations for the Johor-Singapore Rapid Transit System (RTS), a rail link intended to ease cross-border movement, as evidence that the broader JS-SEZ agenda is advancing.

  ## What the JS-SEZ is

  The JS-SEZ was formally established in January 2025 as a cross-border economic zone spanning key areas of the southern Malaysian state of Johor, including Johor Bahru — the city directly across the Johor Strait from Singapore — as well as Iskandar Puteri, Pasir Gudang, Sedenak and Desaru. The zone is designed to attract manufacturing, logistics and technology investment by combining Malaysia's lower land and labor costs with proximity to Singapore's financial and logistics infrastructure.

  ## Election backdrop

  The public dispute over the master plan's release comes nine days before Johor holds state elections. All 56 state seats are contested on July 11, 2026, with the state's ruling Barisan Nasional coalition facing Pakatan Harapan across every constituency. The master plan's status and investor confidence in the JS-SEZ are consequential issues for both camps ahead of polling day.

  ## What it means

  The public disagreement between a state government and the federal ministry overseeing a flagship cross-border investment zone is unusual and underscores the governance complexity of a project that requires coordination between two national governments, multiple Malaysian ministries and a state administration. Investors and project developers seeking certainty on incentive structures and land-use rules are caught between a state demanding faster disclosure and a federal ministry that says the document is not yet ready for release.

  The ministry has not provided a date for when the master plan will be published.

  馬來西亞經濟部與柔佛州政府於7月2日就柔佛—新加坡特別經濟區（JS-SEZ）總藍圖的發布時機公開決裂。這條跨境發展走廊被兩國政府視為區域經濟未來的核心所在。

  柔佛州務大臣拿督翁哈菲茲·加濟（Datuk Onn Hafiz Ghazi）呼籲位於布城（Putrajaya）的聯邦政府立即公開總藍圖，稱該文件已多次遭到推遲，這種不確定性正在打擊投資者信心。他表示，總藍圖能讓投資者、開發商及規劃人員在激勵措施、投資法規與土地使用決策上獲得明確指引。翁哈菲茲亦主張，該計畫無需新加坡的正式批准，因為兩國的意見已全數納入文件之中。

  經濟部駁斥了這一說法。部方發表聲明指出，總藍圖正在進行最後定稿，以確保政策方向一致、執行層面準備充分，並促進馬新雙方有效合作。部方表示JS-SEZ並未延誤，並指出推動工作已在投資促進、基礎設施建設、人才培育及跨境互通連結等面向全面展開。

  ## 引述投資數據反駁延誤說法

  經濟部長阿克馬爾·納斯魯拉·莫哈末·納西爾（Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir）引述一系列投資數據，為部方立場佐證。2025年，JS-SEZ的已核准投資額達769.8億令吉；僅2026年第一季，便有54.9億令吉的投資獲批；2026年1月至5月間，已篩選出的潛在投資額更達741.2億令吉。

  部方亦指出，柔佛—新加坡捷運系統（RTS）的籌備工作正持續推進，這條旨在便利跨境人員往來的鐵路連接線，正是JS-SEZ整體推動議程穩步前行的具體佐證。

  ## JS-SEZ簡介

  JS-SEZ於2025年1月正式成立，是橫跨馬來西亞南部柔佛州多個關鍵地區的跨境經濟區，涵蓋柔佛巴魯（Johor Bahru）——這座城市隔著柔佛海峽與新加坡相望——以及依斯干達布特拉（Iskandar Puteri）、巴西古當（Pasir Gudang）、士達納（Sedenak）與德沙魯（Desaru）。該經濟區的設計宗旨，在於結合馬來西亞較為低廉的土地與勞動力成本，以及毗鄰新加坡金融與物流基礎設施的地理優勢，藉此吸引製造業、物流業及科技業投資。

  ## 選舉背景

  總藍圖發布之爭，恰在柔佛州議會選舉前九天浮上檯面。56個州議席全數將於2026年7月11日進行角逐，執政的國民陣線（Barisan Nasional，簡稱國陣）聯盟將在每個選區迎戰希望聯盟（Pakatan Harapan，簡稱希盟）。總藍圖的進度與投資者對JS-SEZ的信心，在投票日前夕，對兩大陣營而言都是舉足輕重的議題。

  ## 影響與意義

  州政府與監管這一旗艦跨境投資區的聯邦部會之間公開出現齟齬，實屬罕見，凸顯了這個需要兩國政府、多個馬來西亞部會與州政府協調配合的計畫，在治理層面上的高度複雜性。尋求激勵結構與土地使用規範明確指引的投資者和專案開發商，夾在要求加速公開的州政府與表示文件尚未就緒的聯邦部會之間，處境左右為難。

  部方迄今未提供總藍圖的發布日期。
