body_en: |
  Seth Klarman, who has managed Boston-based hedge fund Baupost Group for 44 years with only five losing years across that span, said in a CNBC interview that his firm holds no positions in OpenAI, Anthropic, or other large language model companies, warning that current AI valuations show clear bubble characteristics and that investors are making excessively optimistic assumptions about an unknowable future.

  Klarman, known as the "Boston Buffett," said OpenAI and Anthropic carry not only extreme valuations but also burn cash continuously at a high rate, leaving them vulnerable to catastrophic failure if they fall behind technologically. That profile, he said, does not meet his definition of a great business.

  ## Why Baupost is avoiding AI model companies

  Klarman described current technology sector valuations as extremely stretched, with investors adopting what he called "new era thinking" to rationalize projections about the distant future. He invoked the baseball concept of a "fat pitch," a slow, hittable throw representing a high-probability opportunity, to describe the conditions Baupost requires before committing capital. He said he prefers to hold cash idle rather than invest when those conditions are absent.

  For companies trading at 40 times earnings, or at valuations where no calculable multiple applies, investing requires blind faith in outcomes that cannot be known, Klarman said. Baupost is not simply cautious on artificial intelligence broadly: the fund is actively seeking what Klarman called "AI agnostics," companies whose operations face no disruption risk from AI and where the technology has almost no effect on the business.

  ## Palantir: a deal that fell through

  Klarman identified the biggest regret of his career during the interview. Fifteen to twenty years ago, Baupost had completed its research and was prepared to acquire $40 million to $50 million in venture capital shares of Palantir Technologies (ticker: PLTR), a large-data analytics company. The seller reversed course at the last moment and the transaction did not close. Klarman said that position, had it been completed, would today carry a value in the tens of billions of dollars.

  ## Where Baupost is deploying capital

  While avoiding AI model companies, Baupost is moving into areas Klarman described as undervalued or overlooked. He said the firm is monitoring distressed debt arising from corporate bond defaults and credit rating downgrades, with specific attention on Brazilian corporate restructuring situations and large-scale private equity debt exchanges.

  In commercial real estate, a sector that has been broadly avoided by investors, Klarman said Baupost is using a bottom-up approach, with a focus on senior assisted living communities that absorbed pandemic stress and went through large-scale bankruptcy processes. He said fundamentals in that segment are at the early stages of a reversal.

  The firm is also accumulating what Klarman described as data-center support land with high option value, acquiring parcels at prices below replacement cost.

  ## Macro warnings

  Klarman extended his cautionary view to the broader macroeconomic environment. US federal debt has surpassed 100 percent of gross domestic product, the broadest measure of economic output, and the annual structural fiscal deficit exceeds $2 trillion, he said. Those conditions are eroding the confidence of overseas creditors in US fiscal discipline. He described US Treasury bonds, historically treated by investors as a risk-free benchmark, as increasingly dangerous.

  On geopolitical risk, Klarman raised the possibility of conflict in the Middle East closing the Strait of Hormuz for several months. That scenario, he said, could drive international crude oil above $150 per barrel, trigger a second wave of inflation, and produce political instability. He warned that markets are far from adequately pricing in those risks, calling them "gray rhinos," meaning foreseeable threats that investors underestimate.

  ## Baupost's investment discipline

  Klarman attributed Baupost's long-term track record to what he called a near-obsessive focus on downside protection. He cited rigorous fundamental research, a policy of carrying no portfolio leverage, a preference for senior securities with structural payment priority in private markets, and a willingness to hold large amounts of cash while waiting for high-probability opportunities. That posture preserved the capacity to deploy capital at market lows during past crises, he said.

  On value investing more broadly, Klarman said the discipline is not synonymous with buying low price-to-earnings or low price-to-book ratios but rather with assessing what a company is actually worth, and that a growing business is worth more by that measure. He warned that traditional businesses with deep structural weaknesses are losing intrinsic value at an accelerating pace, comparing them to "melting ice cubes," and said even a four-times earnings multiple does not make such companies safe to own in a period of rapid technological disruption.

  Since the 2008 financial crisis, markets have spent an unusually long period in low-volatility conditions, Klarman said. In recent months, default and bankruptcy risks in corporate bonds and private credit markets have been rising, which he said generates the kind of mispriced opportunities Baupost seeks when bonds are sold off due to defaults or rating downgrades. Baupost is actively following Brazilian corporate restructuring and large private equity debt-exchange situations as those conditions develop.
body_zh: |
  管理波士頓對沖基金Baupost Group長達44年、任職期間僅歷經五個虧損年度的賽斯．克拉曼（Seth Klarman），日前接受CNBC專訪時表示，旗下基金並未持有OpenAI、Anthropic或其他大型語言模型公司的部位。他警告，當前人工智慧相關公司的估值已明顯出現泡沫特徵，投資人對於未知未來所做的假設過度樂觀。

  外號「波士頓巴菲特」的克拉曼指出，OpenAI與Anthropic不僅估值極度膨脹，更持續以驚人速度燃燒現金，一旦在技術上落後競爭對手，便可能面臨災難性失敗。這樣的特質，他說，完全不符合他對「優質企業」的定義。

  ## Baupost迴避AI模型公司的理由

  克拉曼形容，目前科技類股的估值已被拉抬至極度扭曲的水準，投資人正以他所稱的「新時代思維」來為遙遠未來的各種預測自圓其說。他援引棒球術語「甜蜜好球」（fat pitch）——即緩慢且易於擊打、代表高勝率機會的來球——來描述Baupost願意出手投資所需具備的條件，並說他寧可讓資金閒置，也不願在條件不符時貿然進場。

  對於本益比高達40倍、甚至根本無法計算乘數的公司，投資人所能做的只是對無從預知的結果盲目押注，克拉曼如此表示。Baupost對人工智慧的態度並非全面保守：該基金正積極尋找他所謂的「AI無關企業」，也就是營運完全不受AI顛覆風險影響、且這項技術對其業務幾乎毫無效應的公司。

  ## 與Palantir擦肩而過的遺憾

  克拉曼在訪談中提及他職業生涯中最大的遺憾。十五至二十年前，Baupost已完成盡職調查，準備斥資4,000萬至5,000萬美元買入大數據分析公司Palantir Technologies（股票代碼：PLTR）的創業投資股份，卻在即將成交的最後一刻遭賣方反悔，交易因此告吹。克拉曼說，若這筆投資當時順利完成，如今的市值將高達數百億美元。

  ## Baupost的資金部署方向

  在迴避AI模型公司的同時，Baupost正積極布局克拉曼眼中價值遭低估或被市場忽視的領域。他說，基金正密切追蹤企業債券違約及信用評等遭調降所衍生的困境債機會，並特別關注巴西企業重整案件以及大規模私募股權債務交換情況。

  在長期被投資人敬而遠之的商業不動產領域，克拉曼表示Baupost採取由下而上的分析方式，重點聚焦於曾在疫情期間承受龐大壓力、並歷經大規模破產程序的高齡輔助照護社區。他說，這個次族群的基本面正處於落底翻轉的初期階段。

  此外，基金也正在累積他所描述為具有高度選擇權價值的「資料中心支援用地」，以低於重置成本的價格收購相關地塊。

  ## 總體經濟風險警示

  克拉曼進一步對整體總體經濟環境表達審慎看法。他指出，美國聯邦債務已超過國內生產毛額（GDP）的100%，每年的結構性財政赤字更突破2兆美元，這些情況正在侵蝕海外債權人對美國財政紀律的信心。他形容，歷來被投資人視為無風險基準的美國公債，如今正變得愈來愈危險。

  在地緣政治風險方面，克拉曼提出中東衝突導致霍莫茲海峽封閉數月的可能情境。他說，這一情境可能推動國際原油價格突破每桶150美元，引發第二波通膨，並造成政治動盪。他警告，市場目前對這些風險的定價遠遠不足，並稱其為「灰犀牛」——即可以預見卻被投資人嚴重低估的威脅。

  ## Baupost的投資紀律

  克拉曼將Baupost的長期績效歸功於他所說的「近乎偏執」的下行風險保護意識，並列舉幾項關鍵原則：嚴格的基本面研究、不動用組合槓桿的政策、偏好私人市場中具有結構性優先受償順位的優先級證券，以及願意在等待高勝率機會時持有大量現金。他說，這樣的投資姿態讓Baupost在歷次危機觸底時，都保有充足的火力得以進場部署資金。

  對於價值投資的本質，克拉曼表示，這並不等同於買進低本益比或低股價淨值比的股票，而是要評估一家公司真正值多少錢——就此標準而言，成長型企業往往更具價值。他警告，具有深層結構性弱點的傳統企業，其內在價值正以加速的步伐流失，並將其比喻為「融化中的冰塊」。他說，即使本益比只有四倍，在技術快速顛覆的時代，這類公司也稱不上安全的投資標的。

  自2008年金融危機以來，市場已歷經一段異常漫長的低波動時期。克拉曼指出，近幾個月來，企業債券與私人信貸市場的違約及破產風險持續上升，他說這正是Baupost所尋求的機會類型——當債券因違約或評等遭調降而遭到拋售時，往往出現定價失當的買進機會。Baupost目前正積極追蹤巴西企業重整案件及大型私募股權債務交換情況的最新發展。
