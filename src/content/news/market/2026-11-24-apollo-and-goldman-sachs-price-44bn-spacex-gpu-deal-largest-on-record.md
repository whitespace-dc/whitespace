body_en: |
  Apollo and Goldman Sachs have confirmed the pricing of $44 billion in senior and subordinated notes to fund SpaceX's purchase of Nvidia Vera Rubin graphics processing units (GPUs), marking the largest GPU financing transaction on record, the firms said.

  The deal was oversubscribed and drew anchor commitments from funds and accounts managed by BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management, and capital accounts advised by KKR. Apollo and Goldman Sachs led the transaction.

  Proceeds will fund the acquisition of Nvidia Vera Rubin chips, which SpaceX will deploy to support its terrestrial compute buildout. Hardware delivery is scheduled for the first half of 2027.

  "SpaceX is one of the most ambitious builders of compute in the world, and we are pleased to have led this financing alongside Goldman Sachs," Apollo partner Jamshid Ehsani said. "This transaction reflects the strength of Apollo's integrated platform, where origination, structuring, syndication and principal investing work together to deliver flexible capital at scale. It also reflects our focus on downside-protected, asset-based structures."

  The deal follows SpaceX CEO Elon Musk's confirmation last month that the company was seeking financing for data center and AI infrastructure investments. SpaceX on November 12, 2026 announced plans for two AI supercomputing facilities in Arizona and Texas, with a total planned investment exceeding $74 billion.

  ## Arizona and Texas buildout

  The larger of the two planned facilities would occupy 747 acres (approximately 302 hectares) near Willcox Playa in Cochise County, Arizona. At full operation, that site is projected to require up to 6,000 megawatts (6 gigawatts) of electricity — equal to nearly two-thirds of the record peak demand across Arizona Public Service's (APS) entire service territory recorded this past summer.

  The Arizona facility would draw power from the Sulphur Springs Valley Electric Cooperative and Arizona Electric Power Cooperative, according to the Arizona Daily Star. SpaceX identified the Apache Generating Station, a coal and natural gas plant, as a supply source it said would expand from roughly 1 gigawatt to more than 6 gigawatts of capacity. SpaceX's permit application documents include $3.6 billion in planned grid infrastructure upgrades.

  ## Power costs and community concerns

  The scale of energy demand has raised questions in Arizona about who would bear the cost of building out transmission and generation infrastructure — SpaceX or ratepayers through higher electricity tariffs. APS applies a "growth pays for growth" principle under which large customers are expected to cover the cost of additional infrastructure their consumption requires. Local residents and officials have asked for clarity on cost-sharing arrangements before approving the project.

  At a community meeting, Riley Trettle, head of SpaceX's energy and supercomputing division, outlined the company's promised benefits. SpaceX said the project would self-finance its own infrastructure needs and has proposed independent power generation at the Texas site and dry cooling technology in Arizona. The Arizona Daily Star reported that Trettle said the project would lower residents' electricity bills, cut local property taxes by 20 percent, and create approximately 1,000 permanent jobs, with 5,500 additional positions during the construction phase.

  SpaceX's permit application lists a potential one-time local sales tax benefit of $250 million and projects ongoing annual sales tax revenues of between $35 million and $103 million.

  Nvidia Vera Rubin chip deliveries to SpaceX are scheduled to begin in the first half of 2027.

  Apollo與高盛（Goldman Sachs）已確認完成440億美元優先及次順位票據的定價，為SpaceX收購輝達（NVIDIA）Vera Rubin繪圖處理器（GPU）提供融資。兩家公司表示，此次交易規模創下有史以來最大的GPU融資紀錄。

  本次交易超額認購，並獲得貝萊德（BlackRock）管理之基金與帳戶、高盛資產管理（Goldman Sachs Asset Management），以及KKR旗下資本帳戶的錨定認購承諾，由Apollo與高盛共同主導。

  募資所得將用於收購輝達Vera Rubin晶片，SpaceX將部署這批晶片以支持其陸地運算基礎設施的建設，硬體交付預定於2027年上半年完成。

  Apollo合夥人賈姆希德·埃赫薩尼（Jamshid Ehsani）表示：「SpaceX是全球最具野心的運算基礎設施建設者之一，我們很榮幸能與高盛共同主導此次融資。這筆交易展現了Apollo整合平台的實力——從交易起源、結構設計、聯貸安排到自有資金投資，各環節緊密協作，在規模上提供靈活資本。同時也體現了我們對下行保護型資產基礎結構的高度重視。」

  此次交易緊接在SpaceX執行長伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）上個月確認公司正為資料中心及AI基礎設施投資積極尋求融資之後。SpaceX於2026年11月12日宣布，計畫分別在亞利桑那州與德克薩斯州各興建一座AI超算設施，兩地合計規劃投資總額逾740億美元。

  ## 亞利桑那州與德克薩斯州建設計畫

  兩座規劃設施中規模較大的一座，將落址於亞利桑那州科奇斯郡（Cochise County）威爾科克斯乾湖（Willcox Playa）附近，佔地747英畝（約302公頃）。全面投產後，該設施預計最高將需耗用6,000百萬瓦（6吉瓦）電力，幾乎相當於亞利桑那公共服務公司（Arizona Public Service，APS）今年夏季所記錄之全轄區尖峰需求的三分之二。

  據《亞利桑那每日星報》（Arizona Daily Star）報導，亞利桑那設施將由硫磺泉谷電力合作社（Sulphur Springs Valley Electric Cooperative）與亞利桑那電力合作社（Arizona Electric Power Cooperative）提供電力。SpaceX在許可申請文件中指明阿帕契發電站（Apache Generating Station）——一座燃煤與天然氣混合發電廠——為供電來源之一，並表示該電站容量將從約1吉瓦擴建至逾6吉瓦。申請文件同時顯示，SpaceX規劃投入36億美元用於電網基礎設施升級。

  ## 電費成本與社區疑慮

  如此龐大的用電規模，在亞利桑那州引發了各界對於輸電與發電基礎設施建設費用歸屬的疑問——究竟應由SpaceX自行承擔，還是透過調漲電費的方式轉嫁給一般用戶？APS奉行「成長者負擔成長成本」的原則，要求大型用電客戶自行負擔其用電需求所衍生的額外基礎設施費用。當地居民與官員也要求在批准專案之前，先行釐清費用分攤的具體安排。

  在一場社區說明會上，SpaceX能源與超算部門主管萊利·特雷特爾（Riley Trettle）說明了公司承諾帶來的各項效益。SpaceX表示，該專案將自籌基礎設施建設資金，並提議在德克薩斯州設施採用獨立發電方案，在亞利桑那州設施則導入乾式冷卻技術。《亞利桑那每日星報》報導，特雷特爾表示，該專案將有助於降低當地居民的電費帳單，並使地方財產稅下降20%，預計創造約1,000個永久職位，施工期間另有5,500個職位。

  SpaceX的許可申請文件列出潛在的一次性地方銷售稅收益達2.5億美元，並預估每年可持續帶來3,500萬至1.03億美元的銷售稅收入。
