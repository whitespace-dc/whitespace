body_en: |
  Alibaba Cloud has launched its first cloud region in South America, bringing local infrastructure to Brazil and expanding the company's global network to 31 regions spanning 106 availability zones.

  The Brazil region delivers compute, storage, containers, networking, security, databases, big data, and cloud-native services, Alibaba Cloud said in a press release. The company also said it plans to introduce agentic AI services in the country — tools it described as designed to streamline the development and operation of enterprise-scale AI agents within a secure local environment — though it did not disclose a timeline.

  ## Target sectors and local partners

  Alibaba Cloud said it is initially targeting Brazilian enterprises in fintech, AI-native companies, and independent software vendors. Two local partners anchor its go-to-market strategy. Insi, a technology solutions provider, will support enterprise cloud transformation. 4Linux, an open-source software and enterprise IT specialist, will focus on bringing generative AI to Brazilian businesses.

  "Brazil is one of the world's most dynamic digital economies and a new market central to Alibaba Cloud's expansion in Latin America," said Allen Guo, Alibaba Cloud Intelligence General Manager of the Latin America Region and Vice President of International Business. Guo said the company aims to connect Brazilian customers to Asia and the rest of its global network through local cloud and AI infrastructure.

  Roberto Certo, Chief Revenue Officer of Insi, said local infrastructure addresses data residency requirements — regulations requiring that data be stored and processed within a country's borders — alongside performance and security needs. "With a region now operating locally, we can serve these customers with the performance, security and data residency they require," Certo said.

  Marcelo Marques, Co-founder of 4Linux, said the arrangement gives customers a foundation to build and scale AI solutions, citing Alibaba Cloud's openness and commitment to partners.

  ## Expansion context

  The Brazil launch is part of a sustained run of new Alibaba Cloud region openings. Earlier this year the company opened regions in France, Malaysia, and Mexico, and launched a third data center in South Korea, according to prior reporting by w.media. Brazil is the first Alibaba Cloud region on the South American continent.

  An availability zone is a physically distinct data center facility within a cloud region, engineered to isolate customers from localized outages. With Brazil online, Alibaba Cloud operates 106 such zones across its 31 regions.

  ## What the launch means for the region

  Local infrastructure gives Brazilian enterprises, startups, developers, and public institutions the option to run workloads without routing data through overseas facilities, addressing both latency and data sovereignty concerns. Alibaba Cloud said it is continuing to build out its partner ecosystem in Brazil as part of its broader Latin American expansion.

  The company said it plans to grow that partner network and introduce agentic AI services in Brazil, with the region positioned as the hub for its Latin America operations going forward.

  阿里雲在南美洲正式開設首座雲端區域，為巴西帶來在地化基礎設施，並將公司全球網路擴展至橫跨106個可用區的31個區域。

  阿里雲在新聞稿中表示，巴西區域提供運算、儲存、容器、網路、資安、資料庫、巨量資料及雲端原生服務。該公司並透露，計畫在巴西推出代理式AI服務——其定位為協助企業在安全的在地環境中，簡化企業級AI代理的開發與維運——惟目前未揭露具體時程。

  ## 目標產業與在地合作夥伴

  阿里雲表示，初期鎖定的目標客群為金融科技業者、AI原生企業及獨立軟體供應商等巴西企業。兩家在地合作夥伴是其市場推廣策略的核心支柱：科技解決方案供應商Insi將協助企業進行雲端轉型；專注於開源軟體與企業IT的4Linux則將致力於為巴西企業導入生成式AI應用。

  「巴西是全球最具活力的數位經濟體之一，也是阿里雲拓展拉丁美洲業務的關鍵新市場，」阿里雲智能國際業務副總裁暨拉丁美洲區域總經理Allen Guo表示。Guo指出，公司目標是透過在地的雲端與AI基礎設施，將巴西客戶連結至亞洲及其全球網路。

  Insi首席營收長Roberto Certo表示，在地基礎設施能夠滿足資料在地化要求——亦即法規規定資料必須在境內儲存與處理的合規需求——同時兼顧效能與資安需求。「有了在當地正式運作的區域，我們便能以客戶所要求的效能、資安及資料在地化標準來服務他們，」Certo說道。

  4Linux共同創辦人Marcelo Marques表示，這項合作為客戶奠定了建構與擴展AI解決方案的基礎，並高度肯定阿里雲的開放態度與對合作夥伴的承諾。

  ## 全球擴展脈絡

  巴西的啟用，是阿里雲近期持續開設新區域系列動作的一部分。根據w.media先前的報導，該公司今年稍早已陸續在法國、馬來西亞及墨西哥開設區域，並在南韓啟用第三座資料中心。巴西則是阿里雲在南美洲大陸的首座雲端區域。

  可用區是雲端區域內在實體上相互獨立的資料中心設施，設計目的在於將客戶的業務與局部性故障相互隔離。隨著巴西區域正式上線，阿里雲目前在31個區域中共運營106個可用區。

  ## 此次啟用對該地區的意義

  在地基礎設施讓巴西的企業、新創公司、開發者及公共機構得以在無需將資料路由至海外設施的情況下執行工作負載，同時兼顧延遲效能與資料主權的雙重考量。阿里雲表示，正持續在巴西擴展其合作夥伴生態系，作為更廣泛拉丁美洲擴張計畫的一環。

  該公司表示，計畫在巴西進一步壯大合作夥伴網路並引進代理式AI服務，並將巴西區域定位為未來拉丁美洲業務的核心樞紐。
