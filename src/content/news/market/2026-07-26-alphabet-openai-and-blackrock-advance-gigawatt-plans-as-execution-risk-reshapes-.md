body_en: |
  The data center and AI infrastructure market is entering what developers and investors describe as a more demanding execution phase, as a convergence of megadeals, tightening utility requirements and more explicit financing conditions reshapes the terms on which gigawatt-scale projects advance.

  Alphabet raised its 2026 capital expenditure forecast to between $195 billion and $205 billion — $15 billion above its previous guidance and more than double the $91.5 billion the company spent in 2025. OpenAI announced a 3.2-gigawatt campus in Effingham County, Georgia, backed by a 25-year power supply agreement with Georgia Power. BlackRock and partners acquired Aligned Data Centers at an enterprise value of approximately $40 billion. And BlackRock is leading a debt raise of at least $12 billion for Meta's planned El Paso campus, with proposed bonds being offered at yields above 7 percent.

  Land, power and capital remain available, but not on the same terms everywhere, according to reporting on the sector. Proposed developments are also drawing resistance across North America and beyond, adding a public-scrutiny dimension to the execution challenge. Projects most likely to advance are those that combine a credible customer, a durable power path, institutional financing and a development strategy capable of surviving that scrutiny.

  ## Hyperscaler capital commitments rise sharply

  Alphabet's revised forecast of $195 billion to $205 billion in 2026 capital expenditures follows a quarter in which Google Cloud, the company's cloud computing division, reported revenue of $24.8 billion, up 82 percent year over year. The revised forecast is more than twice Alphabet's 2025 capital spending of $91.5 billion.

  The largest platforms are committing unprecedented cash to facilities, chips, networks and power systems whose economics will be measured over many years. The cloud growth explains the spending without removing its financial pressure.

  ## OpenAI signs 3.2 GW power contract for Georgia campus

  OpenAI's Project Camellia in Effingham County, Georgia, would be one of the largest AI infrastructure developments yet proposed. OpenAI says it is designing and developing the campus itself and has contracted with Georgia Power for 3.2 gigawatts of capacity to be delivered in phases from 2028 through 2032. Local reporting places the initial investment at at least $20 billion across roughly 1,400 acres.

  The 25-year power supply agreement is structured to cover infrastructure costs, protect residential ratepayers, use closed-loop cooling and reduce load during grid stress periods. OpenAI has also described an $80 million community-benefit commitment tied to the project.

  The Georgia campus illustrates a broader shift in what major projects require before construction begins. A power contract and an industrial site are no longer sufficient on their own; developers increasingly need credible answers on ratepayer protection, water use, grid reliability and direct community value before the first data hall is built, according to reporting on the sector.

  ## Institutional investors scale up through Aligned acquisition

  The Artificial Intelligence Infrastructure Partnership (AIP), MGX and BlackRock's Global Infrastructure Partners acquired Aligned Data Centers at an enterprise value of approximately $40 billion and committed an additional $5 billion for expansion. Aligned says its portfolio spans 51 campuses with more than 6.4 gigawatts of operating and planned capacity.

  The deal is AIP's first investment. The partnership aims to mobilize $30 billion in equity and up to $100 billion including debt, using Aligned as a platform for deploying infrastructure capital across multiple markets. The closing shows how institutional capital is valuing development platforms that can repeatedly convert power and land into operating capacity.

  Hut 8's second lease at its Beacon Point campus in Nueces County, Texas, illustrates the same institutionalization from the developer side. The company signed a 15-year lease for 352 megawatts (MW) of IT capacity with an investment-grade tenant, bringing total signed IT capacity at the campus to 704 MW across two phases. The combined base-term contract value is $19.6 billion, with 3 percent annual rent escalators.

  The Beacon Point agreements are tied to 1,000 MW of utility capacity secured through AEP Texas. The signed IT leases total 704 MW against that 1,000 MW of utility capacity, a gap that reflects the overhead required to support the compute environment. A signed lease with an investment-grade customer has become one of the most valuable assets in AI infrastructure, according to reporting on the deals. Power defines the opportunity, but contracted cash flow determines how much of it can be financed.

  ## Financing market prices duration and execution risk

  Capital remains available for AI infrastructure development, but lenders are pricing duration, concentration and execution risk more explicitly.

  BlackRock is leading a debt raise of at least $12 billion for Meta's planned campus in El Paso, Texas. The ownership structure reportedly gives BlackRock-affiliated investors an 80 percent stake and Meta a 20 percent stake, with Meta committing to a long-term lease. The campus is expected to reach approximately 1 gigawatt of capacity. The proposed bonds were reportedly being offered at yields above 7 percent, roughly 40 basis points above the debt supporting Meta's Hyperion campus in Louisiana. Meta remains responsible for cost overruns and is bound by a 20-year lease with substantial early-termination protections.

  In Wisconsin, Oracle could be required to provide more than $7 billion in collateral under a tariff designed to protect utility customers from the risk of serving very large loads. The requirement is linked to Oracle's credit rating and the infrastructure utilities would need to build for a proposed 1-gigawatt AI campus. Oracle has challenged provisions that prevent utilities from waiving the collateral rules.

  The Wisconsin case demonstrates that a globally recognized customer and a multibillion-dollar plan do not eliminate stranded-asset risk. Utilities still need to know who pays if a load arrives late, falls short of forecast or never materializes, according to reporting on the sector.

  ## Compute capacity emerges as a tradable product

  Meta is reportedly in early discussions to rent as much as $10 billion of AI computing capacity to Anthropic, with monthly payments over approximately two years. The reported talks concern a possible compute-rental agreement rather than a signed data center development deal, but they point toward a more fluid market in which accelerators, power and data hall capacity can be used internally or leased to another AI laboratory.

  Fluidstack disclosed an $830 million Series A at a $7.5 billion valuation. The company said the financing closed in January; the announcement came in July. Fluidstack is positioning itself as a rapid deployment platform for custom AI data centers.

  General Compute secured a debt facility of up to $400 million from Upper90 to expand an inference-focused cloud built around specialized accelerators. Reports say the chips will serve as collateral for the facility.

  These businesses are being financed as a combination of equipment lessor, data center operator and infrastructure developer. Their viability depends on utilization: accelerators only support debt when customers keep them busy.

  ## Texas tests integrated generation model

  Texas has become the primary laboratory for integrating power generation directly into campus architecture rather than treating utility supply as an external service arranged after site selection.

  Crusoe and Lancium announced a 1-gigawatt AI campus on 270 acres in Childress County, Texas. Lancium will own the land, secure the grid interconnection and manage the energy infrastructure; Crusoe will design, build and operate the data center. Construction is expected to begin in the third quarter of 2026. The campus will use energy storage and closed-loop, non-evaporative liquid cooling.

  Liberty Energy and PowerBridge are advancing a joint venture to support the planned 2-gigawatt Alpha Digital Campus in West Texas. The initial phase is expected to include more than 300 megawatts of generation, with first power targeted for late 2027 and the first half of 2028.

  SpaceX and xAI are also reportedly exploring additional large-scale Texas data center capacity, though no final site or capacity figure has been publicly confirmed.

  In Kentucky, Rubix Data Centers, part of Submer Group, has proposed a project at a former steelmaking site near Ashland.

  ## What the pattern signals

  The convergence of these announcements describes a buildout that remains well-funded and expanding in scale, but where the conditions for project advancement have grown more demanding across every dimension: financing yield spreads are widening, utilities are imposing collateral requirements tied to customer creditworthiness, community-benefit commitments are becoming standard, and long-term leases with investment-grade tenants are now prerequisites for institutional debt placement rather than optional enhancements.

  Construction at the Crusoe-Lancium campus in Childress County, Texas, is scheduled to begin in the third quarter of 2026, marking one of the first gigawatt-scale AI campus projects to move from announcement to groundbreaking under the more demanding conditions now defining the sector.

  資料中心與AI基礎設施市場，正進入開發商與投資人所稱的「更嚴苛執行階段」。大型交易匯聚、公用事業要求收緊，以及更明確的融資條件，三股力量交匯，正在重塑吉瓦級計畫推進的各項基本條件。

  Alphabet將2026年資本支出預測上調至1,950億至2,050億美元，較先前指引高出150億美元，也超過2025年實際支出915億美元的兩倍以上。OpenAI宣布在喬治亞州埃芬漢縣（Effingham County）興建3.2吉瓦園區，並取得喬治亞電力公司（Georgia Power）25年供電協議作為支撐。貝萊德與合作夥伴以約400億美元企業價值收購Aligned Data Centers。此外，貝萊德正為Meta計畫中的艾爾帕索（El Paso）園區主導至少120億美元的融資案，據報導擬發行債券的殖利率超過7%。

  土地、電力與資本依然可及，但各地取得條件不盡相同。擬議中的開發案也在北美各地及其他地區遭遇民間抵制，為執行挑戰增添了公眾審查的維度。最有可能推進的計畫，是那些兼具可信客戶、穩固電力路徑、機構融資，以及能夠在公眾審查中站穩腳跟的開發策略的計畫。

  ## 超大型雲端業者資本承諾大幅攀升

  Alphabet將2026年資本支出修正預測上調至1,950億至2,050億美元，此前一季，旗下雲端運算部門Google Cloud報告營收達248億美元，年增82%。修正後的預測超過Alphabet 2025年資本支出915億美元的兩倍以上。

  各大平台正以前所未有的規模，投入資金建設設施、晶片、網路與電力系統，這些投資的經濟效益將在未來多年逐步衡量。雲端業務的成長固然說明了這筆龐大支出的合理性，但也無法消弭其帶來的財務壓力。

  ## OpenAI為喬治亞州園區簽署3.2吉瓦供電合約

  OpenAI的「卡美利亞計畫」（Project Camellia）位於喬治亞州埃芬漢縣，將成為迄今提出的規模最大AI基礎設施建設案之一。OpenAI表示，該公司將自行設計與開發園區，並與喬治亞電力公司簽約，取得3.2吉瓦容量，分階段於2028年至2032年間交付。當地媒體報導，初期投資金額至少達200億美元，園區面積約1,400英畝。

  這份為期25年的供電協議涵蓋基礎設施成本、保護住宅用電戶，採用閉迴路冷卻系統，並在電網壓力緊繃時段降低負載。OpenAI亦表示將為此計畫投入8,000萬美元的社區回饋方案。

  喬治亞州園區的案例，清楚說明大型計畫在動工之前所須滿足的條件已大幅提高。根據業界報導，僅憑一份供電合約與一塊工業用地已不再足夠；開發商在建設第一座資料廳之前，愈來愈需要就用電戶保護、用水規劃、電網可靠性及直接社區效益提出可信的具體答覆。

  ## 機構投資人透過收購Aligned Data Centers擴大布局

  「人工智慧基礎設施合夥」（AIP）、MGX與貝萊德旗下全球基礎設施夥伴（Global Infrastructure Partners），以約400億美元企業價值收購Aligned Data Centers，並另行承諾50億美元用於後續擴張。Aligned表示，其投資組合涵蓋51座園區，運營中及規劃中容量合計逾6.4吉瓦。

  這筆交易是AIP的首筆投資。該合夥機構目標動員300億美元股權資金，含負債在內最高達1,000億美元，以Aligned作為在多個市場部署基礎設施資本的平台。此次交割顯示，機構資本正為能夠反覆將電力與土地轉化為運營容量的開發平台，給出高度估值。

  Hut 8在德克薩斯州努埃塞斯縣（Nueces County）Beacon Point園區的第二份租約，同樣印證了開發商端的機構化趨勢。該公司與一位投資等級租戶簽署了352百萬瓦（MW）IT容量的15年租約，使園區兩期合計已簽署的IT容量達到704 MW。兩份租約基本期的合約總值為196億美元，年租金遞增率為3%。

  Beacon Point的協議綁定了透過AEP Texas取得的1,000 MW公用事業容量。已簽署的IT租約合計704 MW，對應1,000 MW的公用事業容量，此差距反映了支撐運算環境所需的額外系統開銷。根據業界報導，與投資等級客戶簽署的租約，已成為AI基礎設施中最具價值的資產之一。電力定義了機遇的上限，但有合約保障的現金流，決定了其中有多少能夠實現融資。

  ## 融資市場明確為存續期間與執行風險定價

  AI基礎設施開發的資本依然充裕，但貸款方正更明確地將存續期間、集中度與執行風險納入定價考量。

  貝萊德正為Meta計畫在德克薩斯州艾爾帕索興建的園區，主導一筆至少120億美元的融資案。據報導，所有權結構中貝萊德關聯投資人持股80%、Meta持股20%，Meta承諾簽署長期租約。該園區預計容量約1吉瓦。擬發行債券的殖利率據報超過7%，較支撐Meta路易斯安那州Hyperion園區的債務高出約40個基點。Meta仍須承擔成本超支責任，並受一份附有重大提前終止保護條款的20年租約所約束。

  在威斯康辛州，甲骨文（Oracle）可能須依據一項旨在保護公用事業客戶免受超大型負載風險的費率規定，提供超過70億美元的擔保。此要求與甲骨文的信用評等，以及公用事業業者為其規劃中的1吉瓦AI園區所需建設的基礎設施直接掛鉤。甲骨文已對阻止公用事業豁免擔保規定的相關條款提出異議。

  威斯康辛州案例證明，即便是全球知名客戶加上數十億美元的建設計畫，也無法消除擱置資產的風險。根據業界報導，公用事業業者仍需確認，若負載延遲到位、未達預期規模或最終未能實現，由誰來承擔損失。

  ## 運算容量成為可交易商品

  Meta據報正與Anthropic就租用最高100億美元AI運算容量一事進行初步磋商，付款期間約兩年、按月支付。據報導的談判涉及可能的運算租用協議，而非已簽署的資料中心開發合約，但這指向一個更為靈活的市場——加速器、電力與資料廳容量既可自用，亦可出租給其他AI實驗室。

  Fluidstack揭露一輪8.3億美元的A輪融資，估值達75億美元。該公司表示融資已於1月完成，相關公告則遲至7月才發布。Fluidstack將自身定位為客製化AI資料中心的快速部署平台。

  General Compute向Upper90取得最高4億美元的債務融資額度，用於擴展一套以專用加速器為核心的推論雲端服務。報導指出，這批晶片將作為該融資額度的擔保品。

  這些企業正以設備租賃商、資料中心業者與基礎設施開發商三位一體的組合形態獲得融資。其存續能力取決於使用率——唯有客戶持續保持高使用量，加速器才能支撐起債務。

  ## 德克薩斯州測試整合式發電模型

  德克薩斯州已成為將發電直接整合進園區架構的主要試驗場，而非在選定廠址之後，才將公用事業供電視為外部服務另行安排。

  Crusoe與Lancium宣布在德克薩斯州奇爾德雷斯縣（Childress County）270英畝土地上興建1吉瓦AI園區。Lancium將持有土地、取得電網併網許可並管理能源基礎設施；Crusoe則負責設計、建造與運營資料中心。建設預計於2026年第三季開工。園區將採用儲能系統，並配備閉迴路非蒸發式液冷技術。

  Liberty Energy與PowerBridge正推進一項合資計畫，以支援西德克薩斯州計畫中的2吉瓦Alpha Digital Campus。初期階段預計包含逾300 MW的發電容量，首度供電目標定在2027年底至2028年上半年。

  SpaceX與xAI據報也在探索德克薩斯州其他大規模資料中心容量，但目前尚未公開確認最終選址或容量規模。

  在肯塔基州，Submer集團旗下的Rubix Data Centers已提出在阿什蘭（Ashland）附近一處前鋼鐵廠舊址興建計畫。

  ## 趨勢所指

  上述公告的匯聚，描繪出一場資金依然充足、規模持續擴大的建設浪潮，但計畫推進的條件已在各個面向全面趨嚴：融資殖利率利差持續擴大，公用事業業者依客戶信用狀況祭出擔保要求，社區回饋承諾逐漸成為標準配備，與投資等級租戶簽訂的長期租約，如今已是機構債務安排的先決條件，而非可有可無的錦上添花。

  Crusoe-Lancium在德克薩斯州奇爾德雷斯縣的園區建設預計於2026年第三季開工，這將是首批在當前更嚴苛的條件下，從宣布邁向正式動工的吉瓦級AI園區計畫之一，也標誌著整個產業一個值得關注的新里程碑。
