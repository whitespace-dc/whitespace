body_en: |
  PT Telkom Data Ekosistem — known commercially as NeutraDC and an operating company of state-owned PT Telkom Indonesia — has filled all 21.5 megawatts of IT capacity at its NeutraDC Hyperscale Data Center (HDC) in Cikarang, West Java, designated JKT-1, as of the fourth quarter of 2026, the company said. The sellout triggers a phased expansion plan targeting 400 MW of new data center capacity, with a longer-term aspiration to reach 1 gigawatt (GW) across Indonesia and Southeast Asia.

  A megawatt of data center IT load can broadly support thousands of servers; at hyperscale densities increasingly demanded by artificial intelligence workloads, 21.5 MW represents a facility of meaningful national scale, though well short of the largest campuses now being built globally. NeutraDC's 1 GW aspiration, if realized, would represent roughly 46 times the Cikarang building's current footprint.

  ## Capacity and expansion plans

  NeutraDC said the Cikarang facility reached full occupancy after all customers began operating their infrastructure. The company described the sellout as a signal of sustained market demand for digital infrastructure, particularly for AI-related computing workloads.

  The phased target calls first for 400 MW, with 1 GW framed as an aspirational ceiling. Expansion sites under consideration include additional land at Cikarang and other strategic locations. NeutraDC said it is also exploring land parcels within industrial zones recommended by Danantara Indonesia — the Indonesian state investment holding entity — for feasibility review. Site selection criteria include energy availability, connectivity, and supporting infrastructure readiness, the company said.

  ## Danantara and ecosystem collaboration

  NeutraDC said collaboration with stakeholders inside the TelkomGroup digital ecosystem and with portfolio companies of Danantara Indonesia forms part of its site-identification process. The company did not disclose specific partners, timelines, or capital commitments for the expansion.

  ## Service lines

  NeutraDC offers capacity through three service categories:

  - **Neutra Colocation** — physical facility space for customer IT infrastructure
  - **Neutra Compute** — AI computing resources
  - **Neutra Connect** — connectivity between customer systems and digital ecosystems

  The company said all three lines are designed to support AI application development and operation.

  ## Sustainability considerations

  NeutraDC said expansion planning accounts for resource-use efficiency and supporting infrastructure readiness alongside market demand and capacity requirements, framing responsible growth as a condition of the buildout rather than a secondary concern.

  ## What the expansion signals

  The sellout of Cikarang's JKT-1 building and NeutraDC's subsequent pivot to large-scale expansion reflect the pressure AI computing demand is placing on Indonesia's data center market. As an operating company of Telkom Indonesia, NeutraDC sits at the intersection of state infrastructure policy and commercial hyperscale development, giving its expansion plans relevance for both private operators and government digital economy initiatives.

  NeutraDC CEO Rakhmad Tunggal Afifuddin said the full occupancy of the Cikarang building demonstrates continuous growth in market demand for digital infrastructure, and that future builds must remain aligned with customer needs and technological development, not simply add raw capacity.

  The company's next disclosed milestone is the phased development toward 400 MW, with site assessments for the broader 1 GW program ongoing across Indonesia and Southeast Asia.
body_zh: |
  PT Telkom Data Ekosistem——商業名稱為NeutraDC，印尼國營企業PT Telkom Indonesia旗下的營運子公司——宣布，位於西爪哇加拉讓的NeutraDC超大規模資料中心（Hyperscale Data Center，HDC）、代號JKT-1，已於2026年第四季達到21.5百萬瓦（MW）IT容量的滿載狀態。據該公司表示，所有客戶全數開始啟用其基礎設施後，設施即告售罄。此一售罄結果觸發了分階段擴建計畫，目標是新增400MW資料中心容量，並以在印尼及東南亞地區達到1吉瓦（GW）為長期願景。

  一個百萬瓦的資料中心IT負載，大致可支撐數千台伺服器；在人工智慧（AI）工作負載所需的超大規模高密度配置下，21.5MW代表一座具有相當國家規模意義的設施，雖然與目前全球正在興建的最大園區相比仍有落差。若NeutraDC的1GW願景得以實現，其規模將約為加拉讓現有廠房容量的46倍。

  ## 容量與擴建計畫

  NeutraDC表示，加拉讓設施在所有客戶開始運作其基礎設施後達到全數滿載。公司將此次售罄形容為數位基礎設施市場持續需求的一項訊號，尤其是針對AI相關運算工作負載的需求。

  分階段擴建計畫首先以400MW為目標，1GW則定位為最終願景上限。目前正在評估的擴建選址，包括加拉讓的額外土地及其他策略性地點。NeutraDC表示，也正在探索印尼國家投資控股機構Danantara Indonesia所推薦的工業區土地，進行可行性評估。公司表示，選址標準涵蓋電力供應、網路連線能力，以及配套基礎設施的整備程度。

  ## Danantara與生態系協作

  NeutraDC表示，與TelkomGroup數位生態系內各利害關係人，以及Danantara Indonesia旗下投資組合企業的合作，是其選址流程的一環。公司並未揭露擴建計畫的具體合作夥伴、執行時程或資本承諾。

  ## 服務項目

  NeutraDC透過三大服務類別提供容量：

  - **Neutra Colocation**——為客戶IT基礎設施提供實體設施空間
  - **Neutra Compute**——AI運算資源
  - **Neutra Connect**——客戶系統與數位生態系之間的網路連接

  公司表示，三項服務均旨在支援AI應用程式的開發與運作。

  ## 永續發展考量

  NeutraDC表示，擴建規劃在考量市場需求與容量需求的同時，也將資源使用效率與配套基礎設施整備程度一併納入，將負責任的成長定位為擴建的前提條件，而非次要考量。

  ## 擴建的意涵

  加拉讓JKT-1廠房售罄，以及NeutraDC隨後轉向大規模擴建，反映出AI運算需求正對印尼資料中心市場造成的壓力。作為Telkom Indonesia的營運子公司，NeutraDC處於國家基礎設施政策與商業超大規模開發的交匯點，使其擴建計畫對民間業者與政府數位經濟施政均具有重要參考意義。

  NeutraDC執行長Rakhmad Tunggal Afifuddin表示，加拉讓廠房的全數滿載，證明了數位基礎設施市場需求的持續成長，而未來的建設必須與客戶需求及技術發展保持一致，而非單純擴增容量。

  公司下一個已公開的里程碑，是朝400MW分階段推進，同時持續在印尼及東南亞各地進行更廣泛的1GW計畫選址評估。
