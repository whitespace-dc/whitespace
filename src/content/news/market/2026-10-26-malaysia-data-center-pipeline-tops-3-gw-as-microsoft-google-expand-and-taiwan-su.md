body_en: |
  Malaysia has emerged as Southeast Asia's largest new data center market by volume of planned additions, with more than 3 gigawatts of capacity under construction or in planning and an existing installed base exceeding 1 gigawatt, as Microsoft and Google accelerate deployments and Taiwan supply chain manufacturers expand production capacity in the country.

  Industry research firm Digitimes reports that demand from major cloud service providers — companies that operate large-scale shared computing infrastructure — has shifted out of Singapore and into Malaysia, Indonesia, and Thailand. Malaysia stands out as the single largest new-build market among those destinations.

  ## Microsoft and Google drive the next buildout phase

  AWS, Microsoft, and Oracle have already brought data centers online in Malaysia over the past two years, according to Digitimes. Microsoft and Google are now the primary drivers of the next round of capacity additions, the firm said, and are extending their deployment footprints south from Kuala Lumpur and Selangor state into Johor and Negeri Sembilan states.

  Digitimes described the shift in strategic intent as a transition from "establishing local cloud service capability" to "expanding physical computing power and data center capacity" — a change in scale rather than geography alone.

  Malaysia's available land, access to large-scale power supply, and government support through investment incentives, power commitments, and data center policy have helped bring projects to completion, according to Digitimes.

  ## Taiwan ODM and component manufacturers establish footholds

  Taiwan's Wiwynn, an original design manufacturer — a company that designs and builds servers under contract for technology brands — has been operating a full-scale production facility in Johor Bahru, the capital of Johor state, since launching front-end and back-end server production lines in 2023 and 2024. The facility currently operates at high utilization and serves as Wiwynn's primary production base for Southeast Asia and the broader ASEAN market, according to Digitimes. Wiwynn is continuing to expand and retrofit factory floor space at the Malaysia site.

  Other Taiwan companies established in Malaysia include Chenbro, a server chassis manufacturer, along with Ablecom, Igoway, Lightsource, and Jing Ju, which are setting up AI optical communications and module production lines in the country. Digitimes noted that capacity from these manufacturers is expected to grow incrementally over the coming years as expansions and new builds come online.

  Among other Taiwan original design manufacturers, Quanta Computer and Inventec have built server production lines in Thailand, while Wistron and a Foxconn unit are operating in Vietnam.

  ## Power: access and speed both cited as requirements

  Japan's Jera, described as Japan's largest power generation company, said at the Singapore International Energy Week conference on October 25 that Malaysia holds the highest potential among Asian countries to increase power generation capacity and import liquefied natural gas — a chilled fuel used in gas-fired power plants — for data centers, based on Jera's own projections.

  Jera Asia chief executive Izumi Kai said adequate transmission capacity and access to land and water were necessary conditions alongside raw generation capacity to attract data center developers. He said developers from multiple countries, not only Japanese firms, are targeting Malaysia, alongside Thailand and Indonesia.

  Kai highlighted a constraint that goes beyond supply volume: "For AI and data centres, it is not just about access to power. Speed to power matters just as much." Jera said it is exploring ways to use existing generation sites to deliver reliable power faster in response to AI-driven demand.

  On the fuel mix, Kai said LNG can provide flexible, reliable capacity to balance electricity grids as renewable energy capacity grows, though its role will vary by market. "In some markets, it will remain the backbone fuel, but in other regions, it will increasingly complement renewables," he said.

  Kai also said capital is available for power infrastructure investment but that projects must demonstrate financial viability to attract it. "Projects need to be bankable. For long-term infrastructure, investors need predictable economics and appropriate risk sharing," he said. LNG-to-power development requires fuel supply, infrastructure, and generation capacity to advance in coordination rather than in isolation, he added.

  ## Significance

  The simultaneous movement of hyperscale cloud operators, Taiwan contract manufacturers, and international energy developers into Malaysia reflects the volume of AI infrastructure demand that has overflowed Singapore's constrained land and power availability. With more than 3 gigawatts of new capacity in the pipeline — the largest new-build total in Southeast Asia at this stage, according to Digitimes — Malaysia is absorbing a substantial portion of the region's data center investment.

  Jera said it is working to meet the speed and scale of AI demand without losing momentum on decarbonization, through solutions that combine firm power sources, renewables, and batteries.

  馬來西亞已躍升為東南亞規劃新增容量最大的資料中心市場，目前在建及規劃中的容量合計逾3吉瓦（GW），既有裝機容量亦超過1GW。隨著微軟與Google加速部署，台灣供應鏈製造商也在當地持續擴大生產能量。

  產業研究機構集邦科技（Digitimes）指出，主要雲端服務供應商——亦即經營大規模共享運算基礎建設的企業——的需求已從新加坡轉移至馬來西亞、印尼與泰國。在上述目的地中，馬來西亞以單一新建市場規模居冠。

  ## 微軟與Google引領下一波建設浪潮

  根據集邦科技的資料，AWS、微軟與甲骨文（Oracle）過去兩年已相繼在馬來西亞建立資料中心並投入運營。集邦科技表示，微軟與Google目前是推動下一輪容量擴充的主要力量，正將部署版圖從吉隆坡及雪蘭莪州向南延伸至柔佛州與森美蘭州。

  集邦科技將此一策略重心轉變描述為：從「建立在地雲端服務能力」過渡至「擴充實體運算力與資料中心容量」——這不僅是地理版圖的拓展，更是規模層次上的躍升。

  據集邦科技指出，馬來西亞充足的土地資源、大規模電力供應的取得管道，以及政府透過投資獎勵、電力承諾與資料中心政策所提供的支持，均有助於相關專案順利推進落地。

  ## 台灣ODM與零組件廠商站穩馬來西亞灘頭堡

  台灣原始設計製造商（ODM）緯穎科技（Wiwynn）——專為科技品牌依規格設計並製造伺服器的委外代工業者——自2023年及2024年分別在柔佛州首府新山（Johor Bahru）建立前段與後段伺服器生產線以來，已在當地運營全規模量產設施。集邦科技表示，該廠目前維持高稼動率，是緯穎服務東南亞及更廣泛東協市場的主要生產基地；緯穎仍持續擴建並翻新馬來西亞廠區的廠房空間。

  其他已在馬來西亞建立據點的台灣廠商，包括伺服器機殼製造商勤誠興業（Chenbro），以及正在當地設立AI光通訊與模組生產線的雅浚科技（Ablecom）、Igoway、Lightsource與晶矩（Jing Ju）。集邦科技指出，隨著擴產計畫與新建廠房陸續完工，這些廠商的產能預計將在未來數年間逐步提升。

  其他台灣ODM廠商方面，廣達電腦（Quanta Computer）與英業達（Inventec）已在泰國建立伺服器生產線，緯創資通（Wistron）與鴻海（Foxconn）旗下一個事業單位則在越南設廠運營。

  ## 電力：供應量與接電速度同受重視

  日本最大發電企業JERA在10月25日於新加坡國際能源週（Singapore International Energy Week）表示，根據JERA自身的預測，馬來西亞在亞洲各國中擁有最高潛力，可望提升發電容量並進口液化天然氣（LNG）——一種用於燃氣電廠的冷卻燃料——以支應資料中心需求。

  JERA亞洲執行長甲斐泉（Izumi Kai）表示，充足的輸電容量，以及土地與水源的取得，是吸引資料中心開發商進駐的必要條件，與原始發電容量同等重要。他指出，鎖定馬來西亞的開發商來自多個國家，並不限於日本企業，泰國與印尼同樣是各方覬覦的目標市場。

  甲斐泉進一步點出一項超越供應規模的關鍵制約：「對於AI與資料中心而言，電力的取得不是唯一關鍵，接電速度同樣至關重要。」JERA表示，正積極研究如何善用現有發電廠址，以更迅速的方式提供穩定電力，回應AI驅動的龐大需求。

  在燃料組合方面，甲斐泉表示，隨著再生能源裝機容量持續成長，LNG可提供靈活且穩定的容量，協助電網平衡調度，但其所扮演的角色因市場而異。「在某些市場，LNG仍將是主力燃料；但在其他地區，它的角色將日益轉向輔助再生能源。」他說。

  甲斐泉也指出，電力基礎建設投資雖然資金充沛，但專案必須展現財務可行性，方能吸引投資人。「專案必須具備可融資性。對於長期基礎建設，投資人需要可預期的經濟效益與合理的風險分擔機制。」他說。他補充，LNG發電開發需要燃料供應、基礎建設與發電容量三者協調並進，而非各自孤立推進。

  ## 整體意義

  超大規模雲端業者、台灣委外代工製造商與國際能源開發商同步進駐馬來西亞，反映出AI基礎建設需求已大幅溢出新加坡有限的土地與電力供給。根據集邦科技，馬來西亞的新建容量管線逾3GW，是東南亞現階段新建規模最大的市場，正在吸納該地區相當大比例的資料中心投資。

  JERA表示，正致力於在不失去去碳化動能的前提下，因應AI需求在速度與規模上的雙重挑戰，方向上將整合穩定電源、再生能源與電池儲能等多元解方。
