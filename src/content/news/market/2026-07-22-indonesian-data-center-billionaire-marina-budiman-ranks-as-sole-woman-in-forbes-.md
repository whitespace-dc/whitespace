body_en: |
  Marina Budiman, co-founder and President Commissioner of PT DCI Indonesia Tbk (DCII), has become the only woman in Indonesia's top 10 wealthiest individuals, according to a Forbes ranking dated July 14, 2026, with a net worth of $5.7 billion.

  Forbes placed Budiman at No. 8 among Indonesia's richest and No. 727 globally. Her estimated fortune equals approximately 102.9 trillion Indonesian rupiah, according to Kompas.com, which cited Forbes. Ranked one position above her at No. 7 is Otto Toto Sugiri, her co-founder and decades-long business partner, whom Indonesian business media have referred to as "Bill Gates Indonesia."

  Budiman's wealth derives primarily from her 22.51 percent stake in DCII, Indonesia's publicly traded data center operator, making her its second-largest individual shareholder after Sugiri. DCII listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange in 2021 at an initial offering price of 420 rupiah per share. The company's share value has continued rising since listing, Kompas.com reported, citing growing market confidence in DCII's business outlook.

  ## Career and partnership with Sugiri

  Born in 1961, Budiman studied economics and finance at the University of Toronto in Canada before returning to Indonesia to begin a career in technology. Her professional relationship with Sugiri began in 1985, when both worked at Bank Bali. In 1989, she joined Sigma Cipta Caraka, an information technology company Sugiri had co-founded with other former Bank Bali colleagues.

  ## Indonesia's first internet provider

  In 1994, Budiman and Sugiri co-founded Indonet, which Kompas.com identifies as Indonesia's first internet service provider (ISP), enabling Indonesian consumers to access the internet for the first time. Both founders subsequently divested their Indonet holdings in 2023.

  ## DCI Indonesia: Tier-IV data centers

  Budiman, Sugiri, and Han Arming Hanafia founded PT DCI Indonesia Tbk in 2011. According to Kompas.com, DCII describes itself as the operator of Southeast Asia's first Tier-IV data center — Tier-IV being the highest tier in the industry's four-level classification system for facility redundancy and reliability. The company operates three locations: Cibitung, Karawang, and Jakarta.

  ## Significance for Indonesia's digital infrastructure sector

  Budiman's entry into the Forbes Indonesia top 10 reflects the capital creation potential of the country's data center sector as demand for digital infrastructure expands. She remains the only woman among Indonesia's 10 wealthiest individuals as of the July 14, 2026 ranking. DCII's three operating sites are concentrated in the greater Jakarta region and the West Java industrial corridors of Cibitung and Karawang.

  根據2026年7月14日《富比士》發布的最新排行榜，PT DCI Indonesia Tbk（DCII）共同創辦人暨總裁監察委員Marina Budiman，以57億美元的淨資產，成功躋身印尼十大富豪之列，同時也是榜單上唯一的女性。

  《富比士》將Budiman列為印尼富豪第8名、全球第727名。據《Kompas.com》引述《富比士》數據，其財富換算約達102.9兆印尼盾。排名緊鄰其上、位居第7的，正是她的共同創辦人及數十年事業夥伴Otto Toto Sugiri——印尼商業媒體將他稱為「印尼的比爾・蓋茲」。

  Budiman的財富主要來自她持有DCII 22.51%的股份；DCII是印尼唯一掛牌上市的資料中心業者，Budiman為公司第二大個人股東，僅次於Sugiri。DCII於2021年在印尼證券交易所上市，首次公開發行價格為每股420印尼盾。《Kompas.com》報導，隨著市場對DCII業務前景的信心持續增強，公司股價自上市以來持續走揚。

  ## 職涯歷程與和Sugiri的合夥關係

  Budiman於1961年出生，曾赴加拿大多倫多大學攻讀經濟與財務，學成後返回印尼投身科技業。1985年，她與Sugiri同在巴厘銀行（Bank Bali）任職，兩人的合作關係由此展開。1989年，她加入Sigma Cipta Caraka——一家由Sugiri與其他前巴厘銀行同事共同創辦的資訊科技公司。

  ## 印尼首家網際網路服務業者

  1994年，Budiman與Sugiri共同創立Indonet。《Kompas.com》指出，Indonet是印尼第一家網際網路服務提供者（ISP），讓印尼消費者得以首次體驗網路。兩位創辦人其後均於2023年出售了各自持有的Indonet股份。

  ## DCI Indonesia：Tier-IV資料中心

  2011年，Budiman、Sugiri與Han Arming Hanafia三人共同創立PT DCI Indonesia Tbk。據《Kompas.com》報導，DCII自稱是東南亞第一座Tier-IV資料中心的營運業者——Tier-IV是業界四級設施分類標準中的最高等級，代表最高水準的備援能力與可靠性。目前公司旗下設有三處營運據點：芝比通（Cibitung）、卡拉旺（Karawang）及雅加達。

  ## 對印尼數位基礎建設產業的意義

  Budiman躋身《富比士》印尼前十大富豪，反映出隨著數位基礎建設需求持續擴張，印尼資料中心產業所蘊含的龐大財富創造潛力。截至2026年7月14日的排行榜，她依然是印尼十大富豪中唯一的女性。DCII旗下三座營運據點均集中於大雅加達都會區，以及西爪哇省的芝比通與卡拉旺工業走廊。
