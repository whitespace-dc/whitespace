body_en: |
  The Philippines posted record merchandise exports of $64.04 billion in the first eight months of 2026, a 14.8% year-on-year increase led by a 26.2% surge in electronics shipments, and the government is pressing to convert that performance into investment in higher-value chip design and engineering activities.

  Executive Secretary Ralph G. Recto, who chairs the government's Semiconductor and Electronics Industry Advisory Council, said in a statement Sunday that the Philippines has positioned itself for investment beyond assembly and manufacturing. "Investors now see that we are an investment choice not only for manufacturing and assembly, but also for chip design, engineering, research, and innovation," Mr. Recto said.

  ## Record export figures

  Electronic products accounted for $37.1 billion of the $64.04 billion export total in the January-to-August period, according to the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA). In August alone, total merchandise exports reached $9.11 billion, the highest monthly figure since the PSA began tracking the indicator in 1991, with electronics comprising $6.2 billion, or 68.1% of that month's total.

  The Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines Foundation, Inc. (SEIPI), the sector's industry group, raised its full-year 2026 electronics export growth forecast to 10%, which would bring the annual total to approximately $54 billion.

  ## Government roadmap

  Mr. Recto outlined a government roadmap targeting $110 billion in combined semiconductor and electronics exports by 2030. Specific goals include raising the country's share of global semiconductor assembly, testing and packaging from 4% to 7%, and developing a domestic integrated circuit (IC) design industry generating $2 billion to $3 billion in annual exports. The government also aims to capture 4% of global electronics manufacturing services.

  ## Infrastructure and investment

  To anchor advanced manufacturing capacity, the government is developing the Luzon Economic Corridor and a Philippine Technology Corridor as geographic hubs for the sector, Mr. Recto said.

  Toshiba Corp. has announced plans to expand its Philippine operations over the next three years, a move expected to create approximately 3,000 jobs. Mr. Recto cited the expansion as an example of investment in technology-related manufacturing.

  ## Market and policy context

  The United States remained the Philippines' top export market, with $12.3 billion in orders during the measured period, Mr. Recto said. SEIPI attributed the strength of order growth in part to the Corporate Recovery and Tax Incentives for Enterprises to Maximize Opportunities for Reinvigorating the Economy Act, known as CREATE MORE.

  ## Significance

  Electronics shipments represented 68.1% of total Philippine merchandise exports in August, and Mr. Recto cited expanding demand for artificial intelligence (AI), semiconductors, and data centers as forces opening new opportunities for Philippine workers, engineers, and businesses. The industry's current concentration in assembly and packaging makes the push into IC design a significant shift in strategy.

  The government's 2030 target is $110 billion in semiconductor and electronics exports, to be supported by the development of the Luzon Economic Corridor and Philippine Technology Corridor as advanced manufacturing hubs.

  菲律賓2026年前八個月商品出口額達640.4億美元，年增14.8%，電子產品出貨量勁揚26.2%領漲，政府正積極將這份亮眼成績轉化為晶片設計與工程等高附加值領域的投資。

  兼任政府半導體與電子產業諮詢委員會主席的行政長官拉爾夫·雷克托（Ralph G. Recto）在週日的聲明中表示，菲律賓已將自身定位為超越組裝與製造的投資目的地。「投資者現在看到，我們不僅是製造和組裝的投資選擇，也是晶片設計、工程、研發與創新的投資之選，」雷克托說道。

  ## 出口創歷史紀錄

  根據菲律賓統計局（PSA）資料，今年一月至八月間，電子產品出口額達371億美元，占商品出口總額640.4億美元的大宗。單就八月單月而言，商品出口總額達91.1億美元，創下PSA自1991年開始追蹤此指標以來的單月最高紀錄，其中電子產品占62億美元，比重高達68.1%。

  菲律賓半導體與電子產業基金會（SEIPI）已將2026年全年電子出口成長預測上修至10%，若成真，全年總額將達約540億美元。

  ## 政府路線圖

  雷克托概述政府路線圖，目標是在2030年前達成半導體與電子業合計出口1,100億美元。具體目標包括：將菲律賓在全球半導體封裝測試市場的占有率從4%提升至7%，並培育年出口額達20至30億美元的本土積體電路（IC）設計產業。政府亦希望奪下全球電子製造服務4%的市場份額。

  ## 基礎建設與投資

  雷克托表示，為鞏固先進製造能量，政府正開發呂宋經濟走廊與菲律賓科技走廊，作為產業的地理樞紐。

  東芝公司已宣布計劃在未來三年擴大在菲律賓的營運規模，預計創造約3,000個就業機會。雷克托以此作為科技相關製造業投資的具體案例。

  ## 市場與政策背景

  雷克托表示，美國仍是菲律賓最大出口市場，在統計期間訂單金額達123億美元。SEIPI將訂單強勁成長部分歸因於《企業復甦暨稅務激勵最大化再振興經濟機遇法》（CREATE MORE）。

  ## 重要意涵

  電子出口在八月占菲律賓商品出口總額的68.1%。雷克托指出，人工智慧（AI）、半導體與資料中心需求持續擴張，正為菲律賓的工作者、工程師和企業開創新商機。由於菲律賓電子業目前高度集中於組裝與封裝，轉型至IC設計代表策略上的重大轉向。

  政府2030年目標為半導體與電子出口合計達1,100億美元，並將以呂宋經濟走廊與菲律賓科技走廊作為先進製造樞紐予以支撐。
