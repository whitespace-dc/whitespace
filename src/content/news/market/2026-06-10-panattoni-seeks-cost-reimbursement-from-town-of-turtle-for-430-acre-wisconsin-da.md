body_en: |
  Real estate developer Panattoni is seeking a cost reimbursement agreement from the Town of Turtle, Wisconsin, for Project Cornmaze, a proposed 430-acre data center development straddling the towns of Beloit and Turtle in Rock County. The Town of Turtle's board was scheduled to discuss and potentially vote on the agreement on June 9, 2026.

  A cost reimbursement agreement is a financial arrangement in which a local government repays a contractor for specified costs incurred during project development. Panattoni's request places part of its pre-development expenditure on the municipality at a stage when the project has not yet received final approval and faces active local opposition.

  ## Project origins and developer transition

  Project Cornmaze had previously been attributed to Cambrin LLC, an entity that shares its name and email address with documents connected to Meta's confirmed data center in Beaver Dam, Wisconsin, according to the source. In early May 2026, local government officials disclosed that Panattoni had informed them it was assuming control of the project. The company subsequently established two websites dedicated to the development.

  The property involved is owned by Rock Road Companies Inc, headquartered in Janesville, Wisconsin. Pre-development negotiations have been underway since June 2025. Beloit and Turtle sit on the Wisconsin-Illinois border within Rock County.

  ## Community opposition

  Project Cornmaze has attracted significant local resistance. A public Facebook group called "No Beloit Data Center" had accumulated approximately 3,000 members as of the time of the source report. A widely circulated flyer associated with opponents calls on residents to urge Wisconsin's governor to impose a moratorium on data center construction and to press county and local authorities to prohibit non-disclosure agreements, or NDAs, between developers and government bodies.

  ## Panattoni's data center push

  Panattoni was founded in 1986 as an industrial real estate firm and has developed approximately 625 million square feet of office, logistics, and industrial space in the United States alone. In August 2024, the company announced an entry into data center development with a stated target of building 1 gigawatt — enough capacity to power hundreds of thousands of homes and representing a substantial slice of the hyperscale construction market — by 2030. The company listed 21 active data center projects on its website at the time of reporting.

  Panattoni also operates a European unit, which formed a dedicated data center team in July 2025 after hiring several former staff from Colt Data Centre Services.

  ## What it means

  The cost reimbursement request illustrates a dynamic increasingly visible in contested data center siting: developers seeking to transfer early-stage financial exposure to host municipalities before projects have secured community or regulatory endorsement. The opposition in Beloit and Turtle, including calls for NDAs to be banned between developers and local governments, reflects broader tensions in communities where large-scale data center projects are proposed without full public disclosure of the involved parties.

  The Town of Turtle board meeting on June 9 represents the next formal decision point for the agreement.

  所謂費用償還協議，是地方政府就開發商在計畫推進期間所產生的特定費用給予補償的財務安排。Panattoni提出此申請的時間點，正值計畫尚未獲得最終核准、且面臨當地居民積極反對之際，等同於在尚無定案的情況下，將部分前期開支轉嫁至地方政府。

  ## 計畫緣起與開發商更迭

  「玉米迷宮計畫」最初被認為是由Cambrin LLC主導，該實體的名稱與電子郵件地址均出現在與Meta位於威斯康辛州海狸壩（Beaver Dam）的已確認資料中心相關文件之中。2026年5月初，地方政府官員對外披露，Panattoni已告知他們將接手主導此計畫，該公司隨後也建立了兩個專屬於此開發案的網站。

  涉及的土地產權歸屬洛克路公司（Rock Road Companies Inc）所有，該公司總部設於威斯康辛州詹斯維爾市（Janesville）。前期開發談判自2025年6月便已展開。貝洛伊特與龜鎮位於威斯康辛州與伊利諾州交界處，同屬洛克郡。

  ## 社區反對聲浪

  「玉米迷宮計畫」引發了強烈的在地抵制。一個名為「反對貝洛伊特資料中心」（No Beloit Data Center）的公開Facebook社團，在報導時已累積約3,000名成員。反對陣營廣為流傳的一份傳單呼籲居民向威斯康辛州長施壓，要求對資料中心建設實施暫停令，並要求郡及地方主管機關禁止開發商與政府機構之間簽訂保密協議（NDA）。

  ## Panattoni的資料中心布局

  Panattoni成立於1986年，最初為一家工業不動產開發商，在美國境內累計開發面積約達6億2,500萬平方英尺的辦公、物流及工業空間。2024年8月，該公司宣布跨足資料中心開發，目標是在2030年前建成達1吉瓦（GW）的容量——足以供應數十萬戶家庭用電，在超大規模（hyperscale）資料中心建設市場中占有相當分量。報導時，該公司官網列有21個進行中的資料中心計畫。

  Panattoni旗下亦設有歐洲業務部門，該部門在2025年7月從Colt Data Centre Services挖角數名前員工後，正式組建了專屬的資料中心團隊。

  ## 事件意涵

  此次費用償還申請，揭示了一種在具有爭議的資料中心選址案中日益明顯的現象：開發商在計畫尚未獲得社區認可或監管核准之前，便設法將前期財務風險轉移至地主政府。貝洛伊特與龜鎮的反對聲浪，包括呼籲禁止開發商與地方政府簽訂保密協議，折射出更廣泛的社會張力——當大型資料中心計畫在未完整對外揭露相關當事人的情況下提案時，所在社區往往面臨此類衝突。

  龜鎮鎮委員會於6月9日召開的會議，是這份費用償還協議能否推進的下一個關鍵決策節點。
