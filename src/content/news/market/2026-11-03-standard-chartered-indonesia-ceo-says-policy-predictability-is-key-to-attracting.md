body_en: |
  Standard Chartered Indonesia CEO Donny Donosepoetro OBE said investors can accept risk in emerging markets but require consistent, predictable regulatory environments as a precondition for committing capital, framing policy stability as the central variable in Indonesia's competition for foreign investment.

  Speaking at Kompas.com's Naratama program under the theme "Beyond Resilience" on September 15, 2026, Donosepoetro said investor preference for predictability spans the full regulatory framework that touches a given sector: domestic content requirements, import and export rules, production quotas, and domestic market obligations. Policy predictability, he said, means continuity and consistency in those rules over time, giving investors the ability to model and price risk.

  "Investors will prefer policy predictability over risk itself," Donosepoetro said. "Of course, risk still plays a role because there will be a risk-reward calculation." He said that conversely, policy changes that are difficult to predict lead investors to reconsider deploying capital altogether.

  ## Indonesia's economic position

  Indonesia's economy grew 5.29 percent year-on-year in the second quarter of 2026, according to Badan Pusat Statistik (BPS), the country's national statistics agency. Donosepoetro described growth as stable and resilient around the 5 percent range, and cited Indonesia's domestic market scale, human resources, and natural resource endowments as the country's primary investment draws.

  He held up Singapore as a reference for building a trade and investment hub despite limited natural resources, and said Indonesia faces the challenge of translating its broader resource base into a clearly defined value proposition for investors, one covering legal certainty, institutional quality, human capital, connectivity, and strategic location. The task, he said, is assembling those elements into an offering that can compete with other countries in the region.

  ## Downstream investment and supply chain positioning

  Total investment realization in Indonesia in 2025 reached Rp 1,931.2 trillion, according to the Ministry of Investment and Downstream Industries (BKPM). Within that total, downstream processing accounted for Rp 584.1 trillion, or 30.2 percent, growing 43.3 percent year-on-year. Downstream processing, known in Indonesia as hilirisasi, refers to the conversion of raw materials into higher-value goods before export rather than shipping commodities in unprocessed form.

  Donosepoetro said the ongoing shift in global supply chains and the China Plus One strategy, under which multinational companies diversify manufacturing away from a single concentration in China, opens an opportunity for Indonesia. He cautioned, however, that Indonesia should identify the specific supply chain segments where it holds a natural advantage rather than attempting to replicate China's full manufacturing scale.

  "We don't have to become China as it was, pushing all production from global companies into Indonesia, because that is not possible in my view," he said. "We play our part. We have the right to win."

  He identified automotive, electric vehicles (EVs), aircraft, electronics manufacturing, and data centers as sectors through which Indonesia can develop value-added export capacity rather than remaining a raw commodity supplier. The current hilirisasi push, he said, is the starting point for that transition, with the next step being to increase value addition so that Indonesia exports finished or near-finished products rather than only first-stage processed goods.

  ## Data centers, artificial intelligence, and energy demand

  Donosepoetro identified data centers as a significant near-term opportunity for Indonesia within the broader digital economy, saying the country has substantial potential to play a large role in global digital infrastructure. He noted that growing adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) is driving increased demand for computing infrastructure and, in turn, for large-scale power supply, linking digital infrastructure development directly to the energy sector.

  Data centers, he said, present opportunities for countries across ASEAN, and Indonesia's scale positions it to capture a substantial share of that demand.

  ## Energy transition financing

  Energy transition projects emerged as a parallel priority. Donosepoetro cited the 145-megawatt (MW) Cirata Floating Solar Power Plant as a demonstration that Indonesia's energy transition requirements can be converted into commercially structured projects capable of attracting private-sector financing. The Cirata project, a floating solar installation, illustrates the link between investor appetite and project bankability, he said.

  Standard Chartered, he said, is monitoring financing needs across multiple segments of the energy transition, including transmission and distribution networks, green buildings, and broader infrastructure. He noted that data center growth requires large and reliable power supplies, making digital infrastructure development and energy transition increasingly interdependent in Indonesia's investment pipeline.

  ## Bankable projects as a prerequisite for capital

  Converting investor interest into committed financing requires that projects be commercially viable and carry clear financing structures, Donosepoetro said. Investor interest in Indonesian energy transition projects is already significant, he said, but that interest translates into capital only when projects are properly structured to be financeable.

  Standard Chartered has operated in Indonesia for 163 years, according to Donosepoetro, and he positioned the bank's international network and cross-border financing capabilities as a channel for connecting Indonesian projects with global capital sources.

  The 43.3 percent year-on-year growth in downstream investment in 2025 reflects momentum in the supply chain repositioning Donosepoetro outlined. His stated next priority is increasing value addition within that downstream base, moving Indonesia from first-stage processing toward value-added exports in EVs, aircraft, electronics manufacturing, and digital infrastructure.
body_zh: |
  渣打銀行印尼執行長唐尼·多諾塞波特羅（Donny Donosepoetro OBE）表示，投資人能夠接受新興市場的風險，但一致且可預期的監管環境是其承諾投入資本的先決條件，他將政策穩定性定位為印尼爭取外資競爭中的核心變數。

  他於2026年9月15日出席Kompas.com《Naratama》節目，主題為「超越韌性」（Beyond Resilience），表示投資人對可預期性的偏好貫穿整個監管框架，涵蓋國內含量要求、進出口規定、生產配額及國內市場義務。他說，政策可預期性意味著上述規定能夠長期延續且保持一致，讓投資人得以建立模型、為風險定價。

  「投資人寧可要政策可預期性，也不只是單純計算風險，」多諾塞波特羅說。「當然，風險依然存在，因為還是會有風險報酬的計算。」他反之也指出，難以預測的政策變化將使投資人重新考慮是否部署資本。

  ## 印尼的經濟地位

  根據印尼國家統計局（BPS）的數據，2026年第二季印尼經濟年增5.29%。多諾塞波特羅形容此一成長穩健且具韌性，維持在5%左右的水準，並列舉印尼的國內市場規模、人力資源及天然資源稟賦為吸引投資的主要優勢。

  他以新加坡為參考範例，說明如何在天然資源有限的條件下建立起貿易與投資樞紐，並指出印尼面臨的挑戰在於如何將更豐富的資源稟賦轉化為清晰的投資者價值主張——涵蓋法律確定性、制度品質、人力資本、互聯互通及戰略位置。他說，任務在於將這些要素整合成足以與區域其他國家競爭的完整投資方案。

  ## 下游投資與供應鏈布局

  根據印尼投資暨下游工業部（BKPM），2025年印尼整體投資實現總額達1,931.2兆印尼盾。其中，下游加工業占584.1兆印尼盾，比重為30.2%，年增43.3%。印尼稱為「hilirisasi」的下游化政策，是指在出口前將原物料轉化為更高附加價值的商品，而非以未加工形式直接出口大宗商品。

  多諾塞波特羅表示，全球供應鏈持續重組，以及跨國企業分散生產、不再過度集中於中國的「中國加一」（China Plus One）策略，為印尼創造了新機遇。然而他也警告，印尼應找出自身在哪些特定供應鏈環節具有天然優勢，而非試圖複製中國的完整製造規模。

  「我們不必成為過去的中國，把全球企業的所有生產都移入印尼，因為我認為那是不可能的，」他說。「我們扮演好自己的角色。我們有贏的本錢。」

  他點名汽車、電動車（EV）、航空器、電子製造及資料中心等產業，認為印尼可藉此發展高附加價值的出口能力，而不是停留在原料供應商的角色。他表示，當前的下游化推進是這項轉型的起點，下一步是提升附加價值，讓印尼出口成品或近成品，而不只是初級加工品。

  ## 資料中心、人工智慧與能源需求

  多諾塞波特羅將資料中心列為印尼在更廣泛數位經濟中的近期重要機遇，表示該國在全球數位基礎設施中具有龐大潛力，足以扮演舉足輕重的角色。他指出，人工智慧（AI）使用的持續擴大推動了對運算基礎設施的需求，進而帶動大規模電力供應需求，直接將數位基礎設施的發展與能源產業相連結。

  他表示，資料中心為東協各國帶來機遇，而印尼的規模使其有能力在這波需求中拿下相當可觀的份額。

  ## 能源轉型融資

  能源轉型計畫同時成為另一項優先議題。多諾塞波特羅以145百萬瓦（MW）的奇拉達浮動太陽能電廠（Cirata Floating Solar Power Plant）為例，說明印尼的能源轉型需求可以轉化為商業結構完善、足以吸引民間部門融資的具體專案。他說，這座浮動太陽能裝置充分展現了投資人興趣與專案可融資性之間的連結。

  他表示，渣打銀行正密切關注能源轉型多個環節的融資需求，包括輸配電網路、綠建築及更廣泛的基礎設施。他指出，資料中心的成長需要龐大且穩定的電力供應，使數位基礎設施的發展與能源轉型在印尼的投資版圖中日益相互依存。

  ## 可融資專案是吸引資本的先決條件

  多諾塞波特羅表示，要將投資人的興趣轉化為已承諾的融資，專案必須在商業上具有可行性，並備有清晰的融資架構。他說，投資人對印尼能源轉型專案的興趣已相當顯著，但這份興趣唯有在專案經過妥善結構安排、具備可融資性的前提下，才能真正轉化為資本。

  渣打銀行在印尼已有163年的營運歷史。多諾塞波特羅以該行的國際網絡及跨境融資能力為定位，作為連結印尼專案與全球資本來源的橋樑。

  2025年下游投資年增43.3%的亮眼成績，印證了多諾塞波特羅所描繪的供應鏈重新布局動能。他表示，下一個優先目標是在此一下游基礎上進一步提升附加價值，推動印尼從初級加工走向電動車、航空器、電子製造及數位基礎設施等領域的高附加價值出口。
