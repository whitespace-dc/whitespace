body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers, the Singapore-based hyperscale operator whose largest customer is ByteDance, the Chinese owner of TikTok, has put its multibillion-dollar US initial public offering on hold, according to sources familiar with the matter.

  The company had been targeting a raise of as much as US$5 billion through a listing as soon as the fourth quarter, Bloomberg News reported previously. DayOne has since begun reassessing options, including a delay or a smaller listing, as volatility increased around data centre and artificial intelligence stocks. A representative for DayOne declined to comment.

  Sources said DayOne may still pursue an IPO in 2027 if market sentiment improves. Deliberations are ongoing and no final decisions have been made, the sources added, asking not to be identified because the information is not public.

  ## IPO process and advisers

  DayOne filed for the IPO in October and has been working with Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup and BNP Paribas on the listing.

  ## Market conditions driving the reassessment

  Investor doubts about the financial return on large-scale AI infrastructure spending have driven volatility across global markets. The Nasdaq 100 has fallen more than 6% in November, putting it on course for its worst November performance since 2008.

  The retreat has affected several data centre-related listings. In October, Firmus Grid abandoned what would have been one of Australia's largest-ever IPOs. Blackstone-backed AirTrunk is delaying a listing of a real estate investment trust in Singapore until 2027.

  Not all digital infrastructure plays have struggled. Anthropic staged what sources described as a record-breaking US$107.3 billion IPO and private placement, rising 17% on its trading debut on 19 November to become the world's seventh-most valuable company.

  ## US chip access restrictions

  DayOne operates hyperscale data centres — large-scale facilities designed to serve major cloud and technology tenants — in Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, markets that have seen rapid development driven by demand from Chinese technology companies including ByteDance and Alibaba Group. In November, the US government put interim rules in place covering those four countries to restrict China's access to advanced AI chips and servers, requiring data centre operators to obtain a licence to purchase American technology hardware.

  ## Backers

  DayOne's investors include Coatue, Hillhouse, GDS Holdings, SoftBank Vision Fund and Citadel's Ken Griffin.

  ## Significance

  The pause illustrates the pressure facing data centre operators that built their businesses servicing Chinese technology firms, as geopolitical constraints on AI hardware access compound existing uncertainty over AI infrastructure valuations. DayOne's potential path to public markets now points to 2027 at the earliest, subject to improvement in market conditions, according to the sources.
body_zh: |
  總部設於新加坡、以TikTok母公司字節跳動為最大客戶的超大規模資料中心業者DayOne Data Centers，已暫停其數十億美元的美國首次公開募股（IPO）計畫，知悉內情的消息人士透露。

  彭博新聞此前曾報導，DayOne原定最快於今年第四季完成掛牌，目標募資金額高達50億美元。然而，隨著資料中心及人工智慧（AI）類股波動加劇，DayOne已開始重新評估各項選項，包括延後上市或縮減募資規模。DayOne公司代表拒絕就此事發表評論。

  知情人士表示，若市場情緒好轉，DayOne仍可能於2027年尋求IPO。目前各方評估仍在進行中，尚未作出最終決定，消息人士因相關資訊尚未對外公開而要求匿名。

  ## IPO流程與承銷團隊

  DayOne已於10月提交IPO申請，並委託摩根士丹利、摩根大通、美國銀行、花旗集團及法國巴黎銀行共同擔任承銷商。

  ## 市場環境促使重新評估

  投資人對大規模AI基礎設施支出財務回報的疑慮，已在全球市場引發廣泛波動。那斯達克100指數11月以來累計下跌逾6%，有望創下2008年以來最差的11月表現。

  此波回檔已波及多宗資料中心相關企業的上市計畫。10月，Firmus Grid宣告放棄原本有望成為澳洲史上規模最大IPO之一的掛牌計畫；黑石集團旗下的AirTrunk則將在新加坡發行不動產投資信託的計畫押後至2027年。

  並非所有數位基礎設施相關項目都陷入困境。知情人士形容，Anthropic完成了一宗破紀錄的IPO暨私募配售，合計規模達1,073億美元，並於11月19日掛牌首日大漲17%，一舉躍升為全球市值第七大企業。

  ## 美國晶片出口管制

  DayOne在新加坡、馬來西亞、泰國及印尼營運超大規模資料中心——即專門服務大型雲端與科技租戶的大型設施。上述市場近年因字節跳動、阿里巴巴集團等中國科技企業的強勁需求而快速發展。今年11月，美國政府針對上述四個國家頒布臨時規定，限制中國取得先進AI晶片與伺服器，要求資料中心業者在採購美國科技硬體前須事先申請許可證。

  ## 股東背景

  DayOne的投資人包括Coatue、高瓴資本、萬國數據、軟銀願景基金，以及城堡投資的肯·葛里芬（Ken Griffin）。

  ## 意義

  此次暫停IPO充分說明了仰賴中國科技企業業務起家的資料中心業者所承受的壓力——在AI硬體取得受到地緣政治限制的背景下，AI基礎設施估值本已存在的不確定性更因此雪上加霜。根據知情人士說法，DayOne邁向公開市場的路徑，如今最快要到2027年才有可能實現，且前提是市場環境須有所改善。
