body_en: |
  IJM Corp Bhd has emerged as the frontrunner to win a contract worth approximately RM500 million to construct a data centre in Johor for K2 Strategic Infrastructure Malaysia Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Kuok group, with sources telling The Edge the deal is effectively concluded and a formal announcement could follow within days.

  Two independent sources confirmed the outcome to The Edge. "It's in the bag, it's a matter of time before it's announced," one said. A second source said IJM beat several competing companies for the work. IJM executives could not be reached for comment after business hours.

  The award, once formally announced, would push IJM's construction order book past RM11 billion and bring its current financial year order book replenishment to RM3.5 billion, The Edge reported.

  ## K2 and its capacity expansion programme

  K2 Strategic Infrastructure Malaysia is a turnkey provider of hyperscale data centre campuses — large, purpose-built facilities designed to serve cloud operators and major technology tenants at scale — led by managing director and chief executive Kuok Meng Wei, 42, grandson of billionaire Robert Kuok Hock Nien. In addition to its Malaysian assets, K2 operates four data centres in Dublin, Ireland, one in Cikarang, Indonesia, and one in Rayong, Thailand.

  K2's existing Johor presence consists of two facilities in Kulai, designated JHR04 and JHR01-03, with a combined designed capacity of 234 megawatts (MW), the standard unit used to measure a data centre's electrical power draw and, by extension, its processing scale. According to K2's website, the company has 114 MW in operation globally, 82 MW under construction, and approximately 350 MW in what it describes as the pre-construction project phase.

  According to news reports cited by The Edge, Kuok Meng Wei's mandate is to grow K2's total capacity to 1,200 MW by 2030, a tenfold increase from current levels.

  ## IJM's run of major awards

  The K2 contract would be the latest in a string of large wins for IJM's construction arm. The week before The Edge's reporting, IJM signed a RM1.23 billion contract with AT&S Austria Technologie & Systemtechnik (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd to build semiconductor manufacturing facilities at the Kulim Hi-Tech Park in Kedah. That award followed more than RM900 million in semiconductor and medical technology project wins secured since August 2026.

  In April 2026, IJM signed a RM658 million agreement with Sime Darby Property Bhd to construct a hyperscale data centre at Elmina Business Park in Selangor.

  IJM's construction tender book stands at approximately RM18 billion and includes bids for the Penang airport expansion and a RM1 billion civil servant housing development in Nusantara, Indonesia.

  ## Financial performance and listing plans

  IJM posted net profit of RM137.4 million on revenue of RM2.29 billion for the first quarter of its current financial year, ended June 30, 2026, against net profit of RM95.6 million on revenue of RM1.73 billion for the same period a year earlier. The company attributed the improvement primarily to its construction division.

  For the full financial year ending March 31, 2027, IJM said its construction division "is expected to perform strongly in FY2027 underpinned by its strong order book of RM14.5 billion (including our share from joint ventures and associates) comprising hyperscale data centres, advanced manufacturing and warehousing facilities and road infrastructure projects," and added that "the group is confident it will perform better operationally this financial year."

  As of end-June 2026, IJM held deposits, cash and bank balances of RM2.26 billion. Short-term debt commitments stood at RM2.48 billion, long-term borrowings at RM1.25 billion, and retained profits at RM4.11 billion. Net cash flow from operating activities was RM353 million for the quarter.

  IJM plans to list its construction arm, potentially as early as early next year, The Edge reported. The Edge noted that the company could become more aggressive in pursuing new contracts in the months ahead given that planned float.

  ## What it means

  For K2, the Johor contract extends a construction programme already running at 82 MW under development and roughly 350 MW in the pre-construction phase, as the company works toward its 2030 capacity target. For IJM, a confirmed order book exceeding RM11 billion — alongside a tender pipeline of RM18 billion that includes the Penang airport expansion and the Nusantara housing project — sets the terms under which the construction arm is expected to approach its planned public listing early next year.

  ## K2及其產能擴張計畫

  K2 Strategic Infrastructure Malaysia是超大規模資料中心園區的統包服務商——這類大型專用設施專為雲端服務業者及大型科技租戶提供規模化服務——由董事總經理兼執行長郭明威（Kuok Meng Wei，42歲）領導，其祖父為億萬富翁郭鶴年（Robert Kuok Hock Nien）。除馬來西亞資產外，K2在愛爾蘭都柏林設有四座資料中心，另在印尼格拉朗（Cikarang）及泰國羅勇（Rayong）各有一座。

  K2在柔佛現有兩座設施，位於古來（Kulai），分別命名為JHR04及JHR01-03，合計設計容量為234百萬瓦（MW），此為衡量資料中心電力需求乃至其處理規模的標準單位。根據K2官網，公司目前全球在運行容量為114 MW，在建中的有82 MW，另有約350 MW處於其所稱的「施工前期」階段。

  據《邊緣》引述的新聞報導，郭明威的目標是在2030年前將K2總容量擴增至1,200 MW，約為現有規模的十倍。

  ## IJM連獲重大合約

  K2的這筆合約將是IJM建築部門近期一系列重大訂單中的最新一筆。就在《邊緣》報導前一週，IJM與奧地利科技暨系統技術（馬來西亞）有限公司（AT&S Austria Technologie & Systemtechnik (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd）簽署一份12.3億令吉的合約，將在吉打州居林高科技園區（Kulim Hi-Tech Park）興建半導體製造設施。此訂單更早之前，IJM自2026年8月起已獲得逾9億令吉的半導體及醫療科技專案合約。

  2026年4月，IJM與森那美產業（Sime Darby Property Bhd）簽署一份6.58億令吉的協議，將在雪蘭莪州依明尼亞商業園（Elmina Business Park）興建一座超大規模資料中心。

  IJM目前建築工程投標案件總額約達180億令吉，涵蓋檳城機場擴建工程，以及位於印尼努山塔拉（Nusantara）、規模達10億令吉的公務員住宅開發案。

  ## 財務表現與上市計畫

  IJM截至2026年6月30日止的本財政年度首季，淨利為1.374億令吉，營收為22.9億令吉；相較去年同期淨利9,560萬令吉、營收17.3億令吉，成長顯著。公司將此一改善主要歸因於建築部門的表現。

  就截至2027年3月31日的整個財政年度，IJM表示，建築部門「預計將在2027財年繳出強勁成績，以145億令吉的強大訂單簿（含合資及聯營公司份額）為後盾，業務範疇涵蓋超大規模資料中心、先進製造與倉儲設施以及道路基礎建設專案」，並補充「集團有信心本財政年度的整體營運表現將優於上一年度」。

  截至2026年6月底，IJM持有存款、現金及銀行結餘共22.6億令吉。短期債務承諾為24.8億令吉，長期借款為12.5億令吉，保留盈餘為41.1億令吉。當季來自營運活動的淨現金流入為3.53億令吉。

  據《邊緣》報導，IJM計畫將其建築部門分拆上市，最快可能於明年初掛牌。《邊緣》並指出，鑑於此一上市計畫，公司未來數月可能將更積極爭取新合約。

  ## 後續影響

  對K2而言，柔佛這筆合約延伸了其正在推進的建設計畫——目前已有82 MW在建，另有約350 MW處於施工前期階段——使公司朝2030年產能目標持續邁進。對IJM而言，確認訂單簿突破110億令吉，加上達180億令吉且涵蓋檳城機場擴建及努山塔拉住宅案的投標管線，為建築部門明年初的計畫上市奠定了重要基礎。
