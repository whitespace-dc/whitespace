body_en: |
  Microsoft launched its largest India data center in Hyderabad on Thursday, signing Adani Group and HDFC Bank as early customers and raising its total cloud regions in the country to four.

  The facility, called India South Central, joins existing Microsoft cloud regions in Pune, Chennai, and Mumbai. The company describes itself as India's largest cloud-computing provider.

  Microsoft has committed approximately $20.5 billion to expand its India operations, according to the company. The investment is premised on a market of more than one billion internet users and what the company describes as one of the world's deepest pools of technology talent.

  ## Customers and cloud performance

  Adani Group, the Indian conglomerate, and HDFC Bank, one of India's largest private lenders, are among the first confirmed users of the new facility. Microsoft did not disclose the terms of those agreements.

  Azure, Microsoft's cloud-computing division, has recorded double-digit revenue growth in India for each of the past two years, the company said. Microsoft also operates two data centers in India in partnership with Jio, the telecommunications and digital services arm of billionaire Mukesh Ambani's Reliance Industries.

  ## Why the investment

  Microsoft India President Puneet Chandok said creating value with artificial intelligence "requires trusted infrastructure close to where data lives, teams work and decisions are made," and described the Hyderabad facility as "a critical part of that foundation."

  The Hyderabad opening reflects a broader race among large technology companies to secure AI infrastructure capacity in India. Alphabet, Google's parent company, and Amazon are also directing significant capital toward Indian data center capacity, drawn by the country's large potential base of AI users, according to Reuters.

  ## Competitive and regulatory environment

  Not all expansion efforts in India have proceeded without friction. Environmentalists have accused Indian authorities of approving a planned Google data center hub without adequately assessing risks to local water supplies and wildlife. The government has rejected those allegations.

  With four cloud regions now operational and two additional facilities running through the Jio partnership, Microsoft has the broadest declared infrastructure footprint among the major cloud providers currently active in India.

  微軟週四在印度海德拉巴正式啟用其規模最大的印度資料中心，並簽下阿達尼集團與HDFC銀行作為首批客戶，使其在印度的雲端區域總數增至四個。

  這座新設施命名為「印度中南部」，與微軟現有位於浦那、清奈及孟買的三個雲端區域共同構成完整布局。微軟自稱是印度規模最大的雲端運算供應商。

  根據微軟公司的說法，其已承諾投入約205億美元擴展在印度的業務。這項投資立基於印度逾10億網路用戶的龐大市場，以及微軟所形容的全球數一數二深厚的科技人才庫。

  ## 客戶與雲端表現

  印度大型企業集團阿達尼集團，以及印度規模最大的民營銀行之一HDFC銀行，均已確認成為這座新設施的首批用戶。微軟並未披露相關合約條款。

  微軟表示，旗下雲端運算部門Azure在過去兩年中，每年皆在印度錄得雙位數的營收成長。此外，微軟也在印度與電信暨數位服務業者吉歐（Jio）合作，共同經營兩座資料中心；吉歐隸屬於印度億萬富翁穆克什·安巴尼（Mukesh Ambani）旗下的信實工業（Reliance Industries）。

  ## 投資動機

  微軟印度總裁普尼特·錢多克（Puneet Chandok）表示，運用人工智慧創造價值「需要貼近資料所在地、團隊工作場所與決策現場的可信賴基礎設施」，並形容海德拉巴設施是「這一基礎的關鍵組成部分」。

  海德拉巴資料中心的啟用，反映出大型科技公司爭相在印度搶占人工智慧基礎設施的廣泛趨勢。據路透社報導，Alphabet（Google母公司）與亞馬遜也正將大量資本投入印度資料中心建設，看好該國龐大的潛在人工智慧用戶群。

  ## 競爭格局與監管環境

  然而，並非所有在印度的擴張計畫都進展順遂。環保人士指控印度當局在未充分評估當地水資源與野生動物風險的情況下，便核准了Google一處規劃中的資料中心園區；對此，印度政府已予以否認。

  目前，微軟在印度已有四個雲端區域正式運作，並透過與吉歐的合作關係另行經營兩座設施，在目前活躍於印度市場的各大主要雲端供應商中，擁有宣布在案的最廣泛基礎設施版圖。
