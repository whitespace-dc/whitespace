body_en: |
  CambridgeNexus (CNEX), a company building enterprise AI compute infrastructure platforms, announced on July 18 its entry into the Taiwan market, naming Taiwan as one of its Asian strategic hubs and the starting point for an AI compute infrastructure and artificial intelligence ecosystem push across the region.

  ## AI Factory-as-a-Service Model

  CNEX's core offering is what it calls "AI Factory-as-a-Service," a model intended to let enterprises access high-performance compute capacity and related services without constructing their own GPU (graphics processing unit) data centers or bearing independent costs for facilities, power, cooling, networking, and operations integration, the company said. CNEX frames the model around the premise that enterprise AI competition has shifted from access to AI models toward access to stable, scalable, and operable compute infrastructure.

  The company said the critical capability enterprises will need going forward is not simply owning more GPUs, but reliably delivering what it calls "AI Capacity" — the sustained, operational throughput needed to run AI applications at scale.

  ## AI Compute Passport

  Among the products announced alongside the Taiwan launch is the "AI Compute Passport," which CNEX describes as a single credential that allows an enterprise to flexibly connect to different AI factory nodes according to demand, accessing compute and foundry services on request. The company said the passport is designed to increase deployment efficiency and resource-allocation flexibility across its network of facilities.

  ## Product Ecosystem

  CNEX's platform ecosystem comprises three components: AI Foundry, Cortex, and AboveCloud. AboveCloud is powered by ProphetStor and originates from Silicon Valley, with a focus on AI cloud and GPU resource optimization, according to the company. Together, the company said, these components are intended to cover the full range from compute procurement and platform management to service support, forming what it calls an AI factory architecture capable of supporting enterprise-scale deployment.

  ## Taiwan Rationale and Infrastructure Sites

  CNEX cited Taiwan's ICT (information and communications technology) supply chain, engineering talent base, and enterprise digital transformation demand as the basis for selecting the island as a regional hub. The company said it has designated a Kaohsiung compute center and Taiwan AI Factories as key infrastructure sites within its Asian buildout. No capacity figures or facility specifications were disclosed.

  ## Leadership and Team

  CNEX founder and CEO Ken Huang has more than 29 years of experience in enterprise information technology and AI infrastructure, according to the company. Prior roles include positions at IBM, JPMorgan Chase, Partners HealthCare, and Forrester Research, with work concentrated on large-scale enterprise architecture, cloud platforms, and AI data center construction. The company said its AI infrastructure team includes senior engineers and technical experts from North America, Asia, and Europe.

  ## Dual-Headquarters Structure

  Huang described CNEX's operating model as a dual-core structure: the United States serves as the global AI Factory operational and innovation headquarters, while Taiwan functions as the Asia-Pacific strategic center for market expansion, industry partnerships, and next-generation AI infrastructure development, the company said.

  ## NVIDIA Discussions

  CNEX said it has begun preliminary discussions with the NVIDIA Cloud Partner Program (NCP) team to explore potential future cooperation. The company described these as initial exchanges and did not characterize them as a concluded partnership or commercial agreement.

  ## Funding Round

  CNEX said it plans to launch a new funding round in conjunction with its Taiwan and Asia expansion to support AI factories, compute nodes, platform capabilities, and regional growth. The company did not disclose a target size, lead investors, or timeline for the round.

  ## Significance for Taiwan and the Region

  The announcement is an early-stage market entry by a company positioning compute infrastructure — rather than AI models or cloud services — as the next layer of digital buildout that enterprises and regions will compete over. For Taiwan, CNEX's entry represents an external bet that the island's semiconductor and supply chain ecosystem can anchor enterprise AI infrastructure deployment across the Asia-Pacific market.

  CNEX said it will continue to develop infrastructure platforms capable of supporting AI operations at scale, with Taiwan and Asia as key focus markets, as it moves forward with its funding round and regional expansion.
body_zh: |
  **CambridgeNexus（CNEX）**於7月18日宣布正式進入台灣市場，將台灣命名為其亞洲策略樞紐之一，並以此作為推動亞洲地區AI運算基礎設施與人工智慧生態系布局的起點。

  ## AI工廠即服務模式

  CNEX的核心服務稱為「AI工廠即服務」（AI Factory-as-a-Service），該公司表示，這套模式旨在讓企業無須自行興建GPU（圖形處理器）資料中心，或獨力承擔設施、電力、散熱、網路與營運整合等成本，即可取得高效能運算產能及相關服務。CNEX將此模式定位在一個前提之上：企業在AI領域的競爭，已從能否取得AI模型，轉向能否掌握穩定、可擴充且可運維的運算基礎設施。

  該公司表示，企業未來真正需要的關鍵能力，並非單純擁有更多GPU，而是能夠持續可靠地提供其所稱的「AI產能」——即大規模運行AI應用所需、持續且可實際運作的吞吐量。

  ## AI運算通行證

  此次台灣發布會同步推出的產品之一，是「AI運算通行證」（AI Compute Passport）。CNEX將其描述為一種單一憑證，讓企業能夠依據需求彈性接取不同的AI工廠節點，隨時取用運算與代工服務。該公司表示，這張通行證的設計目的，在於提升企業在旗下設施網路中的部署效率與資源調配彈性。

  ## 產品生態系

  CNEX的平台生態系由三大元件組成：AI Foundry、Cortex，以及AboveCloud。其中AboveCloud由ProphetStor提供技術支援，源自矽谷，專注於AI雲端與GPU資源優化。該公司表示，這三大元件合力覆蓋從運算採購、平台管理到服務支援的完整範疇，構成其所稱能夠支援企業規模部署的AI工廠架構。

  ## 選擇台灣的理由與基礎設施據點

  CNEX指出，台灣完備的ICT（資訊與通訊技術）供應鏈、優質的工程人才庫，以及企業數位轉型的旺盛需求，是該公司將台灣選定為區域樞紐的依據。該公司表示，已將高雄運算中心與台灣AI工廠列為亞洲布局中的關鍵基礎設施據點。目前並未揭露任何產能數字或設施規格。

  ## 領導團隊

  CNEX創辦人兼執行長Ken Huang擁有逾29年企業資訊科技與AI基礎設施領域的資歷。過去任職單位包括IBM、摩根大通（JPMorgan Chase）、Partners HealthCare及佛瑞斯特研究（Forrester Research），工作重心集中於大型企業架構、雲端平台與AI資料中心建置。該公司表示，其AI基礎設施團隊涵蓋來自北美、亞洲及歐洲的資深工程師與技術專家。

  ## 雙核心總部架構

  Huang將CNEX的營運模式描述為雙核心架構：美國作為全球AI工廠的營運與創新總部，台灣則作為亞太策略中心，負責市場拓展、產業夥伴關係建立，以及下一代AI基礎設施的研發。

  ## 與輝達的洽談

  CNEX表示，已開始與輝達雲端合作夥伴計畫（NVIDIA Cloud Partner Program，NCP）團隊進行初步洽談，探討未來潛在合作的可能性。該公司將這些接觸定性為初期交流，並未將其描述為已完成的夥伴關係或商業協議。

  ## 融資計畫

  CNEX表示，計畫配合台灣與亞洲市場的擴張，發起新一輪融資，以支援AI工廠建置、運算節點擴充、平台能力強化及區域業務成長。該公司並未揭露目標融資規模、領投方或時程。

  ## 對台灣與區域的意義

  這次公告，是一家以運算基礎設施——而非AI模型或雲端服務——定位為企業與各地區將競相角逐之下一層數位建設的公司，在發展初期進入市場的舉動。對台灣而言，CNEX的進場代表一個外部押注：押注台灣的半導體與供應鏈生態系，有能力作為企業AI基礎設施在亞太市場部署的錨點。

  CNEX表示，在推進融資計畫與區域擴張的過程中，將持續開發能夠支援大規模AI營運的基礎設施平台，並以台灣與亞洲作為重點市場。
