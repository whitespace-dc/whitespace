body_en: |
  Tokyu Land Corporation and Tokyo-based Flower Communications plan to bring a 15 MW colocation data center online in Hokkaido's Ishikari City in August 2026, running on 100% renewable energy drawn from an on-site solar installation, Nikkei xTECH reported.

  The facility, named Ishikari Renewable Energy Data Center No. 1, sits on a roughly 14,200-square-meter site in the Ishikari Bay New Port Area, which Nikkei xTECH describes as one of Hokkaido's largest industrial zones. The three-story steel-frame building covers approximately 10,000 square meters of total floor area and carries a power reception capacity of 15 MW — a scale the publication classifies as mid-sized. Tokyo's Asai Ken Architectural Research Institute designed the building; Sapporo-based Nakayamagumi handled construction. The structure completed in March 2026.

  ## Layout and Tenancy Model

  The ground floor serves as an equipment floor housing mechanical and electrical systems. The second and third floors together contain six data hall sections, each capable of holding approximately 190 server racks. The developers will lease those sections to data center operators, with tenants supplying their own servers and racks, Nikkei xTECH reported. Broadband Tower has already committed to occupying space in the facility and will also participate in its operations, according to the report.

  ## Renewable Power Supply

  The data center will draw electricity from an adjacent solar power plant to achieve 100% renewable energy operation. To address Hokkaido's heavy winter snowfall, the facility uses a vertical solar panel mounting system — a configuration that allows the panels to generate power even when snow accumulates, Nikkei xTECH reported. A dedicated private transmission line will carry the renewable power directly into the facility, according to a section heading in the publication's article.

  ## Ishikari as a Renewable Energy Data Center Cluster

  Ishikari City hosts concentrations of offshore wind generation equipment and other renewable energy infrastructure, making it an attractive location for operators seeking clean power supply, Nikkei xTECH reported. The publication noted that multiple data center projects using renewable energy are currently under construction in the city, with Ishikari Renewable Energy Data Center No. 1 among the first scheduled to open. The facility is set to begin operations in August 2026.

  ## 東急不動產與Flower Communications將於8月在石狩開設百分百再生能源資料中心

  東急不動產與總部位於東京的Flower Communications計畫於2026年8月在北海道石狩市正式啟用一座15MW的共置資料中心，電力來源百分之百為現地太陽能設施所供應的再生能源，此消息由《日經xTECH》報導。

  這座設施命名為「石狩再生能源資料中心一號」，坐落於面積約14,200平方公尺的石狩灣新港區內——《日經xTECH》將此工業區描述為北海道規模最大的工業區之一。建築本體為三層樓鋼骨結構，總樓地板面積約10,000平方公尺，受電容量達15MW，《日經xTECH》將其規模定位為中型資料中心。建築設計由東京的淺井謙建築研究所負責，施工則由札幌的中山組承包，整棟建物已於2026年3月竣工。

  ## 樓層配置與租賃模式

  一樓作為設備層，設置機械與電氣系統。二樓與三樓共劃分為六個機房區塊，每個區塊可容納約190個伺服器機架。開發業者將以區塊為單位出租給資料中心業者，承租方自行提供伺服器與機架，《日經xTECH》如此報導。Broadband Tower已確認將進駐該設施，並將參與營運，據該報導指出。

  ## 再生能源供電架構

  這座資料中心將從鄰近的太陽能電廠引電，以實現百分之百再生能源的運營目標。為因應北海道冬季積雪量大的特性，設施採用垂直式太陽能板安裝系統，此配置即便在積雪情況下仍可持續發電，《日經xTECH》報導。根據該媒體文章中的段落標題，一條專用的自用輸電線路將直接將再生電力輸送至設施內部。

  ## 石狩：再生能源資料中心聚落的成形

  石狩市境內匯集了離岸風力發電設備及其他再生能源基礎設施，使其成為尋求潔淨電力來源的業者眼中極具吸引力的選址，《日經xTECH》報導。該媒體指出，目前已有多個採用再生能源的資料中心專案在石狩市同步興建中，「石狩再生能源資料中心一號」是其中預計最早開幕的設施之一。這座資料中心預計於2026年8月正式啟用。
