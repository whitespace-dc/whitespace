body_en: |
  Melbourne has overtaken Sydney in total forecast data centre pipeline capacity, according to the second edition of the Australian Data Centre Forecast Report released by Data Centres Australia and produced with DC Byte. The report projects national operational capacity will reach 3.9 gigawatts (GW) — a measure of sustained electrical power draw — by 2030, up from 1.6 GW currently across 163 tracked facilities.

  The development pipeline has expanded sharply over the six months covered by the report, with 5.2 GW added to the total national forecast since April. Operational capacity rose by 0.1 GW during that period, while capacity under construction doubled from the previous period, split evenly between Sydney and Melbourne.

  The most significant shift was projects moving from early-stage planning into committed development. Melbourne recorded 1.2 GW advancing into the committed category during the period, compared with 0.7 GW in Sydney.

  ## Planning approvals driving Melbourne's advance

  The report attributes Melbourne's lead to faster planning and approvals processes. It links the movement of projects into committed status to the Victorian Government's Development Facilitation Program, which it contrasts with the New South Wales State Significant Development process.

  Melbourne's total forecast pipeline increased by 1.7 GW over the period, with only a 0.3 GW rise in early-stage projects. The report says that pattern indicates most of the increase came from schemes progressing through the development process rather than from newly announced sites. It also cites what it describes as an "open for business" message from the Victorian Government as a factor.

  ## Sydney pipeline contracts

  Sydney recorded a decline in forecast pipeline volume. Its total pipeline dropped by 3.6 GW, while early-stage capacity fell by 4.4 GW.

  The report says the decline likely reflects a combination of energy constraints, less certain policy approval timelines, and recent policy announcements in New South Wales. It says investors and developers are becoming more selective about where they place large-scale digital infrastructure projects.

  ## Geographic spread beyond the two main hubs

  The report also documents a broader geographic shift in data centre development. The share of total forecast pipeline outside Sydney and Melbourne has risen from 19 percent to 31 percent over two years. Early-stage projects have been recorded in Queensland, Tasmania, South Australia, and Western Australia.

  The report says that trend suggests developers are looking beyond the two largest east coast markets as power availability, planning settings, and land options shape site selection. It notes that state governments could face both investment opportunities and pressure to prepare electricity networks, planning frameworks, and industrial land for data-intensive facilities.

  ## Demand and supply constraints

  The projected rise to 3.9 GW of operational capacity by 2030 is tied, according to the report, to growing demand for cloud services and artificial intelligence compute. The report notes that supply growth will not depend on demand alone, citing energy access, capital availability, and national standards for AI infrastructure as factors that could shape the market.

  With 97 additional facilities forecast by 2030, the report says Australia's data centre sector remains on track for substantial expansion, though growth will not be distributed evenly across the country. It concludes that policy settings are emerging as a major differentiator between states, with faster approvals and clearer planning signals appearing to benefit Melbourne and a broader group of regional markets.

  ## 墨爾本超越雪梨，躍居澳洲資料中心開發管線龍頭

  根據「澳洲資料中心」（Data Centres Australia）與DC Byte合作發布的第二版《澳洲資料中心預測報告》，墨爾本在資料中心預測開發管線總容量上已超越雪梨。報告預測，全國資料中心運營容量將從目前163座追蹤設施的1.6吉瓦（GW）——以持續電力需求計——成長至2030年的3.9吉瓦。

  報告所涵蓋的六個月期間內，開發管線急速擴張，全國預測總容量自四月起新增5.2吉瓦。同期運營容量增加0.1吉瓦，而在建容量則較前期翻倍，雪梨與墨爾本各占一半。

  最值得關注的變化，是多項計畫從早期規劃階段正式進入確定開發。同期墨爾本有1.2吉瓦的項目晉升至「確定」類別，雪梨則為0.7吉瓦。

  ## 規劃審批加速推動墨爾本領先

  報告將墨爾本的領先優勢歸因於更快速的規劃與審批流程。報告指出，項目得以進入確定開發狀態，與維多利亞州政府推行的「開發促進計畫」（Development Facilitation Program）密切相關，並將其與新南威爾斯州的「重大州級開發」（State Significant Development）程序相比較。

  墨爾本的預測開發管線總量在此期間增加1.7吉瓦，其中早期階段項目僅增加0.3吉瓦。報告指出，這一模式說明大部分增量來自既有計畫在開發流程中持續推進，而非新宣布的開發場址。報告亦將維多利亞州政府釋出的「歡迎投資」訊號列為推動因素之一。

  ## 雪梨開發管線收縮

  雪梨的預測開發管線總量則出現下滑，整體管線減少3.6吉瓦，早期階段容量更下降4.4吉瓦。

  報告指出，此次下滑可能源於能源供應限制、政策審批時程不確定性，以及新南威爾斯州近期的相關政策宣示等多重因素交互影響。報告也指出，投資方與開發商在大型數位基礎設施的選址決策上愈趨謹慎。

  ## 開發版圖擴及兩大樞紐以外地區

  報告同時記錄了資料中心開發在地理上的更廣泛擴散趨勢。過去兩年間，雪梨與墨爾本以外地區占全國預測開發管線總量的比重，已從19%上升至31%。昆士蘭州、塔斯馬尼亞州、南澳大利亞州及西澳大利亞州均出現早期階段開發項目的紀錄。

  報告認為，這一趨勢顯示開發商正將目光投向兩大東岸主要市場以外的地區，電力供應條件、規劃政策環境與土地取得選項，共同左右著選址決策。報告也指出，各州政府既面臨吸引投資的機遇，也承受著為資料密集型設施預先整備電網、規劃框架及工業用地的壓力。

  ## 需求成長與供給限制

  報告指出，運營容量預計在2030年達到3.9吉瓦，背後的驅動力來自雲端服務與人工智慧運算需求的持續攀升。然而報告也強調，供給成長並非單純取決於需求，能源取得、資本供給，以及人工智慧基礎設施的國家標準，均可能對市場走向產生深遠影響。

  報告預測，2030年前將新增97座設施，澳洲資料中心產業的大幅擴張步伐仍在持續，但成長分布並不均衡。報告最終結論指出，各州的政策環境正日益成為競爭分野的關鍵，審批速度更快、規劃訊號更明確的市場——以墨爾本為首，以及更廣泛的區域市場——似乎正從中受益。
