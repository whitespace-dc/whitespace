body_en: |
  Google has agreed to pay SpaceX approximately $920 million per month for access to artificial intelligence computing infrastructure, SpaceX disclosed in a U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) filing dated June 5. The deal covers roughly 110,000 NVIDIA graphics processing units (GPUs) housed in SpaceX data centers and runs from October 2026 through June 2029.

  ## Contract terms and staging

  The arrangement ramps up gradually before the full contract period begins. Through September 2026, SpaceX will provide capacity at discounted rates on a staged schedule. Full contract pricing takes effect in October 2026.

  If SpaceX fails to deliver the promised GPU count by September 30, 2026, Google has the right to terminate immediately or, following a one-month grace period, accept whatever capacity is available at a proportionally reduced rate. From December 31, 2026, either party may exit the agreement with 90 days' notice.

  ## Google cites Gemini Enterprise demand

  A Google Cloud spokesperson told outlets including TechCrunch that the contract was signed to "secure bridging capacity to meet surging demand from customers for the Gemini Enterprise agent platform that far exceeded expectations." Gemini Enterprise, announced in October 2025, is a subscription service for large enterprises built around Google's Gemini AI models.

  Google's description of the deal as "bridging capacity" indicates the company is treating SpaceX's infrastructure as a near-term supplement rather than a permanent arrangement, while it works to expand its own data center footprint.

  ## SpaceX's second major infrastructure deal

  The Google agreement is SpaceX's second large infrastructure contract disclosed in recent months. In May, Anthropic announced it would use computing capacity at SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center for $1.25 billion per month. SpaceX also completed a merger with xAI in February; the combined entity was valued at $1.75 trillion following that transaction, according to the ITmedia report.

  The back-to-back infrastructure deals position SpaceX as a significant third-party AI computing provider operating alongside the major cloud platforms. The next material milestone under the Google contract is SpaceX's obligation to deliver the agreed GPU allocation by September 30, 2026.

  ## 合約條款與分階段部署

  正式合約期開始前，雙方設有過渡安排。在2026年9月底前，SpaceX將按分階段時程，以折扣價格提供算力。2026年10月起，正式合約定價將全面生效。

  若SpaceX未能於2026年9月30日前完成承諾的GPU數量交付，Google有權立即終止合約；或在一個月寬限期後，按實際提供算力的比例調降費率繼續執行。自2026年12月31日起，任一方均可提前90天通知退出協議。

  ## Google援引Gemini Enterprise需求

  Google Cloud發言人向TechCrunch等媒體表示，此合約係為「確保過渡期算力，以因應客戶對Gemini Enterprise代理人平台的爆發性需求——該需求遠遠超出預期」而簽訂。Gemini Enterprise於2025年10月發布，是一項以Google Gemini AI模型為核心、面向大型企業的訂閱制服務。

  Google將此合約定位為「過渡算力」，顯示該公司將SpaceX的基礎設施視為近期補充方案而非長期安排，同時持續擴充自有資料中心規模。

  ## SpaceX第二筆重大基礎設施合約

  這項與Google簽訂的協議，是SpaceX近幾個月內第二筆對外揭露的大型基礎設施合約。今年5月，Anthropic宣布將使用SpaceX旗下Colossus 1資料中心的算力，月費達12.5億美元。此外，SpaceX亦於今年2月完成與xAI的合併；據ITmedia報導，合併後的合體估值達1.75兆美元。

  連續簽下多筆基礎設施合約，使SpaceX躍升為舉足輕重的第三方AI算力供應商，得以與各大雲端平台並列競逐。在Google合約的下一個關鍵里程碑方面，SpaceX須於2026年9月30日前完成約定GPU配額的交付義務。
