  ## Data centre spending drives record quarterly jump

  Australian private capital spending rose 12.2% in the September quarter to an inflation-adjusted A$59.5 billion ($39.37 billion), the Australian Bureau of Statistics reported Thursday, producing the country's largest rise in business investment since 2011, when the mining boom was at its peak. The result far exceeded market forecasts of a 4.4% gain.

  The surge was led by an 87% jump in spending on information media and telecommunications equipment, which the ABS attributed to record investment in server racks and processing gear for data centres. Much of that equipment was imported, the bureau noted, making it an offsetting drag on gross domestic product growth.

  ## Broader capital spending breakdown

  Overall spending on plant and machinery rose 23.1% in the quarter. Spending on buildings and structures increased a comparatively modest 1.7%. An ABS survey of firms showed they planned to spend A$223.7 billion in the year to June 2027, 11.8% higher than the previous estimate.

  ## ICT spending could nearly double, analyst says

  Abhijit Surya, senior Asia-Pacific economist at Capital Economics, said spending on information and communications technology could nearly double in the 2027 financial year. He contrasted that trajectory with the rest of the economy, where capital spending was "struggling to tread water."

  ## Rate cut timeline under pressure

  The data lands as the Reserve Bank of Australia — the country's central bank — has already raised interest rates five times this year, triggering what the ABS described as the biggest housing downturn in 40 years and weighing on consumer spending and the labour market. Surya said the capacity pressures implied by the data centre investment surge suggested the RBA "won't be in a rush to cut rates anytime soon."

  Markets are still pricing the next rate move as a cut, but do not fully price a first reduction until August. A June cut is currently seen as an 83% probability. The Australian dollar rose 0.2% to US$0.6640 on the data, while three-year government bond futures reversed earlier gains to fall three ticks to 95.51.

  ## What this means for the sector

  The scale of the data centre investment — large enough to shift the headline national capital spending figure by several percentage points on its own — signals that Australia has become a substantial destination for digital infrastructure buildout. The import-heavy nature of the spending, with server and processing equipment sourced from abroad, means the economic multiplier for Australia is narrower than a comparable surge in domestically sourced capital goods, but the structural commitment to local capacity is reflected in the forward spending plans firms reported to the ABS.

  Firms surveyed by the ABS plan to spend A$223.7 billion across the full year to June 2027, 11.8% above their prior forecast for that period.

  ## 資料中心支出推動創紀錄單季漲幅

  澳洲統計局（ABS）週四公布，澳洲私人資本支出在今年第三季（七至九月）經通膨調整後增長12.2%，達到595億澳幣（折合393.7億美元），創下澳洲自2011年礦業榮景高峰以來最大的商業投資成長幅度，遠超市場預期的4.4%漲幅。

  此波漲勢由資訊媒體與電信設備支出的87%暴增帶動，澳洲統計局將此歸因於資料中心伺服器機架與處理設備的創紀錄投資。統計局並指出，這批設備大多仰賴進口，因而成為抵銷國內生產毛額（GDP）成長的拖累因素。

  ## 整體資本支出細項

  整體機器及設備支出在該季上漲23.1%，建築與廠房支出則相對溫和，僅增加1.7%。澳洲統計局的企業調查顯示，受訪企業計畫在截至2027年6月的年度內，共支出2,237億澳幣，較上一次估計高出11.8%。

  ## 分析師：資通訊科技支出或將翻近一倍

  凱投宏觀（Capital Economics）亞太區資深經濟學家阿比吉特．蘇利亞（Abhijit Surya）表示，資訊通訊科技支出在2027財政年度有可能翻近一倍。他並以此與整體經濟表現做出對比——在資料中心以外的領域，資本支出仍在「苦苦維持不跌」。

  ## 降息時程承壓

  此數據發布之際，澳洲央行澳洲儲備銀行（Reserve Bank of Australia，RBA）今年已五度升息，澳洲統計局形容，此舉引發了40年來最嚴重的房市下行，並拖累消費者支出與就業市場。蘇利亞表示，資料中心投資熱潮所暗示的產能壓力，意味著澳洲儲備銀行「短期內不會急於降息」。

  市場目前仍將下一次利率動向定價為降息，但首次降息的完整定價要到八月才出現。六月降息目前被視為有83%的機率。受此數據激勵，澳幣兌美元上漲0.2%，報0.6640美元；三年期公債期貨則反轉先前漲勢，下跌三個跳動點，報95.51。

  ## 對產業的意義

  此波資料中心投資規模龐大，光憑一己之力便足以令全國資本支出的總體數字出現數個百分點的位移，意味著澳洲已成為數位基礎建設部署的重要目的地。然而，由於伺服器與處理設備主要向海外採購，以進口為主的支出結構，使澳洲所能獲得的經濟乘數效益，遠不及同等規模的本土資本財投資；即便如此，業界對本地產能的長期承諾，仍充分體現在企業向澳洲統計局申報的未來支出計畫之中。

  受訪企業計畫在截至2027年6月的完整財政年度內，共支出2,237億澳幣，較此前對同期的預測高出11.8%。
