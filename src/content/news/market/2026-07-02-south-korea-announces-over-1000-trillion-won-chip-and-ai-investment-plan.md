body_en: |
  South Korea's President Lee Jae Myung on Monday announced a national industrial strategy centered on semiconductors and artificial intelligence, with Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix pledging 800 trillion won to build new chip fabrication clusters in the country's southwestern region while the government targets more than 1,000 trillion won in AI data center investment by 2035.

  ## Three-Pillar Framework

  The plan rests on three pillars: semiconductors, physical AI, and data centers, Lee said at an announcement flanked by the chairmen of both Samsung and SK Hynix. "We must secure the core elements of AI faster than any other country," Lee said, according to Reuters.

  Industry Minister Kim Jung-kwan said Samsung and SK Hynix will each build two new chip fabrication sites in southwestern South Korea, funded by the combined 800 trillion won private commitment. Samsung Chairman Jay Y. Lee said his company has selected Gwangju, in the southwest, as the location for its new chip cluster. SK Hynix Chairman Chey Tae-won said his company needs more time to finalize a site and secure necessary infrastructure in the southwestern region.

  The city of Gwangju and South Jeolla Province are also expected to contribute between 5 trillion and 20 trillion won toward the projects, President Lee said. A further 81 trillion won is anticipated for a chip packaging cluster in the Chungcheong region near Seoul.

  ## AI Data Center Targets

  Science Minister Bae Kyung-hoon said South Korea plans to invest 550 trillion won in AI data centers by 2029, rising to more than 1,000 trillion won by 2035. The strategy includes decentralizing data center infrastructure to support regional economic growth outside the Seoul metropolitan area.

  ## DRAM Production Doubling

  Industry Minister Kim said South Korea intends to double its output of dynamic random-access memory, or DRAM — the semiconductor memory type used in laptops, smartphones, and other electronics — within five years. That effort will require accelerating factory construction in the Seoul metropolitan area through the mid-2030s. High bandwidth memory, or HBM, used in AI accelerator chips, is manufactured by stacking multiple layers of DRAM.

  Existing production sites near Yongin and Pyeongtaek have reached their capacity limits, President Lee said, making new facilities urgent.

  ## Infrastructure Constraints

  SK Hynix Chairman Chey noted that building the company's Yongin cluster took nine years and that chip factories require large amounts of land, electricity, water, and skilled labor. Industry experts said dispersing chip investment beyond Seoul could ease infrastructure bottlenecks, but also warned that advanced fabrication facilities require abundant power, water, sophisticated logistics, broad supplier networks, and highly skilled workforces — resources that may not mature quickly enough in new regions to keep pace with rising AI-driven demand.

  Lee Jong-ho, a professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University, pointed to long-term demand uncertainty as a material risk. "It would be ideal if demand remained strong for 20 or 30 years, but no one can know that with certainty. If demand declines, the consequences would be severe," he said.

  ## Robotics Component

  The plan also calls for a robotics and components cluster at Saemangeum on the country's west coast, where Hyundai Motor is already investing, aimed at competing with humanoid robotics advances in China and other countries.

  ## Political Dimension

  Opposition politicians criticized the southwestern chip cluster plans as politically motivated, noting that 85 percent of voters in the region supported President Lee in last year's presidential election. The announcement came as Lee's approval rating has fallen for six consecutive weeks, reaching 46.5 percent according to polling firm Realmeter.

  The plan's significance for the data center industry lies in its scale and stated timeline: South Korea is committing to build new fab capacity in a region with no established semiconductor supply chain, while doubling national DRAM output and deploying more than 1,000 trillion won in AI data center infrastructure by 2035. Samsung has confirmed Gwangju as its southwestern cluster site, while SK Hynix continues evaluating locations and infrastructure requirements in the region.

  ## 南韓宣布逾千兆韓元晶片與AI投資大計

  南韓總統李在明週一正式公布以半導體與人工智慧為核心的國家產業戰略。三星電子與SK海力士承諾合計投入800兆韓元，在南韓西南地區打造全新晶片製造聚落，政府同步訂下2035年前吸引逾千兆韓元AI資料中心投資的目標。

  ## 三大支柱框架

  李在明表示，此一戰略以半導體、實體AI與資料中心三大支柱為架構。他在三星與SK海力士兩大集團董事長陪同下出席發布記者會，並宣告：「我們必須比任何國家都更快掌握AI的核心要素。」（引自路透社）

  產業部長金正寬表示，三星電子與SK海力士將各自在南韓西南部興建兩座新晶片製造廠，800兆韓元的私部門承諾即用於支應上述建設。三星電子董事長李在鎔表示，公司已選定西南部城市光州作為新晶片聚落的建廠地點。SK海力士董事長崔泰源則表示，公司仍需更多時間確認西南部的最終選址，並完成必要基礎設施的整備工作。

  李在明總統進一步指出，光州市與全羅南道預計將共同提撥5兆至20兆韓元挹注相關建設；此外，位於首爾近郊忠清地區的晶片封裝聚落預計另可帶動81兆韓元投資。

  ## AI資料中心投資目標

  科學部長裴京訓表示，南韓計畫於2029年前在AI資料中心領域投入550兆韓元，並於2035年前進一步提升至逾千兆韓元。此一戰略同時強調將資料中心基礎設施的布局去中心化，以支撐首都圈以外地區的區域經濟發展。

  ## DRAM產能倍增計畫

  產業部長金正寬表示，南韓預計在五年內將動態隨機存取記憶體（DRAM）的產量提升一倍。DRAM是廣泛應用於筆記型電腦、智慧型手機及各類電子裝置的半導體記憶體。為達成此一目標，首都圈的工廠建設須在2030年代中期以前持續加速推進。高頻寬記憶體（HBM）則是應用於AI加速晶片的關鍵元件，其製程是將多層DRAM堆疊而成。

  李在明總統指出，鄰近龍仁與平澤的現有生產基地已達產能上限，新建設施已刻不容緩。

  ## 基礎設施瓶頸

  SK海力士董事長崔泰源指出，該公司在龍仁打造晶片聚落耗時長達九年，而晶片製造廠的興建需要大量土地、電力、水源與熟練勞動力。產業界專家表示，將晶片投資分散至首都圈以外確實有助於紓解基礎設施瓶頸，但同時也警告，先進製造廠所需的充足電力、水資源、精密物流、完整供應鏈與高技術人力，在新興地區未必能及時培育成熟，以跟上AI需求持續攀升的步伐。

  首爾國立大學電機與電腦工程學系教授李鐘浩則點出長期需求的不確定性是一項重大風險。他表示：「若需求能維持二、三十年固然理想，但沒有人能對此篤定。一旦需求下滑，後果將十分嚴峻。」

  ## 機器人產業布局

  此次計畫亦納入機器人與零組件聚落的規劃，預計設置於西海岸的新萬金地區——現代汽車已在當地展開投資——目標是與中國及其他國家的人形機器人技術發展相抗衡。

  ## 政治角力面向

  在野黨政治人物批評西南部晶片聚落計畫具有政治動機，並指出該地區有85%的選民在去年總統大選中投票支持李在明。此次發布會召開之際，李在明的支持率已連續六週下滑，依民調機構Realmeter的數據，目前已降至46.5%。

  就資料中心產業而言，此一計畫的重要性在於其規模與時程承諾：南韓決心在毫無既有半導體供應鏈的地區新建晶圓廠產能，同時將全國DRAM產量加倍，並於2035年前部署逾千兆韓元的AI資料中心基礎設施。目前三星已確認光州為西南部聚落的建廠地點，SK海力士則仍在評估該地區的選址與基礎設施需求。
