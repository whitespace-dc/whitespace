body_en: |
  Telkom Indonesia's enterprise business unit generates roughly Rp 19 trillion, equal to about 11% of total group revenue, the state-owned telecommunications company said, as it restructures the division and works toward closing a gap against global peers whose enterprise segments contribute 20–30% or more of group income.

  Veranita Yosephine, Director of Enterprise and Business Service at Telkom, disclosed the figures during a strategic discussion with Danantara — formally Badan Pengelola Investasi Danantara, Indonesia's state investment management agency — involving Chief Operating Officer Dony Oskaria and Managing Director for Business-2 Setyanto Hantoro. The meeting focused on the direction of Telkom Enterprise's development, the company said in a written statement published Monday, August 17, 2026.

  ## Revenue share and the gap against global operators

  At 11% of total revenue, Telkom Enterprise sits well below the enterprise divisions of comparable global telecommunications companies, which the company said contribute between 20% and 30% or more of group revenue at some operators. Telkom described the gap as significant room for growth.

  "With the right transformation, we are optimistic that Telkom Enterprise has the potential to grow stronger, improve profitability, and become one of Telkom's growth engines going forward," Veranita said.

  ## Restructuring under TLKM 30

  The enterprise push runs alongside Telkom's broader TLKM 30 corporate transformation strategy. Under that program, Telkom Enterprise is undergoing a restructuring aimed at reforming the organization, strengthening governance, and simplifying business processes to make the unit more efficient and market-oriented in the B2B segment, the company said.

  Telkom said the reorganization is a strategic step to ensure Indonesia has a digital foundation capable of supporting future technology requirements, and frames it as part of a synergy between Telkom and Danantara to build stronger national digital capabilities.

  ## Services and target segments

  Telkom Enterprise targets government agencies, state-owned enterprises (BUMN), and private-sector clients. Its portfolio covers artificial intelligence (AI), cloud computing, data centers, managed services, the Internet of Things (IoT), and cybersecurity, the company said.

  Veranita said those digital services depend on high-capacity, secure, and resilient connectivity to function effectively, which is why Telkom continues to reinforce its enterprise infrastructure alongside its service offering.

  "Indonesia needs strong national digital capabilities to answer the challenges of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data sovereignty, and AI sovereignty. Telkom is ready to take on that strategic role through strengthening connectivity and integrated digital solutions to support digital transformation across various sectors," she said.

  ## Business process changes

  Internally, Telkom said it is simplifying business processes and strengthening governance through the application of digital technology. The company said the effort is designed to increase internal efficiency, accelerate service delivery to customers, and create openings for new business development that is more responsive to market needs.

  ## What it means

  The explicit comparison Telkom draws between its current 11% enterprise revenue share and the 20–30%-plus levels at some global peers sets a measurable benchmark for the division's expansion. Telkom said it plans to deepen integration across connectivity, digital infrastructure, data centers, cloud, and cybersecurity services as the foundation for delivering end-to-end digital solutions to B2B customers, and said sustained enterprise development would support Indonesia's broader digital sovereignty objectives.

  印尼電信（Telkom Indonesia）表示，旗下企業業務部門約創造印尼盾19兆盧比的營收，約當集團總營收的11%；這家國營電信公司正推動部門重組，並努力縮小與全球同業之間的差距——後者的企業部門往往貢獻集團總收入的20%至30%甚至更多。

  印尼電信企業暨商業服務事業群負責人維拉妮塔·優瑟芬（Veranita Yosephine）在一場戰略討論會上披露了上述數字。與會人士包括印尼國家投資管理機構丹安塔拉（Badan Pengelola Investasi Danantara，Danantara）的營運長唐尼·奧斯卡里亞（Dony Oskaria），以及第二業務群常務董事塞蒂安托·韓托羅（Setyanto Hantoro）。印尼電信在2026年8月17日（星期一）發布的書面聲明中表示，此次會議聚焦於印尼電信企業部門的發展方向。

  ## 營收佔比與全球業者的差距

  印尼電信企業部門目前佔集團總營收的11%，遠低於全球可比電信業者的水準——後者的企業部門普遍貢獻集團收入的20%至30%，部分業者甚至更高。印尼電信將這一差距定性為龐大的成長空間。

  維拉妮塔表示：「透過正確的轉型，我們樂觀地認為印尼電信企業部門具備進一步壯大的潛力，能夠提升獲利能力，並成為印尼電信未來的成長引擎之一。」

  ## TLKM 30策略下的重組

  企業業務的擴展計畫與印尼電信整體的TLKM 30企業轉型策略並行推進。在此框架下，印尼電信企業部門正進行重組，目標在於改革組織架構、強化公司治理，並簡化業務流程，使該部門在B2B市場中更具效率且更貼近市場需求。

  印尼電信表示，此次重組是一項戰略舉措，旨在確保印尼建立起能夠支撐未來技術需求的數位基礎，並將其定位為印尼電信與丹安塔拉協同合作的一環，致力於打造更強大的國家數位能力。

  ## 服務項目與目標客群

  印尼電信企業部門的服務對象涵蓋政府機關、國營企業（BUMN）及私人企業。服務組合包括人工智慧（AI）、雲端運算、資料中心、代管服務、物聯網（IoT）及資安等領域。

  維拉妮塔指出，上述數位服務的有效運作有賴於高容量、安全且具備韌性的連網能力，這也是印尼電信持續強化企業基礎設施的原因。

  「印尼需要強大的國家數位能力，以應對人工智慧、資安、資料主權及AI主權等挑戰。印尼電信已準備好透過強化連網能力與整合性數位解決方案，肩負這一戰略角色，支援各領域的數位轉型，」她說。

  ## 業務流程調整

  在內部管理層面，印尼電信表示，正透過導入數位科技來簡化業務流程、強化公司治理。公司指出，此舉旨在提升內部效率、加快對客戶的服務交付速度，並為更貼近市場需求的新業務開發創造機會。

  ## 意義與展望

  印尼電信明確點出其企業部門目前11%的營收佔比，與部分全球業者20%至30%以上水準之間的差距，為該部門的擴張設下可衡量的基準。印尼電信表示，計畫深化連網能力、數位基礎設施、資料中心、雲端及資安等服務的整合，作為向B2B客戶提供端到端數位解決方案的基礎，並強調持續發展企業業務，將有助於支撐印尼更宏觀的數位主權目標。
