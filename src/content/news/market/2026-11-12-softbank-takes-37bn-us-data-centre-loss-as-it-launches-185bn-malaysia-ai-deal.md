body_en: |
  SoftBank Group has written off $3.7 billion in power equipment purchased in advance for US artificial intelligence data centre projects, disclosing the charge in the same week it committed up to $1 billion to two AI infrastructure developments in Malaysia structured around staged, milestone-tied funding.

  The ¥597.2 billion charge was disclosed as part of SoftBank's fiscal second-quarter results on 10 November 2026. Combined with higher finance costs and weaker investment gains, it produced a ¥1.12 trillion ($7.1 billion) net loss — the company's first net loss in almost two years, Bloomberg reported. Analysts had on average forecast a profit of ¥129 billion.

  ## What produced the US charge

  SoftBank made advance payments for power generation equipment across its US AI data centre portfolio out of concern over supply shortages and long lead times. CNBC reported that cumulative advance payments reached $9.48 billion by the end of September.

  The write-off has two components: ¥457 billion in advance payments that cannot be recovered, and ¥140.2 billion in costs from contract changes and settlements. SoftBank did not specify which projects the written-off orders covered.

  "We made bridge investments in advance to secure power generation equipment in anticipation of shortage," a SoftBank executive said on the earnings call. When project requirements changed, the subsidiary holding those orders revised its procurement plans.

  One of the projects in SoftBank's US portfolio is the PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, where the company holds a 20-year lease agreement with OpenAI for eight gigawatts of IT capacity — equivalent to a large share of current US data centre capacity. SoftBank said it has no plans to downsize that project, but chief financial officer Yoshimitsu Goto told analysts the company was conducting a "thorough reassessment," including a review of its procurement strategy, Bloomberg reported. Shares in power equipment makers GE Vernova and Bloom Energy fell after the results were published.

  ## The Malaysia agreements and their structure

  On 9 November, the day before the earnings release, SoftBank signed joint development agreements with Grab, the Southeast Asian superapp and ride-hailing and payments platform, and Petroleum Sarawak Berhad (PETROS), the state energy company of the Malaysian state of Sarawak. The agreements cover one AI infrastructure project in Sarawak and a second in Peninsular Malaysia, with a combined long-term target of at least five gigawatts of AI compute capacity.

  Total seed capital across both projects is $1.85 billion, of which SoftBank is contributing $1 billion.

  The Sarawak project has $1 billion in initial seed capital: $800 million from SoftBank, which will be the majority owner, and $100 million each from Grab and PETROS. The Peninsular Malaysia project carries $850 million in seed funding: $650 million from Grab, which is the founding shareholder and lead developer, and $200 million from SoftBank. Additional funding for the peninsula project is expected from third-party equity investors and non-recourse project financing, meaning debt backed by the project's own assets and revenues rather than SoftBank's balance sheet.

  Investment under both agreements will be released in stages, tied to development milestones, signed customer contracts, and other closing conditions. That structure represents a step beyond a framework agreement signed in Kuching on 8 October, which had only committed the parties to explore the projects.

  "AI infrastructure will power the next wave of growth, and Malaysia has the talent and ambition to help lead it," SoftBank chairman and chief executive Masayoshi Son said in a statement.

  ## What remains unresolved in Malaysia

  Neither project has a named hyperscaler customer, a confirmed site, a specified power source, or a disclosed completion date. The parties said they will seek additional investors and hyperscaler customers.

  Power supply is a central open question in Sarawak, which markets itself on hydropower. State-owned Sarawak Energy operates the 2,400-megawatt Bakun and 944MW Murum hydroelectric dams and is building the 1,285MW Baleh dam. However, the energy partner named in the agreements is PETROS, not Sarawak Energy, and PETROS's role draws on its gas and liquefied natural gas-to-power work as master developer of the Kuching Low-Carbon Hub. The parties said both projects will follow Malaysia's renewable energy guidelines.

  "This is about preparing Sarawak for its next phase of economic transformation beyond oil and gas," said Janin Girie, group chief executive of PETROS.

  Connectivity is an additional constraint. Malaysia's deputy communications minister told parliament that the Batam Sarawak Internet Cable System, Sarawak's international subsea link to Batam island in Indonesia, carries 48 terabits per second of capacity. Telekom Malaysia's domestic SKRM cable system, linking the peninsula to Sarawak's coastal cities, carries roughly 9.5 terabits per second. A multi-gigawatt AI campus, running thousands of servers, would require substantially more international bandwidth than those systems currently provide.

  The location of the Peninsular Malaysia project has not been disclosed. Johor, Malaysia's main data centre hub located at the southern tip of the peninsula, has a colocation vacancy rate of just 0.7%, according to property consultancy Knight Frank, and authorities there have tightened approvals on power and water supply.

  Malaysian prime minister Anwar Ibrahim welcomed the deal and, in a speech in Melaka on 24 October, said Son had chosen Kuching and Peninsular Malaysia for $50 billion of AI infrastructure investment. SoftBank has not confirmed that figure.

  ## What the contrast between the two sets of commitments means

  The US loss illustrates the cost of a procurement approach that has become common across the AI data centre industry: ordering generators and other power equipment two years or more in advance and committing to suppliers before project designs are final, because power availability has become the primary constraint on where AI facilities can be built. The specific risk SoftBank encountered — paying for equipment that could not be absorbed when project specifications changed — will raise scrutiny of advance-procurement strategies more broadly.

  The Malaysia structure, with capital staged against milestones and customer contracts, shifts the sequencing: funding follows commitment rather than preceding it. Whether that discipline holds once a major cloud customer signs and commercial pressure to lock in scarce equipment returns will determine how much of the five-gigawatt target the two projects ultimately deliver.

  SoftBank also agreed in principle to a five-year lock-up on Grab shares held directly by a SoftBank entity, as part of the broader Malaysia arrangements.
body_zh: |
  軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）已就美國人工智慧資料中心專案的預付電力設備款項認列37億美元損失，並在同一週與Grab及砂拉越石油公司（PETROS）就馬來西亞兩項AI基礎設施開發簽署種子資金協議，承諾投入金額最高達10億美元，兩份協議均設有分階段里程碑撥款機制。

  5,972億日圓的沖銷費用，作為軟銀2026年11月10日公布的第二財季業績的一部分而予以揭露。加上財務成本上升及投資收益疲弱，軟銀合計錄得1.12兆日圓（約71億美元）的淨虧損——這是該公司近兩年來首度出現淨虧損，彭博社（Bloomberg）對此進行了報導。分析師的平均預估原為獲利1,290億日圓。

  ## 美國虧損的來龍去脈

  軟銀為其美國人工智慧資料中心投資組合預先購置發電設備，出發點是擔憂供應緊缺與漫長的交貨期。CNBC報導，截至9月底，軟銀的累計預付款項已達94.8億美元。

  此次認列損失分為兩部分：無法收回的4,570億日圓預付款，以及1,402億日圓的合約變更與和解成本。軟銀並未具體說明遭沖銷的訂單涉及哪些專案。

  「我們提前進行過渡性投資，以在預期缺貨的情況下確保發電設備的供應，」軟銀一名高階主管在法說會上表示。當專案需求改變後，持有這些訂單的子公司隨即修訂了採購計畫。

  軟銀美國投資組合中的專案之一，是位於俄亥俄州派克郡（Pike County）的PORTS-Pike科技園區。軟銀持有與OpenAI簽訂的一份二十年租約，提供八吉瓦（GW）的IT容量——相當於美國現有資料中心總容量的一大部分。軟銀表示並無縮減該專案的計畫，但據彭博報導，財務長後藤芳光在法說會上告訴分析師，公司正在進行「全面重新評估」，其中包括對採購策略的檢討。業績公布後，電力設備製造商GE Vernova及Bloom Energy的股價隨即走低。

  ## 馬來西亞協議及其架構

  在業績發布的前一天，即11月9日，軟銀分別與東南亞超級應用程式暨叫車、支付平台Grab，以及馬來西亞砂拉越州國有能源公司砂拉越石油公司（PETROS）簽署聯合開發協議。協議涵蓋砂拉越的一個AI基礎設施專案，以及馬來半島的另一個專案，兩者的長期目標合計至少達五吉瓦的AI算力容量。

  兩個專案的種子資金合計為18.5億美元，其中軟銀出資10億美元。

  砂拉越專案的初始種子資金為10億美元：軟銀出資8億美元並將持有多數股權，Grab與PETROS各出資1億美元。馬來半島專案的種子資金為8.5億美元：Grab出資6.5億美元，為創始股東及主導開發商；軟銀出資2億美元。半島專案預計將另從第三方股權投資人及無追索權專案融資中取得額外資金，即以專案本身的資產與收益為擔保的債務，而非以軟銀的資產負債表作為後盾。

  兩份協議的投資均採分階段撥款方式，與開發里程碑、已簽署的客戶合約及其他交割條件掛鉤。此一架構較10月8日在古晉（Kuching）簽署的框架協議更進一步——彼時各方僅承諾就相關專案進行探索性研究。

  「AI基礎設施將驅動下一波成長浪潮，馬來西亞擁有協助引領這一進程所需的人才與雄心，」軟銀董事長兼執行長孫正義在聲明中表示。

  ## 馬來西亞仍待解決的問題

  兩個專案目前均無具名的超大規模雲端廠商客戶、確認的廠址、明確的電力來源，亦無已公布的完工日期。各方表示將積極尋求其他投資人與超大規模雲端廠商。

  電力供應是砂拉越最核心的未解問題，該州以豐富的水力發電資源作為招商賣點。州屬的砂拉越能源公司運營裝置容量2,400百萬瓦（MW）的巴貢（Bakun）水壩及944百萬瓦的木南（Murum）水壩，並正在興建裝置容量1,285百萬瓦的巴拉（Baleh）水壩。然而，協議中指名的能源合作夥伴是PETROS，而非砂拉越能源，PETROS的角色奠基於其天然氣及液化天然氣（LNG）轉電力業務，以及身為古晉低碳樞紐（Kuching Low-Carbon Hub）主開發商的職能。各方表示兩個專案均將遵循馬來西亞再生能源相關法規。

  「這是要為砂拉越邁向超越石油與天然氣的下一階段經濟轉型做好準備，」PETROS集團執行長賈寧吉里（Janin Girie）表示。

  網路連線能力是另一項制約因素。馬來西亞副通訊部長向國會表示，連接砂拉越與印尼峇淡島（Batam）的峇淡砂拉越國際海底電纜系統（Batam Sarawak Internet Cable System），目前的頻寬容量為每秒48兆位元（Tbps）。馬來西亞電信（Telekom Malaysia）連接馬來半島與砂拉越沿海城市的國內SKRM電纜系統，頻寬約為每秒9.5兆位元。一座達數吉瓦規模的AI園區，運行數以千計的伺服器，所需的國際頻寬將遠超現有系統的承載能力。

  馬來半島專案的選址尚未對外公開。柔佛州（Johor）作為馬來西亞主要的資料中心樞紐，位於半島最南端，根據地產顧問公司萊坊（Knight Frank）的資料，當地共置機房的空置率僅有0.7%，且當地政府已收緊電力與水資源的審批。

  馬來西亞總理安華依布拉欣（Anwar Ibrahim）對此協議表示歡迎，並在10月24日於馬六甲（Melaka）發表的演說中表示，孫正義已選定古晉及馬來半島進行500億美元的AI基礎設施投資。軟銀迄今尚未確認這一數字。

  ## 兩組承諾的對比意義

  美國的虧損揭示了一種在AI資料中心產業已相當普遍的採購模式所潛藏的代價：提前兩年甚至更長時間訂購發電機及其他電力設備，在專案設計最終定案前便向供應商做出承諾——這是因為電力供應已成為AI設施選址的首要限制因素。軟銀此次所遭遇的具體風險——在專案規格改變後，已採購設備無從消化——將使預先採購策略在業界受到更廣泛的審視。

  馬來西亞的架構以里程碑及客戶合約為條件分階段撥款，調整了投資的先後順序：資金跟隨承諾而來，而非超前佈局。這種資本紀律能否在主要雲端客戶完成簽約、商業壓力迫使各方重新搶進稀缺設備時依然維持，將決定兩個專案最終能落實多少比例的五吉瓦目標。

  此外，作為馬來西亞整體安排的一部分，軟銀旗下一個直接持有Grab股份的實體原則上同意，對所持Grab股份設置五年鎖定期。
