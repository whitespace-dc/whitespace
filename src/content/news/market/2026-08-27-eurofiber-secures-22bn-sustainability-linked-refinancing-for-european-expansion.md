body_en: |
  Eurofiber has secured €2.2 billion ($2.56 billion) in long-term sustainability-linked debt financing, replacing an existing €1.5 billion ($1.75 billion) facility and expanding the European fiber and data center operator's capacity to invest across the continent's digital infrastructure.

  The transaction was supported by a broad group of banks and institutional investors and comprises significant undrawn committed debt facilities, the company said. Australian investment manager Macquarie Asset Management confirmed separately that it provided €125 million ($145 million) in senior term loan financing as part of the deal.

  BNP Paribas and Rothschild & Co served as financial advisors to Eurofiber. Clifford Chance provided legal advice to Eurofiber, Antin Infrastructure Partners, and PGGM Infrastructure Fund in connection with the refinancing.

  ## Sustainability-linked structure

  The financing is structured as a Sustainability-Linked Loan (SLL) — a form of debt in which the interest margin adjusts based on whether the borrower meets predefined environmental, social, and governance targets — aligned with the Loan Market Association's (LMA) Sustainability-Linked Loan Principles, the company said.

  The margin adjustment mechanism is tied to three key performance indicators, assessed annually. Eurofiber must reduce Scope 1, 2, and 3 greenhouse gas emissions — covering direct operations, purchased energy, and supply chain respectively — in line with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) methodology. It must also meet a circularity target and increase gender diversity across its workforce.

  ## Eurofiber's network and assets

  Eurofiber operates a 77,500-kilometer fiber network spanning the Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, France, and Germany, alongside eight data centers in the Netherlands. Two of those data centers were acquired in 2024 through the company's purchase of Bytesnet.

  The refinancing follows a period of active portfolio management. In June, Eurofiber agreed to sell four of its French data centers to Etix, an edge data center operator. In February, it acquired LuxNetwork, a Luxembourg-based connectivity provider.

  ## Use of proceeds

  The company said the new facility will support further investment in its fiber network, expansion of its cloud infrastructure service, and customer-driven innovation in its core markets.

  "This refinancing confirms the strength of Eurofiber's business model and the confidence of our financing partners in our long-term strategy. The new platform provides a diversified and scalable capital structure that supports our investment plans, enhances our financial flexibility and positions us well for continued growth in Europe's digital infrastructure market," said Alex Goldblum, Eurofiber's chief executive.

  ## Eurofiber完成22億歐元永續連結再融資，佈局歐洲數位基礎建設

  Eurofiber已完成22億歐元（約新台幣828億元）長期永續連結債務融資，取代原有15億歐元（約新台幣565億元）的既有額度，大幅擴充這家歐洲光纖與資料中心業者在歐洲大陸投資數位基礎建設的財務空間。

  本次交易由多家銀行及機構投資人共同支持，包含大量尚未動用的承諾債務額度。澳洲投資管理公司麥格理資產管理（Macquarie Asset Management）另行確認，已提供1億2,500萬歐元（約新台幣47億元）的優先定期貸款作為本次交易的一部分。法國巴黎銀行（BNP Paribas）與羅斯柴爾德公司（Rothschild & Co）擔任Eurofiber的財務顧問，偉凱律師事務所（Clifford Chance）則就本次再融資為Eurofiber、Antin Infrastructure Partners及PGGM Infrastructure Fund提供法律諮詢服務。

  ## 永續連結融資架構

  本次融資採用永續連結貸款（Sustainability-Linked Loan，SLL）架構——即一種依借款方是否達成預先設定的環境、社會與治理（ESG）目標，來調整貸款利率加碼的債務形式——並符合貸款市場協會（LMA）永續連結貸款準則的規範。

  利率調整機制與三項關鍵績效指標（KPI）掛鉤，每年進行評估。Eurofiber須依據科學基礎減量目標倡議（SBTi）方法，削減範疇一、範疇二及範疇三的溫室氣體排放量，分別涵蓋直接營運排放、購入能源排放及供應鏈排放。此外，公司還須達成循環經濟目標，並提升整體員工的性別多元程度。

  ## Eurofiber網路與資產概況

  Eurofiber目前營運一條全長7萬7,500公里的光纖網路，跨越荷蘭、比利時、盧森堡、法國及德國，並在荷蘭擁有八座資料中心，其中兩座係於2024年透過收購Bytesnet所取得。

  本次再融資前，Eurofiber已進行一系列積極的資產組合調整。今年6月，Eurofiber同意將旗下四座法國資料中心出售予邊緣資料中心業者Etix；今年2月，則收購了盧森堡連線服務商LuxNetwork。

  ## 資金用途

  Eurofiber表示，新融資額度將用於進一步投資光纖網路、擴充雲端基礎架構服務，以及在核心市場推動以客戶需求為導向的創新。

  Eurofiber執行長Alex Goldblum表示：「本次再融資證明了Eurofiber商業模式的穩健性，以及融資夥伴對我們長期策略的信心。新的融資平台提供多元且可擴展的資本結構，支撐我們的投資計畫、強化財務靈活性，並使我們在歐洲數位基礎建設市場保持持續成長的有利地位。」
