body_en: |
  Texas regulators approved a centralized process to screen large power users seeking grid connections in June 2026, as developers including Microsoft, Google, and Nvidia moved ahead with AI data center campuses that co-locate generation on-site to avoid the state's growing interconnection backlog.

  The Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT), the state's top electricity regulator, approved ERCOT's "Batch Zero" initiative: a one-time statewide study that evaluates very large new electricity customers through a single, consistent process rather than separate utility-by-utility reviews. ERCOT (the Electricity Reliability Council of Texas) operates most of the state's power grid and wholesale electricity market. The move targets the backlogs created when speculative developers held queue positions without firm evidence of intent to build, consuming grid-planning resources that ERCOT says should go to projects with a high likelihood of coming online.

  ERCOT projects that Texas electricity demand could approach 368 gigawatts (GW) by 2032, driven by AI data centers. Both new generation capacity and new transmission infrastructure must expand in step for that demand to be served, ERCOT has warned, noting that supply gaps will affect when projects receive first power, what they pay for electricity, and whether they ultimately reach operation.

  ## New queue criteria separate committed from speculative projects

  Under Batch Zero, projects must satisfy readiness criteria: site control, permits in progress, financing plans, equipment orders, and realistic power-use forecasts. Projects that cannot meet those criteria will not receive priority in grid planning. The change is designed to concentrate limited interconnection resources on buildable developments.

  The initiative extends Senate Bill 6 (SB-6), enacted in June 2025, which shifted more grid-connection costs and reliability obligations to very large ERCOT customers, defined roughly as those drawing 75 megawatts (MW) or more. The law is designed to prevent residential ratepayers from subsidizing large industrial loads and to require those customers to be capable of reducing consumption or accepting curtailment during grid emergencies.

  CPower Energy senior regulatory affairs analyst Claire Swingle wrote in June 2025 that SB-6's requirements can benefit data center operators. Facilities that participate in demand response programs, accepting payment to reduce power use during grid stress events, can recover costs while meeting their reliability obligations under the law.

  ## Developers co-locate generation to cut timelines

  Texas's leading announced projects are pairing data centers with dedicated power generation rather than drawing from the public grid.

  Project Kilby, located near Pecos in West Texas, places a Microsoft-operated AI campus alongside approximately 2.67 GW of co-located natural-gas generation developed by Chevron's Energy Forge One and Engine No. 1. The project targets first power in 2028 and is designed to grow to approximately 2 GW of data center capacity over five to seven years.

  Google and energy developer Intersect are developing a Texas campus with approximately 1 GW of dedicated power drawn mostly from wind, solar, and batteries, supplemented by on-site gas generation to maintain a continuously available supply.

  At Abilene, Crusoe is adding a 900-MW AI data center to a campus operated for Microsoft, bringing that campus's total planned capacity to 2.1 GW. Nvidia has partnered with data center operator IREN to designate IREN's 2-GW Sweetwater campus as a flagship AI facility, with a plan to deploy up to 5 GW of Nvidia-optimized infrastructure across IREN's global portfolio.

  ## Off-grid options emerge as interconnection costs rise

  BaRupOn, an industrial developer, was advancing a 700-acre site in Liberty County, Texas, through the public interconnection process but encountered approximately $35 million in connection costs and a potential delay pushing first power to 2029. The company is now reviewing an off-grid, behind-the-meter design, with options including gas-fired generation and modular microreactors.

  Exowatt opened an Austin hub in March 2026 to deploy modular renewable power systems that deliver electricity directly to data centers behind the utility meter, targeting sites where grid connections would impose delays or excessive cost.

  ## Grid infrastructure and federal coordination

  Texas's CREZ transmission corridors, originally built to move West Texas wind power and rated at 345 kilovolts (kV), are becoming a critical enabler for AI data center development. However, a substantial gap remains between announced gigawatt-scale projects and physical deployment: ERCOT's large-load interconnection queue remains crowded and key electrical equipment is on back-order.

  The U.S. Department of Energy is developing a platform called Agora that models how hyperscale AI data centers consume power, including fast-changing consumption bursts, and specifies how those facilities should behave during grid events such as brief disturbances and reconnections after outages. The platform is informing ERCOT's and Texas A&M's work on interconnection and operating requirements for large electronic loads.

  ## Texas challenges Northern Virginia

  Texas's available power capacity, land supply, and fewer siting constraints are enabling multi-gigawatt campuses to advance faster than in established markets. As of May 2026, Texas had moved ahead of Northern Virginia in global data center rankings.

  ## Outlook

  The Batch Zero study is designed to give Texas regulators a current, consistent picture of which queued projects are likely to be completed, concentrating grid-planning resources on viable developments. ERCOT has stated that rapid AI demand will land on schedule only if supply expands in step, with both generation and transmission growth shaping when sites receive power, what developers pay for it, and which announced projects ultimately come online. Project Kilby's targeted first-power date of 2028 is the nearest confirmed milestone for the power-first model at scale.

  ## 德州批准新電網審查規則，AI資料中心業者紛紛自備電力

  德州監管機構於2026年6月批准了一套針對大型用電戶申請電網併網的集中審查程序，與此同時，微軟、Google、輝達等業者紛紛推進自備發電設施的AI資料中心園區，藉此規避德州日益嚴重的併網排隊壅塞問題。

  德州公用事業委員會（PUCT）是德州最高電力監管機關，已批准ERCOT推出「Batch Zero」計畫——一項全德州一次性的集中評估機制，將極大型新用電戶納入單一、一致的審查流程，取代過去各電力公司各自為政的逐案審查方式。ERCOT（德州電力可靠度委員會）負責管理德州大部分電網及電力批發市場。此舉的目的，在於解決過去投機型開發商佔據排隊位置卻無實質建設意圖所造成的積壓問題——ERCOT指出，這些資源應優先投入具高度建設可能性的專案。

  ERCOT預測，受AI資料中心需求帶動，德州電力需求到2032年可能逼近368吉瓦（GW）。ERCOT警告，新增發電容量與輸電基礎設施必須同步擴張，才能滿足這股龐大需求，並指出供給缺口將影響專案首次供電的時間點、電費成本，以及最終能否順利上線。

  ## 新排隊標準區隔有意建設與投機性專案

  在Batch Zero機制下，專案必須符合一系列準備就緒的條件：包括取得土地控制權、許可申請進行中、具備融資計畫、已下訂設備，以及提出合理的用電預測。無法達到這些條件的專案，將無法在電網規劃中取得優先順位。此變革旨在將有限的併網資源集中用於真正可落地的開發案。

  此計畫延伸自2025年6月通過的《第6號參議院法案》（SB-6）。該法案將更多電網連接成本與供電可靠度義務轉移至大型ERCOT用電戶，大致定義為用電量達75百萬瓦（MW）以上的用戶。該法旨在防止一般住宅用戶補貼大型工業用電負載，並要求此類用電戶在電網緊急事件期間具備降低用電或接受限電的能力。

  CPower Energy資深法規事務分析師克萊兒・斯溫格（Claire Swingle）於2025年6月撰文指出，SB-6的相關規定其實對資料中心業者有利。參與需量反應計畫的設施，在電網吃緊時段接受報酬換取降低用電，不僅可回收成本，同時也履行了法規要求的供電可靠度義務。

  ## 業者自備發電縮短建設時程

  德州已宣布的指標性專案，皆採取資料中心搭配專屬發電設施的模式，而非依賴公用電網供電。

  「Kilby計畫」位於西德州佩科斯市附近，將微軟營運的AI園區與約2.67 GW的自建天然氣發電設施並排設置，後者由雪佛龍旗下的Energy Forge One與Engine No. 1共同開發。該專案預計2028年首次供電，設計目標是在五至七年內擴展至約2 GW的資料中心容量。

  Google與能源開發商Intersect正在德州合作開發一座園區，配備約1 GW的專屬電力，主要來源為風能、太陽能及電池儲能，並輔以現場天然氣發電，以確保全天候穩定供電。

  在阿比林市，Crusoe正在一座微軟委託運營的園區新增一座900 MW的AI資料中心，使該園區的總規劃容量達到2.1 GW。輝達則與資料中心業者IREN合作，將IREN位於斯威特沃特、容量達2 GW的園區指定為旗艦AI設施，並計畫在IREN的全球投資組合中部署最多5 GW的輝達優化基礎設施。

  ## 離網方案興起，因應併網成本攀升

  工業開發商BaRupOn原本透過公開流程，推進德州自由郡一處700英畝土地的併網申請，卻面臨約3,500萬美元的連接費用，以及首次供電時間可能推遲至2029年的風險。該公司目前正評估採用離網、表後計量的設計方案，選項包括天然氣發電及模組化微型核反應爐。

  Exowatt於2026年3月在奧斯汀設立中心，部署模組化再生能源電力系統，直接以表後計量方式向資料中心供電，鎖定那些因電網連接而面臨延誤或過高成本的場址。

  ## 電網基礎設施與聯邦協調

  德州原本為疏運西德州風電而建設的CREZ輸電走廊，電壓等級為345千伏（kV），如今正成為AI資料中心發展的重要骨幹基礎設施。然而，已宣布的吉瓦級專案與實際部署之間仍存在相當大的落差：ERCOT的大型負載併網排隊依然壅塞，關鍵電力設備也面臨供貨延誤。

  美國能源部正在開發一套名為「Agora」的平台，用以模擬超大規模AI資料中心的用電行為，包括快速變動的瞬間用電尖峰，並規範這些設施在電網事件（如短暫擾動與停電後重新併網）期間應有的應對方式。此平台正在為ERCOT與德州農工大學研擬大型電子負載的併網及運營要求提供參考依據。

  ## 德州挑戰北維吉尼亞的龍頭地位

  德州充裕的可用電力容量、廣大的土地供給，以及相對寬鬆的設址限制，使多吉瓦級園區得以比成熟市場更快推進。截至2026年5月，德州已在全球資料中心排名中超越北維吉尼亞，躍居領先地位。

  ## 展望

  Batch Zero審查計畫旨在為德州監管機關提供一幅即時、一致的排隊專案完工可能性全貌，將電網規劃資源集中用於具可行性的開發案。ERCOT表明，唯有發電與輸電雙管齊下、同步成長，AI帶來的龐大用電需求才能如期獲得滿足；這兩者的擴張進度，將決定各場址何時能獲得供電、開發商須支付多少電費，以及哪些已宣布的專案最終能夠落地。Kilby計畫預定於2028年首次供電，是目前「電力優先」大規模建設模式中，最近期且已確認的里程碑。
