body_en: |
  Intel posted third-quarter 2026 revenue of $16.5 billion, up 21% year-over-year, with its server and AI chip business delivering its fastest growth in 15 years while its personal computer segment contracted under the weight of elevated memory prices.

  ## Data center and AI segment results

  The Data Center and AI (DCAI) group — which sells Xeon server processors and related silicon to cloud providers, enterprises, and AI-focused operators — reported revenue of $7.3 billion for the quarter, a 78% increase year-over-year. Segment operating income reached $3.0 billion, representing a 41.5% margin. Chief Financial Officer David Zinsner described the result as Intel's strongest data center revenue growth in 15 years.

  Demand was broad-based across hyperscale cloud providers, enterprise customers, and what Intel calls "neo cloud" customers — smaller, AI-focused cloud operators. Revenue from "Purpose Built Silicon," a category covering custom and specialized compute products, more than doubled year-over-year.

  Chief Executive Lip-Bu Tan said Intel's immediate priority is unlocking additional supply to meet what he called extraordinary demand for the Xeon processor family. On the earnings call, he attributed the supply constraint to substrate availability — the layered circuit boards that carry processor chips — and said the bottleneck would take time to resolve.

  ## Competitive position and demand drivers

  Intel acknowledged on the call that market demand has shifted toward AMD's 192-core Turin server processors, for which Intel does not have a direct competitive product. Despite that gap, partner feedback cited in ServeTheHome's coverage indicated that Xeon 6 is selling because server demand overall is strong enough to absorb multiple product lines. Systems pairing NVIDIA graphics processing units with Intel Xeon CPUs — supported by an investment relationship between the two companies — are also contributing to Xeon 6 sales volume and pricing.

  On the call, Intel indicated it is evaluating directing capacity from its 18A manufacturing process node toward data center products, subject to the substrate supply situation.

  ## PC segment contracts

  Intel's Client Computing and Physical AI Group (CCPG), which covers consumer and commercial PC processors, reported revenue of $8.1 billion, a 5% decline year-over-year. Intel attributed the contraction to industry-wide shortages of DRAM (dynamic random-access memory) and NAND flash — the two primary memory technologies in personal computers — which are pushing system prices higher and suppressing end-market demand.

  Intel said it expects the PC market slowdown to continue through at least the first half of 2027, and that memory and NAND pricing pressure is expected to persist into 2027 as well.

  ## Intel Foundry losses narrow

  Intel Foundry, the company's contract chip manufacturing division, posted $6.7 billion in revenue, up 58% year-over-year. Intel noted that figure includes intersegment transactions — sales between Intel business units — rather than external customer revenue alone. The segment recorded an operating loss of $1.9 billion, a negative 27.7% margin, but that is an improvement from the $2.3 billion loss reported in the same quarter a year ago.

  Improved manufacturing yields and faster cycle times are reducing per-wafer costs, the company said. Intel released the Intel 14A 0.9 process design kit — technical documentation that enables chip designers to develop products targeting the 14A manufacturing node — in October, describing the release as a previously committed milestone. Manufacturing expansion continues across multiple sites, including facilities in Ohio.

  ## Overall financial results

  At the company level, non-GAAP gross margin for the quarter reached 44.1% and non-GAAP earnings per share came in at $0.52.

  ## What the results signal

  The sharp divergence between Intel's data center and client segments reflects a supply-chain dynamic in which DRAM and NAND capacity is being priced for the value it generates attached to AI server infrastructure, leaving PC system builders facing elevated component costs. Intel's earnings call commentary offered no indication that memory pricing would ease by the first quarter of next year.

  On the server side, the binding constraint on Intel's Xeon output is now substrate supply rather than chip fabrication capacity, according to the company. Resolution of that bottleneck will determine how quickly Intel can convert elevated data center demand into incremental revenue.

  Intel's most concrete forward-looking commitment is continued expansion of its manufacturing footprint, including Ohio, alongside the rollout of the 14A process design kit released in October 2026.
body_zh: |
  英特爾（Intel）公布2026年第三季營收165億美元，年增21%，伺服器與AI晶片業務交出15年來最快速的成長成績，然而個人電腦部門卻在記憶體價格高漲的重壓下出現萎縮。

  ## 資料中心暨AI部門業績

  英特爾資料中心暨人工智慧（DCAI）部門，主要向雲端業者、企業客戶及以AI為主的業者銷售Xeon伺服器處理器及相關晶片，本季營收達73億美元，年增78%。部門營業利益達30億美元，利潤率為41.5%。財務長大衛·金斯納（David Zinsner）表示，這是英特爾15年來資料中心營收最強勁的成長表現。

  需求面呈現廣泛增長，來源涵蓋超大規模雲端業者、企業客戶，以及英特爾所稱的「新型雲端」（neo cloud）客戶——即規模較小、以AI為主的雲端業者。涵蓋客製化及特殊運算產品的「Purpose Built Silicon」（專用矽晶片）類別，營收年增逾一倍。

  執行長陳立武（Lip-Bu Tan）表示，英特爾當前的首要之務是釋出更多產能，以因應他所形容的Xeon處理器系列「異常強烈的需求」。在法說會上，他將供應瓶頸歸因於基板（substrate）——即承載處理器晶片的多層電路板——的可用量不足，並表示此一瓶頸需要一段時間才能解決。

  ## 競爭地位與需求驅動力

  英特爾在法說會上坦承，市場需求正向超微（AMD）192核心「Turin」伺服器處理器轉移，而英特爾目前並無直接對應的競爭產品。儘管如此，根據ServeTheHome報導所引述的合作夥伴反饋，由於整體伺服器需求夠強，足以支撐多條產品線並行熱賣，Xeon 6依然銷售暢旺。此外，搭載輝達（NVIDIA）繪圖處理器（GPU）與英特爾Xeon CPU的伺服器系統——背後有兩家公司投資合作關係為後盾——同樣帶動了Xeon 6的銷售量與定價表現。

  英特爾在法說會上表示，正在評估是否將18A製程節點的部分產能轉投入資料中心產品，但最終仍取決於基板供應狀況。

  ## 個人電腦部門萎縮

  英特爾客戶端運算暨實體AI部門（CCPG）涵蓋消費性及商用PC處理器，本季營收81億美元，年減5%。英特爾將此一萎縮歸因於業界DRAM（動態隨機存取記憶體）及NAND快閃記憶體供應短缺——這兩種記憶體技術是個人電腦的核心元件——缺貨推升了系統售價，進而壓抑終端市場需求。

  英特爾預計，個人電腦市場的放緩態勢將至少延續至2027年上半年，記憶體與NAND的價格壓力亦預計持續至2027年。

  ## 英特爾晶圓代工虧損收窄

  英特爾晶圓代工（Intel Foundry）為公司旗下的委託製造部門，本季營收67億美元，年增58%。英特爾指出，此數字涵蓋內部部門間交易——即各業務單位之間的銷售——並非單純外部客戶營收。該部門本季錄得19億美元的營業虧損，利潤率為負27.7%，但已較去年同期23億美元的虧損顯著改善。

  公司表示，製程良率提升及生產週期縮短，正持續降低每片晶圓的製造成本。英特爾於10月發布Intel 14A 0.9製程設計套件（PDK）——即供晶片設計師開發以14A製程節點為目標之產品所需的技術文件——並表示此為先前已承諾的里程碑。製造產能擴張持續推進，涵蓋多個廠區，包括位於俄亥俄州的生產設施。

  ## 整體財務表現

  就公司整體而言，本季非GAAP毛利率達44.1%，非GAAP每股盈餘為0.52美元。

  ## 業績透露的市場訊號

  英特爾資料中心與個人電腦兩大部門之間的鮮明落差，反映出一種供應鏈動態：DRAM與NAND的產能定價，體現的是其與AI伺服器基礎設施結合所能創造的價值，導致PC系統組裝商承受偏高的零件成本壓力。英特爾在法說會上的評論，未給出任何記憶體價格將於明年第一季前趨緩的跡象。

  伺服器業務方面，英特爾表示，Xeon產出目前的關鍵瓶頸在於基板供應，而非晶片製造產能本身。此一瓶頸何時得以化解，將決定英特爾能以多快的步調，將強勁的資料中心需求轉化為實際的增量營收。

  英特爾目前最具體的前瞻性承諾，是持續擴充製造版圖——包括俄亥俄州廠區的建設——並推進2026年10月已發布之14A製程設計套件的後續部署。
