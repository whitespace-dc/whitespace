  ## Controversy erupts over New Clark City industrial hub

  A planned artificial-intelligence and advanced-manufacturing hub at New Clark City in Capas, Tarlac — the Philippines' most concrete project under the international Pax Silica initiative — has generated public protests and community opposition that analysts and officials now attribute primarily to failures in government communication rather than to the merits of the project itself.

  On September 10, as more than 600 investors and government officials gathered at the Grand Hyatt hotel in Bonifacio Global City (BGC) for the inaugural Luzon Economic Corridor (LEC) Investment Forum, protesters outside the venue carried placards reading "Reject Pax Silica." Five paralegals were arrested during the demonstration.

  The opposition centers on unanswered questions about a 4,000-acre (approximately 1,620-hectare) site designated for a hub covering AI, advanced manufacturing, critical-mineral supply chains, energy, and logistics. Communities surrounding the property have raised concerns about the adequacy of water supply, energy, transport infrastructure, and workforce for the planned facility, as well as the displacement of indigenous peoples and the absence of a comprehensive environmental assessment. Capas Mayor Roseller Rodriguez stated that "no other proximate community stands to bear the brunt of the environmental issues of the proposed Pax Silica project than the municipality of Capas."

  ## What Pax Silica is

  Pax Silica is an international technology-security initiative whose name combines the Latin words for peace (*pax*) and silica, the raw material from which silicon — the foundation of semiconductor technology — is derived. The initiative is designed to build secure supply chains for semiconductors, artificial intelligence, critical minerals, and advanced manufacturing that are less dependent on geopolitically sensitive sources, particularly China, which currently holds monopolistic or near-monopolistic control over several of those sectors.

  The Philippines joined Pax Silica in April 2026, becoming the initiative's 13th signatory. Other members include the United States, Japan, South Korea, India, Singapore, Australia, Israel, the United Kingdom, Spain, and the European Union.

  ## The LEC connection the government failed to explain

  The root of the controversy, according to the BusinessWorld analysis, is that the Philippine government publicized Pax Silica without first adequately explaining the Luzon Economic Corridor (LEC), the broader multilateral infrastructure framework on which the Clark hub depends entirely.

  The LEC is a major multilateral infrastructure and development initiative connecting key economic and logistics hubs across Luzon. It was first launched in April 2024 — more than two years before the Pax Silica controversy peaked — under the umbrella of the G7 Partnership for Global Infrastructure and Investment (PGI), which involves the United States, Japan, and multiple allied partners.

  The LEC is structured around four industrial nodes: Subic Bay, serving as a deep-water port, shipbuilding, and maritime trade hub; Clark, functioning as an aviation center, technology park, and high-tech manufacturing node with an emphasis on high-value semiconductors; the National Capital Region (NCR), designated as the main economic and financial center; and Batangas, serving as a major industrial port, manufacturing, and energy hub south of Manila.

  Without LEC infrastructure in place, the Clark hub cannot function. The corridor is intended to supply the water, energy, transport and logistics, and skilled workers that factories at the New Clark City site would require. A central planned project is the Subic-Clark-Manila-Batangas Railway, which would link major deep-water ports and reduce congestion in Manila's urban transit system. Other LEC components include grid-stability upgrades, fuel distribution pipelines, civil nuclear education programs, renewable-energy integration, semiconductor assembly and electronics supply-chain expansion, and deployment of secure fifth-generation (5G) wireless networks across industrial parks.

  ## Investment forum and U.S. energy grant

  The September 10 to 11 LEC Investment Forum, which the analysis argues should have been held before Pax Silica was ever publicly announced, convened over 600 international investors, industry executives, and government leaders. A headline outcome was the official signing of a $60-million Philippines Energy Threshold Program grant delivered through the U.S. Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC). The grant is designed to support the corridor's clean energy infrastructure, addressing grid stability, institutional capacity, and the energy loads anticipated from industrial zones already operating in the Calabarzon and Central Luzon regions.

  Spain, one of the more recent countries to join the LEC, announced at around the same time that it will pursue projects in railways, modular infrastructure, aviation, shipbuilding, air-navigation management, and renewable-energy connectivity under the corridor framework.

  In his fourth State of the Nation Address, President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. announced that the Philippines is prepared to consider establishing a modular nuclear energy plant to reduce energy costs, with the United States and South Korea identified as potential technology partners within the LEC framework.

  ## Industrial alignment and real estate expansion

  Industrial developments at Clark and Subic are formally aligning with Pax Silica. Key real-estate projects — including the planned hub at New Clark City and the TARI estate in Tarlac — are expanding to accommodate high-value electronics, hardware manufacturing, and automated warehousing operations.

  ## What comes next

  The controversy illustrates the political risk of sequencing: announcing a high-profile international initiative before the infrastructure rationale, environmental process, and community consultation that underpin it are in place. The LEC Investment Forum and the MCC energy grant signing represent the government's first major public steps toward filling that explanatory gap. Whether those steps satisfy local officials and affected communities — including indigenous groups facing potential displacement — will shape the pace at which the Clark hub advances from designation to construction.

  ## 爭議浮現：新克拉克市工業園區計畫引發風波

  菲律賓塔拉克省卡帕斯市新克拉克市規劃中的人工智慧與先進製造業園區，作為國際倡議Pax Silica在菲律賓最具體的落地計畫，已引發公眾抗議與在地反對聲浪。分析人士與官員目前均指出，這波反對主要源自政府溝通失當，而非計畫本身的是非功過。

  9月10日，超過600名投資人與政府官員聚集在馬尼拉博尼法西奧全球城（BGC）的君悅酒店，出席首屆呂宋經濟走廊（LEC）投資論壇。與此同時，會場外的抗議者高舉「拒絕Pax Silica」標語進行示威，五名法律助理人員在示威現場遭到逮捕。

  反對聲浪的核心，圍繞著一塊約4,000英畝（約1,620公頃）的預定用地——菲律賓政府規劃在此建立涵蓋人工智慧、先進製造、關鍵礦產供應鏈、能源與物流的綜合園區。周邊社區對該計畫的用水供應、電力、交通基礎設施與勞動力來源均提出質疑，同時也關切原住民族遷離問題，以及完整環境評估報告付之闕如的情況。卡帕斯市長羅塞勒·羅德里格斯（Roseller Rodriguez）表示，「沒有任何鄰近社區，比卡帕斯市更直接承受Pax Silica計畫所帶來的環境衝擊。」

  ## Pax Silica 是什麼？

  Pax Silica是一項國際科技安全倡議，其名稱結合了拉丁文「pax」（和平）與「silica」（矽石，亦即半導體核心原料矽的來源）。這項倡議旨在建立半導體、人工智慧、關鍵礦產與先進製造業的安全供應鏈，降低對地緣政治敏感來源的依賴，尤其針對中國——中國目前在上述多個領域握有近乎壟斷的主導地位。

  菲律賓於2026年4月正式加入Pax Silica，成為第13個簽署國。其他成員包括美國、日本、南韓、印度、新加坡、澳洲、以色列、英國、西班牙及歐盟。

  ## 政府未能說清楚的呂宋經濟走廊關聯

  根據《商業世界報》（BusinessWorld）的分析，這場爭議的根源在於：菲律賓政府在未能充分說明呂宋經濟走廊（LEC）的情況下，便急於對外宣傳Pax Silica。而克拉克園區的一切，恰恰完全仰賴LEC這個更宏觀的多邊基礎設施框架。

  呂宋經濟走廊是一項重大多邊基礎設施與發展倡議，旨在串聯呂宋島各主要經濟與物流樞紐。該走廊早在2024年4月便已正式啟動——比Pax Silica爭議高峰早了逾兩年——由G7「全球基礎設施與投資夥伴關係」（PGI）框架發起，主要參與方包括美國、日本及多個盟友夥伴。

  LEC以四個工業節點為核心架構：蘇比克灣，定位為深水港、造船業及海洋貿易樞紐；克拉克，定位為航空中心、科技園區及高科技製造節點，側重高附加價值半導體產業；首都圈（NCR），定位為主要經濟與金融中心；以及八打雁（Batangas），定位為馬尼拉以南的主要工業港口、製造業及能源樞紐。

  若無LEC基礎設施到位，克拉克園區根本無從運作。這條走廊本就是為未來進駐新克拉克市廠房所需的水資源、電力、運輸物流及技術勞動力提供保障。其中最核心的計畫是一條蘇比克—克拉克—馬尼拉—八打雁鐵路，可串聯各大深水港，並舒緩馬尼拉都會區的交通壅塞問題。LEC其他組成項目還包括：電網穩定升級、燃料輸配管線、民用核能教育計畫、再生能源整合、半導體封裝與電子供應鏈擴充，以及在各工業園區部署安全第五代（5G）無線網路。

  ## 投資論壇與美國能源補助款

  9月10日至11日舉行的LEC投資論壇，分析報告認為本應在Pax Silica公開宣傳之前便先行召開。此次論壇匯聚超過600名國際投資人、業界高層及政府要員，最具指標性的成果是正式簽署一筆6,000萬美元的「菲律賓能源門檻計畫」補助款，透過美國千禧挑戰公司（MCC）撥付。這筆資金專為走廊的潔淨能源基礎設施提供支援，涵蓋電網穩定、機構能力建設，以及卡拉巴松（Calabarzon）和中呂宋地區既有工業區預期產生的電力需求。

  在較近期加入LEC的成員中，西班牙宣布將在走廊框架下推動鐵路、模組化基礎設施、航空、造船、航空導航管理及再生能源連結等領域的相關計畫。

  菲律賓總統小費迪南德·馬科斯（Ferdinand Marcos Jr.）在其第四次國情咨文中宣布，菲律賓已準備好考慮興建模組化核能電廠以降低能源成本，並點名美國與南韓為LEC框架下的潛在技術合作夥伴。

  ## 工業整合與房地產擴展

  克拉克與蘇比克的工業發展正式與Pax Silica接軌。包括新克拉克市預定園區及塔拉克省TARI園區在內的重要不動產開發計畫，均正擴大規模，以容納高附加價值電子產品、硬體製造及自動化倉儲業務。

  ## 後續發展

  這場爭議揭示了推進順序上的政治風險——在基礎設施依據、環境評估程序與社區諮詢尚未就緒之前，便搶先對外宣布高規格國際倡議，必然招致反彈。LEC投資論壇的召開與MCC能源補助款的簽署，是菲律賓政府首次採取的重大公開行動，試圖填補這道說明上的缺口。至於這些行動能否讓地方官員及受影響社區——包括面臨潛在遷離的原住民族群——感到滿意，將直接左右克拉克園區從規劃到動工的推進速度。
