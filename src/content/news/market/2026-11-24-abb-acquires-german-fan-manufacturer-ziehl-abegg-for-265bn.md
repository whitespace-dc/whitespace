body_en: |
  ABB has acquired Ziehl-Abegg, a German manufacturer of fans, integrated electric motors, and ventilation and control technologies, for $2.65bn, the Swiss multinational technology company announced.

  The deal adds Ziehl-Abegg's electronically commutated (EC) fan technology — a type of brushless electric motor known for high energy efficiency — to ABB's existing portfolio of electric motors and drives. ABB said the addition broadens its offering for mission-critical cooling systems.

  ## About Ziehl-Abegg

  Ziehl-Abegg is headquartered in Künzelsau, in the Baden-Württemberg region of southern Germany. The family-owned business was founded in Berlin in 1910 by Emil Ziehl as a manufacturer of electric motors. Following World War II, the company relocated its headquarters to southern Germany.

  The company employs approximately 6,300 people across operations in Europe, North America, South America, and Asia. It is expected to generate sales of approximately $1.4bn in 2026.

  ## Recent investment

  Ziehl-Abegg has been expanding its manufacturing footprint ahead of the acquisition. In 2024, the company opened a centre in the United States dedicated to manufacturing, logistics, and research and development, representing an investment of €100m (approximately $106m at current exchange rates). In 2026, the company launched a separate global investment programme of €90m to increase capacity in Europe, North America, and Asia.

  ## Strategic rationale

  ABB's Motion business president Brandon Spencer said the company had long admired Ziehl-Abegg's technical capabilities. "We have always admired Ziehl-Abegg's technical excellence, leading research and development capabilities and strong customer focus, as well as the company's entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to innovation," Spencer said.

  For the data center and industrial cooling sectors, the acquisition is significant. Cooling systems in hyperscale data centers and other mission-critical facilities increasingly rely on EC fans and integrated motor-drive packages for energy efficiency and controllability. ABB, which already supplies variable-speed drives and motors widely used in such applications, gains a vertically integrated fan product line through the deal.

  Ziehl-Abegg's ongoing €90m global capacity expansion programme, launched in 2026, signals that production growth is planned across multiple regions as demand for high-efficiency cooling equipment rises.

  瑞士跨國科技集團ABB宣布，以26.5億美元收購德國風扇、整合式電動馬達及通風控制技術製造商Ziehl-Abegg。

  此次交易將Ziehl-Abegg的電子換向（EC）風扇技術——一種以高能源效率著稱的無刷電動馬達——納入ABB現有的電動馬達與驅動器產品組合。ABB表示，此舉將進一步擴充其在關鍵任務冷卻系統領域的解決方案。

  ## 關於Ziehl-Abegg

  Ziehl-Abegg總部設於德國南部巴登-符騰堡邦的屈恩策爾紹（Künzelsau）。這家家族企業由Emil Ziehl於1910年在柏林創立，起初專注於電動馬達製造。二戰結束後，公司將總部遷往德國南部。

  目前，Ziehl-Abegg在歐洲、北美、南美及亞洲均設有營運據點，員工總數約6,300人。預計2026年營收約達14億美元。

  ## 近期投資動向

  在收購案敲定之前，Ziehl-Abegg已積極拓展製造版圖。2024年，該公司在美國設立了一座整合製造、物流及研發功能的中心，投資金額達1億歐元（依目前匯率約合1.06億美元）。2026年，公司另行啟動一項9,000萬歐元的全球擴產計畫，目標是提升在歐洲、北美及亞洲的生產能力。

  ## 策略布局

  ABB動力事業部總裁Brandon Spencer表示，公司長期以來對Ziehl-Abegg抱持高度欣賞。「我們一直對Ziehl-Abegg卓越的技術實力、領先的研發能力、對客戶的高度重視，以及公司的創業精神與創新承諾深感欽佩，」Spencer說道。

  就資料中心與工業冷卻產業而言，這項收購案具有重要的戰略意義。超大規模資料中心及其他關鍵任務設施的冷卻系統，愈來愈仰賴EC風扇與整合式馬達驅動套件，以實現節能目標並提升控制精準度。ABB本已在相關應用場域廣泛供應變速驅動器與馬達；透過此次收購，ABB更進一步取得垂直整合的風扇產品線。

  Ziehl-Abegg於2026年啟動、規模達9,000萬歐元的全球擴產計畫，顯示隨著高效冷卻設備需求持續升溫，各地區的產能擴充均已列入規劃。
