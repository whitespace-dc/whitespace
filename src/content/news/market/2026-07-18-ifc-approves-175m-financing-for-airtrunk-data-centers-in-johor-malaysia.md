body_en: |
  The International Finance Corporation (IFC), the private-sector lending arm of the World Bank Group, has approved a financing package of up to $175 million for data center operator AirTrunk Group to support two facilities in Johor, Malaysia, according to a disclosure published on the IFC's website. The package combines debt financing and equity investment, and was pending formal signing by the IFC board as of July 14.

  The financing covers two projects: JHB1, a 150-megawatt (MW) fully contracted hyperscale data center, and JHB2, a 270-MW greenfield facility to be developed on an adjacent site. A megawatt of data center capacity is a common measure of the power available to run servers and cooling equipment; at that scale, both facilities rank among the largest single-site deployments in the region. JHB2, at 270 MW, has not yet been built.

  ## What the IFC says the money is for

  The IFC described the financing's purpose as developing and expanding infrastructure "required to support delivery of more digital services in Malaysia and the Asia Pacific region." The institution also said it expects the projects to create jobs during both construction and operations, and to "enhance the competitiveness of the data hosting services market in Malaysia and EAP region by facilitating the expansion of a leading data center provider."

  ## AirTrunk's ownership

  AirTrunk's key shareholders are Blackstone, the U.S.-based global alternative asset manager, and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB), a Canadian institutional investor.

  ## Broader context

  The Johor financing comes alongside a separate reported development: AirTrunk is planning to list a Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT), a vehicle that allows investors to hold stakes in income-producing real estate assets, targeting a raise of approximately $1.5 billion, with the listing expected sometime in September or October, according to earlier reporting.

  世界銀行集團旗下負責私部門融資的國際金融公司（IFC），已批准一筆最高達1.75億美元的融資方案，對象為資料中心營運商AirTrunk集團，用以支持該集團在馬來西亞柔佛州興建的兩座設施。此消息來自IFC官網所發布的揭露文件。本融資方案結合債務融資與股權投資兩種形式，截至7月14日仍待IFC董事會正式簽署。

  此次融資涵蓋兩個專案：其一為JHB1，是一座已全數簽約、裝置容量達150百萬瓦（MW）的超大規模資料中心；其二為JHB2，是一座規劃裝置容量達270 MW、將在毗鄰地塊上開發的全新建案。百萬瓦是衡量資料中心可用電力容量的常見單位，用以支撐伺服器運算及冷卻設備所需的電力；以此規模而言，兩座設施均堪稱區域內規模最大的單一站點部署之列。其中JHB2的270 MW容量尚未動工興建。

  ## IFC說明資金用途

  IFC表示，此次融資的目的在於開發並擴建「支持馬來西亞及亞太地區提供更多數位服務所需」的基礎建設。IFC同時指出，預期這兩個專案在建設期與營運期均將創造就業機會，並將「藉由協助一家領先資料中心業者擴張，提升馬來西亞及東亞太平洋地區資料代管服務市場的競爭力」。

  ## AirTrunk的股權結構

  AirTrunk的主要股東為美國全球另類資產管理公司百仕通（Blackstone），以及加拿大機構投資人加拿大退休金計畫投資委員會（CPPIB）。

  ## 更廣泛的背景
