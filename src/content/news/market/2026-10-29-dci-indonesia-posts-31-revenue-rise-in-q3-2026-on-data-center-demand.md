body_en: |
  PT DCI Indonesia Tbk (ticker: DCII), the Jakarta-listed data center operator backed by investors Otto Toto Sugiri and Anthony Salim, reported a 31.49% year-on-year increase in revenue and an 11.5% rise in net profit for the January–September 2026 period, according to the company's third-quarter results.

  The figures cover the first three quarters of the 2026 financial year. The company did not disclose absolute revenue or profit figures in the published summary.

  ## Data center and colocation drive results

  DCII's data center and colocation segment — the business of housing and managing servers and network equipment for corporate clients — accounted for 94.27% of total company revenue in the period, the company said. The result confirms that segment as the dominant and effectively sole engine of the company's top-line growth.

  Colocation services allow businesses to place their own IT hardware inside a third-party facility, paying for physical space, power, and connectivity rather than building and operating their own infrastructure.

  ## Ownership and market position

  DCII is a portfolio company of Otto Toto Sugiri, a prominent figure in Indonesia's technology sector, and Anthony Salim, head of the Salim Group conglomerate. Bisnis.com described the company as occupying a strong market position among Indonesia's listed data center operators.

  ## Significance

  The results indicate sustained demand for data center capacity in Indonesia through the first nine months of 2026, with revenue growing at a pace that outstripped profit expansion — a gap that may reflect rising operating or capital costs associated with scaling capacity. The near-total revenue concentration in data center and colocation services leaves the company closely tied to the trajectory of that single market segment.

  PT DCI Indonesia Tbk（股票代號：DCII）是一家在雅加達掛牌上市的資料中心業者，背後投資人包括奧托·托托·蘇吉里（Otto Toto Sugiri）與安東尼·薩林（Anthony Salim）。根據該公司第三季財報，2026年1月至9月期間，營收較去年同期成長31.49%，淨利則增加11.5%。

  上述數據涵蓋2026財年前三季。公司在已公布的財報摘要中，並未揭露營收與獲利的絕對金額。

  ## 資料中心與共置機房業務推動業績

  DCII的資料中心與共置機房部門——即為企業客戶代管並維運伺服器與網路設備的業務——在本報告期間貢獻了公司總營收的94.27%。此一結果確立了該部門作為公司整體營收成長主要引擎、乃至幾乎唯一動力來源的地位。

  所謂共置機房服務，是指企業將自有IT硬體設備部署於第三方機房設施之中，按所使用的實體空間、電力與網路連線付費，而無需自行興建與營運基礎設施。

  ## 股權結構與市場地位

  DCII是印尼科技界知名人士奧托·托托·蘇吉里，以及薩林集團（Salim Group）負責人安東尼·薩林旗下的投資組合公司。印尼財經媒體《Bisnis.com》指出，DCII在印尼掛牌上市的資料中心業者之中，市場地位十分穩固。

  ## 數據的意義

  此次財報顯示，印尼資料中心容量需求在2026年前九個月持續維持強勁態勢，惟營收成長幅度明顯超過獲利成長幅度——這一差距或許反映出擴充容量所帶來的營運成本或資本支出有所上升。此外，公司營收高度集中於資料中心與共置機房單一業務，意味著其整體表現將與該市場區塊的走向緊密連動。
