body_en: |
  Telekom Malaysia Bhd's data centres in the Klang Valley and Iskandar Puteri are serving roughly equal numbers of Western and Chinese hyperscaler customers, the company disclosed during its third-quarter financial year 2026 results briefing, according to research notes published by multiple investment banks.

  ## Customer mix and workload type

  Hong Leong Investment Bank Research said TM disclosed the even Western-Chinese split at its existing facilities during the Q3 briefing. Demand at those sites is largely for central processing unit (CPU)-based cloud workloads — the standard compute infrastructure used to run cloud services — rather than graphics processing units (GPUs), which are purpose-built for artificial intelligence training and inference. GPU demand comes mainly from TM's internal use, government clients, and local enterprises, Hong Leong Investment Bank Research said.

  ## US chip compliance and colocation model

  RHB Research quoted TM's management as saying the group is fully compliant with US guidelines on advanced chips — export-control rules that restrict the sale of high-performance semiconductors to certain customers and geographies. TM's data centres cater predominantly to colocation customers, RHB Research said. Colocation facilities provide tenants with space, power, and cooling while the tenants operate their own equipment, as distinct from cloud services where the operator manages the hardware.

  ## TM Nxera Phase One progress

  TM Nxera's 64-megawatt Phase One data centre — a capacity that can support tens of thousands of servers depending on power density — is 70% contracted and on track to be fully ready for service by the first quarter of 2027, according to Hong Leong Investment Bank Research. Planning for Phase Two is already underway, the research house said.

  ## Financial results and guidance

  TA Research said TM has maintained its full-year 2026 guidance for low single-digit revenue growth and earnings before interest and tax (EBIT) broadly in line with FY25 levels. TM reported a core profit of RM1.29 billion in the first nine months of FY2026, after stripping out a RM127.3 million write-down of unutilised prepaid 5G capacity with Digital Nasional Bhd and other exceptional items, TA Research said.

  The nine-month result came in within expectations, accounting for 70% of TA Research's full-year estimate and 76.2% of consensus full-year forecasts, TA Research said.

  ## Significance

  The even split across Western and Chinese hyperscalers at TM's existing facilities, alongside management's confirmation of full compliance with US advanced-chip guidelines and a predominantly colocation operating model, describes a demand base and compliance posture TM chose to highlight at the quarterly briefing. With Phase One 70% contracted and Phase Two in planning, TM Nxera's next capacity increment is scheduled to come fully online in the first quarter of 2027.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞電信有限公司（Telekom Malaysia Bhd）在巴生河流域及依斯干達布城的資料中心，所服務的超大規模雲端客戶中，西方與中國業者比例約各占一半。公司於 2026 財年第三季業績說明會上披露上述資訊，消息來源為多家投資銀行相繼發布的研究報告。

  ## 客戶組成與工作負載類型

  豐隆投資銀行研究部（Hong Leong Investment Bank Research）指出，馬來西亞電信於第三季說明會上披露，現有設施的西方與中國客戶比例相當。這些據點的需求，主要來自以中央處理器（CPU）為主的雲端工作負載——亦即用於支撐雲端服務的標準運算基礎架構——而非專為人工智慧訓練與推論設計的繪圖處理器（GPU）。豐隆投資銀行研究部表示，GPU 需求主要來自馬來西亞電信的內部使用、政府客戶及本地企業。

  ## 美國晶片法規遵循與主機代管模式

  RHB Research 引述馬來西亞電信管理層表示，集團完全符合美國對高階晶片的相關規範——即限制高性能半導體向特定客戶及地區出售的出口管制規定。RHB Research 指出，馬來西亞電信的資料中心主要服務主機代管（colocation）客戶。主機代管設施向租戶提供空間、電力與冷卻基礎設施，由租戶自行負責管理設備，有別於由業者統一管理硬體的雲端服務模式。

  ## TM Nxera 第一期建設進度

  根據豐隆投資銀行研究部，TM Nxera 容量達 64 百萬瓦（MW）的第一期資料中心，簽約率已達 70%，預計於 2027 年第一季全面投入服務；以此容量規模，依功率密度不同，可支援數萬台伺服器。研究機構亦指出，第二期的規劃工作已同步展開。

  ## 財務業績與前景展望

  TA Research 表示，馬來西亞電信維持 2026 全年度低個位數營收成長，以及息稅前利潤（EBIT）與 2025 財年大致持平的全年指引。TA Research 指出，扣除與 Digital Nasional Bhd 之間 1.273 億馬幣的未使用預付 5G 容量減記及其他特殊項目後，馬來西亞電信 2026 財年首九個月核心獲利為 12.9 億馬幣。

  TA Research 表示，九個月業績符合市場預期，相當於該機構全年預估值的 70%，以及市場共識全年預測的 76.2%。

  ## 重要意義

  馬來西亞電信現有設施中西方與中國超大規模業者比例相當，加上管理層確認完全符合美國高階晶片出口管制規範、以主機代管為主要營運模式，這些均是馬來西亞電信選擇在季度說明會上特別強調的需求結構與合規立場。隨著第一期簽約率達 70%，第二期規劃亦已展開，TM Nxera 下一個容量增量預計將於 2027 年第一季全面上線。
