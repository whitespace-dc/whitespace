body_en: |
  Vantage Data Centers, the hyperscale (very large-scale) data center developer backed by DigitalBridge Group Inc., is in discussions to sell its Malaysian data center portfolio at a potential valuation of US$2 billion, Bloomberg reported, citing people with knowledge of the matter.

  Vantage has engaged a financial adviser to explore the transaction and talks are continuing, according to the report. The company could still choose not to proceed with a deal. Neither Vantage nor DigitalBridge responded to requests for comment.

  ## Malaysia portfolio

  The assets under consideration span two locations. In Johor, in southern Malaysia, Vantage owns the JHB1 campus — a site of nearly 73 acres comprising three data centers with a combined capacity of 300 megawatts (MW), a measure of the electrical power the facility can deliver to computing equipment. Vantage completed the acquisition of the campus from Yondr in November 2025; at the time, it was described as one of the largest, if not the largest, data center campus in Malaysia. One of the three buildings is operational; the remaining two are under development.

  In Cyberjaya, a technology district outside Kuala Lumpur, Vantage operates the KUL1 data center at 31 MW and is developing a second campus on an adjacent plot spanning 4.8 million square feet. When fully built out, that expansion is expected to deliver 436 MW of capacity across six data centers.

  ## Corporate context

  DigitalBridge, the infrastructure investment firm that controls Vantage, is separately the subject of an acquisition approach by Japan's SoftBank Group for approximately US$3 billion.

  Vantage's global portfolio spans 26 markets across five continents, with a total stated capacity of 9 gigawatts (GW) — or 9,000 MW — according to the company's website.

  ## What a sale would mean

  A completed transaction at the reported figure would crystallize a US$2 billion value for the Malaysian assets against the backdrop of Vantage's 9 GW global platform. The reported sale discussions coincide with SoftBank's separate bid for Vantage's parent, DigitalBridge.

  The sale process remains at an early stage, with Vantage still working with a financial adviser and no agreement yet reached.

  此次洽售的資產分布於兩個地點。在馬來西亞南部的柔佛州，Vantage擁有JHB1園區——該地占地近73英畝，包含三座資料中心，合計容量達300百萬瓦（MW）；MW為衡量設施可向運算設備提供電力規模的單位。Vantage於2025年11月完成向Yondr收購該園區；當時被描述為馬來西亞規模數一數二甚至最大的資料中心園區。三棟建築中已有一棟正式投入運營，其餘兩棟仍在開發建設中。

  ## 企業背景

  控股Vantage的基礎建設投資公司DigitalBridge，另行成為日本軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）收購洽談的對象，收購估值約為30億美元。

  根據Vantage官方網站，其全球資產組合橫跨五大洲26個市場，公司宣稱的總容量達9吉瓦（GW）——即9,000MW。

  ## 出售的意義

  若此筆交易以前述金額完成，將在Vantage 9GW全球平台的格局下，確立馬來西亞資產的20億美元價值。此次傳出的出售洽談，與軟銀另行競購Vantage母公司DigitalBridge的動作同步展開。

  整體出售程序目前仍處於初步階段，Vantage仍與財務顧問持續合作，雙方尚未達成任何協議。
