body_en: |
  LFB Group, the former Lennox EMEA business, has acquired French air handling unit (AHU) manufacturer Hydronic from the Eoliance air treatment group, the companies announced.

  Hydronic, established in 1976 and based in Mortagne-au-Perche in Normandy, designs, develops, manufactures and markets AHUs. Its primary product is the MCX platform, described as a new generation of fully parametric and flexible air handling units. AHUs are mechanical systems that condition and circulate air as part of heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) installations.

  ## Strategic fit for LFB

  LFB said the acquisition strengthens a portfolio that already includes refrigeration brands Friga-Bohn and Apx DCS. The group operates across 46 countries, a commercial footprint that Eoliance said Hydronic would now be able to draw on.

  Eoliance said the deal would give Hydronic access to industrial synergies, purchasing scale and research and development resources it could not access as a standalone unit within the Eoliance group.

  ## MCX platform deployment

  "This transaction will allow Hydronic to continue its development and accelerate the deployment of the MCX platform on a European scale," a company statement said. The MCX platform is Hydronic's flagship product line and the stated focus of the post-acquisition growth plan.

  ## 策略布局的最佳契合

  LFB表示，此次收購進一步鞏固了旗下已涵蓋冷凍品牌Friga-Bohn與Apx DCS的產品組合。集團業務橫跨46個國家，Eoliance表示，Hydronic如今得以借助這一廣泛的商業版圖拓展市場觸角。

  Eoliance指出，此交易將使Hydronic獲得作為Eoliance旗下獨立部門時所無法取得的產業協同效益、採購規模優勢及研發資源。

  ## MCX平台部署加速

  「此次交易將使Hydronic得以延續其發展動能，並在歐洲規模上加速部署MCX平台，」公司聲明表示。MCX平台是Hydronic的旗艦產品線，也是收購後成長計畫的明確推進重心。
