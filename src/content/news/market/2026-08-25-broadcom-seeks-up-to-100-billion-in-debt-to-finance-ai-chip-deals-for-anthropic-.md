body_en: |
  Broadcom is in talks with a group of lenders to raise more than $60 billion in debt to finance purchases of AI chips and data center equipment for companies including Anthropic, with a potential junior tranche pushing the total to as much as $100 billion, according to people with knowledge of the matter.

  The financing structure centers on a senior-secured tranche of between $60 billion and $70 billion, a portion of which Broadcom would guarantee. A roughly $30 billion junior debt tranche may also be included. The debt would be issued through a special-purpose vehicle, an entity created specifically to hold the financing.

  ## Parties and Structure

  Blackstone and Apollo Global Management are in talks to participate in the deal, the sources said, following a partnership the three companies struck in June to co-finance computing infrastructure. Talks are continuing, details may change, and the financing may be deployed incrementally rather than all at once. Spokespeople for Broadcom, Anthropic, Apollo, and Blackstone all declined to comment.

  The proposed deal is modeled on the $35 billion agreement that established the group's AI XPV platform. In that transaction, Broadcom backstopped the bulk of the debt while investors including Apollo and Blackstone used the proceeds to purchase custom AI chips, which were then leased to Anthropic. That structure enabled the senior debt tranches to achieve investment-grade credit ratings, reducing borrowing costs.

  ## Scale of the Build-Out

  The AI XPV partnership has set a target of financing more than 20 gigawatts of computing power. The sources described that level as roughly equivalent to the output of 20 nuclear power plants, and said reaching it will require hundreds of billions of dollars in total. The new deal would represent a second major tranche toward that figure.

  The financing is part of a broader surge in capital formation for AI infrastructure. Earlier this month, Nvidia announced that a coalition of financial institutions including BlackRock and Goldman Sachs were arranging more than $500 billion to help fund the AI build-out.

  ## Broadcom's Competitive Position

  For Broadcom, the financing arrangement drives demand for its custom AI chips and data center equipment in competition with Nvidia. Broadcom's chief executive said in March that the company expects AI chip sales to exceed $100 billion next year. The chipmaker has also struck a separate supply agreement with Apple expected to be worth more than $30 billion, and produces custom AI chips for companies including OpenAI, agreements that have driven a significant rise in its valuation.

  For AI companies such as Anthropic, maker of the Claude AI platform, the structure provides access to computing capacity without bearing the full capital cost directly.

  Broadcom shares rose as much as 1.1 percent in late trading after Bloomberg News reported on the discussions; the stock had gained 5.2 percent through the year-to-date close before the report.

  博通（Broadcom）正與一批貸款機構洽談，計劃舉借逾600億美元債務，為包括Anthropic在內的企業融資採購AI晶片及資料中心設備；若加入次順位融資，總額最高可達1,000億美元。知情人士透露了上述消息。

  本次融資架構的核心，是一筆規模介於600億至700億美元之間的優先擔保債券，博通將為其中一部分提供擔保。方案中另可能納入約300億美元的次順位債券。這批債務將透過一家特殊目的機構（SPV）發行，該機構係專為持有此筆融資而設立。

  ## 參與方與架構

  知情人士表示，黑石（Blackstone）與阿波羅全球管理（Apollo Global Management）正洽談加入這項交易。三方早於今年6月已建立合作夥伴關係，共同為運算基礎設施提供聯合融資。目前談判仍在進行中，細節可能有所更動；融資亦可能分批逐步部署，而非一次到位。博通、Anthropic、阿波羅與黑石的發言人均拒絕置評。

  本次交易以先前那筆350億美元協議為藍本——正是那筆協議催生了三方的AI XPV平台。在原始交易中，博通為大部分債務提供背書，阿波羅、黑石等投資方則以所得資金採購客製化AI晶片，再將晶片租賃給Anthropic使用。這套架構使優先債券部分取得了投資等級信用評等，從而有效降低了借貸成本。

  ## 建設規模

  AI XPV合作夥伴關係設定的目標，是為超過20吉瓦（GW）的運算能力提供融資。知情人士指出，這一規模大致相當於20座核電廠的總發電量，而要達到這個目標，所需總資金規模將高達數千億美元。此次新交易將成為邁向這一目標的第二筆重大融資。

  本次融資是AI基礎設施資本籌募浪潮的一環。本月稍早，輝達（Nvidia）宣布，包括貝萊德（BlackRock）與高盛（Goldman Sachs）在內的金融機構聯盟，正為AI基礎設施建設籌措逾5,000億美元的龐大資金。

  ## 博通的競爭布局

  對博通而言，這套融資安排有助於為其客製化AI晶片及資料中心設備創造需求，直接與輝達展開競爭。博通執行長今年3月表示，公司預計明年AI晶片銷售額將突破1,000億美元。這家晶片製造商另與蘋果（Apple）達成了獨立供應協議，預計金額逾300億美元；與此同時，博通也為包括OpenAI在內的多家企業生產客製化AI晶片，這些合約已顯著推升其公司估值。

  對於Anthropic——旗下擁有Claude AI平台的人工智慧公司——而言，這套架構讓其得以取得充裕的運算資源，而無需直接承擔全部資本支出。
