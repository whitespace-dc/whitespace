body_en: |
  The European Union is preparing a €525 million (approximately $590 million) investment to strengthen semiconductor startups and narrow the technology gap with the United States and China, according to Kontan.

  The funding is divided into two streams: €440 million will be distributed as grants to semiconductor startups, and €85 million will be directed toward building a European chip design platform.

  ## Investment structure

  The grant component — €440 million — represents the larger share of the initiative and targets early-stage semiconductor companies. The remaining €85 million is earmarked specifically for the development of a shared European chip design infrastructure, which would give startups and researchers a common platform for designing semiconductors rather than relying on tools and ecosystems based outside the region.

  ## Strategic context

  The investment forms part of Europe's broader effort to establish technological self-sufficiency in semiconductors, an industry facing surging demand driven by artificial intelligence applications, according to Kontan. The initiative is framed as a response to the competitive positions held by the United States and China in chip development and production.

  The push for a dedicated European chip design platform signals that the EU's semiconductor ambitions extend beyond manufacturing capacity to the upstream design phase, where much of a chip's intellectual and commercial value originates.

  If executed as described, the initiative would direct nearly three-quarters of its total funding to direct startup support, with the remaining quarter building shared design infrastructure intended to serve the broader European semiconductor ecosystem.

  此次資金分為兩大流向：4.4億歐元將以補助款形式發放給半導體新創公司，另外8,500萬歐元則專門用於建構歐洲晶片設計平台。

  ## 投資架構

  補助款部分——4.4億歐元——占此次計畫的最大比重，主要針對處於早期階段的半導體企業。其餘8,500萬歐元則專款用於開發歐洲共用晶片設計基礎設施，讓新創公司與研究人員能共享一套半導體設計平台，不必再仰賴源自歐洲以外地區的工具與生態系統。

  ## 戰略背景

  根據Kontan，這項投資是歐洲在半導體領域尋求技術自主更宏觀布局的一環。半導體產業正因人工智慧應用需求激增而承受巨大壓力，此舉也被視為回應美國與中國在晶片開發與生產方面既有競爭優勢的戰略行動。

  致力打造專屬歐洲晶片設計平台的決心，說明歐盟在半導體領域的野心不僅止於製造產能，更延伸至上游設計階段——而晶片的智慧財產與商業價值，大部分正是在這個階段形成的。
