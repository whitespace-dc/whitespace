body_en: |
  Nvidia was in talks to invest hundreds of millions of dollars in Cloverleaf Infrastructure, a startup that secures power supply agreements for data center development sites, the Wall Street Journal reported on August 21, with people familiar with the matter saying a deal could close as early as that morning.

  Cloverleaf had been working with JPMorgan Chase in recent months on strategic options that included a potential sale of the business, according to those people.

  ## What Cloverleaf does

  Founded in 2024, Cloverleaf raised $300 million that year from energy investors NG and Sandbrook Capital. The company specializes in negotiating power supply agreements with utilities, enabling data center developers to build on land with a secured electricity connection. Over the past two years, Cloverleaf has sold land plans with associated power capacity totaling more than 7 gigawatts (GW) — a gigawatt is a unit of electrical power capacity equal to one billion watts — to data center developers. Customers include Oracle and OpenAI.

  ## Nvidia's role in early-stage development

  If completed, the Cloverleaf investment would further establish Nvidia's role at the earliest stages of the data center development cycle, before sites are construction-ready, according to the people cited by the Journal. By directing capital toward power-secured land at that stage, sometimes years before a data center is operational, Nvidia aims to ensure future facilities have adequate electricity to run its chips, those people said.

  The Cloverleaf talks follow Nvidia's $2 billion investment earlier this year in Lancium, the energy developer behind a campus in Abilene, Texas. OpenAI leases computing capacity from Oracle at that campus.

  《華爾街日報》於8月21日報導，輝達（Nvidia）正與新創公司Cloverleaf Infrastructure洽談，擬投資數億美元。知情人士透露，雙方協議最快於當日上午即可正式拍板。

  Cloverleaf近幾個月來已委託摩根大通（JPMorgan Chase）協助評估策略選項，其中包括出售整家公司的可能性。

  ## Cloverleaf的業務模式

  Cloverleaf成立於2024年，同年即從能源投資機構NG與Sandbrook Capital募得3億美元資金。該公司專長在於代表客戶與電力公司談判供電協議，協助資料中心開發商在已確保電力連線的土地上進行建設。過去兩年間，Cloverleaf已向多家資料中心開發商售出附帶電力容量的用地計畫，累計規模超過7吉瓦（GW）——1吉瓦即10億瓦的電力容量單位——客戶涵蓋甲骨文（Oracle）與OpenAI。

  ## 輝達搶進資料中心開發早期階段

  知情人士向《華爾街日報》表示，一旦此次投資案完成，將進一步確立輝達在資料中心開發週期最早期階段的關鍵地位——早在建設工程就緒之前，輝達便已深度介入。透過在這個階段將資金導向已確保供電的用地，有時甚至早於資料中心正式營運數年之前，輝達的目的在於確保未來的設施擁有足夠的電力來驅動其晶片。

  此次與Cloverleaf的洽談，緊接在輝達今年稍早以20億美元投資能源開發商Lancium之後。Lancium在德克薩斯州艾比林（Abilene）開發了一座大型園區，OpenAI正是在該園區向甲骨文租用運算資源。
