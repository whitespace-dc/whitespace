body_en: |
  Taiwan-based AI infrastructure software company INFINITIX has signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with Sarawak Information Systems (SAINS), the primary ICT partner of the Sarawak state government in Malaysia, to jointly develop GPU private cloud, AI cloud services, and computing power platforms across Southeast Asia, the company announced.

  The agreement covers four areas: GPU private cloud deployment, AI computing power platforms, technical advisory services, and what the company describes as a Token Economy — a framework for pricing and transacting access to AI computing capacity as a service.

  ## What each party brings

  INFINITIX will contribute two proprietary platforms. The first, AI-Stack, is a heterogeneous computing power management platform — software designed to schedule and allocate GPU resources across hardware from different vendors. The second, ixCSP, is an AI cloud operations platform aimed at cloud service providers. Together, the company said, the platforms are intended to help governments and enterprises deploy GPU private clouds and monetize computing capacity as a service, while accelerating deployment of generative AI, Agentic AI (AI systems that autonomously execute multi-step tasks), and large language models (LLMs).

  SAINS, founded in 1991, employs more than 1,100 professionals and has built more than 300 information systems for the Sarawak state government, according to the announcement. The company has active programs in smart city development, Digital Twin (real-time digital replicas of physical systems), Agentic AI, and Sovereign AI Infrastructure — computing capacity owned and operated within a jurisdiction's own borders to preserve data control. SAINS has also been developing GPU-as-a-Service (GaaS) and LLM-as-a-Service (LLMaaS) offerings for government and enterprise customers.

  INFINITIX is a certified NVIDIA Solution Advisor global partner and a recipient of AMD's GPU Ecosystem Construction Partner Award, the company said. It has been expanding operations in Japan, South Korea, and Southeast Asia.

  ## The sovereign AI rationale

  INFINITIX Chief Executive and co-founder Chen Wen-yu said the global AI industry's competitive focus has shifted from model capability to computing power management, with the critical challenge now being efficient GPU resource scheduling and building commercially sustainable AI service models. The partnership with SAINS, he said, combines SAINS's experience in government digital governance and data center operations with INFINITIX's computing management platforms to build AI infrastructure that addresses computational efficiency, data security, and data sovereignty simultaneously.

  The company framed the deal within a broader industry shift: as sovereign AI — meaning nationally or regionally controlled AI computing and data — becomes a policy priority for governments pursuing digital transformation, demand for localized GPU infrastructure has grown alongside it.

  ## Southeast Asia expansion context

  The SAINS agreement is part of INFINITIX's broader push to build overseas partnerships around AI computing platforms, AI cloud economics, and what it calls Token Factory — a model for packaging and selling computing capacity. The company said the deal is intended to strengthen its Southeast Asia market position and expand regional AI cloud services and computing ecosystems for both government and enterprise customers.

  SAINS's role as the Sarawak state government's principal ICT partner positions the collaboration to pursue public-sector AI infrastructure contracts in the region. Both parties said they plan to integrate their technical and market resources to accelerate AI digital transformation for government and enterprise clients across Southeast Asia.

  ## INFINITIX與馬來西亞SAINS簽署MOU　攜手布建東南亞GPU雲端與AI基礎設施

  台灣AI基礎設施軟體公司INFINITIX宣布，已與馬來西亞沙勞越州政府主要ICT合作夥伴——沙勞越資訊系統公司（Sarawak Information Systems，簡稱SAINS）簽署合作備忘錄（MOU），雙方將共同開發GPU私有雲、AI雲端服務及運算力平台，業務範圍涵蓋整個東南亞地區。

  此次合作涵蓋四大面向：GPU私有雲部署、AI運算力平台、技術顧問服務，以及公司所稱的「Token Economy」——一套將AI運算力存取服務化、並加以定價與交易的架構機制。

  ## 雙方各自的資源與優勢

  INFINITIX將貢獻兩套自有平台。其一為「AI-Stack」，這是一套異質運算力管理平台，專為跨不同硬體廠商的GPU資源進行排程與調度而設計；其二為「ixCSP」，是一套面向雲端服務供應商的AI雲端營運平台。INFINITIX表示，兩套平台合力之下，可協助政府與企業部署GPU私有雲、將運算力商品化為服務，同時加速生成式AI、代理型AI（Agentic AI，即能自主執行多步驟任務的AI系統）及大型語言模型（LLM）的落地應用。

  SAINS成立於1991年，現有逾1,100名專業人員，累計已為沙勞越州政府建置超過300套資訊系統。該公司目前在智慧城市發展、數位孿生（Digital Twin，即實體系統的即時數位複製模型）、代理型AI及主權AI基礎設施等領域均有積極布局——所謂主權AI基礎設施，係指在特定管轄範圍內自主擁有並運營的AI運算能量，以確保資料主權。SAINS亦已著手為政府與企業客戶開發「GPU即服務」（GaaS）及「LLM即服務」（LLMaaS）等產品。

  INFINITIX表示，該公司是輝達（NVIDIA）認證的全球解決方案顧問夥伴，並榮獲AMD頒發的GPU生態系建構夥伴獎，目前正積極拓展在日本、韓國及東南亞的業務版圖。

  ## 主權AI的戰略邏輯

  INFINITIX執行長暨共同創辦人陳文宇表示，全球AI產業的競爭焦點已從模型能力轉向運算力管理，當前最關鍵的挑戰在於如何有效調度GPU資源，並建立具備商業永續性的AI服務模式。他指出，此次與SAINS的合作，結合了SAINS在政府數位治理與資料中心營運上的豐富經驗，以及INFINITIX的運算管理平台，旨在打造能同步兼顧運算效能、資料安全與資料主權的AI基礎設施。

  公司將這項合作定位於更宏觀的產業趨勢之中：隨著主權AI——即由國家或地區自主掌控AI運算能力與資料——逐漸成為各國政府推動數位轉型的政策優先項目，在地化GPU基礎設施的需求也隨之水漲船高。

  ## 東南亞擴張的戰略脈絡

  與SAINS簽署的這份協議，是INFINITIX積極拓展海外夥伴關係、圍繞AI運算平台、AI雲端經濟學及其所稱「Token Factory」模式（一種將運算力打包銷售的商業模型）大力布局的重要一環。公司表示，此次合作旨在鞏固其在東南亞市場的地位，並為政府與企業客戶擴展區域AI雲端服務與運算生態系。

  SAINS作為沙勞越州政府的首席ICT合作夥伴，此一定位使雙方的合作具備積極爭取區域公部門AI基礎設施合約的有利條件。雙方均表示，計畫整合各自的技術與市場資源，加速推動東南亞政府及企業客戶的AI數位轉型進程。
