body_en: |
  Indonesia's state investment manager Danantara is targeting between $20 billion and $30 billion — equivalent to Rp 362.6 trillion to Rp 544 trillion at a rate of Rp 18,100 per dollar — in strategic industrial projects over the next two to three years, the agency's chief technology officer said on October 26, 2026.

  Sigit Puji Santosa, Danantara's CTO, made the announcement at the Danantara Technology Innovation Investment Forum (DTIIF) in Jakarta, outlining a portfolio of more than 40 strategic projects the agency is preparing for a five-year horizon. Semiconductors and advanced materials are the stated priority sectors.

  ## Semiconductor and advanced materials push

  Sigit said Danantara's semiconductor development program spans six areas, including control chips and chips for data centers. The stated aim is to build domestic capacity to produce technology devices, not merely supply infrastructure for them.

  The advanced materials program covers processing of rare earth elements, copper, aluminum, and stainless steel, as well as the development of value-added metal products for manufacturing industry. In minerals more broadly, Danantara said it intends to direct investment beyond extraction into processing, component manufacturing, subsystems, and finished goods.

  "Minerals, advanced materials, and semiconductors are the development priorities because they have the potential to deliver significant economic impact," Sigit said at the forum.

  ## Five industrial clusters

  Danantara's project portfolio is organized around five main industrial clusters: energy; food and health technology; infrastructure, mobility, and telecommunications; defense, maritime, and aerospace; and advanced materials. Additional clusters cover electronics, machinery, financial technology, and semiconductors.

  The pharmaceutical segment includes domestic production of active pharmaceutical ingredients, vaccines, biological drugs, biosimilars, and medical equipment.

  The energy cluster encompasses a 100-gigawatt (GW) solar power development — 100 GW is roughly equivalent to the combined installed power capacity of many mid-sized nations — alongside coal-to-dimethyl ether (DME) conversion, nuclear power generation, an oil refinery, and electric vehicle (EV) battery value chain development.

  ## Approach: existing industry first, new builds if needed

  Sigit said Danantara will prioritize strengthening existing industries, including state-owned enterprises (BUMN — Badan Usaha Milik Negara), before looking to outside partners. If domestic capacity falls short, the agency said it will open cooperation with other companies. Where no suitable existing entity exists, Danantara said it is prepared to build new industries alongside strategic partners.

  ## Significance

  The scale of Danantara's stated investment target — up to $30 billion across strategic industries over a three-year window — positions the agency as a primary driver of Indonesia's effort to move up the industrial value chain beyond raw material extraction and product assembly. The explicit inclusion of data center chips and rare earth processing reflects demand pressures from the global technology build-out and places Indonesia in direct competition with other Southeast Asian nations pursuing similar downstream industrial strategies.

  Danantara said it plans to release further detail on partnership structures for projects where domestic industrial capacity is insufficient to meet the program's requirements.
body_zh: |
  印尼國家投資管理機構Danantara宣布，計畫在未來兩至三年內投入200億至300億美元（按匯率1美元兌18,100印尼盾換算，約合362.6兆至544兆印尼盾）於策略性產業項目。該機構技術長於2026年10月26日公開宣布此一目標。

  Danantara技術長西吉特·普吉·桑托薩（Sigit Puji Santosa）在雅加達舉辦的「Danantara科技創新投資論壇」（DTIIF）上發表上述聲明，並概述該機構正為五年期視野所籌備的逾40項策略計畫。半導體與先進材料為明確的優先發展領域。

  ## 半導體與先進材料布局

  西吉特表示，Danantara的半導體發展計畫涵蓋六大領域，包括控制晶片以及資料中心用晶片。其明確目標在於建立自主生產科技裝置的國內產能，而非僅提供相關基礎設施。

  先進材料計畫涵蓋稀土元素、銅、鋁及不鏽鋼的加工處理，以及面向製造業的高附加價值金屬產品開發。在礦產領域，Danantara表示將引導投資跨越採掘環節，延伸至加工、零組件製造、次系統及最終成品。

  「礦產、先進材料與半導體是發展優先項目，因為這些領域具備創造重大經濟效益的潛力，」西吉特在論壇上表示。

  ## 五大產業聚落

  Danantara的項目組合圍繞五大主要產業聚落規劃：能源；食品與健康科技；基礎設施、交通運輸與電信；國防、海事與航太；以及先進材料。其他聚落則涵蓋電子、機械、金融科技與半導體。

  製藥領域包含原料藥（API）、疫苗、生物藥品、生物相似藥及醫療器材的國內自製生產。

  能源聚落涵蓋100吉瓦（GW）太陽能開發計畫——100 GW大致相當於許多中型國家已安裝發電容量的總和——此外還包括煤炭轉二甲醚（DME）、核能發電、煉油廠，以及電動車（EV）電池價值鏈的建構。

  ## 策略方針：優先強化現有產業，必要時再新建

  西吉特表示，Danantara將優先強化現有產業，包括國營企業（BUMN，即Badan Usaha Milik Negara），再尋求外部合作夥伴。若國內產能不足，該機構表示將開放與其他企業合作。若不存在合適的既有主體，Danantara表示已準備好與策略夥伴共同建立新產業。

  ## 重要意義

  Danantara所揭示的投資目標規模——三年內策略產業最高投入300億美元——使該機構成為印尼推動產業升級、脫離原物料採掘與產品組裝的主要驅動力。明確納入資料中心晶片與稀土加工，反映了全球科技基礎建設擴張所帶來的需求壓力，也使印尼在追求類似下游產業策略的東南亞各國競爭格局中直接展開較量。

  Danantara表示，針對國內產業產能不足以滿足計畫需求的項目，將進一步公布合作架構的詳細說明。
