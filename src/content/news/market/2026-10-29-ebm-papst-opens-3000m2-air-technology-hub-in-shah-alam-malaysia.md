  ## EBM-Papst opens Air Technology Hub in Shah Alam

  German fan and motor manufacturer EBM-Papst has officially opened an Air Technology Hub in Shah Alam, Malaysia, adding a facility designed to bring assembly, technical support, and customer services closer to buyers across Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

  The facility covers approximately 3,000 square metres, divided across a 2,000m² warehouse, a 500m² assembly and laboratory area, and a 500m² office area. The warehouse will hold selected products in local stock, with the aim of improving availability and shortening delivery times for regional customers.

  ## Facility scope and services

  Beyond warehousing, the hub supports local assembly of high-demand products across multiple application types, the company said. Additional capabilities include quality analysis, digital services, and customer training.

  The hub also extends services across the product lifecycle. These include local return material authorisation — a process by which faulty goods are assessed and cleared for return to a manufacturer — as well as repair and refurbishment of selected products, fan health monitoring, and retrofit proof-of-concept work. EBM-Papst said the training programmes are intended for customers, partners, and employees, with a stated goal of knowledge transfer and local talent development.

  A customer experience centre within the facility allows customers and partners to examine EBM-Papst technologies for different applications and discuss system performance improvements through intelligent controls, digital connectivity, and retrofit solutions.

  ## Why Malaysia

  EBM-Papst said Malaysia was selected for the hub because of its established industrial base, skilled workforce, and strategic location within Southeast Asia. The company did not provide further detail on those selection criteria.

  ## Regional network context

  The Shah Alam hub forms part of EBM-Papst's regional hub network in Asia-Pacific. The company's other hub in the network is the South China Air Technology Hub in Guangdong, China. The Malaysia opening extends that network into Southeast Asia.

  The addition of a hub with local assembly and stocking capacity is significant for data centre and industrial customers in the region, for whom fan and motor lead times and local technical support directly affect equipment uptime and procurement planning. EBM-Papst did not disclose investment figures, staffing numbers, or a timeline for further regional expansion.

  ## EBM-Papst於莎阿南設立空氣技術樞紐

  德國風扇與馬達製造商EBM-Papst日前正式在馬來西亞莎阿南啟用一座空氣技術樞紐（Air Technology Hub），此設施旨在將組裝、技術支援及客戶服務更貼近馬來西亞及東南亞各地的買家。

  該設施佔地約3,000平方公尺，分為2,000平方公尺的倉庫區、500平方公尺的組裝及實驗室區，以及500平方公尺的辦公室區。倉庫將在當地備有精選產品庫存，目標是提升區域客戶的產品取得便利性，同時縮短交貨時間。

  ## 設施範疇與服務項目

  EBM-Papst表示，除倉儲功能外，此樞紐亦支援多種應用類型之高需求產品的在地組裝，另提供品質分析、數位服務及客戶培訓等多項能力。

  此樞紐同時涵蓋完整的產品生命週期服務，包括在地退貨授權（RMA）——即評估瑕疵品並核准其退回製造商的流程——以及精選產品的維修與翻新、風扇健康狀態監測，以及改裝方案的概念驗證（Proof-of-Concept）等項目。EBM-Papst表示，培訓計畫的服務對象涵蓋客戶、合作夥伴及員工，明確目標為知識移轉與在地人才培育。

  設施內設有客戶體驗中心，讓客戶與合作夥伴得以實際了解EBM-Papst針對不同應用場景的技術，並就如何透過智慧控制、數位連結及改裝解決方案提升系統效能進行深入討論。

  ## 選址馬來西亞的考量

  EBM-Papst表示，選擇馬來西亞作為樞紐設點，主要考量當地成熟的工業基礎、具備專業技術的勞動力，以及其在東南亞的優越地理位置。該公司並未就上述選址標準提供進一步說明。

  ## 區域網路架構

  莎阿南樞紐是EBM-Papst亞太區域樞紐網路的一部分。該公司在此網路中的另一個樞紐，為位於中國廣東的華南空氣技術樞紐。馬來西亞新據點的啟用，將這一網路進一步延伸至東南亞地區。

  對於區域內的資料中心及工業客戶而言，具備在地組裝與備貨能力的樞紐意義重大——風扇與馬達的備料前置時間，以及在地技術支援的即時性，直接影響設備的運作穩定性與採購規劃。EBM-Papst並未披露投資金額、員工人數，亦未提及進一步區域擴張的時間表。
