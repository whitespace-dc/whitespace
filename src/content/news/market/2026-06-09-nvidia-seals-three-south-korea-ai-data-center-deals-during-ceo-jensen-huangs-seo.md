  ## Nvidia announces three South Korea AI partnerships

  Nvidia announced agreements with South Korean memory chipmaker SK Hynix, internet company Naver, and industrial conglomerate Doosan Group on Monday, with each partner committing to build or supply AI data center infrastructure using Nvidia technology. The deals were signed during a visit to Seoul by Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang, who arrived on June 5. None of the parties disclosed financial terms for any of the agreements.

  ## SK Hynix and SK Telecom

  SK Hynix and Nvidia signed a multi-year technology partnership targeting next-generation memory for AI data centers worldwide, the two companies said. SK Hynix, one of the world's two largest memory chip manufacturers alongside Samsung Electronics, said the agreement would allow it to enter new AI segments — including personal AI and physical AI, meaning systems that interact with and operate in the physical world — and would support stable supply across the long development cycles of advanced memory semiconductors.

  SK Telecom, a sister company to SK Hynix within the broader SK Group, said it would use Nvidia technology to build a gigawatt-scale AI cloud in South Korea. A gigawatt represents one billion watts of power capacity, a scale associated with the largest tier of hyperscale data center campuses. SK Telecom said the first AI data center under that program is expected to come online in 2027.

  ## Naver

  Nvidia said internet conglomerate Naver would use its technology to build AI factories — large-scale facilities dedicated to AI computing — at gigawatt scale, aimed at meeting rising global demand for AI services and physical AI applications.

  ## Doosan Group

  Doosan Group, which operates a robotics division and produces materials used in Nvidia's Blackwell chips — the company's most powerful processor line — also agreed to build AI data centers using Nvidia technology. Doosan said it expects its energy solutions to be applied to Nvidia data center platforms and that it would adopt Nvidia's physical AI technology.

  ## Industry significance

  South Korea is a major manufacturing hub in Asia, with significant production capacity in semiconductors, electronics, automotive, and shipbuilding, Reuters reported. SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics, the country's two dominant memory chipmakers, supply a component — memory chips — that is central to AI data center operations. The multi-year nature of the SK Hynix technology agreement reflects the extended lead times required to develop and qualify advanced memory products for next-generation AI systems.

  SK Telecom's first Nvidia-powered AI data center in South Korea is scheduled to come online in 2027.

  ## 輝達宣布韓國三項AI合作協議

  輝達週一宣布與韓國記憶體晶片廠SK海力士、網路公司Naver及工業集團斗山集團簽署合作協議，三方均承諾以輝達技術興建或供應AI資料中心基礎設施。這批協議是在輝達執行長黃仁勳親赴首爾訪問期間簽署，黃仁勳於6月5日抵達首爾。各方均未揭露任何協議的財務條款。

  ## SK海力士與SK電信

  SK海力士與輝達簽署多年期技術合作協議，目標是為全球各地的AI資料中心開發下一代記憶體，兩家公司表示。SK海力士與三星電子並列全球最大記憶體晶片製造商之一，該公司表示，這項協議將使其得以進軍新的AI應用領域，包括個人AI與實體AI——也就是能夠與現實世界互動並在其中運作的系統——並將支持先進記憶體半導體漫長開發週期所需的穩定供應。

  SK電信是SK海力士在SK集團旗下的姊妹公司，表示將採用輝達技術在韓國興建吉瓦級（gigawatt scale）AI雲端設施。一吉瓦相當於十億瓦的電力容量，這一規模通常與規模最大的超大型資料中心園區相當。SK電信表示，該計畫的首座AI資料中心預計於2027年正式上線。

  ## Naver

  輝達表示，網路集團Naver將採用其技術，以吉瓦級規模興建AI工廠——即專門用於AI運算的大型設施——以因應全球對AI服務及實體AI應用日益旺盛的需求。

  ## 斗山集團

  斗山集團旗下設有機器人事業部門，並生產輝達Blackwell晶片——該公司最強大的處理器產品線——所需的材料，斗山集團同樣同意採用輝達技術興建AI資料中心。斗山表示，預計其能源解決方案將應用於輝達的資料中心平台，並將採用輝達的實體AI技術。

  ## 產業意義

  據路透社報導，韓國是亞洲主要製造重鎮，在半導體、電子、汽車及造船領域均具備相當可觀的生產能量。SK海力士與三星電子是韓國兩大主力記憶體晶片廠，所供應的記憶體晶片正是AI資料中心運作的核心元件。SK海力士這份多年期技術合作協議，也反映了為下一代AI系統開發並驗證先進記憶體產品所需的漫長前置時程。

  SK電信首座採用輝達技術的韓國AI資料中心，預計將於2027年正式上線。
