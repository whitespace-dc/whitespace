body_en: |
  Gamuda Bhd has secured a RM1.71 billion (approximately USD 390 million) contract from an unnamed US multinational technology firm to build a hyperscale data center in Port Dickson, Negeri Sembilan, the Malaysian construction and infrastructure group disclosed in a filing with Bursa Malaysia, the country's stock exchange.

  The work will be carried out by Gamuda Engineering Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the group. The scope covers three distinct packages: site infrastructure works, building core and shell construction, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) fitout construction. MEP fitout refers to the installation of the power, cooling, and cabling systems that make a data center building operational.

  The project is a single-storey hyperscale facility — a large-scale data center designed to support the compute and storage demands of major cloud and technology platforms. Construction is scheduled to commence in the third quarter of 2026 and reach completion in the second quarter of 2028, giving the project a build timeline of approximately two years.

  ## Financial contribution

  Gamuda said it expects the project to contribute positively to its revenue and earnings from the financial year ending July 31, 2027, through to completion. The company's financial year runs to the end of July, meaning the first full year of revenue recognition from this contract will be fiscal year 2027, with further contribution expected into fiscal year 2028.

  ## Significance for Gamuda and the region

  At RM1.71 billion, the contract represents a substantial single award for Gamuda Engineering and signals continued inbound investment in Malaysian data center infrastructure from US technology companies. Port Dickson, a coastal town in Negeri Sembilan roughly 90 kilometers south of Kuala Lumpur, is the project's location. The identity of the US multinational client was not disclosed in the Bursa filing.

  The project is scheduled to begin site works in the third quarter of 2026.

  ## Gamuda獲17.1億令吉合約　將在波德申興建超大規模資料中心

  Malaysia建設暨基礎建設集團Gamuda Bhd宣布，已從一家未具名美國跨國科技公司手中取得一份總值17.1億令吉（約3.9億美元）的合約，將在森美蘭州波德申興建一座超大規模資料中心。Gamuda在向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）提交的申報文件中披露了這項消息。

  實際工程將由Gamuda旗下全資子公司Gamuda Engineering Sdn Bhd負責執行。工程範疇涵蓋三大獨立標段：基地基礎設施工程、建築核心與外殼工程，以及機械、電氣與管路（MEP）裝修工程。所謂MEP裝修，係指電力、冷卻及佈線系統的安裝作業，這些系統是讓資料中心建築正式投入運作的關鍵所在。

  本案為單層超大規模設施，此類大型資料中心專為支應大型雲端平台及科技業者的運算與儲存需求而設計。工程預計於2026年第三季正式動工，並於2028年第二季竣工，整體施工期約兩年。

  ## 財務貢獻

  Gamuda表示，預計本合約將從截至2027年7月31日的財政年度起，持續至完工為止，為集團的營收與獲利帶來正面貢獻。由於Gamuda財政年度截止於每年7月底，因此本合約的首個完整收入認列年度為2027財年，並預期在2028財年持續挹注業績。

  ## 對Gamuda及區域的意義

  17.1億令吉的合約金額對Gamuda Engineering而言是一筆相當可觀的單一得標金額，同時也顯示美國科技公司持續加大對馬來西亞資料中心基礎建設的投資力道。本案建設地點波德申為森美蘭州一座濱海小鎮，位於吉隆坡以南約90公里處。Bursa Malaysia的申報文件並未揭露這家美國跨國客戶的身份。

  本工程預計於2026年第三季展開基地作業。
