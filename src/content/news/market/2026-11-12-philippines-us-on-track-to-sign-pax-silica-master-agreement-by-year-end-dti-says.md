body_en: |
  The Philippines and United States remain on track to finalize the master agreement for Pax Silica by the end of 2026, the Philippine Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) said Tuesday, though both governments have ruled out a signing ceremony during President Donald Trump's visit to Manila for the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Summit this month.

  DTI Undersecretary Ceferino S. Rodolfo told reporters on the sidelines of the Fourth ASEAN Investment Forum that the decision reflects scheduling priorities, not stalled negotiations. "While there will be no announcement during the upcoming meeting between President Trump and President Marcos, this is simply a matter of timing," Rodolfo said.

  Rodolfo said both sides want Pax Silica — a US-led alliance linking supply chains for artificial intelligence (AI), semiconductors, and critical minerals — to have "its own dedicated moment" rather than compete for attention at an already crowded diplomatic calendar. "With ASEAN, APEC (Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation), and several major initiatives occurring in close succession, we felt it would be better for the advanced manufacturing hub to have its own moment rather than compete within an already crowded diplomatic agenda," he said.

  ## No summit announcement, no breakdown

  The Philippines is hosting the 49th ASEAN Summit and Related Summits from November 15 to 17. US Ambassador to the Philippines Lee Lipton confirmed on Saturday that there would be no Pax Silica announcement during or after the summit.

  Rodolfo said there are no substantial disagreements blocking the two countries from finalizing the deal. "I think we're still on track towards having an agreement by the end of this year," he said, adding that if the agreement is signed on schedule, the subsequent two-year development and masterplanning period also remains on track.

  The statement adjusts earlier expectations. Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA) President and Chief Executive Officer Joshua M. Bingcang had previously said both sides were targeting a signing during the ASEAN Summit.

  Rodolfo also said growing domestic opposition to the hub is not the reason the signing was removed from the summit agenda. "That has always been part of the equation, that there should be consultations, and there should be more communication efforts with the affected stakeholders," he said.

  ## The hub

  The flagship Philippine component of Pax Silica is a proposed 1,619-hectare AI-native industrial hub to be built in New Clark City, Tarlac. The BCDA will serve as the Philippine signatory to the master agreement, which its American counterpart will also sign. Groundbreaking is planned two years after the agreement is signed.

  The Philippines is positioning the hub as a path to move beyond low-value assembly, testing, and packaging of semiconductors to become an advanced manufacturing hub for tech hardware.

  ## Opposition

  The project faces scrutiny over its heavy energy and water requirements, the potential displacement of local communities, and environmental impacts. Rodolfo acknowledged the opposition but described consultations and stakeholder communication as an expected part of the process, not an obstacle to signing.

  With a year-end target for the master agreement now the stated goal, Rodolfo said the overall project timeline — including the two-year development period that follows — remains intact.

  菲律賓貿工部（DTI）週二表示，菲美兩國仍按計畫於2026年底前完成「帕克斯矽谷」（Pax Silica）總協議，儘管雙方均已確認，不會在美國總統唐納．川普本月赴馬尼拉出席東南亞國家協會（東協）峰會期間舉行簽署典禮。

  貿工部次長塞費里諾．羅多爾福（Ceferino S. Rodolfo）在第四屆東協投資論壇場邊接受記者採訪時表示，此一決定反映的是行程安排上的考量，而非談判陷入僵局。「雖然川普總統與馬科斯總統即將舉行的會面不會有任何宣布，但這純粹是時機的問題，」羅多爾福說。

  羅多爾福表示，雙方都希望帕克斯矽谷——這個由美國主導、串聯人工智慧（AI）、半導體與關鍵礦產供應鏈的聯盟——能夠擁有「專屬的亮相時刻」，而不是在已十分擁擠的外交行事曆中爭搶版面。「東協、亞太經合會（APEC）以及多項重大倡議接連登場，我們認為，讓這個先進製造業樞紐擁有自己的舞台，好過在已十分繁忙的外交議程中相互競奪目光，」他說。

  ## 峰會不宣布，談判未破裂

  菲律賓正主辦第49屆東協峰會及相關高峰會議，會期為11月15日至17日。美國駐菲大使李．利普頓（Lee Lipton）週六已證實，峰會期間及結束後均不會有帕克斯矽谷的相關宣布。

  羅多爾福表示，目前並無任何實質分歧阻礙兩國完成協議。「我認為我們仍在軌道上，年底前可以達成協議，」他說，並補充指出，若協議如期簽署，後續兩年的開發與總體規劃期亦可如期推進。

  這番表態調整了外界早先的預期。基地轉換暨開發署（BCDA）署長暨執行長約書亞．賓坎（Joshua M. Bingcang）此前曾表示，雙方的目標是在東協峰會期間完成簽署。

  羅多爾福也表示，國內日益升高的反對聲浪，並非簽署典禮從峰會議程中移除的原因。「這向來就是方程式的一部分——應該要有諮詢，應該要加強與受影響利害關係人的溝通，」他說。

  ## 樞紐計畫概述

  帕克斯矽谷的菲律賓旗艦計畫，是一座擬建於打拉省新克拉克城（New Clark City）、占地1,619公頃的AI原生工業樞紐。基地轉換暨開發署將代表菲律賓方面在總協議上簽字，美方代表亦將共同署名。動土典禮預計在協議簽署後兩年內舉行。

  菲律賓希望藉由這座樞紐，突破半導體低附加價值組裝、測試與封裝的發展瓶頸，轉型為科技硬體的先進製造中心。

  ## 反對聲浪

  這項計畫在能源與水資源的龐大需求、在地社區可能遭到遷置，以及環境衝擊等議題上，持續面臨外界審視。羅多爾福承認有反對意見存在，但將諮詢與利害關係人溝通定性為過程中可預期的環節，而非簽署的障礙。

  隨著年底完成總協議成為明確目標，羅多爾福表示，整體計畫時程——包括其後的兩年開發期——目前仍維持完整。
