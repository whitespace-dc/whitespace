body_en: |
  Ignis and Acciona have formalized a joint venture to develop a data center in the province of Segovia, in Spain's Castile and León region, the companies announced. The facility will be accompanied by an adjacent solar plant designed to meet its electricity needs from renewable sources.

  The transaction is structured through Ignis Data Epta, a company incorporated in 2024 that was previously wholly owned by Ignis P2X — the data center development platform jointly controlled by Ignis and the private equity firm KKR. Acciona acquired a 50 percent stake in Ignis Data Epta, with Ignis retaining the other half. The transaction also covered the joint acquisition of the co-located solar plant.

  The permits for grid access and connection rights required for the project had already been secured through two Ignis subsidiaries prior to the deal. The new joint venture formalizes the shared ownership structure for both assets without requiring those permits to be reprocessed.

  ## Acciona's push toward 2 gigawatts

  The Segovia deal is one piece of Acciona's stated goal of reaching 2 gigawatts (GW) of data center capacity. For context, 2 GW is sufficient to power facilities on par with several of the largest hyperscale campuses in Europe combined. Acciona, chaired by José Manuel Entrecanales, created a standalone unit called Acciona Data Centers to pursue that target. Until less than two years ago, the company handled data center work within its Construction division.

  ## A precedent from Fuenlabrada

  Acciona points to a recent agreement with developer Apto as the model for the Segovia arrangement. Under that deal, Acciona joined a data center campus Apto is building in Fuenlabrada, in the Madrid region, providing 82 megawatts (MW) of grid access and connection rights alongside a long-term power purchase agreement (PPA) — a contract locking in electricity supply at an agreed price — tied to a 94 MW solar plant developed and built by Ignis.

  The Segovia project follows the same structure: a data center paired with a dedicated renewable energy source, co-developed and co-owned by both partners.

  ## Significance

  The use of Ignis Data Epta as the transaction vehicle, rather than a newly created entity, means the project's regulatory groundwork carries over intact. Grid connection rights for large power consumers are scarce in Spain and can take years to obtain, making the preservation of previously secured permits a practical advantage for the timeline.

  Acciona's creation of a dedicated data center division and its articulated 2 GW capacity target represent the clearest forward-looking indicator of the company's intended scale in the sector, with the Segovia project and the Fuenlabrada campus among the concrete steps toward that goal.

  ## Ignis與Acciona攜手合資，在塞哥維亞打造太陽能供電資料中心

  Ignis與Acciona宣布正式成立合資公司，計畫在西班牙卡斯提亞－萊昂自治區的塞哥維亞省開發一座資料中心。該設施將配置一座緊鄰的太陽能光電廠，專門負責提供再生能源電力，滿足資料中心的用電需求。

  本次交易以Ignis Data Epta為載體進行。Ignis Data Epta於2024年設立，原為Ignis P2X全資子公司——而Ignis P2X正是Ignis與私募股權公司KKR共同掌控的資料中心開發平台。Acciona此番取得Ignis Data Epta 50%的股份，Ignis則保留另外50%。交易範圍亦涵蓋雙方共同收購毗鄰的太陽能光電廠。

  值得關注的是，本案所需的電網接入許可及連線權利，早在交易完成前便已由Ignis旗下兩家子公司取得。新合資架構的確立，讓雙方得以在不重新申請許可的前提下，正式共有兩項資產。

  ## Acciona衝刺2吉瓦容量目標

  塞哥維亞案是Acciona公開宣示達成2吉瓦（GW）資料中心容量目標的具體布局之一。就規模而言，2吉瓦的容量相當於歐洲數座最大型超大規模資料中心園區的總和。由荷西・曼努埃爾・安特雷卡納萊斯（José Manuel Entrecanales）擔任董事長的Acciona，專門成立了「Acciona Data Centers」獨立事業部來追求這一目標。就在不到兩年前，該公司的資料中心業務還隸屬於建設部門。

  ## 以弗恩拉布拉達案為前例

  Acciona以近期與開發商Apto達成的協議，作為塞哥維亞合作模式的先例。在那項合作中，Acciona加入Apto正在馬德里地區弗恩拉布拉達興建的資料中心園區，提供82百萬瓦（MW）的電網接入及連線權利，並簽署長期購電協議（PPA）——即以約定價格鎖定電力供應的合約——對應的是由Ignis開發興建、容量達94百萬瓦的太陽能光電廠。

  塞哥維亞案遵循相同架構：資料中心與專屬再生能源設施配對，由兩家合作夥伴共同開發、共同持有。

  ## 意義與展望

  以Ignis Data Epta作為交易載體，而非另立新公司，意味著本案先前取得的法規基礎得以完整延續。在西班牙，大型用電戶的電網連線權利極為稀缺，申請流程往往耗時數年，因此保全既有許可對於縮短開發時程具有實質優勢。

  Acciona成立專屬資料中心事業部，並明確訂定2吉瓦的容量目標，是迄今最清晰的前瞻指標，充分揭示其在這一產業的佈局企圖心。塞哥維亞案與弗恩拉布拉達園區，正是朝這一目標邁進的具體行動。
