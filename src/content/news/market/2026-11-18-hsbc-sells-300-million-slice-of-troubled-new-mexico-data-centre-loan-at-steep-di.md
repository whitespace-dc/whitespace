body_en: |
  HSBC Holdings sold roughly $300 million of its stake in a troubled construction loan tied to Oracle's data centre campus in New Mexico at prices in the mid-80 cents on the dollar — well below face value — shortly after Oracle issued a force majeure notice to the project's developer, according to people familiar with the matter.

  The sale took place several weeks ago, those people said, asking not to be identified discussing private information. A representative for HSBC declined to comment; a representative for Oracle did not have an immediate comment.

  ## The loan and the project

  The $300 million piece is a slice of an $18 billion financing package — referred to in market parlance as Project Miner — that a syndicate of about 20 banks assembled to fund construction of a data centre campus in Doña Ana County in southern New Mexico. The loan is structured in different tranches, each tied to individual buildings at the site.

  The campus, known as Project Jupiter, is designed to handle 2.45 gigawatts of power load and is a central component of the Stargate AI infrastructure build-out that US President Donald Trump unveiled in early 2025 alongside leaders of Oracle, OpenAI, and SoftBank Group.

  ## Why HSBC sold at a loss

  Banks holding construction project financing loans typically keep them on their balance sheets rather than trading them in the secondary market. When they do seek to exit before a project is complete, they generally must accept a discount larger than the upfront fees they received on the loan, resulting in a net loss.

  HSBC's decision to sell came shortly after Oracle sent a force majeure notice to the project's developer, a unit of Blue Owl Capital. Force majeure is a contractual mechanism that, if accepted, allows a party to defer or suspend payment obligations when a project is disrupted by events beyond its control. Bloomberg reported Oracle's notice in late September.

  Lenders in syndicated loan markets are generally free to sell their stakes in the secondary market, though in some circumstances they must wait for a pooled sell-down agreement — a clause that restricts sales until a set period expires — before doing so.

  ## Setbacks at Project Jupiter

  The New Mexico campus has been beset by regulatory hurdles and opposition. Key among its difficulties are delays in acquiring air and pipeline permits that the project requires to proceed, according to the sources.

  ## Broader concerns in AI infrastructure lending

  HSBC's move reflects mounting anxiety among lenders about their exposure to AI-related debt. That anxiety has been compounded by regulatory pushback following a Democratic sweep in the US midterm elections and by the abrupt departure of Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang, whom the sources describe as a linchpin of the AI industry's rapid expansion, which has raised fresh questions about the pace of AI growth.

  The willingness of at least one bank in the Project Miner syndicate to absorb a secondary-market loss signals that some lenders are prioritising balance-sheet reduction over waiting for the New Mexico project's construction and permitting uncertainties to be resolved.

  ## 貸款與計畫

  這3億美元部位，僅是業界稱為「礦工計畫」（Project Miner）的180億美元融資套案中的一個切片。該融資由約20家銀行組成的銀行團共同籌組，用以資助位於新墨西哥州南部多納安納縣（Doña Ana County）一座資料中心園區的建設工程。這筆貸款劃分為多個檔次，各檔次分別對應園區內的個別建築。

  ## 匯豐為何折價出售

  持有建設工程融資貸款的銀行，通常會將其保留在自身的資產負債表上，而非在次級市場上交易。當銀行在工程竣工前尋求退出時，一般必須接受大於當初貸款所收取承銷費用的折讓，因此將形成淨虧損。

  匯豐的出售決定，時間點緊接在甲骨文向計畫開發商——藍貓頭鷹資本（Blue Owl Capital）旗下一個子公司——發出不可抗力通知之後。不可抗力是一種合約機制，若獲對方接受，可允許一方在計畫因超出其控制範圍的事件而遭受干擾時，推遲或暫停付款義務。彭博社曾於9月下旬報導甲骨文發出上述通知一事。

  在聯貸市場中，貸款方通常可自由在次級市場出售其持有的部位，但在某些情況下，須等待「聯合減持協議」（pooled sell-down agreement）所設定的限售期屆滿後，方可進行交易。

  ## 木星計畫的挫折

  新墨西哥州的這座園區迄今已飽受監管障礙和各方反對的困擾。消息人士指出，其所面臨的主要困難之一，是取得計畫推進所需的空氣排放許可及管線許可方面的一再延誤。

  ## AI基礎建設融資的廣泛隱憂

  匯豐的舉動，反映出貸款機構對其AI相關債務曝險與日俱增的憂慮。多重因素進一步加劇了這股憂慮：美國期中選舉民主黨大勝後所引發的監管阻力，以及輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳的突然離職——消息人士形容他是AI產業快速擴張的關鍵人物——由此對AI的成長步伐引發了新一輪疑慮。

  「礦工計畫」銀行團中，至少有一家銀行願意承受次級市場的交易虧損，這一訊號意味著，部分貸款機構選擇優先縮減資產負債表規模，而不願繼續等待新墨西哥州工程的施工與取得許可等不確定性獲得解決。
