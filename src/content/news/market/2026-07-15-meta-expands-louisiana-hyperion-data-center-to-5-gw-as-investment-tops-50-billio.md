body_en: |
  Meta said Monday its Hyperion data center campus in Richland Parish, Louisiana, will expand to 5 gigawatts (GW) of compute capacity, with total planned investment rising to more than $50 billion, up from the 2 GW and $27 billion projected when the project was first outlined in October 2025.

  The Richland Parish facility, once expanded, will be the largest in Meta's global data center fleet. Meta broke ground on the project in December 2024. A data center's compute capacity, measured in gigawatts of electrical power it can draw, indicates the scale of computing hardware it can sustain. The campus houses Meta's artificial intelligence (AI) training cluster, also named Hyperion, which supports development of large language models, the software underlying tools such as ChatGPT.

  ## Power supply

  To deliver electricity at that scale, Meta said it is working with Entergy, a utility that operates across several U.S. states including Louisiana. Under a March 2026 agreement between the two companies, Meta will fund seven new gas-fired generating plants, three grid-scale battery storage installations, nuclear uprates at existing plants to increase their output, and other purchased power. Meta said it will also fund up to 2.5 GW of clean and renewable energy generation and will match the campus's total electricity consumption with clean and renewable energy investments. The company said it intends to pay for all energy, water, and related infrastructure it uses.

  ## Economic and community impact

  Since breaking ground in December 2024, Meta said local Louisiana businesses have received more than $1.6 billion in contracts from the project. The expansion carries a further commitment: Meta plans to invest $1 billion in local infrastructure improvements, including roads and water and wastewater systems.

  Increased tax revenues from the data center have reached Richland Parish schools. Teachers in the parish recently received annual bonuses of up to $50,000, a 400% increase from the prior year, the company said.

  Louisiana Governor Jeff Landry cited the state's regulatory climate as a draw for large-scale investment. "In just two years, we've secured more than $150 billion in new investment by creating an environment where companies can move quickly and build at scale," Landry said. "Projects like this don't just grow our state's economy, they ensure America continues to lead in the technologies that will define the next generation."

  Rachel Peterson, Meta's Vice President of Data Centers, said the project reflects combined infrastructure and community investment. "With more than $1.6 billion already contracted with local companies and thousands of jobs being supported, we're delivering real economic impact alongside the AI infrastructure that will power the future," Peterson said.

  ## Environmental opposition

  The expansion has drawn sustained pushback from environmental and consumer advocacy groups. Earthjustice, a U.S. environmental law organization, had sought to investigate the financing structure of the Louisiana project; that request was denied earlier this year. Earthjustice had argued the arrangement could shift project costs onto utility customers if Meta exits the project before Entergy recovers its investment. Critics have also raised concerns about data centers' effects on local power grids, electricity rates, water use, and noise, as well as the limited number of permanent jobs such facilities typically create.

  ## Concurrent expansion across North America

  The Richland Parish announcement extends a run of data center commitments by Meta across North America in 2026. In March, the company announced its El Paso, Texas campus, a 1 GW project, would be powered by a 366-megawatt (MW) array of modular gas generators. Meta is also investing more than $10 billion in a data center in Lebanon, Indiana, designed to handle both AI workloads and core Meta products, with 1 GW of planned compute capacity.

  Earlier in July, Meta announced CAD $13 billion (approximately $9.18 billion) in investment for a 1 GW AI data center in Sturgeon County, Alberta, Canada, the company's first facility in the country, with capacity expandable to 1.8 GW. Alberta's premier described the province as "the ideal location to build data centers in North America," citing affordable electricity prices, flexible power generation, a cooler climate, and a skilled workforce. The project is expected to create thousands of jobs and deliver approximately $175 million in annual economic benefits to Alberta residents, the company said.

  Last year, Meta formed a joint venture with Blue Owl Capital focused on data center construction and management.

  Reuters reported that Meta currently has 32 data centers globally in operation or under construction, 28 of them in the United States.

  ## Capital spending trajectory

  Meta, along with rivals including Microsoft, Alphabet, and Amazon, has been channeling billions of dollars into AI data centers as demand for computing capacity continues to outpace supply, Reuters reported. All have drawn on state-level tax incentives and energy agreements offered by governments seeking economic gains from AI development. U.S. President Donald Trump said last year that Meta's Louisiana data center project would cost $50 billion.

  Meta has pledged to invest $600 billion in U.S. infrastructure and jobs over the next three years. The company said in April that capital expenditure for 2026 could reach $145 billion, up from an upper estimate of $135 billion projected in January 2026 and nearly double the $72 billion spent in 2025.
body_zh: |
  Meta週一宣布，位於路易斯安那州里奇蘭教區（Richland Parish）的Hyperion資料中心園區將擴建至5吉瓦（GW）的運算容量，計畫總投資金額亦將提升至逾500億美元。相較於2025年10月專案首次公布時所訂定的2 GW容量與270億美元投資規模，此次宣布幾乎使兩項指標雙雙翻倍。Meta已於2024年12月正式動工。

  里奇蘭教區園區擴建完成後，將成為Meta全球資料中心版圖中規模最大的設施。資料中心的運算容量以電力耗電量（吉瓦）衡量，代表其能夠支撐的運算硬體規模。該園區設有Meta的人工智慧（AI）訓練叢集，同樣命名為「Hyperion」，用以支援大型語言模型的開發——這類軟體正是ChatGPT等工具的底層核心。

  ## 電力供應

  為在如此龐大的規模下穩定供應電力，Meta表示正與Entergy合作——Entergy是一家在路易斯安那州等多個美國州份營運的電力公用事業公司。根據兩家公司於2026年3月簽訂的協議，Meta將出資興建七座新型天然氣發電廠、三座電網級電池儲能設施，並對現有核電廠進行提升功率改造以增加其發電量，同時透過其他購電安排補充供電。Meta表示，亦將出資建設最多2.5 GW的清潔與再生能源發電設施，並承諾以清潔及再生能源投資抵消園區的全部用電量。該公司表示，所有能源、用水及相關基礎設施費用均由其自行承擔。

  ## 經濟與社區影響

  自2024年12月動工以來，Meta表示路易斯安那州當地企業已透過本專案取得逾16億美元的合約。此次擴建更進一步承諾：Meta計畫在當地基礎設施改善方面投入10億美元，涵蓋道路建設及供排水系統升級。

  資料中心帶來的稅收增加已直接惠及里奇蘭教區的學校。Meta表示，該教區教師近期獲得每年最高5萬美元的獎金，較前一年大幅增加了400%。

  路易斯安那州州長傑夫·蘭德里（Jeff Landry）引述該州的監管環境，將其視為吸引大規模投資的重要誘因。他表示：「短短兩年內，我們藉由打造一個讓企業得以快速行動、大規模建設的環境，已爭取到逾1,500億美元的新投資。這類專案不僅壯大了我們州的經濟，更確保美國持續在定義下一個世代的技術領域保持領先。」

  Meta資料中心副總裁瑞秋·彼得森（Rachel Peterson）表示，此專案體現了基礎設施投資與社區發展的結合。她說：「已有逾16億美元與當地企業簽訂合約，並支撐起數千個就業機會，我們正在AI基礎設施之外，切實帶來真正的經濟影響力，為未來提供動力。」

  ## 環保人士的反對聲浪

  此次擴建持續引發環保及消費者倡議團體的強烈反彈。美國環境法律組織「地球正義」（Earthjustice）曾尋求對路易斯安那州專案的融資結構展開調查，但該請求於今年稍早遭到駁回。地球正義曾主張，若Meta在Entergy回收投資之前退出專案，相關安排可能將部分費用轉嫁至電力用戶。批評者亦對資料中心對當地電網、電費、用水、噪音的影響，以及此類設施通常創造的永久職位數量偏少等問題表達高度關切。

  ## 北美資料中心同步擴張

  里奇蘭教區的擴建宣布，延續了Meta在2026年於北美接連承諾興建資料中心的浪潮。今年3月，Meta宣布德克薩斯州艾爾帕索（El Paso）園區為1 GW專案，將由一組366百萬瓦（MW）的模組化燃氣發電機組供電。Meta也正在印第安納州黎巴嫩市（Lebanon）斥資逾100億美元興建一座資料中心，設計上同時兼顧AI工作負載與Meta核心產品，計畫運算容量為1 GW。

  7月初，Meta宣布將在加拿大亞伯達省史特金縣（Sturgeon County）投入逾130億加幣（約91.8億美元），興建一座1 GW的AI資料中心，這也是Meta在加拿大的首座設施，容量最終可擴展至1.8 GW。亞伯達省省長形容該省是「在北美興建資料中心的理想地點」，理由包括電價實惠、電力供應彈性、氣候較為涼爽，以及擁有技術熟練的勞動力。Meta表示，該專案預計創造數千個就業機會，並每年為亞伯達省居民帶來約1.75億美元的經濟效益。

  去年，Meta與Blue Owl Capital成立了一家專注於資料中心建設與管理的合資企業。

  據路透社報導，Meta目前全球共有32座資料中心正在運營或建設中，其中28座位於美國境內。

  ## 資本支出走勢

  據路透社報導，Meta與微軟（Microsoft）、Alphabet、亞馬遜（Amazon）等競爭對手，持續將數十億美元投入AI資料中心，原因在於運算容量的需求不斷超越供給。這些企業均善用各州提供的稅收優惠與能源協議，而各地政府亦期望藉此從AI發展中獲取經濟紅利。美國總統唐納·川普（Donald Trump）去年曾表示，Meta路易斯安那州資料中心專案的造價將達500億美元。

  Meta承諾未來三年將在美國基礎設施與就業機會上投入6,000億美元。該公司於今年4月表示，2026年資本支出可能高達1,450億美元，高於2026年1月所預估的上限1,350億美元，幾乎是2025年實際支出720億美元的兩倍。
