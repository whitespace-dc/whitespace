body_en: |
  Amazon announced Thursday it will invest an additional $13 billion in India's AI and cloud infrastructure by 2030, bringing the company's total planned commitment in the country to $48 billion through the same period.

  The new funding is on top of a $35 billion investment Amazon announced last year, which covered e-commerce operations, export growth, and job creation alongside cloud and AI. Of the combined $48 billion, more than $21 billion is directed specifically at AI and cloud infrastructure between 2026 and 2030, the company said.

  ## Announcement context

  Amazon CEO Andy Jassy disclosed the expanded commitment during a meeting with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi. Jassy subsequently posted on the social platform X: "Shared that we're investing $48 billion over the coming five years, including $21+ billion in AI and cloud infrastructure."

  Modi welcomed the announcement publicly, writing on X: "I welcome Amazon's record $48 billion investment in India. This will create new opportunities for our youth. At the same time, it shows the growing interest across the world to invest in India!"

  ## What the investment covers

  The $13 billion allocation will expand AWS (Amazon Web Services) data center capacity in two existing regions: Mumbai and Hyderabad. According to the company, the build-out will give startups, enterprises, and government organizations access to custom AI chips, managed AI services, cloud technologies, and developer tools.

  In a statement, Jassy described Amazon's rationale: "The response has been tremendous, with strong growth especially across our e-commerce, AI, and cloud businesses. As we grow Amazon in India, our business priorities continue to align with India's priorities of democratizing access to AI, digitizing small businesses, creating jobs, and enabling exports."

  ## Competitive context

  The announcement places Amazon alongside other major U.S. technology companies that have made large-scale India commitments. Microsoft has pledged $17.5 billion for AI and cloud infrastructure in the country, while Google has committed $15 billion over five years to build AI data centers there, according to the sources. Amazon's $21 billion-plus AI and cloud figure exceeds both of those pledges individually.

  ## Significance

  The scale of the combined commitments from Amazon, Microsoft, and Google reflects India's emergence as a priority market for hyperscale cloud and AI infrastructure investment. For Amazon specifically, the additional $13 billion extends a spending trajectory that now totals $48 billion through 2030 — a figure that includes not just data center hardware but also the company's e-commerce, export, and employment commitments in the country.

  Amazon said the expanded AWS capacity in Mumbai and Hyderabad is intended to serve customers globally, positioning the two regions as export infrastructure as well as domestic compute capacity.

  ## 亞馬遜追加130億美元投資印度，雲端與AI承諾總額上看480億美元

  亞馬遜週四宣布，將在2030年前額外投入130億美元，用於擴建印度的AI與雲端基礎設施，使該公司在同一期間於印度的總計畫投資額達到480億美元。

  此次新增資金是在亞馬遜去年宣布的350億美元投資計畫之外追加的。去年的計畫涵蓋電子商務營運、出口成長及就業創造，同時也包含雲端與AI項目。亞馬遜表示，在合計的480億美元中，有逾210億美元將於2026年至2030年間專門投入AI與雲端基礎設施。

  ## 宣布背景

  亞馬遜執行長安迪·賈西（Andy Jassy）在與印度總理納倫德拉·莫迪（Narendra Modi）於新德里舉行會談期間，正式披露了這項擴大投資承諾。賈西隨後在社群平台X上發文表示：「我們分享了未來五年將投資480億美元的計畫，其中包括逾210億美元用於AI與雲端基礎設施。」

  莫迪也公開表示歡迎，在X上寫道：「我對亞馬遜創紀錄的480億美元投資印度表示歡迎。這將為我們的年輕人創造新的機遇，同時也展現了全球各地對投資印度日益高漲的興趣！」

  ## 投資涵蓋範疇

  此次130億美元的資金將用於擴充AWS（亞馬遜雲端運算服務）在兩個現有區域的資料中心容量，分別是孟買與海德拉巴。亞馬遜表示，此次建設將讓新創企業、大型企業及政府機構得以使用自研AI晶片、托管AI服務、雲端技術及開發者工具。

  賈西在聲明中闡述了亞馬遜的投資邏輯：「市場反應十分熱烈，尤其在電子商務、AI與雲端業務方面均呈現強勁成長。隨著亞馬遜在印度持續壯大，我們的業務重心也持續與印度的優先目標相契合，包括普及AI應用、推動小型企業數位化、創造就業機會，以及促進出口。」

  ## 競爭格局

  此次宣布使亞馬遜躋身於一眾承諾在印度大規模投資的美國科技巨頭之列。據相關資料顯示，微軟已承諾在印度投入175億美元用於AI與雲端基礎設施，Google則承諾在五年內投入150億美元，於當地建設AI資料中心。亞馬遜逾210億美元的AI與雲端投資金額，分別超越上述兩家公司的個別承諾額度。

  ## 重要意義

  亞馬遜、微軟與Google合計龐大投資承諾的規模，反映出印度正日益成為超大規模雲端與AI基礎設施投資的重點市場。就亞馬遜而言，此次追加的130億美元，使其在印度的支出軌跡延伸至2030年共達480億美元，涵蓋範疇不僅限於資料中心硬體，亦包括該公司在印度的電子商務、出口及就業相關承諾。

  亞馬遜表示，孟買與海德拉巴擴建後的AWS容量旨在服務全球客戶，將兩地定位為出口基礎設施，同時也作為國內運算容量加以佈局。
