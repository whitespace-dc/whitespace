body_en: |
  Rosado Bhd, a Kuala Lumpur-based contractor specialising in mechanical and electrical (M&E) engineering works for data centre projects, has filed a draft prospectus for an initial public offering (IPO) on Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market, according to the filing.

  The proposed listing will offer up to a combined 994.5 million shares, comprising a public issue of 689 million new shares and an offer for sale of 305.5 million existing shares, representing a total stake of 25.5% in the enlarged company. The final offer price has not yet been set.

  ## Use of proceeds

  The primary use of IPO proceeds is the posting of performance bonds — bank guarantees that contractors must provide to project owners as security for contract execution. As of end-April 2025, Rosado had RM40.7 million in performance bonds outstanding against an order book of RM817.1 million, according to the prospectus. The company said it may require up to RM163.4 million in additional bonds to pursue a pipeline of projects worth RM1.63 billion that are currently under tender.

  Beyond bonding requirements, the company plans to use proceeds to purchase project equipment, including power transformers, earthing transformers, telecontrol systems, and control and protection relay panels. The remaining funds will cover subcontractor payments, project materials, and listing expenses.

  ## Business and financials

  Rosado's core business is electrical M&E engineering for data centre projects. The company also carries out related civil and structural works — earthworks, trenching, ducting, and foundation works — and provides maintenance services for power substations and distribution infrastructure.

  For the financial year ended May 31, 2025, Rosado recorded revenue of RM345.9 million and profit after tax of RM100.2 million, according to the draft prospectus.

  ## Share structure and controlling shareholder

  The 689 million new shares in the public issue are allocated as follows: 195 million shares for the Malaysian public, 38 million shares for eligible persons such as directors and employees, 274 million shares for selected investors via private placement, and 182 million shares reserved for Bumiputera investors under Malaysia's equity participation requirements.

  All proceeds from the 305.5 million offer-for-sale shares will go to TSLIM Holdings Sdn Bhd, the private investment vehicle of managing director and chief executive officer Datuk Lim Tiew Sin and his family. TSLIM Holdings currently holds a 97% stake in Rosado; following the IPO, that stake will fall to 72.03%.

  Malacca Securities Sdn Bhd is acting as principal adviser, sponsor, underwriter, and placement agent for the transaction.

  ## Significance for Malaysia's data centre supply chain

  The listing would bring a specialist data centre M&E contractor to public markets in Malaysia at a time when hyperscale and colocation investment in the country is expanding. Rosado's order book and tender pipeline figures indicate the scale of electrical infrastructure work being contracted to support that buildout. The IPO, if completed, would give Rosado access to the capital needed to post bonds on larger contracts, which the company identified as a direct constraint on its ability to bid for and execute new work.

  The company's next step is regulatory review of the draft prospectus before a final prospectus and offer price are published.

  ## Rosado申請ACE市場IPO，籌資馬來西亞資料中心機電工程合約

  專注資料中心專案機電（M&E）工程的吉隆坡承包商Rosado Bhd，已向Bursa Malaysia ACE市場提交首次公開募股（IPO）草擬招股書。

  本次擬上市合計發行最多9億9,450萬股，其中包括6億8,900萬股公開新股發行，以及3億550萬股現有股份的出售要約，合計佔擴大後公司股本的25.5%。最終發行價格尚未確定。

  ## 募資用途

  IPO所得款項的主要用途，是繳交履約保證金——即承包商須向業主提供的銀行保函，作為合約執行的擔保。根據招股書，截至2025年4月底，Rosado的未結清履約保證金金額為4,070萬令吉，在手訂單簿規模則達8億1,710萬令吉。公司表示，為競投目前處於議標階段、合計價值達16億3,000萬令吉的潛在專案，可能還需追加最多1億6,340萬令吉的履約保證金。

  除保證金需求外，公司亦計劃以募資款項採購專案設備，包括電力變壓器、接地變壓器、遙控系統，以及控制與保護繼電器盤。其餘資金將用於支付分包商費用、專案材料及上市費用。

  ## 業務概況與財務表現

  Rosado的核心業務為資料中心專案的電氣機電工程。公司亦承接相關土木與結構工程，包括土方工程、溝槽開挖、管道鋪設及基礎工程，並提供電力變電站與配電基礎設施的維護服務。

  截至2025年5月31日的財政年度，Rosado錄得營收3億4,590萬令吉、稅後盈利1億220萬令吉。

  ## 股份結構與控股股東

  此次公開新股發行的6億8,900萬股分配如下：1億9,500萬股供馬來西亞公眾認購；3,800萬股供董事及員工等符合資格人士認購；2億7,400萬股透過私募方式配售予特定投資人；另有1億8,200萬股依馬來西亞股權參與規定，保留予土著投資人。

  3億550萬股出售要約所得款項將全數歸予TSLIM Holdings Sdn Bhd，即董事總經理兼執行長拿督林朝信（Datuk Lim Tiew Sin）及其家族的私人投資工具。TSLIM Holdings目前持有Rosado 97%股權；IPO完成後，持股比例將降至72.03%。

  馬六甲證券（Malacca Securities Sdn Bhd）擔任本次交易的首席顧問、保薦人、承銷商及配售代理。

  ## 對馬來西亞資料中心供應鏈的意義

  在超大規模雲端業者與共置機房業者持續加碼馬來西亞投資之際，此次上市將為資料中心機電工程專業承包商開闢一條進入公開市場的管道。Rosado的在手訂單簿與議標管線數字，充分反映出支撐這波建設浪潮所需電氣基礎設施工程的龐大規模。一旦IPO順利完成，Rosado將取得所需資本，得以就規模更大的合約繳交保證金——公司已明確指出，這正是目前制約其投標與承接新業務能力的關鍵瓶頸。

  公司的下一步，是等待主管機關審查草擬招股書，待審查完成後再公布正式招股書及最終發行價格。
