body_en: |
  South Korea's three largest telecommunications operators — SK Telecom, KT, and LG Uplus — spent a combined 3.68 trillion Korean won (more than $2.75 billion) on capital expenditures in the first nine months of 2026, up 13.7 percent from the same period a year earlier, with AI data center construction accounting for most of the increase, according to regulatory filings.

  The figures show all three carriers pressing ahead with data center investment even as broader questions about the returns on AI infrastructure have grown more prominent in the industry.

  ## Company-by-company spending

  LG Uplus posted the sharpest acceleration, with third-quarter capex rising roughly 30 percent year-on-year to 569.8 billion won, driven primarily by spending on its AI data center project in Paju, Gyeonggi Province. The facility is planned as a four-building campus rated at a total power capacity of 200 megawatts (MW) — the measure of how much electrical power the site can continuously draw and deliver to computing equipment — with buildings scheduled to open in stages before 2028. The first building, rated at 50 MW, is due for completion in June 2027. LG Uplus said the full project has confirmed customer demand.

  KT, including its major subsidiaries, recorded first-nine-month capex of 1.599 trillion won, up about 20 percent year-on-year. The company plans to invest a total of 6 trillion won over the next five years in AI-related infrastructure and to expand its data center power capacity to more than 1 gigawatt (GW) — equivalent to 1,000 MW, or roughly five times the total capacity of the LG Uplus Paju campus — by 2031.

  SK Telecom's capex, including its newly formed AI data center subsidiary SK Hyper, rose 3 percent year-on-year to 1.234 trillion won in the same period. Excluding SK Hyper, the company's capex actually declined, reflecting a deliberate strategy of bringing in outside investors to share the financial burden of large-scale data center development.

  ## Expansion beyond mobile

  The three operators are building AI data center capacity to open revenue streams beyond their core mobile communications businesses, the report noted. All three increased spending despite what the report described as growing market-level skepticism about AI investment, suggesting company executives retain confidence in the business outlook.

  ## Profitability questions

  Analysts cited in the report cautioned that the revenue structures of telecom operations and AI data centers differ significantly. Telecoms generate income from a broad subscriber base, while data center returns depend on a facility's ability to attract customers, its utilization rate, and the specific terms of contracts signed — variables that can produce widely divergent outcomes across operators.

  Data centers require substantial upfront capital outlays for land, buildings, power infrastructure, and cooling systems. Once a facility is operational, ongoing costs — depreciation, electricity, and maintenance — persist. GPU depreciation was singled out as a particular concern.

  Securing sufficient customer commitments before a facility opens and sustaining high utilization afterward are the two factors the report identified as most critical to recovering capital investment. Long-term contracts improve revenue predictability, but ultimate profitability still depends on electricity prices, operating costs, and the timing of equipment deployment, the report said.

  KT plans to surpass 1 GW of data center power capacity by 2031; LG Uplus expects its first Paju building to reach completion by June 2027.
body_zh: |
  南韓SK電信、KT及LG Uplus三大電信業者，根據監管申報文件顯示，2026年前三季合計資本支出達3.68兆韓元（逾27.5億美元），較前一年同期成長13.7%，增幅主要來自AI資料中心建設，三家業者的相關投資步伐均持續加快。

  儘管業界對AI基礎設施投資報酬率的疑慮日益升溫，三家電信業者仍持續推進資料中心投資，未有退縮跡象。

  ## 各業者支出概況

  LG Uplus增速最為突出，第三季資本支出年增約30%至5,698億韓元，主要來自旗下位於京畿道坡州市的AI資料中心建設支出。該設施規劃為四棟建築組成的園區，總電力容量達200百萬瓦（MW）——即該場址可持續汲取並供應給運算設備的電力上限——預計於2028年前分階段啟用。首棟建築電力容量為50 MW，預計於2027年6月竣工。LG Uplus表示，整體專案已獲得客戶需求確認。

  KT（含主要子公司）前三季資本支出為1兆5,990億韓元，年增約20%。該公司計畫在未來五年合計投入6兆韓元於AI相關基礎設施，並於2031年前將資料中心電力容量擴增至超過1吉瓦（GW）——相當於1,000 MW，約為LG Uplus坡州園區總容量的五倍。

  SK電信（含新成立的AI資料中心子公司SK Hyper）同期資本支出年增3%至1兆2,340億韓元。若剔除SK Hyper的貢獻，SK電信資本支出實際上呈現下滑，反映出該公司刻意引入外部投資人、共同分擔大規模資料中心開發財務負擔的策略方向。

  ## 拓展行動通訊以外的業務版圖

  報告指出，三家業者積極建置AI資料中心容量，目的在於開拓核心行動通訊業務以外的新營收來源。在市場對AI投資疑慮持續升溫的情況下，三家業者的支出仍同步增加，顯示各公司高層對業務前景依然抱持信心。

  ## 獲利能力的疑慮

  報告引述分析師示警，電信營運與AI資料中心的營收結構存在顯著差異。電信業者的收入來自龐大的用戶基礎，而資料中心的報酬則取決於設施的招客能力、使用率，以及所簽訂合約的具體條款——這些變數可能導致各業者之間出現懸殊的經營結果。

  資料中心需要大量前期資本投入，涵蓋土地、建築、電力基礎設施及冷卻系統。設施一旦投入運營，折舊、電費及維護等持續性成本便隨之而來，其中GPU折舊被特別點名為一項值得高度關注的課題。

  報告指出，在設施開幕前取得充足的客戶承諾，以及開幕後維持高使用率，是回收資本投資最關鍵的兩項因素。長期合約有助於提升營收可預測性，但最終獲利能力仍取決於電價、營運成本及設備部署時程。

  KT計畫於2031年前使資料中心電力容量突破1 GW；LG Uplus則預計首棟坡州建築將於2027年6月完工。
