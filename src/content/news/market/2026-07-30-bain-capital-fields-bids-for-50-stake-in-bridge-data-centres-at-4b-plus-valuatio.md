body_en: |
  Bain Capital is fielding bids from a field of global financial and strategic investors for roughly half of Bridge Data Centres, a Southeast Asian data centre operator, with a potential deal valuing the company at more than US$4 billion, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified because the deliberations are private.

  The prospective buyers include investment firm Sixth Street Partners, South Korea's SK Telecom Co, Singapore state investor GIC Pte Ltd, and La Caisse — the Canadian institutional investor formerly known as CDPQ — the people said. Bain is weighing a sale of approximately 50% of the company while retaining the remaining stake, they added.

  The size of any stake sold, and the valuation, may still change, and no final decisions have been made, the people said. Representatives for Sixth Street, La Caisse, GIC, and Bain declined to comment; SK Telecom and Bridge Data Centres did not immediately respond to requests for comment.

  ## Deal background

  Bain Capital, the Boston-based private equity firm, acquired Bridge Data Centres in 2017. People familiar with the matter said in December that Bain was considering options including bringing in fresh backers for the business. Bain subsequently engaged Citigroup Inc and JPMorgan Chase & Co to advise on the transaction, separate people said at the time.

  Bridge Data Centres is headquartered in Singapore and operates facilities in India, Malaysia, and Thailand.

  ## Investor appetite for digital infrastructure

  The process reflects a broader wave of investor demand for data centre assets driven by the computing power required to support the expansion of artificial intelligence. Global investors have been drawn to digital infrastructure for this reason, the sources noted.

  Bain itself completed a separate data centre transaction in January, selling WinTrix DC Group's China data centre business in a deal valued at approximately US$4 billion.

  ## What the deal would mean

  A transaction at the indicated valuation would rank among the larger data centre asset deals in Southeast Asia, a region where demand for digital infrastructure has accelerated alongside AI workload growth. The involvement of both financial buyers — Sixth Street and La Caisse — and a strategic telecommunications operator in SK Telecom illustrates the range of capital types competing for such assets.

  The next material development will be whether the parties reach agreement on stake size and price, with Bain's advisers at Citigroup and JPMorgan continuing to manage the process.

  貝恩資本（Bain Capital）正向多家全球金融與策略投資人徵詢收購報價，標的為東南亞資料中心業者Bridge Data Centres約半數股權，知情人士透露，潛在交易估值逾40億美元。由於相關討論屬私密性質，消息人士要求匿名。

  潛在買家包括投資公司Sixth Street Partners、南韓電信商SK電信（SK Telecom）、新加坡政府投資公司GIC，以及加拿大機構投資人La Caisse（前稱CDPQ）。知情人士表示，貝恩考慮出售約50%的股份，同時保留其餘持股。

  知情人士指出，最終出售股權比例與估值仍可能調整，且尚未做出任何最終決定。Sixth Street、La Caisse、GIC及貝恩的發言人皆拒絕置評；SK電信與Bridge Data Centres則未即時回應媒體詢問。

  ## 交易背景

  貝恩資本為總部位於波士頓的私募股權公司，於2017年收購Bridge Data Centres。知情人士曾於去年12月透露，貝恩正評估各種選項，包括引進新投資人。隨後，貝恩委託花旗集團（Citigroup）及摩根大通（JPMorgan Chase）擔任交易顧問，另有知情人士當時表示。

  Bridge Data Centres總部設於新加坡，並在印度、馬來西亞及泰國設有資料中心設施。

  ## 投資人對數位基礎建設的高度興趣

  此次招標程序反映出全球投資人對資料中心資產的廣泛需求浪潮，這股需求源自人工智慧（AI）持續擴張所需的龐大運算能力。知情人士指出，正是這一趨勢吸引全球投資人競相布局數位基礎建設。

  貝恩本身亦於今年1月完成了另一筆資料中心交易，將WinTrix DC Group旗下中國資料中心業務出售，交易金額約40億美元。

  ## 交易意義

  若以上述估值成交，此案將躋身東南亞規模最大的資料中心資產交易之列。東南亞地區的數位基礎建設需求，已隨AI工作負載的成長而快速攀升。財務型買家Sixth Street與La Caisse，以及電信策略投資人SK電信同台競逐此案，充分展現各類型資本爭相搶進此類資產的態勢。

  下一個關鍵進展，在於各方能否就股權比例與成交價格達成共識；目前貝恩委聘的花旗集團及摩根大通顧問團隊，仍持續主導整個交易流程。
