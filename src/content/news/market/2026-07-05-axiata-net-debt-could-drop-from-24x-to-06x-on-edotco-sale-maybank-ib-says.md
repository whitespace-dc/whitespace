body_en: |
  Axiata Group Bhd could cut its net debt load by as much as three-quarters if a sale of tower subsidiary Edotco Group proceeds at the upper end of analyst estimates, Maybank Investment Bank said Thursday.

  Maybank IB modeled the divestment at 6.5 to 9.0 times enterprise value to earnings before interest, tax, and amortisation (EV/Ebitda) — a standard valuation multiple for infrastructure assets — implying proceeds of RM3.1 billion to RM5.6 billion flowing to Axiata. The Kuala Lumpur-listed telecommunications group holds a 63% stake in Edotco.

  On those figures, Axiata's net debt-to-Ebitda ratio — a measure of how many years of operating earnings it would take to retire borrowings — would fall from 2.4 times before the transaction to between 0.6 and 1.2 times, compared with the 2.4 times recorded at the end of the first quarter of 2025, Maybank IB said.

  ## Edotco's scale and ownership

  Edotco operates and manages more than 47,000 telecommunications towers across eight countries: Malaysia, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, Pakistan, the Philippines, and Laos. Beyond Axiata's 63% holding, sovereign wealth fund Khazanah Nasional Bhd owns 32% of the company, with the remainder held by the Retirement Fund Inc, known as KWAP.

  ## Divestment timeline and scope

  Axiata management has stated an intention to divest both Edotco and infrastructure unit Linknet, and is working to formalize potential transactions in 2026, Maybank IB said. The bank described Edotco as more significant to Axiata than Linknet given its relative size and profitability. Those potential transactions are anticipated to be formalized this year, Maybank IB added.

  ## Analyst stance

  Maybank IB maintained a buy rating on Axiata with an unchanged target price of RM2.90. Assuming an 11 sen dividend per share for financial year 2026, the bank said Axiata offers a dividend yield of 5%. It described Axiata's overall risk-reward as positive, citing net profit recovery and balance sheet repair as potential re-rating catalysts.

  A successful divestment at the stated valuations would mark a substantial improvement in Axiata's financial position and could reduce the leverage concerns that have weighed on the stock. Management has indicated it expects to finalize the structure of the Edotco transaction during 2026.

  馬來亞銀行投資銀行（Maybank IB）週四表示，若亞洲電信集團（Axiata Group Bhd）出售旗下電信塔子公司Edotco Group的交易以分析師估值上限成交，集團淨負債規模最多可削減四分之三。

  Maybank IB以企業價值對息稅折舊攤銷前利潤（EV/Ebitda）6.5至9.0倍為基礎進行估值——此為基礎設施資產的標準評估倍數——估算Axiata可望獲得31億至56億馬來西亞令吉的出售收益。這家掛牌於吉隆坡的電信集團持有Edotco 63%的股權。

  依據上述數字，Axiata的淨負債對EBITDA比率——衡量企業需耗費多少年的營業利潤方可清償借款的指標——將從交易前的2.4倍，下降至0.6至1.2倍之間；相較之下，2025年第一季末所記錄的比率為2.4倍，Maybank IB指出。

  ## Edotco的規模與股權結構

  Edotco在馬來西亞、印尼、孟加拉、柬埔寨、斯里蘭卡、巴基斯坦、菲律賓及寮國等八個國家，運營及管理逾47,000座電信塔。除Axiata持股63%外，主權財富基金哈薩納國家有限公司（Khazanah Nasional Bhd）持有32%股權，其餘股份則由退休基金局（Retirement Fund Inc，簡稱KWAP）持有。

  ## 出售時程與範圍

  Maybank IB表示，Axiata管理層已表明出售Edotco及基礎設施部門Linknet的意向，並正積極推進潛在交易的正式程序，預計於2026年完成。該行指出，考量Edotco相對於Linknet的規模與獲利能力，Edotco對Axiata的重要性更為顯著。Maybank IB補充，上述潛在交易預計將於今年內正式確立。

  ## 分析師觀點

  Maybank IB維持對Axiata的「買進」評等，目標股價維持不變，為2.90令吉。以2026財年每股股息11仙計算，該行表示Axiata提供5%的股息殖利率，並形容Axiata整體風險報酬比為正面，引述淨利潤回升及資產負債表修復為潛在的股票重新評級催化因素。

  若依上述估值成功完成出售，將標誌著Axiata財務狀況大幅改善，並可望紓解長期壓抑股價的槓桿疑慮。管理層已表示，預計將於2026年內敲定Edotco交易的架構安排。
