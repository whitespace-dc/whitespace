body_en: |
  PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia (InfraNexia), the wholesale digital-infrastructure subsidiary of state-owned PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk, reported revenue of Rp 10.5 trillion (Indonesian rupiah) and net profit of Rp 1.04 trillion for the nine months through September 2026, the company said in a press release dated November 17. EBITDA, a measure of operating profitability that excludes interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization, reached Rp 5.3 trillion, for a margin of 50.6%.

  The results arrive as InfraNexia completed a second tranche of asset transfers from Telkom: wholesale fiber-connectivity business and assets valued at Rp 49.86 trillion moved to the subsidiary effective October 1, 2026, enlarging the company's operational scale going into the fourth quarter.

  ## External revenue

  Revenue from customers outside TelkomGroup, the broader Telkom corporate family, rose 22.1% year-on-year to Rp 1.42 trillion. That segment remains a minority of total income, with intra-group TelkomGroup revenue constituting the dominant share. InfraNexia said its external customer base spans telecommunications operators, internet service providers (ISPs), digital service providers, and corporate clients.

  ## Wholesale network segment

  The wholesale network segment, which includes three product lines called Metronexia, Wavenexia, and IP Nexia, grew revenue 20.3% year-on-year against the same nine-month period in 2025, when those portfolios were managed directly by Telkom. Wavenexia posted the fastest revenue growth at 29.7%, followed by Metronexia at 17.8% and IP Nexia at 14.1%.

  Capacity additions outpaced revenue growth in several lines. Bandwidth in service, the volume of activated network capacity under customer contracts, rose 89.0% year-on-year for Wavenexia, 44.7% for Metronexia, and 14.1% for IP Nexia.

  ## FTTx and infrastructure sharing

  The FTTx (fiber to the x, referring to fiber-optic broadband connections delivered to homes, buildings, or other premises) and infrastructure-sharing segment also expanded. Active bitstream connections, a wholesale broadband access product sold by network owners to downstream service providers, grew 301% year-on-year. VULA (Virtual Unbundled Local Access), a related wholesale access product that gives downstream operators more control over how they deliver service to end users, grew 166% year-on-year.

  As of end-September 2026, 355,000 utility poles had been made available to industry partners under infrastructure-sharing arrangements, in which operators share physical network assets rather than build duplicate structures.

  ## Profitability and capital spending

  The 50.6% EBITDA margin was supported by consistent cost controls and more integrated asset management, the company said. Capital expenditure as a proportion of revenue, the capex-to-revenue ratio, stood at 8.3% for the nine-month period.

  Finance Director Sandhra Utsman said in the press release that the results demonstrated growth and financial discipline advancing in step. With EBITDA margin maintained above 50% and strong operating cash flow, InfraNexia has the financial foundation to support its next growth stage, she said.

  ## Asset transfer program

  The consolidation of Telkom's wholesale infrastructure assets into InfraNexia has proceeded in two tranches. Phase 1, covering wholesale infrastructure assets, took effect January 1, 2026. Phase 2, covering wholesale fiber-connectivity assets valued at Rp 49.86 trillion, took effect October 1, 2026.

  Telkom Director Utama (president director) Nanang Hendarno said the transfer program was designed to optimize asset utilization, sharpen management focus, and open broader business growth opportunities. "With an increasingly strong focus, InfraNexia has room to develop its capabilities and expand asset utilization for more industry players," Hendarno said.

  ## Workforce

  InfraNexia's headcount reached 1,396 employees at the end of September 2026, up from 1,128 at the close of 2025, as the company grew its organizational capacity alongside its expanded mandate.

  ## Industry implications

  The sharp rise in wholesale access metrics, with bitstream connections tripling and VULA connections more than doubling year-on-year, points to growing uptake of InfraNexia's products by third-party network operators. The 355,000 poles under infrastructure-sharing arrangements indicate that physical asset co-use is gaining adoption among carriers.

  The Phase 2 transfer, at Rp 49.86 trillion, represents a substantial addition to InfraNexia's asset base and increases its capacity to serve operators beyond the TelkomGroup family, in line with TelkomGroup's stated goal of building a more competitive business structure.

  InfraNexia Director Utama Lukman Hakim Abd Rauf said the completion of Phase 2 brought the company into a new chapter with significantly greater scale. InfraNexia said it will optimize assets from both transfer phases, improve service quality through network modernization, and expand collaboration with industry players. The company noted it is operating in partnership with Danantara Indonesia with a commitment to reliable, efficient, and sustainable digital infrastructure.
body_zh: |
  PT Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia（InfraNexia）是印尼國營電信業者PT Telkom Indonesia（Persero）Tbk旗下的批發數位基礎建設子公司。該公司於11月17日發布的新聞稿中表示，2026年前九個月（截至9月底）營收達10.5兆印尼盾，淨利為1.04兆印尼盾。扣除利息、稅項、折舊及攤銷前盈餘（EBITDA）達5.3兆印尼盾，EBITDA利潤率為50.6%。

  此次財報發布之際，InfraNexia同步完成了母公司Telkom的第二批資產移轉：批發光纖連線業務及相關資產，合計估值49.86兆印尼盾，自2026年10月1日起正式移轉至該子公司，大幅擴增公司進入第四季後的營運規模。

  ## 外部客戶營收

  來自TelkomGroup集團體系以外客戶的營收年增22.1%，達1.42兆印尼盾。外部客戶收入在總收入中仍屬少數，集團內部TelkomGroup相關收入仍占大宗。InfraNexia表示，外部客戶涵蓋電信業者、網路服務提供商（ISP）、數位服務業者及企業客戶。

  ## 批發網路業務

  批發網路業務部門涵蓋Metronexia、Wavenexia及IP Nexia三條產品線。對比2025年同期（彼時這些業務由Telkom直接管理），該部門前九個月營收年增20.3%。其中Wavenexia營收成長最快，年增29.7%；其次為Metronexia，年增17.8%；IP Nexia則年增14.1%。

  各產品線的容量建置速度超過了營收成長幅度。就在用頻寬（即客戶合約中已啟用的網路容量）而言，Wavenexia年增89.0%，Metronexia年增44.7%，IP Nexia年增14.1%。

  ## FTTx與基礎建設共享

  FTTx（光纖到戶／到樓／到設施，泛指將光纖寬頻延伸至住宅、建築物或其他場所的連線服務）及基礎建設共享業務同樣持續擴大。主動位元流（bitstream）連線數為網路業者向下游服務提供商銷售的批發寬頻接取產品，年增301%。VULA（虛擬無捆綁本地迴路接取）為另一項相關批發接取產品，讓下游業者對終端用戶的服務交付方式擁有更大掌控空間，年增166%。

  截至2026年9月底，已有35.5萬根電線桿在基礎建設共享安排下開放供業界夥伴使用，讓電信業者能夠共用實體網路資產，無需重複建設。

  ## 獲利能力與資本支出

  公司表示，50.6%的EBITDA利潤率得益於持續的成本控管及更整合的資產管理。前九個月資本支出占營收比率為8.3%。

  財務長Sandhra Utsman在新聞稿中表示，此次業績展現了業務成長與財務紀律並駕齊驅的成果。她說，在EBITDA利潤率維持50%以上、營運現金流強勁的基礎上，InfraNexia已具備支撐下一階段成長的財務實力。

  ## 資產移轉計畫

  Telkom旗下批發基礎建設資產整合至InfraNexia的進程分兩批推進。第一批涵蓋批發基礎建設資產，自2026年1月1日起生效；第二批涵蓋估值49.86兆印尼盾的批發光纖連線資產，自2026年10月1日起生效。

  Telkom董事長Nanang Hendarno表示，此次移轉計畫旨在優化資產利用效率、強化管理聚焦，並開拓更廣泛的業務成長機遇。他說：「在更聚焦的經營方向下，InfraNexia有空間發展自身能力，並向更多業界夥伴擴大資產利用。」

  ## 人員編制

  截至2026年9月底，InfraNexia員工人數達1,396人，較2025年底的1,128人增加，反映公司在擴大職責範疇的同時，也持續強化組織能量。

  ## 產業意義

  批發接取指標的大幅躍升——位元流連線數成長三倍、VULA連線數成長逾兩倍——顯示第三方網路業者對InfraNexia產品的採用率持續攀升。35.5萬根電線桿納入基礎建設共享安排，亦說明實體資產共用模式正在電信業者之間逐漸普及。

  第二批資產移轉規模達49.86兆印尼盾，大幅充實InfraNexia的資產規模，提升其服務TelkomGroup體系以外業者的能力，符合TelkomGroup致力打造更具競爭力企業架構的既定目標。

  InfraNexia董事長Lukman Hakim Abd Rauf表示，第二批移轉的完成，標誌著公司以更大的規模踏入全新篇章。InfraNexia表示，將優化兩批移轉資產的利用效益、透過網路現代化提升服務品質，並擴大與業界夥伴的合作。公司並指出，目前正與Danantara Indonesia攜手合作，致力打造可靠、高效且永續的數位基礎建設。
