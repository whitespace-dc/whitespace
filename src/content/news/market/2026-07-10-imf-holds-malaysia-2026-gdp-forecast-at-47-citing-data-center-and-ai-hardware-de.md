body_en: |
  The International Monetary Fund held Malaysia's 2026 gross domestic product growth forecast at 4.7% in its July 2026 World Economic Outlook Update, citing data center activity and a broader global technology upcycle as the primary growth drivers, even as the fund warned of mounting downside risks from geopolitical conflict and commodity price pressures.

  The IMF projects Malaysia's growth will ease to 4.3% in 2027. "In Malaysia, the economy is projected to grow at a rate of 4.7% in 2026, benefitting from data centre activity and the upturn in the global technology cycle," the fund said in the report.

  ## Malaysia among top AI hardware exporters

  The IMF named Malaysia alongside South Korea, Taiwan, and Thailand as the world's top four net exporters of AI-related hardware. All four economies recorded stronger-than-expected first-quarter growth, which the fund attributed to the technology-led demand surge tied to artificial intelligence adoption. AI-related hardware includes processors, memory, and the networking and server equipment that underpin data center build-outs globally.

  ## Global backdrop

  Worldwide, the IMF projected economic growth will slow to 3.0% in 2026, down from 3.5% in 2025, before recovering to 3.4% in 2027. The fund said drag from the Middle East conflict is being partly offset by stronger demand from the global technology cycle, particularly AI adoption.

  Global inflation is expected to rise to 4.7% in 2026 from 4.1% in 2025, before easing to 3.9% in 2027, as higher energy and food prices reverse earlier disinflation progress. Crude oil prices are projected to rise by nearly one-third in 2026 compared with the prior year, with natural gas and fertilizer prices also expected to move higher. The IMF said energy prices are projected to remain above pre-war levels.

  ## Diverging country outlooks

  The IMF drew a clear dividing line between economies exposed to technology-related trade and investment, which it said are likely to outperform, and energy-importing countries with limited participation in the technology supply chain, which could face weaker activity. Malaysia's position in the AI hardware export chain places it in the stronger-outlook category under that framework.

  ## Risks

  The fund cautioned that risks remain tilted to the downside. It cited renewed conflict in the Middle East, higher commodity prices, supply-chain disruptions, trade fragmentation, and a possible correction in AI-driven market valuations as the principal threats to the baseline forecast.

  ## Policy priorities

  For policymakers, the IMF said priorities include preserving price stability, rebuilding fiscal buffers, and investing in energy security, digital infrastructure, and workforce skills to better capture gains from AI and digitalization.

  The IMF's July 2026 World Economic Outlook Update is scheduled to be followed by the full October World Economic Outlook, which will include updated country-by-country projections across the fund's full membership.

  ## IMF維持馬來西亞2026年GDP預測4.7%，資料中心與AI硬體需求為關鍵支撐

  國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）在2026年7月發布的《世界經濟展望》更新報告中，維持馬來西亞2026年國內生產毛額（GDP）成長預測4.7%不變，並將資料中心活躍度及全球科技景氣大幅上揚列為主要成長動能。與此同時，IMF也警告，地緣政治衝突與大宗商品價格壓力正使下行風險持續升高。

  IMF預測，馬來西亞2027年的經濟成長率將放緩至4.3%。該基金在報告中表示：「馬來西亞經濟預計在2026年以4.7%的速度成長，受惠於資料中心活動以及全球科技循環的回升。」

  ## 馬來西亞躋身全球AI硬體出口大國

  IMF將馬來西亞與南韓、台灣及泰國並列為全球四大AI相關硬體淨出口國。這四個經濟體在第一季均繳出優於預期的成長成績，IMF將此歸因於人工智慧普及所帶動的科技主導需求浪潮。AI相關硬體涵蓋處理器、記憶體，以及支撐全球資料中心建置的網路與伺服器設備。

  ## 全球整體背景

  在全球層面，IMF預測2026年全球經濟成長率將從2025年的3.5%放緩至3.0%，並於2027年回升至3.4%。IMF表示，中東衝突帶來的拖累，部分由全球科技循環——尤其是AI普及——所帶動的強勁需求所抵銷。

  全球通膨率預計將從2025年的4.1%上升至2026年的4.7%，並於2027年回落至3.9%，原因在於能源與糧食價格走高，逆轉了先前的通膨下行進程。2026年原油價格預計將較前一年上漲近三分之一，天然氣與化肥價格亦預期走高。IMF表示，能源價格預計將持續維持在戰前水準以上。

  ## 各國前景分歧

  IMF明確區分了兩類經濟體的前景差異：一類是深度參與科技相關貿易與投資的經濟體，預計表現將優於大盤；另一類則是能源進口國，且在科技供應鏈中參與度有限，可能面臨較疲弱的經濟活動。馬來西亞在AI硬體出口鏈中的地位，使其在上述框架下被歸入前景較樂觀的一類。

  ## 風險因素

  IMF警告，當前風險仍偏向下行。報告中點名中東衝突再起、大宗商品價格走高、供應鏈受阻、貿易碎片化，以及AI驅動資產估值出現修正等，為基準預測面臨的主要威脅。

  ## 政策優先事項

  在政策建議方面，IMF指出，各國決策者的優先要務包括：維護物價穩定、重建財政緩衝空間，以及投資能源安全、數位基礎建設與勞動力技能，以更充分地把握AI與數位化帶來的發展紅利。

  IMF的2026年7月《世界經濟展望》更新報告發布後，完整版將於今年10月的《世界經濟展望》正式出爐，屆時將涵蓋對IMF全體成員國逐一更新的經濟預測數據。
