body_en: |
  Helsinki-based AI cloud provider Verda has appointed Mikko Einiö as Chief Financial Officer, giving the company a senior finance executive with deep capital markets experience as it pushes to scale its on-demand computing business globally.

  Einiö, who will be based in London, takes responsibility for financial planning, capital raising, and capital allocation. He joins from Nokia, where he served as Head of Corporate Development. Before Nokia, he spent nearly two decades at Morgan Stanley in London and New York, advising on equity and debt financings, mergers and acquisitions, and initial public offerings.

  Verda said the hire comes after a period of rapid revenue growth. In June, the company reported an annualised revenue run rate of approximately $120 million — a tenfold increase over the previous 18 months.

  ## Capital demands of AI infrastructure build-out

  The appointment reflects pressure on AI infrastructure companies to secure large amounts of funding while sustaining heavy capital spending on data centres, server hardware, and network expansion. Verda has raised more than $200 million to date from investors including Lifeline Ventures, byFounders, J12 Ventures, Skaala, Varma, and Tesi, as well as financial institutions.

  Verda's business model centres on on-demand access to computing resources for AI workloads, with no requirement for a sales process or long-term contract. The company manages the full stack — from physical data centre and server hardware through to the cloud platform that customers use directly. It employs around 150 people from more than 30 nationalities and operates across Europe, the United States, and Asia.

  Verda is also designated an NVIDIA Preferred Partner, a status the company said places it within the supply chain of the dominant AI chip supplier.

  ## London expansion

  London is set to become one of Verda's main global hubs. Einiö will be based there, and the company said it plans to hire more than 100 employees in the city over the next six months.

  Founder and Chief Executive Officer Ruben Bryon said the hire was directly tied to the capital requirements of global infrastructure expansion. "AI is becoming critical infrastructure, and our goal is to build the platform frontier AI teams can depend on as they scale. We are scaling at pace, and Mikko brings more than two decades of experience raising and deploying capital for some of the world's leading companies. He will be central as we expand our infrastructure to meet the global demand for compute," Bryon said.

  Einiö framed the opportunity in terms of European ambition in AI infrastructure. "Building AI infrastructure at global scale requires access to significant capital, disciplined capital allocation and flawless operational execution. The coming years represent a critical opportunity for European companies to scale and establish themselves as global leaders in AI infrastructure. Verda stood out to me because Ruben and the team combine bold global ambition with an exceptional pace of execution and a clear understanding of what it takes to succeed in this market. I look forward to helping build the financial and capital platform needed to turn that ambition into sustained global growth," he said.

  ## Significance for the sector

  The pattern of AI infrastructure and cloud companies recruiting executives with investment banking and corporate finance backgrounds has become more pronounced as the sector competes for large capital pools. For companies operating at Verda's stage — rapid revenue growth, heavy capital requirements, and international expansion — the ability to raise and deploy funding efficiently can be as consequential as customer acquisition. Verda plans to hire more than 100 staff in London alone over the next six months.

  ## Verda延攬Mikko Einiö出任財務長，AI雲端業者加速全球布局

  總部位於芬蘭赫爾辛基的AI雲端供應商Verda宣布任命Mikko Einiö擔任財務長，為公司引進一位在資本市場擁有深厚背景的資深財務主管，以支撐其隨選運算業務的全球擴張計畫。

  Einiö將常駐英國倫敦，全面負責財務規劃、資金募集與資本配置。他此前任職於諾基亞，擔任企業發展部門主管；加入諾基亞之前，他在倫敦與紐約的摩根士丹利服務近二十年，專注於股權與債務融資、併購交易及首次公開發行等業務。

  Verda表示，此次延攬人才恰逢公司營收急速成長之際。今年六月，該公司公布的年化營收規模約達1.2億美元（約新台幣38億元），較前18個月成長十倍。

  ## AI基礎設施建置的資本壓力

  此次人事任命，反映出AI基礎設施業者在大規模籌資的同時，仍須持續投入大量資本於資料中心、伺服器硬體及網路擴建的現實壓力。Verda迄今已累計募資逾2億美元，投資方包括Lifeline Ventures、byFounders、J12 Ventures、Skaala、Varma、Tesi等創投基金，以及多家金融機構。

  Verda的商業模式以隨選方式提供AI工作負載所需的運算資源，無需繁複的銷售流程，亦不要求客戶簽訂長期合約。公司管理完整的技術堆疊——從實體資料中心與伺服器硬體，到客戶直接使用的雲端平台，一手包辦。目前Verda員工規模約150人，來自逾30個國籍，業務遍及歐洲、美國與亞洲。

  此外，Verda也獲得輝達（NVIDIA）認證的「優選合作夥伴」資格，公司表示此一地位使其得以進入AI晶片龍頭的供應鏈體系。

  ## 倫敦擴張計畫

  倫敦即將成為Verda全球主要樞紐之一。Einiö將以倫敦為主要駐點，公司並表示計畫在未來六個月內於當地新招募逾百名員工。

  創辦人暨執行長Ruben Bryon表示，此次延攬人才與全球基礎設施擴張的資本需求直接相關。他說：「AI正在成為關鍵基礎設施，我們的目標是打造一個讓前沿AI團隊在擴張過程中得以依賴的平台。我們正在快速擴張，而Mikko擁有逾二十年為全球頂尖企業籌資與部署資本的經驗，在我們推進基礎設施以因應全球運算需求的過程中，他將扮演核心角色。」

  Einiö則以歐洲在AI基礎設施領域的宏大志向來詮釋這次機會。他表示：「在全球規模上建構AI基礎設施，需要取得充裕的資本、嚴謹的資本配置紀律，以及無懈可擊的營運執行力。未來數年，對歐洲企業而言是一個關鍵機遇，可藉此擴大規模、確立在全球AI基礎設施領域的領導地位。Verda之所以吸引我，在於Ruben與團隊兼具大膽的全球視野、出色的執行速度，以及對於如何在這個市場取得成功的清晰認識。我期待協助建構所需的財務與資本平台，將這份雄心轉化為持久的全球成長。」

  ## 對產業的意義

  AI基礎設施與雲端業者積極延攬具備投資銀行與企業財務背景主管的趨勢，隨著業界競相爭取大規模資本而愈發明顯。對於處於Verda這一發展階段的公司——營收快速成長、資本需求龐大、同步推進國際擴張——高效率地募集與部署資金，其重要性絲毫不亞於客戶開發。Verda計畫單在倫敦一地，便於未來六個月內新增逾百名員工。
