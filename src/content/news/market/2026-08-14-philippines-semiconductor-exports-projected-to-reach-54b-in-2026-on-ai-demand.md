  ## Philippines Semiconductor Exports Projected to Reach $54B in 2026 on AI Demand

  The Philippines' semiconductor and electronics industry group raised its 2026 export growth forecast to 10%, projecting full-year shipments of $53–54 billion, after AI infrastructure buildout drove exports well beyond expectations in 2025, the group's president said on Friday.

  Danilo C. Lachica, president of the Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines Foundation, Inc. (SEIPI), the main industry association for the sector, said the revised forecast compares with an initial projection of 5% growth. "Initially, we were conservative at 5%, but looking at the performance of the other industries, we upped it to 10% for 2026," he told reporters on the sidelines of an industry event.

  Electronics exports rose 16.11% to $49.64 billion in 2025, up from $42.75 billion in 2024, according to SEIPI data — a result Lachica said the group had not anticipated. "For 2025, we did not expect exports to hit 16%, so we only projected flat growth because of the geopolitical wars and supply-chain disruption, but what happened is export growth was driven by AI," he said.

  ## First-Half 2026 Already Running Ahead

  Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) data for the first half of 2026 show the sector already running at an elevated pace. Electronic products — the country's top export category — rose 20.7% to $26.1 billion in the January–June period, from $21.62 billion in the same period a year earlier. Semiconductors, which account for the bulk of electronic product exports, climbed 21.7% to $19.5 billion in that span, from $16.02 billion a year ago.

  ## Where the Philippines Fits in the Semiconductor Chain

  The Philippines holds approximately 5% of the global semiconductor market as of 2025, according to Lachica. That position rests almost entirely on back-end manufacturing — the assembly, testing, and packaging of chips after wafers have been fabricated elsewhere — along with growing integrated circuit (IC) design activity. The country does not produce AI chips directly; instead, Lachica said, it specializes in support equipment used to power AI engines and data centers.

  "Since AI and data centers are still gonna grow, and then there's automotive electronics, so we're projecting 10% growth in exports," he said.

  Hong Kong is the top destination for Philippine-made electronics and semiconductor exports, followed by the United States and China. China is also the top source of electronics imports into the Philippines, driven primarily by silicon wafers — the thin discs of semiconductor material that are the starting point for chip manufacturing.

  ## Wafer Fabrication Gap

  The absence of domestic wafer fabrication — the front-end process of printing circuit patterns onto silicon wafers — is the central constraint on the Philippines' ability to move up the semiconductor value chain, Lachica said. "Without the wafer fab, we're not realizing the full potential to complete the semiconductor supply chain," he said.

  SEIPI plans to resubmit to the Department of Science and Technology a proposal to establish a lab-scale wafer fabrication facility by 2027. The proposal is framed as a proof-of-concept rather than commercial production. "It will be the proof-of-concept to show the world that we can specialize in wafer fab," Lachica said.

  Lachica said attracting a major wafer foundry — such as Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC) or Texas Instruments (TI) — is not a near-term possibility without an established domestic track record. "You can't convince like a TSMC or a TI to build a wafer fab in the Philippines if we don't have the track record," he said, responding to a question about whether a fab could be sited at the planned Pax Silica AI-native hub in New Clark City, Tarlac province.

  ## Pax Silica and Upstream Ambitions

  Department of Economy, Planning, and Development Secretary Arsenio M. Balisacan said the Pax Silica development in New Clark City, Tarlac — described as an AI-native hub — could lift the value of the country's electronics and semiconductor exports if it succeeds in attracting relevant industries. "If you look at our semiconductors and electronics, they are somewhat left behind because of the new technologies," Balisacan told reporters at a separate briefing last week. "So, with this Pax Silica, if we succeed in attracting these kinds of industries, it is going to be a game changer for that sector to become more robust."

  SEIPI is separately looking to expand the country's share of the electronics manufacturing services (EMS) market — the segment covering complete assembly of consumer electronic devices such as mobile phones. "It's a very big trillion-dollar market, but our share is only 1% for now," Lachica said.

  ## Significance for the Region

  The Philippines' position as a back-end semiconductor hub means its export performance is directly linked to the pace of AI infrastructure investment globally, rather than to chip design or fabrication cycles. The 2025 outperformance — 16% growth against a flat forecast — illustrates how quickly AI-driven demand can move export totals for a country concentrated in support components and packaging.

  SEIPI's planned resubmission of the lab-scale wafer fabrication proposal to the Department of Science and Technology is the next concrete step the group has identified toward building a domestic front-end capability, with a target submission date tied to a 2027 facility.

  ## 菲律賓半導體出口預計2026年在AI需求帶動下突破540億美元

  菲律賓半導體與電子產業公會將2026年出口成長預測上調至10%，預計全年出貨金額達530至540億美元。理事長週五表示，AI基礎建設的大規模擴張在2025年帶動出口遠超預期，是此次上調的直接原因。

  菲律賓半導體暨電子產業基金會（Semiconductor and Electronics Industries in the Philippines Foundation, Inc.，SEIPI）理事長達尼洛·拉奇卡（Danilo C. Lachica）指出，修正後的預測相較於原先5%的保守估計已大幅提升。他在一場產業活動場邊向記者表示：「我們最初保守估計5%，但看到其他產業的表現後，我們將2026年的預測上調至10%。」

  根據SEIPI統計數據，2025年菲律賓電子出口年增16.11%，達496.4億美元，較2024年的427.5億美元大幅成長——這個結果是拉奇卡坦言始料未及的。「2025年的部分，我們並沒有預期出口成長會達到16%，當時因為地緣政治衝突和供應鏈中斷，我們只預測持平，但實際上出口成長是由AI所驅動的，」他說。

  ## 2026年上半年已超前跑

  菲律賓統計局（Philippine Statistics Authority，PSA）的2026年上半年數據顯示，該產業已維持在相當強勁的成長步調。電子產品作為菲律賓最大出口類別，今年1至6月期間年增20.7%，達261億美元，去年同期為216.2億美元。其中，占電子產品出口大宗的半導體同期成長21.7%至195億美元，去年同期為160.2億美元。

  ## 菲律賓在半導體供應鏈中的定位

  拉奇卡表示，截至2025年，菲律賓約占全球半導體市場的5%。這個地位幾乎完全建立在後段製程上——即晶圓在其他地方完成製造後，於菲律賓進行封裝、測試與組裝——同時也有日益成長的積體電路（IC）設計業務。菲律賓本身並不直接生產AI晶片；拉奇卡表示，菲律賓的強項在於供應用於驅動AI引擎與資料中心的支援設備。

  「由於AI和資料中心的需求仍將持續成長，再加上汽車電子市場，我們預測出口成長率可達10%，」他說。

  香港是菲律賓電子及半導體產品的最大出口目的地，其次依序為美國和中國。另一方面，中國也是菲律賓最大的電子產品進口來源國，主要進口品項為矽晶圓——這種薄圓片正是晶片製造的起點原材料。

  ## 晶圓製造的缺口

  拉奇卡表示，缺乏本土晶圓製造能力——即在矽晶圓上蝕刻電路圖案的前段製程——是菲律賓無法在半導體價值鏈中向上提升的核心瓶頸。「沒有晶圓廠，我們就無法充分發揮潛力、打造完整的半導體供應鏈，」他說。

  SEIPI計畫重新向科學技術部提交一份提案，目標於2027年建立一座實驗室規模的晶圓製造設施。這份提案定位為概念驗證，而非商業量產。「這將向全世界證明，我們有能力在晶圓製造領域建立專業，」拉奇卡說。

  他表示，若缺乏本國既有的實績，要在短期內吸引台積電（TSMC）或德州儀器（Texas Instruments，TI）等大型晶圓代工廠赴菲設廠，並不現實。「如果我們沒有實績，就無法說服台積電或德州儀器在菲律賓建造晶圓廠，」他在回應一個問題時說——該問題涉及是否能在打拉省（Tarlac）新克拉克市（New Clark City）計畫中的Pax Silica AI原生園區內設置晶圓廠。

  ## Pax Silica與上游產業雄心

  菲律賓經濟規劃發展部部長阿塞尼奧·巴利薩坎（Arsenio M. Balisacan）表示，若打拉省新克拉克市的Pax Silica開發計畫——定位為AI原生樞紐——能成功吸引相關產業進駐，將有望提升菲律賓電子及半導體出口的整體附加價值。他上週在一場獨立的記者簡報會上說：「如果我們看看半導體和電子業，由於新技術的浪潮，它們在某種程度上已經落後了。如果Pax Silica能夠成功吸引這類產業，對於這個產業要變得更加強健，將是一個翻轉全局的契機。」

  SEIPI也另外積極拓展菲律賓在電子製造服務（EMS）市場的占有率——這個市場涵蓋手機等消費電子產品的完整組裝業務。「這是一個規模達兆美元的龐大市場，但我們目前的市占率只有1%，」拉奇卡說。

  ## 對區域的意義

  菲律賓作為後段半導體製造重鎮的定位，意味著其出口表現與全球AI基礎建設投資的節奏直接掛鉤，而非取決於晶片設計或前段製造週期。2025年大幅超越預期的表現——在持平預測下達成16%的成長——清楚說明了AI驅動需求能以多快的速度拉抬一個集中於支援元件與封裝業務的國家出口總額。

  SEIPI計畫將實驗室規模晶圓製造設施提案重新遞交科學技術部，並以2027年完成設施建置為目標，這是該公會目前所確認的、邁向國內前段製程能力的下一個具體步驟。
