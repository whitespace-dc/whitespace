body_en: |
  PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance (IIF) has committed approximately Rp 601.4 billion in financing across two projects: a hyperscale data center campus in Bekasi, West Java, and a cardiac specialty hospital in Medan, North Sumatra, the company announced on Monday.

  ## Data center: GAIA Campus, Bekasi

  The larger of the two allocations — Rp 358.3 billion, equivalent to US$20 million — represents IIF's participation in a syndicated loan to PT Digital Gayana Ekagrata (DGE), the developer of GAIA Campus. The campus is located within the Greenland International Industrial Center (GIIC) in Bekasi.

  The syndicated facility supports phase one of GAIA Campus, which comprises two buildings designated CGK1 and CGK2 with a combined capacity of 58.9 megawatts (MW). Hyperscale describes data center infrastructure designed to scale computing and storage capacity to very large sizes. IIF said the campus is intended to meet demand for digital infrastructure services including data storage, management, and technology-based services for businesses.

  IIF said the project forms part of efforts to strengthen the supporting infrastructure for Indonesia's digital economy as demand for computing capacity and data management increases.

  ## Hospital: Medan Heart Center

  The remaining Rp 243.11 billion goes to PT Medan Kardia Indonesia to fund the development and operations of Medan Heart Center, a dedicated cardiac hospital in Medan, North Sumatra.

  The facility is planned as a Class B hospital with a minimum of 75 beds, built on approximately 5,300 square meters of land with 8,000 square meters of floor area. It will provide diagnostic, interventional, and cardiovascular care services.

  IIF said the hospital is intended to expand access to specialist cardiac and vascular care for residents of Medan and the broader North Sumatra region. The company also cited reducing the need for patients to travel long distances for specialist referrals and supporting more integrated healthcare outside major metropolitan areas.

  ## IIF's rationale

  Rizki Pribadi Hasan, President Director and CEO of IIF, said in Monday's statement that the financing for both projects is aimed at addressing digital infrastructure needs and broadening access to quality healthcare. He said the measure of success in infrastructure financing extends beyond project completion to the long-term value and impact generated for businesses and society.

  IIF said both transactions reflect its continuing role in infrastructure financing through funding solutions and advisory services. The company described the two sectors as contributors to economic competitiveness and improved public service quality.
body_zh: |
  PT Indonesia Infrastructure Finance（IIF）週一宣布，將就兩項計畫承諾提供合計約 6,014 億印尼盾的融資：一為西爪哇省勿加泗的超大規模資料中心園區，另一為北蘇門答臘省棉蘭的心臟病專科醫院。

  ## 資料中心：GAIA 園區，勿加泗

  兩筆融資中規模較大的一筆為 3,583 億印尼盾（折合 2,000 萬美元），代表 IIF 參與向 PT Digital Gayana Ekagrata（DGE）提供的聯合貸款；DGE 為 GAIA 園區的開發商，園區坐落於勿加泗綠地國際工業城（GIIC）內。

  此聯合貸款用以支持 GAIA 園區第一期工程，包含兩棟分別命名為 CGK1 與 CGK2 的建築，合計容量達 58.9 百萬瓦（MW）。超大規模（hyperscale）係指專為大規模擴展運算與儲存容量而設計的資料中心基礎設施。IIF 表示，該園區旨在滿足企業對數位基礎設施服務的需求，涵蓋資料儲存、管理及科技應用服務。

  IIF 指出，此計畫是強化印尼數位經濟支撐基礎設施的一環，以因應日益增長的運算容量與資料管理需求。

  ## 醫院：棉蘭心臟中心

  其餘 2,431.1 億印尼盾將撥付予 PT Medan Kardia Indonesia，用於建設並營運「棉蘭心臟中心」（Medan Heart Center）——一家位於北蘇門答臘省棉蘭的心臟病專科醫院。

  該設施規劃為 B 級醫院，最少設置 75 張病床，建地面積約 5,300 平方公尺，樓地板面積約 8,000 平方公尺，將提供診斷、介入治療及心血管照護服務。

  IIF 表示，此醫院旨在提升棉蘭及北蘇門答臘廣大地區居民取得心臟與血管專科醫療的機會。公司亦指出，此舉有助於減少病患為尋求專科轉診而長途奔波的需要，並支持主要都會區以外更加整合的醫療服務體系發展。

  ## IIF 的投資理念

  IIF 總裁暨執行長 Rizki Pribadi Hasan 在週一的聲明中表示，兩項計畫的融資旨在因應數位基礎設施需求，並擴大優質醫療的可及性。他強調，基礎設施融資的成功衡量標準，不僅止於計畫完工，更延伸至為企業與社會長遠創造的價值與影響。

  IIF 表示，兩筆交易體現了公司透過資金解決方案與顧問服務持續投入基礎設施融資的角色定位。公司並指出，數位基礎設施與醫療此兩大領域，皆是提升經濟競爭力及改善公共服務品質的重要貢獻力量。
