  ## INET opens Jakarta colocation site at Gedung Cyber through data center subsidiary SIDI

  PT Sinergi Inti Andalan Prima Tbk. (INET), a publicly listed Indonesian digital infrastructure company, opened its first data center in Jakarta on August 11, 2026, inaugurating the SIDI JKT01 facility at Gedung Cyber 1 in South Jakarta through wholly owned subsidiary PT Sinergi Inti Data Indonesia (SIDI).

  The facility occupies the fifth floor of Gedung Cyber 1 at Jalan Kuningan Barat Raya No. 8, Mampang Prapatan, South Jakarta. The site will provide colocation services — meaning customers rent physical space and power to house their own servers, network hardware, and related equipment — to businesses in the Jakarta area, the company said.

  ## Strategic rationale

  INET President Director Muhammad Arif said the location inside Gedung Cyber was chosen for its position within what he described as the national connectivity and interconnection ecosystem. "Gedung Cyber has a strong position in the national connectivity ecosystem. With the operation of Data Center SIDI JKT01 at this location, INET can strengthen services for customers who need data center access in an area close to traffic and interconnection hubs," Arif said in an official statement on August 12.

  Gedung Cyber 1 is a commercial tower in the Kuningan business district that houses numerous technology and telecommunications tenants, making it an established point of network presence in Jakarta.

  ## Subsidiary and service focus

  SIDI CEO Saripudin, who also serves as a Director of INET, said the inauguration marks the start of the subsidiary's stepwise build-out of data center operations in the capital. "Data Center SIDI JKT01 we have prepared to support the needs of customers who require data center facilities with a strategic location. This inauguration is an important step for SIDI in strengthening data center services gradually," Saripudin said.

  Saripudin added that SIDI will concentrate on service readiness, facility maintenance, and operational support for customers at JKT01.

  ## Significance

  The opening adds a colocation business line to INET's existing portfolio of connectivity and digital infrastructure services, which the company has previously built around network and submarine cable projects. INET has separately announced plans for the NewConnext corporate connectivity product and the ASEAN Fiber Connect submarine cable system, according to earlier reporting cited in the source publication. The JKT01 site represents the first data center asset disclosed under the SIDI subsidiary.

  SIDI's stated next focus will be maintaining operational readiness and expanding customer support at the JKT01 facility as the company builds out its data center portfolio incrementally.

  ## INET透過資料中心子公司SIDI在Gedung Cyber開設雅加達主機代管站

  PT Sinergi Inti Andalan Prima Tbk.（INET）是一家印尼上市數位基礎設施業者，旗下全資子公司PT Sinergi Inti Data Indonesia（SIDI）於2026年8月11日正式啟用首座雅加達資料中心，地點為南雅加達Gedung Cyber 1的SIDI JKT01設施。

  SIDI JKT01設施坐落於南雅加達Mampang Prapatan區Jalan Kuningan Barat Raya No. 8的Gedung Cyber 1五樓。公司表示，該站點將向雅加達地區企業提供主機代管服務——亦即客戶租用實體空間與電力，用以安置自有伺服器、網路硬體及相關設備。

  ## 策略考量

  INET總裁穆罕默德·阿里夫（Muhammad Arif）表示，選址Gedung Cyber的原因，在於他所形容的「全國網路連通與互連生態系」中的關鍵地位。阿里夫於8月12日在官方聲明中指出：「Gedung Cyber在全國網路連通生態系中占有強勢地位。SIDI JKT01資料中心在此落地營運後，INET得以強化對需要在流量集散與互連樞紐附近取用資料中心的客戶之服務。」

  Gedung Cyber 1是位於Kuningan商業區的一棟商辦大樓，進駐了眾多科技與電信租戶，是雅加達歷史悠久、成熟的網路匯聚節點之一。

  ## 子公司定位與服務重心

  SIDI執行長薩里普丁（Saripudin）同時兼任INET董事，他表示此次揭牌啟用標誌著該子公司在首都循序漸進推展資料中心業務的起點。薩里普丁說：「SIDI JKT01資料中心已做好準備，以支援需要具備策略地點之資料中心設施的客戶。此次啟用對SIDI逐步強化資料中心服務而言，是重要的一步。」

  薩里普丁進一步表示，SIDI將聚焦於JKT01站點的服務就緒度、設施維護，以及對客戶的營運支援。

  ## 重要意義

  此次開幕為INET現有的網路連通與數位基礎設施服務組合增添了主機代管業務線；INET此前的業務佈局主要以網路與海底電纜專案為核心。依據相關媒體先前的報導，INET另行宣布了NewConnext企業連網產品及ASEAN Fiber Connect海底電纜系統計畫。JKT01站點是目前已揭露的首個歸屬SIDI子公司名下的資料中心資產。

  SIDI表示，下一步將著重於JKT01設施的營運就緒度維護，並在公司逐步建立資料中心資產組合的過程中，持續擴大對客戶的支援服務。
