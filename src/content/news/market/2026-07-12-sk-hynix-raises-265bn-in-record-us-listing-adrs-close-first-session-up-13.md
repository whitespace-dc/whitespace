body_en: |
  SK Hynix raised $26.5 billion in a Nasdaq American depositary receipt (ADR) offering priced July 10, setting the record for the largest US share sale by a foreign company and closing its debut session on July 11 up 13% above the offer price.

  The South Korean chipmaker sold 177.9 million ADRs — each representing one-tenth of a Seoul-traded ordinary share — at $149 apiece. The ADRs opened at $170 and closed at $168.01 in their first session, with more than 106 million units traded, according to Bloomberg data. At $26.5 billion, the offering surpassed Alibaba Group Holding's US debut to become the third-largest debut share sale in history, Bloomberg data showed.

  The offering was more than seven times oversubscribed, with investor interest approaching $200 billion, according to deal terms. The top 25 accounts received roughly two-thirds of the ADRs, and nearly half went to the 10 largest orders. Cornerstone investors Baillie Gifford, Coatue Management, and Situational Awareness Partners received approximately $5 billion of ADRs, below the $7 billion maximum indicated in US regulatory filings, according to people familiar with the matter. Bank of America, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan Chase led the offering, with nine other firms participating.

  The ADRs trade on a when-issued basis on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol SKHYV, with regular-way trading scheduled to begin July 13 under the symbol SKHY.

  SK Hynix plans to use the proceeds to build additional manufacturing capacity and purchase extreme ultraviolet lithography (EUV) machines — the specialized equipment required for producing leading-edge semiconductors — according to company filings. In June, the company said it aimed to double its wafer-processing capacity over the next five years.

  ## HBM market position and AI demand

  SK Hynix is the world's leading supplier of high-bandwidth memory (HBM), a chip architecture that stacks multiple memory layers to achieve faster data transfer for artificial intelligence computing workloads. The company controlled 57% of global HBM revenue in the fourth quarter of 2025, according to Counterpoint Research. An early commitment to HBM development made SK Hynix the primary supplier for Nvidia's AI processors while Samsung Electronics was slower to respond, sources noted.

  The offering absorbed heavy demand despite recent volatility in chipmaker shares tied to questions about the sustainability of data center spending. The Edge Malaysia reported the market comfortably absorbed the $26.5 billion sale. The ADRs priced at roughly a 3% premium to Thursday's Seoul closing price and were trading at approximately a 16% premium to Seoul-listed common stock by end of the first session, partly because restrictions on converting Korean shares into US instruments limit arbitrage between the two markets. That premium has also helped narrow SK Hynix's valuation gap with US rival Micron Technology.

  "There was clearly a latent demand by US investors for the shares," said Steve Sosnick, chief strategist at Interactive Brokers, who cited rapid growth of the Roundhill Memory exchange-traded fund (DRAM) as evidence that US investors had been using the fund as a proxy for exposure to SK Hynix and Samsung before the ADR listing.

  Sanghyun Park, founder of Clepsydra Capital, said the pricing showed "global funds are completely fine paying a bit of a toll to bypass local" index and currency-related friction for direct access to the company's HBM position.

  Chief executive Kwak Noh-Jung rang the Nasdaq opening bell and addressed investors directly. "HBM stands at the heart of the AI revolution," Kwak said.

  ## Memory shortage outlook

  Kwak, in remarks The Edge Malaysia described as his first English-language interview, said memory chip shortages will likely persist beyond 2030, citing both the company's internal analysis and customer behavior. "Customers are signing long-term contracts because they believe that the shortage situation will last for longer," he said. The shortage extends beyond AI-specific HBM to affect memory supply for computers, phones, and vehicles, Kwak said.

  Micron Technology chief executive Sanjay Mehrotra offered a comparable view last month. "We currently do not have line of sight as to when memory supply will be able to catch up with increasing demand," Mehrotra said in a post-earnings conference call, according to the report.

  Ryuta Makino, a research analyst at Gabelli Funds — whose funds are invested in SK Hynix's common shares — said the supply-demand balance will remain tight until at least 2028, partly because the industry is being deliberate about adding production volume. "There will still be up and down years, but the down years will be less than they used to be," Makino said.

  Di Zhou, a portfolio manager at Thornburg Investment Management, identified the principal downside risk. "If we see any weakness or softness in the demand side, that would be the key risk to derail this memory party," Zhou said.

  Kwak said the rationale for the US listing included closer collaboration with AI customers and greater access to US AI talent. He did not rule out eventual US manufacturing but said any site would need to meet the company's requirements for electricity, water, and available talent.

  ## US IPO context and forward steps

  SK Hynix's ADR offering took place against a backdrop of record US capital markets activity. US listings excluding blank-check firms raised $126.8 billion in the first half of this year, a record, led by SpaceX's $86.2 billion initial public offering in June, the largest in history. Alphabet is separately set to raise $85 billion through the latter part of the year for AI investment.

  The offering caps a recovery from the company's origins in a creditor-led bailout in South Korea that combined two predecessor firms, LG Semiconductor and Hyundai Electronics, following years of boom-and-bust memory cycles. SK Hynix's Seoul-listed market capitalization has more than tripled this year. Its Seoul-traded stock has moved at least 5% in either direction on more than 50 sessions so far in 2026, compared with roughly 37 such sessions across all of last year and approximately 44 across the preceding three years combined, according to Bloomberg calculations.

  Chairman Chey Tae-won signaled in a Bloomberg Television interview that the company could issue additional US shares if returns are strong enough to sustain investor demand. "The first thing we have to do is keep the stock price stable, and then hopefully in the long run we can have the upside potential," Chey said.

  SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics are also set to invest in South Korean projects through a government-led initiative worth at least $880 billion, according to sources.

  Tokyo-based Kioxia Holdings, whose Japan-listed shares have risen more than 2,900% over the past year, is also preparing a US offering, a signal that SK Hynix's ADR structure may attract further Asian chipmakers to US markets.
body_zh: |
  SK 海力士於7月10日完成那斯達克美國存託憑證（ADR）定價，共募集265億美元，創下外國企業在美最大股票銷售紀錄，並於7月11日首個交易日收盤時，較發行價上漲13%。

  這家南韓晶片製造商共售出1億7,790萬份ADR——每份代表在首爾交易所掛牌普通股的十分之一——每份定價149美元。據彭博數據，ADR首個交易日開盤報170美元，收盤報168.01美元，成交量逾1億600萬份。以265億美元計，此次發行規模超越阿里巴巴集團赴美上市，成為史上第三大新股發行，彭博數據顯示。

  此次發行獲超額認購逾七倍，投資人認購興趣接近2,000億美元。據交易條款，前25大帳戶獲配約三分之二的ADR，最大的10筆訂單則獲配近一半。知情人士透露，基石投資人柏基投資（Baillie Gifford）、Coatue Management及Situational Awareness Partners共獲配約50億美元的ADR，低於美國監管申報文件中所示70億美元的上限。美國銀行、花旗集團、高盛及摩根大通擔任此次發行的主承銷商，另有九家機構共同參與。

  ADR目前在那斯達克全球精選市場以「SKHYV」為代號進行待交割交易，預計於7月13日以「SKHY」代號正式展開一般交易。

  根據公司申報文件，SK 海力士計畫將此次募資所得用於擴建製造產能，並採購極紫外光微影（EUV）設備——這是生產先進半導體所必需的專業儀器。該公司於6月表示，目標是在未來五年內將晶圓加工產能翻倍。

  ## HBM市場地位與AI需求

  SK 海力士是全球高頻寬記憶體（HBM）的領導供應商。HBM是一種將多層記憶體堆疊以提升人工智慧運算工作負載資料傳輸速度的晶片架構。根據Counterpoint Research的數據，2025年第四季，該公司掌握全球HBM營收的57%。知情人士指出，SK 海力士因早期投入HBM開發，成為輝達AI處理器的主要供應商，而三星電子的反應則相對遲緩。

  此次發行在近期晶片股因市場質疑資料中心支出可持續性而出現波動的情況下，仍吸收了強勁需求。《馬來西亞邊鋒》（The Edge Malaysia）報導，市場輕鬆消化了265億美元的發行規模。ADR定價較週四首爾收盤價溢價約3%，至首個交易日結束時，較首爾掛牌普通股溢價約16%，部分原因在於韓國股票轉換為美國存託憑證的限制壓縮了兩地市場之間的套利空間。此溢價也有助於縮小SK 海力士與美國競爭對手美光科技（Micron Technology）之間的估值差距。

  「美國投資人對這檔股票顯然存在潛在需求，」盈透證券（Interactive Brokers）首席策略師史帝夫·索斯尼克（Steve Sosnick）表示。他援引Roundhill記憶體指數股票型基金（DRAM）的快速成長作為佐證，指出在ADR掛牌之前，美國投資人一直以該基金作為取得SK 海力士與三星電子曝險的替代工具。

  Clepsydra Capital創辦人朴相鉉（Sanghyun Park）表示，此次定價顯示「全球基金完全願意支付一定的通行費，繞過本地」與指數及匯率相關的摩擦，以直接持有該公司HBM部位。

  執行長郭魯正（Kwak Noh-Jung）親自敲響那斯達克的開盤鐘，並直接向投資人致詞。「HBM站在AI革命的核心，」郭魯正說。

  ## 記憶體短缺前景

  《馬來西亞邊鋒》報導，郭魯正在其首次英語媒體專訪中表示，記憶體晶片的短缺狀況可能持續到2030年以後，他援引公司內部分析及客戶行為作為依據。「客戶正在簽署長期合約，因為他們相信短缺局面將持續更長時間，」他說。郭魯正指出，此次短缺不僅限於AI專用HBM，也波及電腦、手機及汽車的記憶體供應。

  美光科技執行長桑杰·梅赫羅特拉（Sanjay Mehrotra）上個月也表達了相近的看法。報導引述梅赫羅特拉在財報後電話會議上的說法：「我們目前無法預見記憶體供給何時能夠趕上日益增長的需求。」

  Gabelli基金研究分析師牧野龍太（Ryuta Makino）——其旗下基金持有SK 海力士普通股——表示，供需失衡的局面至少將持續到2028年，部分原因是業界在擴大生產量方面態度審慎。「仍然會有好年和壞年，但壞年的幅度將比過去來得小，」牧野說。

  桑伯格投資管理（Thornburg Investment Management）投資組合經理狄·周（Di Zhou）點出主要下行風險。「如果需求端出現任何疲軟或放緩，那將是打亂這場記憶體盛宴的關鍵風險，」她說。

  郭魯正表示，赴美上市的考量包括與AI客戶更緊密的合作，以及更廣泛地接觸美國AI人才。他不排除未來在美設廠的可能性，但表示任何廠址都必須符合公司對電力、水資源及可用人才的要求。

  ## 美國IPO背景與後續動向

  SK 海力士的ADR發行，發生在美國資本市場創紀錄活躍的背景之下。今年上半年，美國上市案（不含空白支票公司）共募集1,268億美元，創下歷史紀錄，由SpaceX於6月完成的862億美元首次公開發行居首，為史上規模最大的IPO。Alphabet則另計畫在今年下半年單獨募集850億美元，用於AI投資。

  此次發行為這家公司走出困境畫下句點——SK 海力士的前身，是在南韓債權人主導的紓困計畫下，將LG半導體與現代電子兩家公司合併，歷經多年記憶體產業的繁榮與衰退循環之後，才得以浴火重生。SK 海力士的首爾掛牌市值今年已翻逾三倍。據彭博計算，其首爾掛牌股票今年迄今已在逾50個交易日出現至少5%的單日漲跌，相比之下，去年全年約有37個此類交易日，前三年合計則約44個。

  集團董事長崔泰源（Chey Tae-won）在彭博電視專訪中暗示，若報酬率足夠強勁、足以維繫投資人需求，公司可能進一步在美增發股票。「我們首先要做的是維持股價穩定，然後希望從長遠來看，能夠創造上漲空間，」崔泰源說。

  SK 海力士與三星電子也計畫透過南韓政府主導、規模至少達8,800億美元的計畫，投資南韓境內的相關項目。

  總部位於東京的鎧俠控股（Kioxia Holdings）——其在日本掛牌的股票過去一年漲幅逾2,900%——也正籌備赴美上市，這一動向顯示，SK 海力士的ADR架構或將吸引更多亞洲晶片製造商進軍美國市場。
