body_en: |
  Nvidia shares fell 6.4% on Monday while the Nasdaq Composite gained 1.8%, after the chipmaker's board opened an investigation into Chief Executive Jensen Huang over suspected involvement in foreign intelligence activities. The sharp divergence from the broader market reflected Wall Street's reassessment of risks tied to the probe, with analysts citing three main concerns: management distraction, the prospect of tighter chip export controls, and leadership uncertainty weighing on the stock.

  ## Megaspeed connection and Asia sales scrutiny

  The investigation involves senior executives at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based computing capacity company. Analyst Goldberg said he expects Nvidia's Southeast Asia sales to face a new round of strict scrutiny as a result of the case.

  Goldberg added that if restrictions on the China market are tightened further, the impact could extend beyond chip revenue. Chinese artificial intelligence researchers could be pushed toward domestic alternatives, Goldberg said, which would weaken Nvidia's CUDA software ecosystem.

  ## Analyst views on severity

  Bernstein analyst Stacy Rasgon said he is skeptical of claims that Huang was influenced by intelligence operatives and lobbied on China's behalf. Even so, Rasgon said the reports would at minimum severely distract the company, and could hand both Democratic and Republican lawmakers additional grounds to push for stricter data center regulation.

  Rasgon said that if Huang were removed, China could paradoxically benefit from the outcome. He nonetheless called the allegations negative for Nvidia and said the resulting concerns are likely to persist for some time.

  Gary Black, founder of The Future Fund, expressed greater doubt about Huang's ability to remain in his role. Writing on the social platform X, Black said he doubts Huang can continue as chief executive. Black said he does not expect the company's long-term value to be permanently damaged, but that succession uncertainty could prompt many fund managers to reduce their Nvidia holdings over the coming weeks.

  ## Governance and export compliance

  A Citigroup analyst team led by Atif Malik identified three focal areas for investors: corporate governance, regulatory and export-control compliance, and long-term leadership stability. The team focused in particular on compliance questions related to the flow of Nvidia's H200 chip to China.

  As of the report's filing, Huang had not announced any departure from the role, and Nvidia said he would continue to lead the company. Multiple analysts said the investigation's uncertainty is unlikely to clear from the stock price in the near term, even as views on the ultimate extent of the damage diverged across Wall Street.

  ## 超速公司連結與亞洲業務受審

  此次調查涉及總部位於新加坡、從事運算資源業務的超速公司（Megaspeed）旗下數名高階主管。分析師高柏格（Goldberg）表示，他預期輝達在東南亞的銷售業務，將因本案而面臨新一輪嚴格審查。

  ## 分析師對嚴重程度看法分歧

  伯恩斯坦（Bernstein）分析師史黛西．拉斯岡（Stacy Rasgon）表示，他對黃仁勳受情報人員影響、並為中國遊說一事抱持懷疑態度。儘管如此，拉斯岡仍表示，相關報導至少會嚴重分散公司注意力，更可能給民主黨與共和黨議員提供額外的理由，推動對資料中心實施更嚴格的監管。

  拉斯岡指出，若黃仁勳遭到撤換，中國反而可能從中受益，這一結果頗為弔詭。儘管如此，他仍將此次指控定性為對輝達不利，並表示由此引發的疑慮短期內難以消散。

  未來基金（The Future Fund）創辦人蓋瑞．布萊克（Gary Black）則對黃仁勳能否留任執行長一職表達了更深的懷疑。他在社群平台X上發文表示，他不認為黃仁勳能夠繼續擔任執行長。布萊克說，他並不預期公司的長期價值會受到永久性損傷，但接班人選的不確定性，可能促使許多基金經理人在未來數週內陸續減持輝達持股。

  ## 公司治理與出口法規遵循

  花旗集團（Citigroup）由阿提夫．馬利克（Atif Malik）領銜的分析師團隊，為投資人點出三大關注焦點：公司治理、法規與出口管制遵循，以及長期領導層的穩定性。該團隊特別聚焦於輝達H200晶片流向中國的法規遵循問題。

  截至報告發布時，黃仁勳尚未宣布離職，輝達方面也表示他將繼續主導公司營運。多名分析師表示，即使華爾街對最終損害程度看法不一，調查所帶來的不確定性，短期內仍難以從股價中消除。
