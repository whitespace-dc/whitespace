body_en: |
  NEXTDC CEO Craig Scroggie will forfeit his fiscal 2027 bonus opportunities after the Australian data centre operator last week cut its revenue forecast and suspended future project development, the company said on Monday.

  The board has withdrawn the resolution that would have sought shareholder approval to grant Scroggie performance rights and restricted rights under the company's fiscal 2027 long-term incentive plan, NEXTDC said. Long-term incentive plans typically tie executive pay to multi-year performance targets such as earnings growth or share price returns.

  Non-executive directors are also taking a financial hit. The board separately withdrew a resolution that would have increased the maximum aggregate annual remuneration for non-executive directors by A$300,000 (approximately US$210,660 at current exchange rates). Incumbent non-executive directors have additionally agreed to an immediate voluntary 10% reduction in their current fees, the company said.

  ## Melbourne project delay and revenue deferral

  The governance moves follow a disclosure last week in which NEXTDC said delays to electricity network upgrades at one of its major Melbourne projects would cause approximately A$140 million in contracted customer revenue to be deferred out of the current financial year. The company did not specify in that announcement when the deferred revenue is expected to be recognised.

  Electricity network upgrades are a critical dependency for large data centres, which require substantial grid connections to power and cool their facilities. A delay in network readiness can prevent a facility from accepting customers even if the building itself is complete.

  ## Board changes

  Current chairman Douglas Flynn has reaffirmed his intention to retire from the board once a successor is appointed and a short handover period is completed. NEXTDC said the board expects to appoint two new non-executive directors during the first calendar quarter of 2027.

  The company is scheduled to hold its annual general meeting on November 20.

  ## Significance

  The combination of executive pay forfeitures, director fee cuts, and withdrawn remuneration resolutions signals that NEXTDC's board is moving to align leadership accountability with the financial impact on shareholders from the forecast reduction and project pause. The A$140 million revenue deferral and development freeze at a major Melbourne site illustrate the exposure data centre operators face when grid infrastructure timelines slip, a risk that has become more prominent across the sector as power demand from new facilities outpaces utility upgrade schedules.

  NEXTDC's board expects to have two new non-executive directors in place and a new chairman appointed by the end of the first calendar quarter of 2027.

  ## 墨爾本工程延誤與營收遞延

  上述治理層面的調整，源於公司上週披露的一則消息：NEXTDC旗下一個主要墨爾本工程，因電網升級工程延誤，導致約1.4億澳元的合約客戶營收將從本財年遞延至未來期間。公司在當時的公告中並未說明這批遞延營收預計何時認列入帳。

  電網升級是大型資料中心不可或缺的關鍵依賴環節——這類設施需要龐大的電網接入容量，以維持設備運作並支撐冷卻系統。一旦電網準備工作出現延誤，即便建築本體已竣工，設施仍可能無法接納任何客戶進駐。

  ## 董事會異動

  現任董事長Douglas Flynn已重申退出董事會的意向，待繼任人選確定並完成短暫交接後將正式卸任。NEXTDC表示，董事會預計於2027年第一季日曆年度內任命兩名新任非執行董事。

  公司訂於11月20日召開年度股東大會。
