body_en: |
  The Philippine Board of Investments (BoI) is targeting P4.5 trillion in investment pledges over the next two years under an updated Strategic Investment Priority Plan (SIPP), a government framework that sets which industries qualify for fiscal incentives, with artificial intelligence (AI), digital infrastructure, and renewable energy (RE) named as headline priorities.

  The P4.5 trillion target represents a 33% increase over the P3.38 trillion in approved investments recorded under the previous 2022–2025 SIPP, according to Trade Undersecretary and BoI Managing Head Ceferino S. Rodolfo.

  ## The new priority sectors

  The 2026 SIPP, signed in May, expands the list of industries eligible for incentives under Republic Act No. 12066, known as the CREATE MORE Act, which governs tax and fiscal perks for registered enterprises. Priority sectors named by Rodolfo include mining and mineral processing; digital infrastructure such as data centers, fiber optic networks, and submarine cables; advanced manufacturing including components for AI data centers; and tourism.

  The plan organizes eligible activities into three tiers. Tier I covers modern agriculture, state-of-the-art construction, mobile healthcare, ecological zones, and climate-related projects including carbon capture, waste-to-value, circular economy initiatives, and forest management for carbon credits. Tier II includes defense services, desalination, electric vehicle infrastructure, sustainable aviation fuel, and critical mineral processing. Tier III — the highest tier, typically carrying the strongest incentive packages — includes AI, quantum computing, cybersecurity, hydrogen and nuclear energy, and advanced research and design.

  ## BoI's role and IPA structure

  Rodolfo said the BoI's own focus within the new SIPP will be on large-scale strategic investments, particularly in RE infrastructure. Other investment promotion agencies (IPAs) — government bodies authorized to register and incentivize investors in specific zones or sectors — will cover advanced manufacturing and export-oriented projects, he said.

  A project that aligns with the SIPP may apply for registration with the relevant IPA to access the available incentives.

  ## New Clark City and the Pax Silica initiative

  The new SIPP is expected to simplify the incentives regime for investors locating in New Clark City in Tarlac province, according to Erwin Kenneth R. Peralta, vice-president of the Bases Conversion and Development Authority's Investment Promotions and Marketing Department. Peralta said the tier system would address RE investment needs in New Clark City as well as AI, data center, and semiconductor manufacturing requirements.

  The Philippines is positioning New Clark City as a hub for AI and semiconductor manufacturing, including through a planned 1,618-hectare AI-native development under the US-led Pax Silica initiative, the BusinessWorld report noted.

  ## Luzon Economic Corridor alignment

  Rodolfo said the updated SIPP also aligns with the Luzon Economic Corridor (LEC), an 11-country investment partnership launched in 2024 by the Philippines, the United States, and Japan. The LEC targets investment in logistics, logistics infrastructure, and advanced manufacturing in key areas including Metro Manila, Batangas, Subic, and Clark.

  The BoI launched a nationwide SIPP roadshow on July 10, beginning with its Luzon leg. Roadshows for investors in the Visayas and Mindanao regions are scheduled for coming weeks. Rodolfo also said the agency is considering aligning the SIPP with the six-year Philippine Development Plan.

  ## What the plan means for investors

  Jonathan L. Ravelas, senior adviser at Reyes Tacandong & Co., said the 2026 SIPP shifts the country's investment strategy from volume to value. "The new SIPP shifts the country's focus from simply attracting more investments to attracting higher-value investments in areas such as AI, digital infrastructure, renewable energy, advanced manufacturing, and other frontier technologies where global capital is increasingly moving," Ravelas said.

  However, Ravelas cautioned that incentives alone are insufficient. The government must also accelerate regulatory approvals, reduce the cost of doing business, upskill local talent, and maintain policy consistency to sustain investor confidence, he said. "Incentives may open the door, but ease of doing business, infrastructure, human capital, and execution will determine whether investors choose to stay and expand in the Philippines," he added.

  The BoI will continue its regional roadshow series with Visayas and Mindanao legs in the weeks ahead.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓投資委員會（BoI）在更新版《戰略投資優先計畫》（SIPP）架構下，目標在未來兩年內吸引4.5兆披索的投資承諾。這套由政府制定的框架明確規範哪些產業可享有財政獎勵措施，而人工智慧（AI）、數位基礎設施及再生能源（RE）被列為首要重點項目。

  貿易部次長暨投資委員會執行長塞費里諾·羅多爾福（Ceferino S. Rodolfo）表示，4.5兆披索的目標，較前次2022至2025年SIPP期間所核准的3.38兆披索投資額高出33%。

  ## 新優先產業

  2026年版SIPP於今年5月簽署，擴大了依據《共和國法第12066號》（Republic Act No. 12066，即「CREATE MORE法案」）可享有獎勵措施的產業清單；該法案規範了已登記企業的稅務與財政優惠。羅多爾福點名的優先產業包括：採礦與礦物加工；資料中心、光纖網路及海底電纜等數位基礎設施；含AI資料中心零組件在內的先進製造業；以及觀光旅遊業。

  這套計畫將可申請的活動分為三個層級。第一層級涵蓋現代農業、先進建設、行動醫療、生態區，以及與氣候相關的項目，包括碳捕捉、廢棄物轉化利用、循環經濟倡議，及用於取得碳權的森林管理。第二層級包含國防服務、海水淡化、電動車基礎設施、永續航空燃料及關鍵礦物加工。第三層級為最高等級，通常提供最優厚的獎勵方案，涵蓋AI、量子運算、資安、氫能與核能，以及先進研究與設計。

  ## 投資委員會的定位與投資促進機構架構

  羅多爾福表示，投資委員會在新SIPP架構下的工作重心，將放在大規模策略性投資，尤其是再生能源基礎設施。至於先進製造業及以出口為導向的項目，則將由其他投資促進機構（IPA）——即經政府授權、負責在特定區域或產業登記並提供獎勵給投資人的機關——負責推動。

  符合SIPP條件的計畫，可向相關投資促進機構申請登記，以取得相應的獎勵措施。

  ## 新克拉克市與Pax Silica計畫

  依據《商業世界》（BusinessWorld）的報導，新版SIPP預計將為選擇進駐塔拉克省（Tarlac）新克拉克市的投資人，簡化現行的獎勵制度。基地轉換暨開發局（BCDA）投資促進暨行銷部副總裁厄文·肯尼斯·培羅塔（Erwin Kenneth R. Peralta）表示，層級制度將能滿足新克拉克市在再生能源方面的投資需求，同時也涵蓋AI、資料中心及半導體製造等領域的需求。

  菲律賓正積極將新克拉克市打造為AI與半導體製造的核心基地，其中包括一項由美國主導的Pax Silica計畫，規劃面積達1,618公頃的AI原生開發園區。

  ## 呂宋經濟走廊的銜接

  羅多爾福表示，更新版SIPP亦與「呂宋經濟走廊」（LEC）相互接軌。呂宋經濟走廊是菲律賓、美國及日本於2024年聯合發起的11國投資夥伴關係，目標是在大馬尼拉、八打雁（Batangas）、蘇比克（Subic）及克拉克（Clark）等關鍵地區，推動物流、物流基礎設施及先進製造業的投資。

  投資委員會於7月10日展開全國SIPP巡迴說明活動，首站從呂宋區域率先登場。維薩亞斯（Visayas）及民答那峨（Mindanao）地區的投資人說明會，預計於未來數週內陸續舉行。羅多爾福亦透露，該機構正考慮將SIPP與為期六年的菲律賓發展計畫相互對接。

  ## 計畫對投資人的意義

  瑞耶斯塔坎東公司（Reyes Tacandong & Co.）資深顧問強納森·拉韋拉斯（Jonathan L. Ravelas）表示，2026年版SIPP標誌著菲律賓投資策略從「重量」轉向「重質」。他說：「新版SIPP讓菲律賓的重心，從單純吸引更多投資，轉向吸引更高價值的投資，聚焦於AI、數位基礎設施、再生能源、先進製造業及其他全球資本正加速匯聚的前沿科技領域。」

  然而，拉韋拉斯也警示，獎勵措施本身並不足夠。政府必須同步加快法規審批速度、降低營商成本、提升本地人才技能，並維持政策一貫性，才能持續鞏固投資人信心。他進一步補充：「獎勵措施或許能打開大門，但最終能否讓投資人選擇留下來、並在菲律賓擴大布局，將取決於營商便利度、基礎設施水準、人力資本，以及實際的執行力。」

  投資委員會將持續推進全國巡迴說明活動，後續的維薩亞斯及民答那峨場次，將於未來數週內陸續登場。
