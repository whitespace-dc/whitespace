body_en: |
  Qualcomm has agreed to acquire Modular, an AI software company that built a platform allowing developers to run AI models across different chip types without rewriting code for each, as the semiconductor company seeks to strengthen its software layer across edge and data centre AI deployments.

  Modular's stack supports central processing units (CPUs), graphics processing units (GPUs), neural processing units (NPUs), and custom application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs). Qualcomm said that hardware-portable approach would underpin a broader software layer spanning devices, edge systems, and data centres.

  No financial terms were disclosed. The transaction is expected to close in the second half of 2026, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals.

  ## What Modular brings to Qualcomm

  Modular was founded by engineers involved in building parts of today's AI infrastructure, according to the sources. Its platform is designed around a vendor-neutral philosophy: developers build once and deploy across different hardware environments, reducing the separate optimisation work each new chip type would otherwise require.

  Qualcomm said the acquisition would deepen the software side of its data centre strategy, specifically in inference, orchestration, and deployment across distributed AI systems. Inference refers to the process of running a trained AI model to generate outputs, as distinct from the resource-intensive training phase.

  The deal also gives Qualcomm engineering talent from Modular alongside the platform itself. Qualcomm said it intends to use that combination to make its hardware more accessible to developers earlier in deployment cycles and to support systems that mix chips and accelerators from more than one vendor.

  ## Strategic context

  Qualcomm has long been associated with mobile and connected-device chips, but has been widening its AI strategy to cover on-device systems, edge computing, and, more recently, data centre workloads. The Modular acquisition signals a stronger emphasis on software as a complement to that hardware expansion.

  The move reflects a broader pattern in the chip industry. As AI workloads spread beyond centralised cloud systems to edge devices and distributed infrastructure, software that can move efficiently across varied hardware has become a competitive asset. Qualcomm said portability of the kind Modular provides could lower total cost of ownership for customers deploying AI across mixed environments.

  The market for AI infrastructure is increasingly shaped by efficiency and deployment costs rather than raw model size alone, Qualcomm argued, meaning that developers need software capable of translating chip-level performance into practical services across a range of hardware and operating environments.

  ## What executives said

  Cristiano Amon, President and Chief Executive Officer of Qualcomm, framed the deal as a response to a structural shift in how AI computing is architected. "As agentic AI scales across data centers and edge environments, the industry is moving toward disaggregated, multi-vendor architectures that demand a more open and modern software foundation," Amon said. "We believe the future belongs to developer-friendly, horizontal platforms that can run across diverse compute environments and give customers real choice in how and where they deploy AI."

  Agentic AI refers to AI systems that operate autonomously to complete multi-step tasks, as distinct from models that respond to single prompts.

  Chris Lattner, Co-founder and Chief Executive Officer of Modular, said the transaction would extend the company's core mission. "Modular was founded on the belief that AI needs a more open and efficient software foundation that can span diverse hardware and deployment environments," Lattner said. "Joining Qualcomm gives us the scale and platform reach to accelerate that mission."

  ## Industry significance

  The acquisition positions Qualcomm on the portability side of a live debate in the AI market: whether customers should tie their software stacks to a single hardware supplier or use tools that can shift workloads between different systems. As competition grows in both edge AI and data centre inference, vendor-neutral software has become a differentiator for hardware companies seeking developer adoption.

  For Qualcomm specifically, buying Modular adds a software asset that spans several layers of AI deployment, from model execution to workload management across mixed hardware. It also strengthens the company's standing with developers and enterprise customers who are deploying AI systems across both devices and larger-scale infrastructure.

  The transaction is expected to close in the second half of 2026, pending regulatory approvals.

  ## 高通宣布收購AI軟體公司Modular　強化邊緣到資料中心部署

  高通宣布收購AI軟體公司Modular。Modular所建構的平台，讓開發者無需針對每種晶片重新撰寫程式碼，即可跨不同硬體架構執行AI模型。這家半導體業者此舉意在強化其橫跨邊緣運算與資料中心AI部署的軟體層。交易金額未予揭露，依慣例完成條件及主管機關審查通過後，預計於2026年下半年正式交割。

  Modular的技術堆疊支援中央處理器（CPU）、圖形處理器（GPU）、神經處理器（NPU）以及客製化特定應用積體電路（ASIC）。高通表示，這種可跨硬體移植的設計，將成為橫跨終端裝置、邊緣系統與資料中心的更廣泛軟體層基礎。

  ## Modular為高通帶來什麼

  據相關資訊，Modular由參與建構當今AI基礎架構的工程師共同創辦。其平台以廠商中立的理念為核心設計：開發者只需建構一次，即可跨不同硬體環境部署，大幅降低每引入一種新晶片所需的個別最佳化工作。

  高通表示，此次收購將深化其資料中心策略的軟體面，具體而言涵蓋分散式AI系統的推論、協作調度以及部署。所謂推論，係指執行已訓練完成的AI模型以產生輸出結果的過程，有別於資源耗用密集的訓練階段。

  這項交易同時為高通取得Modular的工程人才與平台本身。高通表示，將結合兩者，讓自家硬體在部署週期更早期便能被開發者所用，並支援混合使用多家廠商晶片與加速器的系統。

  ## 策略背景

  高通長期以行動與連網裝置晶片為人所知，但近年來已持續擴大AI策略版圖，涵蓋裝置端系統、邊緣運算，以及更近期的資料中心工作負載。收購Modular一事，標誌著高通在硬體擴張之外，更加重視軟體作為互補的戰略方向。

  此舉反映了晶片產業的整體趨勢。隨著AI工作負載從集中式雲端系統向邊緣裝置與分散式基礎架構擴散，能夠在不同硬體間高效移轉的軟體，已成為重要的競爭資產。高通表示，Modular所提供的可移植性，有助於降低客戶在混合環境中部署AI的整體擁有成本。

  高通主張，AI基礎架構市場的競爭格局，日益取決於效率與部署成本，而非單純的模型規模大小，這意味著開發者需要能夠將晶片層級效能轉化為實際服務、並可在多種硬體與作業環境中運行的軟體。

  ## 高層主管表示

  高通總裁兼執行長克里斯蒂亞諾·亞蒙（Cristiano Amon）將此次交易定位為回應AI運算架構結構性轉變的舉措。他表示：「隨著代理式AI跨資料中心與邊緣環境擴展規模，產業正朝向去中心化、多供應商架構演進，這需要更開放、更現代的軟體基礎。我們相信，未來屬於對開發者友善、可跨多元運算環境執行的水平式平台，讓客戶真正能夠自主選擇AI的部署方式與場域。」所謂代理式AI，係指能夠自主完成多步驟任務的AI系統，有別於僅回應單一提示的模型。

  Modular共同創辦人兼執行長克里斯·拉特納（Chris Lattner）表示，此次交易將延伸公司的核心使命。他說：「Modular創立的初衷，在於相信AI需要更開放、更高效的軟體基礎，使其得以橫跨多元硬體與部署環境。加入高通，讓我們能夠獲得加速實踐這項使命所需的規模與平台觸及。」

  ## 產業意義

  此次收購讓高通在AI市場一場進行中的論戰中，站穩可移植性的一方——這場論戰的核心，在於客戶究竟應將軟體堆疊綁定於單一硬體供應商，抑或採用能在不同系統間靈活移轉工作負載的工具。隨著邊緣AI與資料中心推論領域的競爭日趨激烈，廠商中立的軟體已成為硬體業者爭取開發者採用的差異化利器。

  就高通自身而言，收購Modular補充了一項橫跨多層AI部署的軟體資產，從模型執行到跨混合硬體的工作負載管理皆涵蓋在內。此舉同時強化了高通在開發者社群及企業客戶中的地位，這些客戶正將AI系統部署於終端裝置與大規模基礎架構之上。

  本次交易待主管機關審查通過後，預計於2026年下半年完成交割。
