body_en: |
  Kenanga Investment Bank declared a second wave of data center development underway in Malaysia on Wednesday, projecting 17 to 21 new facilities in the pipeline across three states and up to four project roll-outs before year's end, even as total construction contract awards fell year-on-year in the first half of 2026.

  The research house published the assessment in a construction sector note, maintaining an "overweight" rating — indicating it expects the sector to outperform the broader market — citing "structurally underpinned" demand from persistent data center requirements and sustained capital expenditure commitments from global technology companies through 2026.

  ## Pipeline and locations

  New mega-campuses planned for Negeri Sembilan, Selangor, and Johor form the core of the projected pipeline of 17 to 21 structures, Kenanga said. The research house anticipates as many as four project roll-outs in the months remaining in 2026.

  Kenanga said data center projects are expected to "remain the primary growth driver for the sector in 2026," with a development cycle it projects extending through 2030.

  ## Power utility agreements

  Tenaga Nasional Bhd (TNB), Malaysia's national electricity utility, signed energy supply agreements for three additional data centers in the first quarter of 2026, bringing the cumulative total to 59 projects, of which 36 have already been completed. Energy supply agreements represent a formal commitment by TNB to provide power to a facility and are closely tracked as a leading indicator of committed data center construction.

  ## Awards slowdown

  Total construction contract awards in Malaysia reached RM108 billion (Malaysian ringgit) in the first six months of 2026, down from RM125 billion in the same period in 2025, according to the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB). Commercial and industrial projects — a category that includes data centers — accounted for more than two-thirds of first-half awards by value, while infrastructure represented just 12%.

  Kenanga said matching the 2025 pace "remains a tall order," but still projected RM180 billion worth of construction jobs across the full year, supported by continued public-sector project releases and sustained private-sector momentum in data centers.

  ## Significance

  The shift in the composition of awards — with infrastructure falling to 12% of first-half value and commercial and industrial work taking the majority — illustrates how private data center investment has become the dominant engine of Malaysian construction activity. Kenanga's development timeline extending to 2030 suggests the sector anticipates a sustained multi-year build cycle rather than a near-term peak.

  For investor exposure to data center activity and the broader project pipeline, Kenanga named three large-cap contractors: Gamuda Bhd, Sunway Construction Group Bhd, and IJM Corporation Bhd.

  ## 肯納加宣告馬來西亞資料中心第二波浪潮　上半年建案得標額年減

  肯納加投資銀行（Kenanga Investment Bank）週三宣告，馬來西亞資料中心第二波發展浪潮已然成形，預估三州合計將有17至21座新設施進入開發管線，今年底前可望有多達四項專案陸續啟動——即便馬來西亞2026年上半年整體建案得標金額較去年同期出現下滑。

  這家研究機構在一份建築類股報告中發布上述評估，維持「增持」評等——意即預期該類股表現將優於大盤——理由是全球科技企業對資料中心的需求持續存在，且對2026年資本支出的承諾未曾鬆動，整體需求具備「結構性支撐」。

  ## 管線與地點

  肯納加表示，規劃中的17至21座新設施，核心為預定在森美蘭州、雪蘭莪州及柔佛州興建的大型園區。該研究機構預期，2026年剩餘幾個月內將有多達四項專案正式啟動。

  肯納加指出，資料中心專案預計「仍將是2026年帶動類股成長的主要引擎」，並預測此波開發周期將延續至2030年。

  ## 電力供應協議

  馬來西亞國家電力公司（Tenaga Nasional Bhd，TNB）在2026年第一季新簽三份資料中心電力供應協議，累計協議總數達59項，其中36項已竣工。電力供應協議代表TNB向設施正式承諾供電，是追蹤資料中心確定性建設進度的重要領先指標。

  ## 得標額放緩

  根據馬來西亞建設業發展局（Construction Industry Development Board，CIDB）數據，2026年上半年全國建案得標金額達1,080億馬幣，低於2025年同期的1,250億馬幣。商業與工業類專案——涵蓋資料中心——按金額計算佔上半年得標總額逾三分之二，基礎建設則僅佔12%。

  肯納加坦言，要追平2025年的步伐「難度不小」，但仍預測全年建案得標總額可達1,800億馬幣，並指出持續釋出的公共部門專案以及私部門資料中心的穩健動能將提供支撐。

  ## 意義與展望

  得標組成結構的轉變——基礎建設佔上半年金額比重降至12%，商業與工業類則取得壓倒性份額——清楚呈現出私人資料中心投資已成為馬來西亞建築業活動的主要引擎。肯納加將開發時程延伸至2030年，意味著業界預期這將是一個長達數年的持續建設周期，而非短期高峰後的迅速退潮。

  就投資人布局資料中心活動及整體專案管線而言，肯納加點名三家大型承包商：云頂建設（Gamuda Bhd）、陽光建設集團（Sunway Construction Group Bhd），以及IJM機構（IJM Corporation Bhd）。
