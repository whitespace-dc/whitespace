body_en: |
  PT Telkom Indonesia convened regulators, strategic state-owned enterprises, enterprise clients, and eight global technology partners on June 3, 2026 to translate a national digital sovereignty declaration into a structured implementation blueprint organized around three pillars: sovereign cloud, artificial intelligence, and cybersecurity.

  The National Digital Sovereignty Forum, held at Telkom Landmark Tower in Jakarta under the title "Digital Ecosystem Collaboration for Sovereign National Innovation," was the second event in a two-part sequence. The first, a declaration titled "Awakening of Indonesia's Technology Sovereignty," took place on May 20, 2026 and established a shared vision. The June 3 forum was designed to convert that vision into a measurable blueprint and roadmap with defined implementation steps, Telkom said in a press release.

  Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs (Kemenkomdigi) participated in the forum. Telkom said that attendance affirmed the importance of regulator-industry alignment in building a national digital foundation capable of supporting economic growth and protecting Indonesia's strategic interests in the digital era.

  ## Three-pillar framework

  The forum structured its work around Sovereign Cloud, Sovereign AI, and Sovereign Cybersecurity. Telkom described the three as the framework needed to ensure Indonesia develops domestic computing capacity, AI capability, and digital security systems that can be sustained and expanded inside the country.

  Telkom used the forum to advance AdyaCakra, a sovereign digital stack the company described as integrating national capabilities with leading global technology to serve government, state-owned enterprises, and strategic industrial sectors.

  AdyaCakra's cloud layer draws on NeuCentrIX, NeutraDC Hyperscale, and edge infrastructure. Its AI layer includes AI Hub and Agentic AI BigBox. Its cybersecurity layer comprises Telkom CSOC (Cyber Security Operations Center), sovereign threat intelligence, and managed detection and response services.

  ## Statements from regulators and executives

  Sonny Hendra Sudaryana, Director of Digital Ecosystem Development at Kemenkomdigi, said synergy among regulators, industry, and technology providers is essential to ensure national digital transformation proceeds safely, inclusively, and sustainably. "Strengthening sovereign cloud, AI, and cybersecurity is an important step in safeguarding national data security, increasing trust in Indonesia's digital ecosystem, and ensuring national digital transformation can proceed safely, inclusively, and sustainably," Sonny said, as quoted in Telkom's press release.

  Seno Soemadji, Telkom's Director of Strategic Business Development and Portfolio, framed digital sovereignty as a matter of national competitiveness rather than a purely technical question. "Digital sovereignty is no longer merely a technology issue, but the foundation of the nation's competitiveness, resilience, and sustainability in the future. Indonesia must be able to build control over its own data, AI, cloud, and cybersecurity so that it is not only a digital market, but also the architect of its own digital future," Seno said.

  Seno connected the initiative to Telkom's TLKM 30 transformation program, under which the company said it is reinforcing its role as a connectivity provider and service enabler for a more resilient, secure, and independent national digital foundation.

  ## Eight global partners sign collaboration agreements

  Eight global technology partners signed "intentional collaboration" agreements at the forum. Telkom said the agreements are intended to accelerate national capability development, knowledge transfer, and technology adoption that generates added value for Indonesia. The source did not identify the eight partners by name.

  ## What it means

  The forum gives Indonesia's dominant state-owned telecommunications carrier a defined commercial and technical architecture for domestic digital infrastructure, with government backing signaled by ministerial attendance. Moving from the May declaration to a June blueprint with signed partner agreements represents a concrete step toward operationalizing the sovereignty agenda rather than leaving it at the level of policy aspiration.

  Telkom said the National Digital Sovereignty Forum and the AdyaCakra stack are intended to support Indonesia Digital Vision 2030, the government's long-term digital development target, as well as the company's own TLKM 30 transformation agenda.
body_zh: |
  PT印尼電信（Telkom Indonesia）於2026年6月3日召集監管機關、策略性國營企業、企業客戶及八家全球科技夥伴，將國家數位主權宣言轉化為一套以三大支柱為核心的結構化實施藍圖，三大支柱分別為：主權雲端、人工智慧，以及資安。

  這場名為「數位生態系統協作，驅動國家主權創新」的「國家數位主權論壇」，於雅加達Telkom Landmark Tower舉行，是前後兩場系列活動的第二場。第一場活動以「印尼科技主權的覺醒」為題，於2026年5月20日宣示共同願景；6月3日的論壇則旨在將願景轉化為可衡量的藍圖與路線圖，並訂定明確的實施步驟，Telkom在一份新聞稿中表示。

  印尼通訊與數位事務部（Kemenkomdigi）亦出席了此次論壇。Telkom表示，主管機關的蒞臨確認了監管單位與產業界攜手合作、共同建構國家數位基礎的重要性，此一基礎須能支撐經濟成長，並在數位時代捍衛印尼的戰略利益。

  ## 三大支柱架構

  論壇圍繞主權雲端、主權AI及主權資安三大主軸推展工作。Telkom表示，這三項支柱是確保印尼在國內建立並持續擴展本土運算能量、AI能力及數位安全體系的必要框架。

  Telkom藉此論壇深化推進AdyaCakra——一套Telkom描述為整合本國能量與全球頂尖科技、服務政府、國營企業及戰略性工業部門的主權數位整合堆疊。

  AdyaCakra的雲端層涵蓋NeuCentrIX、NeutraDC超大規模資料中心及邊緣基礎建設；AI層則包含AI Hub與Agentic AI BigBox；資安層由Telkom CSOC（網路安全營運中心）、主權威脅情資及託管式偵測與回應服務共同組成。

  ## 監管機關與企業主管發言

  通訊與數位事務部數位生態系統發展司司長Sonny Hendra Sudaryana表示，監管機關、產業界與科技供應商之間的協同合作，是確保國家數位轉型安全、包容且永續推進的關鍵。「強化主權雲端、AI與資安，是保障國家資料安全、提升印尼數位生態系統信任度，以及確保國家數位轉型能夠安全、包容、永續發展的重要步驟，」Sonny在Telkom的新聞稿中如此表示。

  Telkom策略業務發展暨投資組合董事Seno Soemadji將數位主權定位為國家競爭力議題，而非單純的技術問題。「數位主權不再只是科技議題，而是國家未來競爭力、韌性與永續性的根基。印尼必須能夠掌控自身的資料、AI、雲端與資安，如此才不只是數位市場的消費者，更能成為自身數位未來的建構者，」Seno說。

  Seno並將此一倡議連結至Telkom的TLKM 30轉型計畫。Telkom表示，該計畫旨在強化其作為連接服務提供者及服務賦能者的角色，以建立更具韌性、更安全、更自主的國家數位基礎。

  ## 八家全球夥伴簽署合作協議

  八家全球科技夥伴在論壇上簽署了「意向合作」協議。Telkom表示，相關協議旨在加速本國能力建設、知識移轉及科技導入，進而為印尼創造附加價值。消息來源未揭露八家夥伴的具體名稱。

  ## 意義與展望

  此次論壇使印尼最具主導地位的國營電信業者，在政府部會出席背書下，擁有了一套明確的商業與技術架構，以推進本土數位基礎建設。從5月的願景宣言，到6月完成藍圖制定並簽署夥伴協議，代表印尼正以具體行動推動主權議程落地，而非停留在政策層面的空泛宣示。

  Telkom表示，「國家數位主權論壇」及AdyaCakra整合堆疊，旨在支持政府的長期數位發展目標「印尼數位願景2030」，同時也呼應公司自身的TLKM 30轉型計畫。
