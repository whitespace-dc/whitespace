body_en: |
  Digital Realty will pay Blackstone $3.5 billion to acquire the asset manager's stakes in three Northern Virginia data centers, the companies said Monday, expanding the real estate investment trust's holdings in the world's largest data center market.

  The deal, structured as $1.2 billion in cash and $2.3 billion in Digital Realty shares, was expected to close on Tuesday.

  ## What Digital Realty is buying

  Under the terms, Digital Realty will take on Blackstone's 80% interest in two 96-megawatt data centers in Manassas, Virginia, and a 50% interest in a 96-megawatt facility in Sterling, Virginia. A megawatt of data center capacity can power hundreds of server racks; 96 megawatts represents a large hyperscale facility. The total value of the three assets, including debt and planned capital expenditures, is $7.8 billion, the companies said.

  Two of the facilities are expected to stabilize — meaning reach full occupancy and begin generating contracted rent — in the first half of 2027, with the third following in the first half of 2028.

  ## Financial terms and outlook

  Digital Realty said the transaction is expected to be accretive to Core Funds From Operations (Core FFO) per share, the primary earnings metric for real estate investment trusts, in both 2027 and 2028 as development completes and rents commence. CFO Matt Mercier was quoted making that projection in the companies' announcement.

  Shares of Digital Realty fell 2.4% in extended trading following the announcement.

  The companies described all three facilities as fully leased hyperscale assets — data centers built to the specifications of large cloud and technology tenants.

  ## Existing partnership and broader joint ventures

  The transaction grows out of an existing joint venture between Digital Realty and Blackstone. Chief Investment Officer Greg Wright said the companies "have developed a strong partnership" through the ongoing development of these assets, and that the deal "reflects the next phase of that relationship."

  Digital Realty and Blackstone said they continue to operate joint ventures covering remaining data center investments in Northern Virginia as well as facilities in Paris and Frankfurt.

  ## Context: Northern Virginia demand

  Northern Virginia is described by the companies as the world's largest data center market. Demand for capacity there has surged as cloud computing and artificial intelligence workloads require more infrastructure, the companies said.

  With two of the three facilities scheduled to begin generating full rent revenue in 2027 and the third in 2028, the acquisition positions Digital Realty to benefit from that demand over the next two years as the assets move from development into stable operation.

  ## Digital Realty斥資35億美元購入黑石集團維吉尼亞州資料中心股權

  Digital Realty週一宣布，將以35億美元收購資產管理公司黑石集團（Blackstone）在北維吉尼亞州三座資料中心的持股，此舉進一步擴大了這家不動產投資信託公司（REIT）在全球最大資料中心市場的佈局。

  這筆交易採現金加股票結構，其中12億美元以現金支付，另23億美元以Digital Realty股票換股，預計週二正式完成交割。

  ## Digital Realty收購標的

  依據交易條款，Digital Realty將取得黑石集團在維吉尼亞州馬納薩斯（Manassas）兩座各96百萬瓦資料中心的80%股權，以及位於維吉尼亞州斯特靈（Sterling）一座96百萬瓦設施的50%股權。一百萬瓦的資料中心容量可供應數百個伺服器機架運作，96百萬瓦的規模代表的是大型超大規模（hyperscale）設施。兩家公司表示，三項資產的總價值（含負債及計畫資本支出）合計達78億美元。

  其中兩座設施預計於2027年上半年「穩定化」——即達到滿載使用率並開始產生合約租金收入；第三座設施則預計於2028年上半年步入穩定營運。

  ## 財務條款與前景展望

  Digital Realty表示，隨著開發工程完工、租金陸續入帳，本次交易預計將在2027年及2028年對「核心營運資金」（Core FFO）每股盈餘產生增益效果——Core FFO為不動產投資信託公司最主要的獲利衡量指標。上述預測引自公司財務長（CFO）麥特．墨西耶（Matt Mercier）在兩家公司聯合聲明中的發言。

  消息公布後，Digital Realty盤後股價下跌2.4%。

  兩家公司將上述三座設施定性為已全數租出的超大規模資產，即依照大型雲端及科技租戶規格量身打造的資料中心。

  ## 既有合作關係與更廣泛的合資事業

  此次收購源自Digital Realty與黑石集團之間既有的合資架構。Digital Realty首席投資長（CIO）葛瑞格．萊特（Greg Wright）表示，兩家公司透過共同開發上述資產，「已建立起穩固的夥伴關係」，而此次交易「標誌著雙方合作邁入下一個階段」。

  Digital Realty與黑石集團表示，雙方仍持續維運涵蓋北維吉尼亞州其餘資料中心投資、以及法國巴黎與德國法蘭克福設施的合資事業。

  ## 背景：北維吉尼亞州的強勁需求

  兩家公司將北維吉尼亞州定位為全球規模最大的資料中心市場。由於雲端運算與人工智慧（AI）工作負載對基礎設施的需求急劇攀升，當地的資料中心容量需求已大幅增加。

  三座設施中，有兩座預定於2027年開始貢獻完整租金收入，第三座則於2028年跟進，此次收購部署讓Digital Realty得以在未來兩年內，隨著這些資產從開發階段轉入穩定營運，充分受惠於市場持續升溫的龐大需求。
