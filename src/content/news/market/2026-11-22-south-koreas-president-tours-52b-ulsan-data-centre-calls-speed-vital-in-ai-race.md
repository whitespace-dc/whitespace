body_en: |
  South Korean President Lee Jae Myung on Saturday toured a data centre under construction in Ulsan, the southeastern industrial port city, declaring urgency in the country's pursuit of artificial intelligence infrastructure as the facility's developers plan an eventual build-out of roughly one gigawatt of capacity.

  "The whole world is competing right now, and speed is vital," Lee said in a public video recorded during the visit.

  ## The project

  The Ulsan data centre is a joint development between SK Group, South Korea's second-largest conglomerate by assets, and Amazon Web Services (AWS), the cloud computing division of Amazon. South Korea's science ministry said last year that the estimated cost runs to 7 trillion won, equivalent to approximately $5.2 billion at current exchange rates, through 2029. Commercial operations are scheduled to begin in the second half of 2027.

  SK Group Chairman Chey Tae-won said in September that the facility would reach an initial capacity of 103 megawatts (MW) by 2029. For context, 103 MW is roughly enough power to serve tens of thousands of homes continuously, though data centres direct that electricity entirely to servers and cooling systems. Chey added that the group has plans to expand the site by nearly 900 MW beyond that initial phase, which would bring eventual total capacity close to one gigawatt — a scale comparable to the largest hyperscale campuses being developed globally. Chey also said SK Group is planning further partnerships with global technology companies as the project advances.

  ## Regional development and workforce

  President Lee said AI-related investment in South Korea has been concentrated largely around Seoul, and that the Ulsan project demonstrates regional growth is achievable. He expressed hope that the project's success would attract further investment from global AI companies.

  After being told that roughly 900 workers were employed at the construction site, Lee flagged a workforce constraint: advanced AI and data centre facilities face a shortage of qualified construction workers. He called on government ministries to examine how to train the workforce needed to build data centres and AI infrastructure at scale.

  ## Significance

  The presidential visit signals that data centre development has become a matter of national economic policy in South Korea, not simply a private-sector infrastructure decision. The Ulsan project, as one of the country's largest announced AI infrastructure investments, is being cited by the government as a model for distributing that development outside the capital region. The planned expansion to nearly one gigawatt, if completed, would position the site among the larger data centre campuses in Asia. Construction workforce capacity, Lee indicated, is now a policy constraint that ministries will need to address if the country is to meet its infrastructure ambitions.
body_zh: |
  南韓總統李在明週六視察了位於東南部工業港口城市蔚山的一座資料中心建設工地，宣示南韓在追求AI基礎設施的過程中必須保持高度緊迫感。開發商規劃最終將整體容量擴建至約1吉瓦（GW）。

  「全世界正在激烈競爭，速度至關重要，」李在明在視察期間拍攝的公開影片中如此表示。

  ## 計畫概要

  蔚山資料中心由韓國資產規模第二大的財閥SK集團，以及亞馬遜旗下雲端運算部門亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）聯合開發。南韓科學部去年公布，整體建設費用預計至2029年將達7兆韓元，依目前匯率換算約52億美元，商業營運則預計於2027年下半年正式啟動。

  SK集團會長崔泰源今年9月表示，園區初期容量預計於2029年達到103百萬瓦（MW）。就規模而言，103 MW大約足以持續供應數萬戶家庭的用電需求，但資料中心會將這些電力全數導入伺服器與冷卻系統。崔泰源進一步指出，集團規劃在初期之外再擴充近900 MW，屆時整體總容量將接近1 GW，可媲美全球正在開發中的最大型超大規模資料中心園區。他也透露，SK集團正積極洽談與更多全球科技大廠的合作計畫，以推進這項工程。

  ## 區域發展與人力資源

  李在明指出，南韓的AI相關投資長期高度集中於首爾周邊，而蔚山計畫的推進，正好證明區域均衡發展是可以實現的。他期望這項計畫的成功能吸引更多國際AI企業前來投資。

  在得知工地目前約有900名工人投入施工後，李在明點出人力資源的結構性瓶頸：先進AI設施與資料中心正面臨合格建築工人嚴重短缺的問題。他呼籲相關政府部會研議，如何有系統地培訓大規模建設資料中心與AI基礎設施所需的專業人才。

  ## 重要意義

  此次總統親赴視察，象徵資料中心建設在南韓已從單純的民間基礎設施決策，躍升為國家經濟政策的核心議題。蔚山計畫作為南韓迄今宣布規模最大的AI基礎設施投資案之一，已被政府援引為將相關發展拓展至首都圈以外地區的示範典範。若近1 GW的擴建計畫順利完工，蔚山園區將躋身亞洲規模最大的資料中心園區之列。李在明亦指出，建築勞動力的供給能力，如今已成為各部會必須積極因應的政策制約——唯有突破這道瓶頸，南韓才能實現其宏大的AI基礎設施建設目標。
