body_en: |
  Australian data centre operator NextDC said Monday it would stop work on all projects without customer contracts after power delays and community backlash halted its flagship M3 expansion in Melbourne — a setback that cut the company's earnings forecasts, sent its share price down 42%, and attracted a A$715 million (US$492 million) opportunistic stake purchase by two major rivals.

  The decision marks a significant strategic retreat for a company its 2026 annual report lists as having 11 centres either in development or in the planning stages.

  ## The Melbourne setback

  Work on the M3 data centre expansion — NextDC's flagship Melbourne facility — was suspended due to power supply delays and opposition from local residents. The halt forced the company to revise its earnings guidance downward last month, triggering the share price collapse.

  Chief executive Craig Scroggie, speaking at the UBS Australasia conference on Monday, said winning community support for M3 is now the company's top priority. "Anything that didn't have a customer contract and a customer commitment that we absolutely have to deliver on is now no longer a priority," he said. He did not specify how many of the 11 pipeline projects would be paused.

  Scroggie said resuming paused work would require demonstrating the problem has been solved and trust re-established. "That work can't restart until we clearly communicate that we've solved this problem, that we've earned the trust of everybody that's been supporting us, and then we will get back to work," he said.

  ## Rivals move in

  Following the share price decline, Goodman Group — an Australian property and infrastructure developer — and its New York-listed partner Digital Realty together acquired a 9.9% stake in NextDC last week for A$715 million. The two companies said they had no current plans to launch a takeover bid but reserved the right to do so.

  In a statement, Goodman and Digital Realty said they would "look forward to a potential collaboration with the NextDC board and management on the ways and forms in which the parties may help to address the challenges facing the company."

  Scroggie acknowledged the timing of the investment was not surprising. "When you have a challenge like we've had in the last couple of weeks, it's not really any surprise to me that people ... look at that and say, now might be the time," he said. NextDC's share price recovered its losses following the stake acquisition.

  ## Community resistance and AI backlash

  Scroggie framed the Melbourne opposition as part of a broader global trend. "That's unfortunately probably reflective of the global environment in relation to the engagement, social licence, people's feelings about artificial intelligence and data centres being the physical embodiment of that," he said.

  The remarks point to a growing challenge for data centre developers worldwide: large-scale facilities, often associated in public discourse with the energy demands of artificial intelligence workloads, are facing increased scrutiny from local communities over power consumption, land use, and neighbourhood impact.

  ## What it means

  The pause on uncontracted projects signals that NextDC is prioritising capital discipline and stakeholder management over growth velocity — a marked shift for what had been described as Australia's most prolific data centre developer. With Goodman and Digital Realty now holding a near-10% stake and explicitly reserving takeover rights, the company's ownership structure and strategic direction remain in flux.

  Scroggie said the company would not restart paused development work until it had demonstrably resolved the community and power issues surrounding M3.

  ## 墨爾本的挫敗

  NextDC在墨爾本的旗艦設施M3資料中心擴建工程，因電力供應延遲及當地居民反對而被迫暫停施工。工程停擺迫使公司上個月下修獲利展望，進而引發股價崩跌。

  執行長克雷格·斯克羅吉（Craig Scroggie）週一出席瑞銀澳大拉西亞投資會議時表示，贏得社區對M3案的支持，如今已成為公司最優先的任務。他說：「凡是沒有客戶合約、沒有客戶承諾、絕對必須如期交付的項目，現在全都不再是優先要務。」他未說明11個在手案子中，究竟有多少將被列入暫停名單。

  斯克羅吉指出，重啟暫停中的工程有其前提條件。「在我們清楚說明問題已獲解決、重新贏得所有支持者的信任之前，這些工程不會重啟，」他說，「做到這些，我們才會重返工地。」

  ## 競爭對手趁機布局

  股價重挫後，澳洲地產暨基礎建設開發商古德曼集團，以及其在紐約掛牌的合作夥伴Digital Realty，上週聯手以澳幣7億1,500萬元購入NextDC 9.9%的股份。兩家公司表示，目前並無發動收購要約的計畫，但保留日後行使此一權利的選擇。

  古德曼集團與Digital Realty在聲明中表示，「期待與NextDC董事會及管理層就雙方如何協助解決公司所面臨的挑戰，探討各種可能的合作方式與形式。」

  斯克羅吉坦言，此次投資的時機點並不令他意外。「當我們在過去幾週遭遇這樣的困境，不難想像會有人……看著這個情況，認為現在或許是進場的好時機，」他說。NextDC股價在入股消息公布後，已回補之前的跌幅。

  ## 社區阻力與人工智慧反彈

  斯克羅吉將墨爾本的反對聲浪定位為更廣泛全球趨勢的縮影。「這或許恰恰反映了當前全球在推動社會認同、民眾對人工智慧的觀感，以及將資料中心視為AI實體化身等議題上的整體氛圍，」他說。

  這番話點出了全球資料中心開發商日益棘手的共同挑戰：大型資料中心設施因在公眾論述中被與人工智慧工作負載的龐大能源需求畫上等號，正在各地社區面臨愈來愈嚴格的審視，涵蓋電力消耗、土地使用及對周邊社區的衝擊等面向。

  ## 後續影響

  暫停無合約開發案的決定，意味著NextDC將資本紀律與利害關係人管理置於成長速度之前——對於這家素來被視為澳洲最積極進取的資料中心開發商而言，此舉是一次明顯的策略轉向。隨著古德曼集團與Digital Realty合計持有近一成股份，並明確保留發動收購的選擇權，NextDC的股權結構與策略走向仍充滿變數。

  斯克羅吉表示，在明確解決M3案的社區爭議與電力問題之前，公司不會重啟任何暫停的開發工程。
