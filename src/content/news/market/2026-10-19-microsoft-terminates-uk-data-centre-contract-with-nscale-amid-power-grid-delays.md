body_en: |
  Microsoft has terminated its contract with British AI infrastructure provider Nscale for a planned data centre in Loughton, Essex, Britain's Telegraph newspaper reported Sunday. National power grid officials had told Nscale they did not expect to supply power to the site until the 2030s, the Telegraph reported. Microsoft declined to comment when contacted by Reuters.

  Microsoft was one of Nscale's two largest customers. The Telegraph did not specify a stated reason for Microsoft's withdrawal, but reported that Nscale had faced repeated delays with the project.

  ## Power supply constraint

  Nscale attributed the problem to a constraint affecting the wider London grid, not the Loughton site alone. "Our relationship with Microsoft remains strong and long-term, and this unfortunate London-wide grid issue has no impact on our other contracts," an Nscale spokesperson told Reuters.

  The company said it remains committed to the Loughton project and is "exploring alternative power sources for the site, in addition to other expansion sites in the UK."

  ## IPO timing

  The termination comes weeks after Nscale filed for an initial public offering (IPO) in the United States. The company's IPO prospectus listed Microsoft and AI company Anthropic as its expected major customers, according to the reports.

  Nscale provides infrastructure — compute capacity, power supply, and data centres — as well as software for training and operating artificial intelligence models. The company is backed by Nvidia and competes against AI infrastructure providers CoreWeave, Nebius, Crusoe, and Lambda.

  Nscale has grown from $100 million to more than $103 billion in total contracted value since its launch roughly two and a half years ago, according to Reuters.

  ## Significance

  The Loughton exit is a concrete example of how power grid availability in the London area is constraining data centre development in the UK. A customer of Microsoft's scale withdrawing from a specific site over a grid delay that may extend into the 2030s underscores the timeline risk that power infrastructure constraints pose to operators with active construction pipelines.

  Nscale's US IPO, in which Microsoft and Anthropic are cited in the prospectus as major customers, remains ongoing following the contract termination.

  微軟已終止與英國AI基礎設施業者Nscale在艾塞克斯郡洛頓（Loughton, Essex）計畫興建之資料中心合約，英國《電訊報》（Telegraph）週日報導。報導指出，英國國家電網官員曾告知Nscale，預計要到2030年代才能為該場址正式供電。路透社（Reuters）就此事致電微軟詢問，微軟拒絕置評。

  微軟是Nscale兩大最重要客戶之一。《電訊報》未說明微軟退出的明確原因，但報導指Nscale在該專案上已多次遭遇延誤。

  ## 電力供應瓶頸

  Nscale將問題歸因於影響大倫敦地區整體電網的供電瓶頸，並非洛頓場址的單一問題。Nscale發言人向路透社表示：「我們與微軟的合作關係依然穩固且著眼長期，這起不幸的倫敦全域電網問題，對我們其他合約毫無影響。」

  Nscale表示仍致力推進洛頓計畫，並正在「為該場址探索替代電源，同時也評估英國境內其他擴張據點」。

  ## IPO時機

  此次合約終止，距Nscale向美國申請首次公開募股（IPO）僅數週之遙。根據相關報導，Nscale的IPO招股說明書中，將微軟與AI公司Anthropic列為預期的主要客戶。

  Nscale提供運算能力、電力供應與資料中心等基礎設施，同時亦提供用於訓練和運行人工智慧模型的軟體。該公司獲輝達（Nvidia）入股支持，與AI基礎設施業者CoreWeave、Nebius、Crusoe及Lambda同場競逐。

  自約兩年半前成立至今，Nscale的總承包合約金額已從1億美元成長至逾1,030億美元，路透社報導指出。

  ## 重要意義

  洛頓場址的合約終止，是倫敦地區電網供電能力正在制約英國資料中心發展的具體例證。以微軟這般規模的客戶，僅因電網延誤可能延伸至2030年代便從特定場址撤手，凸顯電力基礎設施瓶頸對手握積極建設管線的業者所帶來的時程風險。

  Nscale在美國進行的IPO，招股說明書中仍將微軟與Anthropic列為主要客戶；在此次合約終止後，該案仍持續推進中。
