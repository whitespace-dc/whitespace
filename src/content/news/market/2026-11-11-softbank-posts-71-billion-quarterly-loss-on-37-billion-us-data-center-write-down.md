body_en: |
  SoftBank Group Corp swung to a ¥1.12 trillion ($7.1 billion) net loss in its fiscal second quarter, the company's first quarterly loss in nearly two years, after writing down $3.7 billion in advance payments for power-generation equipment tied to its US data-center expansion. Analysts had on average expected a profit of ¥129 billion for the period.

  ## Write-down and procurement review

  The charge was recorded through SoftBank subsidiary Silver Bands 7 (SB7), which is also backed by SoftBank Vision Fund 2. SB7 had paid $2.85 billion in non-refundable advances, plus $880 million in contract modifications and settlements, to secure power-generation equipment for the 8-gigawatt PORTS-Pike Technology Campus in Pike County, Ohio, and other US data-center projects. As part of the revised terms, SB7 became entitled to rebates of up to $2.1 billion on future equipment purchases over ten years, though the exact figure had not been determined as of September 30, 2026.

  Chief financial officer Yoshimitsu Goto said during the November 10 earnings conference that the company had "previously hurried to secure" the equipment it needs for AI data centers and is now conducting "a thorough reassessment," including a review of its procurement strategy. No plan to downsize the Ohio campus exists, he said, and he did not provide specifics on what drove the change in approach.

  The Ohio campus is structured with OpenAI as a 20-year tenant, with Nvidia providing credit support for the arrangement, according to company disclosures.

  ## Other quarterly charges

  The July-September quarter also absorbed a ¥321.9 billion derivative loss from a prepaid forward sales contract on Intel Corp shares. Stock price declines at chipmakers Arm Holdings plc and Intel, and at South Korean e-commerce company Coupang Inc, further weighed on results.

  The Vision Fund segment, which holds stakes in hundreds of technology start-ups, reported a quarterly loss of ¥81.6 billion against analyst expectations of a profit, though it booked a ¥85.4 billion quarterly investment gain driven by higher valuations at ByteDance Ltd and Lenskart Solutions Ltd.

  For the first half of SoftBank's fiscal year (April through September), the group swung to a net loss of $4.8 billion from a profit of $20 billion in the same period a year earlier. Net sales rose 13.3 percent to $26.4 billion.

  ## Financial position

  SoftBank's loan-to-value ratio (LTV) — the proportion of borrowings relative to the value of its asset holdings — stood at 15.9 percent at the end of September, within the 25 percent ceiling the company has set as a financial stability threshold, Goto said. The company's net asset value (NAV) — the worth of its holdings after deducting debt — was ¥59.8 trillion at September 30 but had fallen to an estimated ¥44.7 trillion as of November 9, reflecting subsequent market moves, he said, a decline of ¥15.1 trillion.

  Group-wide interest expenses for the six months to September more than doubled year on year to ¥676 billion. SoftBank prepaid $30 billion of a $40 billion bridge financing facility during the period, raising $7.35 billion through prepaid forward contracts backed by its Intel stake and a further $5 billion through a margin loan backed by Arm shares.

  Kazuki Sawamura, a strategist at Shinkin Asset Management, told Bloomberg the data-center write-down could be treated as temporary. "The more worrying factor is the increase in financial cost," Sawamura said. "If investment income and the value of its holdings don't grow steadily from here, the interest burden could squeeze earnings and heighten concern about SoftBank's financial health." Sharon Chen, a credit analyst at Bloomberg, said that after SoftBank spent $11 billion on data-center investments in the first half of the fiscal year, there was a risk that uncertainty would increase.

  ## Musk's default claim

  Before the earnings disclosure, SpaceX chief executive Elon Musk posted on the social platform X on November 6 that "SoftBank is defaulting on their turbine orders," referring to the turbine equipment used to generate power for data centers. Goto directly rejected the claim at the earnings conference, calling it "completely without basis," and said the company's AI infrastructure plans remain on schedule. "I don't understand why that post was made," Goto said.

  ## US midterm elections and project timelines

  The results came days after the November 3 US midterm elections, in which the Democratic Party retook both chambers of Congress. Goto said SoftBank needs to "watch closely" the effect of the election results on AI investment sentiment. Several Democratic candidates had run on platforms opposing data-center construction, and community opposition focused on electricity costs and water consumption had been widespread ahead of and independent of the election outcome, he said.

  Goto acknowledged that pre-construction projects could face delays as new government responses emerge, calling it "one of several scenarios," and said revised schedules would be drawn up if needed. SoftBank has sought to address local concerns by developing its own generation capacity, including 900 megawatts of generation in Milam County, Texas, comparable to the capacity draw of the data center under construction there, and by recycling water to minimize consumption.

  Goto was also asked whether SoftBank chairman Masayoshi Son's relationship with the US president — who co-announced the Stargate AI infrastructure initiative at the White House in January 2025 — would be weakened by the Republican defeat. Goto said the impact was not closely tied to election results, as individual projects had already been committed and were proceeding independently.

  ## Circular funding concern

  SoftBank and its AI ecosystem have drawn scrutiny over the structure of capital flows within those arrangements. During the earnings Q&A, analysts raised the example of SoftBank investing in OpenAI, OpenAI renting space in SoftBank subsidiary SB Energy's data centers, and Nvidia providing credit support for OpenAI within those structures. Goto said lenders examine where loan repayments ultimately originate, which in these arrangements is the large technology companies acting as tenants — companies he described as having credit quality far superior to SoftBank's own. He said treating the circular structure as a systemic risk requires careful, calm analysis.

  ## AI investments and valuations

  SoftBank invested $20 billion in OpenAI during the first two quarters of its fiscal year, with a further $10 billion in October, bringing cumulative investment in OpenAI to $64.6 billion for a stake of approximately 13 percent. The company also acquired data-center operator DigitalBridge Group Inc during the reporting period for $3.1 billion.

  AI valuations have declined broadly. OpenAI raised approximately $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation after initially seeking $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation, Bloomberg News reported. Anthropic PBC is being valued by potential investors at as low as $1.1 trillion, down from a previously cited $2 trillion figure, according to people familiar with the matter.

  SoftBank's shares have fallen more than 50 percent from their record high set in June and are down 12 percent from the start of the year. The decline has lifted the company's credit default swaps — a market measure of the default risk priced into its bonds — to their highest levels in years.

  ## Outlook

  Goto said the current environment does not call for aggressive expansion and that some caution is appropriate, while the company remains committed to AI infrastructure over the medium to long term. If OpenAI's current fundraising round closes and its valuation is confirmed at a higher level, Goto said, it could have a positive effect on SoftBank's NAV in the October-December quarter.
body_zh: |
  軟銀集團（SoftBank Group Corp）本財年第二季轉為淨虧損1兆1,200億日圓（約71億美元），創下近兩年來首度單季虧損，原因是對旗下美國資料中心擴張計畫所預付的發電設備款項認列37億美元減損。分析師原本平均預估該季將錄得1,290億日圓的獲利。

  ## 減損認列與採購策略檢討

  此次減損透過軟銀子公司Silver Bands 7（SB7）入帳，SB7亦由軟銀願景基金2（SoftBank Vision Fund 2）提供支持。SB7為確保俄亥俄州派克郡（Pike County）8吉瓦規模的PORTS-Pike技術園區及其他美國資料中心開發案所需的發電設備，已支付28.5億美元不可退款預付款，另加8.8億美元的合約修訂與和解金。根據修訂後的條款，SB7可在未來十年的設備採購中獲得最高21億美元的回扣，惟截至2026年9月30日，確切金額尚未確定。

  財務長後藤芳光（Yoshimitsu Goto）在11月10日法說會上表示，公司過去「急於搶先確保」人工智慧資料中心所需設備，現正進行「全面性的重新評估」，包括採購策略的檢討。他表示目前沒有縮減俄亥俄園區規模的計畫，亦未透露促使策略轉向的具體原因。

  根據公司揭露，俄亥俄園區以OpenAI作為20年長期租戶，並由輝達（Nvidia）為該租賃安排提供信用擔保。

  ## 其他單季虧損項目

  本財年第二季（7至9月）還承受了3,219億日圓的衍生性商品損失，起因為英特爾（Intel Corp）股票的預付遠期銷售合約。晶片製造商安謀控股（Arm Holdings plc）與英特爾的股價下跌，以及南韓電商公司Coupang Inc股價走弱，也進一步拖累了整體業績。

  願景基金部門持有數百家科技新創公司股份，本季錄得816億日圓的虧損，表現遜於市場預期的獲利；但受益於字節跳動（ByteDance Ltd）與Lenskart Solutions Ltd估值上揚，仍認列了854億日圓的季度投資收益。

  軟銀本財年上半年（4月至9月）整體由盈轉虧，淨虧損48億美元，而去年同期則為獲利200億美元。淨營收較去年同期成長13.3%，達264億美元。

  ## 財務狀況

  後藤在法說會上表示，軟銀的貸款對資產價值比（LTV）——即借款占持有資產總值的比例——於9月底為15.9%，低於公司設定的25%財務穩健上限。他指出，公司的淨資產價值（NAV）——即持有資產扣除負債後的淨值——於9月30日為59.8兆日圓，但受後續市場波動影響，截至11月9日估計已降至44.7兆日圓，縮水15.1兆日圓。

  截至9月底的半年度集團利息支出較去年同期大幅增加逾一倍，達6,760億日圓。軟銀在此期間預償了400億美元過橋融資中的300億美元，並透過以英特爾股票為擔保的預付遠期合約籌得73.5億美元，另以安謀股票為擔保的保證金貸款再籌50億美元。

  信金資產管理公司（Shinkin Asset Management）策略師澤村和樹（Kazuki Sawamura）向彭博社（Bloomberg）表示，資料中心的減損或許可視為一次性事件。「更令人憂慮的是財務成本的上升，」澤村說，「若投資收益與持股價值未能從此穩步成長，利息負擔可能壓縮獲利，並加劇外界對軟銀財務健康狀況的疑慮。」彭博信用分析師Sharon Chen則指出，在軟銀本財年上半年投入110億美元於資料中心建設之後，不確定性進一步升高的風險已不容忽視。

  ## 馬斯克的違約指控

  在法說會財報揭露前，SpaceX執行長伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）於11月6日在社群平台X上發文稱「軟銀正在違約渦輪訂單」，指的是用於資料中心發電的渦輪機設備。後藤在法說會上正面駁斥這一說法，稱其「完全毫無根據」，並表示公司的人工智慧基礎建設計畫仍如期推進。「我不明白為什麼會有那則貼文，」後藤說道。

  ## 美國期中選舉與專案時程

  本次財報公布時間，恰在11月3日美國期中選舉結果揭曉後數日——民主黨在此次選舉中奪回參眾兩院的多數席次。後藤表示，軟銀需要「密切觀察」選舉結果對人工智慧投資情緒的影響。他說，多位民主黨候選人以反對資料中心興建作為競選政見，而社區居民對電費上漲與用水消耗問題的反對聲浪，早在選前即已存在，且不論選舉結果如何都持續發酵。

  後藤坦承，尚未動工的開發案可能因新政府政策走向而面臨延誤，稱此為「幾種可能情境之一」，並表示若有必要將重新規劃時程。軟銀已透過自建發電能源尋求回應在地疑慮，包括在德克薩斯州米蘭郡（Milam County）開發900百萬瓦的發電容量——相當於當地在建資料中心的用電需求——並以廢水回收利用將耗水量降至最低。

  後藤也被問及，軟銀董事長孫正義（Masayoshi Son）與美國總統的關係——兩人曾於2025年1月在白宮共同宣布星際之門（Stargate）人工智慧基礎建設計畫——是否會因共和黨選敗而受到影響。後藤表示，影響程度與選舉結果的關聯性並不緊密，因為個別專案皆已落地確認，並在獨立推進之中。

  ## 循環資金疑慮

  軟銀及其人工智慧生態系的資本流動結構，引發了外界審視。法說會問答環節中，分析師舉例指出：軟銀投資OpenAI，OpenAI租用軟銀子公司SB Energy旗下的資料中心，輝達再為上述結構中的OpenAI提供信用擔保。後藤表示，放款方會審查貸款還款的最終來源——在這些安排中，還款來源為擔任租戶的大型科技公司，他形容這些公司的信用品質遠優於軟銀本身。他指出，要將這種循環結構定性為系統性風險，需要審慎、冷靜地加以分析。

  ## 人工智慧投資與估值

  軟銀在本財年前兩個季度共向OpenAI投資200億美元，10月再追加100億美元，累計對OpenAI的投資額達646億美元，持股比例約13%。軟銀也在報告期間以31億美元收購資料中心營運商DigitalBridge Group Inc。

  人工智慧相關估值整體走低。彭博新聞報導，OpenAI最初尋求以1.4兆美元估值募集300億美元，最終以1.25兆美元估值完成約110億美元的融資。據知情人士透露，Anthropic PBC目前正以低至1.1兆美元的估值接受潛在投資人評估，較先前流傳的2兆美元大幅縮水。

  軟銀股價自6月創下歷史高點以來已下跌逾50%，今年以來也下滑12%。股價持續走跌，使公司的信用違約交換（CDS）——衡量債券違約風險的市場指標——攀升至多年來的高點。

  ## 展望

  後藤表示，當前環境不宜積極擴張，保持若干審慎態度是必要的，但公司在中長期仍堅定致力於人工智慧基礎建設。他指出，若OpenAI目前的融資輪次順利完成、估值獲得更高水準確認，將可能對軟銀在10至12月季度的淨資產價值產生正面影響。
