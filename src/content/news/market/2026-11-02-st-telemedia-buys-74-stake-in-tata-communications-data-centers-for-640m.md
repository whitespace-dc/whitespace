body_en: |
  ST Telemedia, a unit of Singapore state investor Temasek Holdings, has agreed to pay US$640 million for a 74% stake in a portfolio of data centers owned by India's Tata Communications, the two companies said in a joint statement.

  The deal covers data centers in India and Singapore. Tata Communications will retain the remaining 26% stake in the combined entity, which will operate under ST Telemedia's data center subsidiary, ST Telemedia Global Data Center.

  ## What the deal includes

  The acquisition gives ST Telemedia Global Data Center access to 14 data centers located in key Indian cities, including Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore, according to the joint statement.

  ## What the parties said

  ST Telemedia chief executive Sio Tat Hiang described the addition of India to ST Telemedia Global Data Center's portfolio as "a major impetus to advance the company's ambition to be a significant global data center provider."

  ## Significance

  The transaction expands ST Telemedia's data center footprint into one of Asia's largest and fastest-growing markets, adding 14 facilities in major Indian commercial hubs to its existing portfolio. Tata Communications, by retaining a 26% stake, preserves a financial interest in the business while monetizing the majority of its data center assets.

  此次交易涵蓋印度與新加坡的資料中心資產。塔塔通訊將保留合併後實體26%的股權，該實體將隸屬於ST Telemedia的資料中心子公司「ST Telemedia全球資料中心」（ST Telemedia Global Data Center）旗下營運。

  ## 交易涵蓋範圍

  根據聯合聲明，此次收購使ST Telemedia全球資料中心得以進駐印度主要城市的14座資料中心，涵蓋德里、孟買及班加羅爾等重要據點。

  ## 雙方說法

  ST Telemedia執行長蕭達祥（Sio Tat Hiang）表示，將印度納入ST Telemedia全球資料中心的版圖，是「推動公司成為舉足輕重的全球資料中心供應商這一目標的重大動力」。

  ## 市場意義

  此次交易將ST Telemedia的資料中心布局延伸至亞洲規模最大、成長最快速的市場之一，在其現有投資組合之外，進一步於印度主要商業重鎮新增14座設施。塔塔通訊藉由保留26%股權，在將旗下資料中心資產大部分變現的同時，仍得以維持對該業務的財務利益。
