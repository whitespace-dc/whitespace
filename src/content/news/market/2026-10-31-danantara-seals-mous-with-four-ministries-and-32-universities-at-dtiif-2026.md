body_en: |
  Danantara Indonesia signed memoranda of understanding with four government ministries, the national research and innovation agency, and 32 universities at its Danantara Technology Innovation Investment Forum (DTIIF 2026) in Jakarta, while state-owned enterprises in energy, digital infrastructure, and transportation concluded separate cooperation agreements during the three-day event.

  The forum ran from October 26 to 28, 2026, at the Fairmont Jakarta, drawing more than 3,000 attendees and 274 speakers and moderators, according to the organizers. Programming included seven Distinguished Lecture sessions, 21 plenary sessions, and 38 panel and pitching sessions covering six strategic sectors: energy; telecommunications and digital infrastructure; agriculture, food, and health; advanced materials and semiconductors; finance and banking; and transportation and infrastructure.

  ## Government and research partnerships

  On the forum's first day, Danantara signed MOUs with the Ministry of Finance, the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Technology, the Ministry of Industry, and BRIN — Indonesia's national research and innovation agency (Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional). The aim, Danantara said, is to connect research agendas, talent development, industry requirements, funding, and strategic investment opportunities.

  A separate agreement between Danantara, BRIN, and 32 universities is directed at building a joint research, academic, and industry partnership platform intended to bring research outputs closer to commercial needs.

  ## Health and life sciences

  Danantara Development Management Fund (DDMF) joined with the Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB), state pharmaceutical company Indofarma, and PT EBM Saintifik dan Teknologi to develop kratom-based phytopharmaceuticals — medicines derived from plant matter. DDMF also opened discussions with Thermo Fisher Scientific on life sciences technology cooperation, the organizers said.

  ## Digital infrastructure and power

  State telecommunications operator Telkom Indonesia and state electricity company PLN agreed to align their data center development plans with electricity supply readiness. Data center infrastructure and power planning were among the digital infrastructure topics on the forum agenda.

  ## Electric vehicles

  State electronics and defense manufacturer LEN, PLN, and petroleum logistics company Pertamina Patra Niaga agreed to strengthen the Molinas (Motor Listrik Nasional, or National Electric Motor) ecosystem, covering charging infrastructure, energy supply, and electric vehicle deployment.

  ## Biodiesel

  Pertamina New & Renewable Energy — the renewables unit of state oil and gas company Pertamina — and Agrinas Palma signed a Heads of Agreement, a preliminary accord, for the construction and operation of a fatty acid methyl ester (FAME) biodiesel production facility in Indragiri Hilir, Riau province.

  ## Other sectors

  Partnerships at DTIIF 2026 also covered maritime, steel, cybersecurity, post-quantum cryptography — encryption designed to resist attacks by future quantum computers — biotechnology, and robotics, the organizers said, without providing further details on specific agreements in those areas.

  ## Significance and next steps

  Danantara CTO and DTIIF 2026 chair Sigit Puji Santosa described the agreements as a first step toward strengthening national technology and industry collaboration. Danantara said it will monitor the partnerships through to implementation.

  The follow-on event, the New Asia Forum, is scheduled for December 3-4, 2026, in Bali, where Danantara said further collaboration and investment activities will continue.
body_zh: |
  達南塔拉印尼（Danantara Indonesia）在雅加達舉辦的「達南塔拉科技創新投資論壇」（Danantara Technology Innovation Investment Forum，DTIIF 2026）上，與四個政府部會、國家研究與創新機構及32所大學簽署合作備忘錄，同時能源、數位基礎建設及交通運輸等領域的多家國有企業，亦在這場為期三天的活動中達成個別合作協議。

  本次論壇於2026年10月26日至28日在雅加達費爾蒙酒店（Fairmont Jakarta）舉行，據主辦單位表示，共吸引逾3,000名與會者及274位講者與主持人。論壇內容涵蓋七場傑出講座、21場全體會議，以及38場小組討論與提案簡報，聚焦六大策略性領域：能源；電信與數位基礎建設；農業、糧食與健康；先進材料與半導體；金融與銀行；以及交通運輸與基礎建設。

  ## 政府與研究合作夥伴關係

  論壇首日，達南塔拉分別與財政部、高等教育科學技術部、工業部，以及印尼國家研究與創新機構（BRIN，Badan Riset dan Inovasi Nasional）簽署合作備忘錄。達南塔拉表示，此舉旨在串聯研究議程、人才培育、產業需求、資金來源與策略性投資機會。

  達南塔拉、BRIN與32所大學另行簽署一項協議，目標是建立涵蓋研究、學術與產業的聯合合作平台，讓研究成果更貼近商業化需求。

  ## 健康與生命科學

  達南塔拉發展管理基金（DDMF）與萬隆理工學院（ITB）、國有製藥公司Indofarma，以及PT EBM Saintifik dan Teknologi合作，共同開發以卡痛（kratom）為基礎的植物性藥品——即從植物萃取製成的藥物。主辦單位表示，DDMF亦就生命科學技術合作事宜，與賽默飛世爾科技（Thermo Fisher Scientific）展開初步洽談。

  ## 數位基礎建設與電力

  國有電信業者電信印尼（Telkom Indonesia）與國有電力公司PLN達成共識，將資料中心開發計畫與電力供應準備進度相互配合。資料中心基礎建設與電力規劃，均列入本次論壇數位基礎建設議程之中。

  ## 電動車

  國有電子暨國防製造商LEN、PLN，以及石油物流公司Pertamina Patra Niaga三方同意強化「Molinas」（Motor Listrik Nasional，即國家電動馬達）生態系，涵蓋充電基礎建設、能源供應及電動車推廣部署。

  ## 生質柴油

  國有石油天然氣公司Pertamina旗下再生能源子公司「Pertamina New & Renewable Energy」，與Agrinas Palma簽署意向書（Heads of Agreement，即初步協議），就在廖內省（Riau）印拉吉里希利爾（Indragiri Hilir）興建並營運一座脂肪酸甲酯（FAME）生質柴油生產設施達成共識。

  ## 其他領域

  主辦單位表示，DTIIF 2026的合作範疇亦涵蓋海事、鋼鐵、網路安全、後量子密碼學（即專為抵禦未來量子電腦攻擊所設計的加密技術）、生物技術及機器人等領域，惟未就上述各領域的具體協議提供進一步細節。

  ## 重要意義與後續步驟

  達南塔拉技術長暨DTIIF 2026主席西吉特·普吉·桑托薩（Sigit Puji Santosa）將這些協議定位為強化國家科技與產業合作的第一步。達南塔拉表示，將持續追蹤各項合作夥伴關係的落實進度。

  後續活動「新亞洲論壇」（New Asia Forum）預計於2026年12月3日至4日在峇里島（Bali）舉行，達南塔拉表示，屆時將持續推動進一步的合作與投資活動。
