body_en: |
  Nvidia is negotiating an investment of as much as $3 billion (approximately NT$96 billion) in SB Energy, SoftBank Group's energy and data center infrastructure subsidiary, according to a report by The Information cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times. The prospective deal would extend Nvidia's financial reach from computing hardware into power infrastructure and forms part of a roughly $100 billion financing structure backing OpenAI's AI data center campus in Ohio.

  ## Investment Structure and IPO Timetable

  The investment would be split into two tranches, Commercial Times reported. Nvidia would contribute approximately $1.5 billion when a contract for the Ohio data center project is signed, with a further $1.5 billion committed to SB Energy at its initial public offering (IPO). SB Energy could list as early as September and is targeting proceeds of at least $5 billion from the offering.

  ## Ohio Data Center Financing

  The SB Energy stake is one component of a larger financing arrangement involving Nvidia, OpenAI, and SB Energy for the Ohio campus. The Wall Street Journal previously reported that Nvidia adjusted its support commitment for the Ohio project, with the initial guarantee now expected to be below $120 billion — well down from the $250 billion figure discussed in earlier negotiations, according to Commercial Times.

  ## About SB Energy

  SB Energy was founded in 2019 and develops large-scale power and data center infrastructure. The company is currently building multiple data center campuses to meet rapidly growing AI computing demand and already counts SoftBank and OpenAI among its backers. A Nvidia investment would further consolidate financial relationships among the three companies in AI infrastructure.

  ## Competition Extends from GPUs to Power Architecture

  The prospective deal reflects how AI infrastructure competition is expanding beyond graphics processing units (GPUs) — the chips at the core of AI training and inference — into power and energy systems. As AI data centers scale toward gigawatt (GW)-level capacity — one gigawatt is roughly the continuous output of a large nuclear reactor, or enough to power a mid-size city — power supply design has become a strategic variable alongside computing hardware.

  Nvidia is actively promoting an 800-volt direct-current (800 VDC) power architecture for data centers, a design suited to the high power densities AI workloads require. Taiwan-based manufacturers Delta Electronics and Lite-On Technology have both entered Nvidia's 800 VDC supply ecosystem, Commercial Times reported. As AI capital spending broadens from servers into power systems, liquid cooling, and data center infrastructure, those suppliers stand to benefit from that downstream expansion.

  ## Nvidia's Broader Ecosystem Commitments

  Bank of America analyst Vivek Arya reported that Nvidia has committed a combined $70 billion in direct equity investment to ecosystem partners. Disclosed commitments include $30 billion to OpenAI, up to $10 billion to Anthropic, and $5 billion to Safe Superintelligence.

  ## Upcoming Earnings

  Nvidia is scheduled to report results for its May-to-July fiscal quarter on August 26. The company has guided for revenue of approximately $91 billion, plus or minus 2 percent, a roughly 95 percent increase from the same quarter a year earlier, with data center operations expected to account for the largest share. SB Energy's IPO, targeted for as early as September, represents the next milestone in the Ohio project financing timeline.
body_zh: |
  根據《The Information》報導，並經台灣《工商時報》引述，輝達（Nvidia）正與軟銀集團（SoftBank Group）旗下能源與資料中心基礎設施子公司SB Energy洽談，擬投資金額最高達30億美元（約新台幣960億元）。這筆潛在交易將使輝達的財務觸角從運算硬體延伸至電力基礎設施，並構成支持OpenAI俄亥俄州AI資料中心園區約1,000億美元融資架構的一部分。

  ## 投資結構與IPO時程

  《工商時報》報導，此項投資將分兩批進行。待俄亥俄州資料中心專案合約簽署時，輝達將先行投入約15億美元；SB Energy完成首次公開發行（IPO）時，輝達將再投入15億美元。SB Energy最快可於今年9月掛牌上市，目標籌資金額至少達50億美元。

  ## 俄亥俄州資料中心融資

  SB Energy的股權投資，是輝達、OpenAI與SB Energy為俄亥俄州園區共同建構的更大規模融資安排之一。《工商時報》引述《華爾街日報》此前報導指出，輝達已調整對俄亥俄州專案的支持承諾，初期擔保金額預計將低於1,200億美元，較早期談判時討論的2,500億美元大幅縮水。

  ## 關於SB Energy

  SB Energy成立於2019年，主要從事大型電力與資料中心基礎設施開發。目前，該公司正積極興建多座資料中心園區，以應對AI運算需求的快速增長，現有股東已包括軟銀與OpenAI。若輝達完成投資，將進一步鞏固三家公司在AI基礎設施領域的財務連結。

  ## 競爭從GPU延伸至電力架構

  這筆潛在交易反映出，AI基礎設施競爭的版圖正從繪圖處理器（GPU）——AI訓練與推論的核心晶片——擴展至電力與能源系統。隨著AI資料中心的規模邁向吉瓦（GW）等級——1吉瓦約相當於一座大型核電廠的持續輸出功率，或足以供應一座中型城市的用電——電力供應設計已成為與運算硬體同等重要的戰略變數。

  輝達目前正積極推廣資料中心採用800伏特直流電（800 VDC）電力架構，這種設計適合AI工作負載所需的高功率密度環境。《工商時報》報導，台灣廠商台達電（Delta Electronics）與光寶科技（Lite-On Technology）均已進入輝達800 VDC的供應鏈生態系。隨著AI資本支出從伺服器擴展至電力系統、液冷技術及資料中心基礎設施，上述供應商預期將受惠於這波下游擴張趨勢。

  ## 輝達的生態系投資承諾

  美國銀行分析師Vivek Arya報告指出，輝達對生態系夥伴的直接股權投資承諾合計達700億美元。已披露的承諾包括：投資OpenAI 300億美元、投資Anthropic最高100億美元，以及投資Safe Superintelligence 50億美元。

  ## 即將公布的財報

  輝達預計於8月26日公布5月至7月會計季度業績。該公司已指引當季營收約910億美元，上下浮動2%，較去年同期成長約95%，其中資料中心業務預計佔最大份額。SB Energy的IPO目標最快於9月完成，為俄亥俄州專案融資時程的下一個重要里程碑。
