body_en: |
  Indonesia's industrial zone managers are preparing to present President Prabowo Subianto with a formal account of what they say are persistent obstacles preventing investment commitments from becoming on-the-ground factories and jobs, according to Kontan reporting published August 4, 2026.

  Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia (HKI), the Indonesian Industrial Zone Association, will bring the issue to the president, the report said. The organization's intervention comes as the Prabowo administration has positioned investment attraction as a primary driver of economic growth.

  ## The realization gap

  Industrial zone operators say large volumes of investment interest have not converted into construction activity or employment at the pace commitments would suggest. HKI did not specify in the visible portion of the report which obstacles are most responsible, but characterized them as numerous and as yet unresolved.

  The disconnect between pledged and realized investment is a known friction point in Indonesia's industrial development framework. Commitments recorded by investment-promotion bodies do not always account for regulatory, infrastructure, or land-clearance delays that can defer or cancel actual construction.

  The Kontan report was illustrated with a reference to KEK Industripolis Batang, a Special Economic Zone (KEK — Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus) in Central Java, signaling that the issue extends to government-designated priority development corridors.

  ## Significance

  If HKI's concerns reach the presidential level, the outcome could shape how Indonesia's investment facilitation agencies reconcile headline commitment figures with verified groundbreaking data — a distinction that matters for infrastructure planning, utility provisioning, and workforce development across the country's industrial zones.

  HKI has said it intends to bring the matter to President Prabowo Subianto, though no date or format for that meeting has been publicly confirmed.
body_zh: |
  ## 投資承諾與實際落地之間的鴻溝

  據《康坦》（Kontan）於2026年8月4日發布的報導，印尼工業區業者正準備向總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托（Prabowo Subianto）正式陳情，說明長期阻礙投資承諾轉化為實體工廠與就業機會的種種障礙。

  印尼工業區協會（Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia，HKI）將把這項問題帶上總統層級，報導如是指出。此番介入，恰逢普拉伯沃政府將招商引資定位為驅動經濟成長的核心施政方針之際。

  ## 落實落差

  工業區業者表示，大量投資意向書並未以承諾所示的速度轉換為實際動工或人力僱用。HKI在報導可見部分中並未點名哪些障礙肇因最深，僅將其描述為「為數眾多且迄今未解」。

  承諾投資與實際到位之間的落差，是印尼工業開發框架中已知的摩擦點。招商機構所登錄的投資承諾，往往未能充分反映法規審查、基礎設施配套或土地徵清所造成的延宕，而這些因素均可能推遲乃至取消實際動工計畫。

  《康坦》的報導以中爪哇的峇當工業極（KEK Industripolis Batang）——即「特別經濟區」（Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus，KEK）——作為配圖案例，顯示此一問題已延伸至印尼政府指定的優先發展廊帶。

  ## 事件意義

  若HKI的訴求得以觸達總統層級，其結果可能牽動印尼投資促進機構如何調和標題式承諾數字與實際動工資料之間的落差——這項區別對全國各工業區的基礎設施規劃、公用設施配置與人力發展均舉足輕重。

  HKI表示有意將此事提交普拉伯沃總統，但目前尚未公開確認會面的日期或形式。
