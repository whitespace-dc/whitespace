body_en: |
  PT Telkom Data Ekosistem, operating under the NeutraDC brand and a subsidiary of PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk. (ticker: TLKM), recorded 49.9 megawatts (MW) of effective data center capacity in the first half of 2026, according to Bisnis.com. The figure reflects active expansion across two sites and positions the data center segment as a growth pillar for the broader Telkom group.

  Capacity additions driving the 49.9 MW figure include a 21.5 MW expansion at Cikarang, located in West Java, and an 18 MW expansion at Batam, the island free-trade zone near Singapore. Together those two projects account for 39.5 MW of the total effective capacity base.

  ## Planned expansion

  NeutraDC has outlined a roadmap for a second phase of development at its Cikarang site, scheduled for 2027. The source did not provide capacity figures for that planned phase.

  ## Significance for Telkom

  Analysts cited by Bisnis.com describe the data center segment as under-appreciated relative to its contribution to Telkom's long-term growth outlook. The segment is characterized as one of the company's key supporting pillars, with the capacity expansion program said to improve the visibility of that long-term growth.
