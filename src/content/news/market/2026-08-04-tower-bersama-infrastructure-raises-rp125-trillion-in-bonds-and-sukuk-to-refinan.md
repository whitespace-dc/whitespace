body_en: |
  PT Tower Bersama Infrastructure Tbk. (TBIG), the Indonesian telecommunications tower company, launched a combined Rp1.25 trillion (approximately $77 million) debt offering on August 3, 2026, comprising conventional bonds and Islamic sukuk, with the bulk of the proceeds set to retire a bank loan falling due within days of issuance.

  The offering pairs two instruments: Rp952.19 billion in Obligasi Berkelanjutan VII Tahap IV (Continuous Bond VII, Phase IV) and Rp302.16 billion in Sukuk Ijarah Berkelanjutan I Tahap IV (Continuous Sukuk Ijarah I, Phase IV). Sukuk ijarah is an Islamic capital-market instrument structured around asset leases rather than interest, making it compliant with Islamic finance principles. Together they bring the total raise to Rp1.254 trillion.

  ## Bond and sukuk structure

  The conventional bonds are split into two series. Series A carries a face value of Rp281.70 billion, a three-year tenor maturing August 6, 2029, and a fixed annual coupon of 8.00%. Series B, the larger tranche, totals Rp670.50 billion with a five-year tenor to August 6, 2031, and a fixed coupon of 8.25%. Both series pay quarterly, with the first coupon scheduled for November 6, 2026, according to the company's prospectus.

  The sukuk follow the same maturity schedule. Sukuk Series A amounts to Rp230.21 billion over three years, generating an annual ijarah return of Rp18.42 billion — equivalent to Rp80 million per Rp1 billion of outstanding balance. Sukuk Series B totals Rp71.96 billion over five years, with an annual return of Rp5.94 billion, or Rp82.5 million per Rp1 billion outstanding. Ijarah payments are also quarterly, beginning November 6, 2026.

  ## Use of proceeds: a time-sensitive refinancing

  The transaction is structured to replace expiring bank debt. As of July 17, 2026, TBIG owed PT Bank UOB Indonesia a principal balance of Rp1.82 trillion at an interest rate of 7.05% per year, with that loan maturing August 13, 2026 — seven days after the bonds are scheduled for electronic distribution on August 6.

  Net proceeds from the bonds, approximately Rp945.53 billion, and net proceeds from the sukuk, approximately Rp300.20 billion, will be applied to partial repayment of that UOB Indonesia loan, the prospectus states. The remaining balance after those payments will be covered through the company's operating cash flow. TBIG reported net cash from operating activities of Rp2.02 trillion in the first quarter of 2026.

  The coupon rate on the new five-year bonds — 8.25% — is roughly 120 basis points above the 7.05% rate on the bank loan being retired. The higher rate reflects the longer tenor and capital-market pricing rather than the bilateral bank terms.

  ## Rating and market access

  Fitch Ratings Indonesia assigned TBIG a credit rating of AA+(idn) for the issuance, the second-highest investment-grade category on the national rating scale, which the rating firm said reflects the company's very strong capacity to meet its financial obligations.

  ## Issuance timeline

  The public offering period runs on August 3, 2026. Allotment is scheduled for August 4, with refund of unallotted orders on August 5. Electronic distribution — the formal issuance date — is set for August 6, and the bonds and sukuk are to be listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (Bursa Efek Indonesia, IDX) on August 7.

  Seven underwriters are managing the offering: PT Indo Premier Sekuritas, PT Trimegah Sekuritas Indonesia Tbk., PT CIMB Niaga Sekuritas, PT DBS Vickers Sekuritas Indonesia, PT Aldiracita Sekuritas Indonesia, PT BCA Sekuritas, and PT RHB Sekuritas Indonesia. PT Bank Tabungan Negara (Persero) Tbk. serves as trustee.

  ## Significance

  The deal illustrates a pattern common among Indonesian infrastructure borrowers: using the domestic bond and sukuk market to extend the maturity profile of short-dated bank debt and diversify funding sources. By pairing a conventional bond with a sukuk tranche, TBIG also broadens its investor base to include Islamic funds. The combined Rp1.254 trillion raise covers roughly 69% of the Rp1.82 trillion UOB balance outstanding as of mid-July, with operating cash flow expected to cover the remainder.

  The bonds and sukuk are set to begin trading on the IDX on August 7, 2026.

  PT Tower Bersama Infrastructure Tbk.（TBIG），印尼電信鐵塔業者，於2026年8月3日啟動合計1.254兆印尼盾（約7,700萬美元）的混合債務發行，涵蓋傳統債券與伊斯蘭債券（sukuk）兩種工具，所募資金大部分將用於償還一筆數日後即將到期的銀行借款。

  本次發行包含兩種金融工具：面額9,521.9億印尼盾的「第七期連續債券第四階段」（Obligasi Berkelanjutan VII Tahap IV），以及面額3,021.6億印尼盾的「第一期連續伊賈拉伊斯蘭債券第四階段」（Sukuk Ijarah Berkelanjutan I Tahap IV）。伊賈拉伊斯蘭債券（sukuk ijarah）是一種以資產租賃為架構、而非以利息為基礎的伊斯蘭資本市場工具，符合伊斯蘭金融教義。兩者合計募資總額為1.254兆印尼盾。

  ## 債券與伊斯蘭債券架構

  傳統債券分為兩個系列。A系列票面金額為2,817億印尼盾，期限三年，到期日為2029年8月6日，固定年票息率為8.00%。B系列規模較大，合計6,705億印尼盾，期限五年，到期日為2031年8月6日，固定票息率為8.25%。兩個系列均按季付息，首次付息日預定為2026年11月6日，此依公司公開說明書所載。

  伊斯蘭債券部分採相同到期時程。伊斯蘭債券A系列金額為2,302.1億印尼盾，期限三年，年度伊賈拉收益為184.2億印尼盾，相當於每10億印尼盾餘額產生8,000萬印尼盾的年收益。伊斯蘭債券B系列合計719.6億印尼盾，期限五年，年度收益為59.4億印尼盾，即每10億印尼盾餘額產生8,250萬印尼盾。伊賈拉款項同樣按季支付，首次付款日同為2026年11月6日。

  ## 資金用途：時間緊迫的再融資

  本次交易的設計旨在取代即將到期的銀行借款。截至2026年7月17日，TBIG積欠PT Bank UOB Indonesia的本金餘額為1.82兆印尼盾，年利率7.05%，該筆貸款到期日為2026年8月13日——距離債券預定於8月6日電子化交割的日期僅七天。

  依公開說明書所載，本次債券的淨募資金額約9,455.3億印尼盾，伊斯蘭債券淨募資金額約3,002億印尼盾，均將用於部分償還上述大華印尼銀行貸款。償還後的剩餘餘額將由公司營業現金流支應。TBIG於2026年第一季的營業活動淨現金流為2.02兆印尼盾。

  新發行五年期債券的票息率為8.25%，較所償還銀行貸款的7.05%利率高出約120個基點。此一利差反映的是較長的借款期限與資本市場定價機制，而非雙邊銀行協議的條款。

  ## 信用評等與市場准入

  惠譽評等印尼（Fitch Ratings Indonesia）為本次發行賦予TBIG「AA+（idn）」信用評等，為全國評等體系中的第二高投資等級。惠譽表示，此評等反映該公司履行財務義務的能力極為強健。

  ## 發行時程

  公開申購期間為2026年8月3日。配售作業預定於8月4日完成，未獲配售的資金將於8月5日退還投資人。電子化交割（正式發行日）訂於8月6日，債券及伊斯蘭債券預定於8月7日在印尼證券交易所（Bursa Efek Indonesia，IDX）掛牌上市。

  本次發行由七家承銷商共同承辦：PT Indo Premier Sekuritas、PT Trimegah Sekuritas Indonesia Tbk.、PT CIMB Niaga Sekuritas、PT DBS Vickers Sekuritas Indonesia、PT Aldiracita Sekuritas Indonesia、PT BCA Sekuritas，以及PT RHB Sekuritas Indonesia。受託機構由PT Bank Tabungan Negara（Persero）Tbk.擔任。

  ## 意義與影響

  此次交易呈現印尼基礎建設借款人的常見操作模式：透過國內債券與伊斯蘭債券市場，將短期銀行借款的到期期限延長，同時分散融資來源。藉由傳統債券與伊斯蘭債券雙軌並行，TBIG亦將投資人基礎拓展至伊斯蘭基金領域。本次合計1.254兆印尼盾的募資，約涵蓋截至七月中旬尚餘1.82兆印尼盾大華銀行貸款餘額的69%，其餘部分預計由營業現金流補足。

  債券及伊斯蘭債券預定於2026年8月7日起在IDX正式交易。
