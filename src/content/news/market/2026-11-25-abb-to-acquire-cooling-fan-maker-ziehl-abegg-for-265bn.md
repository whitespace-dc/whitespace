body_en: |
  ABB has agreed to acquire Ziehl-Abegg, a German manufacturer of cooling fans and ventilation systems, for an enterprise value of $2.65 billion and an equity value of $2.22 billion.

  The Künzelsau, Germany-based company is projected to generate approximately $1.4 billion in revenue for full-year 2026, with an operational EBITA (earnings before interest, taxes, and amortisation) margin in the mid-single-digit range. Ziehl-Abegg makes axial, centrifugal, and process fans, internal and external rotor motors, ventilation systems, and integrated control systems, serving customers across markets that include data centers and HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) applications.

  The transaction is expected to close in the second half of 2027, pending regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions.

  ## ABB's case for the deal

  ABB's Motion business area president Brandon Spencer described Ziehl-Abegg as "highly complementary" to ABB's existing portfolio of motors and drives. "Together, we can create a more comprehensive offering of components for cooling and ventilation systems customers, helping us better address their needs, strengthen our position in attractive markets and support profitable growth," Spencer said. He also cited a shared European heritage, noting both companies carry more than 100 years of technological history.

  ## Ziehl-Abegg's position

  Ziehl-Abegg CEO Joachim Ley said the company had "made unprecedented investments across our global footprint to meet surging customer demand" and described the deal as "the natural continuation of our growth trajectory." Ley said ABB is "an outstanding fit" for Ziehl-Abegg's culture, its research and development commitments, and its workforce.

  ## Closing timeline

  The acquisition moves ABB's Motion segment into dedicated cooling and ventilation hardware. Completion depends on regulatory clearance, with a targeted close in the second half of 2027.

  ## ABB斥資26.5億美元收購冷卻風扇製造商Ziehl-Abegg

  ABB宣布，同意以企業價值26.5億美元、股權價值22.2億美元，收購德國冷卻風扇與通風系統製造商Ziehl-Abegg。

  這家總部位於德國京策爾紹（Künzelsau）的企業，預計2026年全年營收約達14億美元，營業EBITA（息稅攤銷前利潤）利潤率落在中個位數區間。Ziehl-Abegg的產品線涵蓋軸流風扇、離心風扇與工業製程風扇、內轉子與外轉子馬達、通風系統及整合式控制系統，服務對象遍及資料中心與暖通空調（HVAC）等多個市場。

  本次交易預計於2027年下半年完成，仍須取得相關監管機關核准及其他慣例交割條件。

  ## ABB的收購理由

  ABB動力事業部總裁布蘭登．斯賓塞（Brandon Spencer）表示，Ziehl-Abegg與ABB現有的馬達及驅動器產品組合「高度互補」。他說：「雙方攜手，能為冷卻與通風系統客戶提供更完整的元件解決方案，幫助我們更有效回應客戶需求、鞏固在優質市場的地位，並推動獲利成長。」斯賓塞也特別提及兩家公司共同的歐洲傳承，指出雙方皆擁有逾百年的技術發展歷史。

  ## Ziehl-Abegg的市場地位

  Ziehl-Abegg執行長約阿希姆．萊伊（Joachim Ley）表示，公司近年「在全球據點投入了前所未有的資本，以因應客戶需求的急速攀升」，並將此次收購定位為「我們成長軌跡的自然延續」。萊伊表示，ABB在企業文化、研發投入及員工照顧等面向，都與Ziehl-Abegg「高度契合」。

  ## 交割時程

  此次收購將使ABB動力業務部門正式跨入專用冷卻與通風硬體領域。交易能否如期完成，取決於各地監管機關的審查進度，目標交割時間訂於2027年下半年。
