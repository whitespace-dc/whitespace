body_en: |
  Nvidia has paused a revenue-sharing financing program it launched less than two months ago to support small artificial intelligence cloud companies, the Wall Street Journal reported on Aug. 27, citing people familiar with the matter.

  The chip designer pulled back from the program last week, according to the Journal, which said Nvidia could still revamp the initiative or fold it into another program. An Nvidia spokesperson told the Journal: "The new business model... that opens up compute access to the fast-growing AI ecosystem is still in place and continues to evolve due to high demand."

  ## How the program worked

  Under the initiative, Nvidia offered credit support to small AI cloud companies — firms that rent out computing capacity powered by Nvidia chips — in exchange for a share of their revenues. Nvidia would rent compute capacity back from those cloud customers if they were unable to sell it, providing them with a guaranteed buyer and making it easier for the firms to borrow the capital needed to purchase Nvidia's AI chips. Nvidia would earn revenue both on the hardware sale and on a portion of the cloud revenues generated from Nvidia-powered capacity.

  Under the proposed deal terms, Nvidia would receive 50 percent of any revenue cloud providers earned through its chips beyond a certain threshold, the Journal reported.

  Nvidia said during its earnings call this week that the model had the potential to drive billions of dollars in revenue over the medium to long term.

  ## Concerns over control and antitrust risk

  The pause follows a series of friction points that emerged in the program's early weeks. Some Nvidia employees expressed concerns to current and potential customers that the initiative could draw antitrust scrutiny, the Journal reported, noting sensitivities around the extent to which Nvidia could dictate how its customers conduct business.

  Nvidia also irked some potential partners with the degree of control it sought, according to the report. The company told some cloud providers they could rent its chips only to approved customers, and signalled a preference for capacity to be distributed among multiple smaller firms rather than concentrated with one large customer.

  ## Investor scrutiny over circular deals

  Investor concern has grown in recent months as Nvidia has increased its financial involvement in the AI ecosystem it supplies, raising questions about so-called circular deals — arrangements in which a chip supplier finances the purchases of its own hardware, which critics argue could artificially inflate demand figures.

  In August, Nvidia helped arrange $500 billion in financing from major U.S. financial institutions for its customers. The company also agreed to guarantee up to $105 billion to help OpenAI lease a large data center.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The pause signals that Nvidia's effort to move beyond hardware sales and capture a share of downstream cloud revenues faces both regulatory uncertainty and commercial resistance from potential partners. The outcome of any revamped program will be closely watched by cloud infrastructure firms and investors assessing whether Nvidia can sustainably expand into financing and revenue-sharing arrangements without triggering antitrust review or distorting demand signals in the AI chip market.

  Nvidia said the broader business model initiative remains active and continues to evolve.

  ## 輝達暫停與AI雲端業者的分潤融資計畫

  據《華爾街日報》於8月27日引述知情人士報導，輝達已暫停一項不到兩個月前甫推出、專為支持小型人工智慧雲端業者所設計的分潤融資計畫。

  報導指出，輝達於上週撤回該計畫，但《華爾街日報》同時提及，輝達仍有可能對計畫進行改版，或將其併入另一項方案。輝達發言人向《華爾街日報》表示：「這套開放運算資源、服務快速成長的AI生態系的新商業模式……目前仍然運作中，並因需求強勁而持續演進。」

  ## 計畫運作方式

  根據該計畫的設計，輝達向小型AI雲端業者——即租用以輝達晶片為核心的運算資源的企業——提供信用支持，以換取這些業者部分營收的分潤。若雲端業者未能順利出售其運算資源，輝達將回租該算力，等同於為這些業者提供一個有保障的買家，使其更容易取得購買輝達AI晶片所需的融資。輝達因此可同時獲得硬體銷售的收益，以及由輝達算力所創造的雲端營收中的一定比例。

  《華爾街日報》報導，根據擬議的交易條款，雲端業者透過輝達晶片所產生、超過特定門檻的營收，輝達將抽取其中50%作為分潤。

  輝達本週在法說會上表示，這套模式在中長期具有創造數十億美元營收的潛力。

  ## 管控疑慮與反壟斷風險

  此次暫停，源自計畫推出初期便已浮現的一連串摩擦。《華爾街日報》報導，部分輝達員工曾向現有及潛在客戶表達疑慮，認為此計畫可能引發反壟斷審查，原因在於外界對輝達主導客戶業務經營方式的程度相當敏感。

  報導亦指出，輝達對管控權的要求程度也惹惱了部分潛在合作夥伴。輝達曾告知部分雲端業者，其晶片租用對象必須限定為輝達核准的客戶，並表明傾向將算力分散提供給多家較小型企業，而非集中在單一大型客戶身上。

  ## 投資人對循環交易的疑慮

  近幾個月來，隨著輝達加深對其供應的AI生態系的金融介入程度，投資人的疑慮持續升溫。外界對所謂「循環交易」的質疑聲浪也隨之增加——這類安排是指晶片供應商為客戶購買自家硬體提供融資，批評者認為此舉可能人為墊高需求數字。

  今年8月，輝達協助安排美國主要金融機構向其客戶提供高達5,000億美元的融資。該公司亦同意為OpenAI承租大型資料中心提供最高達1,050億美元的擔保。

  ## 對產業的意涵

  此次暫停釋出一個訊號：輝達試圖跨越硬體銷售的邊界、進一步分享下游雲端營收的努力，在法規層面面臨高度不確定性，在商業層面也遭遇潛在合作夥伴的抵制。任何改版計畫的最終走向，將受到雲端基礎設施業者及投資人的密切關注——各界正在評估，輝達能否在不引發反壟斷審查、或不扭曲AI晶片市場需求訊號的前提下，以可長可久的方式擴展至融資與分潤領域。

  輝達表示，整體商業模式計畫目前仍在推進，並持續演進調整中。
