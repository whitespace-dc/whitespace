  Singapore-based Ropedia has raised US$22 million (SG$28.4 million) in a pre-Series A funding round, bringing its total capital raised to US$30 million (SG$38.7 million), as it scales a data-infrastructure platform that supplies real-world human interaction datasets to robotics and embodied AI developers.

  The round was led by venture investors with experience in AI, deep tech, and infrastructure in Southeast Asia, the company said. A prior raise attracted angels and investors connected to Google, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), NVIDIA, and Amazon.

  ## What Ropedia builds

  Ropedia operates at the data-collection and processing layer of the physical AI stack — the segment of the industry concerned with building AI systems that interact with the physical world, such as industrial robots and humanoid machines. The company contends that physical AI development faces a constraint analogous to those that shaped earlier technology waves: just as cloud computing required data centres and large language models drew on text from the internet, physical intelligence requires large volumes of high-quality human interaction data captured in real-world environments.

  The company's platform spans the full data pipeline. Using proprietary wearable hardware, Ropedia captures egocentric (first-person-perspective) video, depth, motion, and audio from humans performing tasks. That data is then synchronised, processed, and delivered to customers either through Xperience-10M — which the company describes as one of the world's largest Human Experience Datasets — or through custom Data-as-a-Service (DaaS) packages tailored to specific robotics or embodied AI applications.

  Ropedia claims its approach reduces data-collection costs by up to 50 times compared with traditional methods. The company said its wearable capture device has entered mass production to support larger-scale deployments. It currently serves more than 20 robotics and foundation model companies across North America, China, and Singapore.

  ## Founding team and academic origins

  Ropedia is a deep-tech spinout from Nanyang Technological University (NTU) in Singapore — a structure the company describes as rare in Asia. The three founders share overlapping academic and professional histories. Chief Executive Officer Chen Zhaoxi and Chief Technology Officer Fangzhou Hong both studied at Tsinghua University in China — Chen in automation, Hong in software engineering — before completing PhDs at NTU within the MMLab research ecosystem, where their work covered 3D perception, human reconstruction, spatial intelligence, and egocentric vision. Both also completed research internships at Meta Reality Labs, working on avatar technology, first-person perception, and human-centred spatial computing.

  Chief Scientist Liu Ziwei, who served as PhD supervisor to Chen and Hong, is an associate professor and computer-vision researcher at NTU. Liu completed his undergraduate education at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in China and earned his PhD from the Chinese University of Hong Kong.

  ## Use of proceeds and expansion plans

  Ropedia said it will deploy the new capital across four areas: expanding global data collection operations; growing its teams in Singapore and the United States; increasing manufacturing capacity for its wearable capture hardware to support larger fleet deployments; and developing its data platform with annotation tooling, quality analytics, and compliance infrastructure. The company also plans to expand its AI research team's work on data foundation models and world models.

  ## Industry significance

  The round reflects investor appetite for companies addressing the data bottleneck in physical AI development. While progress on the software and model side of robotics has accelerated, the availability of diverse, real-world human interaction data at scale remains a limiting factor for model generalisation — the ability of an AI system to perform reliably in varied real-world conditions after training in controlled settings. By increasing the scale and geographic diversity of its datasets, Ropedia aims to help customers build models that operate effectively outside laboratory environments and into factories, workplaces, and homes.

  The company plans to extend its customer reach across North America, China, and Singapore as it increases both the volume and task diversity of its Xperience-10M dataset through the expanded fleet deployments the new funding is intended to support.

  ## Ropedia完成2,200萬美元Pre-A輪融資，擴大實體AI資料基礎建設

  總部位於新加坡的Ropedia宣布完成2,200萬美元（約新幣2,840萬元）Pre-A輪融資，累計募資總額達3,000萬美元（約新幣3,870萬元）。此次融資將用於擴展該公司的資料基礎建設平台，為機器人與具身AI開發者提供真實世界的人類互動資料集。

  本輪融資由深耕東南亞AI、深科技與基礎建設領域的創投機構領投。公司表示，先前的融資輪次吸引了與Google、Andreessen Horowitz（a16z）、輝達及亞馬遜相關的天使投資人與投資機構共同參與。

  ## Ropedia的產品與服務

  Ropedia深耕實體AI技術棧的資料蒐集與處理層——這一領域專注於打造能與真實世界互動的AI系統，例如工業機器人與人形機器。該公司認為，實體AI的發展正面臨一道制約瓶頸，與過去幾波技術浪潮如出一轍：正如雲端運算需要資料中心，大型語言模型仰賴網路上的文字語料，實體智慧同樣需要大量在真實環境中蒐集的高品質人類互動資料。

  Ropedia的平台涵蓋完整的資料處理流程。透過自行研發的可穿戴硬體，Ropedia能擷取人類執行各項任務時的第一人稱視角影像、深度資訊、動作數據與音訊。這些資料經過同步與處理後，以兩種形式交付給客戶：一是「Xperience-10M」——公司描述其為全球最大規模的人類體驗資料集之一；二是依據特定機器人或具身AI應用需求量身打造的客製化「資料即服務」（DaaS）方案。

  Ropedia表示，其方法相較傳統資料蒐集方式可降低最多50倍的成本。公司指出，其可穿戴式擷取裝置已進入量產階段，以支援更大規模的部署需求。目前，Ropedia已服務北美、中國及新加坡地區逾20家機器人與基礎模型公司。

  ## 創辦團隊與學術淵源

  Ropedia是新加坡南洋理工大學（NTU）的深科技衍生新創，公司形容這樣的孵育模式在亞洲相當罕見。三位創辦人擁有交疊的學術與職涯背景。執行長陳兆熙與技術長洪方舟均畢業於中國清華大學——陳兆熙主修自動化，洪方舟主修軟體工程——兩人隨後皆在南洋理工大學的MMLab研究生態系完成博士學位，研究方向涵蓋3D感知、人體重建、空間智慧與第一人稱視角視覺。兩人亦曾至Meta Reality Labs完成研究實習，從事虛擬化身技術、第一人稱感知及以人為中心的空間運算相關工作。

  首席科學家劉子維曾任陳兆熙與洪方舟的博士指導教授，現為南洋理工大學副教授暨電腦視覺研究員。劉子維本科就讀於中國華中科技大學，並於香港中文大學取得博士學位。

  ## 資金用途與擴張計畫

  Ropedia表示，此次新募資金將集中投入四大方向：擴大全球資料蒐集業務、擴充新加坡與美國的團隊規模、提升可穿戴式擷取硬體的製造產能以支援更大規模的裝置部署，以及持續強化資料平台，包括標注工具、品質分析與法規合規基礎建設。公司亦計畫擴大AI研究團隊在資料基礎模型與世界模型方面的研究工作。

  ## 產業意義

  本輪融資反映出投資界對於解決實體AI資料瓶頸問題的高度興趣。儘管機器人領域的軟體與模型技術已快速推進，但在規模化、多元化的真實世界人類互動資料供給方面，仍是制約模型泛化能力的關鍵因素——所謂泛化能力，是指AI系統在受控環境中訓練後，能否在各種真實世界條件下穩定發揮作用。透過擴大資料集的規模與地理多樣性，Ropedia期望協助客戶建構出能在實驗室以外、真正走入工廠、職場與家庭環境的AI模型。

  該公司計畫持續拓展北美、中國及新加坡的客戶群，並藉助新一輪融資所支持的擴大部署，提升Xperience-10M資料集的資料量與任務多樣性。
