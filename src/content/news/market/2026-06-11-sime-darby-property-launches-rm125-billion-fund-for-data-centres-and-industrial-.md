body_en: |
  Sime Darby Property Berhad has launched a RM1.25 billion (US$300 million) closed-end fund to develop build-to-suit data centres and industrial assets inside its Malaysian townships, drawing full capital commitments from three institutional investors at the fund's first close.

  The fund, named New Economy Venture, is Shariah-compliant, structured to conform with Islamic finance principles, and carries a five-year life. Its investors are the Employees Provident Fund (EPF), Malaysia's national retirement savings fund; the Armed Forces Fund Board (Lembaga Tabung Angkatan Tentera, or LTAT); and Great Eastern Life Assurance (Malaysia) Berhad.

  ## Capital structure

  Sime Darby Property is acting as general partner and has committed RM500.1 million as a co-investor at first close. The remaining commitments within the main fund are:

  - LTAT: RM200 million
  - Great Eastern: RM199.9 million
  - EPF: RM100 million

  EPF has also committed RM250 million to a sidecar vehicle, a separate fund that runs alongside the main vehicle to invest in specific projects. The main fund and sidecar together reach the RM1.25 billion total. The fund may admit additional limited partners at a later stage, the company said.

  Great Eastern is a returning participant, having invested in Sime Darby Property's earlier Industrial Development Fund. The company cited its re-participation as an affirmation of the developer's record in originating and managing institutional real estate assets.

  ## Seed assets and timeline

  New Economy Venture has secured two seed assets within Sime Darby Property's Elmina Business Park and City of Elmina, both located in its flagship integrated township. Together they represent approximately 85 percent of the fund's target size and are underpinned by long-term leases. Construction of both assets is expected to be completed in the second half of 2027.

  ## Background and strategy

  New Economy Venture is Sime Darby Property's second institutional real estate fund. Its first, the Industrial Development Fund, was launched in 2022 as a joint venture with LOGOS Property and targeted RM1 billion in industrial assets. New Economy Venture broadens that model to include data centres alongside industrial and logistics developments.

  Sime Darby Property's investment and asset management division currently oversees approximately RM4.4 billion in assets under management, including a long-term hyperscale data centre lease that came into operation in April 2026. In a hyperscale lease, a single large tenant occupies an entire facility.

  Group Managing Director and Chief Executive Datuk Seri Azmir Merican said the fund would enable Sime Darby Property to diversify its recurring income through investment yield and fee-based income from data centre and warehouse developments. The fund forms part of the company's SHIFT32 strategy, which centres on institutionalising investment management capabilities and growing the group's recurring income base.

  The launch channels committed institutional capital into Malaysia's data centre and industrial property pipeline. Both asset classes are in high demand in the country's real estate market, the company said. By drawing on third-party institutional capital rather than its own balance sheet, Sime Darby Property described the structure as a capital-light approach to accelerating development within its townships.

  Construction of the two seed assets is scheduled for completion in the second half of 2027.
body_zh: |
  實達置地（Sime Darby Property Berhad）宣布推出規模達12.5億令吉（約3億美元）的封閉型基金，用以在其馬來西亞城鎮內開發量身打造的資料中心與工業資產，並於基金首次關閉時獲得三家法人投資機構的全額資本承諾。

  這檔基金名為「新經濟創投」（New Economy Venture），符合伊斯蘭教法，採伊斯蘭金融架構，存續期間為五年。投資人包括：馬來西亞國家退休儲蓄機構僱員公積金局（EPF）、武裝部隊基金局（Lembaga Tabung Angkatan Tentera，簡稱LTAT），以及英保大人壽保險（馬來西亞）有限公司（Great Eastern Life Assurance (Malaysia) Berhad）。

  ## 資本結構

  實達置地擔任普通合夥人，並於首次關閉時以共同投資人身份承諾出資5億100萬令吉。主基金其餘承諾出資額如下：

  - LTAT：2億令吉
  - 英保大：1億9,990萬令吉
  - 僱員公積金局：1億令吉

  僱員公積金局另外向附屬車輛（sidecar vehicle）承諾出資2億5,000萬令吉，該附屬基金與主基金並行運作，專門投資特定項目。主基金與附屬車輛合計達到12.5億令吉的總規模。公司表示，基金後續可能引入其他有限合夥人。

  英保大曾參與實達置地早前推出的「工業發展基金」（Industrial Development Fund），此次再度入股，被公司引述為對其在法人不動產資產的開發與管理能力的肯定。

  ## 種子資產與時程

  「新經濟創投」已在實達置地旗艦整合城鎮內的埃爾米納商業園區（Elmina Business Park）及埃爾米納城（City of Elmina）取得兩項種子資產，合計約占基金目標規模的85%，並以長期租約作為支撐。兩項資產的建設預計於2027年下半年竣工。

  ## 背景與策略

  「新經濟創投」是實達置地推出的第二檔法人不動產基金。第一檔「工業發展基金」於2022年以與LOGOS Property合資的形式設立，目標投資規模為10億令吉的工業資產。「新經濟創投」在此基礎上擴大範疇，將資料中心納入工業及物流開發之外的投資項目。

  實達置地的投資與資產管理部門目前管理約44億令吉的資產，其中包括一筆於2026年4月正式投入營運的超大規模資料中心長期租賃案。所謂超大規模租賃，是指由單一大型租戶獨占整棟設施。

  集團執行董事暨執行長拿督斯里阿茲米爾·梅里坎（Datuk Seri Azmir Merican）表示，這檔基金將使實達置地得以透過資料中心和倉儲開發所帶來的投資收益及費用型收入，多元化其經常性收入來源。該基金亦是公司「SHIFT32」策略的一環，其核心目標在於推動投資管理能力的機構化，並擴大集團的經常性收入基礎。

  此次基金的推出，將法人機構的承諾資本注入馬來西亞資料中心與工業地產的開發管線。公司表示，上述兩類資產在馬來西亞不動產市場均需求旺盛。藉由引入第三方法人資本而非動用自有資產負債表，實達置地將此架構定位為在旗下城鎮內加速開發的「輕資本」模式。

  兩項種子資產的建設工程預計於2027年下半年完工。
