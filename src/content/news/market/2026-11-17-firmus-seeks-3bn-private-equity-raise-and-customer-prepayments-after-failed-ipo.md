body_en: |
  Australian neocloud Firmus is seeking $3 billion in private equity and advance payments from major customers including Nvidia, OpenAI, and Meta, after abandoning a planned stock market listing it attributed to market volatility, the Australian Financial Review reported on November 16, 2026.

  ## Private raise and customer prepayments

  The fundraising effort pairs an equity round with prepayments from some of Firmus' largest customers. Those advance payments could finance up to 12.5 percent of Firmus' contracts currently under development, the AFR reported, citing three anonymous sources with knowledge of the plan. Firmus declined to comment to the AFR.

  A neocloud is a company that builds and operates large-scale AI computing infrastructure for rent. As of September 2026, Firmus had 916 megawatts (MW) of contracted capacity under customer agreements, but only 46 MW was operational, leaving the bulk of its committed pipeline still to be built.

  ## Failed IPO and prior private funding

  Firmus previously attempted an initial public offering (IPO), which would have listed its shares on a public stock exchange. That launch did not proceed; the company blamed market volatility. Firmus' most recent private funding round raised $2 billion, giving the company a post-money valuation of $10.5 billion.

  ## Sector-wide shift toward prepayments

  Prepayments have become an increasingly prominent funding tool across the neocloud sector, as Data Center Dynamics noted that "the once seemingly endless well of private capital has begun to dry up."

  Both CoreWeave and Nebius, two listed neoclouds, addressed the funding environment in quarterly earnings released the same week. CoreWeave chief executive Mike Intrator described "recent volatility in the capital markets." The company said more than 75 percent of its committed capital expenditure (capex) — infrastructure spending already committed to contracts — is tied to investment-grade and enterprise customers, with the remainder linked to leading AI laboratories and AI-native companies. CoreWeave said the structure of those customer commitments allows it to finance between 70 and 100 percent of the associated capex.

  Nebius reported that deals signed in its most recent quarter carried prepayments sufficient to cover 100 percent of the associated capex. Chief financial officer Maria del Dado Alonso Sanchez described a flexible approach to capital allocation: "If the terms in the capital market are not attractive, we can allocate more capacity to the long-term contracts. If, on the other hand, we have the availability to raise capital at attractive terms, we will be very opportunistic about it, and we will shift more of those allocations."

  ## What it means for the sector

  The pattern across Firmus, CoreWeave, and Nebius signals a structural shift in how AI infrastructure companies fund construction: customer prepayments are absorbing a larger share of the capital burden as public and private equity markets become more selective. For Firmus, completing the $3 billion raise and securing advance customer payments would fund the buildout of a contracted pipeline that stood at 916 MW as of September 2026, against 46 MW in operation.

  ## 私募融資與客戶預付款

  此輪融資計畫結合了股權募資與大客戶預付款兩條路線。《澳洲金融評論報》援引三名匿名知情人士的說法指出，預付款最多可為Firmus目前在建合約的12.5%提供資金。Firmus對此拒絕回應《澳洲金融評論報》的採訪請求。

  新世代雲端運算商（neocloud）是指專門建設並營運大規模AI運算基礎設施、以租賃方式對外提供服務的企業。截至2026年9月，Firmus已與客戶簽訂合約，取得916百萬瓦（MW）的承諾容量，但目前實際投入運營的僅有46 MW，絕大部分已承諾的建設管線尚待完工。

  ## IPO受挫與先前私募融資歷程

  Firmus此前曾嘗試進行首次公開募股（IPO），將股票在公開交易所掛牌上市，但該計畫最終未能成行，公司將原因歸結於市場波動。Firmus最近一輪私募融資籌得20億美元，使公司投後估值達到105億美元。

  ## 業界廣泛轉向預付款模式

  預付款作為融資工具，在新世代雲端運算商領域的應用日益普遍。《資料中心動態》（Data Center Dynamics）指出，「曾經看似源源不絕的私募資本水井，已開始逐漸乾涸。」

  同週，兩家已掛牌上市的新世代雲端運算商CoreWeave與Nebius在各自的季度財報說明中，也觸及了融資環境的問題。CoreWeave執行長麥克・英特拉托（Mike Intrator）談及「資本市場近期的波動」。該公司表示，其已承諾合約資本支出（capex）中，逾75%與投資等級及企業客戶掛鉤，其餘則與領先的AI實驗室及AI原生企業相關。CoreWeave指出，客戶承諾的結構安排，使其得以為相關資本支出籌措70%至100%的資金。

  Nebius則報告，本季度簽訂的合約所附帶的預付款，已足以支應相關資本支出的100%。財務長瑪麗亞・德爾・達多・阿隆索・桑切斯（Maria del Dado Alonso Sanchez）描述了一套靈活的資本配置策略：「若資本市場的條件不夠理想，我們可以將更多容量分配給長期合約；反之，若有機會以優惠條件募集資金，我們就會積極把握機遇，相應地調整配置比例。」

  ## 對業界的啟示

  Firmus、CoreWeave與Nebius呈現出的共同模式，預示著AI基礎設施企業籌資建設的方式正出現結構性轉變：隨著公開及私募股權市場變得更加挑剔，客戶預付款正承擔起越來越大的資本籌措責任。對Firmus而言，若能順利完成30億美元融資並取得客戶預付款，將為其建設管線的後續工程提供資金支撐——截至2026年9月，該管線承諾容量為916 MW，而目前實際運營的僅有46 MW。
