body_en: |
  Racks Central, a Singapore-headquartered data center platform, has secured an undisclosed sum from the China ASEAN Investment Cooperation Fund II (CAF II), a US$1 billion vehicle advised by ESR, a real asset owner and manager, to finance its expansion across Southeast Asia.

  The investment marks a significant capital commitment to Racks Central's regional growth strategy, beginning with a large-scale development in Johor, Malaysia, where the company is building what it describes as an AI factory comprising four developments with a combined capacity of up to 510 megawatts (MW). For context, 510 MW is roughly equivalent to the output of a mid-sized power station and represents a substantial step up from Racks Central's current footprint: the company presently operates a 12 MW colocation data center in Singapore.

  ## ESR's role and the CAF II fund

  ESR acts as sub-advisor to CAF II, which has a total capitalization of US$1 billion. ESR describes itself as having a data center development pipeline of over 3 gigawatts (GW) across Asia-Pacific. The firm's co-founder and co-CEO, Jeffrey Shen, said the rapid acceleration of artificial intelligence (AI) adoption and digital transformation is reshaping data center requirements across the region, and that the investment would support Racks Central's growth into a scalable regional platform serving hyperscale and enterprise customers.

  ## Expansion focus and stated ambitions

  According to Racks Central's press release, the capital will enable the company to develop AI-ready and large-scale data centers across Southeast Asia. Bobby Wee, founder and CEO of Racks Central, said the partnership with ESR and CAF II would support the company's goal of building what he called an AI-ready data center corridor across the region, enabling customers to scale as digital demand grows.

  The Johor development is identified as the starting point for this expansion. Johor, located at the southern tip of peninsular Malaysia directly across the strait from Singapore, has attracted significant data center investment in recent years given its land availability and proximity to Singapore's established connectivity infrastructure.

  ## Significance

  The transaction illustrates continued capital flows from China-linked investment vehicles into Southeast Asian digital infrastructure, channeled through established real asset managers. For Racks Central, the deal represents a move from operating a single-site colocation facility to pursuing a multi-country, hyperscale-capable platform. The Johor project alone, at up to 510 MW across four developments, would position the company among the larger data center developers in the subregion if completed as planned.

  ## Racks Central獲中國東協基金注資，矢志打造東南亞AI資料中心走廊

  總部設於新加坡的資料中心平台Racks Central，日前宣布獲得「中國東協投資合作基金II」（CAF II）的策略性投資，金額未予公開。CAF II為一支規模達10億美元的基金，由不動產資產管理公司ESR擔任顧問，此次注資將用於支持Racks Central在東南亞全區的擴張佈局。

  此筆投資標誌著外部資本對Racks Central區域成長策略的重大承諾，首站鎖定馬來西亞柔佛州。Racks Central計畫在當地打造一座「AI工廠」，整體由四個開發案組成，合計容量最高可達510百萬瓦（MW）。作為參考基準，510MW約相當於一座中型發電廠的輸出規模，對照Racks Central目前在新加坡僅12MW的主機代管（colocation）資料中心，此番擴張幅度可謂相當可觀。

  ## ESR與CAF II基金的角色

  ESR在CAF II基金中擔任次顧問（sub-advisor）一職；該基金總資本規模達10億美元。ESR表示，其在亞太地區的資料中心開發管線已超過3百萬瓦（GW）。ESR共同創辦人暨共同執行長沈晉初（Jeffrey Shen）表示，人工智慧（AI）的快速普及與數位轉型正重塑整個區域對資料中心的需求格局，而此次投資將支持Racks Central成長為一個可擴展的區域性平台，為超大規模（hyperscale）及企業客戶提供服務。

  ## 擴張重心與發展藍圖

  根據Racks Central的新聞稿，這筆資金將使公司得以在東南亞全區開發具備AI就緒能力的大型資料中心。Racks Central創辦人暨執行長Bobby Wee表示，與ESR及CAF II的合作夥伴關係，將助力公司實現其目標——在東南亞建立一條「AI就緒資料中心走廊」，讓客戶能隨數位需求成長而彈性擴展規模。

  柔佛州被定位為此次擴張的起點。柔佛州位於馬來半島最南端，隔著柔佛海峽與新加坡遙遙相對，近年來已吸引大量資料中心投資進駐，主要得益於當地土地供應充裕，以及鄰近新加坡完善連線基礎設施的地緣優勢。

  ## 交易意義

  此次交易進一步印證了中國相關投資工具持續透過成熟的不動產資產管理公司，將資金挹注於東南亞數位基礎設施的趨勢。對Racks Central而言，這筆交易代表公司從單一據點的主機代管業者，躍升為追求跨國、具備超大規模能力平台的重要轉折。光是柔佛州的開發案，四個案場合計最高達510MW的容量，若能如期完成，將使Racks Central躋身該次區域規模最大的資料中心開發商之列。
