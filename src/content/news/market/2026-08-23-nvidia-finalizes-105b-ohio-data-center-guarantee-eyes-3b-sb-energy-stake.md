body_en: |
  Nvidia has finalized a guarantee of up to $105 billion to support financing for OpenAI's planned AI data center campus in Pike County, Ohio, according to securities filings disclosed August 17, while the chipmaker is separately in discussions to invest as much as $3 billion in SB Energy, the SoftBank Group subsidiary that will build and operate the facility.

  ## How the guarantee figure was revised

  The $105 billion ceiling confirmed in the securities documents is lower than figures reported at earlier stages of the deal. The Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia had revised its plans to guarantee less than $120 billion, itself down from $250 billion previously discussed; the final filings settled the ceiling at $105 billion. The reduction came in response to investor concerns about the extent of risk Nvidia was assuming, according to Commercial Times.

  ## Project scope and schedule

  The campus is initially planned at 4.25 gigawatts (GW) of computing capacity, with an option to add a further 3.75 GW, bringing potential total capacity to 8 GW. SB Energy and its partners plan to supply 10 GW of electricity for the project and will invest at least $4.2 billion to upgrade the local power grid. Facilities are expected to come online in phases starting in 2028, with Nvidia supplying the AI computing hardware.

  OpenAI will use the campus under a 20-year lease. SB Energy will handle construction and management.

  ## Nvidia's proposed SB Energy equity stake

  Separate from the guarantee, Nvidia is in talks to acquire an equity stake in SB Energy worth as much as $3 billion, the Information reported, citing people familiar with the discussions. Under the structure being discussed, Nvidia would invest $1.5 billion when the Ohio deal is formally signed and the remaining $1.5 billion through SB Energy's planned initial public offering (IPO) — the process by which a private company sells shares to the public for the first time. Commercial Times reported Nvidia's planned equity investment at $1.5 billion.

  SB Energy is aiming to go public as soon as September and could raise at least $5 billion in the offering, the Information said. The company was founded in 2019, is also backed by OpenAI, and develops large-scale power and data center infrastructure.

  ## Job projections and computing demand

  OpenAI has estimated the Ohio project will generate 35,000 construction-related jobs by 2032 and approximately 2,500 permanent positions. OpenAI President Greg Brockman, in recent media interviews, described computing power as the "basic resource" of the AI industry and compared it to oil, characterizing it as an increasingly scarce input.

  Nvidia Chief Executive Jensen Huang said the company's aim is to give OpenAI access to long-term infrastructure it can deploy at scale and upgrade as successive technology generations become available, improving both computing capacity and the cost efficiency of AI models.

  ## Forward outlook

  Nvidia's concurrent roles as financial guarantor, hardware supplier, and proposed equity holder in SB Energy give the chipmaker a stake across the financial, operational, and technical layers of the Ohio project. SB Energy's September IPO target, with a potential raise of at least $5 billion, will serve as a near-term test of investor appetite for dedicated AI infrastructure at this scale.

  ## 擔保金額的修訂過程

  證券文件確認的1,050億美元上限，低於先前各階段洽談所傳的數字。《華爾街日報》曾報導，輝達已將擔保計畫修訂為不超過1,200億美元，而更早之前討論的金額曾高達2,500億美元；最終申報文件將上限敲定在1,050億美元。據《工商時報》報導，此次金額下調，係因應投資人對輝達所承擔風險幅度的疑慮。

  ## 專案規模與時程

  該園區初期規劃算力容量為4.25吉瓦（GW），並設有額外擴增3.75 GW的選項，潛在總容量最高可達8 GW。SB Energy及其合作夥伴計畫為此專案供應10 GW電力，並將投入至少42億美元升級當地電網。設施預計自2028年起分階段啟用，AI運算硬體將由輝達供應。

  OpenAI將以20年租約使用該園區，建設與管理工作則由SB Energy負責。

  ## 輝達擬入股SB Energy

  《The Information》引述知情人士報導，輝達正洽談以最高30億美元取得SB Energy股權，此舉與前述融資擔保屬不同性質的安排。據悉，雙方討論中的架構為：俄亥俄州合約正式簽署時，輝達投入15億美元；其餘15億美元則透過SB Energy計畫中的首次公開募股（IPO）——即私人公司首度向公眾公開發行股票的程序——完成投入。《工商時報》則報導，輝達擬投入的股權金額為15億美元。

  SB Energy最快有意於今年9月掛牌上市，募資規模可能至少達50億美元。該公司成立於2019年，同時獲OpenAI支持，主要業務為大規模電力及資料中心基礎設施的開發。

  ## 就業預測與算力需求

  OpenAI估計，俄亥俄州此一專案將於2032年前創造3萬5,000個建設相關工作機會，以及約2,500個永久職位。OpenAI總裁葛雷格·布羅克曼（Greg Brockman）近期接受多家媒體訪問時，將算力形容為AI產業的「基本資源」，並類比為石油，稱其正成為一種日益稀缺的生產要素。

  輝達執行長黃仁勳表示，公司目標是讓OpenAI取得可大規模部署、並隨每一個技術世代持續升級的長期基礎設施，從而提升算力容量，並改善AI模型的成本效益。

  ## 前景展望

  輝達在俄亥俄州專案中同時扮演融資擔保方、硬體供應商，以及SB Energy擬議股東三重角色，使其在該案的財務、營運與技術各層面均具直接利害關係。SB Energy以今年9月為上市目標、潛在募資規模至少50億美元，將成為近期檢驗投資人對這一規模專屬AI基礎設施胃口的重要試金石。
