body_en: |
  Telkom Indonesia's NeutraDC Nxera Batam joint venture data center has contracted all available capacity in its first building, BTM-1, before the facility is scheduled to open in 2026, the state-owned telecoms operator announced June 5, and has begun planning a second building, BTM-2, at the same site.

  The full absorption of BTM-1 before commercial operations begin pushed Telkom, through its data center operating company NeutraDC (formally PT Telkom Data Ekosistem), to start preparing BTM-2. The Batam campus is planned for a total capacity of 100 megawatts (MW) across its phased development.

  Gorilla Technology, a London-headquartered provider of artificial intelligence (AI), security, and data analytics solutions with operations across Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and the Americas, has signed an agreement to use capacity at BTM-1 to support AI infrastructure and digital service deployments across Asia, the company said. The signing took place in Bangkok, Thailand, in May 2026, with NeutraDC Group CEO Andreuw Th.A.F and Gorilla Technology Chairman and CEO Jay Chandan present at the ceremony.

  Telkom's data center business generated Rp399 billion (Indonesian rupiah) in revenue in the first quarter of 2026, the company said.

  ## SIJORI corridor demand

  The Batam site serves the SIJORI corridor — the Singapore-Johor-Riau Islands sub-regional economic zone — where the company said data center capacity demand is high and growing. NeutraDC Nxera Batam CEO Indrama YM Purba cited Batam's strong international connectivity, proximity to regional economic centers, and a developing investment environment as the factors driving demand.

  "We see very high data center capacity needs in the SIJORI area. This is the basis for plans to develop further capacity in Batam," Purba said.

  ## TLKM 30 strategy context

  Telkom frames the BTM-1 sellout as evidence of progress under its TLKM 30 strategic blueprint, which identifies digital infrastructure as one of the company's main pillars of growth. Telkom Director of Strategic Business Development and Portfolio Seno Soemadji said high international partner interest in Batam shows Indonesia is becoming a meaningful node in the regional digital ecosystem.

  "As cloud, AI, and digital services demand increases in Southeast Asia, we see digital infrastructure as one of the main foundations for future digital economic growth. The high market demand for BTM-1 is a positive signal for Telkom to continue development through BTM-2," Seno said.

  NeutraDC Group CEO Andreuw said securing Gorilla Technology as a tenant was an important milestone in accelerating globally-standardized data center development in Indonesia, and that the high market interest validated the facility's infrastructure readiness and service quality.

  ## Sustainability and local development

  The 100 MW Batam campus is being built with energy-efficient technology and global operational governance standards, Telkom said. The company projected that the development will create local employment, develop digital talent in Batam, and strengthen the city's position as a technology investment destination.

  With BTM-1 fully contracted before its opening and BTM-2 planning underway, the NeutraDC Nxera Batam campus is intended to serve AI, cloud, and digital services demand across the SIJORI region and the broader Southeast Asian market, Telkom said. The company has not disclosed a capacity figure or timeline for BTM-2.

  印尼國營電信商Telkom Indonesia旗下NeutraDC Nxera巴淡島合資資料中心，已在第一棟建築BTM-1預計於2026年開幕前，將全部可用容量完成簽約。Telkom於6月5日宣布此消息，並已著手規劃位於同一園區的第二棟建築BTM-2。

  BTM-1在正式商業運營啟動前即被完全消化，促使Telkom透過旗下資料中心營運公司NeutraDC（正式名稱為PT Telkom Data Ekosistem）展開BTM-2的籌備工作。巴淡島園區按分期開發計畫，總容量規劃達100百萬瓦（MW）。

  人工智慧（AI）、資安及數據分析解決方案供應商Gorilla Technology，總部設於倫敦，業務遍及亞洲、中東、歐洲及美洲，已簽署協議，使用BTM-1的容量，以支援其在亞洲的AI基礎設施與數位服務部署。簽約儀式於2026年5月在泰國曼谷舉行，NeutraDC集團執行長Andreuw Th.A.F與Gorilla Technology董事長暨執行長Jay Chandan均親赴現場出席典禮。

  Telkom資料中心業務在2026年第一季創下3,990億印尼盾的營收。

  ## SIJORI走廊需求

  巴淡島園區服務SIJORI走廊——即新加坡－柔佛－廖內群島次區域經濟圈——該公司表示，此走廊的資料中心容量需求強勁且持續成長。NeutraDC Nxera巴淡島執行長Indrama YM Purba指出，巴淡島優越的國際連網能力、緊鄰區域經濟中心的地理位置，以及日趨成熟的投資環境，是帶動需求的關鍵因素。

  「我們在SIJORI地區看到非常高的資料中心容量需求，這正是我們規劃在巴淡島進一步擴充容量的基礎，」Purba說道。

  ## TLKM 30策略脈絡

  Telkom將BTM-1的完售視為旗下TLKM 30戰略藍圖推進有成的具體佐證；TLKM 30將數位基礎設施列為公司主要成長支柱之一。Telkom策略業務發展與投資組合董事Seno Soemadji表示，國際合作夥伴對巴淡島的高度關注，顯示印尼正成為區域數位生態系中舉足輕重的節點。

  「隨著東南亞對雲端、AI及數位服務的需求持續攀升，我們將數位基礎設施視為未來數位經濟成長的重要基石之一。BTM-1的高度市場需求，是Telkom持續透過BTM-2推進發展的正面訊號，」Seno說道。

  NeutraDC集團執行長Andreuw表示，成功將Gorilla Technology納為承租戶，是加速印尼資料中心達到全球標準的重要里程碑，而市場的高度興趣也驗證了該設施在基礎設施準備度與服務品質上的實力。

  ## 永續發展與在地培育

  巴淡島100 MW園區採用節能技術及全球營運治理標準進行興建，Telkom表示。公司預期此開發案將創造在地就業機會、培育巴淡島的數位人才，並鞏固該城市作為科技投資目的地的地位。

  隨著BTM-1在開幕前即完成全數簽約，BTM-2的規劃工作也同步展開，NeutraDC Nxera巴淡島園區旨在服務SIJORI地區及更廣大東南亞市場的AI、雲端及數位服務需求，Telkom表示。公司目前尚未揭露BTM-2的容量數字或時程安排。
