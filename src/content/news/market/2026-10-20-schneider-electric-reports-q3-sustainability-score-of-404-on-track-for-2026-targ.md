body_en: |
  Schneider Electric's sustainability impact score reached 4.04 at the end of the third quarter of 2026, the company reported, keeping it on track for its full-year target of 4.20 out of 10 under its Impact 2030 roadmap.

  The score aggregates progress across four areas: the company's own operations, supply chain decarbonization, workforce and skills programmes, and community initiatives. The nine-month update covers the period since the beginning of 2026.

  ## Own operations and customer impact

  Schneider Electric said it has cut its Scope 1 and 2 carbon emissions — those arising directly from its operations and purchased energy — by 83% against a 2017 baseline. During the first nine months of 2026, the company's products and services helped customers save or electrify 211 million megawatt-hours of energy.

  Cumulatively since 2018, those products and services have helped customers save and avoid 943 million tonnes of CO2 emissions, according to the company.

  ## Supply chain decarbonization

  The supply chain was the fastest-moving area in the quarter. The number of suppliers qualifying under Schneider Electric's Zero Carbon Pathway programme — which asks participants to quantify operational emissions, set reduction targets, and calculate indirect emissions through product carbon footprints or company-level Scope 3 reporting — rose from 15 at the end of the first half of 2026 to 135 by the end of the third quarter. The company's target is 1,500 qualifying suppliers by 2030.

  To support suppliers working toward qualification, Schneider Electric deployed AI-based digital tools to more than 1,200 suppliers during 2026. It also ran 10 supplier bootcamps across nine cities on three continents, alongside more than 250 live coaching sessions and approximately 35 on-site reviews.

  On materials, the company said responsible materials — a category covering steel, aluminium, thermoplastics, electronics, electrical components, and packaging — accounted for 22% of the areas covered by the programme, up from a 19% baseline recorded in 2025.

  Circular services, which cover customer-owned equipment receiving maintenance, spare parts, take-back, and refurbishment, increased 1.2 times from the end of 2025. Schneider Electric has set a target of doubling that activity by 2030.

  ## Workforce training and access programmes

  Schneider Electric said 82% of its employees completed designated 2026 Energy Tech upskilling courses in the first nine months of the year. That follows an AI training programme that reached more than 142,000 employees in 2025, according to the company.

  Beyond its own workforce, Schneider Electric said it has helped 69 million people gain access to sustainable electricity since 2009, adding 1.9 million people compared with the first-half total. It has also trained 1.5 million young people in skills related to electrification and sustainability since 2009, adding 90,000 since the first-half report. Its target is 3 million by 2030.

  ## Site-level and community programmes

  At the site level, the company's Impact Workplaces initiative — which measures climate, resource efficiency, people, and community factors at individual facilities — expanded from five sites at the end of the first half to 59 by the end of the third quarter. Schneider Electric's target is 100 such workplaces by 2030.

  Employee volunteering also increased. The company said 9% of employees are now engaged as Change Agents, with more than 6,000 additional employees completing a volunteering activity during the quarter. The programme extended its geographic reach from 49 to 57 countries.

  ## What the results mean

  The nine-month figures show Schneider Electric making measurable progress across all four pillars of Impact 2030 with one quarter remaining in the year. The supply chain results are particularly significant for the broader industry: a near-tenfold increase in qualifying suppliers in a single quarter, from 15 to 135, indicates that the company's combination of AI tools, coaching, and bootcamps is beginning to move its supplier base toward structured emissions accounting at scale. Whether that pace can be sustained toward the 2030 target of 1,500 qualifying suppliers remains a key variable.

  "The strong results we are seeing nine months into Impact 2030 reinforce our conviction that advancing Energy Tech can power progress for all," said Esther Finidori, Chief Sustainability Officer at Schneider Electric. "From scaling electrification and supplier decarbonization, to investing in skills and strengthening the role of our people and sites in their local communities, we see momentum across multiple Impact 2030 programs."

  Schneider Electric's full-year 2026 sustainability score target remains 4.20 out of 10, with the fourth-quarter update expected to show whether the company closes the gap from its current 4.04 result.
body_zh: |
  施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）公布，2026年第三季末，其永續影響評分達到4.04分，依據「Impact 2030」永續路線圖，持續朝全年目標4.20分（滿分10分）穩步推進。

  此一評分匯整四大面向的進展：公司自身營運、供應鏈去碳化、人才培育與技能提升計畫，以及社區推廣項目。這份涵蓋九個月的進度報告，統計期間為2026年初至今。

  ## 自身營運與客戶影響

  施耐德電氣表示，相較於2017年基準年，其第一範疇（Scope 1）與第二範疇（Scope 2）碳排放——亦即直接來自公司營運及外購電力所產生的排放——已削減83%。2026年前九個月，該公司的產品與服務協助客戶節省或電氣化達2億1,100萬MWh的能源。

  自2018年以來累計，上述產品與服務已協助客戶節省並避免排放共9億4,300萬公噸的二氧化碳。

  ## 供應鏈去碳化

  供應鏈是本季進展最為迅速的領域。符合施耐德電氣「零碳路徑計畫」（Zero Carbon Pathway）資格的供應商數量，從2026年上半年末的15家，大幅躍升至第三季末的135家。該計畫要求參與廠商量化自身營運排放、設定減排目標，並透過產品碳足跡或公司層級第三範疇（Scope 3）報告計算間接排放。公司目標是在2030年前讓1,500家供應商取得資格。

  為協助供應商達到資格門檻，施耐德電氣在2026年向逾1,200家供應商部署了人工智慧數位工具，並在橫跨三大洲、九座城市舉辦了10場供應商培訓工作坊，另搭配逾250場線上輔導課程及約35次現場審查。

  在材料方面，該公司表示「負責任材料」——涵蓋鋼鐵、鋁、熱塑性塑料、電子元件、電氣元件及包裝材料——在計畫涵蓋範疇中佔比已達22%，較2025年所記錄的19%基準值有所提升。

  循環服務項目——涵蓋客戶自有設備的維護保養、零件供應、回收及翻新服務——相較2025年底成長1.2倍，施耐德電氣的目標是在2030年前將此業務規模倍增。

  ## 員工培訓與電力普及計畫

  施耐德電氣表示，2026年前九個月，82%的員工完成了指定的2026年能源科技技能提升課程。此前，公司於2025年推行的人工智慧培訓計畫已觸及逾14萬2,000名員工。

  在自身員工之外，施耐德電氣表示自2009年以來已協助6,900萬人獲得永續電力，較上半年數字新增190萬人。自2009年起，公司也已培訓150萬名年輕人習得與電氣化及永續發展相關的技能，較上半年報告新增9萬人。其目標是在2030年前累計達300萬人。

  ## 廠區與社區計畫

  在廠區層面，公司的「影響力工作場所」（Impact Workplaces）計畫——針對個別設施評量氣候表現、資源效率、員工關係及社區參與等面向——從上半年末的5個廠區擴展至第三季末的59個。施耐德電氣的目標是在2030年前建立100個此類工作場所。

  員工志工參與度也有所提升。公司表示，目前已有9%的員工擔任「變革推手」（Change Agents），本季另有逾6,000名員工完成志工活動。該計畫的地理覆蓋範圍也從49個國家擴展至57個國家。

  ## 數據意涵

  九個月的數據顯示，在全年僅剩最後一季的情況下，施耐德電氣在Impact 2030四大支柱上均取得了可衡量的進展。供應鏈的成果對整體產業而言尤具意義：單季內符合資格的供應商數量近乎十倍增長——從15家躍升至135家——顯示公司結合人工智慧工具、專業輔導及培訓工作坊的策略組合，已開始有效推動供應商在規模化排放核算體系的建立上取得突破。這樣的成長速度能否持續，進而朝向2030年1,500家供應商的目標邁進，仍是一大關鍵變數。

  施耐德電氣首席永續長（Chief Sustainability Officer）Esther Finidori表示：「我們在Impact 2030執行九個月後所看到的亮眼成果，更加堅定了我們的信念——推動能源科技的進步，能為所有人創造向前的動力。從擴大電氣化規模與供應商去碳化，到投資技能培育，並強化我們的員工及廠區在當地社區所扮演的角色，我們在Impact 2030多項計畫中都看到了強勁的前進動能。」

  施耐德電氣2026年全年永續評分目標維持在滿分10分中的4.20分，第四季進度更新預計將揭示公司能否縮短與目前4.04分之間的差距。
