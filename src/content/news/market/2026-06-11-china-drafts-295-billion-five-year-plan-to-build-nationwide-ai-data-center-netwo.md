body_en: |
  Chinese government agencies are drafting a plan to spend approximately two trillion yuan ($295 billion) over the next five years building a nationwide network of interconnected data centers, with a requirement that at least 80 percent of key technology — including artificial intelligence chips — come from domestic suppliers, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg.

  The National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC), China's top economic planning body, is among the agencies working on the blueprint, the people said. State-owned carriers China Mobile and China Telecom would operate the bulk of the facilities and ensure interoperability across the network, one of the people said. The plan is explicitly designed to rely on domestic suppliers such as Huawei Technologies for AI chips, effectively displacing Nvidia and Advanced Micro Devices from a significant share of what would become one of the largest computing infrastructure buildouts in history.

  Shares of major Chinese data center operators rose sharply after Bloomberg published its report. GDS Holdings climbed as much as 12 percent in pre-market US trading, while Vnet Group rose 17 percent.

  ## Funding and Structure

  The two trillion yuan would be raised primarily through sovereign debt, including ultra-long-term special government bonds with maturities of more than 10 years, as well as state-backed funds for strategic industry investment, the people said. Bank loans and private capital would supplement those sources. The figure does not include separate spending by private technology companies such as Alibaba Group and Tencent Holdings.

  The blueprint remains in early discussions and details could change, the people said. The effort nonetheless signals Beijing's commitment to advanced technology investment even as government debt pressures have led to spending cuts in other areas, according to the report.

  ## Six Networks Programme

  The data center plan is a principal component of China's "Six Networks" (六网) infrastructure programme, which was announced in March 2026 and covers construction across six categories: water networks, a new power grid, computing networks, next-generation communications, urban underground pipe networks, and logistics networks. The Six Networks programme carries a total stated investment target of seven trillion yuan.

  Beyond the data centers themselves, China also plans to integrate a new power grid with the AI infrastructure project, the people said. That integration could push the total projected investment to at least five trillion yuan.

  ## Domestic Chip Ecosystem

  The 80-percent domestic sourcing target reflects a broader effort to reduce reliance on US semiconductors. In May, nine domestically produced AI chips — from companies including Huawei, Alibaba, Shanghai Biren Technology, and Moore Threads Technology — passed a security review conducted by a Chinese technology-security agency, opening the door to their wider use in sectors with higher security requirements, according to Bloomberg.

  Separately, Washington has agreed to permit Nvidia to sell its previous-generation H200 AI chips to Chinese customers, a significant easing of earlier export controls. The H200 is approximately one generation behind Nvidia's current Blackwell architecture. Shipments have not yet begun, Bloomberg reported, a sign that Beijing is growing more confident in substituting locally made hardware for some AI computing capacity.

  ## Scale and Comparisons

  The two trillion yuan figure is substantially smaller than US private-sector commitments. US technology leaders including Meta Platforms and Microsoft are setting aside a combined $725 billion for AI investment in 2026 alone, according to Bloomberg. Chinese data centers generally cost less to build and operate than American ones because of lower labor, component, and construction costs, as well as local government incentives, the report noted.

  ## Analyst View

  Charlie Dai, principal analyst at research firm Forrester Research, said a unified national computing network would pool fragmented regional resources and give enterprises broader access to high-performance computing. It would also accelerate AI model development and the expansion of agentic and physical AI services across industries, Dai said. "Elevating it to a national strategy ensures policy alignment, capital mobilisation," he said. Dai added that inland provinces are likely to attract more digital-industry investment and talent, and that enterprises in finance, manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics sectors would gain access to more affordable and flexible AI capacity.

  The NDRC, China's Ministry of Finance, China Mobile, and China Telecom did not respond to requests for comment, Bloomberg reported.

  ## Significance

  If executed, the plan would represent Beijing's most aggressive effort yet to establish a domestic AI computing foundation independent of US technology. Local infrastructure suppliers, led by Huawei, are positioned as the primary beneficiaries of procurement under the 80-percent domestic sourcing mandate. Private technology companies such as Alibaba and Tencent are not the primary targets of the investment, though it remains unclear how their existing data hubs would function alongside the envisioned state-run network.

  The broad goal is to connect China's currently fragmented data facilities into a cohesive national computing network by 2028, with applications across public-sector services including healthcare, transportation, and city management, according to the people familiar with the plan.

  ## 中國擬定兩兆人民幣五年計畫　打造全國AI資料中心網路

  據彭博社引述知情人士報導，中國政府機構正在起草一項計畫，預計在未來五年內投入約兩兆人民幣（約295億美元），建設一套覆蓋全國的互聯資料中心網路。根據該計畫，關鍵技術——包括人工智慧晶片在內——至少須有八成來自國內供應商。

  知情人士表示，中國最高經濟規劃機構國家發展改革委員會（發改委）是參與籌備這份藍圖的機構之一。其中一名知情人士指出，國有電信業者中國移動與中國電信將負責營運大部分設施，並確保整個網路的互通性。此計畫明確設計為以華為技術等國內供應商提供的AI晶片為主，實質上將把輝達與超微半導體（AMD）排除在這項堪稱史上最大規模運算基礎建設之外。

  彭博社發布報導後，中國主要資料中心營運商股價應聲大漲。GDS Holdings在美國盤前交易中一度飆升12%，Vnet Group更暴漲17%。

  ## 資金來源與架構

  知情人士表示，兩兆人民幣的資金將主要透過主權債務籌措，包括期限逾十年的超長期特別國債，以及國家支持的戰略產業投資基金。銀行貸款與民間資本則作為補充來源。值得注意的是，這一數字並不包含阿里巴巴集團、騰訊控股等私人科技公司另行投入的資本。

  知情人士表示，這份藍圖目前仍處於初步討論階段，細節可能有所調整。儘管如此，上述報導指出，此舉仍清楚顯示北京在政府債務壓力導致其他領域削減支出的情況下，依然堅定投入先進技術建設的決心。

  ## 「六網」建設計畫

  資料中心計畫是中國於2026年3月宣布的「六網」基礎建設計畫的核心組成部分。「六網」涵蓋六大類別的建設工程：水網、新型電網、算力網路、新一代通訊、城市地下管網與物流網路，整體投資目標達七兆人民幣。

  知情人士表示，除資料中心本身之外，中國還計畫將新型電網與AI基礎建設計畫整合。一旦納入電網投資，整體預估投資規模可能攀升至至少五兆人民幣。

  ## 國產晶片生態系

  八成國產採購目標，反映出中國更廣泛減少對美國半導體依賴的戰略布局。彭博社報導，今年5月，包括華為、阿里巴巴、上海壁仞科技與摩爾執行緒等企業所研發的九款國產AI晶片，通過中國資訊安全機構的安全審查，為其在更高安全需求的領域廣泛應用打開大門。

  另一方面，美國政府已同意允許輝達向中國客戶銷售上一代H200 AI晶片，這是對此前出口管制的一大鬆綁。H200在效能上約落後輝達現行Blackwell架構一個世代。彭博社報導指出，相關出貨尚未啟動，顯示北京對以國產硬體替代部分AI運算能力愈來愈有信心。

  ## 規模與比較

  兩兆人民幣的規模，與美國民間企業的投入相比仍顯遜色。據彭博社報導，Meta Platforms與微軟等美國科技巨頭，光是2026年的AI投資承諾合計便高達7,250億美元。報導也指出，中國資料中心的建設與營運成本普遍低於美國，原因在於勞動力、零組件及建設成本較低，加上地方政府提供的優惠措施。

  ## 分析師觀點

  調研機構Forrester Research首席分析師戴查理表示，統一的全國算力網路將整合目前分散於各地的區域資源，讓企業得以更廣泛取用高效能運算能力，同時也將加速AI模型開發，以及跨產業的智慧代理人（agentic AI）與實體AI服務擴展。他說：「將其提升至國家戰略層級，可確保政策方向一致、資本有效動員。」戴查理並補充，內陸省份可望吸引更多數位產業投資與人才，金融、製造、醫療及物流等產業的企業也將獲得更經濟實惠、更彈性的AI算力。

  據彭博社報導，發改委、財政部、中國移動及中國電信均未回應置評請求。

  ## 重要意義

  若此計畫付諸實施，將是北京迄今最積極的一次行動，旨在建立獨立於美國技術之外的國內AI運算基礎。在八成國產採購強制要求下，以華為為首的本土基礎建設供應商將成為最大受益者。阿里巴巴、騰訊等私人科技公司並非此次投資的主要目標，但其現有資料中心如何與規劃中的國家級網路協同運作，目前仍不明朗。

  據知情人士透露，整體目標是在2028年前，將中國現有分散的資料設施整合成一套統一的全國算力網路，應用範圍涵蓋醫療、交通與城市管理等公共服務領域。
