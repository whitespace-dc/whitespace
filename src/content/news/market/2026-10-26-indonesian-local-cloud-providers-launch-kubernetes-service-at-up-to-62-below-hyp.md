body_en: |
  Arupa Cloud Nusantara and UK-based cloud provider Civo have launched a jointly operated cloud region in Jakarta, competing with global hyperscalers on price and data-residency grounds in Indonesia's expanding cloud infrastructure market.

  The two companies claim their managed Kubernetes — an open-source container orchestration system that automates the deployment and scaling of software applications across cloud infrastructure — costs 49 to 62 percent less per month than equivalent hyperscaler configurations in Jakarta. That comparison is based on a general-purpose three-node Kubernetes cluster at specific pricing assumptions published by Civo, and the companies acknowledge it may not reflect total costs across all workload types.

  ## What the service includes

  The Civo Indonesia Region offers:

  - Managed Kubernetes
  - Virtual machines
  - Managed PostgreSQL databases
  - Load balancers
  - Block storage

  Arupa handles all operations and maintenance of the Indonesia region, with data center infrastructure in Jakarta that the companies describe as Tier IV certified — the highest standard for data center fault tolerance and uptime. Customers are billed in Indonesian rupiah through Arupa, with usage metered hourly and invoiced monthly. Civo says it charges no fees for inbound or outbound data transfer on the Jakarta region.

  ## Data residency as a differentiator

  Civo states that all customer data — including control plane data, backups, and operational logs — remains within Indonesia. The companies are positioning this as a selling point for organizations that weigh data location, operational control, and local technical support when selecting a cloud provider.

  Civo Chief Commercial Officer Simon Hansford said demand for manageable infrastructure with transparent pricing represents an opening for cloud-native services. "Through the partnership with Arupa, we are bringing the Civo Kubernetes-first cloud platform directly to companies in Indonesia, supported by local experts and an operational model designed to suit the needs of the Indonesian market," Hansford said.

  Arupa Chief Executive Officer Reza Kertadjaja said the partnership combines Civo's cloud platform with technology consulting and local technical support from Arupa, with the aim of helping customers manage infrastructure and develop digital services.

  ## GPU compute and AI workloads

  Arupa and Civo plan to add GPU Compute and GPU Kubernetes services in Jakarta in December 2026, with reservations already open through Arupa. GPU-based compute is required for training and running artificial intelligence applications. The companies have not disclosed the GPU capacity to be deployed in Indonesia, the investment involved, target customers, or projected revenue from those services.

  ## Market significance

  The launch broadens the choice of cloud infrastructure available to Indonesian enterprises and developers at a time when demand for AI application infrastructure is increasing. By operating locally, billing in rupiah, and claiming substantial cost advantages on container workloads, Arupa and Civo are targeting a segment of the market that global hyperscalers — large-scale public cloud providers such as those based in the United States — have historically dominated. The planned December 2026 GPU availability will mark the companies' direct entry into AI infrastructure competition in the Indonesian market.

  Arupa Cloud Nusantara 與英國雲端業者 Civo 攜手在雅加達建立共同營運的雲端區域，在印尼持續擴張的雲端基礎設施市場中，以低廉定價與資料在地化為訴求，正面迎戰全球超大型雲端業者。

  兩家公司宣稱，其託管式 Kubernetes──一套開源容器編排系統，可自動化跨雲端基礎設施的軟體應用程式部署與擴展──每月費用較雅加達同等超大型業者配置低 49 至 62%。這項比較依據 Civo 公開的特定定價假設，以一般用途三節點 Kubernetes 叢集為基準，兩家公司亦坦承，此數字未必能反映所有工作負載類型的整體成本。

  ## 服務內容一覽

  Civo 印尼區域提供以下服務：

  - 託管式 Kubernetes
  - 虛擬機器
  - 託管式 PostgreSQL 資料庫
  - 負載平衡器
  - 區塊儲存

  印尼區域的所有維運工作由 Arupa 負責，資料中心基礎設施位於雅加達，兩家公司表示已通過 Tier IV 認證──此為資料中心容錯能力與可用性的最高標準。客戶透過 Arupa 以印尼盾計費，用量以小時計算，每月開立發票。Civo 表示，雅加達區域的資料傳輸（無論流入或流出）均不另行收費。

  ## 資料在地化作為差異化優勢

  Civo 表示，所有客戶資料──包括控制平面資料、備份及操作日誌──均存放於印尼境內。兩家公司將此定位為一項銷售亮點，瞄準在選擇雲端業者時，將資料存放地點、營運控制權及本地技術支援列為重要考量的企業客戶。

  Civo 首席商務官 Simon Hansford 表示，市場對定價透明、易於管理之基礎設施的需求，正為雲端原生服務創造切入機會。「透過與 Arupa 的合作，我們將 Civo 以 Kubernetes 為核心的雲端平台直接帶進印尼企業，由在地專家提供支援，並以適合印尼市場需求的營運模式運作，」Hansford 說。

  Arupa 執行長 Reza Kertadjaja 表示，這項合作結合了 Civo 的雲端平台與 Arupa 的科技顧問服務及本地技術支援，目標是協助客戶管理基礎設施、開發數位服務。

  ## GPU 運算與 AI 工作負載

  Arupa 與 Civo 計畫於 2026 年 12 月在雅加達推出 GPU 運算及 GPU Kubernetes 服務，目前已開放透過 Arupa 預約。GPU 運算是訓練與執行人工智慧應用程式的必要條件。兩家公司目前尚未揭露預計在印尼部署的 GPU 容量、相關投資規模、目標客群或預估營收。

  ## 市場意義

  此次服務上線，在 AI 應用基礎設施需求持續升溫之際，為印尼企業與開發者拓寬了雲端基礎設施的選擇空間。Arupa 與 Civo 透過在地化營運、以印尼盾計費，並宣稱在容器工作負載上具備顯著成本優勢，鎖定的正是長期由超大型全球公有雲業者（例如以美國為基地的大型雲端供應商）主導的市場區塊。預計 2026 年 12 月推出的 GPU 服務，將標誌著兩家公司正式進軍印尼 AI 基礎設施的競爭市場。
