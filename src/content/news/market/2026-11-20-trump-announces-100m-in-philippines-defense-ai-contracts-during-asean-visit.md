body_en: |
  US President Donald Trump announced two defense contracts with the Philippines totaling $100 million during a two-day state visit to Manila, where he also attended the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) Summit alongside President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. The deals were part of a wider package the White House said exceeded $17 billion in commercial and strategic investments.

  According to a fact sheet distributed by the White House, the Philippine Department of National Defense (DND) signed a $37 million contract with an unnamed US firm to deliver AI-enabled mission support, with the stated aim of expanding defense cooperation and interoperability between the two countries. The DND also committed to a separate $63 million initiative to modernize its defense cloud infrastructure for artificial intelligence use cases.

  Both contracts fall under the US SI (Strategic Investment) Exports Program, a framework through which Washington is channeling sovereign cloud infrastructure from US AI technology providers to partner governments.

  ## Broader ASEAN commitments

  The White House said the Philippines deals are part of a wider effort to deploy US AI technology across Southeast Asia. The US committed to developing a sovereign AI cloud for the Malaysian government through a vehicle called US-ASEAN SI Spark. In Brunei, the country's national telecommunications operator, Unified National Networks, said it intends to award the Rimba Data Center tender — described as the largest AI infrastructure project in Brunei — to US power and thermal management company Vertiv, with the full $1 billion buildout to be sourced from US vendors.

  ## Pax Silica and the contested New Clark City hub

  Trump's visit arrived one month after the Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), a Philippine government corporation, submitted the legal framework for the proposed Pax Silica hub in New Clark City. Pax Silica — Latin for Silicon Peace — is a US government initiative launched in December 2025 to secure supply chains for rare earth elements, semiconductors, and other components essential to AI infrastructure, with an explicit goal of reducing dependence on Chinese technology. The Philippines joined the initiative in April 2026.

  The New Clark City hub is designed as an AI-focused investment zone supporting manufacturing and supply chains for semiconductors and related technologies. The BCDA has projected it could attract up to $70 billion in investment and generate more than 130,000 jobs.

  The project has drawn significant domestic opposition. As reported by Al Jazeera, Filipino citizens have raised concerns spanning national sovereignty, environmental impact, community displacement, indigenous land rights, and human security. Ahead of Trump's arrival in Manila, protests erupted and police arrested more than 200 demonstrators opposing Pax Silica.

  President Marcos Jr. moved to separate the state visit from the hub controversy, telling the Philippine News Agency that no Pax Silica agreement would be signed during Trump's trip. "There is no signing. Not on this trip. We are not covering that on this trip," Marcos said.

  ## What the deals signal

  The $100 million in defense AI contracts represents a concrete near-term commitment within a partnership that the two governments are positioning as a template for US-aligned sovereign AI infrastructure across Southeast Asia. The SI Exports Program framework — now applied in the Philippines, Malaysia, and Brunei — indicates Washington is using bilateral and multilateral mechanisms to route AI cloud buildout toward US vendors across the region.

  The Rimba Data Center award in Brunei, if finalized as indicated, would be the largest single AI infrastructure project commitment to emerge from Trump's ASEAN visit, at $1 billion sourced from US suppliers.

  美國總統唐納．川普在對馬尼拉進行為期兩天的國是訪問期間，宣布與菲律賓簽署兩份合計1億美元的國防合約。此行川普亦出席了東南亞國協（ASEAN）峰會，與菲律賓總統小費迪南德．馬可仕（Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.）同台。白宮表示，這些合約是一項規模更大方案的組成部分，該方案的商業與戰略投資總額超過170億美元。

  根據白宮發布的情況說明書，菲律賓國防部（DND）與一家未具名的美國企業簽署了一份價值3,700萬美元的合約，旨在提供具備AI能力的任務支援，明確目標是深化兩國的國防合作與互通性。菲律賓國防部另行承諾一項規模6,300萬美元的計畫，以推動其國防雲端基礎設施現代化，支援人工智慧相關應用場景的部署。

  兩份合約均依據美國「戰略投資出口計畫」（SI Exports Program）框架簽署。這是華盛頓藉以將美國AI技術商的主權雲端基礎設施引導至夥伴國政府的政策機制。

  ## 更廣泛的東協承諾

  白宮表示，與菲律賓的交易是美國在整個東南亞推廣AI技術這一更廣泛努力的組成部分。美方承諾透過名為「美國—東協SI火花」（US-ASEAN SI Spark）的載體，為馬來西亞政府建構主權AI雲端。在汶萊，該國國家電信業者「統一國家網路」（Unified National Networks）表示，擬將林巴資料中心（Rimba Data Center）標案判給美國電力與熱管理公司威帝（Vertiv）。林巴資料中心被描述為汶萊規模最大的AI基礎設施專案，整體10億美元的建設費用將全數向美國供應商採購。

  ## Pax Silica與新克拉克市爭議

  川普此行抵達菲律賓前一個月，菲律賓政府法人「基地轉型暨發展局」（BCDA）甫提交了擬議中「新克拉克市Pax Silica園區」的法律框架。Pax Silica——拉丁文意為「矽之和平」——是美國政府於2025年12月發起的倡議，目的在確保稀土元素、半導體及其他AI基礎設施所需關鍵零組件的供應鏈安全，並明確以降低對中國技術的依賴為目標。菲律賓於2026年4月正式加入該倡議。

  新克拉克市園區規劃定位為AI導向的投資專區，支援半導體及相關技術的製造與供應鏈發展。基地轉型暨發展局預估，園區最高可吸引700億美元投資、創造逾13萬個就業機會。

  這項計畫在菲律賓國內引發相當大的反對聲浪。據半島電視台報導，菲律賓民眾對國家主權、環境衝擊、社區遷置、原住民土地權利及人類安全等問題提出質疑。川普抵達馬尼拉前夕，抗議活動隨即爆發，警方逮捕了逾200名反對Pax Silica的示威者。

  馬可仕總統試圖將國是訪問與園區爭議切割，他告訴菲律賓國家通訊社，川普訪菲期間不會簽署任何Pax Silica協議。「這趟不會簽署。不是這趟。我們這趟不處理那個。」馬可仕說。

  ## 合約所傳達的訊號

  這筆1億美元的國防AI合約，代表兩國政府在夥伴關係框架下一項具體的近期承諾——兩國正將此模式定位為美國陣營主權AI基礎設施布局於東南亞各地的範本。SI出口計畫框架目前已適用於菲律賓、馬來西亞及汶萊，顯示華盛頓正運用雙邊與多邊機制，將整個區域的AI雲端建設導向美國供應商。

  若汶萊的林巴資料中心標案依預期定案，將是川普此趟東協行所催生規模最大的單一AI基礎設施承諾，總額達10億美元，全數由美國供應商供貨。
