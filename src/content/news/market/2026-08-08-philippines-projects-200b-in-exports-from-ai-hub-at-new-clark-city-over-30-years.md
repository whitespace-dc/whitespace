body_en: |
  The Philippine government expects the proposed Pax Silica artificial intelligence hub in New Clark City, Tarlac to generate up to $200 billion in export revenues over 30 years once fully developed, the Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA) said Friday.

  BCDA President and Chief Executive Officer Joshua "Jake" M. Bingcang told reporters that the 1,619-hectare (about 4,000-acre) AI-native acceleration hub will be built in three phases of approximately 500 hectares each, with construction beginning in 2028. The BCDA is the Philippine government agency responsible for converting former military bases into productive civilian use and managing New Clark City.

  ## Scale and timeline

  Bingcang said the first phase of roughly 500 hectares could be completed in three to five years. He said the agency expects 10 to 20 semiconductor companies to locate within the hub during that initial phase.

  The hub is being developed under Pax Silica, a coalition the Philippines joined in April. The arrangement involves Manila and Washington co-developing the New Clark City site to manufacture components deemed critical to AI infrastructure supply chains. Those components include AI chips, memory and storage, high-speed networking, data centers, and power and cooling systems.

  Trade Undersecretary Ceferino S. Rodolfo described Pax Silica as an "international non-binding declaration of shared principles focused on securing and coordinating supply chains for advanced technologies essential to the AI era." While the agreement carries no binding obligations, Rodolfo said the branding as a trusted partner in the coalition is intended to help attract and retain long-term investment.

  ## Power constraints rule out hyperscale data centers

  Bingcang said no hyperscale data centers — large-scale facilities operated by major cloud providers that typically require hundreds of megawatts of electricity and substantial water for cooling — will be built inside the Pax Silica hub. He cited New Clark City's power and water capacity as insufficient to meet the requirements of AI-driven data centers at that scale.

  Bingcang noted that one unnamed American company had canceled plans to build a hyperscale data center in New Clark City and redirected its investment to Malaysia because the Tarlac site could not meet its power needs. "Even if we will invite hyperscalers, they will not go," he said.

  The disclosure creates a direct tension with an earlier position from the Department of Information and Communications Technology, which had said data centers within the industrial hub could reach at least 400 megawatts in capacity.

  ## Land use and community concerns

  The project has drawn scrutiny over environmental impact, alleged displacement of local communities, and national interest implications. Rodolfo addressed those concerns directly at the briefing, stating the hub will be developed on government-owned land already designated for industrial use. "It's a titled public land for industrial use. No farmers, no indigenous communities will be displaced," he said.

  ## Significance

  The Pax Silica hub represents one of the more ambitious infrastructure commitments in Southeast Asia's emerging AI supply chain landscape. Its success hinges on resolving the power and water constraints that have already cost the site at least one major investor, and on translating a non-binding international declaration into durable, long-term capital commitments. Philippine officials say coordinated investment attraction under the Pax Silica framework, rather than binding obligations, is the mechanism intended to secure that outcome.

  The BCDA expects the first 500-hectare phase to be operational within three to five years of the 2028 construction start.

  ## 菲律賓：新克拉克市AI園區三十年出口額上看2,000億美元

  菲律賓政府表示，預計在塔拉克省新克拉克市規劃興建的Pax Silica人工智慧園區，一旦全面開發完成，三十年內可望創造高達2,000億美元的出口收益。基地轉型暨發展局（BCDA）於週五發布上述預估。

  BCDA局長兼執行長Joshua「Jake」M. Bingcang向媒體表示，這座占地1,619公頃（約4,000英畝）的AI原生加速園區，將分三期興建，每期約500公頃，預計於2028年正式動工。BCDA是菲律賓政府負責將前軍事基地轉型為民間生產用途，並統籌管理新克拉克市的主管機關。

  ## 規模與時程

  Bingcang表示，第一期約500公頃的工程可在三至五年內完工。他說，預計將有10至20家半導體公司在第一期開發期間進駐園區。

  此園區由菲律賓今年4月加入的Pax Silica聯盟主導開發。根據該協議安排，馬尼拉與華盛頓共同開發新克拉克市基地，生產被認定為人工智慧基礎設施供應鏈關鍵要件的零組件，包括AI晶片、記憶體與儲存裝置、高速網路設備、資料中心，以及電力與冷卻系統。

  貿易部次長Ceferino S. Rodolfo形容Pax Silica是「一項以確保與協調先進技術供應鏈為核心的國際非約束性共同原則宣言，而這些技術對AI時代至關重要。」儘管協議不具法律約束力，Rodolfo表示，以可信賴夥伴身份加入聯盟，有助於吸引並留住長期投資。

  ## 電力限制排除超大型資料中心

  Bingcang表示，Pax Silica園區內不會興建超大型資料中心——這類由大型雲端業者營運的大規模設施，通常需要數百百萬瓦的電力及大量用水進行冷卻。他指出，新克拉克市的電力與供水容量，不足以支應這種規模的AI資料中心所需。

  Bingcang透露，一家未具名的美國企業原本計劃在新克拉克市興建超大型資料中心，最終因塔拉克省基地無法滿足其電力需求，而將投資轉移至馬來西亞。他說：「就算我們邀請超大型業者進駐，他們也不會來。」

  此番表態與資訊通訊技術部早前的立場形成明顯矛盾。資訊通訊技術部曾表示，該工業園區內的資料中心容量至少可達400MW。

  ## 土地使用與社區疑慮

  這項計畫引發外界對環境衝擊、疑似迫遷在地社區，以及國家利益等層面的質疑。Rodolfo在簡報中直接回應上述疑慮，強調園區將在已劃定為工業用途的政府所有土地上開發。他說：「這是已登記的工業用途公有地。不會有農民或原住民族社區遭到迫遷。」

  ## 重要意義

  Pax Silica園區是東南亞新興AI供應鏈版圖中野心最大的基礎建設承諾之一。其成敗，繫於能否解決已導致至少一位重量級投資人出走的電力與供水瓶頸，以及能否將一紙不具約束力的國際宣言，轉化為持久、長期的資本承諾。菲律賓官員表示，透過Pax Silica框架協調招商引資——而非依賴具約束力的義務條款——正是確保上述目標實現的核心機制。

  BCDA預計，第一期500公頃工程將在2028年動工後三至五年內正式啟用。
