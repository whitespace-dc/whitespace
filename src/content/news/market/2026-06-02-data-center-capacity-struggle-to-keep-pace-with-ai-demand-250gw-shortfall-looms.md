# Data center capacity struggle to keep pace with AI demand; 250GW shortfall looms

AI data center demand is significantly outpacing industry capacity expansion despite major investment commitments. A new report projects that an additional 250GW of power capacity is required under an "aggressive but still plausible" growth scenario. The infrastructure gap persists even as companies make what executives describe as "existential" investments in facility expansion.

## 中文

# 資料中心容量跟不上人工智慧需求　預計短缺250GW

人工智慧資料中心需求正大幅超越業界容量擴張速度，儘管有重大投資承諾。新報告指出，在「積極但仍具合理性」的成長情境下，需要額外的250GW電力容量。儘管企業投入了領導人所稱「關乎生存」的設施擴張投資，基礎設施缺口依然存在。
