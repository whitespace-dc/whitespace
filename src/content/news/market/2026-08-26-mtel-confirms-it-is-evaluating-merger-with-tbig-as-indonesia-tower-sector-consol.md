body_en: |
  PT Dayamitra Telekomunikasi Tbk (MTEL) has confirmed it is evaluating a possible merger with PT Tower Bersama Infrastructure Tbk (TBIG), according to Kontan, as Indonesia's listed tower companies respond to a changing competitive environment by pursuing consolidation and fiber network expansion.

  MTEL's acknowledgment of the evaluation follows reports that the company has begun submitting a transaction proposal, which Kontan said has driven renewed and intensifying public attention to the deal concept.

  ## A deal idea that keeps returning

  The prospect of combining MTEL and TBIG is not new. Discussion of a potential merger between the two tower operators first emerged in 2015, resurfaced last year, and has now returned more forcefully, Kontan reported.

  Hendy Santoso, E-Hub Sales Manager at YB Sekuritas Sudirman, told Kontan the merger remains at the level of rumor and is still in an early evaluation stage. MTEL has confirmed it is assessing the possibility of the corporate action, but Telkom — MTEL's parent company — has not yet provided detailed information on the matter, according to the report.

  ## Significance

  If completed, a combination of MTEL and TBIG would represent a major consolidation among Indonesia's publicly listed tower infrastructure companies. The competitive map for tower issuers is shifting, Kontan reported, with consolidation and fiber expansion emerging as the primary strategic responses available to operators navigating that change. The next material development will depend on whether Telkom provides formal guidance and whether MTEL advances a transaction proposal beyond the evaluation phase.

  據《Kontan》報導，PT Dayamitra Telekomunikasi Tbk（MTEL）已確認正在評估與PT Tower Bersama Infrastructure Tbk（TBIG）合併的可能性。在競爭環境急速轉變之際，印尼上市鐵塔業者紛紛以整合與光纖網路擴張作為因應之道。

  MTEL發表聲明承認正在進行評估，此前有報導指出該公司已開始提交交易提案。《Kontan》表示，此舉引發外界對這項合併構想重燃且持續升溫的高度關注。

  ## 屢屢浮現的合併構想

  MTEL與TBIG合併的可能性並非新鮮事。這兩家鐵塔業者之間的合併討論最早出現於2015年，去年再度浮上檯面，如今又以更強勁的態勢捲土重來，《Kontan》如此報導。

  YB Sekuritas Sudirman的電子中心業務經理Hendy Santoso向《Kontan》表示，合併消息目前仍停留在傳言階段，整體進程仍處於早期評估階段。據報導，MTEL已確認正在評估此一企業行動的可行性，但MTEL母公司Telkom迄今尚未就此事提供任何詳細說明。

  ## 重大意義

  若合併案順利完成，MTEL與TBIG的結合將是印尼上市鐵塔基礎設施業者之間的一次重大整合。《Kontan》報導指出，鐵塔發行商的競爭版圖正在重塑，整合與光纖擴張已成為業者在此變局中可採行的主要戰略因應方向。下一個關鍵進展，將取決於Telkom是否提出正式指引，以及MTEL能否將交易提案推進至評估階段之外。
