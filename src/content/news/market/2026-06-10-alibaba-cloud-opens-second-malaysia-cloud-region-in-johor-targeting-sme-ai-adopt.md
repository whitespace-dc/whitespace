body_en: |
  Alibaba Cloud launched a public cloud region in Johor on June 9, its second in Malaysia, comprising two data centres and expanding the company's total in-country facility count to five. The company described that footprint as its largest infrastructure presence in Southeast Asia.

  The Johor region is part of Alibaba's previously announced US$53 billion (RM223 billion) investment in AI and cloud infrastructure in 2025 and extends the group's global network to 104 availability zones across 32 regions.

  ## SME focus and pricing pitch

  Alibaba Cloud Intelligence Malaysia general manager Choong Hon Keat said small and medium enterprises (SMEs) remain the company's primary target segment in the country. "Over the years we have been focusing on serving the SME market," he said at a media briefing in Kuala Lumpur on Tuesday. He added that the company is also active in micro, small and medium enterprise (MSME) programmes and that government-led digital initiatives are driving AI uptake among smaller firms.

  The company's head of solutions architect for Malaysia, Lin En Shu, framed its competitive position as cost, not capability parity with premium Western models. "Our biggest strength is really a similar level of intelligence at a fraction of the cost," he said, citing the Qwen family of large language models as an example. He compared the choice of AI model to selecting fuel grades at a petrol station, arguing that most businesses select a model sufficient for the task rather than the most advanced option available. Lin described tokens — the unit of compute that AI workloads consume — as the primary running cost enterprises now track. "Tokens are the fuel for AI," he said.

  ## Regional reach and data sovereignty

  Although the Johor region is positioned to serve domestic Malaysian demand, Choong confirmed it operates as a public cloud region, meaning customers in other countries can also use it. "Singapore will definitely also be using it," he said.

  On data sovereignty — a stated concern among Malaysian enterprise customers — Lin said the company keeps Malaysian customer data in-country and runs AI inference on Nvidia L20 and H20 graphics processing units (GPUs) that are configured for the Chinese market. "This is purpose-built for Malaysians, knowing that data sovereignty is really a big thing here," he said, adding that most local customers want model inference served locally so that data does not leave the country.

  ## Partner ecosystem and government ties

  Choong said Alibaba Cloud has signed more than 300 system integrators, resellers, distributors and partners in Malaysia and has increased AI training for those partners over the past one to two years. The company has also signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with the Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) to support SME requirements and works with the Ministry of Digital.

  ## Customers and planned services

  Existing customers using Alibaba Cloud infrastructure in Malaysia include TNG Digital, the operator of the Touch 'n Go eWallet app, which uses a unified data platform for search and recommendation functions. YTL AI Labs runs its ILMU family of Malay-language AI models on the platform. Creative AI companies TabSpace.ai and Morphyx.io use Alibaba Cloud's Wan and HappyHorse video generation models through the company's Model Studio service.

  Alibaba Cloud plans to introduce a suite of agentic AI services in Malaysia in the second half of 2025, including AgentRun, an agent development and build platform; STAROps; ACS Agent Sandbox; Agent Security Center; AI Security Guardrails 2.0; and an Agentic SOC (security operations centre).

  ## 聚焦中小企業與定價策略

  阿里雲智慧馬來西亞總經理鍾漢傑（Choong Hon Keat）表示，中小企業（SME）始終是該公司在當地的首要目標市場。他在週二於吉隆坡舉行的媒體簡報會上表示：「多年來，我們一直專注於服務中小企業市場。」他補充說，公司也積極參與微型、小型及中型企業（MSME）相關計畫，並指出政府主導的數位化政策正在推動規模較小的企業加快採用AI。

  阿里雲馬來西亞解決方案架構主管林恩樹（Lin En Shu）則將該公司的競爭優勢定位在成本，而非與西方高端模型並駕齊驅的能力。他說：「我們最大的優勢在於，以極低的成本提供相近水準的智慧能力。」他以旗下千問（Qwen）大型語言模型家族為例，並以加油站選擇燃油標號來比喻AI模型的選擇，認為多數企業會選擇足以應付任務的模型，而非最先進的選項。林恩樹將token——即AI工作負載所消耗的運算單位——描述為企業目前追蹤的主要運算成本，並說：「Token就是AI的燃料。」

  ## 服務範圍與資料主權

  儘管柔佛區域的定位以服務馬來西亞國內需求為主，鍾漢傑確認該區域屬於公有雲區域，其他國家的客戶同樣可以使用。「新加坡客戶肯定也會用到，」他說。

  在資料主權方面——此議題被馬來西亞企業客戶列為重要考量——林恩樹表示，阿里雲將馬來西亞客戶資料保存於境內，並使用針對中國市場配置的輝達L20與H20繪圖處理器（GPU）在本地執行AI推論。「這是專為馬來西亞人打造的，因為我們知道資料主權在這裡確實至關重要，」他說，並補充大多數本地客戶希望模型推論在本地執行，以確保資料不會離開國境。

  ## 合作夥伴生態系與政府關係

  鍾漢傑表示，阿里雲在馬來西亞已簽約逾300家系統整合商、經銷商、代理商與合作夥伴，並在過去一至兩年間加強了對這些夥伴的AI培訓。此外，阿里雲已與馬來西亞數位經濟機構（MDEC）簽署合作備忘錄（MOU），以支援中小企業的需求，並與數位部保持合作。

  ## 現有客戶與計畫推出的服務

  目前在馬來西亞使用阿里雲基礎設施的客戶包括：TNG Digital，即Touch 'n Go電子錢包的營運商，使用統一資料平台處理搜尋與推薦功能；YTL AI Labs在此平台上運行其ILMU馬來語AI模型家族。創意AI公司TabSpace.ai與Morphyx.io則透過阿里雲的Model Studio服務，使用Wan與HappyHorse影片生成模型。

  阿里雲計畫在2025年下半年於馬來西亞推出一系列代理式AI服務，包括AgentRun（代理程式開發與建置平台）、STAROps、ACS Agent Sandbox、Agent Security Center、AI Security Guardrails 2.0，以及代理式資安維運中心（Agentic SOC）。
