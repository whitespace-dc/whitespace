body_en: |
  West River Engineering Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Kuala Lumpur-listed West River Bhd, has secured a RM22.56 million subcontract to carry out electrical works for a consumer landing station at a data centre development in Iskandar Puteri, Johor. The identity of the main contractor awarding the subcontract was not disclosed.

  West River Engineering specialises in electrical engineering and intelligent building solutions. The scope of work covers the design, supply, construction, handover and maintenance of the landing station.

  ## Project scope

  The landing station is one element of a phased campus being developed by Digital Hyperspace Malaysia Sdn Bhd at the SILC Industrial Area in Iskandar Puteri. The full development comprises a two-storey consumer landing station, a three-storey data centre, a three-storey mechanical and electrical building, and a four-storey office building.

  ## Timeline and contract terms

  Work on the landing station commenced on June 20 and is scheduled for completion by October 30. Following handover, the contract imposes an 18-month defects liability period — a clause requiring the contractor to remedy faults identified after the client accepts the project.

  If West River fails to meet the October 30 deadline, including any approved extensions, the company is liable for liquidated damages of RM22,000 per calendar day for the period of delay.

  ## Market reaction

  West River shares rose 3.1% to 33 sen on Tuesday following the announcement, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM118 million.

  ## Company outlook

  West River managing director Lim Yong Lai said in a statement that the award demonstrates confidence in the company's capabilities and that the company is committed to delivering the work safely and on time. West River said it plans to use the contract to build its technical track record in data centre electrical systems, with a view to pursuing further opportunities across the digital infrastructure, commercial, industrial and related engineering sectors.

  ## 工程範疇

  此落地站為Digital Hyperspace Malaysia私人有限公司在柔佛伊斯干達布城SILC工業區分期開發園區的組成項目之一。整體開發計畫包含一棟兩層樓消費者落地站、一棟三層樓資料中心、一棟三層樓機電大樓，以及一棟四層樓辦公大樓。

  ## 時程與合約條款

  落地站工程已於6月20日正式開工，預計於10月30日前竣工。移交完成後，合約設有18個月的瑕疵責任期，要求承包商針對業主驗收後所發現的缺失進行修繕。

  若西河工程未能在10月30日截止日期（含任何核准之展延期限）前完工，公司須就延誤期間每個日曆天賠付2萬2,000馬幣的違約損害賠償金。

  ## 市場反應

  消息公布後，西河有限公司股價於週二上漲3.1%，收報每股33仙，公司市值達1億1,800萬馬幣。

  ## 公司展望

  西河有限公司董事總經理林永來（Lim Yong Lai）在聲明中表示，此次合約的得標充分體現外界對公司能力的肯定，並強調公司致力於安全、準時地完成工程。西河表示，計畫藉由本合約累積資料中心電氣系統領域的技術資歷，進而尋求拓展數位基礎設施、商業、工業及相關工程領域的業務機會。
