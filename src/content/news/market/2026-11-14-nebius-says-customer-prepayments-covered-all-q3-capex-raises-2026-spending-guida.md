body_en: |
  ## Prepayments cover full capex cost for first time

  Nebius Group, an Amsterdam-headquartered artificial intelligence infrastructure and neocloud company, said prepayments on its third-quarter 2026 agreements covered more than 100 percent of the associated capital expenditure — including data center construction and networking — up from 50 to 60 percent in the second quarter. The milestone suggests the company has found a financing model that funds expansion partly through advance payments from customers before infrastructure is built.

  The company simultaneously raised its capex guidance for full-year 2026 to approximately $27 billion, above a previous range of $20 billion to $25 billion, citing investment in capacity for 2027, particularly at its own sites.

  ## Forward guidance on prepayment coverage

  Chief Executive Officer and founder Arkady Volozh told investors that Nebius expects prepayments and financing against existing contracts with investment-grade customers to cover between 75 and 85 percent of its 2027 capital expenditure. In a letter to shareholders, Volozh addressed what he described as recent industry-wide questions about how the AI infrastructure build-out will be financed, saying the company's focus remains on building a "healthy, profitable business for the long term."

  ## Revenue and earnings

  For the third quarter, Nebius reported revenue of $980.8 million, a 571 percent increase from $146.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA reached $465.8 million, compared with a loss of $5.2 million in the year-earlier period. Net losses narrowed from $119.6 million to $37.5 million.

  Full-year 2026 revenue is now expected to come in at the low end of the $3 billion to $3.4 billion guidance range. The company projects 2027 revenue of between $12 billion and $13 billion and expects to reach pre-tax breakeven by the end of that year.

  ## Capacity and site expansion

  Nebius said it now has more than 4 gigawatts of power capacity contracted across its sites — a measure of the total electricity committed to run servers and cooling — and is on track to reach 5 gigawatts of contracted power by the end of 2026.

  New facilities in the pipeline include a planned 100-megawatt multi-campus AI data center project in Alberta, Canada, for which the company said it has secured exclusive site rights. A 100 MW facility can support roughly tens of thousands of AI accelerator chips depending on hardware density. That project is expected to come online in 2027. Also slated for 2027 are a 50 MW colocation facility in South Carolina and two colocation facilities in India, which Nebius described as its first sites in the Asia-Pacific region.

  ## Asset-light model

  Nebius told shareholders that an asset-light strategy — using third-party colocation space rather than building all capacity on owned land — would contribute to the company's ability to expand beyond its own contracted power in a cost-efficient manner.

  ## Industry significance

  The shift from partial to full capex coverage by customer prepayments is significant for the broader AI infrastructure sector, which has faced scrutiny over whether hyperscale spending can be sustained without traditional debt or equity financing. By sourcing construction capital directly from customer commitments, Nebius is demonstrating one model for funding large-scale AI data center build-outs, a question the company acknowledged has become a focus of industry debate. The company's projected 75 to 85 percent prepayment coverage of 2027 capex indicates it expects the approach to continue, though at a lower coverage ratio than the 100 percent achieved in Q3 2026.

  ## 預付款首次涵蓋全部資本支出

  Nebius集團總部位於荷蘭阿姆斯特丹，是一家人工智慧基礎設施暨neocloud新型雲端業者。該公司表示，2026年第三季簽訂合約所對應的客戶預付款，已超過相關資本支出（涵蓋資料中心建設與網路建置）的100%，較第二季的50至60%大幅提升。這一里程碑顯示，Nebius已建立起一套融資模式，能在基礎設施正式動工之前，透過客戶的預付款來支應部分擴張成本。

  公司在此同時，將2026全年資本支出指引上調至約270億美元，高於先前200億至250億美元的指引區間，理由是加碼投資2027年的產能，尤其集中在自有場地。

  ## 預付款涵蓋比例的前瞻指引

  執行長暨創辦人阿爾卡季·沃洛日（Arkady Volozh）向投資人表示，Nebius預計來自具投資等級評等客戶現有合約的預付款及融資，將能涵蓋2027年資本支出的75%至85%。沃洛日在致股東信中，回應他所描述的業界近期對AI基礎設施建設籌資方式的廣泛討論，表示公司始終專注於「長期建立健全、獲利的企業」。

  ## 營收與獲利

  第三季方面，Nebius營收達9億8,080萬美元，較2025年第三季的1億4,610萬美元成長571%。調整後EBITDA達4億6,580萬美元，去年同期則為虧損520萬美元。淨虧損從1億1,960萬美元收窄至3,750萬美元。

  2026全年營收預計將落在30億至34億美元指引區間的低端。公司預計2027年營收介於120億至130億美元之間，並預期於當年底前達到稅前損益兩平。

  ## 產能與場地擴張

  Nebius表示，目前各場地已簽約的電力容量合計超過4吉瓦（GW）——這是衡量供應伺服器運作及冷卻系統所需電力規模的指標——並有望在2026年底前，將已簽約電力容量推進至5吉瓦。

  籌建中的新設施包括：計畫在加拿大亞伯達省（Alberta）建置一座100百萬瓦（MW）的多園區AI資料中心，Nebius表示已取得該場地的獨家使用權。一座100MW的設施，依硬體密度不同，可支援約數萬顆AI加速晶片。該項目預計於2027年投入運營。同樣規劃於2027年上線的，還有位於美國南卡羅萊納州的50MW代管設施，以及印度兩座代管設施——Nebius將後者定位為進軍亞太地區的首批場地。

  ## 輕資產模式

  Nebius向股東表示，採用「輕資產」策略——即租用第三方代管空間，而非全數在自有土地上興建產能——將有助於公司以更具成本效益的方式，將擴張規模延伸至自有已簽約電力容量之外。

  ## 產業意義

  從客戶預付款由部分涵蓋轉為完全涵蓋資本支出，對整個AI基礎設施產業而言意義重大。外界一直質疑，超大規模業者的龐大支出能否在不依賴傳統債務或股權融資的情況下持續。透過直接將客戶承諾轉化為建設資金，Nebius示範了一套為大規模AI資料中心建設籌資的可行模式——而公司也坦承，這一問題已成為業界辯論的焦點。公司預計2027年資本支出的75%至85%將由預付款涵蓋，顯示這套做法有望延續，儘管覆蓋比例低於2026年第三季所達到的100%。
