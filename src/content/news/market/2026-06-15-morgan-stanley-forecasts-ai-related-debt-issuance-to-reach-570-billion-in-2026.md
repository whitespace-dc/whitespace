body_en: |
  Morgan Stanley forecasts AI-related global debt issuance will reach nearly $570 billion in 2026, more than doubling year-over-year, as the four dominant hyperscalers — Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, and Meta — exhaust the capacity of operating cash flows to fund capital spending at the scale AI infrastructure now demands.

  Through May 31, 2026, AI-related debt issuance had already totaled nearly $236 billion, according to the bank — four times the volume recorded in the same period of 2025. Morgan Stanley expects the pace to accelerate in the second half of the year.

  ## Capex driving the shift to debt markets

  Tech companies that have long relied on strong internal cash flows are turning to debt financing as investment requirements surge, Morgan Stanley said. The four hyperscalers — shorthand for the largest cloud infrastructure providers — are expected to spend a combined $700 billion in capital outlays in 2026. That figure is projected to surpass $1 trillion in 2027, Morgan Stanley added.

  The scale of that spending is outpacing what retained earnings alone can support, pushing companies toward public and private bond markets as a supplemental funding channel.

  ## Broadening the investor base

  Hyperscalers are not only issuing more debt but issuing it in a wider range of currencies. "Hyperscalers have been broadening their investor base through non-USD issuance," Morgan Stanley said, referring to bonds denominated in currencies other than the U.S. dollar — a strategy that draws in overseas institutional investors and diversifies funding risk.

  ## Bond price action driven by supply

  Despite healthy underlying credit conditions, Morgan Stanley cautioned that near-term bond price movements reflect the sheer volume of expected supply more than fundamental credit quality. "Fundamental (economic) backdrop remains strong, but for now we think (bond) price action is being mostly driven by supply expectations," the bank said.

  ## Chip financing moves shorter

  Financing for semiconductor companies — whose chips are central to AI data center buildouts — is shifting in structure as well. Morgan Stanley said funding in that segment is moving toward shorter-term instruments that are fully repaid over their life, in both public and private markets, rather than the longer-dated bonds more typical of investment-grade corporate issuance.

  ## What this means for credit markets

  The trajectory Morgan Stanley describes represents a structural shift in how large-scale AI infrastructure is financed. With hyperscaler capex forecast to cross $1 trillion in 2027, credit markets are becoming a primary funding mechanism for AI buildout rather than a secondary one. The broadening of issuance into non-dollar currencies further indicates that the capital demand exceeds what any single investor market can absorb on its own.
body_zh: |
  摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）預測，2026年全球AI相關債券發行規模將達近5,700億美元，年增逾一倍，原因是Alphabet、亞馬遜（Amazon）、微軟（Microsoft）和Meta等四大超大規模雲端業者（hyperscaler）的資本支出急遽擴張，光靠營業現金流已無法支撐AI基礎設施所需的龐大投資，這些企業因此大舉轉向債券市場。

  截至2026年5月31日，AI相關債券發行量已累計至近2,360億美元，較2025年同期高出四倍。摩根士丹利預期，下半年發行步調將進一步加快。

  ## 資本支出驅動業者轉向債市融資

  摩根士丹利指出，長期依賴強勁內部現金流的科技業者，正因投資需求急速攀升而轉向債務融資。四大超大規模雲端業者——即規模最大的雲端基礎設施供應商的統稱——預計2026年合計資本支出將達7,000億美元，摩根士丹利並預測，此一數字將在2027年突破1兆美元。

  由於投資規模已超出保留盈餘所能支撐的上限，企業正將公開及私募債券市場列為補充融資管道。

  ## 擴大投資人基礎

  超大規模雲端業者不僅擴大債券發行量，也同步拓寬發行幣別。摩根士丹利表示：「超大規模業者正透過非美元計價發行來拓展投資人基礎」，意即以非美元幣別發行債券，藉此吸引海外機構投資人，並分散融資風險。

  ## 債券價格走勢受供給面主導

  儘管整體信用基本面仍屬健康，摩根士丹利仍提醒，近期債券價格走勢反映的主要是龐大供給預期，而非發行人的基本信用品質。該行表示：「基本（經濟）面仍屬強勁，但就目前而言，我們認為（債券）價格走勢大體上是由供給預期所驅動。」

  ## 晶片融資轉向短期工具

  半導體廠商的融資結構同樣出現變化——這些企業生產的晶片是AI資料中心建設的核心零組件。摩根士丹利表示，該領域的融資正轉向到期前可全額償還的短期工具，無論在公開市場或私募市場皆然，而非投資等級企業債慣見的長天期債券。

  ## 對信貸市場的意涵

  摩根士丹利所描繪的發展軌跡，代表大規模AI基礎設施的融資方式正在出現結構性轉變。隨著超大規模業者的資本支出預計在2027年突破1兆美元，信貸市場正從輔助性融資管道，躍升為AI建設的主要融資機制。發行幣別向非美元貨幣進一步擴展，更顯示資金需求之龐大，已非任何單一投資市場所能獨力承擔。
