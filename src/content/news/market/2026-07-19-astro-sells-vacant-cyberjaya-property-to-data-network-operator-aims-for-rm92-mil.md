body_en: |
  Astro Malaysia Holdings Bhd has agreed to sell a 4.5-acre freehold commercial property in Cyberjaya — comprising a six-storey technical building and ancillary structures — to data network operator AIMS Central Sdn Bhd for RM92 million, the company disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia filing on July 17.

  The disposal price stands 31.22% above the property's independent valuation of RM85 million, assessed as at March 2, and significantly exceeds the asset's net book value of RM53.78 million recorded as at January 31, Astro said. The property is currently untenanted.

  ## Transaction structure

  The seller is MEASAT Broadcast Network Systems Sdn Bhd, a subsidiary of Astro. The buyer, AIMS Central Sdn Bhd, is a wholly owned subsidiary of AIMS Data Centre Holding Sdn Bhd, a company principally involved in information services and the operation of data networks.

  After transaction costs, Astro expects net proceeds of RM85.7 million. The company said those funds will be directed toward working capital and other corporate purposes.

  ## Earnings and balance sheet impact

  Astro said the disposal is expected to contribute positively to both earnings and net assets for the financial year ending January 31, 2027. The company classified the Cyberjaya site as a non-core asset and said the transaction enables the group to realise its value.

  ## Financial context

  The sale comes as Astro's core business continues to contract. For the first quarter ended April 30, 2026, net profit fell 88% year-on-year to RM1.56 million from RM13.48 million in the prior-year period. The company recorded a pre-tax loss in the quarter; the positive after-tax result was preserved solely by a RM4.5 million tax credit. Revenue declined 6.2% to RM659.62 million, with lower subscription income the primary driver.

  Astro shares closed unchanged at 6.5 sen on Friday, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM339.7 million. The expected net proceeds of RM85.7 million represent roughly 25% of that market value, underscoring the relative scale of the disposal.

  ## Significance

  The transaction converts a vacant technical property into cash at a price that exceeds both the independent valuation and the book value by a substantial margin. For Astro, a pay-television operator reporting declining subscription revenue and a pre-tax loss in its most recent quarter, the proceeds provide a meaningful injection of working capital from a property that was generating no rental income. The deal is expected to settle and flow through Astro's accounts before the financial year ends on January 31, 2027.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞Astro控股（Astro Malaysia Holdings Bhd）已同意將旗下位於賽城（Cyberjaya）的一處4.5英畝永久業權商業物業——包含一棟六層樓技術大樓及附屬建築——以9,200萬令吉出售予資料網路業者AIMS Central Sdn Bhd。Astro於7月17日向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）提交的申報文件中揭露了這項交易。

  此次出售價格較物業獨立估值8,500萬令吉溢價31.22%，估值日期為3月2日；同時也大幅超越該資產截至1月31日帳面淨值5,378萬令吉。Astro表示，該物業目前並無租戶。

  ## 交易架構

  賣方為MEASAT Broadcast Network Systems Sdn Bhd，即Astro旗下子公司。買方AIMS Central Sdn Bhd則是AIMS Data Centre Holding Sdn Bhd的全資子公司，後者主要從事資訊服務及資料網路營運業務。

  扣除交易成本後，Astro預計可獲得淨收益8,570萬令吉。公司表示，所得款項將用於營運資金及其他企業用途。

  ## 獲利及資產負債表影響

  Astro表示，此次出售預計將對截至2027年1月31日止本財政年度的盈利及淨資產帶來正面貢獻。公司將賽城這處地產列為非核心資產，並指出此交易有助於集團實現資產價值。

  ## 財務背景

  此次出售之際，Astro核心業務持續萎縮。截至2026年4月30日的第一季，淨利潤年減88%，從去年同期的1,348萬令吉大幅滑落至156萬令吉。公司該季稅前錄得虧損，稅後之所以仍能維持正值，完全有賴於450萬令吉的稅收抵免。營收下滑6.2%至6億5,962萬令吉，主要驅動因素為訂閱收入減少。

  Astro股票週五收盤報6.5仙，與前日持平，公司市值為3億3,970萬令吉。預計淨收益8,570萬令吉約相當於市值的25%，凸顯出這筆資產出售的相對規模。

  ## 交易意義

  這筆交易將一處閒置技術物業轉換為現金，且成交價格同時超越獨立估值及帳面價值，溢幅相當可觀。對於Astro這家訂閱收入持續下滑、最近一季稅前錄得虧損的付費電視業者而言，這批收益為一處原本毫無租金收入的物業注入了可觀的營運資金。這筆交易預計將在2027年1月31日財政年度結束前完成交割並計入Astro帳目。
