body_en: |
  Two Indonesian brokerages have issued buy recommendations on domestic technology and telecommunications stocks, projecting that the country's data center capacity will grow from approximately 580 megawatts (MW) as of the first half of 2026 to 3.5 gigawatts (GW) by 2030 as artificial intelligence workloads and cloud adoption accelerate.

  BRI Danareksa Sekuritas analysts Kafi Ananta Azhari and Erindra Krisnawan put the implied compound annual growth rate (CAGR) — the steady yearly expansion rate required to reach a target over a defined period — at approximately 56.7 percent from 2026 through 2030. Separately, Samuel Sekuritas Indonesia Managing Director of Research Harry Su described AI infrastructure and data center construction as a long-term catalyst for technology stocks listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), while cautioning that the impact would not be uniform across issuers.

  ## Stock picks and price targets

  BRI Danareksa rated three companies as buys:

  - PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk. (TLKM), target price Rp3,500 per share
  - PT Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison Tbk. (ISAT), target price Rp2,500 per share
  - PT XLSmart Telecom Sejahtera Tbk. (EXCL), target price Rp3,600 per share

  Samuel Sekuritas rated three companies as buys:

  - PT DCI Indonesia Tbk. (DCII), target price Rp204,000 per share
  - PT Indointernet Tbk. (EDGE), target price Rp4,850 per share
  - PT Telkom Indonesia (TLKM), target price Rp3,450 per share

  ## Capacity pipeline concentrated in Jakarta and Batam

  The expansion pipeline is concentrated in two locations, according to BRI Danareksa. Jakarta holds a planned pipeline of approximately 1.7 GW against current operational capacity of 322 MW. Batam, a special economic zone near Singapore, holds a pipeline of approximately 1.4 GW against operational capacity of 126 MW.

  The analysts cited three primary demand drivers: rising cloud adoption, graphics processing unit (GPU) computing for AI model training and inference, and demand spilling over from Singapore and other parts of Southeast Asia.

  ## Telkom's NeutraDC divestiture

  Telkom's data center subsidiary NeutraDC currently operates approximately 90.4 MW of live capacity and targets expansion beyond 300 MW by 2030, BRI Danareksa said. Telkom is processing a divestiture of a 70 percent stake in NeutraDC, targeting a transaction value of US$1 billion to US$1.5 billion. BRI Danareksa said the entry of a global data center operator as a strategic partner could strengthen NeutraDC's operational capabilities and accelerate its expansion timeline.

  ## Indosat's BDx Indonesia stake

  Indosat's data center exposure comes through a 25 percent ownership stake in BDx Indonesia, which operates a 30 MW edge data center and a 72 MW hyperscale facility — designed for large-scale cloud and AI workloads — at the CGK4 Jatiluhur site. That facility is designed to scale to 500 MW, BRI Danareksa said.

  ## Pure-play data center stocks

  Su described DCII — the data center company controlled by Toto Sugiri — as among the most attractive picks because it is a pure-play data center operator with direct exposure to AI demand growth. He also highlighted EDGE (Indointernet) for its combination of data center, cloud, and connectivity businesses that support the broader digital ecosystem. Telkom, Su said, holds the largest-scale assets in fiber, data center, and cloud, though AI's contribution to Telkom's total revenue remains relatively small.

  ## Valuation risk

  Both firms flagged valuation as a key risk. Su said much of the sector's growth expectations may already be reflected in current stock prices, particularly for DCII and EDGE, which carry premium valuations.

  "Valuations are the primary factor. Stocks with profit growth that can match market expectations remain attractive, while stocks with overly premium valuations carry correction risk if expansion falls short of expectations," Su said, as quoted by Bisnis.com.

  Su also cautioned that rising data center capacity does not automatically translate into higher profits. Occupancy rates, tenant contracts, rental pricing, and capital expenditure efficiency will determine how much of a capacity build-out converts into earnings.

  Su noted that Indonesian listed companies should not expect valuation multiples equivalent to global technology companies such as Nvidia, Microsoft, or Amazon, because most Indonesian issuers operate on the infrastructure side of AI — as facility, connectivity, and cloud capacity providers — rather than as chip designers, AI model developers, or global cloud platform operators. A valuation premium remains applicable, he said, but will typically be lower than for global peers.

  ## Adjacent sector effects

  The data center expansion is expected to generate demand in adjacent sectors, according to Samuel Sekuritas. AI workloads' requirements for fiber connectivity, land for facility construction, and large-scale power consumption are expected to open growth opportunities for telecommunications operators, industrial estate developers, and energy companies.

  BRI Danareksa described Indonesia's data center industry as being in an early stage of growth with a substantial expansion pipeline ahead. Telkom's planned NeutraDC divestiture — targeting US$1 billion to US$1.5 billion for a 70 percent stake — and BDx Indonesia's Jatiluhur facility, designed to expand from its current combined 102 MW to 500 MW, represent two of the more concrete near-term milestones for the sector.

  - PT DCI Indonesia Tbk.（DCII），目標價每股盧比204,000元

  ## 擴建管線集中雅加達與巴淡島

  BRI Danareksa指出，擴建管線集中於兩大地點。雅加達的計畫管線容量約達1.7 GW，目前運營中容量為322 MW；巴淡島——鄰近新加坡的特別經濟區——計畫管線容量約為1.4 GW，目前運營中容量為126 MW。

  分析師列出三大主要需求驅動力：雲端採用持續升溫、用於AI模型訓練與推論的繪圖處理器（GPU）運算需求，以及從新加坡與東南亞其他地區溢出的需求。

  ## Telkom旗下NeutraDC的股權出售

  BRI Danareksa表示，Telkom的資料中心子公司NeutraDC目前運營中容量約為90.4 MW，目標是在2030年前擴展至300 MW以上。Telkom正在推進出售NeutraDC 70%股權的交易，目標交易金額為10億至15億美元。BRI Danareksa認為，若有全球資料中心業者以策略夥伴身分入股，可望強化NeutraDC的營運能力並加速其擴建時程。

  ## Indosat持有BDx Indonesia股權

  Indosat對資料中心的曝險，來自於持有BDx Indonesia 25%的股權。BDx Indonesia在CGK4 Jatiluhur廠區運營一座30 MW的邊緣資料中心，以及一座72 MW的超大規模設施——專為大型雲端與AI工作負載而設計。BRI Danareksa表示，該設施設計容量可擴展至500 MW。

  ## 純資料中心概念股

  Su表示，DCII——由Toto Sugiri掌控的資料中心公司——是最具吸引力的標的之一，原因在於其為純資料中心業者，可直接受惠於AI需求成長。他亦特別看好EDGE（Indointernet），理由是該公司兼具資料中心、雲端與連網業務，能支撐更廣泛的數位生態系。Su指出，Telkom在光纖、資料中心與雲端方面持有規模最大的資產，但AI對Telkom整體營收的貢獻目前仍相對有限。

  ## 估值風險

  兩家券商均將估值視為關鍵風險。Su表示，市場對此一板塊的成長預期，可能已在相當程度上反映於目前股價，尤其是DCII與EDGE，兩者的估值均屬溢價水準。

  「估值是最主要的因素。獲利成長能夠符合市場預期的標的仍具吸引力；但估值過高的標的，一旦擴建進度不如預期，便存在修正風險，」Su接受《Bisnis.com》採訪時如此表示。

  Su也提醒，資料中心容量的提升，並不會自動轉化為更高的獲利。入駐率、租戶合約、租金定價，以及資本支出效率，將決定多少建設量能最終轉換為實際盈利。

  Su指出，印尼上市公司不應期待獲得與輝達、微軟或亞馬遜等全球科技公司相當的估值倍數，因為印尼多數發行公司是在AI的基礎建設端——以設施、連網及雲端容量供應商身分——運作，而非晶片設計商、AI模型開發商或全球雲端平台業者。他表示，一定程度的估值溢價仍屬合理，但通常會低於全球同業。

  ## 相鄰產業的連帶效應

  根據Samuel Sekuritas，資料中心擴建預計將帶動相鄰產業的需求。AI工作負載對光纖連網、建設廠區用地及大規模電力消耗的需求，預計將為電信業者、工業園區開發商及能源業者帶來成長機會。

  BRI Danareksa形容印尼資料中心產業目前仍處於成長初期，前方仍有龐大的擴建管線。Telkom計畫出售NeutraDC 70%股權（目標交易金額10億至15億美元），以及BDx Indonesia Jatiluhur廠區設計規劃從目前合計102 MW擴展至500 MW，是此一產業近期最具體的兩大里程碑。
