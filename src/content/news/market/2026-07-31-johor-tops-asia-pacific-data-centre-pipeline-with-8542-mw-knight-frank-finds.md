body_en: |
  Johor held the largest incoming data centre pipeline in Asia-Pacific at 8,542 megawatts (MW) and the region's lowest co-location vacancy rate at 0.7% in the first half of 2026, according to Knight Frank's Data Centre Atlas 2026 report released Thursday. The state's total market value reached US$39.11 billion (RM159.9 billion), ranking second in the region behind Japan.

  The 0.7% vacancy rate — the share of co-location space available to tenants — placed Johor well ahead of comparable markets: Singapore stood at 4.9%, Jakarta at 20.5%, and Bangkok at 23.3%, the report said. In live IT capacity, the installed power actively serving customers, Johor's 1,110 MW ranked third across Asia-Pacific, behind Tokyo at 1,473 MW and Singapore at 1,118 MW.

  ## Investment activity in the first half of 2026

  Disclosed data centre-related land transactions in Johor totalled RM1 billion across 163.6 acres in the first half of 2026, according to the report. The period also included a RM12.7 billion investment commitment from an Australian hyperscale specialist — a company that builds and operates very large data centres for cloud and internet platforms — to develop two new facilities adding 280 MW of capacity.

  In the Klang Valley, which encompasses Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, disclosed transactions reached RM1.4 billion involving 293.1 acres over the same period, reversing subdued deal activity in the second half of 2025. Knight Frank Malaysia valuation and advisory executive director Justin Chee attributed the rebound partly to state-linked corporations and local developers taking direct roles in building and investing in new data centre sites.

  ## Cost pressures begin to narrow Johor's advantage

  Johor's growth has been underpinned by relative cost advantages in land and power, but those margins are narrowing, the report noted. Rising electricity and water tariffs are adding up to RM120 million per year in power costs for a 50 MW facility — a scale roughly sufficient to support a mid-sized cloud region — pushing operators to invest more heavily in renewable energy and alternative power solutions, Chee said.

  "What began as a secondary market to Singapore has matured into a fully institutionalised market, supported by significant regional and global capital inflows," Chee said in a statement. "However, rising electricity and water tariffs are narrowing the cost gap."

  To manage the pace of development, Johor has established a Special Technical Committee charged with evaluating proposed data centre projects against criteria including water requirements, electricity demand, utility infrastructure, and site suitability, according to the report.

  ## Policy backdrop and spillover to other states

  Knight Frank Malaysia group managing director Keith Ooi cited ongoing Middle East tensions as a factor directing capital toward markets with policy stability and predictable operating conditions. The Malaysian federal government launched the RM160 million Digital Future Action Plan 2026-2030 in October 2025, which the report identified as a catalytic policy supporting Klang Valley data centre demand.

  Investment interest has also reached beyond Johor and the Klang Valley. The report cited a proposed US$1.1 billion (RM4.1 billion) artificial intelligence data centre project and a separate RM9 billion data centre proposal in Melaka, as well as a RM1.0 billion AI park development announced for Perak.

  With Johor's Special Technical Committee now screening projects for resource and infrastructure fit, and cost pressures from power and water tariffs accelerating operator investment in renewables, the governance and cost structure of Malaysia's leading data centre market are set to shape its next phase of expansion.
body_zh: |
  根據萊特弗蘭克週四發布的《2026年資料中心地圖集》報告，馬來西亞柔佛州在2026年上半年以8,542百萬瓦（MW）的待建資料中心管線規模位居亞太地區之冠，共置空置率僅0.7%，為全區最低。柔佛州資料中心市場總值達391.1億美元（約1,599億令吉），在區域排名中僅次於日本。

  報告指出，0.7%的空置率——即可供租戶承租的共置空間比例——使柔佛遠優於其他可比市場：新加坡為4.9%、雅加達為20.5%、曼谷則達23.3%。在活躍IT裝機容量方面（即目前實際對客戶提供服務的供電規模），柔佛的1,110 MW排名亞太第三，僅次於東京的1,473 MW與新加坡的1,118 MW。

  ## 2026年上半年投資動態

  報告顯示，2026年上半年柔佛州已公開揭露的資料中心相關土地交易總額達10億令吉，涉及面積163.6英畝。同期，一家澳洲超大型資料中心專業業者——即專門為雲端及網路平台興建與營運超大規模資料中心的企業——宣布投入127億令吉的投資承諾，將興建兩座新設施，合計新增280 MW的供電容量。

  在涵蓋吉隆坡與雪蘭莪的巴生河流域，同期已揭露的交易規模達14億令吉，涉及293.1英畝土地，扭轉了2025年下半年相對低迷的市場交易態勢。萊特弗蘭克馬來西亞估價與顧問服務執行董事Justin Chee將此波反彈部分歸因於國聯企業與本地開發商直接參與新資料中心場址的興建與投資。

  ## 成本壓力逐漸侵蝕新山的競爭優勢

  柔佛州的快速成長，長期以來受惠於土地與電力的相對低廉，但這項成本差距正在縮小，報告對此有所提醒。上漲的電費與水費，使一座50 MW規模設施——大致相當於支撐一個中型雲端區域所需的規模——每年增加多達1.2億令吉的電力成本，迫使業者更積極投資再生能源與替代電力方案，Chee說道。

  「新山從新加坡的次級市場發展至今，已蛻變為一個完全機構化的成熟市場，獲得大量區域與全球資本持續挹注，」Chee在聲明中表示。「然而，電費與水費的上漲，正在逐步縮小這裡的成本優勢。」

  為了管控開發速度，柔佛州已成立一個特別技術委員會，負責依據用水需求、電力需求、公共設施基礎建設及場址適宜性等標準，審核各項資料中心開發提案，報告指出。

  ## 政策背景與外溢效應延伸至其他州屬

  萊特弗蘭克馬來西亞集團董事總經理Keith Ooi指出，持續的中東地緣緊張局勢，正促使資金流向政策穩定、營運環境可預期的市場。馬來西亞聯邦政府於2025年10月推出總額1.6億令吉的「2026至2030年數位未來行動計劃」，報告認為此舉是推動巴生河流域資料中心需求的重要政策催化劑。

  投資熱潮亦已蔓延至柔佛與巴生河流域以外的地區。報告提及馬六甲有一項擬議中的11億美元（約41億令吉）人工智慧資料中心計劃，以及另一項90億令吉的資料中心開發提案；此外，霹靂州也宣布了一項10億令吉的人工智慧園區開發計劃。

  隨著柔佛特別技術委員會開始就資源與基礎設施適配性篩選開發案，加上電費與水費壓力加速業者投資再生能源，馬來西亞首席資料中心市場的治理架構與成本結構，將深刻形塑其下一階段的擴展格局。
