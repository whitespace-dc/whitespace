body_en: |
  STTGDC, a global colocation data center provider, has secured a US$590 million green loan facility to finance the fit-out and ongoing operation of its two-building campus in Inzai City in the Greater Tokyo area, the company announced. The campus — comprising STTGDC TYO1 and STTGDC TYO2, located within Goodman Business Park — will provide a combined 70.8 megawatts of IT load capacity upon completion.

  The syndicated facility was arranged by Standard Chartered as Lead Coordinator, with Mizuho Bank, OCBC, Société Générale, and UOB participating as lenders. Standard Chartered and Société Générale also served as Green Loan Coordinators.

  ## Loan structure and environmental criteria

  STTGDC said the facility was structured in accordance with the Green Loan Principles, a globally recognised framework that requires loan proceeds to be applied to qualifying green assets. To meet the facility's eligibility criteria, the data centers must attain an agreed green building certification and achieve a design Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) — a measure of total facility energy consumption relative to IT equipment energy use, where lower is more efficient — of 1.3 or below.

  Nelson Lim, Group Chief Financial Officer of STTGDC, said the green-loan structure ties the company's financing strategy directly to the environmental performance of its assets. "It provides the capital foundation to advance the campus while maintaining a disciplined approach to growth," Lim said. "Importantly, the green-loan structure aligns our financing strategy with the environmental performance of the assets we develop, reinforcing how sustainability is integrated into our capital allocation and investment decisions."

  ## TYO1: operational facility

  STTGDC TYO1, the campus's first building, is already operational and has achieved LEED Gold certification — a third-party green building standard administered by the U.S. Green Building Council. The company said TYO1 targets an annualized PUE of less than 1.25, below the 1.3 design threshold required under the loan.

  The facility uses a closed-loop cooling system designed to eliminate ongoing water consumption under normal operating conditions. TYO1 holds ISO 22301 (business continuity management), ISO 27001 (information security management), and ISO/IEC 20000 (IT service management) certifications, and complies with Japan Data Center Council guidelines. STTGDC said TYO1 has maintained carbon-neutral operations for Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions — direct emissions from owned sources and indirect emissions from purchased energy, respectively — since it commenced operations, and continues to pursue renewable energy procurement.

  ## TYO2: AI and high-density workloads

  STTGDC TYO2 is being developed to support high-density artificial intelligence and accelerated-computing workloads, which require significantly greater power per server rack than traditional enterprise IT. The facility includes provision for future liquid-cooling deployment, a cooling method suited to the thermal demands of AI accelerator hardware. TYO2 is targeting TIA-942-C Design Certification, a data center infrastructure standard, as well as LEED Gold.

  Kiyoshi Maeda, Chief Executive Officer for Japan at STTGDC, said the campus is central to the company's long-term commitment to the country. "Customers need infrastructure that can scale with their ambitions while delivering the resilience and efficiency required for mission-critical operations," Maeda said.

  ## Market positioning

  STTGDC said the campus is designed to serve hyperscalers — large-scale cloud and internet companies that operate at significant infrastructure scale — alongside cloud service providers and enterprises with growing cloud, AI, and digital infrastructure requirements in Japan.

  The $590 million financing, structured as a green loan tied to measurable efficiency and certification benchmarks, reflects a broader pattern in data center capital markets of linking large-scale infrastructure debt to sustainability performance. The involvement of five banks across multiple geographies — including a Japanese lender in Mizuho Bank and regional institutions OCBC and UOB — indicates broad lender appetite for the asset class in the Tokyo market.

  With TYO1 already operational and TYO2 in development targeting AI-density workloads and future liquid-cooling capability, the 70.8 MW campus positions STTGDC to pursue customer growth in Japan as demand for cloud and AI compute infrastructure expands.
body_zh: |
  STTGDC是一家全球主機代管資料中心業者，日前宣布完成一筆5.9億美元的綠色聯合貸款，用於資助其位於大東京地區印西市的雙棟園區建設及後續營運。公司表示，該園區由STTGDC TYO1與STTGDC TYO2兩棟建築組成，坐落於Goodman Business Park內，預計完工後合計提供70.8百萬瓦（MW）的IT負載容量。

  本次聯貸由渣打銀行（Standard Chartered）擔任主協調行，瑞穗銀行（Mizuho Bank）、華僑銀行（OCBC）、法國興業銀行（Société Générale）及大華銀行（UOB）以貸款行身分參與。渣打銀行與法國興業銀行亦同時擔任綠色貸款協調行。

  ## 貸款架構與環保標準

  STTGDC表示，本次貸款依據「綠色貸款原則」（Green Loan Principles）架構設計，這是一套獲國際廣泛認可的框架，要求貸款資金必須用於符合資格的綠色資產。為達到本次貸款的資格條件，相關資料中心必須取得雙方議定的綠建築認證，且設計電力使用效率（Power Usage Effectiveness，PUE）須達1.3或以下。PUE是衡量設施總耗電量相對於IT設備耗電量的指標，數值越低代表能源使用效率越高。

  STTGDC集團財務長林奈爾遜（Nelson Lim）表示，綠色貸款架構將公司的融資策略與資產的環境表現直接掛鉤。「這為推進園區建設提供了資本基礎，同時確保我們對成長維持嚴謹的態度，」林奈爾遜說。「更重要的是，綠色貸款架構讓我們的融資策略與所開發資產的環境表現相互契合，強化了永續發展在我們資本配置與投資決策中的核心地位。」

  ## TYO1：已投入營運的設施

  園區首棟建築STTGDC TYO1已正式投入運營，並已取得LEED金級認證——此為美國綠建築委員會（U.S. Green Building Council）所管理的第三方綠建築標準。公司表示，TYO1的年化PUE目標低於1.25，優於貸款條款所要求的1.3設計門檻。

  TYO1採用封閉式循環冷卻系統，在正常運作條件下可消除持續性用水需求。該設施持有ISO 22301（業務持續管理）、ISO 27001（資訊安全管理）及ISO/IEC 20000（IT服務管理）等認證，並符合日本資料中心協議會的相關規範。STTGDC表示，TYO1自投入運營以來，在範疇一（Scope 1，自有設備直接排放）及範疇二（Scope 2，外購電力間接排放）的碳排放方面均已維持碳中和，並持續積極推動再生能源採購。

  ## TYO2：面向AI與高密度工作負載

  STTGDC TYO2目前正在開發中，旨在支援高密度人工智慧（AI）與加速運算工作負載。此類工作負載每個伺服器機架所需的電力，遠超傳統企業IT設施。TYO2預留了未來部署液冷技術的擴充空間，液冷是一種適用於AI加速器硬體高散熱需求的冷卻方式。TYO2的目標是取得TIA-942-C設計認證（一項資料中心基礎設施標準）及LEED金級認證。

  STTGDC日本區執行長前田清（Kiyoshi Maeda）表示，該園區是公司對日本市場長期承諾的核心所在。「客戶需要能隨其業務野心持續擴展的基礎架構，同時具備關鍵任務營運所要求的韌性與效率，」前田清說。

  ## 市場定位

  STTGDC表示，該園區的設計服務對象涵蓋超大規模業者——即以龐大基礎架構規模運作的大型雲端與網際網路公司——以及在日本具有不斷增長的雲端、AI與數位基礎架構需求的雲端服務供應商與企業客戶。

  這筆5.9億美元的融資以綠色貸款形式設計，並與可量化的能源效率及認證標準相掛鉤，反映出資料中心資本市場中將大規模基礎設施債務與永續績效連結的更廣泛趨勢。參與本次聯貸的五家銀行來自多個地區，涵蓋日本在地的瑞穗銀行，以及區域性金融機構OCBC與UOB，顯示各方貸款機構對東京市場此類資產的強烈興趣。

  隨著TYO1已正式投入運營、TYO2持續推進開發並鎖定AI高密度工作負載與未來液冷能力，這座70.8 MW的園區將使STTGDC在雲端與AI運算基礎架構需求持續擴張的浪潮中，得以積極深耕日本市場、拓展客戶基礎。
