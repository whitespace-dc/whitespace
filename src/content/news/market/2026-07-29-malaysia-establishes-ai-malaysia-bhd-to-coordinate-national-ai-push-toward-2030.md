  ## Malaysia formalizes national AI body, launches action plan and safety institute

  Malaysia has established AI Malaysia Bhd, a government-owned national artificial intelligence entity under the Ministry of Digital, Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim announced. The move formally institutionalizes and expands the National AI Office (NAIO), which was set up on December 12, 2024, into a permanent statutory body charged with coordinating AI policy, implementation, safety, and governance across the federal government as part of the country's AI Nation 2030 ambition.

  The launch, however, drew notice for what it did not include: no chief executive officer was named. Ecosystem observers had widely expected a CEO announcement at the event. Sam Majid, who served as CEO of NAIO and whose foundational work the Malaysian AI ecosystem has broadly acknowledged, completed his one-year contract plus a six-month extension by end of May, according to Digital News Asia.

  ## Mandate and structure

  According to the Ministry of Digital, AI Malaysia will serve as the lead body integrating AI policy strategy with implementation at the national level through what it describes as a whole-of-government ecosystem approach. Its principal responsibilities are organized into five areas:

  - **Policy and implementation coordination**: Connecting national policy strategies with implementation efforts across agencies
  - **High-impact monitoring**: Tracking outcomes and achievements under the National AI Action Plan 2030
  - **Trusted AI governance**: Developing ethics codes, standards, trust mechanisms, and AI-related policy coordination
  - **Strategic collaboration**: Managing international AI partnerships and collaborations
  - **AI Safety Institute function**: Serving as the national AI Safety Institute (described further below)

  Digital Minister Gobind Singh Deo said the establishment of AI Malaysia Berhad was a strategic step toward comprehensively integrating policy coordination and implementation at the national level.

  ## National AI Action Plan 2026–2030

  The government launched the National AI Action Plan 2026–2030 alongside AI Malaysia, describing it as a core nation-building agenda. The plan contains 28 discrete initiatives divided into two equal groups: 14 sectoral initiatives intended to have a direct impact on Malaysia's socioeconomic growth, and 14 supporting initiatives organized under five foundational enablers — globally competitive human capital, market-driven innovation, data and computing infrastructure, responsible governance, and sustainable financing and investment.

  The plan is aligned with the objectives of the Thirteenth Malaysia Plan and the Malaysia MADANI framework, the government's overarching development strategy, both of which place AI at the center of economic growth, public-sector transformation, and social development, the ministry said.

  ## Malaysia AI Safety Institute

  A Malaysia AI Safety Institute (MAISI) is being established as a distinct initiative within AI Malaysia. Its mandate is to build national technical capabilities in AI safety by drawing expertise from government, industry, academia, and research institutions.

  MAISI will operate through three pillars: AI research, development, commercialization, and innovation (RDCI), encompassing safety assessments, testing, and AI red-teaming; strategic collaboration for talent development, expertise sharing, and technology transfer at national and international levels; and governance, safety, and regulatory support through standards development, risk management, and compliance assurance.

  The ministry said MAISI is intended to build public confidence, strengthen national resilience against AI-related risks, and position Malaysia as a regional hub for AI safety and governance.

  ## AI Governance Bill in drafting

  A dedicated AI Governance Bill is currently being drafted as Malaysia's principal legislative instrument for the sector, the ministry said. The bill adopts a risk-based regulatory approach and is open for public consultation. The government has targeted completion of the bill by the end of this year.

  The ministry described the bill's intended purpose as providing clear legal safeguards and regulatory certainty without constraining innovation, while strengthening public and investor confidence in the country's digital ecosystem.

  ## Significance for regional positioning

  The establishment of a formal national AI body, a structured multi-year action plan, a dedicated safety institute, and pending primary legislation together represent a significant formalization of Malaysia's government AI apparatus. The concurrent absence of a named CEO for AI Malaysia leaves a key operational question open as the entity begins work.

  The AI Governance Bill, currently in public consultation, is targeted for completion by the end of 2026.

  ## 馬來西亞正式設立國家AI機構，並發布行動計畫與安全研究院

  馬來西亞總理安華·易卜拉欣宣布，政府已於數位部旗下成立國有人工智慧機構——AI Malaysia Bhd。此舉將2024年12月12日設立的國家人工智慧辦公室（NAIO）正式制度化並擴大編制，使其升格為常設法定機構，負責統籌協調全聯邦政府的AI政策、執行、安全與治理工作，以實現該國「2030人工智慧國家」的發展目標。

  然而，此次發布會也因一個缺席而引人注目——活動上並未宣布執行長人選。生態系觀察人士普遍預期會在典禮上同步公布CEO，但這項期待落空。曾任NAIO執行長的薩姆·馬吉德（Sam Majid）在業界被廣泛肯定對馬來西亞AI生態系奠基的貢獻，其一年合約加上六個月延長期已於今年五月底屆滿，此事由《Digital News Asia》報導披露。

  ## 職能定位與組織架構

  根據數位部說明，AI Malaysia將作為國家層級整合AI政策策略與執行的主責機構，採取其所描述的「全政府生態系取向」。其主要職責劃分為五大面向：

  - **政策與執行協調**：銜接各機關的國家政策策略與執行工作
  - **高影響力成效追蹤**：監測並記錄《2030國家人工智慧行動計畫》下的施政成果
  - **可信賴AI治理**：制定倫理規範、標準、信任機制及相關AI政策協調
  - **策略合作**：管理國際AI夥伴關係與合作事務
  - **AI安全研究院職能**：擔任國家層級AI安全研究院的角色（詳見下節）

  數位部長哥賓·辛格·迪歐（Gobind Singh Deo）表示，成立AI Malaysia Berhad是在國家層級全面整合政策協調與執行的策略性步驟。

  ## 2026至2030年國家人工智慧行動計畫

  政府與AI Malaysia同步發布《2026至2030年國家人工智慧行動計畫》，將其定位為核心的國家建設議程。該計畫共涵蓋28項具體行動方案，分為兩大等量群組：14項部門性方案，旨在直接帶動馬來西亞的社會經濟成長；另14項支持性方案則依循五大基礎支柱加以組織，分別為具全球競爭力的人才資本、市場驅動的創新、資料與運算基礎設施、負責任的治理，以及永續融資與投資。

  數位部指出，該計畫與《第十三大馬計畫》及馬來西亞MADANI框架兩項政府整體發展策略的目標緊密接軌，兩者均將人工智慧置於經濟成長、公共部門轉型與社會發展的核心地位。

  ## 馬來西亞AI安全研究院

  馬來西亞AI安全研究院（MAISI）將作為AI Malaysia旗下的獨立計畫設立，其任務是匯聚政府、產業、學術界及研究機構的專業能量，建構國家層級的AI安全技術能力。

  MAISI的運作將依循三大支柱：其一為AI研究、發展、商業化與創新（RDCI），涵蓋安全評估、測試及AI紅隊演練；其二為策略合作，致力於人才培育、專業交流及國內外技術移轉；其三為治理、安全與法規支援，透過標準制定、風險管理與合規保證加以落實。

  數位部表示，MAISI旨在強化社會大眾對AI的信心、提升國家抵禦AI相關風險的韌性，並將馬來西亞打造為區域AI安全與治理樞紐。

  ## AI治理法案草擬中

  數位部表示，一部專責的《人工智慧治理法案》目前正在草擬階段，將成為馬來西亞該領域的主要立法工具。該法案採取風險導向的監管取徑，並已開放公眾諮詢，政府目標是在今年底前完成立法。

  數位部說明，該法案的立法目的在於提供明確的法律保障與監管確定性，同時不妨礙創新發展，並強化社會大眾及投資人對馬來西亞數位生態系的信心。

  ## 區域定位的戰略意涵

  正式設立國家AI機構、推出結構完整的多年期行動計畫、籌建專責安全研究院，加上即將到來的主要立法，這一系列舉措共同代表馬來西亞政府AI體制的重大制度化進程。然而，AI Malaysia迄今仍未任命執行長，這在機構甫開始運作之際留下了一個關鍵的執行層懸念。

  目前正在公開諮詢中的《人工智慧治理法案》，目標在2026年底前完成立法程序。
