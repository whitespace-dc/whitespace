body_en: |
  Frankfurt-based real estate investor Westend Real Assets has acquired two colocation data centers in Hamburg, Germany, for a combined price exceeding €100 million ($111 million), the firm confirmed to Data Center Dynamics.

  The two facilities — known as Leuchtturm, located in the city's Alsterdorf district, and Rahlstedt, in the district of the same name — are operated by Akquinet, an IT solutions provider headquartered in Hamburg. Both sites were acquired as part of a broader transaction in which Westend Real Assets purchased Green Datahub (GDH), a property portfolio company previously owned by Deutsche Beteiligungs AG (DBAG), a Frankfurt-listed private equity investor that manages approximately €2.8 billion ($3.1 billion) in assets.

  The acquisition was made on behalf of Westend Real Assets' alternative investment fund, HAL Digitale Infrastruktur Deutschland 1, which focuses specifically on colocation data centers in Germany. Westend Real Assets is a subsidiary of Westend Bank and manages an estimated €750 million ($835 million) in assets overall. The precise financial terms of the broader GDH transaction were not disclosed.

  ## The two sites

  Leuchttum was built in 2013 and comprises more than 1,800 square meters (19,375 sq ft) of IT space across eight system rooms. According to DataCenterMap, it was the first colocation data center in Germany to receive the Blue Angel environmental certification, a German government label awarded for energy efficiency. Rahlstedt, completed in 2024, is the larger of the two, offering 2,350 square meters (25,295 sq ft) of IT space across 12 system rooms and housing 724 server cabinets.

  Both facilities are already operational and have long-term leases in place with Akquinet as the operator. "With Alsterdorf and Rahlstedt, we are acquiring two established sites with long-term leases that are already operational and offer scope for further growth," a Westend Real Assets spokesperson told DCD. "By acquiring the two Hamburg sites, we are capitalizing on current market opportunities in the regional colocation data center segment and strengthening the fund's portfolio."

  ## Regional colocation focus

  The deal reflects the stated strategy of HAL Digitale Infrastruktur Deutschland 1 to build a portfolio of German colocation assets outside of the country's primary hyperscale markets. Regional colocation — facilities that serve enterprise and mid-market customers in secondary cities rather than the Frankfurt, Berlin, or Munich hyperscale corridors — has drawn increasing institutional interest as investors seek stabilized, income-generating infrastructure assets with existing tenancy.

  The acquisition adds two operational sites with a combined IT footprint of roughly 4,150 square meters to the fund's holdings. Westend Real Assets said the transaction advances the fund's target performance objectives and that both Hamburg sites offer capacity for further expansion.

  法蘭克福不動產投資業者西端實資（Westend Real Assets）已向《Data Center Dynamics》確認，以合計逾1億歐元（約1.11億美元）的代價，收購德國漢堡兩座共置資料中心。

  兩座設施分別名為Leuchtturm，座落於該市阿爾斯特多夫（Alsterdorf）區，以及Rahlstedt，位於同名區域；兩者均由總部設於漢堡的IT解決方案業者Akquinet負責營運。此次收購屬於一項規模更大的交易的一部分——西端實資買下了Green Datahub（GDH），這是一家房地產投資組合公司，此前由在法蘭克福上市、管理資產規模約達28億歐元（約31億美元）的私募股權機構德意志參股（Deutsche Beteiligungs AG，DBAG）所持有。整體GDH交易的具體財務條款並未對外披露。

  此次收購以西端實資旗下另類投資基金「HAL Digitale Infrastruktur Deutschland 1」的名義進行，該基金專注於德國境內的共置資料中心投資。西端實資隸屬西端銀行（Westend Bank），整體管理資產規模估計達7.5億歐元（約8.35億美元）。

  ## 兩座設施概況

  Leuchtturm建於2013年，IT機房面積逾1,800平方公尺（約1萬9,375平方英尺），分佈於八個系統機房。根據DataCenterMap的資料，這是德國第一座獲得「藍天使」（Blue Angel）環保認證的共置資料中心——藍天使認證由德國政府頒發，專門表彰節能環保的設施或產品。Rahlstedt於2024年竣工，規模較大，IT機房面積達2,350平方公尺（約2萬5,295平方英尺），設有12個系統機房，可容納724個伺服器機架。

  兩座設施目前均已投入運營，並與營運商Akquinet簽有長期租約。西端實資發言人向DCD表示：「收購阿爾斯特多夫與拉爾斯泰特兩處，意味著我們取得了兩個已投入運營、簽有長期租約且仍具進一步成長空間的成熟據點。透過此次收購，我們正把握區域共置資料中心市場的當前機遇，並強化基金的投資組合。」

  ## 聚焦區域共置市場

  這筆交易體現了「HAL Digitale Infrastruktur Deutschland 1」基金的既定策略——在德國超大規模資料中心的主要市場以外，於全國各地建立共置資產投資組合。所謂區域共置設施，是指在法蘭克福、柏林或慕尼黑等超大規模資料中心核心廊道以外的次級城市，主要服務企業及中型市場客戶的資料中心。隨著投資人積極尋求已有穩定租戶、能產生穩定收益的基礎設施資產，此類設施正吸引日益強烈的機構投資興趣。

  此次收購使基金新增兩座運營中的設施，合計IT機房面積約4,150平方公尺。西端實資表示，此次交易有助於推進基金的目標績效，且漢堡兩座設施均具備進一步擴容的空間。
