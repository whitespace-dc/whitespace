body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers has secured a HK$1.86 billion (US$237 million) loan from four banks to fund capital expansion and refinancing at a Hong Kong data center, according to people familiar with the matter, extending a run of large financing deals for the Singapore-based company.

  ## Loan terms and lenders

  The five-year facility is priced at 152 basis points above the Hong Kong Interbank Offered Rate (HIBOR), the benchmark short-term borrowing rate among banks operating in Hong Kong. A reduced margin is available if DayOne meets sustainability-linked performance targets, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing private matters.

  Bank of East Asia contributed the largest single share at HK$955 million. DBS Group Holdings, Oversea-Chinese Banking Corp (OCBC), and United Overseas Bank (UOB) each provided HK$300 million. The borrower is a special purpose vehicle, the people added.

  Proceeds will be used for refinancing and capital expansion at the Hong Kong data center. DayOne and DBS declined to comment; OCBC, Bank of East Asia, and UOB did not respond to requests for comment.

  ## DayOne's recent financing activity

  The Hong Kong loan adds to several large transactions completed or under way at the company. Earlier this year DayOne closed half of a US$7 billion-equivalent loan, described by people familiar with the matter as one of the largest artificial intelligence (AI)-related borrowings in Asia. It also obtained a four-year S$530 million (US$414 million) loan to develop its first data center in Singapore.

  In June, DayOne closed a US$4.5 billion Series C funding round with investors including SoftBank Vision Fund, Coatue Management, and Ken Griffin.

  ## IPO timing under review

  DayOne has been planning a US initial public offering but is weighing its options, including a potential delay or reduction in deal size, as volatility around data center valuations increases, the people familiar with the matter said. The company has been pushing forward with AI-related projects at a time of growing investor wariness about whether spending on AI infrastructure will pay off.

  ## Sector-wide financing wave

  The loan is part of a broader surge in debt financing for data center development tied to AI demand. Moody's projects at least US$3 trillion in data center-related investment over the next five years.

  Recent comparable transactions include an A$4.25 billion (US$3 billion) green loan secured by Blackstone-owned AirTrunk for a Sydney data center campus, and an A$8.6 billion facility being sought by Blue Owl Capital-owned Stack Infrastructure for a Melbourne project.

  ## Company background

  DayOne was formerly known as GDS International, an international affiliate of Chinese data center operator GDS Holdings. The company rebranded last year and has since drawn international institutional investors. It operates or is developing data centers in Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong, Thailand, Finland, and Indonesia, according to its website.

  Separately, DayOne is in early-stage talks with banks for a potential US$500 million bond offering.

  DayOne Data Centers已取得18.6億港元（約2.37億美元）的銀行貸款，由四家銀行聯合承貸，用於香港一座資料中心的資本擴張與再融資，知情人士透露。這是這家總部位於新加坡的資料中心業者近期大型融資交易的最新一筆。

  ## 貸款條件與聯貸行

  這筆五年期融資的利率定於香港銀行同業拆款利率（HIBOR）加碼152個基點，HIBOR為香港銀行業之間短期借貸的基準利率。若DayOne達成與永續發展掛鉤的績效目標，可適用較低的加碼利率，知情人士透露，但要求匿名以保護私密商業資訊。

  東亞銀行認購最大單筆份額，金額達9.55億港元；星展集團、華僑銀行（OCBC）及大華銀行（UOB）則各認購3億港元。借款主體為一家特殊目的公司，知情人士補充道。

  所得款項將用於該香港資料中心的再融資及資本擴張。DayOne與星展銀行均拒絕置評；華僑銀行、東亞銀行及大華銀行則未回應置評要求。

  ## DayOne近期融資動態

  此筆香港貸款延續了DayOne近期一連串大型融資的態勢。今年稍早，DayOne完成了一筆等值70億美元貸款的一半，知情人士稱此為亞洲規模最大的人工智慧（AI）相關借貸之一；公司亦取得一筆四年期、5.3億新加坡元（約4.14億美元）的貸款，用於開發其在新加坡的第一座資料中心。

  今年6月，DayOne完成45億美元的C輪募資，投資人包括軟銀願景基金（SoftBank Vision Fund）、Coatue Management及肯·格里芬（Ken Griffin）。

  ## IPO時程仍待評估

  DayOne一直規劃赴美進行首次公開募股（IPO），但由於資料中心估值波動加劇，公司目前正評估各種選項，包括可能延後上市時程或縮減募資規模，知情人士表示。在投資者對AI基礎設施支出能否帶來實質回報的疑慮日益升溫之際，DayOne仍持續推進與AI相關的建設專案。

  ## 產業融資浪潮

  此筆貸款是AI需求推動下、資料中心債務融資浪潮中的一環。信評機構穆迪（Moody's）預測，未來五年全球資料中心相關投資金額將至少達3兆美元。

  近期可供比較的類似交易包括：黑石集團（Blackstone）旗下的AirTrunk，為雪梨一座資料中心園區取得42.5億澳元（約30億美元）的綠色貸款；以及藍梟資本（Blue Owl Capital）旗下的Stack Infrastructure，正為墨爾本一個項目尋求86億澳元的融資。

  ## 公司背景

  DayOne前身為GDS International，是中國資料中心業者萬國數據（GDS Holdings）的海外關係機構。該公司去年完成品牌重塑，此後陸續吸引多家國際機構投資人加入。根據其官方網站，DayOne目前在新加坡、馬來西亞、香港、泰國、芬蘭及印尼等地營運或籌建資料中心。

  另外，DayOne目前正與多家銀行就可能發行的5億美元債券進行初步洽談。
