body_en: |
  Hyperco has filed a permit application to build a second data center in Koria, a district of Kouvola in southern Finland, targeting 100 megawatts (MW) of capacity in a two-story, 56,670-square-metre (609,990 sq ft) building on a plot adjacent to a facility already under construction, local media including Yle and Kouvolan Sanomat reported.

  The application was filed earlier this month. The Kouvola Technical Committee is scheduled to consider it on Tuesday, June 16, 2026.

  Hyperco has asked to be permitted to begin foundation work before the building permit formally takes effect, citing the need to complete groundwork before the soil freezes in winter.

  ## First site still under construction

  Construction on Hyperco's first Koria building began last year. That single-story facility is being developed alongside data center developer DayOne and is set to serve TikTok, with completion scheduled for May 2027. The proposed second building would sit on an adjacent plot.

  Kouvola is the administrative capital of the Kymenlaakso region, approximately 135 kilometres (83 miles) northeast of Helsinki.

  ## Company background

  Hyperco was founded in 2020. In April 2025, it was acquired by Edgnex, the data center arm of Dubai-based real estate firm Damac.

  The company made its first property acquisition in 2022, purchasing a 16,900-square-metre (181,900 sq ft) facility in Vantaa from Finnish family office TS-Yhtymä for more than €35 million ($36.4 million). That building at the time had two long-term tenants: networking and colocation provider Equinix and technology and defence firm Thales.

  ## Broader Finland pipeline

  Beyond Kouvola, Hyperco has projects in development in Espoo, Pyhäjoki, and Lohja. The company is also linked to a separate site in the Voikkaa industrial area within Kouvola municipality, meaning the city could ultimately host multiple distinct Hyperco campuses.

  The second permit filing signals that Kouvola is taking shape as a multi-building campus location for Hyperco, extending a development push across Finland that has accelerated since the Edgnex acquisition. The Kouvola Technical Committee's decision on June 16 will determine whether Hyperco can proceed with pre-winter foundation work on the 100 MW building.

  北歐資料中心業者 Hyperco 已向芬蘭南部科沃拉市（Kouvola）的科里亞（Koria）地區提出第二座資料中心的建照申請。據當地媒體 Yle 及《科沃拉新聞》（Kouvolan Sanomat）報導，這棟兩層樓建築規劃樓地板面積達 56,670 平方公尺（60 萬 9,990 平方英尺），目標容量為 100 MW，基地緊鄰 Hyperco 已在施工中的第一座設施。

  申請案已於本月稍早提出。科沃拉技術委員會（Kouvola Technical Committee）預計於 2026 年 6 月 16 日（星期二）審議此案。

  Hyperco 在申請中要求取得在建照正式生效前即可率先進行基礎工程的許可，理由是必須趕在冬季土壤凍結之前完成地基作業。

  ## 第一座廠房仍在施工中

  Hyperco 在科里亞的第一棟建物於去年動工。這座單層樓設施由 Hyperco 與資料中心開發商 DayOne 合作開發，預計將為 TikTok 提供服務，完工時間訂於 2027 年 5 月。第二棟擬建建物將座落於相鄰地塊。

  科沃拉是 Kymenlaakso 地區的行政中心，位於赫爾辛基（Helsinki）東北方約 135 公里（83 英里）處。

  ## 公司背景

  Hyperco 成立於 2020 年，並於 2025 年 4 月被 Edgnex 收購。Edgnex 是杜拜房地產集團 Damac 旗下的資料中心事業部門。

  Hyperco 的首次不動產收購發生於 2022 年，當時以逾 3,500 萬歐元（約 3,640 萬美元）的價格，向芬蘭家族辦公室 TS-Yhtymä 購入位於萬塔（Vantaa）的一棟 16,900 平方公尺（18 萬 1,900 平方英尺）廠房。該建物當時已有兩家長期租戶：網路與主機代管服務業者 Equinix，以及科技與國防企業 Thales。

  ## 芬蘭更廣泛的開發佈局

  除科沃拉外，Hyperco 在埃斯波（Espoo）、皮耶約基（Pyhäjoki）及洛赫亞（Lohja）亦有開發中的專案。此外，該公司還與科沃拉市轄內 Voikkaa 工業區的另一塊基地有所關聯，意味著這座城市最終可能擁有多個彼此獨立的 Hyperco 園區。

  第二份建照申請的提出，顯示科沃拉正逐步成形為 Hyperco 多棟建物並立的園區重鎮，延續了該公司自 Edgnex 收購案完成後加速推進的芬蘭擴張態勢。科沃拉技術委員會將於 6 月 16 日就此案作出裁示，決定 Hyperco 能否在冬季來臨前對這棟 100 MW 建物展開基礎工程。
