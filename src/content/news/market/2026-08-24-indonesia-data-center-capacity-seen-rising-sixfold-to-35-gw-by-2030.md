body_en: |
  Indonesia's data center industry carried roughly 580 megawatts (MW) of installed IT capacity as of the first half of 2026 and is on course to reach 3.5 gigawatts (GW) by 2030, equity research analysts at BRI Danareksa Sekuritas wrote in a note published Saturday. One gigawatt equals 1,000 MW; the projected increase represents a near-sixfold rise over four years.

  Analysts Kafi Ananta and Erindra Krisnawan at BRI Danareksa Sekuritas, an Indonesian securities firm, described the industry as still in its early stages of growth, even as operators build out a large expansion pipeline. That combination — modest current installed base, substantial planned capacity — is what the analysts said creates opportunity for publicly listed companies in Indonesia to capture rising demand for digital infrastructure in the years ahead.

  ## Early stage, large pipeline

  The 580 MW figure represents the IT load capacity already energized and in service across Indonesia's data center market in H1 2026. IT load capacity measures the power available to run servers and networking equipment inside a facility, excluding the overhead used for cooling and other building systems, and is the standard industry benchmark for comparing market scale.

  The projected 3.5 GW target for 2030 implies compound annual additions well above Indonesia's current installed base. The BRI Danareksa Sekuritas analysts framed the gap between present capacity and that target as the defining characteristic of the market: a long runway for growth rather than a mature, saturated sector.

  ## What it means for listed companies

  The analysts tied the capacity outlook directly to investment prospects for Indonesian stock exchange-listed companies, or emiten, with data center exposure. The early-stage characterization suggests that much of the revenue and earnings growth tied to the projected 3.5 GW build-out has yet to materialize in public company financials, leaving room for multiple years of expansion ahead for operators and infrastructure providers with existing market positions.

  The 3.5 GW projection for Indonesia by 2030 is the most forward-looking figure the analysts cited.

  ## 印尼資料中心裝置容量預計2030年前增至3.5GW，六倍成長可期

  印尼資料中心產業於2026年上半年的IT裝置容量約達580百萬瓦（MW），印尼券商BRI Danareksa Sekuritas的股票研究分析師於週六發布研究報告指出，預計至2030年，該市場容量將達到3.5吉瓦（GW）。1吉瓦等於1,000百萬瓦；此一預測意味著四年間近六倍的成長幅度。

  BRI Danareksa Sekuritas分析師Kafi Ananta與Erindra Krisnawan在報告中指出，儘管業者正積極推進大規模擴建計畫，印尼資料中心產業整體仍處於成長初期階段。正是這種「現有裝置基礎相對有限、計畫擴建規模龐大」的組合，讓分析師認為，印尼上市公司有機會在未來數年間持續捕捉數位基礎建設需求攀升所帶來的商機。

  ## 早期階段，擴建管線充沛

  580 MW的數字代表2026年上半年印尼全國資料中心市場已完成通電、正式投入使用的IT負載容量。IT負載容量衡量的是資料中心設施內可供伺服器及網路設備實際運行的電力容量，不含冷卻系統及其他建築設備所需的額外電力開銷，是業界比較市場規模時通用的標準指標。

  2030年3.5 GW的預測目標，意味著每年新增容量將遠超印尼目前的裝置基礎。BRI Danareksa Sekuritas的分析師將現有容量與目標之間的巨大差距，定性為這個市場最核心的特徵——代表的不是一個趨於成熟、接近飽和的市場，而是一條仍有大量空間可供成長的長跑賽道。

  ## 上市公司的投資意涵

  分析師將此一容量展望與印尼證券交易所上市公司（即印尼慣稱的emiten）在資料中心領域的投資前景直接掛鉤。市場仍處早期階段的定性，意味著預計3.5 GW建設規模所帶動的營收與獲利成長，尚未大量反映在上市公司的財務報表中，現有業者與基礎建設供應商未來仍有多年擴張空間可期。

  分析師在報告中援引的最具前瞻性數據，即為印尼2030年3.5 GW的容量預測目標。
