  ## Groundbreaking Set for October 20

  Is-Fun International (是方電訊) will break ground on a NT$3.3 billion (approximately US$100 million) AI data center in Huwei, Yunlin County, Taiwan on October 20, 2026, with pre-lease letters of intent already exceeding 50 percent of capacity, the company said. The facility is scheduled to enter commercial operation in the third quarter of 2028.

  The volume of pre-lease interest signals continued strong demand for AI-oriented data center infrastructure, according to Is-Fun. The company said the Huwei AIDC (AI Data Center) will be modeled on its operating LY2 facility — the Lianyun AI Intelligent Data Center — and will be built to the international Tier 4 standard, the highest classification for data center reliability and redundancy.

  ## Technical Specifications

  The Huwei facility will support both air-cooling and liquid water-cooling thermal management solutions, giving tenants flexibility to house both conventional and high-density AI compute hardware. The design also meets the requirements of Taiwan's EEWH green building certification (Ecology, Energy Saving, Waste Reduction, and Health), the company said.

  ## High-Profile Attendance at Ceremony

  Is-Fun Chairman Shao Hong-jia (邵泓嘉) and Chief Executive Liu Yao-yuan (劉耀元) will host the groundbreaking. The guest list reflects the facility's positioning as a regional infrastructure hub: attendees include Telstra Taiwan General Manager Joanne Wang, Taiwan Financial Holdings Chairwoman Ling Chung-yuan (凌忠嫄), Chunghwa Telecom Executive Vice President Chen Yuan-kai (陳元凱), and representatives from TSMC and Formosa Plastics Group.

  Government representation will include Yunlin County Deputy Commissioner Chen Bi-jun (陳璧君), Central Taiwan Science Park Bureau Director Hsu Mao-hsin (許茂新), and Ministry of Economic Affairs Energy Administration Deputy Director Chen Chung-hsien (陳崇憲).

  ## Japan Connectivity Corridor

  Nagatakatoshi Yanagawa (柳川直隆), the director of Hokkaido Ishikari Renewable Energy Data Center in Japan, will travel to Taiwan specifically for the ceremony. Is-Fun and the Hokkaido Ishikari facility are currently planning a high-speed Asia AI computing corridor linking Taipei to Hokkaido, the company said, designed to help Taiwanese businesses deploy cross-border AI service nodes in the Japanese market.

  ## Financial Context

  Is-Fun reported cumulative revenue of NT$3.103 billion for the first nine months of 2026, up 2.82 percent year-on-year. First-half earnings per share stood at NT$7.57. CEO Liu said the company's outlook for the fourth quarter is positive, with Q4 revenue and profit expected to surpass Q3 results and full-year performance projected to exceed 2025 levels.

  The company said it has recently added new customers from domestic and international AI computing providers as well as financial-sector clients. As those customers accelerate their move-in and the share of higher-margin services rises, Is-Fun is targeting a full-year gross margin above 50 percent.

  ## Industry Significance

  The Huwei project extends Is-Fun's positioning as a large-scale international IDC ecosystem in Taiwan, combining network interconnection, cloud services, and international bandwidth. The facility's Tier 4 build standard and dual-cooling capability are aimed directly at the infrastructure requirements of AI workloads, which demand higher power density and more resilient cooling than conventional enterprise computing. The 50-percent pre-lease rate ahead of groundbreaking, and the planned Taipei-Hokkaido AI corridor, indicate that demand from both domestic and cross-border AI deployment is driving the investment.

  The Huwei AIDC is scheduled to begin contributing revenue and profit to Is-Fun's financial results from Q3 2028.

  ## 動土典禮訂於10月20日

  是方電訊（Is-Fun International）將於2026年10月20日在雲林縣虎尾鎮舉行AI資料中心動土典禮，總投資額達新台幣33億元（約合1億美元），目前預租意向書已超過可租用容量的五成，該公司表示。此設施預計於2028年第三季正式投入商業營運。

  如此高度的預租需求，顯示市場對AI導向資料中心基礎設施的需求持續強勁，是方電訊表示。該公司指出，虎尾AIDC（AI資料中心）將以旗下已在營運的LY2設施——聯雲AI智慧資料中心——為藍本進行規劃，並將依照國際Tier 4標準興建，這是資料中心可靠性與備援能力的最高等級認證。

  ## 技術規格

  虎尾設施將同時支援氣冷與液態水冷兩種散熱解決方案，讓租戶在部署一般運算設備與高密度AI運算硬體時均享有充分彈性。此外，其設計亦符合台灣綠建築標章（EEWH，涵蓋生態、節能、減廢、健康四大指標）的認證要求，是方電訊表示。

  ## 動土典禮重量級出席陣容

  是方電訊董事長邵泓嘉與執行長劉耀元將共同主持此次動土典禮。來賓名單充分彰顯該設施作為區域基礎設施樞紐的定位：出席嘉賓包括Telstra台灣區總經理王喬安（Joanne Wang）、台灣金控董事長凌忠嫄、中華電信執行副總陳元凱，以及台積電與台塑集團的代表人士。

  政府方面，出席代表包括雲林縣副縣長陳璧君、中部科學園區管理局局長許茂新，以及經濟部能源署副署長陳崇憲。

  ## 日本連接走廊

  日本北海道石狩再生能源資料中心董事柳川直隆（Nagatakatoshi Yanagawa）將專程赴台出席典禮。是方電訊表示，該公司正與北海道石狩設施合作規劃一條高速亞洲AI運算走廊，串連台北至北海道，旨在協助台灣企業在日本市場部署跨境AI服務節點。

  ## 財務概況

  是方電訊2026年前三季累計營收達新台幣31.03億元，年增2.82%。上半年每股盈餘（EPS）為新台幣7.57元。執行長劉耀元表示，公司對第四季展望正向，預期第四季營收與獲利均將超越第三季，全年績效亦預計優於2025年水準。

  該公司表示，近期已陸續新增來自國內外AI運算業者及金融業客戶。隨著這批客戶加快進駐腳步、高毛利服務佔比持續提升，是方電訊目標將全年毛利率拉高至50%以上。

  ## 產業意義

  虎尾專案進一步強化是方電訊在台灣建構大型國際IDC生態系的市場定位，整合網路互聯、雲端服務與國際頻寬等多元能力。Tier 4建置標準與雙模冷卻設計，直接瞄準AI工作負載的基礎設施需求——此類應用對電力密度與散熱穩定性的要求，均遠高於傳統企業運算。動土前即達五成預租率，加上規劃中的台北至北海道AI走廊，均顯示國內外AI部署需求正持續推動相關投資。

  虎尾AIDC預計自2028年第三季起，開始挹注是方電訊的營收與獲利。
