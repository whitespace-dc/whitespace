body_en: |
  VITRO, a wholly-owned subsidiary of ePLDT — itself a unit of Philippine telecoms company PLDT — has filed a Real Estate Investment Trust (REIT) plan and registration statement with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) of the Philippines, moving the company closer to an initial public offering (IPO) it expects to complete in the fourth quarter of 2026. The offering is expected to raise as much as US$400 million and, if listed, would make VITRO REIT the first digital infrastructure REIT in the Philippines.

  A REIT is a publicly traded vehicle that pools income-producing real assets — in this case, data centers — and distributes the returns to shareholders, allowing retail and institutional investors to gain exposure to infrastructure they could not otherwise access directly.

  ## Offer structure and size

  According to the SEC filing, ePLDT will make available up to 1.9 billion secondary common shares in VITRO REIT, with an over-allotment option of up to an additional 286 million secondary common shares. An over-allotment option, sometimes called a greenshoe, gives the issuer the ability to sell extra shares if investor demand exceeds the base offering. The shares are priced at up to US$0.18 each. Together, the base offer and the over-allotment option represent 48.95 percent of VITRO REIT's issued and outstanding stock.

  ## Portfolio

  The IPO will be underpinned by a portfolio of eight data center assets, spanning both Tier 2 and Tier 3 facilities — classifications that indicate increasing levels of redundancy, reliability, and uptime — with a combined capacity of 24 megawatts (MW). To put that in context, 24 MW is sufficient to power a mid-sized colocation facility serving enterprise, hyperscale cloud, and general enterprise customers, which the filing confirms are VITRO REIT's target tenants.

  ## Market context

  The filing cites the Philippines colocation data center market as having been valued at US$153 million in 2024, with a projected compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 27.68 percent through 2030, by which point the market is expected to reach US$663 million. The filing attributes that growth trajectory to rising demand for artificial intelligence services, broader digitalization trends, and government support for digital infrastructure.

  ## Company statements

  "Today's filing marks an important step in our efforts to unlock value from PLDT Group's digital infrastructure portfolio while supporting the continued expansion of VITRO REIT's data center platform," Victor Genuino, President and CEO of both ePLDT and VITRO REIT, said in a press release. Genuino also described the IPO as creating "an opportunity for investors to participate in the growth of one of the country's most critical digital infrastructure sectors."

  ## What comes next

  PLDT said it will issue a separate disclosure regarding VITRO REIT's listing on the Philippine Stock Exchange once the SEC formally acknowledges the submitted registration statement. The IPO itself remains subject to regulatory review and approval before the expected Q4 2026 listing.

  ## PLDT旗下VITRO申請菲律賓首檔數位基礎設施REIT IPO

  ePLDT的全資子公司VITRO——而ePLDT本身隸屬於菲律賓電信公司PLDT——已正式向菲律賓證券交易委員會（SEC）遞交不動產投資信託（REIT）計畫書及申請說明書，距該公司預計於2026年第四季完成的首次公開募股（IPO）又邁進一步。本次募股預計最高可籌集4億美元，一旦順利掛牌，VITRO REIT將成為菲律賓首檔數位基礎設施REIT。

  REIT是一種公開交易的投資工具，透過匯集能產生收益的實體資產——本案為資料中心——並將收益分配給股東，使散戶及法人投資人得以參與原本難以直接投資的基礎設施項目。

  ## 發行架構與規模

  根據向SEC提交的申請文件，ePLDT將提供最多19億股VITRO REIT次級普通股，另設有最多2億8,600萬股次級普通股的超額配售選擇權。超額配售選擇權（有時稱為「綠鞋條款」）賦予發行方在投資人需求超過基本發行量時，得額外出售股票的權利。每股訂價最高為0.18美元。基本發行量加上超額配售選擇權，合計占VITRO REIT已發行及流通股份的48.95%。

  ## 資產組合

  本次IPO以八座資料中心資產作為支撐，涵蓋Tier 2及Tier 3等級設施——此分級代表冗餘度、可靠性與正常運行時間的遞增水準——合計容量達24百萬瓦（MW）。就規模而言，24 MW足以支撐一座中型主機代管設施，服務企業、超大規模雲端及一般企業客戶，申請文件亦確認上述客群正是VITRO REIT的目標租戶。

  ## 市場背景

  申請文件引用資料指出，菲律賓主機代管資料中心市場於2024年的規模達1億5,300萬美元，預計至2030年的複合年均成長率（CAGR）為27.68%，屆時市場規模可望達到6億6,300萬美元。文件將此成長軌跡歸因於人工智慧服務需求攀升、數位化趨勢持續深化，以及政府對數位基礎設施的政策支持。

  ## 公司聲明

  「今日的申請是我們致力於釋放PLDT集團數位基礎設施投資組合價值、同時支持VITRO REIT資料中心平台持續擴張的重要一步，」ePLDT及VITRO REIT總裁暨執行長Victor Genuino在新聞稿中表示。Genuino同時將本次IPO定位為「為投資人創造參與菲律賓最關鍵數位基礎設施領域成長的機會」。

  ## 後續進展

  PLDT表示，待SEC正式受理已提交的申請說明書後，將另行發布VITRO REIT於菲律賓證券交易所掛牌的相關公告。IPO本身仍須經過主管機關審查與核准，方能如期於2026年第四季完成掛牌。
