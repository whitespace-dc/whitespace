body_en: |
  Singapore has accumulated $9.3 billion in artificial intelligence funding through July 2026, while Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, and Thailand together have attracted under $40 million combined, according to a report by market data platform Tracxn.

  Singapore's total is more than 230 times the combined figure for those four neighboring countries. Tracxn noted that the figures measure where companies chose to incorporate and raise funds, not where AI products are built or deployed; many firms that register in Singapore develop and sell their technology elsewhere in the region.

  ## Late-stage deals now dominate regional investment

  Across Southeast Asia, AI-company funding has accelerated sharply over the past two years. Tracxn data show approximately 67 percent of all AI investment in the region has been raised since the start of 2025. The composition of that investment has also shifted: before 2024, capital flowed mainly to early-stage startups testing products and markets; since 2025, large late-stage transactions have dominated.

  Late-stage AI funding in Southeast Asia reached $3.5 billion in the first seven months of 2026, up from $1.3 billion for all of 2025, according to Tracxn.

  A single transaction accounts for most of that growth. Kling AI's $2.8 billion Series D round contributed roughly 68 percent of the region's total AI funding through July 2026, Tracxn reported — a concentration that means the headline regional figures are heavily shaped by one company's capital raise.

  ## Singapore's financing infrastructure

  Singapore's Economic Development Board reports that the city-state hosts more than 4,500 technology startups, 500 venture capital firms, and 220 incubators and accelerators. That density of financing infrastructure separates Singapore from its neighbors, which Tracxn said still lag in investor networks, late-stage capital availability, and global investor participation.

  The Singapore government designated AI a national economic priority through its National AI Strategy 2.0, launched in 2023, which targets global AI leadership by 2030 and covers talent training, computing capacity, enterprise adoption, and regulatory development.

  In 2026 the government added the National AI Impact Programme, aimed at driving AI adoption by companies and workers. Tracxn characterized the programme as a shift in policy emphasis from developing AI technology to putting it to work in actual business settings.

  ## What the gap means for the region

  Tracxn's report drew a distinction between the concentration of AI funding and the geography of AI development. The disparity in capital, the report said, is less about which country is producing more innovation and more about which one offers easier access to large-scale financing.

  Startup AI activity is growing across Southeast Asia, Tracxn noted, but the conditions for late-stage investment — the large rounds that allow proven companies to scale — remain concentrated in Singapore. The shift toward fewer, larger transactions signals investor preference for companies with demonstrated revenue and expansion capacity over broad early-stage bets spread across the region.

  Singapore's 2026 National AI Impact Programme represents the government's next policy phase: converting the city-state's established capital-raising advantage into measurable AI deployment across businesses and the workforce.

  根據市場數據平台Tracxn的報告，新加坡截至2026年7月已累積93億美元的人工智慧資金，而印尼、越南、馬來西亞與泰國四國合計吸引的資金不足4,000萬美元。

  新加坡的總額是上述四個鄰國合計數字的230倍以上。Tracxn指出，這些數據反映的是企業選擇設立並募資的地點，而非AI產品實際開發或部署的所在地；許多在新加坡登記的公司，其技術的開發與銷售實際上遍及東南亞各地。

  ## 晚期交易主導區域投資格局

  縱觀整個東南亞，AI企業的募資活動在過去兩年間大幅加速。Tracxn的數據顯示，該地區約67%的AI投資是在2025年初以後募得的。投資的組成結構也已出現轉變：2024年以前，資金主要流向處於測試產品與市場階段的早期新創公司；2025年以來，規模龐大的晚期交易已成為主流。

  根據Tracxn的數據，2026年前七個月東南亞晚期AI募資金額達35億美元，相較於2025年全年的13億美元大幅攀升。

  這一成長主要由單筆交易所驅動。Tracxn報告指出，Kling AI的28億美元D輪融資，佔截至2026年7月東南亞AI資金總額的約68%——這種高度集中的現象，意味著區域整體數字在很大程度上受到單一公司募資活動的牽引。

  ## 新加坡的融資基礎建設

  新加坡經濟發展局的資料顯示，這個城市國家擁有逾4,500家科技新創公司、500家創投公司，以及220家育成與加速器機構。如此密集的融資基礎建設使新加坡與周邊國家拉開了差距；Tracxn指出，這些鄰國在投資人網絡、晚期資金取得，以及全球投資人參與度等方面仍相對落後。

  新加坡政府於2023年推出「國家AI戰略2.0」，將人工智慧列為國家經濟優先要務，目標是在2030年前躋身全球AI領導地位，計畫涵蓋人才培育、運算能量、企業導入及法規建設等面向。

  2026年，政府進一步推出「國家AI影響計畫」，旨在推動企業與勞動者廣泛採用AI技術。Tracxn將這項計畫定性為政策重心的一次轉移——從發展AI技術本身，轉向將其落地應用於真實的商業環境。

  ## 差距對整個區域的意義

  Tracxn的報告區分了AI資金集中與AI發展地理分布之間的差異。報告指出，資金上的落差，與其說是哪個國家產出了更多創新，不如說是哪個國家能更便捷地取得大規模融資。

  Tracxn指出，AI新創活動正在東南亞各地持續成長，但晚期投資所需的條件——也就是能讓已獲驗證的企業進一步擴張的大額融資——仍高度集中於新加坡。交易走向「更少、更大」的趨勢，反映出投資人傾向於選擇具備可驗證營收與擴張能力的企業，而非將資金分散押注在各地的早期新創。

  新加坡2026年推出的「國家AI影響計畫」代表政府的下一個政策階段：將這座城市國家已確立的募資優勢，轉化為企業與勞動力中可量化的AI實際部署成果。
