body_en: |
  A Manila lawmaker has called on Philippine government agencies to overhaul engineering regulations and expand technical training to close a workforce gap he says could prevent the country from capturing jobs in semiconductor manufacturing, artificial intelligence, and other advanced industries tied to the US-led Pax Silica initiative.

  Manila Rep. Rolando M. Valeriano on Sunday urged the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), the Commission on Higher Education (CHED), and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) to work with industry leaders to address engineer and technician shortages. He grounded the call in an assessment by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) that identified shortages of skilled engineers in the Philippine semiconductor industry.

  "We need to address these shortages before more advanced manufacturing companies establish operations in the country," Valeriano said in a statement.

  ## Curriculum and training demands

  Valeriano called on CHED to coordinate with universities to update engineering curricula to reflect the requirements of advanced manufacturing. He urged TESDA to expand specialized training in semiconductor operations, robotics, and industrial automation.

  He also called on the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) to identify technical positions expected to be in demand in coming years, framing early mapping of workforce needs as a prerequisite for effective training investment.

  ## Legislation under review

  Valeriano pointed to two pending legislative measures as instruments for reform. House Bill No. 7274 would update professional regulation laws governing engineering and related technical fields, amending Republic Act No. 8495 — the Philippine Mechanical Engineering Act of 1998. He called on Congress to review and modernize that law alongside other professional regulation statutes.

  The second measure, House Bill No. 11758, the proposed Advanced Industries Development Act, would establish an Advanced Industries Zone in New Clark City, Tarlac. Under that bill, companies employing foreign nationals in certain positions would be required to train at least two Filipino regular employees for every foreign national in those roles — a provision designed to ensure technology and skills transfer to local workers.

  ## What comes next

  Valeriano's statement positions workforce readiness as a prerequisite for investment attraction rather than a consequence of it, with regulatory modernization, curriculum reform, and legislative action presented as parallel tracks. The proposed Advanced Industries Zone in New Clark City, Tarlac, is the most concrete geographic focal point identified for the country's advanced manufacturing ambitions under Pax Silica. Whether the PRC, CHED, TESDA, DTI, and DOLE respond with coordinated action, and whether HB 7274 and HB 11758 advance through Congress, will determine the pace of the workforce build-out he is calling for.
body_zh: |
  馬尼拉一名國會議員呼籲菲律賓政府各機關全面改革工程師執照法規、擴大技職訓練，以彌補他所指出的人力缺口。他警告，若此問題不及時解決，恐將使菲律賓無從把握美國主導的「矽石和平」（Pax Silica）倡議所帶來的半導體製造、人工智慧及其他先進產業就業機會。

  馬尼拉眾議員羅蘭多·M·瓦列里亞諾於週日呼籲專業管理委員會（PRC）、高等教育委員會（CHED）及技術教育及技能發展局（TESDA）與業界領袖攜手合作，共同解決工程師與技術人員短缺的問題。他援引經濟合作暨發展組織（OECD）的一份評估報告——該報告點名指出菲律賓半導體產業存在熟練工程師嚴重短缺的困境。

  「我們必須在更多先進製造業者於菲律賓設立據點之前，解決這些人才缺口，」瓦列里亞諾在聲明中表示。

  ## 課程與訓練需求

  瓦列里亞諾呼籲CHED與各大學協調，更新工程系所課程，使其切實反映先進製造業的實際需求。他也敦促TESDA擴大半導體操作、機器人技術及工業自動化等領域的專業訓練。

  他同時呼籲貿易工業部（DTI）與勞動就業部（DOLE）提前盤點未來幾年預期短缺的技術性職位，將勞動力需求的早期規劃定位為有效訓練投資不可或缺的前提條件。

  ## 待審法案

  瓦列里亞諾指出兩項待審法案可作為推動改革的法制工具。第7274號眾議院法案將更新管理工程及相關技術領域的專業規範法律，修訂對象為共和國法第8495號——即1998年頒布的《菲律賓機械工程法》。他呼籲國會在審查這部法律的同時，一併現代化其他專業管理法規。

  第二項法案為第11758號眾議院法案，即擬議中的《先進產業發展法》，旨在塔拉克省新克拉克城（New Clark City）設立「先進產業區」。依據該法案，凡聘用外籍人士擔任特定職位的企業，每聘用一名外籍人士，須同時培訓至少兩名菲律賓本地正式員工——此條款旨在確保技術與技能能夠實質移轉給本地勞工。

  ## 後續展望

  瓦列里亞諾的聲明將勞動力準備程度定位為吸引投資的前提，而非投資到位後才跟進的配套措施，並將法規現代化、課程改革與立法推動視為必須同步並進的三條軌道。位於塔拉克省新克拉克城的擬議「先進產業區」，是目前菲律賓在Pax Silica框架下推動先進製造業雄心、最具體的地理焦點所在。PRC、CHED、TESDA、DTI與DOLE能否展開協調行動，以及第7274號與第11758號眾議院法案能否順利在國會推進，將決定他所倡議的人力建設能以多快的速度付諸實現。
