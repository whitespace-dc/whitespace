body_en: |
  Indigo has appointed Nick Barton as Chief Revenue Officer, placing a former Aqua Comms executive in the group's top commercial role as the company pushes to expand across subsea, cloud, and network infrastructure markets in more than 90 countries.

  Barton joins from Aqua Comms, where he served as Chief Commercial Officer. Before that, he held senior commercial roles at Verizon Business and Colt Technology Services, giving him broad exposure to cross-border network services and large international enterprise customers.

  In his new role, Barton will lead commercial strategy and international growth, and manage strategic relationships across subsea, data centre, cloud, carrier, fixed, and mobile networks, according to the company.

  ## Indigo's operational scale

  Founded in 1998, Indigo describes itself as an operational partner for critical digital infrastructure, offering design, deployment, support, and scaling services through a consistent model across markets. The company operates in more than 90 countries, providing delivery, support, field engineering, and service operations for around-the-clock control of digital infrastructure.

  Its customer base spans hyperscalers, carriers, subsea cable owners, mobile network operators, data centre operators, and other digital infrastructure providers. Indigo handles more than 30,000 incidents per year through its network and security operations centres and global service desk, supported by more than 3,500 engineers.

  ## Subsea activity and growth context

  The appointment comes as Indigo has been active in subsea operations. The company has agreed heads of terms with Aqua Comms on the possible acquisition of Aqua Comms' network operations centre, and has formed a partnership with Trans Pacific Networks covering subsea operations across the United States and Asia.

  Barton's prior role at Aqua Comms makes him directly familiar with at least one of those ongoing deals. His mandate at Indigo is intended to align commercial operations more closely with the company's global operating model, as competition increases across international connectivity, cloud infrastructure, and data centre services.

  ## Executive statements

  Barton said the company's existing operational base provided scope for further expansion. "Indigo has a strong foundation supporting hyperscalers, carriers and network operators with the operational experience needed to keep essential infrastructure running reliably," he said. "With a best-in-class global operating model, the opportunity now is to translate that expertise into hyperscale performance for every customer."

  Chief Executive Officer Michel Robert said the timing of the hire reflects Indigo's current growth trajectory. "Nick's appointment comes at an important time for Indigo as we continue to scale our global service capabilities," Robert said. "His commercial experience, customer insight and background across hyperscaler accounts and international connectivity make him the perfect fit to support Indigo's next stage of growth."

  ## Significance

  The appointment signals that Indigo is prioritising commercial coordination across its international footprint as it seeks to deepen relationships with hyperscalers and subsea infrastructure customers. Bringing in an executive with direct experience at a company currently in acquisition discussions with Indigo suggests the company wants commercial leadership with first-hand knowledge of the deals and market segments it is actively pursuing.

  Indigo's next stage of international expansion will include pursuing the potential acquisition of Aqua Comms' network operations centre and continuing its subsea partnership with Trans Pacific Networks across the US and Asia.

  ## Indigo延攬前Aqua Comms高管Nick Barton出任首席營收長

  Barton此前任職Aqua Comms，擔任首席商務長（Chief Commercial Officer）。在此之前，他曾於Verizon Business及Colt Technology Services擔任資深商務要職，累積了豐富的跨境網路服務與大型跨國企業客戶經驗。

  據公司表示，Barton在新職位上將統籌商業策略與國際業務拓展，並負責管理橫跨海底電纜、資料中心、雲端、電信業者、固網及行動網路等各領域的策略夥伴關係。

  ## Indigo的營運規模

  Indigo成立於1998年，自我定位為關鍵數位基礎建設的營運夥伴，透過跨市場一致性模式，提供設計、部署、支援與擴展服務。公司業務遍及逾90個國家，提供交付、支援、現場工程及服務營運，以全天候掌控數位基礎建設。

  其客戶群涵蓋超大規模雲端業者、電信業者、海底電纜業主、行動網路業者、資料中心業者及其他數位基礎建設供應商。Indigo透過旗下網路與資安營運中心及全球服務台，每年處理逾3萬件事件，背後有逾3,500名工程師提供支援。

  ## 海底電纜業務與成長背景

  此次任命正值Indigo在海底電纜業務上積極布局之際。公司已與Aqua Comms就收購其網路營運中心達成原則性協議，並與Trans Pacific Networks締結夥伴關係，共同拓展涵蓋美國及亞洲的海底電纜業務。

  Barton先前在Aqua Comms的任職經歷，使他對上述進行中的交易之一有著直接的第一手了解。他在Indigo的任務，在於將商業運作與公司全球營運模式更緊密地結合，應對國際互連、雲端基礎建設及資料中心服務領域日趨激烈的競爭。

  ## 高層聲明

  Barton表示，公司現有的營運基礎為進一步擴張提供了廣闊空間。他說：「Indigo有堅實的根基，以所需的營運經驗支援超大規模雲端業者、電信業者及網路業者，確保關鍵基礎建設穩定可靠地運行。憑藉業界頂尖的全球營運模式，我們現在的機會，就是將這份專業轉化為每位客戶都能享有的超大規模效能。」

  執行長Michel Robert表示，此次延攬的時機充分反映了Indigo當前的成長軌跡。「Nick的加入對Indigo而言恰逢其時，正值我們持續擴大全球服務能量之際，」Robert說，「他豐富的商務經驗、對客戶需求的深刻洞察，以及橫跨超大規模雲端業者帳戶與國際互連業務的背景，使他成為支持Indigo下一階段成長的最佳人選。」

  ## 意義與展望

  此次任命彰顯Indigo正致力於統籌協調其國際版圖的商業運作，以期深化與超大規模雲端業者及海底電纜基礎建設客戶的合作關係。延攬一位對目前正與Indigo進行收購洽談的公司具備直接任職經驗的主管，顯示公司希望商業領導層對其正積極推進的交易與目標市場擁有第一手知識。

  Indigo下一階段的國際擴張，將包括推進收購Aqua Comms網路營運中心的潛在交易，以及持續深化與Trans Pacific Networks在美國及亞洲的海底電纜夥伴關係。
