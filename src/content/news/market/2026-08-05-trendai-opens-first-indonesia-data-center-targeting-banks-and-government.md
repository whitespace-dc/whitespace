body_en: |
  TrendAI, the cybersecurity company formerly known as Trend Micro, has opened its first data center in Indonesia, citing growing regulatory pressure on banks, government agencies, and large enterprises to store data within the country's borders. The facility began operating on July 1, 2026, and was publicly announced on August 4.

  Country Manager Fetra Syahbana disclosed the launch at a media briefing in Jakarta, describing it as the fulfillment of a commitment the company made public at the end of last year. "With this data center, Indonesia has been upgraded," Syahbana said.

  ## Regulatory Drivers

  TrendAI identified three regulatory frameworks as the primary demand signals for a local facility. The first is Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection — Indonesia's national data protection statute, commonly abbreviated UU PDP — which imposes data-handling obligations on organizations operating in the country. The second is rules issued by the Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK), Indonesia's Financial Services Authority, which apply specifically to the financial sector. The third is a trend in government procurement requiring that data be stored domestically.

  Syahbana said customer data can now be processed and stored entirely within Indonesia, removing the need for clients to route sensitive information through offshore infrastructure.

  ## Three Pillars

  The company says the data center is built around three operational priorities:

  - **Data residency**: customer account data and security telemetry are stored within Indonesia
  - **Compliance**: the facility is structured to help customers meet local regulatory requirements
  - **Latency and reliability**: processing data in-country reduces round-trip times and improves service availability

  ## Target Sectors and Platform

  TrendAI is directing the facility at organizations with the highest data-localization requirements. Syahbana named banking and financial services, government, telecommunications, critical infrastructure, and large enterprises as the primary target segments.

  The data center will underpin TrendAI Vision One, the company's AI-integrated cybersecurity platform that combines threat detection, cyber-risk management, and incident response in a single service. With local infrastructure, Vision One will handle account data, security telemetry, and backup and disaster-recovery data for Indonesian customers within the country, the company said.

  ## Significance

  The launch positions TrendAI to compete directly for regulated enterprise and public-sector contracts in Southeast Asia's largest economy, where data-localization requirements have become a concrete procurement criterion rather than a compliance aspiration. For the Indonesian market, the facility marks a shift from relying on regional data centers elsewhere in Asia to having dedicated in-country capacity for a major cybersecurity vendor's cloud platform.

  TrendAI said the data center commitment was announced publicly at the end of 2025, with the July 1, 2026 operational start representing the delivery of that pledge.
body_zh: |
  TrendAI（前身為趨勢科技）的資安公司在印尼開設了首座資料中心，原因是銀行、政府機關及大型企業將資料儲存於境內的法規壓力持續升高。該設施於2026年7月1日正式啟用，8月4日對外公告。

  印尼區總經理費特拉·賽巴納（Fetra Syahbana）在雅加達的媒體說明會上宣布此消息，並將其定位為公司去年底公開所作承諾的兌現。「有了這座資料中心，印尼已獲得升級，」賽巴納說。

  ## 法規驅動力

  TrendAI點名三項法規框架，作為在當地建置設施的主要需求訊號。第一是2022年第27號個人資料保護法——印尼的全國性資料保護法規，通常以縮寫UU PDP稱之——對在印尼境內營運的機構訂有資料處理義務。第二是印尼金融服務管理局（Otoritas Jasa Keuangan，簡稱OJK）所頒布的規定，專門適用於金融業。第三則是政府採購的趨勢，要求資料必須在境內儲存。

  賽巴納表示，客戶資料現已可完全在印尼境內完成處理與儲存，客戶無需再將敏感資訊導入境外基礎設施。

  ## 三大支柱

  公司表示，此資料中心圍繞三項營運核心打造：

  - **資料在地化**：客戶帳戶資料與資安遙測資料均儲存於印尼境內
  - **法規遵循**：設施架構有助客戶符合當地法規要求
  - **延遲與可靠性**：在境內處理資料可縮短來回傳輸時間，提升服務可用性

  ## 目標產業與平台

  TrendAI將此資料中心的服務對象鎖定於資料在地化需求最高的機構。賽巴納點名銀行與金融服務業、政府機關、電信業、關鍵基礎設施及大型企業，為主要目標客群。

  此資料中心將作為TrendAI Vision One平台的核心支撐。Vision One是該公司整合AI的資安平台，將威脅偵測、網路風險管理與事件應變整合於單一服務之中。有了在地基礎設施，Vision One將在印尼境內處理當地客戶的帳戶資料、資安遙測資料，以及備份與災難復原資料。

  ## 重要意義

  此次開設資料中心，讓TrendAI得以直接爭取東南亞最大經濟體中受法規約束的企業與公部門合約。在印尼，資料在地化要求已從合規層面的期望，演變為採購決策上的具體條件，而非僅止於法規目標。對印尼市場而言，此設施標誌著一項重大轉變——從仰賴亞洲其他地區的區域資料中心，轉向由主要資安廠商在境內建置專屬雲端平台產能。

  TrendAI表示，資料中心的投入承諾已於2025年底公開宣布，2026年7月1日正式啟用，是對當初承諾的實現與交付。
