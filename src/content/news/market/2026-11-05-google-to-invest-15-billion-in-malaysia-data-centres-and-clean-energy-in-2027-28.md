body_en: |
  Google will invest US$15 billion in Malaysia over 2027 and 2028, directing spending at new data centre campuses, clean energy infrastructure, water security, and worker training, Alphabet announced November 4.

  Ruth Porat, Alphabet and Google's president and chief investment officer, made the announcement at the Mantap Malaysia Bersama AI programme at Universiti Malaya in Kuala Lumpur.

  The new campuses will be built in the states of Selangor and Negeri Sembilan and will join Google's existing campus at Elmina Business Park in Selangor, which is already operational. Construction on the new facilities is scheduled for 2027 and 2028.

  ## Economic footprint

  Google said the construction phase is expected to contribute an annual average of US$1.1 billion to Malaysia's gross domestic product (GDP) and support more than 37,000 local jobs per year. Once operational, the campuses are projected to sustain more than 9,000 jobs per year, with wages averaging 45% above the national median.

  Roles will include data centre technicians, mechanical engineers, electrical engineers, logistics operations managers, and physical security managers, along with contractor positions with local suppliers and indirect employment at businesses serving campus workers, the company said.

  ## Power and clean energy

  Google framed the energy component as a "bring your own power" approach. The company said it will add more than one gigawatt (1 GW) of new clean energy capacity to Malaysia's national grid and will pay for 100% of the power its campuses consume, plus the direct infrastructure costs to connect those campuses to the grid, "while complying with all applicable local regulations," Google said.

  A strategic collaboration with Tenaga Nasional Berhad (TNB), Malaysia's national electricity utility, will anchor the clean energy component. Through Malaysia's Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme (CRESS), a programme that allows large consumers to procure power directly from renewable generators, Google said it will enable development of new clean energy generation facilities.

  Google said its Malaysian campuses will operate at a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) of 1.12 to 1.14. PUE measures total facility power draw relative to the power delivered to computing equipment; a value closer to 1.0 indicates less energy lost to cooling and other overhead.

  ## Workforce development

  Google announced four new training programmes targeting 800,000 Malaysians over the next five years, the company said.

  ## Background

  Porat described the investment as building on Google's more than 15-year presence in Malaysia and expanding on a digital infrastructure commitment the company announced in 2024. "This investment aligns with Malaysia's development priorities, providing the foundational computing capacity to deliver the upside of innovation across the country while adding local power and water resources as our operations scale," she said.

  "Google's announcement reflects our confidence in the strength of Malaysia's leadership, its skilled workforce, and enthusiasm for digital transformation," Porat said.

  The TNB collaboration will also explore around-the-clock clean energy projects designed to maintain reliable power supply independent of solar generation availability, Porat said.
body_zh: |
  Alphabet 於11月4日宣布，谷歌（Google）將在2027年至2028年間於馬來西亞投入150億美元，資金用途涵蓋新建資料中心園區、潔淨能源基礎設施、水資源安全及員工培訓等面向。

  Alphabet 暨谷歌總裁兼首席投資長路絲・波拉特（Ruth Porat）在吉隆坡馬來亞大學舉辦的「Mantap Malaysia Bersama AI」計畫發表會上正式宣布這項投資。

  兩處新園區將分別落腳雪蘭莪州（Selangor）與森美蘭州（Negeri Sembilan），並與谷歌現有、已投入運營的雪蘭莪州 Elmina Business Park 園區並列。新設施的建設工程預計於2027年及2028年間展開。

  ## 經濟效益

  谷歌表示，建設階段預計每年平均為馬來西亞國內生產毛額（GDP）貢獻11億美元，並每年支撐逾3萬7,000個本地工作機會。園區正式運營後，預計每年將維繫逾9,000個就業職位，薪資水準平均較全國中位數高出45%。

  職缺類別涵蓋資料中心技術人員、機械工程師、電氣工程師、物流運營主管及實體安全主管，另有與在地供應商合作的承包商職位，以及服務園區員工之周邊商家所間接帶動的就業機會。

  ## 電力與潔淨能源

  谷歌將此次能源計畫定調為「自備電力」（bring your own power）模式。公司表示，將為馬來西亞國家電網新增逾1 GW（百萬瓩）的潔淨能源容量，並承擔旗下園區100%的用電費用，以及將園區接入電網的直接基礎設施成本，「同時遵守所有適用的當地法規」。

  此次潔淨能源計畫的核心，是與馬來西亞國家電力公司（Tenaga Nasional Berhad，TNB）簽訂策略合作協議。透過馬來西亞「企業再生能源供應計畫」（Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme，CRESS）——該計畫允許大用電戶直接向再生能源業者採購電力——谷歌表示將藉此推動新潔淨能源發電設施的開發建設。

  谷歌指出，其馬來西亞園區的電源使用效率（Power Usage Effectiveness，PUE）將維持在1.12至1.14之間。PUE 指標衡量設施總用電量相對於輸送至運算設備電量的比值，數值愈接近1.0，代表耗散於冷卻系統及其他輔助設施的能源愈少、效率愈高。

  ## 人才培育

  谷歌宣布四項全新培訓計畫，預計在未來五年內培訓80萬名馬來西亞民眾。

  ## 背景

  波拉特表示，此次投資是在谷歌深耕馬來西亞逾15年的基礎上更進一步，也是對該公司2024年所宣布之數位基礎設施承諾的延伸與擴展。「這項投資與馬來西亞的發展優先目標高度契合，將為全國各地的創新紅利提供基礎運算能力，同時隨著我們的營運規模擴大，為當地補充電力與水資源，」她說。

  「谷歌的宣布反映了我們對馬來西亞領導層實力、技術人才素質，以及各界對數位轉型熱情的充分信心，」波拉特表示。

  波拉特亦指出，與 TNB 的合作也將探索全天候潔淨能源專案，目標是在不仰賴太陽能發電的情況下，仍能維持穩定可靠的電力供應。
