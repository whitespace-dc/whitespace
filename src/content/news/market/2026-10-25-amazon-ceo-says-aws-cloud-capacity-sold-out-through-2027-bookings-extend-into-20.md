body_en: |
  Amazon Web Services (AWS), the cloud computing division of Amazon, has sold out the bulk of its available computing capacity through 2027, with advance bookings already placed on a substantial share of 2028 supply, Amazon Chief Executive Andy Jassy said on October 23, 2026. The disclosures, made in a video podcast alongside Andreessen Horowitz co-founder Ben Horowitz and general partner Erik Torenberg, underscore the degree to which AI-driven infrastructure demand is outpacing the industry's ability to bring new capacity online.

  Jassy said demand from AI laboratories and enterprises seeking infrastructure to develop and run AI applications has been, in his telling, nearly limitless. "The vast majority of capacity for 2027 is sold," he said, according to reporting by Reuters. "We always keep a certain amount of capacity for on-demand customers and for our inference services — Bedrock or EC2 — but most of the large-block capacity is already sold. A substantial portion of 2028 capacity has also been committed."

  Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud, known as EC2, is AWS's virtual server service that allows customers to run applications on cloud-hosted hardware. Amazon Bedrock is a separate service that gives customers access to AI models for building generative AI applications — software that can produce text, images, or other outputs on demand.

  ## Capex and the case for further investment

  Amazon stated earlier in 2026 that it planned to allocate approximately $220 billion in capital expenditure across the full year, covering infrastructure buildout for both its cloud computing business and AI-related needs. On the podcast, Jassy pointed to the sold-out state of 2026 capacity as justification for spending above that level. "We could invest significantly more and it would not be a poor use of capital," he said.

  The comments come days before Amazon is scheduled to report third-quarter 2026 financial results on October 29.

  ## What it means for the market

  The sold-out status Jassy described — spanning 2026, the majority of 2027, and a meaningful slice of 2028 — signals that large cloud buyers, primarily AI developers and enterprises integrating AI into their operations, are locking in long-term infrastructure commitments rather than relying on short-term or on-demand purchasing. The pattern forces AWS to make investment decisions years in advance of the revenue they are intended to support, increasing both the scale and the lead-time of its capital programs.

  Amazon's next public update on these dynamics will come with its third-quarter earnings release on October 29, 2026.

  ## AWS產能售罄至2027年，預訂已延伸至2028年

  亞馬遜旗下雲端運算部門亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）大部分可用運算產能已售罄至2027年，且2028年供應量亦已有相當比例遭到預訂鎖定。亞馬遜執行長安迪·賈西於2026年10月23日公開揭露上述情況。他在一場與Andreessen Horowitz共同創辦人本·霍羅維茲（Ben Horowitz）及合夥人艾瑞克·托倫柏格（Erik Torenberg）共同參與的影音Podcast中發表此番談話，充分說明AI基礎設施需求正以遠超業界新增產能速度的態勢持續攀升。

  賈西表示，AI實驗室與企業客戶為了開發並運行AI應用程式而尋求基礎設施的需求，在他看來幾乎是無窮無盡的。據路透社報導，他說：「2027年絕大多數的產能已經售出。我們始終會為隨需應變的客戶以及Bedrock或EC2等推論服務保留一定比例的產能，但大部分大型區塊產能都已售出。2028年相當大比例的產能也已獲得承諾。」

  亞馬遜彈性運算雲端（Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud，簡稱EC2）是AWS旗下的虛擬伺服器服務，讓客戶得以在雲端託管的硬體上執行應用程式。亞馬遜Bedrock則是另一項獨立服務，讓客戶能夠存取AI模型，用以建構生成式AI應用程式——這類軟體可依需求產出文字、圖像或其他內容。

  ## 資本支出與進一步投資的理由

  亞馬遜於2026年稍早曾表示，計畫全年投入約2,200億美元的資本支出，涵蓋雲端運算業務與AI相關需求的基礎設施建設。賈西在這場Podcast中以2026年產能已售罄的現況，作為超額支出的正當理由。他說：「我們可以投入更多資金，而且這絕不會是對資本的不當運用。」

  此番談話距亞馬遜預定於10月29日公布2026年第三季財報，僅剩數日。

  ## 對市場的意義

  賈西所描述的售罄狀況——橫跨2026年全年、2027年大部分，以及2028年相當大的比例——顯示大型雲端採購方（主要是AI開發商以及將AI整合進營運的企業）正紛紛鎖定長期基礎設施承諾，而非仰賴短期或隨需應變的採購模式。這樣的趨勢迫使AWS必須在相關收益實現前的數年間就做出投資決策，大幅提升其資本計畫的規模與前置時間。
