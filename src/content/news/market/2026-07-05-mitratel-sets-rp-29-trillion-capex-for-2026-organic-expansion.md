body_en: |
  PT Dayamitra Telekomunikasi Tbk (MTEL), the listed Indonesian telecommunications tower company known as Mitratel, has allocated Rp 2.9 trillion (capital expenditure, or capex) for organic expansion in 2026, President Director Theodorus Ardi Hartoko said at a public presentation on 30 June 2026.

  The budget targets 2,500 new organic tenants — tower tenancy agreements signed and built by Mitratel itself rather than acquired — and 9,000 kilometres of additional billable fiber-optic network length over the course of the year, Hartoko said.

  ## Spectrum auction as a demand catalyst

  Hartoko, who goes by Teddy, pointed to an upcoming radio-frequency spectrum auction as a near-term growth opportunity. Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital (Komdigi) plans to auction the 700 MHz and 2.6 GHz frequency bands, which are expected to be contested by PT Telekomunikasi Selular, PT XLSMART Telecom Sejahtera Tbk (EXCL), and PT Indosat Tbk (ISAT), according to Kontan.

  New spectrum allocations typically require operators to densify or extend their network infrastructure, which in turn drives demand for tower space and fiber backhaul — the services Mitratel sells. Hartoko said the auction represents "a new opportunity for Mitratel to expand, both in terms of reach and in strengthening existing coverage areas."

  ## Industry backdrop: flat growth, macro headwinds

  Hartoko described the broader Indonesian tower industry's 2026 trajectory as "relatively flat," with revenue growth for the sector projected at 1.2 percent. He cited macroeconomic uncertainties — including geopolitical tensions, U.S. Federal Reserve interest-rate dynamics, and global oil prices — as factors weighing on the industry this year.

  The company also flagged the adoption of new technologies as a source of incremental demand. Hartoko specifically mentioned "Internet Rakyat" — a government-linked affordable internet initiative — being pursued by fixed-wireless providers Surge and MyRepublic as a potential revenue opportunity for Mitratel's infrastructure.

  ## Next-generation towerco model

  Management described an industry shift toward what it called the "next-gen towerco" model: an integrated ecosystem that includes Power-as-a-Service (PaaS), whereby tower companies supply managed power to tenants rather than simply leasing steel. Hartoko said Mitratel intends to enter the Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) business as part of this broader repositioning.

  Revenue and EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation — a standard measure of operating cash generation) are projected to grow in line with the wider tower industry, the company said, without providing specific numeric guidance.

  ## What it means for the market

  The Rp 2.9 trillion capex commitment, directed entirely at organic growth rather than acquisitions, signals that Mitratel is prioritising network build-out at a time when the Indonesian tower sector faces muted top-line expansion. The planned addition of 9,000 kilometres of billable fiber places the company's infrastructure strategy beyond passive tower leasing and into active network services — a direction consistent with the PaaS and FWA initiatives Hartoko outlined.

  The timing and outcome of the Komdigi spectrum auction for 700 MHz and 2.6 GHz bands will be a key variable determining whether operator capex translates into accelerated tenancy demand for Mitratel through the remainder of 2026.

  ## Mitratel 編列29億盧比資本支出，推動2026年有機擴張

  PT Dayamitra Telekomunikasi Tbk（MTEL），即印尼上市電信鐵塔業者 Mitratel，董事長 Theodorus Ardi Hartoko 於2026年6月30日的公開說明會上宣布，公司已為2026年有機擴張編列2兆9,000億印尼盧比（資本支出，capex）。

  根據 Hartoko 的說明，此預算目標為全年新增2,500個有機租戶——即由 Mitratel 自行簽約並建設、而非透過收購取得的鐵塔租賃合約——以及9,000公里的額外可計費光纖網路長度。

  ## 頻譜拍賣作為需求催化劑

  Hartoko（常以 Teddy 為人所稱）指出，即將登場的無線電頻率頻譜拍賣是短期內的成長契機。根據《Kontan》報導，印尼通訊暨數位部（Komdigi）計畫拍賣700 MHz 及2.6 GHz 頻段，預計將由 PT Telekomunikasi Selular、PT XLSMART Telecom Sejahtera Tbk（EXCL）及 PT Indosat Tbk（ISAT）競標。

  新的頻譜配置通常要求電信業者加密或延伸其網路基礎設施，進而帶動對鐵塔空間及光纖回傳服務的需求——而這正是 Mitratel 的核心業務。Hartoko 表示，此次拍賣「為 Mitratel 帶來新的擴張機會，無論是在覆蓋範圍的延伸，還是在強化現有涵蓋區域方面」。

  ## 產業背景：成長趨緩、總體環境逆風

  Hartoko 形容印尼鐵塔產業2026年的整體走勢「相對平緩」，預估整體產業營收成長率僅約1.2%。他列舉多項總體經濟不確定因素，包括地緣政治緊張局勢、美國聯準會（Fed）的利率動向，以及全球油價走勢，認為這些都是今年對產業形成壓力的原因。

  公司同時點出新技術的導入將帶來額外需求。Hartoko 特別提及「Internet Rakyat」——一項由政府推動的普惠網路計畫——目前由固定無線寬頻業者 Surge 及 MyRepublic 積極推進，他認為這對 Mitratel 的基礎設施業務而言是潛在的營收機會。

  ## 次世代鐵塔公司模式

  管理層描述產業正朝所謂「次世代鐵塔公司」（next-gen towerco）模式轉型：一種整合型生態系統，其中包含「電力即服務」（Power-as-a-Service，PaaS）——即鐵塔公司以管理式電力服務供應租戶，而非單純出租鐵塔鋼構。Hartoko 表示，Mitratel 計畫進軍固定無線接入（Fixed Wireless Access，FWA）業務，作為這一整體轉型定位的一環。

  公司表示，營收與 EBITDA（即未計利息、稅項、折舊及攤銷前盈餘，為衡量營運現金產出的標準指標）預計將與整體鐵塔產業同步成長，但未提供具體數字指引。

  ## 市場意涵

  2兆9,000億盧比的資本支出承諾全數用於有機成長而非收購，顯示 Mitratel 在印尼鐵塔產業頂線擴張動能趨緩之際，仍以積極的網路建設為優先策略。計畫新增的9,000公里可計費光纖，也說明公司的基礎設施策略已超越被動式鐵塔出租，延伸至主動式網路服務——此方向與 Hartoko 所闡述的 PaaS 及 FWA 布局一脈相承。

  Komdigi 針對700 MHz 及2.6 GHz 頻段的頻譜拍賣時程與結果，將是決定電信業者資本支出能否在2026年下半年加速轉化為 Mitratel 租戶需求的關鍵變數。
