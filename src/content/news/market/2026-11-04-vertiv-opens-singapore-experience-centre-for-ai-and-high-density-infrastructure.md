body_en: |
  Vertiv has opened the Vertiv Experience Centre Singapore, a hands-on facility where customers and partners can interact with the company's integrated power, cooling and data centre infrastructure systems designed for artificial intelligence (AI) and high-density computing workloads.

  The facility hosts live demonstrations rather than static displays, allowing visitors to see intelligent modular data centre infrastructure, thermal management equipment, power management systems and monitoring tools operating together under AI and dense computing loads.

  ## Technologies on display

  Featured products include Vertiv SmartRun, a prefabricated overhead infrastructure platform that combines power distribution, liquid cooling piping, networking and electrical busway in a single unit. The site also showcases Vertiv SmartAisle 3, which the company describes as its latest generation of modular data centre infrastructure.

  Other technologies on display include liquid cooling systems for direct-to-chip and other high-density cooling applications, critical power systems, switchgear, power distribution equipment, and infrastructure management and monitoring tools designed to give operators greater visibility over site conditions and operations.

  The centre is designed to illustrate an end-to-end approach to AI infrastructure spanning power, cooling and IT systems — reflecting what the company describes as the need to treat those elements as an integrated design problem rather than separate disciplines.

  ## Singapore context

  Singapore is one of Asia's main digital infrastructure hubs, and the opening comes as AI workloads increase pressure on data centre operators to manage rising power density and cooling demand. Across the sector, suppliers have been expanding liquid cooling, power management and integrated infrastructure offerings as operators adapt facilities for more intensive computing loads.

  Antony Szafranek, Senior Director for Strategic Accounts at Vertiv Asia, said the centre reflects changing customer requirements across the region. "Organisations across Asia are scaling their digital infrastructure to support AI innovation, creating new demands for reliability, efficiency and speed of deployment," Szafranek said. "The Experience Centre gives customers and partners a hands-on environment to explore how integrated power and cooling technologies can support higher-density computing while maintaining operational resilience."

  ## Integration as a selling point

  Daniel Sim, Senior Director for AI Ecosystem Alliances and Channel Partners at Vertiv Asia, said customers need to assess how power, cooling and IT elements work together rather than in isolation. "The pace of AI-driven transformation requires greater integration of power, cooling and IT infrastructure," Sim said. "At the Experience Centre, customers can see how these technologies work together to address the infrastructure requirements of AI and high-density computing."

  The centre's framing around integration mirrors a broader industry shift as AI servers consume more energy and generate more heat than conventional enterprise systems, placing greater demands on coordinated electrical and thermal design.

  ## Company background

  Vertiv, listed in New York, sells power, cooling and IT infrastructure products for data centres, communications networks and industrial facilities. The company says it operates in more than 130 countries. The Singapore facility is open to customers, channel partners, consultants and other industry stakeholders, and is intended to support AI infrastructure planning as organisations across Asia continue to scale digital infrastructure for AI workloads.

  ## 新加坡布局背景

  新加坡是亞洲主要數位基礎設施樞紐之一，此次開幕正值AI工作負載持續推升資料中心業者在電源密度與冷卻需求管理上的壓力之際。在整個產業中，隨著業者著手將設施改造以因應更高強度的運算負載，供應商紛紛擴大液冷、電源管理及整合式基礎設施的產品線。

  Vertiv亞洲策略客戶資深總監Antony Szafranek表示，體驗中心反映了區域客戶需求的轉變。「整個亞洲的企業正加速擴展數位基礎設施以支援AI創新，對可靠性、效率與部署速度提出了全新要求，」Szafranek說。「體驗中心為客戶與合作夥伴提供了一個親身體驗的環境，讓他們了解整合式電力與冷卻技術如何在維持營運韌性的同時，支援更高密度的運算需求。」

  ## 整合作為核心賣點

  Vertiv亞洲AI生態系聯盟暨通路合作夥伴資深總監Daniel Sim表示，客戶必須評估電力、冷卻與IT要素如何協同運作，而非孤立看待。「AI驅動的轉型步伐要求電力、冷卻與IT基礎設施有更高程度的整合，」Sim說。「在體驗中心，客戶可以親身見證這些技術如何協同運作，以滿足AI與高密度運算的基礎設施需求。」

  體驗中心以整合為核心定位，呼應了整個產業的轉型趨勢——AI伺服器的能耗與散熱量遠超過傳統企業系統，對電氣與熱能的協調設計提出了更高的要求。

  ## 公司背景

  Vertiv在紐約掛牌上市，為資料中心、通訊網路及工業設施提供電力、冷卻及IT基礎設施產品，該公司表示業務遍及逾130個國家。新加坡體驗中心開放予客戶、通路合作夥伴、顧問及其他業界相關人士參訪，旨在支援AI基礎設施規劃工作，協助亞洲各地企業持續擴展面向AI工作負載的數位基礎設施。
