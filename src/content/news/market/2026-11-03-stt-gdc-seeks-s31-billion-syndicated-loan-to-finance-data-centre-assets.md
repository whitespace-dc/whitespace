body_en: |
  STT GDC Pte Ltd, one of Asia's largest data centre operators, is negotiating a S$3.1 billion (US$2.4 billion) syndicated loan with a group of Singapore and international banks, according to people familiar with the matter, as the artificial intelligence buildout continues to drive large-scale debt financing across the sector.

  The three-year facility carries an interest margin of 165 basis points over the Singapore Overnight Rate Average (SORA), the benchmark overnight lending rate used in Singapore dollar transactions, and includes an extension option of up to two years, the people said, asking not to be identified discussing private details. Proceeds will be used as financing for STT GDC's assets, they said.

  ## Banks and deal structure

  Standard Chartered, United Overseas Bank (UOB), Oversea-Chinese Banking Corp (OCBC) and DBS Bank are working on the planned borrowing as global coordinators, mandated lead arrangers and bookrunners, the people said.

  Representatives from STT GDC, Standard Chartered, DBS, KKR and UOB declined to comment. Singtel and OCBC did not respond to requests for comment.

  ## Ownership context

  A group led by KKR & Co and Singapore Telecommunications Ltd (Singtel) completed its acquisition of STT GDC in September, after earlier agreeing to buy the data centre company for S$6.6 billion. DBS, OCBC and UOB previously participated in a separate S$5 billion sustainability-linked loan arranged to finance that purchase.

  Established in 2014, STT GDC operates facilities across 12 countries, including India, South Korea, Japan and Malaysia, as well as the United Kingdom, Germany and Italy, according to its website.

  ## AI debt wave

  STT GDC's loan is part of a broader surge in debt financing tied to the AI infrastructure boom. Globally, AI-related borrowing has flooded markets with more than US$650 billion of debt since the start of 2025, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. That volume has drawn scrutiny from investors who say the binge is pushing up bond yields and inflating what they regard as high valuations for AI-linked companies.

  Moody's Ratings expects at least US$3 trillion of data centre-related investment over the next five years, a figure that illustrates the scale of capital demand the sector is expected to place on debt markets.

  The STT GDC deal reflects how operators across the Asia-Pacific region are tapping syndicated loan markets to fund the expansion and maintenance of existing data centre portfolios rather than waiting for equity or project-finance structures, as demand for computing capacity tied to AI workloads continues to grow.

  ## 股權背景

  由 KKR 與新加坡電信（Singtel）聯合領軍的財團，已於9月完成對 STT GDC 的收購，雙方此前已協議以66億新加坡元買下這家資料中心公司。星展銀行、華僑銀行及大華銀行此前也曾參與一筆50億新加坡元的永續連結貸款，該貸款正是為了為這筆收購提供融資而設。

  ## AI 債務浪潮

  STT GDC 此次貸款，是 AI 基礎建設熱潮帶動債務融資全面升溫的縮影。彭博彙整的數據顯示，自2025年初迄今，與 AI 相關的借貸已向全球市場注入逾6,500億美元的債務。如此龐大的規模已引發投資人高度關注，他們認為這股借貸狂潮正推高債券殖利率，並進一步推升 AI 相關企業本已偏高的估值。

  穆迪信評預估，未來五年資料中心相關投資至少將達3兆美元，這一數字充分說明該產業預計將對債務市場形成的龐大資金需求規模。
