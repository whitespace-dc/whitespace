body_en: |
  AirTrunk has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Nara Chandrababu Naidu for two proposed data center campuses totaling 1.8 gigawatts (GW) of capacity near Visakhapatnam, the Asia-Pacific data center company announced November 14, 2026.

  The two sites sit in Anakapalli district: a 400 megawatt (MW) campus at Rambilli, a village approximately 60 kilometers southwest of Visakhapatnam, and a 1.4GW campus at Tootipala, approximately 70 kilometers west of the city. One GW equals 1,000 MW. Full details of either project have not been shared, the company said.

  "Digital infrastructure can support economic growth and create new opportunities for local communities," said Carly Wishart, AirTrunk managing director, corporate and international. "We're grateful to the Andhra Pradesh Government for its partnership, and look forward to working closely with government and community partners as the proposal progresses. Our focus is on responsible planning and helping people build the skills to take part in the opportunities ahead."

  ## Ownership and existing portfolio

  AirTrunk was founded in 2018. In 2024, Blackstone and the Canadian Pension Plan (CPP) acquired the company for US$16.1 billion, described at the time as the largest-ever deal in the data center sector. The company has campuses in operation and under development in Sydney and Melbourne (two sites) in Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong, Johor in Malaysia, and Tokyo and Osaka in Japan.

  ## India entry and existing footprint

  AirTrunk entered India earlier in 2026 through the acquisition of Lumina CloudInfra, an Indian data center operator. Lumina was founded by alternative investment firm Blackstone in November 2022 with plans for 600MW of data center capacity across India. AirTrunk currently lists projects in the Chandivali and Navi Mumbai areas near Mumbai, as well as in Chennai and Hyderabad.

  The 1.8GW Andhra Pradesh proposal exceeds the 600MW India target Lumina had outlined at its founding, and would extend AirTrunk's India presence beyond its existing Mumbai, Chennai, and Hyderabad locations.

  ## Visakhapatnam competitive landscape

  Visakhapatnam, also referred to as Vizag, has drawn a cluster of data center operators. Others active in the region include Colt, AdaniConneX, Google, and Sify.

  ## AirTrunk與印度安得拉邦簽署1.8GW資料中心意向書

  AirTrunk於2026年11月14日宣布，已與安得拉邦首席部長納拉·昌德拉巴布·奈杜簽署合作意向書（MoU），計畫在維沙卡帕特南（Visakhapatnam）附近興建兩座資料中心園區，合計容量達1.8GW（十億瓦）。

  兩處基地均位於阿納卡帕利縣（Anakapalli district）：其一為拉姆比利（Rambilli）的400MW園區，座落於維沙卡帕特南西南方約60公里的村落；其二為圖蒂帕拉（Tootipala）的1.4GW園區，位於該市西方約70公里處。1GW等於1,000MW。公司表示，目前兩項計畫的完整細節尚未對外公開。

  AirTrunk企業暨國際業務董事總經理卡莉·威夏特（Carly Wishart）表示：「數位基礎設施能夠支撐經濟成長，並為當地社區創造新的機會。我們衷心感謝安得拉邦政府的合作夥伴關係，期待隨著計畫推進，能與政府及社區夥伴緊密合作。我們的重心在於負責任的規劃，以及協助民眾培養參與未來機遇所需的技能。」

  ## 母公司背景與現有資產

  AirTrunk成立於2018年。2024年，私募股權巨頭黑石集團（Blackstone）與加拿大退休金計畫（CPP）以161億美元完成收購，此案當時被譽為資料中心產業史上規模最大的併購交易。目前，AirTrunk在澳洲雪梨及墨爾本（各有兩處基地）、新加坡、香港、馬來西亞柔佛，以及日本東京與大阪均設有營運中或開發中的園區。

  ## 進軍印度與現有布局

  AirTrunk於2026年稍早透過收購印度資料中心業者Lumina CloudInfra正式進入印度市場。Lumina由另類投資機構黑石集團於2022年11月創立，原訂計畫在印度各地部署合計600MW的資料中心容量。AirTrunk目前在印度的專案涵蓋孟買附近的錢迪瓦利（Chandivali）及納維孟買（Navi Mumbai）地區，以及清奈（Chennai）與海得拉巴（Hyderabad）。

  此次安得拉邦1.8GW的提案，已超越Lumina創立之初所設定的600MW印度目標，並將使AirTrunk的印度版圖進一步延伸至孟買、清奈及海得拉巴以外的新據點。

  ## 維沙卡帕特南市場競爭態勢

  維沙卡帕特南（又稱Vizag）近年已吸引多家資料中心業者聚集。目前在該地區積極布局的業者包括Colt、AdaniConneX、Google及Sify。
