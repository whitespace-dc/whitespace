body_en: |
  The Philippines signed the Pax Silica Declaration of Inclusion in April and is targeting a full agreement with the US-led AI and semiconductor supply chain initiative by November, with qualifying investors at the planned hub eligible for income tax holidays, VAT zero-rating, and import duty exemptions under the country's CREATE MORE Act, according to government officials and a tax analysis published in BusinessWorld.

  The initiative centers on a first-of-its-kind AI-native industrial acceleration hub planned for New Clark City in Tarlac province, to be built within a 4,000-hectare industrial zone managed by the Bases Conversion Development Authority (BCDA).

  ## What Pax Silica is

  Pax Silica is a US-led international initiative announced by the US Department of State in December 2025. The name combines the Latin word *pax*, meaning peace and stability, with *silica*, the precursor to silicon used in semiconductor manufacturing. The initiative counts 23 state signatories and promotes a shared ecosystem of AI developers and vendors aimed at securing global AI and semiconductor supply chains through cooperation among allies and trusted partner nations.

  ## Why the Philippines was included

  The Philippines is described in the BusinessWorld analysis as one of the United States' oldest allies. The country's inclusion is motivated by its natural and mineral resources, strategic location, and skilled workforce.

  The BCDA's industrial zone at New Clark City will host the initiative's first AI-native industrial acceleration hub. Government officials said the hub could attract investment from semiconductor and energy companies, create jobs, and lay groundwork for a broader innovation ecosystem capable of drawing foreign investment over the long term.

  ## The tax incentive framework

  The central mechanism for attracting investors is the CREATE MORE Act, under which participating firms may register with Investment Promotions Agencies (IPAs) such as the BCDA and the Board of Investment (BoI).

  Eligibility begins with classification under the 2026 Strategic Investment Promotions Program (SIPP), signed in May, which designates semiconductors and electronics manufacturing, energy, and sustainability-driven industries as tier 1 activities, the highest-priority SIPP category. Those designations align with the requirements of the Tarlac hub, the analysis noted.

  Registered enterprises may qualify for the following incentives under the Tax Code, subject to IPA compliance requirements:

  - An **Income Tax Holiday (ITH)** during early years of operations, deferring corporate income tax liability
  - After the ITH period, either an **Enhanced Deductions Regime (EDR)** or a **Special Corporate Income Tax (SCIT)** rate, depending on the applicable incentive package
  - **VAT zero-rating** on purchases from local suppliers directly attributable to registered activities, on payments to non-resident foreign suppliers for services rendered in the Philippines, and on payments to non-resident digital service providers
  - Exemption from duties and taxes on imports

  ## Transfer pricing and the global minimum tax

  The initiative is expected to draw large multinational enterprises (MNEs), making transfer pricing a key compliance area, particularly for cross-border transactions involving digital services, intellectual property, and shared services. Related-party transactions must adhere to the arm's length principle, meaning they must be priced as if conducted between unrelated parties, and must be supported by adequate documentation, the analysis noted.

  Separately, the Department of Finance (DoF) is pursuing a proposed Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (QDMTT), which would require large MNEs to pay a minimum effective tax rate of 15% on Philippine-sourced income. The measure is designed to ensure any top-up tax is collected by the Philippines rather than by foreign tax authorities, preserving the country's taxing rights.

  For payments to non-resident foreign corporations (NRFCs) covering business profits, royalties, interest, dividends, and other cross-border flows, preferential rates may be available under applicable tax treaties. To claim those benefits, Philippine entities must obtain a Tax Residency Certificate from the NRFC and a Certificate of Entitlement to Tax Treaty Benefits from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR). Non-compliance results in denial of treaty benefits and imposition of standard tax rates.

  ## Environmental concerns

  Environmental groups and local stakeholders have raised concerns about the resource demands that data centers, manufacturing facilities, and AI infrastructure place on electricity, water, and land, according to the BusinessWorld analysis. El Niño effects have already strained water supply in Central Luzon, the region where New Clark City is located, intensifying scrutiny of the additional demands a large-scale industrial hub would impose.

  Critics have also raised issues of land use, ecosystem preservation, biodiversity protection, and effects on surrounding communities. Concerned groups have called for thorough environmental impact assessments and demonstrated compliance with environmental regulations before major development activity proceeds.

  ## Significance

  The Pax Silica hub would establish the Philippines as a node in a US-aligned semiconductor and AI supply chain network spanning 23 signatory nations. The tax incentive framework, combining holidays, reduced rates, VAT relief, and import exemptions, is intended to reduce business costs and improve project viability for capital-intensive investments, according to the analysis.

  The proposed QDMTT, if enacted, would place a floor on the effective benefit of income tax holidays for large multinationals by requiring a minimum 15% effective tax rate on Philippine-sourced income.

  The Philippines is targeting November for signing the full Pax Silica Agreement, which would formalize its participation among the initiative's 23 signatory nations.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓已於四月簽署《Pax Silica納入宣言》，並以十一月為目標，力求與這項由美國主導的人工智慧及半導體供應鏈倡議簽訂完整協議。依據菲律賓《CREATE MORE法》，在規劃中園區設立的合格投資人將可享有所得稅假期、加值稅零稅率及進口關稅豁免等優惠。上述資訊來自政府官員說法，以及《商業世界》（BusinessWorld）刊載的一份稅務分析報告。

  此倡議的核心，是一座規劃中位於塔拉克省新克拉克城（New Clark City）、全球首創的AI原生產業加速基地，將興建於由基地轉換開發局（BCDA）管轄的四千公頃工業區內。

  ## Pax Silica是什麼

  Pax Silica是一項由美國主導的國際倡議，於2025年12月由美國國務院宣布啟動。其名稱結合了拉丁文「pax」（意指和平與穩定）及「silica」（二氧化矽，即半導體製造所用矽材料的前驅物質）。該倡議現有23個國家簽署，致力於建立AI開發商與供應商的共享生態系，目標透過盟友與可信賴夥伴國之間的合作，保障全球人工智慧及半導體供應鏈的安全。

  ## 菲律賓為何獲納入

  《商業世界》的分析將菲律賓定位為美國歷史最悠久的盟友之一。菲律賓被納入此倡議，主要基於其豐富的天然資源與礦產、優越的戰略位置，以及具備專業技能的勞動力。

  BCDA管轄的新克拉克城工業區將承接該倡議首座AI原生產業加速基地。政府官員表示，這座基地有望吸引半導體及能源企業投資、創造就業機會，並為更廣泛的創新生態系奠定基礎，進而在長期持續吸引外資。

  ## 租稅優惠框架

  吸引投資人的核心機制為《CREATE MORE法》。參與企業可向投資促進機構（IPA）登記，包括BCDA及投資委員會（BoI）。

  取得資格的前提，是依據今年五月簽署的《2026年策略性投資促進計畫》（SIPP）進行分類認定。該計畫將半導體及電子製造業、能源產業，以及以永續發展為導向的產業列為第一類活動，即SIPP最高優先等級。分析報告指出，上述認定與塔拉克基地的需求高度契合。

  依據稅法規定，已登記企業在符合IPA合規要求的前提下，可申請以下優惠：

  - **所得稅假期（ITH）**：在營運初期享有，可遞延企業所得稅負擔
  - ITH期滿後，可選擇**加強扣除額制度（EDR）**或**特別企業所得稅（SCIT）**稅率，依適用的優惠方案而定
  - **加值稅零稅率**：適用於向本地供應商採購且直接歸屬於登記業務的支出、向在菲提供服務的非居民外國供應商之款項，以及向非居民數位服務提供商之款項
  - 進口品免徵關稅及相關稅捐

  ## 移轉訂價與全球最低稅負

  此倡議預計將吸引大型跨國企業（MNE）進駐，使移轉訂價成為重要的合規課題，尤其是涉及數位服務、智慧財產及共享服務的跨境交易。分析報告指出，關聯方交易須遵守常規交易原則，亦即交易定價必須如同與非關聯方進行般，並備妥充分的相關文件。

  另一方面，財政部（DoF）正推動擬議中的「合格境內最低補充稅」（QDMTT），要求大型跨國企業就其菲律賓來源所得，繳納最低15%的實質有效稅率。此措施旨在確保任何補充稅由菲律賓收取，而非由外國稅務機關徵繳，以維護菲律賓的課稅權。

  針對支付給非居民外國企業（NRFC）的款項，包括營業利潤、權利金、利息、股利及其他跨境資金流，適用的租稅協定或可提供優惠稅率。若要主張相關優惠，菲律賓實體必須向NRFC取得稅務居民證明，並向國家稅務局（BIR）申請租稅協定優惠資格證書。若未依規定辦理，將被拒絕適用協定優惠，並須依標準稅率課徵。

  ## 環境疑慮

  據《商業世界》分析報告，環保團體及當地利害關係人對資料中心、製造設施及AI基礎建設在電力、水資源及土地方面的龐大需求表達關切。聖嬰現象（El Niño）已對新克拉克城所在的中呂宋地區造成供水壓力，使各界對大型工業基地可能帶來的額外負擔更加關注。

  批評者也提出土地利用、生態系保護、生物多樣性維護及對周邊社區影響等議題，呼籲在重大開發活動展開之前，必須完成完整的環境影響評估，並確實遵守相關環保法規。

  ## 重要意義

  Pax Silica基地將使菲律賓成為橫跨23個簽署國、親美半導體與AI供應鏈網絡中的重要節點。分析報告指出，這套涵蓋稅假期、降低稅率、加值稅減免及進口豁免的租稅優惠框架，旨在降低企業營運成本、提升資本密集型投資的計畫可行性。

  擬議中的QDMTT一旦立法通過，將對大型跨國企業的所得稅假期實際優惠設定底線——要求其菲律賓來源所得的實質有效稅率不得低於15%。

  菲律賓目標在今年十一月簽署完整的《Pax Silica協議》，正式確立其在這項23國倡議中的參與地位。
