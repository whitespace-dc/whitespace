  ## COIN acquires stake in Bitera Data Center operator

  PT Indokripto Koin Semesta Tbk (COIN), a crypto-asset company listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (Bursa Efek Indonesia, or BEI), invested Rp 200 billion (Indonesian rupiah) on November 16, 2026 to take a 10% new-share stake in PT Mitra Informatika Gemilang (MIG), the operator of Bitera Data Center. The investment is a strategic diversification away from crypto assets and into digital infrastructure, Kontan reported.

  COIN's move is the latest in a widening field of BEI-listed companies from multiple sectors entering the data center business. The article from Kontan, published November 18, 2026, frames the expansion in the context of Indonesia's growing digital economy and rising demand linked to artificial intelligence (AI), and names PT Dian Swastika Sentosa Tbk (DSSA), PT Indosat Tbk (ISAT), COIN, and PT Indointernet Tbk (INET) as companies whose data center positions are under comparison by investors.

  ## Capital requirements and risk factors

  Despite the growth narrative, Kontan cautioned that expansion into digital infrastructure demands large capital expenditure, secure energy supply, and the ability to sign paying customers. Without those three elements in place, the publication said, large capacity already built risks failing to generate optimal returns within a reasonable timeframe.

  The warning is relevant to any BEI-listed issuer committing capital to data center capacity in advance of confirmed demand — a pattern common in infrastructure plays where construction timelines and customer contract cycles do not always align.

  ## Significance

  COIN's entry into data center infrastructure via a minority stake in MIG represents a path being taken by companies outside the traditional telecoms and technology sectors, using the data center build-out as a diversification vehicle. For investors tracking Indonesia's digital infrastructure space, the range of entrants — spanning crypto, telecoms, and internet services — signals that competitive pressure on capacity, energy procurement, and anchor tenants is likely to intensify as more issuers commit capital to the sector.

  COIN's Rp 200 billion investment in MIG, announced November 16, 2026, is the most recently disclosed transaction among the group.

  ## COIN入股Bitera資料中心營運商

  印尼證券交易所（Bursa Efek Indonesia，BEI）上市的加密資產公司PT Indokripto Koin Semesta Tbk（COIN），於2026年11月16日投資2,000億印尼盧比，取得Bitera資料中心營運商PT Mitra Informatika Gemilang（MIG）10%新股股權。印尼財經媒體Kontan報導，此舉是COIN刻意從加密資產轉向數位基礎設施的策略多元化布局。

  COIN此番出手，是BEI各產業上市公司競相進軍資料中心市場的最新案例。Kontan於2026年11月18日刊出的報導，將這波擴張浪潮置於印尼數位經濟成長、人工智慧（AI）帶動需求上升的背景下加以詮釋，並點名PT Dian Swastika Sentosa Tbk（DSSA）、PT Indosat Tbk（ISAT）、COIN以及PT Indointernet Tbk（INET），作為投資人現階段關注、比較其資料中心布局的四家公司。

  ## 資本需求與風險因素

  儘管成長題材引人矚目，Kontan仍提出警示：進軍數位基礎設施需要龐大的資本支出、穩定的電力供應，以及與付費客戶簽約的能力。報導指出，若上述三項條件無法到位，已先行興建的大量機房容量，恐怕難以在合理時間內創造最佳投資報酬。

  這項警示對任何在需求尚未確認的情況下便率先投入資料中心產能的BEI上市公司都深具參考意義——這正是基礎建設投資中常見的模式：建設時程與客戶合約周期往往難以完全吻合。

  ## 意義

  COIN透過取得MIG少數股權的方式進入資料中心基礎設施領域，代表傳統電信與科技產業以外的企業，正以資料中心建設作為多元化布局的載體。對追蹤印尼數位基礎設施動態的投資人而言，進場者涵蓋加密資產、電信及網路服務等不同背景，意味著在產能、電力採購及主力租戶的競爭壓力上，隨著更多上市公司相繼投入資本，競爭態勢將可能持續升溫。

  COIN以2,000億印尼盧比投資MIG一事，於2026年11月16日正式對外宣布，是上述各公司中目前最新揭露的一筆交易。
