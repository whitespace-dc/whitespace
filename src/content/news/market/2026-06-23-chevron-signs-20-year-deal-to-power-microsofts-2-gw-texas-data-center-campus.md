body_en: |
  Chevron and Microsoft have signed a 20-year agreement for Chevron to develop a dedicated natural gas power facility for Microsoft's data center campus in Pecos, West Texas, the two companies announced Monday.

  The facility, called Project Kilby, will supply electricity exclusively to the Microsoft campus and is expected to ramp to 2.67 gigawatts (GW) of generating capacity over time, with first power targeted for 2028, Chevron said. A co-located facility is one built adjacent to and dedicated solely to a specific customer rather than feeding the public electricity grid. The Pecos campus is expected to expand Microsoft's data center capacity by 2 GW.

  ## Investment and Jobs

  Microsoft described the data center development as a multi-billion-dollar investment to be deployed over five to seven years. The project is expected to support more than 6,000 construction jobs and hundreds of permanent operational roles, Microsoft said.

  ## Generation Partners and Equipment

  Chevron had previously disclosed partnerships with investment firm Engine No. 1 and electric services company GE Vernova on Project Kilby. The majority of the project's generation will come from GE Vernova turbines, Chevron said, with additional capacity supplied by Solar Turbines, a subsidiary of heavy-equipment manufacturer Caterpillar.

  Chevron said Project Kilby is expected to rank among the largest co-located natural gas power projects in the United States.

  ## AI Demand Driving Power Deals

  Technology companies, including Microsoft, are moving to secure dedicated electricity supplies for data centers that support artificial intelligence services such as ChatGPT and Copilot, according to the announcement. Chevron characterized the project as supporting "the next phase of American AI growth by leveraging America's natural gas advantage."

  ## Next Steps

  Chevron expects to announce a final investment decision on Project Kilby by the end of this year. First power is targeted for 2028, with the facility ramping to its full 2.67 GW capacity over time thereafter.

  微軟將此次資料中心開發定位為數十億美元等級的投資，預計分五至七年分批投入。微軟表示，該專案預計將帶動逾6,000個建設工期職缺，並創造數百個長期營運職位。

  ## 發電夥伴與設備

  雪佛龍先前已公開宣布與投資公司Engine No. 1及電力服務企業GE Vernova攜手合作推進Kilby專案。雪佛龍表示，專案的主要發電量將來自GE Vernova的燃氣渦輪機，另有部分容量由重型設備製造商卡特彼勒旗下子公司Solar Turbines提供。

  雪佛龍指出，Kilby專案預計將躋身美國規模最大的天然氣共址發電專案之列。

  ## AI需求推動供電協議

  根據雙方公告，包括微軟在內的科技公司正積極為支援人工智慧服務的資料中心確保專屬電力來源，相關服務包括ChatGPT與Copilot等。雪佛龍將此專案定位為「善用美國天然氣優勢，支撐美國AI發展下一階段」的重要布局。

  ## 後續時程

  雪佛龍預計於今年底前宣布Kilby專案的最終投資決策。首批電力目標於2028年上線，此後發電設施將逐步擴增至完整的2.67 GW容量。
