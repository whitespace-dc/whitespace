body_en: |
  Accton Technology (2345.TW) is simultaneously expanding production facilities across five countries to meet accelerating demand from artificial intelligence data centers and high-speed networking markets, the company said at its annual shareholders' meeting on June 11. Management described current customer order intake as stronger than at the end of 2025 and said the second half of 2026 outlook is cautiously optimistic.

  ## Taiwan expansion

  In Taiwan, Accton operates existing plants in Zhubei and Zhunan that are in stable production. Its Taoyuan Guishan factory is currently shipping in small volumes and is expected to scale to higher-volume output in the third quarter of 2026, the company said. A fourth Taiwan site, the Nanliao facility, previously used as a warehouse, has been approved for conversion into a production facility; Accton said it expects new lines there to begin output in 2027.

  ## Overseas buildout

  Accton's Vietnam factory has already reached mass-production capability, and the company is planning to add a third building at that site to accommodate a larger share of higher-end products. In the United States, the Dallas factory is targeted for equipment installation by the end of 2026, with mass production scheduled for 2027; the site will focus on rack-integrated products — systems that combine servers, switches, thermal management, and power management into a single integrated chassis. Facilities in Singapore and Malaysia are planned to come online across 2026 and 2027, covering optical modules, CPO (co-packaged optics, a technology that integrates optical components directly with silicon chips to reduce latency and power consumption) switches, and related high-speed optical communications equipment.

  ## Product lines

  Accton's three main product lines — optical communications, switches, and rack-integrated solutions — are all benefiting from AI data center build-outs, the company said. Optical communications is the fastest-growing segment by demand intensity, with rapid uptake of 400G and 800G (gigabit-per-second) transceivers and pressure toward still-higher speeds. The company said demand for switches spans both conventional and next-generation high-speed switching architectures. Rack-integrated solutions, which bundle compute, networking, cooling, and power into a single deliverable unit, are described as one of the company's fastest-growing product lines as data center operators scale AI infrastructure.

  ## Emerging technology positioning

  Accton said it has been building out product portfolios around CPO and OCS (optical circuit switching, which uses light rather than electronics to route signals, reducing power consumption at high port counts). The company said these technologies are expected to increase the strategic importance of optical communications supply chains, and that its investments in those areas position it to benefit as adoption grows.

  ## Financials

  Shareholders approved the 2025 financial statements and profit distribution at the June 11 meeting. Accton reported 2025 earnings per share of NT$47.13 and approved a cash dividend of NT$15 per share.

  ## Industry significance

  The five-country production footprint reflects a broader industry shift among Taiwanese networking hardware manufacturers toward geographic diversification, driven by customer requirements for supply-chain resilience and, in the case of the U.S. facility, proximity to end markets. Accton said overall demand is running ahead of what it had projected at the end of 2025, which it attributed to continued acceleration in AI server deployments, cloud data center expansion, and upgrades to high-speed transmission infrastructure. The Dallas factory's 2027 mass-production target and the planned third Vietnam building represent the furthest forward points in Accton's current disclosed expansion schedule.

  ## 光聖科技五國擴產，全力迎戰AI資料中心需求浪潮

  光聖科技（股票代號2345）在6月11日舉行的股東常會上表示，公司正同步在五個國家擴充生產設施，以應對人工智慧資料中心及高速網路市場加速成長的需求。管理層指出，目前客戶下單力道強於2025年底，對2026年下半年的展望審慎樂觀。

  ## 台灣擴產布局

  在台灣，光聖目前在竹北與竹南均設有既有廠房，生產狀況穩定。桃園龜山廠目前已少量出貨，預計於2026年第三季正式放量生產。第四座台灣廠區「南寮廠」原先作為倉儲使用，已獲准轉型為生產基地，光聖表示預計2027年於該廠導入新生產線並正式量產。

  ## 海外產能建置

  在海外方面，越南廠已具備量產能力，公司並計畫增建第三棟廠房，以承接更多高階產品訂單。美國達拉斯廠預計於2026年底完成設備安裝，並於2027年進入量產階段，主攻「機架整合」產品——亦即將伺服器、交換器、熱管理及電源管理整合於單一機箱的完整系統解決方案。新加坡與馬來西亞的廠房則規劃於2026至2027年間陸續投入運營，聚焦光學模組、CPO（共封裝光學，一種將光學元件直接與矽晶片整合以降低延遲及耗電量的技術）交換器，以及相關高速光通訊設備。

  ## 產品線概況

  光聖旗下三大產品線——光通訊、交換器及機架整合解決方案——均受惠於AI資料中心的大規模建置。光通訊是需求成長最為強勁的業務，400G與800G（Gbps，十億位元每秒）光收發模組快速普及，且市場對更高速率的需求仍持續升溫。交換器方面，傳統架構與新世代高速交換架構的需求均告活絡。機架整合解決方案將運算、網路、散熱與電源整合為單一交付單元，隨著資料中心業者積極擴建AI基礎設施，這條產品線已成為公司成長最快的業務之一。

  ## 新興技術布局

  光聖表示，公司持續布建CPO及OCS（光路由交換，一種以光取代電子訊號進行路由的技術，可在高埠數環境下有效降低耗電量）相關產品線。公司認為這兩項技術將進一步提升光通訊供應鏈的戰略地位，而光聖在此領域的前期投入，將使其在相關技術普及後取得競爭先機。

  ## 財務表現

  股東常會同時通過2025年財務報表及盈餘分配案。光聖2025年每股盈餘（EPS）為新台幣47.13元，並核准每股配發新台幣15元現金股利。

  ## 產業意義

  五國生產版圖的布局，反映台灣網路硬體製造商向地理分散化轉型的整體趨勢，背後驅動力包括客戶對供應鏈韌性的要求，以及美國廠就近服務終端市場的考量。光聖表示，整體需求超出2025年底的預期，歸因於AI伺服器部署持續加速、雲端資料中心擴張，以及高速傳輸基礎設施的全面升級。達拉斯廠2027年的量產目標，以及越南廠規劃中的第三棟廠房，代表光聖目前已對外揭露的擴產計畫中時間軸最為前推的兩個里程碑。
