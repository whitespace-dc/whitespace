body_en: |
  Keppel DC REIT has acquired a 100% stake in a data centre in Cardiff, United Kingdom, for £34 million (S$59.3 million), the Singapore-listed real estate investment trust (REIT) disclosed in a filing to the Singapore Exchange.

  The facility is leased to one of the largest global cloud services providers on a triple-net lease — a structure under which the tenant bears property-related costs such as taxes, insurance, and maintenance — for 15 years beginning in June. The REIT's manager said it intends to fund the acquisition entirely through bank borrowings.

  ## Property and valuation

  The data centre is located south of Dunleavy Drive in Cardiff and comprises a two-storey facility connected to a three-storey office block, with a total lettable area of 7,380 square metres. Independent property consultant CBRE valued the 100% stake at £34.0 million as of 15 September, equal to the acquisition price.

  ## Portfolio impact

  The deal extends Keppel DC REIT's weighted average lease expiry (WALE) — the average remaining lease duration across a portfolio, weighted by rental income — from 8.7 years to 9.5 years. It also increases the share of the portfolio held under master leases, arrangements in which a single tenant leases an entire facility, from 31.6% to 35%.

  The manager said the acquisition aligns with its strategy of buying quality income-producing data centre properties to enhance total return to unitholders. The lease includes annual rental escalations, which the manager said provide rental and capital value growth opportunities. The deal also adds another established client to the REIT's existing tenant base, the manager said.

  ## Significance

  The Cardiff deal increases the REIT's concentration of long-term, triple-net-leased assets and pushes the portfolio's average lease duration above nine years. With full bank borrowing funding, no equity issuance is required to complete the transaction, the manager indicated.

  Units in Keppel DC REIT closed 0.8% lower at S$1.21 on the day of the filing.
body_zh: |
  吉寶DC REIT（吉寶數據中心房地產投資信託）宣布，已以3,400萬英鎊（約5,930萬新加坡幣）的價格，收購英國威爾斯卡地夫一座資料中心100%股權。這家在新加坡掛牌上市的房地產投資信託（REIT）已就此交易向新加坡交易所提交申報文件。

  該設施由全球最大雲端服務供應商之一承租，租約形式為三重淨租約——即由承租方承擔物業相關費用，包括稅費、保險及日常維護——租期15年，自今年6月起計。信託管理人表示，擬以全數銀行借款方式為本次收購提供資金，無需發行新股。

  ## 物業概況與估值

  這座資料中心位於卡地夫鄧利維道（Dunleavy Drive）以南，由一棟兩層樓的設施與一棟三層辦公大樓相連而成，總可出租面積為7,380平方公尺。獨立物業顧問世邦魏理仕（CBRE）以9月15日為評估基準日，對該100%股權的估值為3,400萬英鎊，與收購價格相符。

  ## 投資組合影響

  此次交易使吉寶DC REIT的加權平均租約到期年限（WALE）——即按租金收入加權計算的投資組合平均剩餘租約年限——從8.7年延長至9.5年。此外，主租約（即由單一承租方租用整棟設施的安排）在投資組合中所占的比例，亦從31.6%提升至35%。

  管理人表示，此次收購符合公司長期策略，即透過買入優質且能穩定創造收益的資料中心物業，以提升基金持有人的整體報酬。租約內含每年租金遞增條款，管理人指出，此安排為租金及資本價值的成長提供了空間。此外，這筆交易也為吉寶DC REIT的現有租戶組合增添了一位具市場地位的新客戶。

  ## 市場意義

  卡地夫這筆交易進一步提高了吉寶DC REIT投資組合中長期三重淨租約資產的占比，並使整體投資組合的平均租約年限突破九年大關。由於收購資金全數以銀行借款支應，管理人表示，完成交易無需進行任何股權募集。

  吉寶DC REIT的基金單位，於公告申報當日收盤下跌0.8%，收報1.21新加坡幣。
