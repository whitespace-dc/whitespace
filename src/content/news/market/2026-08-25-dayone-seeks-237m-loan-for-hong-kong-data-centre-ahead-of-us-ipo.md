body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers is seeking a five-year HK$1.86 billion (US$237 million) loan from three Singapore-based banks to refinance and expand its Hong Kong data centre, as the company accelerates borrowing ahead of a planned US initial public offering (IPO).

  ## Loan structure

  The Singapore-headquartered data centre operator is in discussions with DBS Group Holdings, Oversea-Chinese Banking Corp (OCBC) and United Overseas Bank (UOB) for the facility, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified when discussing private transactions. The borrower is a special purpose vehicle — a standalone legal entity used to isolate the assets and liabilities of a specific project.

  Proceeds would go toward refinancing existing debt and capital expansion for the Hong Kong data centre. DayOne, DBS, OCBC and UOB all declined to comment.

  ## Recent borrowing activity

  The Hong Kong loan follows a string of large financings in quick succession. Earlier this month, the same three banks jointly provided a four-year, S$530 million (US$417 million) loan to develop DayOne's first Singapore-based data centre, the lenders announced in a joint statement. DayOne is also in discussions to roughly double an existing credit facility to approximately US$7 billion — a transaction that would be the largest data centre loan by any company in Asia, according to the people familiar with the matter.

  ## Company background

  Formerly known as GDS International, DayOne was established by GDS Holdings to manage its data centre assets and operations outside mainland China. The company rebranded as DayOne last year and in June closed a US$4.5 billion Series C funding round. Its investor base includes SoftBank Vision Fund, Coatue Management and Ken Griffin. DayOne lists seven data centre facilities as planned or operational, with locations in Singapore, Malaysia, Japan, Hong Kong and Finland.

  ## Regional financing context

  DayOne's capital-raising is part of a wider wave of data centre financing across the Asia-Pacific, driven by surging demand for artificial intelligence computing infrastructure. Thailand's True Internet Data Center is seeking an approximately US$2 billion-equivalent loan for a project in that country. Blue Owl Capital's Stack Infrastructure is pursuing an A$8.5 billion (US$5.9 billion) syndication loan to fund a facility in Melbourne, Australia. The activity signals continued bank appetite in the region for large-scale data centre debt.

  ## IPO plans

  DayOne is weighing a US IPO that could raise around US$5 billion, with a potential launch as soon as next quarter, according to the people familiar with the matter.

  DayOne資料中心正向三家總部位於新加坡的銀行尋求5年期港幣18.6億元（約2.37億美元）貸款，用於旗下香港資料中心的再融資與擴建，同時加快融資腳步，為計畫中的美國首次公開發行（IPO）預做準備。

  ## 貸款結構

  這家總部位於新加坡的資料中心營運商，正就上述信貸安排與星展集團控股（DBS Group Holdings）、華僑銀行（OCBC）及大華銀行（UOB）展開磋商。據知情人士表示，由於涉及私下交易，相關人士要求匿名。借款方為一家特殊目的載體（SPV）——即專為隔離特定專案資產與負債而設立的獨立法人實體。

  所得資金將用於償還現有債務，以及香港資料中心的資本擴張。DayOne、星展銀行、華僑銀行及大華銀行均婉拒置評。

  ## 近期融資動態

  香港這筆貸款，是DayOne近期接連完成大規模融資的最新一例。本月稍早，上述同三家銀行聯合提供一筆4年期、新幣5.3億元（約4.17億美元）的貸款，用於開發DayOne在新加坡的首座資料中心，三家貸款行已就此聯合發表聲明。據知情人士透露，DayOne目前也在磋商，擬將一筆現有信貸額度擴大約一倍至70億美元左右——一旦成交，將成為亞洲有史以來單一企業規模最大的資料中心貸款。

  ## 公司背景

  DayOne前身為GDS International，由GDS Holdings創立，負責管理其中國大陸以外的資料中心資產與業務。公司去年完成品牌更名，今年6月並完成C輪融資，募得45億美元。其投資人陣容包括軟銀願景基金（SoftBank Vision Fund）、Coatue Management及肯·葛里芬（Ken Griffin）。目前DayOne旗下共有7座資料中心處於規畫中或已投入營運，據點遍及新加坡、馬來西亞、日本、香港及芬蘭。

  ## 區域融資背景

  DayOne此番密集融資，是亞太地區資料中心融資浪潮的縮影。受人工智慧運算基礎設施需求急速攀升所驅動，整個區域的大規模融資動能持續湧現。泰國True互聯網資料中心（True Internet Data Center）正為當地一個專案尋求等值約20億美元的貸款；藍貓頭鷹資本（Blue Owl Capital）旗下Stack Infrastructure，則正在爭取一筆澳幣85億元（約59億美元）的銀團貸款，用以資助位於澳洲墨爾本的資料中心建設。這一系列動態，顯示亞太區銀行對大規模資料中心債務的胃口依然旺盛。

  ## IPO計畫

  據知情人士透露，DayOne正評估赴美掛牌，目標募資規模約50億美元，最快可能於下一季正式啟動。
