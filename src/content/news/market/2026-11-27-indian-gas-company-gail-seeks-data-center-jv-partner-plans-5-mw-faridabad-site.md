body_en: |
  GAIL India, the state-owned natural gas distributor, is seeking an equal joint venture partner to enter the data center industry, with plans to build a 5-megawatt (MW) facility in Faridabad, Haryana, on company-owned land as its first project, according to an expression of interest reported by the Economic Times.

  The joint venture would be structured as an equal partnership. GAIL would contribute land and access to its fiber network; the joint venture entity would be responsible for arranging power and water supply and for establishing, operating, and commercializing the facilities, the Economic Times reported.

  ## Partner selection criteria

  GAIL is evaluating prospective partners against three criteria, according to the expression of interest: ownership of at least one operational data center with a minimum of 1 MW of capacity, demonstrated ability to book capacity at the planned Faridabad site, and revenue performance over the most recent three financial years.

  ## GAIL's infrastructure base

  GAIL owns a 15,800-kilometer optical fiber network that runs alongside its natural gas pipelines across India, as well as land parcels in multiple states. Its access to natural gas, including at pipeline compressor stations, could also be used to fuel on-site power generation for the energy-intensive facilities, the report noted.

  Subject to the Faridabad facility's performance and favorable site assessments, GAIL plans to develop additional data centers in Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, and Maharashtra.

  ## India's data center market

  India is the third-largest data center market in the Asia-Pacific region by operational capacity, according to real estate services firm Cushman & Wakefield. The country's development pipeline reached nearly 3.9 gigawatts (GW) as of the first half of 2026, driven by rising data consumption, cloud adoption, and artificial intelligence workloads, with global hyperscalers, cloud providers, and domestic operators continuing to expand their investments, according to W.Media.

  GAIL is the latest company to enter a sector that has drawn increased investment as demand for digital infrastructure grows across India. The company has not yet selected a joint venture partner; the expression of interest process is ongoing.

  印度國營天然氣分銷商GAIL India正尋求對等合資夥伴，計畫進軍資料中心產業，首個項目擬在哈里亞納邦法里達巴德（Faridabad）的公司自有土地上興建一座5百萬瓦（MW）設施。此一意向書計畫由《經濟時報》（Economic Times）率先披露。

  依據《經濟時報》的報導，此次合資架構採對等持股方式：GAIL將提供土地及其光纖網路的接取權，合資公司則負責安排電力與水源供應，並負責建設、營運及商業化相關設施。

  ## 合資夥伴遴選條件

  依據意向書，GAIL以三項標準評估潛在合資夥伴：其一，須擁有至少一座裝置容量不低於1MW的實際運營資料中心；其二，須具備在法里達巴德預定據點預訂容量的能力；其三，須提供最近三個財政年度的營收表現。

  ## GAIL基礎建設概況

  GAIL在印度境內擁有一條全長1萬5,800公里的光纖網路，沿其天然氣管線鋪設，並持有分布於多個邦的土地資產。報導指出，GAIL亦可透過天然氣管線壓縮機站等設施，將天然氣用於資料中心的現地發電，以支應這類高耗能設施的用電需求。

  若法里達巴德設施運營成效良好、各候選地點評估結果正面，GAIL計畫進一步在古吉拉特邦、北方邦、哈里亞納邦及馬哈拉什特拉邦設立更多資料中心。

  ## 印度資料中心市場

  根據房地產服務業者高緯物業（Cushman & Wakefield）的資料，印度是亞太地區按實際運營容量計算的第三大資料中心市場。據W.Media統計，截至2026年上半年，印度資料中心開發管線規模已達近39億瓦（3.9GW），成長動能來自數據消費攀升、雲端運算普及，以及人工智慧工作負載需求擴大，全球超大規模雲端業者、雲端服務供應商與本土業者持續加碼投資。

  GAIL是最新一家進軍資料中心產業的企業，加入了隨印度數位基礎建設需求成長而湧入的投資浪潮。目前GAIL尚未選定合資夥伴，意向書徵求程序仍在進行中。
