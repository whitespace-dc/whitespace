body_en: |
  French cloud operator OVHcloud posted levered free cash flow of €24.7 million for the fiscal year ended August 31, 2026, its first positive cash generation since its 2021 stock market listing, and forecast revenue growth exceeding 10% for the coming fiscal year. The company's shares rose more than 10% on the day of the announcement and have more than doubled in value since the start of 2026.

  Levered free cash flow — operating cash after capital spending and debt service costs — reaching positive territory means OVHcloud now covers its heavy infrastructure investment from its own operations, a threshold the company had not crossed in five years as a public company. The prior fiscal year's levered free cash flow was an outflow of €27.0 million, making the year-over-year swing roughly €52 million.

  ## Revenue and profitability

  Total revenue for fiscal 2026 grew 7.2% to €1.15 billion ($1.29 billion). Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation — EBITDA, a measure of core operating profitability before non-cash charges — rose 9.7% to €480.5 million, pushing the adjusted EBITDA margin to 42.0%. The company is targeting a margin above 42.5% for fiscal 2027.

  ## Segment performance

  Growth accelerated in the fourth quarter, driven by the Digital Scalers segment, which serves customers spending more than €2,000 per month on OVHcloud services, Chief Executive Octave Klaba told Reuters. Digital Scalers posted comparable revenue growth of 12.4% to €359.1 million and accounted for 31% of total company sales. Public cloud revenue grew 18.9% to €255.1 million.

  Klaba, who returned to the chief executive role approximately a year before the results were reported, said OVHcloud had reorganised its product offering since his return and that the fiscal year had been one of "discipline" and a return to fundamentals.

  ## Debt and liquidity

  Net debt stood at €1.16 billion at fiscal year-end, equal to 2.7 times adjusted EBITDA, down from 2.9 times at the close of the prior fiscal year. The company reported €255 million in available liquidity and said no significant debt repayment falls due before fiscal 2030.

  ## What it means

  OVHcloud describes itself as Europe's biggest hyperscaler and positions itself as an alternative to Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, and Amazon Web Services. Reaching self-funding status on cash generation removes a structural concern that had weighed on the company since its IPO and underpins its ability to sustain the capital spending required to compete in the cloud infrastructure market.

  For the fiscal year through August 2027, OVHcloud forecast revenue growth exceeding 10% alongside continued positive free cash flow generation, and is targeting an adjusted EBITDA margin above 42.5%.

  法國雲端業者 OVHcloud 公布截至 2026 年 8 月 31 日財政年度財報，槓桿自由現金流達 2,470 萬歐元，為該公司自 2021 年掛牌上市以來首度轉正，並預測下一財政年度營收成長將超過 10%。消息公布當天，公司股價單日漲幅逾一成，自 2026 年初以來累計漲幅已超過一倍。

  所謂槓桿自由現金流，係指扣除資本支出與債務償還成本後的營運現金。這項數字轉為正值，意味 OVHcloud 現已能以自身營運產生的現金支應龐大的基礎設施投資——這是公司自上市以來五年間始終未能跨越的門檻。上一財政年度的槓桿自由現金流為流出 2,700 萬歐元，兩個財政年度相比，年度間擺幅約達 5,200 萬歐元。

  ## 營收與獲利能力

  2026 財政年度總營收年增 7.2%，達 11.5 億歐元（約 12.9 億美元）。調整後息稅折舊攤銷前利潤（EBITDA，衡量核心營運獲利能力、排除非現金費用的指標）年增 9.7%，達 4.805 億歐元，調整後 EBITDA 利潤率因此提升至 42.0%。公司設定的目標，是在 2027 財政年度將此利潤率進一步推升至 42.5% 以上。

  ## 各業務部門表現

  第四季成長明顯加速，主要動力來自「Digital Scalers」業務部門。執行長奧克塔夫·克拉巴（Octave Klaba）向路透社表示，該部門服務的是每月在 OVHcloud 消費逾 2,000 歐元的客戶。Digital Scalers 部門可比營收年增 12.4%，達 3.591 億歐元，佔公司總營收的 31%；公有雲營收則年增 18.9%，達 2.551 億歐元。

  克拉巴約在財報公布前一年重返執行長一職。他表示，回任以來 OVHcloud 已重新整頓產品線，本財政年度是著重「紀律」、回歸基本面的一年。

  ## 債務與流動性

  截至財政年度末，淨負債為 11.6 億歐元，相當於調整後 EBITDA 的 2.7 倍，較上一財政年度末的 2.9 倍有所改善。公司報告可用流動性為 2.55 億歐元，並表示在 2030 財政年度之前沒有重大債務到期還款。

  ## 意義解讀

  OVHcloud 自我定位為歐洲規模最大的超大規模雲端服務商（hyperscaler），並以微軟 Azure、Google Cloud 與亞馬遜雲端運算服務（AWS）的替代選項自居。現金流轉為自給自足，消除了自 IPO 以來持續壓抑公司表現的結構性隱憂，也為 OVHcloud 在雲端基礎設施市場持續進行必要資本支出奠定了基礎。

  針對截至 2027 年 8 月的財政年度，OVHcloud 預測營收成長將超過 10%，同時維持自由現金流持續轉正，並設定調整後 EBITDA 利潤率高於 42.5% 的目標。
