body_en: |
  The Philippine Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) has concluded the consultation phase for a nationwide investment corridor called SIKAT — short for Super Intelligence, Knowledge, and Advanced Technology — linking mineral processing sites in Mindanao with digital infrastructure projects in the provinces of Aurora and Batangas and an integrated circuit design hub in Muntinlupa City.

  Trade Undersecretary Ceferino S. Rodolfo announced the development on Wednesday on the sidelines of the 40th Confederation of Asia-Pacific Chambers of Commerce and Industry Conference. "It's a technology corridor encompassing the AI ecosystem from minerals to advanced manufacturing, and digital infrastructure," Rodolfo told reporters.

  ## What SIKAT covers

  The corridor spans four distinct project categories, according to Rodolfo:

  - Mineral processing facilities in Mindanao
  - Digital infrastructure developments in Batangas province
  - Digital infrastructure developments in Aurora province
  - An integrated circuit design hub in Muntinlupa City, Metro Manila

  The Board of Investments (BoI), which is chaired by Trade Secretary Maria Cristina A. Roque, has already briefed government and industry stakeholders on the initiative.

  ## Relationship to existing programs

  SIKAT is positioned as complementary to two existing initiatives: the Pax Silica AI-native hub under development in New Clark City, and the Luzon Economic Corridor. DTI did not specify in its announcement how project financing, timelines, or regulatory frameworks for SIKAT will be structured, nor did it quantify investment targets.

  ## Policy rationale

  Roque framed the corridor in terms of industrial policy driven by AI adoption. "In the age of artificial intelligence and digital transformation, we need to turn technological disruption into concrete industrial strength, quality jobs, and sustainable prosperity for our people," she said in a statement quoted by DTI.

  The corridor's design — running from upstream mineral processing through advanced manufacturing to digital infrastructure — reflects a stated DTI goal of using AI and digital transformation to drive industrial growth and exports.

  ## What comes next

  With the consultation stage complete, the BoI is expected to move SIKAT toward formal investment promotion and project development phases, though DTI did not announce a specific timeline for those steps.

  ## 菲律賓啟動SIKAT走廊：串聯棉蘭老礦產與奧羅拉數位基礎建設

  菲律賓貿易工業部（DTI）已完成一項名為SIKAT全國性投資走廊的諮詢階段。SIKAT為「超級智慧、知識與先進科技」（Super Intelligence, Knowledge, and Advanced Technology）的縮寫，計畫將棉蘭老島的礦產加工基地，與奧羅拉省及描水省的數位基礎建設項目，以及大馬尼拉區蒙廷盧帕市的積體電路設計中心緊密相連。

  貿易工業部次長塞費里諾·S·羅多爾福（Ceferino S. Rodolfo）於本週三在第40屆亞太商工總會年會場邊宣布這項進展。他向媒體表示：「這是一條科技走廊，涵蓋從礦產到先進製造業、以及數位基礎建設的完整AI生態系。」

  ## SIKAT涵蓋範圍

  根據羅多爾福的說明，這條走廊橫跨四大項目類別：

  - 棉蘭老島的礦產加工設施
  - 描水省的數位基礎建設開發項目
  - 奧羅拉省的數位基礎建設開發項目
  - 蒙廷盧帕市（大馬尼拉區）的積體電路設計中心

  由貿工部長瑪麗亞·克里斯蒂娜·A·洛克（Maria Cristina A. Roque）擔任主席的投資委員會（BoI），已就這項計畫向政府與產業界的利害關係人進行簡報。

  ## 與現有計畫的關係

  SIKAT被定位為兩項現有計畫的互補方案：一是正在新克拉克市開發中的「帕克斯矽谷AI原生樞紐」（Pax Silica AI-native hub），二是「呂宋經濟走廊」。貿工部在公告中並未具體說明SIKAT的專案融資方式、推動時程或監管框架，也未設定量化的投資目標。

  ## 政策背景

  洛克部長以AI應用驅動的產業政策為框架，詮釋這條走廊的戰略意義。她在貿工部引述的聲明中表示：「在人工智慧與數位轉型的時代，我們必須將科技顛覆轉化為具體的產業實力、優質就業機會，以及人民的永續繁榮。」

  這條走廊的設計——從上游礦產加工，貫穿先進製造業，直至數位基礎建設——體現了貿工部以AI與數位轉型帶動工業成長與出口的既定目標。

  ## 後續展望

  隨著諮詢階段告一段落，投資委員會預計將推動SIKAT進入正式的投資推廣與項目開發階段，但貿工部目前尚未宣布相關步驟的具體時程。
