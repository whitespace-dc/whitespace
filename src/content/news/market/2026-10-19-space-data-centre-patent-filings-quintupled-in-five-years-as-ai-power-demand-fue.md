  ## Patent count and investment both rising

  Patent filings for technology to perform computing in space have increased fivefold over a five-year period, and significant funds are flowing into the space data centre sector, according to independent analysis published October 15 by Nikkei xTECH, the Japanese technology news outlet.

  The finding pushes back against the perception that space data centres — orbital or otherwise extraterrestrial facilities designed to host computing workloads — are a topic driven primarily by media attention and corporate announcements. Nikkei xTECH said its analysis shows that research-and-development momentum is also accelerating alongside the investment, with the sharp rise in patent disclosures as the primary indicator.

  ## AI power demand identified as primary driver

  The publication attributes the sector's growth directly to power shortages made apparent by the widespread adoption of artificial intelligence. Data centres that train and serve AI models are among the most electricity-intensive facilities built at scale, and the resulting strain on terrestrial power grids and energy infrastructure has prompted industry and investors to look at space-based computing as one potential remedy.

  The piece notes that large technology companies — characterised in the article as "big tech" — have begun entering the space data centre field, lending the sector credibility beyond early-stage startups. Nikkei xTECH did not name specific companies in the publicly available portion of the article.

  ## Context within the Technology Future Investment Index

  The analysis is part of Nikkei xTECH's recurring "Technology Future Investment Index," a ranking series that tracks patent activity and capital flows across emerging technology categories to identify sectors with the strongest underlying development momentum. Space solar power — a technology closely related to space-based computing infrastructure in that it also relies on orbital hardware — ranked first in an earlier edition of the same index published in April 2026.

  ## Significance

  The fivefold rise in space computing patents over five years, taken together with documented investment inflows, suggests the sector has moved past conceptual discussion into competitive intellectual-property positioning. That shift typically precedes commercial deployment activity, making the patent data a leading indicator of near-term market entrants and product timelines.

  Nikkei xTECH's full quantitative breakdown of the patent data, investor identity, and funding volumes remains behind its subscriber paywall, with the publication indicating that additional detail appears in subsequent pages of the article.

  ## 專利申請數量與投資金額雙雙攀升

  根據日本科技媒體日經xTECH於10月15日發布的獨立分析報告，在太空中執行運算任務的相關技術專利申請量，在五年內成長了五倍，同時有大量資金持續流入太空資料中心這個新興產業。

  這項研究結果，有力地反駁了外界認為太空資料中心議題不過是媒體炒作與企業公關噱頭的刻板印象。太空資料中心是指建置於軌道或其他地外環境、用以承載運算工作負載的設施。日經xTECH表示，其分析顯示，研發動能也在投資升溫的同時同步加速，而專利申請數量的急劇攀升，正是最直接的佐證指標。

  ## AI用電需求確立為主要驅動力

  報告將這個產業的快速成長，直接歸因於人工智慧大規模普及後所暴露的電力短缺問題。用於訓練及提供AI模型推論服務的資料中心，是目前規模化建設中耗電量最為龐大的設施之一，地面電網與能源基礎建設因此承受的沉重壓力，促使業界與投資人開始將太空運算視為潛在的解決方案之一。

  報告中亦指出，文章所稱的「大型科技公司」已陸續進入太空資料中心領域，為這個產業帶來了超越早期新創階段的市場公信力。不過，日經xTECH在文章的公開部分並未具名點出特定企業。

  ## 「科技未來投資指數」的研究脈絡

  此次分析是日經xTECH定期發布的「科技未來投資指數」系列報告的一部分。該指數追蹤各新興科技類別的專利活動與資金流向，藉此找出研發動能最強勁的產業領域。在2026年4月發布的前一期同系列報告中，太空太陽能發電——一項在依賴軌道硬體這一點上與太空運算基礎設施高度相關的技術——奪得了第一名的位置。

  ## 研究意義

  太空運算專利在五年內成長五倍，加上有據可查的資金持續流入，顯示這個產業已從概念性討論階段，邁入了激烈的智慧財產權布局競爭。這種轉變通常預示著商業部署活動即將展開，使得專利數據成為研判近期市場新進者與產品時程的領先指標。

  日經xTECH針對專利數據的完整量化分析、投資方身份及融資規模等細節，仍置於訂閱付費牆之後，該媒體表示，更詳盡的資訊收錄於文章的後續頁面中。
