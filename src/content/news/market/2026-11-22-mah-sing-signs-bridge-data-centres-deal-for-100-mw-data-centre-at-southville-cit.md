body_en: |
  Malaysian property developer Mah Sing Group Berhad signed a collaboration agreement with Bridge Data Centres (BDC), a company primarily owned by Bain Capital, to build a data centre of up to 100 megawatts (MW) of power capacity at Southville City in Selangor, marking Mah Sing's first entry into the digital infrastructure sector. Deputy Prime Minister Dato' Sri Fadillah Yusof attended the signing ceremony.

  The initial BDC facility will occupy 17.55 acres within Southville City, a 428-acre freehold township 19 kilometres from Kuala Lumpur City Centre. Mah Sing has designated 150 acres at the site for data centre development overall, targeting a total planned campus capacity of up to 500 MW. The company said the hub is designed to serve AI workloads, hyperscale cloud operators, and retail and enterprise customers.

  ## Government endorsement

  Deputy Prime Minister Dato' Sri Fadillah, who also serves as Minister of Energy Transition and Water Transformation, said the project "aligns with Malaysia's digital transformation agenda" and would position the country as "the digital hub of ASEAN." He cited government policies including a smart grid programme and renewable energy incentives as supporting factors.

  ## Location and connectivity

  Southville City sits approximately 20 kilometres from both Cyberjaya and Bukit Jalil, the two established data centre clusters in greater Kuala Lumpur, forming what Mah Sing described as a strategic triangle of digital infrastructure hubs.

  The site also lies within 50 kilometres of a planned cable landing station at Morib, Selangor, operated by Telekom Malaysia (TM). That station will serve as a landing point for the SEA-ME-WE-6 submarine cable system, and its proximity will allow the hub to supply dark fiber, or unlit fiber-optic cable capacity, to tenants, Mah Sing said. TM's Morib station was described in the announcement as expected to complete in the first quarter of 2025.

  ## BDC on demand

  BDC President Eric Fan said the company's "expansion journey in Malaysia has yielded strong results due to the accelerated demand for quality and scalable data centres," and that Southville City's locational attributes gave the company confidence in end-user interest. Fan added that the partnership would allow BDC to draw on "Malaysia's superior infrastructure, skilled workforce, and prime geographic location."

  ## Mah Sing's strategic rationale

  Mah Sing Group Chairman Tan Sri Dato' Sri Leong Hoy Kum framed the move as a deliberate diversification from the company's property development business, which currently covers landed and high-rise residences, townships, offices, retail spaces, and industrial projects. He said data centre income would be recurring, providing what he called "a more resilient financial foundation amidst market fluctuations" and reducing the company's exposure to property cycle risk.

  ## Expansion pipeline

  Beyond Southville City, Mah Sing identified its MSS Business Park in Sepang, Selangor, as a candidate for future data centre collaboration. The company cited the site's proximity to TM's Morib cable landing station as a comparable connectivity advantage to Southville City.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞房地產開發商馬星集團（Mah Sing Group Berhad）與貝恩資本（Bain Capital）旗下Bridge Data Centres（BDC）簽署合作協議，計畫在雪蘭莪州Southville City興建最高達100百萬瓦（MW）供電容量的資料中心，標誌著馬星集團首度跨足數位基礎建設領域。副總理拿督斯里法迪拉·尤索夫（Dato' Sri Fadillah Yusof）出席了簽約儀式。

  BDC首期設施將座落於Southville City內17.55英畝的土地上。Southville City是一個佔地428英畝的永久地契城鎮，距吉隆坡市中心約19公里。馬星集團已將園區內150英畝土地規劃為資料中心開發用途，目標整體園區總容量最高達500MW。公司表示，此一資料中心樞紐專為服務AI運算工作負載、超大規模雲端業者，以及零售與企業客戶而設計。

  ## 政府背書

  副總理法迪拉同時兼任能源轉型與水務轉型部長，他表示此項目「契合馬來西亞的數位轉型議程」，將助力馬來西亞成為「東南亞的數位樞紐」。他並援引政府推動的智慧電網計畫及再生能源激勵措施，作為支持項目發展的有利因素。

  ## 地理位置與連網優勢

  Southville City距大吉隆坡地區兩大既有資料中心聚落——賽城（Cyberjaya）與武吉加里爾（Bukit Jalil）——均約20公里，馬星集團形容三地構成數位基礎建設樞紐的「策略三角」。

  此外，該地點距雪蘭莪州莫里布（Morib）的一座計畫海纜登陸站不到50公里，由馬來西亞電信（TM）負責營運。該登陸站將作為SEA-ME-WE-6海底電纜系統的登陸點，借助此一地緣優勢，園區可向租戶提供暗光纖（即尚未點亮的光纖電纜容量）服務，馬星集團如是說。公告中指出，TM莫里布登陸站預計於2025年第一季竣工。

  ## BDC談市場需求

  BDC總裁Eric Fan表示，公司「在馬來西亞的擴張歷程中，因優質、可擴展資料中心的需求加速而交出亮眼成績」，並指出Southville City的區位特性讓公司對終端用戶需求深具信心。他補充，此次夥伴關係將使BDC得以充分借助「馬來西亞優越的基礎建設、技術熟練的勞動力及絕佳的地理位置」。

  ## 馬星集團的策略盤算

  馬星集團主席丹斯里拿督斯里梁海金（Tan Sri Dato' Sri Leong Hoy Kum）將此次佈局定位為刻意從旗下房地產開發業務推動多元化——集團現有業務涵蓋有地住宅與高樓住宅、城鎮開發、辦公室、零售空間及工業項目。他表示，資料中心收入屬於經常性收入，能提供他所稱的「在市場波動中更具韌性的財務基礎」，並降低集團對房地產週期風險的暴露程度。

  ## 擴張管線

  除Southville City之外，馬星集團亦將位於雪蘭莪州雪邦（Sepang）的MSS商業園區列為未來資料中心合作的候選地點。公司援引該地點鄰近TM莫里布海纜登陸站，擁有與Southville City相當的連網優勢。
