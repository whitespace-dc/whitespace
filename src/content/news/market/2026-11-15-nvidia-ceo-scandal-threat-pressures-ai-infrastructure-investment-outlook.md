body_en: |
  Reports that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang could be forced out amid allegations of a personal scandal have triggered broad market concern over the future of the chipmaker's artificial intelligence infrastructure spending commitments, which total $164.5 billion, according to coverage by Kontan.

  Nvidia, the semiconductor company at the center of the global AI hardware buildout, carries a market capitalization of $4 trillion. Any leadership transition is considered capable of altering the strategic and funding direction of a company whose chip supply underpins data center expansion worldwide.

  ## Market reaction

  The uncertainty immediately weighed on Nvidia's share price and that of its partners, while shares of competing chipmakers moved higher, Kontan reported. The divergence reflects investor concern that a change at the top could disrupt capital allocation plans and slow the pace of infrastructure commitments that have supported the broader AI supply chain.

  ## What is at stake

  Nvidia's $164.5 billion in AI infrastructure investment commitments represents a major share of the capital flowing into global data center capacity. AI infrastructure refers to the physical computing hardware — servers, chips, networking equipment, and facilities — required to train and run large artificial intelligence models. Disruption to Nvidia's funding strategy would affect not only the company but the network of suppliers, cloud providers, and data center operators that depend on its procurement and partnership activity.

  ## What comes next

  Investors are now focused on Nvidia's financial results, due the following week at the time of reporting, as the first formal opportunity to assess whether the company's investment trajectory and strategic direction remain intact under the scrutiny surrounding its chief executive.
