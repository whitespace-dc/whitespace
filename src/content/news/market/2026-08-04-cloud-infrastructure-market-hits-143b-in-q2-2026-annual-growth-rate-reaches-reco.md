body_en: |
  ## Market posts record growth rate

  Global cloud infrastructure revenue reached $143 billion in the second quarter of 2026, a 43% increase from the same period a year earlier, according to new data from Synergy Research Group. The firm said the growth rate is the highest ever recorded for the market, which it defines as the combined total of infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS), platform-as-a-service (PaaS), and hosted private cloud services.

  The $143 billion quarterly total represents more than double the market's size from three years prior, Synergy Research said.

  ## Big three hold position

  Amazon Web Services retained its lead with 28% of the global market, unchanged from the previous quarter. Microsoft Azure fell one percentage point to 20%, and Google Cloud gained one percentage point to 15%, according to Synergy Research's data as reported by Publickey.

  All three providers held or grew their combined share even as the overall market expanded at a record pace. Publickey, citing the Synergy Research figures, noted that the largest cloud vendors maintaining their positions during a period of extraordinary market-wide growth indicates the three companies are driving that expansion rather than being diluted by it.

  ## What the figures mean

  A 43% annual growth rate on a $143 billion quarterly base implies an annualized run rate approaching $600 billion for the sector. The more-than-doubling of market size in three years underscores the sustained acceleration in enterprise and hyperscaler cloud adoption, though Synergy Research did not break out specific demand drivers in the reported findings.

  Google Cloud's one-point share gain to 15% is notable in the context of a fast-growing market: holding share in an expanding market requires absolute revenue growth proportional to the market's rate of increase, meaning a share gain represents outpacing the already-elevated market average. Azure's one-point decline to 20%, conversely, indicates its revenue growth came in below the market average for the quarter, though the firm still commands the second-largest position globally.

  ## 市場成長率創歷史新高

  根據Synergy Research Group最新數據，全球雲端基礎設施市場2026年第二季營收達1,430億美元，較去年同期成長43%。該研究機構指出，此成長率為該市場有紀錄以來最高，其統計範疇涵蓋基礎設施即服務（IaaS）、平台即服務（PaaS）及託管私有雲服務的合計總額。

  Synergy Research表示，1,430億美元的單季規模，已超過三年前市場規模的兩倍以上。

  ## 三大巨頭守住版圖

  根據Synergy Research的數據（由Publickey報導），亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）以28%的全球市佔率蟬聯龍頭，與前一季持平。微軟Azure市佔率下滑一個百分點至20%，谷歌雲端（Google Cloud）則上升一個百分點至15%。

  在整體市場以創紀錄速度擴張的同時，三大業者的合計市佔率仍維持不變甚至有所提升。Publickey引述Synergy Research數據指出，三大雲端龍頭能在市場整體高速成長期間守住陣地，意味著正是這三家企業在驅動市場擴張，而非被市場所稀釋。

  ## 數字背後的意義

  在1,430億美元單季基期上實現43%的年增率，意味著該產業的年化營收規模正逼近6,000億美元。市場規模在三年內翻逾一倍，凸顯了企業與超大規模雲端業者在雲端採用上的持續加速，儘管Synergy Research在其報告中並未進一步拆解具體的需求驅動因素。

  谷歌雲端市佔率上升一個百分點至15%，在高速成長的市場背景下格外值得關注：在持續擴張的市場中維持市佔率，代表絕對營收必須以不低於市場平均的速度成長，因此市佔率的提升，意味著其成長幅度已超越本已相當高的市場平均水準。相反地，Azure市佔率下滑一個百分點至20%，則顯示其本季營收成長低於市場平均，儘管微軟仍穩居全球第二大雲端業者的地位。
