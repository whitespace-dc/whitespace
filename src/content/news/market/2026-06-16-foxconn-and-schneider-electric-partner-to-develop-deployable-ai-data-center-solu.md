body_en: |
  Foxconn and Schneider Electric announced a strategic partnership on June 15 to jointly develop and scale next-generation artificial intelligence (AI) data centers, combining the Taiwanese manufacturer's advanced computing platforms and global factory network with the French energy equipment maker's power systems, cooling technology, and energy management capabilities. Joint production under the agreement is set to begin later in 2026.

  ## Partnership scope

  The two companies said they will co-develop reference architectures for AI data centers and produce integrated, immediately deployable solutions designed to help customers build and operate AI infrastructure faster, more efficiently, and with better advance planning.

  Specific technical areas under joint development include closed-loop energy optimization, modular power and cooling systems, and standardized design frameworks. The stated goal is a repeatable, high-performance AI data center blueprint that can be deployed and scaled globally.

  ## Power as the central constraint

  Foxconn characterized electricity supply as one of the biggest bottlenecks in AI data center development. The company said the Schneider Electric deal is its third successive power-sector alliance, following earlier partnerships with Teco Electric and Mitsubishi Electric. Foxconn said the rationale across all three is to expand into different markets, develop new technologies, produce related products, and address tight electricity supply through better generation efficiency and smarter energy solutions.

  ## What each party contributes

  Foxconn brings advanced computing platforms, AI server cabinet integration, and its global manufacturing footprint to the partnership. Schneider Electric contributes power system design, cooling technology, and energy management.

  Schneider Electric Chief Executive Olivier Blum said AI demand is accelerating and that energy systems have become a critical driver as computing scale expands. He said the company is focused on integrating power, cooling, and digital capabilities to build efficient and sustainable AI data centers.

  Foxconn Chairman Young Liu said the industry needs new models for AI infrastructure design, construction, and delivery, and that the partnership aims to produce faster, smarter, and more sustainable deployments for customers.

  ## Industry significance

  The partnership reflects what both companies described as a fundamental shift in AI infrastructure demand, in which power supply, cooling, and energy management have become co-equal priorities alongside compute hardware. By pursuing successive alliances with established electrical equipment makers, Foxconn said it is working to offer clients faster deployment, greater efficiency, and better pre-planned AI infrastructure across global markets.

  Joint production under the Foxconn-Schneider Electric agreement is scheduled to begin later in 2026.

  鴻海與法國施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）6月15日宣布建立策略合作夥伴關係，雙方將攜手研發並擴大規模，推出新世代人工智慧（AI）資料中心解決方案。此次合作結合鴻海先進的運算平台與遍布全球的工廠網路，以及施耐德電氣在電力系統、冷卻技術與能源管理方面的核心能力，雙方依據協議展開的聯合生產，預計於2026年下半年正式啟動。

  ## 合作範疇

  兩家公司表示，將共同開發AI資料中心的參考架構，並生產整合度高、可即時部署的解決方案，協助客戶以更快的速度、更高的效率建置與營運AI基礎設施，同時提升前期規劃的精準度。

  雙方聯合開發的具體技術領域，涵蓋閉迴路能源優化、模組化電力與冷卻系統，以及標準化設計框架。最終目標是打造一套可重複套用、高效能的AI資料中心藍圖，使其能夠在全球各地靈活部署與擴展規模。

  ## 電力成為核心瓶頸

  鴻海指出，電力供應是AI資料中心開發過程中最大的瓶頸之一。與施耐德電氣的合作，是鴻海繼先前分別與東元電機、三菱電機締結夥伴關係之後，第三度與電力產業相關企業結盟。鴻海說明，三項合作背後的共同邏輯，在於開拓不同市場、研發新技術、生產相關產品，並透過提升發電效率與導入更智慧的能源解決方案，積極因應電力供給緊張的問題。

  ## 雙方各自貢獻

  鴻海為此次合作帶來先進運算平台、AI伺服器機櫃整合能力，以及遍布全球的製造版圖；施耐德電氣則貢獻電力系統設計、冷卻技術與能源管理專業。

  施耐德電氣執行長奧利維爾·布魯姆（Olivier Blum）表示，AI需求正以飛快的速度成長，隨著運算規模持續擴大，能源系統已成為關鍵驅動因素。他指出，公司致力於整合電力、冷卻與數位能力，以建構高效且永續的AI資料中心。

  鴻海董事長劉揚偉則表示，產業需要全新的AI基礎設施設計、建置與交付模式，此次合作的目標正是為客戶實現更快速、更智慧、更永續的部署方案。

  ## 產業意義

  此次合作反映出兩家公司共同描述的AI基礎設施需求結構性轉變——電力供應、冷卻技術與能源管理，已與運算硬體並列為同等重要的優先考量。鴻海表示，透過接連與成熟電力設備廠商締結聯盟，公司正積極為全球市場的客戶提供部署速度更快、效率更高、預先規劃更完善的AI基礎設施。
