body_en: |
  Global steel demand is undergoing a structural shift, with AI data centers, power infrastructure, new energy, and automotive manufacturing emerging as growth engines to replace the construction sector, which has weakened as China's real estate market continues to contract, market participants said.

  ## Construction demand falls while industrial demand holds steady

  Market analysts cited by Taiwan's Economic Daily News said China's housing market has not fully recovered, leaving construction steel demand with limited room for improvement. New energy, grid upgrades, automotive, and broader manufacturing investment have remained steady, the analysts said, meaning overall demand has not declined across the board but is rotating from traditional construction toward industrial and high-end manufacturing applications.

  ## AI infrastructure emerges as a distinct steel consumer

  AI infrastructure has drawn particular market attention as a new source of steel consumption, the report said. The buildout of AI data centers requires steel for structural frames and mechanical-electrical systems. Associated demand extends to ultra-high-voltage transmission and distribution systems, transformers, energy equipment, and cooling systems — all requiring large volumes of high-quality steel, according to the report. That demand profile is raising the strategic importance of specialty steel, high-strength steel, and electromagnetic steel.

  ## Grid upgrades and renewables partially offset property shortfall

  Countries worldwide are actively promoting grid upgrades alongside offshore wind, solar, and energy-storage construction, the report noted. The resulting demand for high-performance steel is gradually offsetting part of the shortfall created by the cooling real estate market.

  ## Product mix and technical capability displace volume as the competitive edge

  Industry analysts quoted in the report said future competitiveness in the steel sector will be determined less by raw production volume and more by product mix and technical capability. Steel mills with portfolios weighted toward high-value products are expected to hold a competitive advantage over general producers.

  Taiwan's China Steel Corporation (中鋼, CSC) was cited as an example of a producer pursuing this repositioning. The company has been advancing a high-value transformation, building positions in electromagnetic steel, high-strength automotive steel, energy steel, and low-carbon steel — product categories aligned with the global shift toward high-end manufacturing, the report said. Downstream processors and distribution channel operators, including Shin Kong Steel (新光鋼), were also identified as potential beneficiaries of energy construction, public works projects, and manufacturing investment.

  ## Outlook

  Industry participants said global steel demand will remain subject to the trajectory of China's housing market, but three structural trends — AI infrastructure, energy transition, and high-end manufacturing — are progressively establishing new demand pillars to underpin the market.
body_zh: |
  ## 建築需求下滑，工業需求維持穩健

  據《經濟日報》引述市場分析師的看法，中國房市尚未完全復甦，建築用鋼的需求改善空間十分有限。新能源、電網升級、汽車製造及整體製造業投資則維持穩定，分析師指出，這意味著整體需求並非全面衰退，而是從傳統建築領域轉向工業及高端製造應用。

  ## AI基礎建設崛起，成為鋼鐵消費新亮點

  報告指出，AI基礎建設已受到市場高度關注，被視為鋼鐵消費的全新來源。AI資料中心的大規模建設，需要鋼材用於結構框架與機電系統；相關需求更延伸至超高壓輸配電系統、變壓器、能源設備及冷卻系統，這些領域均需大量高品質鋼材。此一需求結構正進一步提升特殊鋼、高強度鋼及電磁鋼的戰略重要性。

  ## 電網升級與再生能源部分填補房市缺口

  報告亦指出，全球各國正積極推動電網升級，並同步推進離岸風電、太陽能及儲能設施的建設。由此帶動的高性能鋼材需求，正逐步填補房地產市場降溫所造成的需求缺口。

  ## 產品結構與技術能力取代產量，成為競爭核心

  報告引述產業分析師的看法，未來鋼鐵業的競爭優勢將不再取決於原始產量，而是更多仰賴產品結構與技術能力。產品組合偏重高附加價值的鋼廠，預計將比一般生產商更具競爭優勢。

  報告以台灣中鋼（CSC）為例，說明業者如何積極推動此一轉型布局。中鋼持續深耕高附加價值轉型，在電磁鋼、高強度汽車用鋼、能源鋼及低碳鋼等產品類別建立市場地位，這些品項均與全球邁向高端製造的趨勢高度契合。報告亦指出，下游加工廠及通路業者（包括新光鋼在內），同樣有望受惠於能源建設、公共工程及製造業投資的持續擴大。

  ## 展望

  業界人士表示，全球鋼鐵需求仍將受中國房市走勢牽動，但AI基礎建設、能源轉型與高端製造三大結構性趨勢，正逐步構築起支撐市場的全新需求支柱。
