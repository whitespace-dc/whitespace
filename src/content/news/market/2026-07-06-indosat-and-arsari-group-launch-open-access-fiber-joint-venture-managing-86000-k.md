  ## Indosat and Arsari Group Launch Open-Access Fiber Joint Venture Managing 86,000 km of Network

  PT Indosat Tbk (ticker: ISAT), the operator known as Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, and Arsari Group, the investment conglomerate owned by Aryo Djojohadikusumo, formally launched PT Infra Fiber Teknologi (IFT) in early July 2026 as an independent fiber optic infrastructure platform serving the wholesale connectivity market in Indonesia.

  The new entity immediately took operational control of more than 86,000 kilometers of fiber optic cable, transferred through an asset contribution (known in Indonesian corporate law as *inbreng*) from Indosat and its subsidiary PT Lintasarta, according to Bisnis.com. That scale places IFT among the largest fiber infrastructure operators in the region by network length.

  Indosat raised Rp 11.7 trillion (approximately USD 720 million at mid-2026 exchange rates) from the divestiture of those fiber optic assets to Arsari Group, according to a separately reported headline cited by Bisnis.com.

  ## Network Scope and Architecture

  IFT's infrastructure spans three distinct network layers, the company said. The first is a domestic subsea fiber optic network connecting Indonesia's major islands. The second is a national terrestrial backbone. The third covers metropolitan and non-metropolitan access links.

  This multi-layer topology allows IFT to carry interisland data traffic as well as last-mile and middle-mile capacity within cities and industrial zones.

  ## Open-Access Wholesale Model

  IFT operates on an open-access basis, positioning itself as a neutral, independent wholesale provider rather than as a retail or consumer-facing operator. Its stated customer categories include mobile network operators, internet service providers (ISPs), data center operators, hyperscaler cloud companies, multinational corporations, and government agencies.

  The company has organized its commercial offering into three service lines: Submarine Fiber for interisland connections; Inland Terrestrial Fiber for flexible on-land routing; and Fiber Access (FTTX), a category that covers Fiber to the Home (FTTH), Fiber to the Office (FTTO), and Fiber to the Building (FTTB) deployments.

  By operating on a carrier-neutral basis, IFT avoids competing directly with its own customers — a model that allows multiple operators to share the same underlying infrastructure rather than each building parallel networks.

  ## Strategic Rationale

  Bisnis.com described the venture as part of a broader consolidation and optimization of digital network assets in Indonesia. The company said the entity was created to accelerate connectivity in underserved areas and to strengthen the national telecom backbone ahead of growing demand from artificial intelligence (AI) computing workloads and AI-related deployments in both industry and government.

  Indosat contributed established telecommunications engineering expertise, while Arsari Group brought domestic investment infrastructure, according to the publication's account of the partnership rationale.

  ## Governance and Values

  IFT's management has articulated four operating principles: Reliability, covering network stability to international standards; Scalability, referring to network architecture designed to expand with data traffic growth; Security, covering protection of customer data flows; and Partnership, emphasizing long-term collaboration with digital industry participants.

  The company said it aims to foster a more competitive and integrated operating environment across Indonesia's information technology sector.

  ## Significance

  The formation of IFT represents a structural shift in how a major Indonesian mobile operator manages its passive infrastructure. By carving out fiber assets into a standalone, open-access entity, Indosat converts a capital-intensive network into a separately capitalized platform that serves the entire industry — not only its own retail operations. For the Indonesian digital infrastructure market, the arrival of a neutral wholesale fiber operator managing more than 86,000 kilometers of cable creates a new shared-infrastructure layer that ISPs, data center operators, and hyperscalers can access without negotiating with a competing carrier.

  IFT's stated expansion priority is extending fiber infrastructure to underserved areas of the Indonesian archipelago, which the company identified as its primary geographic focus going forward.

  ## Indosat與Arsari集團合資成立開放接取光纖公司，管理逾8萬6千公里網路

  PT Indosat Tbk（股票代號：ISAT，即Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison）與由Aryo Djojohadikusumo掌控的投資集團Arsari Group，於2026年7月初正式啟動PT Infra Fiber Teknologi（IFT），以獨立光纖基礎建設平台的身份服務印尼批發連線市場。

  根據Bisnis.com報導，IFT成立後隨即取得逾8萬6千公里光纖電纜的營運控制權，這批資產透過印尼公司法所稱的「inbreng」（資產出資）方式，由Indosat及其子公司PT Lintasarta轉移而來。以網路長度計算，IFT的規模已躋身區域最大光纖基礎建設業者之列。

  Bisnis.com另引述相關報導指出，Indosat透過將上述光纖資產出售予Arsari集團，共募得新台幣約2,340億元（印尼盾11兆7,000億盧比，依2026年年中匯率約折合7億2,000萬美元）。

  ## 網路範疇與架構

  IFT表示，其基礎建設涵蓋三個不同的網路層次：第一層為國內海底光纖網路，連結印尼各主要島嶼；第二層為全國陸上骨幹網路；第三層則涵蓋都會及非都會地區的接取鏈路。

  這種多層拓樸架構使IFT得以承載島際資料流量，同時也能提供城市與工業園區內的最後一哩及中間里程接取容量。

  ## 開放接取批發模式

  IFT以開放接取方式運營，定位為中立、獨立的批發服務提供者，而非面向一般消費者的零售業者。其主要客戶類型包括：行動網路業者、網路服務提供者（ISP）、資料中心業者、超大規模雲端公司、跨國企業及政府機關。

  在商業服務方面，IFT規劃了三條服務線：專供島際連線的「海底光纖」（Submarine Fiber）；提供陸上彈性路由的「內陸地面光纖」（Inland Terrestrial Fiber）；以及「光纖接取（FTTX）」，涵蓋光纖到府（FTTH）、光纖到辦公室（FTTO）及光纖到樓（FTTB）等多種佈建形式。

  藉由以電信業者中立方式運營，IFT得以避免與自身客戶直接競爭——這種模式讓多家業者得以共享同一底層基礎建設，無需各自重複建設平行網路。

  ## 策略考量

  Bisnis.com將此次合資描述為印尼數位網路資產整合優化的重要一環。IFT表示，成立本公司旨在加速偏遠地區的連線普及，並在人工智慧（AI）運算工作負載及相關應用於產業與政府部門需求快速增長之前，強化全國電信骨幹基礎建設。

  就合作背景而言，Indosat帶來成熟的電信工程專業能力，Arsari集團則提供本土投資基礎建設資源。

  ## 治理理念

  IFT管理層提出四項營運原則：「可靠性」（Reliability），確保網路穩定性符合國際標準；「可擴展性」（Scalability），指網路架構具備隨資料流量成長而擴充的能力；「安全性」（Security），保護客戶資料流的安全；以及「夥伴關係」（Partnership），強調與數位產業參與者建立長期合作。

  IFT表示，公司致力於在印尼資訊科技產業建立更具競爭力與整合度的營運環境。

  ## 市場意義

  IFT的成立，代表印尼主要行動業者在管理被動基礎建設方面出現結構性轉變。透過將光纖資產獨立為單一開放接取實體，Indosat將一項資本密集型網路轉化為獨立資本化平台，服務整個產業——而不僅限於其自身的零售業務。

  對印尼數位基礎建設市場而言，一家中立批發光纖業者管理逾8萬6千公里電纜的出現，為ISP、資料中心業者及超大規模雲端公司建立了全新的共享基礎建設層，各方皆可直接接取，無需與競爭性電信業者另行談判。

  IFT表示，未來優先擴展方向為將光纖基礎建設延伸至印尼群島偏遠地區，並將此列為首要地理發展重點。
