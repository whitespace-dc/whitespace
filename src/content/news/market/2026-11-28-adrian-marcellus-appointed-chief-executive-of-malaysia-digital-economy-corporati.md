body_en: |
  Adrian Marcellus, former chief executive of MyDigital Corporation, has been appointed chief executive of Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC), the government agency responsible for advancing Malaysia's digital economy and its stated goal of becoming an artificial intelligence (AI) nation by 2030.

  ## Shift in emphasis

  Marcellus takes the role as MDEC enters what the agency describes as a new phase focused on converting existing digital infrastructure investments into domestic economic value, locally developed technology, and high-value employment. MDEC Chairman Ganesh Kumar Bangah said Marcellus's background in national digital policy and the private sector would strengthen the agency's efforts.

  "Malaysia has made significant progress in attracting digital investments and building the infrastructure to support our AI ambitions. The next challenge is to ensure that these strengths translate into greater economic value, stronger Malaysian businesses and meaningful opportunities for the rakyat," Ganesh said, using the Malay word for citizens.

  ## Investment figures

  MDEC said it secured close to US$44 billion (180 billion Malaysian ringgit) in digital investments from 2025 to August 2026, with those commitments projected to generate more than 42,000 high-value jobs. The agency described this investment base as the foundation for its next growth phase.

  ## Marcellus's policy record

  Before joining MDEC, Marcellus served as CEO of MyDigital Corporation and as head of MYCentre4IR, the Malaysia Centre for the Fourth Industrial Revolution. In those roles he played a central part in developing the Malaysia Digital Action Plan 2030 (MD2030), which incorporates the National Fourth Industrial Revolution Policy.

  He also contributed to the creation of the Government Nucleus Unit, now operating as GovTech under the Ministry of Digital, and the National AI Office, since incorporated as AI Malaysia. He expanded the Rakyat Digital programme, a government initiative to widen digital access among citizens, and worked with the World Economic Forum and industry partners through the Transitioning Industrial Clusters initiative to accelerate technology adoption across industrial sectors.

  ## AI access and domestic capability

  Marcellus identified accelerating AI and digital adoption among micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) as a central priority, alongside building Malaysian technology companies capable of competing globally.

  "The success of an AI economy is measured not simply by investment or adoption, but by the value it creates, productivity gains and opportunities it generates. We must accelerate digital and AI adoption across all sectors, particularly among MSMEs, by making these technologies more accessible, affordable and practical," Marcellus said.

  MDEC said it will work with government agencies and industry partners to improve access to AI computing resources, models and solutions through a proposed National AI Compute Exchange (NACX). The NACX is intended to lower barriers for smaller businesses seeking to adopt AI tools.

  ## Policy context and targets

  The appointment aligns with the Thirteenth Malaysia Plan and MD2030, which set a national target for the digital economy to contribute 30% of gross domestic product (GDP), a standard measure of total economic output, by 2030. MDEC, which has operated for three decades, said it will continue to develop new areas of growth, attract high-value strategic investments, and strengthen the global competitiveness of Malaysian technology companies.

  MDEC's stated next steps include advancing the NACX proposal, deepening AI and digital adoption across sectors with particular focus on smaller enterprises, developing local talent, and attracting further strategic investments in support of the AI Nation 2030 objective.

  ## Adrian Marcellus出任馬來西亞數位經濟機構執行長

  前MyDigital Corporation執行長Adrian Marcellus已獲任命為馬來西亞數位經濟機構（MDEC）執行長。MDEC是負責推動馬來西亞數位經濟發展、並致力於在2030年前使馬來西亞成為人工智慧（AI）國家的政府機構。

  ## 政策重心轉移

  Marcellus接任此職之際，MDEC正邁入其所稱的新階段——將重心從吸引數位投資，轉向把現有數位基礎設施投資轉化為本土經濟價值、在地研發技術以及高附加價值就業機會。MDEC主席Ganesh Kumar Bangah表示，Marcellus在國家數位政策與私部門累積的豐富歷練，將強化機構的推進力道。

  「馬來西亞在吸引數位投資、建立支撐AI雄心的基礎設施方面已取得顯著進展。下一個挑戰，是確保這些優勢能夠轉化為更高的經濟價值、更強大的馬來西亞企業，以及對人民（rakyat）真正有意義的機會，」Ganesh說道，其中「rakyat」為馬來語「公民」之意。

  ## 投資成果

  MDEC表示，2025年至2026年8月間，機構共促成近440億美元（約1,800億馬來西亞令吉）的數位投資，相關承諾預計將創造逾42,000個高附加價值就業機會。機構將這一投資基礎定位為下一個成長階段的根基。

  ## Marcellus的政策資歷

  加入MDEC之前，Marcellus曾擔任MyDigital Corporation執行長，以及馬來西亞第四次工業革命中心（MYCentre4IR）主任。任職期間，他在制定《馬來西亞數位行動計畫2030》（MD2030）的過程中扮演核心角色，該計畫涵蓋《國家第四次工業革命政策》。

  他亦參與創建政府核心單位（Government Nucleus Unit），該單位現以GovTech之名在數位部旗下運作，以及國家AI辦公室，該辦公室目前已整合為「AI Malaysia」。他擴大推動「人民數位計畫」（Rakyat Digital），這是一項旨在拓寬公民數位近用機會的政府倡議；他也透過「產業群聚轉型倡議」（Transitioning Industrial Clusters），與世界經濟論壇及產業夥伴合作，加速各工業部門的技術採用。

  ## 普及AI與強化本土能量

  Marcellus將加速微型、中小企業（MSMEs）採用AI與數位技術列為核心優先要務，同時致力於培育具備全球競爭力的馬來西亞科技企業。

  「AI經濟的成功，不能單以投資額或採用率來衡量，而要看它所創造的價值、生產力提升，以及所帶來的機會。我們必須透過讓技術更易取得、更經濟實惠、更切實可用，加速各產業——尤其是中小企業——的數位與AI採用，」Marcellus說。

  MDEC表示，將與政府機構及產業夥伴合作，透過擬議中的「國家AI算力交換平臺」（National AI Compute Exchange，NACX），改善各界取用AI運算資源、模型與解決方案的管道。NACX旨在降低規模較小的企業採用AI工具的門檻。

  ## 政策背景與目標

  此次任命與《第十三馬來西亞計畫》及MD2030相呼應。上述計畫設定了國家目標：數位經濟對國內生產毛額（GDP）的貢獻比重，須在2030年前達到30%。GDP是衡量一國整體經濟產出的標準指標。已運作逾三十年的MDEC表示，將持續開拓新成長領域、吸引高價值策略性投資，並強化馬來西亞科技企業的全球競爭力。

  MDEC公布的下一步行動包括：推進NACX提案、深化各產業的AI與數位採用（尤其聚焦中小企業）、培育本土人才，以及進一步吸引策略性投資，以支持「2030 AI國家」目標的實現。
