body_en: |
  Batam, Indonesia's free-trade island adjacent to Singapore, has attracted approximately Rp140 trillion in data center investment commitments from global investors, according to the Batam Free Trade Zone Authority (BP Batam), as rising demand for cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and regional digital services reorients the city away from its manufacturing base.

  Investors from Singapore, Hong Kong, China, and the United States are building large-capacity facilities in Batam's Nongsa and Kabil districts, drawn by the island's proximity to Singapore and Malaysia.

  ## Anchor projects

  The largest announced project is the Equator Gate System Batam (EGSB) AI Data Center, located at Teluk Mata Ikan in the Nongsa area, developed in partnership with Range IDC, a Chinese data center operator. BP Batam values the project at approximately US$5 billion, or roughly Rp88 trillion, and describes it as Range IDC's first international expansion outside China.

  At Kabil Industrial Tech Park (KITP), DayOne is developing a hyperscale data center campus — a large-scale facility designed to serve major cloud and enterprise customers — with a total electricity requirement of 511 MVA, or approximately 450 MW. NeutraDC, partnering with Nxera, is building a separate facility targeting up to 100 MW of capacity to serve cloud, AI, and regional digital service needs.

  Oracle has been linked to cloud service development in Batam, though the company remains in an exploratory phase with no final investment decision announced, according to BP Batam.

  ## Energy supply as the binding constraint

  The combined power requirements of the data center cluster have made electricity supply the central variable for Batam's transformation. The Salim Group, an Indonesian conglomerate, is reportedly studying the construction of a power plant with approximately 1,050 MW of generating capacity to support data center, AI, and modern industrial zone demand, according to BP Batam. If built, that project would represent the primary enabling infrastructure for the full realization of the announced investment pipeline.

  ## 2025 investment results

  Batam's free-trade zone — formally the Batam Free Trade and Free Port Zone (KPBPB) — recorded Rp69.30 trillion in realized investment during 2025, surpassing its Rp60 trillion annual target. BP Batam attributed that result to growing investor interest in digital and energy sectors. The zone's economic growth rate reached 6.76% for the year.

  By sector, services — a category that encompasses digital activities — contributed the largest share of realized investment at Rp9.99 trillion. Machinery, electronics, and electrical equipment attracted Rp6.08 trillion, while electricity, gas, and water recorded Rp5.80 trillion. Investment specifically attributed to the digital sector totaled Rp8.557 trillion across 2025.

  "This achievement is not just a number. It reflects investor confidence in the ecosystem we are building in Batam," BP Batam head Amsakar Achmad said.

  ## Nongsa Digital Park

  Much of the digital investment activity is concentrated in Nongsa Digital Park (NDP), which holds Special Economic Zone (KEK) status granting tax and regulatory incentives to tenants. NDP recorded 836 direct employees in the fourth quarter of 2025, with BP Batam noting additional activity through supply chains and support services.

  "Nongsa Digital Park is a positive early indicator because it shows investment interest, cross-country partnerships, and growing activities in data centers, technology development, and the digital economy," Amsakar said.

  Singapore ranks as one of the largest source countries for Batam's inbound investment, according to BP Batam. Hong Kong, the United States, and Malaysia also contribute, particularly in the digital sector.

  ## What comes next

  If the announced projects reach completion, Batam would shift from a manufacturing-oriented free-trade zone into a regional center for high-technology industry and digital infrastructure in western Indonesia. Amsakar identified geography and connectivity as the primary competitive factors. "What is stronger is the combination of strategic factors: geographical proximity to Singapore, fast connectivity, and the opportunity to build cross-border operating models to support regional needs," he said.

  The Salim Group's reported study of a 1,050 MW power plant represents the most consequential near-term decision for the cluster: a construction commitment there would give the announced data center projects the power base they require to proceed at full scale.
body_zh: |
  巴淡自由貿易區管理局（BP Batam）指出，毗鄰新加坡的印尼自由貿易島巴淡島，已吸引來自全球的業者作出約140兆印尼盾的資料中心投資承諾。隨著雲端運算、人工智慧及區域數位服務的需求持續攀升，巴淡島正逐漸從以製造業為根基的城市，蛻變為數位基礎建設重鎮。

  來自新加坡、香港、中國及美國的投資者，正在巴淡島的Nongsa與Kabil兩大區域興建大型資料中心。這座島嶼緊鄰新加坡與馬來西亞的地緣優勢，正是吸引各方資金匯聚的核心因素。

  ## 指標性投資項目

  目前已宣布的最大規模項目，是位於Nongsa地區Teluk Mata Ikan的「赤道門戶系統巴淡」（Equator Gate System Batam，EGSB）AI資料中心，由中國資料中心業者Range IDC合作開發。BP Batam估計該項目總投資額約50億美元，折合約88兆印尼盾，並將其定位為Range IDC在中國以外首次進行的國際擴張。

  在卡比爾工業科技園區（Kabil Industrial Tech Park，KITP），DayOne正在開發一座超大規模資料中心園區——此類大型設施專為服務主要雲端業者及企業客戶而設計——整體用電需求達511 MVA（約450 MW）。NeutraDC則與Nxera攜手，另建一座目標容量達100 MW的獨立設施，服務雲端、AI及區域數位服務需求。

  甲骨文（Oracle）據悉有意在巴淡島拓展雲端服務業務，但根據BP Batam表示，目前仍處於探索評估階段，尚未作出最終投資決定。

  ## 電力供應：發展的關鍵制約

  資料中心聚落的整體用電需求，已使電力供應成為巴淡島轉型的核心變數。根據BP Batam透露，印尼大型企業集團薩林集團（Salim Group）據報正在評估興建一座總裝置容量約1,050 MW的發電廠，以因應資料中心、AI及現代產業園區的電力需求。若該項目付諸實施，將成為讓已宣布投資計畫得以全面落地的首要基礎建設。

  ## 2025年投資成果

  巴淡自由貿易港區——正式名稱為「巴淡島自由貿易暨自由港區」（KPBPB）——在2025年全年實現投資額達69.3兆印尼盾，超越原訂的60兆印尼盾年度目標。BP Batam將此佳績歸功於投資者對數位及能源領域日益濃厚的興趣，而該自由貿易區全年經濟成長率亦達6.76%。

  以各行業別而言，涵蓋數位活動的服務業，以9.99兆印尼盾貢獻了最高的實現投資比重；機械、電子及電氣設備業吸引了6.08兆印尼盾；電力、燃氣及供水業則達5.8兆印尼盾。2025年全年，專屬於數位領域的投資金額合計為8.557兆印尼盾。

  「這項成就不只是數字，它反映的是投資者對我們在巴淡島所打造生態系統的信心。」BP Batam局長安薩卡·阿哈馬德（Amsakar Achmad）說道。

  ## Nongsa數位園區

  大部分數位投資活動集中於Nongsa數位園區（NDP）。該園區擁有特別經濟區（KEK）地位，可為進駐業者提供租稅及法規優惠。NDP在2025年第四季共有836名直接僱員，BP Batam並指出，透過供應鏈及支援服務所帶動的關聯活動亦持續活絡。

  「Nongsa數位園區是一個正面的早期指標，清楚顯示出投資興趣、跨國合作，以及資料中心、科技開發與數位經濟領域日益蓬勃的活動態勢。」安薩卡說道。

  根據BP Batam統計，新加坡是巴淡島境外直接投資的最大來源國之一；香港、美國及馬來西亞亦有所貢獻，尤以數位領域為甚。

  ## 後續發展展望

  若已宣布的各項投資計畫均能順利完工，巴淡島將從製造業導向的自由貿易區，轉型為印尼西部的區域高科技產業與數位基礎建設中心。安薩卡點出地理位置與連結能力是首要競爭優勢：「更有力的，是多項戰略因素的結合：緊鄰新加坡的地緣優勢、快速的網路連結，以及建立跨境營運模式、支援區域需求的機遇。」

  薩林集團正在評估中的1,050 MW發電廠計畫，是這個資料中心聚落近期最具關鍵性的決策：一旦確認動工，便能為已宣布的各資料中心項目提供以全規模推進所需的電力基礎。
