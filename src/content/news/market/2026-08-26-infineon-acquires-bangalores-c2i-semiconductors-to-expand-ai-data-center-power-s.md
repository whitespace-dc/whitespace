body_en: |
  Infineon Technologies on Monday announced the acquisition of C2i Semiconductors, a company headquartered in Bengaluru, India, that develops software-defined multi-phase controllers and intelligent power stages for artificial-intelligence data center applications. The transaction is expected to close in the third quarter of 2026, Infineon said.

  ## What C2i Semiconductors does

  C2i Semiconductors builds what the company calls software-defined power solutions: systems that combine advanced power semiconductors, intelligent digital control circuitry, and software algorithms to optimize power conversion, voltage regulation, and system performance in real time. The company's technology approaches power delivery from a system level, covering the full path from the electricity grid to the processor core, Infineon said, with the goal of building a smarter and more adaptive power architecture for AI infrastructure.

  The engineering problem the technology addresses is specific to AI data centers. Processors built for AI workloads generate highly dynamic and rapidly changing power demands, requiring supply systems that respond faster and more precisely to sudden load fluctuations — known as transient events — while maintaining high efficiency and system stability. Software-defined power management handles those requirements through algorithmic control layered on top of the underlying hardware, rather than through purely analog circuitry.

  ## How the acquisition extends Infineon's portfolio

  Infineon said the acquisition complements its existing power semiconductor portfolio, which spans silicon (Si), silicon carbide (SiC), and gallium nitride (GaN) technologies. SiC and GaN are wide-bandgap semiconductor materials that switch at higher frequencies and with lower energy losses than conventional silicon, making them well-suited to the high-density power conversion demands of modern AI servers and high-performance computing (HPC) platforms.

  By combining C2i's digital power expertise with that hardware base — along with Infineon's global scale, application know-how, and vertical power delivery solutions — Infineon said it expects to offer an integrated stack covering the complete power delivery chain from grid to processor core for AI server customers. The combined offering is also being positioned for compatibility with substrate-integrated voltage regulators (SIVRs), a next-generation packaging approach that places the voltage regulator in very close proximity to the processor to reduce power delivery losses at high current densities.

  ## India engineering footprint

  Infineon said the deal expands its engineering capabilities in India and reinforces the country's role as a strategic innovation hub within its global research-and-development network. C2i Semiconductors is based in Bengaluru, which is India's principal center for semiconductor design and software engineering talent. Infineon said the transaction adds highly specialized software-defined power management expertise to its global R&D network.

  ## Industry significance

  The acquisition positions Infineon to offer a more complete solution across the AI data center power delivery chain at a time when AI processor generations are placing rising demands on power density and transient response speed. By adding software-defined control technology to its power semiconductor hardware portfolio, Infineon moves closer to a full-stack offering for AI server and HPC platform makers rather than supplying discrete components alone.

  Infineon said the two companies will work together to accelerate innovation in AI power systems to support the performance and efficiency requirements of next-generation AI infrastructure. The transaction is expected to close in Q3 2026.

  ## C2i Semiconductors的業務

  C2i Semiconductors開發該公司所稱的「軟體定義電源解決方案」：系統整合了先進電源半導體、智慧型數位控制電路，以及軟體演算法，能夠即時優化電力轉換、電壓調節與系統效能。英飛凌表示，C2i的技術從系統層面切入電源供應，覆蓋從電網到處理器核心的完整路徑，目標是為AI基礎設施打造更智慧、更具自適應能力的電源架構。

  這項技術所要解決的工程難題，是AI資料中心特有的挑戰。專為AI工作負載設計的處理器，會產生高度動態、急速變化的電源需求，要求供電系統能夠更快速、更精準地因應突發的負載波動——即所謂的「暫態事件」（transient events）——同時維持高效率與系統穩定性。軟體定義電源管理透過在底層硬體之上疊加演算法控制來滿足上述需求，而非仰賴純類比電路。

  ## 此收購如何擴展英飛凌的產品組合

  英飛凌表示，此次收購與其現有電源半導體產品組合相輔相成，涵蓋矽（Si）、碳化矽（SiC）及氮化鎵（GaN）技術。碳化矽與氮化鎵均為寬能隙半導體材料，切換頻率高、能量損耗低於傳統矽材料，非常適合現代AI伺服器與高效能運算（HPC）平台對高密度電力轉換的需求。

  英飛凌表示，藉由將C2i的數位電源專業技術與上述硬體基礎相結合——再加上英飛凌的全球規模、應用知識與垂直電源供應解決方案——英飛凌預計能為AI伺服器客戶提供整合性的完整電源供應鏈，從電網到處理器核心一手包辦。此整合方案同時定位為與基板整合電壓調節器（SIVR）相容，這是一種新世代封裝方式，將電壓調節器置於緊鄰處理器的位置，以降低高電流密度下的電源傳輸損耗。

  ## 印度工程布局

  英飛凌表示，此交易擴大了公司在印度的工程實力，並強化印度在其全球研發網絡中作為策略性創新樞紐的角色。C2i Semiconductors總部位於班加羅爾，這座城市是印度半導體設計與軟體工程人才的主要重鎮。英飛凌表示，此次交易為其全球研發網絡引進了高度專業化的軟體定義電源管理技術。

  ## 產業意義

  此次收購讓英飛凌得以在AI資料中心電源供應鏈中提供更完整的解決方案，恰逢AI處理器世代不斷對電源密度與暫態響應速度提出更高要求之際。透過將軟體定義控制技術納入其電源半導體硬體產品組合，英飛凌向AI伺服器及HPC平台製造商提供全堆疊解決方案的目標又邁進一步，而不再只是單純供應離散元件。

  英飛凌表示，兩家公司將攜手合作，加速AI電源系統的創新，以支援下一代AI基礎設施的效能與效率需求。此交易預計於2026年第三季完成。
