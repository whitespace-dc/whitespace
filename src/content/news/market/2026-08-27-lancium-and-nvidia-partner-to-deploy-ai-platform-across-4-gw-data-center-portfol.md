body_en: |
  Lancium, the Blackstone-backed energy infrastructure company, has announced a strategic partnership with NVIDIA to deploy the chipmaker's full-stack AI factory platform across its entire data center portfolio, which spans 4 gigawatts (GW) of leased capacity and a further 15 GW in the development pipeline.

  ## Scope of the deployment

  The agreement covers NVIDIA's complete technology stack — accelerated computing hardware, networking, and software — giving NVIDIA's customers and partners access to large-scale, power-ready capacity for AI workloads, Lancium said in a press release. The company operates what it calls AI factory campuses, purpose-built facilities designed for high-density AI computing.

  Central to the partnership is NVIDIA's DSX MaxLPS design and operating framework, which Lancium said it will adopt across its facilities. DSX MaxLPS allows a data center operator to fit up to 40 percent more graphics processing units (GPUs) within the same electrical power envelope, a significant density gain at the scale Lancium is targeting. Lancium will also deploy NVIDIA's DSX Flex, a tool that adjusts power consumption at an AI factory in real time, enabling what the company describes as grid-responsive campuses capable of modulating their draw on the electricity grid.

  ## Executive statements

  "Partnering with NVIDIA — the definitive technology platform for AI computing — ensures that every campus in our portfolio will be deployed with the industry's most advanced technology and that NVIDIA's customers will have access to the capacity they need to compete and lead in the AI era," said Michael McNamara, Lancium's chief executive officer and co-founder.

  Nico Caprez, NVIDIA's vice president of global AI infrastructure growth, described the arrangement in terms of industrial-scale infrastructure. "AI factories are the essential infrastructure of this new industrial era," Caprez said. "Together with Lancium, we're enabling a new generation of gigawatt-scale, power-ready AI factories — enabling cloud partners, AI natives and AI infrastructure developers deploy the full NVIDIA computing stack faster, operate more efficiently and turn energy into intelligence at unprecedented scale."

  Bilal Khan, a senior managing director at Blackstone, said the partnership reflected Lancium's position at the intersection of energy and AI infrastructure, which he described as two of Blackstone's "highest conviction investment themes."

  ## Blackstone backing and NVIDIA's broader capital push

  Lancium is owned by funds managed by Blackstone Energy Transition Partners and Blackstone Multi-Asset Investing. The deal follows a separate NVIDIA-led initiative, reported earlier this month, in which NVIDIA announced partnerships with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR to mobilize more than $500 billion for AI infrastructure construction — what NVIDIA described as the beginning of an open capital market for AI infrastructure.

  ## What the deal means for the industry

  The partnership gives NVIDIA a committed large-scale deployment vehicle at a moment when demand for AI computing capacity is pressing against the availability of power-ready sites. For Lancium, standardizing on a single vendor's full stack — hardware, networking, and software — reduces integration complexity and may accelerate the pace at which new campuses can become operational.

  Lancium is currently developing a 1 GW AI data center campus in Childress, Texas, in partnership with Crusoe, an AI factory company. That facility is being built to serve the computing requirements of an unnamed hyperscaler.

  ## Lancium與輝達攜手，在4GW資料中心組合部署AI工廠平台

  Blackstone旗下的能源基礎設施公司Lancium宣布與輝達（NVIDIA）達成策略合作，將在旗下整個資料中心組合中全面部署輝達的完整AI工廠平台。Lancium目前擁有4GW的租賃容量，另有15GW正在開發管線中。

  ## 部署範疇

  根據Lancium發布的新聞稿，此合作涵蓋輝達完整的技術堆疊，包括加速運算硬體、網路設備及軟體，讓輝達的客戶與合作夥伴得以取用大規模、備妥電力的AI運算容量。Lancium將旗下設施定位為「AI工廠園區」，係專為高密度AI運算所打造的目的性設施。

  此次合作的核心，是輝達的DSX MaxLPS設計與營運框架。Lancium表示，將在旗下所有設施中採用這套框架。DSX MaxLPS可讓資料中心業者在相同用電量的前提下，多部署最多四成的繪圖處理器（GPU），在Lancium所瞄準的規模下，這是相當可觀的密度提升。此外，Lancium也將部署輝達的DSX Flex工具，該工具能即時調整AI工廠的用電量，使各園區得以實現所謂的「電網響應式」運作模式，依需求動態調節從電網取用的電力。

  ## 高層聲明

  Lancium執行長暨共同創辦人麥可．麥克納馬拉（Michael McNamara）表示：「與輝達——AI運算領域無可置疑的技術平台——建立合作關係，確保我們組合中的每座園區都將採用業界最先進的技術，也確保輝達的客戶能夠取得所需的容量，在AI時代保持競爭力並引領市場。」

  輝達全球AI基礎設施成長副總裁尼可．卡普雷茲（Nico Caprez）則以工業規模的基礎設施來描述這項合作：「AI工廠是這個新工業時代不可或缺的基礎設施。我們與Lancium攜手，正在催生新一代GW級、備妥電力的AI工廠，幫助雲端合作夥伴、AI原生企業及AI基礎設施開發商更快部署完整的輝達運算堆疊、更有效率地運營，並以前所未有的規模將能源轉化為智能。」

  Blackstone資深董事總經理畢拉爾．汗（Bilal Khan）則表示，這項合作夥伴關係彰顯了Lancium在能源與AI基礎設施交匯點上的定位，而他將這兩者描述為Blackstone「最具高度信念的兩大投資主題」。

  ## Blackstone的資金支持與輝達更宏觀的資本布局

  Lancium由Blackstone能源轉型合夥人基金及Blackstone多元資產投資基金所管理的資金持有。此次合作緊接在輝達本月稍早宣布的另一項重大舉措之後——輝達宣布與阿波羅（Apollo）、貝萊德（BlackRock）、Blackstone、布魯克菲爾德（Brookfield）、高盛（Goldman Sachs）及KKR共同合作，動員逾5,000億美元資金用於AI基礎設施建設，輝達將此形容為AI基礎設施開放資本市場的濫觴。

  ## 這項合作對產業意味著什麼

  對輝達而言，這項合作在AI運算容量需求持續攀升、備妥電力的場址日益稀缺的當下，提供了一個承諾大規模部署的具體載體。對Lancium而言，統一採用單一廠商的完整堆疊——涵蓋硬體、網路及軟體——可降低整合複雜度，有望加快新園區投入運營的速度。

  Lancium目前正與AI工廠公司Crusoe合作，在德州奇爾德雷斯（Childress）開發一座1GW的AI資料中心園區，該設施將用於服務一家未具名超大規模雲端業者的運算需求。
