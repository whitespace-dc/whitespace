body_en: |
  Singapore has awarded 200 megawatts of new data centre capacity to four operators — Digital Realty, Equinix, Keppel Data Centres, and ST Telemedia Global Data Centres — splitting the allocation evenly at 50MW each, the country's Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) said on Friday.

  The move is part of Singapore's effort to bolster its standing as a regional hub for artificial intelligence infrastructure.

  ## Sustainability commitments

  All four recipients have committed to exceed IMDA's minimum sustainability requirements, the authority said. As a condition of the allocation, each firm will source more than half of its data centre capacity from green energy.

  ## Scale and power demand

  Singapore currently hosts more than 1.4 gigawatts (GW) of total data centre capacity — enough to power a large city's worth of computing workloads. That infrastructure already accounts for approximately 7% of the city-state's total electricity consumption, a figure IMDA projects will rise to 12% by 2030.

  A megawatt of data centre capacity is a standard industry measure of power draw; a 50MW facility is a mid-to-large campus by regional standards, capable of supporting significant cloud or AI computing loads.

  ## Regional competition

  The award comes as Singapore faces intensifying competition from neighbouring Malaysia, which has displaced it as the region's fastest-growing data centre hub, according to the source report. The 200MW allocation signals a deliberate effort by Singapore's government to recapture momentum in the sector.

  With data centres projected to consume 12% of national power by 2030, the expansion also underscores the supply-side pressure Singapore must manage as AI-driven demand for computing infrastructure accelerates across Southeast Asia.

  新加坡資訊通信媒體發展局（IMDA）週五宣布，已將200百萬瓦（MW）的新資料中心容量授予四家業者——Digital Realty、Equinix、Keppel資料中心及ST Telemedia全球資料中心，四家業者各獲分配50MW，配額均等。

  此舉是新加坡致力強化自身作為區域人工智慧基礎設施樞紐地位的重要一環。

  ## 永續承諾

  IMDA表示，四家獲配業者均承諾超越該局所訂定的最低永續性要求。作為取得配額的條件之一，各業者須確保其資料中心容量有逾半數來自綠色能源。

  ## 規模與用電需求

  新加坡目前的資料中心總容量已超過1.4吉瓦（GW），足以支撐相當於一座大型城市的運算工作負載。這些基礎設施目前約佔新加坡全國總用電量的7%，IMDA預估此一比例將於2030年攀升至12%。

  在業界中，百萬瓦是衡量資料中心用電規模的標準單位；以區域標準而言，50MW的設施屬於中大型園區，足以承載相當規模的雲端運算或AI運算需求。

  ## 區域競爭態勢

  此次配額的宣布，正值新加坡面臨來自鄰國馬來西亞日趨激烈的競爭壓力。據報導，馬來西亞已取代新加坡，躍升為區域內成長最快速的資料中心重鎮。200MW的配額釋出，顯示新加坡政府正積極採取行動，意圖重奪產業發展的主導地位。

  隨著資料中心用電比例預計在2030年達到全國用電量的12%，此波擴張也凸顯出新加坡在供給面所承受的壓力——尤其是在AI帶動的運算基礎設施需求持續加速之際，這股浪潮已席捲整個東南亞地區。
