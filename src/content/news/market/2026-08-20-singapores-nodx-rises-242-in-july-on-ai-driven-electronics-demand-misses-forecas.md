body_en: |
  Singapore's non-oil domestic exports (NODX) — domestically produced goods shipped abroad, excluding oil — rose 24.2% in July, extending June's 20.8% increase and marking another month of AI-driven electronics gains, according to figures released by Enterprise Singapore on Aug. 17. The result missed the 26.5% median forecast in a Bloomberg economist poll.

  Electronics NODX grew 112% in July, building on 105.1% growth in June, as global demand for AI infrastructure hardware continued to pull Singapore's shipments higher. Non-electronics NODX fell 2.3%, declining for the second consecutive month.

  ## Electronics breakdown

  Within electronics, disk media products posted the largest gain at 339.1%, while personal computers rose 120.8%. Integrated circuits — chips used in AI servers, memory systems, and related hardware — grew 84.5%, according to Enterprise Singapore's figures.

  The United States was the top destination among Singapore's ten largest NODX markets, with electronics shipments there rising 378.8%. South Korea and Taiwan, both central to global semiconductor manufacturing, grew 112.1% and 122.1% respectively, though both moderated from June levels. Maybank economists Chua Hak Bin and Brian Lee noted that US-bound shipments were driven by disk media products, PCs, and telecommunications equipment.

  Taiwan's growth rate fell sharply — from above 120% in June to just above 30% in July. Zavier Wong, a market analyst at trading platform eToro, flagged the divergence between Taiwan and the US as worth monitoring heading into August, noting that Singapore's shipments are booked unevenly from month to month. Wong added that as long as capital expenditure guidance from major hyperscalers — large cloud computing companies such as those operating at US scale — continues to climb, electronics NODX is unlikely to reverse on its own.

  DBS senior economist Chua Han Teng said the electronics sector will continue to support NODX growth, underpinned by substantial AI infrastructure investment signalled by major US hyperscalers, which drives external demand for Singapore's electronics and precision engineering exports.

  Electronics gains also broadened geographically. UOB's global economics and markets research team said momentum increased in Thailand, India, and Indonesia, pointing to rising AI adoption by businesses and growing end-consumer demand in those markets.

  ## Non-electronics contraction

  Pharmaceuticals led the non-electronics decline, falling 56.7%. Petrochemicals dropped 22.5% and food preparations fell 17.9%.

  DBS's Chua said supply chain disruptions are likely to continue weighing on non-electronics exports. Petrochemical exports in particular are expected to remain weak due to feedstock constraints — feedstocks are the raw materials used to manufacture products such as biofuels and plastics — stemming from ongoing disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz shipping corridor.

  Chua also said non-electronics NODX to the United States will likely remain subdued, dragged by a 12.5% levy arising from a probe by the US Office of the United States Trade Representative into forced labour concerns. Enterprise Singapore's data suggests this levy affects roughly one-third of Singapore's domestic exports.

  ## Full-year outlook revised sharply higher

  The July figures follow Enterprise Singapore's decision to upgrade its full-year 2026 NODX forecast to 14% to 16% growth, up from an earlier projection of 3% to 5%, reflecting what the agency described as an exceptionally strong first-half performance led by electronics.

  Analysts quoted by The Straits Times were broadly positive on the second-half NODX outlook but cautioned that performance will likely remain uneven, with electronics continuing to outpace non-electronics exports.

  ## 新加坡7月非石油國內出口年增24.2%，AI帶動電子產品需求強勁，惟仍不及市場預期

  新加坡貿易機構新加坡企業發展局（Enterprise Singapore）於8月17日公布最新數據，7月非石油國內出口（NODX）——即本地生產、排除石油後對外出口的商品——年增24.2%，延續6月20.8%的增幅，再度受惠於AI驅動的電子產品出口熱潮。惟此數字未達彭博經濟學家調查的26.5%中位數預測。

  7月電子產品NODX年增112%，延續6月105.1%的強勁成長，主要來自全球對AI基礎建設硬體的持續需求，帶動新加坡出口持續攀升。非電子產品NODX則年減2.3%，連續第二個月出現萎縮。

  ## 電子產品出口細項

  電子產品出口細項中，磁碟媒體產品漲幅最大，年增339.1%；個人電腦年增120.8%；用於AI伺服器、記憶體系統及相關硬體的積體電路（晶片）則年增84.5%，均依據新加坡企業發展局公布數據。

  在新加坡前十大NODX目的地中，美國高居首位，對美電子產品出口年增378.8%。韓國與台灣同為全球半導體製造重鎮，分別年增112.1%及122.1%，惟成長速度較6月有所放緩。馬來亞銀行（Maybank）經濟學家蔡學敏（Chua Hak Bin）及李明輝（Brian Lee）指出，對美出口增長主要由磁碟媒體產品、個人電腦及電信設備所驅動。

  台灣的出口成長率出現明顯回落，從6月逾120%驟降至7月僅略高於30%。交易平台eToro市場分析師黃澤維（Zavier Wong）指出，台灣與美國之間的出口成長分歧值得在進入8月後持續關注，並提醒新加坡出口數據本就存在月度波動、難以逐月均勻比較的特性。黃澤維補充，只要主要大型雲端業者（hyperscaler，即如美國規模的大型雲端運算公司）的資本支出指引持續上調，電子產品NODX便不太可能自行反轉。

  星展銀行（DBS）高級經濟學家蔡漢騰（Chua Han Teng）表示，電子產品類別將持續支撐NODX成長，背後支柱是美國主要大型雲端業者所釋出的龐大AI基礎建設投資訊號，帶動外部市場對新加坡電子產品及精密工程出口的需求。

  電子產品出口的成長動能也逐漸擴及更多地區。大華銀行（UOB）全球經濟與市場研究團隊指出，泰國、印度及印尼的出口動能明顯增強，反映當地企業AI採用率提升以及終端消費者需求持續擴大。

  ## 非電子產品出口萎縮

  非電子產品出口方面，製藥品跌幅最深，年減56.7%；石化產品年減22.5%；食品製劑年減17.9%。

  星展銀行蔡漢騰指出，供應鏈干擾預計將持續拖累非電子產品出口表現。石化出口尤其可能持續低迷，原因在於原料——即用於製造生質燃料、塑膠等產品的原材料——供應受到荷莫茲海峽（Strait of Hormuz）航運通道持續干擾，形成供給瓶頸。

  蔡漢騰亦指出，新加坡對美非電子產品NODX可能持續疲軟，主因美國貿易代表署（USTR）針對強迫勞動議題展開調查後，對相關商品加徵12.5%的稅率。新加坡企業發展局數據顯示，此稅率影響範圍約達新加坡國內出口的三分之一。

  ## 全年展望大幅上調

  7月數據公布前，新加坡企業發展局已將2026年全年NODX成長預測上調至14%至16%，遠高於此前預估的3%至5%，理由是以電子產品為首的上半年表現異常亮眼。

  多位接受《海峽時報》（The Straits Times）訪問的分析師對下半年NODX前景整體持正面看法，惟警告表現可能仍將參差不齊，電子產品出口將持續領先非電子產品出口。
