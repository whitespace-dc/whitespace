body_en: |
  Taiwan's stock exchange is positioning its Taiwan Innovation Board — known in Chinese as tib-創 (tib-chuang), where 創 means innovation — as the primary capital market pathway for domestic AI infrastructure suppliers in thermal management, power management, and advanced packaging, citing a market-capitalization-first listing framework that allows pre-profit companies to reach international investors.

  The Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE) outlined the initiative on July 14, 2026, framing tib-創 as the institutional answer to a structural mismatch it identified in Taiwan's AI supply chain: companies that are technically advanced and already embedded in global AI chip designers' supply chains, but that have not yet reached the profitability thresholds required for listing on Taiwan's main boards.

  ## Policy Designation and Government Backing

  The Executive Yuan's "AI New Ten Constructions" program — specifically its tenth initiative, which allocates NT$100 billion (approximately US$3.1 billion at current rates) to drive innovation — formally designates tib-創 as what the government calls an "Asian Innovation Fundraising Platform." The exchange said this marks a transition in Taiwan's capital markets from an "institution-building phase" into an "ecosystem acceleration phase."

  President Lai Ching-te's administration has framed tib-創's development under the explicit policy goal of positioning Taiwan as an "Asian Nasdaq," the exchange said.

  TWSE Chairman Lin Hsiu-ming said Taiwan's innovative strength has historically shaped global markets through hardware supply chains, but that the next decade's competitiveness will depend on software, AI, and digital services. He described tib-創's mission as serving as "the capital market engine" of that transition, allowing high-growth technology companies to reach global investors before achieving profitability.

  ## Supply Chain Position and the Listing Gap

  The exchange said Taiwanese suppliers in three core categories already hold key positions in global AI chip designers' supply chains: thermal management (liquid cooling systems, phase-change materials, and vapor chambers used to control heat in high-density AI servers); power supply (high-efficiency power modules for data centers); and advanced packaging, specifically CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate, a process that stacks chips and memory in a single package) and HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) substrates and related testing services.

  The exchange argued that these companies' competitive advantages reside primarily in intangible assets — patent portfolios, thermal management algorithms, manufacturing process knowledge, and long-term customer supply contracts — rather than in the fixed-asset scale that appears on traditional balance sheets. That characteristic, it said, makes them poorly served by conventional listing criteria that weight profitability and tangible assets.

  ## Listing Criteria

  tib-創 evaluates listing candidates on three criteria, according to the exchange:

  - **Technology moat:** differentiation established through intellectual property, algorithm patents, or process certifications
  - **Market expansion:** customer retention rates, overseas revenue share, and total addressable market (TAM) size
  - **Capital use clarity:** verifiable plans for how raised funds will accelerate R&D, production capacity, or market positioning

  The market-capitalization-centric design is intended to allow companies to communicate value through technology potential and market scalability before profitability matures, the exchange said.

  ## Seven Priority Sectors

  The exchange identified seven industry categories as priority listing candidates under the AI infrastructure theme:

  1. Thermal management and heat dissipation systems: liquid cooling, phase-change materials, and vapor chambers supporting data center energy efficiency
  2. Power management and high-efficiency power modules: server power supplies, uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), and power distribution systems providing the energy base for AI compute expansion
  3. Advanced packaging and AI chip substrates: CoWoS, HBM substrates, and advanced testing; the exchange described this as Taiwan's "core position" in the global AI compute supply chain
  4. Intelligent computing infrastructure: AI inference accelerators, edge computing modules, and rack system integration
  5. Electric vehicle key components: motor drive systems, electronic control modules, and automotive power management, where the exchange said Taiwanese suppliers have already established international competitiveness
  6. AI cybersecurity and zero-trust architecture: subscription-based AI security companies with annual recurring revenue (ARR, the portion of revenue under repeating contracts) of 90% or more
  7. Digital content and AI creative technology: cultural intellectual property digitization, extended reality (XR) content production, and AI-assisted creation

  ## Market Forecasts

  International market research institutions, cited by the exchange without identification, project that global AI infrastructure spending — encompassing data centers, power management, thermal management, and advanced packaging — will enter a high-growth phase from 2026 to 2028. Asia-Pacific capital expenditure compound annual growth rates (CAGR, the year-over-year growth rate over a multi-year period) are projected to exceed the global average during that window, the exchange said, without specifying the underlying figures.

  ## Second-Half 2026 Plans

  The exchange said it plans to deepen engagement with semiconductor, AI infrastructure, electric vehicle, and digital content industries in the second half of 2026, anchoring activity around SEMICON Taiwan 2026 in September and the "2026 Taiwan Week x Asian Innovation Cup Competition." It said it is expanding dialogue channels with venture capital institutions in Southeast Asia, Japan, Europe, and the United States.

  ## Industry Significance

  The initiative reflects a coordinated effort by Taiwan's financial regulatory infrastructure and executive branch to prevent technically advanced but pre-profit domestic suppliers from being delayed in accessing long-term institutional capital at a point when global AI infrastructure investment is accelerating. By formally embedding tib-創 in national AI policy and opening investor eligibility, Taiwanese authorities are attempting to replicate, for the hardware supply chain, the early-stage capital access model that technology-focused exchanges in other markets have used to attract growth companies before profitability.

  The exchange said that for AI infrastructure companies currently scaling operations and evaluating capital market strategy, the second half of 2026 represents a critical alignment window: policy support is in place, investor eligibility restrictions have been fully opened, and the overlap between the industry narrative and international capital interest is at a recent peak, according to the exchange.

  ## 台灣證交所推介創新板　為AI基礎建設供應商打通上市路

  台灣證券交易所正積極將「台灣創新板」——中文簡稱tib-創（「創」意為創新）——定位為國內散熱管理、電源管理與先進封裝等AI基礎建設供應商進入資本市場的主要管道。其核心訴求，在於以市值為主的上市框架，讓尚未獲利的企業得以接觸國際投資人。

  台灣證券交易所（TWSE）於2026年7月14日對外說明這項計畫，將tib-創定位為解決台灣AI供應鏈結構性落差的制度性方案——這些企業技術上已具備相當競爭力，且早已深嵌於全球AI晶片設計大廠的供應鏈中，卻仍未達到在台灣主板上市所需的獲利門檻。

  ## 政策定位與政府支持

  行政院「AI新十大建設」計畫——具體而言，其第十項建設專案撥款新台幣1,000億元（依當前匯率約合美金31億元）以驅動創新——正式將tib-創定名為政府所稱的「亞洲創新籌資平台」。交易所表示，此舉標誌著台灣資本市場從「制度建構階段」正式邁入「生態系加速階段」。

  交易所指出，賴清德總統領導的政府已將tib-創的發展，明確置於「將台灣打造為亞洲那斯達克」的政策目標之下。

  台灣證交所董事長林修銘表示，台灣的創新實力歷來透過硬體供應鏈形塑全球市場，但未來十年的競爭力將取決於軟體、AI與數位服務。他將tib-創的使命定位為這場轉型的「資本市場引擎」，讓高成長科技企業在尚未獲利之前，便能接觸全球投資人。

  ## 供應鏈位置與上市落差

  交易所指出，台灣供應商在三大核心類別中，已在全球AI晶片設計大廠的供應鏈中佔據關鍵地位：散熱管理（液冷系統、相變材料及均熱板，用於控制高密度AI伺服器的熱能）；電源供應（資料中心高效電源模組）；以及先進封裝，尤其是CoWoS（晶圓上晶片基板，一種將晶片與記憶體堆疊於單一封裝的製程）與HBM（高頻寬記憶體）基板及相關測試服務。

  交易所認為，這些企業的競爭優勢主要蘊藏於無形資產——專利組合、散熱管理演算法、製造製程知識，以及長期客戶供貨合約——而非傳統資產負債表上所呈現的固定資產規模。正因如此，它們並不適合以獲利能力與有形資產為重的傳統上市標準。

  ## 上市審查標準

  依據交易所說明，tib-創依三項標準評估上市候選企業：

  - **技術護城河：** 透過智慧財產權、演算法專利或製程認證所建立的差異化優勢
  - **市場擴張性：** 客戶留存率、海外營收佔比，以及可服務的總目標市場（TAM）規模
  - **資金用途明確性：** 可驗證的計畫，說明募集資金如何加速研發、產能或市場布局

  這套以市值為核心的設計，旨在讓企業在獲利能力成熟之前，便能透過技術潛力與市場規模向外傳遞其價值，交易所表示。

  ## 七大優先產業

  交易所在AI基礎建設主題下，識別出七大產業類別為優先上市對象：

  1. **散熱管理與熱能疏散系統：** 液冷、相變材料及均熱板，支援資料中心能源效率
  2. **電源管理與高效電源模組：** 伺服器電源供應器、不斷電系統（UPS）及電力分配系統，為AI運算擴張提供能源基礎
  3. **先進封裝與AI晶片基板：** CoWoS、HBM基板及先進測試；交易所將此定位為台灣在全球AI運算供應鏈中的「核心地位」
  4. **智慧運算基礎建設：** AI推論加速器、邊緣運算模組與機架系統整合
  5. **電動車關鍵零組件：** 馬達驅動系統、電子控制模組與車用電源管理，交易所表示台灣供應商在此領域已建立國際競爭力
  6. **AI資安與零信任架構：** 年度經常性收入（ARR，即重複合約所貢獻的營收比例）達90%以上的訂閱制AI資安企業
  7. **數位內容與AI創意科技：** 文化智慧財產數位化、延展實境（XR）內容製作及AI輔助創作

  ## 市場預測

  交易所援引國際市場研究機構（未具名）的預測，全球AI基礎建設支出——涵蓋資料中心、電源管理、散熱管理及先進封裝——將於2026年至2028年間進入高速成長階段。交易所表示，亞太地區資本支出的年複合成長率（CAGR，即多年期間的逐年成長率）預計將超越全球平均水準，但未公布具體數字。

  ## 2026年下半年計畫

  交易所表示，計畫於2026年下半年深化與半導體、AI基礎建設、電動車及數位內容產業的交流，核心活動將圍繞9月的「SEMICON Taiwan 2026」以及「2026 Taiwan Week x亞洲創新盃競賽」。交易所並指出，正持續擴大與東南亞、日本、歐洲及美國創投機構的對話管道。

  ## 產業意義

  這項計畫，反映台灣金融監理體系與行政院的協同努力——在全球AI基礎建設投資加速之際，防止技術先進卻尚未獲利的國內供應商，在取得長期機構資本的路上延誤過久。透過將tib-創正式納入國家AI政策、並全面開放投資人資格，台灣主管機關正試圖為硬體供應鏈複製一套早期資本接觸模式——這套模式，已被其他市場以科技為導向的交易所用於在企業獲利之前吸引成長型公司。

  交易所表示，對於目前正在擴大規模、評估資本市場策略的AI基礎建設企業而言，2026年下半年是一個關鍵的時間視窗：政策支持已到位，投資人資格限制已全面鬆綁，而產業敘事與國際資本關注的交集，也正處於近期的高峰，交易所如此指出。
