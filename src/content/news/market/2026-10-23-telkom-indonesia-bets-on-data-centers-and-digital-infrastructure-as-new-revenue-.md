  ## Telkom Indonesia bets on data centers and digital infrastructure as new revenue drivers

  PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk (TLKM), the country's dominant state-owned telecommunications group, is pushing digital infrastructure and data centers as the primary new engines of growth while defending its core network connectivity business against intensifying competition and domestic stock market volatility, the company's first-half 2026 results indicate.

  The company absorbed Rp 10.8 trillion (Indonesian rupiah) in capital expenditure — spending on physical assets and infrastructure — during the first six months of 2026, equal to approximately 14.2% of total revenue for the same period, according to Kontan.

  ## Capital deployment and strategic direction

  TLKM is pursuing what it describes as a selective approach: maintaining network connectivity as its foundational business while directing investment toward digital assets with higher growth profiles. That framing positions data centers and digital infrastructure as additive to, rather than replacements for, the legacy telecoms franchise.

  The company is executing this strategy against a backdrop of tight competition across the Indonesian telecoms and technology sector alongside volatility in domestic equity markets, Kontan reported.

  ## Significance

  For the Indonesian digital infrastructure market, the scale of TLKM's first-half capex — Rp 10.8 trillion across six months — signals that the country's largest telecoms operator is committing substantial capital to repositioning itself around data center and digital infrastructure revenues. How effectively that capital translates into earnings growth will determine whether the shift offsets margin pressure from the competitive core telecoms environment.

  ## 印尼電信押注資料中心與數位基礎設施，盼開闢新營收來源

  PT Telkom Indonesia Tbk（TLKM）是印尼最大的國營電信集團，根據該公司2026年上半年財報，正將數位基礎設施與資料中心定位為主要的新成長引擎，同時持續鞏固核心網路連線業務，以抵禦競爭日趨激烈的電信與科技市場，以及國內股市的持續動盪。

  據《Kontan》報導，該公司在2026年前六個月投入10.8兆印尼盾（Rp）的資本支出——即實體資產與基礎設施建設方面的支出——相當於同期總營收的約14.2%。

  ## 資本配置與策略方向

  TLKM採取其所稱的「精選式」策略：以網路連線為基礎核心業務，同時將投資導向具備更高成長潛力的數位資產。此一定位將資料中心與數位基礎設施視為傳統電信業務的加值延伸，而非取而代之。

  據《Kontan》報導，該公司在印尼電信與科技產業競爭白熱化、國內股市震盪起伏的大環境下，持續推進上述策略布局。

  ## 市場意義

  就印尼數位基礎設施市場整體而言，TLKM上半年高達10.8兆印尼盾的資本支出規模，清楚顯示這家全國最大電信業者正投入大量資金，積極朝資料中心與數位基礎設施營收方向轉型。這批資本能否有效轉化為獲利成長，將決定此一轉型策略能否抵銷核心電信業務在競爭壓力下所承受的利潤空間侵蝕。
