body_en: |
  PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (ticker: WIFI) and Wuhan FiberHome International Technologies Co. Ltd., a subsidiary of China's FiberHome Technologies Group, signed a strategic partnership agreement at Mobile World Congress 2026 in Shanghai to accelerate the construction of digital infrastructure across Indonesia.

  The agreement covers a broad scope of network development initiatives across fixed and wireless connectivity, optical transport, and emerging technologies.

  ## Scope of the agreement

  According to the companies, the partnership targets the following deliverables:

  - Up to 3,000 Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) sites — base stations that deliver broadband wirelessly to homes and businesses, eliminating the need for physical cable to every premise
  - 1.6 million FWA Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) units — the modems or routers installed at end-user locations
  - 300,000 full turnkey Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) homepass units — premises passed by fiber optic cable and ready for connection
  - A National Cross-Island Backbone and Metro Access Optical network, incorporating DWDM (Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing, a technology that carries multiple data channels over a single fiber), MPLS (a traffic-routing protocol), and OSP (outside plant, meaning the physical cable and conduit infrastructure)
  - PON (Passive Optical Network) equipment, including OLT (Optical Line Terminals, the network-side hardware) and ONT (Optical Network Terminals, the customer-side hardware)
  - Centralized solar photovoltaic power generation projects
  - A next-generation railway mobile communications system

  ## Parties and statements

  Shannedy Ong, a director at PT Solusi Sinergi Digital, said in a press statement dated June 25, 2026, that the partnership is intended to build connectivity that is reliable, affordable, and broadly accessible. "Every network that is built opens access to economic opportunities, education, public services, and innovation," Ong said.

  Jason Lin, Chief Executive Officer of Wuhan FiberHome Indonesia, said Indonesia has significant potential to become one of the region's largest digital economies. Lin said the collaboration with WIFI, which the company refers to by its brand name SURGE, aims to deliver technology supporting resilient and sustainable digital infrastructure.

  ## Context

  The partnership was described by both parties as an extension of an existing strategic relationship between FiberHome and SURGE that had previously been established through a memorandum of understanding. The June agreement marks a transition toward implementation-focused collaboration, the companies said.

  The signing took place on the sidelines of MWC 2026 in Shanghai, one of the telecommunications industry's major annual trade events.

  ## Significance

  The agreement positions WIFI to pursue simultaneous expansion across multiple infrastructure segments — fixed fiber, wireless broadband, and national backbone — through a single vendor relationship with a large Chinese equipment supplier. The stated targets, including 1.6 million CPE units and 300,000 FTTH homepasses, represent large-scale deployment commitments for the Indonesian market. The inclusion of solar power and railway communications indicates the partnership extends beyond conventional broadband into adjacent infrastructure sectors.

  ## 印尼WIFI於MWC上海與中國烽火通信簽署數位基礎建設合作協議

  PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk（股票代號：WIFI）與武漢烽火國際技術有限公司（中國烽火科技集團旗下子公司）日前於2026年上海世界行動通訊大會（MWC 2026）上正式簽署戰略合作協議，雙方將攜手加速印尼全國數位基礎建設的推進。

  此次協議涵蓋固定與無線連線、光傳輸及新興技術等多個面向的網路建設項目，合作範疇相當廣泛。

  ## 協議涵蓋範疇

  根據雙方公告，本次合作預計達成以下具體目標：

  - 最多3,000座固定無線接入（FWA）基站——以無線方式向住宅與商業用戶提供寬頻服務，無需將實體線纜鋪設至每一處用戶端
  - 160萬套FWA用戶端設備（CPE）——即安裝於終端用戶場所的數據機或路由器
  - 30萬戶全套光纖到府（FTTH）接入戶——指光纖線纜已鋪設至門口、隨時可供連線的用戶
  - 一套全國跨島骨幹網路暨都會接入光網路，整合DWDM（密集波長分割多工，一種在單根光纖上同時傳輸多個資料頻道的技術）、MPLS（流量路由通訊協定）及OSP（外部線纜與管道等實體基礎設施）
  - PON（被動光纖網路）設備，包含OLT（光線路終端，即網路端硬體）及ONT（光網路終端，即用戶端硬體）
  - 集中式太陽能光伏發電專案
  - 下一代鐵路行動通訊系統

  ## 雙方立場與聲明

  PT Solusi Sinergi Digital董事Shannedy Ong於2026年6月25日發表聲明表示，此次合作旨在建立可靠、實惠且廣泛普及的連線環境。「每一張建成的網路，都為民眾開啟通往經濟機會、教育資源、公共服務與創新的大門，」Ong說道。

  武漢烽火印尼執行長Jason Lin則表示，印尼具備成為區域最大數位經濟體之一的巨大潛力。Lin指出，與WIFI——即其品牌名稱SURGE——的合作，目標在於提供支撐韌性與永續數位基礎建設的技術能量。

  ## 背景

  雙方均表示，本次協議是烽火科技與SURGE既有戰略關係的延伸——此前雙方已透過備忘錄確立合作基礎。6月份簽署的這份協議，標誌著雙方合作正式從框架共識邁向具體落實階段。

  簽約儀式在2026年上海MWC大會期間場邊舉行。MWC是電信產業每年最重要的國際展會之一。

  ## 意義與展望

  此次協議使WIFI得以透過與單一大型中國設備供應商的合作，同步推進固定光纖、無線寬頻及全國骨幹網路等多個基礎建設領域的擴張布局。協議所載明的目標——包括160萬套CPE設備及30萬戶FTTH接入戶——代表印尼市場大規模的部署承諾。太陽能發電與鐵路通訊系統的納入，更顯示此次合作已超越傳統寬頻範疇，延伸至鄰近基礎設施領域。
