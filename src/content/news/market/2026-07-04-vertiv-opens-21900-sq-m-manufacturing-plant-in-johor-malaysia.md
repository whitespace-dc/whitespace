body_en: |
  Vertiv has opened a 21,900-square-metre manufacturing facility in Senai, Johor, Malaysia, targeting demand for artificial intelligence and high-density computing infrastructure across Southeast Asia, North Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.

  The plant will manufacture, assemble, and test thermal and power infrastructure for data centre environments. Products to be built at the site include the Vertiv CoolChip coolant distribution unit (CDU) — equipment that moves liquid coolant to and from servers to remove heat — along with direct-to-chip and rear-door heat exchangers designed for dense server racks. The facility will also produce the Vertiv Power Module and Vertiv Power Skid systems, which are modular electrical distribution units, as well as the SmartRun prefabricated overhead infrastructure system.

  The site incorporates a witness-testing environment that allows customers to validate liquid cooling and integrated power systems under conditions that replicate their own data centre sites before equipment is shipped. Vertiv said the testing capability covers the full range of CDU capacities and supports simultaneous testing of multiple power modules and skids.

  ## Products and regional scope

  The Johor plant adds regional manufacturing, engineering, logistics, and deployment support to Vertiv's Asia operations. The company described the move as part of broader capacity planning as customers source power and cooling systems for successive generations of AI infrastructure.

  "Asia continues to be one of the fastest-growing regions for AI and digital infrastructure investment, and expanding our manufacturing footprint in Malaysia aims to further enhance our ability to support customers with quality, speed, scale, and resilience," said Giordano Albertazzi, chief executive of Vertiv.

  Albertazzi also said the plant would help serve larger and more complex computing deployments. "This facility represents another important step in our continuous capacity planning and deployment strategy as we further expand our regional and global manufacturing capabilities," he said.

  ## Johor as a manufacturing and data centre hub

  Johor has emerged as a hub for both manufacturing and data centre development, supported by its transport and logistics connections to Singapore and its position in one of Southeast Asia's fastest-growing industrial markets, according to Vertiv.

  The investment follows broader industry movement to add capacity across Asia in response to demand from AI workloads and digital infrastructure projects. Vertiv said the Johor site would strengthen its supply chain resilience in the region.

  ## Jobs and timeline

  When fully operational, the facility is expected to bring up to 500 skilled jobs to the region. Full operations are expected in 2027.

  Vertiv said the Johor facility is intended to help customers deploy digital infrastructure more efficiently as compute requirements evolve. "As compute requirements evolve across multiple generations of AI infrastructure, customers need partners to provide power, cooling, and infrastructure solutions at scale. The Johor facility enhances our ability to help customers deploy critical digital infrastructure more efficiently while supporting long-term growth across Asia," Albertazzi said.

  The plant's witness-testing capability — where operators can observe equipment performance before it leaves the factory — reflects a broader shift in the data centre supply chain toward localised manufacturing and pre-shipment validation, particularly for liquid cooling and modular power systems being installed for large AI clusters.

  Vertiv已在馬來西亞柔佛州實納（Senai）正式啟用一座占地2萬1,900平方公尺的製造設施，目標是滿足東南亞、北亞、澳洲及紐西蘭等地對人工智慧（AI）與高密度運算基礎設施日益高漲的需求。

  廠區負責資料中心散熱與電源基礎設施的製造、組裝及測試作業。廠內生產的產品涵蓋Vertiv CoolChip冷媒分配單元（CDU）——這是一種將液態冷媒輸送至伺服器並帶走熱能的設備——以及專為高密度伺服器機架設計的直接晶片液冷（direct-to-chip）熱交換器與後門式熱交換器。此外，廠區亦將生產Vertiv Power Module與Vertiv Power Skid系統（均屬模組化配電單元），以及SmartRun預製架空基礎設施系統。

  廠區內建有見證測試環境，讓客戶能在設備出貨前，於模擬自身資料中心現場條件的環境中驗證液冷與整合電源系統的性能。Vertiv表示，此測試能力涵蓋CDU的完整容量範圍，並可同時測試多組電源模組與電源橇裝設備。

  ## 產品與服務區域

  柔佛廠為Vertiv的亞洲業務增添了在地化的製造、工程、物流及部署支援能量。Vertiv指出，此舉是其更大規模產能規劃的一環，目的在於因應客戶為歷代AI基礎設施採購電源與散熱系統的需求。

  「亞洲持續是AI與數位基礎設施投資成長最迅速的區域之一，在馬來西亞擴大製造版圖，旨在進一步提升我們以品質、速度、規模與韌性服務客戶的能力，」Vertiv執行長Giordano Albertazzi表示。

  Albertazzi也指出，這座廠房將有助於支援規模更大、更複雜的運算部署。「這座設施代表我們在持續性產能規劃與部署策略上又向前邁進了重要一步，我們將持續擴充在地與全球的製造能力，」他說。

  ## 柔佛：製造與資料中心樞紐

  根據Vertiv的說法，柔佛已躍升為製造業與資料中心開發的重要樞紐，得益於其與新加坡之間便捷的交通物流連結，以及在東南亞成長最快速工業市場之一中的優越區位條件。

  此次投資呼應了業界持續在亞洲擴充產能的整體趨勢，背後驅動力來自AI工作負載與數位基礎設施建設的強勁需求。Vertiv表示，柔佛廠將強化其在該地區的供應鏈韌性。

  ## 就業機會與時程規劃

  廠區全面投入運作後，預計將為當地帶來最多500個技術職缺，預計於2027年達成全面運作。

  Vertiv表示，柔佛廠的設立旨在協助客戶隨著運算需求的演進，更有效率地部署數位基礎設施。「隨著運算需求跨越多個世代的AI基礎設施持續演進，客戶需要能夠大規模提供電源、散熱與基礎設施解決方案的合作夥伴。柔佛廠提升了我們協助客戶更有效率地部署關鍵數位基礎設施的能力，同時支持亞洲的長期成長，」Albertazzi說。

  廠區的見證測試能力——讓業者得以在設備離廠前親眼確認其運作表現——折射出資料中心供應鏈更廣泛的發展趨勢：朝向在地化製造與出貨前驗證的方向轉型，尤其是針對大型AI叢集所部署的液冷與模組化電源系統而言更是如此。
