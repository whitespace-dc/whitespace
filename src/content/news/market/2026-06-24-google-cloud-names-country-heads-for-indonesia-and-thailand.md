body_en: |
  Google Cloud has appointed Karim Siregar as Country Director for Indonesia and Saruj Thipsena as Country Manager for Thailand, adding dedicated country-level leadership as the company expands its cloud and artificial intelligence business across Southeast Asia.

  Both executives report to Mark Micallef, Google Cloud's Managing Director for Southeast Asia. Their mandates cover local operations, market strategy, and customer engagement in their respective countries.

  ## Siregar's role in Indonesia

  Siregar joins from BFI Finance Indonesia, where he was Chief Information Officer. His career spans more than three decades across Indonesia's banking and technology sectors, including time as founding President Director of Bank Jago, Chief Executive Officer of Dkatalis Digital Labs, and senior technology roles at Bank BTPN, Bank Danamon, and Bank Mega.

  Micallef said Indonesian customers are moving from AI experimentation toward scaled deployment. "As organizations look to move past the initial phase of AI experimentation, the mandate has shifted entirely to scalable, real-world deployment that drives immediate value for their end-users," he said.

  Siregar said a key priority in the role would be enabling AI deployment while keeping sensitive data within national borders. He cited Google Cloud's Indonesia BerdAIa program and a dedicated local security operations data region in Jakarta as the frameworks available to Indonesian organizations building and scaling AI systems onshore.

  Google Cloud named Bank Central Asia, Bank Rakyat Indonesia, Telkom Indonesia, Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, Astra International, and e-commerce platform Bukalapak among its existing customers in Indonesia.

  ## Thipsena's role in Thailand

  Thipsena joins from STelligence, a Thailand-based AI and data analytics company, where he was Executive Director and Chief Technology Officer. Before that, he held two senior positions at Microsoft Thailand: Deputy Managing Director of the Cloud Solution Unit and Chief Information Security Officer. His prior work includes contributing to Thailand's TH2OECD national project and collaborating with the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific (UN ESCAP) on an AI-assisted legal and compliance research system.

  In Thailand, Thipsena will lead go-to-market strategy, customer and partner engagement, and business operations. Google Cloud has committed US$1 billion to investment in Thailand and opened a cloud region in Bangkok. Micallef said that level of infrastructure commitment requires matching local execution. "As we enter this next phase, world-class infrastructure must be matched by deep local collaboration," he said.

  Thipsena said successful AI adoption depends on pairing innovation with data governance. "Digital transformation only succeeds when it balances cutting-edge innovation with strict data governance and trust," he said.

  Google Cloud named Government Savings Bank, KASIKORN Business-Technology Group, the Stock Exchange of Thailand, Central Retail, Minor Hotels, True Digital Group, and Chulalongkorn University among its existing customers in Thailand.

  ## Southeast Asia competitive backdrop

  The appointments come as cloud providers compete for market share across Southeast Asia, where demand for AI tools and cloud services is rising among banks, retailers, telecommunications companies, and public sector organizations, according to Google Cloud. The company said its customer bases in both Indonesia and Thailand span financial institutions, consumer companies, startups, universities, and large conglomerates.

  Google Cloud has committed US$1 billion to Thailand and operates a cloud region in Bangkok; in Indonesia, it runs a local security operations data region in Jakarta and the BerdAIa industry program. Both new country heads will draw on those assets as they build out their respective local operations.

  Google Cloud 宣布任命 Karim Siregar 擔任印尼國家總監（Country Director）、Saruj Thipsena 擔任泰國國家總經理（Country Manager），在公司持續擴展東南亞雲端與人工智慧業務之際，為兩個重要市場補強了專屬的國家層級領導力。

  兩位高階主管均向 Google Cloud 東南亞區董事總經理 Mark Micallef 匯報，職責涵蓋各自國家的在地營運、市場策略及客戶拓展。

  ## Siregar 在印尼的職責

  Siregar 此前任職於 BFI Finance Indonesia，擔任資訊長。他在印尼銀行與科技領域深耕逾三十年，職涯亮點包括擔任 Bank Jago 的創始董事長、Dkatalis Digital Labs 執行長，以及在 Bank BTPN、Bank Danamon、Bank Mega 等金融機構擔任資深科技主管。

  Micallef 表示，印尼企業客戶正從 AI 實驗階段邁向規模化部署。他說：「當各大組織準備跨越 AI 實驗的初始階段，整個使命已全面轉向可規模化、可落地的真實部署，並為終端用戶創造即時價值。」

  Siregar 則表示，在確保敏感資料不出境的前提下順利推動 AI 部署，將是他上任後的首要優先任務。他援引 Google Cloud 的印尼 BerdAIa 產業計畫，以及在雅加達設立的專屬在地安全維運資料區域，視之為印尼企業在境內建置與擴展 AI 系統的核心框架。

  Google Cloud 點名 Bank Central Asia、Bank Rakyat Indonesia、Telkom Indonesia、Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison、Astra International，以及電子商務平台 Bukalapak，均為其在印尼的現有客戶。

  ## Thipsena 在泰國的職責

  Thipsena 此前任職於泰國 AI 與資料分析公司 STelligence，擔任執行董事兼技術長。在此之前，他曾在 Microsoft 泰國分公司身兼兩項資深要職：雲端解決方案事業部副董事總經理，以及資訊安全長。他的資歷還包括參與泰國 TH2OECD 國家計畫，以及與聯合國亞洲及太平洋經濟社會委員會（UN ESCAP）合作，共同開發以 AI 輔助的法律與合規研究系統。

  在泰國，Thipsena 將主導市場拓展策略、客戶與夥伴關係管理，以及業務營運。Google Cloud 已承諾向泰國投入十億美元，並在曼谷正式啟用雲端區域。Micallef 表示，如此規模的基礎建設投入，必須有相應深度的在地執行力相互配合。他說：「當我們邁入下一個階段，世界級的基礎建設必須與深度在地協作相互呼應。」

  Thipsena 指出，成功的 AI 導入有賴於創新與資料治理並行推進。他說：「數位轉型唯有在兼顧尖端創新與嚴格資料治理及信任的前提下，方能真正成功。」

  Google Cloud 點名的泰國現有客戶包括：泰國政府儲蓄銀行、KASIKORN Business-Technology Group、泰國證券交易所、Central Retail、Minor Hotels、True Digital Group，以及朱拉隆功大學。

  ## 東南亞競爭格局

  這兩項人事任命的時間點，正值各大雲端業者在東南亞市場激烈角逐之際。根據 Google Cloud 的說法，銀行、零售、電信業者及公部門組織對 AI 工具與雲端服務的需求正持續攀升。該公司表示，其在印尼與泰國的客戶群橫跨金融機構、消費品公司、新創企業、大學院校與大型企業集團。

  Google Cloud 已承諾向泰國投入十億美元，並在曼谷設有雲端區域；在印尼則於雅加達運營在地安全維運資料區域，並推動 BerdAIa 產業計畫。兩位新任國家主管將充分借助上述資源，在各自市場深化在地布局。
