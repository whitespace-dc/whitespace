body_en: |
  YTL Power International Bhd's fourth-quarter net profit fell 48% year-on-year to RM423.5 million, dragged by a four-year low in earnings from its Singapore electricity subsidiary, even as analysts pointed to a growing data centre pipeline as the catalyst for a rebound.

  The Kuala Lumpur-listed utility reported revenue of RM6.32 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, up nearly 14% from RM5.55 billion a year earlier. Net profit, however, collapsed from RM817.7 million in the same period last year, with Singapore subsidiary PowerSeraya the primary cause of the shortfall.

  ## PowerSeraya drag

  PowerSeraya's profit before tax for the quarter reached a four-year low at RM258 million, declining a further 2% quarter-on-quarter. The Singapore power generation and electricity retail business has been the group's most significant earnings contributor, and its deterioration was severe enough to prompt CIMB Securities to cut its core net profit forecast for YTL Power by 22% for the financial year ending June 30, 2027 (FY2027) and by 16% for FY2028. CIMB attributed both reductions primarily to lower expected contributions from PowerSeraya.

  Key risks the brokerage identified include a steeper-than-expected drop in PowerSeraya's earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation — measured per megawatt-hour (MWh), a standard efficiency metric for power generators — as well as slower-than-expected data centre expansion.

  ## Data centre pipeline underpins upgrade

  Despite the forecast cuts, CIMB Securities upgraded its rating on YTL Power from 'hold' to 'buy' and raised its target price to RM6.10, citing the company's data centre construction programme as the basis for longer-term confidence.

  YTL Power is currently developing two new data centres — designated JDC 5 and JDC 11 — at its Green Data Centre Park (GDCP) in Kulai, in the southern Malaysian state of Johor. JDC 5 is scheduled for completion by mid-2027; JDC 11 is targeted for end-2027. Neither facility has yet signed tenants.

  "While tenants have not been signed up yet for these two new data centres, we believe YTL Power has received keen interest, as it typically does not build data centres speculatively," CIMB Securities said in a note published Friday.

  Beyond the GDCP facilities, YTL Power has plans to acquire land at Sedenak Tech Park West to develop an additional data centre campus. CIMB said the combination of that planned acquisition and the two GDCP projects increased its confidence that the company's contracted capacity could grow at an accelerated pace.

  ## Offsetting subsidiaries

  MBSB Research, in a separate note, highlighted that PowerSeraya's weakness was partially offset by stronger performances from two other YTL Power subsidiaries: Wessex Water, a regulated water utility in the United Kingdom, and Ranhill Utilities Bhd, a Malaysian water and environmental services company listed on Bursa Malaysia.

  Wessex Water posted revenue of RM1.64 billion for the quarter, a 1.9% decline year-on-year attributed to the strengthening of the Malaysian ringgit against sterling. Despite the revenue dip, Wessex Water's profit before tax rose 73.8% year-on-year to RM162.3 million, driven by a price increase permitted by Ofwat, the UK Water Services Regulation Authority that sets the prices regulated water companies can charge.

  Ranhill SAJ, the water services unit under Ranhill Utilities, is expected to continue benefiting from higher tariffs. MBSB Research noted that management is studying the possibility of developing its own water treatment plant.

  MBSB maintained its 'buy' rating on YTL Power and revised its target price upward to RM5.98 from RM5.67. "We continue to like YTL Power for its strategic expansion into data centres, which are now becoming a new growth area for the group," the research house said.

  ## Significance

  The quarter illustrates a structural transition underway at YTL Power: electricity generation in Singapore, long the group's earnings engine, is under pressure, while data centre development in Johor is emerging as the intended replacement growth driver. Whether the pivot succeeds at the pace analysts anticipate depends on how quickly JDC 5 and JDC 11 secure tenants, and whether the Sedenak Tech Park West land acquisition proceeds.

  Management at Ranhill SAJ is currently evaluating whether to develop its own water treatment plant, a decision that could affect the subsidiary's capital requirements and longer-term earnings profile.

  YTL Power International Bhd第四季淨利年減48%至4億2,350萬令吉，主要拖累來自旗下新加坡電力子公司近四年最低的獲利表現；不過，分析師指出，持續擴大的資料中心建設管線將成為業績回升的催化劑。

  這家掛牌於吉隆坡的公用事業集團公告，截至2026年6月30日止季度，營收達63億2,000萬令吉，較去年同期的55億5,000萬令吉成長近14%。然而，淨利卻從去年同期的8億1,770萬令吉大幅下滑，新加坡子公司PowerSeraya是此次獲利缺口的主要根源。

  ## PowerSeraya拖累業績

  PowerSeraya本季稅前獲利跌至近四年低點，僅達2億5,800萬令吉，且較上一季再度下滑2%。這家從事新加坡電力發電與電力零售業務的子公司，向來是集團最重要的獲利貢獻來源，其業績惡化之嚴重，促使CIMB證券分別將YTL Power截至2027年6月30日止財年（FY2027）的核心淨利預測下修22%、2028財年（FY2028）下修16%。CIMB將兩項下修均主要歸因於PowerSeraya預期貢獻降低。

  該券商點名的主要風險包括：PowerSeraya每兆瓦時（MWh）——此為衡量發電業者營運效率的標準指標——稅息折舊攤銷前獲利（EBITDA）跌幅超乎預期，以及資料中心擴張進度不如預期。

  ## 資料中心管線支撐評等上調

  儘管下修了獲利預測，CIMB證券仍將YTL Power的投資評等由「持有」上調至「買進」，並將目標價調高至6.10令吉，理由是公司的資料中心建設計畫為長期展望提供了信心基礎。

  YTL Power目前正在馬來西亞南部柔佛州古來的綠色資料中心園區（GDCP）興建兩座新資料中心，分別命名為JDC 5與JDC 11。JDC 5預計於2027年中完工，JDC 11則以2027年底為目標竣工期程。兩座設施目前均尚未簽署租戶。

  CIMB證券在週五發布的研究報告中指出：「雖然這兩座新資料中心尚未簽入租戶，但我們相信YTL Power已獲得濃厚興趣，因為該公司通常不會在毫無依據的情況下投機性地興建資料中心。」

  除GDCP設施之外，YTL Power另計畫在實達納科技園西區（Sedenak Tech Park West）收購土地，以開發另一座資料中心園區。CIMB表示，該計畫收購案與前述兩項GDCP開發案的組合，令其更有信心認為公司的合約容量有望以加速步調持續增長。

  ## 其他子公司提供緩衝

  MBSB Research在另一份研究報告中指出，PowerSeraya的疲弱業績，部分由YTL Power旗下另外兩家子公司的亮眼表現所抵銷：一是英國受監管水務事業Wessex Water，另一是在馬來西亞交易所掛牌的水務與環境服務公司Ranhill Utilities Bhd。

  Wessex Water本季營收為16億4,000萬令吉，年減1.9%，主因馬來西亞令吉兌英鎊走強所致。儘管營收小幅下滑，Wessex Water稅前獲利仍年增73.8%至1億6,230萬令吉，主要受惠於英國水務服務監管機構Ofwat批准的水費調漲——Ofwat為負責核定受監管水務公司可收費標準的主管機關。

  Ranhill Utilities旗下負責水務服務的子公司Ranhill SAJ，預計將持續受惠於較高的水費費率。MBSB Research指出，管理階層正在評估自行開發水處理廠的可行性。

  MBSB維持對YTL Power的「買進」評等，並將目標價由5.67令吉上調至5.98令吉。該研究機構表示：「我們持續看好YTL Power積極布局資料中心的策略，資料中心如今正成為集團嶄新的成長動能。」

  ## 結構轉型意義

  本季財報呈現出YTL Power正在經歷的結構性轉型：長期作為集團獲利引擎的新加坡電力發電業務正承受壓力，而柔佛州的資料中心開發業務則逐步浮現，成為預期中的新成長驅動力。這場業務轉型能否以分析師預期的速度推進，關鍵在於JDC 5與JDC 11能多快簽入租戶，以及實達納科技園西區的土地收購案能否順利推展。

  Ranhill SAJ管理階層目前正評估是否自行興建水處理廠，這項決策將可能影響該子公司的資本需求與長期獲利結構。
