body_en: |
  NVIDIA has announced plans to produce up to $500 billion in AI infrastructure inside the United States through a manufacturing and supply chain partner network spanning 43 states, while separately unveiling a revenue-sharing model intended to expand AI computing capacity globally through third-party cloud providers.

  The company named partners including TSMC, Foxconn, Wistron, Corning, Lumentum, Coherent, and Amkor as involved across semiconductor fabrication, server and rack assembly, optical connectivity, and AI factory construction. NVIDIA said AI demand it generates is expected to contribute $485 billion to US GDP in 2026 alone and support more than 100,000 jobs tied to AI infrastructure.

  ## Supply chain structure

  NVIDIA described the domestic buildout as covering three layers of physical AI infrastructure: semiconductor fabrication plants for AI logic and memory chips; electronics manufacturing facilities producing boards, servers, and systems; and AI factories — the company's term for large-scale data centers focused on producing AI training and inference output rather than storing and transmitting files. Jensen Huang, NVIDIA's founder and chief executive, has described the core output of these facilities as tokens — which he characterized as the raw material for code, answers, designs, actions, and services.

  "AI is driving a once-in-a-generation opportunity to reinvigorate American manufacturing and supply chains," Huang said.

  Advanced semiconductor manufacturing for NVIDIA's Blackwell AI chips is already under way at TSMC's Phoenix, Arizona facility, the company said. NVIDIA added that it plans AI supercomputer manufacturing plants with Foxconn in Houston and with Wistron in Fort Worth, Texas.

  ## Partner facilities

  Several facilities are at different stages of planning and construction. Foxconn, the Taiwanese contract manufacturer, confirmed it is building a plant in Houston for NVIDIA AI systems including GB300 tray modules — high-density hardware assemblies used in AI server racks. NVIDIA said Foxconn engineers are using digital twins — software-based simulations of physical facilities built on NVIDIA libraries and open models — to design and validate the plant's structure, robotics systems, and workflows.

  Wistron, another Taiwanese electronics manufacturer, is setting up an advanced manufacturing facility in Fort Worth to assemble and test NVIDIA AI systems. NVIDIA said that plant was first designed as a digital twin built on NVIDIA AI and Omniverse software libraries.

  Optical networking supplier Coherent has broken ground on an expanded facility in Sherman, Texas, to scale production of indium phosphide components — compound semiconductors used in high-speed transceivers for AI networking. That project is expected to create 1,000 jobs. Corning is expanding US manufacturing for optical connectivity products in North Carolina and Texas, with NVIDIA projecting those expansions will open more than 3,000 jobs. Lumentum is also expanding US production and deepening optics research and development work with NVIDIA in North Carolina.

  In Manassas, Virginia, NVIDIA and data center operator Digital Realty have developed what NVIDIA described as an AI factory blueprint using US suppliers across the full technology stack, which the company said can be replicated across the industry.

  ## Revenue-sharing model

  Alongside the manufacturing push, NVIDIA disclosed a commercial model for deploying AI computing capacity through cloud providers. Under the arrangement, AI cloud providers build large, multi-tenant AI factories — computing facilities shared across multiple customers — through NVIDIA's DSX platform. NVIDIA said it will use revenue-sharing and credit support to help providers deploy capacity accessible to startups, model developers, enterprises, and research institutions, reducing the upfront capital burden on smaller organizations. In return, NVIDIA receives a share of cloud revenue generated by that computing capacity, establishing a recurring income stream alongside hardware sales.

  Australian companies Sharon AI and Firmus are among the first partners in the arrangement. Sharon AI signed a six-year strategic agreement with NVIDIA to add 72 megawatts of data center capacity in Australia. NVIDIA said nearly 40 countries have now deployed AI factories built on its infrastructure.

  ## Energy and cooling

  NVIDIA identified energy, cooling, and water use as constraints shaping the wider buildout. The company said its Rubin generation of AI infrastructure — the next hardware platform in its roadmap — will be the first to use 100% liquid cooling. Ali Heydari, NVIDIA's director of data center cooling and infrastructure, said the company's DSX reference design for AI factories eliminates water consumption entirely.

  "The NVIDIA DSX reference design for AI factories has zero water consumption — we have eliminated massive amounts of power usage and pretty much all the water usage," Heydari said.

  NVIDIA said it is working with Emerald AI and energy companies on flexible data centers capable of adjusting power draw in response to grid conditions, as part of an effort to align infrastructure growth with local power availability. The company named Caterpillar, Vertiv, Schneider Electric, Eaton, Jacobs, Siemens, Trane Technologies, and GE Vernova among companies helping design, power, cool, and operate AI infrastructure in the US. Caterpillar is also using NVIDIA technologies in its machines, power systems, and factory operations, according to the company.

  Research from Public First cited by NVIDIA suggested the infrastructure buildout draws on a broad employment base including electricians, plumbers, HVAC technicians, pipefitters, and construction workers.

  ## Applications and employment

  NVIDIA connected the infrastructure investment to active deployments in healthcare and science. In healthcare, Abridge is using NVIDIA's Nemotron open models and the Blackwell platform for a clinical conversation foundation model deployed across more than 300 US health systems and processing more than 2.5 million clinical conversations each week, NVIDIA said. Aidoc's aiOS platform is deployed across more than 100 US health systems and 1,300 US hospitals; NVIDIA said the system has analyzed more than 130 million patient cases to date, including more than 50 million patient scans in the US.

  In science, NVIDIA said it is working with Oracle and the US Department of Energy on new supercomputing systems at Argonne National Laboratory. The company also said its Earth-2 open models are being used to improve weather and climate forecasting, including more localized storm predictions.

  NVIDIA cited labor market data suggesting AI adoption is broad: roughly three-quarters of frontline workers are now regular AI users, with 42% reporting AI saves them a full workday each week. The company also cited research from Ramp showing that companies making larger AI investments have seen faster employment growth, including in entry-level hiring.

  The combined manufacturing and cloud announcements represent NVIDIA's most detailed public accounting of its US production ambitions, linking specific partners, facility locations, and job projections to the $500 billion infrastructure target. The DSX revenue-sharing model adds a recurring-income component to NVIDIA's business alongside hardware sales, and shifts some of the capital burden of AI infrastructure deployment from buyers to cloud partners.

  Foxconn chairman Liu Yang-wei has forecast that global data center investment will reach $1.6 trillion by 2030 as AI computing demand continues to expand. Huang, speaking at a manufacturing groundbreaking event, said the domestic manufacturing shift will grow more intense over the decade ahead. "The onshoring of manufacturing is intensifying," he said. "I think that that's going to intensify over the next decade, because we are seeing the renaissance of manufacturing here in the United States."
body_zh: |
  輝達（NVIDIA）宣布計畫透過橫跨美國43州的製造與供應鏈夥伴網路，在境內生產最多5千億美元的AI基礎設施，並同步揭露一套營收分潤模式，旨在透過第三方雲端供應商在全球擴展AI運算容量。

  輝達點名的夥伴包括台積電、富士康、緯創、康寧（Corning）、Lumentum、Coherent及Amkor，分別參與半導體晶圓製造、伺服器與機架組裝、光學連接，以及AI工廠的興建。輝達表示，其帶動的AI需求預計光在2026年就為美國GDP貢獻4,850億美元，並支撐逾10萬個與AI基礎設施相關的就業機會。

  ## 供應鏈架構

  輝達將這波國內建設描述為涵蓋實體AI基礎設施的三個層次：用於生產AI邏輯晶片與記憶體晶片的半導體晶圓廠；生產電路板、伺服器與系統的電子製造設施；以及「AI工廠」——這是輝達對大型資料中心的自有稱謂，這類設施的重心在於產出AI訓練與推論結果，而非儲存與傳輸檔案。輝達創辦人暨執行長黃仁勳將這些設施的核心產出定義為「token」，並形容其為程式碼、答案、設計、行動與服務的原始材料。

  黃仁勳表示：「AI正驅動著一個千載難逢的契機，足以重振美國製造業與供應鏈。」

  輝達表示，其Blackwell AI晶片的先進半導體製造工作已在台積電位於亞利桑那州鳳凰城的廠區展開。輝達並透露，計畫與富士康在德州休士頓、與緯創在德州沃斯堡分別設立AI超級電腦製造廠。

  ## 夥伴設施

  多項設施目前處於不同規劃與興建階段。台灣代工製造商富士康確認正在休士頓興建一座廠房，用於生產包括GB300托盤模組在內的輝達AI系統——這是一種用於AI伺服器機架的高密度硬體組件。輝達指出，富士康工程師正以數位孿生技術——即基於輝達函式庫與開放模型所建構的廠房軟體模擬——設計並驗證廠房結構、機器人系統及作業流程。

  另一家台灣電子製造商緯創則正在沃斯堡設立先進製造設施，用以組裝與測試輝達AI系統。輝達表示，該廠房最初同樣以數位孿生技術進行設計，並建構於輝達AI與Omniverse軟體函式庫之上。

  光學網路供應商Coherent已在德州謝爾曼（Sherman）破土動工，擴大磷化銦（indium phosphide）元件的生產規模——這是一種用於AI網路高速收發器的化合物半導體。該專案預計創造1,000個就業機會。康寧則在北卡羅來納州與德州擴大光學連接產品的美國製造產能，輝達預計這些擴廠計畫將新增逾3,000個就業機會。Lumentum也正在北卡羅來納州擴大美國產能，並與輝達深化光學研發合作。

  在維吉尼亞州馬納薩斯（Manassas），輝達與資料中心業者Digital Realty共同開發出一套AI工廠藍圖，採用涵蓋完整技術堆疊的美國供應商，輝達表示此藍圖可在業界複製推廣。

  ## 營收分潤模式

  與製造業推進計畫並行，輝達揭露了一套透過雲端供應商部署AI運算容量的商業模式。在此安排下，AI雲端供應商透過輝達的DSX平台興建大型多租戶AI工廠——亦即可跨多個客戶共享使用的運算設施。輝達表示，將透過營收分潤與信用支持，協助供應商部署可供新創公司、模型開發者、企業及研究機構取用的運算容量，降低規模較小組織的前期資本負擔。作為交換，輝達將獲得該運算容量所創造雲端營收的一定比例，在硬體銷售之外建立起一條持續性收入來源。

  澳洲企業Sharon AI與Firmus是此安排的首批夥伴之一。Sharon AI與輝達簽署了一份為期六年的策略合作協議，將在澳洲新增72百萬瓦（MW）的資料中心容量。輝達表示，目前已有近40個國家部署了以其基礎設施建構的AI工廠。

  ## 能源與冷卻

  輝達點出能源、冷卻與用水是影響整體建設的制約因素。該公司表示，其產品路線圖上的下一代AI基礎設施平台Rubin，將是首個全面採用液態冷卻的系統。輝達資料中心冷卻與基礎設施總監阿里·海達里（Ali Heydari）表示，輝達針對AI工廠所推出的DSX參考設計已完全排除耗水問題。

  海達里表示：「輝達AI工廠的DSX參考設計零水耗——我們已大幅削減電力使用量，並幾乎完全消除了用水。」

  輝達表示，正與Emerald AI及多家能源公司合作，研發能夠依據電網狀況調整用電量的彈性資料中心，作為將基礎設施擴張與當地電力供應能力相匹配的一部分努力。輝達點名卡特彼勒（Caterpillar）、Vertiv、施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）、伊頓（Eaton）、Jacobs、西門子（Siemens）、特靈科技（Trane Technologies）及GE Vernova等企業，協助設計、供電、冷卻並營運美國AI基礎設施。輝達並指出，卡特彼勒也在其機械設備、電力系統及工廠營運中採用輝達技術。

  輝達引用Public First的研究指出，此波基礎設施建設依賴廣泛的勞動力基礎，涵蓋電工、水電工、暖通空調（HVAC）技術人員、管道工及建築工人。

  ## 應用場景與就業

  輝達將這波基礎設施投資與醫療及科學領域的實際應用相連結。在醫療方面，Abridge正運用輝達的Nemotron開放模型及Blackwell平台，建置一個臨床對話基礎模型，已部署於逾300家美國醫療體系，每週處理逾250萬次臨床對話。輝達表示，Aidoc的aiOS平台已部署於逾100家美國醫療體系及1,300家美國醫院，迄今已分析逾1.3億個患者案例，其中包括在美國境內逾5,000萬次患者掃描。

  在科學方面，輝達表示正與甲骨文（Oracle）及美國能源部合作，在阿貢國家實驗室（Argonne National Laboratory）建置新型超級運算系統。輝達並表示，其Earth-2開放模型正被用於改善天氣與氣候預報，包括更在地化的風暴預測。

  輝達援引勞動市場數據，顯示AI採用已相當普遍：約四分之三的第一線工作者現為AI的定期使用者，其中42%表示AI每週為其節省整整一個工作天。輝達並引用財務管理平台Ramp的研究指出，加大AI投資的企業已見到更快的就業成長，包括在入門級職位的招募上。

  上述製造業與雲端業務的雙重宣告，代表輝達迄今對其美國生產計畫最詳盡的公開說明，將具體夥伴、設施地點與就業預測一一連結至5千億美元的基礎設施目標。DSX營收分潤模式在硬體銷售之外為輝達增添一條持續性收入，並將AI基礎設施部署的部分資本負擔從買方轉移至雲端夥伴。

  富士康董事長劉揚偉預測，隨著AI運算需求持續擴張，全球資料中心投資規模到2030年將達到1.6兆美元。黃仁勳在一場製造廠破土典禮上發表談話時表示，製造業回流美國的趨勢在未來十年將更加深化。他說：「製造業回流正在加速。我認為這股趨勢在未來十年只會愈演愈烈，因為我們正見證美國製造業的文藝復興。」
