body_en: |
  Globe Telecom, Inc. is pressing ahead with data center investment in the Philippines, citing rising demand for artificial intelligence (AI) and cloud services as drivers, the Ayala-led telecommunications company said Sunday.

  The company holds its data center interests through ST Telemedia Global Data Centres (STT GDC) Philippines, a joint venture with Ayala Corp. and Singapore-based ST Telemedia Global Data Centres. STT GDC Philippines currently operates seven data centers in the country with a combined IT load of nearly 150 megawatts (MW). IT load refers to the power capacity available to run servers and computing equipment — a standard measure of data center scale.

  In April, STT GDC Philippines said it would add 4 MW of capacity to its Fairview data center by the end of 2026 to meet demand driven by AI and cloud computing.

  ## Infrastructure strategy

  Globe frames data centers as complementary to its submarine cable investments, with both asset classes forming part of the digital backbone it says is needed to support the broader economy and strengthen operational resilience. The company did not provide financial figures for its data center commitments in the release.

  "Digital infrastructure works hand in hand with today's advanced technologies. It enables businesses to operate, governments to deliver services, and people to stay connected," Globe President and Chief Executive Officer Carl Raymond R. Cruz said in a media release.

  STT GDC Philippines President and Chief Executive Officer Carlomagno E. Malana said customers are seeking more than raw capacity. "They want infrastructure that is reliable, energy efficient, resilient, and designed to support their long-term growth," he said.

  ## Environmental approach

  The company said it factors environmental impact into facility development. Its Fairview campus uses a closed-loop cooling system that recycles water to cool equipment, reducing freshwater consumption compared with open-loop alternatives.

  Globe added that data center development should be approached cautiously and responsibly as demand for digital services continues to grow, without elaborating on specific constraints or risks it has identified.

  ## Significance

  With seven operating facilities and a capacity expansion already announced for Fairview, STT GDC Philippines is positioned as one of the more active data center operators in the Philippine market. Globe's emphasis on AI and cloud as demand drivers reflects a pattern seen across Southeast Asia, where hyperscaler and enterprise workloads are prompting telecoms and infrastructure companies to scale up local colocation capacity. The Fairview expansion is scheduled for completion by the end of 2026.

  ## Globe Telecom擴大資料中心布局，AI與雲端需求成關鍵驅力

  菲律賓電信業者Globe Telecom, Inc.日前表示，將持續在菲律賓加碼投資資料中心，並以人工智慧（AI）與雲端服務需求持續攀升為主要驅動力。這家由Ayala集團主導的電信公司於週日發布聲明，說明其資料中心布局策略。

  Globe Telecom透過ST Telemedia Global Data Centres（STT GDC）Philippines持有其資料中心權益。STT GDC Philippines為Globe Telecom與Ayala Corp.及新加坡商ST Telemedia Global Data Centres共同成立的合資公司。目前，STT GDC Philippines已在菲律賓營運七座資料中心，合計IT負載接近150百萬瓦（MW）。所謂IT負載，係指可供伺服器與運算設備使用的電力容量，為業界衡量資料中心規模的標準指標。

  今年4月，STT GDC Philippines宣布將於2026年底前，為旗下Fairview資料中心新增4 MW容量，以因應AI與雲端運算所帶動的需求成長。

  ## 基礎設施策略

  Globe Telecom將資料中心定位為其海底電纜投資的互補資產，認為兩類資產共同構成數位骨幹，不僅可支撐整體經濟發展，也有助於強化營運韌性。該公司在聲明中並未揭露資料中心相關投資的具體財務數字。

  Globe Telecom總裁暨執行長Carl Raymond R. Cruz在媒體聲明中表示：「數位基礎設施與當今先進科技相輔相成。它讓企業得以順暢運作、讓政府能夠提供服務，也讓民眾保持緊密連結。」

  STT GDC Philippines總裁暨執行長Carlomagno E. Malana則指出，客戶的需求已不僅止於基本容量。他說：「他們需要的是可靠、節能、具備韌性，且能支持其長期成長的基礎設施。」

  ## 環境永續方針

  公司表示，在設施開發過程中，已將環境影響納入考量。旗下Fairview園區採用閉迴路冷卻系統，透過水資源循環再利用為設備降溫，相較於開放式冷卻系統，可有效降低淡水消耗量。

  Globe Telecom亦強調，隨著數位服務需求持續擴張，資料中心的開發應審慎、負責任地推進，但並未進一步說明其所識別的具體限制或風險。

  ## 市場意義

  在菲律賓資料中心市場中，STT GDC Philippines以七座在營設施以及已公告的Fairview擴容計畫，躋身最活躍的業者之列。Globe Telecom以AI與雲端作為需求驅動力的論述，與東南亞地區整體趨勢不謀而合——超大規模雲端業者與企業級工作負載的擴張，正促使各地電信商與基礎設施業者積極擴充本地共置容量。Fairview資料中心的擴容工程預計於2026年底完工。
