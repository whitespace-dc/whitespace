body_en: |
  Kingboard, a Taiwan-based copper foil manufacturer, has approved a factory in Penang, Malaysia, with an initial estimated investment of NT$8 billion to NT$10 billion and planned annual capacity of 30,000 metric tonnes, targeting HVLP4 — fourth-generation ultra-low surface roughness copper foil used in advanced printed circuit board applications — and other high-end products for AI server customers, Chairman Li Si-hsien said Tuesday.

  Li disclosed the decision on the sidelines of the 2026 TPCA Show, Taiwan's circuit board industry international exhibition. He said the Malaysia move was driven by supply chain diversification requirements and the need for overseas utilities infrastructure. The Penang site was selected for its location adjacent to Penang port, reliable electricity and water supply, and tariff advantages, the company said. Kingboard described the facility as a key overseas base intended to serve Southeast Asian and global customers.

  ## Construction schedule

  The factory will be built in two phases. Phase one production is scheduled to start in the third quarter of 2028. Phase two is planned to reach full volume in the second half of 2029.

  ## Thermal copper foil for high-power AI boards

  At the same event, Kingboard publicly demonstrated GP999, a newly developed copper foil product designed for heat dissipation in high-power computing boards, for the first time. Li said power consumption in AI chips now routinely exceeds 2,000 watts and in some cases reaches 2,300 watts, making heat generated within the PCB (printed circuit board) itself a significant obstacle for system-level thermal management.

  GP999 is designed to reduce temperatures significantly compared with traditional RTF (reverse treated foil), the conventional copper foil type in PCB manufacturing, the company said. Li identified targeted applications as GPU (graphics processing unit) boards, switch trays, and four-to-six-layer power planes inside AI servers.

  The product is in system-level verification with contract electronics manufacturers Inventec (TWSE: 2356) and Quanta Computer (TWSE: 2382), as well as with chip manufacturers, the company said. Small-volume shipments have already begun.

  ## Revenue mix and near-term outlook

  High-end products, broadly defined, accounted for 15 to 20 percent of Kingboard's revenue in the third quarter of 2026, a share the company said has grown each consecutive quarter. Despite elevated summer electricity costs in the third quarter, overall profitability is expected to exceed that of the second quarter of 2026, Li said. The company expects the upward earnings trend to continue in the fourth quarter.

  The Penang factory's second phase is scheduled to begin ramping production volume in the second half of 2029.
body_zh: |
  台灣銅箔製造商建滔銅箔已核准在馬來西亞檳城設立工廠，初期估計投資額為新台幣80億至100億元，規劃年產能3萬公噸，目標產品為HVLP4——第四代超低表面粗糙度銅箔，適用於先進印刷電路板應用——及其他高階銅箔，以AI伺服器客戶為主要市場。董事長李思賢週二表示。

  李思賢是在2026年TPCA展覽會場外透露這項決定。TPCA展為台灣電路板產業的國際展覽。他表示，此次赴馬來西亞設廠，是受到供應鏈多元化需求及海外公用事業基礎設施需求所驅動。該公司指出，檳城廠址的選定，係因其緊鄰檳城港、電力與水源供應穩定，以及具備關稅優惠優勢。建滔將這座設施定位為重要的海外生產基地，目標服務東南亞及全球客戶。

  ## 建廠時程

  工廠將分兩期興建。第一期生產預計於2028年第三季啟動，第二期則規劃於2029年下半年達到完整量產規模。

  ## 高功率AI板散熱銅箔

  在同一場展覽上，建滔首度公開展示全新開發的銅箔產品GP999，該產品專為高功率運算板的散熱需求而設計。李思賢表示，AI晶片功耗如今動輒超過2,000瓦，部分情況甚至達到2,300瓦，PCB（印刷電路板）本身所產生的熱量，已成為系統層級熱管理的重大瓶頸。

  GP999的設計目標是相較於傳統RTF（反轉處理銅箔）——即PCB製造中慣用的傳統銅箔類型——大幅降低溫度。李思賢點名的目標應用涵蓋GPU（圖形處理器）板、交換器托盤，以及AI伺服器內部四至六層的電源平面。

  該產品目前正在電子代工廠英業達（股票代號：2356）、廣達電腦（股票代號：2382）及晶片製造商處進行系統層級驗證，小量出貨已經展開。

  ## 營收結構與近期展望

  以廣義定義的高階產品，在2026年第三季佔建滔整體營收比重達15%至20%，該公司表示此一比重已連續多季持續成長。儘管第三季夏季電費偏高，整體獲利能力預計仍將超越2026年第二季，李思賢說。該公司預期第四季獲利成長趨勢將延續。

  檳城廠第二期預計於2029年下半年開始擴大量產規模。
