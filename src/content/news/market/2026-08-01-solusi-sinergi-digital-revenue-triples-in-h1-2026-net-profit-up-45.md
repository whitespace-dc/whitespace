body_en: |
  PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk (IDX: WIFI), a digital services company affiliated with Hashim S. Djojohadikusumo, reported first-half 2026 revenue of Rp 1.57 trillion, a 205.94% increase from Rp 513.46 billion in the equivalent period of 2025.

  Net profit attributable to owners of the parent entity rose 45.30%, from Rp 227.91 billion to Rp 331.16 billion over the same interval.

  ## Gross and operating profit

  Gross profit grew 142.27%, from Rp 392.36 billion to Rp 950.58 billion, according to an analysis by Jakarta-based brokerage Phintraco Sekuritas. Operating profit rose 104%, reaching Rp 773.22 billion.

  ## Cost expansion tracks capacity build-out

  The cost of revenue increased substantially alongside the top-line gain, rising from Rp 121.10 billion to Rp 620.31 billion — a more than fivefold increase. Phintraco Sekuritas said the continued expansion in gross profit, despite the higher cost base, indicates that business growth remained strong even as the company extended its service capacity.

  ## Significance

  The gap between the 205.94% revenue increase and the 45.30% net profit increase reflects the scale of investment required to support WIFI's capacity expansion. Phintraco Sekuritas was reviewing the company's stock prospects in light of the results.
body_zh: |
  ## Solusi Sinergi Digital 2026上半年營收翻三倍，淨利成長45%

  與Hashim S. Djojohadikusumo旗下關聯的數位服務業者PT Solusi Sinergi Digital Tbk（印尼股市代號：WIFI）公布2026年上半年財報，營收達1.57兆印尼盾，較2025年同期的5,134.6億印尼盾大幅成長205.94%。

  歸屬母公司股東之淨利同期由2,279.1億印尼盾增至3,311.6億印尼盾，年增幅達45.30%。

  ## 毛利與營業利益

  根據雅加達券商Phintraco Sekuritas的分析，毛利由3,923.6億印尼盾成長至9,505.8億印尼盾，增幅142.27%；營業利益則成長104%，達7,732.2億印尼盾。

  ## 成本擴張反映產能建置

  隨著營收大幅攀升，營業成本亦同步顯著增加，由1,211.0億印尼盾升至6,203.1億印尼盾，漲幅超過五倍。Phintraco Sekuritas指出，儘管成本基礎明顯提高，毛利仍持續擴張，顯示該公司在積極拓展服務產能的同時，業務成長動能依然強勁。

  ## 意義

  營收成長205.94%與淨利成長45.30%之間的落差，反映出WIFI為支應產能擴張所需投入的龐大資本規模。Phintraco Sekuritas目前正依據上述財報結果，評估該公司股票的後市展望。
