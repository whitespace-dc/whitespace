body_en: |
  Samsung Electronics is in talks to acquire Modine Manufacturing Co., a Racine, Wisconsin-based maker of cooling systems for data centers, in a potential transaction that could come together as soon as within the coming weeks, according to people familiar with the matter cited by Bloomberg News.

  No final decision has been made and the discussions could still fall through; another bidder could also emerge, the people said, requesting anonymity because the matter is private. Representatives for both Samsung and Modine declined to comment, Bloomberg reported.

  Modine's shares closed regular trading Thursday in New York at $106.10, giving the company a market value of approximately $5.6 billion. The stock jumped to more than $135 in after-hours trading — a gain of roughly 28 to 29 percent — lifting the implied market value to approximately $7.28 billion, according to Data Center Dynamics. The shares had already fallen about 15 percent since November 3, when Modine reported quarterly results that disappointed investors.

  ## Modine's business and recent financial results

  Modine makes heavy-duty cooling and heating systems for large commercial buildings and industrial machines, with its fastest-growing segment serving hyperscale data center operators — companies that build and run very large-scale cloud and AI infrastructure. The company acquired cooling specialist Airedale in 2005 and the Scott Springfield business in 2024, and describes itself as one of the largest end-to-end cooling solutions providers in the data center industry, offering both air-side and liquid cooling technologies, according to Data Center Dynamics. In October, Modine spun off its automotive business to concentrate entirely on its thermal management operations, The Edge Malaysia reported.

  Modine posted $994.4 million in revenue for its fiscal second quarter of 2027, beating revenue forecasts but missing earnings-per-share estimates, Data Center Dynamics reported. Data center-specific sales rose 95 percent year over year, driven primarily by U.S. hyperscale demand.

  On the November 3 earnings call, chief executive Neil Brinker disclosed a major new contract. "We recently received a major new program award with a strategic hyperscaler customer," Brinker said, according to Data Center Dynamics. "Through this partnership, we will supply more than $400 million of custom air handling units through calendar year 2027 to cool next-generation data centers." Brinker said the company is launching a third dedicated data center manufacturing facility in Calgary while increasing throughput at a facility in Franklin, Wisconsin.

  The company also flagged headwinds: higher material costs, broader inflation, tariffs, and supply chain challenges are weighing on margins, Brinker said, per Data Center Dynamics.

  ## Samsung's existing thermal solutions push

  Samsung, headquartered in Suwon, South Korea, carries a market value exceeding $900 billion and makes products ranging from memory chips and smartphones to home appliances and thermal solutions, The Edge Malaysia reported. A Modine acquisition would represent a larger move in the data center cooling segment than any Samsung has previously made.

  The company has been building scale in thermal management through prior deals. Data Center Dynamics reported that Samsung spent $1.68 billion on German heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) specialist FläktGroup this past May. The Edge Malaysia, citing Bloomberg, reported the same acquisition at €1.5 billion (approximately $1.7 billion), describing it as completed last year. In September, Samsung announced plans to build a factory in South Korea to manufacture coolant distribution units (CDUs), fan wall units, computer room air handlers (CRAHs), and air handling units (AHUs) for AI data centers, semiconductor fabrication plants, and battery manufacturing facilities, according to Data Center Dynamics.

  ## Sector-wide consolidation around AI infrastructure

  The potential deal reflects a broader wave of mergers and acquisitions among companies supplying equipment to the AI-driven data center build-out. Samsung's Asian competitors Daikin Industries and LG Electronics, as well as U.S.-based Vertiv Holdings — a maker of power management and thermal infrastructure equipment — have also been stepping up investments and dealmaking to capture data center growth, The Edge Malaysia reported.

  Modine's 95 percent year-over-year increase in data center revenue and its newly announced $400 million-plus hyperscaler supply contract illustrate why companies with established hyperscale customer relationships have drawn acquisition interest. If completed, a Samsung-Modine combination would add a U.S.-based manufacturing footprint and an existing hyperscaler customer base to Samsung's thermal solutions portfolio, alongside the Korean production capacity and FläktGroup operations the company has already assembled.
body_zh: |
  據彭博社引述知情人士報導，三星電子（Samsung Electronics）正與位於威斯康辛州拉辛市（Racine, Wisconsin）的資料中心冷卻系統製造商 Modine Manufacturing Co. 展開收購談判，潛在交易最快可能在未來數週內達成。

  消息人士以事涉機密為由要求匿名表示，目前尚未做出最終決定，談判仍有破局可能，亦不排除其他競購方加入。彭博報導指出，三星與 Modine 雙方代表均拒絕就此事置評。

  Modine 股票週四在紐約收盤價為每股 106.10 美元，公司市值約達 56 億美元。盤後交易中，該股一度飆升至逾每股 135 美元，漲幅達 28 至 29%，據 Data Center Dynamics 報導，隱含市值隨之躍升至約 72.8 億美元。值得注意的是，自 Modine 於 11 月 3 日公布令投資人失望的季度財報後，股價此前已累計下跌約 15%。

  ## Modine 的業務概況與近期財報

  Modine 主要為大型商業建築及工業機械製造重型冷暖氣系統，其中成長最快的業務部門服務超大規模資料中心（hyperscale data center）業者——也就是那些建置與營運大型雲端及 AI 基礎建設的企業。該公司於 2005 年收購冷卻專業廠商 Airedale，並於 2024 年收購 Scott Springfield 業務。據 Data Center Dynamics 報導，Modine 自詡為資料中心產業規模最大的端對端冷卻解決方案供應商之一，同時提供氣側（air-side）與液冷（liquid cooling）技術。The Edge Malaysia 報導指出，Modine 於今年 10 月完成汽車業務分拆，將全部資源集中於熱管理（thermal management）業務。

  根據 Data Center Dynamics 報導，Modine 2027 財年第二季繳出 9.944 億美元的營收成績，雖超越市場營收預期，但每股盈餘未達標準。資料中心相關業務的銷售額年增 95%，主要動能來自美國超大規模業者的強勁需求。

  在 11 月 3 日的法說會上，執行長尼爾·布林克（Neil Brinker）披露了一項重大新合約。據 Data Center Dynamics 報導，布林克表示：「我們近期與一位策略性超大規模客戶取得了重大新合約。透過這項合作關係，我們將在 2027 年底前供應逾 4 億美元的客製化空氣處理機組，用以冷卻次世代資料中心。」布林克並表示，公司正在加拿大卡加利（Calgary）設立第三座專屬資料中心製造廠，同時擴大威斯康辛州法蘭克林（Franklin, Wisconsin）廠的產出。

  此外，Modine 亦揭示多項逆風因素：據 Data Center Dynamics 報導，布林克指出，材料成本上揚、整體通膨壓力、關稅衝擊以及供應鏈挑戰，正持續對公司利潤空間形成壓力。

  ## 三星在熱管理領域的既有布局

  三星總部位於南韓水原市（Suwon），市值逾 9,000 億美元，產品線涵蓋記憶體晶片、智慧型手機、家用電器乃至熱管理解決方案，據 The Edge Malaysia 報導。若成功收購 Modine，將是三星迄今在資料中心冷卻領域規模最大的一筆購併。

  三星此前已透過多起交易積累熱管理業務的規模。Data Center Dynamics 報導指出，三星於今年 5 月以 16.8 億美元收購德國暖通空調（HVAC）專業廠商 FläktGroup；The Edge Malaysia 引述彭博社報導則以 15 億歐元（約 17 億美元）估算同一筆交易，並描述該收購已於去年完成。今年 9 月，三星宣布計畫在南韓建設新廠，專門生產冷卻液分配裝置（CDU）、風牆機組（fan wall unit）、電腦室空調（CRAH）及空氣處理機組（AHU），供應 AI 資料中心、半導體晶圓廠及電池製造廠使用，據 Data Center Dynamics 報導。

  ## AI 基礎建設帶動產業整合浪潮

  此次潛在收購案，折射出 AI 驅動的資料中心建設熱潮下，設備供應商之間掀起的更大規模購併浪潮。The Edge Malaysia 報導指出，三星在亞洲的競爭對手大金工業（Daikin Industries）與 LG 電子，以及美國電源管理與熱基礎建設設備製造商 Vertiv Holdings，也紛紛加快投資步伐與購併布局，積極搶進資料中心成長商機。

  Modine 資料中心業務年增 95% 的亮眼成長，以及剛宣布的逾 4 億美元超大規模供應合約，正是說明為何擁有成熟超大規模客戶關係的業者能吸引收購目光的最佳佐證。若此次交易順利完成，三星與 Modine 的結合，將為三星的熱管理解決方案版圖增添以美國為基地的製造據點與現有的超大規模客戶群，與三星已整合的南韓生產能力及 FläktGroup 業務相輔相成。
