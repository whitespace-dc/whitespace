body_en: |
  Nvidia has introduced a revenue-sharing financing model for AI cloud providers, under which operators acquire Nvidia computing infrastructure through credit support and pay the chipmaker a share of revenue generated from cloud services running on that hardware.

  The arrangement marks a structural shift in how Nvidia monetizes its products. Rather than collecting only the initial sale price of its hardware, the company would receive ongoing income tied to usage of financed systems by paying cloud customers.

  ## How the model works

  Under the program, AI cloud operators procure Nvidia infrastructure with credit support. Nvidia receives its standard product revenue from the equipment sale, plus a percentage of cloud service revenue generated from the supported capacity. The company said the structure is intended to make large-scale infrastructure deployments more accessible, particularly for companies that lack the balance sheet strength typically required to fund capital-intensive data center buildouts.

  Nvidia cast the financing model as a response to a persistent capital access problem: many AI cloud operators need large hardware deployments but have struggled to secure conventional financing, even when they can demonstrate likely customer demand.

  ## Early deployments

  Two companies are among the first to operate under the program. Sharon AI is deploying up to 40,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GB300 GPUs (graphics processing units, the accelerator chips that power large-scale AI workloads). Firmus Technologies is developing what Nvidia calls a DSX AI factory campus in Batam, Indonesia, projected to reach 360 megawatts of electrical capacity — a very large power draw for a data center facility — and up to 170,000 Nvidia GPUs.

  Nvidia uses the term "DSX AI factory" to describe large-scale, GPU-dense facilities organized around customer demand in specific regions. The company said the model is also intended to give customers faster access to compute than conventional buildouts allow, by avoiding the sequential steps of site selection, power procurement, construction, and hardware installation.

  James Manning, co-founder and CEO of Sharon AI, called the collaboration "a pivotal moment in Sharon AI's mission to deliver sovereign, large-scale AI compute infrastructure." Tim Rosenfield, co-CEO of Firmus Technologies, said that "AI-native companies need access to scalable, energy- and cost-efficient compute infrastructure to compete globally," and that the Batam facility is being built as an Nvidia DSX-aligned AI factory to help customers access compute for building and scaling AI.

  ## Shift from training to inference

  Nvidia said the financing model reflects where AI computing demand is now concentrated. The market has shifted from training large AI models — a phase requiring massive bursts of compute — toward production inference, in which deployed models run continuously to serve commercial customers at scale. Inference workloads, along with fine-tuning and high-volume agentic inference (where AI agents process large numbers of automated tasks), generate steady, ongoing demand for GPU capacity rather than episodic peaks.

  Nvidia identified model builders, inference providers, and enterprises seeking immediate cloud access as the primary targets for the program. It also named AI-native businesses, citing Baseten, Fireworks AI, and Together AI as examples of the customer segment driving this demand.

  For cloud operators, Nvidia said, the shift from experiment to commercial product has increased the need for computing supply that can expand with usage while preserving flexibility during the transition from pilot projects to full deployment — arrangements that traditional fixed infrastructure commitments do not easily accommodate.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The financing structure gives Nvidia an ongoing financial stake in the AI cloud market beyond the point of hardware sale. Rather than collecting a one-time payment and relying on customers to make those deployments profitable on their own, Nvidia would share in cloud revenues — aligning its economics with the operators that serve AI developers and enterprises.

  For AI cloud operators, and especially for emerging companies without large balance sheets, the program represents an alternative path to capacity at a scale that has typically been difficult to fund.

  The Firmus Batam campus, if completed at its projected 170,000-GPU scale, would represent one of the largest AI infrastructure deployments described under the program to date.

  ## 輝達推出營收分潤融資模式，服務AI雲端業者

  輝達宣布為AI雲端服務供應商推出一套營收分潤融資模式。在此架構下，業者可透過信貸支持取得輝達運算基礎設施，並將在該硬體上經營雲端服務所產生的部分營收回饋給這家晶片製造商。

  這項安排標誌著輝達商業模式的結構性轉變。輝達不再只是收取硬體的一次性售價，而是將透過雲端客戶實際使用融資系統所產生的持續性收入，獲得一份與使用量掛鉤的長期收益。

  ## 運作模式

  在此計畫下，AI雲端業者以信貸支持採購輝達基礎設施。輝達除了收取設備銷售的正常產品收入外，還將獲得受支持產能所衍生的雲端服務營收的一定比例。輝達表示，這一架構旨在降低大規模基礎設施部署的門檻，尤其是針對那些不具備傳統資本密集型資料中心建設所需資產負債表實力的企業。

  輝達將這套融資模式定位為解決長期存在的資本取得難題——許多AI雲端業者需要大規模硬體部署，卻難以獲得傳統融資，即便他們能夠證明存在可期的客戶需求，問題依然棘手。

  ## 初期部署案例

  目前已有兩家公司率先採用此計畫。Sharon AI正在部署多達四萬張輝達Grace Blackwell GB300 GPU（圖形處理器，也就是驅動大規模AI工作負載的加速晶片）。Firmus Technologies則在印尼峇淡島（Batam）建置一座輝達所稱的「DSX AI工廠園區」，預計電力容量將達360百萬瓦（MW）——對一座資料中心設施而言是極為龐大的用電規模——並計畫部署多達十七萬張輝達GPU。

  輝達使用「DSX AI工廠」一詞，指稱以特定區域客戶需求為核心、高密度部署GPU的大型設施。輝達表示，這套模式也旨在讓客戶能比傳統建置流程更快取得運算資源，省去選址、電力採購、施工及硬體安裝等環環相扣的繁複步驟。

  Sharon AI共同創辦人暨執行長James Manning表示，這項合作是「Sharon AI實現主權、大規模AI運算基礎設施使命的關鍵時刻」。Firmus Technologies聯席執行長Tim Rosenfield則說：「AI原生企業需要具備擴展性、兼顧能源效率與成本效益的運算基礎設施，才能在全球市場競爭」，並指出峇淡島設施正依循輝達DSX規格打造為AI工廠，協助客戶取得建構與擴展AI所需的運算能力。

  ## 從訓練轉向推論

  輝達表示，這套融資模式反映了當前AI運算需求的集中趨勢。市場重心已從訓練大型AI模型——此階段需要龐大的突發性運算資源——轉向生產推論階段；在推論階段，已部署的模型持續不間斷地運行，以商業規模為客戶提供服務。推論工作負載，加上模型微調（fine-tuning）及大量代理推論（agentic inference，即AI代理人處理大批自動化任務），產生的是對GPU產能穩定且持續的需求，而非間歇性的尖峰需求。

  輝達將模型開發商、推論服務供應商，以及尋求即時雲端存取的企業列為此計畫的主要目標族群。輝達同時點名AI原生企業，並以Baseten、Fireworks AI及Together AI為例，說明這類客群正是推動此需求的核心力量。

  對雲端業者而言，輝達表示，從實驗性部署到商業產品的轉型，使得業者對能隨使用量彈性擴展的運算供應需求大增，同時也需要在從試行專案過渡到全面部署期間保有靈活性——這些都是傳統固定式基礎設施承諾難以輕易滿足的條件。

  ## 對產業的影響

  這套融資架構賦予輝達在AI雲端市場中超越硬體銷售時間點的持續性財務利益。輝達不再只是一次性收款、將部署獲利與否留給客戶自行承擔，而是以分潤雲端營收的方式，將自身利益與服務AI開發者及企業的雲端業者緊密連結。

  對AI雲端業者而言，尤其是缺乏雄厚資產負債表的新興企業，此計畫提供了一條以往難以企及之規模建立產能的替代路徑。

  若Firmus峇淡島園區最終如計畫以十七萬張GPU的規模建成，將成為迄今在此計畫框架下所描述規模最大的AI基礎設施部署案例之一。
