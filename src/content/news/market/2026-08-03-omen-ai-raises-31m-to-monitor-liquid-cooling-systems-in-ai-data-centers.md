body_en: |
  San Francisco startup Omen AI has raised $31 million to deploy continuous sensor monitoring of liquid cooling systems in AI data centers, the company announced. The round brings Omen's total funding to $41.5 million.

  The raise was led by Nava Ventures. Other participants include venture firm CRV, former Facebook chief operating officer Sheryl Sandberg, German filtration manufacturer Mann+Hummel, and executives at AI cloud startup TensorWave, which is also an Omen customer.

  Omen was founded in 2024 by Zach Laberge, who is 20 years old and serves as chief executive. The company employs 30 people, the majority of whom, like Laberge, left college before graduating. Laberge has said the company prioritizes demonstrated ability over academic credentials in hiring.

  ## The problem Omen is targeting

  As AI processors generate more heat than previous generations of chips, data centers have accelerated adoption of liquid cooling — systems that circulate a coolant fluid through or near hardware to carry heat away. That shift creates a new operational risk: coolant can degrade over time through contamination, the accumulation of metal particles shed by pipes and fittings, or bacterial growth.

  Under conventional practice, operators must collect coolant samples and send them to an external laboratory, a process that can take several days to return results. Omen's product replaces that workflow with sensors that analyze coolant condition continuously and in real time, the company said.

  Laberge has described the value of the approach as early detection: catching a coolant problem before it forces an operator to shut down an AI chip cluster and flush and replace degraded fluid. The company says unplanned outages of that kind can cost operators millions of dollars in lost compute time.

  ## Pivot from construction to data centers

  Omen's underlying technology was not originally built for data centers. When the company was founded in 2024, its target market was heavy construction equipment — specifically fluid monitoring for excavators and similar machinery. Laberge subsequently concluded that applying the same fluid-sensing capability to data center cooling systems represented a substantially larger commercial opportunity. The company said its sensors can also be used to analyze fluids in general industrial machinery.

  ## Business model and customers

  Omen sells both permanently installed sensor units and portable devices. It charges on a subscription basis, with pricing tied to the power capacity of the installation, measured in megawatts. One megawatt is roughly enough electricity to power several hundred homes continuously, and in a data center context serves as a proxy for the density of compute equipment requiring cooling.

  The company said it has more than ten customers. Most are neocloud operators — a term for cloud providers that focus specifically on AI workloads — and Omen said it has begun engaging with larger, more established cloud providers.

  ## Use of proceeds

  Omen said it will use the new capital to expand its engineering team and increase the scale of its hardware manufacturing operations.

  舊金山新創公司 Omen AI 宣布完成 3,100 萬美元融資，將用於在 AI 資料中心部署液冷系統的持續感測監控服務，累計募資總額因此達到 4,150 萬美元。

  本輪融資由 Nava Ventures 領投，參與方包括創投公司 CRV、前臉書營運長雪柔．桑德伯格（Sheryl Sandberg）、德國濾材製造商 Mann+Hummel，以及 AI 雲端新創公司 TensorWave 的多位高層主管——TensorWave 同時也是 Omen 的客戶之一。

  Omen 由現年 20 歲的扎克．拉貝爾吉（Zach Laberge）於 2024 年創立，並擔任執行長。公司目前共有 30 名員工，其中大多數人和拉貝爾吉一樣，在取得學位之前便已離開大學。拉貝爾吉曾表示，公司在招募人才時，優先重視實際能力，而非學歷資格。

  ## Omen 瞄準的問題

  隨著 AI 處理器產生的熱量遠超前幾代晶片，資料中心加速採用液冷系統——亦即讓冷卻液循環流過或貼近硬體，藉此帶走熱能的冷卻裝置。這項轉變帶來了新的營運風險：冷卻液可能因污染、管路與接頭脫落的金屬微粒持續積累，或細菌滋生等因素，隨時間逐漸劣化。

  在傳統作業方式下，維運人員必須採集冷卻液樣本並送往外部實驗室化驗，這一過程往往需要數天才能取得結果。Omen 的產品以能夠持續、即時分析冷卻液狀態的感測器，取代了上述繁瑣的作業程序。

  拉貝爾吉將這套方案的核心價值定位於「提早偵測」：在冷卻液劣化迫使維運人員關閉 AI 晶片叢集、清洗並更換劣化冷卻液之前，就及早發現問題。公司表示，此類非計畫性停機可能讓維運商損失數百萬美元的算力時間。

  ## 從建築機械跨足資料中心

  Omen 的核心技術最初並非為資料中心而生。2024 年公司成立之初，目標市場是重型建築機械，具體而言是針對挖土機等設備的液體監控。拉貝爾吉其後研判，將同樣的液體感測能力應用於資料中心冷卻系統，將代表遠更龐大的商業機會。公司表示，其感測器同樣適用於一般工業機械的液體分析。

  ## 商業模式與客戶

  Omen 同時銷售永久安裝型感測器裝置與可攜式設備，並採訂閱制收費，定價依據安裝場地的電力容量（以百萬瓦特計）計算。一百萬瓦特的電力大約可持續供應數百戶家庭用電，在資料中心環境中則作為衡量需要冷卻的運算設備密度的參考指標。

  公司表示目前客戶超過十家，大多數為「新雲端」（neocloud）業者——即專注於 AI 工作負載的雲端服務供應商——Omen 並表示已開始接觸規模更大、更具知名度的傳統雲端服務商。

  ## 資金用途

  Omen 表示，此輪新募資金將用於擴充工程團隊，並擴大硬體製造規模。
