body_en: |
  Malaysia's Ministry of Digital launched an upgraded Rakyat Digital portal on 12 August 2026, pairing the revamped national learning platform with a programme to provide 100,000 young Malaysians three months of free access to generative artificial intelligence applications — the government's largest concrete AI-access commitment to date under its AI Nation by 2030 agenda.

  Minister of Digital Gobind Singh Deo unveiled the platform at the AI Malaysia Takeover 2026 (AIMTO_26) event at The Campus, Ampang. The upgraded portal, developed by the Ministry of Digital and driven by MyDIGITAL Corporation, consolidates multiple digital learning initiatives under a single user account.

  ## Free AI access programme structure

  The centrepiece of the launch is a government-funded generative AI access programme open to Malaysians aged 18 to 30. The programme opens for enrolment immediately at www.rakyatdigital.gov.my and begins 31 August 2026, rolling out in four cohorts of 25,000 participants. The first cohort starts 16 September 2026.

  Access is conditional on completing six learning modules: AI Safety, CyberSAFE for Rakyat, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Cloud for Rakyat, and a new module covering MADANI Malaysia values and principles. The ministry did not specify which generative AI applications are included, describing them as "leading" products subject to applicable terms and conditions.

  ## Platform history and pillars

  Rakyat Digital was first launched in January 2024 as a digital literacy initiative. The ministry said it has since evolved into what it calls Malaysia's Digital Learning Hub. The upgraded portal is structured around three priorities: Awareness, Access, and Adoption.

  Under Awareness, the platform provides free foundational digital and AI learning modules through a Digital Literacy Hub. Under Access, the generative AI programme sits alongside a government-funded Global Learning Pass granting Malaysians entry to more than 16,000 courses and professional certifications on Coursera, the online education platform, sourced from universities and industry partners worldwide.

  ## AI Builders Hub and next steps

  Gobind said the platform will add an AI Builders Hub from October 2026, offering what he described as a hybrid pathway for Malaysians to apply learned skills, experiment with AI, and develop practical solutions. "The aim is to help more Malaysians move from learning and using technology to building solutions that are Made by Malaysia," he said, as quoted by Digital News Asia.

  Separately, Gobind confirmed that a board is being established for AI Malaysia Berhad, the entity launched by Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim on 28 July 2026. A chairman has been appointed along with the chief secretary, the deputy secretary-general of the Ministry of Digital, and two additional board members whose appointments were signed the day before the portal launch. Gobind said a press conference to introduce the board is expected within approximately two weeks.

  ## National targets

  The portal upgrade is framed as a delivery mechanism for two strategic thrusts within the Malaysia Digital 2030 (MD2030) Action Plan, the government's national digital roadmap.

  The first thrust, Digital Talent, targets 80% digital literacy across the national population, the reskilling of 700,000 workers, and the creation of alternative learning pathways and a National Learning Platform. The second thrust, Digital Society, focuses on equitable distribution of digital progress and includes a target of producing 5,000 rural digital entrepreneurs.

  The ministry said the initiative is being implemented through a whole-of-nation approach involving government, industry, academia, and community and talent development partners, with the stated goal of ensuring rural communities, senior citizens, persons with disabilities, and micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) are included.

  ## Significance

  The programme ties AI tool access to demonstrated baseline competency through mandatory module requirements rather than open enrolment, a design that distinguishes it from broader digital voucher schemes. Its phased four-cohort structure limits initial scale while providing a mechanism for the government to manage demand and assess outcomes before successive cohorts begin.

  The October 2026 AI Builders Hub launch will mark the next test of the platform's ambition, moving its stated purpose from awareness and access toward applied AI development by Malaysian participants.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞數位部於2026年8月12日正式推出升級版Rakyat Digital入口網站，同步啟動一項補助計畫，提供10萬名年齡介於18至30歲的馬來西亞年輕人享有三個月免費使用生成式人工智慧應用程式的資格。這是政府迄今在「2030年AI國家」議程下所做出的規模最大具體AI使用承諾。

  數位部長哥賓星（Gobind Singh Deo）在安邦（Ampang）The Campus舉辦的「AI Malaysia Takeover 2026」（AIMTO_26）活動上正式揭幕。升級後的入口網站由數位部主導、MyDIGITAL公司驅動建置，將多項數位學習計畫整合於單一使用者帳號之下。

  ## 免費AI使用計畫架構

  此次發布的核心，是一項由政府資助、面向18至30歲馬來西亞公民的生成式AI使用計畫。計畫即日起於 www.rakyatdigital.gov.my 開放報名，訂於2026年8月31日正式啟動，以每梯次25,000人的規模分四批推進，首梯次將於2026年9月16日展開。

  參與資格以完成六個學習模組為前提，分別為：AI安全（AI Safety）、網路安全（CyberSAFE for Rakyat）、生成式AI（Generative AI）、代理式AI（Agentic AI）、雲端基礎（Cloud for Rakyat），以及一個涵蓋MADANI馬來西亞價值觀與原則的全新模組。數位部未具體說明計畫包含哪些生成式AI應用程式，僅描述為「領先」產品，並受相關條款與條件約束。

  ## 平台沿革與三大支柱

  Rakyat Digital最早於2024年1月以數位素養計畫形式推出，數位部表示，平台此後已演進為馬來西亞的「數位學習樞紐」。升級後的入口網站圍繞三大優先方向建構：認知（Awareness）、取用（Access）與應用（Adoption）。

  在「認知」層面，平台透過數位素養中心提供免費的基礎數位與AI學習模組。在「取用」層面，生成式AI計畫與政府資助的「全球學習通行證」並列推出，後者讓馬來西亞民眾得以進入線上教育平台Coursera，取用來自全球各大學與產業夥伴的逾1萬6,000門課程及專業認證。

  ## AI建造者中心與後續計畫

  哥賓星表示，平台將於2026年10月新增「AI建造者中心」（AI Builders Hub），為馬來西亞民眾提供一條混合式路徑，讓他們得以應用所學技能、實驗AI工具，並發展實用解決方案。「目標是幫助更多馬來西亞人，從學習和使用科技，進一步邁向打造『馬來西亞製造』解決方案的行列，」他接受《Digital News Asia》引述時如此表示。

  另外，哥賓星也確認，AI Malaysia公司（AI Malaysia Berhad）的董事會正在籌組中——該公司由總理安華（Anwar Ibrahim）於2026年7月28日宣布成立。目前已完成董事長任命，連同秘書長、數位部副秘書長與另外兩名董事會成員的遴選，相關任命文件於入口網站發布前一天簽署。哥賓星表示，預計在約兩週內召開記者會，正式介紹董事會成員。

  ## 國家目標

  此次入口網站升級，定位為「馬來西亞數位2030（MD2030）行動計畫」——即政府國家數位路線圖——旗下兩大戰略方向的落實機制。

  第一個方向為「數位人才」，目標涵蓋將全國數位素養普及率提升至80%、完成70萬名勞工的技能再培訓，以及建立替代學習管道與國家學習平台。第二個方向為「數位社會」，聚焦於數位發展成果的公平分配，並設定培育5,000名農村數位創業家的具體目標。

  數位部表示，此計畫以涵蓋政府、產業、學術界及社區與人才發展夥伴的「全國協力」模式推動，明確將農村社區、長者、身心障礙者及微中小企業（MSME）納入受惠範圍。

  ## 政策意義

  此計畫將AI工具使用資格與基本能力認證綁定，透過強制完成模組的設計取代開放式報名，使其有別於一般廣泛性數位補助方案。分批四梯次的結構在初期限制了整體規模，但也為政府提供了管控需求、評估成效的機制，有助於在後續梯次啟動前進行滾動調整。

  2026年10月即將上線的AI建造者中心，將成為該平台的下一個重要驗收節點，標誌其目標從「認知」與「取用」，正式跨入由馬來西亞參與者主導的AI應用開發領域。
