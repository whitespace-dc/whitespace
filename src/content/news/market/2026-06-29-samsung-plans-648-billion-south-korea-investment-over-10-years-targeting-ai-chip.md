body_en: |
  Samsung Group will announce a pledge of 1,000 trillion won ($648 billion) in South Korea over the next decade, according to a report by the Maeil Business Newspaper, with spending targeting AI data centres, chip factories, batteries, and displays as South Korea seeks to convert surging AI-driven memory demand into broader economic development beyond the capital region.

  The announcement is set for Monday, when top executives from Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix — two firms that have recorded large profits as AI drives sustained demand for high-end memory chips — will meet with President Lee Jae Myung to outline investment plans, the newspaper reported Friday without citing sources. The presidential office said it will announce "three mega-projects" covering semiconductors, AI data centres, and robotics, to be outlined jointly by government and industry.

  ## Scale and composition of the pledge

  The 1,000 trillion won figure spans Samsung Group's major business lines. The plan includes a potential 300 trillion won push to build semiconductor fabrication facilities in South Korea's southwestern region, the Maeil Business Newspaper said. Additional spending would go to AI data centres, battery manufacturing, and displays — reflecting the breadth of Samsung Group's industrial portfolio, which includes chip giant Samsung Electronics, battery maker Samsung SDI, and IT services company Samsung SDS. Samsung and SK Hynix declined to comment.

  ## Accelerating timelines driven by AI demand

  South Korea dominates global manufacturing of the high-end memory chips — including the high-bandwidth memory used in AI accelerators — that are critical components in AI data centres. That position has made Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix central beneficiaries of the current AI investment cycle.

  Presidential policy adviser Kim Yong-beom said SK Hynix and Samsung may need to pull forward projects originally planned for the 2040s to the mid-2030s because AI-driven memory demand is growing faster than anticipated, leaving insufficient land, power, and water in the greater Seoul region for further expansion.

  ## The push to redirect investment beyond Seoul

  The proposed geographic shift of investment to South Korea's southwest is the most contested element of the plan. The Seoul metropolitan area accounted for 52.8% of South Korea's gross regional domestic product in 2024. Political pressure to spread industrial capacity has intensified under President Lee, who has promoted a plan for "five regional hubs and three special self-governing provinces" to counterbalance Seoul's economic concentration.

  Local media have reported that Samsung Electronics is considering Gwangju — a southwestern city described in official data as having one of the country's smaller regional economies and below-average per-capita output — as a potential investment site. Lee received approximately 85% of the vote in Gwangju and neighbouring South Jeolla province in the June 2025 presidential election, compared with 49.42% nationally.

  ## Political opposition and conflict-of-interest accusations

  Opposition lawmakers from the People Power Party have accused the administration of directing semiconductor investment to the ruling party's political base ahead of an internal party leadership contest. "Where semiconductor factories are built should be decided by companies, not by the president," PPP spokesperson Park Sung-hoon said. The regional politics of semiconductor siting became a flashpoint ahead of South Korea's June 3 local elections, with candidates across multiple regions pitching their areas as the next chip hub. Proposals reported in local media ranged from a 500 trillion won chip complex in the southwest to expanded clusters elsewhere.

  Lee's approval rating stood at 51% as of late June, its lowest since his June 2025 inauguration, according to Gallup Korea.

  ## Workforce and infrastructure doubts

  Experts have raised doubts about whether South Korea's southwest can support high-volume semiconductor manufacturing. "Securing skilled workers will be extremely difficult in the southwest, and that will determine whether the project succeeds or fails," said Kim Tae-yun, a professor of administration at Hanyang University. Without a genuinely advanced fabrication facility, he said, the regional economic benefit could be limited — "it risks becoming little more than a construction project and a real estate boost."

  Existing chipmaking cities have also expressed concern. Icheon, where SK Hynix operates major plants, is heavily dependent on those operations. "Most of the city's tax revenue comes from SK's chip plant, and our welfare depends on it," said Jo Jun-taek, head of a grassroots group in Icheon, warning that a new southwestern cluster could prompt SK Hynix to reduce output in Icheon and eventually close facilities there.

  ## What the plan signals for South Korea's industrial map

  If realised at the proposed scale, the investment would represent a significant reorientation of South Korea's semiconductor manufacturing geography — concentrating production capacity outside the greater Seoul area on a scale not previously attempted. The initiative also signals that South Korean chipmakers view AI-driven memory demand as durable enough to justify accelerating investment timelines by roughly a decade.

  The presidential office is scheduled to detail the three mega-projects on Monday, when Samsung Group's formal pledge is expected to be made public.
body_zh: |
  根據《每日經濟新聞》（Maeil Business Newspaper）報導，三星集團計畫在未來10年內於南韓投入1,000兆韓元（約6,480億美元），資金將用於AI資料中心、晶片廠、電池及顯示器製造。此一計畫背後的驅動力，是南韓希望將AI帶動的記憶體需求熱潮，轉化為首都圈以外更廣泛的經濟發展動能。

  根據該報週五報導（未引述消息來源），三星電子（Samsung Electronics）與SK海力士（SK Hynix）的高層主管，預計將於週一與總統李在明（Lee Jae-myung）會面，說明各自的投資計畫。兩家公司均因AI持續帶動高端記憶體晶片需求而創下豐厚獲利。總統府表示，將公布涵蓋半導體、AI資料中心及機器人三大領域的「三大巨型計畫」，由政府與產業界聯合宣示。

  ## 投資規模與組成

  此1,000兆韓元的投資計畫橫跨三星集團旗下多條主要業務線。《每日經濟新聞》指出，其中包括一項可能高達300兆韓元的方案，用於在南韓西南地區興建半導體晶圓廠；其餘資金則將投入AI資料中心、電池製造及顯示器，充分反映三星集團龐大的產業版圖——旗下囊括晶片巨頭三星電子、電池製造商三星SDI（Samsung SDI），以及資訊科技服務公司三星SDS（Samsung SDS）。三星與SK海力士均表示不予置評。

  ## AI需求驅動，投資時程提前

  南韓在全球高端記憶體晶片製造居主導地位，包括AI加速器所需的高頻寬記憶體（HBM），這是AI資料中心不可或缺的核心零組件，也使三星電子與SK海力士成為當前AI投資浪潮的最大受益者。

  總統政策顧問金容范（Kim Yong-beom）表示，SK海力士與三星原本規劃於2040年代推進的專案，可能需提前至2030年代中期，原因是AI帶動的記憶體需求成長速度超乎預期，導致大首爾地區的土地、電力與水資源嚴重不足，無法支撐進一步擴張。

  ## 投資重心往首爾以外移動

  將投資重心移往南韓西南部，是整個計畫中爭議最大的一環。2024年，首爾都會圈占南韓區域生產總值的52.8%。在李在明施政下，分散產業重心的政治壓力持續升高——他積極推動「五大地方樞紐與三個特別自治省」計畫，以平衡首爾過度集中的經濟態勢。

  當地媒體報導，三星電子正考慮以西南部城市光州（Gwangju）作為潛在投資地點。官方數據顯示，光州是南韓區域經濟規模較小、人均產出低於全國平均的城市之一。在2025年6月總統大選中，李在明在光州及鄰近全羅南道（South Jeolla province）獲得約85%的選票，遠高於全國49.42%的得票率。

  ## 政治反彈與利益衝突指控

  在野的國民力量黨（People Power Party，PPP）立委批評，執政當局此舉是將半導體投資導向執政黨的政治票倉，且時間點恰在黨內領導層改選之前。「晶圓廠該蓋在哪，應由企業決定，而不是總統，」國民力量黨發言人朴成勛（Park Sung-hoon）說。半導體廠選址的區域政治角力，早在南韓6月3日地方選舉前便已引燃，各地候選人紛紛力推自己的選區是下一座晶片聚落的首選。據當地媒體報導，相關提案從西南地區500兆韓元晶片園區，到其他地區的擴建計畫，不一而足。

  截至6月下旬，李在明的支持率為51%，是他2025年6月就任以來的最低點，數據來自蓋洛普韓國（Gallup Korea）。

  ## 人力與基礎建設的隱憂

  專家對西南地區能否支撐大規模晶片製造表示懷疑。「在西南部羅致技術熟練的勞動力將極為困難，而這正是計畫成敗的關鍵，」漢陽大學（Hanyang University）行政學教授金泰允（Kim Tae-yun）表示。他指出，若缺乏真正先進的晶圓廠，對當地經濟的實質效益可能相當有限——「恐怕淪為一個建設工程加上房地產行情的炒作。」

  現有晶片城市同樣憂心忡忡。SK海力士在利川（Icheon）設有主要廠區，該市高度依賴這些廠房的運作。「市府大部分稅收來自SK的晶片廠，我們的福祉仰賴於此，」利川市民草根團體負責人趙俊澤（Jo Jun-taek）警告，一旦西南部出現新的晶片聚落，可能促使SK海力士縮減在利川的產能，最終關閉當地設施。

  ## 計畫若落實，南韓產業版圖將大幅重塑

  若能依計畫規模付諸實現，這筆投資將代表南韓半導體製造版圖的重大重塑——以前所未有的規模，將大量產能集中於首都圈以外。這項計畫也釋出一個明確訊號：南韓晶片製造商認為AI帶動的記憶體需求具有長期支撐力，足以支持將投資時程提前約十年的決策。

  總統府預計於週一公布三大巨型計畫詳情，屆時三星集團的正式投資承諾也將對外公開。
