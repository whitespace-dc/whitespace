body_en: |
  Indonesia's Deputy Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs, Dradjad Hari Wibowo, said Tuesday that data center operators investing in Batam must provide their own seawater desalination and electricity infrastructure, warning that industrial demand should not compete with household supply.

  Dradjad made the remarks during a visit to Batam on the evening of November 10, 2026, as officials assessed the island's investment climate. His comments come as Batam, located in Riau Islands province near Singapore, draws growing interest from data center developers.

  ## Water and power requirements for data centers

  On water supply, Dradjad said data center operators are required to install their own SWRO — seawater reverse osmosis — facilities, a technology that converts saltwater into freshwater suitable for industrial cooling and operations. He acknowledged that some data center projects are currently drawing on government-managed water sources, but said local authorities and the Batam Business Zone Authority, known by its Indonesian acronym BP Batam, have conducted calculations to ensure industrial demand does not reduce service to residents.

  A SWRO facility capable of serving the data center industry is already under development at a reported cost of 600 billion Indonesian rupiah, according to a related report cited in the source article. Dradjad said projections provided to him indicate that once the SWRO facility becomes operational in 2027, Batam's total water availability could reach a surplus of nearly 500 liters per second.

  On electricity, Dradjad drew a distinction between industrial and household tariff structures. Data centers, he said, draw from dedicated industrial power supplies and pay rates above the economically subsidized tariff charged to households, which means their consumption should not reduce the electricity available to residential customers. He nonetheless stated that operators must have their own power sources.

  ## Investment climate and governance changes

  Dradjad described Batam's investment environment as significantly improved, citing the resolution of a long-running jurisdictional overlap between BP Batam and the Batam City Government. The end of that dual-authority structure, he said, has simplified institutional arrangements and accelerated permitting. He added that Batam is "on the right track" despite consumer price inflation running at around 3 percent.

  ## Significance for regional data center development

  The minister's remarks set out an explicit policy position: data center investors in Batam are expected to internalize infrastructure costs for water and power rather than rely on public utility networks. The 2027 target date for the SWRO facility coming online represents the nearest-term milestone that would shift Batam's water balance from constrained to surplus, according to the projections Dradjad cited.
body_zh: |
  印尼經濟統籌部副部長Dradjad Hari Wibowo週二表示，在峇淡島投資的資料中心業者必須自行建置海水淡化及電力基礎設施，警告工業需求不應與民生供應相互競爭。

  Dradjad於2026年11月10日夜間赴峇淡島視察時發表上述談話，當時官員正在評估該島的投資環境。峇淡島位於廖內群島省，鄰近新加坡，近年吸引愈來愈多資料中心開發商前往布局。

  ## 資料中心的水電自備要求

  在水源方面，Dradjad表示資料中心業者必須自行安裝海水逆滲透（SWRO）設施——這項技術能將海水轉化為適合工業冷卻與作業的淡水。他坦承，目前部分資料中心專案仍在使用政府管理的水源，但表示地方當局與峇淡島工商特區管理局（即BP Batam）已完成試算，確保工業用水需求不會壓縮居民的供水服務。

  根據原報導引用的相關資料，一座足以供應資料中心產業所需的SWRO設施目前正在興建，預計造價達6,000億印尼盾。Dradjad表示，提供給他的預測數據顯示，一旦該SWRO設施於2027年正式啟用，峇淡島的總水資源供應量有望出現每秒近500公升的盈餘。

  在電力方面，Dradjad區分了工業用電與民生用電的費率結構。他指出，資料中心使用的是專屬工業電力，所支付的費率高於政府補貼的民生優惠費率，因此其用電量理應不會影響住宅用戶的電力供應。儘管如此，他仍明確要求業者必須擁有自備電源。

  ## 投資環境與治理改革

  Dradjad形容峇淡島的投資環境已大幅改善，主因是長期困擾當地的BP Batam與峇淡市政府雙重管轄重疊問題獲得解決。他表示，此一雙重管轄架構的終結簡化了制度安排、加速了審批流程。他補充道，儘管消費者物價通膨率約在3%左右，峇淡島仍「走在正確的軌道上」。

  ## 對區域資料中心發展的意義

  這位部長的談話確立了明確的政策立場：在峇淡島投資的資料中心業者，預期須將水電基礎設施的成本內部化，不得倚賴公共事業網路。按照Dradjad引用的預測數據，SWRO設施預計於2027年投入運作，這是最近的一個重要里程碑，屆時峇淡島的水資源狀況將從吃緊轉為充裕。
