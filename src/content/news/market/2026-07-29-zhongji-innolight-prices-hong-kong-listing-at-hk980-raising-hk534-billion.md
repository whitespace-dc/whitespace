  Chinese optical parts maker Zhongji Innolight priced its Hong Kong listing at HK$980 per H share on July 28, raising HK$53.41 billion (S$8.8 billion) through the sale of 54.5 million Hong Kong shares, the company said. The deal ranks as Asia's second-largest share offering in 2026 and is set to become Hong Kong's biggest share sale in nearly seven years.

  The final offer price came in below the maximum of HK$1,010 set when the deal launched last week. Shares are scheduled to begin trading on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 30.

  ## Scale and market context

  The listing is Hong Kong's largest since Alibaba raised US$12.9 billion (S$16.7 billion) in a secondary listing in 2019, according to data from financial data firm LSEG. Across Asia in 2026, the deal trails only the US$8.6 billion Shanghai initial public offering by Chinese memory chipmaker CXMT, which saw its shares surge 466 percent on their first trading day on July 27.

  Zhongji Innolight is already listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. The Hong Kong offering adds a second listing venue and a new pool of international capital.

  ## What the company makes and who buys it

  Zhongji Innolight manufactures optical transceivers, devices that convert electrical signals into light pulses to enable high-speed data transmission through fibre-optic cables. The components are widely used in data centres, cloud computing networks, and artificial intelligence systems.

  The company generated 61.7 percent of its revenue from the United States in the first quarter of 2026, its regulatory filings showed.

  ## Financial performance

  First-quarter net profit nearly quadrupled year-on-year to 6.32 billion yuan (S$1.2 billion), up from 1.69 billion yuan in the same period a year earlier. Revenue nearly tripled to 19.5 billion yuan from 6.67 billion yuan over the same period. The company attributed the growth to stronger demand from major customers investing in AI infrastructure.

  ## Use of proceeds

  Zhongji Innolight said it will use proceeds from the listing for research and development, global manufacturing expansion, supply chain upgrades, acquisitions, and general working capital.

  ## Regulatory risk and the US market

  In June, the US Department of Defense placed Zhongji Innolight on its list of "Chinese military companies." The company has said that designation does not, by itself, restrict business with US customers or trading in its securities. The company's continued US revenue dependence — at 61.7 percent of first-quarter sales — makes the designation a watchpoint for investors given the potential for further US government action.

  ## Broader significance

  The listing arrives as Chinese technology companies accelerate fundraising in Hong Kong's equity market. It also coincides with China's stated effort to develop domestic AI capabilities as US-led export controls restrict Chinese firms' access to advanced semiconductors. Optical transceivers sit at a layer of the AI supply chain that remains less restricted than the chips themselves, which has supported demand for Zhongji Innolight's products. At the same time, the offer price landing below its ceiling reflects what the source described as investor caution amid volatility in global chip-related stocks.

  Zhongji Innolight shares are due to begin trading in Hong Kong on July 30.

  ## 中際旭創香港掛牌定價每股980港元，募資534億港元

  中國光學零組件大廠中際旭創於7月28日確定香港H股發行價，每股定價980港元，透過發售5,450萬股香港股份，合計募資534.1億港元（約88億新加坡元）。此次交易規模躋身2026年亞洲第二大股票發行案，並有望成為香港近七年來最大規模的股票上市案。

  最終定價低於上週啟動募股時所設定的每股1,010港元上限。中際旭創股票預計於7月30日在香港交易所正式掛牌交易。

  ## 規模與市場背景

  根據金融數據機構LSEG的資料，此次上市是香港自2019年阿里巴巴完成次級上市、募資129億美元（約167億新加坡元）以來規模最大的股票發行案。在2026年亞洲市場中，中際旭創此次募資規模僅次於中國記憶體晶片製造商長鑫存儲（CXMT）在上海的首次公開募股——後者募資86億美元，並於7月27日首日掛牌即大漲466%。

  中際旭創目前已在深圳證券交易所上市，此次赴港掛牌為其增添第二個上市地點，同時開拓新的國際資本來源。

  ## 公司產品與客戶

  中際旭創主要製造光收發模組，這類元件能將電訊號轉換為光脈衝，使資料得以透過光纖纜線進行高速傳輸。相關產品廣泛應用於資料中心、雲端運算網路及人工智慧系統。

  根據公司向監管機構提交的文件，2026年第一季中際旭創有61.7%的營收來自美國市場。

  ## 財務表現

  2026年第一季，中際旭創淨利潤較去年同期大幅成長近四倍，達到63.2億人民幣（約12億新加坡元），高於去年同期的16.9億人民幣。同期營收亦近乎三倍增長，從66.7億人民幣攀升至195億人民幣。公司將此一強勁成長歸因於主要客戶持續加碼投資AI基礎設施所帶動的旺盛需求。

  ## 募資用途

  中際旭創表示，本次上市所得款項將用於研發投入、全球製造產能擴張、供應鏈升級、併購活動及一般營運資金。

  ## 法規風險與美國市場

  今年6月，美國國防部將中際旭創列入「中國軍事企業」名單。對此，公司表示該項認定本身並不直接限制其與美國客戶的業務往來，亦不影響其證券交易。然而，中際旭創高達61.7%的第一季營收仍來自美國市場，在美國政府可能採取進一步行動的背景下，這一認定勢必成為投資人持續關注的風險指標。

  ## 更深遠的意義

  此次上市恰逢中國科技企業加速赴港籌資之際，同時也與中國積極推動發展本土AI能力的國家戰略不謀而合——後者部分源自美國主導的出口管制措施限縮了中國企業取得先進半導體的管道。光收發模組所處的AI供應鏈層次，目前受到的出口管制限制相對少於晶片本身，這也支撐了外界對中際旭創產品的持續需求。與此同時，最終定價未能觸及上限，反映出投資人在全球晶片相關股票持續震盪的環境下普遍抱持審慎態度。

  中際旭創股票預計於7月30日在香港正式掛牌交易。
