  ## Keppel and Alpha DC Fund to develop first greenfield data center in Malaysia's Johor

  Alpha Data Centre Fund (Alpha DC Fund) and Keppel Data Centres Holding have agreed to develop a high-availability, build-to-suit (BTS) data center in an industrial park in Johor, Malaysia, in what the parties describe as their maiden greenfield project in the country.

  The facility's customer has not been named publicly, identified only as a "leading technology company." Financial terms were not disclosed.

  ## The parties and their relationship

  Alpha DC Fund is managed by Alpha Investment Partners, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Keppel Corp's asset management arm. Keppel Data Centres is the data centre division of Keppel Corp, making the two entities affiliates operating under the same parent group.

  A build-to-suit arrangement means the facility is designed and constructed to meet a specific tenant's technical and operational requirements, rather than developed speculatively for the open market. High-availability is an industry term for infrastructure engineered to minimize downtime, typically through redundant power, cooling, and network systems.

  ## Significance

  The project marks the first greenfield — that is, newly constructed from undeveloped land, as opposed to acquiring or expanding an existing facility — data center that Alpha DC Fund and Keppel Data Centres have pursued together in Malaysia. Johor, which borders Singapore, has emerged as a location for data center investment given land availability and proximity to the city-state's connectivity infrastructure.

  No timeline for completion or capacity figures were disclosed at announcement.

  ## 吉寶與Alpha DC基金攜手 於柔佛開發馬來西亞首座綠地資料中心

  Alpha Data Centre基金（Alpha DC基金）與吉寶資料中心控股已達成協議，將在馬來西亞柔佛州一處工業園區內合作開發一座高可用性、量身定制（BTS）資料中心。雙方將此項目定位為其在馬來西亞的第一個綠地開發案。

  該設施的客戶目前尚未公開揭露，僅被描述為「一家領先的科技公司」。財務條款亦未對外披露。

  ## 雙方關係與背景

  Alpha DC基金由Alpha Investment Partners負責管理，而Alpha Investment Partners是吉寶集團資產管理部門的全資子公司。吉寶資料中心則是吉寶集團旗下的資料中心業務部門，因此兩者實為同一母集團下的關聯機構。

  所謂量身定制（BTS）安排，是指依照特定租戶的技術與營運需求來設計並興建設施，而非以投機方式為開放市場開發。高可用性則是業界術語，指基礎設施透過備援電力、冷卻及網路系統等設計，將停機時間降至最低。

  ## 項目意義

  此案是Alpha DC基金與吉寶資料中心在馬來西亞首度共同推進的綠地開發項目——所謂綠地，即指在未開發土地上從頭興建的全新設施，有別於收購或擴建現有設施。柔佛州與新加坡接壤，近年來因土地供應充裕，且毗鄰新加坡的連接基礎設施，已逐漸成為吸引資料中心投資的熱門地點。

  此次宣布時，雙方並未披露任何完工時程或容量數據。
