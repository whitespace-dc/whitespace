body_en: |
  The United States has placed its Pax Silica initiatives in the Philippines at the top of its economic priority list, Philippine Finance Secretary Frederick D. Go said following a meeting with US Ambassador Lee Lipton on July 9, raising the profile of a program Washington and Manila hope will generate large-scale employment and position the country as a hub for artificial intelligence and semiconductor manufacturing.

  Go told reporters that Lipton conveyed Washington's intent to accelerate Pax Silica-related developments in the Philippines. "He expressed that on the economic side, the Pax Silica project is on top of the priority list of the Americans," Go said. He quoted the ambassador's framing as a speed imperative: "If we're going to do this, we might as well do it as fast as possible so that we can provide jobs and be a significant player in the technology of the future."

  The Finance Secretary said the Philippines' participation is expected to produce "hundreds and thousands of jobs" for Filipino workers, though he did not provide a specific figure or timeline for job creation.

  ## What Pax Silica is

  Pax Silica is a US-led multilateral initiative aimed at securing global AI supply chains and countering China's expanding role in technology manufacturing. The Philippines joined in April as one of 23 signatories. Other members include Argentina, Australia, Chile, Costa Rica, El Salvador, the European Union, Finland, Germany, Greece, India, Israel, Japan, Kazakhstan, the Netherlands, Norway, Panama, Qatar, South Korea, Singapore, Sweden, the United Arab Emirates, and the United Kingdom.

  ## The New Clark City hub

  The centerpiece of the Philippines' participation is a 1,618-hectare — roughly 4,000 acres — AI-native development in New Clark City in Tarlac province, north of Manila. Manila and Washington are jointly developing the site to attract investment in AI infrastructure and chip manufacturing. Go described the project as potentially "a generational type of project," adding that establishing the ecosystem could catalyze further investment: "Once you have the ecosystem, one thing leads to another."

  ## Framework agreement targeted for 2026

  Go has previously said the Philippines is working to sign a formal framework agreement under Pax Silica before the end of this year. No date has been set publicly, and no details on the agreement's structure were disclosed following the July 9 meeting.

  The US designation of Philippines Pax Silica work as a top economic priority adds diplomatic weight to a project that, if completed at the scale described, would represent one of the largest planned technology infrastructure investments in Southeast Asia. For Philippine policymakers, success would depend on translating the stated US urgency into finalized agreements, committed capital, and a functioning framework for chip and AI sector development on the Tarlac site.

  The Philippines is targeting a Pax Silica framework agreement by the end of 2026.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓財政部長郭弗德瑞克在與美國駐菲大使李普頓於7月9日會面後表示，美國已將Pax Silica計畫在菲律賓的相關推動工作列為最高經濟優先要務。此舉大幅提升了這項計畫的政治能見度——美菲兩國政府期望藉此創造大規模就業機會，並將菲律賓打造為人工智慧與半導體製造的重要樞紐。

  郭部長向媒體表示，大使李普頓已轉達華府加速在菲律賓推進Pax Silica相關發展的意圖。「他表示，就經濟層面而言，Pax Silica計畫是美方優先清單上的首要項目，」郭部長說道。他引述大使的說法，強調速度的迫切性：「如果我們要推動這件事，就應該盡可能快速推進，這樣才能創造就業機會，並在未來的科技領域成為舉足輕重的參與者。」

  郭部長表示，菲律賓參與此計畫預計將為菲律賓勞工創造「數十萬個」就業機會，但他並未提供具體數字或就業創造的時程。

  ## Pax Silica計畫簡介

  Pax Silica是一項由美國主導的多邊倡議，旨在確保全球人工智慧供應鏈的安全，並抗衡中國在科技製造領域日益擴張的影響力。菲律賓於今年4月加入，成為23個簽署方之一。其他成員包括：阿根廷、澳洲、智利、哥斯大黎加、薩爾瓦多、歐盟、芬蘭、德國、希臘、印度、以色列、日本、哈薩克、荷蘭、挪威、巴拿馬、卡達、南韓、新加坡、瑞典、阿拉伯聯合大公國及英國。

  ## 新克拉克市開發計畫

  菲律賓參與Pax Silica計畫的核心，是位於馬尼拉以北塔拉克省（Tarlac）新克拉克市（New Clark City）的一塊面積達1,618公頃（約4,000英畝）的「AI原生」開發基地。菲美兩國正聯手開發該基地，以吸引人工智慧基礎設施及晶片製造領域的投資。郭部長將此計畫形容為潛在的「跨世代型計畫」，並補充說明，建立完整的產業生態系將帶動更多後續投資：「一旦生態系建立起來，就會產生連鎖效應，一件事接著一件事發展下去。」

  ## 目標於2026年簽署框架協議

  郭部長此前曾表示，菲律賓正積極爭取在今年底前簽署Pax Silica正式框架協議。目前尚未公開訂定簽署日期，7月9日會談後也未披露任何關於協議架構的細節。

  美國將菲律賓Pax Silica工作列為最高經濟優先要務，為這項計畫增添了重要的外交分量。若此計畫能按所述規模完成，將成為東南亞有史以來規模最大的科技基礎設施投資計畫之一。對菲律賓決策者而言，成功的關鍵在於能否將美方所表達的迫切性，轉化為最終簽署的協議、確實到位的資本，以及在塔拉克基地建立運作完善的晶片與人工智慧產業發展框架。

  菲律賓的目標，是在2026年底前完成Pax Silica框架協議的簽署。
