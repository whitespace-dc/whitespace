body_en: |
  Investors have expressed interest in building approximately 1.3 gigawatts (GW) of new data center capacity in Indonesia at an estimated cost of $15 billion to $20 billion, Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto said on July 10, a pipeline that would more than double the country's current installed base of 580 megawatts (MW).

  Airlangga made the remarks in Jakarta, as quoted by national news agency Antara. He said the $15–20 billion investment range is tied specifically to projects in Batam that are "already in the pipeline."

  ## Investors and locations

  Among the parties that have expressed interest, Airlangga named U.S. technology company Nvidia, which he said plans to work with an Australian partner. PT Telkom Indonesia is also set to invest directly in the sector, he said.

  In Karawang, Airlangga said "almost all Big Tech" companies are preparing to expand data center development. He did not identify specific firms at that location.

  ## DAMAC Digital's separate pipeline

  Susiwijono Moegiarso, secretary of the Coordinating Ministry for Economic Affairs, had previously disclosed that UAE-based DAMAC Digital is preparing to build a data center in Indonesia with capacity of approximately 1.2 GW to 1.3 GW. Susiwijono said that volume represents roughly double the country's current total national data center capacity, which he estimated at approximately 600 MW.

  ## Workforce development

  The government has partnered with Arm Ltd., a UK-based semiconductor company, with a target of bringing approximately 15,000 engineers into the Arm ecosystem, Airlangga said. He described the collaboration as part of a deliberate upstream strategy for Indonesia's digital industry — distinct from, and complementary to, a separate government policy focused on downstream mineral processing. Arm is also a partner of Nvidia, he noted.

  "The key to all of this is human resources," Airlangga said.

  ## AI as the demand driver

  Airlangga attributed the surge in data center investment to rapid growth in artificial intelligence (AI) applications, saying data centers are "becoming key for AI."

  ## Significance

  If the prospective projects are built, Indonesia's installed data center capacity would rise from approximately 580 MW to more than 1.8 GW — a more than threefold increase from the current base. The government has set a target of approximately 15,000 engineers through the Arm partnership as part of the human capital base it says is required to support that expansion.
body_zh: |
  印尼統籌經濟事務部長艾爾朗加·哈爾達托（Airlangga Hartarto）7月10日表示，已有投資者有意在印尼新建約1.3 GW的資料中心容量，預計投資金額達150億至200億美元，若全數落地，將使印尼目前580 MW的裝置容量增加逾一倍。

  艾爾朗加在雅加達發表上述談話，印尼國家通訊社安塔拉（Antara）隨後予以報導。他表示，150億至200億美元的投資金額，指的是已「進入開發管線」的巴淡島（Batam）相關計畫。

  ## 投資者與建設地點

  艾爾朗加點名了部分有意投資的企業，包括美國科技公司輝達（Nvidia）——他表示輝達計畫與一家澳洲合作夥伴共同推進相關建設。印尼國營電信業者PT Telkom Indonesia也將直接投入該領域。

  在卡拉旺（Karawang），艾爾朗加表示「幾乎所有大型科技公司」都正籌備擴建資料中心，但未具體點名在該地點布局的企業。

  ## DAMAC Digital的獨立開發計畫

  印尼統籌經濟事務部秘書長蘇西維喬諾·莫吉亞索（Susiwijono Moegiarso）此前已披露，總部位於阿聯酋的DAMAC Digital正籌備在印尼興建容量約1.2 GW至1.3 GW的資料中心。蘇西維喬諾表示，這一規模約相當於印尼目前全國資料中心總容量的兩倍，他估計後者目前約為600 MW。

  ## 人才培育

  艾爾朗加表示，印尼政府已與英國半導體公司安謀（Arm Ltd.）攜手合作，目標是將約1萬5,000名工程師納入Arm生態系。他將此次合作定位為印尼數位產業刻意布局的上游策略，有別於政府另一項聚焦於礦物下游加工的政策，兩者相輔相成。艾爾朗加也提到，安謀同時是輝達的合作夥伴。

  「這一切的關鍵在於人才，」艾爾朗加說。

  ## 人工智慧帶動需求

  艾爾朗加將資料中心投資熱潮歸因於人工智慧（AI）應用的快速成長，並表示資料中心「正成為AI的核心基礎」。

  ## 意義

  若上述潛在計畫均付諸實現，印尼的資料中心裝置容量將從目前約580 MW躍升至逾1.8 GW，相較於現有基礎增加逾三倍。政府透過與安謀的合作，設定了培育約1萬5,000名工程師的目標，視其為支撐上述大規模擴張所需人力資本基礎的重要一環。
