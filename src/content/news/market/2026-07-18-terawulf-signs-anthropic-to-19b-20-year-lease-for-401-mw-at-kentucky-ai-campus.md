body_en: |
  TeraWulf has signed Anthropic, the AI company behind the Claude family of large language models, to a 20-year, $19 billion lease covering approximately 401 megawatts (MW) of critical IT capacity at its Justified Data campus in Hawesville, Kentucky — one of the largest dedicated AI infrastructure lease agreements announced to date.

  The deal, signed simultaneously with the sale of TeraWulf's majority interest in a Texas joint venture, marks a deliberate reorientation of the company's AI infrastructure portfolio toward assets it owns and operates directly.

  ## Lease Terms and Timeline

  The Anthropic agreement is expected to generate approximately $19 billion in contracted revenue over its initial 20-year term. Initial capacity is scheduled to enter service during the second half of 2027, with the full 401-MW deployment expected to be online by early 2028. Anthropic's payment obligations are expected to receive investment-grade credit support, according to TeraWulf. The lease includes two successive five-year renewal options, potentially extending Anthropic's occupancy to 30 years. Rent will begin as each portion of the leased premises is delivered.

  The 401 MW figure refers to critical IT load — the power drawn directly by servers, networking and cooling infrastructure serving computing workloads — out of a gross site capacity of approximately 480 MW.

  The lease represents a rapid commercial outcome. TeraWulf acquired the Hawesville site only in February 2026 and has now committed the campus's full available capacity to a single anchor customer.

  ## A Former Aluminum Smelter Converted to AI Campus

  Justified Data is being developed on the site of the former Century Aluminum smelter in Hawesville, a small Ohio River community in western Kentucky. The industrial plant had reportedly consumed approximately 480 MW continuously during its operating life, leaving behind the high-voltage transmission connections and substation infrastructure that a large-scale AI deployment requires.

  TeraWulf acquired the property in February 2026, paying $200 million in cash and granting the seller a 6.8% non-dilutive minority interest in the subsidiary established to develop the campus. Including the estimated value of that interest and transaction costs, TeraWulf reported total acquisition consideration of approximately $301.9 million. The property includes more than 250 buildable acres, multiple high-voltage transmission lines, an energized onsite substation and direct access to the regional transmission system.

  ## The Abernathy Sale

  To fund the strategic shift, TeraWulf is selling its 50.1% interest in the Abernathy data center joint venture near Abernathy, Texas, to an investor group led by Fluidstack, TeraWulf's partner in the project.

  TeraWulf and Fluidstack established the Abernathy venture in 2025 to develop a 168-MW critical IT load campus on approximately 120 acres, with a total utility power requirement of approximately 240 MW. Fluidstack committed to a 25-year lease at the campus, with Google providing approximately $1.3 billion in credit support for Fluidstack's obligations. The joint venture subsequently issued $1.3 billion in senior secured notes to support construction. The Abernathy agreements were projected to produce approximately $9.5 billion in contracted revenue for the venture over the initial 25-year term. Construction has been advancing toward delivery during the second half of 2026.

  TeraWulf agreed to sell its interest for approximately $530 million, compared with its $450 million investment, with consideration to be paid in three installments through April 2027. TeraWulf said proceeds are expected to support investment in infrastructure opportunities it intends to own and operate directly.

  ## A Shift Toward Direct Ownership

  Paul Prager, TeraWulf's chairman and chief executive officer, framed the paired transactions as a strategic move toward infrastructure platforms where TeraWulf controls the assets, operations and customer relationships directly. He said the company's strategy is centered on owning and operating critical infrastructure, maintaining direct relationships with customers and controlling the long-term evolution of its campuses.

  The Abernathy sale does not sever the TeraWulf-Fluidstack relationship. Fluidstack remains a tenant at TeraWulf's Lake Mariner campus in Barker, New York, and the two companies have built a pipeline of AI infrastructure projects together. At Justified Data, however, TeraWulf is maintaining a direct customer relationship with Anthropic rather than routing it through a joint venture structure.

  The transaction structure also illustrates a capital-recycling model: TeraWulf originated the Abernathy project, helped secure a customer and financing structure, advanced construction and is now monetizing its interest before the campus reaches full operations — with proceeds being redirected toward sites such as Justified Data where the company intends to retain direct ownership.

  ## A Layered Network of AI Infrastructure Relationships

  The transactions illustrate what the source describes as an increasingly complicated network of relationships connecting AI model companies, neocloud providers, data center developers, infrastructure investors and large technology companies.

  Fluidstack is both a tenant and development partner at several TeraWulf projects. Google has provided financial backing for portions of Fluidstack's infrastructure commitments and has received an equity interest in TeraWulf through warrants associated with those arrangements. Anthropic has separately identified Fluidstack as a development partner for other U.S. infrastructure investments — including purpose-built data centers in Texas and New York, announced as part of Anthropic's stated plan to invest $50 billion in American computing infrastructure. At Justified Data, however, the arrangement is a direct lease between Anthropic and TeraWulf.

  ## Brownfield Reuse as a Development Strategy

  The Justified Data campus illustrates an approach in which former energy-intensive industrial sites can be converted into high-density computing campuses without the years-long delays associated with building new transmission facilities. The Hawesville site's historical power consumption of approximately 480 MW provided the electrical backbone required for a large AI deployment before TeraWulf began development work.

  TeraWulf acquired the property for total consideration of approximately $301.9 million and has secured $19 billion in contracted lease revenue from a single anchor tenant. Initial delivery is targeted for the second half of 2027, with the full ramp to 401 MW expected by early 2028.
body_zh: |
  TeraWulf已與Claude系列大型語言模型背後的AI公司Anthropic，簽署一份為期20年、金額達190億美元的租約，涵蓋其位於肯塔基州霍斯維爾（Hawesville）Justified Data園區約401百萬瓦（MW）的關鍵IT算力——這是迄今宣布規模最大的專屬AI基礎設施租賃協議之一。

  這份協議與TeraWulf出售德州合資企業多數股權的交易同步簽署，標誌著該公司AI基礎設施投資組合的策略性轉向——聚焦於自主持有並直接營運的資產。

  ## 租約條款與時程

  此次Anthropic協議預計在初始20年期間帶來約190億美元的合約收入。初期算力預計於2027年下半年投入服務，全部401 MW的部署預計於2028年初完成上線。據TeraWulf表示，Anthropic的付款義務預計將獲得投資等級的信用支持。租約包含兩次各五年的續約選擇權，可能將Anthropic的使用期限延伸至30年。租金將自各期租賃空間交付時起算。

  所謂401 MW，係指關鍵IT負載——即直接由伺服器、網路設備及為運算工作負載服務的冷卻基礎設施所耗用的電力——佔園區總容量約480 MW的部分。

  這份租約展現了快速的商業成果。TeraWulf僅於2026年2月才取得霍斯維爾場址，如今已將園區全部可用算力承諾給單一主力客戶。

  ## 前鋁冶煉廠改建為AI園區

  Justified Data正在肯塔基州西部俄亥俄河畔小社區霍斯維爾的前世紀鋁業（Century Aluminum）冶煉廠舊址上開發。據報導，這座工業廠房在其運營期間曾持續耗電約480 MW，遺留下大規模AI部署所需的高壓輸電連線及變電所基礎設施。

  TeraWulf於2026年2月收購該物業，支付2億美元現金，並授予賣方在負責開發園區的子公司中6.8%的非稀釋性少數股權。計入該股權的估計價值及交易成本，TeraWulf公告的收購總代價約為3億190萬美元。該物業包含超過250英畝的可建設用地、多條高壓輸電線路、一座已通電的場內變電所，以及直接接入區域輸電系統的管道。

  ## 出售Abernathy股權

  為支應這項策略轉型，TeraWulf正將其在德州阿伯納西（Abernathy）附近Abernathy資料中心合資企業50.1%的股權，出售給由TeraWulf在該項目的夥伴Fluidstack領導的投資者集團。

  TeraWulf與Fluidstack於2025年成立Abernathy合資企業，計畫在約120英畝土地上開發一座168 MW關鍵IT負載園區，總電力需求約為240 MW。Fluidstack承諾在該園區簽訂25年租約，Google則為Fluidstack的義務提供約13億美元的信用支持。合資企業隨後發行13億美元優先擔保票據以支持建設工程。Abernathy協議預計在初始25年期間為合資企業帶來約95億美元的合約收入。建設工程持續推進，預計於2026年下半年交付。

  TeraWulf同意以約5億3,000萬美元出售其股權，相較於其4億5,000萬美元的投資有所獲利，款項將分三期支付至2027年4月。TeraWulf表示，所得款項預計用於支持該公司打算自主持有並直接營運的基礎設施投資機會。

  ## 轉向直接持有的策略布局

  TeraWulf董事長兼執行長保羅·普雷格（Paul Prager）將這兩筆配套交易定位為一項策略行動，目標是打造TeraWulf能直接掌控資產、營運及客戶關係的基礎設施平台。他表示，公司的策略以持有並營運關鍵基礎設施、與客戶維持直接關係、掌控園區長期發展方向為核心。

  出售Abernathy股權並不代表TeraWulf與Fluidstack關係的終結。Fluidstack仍是TeraWulf位於紐約州巴克（Barker）的Lake Mariner園區的租戶，兩家公司也共同建立了一條AI基礎設施專案管線。然而在Justified Data，TeraWulf選擇與Anthropic維持直接的客戶關係，而非透過合資企業架構來處理。

  這項交易結構也展示了一種資本循環模式：TeraWulf發起Abernathy項目、協助確保客戶與融資架構、推進建設工程，如今在園區達到完整營運之前便將股權變現——並將所得款項重新導向Justified Data等TeraWulf打算保留直接所有權的場址。

  ## 層疊交織的AI基礎設施關係網絡

  這些交易呈現出一個日益複雜的關係網絡，將AI模型公司、新興雲端服務供應商、資料中心開發商、基礎設施投資者與大型科技公司串聯在一起。

  Fluidstack在多個TeraWulf項目中既是租戶，也是開發夥伴。Google為Fluidstack基礎設施承諾的部分提供了財務支持，並透過與這些安排相關的認股權證取得了TeraWulf的股權。Anthropic另已將Fluidstack列為其他美國基礎設施投資的開發夥伴——包括在德州及紐約州興建的專屬資料中心，這是Anthropic宣稱投資500億美元於美國算力基礎設施計畫的一部分。然而在Justified Data，雙方的安排是Anthropic與TeraWulf之間的直接租約。

  ## 舊工業地再利用作為開發策略

  Justified Data園區展示了一種將前高耗能工業場址轉型為高密度運算園區的方法，得以規避新建輸電設施所需動輒數年的漫長等待。霍斯維爾場址歷史上約480 MW的用電規模，在TeraWulf展開開發工作之前便已奠定大規模AI部署所需的電力骨幹。

  TeraWulf以約3億190萬美元的總代價收購該物業，並已從單一主力租戶處確保190億美元的合約租賃收入。初期交付目標為2027年下半年，全面提升至401 MW則預計於2028年初完成。
