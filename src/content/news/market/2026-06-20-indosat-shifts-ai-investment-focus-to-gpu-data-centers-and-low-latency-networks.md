body_en: |
  PT Indosat Tbk. (ticker: ISAT), Indonesia's publicly listed telecommunications operator, is directing strategic investment toward high-density GPU (graphics processing unit) data centers and low-latency networks rather than AI model or algorithm development, the company's Director and Chief Legal and Regulatory Officer said Friday at the Indonesia Ethical AI Summit in Jakarta.

  Reski Damayanti argued that Indonesia's digital industry has placed excessive emphasis on AI algorithm updates and application development while underinvesting in the physical foundation those systems require. "When we talk about optimizing AI, the challenge doesn't stop at providing smart models or algorithms. We need solid, resilient, and equitable infrastructure support to ensure this technology can be accessed and deliver real economic impact to society," she said, as quoted by Bisnis.com.

  ## Infrastructure as the core investment thesis

  Indosat has partnered with global technology companies to deliver high-performance GPU-based cloud computing services domestically, the company said. GPU-based infrastructure is used to accelerate the parallel computations required by AI workloads, and high-density configurations pack more processing capacity into a given facility footprint. The company said those facilities are intended to serve both corporations and local startups seeking to develop AI capabilities without relying on overseas compute resources.

  The investment also extends to low-latency network coverage across the Indonesian archipelago. Latency — the delay between a data request and its response — is a limiting factor for real-time AI applications, and Indosat said broad, low-latency connectivity is a prerequisite for the technology to function at scale outside major urban centers.

  ## Data sovereignty as a driver

  A central rationale for domestic infrastructure build-out is data sovereignty. Indosat said that without an adequate local foundation, Indonesian industries would remain dependent on foreign infrastructure, limiting their operational flexibility. Processing large volumes of data domestically, under high cybersecurity standards, is integral to the strategy, the company said.

  Damayanti said a dependable domestic AI infrastructure base would produce a multiplier effect across Indonesia's industrial sectors. She cited logistics network optimization, more accurate agricultural forecasting, and higher-quality telemedicine enabled by real-time medical data analysis as specific applications. She noted that realizing those scenarios requires both energy-efficient infrastructure and uninterrupted data transmission across the country's dispersed island geography.

  "The main reason we are making large investments in this infrastructure is to create a stable operational foundation. Without adequate connectivity infrastructure and data centers, even the most advanced AI applications will not be able to work optimally in the field," Damayanti said.

  ## Positioning Indonesia in Southeast Asia's digital economy

  Indosat framed the investment program as a mechanism to close Indonesia's digital divide and establish the country as a leading participant in Southeast Asia's digital economy. The company said the build-out is intended to stimulate productivity across industry sectors, accelerate public-service modernization, and support locally relevant AI innovation suited to domestic market needs.

  The company said its current infrastructure investment is designed to serve as the primary engine of national digital economic growth and to give local businesses globally competitive capabilities ahead of the next wave of technological disruption.
body_zh: |
  PT Indosat Tbk.（股票代碼：ISAT）是印尼的上市電信業者，目前正將策略投資方向轉往高密度GPU（繪圖處理器）資料中心與低延遲網路，而非AI模型或演算法開發。該公司董事兼法務暨法規事務長週五於雅加達「印尼倫理AI峰會」上公開表達了上述立場。

  Reski Damayanti指出，印尼數位產業長期過度聚焦於AI演算法更新與應用程式開發，對這些系統所需的實體基礎設施投入卻嚴重不足。她說：「當我們談到優化AI時，挑戰不僅止於提供聰明的模型或演算法。我們需要穩固、有韌性且公平可及的基礎設施支援，才能確保這項技術真正被取用，並為社會帶來具體的經濟效益。」上述談話引自《Bisnis.com》的報導。

  ## 基礎設施作為核心投資主軸

  Indosat已與多家全球科技公司展開合作，在印尼國內提供高效能GPU雲端運算服務。GPU基礎設施用於加速AI工作負載所需的平行運算，而高密度配置則能在相同的設施佔地面積內，容納更高的運算能量。該公司表示，這些設施旨在同時服務企業客戶與本地新創業者，讓他們毋須仰賴海外運算資源，即可自力發展AI能力。

  此投資計畫亦延伸至覆蓋印尼群島全境的低延遲網路建設。延遲是指從資料請求發出到收到回應之間的時間差，是即時AI應用的關鍵制約因素。Indosat表示，廣泛且低延遲的連線能力，是這項技術能在主要都市以外的地區大規模落地運作的必要前提。

  ## 資料主權作為推動力

  推動國內基礎設施建設的核心理由在於資料主權。Indosat表示，若缺乏充足的本地基礎，印尼各產業將持續依賴外國基礎設施，進而限制其營運彈性。在高水準資安標準下於國內處理大量資料，是這項策略不可或缺的一環。

  Damayanti表示，可靠的國內AI基礎設施將對印尼各產業部門產生乘數效應。她具體舉例，包括物流網路優化、更精準的農業氣候預測，以及透過即時醫療資料分析所實現的高品質遠距醫療服務。她並指出，要實現上述願景，既需要節能的基礎設施，也必須在全國分散的群島地理條件下維持不間斷的資料傳輸。

  「我們之所以在這些基礎設施上進行大規模投資，主要目的是建立穩定的營運基礎。若沒有完善的連線基礎設施與資料中心，即便是最先進的AI應用，也無法在現場發揮最佳效能，」Damayanti說。

  ## 將印尼定位為東南亞數位經濟要角

  Indosat將這項投資計畫定位為縮短印尼數位落差、確立印尼在東南亞數位經濟中扮演領導角色的重要機制。該公司表示，此次基礎設施建設旨在刺激各產業部門的生產力、加速公共服務現代化，並支持符合本土市場需求的在地AI創新發展。

  該公司表示，目前這波基礎設施投資的設計定位，是驅動全國數位經濟成長的主要引擎，並期望在下一波技術浪潮到來之前，賦予本地企業足以與全球競爭的實力。
