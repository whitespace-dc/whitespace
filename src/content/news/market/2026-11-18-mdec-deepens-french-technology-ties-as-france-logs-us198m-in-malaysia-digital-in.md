body_en: |
  Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation (MDEC) strengthened ties with French technology companies during French President Emmanuel Macron's official visit to the country, hosting a Digital Investment Breakfast that brought together French business stakeholders to explore investment opportunities and Malaysia's potential as a base for regional expansion.

  France accounted for US$198.51 million (RM812.68 million) in approved Malaysia Digital investments between 2024 and 2026, according to MDEC, ranking fifth among European countries by investment value and generating hundreds of projected knowledge-worker opportunities.

  ## French companies in Malaysia

  Six French-linked companies hold Malaysia Digital (MD) Status, a government designation providing investment facilitation, applicable incentives and industry connections to qualifying firms. They are AXA Group Operations Malaysia, CACEIS Malaysia (a unit of Crédit Agricole Group), Sanofi-Aventis (Malaysia), Orange Business Services Malaysia, Schneider Electric IT Malaysia and CMA CGM.

  MDEC said several companies are exploring further expansion in Malaysia, with new plans centered on establishing Asia-Pacific technology and delivery hubs in agentic AI (artificial intelligence systems designed to autonomously execute complex, multi-step tasks), cybersecurity and automation.

  ## Investment rationale

  Digital Minister Gobind Singh Deo said Malaysia's goal of becoming an AI Nation by 2030 creates openings for French participation, citing France's established strengths in AI, aerospace, cybersecurity, industrial technology and digital innovation. "By bringing together French expertise and Malaysia's growing digital capabilities, we can create new opportunities for technology development, knowledge transfer and high-value job creation," he said. The minister said he wants investments to deliver tangible benefits to the economy, strengthen local capabilities and enable Malaysian companies to participate more meaningfully in the global digital value chain.

  MDEC Acting CEO Azlan Sharom said Malaysia offers French companies more than a market presence. "They can use Malaysia as a regional base to develop technology capabilities, deliver services and expand into ASEAN," he said, referring to the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, the Southeast Asian regional grouping. Sharom said MDEC aims to help companies connect with local partners and build long-term partnerships that translate into meaningful investments and stronger local capabilities.

  ## Malaysia's offer to investors

  Malaysia's expanding data centre and cloud infrastructure, skilled workforce, strategic ASEAN location and established multinational ecosystem are the conditions MDEC said make the country a viable base for scaling digital operations and accessing regional markets. Through Malaysia Digital, eligible companies can access investment facilitation, applicable incentives, industry connections and support for establishing or expanding operations.

  ## Bilateral coordination

  The bilateral investment figures and the expansion plans under discussion indicate that Malaysia is positioning itself as a preferred ASEAN gateway for French technology firms seeking regional scale in areas including AI and cybersecurity. MDEC said it will continue collaborating with Business France, CCI France Malaysia and La French Tech Malaysia to strengthen bilateral investment opportunities and increase French participation in Malaysia's digital economy.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞數位經濟機構（MDEC）趁法國總統艾曼紐・馬克宏（Emmanuel Macron）正式訪馬之際，舉辦數位投資早餐會，廣邀法國企業利害關係人出席，探討投資機遇以及馬來西亞作為區域擴張基地的潛力，進一步深化雙方科技合作關係。

  根據MDEC統計，2024年至2026年間，法國在馬來西亞數位（Malaysia Digital）核准投資金額達1億9,851萬美元（約馬幣8億1,268萬令吉），在歐洲各國中排名第五，並有望創造數以百計的知識型工作機會。

  ## 法國企業在馬來西亞布局

  目前共有六家法資企業持有馬來西亞數位（MD）資格——這是馬來西亞政府授予符合資格企業的官方認定，可享有投資輔導、適用獎勵措施及產業資源整合等支援。這六家企業分別為：AXA集團馬來西亞營運公司、CACEIS馬來西亞（法國農業信貸集團旗下子公司）、賽諾菲馬來西亞（Sanofi-Aventis Malaysia）、Orange商業服務馬來西亞（Orange Business Services Malaysia）、施耐德電氣IT馬來西亞（Schneider Electric IT Malaysia），以及達飛集團（CMA CGM）。

  MDEC表示，目前已有多家企業積極評估在馬來西亞進一步擴展的可能性，新一波布局計畫聚焦於在當地建立亞太區科技與交付中樞，重點領域涵蓋代理式人工智慧（即設計用以自主執行複雜多步驟任務的AI系統）、網路安全與自動化。

  ## 投資考量

  數位部長哥賓星・迪奧（Gobind Singh Deo）表示，馬來西亞以2030年成為「AI國家」為目標，此一願景正為法國參與創造切入契機，他並點出法國在人工智慧、航太、網路安全、工業科技及數位創新等領域的既有優勢。「結合法國的專業技術與馬來西亞不斷成長的數位實力，我們得以開創科技發展、知識轉移及高附加價值就業的新機遇，」他說。部長同時強調，他期待投資能為經濟帶來具體效益、強化在地能量，並讓馬來西亞企業更積極地參與全球數位價值鏈。

  MDEC代理執行長阿茲蘭・沙隆（Azlan Sharom）指出，馬來西亞能為法國企業提供的，遠不止於市場布局。「企業可以馬來西亞為區域基地，發展科技能力、提供服務，並向東協各國拓展，」他說——東協即東南亞國家協會，為東南亞區域組織。沙隆表示，MDEC致力協助企業媒合在地合作夥伴，建立長期夥伴關係，進而轉化為有意義的投資成果與更強勁的在地能量。

  ## 馬來西亞對投資者的吸引力

  MDEC指出，馬來西亞持續擴建的資料中心與雲端基礎設施、充沛的技術人才、東協核心的戰略位置，以及成熟的跨國企業生態系，是該國作為數位營運規模化基地、進而輻射區域市場的核心優勢條件。透過馬來西亞數位計畫，符合資格的企業可獲得投資輔導、適用獎勵措施、產業資源整合，以及進駐或擴展營運的相關支援。

  ## 雙邊協調

  上述雙邊投資數據與洽談中的擴展計畫，顯示馬來西亞正積極將自身定位為法國科技企業尋求東協區域規模化——尤其是在人工智慧與網路安全領域——的優先門戶。MDEC表示，將持續與法國商務辦事處（Business France）、法馬商會（CCI France Malaysia）及法國科技聯盟馬來西亞（La French Tech Malaysia）攜手合作，強化雙邊投資機遇，擴大法國企業在馬來西亞數位經濟的參與規模。
