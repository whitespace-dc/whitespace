body_en: |
  The Principality of Asturias announced the Digital Valley Asturias project this week, a €1.226 billion ($1.4bn) data centre campus planned for the municipality of Salas in northern Spain, with construction targeted to begin in the second half of 2027 and operations to commence by 2030.

  The announcement was made at a presentation attended by Adrián Barbón, president of the Principality of Asturias. Armando Layna, co-director of Digital Valley and Sales For You Consulting (S4U), a consulting firm involved in the project, said the development has been in preparation for two years in collaboration with the regional government.

  ## Campus scale and technical specifications

  The campus will comprise ten data centre modules with a combined computing capacity of 120 megawatts (MW) — a measure of the electrical power the facility will draw to run servers and cooling systems at full load. The site will occupy 154,585 square meters (approximately 1.66 million square feet) at the Nonaya Este industrial park in Salas, of which 46,865 square meters (504,450 sq ft) will be allocated to operational facilities.

  The development will also include a dedicated electrical substation to supply grid power and a photovoltaic installation consisting of 350 solar panels.

  ## Employment across construction and operations

  During the construction phase, the project is expected to employ up to 3,000 workers, according to Layna. Once operational, the campus will support approximately 120 direct jobs and roughly 960 indirect jobs linked to its operations, officials said at the presentation.

  ## Construction timeline

  Site work at the Nonaya Este industrial park is scheduled to begin in the second half of 2027, with the campus targeting operational status by 2030 — a build-out period of approximately two and a half to three years.

  ## Project background and water use

  Layna described Digital Valley Asturias as a "virtual campus" developed over two years alongside the Asturian government. He characterized data centres as functioning like "large refineries of the digital economy" — infrastructure that processes and stores data at scale — and described them as "silent" facilities whose presence goes largely unnoticed until an outage occurs.

  Layna also addressed what he called "myths" around water consumption at data centre sites, stating that the Salas campus will use "the same amount as any closed-loop cooling system." A closed-loop system recirculates water within the cooling circuit rather than drawing on and discharging fresh water continuously, which limits net consumption compared with open evaporative cooling approaches.

  ## Regional context

  Barbón described Asturias as "a land of opportunities" during the presentation and argued that "it is the companies that announce projects once they are ready," a remark he framed as implicit criticism of political rivals he accused of what he called "showboating."

  Construction at Nonaya Este in Salas is scheduled to begin in the second half of 2027, with the full 120MW, ten-module campus expected to be operational by 2030.

  ## 阿斯圖里亞斯宣布12.26億歐元資料中心園區　薩拉斯市2030年目標達120MW

  西班牙北部阿斯圖里亞斯親王區本週宣布「Digital Valley Asturias」資料中心園區計畫，總投資額達12.26億歐元（約14億美元），預定落址於薩拉斯市，目標於2027年下半年動工、2030年正式投入營運。

  此一宣布係於一場發表會上正式公告，阿斯圖里亞斯親王區主席阿德里安·巴爾邦（Adrián Barbón）出席見證。參與本案的顧問公司「Sales For You Consulting」（S4U）共同負責人阿曼多·萊納（Armando Layna）表示，這項開發案已與區域政府合作籌備長達兩年。

  ## 園區規模與技術規格

  此座園區將由十座資料中心模組構成，合計運算容量為120百萬瓦（MW）——此數字代表設施在伺服器與冷卻系統全速運行時所需的電力負載。園區選址於薩拉斯市的諾納亞東部（Nonaya Este）工業園區，占地面積達154,585平方公尺（約166萬平方英尺），其中46,865平方公尺（約50萬平方英尺）將作為營運設施使用。

  此開發案亦將設置專屬電力變電站以供應電網電力，並配備一座由350片太陽能板組成的光伏發電裝置。

  ## 建設與營運期就業人數

  據萊納表示，建設階段預計將雇用多達3,000名工人。園區正式營運後，估計將創造約120個直接就業機會，以及與營運相關的約960個間接就業機會，官員在發表會上如此說明。

  ## 建設時程

  諾納亞東部工業園區的地面工程預計於2027年下半年展開，園區目標於2030年達到可營運狀態，整體建設期約為兩年半至三年。

  ## 計畫背景與用水議題

  萊納將「Digital Valley Asturias」形容為一座與阿斯圖里亞斯政府歷時兩年共同打造的「虛擬園區」。他以「數位經濟的大型煉油廠」來比喻資料中心的角色——亦即大規模處理與儲存資料的基礎設施——並指出這類設施屬於「低調」的存在，通常在發生中斷事故之前幾乎不引人注意。

  萊納亦針對外界對資料中心用水量的「迷思」作出回應，表示薩拉斯園區的用水量「與任何封閉循環冷卻系統相同」。封閉式循環系統會在冷卻迴路內部循環利用水資源，而非持續抽取並排放大量新鮮水源，相較於開放式蒸發冷卻方式，其實際耗水量明顯較低。

  ## 區域背景

  巴爾邦主席在發表會上稱阿斯圖里亞斯為「機遇之地」，並主張「企業只有在計畫準備就緒後才會宣布」，此番言論被外界解讀為他對政治對手的隱性批評，暗指後者熱衷於他所稱的「作秀」行為。

  諾納亞東部工業園區的建設工程預計於2027年下半年啟動，全規模的120MW、十模組園區則預計於2030年前全面投入營運。
