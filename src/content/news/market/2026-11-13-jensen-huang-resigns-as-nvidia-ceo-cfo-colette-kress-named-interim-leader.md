body_en: |
  Jensen Huang resigned as president, chief executive officer, and board member of NVIDIA Corporation, effective immediately, the company announced Thursday in a press release published shortly before 6:00 a.m. Pacific time. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive while NVIDIA's board conducts a search for a permanent successor, the company said.

  NVIDIA shares fell more than 10% in premarket trading Thursday, according to Reuters, as investors assessed the implications for a company valued at approximately $4 trillion and the broader artificial intelligence infrastructure sector.

  The company's announcement did not specify a reason for Huang's resignation.

  ## Events preceding the resignation

  The departure follows several days of escalating controversy over Huang's reported personal relationships, questions about potential foreign intelligence activity, and corporate governance concerns.

  On November 8, Huang sent an email to NVIDIA employees acknowledging an extramarital relationship with a Chinese businesswoman and apologizing for his conduct. That same day, the board announced an independent investigation, retaining law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz and former acting U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York Joon H. Kim to lead it. The board stated at the time that it had no reason to believe company secrets or national security had been compromised.

  U.S. intelligence authorities had flagged concerns that the businesswoman may have had connections to Chinese intelligence, raising questions about whether Huang had been a target of a foreign intelligence operation, according to reporting cited by multiple sources. Congressional calls for Huang's resignation followed, alongside questions about semiconductor export controls and the security of advanced AI technology. The espionage allegations remain contested, and no compromise of NVIDIA technology has been publicly established.

  On November 11, the New York Times reported a second relationship, this one with Kimberly Powell, an NVIDIA vice president who oversees the company's health care business. Reuters and others reported that NVIDIA's board convened a meeting that same evening to determine whether to remove Huang.

  ## Interim leadership and new board structure

  Kress joined NVIDIA in 2013 and has served as the company's executive vice president and CFO. She will retain her finance responsibilities during the transition and will lead NVIDIA's third-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings call, scheduled for November 17.

  The board also appointed Harvey C. Jones as NVIDIA's first-ever board chairman. Jones is a managing partner at Square Wave Ventures and a former CEO of Synopsys, and has served as a longtime NVIDIA director. Jones said the company expects its operations, customer commitments, and product roadmap to continue without interruption.

  The board stated it has full confidence in Kress and the leadership team, and that it will consider both internal and external candidates for the permanent chief executive role.

  ## The succession challenge

  Huang co-founded NVIDIA in 1993 and led the company for 33 years. He maintained more than 60 direct reports and a highly centralized management structure that allowed him to drive major strategic decisions, including bets on CUDA and automotive applications, according to ServeTheHome. NVIDIA has no clearly defined internal successor.

  The centralized structure that enabled Huang to move the company quickly also means his unplanned departure leaves a large gap in NVIDIA's managerial hierarchy, ServeTheHome noted.

  ## What is at stake for AI data center infrastructure

  Under Huang, NVIDIA evolved from a graphics chip supplier into a central architect of the AI data center buildout, shaping not only computing platforms but also facility design, networking, cooling, electrical distribution, and project financing structures, according to Data Center Frontier.

  In August, NVIDIA announced agreements with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs, and KKR aimed at mobilizing more than $500 billion in potential third-party financing for AI infrastructure. Data Center Frontier noted that figure represents prospective capital mobilization, not committed NVIDIA spending, but said it illustrates the company's growing influence over how AI infrastructure projects are structured and financed.

  NVIDIA has also been extending its DSX reference architecture into physical infrastructure. Its DSX Ready program establishes technical requirements for battery energy storage and liquid cooling. The company's DSX POD initiative targets factory-integrated AI computing infrastructure designed for deployment at prepared sites in as little as 14 days. These programs involve coordination among data center operators, equipment manufacturers, utilities, construction firms, and financial institutions, according to Data Center Frontier.

  There is no current indication that the leadership change has disrupted chip deliveries or delayed announced AI factory projects. Nevertheless, Huang's prominence in assembling and maintaining that ecosystem raises questions about strategic continuity, capital allocation, and coordination that the transition will need to address.

  ## Broader pressures on AI infrastructure financing

  The leadership change arrives as the AI infrastructure buildout faces broader scrutiny over investment pace and the industry's ability to finance, construct, and energize the facilities required to meet demand.

  On November 10, CoreWeave, an AI cloud provider backed by NVIDIA, reported third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, an increase of approximately 168% year over year, alongside a contracted revenue backlog of $132.5 billion. The company also reported a net loss of $709 million for the quarter, illustrating the capital intensity of scaling AI computing infrastructure at speed.

  Separately, neocloud provider Nebius disclosed Thursday that delays in bringing third-party capacity online had pushed some expected deployments into the first half of 2027, even as it raised its capital spending outlook.

  NVIDIA's earnings call on November 17 will be the first major public forum for the company's new leadership to address those questions directly. Kress will present the company's third-quarter fiscal 2027 results and lead the call.
body_zh: |
  輝達公司（NVIDIA）於週四發表聲明，宣布黃仁勳即日起辭去總裁、執行長及董事職務。聲明以新聞稿形式於太平洋時間上午6時前不久對外發布。財務長柯蕾特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）將在董事會展開永久繼任人選搜尋期間，出任代理執行長。

  根據路透社報導，受投資人對這家市值約4兆美元科技巨頭前景的憂慮拖累，輝達股價在週四盤前交易中下跌逾10%，AI基礎設施整體產業亦受波及。

  輝達在聲明中並未說明黃仁勳辭職的原因。

  ## 辭職前夕的一連串風波

  黃仁勳的離職，發生在數日來爭議持續升溫的背景下，涉及其據稱的私人情感關係、潛在外國情報滲透疑慮，以及公司治理問題。

  11月8日，黃仁勳向輝達員工發出電子郵件，承認與一名中國女商人存在婚外情，並為自身行為道歉。同日，董事會宣布啟動獨立調查，委託律師事務所Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz及前美國紐約南區代理聯邦檢察官金俊烜（Joon H. Kim）主導調查。董事會當時表示，目前沒有理由認為公司機密或國家安全已遭洩露。

  根據多方報導所引述的消息，美國情報機關已示警，指該名女商人可能與中國情報機構存在關聯，引發外界質疑黃仁勳是否已淪為境外情報行動鎖定的目標。國會隨後出現要求黃仁勳辭職的聲浪，並就半導體出口管制及先進AI技術安全性提出質疑。間諜相關指控至今仍具爭議，輝達技術遭竊取一事亦未獲任何公開證實。

  11月11日，《紐約時報》報導了第二段關係，對象為輝達掌管醫療保健業務的副總裁金柏莉·鮑爾（Kimberly Powell）。路透社等媒體亦報導，輝達董事會於同日晚間召開緊急會議，討論是否撤換黃仁勳。

  ## 代理領導層及董事會新架構

  克雷斯於2013年加入輝達，現任執行副總裁暨財務長。在過渡期間，她將繼續兼管財務工作，並主持預定於11月17日舉行的輝達2027財年第三季法說會。

  董事會同時任命哈維·C·瓊斯（Harvey C. Jones）為輝達史上首位董事會主席。瓊斯現為Square Wave Ventures管理合夥人，曾任新思科技（Synopsys）執行長，並長期擔任輝達董事。瓊斯表示，公司預計營運、客戶承諾及產品藍圖均將不受影響，持續推進。

  董事會表示對克雷斯及領導團隊充滿信心，並將同時考量內部及外部候選人，以尋覓永久執行長人選。

  ## 接班人難題

  黃仁勳於1993年共同創辦輝達，領導公司長達33年。根據ServeTheHome的報導，他直接管轄逾60名直屬部屬，建立起高度集權的管理架構，藉此推動重大策略決策，包括押注CUDA及車用應用等領域。輝達目前並無明確的內部接班人選。

  這種使輝達得以快速應變的集權架構，同時也意味著黃仁勳的突然離去，將在輝達的管理層級中留下巨大缺口，ServeTheHome指出。

  ## AI資料中心基礎設施面臨的衝擊

  在黃仁勳的帶領下，輝達從繪圖晶片供應商蛻變為AI資料中心建設的核心架構師，不僅主導運算平台的走向，更深入影響設施設計、網路建置、散熱方案、電力配置乃至專案融資架構，Data Center Frontier如此報導。

  今年8月，輝達宣布與阿波羅（Apollo）、貝萊德（BlackRock）、黑石（Blackstone）、布魯克菲爾德（Brookfield）、高盛（Goldman Sachs）及KKR達成協議，目標是為AI基礎設施調動逾5,000億美元的潛在第三方融資。Data Center Frontier指出，這一數字代表的是潛在資本調動規模，而非輝達本身的承諾支出，但足以說明該公司對AI基礎設施專案融資結構的影響力日益深遠。

  輝達也持續將其DSX參考架構延伸至實體基礎設施領域。DSX Ready計畫為電池儲能系統及液冷設備訂立技術規範；DSX POD計畫則以工廠整合的AI運算基礎設施為目標，可在預備好的場地內最快14天完成部署。這些計畫涉及資料中心業者、設備製造商、公用事業公司、營建商及金融機構的協調合作，Data Center Frontier如此指出。

  目前尚無跡象顯示此次領導層更迭已影響晶片交貨，或延誤已宣布的AI工廠專案推進。然而，黃仁勳在整合與維繫這一生態系中所扮演的核心角色，使外界對策略延續性、資本配置及生態系協調等議題不免產生疑慮，而這些正是本次權力交接必須正面回應的課題。

  ## AI基礎設施融資面臨更廣泛的壓力

  此次領導層更迭發生之際，AI基礎設施建設的投資步伐，以及業界能否及時籌資、興建並完成供電以應對需求，正受到更廣泛的審視。

  11月10日，獲輝達入股的AI雲端供應商CoreWeave公布第三季營收為36.6億美元，較去年同期成長約168%，合約收入積壓金額達1,325億美元。該公司同期亦報告淨虧損7.09億美元，凸顯出以高速擴建AI運算基礎設施的高度資本密集特性。

  另一方面，新型雲端供應商Nebius亦於週四披露，第三方產能建置延誤已將部分預期部署推遲至2027年上半年，儘管如此，該公司仍上調了資本支出展望。

  11月17日的輝達法說會，將是輝達新領導班子首度在公開場合回應上述問題的重要時刻。克雷斯將發布輝達2027財年第三季業績並主持本次法說會。
