body_en: |
  NRG Energy has agreed to sell approximately 4.9 gigawatts (GW) of natural gas generation capacity across three U.S. power markets to LS Power for $4 billion, the company said during its Nov. 9 earnings call. The deal came after LS Power approached NRG with an unsolicited offer.

  The $4 billion price represents roughly eight times the plants' combined annual adjusted EBITDA — earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization — of approximately $500 million, NRG President and CEO Robert Gaudette said. NRG's third-quarter adjusted EBITDA was $2 billion, the company separately disclosed.

  ## Use of proceeds

  NRG plans to direct the proceeds as follows: $1 billion toward a stock buyback, $2 billion toward debt reduction, and the remaining $1 billion reserved for "flexibility to reinvest in our long-term strategy through attractive contracted growth opportunities or further reduce debt," Gaudette said. The transaction is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals.

  ## Plants included in the sale

  The six facilities span three regional grid operators:

  - **ISO-New England (ISO-NE):** The 592-megawatt (MW) combined-cycle gas turbine (CCGT) Ocean State plant and the 322-MW combustion-turbine Wallingford plant.
  - **Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT):** The 1.2-GW CCGT Jack County plant, the 269-MW CCGT Johnson County plant, and the 526-MW steam turbine/combustion turbine R.W. Miller plant.
  - **New York Independent System Operator (NYISO):** The 1.9-GW CCGT and steam turbine Ravenswood plant.

  A combined-cycle gas turbine plant uses both a gas turbine and a steam turbine to extract electricity from a single fuel input, making it more efficient than single-cycle combustion units. A gigawatt equals 1,000 megawatts, enough to power a large city.

  ## Valuation benchmark: Jack County

  Gaudette singled out Jack County as a notable pricing reference point. The plant carries an implied value of approximately $1,400 per kilowatt (kW), he said. By comparison, NRG is developing 1.5 GW of new natural gas generation in Texas for "just above $1,000/kW," he said. "That comparison demonstrates the value we're realizing from selected existing assets while developing newer, more efficient generation at a lower cost," Gaudette said.

  ## NRG's contracted data center pipeline

  The asset sale proceeds alongside NRG's development of contracted generation for large technology customers. NRG is building a 1.2-GW combined-cycle natural gas plant in Texas to supply power to an unnamed cloud and artificial intelligence hyperscaler, Gaudette said. That project is "on track," and he said he expects "approved agreements and an announcement by year-end."

  NRG holds a development agreement with equipment manufacturer GE Vernova and construction firm Kiewit covering 5.4 GW of turbine capacity. The company's pipeline of opportunities now exceeds both the turbine allocations and engineering, procurement, and construction capacity secured under that agreement, Gaudette said. NRG is also "advancing additional project opportunities with the same customer beyond the initial 1.2 GW," he added.

  ## ERCOT market conditions

  Gaudette described power prices in ERCOT — the Texas grid covering most of the state — as "depressed," a condition he said is factored into NRG's 2027 guidance and long-term outlook. "Right now we're in a world where battery build and solar have kind of dominated the last two years of build, and demand hasn't been fast enough to absorb all of it," he said, calling 2027 "weak."

  He said the region's demand queue includes not only data centers but "multiple gigawatts of manufacturing facilities, expansions of industrial facilities," which he said would absorb excess capacity and tighten the market over time. "NRG is very well positioned to capture value both on the retail side and the generation side as this market tightens and our customers need us more," Gaudette said.

  ## Political environment

  Asked by an analyst whether the results of the recent midterm elections posed any risk to the 1.2-GW Texas project, Gaudette said the political environment had "actually strengthened this project and our projects." He said he had spoken directly with the unnamed customer following the elections and that "this project is going to happen."

  ## What it means

  The transaction converts six gas plants — whose earnings depend on regional power market conditions — into $4 billion in cash, which NRG is directing toward balance-sheet reduction and new contracted capacity serving large technology customers. The sale price of roughly eight times EBITDA, and an implied $1,400 per kilowatt for Jack County against new-build costs Gaudette cited at just above $1,000 per kilowatt, reflects the premium LS Power paid for dispatchable existing capacity.

  NRG expects the LS Power transaction to close in the second quarter of 2027 pending regulatory approvals, and expects to announce contracted agreements for the 1.2-GW Texas hyperscaler plant before the end of this year.
body_zh: |
  NRG Energy於11月9日法說會上宣布，已同意以40億美元（約新台幣1,320億元）將分布於美國三大電力市場、合計約4.9 GW的天然氣發電容量出售給LS Power，此案源於買方主動提出的收購要約。

  NRG總裁兼執行長Robert Gaudette表示，40億美元的售價約相當於這批電廠合計年度調整後EBITDA（稅息折舊及攤銷前利潤）約5億美元的八倍。公司另行揭露，NRG第三季調整後EBITDA為20億美元。

  ## 資金用途

  Gaudette說明，NRG計畫將出售所得資金分配如下：10億美元用於股票回購，20億美元用於降低負債，剩餘10億美元則保留彈性，「用於透過具吸引力的合約成長機會再投資我們的長期策略，或進一步減債」。本次交易預計於2027年第二季完成，仍待監管機關核准。

  ## 納入出售的電廠

  此次出售的六座電廠分布於三個區域電網調度業者管轄範圍：

  - **ISO新英格蘭（ISO-NE）：** 裝置容量592 MW的Ocean State複循環燃氣渦輪（CCGT）電廠，以及322 MW的Wallingford燃氣渦輪電廠。
  - **德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）：** 1.2 GW的Jack County CCGT電廠、269 MW的Johnson County CCGT電廠，以及526 MW的R.W. Miller蒸汽渦輪／燃氣渦輪電廠。
  - **紐約獨立系統調度業者（NYISO）：** 1.9 GW的Ravenswood CCGT暨蒸汽渦輪電廠。

  複循環燃氣渦輪電廠同時運用燃氣渦輪與蒸汽渦輪，從單一燃料輸入中提取電力，發電效率高於單循環燃氣機組。1 GW等於1,000 MW，足以供應一座大型城市的用電需求。

  ## 估值基準：Jack County電廠

  Gaudette特別點名Jack County電廠，將其列為本次交易的重要定價參考點。他表示，該廠的隱含每千瓦（kW）價值約為1,400美元。相較之下，NRG目前正在德州開發1.5 GW的新天然氣發電設施，每kW成本「略高於1,000美元」。「這個比較充分說明了我們從部分現有資產中實現的價值，同時又能以更低成本開發效率更高的新型發電設施，」Gaudette說。

  ## NRG的資料中心合約發電開發管線

  此次資產出售與NRG為大型科技客戶開發合約電力的進程同步推進。Gaudette表示，NRG正在德州興建一座1.2 GW複循環天然氣電廠，專為一家未具名的雲端運算與人工智慧超大規模業者供電。他說這項計畫「進展順利」，並預期「在年底前完成協議核准並對外公告」。

  NRG與設備製造商GE Vernova及建設公司Kiewit簽有開發合作協議，涵蓋5.4 GW的渦輪機容量。Gaudette表示，公司目前的商機管線已超出該協議所確保的渦輪機配額及工程採購建造（EPC）產能。他補充，NRG也正在「與同一客戶推進超出初始1.2 GW的額外專案機會」。

  ## ERCOT市場狀況

  Gaudette形容ERCOT電力現貨價格「低迷」，並指出這一狀況已納入NRG的2027年財務預測與長期展望。「目前我們所處的環境是，電池儲能與太陽能在過去兩年主導了建設格局，而需求成長的速度還不足以消化所有新增供給，」他說，並稱2027年將是「疲弱的一年」。

  他表示，德州電網的待接入需求不僅來自資料中心，還包括「多個GW級的製造設施和工業設施擴建項目」，這些需求將逐步消化過剩產能，使市場供需趨於緊張。「隨著市場收緊、客戶對我們的需求增加，NRG無論在零售側還是發電側，都處於極有利的位置，能夠充分創造價值，」Gaudette說。

  ## 政治環境

  針對分析師詢問近期美國期中選舉結果是否對德州1.2 GW專案帶來風險，Gaudette表示政治環境「實際上強化了這個專案以及我們的各項計畫」。他說，選舉結束後他已直接與那位未具名客戶溝通，確認「這個專案一定會推進」。

  ## 交易意涵

  本次交易將六座仰賴區域電力現貨市場收益的天然氣電廠換成40億美元現金，NRG計畫將這筆資金用於改善財務結構，並開發服務大型科技客戶的新合約電力容量。以約八倍EBITDA的售價成交，Jack County電廠每kW隱含價值達1,400美元，遠高於Gaudette所提略高於每kW 1,000美元的新建成本，充分反映出LS Power為可調度現有產能所支付的溢價。

  NRG預計LS Power交易最快於2027年第二季取得監管核准後正式完成，並預計在今年底前公告德州1.2 GW超大規模電廠的合約協議。
