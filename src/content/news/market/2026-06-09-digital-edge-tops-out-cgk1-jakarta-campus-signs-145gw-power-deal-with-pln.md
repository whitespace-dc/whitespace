body_en: |
  Digital Edge has completed the structural frame of CGK1, the first building at its 500-megawatt campus in Bekasi, Greater Jakarta, and signed a 1.45-gigawatt power supply agreement with Indonesian state utility PT PLN (Persero). The Singapore-based data center company said the deal is believed to be the largest single-campus power commitment ever secured for a data center in Indonesia.

  CGK1 is expected to enter service in Q4 2026. A second building is scheduled to follow in Q1 2027 and a third in Q2 2027.

  ## Power agreement structure

  The 1.45 GW agreement is structured as two independent feeds of 725 MVA (megavolt-amperes) each. Digital Edge said the dual-feed arrangement is designed to provide greater resilience in supporting AI and cloud workloads.

  PT PLN President Director Darmawan Prasodjo said the utility is "committed to delivering reliable and sustainable electricity supply to support the needs of this strategic sector, while further strengthening Indonesia's position as a digital economy hub in Southeast Asia."

  ## Campus scale and financing

  The CGK campus is a US$4.5 billion multi-phase development at the GIIC Industrial Estate in Bekasi, with a total planned IT capacity of 500 MW. Digital Edge describes it as one of Indonesia's largest AI-ready hyperscale data center campuses.

  In March, the company secured US$665 million in financing for the first phase — a figure Digital Edge said represented Indonesia's largest data center green loan at the time.

  ## Design and sustainability targets

  The campus targets an annualized PUE (power usage effectiveness — the ratio of total facility energy drawn to energy delivered to IT equipment) of 1.25. The facility is also designed to achieve LEED certification, incorporate recycled water systems, and integrate renewable energy.

  ## Location and connectivity

  The CGK site sits less than 15 kilometers from other major data center clusters in the Cikarang area and approximately 40 kilometers from Digital Edge's downtown Jakarta facilities, EDGE1 and EDGE2. Network connectivity will be provided by Indonet, Digital Edge's wholly owned Indonesian telecommunications subsidiary, using new 100 percent underground fiber routes.

  ## Company and regional context

  Digital Edge operates data center and fiber infrastructure across Asia-Pacific, with operations in China, Indonesia, India, and Japan. The company said it has 1.8 GW of secured IT power across its platform.

  Stephanus Oscar, CEO of Digital Edge Indonesia, said the topping out of CGK1, "backed by the largest single-campus power agreement in Indonesia, reflects our ability to scale in support of the country's digital growth. By securing power upfront, we are enabling hyperscale and AI deployments with the certainty and speed the market requires."

  ## What it means

  The 1.45 GW power commitment to a single campus sets a new benchmark for data center power procurement in Indonesia and illustrates the scale of utility-level agreements now being pursued as hyperscale and AI infrastructure demand expands across Southeast Asia. The campus's three-building rollout is scheduled to complete by Q2 2027.

  Digital Edge完成位於大雅加達勿加泗（Bekasi）500MW園區首棟建築CGK1的結構工程封頂，並與印尼國家電力公司PT PLN（Persero）簽署一份1.45吉瓦（GW）供電協議。這家總部位於新加坡的資料中心業者表示，此協議被認為是印尼史上單一園區規模最大的資料中心電力承諾。

  CGK1預計於2026年第四季正式投入服務。第二棟建築排定於2027年第一季啟用，第三棟則預計於2027年第二季接續完工。

  ## 供電協議架構

  這份1.45GW協議採雙路獨立饋電設計，各路容量為725 MVA（百萬伏安）。Digital Edge表示，雙路饋電架構旨在為人工智慧（AI）與雲端工作負載提供更高的供電韌性。

  PT PLN總裁達爾曼萬·普拉索迪佑（Darmawan Prasodjo）表示，該公司「致力提供可靠且永續的電力供應，以支持此一策略性產業的需求，同時進一步鞏固印尼作為東南亞數位經濟樞紐的地位。」

  ## 園區規模與融資

  CGK園區是位於勿加泗GIIC工業園區的多期開發計畫，總投資額達45億美元，規劃IT容量合計500MW。Digital Edge將其定位為印尼規模最大的AI就緒超大規模資料中心園區之一。

  今年3月，該公司為第一期工程取得6.65億美元的融資；Digital Edge表示，這筆融資金額創下當時印尼資料中心綠色貸款的最高紀錄。

  ## 設計與永續目標

  該園區的年化PUE（電力使用效率，即設施總耗電量與輸送至IT設備電量之比值）目標值為1.25。此外，設施規劃取得LEED認證、導入回收水系統，並整合再生能源。

  ## 地理位置與網路連接

  CGK場址距離芝卡朗（Cikarang）地區其他主要資料中心聚落不到15公里，距Digital Edge位於雅加達市中心的設施EDGE1與EDGE2約40公里。網路連接服務將由Digital Edge旗下全資印尼電信子公司Indonet提供，透過全新百分之百地下光纖路由建置。

  ## 公司背景與區域布局

  Digital Edge在亞太地區營運資料中心與光纖基礎設施，據點遍及中國、印尼、印度與日本。公司表示，旗下平台的確保IT電力容量合計達1.8GW。

  Digital Edge印尼執行長史蒂芬努斯·奧斯卡（Stephanus Oscar）表示，CGK1的封頂「以印尼史上最大單一園區供電協議為後盾，彰顯我們擴大規模、支持印尼數位成長的能力。藉由事先確保電力供應，我們正以市場所需的確定性與速度，推動超大規模與AI部署。」

  ## 產業意義

  對單一園區承諾1.45GW電力，為印尼資料中心電力採購樹立了全新標竿，也充分說明隨著超大規模與AI基礎設施需求在東南亞持續擴大，業界向電力公司爭取的供電協議規模已今非昔比。CGK園區三棟建築的建設進程預計於2027年第二季全數完成。
