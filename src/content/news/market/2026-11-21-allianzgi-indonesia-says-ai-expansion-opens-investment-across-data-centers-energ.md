body_en: |
  PT Allianz Global Investors Asset Management Indonesia said Friday that the expansion of artificial intelligence (AI) is opening investment opportunities across multiple sectors of the Indonesian economy, extending well beyond companies that develop AI technology itself.

  Adi Saugi, CFA, President Director of AllianzGI Indonesia, said the increasing demand for computing capacity, digital services, and AI-based solutions can drive investment across the economy. He described Indonesia's AI ecosystem as still in an early stage of development but said the technology is taking on a growing role in the broader economy.

  "Long-term opportunities for Indonesia will likely come not only from direct investment in AI hardware, but also from productivity gains as AI adoption spreads across various economic sectors," Saugi said in a written statement.

  ## Sectors in scope

  AllianzGI Indonesia identified the full AI value chain as the investment opportunity — not just AI developers. The firm pointed to semiconductors, data centers, cloud computing infrastructure, and software developers as relevant segments. It also noted that companies outside the technology sector that use AI to improve their productivity and competitiveness represent potential opportunities.

  Data center capacity expansion tied to AI across Southeast Asia was cited as one specific area of opportunity for Indonesia. The firm said Indonesia holds several supporting factors to attract investment in that segment: a large domestic market, improving connectivity, available power supply, and a competitive cost structure. Those conditions, AllianzGI Indonesia said, position the country to benefit from subsequent waves of AI investment in the Southeast Asian region.

  ## Selectivity over trend-following

  Despite the broad framing, Saugi said AllianzGI Indonesia intends to remain selective. The firm said it will focus on companies able to convert AI investment into sustainable profit growth and cash flow, rather than those simply following the AI trend.

  "We will remain selective by focusing on companies that can convert AI investment into sustainable earnings growth and cash flow — not merely those riding the AI trend," Saugi said.

  ## What it means

  The statement reflects how asset managers in the region are positioning AI-linked investment theses: not as a narrow technology-sector bet, but as an infrastructure and productivity story spanning energy, telecoms, and enterprise software. For Indonesia, the argument rests on the country's domestic market scale and cost base making it a viable destination for regional data center build-out, with spillover benefits for sectors that adopt AI operationally.

  AllianzGI Indonesia said it sees the country's AI ecosystem remaining in an early development phase, with long-term opportunity contingent on how broadly AI adoption spreads across the domestic economy.
body_zh: |
  安聯環球投資印尼資產管理公司（PT Allianz Global Investors Asset Management Indonesia）週五表示，人工智慧（AI）的快速擴張正為印尼經濟多個領域創造投資機遇，影響範圍遠超過直接開發AI技術的企業本身。

  該公司總裁董事、特許財務分析師（CFA）Adi Saugi表示，運算需求、數位服務與AI解決方案的持續成長，將帶動全產業鏈的投資機會。他形容印尼的AI生態系目前仍處於發展早期階段，但這項技術在整體經濟中所扮演的角色正與日俱增。

  Saugi在一份書面聲明中表示：「印尼的長期機遇，不僅來自對AI硬體的直接投資，也將來自AI普及後在各經濟部門帶來的生產力提升。」

  ## 投資範疇

  安聯GI印尼將整個AI價值鏈視為投資機會，而非僅限於AI開發商。該公司點名半導體、資料中心、雲端運算基礎建設及軟體開發商為相關投資標的，同時也指出，科技業以外、透過導入AI提升生產力與競爭力的企業，同樣具有潛在投資價值。

  東南亞地區因應AI需求而擴建資料中心容量，被列為印尼的一個具體投資機會。該公司表示，印尼具備多項有利條件，可望吸引資料中心投資，包括龐大的國內市場、持續改善的網路連結環境、充裕的電力供應，以及具競爭力的成本結構。安聯GI印尼指出，這些條件使印尼得以在東南亞地區後續AI投資浪潮中受惠。

  ## 選股重於追趨勢

  儘管投資框架涵蓋範圍廣泛，Saugi仍強調，安聯GI印尼將維持嚴格的選股紀律。該公司表示，將聚焦於能夠將AI投資轉化為可持續獲利成長與現金流的企業，而非盲目跟隨AI熱潮的業者。

  Saugi表示：「我們將保持選股的嚴謹性，專注於能夠將AI投資轉化為可持續盈餘成長與現金流的企業——而非單純搭上AI順風車的公司。」

  ## 市場意涵

  上述聲明反映出區域資產管理業者在布局AI相關投資論點時的思維轉變：不再將其視為狹義的科技類股賭注，而是橫跨能源、電信及企業軟體的基礎建設與生產力投資故事。就印尼而言，此一論點的核心在於，該國龐大的內需市場規模與成本優勢，使其成為區域資料中心建設的可行目的地，並進一步為導入AI的各產業帶來外溢效益。

  安聯GI印尼表示，預期印尼的AI生態系仍將維持在發展早期階段，長期機遇的實現有賴於AI普及程度能夠在國內經濟中廣泛擴散。
