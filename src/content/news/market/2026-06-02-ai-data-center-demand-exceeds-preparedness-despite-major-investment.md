# AI Data Center Demand Exceeds Preparedness Despite Major Investment

A new report reveals that AI data center demand is exceeding the industry's preparedness despite major capital investment. The analysis projects a need for an additional 250GW of capacity under an aggressive but plausible scenario. This substantial gap highlights infrastructure constraints the data center sector faces in meeting future AI computing demands.

## 中文

# AI資料中心需求超出業界準備能力

一份新報告指出，儘管進行龐大投資，人工智慧資料中心的需求仍遠超業界準備能力。該分析預測在「積極但可能」的情景下，需要增加250GW的容量。這表明資料中心產業在滿足未來人工智慧運算需求時面臨重大基礎設施限制。
