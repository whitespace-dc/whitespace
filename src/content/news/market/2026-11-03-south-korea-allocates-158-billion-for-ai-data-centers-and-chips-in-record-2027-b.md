body_en: |
  South Korean President Lee Jae Myung on Monday announced the country's largest-ever national budget for 2027, allocating KRW 21.3 trillion (US$ 15.83 billion) to physical artificial intelligence infrastructure, AI data centers, and semiconductors — nearly double what was allocated in 2026 — as part of a total spending plan of KRW 820.9 trillion (US$ 610.14 billion).

  The 2027 budget represents a 12.8 percent increase over the prior year, according to CNBC as cited by W.Media. The AI and semiconductor envelope accounts for roughly 2.6 percent of total planned expenditure. A separate KRW 4.7 trillion (US$ 3.5 billion) has been earmarked specifically to develop a sovereign AI model — a government-backed foundational AI system intended to operate independently of foreign providers.

  ## Workforce and education investment

  The government will train 2,000 specialists to lead semiconductor design and the broader chip industry. Students at select South Korean universities will be able to enroll in company-linked degree programs that provide a direct pathway to employment at Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, the country's two largest chipmakers, according to the announcement.

  ## Export backdrop

  The budget unveiling came alongside strong trade data. South Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources reported on November 1 that monthly exports had hit an all-time high, with cumulative exports for the year surpassing US$ 900 billion. Semiconductor exports posted the highest level for any October on record. Lee said the country was within reach of achieving US$ 1 trillion in annual exports.

  "It is not enough for the national economy to simply grow. Those outcomes must connect to opportunities and changes in the lives of each and every citizen," Lee said in a statement posted to X. "We will create opportunities for youth to take on challenges, provide small business owners with the strength to rise again, and build new engines of growth for our regions."

  ## Debt financing

  Lee said the increased spending would result in virtually no net increase in government debt. The government plans to issue new bonds to repay existing obligations rather than expand the overall debt load, meaning the higher expenditure is structured as a refinancing exercise rather than a net borrowing increase.

  ## National AI data center program

  The 2027 budget extends commitments Lee announced in June, when he unveiled three megaprojects aimed at advancing South Korea's position in critical technologies. One of those projects is a national AI data center program that includes an 8.4-gigawatt (GW) facility — a figure that refers to total power capacity planned for the site — set to be built by 2029 in partnership with the private sector.

  ## What it means

  The combined allocation across AI infrastructure, sovereign AI model development, semiconductor investment, and workforce training represents a coordinated national strategy linking chip supply, AI capability, digital infrastructure, and employment into a single budget cycle. The near-doubling of the AI and semiconductor line from 2026 to 2027, alongside the 8.4 GW data center program already in motion, indicates the government is accelerating rather than initiating this push. The 8.4 GW facility is scheduled for completion by 2029.

  依據W.Media援引CNBC的報導，2027年預算較前一年度成長12.8%。此外，政府另行劃撥4.7兆韓元（約35億美元），專門用於開發「主權AI模型」——一套由政府主導、旨在擺脫對境外業者依賴的本土基礎AI系統。

  ## 人才培育與教育投資

  政府將培育2,000名專業人才，以引領半導體設計及整體晶片產業的發展。根據本次公告，部分南韓大學的學生將可就讀與企業合作的學位課程，課程設計直接銜接三星電子（Samsung Electronics）與SK海力士（SK Hynix）——南韓規模最大的兩家晶片製造商——的就業管道。

  ## 出口背景

  預算發布之際，恰逢南韓交出亮眼的貿易成績單。南韓產業通商資源部於11月1日公布，當月出口額創下歷史新高，年度累計出口金額已突破9,000億美元。其中，半導體出口更締造了有史以來最高的10月份單月紀錄。李在明表示，南韓距離實現年度出口額1兆美元的目標已近在咫尺。

  李在明在X平台官方帳號發文表示：「國家經濟的成長本身並不足夠，這些成果必須落實為每一位國民生活中可感受到的機會與改變。我們將為年輕人創造勇於挑戰的機會，為小型企業主提供重新出發的力量，並為各地區打造新的成長引擎。」

  ## 債務融資

  李在明表示，支出規模的擴大幾乎不會造成政府淨債務的實質增加。政府計畫透過發行新債來償還現有債務，而非擴大整體債務規模，意味著此次支出增加是以再融資（refinancing）的形式運作，並非淨借貸的增加。

  ## 國家AI資料中心計畫

  2027年預算延續了李在明今年6月所宣示的政策承諾。當時，他公布了三項旨在提升南韓關鍵技術競爭力的重大計畫，其中之一即為國家AI資料中心計畫。該計畫包含一座總電力容量達8.4吉瓦（GW）的設施，預計以公私合作方式於2029年完工啟用。

  ## 政策意涵

  此次橫跨AI基礎建設、主權AI模型開發、半導體投資及人才培育的綜合預算配置，體現了一套將晶片供應、AI能力、數位基礎建設與就業市場緊密連結於同一預算周期的國家協調戰略。AI與半導體預算線項從2026年到2027年近乎倍增，加上已啟動的8.4吉瓦資料中心計畫，顯示政府正在加速推進這波布局，而非才剛起步。上述8.4吉瓦設施預定於2029年竣工。
