body_en: |
  Indonesia has joined 28 other countries as a founding member of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO), a new intergovernmental body created to govern and develop artificial intelligence in ways its founders describe as inclusive, safe, and human-centered. Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto announced the membership on July 17, saying Indonesia is also preparing a national AI roadmap to guide technology development across economic sectors.

  The Indonesian cabinet approved the decision to join WAICO in a restricted meeting on July 13, Airlangga said. He described the move as a follow-through on direction from President Prabowo Subianto to accelerate domestic AI development and application.

  "Indonesia's presence in the organization is expected to help bridge the technology gap while strengthening the development of AI that is safe, trustworthy, and ethical," Airlangga said in a media briefing delivered via videoconference.

  ## Founding membership and timeline

  As of the announcement, 29 countries had signed onto WAICO's founding documents. Named signatories include Malaysia, Cambodia, Russia, Cuba, and Oman. The founding window remains open until July 31, meaning the membership count could still rise.

  WAICO was formally announced at the opening ceremony of the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) 2026 in Shanghai. Chinese President Xi Jinping, United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres, and leaders of other founding states attended the launch. The organization is structured as an intergovernmental body operating under the principles of the UN Charter.

  Xi Jinping, in remarks at the launch, argued that AI development should not be dominated by a single country. "AI development should not be a solo performance by one country, but a symphony of global collaboration," Xi said, according to the Bisnis.com report.

  Guterres welcomed the formation of WAICO as a multilateral forum for responsible and inclusive AI use, and said the body could support achievement of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

  ## Indonesia's stated objectives

  Airlangga said membership is intended to give Indonesia improved access to technology transfer, expanded research collaboration, and a seat in shaping global AI governance rules. He said a specific goal is ensuring that small and medium-sized enterprises — a large segment of the Indonesian economy — have equal opportunity to adopt and benefit from AI tools.

  The national AI roadmap Airlangga referenced would serve as a guiding framework for AI deployment across sectors, though no timeline for its completion was given in the announcement.

  For developing nations more broadly, Indonesian officials framed WAICO membership as a mechanism to ensure that countries outside the current AI technology frontier have their interests represented in governance frameworks being written now.

  ## Indonesia-China digital and economic ties

  The WAICO announcement coincided with a broader diplomatic and trade visit to Shanghai. During the trip, Indonesia signed a memorandum of understanding with Chinese Commerce Minister Wang Wentao covering evaluation of industrial and trade cooperation, including the digital sector.

  Airlangga cited the current scale of the bilateral economic relationship: Indonesia-China trade stands at approximately $160 billion, while Chinese direct investment in Indonesia totals around $7 billion, with an additional roughly $10 billion flowing through Hong Kong. He was also scheduled to meet with Huawei and BYD during the visit to discuss technology cooperation and AI investment opportunities, according to the report.

  印尼與其他28個國家共同成為世界人工智慧合作組織（WAICO）創始會員國。WAICO是一個新設的政府間機構，旨在以創始國所稱「具包容性、安全且以人為本」的方式治理並推動人工智慧發展。印尼統籌經濟部長艾爾朗加·哈爾塔托（Airlangga Hartarto）於7月17日宣布入會消息，並表示印尼正籌備一份國家AI路線圖，以引導人工智慧技術在各經濟部門的發展。

  艾爾朗加表示，印尼內閣已於7月13日的限制性會議中核准加入WAICO的決定。他指出，此舉是落實總統普拉博沃·蘇比安托（Prabowo Subianto）加速國內AI發展與應用指示的具體行動。

  「印尼參與這個組織，有望協助縮小技術落差，同時強化安全、可信賴且符合倫理的AI發展，」艾爾朗加在視訊媒體簡報會上表示。

  ## 創始會員資格與時程

  截至公告時，已有29個國家簽署WAICO創始文件，明確列名的簽署國包括馬來西亞、柬埔寨、俄羅斯、古巴及阿曼。由於創始窗口將開放至7月31日，會員國數量仍有可能持續增加。

  WAICO於上海世界人工智慧大會（WAIC）2026開幕典禮上正式宣告成立，中國國家主席習近平、聯合國秘書長安東尼奧·古特雷斯（Antonio Guterres）及多個創始國領導人均出席了成立典禮。該組織以政府間機構形式運作，依聯合國憲章原則為基礎。

  習近平在成立典禮的致詞中主張，AI發展不應由單一國家主導。根據Bisnis.com的報導，習近平表示：「AI發展不應是一國的獨角戲，而應是全球合作的交響樂章。」

  古特雷斯對WAICO作為負責任且具包容性AI應用多邊論壇的成立表示歡迎，並指出該組織有助於推進聯合國永續發展目標的實現。

  ## 印尼的既定目標

  艾爾朗加表示，加入WAICO旨在讓印尼獲得更好的技術移轉管道、擴大研究合作空間，並在形塑全球AI治理規則的過程中取得一席之地。他說，一個明確目標是確保中小企業——印尼經濟的重要支柱——在採用及受益於AI工具方面享有平等機會。

  艾爾朗加所提及的國家AI路線圖，將作為各部門AI應用的指導框架，惟公告中未說明完成的具體期限。

  印尼官員就廣大開發中國家而言，將加入WAICO定位為一項重要機制，目的是確保目前尚未位居AI技術前沿的國家，其利益能在當前正在制定的治理框架中獲得充分代表。

  ## 印尼與中國的數位及經濟關係

  WAICO公告恰逢印尼展開對上海更廣泛的外交與貿易訪問行程。訪問期間，印尼與中國商務部長王文濤簽署了一份備忘錄，內容涵蓋工業與貿易合作的評估，包含數位領域在內。

  艾爾朗加引述了雙邊經濟往來的現況規模：印中貿易額約達1,600億美元，中國對印尼的直接投資約為70億美元，另有約100億美元透過香港流入。根據報導，他在此次訪問行程中也預計分別與華為及比亞迪（BYD）會面，商討技術合作及AI投資機會。
