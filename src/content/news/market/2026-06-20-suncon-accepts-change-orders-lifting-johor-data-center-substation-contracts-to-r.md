body_en: |
  Sunway Construction Group Berhad (SunCon), a Malaysian vertically integrated construction firm, has accepted change orders that lift the total value of two data center substation contracts in Johor to RM 865.6 million (US$209.3 million), up RM 664.4 million (US$160.7 million) from the prior award, the company said in a Bursa Malaysia filing this week.

  The original contract was a Project Services Request accepted by SunCon on April 21, 2026, covering the construction and completion of electrical substations — the high-voltage switching and transformer infrastructure that feeds power to data center campuses — for the two Johor projects. The work was commissioned by an unnamed multinational technology corporation headquartered in the United States.

  The revised figure of RM 865.6 million is stated inclusive of withholding taxes, sales and service tax, and other applicable taxes, the company said. One project is targeted for completion in the fourth quarter of 2027, with the second scheduled for the second quarter of 2028.

  ## Financial impact

  SunCon said the contracts will not affect its share capital or the shareholdings of substantial shareholders because no new shares will be issued. The company expects both projects to contribute positively to earnings for its financial year ending December 31, 2026.

  The awards add to a strong order-intake year for SunCon. The company has secured RM 4.2 billion in new orders so far in the current financial year, and its total outstanding order book now stands at RM 8.8 billion — already exceeding the value of its full-year 2025 order book.

  ## Data center exposure

  SunCon has concentrated a significant share of its recent business in the data center sector. In the 2025 financial year the company secured RM 2.9 billion in what it described as high-growth data center projects. The Johor substation awards, and the scale of the change orders applied to them, indicate that demand from hyperscale and large enterprise clients in the region has continued at pace into 2026.

  Johor, which borders Singapore and has attracted substantial investment in land-intensive data center development, remains a focal point for that activity. The identity of the US multinational client has not been disclosed.

  SunCon's outstanding order book of RM 8.8 billion, reached before the financial year is complete, is the most forward-looking figure the company has disclosed.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞垂直整合型建設公司陽光建設集團（Sunway Construction Group Berhad，簡稱SunCon）本週向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）申報，宣布接受變更令，將柔佛州兩座資料中心變電站合約的合計總值調升至8.656億令吉（約2.093億美元），較先前得標金額增加6.644億令吉（約1.607億美元）。

  原始合約係SunCon於2026年4月21日接受的「專案服務申請」（Project Services Request），內容涵蓋為柔佛州兩項目建造並完工電力變電站——即為資料中心園區供電的高壓開關及變壓器基礎設施。該工程係由一家總部位於美國、名稱未予披露的跨國科技企業委託興建。

  公司表示，修訂後的8.656億令吉總額已涵蓋預扣稅、銷售與服務稅及其他適用稅款。其中一項工程預計於2027年第四季完工，另一項則排定於2028年第二季竣工。

  ## 財務影響

  SunCon表示，由於不涉及新股發行，上述合約不會影響公司股本或主要股東持股比例。公司預期兩項工程均將對截至2026年12月31日止財政年度的盈利產生正面貢獻。

  此次得標為SunCon今年以來強勁的接單表現再添一筆。公司在本財政年度迄今已取得42億令吉新訂單，未完成訂單總額目前達88億令吉，在財政年度尚未結束之際便已超越2025年全年訂單簿的規模。

  ## 資料中心業務佈局

  SunCon近期業務已將相當大比重集中於資料中心領域。在2025財政年度，公司共承接其所稱「高成長資料中心專案」合計29億令吉。柔佛州此次變電站合約及其大規模變更令，顯示區域內超大規模及大型企業客戶的需求在2026年持續維持強勁步伐。

  毗鄰新加坡的柔佛州因地價相對低廉、土地供應充裕，吸引了大量資料中心投資湧入，至今仍是此類發展的重要樞紐。美國跨國客戶的身份目前尚未對外揭露。

  SunCon在財政年度結束前即已達到88億令吉的未完成訂單規模，是公司迄今披露最具前瞻性的一項數據。
