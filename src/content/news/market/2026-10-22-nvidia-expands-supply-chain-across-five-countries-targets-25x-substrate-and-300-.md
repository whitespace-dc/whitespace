body_en: |
  NVIDIA on October 21 announced agreements with six manufacturing partners spanning Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, the United States, and Singapore to expand capacity for IC substrates, advanced packaging, semiconductor testing, and optical engines, targeting a 2.5-fold increase in substrates and more than 300 percent growth in advanced packaging by 2028.

  The named partners are Amkor, IBIDEN, KYEC (King Yuan Electronics), Samsung Electro-Mechanics, SPIL (Siliconware Precision Industries), and Unimicron. The agreements combine capacity reservation prepayments — including billions of dollars to IBIDEN and a disclosed $1.1 billion to KYEC — with multi-year build-out timelines extending through 2028.

  NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang said the company is in the "largest infrastructure build-out in human history" and identified capacity as "the core limiting factor." Huang thanked supply chain partners for "actively expanding capacity globally at scale to help us deliver the AI supercomputing systems needed to drive this new industrial revolution."

  ## Substrate supply: 2.5x total capacity in three years, three partners

  NVIDIA plans to grow total IC substrate capacity — the layered circuit boards that mount chips and carry power and signal connections — to 2.5 times the current level within three years, through deals with IBIDEN, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, and Unimicron.

  IBIDEN, the Japanese substrate manufacturer, is expanding its Ono factory to three times its current capacity under an agreement signed and disclosed earlier in 2026. NVIDIA provided billions of dollars in capacity reservation prepayments for the Ono build-out; new production is scheduled to begin ramping in the first quarter of 2028.

  Samsung Electro-Mechanics is entering NVIDIA's substrate supply chain for the first time, initially providing capacity from its existing South Korean operations. The partnership also covers factory upgrades and new construction at Samsung Electro-Mechanics facilities in Sejong and Busan. By 2028, NVIDIA-allocated substrates are expected to account for a "significant proportion" of Samsung Electro-Mechanics' total annual package substrate output, the announcement said.

  Unimicron, a Taiwanese IC substrate maker, signed a capacity reservation agreement covering the expansion of its KF substrate production in Taiwan. The new capacity is scheduled to come online in the second quarter of 2027 and reach full utilization by mid-2028.

  ## Advanced packaging: Amkor to grow South Korean interposer wafer capacity more than 300%

  NVIDIA and Amkor plan to increase interposer wafer capacity in South Korea by more than 300 percent by the end of 2028. Interposers are intermediary silicon connection layers used to assemble multi-die chip packages at high density. The expansion coincides with Amkor developing new manufacturing facilities in South Korea and will support both current and next-generation NVIDIA high-end platforms, according to the announcement.

  ## Testing: $1.1 billion prepayment to KYEC for U.S. site, total capacity to double by 2027

  NVIDIA projects total semiconductor test capacity will double between 2026 and 2027, backed by facility build-outs in the United States, Taiwan, and Singapore.

  The single largest disclosed prepayment for testing is $1.1 billion to KYEC for new facilities and equipment the company will build in the United States, targeting high-volume production by April 2027. KYEC is a Taiwanese semiconductor testing company.

  NVIDIA is also expanding test operations in Singapore, with that facility targeting large-scale production by February 2027.

  ## Optical engines: SPIL targeting 15x capacity increase by mid-2027, then doubling again

  NVIDIA and Taiwan's SPIL are expanding optical engine production to support co-packaged optics (CPO), a packaging approach that integrates optical components directly alongside compute chips to meet high-speed interconnect and communications demands at large-computing scale.

  The expansion is staged across two phases. SPIL is targeting a 15-fold increase in optical engine capacity by mid-2027. A further doubling is targeted by the end of 2028, for a combined increase of roughly 30 times the current production baseline.

  ## Scope and forward timeline

  The program spans five countries and four segments of the chip manufacturing supply chain: substrates, advanced packaging, back-end testing, and optical interconnect components. NVIDIA said the initiative is intended to strengthen supply chain resilience and redundancy while meeting what it described as unprecedented demand for its accelerated computing and AI factory platforms.

  The latest capacity milestone in the disclosed timeline is Unimicron's KF substrate expansion in Taiwan reaching full utilization in mid-2028.
body_zh: |
  輝達（NVIDIA）於10月21日宣布與六家製造夥伴簽署協議，合作範圍橫跨台灣、日本、韓國、美國及新加坡，目標是擴充IC基板、先進封裝、半導體測試及光引擎的生產產能，預計至2028年，IC基板總產能將提升至現有水準的2.5倍，先進封裝成長幅度更將超過300%。

  六家合作夥伴分別為Amkor、IBIDEN、京元電子（KYEC）、三星電機（Samsung Electro-Mechanics）、矽品精密（SPIL）及欣興電子（Unimicron）。協議內容涵蓋產能預留預付款——其中包括向IBIDEN支付數十億美元，以及向京元電子支付已對外揭露的11億美元——並附帶延伸至2028年的多年擴建時程。

  輝達創辦人暨執行長黃仁勳表示，公司正身處「人類有史以來規模最大的基礎建設擴張」浪潮之中，並指出產能是「核心限制因素」。黃仁勳感謝供應鏈夥伴「積極在全球各地大規模擴充產能，協助我們交付推動這場新工業革命所需的AI超級運算系統」。

  ## 基板供應：三年內總產能增至2.5倍，三家夥伴聯手布局

  輝達計畫透過與IBIDEN、三星電機及欣興電子的合作，在三年內將IC基板總產能提升至現有水準的2.5倍。IC基板是用於承載晶片、傳導電源與訊號連接的多層電路板。

  日本基板製造商IBIDEN正在將旗下小野廠的產能擴大至目前的三倍，相關協議已於2026年稍早簽署並對外揭露。輝達為小野廠的擴建計畫提供了數十億美元的產能預留預付款；新產能預計於2028年第一季開始放量生產。

  三星電機此次首度加入輝達的基板供應鏈，初期將由其現有的韓國廠區供貨。此合作夥伴關係亦涵蓋三星電機位於世宗（Sejong）及釜山（Busan）廠區的升級改造與新建工程。根據公告，預計至2028年，輝達配額的基板將佔三星電機年度封裝基板總出貨量的「相當大比例」。

  台灣IC基板廠欣興電子簽署了一份產能預留協議，內容涵蓋其在台灣的KF基板產能擴充。新增產能預計於2027年第二季上線投產，並於2028年中達到滿載。

  ## 先進封裝：Amkor韓國中介層晶圓產能將增逾300%

  輝達與Amkor計畫於2028年底前，將韓國的中介層（interposer）晶圓產能提升逾300%。中介層是一種矽材質的中介連接層，用於高密度多晶粒晶片封裝的組裝。此次擴產恰逢Amkor在韓國開發新製造設施，並將同時支援輝達現有及下一代高階平台，公告指出。

  ## 測試：向京元電子預付11億美元布局美國廠區，總產能目標於2027年倍增

  輝達預計2026年至2027年間，半導體測試總產能將翻倍，支撐力道來自美國、台灣及新加坡的廠房建設。

  在測試領域，單筆最高揭露預付款為向台灣半導體測試大廠京元電子預付的11億美元，用於在美國興建新廠房及採購設備，目標是於2027年4月前達到大量生產規模。

  輝達同步在新加坡擴大測試業務，該廠目標於2027年2月前達到大規模量產。

  ## 光引擎：矽品目標2027年中產能增至15倍，其後再翻倍

  輝達與台灣矽品精密攜手擴大光引擎生產規模，以支援共封裝光學（CPO，co-packaged optics）技術。CPO是一種將光學元件直接與運算晶片整合封裝的方式，旨在滿足大型運算場域對高速互連與通訊的需求。

  擴產計畫分兩階段執行。矽品目標於2027年中前，將光引擎產能提升至目前的15倍；其後至2028年底前再翻倍，兩階段合計產能增幅約為現有產能基準的30倍。

  ## 布局範疇與時程展望

  整體計畫橫跨五個國家、涵蓋晶片製造供應鏈的四大環節：基板、先進封裝、後段測試及光學互連元件。輝達表示，此計畫旨在強化供應鏈韌性與備援能力，同時因應其加速運算與AI工廠平台前所未見的龐大需求。

  目前已揭露時程中最晚的產能里程碑，為欣興電子在台灣的KF基板擴產計畫於2028年中達到滿載。
