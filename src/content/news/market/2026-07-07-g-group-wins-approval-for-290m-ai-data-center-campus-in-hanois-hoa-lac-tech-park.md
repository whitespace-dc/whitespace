body_en: |
  Vietnamese technology investment firm G-Group has received government investment certification for G-Campus, a 7,600 billion VND (approximately $290 million) artificial intelligence data center campus in the Hoa Lac High-Tech Park on the outskirts of Hanoi, the company announced. At the same ceremony, G-Group signed a strategic cooperation agreement with the Hanoi People's Committee and OTECH, an Omani information and communications technology company, to jointly invest in, develop, and operate AI data centers in Vietnam.

  ## Project scope and technical specifications

  G-Campus will occupy more than 38,000 square meters within Hoa Lac High-Tech Park and is structured as four functional zones. Zones 1 and 2 are designated for research and development of AI, high-performance computing (HPC), and cybersecurity products. Zones 3 and 4 will house a data center targeting Tier III certification from the Uptime Institute, the independent body that sets global data center reliability standards, with an initial IT power capacity of 20 megawatts (MW), expandable to 30 MW. For reference, 20 MW of IT load is sufficient to support large-scale cloud or AI training workloads and is broadly considered a mid-to-large facility by regional standards.

  ## Partner roles under the cooperation agreement

  The three parties have divided responsibilities under the cooperation framework. The Hanoi People's Committee will connect ecosystem partners, develop necessary infrastructure, and ensure regulatory compliance. OTECH, which is part of the Omantel Group, an Omani telecommunications operator, will contribute experience in operating national-scale data center infrastructure and sovereign cloud management. G-Group will handle investment, capital structure, and commercialization of the facility in the Vietnamese market, according to the company's press release.

  ## Strategic rationale

  Phung Anh Tu, Chairman of G-Group, said the project is intended as a long-term commitment to Hanoi's digital development. "What G-Group aims for is not to build the largest data center, but to build a platform that is modern enough, secure enough, and open enough to serve research, technology development, digital transformation, and large-scale AI applications," he said, as quoted in the company's statement.

  ## Alignment with Hanoi's digital infrastructure targets

  G-Campus is positioned to support Hanoi's stated goal of achieving an average of four Internet of Things (IoT) connections per citizen by 2030 and establishing a digital government model, according to the company's statement. Hanoi has projected it will need at least two mega data centers and a network of edge data centers to handle rising data volumes as those targets are pursued.
body_zh: |
  越南科技投資公司G-Group宣布，已獲得政府投資認證，將在河內近郊的和樂高科技園區興建人工智慧資料中心園區G-Campus，總投資額達7.6兆越南盾（約2.9億美元）。同場典禮上，G-Group與河內人民委員會及阿曼資通訊科技業者OTECH簽署三方策略合作協議，三方將共同投資、開發並營運越南境內的AI資料中心。

  ## 專案規模與技術規格

  G-Campus將在和樂高科技園區內占地逾3萬8千平方公尺，整體規劃分為四大功能區。第一區與第二區專用於人工智慧、高效能運算（HPC）及資安產品的研究開發；第三區與第四區則將設置資料中心，目標取得Uptime Institute的Tier III認證——Uptime Institute為制定全球資料中心可靠性標準的國際獨立機構。資料中心初始IT供電容量設定為20百萬瓦（MW），未來可擴充至30 MW。就規模而言，20 MW的IT負載足以支援大規模雲端或AI模型訓練工作負載，以區域標準衡量屬於中大型設施。

  ## 合作協議下各方職責

  依據三方合作框架，各方分工明確。河內人民委員會負責串聯生態系夥伴、建設必要基礎設施，並確保各項法規遵循。OTECH隸屬於阿曼電信業者Omantel集團，將貢獻其在國家級資料中心基礎設施運營及主權雲端管理方面的豐富實務經驗。G-Group則負責投資規劃、資本結構安排，以及推動該設施在越南市場的商業化落地，以上資訊均依據公司新聞稿所載。

  ## 策略布局

  G-Group董事長Phung Anh Tu表示，此專案代表集團對河內數位發展的長期承諾。他在公司聲明中被引述說道：「G-Group的目標，不是打造規模最大的資料中心，而是建立一個夠現代、夠安全、夠開放的平台，以服務研究、技術開發、數位轉型及大規模AI應用。」

  ## 與河內數位基礎建設目標的契合

  根據公司聲明，G-Campus的定位是支援河內官方設定的2030年目標——達到平均每位市民擁有四個物聯網（IoT）連線，並建立數位政府治理模式。河內預估，隨著上述目標的推進，資料流量將持續攀升，屆時至少需要兩座超大型資料中心，以及一套邊緣資料中心網路，方能因應與日俱增的資料處理需求。
