body_en: |
  Indonesian property developer Sinarmas Land has formalized partnerships with Monash University Indonesia and Malaysia's Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM) to develop an artificial intelligence center and a medical research cluster within a 60-hectare special economic zone it is building at Bumi Serpong Damai (BSD) City, a large planned township in Banten province west of Jakarta.

  Both agreements were signed at the opening of the Global Sustainable Development Congress (GSDC) 2026 at ICE BSD City, an event the company said drew thousands of delegates from 120 countries.

  ## Deals and their scope

  The two agreements differ in legal weight and focus. Sinarmas Land's Digital Hub entity signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) — a formal commitment to collaborate — with Monash University Indonesia covering education, research, and digital talent development. Under the MoU, Monash University will provide technical support for the construction of an AI Center of Excellence at BSD City. Sinarmas Land said the facility is intended to produce artificial intelligence innovation integrated with smart city requirements and future industries.

  The second document, a Letter of Intent (LOI) signed with UKM, is a preliminary expression of interest rather than a binding commitment. It covers plans for joint exploration in academic, research, innovation, medical, and health sectors, with activity to be located in the KEK ETKI corridor at BSD City.

  Ferdinand Sadeli, Deputy Group CEO for Investment International Tech and Emerging at Sinarmas Land, signed both documents. Professor Matthew Nicholson, Pro Vice-Chancellor and President of Monash University Indonesia, signed the MoU on Monash's behalf. Professor Dato' Dr. Sufian bin Jusoh, Vice-Chancellor of UKM, signed the LOI.

  ## The special economic zone

  Both agreements are oriented toward the KEK ETKI — Indonesia's term for a Special Economic Zone (Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus, or KEK) designated for Education, Technology, and International Health (ETKI), also referred to by Sinarmas Land as D-HUB SEZ. The zone occupies 60 hectares within BSD City.

  The company said the overarching intent is to apply research capabilities from international institutions toward producing internationally skilled workers domestically, rather than relying on Indonesians studying abroad.

  ## Existing education base

  Sinarmas Land cited an existing education ecosystem at BSD City as the foundation for this expansion. The township already hosts more than 233 actively operating educational institutions, including more than 64 pre-school institutions, the company said.

  ## What it means

  The deals extend Sinarmas Land's effort to anchor BSD City as an international hub for education and technology by adding AI and health research as defined functions within the KEK ETKI zone. The Monash MoU identifies a specific deliverable — the AI Center of Excellence — while the UKM LOI leaves the precise scope of medical and health collaboration to be determined in subsequent negotiations.

  The AI Center of Excellence, backed by Monash University's technical support, is the most concrete near-term development the announcements identify.

  印尼房地產開發商金光置地（Sinarmas Land）已正式與印尼莫納什大學及馬來西亞國立大學（UKM）攜手，計畫在位於雅加達西側萬丹省大型規劃城市布米瑟龐達邁（Bumi Serpong Damai，BSD）市的一座占地60公頃特別經濟區內，開發一座人工智慧中心與醫療研究聚落。

  兩份協議均在2026年全球永續發展大會（Global Sustainable Development Congress，GSDC）於ICE BSD市的開幕式上完成簽署。金光置地表示，此次大會吸引來自120個國家的數千名代表與會。

  ## 協議內容與範疇

  兩份文件在法律效力與業務重點上各有不同。金光置地旗下Digital Hub實體與印尼莫納什大學簽署了一份涵蓋教育、研究及數位人才培育的合作備忘錄（MoU），屬於正式的合作承諾。根據備忘錄，莫納什大學將為BSD市AI卓越中心的建設提供技術支援。金光置地表示，該設施旨在產出與智慧城市需求及未來產業相結合的人工智慧創新成果。

  第二份文件則是與UKM簽署的意向書（LOI），屬於初步的合作意向表達，尚不具法律約束力。意向書涵蓋在學術、研究、創新、醫療及健康領域進行聯合探索的計畫，相關活動將落地於BSD市的KEK ETKI廊帶。

  金光置地投資國際科技與新興事業副集團執行長費迪南·薩德里（Ferdinand Sadeli）代表公司簽署了上述兩份文件。莫納什大學印尼分校副校長兼校長馬修·尼可森教授（Professor Matthew Nicholson）代表莫納什方面簽署備忘錄；UKM副校長蘇菲安·賓·朱索教授（Professor Dato' Dr. Sufian bin Jusoh）則代表UKM簽署意向書。

  ## 特別經濟區簡介

  兩份協議均以KEK ETKI為核心，這是印尼針對教育、科技與國際健康（Education, Technology and International Health，ETKI）所設立的特別經濟區（Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus，KEK），金光置地亦將其稱為D-HUB SEZ。該經濟區占地60公頃，坐落於BSD市境內。

  金光置地表示，此計畫的整體目標在於引入國際機構的研究能量，在國內培育具國際競爭力的人才，從而減少印尼學生赴海外求學的依賴。

  ## 既有教育基礎

  金光置地指出，BSD市現有的教育生態系是推動本次擴展的基石。據該公司表示，這座城市目前已有逾233所正式運作的教育機構進駐，其中包括64所以上的學前教育機構。

  ## 協議意義

  此次協議進一步強化了金光置地將BSD市打造為國際教育與科技樞紐的既定方向，同時也正式將AI與健康研究列為KEK ETKI經濟區的核心功能。其中，與莫納什大學簽署的備忘錄明確指向一項具體成果——AI卓越中心；而與UKM簽署的意向書則將醫療與健康合作的確切範疇，留待後續談判進一步定案。

  由莫納什大學提供技術支援的AI卓越中心，是本次公告中最具體、最可能在近期內付諸實現的發展項目。
