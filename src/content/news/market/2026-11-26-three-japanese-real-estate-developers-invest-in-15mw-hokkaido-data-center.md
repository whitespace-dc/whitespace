body_en: |
  Three Japanese real estate developers have announced investments in a 15-megawatt (MW) data center in Ishikari City, Hokkaido. Daibiru, Sankei Building, and Osaka Gas Urban Development will invest in the Ishikari Renewable Energy Data Center No. 1, a facility developed and managed by Tokyu Land Corporation that entered commercial service on August 1, 2026.

  For Daibiru and Osaka Gas Urban Development, the transaction marks their first entry into the data center market. Sankei Building has limited prior exposure through one earlier project.

  ## The facility

  The Ishikari Renewable Energy Data Center No. 1 occupies approximately 10,000 square meters (108,400 sq ft) and is supplied by renewable energy plants in Ishikari City. Tokyu Land Corporation developed the site and continues to manage it.

  ## Investor backgrounds and rationale

  Daibiru, founded in 1923, has historically focused on office real estate in Osaka and Tokyo. The company said the investment forms part of a strategy to expand capital-gains investment in Japan and overseas, which it described as a priority. Daibiru cited widespread adoption of generative artificial intelligence (AI) and the resulting increase in data center demand as the reason for expanding its investment scope into the sector. The company said it aims to capture new business opportunities in data centers and build sector expertise.

  Sankei Building, established in 1951, has concentrated on residential, office, and commercial development. In 2023, the company joined broadcaster Kansai Television to develop a 13-story carrier-neutral facility in Osaka for Japanese internet service provider Optage, its first data center project. Carrier-neutral means the building offers connectivity from multiple network operators, allowing tenants to select their provider. The Hokkaido investment is Sankei's second data center commitment.

  Osaka Gas Urban Development is the real estate and urban development subsidiary of the Daigas Group. Its prior work has focused on residential and commercial district development, and this is its first investment in a data center asset.

  Daibiru said it intends to use the Hokkaido investment to build data center expertise, with the company describing expansion into the sector as one of its priority strategies.
body_zh: |
  三家日本不動產開發商宣布，將共同投資位於北海道石狩市、容量達15百萬瓦（MW）的資料中心。大備魯（Daibiru）、產經建設（Sankei Building）及大阪燃氣都市開發（Osaka Gas Urban Development）確認加入由東急不動產（Tokyu Land Corporation）開發並持續營運的「石狩再生能源資料中心一號館」，該設施已於2026年8月1日正式投入商業營運。

  對大備魯及大阪燃氣都市開發而言，此次交易標誌著兩家公司首度跨足資料中心市場；產經建設則曾透過一件先前的專案累積過有限的相關經驗。

  ## 設施概況

  「石狩再生能源資料中心一號館」佔地約一萬平方公尺（約10萬8,400平方英尺），電力來源為石狩市境內的再生能源發電設施。東急不動產負責整體開發，並持續執行營運管理工作。

  ## 各投資方背景與投資理由

  大備魯創立於1923年，長期深耕大阪與東京的辦公室不動產市場。該公司表示，此次投資是將資本利得投資擴展至日本國內外的策略重點之一。大備魯指出，生成式人工智慧（AI）的廣泛普及帶動資料中心需求大幅成長，是促使該公司將投資觸角延伸至這一領域的主要原因；公司表示希望藉此掌握資料中心的新商機，並深化相關產業專業知識。

  產經建設成立於1951年，業務重心集中於住宅、辦公室及商業開發。2023年，該公司與關西電視台（Kansai Television）合作，在大阪為日本網路服務供應商歐普太吉（Optage）共同開發一棟13層樓的電信中立機房大樓，成為其首項資料中心專案。所謂「電信中立」，是指建築物可同時引入多家電信業者的網路連線，讓承租戶自由選擇服務供應商。此次北海道投資，是產經建設的第二個資料中心承諾。

  大阪燃氣都市開發是大阪燃氣集團（Daigas Group）旗下的不動產與都市開發子公司，過去業務以住宅及商業區域開發為主，此次為該公司首度投資資料中心資產。

  大備魯表示，計畫借助此次北海道投資建立資料中心領域的專業能力，並將拓展至此一產業列為優先發展策略之一。
