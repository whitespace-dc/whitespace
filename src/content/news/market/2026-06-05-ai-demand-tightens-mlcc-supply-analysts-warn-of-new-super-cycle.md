body_en: |
  Demand from artificial intelligence infrastructure is pushing supply of high-end multilayer ceramic capacitors (MLCCs) — small passive components used to filter and store electrical charge in electronic circuits — toward tightness, with analysts flagging the conditions as a potential precursor to a new industry super cycle, according to a report by DigiTimes Asia.

  MLCCs are among the most widely used passive components in electronics, appearing in servers, power modules, and networking equipment in large volumes. High-end variants, which operate at tighter tolerances and higher capacitances than commodity parts, are increasingly required by AI accelerator boards and the dense power-delivery circuitry of GPU-based servers.

  ## Supply tightness and the super cycle warning

  DigiTimes Asia reported that supply warnings have emerged specifically for high-end passive components, with the MLCC segment drawing particular attention. The publication described the situation as positioning the MLCC market for a rally, using the term "super cycle" — an extended period of elevated demand and pricing that exceeds normal cyclical patterns — to characterize what analysts see as a possible trajectory.

  The report did not specify which manufacturers or buyers are most directly affected, nor did it provide shipment figures, pricing data, or timelines for when tightness might peak or ease.

  ## AI infrastructure as demand driver

  The source attributes the supply pressure directly to AI-related buildout, consistent with the broader expansion of data center capacity to support large language models and related workloads. AI servers typically carry significantly higher passive component counts per unit than conventional servers, amplifying demand even when unit shipments grow modestly.

  ## Industry significance

  If the conditions described by DigiTimes Asia develop into a sustained super cycle, MLCC producers — predominantly based in Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan — would be positioned for an extended revenue expansion, while system builders and contract manufacturers could face component allocation constraints and cost pressure. The last major MLCC super cycle, driven by automotive and smartphone demand, resulted in widespread shortages and sharp price increases across the supply chain.

  ## AI需求拉緊高階MLCC供應，分析師示警新一輪超級週期來襲

  根據《DigiTimes Asia》的報導，來自人工智慧基礎建設的需求正推動高階多層陶瓷電容（MLCC）——一種用於電子電路中濾波與儲存電荷的小型被動元件——的供應趨向吃緊，分析師指出此一情況可能是新一輪產業超級週期的前兆。

  MLCC是電子產品中使用最廣泛的被動元件之一，大量應用於伺服器、電源模組與網路設備之中。相較於一般規格品，高階MLCC具備更嚴格的公差與更高的電容值，在AI加速卡以及GPU伺服器密集的電源供應電路上的需求正與日俱增。

  ## 供應吃緊與超級週期示警

  《DigiTimes Asia》報導，高階被動元件的供應警訊已然浮現，其中MLCC領域尤受關注。該媒體以「超級週期」——意指需求與價格長期偏高、超越正常景氣循環的一段時期——來形容分析師所預判的可能走向，並指出MLCC市場正蓄積反彈動能。

  報導並未具體指出哪些製造商或採購方受到最直接的衝擊，亦未提供出貨量數據、定價資訊，或供應吃緊何時可能達到高峰或趨於緩和的時程。

  ## AI基礎建設作為需求驅動力

  報導將供應壓力的成因直接歸因於AI相關建設的擴張，與資料中心為支撐大型語言模型及相關工作負載而大幅擴充產能的整體趨勢一致。AI伺服器每台所需的被動元件數量，通常遠高於傳統伺服器，因此即便整機出貨量僅溫和成長，對被動元件的需求也會大幅放大。

  ## 產業影響

  若《DigiTimes Asia》所描述的情況演變為持續性的超級週期，以日本、南韓與台灣為主要生產基地的MLCC製造商，將迎來一段較長的營收擴張期；而系統組裝業者與電子代工廠則可能面臨元件配額受限與成本上升的壓力。上一次重大的MLCC超級週期由汽車與智慧型手機需求所驅動，最終導致整個供應鏈出現大規模缺貨與價格急漲的局面。
