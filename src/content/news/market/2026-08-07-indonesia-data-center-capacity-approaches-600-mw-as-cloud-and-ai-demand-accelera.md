body_en: |
  Indonesia's data center industry is on track to approach 600 megawatts (MW) of total installed capacity by the end of 2026, as demand from cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and broad digital transformation continues to drive expansion across the country.

  The Indonesian Data Center Provider Association, known by its Indonesian acronym IDPRO, reported that national data center capacity stood at approximately 520 MW as of June 2026. IDPRO members represent more than 80 percent of total national data center capacity, making the figure a reliable proxy for the country's overall installed base.

  ## What the capacity figures mean

  A megawatt of data center capacity refers to the amount of electrical power a facility can continuously deliver to IT equipment. At 520 MW, Indonesia's data center sector is already substantial by regional standards; the forecast rise to near 600 MW by December 2026 would represent an increase of roughly 15 percent within a single year.

  ## Drivers of expansion

  IDPRO attributed the capacity growth to three converging forces: accelerating adoption of cloud computing services, the emergence of artificial intelligence workloads that require dense computing infrastructure, and ongoing digital transformation initiatives across multiple sectors of the Indonesian economy.

  ## Industry context

  IDPRO described the current period as a continuing expansion phase for the domestic data center industry, suggesting the growth is not a one-time surge but part of a sustained trajectory.

  # 印尼資料中心裝機容量逼近600 MW，雲端與AI需求持續加速

  在雲端運算、人工智慧及全面數位轉型浪潮持續推進下，印尼資料中心產業正全速擴張，總裝機容量預計於2026年底前逼近600百萬瓦（MW）。

  印尼資料中心業者協會（Indonesian Data Center Provider Association，印尼文縮寫為IDPRO）公布資料顯示，截至2026年6月，該國資料中心總容量約為520 MW。由於IDPRO會員企業的裝機容量已涵蓋全國逾八成，此一數字可視為反映全國整體裝機規模的可靠指標。

  ## 容量數字的意義

  資料中心容量以百萬瓦計，代表機房能夠持續供應給IT設備的電力規模。520 MW的規模，已讓印尼在東南亞地區名列前茅；若如預測般，於2026年12月前進一步接近600 MW，則單年成長幅度將達約15%，擴張速度相當顯著。

  ## 推動擴張的三大動力

  IDPRO指出，此波容量成長主要由三股力量交匯推動：雲端運算服務的加速普及、對高密度運算基礎設施需求殷切的人工智慧工作負載崛起，以及印尼各產業持續推進的數位轉型計畫。

  ## 產業現況

  IDPRO表示，印尼國內資料中心產業目前正處於持續擴張階段，顯示這波成長並非短暫的一次性爆發，而是一條延續性的長期成長軌跡。
