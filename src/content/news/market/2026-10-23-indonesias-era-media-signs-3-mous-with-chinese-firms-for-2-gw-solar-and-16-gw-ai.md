body_en: |
  PT Era Media Sejahtera Tbk. (stock ticker: DOOH), an Indonesian publicly listed company, signed three memoranda of understanding (MoUs) with Chinese firms in Wuhan on October 19, 2026, to develop up to 2 gigawatts (GW) of solar generation capacity and a 1.6 GW artificial intelligence (AI) data center campus in West Java, Indonesia.

  The three counterparties are Jiangxi Jiusheng International Electric Power Engineering Co., Ltd., China Construction Third Engineering Bureau Group Co., Ltd. (CSCEC Third Bureau), and GOME FinTech Asset Management Holdings Limited. Each agreement covers a distinct layer of the planned development: energy supply, physical construction, and early capacity leasing.

  ## The Three Agreements

  Under the first MoU, Jiangxi Jiusheng will supply equipment, materials, and grid-interconnection facilities for a solar photovoltaic (PV) project DOOH is developing in Indonesia. The project is planned to reach a total capacity of 2,000 megawatts (MW), or 2 GW, built in phases. The first phase is set at 300 MWac (megawatts alternating current), equivalent to approximately 390 MWp (megawatts peak, a standard measure of a solar panel's rated output under ideal conditions). Jiangxi Jiusheng CEO Li Guofan said the firm is committed to providing reliable equipment and interconnection facilities to enable the solar plant to serve as a clean energy source for DOOH's data center, starting from the initial 300 MWac phase through to the full 2 GW build-out.

  The second MoU, with CSCEC Third Bureau — a subsidiary of one of China's largest state-owned construction groups — covers construction of the AI data center campus in West Java. The campus is planned at up to 1.6 GW of IT load capacity, developed in stages, with the first phase targeting 50 MW of IT capacity. The scope includes design coordination, construction management, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) work. MEP systems are the power distribution, cooling, and building services infrastructure that keep data center servers running. CSCEC Third Bureau General Manager Liu Peng cited the firm's international construction experience as the basis for its readiness to support the project.

  The third MoU, with GOME FinTech Asset Management Holdings Limited, establishes the framework for leasing 50 MW of IT capacity from DOOH's data center. That capacity is planned to be handed over in stages through 2027, aligned with the first construction phase. Wei Lai, who holds the authorized representative role at GOME FinTech and also serves as Chief Strategy Officer of Tong Tong AI Social Group Limited, described the lease as an initial step to support the group's AI business expansion in the region.

  ## West Java Site and the Integrated Ecosystem Logic

  DOOH said it selected West Java for the data center campus because of its proximity to Jakarta, which the company described as the national center of demand for digital services, as well as the availability of fiber optic networks and electricity infrastructure in the area.

  DOOH's authorized representative Ma Lei framed the three agreements as an attempt to link the three inputs that AI infrastructure requires — energy, facilities, and users — within a single ecosystem. "AI requires three things simultaneously: energy, facilities, and users. Through these three MoUs, the company is beginning to assemble all three within one ecosystem," Ma Lei said in an official statement on October 22, 2026.

  The solar project is designed specifically to supply power to the data center, with DOOH intending to integrate renewable energy supply with its data center capacity offering.

  ## Conditions Precedent and Next Steps

  DOOH disclosed that the three MoUs are preliminary agreements and do not yet contain full implementation terms. Investment values, execution schedules, phased capacity milestones, precise site locations, lease durations, and other contractual terms are to be defined in binding definitive agreements.

  The company further stated that converting the MoUs into definitive agreements is contingent on obtaining approval from PT PLN (Persero), Indonesia's state-owned electricity utility, which controls grid access and power purchase arrangements in the country.

  ## Significance

  The MoUs signal DOOH's entry into two capital-intensive sectors — utility-scale renewable energy and large-footprint AI data center infrastructure — that are attracting substantial investment in Southeast Asia. At 1.6 GW, the planned campus would rank among the largest AI data center developments announced in the region. However, the agreements remain non-binding at this stage, and the project's progression depends on regulatory approvals, particularly from PLN, as well as the negotiation of definitive contracts covering financing and construction terms.

  The first phase of the data center, at 50 MW of IT capacity, is planned for delivery in stages through 2027, with GOME FinTech named as the initial anchor tenant for that phase.
body_zh: |
  PT Era Media Sejahtera Tbk.（股票代碼：DOOH）是印尼一家公開上市公司，於2026年10月19日在武漢與三家中資企業簽署三份合作備忘錄（MoU），計畫在印尼西爪哇省開發最高達2吉瓦（GW）的太陽能發電裝置容量，以及一座1.6吉瓦的人工智慧（AI）資料中心園區。

  三方簽約對象分別為：江西九盛國際電力工程有限公司、中國建築第三工程局集團有限公司（中建三局），以及國美金融科技資產管理控股有限公司。每份備忘錄各自對應計畫開發項目的不同層面：能源供應、實體建設，以及早期容量租賃。

  ## 三份備忘錄內容

  第一份備忘錄的簽約方為江西九盛，內容涵蓋為DOOH在印尼開發的太陽光電計畫提供設備、材料及併網設施。該計畫規劃總裝置容量為2,000百萬瓦（MW），即2吉瓦，採分階段建設，首期容量定為300 MWac（交流百萬瓦），約相當於390 MWp（峰值百萬瓦，係太陽能板在理想條件下額定輸出的業界標準計量單位）。江西九盛執行長李國凡表示，該公司承諾提供可靠的設備與併網設施，使太陽能電廠得以成為DOOH資料中心的清潔能源來源，從首期300 MWac階段一路推進至完整的2吉瓦規模。

  第二份備忘錄由中建三局簽署——中建三局為中國最大國有建設集團之一的旗下子公司——內容涵蓋西爪哇AI資料中心園區的施工工程。該園區規劃IT負載容量最高達1.6吉瓦，採分階段推進，首期目標為50百萬瓦的IT容量。合作範圍包括設計協調、施工管理，以及機械、電氣及管線（MEP）工程。MEP系統係指維持資料中心伺服器正常運作所需的配電、冷卻及樓宇設備基礎設施。中建三局總經理劉鵬表示，該公司豐富的國際建設經驗，是其具備支援本計畫能力的重要依據。

  第三份備忘錄簽約方為國美金融科技資產管理控股有限公司，確立向DOOH資料中心租賃50百萬瓦IT容量的框架安排。相關容量計畫於2027年前分批交付，與首期建設進度相互配合。國美金融科技授權代表韋來同時身兼同同AI社交集團有限公司首席策略長，他表示此次租賃是支持集團在東南亞地區拓展AI業務的初步行動。

  ## 西爪哇選址邏輯與整合生態系布局

  DOOH表示，選擇西爪哇作為資料中心園區址地，原因在於該省鄰近雅加達——DOOH將其定位為全國數位服務需求的核心區域——加上當地光纖網路及電力基礎設施的可及性亦是重要考量。

  DOOH授權代表馬磊將三份備忘錄的簽署定性為一項嘗試，旨在將AI基礎設施所需的三大要素——能源、設施與用戶——整合於同一生態系之內。馬磊於2026年10月22日的官方聲明中指出：「AI同時需要三件事：能源、設施與用戶。透過這三份備忘錄，公司正開始在同一生態系內將三者齊聚。」

  太陽光電計畫的設計目標，即為資料中心專屬供電，DOOH計畫將再生能源供應與資料中心容量服務整合為一體。

  ## 先決條件與後續步驟

  DOOH揭露，三份備忘錄均屬初步協議，尚未載明完整的執行條款。投資金額、執行時間表、分階段容量里程碑、場址精確位置、租賃期限及其他合約條款，將於後續具有法律約束力的正式協議中加以確定。

  公司進一步指出，備忘錄轉化為正式協議的先決條件，是必須取得印尼國營電力公司PT PLN（Persero）的核准。PT PLN掌控全國電網接取及電力購售安排，其核准是推進本計畫的關鍵監管要件。

  ## 意義

  此次備忘錄的簽署，標誌著DOOH正式進軍兩個資本密集型領域——公用規模再生能源，以及大規模AI資料中心基礎設施。目前，這兩個領域均在東南亞吸引大量投資。以1.6吉瓦的規劃容量而言，若該園區能夠落成，將躋身東南亞地區已公告的最大規模AI資料中心開發案之列。惟目前各份備忘錄仍屬非約束性文件，項目能否如期推進，有賴監管機構核准（尤以PT PLN為關鍵），以及涵蓋融資與建設條款之正式合約的談判成果。

  資料中心首期50百萬瓦IT容量，計畫於2027年前分批交付，國美金融科技已獲指定為該階段的初始主力租戶。
