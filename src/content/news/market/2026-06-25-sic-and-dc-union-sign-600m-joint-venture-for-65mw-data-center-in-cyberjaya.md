body_en: |
  Selangor Industrial Corporation (SIC) and DC Union have signed a joint venture agreement to develop a 65-megawatt (MW) data center in Cyberjaya, Malaysia, at a project value of RM 2.5 billion (approximately US$602.5 million), the companies announced.

  The facility will be built and operated through SIC Quantum Data Centre, a new joint venture company formed by the two parties. Vinodh Mariappa has been appointed Managing Director of the entity. Both SIC and DC Union will contribute assets and expertise to the venture, the companies said.

  SIC is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Selangor State Development Corporation (PKNS), a state-linked development body in Selangor, Malaysia. The project is the first of its kind within the PKNS Group portfolio, according to SIC Group Chief Executive Officer Haji Saharom Mohni.

  "Through this partnership, SIC acts as a strategic partner supporting the state's digital infrastructure development, while DC Union's expertise and experience will help ensure the project is executed according to international industry standards," Mohni said in a press release.

  ## Planned facilities

  In addition to the 65MW data center, SIC Quantum Data Centre will establish a Sovereign AI Command Centre and a Centre of Excellence focused on artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities, data security, and digital innovation in Cyberjaya, the companies said.

  Mohni said the data center will serve as a supporting platform for the Selangor Smart State agenda, a state government initiative targeting digital infrastructure development, and will be accessible to government agencies, local authorities, and private-sector organizations for data storage and processing.

  ## Cyberjaya's position as a regional data center hub

  Cyberjaya, a purpose-built technology district in Selangor, hosted more than 20 data centers by January 2026, according to data from market research firm Mordor Intelligence cited in the announcement. Hyperscalers, large-scale cloud computing providers, including Amazon Web Services (AWS), Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud have established operations in the area.

  Mordor Intelligence data cited by the companies attributed Cyberjaya's expansion in part to its role as a viable alternative to land-constrained Singapore, with Malaysia benefiting from a broader regional increase in data center investment.

  "Malaysia has an immense opportunity to emerge as a leading AI and digital infrastructure hub in Southeast Asia. This strategic collaboration is an important step in developing sovereign digital capabilities capable of supporting innovation, attracting strategic investments, and helping organizations fully leverage the potential of AI technology," Tecsun Yeep, Chief Executive Officer of DC Union, said in the press release.

  ## PKNS Group's first data center project

  The SIC-DC Union deal marks the first data center project within the PKNS Group ecosystem, according to SIC. Mohni described it as a strategic step toward strengthening the group's participation in the digital industry and high-impact technology.

  Yeep said DC Union's plans extend beyond the physical facility. "Our vision extends beyond mere infrastructure development, focusing instead on building a digital ecosystem that supports talent development, technological innovation, and strategic collaboration between the public and private sectors," he said.

  The Sovereign AI Command Centre and Centre of Excellence are planned as components of the initial development scope alongside the 65MW data center.

  ## SIC與DC Union簽署6億美元合資協議　在網絡城打造65MW資料中心

  雪蘭莪工業機構（Selangor Industrial Corporation，SIC）與DC Union宣布，雙方已簽署合資協議，將在馬來西亞網絡城（Cyberjaya）共同開發一座65百萬瓦（MW）資料中心，總投資額達25億令吉（約6億250萬美元）。

  這座設施將透過兩家公司共同成立的新合資公司「SIC Quantum Data Centre」負責興建與營運。Vinodh Mariappa獲任命為該公司董事總經理。雙方表示，SIC與DC Union均將為此合資事業挹注資產與專業技術。

  SIC是雪蘭莪州發展機構（PKNS）的全資子公司，PKNS為馬來西亞雪蘭莪州的州屬開發機構。SIC集團執行長哈吉·薩哈羅姆·莫尼（Haji Saharom Mohni）表示，這是PKNS集團旗下同類型專案的首例。

  莫尼在新聞稿中指出：「透過此次合作，SIC作為策略合作夥伴，支援雪州的數位基礎設施發展；而DC Union的專業知識與豐富經驗，將確保本專案按照國際產業標準執行。」

  ## 規劃設施

  除65MW資料中心外，SIC Quantum Data Centre還將在網絡城設立一座「主權AI指揮中心」（Sovereign AI Command Centre），以及一座專注於人工智慧（AI）能力、資料安全與數位創新的「卓越中心」（Centre of Excellence）。

  莫尼表示，這座資料中心將作為雪蘭莪智慧州（Selangor Smart State）議程的支援平台，而該議程是雪州政府以數位基礎設施發展為目標的施政計畫，資料中心將供政府機構、地方當局及私部門組織用於資料儲存與處理。

  ## 網絡城的區域資料中心樞紐地位

  網絡城是雪蘭莪州專門規劃的科技園區。根據公告引用市場研究機構Mordor Intelligence的數據，截至2026年1月，網絡城已進駐逾20座資料中心。Amazon Web Services（AWS）、Microsoft Azure及Google Cloud等超大規模雲端運算業者均已在當地設立據點。

  公告引用Mordor Intelligence的數據指出，網絡城的擴張，部分原因在於其作為土地資源受限的新加坡之可行替代選址，馬來西亞也因此受惠於整個東南亞地區資料中心投資規模的整體增長。

  DC Union執行長葉德遜（Tecsun Yeep）在新聞稿中表示：「馬來西亞擁有絕佳機遇，有望躋身東南亞領先的AI與數位基礎設施樞紐。這次策略合作是發展主權數位能力的重要一步，有助於支持創新、吸引策略性投資，並協助各組織充分發揮AI技術的潛力。」

  ## PKNS集團首個資料中心專案

  SIC與DC Union的合作案，是PKNS集團生態系中的首個資料中心專案。莫尼將此形容為強化集團參與數位產業與高影響力科技領域的策略性步伐。

  葉德遜表示，DC Union的計畫不僅止於實體設施建設。「我們的願景超越單純的基礎設施開發，而是著眼於構建一個數位生態系，支持人才培育、技術創新，以及公私部門之間的策略合作。」

  主權AI指揮中心與卓越中心，均列入與65MW資料中心並行推進的初期開發範疇。
