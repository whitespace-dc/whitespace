body_en: |
  Microsoft has launched a new $2.5 billion business unit called Microsoft Frontier Company, dedicated to helping corporate clients implement the company's artificial intelligence services, the company announced Thursday, July 2, 2026.

  The unit will be staffed by 6,000 engineers and industry experts, including technical consultants, forward deployed engineers — specialists placed directly at customer sites to tailor AI to specific business needs — experienced sales staff, and support personnel. Rodrigo Kede Lima, who previously led Microsoft's business operations in Asia, will head the new organization.

  ## Scale and positioning

  At $2.5 billion, the investment is more than double the $1 billion that Amazon Web Services announced two days earlier for a comparable internal AI organization. AWS announced its initiative using what the industry calls the forward deployed engineer, or FDE, model — the same approach Microsoft Frontier Company will broadly employ, though Microsoft framed its unit in different terms.

  Judson Althoff, Microsoft's chief executive of commercial business, said the organization goes beyond the standard FDE model. "Frontier Company will be the largest and most capable outcome-driven engineering organization in the industry," Althoff said, according to Kompas.com. Despite that framing, some observers quoted by the publication noted the Frontier Company concept retains substantial similarities to the FDE approach.

  AI companies including Anthropic and OpenAI have also established joint ventures using FDE-style models, though those efforts have been backed by private investment funding rather than direct corporate capital, according to the source report.

  ## Early partners and competitive position

  Microsoft named several launch partners for the new unit: London Stock Exchange Group, consumer goods company Unilever, agricultural cooperative Land O'Lakes, and consulting firm Accenture.

  The company argued it holds a structural advantage over rivals because it already has engineers embedded at many Fortune 500 companies, which the announcement suggested would accelerate AI deployment timelines for enterprise clients.

  ## Industry significance

  The announcements from Microsoft and AWS within 48 hours of each other signal that large technology companies are moving to institutionalize AI implementation as a distinct, heavily capitalized business function, competing directly with consultancies and AI-native firms that have built practices around deploying large language models for enterprise customers. Microsoft's Frontier Company represents the larger bet, combining a dedicated organizational structure with a pre-existing enterprise customer base that the company said positions it to move faster than newer entrants.
body_zh: |
  微軟於2026年7月2日（週四）正式宣布，成立一個名為「Microsoft Frontier Company」的新事業部，投資規模達25億美元，專門協助企業客戶導入該公司的人工智慧服務。

  該事業部將配備6,000名工程師與產業專家，涵蓋技術顧問、「前線部署工程師」（forward deployed engineer，FDE）——即直接派駐客戶現場、依照其特定業務需求量身調整AI系統的專業人員——以及資深業務人員與支援人力。曾主掌微軟亞洲業務營運的Rodrigo Kede Lima，將出任新組織負責人。

  ## 規模與市場定位

  以25億美元計，此次投資規模超過亞馬遜雲端服務（AWS）兩天前同類計畫的兩倍——AWS當時宣布的金額為10億美元。AWS的計畫採用業界所稱的「前線部署工程師」（FDE）模式；Microsoft Frontier Company亦將廣泛採行此一模式，不過微軟在對外說明時以不同框架加以詮釋。

  微軟商業事業執行長Judson Althoff表示，這個組織已超越標準FDE模式的範疇。「Frontier Company將成為業界規模最大、能力最強、以成果為導向的工程組織，」Althoff如此表示（引自Kompas.com的報導）。儘管如此，該報導亦引述部分觀察人士的看法，指出Frontier Company的概念與FDE模式仍保有相當程度的相似性。

  此外，根據原始報導，Anthropic、OpenAI等AI公司亦已採用FDE式模式設立合資企業，但這些計畫係以私人投資資金挹注，而非直接動用企業自有資本。

  ## 首批合作夥伴與競爭優勢

  微軟公布了新事業部的幾個創始合作夥伴：倫敦證券交易所集團（London Stock Exchange Group）、消費品大廠聯合利華（Unilever）、農業合作社Land O'Lakes，以及顧問公司埃森哲（Accenture）。

  微軟主張，相較於競爭對手，公司在結構上具備先天優勢——其工程師早已深入許多《財星》500大企業（Fortune 500）的內部，而此次公告亦暗示，這將有效加快企業客戶的AI部署時程。

  ## 產業意義

  微軟與AWS在48小時內相繼宣布上述計畫，顯示大型科技公司正積極將AI導入工作體制化，視之為一項獨立且資本密集的商業功能，直接與長期以部署大型語言模型（LLM）服務企業客戶為核心業務的顧問業者及AI原生公司正面競爭。微軟的Frontier Company代表著更大規模的押注——它結合了專屬的組織架構，以及公司聲稱足以令其比新進業者行動更快的既有企業客戶基礎。
