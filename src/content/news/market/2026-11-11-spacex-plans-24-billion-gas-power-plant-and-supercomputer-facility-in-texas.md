body_en: |
  SpaceX is planning a natural gas power plant and supercomputer facility in Starbase, Texas, with an estimated initial investment of approximately $24 billion, according to a public hearing notice first reported by Bloomberg News.

  The company, which earlier this year acquired xAI — the artificial intelligence laboratory founded by Elon Musk — has applied for a property tax abatement for the project. The Starbase City Commission is scheduled to consider the abatement and a related economic development agreement at a public meeting on December 9.

  ## Project scope

  The $24 billion figure represents estimated capital investment for the project's initial phases only, according to the notice. The work may proceed in multiple stages and could include future expansions. SpaceX would construct, own, and operate both facilities.

  The public hearing notice provided no details on the power plant's generating capacity or the scale of the planned computing operation, nor did it specify the size or duration of the requested abatement.

  ## Grid bypass and pipeline infrastructure

  Building its own generation capacity would allow SpaceX to skip a years-long wait to connect a large industrial load to Texas' main power grid, according to Reuters. SpaceX affiliate Lone Star Mineral Development has separately filed plans for an 8-mile natural gas pipeline to Starbase — named Starpipe — scheduled for service by January 2027. According to filings Reuters reviewed in June, that pipeline will feed a planned plant to convert natural gas into liquid methane for Starship rockets.

  ## Opposition and political backdrop

  The project moves forward as public resistance to new data centers grows. Voters rejected data center proposals in 40 of 41 local ballot measures in the midterm elections, according to Reuters. Democratic candidates who had campaigned on pausing new data center construction won control of the U.S. Congress and the Texas governor's office.

  ## AI revenue and compute clients

  SpaceX reported a 161 percent jump in total revenue and a ninefold increase in AI revenue in its third quarter. The company has been renting computing capacity to Google, Anthropic, and others.

  If built at the stated scale, the Starbase investment would rank among the largest private investments in the Rio Grande Valley, according to the filing. The December 9 Starbase City Commission meeting will be the first public forum at which the tax abatement and economic development agreement face formal review.

  根據彭博新聞率先披露的公聽會公告，SpaceX計畫在德州星際基地（Starbase）興建天然氣發電廠及超級電腦設施，初期資本投資估計約240億美元。

  該公司今年稍早收購了由伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）創辦的人工智慧實驗室xAI，目前已就此計畫申請財產稅減免。星際基地市委員會預定於12月9日召開公開會議，審議減稅申請及相關經濟發展協議。

  ## 計畫規模

  據上述公告，240億美元僅為計畫初期階段的預估資本投資，工程可能分多期推進，並可能包含未來擴建項目。兩座設施將由SpaceX自行興建、持有並營運。

  公聽會公告並未說明發電廠的裝置容量或超級電腦設施的規模，亦未載明申請減免的金額或期限。

  ## 自建電網與管線基礎設施

  根據路透社報導，自行興建發電設施可讓SpaceX跳過大型工業用電接入德州主要電網往往需要等待數年的漫長程序。SpaceX旗下關聯企業孤星礦產開發公司（Lone Star Mineral Development）另行申報了一條長約8英里、通往星際基地的天然氣管線計畫，命名為「Starpipe」（星管），預計於2027年1月投入使用。路透社今年6月審閱的相關申報文件顯示，該管線將供應一座計畫中的轉化工廠，用以將天然氣轉化為液態甲烷，作為星艦（Starship）火箭的燃料。

  ## 民間反彈與政治背景

  此計畫推進之際，社會各界對新建資料中心的抗拒情緒日益高漲。據路透社報導，在期中選舉中，選民在41項地方資料中心相關公投案中否決了其中40項。主張暫緩新建資料中心的民主黨候選人，則贏得了美國國會及德州州長辦公室的控制權。

  ## 人工智慧營收與運算業務客戶

  SpaceX公布第三季財報顯示，總營收年增161%，人工智慧相關營收更成長九倍。該公司目前已向Google、Anthropic等業者出租運算資源。

  若依公告所述規模付諸實現，星際基地此投資案將躋身里奧格蘭德河谷（Rio Grande Valley）史上規模最大的私人投資之列。12月9日的星際基地市委員會會議，將是財產稅減免案與經濟發展協議首度接受正式公開審查的論壇。
