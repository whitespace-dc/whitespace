  ## US Political Shift Rattles Global Tech Sentiment

  Concerns about a slowdown in artificial intelligence (AI) spending and tighter US regulation of data center construction are pressuring shares of Indonesia-listed data center companies, following a Democratic Party majority victory in last week's United States midterm elections, according to Kontan.

  The midterm result, reported on November 12, 2026, prompted investors to reassess the pace of AI-driven capital expenditure and the regulatory environment for data center development in the United States. That reassessment produced simultaneous sell-offs across technology and semiconductor stocks on global exchanges, Kontan reported.

  The negative sentiment transmitted directly to the Indonesia Stock Exchange (Bursa Efek Indonesia, or BEI), where shares of domestic data center issuers (publicly listed operators) moved lower in line with the global correction.

  ## AI Sentiment as a Growth Driver Now Under Pressure

  The Kontan report described AI sentiment as having been the primary engine of growth for the global technology industry before the current period of uncertainty. The post-election dynamics represent a shift in that backdrop, with the market now pricing in the possibility that AI investment could decelerate and that US data center construction could face more restrictive oversight.

  ## Implications for Indonesia's 2027 Outlook

  The article frames the global correction as the defining near-term risk for Indonesia's domestic data center sector heading into 2027. Because Indonesian data center stocks have tracked global technology sentiment closely, any sustained softening in AI-related capital flows or regulatory tightening in the United States carries potential spillover consequences for local operators and investors monitoring the sector's growth trajectory.

  ## 美國政治格局轉變衝擊全球科技市場情緒

  根據《Kontan》報導，美國期中選舉民主黨贏得國會多數席次，外界對人工智慧（AI）支出可能趨緩、以及美國資料中心建設將面臨更嚴格管制的疑慮持續升溫，使在印尼股市掛牌的本土資料中心業者股價承壓。

  此次期中選舉結果於2026年11月12日見報，促使投資人重新評估AI帶動的資本支出步調，以及美國資料中心開發所處的監管環境。《Kontan》指出，這波重新評估引發全球各大交易所科技股與半導體股同步下挫。

  上述負面市場情緒直接傳導至印尼證券交易所（Bursa Efek Indonesia，BEI），國內資料中心掛牌業者的股價隨全球修正走勢同步走低。

  ## AI情緒作為成長動力如今面臨壓力

  《Kontan》報導指出，在當前這波不確定性浮現之前，AI市場情緒一直是推動全球科技產業成長的主要引擎。選後情勢的演變代表此一背景正在出現轉折，市場目前已開始將AI投資可能減速、以及美國資料中心建設可能受到更嚴格監管的風險納入股價定價之中。

  ## 對印尼2027年展望的影響

  該報導將此次全球修正定性為印尼本土資料中心產業邁向2027年最主要的近期風險。由於印尼資料中心類股長期與全球科技市場情緒高度連動，一旦AI相關資本流動持續疲軟，或美國監管環境進一步收緊，均可能對當地業者，以及密切追蹤該產業成長軌跡的投資人，帶來不容忽視的外溢效應。
