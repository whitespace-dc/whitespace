body_en: |
  Property investment in Indonesia is shifting beyond Jakarta, with real estate advisory firm JLL identifying data centers, industrial land, and aging office conversions as the three sectors with the strongest near-term prospects, the firm said on Thursday.

  JLL Indonesia Country Head Farazia Basarah said Jakarta remains Indonesia's primary economic center but argued that medium-term opportunities increasingly lie elsewhere. "In the medium term, the increasingly attractive opportunities lie in how government regulation, infrastructure development and investment help build new economic ecosystems across various other regions of the country," Basarah said in a press statement dated October 22.

  ## Central Java flagged as emerging industrial hub

  JLL singled out Batam, Kendal, and Semarang in Central Java as areas with potential to become new industrial growth centers. The firm cited manufacturing expansion and infrastructure support as the drivers opening property opportunities in those corridors.

  Industrial and logistics property ranks first among JLL's three identified opportunities, propelled by supply chain diversification and corporate relocation and expansion activity.

  ## Data centers rise on AI and cloud demand

  Data centers form the second opportunity. JLL said demand is rising on the back of artificial intelligence (AI) adoption, cloud computing investment, and data localization requirements — rules that compel companies to store certain data within Indonesia's borders rather than on overseas servers.

  ## Office and retail conversions round out the three sectors

  The third opportunity is the conversion of older office and retail buildings to uses that better match current market demand. JLL did not specify what those alternative uses are, but the identification of this category reflects broader trends in underutilized commercial stock.

  ## Selective placement now matters as much as market exposure

  Despite broadening opportunity, JLL cautioned that the market has become more selective. Dr. Yang Liang Chua, JLL's Head of Research and Advisory for Southeast Asia, said success will depend less on broad geographic exposure and more on precise capital deployment. "Going forward, success will not only be determined by exposure to a region broadly, but also by the ability of investors to place capital precisely in the corridors, sectors, and asset strategies that have the best prospects," Chua said.

  The firm's assessment positions corridor-level and sector-level selectivity — rather than country-level allocation — as the defining factor for property investment outcomes in Indonesia in the period ahead.
body_zh: |
  印尼不動產投資機會正逐步向雅加達以外擴展，房地產顧問機構仲量聯行（JLL）指出，資料中心、工業用地，以及老舊辦公室轉型是近期前景最看好的三大板塊，其中尤以中爪哇省巴鄧（Batang）、肯達爾（Kendal）及三寶瓏（Semarang）廊道最受矚目，仲量聯行於週四發布上述看法。

  仲量聯行印尼總裁法拉齊亞·巴薩拉（Farazia Basarah）表示，雅加達仍是印尼的主要經濟重鎮，但她認為中期機遇正愈來愈多地出現在其他地方。「從中期來看，愈來愈具吸引力的機會在於：政府法規、基礎設施建設與投資如何協力在全國各地打造全新的經濟生態系。」巴薩拉在10月22日發布的新聞稿中如此表示。

  ## 中爪哇省崛起為新興工業重鎮

  仲量聯行特別點名中爪哇省的巴淡（Batam）、肯達爾（Kendal）及三寶瓏（Semarang），認為這些地區具備潛力，可發展為新興工業成長中心。該機構指出，製造業擴張與基礎設施支持，是帶動上述廊道不動產機遇的主要驅動力。

  在仲量聯行列出的三大機遇中，工業暨物流不動產排名第一，背後動能來自供應鏈多元化，以及企業遷址與擴張的活動。

  ## 資料中心乘AI與雲端需求而起

  資料中心是第二大機遇。仲量聯行指出，相關需求持續攀升，背後推手包括人工智慧（AI）的廣泛應用、雲端運算投資，以及資料在地化法規——此類規定要求企業將特定資料儲存於印尼境內，而非境外伺服器。

  ## 辦公與零售空間轉型列居第三

  第三大機遇是將老舊辦公及零售建築改造為更符合現有市場需求的用途。仲量聯行並未具體說明這些替代用途為何，但將此類別納入清單，反映出商業空間閒置利用不足的廣泛趨勢。

  ## 精準部署資本，重要性不亞於市場布局

  儘管機遇面向持續擴大，仲量聯行也警示市場已趨於選擇性。仲量聯行東南亞研究暨諮詢業務主管蔡揚亮博士（Dr. Yang Liang Chua）表示，未來的成功不再只取決於廣泛的地理布局，而更在於能否精準部署資本。「往後，成功與否不僅取決於對某一地區的整體曝險，更取決於投資者能否將資本精準配置於最具前景的廊道、板塊與資產策略。」蔡揚亮說。

  仲量聯行的整體評估指出，在印尼不動產投資的未來格局中，決定成敗的關鍵將從國家層級的資產配置，轉移至廊道層級與板塊層級的精準選擇，而非單純仰賴對印尼整體市場的廣泛布局。
