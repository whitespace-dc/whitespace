body_en: |
  Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) and the New Energy and Industrial Technology Development Organization (NEDO) on June 4, 2026 announced 16 project selections for the fourth round of GENIAC, the government's AI development support program, with a stated strategic orientation toward integrating data from Japan's industrial worksites rather than scaling general-purpose AI models.

  The 16 selected organizations span a broad range of sectors: behavior recognition AI developer Agira; AI developer ABEJA; drug discovery and biotech firm Epigenetic AI; financial AI developer Elith; telecommunications carrier KDDI; AI research firm Sakana AI; business contact management company Sansan; sustainability management support firm Sherpa & Company; disaster information analysis company Spectee; voice AI developer DubGuild; medical AI firm Direava; autonomous driving technology company newmo; bio-AI developer biomy; deep learning research firm Preferred Networks; e-commerce platform Mercari; and construction technology firm ONESTRUCTION.

  ## Strategy: on-site data over model size

  Speaking at a kickoff event held the same day, Takuya Watanabe, Director of the AI Industry Strategy Office within METI's Commerce and Information Policy Bureau, articulated the rationale for the round's direction. "It is difficult for Japan to stand out with general-purpose AI models," Watanabe said. "Incorporating on-site data from fields where Japan is strong, rather than data from the internet, is very important."

  Watanabe described two complementary strategic priorities alongside that data focus. The first is research and development in physical AI and new learning methods. The second is what he termed "AI-Ready" organizations, a state in which all companies restructure their business processes so that AI can utilize even tacit knowledge — the kind of institutional know-how that is rarely written down. Both elements, he said, are necessary preconditions for raising Japanese economic productivity and strengthening the domestic AI industry.

  According to Nikkei xTECH, the fourth round's selection criteria reflect this orientation: the round favors projects centered on "which industry's on-site knowledge and data to incorporate into AI" over projects aimed at "how large a model to build."

  ## Sectoral spread and implications

  The selection list covers healthcare, finance, construction, disaster response, voice and audio, biotechnology, autonomous mobility, sustainability, and general e-commerce, indicating METI is pursuing broad sectoral coverage rather than concentrating support in a single vertical. The explicit pivot away from general-purpose model development and toward domain-specific, worksite-grounded AI signals a deliberate differentiation strategy within Japan's government-backed AI effort, one premised on the country's comparative strength in industrial and operational data rather than in internet-scale training corpora.

  The June 4 kickoff event marked the formal start of the fourth-round projects.
body_zh: |
  日本經濟產業省（METI）與新能源暨產業技術綜合開發機構（NEDO）於2026年6月4日，公布GENIAC政府AI開發支援計畫第四輪的16個入選案。此輪計畫明確揭示戰略方向：重點已從擴大通用AI模型規模，轉向整合日本產業現場資料、開發領域專用AI。

  16個入選機構橫跨多元領域：行為辨識AI開發商Agira、AI開發商ABEJA、藥物探索暨生技公司Epigenetic AI、金融AI開發商Elith、電信業者KDDI、AI研究機構Sakana AI、企業名片管理公司Sansan、永續管理支援公司Sherpa & Company、災害資訊分析公司Spectee、語音AI開發商DubGuild、醫療AI公司Direava、自動駕駛技術公司newmo、生醫AI開發商biomy、深度學習研究機構Preferred Networks、電商平台Mercari，以及建設技術公司ONESTRUCTION。

  ## 戰略轉向：現場資料優先於模型規模

  在同日舉辦的啟動活動上，經產省商務資訊政策局AI產業策略室室長渡邊拓也（Takuya Watanabe）闡述了此輪計畫方向的邏輯依據。他表示：「日本要在通用AI模型上出奇制勝，並不容易。比起來自網路的資料，將日本具有優勢之領域的現場資料融入AI，才是至關重要的做法。」

  渡邊拓也在資料重心之外，進一步提出兩項互補的戰略重點。第一項是物理AI與新學習方法的研發；第二項是他所稱的「AI就緒」（AI-Ready）組織——亦即企業全面重整業務流程，使AI得以運用包括隱性知識在內的一切機構積累——那些鮮少見諸文字的組織know-how。他指出，兩者都是提升日本經濟生產力、強化國內AI產業的必要前提。

  據《日經xTECH》報導，第四輪的評選標準充分反映了這一取向：相較於「要打造多大規模的模型」，此輪更偏好以「將哪個產業的現場知識與資料融入AI」為核心的計畫。

  ## 跨領域佈局與意涵

  入選名單涵蓋醫療、金融、建設、災害應變、語音與音訊、生物技術、自動駕駛、永續經營及一般電商等領域，顯示METI致力於廣泛的跨產業佈局，而非將支援集中於單一垂直市場。從通用模型開發明確轉向領域專用、現場資料驅動AI，透露出日本政府AI推動策略蓄意走出差異化路線——其立論前提，在於日本在產業與營運資料上的比較優勢，而非網路規模訓練語料庫的競爭力。

  6月4日的啟動活動，正式宣告第四輪計畫的開展。
