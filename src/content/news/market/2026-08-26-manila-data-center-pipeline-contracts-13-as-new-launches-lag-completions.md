body_en: |
  Manila's data center development pipeline contracted 13% in the first half of 2026, falling to 97 megawatts (MW) as recently completed facilities entered service faster than new project announcements replenished the queue, according to real estate consultancy Cushman & Wakefield's Asia Pacific Data Centre H1 2026 Update.

  The total pipeline — which includes capacity both under construction and in the planning stage — stood at 97 MW at the end of June, down from the prior period. Capacity actively under construction fell sharply, from 22 MW to 5 MW, as projects reached delivery. Planned but not-yet-started capacity edged up to 92 MW.

  Cushman & Wakefield said the decline indicated that new project announcements had yet to fully replace recently delivered supply.

  ## Vacancy and operational capacity

  Operational information technology (IT) capacity — the installed power available for customer use — grew 22% to 90 MW as newly completed facilities came online, bringing Manila's network to 28 data centers operated by 16 companies.

  Despite the additional supply, Manila's colocation vacancy rate — the share of available capacity without a tenant — improved to 43.6% from 48% in the previous six-month period, suggesting that demand absorbed part of the new capacity, according to the report. Colocation is a model in which operators rent out space, power, and cooling to multiple customers rather than using facilities for a single tenant.

  Even with that improvement, Manila posted the highest vacancy rate among the 15 primary and secondary Asia-Pacific markets individually profiled in the report. Among comparable secondary markets, Ho Chi Minh City recorded a vacancy rate of 34.8%, Perth 34.3%, Delhi National Capital Region 10.9%, Auckland 6.4%, and Taipei 5.6%. Primary markets ranged from 0.7% in Johor, Malaysia, to 22.1% in Bangkok.

  Cushman & Wakefield noted that Manila continued to hold significant available capacity relative to other regional markets.

  ## Manila against the regional trend

  The pipeline contraction in Manila ran counter to the broader Asia-Pacific trajectory. Developers across the region added 7,103 MW to the pipeline during the first half of 2026, lifting the regional total to 26,455 MW — of which 4,764 MW was under construction and 21,691 MW was in planning. An additional 1,372 MW of new operational capacity came online across Asia-Pacific during the period.

  Regional colocation vacancy eased to 10.3% from 10.9% in the second half of 2025 as demand continued to absorb new supply.

  Cushman & Wakefield said artificial intelligence (AI) and cloud investment across Asia-Pacific had entered a period of rapid but power-constrained execution, with electricity availability becoming a primary bottleneck. The consultancy said new capacity was increasingly shifting away from saturated hubs toward peripheral areas and new locations, and that investors favored larger projects with clear AI-driven demand and the capacity to support hyperscale — very large, single-tenant cloud and AI — operations.

  ## Manila's market classification and investment signals

  Cushman & Wakefield classifies Manila as an emerging data center market, alongside cities including Pune, Batam, Taipei City, Auckland, Perth, Bengaluru, Canberra, Busan, Ho Chi Minh City, Hanoi, and Brisbane. Emerging markets collectively accounted for 7% of Asia-Pacific's operational data center capacity. Their aggregate vacancy rate declined to 18% in the first half from 26% in the previous six months, which Cushman & Wakefield said reflected improving digital infrastructure and enterprise demand.

  For the Philippines specifically, the consultancy cited several developments it said supported longer-term sector growth: the launch of the National Artificial Intelligence Center for Research and Innovation, which is intended to support AI research, advanced computing, and industry collaboration; a $170-million commitment to the Philippine data center sector by the International Finance Corp. (IFC), the private-sector arm of the World Bank Group; and the commissioning of a 12-MW data center near Manila by Converge Information and Communications Technology Solutions, Inc.

  ## What it means

  Manila's pipeline contraction does not signal demand weakness alone — the simultaneous drop in vacancy and rise in operational capacity show that existing projects are being built out and, to a degree, absorbed. The more consequential signal is the gap in new project announcements: with only 5 MW actively under construction, near-term supply growth is limited, and the market's ability to attract larger AI- or hyperscale-driven projects will depend on whether developers bring new schemes forward.

  Cushman & Wakefield said future data center investment across Asia-Pacific is expected to concentrate in markets that can offer scalable power supply, developable land, and strong network connectivity, identifying those three factors as the primary determinants of the pace of new capacity development.
body_zh: |
  根據房地產顧問公司Cushman & Wakefield發布的《亞太資料中心2026年上半年更新報告》，馬尼拉資料中心開發管線在2026年上半年縮減13%，降至97百萬瓦（MW），近期完工設施上線的速度超過新案宣布的補充速度，使菲律賓首都成為亞太地區報告所追蹤市場中代管機房空置率最高的城市。

  總開發管線——涵蓋施工中及規劃階段的容量——於6月底達97 MW，較前期下滑。積極施工中的容量從22 MW大幅降至5 MW，原因是多個專案陸續完工交付；尚未動工的規劃容量則小幅升至92 MW。

  Cushman & Wakefield指出，此一下滑顯示新案宣布尚未充分填補近期交付供給所留下的缺口。

  ## 空置率與營運容量

  可供客戶使用的營運資訊技術（IT）容量成長22%，達90 MW，多座新完工設施相繼上線，使馬尼拉的資料中心網絡擴展至28座、由16家業者共同營運。

  儘管供給增加，馬尼拉的代管機房空置率——即無租戶的可用容量占比——仍從前一個半年期的48%改善至43.6%，顯示需求已吸納部分新增容量。代管機房模式係指業者將空間、電力及冷卻設施租予多家客戶，而非僅服務單一租戶。

  即便有所改善，馬尼拉在報告個別分析的15個亞太一線與二線市場中，仍錄得最高空置率。在可比較的次要市場中，胡志明市空置率為34.8%、伯斯34.3%、德里國家首都地區10.9%、奧克蘭6.4%、台北5.6%。一線市場空置率則介於馬來西亞柔佛的0.7%至曼谷的22.1%之間。

  Cushman & Wakefield指出，相較於其他區域市場，馬尼拉持續保有相當可觀的可用容量。

  ## 馬尼拉與區域走勢比較

  馬尼拉管線萎縮的走向，與亞太地區整體趨勢背道而馳。2026年上半年，亞太地區開發商共新增7,103 MW進入開發管線，使區域合計達26,455 MW——其中4,764 MW處於施工階段，21,691 MW處於規劃階段。同期，亞太地區另有1,372 MW的新增營運容量上線。

  區域代管機房空置率從2025年下半年的10.9%微降至10.3%，反映需求持續消化新增供給。

  Cushman & Wakefield表示，人工智慧（AI）與雲端投資在亞太地區已進入快速但受電力制約的執行階段，電力供應成為首要瓶頸。該公司指出，新增容量正逐漸從飽和樞紐轉移至周邊地區及新興地點，投資人青睞具備明確AI需求驅動、且能支援超大規模——即超大型單一租戶雲端與AI——營運能力的大型專案。

  ## 馬尼拉的市場定位與投資信號

  Cushman & Wakefield將馬尼拉歸類為新興資料中心市場，同列此類別的城市包括浦那、峇淡島、台北市、奧克蘭、伯斯、班加羅爾、坎培拉、釜山、胡志明市、河內及布里斯本。新興市場合計占亞太地區營運資料中心容量的7%。其整體空置率從前半年度的26%降至18%，Cushman & Wakefield認為這反映出數位基礎設施的改善及企業需求的增長。

  就菲律賓而言，Cushman & Wakefield點出數項其認為有助於該市場長期成長的進展：國家人工智慧研究與創新中心（National Artificial Intelligence Center for Research and Innovation）正式啟動，旨在支援AI研究、先進運算及產業合作；國際金融公司（IFC）——世界銀行集團旗下私部門業務機構——承諾向菲律賓資料中心產業投入1.7億美元；以及Converge資訊通訊科技公司（Converge Information and Communications Technology Solutions, Inc.）在馬尼拉近郊啟用一座12 MW的資料中心。

  ## 市場意涵

  馬尼拉管線縮減並不單純意味著需求疲軟——空置率同步下降、營運容量同步上升，顯示既有專案正持續完工，且在一定程度上被市場消化吸納。更值得關注的信號在於新案宣布出現斷層：目前積極施工中的容量僅5 MW，近期供給成長空間十分有限，市場能否吸引更大規模的AI或超大規模專案進駐，將取決於開發商能否及時推出新計畫。

  Cushman & Wakefield表示，預期亞太地區未來的資料中心投資將集中於能提供可擴充電力供應、可開發土地及強健網路連線能力的市場，並將此三項因素列為決定新容量開發速度的主要關鍵。
