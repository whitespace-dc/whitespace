body_en: |
  PT Jababeka Tbk, the Indonesian industrial estate developer, presented 16 investment-ready projects to Chinese and regional investors at the ASEAN-China Industrial Cooperation Forum (ACICF) held November 3–5, 2026, at Kota Jababeka in Cikarang, West Java, the company said. The three-day event produced eight signed memoranda of understanding (MoUs), which are non-binding framework agreements that typically precede formal investment contracts.

  The forum drew approximately 200 delegates and was used to facilitate business matching and networking between Chinese investors and Indonesian business operators, according to Jababeka. Setyono Djuandi Darmono, founder and chairman of PT Jababeka Tbk, led the company's push to deepen collaboration between the two sides through the forum.

  ## Sectors on offer

  The 16 projects span energy, artificial intelligence (AI), transportation, and industrial estate development, Jababeka said. No further breakdown of individual projects, deal values, or counterparty names was disclosed in the available reporting.

  ## Forum context

  The ACICF was held at Kota Jababeka, Jababeka's flagship industrial city in Cikarang, which serves as the venue and commercial backdrop for the investment pitches. The forum's agenda included references to several of Indonesia's special economic zones (SEZs) — designated areas with streamlined regulations and tax incentives intended to attract foreign manufacturing — including KEK Kendal in Central Java, KEK Morotai in North Maluku, and KEK Tanjung Lesung in Banten, according to Jababeka's promotional materials.

  ## Significance

  The forum signals continued effort by Indonesian industrial estate operators to position Chinese capital as a primary source of foreign direct investment in manufacturing and high-technology infrastructure, at a time when supply chain diversification is prompting Chinese firms to seek production bases in Southeast Asia. The signing of eight MoUs in a single event reflects active deal-making interest, though MoUs carry no binding financial commitments until formal agreements follow.
body_zh: |
  印尼產業園區開發商嘉柏卡（PT Jababeka Tbk）表示，該公司於2026年11月3日至5日在西爪哇省西卡朗的嘉柏卡城（Kota Jababeka）舉辦東協─中國產業合作論壇（ACICF），向中國及區域投資者發表16項已備妥的投資方案。這場為期三天的活動共簽署八份意向書（MOU）；意向書為非約束性框架協議，通常是正式投資合約的前置步驟。

  論壇吸引約200名代表出席。嘉柏卡表示，此次活動旨在促進中國投資者與印尼業者之間的商業配對與交流。嘉柏卡創辦人兼董事長塞提約諾・朱安迪・達爾莫諾（Setyono Djuandi Darmono）主導本次論壇，積極推動雙方深化合作。

  ## 招商領域

  嘉柏卡表示，16項投資方案涵蓋能源、人工智慧（AI）、交通及產業園區開發等領域。現有報導未進一步揭露各項目的詳細內容、交易金額或對方名稱。

  ## 論壇背景

  東協─中國產業合作論壇在嘉柏卡的旗艦產業城——西卡朗嘉柏卡城舉行，該城市既是論壇場地，也為本次投資推介提供了商業背景。論壇議程提及印尼數處經濟特區（SEZ）——即以簡化法規與稅收優惠吸引外資製造業進駐的指定區域，包括中爪哇的肯達爾經濟特區（KEK Kendal）、北馬魯古的莫羅泰經濟特區（KEK Morotai），以及萬丹的丹絨勒頌經濟特區（KEK Tanjung Lesung），上述資訊見於嘉柏卡的宣傳資料。

  ## 意義

  此次論壇顯示，印尼產業園區業者持續致力於將中國資本定位為製造業與高科技基礎設施外商直接投資（FDI）的主要來源。在供應鏈多元化浪潮的推動下，中國企業正積極尋求東南亞生產基地，印尼也藉此機遇加強招商引資。單一活動即促成八份意向書的簽署，反映各方積極洽談的強烈意願；惟意向書在正式協議簽訂之前，不具任何具體財務承諾。
