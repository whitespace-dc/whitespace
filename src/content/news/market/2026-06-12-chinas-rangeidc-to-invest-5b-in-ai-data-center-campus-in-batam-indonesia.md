body_en: |
  China-based Range Intelligent Computing Technology Company Limited (RangeIDC) plans to invest $5 billion in an artificial intelligence data center campus in Batam, Riau Islands province, Indonesia, marking the firm's first international expansion.

  ## Site and construction

  The proposed campus will occupy a 30-hectare site in Nongsa, Batam. Construction will be undertaken by PT Equator Gate System Batam (EGSB), an AI data center developer in Indonesia that RangeIDC backs.

  Before breaking ground, EGSB has already secured an electricity supply. PT PLN Batam, a subsidiary of Perusahaan Listrik Negara (PLN), Indonesia's state electricity company, has signed a Power Purchase Agreement with EGSB to deliver power to the facility.

  ## RangeIDC

  Listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, RangeIDC is one of the largest data center companies in China. The Batam campus would be its first project outside the country.

  ## Projected economic impact

  The investment is expected to create between 700 and 800 professional jobs and to draw related industries, including cloud computing, semiconductor ecosystems, and AI services, to the island, according to Fary Djemy Francis, Deputy for Investment at the Batam Free Trade Zone and Free Port Authority (BP Batam).

  Li Claudia Chandra, Vice Chairwoman of BP Batam, said the investment marked a turning point for Batam. "This AI data center investment marks a pivotal moment to accelerate Batam's transformation towards a technology-, innovation-, and digitalisation-based economy," she told ANTARA News Agency in late May.

  Francis described Batam as moving beyond its role as a manufacturing zone toward a "global digital hub" and said BP Batam would fast-track the execution mechanisms needed to accelerate the investment's realization.
body_zh: |
  中國睿跨智算科技股份有限公司（RangeIDC）計畫在印尼廖內群島省峇淡島投資50億美元，興建一座人工智慧資料中心園區，此案也是該公司首度跨足海外市場。

  ## 選址與建設

  擬建園區預計座落於峇淡島農沙（Nongsa）地區，佔地30公頃。建設工程將由PT Equator Gate System Batam（EGSB）負責執行，EGSB為印尼本地的AI資料中心開發商，背後由RangeIDC提供支持。

  動工前，EGSB已率先完成電力供應的安排。印尼國家電力公司（Perusahaan Listrik Negara，PLN）旗下子公司PT PLN Batam已與EGSB簽署購電協議（Power Purchase Agreement），確保園區的電力供應無虞。

  ## 睿跨IDC

  RangeIDC在深圳證券交易所掛牌上市，為中國規模最大的資料中心業者之一。峇淡島園區一旦落成，將是該公司首個海外據點。

  ## 預期經濟效益

  根據峇淡島自由貿易區暨自由港管理局（BP Batam）投資副局長法利·傑米·法蘭西斯（Fary Djemy Francis）的說法，這項投資預計將為島上帶來700至800個專業就業機會，並吸引雲端運算、半導體生態系及AI服務等相關產業進駐。

  BP Batam副主席李·克勞蒂亞·珊德拉（Li Claudia Chandra）表示，這項投資標誌著峇淡島的歷史轉捩點。她在5月下旬接受印尼國家通訊社ANTARA採訪時表示：「這項AI資料中心投資，是加速峇淡島轉型為以科技、創新與數位化為核心之經濟體的關鍵時刻。」

  法蘭西斯則指出，峇淡島正逐步超越傳統製造業重鎮的定位，朝「全球數位樞紐」邁進，並強調BP Batam將全力推動相關執行機制，加速這項投資的落地實現。
