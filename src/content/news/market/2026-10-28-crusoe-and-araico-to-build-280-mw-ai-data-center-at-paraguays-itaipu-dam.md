  ## Crusoe and Araico to build 280 MW AI data center at Paraguay's Itaipú Dam

  U.S. artificial intelligence infrastructure company Crusoe has announced a partnership with Paraguayan energy and infrastructure developer Araico to construct a high-performance AI compute facility adjacent to the Itaipú Hydroelectric Dam, with an initial phase targeting 106 megawatts of IT capacity online by mid-2027 and options to expand to approximately 280 MW over time.

  The facility will be located next to the Itaipú substation in Paraguay, giving it direct access to one of the world's largest renewable hydroelectric power sources. Crusoe said the project will serve customers in both the United States and Latin America through its Crusoe Cloud platform. The company described the deal as its first AI cloud deployment in Latin America.

  ## Scale and timeline

  The project's initial deployment will reach up to 106 MW of IT capacity — enough to power a large-scale AI training and inference workload — with contractual options to scale to roughly 280 MW. The companies did not specify a timeline for the full buildout. Construction is set to begin ahead of the mid-2027 first-deployment target.

  For context, 280 MW of total power capacity at a data center is at the larger end of hyperscale-class facilities, capable of supporting thousands of high-density AI accelerator servers.

  ## Paraguay's energy position

  Paraguay's attractiveness for data center investment rests on the Itaipú Dam, a binational hydroelectric facility on the Paraná River that the country co-operates with Brazil. The dam's output makes Paraguay one of the world's largest per-capita renewable electricity producers.

  CBRE's Global Data Center Trends 2026 report, cited in the announcement, identified Paraguay's abundant hydroelectric power and agreeable regulatory environment as factors drawing investors to the country.

  Cully Cavness, Crusoe's chief strategy officer, president, and co-founder, said the company's strategy is designed to "unlock and add value" to large, stranded or underutilized power resources through digital infrastructure. Alejandro Zuccolillo, Araico's chief executive, said the partnership brings "world-class AI infrastructure to Paraguay while showcasing our nation's renewable energy leadership on a global scale."

  Paraguay's President Santiago Peña said the project "directly unlocks new value from our natural resources" and described it as a model for converting the country's energy surplus into technology investment, jobs, and digital development.

  ## Regional market context

  The announcement comes as South America's data center construction market is expanding. According to research firm Mordor Intelligence, the regional construction market is projected to grow from $5.72 billion in 2026 to $8.88 billion by 2031.

  For Crusoe, the Paraguay facility marks a geographic extension of an infrastructure strategy centered on locating compute capacity near abundant, low-cost renewable power rather than in established markets where energy constraints are increasingly acute. The project gives the company a foothold in Latin America at a time when AI infrastructure demand is driving operators to source large power blocks wherever they can be secured at scale.

  ## Crusoe與Araico攜手在巴拉圭伊泰普水壩興建280 MW AI資料中心

  美國人工智慧基礎設施業者Crusoe宣布與巴拉圭能源及基礎設施開發商Araico達成合作，計畫在伊泰普水力發電廠（Itaipú Hydroelectric Dam）旁興建一座高效能AI運算設施。首期目標為2027年中前達成106百萬瓦（MW）IT容量的上線部署，並保有擴建至約280 MW的選項。

  該設施將坐落於巴拉圭境內的伊泰普變電站旁，可直接取用全球最大再生水力電源之一。Crusoe表示，此計畫將透過旗下Crusoe Cloud平台，同時服務美國及拉丁美洲客戶，並將其定位為公司首個進軍拉丁美洲的AI雲端部署案。

  ## 建設規模與時程

  此計畫首期部署將達最高106 MW的IT容量——足以支撐大規模AI訓練與推論工作負載——並設有擴建至約280 MW的合約選項。雙方目前尚未公布完整建設的時程規劃，施工將於2027年中首期部署目標前展開。

  就規模而言，280 MW的資料中心總用電容量已達超大規模（hyperscale）等級設施的較高段，能夠支援數千台高密度AI加速器伺服器的運作。

  ## 巴拉圭的能源優勢

  巴拉圭對資料中心投資者的吸引力，主要來自伊泰普水壩——這座橫跨巴拉那河（Paraná River）的跨國水力發電廠由巴拉圭與巴西共同營運，其龐大的發電量使巴拉圭躋身全球人均再生電力產量最高的國家之列。

  此次公告引用了CBRE發布的《2026年全球資料中心趨勢》報告，報告中將巴拉圭充沛的水力電力資源及友善的法規環境，列為吸引投資者進駐的關鍵因素。

  Crusoe共同創辦人暨首席策略長兼總裁Cully Cavness表示，公司策略旨在透過數位基礎設施，「釋放並提升」大量閒置或未充分利用的電力資源的價值。Araico執行長Alejandro Zuccolillo則表示，這項合作將「把世界級的AI基礎設施帶進巴拉圭，同時讓我國的再生能源領導地位在全球舞台大放異彩。」

  巴拉圭總統聖地牙哥·裴尼亞（Santiago Peña）表示，此計畫「直接從我國天然資源中開創出新的價值」，並將其形容為把該國能源盈餘轉化為科技投資、就業機會與數位發展的典範之作。

  ## 區域市場背景

  此次宣布，正值南美洲資料中心建設市場持續擴張之際。根據研究機構Mordor Intelligence的資料，該地區的建設市場規模預計將從2026年的57.2億美元，成長至2031年的88.8億美元。

  對Crusoe而言，巴拉圭設施標誌著其基礎設施策略在地理版圖上的延伸——該策略的核心，在於將運算資源部署於擁有豐沛、低成本再生電力的地點，而非佈局在能源限制日益嚴峻的既有市場。在AI基礎設施需求驅使業者四處尋覓可大規模取得大容量電力區塊的當下，這項計畫為Crusoe在拉丁美洲取得了重要的戰略立足點。
