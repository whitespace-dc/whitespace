body_en: |
  Indonesia's industrial estate association projects that land demand will surge in the second half of 2026, led by hyperscale data center operators seeking large dedicated plots alongside sustained manufacturing inflows, as the government prepares a national coordinating body designed to cut chronic regulatory delays.

  ## Data centers emerge as primary driver of land demand

  Akhmad Ma'ruf Muhammad, chairman of Himpunan Kawasan Industri (HKI), the Indonesian industrial zone association, told Bisnis.com on July 14 that demand for industrial land in H2 2026 would be heavily supported by the data center sector. Data center operators require large, fixed land areas to maintain data sovereignty and efficient regional connectivity, he said.

  Manufacturing demand is running in parallel. Indonesia's Ministry of Investment and Downstreaming/Investment Coordinating Board (BKPM) recorded investment realization of Rp498.8 trillion in the first quarter of 2026, equal to 24.4% of the government's full-year target of Rp2,041.3 trillion. That leaves Rp1,542 trillion in investment still targeted for Indonesia before year-end, according to BKPM data cited by HKI.

  HKI projects significant escalation in industrial plot demand across several main Java corridors. New zone development is also planned in the Kepulauan Riau archipelago, covering Tanjung Pinang and Batam. On Java, growth is expected in Gresik and Sidoarjo in East Java, West Java, Central Java, and in a new development on the island of Madura.

  ## Semarang identified as north Java's most competitive industrial corridor

  Property consultancy Colliers Indonesia identifies Semarang as likely to be the most competitive industrial area along Java's northern coast. Ferry Salanto, Senior Associate Director at Colliers Indonesia, said the zone's appeal has shifted beyond low land prices and competitive minimum wages.

  "We see Semarang starting to offer industrial ecosystem quality. Developers there are not only selling land; they are also starting to be concerned about operational certainty for investors," Salanto said.

  Global manufacturing investors now prioritize reliable supporting facilities to mitigate business disruption risks, Salanto said. Industrial zone developers in the Semarang corridor are responding by strengthening internal utilities, including drainage systems, energy supply security, and logistics network integration.

  The Greater Semarang industrial footprint is expanding outward, with growth extending west into Kendal and Batang regencies and east toward Demak regency, according to Colliers. Two Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus (KEK), or Special Economic Zones, operating in the corridor provide fiscal and non-fiscal incentives that attract large-scale industrial players. Connectivity through the Trans-Java toll road network and direct sea port access add to the region's logistics position.

  ## Licensing delays drive push for industrial zone law

  HKI has been pressing for accelerated passage of a draft Industrial Zone Law, known in Indonesian as the Rancangan Undang-Undang Kawasan Industri (RUU Kawasan Industri), citing persistent delays caused by regional licensing bureaucracy and land availability constraints. Ma'ruf described the problems as recurring across successive administrations and called for a dedicated investment acceleration task force. HKI has called for the bill to be finalized within 2026.

  ## Government plans national council to coordinate industrial zone policy

  The government is preparing a structural response. Tri Supondy, Director General of the Resilience, Regional Affairs, and International Industrial Access (KPAII) directorate at the Ministry of Industry, said industrial zone development requires stronger inter-ministerial coordination because it spans spatial planning, energy, infrastructure, investment, and logistics. He said a National Industrial Zone Council, or Dewan Kawasan Industri Nasional (DKIN), would be established to address this.

  PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk. (KIJA), a listed industrial zone developer, welcomed the DKIN initiative but called for precise authority limits on the body. Corporate Secretary Muljadi Suganda said industrial zone expansion integrates spatial planning, infrastructure, utilities, and investment, making cross-ministerial policy harmonization a necessity.

  The Semarang corridor and the Riau Islands, including Batam, are the primary growth destinations identified by HKI and Colliers for the coming expansion. The pace of land absorption will depend in part on how quickly policymakers can pass the Industrial Zone Law and stand up the DKIN council to address the licensing and land constraints HKI says have persisted for years.

  印尼工業區協會預測，工業用地需求將於2026年下半年大幅攀升，超大規模資料中心業者積極尋求大型專屬地塊，製造業投資亦持續湧入；與此同時，印尼政府正積極籌備一個全國性協調機構，以解決長年困擾業界的法規審批延宕問題。

  ## 資料中心躍居工業用地需求主要驅動力

  印尼工業區協會（Himpunan Kawasan Industri，HKI）主席阿赫馬德·馬魯夫·穆罕默德（Akhmad Ma'ruf Muhammad）7月14日接受Bisnis.com採訪時指出，資料中心產業將大力支撐2026年下半年的工業用地需求。他表示，資料中心業者需要大面積的固定用地，以維護資料主權並確保高效的區域連結性。

  製造業需求同步升溫。印尼投資暨下游化部／投資協調委員會（BKPM）統計，2026年第一季投資實現額達498.8兆印尼盾，相當於政府全年目標2,041.3兆印尼盾的24.4%。根據HKI引用的BKPM數據，印尼今年底前仍有1,542兆印尼盾的投資目標有待達成。

  HKI預測，爪哇島多條主要走廊的工業地塊需求將顯著成長。廖內群島（Kepulauan Riau）方面，丹戎檳榔（Tanjung Pinang）與巴淡（Batam）亦規劃新增工業區。爪哇島上，成長重心則包括東爪哇的格雷西克（Gresik）與泗水（Sidoarjo）、西爪哇、中爪哇，以及馬都拉島（Madura）的新開發地帶。

  ## 三寶壟躍居北爪哇最具競爭力工業走廊

  房地產顧問公司高力國際（Colliers Indonesia）指出，三寶壟（Semarang）極有可能成為爪哇北岸競爭力最強的工業區。高力印尼高級副董事費里·薩蘭托（Ferry Salanto）表示，該區域的吸引力已不再僅止於低廉的地價與具競爭力的最低工資。

  薩蘭托說：「我們看到三寶壟開始在工業生態系統的品質上嶄露頭角。當地開發商不只是在賣地，也開始致力於為投資者提供營運確定性。」

  薩蘭托進一步指出，全球製造業投資者如今更重視可靠的配套設施，以降低業務中斷的風險。三寶壟走廊的工業區開發商正積極因應市場需求，持續強化園區內部設施，涵蓋排水系統、能源供應保障及物流網絡整合。

  根據高力國際資料，大三寶壟工業腹地持續向外擴張，向西延伸至肯達爾（Kendal）與峇當（Batang）縣，向東則拓展至德馬克（Demak）縣。走廊內目前有兩個特別經濟區（Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus，KEK）已開始運作，提供財稅與非財稅優惠，吸引大型工業業者進駐。貫穿爪哇的高速公路網絡與直達海港的運輸通道，更進一步鞏固了該地區的物流優勢。

  ## 審批延宕推動工業區立法加速

  HKI持續推動《工業區法》草案（印尼語：Rancangan Undang-Undang Kawasan Industri，RUU Kawasan Industri）加速立法，理由是地方政府繁瑣的審批程序與土地供應不足問題長期存在。馬魯夫表示，這些問題在歷屆政府任期內反覆出現，並呼籲組建專責的投資加速工作小組。HKI要求上述法案於2026年內完成立法。

  ## 政府籌組全國委員會統籌工業區政策

  政府正準備推出結構性因應方案。印尼工業部韌性、區域事務與國際工業准入局（KPAII）局長特里·蘇彭地（Tri Supondy）表示，工業區開發涵蓋空間規劃、能源、基礎建設、投資與物流等多個層面，需要更強而有力的跨部會協調機制。他宣布，政府將成立「國家工業區委員會」（Dewan Kawasan Industri Nasional，DKIN）以應對上述挑戰。

  上市工業區開發商PT Kawasan Industri Jababeka Tbk.（KIJA）對DKIN的成立表示歡迎，但同時呼籲明確界定該機構的職權範圍。公司秘書穆爾賈迪·蘇甘達（Muljadi Suganda）表示，工業區擴張整合了空間規劃、基礎建設、公用事業與投資等多個面向，跨部會政策的協調整合實屬必要。

  就HKI與高力國際所點名的本波擴張熱點而言，三寶壟走廊與廖內群島（含巴淡島）是兩大首要成長目的地。土地吸納的速度，將部分取決於政策制定者能否儘速通過《工業區法》、並啟動DKIN委員會，從而化解HKI所描述的那些長年積累的審批瓶頸與土地供應限制。
