body_en: |
  DayOne Data Centers, the Singapore-based data centre operator, has confidentially filed for an initial public offering in the United States and is considering raising approximately US$5 billion from the listing, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified discussing private information.

  The company is aiming to list as early as next quarter. Bloomberg News previously reported that DayOne may target a valuation of around US$20 billion from the offering. Considerations are ongoing and details, including size and timing, may change, the people said. DayOne did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

  ## Series C funding round

  DayOne closed a US$4.5 billion Series C funding round in June, led by its two largest shareholders, Coatue Management and Hillhouse. New investors in the round included Achi Capital Partners and the Indonesia Investment Authority. The company said the proceeds will accelerate its expansion across Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, Japan, Hong Kong, Finland and Spain.

  ## Scale and investor base

  Founded in 2022, DayOne has secured more than 1.5 gigawatts of committed capacity bookings across the Asia-Pacific region and Europe. A gigawatt is a measure of electrical power capacity, used in the data centre industry to indicate how much electricity a portfolio of facilities can draw at full load. The company's other investors include China's GDS Holdings, SoftBank Vision Fund and Ken Griffin's Citadel.

  ## Data centre IPO activity

  DayOne's filing joins a growing pipeline of data centre operators pursuing US listings. Switch and Nscale are also lining up IPOs in the United States as soon as this year. Investor interest in the sector has been surging with the rise of artificial intelligence, the sources noted.

  Australia's Firmus Technologies said last week it had received commitments for a US$2 billion investment round. Coatue, one of DayOne's biggest shareholders, participated in that round as well.

  Dallas-based Csquare, backed by Brookfield Corp, raised US$1.05 billion in an IPO last month after pricing its shares at US$21 each, below the marketed range. Csquare shares closed at US$21.60 on Monday in New York.

  ## C輪融資

  DayOne於今年6月完成45億美元的C輪融資，由其兩大最主要股東蔻圖管理（Coatue Management）與高瓴（Hillhouse）共同領投。本輪新加入的投資人包括Achi Capital Partners及印尼投資局（Indonesia Investment Authority）。公司表示，所募資金將用於加速推進其在新加坡、馬來西亞、印尼、泰國、日本、香港、芬蘭及西班牙等地的業務擴張。

  ## 規模與投資人陣容

  DayOne成立於2022年，迄今已在亞太地區及歐洲取得逾1.5吉瓦（GW）的確定容量預訂。吉瓦為電力容量單位，資料中心業界以此衡量旗下設施組合在全載狀態下的最大用電規模。該公司其他投資人還包括中國的萬國數據（GDS Holdings）、軟銀願景基金（SoftBank Vision Fund），以及投資人肯·葛里芬（Ken Griffin）旗下的城堡投資（Citadel）。

  ## 資料中心IPO動態

  DayOne的申報案加入了一批正積極籌謀美國上市的資料中心業者行列。Switch與Nscale同樣規劃最快於今年在美掛牌。知情人士指出，隨著人工智慧（AI）浪潮興起，投資人對這個產業的熱情持續高漲。

  澳洲Firmus Technologies上週宣布已獲得20億美元的投資承諾。DayOne的大股東蔻圖管理亦參與了該輪融資。

  總部位於達拉斯、獲布魯克菲爾德公司（Brookfield Corp）支持的Csquare，上個月以每股21美元的價格完成IPO，低於原先的定價區間，合計募資10.5億美元。Csquare股票週一在紐約收報21.60美元。
