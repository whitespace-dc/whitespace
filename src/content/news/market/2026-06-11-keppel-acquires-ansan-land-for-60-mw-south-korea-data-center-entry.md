body_en: |
  Keppel Ltd has acquired a development site in Ansan, South Korea through its private fund Keppel Data Centre Fund III (KDCF III) to build a 60-megawatt (MW) data center, marking the Singapore-based asset manager and operator's first entry into the South Korean market.

  Under the transaction, KDCF III will take an approximately 73 percent effective stake in a special purpose vehicle that owns the Ansan site, the company said in a press release. The site is located in South Korea's Seoul Metropolitan Area (SMA) and has already obtained construction permits and power approvals. The facility is scheduled to begin operations by 2030.

  ## Project scope and customer targets

  The 60 MW facility is intended to be built to Tier III-equivalent specifications — an industry designation meaning the facility is designed to allow maintenance and repairs without taking operations offline. Target customers include hyperscalers, large-scale cloud infrastructure operators, cloud service providers, and corporate customers, the company said.

  The Ansan project is the second investment made by KDCF III. As of the end of 2025, KDCF III held approximately S$2.7 billion (roughly US$2.7 billion) in funds under management. Keppel's broader Connectivity segment, which includes KDCF III and other funds, totaled S$13 billion (US$10 billion) at the same date.

  Keppel said the transaction is not expected to have a material impact on its net tangible assets per share or earnings per share for the current financial year.

  ## Why South Korea

  Keppel executives cited supply constraints in the SMA alongside strong demand from cloud providers and hyperscalers as the central factors behind the investment decision.

  "South Korea represents a compelling growth market for digital infrastructure, underpinned by strong demand from cloud service providers and hyperscalers, alongside limited new supply in the SMA to deliver scalable, high-quality and AI-ready infrastructure solutions that meet the evolving needs of our customers," said Manjot Singh Mann, CEO of Keppel's Connectivity Division.

  Lee Hui Fang, Keppel's Chief Investment Officer, described South Korea as "one of Asia's most exciting data center investment markets," citing what she called "the convergence of burgeoning AI workloads and a power-constrained market in SMA" as the entry rationale.

  Keppel cited projections showing that new data center power demand in the Seoul Metropolitan Area could reach approximately 2.3 gigawatts (GW) by 2030. To put that figure in context, 2.3 GW is roughly equivalent to 38 facilities the size of the planned Ansan development.

  ## South Korea's data center backdrop

  South Korea's data center sector has expanded in recent years, driven by cloud adoption, growing AI-related demand, and government efforts to develop the country's AI industry, according to the company.

  ## Portfolio position and significance

  The Ansan project will be added to Keppel's existing data center portfolio, which comprised 39 facilities with a combined gross power capacity exceeding 800 MW as of the end of 2025. South Korea was not previously represented in that portfolio, making this acquisition the company's entry into a new national market.

  The deal illustrates how institutional capital is moving toward power-constrained markets in Asia-Pacific where AI workload demand from hyperscalers and cloud providers is outpacing new supply, particularly in high-density urban corridors such as the SMA.

  Keppel projects the Ansan facility will be operational by 2030.

  ## 首度進軍南韓市場

  吉寶集團（Keppel Ltd）透過旗下私募基金「吉寶資料中心基金三號」（Keppel Data Centre Fund III，KDCF III），在南韓安山市取得一處開發用地，計畫興建一座60百萬瓦（MW）資料中心，標誌著這家新加坡資產管理暨營運商正式踏入南韓市場。

  根據交易條款，KDCF III將取得持有安山用地之特殊目的公司約73%的有效股權，吉寶集團在新聞稿中表示。該用地位於南韓首爾都會區（Seoul Metropolitan Area，SMA），目前已取得建設許可及電力核准，設施預計於2030年前正式投入營運。

  ## 專案規模與目標客群

  這座60 MW設施將依Tier III同等標準興建——此為業界通用規格，代表設施在不中斷營運的情況下，即可進行維護與修繕作業。吉寶表示，目標客戶涵蓋超大規模雲端業者（hyperscaler）、大型雲端基礎設施運營商、雲端服務供應商及企業客戶。

  安山專案是KDCF III的第二筆投資。截至2025年底，KDCF III管理資產規模約達27億新加坡元（約合27億美元）。吉寶集團旗下涵蓋KDCF III及其他基金的「連結事業群」（Connectivity segment），在同一時間點的管理規模合計高達130億新加坡元（約100億美元）。

  吉寶表示，本次交易預計不會對本財年每股有形淨資產或每股盈餘造成重大影響。

  ## 選擇南韓的理由

  吉寶高層指出，首爾都會區供給受限、加上雲端服務供應商與超大規模業者需求強勁，是此次投資決策的核心考量。

  吉寶連結事業群執行長 Manjot Singh Mann 表示：「南韓是數位基礎設施極具吸引力的成長市場，背後有雲端服務供應商與超大規模業者的強勁需求作為支撐，加之首爾都會區新增供給有限，這讓我們得以提供可擴充、高品質且AI就緒的基礎設施解決方案，滿足客戶持續演進的需求。」

  吉寶集團首席投資長 Lee Hui Fang 形容南韓為「亞洲最令人振奮的資料中心投資市場之一」，並以她所稱「AI工作負載蓬勃興起與首爾都會區電力受限市場相互匯聚」作為進場的核心理由。

  吉寶引用市場預測數據指出，首爾都會區新增資料中心用電需求至2030年可望達到約2.3吉瓦（GW）。以此數字換算，2.3 GW約相當於38座安山規劃設施的合計規模。

  ## 南韓資料中心市場背景

  根據吉寶的說法，南韓資料中心產業近年來持續擴張，成長動能來自雲端服務普及、AI相關需求升溫，以及政府積極推動本國AI產業發展等多重因素。

  ## 投資組合定位與戰略意義

  安山專案將納入吉寶現有資料中心投資組合。截至2025年底，該投資組合共計39座設施，合計總電力容量逾800 MW。南韓過去從未出現在這份版圖之中，因此本次收購標誌著吉寶首度進入這一全新國家市場。

  這筆交易清楚說明，機構資本正持續流向亞太地區電力供給受限的市場——在這些市場中，超大規模業者與雲端服務供應商所帶來的AI工作負載需求，正快速超越新增供給，首爾都會區等高密度城市廊帶尤為明顯。
