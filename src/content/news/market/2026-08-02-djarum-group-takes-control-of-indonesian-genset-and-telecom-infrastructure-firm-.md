  ## Djarum Group Takes Control of Indonesian Genset and Telecom Infrastructure Firm BACH

  Indonesian conglomerate Djarum Group took a controlling stake in newly listed PT Bach Multi Global Tbk (BACH) in July 2026, marking the group's latest move into generator set (genset) sales and rental and telecommunications infrastructure construction and maintenance services, Kontan reported on July 31.

  BACH is described by Kontan as a newly listed company — meaning it recently completed an initial public offering on the Indonesian stock exchange. Djarum Group's acquisition of a controlling position makes the conglomerate the primary shareholder directing the company's strategic decisions.

  ## Acquisition as Core Strategy

  Kontan characterized the BACH transaction as part of a pattern, describing Djarum Group's business wings as continuing to expand and identifying acquisition as the group's primary strategy for entering new sectors and strengthening existing businesses.

  The BACH deal adds two business lines to the conglomerate's portfolio: the sale and rental of gensets — portable or stationary electrical generators used to supply power independently of the main grid — and the construction and maintenance of telecommunications infrastructure.

  ## Significance

  The move signals that Djarum Group is extending its conglomerate footprint into infrastructure-adjacent services, specifically the equipment and services that underpin both power supply and telecommunications network build-out. The July 2026 acquisition of BACH is the most recent disclosed step in that strategy, according to Kontan.

  ## Djarum集團取得印尼發電機組與電信基礎設施公司BACH控制權

  印尼綜合企業集團Djarum Group於2026年7月取得甫完成首次公開發行的PT Bach Multi Global Tbk（BACH）控股權，正式踏入發電機組（genset）銷售租賃，以及電信基礎設施建設與維護服務領域。印尼財經媒體《Kontan》於7月31日對此進行了報導。

  BACH依《Kontan》的描述為一家甫上市的公司，意即該公司近期剛完成在印尼證券交易所的首次公開募股。Djarum Group取得控股地位後，成為主導公司策略決策的最大股東。

  ## 併購作為核心策略

  《Kontan》將此次BACH交易定性為一種模式的延續，指出Djarum Group旗下各事業版圖持續擴張，並將「併購」列為集團切入新產業及鞏固既有業務的主要策略。

  此次BACH交易為這家綜合企業的投資組合新增了兩條業務線：其一為發電機組的銷售與租賃，所謂發電機組係指可攜式或固定式的電力發電設備，用於在不依賴主電網的情況下獨立供電；其二則是電信基礎設施的建設與維護服務。

  ## 意義與展望

  此舉顯示Djarum Group正將集團版圖延伸至基礎設施相關服務領域，尤其是支撐電力供應與電信網路建設的設備與服務。根據《Kontan》的報導，2026年7月對BACH的收購，是集團目前為止在這一策略方向上最新公開揭露的一步。
