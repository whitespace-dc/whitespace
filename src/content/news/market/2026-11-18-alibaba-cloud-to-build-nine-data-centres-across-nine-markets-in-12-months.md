body_en: |
  Alibaba Cloud announced Monday it will build nine new data centres across nine markets over the next 12 months, with five-year investment in AI infrastructure set to exceed the company's previously disclosed figure of 380 billion yuan.

  The announcement came at the company's annual summit in Hong Kong. Yuan Zhiming, vice president of international business and general manager for the Asia Pacific North region, identified the nine target markets as Hong Kong, Malaysia, Turkey, Brazil, Germany, the United Arab Emirates, France, the Netherlands, and Finland. The nine markets will advance simultaneously, Yuan said, though the Hong Kong facility may progress faster than those in other markets. Specific plans are to be released at an appropriate time.

  ## Construction targets

  Alibaba Cloud said it aims to complete each new facility in under 100 days and to raise compute density per rack by five to ten times. Rack density is the amount of processing power deployable within a single standard equipment cabinet; higher density allows more AI workloads to be run in a given footprint.

  The company said it plans to expand total global data centre power capacity to 20 gigawatts (GW) by 2032. Power capacity, measured in GW (one GW equals one billion watts), represents the total electrical load a data centre portfolio can sustain to run servers and cooling systems. Alibaba Cloud attributed the target to rapid growth in demand for AI compute.

  ## Investment scale

  Alibaba Cloud said five-year investment in related infrastructure will far exceed 380 billion yuan, the figure the company had previously disclosed publicly. No revised total was provided.

  ## Hong Kong operations

  Alibaba Cloud said it currently serves more than 65,000 customers in Hong Kong and has ranked first by revenue in the infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS) market there for six consecutive years. IaaS refers to cloud services that deliver foundational compute, storage, and networking resources on demand. More than 95 percent of the company's enterprise customers in Hong Kong have activated AI services, the company said.

  Alibaba Cloud said it has launched Qwen4-Max, its latest AI model, in Hong Kong. Chips developed by Pingtouge, Alibaba's in-house semiconductor unit, are deployed in Hong Kong and Macau for use in financial services and telecommunications, the company added.

  ## Media partnership

  Alibaba Cloud announced a collaboration with Hong Kong-based Media Asia Entertainment, combining Alibaba's video generation model with Media Asia's film and television intellectual property assets to introduce AI tools to Hong Kong's film industry. The first project under the partnership will be an AI-assisted short film based on a classic Hong Kong film.

  ## Significance

  The simultaneous nine-market buildout spans Europe, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and the Americas alongside continued expansion in Hong Kong, indicating Alibaba Cloud's intent to establish AI-ready infrastructure across multiple international regions in parallel rather than sequentially. The stated 20 GW power target by 2032 and the commitment to exceed a 380 billion yuan investment ceiling define the scale the company is planning for its international AI and cloud services footprint.

  Alibaba Cloud said detailed construction plans for each market will be announced at an appropriate time.
body_zh: |
  阿里雲週一宣布，將在未來12個月內於九個市場各新建一座資料中心，對人工智慧基礎設施的五年投資金額將超越先前公開揭露的3,800億元人民幣。

  此次宣布是在香港年度高峰會上正式對外發布。阿里雲國際業務副總裁暨亞太北區總經理袁志明公布了九個目標市場，分別為香港、馬來西亞、土耳其、巴西、德國、阿拉伯聯合大公國、法國、荷蘭及芬蘭。袁志明表示，九個市場將同步推進，但香港的進度或許會比其他市場更快。各地的具體計畫將於適當時機另行公布。

  ## 建設目標

  阿里雲表示，目標是在100天內完成每座新設施的建設，並將每機架的運算密度提升五至十倍。機架密度是指在單一標準設備機櫃內可部署的運算能力；密度越高，在相同空間內便能執行更多人工智慧工作負載。

  阿里雲計畫於2032年前，將全球資料中心的總電力容量擴大至20吉瓦（GW）。電力容量以吉瓦為計量單位（1吉瓦等於10億瓦），代表整個資料中心體系能夠持續承載以驅動伺服器與冷卻系統的總電力負荷。阿里雲將這一目標的由來歸因於人工智慧運算需求的快速成長。

  ## 投資規模

  阿里雲表示，相關基礎設施的五年投資金額將遠超3,800億元人民幣——此為該公司先前對外公開揭露的數字。目前並未提供經修訂的新版總額。

  ## 香港業務

  阿里雲表示，目前在香港服務超過65,000名客戶，並已連續六年在當地基礎設施即服務（IaaS）市場中以營收位居第一。IaaS是指按需提供基礎運算、儲存與網路資源的雲端服務。阿里雲指出，香港逾95%的企業客戶已開通人工智慧服務。

  阿里雲表示，已在香港推出旗下最新人工智慧模型Qwen4-Max。阿里旗下自研半導體部門平頭哥所開發的晶片，目前已部署於香港及澳門，用於金融服務與電信領域。

  ## 媒體合作

  阿里雲宣布與香港媒體公司美亞娛樂資訊（Media Asia Entertainment）展開合作，結合阿里的影片生成模型與美亞的影視智慧財產資產，為香港電影業引入人工智慧工具。雙方合作的首個項目，將是以一部香港經典電影為藍本、由人工智慧輔助製作的短片。

  ## 意義

  此次九大市場同步建設的範圍橫跨歐洲、中東、東南亞及美洲，並延續香港的擴張步伐，顯示阿里雲有意在多個國際區域並行布建具備人工智慧能力的基礎設施，而非依序推進。2032年前達成20吉瓦電力目標的承諾，以及超越3,800億元人民幣投資上限的表態，共同勾勒出該公司在國際人工智慧與雲端服務版圖上的規劃規模。

  阿里雲表示，各市場的詳細建設計畫將於適當時機另行宣布。
