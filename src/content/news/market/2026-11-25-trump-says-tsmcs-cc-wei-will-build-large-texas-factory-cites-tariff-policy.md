body_en: |
  U.S. President Donald Trump said in a television interview that TSMC Chairman C.C. Wei will build a large factory in Texas, adding to the chipmaker's existing Arizona investment, and attributed both commitments to U.S. tariff policy.

  Trump made the remarks during a roughly one-hour phone interview on CNBC's morning program "Squawk on the Street." He described Wei — full name Wei Zheji, chairman of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), the contract chipmaker that fabricates silicon for leading chip designers — as "another friend of mine" and among a group of "outstanding people."

  ## Texas factory and Arizona investment

  Trump said Wei "will manufacture a very large portion of chips within the United States." On Arizona, he cited tariffs as the driver: "Because of tariff issues, he is investing hundreds of billions of dollars in Arizona." He added that Wei "will establish a large factory in Texas," and said separately, "I think he is starting to set up another factory, and the scale is very large."

  The remarks arose as Trump pushed back against host Jim Cramer's suggestion that the artificial intelligence (AI) industry revolves around NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang. Trump said NVIDIA — the dominant designer of AI accelerator chips — is "built on chips they don't actually manufacture," then pivoted to Wei: "You know what a great business that is, right? Those chips are made by Mr. Wei."

  ## NVIDIA reinstatement

  Trump's TSMC comments came in the same interview in which he discussed the reinstatement of NVIDIA's Jensen Huang. NVIDIA's board announced earlier that day it had voted unanimously to restore Huang as president, chief executive, and board director. The board said it was satisfied with Huang's candor and had confirmed with senior U.S. government officials that the reinstatement posed no national security risk. Trump claimed credit for facilitating Huang's return and called him a "national treasure."

  ## What it means

  Trump's framing casts U.S. tariff policy as the primary lever drawing large-scale semiconductor investment to American soil, positioning TSMC's moves as responses to trade pressure rather than purely market-driven decisions. The Texas factory remarks were made by the president, not by TSMC itself, and no independent confirmation from the company was included in the available reporting. If a Texas facility proceeds as Trump described, it would extend TSMC's U.S. manufacturing presence beyond its Arizona projects to a second state.

  美國總統川普接受CNBC電視專訪時表示，台積電（Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.，TSMC）董事長魏哲家將在德州興建一座大型工廠，為台積電在亞利桑那州既有投資之外的新布局，並將兩項承諾均歸因於美國關稅政策。

  川普係在CNBC早間財經節目《Squawk on the Street》中，透過長達約一小時的電話連線發表上述談話。他以全名介紹台積電董事長魏哲家——台積電是全球最大晶圓代工廠，專為頂尖晶片設計公司製造矽晶片——稱其為「我的另一位朋友」，並將他列為一群「傑出人士」之一。

  ## 德州建廠與亞利桑那州投資

  川普表示，魏哲家「將在美國境內製造相當大比例的晶片」。談到亞利桑那州的投資時，他以關稅為主要驅力，說道：「因為關稅問題，他正在亞利桑那州投資數千億美元。」川普進一步補充，魏哲家「將在德州設立一座大型工廠」，並另外說：「我認為他正著手設立另一座工廠，而且規模非常大。」

  這番話的背景，是川普反駁主持人吉姆·克拉默（Jim Cramer）所提出「人工智慧（AI）產業以輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳為核心」的說法。川普指出，輝達——AI加速晶片市場的主導設計商——「建立在自己根本不製造的晶片之上」，隨即話鋒一轉提及魏哲家：「你知道這是多好的生意嗎？那些晶片都是魏先生做的。」

  ## 輝達復職風波

  川普在同一場專訪中談及台積電之餘，也討論了輝達黃仁勳的復職一事。輝達董事會於同日稍早宣布，全體董事一致通過決議，恢復黃仁勳的總裁、執行長及董事席位。董事會表示對黃仁勳的坦誠態度感到滿意，並已向美國政府高層官員確認，此次復職不構成國家安全風險。川普宣稱是他居中斡旋才促成黃仁勳的回歸，並稱其為「國家瑰寶」。

  ## 意義解讀

  川普的論述框架，將美國關稅政策定位為吸引大規模半導體投資進駐美國本土的主要槓桿，將台積電的系列動作詮釋為因應貿易壓力的回應，而非純粹基於市場考量的主動決策。值得注意的是，德州建廠的說法出自川普本人，並非台積電官方聲明，現有報導中亦未有台積電方面的獨立確認。若德州廠如川普所描述的順利推進，台積電在美國的製造版圖將從亞利桑那州的既有計畫，進一步延伸至第二個州。
