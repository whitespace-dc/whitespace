body_en: |
  Singapore-based AI cloud provider Aolani and infrastructure software firm Rafay Systems have deployed NVIDIA DSX OS on NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 infrastructure, with the two companies describing the project as among the first of its kind in the industry.

  The deployment pairs Aolani's AI infrastructure with Rafay's software for orchestration, automation, multi-tenancy, and lifecycle management. According to the companies, users can provision Kubernetes clusters — the open-source container management system widely used to run AI workloads — virtual machines, AI workspaces, and inference environments through a self-service model, without requiring extensive manual setup. The platform also provides central governance, policy enforcement, and operational visibility.

  NVIDIA DSX OS is a software layer designed to manage AI systems. The GB200 NVL72 is NVIDIA's high-density accelerated computing infrastructure. Together, those components form the technical base of the deployment.

  ## From hardware to operating platform

  The companies framed the project as addressing a gap that has become more visible as investment in accelerated computing has grown. Installing advanced GPU systems is one task; turning them into a working service environment that multiple customers can access, share, and use securely is another. According to Aolani and Rafay, operators typically must assemble separate software for provisioning, access control, lifecycle management, and developer tooling — a process that can slow deployment and complicate governance.

  The platform is intended to reduce that integration burden. Rather than requiring operators or customers to piece together disparate tools, the deployment is designed to deliver ready access to AI environments on top of the underlying infrastructure, the companies said.

  The arrangement supports model development, training, inference, and AI service delivery. Multi-tenancy — the ability to let multiple users or organizations share infrastructure securely while keeping their workloads separated — is a central feature.

  ## What the chief executives said

  Nicholas Chia, Chief Executive Officer of Aolani, said speed of deployment and governance are central concerns for customers working with advanced AI systems.

  "Aolani has always been committed to delivering faster time-to-value for our customers. Our customers are at the bleeding edge of AI development, and they need to provision, govern and scale from day one in an industry that moves at lightning speed. Building the next generation of AI cloud means solving for more than just compute capacity, but also production-grade platforms that enable operational readiness from the get-go," Chia said.

  Haseeb Budhani, Chief Executive Officer and Co-Founder of Rafay Systems, said the focus of the market debate has shifted.

  "AI infrastructure has entered a new phase. The question is no longer how quickly organizations can deploy GPUs. It's how quickly they can transform that infrastructure into a governed, self-service platform that developers can use and operators can manage at scale," Budhani said.

  ## Company profiles

  Aolani was founded in Singapore in 2023 and focuses on AI cloud infrastructure in Asia. Rafay sells software for managing modern infrastructure and AI workloads for cloud operators, telecommunications providers, enterprises, and sovereign AI operators — state-backed projects building national AI capability. Rafay is part of NVIDIA Inception, the chip company's program for start-ups.

  ## Industry context

  The deployment reflects a broader shift in the AI infrastructure market. As enterprises, cloud providers, and state-backed AI projects install larger and more complex GPU systems, attention is moving toward the software layer that determines whether those systems can be run securely and offered commercially.

  For operators supporting multiple tenants on shared infrastructure, that software layer must allow developers to access resources quickly while giving operators oversight of usage, security policies, and system health. Competitive advantage, the companies argued, is increasingly tied to how quickly infrastructure owners can bring systems into service, onboard customers, and manage shared use without compromising governance.

  Rafay said its role in the project includes simplifying infrastructure bring-up and automating lifecycle management. The companies positioned the deployment as a demonstration of how infrastructure providers can improve utilisation of AI systems by making those systems easier to consume and manage from the point of initial deployment.

  新加坡AI雲端供應商Aolani與基礎設施軟體公司Rafay Systems已在NVIDIA GB200 NVL72基礎架構上完成NVIDIA DSX OS的部署，兩家公司將此次合作描述為業界首批同類型專案之一。

  此次部署結合了Aolani的AI基礎設施與Rafay提供的軟體，涵蓋協調調度、自動化、多租戶管理及生命週期管理等功能。根據兩家公司的說法，使用者無需進行繁瑣的手動設定，即可透過自助服務模式佈建Kubernetes叢集（一套廣泛用於執行AI工作負載的開源容器管理系統）、虛擬機器、AI工作空間及推論環境。此平台同時提供集中式治理、政策執行及營運可視性。

  NVIDIA DSX OS是專為管理AI系統所設計的軟體層，GB200 NVL72則是NVIDIA的高密度加速運算基礎架構，兩者共同構成此次部署的技術核心。

  ## 從硬體到營運平台

  兩家公司將此計畫定位為填補一項日益明顯的缺口——隨著加速運算的投資持續擴大，這道缺口也愈發清晰。安裝先進的GPU系統是一回事，將其轉化為可供多個客戶安全存取、共享與使用的實際服務環境，又是另一回事。根據Aolani與Rafay的說法，業者通常必須自行整合佈建、存取控制、生命週期管理及開發者工具等各類分散軟體，這一過程往往拖慢部署速度、增加治理複雜度。

  此平台旨在降低上述整合負擔。兩家公司表示，這套部署方案的設計目標，是讓業者或客戶無需自行拼湊各種工具，而是能夠在底層基礎架構之上，直接獲得可立即投入使用的AI環境。

  此平台支援模型開發、訓練、推論及AI服務交付。多租戶能力——即允許多位使用者或多個組織在安全隔離各自工作負載的前提下共用同一基礎架構的能力——是其核心特色之一。

  ## 執行長怎麼說

  Aolani執行長Nicholas Chia表示，部署速度與治理能力是客戶在使用先進AI系統時最核心的考量。

  「Aolani始終致力於為客戶創造更快的投資回報。我們的客戶站在AI發展的最前沿，他們必須從第一天起就能夠佈建、治理並快速擴展，因為這個產業的發展速度快如閃電。打造下一代AI雲端，不只是解決運算容量的問題，更要建立能從一開始就確保營運就緒的生產級平台，」Chia說道。

  Rafay Systems執行長暨共同創辦人Haseeb Budhani表示，市場討論的焦點已經轉移。

  「AI基礎設施已進入新的階段。問題不再是組織能多快速地部署GPU，而是能多快速地將這些基礎設施轉化為一個受治理的自助服務平台——讓開發者能夠使用、讓業者能夠大規模管理，」Budhani說道。

  ## 公司簡介

  Aolani於2023年在新加坡創立，專注於亞洲的AI雲端基礎設施。Rafay則銷售用於管理現代基礎設施及AI工作負載的軟體，客群涵蓋雲端業者、電信業者、企業以及主權AI業者——即由國家支持、致力於建構國家AI能力的計畫。Rafay是NVIDIA Inception計畫的成員，此為輝達針對新創公司所設立的合作計畫。

  ## 產業背景

  此次部署反映了AI基礎設施市場更廣泛的轉型趨勢。隨著企業、雲端供應商及國家支持的AI計畫持續建置規模更大、更複雜的GPU系統，外界的目光愈來愈多地轉向軟體層——正是這一層決定了這些系統能否被安全運行、並以商業方式對外提供服務。

  對於在共用基礎設施上支援多個租戶的業者而言，這套軟體層必須讓開發者能夠快速取得資源，同時賦予業者對使用狀況、安全政策及系統健康狀態的全面掌控。兩家公司認為，競爭優勢愈來愈取決於基礎設施擁有者能多快速地讓系統上線、完成客戶導入，並在不損及治理的前提下管理共用資源。

  Rafay表示，其在此次計畫中的角色包括簡化基礎設施的初始建置流程，以及自動化生命週期管理。兩家公司將此次部署定位為一項示範案例，展示基礎設施供應商如何從初始部署之時起，透過讓系統更易於使用與管理，來提升AI系統的整體使用效率。
