  ## AI adoption in Indonesia jumps to 40 percent, AWS study finds

  Forty percent of companies in Indonesia have adopted artificial intelligence (AI), up from 25 percent the previous year, according to a study titled "Unlocking Indonesia's AI Potential 2026" conducted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) and research firm Strand Partners. The findings were released at AWS Summit Jakarta 2026 on August 6, 2026.

  Despite the sharp increase, the study found that most adopters have not advanced far into their AI deployments. Of the 40 percent of companies that have adopted AI, 56 percent are still in what AWS describes as early stages — exploring AI's potential and running experiments to understand its operational impact — rather than running production systems.

  ## Where companies stand in their AI journeys

  Among businesses that have adopted AI, the study breaks down their progress as follows:

  - 56 percent are in early exploration and experimentation
  - 18 percent have deployed AI in select business functions
  - 14 percent are scaling AI use across multiple functions
  - 12 percent have integrated AI fully into their business processes

  Separately, AWS said that 44 percent of AI-adopting companies are now running the technology in production environments — meaning it is being used in live operations rather than in pilot or test settings.

  "This means the AI journey in Indonesia is no longer just a pilot phase. AI is no longer only a proof-of-concept project, but is beginning to be used in company operations," Anthony Amni, Country Manager for AWS Indonesia, said at the event.

  ## Readiness gaps remain a central challenge

  Amni said many Indonesian organizations face significant challenges at the outset of their AI adoption. He identified four key problem areas: business strategy readiness, measuring return on investment (ROI), talent development, and implementing AI governance — the policies and controls that determine how AI systems are managed and overseen.

  "The challenges range from business strategy readiness, ROI measurement, talent development, to the application of AI governance," Amni said.

  ## Productivity gains reported among adopters

  For companies that have moved forward with AI, the business impact is beginning to show. Seventy-five percent of AI-using companies in Indonesia reported productivity improvements, up from 68 percent the year prior, according to the study.

  ## Significance for the region

  The study's findings point to a market in which initial adoption is accelerating rapidly but where the majority of companies have yet to move beyond early experimentation. The gap between the pace of adoption and organizational readiness — in strategy, skills, and governance — represents the central challenge facing Indonesian businesses as AI moves from pilot projects into core operations.

  ## AWS研究：印尼AI導入率躍升至四成

  根據亞馬遜雲端運算服務（AWS）與研究機構Strand Partners共同發布的《解鎖印尼AI潛力2026》報告，印尼目前已有四成企業採用人工智慧（AI），較前一年的25%大幅提升。這份研究報告於2026年8月6日在「AWS Summit Jakarta 2026」峰會上正式公布。

  儘管導入比例急速攀升，研究卻也發現，多數採用AI的企業在實際部署上仍未走得太遠。在已導入AI的四成企業中，有56%仍處於AWS所定義的「早期階段」——亦即正在探索AI潛力、進行實驗性測試，以了解其對業務運作的影響，尚未達到正式上線運行的程度。

  ## 各企業的AI進展現況

  在已導入AI的企業中，研究依進展程度進一步細分如下：

  - 56% 仍處於早期探索與實驗階段
  - 18% 已在特定業務功能中部署AI
  - 14% 正將AI應用擴展至多個業務功能
  - 12% 已將AI全面整合至核心業務流程

  此外，AWS指出，在已採用AI的企業中，有44%正在正式生產環境中運行這項技術，意即AI已實際應用於企業日常運營，而非僅停留於試驗或測試環境。

  AWS印尼區總經理Anthony Amni在峰會上表示：「這意味著印尼的AI之旅已不再只是試驗階段。AI不再只是概念驗證專案，而是開始真正被導入企業的日常營運之中。」

  ## 準備度落差仍是核心挑戰

  Amni指出，許多印尼企業在AI導入初期便面臨相當大的挑戰。他點出四大關鍵問題領域：業務策略準備度、投資報酬率（ROI）衡量機制、人才培育，以及AI治理的落實——即企業如何制定相關政策與管控機制，以監督和管理AI系統的運作。

  「這些挑戰涵蓋了業務策略準備度、ROI衡量、人才培育，乃至AI治理的具體落實，」Amni說道。

  ## 已導入企業回報生產力提升

  對於已積極推進AI應用的企業而言，商業成效已逐漸顯現。根據這份研究，印尼使用AI的企業中有75%回報生產力有所提升，高於前一年的68%。

  ## 對整體市場的意義

  這份研究揭示出一個初期導入快速加速、但多數企業仍未能跨越早期實驗門檻的市場現況。在導入速度與企業整體準備度之間——涵蓋策略規劃、技能培育與治理建置——所存在的落差，正是印尼企業在AI從試驗專案邁向核心業務運營過程中，所面臨的最核心挑戰。
