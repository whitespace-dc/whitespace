body_en: |
  ## Saison Technology Sets July 1 Launch for Enterprise AI Agent Management Platform

  Saison Technology will release Agent Orchestration on July 1, 2026, a platform designed to let companies register, administer, and track multiple AI agents across an entire organization from a single interface, the company announced June 9.

  The product addresses a gap that has emerged as AI agent deployments move beyond individual use: the need to govern which data each agent can access, which large language model (LLM) it runs on, and which employees are permitted to use it. An LLM, in this context, is the underlying AI model — such as those from OpenAI or Anthropic — that powers an agent's reasoning and responses.

  ## What the Platform Does

  Agent Orchestration allows administrators to register multiple AI agents and configure each one's data access permissions, LLM assignment, and authorized user list independently. The platform also tracks token consumption — the unit of text processed by an LLM, which determines compute cost — on a per-model basis, and can compile agent usage data into reports.

  Aki Fukutomari, Director of the Product Management Division at Saison Technology's Development Division, said the company aimed to build a platform that balances the promotion of AI agent use with management and governance controls. The product is intended for organizations that want to deploy AI agents at a team or company-wide level, not merely as tools for individual productivity.

  ## Pricing and Distribution

  Agent Orchestration will be delivered as part of HULFT Square, Saison Technology's existing data-integration platform. Under the AI-Start Pack plan, the feature is included as standard equipment, starting at ¥680,000 per month (excluding tax). Customers on the Standard Plan or Enterprise Plan can add it for ¥200,000 per month (excluding tax) on top of their existing plan fee.

  ## Significance

  The launch reflects a broader shift among enterprise software vendors: as AI agents proliferate within organizations, centralized orchestration and audit infrastructure is becoming a distinct product category. By embedding Agent Orchestration into HULFT Square rather than offering it as a standalone product, Saison Technology positions the feature as an extension of data governance work that many enterprise customers are already performing through the platform.

  The service becomes available to customers on July 1, 2026.

  ## Saison Technology定於7月1日上線企業AI代理人管理平台

  Saison Technology於6月9日宣布，將在2026年7月1日正式推出「Agent Orchestration」平台。這套平台專為企業設計，讓組織得以透過單一介面，對全企業範疇內的多個AI代理人進行登錄、管理與追蹤。

  此產品的問世，回應了AI代理人部署規模擴大後浮現的治理缺口——隨著應用場景從個人使用延伸至組織層級，企業迫切需要一套機制，管控每個代理人可存取哪些資料、採用哪個大型語言模型（LLM），以及哪些員工有權使用。所謂LLM，即驅動代理人進行推論與回應的底層AI模型，例如OpenAI或Anthropic等廠商所提供的模型。

  ## 平台功能說明

  Agent Orchestration允許管理員登錄多個AI代理人，並針對各代理人分別設定資料存取權限、指定所使用的LLM，以及建立授權使用者名單。平台亦能依模型別追蹤Token用量——Token為LLM處理文字的計量單位，直接影響運算成本——並可將代理人的使用紀錄彙整成報表。

  Saison Technology開發本部產品管理部部長Aki Fukutomari表示，公司致力於打造一個在促進AI代理人應用的同時，兼顧管理與治理需求的平台。此產品的目標客群，是希望在團隊或全公司層級部署AI代理人的組織，而非僅將AI作為個人生產力工具的使用情境。

  ## 定價與銷售方式

  Agent Orchestration將作為Saison Technology現有資料整合平台HULFT Square的組成部分提供。在AI-Start Pack方案下，此功能作為標準配備內建其中，起價為每月68萬日圓（未含稅）。選用Standard Plan或Enterprise Plan的用戶，則可以每月加付20萬日圓（未含稅）的方式，將此功能附加至現有方案之上。

  ## 產業意義

  此次發布反映了企業軟體廠商的整體趨勢轉變：隨著AI代理人在組織內部的數量持續增加，集中式統籌管理與稽核基礎架構正逐漸成為一個獨立的產品類別。Saison Technology選擇將Agent Orchestration整合進HULFT Square，而非作為獨立產品推出，此舉將該功能定位為資料治理工作的延伸——而資料治理本就是眾多企業客戶透過這套平台日常執行的核心業務。

  本服務將於2026年7月1日起正式開放客戶使用。
