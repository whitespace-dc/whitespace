body_en: |
  Foxconn subsidiaries used the second day of Hon Hai Technology Day 2026 (HHTD 2026) to detail the operational constraints facing AI data center builders, with Visionbay.ai CEO Yao Yan-zong citing power architecture and supply chain coordination as the defining challenges, while the company's existing 5-megawatt NVIDIA HGX B300 cluster in Taiwan is running at above 90 percent commercial utilization.

  ## Power architecture and supply chain timing

  Yao, speaking at the Taiwan AI Robot Industry Development Conference co-hosted at HHTD 2026, said a single AI data center (AIDC) investment can reach hundreds of millions to billions of dollars, making the synchronization of facility construction with chip and component delivery schedules critical to deploying at maximum speed and efficiency.

  Power consumption has become an equally central constraint, Yao said. Citing NVIDIA's product roadmap, he noted that per-rack power draw has increased substantially, leading Visionbay.ai to adopt 800V HVDC — high-voltage direct current — architecture. The shift reduces energy lost as electricity travels from the grid to the graphics processing units (GPUs) housed in the racks. Foxconn's recent AIDC deployments have standardized on liquid cooling to further improve overall power usage efficiency, Yao added.

  ## Visionbay.ai's 5MW cluster above 90% commercial utilization

  Visionbay.ai, Foxconn's subsidiary dedicated to supercomputing center and cloud computing platform operations, announced in late July that it had brought online Taiwan's largest NVIDIA HGX B300 AI cluster. The facility operates at 5 megawatts (MW) and has signed its first batch of long-term customer lease agreements. Visionbay.ai said the cluster is in commercial operation at above 90 percent utilization.

  Yao said Taiwan's industry has maintained a neutral position as a contract manufacturer serving global customers, adding that establishing sovereign AI infrastructure within Taiwan is an option worth future consideration.

  ## FIT and FII present integrated AI infrastructure hardware

  Also at HHTD 2026, Industrial Foxconn (FII) chairman Zheng Hong-meng and Foxconn Interconnect Technology (FIT) chairman Lu Song-qing appeared on stage together to discuss AI infrastructure development trends. The two executives described a division of roles: FII focuses on AI computing systems while FIT concentrates on high-speed interconnects and key components, with both working in combination to address customer demand for next-generation AI infrastructure.

  Lu described a shift in how customers engage with suppliers. Where customers once asked whether a given specification was achievable at all, they now want higher volumes delivered faster — this afternoon if possible, he said. Zheng added that some customers act as though they want shipments to have arrived the previous day.

  ## FIT's four-technology display

  FIT's exhibition area, branded "Token Factory," demonstrated four categories of technology the company presents as foundational to next-generation AI data centers:

  - **High-speed interconnects:** High-density midplane connectors and CHIPLINK 448G for fast data transfer within servers
  - **Optical communications:** 800G and 1.6T optical modules, co-packaged optics (CPO) designs, and an ELSFP (external light source for pluggable modules) solution for managing heat and maintenance in high-density optical configurations
  - **Power supply:** High-power delivery solutions using hard copper busbars in place of conventional cabling, improving space utilization and power efficiency within racks
  - **Liquid cooling:** Quick-connect fittings, floating docking mechanisms, and leak-proof designs to maintain stable coolant flow to high-power components

  ## Smart mobility power management

  FIT also exhibited power management technology for the software-defined vehicle (SDV) market, co-showing with Foxconn's One Mobility unit an Energy Backbone PLUS (EB+) system. The system uses lightweight aluminum busbars and smart electronic fuses (E-Fuse) to simplify conventional wiring harness architecture and improve power distribution efficiency. High-voltage connectors and an Intelligent HV Safety system for high-current, high-voltage protection were also shown as part of the smart mobility display.

  ## Precision manufacturing for high-density components

  FIT displayed precision mold and component machining capabilities including CNC 3D precision machining and EDM (electrical discharge machining) processes. The company said these achieve tolerances described as fine as a human hair, enabling fabrication of high-speed optical modules, high-density chip sockets, high-speed high-frequency backplane connectors, and micro-optical components.

  ## Industry implications

  The HHTD 2026 presentations frame Foxconn's approach to the AI infrastructure buildout as one of vertical integration, spanning precision-machined components through rack-level power and cooling systems to full cluster operations. For AIDC operators, the standardized adoption of 800V HVDC and liquid cooling — presented by Visionbay.ai as operational defaults in current deployments rather than experimental configurations — points to a shift in how power efficiency is being addressed at the infrastructure level.

  Visionbay.ai's 5MW NVIDIA HGX B300 cluster, running at above 90 percent commercial utilization with long-term leases in place, represents the operating baseline from which the company is making the case for that integrated strategy.
body_zh: |
  鴻海旗下子公司利用2026年鴻海科技日（HHTD 2026）第二天的場合，詳細說明AI資料中心（AIDC）建置者面臨的運營挑戰。Visionbay.ai執行長姚彥宗點名電力架構與供應鏈協調為最關鍵的制約因素，並透露公司在台灣的5百萬瓦（MW）輝達HGX B300叢集，商業使用率已超過九成。

  ## 電力架構與供應鏈時程

  姚彥宗於HHTD 2026聯合舉辦的「台灣AI機器人產業發展論壇」上表示，單一AIDC的投資金額動輒數億至數十億美元，因此廠房建設與晶片及零組件的交貨時程能否緊密同步，是以最快速度、最高效率完成部署的關鍵所在。

  電力消耗已成為同等重要的核心制約。姚彥宗引用輝達的產品藍圖指出，每機架的功耗已大幅攀升，促使Visionbay.ai採用800伏特高壓直流電（HVDC）架構。此一轉變能夠減少電力從電網傳輸至機架內繪圖處理器（GPU）過程中的損耗。他補充指出，鴻海近期的AIDC部署已全面標準化採用液冷系統，以進一步改善整體電力使用效率（PUE）。

  ## Visionbay.ai的5MW叢集商業使用率超過九成

  Visionbay.ai是鴻海旗下專責超級運算中心與雲端運算平台營運的子公司，於今年七月下旬宣布正式上線台灣規模最大的輝達HGX B300 AI叢集。該設施運算功耗達5百萬瓦（MW），並已簽署首批客戶長期租用合約。Visionbay.ai表示，叢集目前處於商業運營狀態，使用率超過九成。

  姚彥宗指出，台灣產業作為服務全球客戶的代工製造商，一向維持中立立場，並補充表示，在台灣境內建立主權AI基礎設施，是未來值得深入考量的選項。

  ## 工業富聯與FIT聯合展示整合AI基礎設施硬體

  同樣在HHTD 2026現場，工業富聯（FII）董事長鄭弘孟與鴻騰精密（FIT）董事長盧松青同台，共同探討AI基礎設施的發展趨勢。兩位高管描述了雙方明確的分工定位：FII聚焦於AI運算系統，FIT則專注於高速互連與關鍵零組件，雙方攜手合作，共同因應客戶對次世代AI基礎設施的需求。

  盧松青描述了客戶與供應商互動方式的轉變。過去，客戶問的往往是某項規格「做不做得到」，如今則是要求更大量、更快速交貨——「最好今天下午就到」。鄭弘孟補充說，有些客戶的態度，彷彿貨物「昨天就應該已經送達」。

  ## FIT四大技術展示

  FIT的展示區以「Token Factory」為主題品牌，呈現該公司視為次世代AI資料中心基礎的四大技術類別：

  - **高速互連：** 高密度中板連接器，以及用於伺服器內部快速資料傳輸的CHIPLINK 448G
  - **光通訊：** 800G與1.6T光學模組、共封裝光學（CPO）設計，以及用於高密度光學配置中管理散熱與維護作業的ELSFP（外部光源可插拔模組）解決方案
  - **電源供應：** 以硬銅匯流排取代傳統纜線的大功率輸送解決方案，改善機架內的空間利用率與電力效率
  - **液冷：** 快速接頭、浮動對接機構，以及防漏設計，確保高功耗零組件獲得穩定的冷卻液流量

  ## 智慧移動電源管理

  FIT亦展出了針對軟體定義車輛（SDV）市場的電源管理技術，與鴻海旗下One Mobility事業部共同展示「Energy Backbone PLUS（EB+）」系統。該系統採用輕量化鋁製匯流排與智慧電子保險絲（E-Fuse），簡化傳統線束架構，並提升電力分配效率。展示內容另包含高壓連接器，以及針對大電流、高電壓防護的「Intelligent HV Safety」智慧高壓安全系統，同列本次智慧移動展示項目。

  ## 高密度零組件精密製造

  FIT展示了精密模具與零組件加工能力，包括CNC三維精密加工與放電加工（EDM）製程。公司表示，上述製程可達到「細如人髮」等級的公差，從而實現高速光學模組、高密度晶片插座、高速高頻背板連接器，以及微型光學元件的精密製造。

  ## 產業意涵

  HHTD 2026的系列發表，呈現出鴻海在AI基礎設施建置浪潮中的垂直整合策略——從精密加工零組件，涵蓋至機架層級的電力與冷卻系統，再延伸至完整叢集的運營管理。對AIDC業者而言，Visionbay.ai呈現的800伏特HVDC與液冷系統，並非實驗性配置，而是現有部署的標準預設選項，這標誌著基礎設施層面因應電力效率問題的方式，正在發生根本性的轉變。

  Visionbay.ai的5MW輝達HGX B300叢集，在長期租約簽署到位的情況下，商業使用率超過九成，成為該公司為整合策略背書的運營基準。
