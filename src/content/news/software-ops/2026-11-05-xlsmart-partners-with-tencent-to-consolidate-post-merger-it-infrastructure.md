body_en: |
  Indonesian telecommunications operator XLSmart (IDX: EXCL) has entered a partnership with Tencent to consolidate and modernize its information technology infrastructure following the merger of XL Axiata and Smartfren, with the company targeting 75% of the consolidation complete by end of 2026 and the remainder finished by mid-2027.

  Director and Chief Information Technology Officer Yessie D. Yosetya disclosed the arrangement on October 29 at the Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Summit (GDES) 2026 in Shenzhen, China. The partnership began at the end of 2025, Yosetya said, and was driven by the need to evaluate and rationalize the separate technology stacks the two predecessor companies had operated independently.

  ## Divergent infrastructure starting points

  The two companies approached infrastructure differently before the merger. One had already migrated a significant share of workloads to cloud services; the other remained largely dependent on on-premise hardware. The consolidation effort targets full migration to cloud across the combined entity, Yosetya said.

  "We then prepared for the future of XLSmart's infrastructure. So we looked for and moved everything to cloud," she told Bisnis.com on the sidelines of the summit.

  ## Scope of the consolidation

  Beyond compute and storage, the modernization covers the consolidation of artificial intelligence (AI) and data platforms that had previously run on separate organizational systems. The unified platform will serve three user groups: retail customers using XLSmart applications, business partners such as distribution outlets, and internal employees.

  Yosetya said infrastructure changes are designed to be transparent to end users, with the expected result being more reliable application performance and greater capacity to handle growing service demand. "From the user's side, it should be easier, smoother, more seamless. We also use the same platform to serve partners. And importantly, internal customers — employees — the way they work and so on," she said.

  ## Timeline and 5G

  XLSmart targets approximately 75% of IT consolidation complete by end of 2026, with remaining integration work scheduled for completion by mid-2027. Yosetya described finishing the foundational phase as a prerequisite before the company can materially accelerate service and application development.

  Fifth-generation (5G) mobile connectivity is expected to underpin the next phase of development. Yosetya said 5G would act as a driver for applications built on top of XLSmart's network, allowing those services to perform better given the higher-bandwidth, lower-latency connectivity the standard provides. "5G will be the driver for connectivity. So whatever applications are later built on top of it will be far easier to build, or will be served better, with the connectivity we have," she said.

  ## What comes next

  Completing the foundational consolidation unlocks faster development cycles. Yosetya framed the current phase as building the base from which innovation can be accelerated, noting that a fragmented infrastructure had previously constrained how quickly the company could build and deploy new services. Once the foundation is in place, she said, engineering capacity can shift from integration work to competitive product development.

  XLSmart expects to complete the remaining IT integration by mid-2027.

  ## XLSmart攜手騰訊整合合併後IT基礎架構

  印尼電信業者XLSmart（印尼證交所代號：EXCL）已與騰訊展開合作，針對XL Axiata與Smartfren合併後所繼承的資訊科技基礎架構進行整合與現代化升級。公司設定的目標是在2026年底前完成75%的整合工作，其餘部分則於2027年中前全部到位。

  董事兼資訊科技長Yessie D. Yosetya於10月29日出席在中國深圳舉辦的「騰訊全球數位生態峰會（GDES）2026」時，公開揭露了上述合作安排。Yosetya表示，雙方合作自2025年底正式啟動，起因在於必須評估並梳理兩家前身公司各自獨立運作所累積的不同技術架構。

  ## 兩家公司截然不同的基礎架構起點

  合併前，兩家公司對待基礎架構的方式大相徑庭。其中一家已將相當比例的工作負載遷移至雲端服務，另一家則仍高度依賴本地端硬體。Yosetya表示，本次整合計畫的目標是將合併後實體的所有業務全面遷移上雲。

  她在峰會場邊接受《Bisnis.com》採訪時表示：「我們接著為XLSmart的基礎架構籌備未來。所以我們找尋方向，並將一切遷移至雲端。」

  ## 整合範疇

  除了運算與儲存之外，此次現代化升級的範疇還涵蓋人工智慧（AI）與資料平台的整合——這些平台過去分別在兩個組織各自的系統上運作。整合後的統一平台將服務三類使用者族群：使用XLSmart應用程式的一般消費者、經銷通路等商業夥伴，以及公司內部員工。

  Yosetya表示，基礎架構層面的改變在設計上對終端使用者而言應是透明無感的，預期成效是應用程式效能更加穩定，並具備更大容量以因應持續成長的服務需求。她說：「從使用者的角度來看，操作應該更簡便、更流暢、更無縫。我們也透過同一平台來服務合作夥伴。更重要的是內部客戶——也就是員工——包括他們的工作方式等各個面向。」

  ## 時程規劃與5G布局

  XLSmart設定的目標是在2026年底前完成約75%的IT整合，其餘整合工作預計於2027年中完成。Yosetya將完成這一基礎奠定階段描述為前提條件，唯有如此，公司才能大幅加速後續的服務與應用程式開發。

  第五代（5G）行動網路預計將成為下一階段發展的核心支柱。Yosetya表示，5G將作為連結驅動力，帶動建構於XLSmart網路之上的各類應用程式，使這些服務得以藉助5G提供的高頻寬、低延遲連線能力，發揮更佳的效能。她說：「5G將成為連結的驅動力。因此，日後在此之上開發的任何應用程式，都能借助我們所擁有的連結條件，更輕鬆地打造，或提供更出色的服務。」

  ## 後續展望

  完成基礎整合將為更快速的開發週期打開大門。Yosetya將現階段定位為奠基工程，強調唯有夯實基礎，才能加速創新步伐；她也指出，分散破碎的基礎架構過去限制了公司建構與部署新服務的速度。她表示，一旦基礎到位，工程能量便能從整合工作轉向具競爭力的產品開發。

  XLSmart預計於2027年中完成其餘IT整合工程。
