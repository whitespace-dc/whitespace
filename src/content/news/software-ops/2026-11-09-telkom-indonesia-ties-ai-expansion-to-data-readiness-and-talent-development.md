body_en: |
  Telkom Indonesia's enterprise director said the state-owned telecoms operator is accelerating AI adoption by focusing on two foundational requirements: organizing data for platform use and building the technical skills of its workforce, positioning the company's integrated digital services portfolio as the delivery vehicle for both.

  Veranita Yosephine, Director of Enterprise and Business Service at PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero) Tbk, made the remarks at the Danantara Technology Innovation Investment Forum (DTIIF) 2026 at the Fairmont Jakarta on October 27, in a panel session titled "From AI Experimentation to Trusted Enterprise Adoption."

  "To expand AI adoption, there are two important aspects that need attention: data foundation readiness and talent and capability development," Veranita said, according to Bisnis.com. "Well-organized data can support optimal use of AI platforms, while improving human resource skills is an important part of preparing organizations for technological developments."

  ## From experiment to infrastructure

  The session addressed a shift in how Indonesian enterprises are approaching AI — no longer treating it as an experimental tool but integrating it into core business infrastructure. Veranita said effective deployment requires an ecosystem spanning connectivity, computing capacity, data centers, and digital security and trust systems.

  Telkom is offering these capabilities through its Telkom Solution subsidiary, which bundles services under five categories: Connectivity+, AI, Data Center, Cloud Service, and Cyber Security. The company said this integrated stack is designed to help enterprises in multiple industries embed AI into operational processes in a measured way.

  ## Cybersecurity as a prerequisite

  Vera noted that cybersecurity is not an add-on but a precondition for responsible AI deployment in enterprise settings. System reliability and protection from digital risks are necessary, she said, to build the trust required for AI to function in business environments where it is increasingly embedded in core processes.

  The panel brought together perspectives from multiple vendors and security firms active in the Indonesian enterprise market. The session was moderated by Hyeonmi Kim of Boston Consulting Group (BCG) and included Andreas Diantoro, President Director of Salesforce Indonesia; Zong Fu Chua, Head of International Business and Commercial at Ensign InfoSecurity; and Patrick Dannacher, Chief Executive Officer of ITSEC Indonesia.

  ## Significance for Indonesia's enterprise AI market

  The forum appearance signals that Telkom is actively positioning itself as a full-stack AI infrastructure partner for Indonesian corporates at a moment when demand is moving from pilots to production deployment. By framing data organization and talent development as the two critical barriers to enterprise AI adoption, the company is pointing to service lines — managed data platforms, training programs, and security services — where it expects to compete.

  Telkom said infrastructure support, data management, security, and talent development together form the basis for helping companies optimize AI use while driving competitiveness and sustainability in their operations.
body_zh: |
  印尼國營電信業者印尼電信（Telkom Indonesia）企業事業部主管表示，公司正在加速 AI 導入，並聚焦兩項基礎條件：整理資料以供平台使用，以及培育員工技術能力，並以公司整合數位服務組合作為推動兩者的執行載體。

  PT Telkom Indonesia（Persero）Tbk 企業暨商業服務董事 Veranita Yosephine，於 10 月 27 日在雅加達費爾蒙酒店（Fairmont Jakarta）舉辦的 2026 年 Danantara 科技創新投資論壇（DTIIF）上發表上述談話，參與題為「從 AI 實驗到受信賴的企業導入」的座談會。

  Veranita 引述 Bisnis.com 報導表示：「要擴大 AI 的採用，有兩個重要面向需要關注：資料基礎的整備，以及人才與能力的培養。整理有序的資料能夠支持 AI 平台的最佳化運用，而提升人力資源技能，則是協助組織為技術發展做好準備的重要一環。」

  ## 從實驗走向基礎建設

  此次座談會探討的核心，正是印尼企業看待 AI 的視角正在經歷的轉變——不再將其視為實驗性工具，而是將其整合進核心業務基礎建設。Veranita 表示，有效的部署需要一個涵蓋連網能力、運算容量、資料中心，以及數位安全與信任體系的完整生態系。

  印尼電信透過旗下子公司 Telkom Solution 提供這些能力，將服務整合為五大類別：Connectivity+（連網服務）、AI、資料中心、雲端服務，以及資安。該公司表示，這套整合技術堆疊旨在協助多個產業的企業客戶，以穩健有序的方式將 AI 嵌入營運流程。

  ## 資安是不可或缺的前提

  Veranita 指出，在企業環境中負責任地部署 AI，資安並非附加選項，而是必要的先決條件。她表示，系統穩定性與數位風險防護是建立信任的必要基礎，而這種信任，正是 AI 在日益深入核心業務流程的商業環境中得以有效運作的關鍵所在。

  此次座談會匯聚了多家活躍於印尼企業市場的廠商與資安業者觀點，由波士頓顧問集團（BCG）的 Hyeonmi Kim 擔任主持人，與談者包括：Salesforce 印尼總裁董事 Andreas Diantoro、Ensign InfoSecurity 國際業務與商務部門主管 Zong Fu Chua，以及 ITSEC 印尼執行長 Patrick Dannacher。

  ## 對印尼企業 AI 市場的意義

  此次論壇亮相，顯示印尼電信正積極將自身定位為印尼企業客戶的全方位 AI 基礎建設夥伴，恰逢市場需求正從概念驗證轉向正式生產部署的關鍵時刻。公司以資料整備與人才培育作為企業 AI 導入的兩大關鍵障礙，實際上也指向其預期競爭的服務範疇——包括託管資料平台、培訓課程與資安服務。

  印尼電信表示，基礎建設支援、資料管理、資安，以及人才培育，共同構成協助企業優化 AI 應用的根本基礎，同時也是推動企業在營運上提升競爭力與永續發展的核心所在。
