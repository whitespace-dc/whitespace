body_en: |
  Hewlett Packard Enterprise announced at its Discover Las Vegas 2026 conference an expansion of its GreenLake cloud platform and Morpheus Software, adding agentic artificial intelligence capabilities designed to give enterprise IT teams unified control over hybrid cloud, virtual machines, and AI infrastructure from a single operational framework.

  The announcements target enterprises contending with rising virtualization costs, new AI workload demands, and fragmented tooling across on-premises and cloud environments. Fidelma Russo, executive vice president and chief technology officer of HPE Hybrid Cloud, said enterprises expanding AI at scale need simpler ways to govern AI infrastructure and modernize hybrid operations without creating fragmentation or unpredictable costs. The new GreenLake capabilities, she said, provide a verified, unified approach to agentic hybrid operations and lay the groundwork for future autonomous operations.

  ## GreenLake Intelligence and the OpsRamp Operations Copilot

  At the center of the announcement is GreenLake Intelligence, described by HPE as an agentic AI framework for hybrid cloud and AI operations. The platform introduces a centralized Agent Registry — a catalog for registering and coordinating AI agents — along with intelligent planning and orchestration tools and secure governance controls.

  HPE highlighted two specific additions within GreenLake Intelligence. The first is the HPE OpsRamp Operations Copilot, a tool that provides observability into AI agents and large language models (LLMs). It is designed to let operators monitor AI resource consumption, govern token-based usage — tokens being the unit of computation charged by LLM services — and track the operational cost of agents and workloads running across multi-vendor AI factories. The Copilot uses full-stack telemetry correlation and agentic root cause analysis to proactively identify potential problems.

  The second addition is a partnership with ServiceNow. HPE said OpsRamp Operations Copilot will integrate GreenLake Intelligence with ServiceNow's autonomous AI work teams, creating what the two companies described as a single source of truth for agentic IT operations — a unified data layer spanning full-stack observability of AI infrastructure through to end-to-end autonomous service delivery.

  ## Morpheus Software: Virtualization Platform With Six New Capabilities

  HPE Morpheus Software, positioned as an enterprise-grade alternative virtualization platform, received six new features. Morpheus allows organizations to provision and manage virtual machines (VMs) — software-based servers — and container workloads under a per-slot subscription model, meaning customers pay based on processor socket count rather than per individual VM.

  The first addition is the HPE Morpheus Orchestration Copilot, now part of GreenLake Intelligence, which automates the end-to-end provisioning and orchestration of infrastructure and workloads. HPE said the tool is built on a bring-your-own-model architecture, letting enterprises use their preferred AI tools while built-in governance guardrails enforce policy. HPE said the Copilot helps resource-constrained teams complete provisioning tasks faster by eliminating multi-step, multi-tool manual processes that drive up cost and introduce error.

  The second addition is HPE Morpheus Central, a new management layer that provides centralized visibility, governance, and control across multiple separate Morpheus deployments — relevant for enterprises running Morpheus at more than one site or business unit.

  Third, software-defined networking (SDN) — the practice of managing network behavior through software rather than hardware configuration — has reached general availability in Morpheus. HPE said the SDN layer delivers zero-trust security (a model that verifies every access request regardless of network location), multi-tenant support, consistent policy enforcement, and VXLAN overlay networking (a technology that creates virtual network segments over existing physical infrastructure). HPE said provisioning time is reduced by up to 60 percent.

  Fourth, Morpheus now integrates with HPE Juniper Apstra for intent-based, closed-loop network automation. Apstra continuously validates real-time network state against the desired configuration, eliminates configuration drift — the gradual divergence of actual settings from stated policy — and automates policy enforcement at scale.

  Fifth, stretched cluster support has reached general availability. A stretched cluster extends a single computing cluster across two active sites, using synchronous replication and automatic failover to provide metro-grade resiliency, meaning the system can survive the failure of one entire site within a metropolitan distance.

  Sixth, HPE Zerto Software now enables live migration of workloads from VMware environments to HPE virtual machines, with continuous data protection throughout the migration process.

  ## HPE and Citrix Expand Private Cloud Partnership

  HPE and Citrix announced plans to deepen their strategic collaboration on private cloud and virtualization. The initial focus is delivering Citrix Desktop-as-a-Service (DaaS) — a cloud-delivered virtual desktop offering — on the GreenLake platform for customers that require on-premises or sovereign deployments, the latter referring to infrastructure kept within a specific jurisdiction to satisfy data residency or regulatory requirements.

  The two companies also plan to integrate Citrix DaaS and Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops into HPE Morpheus Software VM Essentials, using Morpheus as the core virtualization platform. HPE CloudOps would then provide unified orchestration, automation, and observability across GreenLake hybrid deployments.

  ## What the Announcements Mean for IT Operations

  The combined set of announcements reflects HPE's effort to position GreenLake as a single operational control plane for enterprises managing AI workloads alongside conventional virtualized infrastructure — an architecture where governance, observability, and provisioning flow through one platform rather than separate tools.

  The ServiceNow integration extends that model into IT service management, connecting infrastructure-level observability directly to the service delivery workflows that act on it. The six Morpheus additions address specific operational gaps — network provisioning, multi-site resiliency, cross-deployment visibility, and workload migration — that would otherwise require separate products.

  The Citrix DaaS integration with Morpheus VM Essentials and GreenLake hybrid deployment support through CloudOps represents the first planned phase of the expanded Citrix partnership, according to HPE.

  惠普企業（HPE）於2026年Discover拉斯維加斯大會上宣布，將擴展旗下GreenLake雲端平台與Morpheus軟體，並加入代理式人工智慧（Agentic AI）功能，旨在讓企業IT團隊能透過單一運維架構，統一掌控混合雲、虛擬機器與AI基礎架構。

  此次發表針對正面臨虛擬化成本攀升、新AI工作負載需求，以及本地部署與雲端環境工具分散等挑戰的企業。HPE混合雲執行副總裁兼技術長Fidelma Russo表示，正在大規模擴展AI的企業需要更簡便的方式來治理AI基礎架構、現代化混合運維，同時避免碎片化與難以預測的成本。她指出，全新GreenLake功能提供了一套經過驗證、統一的代理式混合運維方法，並為未來的自主運維奠定基礎。

  ## GreenLake Intelligence與OpsRamp Operations Copilot

  此次發表的核心是GreenLake Intelligence——HPE將其定位為混合雲與AI運維的代理式AI框架。該平台引入了集中式代理人登錄庫（Agent Registry），作為登錄與協調AI代理人的目錄，並配備智慧型規劃與協作工具，以及安全治理控管機制。

  HPE在GreenLake Intelligence中重點介紹了兩項新增功能。第一項是HPE OpsRamp Operations Copilot，這是一款提供AI代理人與大型語言模型（LLM）可觀測性的工具，讓運維人員能監控AI資源消耗、治理以Token為計量單位的使用量（Token為LLM服務的運算計費單位），並追蹤在多廠商AI工廠中運行的代理人與工作負載的運維成本。此Copilot採用全堆疊遙測關聯與代理式根本原因分析，主動識別潛在問題。

  第二項新功能是與ServiceNow的合作夥伴關係。HPE表示，OpsRamp Operations Copilot將把GreenLake Intelligence與ServiceNow的自主AI工作團隊整合，兩家公司將此描述為代理式IT運維的「單一可信來源」——一個統一的資料層，涵蓋從AI基礎架構的全堆疊可觀測性，延伸至端對端自主服務交付。

  ## Morpheus軟體：虛擬化平台新增六大功能

  HPE Morpheus軟體定位為企業級替代虛擬化平台，此次新增六項功能。Morpheus讓組織能以每插槽訂閱模式部署及管理虛擬機器（VM，即軟體模擬伺服器）與容器工作負載，亦即客戶依處理器插槽數量付費，而非按個別VM計費。

  第一項新功能是HPE Morpheus Orchestration Copilot，現已納入GreenLake Intelligence，可自動化端對端的基礎架構與工作負載部署及協作。HPE表示，此工具建立在「自帶模型」（BYOM）架構之上，讓企業可使用其偏好的AI工具，同時透過內建的治理護欄強制執行政策。HPE指出，此Copilot能協助資源有限的團隊消除多步驟、多工具的手動流程，加快部署任務，並降低成本與人為錯誤。

  第二項新功能是HPE Morpheus Central，一個全新的管理層，可跨多個獨立的Morpheus部署提供集中式可見度、治理與控管——適用於在多個據點或事業單位運行Morpheus的企業。

  第三項是軟體定義網路（SDN）——即透過軟體而非硬體設定來管理網路行為的做法——現已在Morpheus中正式推出（GA）。HPE表示，此SDN層提供零信任安全性（一種無論網路位置為何均需驗證每次存取請求的模型）、多租戶支援、一致的政策強制執行，以及VXLAN覆疊網路（一種在現有實體基礎架構上建立虛擬網路區段的技術）。HPE指出，部署時間最多可縮短60%。

  第四項是Morpheus現已與HPE Juniper Apstra整合，實現意圖式、閉環網路自動化。Apstra持續驗證即時網路狀態是否符合預期設定，消除設定漂移（即實際設定與既定政策逐漸偏離的現象），並大規模自動執行政策。

  第五項是延伸叢集（Stretched Cluster）支援正式推出。延伸叢集可將單一運算叢集跨兩個主動站台延伸，透過同步複製與自動容錯移轉提供都會級韌性，意即系統可在都會距離範圍內承受單一完整站台的故障。

  第六項是HPE Zerto軟體現已能將工作負載從VMware環境即時遷移至HPE虛擬機器，並在整個遷移過程中持續提供資料保護。

  ## HPE與Citrix深化私有雲合作夥伴關係

  HPE與Citrix宣布計劃深化在私有雲與虛擬化領域的策略合作。初期重點是在GreenLake平台上交付Citrix桌面即服務（DaaS）——一種透過雲端交付的虛擬桌面方案，適用於需要本地部署或主權部署的客戶；後者係指將基礎架構保存於特定司法管轄區，以符合資料駐留或法規要求。

  兩家公司亦計劃將Citrix DaaS與Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops整合至HPE Morpheus軟體VM Essentials，以Morpheus作為核心虛擬化平台。HPE CloudOps則將在GreenLake混合部署中提供統一的協作、自動化與可觀測性。

  ## 這些發表對IT運維的意義

  上述整套發表反映出HPE的策略意圖：將GreenLake定位為企業單一運維控制平面，讓企業在同一平台上管理AI工作負載與傳統虛擬化基礎架構——治理、可觀測性與部署均透過一個平台流通，而非分散於各個獨立工具。

  ServiceNow的整合將此模式延伸至IT服務管理，直接將基礎架構層面的可觀測性與採取行動的服務交付工作流程串聯起來。Morpheus六項新功能則針對特定的運維缺口——網路部署、多站台韌性、跨部署可見度及工作負載遷移——否則這些缺口將需要各自獨立的產品填補。

  根據HPE說法，Citrix DaaS與Morpheus VM Essentials的整合，以及透過CloudOps提供的GreenLake混合部署支援，代表此次擴大Citrix合作夥伴關係的第一個計劃階段。
