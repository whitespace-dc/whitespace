  PT Metrodata Electronics Tbk. (MTDL), an Indonesian IT distribution and solutions company, said Tuesday it expects corporate spending on artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity to lift full-year 2026 net profit and revenue by approximately 10%, after its distribution business carried the bulk of first-half growth.

  President Director Susanto Djaja said corporations that had been deferring IT investment are now under increasing pressure to commit. "Companies that were mostly in wait-and-see mode now have no choice but to realize their IT investments," Susanto said on August 11, 2026. "They need investment for data and AI, for cybersecurity."

  ## First-half performance and segment split

  MTDL reported first-half 2026 revenue growth of 16.7% year-on-year (compared with the same period a year earlier). The company's distribution segment, which sells PCs, laptops, smartphones, telecommunications equipment, and commercial products, grew more than 20%. The Business Solutions and Consulting segment, which covers corporate IT implementation projects, grew about 3% in the same period.

  PC, laptop, and smartphone product categories were the largest contributors to distribution revenue growth, according to the company. Susanto said the gains were not driven purely by volume: increases in technology product prices also contributed to higher sales value. In the smartphone category specifically, the company said consumers are shifting toward value-for-money devices — products at accessible price points that offer strong specifications and features.

  ## H2 strategy pivots to solutions business

  MTDL said it plans to shift the balance of its growth drivers in the second half of 2026. Distribution segment growth is expected to moderate in H2, while the company intends to pursue larger gains in Business Solutions and Consulting by accelerating deal closure and ensuring timely delivery and implementation of contracted projects.

  "In H2 2026, we are working to target corporate IT spending, including AI and security spending and others, so that we can close the deal," Susanto said. The company said it will focus specifically on projects related to AI, data, cybersecurity, and other IT solutions.

  ## Cybersecurity urgency shaping corporate demand

  Susanto said cybersecurity risk is a factor pushing corporations to end prolonged deferrals. Delaying security-related investment creates growing exposure, he argued, particularly as cybersecurity requirements intensify. He said many companies had planned IT budget outlays that they can no longer justify postponing.

  ## Outlook

  MTDL said it retains high confidence in its distribution business given the first-half performance, while identifying the solutions and consulting segment as the greater source of incremental growth opportunity in the second half. The company targets approximately 10% growth in both revenue and net profit by year-end 2026, with the Business Solutions and Consulting segment expected to account for a larger share of that growth than it did in the first half.

  ## Metrodata Electronics 看好企業AI與資安支出，全年目標雙位數成長

  PT Metrodata Electronics Tbk.（MTDL）週二表示，企業在人工智慧（AI）與資安領域的資本支出持續擴張，預計將推動2026年全年淨利與營收各成長約10%。上半年表現主要由通路業務挑大樑，帶動整體成長。

  執行長蘇珊托·賈亞（Susanto Djaja）指出，過去一直採觀望態度的企業，如今面臨愈來愈大的壓力，不得不落實IT投資計畫。他於2026年8月11日表示：「大多數企業過去都還在觀望，但現在別無選擇，必須實現IT投資。他們需要投入資料與AI，也需要投入資安。」

  ## 上半年業績與業務結構

  MTDL公布，2026年上半年營收較去年同期成長16.7%。負責銷售個人電腦、筆記型電腦、智慧型手機、電信設備及商用產品的通路業務部門，同期成長逾20%；負責企業IT導入專案的企業解決方案暨顧問業務部門，同期則成長約3%。

  就通路業務的成長驅動力而言，個人電腦、筆記型電腦及智慧型手機等產品類別貢獻最大。蘇珊托表示，此次成長並非純粹來自出貨量的增加，科技產品售價的上漲同樣推升了整體銷售金額。在智慧型手機類別方面，公司指出消費者正逐漸轉向「物超所值」型裝置——也就是售價親民、但規格與功能表現出色的產品。

  ## 下半年策略轉向解決方案業務

  MTDL表示，計畫在2026年下半年調整成長動能的重心。通路業務的成長步調預計將在下半年趨緩，公司將轉而積極爭取企業解決方案暨顧問業務的更大幅度成長，目標是加速成交、確保已簽約專案能如期交付並順利導入。

  蘇珊托表示：「2026年下半年，我們鎖定企業IT支出，包括AI、資安及其他相關支出，致力促成成交。」公司表示，將特別聚焦AI、資料、資安及其他IT解決方案相關專案。

  ## 資安風險加速企業決策

  蘇珊托指出，資安風險是促使企業終止長期觀望的重要因素之一。他認為，一再推遲資安相關投資，只會讓企業的曝險程度不斷攀升，尤其是在資安合規要求日益嚴格的當下。他表示，許多企業早已規劃好IT預算支出項目，卻一拖再拖，如今已難以繼續延後。

  ## 展望

  MTDL表示，基於上半年的亮麗表現，公司對通路業務的前景仍抱持高度信心，同時也將解決方案暨顧問業務視為下半年增量成長機會最大的來源。公司目標是在2026年底前實現營收與淨利各約10%的成長，且企業解決方案暨顧問業務對整體成長的貢獻比重，預計將高於上半年。
