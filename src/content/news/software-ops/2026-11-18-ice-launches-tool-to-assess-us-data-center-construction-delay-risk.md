body_en: |
  Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), the parent company of the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), launched a tool on Tuesday that estimates the risk of US data-center projects being delayed or halted, as community opposition and political scrutiny threaten the rapid infrastructure expansion driven by artificial intelligence demand.

  ## What the tool does

  The tool evaluates permitting records, electricity and water costs, local economic conditions, and mortgage-market data across more than 3,100 US counties. ICE said its disruption model, trained on thousands of permitting and project outcomes, achieved 77% accuracy.

  "Data center capital is moving faster than local permitting and community consensus and that gap is now a material financial risk," said Larry Lawrence, head of ICE's Geospatial Intelligence team.

  ## Political backdrop

  ICE launched the tool following US midterm elections in which Democrats gained control of both chambers of Congress, a development the company said could strengthen legislative efforts to curb data-center expansion. Democratic concerns center on power consumption, water usage, and pollution from large-scale facilities.

  Democratic lawmakers, including Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, have proposed pausing new data-center construction and expansions until Congress adopts safeguards covering utility costs, environmental damage, and community oversight.

  ## Significance for the sector

  The tool gives investors, developers, and project planners a county-level view of construction disruption risk across the United States. With proposed legislation in Congress that could restrict new builds, the gap between capital deployment timelines and local permitting or community consensus — which Lawrence described as a material financial risk — has become a factor that project finance decisions must now account for.
body_zh: |
  洲際交易所（ICE）——紐約證券交易所（NYSE）的母公司——週二推出一款工具，可估算美國資料中心專案遭延誤或喊停的機率。隨著社區反對聲浪與政治審視持續升溫，由人工智慧需求所帶動的基礎設施快速擴張，正面臨愈來愈大的阻力。

  ## 工具功能

  這套工具整合許可記錄、電力與用水成本、地方經濟狀況，以及全美逾3,100個郡的房貸市場資料進行綜合評估。ICE表示，其干擾預測模型以數千筆許可及專案結果為訓練資料，準確率達77%。

  ICE地理空間情報團隊負責人拉里・勞倫斯（Larry Lawrence）表示：「資料中心資本的流動速度已遠超地方許可程序與社區共識的腳步，這道落差如今已成為實質的財務風險。」

  ## 政治背景

  ICE推出這項工具的時間點，恰在美國期中選舉落幕之後——本次選舉由民主黨取得國會參眾兩院多數。ICE指出，此一發展可能強化立法限制資料中心擴張的力道。民主黨人士關切的焦點，集中在大型設施的電力消耗、用水量及污染問題。

  包括眾議員亞歷山德里亞・奧卡西奧-科爾特斯（Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez）在內的民主黨籍議員，已提案要求暫停新建資料中心及現有設施的擴建，直至國會通過涵蓋公用事業成本、環境損害與社區監督等面向的防護措施為止。

  ## 產業意涵

  這套工具為投資人、開發商及專案規劃人員，提供全美以郡為單位的建設干擾風險視圖。面對國會中可能限制新建案的立法提案，資本部署時程與地方許可程序或社區共識之間的落差——勞倫斯將其形容為「實質的財務風險」——已成為專案融資決策不得不納入考量的關鍵因素。
