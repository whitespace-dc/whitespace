  ## Globe Telecom saves P200M using AI to predict network failures

  Globe Telecom has generated about P200 million in cost savings by using artificial intelligence to predict potential network failures up to three months in advance, allowing technicians to conduct maintenance before service disruptions occur, the Philippine telecommunications company said.

  Jennifer Jane G. Echevarria, vice-president and head of enterprise data and strategic services at Globe, disclosed the figure on the sidelines of the National Retail Conference and Expo. She said the company has recently deployed generative AI for network failure forecasting as part of a broader push to embed AI into its core operating systems rather than run it as a standalone application.

  ## Network reliability as the primary use case

  The predictive network tool represents an expansion of Globe's existing AI capabilities. Echevarria said the company has used machine learning for more than five years to forecast customer churn, behavior, and product uptake opportunities. The network reliability application extends that same model-driven approach to infrastructure, with the proactive maintenance program producing the P200 million in savings she cited.

  Echevarria described Globe's deployment philosophy as combining technology, people, and processes, beginning with a clear definition of the problem before determining whether AI is the appropriate solution. "My advice always is focus first on the problem you're trying to solve. And see if you need AI for that," she said.

  ## AI as a productivity multiplier in other operations

  Beyond network operations, Globe also uses AI to reduce the workload involved in administrative and research tasks, Echevarria said, describing it as a "force multiplier." In market research, AI assists with refining questionnaires and briefs before fieldwork begins, then processes incoming data to help prepare client-facing reports more quickly. Human analysts retain responsibility for the final insights, she said.

  Globe also provides clients with specialized bots after reports are released, enabling follow-up questions without requiring a human representative to respond in real time.

  ## Probabilistic limitations guide where AI is applied

  Echevarria cautioned that because AI is probabilistic rather than deterministic, it is better suited to forecasting than to functions requiring absolute precision. She cited payroll as an example of a domain where AI would be inappropriate. The company applies AI "surgically," she said, targeting use cases where predictive accuracy is sufficient and human oversight can close the remaining gap.

  The approach signals a pattern of incremental, problem-first AI adoption that Globe says has produced measurable financial returns in network operations, with further integration across business functions continuing.

  ## Globe Telecom導入AI預測網路故障　省下逾兩億披索

  菲律賓電信商Globe Telecom表示，藉由人工智慧預測潛在網路故障，最長可提前三個月預警，讓技術人員得以在服務中斷前完成維護作業，累計節省約兩億披索的成本。

  Globe企業資料暨策略服務部門副總裁暨負責人Jennifer Jane G. Echevarria，在「全國零售會議暨博覽會」場邊揭露了這項數字。她表示，公司近期已將生成式AI部署於網路故障預測，作為將AI全面嵌入核心營運系統——而非以獨立應用程式運作——的整體推進計畫之一環。

  ## 網路可靠性是首要應用場景

  這套預測性網路工具，是Globe既有AI能力的延伸擴展。Echevarria指出，公司運用機器學習預測客戶流失、消費行為及產品採購機會，至今已有逾五年經驗。網路可靠性應用將同一套模型驅動方法延伸至基礎架構層面，而那套主動維護方案，正是她所提到節省兩億披索的來源。

  Echevarria將Globe的部署哲學描述為「科技、人才與流程三位一體」，並強調應先清楚定義問題，再判斷AI是否為合適的解方。她說：「我一向建議，先專注於你想解決的問題，再評估是否真的需要AI。」

  ## AI在其他業務中發揮生產力倍增效果

  除了網路營運之外，Globe也將AI用於減輕行政與研究類工作的負擔，Echevarria將其形容為「力量倍增器」。在市場研究方面，AI協助在田野調查開始前優化問卷與研究簡報，並在資料回收後加速處理，幫助更快速地準備對客戶提交的報告。不過，最終洞察判斷的責任仍由人類分析師承擔，她強調。

  Globe也在報告發布後，為客戶提供專屬的智慧機器人，讓客戶得以進一步提問，而無需真人客服即時回應。

  ## 機率性限制決定AI的應用邊界

  Echevarria提醒，由於AI的本質是機率性而非決定性的，因此更適合用於預測，而非需要絕對精確度的作業。她以薪資處理為例，認為這類領域並不適合引入AI。她說，公司採取「精準外科手術式」的AI導入方式，鎖定預測準確度已足夠、且人工監督能彌補剩餘落差的應用場景。

  這套做法呈現出一種循序漸進、以問題為優先的AI採用模式。Globe表示，此模式已在網路營運方面帶來可衡量的財務回報，而AI在各業務功能的進一步整合也持續推進中。
