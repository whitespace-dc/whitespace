body_en: |
  Leaseweb has attained Pinnacle status in Broadcom's VMware Cloud Service Provider (VCSP) program, placing the Dutch infrastructure company at the top tier of the partner hierarchy for VMware cloud delivery across the European Economic Area, Singapore, and the United States.

  The Pinnacle designation is the highest level within Broadcom's Advantage Partner Program for cloud service providers, reserved for partners that meet requirements around certifications, sales performance, and multi-market service delivery. Leaseweb said the recognition formalises its role as an authorised delivery channel for VMware Cloud Foundation — Broadcom's private cloud platform — across the three regions.

  ## What Leaseweb will deliver

  Under the partnership, Leaseweb will offer VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF) in two service formats: as a managed private cloud and through what the company describes as sovereign cloud services. Sovereign cloud, in this context, refers to infrastructure designed to meet data residency requirements and jurisdictional controls — meaning customer data and workloads remain within defined legal and geographic boundaries.

  VMware Cloud Foundation is Broadcom's integrated stack for running both virtual machines and container-based applications. Broadcom positions it as a single operating model spanning on-premises data centres, edge environments, and managed infrastructure supplied through service provider partners such as Leaseweb.

  ## Company profile

  Leaseweb, founded in the Netherlands, operates in 10 countries with infrastructure across 28 data centres. Its service portfolio covers cloud services, dedicated servers, colocation, content delivery, and managed services.

  Co-CEO Lex Boost framed the Pinnacle status as a continuity play for existing VMware customers. "Customers need continuity, control and confidence in the VMware environments that support their business-critical workloads," Boost said. "As a Broadcom VCSP Pinnacle Partner, Leaseweb provides an authorised path forward, delivered through sovereign-by-design infrastructure with the resilience, transparency and scale our customers expect."

  ## Broadcom's partner strategy

  David Cairns, Director of Cloud Service Provider and OEM Sales at Broadcom, said Pinnacle partners are central to the company's effort to move customers to its current private cloud model. "As a Pinnacle partner, Leaseweb's expertise, experience and influence in the industry will play a crucial role in helping to lead our customers to adopting a modern private cloud," Cairns said. He added that VCF is intended to accelerate innovation, control cloud costs, and enable what Broadcom calls sovereignty and security with data control.

  ## Market context

  The announcement comes as organisations reassess private cloud and hosted infrastructure options for business-critical workloads, according to the source material. Managed VMware environments remain common in sectors that require established governance models, internal controls, and predictable deployment patterns, the source notes.

  Sovereign cloud services have attracted attention from businesses and public sector bodies facing stricter compliance requirements and greater scrutiny of cross-border data flows. Leaseweb's emphasis on sovereign-by-design infrastructure positions the offering toward regulated industries where legal and operational control over data is a procurement factor.

  ## Significance

  The Pinnacle designation expands Leaseweb's recognised role in the VMware ecosystem at a moment when infrastructure buyers are weighing compliance, control, and operational continuity more heavily in purchasing decisions. For Broadcom, adding a multi-region provider at the top tier of its partner program extends the geographic reach of VCF delivery through managed and sovereign-cloud channels.

  Leaseweb said the managed private and sovereign cloud services built on VCF are now available to customers across the European Economic Area, Singapore, and the United States.

  Leaseweb 已在 Broadcom 的 VMware 雲端服務供應商（VCSP）計畫中取得 Pinnacle 最高等級認證，使這家荷蘭基礎架構公司在歐洲經濟區、新加坡與美國三個地區的 VMware 雲端服務夥伴體系中位居頂端。

  Pinnacle 認證是 Broadcom Advantage 合作夥伴計畫中雲端服務供應商的最高等級，僅授予在認證資格、業務表現及多市場服務交付上均符合要求的合作夥伴。Leaseweb 表示，此項認可正式確立了其作為 Broadcom 私有雲端平台——VMware Cloud Foundation——在上述三個區域的授權交付管道。

  ## 服務內容

  在此合作夥伴關係下，Leaseweb 將以兩種服務形式提供 VMware Cloud Foundation（VCF）：一是託管私有雲端服務，二是該公司所稱的主權雲端服務。此處所指的主權雲端，是指專為滿足資料駐留要求與司法管轄控制而設計的基礎架構，亦即客戶資料與工作負載將留存於特定法律與地理範疇之內。

  VMware Cloud Foundation 是 Broadcom 用於同時執行虛擬機器與容器化應用程式的整合技術堆疊。Broadcom 將其定位為橫跨本地端資料中心、邊緣運算環境，以及由 Leaseweb 等服務供應商夥伴所提供之託管基礎架構的單一運營模式。

  ## 公司簡介

  Leaseweb 創立於荷蘭，目前在 10 個國家營運，基礎架構遍及 28 座資料中心。其服務組合涵蓋雲端服務、專用伺服器、伺服器代管、內容傳遞，以及代管服務。

  聯合執行長 Lex Boost 將 Pinnacle 認證定位為現有 VMware 客戶的延續性保證。他表示：「客戶需要對支撐其業務關鍵工作負載的 VMware 環境保有延續性、掌控力與信心。身為 Broadcom VCSP Pinnacle 合作夥伴，Leaseweb 提供一條授權的前進路徑，透過以主權設計為核心的基礎架構實現，具備客戶所期待的韌性、透明度與規模。」

  ## Broadcom 的夥伴策略

  Broadcom 雲端服務供應商暨 OEM 業務總監 David Cairns 表示，Pinnacle 合作夥伴在引領客戶轉向現行私有雲端模式的工作中居於核心地位。他說：「作為 Pinnacle 合作夥伴，Leaseweb 在業界的專業、經驗與影響力，將在引導客戶採用現代私有雲端的過程中扮演舉足輕重的角色。」他補充指出，VCF 旨在加速創新、控制雲端成本，並實現 Broadcom 所稱的主權性、安全性與資料掌控能力。

  ## 市場背景

  此次宣布正值各組織重新評估業務關鍵工作負載的私有雲端及託管基礎架構選項之際。在需要既定治理模式、內部管控與可預期部署模式的產業中，託管 VMware 環境至今仍相當普遍。

  主權雲端服務已吸引越來越多面臨嚴格法規合規要求、且跨境資料流動受到更高度審查的企業與公部門機構關注。Leaseweb 對主權設計基礎架構的強調，使其服務更切合法規嚴格產業的需求——在這些產業中，對資料的法律與營運掌控是採購決策的重要考量因素。

  ## 重要意義

  Pinnacle 認證的取得，在基礎架構採購方更加重視法規合規、掌控能力與營運延續性的當下，進一步強化了 Leaseweb 在 VMware 生態系中的認可地位。對 Broadcom 而言，在其合作夥伴計畫最高等級納入一家多區域供應商，也透過託管與主權雲端管道擴展了 VCF 的地理服務覆蓋範圍。

  Leaseweb 表示，基於 VCF 建構的託管私有雲端與主權雲端服務，現已向歐洲經濟區、新加坡與美國的客戶全面開放。
