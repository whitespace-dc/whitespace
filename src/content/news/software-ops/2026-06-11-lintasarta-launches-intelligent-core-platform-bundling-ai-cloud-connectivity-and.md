  ## Lintasarta Launches Intelligent Core Platform Bundling AI, Cloud, Connectivity, and Security

  Indonesian information and communications technology provider Lintasarta has introduced a platform called Intelligent Core, designed to serve as a unified digital foundation for enterprises adopting artificial intelligence, the company announced Wednesday, June 10, 2026.

  The platform bundles four existing Lintasarta service lines — internet and network connectivity, cloud services, cybersecurity, and collaboration and AI tools — into a single integrated offering. The stated goal is to eliminate the need for customers to manage multiple separate systems when running digital transformation or AI initiatives.

  ## Platform design and principles

  Lintasarta said Intelligent Core is built on three guiding principles: digital sovereignty, meaning data and infrastructure control remains with the customer or within national boundaries; service integration, combining the four layers into one managed stack; and what the company describes as a smoother user experience across those layers.

  President Director and CEO Armand Hermawan said the rationale for the platform is that AI cannot operate optimally without a strong, integrated technology foundation beneath it. "Through Intelligent Core, we help customers integrate digital infrastructure and AI in one solution that is secure, reliable, and interconnected," Hermawan said in a company statement.

  ## Industry-specific stacks

  Alongside the core platform, Lintasarta announced a set of preconfigured solution bundles tailored to specific sectors:

  - **Banking:** Secure Banking Stack
  - **Government:** Sovereign Government Stack
  - **Manufacturing:** Smart Factory Stack
  - **Healthcare:** Connected Healthcare
  - **Retail:** Omnichannel Retail

  The company said these vertical stacks are intended to help organizations in each sector accelerate AI and digital transformation adoption, improve operational efficiency, and strengthen system security.

  ## Customer base and scale

  Lintasarta currently serves more than 2,300 corporate customers, supported by 74,196 network connections deployed across multiple industry sectors in Indonesia, according to the company.

  ## Positioning shift

  The Intelligent Core launch signals an intentional shift in how Lintasarta positions itself commercially. The company said it aims to be seen not only as a technology services provider but as a partner that helps enterprises integrate digital and AI technologies according to their specific business needs — a distinction it is framing around advisory and integration capability rather than pure infrastructure supply.

  The company's next step, as stated in the announcement, is expanding that integration partner role across its existing corporate customer base in Indonesia.

  ## Lintasarta推出Intelligent Core平台，整合AI、雲端、連網與資安

  印尼資通訊技術（ICT）業者Lintasarta於2026年6月10日（星期三）正式宣布推出名為Intelligent Core的平台，定位為企業導入人工智慧的統一數位基礎建設。

  該平台整合了Lintasarta旗下四條現有服務線——網際網路與網路連線、雲端服務、資安防護，以及協作與AI工具——合為單一整合方案。其明確目標，是讓客戶在推動數位轉型或AI計畫時，不必再費力管理多套分散的獨立系統。

  ## 平台設計理念

  Lintasarta表示，Intelligent Core建立於三項核心原則之上：其一為「數位主權」，確保資料與基礎架構的控制權歸屬客戶本身、或留存於國境之內；其二為「服務整合」，將上述四個層次合為一套受管理的技術堆疊；其三則是公司所稱橫跨各層次的「更順暢使用者體驗」。

  執行長暨總裁Armand Hermawan表示，推出這套平台的出發點在於：若缺乏穩固且整合的底層技術基礎，AI便無法發揮最佳效能。他在公司聲明中指出：「透過Intelligent Core，我們協助客戶在單一方案中整合數位基礎建設與AI，讓整體架構安全、可靠且相互連結。」

  ## 垂直產業解決方案

  除核心平台外，Lintasarta同步宣布推出一系列針對特定產業預先配置的解決方案套件：

  - **金融銀行：** 安全銀行堆疊（Secure Banking Stack）
  - **政府機關：** 主權政府堆疊（Sovereign Government Stack）
  - **製造業：** 智慧工廠堆疊（Smart Factory Stack）
  - **醫療健康：** 互聯醫療（Connected Healthcare）
  - **零售業：** 全通路零售（Omnichannel Retail）

  公司表示，這些垂直產業堆疊旨在協助各領域組織加速AI與數位轉型的導入、提升營運效率，並強化系統資安。

  ## 客戶規模

  Lintasarta目前服務超過2,300家企業客戶，並在印尼各產業部署了74,196個網路連線節點。

  ## 定位轉型

  Intelligent Core的推出，標誌著Lintasarta商業定位的刻意轉向。公司表示，其目標不僅是作為技術服務供應商，更希望成為協助企業依照自身業務需求整合數位技術與AI的夥伴——此一差異化訴求，著重於顧問諮詢與整合能力，而非單純的基礎架構供應。

  公司在聲明中指出，下一步將在印尼現有企業客戶群中深化拓展這一整合夥伴角色。
