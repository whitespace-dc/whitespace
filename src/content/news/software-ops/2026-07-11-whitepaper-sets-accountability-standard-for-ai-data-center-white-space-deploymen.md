body_en: |
  A whitepaper published July 10, 2026 by Data Center Dynamics identifies the physical field execution layer — not design or equipment supply — as the primary de-risking challenge for AI and high-performance computing (HPC) data center programs operating at hyperscale speed.

  The document argues that once a design is approved and equipment is on order, revenue-bearing capacity still depends on thousands of field actions being completed correctly and in sequence. That dependency, it contends, is where programs are most exposed.

  ## Pressures specific to AI and HPC deployments

  According to the whitepaper, AI and HPC programs place unusual pressure on the physical deployment layer relative to conventional data center work. The factors cited include high rack density, high fiber counts, compressed change windows, specialized testing requirements, and the need to conduct parallel work across live environments — conditions that increase the consequence of field errors and sequencing failures.

  ## What the whitepaper asks buyers to evaluate

  The document reframes the procurement question for decision makers. Rather than asking whether a provider can supply labor, it argues buyers should ask whether a provider can accept measurable accountability for schedule, first-pass quality, operational impact, material readiness, and closeout — across a single site or an entire multi-site program.

  To support that evaluation, the whitepaper provides:

  - A buyer's standard for assessing white-space execution partners
  - A governance scorecard designed to be incorporated into a statement of work and weekly operating cadence
  - Case study data drawn from three anonymized engagements
  - A defined path to evaluate a service called We Install AI on a bounded production scope

  ## Significance

  The whitepaper reflects a broader industry recognition that the physical installation layer has become a program-level risk factor in AI infrastructure buildouts, not merely a downstream logistics concern. By proposing a governance scorecard intended for inclusion in statements of work, the document attempts to move execution accountability from informal expectation to contractual standard — a shift that, if adopted, would change how hyperscale operators structure vendor relationships for white-space deployment.

  The whitepaper offers a bounded production scope as a low-risk entry point for evaluating We Install AI as a deployment partner.

  ## 白皮書為AI資料中心白空間部署樹立問責標準

  Data Center Dynamics於2026年7月10日發布的一份白皮書指出，在以超大規模速度推進的AI與高效能運算（HPC）資料中心專案中，首要的去風險挑戰並非設計或設備供應，而是現場實體執行層。

  白皮書的核心論點是：一旦設計獲得核准、設備完成訂購，能否產生營收的產能，仍取決於數千項現場作業能否依正確順序、精確完成。文件指出，這正是整個專案最脆弱的環節。

  ## AI與HPC部署所面臨的特殊壓力

  白皮書指出，相較於傳統資料中心工程，AI與HPC專案對實體部署層施加了遠超尋常的壓力。所列舉的因素包括：高機架密度、高光纖數量、壓縮的變更作業時窗、專業化的測試需求，以及必須在既有上線環境中同步推進多項作業——這些條件都大幅提高了現場失誤與作業順序錯亂所帶來的後果。

  ## 白皮書要求採購方評估的面向

  本文件重新定義了決策者在採購階段應提出的問題。白皮書認為，採購方不應只問供應商能否提供人力，而應追問：該供應商能否就進度、首次施工品質、營運影響、物料備料狀況，以及結案交付等面向，承擔可量化的問責責任——無論是單一場址或跨多站點的整體專案。

  為協助採購方進行評估，白皮書提供了以下工具：

  - 一套評估白空間執行合作夥伴的採購標準
  - 一份治理評分卡，設計供納入工作說明書（SOW）及每週例行作業節奏使用
  - 源自三個匿名實際案例的案例研究資料
  - 一條在有限的正式生產範圍內評估「We Install AI」服務的明確路徑

  ## 意義

  這份白皮書反映出業界更廣泛的共識：在AI基礎設施建置中，實體安裝層已成為專案層級的風險因素，而非單純的下游物流問題。透過提出一份旨在納入工作說明書的治理評分卡，本文件試圖將執行問責從非正式的期望提升至合約標準——若此趨勢獲得採納，將根本性地改變超大規模業者與白空間部署廠商之間的合作結構。

  白皮書亦提供有限的正式生產範圍作為切入點，讓有意採用者得以低風險地評估「We Install AI」作為部署合作夥伴的實力。
