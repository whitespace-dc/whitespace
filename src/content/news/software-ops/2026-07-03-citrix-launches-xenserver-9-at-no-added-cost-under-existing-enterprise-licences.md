body_en: |
  Citrix has released XenServer 9 and made it available at no additional cost to customers holding existing Citrix Platform Licence, Universal Hybrid Multi Cloud, or Citrix for Private Cloud entitlements, covering deployments of up to 10,000 sockets.

  The 10,000-socket threshold places the entitlement squarely in large enterprise territory. A socket, in server licensing terms, refers to a physical processor slot on a server motherboard, meaning the included coverage extends to sizeable infrastructure estates without requiring a separate purchase.

  ## Pricing model

  Citrix is positioning the licensing approach as a direct response to rising software costs and uncertainty over long-term licensing terms that have led many organisations to review their virtualisation spending. By folding XenServer 9 into agreements customers already hold, Citrix reduces the financial barrier for infrastructure teams considering a platform change, the company said.

  The move comes as enterprise technology buyers have grown more sensitive to virtualisation costs. Many organisations with large on-premises or hybrid deployments have been reassessing both the economics and operational complexity of their current platforms, and Citrix said it intends XenServer 9 to be considered not only for niche deployments but for broad internal use.

  ## Operational changes

  The release centres on lifecycle management, system maintenance, security, and tighter integration with Citrix's desktop and application delivery products, including Citrix DaaS (Desktop as a Service, which delivers Windows desktops and applications from a central platform).

  Citrix said XenServer 9 replaces large, disruptive platform refreshes with an incremental update model, which the company described as designed to lower upgrade risk in production systems, particularly in distributed or large-scale environments where downtime and compatibility problems are difficult to manage. The platform also introduces a consolidated management plane and a more predictable update schedule.

  Driver and lifecycle management have been updated to reduce disruption during hardware changes and software updates, an area Citrix said matters to customers running mixed infrastructure estates spanning multiple server generations and device types.

  ## Security and performance

  XenServer 9 adds hardware-level protections intended to strengthen system integrity from the boot process onward, which Citrix framed as part of a broader effort to improve the security baseline for enterprise deployments and limit exposure to low-level threats. The company did not disclose specific benchmark figures, but said the platform is designed to improve resource utilisation and hardware alignment to deliver more consistent application performance.

  For environments running Citrix DaaS, XenServer 9 adds infrastructure-layer visibility intended to help administrators assess how maintenance work or configuration changes could affect running workloads and end-user sessions before those changes are applied.

  ## Market context

  Citrix framed the launch around pressures that have made virtualisation a sensitive procurement area for enterprise buyers: cost control, operational predictability, security, support for existing hardware, and the ability to manage upgrades without service disruption. Many large organisations continue to run business-critical workloads on-premises for governance, control, and data sovereignty reasons, even as they expand into hybrid environments, and platform decisions for that group often turn on stable economics and manageable operations rather than technical features alone, the company said.

  Citrix said it is positioning XenServer 9 at the centre of its pitch to customers reviewing infrastructure spending, arguing that virtualisation platforms are increasingly judged on long-term cost stability and administrative simplicity.

  XenServer 9 is available now under existing Citrix Platform Licence, Universal Hybrid Multi Cloud, and Citrix for Private Cloud entitlements, with support for deployments of up to 10,000 sockets at no additional cost.

  ## 授權費用

  Citrix 正式發布 XenServer 9，並宣布持有現有 Citrix Platform Licence、Universal Hybrid Multi Cloud 或 Citrix for Private Cloud 授權的客戶，無需額外付費即可取得這套新版平台，涵蓋範圍最高達一萬個插槽的部署環境。

  一萬插槽的門檻，使這項授權資格直接落在大型企業的應用規模。就伺服器授權而言，「插槽」指的是伺服器主機板上的實體處理器插座，意味著現有授權所涵蓋的基礎架構規模相當可觀，客戶無須另行採購即可支應。

  ## 授權定價策略

  Citrix 將此次授權模式定位為對企業軟體成本攀升、以及授權條款長期不確定性的直接回應——這些因素已促使許多組織重新審視虛擬化的支出。Citrix 表示，藉由將 XenServer 9 納入客戶既有的合約範圍，可降低正在評估平台轉換的基礎架構團隊所面臨的財務門檻。

  此舉恰逢企業技術採購方對虛擬化成本愈發敏感之際。許多擁有大規模地端或混合部署環境的組織，正重新評估現有平台的經濟效益與運維複雜度。Citrix 表示，XenServer 9 的定位不只是利基型部署，而是希望客戶廣泛應用於內部基礎架構。

  ## 運維層面的改進

  此次發布的重點涵蓋生命週期管理、系統維護、資安防護，以及與 Citrix 桌面和應用程式交付產品的深度整合，其中包括 Citrix DaaS（Desktop as a Service，即從集中式平台提供 Windows 桌面與應用程式的雲端桌面服務）。

  Citrix 表示，XenServer 9 以漸進式更新模式取代過去大規模、破壞性的平台刷新作業。該公司指出，此設計旨在降低正式環境的升級風險，對於分散式或大規模環境尤其重要，因為這類環境難以承受停機時間和相容性問題。此外，平台也導入了整合式管理介面，並提供更具可預期性的更新排程。

  驅動程式與生命週期管理亦有所更新，以減少硬體變更和軟體更新過程中的作業中斷。Citrix 表示，對於橫跨多個伺服器世代與設備類型的混合基礎架構環境，這項改進格外重要。

  ## 資安與效能

  XenServer 9 新增硬體層級的防護機制，旨在從開機流程起即強化系統完整性。Citrix 將此定位為提升企業部署整體資安基準、降低底層威脅暴露風險的更廣泛努力的一環。該公司未揭露具體的效能測試數據，但表示此平台的設計目標在於改善資源利用率與硬體配置的協調性，以提供更穩定一致的應用程式效能。

  對於執行 Citrix DaaS 的環境，XenServer 9 在基礎架構層新增了可視化功能，協助管理員在實際套用維護作業或設定變更之前，預先評估這些變動可能對執行中的工作負載和使用者連線工作階段造成的影響。

  ## 市場背景

  Citrix 以一系列讓虛擬化成為企業採購敏感議題的市場壓力為背景，推出此次發布：成本控管、運維可預期性、資安強化、對現有硬體的支援，以及在不中斷服務的前提下管理升級的能力。Citrix 指出，許多大型組織基於法遵治理、掌控度與資料主權等考量，即使逐步拓展混合雲環境，仍持續在地端運行關鍵業務工作負載。對這類客群而言，平台選擇往往取決於穩定的經濟效益與可管理的運維，而非單純的技術規格。

  Citrix 表示，在客戶重新審視基礎架構支出之際，公司正將 XenServer 9 置於其核心銷售主張的中心位置，並強調虛擬化平台的評估標準愈來愈著重於長期成本穩定性與管理簡便性。

  XenServer 9 現已正式上市，持有 Citrix Platform Licence、Universal Hybrid Multi Cloud 及 Citrix for Private Cloud 授權的客戶均可免費取得，最高支援一萬個插槽的部署規模，無需額外付費。
