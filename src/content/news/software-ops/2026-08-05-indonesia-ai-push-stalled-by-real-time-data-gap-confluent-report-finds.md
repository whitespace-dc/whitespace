body_en: |
  Nearly four in five IT leaders in Indonesia say limited real-time data infrastructure is impeding artificial intelligence development at their organizations, according to the 2026 Data Streaming Report published July 29 by Confluent, a data streaming platform company that is part of IBM.

  The report found that 79 percent of Indonesian respondents identified real-time data infrastructure constraints as a barrier to AI development. A further 74 percent cited infrastructure and data quality as specific obstacles to deploying autonomous AI, meaning AI systems capable of operating without continuous human direction. The survey covered 4,625 IT leaders across multiple countries.

  ## Indonesia's Compounding Challenges

  The problem extends beyond infrastructure alone. Confluent found that 82 percent of IT leaders in Indonesia face at least three distinct challenges when attempting to move AI from the pilot stage into live operational deployment, the report said.

  "Organizations in Indonesia are not lacking in AI ambition, but our research shows they are being held back by data infrastructure that was never designed for real-time and autonomous systems," Rully Moulany, Area Vice President Asia at Confluent, said in a statement.

  ## Data Streaming Ranked Above AI as a Strategic Priority

  Despite these obstacles, Indonesian IT leaders expressed the strongest confidence in data streaming technology of any country surveyed. Ninety-nine percent of Indonesian respondents said they believe data streaming, the continuous movement and processing of data as it is generated rather than in periodic batches, can increase the impact of AI investment, the highest figure recorded in the report.

  Ninety-eight percent of Indonesian respondents said data streaming platforms could accelerate AI adoption at their companies. The same proportion ranked data streaming as a more important strategic priority than AI solutions and machine learning (ML) applications, according to the report.

  "IT leaders in Indonesia believe — more than almost any other region we surveyed — that data streaming can close this gap. That is why data streaming has displaced AI itself as the top strategic priority in Indonesia," Moulany said.

  ## A Global Pattern

  The infrastructure shortfall Indonesia faces reflects a broader trend. Globally, 72 percent of the 4,625 IT leaders surveyed said a lack of real-time data infrastructure is restricting their ability to scale AI initiatives, and the same share reported facing at least three challenges when trying to expand AI programs.

  The most commonly cited global obstacles were:

  - Infrastructure not capable of processing data in real time: 72 percent
  - Uncertainty about data origin, timeliness, and quality: 66 percent
  - Fragmented data ownership across the organization: 65 percent

  These constraints are weighing most heavily on autonomous AI deployment. Sixty-six percent of global IT leaders cited infrastructure and data quality as barriers specifically to bringing autonomous AI systems online. Only 32 percent of all respondents said they have autonomous AI running in a production environment, and most within that group reported experiencing delays during the deployment process.

  ## Data Readiness as the Limiting Factor

  Shaun Clowes, Chief Product Officer at Confluent, said the primary obstacle for most organizations is not a shortage of AI investment but a lack of readiness in the underlying data systems.

  Moulany said companies that invest in strong data streaming infrastructure now will be best positioned to convert AI pilots into production value.

  ## Confluent報告：即時資料基礎建設缺口拖累印尼AI發展

  根據隸屬IBM旗下的資料串流平台業者Confluent於7月29日發布的《2026資料串流報告》，印尼近五分之四的IT主管表示，即時資料基礎建設的不足正在阻礙各自組織的人工智慧發展。

  報告指出，79%的印尼受訪者將即時資料基礎建設的限制視為AI發展的障礙。另有74%的受訪者將基礎建設與資料品質列為部署自主AI的具體阻礙——所謂自主AI，意指能夠在無須人工持續介入的情況下自行運作的AI系統。此次調查共涵蓋多個國家，合計4,625位IT主管。

  ## 印尼面臨的複合挑戰

  問題不僅止於基礎建設本身。Confluent的報告發現，82%的印尼IT主管在嘗試將AI從試驗階段推進至正式營運部署時，面臨至少三項截然不同的挑戰。

  Confluent亞洲區副總裁Rully Moulany在聲明中表示：「印尼的企業組織在AI雄心上並不匱乏，但我們的研究顯示，他們正被一套從未針對即時與自主系統設計的資料基礎建設所拖累。」

  ## 資料串流的戰略地位超越AI本身

  儘管面臨上述障礙，印尼IT主管對資料串流技術的信心卻是所有受訪國家中最為強烈的。99%的印尼受訪者表示，他們相信資料串流——即在資料產生的當下持續移動與處理，而非以定期批次方式處理——能夠提升AI投資的效益，此比例為報告中所有受訪國的最高值。

  98%的印尼受訪者表示，資料串流平台能夠加速其公司的AI導入進程。同樣比例的受訪者也認為，資料串流比AI解決方案及機器學習（ML）應用更具戰略重要性。

  Moulany說：「印尼的IT主管比我們調查的幾乎任何其他地區都更加相信，資料串流能夠彌補這道缺口。正因如此，資料串流在印尼已取代AI本身，躍升為首要戰略優先項目。」

  ## 全球性的共同趨勢

  印尼所面臨的基礎建設缺口，反映出一個更廣泛的全球趨勢。在全部4,625位受訪IT主管中，72%表示即時資料基礎建設的不足正在限制他們擴大AI計畫的能力，同樣比例的受訪者也表示在推動AI規模化時面臨至少三項挑戰。

  全球受訪者最常提及的障礙依序為：

  - 基礎建設無法即時處理資料：72%
  - 對資料來源、時效性與品質感到不確定：66%
  - 資料所有權在組織內部各自為政、碎片化：65%

  這些限制對自主AI的部署影響最為沉重。66%的全球IT主管將基礎建設與資料品質列為部署自主AI系統的具體障礙。在所有受訪者中，僅有32%表示其組織已有自主AI在正式環境中運行，且這部分受訪者中的大多數都表示在部署過程中曾遭遇延誤。

  ## 資料就緒程度成為關鍵瓶頸

  Confluent首席產品長Shaun Clowes表示，對大多數組織而言，主要障礙並非AI投資不足，而是底層資料系統尚未準備就緒。

  Moulany則指出，現在就積極投資健全資料串流基礎建設的企業，將最有能力把AI試驗專案轉化為正式上線的實際價值。
