body_en: |
  Telecom operators worldwide are turning to artificial intelligence as a growth engine, Forrester Research says in a new report, as the sector faces projected annual revenue expansion of just 0.3% between 2024 and 2027 alongside largely flat margins.

  The research describes AI as a major opportunity for communications service providers (CSPs) — the industry term for network operators — seeking new revenue streams beyond traditional voice and data services. Operators are applying AI across networks, IT systems, and day-to-day operations while expanding into adjacent markets including data centres, graphics processing unit (GPU) compute, cybersecurity, and AI-based services for business customers.

  ## Why operators are moving on AI now

  The impetus, Forrester says, is a sector-wide concern that basic connectivity has become too commoditised to sustain growth. Tom Mouhsian, Principal Analyst at Forrester, said operators are motivated in part by having failed to capture value from earlier technology shifts.

  "Many CSPs admit having missed prior opportunities during the rise of OTT (e.g., streaming services) and cloud computing and feel concerned that their traditional value proposition anchored in network connectivity (e.g., voice and data) is too commoditized," Mouhsian said.

  Forrester argues the current AI cycle is different because operators are not simply adopting the technology but positioning themselves as infrastructure providers in their domestic markets, competing more directly with hyperscale cloud companies and established AI component suppliers — a category the report refers to as "picks and shovels" companies such as chipmakers.

  "That's not something they want to repeat during the AI boom. In fact, dozens of well-known CSPs around the world are boldly entering the AI infrastructure race, challenging the established AI 'picks and shovels' companies (e.g., chipmakers and hyperscalers)," Mouhsian said.

  ## Operational returns cited

  Some of the clearest early evidence of AI returns is appearing in network operations, according to Forrester. The report cites AI-led processes reducing energy consumption by as much as 35%, accelerating network issue detection by 80%, and cutting high-severity incidents by 94%.

  Those figures carry particular weight for an industry that has long struggled to balance capital-intensive network buildouts against limited top-line growth. Lower operating costs and improved network reliability represent an immediate financial case for AI adoption, the report suggests, even before new service revenues become material.

  Forrester also notes that autonomous networks — systems capable of self-healing and real-time optimisation with minimal human intervention, sometimes called zero-touch operations — are moving closer to practical deployment. Most operators currently run at only partial autonomy, the report says, but AI-based orchestration and agentic systems are making self-managing networks a more realistic near-term goal.

  ## Investment pressures and ecosystem requirements

  Operators are building local AI ecosystems shaped by data sovereignty requirements and geopolitical pressures, Forrester says. The report frames investment in AI infrastructure as both a growth strategy and a defensive move: without it, operators risk being squeezed by rising costs and limited pricing power as data traffic grows and customer expectations increase.

  Success will depend less on experimental pilots than on operators building workable ecosystems, Forrester argues. That includes AI governance structures, staff with cross-functional expertise, and partnerships with cloud providers, technology vendors, and software developers. The emphasis the firm identifies is on production-ready deployments and measurable outcomes rather than abstract innovation goals.

  ## Asia-Pacific as a testing ground

  In the Asia-Pacific region, Forrester identifies rapid digital transformation and heavy infrastructure spending as key drivers of the shift. India and Singapore are specifically highlighted as markets expanding AI capacity, supported by public and private sector investment aimed at strengthening digital competitiveness.

  Across the region, operators are also applying AI to shorten product development timelines, improve service delivery, and sharpen operational performance, making Asia-Pacific what Forrester describes as an important testing ground for AI applications in both customer-facing and internal functions.

  ## What the shift means for the sector

  For operators facing investor pressure and rising costs, Forrester says the defining test is whether AI can deliver measurable savings or generate sustainable new revenue streams. The firm notes that in several countries the strategy also aligns with national priorities around local data handling, digital resilience, and sovereign technology development — factors that could accelerate infrastructure build-out beyond purely commercial logic.

  Forrester says the sector's investments are reshaping competitive dynamics, with CSPs seeking to challenge established AI infrastructure suppliers rather than remaining buyers of their services.
body_zh: |
  ## Forrester：電信業年營收僅成長0.3%，紛紛轉向AI基礎建設尋求新動能

  全球電信業者正將人工智慧視為新的成長引擎，Forrester Research在最新報告中指出，該產業在2024至2027年間預計年均營收成長幅度僅0.3%，利潤率亦幾近持平。

  報告將AI定位為通訊服務供應商（CSP，即網路業者的業界通稱）的重大機遇，協助業者在傳統語音與數據服務之外，開拓全新的營收來源。電信商正將AI技術廣泛應用於網路、資訊系統及日常營運，同時積極跨足相鄰市場，包括資料中心、GPU運算、資安服務，以及面向企業客戶的AI加值服務。

  ## 電信業者為何此刻加速布局AI

  Forrester指出，推動這股轉型的根本動力，在於業界普遍意識到基礎連線服務已過度商品化，難以支撐持續成長。Forrester首席分析師Tom Mouhsian表示，電信商之所以積極行動，部分原因在於過去錯失了幾波關鍵的技術浪潮。

  「許多CSP坦承，自己在OTT（例如串流媒體服務）與雲端運算崛起的過程中，未能及時掌握價值，如今也擔憂其以網路連線（例如語音與數據）為核心的傳統價值主張，已被高度商品化，」Mouhsian說道。

  Forrester認為，這一波AI浪潮與過去不同——電信商不再只是單純採用技術，而是積極將自身定位為各自本土市場的基礎建設供應商，與超大規模雲端業者及報告所稱「鎬鏟工具」（picks and shovels）類的既有AI零組件供應商（如晶片製造商）展開更直接的競爭。

  「這一次，他們不願再重蹈覆轍。事實上，全球數十家知名CSP正大膽投身AI基礎建設競賽，向晶片商與超大規模雲端業者等已建立的AI『鎬鏟工具』公司發起挑戰，」Mouhsian說。

  ## 營運成效初見端倪

  根據Forrester的報告，AI帶來的最直接成果，已在網路營運領域率先顯現。報告引述數據指出，AI驅動的流程可將能源消耗降低最多35%、將網路故障偵測速度提升80%、並使高嚴重性事件減少94%。

  這些數字對於長期在資本密集的網路建設與有限營收成長之間艱難取捨的電信業而言，意義格外重大。報告指出，即便尚未看到新服務帶來的實質營收，降低營運成本與提升網路可靠性，已構成電信商導入AI的即時財務誘因。

  Forrester亦指出，自主網路（即具備自我修復與即時優化能力、幾乎不需人工介入的系統，有時也稱為「零接觸營運」）正逐步接近實際部署的階段。報告表示，目前大多數電信商的自動化程度仍屬局部，但以AI為基礎的協調系統與代理式AI架構，正使自我管理網路成為更可及的近期目標。

  ## 投資壓力與生態系建構

  Forrester指出，各電信商正依據資料主權要求與地緣政治壓力，在本地建構AI生態系。報告將AI基礎建設投資定性為既是成長策略，也是防禦性舉措——若不及早布局，電信商在數據流量持續攀升、用戶期待不斷提高的環境下，恐將同時承受成本上漲與定價能力受限的雙重擠壓。

  Forrester強調，電信商能否成功，取決於是否能建立可行的生態系，而非停留在零星的實驗性試點。這包括建立AI治理架構、培養具跨職能專業的人才，以及與雲端供應商、科技廠商及軟體開發商建立夥伴關係。該機構特別強調，業界的重心應放在可實際投產的部署與可衡量的成果，而非空泛的創新目標。

  ## 亞太地區：AI應用的重要試驗場

  在亞太地區，Forrester指出快速推進的數位轉型與大規模基礎建設投資，是推動此波轉型的關鍵驅動力。報告特別點名印度與新加坡，視兩地為積極擴充AI算力的重要市場，背後有公私部門的投資支持，目的在於強化數位競爭力。

  縱觀亞太地區，各電信業者也正運用AI縮短產品開發週期、提升服務交付品質，並強化營運績效，使亞太地區成為Forrester口中AI應用的重要試驗場，涵蓋面向客戶的服務功能與內部作業兩大面向。

  ## 轉型意涵：重塑產業競爭格局

  面對投資人壓力與成本攀升的雙重挑戰，Forrester表示，電信業者最終的關鍵考驗，在於AI能否帶來可衡量的成本節省，或創造可持續的新營收來源。該機構指出，在部分國家，這項策略也與本地數據處理、數位韌性及主權科技發展等國家政策目標相互契合——這些因素可能推動基礎建設投資的速度，超越純粹的商業邏輯。

  Forrester表示，電信業的這波投資正在重塑競爭態勢，各大CSP正努力從既有AI基礎建設供應商的買家角色，轉型成為足以與其抗衡的競爭者。
