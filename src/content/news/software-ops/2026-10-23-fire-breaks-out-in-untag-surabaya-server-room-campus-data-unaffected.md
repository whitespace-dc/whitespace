body_en: |
  ## Fire contained in under 30 minutes

  A fire broke out Wednesday morning in the data center room of the Graha Wiyata building at Universitas 17 Agustus 1945 (Untag) Surabaya, Indonesia, requiring nine fire trucks to contain. Campus data was unaffected because it had already been migrated to a new server room before the incident, according to university rector Harjo Seputro.

  The Surabaya City Fire and Rescue Service (DPKP) received a report of the blaze at 09:46 local time on Wednesday, October 21, 2026. Units from the Sukolilo Post responded immediately. DPKP division chief M. Rokhim said the main fire was extinguished by 09:58 and the situation was declared fully under control at 10:16 — a total incident window of approximately 30 minutes. No casualties or injuries were reported.

  Rokhim said the fire is believed to have been triggered by a fault in an internet server that caused sparks. The fire originated on the second floor of the building, where the data center room is located.

  ## Early detection system and staff response

  Rector Harjo Seputro said smoke in the building was picked up by the campus early detection system, which activated an alarm across the entire building. Campus staff responded immediately using portable fire extinguishers (APAR — alat pemadam api ringan) and coordinated with the city fire service.

  Harjo said campus data remained secure because it had been transferred to a new server room prior to the incident, meaning the fire in the old server room had no effect on data integrity.

  ## Internet disruption under recovery

  Despite the data being safe, the fire caused a disruption to internet connectivity across the campus network. Harjo said Untag's IT technician team was focused on restoring the network so that campus activities could resume.

  ## Significance

  The incident illustrates how a prior infrastructure migration — moving data to a new server room before decommissioning the old one — can limit the operational impact of a physical facility failure. The rapid response of both the campus early detection system and city fire services contained the blaze before it could spread beyond the server room. Network recovery was ongoing as of the time of reporting.

  ## 火勢於30分鐘內撲滅

  印尼泗水「1945年8月17日大學」（Untag）格拉哈維亞塔大樓（Graha Wiyata）的資料中心機房，於週三上午發生火警，共出動九輛消防車前往灌救。校長哈爾約·塞普特羅（Harjo Seputro）表示，由於校方事先已將校園資料遷移至新伺服器機房，本次火災並未影響任何校園資料。

  泗水市消防暨救援局（DPKP）於2026年10月21日（週三）當地時間09：46接獲通報，蘇科利洛（Sukolilo）消防站隨即派員出動。DPKP分局長M·羅希姆（M. Rokhim）說明，主要火勢於09：58已壓制，現場於10：16正式宣告完全受控，整起事件歷時約30分鐘。此次火災未造成任何人員傷亡。

  羅希姆指出，研判起火原因為一台網路伺服器發生故障並產生火花，引發火警。火源位於大樓二樓的資料中心機房。

  ## 早期偵測系統與人員應變

  校長哈爾約·塞普特羅表示，大樓內的煙霧由校園早期偵測系統捕捉，隨即觸發全棟警報。校方人員立即以輕型滅火器（APAR，印尼語alat pemadam api ringan）先行處置，並同步協調市消防局到場支援。

  哈爾約指出，由於校園資料在事發前已轉移至新伺服器機房，舊機房的火災對資料完整性毫無影響，所有校園資料均安然無恙。

  ## 網路中斷，持續恢復中

  儘管資料安全無虞，這場火災仍造成全校園網路連線中斷。哈爾約表示，翁達格大學（Untag）的資訊技術工程師團隊正全力投入網路復原作業，以期讓校園各項活動得以盡快恢復正常運作。

  ## 事件意義

  此次事件清楚說明，在舊設施正式退役前預先完成基礎架構遷移——將資料移往新伺服器機房——如何有效降低實體設施損毀所帶來的營運衝擊。校園早期偵測系統與市消防局的迅速應變，使火勢在蔓延至機房以外之前便已獲得控制。截至報導發出時，網路恢復作業仍在持續進行中。
