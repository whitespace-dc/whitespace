  ## Public sector IT infrastructure lags AI demand

  Seventy-three percent of public sector IT infrastructure is currently unable to run complex artificial intelligence workloads in on-premises environments — systems owned and operated directly by the organizations themselves — according to Nutanix's eighth annual Enterprise Cloud Index (ECI) report released in July 2026.

  The finding comes as government agencies and educational institutions are accelerating AI adoption for operational tasks including determining benefits eligibility and detecting fraud, creating a widening gap between demand for AI capability and the infrastructure available to support it.

  The ECI survey covered public sector organizations spanning central and local government, primary and secondary schools, and higher education institutions. It was conducted by Wakefield Research in November 2025 and drew responses from 1,600 executives in cloud, IT, and engineering roles at manager level or above.

  ## Shadow AI emerges as governance risk

  Beyond infrastructure gaps, the report identified unverified AI adoption — commonly called shadow AI, meaning the use of AI services or applications by individuals or teams without organizational oversight — as a significant governance challenge.

  Ninety-one percent of IT leaders surveyed across government and education organizations said unsanctioned AI use poses risks to their organization's mission and security.

  "The widespread adoption of unverified AI, or shadow AI, has become a primary governance challenge in the public sector," Daryush Ashjari, Chief Technology Officer and Vice President of Solution Engineering for Asia-Pacific and Japan at Nutanix, said in a statement. Ashjari said the focus of public sector organizations is shifting from chasing AI adoption toward operational readiness, and that organizations need to strengthen their infrastructure foundations to run AI safely while maintaining public trust.

  ## Fragmented infrastructure complicates management

  Nutanix noted that AI-supporting infrastructure in public sector organizations is spread across multiple environments, including legacy on-premises systems, private cloud deployments, and modern public cloud services. The report said this fragmentation makes unified system management more difficult, particularly in areas of security, regulatory compliance, and data governance.

  ## Containerization expected to grow

  In response to these pressures, public sector organizations are turning to application containerization — a method of packaging software so it can run consistently across different computing environments — as a way to increase the speed, scalability, and security of AI workloads.

  Eighty-seven percent of public sector technology leaders surveyed said they expect their organizations' reliance on application containerization to increase over the next three years.

  ## What the findings indicate for the sector

  The combination of rising AI use cases, infrastructure unreadiness, and shadow AI exposure places public sector organizations at a critical inflection point, according to Nutanix. As AI becomes embedded in government service delivery and administrative operations, the gap between deployed AI tools and the infrastructure and governance frameworks needed to support them securely represents a near-term operational risk for agencies and institutions.

  Nutanix said public sector organizations are expected to accelerate infrastructure modernization efforts, with containerization adoption serving as a primary near-term mechanism for expanding AI workload capacity.

  ## 公部門IT基礎架構落後AI需求

  根據Nutanix於2026年7月發布的第八屆年度企業雲端指數（ECI）報告，目前有73%的公部門IT基礎架構，無法在地端環境——即由各機構自行擁有並運營的系統——中執行複雜的人工智慧工作負載。

  這項調查結果浮現之際，政府機關與教育機構正加速導入AI，應用於福利資格認定與詐欺偵測等行政作業，使AI能力需求與可支撐基礎架構之間的落差持續擴大。

  ECI調查涵蓋中央與地方政府、中小學及高等教育機構等公部門組織，由Wakefield Research於2025年11月執行，共蒐集來自1,600位雲端、IT及工程領域、經理層級以上高階主管的問卷回覆。

  ## 影子AI成為治理風險

  除基礎架構缺口外，報告亦將未經核准的AI使用——通稱「影子AI」，即個人或團隊在未獲組織授權的情況下，自行使用AI服務或應用程式的行為——點名為重大治理挑戰。

  調查顯示，政府與教育機構中有91%的IT主管認為，未經授權的AI使用對其組織的任務執行及資安構成風險。

  「未經核准AI的廣泛擴散，也就是影子AI，已成為公部門最主要的治理挑戰，」Nutanix亞太暨日本區技術長暨解決方案工程副總裁Daryush Ashjari在一份聲明中表示。Ashjari指出，公部門組織的關注焦點正從追趕AI導入速度轉向強化作業準備度，各機構需要鞏固基礎架構根基，才能在維護公眾信任的同時讓AI安全運行。

  ## 基礎架構分散使管理趨於複雜

  Nutanix指出，公部門組織的AI支援基礎架構分散於多個環境，涵蓋老舊的地端系統、私有雲部署，以及現代化的公有雲服務。報告表示，這種分散化現象使統一的系統管理更加困難，尤其在資安、法規遵循及資料治理等層面更為突出。

  ## 容器化應用預期持續成長

  面對上述壓力，公部門組織正轉向應用程式容器化——一種將軟體封裝打包，使其能在不同運算環境中一致運行的方法——藉此提升AI工作負載的速度、擴展性與安全性。

  受訪的公部門科技主管中，有87%表示預期未來三年內，其組織對應用程式容器化的依賴程度將持續提升。

  ## 調查結果對公部門的意涵

  Nutanix指出，AI應用場景的持續增加、基礎架構整備不足，以及影子AI的風險敞口三者交疊，使公部門組織正站在關鍵的轉折點上。隨著AI逐漸深入政府服務提供與行政作業，已部署的AI工具與能夠安全支撐這些工具所需的基礎架構及治理框架之間的落差，已對各機關與機構構成近期的作業風險。

  Nutanix表示，公部門組織預計將加速基礎架構現代化工程，而容器化的導入將成為近期擴展AI工作負載承載能力的主要機制。
