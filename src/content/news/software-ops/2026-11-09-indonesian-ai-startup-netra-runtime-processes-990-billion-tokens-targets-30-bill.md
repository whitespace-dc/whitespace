body_en: |
  Indonesian AI inference infrastructure startup Netra Runtime, backed by venture capital firm Init-6, has processed 990 billion tokens (the basic units of text that AI language models handle) in roughly 1.5 months since its Indonesian launch, according to Chief Executive Officer Rifky Bujana Bisry. The company is targeting 30 billion tokens processed per day by year-end.

  Netra Runtime currently processes between 6 billion and 12 billion tokens per day, Bisry said. Reaching the year-end target would require more than doubling its current peak daily volume.

  ## Services and pricing structure

  The company operates two commercial models. The first is a Data API service: customers access AI processing through the Netra application, loading credits via QRIS (Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard, Indonesia's national QR-code payment system) or credit card. That service supports integration with AI agents and customers' existing applications.

  The second model targets businesses that already own GPUs (graphics processing units, the specialized chips that power AI computation) but are not running them at full efficiency. Under a licensing arrangement, Netra Runtime applies its optimization software to the customer's existing hardware rather than supplying new equipment.

  ## GPU cost as the target problem

  Bisry said the company's optimization focus emerged from direct conversations with prospective customers. "They asked, can AI be on par?" he said, referring to businesses weighing whether AI performance could justify hardware investment.

  GPUs capable of running high-performance AI carry substantial capital expenditure, costs that Bisry said frequently prevent Indonesian companies from pursuing internal AI development. Netra Runtime's approach optimizes a single GPU unit to deliver greater compute throughput, the company said, reducing both power consumption and physical space requirements significantly.

  The company targets businesses across the size spectrum, from small enterprises to large corporations, covering both organizations that cannot afford GPU hardware outright and those that own underutilized units.

  ## Backing and market conditions

  Netra Runtime is among the most recent additions to Init-6's investment portfolio. Deal terms were not disclosed.

  Bisry cited data privacy concerns and high initial investment costs as the primary barriers businesses face when adopting AI in Indonesia and said Netra Runtime was built to address both. Growing AI demand in the country has opened new market opportunities for digital infrastructure providers, the company said.

  Bisry set a target of 30 billion tokens processed per day by year-end, up from a current peak of 12 billion tokens per day.

  ## 印尼AI新創Netra Runtime累計處理9,900億Token，年底衝刺每日300億目標

  獲創投機構Init-6投資的印尼AI推論基礎建設新創公司Netra Runtime，自在印尼正式上線約一個半月以來，已累計處理9,900億個Token（Token是AI語言模型處理文字時的基本單位）。執行長Rifky Bujana Bisry表示，公司正積極朝年底前每日處理300億個Token的目標前進。

  Bisry指出，Netra Runtime目前每日處理量介於60億至120億個Token之間。若要在年底達成目標，必須將現有的每日最高處理量提升逾一倍。

  ## 服務項目與計費架構

  該公司目前提供兩種商業模式。第一種是資料API服務：客戶透過Netra應用程式取得AI運算能力，可透過QRIS（Quick Response Code Indonesian Standard，印尼全國統一的QR Code支付系統）或信用卡儲值點數使用。此服務支援與AI代理程式及客戶現有應用程式的整合。

  第二種模式則鎖定已持有GPU（繪圖處理器，驅動AI運算的專用晶片）、但未能充分發揮硬體效能的企業。在授權合作模式下，Netra Runtime將其最佳化軟體部署至客戶既有的硬體上，無需另行採購新設備。

  ## 以降低GPU成本為核心目標

  Bisry表示，公司之所以將優化效能列為核心方向，源自與潛在客戶的直接交流。「他們問我，AI能否物有所值？」他說，許多企業正在評估AI的表現能否撐起硬體投資的合理性。

  能夠執行高效能AI的GPU需要相當可觀的資本支出，Bisry表示這樣的成本往往讓印尼企業望而卻步，難以推動內部AI開發。Netra Runtime的做法是針對單一GPU進行最佳化，以提升整體運算吞吐量，據公司表示，這套方案能大幅降低電力消耗並節省實體空間。

  公司的目標客群涵蓋各種規模的企業，從中小型到大型企業皆在範疇之內，包括無力購置GPU硬體的組織，以及持有閒置GPU的企業。

  ## 投資背景與市場環境

  Netra Runtime是Init-6近期投資組合中最新加入的成員之一，雙方並未披露交易條款。

  Bisry將資料隱私疑慮與高昂的初期建置成本，列為印尼企業導入AI時面臨的主要障礙，並表示Netra Runtime的創立初衷正是為了同時解決這兩項問題。公司表示，印尼境內日益增長的AI需求，正為數位基礎建設業者開創全新市場機會。

  Bisry設定的年底目標是每日處理300億個Token，相較於目前每日最高120億個Token，幅度超過一倍。
