body_en: |
  The Royal Australasian College of Surgeons (RACS) has completed a migration of its remaining on-premises workloads to HPE Private Cloud, a move driven by a proposed 300% increase in virtualisation licensing fees that also required three years of payment upfront, the organisations involved announced.

  RACS, a not-for-profit body responsible for training, examining and admitting surgeons to fellowship across Australia and New Zealand, expects the new environment to cut annual virtualisation licensing costs by up to 70%. Chief Information Officer Andreas Diamanti said the college projects total savings of approximately AUD $2.1 million over five years across its on-premises estate and disaster recovery site.

  The migration was completed before the licensing renewal deadline without disruption to member services, including systems used for surgical examinations and training records, according to RACS and its partners.

  ## Infrastructure changes

  The new environment runs on HPE's GreenLake platform, a managed-service model that delivers infrastructure on a consumption or subscription basis. The hardware deployed includes HPE Alletra Storage MP B10000 storage arrays, HPE ProLiant Compute DL360 Gen12 servers and HPE Networking switches.

  The project halved the college's physical server count from eight to four. Storage deduplication — a process that eliminates redundant copies of data — reduced the active data footprint from 70 terabytes to approximately 35 terabytes. RACS also retired redundant development environments, shrinking the number of systems requiring patching, maintenance and security oversight. Estimated annual energy savings from the consolidation reach up to 21,000 kilowatt-hours.

  ## Assessment and delivery

  Advisory firm Champions of Change worked alongside RACS and HPE on the evaluation and migration. Alex Danobrega, Account Director at Champions of Change, said the firm spent hundreds of hours reviewing the market and testing options against RACS's technology, security, compliance and commercial requirements before recommending HPE.

  "HPE emerged as the clear choice. The breadth across compute, storage and networking, together with GreenLake, provides RACS with a comprehensive platform," Danobrega said.

  HPE Services and Champions of Change delivered the migration on schedule. Timing was described as operationally significant because RACS examination systems are monitored in real time during exam periods, leaving limited tolerance for unplanned outages.

  ## Context: licensing cost pressure as a migration driver

  The project reflects a pattern visible across mid-sized enterprise IT environments, where sharply higher renewal pricing on incumbent virtualisation platforms — a market segment dominated by a small number of vendors — has prompted organisations to evaluate alternatives. RACS's decision was explicitly reactive: the proposed 300% fee increase, combined with the requirement to commit to three years of payment in advance, made the status quo commercially untenable, according to the source materials.

  The college's review focused on managing long-term IT spending, simplifying operations and improving internal technical capacity rather than on expanding workload scale.

  ## Forward plans

  The HPE Private Cloud deployment forms part of a broader infrastructure roadmap at RACS. The college is considering disaster recovery optimisation and lower-cost cloud storage tiers to reduce ongoing hosting costs further. RACS is also developing its approach to artificial intelligence and digital services intended to reduce administrative friction for members, according to the announcement. Any future workload placement decisions remain subject to the college's governance, security and approval requirements.

  RACS是負責澳洲與紐西蘭外科醫師培訓、考核及院士資格認定的非營利組織。該機構預計，新環境可將每年虛擬化授權費用削減最高70%。資訊長安德烈亞斯‧迪亞曼提（Andreas Diamanti）表示，學院預計五年內在內部基礎架構及災難復原站點合計節省約澳幣210萬元。

  RACS及其合作夥伴表示，此次遷移在授權續約截止日期前順利完成，未對會員服務造成任何中斷，包括外科考試系統及訓練紀錄等相關系統均維持正常運作。

  ## 基礎架構變更

  新環境建置於HPE GreenLake平台之上，採用受管服務模式，以按用量或訂閱制方式提供基礎架構。所部署的硬體包括HPE Alletra Storage MP B10000儲存陣列、HPE ProLiant Compute DL360 Gen12伺服器，以及HPE網路交換器。

  此次專案將學院的實體伺服器數量從八台削減至四台，減少整整一半。儲存重複資料刪除技術——亦即消除資料中冗餘副本的程序——將現行資料佔用量從70 TB壓縮至約35 TB。RACS同時汰除了冗餘的開發環境，縮減了需要修補、維護及資安管理的系統數量。整合後預計每年可節省最高達2萬1千度（千瓦時）的電力消耗。

  ## 評估與導入過程

  顧問公司Champions of Change與RACS及HPE共同參與評估及遷移作業。該公司客戶總監亞歷克斯‧達諾布雷加（Alex Danobrega）表示，公司投入數百小時審視市場，並針對RACS的技術、資安、法規遵循及商業需求逐一檢驗各項選項，最終推薦HPE。

  達諾布雷加表示：「HPE是明確的最佳選擇。其在運算、儲存與網路方面的完整布局，加上GreenLake，為RACS提供了一個全面性的平台。」

  HPE服務部門與Champions of Change如期完成遷移作業。由於RACS的考試系統在考試期間須進行即時監控，對非計畫性停機的容忍度極低，因此遷移時程在業務上具有重要意義。

  ## 背景：授權費用壓力成為遷移主因

  此次專案反映出中型企業IT環境中一個普遍可見的現象——由少數廠商主導的虛擬化平台市場，在合約續約時大幅調漲費用，促使各機構開始評估替代方案。RACS的決策具有明確的被動性：根據相關資料，擬議中的300%費用漲幅，加上須提前承諾支付三年費用的條件，使得維持現狀在商業上已難以為繼。

  學院此次審查的重點在於管控長期IT支出、簡化營運並提升內部技術能力，而非擴大工作負載規模。

  ## 未來規劃

  HPE私有雲部署是RACS更宏觀基礎架構路線圖的一部分。學院正在評估災難復原最佳化及較低成本的雲端儲存層級，以進一步降低持續性代管費用。根據公告，RACS也正在規劃人工智慧及數位服務的發展方向，目標是減少會員在行政作業上的繁瑣程序。任何未來的工作負載配置決策，仍須符合學院的治理、資安及審核要求。
