# AIOps Reduces Workload for 75%, Yet 90% Report Burden From New AI-Related Tasks

While 75% of IT managers reported workload relief from AIOps, a survey found that 90% consider the new AI-generated tasks burdensome. This paradox reveals a critical challenge: AIOps delivers efficiency gains but simultaneously creates new operational overhead.

## 中文

# AIOps 減輕負荷七成五，新工作負擔卻達九成

儘管 75% 導入 AIOps 的資訊部門感受負荷減輕，調查卻顯示約 9 成人員認為 AI 衍生的新任務成為負擔。此矛盾凸顯出 AIOps 的雙面性：提升效率的同時，也創造新的工作挑戰。
