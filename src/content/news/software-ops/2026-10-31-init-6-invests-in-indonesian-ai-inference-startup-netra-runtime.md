body_en: |
  Indonesian venture capital firm Init-6 has invested in Netra Runtime, an AI inference infrastructure startup based in Indonesia that optimizes how artificial intelligence models run on graphics processing units (GPUs) and cloud services.

  The deal, disclosed October 30, positions Init-6 — co-founded by Bukalapak founders Achmad Zaky and Nugroho Herucahyono — in a segment of the AI stack focused on reducing the cost and improving the speed of running AI workloads, rather than on developing the models themselves. Init-6 Founding Partner Nugroho Herucahyono said the central question for enterprises has shifted from which AI model is most capable to what each unit of output costs to produce.

  ## Two service tiers

  Netra offers two deployment models. The first, called private deployment, runs Netra's software on a customer's existing GPU infrastructure, keeping model processing and data under the enterprise's own control. The second, Netra Cloud, provides managed inference — the process of running a trained AI model to generate outputs — through an application programming interface (API) compatible with OpenAI's format, billed on a per-token basis. A token is the basic unit of text or data that AI language models process.

  The two tiers are intended to serve distinct corporate segments, according to the company: enterprises that already own GPU hardware and want to improve its utilization, and enterprises that prefer to access inference capacity through a cloud service without committing to their own hardware.

  ## Usage and performance figures

  Since its launch, Netra reported processing more than 288 billion tokens through October 28, 2026. During the week of October 21 to 27, the platform averaged 6.14 billion tokens per day, with a single-day record of 12.47 billion tokens. As of October 28, the platform had 1,415 registered users and 19 partners.

  On the technical side, Netra reported a prefill throughput — the rate at which the system processes an input prompt before generating a response — of 73,527 input tokens per second at a concurrency level of 32 simultaneous requests, without caching. In decode testing, a configuration of four AMD Instinct MI350X GPUs produced an aggregate output throughput of 5,062 tokens per second at the same concurrency level. At single-user concurrency on the same four-GPU configuration, per-user decode speed reached 1,152 tokens per second. Netra noted that benchmark results depend on the model, hardware, workload, and test configuration used.

  ## Building domestic AI infrastructure

  Nugroho said the investment reflects Init-6's view that AI development in Indonesia has concentrated on consuming existing models and building applications on top of APIs provided by foreign vendors, rather than on the inference infrastructure that underpins those services. He said Init-6's experience building technology at Bukalapak showed that infrastructure efficiency is determined as much by software as by hardware — a lesson he argued is more relevant in the AI era than before.

  By backing inference infrastructure developed domestically, Init-6 said it aims for more of the economic value generated by AI to be built and held by local companies. In the near term, Init-6 said the focus is on demonstrating Netra's performance for enterprise customers before expanding to global markets.
body_zh: |
  印尼創投公司 Init-6 宣布投資 Netra Runtime，這家總部位於印尼的 AI 推論基礎建設新創，專注於最佳化人工智慧模型在繪圖處理器（GPU）與雲端服務上的執行效率。

  此投資案於 10 月 30 日對外揭露，使 Init-6——由 Bukalapak 創辦人 Achmad Zaky 與 Nugroho Herucahyono 共同創立——正式進入 AI 技術棧中一個關鍵賽道：降低推論成本、提升 AI 工作負載執行速度，而非投入模型本身的開發。Init-6 創始合夥人 Nugroho Herucahyono 表示，企業關注的核心問題已從「哪個 AI 模型能力最強」，轉變為「每單位輸出的生產成本究竟是多少」。

  ## 兩種服務層級

  Netra 提供兩種部署模式。第一種稱為「私有部署」，將 Netra 的軟體運行於客戶自有的 GPU 基礎設施之上，使模型處理流程與資料全程保留在企業自身的管控範疇之內。第二種為 Netra Cloud，透過與 OpenAI 格式相容的應用程式介面（API）提供代管推論服務——所謂推論，即運行已訓練完成的 AI 模型以產生輸出結果的過程——並按每個 Token 計費。Token 是 AI 語言模型處理文字或資料的基本單位。

  據該公司說明，這兩種層級旨在服務性質不同的企業客群：一是已擁有 GPU 硬體、希望提升硬體使用率的企業；二是偏好透過雲端服務取用推論運算能力、不願自行購置硬體的企業。

  ## 使用量與效能數據

  Netra 表示，自平台正式推出以來至 2026 年 10 月 28 日止，累計處理的 Token 總數已突破 2,880 億個。在 10 月 21 日至 27 日當週，平台每日平均處理量為 61.4 億個 Token，單日最高紀錄則達 124.7 億個 Token。截至 10 月 28 日，平台共有 1,415 名註冊用戶及 19 家合作夥伴。

  技術層面，Netra 揭示，在無快取、32 個同時請求的並發條件下，預填充吞吐量——即系統在生成回應前處理輸入提示詞的速率——達每秒 73,527 個輸入 Token。在解碼測試中，以四張 AMD Instinct MI350X GPU 組成的配置，在同等並發條件下，總輸出吞吐量達每秒 5,062 個 Token；在同一四 GPU 配置下切換至單一用戶並發時，每位用戶的解碼速度則可達每秒 1,152 個 Token。Netra 並指出，上述基準測試結果會因使用的模型、硬體、工作負載及測試配置不同而有所差異。

  ## 建立本土 AI 基礎建設

  Nugroho 表示，此次投資反映了 Init-6 的核心判斷——印尼的 AI 發展迄今多集中於使用現有模型，以及在外國廠商提供的 API 之上建構應用程式，而非著力於支撐這些服務的推論基礎建設本身。他說，Init-6 在 Bukalapak 打造技術的歷程讓他們深刻體會：基礎建設效率取決於軟體的程度，絕不亞於硬體——而這個道理在 AI 時代比以往任何時候都更加切要。

  藉由支持在印尼本土研發的推論基礎建設，Init-6 表示，希望讓更多 AI 所創造的經濟價值由當地企業建構並留存。短期內，Init-6 表示將聚焦於向企業客戶充分驗證 Netra 的效能表現，再進一步擴展至全球市場。
