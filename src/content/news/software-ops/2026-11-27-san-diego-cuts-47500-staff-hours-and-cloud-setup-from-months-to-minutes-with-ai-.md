body_en: |
  San Diego's city government has saved more than 47,500 work hours and an estimated $3.8 million in labor and operating costs after rolling out generative AI (artificial intelligence) assistants to all 13,000 of its employees and overhauling the underlying cloud network infrastructure, the city disclosed in a recent internal digital transformation review, according to iThome.

  The hour savings translate to staff time formerly consumed by document drafting, meeting transcription, cross-department data retrieval, and other routine clerical tasks that the AI tooling now handles. The city said employees have been able to redirect that capacity toward work requiring human judgment and direct public interaction.

  ## Cloud connection overhaul

  Alongside the AI deployment, the city's information technology department redesigned the architecture used to connect municipal systems to external cloud data centers. The previous setup required configuration work that could stretch to several months each time a department needed a new cloud connection, the city said. After the architectural changes, the same provisioning now completes in minutes, according to the iThome report.

  The faster connection time is intended to give city departments greater flexibility when launching new digital services or reallocating computing resources in response to unexpected demand.

  ## Citizen services

  The city simultaneously updated its open data platform and expanded the functionality of its online public works reporting system. Through backend data integration and an automated dispatch mechanism, the upgraded system is designed to accelerate how quickly city units process resident complaints related to road maintenance and environmental cleaning, the city said.

  ## Significance

  The San Diego results offer a documented case study for large municipal governments weighing generative AI adoption: the city achieved measurable cost reduction at scale — across a workforce of 13,000 — while pairing the software rollout with infrastructure changes that addressed a longstanding bottleneck in cloud service deployment. The combination of AI-driven administrative efficiency and faster infrastructure provisioning positions the city to respond more quickly to new service demands going forward.

  ## 聖地牙哥導入AI節省4.75萬工時，雲端部署從數個月縮短至數分鐘

  根據iThome報導，美國聖地牙哥市政府在一份近期發布的內部數位轉型評估報告中揭露，該市在向全體1萬3千名市府員工全面部署生成式人工智慧（AI）助理工具、並對底層雲端網路基礎架構進行全面整改之後，累計節省逾4萬7千500個工時，以及估計約380萬美元的人力與營運成本。

  所節省的工時，主要來自過去消耗在文件起草、會議紀錄謄寫、跨部門資料查詢及其他例行文書作業上的員工時間——這些工作如今已由AI工具代為處理。市府表示，員工因此得以將騰出的產能重新導向需要人為判斷與直接對外服務的工作。

  ## 雲端連線架構翻新

  在推行AI工具的同時，市府資訊科技部門也重新設計了市政系統連接外部雲端資料中心所使用的架構。市府指出，在架構改造之前，每當某個部門需要建立新的雲端連線，所需的設定作業往往曠日費時，動輒耗費數個月之久。根據iThome的報導，架構改造完成後，相同的建置程序現在只需數分鐘即可完成。

  連線建置速度的大幅提升，旨在賦予各市府部門更大的彈性，以便在推出新數位服務或因應突發需求重新調配運算資源時，能夠更加靈活應對。

  ## 市民服務升級

  與此同時，市府也更新了開放資料平台，並擴充線上公共工程通報系統的功能。透過後端資料整合與自動派工機制，升級後的系統設計目的在於加快市府各單位處理民眾陳情的速度，涵蓋道路養護及環境清潔等相關投訴案件。

  ## 指標意義

  聖地牙哥的實踐成果，為正在評估導入生成式AI的大型市政府提供了一份有據可查的參考案例：該市在1萬3千人規模的員工隊伍中實現了可量化的大規模降本成效，同時將軟體工具的推行與基礎架構改造相互配套，徹底解決了雲端服務部署長期以來的瓶頸問題。AI驅動的行政效率提升，加上更快速的基礎架構建置能力，使該市得以更迅速地回應未來的新興服務需求。
