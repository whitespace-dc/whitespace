body_en: |
  The data center industry's central challenge in artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure has shifted from engineering design to operational execution at scale, according to a panel of executives published by Data Center Frontier on June 26, 2026.

  The publication's Q2 2026 executive roundtable drew responses from Steve Altizer, president and chief executive of Compu Dynamics; Joe Capes, vice president at Trane Technologies and head of LiquidStack; and Robert Danforth, director of engineering, simulation, and advanced development at Rehlko.

  ## From single clusters to standardized fleets

  Altizer said the defining challenge is keeping pace with the rate of change in IT hardware. New GPU (graphics processing unit, the chip that powers AI workloads) families are introduced every 12 to 18 months, he said, and from one generation to the next, rack power densities, meaning the amount of electricity consumed per server cabinet, can double or even triple.

  At prototype scale, a facility can be designed around a single cluster or a specific density profile. At production scale, that approach becomes a liability, Altizer said, because the facility must support today's deployment while remaining adaptable for the next compute profile. He cited the expected move toward direct-current (DC) power delivery and two-phase liquid cooling, a method that removes heat by cycling a refrigerant between liquid and vapor states, as examples of architectural shifts that production facilities must accommodate without knowing precisely when each will arrive.

  "You are not just talking about adding more power," Altizer said. "You are preparing for major architectural shifts."

  ## Repeatability as the production standard

  Capes framed the shift in terms of consistent delivery across multiple sites. A single pilot project can be made to succeed, he said, but the difficulty starts when an organization must replicate those results at other locations while keeping performance, reliability, and timelines on track. At that point, he said, standardization of infrastructure, installation, commissioning, and day-to-day operations becomes much more important.

  "Small issues that don't seem significant in a single deployment can become major problems when you're building at scale," Capes said.

  He said the companies that distinguish themselves over the next several years will not be solely those that can build AI infrastructure, but those that can scale it, support it, and repeat a positive user experience consistently, including globally.

  ## Coordination across the full delivery chain

  Danforth described the production challenge as a coordination requirement spanning design, construction, operations, maintenance, and supply chain teams. A pilot can demonstrate that a system supports AI workloads under controlled conditions, he said, but production environments require maintaining performance and reliability across multiple facilities while accommodating rapidly evolving workloads and growing capacity requirements.

  "Challenges that can be addressed relatively easily in a single deployment become far more significant when they affect multiple facilities," Danforth said.

  He noted that expectations for reliability do not change as deployments expand. Operators must still deliver uptime and performance while managing increasingly dense compute environments, growing power demands, and more complex power architectures, he said.

  ## Modularity as a structural response

  Altizer said strategic modularity, designing facilities in discrete replicable units rather than as single monolithic structures, helps decouple the lifecycle of the building from the lifecycle of the IT hardware. That framework allows a facility to absorb new power and cooling architectures without requiring a full retrofit each time the IT roadmap changes, he said.

  Compu Dynamics Modular is observing this dynamic in current deployments, Altizer said, and the value of a turnkey modular approach is not simply construction speed but the agility that owners need to keep pace with evolving rack densities, power delivery requirements, and cooling architectures.

  ## Industry implications

  All three executives pointed to organizational capability, rather than any single technology, as the variable that will determine which operators succeed as AI buildouts transition from isolated clusters into portfolios of standardized deployments spanning multiple campuses and regions. Danforth said the organizations that succeed will be those that build repeatable operating models while maintaining the reliability that mission-critical environments demand.
body_zh: |
  根據《Data Center Frontier》於2026年6月26日刊出的一場高層主管座談，資料中心產業在人工智慧（AI）基礎建設領域的核心挑戰，已從工程設計層面轉移至大規模的實際執行層面。

  該媒體舉辦的2026年第二季高層圓桌論壇，收錄了Compu Dynamics總裁暨執行長Steve Altizer、Trane Technologies副總裁暨LiquidStack負責人Joe Capes，以及Rehlko工程、模擬與先進開發總監Robert Danforth三人的觀點。

  ## 從單一叢集到標準化機群

  Altizer表示，當前最核心的挑戰在於跟上IT硬體的快速變革步調。他指出，新一代GPU（圖形處理器，即驅動AI運算工作負載的晶片）每12至18個月便推出一次，而每個世代之間，機架功率密度——亦即每座伺服器機櫃的耗電量——可能翻倍，甚至增至三倍之多。

  在原型規模下，一座設施可以針對單一叢集或特定密度規格進行設計。然而進入量產規模後，這種方式反而成了一大隱患。Altizer說，設施必須同時支援當下的部署需求，並保有適應下一代運算規格的彈性。他舉出預期中的直流電（DC）供電架構轉型，以及兩相液冷技術——一種讓冷媒在液態與氣態之間循環以帶走熱能的散熱方式——作為範例，說明量產設施必須在尚無法確切預知各項技術導入時間點的情況下，便事先做好因應這些架構層面重大轉變的準備。

  「你面對的不僅僅是增加更多電力，」Altizer說，「你必須為重大的架構轉型做好準備。」

  ## 可重複執行性作為量產標準

  Capes從跨站點一致交付的角度詮釋這項轉變。他表示，單一試驗性專案是可以做到成功的，但真正的難度在於：當一個組織必須在其他地點複製相同成果，同時維持效能、可靠性與時程進度時，挑戰才真正浮現。到了這個階段，基礎建設、安裝、試運轉以及日常維運的標準化，重要性便遠遠凌駕其他一切。

  「在單一部署中看似微不足道的小問題，當你大規模建設時，可能演變成重大危機，」Capes說。

  他表示，未來數年能夠脫穎而出的企業，不只是那些有能力建構AI基礎建設的業者，而是那些能夠擴大規模、持續支援，並在全球範圍內一致重現優質使用者體驗的組織。

  ## 跨完整交付鏈的協作要求

  Danforth將量產挑戰定義為一項橫跨設計、施工、維運、維護與供應鏈等團隊的協作要求。他說，試驗計畫或許能證明系統在受控條件下可支援AI運算工作負載，但量產環境要求的是在多個設施中持續維持效能與可靠性，同時還要因應快速演進的工作負載與持續增長的容量需求。

  「在單一部署中相對容易處理的挑戰，一旦影響到多個設施，其嚴重程度便會大幅放大，」Danforth說。

  他指出，隨著部署規模擴大，外界對可靠性的期待並不會因此降低。業者必須在管理日益密集的運算環境、不斷攀升的電力需求，以及愈加複雜的電力架構的同時，仍然交出高可用性與高效能的成績單。

  ## 模組化作為結構性的因應策略

  Altizer表示，策略性模組化——亦即將設施設計成由可複製的離散單元組成，而非採用單一的整體式結構——有助於將建築物的生命週期與IT硬體的生命週期脫鉤。他說，這個框架讓設施得以吸納新的電力與冷卻架構，而無需每次IT技術藍圖改變時都進行全面翻新。

  Altizer表示，Compu Dynamics Modular正在現有部署中觀察到這種動態，而交鑰匙模組化方案的價值不僅在於建設速度，更在於業主應對機架密度演進、電力供應需求調整與冷卻架構變革時所需的靈活應變能力。

  ## 產業意涵

  三位高層主管均指出，在AI建設從孤立叢集轉型為跨多個園區與地區的標準化部署組合的過程中，決定哪些業者能夠脫穎而出的關鍵變數，是組織能力，而非任何單一技術。Danforth表示，能夠成功的組織，將是那些在構建可重複運作模式的同時，仍能維持關鍵任務環境所要求之高可靠性的企業。
