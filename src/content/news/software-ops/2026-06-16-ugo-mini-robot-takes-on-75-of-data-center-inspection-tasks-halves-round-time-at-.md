body_en: |
  ugo, a Tokyo-based robotics company, used Interop Tokyo 2026 to present its "ugo mini" autonomous inspection robot as a direct answer to data center labor shortages — deployments already underway have cut daily inspection time by roughly half at facilities including NTT Data's own power equipment rooms.

  The event ran June 10 through 12 at Makuhari Messe in Chiba, Japan.

  ## Projected technician shortage pushes operators toward automation

  ugo says the number of facility maintenance technicians in Japan is on track to fall by 260,000 from 2000 levels by 2030 and by 400,000 by 2045 — a pace of decline more than 1.5 times faster than the overall working-age population. In parallel, the Japanese government has been abolishing what the company calls "analog regulations," rules that mandate visual, on-site inspections by people, removing a regulatory barrier to robotic substitution.

  ## ugo mini specifications

  ugo mini is a cylindrical unit weighing approximately 16 kilograms. Its central feature is a telescoping vertical pole that extends from a base height of 68 centimeters to a maximum of 186 centimeters — tall enough to reach near-ceiling instruments while also angling a camera downward to capture floor-level piping. The 4K camera mounted at the pole tip adjusts up and down as needed.

  The chassis carries a 3D LiDAR — a sensor that fires laser pulses to measure distances to surrounding objects — enabling the robot to navigate autonomously around obstacles using a pre-loaded facility map. A USB port at the pole tip accepts plug-in attachments including thermal cameras and environmental sensors capable of measuring temperature, humidity, and carbon dioxide. ugo says the design is low-noise and low-dust, meeting the cleanliness requirements typical of data center environments.

  In operation, ugo mini patrols autonomously, captures imagery with its 4K camera, and passes the footage to third-party AI software that reads meter values and converts them to structured data. The system then generates inspection reports automatically.

  ## Measured results at live deployments

  At one unnamed data center's electrical and mechanical room, ugo says the ugo product line — which includes ugo mini — now handles 75 percent of all inspection line items and has cut total inspection time by half.

  NTT Data has deployed the ugo series in power equipment rooms at its own data centers, reducing daily inspection time by approximately 50 percent and enabling remote responses to anomalies detected during overnight and other low-staffing periods. That collaboration received the Minister of Internal Affairs and Communications Award at Japan's Infrastructure Maintenance Award program in 2025, which recognizes outstanding infrastructure maintenance practices.

  ## ugo Pro for multi-floor facilities

  For larger plants and office buildings spanning multiple floors, ugo offers a higher-specification model called "ugo Pro." The unit stands 180 centimeters tall and weighs approximately 54 kilograms. Two robotic arms allow it to operate card readers and press elevator call buttons, enabling autonomous floor-to-floor movement. Beyond inspection, the Pro can also perform security patrol, visitor guidance, and cargo transport duties.

  ## Outlook

  The results at NTT Data and the unnamed data center provide quantified evidence for autonomous inspection in a sector where round-the-clock staffing is costly and increasingly difficult to sustain. ugo projects Japan's maintenance technician workforce will be 400,000 smaller in 2045 than it was in 2000 — a shortfall that, absent automation, facility operators would need to absorb through other means.

  本屆展會於6月10日至12日在日本千葉縣幕張展覽館舉行。

  ## 維修技術人員短缺，業者加速擁抱自動化

  ugo指出，日本的設施維護技術員人數預計到2030年將比2000年時減少26萬人，到2045年更將減少40萬人，其下降速度是整體勞動年齡人口萎縮速度的1.5倍以上。與此同時，日本政府正陸續廢除該公司所稱的「類比法規」——也就是強制要求人員現場目視點檢的規定——從而掃除了以機器人取代人力的法規障礙。

  ## ugo mini 規格

  ugo mini是一款圓柱形機器人，重約16公斤，其核心設計為一根可垂直伸縮的升降桿，底部高度為68公分，最高可延伸至186公分，足以觸及靠近天花板的儀器，同時也能將鏡頭向下俯攝，拍攝地板附近的管線。安裝於桿頂的4K攝影機可依需要上下調整角度。

  機身底盤搭載3D光達（LiDAR，一種透過發射雷射脈衝來測量周圍物體距離的感測器），讓機器人能依據預先載入的場地地圖，自主閃避障礙物進行導航。桿頂備有USB插孔，可外接熱像儀及能量測溫度、濕度與二氧化碳濃度的環境感測器等擴充模組。ugo表示，這款機器人採低噪音、低揚塵設計，符合資料中心環境對潔淨度的一般要求。

  在運作模式上，ugo mini自主執行巡邏並以4K攝影機拍攝影像，再將畫面傳送至第三方AI軟體，由其讀取儀表數值並轉換為結構化資料，系統最終自動生成巡檢報告。

  ## 實際部署的量化成果

  在某未具名資料中心的電機室，ugo表示ugo產品線（涵蓋ugo mini在內）目前已承攬全部巡檢項目的75%，並將整體巡檢時間縮短了一半。

  NTT Data已在自有資料中心的電力設備室導入ugo系列機器人，每日巡檢時間同樣減少約50%，並可在深夜等人力配置較少的時段，針對機器人偵測到的異常即時進行遠端處置。此項合作計畫於2025年榮獲日本「基礎設施維護獎」總務大臣獎，該獎項專門表彰卓越的基礎設施維護實踐。

  ## 適合多樓層設施的 ugo Pro

  針對涵蓋多個樓層的大型廠房及辦公大樓，ugo提供更高規格的機型「ugo Pro」。這款機器人高180公分，重約54公斤，配備兩條機械臂，可操作門禁讀卡機並按下電梯呼叫按鈕，從而實現跨樓層自主移動。除巡檢任務外，ugo Pro還能執行安全巡邏、訪客引導及貨物搬運等工作。

  ## 展望

  NTT Data及前述未具名資料中心的實測成果，為自主巡檢在全天候人力成本高昂、且愈來愈難以為繼的資料中心產業提供了量化佐證。ugo預測，到2045年，日本的維護技術員人力將比2000年少40萬人——若缺乏自動化方案，設施營運商將別無選擇，只能以其他方式填補這一人力缺口。
