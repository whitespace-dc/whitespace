  ## PC Brands Pivot to AI Servers as H2 Demand Outlook Dims

  PC brand manufacturers are countering an expected weakening in PC market demand during the second half of 2026 by leaning on AI server shipments and broader product diversification, according to two reports published June 14–15 by Taiwan's Economic Daily News (經濟日報).

  The reports, both written by reporter Wu Kai-chung in Taipei, identify two primary strategies companies are deploying: building out diversified product portfolios and accelerating AI server sales. Manufacturers that positioned themselves early in the server market are now benefiting from AI server shipping momentum strong enough to offset softer PC sales, the first report states.

  Dell is cited as a representative example of this approach. The U.S. vendor's early investment in server infrastructure, according to Economic Daily News, places it in a position where AI server demand can compensate for any PC revenue shortfall in the back half of the year.

  ## ASUS Faces Component Cost Pressures but Stock Tests NT$1,000

  ASUS (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 2357) presents a more complex picture. The company is contending with shortages and price increases across key components — including memory, CPUs, and SSDs (solid-state drives, the storage chips widely used in PCs and servers) — in 2026, the second report notes. Despite that input cost pressure, ASUS shares have continued rising and approached the NT$1,000-per-share level.

  The Economic Daily News attributed the stock's resilience to market expectations around two drivers: ASUS's positioning in Agentic AI — an approach in which AI systems operate with greater autonomy to complete multi-step tasks — and anticipated strong performance from both its AI server and PC business lines this year.

  ASUS's ability to challenge that share price threshold despite component cost headwinds signals that investors are weighing AI server growth prospects heavily against near-term supply chain pressures, based on the reporting.

  ## Industry Implications

  The two cases together illustrate a structural shift underway among traditional PC brand manufacturers: AI server revenue is becoming a material hedge against cyclical PC softness. Companies with established server businesses are better insulated from consumer PC demand swings than those without.

  ASUS's AI server and PC business are both expected to produce strong results in 2026, according to Economic Daily News, with the Agentic AI product segment drawing particular investor attention going into the second half of the year.

  ## PC品牌大廠轉進AI伺服器，因應下半年需求展望趨淡

  根據台灣《經濟日報》於6月14日至15日接連刊出的兩篇報導，PC品牌製造商正透過布建多元化產品組合、加速AI伺服器出貨等雙重策略，積極因應2026年下半年PC市場需求預期走軟的壓力。

  上述兩篇報導均由駐台北記者吳凱中撰寫，文中點出業者目前主要採行兩大策略：一是擴充多元化產品陣線，二是加速推進AI伺服器銷售。第一篇報導指出，提早布局伺服器市場的廠商，如今正受惠於強勁的AI伺服器出貨動能，足以彌補PC銷售趨緩所造成的缺口。

  報導以戴爾（Dell）作為此一策略的代表性案例。《經濟日報》指出，這家美國大廠早年在伺服器基礎建設上的投資，如今使其具備以AI伺服器需求補足PC營收缺口的能力，在今年下半年的市況中占據有利位置。

  ## 華碩面臨零組件成本壓力，股價挑戰千元關卡

  華碩（台灣證券交易所代號：2357）的處境則更為複雜。第二篇報導指出，該公司在2026年面臨多項關鍵零組件短缺與漲價的挑戰，涵蓋記憶體、CPU及SSD（固態硬碟，即廣泛用於個人電腦與伺服器的儲存晶片）。儘管面臨上游成本壓力，華碩股價仍持續走揚，並逼近每股新台幣1,000元的整數關卡。

  《經濟日報》將股價的韌性歸因於市場對兩大題材的預期：一是華碩在「代理型AI」（Agentic AI）領域的布局——此為一種AI系統以更高自主性完成多步驟任務的技術路徑——二是市場預期華碩今年在AI伺服器與PC兩條業務線上均將繳出亮眼成績。

  華碩股價在零組件成本逆風下仍能挑戰千元門檻，顯示投資人正將AI伺服器的成長前景，與近期供應鏈壓力相互權衡，且前者的分量明顯更重，此為上述報導所呈現的訊息。

  ## 產業意涵

  綜合這兩個案例，可以看出傳統PC品牌製造商正在經歷一場結構性轉變：AI伺服器營收正逐漸成為對抗PC景氣循環波動的實質避險工具。已建立伺服器事業規模的廠商，在承受消費性PC需求震盪時，明顯比尚未布局的業者更具抗壓能力。

  《經濟日報》報導，華碩的AI伺服器與PC業務在2026年均預期將有強勁表現，其中代理型AI產品線更是在下半年進入倒數之際，吸引投資人格外關注的焦點所在。
