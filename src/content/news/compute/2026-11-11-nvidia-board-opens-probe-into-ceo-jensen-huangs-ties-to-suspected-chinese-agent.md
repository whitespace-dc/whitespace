body_en: |
  Nvidia's board on November 8 launched an independent investigation into whether Chief Executive Jensen Huang's relationship with a former executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed International posed a national security or corporate security risk, after US authorities reportedly suspected the individual of working for China.

  A committee of independent directors, working with outside counsel, will oversee the inquiry. The board said it had no reason to believe US national security or Nvidia's proprietary information had been compromised. Huang acknowledged in a message to employees that he had been unfaithful to his wife, apologizing to his family, colleagues, and shareholders, and said he remained fully committed to Nvidia and to supporting US national security.

  The company moved quickly to signal leadership continuity. "Today's news will not slow Nvidia. Jensen is committed to continuing to lead Nvidia as CEO without interruption," spokesperson John Rizzo said, according to the Associated Press.

  ## Huang's role in AI infrastructure investment

  Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and has led the company's transformation from a graphics processor maker into the dominant supplier of accelerators used to train and run artificial intelligence models. Hyperscalers — large-scale cloud operators — cloud providers, and AI developers are making multiyear commitments to Nvidia technology. Those decisions influence data center construction, power procurement, cooling systems, networking deployments, and semiconductor manufacturing capacity.

  An investigation alone would not automatically disrupt those commitments. Nvidia has an established engineering organization, manufacturing relationships, and a large executive team, and its customers have made substantial investments in the company's software and hardware ecosystem. Replacing Huang could, however, introduce uncertainty around the company's strategic direction and its relationships with major customers and suppliers.

  ## Huang's industry relationships seen as hard to replace

  Steven Dickens, chief executive and principal analyst at HyperFrame Research, said Huang's value to Nvidia extends beyond his role as CEO. His relationships with senior technology executives, including Dell Technologies CEO Michael Dell and leaders at major hyperscalers, have developed over decades and would be difficult for a successor to replicate, Dickens said.

  "Those relationships are based on decades," Dickens said. "His relationships with those senior leaders are ridiculously hard to replicate."

  Those relationships carry direct commercial value, helping Nvidia maintain partnerships and coordinate strategy with major customers, Dickens said. He argued that Nvidia's board should evaluate Huang's continued leadership based on his contributions and the company's long-term interests, provided the investigations find no legal wrongdoing or national security implications.

  Jeffrey Sonnenfeld, senior associate dean for leadership studies at Yale School of Management, raised a separate concern: whether the board has adequately prepared for the possibility of an emergency leadership transition. "There's been a failure to develop just a succession plan for a crisis, let alone for the future," Sonnenfeld said.

  ## China ties add a national security dimension

  The investigation arrives amid a prolonged dispute over Nvidia's access to China's AI computing market. US export controls have restricted China's access to Nvidia's most advanced AI processors since 2022. During Nvidia's May 2025 earnings call, Huang described China as a $50 billion AI market effectively closed to US industry following restrictions on the company's H20 processors.

  "The question is not whether China will have AI. It already does," Huang said at that time.

  Stephen Sopko, practice lead for semiconductors and deep technology at HyperFrame Research, drew a distinction between the two strands of the investigation. "The personal failing, while objectionable, is often survivable in global business," Sopko said. "But the intelligence operative angle, if substantiated, is an entirely different dimension given Nvidia's criticality to the current US lead over China in AI."

  ## Potential effects on the semiconductor supply chain

  A prolonged leadership crisis could introduce uncertainty across the semiconductor supply chain. Nvidia relies on Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing for leading-edge processors, while memory suppliers including Micron, Samsung, and SK Hynix provide components essential to its accelerated computing systems. Networking and optical-component vendors also benefit from Nvidia's expanding data center platform.

  A leadership change could prompt customers to examine their exposure to a single technology supplier more closely. AMD, Broadcom, and other competitors are already pursuing opportunities in AI accelerators, custom silicon, and networking. Nvidia's CUDA software ecosystem — the software platform that underpins its AI computing hardware — its developer base, engineering capabilities, and integrated hardware platform would remain in place under a new CEO, with competitors still facing the challenge of convincing customers to move workloads away from Nvidia's established infrastructure.

  Sopko said he would be surprised if succession planning were not already part of Nvidia's strategy, given the company's forward-looking investments in technologies such as photonics and software that could otherwise constrain AI deployment. "AI is just too useful in every stratum of the economy to be stopped or even significantly delayed," he said.

  The independent board committee's investigation is ongoing.

  ## 輝達董事會啟動獨立調查

  輝達董事會於11月8日宣布啟動獨立調查，審視執行長黃仁勳與新加坡美加速國際公司一名前高管的關係，是否對國家安全或公司機密資訊構成威脅。美國當局據報懷疑該名人士替中國從事情報活動。

  此次調查將由獨立董事組成的委員會主導，並邀請外部法律顧問協助。董事會表示，目前無任何跡象顯示美國國家安全或輝達的專有資訊遭到洩漏。黃仁勳在一封致全體員工的信件中承認對妻子不忠，向家人、同事及股東致歉，並強調自己將全力以赴，繼續領導輝達，同時支持美國國家安全相關工作。

  公司迅速採取行動，強調領導層不會出現空窗。輝達發言人約翰·里佐（John Rizzo）據美聯社引述表示：「今日的消息不會讓輝達放慢腳步。黃仁勳將持續擔任執行長，不會中斷。」

  ## 黃仁勳在AI基礎建設投資中的角色

  黃仁勳於1993年共同創辦輝達，帶領公司從繪圖處理器製造商轉型為人工智慧模型訓練與推論加速器的主導供應商。大型雲端業者（超大規模資料中心業者）、雲端服務供應商及AI開發商，紛紛與輝達簽訂多年期技術採購承諾。這些決策牽動資料中心建設、電力採購、散熱系統、網路部署及半導體製造產能等各個環節。

  單是啟動調查，並不會自動動搖這些採購承諾。輝達擁有完善的工程組織、成熟的製造合作關係，以及龐大的高管團隊，客戶也已在輝達的軟硬體生態系統中投入大量資源。然而，若黃仁勳遭到替換，外界對公司策略方向及其與主要客戶、供應商關係的不確定性，恐怕難以避免。

  ## 黃仁勳的業界人脈難以取代

  HyperFrame Research執行長暨首席分析師史蒂芬·狄肯斯（Steven Dickens）指出，黃仁勳對輝達的價值遠不止於執行長一職。他與科技界高層的關係——包括戴爾科技執行長麥可·戴爾（Michael Dell）及各大超大規模業者的領導人——均是歷經數十年積累而成，任何接班人都難以複製。

  「那些關係是幾十年建立起來的，」狄肯斯說，「他與那些高層之間的交情，要複製難如登天。」

  這些關係具有直接的商業價值，有助輝達維繫合作夥伴關係，並與主要客戶協調策略，狄肯斯說。他認為，只要調查結果未發現任何法律違規或國家安全疑慮，輝達董事會應依據黃仁勳對公司的貢獻及長遠利益，評估其是否繼續擔任領導人。

  耶魯大學管理學院領導力研究資深副院長傑弗里·桑能菲爾德（Jeffrey Sonnenfeld）則提出另一層憂慮：董事會是否已充分準備好應對緊急接班的可能性。「不要說為未來規劃接班，就連應對危機的接班計畫，都付之闕如，」桑能菲爾德說。

  ## 中國因素帶來國安層面的疑慮

  此次調查的發生，正值輝達與中國AI運算市場之間的爭議持續延燒之際。自2022年起，美國出口管制已限制中國取得輝達最先進的AI處理器。在輝達2025年5月的法說會上，黃仁勳形容中國是一個規模達500億美元的AI市場，卻因H20處理器遭到出口管制而實質上對美國業者關上大門。

  「問題不在於中國是否會擁有AI——它已經有了，」黃仁勳當時表示。

  HyperFrame Research半導體與深科技業務主管史蒂芬·索普科（Stephen Sopko）對此次調查的兩個面向作出區分。「私生活上的過失，雖然令人不齒，但在全球商業界往往是可以度過的，」索普科說，「但若情報人員滲透的指控獲得證實，那就完全是另一個層次的問題了，因為輝達對美國在AI領域保持領先中國的態勢至關重要。」

  ## 對半導體供應鏈的潛在衝擊

  一旦領導層危機延長，可能在整條半導體供應鏈中引發不確定性。輝達依賴台灣積體電路製造（台積電）生產尖端處理器，同時仰賴美光、三星及SK海力士等記憶體供應商提供加速運算系統所需的關鍵元件。網路設備及光學元件廠商也因輝達不斷擴展的資料中心平台而受惠。

  領導層更迭或許促使客戶更仔細檢視自身對單一技術供應商的依賴程度。超微（AMD）、博通（Broadcom）及其他競爭對手，已紛紛在AI加速器、客製晶片及網路設備領域積極布局。不過，輝達的CUDA軟體生態系統——其AI運算硬體的底層軟體平台——加上其龐大的開發者社群、工程能力及整合硬體平台，在新執行長接手後仍會繼續存在，競爭對手要說服客戶將工作負載遷離輝達既有基礎設施，依然面臨嚴峻挑戰。

  索普科表示，考量輝達在光子學、軟體等前瞻技術上的長期布局——這些技術若無規劃恐將制約AI的部署——他相信接班規劃早已納入輝達的整體策略之中。「AI在每個經濟層面都太有用了，不可能因此停下腳步，甚至明顯放緩，」他說。

  獨立董事委員會的調查目前仍在進行中。
