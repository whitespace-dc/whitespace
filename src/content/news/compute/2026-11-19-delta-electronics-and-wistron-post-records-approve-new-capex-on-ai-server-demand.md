  Delta Electronics (ticker: 2308) and Wistron (ticker: 3231) each reported record quarterly revenue for the third quarter of 2026, driven by sustained AI data center demand for power equipment and servers, and both companies approved fresh capital expenditure to expand capacity.

  ## Delta Electronics: Record Revenue and Expanding Power Solutions

  Delta Electronics, a supplier of power and thermal management equipment for data centers, posted third-quarter revenue of NT$197.059 billion, a new single-quarter record. First-three-quarter earnings per share (EPS — net profit divided by shares outstanding) reached NT$26.64, already surpassing the company's full-year 2025 figure, according to the Commercial Times.

  October revenue climbed to NT$74.666 billion, up 14.2 percent from September and 30.1 percent from October 2025, setting a new single-month record. Cumulative revenue for the first ten months of 2026 reached NT$614.334 billion. The company attributed the growth to continued demand for AI data center power and infrastructure products.

  Delta said at its third-quarter analyst meeting that some inventory was affected by customer order timing adjustments, specification changes, and supplier lead times, and the company expects this inventory to clear gradually beginning in the fourth quarter. Delta also projected fourth-quarter revenue above third-quarter levels, with gross margin no lower than the third-quarter rate.

  The company's expansion is directed toward complete power and cooling solutions for AI data centers, including high-voltage direct-current (HVDC) power supply systems and liquid cooling — technologies suited to the higher power densities of modern AI server racks. Domestic investment advisors cited in the Commercial Times report said demand for high-efficiency power delivery and liquid cooling continues to grow as AI cabinet power density rises, and that Delta's expansion from discrete components to system-level solutions retains room for further growth.

  ## Wistron: AI Servers Drive Triple-Digit Revenue Growth

  Wistron, a contract manufacturer of servers and personal computers, posted third-quarter revenue of NT$1.15 trillion, up 28.3 percent from the second quarter and 102.4 percent from the same period in 2025 — a record for both revenue and profit in a single quarter. First-three-quarter EPS totaled NT$13.54, already exceeding the company's full-year 2025 figure.

  October revenue reached NT$402.615 billion, up 117.6 percent year-on-year, the second-highest single month in the company's history. Cumulative revenue for the first ten months of 2026 stood at NT$3.2934 trillion, up 99.5 percent from the same period in 2025. The company said AI server shipments have become the primary growth driver, offsetting the traditional slow season for personal computers.

  ## Wistron Approves Multi-Site Capital Expenditure

  To support AI business expansion, Wistron approved two capital expenditure items in November 2026: NT$11.9 billion for facility improvements and equipment procurement at its Tainan plant, and NT$939 million for the same purposes at its Hsinchu facility. The company's subsidiaries in the United States and Mexico also approved US$17.76 million in additional capital expenditure to increase server production capacity.

  ## What It Means for the Industry

  The simultaneous revenue records and capital commitments at Delta Electronics and Wistron illustrate the breadth of AI data center supply chain investment across two distinct layers: the power infrastructure layer, where Delta is expanding into higher-voltage DC and liquid-cooled systems, and the server manufacturing layer, where Wistron is building out capacity across Taiwan and North America.

  Wistron's November approval of capital expenditure across three geographies — Tainan, Hsinchu, and North America — represents the company's most recent committed step toward expanding AI server production capacity.

  ## 台達電與緯創創歷史新高，AI伺服器需求推動新一輪資本支出

  台達電（股票代號：2308）與緯創（股票代號：3231）雙雙繳出2026年第三季單季歷史最高營收，兩家公司均受惠於AI資料中心對電源設備與伺服器的持續強勁需求，並相繼核准新增資本支出以擴充產能。

  ## 台達電：營收創新高，電源解決方案持續擴展

  身為資料中心電源與散熱管理設備供應商的台達電，第三季營收達新台幣1,970.59億元，刷新單季歷史紀錄。據《工商時報》報導，前三季每股稅後盈餘（EPS）累計達新台幣26.64元，已超越公司2025年全年水準。

  10月單月營收攀升至新台幣746.66億元，較9月成長14.2％、較2025年10月大幅成長30.1％，同步寫下單月歷史新高。2026年前十月累計營收達新台幣6,143.34億元。公司表示，AI資料中心電源與基礎設施產品的持續需求為主要成長動能。

  台達電在第三季法說會上說明，部分庫存受到客戶訂單時程調整、規格變更及供應商交期等因素影響，預計相關庫存將從第四季起逐步去化。公司並預估第四季營收將優於第三季，毛利率亦不低於第三季水準。

  在業務擴展方向上，台達電聚焦於AI資料中心完整電源與冷卻解決方案，涵蓋高壓直流（HVDC）電源供應系統與液冷技術，這些技術皆適用於現代AI伺服器機架日益提升的高功率密度需求。《工商時報》引述國內投資顧問指出，隨著AI機櫃功率密度持續攀升，高效率電力傳輸與液冷需求仍在擴大，台達電從單一元件延伸至系統級解決方案的策略，仍具備進一步成長空間。

  ## 緯創：AI伺服器帶動營收年增逾一倍

  伺服器與個人電腦委託代工廠商緯創，第三季營收達新台幣1.15兆元，較第二季成長28.3％，較2025年同期大幅成長102.4％，單季營收與獲利均創下歷史新高。前三季EPS累計達新台幣13.54元，同樣已超越2025年全年水準。

  10月單月營收達新台幣4,026.15億元，年增117.6％，為公司史上單月次高紀錄。2026年前十月累計營收達新台幣3兆2,934億元，較2025年同期成長99.5％。公司表示，AI伺服器出貨已成為主要成長動能，足以抵銷個人電腦傳統淡季的影響。

  ## 緯創核准多地點資本支出

  為支應AI業務擴張，緯創於2026年11月陸續核准兩項資本支出案：一是斥資新台幣119億元用於台南廠區的廠房改善與設備採購；二是投入新台幣9.39億元用於新竹廠區的相同用途。此外，緯創在美國與墨西哥的子公司亦核准追加1,776萬美元的資本支出，以提升伺服器生產產能。

  ## 產業意涵

  台達電與緯創同步創下營收新高並作出資本承諾，清楚呈現AI資料中心供應鏈投資橫跨兩個截然不同層次的廣度：其一是電源基礎設施層，台達電正朝更高電壓的直流電源系統與液冷系統擴展；其二是伺服器製造層，緯創則持續在台灣與北美擴充產能。

  緯創11月跨台南、新竹及北美三地核准資本支出，是該公司迄今最新一步擴大AI伺服器生產能力的具體承諾。
