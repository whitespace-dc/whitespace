body_en: |
  Google's flagship AI model Gemini 3.5 Pro has slipped several months behind its planned launch date as the company continues to refine the model's code-generation performance, denting investor confidence in AI sector earnings and sending shares of Taiwan's leading AI server manufacturers sharply lower, according to wire reports cited by Taiwan's Economic Daily News on July 17.

  ## Single-day market cap losses

  The combined market capitalization of Taiwan's AI server original design manufacturers (ODMs, contract firms that design and build servers sold under cloud providers' procurement programs) fell NT$297 billion in a single trading session. Foxconn (TWSE: 2317) recorded the largest individual loss, shedding NT$119.2 billion in market value as its capitalization dropped from NT$3.40 trillion to approximately NT$3.28 trillion. Quanta Computer, Wistron, Inventec, and Wiwynn also saw their shares come under pressure over the same period.

  ## Institutional investors maintain positive outlook

  Despite the selloff, institutional investors cited by the Economic Daily News said the sector's operational fundamentals remain sound. Order visibility for Taiwan's AI server ODMs is nearly confirmed through 2028, they said. North America's five largest cloud service providers (CSPs, companies operating large-scale public cloud infrastructure such as data center compute, storage, and networking) have not changed their AI server procurement expansion plans, according to the same institutional commentary. ODM companies are expected to post new revenue records in 2026, with 2027 performance seen reaching further highs.

  ## Nvidia Vera Rubin production ramp

  NVIDIA's Vera Rubin AI computing platform is scheduled to enter mass production in the second half of 2026, the report said. The first customers for Vera Rubin-based servers will include North America's top five CSPs. Institutional investors said that production ramp is expected to generate broadly distributed business for Foxconn, Quanta, Wistron, Compal, and Inventec, with all five manufacturers seen as positioned to capture orders from the program.

  ## Significance

  The Gemini 3.5 Pro delay illustrates how setbacks in frontier model development can move share prices across the hardware supply chain that builds the servers used to train and run those models, even when the underlying procurement programs remain intact. For Taiwan's ODMs, the tension between near-term sentiment and a multi-year order book that extends to 2028 is likely to define how investors weigh the group until the Vera Rubin production ramp, scheduled for the second half of 2026, begins generating visible revenue.

  Google旗艦AI模型Gemini 3.5 Pro在持續調校程式碼生成效能的過程中，已較原定上市時程落後數月，削弱了投資人對AI產業獲利的信心，並引發台灣主要AI伺服器製造商股價急劇走低。根據《經濟日報》7月17日引述外電報導。

  ## 單日市值損失

  台灣AI伺服器委託設計製造廠商（ODM，即由代工廠自行設計並製造、再透過雲端業者採購計畫銷售的伺服器廠商）合計市值在單一交易日內蒸發2,970億元新台幣。其中鴻海（台股：2317）個股損失最為慘重，市值縮水1,192億元，從約3兆4,000億元下滑至約3兆2,800億元。廣達電腦、緯創資通、英業達及緯穎科技同期股價亦相繼承壓走低。

  ## 法人維持正面展望

  儘管股市出現賣壓，《經濟日報》引述法人表示，產業營運基本面依然穩健。台灣AI伺服器ODM廠商的訂單能見度已幾近確認至2028年。同一法人指出，北美五大雲端服務業者（CSP，即營運大規模公有雲基礎建設、涵蓋資料中心運算、儲存及網路服務的企業）並未改變AI伺服器採購擴張計畫。ODM廠商預計2026年將繳出創新高的營收成績，2027年表現更有望再創新高。

  ## 輝達Vera Rubin量產時程

  輝達（NVIDIA）Vera Rubin AI運算平台預計於2026年下半年進入量產。首批採用Vera Rubin架構伺服器的客戶將涵蓋北美前五大CSP。法人表示，此波量產拉貨預料將廣泛嘉惠鴻海、廣達、緯創、仁寶及英業達，五家廠商均被視為具備承接相關訂單的有利條件。

  ## 市場意義

  Gemini 3.5 Pro的延遲清楚說明，前沿模型開發出現挫折，如何波及負責建造訓練與運行這些模型所需伺服器的整條硬體供應鏈——即便相關採購計畫本身並未生變。對台灣ODM廠商而言，短期市場情緒與延伸至2028年的多年期訂單能見度之間的張力，預計將持續左右投資人對這類股票的評價，直到預定於2026年下半年啟動的Vera Rubin量產開始貢獻可見的營收為止。
