body_en: |
  NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang's return to an active leadership role has temporarily relieved market anxiety about the transition timeline for next-generation AI platforms, while institutional analysts forecast 2027 global server shipments will reach 25.2 million units, a 24% year-on-year increase, Commercial Times reported Tuesday.

  The demand picture nonetheless remains a subject of scrutiny. Markets had been weighing the risks of slowing data center construction and financing pressures on Neoclouds — emerging infrastructure companies that lease GPU capacity to AI developers — the report said.

  ## GPU and ASIC Shipment Projections

  GPU servers — machines built around graphics processing units for AI training and inference — are forecast to grow 47% in 2027 unit shipments. ASIC servers, which use application-specific integrated circuits custom-designed by hyperscale cloud operators for particular AI workloads, are projected to grow 109%.

  NVIDIA's AI server cabinet shipments are estimated to rise from approximately 80,000 cabinets in 2026 to 100,000 in 2027. The VR200 platform, NVIDIA's next-generation rack configuration, is expected to account for 70,000 of those cabinets.

  ## Component Cost Uplift Favors Taiwanese Supply Chain

  The transition from the current GB300 (Grace Blackwell 300) platform to VR200 carries substantial per-cabinet component cost increases. Industry estimates cited by Commercial Times indicate the VR200 adds 90% to per-cabinet IC substrate costs — the interconnect layer that bonds chips to circuit boards — 71% to PCB (printed circuit board) costs, 70% to power components, and 30% to cooling compared with GB300.

  Those increases are expected to sustain growth momentum for Taiwanese supply chain companies: Delta Electronics in power, Auras Technology in cooling, Unimicron Technology and Kinsus Interconnect Technology in substrates, and Gold Circuit Electronics in PCB.

  ## Custom Chip Demand Accelerates; TSMC Reallocates Packaging Capacity

  ASIC demand is broadening across hyperscale cloud operators. Google's TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) server shipments are forecast to grow 117% in 2027; AWS Trainium server volumes are projected to rise 58%. Microsoft, Meta, and OpenAI custom ASIC programs are also expected to contribute incremental shipments.

  TSMC, the foundry that manufactures both NVIDIA's GPUs and cloud operators' custom AI chips, is expected to rebalance its CoWoS capacity — an advanced packaging technology that bonds multiple chips into a single high-performance package — from a roughly 6:4 GPU-to-ASIC split in 2026 toward an approximately 5:5 ratio in 2027, according to market estimates in the report.

  ## Demand May Outstrip Supply

  Downstream supply chain sources cited in the report said customers have collectively planned more than 50 gigawatts of data center capacity for 2027, equivalent to more than 250,000 AI server cabinets. Full fulfillment of that demand is considered unlikely, given potential shortages in power components, memory, CPUs, substrates, and PCBs, alongside foundry capacity constraints and customer financing requirements.

  ## Quarterly Cadence and Rubin Ultra Timing

  GPU cabinet shipments in the first quarter of 2027 are expected to be the full-year low, reflecting platform transition timing and the Lunar New Year production slowdown. Shipments are forecast to grow sequentially in the second and third quarters before easing in the fourth.

  NVIDIA's Rubin Ultra platform, the generation following Rubin, is estimated to ship approximately 3,000 cabinets in 2027, with the main shipment ramp expected to shift into 2028.
body_zh: |
  輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳重返積極的領導崗位，暫時緩解了市場對次世代AI平台轉換時程的疑慮。法人分析師預測，2027年全球伺服器出貨量將達2,520萬台，年增24%，工商時報週二報導。

  儘管如此，需求前景仍受到市場密切關注。報導指出，市場先前已在評估資料中心建設放緩的風險，以及Neocloud業者所面臨的融資壓力——所謂Neocloud，是指將GPU運算能力租賃給AI開發商的新興基礎設施業者。

  ## GPU與ASIC出貨量展望

  GPU伺服器——以圖形處理器為核心、專為AI訓練與推論所設計的機器——預計2027年出貨量將成長47%。ASIC伺服器採用由超大規模雲端業者針對特定AI工作負載客製化設計的特殊應用積體電路（ASIC），預計出貨量將成長109%。

  輝達AI伺服器機櫃出貨量預計將從2026年的約8萬個機櫃，成長至2027年的10萬個。其中，輝達次世代機架配置VR200平台預計將佔其中7萬個機櫃。

  ## 零組件成本攀升，台灣供應鏈受惠

  從現行GB300（Grace Blackwell 300）平台轉換至VR200，每個機櫃的零組件成本將大幅提升。工商時報引述業界估計，相較於GB300，VR200每個機櫃的IC載板成本——即連接晶片與電路板的互連層——將增加90%，印刷電路板（PCB）成本增加71%，電源零組件成本增加70%，散熱成本則增加30%。

  這些成本增幅預計將持續帶動台灣供應鏈廠商的成長動能，涵蓋電源領域的台達電、散熱領域的超眾科技、載板領域的欣興電子與景碩科技，以及PCB領域的金像電。

  ## 客製晶片需求加速，台積電重新調配先進封裝產能

  ASIC需求正在各大超大規模雲端業者之間持續擴大。Google的TPU（張量處理器）伺服器出貨量預計2027年將成長117%；AWS Trainium伺服器出貨量則預計成長58%。微軟、Meta及OpenAI的客製化ASIC計畫，亦預計將貢獻額外出貨量。

  台積電同時承接輝達GPU及雲端業者客製化AI晶片的製造業務，預計將重新調整其CoWoS產能——CoWoS是一種將多顆晶片整合為單一高效能封裝的先進封裝技術。報告引述市場估計，台積電GPU與ASIC的CoWoS產能分配，預計將從2026年的約6比4，調整至2027年的約5比5。

  ## 需求恐將超越供給

  報告引述下游供應鏈消息人士表示，客戶合計已規劃逾50GW的2027年資料中心容量，相當於超過25萬個AI伺服器機櫃。然而，考量電源零組件、記憶體、CPU、載板及PCB可能出現的供應短缺，加上晶圓廠產能限制與客戶融資需求，業界普遍認為需求如數履約的可能性不高。

  ## 季度出貨節奏與Rubin Ultra時程

  2027年第一季的GPU機櫃出貨量預計將是全年最低點，反映平台轉換時程及農曆新年生產放緩的雙重影響。出貨量預計在第二、三季依序回升，第四季則將趨於平緩。

  輝達Rubin Ultra平台為Rubin之後的下一代產品，預計2027年出貨量約為3,000個機櫃，主要出貨量的放量則預計將遞延至2028年。
