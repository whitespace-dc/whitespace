body_en: |
  OpenAI has publicly revealed its Jalapeño Intelligence Platform, an in-house artificial intelligence accelerator developed in partnership with Broadcom, marking the company's entry into custom silicon design for model serving.

  OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman and Broadcom chief executive Hock Tan appeared together to announce the platform. Photographs shared by OpenAI show a packaged accelerator with high-bandwidth memory (HBM) stacks — a type of fast, stacked DRAM used in AI chips — on either side of the die, with what appears to be six HBM stack positions visible in the package.

  ## Chip design and partners

  OpenAI said it taped out — the term for finalizing a chip design and sending it to a semiconductor foundry for fabrication — in nine months, which it attributed to using its own AI model in the design process. The company did not disclose which foundry is manufacturing the chip.

  Broadcom is serving two roles in the platform: it designed the accelerator itself, and its Tomahawk switch silicon — a high-performance Ethernet networking chip widely used in data center fabrics — is providing the networking layer. Celestica, a contract manufacturer, is supplying the board-level and rack-scale infrastructure that houses the accelerator.

  ## Current status

  OpenAI said the Jalapeño silicon is already running GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark in a lab environment. The company said it will disclose architectural details and performance figures over the coming months, leaving the announcement without published specifications or benchmark data at this stage.

  ## Industry context

  OpenAI's move into custom silicon follows a pattern established by large-scale cloud and internet companies — commonly called hyperscalers — that have developed proprietary accelerators to reduce dependence on merchant silicon and optimize chips for their specific model workloads. OpenAI cited massive demand for model serving as the driver for its silicon investment.

  The Jalapeño platform's significance lies in confirming that large AI model developers, not only cloud infrastructure providers or chip startups, are now pursuing custom accelerator programs. OpenAI said further technical and performance details will follow in the months ahead.

  ## OpenAI與博通攜手打造Jalapeño AI晶片平台

  OpenAI正式對外公布其Jalapeño智慧平台（Jalapeño Intelligence Platform）——一款與博通（Broadcom）合作開發的自研AI加速晶片，標誌著這家人工智慧公司正式跨入客製化矽晶片設計領域，以支援模型推論服務。

  OpenAI執行長山姆·奧特曼（Sam Altman）與博通執行長霍克·譚（Hock Tan）聯袂出席發布活動，共同宣布此平台問世。OpenAI公開的照片顯示，這款封裝好的加速晶片在晶粒兩側配置了高頻寬記憶體（HBM）堆疊——一種廣泛應用於AI晶片的高速堆疊式DRAM——從封裝外觀來看，可見六個HBM堆疊位置。

  ## 晶片設計與合作夥伴

  OpenAI表示，此晶片從設計定案到流片（即完成晶片設計並送交半導體晶圓廠製造的業界術語）僅歷時九個月，並將此速度歸功於在設計過程中導入自家AI模型的協助。至於負責製造的晶圓廠為何，OpenAI目前未予披露。

  博通在此平台中扮演雙重角色：一是主導加速晶片本身的設計，二是以旗下Tomahawk交換器矽晶片——一款廣泛應用於資料中心網路架構的高效能乙太網路交換晶片——提供網路層支援。合約製造商Celestica則負責供應搭載該加速晶片的板卡級與機架級基礎設施。

  ## 目前進展

  OpenAI表示，Jalapeño晶片已在實驗室環境中成功運行GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark模型。公司表示將於未來數月內陸續公開架構細節與效能數據，因此本次發布會並未提供任何已發表的技術規格或基準測試成績。

  ## 產業背景

  OpenAI投入客製化晶片的舉措，延續了大型雲端與網際網路業者——業界通稱「超大規模業者」（hyperscaler）——的既有路徑：這些業者長期自行開發專屬加速晶片，目的在於降低對市售晶片的依賴，並針對自身模型工作負載進行最佳化設計。OpenAI點名模型推論服務的龐大需求，是驅動其投資自研晶片的核心動力。

  Jalapeño平台的重要意義，在於確立了一個新趨勢：自研加速晶片計畫，如今已不再是雲端基礎設施業者或晶片新創公司的專屬領域，大型AI模型開發商同樣正全力投入。OpenAI表示，更多技術細節與效能數據將於未來數月內陸續對外公布。
