body_en: |
  Amazon.com is in active talks to sell its Trainium AI accelerator chips directly to third-party data centre operators, the company's AI chief Peter DeSantis said Thursday, a significant expansion that would take Amazon's custom silicon beyond its own cloud platform for the first time at scale.

  DeSantis confirmed the discussions in an interview at the VivaTech conference in Paris but declined to identify prospective buyers. "We view AI infrastructure as rapidly evolving," he said. "And we're constantly looking at ways to get to more customers."

  Amazon shares rose as much as 1.8% on the news, reaching an intraday high of $241.82.

  ## Trainium's reach so far

  Amazon introduced Trainium, its purpose-built AI training accelerator chip, in 2020. To date, the chip has been accessible only through Amazon Web Services (AWS), the company's cloud computing division. Notable AWS customers using Trainium include OpenAI, Anthropic, and Uber Technologies. Amazon said in April that Trainium has generated more than $225 billion in cumulative revenue commitments.

  The third generation of the chip began shipping earlier this year and is "largely sold out," DeSantis said. Amazon said there is already strong demand for a fourth version expected to debut next year.

  ## Jassy signalled the shift in April

  The move was foreshadowed by Amazon chief executive Andy Jassy, who wrote in his April shareholder letter that it is "quite possible" Amazon would sell racks of its chips to third parties. That letter framed the effort as part of a broader repositioning of the company around artificial intelligence, an area where Amazon has faced comparisons to rivals perceived as further ahead.

  ## Sovereign computing demand is a driver

  DeSantis cited demand for computing resources controlled locally — particularly in Europe — as a key factor behind the push to sell chips outside of AWS. Regulatory and policy pressure in some regions has prompted calls for countries to reduce reliance on US technology platforms. DeSantis said AWS has not experienced a negative business impact from that trend, but acknowledged that sovereign computing demand creates an opening for selling hardware that can be operated by local entities under local jurisdiction.

  ## Google moved first

  Amazon is not alone in this approach. In April, Alphabet chief executive Sundar Pichai said Google would begin delivering its Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) — its own Nvidia GPU alternative — to a "select group of customers" for deployment in their own data centres.

  ## Graviton expansion

  Beyond Trainium, DeSantis also cited growth in Amazon's Graviton chips, a general-purpose processor designed for broader computing workloads. Amazon recently began supplying Graviton chips to Meta Platforms. Over the past three years, DeSantis said Amazon has added more Graviton chips to its own computing systems than any other chip type.

  ## Cannibalization concerns dismissed

  DeSantis rejected the argument that selling Trainium externally would undercut AWS cloud revenues. "There's so much underconsumption in AI," he said. "I'm not worried about it."

  ## What it means

  If Amazon completes agreements to supply Trainium to outside operators, it would mark a structural shift in how hyperscaler-developed AI silicon reaches the market, introducing a new class of supplier into a segment currently dominated by Nvidia. For data centre planners and buyers, it opens the possibility of procuring custom AI acceleration hardware directly from a cloud provider — outside of a cloud subscription model — though pricing, support terms, and availability at scale remain to be disclosed. Amazon said strong interest in Trainium 4, expected to launch next year, is already established.

  ## 亞馬遜洽談直售Trainium AI晶片　劍指第三方資料中心

  亞馬遜執行長AI業務的要員彼得·德桑提斯（Peter DeSantis）週四證實，亞馬遜正積極與第三方資料中心業者洽談，計畫直接銷售自研的Trainium AI加速晶片，這將是亞馬遜自製晶片首次大規模進入其雲端平台以外的市場。

  德桑提斯在巴黎VivaTech科技展接受採訪時確認了上述洽談，但拒絕透露潛在買家身分。他表示：「我們認為AI基礎設施正在快速演進，我們也持續思考如何讓更多客戶受惠。」

  消息曝光後，亞馬遜股價當日最多上漲1.8%，盤中高點觸及每股241.82美元。

  ## Trainium的現有布局

  亞馬遜於2020年推出Trainium，這是一款專為AI模型訓練打造的加速晶片。迄今為止，該晶片僅能透過亞馬遜旗下的雲端運算部門Amazon Web Services（AWS）存取使用。目前採用Trainium的知名AWS客戶包括OpenAI、Anthropic以及Uber Technologies。亞馬遜在今年四月表示，Trainium累計帶來的營收承諾已超過2,250億美元。

  第三代Trainium晶片已於今年稍早開始出貨，德桑提斯指出，目前該晶片「幾乎已全數售罄」。亞馬遜並表示，預計明年問世的第四代晶片，現已有強勁的市場需求。

  ## 賈西四月已透露轉型訊號

  此番動向早有伏筆。亞馬遜執行長安迪·賈西（Andy Jassy）在今年四月致股東信中寫道，亞馬遜「極有可能」向第三方出售整架晶片伺服器機架。那封信將此項計畫定位為亞馬遜以人工智慧為核心進行更大範圍戰略轉型的一環——在AI領域，亞馬遜長期被外界拿來與被視為領先一步的競爭對手相互比較。

  ## 數位主權需求是關鍵驅動力

  德桑提斯指出，部分地區——尤其是歐洲——對本地掌控運算資源的需求，是推動亞馬遜在AWS以外銷售晶片的重要因素。若干地區的法規與政策壓力，已促使各國呼籲降低對美國科技平台的依賴。德桑提斯表示，AWS目前尚未因此趨勢而蒙受業務損失，但他坦承，數位主權的運算需求確實為亞馬遜銷售可由在地主體、在當地司法管轄下自行操作的硬體設備，開創了新的市場空間。

  ## Google已搶先一步

  亞馬遜並非第一家採取此策略的業者。今年四月，Alphabet執行長桑達·皮查伊（Sundar Pichai）宣布，Google將開始向「特定客戶群」交付自研的張量處理器（TPU）——也就是Google自行開發的輝達GPU替代方案——供這些客戶部署於自家資料中心。

  ## Graviton晶片版圖擴展

  除了Trainium之外，德桑提斯也提及亞馬遜另一款自研晶片Graviton的成長動能。Graviton是一款通用型處理器，適用於更廣泛的運算工作負載。亞馬遜近期已開始向Meta Platforms供應Graviton晶片。德桑提斯說，過去三年間，亞馬遜在自身運算系統中新增的Graviton晶片數量，已超越任何其他晶片類型。

  ## 自我蠶食疑慮遭駁斥

  對於外部銷售Trainium可能侵蝕AWS雲端營收的疑慮，德桑提斯予以駁斥。「AI領域存在大量尚未被滿足的需求，」他說，「我並不擔心這個問題。」

  ## 市場意義

  若亞馬遜成功與外部業者簽訂Trainium供應協議，將標誌著超大規模雲端業者自研AI晶片進入市場的結構性轉變，並為目前由輝達主導的市場引入一類新型供應商。對資料中心的規劃者與採購方而言，這開啟了一種新的可能性：直接向雲端服務供應商採購客製化AI加速硬體，而無須受限於雲端訂閱模式——儘管定價、技術支援條款及大規模供貨時程等細節尚待公布。亞馬遜表示，預計明年推出的Trainium 4已確立強勁的市場需求。
