body_en: |
  CoreWeave, an AI cloud services provider, has signed a multi-year strategic agreement with Solidigm — the enterprise storage company owned by SK Hynix — to secure priority access to enterprise-grade solid-state drive (SSD) capacity, the company announced.

  The agreement gives CoreWeave preferential allocation of high-capacity enterprise SSDs at a time when the company said overall storage supply is tightening. Financial terms, SSD capacities, and purchase volumes were not disclosed.

  ## Why storage supply has become a bottleneck

  CoreWeave said AI workloads require the integrated scaling of compute, networking, storage, and software infrastructure. As customer AI models move from experimental stages into full production deployment, the company said the ability to expand storage capacity in step with that transition has become a significant constraint in data center capacity planning.

  The direct, long-term supply relationship with Solidigm is intended to ensure that storage resources can grow in parallel with GPU compute and high-speed networking capacity, CoreWeave said.

  ## Broader supply chain implications

  The deal reflects a shift in how cloud operators are approaching supply chain strategy. Under rapid growth in AI server demand, procurement planning — which has focused heavily on GPUs, power infrastructure, and data center space — is now extending to enterprise SSDs as a critical component category, according to the announcement.

  CoreWeave did not provide a timeline for deliveries or specify which Solidigm product lines are covered by the agreement.
body_zh: |
  ## CoreWeave與Solidigm簽訂多年優先供應協議，鎖定企業級SSD

  AI雲端服務商CoreWeave宣布，已與SK海力士旗下企業儲存公司Solidigm簽署多年期戰略協議，取得企業級固態硬碟（SSD）容量的優先分配權。協議的財務條款、SSD容量規格及採購數量均未對外披露。

  ## 為何儲存供應已成瓶頸

  CoreWeave指出，AI工作負載需要運算、網路、儲存與軟體基礎架構的整合式擴展。隨著客戶的AI模型從實驗階段邁入全面正式部署，該公司表示，能否與這一轉變同步擴充儲存容量，已成為資料中心容量規劃上的重大制約因素。

  CoreWeave表示，與Solidigm建立直接的長期供應關係，旨在確保儲存資源能與GPU運算及高速網路容量同步成長。

  ## 供應鏈策略的更廣泛影響

  這項協議反映出雲端業者在供應鏈策略上的轉變。在AI伺服器需求快速成長的背景下，採購規劃過去高度聚焦於GPU、電力基礎設施與資料中心空間，如今已將企業級SSD納入關鍵零組件類別一併考量。

  CoreWeave未說明交貨時程，也未具體指明本次協議涵蓋Solidigm的哪些產品線。
