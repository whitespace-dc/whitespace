body_en: |
  The Bosgame M5 mini desktop carries AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395 processor and 128GB of LPDDR5X memory at a price $500 to $1,100 below the AMD Ryzen AI Halo system, which ships with the same chip and memory configuration, according to a review published by ServeTheHome. The price gap varies by sales channel and current promotional pricing.

  ## Pricing position

  The cost difference against the Ryzen AI Halo is the M5's primary value argument, ServeTheHome said. Because the two platforms share the same CPU and 128GB LPDDR5X memory specification, price becomes the main differentiator when comparing them at equivalent configurations.

  ## Chassis design and thermals

  The M5 uses an angled chassis designed to sit horizontally or mount vertically using an included metal stand. Vent openings run across the top, bottom, and sides. The top vents preclude stacking objects on the unit without blocking airflow. The bottom panel features an access hatch alongside the vents and four rubber feet; two of those feet are mounted on angled surfaces and do not make contact with a flat desk when the unit is in horizontal orientation.

  A three-position performance mode button on the front panel switches between a quiet, low-power setting and a higher-output mode that produces more fan noise, with an intermediate option available. An RGB light strip runs along the chassis.

  ## Front-panel ports

  The front panel provides two USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A ports rated at 10 Gbps, one USB4 port, an SDXC card reader, and a 3.5mm audio jack. ServeTheHome noted that a front-panel audio jack is not present on all competing systems in this category.

  ## Rear-panel ports

  The rear panel carries two USB 2.0 Type-A ports at 480 Mbps, one HDMI output, one DisplayPort output, one additional USB4 port, one USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A port, a 3.5mm audio jack, a DC power input, and a Realtek 2.5GbE wired Ethernet port. ServeTheHome noted that unlike the NVIDIA DGX Spark and AMD Ryzen AI Halo systems, the M5 does not present a port shortage. The review also noted that the additional USB 2.0 ports have practical value beyond desktop use, citing their own practice of connecting USB-to-serial adapters on similar Strix Halo Linux systems for network equipment configuration.

  ## Networking gap

  The 2.5GbE LAN port is the single hardware limitation ServeTheHome flagged. The review said a 10GbE port would have allowed the M5 to compete more directly on specifications with other platforms in the AMD Ryzen AI Max+ family, which AMD markets under the Strix Halo platform name. ServeTheHome noted that the XikeStor SKN-U310GT, a USB adapter using a Realtek RTL8159 controller, can deliver 10GbE speeds as a workaround, though doing so consumes one of the unit's USB ports.

  ## Significance

  The Bosgame M5 enters a growing segment of compact systems built around AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395, competing primarily on price against first-party and higher-positioned designs. The $500-to-$1,100 discount versus the Ryzen AI Halo at identical CPU and memory specifications gives the M5 a clear cost advantage for buyers whose workloads do not require 10GbE local networking. The 2.5GbE Ethernet ceiling remains the primary hardware constraint the review identified for high-throughput data transfer use cases.

  ## Bosgame M5迷你桌機搭載相同CPU與記憶體，售價最多比AMD Ryzen AI Halo便宜3.6萬元

  根據ServeTheHome發布的評測報告，Bosgame M5迷你桌機搭載AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395處理器及128GB LPDDR5X記憶體，售價卻比採用相同晶片與記憶體規格的AMD Ryzen AI Halo系統低了500至1,100美元（約1.6萬至3.6萬元新台幣）。兩者之間的價差會因銷售通路及當時的促銷活動而有所不同。

  ## 定價定位

  ServeTheHome指出，與Ryzen AI Halo相比的價格優勢，正是M5最核心的競爭力所在。由於兩款平台採用相同的CPU及128GB LPDDR5X記憶體規格，在配置相同的情況下，售價便成為兩者最關鍵的區隔因素。

  ## 機箱設計與散熱

  M5採用斜面造型機箱，可橫置平放，也可搭配附贈的金屬支架直立擺放。機身頂部、底部及兩側均設有散熱開孔。由於頂部設有散熱孔，若在上方堆放物品將阻礙氣流，使用時需特別留意。底部面板除了散熱孔之外，另設有一個維修艙蓋及四個橡膠腳墊；其中兩個腳墊位於斜面位置，機器橫置時並無法與平坦桌面完全接觸。

  機身前面板設有一個三段式效能模式切換按鈕，可在低噪音省電模式、高效能模式（風扇聲音較大）以及中間的平衡模式之間切換。機箱側面另設有RGB燈條。

  ## 前面板連接埠

  前面板提供兩個傳輸速率10 Gbps的USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A連接埠、一個USB4連接埠、一個SDXC記憶卡讀卡機，以及一個3.5mm音訊插孔。ServeTheHome特別指出，前面板音訊插孔並非此類產品的標準配置，並非所有競爭機種都有提供。

  ## 後面板連接埠

  後面板則配備兩個傳輸速率480 Mbps的USB 2.0 Type-A連接埠、一個HDMI輸出、一個DisplayPort輸出、一個額外的USB4連接埠、一個USB 3.2 Gen2 Type-A連接埠、一個3.5mm音訊插孔、一個DC電源輸入，以及一個Realtek 2.5GbE有線乙太網路埠。ServeTheHome指出，與NVIDIA DGX Spark及AMD Ryzen AI Halo系統不同，M5並不存在連接埠不足的問題。評測文章也提到，額外的USB 2.0連接埠在桌機使用情境以外同樣具有實用價值，並以評測者本身在類似的Strix Halo Linux系統上連接USB轉序列埠轉接器以進行網路設備設定為例說明。

  ## 網路規格的不足

  2.5GbE區域網路埠是ServeTheHome唯一點名的硬體限制。評測指出，若能配備10GbE連接埠，M5便可在規格層面與AMD Ryzen AI Max+家族（AMD以Strix Halo平台名稱行銷）其他平台更直接競爭。ServeTheHome提到，使用採用Realtek RTL8159控制器的XikeStor SKN-U310GT USB轉接器，可作為實現10GbE速率的替代方案，但這樣做會占用機器上的一個USB連接埠。

  ## 市場意義

  Bosgame M5進入了一個以AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395為核心、正在快速成長的緊湊型系統市場區塊，主要以價格優勢與原廠產品及定位較高的設計一較高下。在CPU與記憶體規格完全相同的前提下，M5相較於Ryzen AI Halo擁有500至1,100美元的折扣空間，對於工作負載不需要10GbE本地網路的使用者而言，成本優勢相當明顯。而2.5GbE乙太網路埠的上限，依然是這份評測所點出的、在高吞吐量資料傳輸使用情境下最主要的硬體限制。
