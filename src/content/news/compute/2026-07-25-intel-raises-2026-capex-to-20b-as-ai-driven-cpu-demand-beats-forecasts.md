body_en: |
  Intel on July 23 reported second-quarter revenue and profit well above analyst estimates and raised its capital expenditure forecast for 2026 to $20 billion, up from a prior target of $18 billion, as demand for data centre central processing units (CPUs) accelerates alongside the build-out of agentic AI infrastructure.

  The results sent Intel shares up 12 per cent in after-hours trade. Chief financial officer David Zinsner told Reuters that booming demand prompted the capex increase, and said spending is expected to be "up meaningfully" again in 2027.

  ## Second-quarter results

  For the quarter ended June 27, Intel reported revenue of $16.13 billion, a 25.4 per cent increase year-on-year and well ahead of the analyst consensus of $14.42 billion compiled by LSEG. Adjusted earnings per share came in at 42 cents, compared with the analyst estimate of 21 cents. Adjusted gross margin was 41.8 per cent against an estimate of 38.8 per cent.

  Intel's data centre and AI business generated $6.26 billion in second-quarter revenue, versus analyst estimates of $5.37 billion. The client computing segment — covering laptop and desktop chips — recorded $8.88 billion, ahead of the $7.89 billion estimate. Zinsner said unit volumes in that segment declined but average selling prices rose as Intel shifted its product mix away from low-cost, entry-level chips toward higher-end devices.

  The company's contract manufacturing, or foundry, business posted $5.77 billion in revenue, above the $5.55 billion estimate.

  ## Third-quarter outlook

  Intel guided third-quarter revenue in the range of $15.8 billion to $16.8 billion, compared with the analyst average of $15.10 billion. Adjusted earnings per share are expected at 38 cents, versus the analyst consensus of 27 cents.

  ## Agentic AI and CPU demand

  Agentic AI — systems in which autonomous software agents carry out tasks such as computer coding on behalf of human users — has driven a resurgence in demand for data centre CPUs. Intel's leadership said earlier in 2026 that the demand surge had caught the company off guard, with orders outstripping its manufacturing capacity.

  To meet that demand, Zinsner said Intel has signed a range of long-term supply agreements with customers, covering both CPUs and specialised chips the company calls XPUs (accelerated processing units). The agreements run between three and five years; some include both volume and price commitments, while others cover volume alone.

  Zinsner cautioned that long-term contracts are not a guaranteed floor. "You can't completely hang your hat on them because when things change, a lot of times things get renegotiated," he said, while adding that customers "are not signing those unless they have real confidence in what they're going to invest."

  ## Foundry business and major customers

  Intel's foundry unit, which manufactures chips for external customers under contract, secured Elon Musk's Tesla as a client for its next-generation 14A manufacturing process, to be used in a chip project Tesla calls "Terafab." In April, US President Donald Trump announced that Apple had agreed to manufacture processors with Intel; neither company has confirmed that arrangement.

  Nvidia, which dominates the market for AI accelerators — graphics processing units (GPUs) repurposed for training and running AI models — is also developing a CPU called "Vera." Major cloud providers including Amazon and Alphabet are designing their own in-house CPUs based on the Arm architecture.

  ## Balance sheet and capital plans

  Zinsner said Intel holds approximately $30 billion in cash and has access to a $10 billion revolving credit facility. He did not rule out a share issuance to fund further expansion, saying the company has "no specific plans at this point" but would not dismiss the possibility.

  ## Market context

  Intel shares had fallen more than 25 per cent from a record closing price on June 22 amid a broader sell-off in chip stocks, though they remain up more than 170 per cent for the year to date. Rival chipmakers Arm Holdings and Advanced Micro Devices each gained more than 3 per cent in extended trading after Intel's results, with the three companies together adding more than $100 billion in combined stock market value.

  The results reinforce the strategy of chief executive Lip-Bu Tan to regain technology leadership and expand Intel's position in AI chip markets. Investors have been watching the data centre and foundry businesses as the primary measures of whether that turnaround is gaining traction.

  Zinsner said the raised capex forecast and multi-year customer agreements together signal confidence in continued growth, and that Intel expects capital spending to rise again in 2027 beyond the newly increased 2026 level of $20 billion.
body_zh: |
  英特爾（Intel）7月23日公布的第二季財報顯示，營收與獲利均大幅超越分析師預期，並將2026年資本支出預測從原訂180億美元上調至200億美元，原因是資料中心中央處理器（CPU）需求隨代理AI基礎建設的擴展持續加速。

  財報發布後，英特爾股價在盤後交易中大漲12%。財務長大衛・金斯納（David Zinsner）向路透社表示，旺盛的需求促使公司提高資本支出，並預計2027年的支出將再度「大幅增加」。

  ## 第二季財報

  截至6月27日的本季，英特爾營收達161.3億美元，年增25.4%，遠超LSEG彙整的市場共識預測144.2億美元。調整後每股盈餘（EPS）為42美分，高於分析師預估的21美分。調整後毛利率為41.8%，亦高於預期的38.8%。

  英特爾資料中心暨AI業務本季營收達62.6億美元，高於分析師預估的53.7億美元。涵蓋筆記型電腦與桌上型電腦晶片的客戶端運算部門，錄得88.8億美元，超過市場預估的78.9億美元。金斯納指出，該部門出貨量有所下滑，但隨著英特爾將產品組合從低價入門級晶片轉向高階裝置，平均售價因此提升。

  公司旗下的委託製造（晶圓代工）業務本季營收達57.7億美元，高於分析師預估的55.5億美元。

  ## 第三季展望

  英特爾預測第三季營收落在158億至168億美元之間，高於分析師平均預估的151億美元。調整後每股盈餘預計達38美分，優於市場共識預測的27美分。

  ## 代理AI與CPU需求

  代理AI（Agentic AI）——即由自主軟體代理代替人類使用者執行電腦程式設計等工作的系統——已帶動資料中心CPU需求重燃。英特爾管理層在2026年初曾表示，這波需求浪潮令公司措手不及，訂單量超出自身的製造產能。

  為因應龐大需求，金斯納表示，英特爾已與客戶簽訂一系列長期供貨協議，涵蓋CPU及公司稱為XPU（加速處理器）的特殊晶片。相關協議年限介於三至五年；部分協議同時包含出貨量與價格承諾，另有部分則僅涵蓋出貨量。

  金斯納也提醒，長期合約並非絕對保障。「你不能完全仰賴這些合約，因為情況一旦改變，很多時候都會重新談判，」他說，但也補充，客戶「若非對自身的投資方向有真正的信心，是不會簽署這些協議的」。

  ## 晶圓代工業務與主要客戶

  英特爾晶圓代工部門負責以委託方式為外部客戶製造晶片，已成功爭取到伊隆・馬斯克（Elon Musk）旗下特斯拉（Tesla）採用其次世代14A製程，用於特斯拉內部稱為「Terafab」的晶片專案。今年4月，美國總統唐納・川普（Donald Trump）宣布蘋果（Apple）已同意委託英特爾製造處理器；但兩家公司均未正式確認此一安排。

  主導AI加速器市場的輝達（NVIDIA）——其圖形處理器（GPU）已被廣泛用於訓練與執行AI模型——亦正在開發一款名為「Vera」的CPU。亞馬遜（Amazon）與Alphabet等主要雲端服務業者，也在自行研發基於Arm架構的自有CPU。

  ## 資產負債表與資本規劃

  金斯納表示，英特爾目前持有約300億美元現金，並可動用100億美元的循環信用額度。對於能否透過發行新股來籌資擴張，他未予排除，僅表示公司目前「尚無具體計畫」，但不會否定此一選項。

  ## 市場背景

  在晶片類股普遍下跌的背景下，英特爾股價從6月22日的收盤歷史高點已下跌逾25%，但年初至今仍累計上漲逾170%。競爭對手安謀控股（Arm Holdings）與超微（AMD）在英特爾財報公布後，盤後交易各漲逾3%，三家公司合計市值增加逾1,000億美元。

  此番財報表現強化了執行長陳立武（Lip-Bu Tan）重奪技術領導地位、擴大英特爾在AI晶片市場地位的既定策略。投資人持續密切關注資料中心與晶圓代工兩大業務，視之為衡量這場企業轉型是否持續奏效的主要指標。

  金斯納表示，資本支出預測上調與多年期客戶協議，共同傳達出公司對持續成長的信心，並預計2027年的資本支出將再度超越2026年新訂的200億美元目標。
