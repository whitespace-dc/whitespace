body_en: |
  Qualcomm unveiled the Dragonfly C1000 data center central processing unit (CPU) at its Investor Day 2026 in New York and announced Meta as the chip's first large-scale customer, marking the semiconductor company's formal entry into the server processor market.

  Under the agreement, Qualcomm will supply Dragonfly C1000 CPUs to power Meta's next-generation servers. Mass production is scheduled to begin in the second half of 2028. Neither company disclosed the contract value, the number of units ordered, or pricing details.

  ## The Dragonfly product line

  The Dragonfly C1000 is part of a broader Dragonfly family that also includes AI accelerators. Qualcomm said the chip line is designed for what it calls "agentic AI" — artificial intelligence systems that execute tasks autonomously and require large-scale computing resources.

  Qualcomm cited energy efficiency as a central design goal. "We designed this data center CPU to deliver superior per-core performance and a breakthrough in power efficiency for large-scale data center deployments," the company said in a statement.

  ## Modular acquisition

  Alongside the chip announcement, Qualcomm disclosed plans to acquire Modular, an AI software company, in an all-stock deal of approximately 19.2 million shares valued at around $4 billion. The transaction is expected to close in the second half of 2026.

  Modular develops software that allows AI models to run across different chip architectures without requiring the underlying code to be rewritten for each one. Qualcomm positioned this capability as an alternative to Nvidia's CUDA platform, the dominant software environment for AI workloads that ties developers to Nvidia hardware.

  Chief Executive Cristiano Amon framed the acquisition as part of a broader platform strategy. "We believe the future of AI is on developer-friendly platforms that can run across multiple computing environments, giving customers more choice in running AI," Amon said.

  ## Reducing smartphone dependence

  The data center push represents a deliberate effort to diversify away from Qualcomm's smartphone business, which accounts for approximately two-thirds of the company's product revenue. The global smartphone market is expected to contract sharply this year, according to information shared at the event.

  Revenue from the Dragonfly C1000 is not expected to contribute meaningfully to Qualcomm's finances until production begins in the second half of 2028, leaving roughly a two-year gap before the new business line could affect results.

  ## ByteDance discussions

  Reuters has reported that Qualcomm is in discussions to provide custom chip design services for ByteDance, the Chinese technology company that owns TikTok, including a possible video processing chip. Neither Qualcomm nor ByteDance has confirmed those talks.

  Qualcomm's Dragonfly C1000 is scheduled to enter mass production in the second half of 2028, with the Modular acquisition targeted to close later this year.
body_zh: |
  高通（Qualcomm）在紐約舉行的2026年投資者日上，正式發表Dragonfly C1000資料中心中央處理器（CPU），並宣布Meta為這款晶片的首位大規模客戶，標誌著這家半導體公司正式跨入伺服器處理器市場。

  依據雙方協議，高通將供應Dragonfly C1000 CPU，為Meta下一代伺服器提供運算動力，量產預計於2028年下半年展開。兩家公司均未透露合約金額、訂購數量及定價細節。

  ## Dragonfly 產品線

  Dragonfly C1000隸屬更廣泛的Dragonfly產品家族，該家族亦涵蓋AI加速器。高通表示，這條晶片產品線專為其所稱的「代理式AI」（agentic AI）而設計，即能自主執行任務、需要大規模運算資源的人工智慧系統。

  高通將能源效率列為核心設計目標。公司在聲明中指出：「我們設計這款資料中心CPU，旨在為大規模資料中心部署帶來卓越的單核心效能，並實現功耗效率的重大突破。」

  ## 收購 Modular

  除晶片發表消息外，高通亦宣布計畫以換股方式收購AI軟體公司Modular，涉及約1,920萬股股票，估值約40億美元。這筆交易預計於2026年下半年完成交割。

  Modular開發的軟體能讓AI模型跨不同晶片架構運行，無需針對每種架構重新改寫底層程式碼。高通將這項能力定位為輝達（NVIDIA）CUDA平台的替代方案——CUDA是目前AI工作負載的主流軟體環境，讓開發者與輝達硬體深度綁定。

  執行長克里斯蒂亞諾·阿蒙（Cristiano Amon）將此次收購定位為更宏觀平台策略的一環。阿蒙表示：「我們相信，AI的未來在於對開發者友善、能跨多種運算環境執行的平台，賦予客戶在運行AI時更多的選擇空間。」

  ## 降低對智慧型手機業務的依賴

  這一波資料中心布局，代表高通刻意尋求多角化經營，以擺脫對智慧型手機業務的高度倚重——後者目前約占公司產品營收的三分之二。根據活動現場分享的資訊，全球智慧型手機市場今年預計將大幅萎縮。

  Dragonfly C1000的營收，預計要等到2028年下半年量產啟動後，才能對高通財務產生實質貢獻；這意味著新事業線從現在起仍有約兩年的過渡期，才能對財報結果產生影響。

  ## 與字節跳動的洽談

  路透社報導，高通正與中國科技公司字節跳動（ByteDance，即TikTok母公司）洽談提供客製化晶片設計服務，合作內容可能涵蓋一款影像處理晶片。高通與字節跳動雙方均未就上述傳聞加以證實。

  高通Dragonfly C1000預計於2028年下半年進入量產階段，而收購Modular的交易則預計於今年稍晚完成。
