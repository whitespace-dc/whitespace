body_en: |
  Cisco has expanded its Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA to support rack-scale deployments, adding the NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 and NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition to a joint AI infrastructure architecture designed for organisations moving AI workloads from pilot into production.

  The updated design integrates NVIDIA Spectrum-X Ethernet networking alongside Cisco's Nexus switching, UCS compute, Hyperfabric, and security products, combined with NVIDIA AI Enterprise software. The architecture targets both new-build (greenfield) data centre environments and existing installations.

  ## What the expanded stack includes

  The full-stack offering incorporates several distinct product layers. On security, Cisco is including AI Defence — its tool for protecting AI applications and workloads — along with a Hybrid Mesh Firewall for network segmentation and policy enforcement, and Splunk for monitoring and log analysis. On the management and automation side, the architecture adds Cisco Nexus Dashboard, UCS Manager, and Intersight, which the company said are intended to simplify deployment and day-to-day operations.

  NVIDIA's contribution spans compute, networking, and software. The GB300 NVL72 — a rack-scale system designed for large training and inference workloads that tightly couples compute and networking — targets customers building what the industry terms AI factories: infrastructure built primarily to train or run large AI models. The RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell Server Edition, by contrast, is positioned for enterprise AI workloads and model deployment at a smaller scale.

  NVIDIA AI Enterprise, a software platform for data science and production AI use cases, is also incorporated into the joint architecture.

  ## Channel and go-to-market

  Cisco said the Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA is intended to be sold through its partner ecosystem, including channel partners and system integrators who work with customers on infrastructure planning, deployment, and ongoing management. The company did not disclose pricing.

  ## Why Cisco is positioning security as a differentiator

  Cisco is framing security as a central component of the design rather than an add-on, arguing that organisations need to manage risk across models, applications, and data flows as AI systems become more embedded in business operations. The inclusion of AI Defence, firewall segmentation, and Splunk-based monitoring reflects an attempt to address concerns that infrastructure procurement and security have historically been handled separately.

  Jeetu Patel, Cisco's Executive Vice President and Chief Product Officer, attributed the expansion to changing customer requirements. "The next era of AI infrastructure can't just deliver raw compute; it must harness that compute through validated, full-stack architectures," Patel said. "Cisco Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA brings together the infrastructure, software, and security needed to help businesses deploy AI at scale, while managing risk, reducing complexity, and enhancing operational reliability."

  Patel also cited vendor complexity as the commercial problem the architecture is intended to solve. "Many are struggling to justify the time and expense of piecing together complex technology from dozens of vendors," he said, describing the offering as "a fast, trusted path to build and secure scalable AI infrastructure."

  ## Industry context

  The announcement continues a pattern among major technology vendors of releasing pre-validated, bundled AI infrastructure stacks that combine hardware, networking, software, and management tooling in a single reference architecture. The approach responds to demand from enterprises facing pressure to accelerate AI deployments without the integration work involved in assembling components from multiple suppliers independently.

  The expansion of the Secure AI Factory builds on a partnership between Cisco and NVIDIA that the companies have been developing around AI data centre infrastructure. Cisco has been working to strengthen its position in AI networking and security as customer spending on data centre systems for model training and inference has grown.

  The architecture's support for rack-scale systems such as the GB300 NVL72 marks an extension of the joint design into larger, more compute-dense deployments than earlier versions of the Secure AI Factory addressed.

  思科（Cisco）已將旗下「安全AI工廠」（Secure AI Factory with NVIDIA）的支援範圍擴展至機架規模部署，在雙方共同打造的AI基礎設施架構中，新增輝達（NVIDIA）GB300 NVL72與輝達RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell伺服器版的支援，協助企業將AI工作負載從試驗階段推進至正式生產環境。

  更新後的設計將輝達Spectrum-X乙太網路整合至思科Nexus交換器、UCS運算平台、Hyperfabric及各項資安產品之中，並搭配輝達AI Enterprise軟體。此架構同時適用於全新建置（greenfield）的資料中心環境，以及既有的現場安裝環境。

  ## 擴展後的全棧架構涵蓋內容

  整套全棧解決方案涵蓋數個不同產品層。在資安方面，思科納入了AI Defence——其用於保護AI應用程式與工作負載的工具——以及用於網路分段與政策執行的混合網狀防火牆（Hybrid Mesh Firewall），並整合Splunk進行監控與日誌分析。在管理與自動化方面，架構加入了思科Nexus Dashboard、UCS Manager及Intersight，據該公司表示，這些工具旨在簡化部署流程與日常維運工作。

  輝達的貢獻涵蓋運算、網路與軟體三大面向。GB300 NVL72是一套機架規模系統，專為大型訓練與推論工作負載設計，將運算與網路緊密整合，目標客戶是正在打造業界所稱「AI工廠」的企業——也就是主要用於訓練或執行大型AI模型的基礎設施。RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell伺服器版則定位於較小規模的企業AI工作負載與模型部署需求。

  此外，輝達AI Enterprise這套面向資料科學與生產AI使用情境的軟體平台，也納入了雙方的共同架構之中。

  ## 通路與市場推廣

  思科表示，安全AI工廠with NVIDIA將透過其夥伴生態系銷售，包含協助客戶規劃基礎設施、執行部署與持續管理的通路夥伴及系統整合商。該公司並未揭露定價資訊。

  ## 思科為何將資安定位為差異化關鍵

  思科將資安定位為設計核心，而非附加功能，並強調隨著AI系統愈來愈深入企業營運，組織必須管理跨模型、應用程式與資料流的風險。納入AI Defence、防火牆分段以及以Splunk為基礎的監控機制，反映出思科試圖回應業界長期以來將基礎設施採購與資安分開處理的問題。

  思科執行副總裁暨首席產品長傑圖·帕特爾（Jeetu Patel）將此次擴展歸因於客戶需求的轉變。「AI基礎設施的下一個時代，不能只是提供原始運算能力；必須透過經過驗證的全棧架構來善用這些運算資源，」帕特爾表示。「思科安全AI工廠with NVIDIA匯集了企業大規模部署AI所需的基礎設施、軟體與資安能力，同時有效管理風險、降低複雜性並提升營運可靠性。」

  帕特爾也指出，廠商複雜性正是這套架構意圖解決的商業痛點。「許多企業正在為從數十個廠商拼湊複雜技術所耗費的時間與成本而苦惱，」他說，並將這套解決方案描述為「一條快速、可信賴的路徑，用於建構並保障可擴展的AI基礎設施」。

  ## 產業背景

  這項發布延續了主要科技廠商推出預先驗證、捆綁式AI基礎設施套件的趨勢——將硬體、網路、軟體與管理工具整合於單一參考架構中。這種做法回應了企業客戶在壓力下加速AI部署、同時又希望避免自行整合多個供應商元件的市場需求。

  安全AI工廠的擴展，建立在思科與輝達圍繞AI資料中心基礎設施長期發展的合作關係之上。隨著客戶在模型訓練與推論用資料中心系統上的支出持續增長，思科一直積極強化其在AI網路與資安領域的競爭地位。

  此次架構對GB300 NVL72等機架規模系統的支援，標誌著這套共同設計向更大型、運算密度更高的部署場景延伸，超越了安全AI工廠早期版本所涵蓋的規模。
