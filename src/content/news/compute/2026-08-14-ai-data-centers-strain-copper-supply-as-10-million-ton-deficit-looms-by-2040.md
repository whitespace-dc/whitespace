  ## AI infrastructure buildout tightens an already strained copper market

  The accelerating construction of AI data centers is adding significant new pressure to global copper supply chains facing a structural shortfall, according to industry analysts and companies operating in the sector. Copper — the primary conductor used in power distribution, busbars, and switchgear throughout a data center — already faces a projected deficit of 10 million metric tons by 2040 absent meaningful expansion of mining and refining capacity, a gap that AI-driven demand is widening.

  Each new hyperscale facility — the large-scale data centers built by major cloud and technology companies — requires roughly 50,000 tons of copper to construct, according to Brendan Smith, co-founder and CEO of SiTration, an MIT spinout focused on recovering copper from mining waste. With hundreds of such facilities planned over the coming decade, Smith told Data Center Knowledge that data centers have become one of the more visible sources of rising copper demand, even though they still represent a relatively modest share of global consumption.

  The supply gap is not primarily a demand-side problem. Smith described copper's predicament as structural: ore grades at existing mines are declining, and the trend cannot be reversed through investment alone. Building a new greenfield copper mine in the United States takes an average of 23 years from discovery to first production, with permitting consuming roughly half that timeline. Only about 3% of the copper consumed globally is fully refined within the US today, with the country exporting raw ore concentrate to other nations for processing — an arrangement that exposes manufacturers to geopolitical disruption and shifting trade policy.

  ## Tariffs lock supply in warehouses, straining fabricators

  Recently proposed US copper tariffs have introduced another layer of volatility. In June, the White House adjusted its tariff regime to impose a 15% transitional rate on metal-intensive industrial and electrical grid equipment, with tariffs on refined copper scheduled to phase in from 2027. Businesses moving to get ahead of that schedule have absorbed large quantities of copper into US warehouses.

  Shashank Sriram, Senior Research Analyst at Wood Mackenzie, said the pre-tariff stockpiling has effectively locked nearly 2% of total global copper demand out of circulation. "A massive amount of physical inventory has effectively been locked out of the global market," he said. Simultaneously, he noted, regulatory crackdowns on scrap trading — specifically stricter enforcement on reverse invoicing — have cut off secondary supply feeds, pushing fabricators onto the spot market for refined cathode at a time when inventory is already constrained.

  More than 50% of US copper needs are met through imports, according to Greg Smith, CEO of Pantheon Electric, a copper manufacturer. "The supplier with the best, most resilient and reliable supply chain wins," he said.

  ## Hyperscalers absorb price spikes, crowding out other buyers

  The pricing dynamics created by large technology companies are reshaping the copper market in ways that extend beyond data centers. Sriram said hyperscalers — unlike traditional utilities, automotive manufacturers, or construction firms — generate revenues that allow them to absorb virtually any metal price spike. "AI isn't creating the physical deficit on its own," he said, "but its bottomless willingness to pay is pricing everyone else out of a structurally disrupted market."

  Copper sits at the top of the supply-risk hierarchy among the critical minerals data centers depend on. Lithium, used in battery energy storage systems, also faces a supply-demand gap, but Smith said the comparison favors lithium: the global lithium market is roughly 10 times smaller than copper by value, and known lithium reserves remain accessible. Cobalt and rare earth elements appear in specialized electronics, magnets, and precision components, but their near-term strain is less acute than copper's.

  ## Manufacturers consolidate, startups mine waste streams

  In July, Pantheon Electric announced it had unified 21 manufacturing plants across North America and Europe into a single platform, becoming by its own account the largest independent US maker of copper conductors and busbars. The combined operation processes more than one million pounds of copper per day and counts Schneider Electric, Eaton, and Prysmian Group among its customers. Greg Smith framed the consolidation as a direct response to supply-chain vulnerability, noting that even a single small component such as a busbar can delay delivery of a multi-million-dollar system. For Pantheon, the response has included building local relationships with suppliers, recyclers, and traders in both the US and Europe.

  SiTration is pursuing a different approach: recovering copper directly from mining wastewater using an electro-extraction process that produces commercial-grade copper cathode without conventional smelting or refining. Smith estimates that roughly $3.5 trillion in critical minerals remain stranded in global mining waste streams, of which approximately $500 billion is copper. In June, the company completed a trial with mining group BHP to recover copper and gold from waste streams in South Australia. It has also worked with Rio Tinto in the United States, and further deployments in Chile are in the pipeline. "The approach is about creating new supply outright, rather than competing for existing supply," Smith said.

  ## Long-term offtake agreements emerge as near-term hedge

  Neither supply-chain consolidation nor waste-stream recovery represents a permanent solution to the underlying structural deficit, and both companies acknowledge as much. The more immediate response across the industry, Sriram said, involves large buyers signing long-term offtake agreements, pre-purchasing material, and locking in supply with fabricators and refiners several years ahead of need. "This doesn't create new supply, but it does reduce exposure to shortages and price spikes," he said.

  Over the medium term, Sriram said recycling is expected to play a growing role in meeting copper demand, as the volume of recoverable material from retired infrastructure increases. Greg Smith characterized access to critical minerals as a national security matter, arguing that strategies such as friendshoring — sourcing materials from allied nations — will be necessary alongside domestic supply development. Copper tariffs on refined material are currently scheduled to begin phasing in from 2027, adding a regulatory deadline to the industry's planning horizon.

  ## AI基礎建設浪潮，加劇已趨緊繃的銅料市場

  根據業界分析師及相關企業的說法，AI資料中心建設的加速推進，正對全球銅料供應鏈帶來顯著的新壓力——而這條供應鏈本已面臨結構性短缺。若採礦與精煉產能未能有效擴張，預估到2040年將出現1,000萬公噸的缺口，AI驅動的需求更使這道鴻溝持續擴大。銅是資料中心配電系統、匯流排及開關設備所採用的主要導電材料。

  麻省理工學院（MIT）衍生新創公司SiTration的共同創辦人暨執行長布蘭登・史密斯（Brendan Smith）表示，每座新興的超大規模資料中心——即大型雲端與科技公司興建的大規模設施——興建時約需消耗5萬公噸銅。SiTration專注於從採礦廢棄物中回收銅料。史密斯向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，未來十年預計將有數百座此類設施陸續落成，使資料中心成為銅需求上升中能見度較高的來源之一，儘管在全球消費總量中佔比仍相對有限。

  供給缺口的根源並非需求面問題。史密斯將銅的困境定性為結構性問題：現有礦場的礦石品位持續下滑，且這一趨勢單憑加大投資並無法逆轉。在美國境內，一座全新的露天銅礦從探勘到首次生產，平均需要23年時間，其中單是取得開採許可就要耗掉約一半的時程。目前，全球消費的銅中僅約3%在美國境內完成精煉，美國將原礦精礦出口至其他國家進行加工——此一安排使製造商長期暴露於地緣政治風險與貿易政策變動之中。

  ## 關稅使庫存積壓倉庫，製造商生存空間遭壓縮

  美國近期提出的銅關稅政策，為市場再添一層變數。今年6月，白宮調整關稅制度，對金屬密集型工業設備及電網設備徵收15%的過渡稅率，精煉銅的關稅則預計自2027年起分階段實施。為搶在時程之前卡位，企業已將大量銅料吸納進美國倉庫。

  Wood Mackenzie高級研究分析師沙尚克・斯里拉姆（Shashank Sriram）表示，這波搶關稅前囤貨的行動，已實際上使約相當於全球銅需求總量近2%的實體庫存退出流通。「大量實體庫存已實際上被鎖在全球市場之外，」他說。與此同時，他指出，針對廢料交易的監管趨嚴——尤其是對虛開發票行為的嚴格執法——已切斷二次供應來源，迫使加工廠在庫存本已吃緊之際，轉向現貨市場採購精煉陰極銅。

  銅製造商Pantheon Electric執行長葛瑞格・史密斯（Greg Smith）表示，美國逾50%的銅需求仰賴進口。「擁有最佳、最具韌性與可靠度供應鏈的供應商，就是最終的贏家，」他說。

  ## 超大規模業者吸收價格衝擊，排擠其他買家

  大型科技公司所形塑的銅價動態，正以超越資料中心範疇的方式重塑整個銅料市場。斯里拉姆表示，超大規模業者——不同於傳統公用事業、汽車製造商或建築業者——憑藉旗下業務創造的龐大營收，幾乎能夠吸收任何程度的金屬價格漲幅。「AI並非憑一己之力造成實體短缺，」他說，「但它那近乎無底線的支付意願，正在結構性受擾的市場中，把其他所有買家都擠出局。」

  在資料中心所依賴的關鍵礦產中，銅的供應風險位居最高層級。用於電池儲能系統的鋰同樣面臨供需缺口，但史密斯認為比較之下鋰的情況較為樂觀：全球鋰市場按價值計算約為銅市場的十分之一，且已知鋰礦儲量仍具可及性。鈷與稀土元素雖出現於特殊電子設備、磁鐵及精密零組件中，但其近期供應壓力不及銅來得迫切。

  ## 製造商整併供應鏈，新創企業另闢廢料回收之路

  今年7月，Pantheon Electric宣布將旗下分布於北美與歐洲的21座製造廠整合為單一平台，並宣稱由此成為美國最大的獨立銅導線及匯流排製造商。整合後的營運規模每日加工銅料逾百萬磅，客戶涵蓋施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）、伊頓（Eaton）及普睿司曼集團（Prysmian Group）。葛瑞格・史密斯將此次整併定位為直接因應供應鏈脆弱性的舉措，並指出即便是匯流排這類微小零組件，一旦缺料，就可能延誤價值數百萬美元的系統出貨。Pantheon的因應策略，還包括在美國與歐洲分別深耕與供應商、回收業者及貿易商的在地合作關係。

  SiTration則走的是另一條路：利用電萃取製程，直接從採礦廢水中回收銅料，毋須經過傳統熔煉或精煉程序，即可產出商業級陰極銅。史密斯估計，全球採礦廢棄物流中仍有約3.5兆美元的關鍵礦產尚待開發，其中銅的價值約達5,000億美元。今年6月，該公司完成了與礦業集團必和必拓（BHP）的合作試驗，成功從南澳洲的廢棄物流中回收銅與黃金。此外，SiTration也曾與力拓（Rio Tinto）在美國展開合作，並計畫進一步在智利部署相關技術。「這套方法的核心，是直接創造新的供應來源，而非與現有供應競爭，」史密斯說。

  ## 長期購售協議成為近期避險工具

  供應鏈整併與廢料回收，均無法從根本上化解供需的結構性缺口，兩家公司對此均直言不諱。斯里拉姆表示，業界目前更即時的因應之道，是由大型買家簽訂長期購售協議、預先採購物料，並在實際需求發生的數年前便與加工廠及精煉商鎖定貨源。「這並不會創造新的供應，但確實能降低面對短缺與價格波動的曝險，」他說。

  就中期展望而言，斯里拉姆預期隨著退役基礎設施的可回收銅料數量逐漸增加，回收業將在滿足銅需求方面扮演日益重要的角色。葛瑞格・史密斯則將關鍵礦產的取得定位為國家安全議題，主張「友岸外包」（friendshoring）——即從盟友國家採購原材料——與推動國內供應開發同樣不可或缺。精煉銅關稅目前預計自2027年起分階段實施，為整個產業的規劃時程增添了一道明確的法規時間壓力。
