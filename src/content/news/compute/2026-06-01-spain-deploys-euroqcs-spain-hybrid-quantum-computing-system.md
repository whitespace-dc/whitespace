# Spain deploys EuroQCS-Spain hybrid quantum computing system

Spain has strengthened its quantum technology commitment with the launch of EuroQCS-Spain through EuroHPC. The hybrid system integrates quantum computing capabilities with Barcelona Supercomputing Center's (BSC-CNS) high-performance computing infrastructure.

## 中文

# 西班牙推出 EuroQCS-Spain 混合量子系統

西班牙通過 EuroHPC 推出 EuroQCS-Spain，加強對量子技術的投資承諾。該混合系統將量子運算能力與巴塞隆納超級運算中心（BSC-CNS）的高效能運算基礎設施整合。
