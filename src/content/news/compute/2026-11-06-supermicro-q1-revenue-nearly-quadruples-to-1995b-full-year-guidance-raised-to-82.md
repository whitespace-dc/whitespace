body_en: |
  Super Micro Computer reported fiscal first-quarter revenue of $19.95 billion on November 3, nearly quadrupling the year-earlier result, and raised its full-year revenue guidance to between $72 billion and $82 billion — up from a prior range of $65 billion to $72 billion. The quarterly figures exceeded the upper end of the company's own preliminary estimates across every reported metric, sending the stock higher in after-hours trading.

  Adjusted earnings per share reached $1.81 for the quarter, well above a consensus analyst estimate of $1.23, according to Commercial Times. Net income was $1.594 billion. Adjusted gross margin came in at 12.8%, above the 12% to 12.5% range the company had guided in early October. Revenue grew roughly 80% from the prior quarter.

  The company's preliminary results, released on October 20, had already moved markets: Supermicro's stock surged more than 15% that day, with shares of Dell, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), and AI cloud providers rising alongside it, the report noted. The November 3 full release extended those gains in after-hours trading.

  For its fiscal second quarter, Supermicro guided revenue of $19.5 billion to $21.5 billion.

  ## Pivot toward full data-center solutions

  Chief Executive Charles Liang said in the earnings report that the company is accelerating its transition from server manufacturer to full AI and IT data-center solutions provider. Through a modular offering called DCBBS, Supermicro combines compute, storage, networking, power supply, liquid cooling, and management software, then bundles deployment and ongoing services with the hardware sale.

  Part of the integration and testing is completed at the factory before delivery, the company said, reducing the time customers need to bring new data-center capacity online. Supermicro said this approach, paired with global manufacturing capacity, helps customers shorten deployment timelines and control total costs. The strategy positions the company as a broader infrastructure partner rather than a single-equipment supplier, the report noted.

  ## Balance sheet

  As of the end of September, Supermicro held approximately $6.9 billion in cash and equivalents. Combined bank borrowings and convertible bonds totaled roughly $8.6 billion. Operating cash flow was a net outflow of $434 million for the quarter, driven by increases in inventory and accounts receivable. Capital expenditure and investments totaled approximately $47 million.

  The company said it raised inventory levels to meet the demands of rapid business growth and continued to expand its global manufacturing footprint to fulfill customer orders and accelerate delivery. The cash position, it said, provides the resources needed to fund ongoing operations, component procurement, and capacity build-out.

  ## Bellwether role amid AI spending uncertainty

  Investors have treated Supermicro as a proxy for overall AI server demand amid persistent questions about whether large-scale AI infrastructure spending can be sustained. The Commercial Times report noted that the company's upward revision to its annual revenue outlook signals that AI server demand remains strong even as market participants continue to debate the durability of AI-driven capital expenditure. Founded in 1993, Supermicro has operated in computing infrastructure for more than three decades, the report said.

  Supermicro's full-year revenue guidance of $72 billion to $82 billion for fiscal year 2027 now represents the company's most recent public forecast for the scale of AI-related data-center build-out it expects to serve over the coming year.

  超微電腦（Super Micro Computer）於11月3日公布第一財季營收199.5億美元，較去年同期成長近四倍，並將全年營收展望上修至720億至820億美元，高於先前預估的650億至720億美元區間。本季各項財務指標均超越公司自身的初步預估上限，帶動盤後股價走高。

  調整後每股盈餘為1.81美元，遠高於分析師共識預估的1.23美元（據《工商時報》報導），超出幅度接近五成。淨利為15.94億美元。調整後毛利率為12.8%，優於公司10月初所預告的12%至12.5%區間。本季營收較前一季成長約80%。

  公司於10月20日發布的初步業績已率先撼動市場：超微電腦股價當日飆漲逾15%，戴爾（Dell）、慧與科技（Hewlett Packard Enterprise，HPE）及AI雲端服務業者股價亦同步走揚。11月3日完整財報發布後，盤後漲勢持續延伸。

  展望第二財季，超微電腦預估營收介於195億至215億美元之間。

  ## 轉型全方位資料中心解決方案供應商

  執行長梁見後（Charles Liang）在財報說明中表示，公司正加速從伺服器製造商轉型為AI與IT全方位資料中心解決方案供應商。超微電腦透過名為DCBBS的模組化方案，整合運算、儲存、網路、電源供應、液態冷卻與管理軟體，並將部署及後續維運服務與硬體銷售捆綁提供。

  公司表示，部分整合與測試作業在出廠前即已完成，大幅縮短客戶建置新資料中心產能所需的時間。超微電腦指出，這套做法搭配全球製造產能，有助客戶壓縮建置時程、有效管控總持有成本。此一策略讓公司從單一設備供應商蛻變為更廣泛的基礎架構整合夥伴。

  ## 資產負債表

  截至9月底，超微電腦持有約69億美元的現金及約當現金。銀行借款與可轉換公司債合計約86億美元。本季營業活動現金流量為淨流出4.34億美元，主要受庫存與應收帳款增加所推動。資本支出及投資合計約4,700萬美元。

  公司表示，為因應業務快速成長的需求而提高庫存水位，並持續擴大全球製造布局，以履行客戶訂單、加快交貨速度。超微電腦表示，現有的現金部位足以支應日常營運、零組件採購及產能擴建所需的資金。

  ## 在AI支出不確定性中扮演指標角色

  在外界持續質疑大規模AI基礎建設支出能否延續的氛圍下，投資人將超微電腦視為整體AI伺服器需求的指標性觀測對象。《工商時報》報導指出，公司上調年度營收展望，意味著即便市場仍在辯論AI驅動資本支出能否持久，AI伺服器需求依然強勁。超微電腦創立於1993年，深耕運算基礎架構領域逾三十年。

  超微電腦目前對2027財年的全年營收展望為720億至820億美元，代表公司就未來一年所能服務的AI相關資料中心建設規模所提出的最新公開預測。
