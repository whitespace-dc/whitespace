body_en: |
  OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman met with roughly 50 Samsung Group executives in Yongin, south of Seoul, on Oct. 31 to advance cooperation on memory chips and artificial intelligence infrastructure, Samsung said in a statement, as shortages across a broad range of memory products strain the global electronics industry and drive chip prices higher.

  The gathering brought together leadership from Samsung's electronics, financial, and biotechnology affiliates. Discussions centred on how AI can reshape industries, corporate decision-making, and business opportunities over the next decade, according to the company.

  ## Stargate partnership and offshore data centres

  The Oct. 31 meeting extends a previously announced partnership under which Samsung agreed to supply memory chips for OpenAI's Stargate infrastructure project. The two companies are also cooperating on AI data centres, including floating offshore facilities, Samsung said.

  Memory chips — the components that store and move data inside servers — account for roughly a third of data centre spending, making Samsung's position as a leading advanced memory manufacturer a critical variable for any large-scale AI build-out.

  ## Scale of AI infrastructure investment

  The five largest players in AI infrastructure are collectively planning to spend more than US$3 trillion in the coming years, according to Bloomberg. That level of demand is intensifying competition for memory supplies across the industry, with data centre operators pressing chipmakers to expand capacity faster than current production schedules allow. Technology executives have warned publicly that chip output is struggling to keep pace.

  ## South Korea as a supply chain destination

  Altman is the latest in a series of American technology leaders to travel to South Korea, which leads global production of advanced memory chips and where AI adoption is accelerating among businesses and consumers. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and Advanced Micro Devices CEO Lisa Su have each visited the country twice since 2025 to strengthen supply chain ties, Bloomberg reported.

  ## Samsung's internal AI push

  Beyond its chip supply relationships with outside partners, Samsung is also expanding AI use within its own operations, applying the technology across research, manufacturing, marketing, and corporate functions, the company said.

  ## What the visit signals

  The repeated, high-level visits by the chief executives of the world's leading AI companies to South Korea's chipmakers reflect the degree to which memory supply has become a strategic constraint on AI infrastructure expansion. With more than US$3 trillion in planned data centre investment requiring a reliable flow of advanced memory, partnerships struck directly at the CEO level are becoming a standard mechanism for securing priority access to constrained supply.

  Samsung said the Stargate memory supply agreement and data centre cooperation — including the floating offshore facility component — remain active work streams following the Oct. 31 meeting.

  OpenAI執行長山姆·阿特曼（Sam Altman）於10月31日在首爾以南的龍仁市，與三星集團約50位高層主管展開會談，推進雙方在記憶體晶片與人工智慧基礎建設上的合作。三星在聲明中指出，多項記憶體產品的短缺正持續衝擊全球電子業，並驅使晶片價格節節攀升。

  此次聚會匯集了三星旗下電子、金融及生技事業群的領導人。據三星表示，會談聚焦於人工智慧如何在未來十年間重塑各產業樣貌、企業決策模式，以及由此衍生的商業機會。

  ## Stargate合作案與離岸資料中心

  10月31日的會面是雙方既有合作關係的延伸。根據先前宣布的合作框架，三星已同意為OpenAI的「Stargate」基礎建設計畫供應記憶體晶片。三星表示，兩家公司也在AI資料中心方面展開合作，其中包括浮動式離岸設施。

  記憶體晶片負責在伺服器內部儲存與傳輸資料，約佔資料中心總支出的三分之一，使三星身為頂尖先進記憶體製造商的地位，成為任何大規模AI建置計畫中不可或缺的關鍵變數。

  ## AI基礎建設投資規模

  據彭博社（Bloomberg）報導，全球AI基礎建設領域的五大業者合計規劃在未來數年投入逾3兆美元。如此龐大的需求正加劇業界對記憶體供應的爭奪，資料中心業者紛紛要求晶片製造商以快於現行生產排程的速度擴充產能。多位科技業高層也已公開示警，指出晶片產量難以跟上市場需求的腳步。

  ## 南韓躍升為供應鏈重鎮

  阿特曼此行，是近期一連串美國科技龍頭造訪南韓的最新案例。南韓是全球先進記憶體晶片的最大產地，人工智慧在當地企業與消費市場的應用也正快速普及。據彭博社報導，輝達（Nvidia）執行長黃仁勳與超微（AMD）執行長蘇姿丰自2025年以來均已兩度訪問南韓，持續深化供應鏈合作關係。

  ## 三星內部AI布局

  除了對外的晶片供應合作，三星也積極在自身營運中擴大AI的應用範疇，將相關技術全面導入研發、製造、行銷與企業管理等各領域。

  ## 此次訪問的深層意義

  全球頂尖AI公司執行長接連親赴南韓晶片製造商進行高層訪問，充分反映出記憶體供應已成為制約AI基礎建設擴張的戰略性瓶頸。在逾3兆美元的資料中心投資計畫亟需穩定先進記憶體供貨的當下，由執行長層級直接洽談的合作協議，正逐漸成為在吃緊供應中確保優先取得資格的標準機制。

  三星表示，Stargate記憶體供應協議與資料中心合作案——包括浮動式離岸設施的相關項目——在10月31日的會面後，仍是雙方持續推進中的工作重點。
