body_en: |
  Nvidia announced on November 12, 2026 that co-founder Jensen Huang has resigned as chief executive officer and from the company's board of directors, the company said. The exit removes the architect of Nvidia's dominant position in the artificial intelligence chip market from day-to-day leadership.

  In the period before the announcement, Huang had been the subject of published reports on two separate matters: an alleged affair with a senior Nvidia executive, and a connection to a former executive at a Singapore-based data-center operating company whom U.S. authorities suspected of acting as a Chinese intelligence asset, according to Nikkei xTECH.

  ## Context and market significance

  Nikkei xTECH described Nvidia as "the central bank of AI," a reference to the company's role as the primary supplier of graphics processing units, or GPUs, that underpin the infrastructure buildout driving the current AI investment cycle. Nvidia's chips power the accelerated computing clusters used by hyperscale cloud operators and the emerging class of AI-focused cloud providers the publication termed "neo-cloud" companies.

  The publication characterized Huang's exit as a potential inflection point for the broader AI boom, citing two concurrent pressures: credit concerns surrounding neo-cloud operators, and uncertainty in AI-related markets following U.S. midterm election results. Nikkei xTECH framed the resignation as a possible signal that the AI investment cycle is entering a corrective phase, though the article noted that assessment as an open question rather than a settled conclusion.

  Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and had led the company continuously since its inception. His public profile became closely identified with the surge in demand for AI accelerator hardware that began accelerating sharply in 2023. The company has not publicly named a successor, according to the available reporting.

  《日經xTECH》將輝達形容為「AI界的中央銀行」，藉此點出該公司作為繪圖處理器（GPU）主要供應商的核心地位——而GPU正是驅動當前AI投資浪潮、支撐基礎建設大規模擴張的關鍵硬體。輝達的晶片為超大規模雲端業者的加速運算叢集提供動力，同時也供應給該媒體所稱「新雲端」（neo-cloud）業者，亦即一批以AI為核心業務的新興雲端服務商。

  《日經xTECH》認為，黃仁勳的出走或許是整個AI榮景的一個轉折點，並點出兩股同步施壓的力量：新雲端業者所面臨的信用隱憂，以及美國期中選舉結果所引發的AI相關市場不確定性。該媒體將黃仁勳辭職定調為AI投資週期可能進入修正階段的潛在訊號，不過文中也指出，此一判斷目前仍是開放性問題，尚無定論。

  黃仁勳於1993年共同創辦輝達，自公司成立以來始終未曾間斷地掌舵。他的公眾形象已與2023年起急速攀升的AI加速運算硬體需求緊密綁定。根據現有報導，輝達迄今尚未公開宣布接班人選。
