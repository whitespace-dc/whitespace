body_en: |
  ASRock Industrial has released the AI Box-A395, a barebones small-tower PC built around AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395 processor, targeting industrial environments and portable local AI inference workloads rather than the consumer desktop mini-PC market the chip has most commonly served.

  The unit pairs AMD's Ryzen AI Max+ 395 — a 16-core, 32-thread processor running up to 5.1GHz with an integrated Radeon 8060S graphics unit carrying 40 compute units — with 128GB of LPDDR5X-8000 memory soldered directly to the board. Two M.2 2280 PCIe Gen4 x4 slots for storage are included but ship empty in the barebones configuration. The review unit tested by ServeTheHome ran Ubuntu Linux 26.04; no operating system is included at purchase.

  ## How the form factor differs from competing systems

  The AI Box-A395 departs from the low-profile, wide-and-flat chassis that has dominated the Ryzen AI Max mini-PC segment. ASRock Industrial chose a vertical micro-tower orientation using a mini-ITX motherboard — a compact PC board standard measuring roughly 170mm square — inside an aluminum chassis measuring 200 x 100 x 232 mm and weighing 2.8 kg. Mounting points on top of the chassis accommodate an optional carrying handle.

  The vertical orientation reduces the system's two-dimensional floor footprint, which ServeTheHome noted is relevant for bench or industrial rack-adjacent deployments. The aluminum body and rows of vertical vent holes prompted a comparison in the review to Apple's 2003 Power Mac G5 enclosure.

  A 400W internal power supply unit is integrated into the chassis, unlike the external power bricks used by many competing systems in this category. The front face of the unit carries no ports, vents, or buttons beyond the ASRock Industrial logo — a design choice ServeTheHome said reflects that the machine is intended to run unattended rather than to serve as a daily-use workstation.

  ## Port layout and connectivity

  All high-speed connectivity sits on the rear panel behind a standard ATX I/O shield cutout. The rear carries two USB-C ports at 40Gbps using the USB4 standard, one USB-C port at 10Gbps, two USB-A ports at 10Gbps, two HDMI 2.1 outputs, a 10 Gigabit Ethernet port driven by a Marvell AQC113 controller, a 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet port using a Realtek RTL8125BG controller, and 3.5mm line-out and mic-in audio jacks.

  The 10GbE port is a notable inclusion. ServeTheHome said 10 Gigabit Ethernet has been rarer than desirable on Ryzen AI Max+ 395 systems given their price and performance tier, making the dual-port network configuration — both a 10GbE and a 2.5GbE interface — a meaningful differentiator.

  Display output runs through two paths. The Radeon 8060S drives both HDMI 2.1 ports natively, and all three USB-C ports support DisplayPort Alternate Mode — a protocol that carries video signals over USB-C connectors — allowing the system to drive up to four monitors simultaneously, which is the maximum the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 silicon supports.

  The top of the chassis holds the power button and the only non-rear I/O: two USB-A ports. Those ports run at 480Mbps, the USB 2.x standard, not the faster USB 3.x speeds found on the rear ports. ServeTheHome cited the absence of any high-speed front-accessible ports as evidence the A395 is built for fixed industrial deployment rather than workstation use, calling ASRock Industrial's own "AI workstation PC" marketing "a bit too optimistic."

  Wireless connectivity is provided by a MediaTek MT7925 module supporting Wi-Fi 7 at 2x2 and Bluetooth 5.4.

  ## Market context

  AMD introduced the Ryzen AI Max family — also known by the engineering code name Strix Halo — positioning the chips across laptops and desktop systems. ServeTheHome noted that the product ecosystem around those chips expanded faster than AMD anticipated, driven primarily by demand for always-on local AI inference systems capable of running models and agents on-device. The mini-PC segment in particular, the review said, "has grown like crazy" in response to that demand.

  The AI Box-A395 is among the first systems in the segment to use a distinctly non-consumer form factor. The barebones configuration — no OS, no storage — and the industrial-grade aluminum chassis position it toward buyers who need the Ryzen AI Max+ 395's processing capability in environments where unattended operation and ruggedness take precedence over front-panel convenience or out-of-box setup.

  The AI Box-A395 is available through Newegg, according to ServeTheHome.

  ## ASRock Industrial AI Box-A395：以 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 打造的工業直立小塔

  ASRock Industrial 正式推出 AI Box-A395，這是一款以 AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 處理器為核心的裸機小塔型電腦，鎖定工業現場與可攜式本地端 AI 推論工作負載，而非該晶片迄今最常服務的消費級桌上型迷你電腦市場。

  此機搭載 AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395——一顆擁有 16 核心、32 執行緒、最高時脈達 5.1GHz、並內建具備 40 個運算單元之 Radeon 8060S 繪圖核心的處理器——並配置 128GB LPDDR5X-8000 記憶體直接焊接於主機板上。機器提供兩組 M.2 2280 PCIe Gen4 x4 儲存插槽，但裸機版本出廠時不隨附儲存裝置。ServeTheHome 所測試的送測機搭載 Ubuntu Linux 26.04；購買時不含作業系統。

  ## 機身外觀與競爭產品的差異

  AI Box-A395 有別於目前 Ryzen AI Max 迷你電腦市場中普遍流行的低矮扁平機身設計。ASRock Industrial 選擇採用 mini-ITX 主機板——一種邊長約 170mm 的緊湊型電腦板標準——並以垂直直立小塔的方式配置，鋁製機箱尺寸為 200 × 100 × 232mm，重量 2.8 公斤。機箱頂部設有安裝孔，可選配攜行提把。

  直立設計縮小了系統的二維水平占地面積，ServeTheHome 指出這對工作台或工業機架旁的部署場景相當實用。鋁製機身搭配縱向排列的散熱孔，令該媒體在評測中將其外觀比擬為蘋果 2003 年推出的 Power Mac G5 機箱。

  機箱內整合了一組 400W 內建電源供應器，有別於同類型許多競品採用的外接變壓器設計。機器正面除了 ASRock Industrial 標誌外，不設任何連接埠、散熱孔或按鍵——ServeTheHome 表示，這項設計取向反映出此機定位為無人值守運行，而非一般日常使用的工作站。

  ## 連接埠配置與連接能力

  所有高速連接埠均集中於後方面板，配合標準 ATX I/O 擋板開口配置。後方面板提供：兩組 40Gbps USB4 規格 USB-C 連接埠、一組 10Gbps USB-C 連接埠、兩組 10Gbps USB-A 連接埠、兩組 HDMI 2.1 影像輸出、一組由 Marvell AQC113 控制器驅動的 10 Gigabit 乙太網路埠、一組採用 Realtek RTL8125BG 控制器的 2.5 Gigabit 乙太網路埠，以及 3.5mm 音訊輸出與麥克風輸入接口。

  10GbE 連接埠是一大亮點。ServeTheHome 指出，考量到 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 系統的售價與效能等級，10 Gigabit 乙太網路在同類產品中的普及率仍不如預期，因此同時具備 10GbE 與 2.5GbE 的雙網路埠配置，成為此機頗具分量的差異化優勢。

  影像輸出提供兩種路徑：Radeon 8060S 原生驅動兩組 HDMI 2.1 輸出，三組 USB-C 連接埠亦全數支援 DisplayPort Alternate Mode——一種透過 USB-C 接頭傳輸影像訊號的協定——使系統最多可同時驅動四台顯示器，也是 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 晶片所支援的上限。

  機箱頂部設有電源按鍵，以及機器唯一的非後置外部連接埠：兩組 USB-A 連接埠。這兩組頂置連接埠僅提供 480Mbps 的 USB 2.x 傳輸速率，並非後方連接埠所具備的較高速 USB 3.x 規格。ServeTheHome 以正面完全缺乏高速連接埠為據，認為 A395 是專為固定式工業部署而設計，並稱 ASRock Industrial 自家標榜的「AI 工作站電腦」行銷說法「略顯樂觀」。

  無線連接方面，機器配備 MediaTek MT7925 模組，支援 Wi-Fi 7（2x2 天線配置）及藍牙 5.4。

  ## 市場背景

  AMD 推出 Ryzen AI Max 系列——工程代號 Strix Halo——定位橫跨筆記型電腦與桌上型電腦市場。ServeTheHome 指出，圍繞這系列晶片所形成的產品生態系，擴張速度超乎 AMD 原本預期，主要驅動力來自外界對能在裝置端持續執行 AI 模型與智慧代理的本地端 AI 推論系統的強烈需求。評測文章表示，迷你電腦市場尤其因應此一需求「爆炸性成長」。

  AI Box-A395 是該市場中率先採用明顯非消費級外觀規格的產品之一。裸機出廠（不含作業系統與儲存裝置）的配置方式，以及工業等級鋁製機身，使其鎖定的買家群體，是那些需要在無人值守操作與耐用性優先於前置面板便利性或開箱即用設定的環境中，運用 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 運算能力的用戶。
