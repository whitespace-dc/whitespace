body_en: |
  Arm, the Cambridge-based chip architecture company owned by Japan's SoftBank, is positioning its processor technology for a larger role in 5G virtual radio access networks (vRAN), an area where the company has so far achieved less success than in smartphones or data centers, according to reporting by Light Reading.

  ## Arm's Current Foothold in 5G

  Arm's processor cores — the fundamental computing building blocks that the company licenses to chip manufacturers — already appear in 5G equipment sold by Nokia. The Finnish vendor has moved away from Intel processors and now uses an Arm-based central processing unit (CPU) manufactured by Marvell Technology to handle what the industry calls "Layer 2 plus" functions. In radio access network terminology, Layer 2 covers data-link processing tasks that are less computationally intensive than the physical-layer signal processing below them.

  For Layer 1 — the most demanding part of the RAN stack, which handles the heavy mathematical processing required to encode and decode radio signals — Marvell supplies Nokia with a separate custom chip that also incorporates some Arm technology.

  ## Arm's Broader Business Context

  Arm derives most of its revenue from licensing its chip blueprints to makers of smartphone processors, but the company has been expanding into other markets. Its latest earnings release lists vehicles and robotics among growth areas, alongside artificial intelligence. The company has also entered the data center market, where processors based on the x86 architecture from Intel and AMD have historically dominated.

  In what the company described as a significant departure from its traditional licensing model, Arm began manufacturing its own CPUs this year.

  ## Significance for the RAN Market

  The mobile network equipment market has long relied on a mix of general-purpose x86 processors and purpose-built silicon. Nokia's shift toward Arm-based silicon from Marvell for Layer 2 processing illustrates how that mix is changing. Whether Arm can extend that presence to the more demanding Layer 1 functions — or attract additional equipment vendors — will determine how much of the vRAN silicon market the company and its partners can capture.

  Arm's existing role in Nokia's RAN products, through Marvell-designed chips covering both Layer 2 and elements of Layer 1, represents the most concrete evidence so far of Arm architecture gaining ground in commercial 5G infrastructure.
body_zh: |
  ## Arm強攻虛擬RAN　諾基亞率先從英特爾轉向Marvell Arm架構處理器

  根據《Light Reading》的報導，總部位於英國劍橋、由日本軟銀持有的晶片架構公司Arm，正將旗下處理器技術定位為5G虛擬無線接入網路（vRAN）的重要角色。相較於在智慧型手機或資料中心市場的亮眼成績，Arm在行動網路領域的斬獲向來有限，如今正謀求更大突破。

  ## Arm在5G領域的現有立足點

  Arm的處理器核心——也就是該公司授權給晶片製造商的基礎運算模組——已出現在諾基亞銷售的5G設備之中。這家芬蘭設備商已逐步捨棄英特爾處理器，改為採用Marvell Technology製造的Arm架構中央處理器（CPU），用以處理業界所稱的「第二層（Layer 2）Plus」功能。在無線接入網路的術語中，第二層涵蓋資料鏈路處理任務，其運算強度低於下層的實體層訊號處理。

  至於第一層（Layer 1）——也就是RAN堆疊中運算需求最為龐大的部分，負責處理無線訊號編解碼所需的大量數學運算——Marvell同樣為諾基亞供應一款獨立的客製化晶片，其中也整合了部分Arm技術。

  ## Arm更宏觀的業務布局

  Arm的營收主要來自將晶片設計藍圖授權給智慧型手機處理器製造商，但近年來該公司持續向其他市場擴張。在最新一季的財報中，Arm將車用電子與機器人列為成長動能，並與人工智慧並列。此外，Arm也進軍資料中心市場，而該市場長期以來由英特爾與AMD的x86架構處理器主導。

  在Arm自身看來，今年起自行生產CPU是對其傳統授權模式的重大轉變。

  ## 對RAN市場的意義

  行動網路設備市場長期以來混用通用型x86處理器與專用矽晶片。諾基亞在第二層處理上轉向Marvell的Arm架構矽晶片，正是這種生態正在改變的具體體現。Arm能否將觸角延伸至運算需求更高的第一層功能，或吸引更多設備廠商跟進，將決定Arm及其合作夥伴最終能在vRAN矽晶片市場中拿下多大份額。

  Arm透過Marvell設計的晶片——涵蓋第二層及部分第一層功能——已在諾基亞的RAN產品中扮演一定角色，這是迄今為止Arm架構在商用5G基礎設施中站穩腳跟最為具體的佐證。
