  ## Mitac commits $106M to San Jose plant equipment

  Mitac (Taiwan stock code: 3706), the Taiwanese server manufacturer, disclosed on July 16 that it will spend approximately $106 million — roughly NT$34.07 billion — on machinery and equipment purchased from Megawatt Construction, Inc. for its new plant in San Jose, California, the company said. The equipment is intended to meet AI server production requirements and power infrastructure needs at the site.

  The San Jose facility is being developed in two phases. Phase one capacity is expected to come online in the third quarter of 2026, with phase two scheduled for completion in the fourth quarter, Mitac said. The newly announced equipment purchase is earmarked for the phase two expansion.

  ## US manufacturing footprint

  The San Jose investment is part of a broader push by Mitac to build manufacturing capacity in the United States. Beyond San Jose, the company said a new plant in Newark, California is also planned to begin operations in the third quarter. A third site in Fremont, California comprises three factory buildings; the first of those is expected to be commissioned in the fourth quarter of 2026, with the remaining buildings coming online in 2027.

  Mitac said the primary function of its US plants is final assembly and shipment of products in rack form — the industry-standard enclosure that houses and interconnects servers, networking gear, and storage in a single unit. The company described tariff flexibility as the central rationale for the US manufacturing strategy, saying the plants allow it to allocate resources and adjust the proportion of assembly work performed domestically in response to changes in trade policy.

  ## Record revenue on hyperscale demand

  Mitac reported June revenue of NT$15.683 billion, a company record, rising 14.99% from May and 80.93% from June 2025. Cumulative revenue for the first half of 2026 reached NT$72.818 billion, up 44.8% year-on-year.

  The company attributed the growth primarily to hyperscale cloud data center rack customers and cloud service providers, which it said have been expanding purchases of its products. Automotive electronics, smart IoT devices, and vehicle telematics products also contributed incremental volume growth, Mitac said.

  Institutional investors expect Mitac's second-half performance to sustain growth as customer component availability improves and the product mix shifts, the company said.

  ## Subsidiary broadens product strategy

  Mitac's subsidiary MiTAC Computing Technology exhibited at the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) on July 17, presenting a rack and liquid-cooling product lineup under the theme of liquid-cooling readiness for AI workloads. The company showed four rack configurations:

  - A 52U high-density AI liquid-cooled rack
  - A G8825Z5 AI GPU air-cooled rack
  - An OCP ORv3-standard liquid-cooled rack (OCP ORv3 is an Open Compute Project specification for high-density, open-architecture rack infrastructure)
  - A TS70A-B8056 high-density storage rack integrating a DDN storage solution

  MiTAC Computing Technology said its strategy is shifting from pure hardware supply toward rack-level, cluster-level, and ecosystem-level integrated solutions — a move the company framed as a response to enterprise customers' transition from generative AI toward agentic AI, meaning AI systems that execute multi-step tasks autonomously rather than simply generating content on request.

  ## Significance

  The $106 million equipment order, combined with simultaneous build-outs in Newark and Fremont, signals that Mitac is accelerating a multi-site US manufacturing presence at a moment when AI infrastructure spending by hyperscale customers is translating directly into record quarterly revenue. The company's stated rationale — tariff-driven assembly flexibility — reflects a broader industry pattern of Taiwanese server manufacturers diversifying production geography. With phase one of the San Jose plant set to start in the third quarter and the Fremont site extending construction into 2027, Mitac's US capacity ramp is expected to continue well into next year.

  ## 神基斥1.06億美元採購聖荷西廠設備

  台灣伺服器廠商神基科技（台股代號：3706）於7月16日揭露，將向Megawatt Construction, Inc.採購約1.06億美元、折合新台幣約340.7億元的機械與設備，用於其位於美國加州聖荷西的新廠。神基表示，此批設備旨在滿足該廠址AI伺服器生產所需，並支應相關電力基礎設施的建置要求。

  聖荷西廠規劃分兩期開發。神基表示，一期產能預計於2026年第三季正式投產，二期則預定於第四季完工。此次公告的設備採購款項，專用於二期擴建工程。

  ## 美國製造版圖持續擴張

  聖荷西廠的投資，是神基大力布局美國製造產能整體戰略的一環。除聖荷西外，神基表示，位於加州紐瓦克（Newark）的另一座新廠，同樣預計於第三季開始投產。第三個廠址則位於加州費利蒙（Fremont），共涵蓋三棟廠房，其中第一棟預計於2026年第四季完工驗收，其餘廠房則將於2027年陸續投入運營。

  神基表示，其美國廠的主要功能為最終組裝，並以機架形式出貨。所謂機架，即業界標準的機架封裝形式，可將伺服器、網路設備及儲存裝置整合於單一機架中並相互連接。公司表示，關稅彈性是推動美國製造策略的核心考量，美國廠的設置讓神基得以靈活調配資源，並依貿易政策的變化，彈性調整在美境內執行組裝作業的比重。

  ## 超大規模需求推升營收創高

  神基公告6月營收達新台幣156.83億元，創下公司歷史新高，較5月成長14.99%，較2025年6月同期大幅增長80.93%。2026年上半年累計營收為新台幣728.18億元，年增44.8%。

  神基將此一成長主要歸因於超大規模雲端資料中心機架客戶及雲端服務業者的強勁需求，表示上述客戶對其產品的採購規模持續擴大。此外，車用電子、智慧物聯網裝置及車輛遠端資訊系統（telematics）產品，亦貢獻了額外的出貨量成長。

  神基表示，法人投資機構預期，隨著客戶零組件供應狀況持續改善、產品組合結構進一步優化，公司下半年表現將維持成長態勢。

  ## 子公司拓展產品策略

  神基旗下子公司神雲科技（MiTAC Computing Technology）於7月17日參展2026世界人工智慧大會（WAIC），以「AI工作負載液冷就緒」為主題，展出一系列機架與液冷產品。參展的四款機架配置包括：

  - 52U高密度AI液冷機架
  - G8825Z5 AI GPU氣冷機架
  - 符合OCP ORv3規範的液冷機架（OCP ORv3為開放運算專案針對高密度、開放架構機架基礎設施所制定的規格標準）
  - 搭載DDN儲存解決方案的TS70A-B8056高密度儲存機架

  神雲科技表示，公司策略正從單純的硬體供應，逐步轉向機架層級、叢集層級及生態系層級的整合解決方案；此一轉型是對應企業客戶從生成式AI邁向代理式AI（agentic AI）趨勢的戰略回應。所謂代理式AI，意指能夠自主執行多步驟任務的AI系統，而非僅止於依需求生成內容。

  ## 市場意義

  此次1.06億美元的設備採購案，加上紐瓦克與費利蒙廠區同步推進的建廠工程，顯示神基正加速在美國建立多點製造布局——而此時，超大規模客戶的AI基礎設施支出，正直接推動公司營收創下單月歷史新高。神基以關稅驅動的組裝彈性作為核心論據，映照出台灣伺服器廠商分散生產地域的更廣泛產業趨勢。隨著聖荷西廠一期預計於第三季啟動、費利蒙廠區的建設期則延伸至2027年，神基在美國的產能擴充步伐，預計將持續加速至明年。
