body_en: |
  Marvell Technology disclosed on 19 August that it will design a broad range of custom semiconductors for Google's Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) ecosystem under a commercial agreement signed 29 July, issuing Google a warrant to acquire up to 58.97 million Marvell shares at $206.58 each, worth roughly $12.2 billion if fully exercised.

  The chip types covered include AI inference accelerators, storage controllers, network interface controllers, memory interface controllers, and near-memory compute devices. Marvell's stock rose more than 10% on the announcement; shares in Broadcom, Google's longstanding custom chip partner, fell more than 5% as markets weighed the addition of a second supplier inside Google's TPU supply chain.

  ## Deal structure and scale

  The warrant vests in tranches tied to purchasing milestones: one tranche unlocks for every $500 million in qualifying revenue, running through Marvell's fiscal year 2033. If all tranches vest, the deal implies as much as $120 billion in cumulative custom silicon revenue for Marvell. At full exercise, Google would become Marvell's fifth-largest shareholder.

  Broadcom remains Google's primary custom chip partner under a separate long-term agreement extended in April that runs through 2031. The Marvell deal adds a second supplier to Google's supply chain rather than replacing the existing relationship.

  ## Equity-linked procurement becoming standard

  The Google-Marvell arrangement fits a broader pattern in hyperscaler chip procurement. In March, Nvidia made a $2 billion strategic investment in Marvell tied to the NVLink Fusion platform. In October 2025, AMD reached an agreement with OpenAI under which OpenAI received an option to acquire up to roughly 10% of AMD in exchange for chip purchase commitments. Marvell chief executive Matt Murphy has previously described the company's custom silicon strategy as central to its growth.

  With the Google deal, Marvell now runs active custom silicon programs with all three major U.S. hyperscalers: Amazon's Trainium application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) work, Microsoft's Maia program, and Google's TPU-adjacent chips. Equity stakes, rather than standard supply contracts, have emerged as the mechanism hyperscalers use to secure chip capacity while chipmakers gain multi-year demand visibility for fabrication and research commitments, according to Capacity Media.

  ## Concentration risk

  Marvell's presence inside all three hyperscaler ecosystems provides the company with broad revenue diversification but also concentrates design and fabrication dependency for the industry. If Marvell's capacity becomes constrained, disruption would propagate across three separate hyperscaler build programs simultaneously, a risk that Capacity Media says deserves more attention in data center site selection and construction timelines.

  ## Infrastructure implications

  Custom inference-optimized silicon, memory processing units designed to sit alongside TPUs, and near-memory compute architectures carry different power and thermal profiles than general-purpose graphics processing unit (GPU) clusters. A data center built around GPU assumptions does not necessarily adapt cleanly to a mixed TPU and memory processing unit rack configuration. As Google, Amazon, and Microsoft diversify their silicon suppliers at the same time, operators need visibility into which chip architecture is arriving at which facility, and when, to plan power and cooling procurement accurately, according to Capacity Media.

  ## Investment and financing implications

  Tying vendor equity to purchasing milestones through 2033 embeds a long-dated option on hyperscaler capital expenditure discipline into Marvell's capitalization table and, by extension, into how lenders and asset-backed securities structures underwriting data center assets should treat compute supply risk. Capacity Media's analysis argues that vendor concentration, chip architecture diversification, and equity-linked supply agreements are becoming as material to digital infrastructure due diligence as power availability and permitting timelines.

  ## Outlook

  Broadcom's parallel agreement with Google extends through 2031, and the purchasing milestone tranches in the Marvell deal run through fiscal 2033, leaving both supplier relationships active simultaneously for at least the next five years.
body_zh: |
  邁威爾科技（Marvell Technology）於 8 月 19 日揭露，該公司將依據 7 月 29 日簽署的商業協議，為 Google 張量處理器（TPU）生態系設計一系列客製半導體，並向 Google 發行認購權證，可認購最多 5,897 萬股邁威爾股票，每股行使價 206.58 美元，若全數行使，總價值約達 122 億美元。

  涵蓋的晶片類型包括 AI 推論加速器、儲存控制器、網路介面控制器、記憶體介面控制器及近記憶體運算裝置。消息公布後，邁威爾股價單日漲逾一成；Google 長期客製晶片合作夥伴博通（Broadcom）的股價則下跌逾 5%，市場正評估 Google TPU 供應鏈納入第二家供應商後的整體影響。

  ## 合約結構與規模

  認購權證依採購里程碑分批解鎖：每達成 5 億美元合格營收即解鎖一個批次，有效期限延伸至邁威爾 2033 會計年度。若所有批次均完成解鎖，此合約意味著邁威爾可累計取得高達 1,200 億美元的客製晶片營收。若 Google 全數行使認購權，將成為邁威爾第五大股東。

  博通依據另一項於今年 4 月延長、有效期至 2031 年的長期協議，仍維持 Google 主要客製晶片合作夥伴的地位。邁威爾此次合約為 Google 供應鏈新增一家供應商，而非取代既有合作關係。

  ## 股權連結採購成為業界新常態

  Google 與邁威爾的安排，符合超大規模資料中心業者在晶片採購上更廣泛的趨勢。今年 3 月，輝達（NVIDIA）以 NVLink Fusion 平台為由，對邁威爾進行 20 億美元的策略性投資。2025 年 10 月，AMD 與 OpenAI 達成協議，OpenAI 以晶片採購承諾換取可認購 AMD 約一成股份的選擇權。邁威爾執行長麥特・墨菲（Matt Murphy）過去曾多次表示，客製晶片策略是公司成長的核心。

  憑藉此次 Google 合約，邁威爾目前已與美國三大超大規模業者均設有進行中的客製晶片專案：亞馬遜（Amazon）的 Trainium 特殊應用積體電路（ASIC）業務、微軟（Microsoft）的 Maia 計畫，以及 Google 的 TPU 周邊晶片。據 Capacity Media 指出，股權持股而非標準供應合約，已成為超大規模業者鎖定晶片產能的機制，讓晶片製造商同時獲得多年需求能見度，以支撐產能建置與研發投入。

  ## 集中風險

  邁威爾同時布局三大超大規模業者的生態系，雖帶來廣泛的營收多元化，卻也使整個產業的設計與製造依賴高度集中於單一廠商。一旦邁威爾產能出現瓶頸，衝擊將同步擴散至三家超大規模業者各自的建置計畫。Capacity Media 指出，這項風險在資料中心選址與建設時程規劃上，值得獲得更多關注。

  ## 基礎設施影響

  客製化推論最佳化晶片、設計以緊鄰 TPU 運作的記憶體處理單元，以及近記憶體運算架構，其功耗與散熱特性均與通用型繪圖處理器（GPU）叢集不同。以 GPU 假設為前提所建置的資料中心，未必能順暢地因應 TPU 與記憶體處理單元混合機架的配置需求。隨著 Google、亞馬遜與微軟同步分散晶片供應來源，業者需要掌握各晶片架構抵達各設施的時程與品項，才能精確規劃電力與冷卻設備的採購，Capacity Media 如此說明。

  ## 投資與融資影響

  將供應商股權與採購里程碑綁定至 2033 年，等同於在邁威爾的資本結構中嵌入一項與超大規模業者資本支出紀律掛鉤的長期選擇權，進而影響承作資料中心資產的貸款機構及資產擔保證券結構，對運算供應風險的評估方式。Capacity Media 的分析認為，供應商集中度、晶片架構多元化，以及股權連結供應協議，在數位基礎設施的盡職調查中，其重要性已不亞於電力供應能力與取得建築許可的時程。

  ## 展望

  博通與 Google 的平行協議有效期延伸至 2031 年，邁威爾合約中的採購里程碑批次則延續至 2033 會計年度，意味著兩組供應商關係在未來至少五年內將同步並存。
