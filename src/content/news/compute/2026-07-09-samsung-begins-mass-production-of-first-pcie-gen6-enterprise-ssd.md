body_en: |
  Samsung Electronics has started mass production of the PM1763, its first solid-state drive built on the PCIe 6.0 (Peripheral Component Interconnect Express Generation 6) interface, targeting AI and high-performance computing server environments.

  The drive delivers sequential read speeds of up to 28,400 megabytes per second (MB/s) and sequential write speeds of up to 21,900 MB/s in its 16-terabyte configuration, according to Samsung. The company said those figures represent more than double the throughput of the PM1753, the previous-generation model.

  ## Performance and form factor

  The PM1763 is built on Samsung's 9th-generation V-NAND (Vertical NAND) flash memory and a newly developed 4-nanometer controller. The drive ships in 4TB, 8TB, and 16TB capacities in the E3.S form factor, a compact sled format suited to dense server deployments.

  Samsung said the drive's throughput allows it to transfer a 40-gigabyte large language model (LLM) in approximately 1.4 seconds, which the company framed as reducing latency between processors and AI accelerators during inference and training workloads.

  ## Liquid cooling and security features

  The PM1763 is designed for liquid-cooled server environments, Samsung said. Newer AI servers increasingly move cooling fans out of compute racks in favor of all-liquid thermal management, which requires storage devices that can operate in fully liquid-cooled configurations.

  On the security side, the drive supports post-quantum cryptography (PQC) algorithms, designed to protect stored data against threats posed by future quantum computers. It also supports TDISP (TEE Device Interface Security Protocol), a specification for securing data pathways in virtualized server environments where a drive may be shared across multiple workloads.

  ## Why PCIe Gen6 and why now

  PCIe Gen6 doubles the theoretical bandwidth available over PCIe Gen5, the current mainstream server interface. Samsung noted the transition is just beginning: some hyperscale central processing units, NVIDIA's Vera processor, and AMD's forthcoming EPYC Venice processor are among the first platforms adding PCIe Gen6 support.

  The PM1763's capacity range, capped at 16TB rather than the higher densities seen in some enterprise SSDs, reflects a focus on high-performance local storage within servers rather than bulk data archiving. Samsung said that approach mirrors the pattern seen in earlier PCIe generation transitions, where the newest interface first appeared in high-performance, lower-capacity drives before extending to higher-density products.

  With PCIe Gen6 server platforms from multiple processor vendors moving toward launch, the PM1763 entering mass production marks storage infrastructure beginning to keep pace with the new interface.
body_zh: |
  三星電子宣布，旗下首款基於 PCIe 6.0（Peripheral Component Interconnect Express 第六代）介面打造的企業級固態硬碟 PM1763 已正式進入量產階段，主攻 AI 及高效能運算伺服器環境。

  以 16 TB 容量規格而言，PM1763 的循序讀取速度最高可達每秒 28,400 MB，循序寫入速度則達每秒 21,900 MB。三星表示，上述吞吐量較前一代機型 PM1753 提升逾一倍。

  ## 效能與外型規格

  PM1763 採用三星第九代 V-NAND（垂直 NAND）快閃記憶體，搭配全新研發的 4 奈米控制器。硬碟提供 4 TB、8 TB 與 16 TB 三種容量選擇，採 E3.S 外型規格——一種適合高密度伺服器部署的緊湊型插槽格式。

  三星指出，PM1763 的吞吐量可在約 1.4 秒內完成一個 40 GB 大型語言模型（LLM）的傳輸，公司強調此舉有助於降低推論與訓練工作負載期間，處理器與 AI 加速器之間的資料傳輸延遲。

  ## 液冷支援與資安功能

  PM1763 專為液冷伺服器環境設計。新一代 AI 伺服器日益將冷卻風扇移出運算機架，轉而採用全液冷散熱架構，因此需要儲存裝置能在全液冷配置下穩定運作。

  資安方面，PM1763 支援後量子密碼學（PQC）演算法，旨在保護儲存資料免受未來量子電腦的潛在威脅。此外，硬碟亦支援 TDISP（TEE Device Interface Security Protocol，可信執行環境裝置介面安全協定）——這是一項用於保障虛擬化伺服器環境中資料傳輸路徑安全的規範，在單一硬碟可能同時供多個工作負載共用的情境下，其安全意義尤為重要。

  ## 為何選擇 PCIe Gen6，時機為何是現在

  PCIe Gen6 的理論頻寬較現行主流伺服器介面 PCIe Gen5 提升一倍。三星指出，這一世代轉型才剛起步：部分超大規模資料中心所採用的中央處理器、輝達（NVIDIA）的 Vera 處理器，以及超微（AMD）即將推出的 EPYC Venice 處理器，均屬最早支援 PCIe Gen6 的平台之列。

  PM1763 目前最高容量為 16 TB，低於部分企業級固態硬碟的更高密度選項；此一定位反映出該產品主打伺服器內高效能本地儲存，而非大容量資料封存。三星表示，這與歷代 PCIe 世代交替時的發展規律一致——最新介面往往率先出現於高效能、相對低容量的產品，再逐步延伸至更高密度的機型。

  隨著多家處理器廠商的 PCIe Gen6 伺服器平台陸續邁向上市，PM1763 正式量產，標誌著儲存基礎設施已開始跟上新一代介面的腳步。
