body_en: |
  Neural Semiconductor Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned subsidiary of US-based Neural Semiconductor Inc., opened a VLSI (very large-scale integration) design centre at GBS@Mayang in Bayan Lepas, Penang, on 26 October 2026, establishing the company's first design operation in Southeast Asia.

  The centre covers the full design cycle for application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) and systems-on-chip (SoCs) — the two dominant categories of custom silicon. Its scope runs from architecture and register-transfer level (RTL) design, the stage at which circuit behaviour is described in code before physical layout begins, through analogue and mixed-signal (AMS) design, radio frequency integrated circuit (RFIC) design, design verification, physical design, custom layout, design-for-test (DFT), and design automation. The centre operates on Cadence and Synopsys toolchains, the dominant commercial electronic design automation (EDA) platforms, and takes end-to-end ownership of design programmes through to tape-out readiness and post-silicon support rather than supplying engineers to work within a client's existing flow, the company said.

  Neural Semiconductor Sdn Bhd was incorporated on 12 January 2026. Recruitment is under way in Penang for physical design, design verification and design automation roles.

  ## Engineering experience drawn from Dhaka affiliate

  The Penang centre is backed by Neural Semiconductor Limited in Dhaka, an affiliated company under common directorship that has more than ten years of experience in production VLSI engineering with customers in Europe, the United States, Japan and South Korea, according to the company. Senior engineers from Dhaka have been relocated to Penang as direct employees; their experience in advanced-node production is intended to allow the new centre to take on such programmes immediately rather than spending years building that track record locally.

  Managing director Mohammed A. Jabbar described the opening as a strategic diversification rather than a routine overseas expansion. "This is not simply an overseas expansion; it is a strategic diversification of our capabilities, markets and talent base. We want to bring our global VLSI experience to Malaysia, create meaningful opportunities for local engineers, and build a sustainable platform that can serve semiconductor companies across the region," he said.

  ## Talent pipeline built through PSDC and PCDA partnerships

  Neural Semiconductor has entered partnerships with the Penang Skills Development Centre (PSDC) and the Penang Chip Design Academy (PCDA), two institutions focused on engineering workforce development in the state. Graduates from each PCDA training batch will be placed directly into live design projects alongside experienced engineering leads on advanced-node programmes from their first year, the company said.

  The company has committed to a structured knowledge-transfer process: senior foreign engineers will be assigned to hand ownership of engineering flows to Malaysian engineers rather than remain in those roles indefinitely, with local engineers identified as successors as each programme matures. "We are committed to building local talent here, and that starts with the fresh graduate pool coming through Penang Chip Design Academy," said Terence Gaspar, head of country operations at Neural Semiconductor.

  ## Design capacity gap cited as market rationale

  The company identified a gap in Malaysia's semiconductor ecosystem in which demand for design services is outpacing the availability of experienced engineering talent, resulting in some design work being directed overseas. The Penang centre aims to perform and invoice that work locally, the company said.

  The centre's work extending through to post-silicon support distinguishes it from engineering-staff augmentation models, in which a service provider supplies personnel who work within the client's own processes and sign-off framework.

  ## Government and agency support

  Jagdeep Singh Deo, deputy chief minister II of Penang, said the centre's knowledge-transfer commitment aligned with the state's PSD@5KM+ programme, which targets a stronger pipeline of local IC design engineers. "The establishment of Neural Semiconductor's VLSI design centre further strengthens Penang's position as a growing IC design hub. This aligns with the objectives of PSD@5KM+ to develop a stronger pipeline of local IC design engineers, deepen our homegrown capabilities and position Penang to play a more meaningful role in the global semiconductor value chain," he said.

  Gaspar credited InvestPenang as the company's first point of contact in Malaysia. MDEC, the Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation, guided the company on structuring the Malaysian operation and on the Malaysia Digital designation process, he said. TalentCorp and the MYXpats team assisted with expatriate arrangements.

  ## Significance

  The centre adds an end-to-end ASIC and SoC design operation to Penang's existing semiconductor manufacturing base, which has historically concentrated on assembly, testing, and packaging rather than front-end chip design. A design operation that owns programmes through tape-out and post-silicon support represents a higher point on the semiconductor value chain than the state has typically housed domestically.

  Neural Semiconductor said its stated aim is to grow the Penang centre into a regional design services platform serving semiconductor companies across Southeast Asia.
body_zh: |
  Neural Semiconductor Sdn Bhd為美國Neural Semiconductor Inc.的全資子公司，於2026年10月26日在檳城峇央勒巴斯的GBS@Mayang正式啟用超大型積體電路（VLSI）設計中心，成為該公司在東南亞設立的首個設計據點。

  該中心涵蓋特殊應用積體電路（ASIC）與系統單晶片（SoC）的完整設計週期——此兩者為客製矽晶片的兩大主流類別。業務範疇從架構設計、暫存器轉移層（RTL）設計（即在進入實體佈局前，以程式碼描述電路行為的階段），延伸至類比與混合訊號（AMS）設計、射頻積體電路（RFIC）設計、設計驗證、實體設計、客製佈局、可測試性設計（DFT）及設計自動化。該中心採用Cadence與Synopsys兩大主流電子設計自動化（EDA）商業工具鏈，並以端到端的方式全程主導設計專案，直至tape-out就緒及後矽支援，而非單純派遣工程師配合客戶既有流程與簽核架構作業，公司表示。

  Neural Semiconductor Sdn Bhd於2026年1月12日在馬來西亞完成法人登記。目前正積極在檳城招募實體設計、設計驗證及設計自動化等職位的工程人才。

  ## 技術根基來自達卡關聯公司

  檳城中心的技術底氣，源自位於孟加拉達卡的關聯公司Neural Semiconductor Limited。據公司介紹，兩家公司在同一董事架構下運作，達卡公司擁有逾十年的量產VLSI工程經驗，客戶遍及歐洲、美國、日本及南韓。達卡的資深工程師已以直接聘僱的形式調派至檳城；其在先進製程量產上的豐富經驗，使新設中心得以立即承接此類專案，無需耗費多年在當地從頭建立相應的資歷與業績。

  董事總經理Mohammed A. Jabbar將此次開幕定位為戰略性多元佈局，而非例行性的海外擴張。「這不僅僅是海外擴張，而是我們在能力、市場與人才基礎上的戰略性多元化。我們希望將全球VLSI經驗帶到馬來西亞，為本地工程師創造有意義的機會，並打造一個能夠服務整個區域半導體企業的永續平台，」他說。

  ## 與PSDC及PCDA建立人才培育合作

  Neural Semiconductor已與專注於工程人才培育的檳城技能發展中心（PSDC）及檳城晶片設計學院（PCDA）建立合作夥伴關係。每批PCDA培訓畢業生將直接投入實際設計專案，從入職第一年起便與資深工程主管並肩參與先進製程專案，公司表示。

  公司承諾推行有結構的知識移轉流程：外籍資深工程師將被指派將工程流程的主導權逐步交接給馬來西亞本地工程師，而非長期占據相關職位；隨著各專案逐漸成熟，本地工程師將被確立為接班人選。「我們致力於在這裡培育本地人才，而這一切從通過檳城晶片設計學院的應屆畢業生開始，」Neural Semiconductor馬來西亞區業務主管Terence Gaspar表示。

  ## 設計產能缺口成為進軍市場的關鍵理由

  公司指出，馬來西亞半導體生態系中存在一個缺口：設計服務需求的增速正超過有經驗工程人才的供給，導致部分設計業務被轉至海外承接。檳城中心的目標，正是讓這些業務得以在本地完成、在本地開立發票。

  延伸至後矽支援的完整業務範疇，也使該中心有別於工程人力派遣模式——後者由服務商提供人員，配合客戶自身的流程與簽核架構作業。

  ## 政府與機構支持

  檳城第二副首席部長Jagdeep Singh Deo表示，該中心的知識移轉承諾與檳城州PSD@5KM+計畫的目標相符，該計畫旨在培育更健全的本地積體電路設計工程師供給管道。「Neural Semiconductor VLSI設計中心的設立，進一步鞏固了檳城作為成長中積體電路設計重鎮的地位。這與PSD@5KM+的目標不謀而合，有助於培育更健全的本地積體電路設計工程人才、深化在地能力，並使檳城在全球半導體價值鏈中扮演更重要的角色，」他說。

  Gaspar表示，InvestPenang是公司在馬來西亞的第一個接洽窗口。馬來西亞數位經濟機構（MDEC）則協助公司規劃馬來西亞業務架構，並指引申請「馬來西亞數位」資格認定的流程。人才機構TalentCorp及MYXpats團隊則協助處理外籍員工相關派遣事宜。

  ## 意義

  此次設立的中心，為檳城現有的半導體製造基礎增添了一個端到端的ASIC與SoC設計據點。檳城的半導體產業歷來以組裝、測試與封裝為重心，前端晶片設計向來較為缺乏。一個能夠全程主導專案直至tape-out及後矽支援的設計據點，代表著比該州過去在本地承接的業務更高層次的半導體價值鏈位置。

  Neural Semiconductor表示，其明確目標是將檳城中心發展為服務東南亞各地半導體企業的區域設計服務平台。
