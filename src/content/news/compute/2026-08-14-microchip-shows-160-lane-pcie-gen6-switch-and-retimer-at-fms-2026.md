body_en: |
  Microchip Technology showed its Switchtec PFX 160xG6, a 160-lane PCI Express Generation 6 (PCIe Gen6) switch chip, at Flash Memory Summit (FMS) 2026, extending its Switchtec line into the newest PCIe generation as it competes against Broadcom, Astera Labs, and Marvell. The company also demonstrated a PCIe Gen6 retimer chip and displayed a DirectFlash storage module at its booth.

  ## Switchtec PFX 160xG6 switch

  The PFX 160xG6 was shown mounted on a test printed circuit board at the show. PCIe, or PCI Express, is the dominant high-speed internal bus standard that connects processors, graphics cards, storage, and networking hardware inside servers. A PCIe switch routes traffic between those components. The chip supports up to 160 lanes and up to 20 ports, though ServeTheHome reported that deployments typically use fewer ports because the switch is generally connected to x16-lane GPUs (graphics processing units) and NICs (network interface controllers).

  Microchip also offers a PSX switch line, described as a more programmable variant designed for storage platforms. While competitors including Astera Labs and Marvell are building Gen6 switches that also support CXL (Compute Express Link, an open interconnect standard for memory and accelerator sharing), Microchip appears to be focusing its Gen6 switch effort on PCIe rather than CXL, according to ServeTheHome.

  ## PCIe switches move from storage niche to AI infrastructure

  Broadcom has held the dominant position in the PCIe switch market since acquiring PLX Technology through Avago more than a decade ago. ServeTheHome noted that the segment has grown in strategic importance as PCIe switches moved from niche roles in storage servers and early deep-learning systems to critical components in large-scale AI infrastructure buildouts.

  ## XpressConnect PCIe Gen6 retimer

  Microchip also showed its XpressConnect PCIe Gen6 retimer operating live on the show floor, retiming a signal across a stack of add-in cards. A retimer regenerates and retimes a high-speed signal, extending the effective reach of a link. PCIe Gen6 signals have shorter inherent reach than earlier generations, while servers and switch chassis are growing physically larger to accommodate more components and greater cooling requirements.

  The XpressConnect can also retime CXL 3.1 links, putting it in competition with products such as Astera Labs' Aries 6, which also handles CXL. Broadcom, Marvell, and Phison are also active in the PCIe retimer space, according to ServeTheHome.

  ## Everpure DirectFlash module

  Also spotted at the Microchip booth was an Everpure DirectFlash module. During FMS week, Everpure announced that a second top-five hyperscaler has committed to adopting its DirectFlash technology. ServeTheHome reported that Meta was the first hyperscaler to deploy DirectFlash.

  Microchip Technology於2026年快閃記憶體高峰會（Flash Memory Summit，FMS）上展示了Switchtec PFX 160xG6——一款160通道PCI Express第六代（PCIe Gen6）交換晶片，正式將其Switchtec產品線延伸至最新一代PCIe規格，並持續與博通（Broadcom）、Astera Labs及Marvell爭奪設計訂單。該公司同時在展台上示範了一款PCIe Gen6重計時器晶片，並展出DirectFlash儲存模組。

  ## Switchtec PFX 160xG6交換器

  PFX 160xG6在展場上安裝於測試用印刷電路板（PCB）亮相。PCIe（即PCI Express）是伺服器內部連接處理器、顯示卡、儲存裝置與網路硬體的主流高速內部匯流排標準；PCIe交換器則負責在這些元件之間路由傳輸流量。該晶片最多支援160個通道及20個埠，不過ServeTheHome指出，實際部署時通常使用較少的埠，原因在於交換器一般連接的是x16通道的GPU（繪圖處理單元）與網路介面控制器（NIC）。

  Microchip另有PSX交換器產品線，定位為專為儲存平台設計、可程式化程度更高的變體。ServeTheHome指出，儘管Astera Labs與Marvell等競爭對手正在打造同時支援CXL（Compute Express Link，一種用於記憶體與加速器共享的開放互連標準）的Gen6交換器，Microchip在Gen6交換器上的策略似乎仍聚焦於PCIe而非CXL。

  ## PCIe交換器從儲存利基走向AI基礎設施核心

  博通自十餘年前透過Avago收購PLX Technology以來，便長期主導PCIe交換器市場。ServeTheHome指出，隨著PCIe交換器從儲存伺服器與早期深度學習系統中的利基角色，晉升為大規模AI基礎設施建置的關鍵元件，這個市場區隔在戰略上的重要性已大幅提升。

  ## XpressConnect PCIe Gen6重計時器

  Microchip亦在展場上現場示範XpressConnect PCIe Gen6重計時器（retimer）的實際運作，對跨越多張擴充卡堆疊的訊號進行重新計時。重計時器的功能是對高速訊號進行再生與重新計時，藉此延伸連結的有效傳輸距離。PCIe Gen6訊號的本質傳輸距離比前幾代更短，而伺服器與交換器機箱為了容納更多元件並因應更高的散熱需求，實體尺寸也在持續增大。

  XpressConnect同樣能對CXL 3.1連結進行重新計時，使其與Astera Labs的Aries 6等同樣支援CXL的產品形成競爭關係。ServeTheHome指出，博通、Marvell與Phison也都活躍於PCIe重計時器市場。

  ## Everpure DirectFlash模組

  Microchip展台上亦可見到Everpure DirectFlash模組的蹤影。FMS展覽期間，Everpure宣布第二家躋身全球前五的超大型雲端業者已承諾採用其DirectFlash技術。ServeTheHome報導，Meta是首家部署DirectFlash的超大型雲端業者。
