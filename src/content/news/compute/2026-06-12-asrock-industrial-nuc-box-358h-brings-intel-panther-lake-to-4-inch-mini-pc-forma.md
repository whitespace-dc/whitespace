body_en: |
  ASRock Industrial has launched the NUC BOX-358H, a barebones mini PC built around Intel's Panther Lake platform that fits in a 117.5 x 110 x 49 mm enclosure and ships without memory, storage, or an operating system.

  ## Processor and integrated graphics

  The BOX-358H centers on the Core Ultra X7 358H, a Panther Lake-generation laptop processor with 16 CPU cores across three tiers: 4 performance cores (P-cores), 8 efficiency cores (E-cores), and 4 low-power efficiency cores (LPE cores), with a peak clock speed of 4.8 GHz. The integrated GPU is Intel's Arc B390, based on the Xe3 graphics architecture with 12 Xe cores.

  ServeTheHome, which reviewed the system, described the BOX-358H as a "significant performance step up" over ASRock Industrial's earlier NUC boxes, attributing the gains to platform-wide improvements in Panther Lake.

  ## Memory and storage

  The system carries two SO-DIMM slots supporting DDR5-5600 memory. The review tested it with 96 GB across two 48 GB modules. Storage is handled by two M.2 slots: one M.2 2280 slot with PCIe Gen 5 x4 bandwidth and one M.2 2242 slot at PCIe Gen 4 x4. The review unit used a 1 TB Crucial P3 Plus NVMe drive in the Gen 4 slot.

  ## Ports and connectivity

  Front-panel I/O includes a Thunderbolt 4 port running at 40 Gbps with DisplayPort alternate mode at DP 2.1 speeds, a USB-C port at 20 Gbps with DP 1.4 alternate mode, a USB-A port at 10 Gbps, and a combo audio jack. The rear panel adds two more USB-A ports at 10 Gbps, two HDMI 2.1 ports, and two 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet ports: one driven by Intel's i226-LM controller (which supports Intel's vPro remote-management platform) and one by the i226-V. Combined, the system can drive up to four displays simultaneously, according to ServeTheHome.

  Wireless connectivity is provided by Intel's BE211 module, supporting Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6.0.

  One connectivity limitation flagged by the review: while Intel SoCs natively support Thunderbolt 4 in pairs, ASRock Industrial installed only a single TB4/USB4 retimer, making the left front USB-C port the sole TB4 connection. The right front USB-C port is limited to USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 speeds at 20 Gbps rather than TB4.

  ServeTheHome also noted that rear ports are labeled only by connector type, not by speed, calling it a "missed opportunity" to surface non-obvious technical information.

  ## Power supply and chassis

  ASRock Industrial ships the BOX-358H with a 120 W (19 V at 6.32 A) external power adapter, which the review noted is physically wider than the NUC itself. Despite the system's compatibility with USB-C power delivery, the company retained a DC barrel connector, which accepts input voltages from 12 V to 24 V to suit industrial installations where fixed USB-C power sources may not be available.

  The system weighs approximately 1 kg (2.2 lbs). The exterior carries no model number, brand name, or stickers, according to the review. Cooling uses a single internal blower fan; air enters through vents on the top and sides and exhausts from the rear. An optional VESA mounting bracket is available for wall or monitor mounting.

  ## Consistent design across generations

  The chassis is functionally unchanged from earlier ASRock Industrial NUC generations, ServeTheHome observed, noting that the only visible exterior update in recent years was a switch from a glossy to a matte finish. The review framed this consistency as deliberate: for industrial customers, it allows newer boxes to directly replace older units without changes to mounting or cabling.

  ## Context: NUC after Intel's exit

  The NUC (Next Unit of Computing) form factor is defined by a roughly 4-inch-by-4-inch footprint at approximately half the volume of a 1-liter mini PC. The format was developed by Intel, which later divested the product line. ASRock Industrial has since become one of the most consistent developers of the platform, releasing NUC systems based on both Intel and AMD hardware across multiple generations, according to ServeTheHome.

  ## Significance

  The BOX-358H places Intel's current-generation Panther Lake mobile silicon, otherwise associated with thin-and-light laptops, into the smallest widely available x86 computing form factor. For deployments in kiosks, digital signage, edge computing nodes, and other space-constrained installations, the continued development of NUC-class hardware with current-generation processors and a PCIe Gen 5 storage slot preserves an option that might otherwise have lapsed when Intel exited the segment.

  The system is listed for sale on Newegg, according to ServeTheHome.

  ## 處理器與內建繪圖核心

  BOX-358H 的核心是 Core Ultra X7 358H，這是一顆 Panther Lake 世代的筆記型電腦處理器，共有 16 個 CPU 核心，分為三個層級：4 個效能核心（P-core）、8 個效率核心（E-core），以及 4 個低功耗效率核心（LPE core），峰值時脈高達 4.8 GHz。內建 GPU 為英特爾 Arc B390，採用 Xe3 繪圖架構，配備 12 個 Xe 核心。

  曾對本系統進行評測的 ServeTheHome 指出，BOX-358H 相較於華擎工業先前的 NUC 機種有「顯著的效能躍升」，並將此進步歸功於 Panther Lake 整個平台層面的全面提升。

  ## 記憶體與儲存

  本系統設有兩個 SO-DIMM 插槽，支援 DDR5-5600 記憶體。評測時以兩條 48 GB 模組共 96 GB 進行測試。儲存方面提供兩個 M.2 插槽：一個 M.2 2280 插槽，頻寬為 PCIe Gen 5 x4；另一個 M.2 2242 插槽，則為 PCIe Gen 4 x4。評測所用機器在 Gen 4 插槽中安裝了一顆 1 TB 的 Crucial P3 Plus NVMe 硬碟。

  ## 連接埠與連線能力

  前面板 I/O 包含：一個以 40 Gbps 速率運行的 Thunderbolt 4 連接埠，支援以 DP 2.1 速率輸出的 DisplayPort 替代模式；一個 20 Gbps 的 USB-C 連接埠，支援 DP 1.4 替代模式；一個 10 Gbps 的 USB-A 連接埠；以及一個複合式音訊插孔。後面板則另有兩個 10 Gbps 的 USB-A 連接埠、兩個 HDMI 2.1 連接埠，以及兩個 2.5 Gigabit 乙太網路連接埠：一個由英特爾 i226-LM 控制器驅動（支援英特爾 vPro 遠端管理平台），另一個則採用 i226-V。據 ServeTheHome 指出，本系統最多可同時驅動四個螢幕。

  無線連線方面，本機搭載英特爾 BE211 模組，支援 Wi-Fi 7 與藍牙 6.0。

  評測也點出一項連線能力上的限制：雖然英特爾 SoC 原生支援兩組 Thunderbolt 4，但華擎工業僅安裝了一個 TB4/USB4 重定時器（retimer），使得前面板左側的 USB-C 連接埠成為唯一的 TB4 接口。前面板右側的 USB-C 連接埠則僅支援 USB 3.2 Gen 2x2，速率為 20 Gbps，而非 TB4。

  ServeTheHome 也注意到，後面板連接埠僅以接口類型標示，未標明傳輸速度，並稱此為「一個錯失的機會」，因為這些非顯而易見的技術規格資訊本應加以呈現。

  ## 電源供應器與機箱

  華擎工業為 BOX-358H 附上一個 120 W（19 V、6.32 A）的外接電源變壓器，評測指出其寬度甚至比 NUC 本體還寬。儘管本系統相容 USB-C 供電（Power Delivery），華擎工業仍保留了 DC 圓形插頭接口，可接受 12 V 至 24 V 的輸入電壓，以適應工業現場可能無法使用固定 USB-C 電源的情境。

  本機重量約為 1 公斤（2.2 磅）。據評測描述，機身外觀不附任何型號、品牌名稱或貼紙。散熱設計採用單一內置渦輪風扇，冷空氣由頂部與側面進氣孔吸入，熱空氣則由後方排出。此外，另提供選購的 VESA 掛架，可將機器固定於牆面或螢幕背面。

  ## 跨世代的一致設計

  ServeTheHome 觀察到，機箱設計與華擎工業先前的 NUC 世代在功能上幾乎完全一致，並指出近年唯一可見的外觀更新，是將亮面改為霧面烤漆。評測認為這種一致性是刻意為之：對工業客戶而言，新款機器可直接取代舊機器，無需改動任何安裝支架或線路配置。

  ## 背景：英特爾退場後的 NUC 生態

  NUC（Next Unit of Computing）規格的特徵是約 4 英吋乘 4 英吋的底面積，體積大約是 1 公升迷你電腦的一半。此規格由英特爾開發，後來英特爾將相關產品線轉讓出售。此後，華擎工業成為持續深耕此平台最穩定的廠商之一，據 ServeTheHome 指出，該公司已在多個世代中推出採用英特爾與 AMD 硬體的 NUC 系統。

  ## 重要意義

  BOX-358H 將英特爾現行世代的 Panther Lake 行動晶片——原本主要應用於輕薄筆電——導入目前市面上最小的 x86 運算規格之中。對於部署在資訊站、數位看板、邊緣運算節點及其他空間受限場所的應用而言，NUC 等級硬體持續搭載現行世代處理器與 PCIe Gen 5 儲存插槽，保留了一個在英特爾退出市場後可能就此消失的選項。

  本系統目前已在 Newegg 上架銷售，據 ServeTheHome 報導。
