  ## Q3 forecast breaks 5 million unit threshold

  Global server shipments are on track to exceed 5 million units in the third quarter of 2026 for the first time on record, according to a Digitimes server industry survey report cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times on August 4. Third-quarter deliveries are projected to grow 2.8% from the prior quarter, breaching the milestone after second-quarter shipments rose only 3.8% quarter-over-quarter amid constraints on memory, central processing units (CPUs), and integrated-circuit substrates.

  For the full year 2026, Digitimes projects total shipments of 19.49 million units, a 17.4% increase over 2025 and a new annual record, even accounting for an anticipated 2.4% sequential decline in the fourth quarter tied to server platform generation transitions and ongoing component imbalances.

  ## Q2 split: cloud buyers outpaced brands

  The second quarter produced sharply divergent results between buyer categories. Large U.S.-based cloud service providers (CSPs) — companies that operate massive data centers and rent computing capacity — grew their server procurement 11.6% quarter-over-quarter. Digitimes attributed that expansion to the accelerating adoption of agentic artificial intelligence applications, which is shifting cloud operators' purchasing from accelerator-based AI servers toward CPU-based compute and storage servers.

  Brand-name server vendors, which sell to enterprise customers rather than operating their own clouds, saw shipments fall 5.6% in the second quarter. The report cited tighter supply constraints relative to CSPs and noted that enterprise customers have shown lower tolerance for server price increases.

  ## Q3 cloud growth slows; brands expected to rebound

  In the third quarter, growth momentum among large U.S. CSPs is expected to slow materially, with procurement rising just 1.7% quarter-over-quarter. Brand vendors, by contrast, are forecast for a recovery: Dell Technologies, Super Micro Computer (Supermicro), and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) are projected to grow their combined shipments 5.8% sequentially in the quarter, according to Digitimes.

  ## Customer rankings: Amazon leads, Meta surges

  Among individual buyers ranked by procurement share in the third quarter, Amazon holds first place with a 21.3% share, unchanged from the second quarter and marking a second consecutive quarter at the top. Meta is forecast to jump to second place with an 18.2% share, up sharply from 15.6% in the second quarter, as a new CPU server platform begins volume production following a pause for model transitions in the prior period.

  Dell ranks third with a 10% share. Microsoft and Supermicro are placed fourth and fifth, respectively; Supermicro is expected to benefit from a recovery in AI server shipments. Google's share fell to 6.7% due to procurement deferrals, but Digitimes expects the company to return to fourth place in the fourth quarter.

  ## Taiwan ODMs: Quanta leads, Wiwynn closing gap

  Among Taiwan-based original design manufacturers (ODMs) — contract makers that build servers to customer specifications — Quanta Computer (2382) is projected to hold the top position in the third quarter but see its global share slip back below 20%. Wiwynn (6669), the second-ranked ODM, is expected to gain share significantly in the third quarter, driven by simultaneous order growth from three major cloud customers: Microsoft, Meta, and Amazon.

  ## Significance

  The Digitimes forecast points to a server market in which AI-driven demand continues to underpin record annual volumes even as individual quarters are constrained by component availability and platform cycles. The shift in CSP spending toward CPU-based compute and storage — rather than exclusively toward GPU accelerators — and the rebound in brand-vendor demand suggest that AI infrastructure build-out is broadening across server categories.

  Digitimes projects that fourth-quarter platform transitions and component shortages will trim shipments slightly before the full-year 2026 total reaches 19.49 million units.

  ## Q3出貨量首度突破500萬台門檻

  根據《電子時報》（Digitimes）伺服器產業調查報告，並經《工商時報》於8月4日引述，全球伺服器出貨量第三季有望史上首度超越500萬台，刷新歷史紀錄。第三季出貨量預估將較前一季成長2.8%，跨越這一重要里程碑。此前第二季出貨量雖已季增3.8%，但受制於記憶體、中央處理器（CPU）及IC載板供應吃緊，整體增幅相對有限。

  展望全年，Digitimes預估2026年全年伺服器出貨總量將達1,949萬台，較2025年成長17.4%，再創歷年新高。儘管第四季受伺服器平台世代交替及零組件供需失衡影響，預計出貨量將季減2.4%，全年表現仍可望穩穩寫下歷史新猷。

  ## Q2分化：雲端客戶採購遠優於品牌廠

  第二季市場呈現明顯分化格局。美國大型雲端服務供應商（CSP）——即自行運營大規模資料中心、並將運算資源出租予用戶的業者——伺服器採購量較上季勁增11.6%。Digitimes指出，AI代理應用（Agentic AI）加速普及，帶動雲端業者的採購重心逐步從以加速器為主的AI伺服器，轉移至以CPU為核心的運算與儲存伺服器。

  相較之下，面向企業客戶銷售、而非自行經營雲端服務的品牌伺服器廠商，第二季出貨量則較上季衰退5.6%。報告指出，品牌廠所面臨的零組件供應壓力相對較緊，加上企業客戶對伺服器漲價的接受度普遍偏低，形成雙重壓力。

  ## Q3雲端動能趨緩；品牌廠預估回溫

  進入第三季，美國大型CSP的採購動能預計將明顯放緩，季增幅縮窄至僅1.7%。品牌廠商則有望迎來反彈：Digitimes預估，戴爾科技（Dell Technologies）、超微電腦（Super Micro Computer，Supermicro）及慧與科技（Hewlett Packard Enterprise，HPE）三家合計出貨量，第三季將較上季成長5.8%。

  ## 客戶排名：亞馬遜穩居榜首，Meta大幅躍升

  以第三季各大買家採購占比排名來看，亞馬遜以21.3%的市佔率穩居第一，與第二季持平，連續兩季蟬聯冠軍。Meta預估將躍升至第二名，市佔率達18.2%，較第二季的15.6%大幅提升；主因是全新CPU伺服器平台在前一季模型交替期間暫停後，第三季正式進入量產出貨。

  戴爾排名第三，市佔率10%。微軟與超微電腦分居第四、第五名；超微電腦預計受惠於AI伺服器出貨回溫。Google因採購遞延，市佔率降至6.7%，但Digitimes預估其第四季將重回第四名。

  ## 台灣ODM廠：廣達領先，緯穎急起直追

  在台灣原廠委託設計製造商（ODM）——即依客戶規格承接伺服器代工生產的製造商——排名方面，廣達電腦（股票代號：2382）預估第三季仍居龍頭地位，但全球市佔率將小幅滑落至20%以下。排名第二的緯穎科技（股票代號：6669）則預計第三季將顯著擴大市佔，主要動力來自微軟、Meta與亞馬遜三大雲端客戶訂單同步放量。

  ## 市場意義

  Digitimes的預測顯示，AI驅動的需求持續支撐伺服器市場年度出貨量屢創新高，即便個別季度仍受零組件供應壓力與平台週期影響而有所起伏。CSP採購重心由GPU加速器轉向CPU運算與儲存伺服器，加上品牌廠需求逐步回溫，顯示AI基礎建設的擴張正逐步延伸至更廣泛的伺服器品類。

  Digitimes預估，第四季平台世代交替及零組件短缺將使出貨量略微回落，但2026年全年出貨總量仍有望觸及1,949萬台的歷史高點。
