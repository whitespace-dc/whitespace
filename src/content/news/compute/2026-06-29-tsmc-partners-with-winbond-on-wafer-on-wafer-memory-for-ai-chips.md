body_en: |
  TSMC and Taiwan chipmaker Winbond Electronics have launched a cooperation in wafer-on-wafer (WoW) advanced 3D stacking technology, adding a domestic DRAM supplier to TSMC's memory supply chain for artificial intelligence chips, according to people familiar with the matter cited by the Economic Daily News.

  The cooperation involves Winbond supplying DRAM and other memory wafers that are stacked vertically with TSMC's logic-process wafers using hybrid bonding technology, according to the report. Neither company confirmed the arrangement: Winbond said it does not comment on individual customers or cooperation cases, and TSMC had not responded by publication time.

  ## Why TSMC Is Expanding Its Memory Supplier Base

  TSMC's WoW program has historically sourced memory wafers from the three dominant global DRAM makers: Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron. Global memory supply is now severely constrained, with international memory manufacturers operating at near-full capacity, according to industry analysts cited by the Economic Daily News. That tightness is prompting the global AI supply chain to seek more diverse and flexible memory sources.

  By adding Winbond, TSMC gains an additional domestic supplier to help stabilize memory supply for WoW production, the report said. The Economic Daily News described the move as part of a broader TSMC effort to build a more complete and resilient domestic AI chip ecosystem alongside its existing international memory partners.

  ## What Wafer-on-Wafer Technology Does

  WoW is an advanced 3D integration method in which logic chips and memory wafers are stacked directly on top of each other using hybrid bonding, a technique that forms tens of thousands to millions of micro-scale copper interconnects between the two layers. The shorter data path between logic and memory raises bandwidth and reduces latency relative to conventional chip packaging, while also improving energy efficiency, according to industry analysts cited in the report.

  WoW is regarded as a key integration technology for AI servers, high-performance computing (HPC), and future edge AI devices, the Economic Daily News reported.

  ## Why Winbond Qualifies

  TSMC sets demanding requirements for WoW partners, according to industry analysis cited in the report. Suppliers must demonstrate mature mass-production capability on 12-inch wafers, high manufacturing yields, specialized process know-how, and wafer integration experience.

  Winbond, which has focused on niche DRAM and NOR Flash markets, has accumulated capabilities in specialized memory processes, wafer manufacturing, and quality management that satisfy those criteria, the report said. The Economic Daily News described those accumulated strengths as the basis for Winbond's selection as a key partner in TSMC's AI memory supply chain.

  ## Significance for Taiwan's Memory Industry

  For Winbond, entry into TSMC's WoW supply chain marks a move from niche memory markets into AI server infrastructure, a segment the Economic Daily News described as core to the global AI chip supply chain.

  Taiwan's memory industry, which has played a supporting role in AI hardware to date, stands to deepen its participation in the supply chain's core as WoW advances toward mass production, the report said. The Economic Daily News said further AI platform deployments using WoW technology could open additional growth opportunities for Winbond.

  ## 台積電攜手華邦電　晶圓堆疊技術強化AI記憶體供應鏈

  據知情人士透露，台積電與台灣晶片製造商華邦電子已就晶圓對晶圓（Wafer-on-Wafer，WoW）先進3D堆疊技術展開合作，為台積電的AI晶片記憶體供應鏈增添一家本土DRAM供應商。此消息由《經濟日報》引述相關人士報導。

  據報導，此次合作涉及華邦電子提供DRAM及其他記憶體晶圓，再透過混合鍵合（hybrid bonding）技術，將這些晶圓與台積電邏輯製程晶圓進行垂直堆疊。對此，兩家公司均未正式確認相關安排：華邦電子表示不評論個別客戶或合作案，台積電截至報導截稿前亦未作出回應。

  ## 台積電為何擴大記憶體供應商版圖

  台積電的WoW計畫過去長期向全球三大DRAM廠採購記憶體晶圓，分別為三星（Samsung）、SK海力士（SK Hynix）及美光（Micron）。然而，《經濟日報》引述產業分析師指出，目前全球記憶體供應嚴重吃緊，國際記憶體製造商產能幾乎已達滿載，這股供給緊繃態勢，正促使全球AI供應鏈積極尋求更多元、更靈活的記憶體來源。

  報導指出，透過納入華邦電子，台積電得以增加一家國內供應商，有助於穩定WoW生產所需的記憶體供應。《經濟日報》將此舉定位為台積電更廣泛佈局的一環，目標是在現有國際記憶體合作夥伴之外，打造一個更完整、更具韌性的國內AI晶片生態系。

  ## 晶圓對晶圓技術的功能與優勢

  WoW是一種先進的3D整合方法，透過混合鍵合技術，將邏輯晶片與記憶體晶圓直接垂直堆疊，在兩層之間形成數以萬計乃至數百萬個微尺度銅質互連。《經濟日報》引述產業分析師表示，這種縮短的邏輯與記憶體資料傳輸路徑，相較於傳統晶片封裝，能提升頻寬、降低延遲，同時改善能源效率。

  WoW技術被視為AI伺服器、高效能運算（HPC）及未來邊緣AI裝置的關鍵整合技術，《經濟日報》如此報導。

  ## 華邦電子的資格與優勢

  《經濟日報》引述產業分析指出，台積電對WoW合作夥伴設有嚴格要求。供應商必須展現12吋晶圓的成熟量產能力、高製造良率、專業製程技術知識，以及晶圓整合經驗。

  長期深耕利基型DRAM及NOR Flash市場的華邦電子，在專業記憶體製程、晶圓製造及品質管理方面累積了相當深厚的實力，符合上述條件。《經濟日報》指出，正是這些累積的競爭優勢，奠定了華邦電子雀屏中選、成為台積電AI記憶體供應鏈關鍵夥伴的基礎。

  ## 對台灣記憶體產業的意義

  對華邦電子而言，進入台積電WoW供應鏈，標誌著其從利基型記憶體市場跨入AI伺服器基礎設施領域——而《經濟日報》將這個領域定位為全球AI晶片供應鏈的核心所在。

  台灣記憶體產業迄今在AI硬體中扮演的多為輔助角色，隨著WoW技術邁向量產，有望在供應鏈核心環節深化參與。《經濟日報》表示，未來採用WoW技術的AI平台若持續擴大部署，將為華邦電子帶來更多成長機會。
