  ## Chinese military activity coincided with record chip investment pledges at Computex

  Taiwan's defence ministry recorded 79 Chinese warplanes operating near the island during last week's Computex technology conference in Taipei, as the chief executives of Nvidia, AMD and Intel made what were described as their deepest-ever commitments to Taiwan's technology supply chain. Taiwan simultaneously blocked entry permits for all 219 mainland Chinese exhibitors registered for the conference, with no application approved.

  Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang said the company plans to spend approximately $150 billion annually in Taiwan, up from roughly $10 billion to $15 billion five years ago. Huang also said Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin chip — its successor to the Blackwell architecture — involves twice as many supply chain partners as Blackwell, with 150 companies in Taiwan alone contributing to its production. AMD chief executive Lisa Su was also present at Computex, alongside representatives of SK Group and Intel.

  ## Coast guard confrontation on the final day

  On the final day of the conference, Taiwan's coast guard confronted Chinese counterparts in the contested South China Sea near the Taiwan-controlled Pratas Islands. Taiwan's coast guard broadcast a warning stating that peace in the Taiwan Strait was vital to the stability of the global economy and the lifeline of the technology industry.

  ## Taiwan's role in the AI hardware supply chain

  Taiwan is home to TSMC, the world's largest contract chipmaker and the primary supplier to both Nvidia and Apple, as well as Foxconn, described as Nvidia's largest server manufacturer. The island also hosts dozens of companies working across the components and systems that power AI data centre infrastructure globally.

  That concentration of production capacity is the source of both the island's strategic importance and its vulnerability, according to the source. The coast guard's statement on the final day of Computex explicitly linked the stability of the Taiwan Strait to the health of the global technology economy.

  ## Permit freeze and declining mainland presence

  Taiwan stalled entry permits for all 219 listed mainland Chinese exhibitors at Computex, issuing no formal rejections. The permit freeze extended a pattern first documented at an earlier Taiwanese trade show in April 2026.

  Mainland travel to Taiwan has been declining in parallel with tightening cross-strait relations. More than 420 Chinese military aircraft were documented operating near the island in the first quarter of 2026 alone, making the 79 sorties recorded during Computex part of a sustained pattern of military activity rather than an isolated incident.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The simultaneous occurrence of record supply chain investment pledges and active military pressure on the same territory illustrates a structural tension in the global AI hardware industry: its most critical manufacturing capacity is concentrated in a geography subject to ongoing geopolitical contestation. Nvidia's disclosure that 150 Taiwanese companies are involved in Vera Rubin production alone indicates the dependency is deepening, not diminishing.

  More than 420 Chinese military sorties near Taiwan were recorded in Q1 2026 before Computex began.

  ## 共軍機艦活動與晶片供應鏈投資創紀錄同步上演

  台灣國防部記錄到，上週在台北舉行的Computex台北國際電腦展期間，共有79架中國軍機在台灣周邊活動，輝達（NVIDIA）、超微（AMD）與英特爾的執行長也在同一時間宣布對台灣科技供應鏈有史以來最深度的投資承諾。與此同時，台灣凍結所有219名已登記中國大陸參展商的入台許可，無一申請獲准。

  輝達執行長黃仁勳表示，該公司計畫每年在台灣投入約1,500億美元，相較五年前的約100億至150億美元大幅攀升。黃仁勳並指出，輝達下一代Vera Rubin晶片——即Blackwell架構的繼任產品——涉及的供應鏈夥伴是Blackwell的兩倍，其中光是台灣就有150家企業參與生產。超微執行長蘇姿丰也出席了本屆Computex，與SK集團及英特爾代表同場現身。

  ## 展覽最後一日爆發海巡對峙

  展覽最後一天，台灣海巡署在台灣實際管轄的東沙群島附近、爭議性的南海海域，與中方海巡船隻正面對峙。台灣海巡署廣播警告，指台海和平對全球經濟穩定及科技產業命脈至關重要。

  ## 台灣在AI硬體供應鏈中的地位

  台灣是全球最大晶圓代工廠台積電（TSMC）的所在地，台積電同時是輝達與蘋果的主要供應商；鴻海（Foxconn）也在台灣，被形容為輝達最大的伺服器製造商。台灣還聚集了數十家企業，涵蓋驅動全球AI資料中心基礎設施所需的各類元件與系統。

  這種高度集中的生產能力，既是台灣戰略地位的來源，也是其脆弱性之所在。海巡署在Computex最後一天發表的聲明，明確將台海穩定與全球科技經濟的健康連結在一起。

  ## 入台許可凍結與陸客來台持續萎縮

  台灣凍結了Computex所有219名已登記中國大陸參展商的入台許可申請，但未正式發出任何駁回通知。這種許可凍結延續了2026年4月一場台灣商展首次出現的先例。

  大陸人士赴台人數與兩岸關係趨緊同步下滑。光是2026年第一季，就有逾420架中國軍機被記錄在台灣周邊活動，Computex期間的79架次不過是這波持續軍事壓力態勢的一環，而非孤立事件。

  ## 對產業的啟示

  供應鏈投資創紀錄與軍事壓力同步發生在同一塊土地上，清楚呈現出全球AI硬體產業的結構性矛盾：最關鍵的製造產能，集中在一個長期面臨地緣政治爭議的地理區域。輝達揭露光是Vera Rubin一款晶片的生產便涉及150家台灣企業，表明這種依賴程度正在持續加深，而非逐漸減少。

  值得注意的是，Computex開幕之前，2026年第一季中國軍機在台灣周邊的出動架次便已逾420架次。
