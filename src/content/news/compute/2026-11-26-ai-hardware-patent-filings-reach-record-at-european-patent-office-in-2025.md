body_en: |
  AI patent publications at the European Patent Office (EPO) reached a record 14,049 in 2025, and more than eight in ten patent applications specifically covering hardware designed to run artificial intelligence were allowed when they closed during the year, according to intellectual property firm Marks & Clerk's sixth annual review of AI patent activity at the office.

  The overall allowance rate across all AI cases closed at the EPO in 2024 stood at 60.7%, up from 45.5% in 2015, and the 2025 figure is higher still, the report said. Total AI publications rose 9.8% in 2025, an acceleration from 1.7% growth the year before.

  ## Hardware for AI: a small but active segment

  Hardware for AI — a category covering chips, accelerators, and related physical components designed specifically to process AI workloads — remains a narrow slice of the broader EPO AI corpus. The segment accounts for 2,437 of the 105,651 AI publications recorded at the EPO since 2000. Its annual count has contracted from 399 in 2022 to 333 in 2025.

  A wider category the report labels Hardware AI, which also includes robots, autonomous vehicles, drones, and other machines controlled by AI algorithms, grew by 121 publications in 2025. Marks & Clerk said that increase was driven entirely by AI-controlled machines rather than by dedicated AI chips.

  Despite the narrower Hardware for AI segment's declining annual count, its allowance rate — over 80% of cases closed in 2025 — outperforms the broader AI field. James Stott, a hardware specialist partner at Marks & Clerk, said in the report that innovators should have no concerns about the inherent patentability of AI hardware given that outcome.

  ## Geography: US dominates filings, Korea leads per capita

  Most Hardware for AI filings at the EPO originate outside Europe. US applicants accounted for 43.1% of Hardware for AI publications between 2021 and 2025, nearly double the 22.9% share held by applicants from states party to the European Patent Convention (EPC). Chinese applicants held 15.7% of the segment and Korean applicants 10.2%, both above their respective shares in the broader AI field at the EPO.

  In aggregate, 73.2% of Hardware for AI publications claimed priority — meaning the original first filing — from outside Europe, including 48.7% from the United States and 12.5% from China.

  On a per capita basis, South Korea led all jurisdictions in 2025 with 17.3 AI publications per million inhabitants, ahead of the United States at 14.8, Japan at 9.5, and EPC member states combined at 7.9. Korea also led per capita in the Hardware for AI segment specifically.

  ## Innovation shifting up the hardware stack

  The report identified a shift in where hardware innovation is concentrating. By 2025, compilers, design tools, and system software had become the largest identifiable Hardware for AI sub-domain at the EPO, while activity in neural-network accelerators — dedicated processors built to run neural network computations — had eased since peaking in 2020. European applicants were most concentrated in neuromorphic circuits, which mimic biological neural structures, and analogue circuits.

  Mike Williams, an AI specialist partner at Marks & Clerk, said the firm's filing data had for several years pointed to AI expanding from the cloud into the physical world, and described the 2025 figures as the strongest confirmation yet of that trend.

  ## Prosecution: divisionals common, opposition rare

  Divisional applications — filings split from an earlier parent application to pursue separate claims — are notably more prevalent in hardware than across AI overall. Divisionals accounted for 14.7% of Hardware for AI publications in 2025, compared with 5.8% for AI as a whole. Divisionals were allowed more often than other cases in every closure year from 2015 to 2025, at rates of 73.2% versus 59.6%.

  Examination is substantively more contested for hardware cases filed directly at the EPO. Category X citations — the most damaging type of prior-art citation, one that challenges every claim in an application — appeared in 74.1% of Hardware for AI applications with a search report. Even so, 57.6% of those that had closed were allowed.

  Opposition, the formal post-grant challenge process at the EPO, remained rare across the sector. Only 459 of the 33,777 AI patents granted by the end of 2025, or 1.4%, had been opposed. None of the 735 Hardware for AI patents identified in the report had faced opposition.

  ## UPC caution among hardware owners

  The Unified Patent Court (UPC), a centralised litigation system that began operating across participating European Union member states in 2023, has seen cautious uptake from hardware patent owners. Of AI patents granted in 2025, 22.6% had been registered as Unitary Patents — the single patent right the UPC underpins — and 41.2% had been opted out of UPC jurisdiction, shielding them from centralised revocation proceedings. The opt-out share has fallen from a peak of 53.7% for patents granted in 2022.

  Hardware for AI owners were more conservative still. In that group, only 14.1% took up the Unitary Patent route and 54.3% opted out, the highest opt-out rate reported for any AI sub-segment in the review.

  Stott noted in the report that hardware proprietors' greater caution about the UPC reflected a broader strategic need to build a European patent strategy from the first filing, given that nearly three-quarters of Hardware for AI cases originate outside Europe.

  ## China and Canada

  Outside Europe, China's national patent office, CNIPA, recorded 6,404 Hardware AI publications in 2025, more than three times the EPO total. Chinese grants in the field have risen sixteenfold since 2018. Chinese applicants file overwhelmingly at home, holding 88% of publications in their domestic market compared with less than 9% at the EPO.

  In Canada, draft guidance under consultation at the time of the report's publication suggested AI hardware patents may face a more accommodating patentability test, while applied AI inventions — those without a physical hardware component — can still run into requirements to cite physical equipment in claims, making such patents harder to enforce.

  ## New entrants

  The hardware segment continues to attract first-time filers. In 2025, 22.2% of Hardware for AI publications at the EPO came from applicants new to the field, up from 18.2% in 2023, though about two-thirds of those new entrants already held AI publications at the EPO in other categories.

  Stott said in the report that following the surge in neural-network architecture filings that peaked around 2020, interest now appears to be diversifying beyond first-generation accelerators into AI-assisted chip design, memory, and neuromorphic circuits.
body_zh: |
  2025年歐洲專利局（EPO）AI專利公告件數達到歷史新高14,049件，專為運行人工智慧而設計的硬體專利申請案，在同年完結的案件中逾八成獲准核可。上述數據來自智慧財產權事務所Marks & Clerk針對EPO AI專利動態所發布的第六份年度回顧報告。

  綜觀2024年EPO所有AI結案的整體獲准率為60.7%，較2015年的45.5%顯著提升；2025年的數字更高於此。AI專利公告總量方面，2025年成長9.8%，明顯快於前一年僅1.7%的增幅。

  ## 硬體AI：小眾卻高度活躍的領域

  「硬體AI」（Hardware for AI）涵蓋晶片、加速器及相關實體元件，專門設計用於處理AI工作負載，在EPO的AI專利整體資料庫中仍屬少數。自2000年迄今，EPO共錄得105,651件AI公告，其中硬體AI占2,437件。該細分領域的年度公告數量從2022年的399件縮減至2025年的333件。

  報告所採用的更廣義類別「Hardware AI」，除上述硬體外，尚涵蓋機器人、自駕車、無人機及其他由AI演算法控制的機械。此類別在2025年新增121件公告，Marks & Clerk指出，增量完全來自AI控制機械，而非AI專用晶片。

  儘管硬體AI細分領域年度公告件數呈現下滑，其獲准率——2025年完結案件中逾八成——仍優於整體AI領域的表現。Marks & Clerk硬體專業合夥人James Stott在報告中表示，以此結果來看，創新者毋須擔憂AI硬體在可專利性方面存在根本疑慮。

  ## 地理分布：美國主導申請，南韓人均稱霸

  EPO的硬體AI申請案大多來自歐洲以外。2021年至2025年間，美國申請人占硬體AI公告的43.1%，幾近歐洲專利公約（EPC）締約國申請人22.9%份額的兩倍。中國申請人占15.7%，南韓申請人占10.2%，兩者均高於各自在EPO整體AI領域的比重。

  就優先權——即最初首次申請地點——來看，硬體AI公告中有73.2%的優先權主張來自歐洲以外，其中美國占48.7%，中國占12.5%。

  以人均計算，南韓在2025年以每百萬人口17.3件AI公告領先所有司法管轄區，其次為美國的14.8件、日本的9.5件，EPC成員國整體則為7.9件。南韓在硬體AI細分領域的人均表現同樣居冠。

  ## 創新重心向硬體堆疊上層移動

  報告指出硬體創新的集中點正在轉移。截至2025年，編譯器、設計工具及系統軟體已成為EPO規模最大的硬體AI子領域，而專為執行神經網路運算所打造的神經網路加速器，其活躍程度自2020年高峰後已有所回落。歐洲申請人的布局則較集中於類神經形態電路——模仿生物神經結構的電路設計——以及類比電路。

  Marks & Clerk AI專業合夥人Mike Williams表示，公司的申請資料多年來持續顯示AI正從雲端向實體世界擴展，而他將2025年的數字視為迄今這一趨勢最有力的佐證。

  ## 審查程序：分案申請普遍，異議案例罕見

  分案申請——從早先母案拆分出來、另行主張獨立請求項的申請方式——在硬體領域明顯較整體AI領域更為普遍。2025年硬體AI公告中，分案申請占14.7%，而整體AI的比例僅5.8%。在2015年至2025年的每一個完結年度，分案申請的獲准率均高於一般案件，分別為73.2%對59.6%。

  直接向EPO提出的硬體案件，在實質審查階段面臨更多挑戰。X類引證——最具殺傷力的現有技術引證類型，可對申請案中的每一項請求項提出質疑——在附有檢索報告的硬體AI申請案中出現比例高達74.1%。儘管如此，已完結的案件中仍有57.6%獲准。

  異議程序——EPO的正式授後異議機制——在整個AI領域仍屬罕見。截至2025年底，EPO共授予33,777件AI專利，其中僅459件（即1.4%）遭到異議。報告所識別的735件硬體AI專利中，更是無一面臨異議。

  ## 硬體專利權人對UPC態度保守

  統一專利法院（UPC）是一套2023年起在歐盟參與成員國間運作的集中訴訟體系，硬體專利權人對其採取審慎態度。在2025年獲准的AI專利中，22.6%已登記為統一專利（Unitary Patent）——即UPC所支持的單一專利權利形式——而41.2%則選擇退出UPC管轄，以規避集中撤銷程序。退出比例已從2022年授權專利的高峰值53.7%下滑。

  硬體AI專利權人更為保守：在此類別中，僅14.1%選擇統一專利途徑，高達54.3%選擇退出，是本次回顧中所有AI子領域裡退出率最高的一個。

  Stott在報告中指出，硬體專利權人對UPC較為謹慎，反映出一項更廣泛的策略需求——鑒於近四分之三的硬體AI案件發源自歐洲以外，業者必須從首次申請之初便建立完整的歐洲專利布局策略。

  ## 中國與加拿大

  在歐洲以外，中國國家知識產權局（CNIPA）2025年共錄得6,404件Hardware AI公告，逾EPO總量的三倍。中國在該領域的專利授予數量自2018年以來已增加十六倍。中國申請人絕大多數選擇在本國申請，在中國境內市場持有88%的公告份額，在EPO的占比則不足9%。

  加拿大方面，報告發布之際，加拿大正就AI硬體專利適用較為寬鬆的可專利性標準徵詢草案意見；然而，不涉及實體硬體元件的應用AI發明，仍可能面臨請求項須引用實體設備的要求，使此類專利的執行難度相對提高。

  ## 新進入者

  硬體AI領域持續吸引首次申請者投入。2025年，EPO的硬體AI公告中有22.2%來自首次涉足該領域的申請人，高於2023年的18.2%；不過，這批新進入者中約有三分之二在EPO其他AI類別中已有既有公告紀錄。

  Stott在報告中表示，繼約2020年前後神經網路架構申請案浪潮達到高峰後，業界的興趣如今似乎正在分散，逐漸超越第一代加速器，向AI輔助晶片設計、記憶體以及類神經形態電路等領域多元發展。
