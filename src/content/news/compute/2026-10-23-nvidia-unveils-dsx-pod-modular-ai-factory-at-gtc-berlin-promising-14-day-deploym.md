body_en: |
  Nvidia unveiled the DSX POD at GTC Berlin, a prefabricated modular reference design for AI data centers built around its Vera Rubin platform, with the company saying factory-built units can reduce deployment times from approximately six months to as little as 14 days.

  Chief executive Jensen Huang framed the product in direct financial terms during his keynote. Each gigawatt-scale data center costs $50 billion to build and is expected to generate tens of billions of dollars per year in revenue, Huang said. A three-month delay in bringing such a facility online costs $10 billion in foregone revenue. By shifting integration work from construction sites into the supply chain, Huang said, the time from installation to production can fall from months to approximately one month.

  Nvidia described "first production token" as a key metric for measuring deployment progress, defining it as how quickly a new AI factory moves from construction to running production workloads. "The value of an AI factory comes from when it starts producing tokens, not when construction is completed," Huang said, according to a report by Commercial Times.

  ## Partners and delivery timeline

  Nvidia said 17 manufacturing and infrastructure partners will produce and deliver DSX POD units globally beginning in early 2027. The partners are Anduril, Armada, Bull, Crusoe, DayOne, Dell Technologies, Delta Electronics, Eaton, Foxconn, Gigabyte, HPE, Lenovo, Pegatron, Schneider Electric, Vertiv, Wistron, and Wiwynn.

  ## How DSX POD works

  The DSX POD integrates Vera Rubin compute systems with networking, power, and liquid cooling infrastructure inside a containerized enclosure. Partners complete system integration and verification at their own factories before shipping finished modules to customer sites. Once site-level power, cooling, and networking connections are in place, the units can be brought online without extensive on-site construction work, Nvidia said.

  Traditional AI infrastructure deployment requires coordinating compute systems, network equipment, rack power distribution, liquid cooling pipelines, and cabling, all of which involve on-site planning, specialized labor scheduling, and logistics, Nvidia said. By pre-completing inter-rack cabling, wiring, pipeline configuration, and functional testing at the factory, the DSX POD reduces on-site complexity and lowers the risk of component delays and installation errors. The design also allows equipment integration and site preparation to proceed simultaneously rather than sequentially, shortening the overall schedule.

  ## Cooling approach and siting

  Nvidia said the DSX POD uses dry coolers rather than evaporative cooling systems, a design the company said could help address community concerns about data center water use.

  Charlie Boyle, Nvidia's vice president of DGX systems, told Data Center Knowledge that the units can be installed inside existing industrial structures and brownfield sites, referring to formerly used industrial or warehouse properties, rather than requiring purpose-built facilities. "I have got people putting these effectively in old, abandoned warehouses," Boyle said. "An old, abandoned building you see every day – suddenly there are lights in there, and it's now a data center."

  Boyle said operators could also site modules where waste heat from the systems can be recovered and used by nearby facilities. "You can put it somewhere you can reuse the heat, like next to a swimming pool," he said.

  On upgradeability, a concern with tightly integrated containerized designs given how closely power, cooling, and compute are coupled, Boyle said customers could deploy containers housing newer Nvidia systems alongside existing modules as their infrastructure needs change.

  Data Center Knowledge noted, citing data from property firm JLL, that in parts of Europe, operators are increasingly looking beyond urban centers for larger campus-style developments, which may limit the available brownfield opportunity in some markets.

  ## Greenpeace protest interrupts keynote

  The keynote was interrupted when a small group of Greenpeace activists entered the auditorium, unfurled a banner reading "Nvidia, clean up your dirty business!" and projected a message onto the stage reading "Clean up Your Dirty Business." Protesters also shouted questions about reports that Nvidia Jetson Orin modules, which Nvidia describes as consumer-grade systems sold to students, developers, and startups, had been found in the wreckage of Russian military drones, and about the environmental consequences of data center expansion.

  Greenpeace confirmed it organized the action. "While Nvidia is building an unchecked AI empire no one consented to, people are paying the price," Benjamin Stephan, Big Tech campaigner at Greenpeace International, said in a statement. "Around the world, communities face damage to water supplies and soil, alongside rising electricity bills because of unchecked expansion of data centers."

  Huang asked security personnel not to overreact as protesters were escorted out. "Everybody has a chance to say something," he said. "I'll talk to you later. But let me get through my keynote."

  Anti-data center demonstrations have become more common, according to Data Center Knowledge, though they are typically staged outside venues rather than inside keynote sessions.

  ## Industry significance

  Prefabricated modular data center products, including container-based designs, have been offered by vendors including Dell, HPE, Schneider Electric, and Vertiv for several years. What Nvidia is betting on, according to Data Center Knowledge, is that a reference architecture endorsed by the company will help standardize deployments and accelerate adoption as operators compete to add AI capacity.

  The DSX POD extends Nvidia's existing DSX infrastructure platform by packaging Vera Rubin compute with supporting infrastructure inside a containerized form factor that partner manufacturers can build, test, and deliver against a common blueprint. Global delivery from the 17 named partner manufacturers is scheduled to begin in early 2027.
body_zh: |
  輝達（Nvidia）於GTC柏林大會上發表DSX POD，這是一款以Vera Rubin平台為核心的預製模組化AI資料中心參考設計。該公司表示，工廠預組裝的機組可將部署時程從約六個月大幅縮短至最短14天。

  執行長黃仁勳在主題演講中以直接的財務語言闡述這項產品的價值。他表示，每座吉瓦（gigawatt）規模的資料中心建設成本高達500億美元，預計每年可創造數百億美元的營收。黃仁勳說，這樣一座設施若延遲三個月上線，將損失100億美元的潛在營收。他表示，透過將整合工作從施工現場移至供應鏈，從安裝到正式投產的時程可從數個月縮短至約一個月。

  輝達將「首個生產詞元」（first production token）定義為衡量部署進度的關鍵指標，意即一座新AI工廠從竣工到開始執行生產工作負載所需的時間。黃仁勳表示：「AI工廠的價值來自於它開始產出詞元的那一刻，而非建設完工的那一刻。」上述言論引自《工商時報》的報導。

  ## 合作夥伴與交貨時程

  輝達表示，17家製造與基礎設施合作夥伴將於2027年初開始在全球生產並交付DSX POD機組。合作夥伴包括：Anduril、Armada、Bull、Crusoe、DayOne、戴爾科技（Dell Technologies）、台達電子（Delta Electronics）、伊頓（Eaton）、鴻海（Foxconn）、技嘉（Gigabyte）、惠普企業（HPE）、聯想（Lenovo）、和碩（Pegatron）、施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）、維諦技術（Vertiv）、緯創（Wistron）以及緯穎（Wiwynn）。

  ## DSX POD 運作方式

  DSX POD 將 Vera Rubin 運算系統與網路、電力及液冷基礎設施整合於貨櫃式外殼之中。合作夥伴在各自工廠完成系統整合與驗證後，再將成品模組運送至客戶場地。一旦場地的電力、冷卻與網路連線就緒，這些機組即可上線，無需大規模的現場施工，輝達表示。

  傳統AI基礎設施部署需要協調運算系統、網路設備、機架配電、液冷管路及佈線，所有環節都涉及現場規劃、專業技工排程以及物流協調，輝達表示。透過在工廠預先完成機架間佈線、電力配線、管路設定及功能測試，DSX POD 可降低現場作業的複雜度，並減少零組件延誤與安裝失誤的風險。此設計同時允許設備整合與場地準備同步推進，而非依序進行，從而縮短整體工期。

  ## 冷卻方案與選址

  輝達表示，DSX POD 採用乾式冷卻器，而非蒸發式冷卻系統，該公司表示這項設計有助於回應社區對資料中心耗水問題的疑慮。

  輝達 DGX 系統副總裁查理．波伊爾（Charlie Boyle）向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，這些機組可安裝於現有工業建築及棕地場址（即過去曾供工業或倉儲使用的閒置土地），無需特地興建專用設施。「我已經看到有人把這些機組直接裝進廢棄的舊倉庫，」波伊爾說，「你每天路過那棟舊廢棄建築——突然間裡頭亮起了燈，它已經變成一座資料中心了。」

  波伊爾表示，業者也可以選擇將模組部署在廢熱可被鄰近設施回收再利用的地點。「你可以把它放在可以再利用熱能的地方，例如游泳池旁邊，」他說。

  關於可升級性的問題——由於電力、冷卻與運算系統高度耦合，緊密整合的貨櫃式設計在這方面向來令人存疑——波伊爾表示，隨著基礎設施需求的演進，客戶可以在現有模組旁並排部署搭載更新款輝達系統的貨櫃。

  《Data Center Knowledge》援引房地產業者仲量聯行（JLL）的數據指出，在歐洲部分地區，業者正日益將眼光投向都會區以外的大型園區開發，這在某些市場可能壓縮了棕地選址的可用空間。

  ## 綠色和平抗議人士闖入打斷主題演講

  主題演講進行途中，一小群綠色和平抗議人士闖入會場，展開一幅寫有「Nvidia，清理你的骯髒生意！」（Nvidia, clean up your dirty business!）的橫幅，並在舞台上投影「清理你的骯髒生意」字樣。抗議者還高聲質問兩件事：其一，據報導輝達 Jetson Orin 模組（輝達將其定位為面向學生、開發者及新創企業的消費級產品）在俄羅斯軍用無人機殘骸中被發現一事；其二，資料中心擴張對環境所造成的衝擊。

  綠色和平確認此次行動由其主導。「輝達正在建立一個未經任何人同意、不受約束的AI帝國，而人們正在為此付出代價，」綠色和平國際大科技倡議人班傑明．史蒂芬（Benjamin Stephan）在聲明中表示，「世界各地的社區正面臨水源與土壤受損的威脅，同時還要承受因資料中心無節制擴張所帶來的電費攀升。」

  黃仁勳在抗議者被護送離場時，要求安保人員不要反應過度。「每個人都有發聲的機會，」他說，「我之後會跟你們談。但讓我先把主題演講講完。」

  根據《Data Center Knowledge》報導，反資料中心抗議活動已愈來愈常見，但通常是在場外舉行，而非直接闖入主題演講廳。

  ## 產業意義

  預製模組化資料中心產品（包括貨櫃式設計）已由戴爾、惠普企業、施耐德電氣及維諦技術等廠商推出多年。根據《Data Center Knowledge》的分析，輝達押注的是：由該公司背書的參考架構，將在業者競相擴充AI產能之際，有效推動部署標準化並加速市場採用。

  DSX POD 延伸了輝達現有的 DSX 基礎設施平台，將 Vera Rubin 運算系統與配套基礎設施封裝於貨櫃式外型之中，供合作製造商依照統一藍圖進行建造、測試與交付。17家指定合作製造商的全球交付作業，預計於2027年初展開。
