body_en: |
  Taiwan-listed silicon capacitor maker Aipu (6531.TW) and wafer foundry PSMC (6770.TW) are set to supply Intel's EMIB-T advanced packaging platform, as MediaTek (2454.TW) works alongside them to target Google's next-generation tensor processing unit (TPU), according to supply chain sources cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times.

  The arrangement marks a broadening of the AI ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit) advanced packaging market beyond TSMC's (2330.TW) CoWoS technology to Intel's EMIB-T platform, as cloud operators including Google, Meta, and AWS accelerate development of their own custom AI chips, analysts cited in the report said.

  ## MediaTek Targeting Google's Next TPU

  Google's next-generation TPU, designated v8t and codenamed "Zebrafish," will rely on MediaTek for I/O die design and back-end packaging services, Commercial Times reported, citing supply chain sources. Analysts quoted in the report said MediaTek's ASIC business could eventually exceed the company's existing mobile chip operations in scale, making it an important medium- to long-term growth driver.

  MediaTek has stated publicly that it will support both TSMC's CoWoS and Intel's EMIB advanced packaging technologies simultaneously, offering customers flexibility in packaging choice. Supply chain sources said MediaTek has been conducting ongoing tests of the EMIB-T platform with Intel, integrating integrated passive device (IPD) silicon capacitors supplied by Aipu.

  ## Aipu's Silicon Capacitor Role

  Aipu produces silicon capacitors under the S-SiCap brand, characterized by high capacitance density, low profile height, and customizable dimensions. The company says the components are suited for 2.5D packaging, silicon interposers, and substrate-embedded configurations — addressing power integrity and signal stability demands in AI server and high-performance computing (HPC) chip designs.

  Analysts cited by Commercial Times said Aipu's silicon capacitor business is entering a "harvest phase," with monthly IPD wafer capacity forecast to potentially approach 10,000 wafers by end of next year, making it a key growth engine over the following two years.

  ## PSMC Recruited to Expand Supply

  Despite the projected capacity ramp, analysts warned that Aipu's existing output may not be sufficient to meet accelerating demand tied to the EMIB-T platform. To address that constraint, Aipu is helping Intel identify PSMC as an additional silicon capacitor production partner, Commercial Times reported, with the aim of building a more flexible supply structure.

  Executives from both Aipu and PSMC are expected to meet with Intel Chief Executive Lip-Bu Tan in the near term to advance discussions on the collaboration, according to market sources cited in the report.

  ## Intel's EMIB-T Technology

  Intel describes EMIB-T as an extension of its EMIB technology. According to Intel's public disclosures cited by Commercial Times, the platform targets high-bandwidth memory (HBM), high-bandwidth UCIe interfaces, and large chiplet architecture designs. It uses through-silicon via (TSV) technology and high-density interconnects to improve chip-to-chip data transfer efficiency while managing bandwidth, power consumption, and package area. Intel positions EMIB-T as better suited to high-bandwidth, high-power AI accelerator applications than conventional packaging architectures.

  ## Supply Chain Implications

  The Aipu-PSMC-MediaTek alignment around Intel's EMIB-T platform indicates that Taiwan's IC design houses, wafer foundries, and advanced packaging specialists are building positions in parallel supply chains serving both TSMC and Intel platforms. Analysts cited by Commercial Times expect this trend to accelerate as Google, Meta, AWS, and other cloud operators deepen their custom AI chip programs.

  The next formal milestone, per market sources cited in the report, is the planned meeting between Aipu and PSMC executives and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan, which is expected to advance the terms of the silicon capacitor supply arrangement.

  ## 愛普、普誠將加入英特爾EMIB-T供應鏈，聯發科同步搶攻Google TPU訂單

  據《工商時報》引述供應鏈消息人士報導，台灣上市矽電容廠愛普（6531）與晶圓代工廠普誠科技（6770）確定將供貨英特爾EMIB-T先進封裝平台，聯發科（2454）也同步與兩家廠商攜手，共同搶攻Google下一代張量處理器（TPU）訂單。

  法人分析師指出，此一合作架構標誌著AI ASIC（特殊應用積體電路）先進封裝市場正從台積電（2330）獨家主導的CoWoS技術，進一步延伸至英特爾EMIB-T平台。隨著Google、Meta、AWS等雲端業者加速自研AI晶片腳步，供應鏈多元化趨勢已然成形。

  ## 聯發科鎖定Google下一代TPU

  《工商時報》引述供應鏈消息人士指出，Google下一代TPU——版本代號「v8t」、研發代號「Zebrafish（斑馬魚）」——將委由聯發科負責I/O晶片設計及後段封裝服務。報告中引述的分析師表示，聯發科ASIC業務長期成長潛力龐大，規模甚至有機會超越現有的手機晶片業務，成為中長期最重要的獲利引擎。

  聯發科已公開表示，將同時支援台積電CoWoS與英特爾EMIB兩大先進封裝技術，讓客戶在封裝選擇上擁有更大彈性。供應鏈消息人士透露，聯發科目前已持續與英特爾進行EMIB-T平台的驗測工作，整合內容包括愛普所供應的整合被動元件（IPD）矽電容。

  ## 愛普矽電容的關鍵角色

  愛普以「S-SiCap」品牌生產矽電容，產品具備高電容密度、低側面高度及可客製化尺寸等特性。該公司表示，這類元件適用於2.5D封裝、矽中介層及基板嵌埋等應用情境，能有效應對AI伺服器與高效能運算（HPC）晶片設計中對電源完整性與訊號穩定性的嚴苛要求。

  《工商時報》引述分析師表示，愛普矽電容業務正式進入「收穫期」，預計至明年底，IPD晶圓月產能有望逼近一萬片，將成為未來兩年最重要的成長動能。

  ## 引進普誠擴充供應能量

  然而，分析師也警告，即便產能持續擴充，愛普現有的產出規模恐怕仍不足以應對EMIB-T平台加速放量所帶動的龐大需求。對此，《工商時報》報導指出，愛普正協助英特爾物色普誠科技作為額外的矽電容生產合作夥伴，目標是建立更具彈性的供應鏈架構，以因應未來需求缺口。

  據市場消息人士引述，愛普與普誠高層預計近期將赴美與英特爾執行長唐仁璽（Lip-Bu Tan）會面，就雙方合作細節進行深入洽談。

  ## 英特爾EMIB-T技術解析

  英特爾將EMIB-T定位為其EMIB技術的延伸版本。根據《工商時報》所引述的英特爾公開揭露資料，EMIB-T平台主要針對高頻寬記憶體（HBM）、高頻寬UCIe介面及大型小晶片（chiplet）架構設計。該平台採用矽穿孔（TSV）技術與高密度互連線路，藉此提升晶片間資料傳輸效率，同時兼顧頻寬、功耗與封裝面積的整體最佳化。英特爾認為，相較於傳統封裝架構，EMIB-T更能勝任高頻寬、高功耗的AI加速器應用需求。

  ## 供應鏈格局的深層意涵

  愛普、普誠與聯發科圍繞英特爾EMIB-T平台所形成的策略聯盟，清楚說明台灣IC設計業者、晶圓代工廠及先進封裝專業廠商，正同步在台積電與英特爾兩大平台之間建立平行供應鏈布局。《工商時報》引述的分析師預期，隨著Google、Meta、AWS等雲端巨頭持續深化自研AI晶片計畫，這股多軌並行的趨勢只會加速擴大。

  據報告引述的市場消息人士指出，下一個正式里程碑，將是愛普與普誠高層與英特爾執行長唐仁璽的預定會面，屆時預計將就矽電容供貨安排的具體條件取得進一步共識。
