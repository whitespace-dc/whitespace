body_en: |
  OpenAI is building a formal business unit to sell artificial intelligence tools to semiconductor companies, pursuing what it internally calls a "new bet" on AI-assisted chip design and company operations, according to a job listing for a deal lead role.

  The position sits within OpenAI's Special Situations team, which the company describes in the listing as "the company's commercial engine for its most complex and consequential opportunities." The effort has not been previously reported.

  ## What the role entails

  The deal lead is expected to identify where AI can create "step-change value" — meaning order-of-magnitude improvements rather than incremental gains — for semiconductor companies and their ecosystem partners. The listing states the hire will pitch OpenAI's models for use in chip development and internal company operations.

  Deals that result from the effort are expected to involve "non-standard partnerships, including revenue share, royalties, and bespoke commercial terms," the listing states. That structure leaves open whether resulting chips would be used by OpenAI itself, though the listing does not describe that as a required outcome.

  The listing also acknowledges direct competition with "rival chip AI automation startups," a category that has attracted significant investment.

  ## Context: OpenAI's own chip work

  The recruitment follows OpenAI's unveiling of Jalapeño, described as OpenAI's first Intelligence Processor — a custom inference chip, meaning a chip designed to run AI models rather than train them — co-designed with semiconductor company Broadcom. OpenAI said its own AI models were used to accelerate parts of Jalapeño's design and optimization process. The company has said Jalapeño is the first of multiple chips underway.

  ## Competitive landscape

  Google's DeepMind division has used its AlphaChip system — an AI tool for automating chip floorplanning, the process of arranging components on a chip — across multiple generations of its Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), which are custom chips Google uses for AI workloads, as well as its Axion processors, which are Arm-based central processing units (CPUs).

  Two founders of AlphaChip left to start Ricursive Intelligence, a standalone AI chip design company that was valued at $4 billion as of January, according to the source material.

  ## Industry significance

  OpenAI's move to commercialize AI for semiconductor design extends the company beyond its core business of developing and licensing AI models, positioning it as a vendor to the chip industry that underpins AI infrastructure broadly. The Special Situations framing — described in the listing as a place where new verticals begin before maturing into "repeatable ways of working" — suggests the company views semiconductor partnerships as a potential long-term commercial line rather than a one-off arrangement.

  OpenAI said in the listing that the Special Situations team's efforts "shape how OpenAI operates at scale," indicating the semiconductor vertical is intended to evolve into a standardized business motion. The company is currently filling the deal lead role.

  該職位隸屬於OpenAI的「特殊情況」（Special Situations）團隊。公司在公告中將其描述為「公司最複雜、最關鍵商機的商業引擎」。

  ## 職位職責

  這名交易主管的任務，是找出AI能為半導體公司及其生態系夥伴創造「跨越式價值」的機會——意即達到數量級的突破，而非漸進式的改善。公告指出，這位人選將向客戶推銷OpenAI模型，應用範疇涵蓋晶片開發以及公司內部的營運管理。

  由此產生的合約預計涉及「非標準夥伴關係，包括營收分潤、授權金，以及客製化商業條款」。這樣的架構讓外界對最終晶片是否將由OpenAI自用一事仍存疑問，不過公告並未將此列為必要條件。

  公告中也坦承，此舉將直接面對「競爭對手的晶片AI自動化新創公司」，這個領域近年來已吸引大量投資。

  ## 背景：OpenAI自身的晶片研發

  此次招募是在OpenAI發表Jalapeño之後展開。Jalapeño被描述為OpenAI首款「智慧處理器」（Intelligence Processor），是一顆專為執行（而非訓練）AI模型所設計的客製化推論晶片，由OpenAI與半導體公司博通（Broadcom）共同設計。OpenAI表示，公司自身的AI模型被用來加速Jalapeño部分設計與最佳化的流程，並指出Jalapeño只是多款進行中晶片計畫的第一顆。

  ## 競爭格局

  Google旗下的DeepMind部門已透過「AlphaChip」系統，將AI應用於多個世代的張量處理器（TPU）以及基於Arm架構的Axion中央處理器（CPU）之上。AlphaChip是一款用於自動化晶片佈局規劃的AI工具，所謂佈局規劃，是指將各元件配置於晶片上的過程；TPU則是Google為AI工作負載所打造的客製化晶片。

  AlphaChip的兩位共同創辦人其後離職，成立了獨立的AI晶片設計公司Ricursive Intelligence。根據資料來源，截至今年一月，該公司估值已達40億美元。

  ## 產業意義

  OpenAI將AI商業化應用於半導體設計，使其業務延伸超越了開發與授權AI模型的核心業務，將自身定位為支撐整體AI基礎設施的晶片產業供應商。「特殊情況」框架——公告描述其為新垂直業務的孵化之地，最終成熟為「可複製的運作方式」——顯示公司將半導體夥伴關係視為潛在的長期商業線，而非一次性安排。

  OpenAI在公告中指出，特殊情況團隊的努力「形塑了OpenAI的規模化運作方式」，顯示半導體垂直業務預計將進化為標準化的商業模式。目前，OpenAI仍在招募這名交易主管。
