body_en: |
  Intel and AMD are securing longer-term purchase commitments from Chinese server customers for data-center central processing units (CPUs), as prices for some products have climbed more than 40% in China since the start of the year, according to two people familiar with the negotiations.

  The agreements under discussion lock in purchase volumes but not prices, the people said. Most cover roughly one year of supply, although both Intel and AMD have discussed commitments of two years or longer with some customers, one of the people added. The sources declined to be identified because they were not authorized to speak to the media. Intel and AMD did not respond to requests for comment.

  Month-on-month CPU price increases in China have topped 10% for some products, one of the sources said.

  ## Why server CPUs are now in short supply

  Server CPUs, which manage general compute tasks across servers, storage, networking, and inference workloads in AI data centers, have historically been easier to obtain than the graphics processing units (GPUs) — chips like those made by Nvidia — used to train and run AI models. The current tightness marks a shift: AI infrastructure buildouts are consuming CPUs in large volumes alongside accelerators, tipping a market that had been relatively loose into shortage conditions.

  Reuters reported earlier this year that Intel and AMD had notified Chinese customers of lengthy delivery waits, with Intel lead times reaching as long as six months for some products.

  Intel chief executive Lip-Bu Tan told analysts in April that demand "continues to run ahead of supply," citing Xeon server CPUs — Intel's primary data-center processor line — as a particular constraint. Tan also referenced a multi-year supply deal with Google and said Intel signed several other long-term contracts in the first quarter of the year.

  AMD, which is scheduled to report quarterly results in early August, has already raised its server CPU market forecast to more than $120 billion by 2030, attributing the upward revision to strong demand tied to agentic AI workloads — AI systems that autonomously execute multi-step tasks.

  ## China's role and the GPU restriction backdrop

  China is one of the world's largest server markets, driven by rapid construction of data center racks, AI computing clusters, and national computing infrastructure, the source report said. That buildout has intensified competition for Intel and AMD processors even as Chinese buyers face separate US government restrictions limiting their access to the most advanced AI GPUs.

  Constrained GPU supply has pushed Chinese cloud providers and internet companies to expand AI deployments using CPU-based and hybrid infrastructure, adding further pressure on server CPU availability.

  Tighter CPU supply and rising prices could raise costs and slow deployment timelines for those companies, the people said.

  ## Echoes of the memory market

  The shift toward longer-term CPU purchase commitments mirrors dynamics already visible in the memory-chip market, where AI-driven shortages have pushed buyers to seek supply security through extended contracts rather than spot purchases.

  Intel's quarterly earnings, due Thursday, are expected to address the CPU shortage directly, with analysts watching for further detail on lead times, pricing, and the pace at which supply capacity is being added. AMD's update is anticipated in early August.
body_zh: |
  英特爾（Intel）與超微（AMD）正積極與中國伺服器客戶簽訂資料中心中央處理器（CPU）的長期採購承諾。據兩名熟悉談判內情的人士表示，自今年年初以來，部分產品在中國的售價已上漲逾40%。

  據悉，雙方洽談中的協議鎖定採購量，但不約定售價。多數合約涵蓋約一年的供貨期，但其中一名消息人士補充，英特爾與超微皆已與部分客戶討論長達兩年甚至更長的承諾。消息來源均以未獲授權對外發言為由，婉拒具名；英特爾與超微亦未回應本媒體的置評請求。

  其中一名消息人士指出，中國市場部分CPU產品的月漲幅已超過10%。

  ## 伺服器CPU為何供不應求

  伺服器CPU負責管理伺服器、儲存、網路，以及AI資料中心推論工作負載的一般運算任務，過去向來比輝達（Nvidia）等廠商生產、用於訓練及執行AI模型的繪圖處理器（GPU）更易取得。如今供應吃緊，標誌著市場出現結構性轉變：AI基礎設施的大規模建置正與加速器並肩大量消耗CPU，使原本供需相對寬鬆的市場陷入短缺局面。

  路透社稍早曾報導，英特爾與超微已知會中國客戶，部分產品的交貨等待期大幅拉長，其中英特爾部分產品的交期甚至達到六個月之久。

  英特爾執行長陳立武（Lip-Bu Tan）今年4月在法說會上向分析師表示，需求「持續超過供給」，並特別點名英特爾主力資料中心處理器Xeon伺服器CPU是供應瓶頸所在。他同時提及與Google簽訂的一項多年供應協議，並透露英特爾在今年第一季另外簽下數項長期合約。

  超微預計於8月初公布季度財報。該公司已將伺服器CPU的市場規模預測上調至2030年逾1,200億美元，並將此一上修歸因於代理型AI（agentic AI）工作負載所帶動的強勁需求——這類AI系統能自主執行多步驟任務。

  ## 中國市場角色與GPU管制背景

  報導指出，在資料中心機架、AI運算叢集及國家算力基礎設施的快速建設驅動下，中國是全球規模最大的伺服器市場之一。此波建設熱潮加劇了市場對英特爾與超微處理器的競逐，而中國買家同時還面臨美國政府針對最先進AI GPU的出口管制限制，取得高階AI晶片的管道受阻。

  GPU供應受限，迫使中國雲端業者與網路企業轉而透過以CPU為主或混合式基礎設施擴展AI部署，進一步加重了伺服器CPU的供應壓力。

  消息人士表示，CPU供應趨緊與價格持續攀升，恐將推高相關企業的營運成本，並拖慢部署時程。

  ## 記憶體市場的前車之鑑

  CPU長期採購承諾的風潮，與記憶體晶片市場已出現的動態如出一轍——AI驅動的短缺促使買家紛紛轉向長期合約，而非仰賴現貨採購，以確保供應無虞。

  英特爾季度財報預計於本週四發布，外界預料將直接觸及CPU短缺議題，分析師將聚焦交期、定價，以及產能擴充進度等細節。超微的財報更新則預計於8月初揭曉。
