body_en: |
  Alphabet's Google has placed an order with Intel to manufacture more than three million tensor processing units (TPUs) — the company's in-house AI accelerator chips — for delivery in 2028, The Information reported on June 8, citing people with direct knowledge of the discussions. Intel shares rose more than 9% in early trading on the news.

  The potential order would mark a significant win for Intel's foundry business, which makes chips designed by other companies, as the chipmaker tries to rebuild its manufacturing reputation after years of setbacks that allowed Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) to become the dominant contract chip producer.

  Nvidia, the leading maker of graphics processing units (GPUs) used in AI data centers, is also evaluating whether Intel's manufacturing technology could be used to produce a processor that combines four graphics chips into a single unit, The Information reported. Nvidia has not placed an order with Intel.

  Intel declined to comment on the report. Alphabet and Nvidia did not immediately respond to requests for comment, according to Reuters, which could not independently verify the story.

  ## TSMC capacity crunch drives diversification

  Soaring chip demand from the artificial intelligence boom has left TSMC struggling to meet supply, according to The Information. That capacity shortfall has pushed several major AI chip design companies to consider Intel as an alternative manufacturer.

  Jacob Bourne, technology analyst at eMarketer, described the reported order as "evidence that AI's biggest players are racing to diversify a supply chain still heavily concentrated in TSMC."

  D.A. Davidson analyst Gil Luria said Google and Nvidia have additional motivation beyond supply-chain diversification. "Supporting Intel supports U.S.-based manufacturing, which is important for the relationship with the U.S. administration," Luria said.

  ## Intel's turnaround under Lip-Bu Tan

  Intel's shares have gained nearly 169% so far this year as the company shows signs of progress under chief executive Lip-Bu Tan. Since Tan took charge, Intel has secured billions of dollars in investment from the Trump administration, Nvidia, and SoftBank, according to Reuters.

  The Trump administration has also actively sought to direct business to Intel, an official said last month. Tesla was reported to be the first major customer for Intel's next-generation 14A manufacturing process, for chips intended for Elon Musk's Terafab project, described as an advanced AI chip complex planned for Austin.

  Last month, the Wall Street Journal reported that Intel reached a preliminary deal to manufacture some chips for Apple devices following more than a year of talks.

  ## Google's TPU strategy

  Google has been developing its TPUs as an alternative to Nvidia's GPUs for AI workloads in its data centers. According to Reuters, sales of TPUs have become a growth driver for Google Cloud revenue.

  If completed, the 2028 order would be among the largest publicly reported commitments to Intel's foundry business and would mark a further step in Intel's effort to attract hyperscale cloud customers alongside the automotive and consumer electronics orders already reported.
body_zh: |
  據《The Information》2026年6月8日引述直接知情人士報導，Alphabet旗下Google已向英特爾下單，委託其製造逾300萬顆張量處理器（TPU）——即Google自研的AI加速晶片——預計2028年交貨。消息曝光後，英特爾股價於盤前交易中大漲逾9%。

  這筆潛在訂單對英特爾晶圓代工業務而言將是一大勝利。晶圓代工的模式是替其他公司設計的晶片代為製造。英特爾近年在製造技術上接連挫敗，拱手讓台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）坐穩全球最大晶圓代工廠的地位，此番若能拿下這筆大單，將有助其重建製造聲譽。

  《The Information》同篇報導亦指出，AI資料中心繪圖處理器（GPU）的領導廠商輝達，正在評估能否借助英特爾的製造技術，生產一款將四顆繪圖晶片整合為單一封裝的處理器。不過輝達目前尚未向英特爾正式下單。

  英特爾拒絕就此報導置評；Alphabet與輝達則未立即回應媒體詢問。路透社表示，該社無法獨立核實上述消息。

  ## 台積電產能吃緊，促使業者積極分散布局

  《The Information》指出，人工智慧熱潮帶動晶片需求急遽攀升，台積電已面臨供應吃緊的困境。這一缺口促使多家主要AI晶片設計業者開始將英特爾納入備選代工廠。

  市調機構eMarketer科技分析師Jacob Bourne形容，這筆據報訂單是「AI界巨頭競相分散供應鏈的佐證，因為供應鏈至今仍高度集中於台積電。」

  D.A. Davidson分析師Gil Luria則指出，Google與輝達有著超越供應鏈分散之外的額外動機。他說：「支持英特爾，就是支持美國本土製造業，這對維繫與美國行政當局的關係具有重要意義。」

  ## 陳立武帶領英特爾走向復甦

  英特爾股價今年迄今已累計大漲近169%，顯示公司在執行長陳立武（Lip-Bu Tan）領導下出現好轉跡象。據路透社報導，自陳立武接掌帥印以來，英特爾已相繼獲得川普政府、輝達與軟銀（SoftBank）的數十億美元投資挹注。

  川普政府一名官員上月也表示，政府已積極設法將訂單引導至英特爾。特斯拉（Tesla）據報是英特爾下世代14A製程的首家重量級客戶，相關晶片將用於Elon Musk的Terafab計畫——一個規劃落腳德州奧斯丁、定位為先進AI晶片複合基地的大型設施。

  上月，《華爾街日報》報導英特爾已與蘋果達成初步協議，雙方在歷經逾一年的洽談後，英特爾將代工製造部分蘋果裝置晶片。

  ## Google的TPU策略

  Google長期投入自研TPU的開發，做為在資料中心AI運算上取代輝達GPU的替代方案。據路透社報導，TPU的銷售已成為Google Cloud營收的一大成長動能。

  若這筆2028年訂單順利成交，將是英特爾晶圓代工業務迄今最大規模的公開承諾之一，也代表英特爾在爭取超大規模雲端客戶方面再進一步——此前英特爾已陸續傳出拿下汽車及消費性電子領域的代工訂單。
