body_en: |
  OpenAI intends to purchase artificial intelligence computing chips from Alphabet's Google, according to a person familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified because the discussions are private. Final terms have not been determined, and both OpenAI and Google declined to comment.

  The move adds Google's tensor processing units, or TPUs — chips Google developed more than a decade ago initially to accelerate its web search engine before they became central to AI workloads — to OpenAI's sourcing strategy alongside its existing reliance on Nvidia graphics processing units, or GPUs.

  ## OpenAI's Multi-Chip Strategy

  The potential TPU purchase is one element of a broader infrastructure push at OpenAI. The company has already announced plans to deploy its own in-house chips, called Jalapeno, to support its AI models later in 2026. Those chips were developed in partnership with semiconductor firm Broadcom.

  OpenAI's internal chip team is led by Richard Ho, who was previously involved in the development of Google's TPUs, according to The Straits Times. The lineage illustrates the degree to which Google's chip program has seeded talent and institutional knowledge across the industry.

  ## TPUs as an Nvidia Alternative

  Google began developing TPUs more than ten years ago. The processors were originally used to improve the efficiency of Google's search engine but have since emerged as one of the primary alternatives to Nvidia's GPUs for AI training and inference workloads.

  Other cloud providers are now offering access to TPUs. Oracle announced on October 27 that TPUs would be available through its cloud computing infrastructure. Google executive Amin Vahdat said in a statement announcing that deal: "The next generation of AI will require a diverse range of compute."

  ## Industry Context

  OpenAI joins a broad group of technology companies developing proprietary AI chips in an effort to reduce dependence on Nvidia, which currently dominates the AI accelerator market. The Straits Times, citing Bloomberg, noted that The Information previously reported OpenAI was planning to buy Google chips.

  If completed, the arrangement would mark a notable commercial relationship between two companies that compete directly in AI products and services. OpenAI has not disclosed the volume or financial terms of any potential chip purchase.

  ## OpenAI擬採購Google TPU晶片 擴大AI算力布局

  據一位知情人士透露，OpenAI計劃向Alphabet旗下的Google採購人工智慧運算晶片。由於相關磋商仍屬私下進行，該人士要求匿名。目前最終條款尚未確定，OpenAI與Google雙方均拒絕就此事發表評論。

  此舉意味著Google的張量處理器（Tensor Processing Unit，TPU）——這款Google十餘年前最初為加速旗下網路搜尋引擎而自主研發、後來逐漸成為AI工作負載核心的晶片——將被納入OpenAI的晶片採購策略，與其現有對輝達繪圖處理器（GPU）的依賴並行運作。

  ## OpenAI的多元晶片策略

  此次潛在的TPU採購，是OpenAI大規模基礎架構擴張計畫的一環。該公司日前已宣布，將於2026年下半年部署自行研發的內部晶片「Jalapeno」，以支援旗下AI模型。這款晶片係與半導體廠商博通（Broadcom）合作開發。

  據《海峽時報》報導，OpenAI內部晶片團隊由Richard Ho領軍，他此前曾參與Google TPU的研發工作。這樣的人才傳承，清楚說明了Google晶片計畫如何在整個產業播下技術種子、輸出制度知識。

  ## TPU：輝達的替代選項

  Google開發TPU至今已逾十年。這款處理器最初用於提升Google搜尋引擎的運算效率，如今已成為AI訓練與推論工作負載領域中，輝達GPU最主要的替代方案之一。

  目前已有其他雲端服務業者開始提供TPU存取服務。Oracle於10月27日宣布，TPU將透過其雲端運算基礎架構對外供應。Google高階主管Amin Vahdat在宣布該合作的聲明中表示：「下一世代的AI將需要多元化的運算資源。」

  ## 產業背景

  OpenAI加入了眾多科技企業自研AI晶片的行列，此舉旨在降低對輝達的依賴——輝達目前主導著整個AI加速器市場。《海峽時報》援引彭博社報導指出，《The Information》此前已報導OpenAI正計劃採購Google晶片。

  若此交易最終完成，將標誌著兩家在AI產品與服務領域直接競爭的企業之間，建立起一段值得關注的商業合作關係。OpenAI目前尚未披露任何潛在晶片採購的數量或財務條款。
