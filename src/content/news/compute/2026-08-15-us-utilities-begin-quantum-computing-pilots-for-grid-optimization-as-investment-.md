body_en: |
  Electric utilities across the United States are deploying quantum computing pilots and funding research partnerships, seeking to engage with the technology earlier than they did with artificial intelligence — whose sudden data center load growth caught the power sector largely unprepared. Startup investment in quantum computing reached $12.6 billion in 2025, six times the 2024 level, and McKinsey described the technology as reaching a "commercial tipping point" in an April market report.

  EPB, the municipal utility serving Chattanooga, Tennessee, has operated a quantum communications network since 2023, built with quantum computing company IonQ, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and the University of Tennessee. The utility plans to install a quantum computer with 36 logical qubits later this year, according to Patrick Swingle, EPB's manager of quantum systems and strategic initiatives. EPB will initially run the system on a hybrid quantum-classical architecture built on NVIDIA's CUDA-Q platform, which is designed to distribute workloads across quantum processing units (QPUs), graphics processing units (GPUs), and traditional central processing units (CPUs).

  The primary application will be optimizing a fleet of 150 batteries deployed across feeders that serve 200,000 meters and 112 substations, Swingle said. EPB plans to use the system to optimize battery charging and discharging schedules against supply-demand and peak load forecasts, and to prepare for future real-time dispatch as loads and weather shift.

  Middle Tennessee Electric (MTE), an electric cooperative covering 365,000 meters and a 2,200-square-mile service territory, is pursuing similar work. MTE began with a focus on cybersecurity before pivoting to explore quantum optimization for the 82 or more batteries it expects to deploy. Brad Gibson, MTE's chief executive officer, described the effort as "a toe-in-the-water moment that can show the opportunities and risks of quantum and its potential impacts on affordability and reliability in a gated way."

  ## The scale of the commercial opportunity

  McKinsey identified more than 300 organizations across medical technologies, pharmaceuticals, financial services, energy, and materials science — including Airbus, E.ON, and JPMorgan Chase — already collaborating with quantum technology companies. Quantum computing companies generated more than $1 billion in revenue worldwide in 2025, a figure McKinsey projects could reach $4.4 billion by 2028. The total estimated economic value from quantum computing is up to $2.7 trillion in the 2030s, McKinsey said, and companies building capabilities and testing use cases now are positioned to capture a share of that value.

  Jeremy Renshaw, director of open power AI and quantum at the Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI), said quantum computing harnesses quantum mechanics to process information in ways classical computers cannot, adding new capabilities that complement rather than replace conventional computing. For a subset of problems — including large-scale optimization and materials science — quantum systems offer "polynomial or even exponential speedup" over classical computers, Renshaw said. "Longer term, quantum computing will be the go-to resource for solving large scale problems," he added.

  For utilities, the optimization use cases align directly with core operating challenges. Isuru Wijesundara, lead enterprise architect for energy strategy and advanced computing at Duke Energy, said utilities "routinely balance thousands of interconnected variables involving generation, transmission, demand, weather, regulatory constraints and customer impacts," and that applying quantum computing to such calculations "would create important value." Duke is working with IBM Quantum and other partners to build "foundational quantum literacy" as the technology matures, Wijesundara said.

  ## What quantum computing's load will mean for the grid

  Whether quantum computing will itself add materially to electricity demand is an open question among industry experts, and one the power sector is watching closely given its experience with AI.

  Aparna Prabhakar, chief strategy and sustainability officer for energy management at power sector hardware and software provider Schneider Electric, said quantum data centers carry a load profile unlike anything utilities have previously planned for. The technology requires continuous cryogenic cooling — maintaining certain components at near absolute zero — plus additional incremental workflow loads layered on top, she said. Prabhakar characterized the resulting reliability and power quality requirements as "at least as demanding as conventional data center loads" and said utilities will need to "build smarter infrastructure." Software-defined power systems that allow utilities to see, orchestrate, and manage the load in real time will be necessary, she said, because simply adding substations will not address the new load profile.

  Renshaw offered a different assessment. Quantum computing's processor power consumption is "comparatively small for those large-scale computations," he said. Even systems that require roughly 15 kilowatts per hour for cryogenic cooling — a figure associated with certain quantum computing modalities — consume "extremely small" energy compared to the gigawatt-scale power demands of AI model training data centers, Renshaw said.

  Despite the disagreement on load magnitude, both Prabhakar and Renshaw said utilities should be engaged in setting standards for quantum computing early, before other industries shape those standards to their own operating requirements.

  Constellation Energy spokesperson David Snyder separately told Utility Dive that quantum computing systems are projected to require relatively modest amounts of power and are "unlikely to materially affect power demand in the foreseeable future."

  ## Major infrastructure investments underway

  Commonwealth Edison (ComEd), the Illinois utility, announced in 2025 that it is providing power and grid infrastructure for the Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park, a 128-acre campus on the former U.S. Steel South Works site on Chicago's South Side. The park is managed by a research organization owned by the board of trustees of the University of Illinois.

  The anchor tenant, PsiQuantum, plans to house what the announcement described as the first utility-scale, error-corrected quantum computer at the facility. IBM and quantum technology company Infleqtion are also listed as tenants. ComEd said it is delivering "the advanced energy infrastructure needed to support utility-scale quantum computing, including cryogenic cooling systems and grid enhancements."

  Constellation Energy is supporting research at the park focused on quantum computing applications in the energy sector, including nuclear reactor fuel assembly optimization, according to an April statement.

  ## What the sector is watching

  The utility sector's stated goal in engaging with quantum computing now is to avoid a repeat of its experience with AI, where electricity demand from data centers grew faster than grid planners had modeled. Industry participants have framed early pilot work as a way to understand quantum's load characteristics and operational requirements before the technology scales.

  McKinsey's analysis indicates the commercial phase is already underway: more than 300 companies are testing quantum applications, revenue exceeded $1 billion in 2025, and investment growth has accelerated sharply. EPB's planned installation of a 36-logical-qubit quantum computer later this year will be among the first operational deployments by a U.S. electric utility.
body_zh: |
  美國各地電力公司正紛紛部署量子運算試驗並資助研究合作，希望比當初面對人工智慧時更早切入這項技術——AI突如其來的資料中心用電需求成長，當年幾乎令整個電力業措手不及。量子運算新創投資在2025年達到126億美元，為2024年的六倍；麥肯錫在四月發布的市場報告中，形容這項技術已觸及「商業引爆點」。

  EPB是服務田納西州查塔努加市的市立電力公司，自2023年起便與量子運算公司IonQ、橡樹嶺國家實驗室及田納西大學合作，共同營運一座量子通訊網路。據EPB量子系統暨策略推展部門主任派翠克・史溫格（Patrick Swingle）表示，該公司計畫在今年稍晚安裝一台具備36個邏輯量子位元的量子電腦。EPB初期將在輝達CUDA-Q平台所建構的量子—古典混合架構上運行這套系統；該平台設計用以將工作負載分配至量子處理器（QPU）、繪圖處理器（GPU）及傳統中央處理器（CPU）上執行。

  史溫格指出，這套系統的首要應用將是最佳化分布於饋線上的150組電池調度，這些饋線的服務範圍涵蓋20萬個電表及112座變電站。EPB計畫利用這套系統，根據供需及尖峰負載預測來最佳化電池充放電時程，並為未來因負載與天氣變化而進行的即時調度預做準備。

  中田納西電力（MTE）是一家電力合作社，服務範圍涵蓋36萬5千個電表及2,200平方英里的供電區域，目前也在推動類似的研究工作。MTE起初以資安為工作重點，後來轉而探索量子最佳化技術，以應用於其預計部署的82組以上電池。MTE執行長布拉德・吉布森（Brad Gibson）將這項工作形容為「試水溫的一刻，能以循序漸進、可管控的方式呈現量子技術的機遇與風險，以及它對電費可負擔性與供電可靠度的潛在影響」。

  ## 商業機遇的規模

  麥肯錫點名逾300個組織，橫跨醫療科技、製藥、金融服務、能源及材料科學等領域——包括空中巴士（Airbus）、E.ON及摩根大通（JPMorgan Chase）——已在與量子科技公司展開合作。量子運算公司在2025年的全球營收超過10億美元，麥肯錫預測這一數字到2028年可達44億美元。麥肯錫指出，量子運算在2030年代可創造的總經濟價值估計高達2.7兆美元，而現在就著手建立能力、測試應用場景的企業，將有機會分得其中一杯羹。

  電力研究院（EPRI）開放電力AI與量子部門主任傑瑞米・倫肖（Jeremy Renshaw）表示，量子運算利用量子力學以傳統電腦無法做到的方式處理資訊，為傳統運算帶來的是補充而非取代的新能力。倫肖指出，針對特定類型的問題——包括大規模最佳化及材料科學——量子系統能比傳統電腦提供「多項式甚至指數級的速度提升」。他補充：「長遠來看，量子運算將成為解決大規模問題的首選工具。」

  對電力公司而言，最佳化應用場景與其核心營運挑戰高度契合。杜克能源（Duke Energy）能源策略暨先進運算首席企業架構師伊蘇魯・維傑蘇達拉（Isuru Wijesundara）表示，電力公司「例行性地需要平衡數千個相互關聯的變數，涉及發電、輸電、需求、天氣、法規限制及客戶影響」，而將量子運算應用於這類計算「將創造重要的價值」。維傑蘇達拉表示，隨著技術持續成熟，杜克能源正與IBM Quantum及其他合作夥伴協力建立「量子基礎素養」。

  ## 量子運算對電網的用電衝擊

  量子運算本身是否會實質增加用電需求，在業界專家之間仍是一個懸而未決的問題；有鑑於AI的前車之鑑，電力業對此高度關注。

  電力業硬軟體供應商施耐德電氣（Schneider Electric）能源管理策略暨永續長阿帕娜・普拉巴卡（Aparna Prabhakar）表示，量子資料中心的用電輪廓與電力公司過往所規劃的任何設施都截然不同。她指出，這項技術需要持續的低溫冷卻——將特定元件維持在接近絕對零度——加上額外疊加的漸進式工作負載。普拉巴卡形容由此產生的可靠度與電力品質要求「至少與傳統資料中心負載一樣嚴苛」，並表示電力公司將需要「建立更智慧的基礎設施」。她強調，能讓電力公司即時監看、統籌調度及管理負載的軟體定義電力系統將不可或缺，因為單純增設變電站無法因應這種全新的負載輪廓。

  倫肖則提出不同的評估。他表示，量子運算的處理器耗電量「相較於這些大規模計算而言相當小」。倫肖指出，即便是某些量子運算模式中需要約每小時15千瓦用於低溫冷卻的系統，與AI模型訓練資料中心動輒以吉瓦計算的龐大用電需求相比，其能耗仍屬「極為微小」。

  儘管雙方對負載規模的看法存在分歧，普拉巴卡與倫肖均表示，電力公司應及早參與量子運算標準的制定，以免其他產業依自身營運需求主導標準走向。

  星座能源（Constellation Energy）發言人大衛・史奈德（David Snyder）另行向《Utility Dive》表示，量子運算系統預計所需電力相對有限，「在可預見的未來不太可能實質影響電力需求」。

  ## 大型基礎建設投資持續推進

  伊利諾州電力公司聯邦愛迪生（ComEd）於2025年宣布，將為伊利諾量子暨微電子園區（Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park）提供電力及電網基礎建設；該園區佔地128英畝，坐落於芝加哥南區前美國鋼鐵南廠舊址。園區由伊利諾大學董事會旗下的研究機構負責管理。

  主力進駐廠商PsiQuantum計畫在園區內設置一台公告所描述的「首台公用規模、具錯誤修正能力的量子電腦」。IBM及量子科技公司Infleqtion亦列名為進駐廠商。ComEd表示，將提供「支持公用規模量子運算所需的先進能源基礎設施，包括低溫冷卻系統及電網強化設施」。

  根據四月份的聲明，星座能源正在支持園區內的研究，聚焦於量子運算在能源領域的應用，包括核反應爐燃料組件最佳化。

  ## 業界的觀察重點

  電力業現階段介入量子運算的明確目標，是避免重蹈AI的覆轍——當年資料中心的用電需求成長速度，遠超過電網規劃人員的預測模型。業界人士將早期試驗工作定位為一種途徑，用以在技術大規模普及之前，先行瞭解量子技術的負載特性及營運需求。

  麥肯錫的分析顯示，商業化階段已然展開：逾300家企業正在測試量子應用，2025年營收突破10億美元，投資成長更是急速加速。EPB計畫於今年稍晚安裝36個邏輯量子位元的量子電腦，將成為美國電力公司中首批正式部署量子運算的案例之一。
