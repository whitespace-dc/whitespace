body_en: |
  Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (TSMC), Asia's largest company by market value, reported May revenue of NT$416.98 billion (US$13.2 billion), a 30% increase from the same month a year earlier, reflecting sustained demand for the advanced chips that power artificial intelligence (AI) systems.

  Combined April and May sales were up approximately 24% year-on-year, according to Bloomberg calculations. Analysts are forecasting a 35% rise in second-quarter sales overall.

  ## AI infrastructure spending underpins demand

  TSMC sits at the center of the global AI buildout by manufacturing cutting-edge semiconductors for chipmakers including Nvidia Corp and Advanced Micro Devices Inc (AMD). That positioning has become increasingly valuable as major technology companies scale their AI infrastructure budgets. Alphabet, Amazon.com, Meta Platforms, and Microsoft are collectively preparing to spend US$725 billion on AI-related investments in 2026, significantly more than previously anticipated, according to the source report.

  TSMC's chief executive officer CC Wei told shareholders earlier this month that global chip supply will fall short of demand for years to come. His comments followed Nvidia chief Jensen Huang's statement that his company remains supply constrained, reinforcing the picture of a market where capacity cannot keep pace with orders.

  ## Guidance raised, capital spending at upper range

  In April, TSMC raised its full-year sales guidance and said capital expenditure for 2026 should trend toward the upper end of an existing forecast range of as much as US$56 billion. Capital expenditure at that scale reflects the long lead times required to build and equip semiconductor fabrication plants capable of producing the most advanced chips.

  ## Consumer electronics exposure a counterweight

  TSMC also supplies chips to smartphone and consumer electronics manufacturers, a segment facing separate headwinds. Those customers are contending with soaring memory chip costs and weaker consumer sentiment tied to rising costs of living, factors that could temper growth in non-AI product lines.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The May figures confirm that AI-driven chip demand remains strong enough to sustain TSMC's growth trajectory even as parts of the broader semiconductor market face pressure. With CEO Wei warning of a prolonged supply shortfall and capital spending guided to its highest end, TSMC's forward outlook is shaped primarily by how quickly it can expand capacity to meet AI customers' orders.

  ## 台積電5月營收年增30%　AI晶片需求持續超出供給

  亞洲市值最大企業、台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司（台積電）公布，5月合併營收達新台幣4,169.8億元（約133億美元），較去年同期成長30%，反映出為人工智慧（AI）系統提供動力的先進晶片需求持續旺盛。

  根據彭博社的計算，台積電4月與5月合計營收較去年同期成長約24%。分析師預估，台積電第二季整體營收將年增35%。

  ## AI基礎建設支出撐起需求

  台積電是全球AI建設浪潮的核心製造商，專責生產供應輝達（Nvidia）、超微（AMD）等晶片大廠所需的頂尖半導體。隨著大型科技公司大幅擴充AI基礎建設預算，台積電的關鍵地位愈發凸顯。根據原報導，Alphabet、亞馬遜、Meta以及微軟等四家科技巨頭，預計2026年在AI相關投資上合計將支出7,250億美元，遠超先前市場預期。

  台積電董事長暨執行長魏哲家本月稍早在股東會上表示，全球晶片供給在未來數年內都將持續低於需求。此番談話呼應了輝達執行長黃仁勳日前的說法——黃仁勳表示輝達仍受限於供給瓶頸，進一步印證了市場產能追趕不上訂單的整體態勢。

  ## 上調全年展望，資本支出趨向預測區間上緣

  台積電於4月上調全年營收展望，並表示2026年資本支出將趨向原預測區間的上緣，該區間上限為560億美元。如此規模的資本支出，反映出興建與裝備能夠生產最先進晶片之半導體晶圓廠所需的漫長前置期。

  ## 消費性電子曝險成為抵消因素

  台積電同時也供應晶片給智慧型手機及消費性電子製造商，但這個市場區塊正面臨不同的逆風壓力。相關客戶正應對記憶體晶片成本飆升，以及因生活費用上漲所導致的消費者信心疲軟，這些因素可能抑制非AI產品線的業績成長。

  ## 產業意涵

  5月營收數字證實，AI驅動的晶片需求依然強勁，足以支撐台積電的成長軌跡，即使整體半導體市場部分領域正承受壓力。在魏哲家警告供給缺口將長期存在、資本支出也被引導至最高端的情況下，台積電的前景展望，主要取決於其能以多快的速度擴充產能，以滿足AI客戶的訂單需求。
