body_en: |
  Intel Foundry and Fortinet have announced a partnership to develop the Fortinet Security Processor 6 (SP6), a custom application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) designed for network security processing. The deal represents a publicly disclosed customer win for Intel's contract chip manufacturing business outside the artificial intelligence sector.

  ## What Intel is contributing

  Intel is providing advanced chip design, packaging, and manufacturing capabilities for the SP6. Fortinet is contributing the security processor expertise. No performance specifications for the SP6 have been disclosed.

  ## Fortinet's history with custom silicon

  Fortinet has designed its own ASICs for years. Its previous generation, the FortiSP5, was manufactured on TSMC's 7-nanometer process node, according to ServeTheHome. Earlier Fortinet products, including the FortiGate FG-40F and FG-60F, used the FortiSOC4, a custom system-on-chip visible inside those units. The FortiSP5 was benchmarked by Fortinet in direct comparison to what were described as industry-standard central processing units (CPUs), likely Intel Xeon D or Atom processors at the time of that chip's launch.

  ## Why the win matters for Intel Foundry

  The deal is notable on two counts. First, Intel is taking a customer that previously manufactured with TSMC, the dominant Taiwan-based contract chipmaker. Second, Fortinet's security processors operate in a product category that overlaps tangentially with Intel's own x86 networking and security processor lineup, making this a win that crosses into adjacent competitive territory for Intel's chip products division.

  Fortinet's business is predominantly software and services, with roughly 70 percent of revenue from services and 30 percent from hardware, according to ServeTheHome. Of that hardware revenue, the security ASIC is only one component. With Fortinet's trailing twelve-month revenue at approximately $7 billion, the hardware segment represents around $2 billion, and the ASIC is a portion of that figure. ServeTheHome characterized the win as a component of a portion of a roughly $2 billion annual revenue stream rather than a multibillion-dollar manufacturing contract in its own right.

  ## Significance for Intel's foundry strategy

  Intel Foundry has been working to build a customer base to compete with TSMC and Samsung in the contract manufacturing market. Publicly disclosed design wins are relatively rare for the business, making the Fortinet announcement a visible data point for Intel's efforts to attract fabless and fab-lite customers. The SP6 agreement shows Intel securing a customer in the networking and security silicon segment, distinct from the AI accelerator deals that have drawn most industry attention to foundry competition.

  No timeline for the SP6's production or commercial availability has been announced.
body_zh: |
  Intel晶圓代工與Fortinet宣布合作，共同開發Fortinet安全處理器6（SP6）——一款專為網路安全處理設計的客製特殊應用積體電路（ASIC）。這筆交易是Intel合約晶片製造業務在人工智慧領域以外的一項公開揭露客戶勝利。

  ## Intel的貢獻

  Intel為SP6提供先進的晶片設計、封裝與製造能力，Fortinet則貢獻安全處理器的專業技術。目前尚未揭露SP6的任何效能規格。

  ## Fortinet客製矽晶片的歷史

  Fortinet多年來一直自行設計ASIC。根據ServeTheHome的資料，其前一代產品FortiSP5採用台積電7奈米製程節點製造。早期的Fortinet產品，包括FortiGate FG-40F與FG-60F，搭載FortiSOC4——一款在這些設備內部可見的客製系統單晶片。Fortinet曾對FortiSP5進行效能基準測試，將其直接與所謂業界標準的中央處理器（CPU）相互比較；在該晶片推出之際，這些CPU可能是Intel的Xeon D或Atom處理器。

  ## 為何這對Intel晶圓代工意義重大

  這筆交易在兩個層面上值得關注。其一，Intel從台積電手中爭取到了一名客戶——台積電是以台灣為據點的主要晶圓代工廠商。其二，Fortinet安全處理器所屬的產品類別，與Intel自家x86網路及安全處理器產品線之間存在間接重疊，使這項勝利跨入了Intel晶片產品部門的相鄰競爭領域。

  根據ServeTheHome的資料，Fortinet的業務以軟體與服務為主，約70%的營收來自服務，30%來自硬體。在硬體營收中，安全ASIC只是組成部分之一。Fortinet過去十二個月的營收約達70億美元，其中硬體部分約占20億美元，而ASIC僅為其中一部分。ServeTheHome將此次勝利定性為約20億美元年營收流中某一部分的組成項目，而非一筆本身即達數十億美元規模的製造合約。

  ## 對Intel晶圓代工策略的意義

  Intel晶圓代工一直致力於建立客戶群，以在晶圓代工市場上與台積電和三星一較高下。公開揭露的設計勝利對該業務而言相對罕見，因此Fortinet的公告成為Intel吸引無晶圓廠及輕晶圓廠客戶努力中，一個具有高能見度的數據點。SP6協議顯示，Intel在網路與安全矽晶片領域攻下一名客戶，與吸引業界大多數目光的AI加速器晶圓代工競爭有所不同，別具意義。

  目前尚未公布SP6的生產時程或商業上市時間表。
