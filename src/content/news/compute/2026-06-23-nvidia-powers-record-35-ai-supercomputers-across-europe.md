body_en: |
  NVIDIA on Monday announced that Europe is developing a record 35 of the company's high-performance computing (HPC) supercomputers, a buildout the chipmaker said will provide next-generation infrastructure to more than 3 million researchers across the continent.

  The systems are intended to advance AI adoption in Europe, accelerate scientific research, and drive industrial innovation, NVIDIA said.

  ## Platforms and compute capacity

  The supercomputers are built on what NVIDIA described as its full-stack infrastructure. Most of the compute capacity powering Europe's AI factories comes from NVIDIA's Blackwell and Hopper platforms, the company said.

  NVIDIA said the systems will support research across five stated domains: climate science, healthcare, clean energy, quantum computing, and basic sciences.

  ## Named deployments

  The expansion encompasses several identified projects:

  - The Barcelona Supercomputing Center's AI Factory
  - BavariaAI's Blue Swan
  - IT4LIA
  - HLRS's HammerHAI
  - NAISS's Mimer AI Factory

  ## Halos for Robotics

  Alongside the supercomputer announcement, NVIDIA introduced Halos for Robotics, which it described as the industry's first full-stack safety system for robotics and physical AI. The system is designed to provide a safety framework for machines that perceive, decide, and act in the physical world, NVIDIA said.

  ## 輝達宣布歐洲創紀錄打造35座AI超級電腦

  輝達週一宣布，歐洲正在建置創紀錄的35座高效能運算（HPC）超級電腦，這批設施將為遍布歐洲大陸的逾300萬名研究人員提供次世代基礎建設。

  輝達表示，這些系統旨在推動AI在歐洲的普及應用、加速科學研究，並帶動產業創新。

  ## 平台與運算能量

  這批超級電腦採用輝達所稱的全堆疊基礎建設架構。輝達指出，驅動歐洲AI工廠的大部分運算能量，均來自輝達的Blackwell與Hopper平台。

  輝達表示，這些系統將支援五大研究領域：氣候科學、醫療保健、潔淨能源、量子運算，以及基礎科學。

  ## 已命名的建置項目

  此次擴建涵蓋數個已公開命名的專案：

  - 巴塞隆納超級運算中心的AI工廠
  - BavariaAI的Blue Swan
  - IT4LIA
  - HLRS的HammerHAI
  - NAISS的Mimer AI工廠

  ## 機器人安全系統Halos

  在超級電腦公告的同時，輝達也發表了機器人安全系統「Halos for Robotics」，輝達將其定位為業界首套針對機器人與實體AI的全堆疊安全系統。輝達表示，該系統旨在為能夠感知、判斷並在實體世界中採取行動的機器提供完整的安全框架。
