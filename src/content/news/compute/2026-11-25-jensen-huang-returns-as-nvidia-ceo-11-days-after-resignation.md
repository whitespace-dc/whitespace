body_en: |
  Nvidia announced on November 23, 2026 that founder Jensen Huang had returned as chief executive officer, eleven days after his resignation, according to Nikkei xTECH. The speed of the reversal — described by the publication as a sudden, surprise comeback — underscored the degree to which the company and the broader technology industry have come to depend on a single individual.

  ## No alternative, analysts say

  Analysts quoted by Nikkei xTECH concluded that recalling Huang was the only viable path available to Nvidia's board. "There was no other option but to call him back," the publication paraphrased their view. The outlet characterized the episode as making visible — at Nvidia and across the tech sector — the extent of reliance on Huang personally.

  ## What the episode reveals

  Nikkei xTECH framed the sequence of events as a demonstration of what it called "charisma dependence" — a structural vulnerability in which a company's perceived value and direction are inseparable from one founder's presence. The publication reported that this dynamic extends beyond Nvidia to the technology industry more broadly.

  Nikkei xTECH noted that additional details about the circumstances of Huang's return, including reported involvement by external political figures in the personnel decision, were covered in a continuation of the article not available in the public preview.
