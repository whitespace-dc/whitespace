body_en: |
  Qisda (2352.TW) said Monday it will begin small-volume shipments of AI servers and 1.6T high-speed switches before year-end, targeting a volume production ramp in 2027, as Chairman Chen Qi-hong told an investor conference that a potential post-election slowdown in U.S. data center construction would relieve component supply pressure rather than harm the company.

  The company reported third-quarter consolidated revenue of NT$56.76 billion, up 8% year-on-year and 8% quarter-on-quarter. Operating gross margin was 17.3%, sustaining the company's stated threshold of above 17%. Net income attributable to the parent company rose 118% year-on-year to NT$548 million, with earnings per share of NT$0.35.

  For the first nine months of 2026, Qisda posted consolidated revenue of NT$161 billion, up 3% year-on-year. Nine-month operating gross margin reached 17.1%, up 0.3 percentage points from the same period in 2025. Nine-month net income attributable to the parent company rose 34% to NT$1.46 billion, delivering EPS of NT$0.92.

  ## AI product roadmap and Q4 outlook

  Chief Executive Officer Ke Shu-fen said the company's high-value-added business unit posted quarterly revenue above NT$30 billion in Q3, a four-year high. The intelligent solutions segment exceeded NT$10 billion in quarterly revenue for the third consecutive quarter and grew 22% year-on-year through the first three quarters. The company said Q4 is expected to outperform Q3.

  Qisda has divided its AI-related work into four business areas: AI vision and display, AI infrastructure, AI solutions and smart manufacturing, and AI healthcare. The AI infrastructure category encompasses AI servers, 1.6T (1.6-terabit-per-second) high-speed switches, advanced liquid cooling, and integrated full-cabinet solutions. Ke said the 1.6T switches are targeted at North American data centers. Medical display, smart fish-detection, and semiconductor materials product lines are also progressing through certification, volume production, and initial shipment stages, the company said.

  ## Post-election data center slowdown

  Market concern has centered on whether the November U.S. midterm elections could disrupt AI infrastructure spending. Chen said some impact is likely, particularly in states where the Democratic Party holds a majority, where he expects stricter restrictions on data center development. He characterized a moderate construction slowdown as a positive development, however.

  Chen explained that Taiwanese manufacturers have accumulated large data center orders they have been unable to execute because of rising DRAM prices and broader component shortages. A temporary reduction in build pace would give the supply chain time to recover and return to what he called a healthier supply-demand balance, he said.

  ## Broader economic and strategic outlook

  Chen expressed optimism about Taiwan's economy in Q4 2026 and through 2027, citing forecasts from Taiwan's Directorate-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics and the International Monetary Fund projecting steady continued growth. He said those conditions support a strong overall technology industry.

  The company described its "fleet strategy" — the cross-subsidiary integration of technology, manufacturing, and market resources — as shifting Qisda from single-product sales toward system integration and multi-sector applications.

  Qisda said AI server and 1.6T switch shipments to North American data centers are expected to scale through 2027 as those product lines move out of their initial small-volume phase.

  佳世達（2352）週一表示，將於2026年底前啟動AI伺服器及1.6T高速交換器小量出貨，目標於2027年進入量產階段。董事長陳其宏在法說會上向投資人表示，美國大選後若出現資料中心建設放緩，對台灣製造商而言反而是正面發展，有助舒緩零組件供應吃緊的壓力。

  公司公布第三季合併營收為新台幣567.6億元，年增8%、季增8%。營業毛利率為17.3%，維持在公司宣示的17%以上門檻。歸屬母公司之淨利年增118%至新台幣5.48億元，每股盈餘為0.35元。

  2026年前三季，佳世達合併營收達新台幣1,610億元，年增3%。前三季營業毛利率為17.1%，較2025年同期成長0.3個百分點。前三季歸屬母公司之淨利年增34%至新台幣14.6億元，每股盈餘為0.92元。

  ## AI產品藍圖與第四季展望

  執行長柯淑芬表示，公司高附加價值事業群第三季單季營收突破新台幣300億元，創四年來新高。智慧解決方案業務連續第三季單季營收超過100億元，前三季年增22%。公司預估第四季表現將優於第三季。

  佳世達將旗下AI相關業務劃分為四大領域：AI視覺與顯示、AI基礎設施、AI解決方案與智慧製造，以及AI醫療照護。AI基礎設施類別涵蓋AI伺服器、1.6T（每秒1.6兆位元）高速交換器、先進液冷系統，以及整合式全機櫃解決方案。柯淑芬表示，1.6T交換器鎖定北美資料中心市場。醫療顯示器、智慧魚類辨識及半導體材料等產品線，目前分別處於認證、量產及初始出貨等不同進度階段。

  ## 大選後資料中心建設放緩

  市場關注11月美國期中選舉是否可能衝擊AI基礎設施資本支出。陳其宏表示，部分衝擊在所難免，尤其是民主黨執政的州，預計將對資料中心開發採取更嚴格的限制。不過，他將溫和的建設放緩定性為正面發展。

  陳其宏解釋，台灣製造商手中積累了大量資料中心訂單，卻因DRAM價格上漲及整體零組件短缺而遲遲無法順利執行。建設步調若能暫時放緩，將給予供應鏈喘息空間，使供需關係回歸他所稱的更健康狀態。

  ## 整體經濟與策略展望

  陳其宏對台灣第四季及2027年的經濟前景抱持樂觀態度，援引行政院主計總處及國際貨幣基金（IMF）的預測，認為穩健成長態勢將持續延伸，有利整體科技產業表現。

  公司說明其「艦隊策略」——透過跨子公司整合技術、製造及市場資源——正引領佳世達從單一產品銷售模式轉型為系統整合與跨領域應用。

  佳世達表示，AI伺服器及1.6T交換器對北美資料中心的出貨規模，預計將隨產品線脫離初期小量出貨階段，於2027年持續擴大。
