body_en: |
  Dell has expanded its AI Factory with NVIDIA with new server hardware, deskside systems, validated software, a layered security architecture, and managed services, the company said, targeting enterprises that want to run autonomous AI agents on infrastructure they own rather than through public cloud services. The program's customer base has reached more than 6,500 organizations, up from 5,000 the prior quarter.

  The expansion targets businesses moving beyond AI pilot projects. Dell cited forecasts that agentic AI will become the largest enterprise workload by 2028 and said token consumption is expected to rise sharply while inference costs fall through the rest of the decade.

  Unclear return on investment remains the primary obstacle for 59 percent of enterprise AI roll-outs, Dell said. The company argued that owning AI infrastructure gives customers more control over spending, data, and operations than relying on public cloud-only models.

  ## New validated software

  The updated platform adds several software offerings validated for the Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA. These include Reflection's Beam open-weight model, Mistral-based tools, Dynatrace for AI observability, Fogsphere for computer vision, and SUSE Linux as an operating system option.

  A new security architecture, built on Red Hat AI Factory software and the NVIDIA OpenShell Reference Architecture, is designed to protect runtime, network, data, and infrastructure layers for organizations running autonomous AI agents at scale.

  Dell Managed Services for AI, a new managed offering, is intended to help customers allocate computing resources, control access, and track AI consumption and costs as deployments move beyond the pilot stage.

  ## Deskside AI expansion

  Dell is extending its deskside AI lineup with validated deployment templates covering configurations from a single developer system to more than 1,000 systems across an enterprise. The templates specify hardware, models, and software stacks for use cases including agentic coding, agentic VSS, Hermes Agent on NemoClaw, and higher education AI lab environments.

  Two new hardware options support the deskside expansion:

  - **Dell Pro Precision with GB10** (64GB unified memory): Available for individual developers and small teams; can be linked in two- or four-machine clusters for larger models and more complex workflows.
  - **Dell Pro Precision with GB300 on Windows**: Provides up to 748GB of coherent memory and 20 petaflops of FP4 compute for deskside deployments managed by enterprise IT teams.

  ## Data center hardware

  In the data center, Dell detailed a modular data center reference design using the NVIDIA DSX POD, which packages compute, storage, networking, rack-level power, and liquid cooling in a containerized unit intended for large-scale AI deployments.

  Two new PowerEdge server lines address different parts of the agentic AI compute stack:

  - **PowerEdge R8822**: Fits standard 19-inch rack environments and supports agent orchestration, sandboxing, reinforcement learning, data processing, and analytics, with support for up to four double-wide GPUs.
  - **PowerEdge M9882**: Handles CPU-based agentic workloads (orchestration, tool use, code execution, and evaluation) alongside separate GPU clusters for inference. Dell said separating CPU and GPU compute lets operators manage power and cooling more efficiently.

  For inference, the PowerEdge XE9810 will be Dell's first rack-scale system using Groq 3 language processing units (LPUs), a chip type designed for inference workloads. The liquid-cooled system is designed for production inference and can operate alongside existing GPU clusters.

  ## Telecom integration

  The expansion extends to telecommunications infrastructure. NVIDIA Aerial RAN Computer on Dell PowerEdge servers will allow communications service providers to consolidate radio access network (RAN) workloads and AI workloads onto a single shared compute platform rather than running them on separate systems.

  ## Industry context

  "Enterprises used to think about AI infrastructure as an IT budget question, but that's changed. AI infrastructure is no longer a cost center. It's a value creation center," said Michael Dell, chief executive officer of Dell. "Together with NVIDIA, we are giving enterprises the integrated platforms they need to move agentic AI from experimentation to results, on infrastructure they own and control."

  Industry observers said the latest move reflects a broader shift in customer priorities. Buyers that spent the past two years testing AI tools are now under pressure to show measurable returns and decide whether those systems should sit in the cloud, on-premises, or across both.

  An observer identified only as Kimball said the market is "entering a new phase where customers are focused less on building AI infrastructure and more on generating measurable business outcomes," describing the expansion as "a mature approach that combines validated solutions, ecosystem integration, automation and services, helping organizations move from pilots to production with greater confidence and less risk."

  Dell cited forecasts that agentic AI will become the largest enterprise workload by 2028.

  此次擴充鎖定正準備跨越AI概念驗證階段的企業。戴爾引述市場預測指出，自主AI將於2028年成為企業最大規模的工作負載，同時預期在本十年剩餘時間內，Token消耗量將大幅攀升，而推論成本則持續下滑。

  戴爾表示，投資報酬率不明確仍是59%企業AI部署案的首要障礙。該公司主張，擁有AI基礎架構能讓客戶對支出、資料及營運取得更大掌控權，優於純粹仰賴公有雲模式。

  ## 新增驗證軟體

  更新後的平台新增多項通過「Dell AI Factory with NVIDIA」驗證的軟體產品，包括：Reflection的Beam開放權重模型、基於Mistral的工具套件、Dynatrace AI可觀測性工具、Fogsphere電腦視覺解決方案，以及SUSE Linux作業系統選項。

  全新安全架構以Red Hat AI Factory軟體及NVIDIA OpenShell參考架構為基礎，旨在保護大規模執行自主AI代理的企業組織在執行期（runtime）、網路、資料及基礎架構各層面的安全。

  Dell Managed Services for AI是一項新推出的託管服務方案，協助客戶在部署規模超越概念驗證階段後，有效分配運算資源、管控存取權限，並追蹤AI使用量與成本。

  ## 桌側AI擴展

  戴爾正擴充其桌側AI產品線，提供涵蓋各種規模的驗證部署範本，從單一開發者系統到企業跨站超過1,000台設備皆有對應配置。這些範本詳細規範了適用於自主程式設計（agentic coding）、自主VSS、Hermes Agent on NemoClaw及高等教育AI實驗室環境等應用情境所需的硬體、模型與軟體堆疊。

  兩款新硬體選項支援此次桌側擴展：

  - **Dell Pro Precision（搭載GB10）**（64GB統一記憶體）：適用於個人開發者及小型團隊；可透過雙機或四機叢集串接，以支援更大型模型及更複雜的工作流程。
  - **Dell Pro Precision（搭載GB300，Windows版）**：提供最高748GB一致性記憶體及每秒20 petaflops的FP4運算效能，適用於由企業IT團隊統一管理的桌側部署。

  ## 資料中心硬體

  在資料中心方面，戴爾詳述了採用NVIDIA DSX POD的模組化資料中心參考設計。該POD將運算、儲存、網路、機架級電力供應及液冷系統整合於容器化單元中，專為大規模AI部署而設計。

  兩款新PowerEdge伺服器系列分別應對自主AI運算堆疊的不同環節：

  - **PowerEdge R8822**：適配標準19吋機架環境，支援代理協作（agent orchestration）、沙箱隔離（sandboxing）、強化學習、資料處理與分析，最多可支援四個雙寬GPU。
  - **PowerEdge M9882**：負責以CPU為主的自主AI工作負載（包括協作、工具呼叫、程式碼執行及評估），並搭配獨立GPU叢集執行推論任務。戴爾表示，CPU與GPU運算分離的架構，有助於讓維運人員更有效率地管理電力與散熱。

  在推論方面，PowerEdge XE9810將成為戴爾首款採用Groq 3語言處理單元（LPU）的機架級系統——LPU是一種專為推論工作負載設計的晶片類型。這款液冷系統專為生產環境推論設計，可與現有GPU叢集並行運作。

  ## 電信整合

  此次擴充亦延伸至電信基礎架構領域。NVIDIA Aerial RAN Computer部署於Dell PowerEdge伺服器上，將使通訊服務提供商能夠把無線接取網路（RAN）工作負載與AI工作負載整合至同一共用運算平台，無需分別以獨立系統運行。

  ## 產業背景

  戴爾科技執行長麥可．戴爾（Michael Dell）表示：「企業過去習慣將AI基礎架構視為IT預算問題，但這樣的觀念已經改變。AI基礎架構不再只是成本中心，而是價值創造中心。我們與輝達攜手，為企業提供所需的整合平台，協助他們在自有且可掌控的基礎架構上，將自主AI從實驗推進到實際成果。」

  業界觀察人士表示，此次動態反映了客戶優先考量的更廣泛轉變。過去兩年花時間測試AI工具的企業買家，如今正承受著展示具體投資回報的壓力，並需決定這些系統應部署於雲端、企業內部，還是橫跨兩者的混合架構。

  一位僅透露姓氏為Kimball的觀察人士表示，市場「正進入一個新階段，客戶關注的重點已從建置AI基礎架構，轉移至創造可衡量的商業成果」，並形容此次擴充是「一套成熟的做法，結合了驗證解決方案、生態系整合、自動化與服務，協助組織以更高的信心、更低的風險從概念驗證走向生產部署。」

  戴爾引述預測指出，自主AI將於2028年成為企業最大規模的工作負載。
