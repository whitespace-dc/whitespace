body_en: |
  The rise of autonomous AI agents is concentrating durable investment demand on compute infrastructure and optical-fiber interconnects, analysts at a Capital Securities forum said October 16, identifying a supply chain running from chip design software to optical transceiver modules as the primary beneficiary.

  Capital Securities (群益金鼎證券) convened the event, billed as the "AGI Winners Forum," to analyze the supply-chain shifts triggered by AI agent development. Huang Pengrui, a senior researcher at Capital Investment Advisory (群益投顧), told attendees that companies including OpenAI and Meta are actively developing AI agents—software that goes beyond generating text to understand stated goals, decompose tasks, integrate information, and execute work across multiple external tools.

  Meta's recently launched personal AI agent, Muse, converts a user's stated objective into an action plan and connects to external tools to carry it out, Huang noted, citing it as a concrete example of the capability shift underway.

  ## From Model Scale to Inference Efficiency

  Huang said the industry has moved away from its earlier focus on model size. With the turn toward agents, priority now falls on inference efficiency—the speed and cost of running a trained model—along with computational performance and energy efficiency. When AI agents must process complex tasks in real time, compute demand rises and systems must integrate heterogeneous resources: central processing units (CPUs), graphics processing units (GPUs), and application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), connected by high-speed links, he said.

  ## Infrastructure Demand More Stable Than Application Layer

  Huang argued that AI models and applications update rapidly and face intense competition, making their revenue trajectory less predictable. Continuously expanding compute infrastructure, by contrast, carries more clearly defined medium-to-long-term demand.

  He identified the relevant supply chain as spanning chip design software companies Synopsys and Cadence; chip architecture and intellectual property provider Arm; Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), which holds pricing advantages in advanced process manufacturing and packaging; and chipmakers NVIDIA, Broadcom, and AMD.

  ## Optical Links Emerge as a System Bottleneck

  Fuh Hwa Securities Investment Trust (復華投信) investment manager Hong Shiting, also presenting at the forum, said AI infrastructure competition has broadened from raw compute speed to data-transfer speed. Data volumes moving between GPUs, network switches, and servers are rising rapidly, lifting the strategic importance of high-speed interconnects, she said.

  Optical communications—which uses light to carry data, offering higher bandwidth and efficiency advantages over longer distances—is gaining ground in AI data centers as a result, according to Hong. Optical transceiver modules, which convert between electrical and optical signals at network interfaces, are being upgraded from 800 gigabits per second (800G) to 1.6 terabits per second (1.6T), with an evolution path toward 3.2T already in progress. That trajectory is driving demand for high-speed optical modules, optical components, and optical chips, she said.

  ## 5G Supply Chain Extends Into AI Data Centers

  Portions of the communications supply chain that previously benefited from fifth-generation (5G) wireless construction are now extending that momentum into AI data center buildouts, both analysts said. Products spanning high-speed optical modules, optical chips, servers, and high-speed transmission components stand to benefit from global AI infrastructure investment expansion.

  Some exchange-traded funds (ETFs) built around 5G and communications technology already hold optical-communications and AI-server companies in their portfolios, Hong noted, reflecting that the investment theme is shifting from 5G to AI data centers. Optical module specifications are set to continue advancing along the 800G-to-1.6T-to-3.2T upgrade curve as AI cluster deployments scale.
body_zh: |
  自主型AI代理人的崛起，正將長期投資需求集中至算力基礎建設與光纖互連，群益金鼎證券論壇分析師於十月十六日指出，並點名從晶片設計軟體到光收發模組的供應鏈為主要受益者。

  群益金鼎證券主辦本次「AGI贏家論壇」，聚焦分析AI代理人發展所帶動的供應鏈結構性轉變。群益投顧資深研究員黃鵬瑞向與會者表示，OpenAI、Meta等業者正積極開發AI代理人——這類軟體不僅止於生成文字，更能理解用戶目標、拆解任務、整合資訊，並跨多個外部工具執行工作。

  黃鵬瑞以Meta近期推出的個人AI代理人Muse為例，具體說明這項能力轉變的現況：Muse能將用戶陳述的目標轉化為行動計畫，並連接外部工具加以執行。

  ## 從模型規模轉向推論效率

  黃鵬瑞表示，產業重心已從早期對模型規模的追求逐漸轉移。隨著代理人時代來臨，當前優先考量轉為推論效率——即運行已訓練模型的速度與成本——以及運算效能與能源效率。他指出，當AI代理人必須即時處理複雜任務時，運算需求隨之攀升，系統也必須整合異質運算資源，包括中央處理器（CPU）、圖形處理器（GPU）與特殊應用積體電路（ASIC），並以高速連結加以串聯。

  ## 基礎建設需求較應用層更為穩定

  黃鵬瑞認為，AI模型與應用服務更新迭代速度快、競爭激烈，營收走勢較難預測；相較之下，持續擴張的算力基礎建設，其中長期需求輪廓更為清晰。

  他點名的相關供應鏈涵蓋：晶片設計軟體業者新思科技（Synopsys）與益華電腦（Cadence）；晶片架構與智慧財產權供應商安謀（Arm）；在先進製程製造與封裝上具備定價優勢的台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）；以及晶片製造商輝達（NVIDIA）、博通（Broadcom）與超微（AMD）。

  ## 光互連成為系統瓶頸

  同場與會的復華投信基金經理人洪詩婷表示，AI基礎建設的競爭已從純粹的運算速度，擴展至資料傳輸速度。GPU、網路交換器與伺服器之間流動的資料量急速成長，高速互連的策略重要性因而大幅提升。

  洪詩婷指出，以光傳輸資料的光通訊技術——具備更高頻寬，且在長距離傳輸上效率優於傳統銅線——正因此在AI資料中心中快速普及。負責在網路介面轉換電訊號與光訊號的光收發模組，目前正從每秒800G升級至每秒1.6T，並已規劃向3.2T演進的路徑。這條升級曲線正驅動對高速光模組、光學元件與光晶片的需求持續成長。

  ## 5G供應鏈延伸至AI資料中心

  兩位分析師均表示，過去受惠於第五代行動通訊（5G）建設的部分通訊供應鏈業者，如今正將這股成長動能延伸至AI資料中心建置。高速光模組、光晶片、伺服器及高速傳輸元件等產品，皆有望受惠於全球AI基礎建設投資規模的持續擴張。

  洪詩婷指出，部分以5G與通訊科技為主題的指數股票型基金（ETF）已將光通訊及AI伺服器相關業者納入持股，反映投資主軸正從5G逐步轉向AI資料中心。隨著AI叢集部署規模持續擴大，光模組規格將沿800G至1.6T再至3.2T的升級曲線持續演進。
