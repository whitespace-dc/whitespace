body_en: |
  A coalition of nine industry groups representing telecommunications, automotive, medical device, and retail sectors has urged the Trump administration to expand memory chip production after AI data center demand drove conventional DRAM contract prices up roughly 93 to 98 percent quarter over quarter in the first quarter of 2026, according to market research firm TrendForce.

  The groups delivered their warning in a June 3 letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent. Signatories include NCTA – The Internet & Television Association, ACA Connects – America's Communications Association, NTCA – The Rural Broadband Association, the Telecommunications Industry Association, AdvaMed, the Medical Device Manufacturers Association, the Alliance for Automotive Innovation, the Retail Industry Leaders Association, and the National Retail Federation.

  "Expanding artificial intelligence (AI) data centers consume an enormous share of available memory chip capacity," the coalition wrote. "The result has been an unprecedented surge in the price of memory chips and reduced supply of these chips for manufacturing and consumer-facing industries."

  ## Memory capacity shifting toward AI systems

  TrendForce reported that manufacturers are directing production toward high-bandwidth memory (HBM) — a specialized, high-speed memory type used in AI accelerator chips — and server-related products, reducing the volume available for other industries. HBM commands higher margins than conventional dynamic random-access memory (DRAM), the chip type used across a broad range of electronics from vehicles to consumer devices.

  Stephen Sopko, semiconductor and deep tech analyst at HyperFrame Research, said the constraints are no longer contained within AI infrastructure. "Wider DRAM constraints are cropping up as well, not only affecting AI and tech but also embedded compute, automotive, telecom, medical devices, and other sectors," Sopko told Data Center Knowledge.

  S&P Global Mobility reached a parallel conclusion for the automotive sector, noting that memory manufacturers are directing investment and production toward higher-margin AI and data center products, tightening supply for automotive customers and driving up automotive memory prices.

  ## Sectors facing disruption

  The coalition identified several industries at risk of supply disruption or cost increases. For telecommunications providers currently investing in broadband expansion and network capacity upgrades, the primary concern is direct infrastructure cost — higher prices for building, maintaining, and upgrading communications networks. The letter also warned of disruptions to automobile and medical device supply chains and potential delivery delays for federal contractors.

  The letter does not specify which memory products are at the center of the strain. Although much attention in the industry has focused on HBM demand from AI systems, the coalition argued the effects are spreading into broader memory markets.

  Sopko said the key variable for operators is how semiconductor manufacturers allocate future production runs. "When the big fabs say they are fully allocated, the real question is: Where are those allocations going?" he said.

  ## Policy recommendations

  The coalition's recommendations to the administration include expanding memory production capacity, strengthening supply-chain cooperation through trade agreements, reviewing CHIPS Act programs — the 2022 federal legislation that created subsidies for domestic semiconductor manufacturing — reducing barriers to alternative sourcing, and monitoring market conditions.

  Sopko noted that the letter takes a specific policy posture: rather than seeking restrictions on AI data center growth, the signatories are asking the government to lower regulatory barriers and accelerate production. "In a time when factions are arising to limit data center expansion, this letter simply asks the government to remove red tape and do what it can to accelerate the production of memory," he said.

  ## A new pressure point in AI infrastructure

  Memory joins power systems, transformers, transmission equipment, cooling technologies, and advanced GPUs as a recognized supply constraint in the AI infrastructure buildout. The coalition's letter represents a formal, cross-sector appeal to treat memory supply as a national supply-chain priority alongside those other inputs already under strain.

  The groups urged the administration to engage both memory manufacturers and buyers to address the imbalance before disruptions deepen across industrial supply chains.
body_zh: |
  由代表電信、汽車、醫療器材及零售等產業的九個團體所組成的聯盟，日前致函川普政府，呼籲擴大記憶體晶片產能。根據市場調查機構集邦科技（TrendForce）的數據，AI資料中心需求在2026年第一季推動傳統DRAM合約價格單季暴漲約93至98%，上述聯盟隨即提出警告。

  這些團體於6月3日聯名致函商務部長霍華德．盧特尼克（Howard Lutnick）及財政部長史考特．貝森特（Scott Bessent）。連署團體包括：NCTA（網路與電視協會）、ACA Connects（美國通訊協會）、NTCA（農村寬頻協會）、電信產業協會（Telecommunications Industry Association）、AdvaMed、醫療器材製造商協會（Medical Device Manufacturers Association）、汽車創新聯盟（Alliance for Automotive Innovation）、零售業領袖協會（Retail Industry Leaders Association），以及全國零售聯合會（National Retail Federation）。

  聯盟在信中寫道：「不斷擴張的人工智慧（AI）資料中心耗用了大量可用的記憶體晶片產能，導致記憶體晶片價格前所未有地飆升，同時也削減了製造業與消費端產業可取得的晶片供應量。」

  ## 記憶體產能持續向AI系統傾斜

  集邦科技指出，製造商正將生產資源導向高頻寬記憶體（HBM）——一種專為AI加速晶片設計的特殊高速記憶體——以及伺服器相關產品，致使其他產業可取得的供應量大幅縮減。HBM的利潤率高於傳統動態隨機存取記憶體（DRAM），後者廣泛應用於從汽車到消費性電子等各類產品。

  HyperFrame Research半導體暨深科技分析師史蒂芬．索普科（Stephen Sopko）表示，供給吃緊的問題已不再侷限於AI基礎設施。他向《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）表示：「更大範圍的DRAM供給限制正逐漸浮現，不僅衝擊AI與科技業，也波及嵌入式運算、汽車、電信、醫療器材等多個領域。」

  標普全球汽車（S&P Global Mobility）對汽車產業得出相近結論，指出記憶體製造商正將投資與生產重心轉向利潤率更高的AI及資料中心產品，導致汽車客戶的供給趨緊，汽車用記憶體價格也隨之走揚。

  ## 多個產業面臨衝擊

  聯盟在信中點名數個面臨供給中斷或成本上升風險的產業。對於當前正大力投資寬頻擴建及網路容量升級的電信業者而言，主要隱憂在於直接的基礎建設成本——亦即建置、維護及升級通訊網路所需費用的持續攀升。信中也警告，汽車與醫療器材供應鏈恐遭衝擊，聯邦承包商的交貨時程亦可能延誤。

  這封信並未明確點出供給緊張的核心記憶體產品為何。儘管業界目前聚焦於AI系統對HBM的強勁需求，但聯盟主張其影響已擴散至更廣泛的記憶體市場。

  索普科表示，對業者而言，關鍵變數在於半導體製造商如何分配未來的生產配額。「當大型晶圓廠表示產能已全數分配完畢，真正的問題是：這些配額究竟流向何方？」他說。

  ## 政策建議

  聯盟向政府提出的建議包括：擴大記憶體產能、透過貿易協定強化供應鏈合作、檢視《晶片法案》（CHIPS Act）相關計畫——該法案為2022年通過的聯邦立法，設立了國內半導體製造補貼——降低替代來源的取得障礙，以及持續監測市場狀況。

  索普科指出，這封信持有一個明確的政策立場：連署團體並非要求限制AI資料中心的擴張，而是呼籲政府降低法規障礙、加速推動生產。「在各方勢力紛紛試圖限制資料中心擴張之際，這封信僅僅是請求政府排除繁瑣法規，並盡其所能加速記憶體生產，」他說。

  ## AI基礎建設的新壓力點

  記憶體如今與電力系統、變壓器、輸電設備、冷卻技術及高階GPU並列，共同成為AI基礎建設擴張中已被明確認定的供給瓶頸。這份跨產業的聯名信函，代表各界正式呼籲政府將記憶體供給列為國家供應鏈優先要務，與其他已面臨壓力的基礎要素一併處理。

  各團體呼籲政府同時與記憶體製造商及採購方積極溝通，在供給失衡情況進一步惡化、衝擊更多工業供應鏈之前，及時採取因應行動。
