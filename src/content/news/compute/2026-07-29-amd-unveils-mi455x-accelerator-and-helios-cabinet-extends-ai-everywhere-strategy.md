body_en: |
  AMD unveiled the MI455X AI accelerator and Helios cabinet at the Advancing AI 2026 annual conference in San Francisco, while chief executive Lisa Su outlined a strategy to extend artificial intelligence from large-scale data centers to personal computers, industrial robots, and edge devices.

  Su told attendees that "AI will change every industry and will exist on every computing platform." The company structured its announcements around three strategic pillars: compute, to build processing engines suited to different AI workloads; open platforms, to accelerate AI development through open hardware standards and software ecosystems; and "Powering AI Everywhere," to extend AI capability to enterprises, PCs, and the physical world.

  ## MI455X and Helios target AI data center and AI factory deployments

  AMD formally launched the MI455X accelerator and Helios cabinet as its primary hardware for AI data center and AI factory compute architecture. The company positioned both products as central to infrastructure for what it called large-scale agentic AI deployments.

  ## Agentic AI makes the case for on-device compute

  Su said AI development will not remain confined to cloud data centers and that intelligence will run "wherever work happens." She described agentic AI — systems that autonomously reason, call external tools, collaborate with other agents, and sustain tasks independently — as a catalyst for shifting more AI inference to the device edge.

  AMD argued that running more AI locally reduces inference latency, strengthens data privacy and reliability, enables offline operation, and allows cloud infrastructure to concentrate on large-scale model training and complex inference. The company described the resulting arrangement as a hybrid compute model in which data center and client-side AI share the workload.

  ## Ryzen AI Halo platform for local agentic AI development

  To support on-device agentic AI development, AMD demonstrated the Ryzen AI Halo platform, positioned as a local development environment for agentic AI applications. The platform carries 128 gigabytes (GB) of unified memory — a single pool shared by the processor and AI engine — and can locally build, test, and run open-weight models with up to 200 billion parameters.

  AMD said it has deepened a partnership with AI model repository Hugging Face to bring optimized models, libraries, and developer tools to Ryzen AI Halo, allowing developers to build and validate AI applications directly on an AI PC without relying on cloud resources.

  ## Ryzen AI Max PRO 400 series pushes local model scale further

  AMD's next-generation Ryzen AI Max PRO 400 series raises unified memory to 192 GB, enabling local support for models up to 300 billion parameters. The company said the added capacity addresses requirements for longer context windows, deeper agent memory, and multi-agent workflows — scenarios where multiple AI agents operate concurrently on a single device.

  ## ROCm.AI aims to simplify GPU software optimization

  AMD said competition in the AI ecosystem has moved beyond hardware performance to software development efficiency. The company announced ROCm.AI, an extension of its ROCm GPU software platform that uses generative AI to help developers optimize GPU code and automatically tune kernel and runtime configurations, with the stated aim of lowering the barrier to AI model deployment.

  AMD said ROCm is no longer positioned solely as a GPU computing platform but as a foundational layer connecting data center GPUs, AI PCs, and developer tooling. It maintains ongoing collaborations with open-source AI projects including PyTorch, vLLM, SGLang, and LM Studio, with the stated goal of enabling the same software environment to run across different hardware platforms. AMD characterized openness as a core element of its AI strategy.

  ## Physical AI extends the strategy to robots and embedded systems

  AMD extended its AI Everywhere framing to physical AI. Su said the next wave of AI will move from understanding the world to interacting with it, encompassing autonomous robots, automated equipment, and intelligent terminals that require real-time AI inference.

  To address that segment, AMD demonstrated a product lineup combining Ryzen Embedded processors, EPYC Embedded processors, and the Versal Adaptive SoC — a system-on-chip integrating adaptive computing resources — targeting industrial equipment, robotics, and edge computing applications. The company announced partnerships with robotics and AI ecosystem companies to deploy AI models to physical devices.

  Su said AI will increasingly participate directly in real-world tasks through sensors, vision systems, and control systems, and described physical AI as an important future growth direction for the company.

  ## What the announcements signal for AI infrastructure planning

  The Advancing AI 2026 disclosures represent AMD's most explicit articulation of a full-stack AI compute strategy, spanning AI factory infrastructure through the MI455X and Helios cabinet, client-side AI through the Ryzen AI platform, and physical computing through its embedded and adaptive chip lineup, with ROCm as the software layer connecting all three tiers.

  The Ryzen AI Max PRO 400 series — supporting 300-billion-parameter models locally on 192 GB of unified memory — is AMD's most specific near-term hardware commitment at the client edge of that stack.
body_zh: |
  AMD在舊金山舉辦的Advancing AI 2026年度大會上發表MI455X AI加速器與Helios機櫃，執行長蘇姿丰（Lisa Su）同步勾勒策略方向，將人工智慧的觸角從大型資料中心延伸至個人電腦、工業機器人與邊緣裝置。

  蘇姿丰在會中向與會者表示，「AI將改變每一個產業，並且存在於每一個運算平台之上。」AMD此次發表以三大策略支柱為核心架構：其一是「運算」，打造適合不同AI工作負載的處理引擎；其二是「開放平台」，透過開放硬體標準與軟體生態系加速AI發展；其三是「AI無所不在」，將AI能力延伸至企業、PC以及實體世界。

  ## MI455X與Helios瞄準AI資料中心與AI工廠部署

  AMD正式發表MI455X加速器與Helios機櫃，作為AI資料中心與AI工廠運算架構的核心硬體。AMD將這兩款產品定位為大規模代理式AI部署基礎設施的關鍵所在。

  ## 代理式AI為端側運算提供有力論據

  蘇姿丰表示，AI發展不會永遠侷限於雲端資料中心，智慧運算將在「工作發生的任何地方」執行。她將代理式AI——能夠自主推理、呼叫外部工具、與其他代理協作，並獨立持續執行任務的系統——描述為推動更多AI推論移往裝置端的關鍵驅動力。

  AMD主張，在本地端執行更多AI可降低推論延遲、強化資料隱私與可靠性、支援離線運作，同時讓雲端基礎架構得以專注於大規模模型訓練與複雜推論。AMD將這樣的架構安排稱為「混合運算模型」，由資料中心與用戶端AI共同分擔工作負載。

  ## Ryzen AI Halo平台支援本地端代理式AI開發

  為支援裝置端代理式AI開發，AMD展示了Ryzen AI Halo平台，將其定位為代理式AI應用程式的本地端開發環境。該平台搭載128 GB統一記憶體——由處理器與AI引擎共享的單一記憶體池——可在本地端建構、測試並執行多達2,000億參數的開放權重模型。

  AMD表示已深化與AI模型儲存庫Hugging Face的合作關係，將最佳化模型、函式庫與開發者工具引入Ryzen AI Halo，讓開發者無需仰賴雲端資源，即可直接在AI PC上建構並驗證AI應用程式。

  ## Ryzen AI Max PRO 400系列進一步提升本地模型規模

  AMD下一代Ryzen AI Max PRO 400系列將統一記憶體提升至192 GB，可在本地端支援多達3,000億參數的模型。AMD表示，增加的記憶體容量能夠滿足更長情境視窗（context window）、更深層代理記憶，以及多代理工作流程的需求——亦即多個AI代理同時在單一裝置上並行運作的應用情境。

  ## ROCm.AI致力簡化GPU軟體最佳化

  AMD表示，AI生態系的競爭焦點已從硬體效能延伸至軟體開發效率。AMD宣布推出ROCm.AI，這是其ROCm GPU軟體平台的延伸，透過生成式AI協助開發者最佳化GPU程式碼，並自動調校核心（kernel）與執行期（runtime）組態，目標是降低AI模型部署的門檻。

  AMD表示，ROCm已不再單純定位為GPU運算平台，而是作為連結資料中心GPU、AI PC與開發者工具鏈的基礎層。AMD持續與PyTorch、vLLM、SGLang、LM Studio等開源AI專案維持合作，目標是讓相同的軟體環境能夠跨不同硬體平台運行。AMD將開放性視為其AI策略的核心要素。

  ## 實體AI將策略延伸至機器人與嵌入式系統

  AMD進一步將「AI無所不在」的論述延伸至實體AI領域。蘇姿丰表示，下一波AI浪潮將從理解世界，轉向與世界互動，涵蓋需要即時AI推論的自主機器人、自動化設備與智慧終端。

  為切入這一市場，AMD展示了結合Ryzen嵌入式處理器、EPYC嵌入式處理器以及Versal Adaptive SoC（一款整合自適應運算資源的系統單晶片）的產品陣容，鎖定工業設備、機器人與邊緣運算應用。AMD並宣布與機器人及AI生態系企業建立合作夥伴關係，將AI模型部署至實體裝置。

  蘇姿丰表示，AI將透過感測器、視覺系統與控制系統，日益直接參與現實世界的任務，並將實體AI描述為公司重要的未來成長方向。

  ## 這波發表對AI基礎架構規劃的意義

  Advancing AI 2026的一系列發表，代表AMD迄今對全層次AI運算策略最明確的完整宣示：以MI455X與Helios機櫃涵蓋AI工廠基礎架構，以Ryzen AI平台深入用戶端AI，以嵌入式與自適應晶片產品線觸及實體運算，ROCm則作為貫穿三個層次的軟體介面。

  其中，Ryzen AI Max PRO 400系列——支援在192 GB統一記憶體上於本地端執行3,000億參數模型——是AMD在整套架構用戶端邊緣最具體的近期硬體承諾。
