body_en: |
  Vultr is taking pre-orders for AMD's Instinct MI455X graphics processing unit (GPU) and the AMD Helios rack-scale AI system, making the cloud provider one of the first to offer the hardware commercially, as AMD positions the products as a direct challenge to Nvidia's dominance in data center AI infrastructure.

  AMD CEO Lisa Su announced at the company's Advancing AI conference in San Francisco on July 23 that Helios is in full production, with shipments from hardware partners scheduled to begin by the end of the third quarter of 2026. Vultr said it is accepting reserved capacity pre-orders now, with the MI455X GPU due in the fourth quarter and broader deployments on MI455X and Helios planned across 2027 and 2028.

  Microsoft said this week it will deploy Helios to power AI workloads including frontier model inference and Azure AI services. Anthropic also announced it will deploy up to 2 gigawatts (GW) worth of GPUs in Helios solutions, a scale that signals the system's entry into the largest tier of AI infrastructure procurement.

  ## What Helios is and how it compares to Nvidia

  Helios is a fully integrated rack-scale AI system — meaning the compute, networking, and software are engineered together at the level of a full rack rather than assembled from separate components. It combines AMD's Epyc server CPUs, Instinct MI455X GPUs, Pensando networking, and AMD's ROCm software platform for AI training and inference.

  The system supports 72 GPUs in a single rack. Su said Helios delivers 15 percent more AI compute, 50 percent more memory, and 50 percent more scale-out bandwidth than Nvidia's Vera Rubin NVL72 system, and up to 30 percent more tokens per dollar. Tokens are the unit of output AI language models generate; cost per token is a key metric for operators running inference workloads at scale.

  AMD said the MI455X GPU delivers 34 times higher token throughput and up to 18 times lower token cost compared with its previous MI355X GPU.

  Four months earlier, Nvidia had unveiled its Vera Rubin AI infrastructure platform. Matt Kimball, vice president and principal analyst at Moor Insights and Strategy, described Helios as a direct answer to Nvidia's NVL72 platforms. "Helios is a very strong response to that," he said.

  ## What Vultr will offer

  Vultr said it will serve as a global provider for Helios and will offer direct liquid cooling options alongside the new systems. Liquid cooling moves heat away from hardware using water or other fluids rather than air, and is increasingly used in dense AI clusters where air cooling cannot keep pace with power densities.

  The MI455X is built on AMD's CDNA (Compute DNA) architecture, which AMD designs specifically for data center compute rather than graphics. Vultr said the chip includes HBM4 (High Bandwidth Memory, fourth generation) with wider memory bandwidth and support for low-precision AI data types — formats that reduce the size of numbers used in AI calculations to speed up processing and reduce memory consumption.

  Vultr said Helios combines AMD compute with AMD Pensando networking — Pensando being AMD's data processing unit (DPU) and networking technology — in a standards-based design intended to let organizations scale AI infrastructure from a single rack to a cluster of racks.

  J.J. Kardwell, Chief Executive Officer of Vultr, said customers were shifting from experimentation to production. "Customers are rapidly moving from AI experimentation into production, and increasingly that means agentic and inference-heavy workloads that require infrastructure built for scale," Kardwell said. "The AMD Instinct MI455X and Helios rackscale architecture give our customers the flexibility, control, and price-to-performance they need to scale their AI initiatives."

  ## The agentic AI demand driving the build-out

  Both AMD and Vultr framed the announcements around agentic AI — software systems capable of completing multi-step tasks with limited human intervention, which require repeated calls to AI models and access to large volumes of data. Su said at her keynote: "Agents are scaling from millions to billions, driving unprecedented infrastructure needs."

  Su said AMD now expects the AI infrastructure market to reach $1.4 trillion by 2030, up from a projection of $500 billion by 2028 she made the previous year. She said the server CPU market, currently valued at $25 billion, could reach $200 billion by 2030, driven partly by the need for processors to orchestrate the steps surrounding AI inference — retrieving data, calling tools, and managing agent workflows — even as GPUs handle the core reasoning.

  Dave McCarthy, Group Vice President of Cloud and Datacenter Infrastructure at IDC, the research firm, linked the Vultr deployment to that demand shift. "As enterprises transition into the agentic era, the massive influx of complex, data-intensive workflows requires a fundamental shift in infrastructure design," McCarthy said. IDC said it expects a tenfold increase in the number and complexity of enterprise AI agents over the next five years.

  Andrew Dieckmann, Corporate Vice President and General Manager of AMD's Data Centre GPU Business Unit, said: "AMD Helios rackscale solution brings the best of AMD compute and networking technology together into a single rack-scale building block, delivering leadership performance, performance per watt, and cost per token for the next wave of AI."

  ## AMD's competitive position

  IDC analyst Ashish Nadkarni said AMD is the only company outside Nvidia that can offer a comparable integrated portfolio spanning GPUs and DPUs for data center and AI infrastructure. "They are making steady progress to be the strong No. 2 in the market," Nadkarni said. "There is nobody else in the semiconductor industry today that can offer what AMD is offering other than Nvidia."

  AMD said its Epyc CPUs reached 46 percent revenue share of the server market last quarter, with more than 60 percent of the Fortune 100 running on Epyc. Su also introduced the Epyc 9006 Series server CPUs at the same event, a family of processors targeting enterprise, cloud, high-performance computing, and AI workloads. AMD said a 256-core Venice CPU offers 2.2 times the throughput of an 88-core Nvidia Vera CPU, while a 96-core Venice chip offers 20 percent better per-core performance.

  Nadkarni noted that AMD's x86 processor architecture gives it an advantage with customers reluctant to rewrite their software for Arm-based systems. "As the market has changed, AMD has grown with it," he said.

  ## What comes next

  Vultr said it serves hundreds of thousands of active customers across 185 countries and offers cloud compute, cloud GPU, bare metal, and storage services. The company disclosed an equity financing in late 2024 that valued it at $3.5 billion.

  Helios hardware partner shipments are scheduled to begin by the end of the third quarter of 2026, with Vultr's own MI455X and Helios deployments extending across 2027 and 2028. The Epyc 9006 SP7 processor, with up to 256 cores, is scheduled to ship in the fourth quarter of 2026, with additional Epyc 9006 variants targeted for the first half of 2027.
body_zh: |
  Vultr正接受AMD Instinct MI455X繪圖處理器（GPU）及AMD Helios機架規模AI系統的預訂，成為最早商業提供此類硬體的雲端業者之一，AMD將這些產品定位為直接挑戰輝達在資料中心AI基礎建設領域主導地位的利器。

  AMD執行長蘇姿丰於7月23日在舊金山「Advancing AI」大會上宣布，Helios已全面量產，硬體合作夥伴的出貨時程定於2026年第三季末展開。Vultr表示，目前已開放容量預訂，MI455X GPU預計於第四季交貨，MI455X與Helios的大規模部署則規劃於2027至2028年間陸續展開。

  微軟本週宣布將部署Helios，以支撐包括前沿模型推論及Azure AI服務在內的AI工作負載。Anthropic也宣布將在Helios解決方案中部署最多達2吉瓦（GW）算力規模的GPU，此一規模標誌著Helios已躋身最高層級的AI基礎建設採購行列。

  ## Helios是什麼，以及與輝達的差異比較

  Helios是一套完全整合的機架規模AI系統，意即運算、網路與軟體在整個機架層面統一設計，而非由獨立元件拼裝而成。系統整合了AMD的EPYC伺服器CPU、Instinct MI455X GPU、Pensando網路技術，以及AMD用於AI訓練與推論的ROCm軟體平台。

  該系統單一機架最多支援72顆GPU。蘇姿丰表示，Helios的AI運算效能比輝達Vera Rubin NVL72系統高出15%，記憶體容量多出50%，橫向擴展頻寬也多出50%，每token成本最多可降低30%。Token是AI語言模型的基本輸出單位，對大規模執行推論工作負載的業者而言，每token成本是關鍵衡量指標。

  AMD表示，MI455X GPU與前代MI355X GPU相比，token吞吐量提升34倍，token成本最多可降低18倍。

  四個月前，輝達才剛發表Vera Rubin AI基礎建設平台。Moor Insights and Strategy副總裁暨首席分析師Matt Kimball將Helios定位為輝達NVL72平台的直接回應。「Helios是非常強力的反擊，」他說。

  ## Vultr的服務方案

  Vultr表示，將擔任Helios的全球供應商，並在新系統上提供直接液冷選項。液冷技術以水或其他液體取代空氣，將熱能從硬體中帶走，在氣冷技術難以因應高功率密度的AI密集叢集中已日益普遍。

  MI455X採用AMD專為資料中心運算設計的CDNA（Compute DNA）架構，而非繪圖運算架構。Vultr表示，該晶片搭載第四代高頻寬記憶體HBM4，具備更寬的記憶體頻寬，並支援低精度AI資料格式——此類格式藉由縮減AI運算中數值的位元大小，達到加速處理、降低記憶體消耗的效果。

  Vultr表示，Helios將AMD運算與AMD Pensando網路技術結合——Pensando為AMD的資料處理器（DPU）與網路技術——採用標準化設計，讓企業能將AI基礎建設從單一機架擴展至多機架叢集。

  Vultr執行長J.J. Kardwell表示，客戶正快速從實驗階段轉向正式生產。「客戶正在快速從AI實驗進入生產，這愈來愈代表代理式及推論密集型工作負載，需要為規模化打造的基礎建設，」Kardwell說。「AMD Instinct MI455X與Helios機架規模架構，賦予我們的客戶擴展AI計畫所需的彈性、掌控力與性價比。」

  ## 推動建設熱潮的代理式AI需求

  AMD與Vultr均將此次宣布定調於代理式AI（agentic AI）——即能以有限人工介入完成多步驟任務的軟體系統，需要反覆呼叫AI模型並存取大量資料。蘇姿丰在主題演講中表示：「AI代理的規模正從數百萬擴展至數十億，帶動前所未有的基礎建設需求。」

  蘇姿丰表示，AMD目前預估AI基礎建設市場規模將於2030年達到1.4兆美元，高於她前一年所預測的2028年5,000億美元。她表示，目前市值250億美元的伺服器CPU市場，到2030年有望成長至2,000億美元，部分原因在於市場需要處理器來協調AI推論周邊的各項步驟——包括擷取資料、呼叫工具，以及管理代理工作流程——即使核心推論運算仍由GPU負責。

  IDC研究機構雲端與資料中心基礎建設集團副總裁Dave McCarthy，將Vultr的部署與這股需求轉移連結在一起。「隨著企業邁入代理式AI時代，複雜且資料密集的工作流程大量湧入，要求基礎建設設計必須進行根本性的轉變，」McCarthy表示。IDC預估，未來五年內，企業AI代理的數量與複雜度將成長十倍。

  AMD資料中心GPU業務部企業副總裁暨總經理Andrew Dieckmann表示：「AMD Helios機架規模解決方案將AMD最優秀的運算與網路技術整合至單一機架規模的建構模組，為下一波AI浪潮提供領先業界的效能、每瓦效能及每token成本。」

  ## AMD的競爭地位

  IDC分析師Ashish Nadkarni表示，AMD是輝達以外，唯一能在資料中心與AI基礎建設領域同時提供涵蓋GPU與DPU整合性產品組合的企業。「他們正穩步邁向市場中強勁的第二名，」Nadkarni說。「在半導體產業中，目前沒有任何其他企業能夠提供AMD所能提供的，輝達除外。」

  AMD表示，其EPYC CPU上季在伺服器市場的營收佔比達46%，財星百大企業中逾六成正在使用EPYC。蘇姿丰也在同場活動中發表EPYC 9006系列伺服器CPU，這是一款鎖定企業、雲端、高效能運算及AI工作負載的處理器家族。AMD表示，256核心的Venice CPU吞吐量是輝達88核心Vera CPU的2.2倍，96核心版本則在每核心效能上高出對手20%。

  Nadkarni指出，AMD的x86處理器架構為其帶來優勢，因為許多客戶不願重新改寫軟體以適配Arm架構系統。「隨著市場變化，AMD也隨之成長，」他說。

  ## 後續展望

  Vultr表示，目前服務橫跨185個國家的數十萬名活躍客戶，提供雲端運算、雲端GPU、裸機及儲存服務。該公司於2024年底完成股權融資，估值達35億美元。

  Helios硬體合作夥伴的出貨時程定於2026年第三季末展開，Vultr自身的MI455X與Helios部署則將延伸至2027至2028年。最多支援256核心的EPYC 9006 SP7處理器預計於2026年第四季出貨，其他EPYC 9006系列產品則預計於2027年上半年陸續推出。
