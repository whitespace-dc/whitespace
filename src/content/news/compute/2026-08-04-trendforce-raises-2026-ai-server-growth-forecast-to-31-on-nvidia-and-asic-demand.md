body_en: |
  Research firm TrendForce has raised its 2026 AI server shipment growth forecast to nearly 31% year-over-year, up from a prior estimate of 28%, driven by stronger-than-expected purchases of NVIDIA GB- and VR-series rack-scale AI server solutions by hyperscale cloud service providers (CSPs) and Tier-2 data center operators, alongside accelerating in-house ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit — chips engineered for a narrow task rather than general computation) deployments at Google and Amazon Web Services in the second half of the year.

  The revision also reflects Chinese cloud operators expanding adoption of local AI solutions to support large language model (LLM — AI systems trained on large text datasets to understand and generate language) workloads, according to TrendForce.

  ## Capital expenditure surges across nine major CSPs

  TrendForce's latest data show that nine major CSPs — Google, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle in the United States, and ByteDance, Tencent, Alibaba, and Baidu in China — will together spend more than $886.7 billion on capital expenditure in 2026, an increase of approximately 90% year-over-year.

  The five North American providers account for nearly 90% of that combined total, with most having at least doubled their individual spending compared with the prior year. TrendForce attributed the acceleration to continued buildout of AI data centers, GPU clusters, and liquid cooling infrastructure to meet compute demand from generative AI and large-scale AI models.

  ## NVIDIA dominates near-term, ASICs expand into 2027

  Among US hyperscalers, TrendForce expects Google to concentrate on its own TPU (tensor processing unit — Google's proprietary AI accelerator chip) through 2026 and 2027, with TPU shipments forecast to continue at a high rate of growth in 2027.

  AWS will keep NVIDIA's GB300 as its primary GPU AI server platform in 2026, while its in-house ASIC ships alongside at a steady pace, according to TrendForce. AWS's proprietary chip capacity is expected to expand further in 2027.

  Meta in 2026 is primarily deploying NVIDIA's GB- and VR-series platforms together with AMD's Helios rack-scale solutions. TrendForce said Meta's in-house ASIC rollout is not expected to accelerate meaningfully until 2027, where it is positioned as a long-term strategy to reduce AI infrastructure costs and improve inference efficiency.

  ## Chinese operators enter a new build cycle

  Chinese CSPs are accelerating their own AI infrastructure investments. TrendForce estimates combined 2026 capital expenditure for ByteDance, Tencent, Alibaba, and Baidu will grow more than 80% year-over-year. ByteDance is committing the largest share of that spending, directed toward large-scale AI data centers, in-house ASIC platforms, and GPU clusters, TrendForce said.

  ## Outlook through 2027: growth moderates but does not reverse

  TrendForce projects total capital expenditure for the nine CSPs to reach approximately $1.3 trillion in 2027, a year-over-year increase of around 50%. The firm said the slower rate of growth compared with 2026 reflects a high base effect rather than a deceleration in AI investment, with absolute spending still expected to reach a new record.

  The research firm said the industry's growth dynamic is expected to shift from GPU-centric expansion toward a broader infrastructure upgrade cycle spanning AI servers, liquid cooling, advanced packaging, high-speed interconnects, power systems, and memory — sustained by demand from AI inference workloads, agentic AI applications, in-house ASICs, and next-generation AI models.

  TrendForce projects the nine major CSPs' combined 2027 capital expenditure at approximately $1.3 trillion.

  ## TrendForce上調2026年AI伺服器成長預測至31%，輝達與ASIC需求為主要驅動力

  研究機構集邦科技（TrendForce）將2026年AI伺服器出貨年增預測上調至近31%，高於先前估計的28%。此次上修的主因，在於超大規模雲端服務業者（CSP）及第二層資料中心業者對輝達GB系列與VR系列機架規模AI伺服器解決方案的採購力道強於預期，同時Google與亞馬遜雲端運算服務（AWS）在下半年加速部署自研ASIC（特殊應用積體電路——專為特定任務而設計，而非通用運算的晶片）。

  集邦科技指出，此次預測修正也反映了中國雲端業者為支援大型語言模型（LLM——以大規模文字資料集訓練，能理解並生成語言的AI系統）工作負載，而擴大採用本土AI解決方案的趨勢。

  ## 九大雲端服務業者資本支出大幅攀升

  集邦科技最新數據顯示，九大CSP——美國的Google、亞馬遜、Meta、微軟與甲骨文，以及中國的字節跳動、騰訊、阿里巴巴與百度——2026年合計資本支出將逾8,867億美元，年增幅約達90%。

  北美五大業者約占合計總額的九成，其中多數業者個別支出較前一年至少翻倍。集邦科技認為，加速的主因是各業者持續擴建AI資料中心、GPU叢集及液冷基礎設施，以因應生成式AI與大規模AI模型帶來的運算需求。

  ## 短期輝達仍居主導地位，ASIC版圖延伸至2027年

  在美國超大規模業者中，集邦科技預期Google將在2026年至2027年間持續聚焦於自研TPU（張量處理器——Google專有的AI加速晶片），TPU出貨量預計在2027年維持高速成長。

  集邦科技指出，AWS在2026年將以輝達GB300作為主要GPU AI伺服器平台，同時穩步推進自研ASIC的出貨。AWS自有晶片產能預計在2027年進一步擴大。

  Meta在2026年主要部署輝達GB系列與VR系列平台，並搭配AMD的Helios機架規模解決方案。集邦科技表示，Meta自研ASIC的大規模推展預計要到2027年才會明顯加速，定位為長期降低AI基礎設施成本、提升推論效率的戰略布局。

  ## 中國業者進入新一輪建設週期

  中國雲端服務業者正加速推進自身的AI基礎設施投資。集邦科技估計，字節跳動、騰訊、阿里巴巴與百度2026年合計資本支出將年增逾80%。其中字節跳動投入占比最大，資金主要流向大規模AI資料中心、自研ASIC平台及GPU叢集，集邦科技指出。

  ## 展望2027年：成長放緩但不逆轉

  集邦科技預測，九大CSP合計資本支出將於2027年達到約1.3兆美元，年增幅約50%。該機構表示，相較於2026年，成長率趨緩反映的是高基期效應，而非AI投資力道的退潮，絕對支出金額仍預計創下歷史新高。

  集邦科技指出，產業成長動能預計將從以GPU為核心的擴張，轉向涵蓋AI伺服器、液冷系統、先進封裝、高速互連、電力系統及記憶體的更廣泛基礎設施升級週期，並由AI推論工作負載、代理型AI應用、自研ASIC及次世代AI模型的需求持續支撐。

  集邦科技預測，九大主要雲端服務業者2027年合計資本支出約達1.3兆美元。
