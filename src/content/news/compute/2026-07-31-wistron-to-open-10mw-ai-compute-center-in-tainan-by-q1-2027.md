body_en: |
  Wistron, the Taiwanese contract manufacturer, is building a 10-megawatt AI compute center in Tainan, with construction of the building expected to finish in October 2026 and equipment installation targeted for the first quarter of 2027, the company's chief technology officer disclosed Wednesday.

  Shen Ching-yao, Wistron's CTO, made the announcement at the sidelines of a press conference marking the establishment of the TAIONE Open Source Foundation, where he serves as a board member. He said the Tainan facility will carry a capacity of 10 MW — megawatts, a unit of electrical power that indicates how much computing infrastructure a facility can support.

  ## Planned users

  The center is intended for three primary uses, Shen said: internal use by the Wistron Group, donated capacity for National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University to conduct academic research, and support for national-level cooperation projects. He added that Wistron does not rule out gradually opening the center to external parties in the future.

  ## Taiwan's hardware-software gap

  Shen also addressed Taiwan's standing in the global AI industry, saying the island holds a significant advantage in hardware but remains some distance from the world's leading edge in compute applications and software.

  He described open source — software whose source code is publicly available for anyone to use and modify — as a "necessary evil" for enterprise AI development. The software development model, he said, has shifted from writing code line by line to relying on open-source ecosystems for rapid iteration and integration. He said companies must continually learn how to divide open-source from proprietary technology while protecting their own distinctive value.

  Shen said Taiwanese enterprises should engage with open-source communities and build sound mechanisms to gradually extend the country's hardware strengths into software and open-source ecosystems, which he said would raise Taiwan's influence in the global AI industry.

  Wistron has set Q1 2027 as its target for completing equipment and system installation at the Tainan facility.
body_zh: |
  緯創資通正在台南興建一座10MW AI運算中心，建築主體預計於2026年10月竣工，設備安裝則以2027年第一季為目標完成。緯創技術長沈清堯於週三透露了這項消息。

  沈清堯是在出席TAIONE開源基金會成立記者會的場邊宣布上述消息，他本人也是該基金會的董事。他說明，台南這座設施的容量為10MW——百萬瓦是電功率單位，代表一座設施能夠支撐的運算基礎建設規模。

  ## 規劃用途

  沈清堯表示，這座中心規劃三項主要用途：緯創集團的內部使用、捐贈算力給國立陽明交通大學從事學術研究，以及支援國家級合作計畫。他補充，緯創不排除未來逐步向外部單位開放。

  ## 台灣的軟硬體落差

  沈清堯也就台灣在全球AI產業中的地位進行闡述，表示台灣在硬體方面具備相當大的優勢，但在運算應用與軟體領域，距離全球頂尖水準仍有一段距離。

  他將開源——即原始碼對所有人公開、可自由使用與修改的軟體——形容為企業AI發展的「必要之惡」。他指出，軟體開發模式已從逐行撰寫程式碼，轉向倚賴開源生態系進行快速迭代與整合；企業必須持續學習如何在開源技術與專有技術之間劃定界線，同時保護自身的差異化價值。

  沈清堯呼籲，台灣企業應積極投入開源社群、建立健全的運作機制，逐步將台灣在硬體方面的強項延伸至軟體與開源生態系，藉此提升台灣在全球AI產業的影響力。

  緯創已將台南設施的設備與系統安裝完成目標定為2027年第一季。
