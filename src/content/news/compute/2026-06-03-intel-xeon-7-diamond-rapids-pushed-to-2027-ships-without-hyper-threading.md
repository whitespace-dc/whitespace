body_en: |
  Intel confirmed at Computex 2026 that its next-generation Xeon 7 processor, codenamed **Diamond Rapids**, will arrive in **2027**. The chip is built on Intel's 18A-P process — the P-core follow-on to Clearwater Forest, the 18A E-core part that Intel launched at the same event.

  ## Platform Specifications

  Diamond Rapids delivers several generational leaps over Xeon 6:

  - **Memory bandwidth**: 16 DDR5 channels, roughly doubling bandwidth versus the prior generation
  - **Interconnect**: PCIe Gen 6 support
  - **Core count**: approximately 50% more cores than Xeon 6
  - **Architecture**: scalable system-on-chip (SoC) design with uniform memory latency, specifically highlighted for bandwidth-limited applications

  ## Hyper-Threading Removed

  Diamond Rapids will ship without Hyper-Threading. Intel has described the feature as important and stated it expects it to return with the subsequent **Coral Rapids** platform. Intel's architectural work on desktop silicon — which moved away from Hyper-Threading in favor of single-core performance — is expected to translate into competitive per-core performance for data center workloads on Diamond Rapids.

  ## Narrowed Product Stack

  Diamond Rapids is launching with fewer platform tiers than originally planned. Intel previously removed an 8-channel Diamond Rapids variant from its roadmap, a significant reduction in lineup breadth.

  ## Competitive Context

  Diamond Rapids enters a crowded server CPU market in 2027. Intel identified the competitive field as including AMD's expanding EPYC portfolio, NVIDIA's Vera CPU deployed at scale in AI clusters, an Arm AGI CPU, a rumored Qualcomm server entry, and custom silicon from hyper-scalers. In 2026 — before Diamond Rapids ships — it will be Intel's Xeon 6+ facing AMD and NVIDIA Vera, not the next-generation part.

  Intel's stated structural advantage is production on Intel Foundry 18A, which avoids contention for TSMC wafer capacity that competing vendors will face.

  Intel 於 Computex 2026 確認，下一代 Xeon 7 處理器代號「Diamond Rapids」將於 **2027 年**推出，採用 Intel 18A-P 製程技術。此為繼 Clearwater Forest（18A E-core 晶片，同樣於本屆 Computex 發表）之後的 P-core 新一代產品。

  ## 平台規格

  Diamond Rapids 在多項指標上較 Xeon 6 有世代級提升：

  - **記憶體頻寬**：16 通道 DDR5，頻寬約為上一代兩倍
  - **傳輸介面**：支援 PCIe Gen 6
  - **核心數量**：較 Xeon 6 多約 50%
  - **架構設計**：可擴展系統單晶片（SoC）架構，具備統一記憶體延遲，針對頻寬受限應用特別強調此特性

  ## 捨棄超執行緒

  Diamond Rapids 出貨時將不支援超執行緒技術。Intel 表示，超執行緒是重要功能，預計將在下一代 **Coral Rapids** 平台回歸。Intel 在桌上型處理器矽晶片上已轉向捨棄超執行緒、強化單核心效能的架構方向，此設計工作預計同樣能為 Diamond Rapids 帶來在資料中心工作負載上具競爭力的每核心效能。

  ## 產品線縮窄

  Diamond Rapids 推出的平台層級較原先規劃更少。Intel 已將 8 通道 Diamond Rapids 方案從路線圖中移除，使整體產品線廣度顯著縮減。

  ## 競爭態勢

  Diamond Rapids 將在 2027 年進入競爭激烈的伺服器 CPU 市場。Intel 點名的競爭對手包括：AMD 持續擴張的 EPYC 產品線、輝達大規模部署於 AI 叢集的 Vera CPU、Arm 的 AGI CPU、傳聞中進軍市場的 Qualcomm，以及各大超大規模業者持續推出的自研晶片。在 Diamond Rapids 尚未上市的 2026 年，面對 AMD 與輝達 Vera 的將是 Intel 現行的 Xeon 6+，而非下一代產品。

  Intel 強調的結構性優勢在於採用 Intel Foundry 18A 自有晶圓廠量產，可避免與其他競爭業者爭搶台積電（TSMC）晶圓產能。
