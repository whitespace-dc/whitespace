body_en: |
  Supermicro has launched 12 server platforms optimized for Intel's new Xeon 6+ processors, offering up to 576 high-performance cores per server and aimed at reducing total cost of ownership (TCO) and energy consumption in large-scale cloud and enterprise data centers.

  The new systems, built around Supermicro's Data Center Building Block Solutions (DCBBS), a modular infrastructure framework using pre-validated components and subsystems, support up to 288 cores per processor socket in dual-socket configurations. The company said the platforms are designed for high-density cloud computing, virtualization environments, 5G data analytics, and other throughput-intensive workloads.

  ## Intel Xeon 6+ performance gains

  Intel's Xeon 6+ processors deliver several architectural advances over the previous generation, according to Supermicro. Core counts have doubled; instructions per clock (IPC, a measure of how much work a processor completes in a single cycle) have improved by up to 17%; last-level cache (the on-chip memory buffer closest to the processor cores) has increased fivefold; and memory speed support has risen 25%.

  ## Product lines

  The 12 new platforms span four product families:

  - **Hyper Series**: Single- and dual-socket 1U and 2U rack-mount servers optimized for maximum performance and configuration flexibility, with support for high memory configurations and advanced networking.
  - **SuperBlade**: A blade architecture fitting up to 10 compute nodes in a 6U chassis, targeting rack-level compute density and shared-infrastructure efficiency at scale.
  - **FlexTwin**: A liquid-cooled, high-density system in which each dual-socket node operates independently while sharing power and cooling infrastructure, designed for cloud and hyperscale environments.
  - **GrandTwin**: A single-socket, multi-node system optimized for high core-count and high-density workloads in cloud environments.

  ## CEO statement

  Charles Liang, Supermicro's president and chief executive officer, said the company worked closely with Intel to optimize its DCBBS for the Xeon 6+ processors. Liang said the new X14 platforms can deliver up to 576 high-performance cores per server with significantly improved performance-per-watt, and that the systems are designed to shorten deployment and time-to-service while reducing TCO and energy consumption in large cloud and enterprise data center environments.

  ## Infrastructure scope

  DCBBS supports a full range of modular AI infrastructure configurations, from single-server and network setups to full rack-scale and data-center-scale deployments, and includes software and services alongside hardware, the company said.

  Supermicro (NASDAQ: SMCI), headquartered in San Jose, California, manufactures products in the United States, Asia, and the Netherlands. The company said the new systems will be on display at the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1, 4th floor, booth N0602, with further product details available at supermicro.com/x14.
body_zh: |
  超微電腦（Supermicro）正式推出12款針對Intel全新Xeon 6+處理器最佳化的伺服器平台，每台伺服器最高可達576顆高效能核心，旨在協助大規模雲端與企業資料中心降低總持有成本（TCO）並減少能源消耗。

  這批新系統以超微電腦的「資料中心積木式解決方案」（Data Center Building Block Solutions，DCBBS）為基礎打造。DCBBS是一套採用預先驗證元件與子系統的模組化基礎架構框架，在雙插槽配置下，每個處理器插槽最高可支援288顆核心。超微電腦表示，這些平台專為高密度雲端運算、虛擬化環境、5G資料分析及其他高吞吐量工作負載而設計。

  ## Intel Xeon 6+ 效能躍升

  根據超微電腦的說明，Intel Xeon 6+處理器相較於上一代在架構上帶來多項進化：核心數量增加一倍；每時脈指令數（IPC，衡量處理器在單一週期內完成工作量的指標）最高提升17%；末級快取（最接近處理器核心的晶片內記憶體緩衝區）擴大五倍；記憶體速度支援則提升25%。

  ## 產品系列

  12款全新平台涵蓋四大產品家族：

  - **Hyper系列**：單插槽與雙插槽1U及2U機架式伺服器，以最大效能與彈性配置為優化目標，支援高容量記憶體配置與進階網路功能。
  - **SuperBlade**：刀鋒式架構，6U機箱內最多可容納10個運算節點，針對機架層級的運算密度與共享基礎架構效率而設計，適合大規模部署。
  - **FlexTwin**：液冷高密度系統，每個雙插槽節點獨立運作，同時共用電源與散熱基礎架構，專為雲端與超大規模（hyperscale）環境設計。
  - **GrandTwin**：單插槽多節點系統，針對雲端環境中高核心數與高密度工作負載進行最佳化。

  ## 執行長聲明

  超微電腦總裁暨執行長梁見後（Charles Liang）表示，公司與Intel密切合作，針對Xeon 6+處理器對DCBBS進行最佳化。他指出，全新X14平台每台伺服器最高可提供576顆高效能核心，每瓦效能大幅提升，且系統設計著眼於縮短部署時程與上線時間，同時降低大型雲端與企業資料中心環境的TCO及能源消耗。

  ## 基礎架構規模

  DCBBS支援完整的模組化AI基礎架構配置，涵蓋範圍從單一伺服器、網路設置，到整機架規模乃至資料中心規模的部署，並在硬體之外同時納入軟體與服務，超微電腦如此說明。

  超微電腦（那斯達克代碼：SMCI）總部位於美國加州聖荷西，在美國、亞洲及荷蘭均設有生產據點。公司表示，上述全新系統將於台北南港展覽館一館四樓N0602展位展出，更多產品詳情可至supermicro.com/x14查詢。
