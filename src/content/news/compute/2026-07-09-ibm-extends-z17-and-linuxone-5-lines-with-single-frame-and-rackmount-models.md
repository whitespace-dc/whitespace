body_en: |
  IBM is expanding its z17 mainframe and fifth-generation LinuxONE product lines with single-frame and rackmount server variants, extending the Telum II-based platform to mid-scale enterprise customers and, through a new entry-level appliance called the LinuxONE Express, to prospective mainframe buyers for the first time.

  The new hardware, planned for release in summer 2026, arrives roughly a year after IBM launched the multi-frame z17 and LinuxONE Emperor 5 systems in 2025. According to ServeTheHome, the z17 and LinuxONE Emperor 5 outpaced sales of the prior z16 generation in their first three quarters on the market, a run IBM has described as its best mainframe sales performance in two decades.

  ## New z17 mid-scale and rackmount systems

  The mid-scale z17 product is the z17 ME2, a turnkey single-frame system configurable with up to two processor draws. That configuration supports up to 16 Telum II processors and 82 cores, paired with up to 18 terabytes of memory. IBM left spare rack units in the frame so customers can install non-IBM equipment such as network switches alongside the mainframe hardware.

  A companion product, the z17 MER, offers equivalent specifications in a disaggregated form: individual rackmount server drawers designed to fit a standard 19-inch rack. The MER format is intended for colocation facilities and other data center environments where IBM's turnkey ME2 rack would not fit the operational model.

  ## LinuxONE Rockhopper 5

  The LinuxONE side of the announcement follows the same structure. IBM is releasing the LinuxONE Rockhopper 5 — continuing IBM's penguin-themed naming convention for the Linux-only product line — available as either a single-frame system or as individual servers for 19-inch racks. The Rockhopper 5 is positioned as the LinuxONE equivalent of the z17 ME2 and MER.

  ## LinuxONE Express entry appliance

  Separate from the Rockhopper 5, IBM is introducing the LinuxONE Express, an 18U rackmount appliance sold as a single turnkey box. The Express is described as the smallest LinuxONE configuration available and is designed explicitly as an on-ramp for prospective customers: a purchase that allows a new buyer to evaluate the LinuxONE ecosystem before expanding to larger configurations. IBM characterized it as a smaller LinuxONE Rockhopper 5 configuration built and sold as a complete unit.

  ## Platform and processor technology

  All of the new systems are built on the same Telum II processor that underpins the z17 multi-frame products launched in 2025. Telum II is an 8-core chip fabricated on Samsung's 5HPP (5-nanometer-class) process, running at 5.5 GHz and measuring approximately 600 square millimeters of die area — a large chip by contemporary standards. The processor carries hardware compatibility guarantees stretching back to IBM's original sixty-year-old System/360 architecture.

  Each Telum II chip includes a 24 TOPS (tera-operations per second, a measure of AI inference throughput) accelerator block integrated directly into the processor. For workloads requiring more AI throughput, IBM also offers the Spyre, a PCIe expansion card that houses a larger version of the same accelerator block as a dedicated chip, used similarly to the on-CPU block but at higher performance. Both the z17 and LinuxONE 5 families support the Spyre card.

  ## Generational performance gains over z16

  Compared with the z16-generation systems the new hardware replaces — including products such as the z16 A02 — IBM says the z17-class platform delivers:

  - Approximately 10% more throughput per core
  - Approximately 20% higher total core counts
  - Approximately 12% greater memory capacity

  The systems carry IBM's stated eight-nines reliability specification, meaning less than one second of unplanned downtime per year.

  ## Industry significance

  The initial z17 and LinuxONE Emperor 5 multi-frame systems were aimed at IBM's largest customers in finance, insurance, healthcare, and other heavily regulated industries. The single-frame and rackmount additions extend the same hardware platform to customers whose workloads do not require a multi-rack installation, filling the same general niche occupied by z16-generation predecessors such as the z16 A02 but with updated performance and feature parity with the larger z17 systems.

  IBM indicated in its pre-briefing that on-premises AI computing is a particular area of focus for the expanded lineup, a positioning that aligns with the security and compliance requirements common among the regulated-industry clients that have historically driven mainframe adoption.

  With the LinuxONE Express and Rockhopper 5 completing the LinuxONE 5 family, IBM's full z17-generation portfolio now spans from enterprise multi-frame configurations down to a single 18U box aimed at prospective customers evaluating the platform for the first time.

  ## IBM擴展z17與LinuxONE 5產品線，推出單框架及機架式新機型

  IBM宣布擴充z17大型主機與第五代LinuxONE產品線，推出單框架及機架掛載伺服器新機型，將基於Telum II處理器的平台延伸至中型企業客戶，並透過全新入門款設備LinuxONE Express，首度向潛在的大型主機新買家敞開大門。

  這批新硬體預計於2026年夏季正式上市，距IBM於2025年發表多框架z17與LinuxONE Emperor 5系統約莫一年。根據ServeTheHome的報導，z17與LinuxONE Emperor 5上市後前三季的銷售表現已超越上一代z16，IBM更將此稱為旗下大型主機業務近二十年來最亮眼的銷售佳績。

  ## z17中型及機架式新系統

  中型z17新品為z17 ME2，這是一款即裝即用（turnkey）的單框架系統，最多可配置兩個處理器抽屜。在此配置下，系統支援最多16顆Telum II處理器、共82個核心，並可搭配最多18TB的記憶體。IBM在框架內預留了額外的機架空間，讓客戶可自行安裝非IBM設備，例如在大型主機硬體旁直接加裝網路交換器。

  另一款配套產品z17 MER則以分散式設計提供相同規格：採用獨立機架掛載的伺服器抽屜，可安裝於標準19吋機架。MER格式主要針對主機代管（colocation）機房及其他無法容納IBM整體式ME2機架的資料中心環境所設計。

  ## LinuxONE Rockhopper 5

  LinuxONE系列的公告採用相同架構。IBM將推出LinuxONE Rockhopper 5——延續IBM以企鵝命名這個純Linux產品線的一貫傳統——可選擇單框架系統或適用於19吋機架的獨立伺服器兩種形式。Rockhopper 5定位為z17 ME2與MER的LinuxONE對應機型。

  ## LinuxONE Express入門款設備

  除Rockhopper 5之外，IBM另行推出LinuxONE Express，這是一款18U機架掛載設備，以單一整合式機箱形式銷售。Express被定位為目前最小的LinuxONE配置，明確設計為潛在新客戶的入門跳板：讓新買家在擴充至更大型配置之前，先行評估LinuxONE生態系統。IBM將其描述為以完整單元形式出廠銷售的精簡版LinuxONE Rockhopper 5。

  ## 平台與處理器技術

  所有新系統均採用與2025年發表的z17多框架產品相同的Telum II處理器。Telum II為8核心晶片，採用三星5HPP（5奈米級）製程製造，運行時脈為5.5 GHz，晶片面積約600平方毫米，以當今標準而言屬於大型晶片。這款處理器提供往回相容保證，可追溯至IBM已有六十年歷史的System/360架構。

  每顆Telum II晶片內建一個24 TOPS（兆次運算每秒，即每秒兆次操作，為AI推論吞吐量的衡量指標）的加速器模組，直接整合於處理器之中。針對需要更高AI吞吐量的工作負載，IBM另提供Spyre——一款PCIe擴充卡，搭載與處理器內建加速器相同架構但規格更大的獨立晶片，使用方式與內建模組相似，但效能更高。z17與LinuxONE 5系列均支援Spyre擴充卡。

  ## 相較z16的世代效能提升

  與新硬體所取代的z16世代系統相比——包括z16 A02等產品——IBM表示z17級平台帶來以下進步：

  - 每核心吞吐量提升約10%
  - 總核心數增加約20%
  - 記憶體容量擴大約12%

  這些系統均符合IBM所宣稱的八個九（eight-nines）可靠性規格，即每年非計畫性停機時間不超過一秒。

  ## 產業意義

  最初的z17與LinuxONE Emperor 5多框架系統，主要針對金融、保險、醫療及其他高度受監管產業的IBM大型客戶。單框架與機架式新機型的推出，將同一硬體平台延伸至工作負載不需要多機架安裝的客戶，填補了z16世代前身（如z16 A02）所佔據的市場定位，同時具備更新的效能，並與較大型的z17系統功能一致。

  IBM在發表前說明中指出，內部部署（on-premises）的AI運算是擴充產品線的重點方向，此一定位與受監管產業客戶在資安與法規遵循方面的需求高度契合，而這類客戶向來是帶動大型主機市場的主力。

  隨著LinuxONE Express與Rockhopper 5完整LinuxONE 5家族陣容，IBM第七代z17世代完整產品組合，現已從企業級多框架配置，一路延伸至以18U單一機箱為形式、專為首次評估平台的潛在客戶所打造的入門方案。
