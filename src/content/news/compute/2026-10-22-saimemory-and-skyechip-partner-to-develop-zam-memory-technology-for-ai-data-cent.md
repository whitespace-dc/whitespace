body_en: |
  Saimemory Corp., a Tokyo-based subsidiary of SoftBank Corp., has announced a design and engineering engagement with SkyeChip Bhd, a company listed on Bursa Malaysia specialising in silicon intellectual property (IP) and silicon products, to advance Saimemory's Z-Angle Memory (ZAM) technology — a next-generation memory architecture aimed at the data centre AI market.

  Under the engagement, SkyeChip will provide design and engineering support as well as custom IP solutions for the ZAM programme, drawing on its expertise in silicon design and integration, the companies said.

  ## What ZAM is designed to do

  ZAM is Saimemory's proprietary next-generation memory technology developed specifically for environments that require the training and inference of large-scale AI models, the company said. Saimemory describes ZAM as targeting high-capacity and high-bandwidth data processing, improved processing performance, and reduced power consumption — characteristics that are increasingly critical as AI workloads scale in data centres.

  ## The memory wall problem

  SkyeChip CEO Fong Swee Kiang framed the engagement around a well-known constraint in AI computing. "AI computing is limited by the memory wall," he said, referring to the bottleneck that develops when processors can generate and consume data at rates that memory systems struggle to match. Fong said SkyeChip's memory interface technology is intended to address that constraint, and described the company as "proud to be associated with Saimemory in its next-generation memory initiative for AI."

  ## The parties

  Saimemory was established as a SoftBank Corp. subsidiary with a mandate to develop next-generation memory for AI workloads and is headquartered in Tokyo. SkyeChip Bhd is listed on Bursa Malaysia, Malaysia's national stock exchange, and focuses on silicon IP and silicon products, with particular expertise in memory interface technology and silicon integration.

  ## What the engagement means for both companies

  Saimemory president and CEO Hideya Yamaguchi said SkyeChip's technical expertise and experience are expected to play "an extremely important role" in realising ZAM. He said the companies aim to build a "strong, long-term partnership that will help create a new market for next-generation memory."

  For SkyeChip, Fong said the engagement extends the company's role in advanced AI memory programmes, without identifying those other programmes by name. The companies said they intend to deepen their collaboration with a view to the future commercialisation of ZAM.

  ## Significance

  The engagement signals growing interest among AI infrastructure suppliers in purpose-built memory architectures as an alternative or complement to existing high-bandwidth memory standards. By pairing a SoftBank-backed memory developer with a Southeast Asian silicon IP firm, the collaboration also reflects an emerging geography of advanced semiconductor design partnerships beyond traditional centres in the United States, Japan, and South Korea.

  No commercialisation timeline, financial terms, or volume commitments were disclosed in the announcement.
body_zh: |
  軟銀旗下子公司、總部位於東京的Saimemory Corp.宣布，與在馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）掛牌、專注於矽智財（IP）及矽晶片產品的SkyeChip Bhd簽訂設計與工程合作協議，共同推進Saimemory的Z角記憶體（Z-Angle Memory，ZAM）技術——這是一種以資料中心AI市場為目標的下一代記憶體架構。

  根據合作協議，SkyeChip將憑藉其矽晶設計與整合專業，為ZAM計畫提供設計與工程支援，以及客製化IP解決方案，雙方公司表示。

  ## ZAM技術的設計目標

  ZAM是Saimemory專為需要訓練及推論大規模AI模型的環境所開發的專有下一代記憶體技術。Saimemory表示，ZAM的目標在於實現高容量、高頻寬的資料處理，提升運算效能並降低功耗——隨著AI工作負載在資料中心的規模持續擴大，這些特性愈發關鍵。

  ## 記憶體牆問題

  SkyeChip執行長馮瑞強（Fong Swee Kiang）以AI運算領域一個廣為人知的制約因素來闡述此次合作背景。他表示：「AI運算受限於記憶體牆。」這一瓶頸源自於處理器產生與消耗資料的速度，遠超記憶體系統所能跟上的上限。馮瑞強指出，SkyeChip的記憶體介面技術正是為了突破這一制約而生，並表示公司「很榮幸能與Saimemory攜手，共同推動AI下一代記憶體計畫」。

  ## 雙方背景

  Saimemory以軟銀旗下子公司的身份成立，肩負為AI工作負載開發下一代記憶體的使命，總部設於東京。SkyeChip Bhd則在馬來西亞國家交易所Bursa Malaysia掛牌上市，專注於矽智財及矽晶片產品，尤以記憶體介面技術與矽晶整合見長。

  ## 合作對雙方的意義

  Saimemory總裁兼執行長山口秀哉（Hideya Yamaguchi）表示，SkyeChip的技術專業與豐富經驗，預計將在實現ZAM的過程中發揮「極為重要的作用」。他表示，雙方公司致力於建立「穩固的長期夥伴關係，共同為下一代記憶體開創全新市場」。

  對SkyeChip而言，馮瑞強表示，此次合作進一步擴展了公司在先進AI記憶體計畫中所扮演的角色，但未透露其他計畫的具體名稱。雙方表示，將持續深化合作關係，著眼於ZAM未來的商業化前景。

  ## 重要意義

  此次合作反映出AI基礎設施供應商對專用記憶體架構的興趣日益升溫，視其為現有高頻寬記憶體標準的替代方案或補充選項。軟銀支持的記憶體開發商與東南亞矽智財公司的強強聯手，也揭示了先進半導體設計合作夥伴關係正在形成新興地理格局，已逐漸延伸至美國、日本與南韓等傳統核心地帶之外。
