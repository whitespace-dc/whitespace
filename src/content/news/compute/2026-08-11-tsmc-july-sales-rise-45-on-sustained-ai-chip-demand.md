  ## TSMC posts 45% July revenue jump

  Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (TSMC), the contract chipmaker that produces semiconductors for Nvidia and Apple, reported July revenue of NT$467.58 billion (US$14.5 billion), a 45% increase from the same month a year earlier, the company said. The figure signals that demand for chips powering artificial intelligence (AI) systems — software and hardware designed to perform tasks that typically require human cognition — remains strong despite broader market uncertainty.

  Analysts had, on average, projected a 46.8% sales increase for the current quarter, according to the report.

  ## Raised forecasts reflect confidence in AI infrastructure spending

  Last month, TSMC lifted both its spending and revenue projections for 2026. The company now expects capital expenditure — the money it spends on factories, equipment, and infrastructure — to reach a record US$60 billion to US$64 billion this year. It also forecast full-year sales growth of slightly above 40% in US dollar terms. TSMC said it expects the strong growth in AI chip demand to extend into 2027 and beyond.

  ## Market volatility weighs on valuations

  Despite the strong revenue figures, TSMC shares have fallen roughly 5% from a peak reached in late June, though they remain more than 50% higher than at the start of the year. Tech valuations broadly stalled in July amid persistent concerns about overcapacity in data centres — facilities that house the computing hardware running AI workloads — and questions over whether the scale of AI-related capital spending will produce meaningful financial returns.

  ## Hyperscaler commitments underpin demand outlook

  Alphabet, Meta Platforms, Microsoft, and Amazon — the four largest spenders in the data centre buildout — have collectively pledged nearly US$2.4 trillion in AI-related commitments over the coming years, according to the report. That level of committed spending provides a forward-looking indicator of sustained chip demand that underpins TSMC's raised forecasts.

  ## 台積電7月營收年增45%

  全球最大晶圓代工廠台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）公布，7月合併營收達新台幣4,675.8億元（約145億美元），較去年同月大幅成長45%。台積電是輝達（Nvidia）與蘋果（Apple）的主要晶片製造夥伴，此次亮眼數字顯示，驅動人工智慧（AI）系統運作的晶片需求依然旺盛——所謂AI系統，泛指能執行通常需要人類認知能力之任務的軟體與硬體——即便整體市場仍面臨不確定性，需求動能並未消退。

  根據報導，分析師對本季度營收的平均預測為年增46.8%。

  ## 上調預測，彰顯對AI基礎建設支出的信心

  台積電上個月同步調高2026年的資本支出與營收展望。公司預計今年資本支出——即用於廠房、設備與基礎建設的投資金額——將達到創歷史新高的600億至640億美元。台積電同時預測，全年以美元計算的營收成長率將略高於40%。公司表示，AI晶片的強勁需求預計將延續至2027年乃至更長遠的未來。

  ## 市場波動壓抑股票估值

  儘管營收表現亮眼，台積電股價自6月底的高點以來已回落約5%，但相較於今年年初，仍累計上漲逾50%。7月間，科技股估值普遍陷入停滯，主因市場持續擔憂資料中心產能過剩——資料中心是承載AI運算工作負載之電腦硬體設施——以及外界對於AI相關資本支出的龐大規模能否帶來實質財務回報抱有疑慮。

  ## 超大規模雲端業者的承諾，支撐需求前景

  根據報導，Alphabet、Meta Platforms、微軟（Microsoft）與亞馬遜（Amazon）——資料中心建設投資規模最大的四家業者——在未來數年內，合計已承諾投入近2.4兆美元的AI相關支出。如此龐大的已承諾投資，為持續性晶片需求提供了前瞻性指標，也成為台積電上調預測的重要依據。
