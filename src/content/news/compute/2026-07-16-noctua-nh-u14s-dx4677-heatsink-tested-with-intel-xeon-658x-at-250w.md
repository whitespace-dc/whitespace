body_en: |
  ServeTheHome has published a quick-look review of the Noctua NH-U14S DX4677 air cooler installed on the Intel Xeon 658X, a 250-watt (W) Intel Xeon W processor, finding the unit functional in that configuration when the correct carrier frame is used.

  The Intel Xeon 658X uses an LGA4710 socket mounting, which differs from the LGA4677 socket the cooler's name references. According to ServeTheHome, the NH-U14S DX4677 accommodates this through a compatible carrier frame, allowing the heatsink to seat correctly on the chip.

  ## What the NH-U14S DX4677 is

  The NH-U14S DX4677 is Noctua's NH-U14S heatsink and fan platform adapted with a larger contact plate to cover the Intel Xeon Sapphire Rapids and Emerald Rapids processor generations, as well as the Intel Xeon 6 series. ServeTheHome notes the cooler is designed for the smaller sockets in that lineup and is not compatible with the higher-end Xeon 6900E+ or 6900P series, which use a different, larger socket.

  The unit ships with two Noctua NF-A15 140mm PWM (pulse-width modulation, a fan-speed control method) fans pre-installed on the heatsink tower. ServeTheHome notes the design carries a tall profile. The cooler arrives pre-assembled in Noctua's signature brown packaging, but installation requires the user to disassemble the unit and then reassemble it onto the motherboard.

  ## Packaging and included accessories

  Noctua ships the NH-U14S DX4677 with thermal interface material — the compound applied between processor and heatsink to improve heat transfer — already applied to the contact plate. ServeTheHome flagged this as a positive, noting in reference to a prior Arctic Freezer 4U-SP5 review that the publication prefers pre-applied thermal interface material on server and workstation coolers.

  The box also includes a Y cable, which allows both fans to run from a single motherboard fan header, and a dedicated installation tool for seating the heatsink. ServeTheHome noted the tool is particularly useful for builders who do not regularly swap processors.

  ## Context

  ServeTheHome purchased the unit through Amazon in preparation for a forthcoming full review of the Intel Xeon 658X. The quick-look article covers unboxing and physical inspection; thermal performance results are expected in the separate processor review.

  ## NH-U14S DX4677 的產品定位

  NH-U14S DX4677 是 Noctua 以 NH-U14S 散熱器與風扇平台為基礎、擴大接觸底板面積後推出的型號，設計目的在於涵蓋 Intel Xeon Sapphire Rapids 與 Emerald Rapids 世代處理器，以及 Intel Xeon 6 系列。ServeTheHome 指出，該散熱器是針對上述產品線中採用較小型腳座的機型所設計，並不相容於採用另一種更大型腳座的高階 Xeon 6900E+ 或 6900P 系列。

  本款散熱器出廠時，塔型主體上已預裝兩顆 Noctua NF-A15 140mm PWM（脈衝寬度調變，即風扇轉速控制技術）風扇。ServeTheHome 特別提到，整體設計高度相當突出。散熱器以 Noctua 招牌棕色包裝出貨，並已完成預組裝，但實際安裝時仍需先將其拆解，再重新組裝固定至主機板上。

  ## 包裝內容與附件

  Noctua 在 NH-U14S DX4677 的接觸底板上預先塗抹了導熱介面材料——即施加於處理器與散熱器之間、用以改善導熱效果的導熱膏——散熱器出廠即已備妥此道工序。ServeTheHome 對此給予正面評價，並援引先前針對 Arctic Freezer 4U-SP5 的評測表示，該媒體偏好伺服器與工作站散熱器在出廠時便預先塗妥導熱介面材料。

  盒裝內亦附有一條 Y 型延長線，可讓兩顆風扇共用主機板上的單一風扇接頭，以及一支專用安裝工具，方便將散熱器確實固定就位。ServeTheHome 指出，這項工具對於不常更換處理器的組裝者而言尤為實用。
