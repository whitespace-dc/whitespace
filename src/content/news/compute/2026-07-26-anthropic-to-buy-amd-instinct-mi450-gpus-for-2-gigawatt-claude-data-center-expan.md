body_en: |
  Anthropic and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), a Taiwan-headquartered semiconductor manufacturer, announced on July 23, 2026, that Anthropic will purchase AMD Instinct MI450 Series graphics processing units (GPUs) installed on AMD Helios rack-scale systems to expand the data center infrastructure running its Claude artificial intelligence (AI) models.

  Anthropic did not disclose the number of GPUs to be purchased. The company said in its official blog that the total compute capacity to be added reaches 2 gigawatts (GW). The deployment is structured in two phases: the first covers 1 GW and is scheduled to begin in the first half of 2027, after which capacity will be expanded to a total of 2 GW.

  For context, according to various reports, 2 GW is roughly equivalent to the electricity consumption of between 600,000 and 1.5 million homes, depending on regional consumption rates and usage patterns. Anthropic noted that the 2 GW figure cannot be translated directly into a GPU count, because that power budget covers not only GPUs but also CPUs, networking equipment, storage, and data center cooling systems. At that infrastructure scale, however, the deployment is estimated to require hundreds of thousands of AI GPUs.

  ## Why Anthropic is adding the capacity

  Anthropic said the additional compute is needed to meet continuously growing customer demand for Claude. Tom Brown, co-founder and Chief Compute Officer at Anthropic, said in a company statement: "Access to compute capacity is key to keeping Claude at the forefront of AI development while meeting demand from our customers."

  ## Software collaboration alongside hardware procurement

  In addition to hardware supply, AMD and Anthropic announced a software development partnership. Claude models will be used to help optimize workloads running on AMD Instinct GPUs and to accelerate development of AMD's ROCm (Radeon Open Compute) open-source software platform. AMD said it will also adopt Claude broadly across its own internal engineering and product development processes.

  The first 1 GW deployment phase is scheduled to begin in the first half of 2027, with the full 2 GW capacity to follow thereafter.

  Anthropicx表示，新增算力是為了因應客戶對Claude日益成長的需求。Anthropic共同創辦人暨首席運算長（Chief Compute Officer）湯姆．布朗（Tom Brown）在公司聲明中表示：「取得充足的運算產能，是讓Claude保持在AI發展前沿、同時滿足客戶需求的關鍵所在。」

  除硬體供應協議外，AMD與Anthropic亦宣布展開軟體開發合作。Claude模型將用於協助優化在AMD Instinct GPU上運行的工作負載，並加速AMD ROCm（Radeon Open Compute）開源軟體平台的開發進程。AMD表示，其公司內部的工程與產品開發流程也將全面導入Claude。
