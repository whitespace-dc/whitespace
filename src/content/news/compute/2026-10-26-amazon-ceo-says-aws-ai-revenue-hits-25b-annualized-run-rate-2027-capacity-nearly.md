body_en: |
  Amazon's chief executive said the company's artificial intelligence business has surpassed $25 billion in annualized revenue, while warning that $220 billion in AI investment commitments is still not enough to keep pace with customer demand, according to statements reported by Anue (鉅亨網).

  The CEO disclosed that Amazon Web Services compute capacity for 2027 is almost entirely sold out, a signal that hyperscaler infrastructure is being booked well in advance by enterprise and cloud customers racing to secure AI workloads.

  ## Scale of investment and the supply gap

  The $220 billion figure represents Amazon's stated AI investment commitment. Despite that level of spending, the CEO characterized it as falling short of what demand requires — an acknowledgment that the company's ability to build and provision infrastructure is being outpaced by orders.

  Selling out 2027 capacity in 2025 means customers are committing to multi-year infrastructure agreements with little visibility into whether additional supply will emerge before their contracts begin. For data center planners and enterprise buyers, near-zero available capacity two years out narrows procurement options and increases pricing leverage for the provider.

  ## What the revenue figure signals

  An annualized run rate of more than $25 billion for AI-related revenue — derived by projecting a recent period's results over a full year — places Amazon's AI business among the largest software and infrastructure revenue streams in the technology sector. The figure encompasses AI services delivered through AWS.

  The combination of a $25 billion revenue run rate, a $220 billion investment program, and a sold-out capacity position through 2027 indicates that Amazon is prioritizing AI infrastructure build-out as a defining capital allocation priority, while the CEO's comments suggest the company believes even that level of spending will leave unfilled demand on the table.
body_zh: |
  亞馬遜執行長表示，公司人工智慧業務的年化營收已超過250億美元，同時警告，即便已承諾投入2,200億美元於AI領域，仍追不上客戶需求的增速。依據鉅亨網的報導，該執行長同時透露，Amazon Web Services（AWS）2027年的運算容量幾乎已全數售出，顯示超大規模雲端業者的基礎設施正被企業與雲端客戶大舉提前預訂，以確保搶先鎖定AI工作負載所需的資源。

  ## 投資規模與供需缺口

  2,200億美元代表亞馬遜公開宣示的AI投資承諾金額。儘管支出規模如此龐大，執行長仍將其定性為不足以滿足需求——這等於坦承，公司建設與部署基礎設施的速度已跟不上訂單持續湧入的腳步。

  在2025年就將2027年的容量售罄，意味著客戶正在簽訂橫跨數年的基礎設施合約，卻幾乎無從判斷在合約正式生效之前，市場上是否還會釋出額外供應。對資料中心規劃人員與企業採購方而言，未來兩年可用容量趨近於零，不僅大幅壓縮了採購選項，也同時提升了供應商在定價上的議價籌碼。

  ## 營收數字所傳達的訊號

  逾250億美元的AI相關年化營收——係以近期某一時段的實際成果，推算至整個完整年度所得出的數字——使亞馬遜的AI業務躋身科技產業中規模最大的軟體與基礎設施營收來源之列。這項數字涵蓋透過AWS提供的各項AI服務。

  250億美元的年化營收、2,200億美元的投資計畫，加上容量售罄至2027年的市場地位，三者合計顯示，亞馬遜正將AI基礎設施的擴建列為最優先的資本配置項目；而執行長的公開說法更進一步暗示，公司認為即便投入如此規模的資金，仍將有一部分市場需求無法獲得滿足。
