body_en: |
  SNS Network Technology Bhd has secured a US$298.66 million (approximately RM1.22 billion) contract to supply high-performance artificial intelligence servers to a Singapore-based customer, the Kuala Lumpur-listed company said Monday — the largest single contract in the group's history.

  The deal was signed Sunday between SNS Network's wholly-owned subsidiary, SNS Network (M) Sdn Bhd, and a Singapore-incorporated private company whose principal business is publishing game software and applications. Under the agreement, SNS Network will supply, deliver, and provision the high-performance AI servers for a data centre located in Malaysia.

  Shares in SNS Network rose as much as 13.4% to an intraday high of 58.5 sen on Monday before closing at 56 sen, up 8.74% or 4.5 sen — the stock's highest closing level in five months. The move lifted the group's market capitalisation to RM945.1 million.

  ## Contract terms and financial impact

  SNS Network said the contract is expected to contribute positively to future earnings and net assets, with the timing dependent on when deliveries are made and when revenue is recognised under accounting rules. The company did not disclose a delivery schedule or break down the contract by product line.

  Managing director Ko Yun Hung said the contract demonstrates the group's ability to deliver enterprise-grade AI infrastructure at scale and reflects customer confidence in its technical and execution capabilities. "As AI adoption accelerates across industries, demand for high-performance computing infrastructure continues to grow," Ko said, adding that SNS Network is positioned to capture opportunities from rising investment in AI data centres and enterprise AI deployments across the region.

  ## Recent financial context

  The contract announcement comes after a difficult quarter for the group. For the first quarter ended April 30, 2026, SNS Network reported net profit of RM2.71 million, down 73.6% from RM10.24 million in the same period a year earlier. Revenue fell 45.9% to RM445 million from RM822.75 million, which the company attributed mainly to the timing of product deliveries and higher operating costs.

  The RM1.22 billion contract, if recognised in a single financial year, would represent more than 2.7 times the group's total revenue in the most recent quarter, illustrating the scale of the deal relative to SNS Network's recent trading activity.

  ## Significance for Malaysia's AI infrastructure build-out

  The transaction adds to evidence of accelerating investment in AI data centre infrastructure in Malaysia, with a Singapore-domiciled entity procuring servers for a facility on Malaysian soil. SNS Network said the deal reinforces its strategic focus on AI infrastructure and reflects continued investment in expanding its AI capabilities.

  The group said it maintains long-standing partnerships with global technology leaders, though it did not name the server manufacturers or technology partners involved in fulfilling the contract.

  SNS Network Technology Bhd宣布，旗下全資子公司SNS Network (M) Sdn Bhd已取得一份總值2億9,866萬美元（約12.2億令吉）的高效能人工智慧伺服器供應合約，買方為一家總部設於新加坡的客戶。這家在吉隆坡掛牌上市的公司於週一表示，這是集團有史以來規模最大的單筆合約。

  合約於週日正式簽署，買方為一家依新加坡法律成立的私人公司，主要業務為遊戲軟體及應用程式的發行。依據合約，SNS Network將負責供應、交付及建置高效能AI伺服器，供馬來西亞境內一座資料中心使用。

  週一，SNS Network股價一度大漲13.4%，盤中最高觸及58.5仙，最終收盤報56仙，上漲4.5仙或8.74%，創下近五個月來最高收盤價。此波漲勢帶動集團市值攀升至9億4,510萬令吉。

  ## 合約條款與財務影響

  SNS Network表示，這份合約預計將對公司未來盈利及淨資產帶來正面貢獻，惟具體時程將取決於實際交貨時間及會計準則下的收入認列時點。公司並未透露交貨時間表，亦未按產品線別揭露合約細節。

  總裁暨執行董事柯雲鴻表示，這份合約充分展現了集團大規模交付企業級AI基礎建設的能力，也反映出客戶對SNS Network在技術與執行能力上的高度信賴。他說：「隨著AI在各產業的應用加速推進，對高效能運算基礎建設的需求持續攀升。」他補充，SNS Network已做好準備，有望掌握區域內AI資料中心投資熱潮及企業AI部署需求所帶來的商機。

  ## 近期財務背景

  這份合約公告的時機，正值集團甫走過一段艱困的季度。在截至2026年4月30日止的第一季，SNS Network報告淨利為271萬令吉，較上年同期的1,024萬令吉大幅下滑73.6%。營收亦從8億2,275萬令吉下跌45.9%至4億4,500萬令吉，公司將此歸因於產品交貨時程落差及營運成本上升。

  若以單一財務年度認列，這份12.2億令吉的合約金額將超過集團最近一季總營收的2.7倍，足以說明此筆交易相較於SNS Network近期業績規模之龐大。

  ## 對馬來西亞AI基礎建設的意義

  這項交易為馬來西亞AI資料中心基礎建設投資持續升溫再添佐證——一家新加坡法人實體為其設於馬來西亞境內的設施採購伺服器。SNS Network表示，這筆合約強化了集團在AI基礎建設領域的策略布局，也反映出外界對持續擴展AI能力的投入意願。

  集團表示，旗下與全球科技龍頭維持長期合作夥伴關係，但未透露參與本次合約履行的伺服器製造商或技術合作夥伴名稱。
