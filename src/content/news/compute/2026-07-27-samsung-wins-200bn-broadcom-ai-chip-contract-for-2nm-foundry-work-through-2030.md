body_en: |
  Samsung Electronics has secured a five-year contract worth more than $200 billion from Broadcom Inc. to manufacture chips for artificial intelligence infrastructure, with the deal running through 2030, Bloomberg reported.

  The agreement ranks among the largest contracts in Samsung's chip foundry business, where the company manufactures chips on behalf of other companies. Samsung said the work will center on 2-nanometer (2nm) and sub-2nm process technology, to be used across multiple Broadcom chip products.

  ## Memory supply and advanced packaging scope

  The contract extends beyond wafer fabrication. Samsung said the agreement expands the two companies' strategic collaboration to cover memory technology, with Samsung set to supply memory components for Broadcom's next-generation AI accelerators. AI accelerators are specialized chips designed to process artificial intelligence workloads at high performance while consuming less power than conventional processors, according to the report. The companies will also jointly develop advanced packaging technology, which integrates multiple semiconductor components into a single package to raise performance and improve energy efficiency.

  ## Competition for AI foundry orders

  Samsung and fellow South Korean chipmaker SK Hynix are both accelerating expansion to secure AI chip orders from global customers as competition with Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), the global chip manufacturing market leader, intensifies, the report said. Rising demand for chips to support AI development is driving manufacturers to pursue more advanced and efficient production technologies.

  ## South Korea's national semiconductor targets

  The South Korean government is pushing the national semiconductor industry to become more competitive. The country is targeting a doubling of memory chip production capacity within five years while strengthening overall manufacturing capabilities to stay ahead of rival nations, according to the report.

  Under the five-year agreement, Samsung will manufacture Broadcom chips at 2nm and below, supply memory for next-generation AI accelerators, and jointly develop advanced packaging technology through 2030.
body_zh: |
  據彭博報導，三星電子從博通取得一份為期五年、總值逾2,000億美元的晶片製造合約，負責生產人工智慧基礎設施所需晶片，合約期限延伸至2030年。

  這項協議是三星晶圓代工業務迄今規模最大的合約之一。三星晶圓代工業務專門代客製造晶片。三星表示，此次合作將聚焦於2奈米（2nm）及次2奈米製程技術，並應用於博通旗下多款晶片產品。

  ## 記憶體供應與先進封裝範疇

  此合約的範疇不僅止於晶圓代工。三星表示，這項協議將兩家公司的策略合作擴展至記憶體技術領域，三星將為博通下一代AI加速器供應記憶體元件。據報導，AI加速器是一種專用晶片，能以高效能處理人工智慧工作負載，同時比傳統處理器更為節能。雙方亦將共同開發先進封裝技術——透過將多個半導體元件整合於單一封裝體內，達到提升效能、改善能源效率的目的。

  ## AI晶圓代工訂單的競爭態勢

  據報導，三星與同為南韓晶片廠商的SK海力士（SK Hynix）皆在加速擴張，積極爭取全球客戶的AI晶片訂單，與此同時，兩家公司與全球晶圓製造龍頭台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電，TSMC）之間的競爭也日趨白熱化。支援AI發展的晶片需求持續攀升，驅使各家製造商競相追求更先進、更高效的生產技術。

  ## 南韓的半導體國家目標

  南韓政府正積極推動本國半導體產業強化競爭力。據報導，該國目標是在五年內將記憶體晶片產能倍增，同時全面強化製造實力，以在與競爭國家的角力中維持領先優勢。

  根據這份為期五年的協議，三星將為博通製造2奈米及以下製程晶片、為下一代AI加速器供應記憶體，並共同開發先進封裝技術，合作期限延伸至2030年。
