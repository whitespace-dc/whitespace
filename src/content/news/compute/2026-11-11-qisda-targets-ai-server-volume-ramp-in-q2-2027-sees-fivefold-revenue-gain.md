body_en: |
  Qisda, the Taiwanese electronics conglomerate, plans to scale up AI server and 1.6-terabit-per-second (Tbps) switch shipments beginning in the second quarter of 2027, executives said at an investor conference on November 10. The company targets AI server revenue in 2027 at least five times higher than its 2026 level.

  ## OCP orders and ramp schedule

  Chief Executive Officer Ke Shu-fen said Qisda attended the Open Compute Project (OCP) Summit in October 2026, displayed a full AI server product line, and secured orders for AI servers and 1.6T switches. Small-volume shipments of those products are scheduled before year-end 2026, with volume production from Q2 2027, Ke said.

  Qisda's AI infrastructure offering covers AI servers, 1.6T high-speed switches, advanced liquid cooling, and cabinet integration, packaged as a scalable AI Rack-Scale full-rack solution, the company said. The 1.6T switches are slated for delivery to North American data centers.

  ## Q4 revenue guidance

  Chairman Chen Chi-hung said he expects fourth-quarter 2026 revenue to come in roughly flat against the third quarter. Concerns about AI data center development following the U.S. midterm elections reflect short-term political conditions, Chen said, adding that a moderate slowdown could give the supply chain a chance to recover to a healthier composition. He said the group would continue to accelerate its AI positioning because long-term AI trends remain intact.

  ## Liquid cooling

  Zeng Wen-xing, general manager of the Intelligent Solutions Business Group, said the group's computing power team, led by its Qiyang unit, has won several new server projects. Qisda has developed bidirectional liquid cooling solutions and expects small-volume production in Q4 2026, with shipments tied to customer schedules, Zeng said.

  ## Networking and silicon photonics

  Li Liang-xiang, general manager of the Network Communications Business Group, said the networking segment can sustain double-digit quarter-on-quarter and year-on-year growth in Q4 2026. The company expects 1.6T switch deliveries to begin before the end of December 2026, Li said, and is investing in next-generation 3.2T switching capacity.

  Qisda began promoting 1.6T and 800-gigabit-per-second (Gbps) silicon photonics optical transceiver modules in 2026 and expects those products to contribute revenue starting in Q4 2027, Li said.

  ## 2027 business outlook

  CEO Ke projected high growth in 2027 for both the Intelligent Solutions Business Group and the networking unit, with stable growth for the medical business group. New products within the commercial and information segments, including automated guided vehicles (AGVs), drones, and quadruped robots, are expected to generate results in 2027, Ke said.

  The company said medical displays, smart fish-detection systems, and semiconductor materials are progressing through certification, mass production, and initial shipment phases.

  The Q2 2027 volume ramp represents Qisda's shift from current small-batch AI server sampling to commercial-scale production. The company set 2026 as the year it changed strategic direction to strengthen AI and semiconductor positioning, according to Chairman Chen. The most distant revenue milestone in its current forward guidance is silicon photonics optical transceivers, projected to begin contributing in Q4 2027.
body_zh: |
  台灣電子集團佳世達科技高階主管於11月10日法人說明會上表示，AI伺服器與每秒1.6太位元（Tbps）交換器出貨量將自2027年第二季起大幅擴增，2027年AI伺服器營收目標較2026年成長至少五倍。

  ## OCP訂單與放量時程

  執行長柯淑芬表示，佳世達出席了2026年10月舉辦的開放運算計畫（OCP）高峰會，完整展示AI伺服器產品線，並取得AI伺服器與1.6T交換器訂單。柯淑芬指出，前述產品預計於2026年底前進行小批量出貨，並自2027年第二季起進入量產階段。

  佳世達的AI基礎建設解決方案涵蓋AI伺服器、1.6T高速交換器、先進液冷技術及機櫃整合，以可擴展的「AI Rack-Scale全機架方案」形式提供。公司表示，1.6T交換器的交付對象主要為北美資料中心。

  ## 第四季營收展望

  董事長陳其宏表示，預期2026年第四季營收與第三季相比大致持平。陳其宏指出，外界因美國期中選舉而對AI資料中心發展產生疑慮，僅反映短期政治因素。他並補充，適度的放緩或許能讓供應鏈獲得喘息空間，回歸更健全的體質；集團將持續加速AI布局，因為AI的長期趨勢依然完整。

  ## 液冷技術

  智慧解決方案事業群總經理曾文星表示，旗下以啟揚為核心的運算力團隊已取得多項新伺服器專案。曾文星說，佳世達已開發出雙向液冷解決方案，預計於2026年第四季進行小量生產，實際出貨時程則視客戶排程而定。

  ## 網路與矽光子

  網路通訊事業群總經理李良祥表示，網路部門於2026年第四季可望維持兩位數的季增率與年增率成長。李良祥指出，1.6T交換器預計於2026年12月底前開始交貨，公司並持續投入下一代3.2T交換容量的研發。

  佳世達自2026年起積極推廣1.6T與每秒800Gbps矽光子光收發模組，李良祥表示，預計上述產品將自2027年第四季起開始貢獻營收。

  ## 2027年業務展望

  執行長柯淑芬預估，智慧解決方案事業群與網路部門在2027年均可望實現高度成長，醫療事業群則維持穩定成長。商業與資訊部門的新產品，包括自動導引車（AGV）、無人機及四足機器人，預計在2027年開始展現成績，柯淑芬說。

  公司表示，醫療顯示器、智慧魚類偵測系統及半導體材料等業務，目前正分別處於認證、量產及初始出貨等不同階段。

  2027年第二季的放量計畫，代表佳世達正式從目前AI伺服器小批量試樣階段邁向商業規模量產。董事長陳其宏表示，公司已將2026年定位為策略轉型年，致力強化AI與半導體的布局。在目前前瞻性業務指引中，最遠期的營收里程碑為矽光子光收發器，預計自2027年第四季起開始貢獻營收。
