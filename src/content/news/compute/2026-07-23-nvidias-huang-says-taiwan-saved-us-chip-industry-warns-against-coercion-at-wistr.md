body_en: |
  NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang said Taiwan saved the American semiconductor and computer industry and that the Taiwan-US technology partnership rests on trust and mutual interest rather than coercion, while also warning that AI chip supply remains severely constrained and is growing at roughly 25 percent annually, remarks he delivered July 21 at the opening ceremony of contract manufacturer Wistron's new factory in Dallas, Texas.

  Huang made the comments in conversations with business and political figures on the sidelines of the Wistron opening.

  ## Taiwan's role in US semiconductor history

  Huang said Washington and US authorities must recognize that Taiwan, not the United States, saved the American semiconductor technology and computer industries. Without Taiwan's support, he said, the US semiconductor industry would likely have been taken over entirely by Japan.

  "Taiwan saved America's semiconductor technology industry and saved America's computer industry, not the other way around," Huang said. He added that establishing partnerships with Taiwan and Taiwanese companies is essential for industrializing the US high-tech sector.

  On the nature of the cooperation, Huang said Taiwanese companies are investing in the United States based on partnership, strategic wisdom, and their own judgment — not under pressure. "I don't think a gun is necessary to point at Taiwan's head," he said, adding that the relationship is grounded in trust. "The partnership is super good."

  ## AI chip demand and the data center shift

  Huang said AI chip supply is far short of demand and growing at approximately 25 percent per year. He attributed the gap to a fundamental change in how data centers function: traditional facilities stored pre-recorded content — photos, videos, and articles — and retrieved it when users requested it. Generative AI models have replaced that retrieve-and-display model with real-time content generation, requiring infrastructure to answer user queries by searching, reading, and producing new outputs such as articles, images, or tables.

  As a result, Huang said, AI data centers now require at least 10 times the chip volume of conventional data centers. He said the world needs large numbers of supercomputing platforms installed at scale to drive AI intelligence infrastructure.

  To illustrate how AI will transform professional services, Huang used the legal sector as an example. Today, a law firm's capital equipment expenditure amounts to little more than food — lawyers converting calories into legal intelligence, in his framing. In an AI-enabled future, legal firms will require energy and hardware investment to run AI models, shifting the industry's cost structure toward compute and infrastructure.

  ## Jobs and industry diffusion

  Huang said AI is already generating employment in manufacturing, construction, and energy sectors tied to AI infrastructure build-out. The next layer of economic activity will be AI model development, and the most consequential layer, he said, will be end-use applications — the spread of AI into healthcare, transportation, manufacturing, finance, education, and scientific research. He said every industry, every person, and every country will need AI intelligence, and that it cannot be outsourced.

  ## Market context

  The remarks came as AI-related chip stocks had pulled back sharply following renewed market questions about the sector's longer-term outlook, according to UDN Money. Institutional investors cited by that outlet said Huang's comments were expected to help restore market confidence. UDN Money identified TSMC and ASE Technology Group as major Taiwanese beneficiaries of the AI chip demand surge.

  輝達（NVIDIA）執行長黃仁勳7月21日在德州達拉斯出席代工製造商緯創新廠開幕典禮時表示，台灣救了美國半導體與電腦產業，台美科技夥伴關係奠基於信任與互利而非脅迫，並同時示警，AI晶片供給仍嚴重不足，每年成長幅度約達25%。

  黃仁勳是在緯創開幕典禮場邊與商界及政界人士對談時發表上述言論。

  ## 台灣在美國半導體史上扮演的角色

  黃仁勳表示，華盛頓及美國當局必須認清，拯救美國半導體技術與電腦產業的是台灣，而非美國自身。他說，若無台灣的支持，美國半導體產業很可能早已全面落入日本之手。

  「台灣救了美國的半導體技術產業，也救了美國的電腦產業，而非反之，」黃仁勳說。他並補充，與台灣及台灣企業建立夥伴關係，對美國高科技產業的工業化至關重要。

  談及合作本質，黃仁勳說，台灣企業赴美投資，是基於夥伴情誼、策略眼光與自身判斷，而非迫於壓力。「我不認為有必要把槍頂在台灣的腦袋上，」他說，並強調這段關係奠基於信任。「這個夥伴關係非常好。」

  ## AI晶片需求與資料中心的轉型

  黃仁勳表示，AI晶片供給遠遠追不上需求，每年成長幅度約達25%。他將這一缺口歸因於資料中心運作模式的根本改變：傳統設施儲存預先錄製的內容——照片、影片與文章——並在使用者要求時加以調取。生成式AI模型已取代這種「調取再顯示」的模式，轉為即時生成內容，基礎設施必須透過搜尋、閱讀、產出全新內容（如文章、圖片或表格）的方式，來回應使用者的查詢需求。

  因此，黃仁勳說，AI資料中心所需的晶片數量至少是傳統資料中心的10倍。他表示，全球需要大量超級運算平台大規模部署，以推動AI智慧基礎設施的建立。

  為說明AI將如何改造專業服務業，黃仁勳以法律業為例。時至今日，律師事務所的資本設備支出幾乎只剩餐費——律師將攝取的卡路里轉化為法律智識，用他的話來說。在AI普及的未來，法律事務所將需要投入能源與硬體資源來運行AI模型，使整個產業的成本結構轉向運算與基礎設施。

  ## 就業與產業擴散

  黃仁勳表示，AI已在製造業、營建業及能源業等與AI基礎設施建設相關的領域創造就業機會。下一層次的經濟活動將是AI模型開發，而最具關鍵意義的一環，他說，是終端應用的普及——AI深入醫療、交通、製造、金融、教育與科學研究等各領域。他表示，每個產業、每個人、每個國家都將需要AI智慧，且這是無法外包的。

  ## 市場背景

  據聯合新聞網財經頻道報導，上述言論發表之際，AI相關晶片股甫因市場對該產業長期展望的疑慮重燃而大幅回落。該媒體援引法人投資機構指出，黃仁勳的談話預計有助於提振市場信心。聯合新聞網財經頻道並點名台積電（TSMC）及日月光投控（ASE Technology Group）為此波AI晶片需求浪潮的主要台灣受惠企業。
