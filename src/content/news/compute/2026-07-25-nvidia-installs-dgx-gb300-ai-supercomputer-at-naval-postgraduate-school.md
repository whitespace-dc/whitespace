body_en: |
  Nvidia has commissioned a DGX GB300 AI supercomputer at the Naval Postgraduate School (NPS) in Monterey, California, giving the U.S. military's graduate university on-premises access to large-scale AI computing for the first time. The system is now available to more than 1,500 in-resident students and 600 faculty for model training, large-scale simulation, and applied research.

  Nvidia founder and Chief Executive Officer Jensen Huang attended the commissioning ceremony on campus. Admiral Samuel Paparo, Commander of the U.S. Pacific Command, also spoke at the event.

  ## What the system provides

  The DGX GB300 — Nvidia's current top-tier AI supercomputer platform — allows users to train foundation models, meaning large general-purpose AI systems, in-house rather than relying on external computing resources. It also supports inference workloads, where a trained model is run to generate outputs, and large-scale simulations.

  NPS and Nvidia have identified several areas of intended use: weather prediction, cybersecurity, disaster resilience, and response planning. Researchers at the school are already working on ocean condition modelling, atmospheric prediction, and digital twins — computational models that replicate real physical environments — and the new system will support those workloads on campus.

  Through a separate partnership with MITRE, a federally funded research organization, NPS has developed a simulation framework built on Nvidia Omniverse libraries. That framework is designed to model navigation and decision-making under uncertain real-world conditions.

  ## Broader collaboration with Nvidia

  The DGX GB300 installation is one part of a wider relationship between Nvidia and NPS. The school has also established an Nvidia AI Technology Centre on its Monterey campus, which serves as a hub for AI research and graduate instruction, with the new supercomputer at its center.

  Nvidia has extended the collaboration through its Deep Learning Institute, which is providing instructor toolkits to NPS faculty so that AI methods can be integrated into graduate teaching across departments, not only in specialist technical programmes.

  ## Infrastructure partners

  Three hardware and infrastructure companies supported the installation alongside Nvidia.

  - **DDN** supplied data infrastructure for the access, management, and protection of research data used in AI workloads.
  - **VAST Data** provided a storage and data management platform designed to operate across edge, core, and cloud environments.
  - **Vertiv** supplied racks, cooling, and power infrastructure, and handled installation, testing, and commissioning.

  ## What military leaders said

  Paparo framed the investment in AI computing as a shift in how officers must be prepared for command. "As we modernize our technology, we must also modernize how we educate our leaders," he said. "Access to advanced computing capability means NPS students and faculty understand the opportunities and responsibilities that come with these technologies."

  He added that future command environments will be defined by faster information flows and compressed decision timelines. "Many of you will command in AI-enabled environments. Information will move at lightning speeds, compressing response times," Paparo said. "Our advantage will come from leaders, like you, who can use the technology to see, understand, decide and act faster while exercising the judgment, experience and leadership that machines cannot provide."

  Huang described AI computing as a practical tool that should be used broadly rather than confined to computer science specialists. "The most important advice that I would give to someone is to engage the technology," he said. "It's not as hard as you think it is."

  ## About the Naval Postgraduate School

  NPS is a graduate university that educates active-duty military officers and international partner-nation students in fields including space operations, ocean science, cybersecurity, and operations research. The school runs research programmes in autonomy, ocean science, and operations planning, including hackathons and projects that require substantial computing resources to scale from prototype to simulation.

  ## Significance

  The installation marks a direct move by the U.S. military to put frontier AI computing infrastructure inside a graduate education institution rather than relying on access to outside resources. Placing a system of this class on campus allows NPS to run the full cycle of AI development — from data management through model training to simulation — without external dependencies. Nvidia's simultaneous investment in faculty instruction through the Deep Learning Institute suggests the intent is to embed AI capability into the curriculum broadly, not only to support dedicated research teams.

  NPS plans to use the system for graduate education and applied research tied to both military operational needs and scientific programmes including ocean and atmospheric modelling.

  輝達已在加州蒙特雷的海軍研究生院（Naval Postgraduate School，NPS）正式啟用DGX GB300人工智慧超級電腦，這是美軍研究生大學首度在校園內獲得大規模AI運算資源。系統現已開放給逾1,500名在校學生及600名教職員使用，支援模型訓練、大規模模擬演算及應用研究。

  輝達創辦人暨執行長黃仁勳出席了在校園內舉行的啟用典禮。美國太平洋司令部司令薩繆爾·帕帕羅上將也在典禮上致詞。

  ## 系統提供的功能

  DGX GB300是輝達目前最頂級的AI超級電腦平台，讓使用者得以在院內自行訓練基礎模型——亦即大型通用型AI系統——無需仰賴外部運算資源。系統也支援推論工作負載，即執行訓練完成的模型以產生輸出結果，並能承擔大規模模擬運算任務。

  NPS與輝達已確定數個預期應用領域：天氣預測、網路安全、災害韌性及應變規劃。校內研究人員目前已在推動海洋狀況建模、大氣預測及數位孿生等研究——數位孿生是能精確複製真實物理環境的運算模型——新系統將在校園內直接支援上述工作負載。

  透過與聯邦資助研究機構MITRE另行建立的合作關係，NPS已開發出一套以輝達Omniverse函式庫為基礎的模擬框架，專門用來模擬在現實世界不確定條件下的導航與決策行為。

  ## 與輝達的更廣泛合作

  DGX GB300的部署只是輝達與NPS更廣泛合作關係的一環。該校也已在蒙特雷校區成立輝達人工智慧技術中心，作為AI研究與研究所教學的重要樞紐，並以這台新超級電腦為核心。

  輝達更透過旗下的深度學習學院（Deep Learning Institute）延伸雙方合作，為NPS教職員提供教師工具包，讓AI方法得以融入各系所的研究所課程，而不僅限於專業技術學程。

  ## 基礎設施合作夥伴

  三家硬體與基礎設施廠商共同支援了本次系統建置。

  - **DDN** 提供資料基礎設施，負責AI工作負載所需研究資料的存取、管理與保護。
  - **VAST Data** 提供儲存與資料管理平台，設計上可跨邊緣、核心與雲端環境運作。
  - **Vertiv** 提供機架、冷卻及電力基礎設施，並負責安裝、測試與啟用工程。

  ## 軍事領導人的發言

  帕帕羅上將將這項AI運算投資定位為軍官培育方式的根本轉變。他表示：「我們在技術現代化的同時，也必須現代化我們培育領導人才的方式。獲得先進運算能力，意味著NPS的學生與教職員能夠理解這些技術所帶來的機會與責任。」

  他進一步指出，未來的指揮環境將由更快速的資訊流動與更緊縮的決策時程所定義。「你們許多人將在AI賦能的環境中指揮作戰。資訊將以閃電般的速度流動，壓縮應變時間，」帕帕羅說。「我們的優勢將來自於像你們這樣的領導人——能夠運用技術，更快地看清局勢、理解情境、做出決策、採取行動，同時發揮機器所無法提供的判斷力、經驗與領導力。」

  黃仁勳則將AI運算定位為一種實用工具，強調應廣泛應用，而非侷限於電腦科學專家。他說：「我最想給人的建議，就是去接觸這項技術。它並不像你想像的那麼難。」

  ## 關於海軍研究生院

  NPS是一所研究所層級的大學，專門培育現役軍官及來自夥伴國的學生，涵蓋太空作戰、海洋科學、網路安全及作戰研究等領域。該校設有自主系統、海洋科學及作戰規劃等研究計畫，包含黑客松及各類專案，這些工作從原型擴展至模擬階段時，均需要大量運算資源。

  ## 意義

  此次部署標誌著美軍直接將前沿AI運算基礎設施引入一所研究所教育機構，而非繼續仰賴外部資源。在校園內配備這個等級的系統，讓NPS得以在院內完整執行AI開發的整個周期——從資料管理、模型訓練乃至模擬運算——不再有外部依賴。輝達同步透過深度學習學院投資教職員培訓，顯示其意圖是將AI能力廣泛嵌入課程，而不僅僅是支援特定研究團隊。

  NPS計畫將這套系統用於研究所教育及應用研究，涵蓋軍事作戰需求，以及海洋與大氣建模等科學計畫。
