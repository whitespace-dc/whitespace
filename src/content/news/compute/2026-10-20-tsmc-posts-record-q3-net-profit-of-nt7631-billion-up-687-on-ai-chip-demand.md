body_en: |
  Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC), the world's largest contract chipmaker, reported third-quarter net profit of NT$763.1 billion (S$30.2 billion) on Oct. 15, a 68.7% jump from the same period a year earlier and the company's sixth straight record quarterly result.

  The figure beat analyst estimates of NT$739.5 billion and surpassed the company's previous quarterly record of NT$706.6 billion, set in the April-to-June period. Quarterly revenue rose 50.9% to NT$1.5 trillion.

  ## Demand and technology ramp

  Chief executive C.C. Wei told an earnings call that demand for TSMC's advanced chips remains "extremely robust," citing a steep ramp-up of 2-nanometer (2nm) process technology — one of the most advanced chip manufacturing nodes commercially available. Wei said the company is trying to "do everything we can, whenever we can, as fast as we can" to meet customer demand, adding: "We have no intention to leave food on the table for others, and we want to capture every piece of business possible."

  TSMC manufactures chips for Nvidia, Apple, AMD, and Broadcom, positioning it, in the words of Zavier Wong, market analyst at eToro, at "the very centre of the AI ecosystem." Wong said that because "every leading AI chip still runs through its factories," TSMC's quarterly results function as a broad health check on the global AI hardware build-out.

  ## US expansion under evaluation

  Wei said the company is exploring potential manufacturing sites "outside Arizona" in the United States and has dispatched an evaluation team to Texas, though he stopped short of providing further details, saying discussions are ongoing. TSMC is already building chip fabrication plants in Arizona as part of a broader push to expand capacity in the US.

  ## Caution on 2027 outlook

  Despite the record result, Wei said the company would be "prudent" in its business planning for 2027, citing rising component prices and broader economic uncertainties.

  ## Investors look past headlines

  TSMC's share price has risen nearly 60% in 2026, which analysts say raises the bar for future earnings announcements. Wong said record profits have become "the new normal" for leading chipmakers, and that investors increasingly "skip straight past the headline numbers and pick through the guidance and the fine print for any warning signs."

  The dynamic was illustrated by the experience of South Korea's Samsung Electronics, which last week forecast a more than 780% leap in third-quarter operating profit from a year earlier — yet saw its shares fall because the forecast missed market expectations.

  Wong also noted a potential demand-side risk: if major AI laboratory customers were to slow spending — due to funding costs or a deliberate moderation of model development, which some voices have called for in recent weeks — chipmakers would "feel it quickly."

  ## Significance for the industry

  TSMC's results confirm that AI-driven semiconductor demand remains the primary growth engine for advanced chip manufacturing. The company's evaluation of Texas as an additional US manufacturing site suggests the geographic diversification of leading-edge chip production is continuing to expand beyond Arizona, though specific commitments have not yet been announced.

  台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司（台積電）於10月15日公布，全球最大晶圓代工廠在2026年第三季繳出淨利新台幣7,631億元（約新加坡幣302億元）的亮眼成績，年增68.7%，連續第六季刷新歷史新高。

  此數字不僅超越分析師平均預估的新台幣7,395億元，更超越前一個歷史紀錄——今年第二季（4月至6月）所創下的7,066億元。本季營收則年增50.9%至新台幣1.5兆元。

  ## 需求與技術爬坡

  執行長魏哲家在法人說明會上表示，台積電先進晶片的需求依然「極為強勁」，並特別提及2奈米（2nm）製程技術正快速爬坡量產——這是目前商業化最先進的晶片製造節點之一。魏哲家表示，公司正竭盡所能、爭分奪秒地滿足客戶需求，並說：「我們無意將到手的商機拱手相讓，我們要把握每一筆可能的訂單。」

  台積電的客戶涵蓋輝達、蘋果、超微（AMD）及博通，使其——如eToro市場分析師Zavier Wong所言——處於「AI生態系的絕對核心」。Wong指出，由於「每一顆頂尖AI晶片仍須經過台積電的廠房生產」，台積電的季度財報實際上已成為全球AI硬體建置熱潮整體健康狀況的晴雨表。

  ## 評估美國擴廠可能性

  魏哲家表示，公司正在評估美國亞利桑那州「以外」的潛在製造基地，並已派遣評估團隊前往德克薩斯州，惟他未進一步透露細節，表示相關討論目前仍在進行中。台積電目前已在亞利桑那州興建晶圓廠，作為擴大美國產能整體布局的一環。

  ## 對2027年展望審慎

  儘管本季創下歷史新高，魏哲家表示，公司在規劃2027年業務時將保持「審慎」態度，原因包括零組件價格上漲以及整體經濟前景的不確定性。

  ## 投資人著眼前瞻指引

  台積電股價在2026年已累計上漲近60%，分析師指出，這使市場對未來財報的預期門檻愈墊愈高。Wong表示，不斷刷新歷史紀錄的獲利已成為頂尖晶片商的「新常態」，投資人愈來愈傾向「略過頭條數字，直接深挖前景指引和細節，尋找任何潛藏的警示訊號」。

  韓國三星電子的遭遇便印證了這一動態——三星上週預告第三季營業利益將較前一年同期大幅躍升逾780%，股價卻應聲走跌，原因在於該預測仍不及市場預期。

  Wong也提醒，需求面存在潛在風險：若主要AI實驗室客戶因資金成本升高，或受近幾週已有部分聲音呼籲的刻意放緩模型開發所影響而縮減支出，晶片製造商將「迅速感受到衝擊」。

  ## 對產業的意義

  台積電本季財報確認，AI驅動的半導體需求仍是先進晶片製造的主要成長引擎。公司針對德克薩斯州作為美國額外製造基地的評估，顯示頂尖製程晶片生產的地理分散趨勢正持續向亞利桑那州以外延伸，惟目前尚未宣布任何具體承諾。
