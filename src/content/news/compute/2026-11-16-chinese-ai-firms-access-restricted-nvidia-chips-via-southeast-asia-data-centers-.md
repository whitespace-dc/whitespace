body_en: |
  A romantic scandal involving former Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang and a Chinese executive at a Singapore-based company that the United States suspects of involvement in artificial intelligence chip smuggling has drawn fresh attention to the pathways through which Chinese firms continue to obtain advanced Nvidia processors in spite of American export controls.

  Chinese AI startups Moonshot AI, Z.ai, and DeepSeek have continued to release high-performance models that compete with US counterparts even as Washington restricts the export of top-tier Nvidia graphics processing units (GPUs) — the chips that form the computational backbone of AI model training. "Chinese vendors are still using Nvidia's advanced chips to train their models," Lian Jye Su, chief analyst at market research firm Omdia, told AFP.

  ## Southeast Asian data centers as a workaround

  The most established route for Chinese firms to access restricted chips involves renting computing capacity at data centers in third countries, particularly across Southeast Asia, according to Stephen Wu, founder of investment fund Carthage Capital. "US export controls limit where chips are shipped, not who uses them," Wu told AFP.

  Wu described a scenario in which Nvidia GPUs physically located in Johor, Malaysia, remain accessible to customers in Shenzhen, China, without breaching US export rules, since the hardware was never shipped to China. White House adviser Michael Kratsios said earlier this year that Moonshot AI had accessed top-tier Nvidia chips hosted in Thailand.

  ## Smuggling allegations

  Physical smuggling has also been alleged. Technology publication The Information reported in December 2025 that DeepSeek developed a new model using thousands of Nvidia chips that had been disassembled abroad before being smuggled into China. DeepSeek did not respond to requests for comment. Nvidia denied the allegation, saying it found no evidence of smuggling and describing such an operation as highly unlikely.

  ## Why Nvidia chips remain in demand

  Nvidia's GPUs were originally designed to render three-dimensional video game graphics. They became central to AI development because of their capacity to process large volumes of digital data simultaneously. Since the launch of ChatGPT in 2022 triggered a global wave of AI investment, demand for Nvidia processors has pushed the company's market capitalization to $4.5 trillion, placing it alongside Apple as one of the two most valuable companies in the world, according to the source.

  Washington has banned exports of Nvidia's most advanced Blackwell and Rubin series GPUs to China, citing military security concerns and the goal of maintaining the competitive edge of domestic AI laboratories. The US relaxed restrictions this year on the H200 series under a condition requiring that the United States receive 25 percent of sales proceeds, but complex approval processes have kept shipments difficult in practice.

  ## What this means for US export policy

  The continued ability of Chinese AI laboratories to train frontier models — through legal remote-compute arrangements in Southeast Asia and alleged physical smuggling — indicates that US export restrictions have constrained but not severed Chinese firms' access to the advanced compute required for state-of-the-art AI development. The legal gap Wu identified, between where chips are physically placed and who can access them remotely, remains unaddressed by current export control rules.

  The revenue-share condition attached to relaxed H200 exports represents the US government's most recent attempt to retain financial leverage over chip access while permitting some trade to continue.

  輝達（Nvidia）前執行長黃仁勳捲入一場情感醜聞——對象是一名任職於新加坡企業的中國高階主管，而該公司正遭美方懷疑涉嫌走私人工智慧晶片——此事已重新引發外界對中國企業持續取得輝達高階處理器管道的關注，儘管美國出口管制持續收緊。

  中國AI新創業者月之暗面（Moonshot AI）、Z.ai與深度求索（DeepSeek），持續推出可與美國同業一較高下的高效能模型；與此同時，華盛頓已限制頂級輝達繪圖處理器（GPU）——也就是構成AI模型訓練運算骨幹的晶片——的出口。市場研究公司Omdia首席分析師蘇連杰（Lian Jye Su）接受法新社（AFP）訪問時表示：「中國廠商仍在使用輝達的高階晶片訓練模型。」

  ## 東南亞資料中心作為繞道途徑

  根據投資基金迦太基資本（Carthage Capital）創辦人史蒂芬．吳（Stephen Wu）的說法，中國企業取得限售晶片最成熟的途徑，是在第三國——尤其是東南亞各地——的資料中心租用算力。史蒂芬．吳接受法新社訪問時指出：「美國出口管制限制的是晶片的運送目的地，而非使用者是誰。」

  他描述了這樣一個情境：實體放置於馬來西亞柔佛（Johor）的輝達GPU，仍可供中國深圳的客戶遠端存取，且不違反美國出口規定，因為相關硬體從未實際運抵中國境內。白宮顧問麥可．克拉措斯（Michael Kratsios）今年稍早曾表示，月之暗面曾存取託管於泰國的頂級輝達晶片。

  ## 走私指控

  此外，實體走私的指控同樣存在。科技媒體《The Information》於2025年12月報導，深度求索曾利用數千片在境外拆解後走私入境中國的輝達晶片，開發出一款新模型。深度求索未回應置評請求。輝達則否認上述指控，表示未發現任何走私證據，並形容此類行動「極不可能發生」。

  ## 輝達晶片需求居高不下的原因

  輝達的GPU最初是為了渲染三維電玩遊戲畫面而設計，後來因具備同時處理海量數位資料的能力，逐漸成為AI開發的核心元件。自2022年ChatGPT問世掀起全球AI投資熱潮以來，對輝達處理器的需求急速攀升，推動該公司市值達到4.5兆美元，與蘋果（Apple）並列全球最有價值的兩家企業之一。

  華盛頓已以軍事安全疑慮及維護本土AI實驗室競爭優勢為由，禁止輝達最先進的Blackwell與Rubin系列GPU出口至中國。美國今年針對H200系列放寬了部分限制，條件是美國須取得25%的銷售收益，但繁複的審批程序使實際出貨至今仍困難重重。

  ## 對美國出口政策的意涵

  中國AI實驗室持續透過在東南亞合法租用遠端算力的安排，以及據稱存在的實體走私行為，訓練前沿模型——這顯示美國出口管制雖已對中國企業取得尖端運算資源造成限制，卻尚未將其管道完全切斷。史蒂芬．吳所指出的法律漏洞——晶片實體所在地與遠端使用者所在地之間的落差——在現行出口管制規定下，仍未獲得正面回應。

  針對H200出口放寬所附加的收益分成條件，代表美國政府迄今最新一次嘗試：在允許部分貿易繼續進行的同時，對晶片存取管道保有財務上的影響力。
