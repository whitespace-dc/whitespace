body_en: |
  Rackspace and AMD have signed an agreement to deploy 30 megawatts of AMD-based compute infrastructure across Rackspace's global data centre estate in a phased rollout beginning in late 2026 and continuing through 2028, the companies announced. The deal makes AMD a strategic technology partner in Rackspace's enterprise AI offering and formalises an earlier memorandum between the two companies.

  Thirty megawatts is a substantial dedicated footprint — enough to power a mid-sized data centre building at high-density server loads — and Rackspace said the capacity will be reserved entirely for AMD-based systems serving enterprise AI workloads. The primary target market is customers in regulated industries, with healthcare cited as an early area of demand.

  ## Hardware and architecture

  The agreement covers AMD Instinct graphics processing units (GPUs) — specialised chips designed for AI computation — including the MI355X and MI350P models, along with future successor products. The deal also includes AMD EPYC central processing units (CPUs), which handle general-purpose compute tasks. Together, those chips will sit inside what Rackspace calls an integrated Enterprise AI Cloud architecture, which the company said allows workloads to be assigned to different types of compute depending on their requirements.

  Dan McNamara, Senior Vice President and General Manager of Compute and Enterprise AI at AMD, said the combination of accelerated and general-purpose compute was central to what enterprises need as they move AI systems into production.

  "As enterprise AI evolves, customers need infrastructure that can deliver the right mix of accelerated and general-purpose compute for each workload," McNamara said. "By bringing together leadership AMD AI compute solutions and Rackspace's governed cloud operating model, we are helping regulated enterprises deploy high-performance AI infrastructure with the openness, scalability and accountability needed to run AI at enterprise scale."

  ## Services and go-to-market

  The agreement is intended to underpin four services the companies had previously outlined: Enterprise AI Cloud, Enterprise Inference Engine, Inference as a Service, and Bare Metal AMD Instinct. Those offerings are designed to span the stack from underlying hardware to managed inference — the process of running a trained AI model against real data to produce outputs.

  Rackspace positioned those services as an alternative to bare metal procurement, in which an enterprise buys or leases raw servers and takes on the work of integrating and operating them itself. Under the managed model, Rackspace assumes operational responsibility. Both companies said they would assign dedicated sales and marketing staff to pursue joint customer opportunities in regulated industries.

  ## Position in Rackspace's broader stack

  The AMD deal adds a silicon supplier to a wider stack Rackspace is assembling. The company has already announced partnerships with Palantir and Uniphore, which it said contribute data, inference and agent-based software layers to the overall offering. Rackspace said its aim is to offer a single AI stack for enterprises rather than requiring customers to integrate components from multiple vendors.

  Gajen Kandiah, Chief Executive Officer of Rackspace, framed the deal in terms of accountability — a theme directed at regulated-sector customers that face compliance and governance requirements.

  "Enterprises in regulated industries need AI infrastructure that is governed from the ground up, with one operator accountable for business outcomes, not a collection of vendors each owning a piece," Kandiah said. "This collaboration combines the right compute with the right operating model and delivers something the market hasn't offered before: a governed AI stack with one accountable partner from silicon to outcomes."

  ## Regulated demand and market context

  Rackspace said demand from regulated industries is being driven by a shift from isolated AI experiments toward agent-based workflows — automated systems in which AI models take sequences of actions — that are tied more closely to core business processes. Healthcare providers were described as among the early organisations seeking accelerated compute for clinical AI and large-scale inference tasks in sensitive operating environments.

  For AMD, the partnership is part of a broader effort to strengthen its position in enterprise AI infrastructure as customer focus moves beyond training large models and toward running mixed workloads in production at scale.

  The approach reflects a pattern visible across the infrastructure sector: vendors bundling hardware, software and operational management to capture a larger share of enterprise AI spending. Governance, auditability and defined operational responsibility are increasingly presented as commercial differentiators as AI deployments move from pilot projects into live business environments.

  At full build-out, the deployment will reach 30 MW of dedicated AMD compute distributed across Rackspace's global data centre portfolio, with the rollout scheduled to complete by 2028.

  Rackspace與AMD宣布簽署協議，將以分階段方式在Rackspace全球資料中心群部署30MW的AMD運算基礎設施，時程自2026年底啟動、延續至2028年。此次合作使AMD正式成為Rackspace企業AI方案的策略技術夥伴，同時也將兩家公司先前簽署的意向書轉化為正式協議。

  30MW是相當可觀的專屬運算規模——以高密度伺服器負載計算，足以供應一棟中型資料中心大樓的全部用電——Rackspace表示，這批容量將完全保留給服務企業AI工作負載的AMD系統使用。主要目標市場鎖定受監管產業的客戶，醫療保健被列為初期需求最為迫切的領域之一。

  ## 硬體架構

  本次協議涵蓋AMD Instinct繪圖處理器（GPU）——專為AI運算設計的特製晶片——包括MI355X與MI350P等型號及未來的後繼產品，同時也納入負責通用運算任務的AMD EPYC中央處理器（CPU）。上述晶片將整合於Rackspace所稱的「企業AI雲端整合架構」之中；Rackspace表示，此架構能依據各項工作負載的需求，將其分配至不同類型的運算資源。

  AMD運算暨企業AI事業資深副總裁暨總經理Dan McNamara表示，加速運算與通用運算的組合，正是企業將AI系統推進至正式生產環境時的核心需求所在。

  「隨著企業AI持續演進，客戶需要能夠為每項工作負載提供最適加速與通用運算組合的基礎設施，」McNamara說，「藉由整合AMD領先的AI算力解決方案與Rackspace的治理雲端營運模式，我們正協助受監管的企業以開放性、可擴展性與問責性，建置高效能AI基礎設施，在企業規模下運行AI。」

  ## 服務項目與市場策略

  此協議旨在為兩家公司先前共同規劃的四項服務提供支撐基礎：企業AI雲端（Enterprise AI Cloud）、企業推論引擎（Enterprise Inference Engine）、推論即服務（Inference as a Service），以及裸機AMD Instinct（Bare Metal AMD Instinct）。這些方案涵蓋從底層硬體到受管推論的完整服務堆疊——所謂「推論」，係指將訓練完成的AI模型應用於真實資料、以產生輸出結果的過程。

  Rackspace將上述服務定位為裸機採購模式的替代選擇——裸機採購係指企業自行購買或租賃原始伺服器，並獨力承擔整合與運維的全部工作；在Rackspace的受管模式下，則由Rackspace承擔所有營運責任。兩家公司表示，將各自指派專屬銷售與行銷人員，攜手爭取受監管產業的客戶商機。

  ## 在Rackspace整體架構中的定位

  AMD協議為Rackspace正在整合建構的更大型技術堆疊增添了一位晶片供應商。Rackspace此前已宣布與Palantir及Uniphore達成合作，並表示兩者分別為整體方案提供資料、推論與代理人型（agent-based）軟體層。Rackspace表示，其目標是為企業客戶提供單一整合的AI技術堆疊，讓客戶無需自行整合來自多家廠商的各式元件。

  Rackspace執行長Gajen Kandiah以「問責制」為核心主軸詮釋此次合作——這一訴求正是針對面臨合規與治理要求的受監管產業客戶所傳達的核心理念。

  「受監管產業的企業需要從根基即內建治理機制的AI基礎設施，由單一營運商對業務成果負責，而非仰賴各自掌管一部分的多家廠商拼湊而成，」Kandiah說，「這次合作結合了正確的算力與正確的營運模式，並實現了市場迄今尚未提供的方案：一個由單一問責夥伴從晶片到成果全程負責的受治理AI技術堆疊。」

  ## 受監管需求與市場背景

  Rackspace表示，受監管產業需求的成長，源自於AI應用正從孤立的實驗性嘗試，轉向與核心業務流程更緊密整合的代理人型工作流程——即由AI模型依序執行一系列動作的自動化系統。醫療保健業者被列為最早尋求加速運算能力的客戶群之一，應用場景涵蓋臨床AI，以及在敏感營運環境中執行的大規模推論任務。

  對AMD而言，此次合作是其在企業AI基礎設施領域強化競爭地位的整體布局之一環——隨著客戶關注重心逐漸從訓練大型模型，轉移至在正式生產環境中大規模運行混合工作負載，AMD正積極鞏固其市場地位。

  這一策略反映出基礎設施產業的普遍趨勢：各廠商將硬體、軟體與營運管理服務捆綁銷售，以期在企業AI支出中攫取更大份額。隨著AI部署從試驗性專案進入實際業務環境，治理能力、可稽核性與明確的營運問責機制，正日益成為商業競爭中的重要差異化利器。

  至全面建置完成時，這批專屬AMD算力將達30MW，分散部署於Rackspace遍及全球的資料中心組合之中，整體建置工程預計於2028年全數完工。
