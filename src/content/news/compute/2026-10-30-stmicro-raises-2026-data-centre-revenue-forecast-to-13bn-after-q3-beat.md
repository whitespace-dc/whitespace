body_en: |
  STMicroelectronics raised its 2026 AI data centre revenue forecast to approximately $1.3 billion on October 29, up from a July projection of more than $1 billion, after third-quarter results beat analyst expectations on demand from data centres and satellite operators.

  The Franco-Italian chipmaker, which makes microcontrollers, sensors, and power semiconductors for the automotive, industrial, and consumer electronics markets, posted Q3 operating income of $429 million excluding one-off charges — nearly double the year-ago figure and above the $400.5 million consensus estimate compiled by financial data provider LSEG. The company also reiterated its 2027 data centre revenue target of well above $2 billion.

  ## Silicon photonics driving data centre demand

  Chief executive Jean-Marc Chery said data centre revenue growth was driven mainly by silicon photonics chips. Silicon photonics devices use light rather than electrical signals to move data between servers. Chery said in the earnings statement: "In an environment where demand remains well above supply, we continue to expand capacity."

  Chery identified data centre demand and low-Earth-orbit (LEO) satellite communications as company-specific drivers contributing to results, on top of what he described as a broader cyclical recovery in the semiconductor market.

  ## Q3 results and Q4 outlook

  Third-quarter revenue was $3.76 billion. For the fourth quarter, STMicro forecast revenue of $4.15 billion, plus or minus 3.5 percent — above the $4.10 billion analyst consensus and above the more than $4 billion the company had projected in July.

  Gross margin widened to 37.3 percent in Q3 from 33.2 percent a year earlier, helped by a higher proportion of sales from more profitable chips and lower costs tied to idle factory capacity. The company expects gross margin to reach approximately 39 percent in Q4.

  Higher sales of communication equipment, computer peripherals, and personal electronics also contributed to the earnings beat, the company said.

  ## Market reaction

  STMicro shares opened 3.7 percent lower on October 29 following the results but recovered to trade slightly positive by 0935 GMT, according to Reuters.

  The raised 2026 forecast and the reiterated 2027 target reflect management's stated position that demand exceeds current supply in the data centre segment, supporting continued capacity expansion. STMicro's 2027 data centre revenue target stands at well above $2 billion, compared with the newly raised 2026 forecast of approximately $1.3 billion.
body_zh: |
  意法半導體（STMicroelectronics）於10月29日將2026年AI資料中心營收預測上調至約13億美元，高於今年7月所提出的逾10億美元預測，原因是第三季業績超越分析師預期，受惠於資料中心業者及衛星營運商的強勁需求。

  這家法義合資晶片製造商主要生產供汽車、工業及消費性電子市場使用的微控制器、感測器及電源半導體。第三季不計一次性費用的營業利益達4.29億美元，幾乎是去年同期的兩倍，並高於金融數據供應商LSEG彙整的市場共識預估4.005億美元。公司同時重申，2027年資料中心營收目標將遠超20億美元。

  ## 矽光子帶動資料中心需求

  執行長Jean-Marc Chery表示，資料中心營收成長主要由矽光子晶片所驅動。矽光子元件以光訊號取代電訊號，在伺服器之間傳輸資料。Chery在財報聲明中指出：「在需求持續遠超供給的環境下，我們持續擴充產能。」

  Chery將資料中心需求及低軌道（LEO）衛星通訊列為推動業績的公司特有動能，並指出這是在半導體市場出現更廣泛景氣循環復甦的背景下，額外帶動業績的兩大驅動力。

  ## 第三季業績與第四季展望

  第三季營收為37.6億美元。展望第四季，意法半導體預測營收為41.5億美元，上下浮動幅度為3.5%，高於分析師共識預估的41億美元，也高於公司今年7月所提出的逾40億美元預測。

  第三季毛利率從去年同期的33.2%擴大至37.3%，主要受益於高利潤晶片銷售占比提升，以及產能閒置相關成本降低。公司預期第四季毛利率將達約39%。

  公司表示，通訊設備、電腦周邊產品及個人電子產品銷售增加，也是業績超越預期的因素之一。

  ## 市場反應

  根據路透社報導，意法半導體股價於10月29日業績公布後開盤下跌3.7%，但隨後回升，至格林威治標準時間09時35分時已轉為小幅上漲。

  2026年預測上調及2027年目標重申，反映出管理層明確表態：資料中心領域的需求超過現有供給，支撐公司持續擴充產能。意法半導體2027年資料中心營收目標為遠超20億美元，相較於此次最新上調後的2026年預測約13億美元，成長空間依然可觀。
