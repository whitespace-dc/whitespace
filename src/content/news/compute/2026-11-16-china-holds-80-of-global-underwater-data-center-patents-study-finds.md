body_en: |
  China holds 80 percent of global patents on underwater data centers (UDCs) — facilities that use seawater as a natural heat sink in place of conventional cooling systems — and has displaced the United States and European nations as the technology's leading innovator, according to a patent study published in the journal Ocean Information Technology and Applications in early November, as reported by the South China Morning Post on November 14.

  The findings emerge as surging demand for artificial intelligence computing has exposed three chronic problems with land-based data centers — power consumption, cooling costs, and land use — that Chinese researchers describe as unresolved. UDCs are identified in the study as a potential solution to all three.

  ## How the patent landscape shifted

  Researchers in the study reviewed global UDC patents filed between 2006 and June 2026, manually selecting 168 relevant samples. The data show a clear three-phase shift.

  Before 2014, annual filings totaled fewer than three worldwide. The field was led by US, Norwegian, and Canadian marine engineering companies and research institutes, whose work focused on pressure-resistant capsule design and underwater construction techniques.

  Between 2015 and 2020, filing volumes rose and priorities shifted toward capsule structure optimization, thermal management, and power supply systems — indicating a move from proving the concept to achieving long-term reliable operation. Microsoft's Project Natick, launched in 2018, deployed a data center on the seafloor off Scotland's coast; after two years of operation, its server failure rate was one-eighth that of comparable land-based hardware.

  From 2021 onward, China became the dominant filer. "Technological innovation has shifted from isolated verification to system integration and engineering implementation," the study's authors wrote. Chinese patents increasingly cover modular clustering, in-situ maintenance (repairs performed without surfacing the units), multi-energy integration, and intelligent operations — capabilities oriented toward large-scale commercial deployment.

  ## Who holds what

  Of the 168 sampled patents, China accounts for 80 percent. South Korea ranks second, with filings concentrated in capsule deployment and structural equipment. US patents address system architecture, power supply, and local data processing. Norwegian, British, and other European patents focus on subsea production control, power supply, and automated operations.

  The study concludes that Western nations have lost their leadership position in what it characterizes as a future category of green computing infrastructure.

  ## The efficiency case for underwater facilities

  Land-based data centers consume approximately 3 percent of global electricity, with a significant share spent on cooling. UDCs draw heat directly into surrounding seawater, sharply reducing both energy consumption and cooling-water demand while requiring no land footprint.

  ## China's first wind-integrated UDC

  The world's first underwater data center integrated with offshore wind power began operating at Shanghai Lingang at the start of this year, according to China Central Television's financial channel. Total investment is 1.6 billion yuan. The full planned capacity is 24 megawatts (MW); the Phase 1 demonstration unit is rated at 2.3 MW.

  The Phase 1 unit weighs 1,950 metric tons, sits in 10 meters of water, and contains four levels of server racks each approximately 160 square meters in area.

  Project leader Chen Xiyi calculated that if the 2.3 MW unit used conventional freshwater cooling, it would consume 40,000 metric tons of freshwater annually — equivalent to roughly 100 years of a typical household's supply. Instead it uses a passive refrigerant-circulation system that channels heat into seawater, reaching zero freshwater consumption.

  A 200 MW offshore wind farm sits 500 meters from the data center, connected by a photoelectric composite cable. Green electricity accounts for more than 95 percent of the facility's direct power supply. At full operational scale, the project is projected to save 61 million kilowatt-hours per year.

  The land-use reduction is equally significant: the 2.3 MW unit requires only 200 square meters of footprint, compared with approximately 2,000 square meters for an equivalent land-based installation, a reduction of more than 90 percent, according to the project team.

  ## Planned scale-up

  Phase 1's 2.3 MW is described by the design and operations team as "just the tip of the iceberg." The team is targeting single-capsule units rated at 5 to 7 MW and plans to deploy multiple capsules in parallel across seabed areas in the Yangtze River Delta, Pearl River Delta, and Bohai Rim regions, extending the offshore-wind-plus-submarine-computing model to additional sites along China's eastern coast.

  The study's authors project that driven by the digital economy and emissions reduction targets, China's UDC industry will move from nearshore deployments toward deeper water, and from standalone computing facilities toward nodes integrated into broader ocean-economy infrastructure. The integration of offshore wind generation with underwater data centers is identified as a primary future direction.
body_zh: |
  根據發表於《海洋資訊技術與應用》期刊的一項專利研究，中國目前持有全球水下資料中心（UDC）八成的相關專利——水下資料中心是一種以海水作為天然散熱媒介、取代傳統冷卻系統的設施——並已取代美國與歐洲各國，躍升為這項技術的主要創新引領者。該研究於11月初發表，《南華早報》於11月14日予以報導。

  此一研究結果浮現之際，人工智慧運算需求正急速攀升，陸地資料中心長期面臨的三大痼疾——耗電量、散熱成本及土地佔用——依然懸而未解，中國研究人員在研究中如此描述。水下資料中心在研究中被認定為同時解決上述三項挑戰的潛在方案。

  ## 專利版圖的結構性轉移

  研究人員針對2006年至2026年6月間申請的全球水下資料中心專利進行系統性審查，手動篩選出168件相關樣本。資料顯示，這段期間呈現出明顯的三階段演變。

  2014年以前，全球每年申請件數不足三件，市場由美國、挪威及加拿大的海洋工程公司與研究機構主導，研究重心集中於耐壓艙體設計及水下施工技術。

  2015年至2020年間，申請量開始攀升，技術重心也逐漸轉向艙體結構優化、熱管理與供電系統，顯示發展方向已從概念驗證邁向長期穩定運行。微軟於2018年啟動的「Natick計畫」（Project Natick）在蘇格蘭外海海床部署了一座資料中心；歷經兩年運作，其伺服器故障率僅為同等規格陸地設施的八分之一。

  自2021年起，中國躍升為主要申請國。研究作者寫道：「技術創新已從單點驗證轉向系統整合與工程落地。」中國專利的涵蓋範疇日益廣泛，包括模組化叢集、原位維護（即無須將設備浮出水面即可進行維修）、多能源整合及智慧化運維，均指向大規模商業化部署的能力建構。

  ## 各國專利分布

  在168件樣本專利中，中國佔八成。南韓排名第二，專利集中於艙體部署及結構設備。美國專利涵蓋系統架構、供電及本地資料處理。挪威、英國及其他歐洲國家的專利則聚焦於海底生產控制、供電系統及自動化運維。

  研究結論指出，西方國家已在這項被定性為未來綠色運算基礎設施的新興領域中，失去原本的領先地位。

  ## 水下設施的效能優勢

  陸地資料中心約消耗全球電力的3%，其中相當大比例用於散熱。水下資料中心可直接將熱能導入周圍海水，大幅降低能源消耗與冷卻用水需求，同時無需佔用任何陸地面積。

  ## 中國首座整合風電的水下資料中心

  全球首座整合離岸風電的水下資料中心已於今年年初在上海臨港正式啟用，據中央電視台財經頻道報導，總投資額為人民幣16億元。完整規劃裝置容量為24百萬瓦（MW）；第一期示範機組額定功率為2.3 MW。

  第一期機組重達1,950公噸，沉置於水深10公尺處，內部設有四層伺服器機架，每層面積約160平方公尺。

  專案負責人陳希義計算，若該2.3 MW機組改採傳統淡水冷卻，每年將消耗4萬公噸淡水，相當於一般家庭約100年的用水量。現行方案採用被動式冷媒循環系統，將熱能導入海水，實現零淡水消耗。

  距資料中心500公尺處設有一座裝置容量200 MW的離岸風場，以光電複合電纜相連。綠電占設施直接供電來源逾95%。待全面投入運行後，預計每年可節省6,100萬度電。

  土地使用方面同樣大幅改善：該2.3 MW機組僅需200平方公尺的占地面積，相比之下，同等規格的陸地設施約需2,000平方公尺，面積縮減逾九成，據專案團隊表示。

  ## 後續擴建規劃

  設計與運營團隊指出，第一期的2.3 MW「不過是冰山一角」。團隊目標是打造單艙額定功率達5至7 MW的機組，並計劃在長三角、珠三角及環渤海地區的海底並排部署多個艙體，將離岸風電結合海底運算的模式推廣至中國東部沿海更多據點。

  研究作者預測，在數位經濟與減碳目標的雙重驅動下，中國水下資料中心產業將從近岸部署走向深水區域，從獨立運算設施演進為整合進更廣泛海洋經濟基礎設施的節點。離岸風電與水下資料中心的整合，被認定為未來的主要發展方向。
