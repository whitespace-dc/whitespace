body_en: |
  Foxconn, the world's largest contract electronics manufacturer, reported third-quarter net profit of NT$77.5 billion (approximately S$3.02 billion), a 34% increase year-on-year that exceeded a consensus analyst estimate of NT$69.6 billion compiled by LSEG, the company said on Nov. 12.

  The Taiwan-based company, formally known as Hon Hai Precision Industry, also projected "high double digit growth" in AI rack shipments for 2027, maintaining its earlier forecast of "strong" revenue growth throughout 2026.

  ## Supply squeeze expected to outlast new capacity

  Despite the strong financial results, Foxconn chairman Young Liu cautioned that supply-side constraints across the AI and consumer electronics industries remain a material risk. Chips, memory, and several key components are currently tight, Liu said at an earnings conference, describing the situation as "a challenge facing the industry as a whole."

  Liu said new manufacturing capacity coming online should gradually ease bottlenecks, but added that overall demand is expected to continue exceeding supply in the coming year. "The tight supply-demand situation may persist for some time," he said.

  ## AI server and iPhone manufacturing context

  Foxconn serves as Nvidia's largest server manufacturer and Apple's primary iPhone assembler, positions that place it at the intersection of two of the most capacity-constrained segments in electronics. The company reported a 47% year-on-year jump in third-quarter revenue in October.

  Most iPhones Foxconn assembles for Apple are produced in China, but the company now manufactures the majority of units destined for the United States market in India. Foxconn is also constructing factories in Mexico and Texas to produce AI servers for Nvidia.

  ## Outlook and market performance

  Liu said the company remains "very confident" about its operations in 2027 and expects continued growth, while noting "several external variables worth continued attention" without specifying them.

  Foxconn shares closed 0.8% lower on Nov. 12 ahead of the earnings release, on a day when Taiwan's main stock index fell 2.6%. Year-to-date, Foxconn shares have risen approximately 4%, underperforming a 49% gain recorded by the broader Taiwan index over the same period.

  The persistence of supply tightness into 2027, as forecast by one of the industry's largest manufacturers, signals continued pressure on lead times and costs for data center operators and hyperscalers building out AI infrastructure. Liu's comments suggest that even as new semiconductor and component capacity is commissioned, demand growth in AI is expected to absorb it without fully relieving constraints in the near term.

  ## 供應吃緊態勢料將延續至新產能開出後

  儘管財務表現亮眼，鴻海董事長劉揚偉仍在法說會上警告，AI及消費性電子產業的供給面限制仍是重大風險。他表示，晶片、記憶體及多項關鍵零組件目前均供應吃緊，並形容這是「整個產業共同面臨的挑戰」。

  劉揚偉指出，陸續釋出的新製造產能應能逐步舒緩供應瓶頸，但他同時強調，整體需求預計在未來一年仍將持續超過供給。他說：「供需吃緊的局面可能還會持續一段時間。」

  ## AI伺服器與iPhone製造背景

  鴻海是輝達最大的伺服器製造商，也是蘋果iPhone的主要組裝廠，同時身處這兩個電子業供應最為緊張的領域，使其置身於供需矛盾的核心。該公司10月公布，第三季營收年增47%。

  鴻海為蘋果代工組裝的iPhone絕大多數在中國生產，但目前供應美國市場的機種已有多數改在印度製造。此外，鴻海也在墨西哥及美國德州興建工廠，專門為輝達生產AI伺服器。

  ## 展望與股市表現

  劉揚偉表示，公司對2027年的營運「非常有信心」，預期成長將持續，並提到有「若干值得持續關注的外部變數」，但未進一步說明具體為何。

  鴻海股票於11月12日財報公布前收跌0.8%，當天台灣加權股價指數下跌2.6%。今年以來，鴻海股價累計上漲約4%，表現遜於同期大盤約49%的漲幅。

  身為業界規模最大製造商之一的鴻海，預測供應吃緊態勢將延續至2027年，意味著正在擴建AI基礎設施的資料中心業者及超大規模雲端服務供應商，將持續承受交貨期拉長與成本攀升的壓力。劉揚偉的談話也暗示，即便新半導體及零組件產能陸續投產，AI端需求的增速也預計將快速消化這些新增供給，供應限制短期內仍難以完全解除。
