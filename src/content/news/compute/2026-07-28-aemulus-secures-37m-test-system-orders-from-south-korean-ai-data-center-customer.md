body_en: |
  Aemulus Holdings Bhd, a Malaysian designer and manufacturer of automated test equipment (ATE), has secured orders totaling US$3.7 million (RM15.11 million) from an unnamed customer in South Korea for test systems serving the data center and artificial intelligence market, the company disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia exchange filing on July 27.

  The orders were placed with Aemulus Corporation Sdn Bhd, the wholly-owned subsidiary through which Aemulus conducts its core operations. The customer's identity was not disclosed.

  ## Order timeline and revenue recognition

  Aemulus said deliveries will be made progressively across two financial years: FY2026, ending September 30, 2026, and FY2027, ending September 30, 2027. Revenue will be recognised on delivery, meaning it will accumulate in stages rather than as a lump sum. The company said the orders are expected to contribute positively to earnings in both periods.

  ## Company profile

  Aemulus is principally involved in the design and development of automated test equipment — machines used to verify the functionality and performance of semiconductors and electronic components — as well as test and measurement instruments and design consultancy services. ATE is a critical step in semiconductor manufacturing, where chips must be screened before deployment in end products.

  ## Market context

  The order targets the data center and AI market, sectors driving elevated demand for advanced semiconductors and, by extension, for the test equipment used to qualify those chips. South Korea is home to major semiconductor manufacturers, making it a significant geography for ATE suppliers.

  Aemulus shares closed 2.6% lower at 37.5 sen on Monday, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM252.2 million (approximately US$61.6 million at the exchange rate implied by the filing). The order value of US$3.7 million represents a material contract relative to that market cap.

  The company expects to begin recognising revenue from the orders before the end of its current financial year, with the remainder flowing through FY2027.

  ## Aemulus獲南韓AI與資料中心客戶370萬美元測試系統訂單

  馬來西亞自動測試設備（ATE）設計與製造商Aemulus Holdings Bhd於7月27日在馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）發布公告，披露公司已從一家未具名的南韓客戶取得總值370萬美元（約馬幣1,511萬令吉）的測試系統訂單，所涉市場為資料中心與人工智慧領域。

  此批訂單由Aemulus旗下全資子公司Aemulus Corporation Sdn Bhd承接，該子公司為集團核心業務的主要運營主體。公告中並未揭露客戶身分。

  ## 交貨時程與營收認列

  Aemulus表示，訂單將分兩個財政年度分批交貨：其一為截至2026年9月30日的2026財政年度（FY2026），其二為截至2027年9月30日的2027財政年度（FY2027）。營收將依交貨進度逐步認列，而非以一次性總額入帳。公司表示，該批訂單預計將對上述兩個財政年度的獲利帶來正面貢獻。

  ## 公司簡介

  Aemulus主要業務涵蓋自動測試設備的設計與研發，這類設備專門用於驗證半導體及電子元件的功能與效能，此外亦提供測試與量測儀器製造及設計顧問服務。自動測試設備是半導體製造流程中的關鍵環節，晶片在正式出貨至終端產品前，必須經過嚴格的篩選與測試程序。

  ## 市場背景

  此批訂單所瞄準的資料中心與AI市場，正是當前推動先進半導體需求大幅攀升的核心動力，連帶也拉抬了用於晶片認證的測試設備需求。南韓擁有多家全球重量級半導體製造商，對ATE供應商而言是不可忽視的重要市場。

  Aemulus股價週一收跌2.6%，報每股37.5仙，公司市值約為馬幣2億5,220萬令吉（依公告所揭示匯率換算，約當6,160萬美元）。相對於該市值規模，此次370萬美元的訂單金額具有一定的重要性。

  公司預計在本財政年度結束前即可開始認列部分訂單營收，其餘款項則將於2027財政年度陸續入帳。
