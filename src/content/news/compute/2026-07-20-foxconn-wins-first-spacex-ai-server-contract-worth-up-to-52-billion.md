body_en: |
  Foxconn has won its first AI server manufacturing contract from SpaceX, covering approximately 13,000 cabinets equipped with Nvidia GB300 processors and valued at an estimated $52 billion, Taiwan's Economic Daily News reported July 20. The award breaks a supply arrangement previously held exclusively by Dell and Super Micro.

  SpaceX, the space company led by Elon Musk, plans to procure the cabinets at a unit price of roughly $4 million each, placing the total at approximately $52 billion, or about NT$1.7 trillion, the report said. Shipments are scheduled to begin in the second half of 2026 and run through the first quarter of 2027.

  Foxconn had previously secured AI server contracts with major North American cloud service providers but lacked SpaceX as a customer. The report described the win as completing a key gap in Foxconn's portfolio of major cloud service provider clients.

  Foxconn declined to comment on the order, consistent with its standard practice on individual customers, but said its AI server business orders remain strong.

  ## Financial implications

  Foxconn Chairman Liu Young-way had previously projected that the group's global AI server market share would exceed 40% in 2026, with full-year AI cabinet shipments expected to grow multiple times year-over-year, increasing quarter by quarter through year-end. Analysts cited in the report noted that Liu's prior revenue guidance for the AI segment did not include the SpaceX contract, meaning the order represents additional upside beyond those earlier forecasts.

  Cloud and network products, led by AI servers, are the largest of Foxconn's four main product lines, and the company said the segment's share is expected to grow further.

  Foxconn posted first-quarter 2026 after-tax net profit of NT$49.919 billion, a record for the same period, up 10% quarter-on-quarter and 19% year-on-year, with earnings per share of NT$3.56. FII, Foxconn's listed mainland China subsidiary, separately pre-announced second-quarter net profit attributable to its parent in the range of RMB 12.8 billion to RMB 13.8 billion, representing year-on-year growth of 86% to 101%.

  ## SpaceX's compute build-out

  SpaceX has been expanding its internal computing capacity and revised its GB300 server cabinet order upward to approximately 13,000 units, the report said. Alongside its own build-out, SpaceX has been developing compute-rental services: the company recently held discussions to supply AI data center computing capacity to the U.S. Department of Defense to help the Pentagon run AI models, and has signed compute agreements with Anthropic and Google. SpaceX also plans to significantly expand its cloud computing business.

  ## Space data centers as a next objective

  Beyond ground-based servers, Foxconn and SpaceX are positioning their relationship to extend into space-based AI data centers, according to industry analysis in the report.

  Foxconn subsidiary Hongbai Technology (鴻佰科技) is planning to collaborate with space computing infrastructure company Ramon.Space to build a space data center, the report said. The project is intended to support earth observation, communications, government missions, and emerging space services.

  Industry analysis cited in the report attributed SpaceX's selection of Foxconn to the manufacturer's simultaneous expertise in AI server manufacturing and the space sector. Companies experienced in AI server assembly are described as lacking space technology know-how, while firms with space capabilities are described as unfamiliar with AI server production. Foxconn, as the world's largest electronics contract manufacturer with presence in both domains, is described in the report as Musk's natural first choice.

  Foxconn's space credentials include development of the "Pearl" low-earth orbit (LEO) satellite, which integrates Foxconn's own camera payload alongside other components. The satellite sector's core competency centers on Assembly, Integration and Testing (AIT), which the report said aligns closely with Foxconn's existing CDMS business model.

  SpaceX plans to launch its first AI satellites as early as next year and to continue expanding AI satellite deployment over the following two years, the report said.

  ## Technology position

  Foxconn cited proprietary capabilities in optical communications and thermal management as supporting its ability to offer end-to-end AI infrastructure service. The company said it is actively developing the co-packaged optics (CPO) market, which it described as an important tool for future business expansion.
body_zh: |
  鴻海科技集團拿下SpaceX首份AI伺服器製造合約，涵蓋約1萬3,000個搭載輝達GB300處理器的機櫃，合約金額估計高達520億美元。此前，這批訂單由戴爾（Dell）與超微電腦（Super Micro）獨家包辦，如今供貨格局正式改寫。

  《經濟日報》7月20日報導，SpaceX由伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）領導，計畫以每個機櫃約400萬美元的單價採購，合計金額約520億美元、折合新台幣約1.7兆元。出貨時程預計從2026年下半年啟動，延續至2027年第一季。

  鴻海先前已取得多家北美主要雲端服務業者的AI伺服器合約，唯獨缺少SpaceX這一客戶。報導指出，此次搶下SpaceX訂單，補全了鴻海在主要雲端服務業者客戶版圖中的重要缺口。

  對於此筆訂單，鴻海依循不評論個別客戶的一貫做法婉拒置評，但表示旗下AI伺服器業務訂單依然強勁。

  ## 財務影響

  鴻海董事長劉揚偉此前預估，集團2026年全球AI伺服器市占率將突破40%，全年AI機櫃出貨量將較去年同期大幅成長，且逐季遞增至年底。報導引述分析師指出，劉揚偉先前所揭露的AI營收展望並未將SpaceX合約納入計算，因此這筆訂單代表原有預測之外的額外上行空間。

  雲端與網路產品是鴻海四大事業群中規模最大的，以AI伺服器為核心，公司表示此一業務的占比預計將進一步擴大。

  鴻海2026年第一季稅後淨利達新台幣499.19億元，創下同期歷史新高，較上季成長10%、較去年同期成長19%，每股盈餘為新台幣3.56元。鴻海在中國大陸的上市子公司工業富聯（FII）另行預告，2026年第二季歸屬母公司淨利預計落在人民幣128億元至138億元之間，年增幅達86%至101%。

  ## SpaceX的運算基礎建設擴張

  SpaceX持續擴充內部運算產能，並將GB300伺服器機櫃採購量上調至約1萬3,000個。在自建產能之外，SpaceX亦積極拓展運算租賃服務：該公司近期與美國國防部洽談，擬提供AI資料中心運算資源協助五角大廈執行AI模型；此外，SpaceX已分別與Anthropic及Google簽署運算服務協議，並計畫大幅擴展雲端運算業務。

  ## 太空資料中心成為下一個目標

  在地面伺服器之外，據報導所引用的產業分析指出，鴻海與SpaceX正將合作關係進一步延伸至太空AI資料中心領域。

  報導指出，鴻海旗下子公司鴻佰科技規劃與太空運算基礎設施公司Ramon.Space合作，共同打造太空資料中心，該專案旨在支援地球觀測、通訊、政府任務及新興太空服務。

  報導所引述的產業分析認為，SpaceX選擇鴻海，在於鴻海同時具備AI伺服器製造與太空領域的雙重專業。熟悉AI伺服器組裝的業者通常欠缺太空技術，而具備太空能力的企業則往往對AI伺服器生產陌生。鴻海身為全球最大電子代工廠，在兩個領域均有深厚布局，報導形容其是馬斯克最自然的首選。

  鴻海在太空領域的實績包括研發「珍珠」低軌道（LEO）衛星，該衛星整合了鴻海自主研發的相機酬載及其他元件。衛星產業的核心競爭力在於組裝、整合與測試（AIT），報導指出這與鴻海現有的CDMS業務模式高度吻合。

  SpaceX計畫最快於明年發射首批AI衛星，並在此後兩年間持續擴大AI衛星部署規模。

  ## 技術定位

  鴻海表示，公司在光通訊與散熱管理方面具備專有技術，足以提供端對端的AI基礎設施服務。鴻海同時指出，正積極拓展共封裝光學（CPO）市場，並將其定位為未來業務擴展的重要利器。
