body_en: |
  Hitachi Vantara reported 12% year-on-year revenue growth in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, with storage revenue up 45%, as enterprises continued to expand artificial intelligence workloads and refresh aging data infrastructure.

  Global product revenue across Hitachi Vantara and third-party offerings rose 31% in the quarter, with gains recorded across the company's block storage lines and all major regional markets, the company said.

  ## Storage segment breakdown

  Within the block storage portfolio — hardware that stores data in fixed-size volumes, typically used for databases and high-performance applications — growth was strongest at the high end. High-end storage revenue increased 54% year on year, midrange storage rose 43%, and overall block revenue was up 49%.

  Regional results varied in scale but were positive across major markets. Product revenue in Europe, the Middle East and Africa rose 73%, while Asia-Pacific posted 52% growth. Block revenue in the United States increased 24%.

  The company attributed the storage gains primarily to demand for its Virtual Storage Platform One, or VSP One, product family. Customers are deploying VSP One to update core infrastructure and expand AI workloads, Hitachi Vantara said.

  ## Market backdrop

  Hitachi Vantara's results track a broader industry trend. IDC, the market research firm, reported that worldwide external OEM enterprise storage systems revenue — covering systems sold under vendor brands rather than under original equipment manufacturer agreements — rose 22.7% year on year in the first three months of 2026, driven by AI infrastructure demand and deferred refresh spending.

  Adrian Johnson, Chief Revenue Officer at Hitachi Vantara, said the quarter reflected shifting priorities among enterprise customers.

  "Every major business priority now has a data infrastructure requirement attached to it," Johnson said. "As organizations expand their use of AI and modernize their most critical applications, they need infrastructure that can deliver more performance and resilience without adding another layer of complexity."

  ## VSP One Block High End

  Alongside the quarterly results, Hitachi Vantara highlighted the expansion of its VSP One line into mission-critical environments through the VSP One Block High End, a system targeting enterprises running mainframe systems, large transactional applications and AI workloads. The product is designed to consolidate what Hitachi Vantara describes as open systems — standard server-based computing environments — and mainframe workloads onto a common platform.

  Key specifications the company cited for the system:

  - Up to 50 million input/output operations per second (IOPS)
  - Eight nines of availability, equivalent to less than 3.2 seconds of downtime per year
  - A 4:1 data reduction guarantee
  - Integrated cyber resilience features

  The system also includes VSP 360, a management tool designed to provide centralized oversight across infrastructure operations.

  Scott Sinclair, Practice Director of Infrastructure, Cloud and DevOps at Omdia, a technology research firm, said the product addresses a tension facing enterprise IT teams.

  "As organizations expand their use of AI while continuing to support mission-critical applications, they need storage infrastructure that can keep pace without making their environments harder to manage," Sinclair said. "VSP One Block High End brings together the performance, resilience and scale these workloads demand in a unified platform, giving customers a practical path to modernize their data foundation while keeping complexity in check."

  ## Security and complexity pressures

  Hitachi Vantara also pointed to survey data indicating broader stress on corporate IT teams. Recent global research cited by the company found that 55% of IT leaders believe data environment complexity makes breach detection harder, while 57% said data loss would be catastrophic to their business.

  ## Industry recognition

  During the quarter, VSP One received several analyst and industry designations. These included the 2026 Global Tech Award for Data Technology and the 2026 CloudX Award in the Cloud, Object and Data Storage category. Hitachi Vantara was also named a Leader and Fast Mover in the 2026 GigaOm Radars for Object Storage and Unstructured Data Management — a distinction the company said it has received for six consecutive years.

  ## Company context

  Hitachi Vantara is the data storage, infrastructure and hybrid cloud management subsidiary of Hitachi, the Japanese industrial conglomerate. Hitachi reported revenue of 10,586.7 billion yen for fiscal 2025 and employs approximately 290,000 people across 606 consolidated subsidiaries worldwide.

  The quarterly figures reinforce how closely enterprise storage spending is tracking investment in AI infrastructure, particularly as companies work to connect newer AI workloads with existing business-critical systems. IDC's reported 22.7% market-wide growth in external enterprise storage revenue for the same period suggests the demand pattern extends well beyond a single vendor.

  ## 日立萬創AI基礎建設需求爆發，儲存營收單季暴增45%、整體年增12%

  日立萬創（Hitachi Vantara）公布2026財年第一季財報，整體營收較去年同期成長12%，儲存營收勁揚45%。在企業持續擴展人工智慧工作負載、並積極汰換老舊資料基礎建設的雙重驅動下，該公司繳出亮眼成績單。

  本季日立萬創自家產品及第三方產品的全球銷售營收合計年增31%，旗下各系列區塊儲存產品線及各主要區域市場均呈現正成長。

  ## 儲存業務分項表現

  在區塊儲存產品組合方面——此類硬體以固定大小的儲存區段保存資料，主要應用於資料庫及高效能運算環境——高階產品線的成長幅度最為突出。高階儲存營收年增54%，中階儲存年增43%，整體區塊儲存營收則成長49%。

  各區域市場的成長幅度雖有差異，但主要市場皆呈正成長。歐洲、中東及非洲地區產品營收年增73%，亞太區年增52%，美國區塊儲存營收則成長24%。

  日立萬創表示，儲存業務的強勁成長主要來自旗下「虛擬儲存平台 One」（Virtual Storage Platform One，簡稱VSP One）產品系列的市場需求。客戶部署VSP One，既用於更新核心基礎建設，也用於擴展AI工作負載。

  ## 市場背景

  日立萬創的亮眼表現，呼應了整體產業的走勢。市場研究機構IDC指出，2026年首季全球外接式OEM企業儲存系統（即以品牌廠商名義銷售、而非以原始設備製造商協議銷售的系統）營收年增22.7%，主要動能同樣來自AI基礎建設需求，以及企業延遲已久的設備汰換支出。

  日立萬創首席營收長（Chief Revenue Officer）艾德里安·強森（Adrian Johnson）表示，本季財報反映了企業客戶優先要務的轉變。

  「如今每一項重大業務目標，背後都有資料基礎建設的需求，」強森說，「隨著各組織擴大AI應用、並將最關鍵的應用程式現代化，他們需要的基礎建設必須能夠在不增加複雜度的前提下，提供更強大的效能與韌性。」

  ## VSP One Block High End

  除了季度財報之外，日立萬創也宣布VSP One產品線進一步延伸至關鍵任務環境，推出VSP One Block High End系統，鎖定同時運行大型主機（mainframe）、大規模交易應用及AI工作負載的企業客戶。此系統的設計目標，是將日立萬創所定義的「開放系統」——即以標準伺服器為基礎的運算環境——與大型主機工作負載整合至單一共通平台。

  該公司列舉的主要規格如下：

  - 最高每秒5,000萬次輸入／輸出操作（IOPS）
  - 八個9的可用性，相當於每年停機時間不超過3.2秒
  - 4比1的資料縮減保證
  - 整合式網路資安韌性功能

  此系統亦內建VSP 360管理工具，可對基礎建設運作提供集中化的統一監控與管理。

  科技研究機構Omdia基礎建設、雲端與DevOps業務總監史考特·辛克萊（Scott Sinclair）表示，這款產品正面回應了企業IT團隊所面臨的兩難處境。

  「各組織在持續支援關鍵任務應用程式的同時，也要擴展AI應用，他們需要儲存基礎建設能夠跟上腳步，又不能讓環境變得更難管理，」辛克萊說，「VSP One Block High End將這些工作負載所需的效能、韌性與擴展性整合於統一平台，為客戶提供一條現代化資料基礎的務實路徑，同時將複雜度控制在可管範圍之內。」

  ## 資安與複雜度的雙重壓力

  日立萬創也引用調查數據，點出企業IT團隊所承受的更廣泛壓力。該公司援引的最新全球調查顯示，55%的IT主管認為資料環境的複雜度使得資安事件偵測更加困難，另有57%的IT主管表示，一旦發生資料遺失將對業務造成災難性影響。

  ## 業界肯定

  本季度內，VSP One獲得多項業界分析機構的評選肯定，包括2026年全球科技獎（Global Tech Award）資料技術類獎項，以及2026年CloudX Award雲端、物件與資料儲存類別獎項。此外，日立萬創在2026年GigaOm物件儲存及非結構化資料管理雷達報告（GigaOm Radars for Object Storage and Unstructured Data Management）中，同時獲評為「領導者」（Leader）與「快速進步者」（Fast Mover）——該公司表示，這項殊榮已連續六年獲得。

  ## 公司背景

  日立萬創是日本工業集團日立（Hitachi）旗下負責資料儲存、基礎建設及混合雲管理的子公司。日立2025財年合併營收達10兆5,867億日圓，旗下606家合併子公司在全球共雇用約29萬名員工。

  本季財報數字再次印證，企業儲存支出與AI基礎建設投資之間的高度連動關係——尤其是在企業努力將新興AI工作負載與現有核心業務系統整合之際。IDC公布同期全球外接式企業儲存營收市場整體成長22.7%，更顯示這股需求浪潮並不侷限於單一廠商，而是席捲整個產業。
