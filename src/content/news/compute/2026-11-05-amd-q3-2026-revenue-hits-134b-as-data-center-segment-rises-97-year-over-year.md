body_en: |
  AMD reported record third-quarter 2026 revenue of $13.377 billion, a 45% increase year over year and 16% sequentially, led by a Data Center segment that nearly doubled in revenue compared with the same period in 2025.

  The Data Center segment generated $8.546 billion in the quarter, up 97% year over year and 27% sequentially. AMD attributed the growth to strong demand for its EPYC server processors — a family of chips designed for high-core-count server deployments — and the launch of its Instinct MI450 graphics processing units (GPUs), accelerators built for artificial intelligence and high-performance computing workloads. The company said major clients, including Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, and Oracle Cloud Infrastructure, are running workloads on Helios rack-scale solutions powered by those chips. A rack-scale solution integrates computing, networking, and power delivery at the level of a full equipment rack rather than individual servers.

  On a generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) basis, AMD posted operating income of $2.832 billion and net income of $2.428 billion, with diluted earnings per share of $1.46. Excluding stock-based compensation, amortization of acquisition-related intangibles, and other items, AMD reported non-GAAP operating income of $3.923 billion, non-GAAP net income of $3.471 billion, and non-GAAP EPS of $2.09.

  ## AMD's data center revenue vs. Intel

  AMD's Data Center segment at $8.546 billion exceeded Intel's data center quarterly revenue of $7.3 billion, which Intel reported the prior week. Intel's data center segment grew 78% year over year in the same period; AMD's grew 97%. Both companies are recording surging demand for AI infrastructure and server processors, with AMD's growth rate running faster.

  AMD's upcoming Venice generation of EPYC processors is expected to extend that position. ServeTheHome's analysis of the two roadmaps anticipates Venice establishing clear x86 server performance leadership, with Intel's competing Diamond Rapids platform — which also introduces PCIe Gen 6, an interconnect standard that doubles the bandwidth of its predecessor — not expected to arrive until 2027.

  ## Client, gaming, and embedded segments

  The combined Client and Gaming segment posted $3.722 billion, down 8% year over year. Client revenue ticked up modestly within the segment while gaming declined. AMD noted that memory and NAND flash storage pricing are constraining that market. The company highlighted its Ryzen AI Halo platform and said AMD featured three AI-related products in its client segment summary.

  The Embedded segment — which serves aerospace and defense, automotive, communications, and industrial markets — generated $1.109 billion, up 29% year over year. AMD's Embedded business encompasses technology from its acquisition of Xilinx, a programmable logic chipmaker whose annual revenue was approximately $3.1 billion to $3.2 billion at the time of that deal.

  ## Fourth-quarter guidance

  AMD guided fourth-quarter 2026 revenue to approximately $17 billion, plus or minus 3%. At the midpoint, that represents year-over-year growth of approximately 66% and sequential growth of roughly 27% from the third quarter.

  ## Industry implications

  AMD's data center revenue has surpassed Intel's in a direct quarterly comparison as hyperscalers and AI labs accelerate GPU and server processor procurement. The Turin-to-Venice platform transition, which AMD is preparing to bring to market, represents a more substantial upgrade than a standard socket refresh: ServeTheHome's analysis notes it adds more cores, updated core designs, higher memory bandwidth, increased DDR5 memory channel count, and PCIe Gen 6 support. That positions AMD ahead of Intel's next platform cycle, which is not expected until 2027.

  AMD's fourth-quarter guidance of approximately $17 billion would, if met, represent the company's largest quarter on record.

  ## AMD 2026年第三季營收達134億美元，資料中心部門年增97%

  AMD公布2026年第三季創紀錄營收137.77億美元，較去年同期成長45%、較上季成長16%，主要動能來自資料中心部門——該部門營收與2025年同期相比近乎翻倍。

  資料中心部門本季貢獻85.46億美元，年增97%、季增27%。AMD將此成長歸因於EPYC伺服器處理器的強勁需求——EPYC是一系列專為高核心數伺服器部署設計的晶片——以及Instinct MI450繪圖處理器（GPU）的正式上市；該GPU是專為人工智慧與高效能運算工作負載打造的加速器。AMD表示，包括Anthropic、Meta、Microsoft、OpenAI及Oracle Cloud Infrastructure在內的主要客戶，均已在搭載上述晶片的Helios機架規模解決方案上運行工作負載。所謂機架規模解決方案，是指在整個設備機架層級——而非單台伺服器層級——整合運算、網路與電力配送的系統架構。

  以一般公認會計原則（GAAP）計算，AMD本季營業利益為28.32億美元，淨利為24.28億美元，稀釋每股盈餘為1.46美元。若排除股票薪酬、收購相關無形資產攤銷及其他項目，AMD非GAAP營業利益為39.23億美元，非GAAP淨利為34.71億美元，非GAAP每股盈餘為2.09美元。

  ## AMD與Intel資料中心營收比較

  AMD資料中心部門本季85.46億美元的營收，已超越Intel前一週公布的同期資料中心季度營收73億美元。Intel資料中心部門同期年增率為78%，AMD則達97%。兩家公司均錄得AI基礎建設與伺服器處理器需求大幅攀升，但AMD的成長速度更為快速。

  AMD即將推出的下一代EPYC處理器Venice，預計將進一步鞏固其領先地位。ServeTheHome針對兩家公司產品路線圖的分析預測，Venice將確立x86伺服器效能的明確領導地位，而Intel的競爭平台Diamond Rapids——該平台同時導入PCIe Gen 6，這是一種頻寬較前代倍增的互連標準——預計要到2027年才會問世。

  ## 客戶端、遊戲及嵌入式部門

  客戶端與遊戲合併部門本季營收為37.22億美元，年減8%。其中客戶端營收在部門內小幅上揚，遊戲則呈下滑。AMD指出，記憶體與NAND快閃儲存體定價正在壓抑該市場表現。公司特別強調旗下Ryzen AI Halo平台，並表示AMD在客戶端部門摘要中列出三款與AI相關的產品。

  嵌入式部門——服務航太國防、汽車、通訊及工業市場——本季貢獻11.09億美元，年增29%。AMD嵌入式業務涵蓋收購可程式邏輯晶片廠商Xilinx所取得的技術；Xilinx在該交易完成時，年營收約為31億至32億美元。

  ## 第四季營收展望

  AMD預估2026年第四季營收約為170億美元，上下誤差範圍3%。以中間值計算，這代表年增率約66%、較第三季季增約27%。

  ## 產業影響

  隨著超大規模雲端業者與AI實驗室加速採購GPU與伺服器處理器，AMD資料中心營收已在單季直接比較中超越Intel。AMD準備推向市場的Turin至Venice平台世代交替，所代表的升級幅度遠超過一般的插槽更換：ServeTheHome的分析指出，新平台新增更多核心、更新的核心架構設計、更高的記憶體頻寬、更多的DDR5記憶體通道數，以及PCIe Gen 6支援。這使AMD在平台週期上領先Intel下一代平台——後者預計要到2027年才會到來。

  AMD第四季約170億美元的營收展望若能達成，將成為該公司有史以來規模最大的單一季度。
