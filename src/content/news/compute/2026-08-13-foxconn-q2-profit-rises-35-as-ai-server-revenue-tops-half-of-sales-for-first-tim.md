body_en: |
  Foxconn reported second-quarter net profit of NT$59.97 billion (approximately S$2.38 billion), a 35 percent year-on-year increase that beat an LSEG analyst consensus of NT$58.8 billion, as the company's cloud and AI server segment crossed 50 percent of quarterly revenue for the first time.

  The result, reported Aug. 12, compared with NT$44.4 billion in the same quarter of 2025. The company, formally known as Hon Hai Precision Industry, is the world's largest contract electronics manufacturer and serves as Nvidia's biggest server maker and Apple's primary iPhone assembler.

  ## AI servers overtake iPhones in revenue mix

  The cloud and networking products segment, which includes AI servers, accounted for 51 percent of second-quarter revenue — the first time the segment has exceeded half of total sales. The smart consumer electronics segment, which includes iPhones, accounted for 29 percent of revenue. The company maintained its prior forecast of "strong" revenue growth for 2026 and does not issue numeric guidance.

  ## Vera Rubin timeline and near-term outlook

  Rotating chief executive Michael Chiang said AI server racks built around Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin platform will enter mass-production preparation in the third quarter, with customer shipments expected to begin in the fourth quarter. "We expect production volumes to increase gradually over the next several quarters, and it will become our major product next year," Chiang said.

  Chiang also said AI cabinet shipments overall would more than double in 2026. Revenue from 800G — 800 gigabit-per-second — and higher-speed switching products is expected to double on a full-year basis. CPO all-optical switches — co-packaged optics, a technology that integrates optical components directly with switch silicon to reduce power consumption and latency — are on track for mass production shipments in the third quarter.

  For the third quarter overall, Chiang said the company expects significant sequential growth and strong year-on-year growth, driven by continued AI business expansion alongside ICT — information and communications technology — consumer products entering their traditional peak season in the second half of the year.

  ## CoWoS packaging capacity as the binding constraint

  The critical variable for the AI server rack market in 2027, Chiang said, will be how much CoWoS packaging capacity chipmakers can secure. CoWoS, or Chip on Wafer on Substrate, is an advanced semiconductor packaging process developed by TSMC — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company — that stacks AI chips to achieve higher performance. Foxconn's primary customers, including Nvidia, depend on TSMC's CoWoS capacity to produce their AI processors.

  "The market currently expects CoWoS capacity to grow by more than 50 per cent next year, but how much of that can ultimately be translated into shipments of next-generation AI server racks will depend on chip supply," Chiang said.

  ## End-to-end integration as competitive differentiation

  Chiang described Foxconn's position in AI infrastructure not as a single-product supplier but as a provider of complete system solutions, spanning the full hardware stack. That stack runs from Level 1 components through GPU modules, motherboards, switch trays, and compute trays to full AI cabinet assembly and the cabling between cabinets.

  Inside the cabinet, Foxconn self-manufactures liquid cooling systems, power management hardware, connectors, busbars — the copper or aluminum bars that distribute high electrical current within a rack — structural components, and high-speed transmission parts. The self-manufacturing rate for high value-added components has exceeded 50 percent, the company said.

  Chiang outlined a three-tier operational model underpinning that strategy. The first tier is a global manufacturing footprint spanning 24 countries and more than 240 facilities. The second is vertical integration backed by automation: CNC — computer numerical control — machining and SMT — surface-mount technology — process automation rates have reached close to 100 percent, the company said, supporting the yield and efficiency needed for high-cost, high-complexity AI products. The third tier is joint development with tier-one customers combined with investment in proprietary technology, with the company positioning itself as a technology manufacturing platform rather than a pure contract assembler.

  Chiang said the engineering and supply-chain demands of AI cabinets — high-power design, liquid cooling, system integration, testing, verification, and customization — mean entry barriers in the segment will continue to rise. Automation in the newest AI cabinet generation has increased significantly, he said, which together with Foxconn's vertical integration and system-delivery capabilities should support further market share gains.

  ## Capital expenditure and geographic expansion

  Foxconn expects capital expenditure to rise 30 percent in 2026 compared with 2025. The company is building factories in Mexico and Texas to manufacture AI servers for Nvidia. On iPhone production, while the majority of devices Foxconn assembles are still made in China, it now produces the bulk of those destined for the United States market in India.

  ## Shares and market context

  Foxconn shares closed 2.7 percent higher on Aug. 12 ahead of the earnings release. The shares have gained 17 percent so far in 2026, trailing the broader Taiwan stock index's 57 percent rise during the same period.

  How quickly Vera Rubin shipment volumes ramp over the coming quarters, and how much of the market's projected CoWoS capacity expansion converts into deliverable AI server racks, will determine whether the platform meets Foxconn's expectation of becoming its principal product in 2027.
body_zh: |
  鴻海精密工業公布，第二季淨利達新台幣599.7億元（約新加坡幣23.8億元），年增35%，優於路孚特（LSEG）分析師共識預估的新台幣588億元，旗下雲端與AI伺服器部門季度營收占比首度突破五成。

  此一財報結果於8月12日公布，相較於2025年同期的新台幣444億元大幅成長。鴻海是全球最大的委託代工電子製造商，同時也是輝達最大的伺服器製造商，以及蘋果iPhone的主要組裝廠。

  ## AI伺服器營收首度超越iPhone占比

  涵蓋AI伺服器業務的雲端與網路產品部門，在第二季貢獻了51%的營收，為該部門首度超越整體銷售額的一半。涵蓋iPhone的智慧型消費性電子產品部門則占營收29%。公司維持先前對2026年「強勁」營收成長的預測，但不提供具體數字指引。

  ## Vera Rubin出貨時程與近期展望

  輪值執行長蔣浩良表示，以輝達下一代Vera Rubin平台為核心打造的AI伺服器機架，將於第三季進入量產準備階段，預計第四季開始向客戶出貨。「我們預期未來數季的生產量將逐步攀升，明年將成為我們的主力產品，」蔣浩良說。

  蔣浩良同時表示，2026年AI機櫃整體出貨量將較2025年倍增以上。800G（每秒800吉比特）及更高速交換器產品的全年營收預計也將翻倍成長。CPO全光交換器——即共封裝光學（co-packaged optics），一種將光學元件直接整合至交換器晶片的技術，可降低耗電量與傳輸延遲——目前已在規劃中，預計第三季進入量產出貨。

  就整體第三季展望而言，蔣浩良表示公司預期將較上季有顯著成長，並維持強勁的年增趨勢，動力來自AI業務的持續擴張，以及ICT（資訊與通訊技術）消費性產品進入下半年傳統旺季。

  ## CoWoS封裝產能成為關鍵制約因素

  蔣浩良指出，2027年AI伺服器機架市場的關鍵變數，在於晶片製造商能夠取得多少CoWoS封裝產能。CoWoS（Chip on Wafer on Substrate，晶圓上晶片基板堆疊封裝）是台積電（台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司）所開發的先進半導體封裝製程，透過堆疊AI晶片來提升運算效能。鴻海的主要客戶，包括輝達在內，均仰賴台積電的CoWoS產能來生產AI處理器。

  「市場目前預期明年CoWoS產能將成長逾五成，但最終有多少能轉化為下一代AI伺服器機架的實際出貨量，將取決於晶片供應狀況，」蔣浩良說。

  ## 端到端整合作為競爭差異化優勢

  蔣浩良將鴻海在AI基礎設施的定位，描述為完整系統解決方案的提供者，而非單一產品供應商，涵蓋完整的硬體技術堆疊。這套堆疊從第一層元件起，歷經GPU模組、主機板、交換器托盤、運算托盤，直至完整的AI機櫃組裝及機櫃間的線纜配置。

  在機櫃內部，鴻海自製液冷系統、電源管理硬體、連接器、匯流排（busbar，即在機架內分配高電流的銅或鋁製導電棒）、結構性元件及高速傳輸零件。公司表示，高附加價值元件的自製率已超過五成。

  蔣浩良進一步闡述支撐此一策略的三層次營運模式。第一層是跨越24個國家、逾240座廠房的全球製造版圖。第二層是以自動化為後盾的垂直整合：CNC（電腦數值控制）加工及SMT（表面黏著技術）製程自動化率均已接近100%，公司表示，以支撐高成本、高複雜度AI產品所需的良率與效率。第三層則是與一線客戶的聯合開發，加上對自有技術的持續投資，將公司定位為技術製造平台，而非純粹的委託代工廠。

  蔣浩良表示，AI機櫃在高功率設計、液冷、系統整合、測試、驗證及客製化方面的工程與供應鏈需求，意味著此一領域的進入門檻將持續墊高。他說，新一代AI機櫃的自動化程度已大幅提升，加上鴻海的垂直整合能力與系統交付能力，應能支撐公司進一步擴大市場份額。

  ## 資本支出與地理布局擴張

  鴻海預計2026年資本支出較2025年增加三成。公司正在墨西哥與德州建設工廠，為輝達製造AI伺服器。在iPhone生產方面，儘管鴻海組裝的iPhone整體仍以中國生產為大宗，但目前銷往美國市場的機型，已有大部分在印度生產。

  ## 股價表現與市場背景

  鴻海股票在8月12日財報公布前收漲2.7%。該股今年迄今已累計上漲17%，落後台灣大盤同期57%的漲幅。

  Vera Rubin出貨量在未來數季的爬坡速度，以及市場預期的CoWoS產能擴增中有多少能真正轉化為可交付的AI伺服器機架，將決定這款平台能否如鴻海預期，在2027年成為公司的核心主力產品。
