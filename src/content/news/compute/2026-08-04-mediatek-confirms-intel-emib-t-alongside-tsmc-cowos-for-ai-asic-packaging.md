body_en: |
  MediaTek has confirmed for the first time that it is deploying Intel's EMIB-T advanced packaging technology alongside TSMC's CoWoS in its AI ASIC — application-specific integrated circuit — projects, the company disclosed at its latest earnings call. The announcement ends months of ambiguity about the identity of MediaTek's second packaging partner and signals a deliberate move away from reliance on a single packaging supply chain.

  MediaTek CEO CC Wei said the company has established itself as a key partner to multiple data center customers and is working closely with both TSMC and its other major advanced packaging partners. The company is combining 2-nanometer process design experience and large-die architecture capabilities with both CoWoS and EMIB-T packaging to help customers develop oversized, high-performance ASIC products for AI infrastructure, Wei said.

  MediaTek had previously indicated at its late-April earnings call that it was simultaneously investing in two different advanced packaging technologies, citing rapid expansion in AI infrastructure demand. At that time, the company did not identify the second technology's provider. The current earnings call is the first instance in which MediaTek explicitly named Intel's EMIB-T as that second technology.

  ## What EMIB-T is

  EMIB-T is the latest iteration of Intel's EMIB — Embedded Multi-die Interconnect Bridge — 2.5D packaging platform. The technology uses small silicon bridges to connect multiple chiplets, replacing the large silicon interposers used in conventional approaches. That substitution reduces packaging cost while preserving high-bandwidth, high-density interconnect capability and supporting integration of HBM — High Bandwidth Memory — according to the Commercial Times report.

  Compared with the existing EMIB architecture, EMIB-T adds TSV — Through Silicon Via — technology, which passes electrical connections vertically through silicon to improve power delivery and signal transmission efficiency. Intel's publicly available data, as cited by the Commercial Times, indicates EMIB-T supports chip integration at roughly eight to ten times the size of a single reticle — the maximum area a lithography machine can expose in one shot — allowing higher packaging density and compute performance while managing cost.

  ## Why a dual-track approach

  AI chip packaging has historically been dominated by CoWoS — Chip on Wafer on Substrate — a TSMC technology. But as AI ASICs push toward reticle-size and beyond-reticle chip designs, no single packaging technology fully addresses the range of customer requirements, the report notes.

  MediaTek said its dual-track strategy is intended to give the company flexibility across different customer needs for cost, performance, and product design, and to reduce exposure to any single supplier. The company said it aims to strike the optimal balance among cost, performance, and volume manufacturing capability depending on the specific customer project.

  The disclosure places packaging on equal footing with semiconductor process technology as a competitive differentiator in AI data center chip development, a framing MediaTek itself used at the April earnings call when it described packaging as no longer merely a manufacturing step but a key determinant of overall system performance.
body_zh: |
  聯發科（MediaTek）在最新一季財報法說會上，首度確認旗下AI ASIC（特定應用積體電路）專案已同步部署Intel的EMIB-T先進封裝技術與台積電（TSMC）的CoWoS。這項宣布終結了外界數月來對聯發科第二封裝合作夥伴身份的諸多猜測，也標誌著公司刻意告別對單一封裝供應鏈依賴的戰略轉型。

  聯發科執行長魏哲家（CC Wei）表示，聯發科已將自身確立為多家資料中心客戶的關鍵夥伴，並與台積電及另一重要先進封裝合作夥伴維持緊密合作。他說，公司正結合2奈米製程設計經驗與大型晶粒架構能力，同步運用CoWoS與EMIB-T兩種封裝技術，協助客戶為AI基礎設施開發超大尺寸、高效能的ASIC產品。

  聯發科早在今年四月下旬的財報法說會上便已表示，公司正同步投入兩種不同的先進封裝技術，並以AI基礎設施需求快速擴張作為布局依據。然而，當時公司並未透露第二種技術的供應商。此次法說會，是聯發科首度明確點名Intel的EMIB-T即為該第二種技術。

  ## EMIB-T是什麼

  EMIB-T是Intel旗下EMIB（嵌入式多晶粒互連橋接）2.5D封裝平台的最新世代。這項技術採用小型矽橋接器連接多個晶粒，取代傳統方案中面積龐大的矽中介層（interposer）。此一替換方式在降低封裝成本的同時，仍能保有高頻寬、高密度的互連能力，並支援HBM（高頻寬記憶體）的整合——以上資訊來自《工商時報》的報導。

  與現行EMIB架構相比，EMIB-T新增了TSV（矽穿孔）技術，透過垂直穿透矽晶片的導通孔傳遞電氣連結，藉此提升電力傳輸與訊號傳輸效率。根據《工商時報》所引述的Intel公開資料，EMIB-T可支援相當於單一光罩（reticle）——即微影設備單次曝光最大面積——約八至十倍大的晶片整合規模，在有效控制成本的前提下，實現更高的封裝密度與運算效能。

  ## 為何採行雙軌策略

  AI晶片封裝領域向來由台積電的CoWoS（晶圓上晶片上基板）技術所主導。然而，隨著AI ASIC設計持續朝光罩極限乃至超光罩尺寸推進，單一封裝技術已難以全面滿足各種客戶需求——報導如此指出。

  聯發科表示，雙軌策略的目的在於讓公司在成本、效能與產品設計等不同客戶需求上保有充分彈性，同時降低對單一供應商的風險集中。公司表示，將依據各客戶專案的具體情況，在成本、效能與量產能力三者之間尋求最佳平衡。

  此次資訊揭露，將封裝技術提升至與半導體製程技術並駕齊驅的競爭差異化地位，成為AI資料中心晶片開發不可或缺的核心競爭力。這套論述框架，正是聯發科自身在四月法說會上所揭示的——公司當時明確指出，封裝已不再僅是一道製造工序，而是決定整體系統效能的關鍵要素。
