  ## NVIDIA Locks In Substrate Capacity as AI Infrastructure Demand Strains Supply

  NVIDIA has signed a capacity reservation agreement with Xinxing Electronics (Unimicron, ticker: 3037), Taiwan's largest IC substrate manufacturer, to secure expanded production of ABF substrates — the high-end interconnect components that link advanced chips to circuit boards. CEO Jensen Huang announced the agreement at GTC Berlin. New capacity at Xinxing is scheduled to come online in the second quarter of 2027, with full utilization expected by mid-2028, according to the report.

  NVIDIA has also entered parallel agreements with Japan's Ibiden and South Korea's Samsung Electro-Mechanics. Across all three partners, NVIDIA's stated goal is to increase the total substrate capacity available to its products to 2.5 times current levels over the next three years.

  Xinxing shares rose more than 3 percent on the day of the announcement.

  ## What ABF Substrates Are and Why They Matter

  ABF (Ajinomoto Build-up Film) substrates are a premium category of printed circuit board (PCB) components. They sit between a semiconductor chip and the main circuit board, routing the chip's electrical signals and power connections. As GPUs and custom AI accelerators integrate more compute dies and high-bandwidth memory (HBM), these substrates must scale accordingly — growing larger in area, finer in circuit geometry, and greater in layer count, all of which raises manufacturing difficulty and yield risk.

  Xinxing and Japan's Ibiden are widely regarded as the two manufacturers capable of producing high-end AI substrates at volume, according to the report.

  ## Supply Chain Bottlenecks Preceded the Deals

  Xinxing Chairman Jian Shanjie, speaking at the TPCA Show industry conference, said that shortages of certain critical materials first emerged one to two years ago and that equipment delivery lead times have continued to lengthen. Expanding production capacity, he said, requires not only building factory space and installing production lines but also confirming that upstream suppliers can deliver sufficient materials and equipment on schedule.

  To coordinate that upstream supply, Xinxing's operations and production teams — accompanied in some cases by customers and their customers' customers — conducted more than a dozen visits to relevant suppliers beginning in early last year. By sharing their own capacity plans and those of end customers, Xinxing said it began receiving positive responses from the supply chain in the second half of this year, with suppliers starting to increase output.

  Jian described this experience as evidence that demand visibility has become a prerequisite for expansion. Upstream suppliers, he said, need to know customers' product timelines and capacity requirements before they can commit investment and ensure new supply arrives when it is needed.

  ## Technical Demands Are Reshaping PCB Suppliers' Role

  Beyond materials and equipment, Jian identified three core technical challenges for AI data center infrastructure: high-speed interconnect, power delivery, and thermal management. System cores in AI hardware can require thousands of amperes of current, demanding simultaneous low-impedance power delivery and noise control. High-speed data transmission requires stable signal integrity, while high heat output creates problems of thermal dissipation, material thermal expansion mismatch, and board warping.

  Those requirements interact with one another, Jian said, which means PCB suppliers must engage earlier in customers' system design processes — contributing to material selection, process integration, and reliability validation rather than simply manufacturing to a finished specification. The role of the PCB supplier, he said, has extended from executing designs to helping customers bring next-generation technology into production.

  ## Talent and Geographic Strategy

  Jian said that even with factory space, land, and equipment in place, a shortage of skilled workers can still prevent a manufacturer from operating. He called on Taiwan's PCB industry to build active partnerships with universities and technical colleges to develop expertise in materials, process engineering, equipment operation, and systems integration, given competition for talent from semiconductor and other high-technology sectors.

  For overseas expansion, Jian acknowledged that replicating a factory in a new location still requires managing differences in local culture, management capability, and supply chain integration. His recommendation was that Taiwan remain the center for advanced research, development, and high-end manufacturing, with overseas facilities focused on customer service and regional volume production.

  ## Significance for the Industry

  The capacity reservation model NVIDIA is deploying — in which the end customer underwrites supplier expansion before production begins — represents a shift from conventional procurement. It transfers demand risk to the buyer in exchange for supply certainty, and in doing so draws the entire substrate supply chain, from raw materials through fabrication, into a coordinated investment cycle tied directly to AI infrastructure buildout timelines.

  NVIDIA's three-year target of reaching 2.5 times current substrate capacity across Xinxing, Ibiden, and Samsung Electro-Mechanics sets the forward production horizon, with Xinxing's Taiwan facility contributing incremental output from Q2 2027 and reaching full capacity in mid-2028.

  ## 輝達鎖定基板產能　AI基礎建設需求拉緊供應鏈

  輝達（NVIDIA）已與台灣最大IC基板製造商欣興電子（Unimicron，股票代碼：3037）簽署產能預約協議，確保ABF基板的擴產供應。ABF基板是連結先進晶片與電路板的高階互連元件。執行長黃仁勳在GTC柏林大會上宣布此項合作。根據報導，欣興的新產能預計於2027年第二季開始投入生產，並於2028年中期達到滿載。

  輝達同時也與日本揖斐電（Ibiden）及南韓三星電機（Samsung Electro-Mechanics）簽署類似的平行協議。輝達宣示的目標是，在未來三年內透過這三家合作夥伴，將可用基板總產能提升至目前水準的2.5倍。

  欣興電子股價在消息宣布當日上漲逾3%。

  ## ABF基板是什麼？為何如此關鍵？

  ABF（味之素增層薄膜）基板是一種高階印刷電路板（PCB）元件，位於半導體晶片與主電路板之間，負責傳導晶片的電訊號與電源連接。隨著GPU與客製AI加速器整合更多運算晶片及高頻寬記憶體（HBM），這些基板也必須同步升級——面積更大、線路線寬更細、層數更多，使製造難度與良率風險大幅提升。

  根據報導，欣興電子與日本揖斐電被業界公認為目前僅有的兩家能夠量產高階AI基板的製造商。

  ## 供應鏈瓶頸早於協議浮現

  欣興電子董事長簡山傑在TPCA Show產業展上表示，部分關鍵材料的短缺問題在一至兩年前便已出現，設備交貨前置時間也持續延長。他指出，擴充產能不僅需要興建廠房、安裝產線，還必須確認上游供應商能按時交付足夠的材料與設備。

  為協調上游供應，欣興的營運與生產團隊——在部分情況下偕同客戶及其客戶的客戶——自去年初起便對相關供應商進行逾十次的實地拜訪。透過分享自身及終端客戶的產能規劃，欣興表示今年下半年已開始獲得供應鏈的正面回應，供應商也陸續啟動增產。

  簡山傑將這段經歷形容為需求能見度已成為擴產前提的最佳印證。他表示，上游供應商必須事先掌握客戶的產品時程與產能需求，才能做出投資承諾，確保新增供應能在有需要時及時到位。

  ## 技術需求重塑PCB供應商的角色

  除材料與設備外，簡山傑還點出AI資料中心基礎建設的三大核心技術挑戰：高速互連、電源供應與散熱管理。AI硬體的系統核心可能需要高達數千安培的電流，要求在低阻抗供電的同時兼顧雜訊控制。高速資料傳輸需要穩定的訊號完整性，而高功率輸出則衍生出熱量排散、材料熱膨脹不匹配及電路板翹曲等問題。

  簡山傑表示，上述需求相互牽動，意味著PCB供應商必須在客戶系統設計的更早期階段介入，在材料選用、製程整合與可靠性驗證方面提供貢獻，而非單純依照完整規格進行製造。他說，PCB供應商的角色已從執行設計延伸至協助客戶將次世代技術推向量產。

  ## 人才培育與海外布局策略

  簡山傑指出，即便廠房、土地與設備均已到位，技術人才不足仍可能讓製造商難以正常運作。他呼籲台灣PCB產業主動與大學及技術學院建立合作關係，在材料、製程工程、設備操作與系統整合等領域培育專業人才，以因應來自半導體及其他高科技產業的人才競爭。

  針對海外擴廠，簡山傑坦承，在新地點複製一座工廠，仍需克服當地文化、管理能力與供應鏈整合等方面的差異。他的建議是：台灣應持續作為先進研發與高階製造的核心基地，海外據點則聚焦於客戶服務與區域量產。

  ## 對產業的深遠意義

  輝達採行的產能預約模式——由終端客戶在量產啟動前即為供應商擴產提供資金保障——代表有別於傳統採購的一種轉變。此模式讓買方承擔需求風險，換取供應確定性，並藉此將整條基板供應鏈——從原材料到成品製造——納入一個與AI基礎建設建設時程直接掛鉤的協同投資週期。

  輝達設定的三年目標，是透過欣興、揖斐電與三星電機，將基板總產能擴增至目前的2.5倍。其中，欣興的台灣廠房將從2027年第二季起逐步貢獻新增產出，並於2028年中期達到滿產。
