body_en: |
  Mizuho Securities has sharply raised its forecasts for TSMC's advanced chip manufacturing and CoWoS advanced packaging capacity, saying accelerating AI-driven server CPU demand requires TSMC to expand at a faster pace than the firm previously modeled.

  ## CoWoS Capacity Revisions

  CoWoS — chip-on-wafer-on-substrate, an advanced packaging technology that stacks memory and logic dies into a single module — is the center of the upgrade. Mizuho lifted its forecast for TSMC's monthly CoWoS output from 120,000 wafers to 140,000 wafers for 2026, and from a range of 170,000–180,000 to a range of 190,000–200,000 wafers per month for 2027.

  ## Advanced Node Capacity Outlook

  On leading-edge manufacturing nodes — the processes that define transistor density and chip performance — Mizuho projects TSMC's N3 (3-nanometer) monthly wafer capacity will reach 170,000 units in 2026 and 200,000 in 2027. N2 (2-nanometer) capacity is forecast at 90,000 wafers per month in 2026, rising to 150,000 in 2027 and 200,000 in 2028. The firm also introduced projections for TSMC's A14 process — described as more advanced than N2 — at 15,000 wafers per month in 2027 and 40,000 in 2028, with meaningful volume production of that generation expected before 2030.

  ## Demand Drivers: AI and Server CPUs

  Mizuho attributes the upward revisions to a broad and deepening set of customers. For 2027, it projects AI server CPU demand — spanning both x86 and ARM architectures — will grow more than 50% year over year. ARM-based server CPU shipments specifically are expected to double between 2026 and 2027.

  Named demand sources include Nvidia's Vera CPU, Intel and AMD server CPUs, and custom silicon from cloud service providers (CSPs) — companies that sell computing capacity over the internet — including Google, AWS, Microsoft, and Meta.

  ## Customer-Level CoWoS Allocations

  Mizuho provides customer-level CoWoS volume estimates. It forecasts TSMC will produce 630,000 CoWoS wafers for Nvidia in 2026, rising to 1,005,000 in 2027, driven by the Vera CPU and Rubin GPU architecture. MediaTek's CoWoS allocation was nearly doubled — from 93,000 to 180,000 wafers — on strong demand for Google's TPU (tensor processing unit, Google's custom AI accelerator chip). Broadcom's 2027 estimate was trimmed modestly to 425,000 wafers.

  In unit terms for 2027, Mizuho forecasts more than 7 million Nvidia Vera CPUs, 5 million AMD Venice CPUs, more than 4 million Google CPUs, more than 3 million AWS CPUs, approximately 1 million Microsoft CPUs, and 100,000 to 200,000 Meta CPUs.

  ## OSAT Capacity Expansion

  The packaging capacity buildout extends beyond TSMC to OSATs — outsourced semiconductor assembly and test companies that handle chip packaging on behalf of chipmakers. ASE, one of the world's largest chip packaging firms, is projected to reach monthly CoWoS capacity of 20,000 wafers in 2026 and 40,000 to 45,000 in 2027, primarily serving AMD Venice and Nvidia Vera CPU programs. Amkor is expected to expand to 20,000 to 25,000 wafers per month by the end of 2027. Mizuho rates both ASE and ASMPT as "Buy," identifying them as beneficiaries of the packaging trend.

  ## Equipment Procurement

  Equipment orders are accelerating in parallel. TSMC and ASE/SPIL (Siliconware Precision Industries Ltd., an ASE subsidiary) are expected to order more than 130 TCB — thermal compression bonding, a precision chip-stacking process — machines from Shibaura in 2026. TSMC also plans to procure 50 to 80 fluxless TCB machines from either K&S or ASMPT between the second half of 2026 and the first half of 2027.

  ## Intel Advanced Packaging Progress

  Mizuho notes that Intel has made progress in its own advanced packaging technology. Intel's EMIB-T chip interconnect is now yielding above 95%, according to the report, and Intel is in discussions with MediaTek, Ampere Computing, AWS, and Tesla for technology adoption.

  ## Ratings and Price Targets

  Mizuho rates TSMC "Buy" with a target price of NT$3,000. It also rates ASML — the Dutch maker of lithography machines used in advanced chip manufacturing — "Buy" with a €2,000 target price, alongside "Buy" ratings for ASE and ASMPT.

  ## Significance

  The scale of the CoWoS and advanced node revisions signals that demand from AI infrastructure is pulling chip capacity forward at a pace that outstrips earlier industry estimates, with packaging and equipment suppliers positioned as secondary beneficiaries.

  Mizuho identifies three variables that will determine whether its elevated forecasts hold: whether Nvidia's Vera and Rubin production schedules proceed as planned; whether CSP custom chip programs accelerate further; and whether TSMC's N2 yield ramps quickly enough to support the projected 2027–2028 capacity expansion.
body_zh: |
  瑞穗證券大幅上調台積電先進製程及CoWoS先進封裝產能預測，表示AI驅動的伺服器CPU需求加速升溫，迫使台積電須以比該機構先前預估更快的速度擴充產能。

  ## CoWoS產能修正

  CoWoS（全名Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate，一種將記憶體與邏輯晶片堆疊整合於單一模組的先進封裝技術）是本次上調的核心項目。瑞穗將台積電2026年CoWoS月產能預測，從12萬片上調至14萬片；2027年的預測範圍則從每月17至18萬片，上調至19至20萬片。

  ## 先進製程產能展望

  在定義電晶體密度與晶片效能的先進製程節點方面，瑞穗預測台積電N3（3奈米）月產能將於2026年達到17萬片，2027年達到20萬片。N2（2奈米）月產能預測為2026年9萬片，2027年增至15萬片，2028年進一步升至20萬片。此外，瑞穗首度提出台積電A14製程的預測——該製程被形容為比N2更先進——預計2027年月產能為1.5萬片，2028年為4萬片，並預期在2030年前進入具規模的量產階段。

  ## 需求驅動力：AI與伺服器CPU

  瑞穗將此波上調歸因於客戶基礎的廣泛擴大與需求持續深化。就2027年而言，該機構預測AI伺服器CPU需求——涵蓋x86與ARM兩大架構——將較前一年成長逾50%。其中，ARM架構伺服器CPU出貨量預計在2026至2027年間倍增。

  主要需求來源包括輝達（NVIDIA）的Vera CPU、英特爾與超微的伺服器CPU，以及雲端服務供應商（CSP，即透過網路販售運算能力的企業）的自研晶片，涵蓋Google、AWS、微軟與Meta。

  ## 客戶層級CoWoS分配

  瑞穗進一步提供各客戶的CoWoS晶圓分配預測。報告指出，台積電2026年為輝達生產的CoWoS晶圓預計達63萬片，2027年進一步成長至100.5萬片，動力來自Vera CPU與Rubin GPU架構。聯發科的CoWoS分配幾乎翻倍，從9.3萬片上調至18萬片，主要受Google TPU（張量處理器，即Google自研AI加速晶片）的強勁需求帶動。博通的2027年預測則小幅下修至42.5萬片。

  以2027年出貨數量計算，瑞穗預測輝達Vera CPU逾700萬顆、超微Venice CPU 500萬顆、Google CPU逾400萬顆、AWS CPU逾300萬顆、微軟CPU約100萬顆，以及Meta CPU 10至20萬顆。

  ## 封裝測試廠產能擴張

  先進封裝產能建置的浪潮已延伸至台積電以外，帶動委外封裝測試廠（OSAT，即代替晶片業者承接封裝業務的廠商）同步擴充。全球最大封裝廠之一的日月光（ASE）預計2026年CoWoS月產能達2萬片，2027年進一步擴增至4至4.5萬片，主要服務超微Venice及輝達Vera CPU專案。Amkor則預計在2027年底前，將月產能擴張至2至2.5萬片。瑞穗對日月光與ASMPT均給予「買進」評等，視兩者為此波先進封裝趨勢的主要受益者。

  ## 設備採購

  設備訂單同步加速。台積電與日月光／矽品精密（SPIL，日月光旗下子公司）預計在2026年向芝浦機械（Shibaura）採購逾130台TCB（熱壓鍵合，一種精密晶片堆疊製程）設備。台積電同時計劃在2026年下半年至2027年上半年間，向K&S或ASMPT採購50至80台無助焊劑TCB設備。

  ## 英特爾先進封裝進展

  瑞穗在報告中亦指出，英特爾在自有先進封裝技術方面取得進展。報告稱，英特爾的EMIB-T晶片互連技術良率目前已超過95%，並正與聯發科、Ampere Computing、AWS及特斯拉（Tesla）洽談技術導入合作。

  ## 評等與目標價

  瑞穗對台積電給予「買進」評等，目標價為新台幣3,000元。對荷蘭光刻機製造商艾司摩爾（ASML）同樣給予「買進」評等，目標價為2,000歐元；日月光與ASMPT亦雙雙獲「買進」評等。

  ## 意義

  CoWoS與先進製程產能的大幅上調，顯示AI基礎建設的需求正以超出業界先前預估的速度，提前拉動晶片產能擴張，封裝廠與設備供應商則順勢成為次級受益者。

  瑞穗點出三項決定其樂觀預測能否實現的關鍵變數：輝達Vera與Rubin的量產時程能否如期推進；CSP自研晶片計畫是否進一步加速；以及台積電N2製程良率能否快速拉升，以支撐2027至2028年預期產能擴張目標的達成。
