body_en: |
  IBM Research has publicly announced a three-dimensional transistor architecture it calls the "nanostack," targeting geometries at the 7-angstrom (0.7 nanometer) node — below 1 nanometer — that the company says will propel the semiconductor industry forward for at least the next decade.

  The new design packs 100 billion transistors and delivers nearly twice the transistor density of IBM's 2nm chips, which the company debuted in 2021. IBM said the nanostack architecture provides up to 50 percent more performance or 70 percent greater energy efficiency for generative AI or cloud applications compared to the 2nm generation.

  ## What the nanostack architecture is

  The nanostack is the industry's first known three-dimensional, nanosheet-based transistor design, IBM said. It is built by vertically stacking and staggering transistors rather than extending them laterally across a silicon wafer. Different material combinations are used within each stacked layer, which IBM says allows the performance and power efficiency of each transistor to be optimized independently from the others.

  IBM said the architecture has been experimentally validated through three milestones: ultra-thin dielectric bonding in CMOS (complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor) integration; demonstration of dual-channel engineering capability; and functional CMOS inverter operation with expected switching performance.

  The research was conducted at IBM's chip research facility in Albany, New York. That facility will soon house a High Numerical Aperture Extreme Ultraviolet — High NA EUV — lithography tool from ASML, a Dutch semiconductor equipment maker whose tools are used for the most advanced chip patterning.

  ## The transistor generation it supersedes

  The nanostack is designed as a long-range successor to Gate All Around, or GAAFET, transistors — the architecture that the three major chip contract fabs, TSMC, Intel, and Samsung, are currently transitioning to.

  GAAFET is itself the successor to FinFET transistors, which entered commercial use with Intel in 2012. FinFET transistors were a solution to the electron leakage problems that afflicted earlier planar, or flat, transistors at small process geometries. A GAAFET transistor takes that approach further, placing a gate — the component that switches a transistor on and off — around the entire channel through which current flows, reducing leakage by enclosing it on all sides. Each GAAFET transistor also uses multiple thin parallel channels, called nanosheets, to further enhance control. Samsung's SF3E, Intel's 18A, and TSMC's N2 process nodes are among the GAAFET generations that have reached high-volume manufacturing in recent years.

  ## Why development of a successor is already underway

  Despite GAAFET only recently entering high-volume production, the timeline for qualifying its successor demands that research begin now. As transistor dimensions continue to shrink, GAAFET nanosheets will become too thin to contain electron leakage reliably, limiting how far the architecture can scale. According to Imec, the independent semiconductor research nonprofit that conducts foundational nanoelectronics research for the industry, GAAFET is expected to run its course in the early-to-mid 2030s — a window that gives current cutting-edge GAAFET nodes roughly five to seven more years of usefulness before a successor is required.

  That compressed window reflects a broader deceleration in transistor scaling. For nearly five decades, it was possible to shrink transistors by approximately 50 percent every two years. In the current decade, the pace has slowed to roughly one scaling step every five years, with SRAM and analog logic designs falling even further behind.

  ## What it means for the industry

  IBM's announcement positions the nanostack as the architecture intended to carry semiconductor scaling through the 2030s and into the following decade. The vertical stacking approach enables density gains that do not depend solely on reducing the lithographic feature size printed on a wafer. IBM also said the stacking design can be extended to additional layers, potentially enabling further transistor density increases in future generations.

  "IBM's latest chip breakthrough marks a landmark moment in computing, pushing technology beyond the nanometer era to the scale of atoms," said Jay Gambetta, director of IBM Research and IBM Fellow. "With our new nanostack architecture, we're not just making smaller transistors, we're reinventing how chips are built to deliver dramatically more power and energy efficiency."

  IBM's Albany research facility, where the nanostack was developed, is set to receive an ASML High NA EUV lithography tool, extending its capability to support further advanced semiconductor research at the site.

  ## IBM宣布次奈米奈米堆疊電晶體架構，鎖定7埃節點

  IBM研究院正式對外公布一種三維電晶體架構，命名為「奈米堆疊」（nanostack），目標製程節點為7埃（0.7奈米），突破1奈米門檻。IBM表示，這項設計將推動半導體產業向前邁進至少十年。

  新架構可容納1,000億顆電晶體，電晶體密度幾乎是IBM 2021年發表的2奈米晶片的兩倍。IBM指出，與2奈米世代相比，奈米堆疊架構在生成式AI或雲端應用上最多可提升50%效能，或提高70%能源效率。

  ## 奈米堆疊架構是什麼

  IBM表示，奈米堆疊是業界首款已知的三維奈米片（nanosheet）電晶體設計。其建構方式是將電晶體垂直堆疊並交錯排列，而非沿矽晶圓水平延伸。每個堆疊層內使用不同的材料組合，IBM表示此舉可讓每顆電晶體的效能與功耗效率各自獨立優化。

  IBM指出，這項架構已透過三個里程碑完成實驗驗證：在CMOS（互補式金屬氧化物半導體）整合中實現超薄介電質接合；展示雙通道工程能力；以及以預期切換效能完成CMOS反相器的功能運作。

  相關研究在IBM位於紐約州奧爾巴尼的晶片研究設施進行。該設施即將引進荷蘭半導體設備廠艾司摩爾（ASML）的高數值孔徑極紫外光（High NA EUV）微影設備——此類設備用於最先進的晶片圖案成像工序。

  ## 奈米堆疊所取代的電晶體世代

  奈米堆疊的設計定位是全環繞閘極電晶體（GAAFET）的長程接班人。GAAFET正是台積電、英特爾與三星三大晶圓代工廠目前正積極轉型導入的架構。

  GAAFET本身是鰭式場效電晶體（FinFET）的後繼者。FinFET於2012年由英特爾率先導入商業量產，當時是為了解決先前平面型電晶體在微縮製程下出現的電子漏電問題。GAAFET更進一步，將閘極——也就是負責切換電晶體開關的元件——環繞在電流流通的整個通道外側，透過四面圍繞的方式降低漏電。每顆GAAFET電晶體還使用多條薄型平行通道，稱為奈米片，以進一步強化控制能力。三星的SF3E、英特爾的18A，以及台積電的N2製程節點，均屬於近年進入大規模量產的GAAFET世代。

  ## 為何現在就已著手開發下一代架構

  儘管GAAFET才剛進入大規模量產，其接班技術的認證時程卻要求研究工作現在就必須展開。隨著電晶體尺寸持續縮小，GAAFET的奈米片將變得過薄，無法可靠地抑制電子漏電，使得這項架構的微縮潛力受到限制。根據獨立半導體研究非營利機構比利時微電子研究中心（Imec）的預估，GAAFET預計將在2030年代初至中期走到盡頭，意味著現行最先進的GAAFET節點僅剩約五至七年的使用壽命，之後便需要接班技術登場。

  這個緊迫的時間窗口，反映出電晶體微縮的整體腳步正在放緩。近五十年來，電晶體每兩年可縮小約50%；進入本十年後，縮小的節奏已減緩至大約每五年才推進一個製程世代，SRAM與類比邏輯設計的進展甚至更為落後。

  ## 對產業的意義

  IBM此次發表，將奈米堆疊定位為承載半導體微縮技術跨越2030年代、延伸至下一個十年的架構。垂直堆疊的方式所帶來的密度提升，不再完全依賴縮小晶圓上微影圖案的線寬。IBM也表示，此堆疊設計未來可延伸至更多層，有望在後續世代實現更高的電晶體密度。

  IBM研究院院長暨IBM院士傑伊・甘貝塔（Jay Gambetta）表示：「IBM最新的晶片突破標誌著運算領域的歷史性時刻，將技術推進到奈米時代之外，進入原子尺度的領域。透過我們全新的奈米堆疊架構，我們不只是在製造更小的電晶體，而是在重新發明晶片的建構方式，以實現大幅躍升的效能與能源效率。」

  奈米堆疊研發所在的IBM奧爾巴尼研究設施，即將迎來一台艾司摩爾High NA EUV微影設備，進一步提升該場址支援更先進半導體研究的能力。
