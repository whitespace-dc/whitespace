body_en: |
  SK Hynix, the South Korean memory chip manufacturer, warned in its third-quarter 2026 financial report that a global shortage of memory chips — including the DRAM (dynamic random-access memory) used in servers, PCs, and smartphones — will persist through 2030, as AI-driven demand outstrips the industry's ability to expand production.

  The company said demand is growing across every category it serves: server DDR5 (the current high-performance standard for server RAM) sales have increased significantly, shipments of HBM4 — the latest generation of High Bandwidth Memory, a stacked chip architecture used in AI accelerators — are rising and are expected to surpass the previous HBM3E generation in both volume and revenue in the fourth quarter of 2026. SK Hynix also said its DRAM and HBM capacity is already sold out through 2029.

  ## Why supply cannot catch up

  SK Hynix attributed the supply gap to two structural constraints. First, bringing new fabrication facilities online takes years. Second, manufacturing each successive generation of memory chips has become more technically complex, reducing the speed at which capacity can be added. The company's M15X fab is targeted to reach full production by the end of 2026, at a rate of roughly 55,000 to 60,000 wafers — the large silicon discs from which individual chips are cut — per month using its 1b-generation DRAM process. Even at that scale, the company said the additional output is not expected to be sufficient to close the demand gap before 2030.

  ## Demand drivers: AI near term, physical AI longer term

  In the near term, SK Hynix said demand will be driven by enterprise and consumer AI applications. Over the medium and longer term, the company forecasts rising requirements from what it called physical AI — a category that includes autonomous vehicle systems and robotics — which are expected to place sustained new loads on memory supply chains.

  Data center infrastructure investment is cited as a primary demand catalyst. Beyond DRAM, SK Hynix supplies LPDDR5 SOCAMM modules developed specifically for AI server configurations, and is developing High Bandwidth Flash (HBF), a storage-class memory technology for AI servers designed to manage datasets that exceed the capacity of conventional RAM.

  ## NAND and storage

  On the storage side, SK Hynix reported growth in sales of its 321-layer TLC NAND — a high-density flash storage technology used in enterprise SSDs — and said it is targeting that process node to account for more than half of its domestic NAND production capacity by the end of 2026.

  ## Industry implications

  A multi-year memory shortage would sustain elevated pricing across the hardware stack, affecting server procurement costs for data center operators, as well as component budgets for consumer devices including smartphones. The constraint on memory supply is not limited to AI infrastructure: SK Hynix noted that pressure would extend across a broad range of electronics.

  The company's forecast that even its planned capacity expansions will fall short of demand through the end of the decade points to a prolonged period of constrained supply for data center planners relying on DRAM and HBM availability for AI system deployments. HBM4 shipments are expected to exceed HBM3E in volume and revenue starting in Q4 2026, with that transition continuing as the primary supply source for AI accelerator memory into the next several years.

  SK海力士為南韓記憶體晶片製造商，該公司在2026年第三季財報中警告，全球記憶體晶片短缺——涵蓋伺服器、個人電腦與智慧型手機所使用的DRAM（動態隨機存取記憶體）——將持續延燒至2030年，原因在於AI帶動的需求已全面超越產業擴產的能力。

  該公司指出，旗下每一業務類別的需求均呈現成長態勢：伺服器DDR5（目前伺服器RAM的高效能標準）銷售大幅提升；HBM4出貨量持續攀升——HBM4是高頻寬記憶體（HBM）的最新世代，採用堆疊晶片架構，專用於AI加速器——預計在2026年第四季，HBM4無論在出貨量或營收上，皆將超越前一代HBM3E。SK海力士並表示，其DRAM與HBM產能已全數售罄至2029年。

  ## 供給為何難以趕上

  SK海力士將供需缺口歸因於兩項結構性制約。其一，新建晶圓廠從動工到量產需耗費數年時間。其二，每一世代記憶體晶片的製造技術日益精密複雜，大幅壓縮了產能擴增的速度。該公司的M15X晶圓廠預計於2026年底達到滿載生產，月產能約為5萬5千至6萬片晶圓——晶圓是從中切割出個別晶片的大型矽圓盤——採用其第1b世代DRAM製程。然而，該公司表示，即便達到這一規模，這批新增產能在2030年以前，預計仍不足以填補供需缺口。

  ## 需求驅動因素：近期AI、長期實體AI

  就短期而言，SK海力士表示需求將由企業與消費者AI應用所帶動。就中長期展望，該公司預測來自其所稱「實體AI」的需求將持續升溫——這一類別涵蓋自駕車系統與機器人技術——預計將對記憶體供應鏈形成持續且全新的負荷壓力。

  資料中心基礎建設投資被列為主要需求催化劑。除DRAM之外，SK海力士亦供應專為AI伺服器配置所開發的LPDDR5 SOCAMM模組，並正在研發「高頻寬快閃記憶體」（High Bandwidth Flash，HBF）——一種面向AI伺服器的儲存級記憶體技術，設計目的是管理超出傳統RAM容量上限的龐大資料集。

  ## NAND與儲存

  在儲存業務方面，SK海力士報告其321層TLC NAND銷售呈現成長——這是一種應用於企業級SSD的高密度快閃儲存技術——並表示，目標是在2026年底前，讓該製程節點佔其國內NAND生產產能的一半以上。

  ## 產業影響

  若記憶體短缺延續數年，將使整個硬體供應鏈長期維持高價格，不僅推升資料中心業者的伺服器採購成本，更衝擊包括智慧型手機在內的消費性裝置零組件預算。記憶體供給的瓶頸並不僅限於AI基礎建設：SK海力士指出，這股壓力將蔓延至廣泛的電子產品領域。

  該公司預測，即便已規劃中的產能擴建全數到位，在本十年結束前供給仍將持續吃緊。對於倚賴DRAM與HBM供應來推進AI系統部署的資料中心規劃人員而言，這意味著一段漫長的供應受限期。HBM4出貨量預計從2026年第四季起，在出貨量與營收上雙雙超越HBM3E，且這一世代交替將在未來數年持續延伸，成為AI加速器記憶體的主要供給來源。
