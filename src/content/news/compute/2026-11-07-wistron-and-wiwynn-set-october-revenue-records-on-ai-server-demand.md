body_en: |
  Wistron and Wiwynn, two Taiwanese contract manufacturers, each set all-time records for October revenue while also posting their second-highest single-month revenue totals on record, the companies announced on November 7.

  Wistron (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 3231) reported October combined revenue of NT$402.615 billion (New Taiwan dollars), up 5.7% from September and up 117.6% from October 2025. Its cumulative revenue for the first ten months of 2026 reached NT$3.29 trillion, a same-period all-time high, up 99.5% year-on-year.

  Wiwynn reported October combined revenue of NT$165.132 billion, down 4.3% month-on-month but up 80.9% from October a year earlier. Its January-through-October 2026 cumulative revenue totaled NT$1.15 trillion, up 54% from the same period in 2025, also a same-period all-time high.

  ## AI servers offset sharp PC and monitor declines

  Within Wistron's October totals, conventional computing and display product lines fell across the board. Laptop shipments reached 1.2 million units, down 20% from September and 42.9% from October 2025. Desktop shipments came to 500,000 units, flat month-on-month and down 44.4% year-on-year. Monitor shipments were 850,000 units, down 10.5% from September and 5.6% from a year earlier.

  AI server shipments held strong, the company said, with the majority of fourth-quarter deliveries concentrated in November and December.

  ## Fourth-quarter and full-year outlook

  Institutional investors cited in the report expect both Wistron and Wiwynn to set new quarterly revenue records in the fourth quarter, driven by continued AI server shipment momentum, and project full-year performance to reach new highs for each company.

  For Wistron specifically, those investors said fourth-quarter growth will come from new project launches and the start of shipments for some VR (virtual reality) platform servers, with AI server volumes forecast to rise 5% to 10% quarter-on-quarter. Because laptop shipment volumes have contracted sharply, server revenue is expected to account for close to 90% of Wistron's total second-half revenue, the investors said.

  With most of Wistron's fourth-quarter AI server deliveries scheduled for November and December, the final two months of the quarter will carry the heaviest weight in determining whether the company reaches a new quarterly record.

  ## AI伺服器需求強勁　緯創、緯穎10月營收雙雙創單月新高

  台灣代工廠緯創與緯穎於11月7日相繼公告，兩家公司10月合併營收均刷新歷史單月紀錄，同時也分別寫下各自有史以來的次高單月營收成績。

  緯創（台灣證券交易所代號：3231）公告10月合併營收為新台幣4,026.15億元，較9月成長5.7%，較2025年10月大幅躍升117.6%。累計2026年前十個月合併營收達新台幣3.29兆元，同樣創下同期歷史新高，年增幅達99.5%。

  緯穎則公告10月合併營收為新台幣1,651.32億元，雖較上月微幅下滑4.3%，但仍較去年同期大幅成長80.9%。2026年1月至10月累計營收合計新台幣1.15兆元，較2025年同期成長54%，同樣締造同期歷史新高。

  ## AI伺服器全力撐盤，PC與顯示器線大幅下滑

  就緯創10月各產品線表現來看，傳統運算及顯示器產品全面下滑。筆記型電腦出貨量為120萬台，較9月衰退20%，較2025年10月更大幅下滑42.9%。桌上型電腦出貨量為50萬台，月持平，但年減幅達44.4%。顯示器出貨量為85萬台，月減10.5%，年減5.6%。

  不過，AI伺服器出貨維持強勁動能。緯創表示，第四季多數出貨集中於11月與12月。

  ## 第四季與全年展望

  法人報告指出，市場預期緯創與緯穎均可望在第四季寫下單季營收新高，主要動力來自AI伺服器持續放量出貨，預估全年業績亦將各自再創歷史新高。

  就緯創而言，法人認為第四季成長動能將來自新專案的陸續量產，以及部分VR（虛擬實境）平台伺服器開始出貨，AI伺服器出貨量預計將較第三季季增5%至10%。由於筆記型電腦出貨量大幅萎縮，伺服器營收佔緯創下半年總營收比重，預計將逼近90%。

  由於緯創第四季AI伺服器出貨量大多集中在11月與12月，這最後兩個月的表現將是決定公司能否順利突破單季營收歷史紀錄的關鍵所在。
