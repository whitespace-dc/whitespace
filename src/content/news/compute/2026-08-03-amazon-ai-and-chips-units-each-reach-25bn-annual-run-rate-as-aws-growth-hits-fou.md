body_en: |
  Amazon said its artificial intelligence and chips businesses each hit $25 billion annual run rates in the second quarter, with both growing at triple-digit percentages year-over-year, as the company posted accelerating cloud revenue that sent its shares to their largest single-day gain in more than a decade.

  Amazon Web Services (AWS), the company's cloud-computing division, posted revenue of $42.2 billion in the quarter ended June 30, a 37% increase year-over-year and the fastest growth rate since the fourth quarter of 2021, the company said. Analysts had forecast $40.6 billion on average, according to data compiled by Bloomberg. It was the fifth consecutive quarter of rising AWS sales.

  Amazon shares rose 15% to $271.58 at the close on Friday in New York — the biggest one-day move since April 2012 — after the results were released Thursday. The stock had gained just 2% through Thursday's close for the year prior to the report.

  ## Capital spending and the AI build-out

  Amazon reported spending more than $53 billion on property and equipment, including proceeds from some asset sales, in the quarter ended June 30. The company also raised its projection for full-year 2026 capital expenditures to $220 billion from a prior estimate of $200 billion. Chief executive Andy Jassy said most of that spending would go toward AI.

  "We're unusually well-positioned for this AI inflection," Jassy said during a post-earnings conference call.

  The scale of that investment pushed free cash flow negative. Amazon reported an outflow of $7.6 billion for the trailing 12 months at quarter's end — meaning the company spent more cash than it generated over that period when capital expenditures are included.

  Jassy said the investment pace would moderate over time. He noted that data centres being built now are designed to operate for 30 years, while the equipment inside will require updates every five to six years, meaning future modernisation costs will be lower relative to the current ground-up construction phase.

  Sky Canaves, an analyst at Emarketer, said in a statement that investors would likely be unfazed by the higher spending and negative cash flow "given the accelerating pace of AWS revenue growth and strict cost controls in other areas."

  ## Broader second-quarter results

  Total company revenue rose 20% to $200.6 billion in the quarter, above the analyst consensus of $197 billion, Amazon said. AWS generates roughly one-fifth of total revenue but accounts for the majority of Amazon's operating profit.

  Online store sales increased 15% to $70.4 billion, compared with the analyst average estimate of $69.9 billion. Amazon also reported receiving $600 million in tariff refunds during the quarter; chief financial officer Brian Olsavsky said some of that amount would be returned to consumers.

  For the third quarter, Amazon guided for revenue of $197 billion to $202 billion and operating income of $22.5 billion to $26.5 billion. Analysts had estimated $203.9 billion in revenue and $25.1 billion in operating profit on average.

  ## Industry context

  Amazon's results followed similar earnings from its major cloud rivals. Microsoft reported its fastest cloud growth since 2022 and said it would hold the line on new capital spending this year; its shares rose their most in a single day since 2008. Alphabet reported heavier spending than anticipated and negative free cash flow for the first time since its initial public offering, and its stock declined.

  ## Significance for data centre and compute markets

  The simultaneous $25 billion annual run rate across both AI services and chips — each expanding at triple-digit rates — signals that Amazon's internal chip development is scaling to a level that registers as a standalone business line comparable to major cloud product categories. The $220 billion capital expenditure commitment for 2026, the majority directed at AI infrastructure according to Jassy, represents one of the largest single-year data centre and compute build-out targets disclosed by any company to date.

  亞馬遜表示，旗下人工智慧與晶片業務在第二季各自達到250億美元的年化營收規模，兩項業務的年增率均呈三位數成長。受這份亮眼財報提振，公司股價創下逾十年來最大單日漲幅。

  亞馬遜雲端運算部門亞馬遜網路服務（Amazon Web Services，AWS）在截至6月30日的季度中，營收達422億美元，年增37%，為2021年第四季以來最快的成長速度。根據彭博社匯整的數據，分析師平均預估為406億美元。這是AWS連續第五個季度繳出營收成長佳績。

  財報係於週四盤後公布，消息傳出後，亞馬遜股價於紐約時間週五收盤大漲15%，報271.58美元，創下自2012年4月以來最大單日漲幅。在這份財報公布之前，亞馬遜股價今年以來的累計漲幅僅約2%。

  ## 資本支出與AI基礎建設

  在截至6月30日的季度中，亞馬遜用於廠房與設備的支出（含部分資產出售收益）逾530億美元。公司亦將2026年全年資本支出預估從原先的2,000億美元上調至2,200億美元。執行長安迪·賈西（Andy Jassy）表示，這些支出的大部分將用於AI相關投資。

  「我們在這波AI轉折點上具備了難得的優勢地位，」賈西在財報後的法說會上表示。

  龐大的投資規模使自由現金流轉為負值。亞馬遜公布，截至本季結束為止的過去12個月，自由現金流淨流出76億美元——意即在計入資本支出後，公司同期的支出已高於其所創造的現金。

  賈西表示，投資步伐將隨時間逐步放緩。他指出，目前興建中的資料中心設計使用年限長達30年，而內部設備則每五至六年需要更新一次，這意味著相較於現階段的全新建設工程，未來現代化改造的成本將相對較低。

  市場研究機構Emarketer分析師Sky Canaves在一份聲明中指出，「鑑於AWS營收成長持續加速，加上其他業務領域的嚴格成本控制，」投資人對較高的支出規模與負自由現金流「可能並不會太在意」。

  ## 第二季整體財報表現

  亞馬遜公布，第二季全公司總營收年增20%至2,006億美元，高於分析師平均預估的1,970億美元。AWS約佔公司總營收的五分之一，但在營業利益的貢獻上卻佔大宗。

  網路商店銷售額年增15%至704億美元，略高於分析師平均預估的699億美元。亞馬遜亦公布本季收到6億美元的關稅退款；財務長布萊恩·奧爾薩夫斯基（Brian Olsavsky）表示，其中部分金額將返還給消費者。

  展望第三季，亞馬遜預估營收介於1,970億至2,020億美元，營業利益則介於225億至265億美元。分析師平均預估的第三季營收為2,039億美元，營業利益為251億美元。

  ## 產業背景

  亞馬遜的財報緊接在主要雲端競爭對手公布業績之後。微軟公布了自2022年以來最快的雲端成長速度，並宣示今年將維持新資本支出規模不變；其股價創下自2008年以來最大單日漲幅。Alphabet則公布支出超乎預期，且自首次公開募股（IPO）以來首度出現負自由現金流，股價因此走低。

  ## 對資料中心與運算市場的意義

  AI服務與晶片兩項業務同步達到各250億美元的年化營收，且均以三位數速度擴張，這顯示亞馬遜的自研晶片業務已擴展至可獨立列為一條業務線的規模，足以媲美主要雲端產品類別。賈西表示，2026年2,200億美元資本支出承諾中的大部分將投注於AI基礎建設，這代表迄今為止任何一家企業所揭露的最大規模單年度資料中心與運算建設目標之一。
