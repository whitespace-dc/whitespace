body_en: |
  ViTrox Corporation Bhd, a Malaysian machine vision specialist, reported a 193% surge in net profit for the first nine months of 2026 as AI infrastructure spending drove accelerated demand for its semiconductor and electronics inspection equipment, but an analyst downgraded the stock on 10 November, warning that supply constraints and a slowing order book could cap further earnings gains.

  The company's nine-month net profit attributable to shareholders reached RM254.8 million (US$62.4 million), up from RM87 million a year earlier, according to a quarterly filing released on 5 November. Revenue for the period doubled, rising 103.9% to US$270 million (RM1.13 billion) from US$135.37 million. The profit margin expanded from 15.7% to 22.6%. (All ringgit figures at RM1 = US$0.245.)

  Third-quarter results accelerated further: revenue for the three months ended 30 September rose 112.2% year-on-year to RM485.1 million, while net profit climbed 241.6% to RM118.6 million.

  ## AI infrastructure powers inspection demand

  AmInvestment Bank analyst Paul Yap Ee Xing, in a 10 November research note, attributed the growth to rising AI infrastructure investment. Communications equipment now accounts for 42% of ViTrox's group revenue, Yap said.

  One unnamed major hyperscaler has adopted ViTrox's QX automated X-ray inspection (AXI) equipment as its standard inspection platform, according to Yap. That customer contributes approximately 15% of group revenue.

  AXI machine shipments nearly tripled to 309 units in the nine-month period from 107 units a year earlier, reflecting increased inspection requirements for advanced electronics and AI hardware, AmInvestment said.

  ## Automated board inspection business more than doubles

  ViTrox's automated board inspection (ABI) business generated RM695 million in the first nine months, more than double the RM335 million recorded in the prior-year period.

  The ABI order backlog stood at RM399 million at the end of September, providing revenue visibility into the first half of 2027, according to AmInvestment.

  ## Analyst downgrades on slowing orders and component shortage

  AmInvestment downgraded ViTrox from Buy to Hold while maintaining its target price at RM10.25. The bank cited two concerns.

  ViTrox's book-to-bill ratio, which measures new orders received relative to equipment shipped and billed, eased to 1.1 times from 1.5 times in the preceding quarter. A ratio above 1.0 indicates order intake still exceeds deliveries, but the decline signals moderating momentum.

  The more immediate concern is X-ray tube availability. X-ray tubes are a critical component of AXI machines, and ViTrox has drawn down its inventory to meet accelerated customer orders, leaving reduced material buffers for coming quarters, AmInvestment said. Efforts to source alternative X-ray tubes face pricing and technical compatibility challenges.

  AmInvestment forecast fourth-quarter revenue of RM403 million to RM459 million, below the third quarter's RM485.1 million.

  ## Outlook

  For the full year 2026, AmInvestment forecast ViTrox revenue of RM1.62 billion and core net profit of RM388.5 million. Revenue growth is projected to slow to 9.7% in 2027, a marked deceleration from the near-doubling recorded in 2026.

  ViTrox's machine vision capabilities were developed before Malaysia launched its National Semiconductor Strategy (NSS) in May 2024, a government initiative to build domestically owned semiconductor technology. The company's current expansion aligns with the NSS goals, AmInvestment noted.

  The central challenge for ViTrox entering 2027 is whether its supply chain, particularly X-ray tube sourcing, can keep pace with customers' increasingly sophisticated inspection requirements, the analyst said.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞機器視覺專業廠商威創科技（ViTrox Corporation Bhd），受AI基礎設施支出加速帶動旗下半導體與電子檢測設備需求大幅成長，2026年前九個月淨利勁揚193%。然而，艾芬安信投資銀行分析師於11月10日調降該股評等，警告供應端限制與訂單動能趨緩，恐壓抑後續獲利空間。

  根據威創科技11月5日公布的季報，前九個月歸屬股東淨利達2億5,480萬馬來西亞令吉（約6,240萬美元），較去年同期的8,700萬令吉大幅躍升；同期營收翻倍，年增103.9%至2億7,000萬美元（約11億3,000萬令吉），前一年同期為1億3,537萬美元，利潤率亦從15.7%提升至22.6%。（令吉匯率以1令吉兌0.245美元計算。）

  第三季業績進一步加速：截至9月30日止三個月，營收年增112.2%至4億8,510萬令吉，淨利更大幅攀升241.6%至1億1,860萬令吉。

  ## AI基礎設施帶動檢測需求

  艾芬安信投資銀行分析師葉依星（Paul Yap Ee Xing）於11月10日發布研究報告，將此一成長歸因於AI基礎設施投資持續升溫。葉依星指出，通訊設備目前已占威創科技集團營收的42%。

  報告指出，一家未具名的大型超大規模雲端業者，已將威創科技的QX自動化X光檢測（AXI）設備採用為其標準檢測平台，該客戶貢獻約15%的集團營收。

  艾芬安信表示，受先進電子與AI硬體檢測需求增加所驅動，前九個月AXI設備出貨量由去年同期的107台近乎三倍成長至309台。

  ## 自動化電路板檢測業務倍增

  威創科技自動化電路板檢測（ABI）業務前九個月營收達6億9,500萬令吉，較去年同期的3億3,500萬令吉超過翻倍。

  截至9月底，ABI在手訂單積壓達3億9,900萬令吉，艾芬安信表示，此一訂單量提供了延伸至2027年上半年的營收能見度。

  ## 分析師因訂單走緩與零組件短缺降評

  艾芬安信將威創科技評等由「買進」調降為「持有」，目標價維持在10.25令吉不變，並提出兩項主要疑慮。

  首先，衡量新接訂單相對於設備出貨及請款量的訂單出貨比，已由上一季的1.5倍降至1.1倍。比值高於1.0倍代表訂單量仍超過出貨量，但此一下滑趨勢顯示動能正在趨緩。

  更迫切的隱憂在於X光管的供應。X光管是AXI設備的關鍵零組件，威創科技為因應客戶加速拉貨，已大量消耗庫存，導致未來幾季的物料緩衝空間不足，艾芬安信表示。尋覓替代X光管的採購努力，同時面臨價格與技術相容性的雙重挑戰。

  艾芬安信預測第四季營收將介於4億300萬至4億5,900萬令吉之間，低於第三季的4億8,510萬令吉。

  ## 展望

  就2026年全年而言，艾芬安信預測威創科技營收將達16億2,000萬令吉，核心淨利3億8,850萬令吉。2027年營收成長率預計將放緩至9.7%，與2026年近乎翻倍的成長相比明顯減速。

  威創科技的機器視覺能力，早在馬來西亞於2024年5月推出「國家半導體策略（NSS）」——這項旨在建立本土半導體技術的政府計畫——之前便已發展成形。艾芬安信指出，該公司目前的擴張方向與NSS目標高度契合。

  分析師表示，威創科技邁入2027年所面臨的核心挑戰，在於其供應鏈——尤其是X光管的採購來源——能否跟上客戶日益精密的檢測需求。
