body_en: |
  Silk Cloud, a cloud subsidiary of Georgian telecommunications provider Silknet, has announced plans to deploy HPE Nvidia HGX B300 systems at data centers in Georgia, with a 2027 launch target pending regulatory permits. UK-based Fuse Energy separately announced a GPU compute offering using the same Nvidia B300 hardware, with its first 128 GPUs scheduled to come online in December 2026.

  ## Silk Cloud's Georgia deployment

  Silk Cloud is a subsidiary of Silknet, a telecommunications provider that has operated in Georgia since 2009, and part of the broader Silk Road Group. The cloud company launched in 2025 and offers cybersecurity, cloud systems, data management, and AI implementation services. The number of GPUs Silk Cloud plans to deploy has not been disclosed.

  The deployment is linked to a new data center that Silknet broke ground on in Tbilisi, Georgia's capital, in August 2026. That facility will provide 2 megawatts (MW) of compute capacity and is expected to be operational in 2027. It will sit on top of Silknet's national fiber infrastructure, which includes more than 4,000 kilometers (2,485 miles) of backbone cable with cross-border connections into Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Turkey, and onward routes to Europe, the Middle East, and Asia.

  Giorgi Ramishvili, chairman of Silk Road Group, said: "This investment is the first step in creating capabilities that will help Georgia position itself in the emerging global AI economy and allow it to become part of the infrastructure on which this economy relies."

  David Mamulaishvili, CEO of Silknet, added: "Georgia should have the opportunity not only to use AI, but also to create it itself. This will contribute to the country's competitiveness, the pace of innovation and the creation of new business opportunities. The introduction of the first Nvidia Blackwell Ultra system in Georgia is part of Silknet's long-term strategy, which envisages promoting advanced digital and AI development and strengthening the company's technological capabilities."

  ## Fuse Energy enters GPU compute

  Fuse Energy, a UK company that describes itself as a full-stack energy business aiming to lower the cost of energy and increase its availability, announced a GPU cloud service called Fuse Compute. The company said it will deploy Nvidia B300 GPUs in UK data centers that it will host and power itself; the operators of those facilities have not been named.

  Fuse has outlined a staged capacity rollout:

  - December 2026: 700 kW, 128 GPUs
  - Q2 2027: 3.25 MW, 1,024 GPUs
  - Q4 2027: 22 MW
  - 2028 and beyond: 116 MW

  The company said it has 142 MW of data center power connections in its pipeline across 15 projects. In a LinkedIn post, it tied the move into compute directly to its existing power capabilities: "The hardest part of scaling compute today is getting power to the site, which is the part we already do. With 142MW of data center connections in the pipeline across 15 projects, compute is our next step. We have the infrastructure to deliver this to businesses globally."

  ## Scale and context

  The two deployments share a hardware choice — the Nvidia B300 Blackwell Ultra GPU, with Silk Cloud specifically using the HPE Nvidia HGX B300 form factor — while operating at markedly different scales and in separate markets. Silk Cloud's announcement, if completed on its 2027 timeline, would introduce Blackwell Ultra compute capacity to Georgia for the first time, according to company executives. Fuse Energy's staged plan, underpinned by its existing pipeline of 142 MW in data center power connections across 15 UK projects, targets 116 MW of GPU capacity from 2028 onward.

  ## Silk Cloud與Fuse Energy分別宣布部署輝達Blackwell Ultra GPU

  Silk Cloud是喬治亞電信業者Silknet旗下的雲端子公司，日前宣布計畫在喬治亞的資料中心部署HPE輝達HGX B300系統，目標於2027年正式啟用，惟仍有待主管機關核發相關許可。英國的Fuse Energy也在同一時間宣布推出採用相同輝達B300硬體的GPU運算服務，首批128顆GPU預計於2026年12月上線。

  ## Silk Cloud的喬治亞部署計畫

  Silk Cloud隸屬於自2009年起在喬治亞深耕的電信業者Silknet，同時也是絲路集團（Silk Road Group）旗下成員。這家雲端公司於2025年正式成立，提供資安防護、雲端系統、資料管理及AI導入等服務。目前Silk Cloud並未透露預計部署的GPU總數量。

  此次部署與Silknet於2026年8月在喬治亞首都提比里斯動土興建的新資料中心密切相關。該設施將提供2百萬瓦（MW）的運算容量，預計2027年正式投入營運。新資料中心將架設於Silknet的全國光纖基礎建設之上——這套骨幹網路綿延逾4,000公里，並與亞美尼亞、亞塞拜然及土耳其跨境相連，進而串通歐洲、中東與亞洲各地。

  絲路集團董事長吉奧吉．拉米什維利（Giorgi Ramishvili）表示：「這項投資是打造相關能力的第一步，將幫助喬治亞在全球新興AI經濟中確立自身定位，並讓喬治亞成為支撐這套經濟體系的基礎建設一環。」

  Silknet執行長大衛．瑪穆萊什維利（David Mamulaishvili）也補充道：「喬治亞不應只是AI的使用者，更應有機會自行創造AI。這將有助於提升國家競爭力、加速創新步伐，並開創新的商業機會。在喬治亞引進首套輝達Blackwell Ultra系統，是Silknet長期策略的重要一環，旨在推動先進數位與AI發展，並強化公司的技術實力。」

  ## Fuse Energy跨足GPU運算市場

  Fuse Energy是一家英國企業，自我定位為全方位能源公司，致力於降低能源成本並擴大能源取用。該公司宣布推出名為「Fuse Compute」的GPU雲端服務，並表示將在英國的資料中心部署輝達B300 GPU，由公司自行負責託管與供電，但目前尚未公開相關設施的業者名稱。

  Fuse Energy規劃分階段擴充容量，時程如下：

  - 2026年12月：700千瓦（kW），128顆GPU
  - 2027年第二季：3.25 MW，1,024顆GPU
  - 2027年第四季：22 MW
  - 2028年及以後：116 MW

  該公司表示，其資料中心電力接入管線已涵蓋15個專案、合計142 MW的儲備容量。Fuse Energy在領英（LinkedIn）貼文中，將此次進軍運算市場與既有電力優勢直接掛鉤：「現今要擴充運算規模，最難的一關是讓電力到位，而這恰恰是我們本來就在做的事。我們在15個專案中已有142 MW的資料中心電力接入管線，運算自然是下一步。我們擁有向全球企業交付運算服務所需的基礎建設。」

  ## 規模比較與整體背景

  這兩項部署在硬體選擇上殊途同歸——均採用輝達B300 Blackwell Ultra GPU，其中Silk Cloud特別選用HPE輝達HGX B300規格——但兩者的部署規模與市場定位截然不同。根據公司高層的說法，若Silk Cloud能如期於2027年完成部署，這將是Blackwell Ultra運算容量首度進入喬治亞。Fuse Energy則以其在英國15個專案共142 MW的資料中心電力接入管線為後盾，分階段推進計畫，目標於2028年起達成116 MW的GPU運算容量。
