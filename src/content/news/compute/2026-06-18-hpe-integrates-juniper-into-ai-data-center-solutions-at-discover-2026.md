body_en: |
  HPE opened Discover 2026 in Las Vegas with a networking-first keynote that formally placed Juniper Networking inside its HPE AI Data Center Solutions portfolio, while CEO Antonio Neri announced more than a dozen new products spanning switches, routers, firewalls, servers, storage, and agentic AI software.

  The structure of the presentation was itself a signal: HPE led with its self-driving network initiative before turning to compute and software, an ordering that reflected the deepening integration of Juniper Networks into the company following its acquisition.

  ## Switching and routing lineup

  The lead announcement was that HPE Juniper Networking is now formally part of HPE AI Data Center Solutions. HPE then disclosed six switching and routing products aimed at AI infrastructure.

  The QFX5252 is a scale-up switch designed for the AMD Helios AI rack. It supports UALink over Ethernet (UALoE) — a high-speed interconnect protocol for linking AI accelerators within a rack — runs the open-source SONiC (Software for Open Networking in the Cloud) network operating system with what HPE calls AI-Native Operations, and is liquid-cooled. According to the source covering the event, the QFX5252 uses a dual Broadcom Tomahawk 6 switching ASIC.

  For scale-out networking — connecting racks to each other and to the broader data center fabric — HPE announced the QFX5250, a liquid-cooled switch rated at 102.5 terabits per second (Tbps) of total throughput.

  The QFX5140 Inference Switch is optimized for AI inference workloads — the process of running a trained AI model against new inputs — and delivers 16 Tbps in a single rack unit (1U). The source noted that 16 Tbps is an uncommon capacity point between the more widely deployed 12.8 Tbps and 25.6 Tbps designs, and identified the likely underlying component as Broadcom's Trident 5 ASIC.

  For inter-data-center routing, HPE announced updates to the PTX12000 family, supporting 800 Gbps routing speeds with ZR and ZR+ coherent optics. Coherent optics encode optical signals so they can travel long distances without electrical signal regeneration; ZR+ extends that reach to approximately 1,000 kilometers.

  The MX301 is a new router aimed at inference edge deployments — locations where AI inference runs close to end users rather than in a central facility. It delivers 1.6 Tbps in a 1U form factor with full-duplex 400 Gigabit Ethernet connectivity and AIOps (artificial-intelligence-driven operations) management, built on HPE's sixth-generation Trio silicon.

  HPE also announced the SRX4700, a quantum-safe firewall delivering 1.4 Tbps in 1U. Quantum-safe cryptography refers to encryption algorithms designed to resist attacks from quantum computers, which can break many encryption standards in use today.

  For enterprise campus and branch networking, HPE said its CX Switches will be managed through HPE Mist, and that HPE Marvis — its AI networking assistant — will gain conversational AI-driven capabilities within HPE Aruba Central for automated network management tasks.

  HPE also detailed an AI Grid built with NVIDIA that combines NVIDIA Spectrum-X networking, BlueField-3 data processing units (DPUs), and ConnectX network adapters alongside HPE Juniper Networking and ProLiant servers.

  ## Compute and AI factory tiers

  On the server side, HPE highlighted the ProLiant Gen12 lineup, including the ProLiant Compute DL394 Gen12 powered by NVIDIA and the ProLiant Compute EL2000, an edge server built on Intel Xeon 6. HPE noted ProLiant Nonstop Updates support on Intel Xeon 6 and announced NVIDIA Vera-based systems, without providing further specification detail in the keynote.

  HPE said confidential computing — a technique that protects data while it is actively being processed in memory — will be standard across its AI Factory portfolio for NVIDIA-based systems.

  HPE structured its AI infrastructure offerings into three tiers for distinct customer segments:

  - **HPE Private Cloud AI**: for enterprises
  - **HPE AI Factory at Scale**: for neoclouds and hyperscale operators
  - **HPE AI Factory Sovereign**: for governments, defense agencies, and similar organizations

  ## Agentic AI and software

  HPE introduced what it calls the Agentic Enterprise framework, aimed at organizations running AI agents at scale. The company said customers will be able to register agents and apply governance and security policies across them, and announced support for NVIDIA OpenShell and NemoClaw, which are NVIDIA frameworks for agent orchestration.

  New inference capabilities announced include multi-node inference — distributing a single large model across multiple servers — a unified inference gateway, and shared key-value (KV) cache. KV caching allows multiple inference requests to reuse previously computed intermediate data, reducing both latency and compute consumption.

  HPE GreenLake received updates under the GreenLake Intelligence banner, with agentic AI applied to operational tasks including automated infrastructure troubleshooting.

  For enterprise virtualization, HPE said its CloudOps platform is expanding the Morpheus environment management tool to cover more customers migrating away from VMware as licensing costs for that platform have increased.

  HPE also announced the Unleash AI partner ecosystem, designed to help customers find validated combinations of HPE and third-party solutions.

  ## Storage

  The HPE Alletra Storage MP X10000 was positioned as the foundation layer for AI-native file and object storage, with real-time data enrichment and native MCP (Model Context Protocol) integration. MCP is an open standard that allows AI models to query and interact with external data sources directly. The HPE Alletra B10000 was cited for block storage workloads.

  ## Security

  HPE's Silicon Root of Trust — a hardware-level mechanism that cryptographically verifies firmware integrity before a server boots — was highlighted as part of the company's cyber resilience messaging. The SRX4700 quantum-safe firewall addressed network-layer security.

  ## Customer references

  HPE cited Vultr, a cloud infrastructure provider, as a partner on next-generation AI infrastructure construction including HPE Networking. Siemens Energy appeared in a customer segment focused on using HPE systems for predictive hardware failure detection.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The networking-first structure of the HPE keynote stands in contrast to Dell Technologies' approach at Dell Tech World 2026, where compute and AI-driven business transformation were the leading themes, according to the source. NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang appeared on stage at Dell Tech World but was not present at HPE Discover, even as NVIDIA products featured throughout HPE's announcements across compute, networking, and software.

  The contrast reflects how the two major server vendors are now presenting themselves in the AI infrastructure market: HPE, following the Juniper acquisition, is positioning as a networking company with AI compute capabilities alongside it, while Dell is positioning as a compute company that sells networking as an accompanying product.

  HPE's three-tier AI Factory structure — enterprise, neocloud and hyperscale, and sovereign — combined with the Unleash AI partner ecosystem, is how the company says it intends to address distinct customer segments in the AI infrastructure market going forward.
body_zh: |
  HPE以「網路優先」為主題揭開Discover 2026拉斯維加斯大會的序幕，正式宣布Juniper Networking納入HPE AI資料中心解決方案組合，執行長Antonio Neri同步發表逾十二項新品，涵蓋交換器、路由器、防火牆、伺服器、儲存系統及智慧型代理AI軟體。

  此次演講的架構本身即是一種訊號：HPE率先以自動駕駛網路（self-driving network）倡議為開場，再轉向運算與軟體，這樣的順序反映了自收購Juniper Networks以來，雙方整合持續深化的現況。

  ## 交換器與路由器產品線

  首要公告是HPE Juniper Networking正式成為HPE AI資料中心解決方案的一部分。HPE隨後揭露六款針對AI基礎架構的交換器與路由器新品。

  QFX5252是一款專為AMD Helios AI機架設計的擴充型（scale-up）交換器，支援透過乙太網路傳輸的UALink（UALoE）——一種用於連結機架內AI加速器的高速互連協定——執行開源SONiC（Software for Open Networking in the Cloud）網路作業系統，並搭載HPE所稱的AI原生操作（AI-Native Operations），且採液冷設計。根據現場報導，QFX5252採用雙Broadcom Tomahawk 6交換ASIC。

  針對橫向擴展（scale-out）網路——即連結各機架彼此及整體資料中心架構——HPE發表QFX5250，這是一款液冷交換器，總吞吐量達每秒102.5兆位元（Tbps）。

  QFX5140推論交換器專為AI推論工作負載最佳化——所謂推論，即以訓練完成的AI模型對新輸入資料進行處理——可在單一機架單元（1U）中提供16 Tbps頻寬。現場報導指出，16 Tbps是介於較普遍部署的12.8 Tbps與25.6 Tbps之間一個少見的容量規格，並研判其底層元件很可能是Broadcom的Trident 5 ASIC。

  針對資料中心間的路由需求，HPE宣布PTX12000系列的更新，支援搭配ZR及ZR+同調光學（coherent optics）的800 Gbps路由速度。同調光學透過對光學訊號進行編碼，使其能在無需電訊號再生的情況下進行長距離傳輸；ZR+更可將傳輸距離延伸至約一千公里。

  MX301是一款全新路由器，針對推論邊緣部署而設計——即讓AI推論在靠近終端使用者的地點執行，而非集中於中央設施——可在1U機架單元中提供1.6 Tbps頻寬，具備全雙工400G乙太網路連線能力與AIOps（人工智慧驅動的運維管理）功能，以HPE第六代Trio矽晶片為基礎。

  HPE同時發表SRX4700量子安全防火牆，可在1U中提供1.4 Tbps。所謂量子安全密碼學，是指專為抵禦量子電腦攻擊而設計的加密演算法——量子電腦有能力破解當今許多主流加密標準。

  企業園區與分支網路方面，HPE表示CX交換器將透過HPE Mist進行管理，而其AI網路助理HPE Marvis也將在HPE Aruba Central中獲得對話式AI驅動能力，以自動化處理各項網路管理任務。

  HPE並詳細說明了與輝達（NVIDIA）共同打造的AI Grid，結合輝達Spectrum-X網路、BlueField-3資料處理器（DPU）與ConnectX網路介面卡，並整合HPE Juniper Networking與ProLiant伺服器。

  ## 運算與AI工廠層級

  伺服器方面，HPE重點展示ProLiant Gen12系列，包括搭載輝達晶片的ProLiant Compute DL394 Gen12，以及以Intel Xeon 6為核心的邊緣伺服器ProLiant Compute EL2000。HPE提到Intel Xeon 6支援ProLiant Nonstop Updates，並發表基於輝達Vera的系統，但主題演講中未提供進一步的規格細節。

  HPE表示，機密運算（confidential computing）——一種在資料於記憶體中進行處理時仍予以保護的技術——將成為其AI Factory組合中輝達系統的標準配備。

  HPE將其AI基礎架構產品分為三個層級，對應不同客戶族群：

  - **HPE Private Cloud AI**：適用於企業客戶
  - **HPE AI Factory at Scale**：適用於新興雲端業者（neocloud）與超大規模業者
  - **HPE AI Factory Sovereign**：適用於政府、國防機構及類似組織

  ## 智慧型代理AI與軟體

  HPE推出其所稱的「智慧型代理企業」（Agentic Enterprise）框架，針對大規模部署AI代理程式的組織而設計。HPE表示，客戶將能註冊代理程式，並對其套用治理與資安政策，同時宣布支援輝達OpenShell與NemoClaw——此二者均為輝達的代理程式協作框架。

  全新推論能力包括：多節點推論（multi-node inference，即將單一大型模型分散至多台伺服器執行）、統一推論閘道（unified inference gateway），以及共享的鍵值（KV）快取。KV快取允許多個推論請求重複利用先前計算所得的中間資料，從而降低延遲並減少運算資源消耗。

  HPE GreenLake以「GreenLake Intelligence」為旗號獲得更新，將智慧型代理AI應用於運維任務，包括自動化的基礎架構故障排除。

  企業虛擬化方面，HPE表示其CloudOps平台正在擴大Morpheus環境管理工具的覆蓋範圍，以協助更多因VMware授權費用上漲而進行平台遷移的客戶。

  HPE也宣布推出Unleash AI合作夥伴生態系，旨在協助客戶找到經過驗證的HPE與第三方解決方案組合。

  ## 儲存

  HPE Alletra Storage MP X10000被定位為AI原生檔案與物件儲存的基礎層，具備即時資料豐富化（real-time data enrichment）功能，並原生整合MCP（Model Context Protocol，模型情境協定）。MCP是一項開放標準，允許AI模型直接查詢並與外部資料來源互動。HPE Alletra B10000則針對區塊儲存（block storage）工作負載。

  ## 資安

  HPE的Silicon Root of Trust——一種硬體層級機制，可在伺服器開機前以密碼學方式驗證韌體完整性——被納入公司網路韌性（cyber resilience）訊息的重點；SRX4700量子安全防火牆則負責網路層的資安防護。

  ## 客戶案例

  HPE引用雲端基礎架構供應商Vultr為次世代AI基礎架構建置的合作夥伴，其中包含HPE網路產品的應用。西門子能源（Siemens Energy）則出現在客戶專區，聚焦於運用HPE系統進行預測性硬體故障偵測。

  ## 產業意涵

  HPE主題演講以網路為優先的架構，與戴爾科技（Dell Technologies）在Dell Tech World 2026的做法形成對比——後者以運算能力與AI驅動的企業轉型為主軸。根據現場報導，輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）出席了Dell Tech World的舞台，但並未現身HPE Discover，即便輝達產品已貫穿HPE在運算、網路與軟體各層面的發表內容。

  這一對比反映了兩大伺服器廠商在AI基礎架構市場上截然不同的自我定位：HPE在完成Juniper收購後，正將自身打造為一家兼具AI運算能力的網路公司；而戴爾則將自身定位為一家附帶銷售網路產品的運算公司。

  HPE的AI工廠三層架構——企業版、新興雲端與超大規模版，以及主權版——搭配Unleash AI合作夥伴生態系，是該公司對外宣示的策略藍圖，說明其如何在AI基礎架構市場中，因應不同客戶族群的差異化需求。
