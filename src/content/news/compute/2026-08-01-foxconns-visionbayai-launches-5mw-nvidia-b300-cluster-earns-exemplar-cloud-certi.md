body_en: |
  Foxconn subsidiary Visionbay.ai has brought its first 5-megawatt (MW) AI computing cluster online, built on NVIDIA HGX B300 systems, and has secured NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud certification, the company announced July 31. The certification places Visionbay.ai among the top ten such providers worldwide and makes it the second in Asia to pass NVIDIA's production-grade AI training validation as an NVIDIA Cloud Partner (NCP).

  The cluster is operating commercially at more than 90% utilization and has signed its first round of customers to long-term lease contracts, Visionbay.ai said. The facility uses a water-cooling architecture, a 2N high-availability design, and NVIDIA Quantum-X800 InfiniBand high-speed networking, supporting large language model (LLM) training and inference workloads.

  ## What the NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud certification means

  NVIDIA's Exemplar Cloud program uses standardized performance verification tools to assess a provider's computing efficiency, network performance, cluster stability, and operational consistency over sustained run times, Visionbay.ai said. The company said obtaining the certification indicates its AI infrastructure meets the conditions required for production deployment, reducing technical risk for enterprises adopting large AI models and accelerating the transition from research and development to commercial deployment.

  The certification also validates what Visionbay.ai calls its core capability to build a "Tokenized AI Factory," the company said.

  ## Tokenized AI Factory service model

  Visionbay.ai offers what it describes as a "Tokenized AI Factory" model, integrating AI infrastructure, GPU cloud services, an AI global operations visibility platform, and a Token Factory service. The combined platform is designed to cover model training, fine-tuning, inference, and AI agent deployment from a single provider, the company said.

  The facility operates under a Tier III information security management standard. Alongside computing capacity, the company said it established day-one operational capability, enabling continuous management and delivery of large GPU clusters and allowing enterprises to deploy AI at faster speed with lower operational risk.

  ## Context: enterprise demand shifting toward production stability

  Visionbay.ai framed the cluster's commercial launch against a broader shift in enterprise AI demand: requirements have moved from simply acquiring GPUs to ensuring stable support for production environments, the company said, driven by the rapid development of generative AI, agentic AI, and multimodal models.

  ## Foxconn's role and expansion plans

  Visionbay.ai is Foxconn's (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 2317) supercomputing center and cloud computing operations platform subsidiary. The company said it will continue expanding AI infrastructure across the Asia-Pacific region based on NVIDIA's Blackwell and Vera Rubin GPU architectures, drawing on Foxconn's vertical integration across AI infrastructure, key components, and software platform operations to deliver end-to-end Tokenized AI Factory solutions to enterprises, governments, and industry partners.
body_zh: |
  鴻海子公司Visionbay.ai於7月31日宣布，首座以輝達HGX B300系統建構、容量達500萬瓦（5MW）的AI運算叢集正式投入商業運營，並取得輝達Exemplar Cloud認證。此一認證使Visionbay.ai躋身全球前十大同類提供商之列，也讓其成為亞洲第二家通過輝達生產級AI訓練驗證、取得輝達雲端合作夥伴（NCP）資格的業者。

  Visionbay.ai表示，該叢集目前商業運轉率已超過90%，並已與首批客戶簽署長期租約合約。該設施採用水冷架構、2N高可用性設計，以及輝達Quantum-X800 InfiniBand高速網路，可支援大型語言模型（LLM）的訓練與推論工作負載。

  ## 輝達Exemplar Cloud認證的意義

  Visionbay.ai指出，輝達Exemplar Cloud計畫採用標準化的效能驗證工具，評估提供商的運算效率、網路效能、叢集穩定性，以及在持續運行期間的營運一致性。該公司表示，取得此認證代表其AI基礎架構已符合生產部署所需條件，有助於降低企業導入大型AI模型的技術風險，並加速從研發到商業部署的轉型進程。

  此認證同時驗證了Visionbay.ai所稱其打造「代幣化AI工廠」（Tokenized AI Factory）的核心能力。

  ## Tokenized AI Factory服務模式

  Visionbay.ai提供其所描述的「代幣化AI工廠」模式，整合AI基礎架構、GPU雲端服務、AI全球營運可視化平台，以及Token Factory服務。該公司表示，這套整合平台設計涵蓋模型訓練、微調、推論及AI代理程式部署，由單一供應商一站式提供。

  該設施依循第三層（Tier III）資訊安全管理標準運作。除運算容量外，Visionbay.ai表示，自首日起即建立完整的營運能力，能夠持續管理並交付大規模GPU叢集，使企業得以更快速、更低營運風險地部署AI。

  ## 背景：企業需求轉向生產環境的穩定性

  Visionbay.ai將此次叢集商業上線，置於企業AI需求更廣泛轉變的脈絡中加以詮釋：在生成式AI、代理式AI（Agentic AI）及多模態模型快速發展的驅動下，企業的需求已從單純取得GPU，轉向確保生產環境獲得穩定支援。

  ## 鴻海的定位與擴張計畫

  Visionbay.ai是鴻海（台灣證券交易所股票代號：2317）旗下的超級運算中心暨雲端運算營運平台子公司。該公司表示，未來將持續以輝達Blackwell與Vera Rubin GPU架構為基礎，擴大在亞太地區的AI基礎架構布局，並運用鴻海橫跨AI基礎架構、關鍵元件及軟體平台營運的垂直整合優勢，向企業、政府及產業夥伴提供端對端的代幣化AI工廠解決方案。
