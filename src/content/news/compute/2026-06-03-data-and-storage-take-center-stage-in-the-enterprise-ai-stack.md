body_en: |
  At Dell Technologies World 2026 in Las Vegas — attended by more than 8,000 people — data and storage were not supporting acts: they were the central message. Jeff Clark, vice chairman and chief operating officer at Dell, said enterprise executives still do not fully appreciate the critical role data plays in both generative and agentic AI. The three-day event was structured to change that, with data-management and storage technologies featured prominently across product announcements and keynotes alike.

  ## Keeping GPUs Fed

  Travis Vigil, senior vice president of product management at Dell, framed the stakes plainly: storage exists to keep GPUs fed, and given the scale of investment in GPU infrastructure, that function is "a pretty important thing." He added that with every successive Nvidia architectural generation, storage systems must keep pace.

  For enterprises, the differentiation AI can deliver depends almost entirely on unstructured data held on-premises. "The ability to take really smart models that are trained on the internet and make them knowledgeable about your specific enterprise is a data management, a data curation, a data cleansing, a data pipelining issue," Vigil said. The knowledge and competitive differentiation enterprises can extract from generative or agentic AI resides largely in that on-premises unstructured data.

  ## Training vs. Inference: Different Demands, Same Foundation

  David Noy, vice president of product management at Dell, distinguished between storage requirements for training and inferencing workloads:

  - **Training:** Cleaning and preparing data is critical for model accuracy, and sustained throughput is required to stream data continuously into GPUs.
  - **Inference:** Curating the right dataset is the priority, ensuring the model returns the required answers.

  Despite those differences, Noy's bottom line applied equally to both: "GPUs without data are just hardware burning electricity. There's no value to it." He anchored the entire infrastructure discussion in business outcomes — something must go in, and something useful must come out the other side.

  ## Tiered Storage Architecture

  Because speed requirements vary across the AI workflow, Vigil said organizations will need multiple tiers of storage:

  - **In-cluster:** Storage sitting inside the compute cluster itself
  - **Near-cluster:** Storage positioned close to the cluster
  - **Data lake:** Storage residing outside the cluster, in the broader data lake

  ## Data Gravity and the On-Premises Reality

  Michael Dell and other executives argued throughout the event that the AI era is accelerating a shift toward hybrid environments, with renewed emphasis on on-premises systems — in enterprise datacenters, at the edge, and in co-location facilities. The guiding principle, as Michael Dell put it: "AI is fueling a renaissance in enterprise hardware, a shift from bits back to atoms."

  A Dell survey cited at the event found that 67 percent of AI workloads run outside the cloud. Dell said that distribution is unlikely to change, given ongoing concerns about data security and sovereignty.

  Vigil pointed to a long-standing principle that has taken on new urgency: "There's gravity to data, and the vast majority of data exists on-premises." The change over the past two to three years, he said, is that generative and agentic AI have made it possible to extract intelligence from that on-premises data — a "step function change" that has sharply raised demand for local infrastructure to help enterprises act on what they already hold.
body_zh: |
  在拉斯維加斯舉行的Dell Technologies World 2026，逾8,000名與會者見證了資料與儲存躍居舞台中心。戴爾副董事長兼營運長Jeff Clark表示，企業高層對資料在生成式AI與代理AI中所扮演的關鍵角色，至今仍未有充分認識。這場為期三天的活動以資料管理與儲存技術為主軸，將相關主題貫穿於所有產品公告與主題演講之中。

  ## 持續為GPU供給資料

  戴爾產品管理資深副總裁Travis Vigil直指核心：儲存系統的首要任務是為GPU持續供給資料，鑒於GPU基礎架構的龐大投資規模，這項功能「至關重要」。他並指出，隨著輝達每一代架構演進，儲存系統都必須同步跟上。

  對企業客戶而言，AI所能帶來的差異化優勢，幾乎完全取決於存放在本地端的非結構化資料。Vigil表示：「將在網際網路上訓練的智慧模型，轉化為能夠深入了解特定企業的知識模型，本質上是資料管理、資料整理、資料清洗與資料流水線的問題。」企業從生成式AI或代理AI中所能提取的知識與競爭差異，大部分存在於這些本地端的非結構化資料之中。

  ## 訓練與推論：需求各異，根基相同

  戴爾產品管理副總裁David Noy區分了訓練與推論工作負載對儲存的不同需求：

  - **訓練端：** 清洗與準備資料對確保模型精準度至關重要，同時需要持續的傳輸效能，將資料不間斷地串流至GPU。
  - **推論端：** 整理適當的資料集是首要任務，確保模型能夠回傳所需的答案。

  儘管需求有別，Noy對兩者的結論卻如出一轍：「沒有資料的GPU不過是在燃燒電力的硬體，毫無價值可言。」他將整個基礎架構討論回歸到業務成果——必須有東西輸入，才能從另一端產出有意義的結果。

  ## 分層儲存架構

  由於AI工作流程各階段對速度的要求不盡相同，Vigil表示企業將需要多層次的儲存架構：

  - **叢集內部：** 位於運算叢集內的儲存
  - **叢集近端：** 緊鄰叢集部署的儲存
  - **資料湖：** 位於叢集外部的儲存，存放於更廣泛的資料湖中

  ## 資料重力與本地端現實

  在整場活動中，戴爾創辦人暨執行長Michael Dell與其他高層一再強調，AI時代正在加速推動企業基礎架構轉向混合環境，並重新著重本地端系統——無論是企業資料中心、邊緣端，還是共置設施。Michael Dell將此定義為：「AI正在推動企業硬體的文藝復興，是一場從位元回歸原子的轉變。」

  活動中引用的一項戴爾調查顯示，67%的AI工作負載在雲端之外運行。戴爾表示，由於資料安全與資料主權方面的顧慮持續存在，這一比例不太可能改變。

  Vigil援引一項長期成立、如今更顯緊迫的原則：「資料具有重力，絕大多數資料存在於本地端。」他表示，過去兩三年來的轉變在於，生成式AI與代理AI讓企業得以從本地端資料中提取智慧——這是一次「階躍式的變化」，大幅推升了企業對本地基礎架構的需求，以便充分運用其既有的資料資產。
