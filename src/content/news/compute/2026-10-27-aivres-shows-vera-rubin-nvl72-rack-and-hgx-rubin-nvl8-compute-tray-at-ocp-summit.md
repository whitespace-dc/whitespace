body_en: |
  Aivres displayed a full NVIDIA AI server lineup at OCP Global Summit 2026 in San Jose, with exhibits spanning a Vera Rubin NVL72 liquid-cooled rack, a 2U HGX Rubin NVL8 compute tray, Vera CPU hardware, an HGX B300 server, and a PCIe GPU server.

  ## KRS8000V4 Vera Rubin NVL72 rack

  The largest exhibit was the KRS8000V4, Aivres's Vera Rubin NVL72 liquid-cooled AI rack. The company had previously shown the rack at NVIDIA GTC 2026; the OCP Summit appearance offered a closer look at surrounding hardware.

  Aivres lists the KRS8000V4 with 18 1U compute trays and nine 1U NVLink switch trays, with the switch trays positioned in the middle of the rack and power shelves at the top and bottom. The configuration supports 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs connected over sixth-generation NVLink, totaling more than 20 terabytes of HBM (high-bandwidth memory) across the GPU domain.

  The rack is 100 percent liquid-cooled and rated to operate with coolant at 45 degrees Celsius. Aivres noted that a typical hot tub runs at 38 to 39 degrees Celsius, putting the coolant temperature in context. The company said the elevated fluid temperature helps cooling efficiency. Beyond the compute and switch trays, the enclosure integrates management switches, an NVLink spine, power distribution, and liquid-cooling infrastructure in a single unit.

  The booth unit was a mix of dummy blanks and one live node, which is common for rack-scale systems at trade shows.

  ## KL2288 HGX Rubin NVL8 compute tray

  Aivres also showed the KL2288, a 2U liquid-cooled compute tray for the NVIDIA HGX Rubin NVL8 platform. The tray pairs eight Rubin GPUs with dual AMD EPYC 9006 "Venice" processors in a fanless design. Aivres said the exhibit demonstrates how an eight-GPU HGX platform can be hosted by x86 CPUs without fans.

  Aivres listed peak performance of 400 petaFLOPS for NVFP4 inference and 280 petaFLOPS for NVFP4 training. The tray carries 2.3 terabytes of HBM4 memory with 176 terabytes per second of total HBM bandwidth.

  The front panel includes a UART port, two USB ports, two RJ45 ports, and a row of eight E1.S drive bays, with space for an NVIDIA BlueField DPU. The rear uses OCP rack quick-disconnect fittings and a busbar for power delivery; no fan exhaust area is present at the rear, consistent with the fanless design.

  Internally, the two processors sit under copper cold plates, and the DDR5 memory modules are also routed through the liquid-cooling loop. Color-coded distribution blocks route coolant through the tray, with red fittings for warm return flow and blue for cooler supply.

  ## Additional booth hardware

  The Aivres lineup also included Vera CPU hardware, an HGX B300 server, and a PCIe GPU server. Aivres did not disclose detailed specifications for those systems at the show.

  Aivres described the KL2288 as an upcoming system.

  ## KRS8000V4 Vera Rubin NVL72機架

  此次展出規模最大的展品為KRS8000V4，即Aivres推出的Vera Rubin NVL72液冷AI機架。該公司先前曾於輝達GTC 2026發表這款機架，本次OCP峰會的亮相則讓外界得以近距離觀察周邊硬體配置。

  Aivres公布的KRS8000V4規格顯示，機架內建18個1U運算托盤與9個1U NVLink交換機托盤，交換機托盤置於機架中段，電源架則分別安裝於頂部與底部。整體配置支援72顆Rubin GPU與36顆Vera CPU，透過第六代NVLink互連，GPU域的HBM（高頻寬記憶體）總容量超過20 TB。

  這款機架採用百分之百液冷設計，額定冷卻液工作溫度為攝氏45度。Aivres特別指出，一般熱水浴缸的水溫約在攝氏38至39度之間，藉此為這個冷卻液溫度提供具體的參照基準，並說明較高的液溫有助於提升散熱效率。除運算托盤與交換機托盤之外，這款機架還將管理交換器、NVLink骨幹網路、配電系統及液冷基礎設施整合於單一機櫃之中。

  展場展示的機架由部分填充假面板的空槽與一個實際運作節點混合組成，這在展覽會場的機架級系統中十分常見。

  ## KL2288 HGX Rubin NVL8運算托盤

  Aivres同時展出KL2288，這是一款專為輝達HGX Rubin NVL8平台設計的2U液冷運算托盤。托盤搭載8顆Rubin GPU，並配備雙AMD EPYC 9006「Venice」處理器，採無風扇設計。Aivres表示，此次展出示範了八GPU HGX平台如何在不使用風扇的情況下，由x86 CPU作為主機平台運行。

  Aivres公布的峰值效能數據為：NVFP4推論達400 petaFLOPS，NVFP4訓練達280 petaFLOPS。托盤搭載2.3 TB的HBM4記憶體，HBM總頻寬為每秒176 TB。

  前面板配備一個UART埠、兩個USB埠、兩個RJ45埠，以及一排8個E1.S硬碟槽，並預留空間供輝達BlueField DPU安裝。後面板採用OCP機架快速斷接接頭與匯流排排進行電源傳輸，後方無風扇排氣區域，與無風扇設計的定位相符。

  機箱內部，兩顆處理器均覆蓋銅製冷板，DDR5記憶體模組亦納入液冷迴路之中。托盤內以色碼分流塊引導冷卻液流向，紅色接頭代表熱回水，藍色接頭代表冷供水。

  ## 其他展示硬體

  Aivres的展出陣容還包括Vera CPU硬體、HGX B300伺服器及PCIe GPU伺服器。Aivres未在展會上公開這些系統的詳細規格。

  Aivres表示，KL2288目前為即將推出的系統。
