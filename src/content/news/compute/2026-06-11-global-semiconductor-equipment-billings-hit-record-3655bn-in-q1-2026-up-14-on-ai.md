body_en: |
  Global semiconductor equipment billings reached a record $36.55 billion in the first quarter of 2026, rising 14% year on year and 1% from the previous quarter, according to SEMI's Worldwide Semiconductor Equipment Market Statistics report. SEMI, the industry association that compiles monthly billing data from its members and the Semiconductor Equipment Association of Japan, said the result was the highest on record for the industry.

  The growth was concentrated in three segments directly tied to artificial intelligence computing: leading-edge logic, DRAM, and advanced packaging. Logic devices at the most advanced manufacturing nodes underpin the processors used in AI systems. DRAM — dynamic random-access memory — supplies the high-bandwidth memory that AI workloads depend on. Advanced packaging, which integrates multiple chip components into a single product to improve performance and density, has attracted fresh capital as chip designers look to extract more capability from existing process technology.

  "The strong start to 2026 reflects continued industry investment in the capacity and infrastructure needed to support AI-driven semiconductor growth," SEMI President and Chief Executive Ajit Manocha said in the release. "Record first-quarter billings highlight ongoing momentum in leading-edge manufacturing and advanced packaging," he added.

  ## What equipment billings measure

  Semiconductor equipment billings are distinct from chip sales. They cover the machinery required to manufacture, test, and package chips — including tools used inside wafer fabrication plants and on advanced packaging lines. Because orders for fabrication tools typically precede increases in production capacity by months or years, sustained growth in billings serves as a forward indicator of manufacturers' confidence in medium-term chip demand.

  SEMI attributed the increase to ongoing factory investment and technology migration rather than a one-off spike, and the report included regional breakdowns alongside the quarter-on-quarter and year-on-year comparisons.

  ## AI reshaping investment priorities

  Recent spending patterns reflect a narrowing of investment priorities around high-performance computing rather than broad-based growth across all semiconductor categories. Growth in data centre processors, high-bandwidth memory, and more complex chip integration has required manufacturers to upgrade both front-end fabrication processes and back-end packaging operations, supporting orders for new equipment even as demand in some mature semiconductor segments has been uneven, SEMI said.

  Building and outfitting advanced chip plants requires substantial multi-year outlays, and equipment purchases account for a large share of that total capital expenditure. Strong billings therefore reflect both new fabrication facility construction and the continued outfitting of existing sites with more sophisticated tools.

  ## Significance

  The record quarterly figure sets a high watermark for the rest of 2026 and signals that chipmakers and their suppliers are still committing funds to major capacity expansion projects despite high costs and broader technology-market uncertainty, according to SEMI. For equipment suppliers exposed to advanced process steps and integration methods, the concentration of spending in AI-related segments has been a particular tailwind.

  SEMI's equipment market data is widely used across the sector as a benchmark for demand conditions, and the $36.55 billion first-quarter result extends a run of spending momentum that began in the preceding quarter.
body_zh: |
  根據SEMI發布的《全球半導體設備市場統計報告》，2026年第一季全球半導體設備出貨額達365.5億美元，創下歷史新高，年增14%，較前一季亦成長1%。SEMI是匯整旗下會員企業及日本半導體設備協會月度出貨數據的產業公會，表示此數字為產業有史以來最高紀錄。

  此波成長集中在與人工智慧運算直接相關的三大領域：先進邏輯晶片、DRAM記憶體及先進封裝。採用最先進製程節點的邏輯元件，是AI系統所使用處理器的核心基礎；DRAM（動態隨機存取記憶體）則提供AI工作負載所仰賴的高頻寬記憶體；先進封裝技術能將多個晶片元件整合為單一產品，提升效能與密度，隨著晶片設計廠商積極尋求從現有製程技術中挖掘更大效能潛力，先進封裝也吸引了新一輪資本湧入。

  SEMI總裁兼執行長Ajit Manocha在聲明中表示：「2026年的強勁開局，反映出業界持續投資於支撐AI驅動半導體成長所需的產能與基礎設施。」他進一步補充：「第一季出貨額創下歷史新高，凸顯了先進製程製造與先進封裝持續向前的強勁動能。」

  ## 設備出貨額的意義

  半導體設備出貨額有別於晶片銷售額，前者涵蓋製造、測試及封裝晶片所需的機具設備，包括晶圓製造廠內部使用的工具，以及先進封裝產線上的各式設備。由於晶圓製造設備的訂單通常早於實際產能提升數個月乃至數年，因此出貨額的持續成長，可視為製造商對中期晶片需求信心的前瞻性指標。

  SEMI將此次成長歸因於持續推進的廠房投資與技術世代遷移，而非單一事件所帶動的短期激增；報告中亦附有各地區的分項數據，並提供季增與年增的比較分析。

  ## AI重塑投資優先順序

  近期的支出模式顯示，投資重心正向高效能運算聚攏，而非橫跨所有半導體類別的全面性成長。SEMI指出，資料中心處理器、高頻寬記憶體，以及愈趨複雜的晶片整合需求持續擴大，促使製造商同步升級前段晶圓製程與後段封裝作業，進而帶動新設備的訂單需求，即便部分成熟半導體領域的需求仍呈現不均衡的狀態。

  興建與裝備先進晶片製造廠需要龐大的多年期資本支出，而設備採購在總資本支出中佔有相當大的比重。因此，強勁的出貨額不僅反映新晶圓廠的建設動能，也代表現有廠房持續導入更精密工具的升級需求。

  ## 指標意義

  SEMI表示，這一創紀錄的單季數字為2026年後續各季立下了高水位標竿，也顯示出晶片製造商及其供應商在成本高企、科技市場整體不確定性猶存的背景下，仍持續對大型擴產計畫投入資金。對於在先進製程步驟與整合方法上具有高度曝險的設備供應商而言，支出集中於AI相關領域，尤其形成一股強勁的順風優勢。

  SEMI的設備市場數據在業界被廣泛作為需求狀況的基準指標，365.5億美元的第一季成績，延續了前一季已然展開的支出動能，讓這波景氣升溫的態勢得以延伸。
