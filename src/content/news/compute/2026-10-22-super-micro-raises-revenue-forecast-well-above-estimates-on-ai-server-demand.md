body_en: |
  Super Micro Computer on Tuesday raised its first-quarter revenue forecast well above analyst expectations, citing continued strong demand for AI server infrastructure, and said shares jumped 11.3% in after-hours trading on the news.

  The company said it now expects first-quarter revenue between $18.5 billion and $19.5 billion. That range is above both its prior guidance of $14.5 billion to $15.5 billion and the average analyst estimate of $15.23 billion compiled by LSEG, a financial data provider.

  Super Micro also lifted its gross margin — the share of revenue left after direct production costs — outlook to between 12% and 12.5%, up from a prior range of 10.4% to 10.8% and above the analyst consensus of 10.15%. The company attributed the improvement primarily to customer and product mix.

  ## Spillover to rivals

  The guidance lifted shares across companies exposed to the AI infrastructure buildout. Rivals Dell and Hewlett Packard Enterprise gained between 3.5% and 4.9% in after-hours trading, according to the report. Nvidia, the dominant supplier of AI accelerator chips, rose 1.5%.

  ## Backdrop: Big Tech spending and a recent selloff

  Super Micro's upward revision comes roughly a week after shares of AI data center-exposed companies fell. That selloff was triggered by a decline in the stock and bonds of CoreWeave, a cloud computing operator, combined with concerns that higher interest rates could delay financial returns for heavily indebted infrastructure operators. Credit ratings firm S&P Global said on Monday that it estimates the world's largest technology companies are likely to carry more AI-linked liabilities than is publicly known.

  Despite those concerns, Super Micro's guidance points to sustained hardware demand from the largest cloud and technology buyers. Microsoft, Google, Amazon, and Meta are expected collectively to spend more than $700 billion on AI infrastructure this year, according to the report.

  ## CFO appointment closes a two-year vacancy

  Separate from the financial guidance, Super Micro last week named Mark Long, a former executive at Western Digital, a data storage company, as its chief financial officer. The company had been searching for a permanent CFO for nearly two years following scrutiny of its financial and accounting practices.

  ## What it means for the sector

  Super Micro's results are a direct measure of how quickly large technology companies are converting AI spending commitments into physical hardware orders. A revenue forecast nearly $4 billion above the prior guidance midpoint signals that order volumes are running ahead of earlier expectations. The margin improvement, driven by product and customer mix rather than cost cuts alone, suggests the company is shifting toward higher-value configurations within its server lineup.

  Super Micro is scheduled to report its full first-quarter results at a later date.

  ## 超微電腦大幅上調營收預測，遠超市場估計，AI伺服器需求強勁

  超微電腦（Super Micro Computer）週二大幅上調第一季營收預測，遠高於分析師預期，公司表示AI伺服器基礎建設的強勁需求持續不退，消息傳出後，股價於盤後交易跳漲11.3%。

  公司最新預測第一季營收將介於185億至195億美元之間，高於此前指引的145億至155億美元，亦遠超金融資料供應商LSEG彙整的分析師平均預估152.3億美元。

  超微電腦同步上調毛利率展望——亦即扣除直接生產成本後的營收佔比——至12%至12.5%，高於先前預估的10.4%至10.8%，也超過市場共識的10.15%。公司將此一改善主要歸因於客戶結構與產品組合的優化。

  ## 帶動同業股價走揚

  此次指引上調連帶提振了一批受惠於AI基礎建設擴張的企業股價。根據報導，競爭對手戴爾（Dell）與慧與科技（Hewlett Packard Enterprise）在盤後交易中分別上漲3.5%至4.9%。AI加速晶片的主要供應商輝達（NVIDIA）則小漲1.5%。

  ## 背景：大型科技公司持續投資，但近期曾遭拋售

  超微電腦此次上調預測，距AI資料中心概念股的一波跌勢約莫一週。當時的拋售，導火線是雲端運算業者CoreWeave股票與債券下跌，加上市場擔憂升息環境可能延後負債累累的基礎建設業者的財務回報時程。信用評等機構標普全球（S&P Global）週一表示，估計全球最大幾家科技公司所承擔的AI相關負債，可能高於外界公開得知的數字。

  儘管外界存有上述疑慮，超微電腦的指引仍顯示大型雲端及科技買家對硬體的需求依然穩健。根據報導，微軟（Microsoft）、谷歌（Google）、亞馬遜（Amazon）與Meta預計今年合計在AI基礎建設上的支出將超過7,000億美元。

  ## 財務長職缺長達兩年，終獲補實

  除財務指引外，超微電腦上週宣布任命馬克．朗（Mark Long）擔任財務長，他曾任職於資料儲存公司威騰電子（Western Digital）。該公司因財務與會計作業遭受外界審視，財務長一職懸缺已近兩年，此次任命方告補實。

  ## 產業意涵

  超微電腦的業績表現，是觀察大型科技公司將AI支出承諾轉化為實際硬體訂單速度的直接指標。此次營收預測較先前指引中間值高出近40億美元，顯示訂單量已超越先前預期。毛利率的改善主要來自產品與客戶結構的升級，而非單純仰賴削減成本，這意味著公司正朝伺服器產品線中更高價值的配置移動。

  超微電腦預計將於稍後公佈完整的第一季業績。
