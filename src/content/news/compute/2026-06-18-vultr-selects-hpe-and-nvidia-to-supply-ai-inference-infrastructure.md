body_en: |
  Cloud infrastructure provider Vultr has selected Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE) and Nvidia to supply its next generation of AI infrastructure, with deployments centered on Nvidia GB300 NVL72 systems as enterprise customers move from AI experimentation into production operations. The companies announced the partnership at HPE Discover 2026 on June 17.

  Under the agreement, Vultr will deploy GB300 NVL72 systems through the Nvidia AI Computing by HPE portfolio. The buildouts will incorporate Nvidia Spectrum-X Ethernet networking and HPE liquid cooling technologies. The companies did not disclose financial terms, deployment timelines, or the locations of planned facilities.

  ## Inference replaces training as primary spending driver

  Vultr CEO J.J. Kardwell said customer demand has shifted sharply over the past year from AI experimentation toward inference workloads — the phase where a trained AI model is used continuously to serve real applications, as opposed to the one-time compute-intensive task of training the model in the first place. Three years ago, most GPU demand at Vultr came from startups and AI developers building and training new models, Kardwell said at a media briefing at HPE Discover. Today, a growing share comes from enterprises running production AI services tied to customer-facing applications and business operations.

  "When you reach a point in the market where you're seeing operating margins for public companies be favorably impacted, we're now seeing changes to the way large public firms think about staffing levels because of the economic impact of these AI capabilities," Kardwell said.

  Ron Westfall, vice president and practice lead for networking and infrastructure at HyperFrame Research, told Data Center Knowledge the shift is reordering infrastructure investment priorities. "The industry is shifting its spending focus toward infrastructure optimized for production throughput, cost efficiency and rack-to-rack networking rather than just raw training power," Westfall said.

  ## Networking emerges as bottleneck at scale

  Future Vultr deployments will use Nvidia Spectrum-X networking with 400 GbE (gigabit Ethernet) and 800 GbE connectivity, designed to support large AI clusters and rack-scale GPU architectures. The emphasis on networking reflects a constraint Kardwell described as fundamental to large cluster design.

  "Very quickly, the bottleneck is the second you leave that rack," Kardwell said, pointing to east-west traffic — data moving between servers within a data center cluster, rather than traffic flowing out to the internet — as the critical performance variable in large AI deployments.

  Westfall said high-bandwidth networking is becoming a competitive differentiator for cloud providers. "When AI clusters expand beyond individual racks, networking evolves from a standard infrastructure component into a performance bottleneck and a key competitive advantage," he said. "Cloud providers deploying optimized network architectures can offer the stable throughput and accelerated job execution required to scale enterprise inference workloads."

  ## Hybrid strategies gain ground

  Kardwell said enterprises are increasingly combining cloud and on-premises AI infrastructure rather than committing to one approach. "The days of companies having a strategy that's just all on-prem or all cloud are over," he said. "It really ends up being an 'and.'"

  He attributed part of the demand for cloud-based AI capacity to the pace of GPU platform evolution outrunning traditional enterprise procurement cycles, which he said typically run six to eighteen months. By the time purchasing decisions are finalized, the capacity organizations planned to acquire is often unavailable, Kardwell said.

  "As a powerful extension of our global cloud infrastructure platform, Vultr is deploying dedicated AI infrastructure focused on GPU architecture and AI inference to accelerate customer innovation while maintaining cost efficiency," he said in a statement.

  ## Global footprint and data sovereignty

  Vultr, which describes itself as the world's largest privately held cloud infrastructure company, currently operates across 33 data center locations in 17 countries. Kardwell said data sovereignty requirements — regulations governing where data must be stored and processed — are becoming more consequential as enterprises integrate AI into core business processes, and the company's geographic spread is designed to help customers meet those residency obligations.

  HPE president and CEO Antonio Neri framed the deal as validation of HPE's AI data center architecture strategy. "Vultr represents a new generation of AI cloud providers, and the company's selection of HPE validates the importance of AI data center architectures designed to support the next wave of global AI growth," Neri said.

  The partnership positions Vultr among a growing set of cloud providers expanding dedicated AI infrastructure as enterprise inference demand grows. Future deployments will feature Nvidia Spectrum-X networking at 400 GbE and 800 GbE speeds alongside GB300 NVL72 systems and HPE liquid cooling.

  ## Vultr選定HPE與輝達供應AI推論基礎設施

  雲端基礎設施供應商Vultr宣布選定惠普企業（HPE）與輝達，共同打造下一世代AI基礎設施，部署核心為輝達GB300 NVL72系統，以服務正從AI實驗階段邁向正式生產環境的企業客戶。三家公司於6月17日在HPE Discover 2026大會上正式對外宣布這項合作。

  根據協議內容，Vultr將透過「Nvidia AI Computing by HPE」產品組合部署GB300 NVL72系統，建置工程將整合輝達Spectrum-X乙太網路以及HPE液冷散熱技術。各方均未揭露財務條款、部署時程或預定設施地點。

  ## 推論取代訓練，成為資本支出主要驅動力

  Vultr執行長J.J. Kardwell表示，過去一年間客戶需求已明顯從AI實驗轉向推論工作負載——即已訓練完成的AI模型被持續用於支撐實際應用的階段，有別於一次性、高運算密度的模型訓練任務。Kardwell在HPE Discover媒體簡報會上指出，三年前Vultr的GPU需求大多來自新創企業與AI開發者，用途以建構及訓練新模型為主；如今，越來越多的需求來自企業客戶，用於運行與顧客端應用及業務營運緊密相連的生產AI服務。

  「當市場發展到這樣一個節點——我們看到上市公司的營業利益率因此獲得正面提升，我們也開始看到大型上市企業因為這些AI能力的經濟效益，開始重新思考人員配置的方式，」Kardwell說道。

  HyperFrame Research網路與基礎設施研究副總裁暨業務主管Ron Westfall向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，這股轉變正在重新排列基礎設施投資的優先順序。「整個產業正將資本支出的重心，從單純的原始訓練算力，轉移到針對生產吞吐量、成本效益以及機架間網路連接所優化的基礎設施，」Westfall說。

  ## 大規模部署下，網路成為瓶頸

  Vultr未來的部署將採用支援400 GbE（Gigabit乙太網路）及800 GbE連接的輝達Spectrum-X網路架構，專為大型AI叢集與機架規模GPU架構設計。這份對網路的高度重視，呼應了Kardwell所描述的大型叢集設計中一項根本性的限制。

  「問題幾乎是在你一離開那個機架的瞬間就出現了，」Kardwell說，並指出東西向流量——即資料中心叢集內伺服器之間的資料傳輸，而非流往外部網際網路的流量——才是大型AI部署中最關鍵的效能變數。

  Westfall表示，高頻寬網路正逐漸成為雲端供應商的競爭差異化要素。「當AI叢集的規模擴展到單一機架之外，網路就不再只是標準的基礎設施元件，而是演變為效能瓶頸，也是重要的競爭優勢，」他說。「部署了優化網路架構的雲端供應商，才能提供企業推論工作負載在擴展規模時所需的穩定吞吐量與加速任務執行能力。」

  ## 混合策略漸成主流

  Kardwell表示，企業越來越傾向將雲端與地端AI基礎設施混合運用，而非只押注單一路線。「企業策略只走全地端或全雲端的時代已經結束了，」他說，「最終會是『兩者兼具』的模式。」

  他將部分雲端AI容量需求，歸因於GPU平台的演進速度已超過傳統企業採購週期——他指出企業採購流程通常需要六個月至十八個月。等到採購決策拍板定案，當初規劃要取得的容量往往早已無法取得，Kardwell說道。

  「作為我們全球雲端基礎設施平台的有力延伸，Vultr正在部署專注於GPU架構與AI推論的專用AI基礎設施，以加速客戶創新，同時維持成本效益，」他在聲明中表示。

  ## 全球佈局與資料主權

  Vultr自詡為全球規模最大的私人持股雲端基礎設施業者，目前在17個國家共設有33處資料中心。Kardwell表示，隨著企業將AI整合至核心業務流程，資料主權要求——即規範資料必須在何處儲存與處理的相關法規——正變得愈發重要，而公司廣泛的地理佈局正是為了協助客戶履行資料在地化義務。

  HPE總裁暨執行長Antonio Neri將這筆交易定位為HPE AI資料中心架構策略獲得市場認可的明證。「Vultr代表了新世代AI雲端供應商，其選擇HPE，正是驗證了專為支援下一波全球AI成長而設計的AI資料中心架構的重要性，」Neri說道。

  這項合作關係，使Vultr躋身一批正積極擴張專用AI基礎設施的雲端供應商行列，以因應日益成長的企業推論需求。未來的部署將以GB300 NVL72系統與HPE液冷散熱為核心，並搭載支援400 GbE及800 GbE傳輸速率的輝達Spectrum-X網路。
