body_en: |
  Qisda Corporation, the Taiwanese conglomerate that operates under the BenQ-Qisda group, unveiled a rack-scale AI infrastructure platform at Computex 2026, bringing together server, liquid-cooling, and high-speed networking products from three subsidiaries under a single integrated offering.

  The exhibit centers on three capability pillars the company labels cooling, compute, and energy efficiency, each supplied by a different unit within the group: Qiyo Technology handles thermal management, Qiyang Technology (其陽科技) supplies servers, and Amtran Technology (明泰科技) contributes switching hardware.

  ## Cooling for racks above 100 kW

  Qisda's cooling approach relies on two-phase direct liquid cooling from subsidiary Qiyo Technology (其曜科技). In two-phase cooling, a low-boiling-point coolant is circulated directly to heat-generating components; as it absorbs heat it vaporizes, carrying thermal energy away far more efficiently than air or single-phase liquid alone. The company said the technology is designed to handle rack power densities that exceed 100 kilowatts — roughly 10 to 20 times the load a conventional air-cooled rack can manage — by improving heat transfer efficiency at high-power chips and reducing strain on broader data-center cooling infrastructure.

  ## Sixth-generation AMD EPYC for AI workloads

  On the compute side, Qisda said Qiyang Technology is deploying the 6th Generation AMD EPYC processor platform. EPYC is AMD's line of x86 server central processing units. The company positioned the platform as suited to generative AI inference, large-model training, and other high-load applications, describing the configuration as high-density and highly scalable.

  ## High-speed switching up to 1.6 terabits per second

  Amtran Technology supplies switching hardware supporting port speeds of 400 gigabits, 800 gigabits, and 1.6 terabits per second. The switches are intended to strengthen data movement both within a rack and between racks — a requirement that grows as AI models distribute computation across many processors simultaneously. Qisda said the networking layer is integrated with Qiyang servers and Qiyo liquid cooling to manage compute, network, and thermal resources as a unified system.

  ## Shifting competition from servers to racks

  Tseng Wen-hsing, general manager of Qisda's Smart Solutions Business Group, said in a statement that AI workload growth is pushing data-center competition away from single-server performance and toward rack-level system efficiency and power management. "Qisda combines the group's capabilities in servers, liquid cooling, networking, and systems integration, focusing on the three keys of thermal management, compute, and energy efficiency, to help customers build more flexible and efficient AI infrastructure," Tseng said.

  ## Industry context

  The Computex showing reflects a broader shift Qisda described in its materials: as AI model scale grows, data-center buyers are increasingly evaluating full-stack architecture — from chip and server through network, cooling, and deployment — rather than individual component performance. The company said the market's requirements now span the entire stack, and that it is positioning itself as a provider of integrated AI infrastructure solutions rather than discrete hardware.

  Qisda said its Computex exhibit uses the rack-scale format as the entry point for demonstrating how its group companies' AI server, liquid-cooling, and high-speed networking work can be coordinated across a single deployment.
body_zh: |
  佳世達股份有限公司（BenQ-Qisda集團旗下事業體）在Computex 2026展出機架級AI基礎架構平台，將旗下三家子公司的伺服器、液冷及高速網路產品整合為單一完整方案，聯合亮相。

  此次展示聚焦於公司所稱的三大能力支柱——散熱、運算與能源效率，分別由集團內不同事業單位負責：其曜科技負責熱管理，其陽科技提供伺服器，明泰科技則貢獻交換器硬體。

  ## 因應超過100千瓦的機架散熱需求

  佳世達的散熱方案採用子公司其曜科技的兩相直接液冷技術。兩相液冷的原理，是將低沸點冷卻液直接循環至發熱元件；冷卻液吸熱後氣化，將熱能帶走，效率遠優於空冷或單相液冷。佳世達表示，這項技術的設計目標是應對每機架功耗超過100千瓦的高密度場景——約為傳統氣冷機架負載的10至20倍——可提升高功耗晶片的熱傳效率，同時減輕整體資料中心冷卻基礎設施的壓力。

  ## 第六代AMD EPYC處理器應對AI工作負載

  在運算方面，佳世達表示其陽科技將部署第六代AMD EPYC處理器平台。EPYC是AMD旗下的x86伺服器中央處理器產品線。佳世達將此平台定位為適合生成式AI推論、大模型訓練及其他高負載應用，並形容其配置具備高密度與高擴充彈性的特點。

  ## 高速交換器支援最高每秒1.6兆位元傳輸

  明泰科技提供的交換器硬體支援每秒400吉位元、800吉位元及1.6兆位元的連接埠速率。這些交換器旨在強化機架內部及機架間的資料傳輸效能——隨著AI模型同時跨多個處理器分散運算，此需求日益迫切。佳世達表示，網路層已與其陽科技的伺服器及其曜科技的液冷系統整合，以統一管理運算、網路與熱管理資源，形成一套協調運作的整體系統。

  ## 競爭重心從單一伺服器轉向機架整體

  佳世達智慧解決方案事業群總經理曾文興在一份聲明中指出，AI工作負載的成長正推動資料中心競爭的焦點，從單一伺服器效能轉向機架級系統效率與電力管理。「佳世達整合集團在伺服器、液冷、網路及系統整合的實力，聚焦散熱管理、運算效能與能源效率三大關鍵，協助客戶建構更靈活、更高效的AI基礎架構，」曾文興說。

  ## 產業背景

  佳世達此次Computex展示，反映出該公司在展覽資料中所描述的更廣泛趨勢：隨著AI模型規模持續擴大，資料中心採購方越來越傾向評估從晶片、伺服器到網路、散熱與部署的全堆疊架構，而非個別元件的效能表現。佳世達表示，市場需求如今已橫跨整個技術堆疊，公司正將自身定位為整合式AI基礎架構解決方案供應商，而非單純的零組件供應商。

  佳世達表示，本次Computex展示以機架級格式為切入點，展現集團旗下各公司的AI伺服器、液冷及高速網路能力，如何在單一部署中協同運作。
