  ## Minisforum N5 Max Combines AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 with Five-Bay NAS Enclosure

  Minisforum has released the N5 Max, a five-bay NAS unit built around the AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395, a 16-core, 32-thread processor based on AMD's Strix Halo architecture, paired with 64GB of LPDDR5X memory and dual 10-gigabit Ethernet ports. The combination merges what ServeTheHome, which reviewed the unit, described as two separate Minisforum product lineages: the high-compute MS-S1 Max platform and the NAS-oriented N5 Pro chassis family.

  The N5 Max measures 199 x 202.4 x 252.3mm and weighs 5.8kg, according to Minisforum's published specifications. A magnetically attached front panel covers five 3.5-inch SATA drive bays, each mounted on a tool-less tray with its own locking latch and accessible without opening the main chassis.

  ## Storage and Networking

  The five SATA bays are driven by a JMicron JMB585 storage controller, which ServeTheHome identified after disassembly. The front panel carries per-bay activity LEDs for each of the five drives alongside separate indicators for both 10GbE ports, LAN1 and LAN2, and a general system-status light. Both 10-gigabit Ethernet ports are located on the rear panel.

  ## Connectivity Layout

  Minisforum tiered the USB connectivity across the front and rear panels. The front panel carries one USB4 port running at 40 gigabits per second; that port also supports display output. Two USB4 v2 Type-C ports on the rear panel are rated by Minisforum at up to 80 gigabits per second each. Additional rear connections include two USB Type-A ports, an HDMI output, and a USB Type-C port. Two large axial fans mount directly above the rear I/O section and route airflow through the drive area of the chassis.

  Rather than the DC barrel jack common on compact systems, the N5 Max uses a three-pin AC mains inlet with a locking connector feeding an internal power supply.

  ## Context

  ServeTheHome noted that the N5 Max is not Minisforum's first use of the Ryzen AI Max+ 395. The company previously shipped the MS-S1 Max with the same processor but equipped it with 128GB of memory and a different port layout. The N5 Max cuts the memory to 64GB while adding the five-bay storage enclosure and dual 10GbE as distinguishing features.

  The review published by ServeTheHome covers the external hardware in detail; the internal motherboard section and performance results are addressed separately in the full review. The publication framed the central question for the platform as whether combining flagship desktop compute with NAS-style storage in one enclosure represents a meaningful product or an exercise in capability for its own sake.

  The N5 Max represents Minisforum's most direct attempt to position the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 as a storage and networking appliance rather than a pure desktop or workstation, a configuration that has no direct precedent in the company's prior lineup.

  ## Minisforum N5 Max：AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 與五槽 NAS 機箱的結合

  Minisforum 正式推出 N5 Max，這是一款以 AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395 為核心的五槽 NAS 裝置。該處理器採用 AMD Strix Halo 架構，擁有 16 核心、32 執行緒，搭配 64GB LPDDR5X 記憶體以及雙 10Gbps 乙太網路埠。科技媒體 ServeTheHome 對此機進行了評測，並指出這款產品融合了 Minisforum 旗下兩條不同的產品線：主打高效能運算的 MS-S1 Max 平台，以及以 NAS 為定位的 N5 Pro 機箱系列。

  根據 Minisforum 公布的規格，N5 Max 機身尺寸為 199 × 202.4 × 252.3 公釐，重量達 5.8 公斤。機身前方附有磁吸式面板，遮蓋五個 3.5 吋 SATA 硬碟槽，每個槽位均配備免工具托架，並附獨立鎖扣，無需開啟主機箱即可存取硬碟。

  ## 儲存與網路

  五個 SATA 槽位由 JMicron JMB585 儲存控制器驅動，此型號係 ServeTheHome 拆機後所確認。前面板上，每個槽位各有一顆硬碟存取指示燈，另設有雙 10GbE 埠（LAN1 與 LAN2）的獨立狀態燈，以及一顆整機系統狀態指示燈。兩個 10Gbps 乙太網路埠均位於機身後方。

  ## 連接埠配置

  Minisforum 在前後面板上採用分層設計配置 USB 連接埠。前面板配備一個 USB4 埠，傳輸速率達每秒 40Gbps，該埠同時支援影像輸出。後方面板則設有兩個 USB4 v2 Type-C 埠，Minisforum 標示其速率最高可達每秒 80Gbps。後面板其他連接埠包括兩個 USB Type-A 埠、一個 HDMI 輸出以及一個 USB Type-C 埠。機身後方 I/O 區域正上方裝有兩個大型軸流風扇，負責將氣流導引穿過機箱內的硬碟區域。

  N5 Max 捨棄了小型機器常見的 DC 圓形電源接頭，改採三針腳 AC 市電入口，搭配鎖扣連接器接入內建電源供應器。

  ## 背景脈絡

  ServeTheHome 指出，N5 Max 並非 Minisforum 首次採用 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 的產品。該公司先前推出的 MS-S1 Max 同樣搭載此處理器，但配備了 128GB 記憶體，且連接埠配置有所不同。N5 Max 將記憶體削減至 64GB，轉而以五槽儲存擴充能力與雙 10GbE 網路作為主要賣點。

  ServeTheHome 的評測目前聚焦於外觀硬體的詳細介紹，內部主機板解析與效能測試結果將另文刊載。該媒體提出的核心問題是：將旗艦桌面處理器與 NAS 式儲存整合於單一機箱，究竟是具有實質意義的產品定位，還是純粹展示技術能耐的行銷操作。

  N5 Max 是 Minisforum 迄今最直接將 Ryzen AI Max+ 395 定位為儲存與網路應用裝置的嘗試，而非單純作為桌機或工作站使用，這樣的產品形式在該公司過去的產品線中並無先例。
