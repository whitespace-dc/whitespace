body_en: |
  Peng Ding Holdings, the mainland China-listed printed circuit board subsidiary of Taiwan's Zhen Ding Technology, disclosed a private share placement plan on July 3 to raise up to RMB 9.6 billion for a new AI-server and optical-module circuit board facility in Jiangsu province, according to the company's filing as reported by the China Securities Journal.

  The placement will fund the Huai'an Qing Ding AI server and high-speed optical module HDI laminate board project. HDI, or high-density interconnect, refers to a category of printed circuit board (often abbreviated PCB) with finer circuit traces and denser connection points than conventional boards. A private placement raises capital by selling newly issued shares to a defined group of qualified investors rather than through a public offering. The project's total budgeted investment is RMB 12.73 billion, of which the RMB 9.6 billion in placement proceeds will cover the largest share.

  ## Planned capacity

  Once complete, the facility will add 655,600 square meters of high-end HDI board annual production capacity, the filing stated. It will be developed partly within Peng Ding's existing industrial park and partly through new factory construction along with supporting utilities infrastructure.

  Planned production equipment includes forming machines, copper-plating lines, drilling systems, lamination presses, automation systems, solder mask machines, and quality-assurance apparatus. The stated aim is to establish an intelligent manufacturing base for high-end PCBs targeting AI servers and high-speed optical modules.

  ## Regulatory status

  The project had received its Jiangsu Province investment project filing certificate as of the announcement date. Environmental impact assessment and energy consumption evaluation procedures remained under way, the company said.

  ## 鵬鼎控股擬定增募資96億人民幣　於江蘇建AI伺服器PCB廠

  台灣臻鼎科技旗下、在中國A股掛牌的印刷電路板子公司鵬鼎控股，於7月3日披露一項私募定增方案，計畫募資最高96億元人民幣，在江蘇省新建一座專門服務AI伺服器與光模組市場的電路板生產基地。此消息根據該公司公告，由中國證券報報導。

  本次定增募集資金將用於「淮安慶鼎AI伺服器及高速光模組HDI積層板專案」。所謂HDI（高密度互連），係指一類電路線路更精細、連接點密度更高的印刷電路板（PCB），其規格遠超傳統電路板。私募定增是指向特定合格投資人發行新股以籌措資金，有別於公開發行。本專案總預算投資額為127.3億元人民幣，其中96億元定增募資款項將承擔最大部分的資金缺口。

  ## 規劃產能

  根據公告，該廠房建成後，將新增年產能65.56萬平方公尺的高端HDI板。專案分兩部分推進：一部分在鵬鼎現有工業園區範圍內開發，另一部分則透過新建廠房，並同步建置配套公用設施基礎建設。

  規劃導入的生產設備涵蓋成型機、鍍銅線、鑽孔系統、壓合機、自動化設備、阻焊機及品質保證儀器等。公司表示，目標是打造一座面向AI伺服器與高速光模組市場的高端PCB智慧製造基地。

  ## 法規審批進度

  截至公告發布日，本專案已取得江蘇省投資項目備案證。環境影響評估及能耗評估相關程序則仍在辦理中，公司對此亦在公告中說明。
