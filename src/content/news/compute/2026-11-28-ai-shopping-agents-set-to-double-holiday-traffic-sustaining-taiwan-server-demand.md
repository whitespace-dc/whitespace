body_en: |
  AI agents deployed by OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Amazon for the US Thanksgiving shopping season are projected to more than double the volume of AI-referred traffic to American retail websites compared with last year, according to Adobe; analysts say the expansion of AI from infrastructure into consumer-facing agent applications will sustain compute demand and preserve order flow to Taiwan's AI server manufacturers.

  ## AI traffic projections and market stakes

  Adobe's forecast of more than doubled AI-driven retail traffic this holiday season marks a measurable shift in how consumers discover and purchase products. McKinsey estimates that AI shopping assistants capable of searching for goods and placing orders on a user's behalf will drive $1 trillion in US economic activity by 2030.

  The size of the existing digital advertising market defines what is at stake for incumbents. eMarketer projects US digital advertising spending will reach $418 billion this year, with Google, Meta, and Amazon collectively capturing close to two-thirds of that total. AI shopping agents that redirect product discovery away from traditional search results and marketplace listings threaten the advertising revenue tied to those flows.

  ## OpenAI as a new advertising rival

  OpenAI has emerged as a direct challenger to the established platforms. Its advertising business, launched roughly six months ago, has already reached $1 billion in annualized revenue. The company disclosed in September that it has 1.2 billion weekly users. If a material share of those users shift to ChatGPT for product research and comparison, Google and Amazon stand to lose both search traffic and the advertising revenue that accompanies it.

  A University of Maryland marketing professor, Daniel McCarthy, is cited in the source as an illustrative case: after seeing an advertisement for an ankle-strengthening product, he screenshotted it and asked ChatGPT whether it was worth buying. ChatGPT, drawing on his history of using a standing desk, recommended a balance board at roughly half the price. McCarthy described the experience as evidence that ChatGPT could serve as a practical shopping tool.

  ## Compute demand and Taiwan's supply chain

  The deployment of AI agents across consumer commerce represents a new and ongoing source of compute demand, extending a cycle that began with large language model training and infrastructure buildout. Because cloud service providers (CSPs) — the hyperscale operators that run AI workloads for the major platforms — have not reduced capital expenditure, the article argues that AI server manufacturers in Taiwan remain positioned to benefit.

  Taiwan's major AI server ODMs (original design manufacturers, which build servers to customer specifications) have secured positions in the supply chains of OpenAI, Google, Meta, and Amazon. Foxconn, listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange under the ticker 2317 and also known as Hon Hai, is described as the leading AI server ODM, having won large orders through Nvidia and AMD GPU platforms. Quanta Computer, Wistron, and Wiwynn are also cited as having secured orders from multiple major customers across those same platforms.

  ## Significance

  The 2026 Thanksgiving shopping season is being treated as an early real-world test of whether AI shopping agents can function as everyday consumer tools rather than limited demonstrations. The results will inform whether agentic AI becomes a durable driver of CSP capital spending or a cyclical peak.

  McKinsey's projection of $1 trillion in AI-assisted US commerce by 2030 represents the upper bound of analyst estimates for how large the agentic commerce market could become if adoption rates continue to rise.

  ## AI流量預測與市場賭注

  Adobe預測今年假日購物季的AI導引零售流量將較去年倍增以上，標誌著消費者探索與購買商品方式的顯著轉變。麥肯錫估計，能夠代替用戶搜尋商品並下單的AI購物助理，到2030年將帶動美國1兆美元的經濟活動。

  現有數位廣告市場的規模，說明了既有業者所面臨的潛在威脅。eMarketer預測，今年美國數位廣告支出將達4,180億美元，其中Google、Meta與亞馬遜合計掌握近三分之二的市占。若AI購物代理人將商品探索行為從傳統搜尋結果與電商平台陳列頁面導離，與這些流量緊密連結的廣告收入將面臨嚴峻衝擊。

  ## OpenAI成為廣告市場新勁敵

  OpenAI已成為挑戰既有平台的直接競爭者。其廣告業務約在六個月前正式上線，年化營收已達10億美元。該公司在9月間揭露，每週活躍用戶人數達12億。若其中有一定比例的用戶轉向ChatGPT進行商品研究與比價，Google與亞馬遜將同時流失搜尋流量以及伴隨而來的廣告收益。

  原文援引馬里蘭大學行銷學教授丹尼爾·麥卡錫的親身案例作為佐證：他在看到一則腳踝強化產品的廣告後，對廣告截圖並詢問ChatGPT是否值得購買。ChatGPT根據他過去使用立式辦公桌的記錄，推薦了一款售價約只有一半的平衡板。麥卡錫表示，這段親身經歷讓他確信，ChatGPT完全可以作為實用的購物工具。

  ## 運算需求與台灣供應鏈

  AI代理人在消費商務領域的部署，形成一股全新且持續的運算需求，進一步延伸了從大型語言模型訓練與基礎建設擴充所啟動的成長週期。由於雲端服務供應商（CSP）——亦即為各大平台承接AI工作負載的超大規模雲端業者——並未削減資本支出，分析師認為台灣AI伺服器廠商仍處於持續受惠的位置。

  台灣幾家主要AI伺服器ODM（原始設計製造商，依客戶規格設計並生產伺服器）已在OpenAI、Google、Meta及亞馬遜的供應鏈中取得席位。鴻海精密（台灣證交所股票代號2317，即國際知名的富士康）被描述為領先的AI伺服器ODM，透過輝達與超微GPU平台取得大量訂單。廣達電腦、緯創資通及緯穎科技，同樣被點名已從上述主要客戶取得橫跨多平台的訂單。

  ## 意義與展望

  2026年感恩節購物季正被業界視為AI購物代理人能否從有限展示走向日常消費工具的首次真實測試。這波測試的結果，將決定代理人式AI究竟能成為CSP資本支出的持久推動力，抑或只是一次週期性高峰。
