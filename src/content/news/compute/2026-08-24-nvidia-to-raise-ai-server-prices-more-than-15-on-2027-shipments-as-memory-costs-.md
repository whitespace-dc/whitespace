body_en: |
  Nvidia has told its largest customers that servers containing its artificial intelligence chips will cost more than 15 percent more on shipments beginning in early 2027, driven by soaring prices for the memory chips those systems require, according to people familiar with the matter who asked not to be identified commenting on communications that have not been made public.

  The increases will apply to systems built around Nvidia's flagship Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell chips, with the precise magnitude varying by chip generation and memory configuration, the sources said. Nvidia did not respond to requests for comment.

  Contract manufacturers that assemble servers for large data center operators — including Microsoft, Alphabet's Google, and Oracle — recently notified those customers of the forthcoming increases, according to the sources.

  ## Why memory is driving the increases

  AI accelerator chips depend on large quantities of dynamic random access memory, or DRAM — a type of high-speed chip memory — to function effectively. Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix, and Micron Technology account for most of the world's DRAM production. While all three have been expanding output, supply has not kept pace with demand generated by the AI infrastructure buildout, pushing DRAM prices sharply higher and giving those manufacturers what the sources describe as unprecedented influence over costs across the AI hardware supply chain.

  Nvidia's accelerator processors are themselves manufactured under contract by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company. Nvidia carries a gross margin — the share of revenue remaining after production costs — of 75 percent, but pressure from memory suppliers limits how much of that position it can deploy to absorb input costs on behalf of customers.

  ## Pricing pressure extends across the industry

  Nvidia is not the only company passing costs through to buyers. Apple and Qualcomm have each said publicly that chip shortages have forced them to charge more for their products, according to the source report. Nvidia has also raised prices on its gaming-oriented PC graphics cards, industry news site Tom's Hardware reported earlier in August.

  ## Consequences for data center operators and potential competitors

  Major technology companies — Amazon, Microsoft, Google, and Meta — are each developing in-house AI chips, but remain dependent on Nvidia purchases for their current data center buildouts. Their ability to reduce that dependence will also turn on whether they can secure adequate DRAM supply from Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.

  The price increases add a further complication to an infrastructure expansion already under stress. Project delays, labor shortages, tightening capital markets, and community resistance to large data center developments have already complicated many planned builds, according to the source report.

  How Nvidia's customers respond — and whether the increases create an opening for rival chip vendors — will likely depend in part on those customers' own ability to secure memory supply, the sources said.

  ## Earnings and investor attention

  Nvidia is scheduled to report fiscal second-quarter earnings next week. Updates from the company, which is the world's most valuable publicly traded company, are closely followed by technology investors and industry planners who have committed large amounts of capital to AI infrastructure.

  消息人士表示，漲價對象為以輝達旗艦款Vera Rubin與Grace Blackwell晶片為核心所建置的系統，調漲幅度將依晶片世代及記憶體配置而有所不同。輝達未回應置評請求。

  據消息人士指出，代大型資料中心業者組裝伺服器的合約製造商——服務對象涵蓋微軟、Alphabet旗下的Google，以及甲骨文——近日已向這些客戶發出即將漲價的通知。

  ## 記憶體為何成為漲價的推手

  AI加速晶片要有效運作，必須倚賴大量的動態隨機存取記憶體（DRAM）——一種高速晶片記憶體。三星電子、SK海力士與美光科技三家業者供應了全球絕大多數的DRAM產能。儘管三家廠商皆持續擴大產能，供應量仍無法趕上AI基礎設施建置所帶動的需求，致使DRAM價格急速攀升，也讓這些製造商對整個AI硬體供應鏈的成本掌握了消息人士形容為「空前」的影響力。

  輝達的加速器處理器本身由台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）代工製造。輝達的毛利率——亦即扣除生產成本後佔營收的比例——達75%，但來自記憶體供應商的壓力，限制了輝達能替客戶吸收多少投入成本的空間。

  ## 漲價壓力蔓延至整個產業

  輝達並非唯一將成本轉嫁給買家的企業。根據消息來源報導，蘋果與高通均曾公開表示，晶片短缺迫使它們調高產品售價。此外，輝達也已對旗下面向遊戲PC市場的顯示卡進行調漲，產業媒體《Tom's Hardware》於今年八月稍早對此有所報導。

  ## 對資料中心業者與潛在競爭對手的影響

  亞馬遜、微軟、Google與Meta等大型科技公司，各自正在研發自家的AI晶片，但就目前的資料中心建置而言，仍高度仰賴向輝達採購。它們能否降低對輝達的依賴，同樣取決於能否從三星、SK海力士與美光取得足夠的DRAM供應。

  此波漲價更為一個已承受重壓的基礎設施擴張計畫雪上加霜。根據消息來源報導，工程延誤、人力短缺、資本市場趨緊，以及社區對大型資料中心開發案的抵制，已令許多原訂建設計畫倍感棘手。

  輝達客戶將如何回應，以及這波漲價是否為競爭對手晶片廠商創造切入機會，在一定程度上將取決於這些客戶自身取得記憶體供應的能力，消息人士如此表示。

  ## 財報與投資人關注焦點

  輝達預計下週公布第二財季財報。作為全球市值最高的上市公司，輝達的最新動態備受科技投資人及產業規劃者的高度關注，這些人已在AI基礎設施上承諾投入龐大資金。
