  ## Caterpillar posts record $20.5B quarter as data center generator demand surges 72%

  Caterpillar reported record second-quarter sales and revenue of $20.5 billion, a 24% increase from $16.6 billion in the same period last year, with power generation retail sales rising 72% year-over-year on what the company described as "very strong demand" for large generator sets and turbines used in data center applications.

  The Texas-based equipment manufacturer's power and energy segment — which includes generators, turbines, and related industrial equipment — posted $8.2 billion in quarterly sales, up 17% from a year earlier, with segment profit climbing 30% to $2 billion. The result reflects deepening orders from hyperscalers, the large cloud and technology companies that operate vast data centers, as well as customers in oil and gas, mining, and marine industries, the company said.

  ## CEO addresses AI demand uncertainty

  Caterpillar CEO and Chairman Joseph Creed used the company's earnings call Tuesday to address investor concern about whether artificial intelligence-driven data center construction would sustain its pace. Chip stocks sold off sharply last week amid broader anxiety about AI capital spending and return on investment. Creed said Caterpillar is in constant discussions with customers but that "no one is slowing down at the moment."

  Power and energy customers are placing orders extending through 2030, Creed said. Caterpillar's total order backlog stands at $72 billion, with about 59% of that — roughly $42.5 billion — expected to be delivered within the next 12 months.

  ## Resuming a discontinued engine platform

  To meet demand, Caterpillar is restarting production of its 10-megawatt medium-speed gas reciprocating engine platform, a product line the company discontinued in 2022 due to what Creed described as "limited industry opportunity" at the time. The company plans to restore 1.5 gigawatts of production capacity through this platform, with initial shipments targeted for the fourth quarter of this year.

  A megawatt is a measure of power output sufficient to supply roughly 750 to 1,000 average U.S. homes; 1.5 gigawatts represents 1,500 megawatts, or the equivalent output of a large utility-scale power plant. For context, a single large hyperscale data center campus can require hundreds of megawatts of power, making backup and supplemental generation a significant procurement category.

  ## Construction and resource segments also grow

  The growth was not confined to power generation. Caterpillar's construction segment posted $8.3 billion in sales for the quarter, up 35% from a year ago, driven by higher-than-expected demand in North America, where regional sales surged 50% to nearly $5.1 billion. Construction segment profit rose 57% to $1.9 billion.

  The company also began delivering its first equipment to Major Projects, a rental joint venture it established to serve customers with multibillion-dollar construction projects across North America, supplementing its existing dealer rental network.

  Caterpillar's resource industries segment — covering mining and rail equipment — reached $4.6 billion in sales, up 20% year-over-year, with segment profit of $693 million, up 23%. In July, the company completed its acquisition of Skycatch, a provider of artificial intelligence-driven spatial data capture and analysis software for the mining industry.

  ## Tariffs and guidance

  Caterpillar's quarterly results included a $392 million refund tied to tariffs that were revoked under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act, a U.S. law that grants the executive branch authority to regulate international commerce during declared national emergencies. Setting that refund aside, the company said it expects tariff-related costs to total $2.2 billion for the full year, according to CFO Kyle Epley.

  Despite that headwind, Caterpillar raised its full-year revenue guidance, now projecting sales and revenue growth in the "mid-to-high teens" compared to last year. The company said it plans to expand capacity and increase throughput in the second half of 2025.

  ## What it means for the industry

  The results signal that demand for large-scale backup and supplemental power generation equipment remains strong enough to justify restarting production lines that were idled only three years ago. The 72% retail sales growth in power generation, the $72 billion backlog, and customer order commitments extending to 2030 indicate that data center operators are locking in generator supply years in advance — a procurement pattern that benefits manufacturers able to scale capacity quickly.

  Caterpillar said the first shipments from the resumed 10-megawatt engine platform are scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of 2025.

  ## 資料中心帶動發電機需求暴增72%，開拓重工創下205億美元季度紀錄

  開拓重工（Caterpillar）公布第二季銷售額與營收雙創歷史新高，達205億美元，較去年同期的166億美元成長24%。其中電力發電零售銷售額年增72%，公司形容這波成長來自資料中心應用大型發電機組與渦輪機的「極為強勁需求」。

  這家總部位於德克薩斯州的設備製造商，其電力與能源事業部——涵蓋發電機、渦輪機及相關工業設備——單季營收達82億美元，年增17%；事業部獲利更大幅攀升30%至20億美元。公司表示，此一亮眼成績反映超大規模業者（即營運龐大資料中心的大型雲端與科技公司）訂單持續深化，石油天然氣、採礦及航運業客戶的需求同樣強勁。

  ## 執行長回應市場對AI需求前景的疑慮

  開拓重工董事長暨執行長約瑟夫·克里德（Joseph Creed）週二在法說會上，直接回應投資人對人工智慧驅動的資料中心建設能否持續高速推進的疑慮。上週晶片股因市場對AI資本支出及投資回報率的廣泛憂慮而大幅下挫。克里德表示，開拓重工持續與客戶密切溝通，目前「沒有任何客戶有放緩的跡象」。

  克里德指出，電力與能源客戶的訂單已排至2030年。開拓重工整體未交訂單金額達720億美元，其中約59%——即大約425億美元——預計將在未來12個月內交貨。

  ## 重啟停產引擎平台

  為因應強勁需求，開拓重工宣布重啟其10百萬瓦中速燃氣往復式引擎平台的生產。這條產品線於2022年停產，克里德當時的說法是「彼時產業機會有限」。公司計畫透過此一平台恢復1.5吉瓦的生產產能，首批出貨目標定於今年第四季。

  1百萬瓦的電力輸出大約可供應750至1,000個美國普通家庭用電；1.5吉瓦即1,500百萬瓦，相當於一座大型公用事業規模電廠的輸出功率。就背景而言，單一大型超大規模資料中心園區的用電需求可達數百百萬瓦，使備用與補充發電設備成為重要的採購項目。

  ## 建設與資源事業部同步成長

  此波成長並不侷限於電力發電領域。開拓重工建設事業部本季營收達83億美元，年增35%，受惠於北美需求超出預期，該地區銷售額暴增50%、接近51億美元。建設事業部獲利大增57%至19億美元。

  公司也開始向「Major Projects」交付首批設備——這是開拓重工為服務北美大型多十億美元建設專案客戶而成立的租賃合資事業，作為現有經銷商租賃網絡的補充。

  開拓重工資源工業事業部——涵蓋採礦及鐵路設備——本季營收達46億美元，年增20%；事業部獲利6.93億美元，年增23%。7月，公司完成對Skycatch的收購，這是一家為採礦業提供AI驅動空間數據採集與分析軟體的服務商。

  ## 關稅衝擊與營收展望

  開拓重工本季業績中包含一筆3.92億美元的退款，來源是依據《國際緊急經濟權力法》（International Emergency Economic Powers Act）遭撤銷的關稅——該法授予行政部門在宣布國家緊急狀態期間管制國際商業往來的權力。剔除這筆退款後，財務長凱爾·埃普利（Kyle Epley）表示，公司預估全年關稅相關成本將達22億美元。

  儘管面臨這項逆風，開拓重工仍上調全年營收展望，目前預估銷售額與營收成長將落在「中高雙位數」區間。公司表示，計畫於2025年下半年擴大產能、提升出貨量。

  ## 產業意義

  此次業績顯示，大型備用及補充發電設備的市場需求依然強勁，足以驅動開拓重工重啟僅三年前才停產的生產線。電力發電零售銷售額72%的增幅、720億美元的訂單積壓，以及客戶訂單承諾延伸至2030年，在在說明資料中心業者正提前數年鎖定發電機供貨——這種採購模式對能夠快速擴充產能的製造商大為有利。

  開拓重工表示，重啟後的10百萬瓦引擎平台首批出貨預計於2025年第四季展開。
