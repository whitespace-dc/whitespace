body_en: |
  Battery-storage company Viridi and energy-as-a-service provider Budderfly have agreed to install Viridi's "fail-safe" battery systems at 100 U.S. commercial facilities enrolled in Budderfly's managed efficiency program, the companies said Wednesday.

  The partnership covers restaurants, retailers, fitness studios and manufacturing sites. The first phase is already underway; early deployments include a Connecticut manufacturing facility and three restaurant franchises, Budderfly CEO Al Subbloie told Facilities Dive.

  Combined with on-site solar panels, upgraded heating and cooling controls, LED lighting and other efficiency measures Budderfly supplies, the battery systems can reduce a commercial customer's energy costs by 60% to 70%, Subbloie said. Customers pay nothing upfront: Budderfly funds, deploys and manages the equipment in exchange for a share of the energy savings and electricity-market revenue those assets generate.

  Initial deployments are concentrated in Massachusetts, Connecticut and California. Viridi CEO Jon Williams described those as "leading markets" for commercial battery systems, citing "robust incentive programs" in New England and a sophisticated demand-response framework in California. Demand response is a utility-managed arrangement in which businesses agree to reduce power consumption during periods of high grid stress in exchange for financial compensation. The companies said they plan to expand to additional states "where battery storage can deliver meaningful value for customers, utilities and the grid."

  ## Commercial storage lags grid-scale deployment

  The U.S. installed 18.9 gigawatts (GW) of energy storage capacity in 2025, but more than 75% of that total came from utilities and independent power developers, according to Wood Mackenzie, an energy consultancy. Those grid-tied systems typically store excess power during overnight or midday periods, when demand and prices are low, then discharge during evenings or extreme weather events, when demand and prices rise.

  The commercial and industrial (C&I) segment has lagged behind grid-scale deployment, Wood Mackenzie noted in its most recent annual energy storage report, citing a mix of economic and regulatory barriers. The consultancy projects C&I installations to grow 39% between 2025 and 2030, driven partly by emerging markets such as Connecticut and New Mexico and anchored by near-term demand in California, Massachusetts and New York.

  ## How the model removes upfront barriers

  Budderfly's structure is designed to clear the capital and operational hurdles that have historically constrained commercial adoption. The company installs and operates efficient lighting, HVAC systems, digital building controls and a proprietary optimization platform at its own cost, then recoups its investment through a share of the savings and market revenue those assets produce.

  "We fund, deploy, and manage the batteries, so customers gain the benefits of advanced energy technology without the upfront capital investment, operational burden or performance risk," Subbloie said.

  ## Viridi's fire-resistant battery design

  Viridi produces modular battery systems ranging in capacity from 50 kilowatt-hours (kWh) to more than 1.2 megawatt-hours (MWh). A 50 kWh unit is two to ten times the capacity of a standard home battery installation; the larger end of the range is sufficient to back up critical systems at midsized commercial and institutional facilities during hours-long outages, the company said.

  Viridi markets its "fail-safe" design as significantly less prone to overheating and fire than conventional lithium-ion batteries, a safety profile it says enables deployment in occupied commercial buildings. Fire risk has been a persistent barrier to wider commercial battery adoption; the National Fire Protection Association released a comprehensive battery safety code this spring.

  "For years, safety concerns have limited the adoption of battery storage across commercial real estate, preventing many building owners from taking advantage of the technology at scale," Williams said.

  ## Significance for C&I energy storage

  The Budderfly-Viridi arrangement illustrates one emerging commercial model for closing the gap between grid-scale and C&I storage deployment: a third-party owner absorbs capital and performance risk while the facility operator captures energy-cost savings with no ownership obligations. Wood Mackenzie's projection of 39% C&I storage growth through 2030 points to expanding opportunity, particularly in states with active incentive or demand-response programs.

  The companies said they expect to announce additional deployment markets as the partnership advances beyond its initial 100-site phase.

  ## Budderfly與Viridi攜手，將在美百處商業設施部署防火儲能系統

  儲能公司Viridi與能源即服務供應商Budderfly週三宣布，雙方已達成協議，將在納入Budderfly效能管理計畫的全美100處商業設施安裝Viridi的「防故障失效」（fail-safe）電池系統。

  此次合作涵蓋餐廳、零售商、健身工作室及製造業廠房等多種業態。Budderfly執行長艾爾．蘇布盧瓦（Al Subbloie）向《Facilities Dive》表示，第一階段已正式啟動，首批部署地點包括康乃狄克州一處製造廠及三家連鎖餐廳加盟店。

  蘇布盧瓦表示，結合現場太陽能板、升級版暖通空調控制設備、LED照明及Budderfly提供的其他節能措施，這套電池系統可協助商業客戶降低60%至70%的能源費用。客戶無需預付任何費用：Budderfly自行出資、部署並管理所有設備，並以這些資產所帶來的能源節省收益及電力市場收入作為回收方式。

  初期部署集中於麻薩諸塞州、康乃狄克州及加利福尼亞州。Viridi執行長喬恩．威廉斯（Jon Williams）將上述地區稱為商業儲能系統的「領先市場」，並指出新英格蘭地區擁有「完善的獎勵計畫」，加州則建立了成熟的需量反應機制。需量反應是一種由電力公司主導的機制，企業同意在電網壓力較大的時段降低用電量，以換取相應的經濟補償。兩家公司表示，未來計畫將合作範圍擴展至其他「電池儲能能為客戶、電力公司及電網帶來實質價值」的州別。

  ## 商業儲能部署落後於電網規模應用

  根據能源諮詢機構Wood Mackenzie的資料，美國2025年新增儲能裝置容量達18.9百萬瓩（GW），但其中逾75%來自公用事業者及獨立電力開發商。這類併網系統通常在需求與電價較低的深夜或午間儲存多餘電力，再於需求與電價攀升的傍晚或極端天氣時段放電。

  Wood Mackenzie在其最新年度儲能報告中指出，商業及工業（C&I）部門的部署進度遠落後於電網規模應用，原因涉及經濟與法規等多重障礙。該機構預測，受康乃狄克州、新墨西哥州等新興市場帶動，加上加州、麻薩諸塞州及紐約州的近期需求作為支撐，C&I儲能裝置容量將在2025年至2030年間成長39%。

  ## 零預付模式如何消除進入門檻

  Budderfly的商業模式旨在突破過去制約商業儲能普及的資本與營運障礙。該公司自行出資安裝並運營高效照明、暖通空調系統、數位樓宇控制設備及自有最佳化平台，再透過分享上述資產產生的節省收益與市場收入來回收投資。

  蘇布盧瓦表示：「我們負責出資、部署與管理電池系統，讓客戶在無需預付資本、承擔營運負擔或績效風險的情況下，享有先進能源技術所帶來的效益。」

  ## Viridi的防火電池設計

  Viridi生產的模組化電池系統容量範圍從50度電（kWh）至逾1,200度電（1.2MWh）不等。50度電的機型容量是標準家用電池裝置的2至10倍；較大容量的機型則足以在停電數小時期間為中型商業及機構設施的關鍵系統提供備援電力。

  Viridi主打其「防故障失效」設計遠比傳統鋰離子電池更不易過熱起火，並表示這項安全特性使其得以在有人員活動的商業建築中部署。火災風險長期以來是商業電池普及的重大障礙；美國國家防火協會（NFPA）已於今年春天發布了一套完整的電池安全規範。

  威廉斯表示：「多年來，安全顧慮限制了電池儲能在商業不動產領域的普及，使許多業主無法大規模採用這項技術。」

  ## 對商業及工業儲能市場的意義

  Budderfly與Viridi的合作模式，呈現了縮小電網規模與C&I儲能部署差距的一種新興商業路徑：由第三方業主承擔資本與績效風險，設施營運方則在無需擁有設備的情況下獲得能源費用節省的效益。Wood Mackenzie預測C&I儲能至2030年將成長39%，顯示市場機會正在擴大，尤其是在設有積極獎勵或需量反應計畫的州別。

  兩家公司表示，隨著合作關係推進至首批100個據點以外，預計將陸續宣布更多新進入的部署市場。
