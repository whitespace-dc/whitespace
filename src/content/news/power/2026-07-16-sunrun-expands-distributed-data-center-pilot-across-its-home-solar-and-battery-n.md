body_en: |
  Sunrun, the residential solar and battery storage company, said July 8 it is expanding a "distributed data center" pilot that places computing nodes (roughly the size of a small desktop computer) inside homes already equipped with its energy systems, offering AI companies a path around the years-long interconnection queues facing large centralized data centers.

  The company serves more than 1.1 million solar and battery customers and says those existing installations represent a deployment base that gives it a structural advantage over hyperscalers (the large technology companies that develop and lease computing capacity in centralized data centers, which can consume hundreds of megawatts of power or more at a single location).

  ## What Sunrun is proposing

  Sunrun will manage the computing nodes around each host home's energy consumption patterns, local electricity rate structure, and available grid service programs, the company said. Customers who host a node will receive compensation, though Sunrun has not specified the amount.

  The company has not disclosed how much it plans to spend on the expanded pilot or how many customers it intends to enroll. A Sunrun webpage invites prospective hosts to join a waitlist. Sunrun did not respond to a Utility Dive request for additional details.

  Sunrun says it "expects to complete the pilot over the coming months" and will assess results before deciding on a broader rollout. In the meantime, it said it is "actively in discussions with enterprise compute offtakers, homebuilders, and utility partners to structure the commercial and deployment frameworks that would support expansion."

  ## Sunrun's grid efficiency case

  Unlike large centralized facilities that add load to already-congested transmission and distribution networks, the smaller residential nodes increase utilization of existing infrastructure, Sunrun said. The company also argues that behind-the-meter computing nodes — those installed at customer premises and drawing on on-site solar and battery power rather than the broader grid — are more resilient to "regional threats" such as rising utility rates, overloaded grids, and power supply shortages.

  "AI companies are scrambling to secure greater access to energy and computing power. We are now using our leadership position in distributed home energy and proven infrastructure to bring compute closer to the sources of energy and inference," said Paul Dickson, Sunrun's President and Chief Revenue Officer.

  ## Prior initiative and expert assessment

  Sunrun's announcement follows a similar initiative disclosed about three months earlier by SPAN, an electrical hardware startup, in partnership with NVIDIA. In an April interview with Latitude Media, SPAN CEO Arch Rao said a 100-megawatt (MW) data center can take up to five years to build at a cost of $15 million per MW. SPAN said it can deploy an equivalent amount of computing capacity across 8,000 residential nodes in six months at $3 million per MW. SPAN's own pilot will install about 1.25 MW of computing capacity at 100 new-build homes.

  Benjamin Lee, a computer architect at the University of Pennsylvania, told Ars Technica in May that the distributed compute model could gain traction as AI inference overtakes training as the dominant type of AI-related processing. Training large models requires thousands of chips working in concert, Lee said, whereas inference workloads can be scaled down to run just a few chips.

  "Computation for AI inference can and should be distributed at the 'edge,' deployed on smaller platforms closer to population centers and users. The strategy could impose much smaller impacts on the grid," Lee said.

  Lee cautioned, however, that the residential scale may not be necessary to achieve those grid benefits. A 20-MW data center could deliver similar advantages, he said. Lee also raised security risks: valuable AI chips placed in private homes may be more exposed to both digital and physical threats than those housed in well-defended data centers.

  ## Significance for the industry

  Sunrun's base of more than 1.1 million existing customers represents what the company calls a structural advantage that hyperscalers cannot quickly replicate, giving it a potential head start if the residential compute model proves viable at scale. The pilot is aimed at fast-growing firms behind leading consumer and enterprise AI models that are seeking compute capacity without queuing for interconnection at traditional data centers.

  Sunrun expects to complete the pilot within months, after which it will decide whether to pursue a broader rollout.
body_zh: |
  住宅太陽能與電池儲能業者Sunrun於七月八日表示，正在擴大一項「分散式資料中心」試點計畫，將大小約與小型桌上型電腦相當的運算節點，部署至已安裝其能源系統的住宅內，為AI企業提供一條繞過大型集中式資料中心多年漫長電網併網排隊的替代路徑。

  Sunrun目前服務逾一百一十萬名太陽能與電池儲能用戶，並表示這些既有安裝構成了龐大的部署基礎，使其在結構上具備優於超大規模業者的先天優勢。所謂超大規模業者，是指在集中式資料中心開發並出租算力的大型科技企業，單一地點的耗電量往往達數百兆瓦以上。

  ## Sunrun的提案內容

  Sunrun表示，公司將配合每戶住宅的能源消耗模式、當地電費結構及可參與的電網服務方案，統一管理各運算節點。提供場地的用戶將獲得報酬，但Sunrun尚未公布具體金額。

  該公司亦未透露擴大試點的預算規模，也未說明計畫招募多少用戶參與。Sunrun官網目前開放有意參與的屋主登記候補。對於《Utility Dive》的進一步採訪請求，Sunrun未予回應。

  Sunrun表示，「預計在未來數月內完成試點」，並將在評估結果後，決定是否推進更大規模的布建。此外，公司表示正「積極與企業算力採購方、建商及電力公司夥伴洽談，以建立支撐擴張所需的商業與部署框架」。

  ## Sunrun的電網效益主張

  Sunrun指出，大型集中式設施會對原本已壅塞的輸配電網路增添負擔，相較之下，規模較小的住宅節點能提升既有基礎設施的使用效率。該公司並主張，位於電表後端的運算節點——即安裝在用戶端、倚賴現場太陽能與電池電力而非仰賴大電網供電的節點——更能抵禦「區域性威脅」，例如電費上漲、電網超載及電力供應短缺等問題。

  「AI企業正競相爭取更充裕的能源與算力。我們現在正運用在分散式家用能源領域的領導地位，以及經過驗證的基礎設施，將運算資源拉近至能源與推論的來源端，」Sunrun總裁兼首席營收長Paul Dickson表示。

  ## 先前類似計畫與專家評析

  Sunrun的宣布，跟進的是大約三個月前SPAN與輝達（NVIDIA）合作所披露的類似計畫。SPAN是一家電氣硬體新創公司。SPAN執行長Arch Rao在四月接受《Latitude Media》採訪時表示，一座一百兆瓦（MW）的資料中心從建設到完工最長可達五年，每兆瓦造價高達一千五百萬美元。SPAN表示，透過在八千個住宅節點分散部署的方式，可在六個月內建構同等算力，每兆瓦造價僅需三百萬美元。SPAN自身的試點計畫，將在一百戶新建住宅安裝約一點二五兆瓦的算力。

  賓州大學（University of Pennsylvania）電腦架構學者Benjamin Lee，於五月接受《Ars Technica》採訪時表示，隨著AI推論超越訓練、成為AI相關處理的主流類型，分散式算力模式可望逐漸受到重視。Lee指出，訓練大型模型需要數千顆晶片協同運作，而推論工作負載則可縮減至僅需數顆晶片即可執行。

  「AI推論的運算可以也應當分散部署於『邊緣』，布建在更貼近人口中心與使用者的小型平台上。這樣的策略對電網的衝擊可望大幅降低，」Lee說道。

  然而Lee也提出警示，認為住宅規模未必是達成上述電網效益的必要條件。他指出，一座二十兆瓦的資料中心同樣能帶來類似優勢。此外，Lee也點出了資安疑慮：部署在私人住宅的高價AI晶片，在數位與實體層面所面臨的威脅，可能遠大於置身防護嚴密之資料中心內的晶片。

  ## 對產業的意義

  Sunrun逾一百一十萬名既有客戶所構成的規模，正是該公司所稱的結構性優勢——超大規模業者在短期內難以複製的先機。若住宅算力模式被證明在規模化上確實可行，Sunrun便可搶得先機。這項試點計畫的目標客群，是那些積極尋求算力、卻不願在傳統資料中心排隊等待電網併網的消費性與企業AI模型領域高速成長企業。

  Sunrun預計在數月內完成試點，屆時將決定是否推進更大規模的布建。
