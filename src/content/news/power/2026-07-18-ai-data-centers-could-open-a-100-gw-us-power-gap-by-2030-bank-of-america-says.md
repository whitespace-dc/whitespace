body_en: |
  AI data centers are on track to create a power supply gap of more than 100 gigawatts (GW) in the United States by 2030, Bank of America analysts said in a new research report, forcing utilities to extend coal plant operations and driving data center developers toward on-site generation as grid capacity falls short of surging demand.

  ## The supply-demand math

  Bank of America forecasts the United States will require more than 230 GW of new generating capacity over the next five years. Regulated utilities are expected to deliver roughly 93 GW of accredited supply — capacity that can reliably be counted on during peak demand periods — leaving a shortfall exceeding 100 GW, according to the report.

  Data centers are the primary driver. AI infrastructure buildout is expected to add roughly 125 GW of new U.S. electric load through 2030, pushing total electricity demand growth to a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) — the annualized pace at which consumption is projected to expand — of 4.1% from 2026 through 2030. That trajectory marks a sharp departure from a decade of largely flat consumption growth, a period when efficiency improvements, LED adoption and distributed solar generation held overall demand in check, BofA analysts said.

  The analysis draws heavily on the firm's semiconductor research team, whose projections for rapid deployment of AI computing hardware — specialized chips and the servers that house them — underpin the electricity demand forecast. Utilities have revised their own demand outlooks upward in each of the three prior years as AI-related power consumption materialized faster than anticipated, the analysts noted.

  ## Equipment bottlenecks steer developers toward on-site generation

  The most immediate constraint is equipment availability. Large gas turbines — the preferred technology for flexible, dispatchable power generation — are largely sold out through 2030, and new units can take years to enter service after shipment. That bottleneck is steering data center developers and utilities toward alternatives, according to BofA.

  Natural gas reciprocating engines, which can be deployed more quickly and respond rapidly to fluctuating power loads, are drawing increased interest as a result. Manufacturers including Caterpillar, INNIO, Rolls-Royce and Wärtsilä have expanded production capacity to meet rising demand, the analysts said.

  More than 7.5 GW of data center projects incorporating on-site generation are already under construction, with a further 60-plus GW in pre-construction stages, according to the report. Rather than operating entirely off-grid, these facilities are expected to combine self-generation with traditional grid connections to improve reliability and shorten project timelines.

  ## Coal retirements delayed, batteries and transmission cited as partial remedies

  As firm, dispatchable capacity tightens, utilities and regulators are keeping existing generating assets online longer. Coal plant retirement dates have been delayed or canceled in Maryland, Wisconsin, Indiana, Utah, Kansas, Nebraska and Mississippi, according to BofA's analysis.

  Battery storage and transmission expansion could also ease reliability pressures, the analysts said, though they cautioned that transmission projects often take years to permit and construct. The Champlain Hudson Power Express took 16 years from planning to energization, the report noted, illustrating the development timelines facing new grid infrastructure.

  Regulatory changes that increase utilization of existing generating assets represent an additional lever, BofA said, without specifying particular rule changes.

  ## Planned additions may overstate real supply

  BofA analysts flagged that planned generation additions risk overstating usable capacity because intermittent resources such as wind and solar contribute less accredited capacity — the share of nameplate rating that counts toward meeting peak demand — than their installed ratings suggest. That dynamic makes firm resources essential even as total renewable generation expands.

  Higher power prices are one potential moderating factor, but their near-term effect is likely to be limited. Academic research cited in the report indicates that electricity demand is relatively price-inelastic: a 10% increase in real electricity prices typically produces only a 1% to 2% decline in consumption.

  ## A constraint on delivery, not demand

  The BofA report frames the supply shortfall as a geographic and infrastructure problem as much as a capacity problem. "The market is no longer constrained by demand — it is constrained by where power can actually be delivered," the analysts wrote in the Global Research Report.

  That distinction has practical consequences for data center siting, utility resource planning and regulatory frameworks governing how the cost of new grid infrastructure is allocated among ratepayers. The concentration of AI-driven data center growth in the United States is prompting utilities and regulators to address those allocation questions directly, according to the report.

  With large turbine supply committed through the end of the decade, the pipeline of more than 60 GW of data center projects in pre-construction with on-site generation represents the industry's most immediate near-term response to the capacity constraint.
body_zh: |
  美國銀行（Bank of America）分析師在最新研究報告中指出，AI資料中心的快速擴張將在2030年前於美國造成逾100吉瓦（GW）的電力供應缺口，迫使公用事業延長燃煤電廠的運營年限，並驅使資料中心開發商轉向自建現場發電設施，以因應電網容量嚴重供不應求的局面。

  ## 供需失衡的數字

  美國銀行預測，美國未來五年將需要逾230吉瓦的新增發電容量。受監管的公用事業預計僅能提供約93吉瓦的認證供電能力——即在尖峰需求時段可靠計入的容量——由此形成逾100吉瓦的供應缺口。

  資料中心是此波需求激增的主要推手。AI基礎設施的大規模建設預計將在2030年前為美國新增約125吉瓦的電力負載，帶動整體電力需求從2026年至2030年以年複合成長率（CAGR）4.1%的速度擴張。這一走勢與過去十年大致持平的用電趨勢形成強烈對比——在那段時期，能源效率提升、LED照明普及以及分散式太陽能發電使整體需求維持低檔，美銀分析師指出。

  本次分析大量援引該行半導體研究團隊的預測，後者對AI運算硬體——包括專用晶片及搭載晶片的伺服器——快速部署的推算，正是電力需求預測的核心依據。美銀分析師也指出，各大公用事業在過去連續三年均已上修自身的需求展望，原因是AI相關用電量的實際成長速度持續超乎預期。

  ## 設備瓶頸促使開發商轉向現場自建發電

  眼前最迫切的制約因素在於設備供應。大型燃氣渦輪機是彈性、可調度電力的首選技術，但其訂單已幾乎售罄至2030年，新機組從出廠到正式上線往往還需數年時間。這一瓶頸正在推動資料中心開發商與公用事業轉向替代方案，美銀表示。

  天然氣往復式引擎因可快速部署、且能迅速響應波動性電力負載而備受矚目。製造商包括卡特彼勒（Caterpillar）、INNIO、勞斯萊斯（Rolls-Royce）與瓦錫蘭（Wärtsilä）均已擴大產能，以滿足日益攀升的需求，分析師指出。

  根據報告，目前已有逾7.5吉瓦的資料中心專案納入現場發電設施並進入施工階段，另有逾60吉瓦的專案處於動工前期。這些設施預計不會完全脫離電網獨立運作，而是結合自建發電與傳統電網連接，以提升可靠性並縮短專案建設時程。

  ## 燃煤退役延宕、儲能與輸電擴建可部分緩解壓力

  隨著穩定可調度的電力供應日趨緊張，公用事業與監管機構正設法讓既有發電資產延長使用年限。美銀分析顯示，馬里蘭州、威斯康辛州、印第安納州、猶他州、堪薩斯州、內布拉斯加州及密西西比州的燃煤電廠退役時程均已延後或取消。

  分析師表示，電池儲能與輸電線路擴建亦有助於舒緩供電可靠性的壓力，但同時警告，輸電專案往往需要多年時間才能完成許可審查與工程建設。報告以尚普蘭哈德遜電力快線（Champlain Hudson Power Express）為例，指出該專案從規劃到通電歷時16年，足以說明電網新基礎設施所面臨的漫長開發時程。

  此外，透過法規修訂提高現有發電資產的使用效率也是可採行的選項之一，美銀表示，但未具體點名任何規則修改方向。

  ## 計畫新增容量可能高估實際可用供電

  美銀分析師提醒，計畫新增發電量有可能高估實際可用容量，原因在於風電、太陽能等間歇性能源的認證容量——即可計入尖峰需求保障的有效比例——遠低於裝置容量所呈現的數字。這一特性使得即便整體再生能源裝置量持續擴大，穩定的可調度電力資源仍不可或缺。

  電價上漲是可能抑制需求的因素之一，但短期內的效果恐怕十分有限。報告引用學術研究指出，電力需求對價格相對不敏感：實質電價每上漲10%，通常僅帶來1%至2%的用電量下滑。

  ## 制約在於電力能否送達，而非需求本身

  美銀報告將供電缺口定性為地理與基礎設施問題，而非單純的容量問題。「市場所面臨的制約已不再是需求不足，而是電力究竟能否被確實送達，」分析師在全球研究報告中如此寫道。

  這一定性對資料中心選址、公用事業資源規劃，以及新增電網基礎設施成本如何在用戶之間分攤的監管框架均有實質影響。美國AI驅動的資料中心建設高度集中，正促使公用事業與監管機構直接面對這些成本分攤問題，報告指出。

  隨著大型渦輪機供應已承諾至本十年末，逾60吉瓦的資料中心動工前期專案若導入現場自建發電，代表的正是業界針對此一容量制約最立竿見影的近期因應之道。
