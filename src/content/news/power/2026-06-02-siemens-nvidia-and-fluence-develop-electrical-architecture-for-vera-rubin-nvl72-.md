# Siemens, Nvidia, and Fluence Develop Electrical Architecture for Vera Rubin NVL72 Data Centers

Siemens, Nvidia, and Fluence have jointly developed a reference electrical and power architecture for data centers running the Vera Rubin NVL72 platform. The architecture encompasses the complete electrical path throughout the data center infrastructure. This collaboration provides a standardized design framework for facilities supporting high-performance computing deployments.

## 中文

# 西門子、輝達與 Fluence 為 Vera Rubin NVL72 資料中心開發電力架構

西門子、輝達和 Fluence 共同開發了一套參考電力架構，用於運行 Vera Rubin NVL72 平台的資料中心。該架構涵蓋資料中心基礎設施的完整電力路徑。此合作為支持高性能運算部署的設施提供標準化設計框架。
