  ## Reservoir Link wins dual Energy Commission licences to ship and import LNG in Malaysia

  Reservoir Link Energy Bhd, a Malaysian oilfield services company listed on Bursa Malaysia, has obtained two licences from the country's Energy Commission allowing it to transport natural gas through existing pipeline and regasification infrastructure and to import liquefied natural gas (LNG) into regasification terminals, the company disclosed in a Bursa Malaysia filing on Monday.

  The licences are issued under Malaysia's third-party access (TPA) framework, a market structure that liberalises the gas sector and permits licensed companies to import, transport, and sell natural gas. Both licences are held under Reservoir Link Sdn Bhd (RSLB), a wholly owned subsidiary, and carry a validity period of 10 years.

  ## What the licences allow

  The gas shipping licence enables RSLB to arrange for natural gas to be processed and transported through regasification terminals and pipeline networks for delivery to end customers. The second licence, designated as an import into regasification terminal licence, permits the company to import LNG into licensed regasification terminals in Malaysia.

  ## Strategic rationale: data centres and energy transition

  Reservoir Link pointed to rising electricity demand from Malaysia's expanding data centre sector as a factor expected to increase the role of natural gas as a transition fuel for power generation. The company did not quantify the demand it expects to serve.

  Executive deputy chairman Thien Chiet Chai described the licences as a key step in Reservoir Link's strategy to diversify beyond oilfield services into energy infrastructure and gas-related businesses. He said the approvals provide a platform to pursue opportunities in Malaysia's natural gas market and to support the country's energy transition.

  ## Financial context: losses widen as oilfield revenue falls

  The licence awards come as Reservoir Link's core business faces pressure. In the third quarter ended March 31, 2026 (3QFY2026), the group's net loss widened to RM22.18 million from RM15.75 million in the same period a year earlier, driven by weaker contributions from its oil- and gas-related activities segment. Revenue for the quarter fell 28.99% to RM15.75 million from RM22.18 million a year ago.

  The group operates three business segments. Oil- and gas-related activities contributed 64.65% of total revenue in the period, renewable energy and related activities accounted for 33.29%, and investment holding and wastewater treatment-related activities made up the remaining 2.06%.

  ## Recent contract activity with PETRONAS

  Separate from the licence awards, Reservoir Link said last month it was appointed as a panel contractor by Petroliam Nasional Bhd (PETRONAS), Malaysia's national oil company, under Package 2 for integrated discovered resource assessment services covering carbon capture, utilisation and storage (CCUS) and contaminants. The group also secured a separate five-year contract from PETRONAS to supply remedial sand control equipment and services.

  ## Market reaction and company scale

  The TPA licences mark Reservoir Link's most direct entry into the gas supply chain, adding a regulated gas marketing and import function to a business built on oilfield services. As of Monday's market close, Reservoir Link shares were down half a sen, or 1.82%, at 27 sen, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM102.9 million (approximately US$23 million at prevailing rates).

  ## Reservoir Link獲馬來西亞能源委員會雙重授權　涵蓋天然氣輸送與LNG進口

  在馬來西亞股票交易所（Bursa Malaysia）掛牌的油田服務公司Reservoir Link Energy Bhd，於週一向Bursa Malaysia提交的申報文件中揭露，已從馬來西亞能源委員會取得兩張執照，允許該公司透過現有管線及再氣化基礎設施輸送天然氣，並將液化天然氣（LNG）進口至再氣化終端。

  這兩張執照依據馬來西亞第三方接取（TPA）框架核發。TPA是一套推動天然氣市場自由化的機制，允許持牌業者進口、輸送並銷售天然氣。兩張執照均由全資子公司Reservoir Link Sdn Bhd（RSLB）持有，有效期各為十年。

  ## 執照賦予的業務範疇

  天然氣輸送執照讓RSLB得以安排天然氣經由再氣化終端及管線網路進行處理與輸送，最終交付至終端用戶。第二張執照定性為「進口至再氣化終端執照」，允許該公司將LNG進口至馬來西亞境內的合法再氣化終端。

  ## 策略布局：資料中心與能源轉型

  Reservoir Link指出，馬來西亞資料中心產業持續擴張、帶動電力需求上升，預計將強化天然氣作為能源轉型過渡燃料的角色。該公司並未量化預期服務的需求規模。

  執行副主席田節財（Thien Chiet Chai）表示，這兩張執照是Reservoir Link策略布局的關鍵一步，有助於公司在油田服務業務之外，多元化跨足能源基礎設施及天然氣相關業務。他說，此次核准為公司提供了進軍馬來西亞天然氣市場、支持國家能源轉型的發展平台。

  ## 財務背景：核心業務承壓、虧損持續擴大

  就在此次獲得執照之際，Reservoir Link的核心業務正承受壓力。截至2026年3月31日的第三季（3QFY2026），集團淨虧損從上年同期的1,575萬令吉擴大至2,218萬令吉，主因為石油及天然氣相關業務部門貢獻縮減。本季營收年減28.99%，從2,218萬令吉降至1,575萬令吉。

  集團旗下設有三個業務部門：石油及天然氣相關業務在本期貢獻總營收的64.65%，再生能源及相關業務占33.29%，投資控股及廢水處理相關業務則占餘下的2.06%。

  ## 近期與馬來西亞國家石油公司的合約動態

  另外，Reservoir Link上個月宣布，已獲馬來西亞國家石油公司Petroliam Nasional Bhd（PETRONAS）指定為合格承包商，參與「第二套件」整合性已發現資源評估服務，業務涵蓋碳捕集、利用與封存（CCUS）及污染物相關服務。集團亦另獲PETRONAS授予一份五年期合約，負責提供修井砂控設備及相關服務。

  ## 市場反應與公司規模

  TPA執照標誌著Reservoir Link最直接切入天然氣供應鏈的一步，在原有油田服務業務基礎上新增了受監管的天然氣行銷與進口功能。截至週一收盤，Reservoir Link股價下跌0.5仙（即半仙），跌幅1.82%，報27仙，公司市值達1億290萬令吉（按當時匯率約合2,300萬美元）。
