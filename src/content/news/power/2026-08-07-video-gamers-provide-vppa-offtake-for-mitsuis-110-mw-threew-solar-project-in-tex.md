body_en: |
  Thousands of individual video gamers have provided offtake for a 110-MW solar project in Hill County, Texas through a voluntary, subscription-based virtual power purchase agreement, renewable energy marketplace Ever.green and gaming energy startup SuperPower said last week.

  The ThreeW solar project, owned by Mitsui & Co. and located south of Dallas, recently reached commercial operations. Players of NBA 2K26 and PGA Tour 2K25 are among those who subscribed to the offering, which channels consumer payments into contracted revenue supporting the facility. The project is expected to generate 280,000 megawatt-hours of electricity annually — enough, SuperPower says, for 10 megawatts of solar capacity to offset the power equivalent of approximately 125 million hours of gaming per year.

  ## How the transaction is structured

  A virtual power purchase agreement, or VPPA, is a financial contract in which a project owner sells energy at an agreed-upon strike price and the counterparty receives the difference between that price and the prevailing wholesale market rate. No electricity flows directly to the buyer. VPPAs transfer energy price exposure from the developer to the offtaker and provide developers with predictable revenue that can support project financing.

  Ever.green, a Seattle-based renewable energy marketplace, facilitated the deal by splitting the project's offtake into portions small enough for smaller companies or individual consumers to claim — an approach the company calls "fractionalized" transactions. SuperPower, a California-based company, developed the consumer subscription product and aggregated the gaming community's collective offtake position.

  Cris Eugster, founder and chief executive of Ever.green and a former Texas utility executive, said the ThreeW arrangement is "one of" the first of its kind, while describing the underlying VPPA structure as established rather than novel. Large technology companies such as Microsoft and Meta routinely sign VPPAs covering hundreds of megawatts at a time, but most organizations and consumers lack the energy footprint to anchor such a contract on their own, Eugster said.

  "There's a lot of gamers out there, millions of them, and it's pretty energy intensive," Eugster told Utility Dive. "And it's a segment people don't really think about."

  Renewable energy certificates, or RECs, offer an alternative structure: they provide contracted revenue to the seller without shifting energy price risk to the buyer, Eugster said. Both instruments — VPPAs and RECs — can offset development costs and reduce a project's market exposure.

  ## ERCOT market context

  The Electric Reliability Council of Texas, or ERCOT, which covers most of the state's power grid, is a competitive wholesale electricity market subject to significant price swings. Contracted revenue secured before or early in construction can offset those risks and give developers flexibility in how they design and pace projects, Eugster said.

  As an example, he said a developer with sufficient upfront offtake might proceed with a solar-only first phase and add battery storage in a later phase as market conditions warrant — rather than committing to a larger, more capital-intensive combined project from the outset.

  Texas has accumulated nearly 30 gigawatt-hours of energy storage on its grid, according to the Solar Energy Industries Association. The rapid battery buildout has dampened wholesale price volatility in recent years, Eugster said, but has also pressured the economics of some proposed storage projects.

  ## Ever.green's broader transaction portfolio

  Ever.green has applied the fractionalized offtake approach to other clean energy projects beyond ThreeW. The company has structured transactions around a repowered wind farm in West Texas — which it says nearly doubled that facility's capacity — a 28-MW solar farm backed by Wells Fargo in South Carolina, and a 3.2-MW solar procurement by a West Virginia school district. The West Texas wind farm included fractional contracts, with the smallest commitment covering 1,000 MWh per year.

  Eugster said the model works for other clean energy resource types and in energy markets outside ERCOT. "Every place is unique and different, but what is common is those projects need long-term offtake to help stand up," he said.

  ## Industry significance

  The VPPA market has historically been dominated by hyperscalers, utilities, and large corporations with energy demands large enough to anchor a contract on their own. The ThreeW transaction demonstrates that aggregated, small-scale commitments — including from individual consumers — can serve the same financing function for project developers, potentially expanding the pool of offtakers available to renewable energy projects seeking contracted revenue.

  ## 電玩玩家透過訂閱制VPPA為德州太陽能案場提供售電承購

  再生能源交易市場平台Ever.green與電玩能源新創公司SuperPower上週共同宣布，數千名個人電玩玩家透過自願性訂閱制虛擬購電協議，為德州希爾郡一座110MW太陽能案場提供售電承購。

  這座ThreeW太陽能案場由三井物產持有，位於達拉斯南方，近期已正式進入商業運轉。參與訂閱的玩家涵蓋《NBA 2K26》與《PGA Tour 2K25》的玩家，消費者的訂閱付款將匯入支撐該案場的合約收益。SuperPower表示，此案場預計每年發電28萬MWh，其中10MW的太陽能容量所產生的電力，相當於每年抵銷約1億2,500萬小時的遊戲用電。

  ## 交易結構說明

  虛擬購電協議（VPPA）是一種金融合約，案場業主以議定的履約價出售電力，交易對手則收取該履約價與當時躉售市場電價之間的差額。實際電力並不直接流向買方。VPPA將電力價格風險從開發商轉移至承購方，同時為開發商提供可支撐專案融資的穩定收益。

  總部位於西雅圖的再生能源交易市場平台Ever.green促成了這筆交易，其做法是將案場的售電量切割為足夠小的份額，讓規模較小的企業或個人消費者也能參與承購——該公司將此方式稱為「碎片化」交易。總部位於加州的SuperPower則負責開發消費者訂閱產品，並整合電玩社群的集體承購部位。

  Ever.green創辦人兼執行長、前德州電力公用事業主管Cris Eugster表示，ThreeW的安排是「最早的案例之一」，同時強調背後的VPPA結構本身是成熟做法，並非創新。他說，微軟、Meta等大型科技公司習慣一次簽訂涵蓋數百MW的VPPA，但多數企業與消費者缺乏足夠的用電規模，無法單獨作為此類合約的承購主體。

  「電玩玩家數以百萬計，玩遊戲本身相當耗電，」Eugster接受《Utility Dive》採訪時表示，「但這個族群是大家平常不太會想到的。」

  再生能源憑證（REC）提供了另一種替代架構：它能為賣方提供合約收益，但不會將電力價格風險轉移給買方，Eugster說。VPPA與REC這兩種工具都能抵銷開發成本、降低案場的市場曝險。

  ## ERCOT市場背景

  德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）管轄該州大部分電網，是一個競爭性躉售電力市場，電價波動幅度相當顯著。Eugster表示，在建設前期或施工初期便確保合約收益，可以對沖這類風險，也讓開發商在設計和推進案場時更具彈性。

  他舉例說明，若開發商事先取得足夠的售電承購，可以先進行僅含太陽能的第一期工程，待市場條件成熟後再於後續階段加入儲能設備，而不必從一開始就投入規模更大、資本需求更高的複合型專案。

  根據太陽能產業協會（SEIA）的資料，德州電網累計儲能量已接近30GWh。Eugster表示，儲能設備的快速擴張近年來確實抑制了躉售電價的波動，但也對部分儲能規劃案的經濟效益造成壓力。

  ## Ever.green更廣泛的交易組合

  除了ThreeW之外，Ever.green已將碎片化售電承購模式應用於其他潔淨能源案場。該公司為西德州一座再驅動風電場——據稱使該設施容量幾乎翻倍——構建了交易架構；此外還有一座由富國銀行支持、位於南卡羅萊納州的28MW太陽能農場，以及西維吉尼亞州一個學區的3.2MW太陽能採購案。西德州風電場的案例中包含碎片化合約，其中最小的承購量為每年1,000MWh。

  Eugster表示，這套模式同樣適用於其他潔淨能源資源類型，以及ERCOT以外的電力市場。「每個地方都有其獨特性，但共通點是：這些案場都需要長期售電承購來撐起整個專案，」他說。

  ## 產業意義

  VPPA市場歷來由超大型雲端服務業者、公用事業公司與大型企業主導，這些機構的用電規模足以單獨支撐一份合約。ThreeW交易案證明，透過集合小規模承購——包括來自個人消費者的承購——同樣能為案場開發商發揮相同的融資功能，有望擴大尋求合約收益的再生能源案場所能接觸到的承購方來源。
