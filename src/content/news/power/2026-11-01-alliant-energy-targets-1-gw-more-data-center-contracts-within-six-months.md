body_en: |
  Alliant Energy expects to sign power contracts for approximately 1 gigawatt (GW) of additional data-center load within the next six months, Chief Financial Officer Robert Durian said Friday on a post-earnings call, building on the 3.4 GW the Madison, Wisconsin-based utility has already contracted with Alphabet's Google, Meta Platforms, and Blackstone-backed QTS Data Centers.

  The new commitments, if secured on that timeline, would bring Alliant's total contracted data-center demand to roughly 4.4 GW — enough to supply several million average U.S. homes. One GW is approximately the continuous output of a large nuclear or coal plant.

  ## Contracted load and near-term milestones

  Alliant's existing 3.4 GW is spread across three large customers: Google, the search and cloud unit of Alphabet; Meta Platforms, the social-media company; and QTS Data Centers, a wholesale colocation operator backed by private-equity firm Blackstone. Durian said the additional 1 GW is expected to begin ramping up between 2029 and 2031, with related capital investments spread across the company's current planning horizon and beyond.

  The first concrete near-term milestone is Meta's planned start of transmission service in Wisconsin in the first quarter of 2027, which Durian described as an early step in the utility's expanding pipeline of large-load developments.

  ## Capital expenditure and earnings guidance

  On Thursday, Alliant raised its four-year capital expenditure forecast by 19% to $15.9 billion for the period 2027 through 2030. The company expects peak demand across its system to grow by more than 60% by 2031.

  Durian said earnings growth could exceed the company's existing 8% annual target if capital spending rises further to support load growth and other investment opportunities. "We continue to work on what I'll call capital upside for different opportunities, including the additional load growth that we may see from the data center prospects that we have," he said.

  Alliant narrowed its 2026 earnings guidance to the upper half of its prior range and issued 2027 guidance of $3.63 to $3.73 per share. The midpoint of that range was in line with Wall Street analyst expectations, according to data compiled by LSEG, a financial data provider.

  ## Industry backdrop

  U.S. electricity consumption reached an all-time high in 2025 and is expected to continue rising through 2026 and beyond, driven by rapid adoption of artificial intelligence expanding data-center capacity and by broader electrification of transportation and industry, according to the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the federal government's primary energy statistics agency.

  ## What it means

  For a utility that serves roughly 1 million electric customers and 427,000 natural gas customers across Iowa and Wisconsin, the data-center pipeline represents a structural shift in load growth that is directly reshaping its capital plan. The 19% increase in the four-year capex forecast reflects spending commitments already underwritten by signed contracts, with Durian signaling that further upside depends on converting the current 1 GW prospect pipeline into agreements.

  Meta's start of transmission service in Wisconsin in the first quarter of 2027 is the next scheduled operational step in that build-out.
body_zh: |
  艾利安特能源（Alliant Energy）財務長羅伯特·杜里安（Robert Durian）週五在法說電話會議上表示，公司預計在未來六個月內，再簽訂約1吉瓦（GW）的資料中心用電合約。這將進一步擴大這家總部位於威斯康辛州麥迪遜的電力公司既有的3.4 GW合約規模——目前簽約客戶包括Alphabet旗下的Google、Meta平台，以及由私募股權巨頭黑石集團（Blackstone）支持的QTS資料中心。

  若上述新合約如期於該時程內完成簽署，艾利安特的資料中心簽約需求總量將達約4.4 GW，足以供應美國數百萬個一般家庭用電。1 GW大約相當於一座大型核電廠或燃煤電廠的持續輸出功率。

  ## 已簽約負載與近期里程碑

  艾利安特現有的3.4 GW合約分散在三大客戶之間：搜尋與雲端服務廠商、隸屬Alphabet的Google；社群媒體巨頭Meta平台；以及由私募股權公司黑石集團支持的批發型共置業者QTS資料中心。杜里安表示，新增的1 GW預計將於2029年至2031年間逐步啟用，相關資本支出則分佈於公司目前的規劃期間及其後。

  最具體的近期里程碑，是Meta計畫於2027年第一季在威斯康辛州啟動輸電服務，杜里安將此描述為該電力公司擴張大容量用電開發管線的早期步驟。

  ## 資本支出與獲利展望

  週四，艾利安特將其四年資本支出預測上調19%，預估2027年至2030年間的資本支出總額為159億美元。公司預計，系統尖峰用電需求到2031年將成長逾60%。

  杜里安表示，若資本支出進一步提高以支應用電成長及其他投資機會，獲利成長幅度有可能超越公司現行每年8%的目標。他說：「我們持續評估不同機會的資本上檔空間，包括我們資料中心潛在客戶所帶來的額外用電成長。」

  艾利安特將2026年每股獲利指引收窄至先前區間的上半段，並發布2027年每股獲利指引為3.63美元至3.73美元。根據金融數據供應商LSEG彙整的數據，該指引中間值與華爾街分析師預期相符。

  ## 產業背景

  根據美國聯邦政府主要能源統計機構——美國能源資訊署（EIA）的數據，美國電力消費於2025年創下歷史新高，預計將在2026年及此後持續攀升，主要驅動力來自人工智慧快速普及所帶動的資料中心擴張，以及交通運輸與工業領域更廣泛的電氣化趨勢。

  ## 意涵分析

  對於一家服務愛荷華州與威斯康辛州約100萬電力客戶及42.7萬天然氣客戶的公用事業而言，資料中心用電管線代表著負載成長結構性的轉變，正直接重塑其資本計畫。四年資本支出預測19%的增幅，反映的是已由簽約合約所支撐的支出承諾；杜里安也暗示，進一步的上檔空間，有賴於將目前1 GW的潛在客戶管線轉化為正式協議。

  Meta於2027年第一季在威斯康辛州啟動輸電服務，是此波建設擴張計畫中下一個預定的實際運作里程碑。
