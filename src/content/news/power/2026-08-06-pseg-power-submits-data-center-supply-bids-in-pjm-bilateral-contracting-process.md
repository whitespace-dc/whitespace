  ## PSEG Power enters PJM's bilateral data center matching process

  Public Service Enterprise Group's unregulated generation subsidiary has submitted multiple power supply proposals in New Jersey and other parts of the PJM Interconnection footprint through the grid operator's bilateral contracting process for large loads, PSEG chair, president and CEO Ralph LaRossa said Tuesday on a second-quarter earnings conference call.

  PJM, which operates the transmission grid serving 13 states and the District of Columbia, has structured its backstop reliability initiative in two parts: a bilateral matching process — already underway — that connects power suppliers with planned large customers such as data centers, and a one-time capacity auction scheduled to begin Sept. 30. Initial bilateral agreements could be announced as soon as August, though PJM said the broader process could extend into next spring.

  LaRossa declined to specify the volume or terms of PSEG Power's proposals, saying PJM's own planning remains unsettled. "As PJM is moving into this [reliability backstop auction] and there's more opportunities for long-term [power purchase agreement] type or utility-like agreements, we see an opportunity that might present itself," he said.

  ## Nuclear capacity as a selling point

  Beyond the bilateral process, PSEG Power is holding separate discussions with potential customers interested in its existing nuclear output, future nuclear capacity upgrades, and other generation resources, LaRossa said. PSEG Power held approximately 3,760 megawatts of nuclear generation capacity in New Jersey and Pennsylvania at the start of 2025 — enough to power roughly three million average U.S. homes — making nuclear a central element of its pitch to large power buyers seeking around-the-clock, low-carbon electricity.

  ## PJM's shifting load picture

  The bilateral process is unfolding against a load forecast that PJM is actively revising. The grid operator said Tuesday it is cutting its projected load for the Commonwealth Edison zone in northern Illinois — which covers the Chicago area — by 1.3 gigawatts (GW) in 2031 and 3.3 GW in 2034, reflecting a reduced data center development pipeline reported by the utility. PJM said those reductions could affect transmission planning in the zone. LaRossa cited the fluid forecast as a reason for his limited disclosure on PSEG Power's proposals.

  ## Earnings and financial guidance

  PSEG reported second-quarter net income of $334 million, down from $585 million in the same period a year earlier, a decline the company attributed mainly to changes in mark-to-market positions — accounting adjustments that reflect changes in the estimated fair value of energy contracts rather than cash operations. Weather-normalized electricity sales at Public Service Electric and Gas, PSEG's regulated New Jersey utility, rose 1% over the prior 12 months.

  PSEG maintained its five-year capital expenditure guidance at $24 billion to $28 billion through 2030, with more than 90% directed at regulated investments, primarily replacing aging infrastructure. The company said it sees potential upside to that figure through nuclear revenue opportunities, competitive transmission projects, and incremental grid investments to connect solar and battery storage. Excluding one-time items, PSEG projected operating earnings growth of 6% to 8% annually through 2030.

  Executive vice president and CFO Daniel Cregg said incremental system investments to connect new generation could provide upside to the current capital growth outlook through 2030.

  A separate regulatory risk: PSEG estimated it could lose approximately $40 million in ongoing income if its regulated utility, PSE&G, loses a 0.5 percentage-point return-on-equity adder it currently receives for being a PJM member.

  ## New Jersey utility model review

  On the regulated side, LaRossa said PSEG is encouraged by a report released last month by the New Jersey Bureau of Public Utilities that examines potential changes to the state's utility business model. The report identified what LaRossa described as promising regulatory frameworks, including multiyear rate plans, performance-based rates, and earnings-sharing mechanisms. The BPU is entering a second phase of its review focused on cost discipline, financing modernization, incentives, shared savings, and performance-based ratemaking, he said.

  ## What comes next

  The immediate near-term marker is PJM's bilateral contracting timeline: the grid operator has indicated initial deals could be announced in August, with the full process potentially running through spring 2026. Whether PSEG Power's proposals advance to signed agreements will depend on how PJM's load forecast and reliability needs continue to evolve.

  ## PSEG Power進入PJM雙邊資料中心媒合程序

  公共服務企業集團（Public Service Enterprise Group，PSEG）旗下非受管制發電子公司，已在紐澤西州及PJM互聯電網覆蓋範圍內其他地區，透過電網業者針對大型負載設立的雙邊議約程序，提交多份供電提案。PSEG董事長兼總裁暨執行長拉爾夫·拉羅薩（Ralph LaRossa）周二在第二季財報電話會議上如此宣布。

  PJM負責管轄跨越13個州及哥倫比亞特區的輸電網路，其後備可靠性方案分為兩大部分：其一為雙邊媒合程序——目前已在進行中——將供電業者與資料中心等計畫中的大型用電客戶串聯；其二為預定於9月30日啟動的單次容量拍賣。初步雙邊協議最快可望於8月宣布，但PJM表示整體程序可能延續至明年春天。

  拉羅薩拒絕透露PSEG Power提案的規模或條款，並以PJM本身的規劃仍存在變數為由。他表示：「隨著PJM推進這項可靠性後備拍賣，長期購電協議或類公用事業型協議的機會愈來愈多，我們認為當中存在有待把握的商機。」

  ## 核能容量作為競爭利器

  除雙邊程序外，PSEG Power也正與潛在客戶進行個別洽談，內容涵蓋現有核能發電輸出、未來核能容量升級及其他發電資源，拉羅薩說。截至2025年初，PSEG Power在紐澤西州與賓夕法尼亞州合計持有約3,760 MW的核能發電容量——足以供應約300萬戶美國普通家庭用電——使核能成為其向尋求全天候、低碳電力之大型買電方推銷的核心賣點。

  ## PJM負載預測持續調整

  雙邊程序的推進，正值PJM積極修訂其負載預測之際。電網業者周二表示，將削減伊利諾州北部聯邦愛迪生（Commonwealth Edison）供電區（涵蓋芝加哥地區）的預測負載，2031年削減1.3 GW、2034年削減3.3 GW，原因是該區公用事業回報的資料中心開發管線有所縮減。PJM指出，這些削減可能影響該區的輸電規劃。拉羅薩引用此一仍在變動中的預測，作為他未能詳細說明PSEG Power提案的理由。

  ## 財報與財務指引

  PSEG公布第二季淨利為3億3,400萬美元，較去年同期的5億8,500萬美元大幅下滑，公司將主因歸結於按市值計價（mark-to-market）部位的變動——此為反映能源合約估計公允價值變化的會計調整，與現金營運績效並無直接關聯。PSEG旗下受管制的紐澤西公用事業子公司「公共服務電氣與燃氣」（Public Service Electric and Gas，PSE&G）經氣候因素調整後的售電量，在過去12個月中年增1%。

  PSEG維持五年資本支出指引不變，2030年前累計投資規模介於240億至280億美元之間，其中逾90%用於受管制投資，主要用途為汰換老舊基礎設施。公司表示，核能營收機會、競爭性輸電專案，以及連接太陽能與電池儲能的增量電網投資，均可為上述數字帶來上行空間。剔除一次性項目後，PSEG預計2030年前每年營業利益成長6%至8%。

  執行副總裁兼財務長丹尼爾·克雷格（Daniel Cregg）表示，連接新發電機組所需的增量系統投資，可為2030年前的現有資本成長展望提供額外上行空間。

  另有一項獨立的法規風險：PSEG估計，若其受管制公用事業PSE&G因身為PJM成員所享有的0.5個百分點股東權益報酬率（ROE）加碼遭到取消，每年將損失約4,000萬美元的持續性收入。

  ## 紐澤西州公用事業模式審查

  在受管制業務方面，拉羅薩表示，PSEG對紐澤西州公共事業局（Bureau of Public Utilities，BPU）上月發布的一份報告感到樂觀，該報告探討州內公用事業商業模式的潛在變革。他指出，報告中點出了若干值得期待的監管框架，包括多年費率計畫、績效導向費率及盈餘共享機制。BPU目前正進入審查第二階段，聚焦於成本管控、融資現代化、激勵措施、共享節省效益及績效導向費率制定。

  ## 後續發展

  近期最具指標意義的時間節點，是PJM的雙邊議約時程：電網業者已表示，初步協議可望於8月宣布，整體程序則可能延續至2026年春天。PSEG Power的提案能否進一步簽署成協議，將取決於PJM負載預測與可靠性需求的後續演變。
