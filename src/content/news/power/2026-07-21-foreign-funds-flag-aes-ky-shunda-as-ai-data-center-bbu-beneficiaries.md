  # Foreign Funds Flag AES-KY, Shunda as AI Data Center BBU Beneficiaries

  US institutional investors have identified Taiwan-listed AES-KY (6781) and Shunda (3211) as key beneficiaries of accelerating demand for battery backup units (BBUs) — rechargeable battery systems that maintain power continuity in data centers — driven by the spread of high-voltage direct current (HVDC) power architectures in AI data center builds, according to a report cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times on July 17, 2026.

  BBUs are becoming standard equipment in AI data centers as HVDC power architectures gain adoption, the foreign institutional investors said. They expect high-power BBUs, HVDC infrastructure, and greater system integration to be the primary drivers of industry growth and manufacturer margins over the coming years.

  ## High-power BBUs carry better margins

  High-power BBU tiers — specifically 8 kW, 12 kW, and 25 kW units — are expected to sustain superior profitability, the investors said, because the technical barriers are higher, engineering complexity is greater, and the pool of qualified suppliers is limited.

  For HVDC-compatible BBUs specifically, customer qualification processes are expected to begin in the first half of 2027, with shipments commencing in the second half of that year. Meaningful revenue contributions from HVDC BBUs are not expected until 2028, which the investors characterized as the industry's next growth wave, one that also carries better gross margins.

  ## Panasonic leads, but CSP diversification opens Taiwan's path

  The investors said Japan's Panasonic is expected to retain its market leadership, citing the company's proprietary cell technology, broad product portfolio, and long-standing relationships with cloud service providers (CSPs) — companies such as hyperscale cloud operators that deploy data centers at scale.

  However, the investors said CSPs are placing increasing emphasis on supply chain diversification, which they described as creating an attractive opening for Taiwanese BBU module suppliers. Taiwan's manufacturers are not attempting to replicate Panasonic's vertically integrated model, the investors noted. Instead, their competitive edge lies in battery module integration, safety certification processes, and close collaboration with power supply unit (PSU) manufacturers.

  ## Second-supplier strategies drive share opportunity

  As more CSPs adopt formal "second supplier" procurement strategies — qualifying an additional vendor alongside an incumbent to reduce concentration risk — AES-KY and Shunda stand to capture additional market share in BBU battery modules, the US investors said.

  Despite the positive industry outlook, both AES-KY and Shunda shares fell during intraday trading on July 17, dragged lower by a broader sell-off in Taiwan equities, the Commercial Times reported.

  ## What comes next

  The timing of HVDC BBU customer certification — expected to begin in the first half of 2027 — will be a key milestone that determines how quickly Taiwanese suppliers can convert their second-supplier positioning into shipping revenue, with 2028 identified as the year when HVDC BBU sales are projected to make a material contribution to results.

  # 外資點名AES-KY、順達　AI資料中心BBU概念雙星

  據《工商時報》2026年7月17日引述外資報告，美國法人機構已將台股AES-KY（6781）與順達（3211）列為關鍵受惠標的，看好兩者在AI資料中心加速導入高壓直流電（HVDC）架構浪潮下，將同步受惠於不斷電電池模組（BBU）——即維持資料中心供電連續性的充電式電池系統——需求的快速攀升。

  外資法人指出，隨著HVDC供電架構在AI資料中心的普及，BBU已逐步成為標準配備。他們預期高功率BBU、HVDC基礎設施建設，以及更高程度的系統整合，將是未來數年推動產業成長與廠商毛利率提升的核心動能。

  ## 高功率BBU毛利率更具優勢

  外資表示，8 kW、12 kW及25 kW等高功率BBU等級產品，預計將維持較優異的獲利水準。原因在於此類產品技術門檻較高、工程複雜度較大，且合格供應商數量有限。

  就HVDC相容BBU而言，客戶認證流程預計將於2027年上半年展開，出貨時程則落在下半年。外資認為，HVDC BBU實質的營收貢獻預計要到2028年才會顯現，並將此定義為產業的下一波成長浪潮，且該波段產品同樣具備較佳的毛利率表現。

  ## 松下穩居龍頭，但CSP分散採購為台廠開路

  外資指出，日本松下（Panasonic）預計仍將維持市場領導地位，優勢來源包括其專有電芯技術、完整的產品組合，以及與雲端服務供應商（CSP）長期深厚的合作關係——CSP泛指以大規模方式部署資料中心的超大型雲端業者。

  然而，外資同時指出，CSP正愈來愈重視供應鏈的多元化布局，認為此一趨勢正為台灣BBU模組供應商創造極具吸引力的切入機會。外資強調，台灣廠商並非試圖複製松下垂直整合的商業模式，而是以電池模組整合能力、安全認證流程，以及與電源供應器（PSU）廠商的緊密協作作為差異化競爭優勢。

  ## 次要供應商策略驅動市占擴張機會

  外資表示，隨著更多CSP正式採行「第二供應商」採購策略——即在主力供應商之外再認證一家備選廠商以分散集中風險——AES-KY與順達有望在BBU電池模組市場進一步搶攻份額。

  儘管產業前景樂觀，《工商時報》報導指出，AES-KY與順達股價在7月17日盤中雙雙下跌，受台股整體賣壓拖累走低。

  ## 後續關鍵里程碑

  HVDC BBU客戶認證時程——預計於2027年上半年啟動——將是決定台灣供應商能否快速將「第二供應商」定位轉換為實際出貨營收的關鍵指標；2028年則被外資點名為HVDC BBU銷售對整體業績產生實質貢獻的時間節點。
