body_en: |
  Dominion Energy raised the total cost estimate for its 2.6-gigawatt Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind project to $11.7 billion, up $288 million from its most recent projection in April, and pushed the completion deadline to the end of 2027, the company said Friday in its second-quarter filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

  The project, commonly abbreviated as CVOW, is 81% complete, with more than 450 megawatts of turbine capacity already in service, Dominion said. Chief Executive Robert Blue told investors on Friday's earnings call that "every type of component is in service and functioning as expected."

  ## Cost drivers and timeline shift

  Three factors account for the $288 million increase, Dominion said: revised network upgrade charges assigned by PJM Interconnection, the regional grid operator; tariffs the Trump administration imposed in April; and updated turbine installation projections.

  The company's previous schedule called for all work to wrap up in early 2027. Dominion now expects to complete the project by the end of 2027.

  Blue projected the finished project would save customers approximately $5 billion in fuel costs over its first 10 years of operation. He said that in recent weeks, as demand peaks emerged, Dominion had done "everything possible at the request of system operators to deliver the maximum possible amount of power from CVOW."

  ## Second-quarter earnings decline

  Rising offshore wind costs contributed to a sharp drop in Dominion's second-quarter net income, which fell to $340 million from $760 million in the second quarter of 2025. Other contributors to the decline included charges related to nonregulated generation assets, higher fuel costs, and Dominion Virginia's June reentry into the PJM capacity market, the company said.

  ## NextEra merger and data center pipeline

  Dominion plans to begin seeking state and federal regulatory approvals for its proposed merger with NextEra Energy in the third quarter and expects to reach financial close by the end of 2027, the company said Friday.

  If the transaction closes, the combined company would be the largest regulated utility in the United States, with 10 million customers and a 130-gigawatt large-load interconnection pipeline, Dominion said. Customers would receive $2.25 billion in credits upon closing.

  Dominion's portion of that pipeline stood at 53.8 gigawatts in July 2026, up 11% from December 2025, reflecting demand from data centers and other large electricity users in "various stages of contracting," the company said.

  The merger faces an early regulatory test at the Virginia State Corporation Commission, which has scheduled hearings beginning November 17. Julien Dumoulin-Smith, a senior stock analyst at investment bank Jefferies, described the review schedule as unusually fast. "The accelerated six-month Virginia regulatory review will give investors a strong view on whether the transaction can be approved or not in the next few months," he said in a Friday note to investors. Dumoulin-Smith predicted the companies would try to reach a settlement before hearings begin.

  ## Nonregulated asset sales and Millstone nuclear

  Dominion agreed in May to sell a portfolio of nonregulated solar assets to Enel, the Italian energy conglomerate, for $140 million; the deal is expected to close by the end of 2026. The company also said it plans to sell its nonregulated renewable natural gas assets before their anticipated retirement dates but did not name a buyer or provide a timeline.

  The company's Millstone nuclear generating station in Connecticut, rated at 2,106 megawatts across two reactors, faces contract uncertainty beyond 2029, when power purchase agreements with utilities Eversource and United Illuminating expire. Dominion has warned that one of the reactors could retire by 2035 without a long-term contract in place.

  Blue said Friday that Connecticut is expected to decide soon whether to allow Millstone to participate in the state's zero-carbon energy procurement process, after which Dominion plans to begin contract negotiations for submission to state regulators. The company estimates its bid would save Connecticut ratepayers $900 million over 10 years.

  ## Outlook

  Dumoulin-Smith said last week that the latest CVOW cost increase and delay were "discouraging" but "unfortunately not a major surprise" for Dominion investors, reflecting the project's history of successive estimate revisions.

  The next major scheduled milestone for Dominion is the Virginia State Corporation Commission hearing on the NextEra merger, set to begin November 17.
body_zh: |
  Dominion能源公司週五在向美國證券交易委員會（SEC）提交的第二季財報中宣布，旗下2.6吉瓦的維吉尼亞近海離岸風電計畫（Coastal Virginia Offshore Wind，簡稱CVOW）總預算上調至117億美元，較今年4月最近一次預估增加2.88億美元，並將完工期限延後至2027年底。

  目前CVOW整體完工進度已達81%，逾450百萬瓦的渦輪機容量已投入運轉。執行長羅伯特．布魯（Robert Blue）在週五的法人說明會上向投資人表示，「每一類型的零組件均已上線，運作符合預期。」

  ## 成本增加因素與時程調整

  Dominion表示，此次增加的2.88億美元由三項因素所致：PJM Interconnection（區域電網調度機構）所核定的電網升級費用調整、川普政府於4月實施的關稅措施，以及渦輪機安裝進度的預測更新。

  公司先前的時程規劃為2027年初完成全部工程，現調整為2027年底完工。

  布魯預估，計畫竣工後，在前10年運轉期間將為用戶節省約50億美元的燃料成本。他表示，近幾週在用電需求出現高峰之際，Dominion已「應電網調度人員要求，竭盡所能從CVOW輸出最大可能的電力。」

  ## 第二季獲利下滑

  離岸風電成本持續攀升是Dominion第二季淨利大幅下滑的因素之一，本季淨利為3.4億美元，遠低於2025年同期的7.6億美元。公司表示，其他拖累獲利的因素還包括非受管制發電資產的相關費用、燃料成本上升，以及Dominion維吉尼亞（Dominion Virginia）於今年6月重新加入PJM電力容量市場。

  ## NextEra合併案與資料中心管線

  Dominion表示，計畫於第三季開始向州政府及聯邦主管機關申請其與NextEra Energy合併案的監管核准，預計於2027年底完成財務交割。

  若此交易完成，合併後的公司將成為美國最大的受管制公用事業，擁有1,000萬名用戶及130吉瓦的大型用電用戶電網互聯管線，Dominion表示。交割完成後，用戶將獲得合計22.5億美元的費用折抵。

  Dominion自身在該管線的持份，於2026年7月達到53.8吉瓦，較2025年12月成長11%，反映出資料中心及其他大型用電戶處於「不同合約階段」的強勁需求。

  合併案的首項監管審查將在維吉尼亞州公司委員會（Virginia State Corporation Commission）進行，該委員會已排定於11月17日開始舉行聽證會。投資銀行傑富瑞（Jefferies）資深股票分析師朱利安．杜穆蘭-史密斯（Julien Dumoulin-Smith）形容此審查時程異常快速。他在週五給投資人的報告中指出：「加速進行的六個月維吉尼亞監管審查，將讓投資人在未來幾個月內對這項交易能否獲批形成明確判斷。」杜穆蘭-史密斯預測，兩家公司將設法在聽證會開始前達成和解協議。

  ## 非受管制資產出售與米爾斯通核電廠

  Dominion已於今年5月同意，以1.4億美元將旗下非受管制太陽能資產組合出售給義大利能源集團Enel，預計於2026年底前完成交割。公司亦表示計畫在預期除役日期前出售其非受管制再生天然氣資產，但未透露買方或時間表。

  位於美國康乃狄克州、裝置容量達2,106百萬瓦、擁有兩座反應爐的米爾斯通核能發電廠（Millstone），面臨合約到期後的不確定性。Eversource及United Illuminating兩家公用事業公司與米爾斯通簽訂的電力購售協議將於2029年到期，Dominion已警告，若無長期合約，其中一座反應爐可能於2035年前除役。

  布魯週五表示，康乃狄克州預計將在近期決定是否允許米爾斯通參與該州的零碳能源採購流程，此後Dominion計畫展開合約談判，並提交州政府主管機關審核。公司估計，此競標方案在10年期間可為康乃狄克州用電戶節省9億美元。

  ## 展望

  杜穆蘭-史密斯上週表示，CVOW最新的成本增加與延期消息「令人失望」，但對Dominion投資人而言「不幸地並不令人意外」，反映出該計畫屢次修正預算的歷程。

  Dominion的下一個重要里程碑，是定於11月17日開始的維吉尼亞州公司委員會NextEra合併案聽證會。
