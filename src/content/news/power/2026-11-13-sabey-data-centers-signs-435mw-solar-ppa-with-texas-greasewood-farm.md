body_en: |
  Sabey Data Centers has signed a ten-year virtual power purchase agreement (vPPA) for 43.5MW of solar capacity from the Greasewood solar farm in Girvin, Pecos County, Texas, the company announced November 12, 2026.

  A vPPA is a financial contract in which a corporate buyer agrees to pay a fixed price for electricity from a renewable generator, with the energy delivered to the grid rather than directly to the buyer's facilities. The arrangement allows companies to support new or existing renewable generation without requiring a physical transmission connection.

  The Greasewood facility, owned by Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP), has been operational since 2021 and is a solar photovoltaic farm — one that converts sunlight directly into electricity using semiconductor panels. Under the agreement, the 43.5MW capacity is estimated to produce approximately 130 gigawatt-hours (GWh) per year, or roughly 1.3 terawatt-hours (TWh) across the full ten-year term. For context, 130GWh is enough to power tens of thousands of average US homes annually.

  ## Net-zero timeline

  Sabey has set a target of net-zero Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by 2029. Scope 1 covers direct emissions from sources a company owns or controls; Scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity. Casey Mason, Sabey's director of energy and sustainability, said the agreement reflects a longer-term approach to that goal. "Our 2029 net-zero goal requires us to think beyond simply buying renewable energy year to year," Mason said. "This agreement is a long-term commitment that supports renewable generation on the grid and moves us meaningfully closer to where we want to be."

  ## Sabey's footprint

  Sabey Data Centers operates facilities across multiple US markets, with sites in operation or development in Quincy, Seattle, and East Wenatchee, Washington; New York City; Austin, Texas; Umatilla, Oregon; Indianapolis, Indiana; and Ashburn, Virginia.

  The deal adds a long-duration renewable energy contract to Sabey's energy portfolio at a time when data center operators are under increasing pressure to demonstrate credible paths to decarbonization. By locking in a ten-year offtake from an already-operational project, Sabey avoids the development risk associated with new-build renewable agreements while securing a fixed price over the contract term.

  Sabey's 2029 net-zero deadline remains the most immediate milestone the company has publicly stated.
body_zh: |
  Sabey資料中心於2026年11月12日宣布，已與位於德州皮克斯郡吉爾文鎮（Girvin, Pecos County）的Greasewood太陽能農場簽訂十年期虛擬購電協議（vPPA），取得43.5MW的太陽能發電容量。

  虛擬購電協議（vPPA）是一種金融契約，企業買方同意以固定價格購買再生能源發電商的電力，所產生的電力直接輸送至電網，而非直接輸往買方設施。這種安排讓企業得以支持新建或既有的再生能源發電，同時無須建置實體輸電連線。

  Greasewood太陽能設施由哥本哈根基礎建設夥伴（Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners，簡稱CIP）所有，自2021年起正式運轉，採用太陽能光伏技術——利用半導體面板將陽光直接轉換為電力。根據協議內容，43.5MW的裝置容量預計每年約可發電130吉瓦時（GWh），十年合約期內合計約達1.3兆瓦時（TWh）。作為參考，130GWh的年發電量足以供應數萬戶美國一般家庭使用。

  ## 淨零排放時程

  Sabey已訂立目標，計畫於2029年達成範疇一與範疇二的淨零排放。範疇一涵蓋企業自有或直接控制的排放源所產生的直接排放；範疇二則涵蓋因購買電力而產生的間接排放。Sabey能源與永續發展總監凱西·梅森（Casey Mason）表示，此次協議體現了公司對上述目標的長期思維。梅森表示：「我們2029年的淨零目標，要求我們必須超越逐年購買再生能源的做法，著眼更長遠。這份協議是長期承諾，能夠支持電網上的再生能源發電，讓我們更實質地朝目標邁進。」

  ## Sabey的業務版圖

  Sabey資料中心在美國多個市場營運設施，目前在華盛頓州昆西、西雅圖及東溫納奇、紐約市、德州奧斯汀、奧勒岡州尤馬提拉、印第安納州印第安納波利斯，以及維吉尼亞州阿什本等地均有設施在運營或開發中。

  此次協議為Sabey的能源組合新增一份長期再生能源合約，時機恰逢資料中心業者面臨日益沉重的壓力，需要展示具有公信力的去碳化路徑。透過鎖定一座已投入運轉的既有案場簽署十年購電合約，Sabey得以規避新建再生能源案場所伴隨的開發風險，同時在合約期間內確保固定電價。

  Sabey公開揭示的最近期里程碑，仍是2029年淨零排放的截止期限。
