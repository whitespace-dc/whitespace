body_en: |
  Amazon Web Services is storing Nvidia's latest-generation GB300 server racks in transit warehouses near planned data center sites because target facilities lack sufficient electrical power to bring the hardware online, according to internal Amazon documents and employee meeting recordings obtained by Business Insider, as reported by Taiwan's Commercial Times on October 22.

  ## Hardware backlog and delivery scale

  AWS internal emails show the company expected to receive approximately $5 billion worth of GB300 racks in August 2026 alone, followed by approximately $3 billion per month on a continuing basis. A portion of that equipment cannot be installed and activated because power at the intended data center locations is not yet available.

  An Amazon spokesperson told the publication that staging hardware near deployment sites while waiting for power is the company's standard practice and constitutes best business practice.

  ## Early ownership, delayed revenue

  To secure scarce Nvidia GPU hardware, Amazon changed its equipment intake process with a program it calls "Early Rack Receipt." Under the arrangement, Amazon takes ownership of racks before they arrive at the data center, rather than waiting until installation is complete. Previously, the company paid only after equipment was physically installed, leaving suppliers to carry financing costs during transit.

  By taking ownership earlier, Amazon now bears those capital costs at the point of receipt. If power or installation is delayed, equipment sits in warehouses tying up capital without generating revenue from computing services.

  Amazon reported in its July second-quarter earnings that its rolling free cash flow had fallen to negative $7.6 billion, the first time it had turned negative in several years. Continued investment in AI infrastructure, combined with equipment that cannot yet be powered, means those capital outlays are not generating offsetting revenue.

  At least one AWS supply chain team has not established a clear forecasting path for routing racks from storage into data centers, according to the documents cited in the report.

  ## Power is the binding constraint

  The practical shortage in AI compute is not physical rack space or hardware availability but powered, operational capacity. Racks stored in warehouses without power cannot provide computing services regardless of their delivery status.

  Goldman Sachs data cited in the report shows that the spot rental index for Nvidia B200 GPUs, tracked among non-hyperscale cloud providers, stood at approximately $6.01 per GPU-hour as of the time of reporting. A GPU-hour is the unit measuring one GPU operating for one hour. That figure is approximately 40 percent above the January 2026 low of $4.29 per GPU-hour, indicating sustained demand for AI computing capacity even as supply-side deployment has lagged.

  ## Industry implications

  With demand for AI compute remaining strong and power commissioning timelines lagging hardware deliveries, compute costs are likely to be transferred to the AI companies, startups, and enterprise customers renting GPU capacity, the report concludes. AWS's situation reflects a constraint facing the broader industry: securing hardware has become easier relative to securing the electrical capacity to operate it.

  Goldman Sachs's B200 spot rental data, showing a 40 percent rise from January 2026 levels, points to continued upward pressure on GPU compute prices as long as power availability trails hardware deployment schedules.
body_zh: |
  亞馬遜雲端服務（AWS）正將輝達最新一代GB300伺服器機架暫存於鄰近預定資料中心選址的過渡倉庫，原因是目標設施尚不具備足夠的電力供應，無法讓這批硬體正式上線。這是《Business Insider》根據取得的亞馬遜內部文件及員工會議錄音所披露的消息，並經台灣《工商時報》於10月22日轉載報導。

  ## 硬體積壓與交貨規模

  AWS內部電子郵件顯示，該公司預計光是2026年8月一個月，就將收到價值約50億美元的GB300機架，此後每個月更將持續接收約30億美元的設備。然而，其中部分設備因預定資料中心所在地的電力尚未到位，目前既無法安裝，也無法啟用。

  對此，亞馬遜發言人向該媒體表示，在等待電力就緒期間，將硬體暫存於部署地點附近，是公司的標準作業程序，也是最佳商業實踐。

  ## 提前持有所有權、延後產生營收

  為確保能取得稀缺的輝達GPU硬體，亞馬遜調整了設備收受流程，推出一項名為「提前收架」（Early Rack Receipt）的計畫。根據這項安排，亞馬遜在機架抵達資料中心之前就取得所有權，而不再等到安裝完成後才辦理移交。過去，公司只有在設備實際完成實體安裝後才付款，讓供應商在運送期間承擔融資成本。

  由於提前取得所有權，亞馬遜現在必須在收貨當下承擔這筆資本成本。一旦電力或安裝進度延誤，設備便只能閒置於倉庫，不僅佔用資本，更無法透過提供運算服務來產生任何營收。

  亞馬遜在7月第二季財報中揭露，其滾動自由現金流已跌至負76億美元，這是近幾年來首度出現負值。在持續投入AI基礎設施建設、設備卻又尚未通電的雙重壓力下，這些資本支出未能帶來相應的營收抵消。

  根據報告引用的文件，至少有一支AWS供應鏈團隊，迄今尚未建立明確的預測路徑，說明如何將暫存倉庫中的機架有序調配至資料中心。

  ## 電力是關鍵制約

  AI運算的實質瓶頸，並非實體機架空間或硬體供應不足，而在於能通電、可實際運作的產能。無論交貨狀態如何，存放在倉庫、尚未通電的機架，都無法提供任何運算服務。

  報告引用高盛（Goldman Sachs）的資料顯示，截至報導時點，在非超大規模雲端供應商之間追蹤的輝達B200 GPU現貨租用指數，約為每GPU小時6.01美元。所謂GPU小時，是指一顆GPU運作一小時的計量單位。這一數字較2026年1月的低點每GPU小時4.29美元上漲約40%，顯示即便供給端的部署進度持續落後，市場對AI運算容量的需求依然強勁。

  ## 產業影響

  在AI運算需求持續強勁、電力開通時程又落後於硬體交貨進度的情況下，報告研判運算成本極可能轉嫁給租用GPU算力的AI公司、新創企業及企業客戶。AWS的處境，正反映出整個產業共同面臨的制約：相較於取得硬體，確保足夠的電力容量來驅動這些設備，已成為更為艱難的課題。

  高盛的B200現貨租用資料顯示，自2026年1月以來漲幅已達40%。只要電力供應持續落後於硬體部署時程，GPU運算價格便將面臨持續的上行壓力。
