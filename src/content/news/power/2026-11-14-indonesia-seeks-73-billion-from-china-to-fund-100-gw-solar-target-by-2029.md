  ## Indonesia Courts Chinese Capital for 100 GW Solar Push

  Indonesia is soliciting investment from Chinese companies to build 100 gigawatts (GW) of solar power capacity — enough to power hundreds of millions of homes — under a program set by President Prabowo Subianto with a 2029 completion target, according to Kontan.

  The estimated cost of the program is $73 billion, equivalent to approximately 1,326 trillion Indonesian rupiah (Rp), making it one of the largest proposed renewable energy buildouts in Southeast Asia.

  ## Scale of the Financing Challenge

  Beyond the headline figure, Indonesia faces three overlapping obstacles, according to the report: securing the full funding requirement, building out sufficient domestic solar panel manufacturing capacity, and establishing clear and stable regulatory conditions for investors.

  The invitation to Chinese firms reflects the scale of capital needed. China is home to the world's dominant solar panel manufacturers and has been a major source of infrastructure financing across Southeast Asia.

  ## What 100 GW Represents

  One gigawatt is roughly equivalent to the output of a large conventional power plant and can supply electricity to hundreds of thousands of households depending on consumption patterns. A 100 GW solar fleet would represent a transformational expansion of Indonesia's generation mix, which currently relies heavily on coal.

  ## Significance

  If realized, the program would position Indonesia as one of the largest solar markets globally. The involvement of Danantara — Indonesia's state investment vehicle — was referenced in connection with the initiative, suggesting a role for state financing alongside foreign capital.

  The 2029 deadline, combined with the scale of funding required and the acknowledged gaps in industrial capacity and regulation, means that securing Chinese investment commitments in the near term is a prerequisite for the program to proceed on schedule.
body_zh: |
  ## 印尼爭取中國資本，全力推動百GW太陽能建設

  根據《Kontan》報導，印尼正向中國企業招攬投資，計畫在2029年完成目標期限前，建置100GW（百吉瓦）的太陽能發電容量——足以供應數億個家庭用電——此計畫由印尼總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托親自設定。

  這項計畫的預估經費高達730億美元，約合新台幣逾2.4兆元，折合印尼盾約1,326兆盾，是東南亞地區規模最大的再生能源建設提案之一。

  ## 融資挑戰的規模有多大

  據報導，除了龐大的總金額之外，印尼還面臨三項相互交疊的難題：籌措全額資金、建立充足的國內太陽能板製造產能，以及為投資者建立清晰且穩定的法規環境。

  此次向中國企業發出邀請，正反映出所需資本規模之巨。中國擁有全球最具主導地位的太陽能板製造商，長期以來也是東南亞基礎建設融資的重要來源。

  ## 百GW究竟代表什麼

  1GW的發電量大致相當於一座大型傳統電廠的輸出，依用電型態不同，可供應數十萬個家庭的電力需求。若印尼建成100GW的太陽能機組，將對該國目前嚴重依賴燃煤的發電結構帶來根本性的轉變。

  ## 計畫的戰略意義

  一旦這項計畫付諸實現，印尼將躋身全球最大太陽能市場之列。報導中提及印尼國家投資機構Danantara與此計畫的關聯，顯示在引進外資之外，國家資本也將扮演一定角色。

  2029年的完成期限，加上所需的龐大資金規模，以及官方坦承的產業產能與法規缺口，意味著在近期內取得中國投資承諾，是這項計畫得以如期推進的先決條件。
