body_en: |
  Taiwan's four major heavy electrical equipment makers are shifting capacity away from solar and offshore wind turnkey projects toward AI data center (AIDC) and U.S. power infrastructure contracts that carry higher gross margins, as order books fill and transformer delivery lead times reach at least two to two and a half years, Commercial Times reported Friday.

  The four companies — Shihlin Electric, Chung-Hsin Electric, Ya-Li Electric, and Hua Cheng Electric, collectively known in Taiwan as the four heavy electrical giants — all carry full orders and full production capacity. Sustained procurement under Taiwan Power Company's (Taipower) grid resilience program has been a common base driver for all four.

  ## Hua Cheng exits offshore wind, books AIDC orders through 2030

  Hua Cheng said it will accept no new offshore wind turnkey contracts this year, citing lower gross margins on that work compared with AIDC projects and exports of power-grade transformers to U.S. utilities. The decision comes after years of deep involvement in Taiwan's offshore wind buildout: Hua Cheng completed the onshore substation for Taiwan's first offshore wind demonstration project — two 8-megawatt (MW) turbines — in 2017, won the 161-kilovolt (KV) extra-high-voltage substation and onshore power system turnkey for the 120MW second phase in 2018, and in 2019 secured the same scope for a wind farm with total installed capacity of 376MW, recognizing revenues in 2020 and 2021.

  Hua Cheng said cumulative AIDC orders have exceeded NT$20 billion and that AIDC is projected to account for more than 15 percent of revenue this year — the first time that segment would cross that threshold. The company named four growth drivers for the year: the Taipower grid resilience plan, infrastructure exports, semiconductor fab expansion, and AIDC. Order visibility extends to 2029, and the company said it has already begun accepting orders for 2029 and 2030 delivery.

  ## Chung-Hsin pursues AIDC turnkey after decade in renewables

  Chung-Hsin Electric completed a 216MW ground-mounted solar farm in 2021 on 200 hectares of Taiwan Sugar Corporation land in Qigu, Tainan — described as long salt-degraded and unfit for agriculture — at a cost of NT$10 billion. A planned follow-on solar project in the Hualien-Taitung region was abandoned following local opposition.

  In 2024, Chung-Hsin received a turnkey contract for an offshore wind booster substation under the government's Round 3-1 offshore wind program, valued at approximately NT$5 billion, with construction scheduled to begin at end of 2025. The company said it is now actively building AIDC capabilities — including supply of air-conditioning systems and power generators for AI computing facilities — and plans to pursue AIDC engineering turnkey contracts this year.

  ## Shihlin targets North American AIDC market over next decade

  Shihlin Electric named five growth drivers for this year: the Taipower grid resilience plan, AIDC, semiconductor supply-chain and fab expansion, North America and other export markets, and transportation and public works. The company said it is particularly bullish on the North American market over the next five to ten years, citing rapid AIDC expansion in the United States.

  Shihlin previously completed a turnkey project for a 180KW solar power plant and booster substation for display maker AUO in Yunlin county, valued at NT$4 billion to NT$5 billion. The company said solar construction turnkey volumes are declining. On energy storage, Shihlin is partnering with Honde Energy on front-of-meter projects and holds approximately an 8 percent equity stake in Apollo for behind-the-meter storage.

  ## Ya-Li wins Nvidia South America AIDC equipment order

  Ya-Li Electric previously took a solar farm turnkey contract for Taiwan Cement's green energy subsidiary valued at over NT$900 million, but said that due to unspecified local circumstances only about 70 percent of associated revenue has been recognized to date. Ya-Li said it is actively expanding its AIDC business and this year received an order for transformers and other heavy electrical equipment from Nvidia's South American subsidiary for an AIDC project.

  ## Industry implications

  The simultaneous exit from new offshore wind turnkey work by Hua Cheng, and the declining solar pipelines reported by Shihlin and Ya-Li, reflect a structural reallocation of Taiwan's heavy electrical manufacturing capacity toward AI-driven infrastructure. With transformer delivery lead times at two to two and a half years, AIDC orders placed today will not be fulfilled until 2028 at the earliest.

  Hua Cheng said it has already begun booking orders for delivery in 2029 and 2030, representing the furthest-forward order visibility disclosed by any of the four companies.
body_zh: |
  台灣四大重電廠商——士林電機、中興電工、亞力電機與華城電機，在訂單全滿、產能全開、變壓器交貨期延伸至兩年至兩年半的情況下，正將產能從太陽能與離岸風電統包業務，轉向毛利率更高的AI資料中心（AIDC）及美國電力基礎建設訂單。《工商時報》週五報導指出，上述四家業者——業界合稱「四大重電廠商」——均呈現接單滿載、產能全開的局面，台灣電力公司（台電）電網韌性強化計畫的持續採購，則是四家共同的基本盤支撐。

  ## 華城退出離岸風電，AIDC訂單能見度延伸至2030年

  華城電機宣布，今年起不再承接新的離岸風電統包合約，理由是相關業務的毛利率不及AIDC專案，也低於外銷電力級變壓器至美國電力公司的獲利水準。這項決策，是在深耕台灣離岸風電建設多年之後所做出的：華城於2017年完成台灣首座離岸風電示範計畫的陸上變電站工程，該計畫設有兩部8百萬瓦（MW）風機；2018年承接120MW第二期計畫的161千伏（KV）超高壓變電站及陸上電力系統統包合約；2019年再以相同工程範疇取得總裝置容量376MW風場的合約，相關營收分別於2020年及2021年認列。

  華城指出，累計AIDC訂單已逾新台幣200億元，預計今年AIDC業務占整體營收比重將首度突破15%。公司點名今年四大成長驅動力：台電電網韌性計畫、基礎建設外銷、半導體廠擴建及AIDC。目前訂單能見度已延伸至2029年，並已開始受理2029年及2030年交貨的訂單。

  ## 中興深耕再生能源十年後，轉進AIDC統包市場

  中興電工曾於2021年，在台南市七股區台糖公司200公頃鹽化土地上（因長期鹽化而不適農耕），完成裝置容量216MW的地面型太陽能電廠，投資金額約新台幣100億元。原計畫在花東地區推進的後續太陽能案場，則因遭遇在地反對而宣告放棄。

  2024年，中興電工承接政府第3-1期離岸風電計畫的離岸升壓站統包合約，合約金額約新台幣50億元，預計於2025年底開工。中興表示，目前正積極建立AIDC相關能力，涵蓋為AI運算設施提供空調系統及發電機組，並計畫於今年爭取AIDC機電統包合約。

  ## 士林電機鎖定北美AIDC市場，看好未來十年商機

  士林電機點名今年五大成長驅動力：台電電網韌性計畫、AIDC、半導體供應鏈及廠房擴建、北美及其他出口市場，以及交通運輸與公共工程。公司對北美市場未來五至十年的發展尤其樂觀，主因是看好美國AIDC快速擴張的趨勢。

  士林電機先前完成面板廠友達光電（AUO）位於雲林縣的180KW太陽能電廠及升壓站統包工程，合約金額約新台幣40億至50億元；公司表示太陽能建設統包業務量正在下滑。在儲能方面，士林與弘德能源合作投入表前型（front-of-meter）儲能案，並持有表後型（behind-the-meter）儲能業者Apollo約8%的股權。

  ## 亞力電機獲輝達南美AI資料中心設備訂單

  亞力電機先前承接台泥旗下綠能子公司的太陽能電廠統包合約，金額逾新台幣9億元，但因當地未經具體說明的情況，相關營收至今僅認列約七成。亞力表示，正積極拓展AIDC業務，並於今年取得輝達（NVIDIA）南美子公司針對AIDC專案所下達的變壓器及其他重電設備訂單。

  ## 產業意涵

  華城退出新增離岸風電統包業務，士林與亞力亦反映太陽能接案管線萎縮，種種跡象顯示台灣重電製造產能正進行結構性重分配，資源大幅向AI驅動的基礎建設集中。在變壓器交貨期長達兩年至兩年半的情況下，現今下達的AIDC訂單，最快也要到2028年才能交貨。

  華城表示已開始受理2029年及2030年交貨的訂單，是四家業者中訂單能見度揭露最為超前的一家。
