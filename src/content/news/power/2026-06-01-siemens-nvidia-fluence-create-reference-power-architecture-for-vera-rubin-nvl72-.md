# Siemens, Nvidia, Fluence Create Reference Power Architecture for Vera Rubin NVL72 Data Centers

Siemens, Nvidia, and Fluence have jointly developed a reference electrical and power architecture for data centers running the Vera Rubin NVL72 platform. The design covers the complete electrical path of the data center infrastructure. This collaboration provides a standardized approach to power distribution and electrical systems for high-performance computing environments.

## 中文

# 西門子、輝達、Fluence 聯手開發 Vera Rubin 資料中心電力架構

西門子、輝達及 Fluence 聯合開發了 Vera Rubin NVL72 平臺資料中心的標準化電力與電氣架構。該設計涵蓋資料中心基礎設施的完整電氣路徑。此合作旨在為高效能運算環境提供標準化的電力配送及電氣系統方案。
