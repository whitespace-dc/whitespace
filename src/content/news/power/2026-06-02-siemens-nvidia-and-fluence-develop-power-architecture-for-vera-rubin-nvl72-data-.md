# Siemens, Nvidia, and Fluence Develop Power Architecture for Vera Rubin NVL72 Data Centers

Siemens, Nvidia, and Fluence have jointly developed a reference electrical and power architecture for data centers running the Vera Rubin NVL72 platform. The architecture design covers the complete electrical path throughout the data center infrastructure. This standardized reference architecture aims to optimize power delivery and management in high-performance computing environments.

## 中文

# 西門子、輝達、Fluence共同開發Vera Rubin NVL72資料中心電力架構

西門子、輝達與Fluence已聯合開發供Vera Rubin NVL72平臺資料中心使用的電力與供電架構參考設計。該設計涵蓋資料中心基礎設施的完整電力傳輸路徑。此標準化參考架構旨在最佳化高效能運算環境中的電力配送與管理。
