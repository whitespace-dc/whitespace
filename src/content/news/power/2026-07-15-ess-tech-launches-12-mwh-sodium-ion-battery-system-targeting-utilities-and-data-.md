body_en: |
  ESS Tech, an Oregon-based energy storage company, launched a modular 1.2-megawatt-hour (MWh) sodium-ion battery system on July 8, targeting utility, commercial, industrial, and data center customers seeking alternatives to the lithium-ion chemistries that dominate the electrochemical storage market.

  The product, called ESS Bridge, is designed as a stackable "building block" that can be combined to deliver up to 4.8 MWh of energy within a standard 20-foot shipping container — roughly the same capacity as the latest iteration of Tesla's Megapack, one of the most widely deployed grid-scale battery systems available. ESS Tech says the system can be configured to provide 16 hours or more of continuous discharge.

  ## Early demand and the April pivot

  The Bridge launch extends a strategic shift ESS Tech announced in late April, when the company said it would procure 8.5 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of sodium-ion battery cells and modules from Alsym Energy — a move ESS Tech described at the time as its entry into the short- and medium-duration energy storage market. Since that announcement, ESS Tech says it has attracted more than $1 billion in "early-stage customer opportunities."

  CEO Drew Buckley said in a statement that ESS Bridge would "meet the demand we're already seeing" from data centers and utilities while offering a "superior" alternative to lithium-ion batteries. "AI workloads are reshaping what data centers need from energy storage, and sodium-ion handles those power needs more effectively than conventional technologies," Buckley said.

  ## A departure from ESS Tech's founding product

  ESS Tech spent its first 15 years commercializing iron flow batteries — a long-duration storage technology the company says can deliver eight to 22 hours of energy capacity over tens of thousands of discharge cycles. The company went public through a reverse merger in 2021 but has struggled to scale that market as competitors rapidly expanded deployments of conventional lithium-based systems. Its stock has fallen from nearly $300 per share shortly after listing to less than $1 per share today.

  ## Sodium-ion's claimed advantages over lithium-ion

  Sodium-ion battery proponents, including ESS Tech, point to several properties they say give the chemistry an edge.

  Sodium-based batteries are less prone to thermal runaway, the chain reaction triggered by system faults or unsafe operating conditions that can cause catastrophic fires. A fire in early 2025 destroyed most of a 300-megawatt (MW) Vistra energy storage facility near Santa Cruz, California. The International Energy Agency has said sodium-ion batteries can operate at virtually any temperature likely to occur on Earth's surface.

  That thermal stability also allows sodium-ion systems to use air cooling rather than the active, liquid-based cooling typically required by lithium-ion batteries. ESS Tech says the simpler cooling approach reduces energy consumption, maintenance demands, and total cost of ownership.

  On supply chain grounds, ESS Tech argues that sodium's abundance in North America reduces customer and manufacturer exposure to the geopolitical risk embedded in lithium-ion supply chains, which are dominated by Chinese companies. The company says this positions sodium-ion favorably against U.S. government restrictions on purchasing from "foreign entities of concern."

  ## Trade-offs

  Sodium-ion technology carries meaningful drawbacks. Elemental sodium is more than three times heavier than lithium, making sodium-based batteries less energy-dense overall — meaning they require a larger physical footprint to deliver the same storage capacity as a lithium-based system. Sodium-ion production volumes and installed capacity also lag far behind lithium-ion globally.

  ## Broader sector momentum

  ESS Tech's announcement follows a run of sodium-ion activity from other companies. Colorado-based Peak Energy said late last year it would deliver up to 4.75 GWh of sodium-based batteries by 2030 to independent power producer Jupiter Power. In June, Peak announced a manufacturing and design partnership supporting General Motors' stationary storage ambitions and said it would build a 4-GWh manufacturing plant in Sacramento, California.

  Taken together, the announcements reflect growing interest from utilities and large electricity consumers in sodium-ion technology as power and transmission constraints tighten across U.S. grids. ESS Tech's Alsym Energy cell procurement — 8.5 GWh announced in late April — provides the supply base that underpins the Bridge product line.
body_zh: |
  ESS Tech 是一家位於美國奧勒岡州的儲能公司，於 7 月 8 日發表模組化 1.2 百萬瓦時（MWh）鈉離子電池系統，鎖定公用事業、商業、工業及資料中心客戶，為其提供有別於目前主導電化學儲能市場之鋰離子電池的另一選擇。

  這款產品名為 ESS Bridge，設計為可堆疊的「積木」，能夠在一個標準 20 呎貨櫃內組合，提供最高 4.8 MWh 的儲能容量——約與 Tesla Megapack 最新一代相當，而 Megapack 是目前全球部署最廣泛的電網級電池系統之一。ESS Tech 表示，這套系統可設定為持續放電 16 小時以上。

  ## 初期需求與四月策略轉型

  Bridge 的上市，延續了 ESS Tech 在四月下旬宣布的策略轉型。當時該公司表示將向 Alsym Energy 採購 8.5 十億瓦時（GWh）的鈉離子電池芯與模組，並將此舉定位為進軍短中期儲能市場的起點。自那次宣布以來，ESS Tech 表示已累積超過 10 億美元的「初期客戶商機」。

  執行長 Drew Buckley 在聲明中表示，ESS Bridge 將「滿足我們已從資料中心和公用事業客戶身上看到的需求」，同時提供比鋰離子電池「更優越」的替代方案。「AI 工作負載正在重塑資料中心對儲能的需求，鈉離子電池處理這些電力需求的能力，比傳統技術更有效，」Buckley 說。

  ## 有別於 ESS Tech 的創始產品

  ESS Tech 在成立初期的 15 年間，一直致力於將鐵液流電池商業化——這是一種長期儲能技術，公司表示可在數萬次放電循環中提供 8 至 22 小時的電力容量。該公司於 2021 年透過反向合併方式公開上市，然而隨著競爭對手迅速擴大傳統鋰基系統的部署規模，ESS Tech 在拓展這個市場時始終面臨困難。其股價已從上市後不久的近 300 美元高點，跌至今日不足 1 美元。

  ## 鈉離子電池宣稱勝過鋰離子的優勢

  包括 ESS Tech 在內的鈉離子電池支持者，列舉了他們認為這種電化學特性具有優勢的數項特點。

  鈉基電池較不容易發生熱失控——這是一種由系統故障或不安全操作條件所引發的連鎖反應，可能導致災難性火災。2025 年初，加州聖塔克魯茲附近一座 Vistra 旗下 300 百萬瓦（MW）的儲能設施，即因一場大火而大部分燒毀。國際能源總署（IEA）也曾指出，鈉離子電池幾乎可在地球表面任何可能出現的溫度範圍內正常運作。

  這種熱穩定性同樣讓鈉離子系統得以採用氣冷方式，而非鋰離子電池通常需要的主動液冷。ESS Tech 表示，更簡便的冷卻方案能夠降低能耗、減少維護需求，並壓低總持有成本。

  在供應鏈層面，ESS Tech 主張鈉元素在北美儲量豐富，可降低客戶與製造商對鋰離子供應鏈潛在地緣政治風險的曝險——鋰離子供應鏈目前由中國企業主導。該公司表示，這使鈉離子電池在面對美國政府針對「外國關注實體」所設的採購限制時，能夠處於相對有利的地位。

  ## 技術取捨

  鈉離子技術也存在不容忽視的缺點。元素鈉的重量是鋰的三倍以上，使得鈉基電池整體能量密度偏低——意味著若要達到與鋰基系統相同的儲電容量，需要佔用更大的實體空間。此外，鈉離子電池的生產規模與全球累計裝置容量，目前也遠遠落後於鋰離子電池。

  ## 產業更廣泛的動能

  ESS Tech 此次宣布，是繼其他多家業者近來一連串鈉離子動態之後的最新進展。科羅拉多州的 Peak Energy 去年下旬表示，將在 2030 年前向獨立電力生產商 Jupiter Power 交付最多 4.75 GWh 的鈉基電池。今年 6 月，Peak 又宣布與通用汽車（General Motors）達成製造與設計合作夥伴關係，支持後者的固定式儲能布局，並表示將在加州沙加緬度興建一座 4 GWh 的製造廠。

  綜合來看，上述宣布反映出，隨著美國電網的電力供給與輸電容量限制日趨收緊，公用事業及大型用電客戶對鈉離子技術的興趣持續升溫。ESS Tech 向 Alsym Energy 採購 8.5 GWh 電池芯的協議——於四月下旬宣布——正是 Bridge 產品線背後的供應基礎。
