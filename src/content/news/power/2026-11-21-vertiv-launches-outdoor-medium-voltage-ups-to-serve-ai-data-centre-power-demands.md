body_en: |
  Vertiv has launched the PowerNexus MV UPS, an outdoor-rated medium-voltage platform that combines an uninterruptible power supply (UPS), fault ride-through capability and AI power smoothing in a single system targeting colocation, neocloud and hyperscale data centre operators.

  The system is designed to sit at the utility connection point — the interface between the power grid and a facility — and is intended to deliver continuous power protection while keeping indoor space available for compute equipment. Its skid-based design, in which critical power components are pre-assembled on a structural frame, allows the equipment to be installed outside the data hall rather than consuming raised-floor or indoor square footage.

  Vertiv says the platform can achieve up to 98.5% peak operating efficiency. When paired with long-duration battery systems, it can support battery-backed operation for up to four hours, which the company says could reduce the role of diesel generators in backup power scenarios.

  ## Performance and protection specifications

  The PowerNexus MV UPS is rated to deliver Class 1 output voltage quality under IEC 62040-3, an international standard that defines performance criteria for UPS systems. That classification requires the tightest tolerance on output voltage during both steady-state and transient conditions.

  According to Vertiv, the system is designed to protect critical loads during grid disturbances and upstream faults, absorb rapid AI-driven load fluctuations before they propagate to the grid, and support grid stability during fault conditions. AI workloads are known to produce sudden, large swings in power demand that can stress both on-site infrastructure and the broader grid connection.

  ## Proof-of-concept testing

  Vertiv recently conducted proof-of-concept demonstrations with QTS, SoftBank and Rosendin at its Global Power R&D and Customer Experience Centre in Bologna, Italy. The tests covered steady-state and dynamic operating conditions across load levels from 0% to 100%.

  Vertiv said the demonstrations confirmed Class 1 voltage accuracy during rapid load changes and validated the system's ability to ride through severe voltage disturbances, including a full voltage sag lasting 150 milliseconds and a 25% overvoltage condition sustained for five seconds.

  Ryan Kuithe, Senior Vice President of Facilities Operations at QTS, said the testing validated the system's behaviour under demanding conditions. "During proof-of-concept testing at Vertiv's Global Power R&D and Customer Experience Centre, we validated the system's ability to maintain stable power through dynamic load changes and voltage disturbances," Kuithe said.

  ## Context: grid queues, space constraints and AI load growth

  Kyle Keeper, Senior Vice President of the Power Management Business Unit at Vertiv, described the pressures that shaped the product's development. "Data centres are facing new infrastructure challenges, including grid queues that can delay deployment and revenue generation, indoor space constraints that limit compute density, and evolving grid requirements that can challenge traditional infrastructure and architectural approaches," Keeper said.

  Grid queues — the waiting periods operators face before utilities can connect new facilities to the network — have become a material constraint on data centre development. By moving the primary power protection equipment to the utility interface and outside the building, Vertiv's design addresses both the grid-interaction and space-efficiency problems simultaneously, the company said.

  ## Design tools and customer simulation

  Vertiv is offering a Power Systems Computer Aided Design (PSCAD) model alongside the hardware. PSCAD is a simulation environment used by power engineers to model electrical system behaviour. The model is intended to allow customers to simulate system performance, validate integration with their existing infrastructure and assess power quality and dynamic interactions with the wider grid before committing to a physical deployment.

  ## Planned medium-voltage laboratory in Bologna

  Vertiv also announced plans for a dedicated medium-voltage laboratory at its large power research and customer centre in Bologna. The facility is intended to support research and development, full-voltage validation, customer demonstrations, service training and system-level testing for medium-voltage power architectures, including MV UPS, medium-voltage to 800 VDC conversion, and battery energy storage technologies.

  Giovanni Zanei, Vice President of Large Power Conversion at Vertiv, said strong customer demand for demonstrations had underlined the need for expanded testing capacity. "By enabling full-voltage research and development, testing, customer demonstrations, and system-level validation, the facility will help customers evaluate new architectures with greater confidence as they plan the power systems required for AI-scale data centres, including 800 VDC," Zanei said.

  ## Industry significance

  The PowerNexus MV UPS launch signals a shift in how power protection equipment is being positioned in large data centre designs — from indoor electrical rooms to outdoor, grid-adjacent installations integrated with long-duration storage. For operators navigating grid interconnection delays, tightening indoor space budgets and the uneven power draw of AI compute clusters, the medium-voltage, outdoor form factor represents a different infrastructure architecture than the traditional low-voltage UPS room approach.

  Vertiv said the planned Bologna medium-voltage laboratory, once complete, will provide the testing infrastructure needed to validate that class of architecture at full operating voltage before customer deployments begin.

  威訊（Vertiv）推出PowerNexus MV UPS，這是一套戶外等級中壓平台，將不斷電系統（UPS）、故障穿越能力及AI電力平滑功能整合於單一系統，目標客群涵蓋共置機房、新型雲端及超大規模資料中心業者。

  該系統設計安裝於市電接入點——即電網與機房設施之間的介面——以持續提供電力保護，同時保留室內空間供運算設備使用。其撬裝式設計將關鍵電力元件預先組裝於結構框架上，可安裝於機房外部，無需占用高架地板或室內樓地板面積。

  威訊表示，此平台最高可達98.5%的峰值運行效率。搭配長效電池系統後，可支援長達四小時的電池備援運行。威訊指出，這有助於降低備用電力場景中柴油發電機的使用需求。

  ## 效能與保護規格

  PowerNexus MV UPS依IEC 62040-3國際標準達到Class 1輸出電壓品質。該標準定義了不斷電系統的效能準則，Class 1分類要求在穩態與暫態條件下均符合最嚴格的輸出電壓容差。

  威訊指出，該系統設計可在電網擾動及上游故障期間保護關鍵負載、在AI驅動的負載快速波動傳導至電網前加以吸收，並在故障條件下支援電網穩定。AI工作負載以突發性、大幅度的功率需求變動著稱，對現場基礎設施及整體電網連接均帶來相當壓力。

  ## 概念驗證測試

  威訊近期在位於義大利波隆那（Bologna）的全球電力研發暨客戶體驗中心，與QTS、軟銀（SoftBank）及Rosendin共同進行概念驗證展示。測試涵蓋從0%至100%負載的穩態與動態運行條件。

  威訊表示，展示結果確認了負載快速變化期間的Class 1電壓精度，並驗證了系統在嚴苛電壓擾動下的穿越能力，包括持續150毫秒的完全電壓驟降，以及持續五秒鐘的25%過電壓狀況。

  QTS設施營運資深副總裁萊恩．奎特（Ryan Kuithe）表示，測試驗證了該系統在嚴苛條件下的表現。「在威訊全球電力研發暨客戶體驗中心進行概念驗證測試期間，我們驗證了系統在動態負載變化及電壓擾動中維持穩定電力的能力，」奎特說。

  ## 背景：電網排隊、空間限制與AI負載成長

  威訊電力管理事業部資深副總裁凱爾．基普（Kyle Keeper）說明了促成此產品開發的種種壓力。「資料中心正面臨新的基礎設施挑戰，包括可能延誤部署與營收實現的電網排隊等待、限制運算密度的室內空間不足，以及可能衝擊傳統基礎設施與架構方案的電網法規持續演變，」基普表示。

  電網排隊——業者在公用事業將新設施接入電網前所需承受的等待時程——已成為資料中心發展的實質瓶頸。威訊表示，透過將主要電力保護設備移至市電介面並置於建築外部，其設計同時解決了電網互動與空間效率兩大問題。

  ## 設計工具與客戶模擬

  威訊在硬體之外，亦提供電力系統電腦輔助設計（PSCAD）模型。PSCAD是電力工程師用於模擬電氣系統行為的模擬環境。此模型旨在讓客戶在正式部署前，模擬系統效能、驗證與既有基礎設施的整合情形，並評估電力品質及與更廣泛電網之間的動態互動。

  ## 波隆那中壓實驗室建置計畫

  威訊同時宣布，計畫在波隆那的大型電力研究暨客戶中心內設立專屬中壓實驗室。該設施將支援研究開發、全電壓驗證、客戶展示、服務訓練，以及涵蓋中壓UPS、中壓轉800 VDC直流電及電池儲能技術等中壓電力架構的系統級測試。

  威訊大型電力轉換副總裁喬萬尼．贊尼（Giovanni Zanei）表示，客戶對展示活動的強烈需求，凸顯了擴充測試能量的必要性。「透過全電壓研發、測試、客戶展示與系統級驗證，此設施將協助客戶在規劃AI規模資料中心所需電力系統（包括800 VDC）時，以更大的信心評估新架構，」贊尼說。

  ## 產業意義

  PowerNexus MV UPS的推出，標誌著大型資料中心設計中電力保護設備定位的轉變——從室內電氣室移向戶外、緊鄰電網的裝置，並與長效儲能系統整合。對於正面對電網互聯延遲、室內空間日趨緊縮及AI運算叢集不均勻功率需求的業者而言，中壓戶外形式代表著有別於傳統低壓UPS機房做法的全新基礎設施架構。

  威訊表示，計畫中的波隆那中壓實驗室落成後，將提供在客戶部署前以完整運行電壓驗證此類架構所需的測試基礎設施。
