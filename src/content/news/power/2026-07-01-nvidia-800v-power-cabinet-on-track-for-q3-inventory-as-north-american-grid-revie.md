body_en: |
  NVIDIA expects to complete inventory buildup of its 800V high-voltage direct-current power cabinet in the third quarter of 2026, targeting customers deploying Vera Rubin architecture chips, according to supply chain data reported by Commercial Times. Research firm TrendForce warned that North American grid interconnection reviews and extended power equipment lead times represent serious bottlenecks that will directly affect supply chain shipment timelines.

  ## NVIDIA extends into power infrastructure

  The 800V cabinet is not standard equipment in current deployments. TrendForce said the solution is being developed because existing rack-mounted power supplies will be unable to meet the demands of the Rubin Ultra generation, which arrives in the second half of 2027 and carries power consumption of up to 660 kilowatts (kW) per rack. The firm projects the high-voltage power solution will see gradual adoption from 2027 and reach large-scale shipments in 2028.

  The move represents NVIDIA extending beyond chip supply into the power infrastructure layer — a domain previously controlled by Taiwan-based manufacturers or by data center customers themselves — by offering a complete cabinet-level solution, according to the report.

  ## Grid interconnection and equipment lead times

  TrendForce identified two compounding constraints on North American data center buildout. The primary obstacle for large cloud service providers pursuing gigawatt (GW)-scale campuses — facilities drawing roughly one billion watts or more of continuous power — is the grid interconnection process, which connects a new facility to the existing electrical transmission network. In regions with strict review requirements, that wait can exceed five years for newly planned data centers, TrendForce said.

  On the equipment side, large high-voltage power transformers carried an average lead time of four to five years as of mid-2025, double the lead time recorded in 2020. Switchgear — the electrical control and protection equipment that works alongside transformers — faces similarly severe supply shortages, the firm said.

  ## What the constraints mean for deployment

  TrendForce said NVIDIA's development of its own power architecture is aimed at helping customers push past the physical power limits of chip design. Whether AI data centers come online on schedule, however, depends on whether the U.S. federal government accelerates interconnection approval processes and whether the power infrastructure supply chain can fill critical component gaps, the firm said.

  NVIDIA's 800V solution is projected to move from gradual adoption starting in 2027 to large-scale shipments in 2028.

  ## 輝達800V高壓直流電力機櫃Q3備貨在即，北美電網審查恐成部署瓶頸

  根據《工商時報》引述的供應鏈數據，輝達預計於2026年第三季完成800V高壓直流（HVDC）電力機櫃的備貨作業，目標客群為部署Vera Rubin架構晶片的資料中心業者。研究機構集邦科技（TrendForce）示警，北美電網併網審查曠日廢時，加上電力設備交貨期持續拉長，已形成嚴峻瓶頸，將直接衝擊供應鏈出貨時程。

  ## 輝達跨足電力基礎設施

  800V電力機櫃並非現行部署的標準設備。集邦科技指出，輝達之所以研發此一解決方案，是因為現有機架式電源供應器將無法滿足Rubin Ultra世代的需求——該系列預計於2027年下半年推出，每機架功耗高達660千瓦（kW）。集邦科技預估，高壓電力解決方案將自2027年起逐步導入，並於2028年進入大規模出貨階段。

  報告指出，此舉代表輝達的業務版圖已從晶片供應延伸至電力基礎設施層，而這一領域過去由台灣製造商或資料中心客戶自行主導，如今輝達以完整機櫃層級解決方案切入市場。

  ## 電網併網審查與設備交貨期雙重壓力

  集邦科技點出兩項相互疊加、制約北美資料中心擴建的結構性因素。對於追求十億瓦（GW）級園區規模——即持續用電量達約十億瓦以上——的大型雲端服務供應商而言，最主要的障礙在於電網併網程序，也就是將新設施接入現有電力輸電網路的審批流程。集邦科技表示，在審查要求嚴格的地區，新建資料中心的等待時間可能超過五年。

  設備端方面，截至2025年中，大型高壓電力變壓器的平均交貨期已達四至五年，較2020年的水準整整翻倍。集邦科技表示，與變壓器搭配運作的電氣控制與保護設備——開關設備（switchgear）——同樣面臨嚴峻的供應短缺問題。

  ## 瓶頸對部署的實質影響

  集邦科技表示，輝達自行研發電力架構，目的在於協助客戶突破晶片設計的物理功耗極限。然而，AI資料中心能否如期上線，取決於美國聯邦政府能否加速推動併網審批程序，以及電力基礎設施供應鏈能否填補關鍵零組件的缺口。
