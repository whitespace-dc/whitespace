body_en: |
  Sunrun, Tesla and Renew Home, the three largest home energy providers in the United States, said Wednesday they are pooling their customer assets to offer 16.8 gigawatts (GW) of distributed energy capacity to utilities and hyperscalers — large technology companies that operate data centers at scale — as rising electricity demand congests the U.S. power grid.

  The "capacity-as-a-solution" agreement combines Sunrun and Tesla's installed residential solar-and-battery systems, which together represent 7.8 GW of capacity, with Renew Home's portfolio of more than 8 million smart thermostats and connected devices. Renew Home says those devices can shift heating and cooling loads by one hour during peak demand periods, contributing an additional 9 GW of flexible capacity. In total, the three companies say they can dispatch resources from 12 million devices across 9 million U.S. homes.

  ## Concentration in data center markets

  The offering is concentrated in states that host the country's largest data center clusters. In Texas — which Chris Rauscher, Sunrun's head of grid services and electrification, described as the country's second-largest data center market — the three companies have 1.3 GW of HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) flexible capacity and 440 megawatts (MW) of battery storage. In California, the third-largest market, they have approximately 1.1 GW of HVAC capacity and 3.6 GW of battery capacity.

  Virginia, home to what the companies describe as one of the world's largest commercial computing clusters, currently holds 37 MW of battery storage and 276 MW of HVAC capacity from the three companies' combined customer base. The companies expect that combined Virginia figure to reach 500 MW by 2030.

  ## The grid access problem

  The announcement targets what the companies call the "speed to power" challenge: data centers need to connect to the grid quickly, but grid expansion is costly and time-consuming. Distributed energy resources — power assets located at homes and businesses rather than at centralized plants — can free up headroom on existing grid infrastructure without requiring new transmission lines or generation facilities, Rauscher told Utility Dive.

  Power system experts have discussed the theoretical potential of distributed resources for years, but the three companies argue the scale now available through aggregated home assets makes the opportunity immediately actionable. "I don't think anyone realizes the scale of the resource available right now," Ben Brown, Renew Home's chief executive, told Utility Dive.

  The companies also said the arrangement would deliver "direct, tangible benefits to middle-class American families" through payments for participating in grid service or capacity programs, Rauscher said.

  ## Smart thermostats as untapped capacity

  A significant portion of the offered capacity comes from demand flexibility — reducing or shifting electricity consumption rather than generating new power. Brown said approximately 80 million U.S. homes have controllable HVAC systems, but only 20 million currently have smart thermostats capable of being managed remotely to shift load during peak periods.

  "That is the sleeping giant of what you can bring to bear for the market in terms of capacity," Brown said. He added that distributed resource adoption is expected to remain strong, citing battery and electric vehicle penetration as still in early stages of growth.

  ## Puerto Rico as a live stress test

  The dynamics driving demand for distributed capacity on the U.S. mainland are already acute in Puerto Rico, according to Rauscher. Rooftop solar now accounts for approximately 20% of the island's capacity mix, and those systems are increasingly paired with stationary batteries.

  Puerto Rico's grid, operated since 2021 by private company LUMA Energy, saw around 225 load-shedding events in the final months of 2025, according to a LUMA executive quoted in local media earlier this month. Above-average temperatures have driven elevated demand on the island this year, Rauscher said, and aging fossil-fuel generators routinely go offline for planned or unplanned maintenance, further compressing already-thin reserve margins.

  LUMA has called upon Sunrun's PowerOn Puerto Rico virtual power plant — a system that aggregates distributed home solar-and-battery assets into a coordinated grid resource — at least six times this month, Rauscher said. In early June, an outage affected approximately 170,000 homes and businesses; LUMA CEO Janisse Quiñones said in a Radio Isla interview that more than 200,000 customers with onsite solar and batteries played a critical role in shortening that event.

  "We always thank the residents when they support us in that," Quiñones said, according to an English translation of a Spanish-language report in El Vocero.

  "Puerto Rico is unfortunately ahead of the curve when it comes to all these dynamics," Rauscher said, adding that similar pressures are likely to intensify on the mainland.

  ## What the agreement signals

  The deal marks a concrete move toward making aggregated home assets a commercially deliverable product for utilities and large power buyers, rather than a theoretical grid resource. The three companies' combined scale — spanning millions of homes and tens of millions of controllable devices concentrated in the markets where data center demand is highest — positions the offering as large enough to factor into capacity procurement decisions by major hyperscalers and grid operators.

  With 80 million homes holding controllable HVAC systems against only 20 million equipped with smart thermostats, the companies see significant near-term room to expand the capacity pool without requiring customers to install new hardware.

  ## Sunrun、Tesla、Renew Home聯手提供168億瓦分散式家庭電力容量，吸引電力公司與超大規模資料中心業者目光

  美國三大家庭能源供應商Sunrun、Tesla與Renew Home周三宣布，三家公司將整合各自客戶資產，向電力公司及超大規模資料中心業者——即大規模經營資料中心的大型科技公司——提供合計168億瓦（GW）的分散式能源容量。此舉背景是美國電力需求持續攀升，電網壅塞問題日趨嚴峻。

  這項「容量即解決方案」合作協議，將Sunrun與Tesla已裝設的住宅太陽能及電池系統合併計算——兩者合計容量達78億瓦——再加上Renew Home旗下逾800萬台智慧溫控器與聯網設備所構成的資產組合。Renew Home表示，這批設備能在尖峰需求時段將暖氣與冷氣負載延移一小時，額外貢獻90億瓦的彈性容量。三家公司表示，整體可調度資源涵蓋900萬戶美國家庭、共1,200萬台設備。

  ## 集中布局於資料中心重鎮

  此方案主要聚焦在美國最大資料中心群落所在的幾個州。在德州——Sunrun電網服務與電氣化業務主管Chris Rauscher形容其為全美第二大資料中心市場——三家公司擁有13億瓦的暖通空調（HVAC）彈性容量，以及4.4億瓦（MW）的電池儲能容量。在加州（全美第三大市場），三家公司合計約有11億瓦的暖通空調容量與36億瓦的電池容量。

  維吉尼亞州擁有各方公認全球規模最大的商業運算集群之一，目前三家公司的客戶資產在當地合計持有3,700萬瓦的電池儲能容量與2.76億瓦的暖通空調容量。三家公司預期，維吉尼亞州的合計容量將於2030年前達到5億瓦。

  ## 電網接入速度的瓶頸

  此次宣布，正是針對業界所稱的「快速供電」挑戰：資料中心需要迅速接入電網，但電網擴建既昂貴又耗時。Rauscher在接受《Utility Dive》採訪時表示，分散式能源資源——即部署於住宅與商業場所而非集中式電廠的電力資產——能在不需要興建新輸電線路或發電設施的前提下，釋放現有電網基礎設施的餘裕容量。

  電力系統專家多年來一直在討論分散式資源的理論潛力，但三家公司認為，透過整合家庭資產所達到的規模，已讓這個機會具備立即可行性。Renew Home執行長Ben Brown在接受《Utility Dive》採訪時表示：「我認為沒有人意識到目前手邊這批資源的規模有多龐大。」

  三家公司也指出，此合作安排將透過讓客戶參與電網服務或容量計畫所獲得的收益，為美國中產階級家庭帶來「直接、具體的好處」，Rauscher如此說道。

  ## 智慧溫控器：尚未開發的龐大容量

  此次提供的大部分容量來自需量彈性——也就是削減或延移用電量，而非另行發電。Brown表示，美國約有8,000萬戶家庭配備可調控的暖通空調系統，但目前只有2,000萬戶安裝了能遠端管理、在尖峰時段移轉負載的智慧溫控器。

  「就市場容量而言，這正是能夠大顯身手的『沉睡巨人』，」Brown說道。他補充，分散式資源的普及預計將持續成長，原因在於電池與電動車的滲透率仍處於早期發展階段。

  ## 波多黎各：真實壓力的即時驗證場域

   Rauscher指出，驅動美國本土對分散式容量需求的諸多因素，在波多黎各早已急迫呈現。屋頂太陽能目前約占該島總容量的20%，且這批系統愈來愈多搭配固定式電池使用。

  波多黎各電網自2021年起由私營公司LUMA Energy負責營運。根據本月稍早地方媒體引述一名LUMA高層的說法，2025年最後幾個月，當地發生了約225起限電事件。Rauscher表示，今年島上氣溫高於平均水準，推升了用電需求；老舊的燃油發電機又頻繁進行計畫或非計畫性維護而停機，進一步壓縮本已捉襟見肘的備轉容量。

  Rauscher表示，LUMA本月至少已六度啟動Sunrun的「PowerOn Puerto Rico虛擬電廠」——該系統將分散的家庭太陽能與電池資產整合為協同運作的電網資源。六月初，一場停電事故影響約17萬戶家庭與企業；LUMA執行長Janisse Quiñones在接受Radio Isla廣播採訪時表示，逾20萬名擁有自用太陽能與電池的客戶，在縮短此次停電時間上扮演了關鍵角色。

  「我們每次都會感謝居民在這方面對我們的支持，」Quiñones表示。此段引言來自西班牙語報紙《El Vocero》的英文翻譯報導。

  「波多黎各不幸地已走在所有這些動態變化的前端，」Rauscher說，並補充，類似的壓力可能將在美國本土進一步加劇。

  ## 此協議釋放的訊號

  這項合作代表一個具體行動，旨在將整合家庭資產從理論上的電網資源，轉化為可商業化交付給電力公司與大型電力買家的實際產品。三家公司的合計規模——涵蓋數百萬戶家庭與數千萬台可調控設備，且集中在資料中心需求最旺盛的市場——使這項方案的體量足以納入超大規模資料中心業者與電網運營商的容量採購決策。

  在8,000萬戶擁有可調控暖通空調系統的家庭中，目前僅2,000萬戶配備智慧溫控器，三家公司因此認為，毋須要求客戶安裝新硬體，短期內仍有相當大的空間進一步擴大容量池。
