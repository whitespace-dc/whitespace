body_en: |
  Indonesia's accelerating adoption of artificial intelligence is straining the country's ability to manage its electricity system, with data center power demand rising faster than new generation and transmission infrastructure can be built, according to Ann Moore, Industry Principal of Power and Utilities at Aveva, an industrial software company that develops digital solutions for the energy, manufacturing, and process industries.

  Moore made the assessment in an exclusive interview with Kompas.com on Monday, July 13, 2026, in Jakarta.

  ## Two agendas, one stressed grid

  Indonesia is simultaneously pressing two large policy goals: attracting AI and data center investment as a driver of economic growth, and accelerating a transition to new and renewable energy toward a Net Zero Emission target by 2060. Moore said those twin agendas have made electricity system management significantly more complex than it was previously.

  "AI and data centers will keep coming because they can drive economic growth. The challenge now is how to ensure the electricity system is ready to support that growth," Moore told Kompas.com.

  The country holds substantial renewable resources — geothermal, hydropower, and solar — which Moore said give Indonesia real potential as an AI investment destination. The problem, she said, is no longer whether the energy sources exist, but whether they can all be managed within a single stable system.

  ## Why building more plants is not enough

  Conventional responses to rising electricity demand center on constructing new generation capacity. Moore argued that approach is insufficient for the AI era. Building new power plants and transmission networks requires large capital outlays and years of development time, while AI adoption and data center construction are advancing far more quickly.

  The gap between those two timelines means utilities must extract more capability from infrastructure already in place. Moore said generators can no longer be assessed purely on how much electricity they produce; they must also be able to respond rapidly to swings in supply and demand.

  The intermittent nature of solar power illustrates the operational challenge. "When solar generates electricity, we have to use it. When production drops, another energy source must immediately take over. So all existing assets now have to be well optimized," Moore said.

  ## What this means for the sector

  For Indonesian grid planners and utilities, the implication is that operational flexibility and asset optimization are becoming as important as raw generation capacity. The faster pace of AI-driven demand growth relative to infrastructure build times means any shortfall in grid responsiveness could constrain data center development or raise reliability risks for existing consumers.

  Moore did not specify what software or technology measures she considered adequate to address the management gap, but framed the issue as one requiring action now given the speed at which AI infrastructure is being deployed.

  ## AI成長為印尼電網管理帶來壓力

  隨著人工智慧在印尼加速普及，該國管理電力系統的能力正承受沉重壓力——資料中心的用電需求成長速度，已遠超新建發電與輸電基礎設施的腳步。這是工業軟體公司艾維瓦（Aveva）電力與公用事業產業主管安·摩爾（Ann Moore）的判斷。艾維瓦專注於為能源、製造及流程工業開發數位解決方案。

  摩爾於2026年7月13日（星期一）在雅加達接受《Kompas.com》獨家專訪時，提出上述看法。

  ## 兩大政策目標，一個吃緊的電網

  印尼目前同步推進兩項重大政策目標：一是吸引AI與資料中心投資，以此作為推動經濟成長的引擎；二是加速推動新能源與再生能源轉型，朝2060年淨零排放目標邁進。摩爾表示，這兩項並行的政策議程，使電力系統管理的複雜程度大幅提升，遠比從前困難。

  「AI與資料中心將持續湧入，因為它們能帶動經濟成長。現在的挑戰在於，如何確保電力系統已做好準備，足以支撐這股成長動能，」摩爾向《Kompas.com》表示。

  印尼擁有豐沛的再生能源資源，包括地熱、水力及太陽能，摩爾認為這賦予印尼成為AI投資目的地的真實潛力。她指出，問題已不再是能源來源是否存在，而是能否將所有能源整合在單一穩定的系統內統籌管理。

  ## 光靠蓋更多電廠還不夠

  面對電力需求上升，傳統的應對之道是興建新的發電容量。然而摩爾認為，這套做法在AI時代已顯不足。興建新電廠與輸電網路需要龐大的資本投入，且往往要耗費數年才能完工；相較之下，AI的普及與資料中心的建設速度卻快得多。

  兩者之間的時程落差，意味著電力業者必須從現有基礎設施中挖掘更大的供電潛能。摩爾說，發電機組不能再只以發電量來衡量其價值，還必須具備對供需波動迅速反應的能力。

  太陽能間歇性發電的特性，正好說明了這項運營挑戰。「太陽能在發電時，我們必須即時消化這些電力；一旦發電量下滑，另一種能源必須立即補位。因此，所有既有資產現在都必須充分優化，」摩爾說。

  ## 對產業的意涵

  對印尼的電網規劃者與電力業者而言，上述情況意味著運營彈性與資產優化的重要性，已與純粹的發電裝置容量同等關鍵。AI驅動的需求成長速度，一旦超越基礎設施的建設進度，電網應變能力若有所不足，可能壓縮資料中心的發展空間，或對現有用戶的供電穩定性構成風險。

  摩爾並未具體說明哪些軟體或技術措施足以彌補管理缺口，但她強調，鑑於AI基礎設施部署速度之快，此一問題必須立即採取行動因應。
