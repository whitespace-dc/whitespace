body_en: |
  The U.S. Department of Energy on Wednesday closed a loan of up to $3.26 billion to a subsidiary of American Electric Power (AEP) to finance approximately 100 transmission projects in Texas, making AEP Texas the fourth utility to receive financing through the Trump administration's Energy Dominance Financing program.

  The loan covers the rebuilding and reconductoring (replacing wire on existing towers to boost current-carrying capacity) of existing transmission lines, along with construction of new transmission infrastructure, spanning roughly 2,800 miles in total, the DOE said. Those upgrades are designed to double the power-carrying capacity of the affected lines.

  ## Demand drivers

  The DOE said the Texas projects are intended to help meet rapidly growing electricity demand from data centers, advanced manufacturing, and oil and natural gas development in the Permian Basin.

  AEP Texas said it has signed letters of agreement supporting up to 41 gigawatts (GW) of potential new load through 2030.

  "Texas is poised for incredible growth over the next five years," AEP Texas President and Chief Operating Officer Adrian Rodriguez said. "This loan supports critical updates to our transmission infrastructure to strengthen reliability, connect new load and generation resources and manage affordability."

  ## Projected consumer savings

  The DOE said the projects are expected to save homes and businesses approximately $685 million in electricity costs over the next 30 years.

  ## AEP's federal funding strategy

  AEP said the Texas loan is part of a broader strategy across its 11-state service territory to secure federal funding to reduce customer costs while supporting growth and investing in reliability and resiliency. A separate AEP subsidiary, AEP Transmission, received a $1.6 billion loan guarantee in October to reconductor and rebuild approximately 5,000 miles of transmission lines across Indiana, Michigan, Ohio, Oklahoma, and West Virginia.

  ## Energy Dominance Financing program

  AEP Texas is the fourth utility to obtain financing under the Energy Dominance Financing program. In February, the DOE announced $26.5 billion in loans to Southern Company subsidiaries Alabama Power and Georgia Power to build or upgrade 16.7 GW of grid resources, including 5 GW of new gas generation. In June, the program closed a $1.6 billion loan to Michigan's DTE Gas to update approximately 800 miles of distribution mains and service lines and rebuild an existing compressor station.

  ## 美能源部批准326億美元貸款　助AEP德州升級逾4,500公里輸電網

  美國能源部（DOE）於本週三正式完成一筆最高32.6億美元的貸款交割，借款方為美國電力公司（AEP）旗下德州子公司，資金將用於德州境內約100項輸電工程，使AEP德州成為川普政府「能源主導融資計畫」（Energy Dominance Financing program）迄今第四家獲得融資的電力業者。

  能源部表示，此筆貸款涵蓋既有輸電線路的重建與換線工程（即在原有鐵塔上更換導線，以提升輸電容量），以及新輸電基礎設施的建設，合計延伸約4,500公里。上述工程完工後，預計可將相關線路的輸電容量提升一倍。

  ## 需求驅動因素

  能源部指出，德州這批輸電工程的目的，在於滿足資料中心、先進製造業，以及西德州二疊紀盆地（Permian Basin）油氣開發所帶來的快速成長用電需求。

  AEP德州表示，截至目前已簽署的意向書，支持至2030年前最多41吉瓦（GW）的潛在新增負載接入。

  AEP德州總裁兼營運長Adrian Rodriguez表示：「德州未來五年將迎來驚人成長。這筆貸款支持我們對輸電基礎設施進行關鍵升級，以強化供電可靠性、連接新的負載與發電資源，並協助管理電價的可負擔性。」

  ## 預估節省用電成本

  能源部表示，這批工程預計將在未來30年內，為家庭及企業用戶節省約6.85億美元的電費支出。

  ## AEP的聯邦融資策略

  AEP表示，德州這筆貸款是其橫跨11州服務區域中，爭取聯邦資金的整體策略一環，目標是在支持業務成長、強化供電可靠性與韌性的同時，降低用戶成本。另一子公司AEP輸電公司（AEP Transmission）則已於去年10月獲得一筆16億美元的貸款擔保，用於在印第安納州、密西根州、俄亥俄州、奧克拉荷馬州及西維吉尼亞州，換線並重建約8,000公里的輸電線路。

  ## 能源主導融資計畫

  AEP德州是取得「能源主導融資計畫」融資的第四家電力業者。今年2月，能源部宣布向南方公司（Southern Company）旗下的阿拉巴馬電力（Alabama Power）及喬治亞電力（Georgia Power）提供共265億美元的貸款，用於建設或升級16.7吉瓦的電網資源，其中包括5吉瓦的新增燃氣發電機組。今年6月，該計畫再向密西根州DTE Gas批准一筆16億美元的貸款，用於更新約1,300公里的配氣主管線與服務管線，並重建一座現有壓縮機站。

  AEP德州目前已簽署意向書，支持至2030年前最多41吉瓦的潛在新增負載接入。
