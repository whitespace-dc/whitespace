body_en: |
  Eaton Corp. CEO Paulo Ruiz said the data center industry's shift from conventional alternating-current power systems to 800-volt direct-current architecture is "real," citing significant capital savings the technology offers, as the electrical equipment giant posted record companywide sales of $8.9 billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 28% year over year.

  Ruiz made the remarks to investors on Tuesday. He said a recent McKinsey analysis found that data centers built on 800-volt DC architecture — in which electricity flows in one direction at a fixed voltage, eliminating the conversion losses associated with alternating current — could reduce non-IT capital expenditures by up to 18%.

  ## Solid-state transformer milestone

  At the center of Eaton's DC strategy is the solid-state transformer, or SST — a device that uses semiconductors and magnets rather than the steel cores and copper windings of conventional transformers, making it more compact and efficient. Ruiz told investors that Eaton's 15-kilovolt, 2-megawatt SST became, "to our knowledge," the first multi-megawatt unit to pass all required tests under a new UL Solutions certification standard for the technology. UL Solutions is a global safety certification organization.

  The milestone follows a separate test conducted earlier this year by the Electric Power Research Institute, the New York Power Authority, and North Carolina State University, in which a 1-megawatt SST operated under real-world conditions cut power losses in half compared with a conventional transformer configuration.

  Despite the technical progress, Ruiz cautioned that broad SST deployment remains some time away. "The timing of that transition is not under our control," he said. He described SSTs as only one of several "key building blocks" required for a full DC architecture rollout, alongside DC circuit breakers, power quality systems, and advanced cooling technologies.

  Ruiz also emphasized that Eaton's existing product line remains competitive regardless of which architecture prevails. "The AC architectures are not bad, and we are fine either way," he said. "We win in either path."

  ## Record backlog and demand

  Eaton's Electrical Americas segment — which sells low- and medium-voltage distribution equipment, circuit protection, and power reliability systems across the United States and the broader Americas — recorded $4.2 billion in third-quarter sales, up 22% year over year. Its order backlog reached $16 billion, up 33% year over year, while orders on a rolling 12-month basis increased 39%.

  Ruiz attributed the segment's widening margins to broad-based demand from data centers and large electrification projects. "Demand continues to run ahead of what we can convert," he said, citing utility investment, grid modernization, industrial reshoring, and AI-driven data center expansion as the principal drivers.

  In 2025, data centers and distributed IT accounted for 21% of Eaton's total sales, with utilities contributing an additional 11%. The company said it expects end-market growth of approximately 11% in 2027.

  ## Scale of the data center pipeline

  Ruiz framed the demand outlook in terms of the gap between announced projects and existing installed capacity. "We track the projects that are announced, and now they amount to 366 gigawatts," he said, referring to the global pipeline of data center capacity under development or planned. That figure compares with an installed base of roughly 50 gigawatts worldwide. "In my opinion, this will last for many years to come," Ruiz said.

  ## Acquisition strategy

  Eaton has pursued a series of acquisitions to increase its exposure to data center infrastructure. In August 2025, the company paid $86 million for Resilient Power Systems, the solid-state transformer developer now central to its DC architecture push.

  In March 2026, Eaton paid approximately $9.6 billion to acquire Boyd Thermal, described in a securities filing as "a U.S.-based global leader in thermal components, systems, and ruggedized solutions for data center, aerospace and other end-markets." Ruiz said the Boyd acquisition would allow Eaton to develop integrated power and cooling architectures and "deliver differentiated grid-to-chip solutions" for data center customers.

  Most recently, Eaton struck a deal for COL Group, an Italian manufacturer of medium-voltage electrical distribution equipment, grid automation technologies, and modular power systems, for approximately $920 million. The company said the transaction would strengthen its sales to data center and utility customers in Europe.

  ## Significance

  The combination of a record backlog, a certification milestone for solid-state transformer technology, and three major acquisitions in roughly 18 months positions Eaton to compete across both the existing AC market and an emerging DC infrastructure segment. The 366-gigawatt announced pipeline — more than seven times current installed capacity — suggests the construction cycle has years to run, though Ruiz acknowledged that the pace of architectural transition to DC remains outside any single supplier's control.

  Eaton predicts approximately 11% end-market growth in 2027, with data centers, utilities, and industrial electrification all cited as contributing segments.
body_zh: |
  伊頓公司（Eaton Corp.）執行長保羅・魯伊茲（Paulo Ruiz）表示，資料中心業界從傳統交流電電力系統轉向800伏特直流電架構的趨勢「確實存在」，並指出這項技術能帶來顯著的資本節省；與此同時，這家電氣設備巨擘公布2026年第三季全公司營收創歷史新高，達89億美元，較前一年同期成長28%。

  魯伊茲於週二向投資人發表上述評論。他援引麥肯錫最新分析指出，採用800伏特直流電架構建置的資料中心——在這種架構中，電流以固定電壓單向流動，消除了交流電轉換所帶來的能量損耗——可將非IT資本支出降低最多18%。

  ## 固態變壓器里程碑

  伊頓直流電策略的核心是固態變壓器（SST）。這種裝置以半導體與磁性材料取代傳統變壓器的矽鋼鐵芯與銅線圈，體積更為緊湊、效率也更高。魯伊茲向投資人表示，伊頓的15千伏、2百萬瓦（2MW）固態變壓器「就我們所知」，已成為首款通過UL Solutions針對該技術制定的全新認證標準所有必要測試的多百萬瓦等級機型。UL Solutions是一家全球性安全認證機構。

  此一里程碑發生在今年稍早另一項獨立測試之後——由美國電力研究院（Electric Power Research Institute）、紐約電力局（New York Power Authority）及北卡羅來納州立大學共同進行。測試結果顯示，一台1MW固態變壓器在實際運作條件下，與傳統變壓器配置相比，電力損耗減少了一半。

  儘管技術持續進展，魯伊茲仍謹慎表示，固態變壓器的廣泛部署仍需一段時日。「這項轉型的時程並非由我們掌控，」他說。他形容固態變壓器只是全面推行直流電架構所需「幾項關鍵構建模組」之一，其餘還包括直流斷路器、電能品質系統以及先進冷卻技術。

  魯伊茲也強調，無論哪種架構最終勝出，伊頓現有的產品線均具備競爭力。「交流電架構並沒有什麼不好，我們兩邊都能應對，」他說，「不論走哪條路，我們都是贏家。」

  ## 創紀錄的積壓訂單與需求

  伊頓電氣美洲事業部——負責在美國及美洲地區銷售低壓與中壓配電設備、電路保護及電力可靠性系統——第三季營收達42億美元，年增22%。訂單積壓量達160億美元，年增33%；以滾動12個月計算，訂單量成長39%。

  魯伊茲將該事業部利潤率持續擴大歸因於資料中心及大型電氣化專案的全面需求。「需求持續超過我們的轉換產能，」他說，並將公用事業投資、電網現代化、工業回流及AI驅動的資料中心擴張列為主要成長動力。

  2025年，資料中心與分散式IT佔伊頓總營收的21%，公用事業另貢獻11%。該公司表示，預計2027年終端市場成長約11%。

  ## 資料中心建設管線規模

  魯伊茲以已公告專案與現有裝置容量之間的落差，來描述需求展望。「我們追蹤已公告的專案，目前合計達366吉瓦（GW），」他說，指的是全球正在開發或規劃中的資料中心容量管線。這一數字相較於全球現有約50吉瓦的裝置基礎，差距懸殊。「依我之見，這樣的情況還會持續多年，」魯伊茲說。

  ## 收購策略

  伊頓透過一系列收購案，積極擴大在資料中心基礎設施領域的佈局。2025年8月，該公司以8,600萬美元收購固態變壓器開發商Resilient Power Systems，如今這家公司已成為伊頓直流電架構布局的核心。

  2026年3月，伊頓以約96億美元收購Boyd Thermal，根據證券申報文件描述，Boyd Thermal是「一家以美國為基地的全球性領導廠商，提供資料中心、航太及其他終端市場所需的熱管理元件、系統及強固化解決方案」。魯伊茲表示，收購Boyd將使伊頓得以開發整合式電力與冷卻架構，並為資料中心客戶「提供從電網到晶片的差異化解決方案」。

  最近，伊頓又達成收購COL Group的協議，COL Group是一家義大利中壓配電設備、電網自動化技術及模組化電力系統製造商，收購金額約為9.2億美元。該公司表示，此交易將強化其在歐洲對資料中心及公用事業客戶的銷售。

  ## 整體意義

  創紀錄的積壓訂單、固態變壓器技術的認證里程碑，以及約18個月內完成的三項重大收購，使伊頓得以在現有交流電市場及新興直流電基礎設施兩大領域同步布局競爭。366吉瓦的已公告建設管線——超過現有裝置容量的七倍以上——顯示這波建設週期仍有數年動能可期，不過魯伊茲坦承，向直流電架構過渡的速度並非任何單一供應商所能掌控。

  伊頓預測2027年終端市場成長約11%，並將資料中心、公用事業及工業電氣化列為推動成長的三大主要板塊。
