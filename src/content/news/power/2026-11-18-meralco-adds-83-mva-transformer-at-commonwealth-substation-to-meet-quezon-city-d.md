body_en: |
  Manila Electric Co. (Meralco) has completed an upgrade of its Commonwealth substation in Quezon City, adding an 83-megavolt-ampere (MVA) power transformer to increase the facility's capacity and serve growing electricity demand in the area, the company said Tuesday.

  The substation operates at 115 kilovolts (kV) stepped down to 34.5 kV — a mid-voltage distribution tier that feeds local networks before power reaches end users. Alongside the transformer, the project involved installing 34.5-kV gas-insulated switchgear (GIS), a compact switching technology that uses pressurized gas rather than open-air busbars, along with the associated protection and control equipment for the substation's second transformer bank, Meralco said.

  ## Customers and projects served

  Meralco identified several large electricity consumers expected to benefit from the expanded substation capacity. San Miguel Corp.'s Metro Rail Transit Line 7 (MRT-7), a rail project under development in the Quezon City corridor, is among the named beneficiaries. Maynilad Water Services, Inc., one of Metro Manila's two privatized water concessionaires, is also listed.

  Public institutions expected to draw on the upgraded substation include the Commission on Audit, the House of Representatives at the Batasang Pambansa complex, and the Polytechnic University of the Philippines Quezon City campus. Commercial and healthcare facilities named by Meralco include Fairview Central Mall and Rosario Maclang Bautista General Hospital.

  ## Utility scale and investment context

  Meralco described the Commonwealth substation upgrade as part of its broader program of investment in expanding and modernizing its distribution facilities to accommodate growing electricity requirements. The company did not disclose the capital cost of the project.

  As of the end of September, Meralco served 8.4 million customers, a 2% increase from 8.2 million a year earlier, according to the company.

  ## Significance

  The addition of a second transformer bank at an existing 115-kV substation is a standard method for utilities to increase firm capacity — the amount of power deliverable even if one transformer is out of service — without constructing an entirely new facility. For the Quezon City corridor, where large infrastructure projects such as MRT-7 are coming online alongside continued residential and commercial growth, the upgrade signals that Meralco is investing in distribution headroom ahead of demand rather than in response to supply failures.

  Meralco's customer base grew by roughly 200,000 connections in the twelve months through September, a pace that sustains the company's stated rationale for continued distribution infrastructure investment.
body_zh: |
  馬尼拉電力公司（Meralco）已完成位於奎松市的聯邦變電站升級工程，新增一座83百萬伏安（MVA）電力變壓器，以提升設施容量、滿足當地持續增長的用電需求，該公司週二表示。

  該變電站電壓等級為115千伏（kV）降壓至34.5千伏——屬於中壓配電層級，負責在電力抵達終端用戶前饋送至在地電網。除變壓器外，本次工程還安裝了34.5千伏氣體絕緣開關設備（GIS）——這是一種以加壓氣體取代露天母線的緊湊型開關技術——以及與第二組變壓器相關的保護與控制設備，Meralco指出。

  ## 受益的客戶與建設項目

  Meralco點名多位大型用電客戶，預計將受惠於變電站擴容後的新增供電能力。聖米格爾集團旗下的都會捷運7號線（MRT-7）是受益對象之一，這條鐵路建設計畫目前正在奎松市走廊一帶積極開發。馬尼拉第一自來水公司（Maynilad Water Services, Inc.）同樣列名其中，該公司是大馬尼拉地區兩家私營供水特許業者之一。

  預計將使用本次升級後變電站電力的公共機構，包括審計委員會、位於巴塔桑潘巴薩大廈的眾議院，以及菲律賓理工大學奎松市校區。商業與醫療機構方面，Meralco點名費爾維尤中央商場與羅薩里奧・麥克朗・包蒂斯塔綜合醫院。

  ## 電力公司規模與投資背景

  Meralco表示，聯邦變電站升級工程是公司大規模擴建與現代化配電設施計畫的一環，旨在因應持續成長的用電需求。公司未披露本次工程的資本支出金額。

  截至今年九月底，Meralco服務客戶數達840萬戶，較一年前的820萬戶增長2%。

  ## 意義

  在既有的115千伏變電站新增第二組變壓器，是電力公司在無需新建完整設施的前提下提升確保容量的標準做法——所謂確保容量，即便在其中一座變壓器停止運作的情況下，仍能持續穩定供電的總容量。對奎松市走廊而言，MRT-7等大型基礎設施建設計畫相繼進入投運階段，加上住宅與商業開發持續推進，本次升級顯示Meralco正採取超前布局策略，預先為配電網絡預留容量餘裕，而非被動等到供電出現短缺才應對。

  Meralco的客戶總數在截至九月的十二個月內增加了約20萬戶，這一增速持續支撐著該公司對外揭示的持續投資配電基礎設施的正當性。
