body_en: |
  The 800-volt direct-current (800VDC) power architecture for AI data centers has reached a point of clear definition, according to DIGITIMES research published in August 2026, with power semiconductor suppliers broadly releasing products targeting the standard as GPU power consumption continues to climb under NVIDIA's product roadmap.

  ## 2026 as the stabilization year

  DIGITIMES identifies 2026 as the critical year in which the 800VDC supply architecture transitions from an emerging direction to an established baseline. Although NVIDIA itself has made few direct moves on power architecture in 2026, power semiconductor companies have released corresponding solutions in the same period, according to the research firm.

  DIGITIMES analyst Yao Jiayang said the post-2026 AI data center power architecture now has a complete profile. The market's competitive outcome will hinge on balancing total component cost, mechanical design, power density, and energy efficiency, Yao said. As 800VDC moves toward standardization, supplier delivery reliability and the depth of system-design collaboration and bargaining leverage with original equipment manufacturers and original design manufacturers (OEM/ODM partners) will become the defining competitive axes for 2027 and 2028, according to DIGITIMES.

  ## Rubin Ultra and the Power Sidecar cabinet

  With NVIDIA's Rubin Ultra GPU expected to enter mass production in 2027, the Power Sidecar — a dedicated power cabinet in the rack architecture — will deliver 800VDC directly to the computing cabinet at that point, according to DIGITIMES analyst Yao.

  Inside the computing cabinet, step-down conversion from 800VDC to lower voltages is required. Beyond the existing 800VDC-to-54VDC conversion path, 12VDC and 6VDC output rails are emerging as additional design targets for power semiconductor suppliers, according to Yao.

  ## GaN's role in step-down conversion

  Gallium nitride (GaN) power components play an indispensable role in this step-down architecture, according to DIGITIMES. The research firm says GaN devices have an opportunity to displace traditional silicon metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistors (MOSFETs) in the secondary-side circuits of those converters.

  ## SiC at the high-voltage end

  Silicon carbide (SiC) power components hold significant positions in both the Power Sidecar cabinet and in solid-state transformer applications, according to DIGITIMES. Some SiC components in development have already reached voltage ratings of 10 kilovolts (kV) — a level the research firm says will drive the broader industry toward higher-power designs over the longer term.

  ## Competitive positioning

  Companies holding technology advantages in SiC junction field-effect transistors (JFETs) or GaN bidirectional switches (BDS) — specifically Infineon, Navitas, and Onsemi — are positioned to widen their competitive advantages through the superior characteristics of those components, according to DIGITIMES analyst Yao.

  Yao said those component-level advantages, combined with the ability to collaborate closely with OEM and ODM partners on system design, will separate the leading suppliers from the field as 800VDC becomes the standard power delivery architecture for AI data centers from 2027 onward.

  ## AI資料中心800VDC電源架構成型，功率半導體商機全面引爆

  根據拓墣產業研究院2026年8月發布的最新研究報告，AI資料中心的800伏特直流（800VDC）電源架構已達到定義清晰的階段。隨著輝達（NVIDIA）產品藍圖持續推動GPU功耗攀升，功率半導體供應商正廣泛推出針對此標準所開發的解決方案。

  ## 2026年：架構穩定的關鍵年

  拓墣將2026年定位為800VDC供電架構從新興方向躍升為確立基準的關鍵轉折點。研究機構指出，儘管輝達本身在2026年鮮少直接著墨於電源架構，但功率半導體業者已在同期相繼推出對應解決方案。

  拓墣分析師姚嘉洋表示，2026年後AI資料中心的電源架構輪廓已趨於完整。他指出，市場競爭結果將取決於如何在元件總成本、機構設計、功率密度與能源效率之間取得最佳平衡。隨著800VDC邁向標準化，供應商的供貨可靠度，以及與原始設備製造商（OEM）和原始設計製造商（ODM）在系統設計上的深度協作能力與議價籌碼，將成為2027至2028年間決定競爭勝負的核心軸線。

  ## Rubin Ultra與Power Sidecar機櫃

  輝達Rubin Ultra GPU預計於2027年進入量產。拓墣分析師姚嘉洋表示，屆時機架架構中的專用電源機櫃——Power Sidecar——將直接向運算機櫃輸送800VDC電力。

  在運算機櫃內部，必須將800VDC降壓轉換至較低電壓。姚嘉洋指出，除現有的800VDC轉54VDC轉換路徑外，12VDC與6VDC輸出電壓軌也正成為功率半導體供應商的額外設計目標。

  ## 氮化鎵（GaN）在降壓轉換中的角色

  根據拓墣研究，氮化鎵（GaN）功率元件在此降壓架構中扮演不可或缺的角色。研究機構表示，GaN元件有機會取代傳統矽基金屬氧化物半導體場效電晶體（MOSFET），在上述轉換器的二次側電路中佔據主導地位。

  ## 碳化矽（SiC）把持高壓端

  碳化矽（SiC）功率元件則在Power Sidecar機櫃與固態變壓器應用中均佔有舉足輕重的地位。拓墣指出，部分在研發中的SiC元件耐壓規格已達10千伏（kV），研究機構認為此一水準將在較長期的時程內驅動整體產業朝向更高功率設計演進。

  ## 競爭格局

  拓墣分析師姚嘉洋表示，在SiC接面場效電晶體（JFET）或GaN雙向開關（BDS）領域擁有技術優勢的業者——具體而言包括英飛凌（Infineon）、Navitas與安森美（Onsemi）——將憑藉這些元件的卓越特性，進一步拉開與競爭對手的領先差距。

  姚嘉洋表示，元件層級的技術優勢，加上與OEM及ODM夥伴在系統設計上緊密協作的能力，將在2027年以後800VDC成為AI資料中心標準供電架構的過程中，使領先供應商與其他競爭者之間拉開決定性的差距。
