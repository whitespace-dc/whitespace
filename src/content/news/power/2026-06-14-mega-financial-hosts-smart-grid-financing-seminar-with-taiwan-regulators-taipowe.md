body_en: |
  Mega Financial Holdings on June 11 hosted a cross-sector seminar on investment and financing strategies for Taiwan's smart grid, bringing together the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC), Taiwan Power Company (Taipower), academic researchers, and Ernst & Young Taiwan to identify how financial institutions can channel capital into energy-transition infrastructure.

  The seminar, titled "Smart Grid Investment and Financing Practicum," was chaired by Mega Financial President Zhang Chuanzhang. Attendees included FSC Deputy Commissioner Zhuang Xiuyuan, Taipower Deputy General Manager and Chief Digital Officer Wu Jinzhong, Taiwan Institute of Economic Research (TIER) Deputy President Zuo Junde, and Ernst & Young Taiwan Executive Vice President Feng Zhiwei.

  ## Mega Financial's sustainable finance role

  Zhang said Mega Financial has joined the FSC's Sustainable Finance Pioneer Alliance and serves as convener of its cross-departmental advisory working group. The holding company has organized four enabling-activities seminars to date, drawing nearly 500 financial industry practitioners and industry representatives through in-person and online participation. The seminars aim to help financial institutions understand the government's net-zero transition policies and direct funds into key industries, Zhang said.

  ## Taipower on grid challenges and AI data center demand

  Wu outlined Taipower's smart grid planning blueprint and deployment progress, and analyzed the intermittency and uncertainty challenges created by renewable energy generation. He described energy storage system applications across multiple grid levels and shared cases in which private-sector participants in Taipower's electricity trading platform have helped stabilize grid frequency.

  Wu specifically addressed the electricity needs of AI data centers, which he characterized as high-power and high-volatility consumers. He proposed that building behind-the-meter microgrids — small-scale power networks located on the customer's side of the utility meter — is a key solution for peak shaving and backup power supply for that segment.

  ## Carbon certificates and CCS

  Zuo of TIER said corporate demand for carbon-free electricity is rising rapidly as international net-zero transition accelerates. He said carbon capture and storage (CCS) — a process that traps carbon dioxide emitted by thermal power plants and stores it underground — has become a necessary addition to fossil-fuel generation facilities. Carbon-abated electricity certificates (CAEC, 去碳電力憑證) combined with green finance instruments can establish new carbon-free electricity business models, helping high-tech manufacturers secure stable carbon-free power while creating new investment and financing opportunities, Zuo said.

  ## Structuring grid assets for project finance

  Feng of Ernst & Young Taiwan examined how smart grid projects, energy storage systems, virtual power plants — networks of distributed energy resources managed collectively — and behind-the-meter microgrids can each be structured as investable assets. He said the key elements are financial models, risk allocation, power purchase agreements (PPAs), and project financing mechanisms. Grid resilience, low-carbon electricity attributes, and corporate electricity demand can be converted into assessable, priceable, and financeable investment targets that support Taiwan's energy transition, Feng said.

  ## Taiwan's smart grid Phase 2

  Taiwan's national smart grid initiative has entered its second phase, moving from pilots into broad application deployment to support renewable energy grid integration and strengthen power system stability and efficiency, Mega Financial said. The company said it will continue to advocate for energy transition and diversified clean energy policies, serve as a communication bridge between government, industry, and peer financial institutions, and work toward Taiwan's 2050 net-zero emissions target.
body_zh: |
  兆豐金融控股公司於6月11日舉辦跨界智慧電網投資與融資策略研討會，邀集金融監督管理委員會（金管會）、台灣電力公司（台電）、學界研究人員及安永台灣共同與會，研議金融機構如何引導資金流入能源轉型基礎建設，適逢台灣全國智慧電網計畫正式邁入第二階段大規模應用部署。

  本次研討會以「智慧電網投融資實務」為題，由兆豐金控總經理張傳章主持。與會者包括金管會副主委莊琇媛、台電副總兼首席數位長吳進忠、台灣經濟研究院（台經院）副院長左峻德，以及安永台灣執行副總裁馮志偉。

  ## 兆豐金控的永續金融角色

  張傳章表示，兆豐金控已加入金管會「永續金融先行者聯盟」，並擔任跨部門諮詢工作小組的召集人。該控股公司迄今已舉辦四場賦能活動研討會，透過實體及線上參與形式，累計吸引近500位金融業從業人員及各界產業代表出席。張傳章指出，這些研討會旨在協助金融機構深入瞭解政府的淨零轉型政策，進而將資金引導至關鍵產業。

  ## 台電談電網挑戰與AI資料中心需求

  吳進忠說明台電智慧電網規劃藍圖及建置推進進度，並分析再生能源發電所帶來的間歇性與不確定性挑戰。他介紹儲能系統在電網各層級的應用情形，並分享民間業者參與台電電力交易平台、協助穩定電網頻率的實際案例。

  吳進忠特別就AI資料中心的用電需求進行說明，將其定性為高電力、高波動的用電型態。他提出，在用戶端建置表後微電網——亦即位於電力公用事業計費表客戶側的小型電力網絡——是因應該族群削峰填谷及備用電力供應需求的關鍵解方。

  ## 碳憑證與碳捕捉及封存

  台經院副院長左峻德表示，隨著國際淨零轉型加速，企業對無碳電力的需求正快速攀升。他指出，碳捕捉與封存（CCS）——一種捕集火力發電廠排放的二氧化碳並將其封存於地下的技術——已成為化石燃料發電設施不可或缺的配套措施。左峻德說，去碳電力憑證（CAEC）結合綠色金融工具，可建立全新的無碳電力商業模式，協助高科技製造商取得穩定的無碳電力，同時為市場創造新的投融資機會。

  ## 電網資產的專案融資結構

  安永台灣馮志偉探討智慧電網專案、儲能系統、虛擬電廠——即集中管理分散式能源資源的電力網絡——以及表後微電網，如何各自被架構為可供投資的資產標的。他指出，關鍵要素包括財務模型、風險分配、購電協議（PPA）及專案融資機制。馮志偉表示，電網韌性、低碳電力屬性與企業用電需求，均可轉化為可評估、可定價、可融資的投資標的，進而有力支持台灣的能源轉型。

  ## 台灣智慧電網第二階段

  兆豐金控表示，台灣全國智慧電網計畫已進入第二階段，從試行示範邁向大規模應用部署，以支援再生能源電網整合，並強化電力系統的穩定性與效率。該公司表示，將持續倡議能源轉型及多元潔淨能源政策，擔任政府、產業與同業金融機構之間的溝通橋樑，並致力於實現台灣2050淨零排放目標。
