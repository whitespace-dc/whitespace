body_en: |
  ABB has launched an ultracapacitor energy storage system designed to allow Irish data centers to comply with a new national grid code that will require them to support the grid during fault events rather than disconnect from it.

  The requirement, known as MPID345, is issued by EirGrid, Ireland's transmission system operator, and targets large demand facilities including data centers. Under MPID345, affected facilities must remain connected to the grid during fault conditions and, once voltage recovers, restore 90 percent of their pre-fault power demand within 500 milliseconds — roughly half a second.

  ## What the system does

  ABB's platform combines ultracapacitor energy storage — a technology capable of absorbing and releasing energy very rapidly, distinct from conventional batteries — with grid-forming power conversion and digital control. The company said the system is compact, modular, and installed behind the meter, meaning on the customer side of the grid connection point, without requiring data centers to redesign existing electrical infrastructure.

  ABB said the system responds to grid disturbances in milliseconds. Unlike conventional backup power approaches — uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), diesel generators, and protection schemes — which isolate a facility from grid events, the company said the new platform allows facilities to actively stabilize the grid while maintaining their own operational continuity.

  ## Ireland's growing data center load problem

  Data centers in Ireland currently consume approximately 24 percent of the country's total electricity supply, according to ABB. The company said that figure is expected to reach 30 percent by 2032.

  That concentration of large electrical loads has created a measurable problem for grid stability. The amount of power shed from the Irish grid during fault events has grown from 74 megawatts (MW) in January 2022 to 387 MW in May 2025, ABB said. When many facilities disconnect simultaneously during a disturbance, the resulting power imbalance can drive frequency excursions that cascade across the entire grid.

  "This is a radical change in what the national grid demands of Ireland's data center operators," said Lee Todd, ABB's vice president of energy and carbon services, electrification service. "When large loads shed simultaneously during a disturbance, the resulting power imbalance can drive frequency excursions that cascade across the entire grid. That is why the country's data center operators will be required to provide grid support."

  ## What comes next

  ABB said Ireland is leading on this type of active grid support requirement, but other jurisdictions are set to follow. Todd named Spain and the U.S. state of Texas as markets where similar requirements are expected to emerge.

  The launch positions ABB's ultracapacitor platform as a compliance solution for operators facing MPID345 deadlines, and potentially for operators in other countries as active grid support mandates extend beyond Ireland.

  ABB推出超級電容儲能系統，協助愛爾蘭資料中心業者遵循全國最新電網規範。該規範要求相關設施在電網發生故障事件時，必須繼續為電網提供支援，而非直接斷開連線。

  這項規範稱為MPID345，由愛爾蘭輸電系統業者EirGrid發布，適用對象為包含資料中心在內的大型需量設施。依據MPID345，受管設施必須在電網故障狀況下維持連線，並在電壓恢復後500毫秒（約半秒）內，將電力需求恢復至故障前的九成。

  ## 系統功能說明

  ABB的平台整合了超級電容儲能技術、構網型電力轉換裝置，以及數位控制系統。超級電容是一種能夠極快速吸收與釋放能量的技術，有別於傳統電池。ABB表示，該系統體積精巧、採模組化設計，安裝於計量表後端，亦即設置在電網接入點的用戶側，資料中心無須大幅改動既有電氣基礎設施。

  ABB指出，該系統可在數毫秒內對電網擾動作出反應。不同於不斷電系統（UPS）、柴油發電機及保護方案等傳統備用電力方式——這些方式會將設施與電網事件隔離——ABB表示，這套新平台能讓設施主動協助穩定電網，同時維持自身營運不中斷。

  ## 愛爾蘭資料中心負載問題日益嚴峻

  ABB表示，愛爾蘭資料中心目前消耗該國約24%的電力供應總量，預計到2032年這一比例將攀升至30%。

  如此龐大的大型電力負載集中，已對電網穩定性造成可量化的衝擊。ABB指出，愛爾蘭電網在故障事件中的甩負荷量，已從2022年1月的74百萬瓦（MW）激增至2025年5月的387 MW。當大量設施在一次擾動中同時斷開連線，隨之而來的功率失衡可能引發頻率偏移，並在整個電網中形成連鎖效應。

  「這對愛爾蘭資料中心業者而言，是全國電網在要求面上的根本性轉變，」ABB電氣化服務事業群能源與碳服務副總裁李．托德（Lee Todd）表示。「當大型負載在一次擾動中同時甩開，所造成的功率失衡可能引發頻率偏移，進而形成席捲整個電網的連鎖反應。這正是愛爾蘭資料中心業者將被要求提供電網支援的原因。」

  ## 後續發展

  ABB表示，愛爾蘭在這類主動式電網支援要求方面走在各國前列，但預料其他地區也將陸續跟進。托德點名西班牙及美國德克薩斯州，認為這兩個市場也可能出現類似要求。

  此次發布將ABB超級電容平台定位為面臨MPID345期限之業者的合規解決方案，並可能進一步擴展至其他國家——隨著主動式電網支援強制要求在愛爾蘭以外持續蔓延，更多地區的業者都將面臨同樣的課題。
