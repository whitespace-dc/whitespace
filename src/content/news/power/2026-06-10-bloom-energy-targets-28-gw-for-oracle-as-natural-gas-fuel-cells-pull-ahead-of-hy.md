body_en: |
  Bloom Energy in April 2026 expanded its relationship with Oracle to support up to 2.8 gigawatts of fuel cell capacity for artificial intelligence data centers, the latest in a series of large commercial agreements that have positioned natural gas and biogas solid oxide fuel cells as the near-term alternative to diesel backup generation most likely to scale, while hydrogen-specific power technologies remain confined to pilots and niche deployments.

  ## Bloom Energy's commercial momentum

  The Oracle agreement is aimed at enabling higher-density AI workloads with faster deployment timelines than traditional power solutions, according to the companies. It follows deals Bloom has announced with Equinix and Compass Datacenters, and a $5 billion strategic partnership with asset manager Brookfield announced in October 2025 to accelerate fuel cell capacity at AI-focused facilities. "Behind-the-meter power solutions are essential to closing the grid gap for AI factories," said Sikander Rashid, global head of AI Infrastructure at Brookfield, in an October 2025 press release.

  Bloom's solid oxide fuel cell systems generate electricity from natural gas, biogas, or hydrogen without combustion. The behind-the-meter model allows operators to install generation capacity on-site, bypassing utility interconnection queues that have lengthened as AI infrastructure demand has grown.

  ## Hydrogen fuel cell pilots have not broadened into deployment

  Microsoft has been the most prominent advocate for hydrogen fuel cells in data centers. A 3 megawatt hydrogen fuel cell system developed with Plug Power, housed in two 40-foot shipping containers at a facility in Latham, New York, kept a data center online for two days to demonstrate its potential as a diesel replacement. A separate pilot with Irish energy company ESB provided up to 250 kilowatts at Microsoft's Dublin campus. Additional hydrogen fuel cell deployments have been completed or are underway at data centers in California, Wyoming, North Carolina, Utah, and Texas, with Bloom Energy and Ballard Power Systems among the main vendors.

  Despite those demonstrations, broader deployment has not followed. NorthC Datacenters installed what was described as Europe's first 500 kilowatt hydrogen fuel cell backup system at its Groningen, Netherlands facility in 2022, but few comparable installations have been completed in the years since.

  "Investment in hydrogen peaked in 2023," said Jon Moore, chief executive of energy transition analyst firm BloombergNEF, in a March 2026 interview with Energy Transition Insider.

  ## US policy retreat removes hydrogen mandate

  In mid-2024, the US Environmental Protection Agency finalized greenhouse gas standards for fossil-fuel power plants that omitted a previously proposed requirement for hydrogen co-firing — the practice of blending hydrogen with natural gas in combustion-based generation. An earlier draft had contemplated requiring plants to co-fire 30 percent hydrogen by volume with natural gas by 2032, rising to 96 percent by 2038. Those requirements were removed from the final rule.

  "Hydrogen was excluded due to its production being expensive and the fact that the US has limited infrastructure to produce, transport, or store it," said Megan Reusser, technology manager at Burns & McDonnell.

  Major US generator manufacturers including Caterpillar and Generac have not aggressively pursued hydrogen engine products. Outside of industrial refineries, hydrogen is not widely available at the volumes data centers would require, and distribution would necessitate significant pipeline and storage investment. Safety and permitting concerns present additional obstacles, and for now hydrogen engines in US data center backup applications appear limited to experimental or lab-scale deployments.

  ## Hydrogen engine options exist but operate at limited scale

  Engine manufacturer Innio Jenbacher offers blended hydrogen-natural gas engines capable of supporting up to 10 megawatts for sites where hydrogen supply is limited, and 100 percent hydrogen units up to 1 megawatt, with 3 megawatt models planned. The company reported an order from NorthC Datacenters for six dual-gas Jenbacher hydrogen engines for emergency backup power in the Netherlands, a market where the Dutch government has invested in approximately 300 kilometers of repurposed natural gas pipeline to carry hydrogen.

  Even in Europe, where policy is generally more supportive of hydrogen, practical constraints are slowing adoption. In cases where excess renewable energy could theoretically feed electrolyzers to produce green hydrogen for storage, directing that surplus power into battery energy storage systems is often more efficient and cost-effective for backup needs. Niche opportunities may exist at data centers adjacent to industrial hydrogen producers such as refineries, but those sites are limited in number.

  ## Renewable diesel: drop-in option, limited uptake

  Renewable diesel, produced by hydrotreating waste animal fats and vegetable oils, is chemically similar to petroleum diesel and is approved for use in many diesel generator sets by manufacturers including Caterpillar and Cummins. It can reduce lifecycle carbon emissions by up to 90 percent compared with conventional diesel. Despite those attributes, it remains a niche choice in data center backup fleets due to supply availability and higher cost relative to conventional diesel.

  Microsoft pledged in 2020 to eliminate diesel from its facilities by 2030. The rapid expansion of AI infrastructure has since prompted some operators, including Microsoft, to revisit sustainability and net-zero timelines.

  ## Outlook

  Natural gas and biogas fuel cells are assessed as best positioned to scale in the data center power mix in the near term, particularly as behind-the-meter capacity that can be deployed faster than utility grid connections. Hydrogen engines and hydrogen fuel cells are expected to progress in niche or policy-supported contexts as infrastructure develops and costs decline. Renewable diesel remains a practical backup option where supply and pricing align, but broad displacement of conventional diesel is not anticipated in the near future.

  Bloom Energy's April 2026 Oracle agreement, covering up to 2.8 gigawatts of solid oxide fuel cell capacity aimed at higher-density AI workloads, is the most recent large-scale commercial commitment to the natural gas fuel cell model.
body_zh: |
  布盧姆能源於2026年4月擴大與甲骨文的合作，為後者的人工智慧資料中心提供最高2.8吉瓦的燃料電池容量，此為一系列大型商業協議中最新的一筆。這些協議已將天然氣與沼氣固態氧化物燃料電池確立為近期最有可能規模化的柴油備用發電替代方案，而氫能發電技術則仍侷限於試點項目與利基型部署。

  ## 布盧姆能源的商業動能

  根據雙方說明，此次甲骨文協議旨在以比傳統電力解決方案更快的部署時程，支援更高密度的AI工作負載。在此之前，布盧姆已先後宣布與Equinix及Compass Datacenters的合作，並於2025年10月與資產管理公司布魯克菲爾德（Brookfield）簽訂50億美元戰略夥伴協議，加速AI導向設施的燃料電池容量建置。布魯克菲爾德AI基礎設施全球負責人希坎德爾·拉希德（Sikander Rashid）在2025年10月的新聞稿中表示：「表後電力解決方案對於彌補AI工廠的電網缺口至關重要。」

  布盧姆的固態氧化物燃料電池系統以天然氣、沼氣或氫氣為燃料，無需燃燒即可發電。表後（behind-the-meter）模式讓業者得以在場址直接安裝發電設備，跳過因AI基礎設施需求急劇成長而大幅延長的電網接入排隊程序。

  ## 氫燃料電池試點尚未擴大為實際部署

  微軟是資料中心採用氫燃料電池最具代表性的倡議者。一套由微軟與Plug Power合作開發、裝設於兩個40英尺貨櫃中的3百萬瓦氫燃料電池系統，部署於紐約州拉瑟姆（Latham）的一處設施內，成功讓一座資料中心持續運行兩天，驗證了取代柴油的可行性。另一項與愛爾蘭能源公司ESB合作的試驗，則在微軟都柏林園區提供最高250千瓦的電力。加州、懷俄明州、北卡羅來納州、猶他州及德州的資料中心也陸續完成或正在推進氫燃料電池部署，布盧姆能源與巴拉德電力系統（Ballard Power Systems）均為主要供應商。

  儘管已有上述示範案例，大規模部署仍未能隨之而來。荷蘭NorthC Datacenters於2022年在格羅寧根（Groningen）設施安裝了一套據稱是歐洲首套500千瓦氫燃料電池備用系統，然而此後數年間，類似規模的安裝案例仍寥寥可數。

  彭博新能源財經（BloombergNEF）執行長喬恩·摩爾（Jon Moore）在2026年3月接受《Energy Transition Insider》訪問時表示：「氫能投資於2023年達到高峰。」

  ## 美國政策退場撤銷氫能共燃規定

  2024年中，美國環境保護署（EPA）就化石燃料發電廠的溫室氣體標準發布最終法規，刪除了草案中原本提議的氫能共燃要求——即在燃燒式發電中將氫氣混入天然氣的做法。早期草案曾考慮要求發電廠於2032年前以30%的體積比混燃氫氣，並在2038年前進一步提高至96%。這些要求最終均未納入正式法規。

  工程公司柏恩斯麥克唐納（Burns & McDonnell）技術經理梅根·魯瑟（Megan Reusser）表示：「氫能之所以被排除在外，是因為其生產成本高昂，且美國在氫能的生產、運輸及儲存方面的基礎設施極為有限。」

  美國主要發電機製造商，包括開拓重工（Caterpillar）和Generac，均未積極投入氫氣引擎產品的研發。除工業煉油廠外，氫氣難以在資料中心所需的規模下廣泛取得，配送方面亦需要大量管線與儲存基礎設施的投資。安全性與許可問題構成額外障礙，就目前而言，美國資料中心備用電源應用中的氫氣引擎，似乎仍僅限於實驗性或實驗室規模的部署。

  ## 氫氣引擎方案存在，但規模有限

  引擎製造商英尼奧詹巴赫（Innio Jenbacher）提供可混燃氫氣與天然氣的引擎，在氫氣供應有限的場址可支援最高10百萬瓦，並供應100%純氫氣機組，容量最高達1百萬瓦，3百萬瓦機型亦已列入規劃。該公司宣布獲得NorthC Datacenters訂單，將為其在荷蘭的緊急備用電源安裝六台雙氣型詹巴赫氫氣引擎——荷蘭政府已投資將約300公里的廢棄天然氣管線改建，用於輸送氫氣。

  即便是在政策整體上較支持氫能的歐洲，實際限制仍在拖慢採用速度。在多餘再生能源理論上可饋入電解槽生產綠氫以供儲能的情境下，將這些剩餘電力導入電池儲能系統，往往在備用電源需求上更具效率與成本效益。緊鄰煉油廠等工業氫氣生產者的資料中心或許存在利基機會，但此類場址的數量相當有限。

  ## 再生柴油：即插即用的選項，但普及率有限

  再生柴油以廢棄動物脂肪及植物油為原料，經加氫處理後製成，化學性質與石油柴油相近，已獲開拓重工和康明斯（Cummins）等多家製造商認可，可用於其柴油發電機組。與傳統柴油相比，再生柴油可將生命週期碳排放降低最高90%。儘管具備上述優點，由於供應可及性有限且成本相對較高，在資料中心備用電源機群中仍屬利基選擇，難以廣泛普及。

  微軟於2020年承諾在2030年前淘汰旗下設施的柴油使用。此後，AI基礎設施的快速擴張促使包括微軟在內的部分業者重新審視其永續發展與淨零時間表。

  ## 展望

  天然氣與沼氣燃料電池被評估為近期在資料中心電力組合中最有條件規模化的技術，尤其是作為可比公用電網接入更快速部署的表後容量。氫氣引擎與氫燃料電池預計將在利基或政策支持的情境下持續推進，並隨著基礎設施日趨完善及成本下降逐步發展。再生柴油在供應與定價條件配合的情況下仍是實用的備用選項，但預計短期內不會大規模取代傳統柴油。

  布盧姆能源2026年4月與甲骨文簽訂的協議，涵蓋旨在支援更高密度AI工作負載的最高2.8吉瓦固態氧化物燃料電池容量，是天然氣燃料電池模式迄今最新的大規模商業承諾。
