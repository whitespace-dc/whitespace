body_en: |
  Taiwan Power Company (Taipower) Chairman Tseng Wen-sheng said on August 24 that the utility plans to supply artificial intelligence data centers directly from individual power plants via dedicated transmission lines, with AI data center operators managing their own connections and distance and cost determined by market forces.

  Tseng made the remarks during an interview on the Digital Taiwan television program, addressing rising public debate over the siting of AI data centers in Taiwan. Under the proposed arrangement, Taipower would construct and extend the dedicated line while the data center operator would assume autonomous management of that connection. "The most stable power supply method is to be as close to the power source as possible," Tseng said.

  ## From blackout response to AI-era backbone

  The direct-supply model has its origins in Taipower's response to a major island-wide blackout on March 3, 2022. Following that event, Tseng said, Taipower reviewed its operations and produced a series of plans to strengthen grid resilience, the most consequential of which was a program to supply electricity directly from power plants to science parks, bypassing the shared transmission backbone.

  Previously, electricity from generating stations flowed first onto the main north-south trunk transmission line before being distributed to end customers. Concerned about congestion on that backbone and the need to spread supply risk, Taipower began deploying direct routes from individual plants to industrial customers. Tseng cited two examples: a line from Datan Power Plant running directly to Taoyuan Aerotropolis, and a line from Tonghsiao Power Plant running directly to Hsinchu Science Park.

  Tseng said this resilience program "unexpectedly" aligned with the subsequent surge in AI-related power demand, and has since become the primary transmission approach for semiconductor and technology companies. Taipower's supply planning has consequently shifted from tracking aggregate electricity volume across the island to precise, site-by-site matching of large power users with specific grid nodes and generating sources, with Taipower now monitoring conditions at each individual facility and science park.

  ## Market-driven siting, not regulatory mandates

  Tseng outlined how Taipower's approach to AI data centers, known in the industry as AI Data Centers or AIDCs, differs from regulatory models applied elsewhere. Globally, he said, controls on AIDC development include location restrictions, price controls, capacity thresholds, and requirements that operators self-generate power. Taipower does not plan to impose full self-generation requirements, but is offering the direct-supply framework as a reference for prospective AIDC developers choosing where to locate.

  Where past policy relied on location controls set administratively, Tseng said the future approach should be market-driven. "I'll pull a dedicated line; you manage it yourself — the distance, cost, and timing are determined by market mechanisms," he said, describing the terms Taipower expects to negotiate with prospective AIDC customers.

  ## Grid capacity and underground infrastructure

  Tseng said AI-driven load growth will require Taipower to upgrade the grid by a full tier, increasing voltage levels and expanding cable capacity as part of a broader renewal program. Taipower has recently proposed a plan to renew underground grid infrastructure, which Tseng said will require active cooperation from local governments. Shared utility conduits in urban areas, a concept long discussed but not fully implemented in Taiwan's city planning, would need to be realized to support the new development, he said.

  ## Supply adequacy and geopolitical framing

  Responding to public concern about whether Taiwan has enough power to meet projected demand, Tseng said Taipower recently exchanged information with the Taiwan Semiconductor Industry Association on power consumption trends and has a clear picture of future industrial load. The Ministry of Economic Affairs' Energy Administration has also published a National Power Supply and Demand Report estimating electricity requirements over the next ten years, incorporating semiconductor plant expansions into its projections, and Taipower has planned supply capacity ahead of those estimates. Tseng said supply is adequate.

  Tseng framed electricity infrastructure as a "sovereign industry," saying Taiwan must develop power supply resilience in the context of geopolitical pressures the island faces.

  On the demand management side, Tseng noted that expanding solar photovoltaic capacity in the greater Taipei metropolitan area could reduce the volume of electricity that needs to be transmitted into the region from distant generators during summer peak periods, easing pressure on the transmission network. Taipower's underground grid renewal plan, requiring local government cooperation, is among the next concrete steps the utility has put forward.
body_zh: |
  台灣電力公司（台電）董事長曾文生8月24日表示，台電計畫透過電廠直供專線，直接為人工智慧（AI）資料中心供電。在此架構下，AI資料中心業者將自行管理連線，線路距離與費用則由市場機制決定。

  曾文生是在接受《數位台灣》電視節目專訪時作出上述表示，回應社會各界對於AI資料中心在台選址議題的高度關注。依照台電規劃，台電負責興建與延伸專屬線路，資料中心業者則自主管理該連線。「最穩定的供電方式，就是盡量靠近電源。」曾文生說。

  ## 從全台大停電到AI時代的輸電主幹

  直供模式的緣起，可追溯至台電對2022年3月3日全台大停電的應變。曾文生表示，那次事件後，台電全面檢討營運作業，提出一系列強化電網韌性的計畫，其中最關鍵的一項，就是從電廠直接供電給科學園區，跳過共用的南北縱貫輸電主幹。

  過去，電廠發出的電力，須先匯入南北縱貫主輸電線，再逐一配送給終端用戶。台電為解決主幹壅塞問題、分散供電風險，開始推動從個別電廠直接拉線至工業用戶的直供路線。曾文生舉出兩個實例：一條從大潭電廠直接延伸至桃園航空城，另一條則從通霄電廠直接通往新竹科學園區。

  曾文生指出，這套韌性強化計畫「意外地」與後來AI相關用電需求的大幅攀升不謀而合，如今已成為半導體及科技業的主要輸電模式。台電的供電規劃也因此從追蹤全台整體用電量，轉型為精準的逐點配對——將大型用電戶與特定電網節點及發電來源一一對應，並針對每座廠房與科學園區進行個別監控。

  ## 市場導向選址，而非行政管制

  曾文生進一步說明，台電對AI資料中心（業界通稱AIDC）的處理方式，與其他地區所採行的管制模式有所不同。他表示，國際上對AIDC發展的管控措施，涵蓋地點限制、價格管制、容量門檻，以及要求業者自行發電等規定。台電目前不打算強制要求業者完全自發自用，而是以直供框架作為有意在台設立AIDC的業者選址時的參考依據。

  曾文生表示，過去的政策仰賴行政機關設定地點管制，未來的方向則應回歸市場機制。「我來拉專線；你自己管——距離、費用、時程，都由市場機制決定。」他說，這是台電預計與AIDC潛在客戶協商的條件。

  ## 電網容量與地下化基礎設施

  曾文生表示，AI帶動的用電成長，將迫使台電將電網提升整整一個等級，包括拉高電壓等級、擴充電纜容量，作為全面更新計畫的一部分。台電近期已提出地下電網基礎設施更新計畫，曾文生強調，此計畫需要各地方政府積極配合。他表示，長期以來在台灣都市規劃中討論卻尚未全面落實的「共同管道」概念，必須付諸實現，才能支撐新一波的建設需求。

  ## 供電充裕與地緣政治框架

  針對社會大眾對台灣是否有足夠電力因應預期需求的疑慮，曾文生表示，台電近期已與台灣半導體產業協會就用電趨勢進行資訊交流，對未來產業用電負載已有清楚掌握。經濟部能源署也已發布「全國電力供需報告」，估算未來十年的用電需求，並將半導體廠的擴產計畫納入預測，台電的供電規劃更已超前於上述估算。曾文生表示，供電無虞。

  曾文生將電力基礎設施定位為「主權產業」，強調台灣必須在面對地緣政治壓力的背景下，致力強化電力供應的韌性。

  在需求管理方面，曾文生指出，若能在大台北都會區擴大推動太陽光電裝置容量，夏季尖峰時段就能減少自遠端電廠向北輸送的電量，從而紓解輸電網路的壓力。台電地下電網更新計畫——仍需仰賴地方政府合作——則是台電目前已提出的具體下一步之一。
