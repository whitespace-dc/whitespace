body_en: |
  Phinergy and the Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centres have completed a formal demonstration of a 72kW/1.4MWh aluminium-air generator (AAG) module at a partner facility near Madison, Wisconsin, marking a key step in an industry-backed programme to validate the technology as a diesel alternative for data centre backup power.

  The test, observed by a delegation from the Hub, confirmed the functionality of all subsystems in the scaled-up module. Technical representatives from Microsoft, Danfoss, Schneider Electric and Vertiv attended, and the parties agreed on next steps for the validation and scale-up programme, according to the announcement.

  The 72kW/1.4MWh module — roughly capable of supplying a small commercial building's peak electrical demand for about 19 hours — is intended as the building block for a larger 500kW/10MWh first-of-a-kind system now being assembled. That system would be large enough to provide sustained backup supply for a medium-scale data centre load.

  ## How the aluminium-air generator works

  Phinergy's system generates electricity using aluminium as a fuel source that the company describes as recyclable. The technology is distinct from lithium-ion batteries — it draws oxygen from the air to react with aluminium plates, producing power without combustion and without the emissions or noise associated with diesel generators. The company said the system is designed to provide on-site reserve power, reducing operator reliance on diesel while maintaining backup supply for critical infrastructure.

  ## Selection and consortium structure

  Phinergy and the Hub began working together in 2025. The Hub is a consortium led by APL Data Centres, Danfoss, Data4, Engie, Google, Microsoft, Schneider Electric and Vertiv — a grouping that spans hyperscale cloud operators, data centre developers and infrastructure equipment suppliers.

  The AAG was selected for MW-scale validation from more than 70 submissions received in response to a global request for information the Hub issued for low-carbon backup power solutions. The Hub's model requires data centre operators and technology suppliers to define technical requirements jointly before moving to larger-scale validation, a process intended to ensure emerging technologies meet operational standards before significant capital is committed.

  ## Why diesel alternatives are in demand

  Diesel generators remain the dominant form of backup power across the data centre sector, but operators are under growing pressure to reduce local emissions and noise. Grid constraints and rising electricity demand driven by cloud computing and artificial intelligence workloads have added urgency to the search for alternatives, according to the announcement.

  The Hub said its collaborative model is designed to reduce barriers to adoption by pooling resources across member companies, rather than leaving each organisation to test emerging technologies independently. That approach is intended to accelerate decisions on whether a technology can advance from pilot stage to commercial deployment.

  Beyond emergency backup, the announcement points to a secondary application: the system could be used during periods of grid constraint to reduce dependence on external electricity supply while keeping operations running. The Hub noted this may appeal to operators facing difficulties securing timely power access for new facilities.

  ## Industry significance

  Aluminium-air systems are one of several technologies under evaluation as the sector looks to move away from diesel, alongside battery storage, hydrogen-based generation and alternative fuels. Each option carries trade-offs around duration, infrastructure requirements, cost and regulatory approval, the announcement noted.

  For a new energy technology, formal validation endorsed by hyperscalers and large infrastructure companies carries commercial weight. Operators typically require evidence that a system can be integrated into existing operating models and meet reliability standards before committing to deployment across large data centre estates.

  "Reaching AAG Module stage is a defining achievement in the AAG's commercialization roadmap," said Emmanuel Levy, Chief Executive Officer of Phinergy. "The AAG Module design has now demonstrated, through a formal validation process endorsed by hyperscalers and leading data center operators, that it meets the technical standards required for data center operation, and we move forward with confidence toward full AAG system validation and deployment."

  Alberto Ravagni, Chief Executive Officer of the Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centres, described the test as confirming aluminium-air as "a promising clean backup power technology" and said it demonstrates how the Hub's collaborative model accelerates adoption of new solutions.

  The next stage is full validation of the 500kW/10MWh system currently being assembled.

  ## Phinergy與資料中心聯盟在威斯康辛完成72kW鋁空氣備用模組驗證

  Phinergy與「資料中心淨零創新中心」（Net Zero Innovation Hub for Data Centres）日前在威斯康辛州麥迪遜（Madison）附近的合作場址，完成了一次72kW／1.4MWh鋁空氣發電機（AAG）模組的正式示範測試，為業界支持的柴油替代技術驗證計畫樹立了重要里程碑。

  本次測試由淨零創新中心代表團全程觀摩，確認了這一放大規格模組中所有子系統的運作功能。來自微軟（Microsoft）、Danfoss、Schneider Electric與Vertiv的技術代表均出席觀測，各方並就後續驗證與擴大規模計畫達成共識。

  這個72kW／1.4MWh模組的供電能力，大致相當於為一棟中小型商業建築提供約19小時的尖峰用電需求，將作為一套容量更大的500kW／10MWh同類首創系統的基本構建單元。該系統目前正在組裝中，建成後足以為中等規模資料中心持續供應備用電力。

  ## 鋁空氣發電機的運作原理

  Phinergy的系統以鋁作為燃料來源發電，並強調鋁具有可回收再利用的特性。這項技術有別於鋰離子電池——它從空氣中汲取氧氣，與鋁板發生反應以產生電力，整個過程無需燃燒，也不會產生柴油發電機所帶來的廢氣或噪音。該公司表示，此系統設計用於提供現場備用電力，能降低運營商對柴油的依賴，同時維持關鍵基礎設施的備用供電能力。

  ## 遴選過程與聯盟架構

  Phinergy與淨零創新中心自2025年起展開合作。該中心是一個由APL Data Centres、Danfoss、Data4、Engie、Google、微軟、Schneider Electric與Vertiv共同領導的聯盟，成員橫跨超大規模雲端業者、資料中心開發商與基礎設施設備供應商。

  鋁空氣發電機能獲選進行百萬瓦規模驗證，是從該中心針對低碳備用電力解決方案發布全球資訊徵詢後收到的逾70份提案中脫穎而出的。該中心的運作模式要求資料中心業者與技術供應商在進入大規模驗證前，共同確立技術需求，以確保新興技術在投入大量資本之前，已達到實際運營標準。

  ## 柴油替代方案為何需求殷切

  柴油發電機目前仍是資料中心業界最主流的備用電力形式，但各家業者正面臨日益強烈的壓力，必須降低在地排放量與噪音。由雲端運算與人工智慧工作負載所驅動的電網限制與用電需求攀升，更加速了業界尋找替代方案的迫切性。

  淨零創新中心表示，其協作模式旨在透過整合成員企業的資源，降低技術導入門檻，避免各組織各自為政、單獨測試新興技術。此舉有助於加速判斷一項技術能否從試驗階段邁向商業部署。

  除緊急備用電力外，聲明中也提及一項次要應用場景：當電網受到限制時，此系統可用於降低對外部電力供應的依賴，同時維持設施正常運作。淨零創新中心指出，這一特性對於在新設施取得及時電力接入上面臨困難的業者而言，可能具有相當吸引力。

  ## 產業意義

  鋁空氣系統是業界在推動告別柴油過程中，正在評估的數種技術之一，其他方案還包括電池儲能、氫能發電及替代燃料等。每種選項在供電時長、基礎設施需求、成本與法規審核等方面各有取捨。

  對於一項新能源技術而言，獲得超大規模雲端業者與大型基礎設施公司背書的正式驗證，具有相當重要的商業分量。業者通常需要確認某套系統能整合至現有運營模式、並符合可靠性標準，才會決定在大型資料中心資產組合中進行大規模部署。

  Phinergy執行長Emmanuel Levy表示：「達到AAG模組階段，是鋁空氣發電機商業化路徑上的決定性成就。AAG模組設計如今已透過一套由超大規模業者及頂尖資料中心運營商背書的正式驗證流程，證明其符合資料中心運營所需的技術標準，我們對於推進完整AAG系統驗證與部署工作充滿信心。」

  淨零創新中心執行長Alberto Ravagni形容此次測試印證了鋁空氣技術是「一項頗具潛力的潔淨備用電力技術」，並表示這充分展現了該中心協作模式如何加速新解決方案的導入。

  下一個階段，將是對目前正在組裝中的500kW／10MWh完整系統進行全面驗證。
