body_en: |
  US electricity demand is projected to rise 39% by 2035 from 2026 levels, but consulting firm ICF warned in a new report that the country's ability to deliver power through its transmission networks, rather than its ability to generate it, has become the binding constraint facing grid planners, utilities, and data center developers.

  The report projects demand will climb 21% by 2030 and 39% by 2035, driven by data centers, industrial expansion, electrification of transportation, and electric heating. Peak demand is expected to increase 25% over the same period. ICF framed the challenge in terms of deliverability: "The central question is no longer just how much electricity demand is forecast, but how much demand can realistically be served — and where," the firm wrote.

  ## Reserve margins tightening

  ICF estimates the US currently holds roughly 26 gigawatts (GW) of generating capacity above the minimum required to maintain grid reliability, about 3% of total installed capacity. That reserve margin is projected to shrink to approximately 20 GW by 2030.

  In two of the country's largest power markets, that buffer has already largely disappeared. PJM Interconnection, which operates the grid serving 13 Mid-Atlantic and Midwest states, and ERCOT (Electric Reliability Council of Texas), which manages the Texas grid, have both seen reserve margins erode as load growth outpaced new capacity additions. "As load growth has outpaced resource additions, any pre-existing excess capacity has quickly been absorbed," the ICF report said.

  ICF projects 445 GW of new generation additions through 2030, spanning solar, energy storage, wind, and natural gas. The firm notes that much of this pipeline remains in development, creating a timing mismatch between when demand materializes and when new infrastructure can come online.

  ## The transmission constraint

  Transmission, the high-voltage network that moves electricity from power plants to local distribution systems, sits at the core of the problem ICF identifies. US investor-owned utilities are projected to spend approximately $178 billion on transmission projects between 2025 and 2028. The US Department of Energy has backed plans for roughly 7,500 miles of new transmission lines by 2030.

  ICF warned that capital commitments alone are insufficient. "Large capital plans will not translate into timely relief in the highest-growth areas without execution that navigates supply chain, siting, permitting, labor, and other challenges," the firm wrote.

  Rob Gramlich, president of Grid Strategies, a power sector advisory firm, said the ICF report captures a problem grid planners are confronting nationwide. "I agree, I think we mainly have a grid delivery problem," Gramlich told Data Center Knowledge. "It's not the only issue, but expanding transmission delivery capacity would help meet demand growth more than any other single action."

  Gramlich described the constraint as geographically broad. "It is acute everywhere that we have significant power demand including Texas, the Mid-Atlantic, Midwest, Southeast, Great Plains, and much of the West," he said.

  Jigar Shah, former director of the US Department of Energy's Loan Programs Office, agreed. "This is what everyone is saying," Shah told Data Center Knowledge. "We don't have a generation problem, we have a wires problem."

  ## Pressure on data center markets

  The consequences are most visible in data center development, where the pace of load growth has made transmission access a primary variable in site selection. Developers are broadening their geographic searches, targeting locations with available transmission capacity, and engaging utilities earlier on energization schedules and interconnection timelines, according to the report. ICF notes that in some markets, transmission access now shapes project schedules as much as land, fiber, or capital availability.

  ICF projects ERCOT demand will rise 53% from 2025 levels by 2035. In PJM, the firm identified mounting transmission constraints around Northern Virginia as well as in emerging data center markets in Illinois and Ohio. Both regions are expected to record annual load growth exceeding 5% through the next decade.

  ## Near-term options

  Because transmission development moves more slowly than large-load interconnection, utilities and grid operators are exploring interim approaches. ICF pointed to phased energization plans, flexible interconnection agreements, conditional service agreements, and grid-enhancing technologies as tools that could allow major loads to come online before major transmission upgrades are completed.

  Shah said extracting more capacity from existing infrastructure is the most immediate path forward. "The best way to solve it is to get more out of the wires we have already paid for with batteries, demand flexibility, and grid enhancing technologies," he said.

  For data center developers specifically, ICF said, the question of power access has shifted from how much generation exists nationally to whether local transmission infrastructure can deliver it.

  ## ICF報告警告：美國輸電網路無法跟上電力需求激增的腳步

  顧問公司ICF在一份最新報告中預測，美國電力需求將於2035年前較2026年水準成長39%，但同時示警：制約電網規劃業者、電力公司與資料中心開發商的關鍵障礙，已不再是發電量，而是將電力送達用戶端的輸電網路。

  報告預測，電力需求將於2030年前成長21%，並於2035年前累計成長39%，驅動力來自資料中心擴張、工業成長、交通運輸電氣化以及電力暖氣的普及。同期的尖峰需求預計也將上升25%。ICF以「可送達性」框架詮釋這項挑戰，報告中寫道：「核心問題已不再只是預測用電需求有多少，而是實際上能供應多少需求——以及供應到哪裡。」

  ## 備轉容量持續收緊

  ICF估計，美國目前持有約26吉瓦（GW）的超額發電裝置容量，即高於維持電網可靠度所需最低標準的部分，約占總裝置容量的3%。這一備轉容量預計將於2030年前收縮至約20 GW。

  在美國兩個最大的電力市場中，這道緩衝空間實際上已大致消耗殆盡。PJM互聯電網負責服務中大西洋與中西部共13個州的電網，德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）則管理德州電網，兩者的備轉容量都因為用電成長速度超過新增裝置容量而快速侵蝕。ICF報告指出：「由於用電成長持續超過電源新增速度，原本存在的超額容量已被迅速吸收殆盡。」

  ICF預測，2030年前將有445 GW的新增發電量陸續投入，涵蓋太陽能、儲能、風電與天然氣。報告也指出，這些在建項目大多仍處於開發階段，導致需求實現的時間點與新基礎設施完工上線之間出現落差。

  ## 輸電瓶頸

  輸電線路——亦即將電力從發電廠輸送至地區配電系統的高壓網路——正是ICF所指出問題的核心所在。預計美國民營電力公司將於2025至2028年間投入約1,780億美元用於輸電建設。美國能源部也支持在2030年前新建約1萬2,000公里輸電線路的計畫。

  ICF警告，光有資本承諾是不夠的。報告寫道：「若無法克服供應鏈、選址、許可審查、勞動力及其他挑戰，龐大的資本計畫並無法及時紓解需求成長最快地區的壓力。」

  電力產業顧問公司Grid Strategies總裁羅伯·格拉姆利奇（Rob Gramlich）表示，ICF的報告精準捕捉到電網規劃業者正在全美各地面對的難題。他向《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）表示：「我認同這個判斷，我們面對的主要是電網輸送問題。這不是唯一的課題，但擴大輸電容量，是所有單一措施中最能有效應對需求成長的一項。」

  格拉姆利奇形容，這項瓶頸在地理上相當廣泛。「在所有用電需求顯著的地方都很嚴峻，包括德州、中大西洋、中西部、東南部、大平原，以及西部大部分地區，」他說。

  美國能源部貸款計畫辦公室前主任吉嘉爾·沙赫（Jigar Shah）也持相同看法。「這是所有人都在說的事，」沙赫向《資料中心知識》表示，「我們沒有發電問題，我們有的是電線問題。」

  ## 資料中心市場承壓

  這場危機的衝擊在資料中心開發領域最為明顯——用電成長的速度，已使輸電接取能力成為選址決策的首要變數。ICF報告指出，開發商正在擴大選址的地理搜尋範圍，鎖定輸電容量充裕的地點，並更早介入與電力公司的溝通，釐清供電時程與併網時間表。ICF也指出，在部分市場中，輸電接取能力對專案時程的影響，已不亞於土地、光纖或資金的可得性。

  ICF預測，ERCOT的電力需求將於2035年前較2025年水準成長53%。在PJM方面，報告點名北維吉尼亞州的輸電瓶頸日益嚴峻，伊利諾州與俄亥俄州等新興資料中心市場也面臨同樣困境，兩個地區預計在未來十年內，年均用電成長率都將超過5%。

  ## 近期可行方案

  由於輸電建設的推進速度遠不及大型用電戶完成併網所需的時間，電力公司與電網運營商正在探索過渡期的替代方案。ICF點出幾項工具，包括分階段供電計畫、彈性併網協議、條件性服務協議，以及電網強化技術，這些措施有望讓大型用電戶在主要輸電升級完工前便能順利上線。

  沙赫表示，從現有基礎設施中挖掘更多容量，是目前最立竿見影的路徑。他說：「最好的解法，就是透過電池、需求彈性，以及電網強化技術，讓我們已經付錢建好的電線發揮更大效益。」

  對於資料中心開發商而言，ICF指出，電力取得的關鍵問題已從「全國發電量夠不夠」，轉移為「當地輸電基礎設施能不能將電力送到」。
