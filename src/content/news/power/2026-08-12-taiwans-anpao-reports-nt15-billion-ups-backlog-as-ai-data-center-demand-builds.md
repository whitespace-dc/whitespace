body_en: |
  Taiwan's Anpao (安葆), a supplier of uninterruptible power supplies (UPS) and related power infrastructure services, reported a backlog of nearly NT$15 billion (New Taiwan dollars) with order visibility extending through 2028, the company said, as accelerating AI data center construction and semiconductor factory expansions drive demand for backup power, distribution controls, and system integration.

  ## Financial results

  Anpao posted first-half 2026 revenue of NT$3.177 billion, gross profit of NT$626 million, and operating profit of NT$422 million. Net profit attributable to the parent company reached NT$316 million, with earnings per share of NT$6.85. Gross margin was approximately 19.7% and operating margin approximately 13.3% for the period.

  The company attributed improving profitability to a better product mix and cost controls, including a centralized procurement strategy for raw materials.

  ## Backlog composition and customers

  Anpao said backlog growth is driven by two factors: semiconductor customers expanding factories both domestically and overseas, and AI data centers requiring high-reliability power infrastructure. Products in demand include backup power generation, UPS systems, power distribution controls, and full system integration. The company said its primary customers are large semiconductor manufacturers.

  Of the current backlog, approximately 60% consists of domestic projects and more than 40% are overseas orders. International orders are concentrated in the United States and Germany.

  ## AI data center segment

  Anpao said revenue from AI data center (AIDC) projects remains concentrated in the domestic market. As AIDC deployments grow in scale, the company said, requirements for power stability and backup capacity have risen, increasing demand for UPS systems, backup generation, and distribution infrastructure.

  ## Capacity expansion in Taiwan

  Anpao is simultaneously expanding manufacturing capacity at two locations in Taiwan. A second factory in Tainan began construction in the second half of 2026 and is expected to begin module production in 2027. The company is also one of the tenants entering the Pingtung Science Park "Semiconductor Supply Chain Zone," a facility anchored by a leading semiconductor manufacturer alongside multiple supply-chain partners. Anpao plans to build a new factory within that zone, with groundbreaking scheduled for the first quarter of 2027.

  ## Singapore subsidiary

  Anpao's board approved on August 11, 2026 the establishment of a second subsidiary in Singapore, with an initial capital contribution of US$100,000. The company said the entity will be used to expand overseas business and broaden its customer base, extending its international footprint beyond existing operations in the United States and Germany.

  The combination of a backlog that extends to 2028 and new manufacturing capacity coming online in Tainan from 2027 and Pingtung from 2027 onwards indicates that Anpao expects demand from semiconductor and AI data center customers to remain elevated well beyond its current order book.
body_zh: |
  台灣不斷電系統（UPS）及相關電力基礎設施服務供應商安葆，公告目前在手訂單接近新台幣150億元，訂單能見度延伸至2028年。公司表示，AI資料中心建設提速與半導體工廠擴產，帶動備援電力、配電控制及系統整合需求持續攀升。

  ## 財務表現

  安葆公告，2026年上半年合併營收新台幣31.77億元，毛利6.26億元，營業利益4.22億元；歸屬母公司淨利3.16億元，每股盈餘6.85元。同期毛利率約19.7%，營益率約13.3%。

  公司表示，獲利能力改善主要來自產品組合優化及成本控管，具體措施包括推行原物料集中採購策略。

  ## 在手訂單組成與客戶結構

  安葆指出，訂單成長主要受兩大因素驅動：半導體客戶在國內外持續擴廠，以及AI資料中心對高可靠度電力基礎設施的需求攀升。需求最為旺盛的產品涵蓋備援發電機組、不斷電系統、電力配送控制設備及整套系統整合服務。公司表示，主要客戶為大型半導體製造商。

  就現有在手訂單結構而言，國內專案約占六成，海外訂單逾四成，國際訂單主要集中於美國與德國。

  ## AI資料中心業務

  安葆表示，AI資料中心（AIDC）相關專案的營收，目前仍以國內市場為主。隨著AIDC建置規模持續擴大，客戶對電力穩定性與備援容量的要求同步提高，進而帶動不斷電系統、備援發電設備及配電基礎設施的需求持續增加。

  ## 台灣產能擴充

  安葆目前正同步在台灣兩處廠區擴增製造產能。位於台南的第二座工廠已於2026年下半年正式動工，預計2027年起開始投入模組生產。此外，安葆也是屏東科學園區「半導體供應鏈專區」的進駐廠商之一；該專區由一家半導體龍頭大廠主導，同時有多家供應鏈夥伴聯袂進駐。安葆計畫於該專區內新建廠房，預計2027年第一季動工。

  ## 新加坡子公司

  安葆董事會於2026年8月11日決議通過，核准在新加坡設立第二家子公司，初始資本額為10萬美元。公司表示，此一子公司將用於拓展海外業務、擴大客戶群，在現有美國及德國據點之外，進一步深化國際布局。

  在手訂單能見度延伸至2028年，加上台南廠預計自2027年起陸續開出新產能、屏東廠亦於同年跟進，顯示安葆研判半導體與AI資料中心客戶的需求，將在現有訂單簿之外持續維持高檔。
