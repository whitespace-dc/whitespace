body_en: |
  Indonesia's Presidential Advisory Council has proposed that the government conduct upstream geothermal exploration directly, arguing that the current arrangement — in which electricity tariffs are fixed before private developers know what a site can produce — creates a structural mismatch that halts projects and suppresses development.

  Hatta Rajasa, Vice Chairman of the Dewan Pertimbangan Presiden (Presidential Advisory Council, abbreviated Wantimpres), made the proposal at the National Energy Forum 2026 in Jakarta on Wednesday, November 25.

  Under the existing framework, private developers awarded geothermal working areas must conduct and fund their own exploration to determine a field's resource potential. Because electricity selling prices are set at the beginning of a project — before that drilling is complete — the agreed tariff may no longer match the actual cost or output profile revealed by exploration. When that happens, developers either abandon the project or request a price revision.

  "When he does geothermal drilling exploration, he sees that there is a mismatch, then he stops and asks for a price increase. That's where it gets stuck, nothing happens," Hatta said, as quoted by Bisnis.com.

  ## Government-led exploration as a de-risking model

  Hatta proposed that the government assume the upstream exploration phase — establishing a field's resource size and viability before offering the project to investors. Under this model, private developers would enter at the construction and generation stage, building and operating a geothermal power plant (Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Panas Bumi, or PLTP) against a dataset the state has already produced. Electricity pricing negotiations would then rest on verified resource information rather than pre-exploration estimates, removing the main trigger for stoppages and tariff-renegotiation requests.

  Hatta described this separation of the high-uncertainty exploration phase from the investment and development phase as the mechanism for delivering the economic certainty that investors currently lack at the outset.

  ## Indonesia's geothermal resource base

  Indonesia holds approximately 23 gigawatts (GW) of geothermal potential — a measure of the maximum power generation capacity that the country's geothermal resources could support at full development — but Hatta said utilization has yet to reach a level commensurate with that endowment. He framed the acceleration of new and renewable energy (Energi Baru dan Terbarukan, or EBT) development, with geothermal as a central component, as a pillar of national energy independence strategy and a path to reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

  ## What the proposal would change

  If adopted, shifting the financial exposure of early-stage geothermal exploration from private developers to the government would represent a structural change to how Indonesia advances its geothermal pipeline. The proposal was put forward at a public forum and had not been announced as formal government policy as of Wednesday. Hatta stated that the scheme is intended to give investors greater economic certainty, with the government providing resource data before any project agreement is reached.
body_zh: |
  印尼總統諮詢委員會（Dewan Pertimbangan Presiden，簡稱Wantimpres）提議，由政府直接執行地熱上游探勘工作，認為現行制度安排——在私人開發商尚未掌握場址實際產能之前便已鎖定電力售價——造成一種結構性落差，導致計畫停擺、整體開發受阻。

  Wantimpres副主席哈塔·拉賈薩（Hatta Rajasa）於11月25日（週三）在雅加達舉行的2026年全國能源論壇上提出上述建議。

  依據現行框架，獲得地熱作業區授權的私人開發商，必須自行進行並資助探勘作業，以確定該地區的資源潛力。由於電力售價是在計畫初期——即鑽探工程完成之前——便已議定，原先談定的費率，往往無法反映探勘後實際揭示的成本或產出狀況。一旦發生這種情況，開發商不是放棄計畫，就是要求重新議價。

  「當他進行地熱鑽探探勘時，發現出現了落差，於是停下來、要求漲價。就卡在這裡了，什麼事都推不動，」哈塔說，引述自Bisnis.com的報導。

  ## 以政府主導探勘作為降低風險的模式

  哈塔提議由政府承擔上游探勘階段——在將計畫向投資人開放之前，先行確定地熱田的資源規模與開發可行性。在此模式下，私人開發商將在建設與發電階段進場，依據政府已完成的資料集，興建並營運地熱發電廠（Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Panas Bumi，簡稱PLTP）。電力定價談判將以經過驗證的資源資訊為依據，而非探勘前的預估數字，從而消除導致計畫停滯與費率重議的主要根源。

  哈塔將這種把高度不確定性的探勘階段與投資開發階段分開處理的做法，描述為給予投資人所欠缺的經濟確定性的核心機制。

  ## 印尼的地熱資源基礎

  印尼擁有約23吉瓦（GW）的地熱潛力——此數字代表在全面開發條件下，印尼地熱資源所能支撐的最大發電容量——但哈塔表示，目前的開發利用程度，尚未達到與這一資源稟賦相稱的水準。他將加速新能源與再生能源（Energi Baru dan Terbarukan，簡稱EBT）的開發、以地熱作為核心組成部分，定位為國家能源自主戰略的支柱，以及降低對化石燃料依賴的重要路徑。

  ## 此提議將帶來哪些改變

  若獲採納，將地熱早期探勘階段的財務風險從私人開發商轉移至政府，將代表印尼推進地熱開發計畫方式的結構性轉變。該提議係於公開論壇上提出，截至週三為止，尚未宣布成為正式政府政策。哈塔表示，此方案旨在給予投資人更高的經濟確定性，由政府在任何計畫協議達成之前，便先行提供完整的資源資料。
