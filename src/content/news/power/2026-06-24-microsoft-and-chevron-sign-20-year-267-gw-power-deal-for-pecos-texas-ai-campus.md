body_en: |
  Microsoft and Chevron have signed a 20-year power supply agreement for a natural gas generation complex near Pecos, Texas, that will provide up to 2.67 gigawatts (GW) of dedicated electricity to a new Microsoft data center campus targeting 2 GW of compute capacity.

  The arrangement, called Project Kilby, pairs a Chevron-led generation development with a co-located Microsoft facility in what the companies describe as a behind-the-meter deal — meaning power flows directly to the data center without passing through the public electricity grid. Chevron said it expects to make a final investment decision by the end of 2026, with first power delivery targeted for 2028.

  ## Structure and partners

  The generation facility will be developed by Energy Forge One, a Chevron subsidiary, alongside investment firm Engine No. 1. Most of the generation capacity will come from gas turbines supplied by GE Vernova, with additional capacity from Solar Turbines, a subsidiary of Caterpillar. Chevron said the project will use non-potable, brackish groundwater for operations.

  The campus is sited in the Permian Basin, where Chevron already operates extensive natural gas production infrastructure, allowing it to supply both fuel and power to the project. The project uses a phased, modular construction approach, meaning generation and compute capacity will be added incrementally.

  ## Scale and economics

  Microsoft said the Pecos campus will add approximately 2 GW of data center capacity over the next five to seven years, and that the development is expected to create more than 6,000 construction jobs and hundreds of permanent operational positions.

  The agreement formalizes discussions that were disclosed in April 2026. Roughly two months elapsed between that disclosure and the signed agreement. Microsoft said it would invest billions of dollars in the campus over the development period but did not specify a project-level figure. Earlier this year the company said it had earmarked $190 billion for overall capital expenditure, of which $25 billion is allocated for data center components.

  ## Why Texas, why now

  Microsoft already operates data centers in San Antonio and has announced expansion plans in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. The company said the Pecos campus is intended to serve customers including startups, governments, healthcare providers, and educational institutions. "Meeting this demand requires not only more data center capacity, but capacity that is predictable, resilient and able to scale quickly. The data center campus in Pecos enables us to deliver on that need," the company said in a statement.

  Noelle Walsh, Microsoft's president of cloud operations and innovation, said the company's approach is to "build where our customers need us, and we build for the long term."

  For Chevron, the project creates a long-term offtaker for a multi-gigawatt power development in a location with ample fuel supply. "Our agreement with Microsoft through Project Kilby represents Chevron's unique ability to deliver power to AI customers with certainty, speed, and at a competitive cost," said Jeff Gustavson, president of Chevron New Energies. Gustavson cited Permian natural gas supply and what he called Chevron's "proven execution capabilities" as competitive advantages.

  Microsoft EVP and COO Carolina Dybeck Happe, in a LinkedIn post announcing the deal, described the partnership as a model that brings together technology, energy, capital, and local communities to support AI growth.

  ## Grid context

  Utilities across major data center markets have warned that large new electricity loads could face lengthy interconnection timelines — the process by which new generation or consumption connects to the public grid. Behind-the-meter arrangements allow developers to sidestep those queues by generating power on-site for a dedicated customer.

  The Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT), which manages the state's main grid, has raised questions in recent planning documents about how much announced AI demand will ultimately materialize, noting that many proposals lack firm commitments to power supply, equipment, or financing. Texas has attracted significant data center investment due to tax incentives, government support, and its energy sector, but that concentration has also placed pressure on existing power infrastructure.

  Project Kilby appears more advanced than many announced developments: Microsoft has executed a 20-year power agreement, turbine suppliers have been named, and the project has a defined customer, a generation plan, and a stated commercial timeline.

  Ihab Osman, an independent strategist focused on data center and mission-critical infrastructure, said the shift toward integrated energy-compute projects marks a structural change in how AI infrastructure is built. "The unit of AI development is no longer the data center; it is the energy-compute complex," Osman said. "The valuable unit is no longer the installed GPU or turbine nameplate. It is the deliverable megawatt that can continuously reach compute."

  ## Industry significance

  Project Kilby joins a group of developments that pair power generation with AI compute infrastructure in a single project. Similar efforts involving Crusoe, Lancium, ExxonMobil, and NextEra Energy reflect increasing competition to secure power, fuel, land, and permits alongside computing capacity, according to Data Center Knowledge.

  Chevron said Project Kilby will begin delivering power in 2028, contingent on the final investment decision it expects to reach by year-end 2026.
body_zh: |
  微軟與雪佛龍簽署為期20年的電力供應協議，將在德州皮科斯附近建設一座天然氣發電設施，為微軟新資料中心園區提供最高2.67吉瓦（GW）的專屬電力，園區目標算力容量達2吉瓦，是美國迄今公開宣布規模最大的能源與算力整合案之一。

  此案名為「基爾比計畫」（Project Kilby），以雪佛龍主導的發電開發案搭配微軟的共址資料中心設施，兩家公司將其定位為「表後」（behind-the-meter）供電方案——意即電力直接輸送至資料中心，不經過公共電網。雪佛龍表示，預計在2026年底前做出最終投資決策，首批供電目標定於2028年。

  ## 架構與合作夥伴

  發電設施將由雪佛龍旗下子公司 Energy Forge One 聯合投資公司 Engine No. 1 共同開發。發電容量大部分來自 GE Vernova 提供的燃氣渦輪機，其餘則來自開拓重工（Caterpillar）旗下子公司 Solar Turbines。雪佛龍表示，本計畫將使用非飲用的鹽鹼地下水供應日常營運。

  園區選址於二疊紀盆地（Permian Basin），雪佛龍在當地早已擁有大規模天然氣生產基礎設施，可同時為本計畫供應燃料與電力。整體計畫採分階段、模組化施工方式，發電與算力容量將逐步擴增。

  ## 規模與經濟效益

  微軟表示，皮科斯園區預計在未來五至七年內新增約2吉瓦的資料中心容量，整體開發預計創造逾6,000個施工就業機會及數百個永久性營運職位。

  本協議正式確立了2026年4月對外披露的洽談進展，從首次披露到正式簽約歷時約兩個月。微軟表示將在開發期間投入數十億美元，但未具體說明個別計畫的投入金額。今年稍早，微軟曾宣布整體資本支出預算達1,900億美元，其中250億美元專門用於資料中心相關設施。

  ## 為何選擇德州、為何是現在

  微軟已在聖安東尼奧（San Antonio）運營資料中心，並宣布擴展達拉斯─沃思堡（Dallas-Fort Worth）地區的計畫。微軟表示，皮科斯園區的目標客群包括新創公司、政府機關、醫療機構及教育單位。微軟在聲明中指出：「滿足這項需求，不僅需要更多資料中心容量，還需要可預測、具韌性、且能快速擴展的容量。皮科斯的資料中心園區讓我們得以實現這一目標。」

  微軟雲端營運與創新事業部總裁諾艾爾・沃爾什（Noelle Walsh）表示，公司的策略方針是「在客戶需要的地方建設，且著眼長遠」。

  對雪佛龍而言，本計畫為其在燃料供應充裕地點所開發的多吉瓦電力項目，提供了長期穩定的電力買方。雪佛龍新能源事業部總裁傑夫・古斯塔夫森（Jeff Gustavson）表示：「我們與微軟透過基爾比計畫達成的協議，體現了雪佛龍以確定性、速度和具競爭力的成本，向AI客戶供電的獨特能力。」古斯塔夫森將二疊紀天然氣供應以及雪佛龍的「成熟執行能力」列為競爭優勢。

  微軟執行副總裁兼營運長卡洛琳娜・迪貝克・哈珀（Carolina Dybeck Happe）在 LinkedIn 發文宣告此合作時，將這項合夥關係描述為「匯聚科技、能源、資本與在地社群，共同支撐AI成長的示範模式」。

  ## 電網背景

  多個主要資料中心市場的電力公司警告，大型新增電力需求恐面臨漫長的電網併網等候時程——即新建電源或新增用電設施接入公共電網所需的行政與工程流程。表後供電方案讓開發商得以繞過這些排隊程序，直接在場址自行發電，專供指定客戶使用。

  管理德州主要電網的德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）在近期規劃文件中提出疑慮，認為已公告的AI需求未必會全數實現，並指出許多提案缺乏對電力供應、設備或融資的確定性承諾。德州憑藉稅收優惠、政府支持與蓬勃的能源產業吸引了可觀的資料中心投資，但高度集中的態勢也對現有電力基礎設施形成壓力。

  基爾比計畫顯然比許多已宣布的開發案更為成熟：微軟已完成20年電力協議的簽署，渦輪機供應商已明確，且具備明確的客戶對象、發電規劃與商業時程。

  專注資料中心及關鍵任務基礎設施的獨立策略顧問伊哈布・奧斯曼（Ihab Osman）表示，朝向能源與算力整合型計畫的轉變，標誌著AI基礎設施建設模式的結構性變革。「AI開發的基本單元已不再是資料中心，而是能源─算力複合體，」奧斯曼說，「具備價值的單元不再是已安裝的GPU或渦輪機銘牌容量，而是能持續穩定輸送至算力設施的可交付百萬瓦。」

  ## 產業意義

  基爾比計畫已加入一批將電力發電與AI算力基礎設施整合於單一開發案的行列。根據《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）的報導，Crusoe、Lancium、埃克森美孚（ExxonMobil）及 NextEra Energy 等企業的類似布局，反映出業界競相搶佔電力、燃料、土地與許可證，同時鞏固算力容量的激烈競爭態勢。

  雪佛龍表示，基爾比計畫將於2028年開始供電，前提是在2026年底前完成最終投資決策。
