  ## WSP says power now drives 35-40% of US revenue as backlog hits record CA$20.1B

  WSP Global, the Canadian engineering and professional services firm, reported that power and energy now accounts for approximately 35% to 40% of its US revenue, up from a near-negligible share five years ago when transportation and infrastructure generated about 80% of US revenue, CEO Alexandre L'Heureux said on an Aug. 6 second-quarter earnings call.

  The shift reflects structural changes in capital spending across the US economy, with L'Heureux citing power and energy, data centers, advanced manufacturing, nuclear, water, defense, and critical minerals as the strongest demand areas. "The strongest areas of demand we see today are directly linked to long-term-duration investment themes," he said.

  ## Financial results

  Revenue for the quarter ended June 26 rose 20% to CA$5.4 billion (approximately US$3.9 billion) from CA$4.5 billion in the same period a year earlier. Backlog — the value of contracted work not yet completed — climbed 23% to a record CA$20.1 billion from CA$16.3 billion, representing 11.6 months of revenue.

  Net income fell 12% to CA$246.1 million from CA$279.4 million a year earlier. WSP attributed the decline to higher acquisition and integration expenses, larger unrealized derivative losses, and increased amortization and depreciation costs that more than offset improved operating performance.

  ## Acquisitions expand utility reach

  WSP's purchases of POWER Engineers and TRC Companies — two US-focused engineering firms with established utility client bases — have enlarged the company's footprint among American power infrastructure owners. Net revenue from WSP's 40 largest global power clients increased 30% year over year. Hard backlog, meaning contracted revenue with high certainty of execution, from those clients in the US rose 20%.

  "I'm very impressed with the scale of the bids that we're pursuing right now," L'Heureux said.

  ## Data centers, water, and nuclear

  Data center revenue grew more than 20% year over year in the first half of 2026, while the data center sales pipeline expanded approximately 30% year over year. Water revenue increased 20%, and the water opportunity pipeline surged 61%, driven by investment in aging infrastructure, PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances, a class of synthetic chemicals requiring remediation) contamination cleanup, water quality mandates, and climate resilience work. WSP is also supporting 22 prospective US nuclear sites, providing site selection, licensing, design, and construction support services.

  CFO Alain Michaud said the company continues to see "accelerating momentum in the U.S." Asked by an analyst whether any US sectors were weighing on growth, L'Heureux acknowledged timing delays in some project awards but said there were no broader deterioration signals. "Our other sectors are performing as planned at this point, so we don't have any disappointments," he said.

  ## Industry significance

  WSP's revenue mix shift illustrates how the buildout of power infrastructure — spanning grid upgrades, data center connections, and new generation — is redirecting engineering services spending away from traditional categories such as transportation. For engineering and construction firms with utility relationships, the pipeline of work tied to electrification, AI-driven data center expansion, and nuclear licensing is generating multi-year contracted backlogs that underpin near-term revenue visibility.

  WSP's hard backlog from its top US power clients stood 20% higher year over year as of the second quarter, pointing to continued near-term work volumes even as net income pressure from integration costs persists.

  ## WSP表示電力業務現佔美國營收35至40%，積壓訂單創歷史新高達201億加元

  加拿大工程與專業服務公司WSP Global公布，電力與能源業務目前約佔其美國營收的35%至40%，相較於五年前幾乎可忽略不計的比重已大幅躍升——彼時交通與基礎設施業務約佔美國營收的80%。執行長Alexandre L'Heureux於8月6日第二季財報電話會議上作出上述說明。

  這一結構性轉變，反映了美國經濟體資本支出布局的深層變化。L'Heureux點名電力與能源、資料中心、先進製造、核能、水務、國防及關鍵礦產，是當前需求最為強勁的領域。他表示：「我們現在所看到的最強勁需求領域，直接連結於具備長期持續性的投資主題。」

  ## 財務成果

  截至6月26日止當季，WSP營收年增20%至54億加元（約39億美元），去年同期為45億加元。積壓訂單——即已簽約但尚未完工的業務價值——年增23%，攀升至創歷史紀錄的201億加元，高於去年同期的163億加元，相當於11.6個月的營收量。

  淨利則年減12%，從去年同期的2.794億加元降至2.461億加元。WSP將此歸因於更高的收購與整合費用、較大幅度的未實現衍生性商品損失，以及增加的攤銷與折舊成本，上述不利因素合計超過了營運績效改善所帶來的效益。

  ## 收購案擴大公用事業客群

  WSP先後收購POWER Engineers與TRC Companies——這兩家以美國市場為主、擁有成熟公用事業客戶基礎的工程公司——使其在美國電力基礎設施業主之間的業務版圖大幅擴張。WSP全球前40大電力客戶的淨營收年增30%，而來自這些客戶在美國的已確認積壓訂單（即具高度執行確定性的合約營收）亦年增20%。

  L'Heureux表示：「我對我們目前正在追求的投標案規模印象非常深刻。」

  ## 資料中心、水務與核能

  2026年上半年，資料中心營收年增逾20%，資料中心銷售管線亦年增約30%。水務營收成長20%，水務機會管線更大幅躍增61%，主要由老化基礎設施的更新投資、全氟及多氟烷基物質（PFAS，即一類需要進行整治的合成化學物質）污染清除工程、水質法規要求，以及氣候韌性相關工程所共同驅動。此外，WSP目前正支援22個美國核能潛力廠址，提供選址、許可審查、設計及建設支援等服務。

  財務長Alain Michaud表示，公司持續看到「美國市場加速成長的動能」。針對分析師詢問美國是否有任何產業板塊拖累整體成長，L'Heureux承認部分專案的授予時程出現延誤，但表示並未見到更廣泛的惡化訊號。「我們其他業務板塊目前表現均符合計畫，因此沒有任何令人失望之處，」他說道。

  ## 產業意義

  WSP營收結構的轉變，清楚呈現出電力基礎設施建設浪潮——涵蓋電網升級、資料中心連結，以及新型發電設施的開發——如何將工程服務支出的重心，從交通等傳統類別逐步轉移。對於擁有公用事業客戶關係的工程與營建公司而言，與電氣化、人工智慧驅動的資料中心擴建，以及核能許可審查相關的工程管線，正在形成跨越多年的合約積壓訂單，為短期營收能見度提供了有力支撐。

  截至第二季，WSP來自美國頂尖電力客戶的已確認積壓訂單較去年同期高出20%，顯示儘管整合成本持續對淨利形成壓力，短期業務量仍可望維持穩健。
