body_en: |
  Three-quarters of the clean electricity capacity the United States was projected to add under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) — the 2022 law that extended and created tax credits for clean energy — will still reach the grid despite the One Big Beautiful Bill Act's (OBBBA) tax credit phaseouts and regulatory rollbacks, according to a study released by the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.

  The report, authored by Lily Bermel, a former research associate at MIT's Center for Energy and Environmental Policy Research and now a visiting fellow at the Columbia Center on Global Energy Policy, compared clean electricity additions, fossil electricity output, and emissions reductions under an IRA trajectory against projections under the OBBBA scenario. Averaged over 2025 through 2035 and relative to 2021 levels, the OBBBA preserves 67% to 74% of the clean energy and emissions reduction benefits the IRA would have delivered, the study concluded.

  ## Onshore wind bears the heaviest losses

  Of all technologies affected, onshore wind faces the steepest decline. The report projects that only 52% of the generation and 47% of the capacity expected under the IRA will be preserved under the OBBBA — the worst outcome among all technology categories examined. Bermel attributed this to onshore wind's high sensitivity to the loss of tax credits.

  The OBBBA moved up the phaseout deadlines for wind and solar projects seeking to qualify for the 48E investment tax credit and the 45Y production tax credit — two key incentives under the IRA. Wind and solar facilities were required to commence construction by July 4 to safe harbor their eligibility for those credits, or face a placed-in-service deadline of December 31, 2027. Some analysts said they anticipate a rise in pricing for wind and solar power purchase agreements — long-term contracts locking in electricity prices — now that the deadline has passed, to compensate for the absence of the credits.

  Other clean technologies face less exposure under the OBBBA. Battery storage and geothermal projects retain the ability to qualify for 100% of the 48E and 45Y credits through 2034. Utility-scale solar and battery storage preserve at least 80% of IRA-projected deployment. Battery storage specifically maintains 83% of its expected capacity buildout, aided by continued tax credit access. Solar's resilience stems from falling module costs, which "absorb most of the policy change shock," the report found, with Bermel concluding that solar is "credit-sensitive but not credit-dependent."

  Offshore wind is less affected than its onshore counterpart because its deployment is "driven by other forces" beyond tax credit sensitivity, the report said.

  ## Supply-side constraints cap both scenarios equally

  The study's central finding is that the gap between IRA and OBBBA outcomes is structurally bounded — not primarily by tax credit policy, but by the physical pace at which clean energy can be permitted, sited, built, and interconnected to the grid. Both scenarios face the same supply-side ceiling, Bermel wrote, meaning the IRA trajectory itself was unlikely to have been fully realized even without the OBBBA.

  "Alleviating supply-side barriers — primarily through permitting reform and transmission buildout — would lift that ceiling, rapidly increasing deployment speed and lowering costs," the report stated. By contrast, restoring wind and solar tax credits "would only recover about 2.5 years of lost solar and storage deployment each year, moving deployment toward its existing ceiling rather than raising it."

  Permitting reform negotiations have repeatedly stalled in Congress. Some Democratic lawmakers have moved to obstruct bipartisan permitting legislation in response to the Trump administration's restrictions on renewable energy projects, including offshore wind farms.

  ## Fossil generation rises; coal retirements slow

  While fossil fuel capacity "barely diverges between scenarios," fossil generation is projected to run 19% higher under the OBBBA scenario, as utilization of existing plants increases. Coal retires more slowly under the OBBBA than it would have under the IRA trajectory's environmental regulations. Gas capacity is actually 3% higher under the IRA trajectory than under the OBBBA, the report said.

  In both the IRA and OBBBA scenarios, the model projects essentially no new nuclear, geothermal, or hydropower capacity. Wind, solar, and storage account for all new clean capacity additions — and therefore all of the projected shortfall between the two scenarios.

  ## Near-term deployment faces additional pressure

  Beyond the legislative changes in the OBBBA, a coordinated set of executive-branch actions — including permitting freezes, stop-work orders, investigations, tariffs, and impoundments — pushes near-term real deployment below even the OBBBA scenario projections, the report said.

  The report's conclusion frames permitting reform and transmission buildout, rather than tax credit restoration, as the primary lever for accelerating clean energy deployment — since supply-side constraints represent the binding limit in either policy environment.
body_zh: |
  根據麻省理工學院（MIT）發布的一項研究，美國在《降低通膨法》（Inflation Reduction Act，IRA）——這部2022年通過、延伸並新設潔淨能源稅額抵免的法律——框架下原本預計新增的潔淨電力裝置容量，四分之三仍將接入電網，不因《一個美麗大法案》（One Big Beautiful Bill Act，OBBBA）的稅額抵免退場安排與法規鬆綁而無緣實現。

  該報告由前MIT能源與環境政策研究中心研究助理、現任哥倫比亞大學全球能源政策中心訪問研究員Lily Bermel執筆，比較了IRA情境與OBBBA情境下的潔淨電力新增量、化石燃料發電量及減排成效。研究結論指出，以2025年至2035年均值對比2021年基準，OBBBA保留了IRA原本可帶來的潔淨能源與減排效益的67%至74%。

  ## 陸域風電承受最重損失

  在所有受影響的技術類別中，陸域風電面臨最陡峭的跌幅。報告預測，IRA情境下預期發電量的52%與預期裝置容量的47%，將在OBBBA情境下得以保留——這是所有受檢視技術類別中最差的結果。Bermel將此歸因於陸域風電對稅額抵免取消的高度敏感性。

  OBBBA提前了風電與太陽能專案取得48E投資稅額抵免及45Y生產稅額抵免資格的截止期限——這兩項是IRA的核心激勵措施。風電與太陽能設施須在7月4日前開工，方可取得上述稅額抵免的安全港資格，否則須在2027年12月31日前完成商業運轉。由於截止期限已過、稅額抵免取消，部分分析師預計風電與太陽能購電協議——鎖定長期電價的合約——的報價將會走揚，以彌補稅額抵免缺失所帶來的成本差距。

  其他潔淨能源技術在OBBBA下的受衝擊程度相對較低。電池儲能與地熱專案仍可在2034年前取得100%的48E及45Y稅額抵免資格。公用事業規模的太陽能與電池儲能，保留了IRA預測部署量的至少80%。電池儲能具體而言維持了預期裝置容量建設的83%，有賴持續的稅額抵免支撐。太陽能的韌性則源於持續下降的模組成本，報告指出這些成本「吸收了大部分的政策衝擊」，Bermel的結論是太陽能「對稅額抵免敏感，但不依賴稅額抵免」。

  離岸風電受到的衝擊低於陸域風電，原因在於其部署「受其他力量驅動」，而非僅憑稅額抵免敏感性，報告如此表示。

  ## 供給端制約同等壓制兩種情境

  該研究的核心發現是：IRA與OBBBA成果之間的差距在結構上是有界的——主要制約並非來自稅額抵免政策，而是潔淨能源取得許可、完成選址、建設竣工及併入電網的實際物理速度。Bermel指出，兩種情境面臨相同的供給端上限，這意味著即便沒有OBBBA，IRA的發展軌跡本身也不太可能完全兌現。

  報告指出：「消除供給端障礙——主要透過許可改革與輸電建設——將拉高這道上限，迅速提升部署速度並降低成本。」相較之下，恢復風電與太陽能稅額抵免「每年僅能彌補約2.5年的太陽能與儲能部署損失，只是讓部署趨近現有上限，而非拉高上限本身。」

  許可改革談判在國會屢屢陷入僵局。部分民主黨議員為回應川普政府對再生能源專案（包括離岸風電場）的限制措施，開始阻撓跨黨派許可立法的推進。

  ## 化石燃料發電增加；燃煤除役放緩

  儘管化石燃料裝置容量「在兩種情境之間幾乎沒有差異」，OBBBA情境下的化石燃料發電量預計比IRA情境高出19%，原因是現有電廠的使用率提升。在OBBBA情境下，燃煤電廠的除役速度比IRA軌跡的環境法規情境下更為緩慢。值得一提的是，燃氣裝置容量在IRA情境下實際上比OBBBA情境高出3%。

  在IRA與OBBBA兩種情境下，模型均預測幾乎不會有新增的核電、地熱或水力發電裝置容量。風電、太陽能與儲能涵蓋所有新增潔淨電力裝置容量——也因此構成兩種情境之間所有預測缺口的來源。

  ## 近期部署面臨額外壓力

  除OBBBA的立法變動外，一系列協調一致的行政部門行動——包括許可凍結、停工命令、調查、關稅與扣押——正將近期實際部署量壓低至OBBBA情境預測值以下，報告如此指出。

  報告的結論表明，無論在哪種政策環境下，供給端制約才是加速潔淨能源部署的真正瓶頸，因此許可改革與輸電建設——而非恢復稅額抵免——才是加速潔淨能源部署的主要著力點。
