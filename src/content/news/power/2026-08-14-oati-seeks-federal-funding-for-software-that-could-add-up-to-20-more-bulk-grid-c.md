  ## OATI applies for DOE funding for transmission software initiative

  Minnesota-based OATI, the company behind 95% of U.S. bulk power transactions, has applied to the U.S. Department of Energy for grant funding for PowerNow, a software-based initiative it says could unlock 10% to 20% more capacity on the bulk transmission grid without building new infrastructure.

  OATI filed its application earlier this summer for a portion of the DOE's $1.9 billion SPARK program, which funds line reconductoring — the replacement of aging wires with higher-capacity conductors — and other advanced transmission technologies. The DOE says it expects to notify applicants of selection decisions later this month, with formal award announcements beginning in October.

  ## Three-lever approach

  PowerNow targets grid capacity through three mechanisms, according to OATI. The first is automated, near-real-time coordination between neighboring transmission operators, replacing data exchanges that currently happen on weekly, daily, or hourly cycles with near-instantaneous updates. The second is software-based dynamic line ratings — a technology that continuously adjusts the rated capacity of a transmission line based on actual environmental and operating conditions, rather than fixed conservative limits set to cover worst-case scenarios. The third is AI-enhanced dispatch of flexible resources, which OATI says can respond to grid needs faster than conventional power generation.

  Kevin Sarkinen, OATI's chief operations officer, said a single line constraint can have an outsized effect on overall grid availability. A study OATI conducted ahead of its SPARK application found that changing a single line's rating limit can shift total capacity availability by 100% to 200%, depending on what is flowing on that line, Sarkinen said. Individual lines, he said, could see capacity gains of hundreds of megawatts — equivalent to the output of a large power plant.

  ## Coordinated deployment required

  OATI says the inter-regional coordination component produces its greatest benefit when neighboring grid operators adopt it at the same time, rather than one at a time. To support that approach, the company has assembled a coalition that includes the California Independent System Operator, the New York Independent System Operator, the Southwest Power Pool, PacifiCorp, Duke Energy, and Florida Power and Light, among others.

  ## Industry context

  PowerNow is one of several software-based efforts emerging to expand headroom on the bulk power system as data centers and other large electricity consumers compete for limited interconnection capacity. GridCARE, a California-based startup, said in March 2026 that it would help National Grid free up capacity in New York, following an earlier pilot with Portland General Electric in Oregon in which it said it unlocked more than 80 megawatts of additional headroom.

  The SPARK program's selection notifications are expected before the end of August 2026, with awards beginning in October.

  ## OATI向能源部申請輸電軟體計畫補助

  總部位於明尼蘇達州的OATI，是美國95%大宗電力交易背後的技術推手，日前已向美國能源部（DOE）申請PowerNow計畫的補助資金。該公司表示，這項以軟體為核心的方案，在無需新建基礎設施的前提下，最多可為大型輸電網路釋出10%至20%的額外容量。

  OATI於今年夏天稍早提交申請，爭取DOE旗下規模達19億美元的SPARK計畫部分資金。SPARK計畫主要資助「線路換導線」工程——即以高容量新導線取代老化線路——以及其他先進輸電技術。DOE表示，預計本月稍晚將通知申請人審核結果，正式的補助公告則將於10月起陸續發布。

  ## 三管齊下

  根據OATI說明，PowerNow透過三項機制提升電網容量。第一項是鄰近輸電業者之間的自動化近即時協調，將目前以週、日或小時為單位的資料交換，升級為近乎即時的資訊更新。第二項是軟體驅動的動態線路額定容量技術，能依據實際環境與運作條件持續調整輸電線路的額定容量，而非沿用以最壞情境為基準的固定保守限值。第三項是AI強化的彈性資源調度，OATI表示，其反應速度比傳統發電機組更快。

  OATI營運長Kevin Sarkinen表示，單一線路的容量限制對整體電網可用量的影響往往遠超預期。他說，OATI在申請SPARK計畫前所進行的一項研究發現，視該線路當下的電力流量而定，僅調整單一線路的額定上限，就能使整體容量可用量變動100%至200%。他指出，個別線路的容量提升幅度甚至可達數百MW，相當於一座大型電廠的發電量。

  ## 須協調同步部署

  OATI表示，跨區域協調機制的效益最大化，有賴鄰近電網業者同步採用，而非逐一導入。為支持這一策略，該公司已組建一個聯盟，成員包括加州獨立系統運營商（California ISO）、紐約獨立系統運營商（NYISO）、西南電力池（Southwest Power Pool）、太平洋電力公司（PacifiCorp）、杜克能源（Duke Energy）以及佛羅里達電力與照明公司（Florida Power and Light）等。

  ## 產業背景

  隨著資料中心及其他大型用電戶爭相搶進有限的併網容量，業界正湧現多項以軟體為基礎的方案，試圖為大型電力系統釋出更多容量餘裕，PowerNow正是其中之一。加州新創公司GridCARE於2026年3月宣布，將協助國家電網（National Grid）在紐約釋出電網容量；此前，GridCARE已在奧勒岡州與波特蘭通用電力公司（Portland General Electric）完成先導計畫，據稱額外釋出超過80 MW的容量餘裕。

  SPARK計畫的審核通知預計將於2026年8月底前發出，補助案則將於10月起陸續公布。
