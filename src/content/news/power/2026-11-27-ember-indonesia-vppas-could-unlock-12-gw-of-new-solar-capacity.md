  ## Ember Report Finds VPPA Mechanism Could Add 12 GW of Solar in Indonesia

  Allowing Indonesian companies to buy electricity directly from new solar projects through virtual power purchase agreements (VPPAs) — contracts in which a company and a renewable energy developer agree on a fixed price, with the financial difference settled between them while the developer continues selling physical power to the grid — could unlock 12 gigawatts (GW) of additional solar capacity, according to a new report from Ember, a global energy think tank.

  Twelve gigawatts is eight times Indonesia's total installed solar capacity of 1.5 GW at the end of 2025, Ember said. The finding is significant because Indonesia launched a 100 GW solar program in August 2026, targeting completion by 2029 — a scale that Ember's Asia Energy Analyst Shabrina Nadhila said the existing policy framework cannot support.

  ## The Policy Gap

  The current policy structure has been in place for more than a decade and produced only 1.5 GW of solar over that period, Nadhila said. Industrial and commercial electricity users want clean power and are willing to pay for it, but their current options are limited to renewable energy certificates — which function as tradeable credits representing a unit of renewable generation — sourced from geothermal and hydropower plants that average more than 20 years in age.

  That age matters. The RE100 initiative, a global corporate scheme that pushes member companies to use 100 percent renewable electricity, does not recognize certificates from plants older than 15 years. This means most of PLN's existing certificate supply — sales of which rose from 0.3 terawatt-hours (TWh) in 2021 to 6.4 TWh in 2025 — does not count toward corporate renewable energy targets.

  Ember estimated that renewable energy met only 8 percent of large industrial and commercial electricity demand in Indonesia in 2025. Under a VPPA framework, that share could rise by 12 percentage points through the addition of 12 GW of solar capacity connected to PLN's grid and industrial estate networks.

  ## How the VPPA Structure Works

  Under the mechanism Ember proposes, a company and a solar developer agree on a fixed contract price. The developer sells the physical electricity to PT PLN (Persero), Indonesia's state-owned utility, as it does today. If the market price PLN pays differs from the contract price, the difference is settled financially between the company and the developer. PLN remains the sole buyer of electricity and operator of the grid, retaining its customers and tariff revenue.

  "The private sector in Indonesia has been pushing for cleaner electricity, and solar can grow fast enough to meet that demand. However, the current policy framework has not been able to keep up," Nadhila said in a statement on Thursday.

  Solar capacity built to serve industrial demand has already reached double the capacity built under PLN's feed-in tariff scheme — a government-set price at which PLN purchases power from generators — indicating private appetite without a formal corporate procurement channel.

  ## Competitive Pressures on Industry

  The demand for new solar is not only driven by voluntary sustainability goals. Exporters face emissions reduction requirements from global brands and from the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), which imposes a carbon cost on certain imports based on their production emissions. A survey of 105 companies cited in the Ember report found that 69 percent were considering relocating operations if Indonesia's power supply remained dependent on fossil fuels.

  ## Regional Comparisons

  Ember pointed to two neighbors as reference points. Vietnam permits direct electricity trading across the grid using a contract for difference mechanism — a structure similar in principle to the VPPA model — and expanded that program in 2026. Malaysia operates an 800 megawatt (MW) corporate VPPA program run through its state utility.

  The report also draws on India's experience, noting that India faced comparable constraints, including fragmented land ownership and limited financial headroom at its state utilities, yet reached 171 GW of installed solar capacity by September 2026. Ember cited India as the primary reference case for the reform package.

  ## Six Recommended Reforms

  The VPPA mechanism is one of six policy reforms Ember put forward. The report also recommends replacing PLN's government-set price caps for solar procurement with competitive auctions. In India, Chile, Germany, and South Africa, Ember said, comparable auction systems reduced contracted solar prices by between 44 and 87 percent.

  Additional recommendations include consolidating land access and project procurement under a single government body working alongside PLN, and reinstating net metering for rooftop solar — an arrangement that lets building owners sell surplus generation back to the grid — with protections for those sellers.

  Ember estimated that the proposed package could save the state budget 1.9 trillion Indonesian rupiah without materially altering PLN's financial position. The report also called for clear rules governing remuneration for battery storage systems.

  ## Significance

  If adopted, the VPPA framework would create a new channel for private capital to finance solar construction outside the existing PLN procurement process, without displacing PLN from its central role in the grid. The 100 GW solar target — which requires adding roughly 98.5 GW to Indonesia's current 1.5 GW base in approximately three years — cannot be reached under the current framework, Nadhila said, and governance reforms including new private-sector financing mechanisms are a prerequisite.

  The Ember report was published on November 26, 2026.

  ## Ember報告：VPPA機制可望為印尼新增12 GW太陽能容量

  根據全球能源智庫Ember發布的最新報告，若允許印尼企業透過虛擬購電協議（VPPA）直接向新太陽能案場採購電力——此類契約由企業與再生能源開發商議定固定電價，雙方就市場電價與契約電價之差額進行財務結算，開發商則繼續將實體電力售予電網——可望解鎖高達12吉瓦（GW）的額外太陽能裝置容量。

  Ember指出，12 GW相當於印尼截至2025年底太陽能裝置容量1.5 GW的八倍。這項發現意義重大，因為印尼已於2026年8月啟動100 GW太陽能計畫，目標於2029年前完成建置——Ember亞洲能源分析師Shabrina Nadhila表示，現行政策框架根本無法支撐如此規模的擴張。

  ## 政策缺口

  Nadhila指出，現行政策架構已施行逾十年，這段期間僅建置了1.5 GW的太陽能裝置容量。工商業用電戶有採用潔淨能源的意願，也願意為此付費，但目前的選項僅限於購買再生能源憑證——這是一種可交易的憑證，代表一定數量的再生能源發電量——而這些憑證的來源為地熱與水力發電廠，平均機組年齡超過二十年。

  機組年齡至關重要。RE100是一項全球企業倡議，要求成員企業達成百分之百使用再生電力的目標，但該倡議不承認超過十五年機組所產生的憑證。這意味著印尼國家電力公司（PLN）現有憑證供應的大部分均不計入企業再生能源目標——而PLN的憑證銷售量已從2021年的0.3兆瓦時（TWh）成長至2025年的6.4 TWh。

  Ember估計，2025年再生能源僅滿足印尼大型工商業用電需求的8%。若建立VPPA框架，透過在PLN電網及工業區電網新增12 GW太陽能裝置容量，可望使這一比例提升12個百分點。

  ## VPPA架構運作方式

  依照Ember所提出的機制，企業與太陽能開發商議定固定契約電價。開發商如同現行做法，將實體電力售予印尼國營電力公司PT PLN（Persero）。若PLN支付的市場電價與契約電價之間存在差額，則由企業與開發商進行財務結算。PLN仍維持其電力唯一買方及電網營運者的角色，保留其客戶群與電費收入。

  Nadhila於週四發表聲明指出：「印尼私部門一直在推動更潔淨的電力，太陽能的成長速度足以滿足這項需求。然而，現行政策框架一直未能跟上。」

  為因應工業用電需求而建置的太陽能容量，已達PLN躉購費率方案——即政府設定的PLN向發電業者收購電力之價格——下建置容量的兩倍，顯示在缺乏正式企業採購管道的情況下，民間需求依然旺盛。

  ## 產業面臨競爭壓力

  推動新建太陽能的動力，不僅來自自願性的永續發展目標。出口業者面臨來自國際品牌及歐盟碳邊境調整機制（CBAM）的減排要求——CBAM依據進口產品的生產排放量徵收碳費。Ember報告引用了一項涵蓋105家企業的調查，發現若印尼電力供應持續依賴化石燃料，69%的受訪企業正考慮將營運遷往其他地區。

  ## 區域比較

  Ember以兩個鄰國作為參照。越南允許透過電網進行直接電力交易，採用差額契約機制——其原理與VPPA模式相近——並於2026年擴大了該方案的規模。馬來西亞則透過其國營電力公司，運行一個規模達800百萬瓦（MW）的企業VPPA方案。

  報告同時援引印度的經驗，指出印度曾面臨相似的制約條件，包括土地所有權分散及州立電力公司財務空間有限，但印度至2026年9月仍達到了171 GW的太陽能裝置容量。Ember將印度列為本次改革方案的主要參照案例。

  ## 六項建議改革措施

  VPPA機制是Ember提出的六項政策改革之一。報告同時建議，以競爭性拍賣制度取代現行PLN太陽能採購的政府設定價格上限。Ember指出，在印度、智利、德國及南非，類似的拍賣制度使太陽能簽約電價降低了44%至87%不等。

  其他建議措施包括：由單一政府機構與PLN協作，整合土地取得與案場採購流程；以及恢復屋頂太陽能的淨計量電表制度——允許建築所有人將多餘發電量回售電網——並為賣方提供相應保障。

  Ember估計，上述改革方案可為國家預算節省1.9兆印尼盾，且不會對PLN的財務狀況造成實質影響。報告亦呼籲制定明確規範，規範電池儲能系統的報酬機制。

  ## 重要意義

  若付諸實施，VPPA框架將在現有PLN採購流程之外，開闢一條以私人資本挹注太陽能建設的新管道，同時不影響PLN在電網中的核心地位。Nadhila表示，100 GW太陽能目標需要在約三年內、於印尼現有1.5 GW基礎上再新增約98.5 GW，在現行框架下根本無法實現，包括引入民間融資機制在內的治理改革，是達成目標的先決條件。

  Ember報告於2026年11月26日發布。
