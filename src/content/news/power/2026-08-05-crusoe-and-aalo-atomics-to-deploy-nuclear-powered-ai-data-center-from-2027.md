body_en: |
  Crusoe Energy and Aalo Atomics announced a strategic partnership on 30 July to power a Crusoe Spark modular data center at Idaho National Laboratory (INL) in Idaho using Aalo's nuclear technology, with a target start date of 2027 and a stated goal of deploying 50-megawatt-electric (MWe) reactor units across Crusoe's broader data center portfolio by the end of 2029.

  The announcement marks Crusoe's second publicly disclosed nuclear partnership. It arrives as the pipeline of small modular reactor (SMR) capacity committed to data center operators has nearly doubled in roughly 18 months, according to the International Energy Agency.

  ## Aalo's reactor milestone and its regulatory limits

  Aalo achieved criticality on its Aalo-X reactor this summer at INL, becoming one of four companies to meet the deadline set under the Department of Energy's (DOE) Reactor Pilot Program, which was established by executive order. Criticality means the reactor sustained a controlled fission chain reaction — the engineering threshold that demonstrates a design works. It is not equivalent to a commercial operating license.

  Aalo chief executive Matt Loszak said the milestone positions the company for full-power operation of the 10 MWe Aalo-X and for future commercial deployment of its larger Aalo Pod product. "As one of only four companies to reach criticality by President Trump's deadline for companies in the DOE Reactor Pilot Program, we are now well positioned for not only the full power operation of our 10 MWe Aalo-X reactor, but also the deployment of the Aalo Pod to power commercial data centres," Loszak said.

  The DOE Reactor Pilot Program provided Aalo with access to INL's federal land and a regulatory pathway that runs alongside the Department of Energy rather than entirely through the Nuclear Regulatory Commission's (NRC) standard commercial licensing process. That pathway was designed to accelerate demonstration-scale testing on government property. Deploying Aalo's 50 MWe Pod at commercial, non-federal data center sites by 2029 will require NRC commercial licensing, a process that has historically taken years.

  ## Deal structure and economics

  Neither company has published contract pricing. The structure is phased: Crusoe is not purchasing reactors at the outset. The 2027 INL deployment, using the Spark modular data center format, functions as a live pilot before any commitment to deploy Aalo Pods and 50 MWe XMR plants across Crusoe's own campuses by the end of 2029.

  Cully Cavness, Crusoe's co-founder and chief strategy officer, described the rationale in terms of reliability and scale. "The next generation of AI infrastructure demands power that is reliable, abundant, clean and at scale, and Aalo's nuclear reactors are built to meet that need," Cavness said.

  Siting a reactor directly at a data center — a configuration known as behind-the-meter generation — would in principle protect Crusoe from the interconnection queues and transmission constraints that affect grid-connected power sources, including renewables acquired through power purchase agreements (PPAs). Whether those economics hold at scale depends on Aalo's ability to build and finance multiple 50 MWe units at commercial sites without the federal cost support built into the DOE pilot program. Capital cost overruns have ended more nuclear projects than technical failures, and factory-built modular reactors at the production volumes Aalo would need remain an unproven manufacturing model.

  ## Crusoe's broader power strategy

  The Aalo partnership is Crusoe's second publicly announced nuclear deal. The company previously partnered with modular nuclear developer Blue Energy on a project in Texas. Separately, Crusoe operates a large AI campus in Abilene, Texas, drawing on conventional power sources alongside infrastructure tied to Oracle and OpenAI's Stargate initiative. The company also has approximately 1.6 gigawatts of capacity under contract to Meta.

  That combination — gas and grid-connected capacity handling near-term demand while nuclear pilots run across more than one reactor vendor — describes a staged and diversified approach rather than a single-technology commitment.

  ## Industry context

  The SMR pipeline tied to data center operators has grown from 25 gigawatts at the end of 2024 to 45 gigawatts currently, according to the IEA. Other activity in the sector includes Rolls-Royce SMR advancing licensing in the United Kingdom and Czech Republic, ARC Clean Technology partnering with Deep Atomic to target North American data centers, and Google pursuing corporate PPAs with Kairos Power alongside the restart of the Duane Arnold nuclear plant.

  The 2027 demonstration and 2029 fleet-deployment timeline would be fast by the standards of nuclear construction. Whether it holds depends on the pace of NRC commercial site licensing, the availability of enriched fuel supply chains, and the willingness of capital markets to finance pre-revenue nuclear manufacturing at scale.

  此次宣布是Crusoe公開披露的第二項核能合作。根據國際能源總署（IEA）的資料，在過去約18個月內，承諾供給資料中心業者的小型模組化反應爐（SMR）容量幾乎翻倍。

  ## Aalo反應爐里程碑及其監管限制

  Aalo今夏在INL成功實現其Aalo-X反應爐的臨界狀態，成為四家在美國能源部（DOE）「反應爐試驗計畫」（Reactor Pilot Program）截止期限前達標的公司之一。該計畫由行政命令設立。所謂「臨界」，是指反應爐維持了可控的核分裂鏈式反應——這是一個能證明設計可行性的工程門檻，並不等同於商業運轉許可。

  Aalo執行長Matt Loszak表示，這項里程碑讓公司進入10 MWe Aalo-X反應爐的全功率運轉階段，並為其更大型的Aalo Pod商業部署奠定基礎。他說：「作為在川普總統為DOE反應爐試驗計畫設定的截止日期前達成臨界的四家公司之一，我們現在不僅在10 MWe Aalo-X反應爐的全功率運轉方面處於有利位置，也為向商業資料中心部署Aalo Pod做好了準備。」

  DOE反應爐試驗計畫為Aalo提供了進入INL聯邦土地的管道，並開闢了一條與能源部並行運作、而非完全走核能管制委員會（NRC）標準商業許可程序的監管路徑。該路徑旨在加速政府土地上示範規模的測試。若要在2029年前將Aalo的50 MWe Pod部署至商業性、非聯邦的資料中心場址，仍需取得NRC商業許可，而這一流程在歷史上往往需要耗費數年時間。

  ## 交易結構與經濟效益

  兩家公司均未公布合約定價。此次合作採分階段架構：Crusoe並非一開始就購入反應爐。2027年在INL的部署採用Spark模組化資料中心形式，作為實地先期試驗，在確認是否於2029年底前在Crusoe自有園區全面部署Aalo Pod與50 MWe XMR電廠之前，提供實際驗證依據。

  Crusoe共同創辦人暨首席策略長Cully Cavness以可靠性與規模為由說明此次合作的戰略邏輯：「新一代AI基礎設施所需的電力必須兼具可靠、充裕、潔淨與規模化，而Aalo的核能反應爐正是為滿足這一需求而生。」

  將反應爐直接設置於資料中心旁——此一配置稱為「表後自發電」（behind-the-meter generation）——原則上可讓Crusoe規避影響電網連接電源的併網排隊與輸電瓶頸問題，包括透過購電協議（PPA）取得的再生能源亦然。這種經濟模式能否在大規模推展後持續成立，取決於Aalo在缺乏DOE試驗計畫聯邦成本支援的情況下，能否在商業場址建設並籌措資金興建多個50 MWe機組。資本成本超支導致核能計畫失敗的案例，比技術故障更為普遍；而工廠化建造的模組反應爐若要達到Aalo所需的生產規模，目前仍是尚待驗證的製造模式。

  ## Crusoe的整體電力策略

  與Aalo的合作是Crusoe公開宣布的第二項核能合作案。此前，Crusoe已與模組核能開發商Blue Energy就德克薩斯州一項計畫建立合作關係。此外，Crusoe在德克薩斯州阿比林（Abilene）營運一座大型AI園區，使用傳統電力來源，並與甲骨文（Oracle）及OpenAI的Stargate計畫相關基礎設施相連結。該公司也與Meta簽訂了約1.6吉瓦容量的合約。

  這種組合模式——以天然氣和電網連接容量應對近期需求，同時在超過一家反應爐供應商進行核能試驗——呈現出的是分階段且多元化的佈局，而非押注單一技術的全面轉型。

  ## 產業背景

  根據IEA資料，與資料中心業者相關的SMR管線容量，已從2024年底的25吉瓦成長至目前的45吉瓦。業界其他動態包括：勞斯萊斯（Rolls-Royce）SMR持續推進英國與捷克的許可程序；ARC Clean Technology與Deep Atomic攜手合作，瞄準北美資料中心市場；Google則在與Kairos Power洽談企業購電協議的同時，積極推動Duane Arnold核電廠的重啟。

  2027年示範運轉及2029年機隊規模部署的時程表，以核能建設的標準而言相當緊迫。能否如期實現，取決於NRC商業場址許可的審核速度、濃縮燃料供應鏈的健全程度，以及資本市場是否願意在尚未產生營收的核能製造規模化階段提供融資支持。
