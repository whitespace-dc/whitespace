body_en: |
  Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto on Tuesday inaugurated 14 solar power plant projects with a combined capacity of 5.3 gigawatts (GW) and estimated total investment of 135 trillion rupiah ($7.62 billion), advancing the country's target to add 100 GW of new solar capacity between 2026 and 2029.

  Of the 14 projects, two are already operational, six are under construction, and six remain to be offered to investors, according to senior energy ministry official Eniya Listiani Dewi.

  ## Scale of the national solar buildout

  The government's 100 GW solar target — enough to power tens of millions of Indonesian households — carries an estimated total investment requirement of 1,140 trillion rupiah, the ministry said. The 14 projects inaugurated Tuesday represent 5.3 GW of that target, or roughly 5 percent of the four-year goal.

  The planned solar farms are projected to cut electricity production costs by 73.9 trillion rupiah annually, according to ministry figures.

  ## Replacing diesel generation

  The program is also designed to displace fossil fuel generation. The government aims to shut down 13 GW of diesel-fired power — generation that burns liquid fuel and is typically the most expensive and most carbon-intensive form of electricity production — and replace it with renewable energy sources.

  Prabowo framed the initiative as part of Indonesia's broader effort to reduce dependence on climate-warming fossil fuels.

  ## What comes next

  Six of the 14 projects have yet to be awarded to investors, meaning a further tranche of procurement activity is expected. The government's 100 GW solar target through 2029 implies a sustained, multi-year buildout that will require continued investor participation well beyond the current project slate.

  印尼總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托週二正式啟用14座太陽能電廠專案，總裝置容量達5.3吉瓦（GW），預估總投資額為135兆印尼盾（約76.2億美元），推進該國訂定的2026年至2029年間新增100吉瓦太陽能容量目標。

  根據能源部高級官員艾妮雅·利斯提亞妮·德威表示，在這14座專案中，目前已有兩座正式投入運營，六座正在施工建設，另有六座尚待向投資人公開招標。

  ## 全國太陽能建設規模

  印尼政府訂定的100吉瓦太陽能目標，足以供應數千萬個印尼家庭的用電需求，能源部估計整體投資需求高達1,140兆印尼盾。週二啟用的14個專案合計5.3吉瓦，約佔這項四年目標的5%。

  根據能源部數據，這批規劃中的太陽能電廠預計每年可降低發電成本達73.9兆印尼盾。

  ## 取代柴油發電

  這項計畫同時以汰換化石燃料發電為目標。政府計畫關閉13吉瓦的柴油燃燒發電機組——柴油發電不僅是燃料成本最高的發電方式，也是碳排放密度最高的電力生產形式——並以再生能源替代。

  普拉伯沃將這項計畫定位為印尼更廣泛努力的一部分，旨在降低對加速氣候暖化的化石燃料的依賴。

  ## 後續展望

  14個專案中仍有六座尚未完成招標，意味著後續將有新一輪的採購作業展開。印尼政府2029年前達成100吉瓦太陽能目標的計畫，意味著未來數年內需要持續推動大規模建設，所需的投資人參與程度也將遠超目前已公布的專案規模。
