body_en: |
  Salt River Project (SRP), the public power utility serving metropolitan Phoenix, said Tuesday it plans to seek board approval in September 2026 to develop the Marigold Energy Center, a mixed-resource generation and storage project roughly 45 miles south of Phoenix designed to meet growing electricity demand in Maricopa and Pinal counties.

  The project would combine 600 megawatts (MW) of solar generation, 400 MW of battery storage, and up to 675 MW of gas generation, alongside a new substation and four miles of 230-kilovolt (kV) transmission lines. In total, the facility's generation and storage capacity could reach approximately 1,675 MW — enough to power hundreds of thousands of homes depending on conditions.

  ## Project scope and timeline

  SRP first announced the Marigold project last year and held community information sessions in Stanfield, Arizona — the nearest town to the proposed site — in December and March at Stanfield Elementary School. The utility said Tuesday it will host an additional in-person open house in Stanfield on July 28 and a virtual meeting on July 29 to share information, take public input, and answer questions ahead of the September board vote.

  The project's web site states it "will build on SRP's commitment to provide Arizona with reliable and affordable power to keep up with economic growth."

  ## Potential partnership with Electrical District No. 3

  SRP identified a possible collaboration with Electrical District No. 3 (ED3), a regional public power utility. "ED3 is evaluating a potential partnership with SRP to procure a portion of the energy generated by the Marigold Energy Center, enhancing its ability to serve local customers with reliable and competitively priced electricity," SRP said.

  ## Broader storage buildout

  Marigold is part of a pattern of storage investments SRP has disclosed in recent months. In June, the utility announced an agreement with Energy Dome — a company backed by Google — to add a 19-MW, 10-hour carbon dioxide-based battery system co-located at SRP's Coronado Generating Station in St. Johns, Arizona. That project was selected through a request for proposals SRP issued in 2024. In October, SRP selected ESS Tech to develop a 5-MW, 50-megawatt-hour (MWh) iron flow battery through a pilot project and storage purchase agreement.

  SRP has described its storage expansion strategy as aimed at meeting rising demand reliably, affordably, and with cleaner energy.

  ## What comes next

  The September board vote will be the decisive near-term milestone for Marigold. Before that, SRP is scheduled to hold the July 28 in-person session in Stanfield and the July 29 virtual open house for public input.
body_zh: |
  服務大鳳凰城都會區的公立電力事業「鹽河計畫」（Salt River Project，SRP）週二表示，計畫於2026年9月向董事會提請批准「萬壽菊能源中心」（Marigold Energy Center）開發案。這是一項混合資源發電與儲能計畫，選址位於鳳凰城以南約45英里處，旨在滿足亞利桑那州馬里科帕郡（Maricopa County）及皮納爾郡（Pinal County）持續成長的電力需求。

  本計畫將整合600百萬瓦（MW）太陽能發電、400 MW電池儲能，以及最高675 MW天然氣發電，並附設一座新建變電站及4英里長的230千伏（kV）輸電線路。整體而言，本設施的發電與儲能總容量可望達到約1,675 MW，視條件而定，足以供應數十萬戶家庭的用電需求。

  ## 計畫規模與時程

  SRP去年首度對外宣布萬壽菊計畫，並分別於去年12月及今年3月，在亞利桑那州斯坦菲爾德（Stanfield）——距預定場址最近的鄉鎮——的斯坦菲爾德小學舉辦社區說明會。SRP週二表示，將於7月28日在斯坦菲爾德額外舉辦一場實體說明會，並於7月29日召開線上虛擬會議，在9月董事會表決前廣徵民意、解答各界疑問。

  計畫官網指出，本案「將以SRP對亞利桑那州的承諾為基礎，提供可靠且價格合理的電力，以跟上經濟成長的步伐。」

  ## 潛在夥伴關係：第3電力區

  SRP點名可能與第3電力區（Electrical District No. 3，ED3）——一家區域性公立電力事業——展開合作。「ED3正在評估與SRP在萬壽菊能源中心的潛在合作夥伴關係，計畫採購部分發電量，以強化其為當地客戶提供可靠且具競爭力電價的服務能力，」SRP表示。

  ## 更廣泛的儲能擴張布局

  萬壽菊計畫是SRP近幾個月陸續揭露的一連串儲能投資方案之一。今年6月，SRP宣布與「能源穹頂」（Energy Dome）——一家獲Google投資支持的企業——簽署協議，在亞利桑那州聖約翰斯（St. Johns）的科羅納多發電站（Coronado Generating Station）共置一套19 MW、可持續放電10小時的二氧化碳電池系統。該案係透過SRP於2024年發出的提案徵求程序所選定。同年10月，SRP另選定ESS Tech公司，透過一項試驗計畫及儲能採購協議，開發一套5 MW、50百萬瓦時（MWh）的鐵液流電池系統。

  SRP將其儲能擴張策略定位為：以更潔淨的能源，可靠且平價地滿足持續攀升的電力需求。

  ## 後續發展

  9月的董事會表決是萬壽菊能源中心近期最關鍵的決策里程碑。在此之前，SRP預定於7月28日在斯坦菲爾德舉辦實體公開說明會，並於7月29日召開線上虛擬說明會，在正式表決前廣納社會各界意見。
