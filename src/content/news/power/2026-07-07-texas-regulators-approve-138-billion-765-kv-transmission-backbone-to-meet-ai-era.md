body_en: |
  The Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) unanimously approved a 765-kilovolt (kV) transmission strategy in April 2025, committing to an estimated $13.8 billion backbone, the first extra-high-voltage transmission network in ERCOT (Electric Reliability Council of Texas) history, to handle projected demand growth driven by data centers, artificial intelligence infrastructure, cryptocurrency mining, and hydrogen production.

  The commission's vote concluded a five-year planning effort that began as a reliability study for electrifying West Texas oil fields and evolved into a framework for accommodating AI-era power demand. ERCOT projected combined demand in the Permian Basin region reaching 23 gigawatts (GW) by 2030 and 26.4 GW by 2038, with non-oil-and-gas industries alone accounting for 11.6 GW of the projected 2030 load.

  ## Cost and alternatives

  ERCOT evaluated three voltage options for its Permian Basin Reliability Plan. The 765 kV option carried a full 2038 plan cost of $13.8 billion, compared with $12.95 billion for a comparable 345 kV plan and $15.32 billion for a 500 kV alternative. PUCT staff concluded that while the 765 kV option cost more than the 345 kV plan, it offered greater transfer capability, lower transmission losses, fewer required corridors, and more flexibility for future load and generation siting.

  Commissioner Kathleen Jackson said the Permian Basin had "outgrown its infrastructure" and argued that the 765 kV option provides "more transfer capability" and longer-term benefits. Building the system "for the future" while using today's dollars was an important consideration, she said.

  ## From oil fields to AI demand

  The planning that produced the 765 kV decision predates the current AI buildout. ERCOT's 2019 Delaware Basin study and its 2021 Permian Basin planning work focused on grid reliability for electrifying oil drilling operations. By 2024, transmission providers were forecasting what ERCOT described as an "exponential increase" in non-oil-and-gas demand, driven primarily by cryptocurrency mining, data centers, and hydrogen electrolysis.

  That new load nearly equaled the oil-and-gas demand that originally justified the Permian reliability initiative: non-oil-and-gas industries accounted for 11.6 GW of projected 2030 demand, almost matching the forecast oil-and-gas load. ERCOT concluded the scale of projected growth justified evaluating extra-high-voltage infrastructure rather than asking how many additional 345 kV lines the region required.

  ERCOT staff noted that large new loads can connect to the grid in as little as six to twelve months, meaning actual demand in the Permian region could exceed even the 2038 forecast before planners anticipated.

  ## Technical and siting advantages

  ERCOT found the 765 kV network would provide 600 megawatts (MW) to 3,000 MW more regional transfer capability than the comparable 345 kV plan and would reduce annual transmission losses by approximately 5%. The higher-voltage network also requires fewer physical transmission corridors to achieve the same capacity, the commission found.

  Higher-capacity transmission increases the number of substations capable of supporting AI infrastructure, easing congestion and expanding developers' siting options, according to ERCOT's analysis. For data center and AI campus developers, that means more viable connection points where campuses requiring hundreds of megawatts can link to the grid.

  ## The CREZ precedent

  The decision echoes the pattern set by Texas's Competitive Renewable Energy Zones (CREZ) buildout, a transmission program originally designed to move West Texas wind generation. As Data Center Knowledge previously reported, CREZ corridors later attracted hyperscale data center development by creating high-capacity transmission infrastructure and substations that made large-load siting practical. The 765 kV plan represents a more deliberate effort to build transmission capacity ahead of demand rather than in response to it.

  ## Implications for site selection

  Neil Osnato, founder of Persistence Analytics Group, told Data Center Knowledge that the 765 kV decision reflects a broader shift in how transmission planners handle uncertainty. "ERCOT cannot know precisely where future load and generation will site in Texas," the ERCOT staff memo states. A higher-capacity network absorbs that uncertainty by providing infrastructure that can accommodate AI data centers, hydrogen production, and oil-and-gas electrification wherever they ultimately emerge.

  Osnato said transmission capacity is becoming a competitive factor in regional competition for AI investment, alongside land, fiber access, tax incentives, and permitting. "The winning regions will not be the ones that simply announce land, tax incentives, or data-center interest," he said. "They will be the ones that can prove power can be delivered at scale, on schedule, and without shifting unpriced risk onto utilities, ratepayers, or other customers."

  ## Statewide ambitions

  Although the reliability plan focuses on West Texas, ERCOT's 2024 Regional Transmission Plan concluded that a statewide 765 kV network would provide greater transfer capability, reduce congestion and transmission losses, and expand siting options across the state. ERCOT staff described the Permian Basin approval as providing the foundation for "a looped, networked statewide plan."

  ERCOT's planning documents found that major portions of both the 345 kV and 765 kV plans would remain necessary even if actual load growth falls approximately 20 GW short of current forecasts, indicating the investment is structured to be resilient to demand uncertainty in either direction.
body_zh: |
  德州公用事業委員會（PUCT）於2025年4月全票通過一項765千伏（kV）輸電策略，承諾投入估計138億美元打造骨幹網路。這是德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）史上首條超高壓輸電網路，目的是因應資料中心、人工智慧基礎設施、加密貨幣挖礦及氫能生產所帶動的預期需求成長。

  委員會此次投票，為長達五年的規劃工作畫下句點。這項規劃最初源自西德州油田電氣化的電網可靠度研究，後來演變為因應AI時代電力需求的整體框架。ERCOT預測，二疊紀盆地（Permian Basin）地區的電力需求總量將於2030年達到23吉瓦（GW），2038年進一步攀升至26.4吉瓦，其中非油氣產業單獨就佔2030年預測負載的11.6吉瓦。

  ## 費用與替代方案

  ERCOT針對二疊紀盆地可靠度計畫評估了三種電壓方案。765 kV方案的完整2038年計畫費用為138億美元，相較之下，同等規模的345 kV方案為129.5億美元，500 kV替代方案則為153.2億美元。PUCT員工的結論是：雖然765 kV方案費用高於345 kV方案，但其輸電容量更大、輸電損耗更低、所需走廊更少，且在未來負載與發電廠選址方面具備更高的靈活性。

  委員凱薩琳·傑克森（Kathleen Jackson）表示，二疊紀盆地已「超出現有基礎設施的承載能力」，並主張765 kV方案能提供「更大的輸電容量」及更長遠的效益。她表示，以今日的資金打造「面向未來」的系統，是一項重要的考量。

  ## 從油田到AI需求

  促成765 kV決策的規劃工作，早在當前AI建設浪潮出現之前便已啟動。ERCOT於2019年進行的德拉瓦盆地（Delaware Basin）研究，以及2021年的二疊紀盆地規劃工作，最初聚焦於油田電氣化的電網可靠度。到了2024年，輸電業者的預測顯示，非油氣產業需求出現ERCOT所稱的「指數級增長」，主要驅動力來自加密貨幣挖礦、資料中心及氫電解作業。

  這些新增負載幾乎與最初促使二疊紀盆地可靠度計畫成立的油氣需求相當：非油氣產業佔2030年預測需求的11.6吉瓦，幾乎與預測的油氣負載持平。ERCOT認為，預期成長的規模已足以支持評估超高壓基礎設施的必要性，而不是僅僅討論該地區還需要增設多少條345 kV線路。

  ERCOT員工指出，大型新負載最快只需六至十二個月便可接入電網，這意味著二疊紀盆地的實際需求，可能在規劃人員預期之前便已超越2038年的預測值。

  ## 技術與選址優勢

  ERCOT發現，765 kV網路的區域輸電容量將比同等的345 kV方案多出600百萬瓦（MW）至3,000百萬瓦，並可將年度輸電損耗減少約5%。委員會也認定，較高電壓的網路在達到相同容量的前提下，所需的實體輸電走廊數量更少。

  根據ERCOT的分析，更高容量的輸電系統可增加具備支援AI基礎設施能力的變電站數量，有效舒緩壅塞、擴大開發商的選址空間。對資料中心及AI園區開發商而言，這意味著有更多可行的接入點，讓需要數百百萬瓦電力的園區得以連接至電網。

  ## CREZ先例

  此次決策呼應了德州競爭性再生能源區（CREZ）建設所確立的模式——那是一項原本為了傳送西德州風力發電而設計的輸電計畫。據《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）先前的報導，CREZ走廊後來吸引了超大規模資料中心進駐，正是因為高容量輸電基礎設施與變電站的到位，使大型負載的選址成為可能。765 kV方案代表一種更具前瞻性的嘗試——在需求形成之前便預先建設輸電容量，而非被動回應。

  ## 對選址策略的影響

  Persistence Analytics Group創辦人尼爾·奧斯納托（Neil Osnato）向《資料中心知識》表示，765 kV的決策反映出輸電規劃者應對不確定性的更廣泛思維轉變。ERCOT員工備忘錄指出：「ERCOT無法準確預知未來負載和發電廠將在德州何處落腳。」更高容量的網路能夠吸收這種不確定性，無論AI資料中心、氫能生產還是油氣電氣化設施最終落腳何處，都能提供相應的基礎設施支援。

  奧斯納托表示，輸電容量正逐漸成為各地區爭奪AI投資時的關鍵競爭要素，與土地、光纖接入、稅收優惠及許可審批並列。「勝出的地區，不會只是那些宣傳土地、稅收優惠或資料中心計畫的地方，」他說，「而是那些能夠證明電力可以大規模、按時交付，且不會將未定價風險轉嫁給公用事業、費率繳納者或其他客戶的地方。」

  ## 全州發展藍圖

  儘管此次可靠度計畫聚焦於西德州，ERCOT 2024年區域輸電計畫的結論指出，全州性的765 kV網路將提供更大的輸電容量、降低壅塞與輸電損耗，並擴大全州的選址選項。ERCOT員工將二疊紀盆地的批准描述為奠定「環狀聯網全州計畫」基礎的重要一步。

  ERCOT的規劃文件顯示，即使實際負載成長比目前預測低約20吉瓦，345 kV與765 kV方案的大部分內容仍有其必要性，顯示這項投資的架構能夠抵禦需求朝任一方向偏離的不確定性。
