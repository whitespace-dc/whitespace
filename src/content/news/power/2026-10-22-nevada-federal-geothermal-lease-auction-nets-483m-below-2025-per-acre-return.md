body_en: |
  The U.S. Bureau of Land Management sold geothermal leases on 60 parcels covering 186,641 acres of federal land across Nevada, collecting $4.83 million — a lower per-acre return than a comparable BLM auction in the same state the previous year.

  Ormat Technologies was the largest buyer in the auction, paying approximately $2.3 million for eight parcels totaling about 20,263 acres.

  ## How this sale compared to prior auctions

  The 2025 Nevada geothermal lease sale covered roughly 280,000 acres and generated about $9.4 million in total receipts. A June 2026 BLM geothermal auction in New Mexico brought in $16.5 million — the second-highest total for such a sale in BLM history. The 2026 Nevada auction covered a smaller acreage and returned $4.83 million.

  ## Parcel breakdown

  BLM originally designated 83 parcels for the auction. Ten were removed before bidding for unspecified reasons; none were withdrawn due to formal protests. Thirteen parcels drew no bids, leaving 60 sold. Parcels were distributed across Lyon, Churchill, Mineral, Humboldt, Esmeralda, Lander, Nye, Eureka, Lincoln, and White Pine counties.

  ## Overlap with NextEra's proposed Sawtooth solar project

  Two of the sold parcels overlap the proposed footprint of NextEra Energy's Sawtooth Energy Center, a 1-gigawatt (GW) solar project — equivalent to 1,000 megawatts of generating capacity — under consideration in Nye County, Nevada. NextEra has a pending right-of-way grant application with BLM for the Sawtooth site.

  United Minerals and Mining paid approximately $15,400 each for those two parcels.

  NextEra had filed comments on the proposed sale, asking BLM to "remove, defer, or reconfigure" three overlapping parcels during final parcel review to prevent "a potentially incompatible leasehold interest" from arising before BLM completed its review of the Sawtooth right-of-way application. BLM rejected that request, stating it "manages the land for multiple use." One of the three originally flagged parcels was not included in the final auction listing.

  In its written response to NextEra, BLM stated that "subsequent authorizations for other uses, including right of ways, must not unreasonably interfere with geothermal operations undertaken by the lease holder," and that NextEra "must collaborate with any potential lessee and adjust its operations accordingly to avoid land use conflicts." NextEra did not respond to a request for comment.

  ## What this means for developers

  The sale confirms BLM's practice of proceeding with geothermal leasing on parcels that overlap pending solar right-of-way applications, rather than deferring to those applicants. Under BLM's stated position, a geothermal lease granted before a solar right-of-way is authorized takes precedence; the solar developer must adjust its project to avoid interfering with geothermal operations.

  NextEra's right-of-way application for the Sawtooth Energy Center remains pending, and the company now faces coordination requirements with United Minerals and Mining on the two leased parcels within its proposed project boundary.

  ## 內華達地熱租約拍賣進帳483萬美元，每英畝收益低於2025年

  美國土地管理局（BLM）完成內華達州聯邦土地地熱租約拍賣，共售出60塊地塊，涵蓋186,641英畝，總收入為483萬美元——每英畝均價低於前一年該局在同一州舉辦的同類拍賣。

  以色列地熱能源公司奧瑪特科技（Ormat Technologies）為本次拍賣最大買主，以約230萬美元標得八塊地塊，合計約20,263英畝。

  ## 與歷次拍賣的比較

  2025年內華達地熱租約拍賣覆蓋面積約28萬英畝，總收入約940萬美元。2026年6月，BLM於新墨西哥州舉辦的地熱拍賣進帳1,650萬美元，創下該局此類拍賣史上第二高紀錄。本次2026年內華達拍賣涵蓋面積較小，最終收入為483萬美元。

  ## 地塊詳情

  BLM原定將83塊地塊納入拍賣。開標前以未公開原因撤除10塊，無任何地塊因正式抗議而撤出。另有13塊地塊無人出價，最終成交60塊。成交地塊分布於萊昂（Lyon）、邱吉爾（Churchill）、礦物（Mineral）、洪堡（Humboldt）、埃斯梅拉達（Esmeralda）、蘭德（Lander）、奈伊（Nye）、尤里卡（Eureka）、林肯（Lincoln）及白松（White Pine）等縣。

  ## 與NextEra鋸齒山太陽能案的重疊地塊

  其中兩塊成交地塊，與NextEra能源公司（NextEra Energy）在奈伊縣提案中的鋸齒山能源中心（Sawtooth Energy Center）預定範圍重疊。該太陽能案規劃裝置容量達1吉瓦（GW），即1,000百萬瓦，NextEra目前已就鋸齒山基地向BLM提出待審的路權申請。

  這兩塊地塊由聯合礦物與採礦公司（United Minerals and Mining）以每塊約15,400美元標得。

  NextEra此前曾就本次拍賣提出書面意見，要求BLM在完成最終地塊審查時「撤除、延後或重新劃定」三塊重疊地塊，以避免在BLM尚未完成鋸齒山路權申請審查前，產生「潛在不相容的租賃權益」。BLM拒絕了這項要求，聲明其「以多目標方式管理土地」。原遭NextEra標記的三塊地塊中，有一塊最終未列入拍賣清單。

  BLM在給NextEra的書面回覆中表示，「後續其他用途的授權，包括路權，不得對租約持有人進行的地熱作業造成不合理干擾」，並指NextEra「必須與潛在承租人協調，並相應調整其營運，以避免土地使用衝突」。NextEra未回應媒體的置評請求。

  ## 對開發商的意義

  本次拍賣確立了BLM的一貫做法——即便地塊與待審太陽能路權申請有所重疊，仍照常推進地熱租約作業，不會對路權申請人讓步。依據BLM的立場，在太陽能路權獲批之前即已核發的地熱租約具有優先效力；太陽能開發商必須調整其計畫，以免干擾地熱作業。

  NextEra針對鋸齒山能源中心提出的路權申請仍在審核中，該公司如今還須就其提案邊界內的兩塊已租賃地塊，與聯合礦物與採礦公司進行協調。
