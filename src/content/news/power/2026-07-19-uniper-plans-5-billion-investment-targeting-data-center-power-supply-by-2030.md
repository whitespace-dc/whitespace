body_en: |
  Uniper, the German energy group bailed out by Berlin during Europe's 2022 energy crisis, said Friday it will invest around €5 billion ($5.72 billion) by 2030 in flexible power generation and renewables, targeting new revenue streams from data centers at its existing power plant sites across Europe.

  ## Investment split

  More than half of the planned €5 billion is earmarked for flexible power generation, Uniper said, with particular emphasis on Germany. Flexible power generation refers to capacity that can adjust output rapidly in response to shifting demand, as distinct from fixed baseload or variable renewable sources.

  ## Data center sites

  Uniper said rising demand for digital infrastructure creates growth opportunities, as growing power consumption from data centers increases the need for reliable, long-term electricity supply. The company has identified more than 10 of its own sites with suitable infrastructure, located strategically along European data hubs. Three projects are already in advanced development, with further investment decisions expected this year. One project in Britain has already been completed.

  "The rising electricity demand from data centres requires powerful, reliable and long-term supply solutions," Chief Executive Michael Lewis said in a statement.

  ## Revenue model

  Uniper plans to generate additional revenue through structured power purchase agreements (PPAs), which are long-term contracts that set terms for electricity delivery between a supplier and a buyer, and through direct supply from its own secured generation capacity where economically viable.

  ## Government stake sale backdrop

  The strategy announcement comes as Berlin prepares to sell its 99.12% stake in Uniper. Potential buyers, including Canada's CPPIB and Czech EPH, were expected to submit letters of interest by mid-June.

  ## Next investment decisions

  With three data center projects in advanced development and one in Britain already completed, Uniper said further investment decisions across its European site portfolio are expected before the end of this year.
body_zh: |
  曾於2022年歐洲能源危機期間獲德國政府紓困的德國能源集團優能（Uniper），週五宣布將於2030年前投入約50億歐元（約57.2億美元），用於靈活發電與再生能源建設，並鎖定旗下現有歐洲電廠廠址，積極開拓資料中心供電的新營收來源。

  ## 投資分配

  優能表示，計劃中的50億歐元有逾半數將用於靈活發電，且重點集中於德國。所謂靈活發電，是指能依據需求變化快速調整輸出的發電容量，有別於固定的基礎負載或輸出不穩定的再生能源。

  ## 資料中心廠址

  優能指出，數位基礎設施需求持續攀升，為公司帶來顯著成長機遇——資料中心用電量不斷擴大，對穩定、長期電力供應的需求也隨之增加。該公司已篩選出逾10處自有廠址，這些廠址均具備適合的基礎設施，且策略性地分布於歐洲各主要資料中樞附近。目前已有3個專案進入深度開發階段，並預計於今年內做出進一步投資決策；英國已有一項專案率先完成。

  執行長麥可．路易斯（Michael Lewis）在聲明中表示：「資料中心的電力需求持續攀升，需要的是強勁、可靠且長期穩定的供電解決方案。」

  ## 營收模式

  優能計劃透過兩種方式創造額外營收：一是簽訂結構化的電力購買協議（PPA）——此類長期合約明訂供電方與買方之間的電力交付條件；二是在經濟效益許可的情況下，直接由自有確保的發電容量對外供電。

  ## 德國政府釋股背景

  此次策略宣示的時間點，恰逢柏林當局準備出售其持有的優能99.12%股權。據悉，潛在買家包括加拿大的加拿大退休金計劃投資局（CPPIB）及捷克的EPH集團，預計須於六月中旬前提交意向書。

  ## 後續投資決策

  隨著3個資料中心專案進入深度開發階段、英國1項專案已率先完工，優能表示將在今年底前，針對旗下歐洲廠址組合做出進一步投資決策。
