  ## Wiwynn and TE Connectivity Show Liquid-Cooled 800V DC Busbar for Next-Gen AI Servers

  Wiwynn and TE Connectivity used Computex 2026 to demonstrate a liquid-cooled 800V DC busbar system and an integrated power cable assembly intended to supply next-generation AI accelerators, according to ServeTheHome, which reported from the Wiwynn booth.

  The centrepiece of the demonstration was the TE Connectivity HVDC IT GEAR TO ELCON MICRO+ Power Cable Assembly — a single drop-in unit that presents an 800V/75A HVDC interface at the IT-gear end and converts to a 400V ELCON MICRO+ interface at the other. The assembly is positioned between two AI accelerator modules inside the chassis and connects to the server's internal power distribution.

  ## What the demo showed

  The physical exhibit was built around a mock-up Open Compute Project ORv3 (Open Rack v3, a rack-scale server standard) server node. The front panel included a management module, four E1.S solid-state drives, and dummy optical network cages indicating the unit is a directional prototype rather than a shipping product. The rear of the node carried a liquid-cooling nozzle and connectors for in-rack scale-up networking, as well as the busbar connector that anchors the power chain shown in the demonstration.

  The two AI accelerator placeholders each measure more than 100 cm² in footprint. The power cable assembly occupies the channel running between them and is designed to slot into position as a complete unit during manufacturing rather than be built up in place on an assembly line — a requirement that reflects the industry's shift toward robotic server assembly, ServeTheHome noted.

  All components in the chassis are expected to be liquid-cooled, per the report, including the busbar itself — a notable departure from air-cooled power distribution components prevalent in current data centre designs.

  ## Why 800V DC matters

  Data centres today predominantly distribute power at 48V DC within server racks, with some deployments reaching higher intermediate bus voltages. Moving to 800V DC at the rack level reduces resistive losses at a given power level — because power equals voltage multiplied by current, doubling voltage allows the same wattage to be carried at half the current, which reduces heat generated in conductors and allows thinner cabling. The TE Connectivity assembly demonstrated at Computex bridges 800V rack-level distribution down to the 400V interface used at the accelerator level.

  Liquid cooling the busbar itself addresses the thermal challenge of concentrating that much current-carrying capacity in the confined space of a high-density AI server chassis.

  ## Industry direction

  The demonstration signals that the transition to 800V DC power distribution is advancing from specification discussions into tangible hardware form factors, with at least one major original design manufacturer and a major connector supplier presenting an integrated system approach. The drop-in power cable assembly design reflects an assumption that next-generation AI server manufacturing will rely heavily on robotics, making modular, self-contained power sub-assemblies preferable to field-assembled wiring.

  The TE Connectivity HVDC IT GEAR TO ELCON MICRO+ Power Cable Assembly as shown at Computex 2026 carries an 800V/75A rating at the rack interface end.

  ## 緯穎與泰科電子展示液冷800V直流匯流排　專為下世代AI伺服器打造

  緯穎（Wiwynn）與泰科電子（TE Connectivity）藉2026年台北國際電腦展（Computex）之機，在緯穎展示攤位聯合呈現一套液冷800V直流匯流排系統及整合式電源線組，目標是為下世代AI加速器提供穩定電力。以上消息由到場採訪緯穎展位的ServeTheHome所報導。

  此次展示的核心元件，是泰科電子推出的「HVDC IT GEAR TO ELCON MICRO+ 電源線組」——一個即插即用的整合式模組，在IT設備端提供800V／75A的高壓直流（HVDC）介面，另一端則轉換為400V的ELCON MICRO+介面。這組線組安置於機箱內兩片AI加速器模組之間，並與伺服器內部的電力分配系統相連。

  ## 展示內容說明

  實體展示以一個仿製的開放運算計畫ORv3（Open Rack v3，即機架規模伺服器標準）伺服器節點為核心。前面板配置了一個管理模組、四個E1.S規格固態硬碟，以及虛設的光纖網路插槽，顯示此為方向性原型機，而非量產商品。節點後方則配備液冷噴嘴、機架內橫向擴充網路的連接埠，以及固定整套電源鏈的匯流排連接器。

  兩個AI加速器預留位置的占板面積均超過100平方公分。電源線組置於兩者之間的走線通道，設計上以完整單元的形式在製造階段直接置入，而非在產線上逐一組裝——ServeTheHome指出，這一設計要求反映出業界正朝機器人自動化伺服器組裝的方向演進。

  根據報導，機箱內所有元件預計均採液冷散熱，包括匯流排本身。這與目前資料中心普遍採用風冷式電力分配元件的做法有明顯差異，是一項值得關注的轉變。

  ## 800V直流電壓的意義

  現今資料中心在伺服器機架內主要以48V直流進行電力分配，部分場合會使用更高的中間匯流排電壓。提升至800V直流的意義在於：在相同功率下可大幅降低導線的電阻損耗——由於功率等於電壓乘以電流，電壓加倍即可以一半的電流傳輸相同的電力，從而減少導線產生的熱量，並允許使用截面積更小的線纜。泰科電子此次展示的電源線組，正是將機架層級的800V配電橋接至加速器層級所使用的400V介面。

  對匯流排本身進行液冷，則是為了解決在高密度AI伺服器機箱有限空間內集中大量載流能力所帶來的散熱挑戰。

  ## 產業發展方向

  此次展示標誌著800V直流電力分配技術的演進，已從規格討論階段推進至具體硬體形態，且至少有一家主要原廠設計製造商與一家主要連接器供應商共同呈現了整合式系統方案。即插即用電源線組的設計思路，也反映出業界預期下世代AI伺服器製造將大量仰賴機器人技術，使模組化、自含式電源子組件比現場配線更具優勢。

  此次在2026年台北國際電腦展亮相的泰科電子HVDC IT GEAR TO ELCON MICRO+電源線組，其機架介面端的額定規格為800V／75A。
