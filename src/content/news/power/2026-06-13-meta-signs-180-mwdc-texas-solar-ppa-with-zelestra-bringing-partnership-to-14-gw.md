body_en: |
  Meta signed a power purchase agreement with renewable energy developer Zelestra for the 180-megawatt-direct-current (MWdc) Palmera Solar Plant in Freestone County, Texas, the companies announced June 9, 2026, expanding a partnership that now encompasses roughly 1.4 gigawatts of solar capacity across eight U.S. projects.

  Direct-current capacity measures output at the solar panels themselves, before conversion to the alternating current carried on the power grid; alternating-current output is typically somewhat lower. At 1.4 GW DC spread across eight sites concentrated in Texas and Indiana, the Meta-Zelestra portfolio represents one of the larger bilateral corporate renewable energy commitments in the U.S. market.

  ## Partnership status: one online, two under construction

  The partnership's first completed project, Zelestra's 81 MWdc Jasper County Solar Project in Indiana, recently reached commercial operation, according to the companies. Two additional Meta-backed plants are now under construction: the 176 MWdc Skull Creek Solar Plant in Anderson County, Texas, and the 200 MWdc Reclamation Solar Project in Gibson County, Indiana. Both are expected to support approximately 400 jobs at peak construction, the companies said.

  The Palmera plant in Freestone County joins those projects as part of the broader eight-project pipeline, all of which the companies said are expected to go online by 2028.

  ## Meta's clean energy goal and AI emissions pressure

  Meta, which owns Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, has set a target of net-zero emissions across its entire supply chain by 2030. The company said electricity from the Palmera plant will help it power operations with 100% clean energy while adding new renewable generation capacity to the grid.

  The company, like other large technology firms, has experienced rising emissions linked to artificial intelligence workloads and has been investing in clean energy and low-carbon solutions to manage AI-related energy demand, according to the source reporting.

  "Meta is committed to bringing new renewable energy to the grid, and our expanding relationship with Zelestra is helping make that possible at scale," said Amanda Yang, Meta's head of clean and renewable energy, in the June 9 release. "These projects aren't just advancing our energy goals — they're creating jobs and delivering long-term value in the communities where they operate."

  ## Zelestra's role

  Zelestra specializes in developing and operating clean energy projects and providing decarbonization services to large corporations. The company's partnership with Meta now spans multiple U.S. states and generation technologies concentrated in solar, with the eight-project pipeline representing the full scope of the current agreement.

  All eight projects in the Meta-Zelestra portfolio are expected to reach commercial operation by 2028.
body_zh: |
  Meta於2026年6月9日宣布，與再生能源開發商Zelestra就德州自由石郡（Freestone County）180百萬瓦直流（MWdc）Palmera太陽能電廠簽署購電協議，雙方合作規模至此擴展至美國八個案場、直流總容量約1.4吉瓦（GW）。

  直流容量指太陽能板端的實際輸出，轉換為電網使用的交流電後，實際輸出通常略低。這八個案場主要集中於德州與印第安納州，Meta與Zelestra共同組成的案場組合以1.4 GW直流總容量，已躋身美國市場規模最大的企業雙邊再生能源承諾之一。

  ## 合作進展：一座已商轉，兩座施工中

  根據雙方說明，合作首個竣工案場——Zelestra位於印第安納州賈斯珀郡（Jasper County）、容量81 MWdc的賈斯珀郡太陽能案場——近期已達成商業運轉。目前另有兩座由Meta支持的電廠正在施工：一是位於德州安德森郡（Anderson County）、容量176 MWdc的Skull Creek太陽能電廠，二是位於印第安納州吉布森郡（Gibson County）、容量200 MWdc的Reclamation太陽能案場。雙方表示，兩座電廠在建設高峰期合計預計創造約400個就業機會。

  自由石郡的Palmera電廠亦納入這一更廣泛的八案場開發管線，所有案場均預計於2028年前投入商轉。

  ## Meta的潔淨能源目標與AI排放壓力

  Meta旗下擁有Facebook、Instagram及WhatsApp，已訂定2030年前達成整體供應鏈淨零排放的目標。公司表示，Palmera電廠所產生的電力將協助其以100%潔淨能源驅動各項營運，同時為電網新增再生能源裝置容量。

  與其他大型科技業者相同，Meta因人工智慧工作負載持續增長而面臨排放量攀升的壓力，並持續投資潔淨能源與低碳解決方案，以因應AI相關能源需求。

  「Meta致力於為電網引入新的再生能源，而我們與Zelestra不斷深化的合作正協助我們以規模化方式實現這一目標，」Meta潔淨與再生能源業務負責人Amanda Yang在6月9日的聲明中表示。「這些案場不僅推動了我們的能源目標，更在所在社區創造就業機會，帶來長遠價值。」

  ## Zelestra的角色

  Zelestra專注於潔淨能源案場的開發與運營，並為大型企業提供去碳化服務。目前Zelestra與Meta的合作橫跨美國多州，以太陽能為主力發電技術，八個案場的開發管線代表現行協議的完整範疇。

  Meta與Zelestra合作組合中的八個案場，均預計於2028年前達成商業運轉。
