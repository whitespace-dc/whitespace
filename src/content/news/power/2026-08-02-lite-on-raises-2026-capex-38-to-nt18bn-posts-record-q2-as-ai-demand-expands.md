body_en: |
  Lite-On Technology Corporation raised its 2026 capital expenditure plan by NT$5 billion (New Taiwan dollars) to NT$18 billion on Friday and called its second-half business outlook "very optimistic," after the Taiwanese electronics maker reported record quarterly earnings driven by rising AI data center demand.

  President Chiu Sen-pin made the announcements at an investor conference, saying expanded demand across AI infrastructure is the primary driver of both the upgraded spending plan and the company's improving profit trajectory.

  ## Record earnings and AI revenue mix

  Lite-On's second-quarter after-tax net profit reached NT$7.116 billion, up 126% year on year and the highest single-quarter result in the company's history. Earnings per share for the quarter were NT$3.14. Gross margin expanded to 27.2%, an improvement of 5.1 percentage points from the same period a year earlier.

  For the first half of 2026, after-tax net profit totaled NT$10.891 billion, with first-half earnings per share of NT$4.80, representing 66% year-on-year growth and a record for any comparable six-month period, the company said.

  AI-related products accounted for more than 25% of second-quarter revenue, and the company expects that share to exceed 30% for the full year. Battery backup units, known as BBUs — systems that provide short-term power reserve to data center equipment during grid interruptions — accounted for close to 20% of cloud division revenue in the quarter. Full-year BBU growth is expected to exceed 70%, matching the pace of the broader cloud business, the company said.

  ## Capex revision and facility build-out

  Chiu said the revised NT$18 billion capex figure already incorporates the cost of constructing and equipping the company's U.S. manufacturing facility. Roughly 30% of the total is allocated to land, factory buildings, and basic mechanical and electrical infrastructure; the remaining 70% is directed toward R&D and testing equipment and automated production lines.

  Chiu noted that Lite-On's power products have expanded in scale from small power supply units to power cabinets weighing 3.5 metric tons, which require larger factory floor space for both assembly and testing. Because planning and building a factory requires three years, the company must expand capacity well in advance of demand, he said.

  Three facilities are scheduled to come online in phases through mid-2027:

  - **Kaohsiung Phase 2, Taiwan**: Production begins Q3 2026; manufacturing of 800VDC power cabinets starts in 2027.
  - **Vietnam new factory**: Production begins Q4 2026; primarily serving low-earth orbit satellite power supply customers.
  - **McKinney, Texas, USA**: Mechanical and electrical engineering begins August 2026; equipment installation complete by year-end 2026; trial production February 2027; volume production of HVDC and BBU systems begins Q2 2027.

  HVDC — high-voltage direct current — is a power distribution architecture used in large-scale data centers.

  ## Product certification and shipment schedule

  The 800VDC power cabinet is scheduled to begin sample testing in August, followed by a 13-week certification process conducted jointly with customers and ODM (original design manufacturer) partners. A second validation phase that integrates IT systems is planned for the fourth quarter, with small-volume shipments expected in November and larger-scale mass production beginning in Q1 2027.

  Lite-On said customers deploying ASIC-based computing platforms are adopting the product at a faster pace. GPU-platform customers are expected to begin rollout and reach mass production in Q2 2027.

  ## Customer expansion and supply chain preparation

  The company said it is now shipping power supplies, BBUs, and cabinets to two additional North American cloud service providers — companies that operate large-scale cloud computing infrastructure — covering both ASIC and GPU platforms. Chiu described current volumes with those customers as small and said the company's stated goal is to increase its supply share and become a primary vendor.

  To manage anticipated supply constraints in the second half of the year, Lite-On said it pre-purchased power components, printed circuit boards, and capacitors six months ahead of schedule, citing the risk of shortages and price increases in those materials.

  ## What it means

  The 38% increase in Lite-On's capex plan, combined with a three-region manufacturing expansion, positions the company to compete in higher-value data center power infrastructure — including BBU and HVDC systems — beyond its traditional power supply business. The company said it expects second-half operations to outperform the first half and that full-year revenue will maintain strong growth.

  The first volume production of HVDC and BBU systems at the McKinney, Texas facility is targeted for Q2 2027.
body_zh: |
  光寶科技股份有限公司周五宣布，將2026年資本支出計畫上調新台幣50億元至180億元，並對下半年業務展望表示「非常樂觀」。這家台灣電子製造商甫公布由AI資料中心需求推動的歷史性單季獲利，創下公司成立以來最高紀錄。

  總裁邱森彬在法人說明會上宣布上述消息，表示AI基礎建設需求的全面擴張，是此次資本支出計畫上調及公司獲利軌跡持續改善的主要驅動力。

  ## 歷史新高業績與AI營收佔比

  光寶科技第二季稅後淨利達新台幣71.16億元，年增126%，創下公司歷史上單季最高紀錄。本季每股盈餘為新台幣3.14元。毛利率擴大至27.2%，較去年同期提升5.1個百分點。

  2026年上半年稅後淨利累計達新台幣108.91億元，上半年每股盈餘為新台幣4.80元，年增66%，同樣創下歷年上半年同期最高紀錄。

  AI相關產品佔第二季營收比重已超過25%，公司預期全年佔比將突破30%。電池備援裝置（BBU）——即在電網中斷期間為資料中心設備提供短暫備援電力的系統——本季在雲端事業部門的營收佔比接近20%。公司表示，全年BBU業務成長幅度預計將超過70%，與雲端整體業務成長步調相當。

  ## 資本支出修正與廠房建設計畫

  邱森彬表示，修訂後的新台幣180億元資本支出數字，已納入美國製造廠的建設與設備費用。其中約30%分配於土地、廠房及基礎機電設施，其餘70%則用於研發及測試設備與自動化生產線。

  邱森彬指出，光寶的電源產品規模已從小型電源供應器擴大至重達3.5公噸的電源機櫃，無論組裝或測試都需要更大的廠房空間。由於規劃並興建一座工廠需要三年時間，公司必須提前擴充產能以因應需求。

  三座廠房預計分階段於2027年中前陸續投產：

  - **台灣高雄第二期廠**：2026年第三季開始生產；800VDC電源機櫃於2027年起量產。
  - **越南新廠**：2026年第四季開始生產；主要服務低軌道衛星電源供應客戶。
  - **美國德州麥金尼廠**：2026年8月開始機電工程作業；設備安裝於2026年底完成；2027年2月試產；HVDC及BBU系統於2027年第二季開始量產。

  HVDC即高壓直流電，是大型資料中心廣泛採用的電力分配架構。

  ## 產品認證與出貨時程

  800VDC電源機櫃預計於8月開始樣品測試，隨後進行為期13週、與客戶及ODM（原廠委託設計製造商）合作夥伴共同進行的認證流程。第四季計畫啟動整合IT系統的第二階段驗證，預計11月開始小批量出貨，2027年第一季進入較大規模的量產階段。

  光寶科技表示，採用ASIC運算平台的客戶導入此產品的速度較快；GPU平台客戶則預計於2027年第二季開始導入並達到量產。

  ## 客戶擴張與供應鏈備料

  公司表示，目前已向另外兩家北美雲端服務業者——即經營大規模雲端運算基礎設施的業者——出貨電源供應器、BBU及電源機櫃，涵蓋ASIC與GPU兩種平台。邱森彬形容目前與這些客戶的出貨量仍屬小規模，並表示公司的明確目標是提高供貨佔比，進而成為主要供應商。

  為因應下半年預期的供應吃緊，光寶科技表示已提前六個月預購電源元件、印刷電路板及電容器，主要考量這些材料存在短缺及漲價風險。

  ## 市場意義

  光寶科技資本支出計畫大幅上調38%，加上橫跨三個地區的製造擴張，使公司得以在傳統電源供應器業務之外，進一步布局更高附加價值的資料中心電源基礎建設領域，包括BBU與HVDC系統。公司表示，預期下半年營運表現將優於上半年，全年營收亦將維持強勁成長動能。

  位於美國德州麥金尼廠的HVDC及BBU系統首批量產，目標訂於2027年第二季達成。
