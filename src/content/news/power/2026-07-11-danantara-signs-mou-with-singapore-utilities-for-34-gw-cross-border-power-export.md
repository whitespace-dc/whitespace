body_en: |
  Indonesia's state investment vehicle Danantara has signed memoranda of understanding with three Singapore energy companies to develop a 3.4 gigawatt (GW) cross-border electricity export project, accelerating a bilateral power trade initiative agreed at the highest levels of government.

  Danantara Investment Management entered the agreements with Keppel Electric, Sembcorp Utilities, and Singapore Energy Interconnections, covering the development of low-carbon electricity trade and the grid interconnection infrastructure required to link the two countries, according to Kontan.

  ## Bilateral backdrop

  The project follows a commitment made between Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto and Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong during a bilateral meeting in Jakarta, Kontan reported. The MOUs represent the formal next step in translating that political agreement into commercial development activity.

  ## Scale and parties

  At 3.4 GW, the targeted export capacity is a significant volume for a bilateral power link. For reference, 3.4 GW is roughly equivalent to the combined output of several large gas-fired power plants and would represent a material addition to Singapore's electricity supply. The three Singapore counterparties — Keppel Electric, Sembcorp Utilities, and Singapore Energy Interconnections — are utilities and infrastructure companies with roles in Singapore's power market and regional energy connectivity.

  The agreements cover both the commercial structure of low-carbon electricity trade and the physical interconnection network needed to move power across the Strait of Singapore between the two countries.

  ## Significance

  The project, if realized, would establish Indonesia as a significant electricity exporter to Singapore and advance Singapore's stated interest in importing low-carbon power from the region. For Indonesia, it represents a new avenue for monetizing power generation capacity under Danantara, the country's sovereign investment manager.

  Danantara said it is accelerating the project's realization following the MOU signings, with the interconnection and trade framework now under joint development with the three Singapore partners.

  ## 達南塔拉與新加坡公用事業簽署備忘錄　推動3.4 GW跨境售電互聯計畫

  印尼國家投資機構達南塔拉已與三家新加坡能源企業簽署合作備忘錄，共同開發一項3.4百萬瓩（GW）的跨境電力出口計畫，加速推動兩國政府最高層級已達成共識的雙邊電力貿易倡議。

  達南塔拉投資管理公司分別與吉寶電力（Keppel Electric）、勝科公用事業（Sembcorp Utilities）及新加坡能源互聯（Singapore Energy Interconnections）簽署協議，內容涵蓋低碳電力貿易的開發合作，以及連結兩國所需的電網互聯互通基礎建設，據《Kontan》報導。

  ## 雙邊合作背景

  此一計畫源於印尼總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托（Prabowo Subianto）與新加坡總理黃循財（Lawrence Wong）在雅加達舉行雙邊會談時所作出的承諾，《Kontan》報導指出。此次備忘錄的簽署，代表將這項政治共識正式轉化為商業開發行動的關鍵一步。

  ## 規模與合作方

  3.4 GW的目標出口容量，對於雙邊電力互聯而言是相當可觀的規模。作為參考，3.4 GW大約相當於數座大型燃氣電廠的合計發電量，將為新加坡的電力供應帶來實質性的增量。三家新加坡合作方——吉寶電力、勝科公用事業及新加坡能源互聯——均為在新加坡電力市場及區域能源互聯互通領域具有重要地位的公用事業與基礎建設公司。

  協議內容同時涵蓋低碳電力貿易的商業架構，以及在新加坡海峽兩岸之間輸送電力所需的實體互聯網路。

  ## 重要意義

  若此計畫得以實現，印尼將成為新加坡重要的電力出口國，同時也有助於推進新加坡從區域進口低碳電力的既定目標。對印尼而言，這為達南塔拉——即印尼主權投資管理機構——旗下的發電產能開闢了一條全新的商業變現途徑。

  達南塔拉表示，在完成備忘錄簽署後，將加速推進計畫落地，互聯互通建設與貿易框架目前已與三家新加坡合作夥伴展開聯合開發。
