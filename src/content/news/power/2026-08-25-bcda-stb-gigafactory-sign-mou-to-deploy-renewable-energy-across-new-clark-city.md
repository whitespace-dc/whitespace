body_en: |
  The Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), the Philippine government agency that manages former military bases converted to civilian use, has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with StB GigaFactory, Inc. to deploy renewable energy (RE) solutions, battery energy storage systems, and electric vehicle mobility infrastructure across New Clark City and other BCDA-managed developments.

  The year-long agreement assigns distinct roles to each party. BCDA will grant StB GigaFactory site access, supply key project data, and provide administrative support. StB GigaFactory will conduct site surveys, technical feasibility studies, and financial modeling, with the stated goal of producing actionable recommendations for clean energy deployment across BCDA-managed properties.

  ## What StB GigaFactory does

  StB GigaFactory describes itself as the Philippines' first manufacturing plant for Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) battery cells — a battery chemistry widely used in stationary energy storage — and Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS). The company operates within Filinvest Innovation Park, which is located inside New Clark City in Tarlac province, north of Manila.

  The company currently employs approximately 500 workers and has said it is targeting a workforce of 2,500 by 2030, according to the BCDA statement.

  ## Scope of the agreement

  The MoU covers renewable energy generation, energy storage, and what BCDA describes as e-mobility solutions. The partnership's stated aim is to build a "clean-energy ecosystem" designed to support grid reliability and sustainable mobility infrastructure for businesses and institutions located within BCDA-managed zones.

  BCDA President and Chief Executive Officer Joshua M. Bingcang said the agreement is intended to integrate energy storage and clean mobility into New Clark City's master plan. "We are building a resilient infrastructure framework that empowers industries, protects the environment, and drives long-term economic growth," Bingcang said in the agency's statement.

  ## Significance

  The agreement is a pre-construction, study-phase arrangement rather than a commitment to build specific infrastructure. Its immediate output is feasibility and financial analysis, not installed capacity. Whether it advances to physical deployment will depend on the results of the technical and financial modeling StB GigaFactory is tasked to complete during the agreement's one-year term.

  For New Clark City, a planned urban development on former military land, the MoU signals an intent to anchor clean energy infrastructure to the site's growth strategy. StB GigaFactory's position as both a potential energy solutions provider and a tenant within the development gives it direct operational interest in the outcome of the feasibility work.

  菲律賓基地轉換與開發局（Bases Conversion and Development Authority，BCDA）是負責管理前軍事基地轉型民用的菲國政府機構，日前與StB GigaFactory, Inc.簽署合作備忘錄（MoU），雙方將攜手在新克拉克市（New Clark City）及BCDA轄下各開發區，共同推動再生能源（RE）解決方案、電池儲能系統（Battery Energy Storage Systems，BESS）以及電動車移動基礎設施的建置工作。

  這份為期一年的協議明確劃分雙方職責。BCDA將提供StB GigaFactory進場勘查的資格、提交關鍵專案資料，並給予行政業務上的協助；StB GigaFactory則負責執行現地勘查、技術可行性評估及財務模型分析，目標是針對BCDA管轄各場址的潔淨能源部署，提出具體可行的建議方案。

  ## StB GigaFactory簡介

  StB GigaFactory自我定位為菲律賓首家磷酸鋰鐵（Lithium Iron Phosphate，LFP）電池芯製造廠，及電池儲能系統（BESS）生產基地——LFP是靜態儲能應用中廣泛採用的電池化學技術。該公司目前設廠於Filinvest創新園區（Filinvest Innovation Park），位置在馬尼拉以北、他甲省（Tarlac）境內的新克拉克市範圍之內。

  根據BCDA發布的聲明，StB GigaFactory目前員工約500人，並表示以2030年為目標，將員工人數擴增至2,500人。

  ## 協議涵蓋範圍

  本份備忘錄涵蓋再生能源發電、能源儲存，以及BCDA所稱的電動移動（e-mobility）解決方案三大面向。雙方合作的既定目標，是建立一套「潔淨能源生態系」，旨在支撐BCDA管轄區域內各企業與機構的電網穩定性，並完善永續移動基礎設施。

  BCDA總裁暨執行長Joshua M. Bingcang表示，此次協議旨在將能源儲存與潔淨移動整合進新克拉克市的整體發展總圖（master plan）之中。「我們正在構建一套具韌性的基礎設施框架，賦能各產業發展、守護自然環境，並驅動長期經濟成長，」Bingcang在該機構聲明中如此表示。

  ## 協議意義

  這份協議屬於動工前的研究評估階段安排，而非承諾興建特定基礎設施的正式協定。其近期產出為可行性與財務分析報告，而非已安裝的實際容量。能否進一步推進至實體建設，將取決於StB GigaFactory在一年協議期內完成技術與財務模型分析的結果。

  對於新克拉克市——這座建立在前軍事用地上的計畫城市——而言，本備忘錄象徵一種意圖：將潔淨能源基礎設施錨定於該址的整體成長策略之中。StB GigaFactory同時身兼潛在能源解決方案供應商與園區租戶的雙重角色，使其對可行性評估結果本身就具有直接的營運利益。
