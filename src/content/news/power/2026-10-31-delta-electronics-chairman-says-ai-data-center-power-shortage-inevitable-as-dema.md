body_en: |
  Delta Electronics Chairman Cheng Ping said Thursday that power shortages at AI data centers are "inevitable" as computing demand grows faster than electrical infrastructure can be built, with his company's investment unit projecting global data center electricity consumption will double within four years.

  Liu Liang-fu, vice president of corporate investment at Delta Electronics, said data centers worldwide currently consume approximately 500 billion kilowatt-hours (kWh) annually, equal to roughly 1.5 percent of total global electricity use. He projected that figure could reach 1 trillion kWh by 2030. Because power infrastructure takes years to plan and construct, new generating capacity will not necessarily keep pace with data center expansion, Liu said — a lag that could delay some construction timelines without redirecting the industry's broader commitment to AI infrastructure investment.

  ## Alternative power sources under evaluation

  Cheng said data center operators are already pursuing diverse on-site generation options to bridge the supply gap, including gas turbine generators, solid oxide fuel cells (SOFC) — ceramic electrochemical devices that convert fuel directly into electricity without combustion — and other local generation solutions. He noted that some manufacturers previously focused on military engine production have pivoted into industrial power generation equipment, which he said reflects the urgency operators feel about securing additional supply.

  Delta Electronics has invested in SOFC technology and said overseas large-scale customers are currently in a validation stage with its systems. Small modular reactors (SMR) — compact, factory-built nuclear units smaller than conventional plants — are attracting market interest, and Cheng said actual deployments could be visible before 2030 as regulatory review processes advance.

  ## Efficiency constraints alongside supply constraints

  Beyond adding generation capacity, Cheng said reducing losses during power conversion and transmission is an equally important challenge. He identified microgrid dispatch optimization, improvements in power conversion efficiency, and shortening transmission distances as measures that can improve overall energy utilization.

  High-voltage direct current (HVDC) systems — which transmit electricity at high voltage in direct-current form, incurring lower conversion losses than conventional alternating-current distribution — represent a key architectural upgrade for data centers, Cheng said. Deploying HVDC requires electricians holding specialized high-voltage certifications, and the United States faces a shortage of qualified workers. He said the long training pipeline required for that credential could further slow adoption of the new architecture.

  Liu added that high-voltage electricians must hold special licenses requiring approximately 8,000 hours of training, or roughly four to five years, and that the labor shortage has already affected construction schedules. Community opposition from residents near proposed data center sites has also caused some U.S. construction plans to be deferred, he said.

  ## Industry implications

  Cheng described data center investment as a structural long-term trend that will persist despite near-term constraints. The combined pressures of power shortages, equipment supply bottlenecks, and skilled labor deficits may produce short-term fluctuations in construction timelines, he said, but will also compel operators and vendors to invest more heavily in advanced power supply technologies and energy efficiency solutions — driving continued evolution in data center power architecture.

  Delta Electronics said it expects the first real-world SMR deployments to become visible before 2030, subject to regulatory progress.
body_zh: |
  台達電（Delta Electronics）董事長鄭平週四表示，隨著運算需求增速遠超電力基礎建設的擴充腳步，AI資料中心的電力短缺「無可避免」；其公司投資部門更預測，全球資料中心用電量將在未來四年內倍增。

  台達電企業投資事業部副總裁劉良甫表示，目前全球資料中心每年用電量約5,000億度（kWh），相當於全球總用電量的約1.5%。他預測，這一數字到2030年可能達到1兆度。劉良甫指出，由於電力基礎建設的規劃與施工需要數年時間，新增發電容量未必能跟上資料中心的擴張腳步，這樣的落差可能拖延部分建設時程，但不會動搖整體產業對AI基礎建設的長期投入。

  ## 替代電源方案持續評估

  鄭平表示，資料中心業者已積極尋求多樣化的現地發電方案以彌補供電缺口，選項涵蓋燃氣渦輪發電機、固態氧化物燃料電池（SOFC）——一種利用陶瓷電化學裝置直接將燃料轉換為電力、無需燃燒的設備——以及其他本地發電解決方案。他指出，部分原本專注於軍用引擎生產的製造商已轉型投入工業發電設備領域，他認為這反映了業者在確保額外電力供應上的迫切需求。

  台達電已投資SOFC技術，並表示海外大型客戶目前正處於系統驗證階段。小型模組化反應爐（SMR）——體積遠小於傳統核電廠、可在工廠預製的緊湊型核能裝置——也引發市場高度關注。鄭平表示，隨著法規審查程序持續推進，2030年以前應可見到實際部署案例。

  ## 供電與效率雙重挑戰

  鄭平表示，除了增加發電容量之外，降低電力轉換與傳輸過程中的損耗同樣是不可忽視的挑戰。他點出微電網調度優化、提升電力轉換效率，以及縮短傳輸距離等措施，均有助於改善整體能源利用率。

  鄭平表示，高壓直流電（HVDC）系統——以高壓直流形式輸送電力，相較於傳統交流配電系統可大幅降低轉換損耗——是資料中心架構升級的關鍵方向。部署HVDC需要持有高壓專業認證的電氣技師，而美國目前正面臨合格人力短缺的困境。他指出，取得該證照所需的漫長訓練週期，可能進一步拖慢新架構的普及速度。

  劉良甫補充指出，高壓電氣技師須持有特殊執照，考取過程約需8,000小時的訓練，相當於約四至五年，勞動力短缺問題已對建設時程造成實質影響。他表示，美國部分資料中心建設計畫也因鄰近社區居民反對而被迫延後。

  ## 產業影響

  鄭平將資料中心投資定調為長期結構性趨勢，認為不會因短期挑戰而中斷。他表示，電力短缺、設備供應瓶頸與技術人力不足等多重壓力，或許會在短期內造成建設時程的波動，但也將迫使業者與供應商加大對先進電源技術及能源效率解決方案的投入，進而推動資料中心電力架構的持續演進。

  台達電表示，在法規進展順利的前提下，預計首批SMR實際部署案例將在2030年前出現。
