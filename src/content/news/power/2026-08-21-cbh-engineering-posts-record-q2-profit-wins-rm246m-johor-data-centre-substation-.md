body_en: |
  CBH Engineering Holding Bhd, a Malaysian provider of mechanical and electrical engineering services, posted a record second-quarter net profit that doubled year-on-year and disclosed a RM246 million contract to install a 275-kilovolt substation for a data centre in Johor, sending shares to consecutive all-time highs over two trading sessions.

  ## Record Q2 results

  Net profit for the three months ended June 30, 2026, rose to RM24.53 million (Malaysian ringgit) from RM10.11 million a year earlier, while revenue doubled to RM120.93 million from RM59.68 million, the company said in a filing with Bursa Malaysia, the country's stock exchange. Both figures set quarterly records.

  For the first half of 2026, net profit reached RM42.73 million, up from RM15.84 million, as revenue climbed to RM210.47 million from RM96.12 million.

  CBH Engineering declared an interim dividend of 2.7 sen per share, payable Sept. 30.

  ## RM246 million Johor substation contract

  CBH Engineering's wholly-owned unit, CBH Engineering Sdn Bhd, signed a RM246 million contract on July 21 for the delivery and installation of high-voltage transformers and associated equipment for a 275kV (kilovolt) substation at a Johor data centre, according to a separate bourse filing. Work commenced July 3 and is scheduled for completion Sept. 15, 2028.

  The contract was awarded by an undisclosed private company incorporated in Malaysia. CBH Engineering described the client as principally engaged in data centre services and telecommunications network services, information technology services, and artificial intelligence graphics processing unit (GPU) services. The client's identity cannot be disclosed due to a non-disclosure agreement, the company said.

  ## Order book and outlook

  CBH Engineering's cumulative outstanding order book stood at RM744 million at end-June. The company said it anticipated "favourable outcomes" in coming quarters as it pursues contracts in both data centre and non-data centre segments, adding that it "remains cautiously optimistic on its prospects in the power distribution systems industry, supported by its competitive positioning, commendable business fundamentals and favourable general industry outlook."

  ## Market reaction

  Shares rose 6.76%, or five sen, to 79 sen on Wednesday following the earnings release, valuing the company at RM1.48 billion. CBH Engineering priced its initial public offering (IPO) at 28 sen when it listed in January 2026; the stock has since more than doubled.

  On Thursday, shares climbed as much as 16.5%, or 13 sen, to a peak of 92 sen, surpassing Bloomberg's consensus target price, before closing at 84.5 sen for a market capitalization of approximately RM1.59 billion. CBH Engineering was among the most actively traded stocks on Bursa Malaysia, with more than 80 million shares changing hands.

  ## Analyst views

  All five research houses covering CBH Engineering rated the stock a "buy" as of Thursday, with target prices ranging from 67 sen to RM1.11, according to The Edge Malaysia. CBH Engineering has nearly doubled its share price in 2026 on the back of contracts tied to new data centres in Malaysia built to support demand for advanced computing and artificial intelligence applications, The Edge Malaysia reported.

  Public Investment Bank raised its target price to 91 sen, applying an 18 times forward earnings multiple versus 17 times previously, citing "consistent job wins that leads to improving earnings trajectory." The bank said the RM744 million order book could provide earnings visibility over the next two years and that growth prospects remain positive, "underpinned by healthy tender pipeline and structural data centre sector tailwinds."

  Phillip Capital, which holds the highest target price among the covering analysts, said second-half 2026 earnings could be even stronger, "supported by an improving contract flow and a robust replenishment pipeline."

  CBH Engineering Holding Bhd（以下簡稱CBH Engineering）為馬來西亞一家機電工程服務供應商，公布第二季淨利按年翻倍並創下歷史新高，同時披露一筆2.46億令吉的合約，將為柔佛一座資料中心興建275千伏（kV）變電站，消息帶動股價連續兩個交易日刷新歷史高位。

  ## 第二季業績創紀錄

  公司向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）呈交的文件顯示，截至2026年6月30日止三個月，CBH Engineering淨利由去年同期的1,011萬令吉大幅增至2,453萬令吉，收入亦同步翻倍，從5,968萬令吉增至1億2,093萬令吉，兩項數據均創下季度歷史紀錄。

  2026年上半年，淨利從1,584萬令吉增至4,273萬令吉，收入則從9,612萬令吉攀升至2億1,047萬令吉。

  CBH Engineering宣派中期股息每股2.7仙，將於9月30日派發。

  ## 2.46億令吉柔佛變電站合約

  CBH Engineering的全資子公司CBH Engineering Sdn Bhd，於7月21日簽署一份2.46億令吉的合約，負責為柔佛一座資料中心的275千伏（kV）變電站提供及安裝高壓變壓器及相關設備。工程已於7月3日展開，預計於2028年9月15日完工。

  此合約由一家在馬來西亞登記成立、未予披露名稱的私人公司所授予。CBH Engineering表示，該客戶主要業務涵蓋資料中心服務、電信網路服務、資訊科技服務，以及人工智慧繪圖處理器（GPU）服務，惟因與客戶簽有保密協議，公司無法公開其身份。

  ## 訂單簿與業務展望

  截至6月底，CBH Engineering累計在手訂單總額達7.44億令吉。公司表示，隨著積極拓展資料中心及非資料中心領域的合約，預期未來數季將錄得「理想成果」，並補充指公司「對電力配電系統行業的前景持審慎樂觀態度，有賴公司的競爭優勢、良好的業務基本面及整體行業的有利展望所支撐」。

  ## 市場反應

  消息公布後，CBH Engineering股價周三上漲6.76%，即每股5仙，收報79仙，公司市值達14.8億令吉。公司於2026年1月上市，首次公開發行（IPO）定價為28仙，上市至今股價已逾翻倍。

  周四，股價盤中最多急升16.5%，即13仙，最高觸及92仙，一度超越彭博市場共識目標價，其後回落，以84.5仙報收，市值約15.9億令吉。當日CBH Engineering躋身馬來西亞交易所交投最活躍股票之列，成交股數逾8,000萬股。

  ## 分析師觀點

  據《The Edge Malaysia》報道，截至周四，追蹤CBH Engineering的五家研究機構均給予「買進」評級，目標價介於67仙至1令吉11仙之間。報道亦指出，2026年以來CBH Engineering股價接近翻倍，主要受惠於馬來西亞新建資料中心帶來的合約，這些資料中心旨在滿足先進運算及人工智慧應用的需求。

  大眾投資銀行（Public Investment Bank）將目標價上調至91仙，所採用的預測本益比從17倍提高至18倍，理由是「持續斬獲合約，帶動獲利軌跡持續向上」。該行表示，7.44億令吉的在手訂單可為未來兩年的獲利提供能見度，增長前景維持正面，「有賴健康的投標管道及資料中心行業的結構性順風所支撐」。

  目標價最高的覆蓋機構Phillip Capital則預期，「受持續改善的合約流入及強勁的補單管道所支撐」，CBH Engineering 2026年下半年的盈利表現或將更為亮眼。
