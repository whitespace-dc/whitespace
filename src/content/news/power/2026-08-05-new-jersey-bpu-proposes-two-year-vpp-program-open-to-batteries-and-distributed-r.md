body_en: |
  New Jersey's Board of Public Utilities released a straw proposal on July 15 outlining a two-year virtual power plant program that would let customer-sited batteries and other distributed energy resources — devices small enough to sit in a home or commercial building — earn payments for distribution-level grid services while also participating in the PJM Interconnection wholesale electricity market.

  The program, described in the proposal as "explicitly transitional," would be administered by the state's four electric distribution companies (EDCs) and begin no later than July 1, 2027. A second, fully market-based and open-access VPP tariff phase would follow starting in 2029 and run indefinitely, the BPU said.

  The BPU accepted written comments on the proposal through Aug. 17 and held an all-day public stakeholder meeting on July 30 at which utility representatives detailed existing and planned battery programs.

  ## Program structure and stacking payments

  The straw proposal's most consequential feature for distributed energy developers and aggregators — companies that bundle small resources into a single grid asset — is the permission to stack payments. Where PJM Interconnection rules allow, enrolled devices could collect compensation for both distribution-level services and wholesale capacity market participation simultaneously, avoiding the double-counting restrictions that have historically limited the value proposition of residential batteries.

  PJM operates the wholesale electricity market across 13 states and the District of Columbia, including New Jersey.

  The two-year transitional program would use existing advanced metering infrastructure, direct load control platforms and demand response customers already enrolled under New Jersey's Triennium energy-efficiency program, the BPU said. That foundation limits the new investment required to launch the first phase.

  The second phase, beginning in 2029, will require "substantial stakeholder inputs, EDC investment, and third-party systems integration around a fully specified service architecture," the BPU said, signaling that the open-access architecture is years away from being fully specified.

  ## Governing principles

  The BPU outlined eight principles for the program. Among those it named explicitly: equitable design and inclusive participation; technology neutrality; non-discriminatory aggregator access; friction-free enrollment and exit for qualified resources; and cross-program coordination to prevent double compensation.

  ## Executive order origin

  The proposal fulfills a directive in Executive Order No. 2, one of several energy-related executive orders that Democratic New Jersey Gov. Mikie Sherrill signed upon taking office in January. That order set capacity targets and timelines for BPU procurement of distributed, community-scale and utility-scale solar and energy storage and required the BPU to develop a VPP program within 180 days. It also directed the agency to look for opportunities to expand third-party electricity supply offerings and to enable policy changes that would allow distributed assets to participate "to the fullest extent possible" in the PJM capacity market.

  The BPU said it would coordinate the straw proposal with a virtual power plant request for information it issued on April 20, the multi-phase Garden State Energy Storage Program proceeding, and several proceedings on passive and managed electric vehicle charging.

  ## Utility programs already in motion

  At the July 30 stakeholder meeting, PSE&G New Jersey said it plans to launch a VPP program in the near term offering an upfront incentive of approximately $5,000 for an 8-kilowatt home battery, with the remaining installation balance repaid through PSE&G's on-bill repayment program. In exchange, the customer would allow PSE&G to discharge the battery during peak-shaving events — periods when the utility calls on the device to reduce demand on the distribution grid.

  Tim Fagan, manager for planning and evaluation at PSE&G, said the utility believes "several thousand" small-scale batteries are already deployed by resilience-minded customers in New Jersey, representing a base that could be enrolled in a pay-for-performance program without requiring new hardware.

  "Certainly we see a lot of opportunities for expansion ... there's an opportunity to enroll existing battery owners into a similar type of VPP program which would be a pay-for-performance type of program," Fagan said at the meeting.

  Fagan also urged the BPU to consider permitting EDCs to own energy storage directly, citing precedents in Massachusetts, New York, Maryland, Hawaii and Minnesota. He said geographically targeted procurements for customer-sited batteries have recently fallen short in Hawaii and Massachusetts, and that utility-owned storage can serve as a backstop to add capacity on congested distribution circuits.

  ## Bring-your-own-battery pilot raises attrition questions

  Andrew Bayne, manager for energy efficiency programs at Pepco Holdings (PHI), described a Delaware "bring your own battery" pilot with between 100 and 300 enrolled devices. Participating Delmarva Power customers receive an estimated $1,080 in annual performance payments deposited directly rather than issued as bill credits, Bayne said.

  PHI is considering whether findings from that pilot could be extended to Atlantic City Electric, its New Jersey utility. But Bayne said the company is still working through fundamental questions about customer retention — specifically, whether annual payments of roughly $1,000 are sufficient to keep customers enrolled when the utility cycles their battery repeatedly, and how many dispatch events customers will accept in a given season before dropping out.

  "These devices behave differently when you call upon them," Bayne said. "That behavior drives customer attrition from the program ... [which affects] the value we can glean."

  ## Significance

  The New Jersey proposal places the state among a growing number of jurisdictions in both restructured electricity markets — where customers can choose their supplier — and vertically integrated utility markets moving toward open-access VPP frameworks that allow third-party aggregators to control residential batteries, electric vehicles and other small-scale resources as a single grid asset. The stacking of distribution and wholesale payments is particularly significant because it improves the economics of residential storage investment, potentially accelerating adoption.

  The BPU's comment period on the straw proposal runs through Aug. 17, with the agency expected to refine the framework based on stakeholder input before issuing a final order.
body_zh: |
  紐澤西公用事業委員會（BPU）於7月15日發布初步提案，概述一項為期兩年的虛擬電廠計畫。該計畫將讓用戶側電池及其他分散式能源資源——包括可安裝於住宅或商業建築的小型設備——在參與PJM互聯公司躉售電力市場的同時，亦能獲取配電層級電網服務的補償。

  該計畫在提案中被定性為「明確的過渡性計畫」，將由全州四家電力配售公司（EDC）負責執行，最遲於2027年7月1日啟動。BPU表示，第二階段將採完全市場化、開放接入的虛擬電廠費率架構，預計自2029年起無限期推行。

  BPU接受書面意見的截止日期為8月17日，並於7月30日舉行全天候公開利害關係人會議，電力公司代表於會中詳述現行及規劃中的電池儲能計畫。

  ## 計畫架構與疊加補償

  對於分散式能源開發商及聚合商（即將小型資源整合為單一電網資產的業者）而言，初步提案最具關鍵意義的特點在於允許疊加補償。在PJM互聯公司規則許可的範圍內，已登錄設備可同時獲取配電層級服務及躉售容量市場參與的雙重報酬，突破了過去長期限制住宅電池價值主張的重複計算限制。

  PJM負責運營涵蓋13個州及哥倫比亞特區（包括紐澤西）的躉售電力市場。

  這項為期兩年的過渡計畫將利用現有的智慧電錶基礎設施、直接負載控制平台，以及已在紐澤西「三年期」節能計畫下登錄的需量反應用戶。BPU表示，此一基礎可降低第一階段所需的新增投資。

  BPU指出，2029年啟動的第二階段將需要「廣泛的利害關係人投入、電力配售公司的資金挹注，以及圍繞完整服務架構規格的第三方系統整合」，意味著開放接入架構距離完整規劃仍需數年時間。

  ## 指導原則

  BPU為該計畫訂定了八項指導原則，明確列舉的包括：公平設計與包容性參與、技術中立、聚合商非歧視性接入、符合資格資源的無障礙登錄與退出機制，以及跨計畫協調以防止重複補償。

  ## 行政命令的起源

  此提案履行了第2號行政命令的指令。該命令是民主黨籍紐澤西州長米琪·雪芮爾（Mikie Sherrill）於今年1月就職時簽署的多項能源相關行政命令之一。該命令設定了BPU採購分散式、社區規模及公用事業規模太陽能與儲能設施的容量目標與時程，並要求BPU在180天內制定虛擬電廠計畫。此外，該命令也指示主管機關尋求擴大第三方電力供應方案的機會，並推動政策變革，讓分散式資產「盡可能全面」參與PJM容量市場。

  BPU表示，將把此初步提案與其於4月20日發布的虛擬電廠資訊徵集（RFI）、多階段的「花園州儲能計畫」程序，以及數項電動車被動式與管理式充電相關程序進行協調整合。

  ## 已在推進的電力公司計畫

  在7月30日的利害關係人會議上，PSE&G紐澤西表示計畫近期推出虛擬電廠計畫，為8千瓦住宅電池提供約5,000美元的前期補貼，其餘安裝費用則透過PSE&G的帳單分期還款計畫償還。作為交換，用戶須允許PSE&G在尖峰削減事件期間——即電力公司要求設備降低配電網用電需求的時段——對電池進行放電調度。

  PSE&G規劃與評估部門經理提姆·費根（Tim Fagan）表示，該公司認為紐澤西目前已有「數千台」由重視備援韌性的用戶自行部署的小型電池，這批設備無需新增硬體即可登錄按績效付費計畫。

  費根在會議上表示：「我們確實看到許多擴展機會……有機會將現有電池用戶納入類似的虛擬電廠計畫，也就是按績效付費模式的計畫。」

  費根同時呼籲BPU考慮允許電力配售公司直接持有儲能設施，援引麻薩諸塞州、紐約州、馬里蘭州、夏威夷及明尼蘇達州的先例。他表示，近期夏威夷和麻薩諸塞州針對用戶側電池的地理定向採購均未能達標，公用事業自有儲能可作為緩解壅塞配電線路容量不足的後備機制。

  ## 自備電池試點引發流失率疑慮

  Pepco Holdings（PHI）節能計畫部門經理安德魯·貝恩（Andrew Bayne）介紹了一項德拉瓦州的「自備電池」試點計畫，目前有100至300台設備登錄。貝恩表示，參與的德馬瓦電力（Delmarva Power）用戶每年可獲得約1,080美元的績效付款，款項直接存入帳戶，而非以帳單折抵方式發放。

  PHI正評估能否將試點計畫的成果延伸至旗下紐澤西電力公司大西洋城電力（Atlantic City Electric）。但貝恩表示，公司仍在研究客戶留存的根本問題——具體而言，每年約1,000美元的補償是否足以在電力公司頻繁調度電池的情況下留住用戶，以及用戶在一個季度內能接受多少次調度事件後才會退出計畫。

  貝恩表示：「這些設備在被調用時的反應各有不同。這種行為差異會導致用戶從計畫中流失……（進而影響）我們能從中獲取的價值。」

  ## 重要意義

  紐澤西此次提案，使該州躋身於日益增多的司法管轄區之列——無論是開放用戶自由選擇供電商的電力市場重組地區，還是垂直整合型電力公司市場，都在朝向開放接入的虛擬電廠架構邁進，允許第三方聚合商將住宅電池、電動車及其他小型資源作為單一電網資產進行統一管理。配電與躉售補償的疊加機制尤具重要意義，因為它改善了住宅儲能投資的經濟性，有望加速市場普及。

  BPU對初步提案的意見徵集期限至8月17日截止，主管機關預計將在彙整利害關係人意見後，進一步完善框架並發布最終命令。
