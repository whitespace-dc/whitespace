body_en: |
  ABB says its digital substation technology can cut grid interconnection installation time by up to half and reduce project costs by 10 to 15 percent, as power providers face data center demand that has grown from projects seeking tens of megawatts to sites requiring multiple gigawatts of capacity.

  ## Scale of demand

  Data center interconnection requests have expanded by an order of magnitude, according to ABB. Where projects once sought 10 to 50 megawatts (MW) per site, today's developments span 500 MW to 3 gigawatts (GW), with one unnamed project projected to require 9 GW at full build.

  ## How digital substations differ from analog

  Traditional substations connect protection relays, sensors, and control systems through point-to-point copper wiring, a labor-intensive method ABB says is a principal bottleneck for new interconnections. ABB's digital substations replace that architecture with Ethernet-based digital communication, reducing total wiring by up to 80 percent, the company said. That change cuts installation time by up to half and simplifies commissioning and testing, ABB said.

  ## Cost impact

  ABB claims its digital substations reduce capital project costs by 10 to 15 percent, driven by lower labor requirements during installation. The company says its digital switchgear — available in indoor and outdoor variants — lowers operating expenses by up to 30 percent compared with conventional equipment, with lower ongoing maintenance costs beyond that.

  Two specific products underpin those maintenance claims. ABB's R-MAG circuit breaker uses magnetic actuation rather than a traditional spring mechanism, giving it fewer moving parts and reducing maintenance frequency, the company said. The R-MAG's digital version also lowers capital costs by substituting Ethernet-based digital communication for point-to-point copper wiring in outdoor substations. ABB's AMVAC circuit breaker is built around a single moving part, which the company says delivers reliability gains and reduced maintenance costs.

  ## AI-powered fault prediction

  ABB says its digital substations incorporate artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) to shift grid operations from reactive to predictive. The company's anomaly detection system continuously monitors voltage and current patterns to identify abnormal behavior before faults occur. A separate AI-powered fault prediction system, which draws on data already collected by protection relays without requiring additional sensors, can forecast potential failures with more than 90 percent accuracy up to one week in advance, ABB said.

  That predictive capability allows operators to pursue condition-based maintenance rather than fixed-schedule cycles or emergency repairs after failures, reducing truck rolls, extending equipment life, and limiting customer-impacting outages, ABB said. The analytics can also be applied to vegetation management, storm preparedness, and wildfire monitoring, the company added.

  ## Space efficiency and integration with existing infrastructure

  ABB's digital substations occupy 50 percent less physical space than analog equivalents, the company said, preserving equipment yard capacity for future expansion. The technology is also designed to integrate with existing analog substations, allowing power providers to add digital capacity incrementally rather than replacing entire installations, ABB said. That approach lets utilities continue to derive value from past investments while modernizing in steps.

  ## Higher-voltage supply and grid flexibility

  Data center operators are shifting to higher medium-voltage (MV) distribution inside their facilities as graphics processing unit (GPU) density increases. ABB said its digital substations simplify delivery of higher-voltage supply at the interconnection point and that, because the company works with both data center operators and the utilities serving them, it can coordinate protection systems and optimize the electrical architecture from the grid connection to the server rack.

  Digital substations also support integration with renewable energy sources, battery storage, and microgrids by enabling a centralized and virtualized protection, control, and automation architecture, in place of the decentralized model driven by individual intelligent electronic devices (IEDs) common in traditional substations, ABB said.

  ## Supply chain

  Long equipment lead times remain an obstacle to faster interconnection. ABB said it operates manufacturing facilities in North America capable of delivering digital substations at scale on accelerated timelines, backed by a global supply chain built with redundancy to prevent component shortages. ABB said it was the first company to bring digital switchgear to market.

  ## 需求規模

  根據ABB的說法，資料中心的電網互連申請量已大幅成長，規模擴張了一個數量級。過去每個場址僅需10至50百萬瓦（MW），如今的開發案規模動輒達500MW至3吉瓦（GW），其中一個未具名專案在全面建設完成後，預計用電需求將高達9GW。

  ## 數位變電站與類比變電站的差異

  傳統變電站透過點對點銅線配線，連接保護繼電器、感測器與控制系統，ABB指出這種耗工的做法是新互連案的主要瓶頸。ABB的數位變電站以乙太網路數位通訊取代上述架構，可將總配線量減少多達80%，ABB表示。這項變革能將安裝工期縮短最多一半，並簡化試運轉與測試程序，ABB表示。

  ## 成本效益

  ABB宣稱，其數位變電站可將資本專案成本降低10%至15%，主要得益於安裝期間所需人力的減少。ABB指出，其數位開關設備（提供室內與室外兩種規格）與傳統設備相比，可降低最多30%的營運費用，此外日常維護成本亦更低。

  有兩款特定產品支撐了前述維護成本的說法。ABB的R-MAG斷路器採用磁力驅動機構，取代傳統彈簧機構，因此活動零件較少、維護頻率得以降低，ABB表示。R-MAG的數位版本亦以乙太網路數位通訊取代室外變電站的點對點銅線配線，藉此壓低資本成本。ABB的AMVAC斷路器則以單一活動零件為核心設計，ABB表示這能帶來可靠度提升與維護成本降低的效益。

  ## AI驅動的故障預測

  ABB表示，其數位變電站整合了人工智慧（AI）與機器學習（ML），使電網運作模式從被動應對轉為主動預測。ABB的異常偵測系統持續監測電壓與電流波形，以便在故障發生前識別異常行為。另一套AI驅動的故障預測系統則直接運用保護繼電器已收集的數據，無需加裝額外感測器，即可提前最多一週預測潛在故障，預測準確率逾90%，ABB表示。

  這項預測能力讓操作人員得以採取依設備狀態進行的維護策略，而非遵循固定排程、或在故障發生後才進行緊急搶修，進而減少出車次數、延長設備使用壽命，並降低影響用戶的停電事故，ABB表示。ABB補充，這套分析系統亦可應用於植被管理、暴風雨備災與野火監測。

  ## 空間效率與現有基礎設施整合

  ABB的數位變電站所占實體空間比類比設備少50%，ABB表示，這有助於保留設備區空間以供未來擴建使用。此外，該技術亦設計為可與現有類比變電站整合，讓電力業者得以逐步增設數位容量，而非全面替換既有設施，ABB表示。這種方式讓公用事業業者得以在分步推進現代化的同時，繼續發揮過往投資的價值。

  ## 更高電壓供電與電網靈活性

  隨著圖形處理器（GPU）密度持續提升，資料中心營運商正逐步朝設施內部採用更高中壓（MV）配電的方向轉型。ABB表示，其數位變電站能簡化在互連點提供更高電壓供電的作業，且由於ABB同時服務資料中心營運商與其供電公用事業業者，因此能協調保護系統，並針對從電網接入點到伺服器機架的完整電氣架構進行最佳化。

  數位變電站亦支援再生能源、電池儲能與微電網的整合，其採用集中式、虛擬化的保護、控制與自動化架構，取代傳統變電站中以個別智慧型電子裝置（IED）驅動的分散式模型，ABB表示。

  ## 供應鏈

  設備採購前置時間過長，仍是加速互連的一大障礙。ABB表示，其在北美設有製造基地，能夠按加速時程大量交付數位變電站，並依托具備備援設計的全球供應鏈，防範零組件短缺風險。ABB表示，該公司是全球首家將數位開關設備推向市場的業者。
