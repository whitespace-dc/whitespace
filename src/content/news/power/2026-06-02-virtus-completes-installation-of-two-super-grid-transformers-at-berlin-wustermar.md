# Virtus completes installation of two super-grid transformers at Berlin Wustermark Campus

Virtus has completed the installation of two super-grid transformers at its Wustermark Campus in Berlin, Germany. The transformers are designed to support power delivery to the 300MW facility. The infrastructure upgrade strengthens the campus's power distribution capacity.

## 中文

# 維爾圖斯完成柏林雙座超級電網變壓器安裝

維爾圖斯已完成在柏林沃斯特馬克園區安裝兩具超級電網變壓器。該變壓器用於支持該300MW園區的電力輸配。本次基礎設施升級強化了園區的電力配送容量。
