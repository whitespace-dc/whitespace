body_en: |
  CenterPoint Energy submitted more than 17 GW of large-load projects to the Electric Reliability Council of Texas' new large-load interconnection process and expects 14 GW of that total to qualify, the company said Tuesday during its second quarter 2026 earnings call. The 14 GW would exceed 65% of the Houston-area utility's current peak system demand of 21 GW.

  Texas regulators approved the new large-load interconnection rules in June. The first cohort of projects to move through the process is designated "Batch Zero."

  ## How the 14 GW breaks down

  Of the 14 GW CenterPoint expects to advance, roughly 10 GW have completed all required ERCOT studies and are eligible for "base load" designation, CEO Jason Wells said. The remaining 4 GW are positioned to qualify as "studied load" because each project has received approval of one of the two required ERCOT studies, Wells said.

  The remaining 3 GW of CenterPoint's Batch Zero submissions — those not included in the 14 GW figure — represent customer demand that is still pending ERCOT approval of the required studies, the company said.

  All 14 GW are backed by signed facility extension agreements, long-term end-user commitments from customers, and approximately $900 million in customer cash commitments and deposits, Wells said. He added that the projects have "clear line of sight to the materials execution capability and system capacity to serve."

  "We are confident the combined 14 GW of base load and study load are well positioned to move forward in the Batch Zero process, given the level of customer commitments already secured," Wells told analysts.

  ## Energization timeline

  CenterPoint anticipates bringing nearly all of the 14 GW online by the end of 2030, Wells said, describing the expected pace based on "projected load ramps sought by customers."

  The utility is projecting 50% load growth overall by the end of 2029. The 14 GW of large-load demand at the transmission level would be supplemented by an additional 2 GW of increased demand at the distribution level — the local network serving homes and businesses — over the next several years, Wells said. The company attributed the distribution-level growth to reshoring of advanced manufacturing and continued population growth in the Greater Houston area.

  ## Capital plan and customer rates

  CenterPoint increased its 10-year capital investment plan by $1.2 billion, bringing the total to $66.7 billion through 2035. The company said the increase reflects incremental investment to support accelerating large-load demand in Houston as well as higher costs tied to its Downtown Houston Revitalization project.

  The utility said the influx of large-load customers is expected to benefit existing residential and commercial ratepayers. Wells said the 14 GW of additional demand would generate more than $5 billion in savings for residential and commercial customers over the next decade, without specifying the mechanism.

  CenterPoint serves almost 2.8 million metered customers in Houston and dozens of retail electricity providers that buy wholesale power.

  ## Q2 2026 earnings

  For the second quarter of 2026, CenterPoint reported consolidated net income of $244 million, up from $198 million in the same period of 2025.

  ## Indiana territory

  While Texas is CenterPoint's largest service territory, the company said it also sees potential for "transformational" large-load opportunities in its Indiana electric service area, where it serves more than 150,000 customers in Southwestern Indiana. Wells said the company has begun work to serve a new customer that would represent "the single largest load we serve in the region," without identifying the customer or stating its size.

  "As a result of commitment from the customer, we have already begun work to serve this load," Wells said, adding that the company is engaged with other potential large-load customers in the same area. He described the associated capital investments as incremental and outside the company's current base plan.

  ## F.B. Culley coal plant

  Separately, CenterPoint is managing a federal directive to keep a coal-fired power plant running in Indiana. The U.S. Department of Energy issued emergency orders in December, March, and June directing CenterPoint to continue operating the F.B. Culley Unit 2 coal plant through September 19, despite the utility's plans to retire the unit.

  CenterPoint filed a complaint with the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, or FERC, seeking creation of a cost recovery and cost allocation mechanism. In March, FERC directed the Midcontinent Independent System Operator, the grid operator for the region, to adopt a tariff amendment authorizing the unit to recover costs. CenterPoint said it plans to make a separate FERC filing to seek recovery of all costs incurred to comply with the DOE emergency orders, and has also filed an application with Indiana regulators to recover any compliance costs not addressed through the federal proceedings.

  ## What it means

  The scale of Batch Zero — and CenterPoint's expectation that 14 GW will advance — illustrates the magnitude of large-load demand materializing in Texas, driven in large part by data centers and industrial customers. If the 14 GW is ultimately energized, it would transform the load profile of the Houston-area grid and require substantial network investment over the next four to five years.

  CenterPoint said the next step for the 3 GW still pending required studies is ERCOT approval of those studies.
body_zh: |
  CenterPoint Energy於週二舉行的2026年第二季財報電話會議上表示，該公司已向德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）新設立的大型負載併網程序提交逾17 GW的專案，並預計其中14 GW將符合資格。這14 GW的容量將超越休士頓地區電力公用事業現行21 GW系統尖峰需求的65%。

  德州監管機構於6月批准了新的大型負載併網規則，首批進入此流程的專案群組被命名為「批次零」（Batch Zero）。

  ## 14 GW的組成架構

  執行長傑森·威爾斯（Jason Wells）表示，在CenterPoint預計推進的14 GW中，約10 GW已完成ERCOT所有必要研究，符合「基礎負載」認定資格。其餘4 GW則因各專案已取得兩項必要ERCOT研究中之一項的核准，有望以「研究負載」資格取得認定。

  公司表示，CenterPoint「批次零」提交案中的其餘3 GW——即未計入14 GW數字的部分——代表仍待ERCOT核准必要研究的客戶需求。

  威爾斯表示，全部14 GW均有已簽署的設施延伸協議、客戶提供的長期終端用戶承諾，以及約9億美元的客戶現金承諾與保證金作為支撐。他補充道，這些專案對材料執行能力與服務所需的系統容量均「具備清晰的推進路徑」。

  威爾斯告訴分析師：「鑑於已確保的客戶承諾規模，我們有信心基礎負載與研究負載合計共14 GW，均在批次零程序中處於有利推進地位。」

  ## 供電時程

  威爾斯表示，CenterPoint預計將14 GW中的絕大多數於2030年底前完成上線，描述此進度預測係基於「客戶所尋求的負載爬升預測」。

  該公用事業公司預計至2029年底整體負載將成長50%。威爾斯表示，輸電層級14 GW的大型負載需求，還將在未來數年內輔以配電層級——即服務住家與商業用戶的本地電網——額外新增的2 GW需求。公司將配電層級的成長歸因於先進製造業回流，以及大休士頓地區持續的人口增長。

  ## 資本計畫與用電費率

  CenterPoint將其十年資本投資計畫上調12億美元，使總額達到2035年底前667億美元。公司表示，此次調增反映了為因應休士頓大型負載需求加速成長所需的增量投資，以及與其「休士頓市中心振興計畫」相關的較高成本。

  該公用事業公司表示，大型負載客戶的大量湧入預計將使現有住宅與商業用電戶受益。威爾斯表示，這14 GW的額外需求將在未來十年為住宅與商業客戶節省逾50億美元，但未具體說明節省的機制為何。

  CenterPoint在休士頓服務近280萬計費用戶，以及數十家向其購買批發電力的零售電力業者。

  ## 2026年第二季財報

  就2026年第二季而言，CenterPoint公告合併淨利為2.44億美元，較2025年同期的1.98億美元有所提升。

  ## 印第安納州服務區域

  雖然德州是CenterPoint最大的服務區域，該公司表示在其印第安納州電力服務區域亦看到「具變革性」的大型負載商機——該公司在印第安納州西南部服務逾15萬名用戶。威爾斯表示，公司已著手為一名新客戶提供服務，該客戶的用電規模將成為「我們在該地區所服務的單一最大負載」，但未透露客戶身份或其用電規模。

  「由於客戶的承諾，我們已著手啟動服務此一負載的相關工程，」威爾斯說，並補充公司目前正與同一地區的其他潛在大型負載客戶積極洽談。他將相關資本投資描述為增量性質，且不在公司目前的基礎計畫範圍內。

  ## F.B. Culley燃煤電廠

  另一方面，CenterPoint正依聯邦指令在印第安納州維持一座燃煤電廠的持續運轉。美國能源部分別於去年12月、今年3月及6月發布緊急命令，指示CenterPoint繼續運轉F.B. Culley第二號機組燃煤電廠至9月19日，儘管該公用事業公司原計畫退役該機組。

  CenterPoint已向聯邦能源監管委員會（FERC）提出申訴，請求建立成本回收與成本分攤機制。今年3月，FERC指示負責該地區電網調度的中大陸獨立系統運轉業者（MISO）採用費率修正案，授權該機組進行成本回收。CenterPoint表示，計畫另行向FERC提出申請，以追償為遵循能源部緊急命令所產生的全部費用，並已向印第安納州監管機構提出申請，就聯邦程序未涵蓋的合規成本部分尋求回收。

  ## 市場意涵

  「批次零」的整體規模——以及CenterPoint預期14 GW將順利推進的信心——充分說明了德州大型負載需求的龐大體量，其驅動力在很大程度上來自資料中心與工業客戶。若這14 GW最終完成供電，將徹底改變休士頓地區電網的負載樣貌，並在未來四至五年內需要大規模的電網投資。

  CenterPoint表示，仍待必要研究核准的3 GW，下一步有待ERCOT核准相關研究。
