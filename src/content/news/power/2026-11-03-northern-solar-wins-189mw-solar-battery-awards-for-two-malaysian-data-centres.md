body_en: |
  Northern Solar Holdings Bhd has secured letters of award for two solar-plus-battery projects with a combined capacity of 189 megawatts (MW) to supply renewable energy to a pair of data centres in Malaysia, the Kuala Lumpur-listed company disclosed in Bursa Malaysia filings on Monday.

  The awards, received through wholly owned subsidiary Northern Solar Assets Sdn Bhd, fall under Malaysia's Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme (CRESS). Together, the two data centres carry a combined annual electricity requirement of approximately 496.69 million kilowatt-hours (kWh).

  ## Project breakdown

  The first award covers a 100MW solar facility paired with a battery energy storage system (BESS), serving a data centre with an annual electricity requirement of 262.8 million kWh.

  The second covers an 89MW solar facility, also equipped with BESS, for a separate data centre with an annual electricity requirement of 233.89 million kWh.

  ## Counterparties undisclosed

  Northern Solar did not disclose the value of either award or identify the awarding parties, citing confidentiality provisions. The company said the first award was issued by a Malaysian company involved in property investment and data centre infrastructure. The second came from a Malaysian subsidiary of a Singapore-based data centre services operator.

  Commencement dates for both projects have yet to be finalised, with further announcements expected once definitive agreements are signed. Northern Solar said it expects the projects to contribute positively to its earnings and net assets per share during their execution.

  ## Shift toward asset ownership

  The awards advance Northern Solar's stated goal of expanding beyond its traditional solar engineering and construction business into owning renewable energy assets and generating recurring income through long-term electricity supply agreements.

  In September, Apex Securities said the group was finalising details of a potential 170MW solar-plus-storage CRESS project that could accelerate that transition. Northern Solar has not clarified whether the two latest awards are related to the project previously described by Apex Securities.

  ## Order book and market position

  Northern Solar also secured a RM34 million contract in October to develop a 9.5MW large-scale solar photovoltaic plant, bringing its total contract wins for the year to RM153.4 million at that point. Its unbilled order book stood at RM189.3 million as of end-June 2026.

  The company transferred its listing from Bursa Malaysia's ACE Market to the Main Market on October 6. Its shares closed down four sen, or 3.54%, at RM1.09 on Monday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM431.2 million.

  Definitive agreements for both CRESS awards remain pending, with commencement dates to be confirmed upon signing.
body_zh: |
  ## Northern Solar獲兩馬來西亞資料中心189MW太陽能儲能標案

  吉隆坡上市公司Northern Solar Holdings Bhd於週一向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）申報，宣布旗下全資子公司Northern Solar Assets Sdn Bhd取得兩項太陽能加電池儲能專案的授標函，合計裝置容量為189百萬瓦（MW），將向馬來西亞兩座資料中心供應再生電力。

  此兩項授標均隸屬於馬來西亞企業再生能源供應計畫（CRESS）框架。兩座資料中心的合計年度用電需求約為4億9,669萬度（kWh）。

  ## 專案細節

  第一項授標涵蓋一座100MW太陽能設施，並配備電池儲能系統（BESS），服務對象為年度用電需求達2億6,280萬度的資料中心。

  第二項授標則涵蓋一座89MW太陽能設施，同樣配備電池儲能系統，服務另一座年度用電需求為2億3,389萬度的資料中心。

  ## 得標方身份保密

  Northern Solar以保密條款為由，未揭露兩項授標的合約金額，亦未透露授標方身份。該公司表示，第一項授標由一家從事房地產投資與資料中心基礎建設的馬來西亞企業所發出；第二項則來自一家總部設於新加坡的資料中心服務業者在馬來西亞的子公司。

  兩項專案的開工日期均尚未確定，待正式協議簽署後將進一步公告。Northern Solar表示，預期這兩項專案在執行期間將對公司盈利及每股淨資產帶來正面貢獻。

  ## 轉型自持資產

  此次授標進一步推進了Northern Solar的既定策略目標——從傳統太陽能工程與施工業務，向自有再生能源資產轉型，並透過長期售電協議創造穩定的經常性收入。

  今年9月，Apex Securities曾表示，Northern Solar正在敲定一項潛在的170MW太陽能加儲能CRESS專案細節，該專案有望加速前述業務轉型。Northern Solar目前尚未澄清此次兩項最新授標是否與Apex Securities先前所提及的專案有所關聯。

  ## 在手訂單與市場地位

  Northern Solar於10月另行取得一份總金額3,400萬令吉的合約，負責開發一座9.5MW大型太陽能光伏電廠，使該公司今年累計合約金額達到1億5,340萬令吉。截至2026年6月底，公司未入帳在手訂單金額為1億8,930萬令吉。

  該公司已於10月6日完成從馬來西亞交易所創業市場（ACE Market）轉板至主板（Main Market）的掛牌手續。週一收盤時，其股價下跌4仙（sen），跌幅3.54%，收報1.09令吉，對應市值約為4億3,120萬令吉。

  兩項CRESS授標的正式協議目前仍在洽談中，開工日期將於協議簽署後另行確認。
