body_en: |
  Delta Electronics, Lite-On Technology, Vertiv, and Schneider Electric each displayed 800-volt direct-current (800VDC) power cabinet solutions at COMPUTEX 2026, which just concluded, as AI data center power infrastructure transitions from traditional alternating-current (AC) systems to high-voltage direct-current (HVDC), the Commercial Times reported.

  ## Vendor exhibits

  Delta Electronics (TWSE: 2308) and Lite-On Technology (TWSE: 2301) both exhibited 800VDC power cabinets conforming to NVIDIA's 660-kilowatt (kW) reference design, integrated with 110kW AC-to-DC power racks and battery backup unit (BBU) systems, the report said. Vertiv exhibited a 900kW-class 800VDC power cabinet featuring a modular, scalable power distribution unit (PDU) design.

  ## Efficiency advantage of HVDC

  Compared with conventional AC supply architectures, HVDC reduces power conversion losses and raises efficiency. Some solutions shown at the event reached power conversion efficiency of up to 98.2%, the report said, a level suited to supporting the high-power demands of next-generation AI computing racks.

  ## BBU and supercapacitor flexibility

  Under the HVDC architecture, BBU modules and supercapacitor modules now share identical physical dimensions and form factor, giving data center operators the flexibility to select between them based on grid stability conditions and backup power requirements, analysts cited by the report said. BBU remains the dominant solution in current deployments, the analysts added.

  ## NVIDIA's Vera Rubin as the near-term catalyst

  NVIDIA is accelerating HVDC adoption through its next-generation Vera Rubin computing platform. The market expects small-volume HVDC shipments to begin in the second half of 2026, with a gradual generational shift in data center power systems to follow, the report said. Some vendors are also developing ±400VDC transitional solutions alongside the primary 800VDC standard; those products are expected to reach actual shipment earlier.

  ## Solid-state transformers emerge

  Solid-state transformers (SST), a next-generation power conversion technology, also drew attention at the show. Conventional power architectures require medium-voltage AC electricity to be stepped down before conversion to DC; an SST bypasses that intermediate stage, converting 22.8-kilovolt (kV) or 33kV medium-voltage AC directly to 800V or 850V DC in a single step to supply AI computing systems.

  Delta Electronics has developed an SST solution with mass-production capability. Deployment timing will depend on hyperscale data center customers' construction schedules, analysts cited by the report said.

  ## Delta's fuel cell addition

  Delta Electronics also exhibited a solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) solution designed to integrate with 800VDC architecture as a green energy and alternative power source for next-generation data centers. The product has reached its final preparation stage and is expected to begin shipping by the end of 2026, the report said.

  ## Industry significance

  The simultaneous display of HVDC-ready products from multiple major vendors at a single industry event signals that the transition from AC to HVDC in AI data center power infrastructure has entered a commercial phase, driven by efficiency requirements and accelerated by NVIDIA's platform roadmap. With ±400VDC transitional products expected to ship first, 800VDC systems following in the second half of 2026, and Delta's SOFC due before year-end, the SST represents the farthest point on the current product roadmap. Small-volume SST shipments are possible as early as 2027, contingent on hyperscale customer deployment schedules, analysts cited by the report said.
body_zh: |
  台達電子、光寶科技、Vertiv與施耐德電氣在甫落幕的COMPUTEX 2026上，分別展出800伏特直流（800VDC）電源機櫃解決方案。據《工商時報》報導，此一趨勢顯示AI資料中心電力基礎建設正加速從傳統交流（AC）系統轉向高壓直流（HVDC）架構。

  ## 各廠商展出概況

  台達電子（上市代號：2308）與光寶科技（上市代號：2301）均展出符合輝達660千瓦（kW）參考設計規格的800VDC電源機櫃，並整合110kW交直流轉換電源機架與電池備援單元（BBU）系統。Vertiv則展出一款900kW級800VDC電源機櫃，採模組化、可擴充式配電單元（PDU）設計。

  ## HVDC的效率優勢

  相較於傳統交流供電架構，HVDC能降低電力轉換損耗、提升整體效率。展會上部分解決方案的電力轉換效率最高可達98.2%，足以支應下世代AI運算機架的高功耗需求。

  ## BBU與超級電容的彈性選配

  在HVDC架構下，BBU模組與超級電容模組現已統一為相同的實體尺寸與外形規格，讓資料中心營運商得以依據電網穩定條件及備援電力需求，靈活選擇適合的方案。報導引述分析師指出，BBU目前仍是主流部署選擇。

  ## 輝達Vera Rubin平台成近期推力

  輝達正透過下世代Vera Rubin運算平台加快HVDC的普及步伐。市場預期HVDC小批量出貨將於2026年下半年啟動，資料中心電力系統此後將逐步完成世代交替。部分廠商亦同步開發±400VDC過渡性解決方案，以搭配800VDC主流規格並行推進；此類產品預計將比800VDC系統更早進入實際出貨階段。

  ## 固態變壓器嶄露頭角

  作為下世代電力轉換技術的固態變壓器（SST），在此次展會上同樣備受關注。傳統電力架構需先將中壓交流電降壓，再轉換為直流電；SST則省去中間降壓環節，可直接將22.8千伏（kV）或33kV的中壓交流電一步轉換為800V或850V直流電，直接供應AI運算系統使用。

  台達電子已開發出具量產能力的SST解決方案。報導引述分析師表示，實際部署時程將視超大規模資料中心客戶的建設進度而定。

  ## 台達電加入固態氧化物燃料電池

  台達電子此次亦展出固態氧化物燃料電池（SOFC）解決方案，設計上可與800VDC架構整合，作為下世代資料中心的綠能及備用電源。報導指出，該產品已進入最終備貨階段，預計於2026年底前正式出貨。

  ## 產業意義

  多家主要廠商在單一產業盛會上同步展示HVDC就緒產品，標誌著AI資料中心電力基礎建設從交流轉向直流的進程已正式進入商業化階段，背後動力來自效率需求的提升，以及輝達平台路線圖的加速牽引。就時程而言，±400VDC過渡性產品預計率先出貨，800VDC系統緊接於2026年下半年跟進，台達電的SOFC則預計在年底前交貨，而SST則是目前產品路線圖上最遠的一個里程碑。報導引述分析師指出，SST小批量出貨最快可能在2027年實現，惟仍須視超大規模客戶的部署時程而定。
