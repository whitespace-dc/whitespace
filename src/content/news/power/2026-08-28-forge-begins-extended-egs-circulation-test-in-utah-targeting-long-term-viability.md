body_en: |
  The Frontier Observatory for Research in Geothermal Energy (FORGE) began a 90-to-120-day extended circulation test of an enhanced geothermal system (EGS) at its Utah site earlier this month in what the U.S. Department of Energy described as "a significant feat" reached by only a handful of EGS projects worldwide.

  FORGE, managed by the University of Utah and funded by the DOE, announced the test in an Aug. 12 release. The extended circulation will allow the research team to "evaluate reservoir performance under continuous flow" over the full test period, with the base run set at 90 days and a possible extension to 120 days.

  ## What the test is designed to answer

  Kristie McLin, FORGE's principal investigator, said the test targets "heretofore unknown variables required to ensure long term EGS production remains commercially viable." She identified two central unknowns: thermal breakthrough and water loss over time.

  "Although major advancements have been achieved in the past few years, there is still a great deal we do not know about thermal breakthrough and water loss over time," McLin said.

  The DOE said on its website that the August test "is the culmination of years of federally funded work and can hold the key to some of the most important steps to advance EGS."

  ## Where EGS commercialization stands

  EGS — a method of generating electricity from heat extracted from underground rock formations — is still in the early stages of commercialization. EGS startup Fervo Energy's Cape Station, a 500-megawatt facility under construction in Beaver County, Utah, is the first commercial-scale EGS project in the U.S. and is anticipated to begin delivering power to the grid later this year.

  ## Fervo's financial picture

  Fervo went public in a May 2026 initial public offering, but its stock has fallen 56% from its initial peak. During the company's second-quarter earnings call, Fervo disclosed new transmission-related curtailment issues affecting 2027 projections. Those issues prompted the company to lower its fiscal year 2027 revenue estimate to $63 million from a prior projection of $79 million, according to an Aug. 14 research note by Jefferies equity analyst Julien Dumoulin-Smith.

  Fervo CFO David Ulrey, speaking on the earnings call, said the curtailment problems were not specific to the company. "This is not new to power development, and it is not unique to Fervo," Ulrey said. He said Fervo's ability to drill "hotter, faster, deeper, and cheaper" represents "an enormous mitigant to a host of factors on our risk register."

  ## Why the FORGE results matter to the broader industry

  McLin said that while FORGE's results in recent years "have been very beneficial to the industry," major gaps in knowledge persist. "This extended circulation will provide answers to the pressing questions about thermal decline and water loss, allowing geothermal companies to continue growing and to flourish," she said.

  The DOE's characterization of the test as both a culmination and a potential inflection point underscores the stakes: EGS developers and their investors require data on sustained reservoir performance before they can confidently scale the technology. The FORGE test is the most direct attempt to generate that data in a federally funded U.S. program.

  Fervo's Cape Station, the sector's first commercial-scale EGS project, is anticipated to begin delivering power to the grid before the end of 2026.
body_zh: |
  美國地熱能源研究前沿觀測站（FORGE）本月稍早在猶他州場址啟動一項為期90至120天的增強型地熱系統（EGS）延長循環測試。美國能源部將此描述為「重大成就」，並指出全球僅有少數EGS計畫曾達到這一階段。

  FORGE由猶他大學負責管理、美國能源部提供資金，並於8月12日發布公告宣布展開此次測試。延長循環測試將使研究團隊得以在整個測試期間內「評估儲層在連續流動條件下的表現」，基礎測試期設定為90天，視情況可延長至120天。

  ## 測試欲解答的核心問題

  FORGE首席研究員克莉絲蒂・麥克林（Kristie McLin）表示，此次測試針對的是「確保EGS長期生產維持商業可行性所必須釐清、迄今仍屬未知的變數」。她點出兩大核心未知數：熱穿透現象（thermal breakthrough）以及長期水損失問題。

  「儘管過去幾年已取得重大進展，但我們對熱穿透及長時間水損失的了解仍有許多不足，」麥克林說道。

  美國能源部在官網上指出，這次8月測試「是多年聯邦資助工作的集大成，有望成為推進EGS最關鍵步驟的突破口」。

  ## EGS商業化現況

  EGS是一種從地下岩層中提取熱能進而發電的技術，目前仍處於商業化的早期階段。EGS新創企業Fervo能源（Fervo Energy）在猶他州比弗郡（Beaver County）興建中的「開普站」（Cape Station）為一座500百萬瓦（MW）的設施，是美國第一個商業規模的EGS計畫，預計於今年稍晚開始向電網供電。

  ## Fervo的財務狀況

  Fervo於2026年5月完成首次公開募股（IPO），然而其股價自上市初期高峰以來已重挫56%。在公司第二季財報電話會議上，Fervo揭露了影響2027年業績展望的新增輸電限電問題。根據傑富瑞（Jefferies）股票分析師朱利安・杜穆蘭-史密斯（Julien Dumoulin-Smith）於8月14日發布的研究報告，上述問題促使Fervo將2027財年營收預估從先前的7,900萬美元下調至6,300萬美元。

  Fervo財務長（CFO）大衛・烏爾雷（David Ulrey）在財報電話會議上表示，這些限電問題並非Fervo所獨有。「這對電力開發來說並不陌生，也不是Fervo獨有的問題，」烏爾雷說。他表示，Fervo具備「更熱、更快、更深、更便宜」的鑽探能力，足以「大幅抵消風險清單上諸多因素帶來的衝擊」。

  ## FORGE成果對整體產業的意義

  麥克林表示，儘管FORGE近年來的成果「對整體產業大有裨益」，但知識層面的重大缺口依然存在。「這次延長循環測試將解答有關熱衰退與水損失的迫切問題，讓地熱企業得以持續成長、蓬勃發展，」她說。

  美國能源部將此次測試定位為多年努力的集大成之作，同時也點出其作為潛在產業轉折點的重要性：EGS開發業者及其投資人在有信心擴大技術規模之前，需要儲層持續運作表現的相關數據作為依據。FORGE測試是美國聯邦資助計畫中，目前最直接嘗試產出這類數據的行動。

  作為整個產業第一個商業規模EGS計畫，Fervo的開普站預計在2026年底前開始向電網供電。
