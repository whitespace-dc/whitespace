body_en: |
  Jati Tinggi Group Bhd (KL:JTGROUP), an electrical engineering group, has secured a RM30.1 million contract to construct power infrastructure for a data centre in Kulai, Johor, the company disclosed in a filing with Bursa Malaysia on Monday.

  The contract was awarded to Jati Tinggi Holding Sdn Bhd, the group's wholly-owned subsidiary, via a letter of acceptance (LOA) from an unnamed Malaysian-incorporated company principally involved in providing data hosting infrastructure, data processing services, and related activities.

  ## Contract scope

  The work covers the design, engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning of two circuits of 275kV (kilovolt) underground cable ducting works and 11kV temporary power cable works in Kulai.

  The RM30.1 million contract sum is inclusive of taxes, insurance coverage, and the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) levy. The contract runs from the date of acceptance to July 25, 2027.

  ## Financial contribution

  Jati Tinggi said the contract is expected to contribute positively to the group's earnings, earnings per share, and net assets per share over the contract period. The company did not provide a specific earnings figure.

  ## Recent contract activity

  The Kulai award follows two contracts secured earlier this month from Tenaga Nasional Bhd (KL:TENAGA), worth a combined RM15.7 million. Those contracts cover underground cable works in Selangor and a submarine power cable monitoring system in Perlis.

  Jati Tinggi shares closed one sen, or 0.84%, higher at RM1.20 on Monday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM531.51 million.

  The Kulai contract runs to July 25, 2027.

  ## Jati Tinggi奪得柔佛資料中心3,010萬令吉高壓電力基礎建設合約

  電氣工程集團Jati Tinggi Group Bhd（吉隆坡交易所代號：JTGROUP）週一向馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）提交的文件中披露，公司已取得一份總值3,010萬令吉的合約，負責在柔佛古來為一座資料中心興建電力基礎建設。

  此合約由集團全資子公司Jati Tinggi Holding Sdn Bhd承接，透過一封接受函（LOA）獲得授予，委託方為一家未具名的馬來西亞本地註冊公司，主要業務涵蓋資料託管基礎建設、資料處理服務及相關活動。

  ## 合約範疇

  本工程涵蓋古來地區兩迴路275千伏（kV）地下電纜管道工程及11千伏臨時電力電纜工程的設計、工程規劃、採購、施工與試運轉等全流程作業。

  3,010萬令吉的合約總額已含稅款、保險費用及建築業發展局（CIDB）徵費。合約自接受函生效日起算，施工期限至2027年7月25日。

  ## 財務貢獻

  Jati Tinggi表示，此合約預計將在合約期間內為集團的盈利、每股盈利及每股淨資產帶來正面貢獻。公司並未提供具體的盈利數字。

  ## 近期合約動態

  古來合約是繼本月稍早從國家能源公司（Tenaga Nasional Bhd，吉隆坡交易所代號：TENAGA）取得兩份合約之後的最新斬獲，前述兩份合約合計金額為1,570萬令吉，分別涉及雪蘭莪州的地下電纜工程，以及玻璃市州的海底電力電纜監控系統。

  Jati Tinggi股票週一收盤上漲1仙，漲幅0.84%，報每股1.20令吉，集團市值為5億3,151萬令吉。

  古來資料中心電力工程合約施工期限至2027年7月25日。
