body_en: |
  Heartland Oil and Calgary-based Orion Power Solutions are proposing a 7-megawatt (MW) Bitcoin mining facility at a natural gas plant in Beaver River, Saskatchewan, according to a notice published by the local municipality. The plant has operated since 1990, and the proposal would add mining equipment within its existing footprint.

  The operation would run on gas drawn from Heartland's own wells rather than from SaskEnergy, the provincial natural gas utility, and would not connect to SaskPower, the provincial electricity grid. Seven megawatts is roughly enough power to supply several thousand average Canadian homes, and in Bitcoin mining terms represents a mid-scale operation.

  ## Project details and approvals

  The companies said the mining computers would require no water supply and that no farmland would need to be converted for the project. Noise testing is planned to assess and address potential community impacts. An open house was advertised for October 15 at the Senior Citizens' Hall in the nearby town of Pierceland.

  The municipal notice does not include a construction timetable, capital cost, or hardware specifications, and does not confirm whether regulatory approvals have been secured.

  ## Companies involved

  Heartland describes itself as a privately owned, natural gas-focused energy company operating in Saskatchewan. Its website lists 76 producing wells, three sales compressor stations, and 450 kilometres of pipelines, with a 100 percent working interest in its wells and infrastructure.

  Orion Power Solutions, based in Calgary, Alberta, provides electricity consulting and services covering power supply, generating equipment, electrical interconnection, and data center setup, operations, and maintenance.

  The companies said the Bitcoin mining project would diversify the gas plant's revenue streams and support its long-term viability, while generating construction and maintenance employment.

  ## Saskatchewan data center context

  The Heartland proposal is one of several data infrastructure developments in Saskatchewan. In September, Bell Canada and the Government of Saskatchewan announced a non-binding Memorandum of Understanding for a phased expansion of up to 900 MW of additional capacity at a planned Bell data center in the province. That project has been described as a potential capital investment of more than $50 billion, which the parties said would represent the largest capital investment in Saskatchewan's history.

  The Heartland mine, if built, would represent a comparatively small-scale use of stranded or captive natural gas for computing workloads — a model in which oil and gas producers consume their own fuel on-site rather than flaring it or selling it into the grid.

  心地石油（Heartland Oil）與卡加利的奧萊恩電力解決方案（Orion Power Solutions）計劃在薩斯喀徹溫省比弗河的一座天然氣廠設立7百萬瓦（MW）比特幣挖礦設施，消息來自當地市政府發布的公告。該廠自1990年起持續運作，新計劃將在現有廠區範圍內增設採礦設備。

  此礦場將以心地石油自家井口抽取的天然氣供電，不仰賴省級天然氣公用事業SaskEnergy，也不接入省級電力公司SaskPower的電網。7百萬瓦的電力規模大約可供數千戶加拿大普通家庭使用，就比特幣挖礦而言屬於中等規模的作業。

  ## 項目細節與審批

  兩家公司表示，挖礦電腦無需用水，也不必將農地轉作他用。現場雜訊測試已納入規劃，以評估並因應可能對周邊社區造成的影響。公告顯示，兩公司已於10月15日在鄰近小鎮皮爾斯蘭（Pierceland）的長者活動中心舉辦公眾說明會。

  市政府公告並未載明施工時程、資本支出或硬體規格，亦未確認是否已取得相關主管機關的核准。

  ## 相關公司

  心地石油自述為一家私人持有、以天然氣為核心業務的能源公司，營運範圍涵蓋薩斯喀徹溫省。其官網資料顯示，公司旗下擁有76口生產井、三座銷售用壓縮機站及450公里管線，並對旗下所有井口與基礎設施持有100%的作業權益。

  總部設於亞伯達省卡加利的奧萊恩電力解決方案，提供電力諮詢與相關服務，業務涵蓋電力供應、發電設備、電力互聯，以及資料中心的建置、營運與維護。

  兩家公司表示，比特幣挖礦計劃將為天然氣廠拓展收入來源、支撐其長期營運，同時也能在施工與維護階段創造就業機會。

  ## 薩斯喀徹溫省資料中心發展背景

  心地石油的提案是薩斯喀徹溫省多項資料基礎建設開發案之一。今年9月，貝爾加拿大（Bell Canada）與薩省政府宣布簽署一份不具約束力的合作備忘錄，計劃分階段在省內一座貝爾資料中心新增最多900MW的額外容量。這項計劃的潛在資本投入據稱超過500億加元，雙方將其定位為薩斯喀徹溫省有史以來規模最大的資本投資。

  心地石油的礦場若順利落成，將是以「困縛型」或「捕獲性」天然氣驅動運算負載的相對小規模應用案例——在此模式下，油氣生產商直接在現場消耗自產燃料，而非就地燃燒排放或輸入電網銷售。
