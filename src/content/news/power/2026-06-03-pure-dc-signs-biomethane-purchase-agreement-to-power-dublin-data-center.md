  ## Pure DC Secures Biomethane Supply for Dublin Campus

  Pure DC has signed a biomethane purchase agreement to supply its Dublin, Ireland data center with renewable gas sourced from Germany, adding a cross-border green fuel strategy to its sustainability toolkit.

  ## What the Agreement Covers

  Biomethane — pipeline-quality renewable gas produced by upgrading raw biogas from organic waste streams — will be procured from German production facilities and delivered via the interconnected European gas grid. Unlike direct power purchase agreements (PPAs) tied to a specific generator location, biomethane agreements work through gas-grid book-and-claim mechanisms: volumes produced in Germany are certificated and matched to consumption in Ireland, allowing operators to claim the renewable attribute even across national borders.

  The move addresses a specific gap in data center decarbonization: backup generation and on-site thermal loads that cannot easily be electrified with renewable electricity alone. Gas-fired standby generators and combined heat and power (CHP) systems remain common in large campuses; substituting conventional natural gas with certified biomethane reduces the Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions associated with those assets without requiring hardware replacement.

  ## Why Germany, Why Now

  Germany has developed one of Europe's largest biomethane production industries, driven by agricultural biogas infrastructure built over the past two decades. Surplus certified biomethane is increasingly exported through bilateral agreements to markets such as Ireland, where domestic production capacity is limited but demand — particularly from data center operators facing scrutiny over their carbon footprints — is rising.

  Dublin remains one of Europe's most concentrated data center markets, hosting a significant share of hyperscale and co-location capacity serving the continent. Irish grid constraints and renewable electricity intermittency have pushed operators to explore diversified clean energy strategies, and biomethane agreements represent one credible pathway to reducing fossil gas dependency in the near term.

  ## Planning Implications

  - **Scope 1 reduction pathway**: Operators running gas generators or CHP on campus can neutralise associated emissions through certified biomethane without capital-intensive generator replacement.
  - **Grid-independent clean energy**: Biomethane purchases complement renewable electricity PPAs, covering loads that sit outside the electricity meter.
  - **Cross-border certificate recognition**: EU energy market rules support cross-border biomethane certificate trading, though procurement teams should confirm certificate registry compliance (e.g., European Biogas Association standards) to satisfy auditors and sustainability reporting frameworks.
  - **Supply chain risk**: German biomethane production is competitive today, but long-term availability and pricing depend on agricultural feedstock policy and competing demand from industrial and transport sectors — factors worth stress-testing in multi-year supply agreements.

  Pure DC's move signals that cross-border biomethane procurement is graduating from pilot to mainstream practice for European data center operators seeking credible near-term fossil gas substitution.

  ## Pure DC為都柏林園區確保生質甲烷供應

  Pure DC已簽署一份生質甲烷採購協議，以德國生產的再生天然氣為其愛爾蘭都柏林資料中心供電，在其永續能源策略中加入跨境綠色燃料方案。

  ## 協議內容

  生質甲烷是將有機廢棄物產生的沼氣提純至管線品質的再生天然氣。本次採購來源為德國生產設施，透過相互連通的歐洲天然氣管網輸送。與綁定特定發電廠地點的直接再生能源購電協議（PPA）不同，生質甲烷協議採用天然氣管網「記帳與主張」機制：德國生產的氣體經過認證後，與愛爾蘭的消費量相匹配，使業者即便跨越國境亦可主張再生能源屬性。

  此舉填補了資料中心去碳化的一項特定缺口：備用發電機及場內熱能負載難以單純依賴再生電力完全電氣化。大型園區仍普遍使用天然氣備用發電機及熱電聯產（CHP）系統；以認證生質甲烷取代傳統天然氣，無需更換硬體設備即可降低這些資產的範疇一及範疇二碳排放。

  ## 為何選擇德國、為何是現在

  德國憑藉過去二十年建立的農業沼氣基礎設施，已發展出歐洲規模最大的生質甲烷生產產業之一。多餘的認證生質甲烷透過雙邊協議愈來愈多地出口至愛爾蘭等市場。愛爾蘭本地生產能力有限，但需求——尤其來自面臨碳足跡審查的資料中心業者——正在上升。

  都柏林仍是歐洲資料中心最密集的市場之一，承載著服務整個歐洲大陸的大量超大規模及共置容量。愛爾蘭電網限制與再生電力間歇性問題促使業者探索多元清潔能源策略，而生質甲烷採購協議在近期代表著減少化石天然氣依賴的可行途徑之一。

  ## 規劃意涵

  - **範疇一減排途徑**：在園區使用天然氣發電機或熱電聯產的業者，可透過認證生質甲烷抵銷相關碳排，無需進行大規模發電機汰換。
  - **獨立於電網的清潔能源**：生質甲烷採購可補充再生電力購電協議，涵蓋電表以外的用電負載。
  - **跨境憑證認可**：歐盟能源市場規則支持跨境生質甲烷憑證交易，但採購團隊應確認憑證登記處合規性（如歐洲沼氣協會標準），以滿足稽核人員及永續報告框架的要求。
  - **供應鏈風險**：德國生質甲烷目前市場競爭力強，但長期供應量與價格取決於農業原料政策及工業、交通等競爭需求——這些因素在簽訂多年期供應協議時值得進行壓力測試。

  Pure DC的此次行動顯示，跨境生質甲烷採購正從試點階段走向主流，成為歐洲資料中心業者在近期尋求可信化石天然氣替代方案的重要選項。
