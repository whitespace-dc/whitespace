body_en: |
  Taiwan Power Company (Taipower) plans to invest NT$30 billion over the next 15 years to build or expand 43 substations, lay 1,922 kilometers of new transmission lines, and add 52,450 megavolt-amperes (MVA) — a measure of grid transformer capacity — as AI-driven semiconductor demand pushes electricity requirements beyond the capacity of the existing network.

  Taipower Chairman Tseng Wen-sheng announced the plan at Taiwan Energy Week, describing the scale as "absolutely unprecedented." The 43 substations comprise 11 extra-high-voltage substations, 20 primary substations, nine 345-kilovolt (kV) switching stations or switchyards, and three 161-kV switching stations.

  ## Semiconductor power demand outlook

  Based on industry development trends, industry statistics, and semiconductor facility power applications, Taipower projects that domestic semiconductor electricity consumption will rise from roughly 52 billion kilowatt-hours (kWh) in 2026 to approximately 80 billion kWh in 2030 and 110 billion kWh by 2035. At that level, the semiconductor industry alone would account for 30 percent of Taiwan's total national power consumption, the utility said.

  The AI wave has driven accelerating expansion in wafer fabrication, advanced packaging, and high-bandwidth memory production, all of which carry high power-density requirements, Taipower said.

  ## Grid strategy

  Taipower describes its approach as building an "integrated power grid" — a term chosen to echo "integrated circuit," the product at the heart of the industries it must serve. The strategy runs across supply, network, and user tracks: diversifying generation sources; routing power plant output directly to industrial parks, adding extra-high-voltage substations, and expanding renewable energy interconnection capacity; and meeting end-user needs.

  Tseng said the existing model — one substation per industrial park — is no longer adequate. He cited Southern Science Park (南科) as the clearest example: by 2031, the park's load will exceed 4 gigawatts (GW, or 4 billion watts), equal to 9 percent of national electricity consumption. The park currently operates two dedicated substations, Nanke and Nanke 2; a third, Nanke 3, must be built. Tseng said he believed no other science park in the world requires three substations bearing the same name.

  ## Construction pipeline

  Among the extra-high-voltage substations targeted at specific industrial parks, five are currently under construction: Guanyuan (觀園), Hengshan (橫山), Gaolian (高煉), Jiake (嘉科), and Shake (沙科). Three more — Longke (龍科), Tongke (銅科), and Nante (南特) — remain in the planning stage, with no construction start dates announced.

  Whether substation construction can keep pace with semiconductor cluster expansion will be the critical determinant of how quickly advanced fabrication and packaging capacity can come online, Taipower said. The three planning-stage substations at Longke, Tongke, and Nante represent the furthest-out commitments in the program.

  ## 台電斥資300億元擴建電網，全力供應AI半導體聚落用電

  台灣電力公司（台電）計畫在未來15年內投入300億元新台幣，新建或擴建43座變電站、鋪設1,922公里新輸電線路，並新增52,450百萬伏安（MVA）的電網變壓器容量。這項龐大計畫的背後推力，是人工智慧（AI）浪潮帶動的半導體用電需求，已超出現有電網所能負荷的範疇。

  台電董事長曾文生在「台灣能源週」活動中宣布這項計畫，直言其規模「絕對前所未有」。43座變電站包括11座超高壓變電站、20座一次變電站、9座345千伏（kV）開關站或開閉所，以及3座161千伏開關站。

  ## 半導體用電需求展望

  根據產業發展趨勢、產業統計數據及半導體廠的用電申請，台電預估國內半導體用電量將從2026年約520億度（kWh），攀升至2030年約800億度，並於2035年達到約1,100億度。台電表示，屆時半導體產業單一行業的用電量，將佔台灣全國總用電量的三成。

  AI浪潮持續推動晶圓製造、先進封裝及高頻寬記憶體（HBM）生產的加速擴張，而上述製程均具備高功率密度的用電特性，台電指出。

  ## 電網布建策略

  台電將其整體方針定名為「整合電網」，此一命名刻意呼應半導體產業核心產品「積體電路」（integrated circuit）的概念，以彰顯服務產業的使命。這套策略涵蓋供給、電網與用戶三大面向：分散發電來源；將電廠輸出直接導引至工業園區、新增超高壓變電站，並擴充再生能源併網容量；同時滿足終端用戶的用電需求。

  曾文生表示，過去「一座科學園區配一座變電站」的模式已不再適用。他以南部科學工業園區（南科）為最鮮明的例子：預計至2031年，南科的用電負載將超過400萬瓩（4 GW），相當於全國用電量的9%。園區目前已有南科及南科二號兩座專屬變電站，南科三號變電站的興建已是勢在必行。曾文生說，他相信全球恐怕沒有任何一座科學園區，需要三座冠上相同名稱的變電站。

  ## 工程建設進度

  在針對特定工業園區所規劃的超高壓變電站中，目前已有五座進入施工階段，分別為觀園、橫山、高煉、嘉科及沙科變電站。另外三座——龍科、銅科及南特變電站——目前仍處於規劃階段，尚未宣布動工日期。

  台電指出，變電站的興建速度能否跟上半導體聚落的擴張腳步，將是先進製造及封裝產能能否如期到位的關鍵決定因素。龍科、銅科及南特三座規劃中的變電站，代表整個計畫中期程最為長遠的承諾。
