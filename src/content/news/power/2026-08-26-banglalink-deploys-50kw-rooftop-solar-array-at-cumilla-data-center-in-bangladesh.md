body_en: |
  Veon-owned Bangladeshi mobile operator Banglalink has deployed a 50-kilowatt (kW) rooftop solar array at its data center in Cumilla, eastern Bangladesh, in what the company describes as the first on-site solar installation by a telecommunications operator at a data center in the country.

  The array occupies 2,500 square feet (232 square meters) of previously unused rooftop space. Banglalink projects the system will generate approximately 85,000 kilowatt-hours (kWh) of electricity per year — enough, the company said, to reduce the Cumilla facility's grid electricity consumption by around six percent. The company also said the installation is expected to cut its carbon emissions by 52 metric tons annually.

  "By integrating solar power into our critical operations, we are reducing emissions, optimizing energy costs, and strengthening the long-term sustainability of our network," said Hüseyin Türker, Banglalink's chief technology and information officer. "At Banglalink, sustainability is not just an environmental commitment; it is a business imperative."

  ## Cumilla follows earlier Gazipur installation

  The Cumilla array is Banglalink's second on-site solar deployment at a data center. The company completed an 80kW solar array at its data center in Gazipur — located near the capital, Dhaka — last year. The Gazipur system is larger, though Banglalink did not provide equivalent output or emissions figures for that site.

  ## Company background

  Banglalink is Bangladesh's third-largest mobile network operator and is wholly owned by Veon, a multinational telecommunications company headquartered in Dubai. In May 2024, Banglalink confirmed the shutdown of its 3G network and said it would redirect resources toward expanding its 4G mobile network. That shutdown followed the completed sale of 2,000 telecom towers in Bangladesh to Summit Towers as part of a $100 million deal.

  ## On-site solar gaining ground among telcos and data center operators

  On-site solar is an approach in which a facility generates electricity directly on its premises — typically on rooftops or adjacent land — rather than sourcing all power from the national grid. For data centers, which run continuously and require stable power, rooftop arrays reduce grid dependency and can lower operating costs alongside emissions.

  Banglalink is not alone in pursuing this model. In 2024, Oman Data Park tapped Omani solar developer Solar Wadi to develop a 1.4-megawatt (MW) on-site solar project to directly power its KOM 4 data center in Rusayl, Oman — a system roughly 28 times the capacity of Banglalink's Cumilla installation.

  Banglalink's Gazipur installation, completed last year, remains the company's larger on-site solar deployment to date.

  Veon旗下孟加拉電信業者Banglalink，在孟加拉東部Cumilla的資料中心建置了一座50千瓦（kW）屋頂太陽能陣列。這是孟加拉境內首例由電信業者在資料中心設施導入現地太陽能裝置的案例。

  「透過將太陽能整合至我們的關鍵營運環節，我們得以降低碳排放、優化能源成本，並強化網路基礎設施的長期永續性，」Banglalink技術暨資訊長Hüseyin Türker表示。「對Banglalink而言，永續發展不僅是環保承諾，更是企業營運的必然之路。」

  ## Cumilla繼Gazipur之後的再一次建置

  Cumilla太陽能陣列是Banglalink在資料中心導入現地太陽能的第二個案例。去年，該公司已在鄰近首都達卡的Gazipur資料中心完成一座80千瓦的太陽能陣列。Gazipur系統的裝置容量較大，惟Banglalink並未對外提供該站點相應的發電量或碳排放削減數據。

  ## 公司背景

  Banglalink是孟加拉第三大行動網路業者，由總部位於杜拜的跨國電信公司Veon全資持有。2024年5月，Banglalink正式確認關閉3G網路，並表示將把資源重新導向4G行動網路的擴張布局。此次3G關網，係在以1億美元規模將孟加拉境內2,000座電信鐵塔出售給Summit Towers之後接續推進的。

  ## 現地太陽能在電信與資料中心業者間日益普及

  現地太陽能是一種直接在設施本身（通常是屋頂或毗鄰土地）發電的模式，而非完全仰賴國家電網供電。對於全天候不間斷運作、且對電力穩定性要求極高的資料中心而言，屋頂太陽能陣列既可降低對電網的依賴，亦有助於同步壓低營運成本與碳排放。

  Banglalink並非唯一採行此模式的業者。2024年，阿曼資料中心業者Oman Data Park委託當地太陽能開發商Solar Wadi，為其位於阿曼Rusayl的KOM 4資料中心開發一套1.4百萬瓦（MW）現地太陽能專案，直接為該設施提供電力——其容量約為Banglalink Cumilla裝置的28倍。

  去年竣工的Gazipur裝置，迄今仍是Banglalink規模最大的現地太陽能部署案例。
