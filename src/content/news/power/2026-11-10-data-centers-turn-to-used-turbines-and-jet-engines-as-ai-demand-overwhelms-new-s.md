body_en: |
  Utilities, data center developers, and AI infrastructure firms are scouring secondary markets for used and refurbished gas turbines after backlogs for new equipment stretched delivery windows into the next decade, according to more than a dozen brokers, service firms, and suppliers interviewed by Reuters.

  Worldwide gas turbine orders now top 100 gigawatts (GW) — a measure of generating capacity — well above the estimated global production capacity of 60–70 GW, according to energy consultancy Wood Mackenzie. In the United States, the average delivery time for a new gas turbine has reached six years, Aurora Tenorio, a senior analyst at Wood Mackenzie, told Reuters. "Delivery conversations [are] firmly focused on the 2030 to 2031 window," Tenorio said of the US market.

  The gap is not closing quickly. While global manufacturing capacity is forecast to rise to around 87 GW by 2028, analysts at investment bank Jefferies project that expected turbine power demand will reach 129 GW by that year — leaving a shortfall of roughly 42 GW. US data centers alone will account for nearly 17 GW of turbine demand this year, Jefferies estimates, a figure that could more than triple by 2028.

  The three companies that dominate gas turbine manufacturing — Siemens Energy, GE Vernova, and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries — have all said the AI boom has driven orders beyond their production capacity.

  ## The secondary market surge

  The backlog has pushed buyers toward second-hand, refurbished, and aeroderivative turbines — machines derived from aircraft jet engines and adapted for stationary power generation. "That secondary market is very, very hot right now," Ana Amicarella, chief executive of equipment services provider EthosEnergy, told Reuters. "We also have seen an escalation on the prices of what people are demanding."

  The global gas turbine supply market is worth about $25 billion a year, though no reliable estimates exist for the secondary market's share of that total, Reuters reported.

  AI infrastructure firm Crusoe placed a concrete example on the record: in April the company ordered 13 aeroderivative gas turbines from US-based ProEnergy — units based on jet engines manufactured by GE Aerospace — describing the move as a strategy to scale quickly. The 13 turbines carry a combined capacity of 650 megawatts (MW), enough to power several hundred thousand homes, and will be deployed at Crusoe's US data center projects. ProEnergy and Crusoe did not respond to Reuters' requests for additional detail.

  Darayus Pardivala, who leads the US aftermarket business of industrial services firm Sulzer, said inquiries arrive on a near-weekly basis. "Every few weeks we get a request to say, 'Can you find a used gas turbine to put into a data centre?'" he told Reuters, declining to name clients or locations.

  ## Pricing and trade-offs

  Cost is a significant driver. Well-maintained used turbines sell for roughly $1.5 million to $1.7 million per MW, and refurbished units for about $1.3 million per MW, according to Zach Machuca, managing partner at Origin Partners, which acquires power equipment for data center developers. New turbines cost approximately $2 million to $2.5 million per MW, according to Aurora Energy Research.

  The discount can be steeper for smaller units. Dan Poole, general manager at equipment broker ReflowX, said two 14.5-MW turbines made by Solar Turbines — a unit of Caterpillar — recently sold for $12.5 million combined, or roughly $430,000 per MW. New equivalents would cost about $35 million and require a five-year wait, Poole said, without disclosing the buyer.

  Used and repurposed equipment carries its own risks. Overhauled turbines can have unclear maintenance histories, limited remaining operational life, and weaker long-term service support, Tenorio said. Aeroderivative units adapted from jet engines raise questions about their suitability for the continuous, round-the-clock duty cycle required of grid-connected power generation.

  Analyst Noor Qureshi of Enverus Intelligence Research said the secondary market is unlikely to resolve the broader supply squeeze. "The market is already constrained, with limited excess supply, so it is unlikely to offset a meaningful share of the delays," she told Reuters. Turbines are also just one obstacle, she noted; air permits, grid access, and other factors are causing independent delays.

  ## Small turbines and mixed fleets

  When larger turbines are unavailable, developers are turning to smaller units and deploying them in large numbers. Some operators assemble fleets of more than 100 small turbines at a single data center site, sometimes mixing models and manufacturers, according to Scott Schreeg, director of sales at US equipment supplier SVI Bremco.

  ## Spillover to utilities and alternatives

  The pressure is not confined to data center operators. The Tennessee Valley Authority, a federally owned US utility, said prices for new, used, and refurbished equipment have "increased significantly" over the past two years. Jim Matheson, chief executive of the National Rural Electric Cooperative Association — which represents non-profit, customer-owned utilities — said its members are facing unprecedented lead times and surging prices. "Co-ops that need new capacity are turning to every viable option," including refurbished turbines, he said.

  The bottleneck has also accelerated interest in alternatives to gas turbines. Finland's Wärtsilä, which manufactures reciprocating gas engines — a different technology that uses pistons rather than a rotating turbine — has booked approximately 3 GW of orders from US data centers since spring of last year, according to Anders Lindberg, president of the company's energy business. "What's driving the market? Lead times, lead times, lead times," Lindberg said.

  ## Significance

  The emergence of an active secondary market for gas turbines — a category that until recently sold almost exclusively to traditional utilities — reflects how data center demand has restructured procurement across the power equipment industry. The supply gap between projected demand of 129 GW and forecast manufacturing capacity of 87 GW in 2028 means pressure on secondary markets, prices, and lead times is set to persist through the end of the decade.
body_zh: |
  根據路透社採訪逾十餘家仲介商、服務公司及供應商的報導，公用事業者、資料中心開發商與人工智慧基礎設施業者，正在大舉搜尋二手及翻新燃氣渦輪機，同時重啟封存機組、改裝退役噴射引擎——新設備訂單積壓已將交貨期推遲至下一個十年。

  全球燃氣渦輪機訂單量如今已逾100吉瓦（以發電容量計算），遠高於能源諮詢公司Wood Mackenzie估計的60至70吉瓦全球年產能。在美國，新型燃氣渦輪機的平均交貨期已長達六年，Wood Mackenzie資深分析師Aurora Tenorio告訴路透社。「交貨討論〔已〕明確聚焦在2030至2031年這個時間窗口，」Tenorio談及美國市場時如此表示。

  供需缺口短期內難以彌合。儘管全球製造產能預計至2028年將提升至約87吉瓦，投資銀行傑富瑞（Jefferies）的分析師預估，屆時渦輪機電力需求預計將達129吉瓦——形成約42吉瓦的缺口。傑富瑞估計，光是美國資料中心今年的渦輪機需求就將近17吉瓦，這個數字到2028年可能增加逾兩倍。

  主導燃氣渦輪機製造市場的三大廠商——西門子能源（Siemens Energy）、奇異威諾華（GE Vernova）及三菱重工（Mitsubishi Heavy Industries）——均表示，人工智慧熱潮帶動的訂單量已超出其產能上限。

  ## 二手市場需求爆發

  訂單積壓促使買家轉向二手、翻新及航空衍生型渦輪機——即由飛機噴射引擎改裝、適用於固定式發電的機組。「那個次級市場現在非常非常熱，」設備服務供應商EthosEnergy執行長Ana Amicarella告訴路透社，「我們也看到人們開出的報價節節攀升。」

  全球燃氣渦輪機供應市場每年規模約為250億美元，惟次級市場所占份額目前尚無可靠估計，路透社報導指出。

  人工智慧基礎設施業者Crusoe提供了一個具體案例：今年四月，該公司向美國的ProEnergy訂購13台航空衍生型燃氣渦輪機，這批機組以奇異航太（GE Aerospace）製造的噴射引擎為基礎，Crusoe形容此舉是快速擴張的策略。13台渦輪機合計容量達650百萬瓦（MW），足以供應數十萬戶家庭的用電需求，將部署於Crusoe在美國的資料中心開發專案。ProEnergy與Crusoe均未回應路透社的進一步詢問。

  工業服務公司蘇爾壽（Sulzer）美國售後服務業務主管Darayus Pardivala表示，詢問幾乎每週都有。「每隔幾週就會收到請求說：『你能幫我找一台二手燃氣渦輪機裝進資料中心嗎？』」他告訴路透社，但婉拒透露客戶名稱或地點。

  ## 價格與取捨

  成本是重要驅動因素。據電力設備採購公司Origin Partners執行合夥人Zach Machuca表示，維護良好的二手渦輪機每百萬瓦售價約150萬至170萬美元，翻新機組則約每百萬瓦130萬美元。根據Aurora Energy Research的資料，新型渦輪機每百萬瓦造價約200萬至250萬美元。

  小型機組的折扣幅度可能更大。設備仲介商ReflowX總經理Dan Poole表示，兩台由卡特彼勒（Caterpillar）旗下Solar Turbines所製、各14.5百萬瓦的渦輪機，最近以合計1,250萬美元成交，每百萬瓦約43萬美元。同等規格的新品要價約3,500萬美元，且須等待五年，Poole說，但未透露買家身分。

  二手及改裝設備也有其自身風險。Tenorio指出，翻修過的渦輪機可能維護紀錄不清、剩餘使用壽命有限，長期售後服務支援也較弱。由噴射引擎改裝的航空衍生型機組，是否適合電網連接發電所需的全天候、持續不間斷運作模式，同樣存在疑慮。

  Enverus Intelligence Research分析師Noor Qureshi表示，次級市場不太可能化解整體供應緊張的局面。「市場本身已供應緊俏，多餘設備有限，因此不太可能顯著抵銷延遲問題，」她告訴路透社。她並指出，渦輪機只是其中一個障礙，空污許可、電網接入及其他因素也各自造成額外延誤。

  ## 小型渦輪機與混合機隊

  在大型渦輪機無從取得的情況下，開發商轉而部署小型機組，且往往以龐大數量因應。美國設備供應商SVI Bremco業務總監Scott Schreeg表示，部分業者在單一資料中心園區組建超過100台小型渦輪機的機隊，有時混用不同型號與廠牌。

  ## 蔓延至公用事業與替代方案

  這股壓力並不僅限於資料中心業者。美國聯邦政府所屬公用事業田納西河谷管理局（Tennessee Valley Authority）表示，新品、二手及翻新設備的價格在過去兩年「大幅攀升」。代表非營利消費者自有公用事業的全國農村電力合作協會（National Rural Electric Cooperative Association）執行長Jim Matheson表示，其會員正面臨前所未有的交貨期延長與急漲的價格。「需要新增容量的合作社，正在嘗試每一個可行選項，」包括翻新渦輪機，他說。

  這個瓶頸也加速了外界對燃氣渦輪機替代方案的興趣。芬蘭瓦錫蘭（Wärtsilä）製造往復式燃氣引擎——一種以活塞運作、有別於旋轉渦輪的不同技術——自去年春天以來，已從美國資料中心斬獲約3吉瓦的訂單，該公司能源業務總裁Anders Lindberg表示。「是什麼在驅動市場？交貨期、交貨期、交貨期，」Lindberg說。

  ## 意義

  燃氣渦輪機二手市場的活絡——這個品類直到不久前幾乎只供應傳統公用事業——反映了資料中心需求已如何重塑整個電力設備產業的採購生態。2028年預估需求量129吉瓦與預測製造產能87吉瓦之間的供需缺口，意味著次級市場所承受的壓力、設備價格的攀升以及交貨期的延長，都將持續延燒至本十年結束。
