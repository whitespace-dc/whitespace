body_en: |
  Taiwan power supply maker Cancon (stock code: 6282) plans to begin mass production of high-voltage direct current (HVDC) power supplies in 2027, targeting next-generation AI data center infrastructure, its chairman said as the company reported record third-quarter revenue.

  Chairman Hsu Chieh-li said the HVDC product line is on track for volume production in 2027 to compete in the AI data center power market. Cancon is simultaneously developing multiple liquid-cooled power solutions by drawing on its existing liquid-cooling mass production experience, Hsu said, as part of a broader push into high-power supply systems and system integration for AI data centers.

  ## Q3 Financial Results

  Third-quarter revenue reached NT$10.805 billion, up 13.56 percent quarter-on-quarter and 33.88 percent year-on-year, a single-quarter record. Gross margin was 22.15 percent, up 0.91 percentage points from the prior quarter.

  Net profit after tax was NT$207 million, equal to earnings per share of NT$0.25 for the quarter. Cancon said the period included approximately NT$49 million in one-time costs related to a product specification dispute, which weighed on earnings. Cumulative earnings per share for the first three quarters reached NT$0.61.

  ## Revenue Mix and Monthly Performance

  Demand from fuel cells, U.S. data centers, and networking equipment drove enterprise-segment power revenue to 59 percent of total Q3 sales, Cancon said, a share the company said continues to grow.

  October revenue was NT$3.453 billion, down 9.91 percent from September on fewer working days but up 32.30 percent year-on-year, the fourth-highest single-month total on record. Cumulative January-to-October revenue of NT$32.355 billion rose 28.56 percent year-on-year, a record for the period and already above the company's full-year 2025 revenue.

  ## Bloom Energy Inverter Supply

  Cancon is the exclusive global supplier of inverters for all units shipped by U.S. fuel cell company Bloom Energy. The inverters convert the 800-volt direct current produced by Bloom's fuel cells into the 480-volt or 415-volt alternating current used by standard equipment. Each Bloom Energy unit sources more than 30 percent of its components from Taiwan's supply chain.

  ## Grid Constraints and SOFC Demand

  Bloom Energy said some of its regional customers face situations where the power grid cannot supply sufficient electricity, prompting large cloud and AI data center operators to evaluate on-site power generation options. That dynamic has increased demand for behind-the-meter supplemental and off-grid solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) equipment, Bloom Energy said, with the company adding that its production capacity is in a supply-constrained state.
body_zh: |
  台灣電源供應器廠商康舒科技（股票代號：6282）計畫於2027年展開高壓直流（HVDC）電源供應器量產，目標鎖定次世代AI資料中心基礎建設。董事長許介立在公司公布第三季創紀錄營收之際，宣布了上述規劃。

  許介立表示，HVDC產品線按計畫將於2027年進入量產，正式競逐AI資料中心電源市場。他進一步指出，康舒科技正運用既有液冷量產經驗，同步開發多款液冷電源解決方案，作為全面布局AI資料中心高功率電源系統與系統整合市場的一部分。

  ## 第三季財務表現

  第三季營收達新台幣108.05億元，季增13.56%、年增33.88%，創單季歷史新高。毛利率為22.15%，較上季提升0.91個百分點。

  稅後淨利為新台幣2.07億元，當季每股盈餘為0.25元。康舒科技表示，本季含有約新台幣4,900萬元的一次性費用，起因為產品規格爭議，對獲利造成一定壓力。累計前三季每股盈餘達0.61元。

  ## 營收結構與月度表現

  燃料電池、美國資料中心及網通設備需求強勁，帶動企業電源營收占第三季整體營收的59%，康舒科技表示此一比重仍持續提升。

  10月營收為新台幣34.53億元，因工作天數較少，較9月下滑9.91%，但仍較去年同期成長32.30%，為歷史單月第四高紀錄。累計1至10月營收達新台幣323.55億元，年增28.56%，不僅創同期歷史新高，金額更已超越公司2025年全年營收。

  ## Bloom Energy逆變器供應

  康舒科技是美國燃料電池大廠Bloom Energy旗下所有出貨機台的全球獨家逆變器供應商。這些逆變器負責將Bloom燃料電池產出的800伏特直流電，轉換成標準設備所使用的480伏特或415伏特交流電。Bloom Energy每台機組有逾30%的零組件來自台灣供應鏈。

  ## 電網限制與SOFC需求

  Bloom Energy表示，其部分區域客戶面臨電網無法提供足夠電力的困境，促使大型雲端及AI資料中心業者開始評估場域自建發電方案。這一動態已推升錶後補充供電及離網型固態氧化物燃料電池（SOFC）設備的需求。Bloom Energy並指出，公司目前的產能處於供不應求的狀態。
