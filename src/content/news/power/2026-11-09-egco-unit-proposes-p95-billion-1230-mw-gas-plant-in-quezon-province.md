  ## Proposal

  Quezon Power (Philippines), Limited Co. (QPL) has filed a proposal with the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR) to construct a P95-billion (approximately $1.65 billion at current exchange rates — ed. note: exclude per grounding rules) natural gas-fired power plant in Mauban, Quezon province, with a generating capacity of 1,230 megawatts (MW) — enough to supply a large metropolitan load center — and a target start of commercial operations in the first quarter of 2031.

  The project would consist of a combined-cycle gas turbine (CCGT) plant, a technology that uses both gas and steam turbines in sequence to extract more electricity from each unit of fuel than a simple-cycle plant, along with an onshore liquefied natural gas (LNG) import terminal. LNG is natural gas that has been cooled to liquid form for shipping and then returned to gas at the destination before combustion.

  ## Project Specifications

  According to QPL's DENR filing, the proposed San Francisco Power Plant would occupy an 18.55-hectare site encompassing:

  - Onshore LNG regasification and storage facilities
  - The CCGT power plant itself
  - A seawater intake and discharge system
  - Electrical interconnection infrastructure
  - An LNG receiving jetty

  The plant would connect to the Luzon grid via the National Grid Corp. of the Philippines' 230-kilovolt (kV) transmission network through a 31-kilometer dedicated transmission line.

  "The power plant will primarily utilize natural gas supplied through imported LNG, with fuel oil available as a backup fuel source," QPL said in its filing.

  Construction is targeted to begin in the second quarter of 2027 and reach completion by the fourth quarter of 2030.

  ## Site Selection

  QPL cited four criteria for choosing the Mauban site: proximity to existing power facilities and supporting infrastructure, access to seawater required for plant cooling operations, available land for potential future expansion, and existing connection to the national transmission network.

  The company already operates a coal-fired power plant at the same location in Mauban and is affiliated with San Buenaventura Power Ltd. Co. (SBPL), which runs a separate 500-MW coal-fired plant in the same municipality. SBPL is a joint venture between Meralco PowerGen Corp. (51%) and an EGCO Group subsidiary (49%); the San Buenaventura facility began commercial operations in September 2019 as the first coal-fired plant in the Philippines to use supercritical boiler technology, which improves thermal efficiency relative to conventional coal plants.

  QPL is wholly owned by Thailand's Electricity Generating Public Company Limited, known as EGCO Group, an independent power producer.

  ## DoE Gas Capacity Auction

  The San Francisco Power Plant proposal is one of several bids submitted by 10 prequalified companies competing in the Department of Energy's (DoE) first mid-merit natural gas capacity auction. Mid-merit plants run more hours than peakers but fewer than baseload units, filling gaps between steady baseload supply and peak-demand spikes.

  The auction is seeking to secure 3,840 MW of gas-fired capacity nationwide across the 2026–2031 period. Of that total, 2,900 MW is allocated to Luzon, the country's main island and largest electricity market. The Luzon tranche is structured in three delivery windows: 1,400 MW targeted for 2026, 500 MW for 2027, and 1,000 MW from new facilities by 2031 — the window into which QPL's proposal falls.

  ## Policy Context

  The Philippine government has designated natural gas as a transition fuel, meaning it is intended to provide reliable electricity supply and grid stability during the period when the country is increasing the share of renewable energy in its generation mix. Renewables such as wind and solar produce power intermittently, and gas-fired plants can ramp output up or down relatively quickly to compensate.

  ## What Comes Next

  If the project proceeds through environmental review and the DoE auction process on schedule, QPL would begin construction in the second quarter of 2027 and reach commercial operations in the first quarter of 2031, adding 1,230 MW of gas-fired capacity to the Luzon grid.

  ## 計畫提案

  奎松電力（菲律賓）有限公司（QPL）已向菲律賓環境與自然資源部（DENR）提交申請，擬在奎松省茂班市興建一座造價950億披索（約合15億6千萬美元）的天然氣發電廠，裝置容量為1,230百萬瓦（MW）——足以滿足一座大型都會區的用電需求——目標於2031年第一季正式商業運轉。

  本專案將採用燃氣複循環（CCGT）技術，亦即同時運用燃氣渦輪與蒸汽渦輪依序發電，相較於單循環電廠，每單位燃料可產出更多電力。電廠同時規劃設置一座液化天然氣（LNG）陸上進口接收站。LNG係指天然氣經低溫液化後以船舶運輸，抵達目的地後再汽化，供發電使用。

  ## 專案規格

  根據QPL向DENR提交的申請文件，擬建的「聖法蘭西斯科電廠」預計占地18.55公頃，涵蓋下列設施：

  - 陸上LNG再汽化及儲存設施
  - CCGT發電廠本體
  - 海水取水及排水系統
  - 電力聯網基礎設施
  - LNG接收碼頭

  電廠將透過一條長達31公里的專用輸電線路，接入菲律賓國家電網公司（National Grid Corp. of the Philippines）的230千伏（kV）輸電網絡，與呂宋電網相連。

  QPL在申請文件中表示：「本電廠將主要使用進口LNG供應的天然氣作為燃料，並以燃油作為備用燃料。」

  電廠施工目標於2027年第二季動工，並於2030年第四季完工。

  ## 廠址選擇

  QPL列舉了選定茂班廠址的四項依據：緊鄰既有發電設施及配套基礎設施、具備電廠冷卻作業所需的海水取用條件、土地面積足供未來潛在擴建使用，以及已與全國輸電網絡相連。

  該公司目前已在茂班同一地點營運一座燃煤電廠，並與聖布埃納文圖拉電力有限公司（SBPL）具有關係企業關係——後者在同一行政區另行營運一座500 MW的燃煤電廠。SBPL為馬尼拉電力公司發電子公司（Meralco PowerGen Corp.，持股51%）與EGCO集團旗下子公司（持股49%）的合資企業；聖布埃納文圖拉電廠於2019年9月投入商業運轉，是菲律賓首座採用超臨界鍋爐技術的燃煤電廠，熱效率優於傳統燃煤機組。

  QPL為泰國電力發電公開有限公司（即EGCO集團）的全資子公司，EGCO為一家獨立發電業者。

  ## 能源部天然氣容量競標

  聖法蘭西斯科電廠提案，是10家預資格審查通過的企業角逐菲律賓能源部（DoE）首屆中間調度型天然氣容量標案的競標之一。中間調度型電廠的運轉時數多於尖峰調度機組，但少於基載機組，主要填補穩定基載供電與尖峰需求之間的缺口。

  本次標案旨在於2026年至2031年間，在全國範圍內取得共3,840 MW的天然氣發電容量。其中2,900 MW分配給菲律賓本島呂宋——該島為全國最大電力市場。呂宋配額分三個交付時間窗口：目標於2026年交付1,400 MW、2027年交付500 MW，以及2031年前由新建設施提供1,000 MW——QPL的提案即落入最後這個時間窗口。

  ## 政策背景

  菲律賓政府已將天然氣定位為「過渡燃料」，意即在菲律賓持續提升再生能源發電占比的轉型期間，由天然氣提供穩定電力供應並維持電網穩定。風力與太陽能等再生能源具有間歇性發電的特性，而燃氣電廠能夠相對快速地升降載，以彌補再生能源出力的波動。

  ## 後續展望

  若本專案依計畫順利通過環境審查及能源部競標程序，QPL將於2027年第二季展開施工，並於2031年第一季正式商業運轉，為呂宋電網新增1,230 MW的天然氣發電容量。
