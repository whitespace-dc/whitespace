body_en: |
  PT Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk, the Indonesian geothermal producer trading under the ticker PGEO, is targeting 1 gigawatt (GW) of self-operated installed capacity by 2028, up from 727 megawatts (MW) today, with 12 priority projects totaling 320 MW identified to close the gap.

  Five of those 12 projects, with a combined capacity of 105 MW, have been pulled forward from schedules that previously called for them to come online after 2028, the company said. The expansion is part of a longer-range plan to reach 1.8 GW of self-operated capacity by 2033, to be achieved through organic growth, acquisitions, and project acceleration, Chief Executive Officer Ahmad Yani said.

  ## Project pipeline

  Three geothermal power plant complexes are central to the near-term plan: Hululais, Lumut Balai, and Lahendong. The facilities are designated PLTP in Indonesian project documents, an abbreviation for pembangkit listrik tenaga panas bumi, meaning geothermal power plant.

  PLTP Hululais Units 1 and 2 have secured a steam sales contract (SSC) — a commercial agreement governing geothermal steam supply — and are being prepared to enter the engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning (EPCC) phase. The company has set a 2028 target for commercial operation at Hululais.

  PLTP Lumut Balai Unit 3 has a power purchase agreement (PPA) in place and has completed two wells. The project has entered front end engineering design (FEED), an early-stage technical planning step that precedes EPCC, with commercial operation targeted for 2030.

  PLTP Lumut Balai Unit 4 is at an earlier stage, with infrastructure construction underway, including approximately 10 kilometers of access road to the drilling site, the company said.

  PLTP Lahendong Units 7 and 8 are also listed among active development projects, though the company provided no contract status or commercial operation schedule for those units.

  ## Drilling strategy

  Exploration and Development Director Edwil Suzandi said drilling underpins both production maintenance and new capacity development. "Drilling is one of PGE's concrete steps in developing geothermal energy utilization, both to open up new capacity potential and to optimize assets already in operation. Maintaining production and steam supply availability is just as important as developing new capacity," Suzandi said.

  ## Financial position

  PGEO reported nine-month 2026 revenue of US$352.2 million, up 10.46% year-on-year (YoY). Net profit rose 21.45% YoY to US$126.62 million, a rate more than double that of revenue growth. Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (EBITDA) climbed 15.58% YoY to US$287.77 million.

  Cash and equivalents stood at US$713.68 million as of 30 September 2026. Total liabilities fell 3.37% over the same period to US$955.55 million, which the company said preserves financial flexibility for continued expansion.

  Chief Financial Officer Fransetya Hasudungan Hutabarat said the results reflect consistent execution of a long-cycle business model. "Geothermal projects at PGE are long-term, multi-year undertakings. Growth is therefore measured not only by capacity additions, but also by the company's ability to maintain profitability and financial health at each stage of development," he said.

  ## Production record

  PGEO produced 4,114 gigawatt-hours (GWh) of geothermal electricity from self-operated working areas in the first nine months of 2026, an all-time high for any nine-month period. The figure exceeded the company's own 3,801 GWh target by 8.22 percentage points, and was 9.86% above the 3,747 GWh produced in the same period of 2025.

  ## What comes next

  Reaching 1 GW by 2028 requires PGEO to add roughly 273 MW to its current base — an increase of approximately 38% — within two years. The acceleration of five projects from post-2028 schedules signals the company is treating the capacity milestone as a near-term operational commitment. Ahmad Yani said the expansion is aligned with Indonesia's national energy transition and energy security agenda.

  The 2033 target of 1.8 GW represents approximately 2.5 times the company's current installed capacity.

  The nearest scheduled milestone in the pipeline is commercial operation at PLTP Hululais, targeted for 2028, which has advanced furthest through the contracting and project development sequence, having secured both an SSC and EPCC preparation status.

  PT Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk（股票代碼：PGEO）為印尼地熱能源生產商，該公司目標在2028年前將自營地熱裝置容量從現有的727百萬瓦（MW）提升至1千兆瓦（GW），並已確立12項優先開發計畫，合計容量達320 MW，以填補這一缺口。

  其中五項計畫合計容量105 MW，原定2028年後才能上線，現已提前納入開發時程。執行長Ahmad Yani表示，此擴張計畫是更長遠願景的一部分，將透過有機成長、併購及加速推進專案三管齊下，於2033年前達到1.8 GW的自營裝置容量。

  ## 開發計畫管線

  近期計畫的核心是三座地熱電廠園區：Hululais、Lumut Balai及Lahendong。這些設施在印尼官方文件中以PLTP為代稱，全名為pembangkit listrik tenaga panas bumi，意即地熱發電廠。

  PLTP Hululais第1及第2機組已取得蒸汽銷售合約（SSC）——即規範地熱蒸汽供應的商業協議——目前正準備進入工程、採購、施工及試運轉（EPCC）階段。公司已將Hululais的商業運轉目標定於2028年。

  PLTP Lumut Balai第3機組已簽署購電協議（PPA），並完成兩口鑽井。該計畫已進入前端工程設計（FEED）階段——FEED為先於EPCC的早期技術規劃步驟——商業運轉目標訂於2030年。

  PLTP Lumut Balai第4機組目前處於較早期階段，基礎設施建設工程正在進行中，包括通往鑽探現場約10公里的進場道路，公司表示。

  PLTP Lahendong第7及第8機組亦列為積極開發中的計畫，惟公司未提供這兩個機組的合約狀態或商業運轉時程。

  ## 鑽探策略

  探勘暨開發總監Edwil Suzandi表示，鑽探工程是維持產能與新增容量的核心基礎。「鑽探是PGE推動地熱能源開發利用的具體行動，不僅用於開拓新產能潛力，也用於優化現有運營資產。維持產能與蒸汽供應穩定，與開發新容量同等重要，」Suzandi表示。

  ## 財務狀況

  PGEO公布2026年前九個月營收為3億5,220萬美元，年增10.46%。淨利年增21.45%至1億2,662萬美元，增速逾營收成長率的兩倍。稅息折舊及攤銷前盈餘（EBITDA）年增15.58%至2億8,777萬美元。

  截至2026年9月30日，現金及約當現金為7億1,368萬美元。同期總負債下降3.37%至9億5,555萬美元，公司表示這為持續擴張保留了充裕的財務彈性。

  財務長Fransetya Hasudungan Hutabarat表示，上述成果反映了長周期商業模式的穩定執行。「PGE的地熱計畫屬於長期、多年期的開發項目，因此衡量成長不僅看裝置容量的增加，更要看公司在每個開發階段維持獲利能力與財務健康的能力，」他表示。

  ## 創下產量紀錄

  PGEO在2026年前九個月，於自營作業區生產了4,114千兆瓦時（GWh）的地熱電力，創下任何九個月期間的歷史新高。這一數字超出公司自訂3,801 GWh目標8.22個百分點，並較2025年同期的3,747 GWh高出9.86%。

  ## 後續展望

  若要在2028年前達成1 GW目標，PGEO必須在兩年內於現有基礎上新增約273 MW——增幅約38%。五個計畫提前納入2028年前時程，顯示公司已將容量里程碑視為近期的具體運營承諾。Ahmad Yani表示，此擴張計畫符合印尼國家能源轉型與能源安全議程。

  2033年的1.8 GW目標，約為公司目前裝置容量的2.5倍。

  在計畫時程中，最近的里程碑為PLTP Hululais的商業運轉，目標年份訂於2028年。該計畫在簽約與專案開發流程上進展最為深入，已取得SSC並達到EPCC準備狀態。
