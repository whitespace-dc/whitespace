body_en: |
  US technology companies are constructing private natural gas power plants at a scale that could rival national grid systems, bypassing public utilities to accelerate AI data center deployment while generating emissions analysts say could exceed 200 million tons of carbon dioxide per year.

  Research firm Cleanview identified approximately 60 data centers planned over the coming years carrying a combined 97 gigawatts (GW) of "behind-the-meter" generation — power plants sited at or near a facility and not connected to the public grid — stretching into the early 2030s, according to Cleanview's analysis shared with AFP. At 97 GW, that total is roughly equal to the entire installed generation capacity of Mexico.

  The most recent project to emerge is a 7.5 GW plant in Texas tied to Amazon data centers. Air pollution permits for the facility were issued in January; Amazon received construction permits last week; and satellite imagery confirmed clearing had begun at the site named in the air permit records. Cleanview subsequently confirmed the Amazon connection directly with the company.

  ## Scale and participants

  Nearly every major hyperscaler is pursuing behind-the-meter projects, Cleanview found. Meta, Microsoft, and Oracle are among those identified. AI laboratories including OpenAI and Anthropic have signed leases for more than 10 GW of such capacity combined.

  xAI, Elon Musk's AI company, effectively established the model in 2024 when it trucked mobile gas turbines to its data center in Memphis, Tennessee. xAI accounts for most of the approximately 2 GW of behind-the-meter capacity operating today.

  Because companies typically do not announce these projects publicly, analysts must piece together air permit filings, construction records, and satellite imagery to link facilities to specific operators.

  ## Equipment and geography

  Rather than conventional utility-grade hardware, the plants rely on a range of non-standard equipment: trailer-mounted generators, turbines originally designed for aircraft and warships, piston engines of the kind found in vehicles, and refurbished turbines from industrial operations.

  Development is concentrated in Republican-leaning states. Texas leads with more than 40 GW of announced behind-the-meter capacity.

  ## Emissions and public health

  Even under conservative assumptions about how many plants are built, how efficient the turbines are, and how often they run, the planned fleet would exceed 200 million tons of CO2 per year, equivalent to the annual emissions of more than 46 million gasoline-powered cars, according to Cleanview's analysis.

  Beyond greenhouse gases, natural gas plants emit fine particulate matter, linked to heart and lung disease; nitrogen oxides, which aggravate respiratory conditions; and hazardous air pollutants including mercury, formaldehyde, and benzene.

  "Powering data centres with behind-the-meter generation that relies heavily on dirty gas or diesel turbines is a surefire way to needlessly pump a variety of hazardous pollutants into local communities' air while worsening climate change," John Rogers, associate director of energy analytics at the Union of Concerned Scientists, told AFP.

  "These dirty plants, which are also subject to less public accountability, are unacceptable when record heat and fires are showing us that we need to change course," Yuqi Zhu, a senior policy analyst at the Natural Resources Defense Council, told AFP.

  ## Legal challenges and federal policy

  The trend has drawn litigation. The NAACP civil rights organization has filed suit against xAI, accusing it of unlawfully operating turbines in Mississippi to power its Memphis data center.

  "We are concerned that if this case is decided against us, data centres across the country will be emboldened to go forward," said Laura Thoms, enforcement director at Earthjustice, which is co-representing the NAACP.

  The Trump administration has created a voluntary "Ratepayer Protection Pledge," under which signatories promise not to pass data center costs to residential electricity customers — a measure Thoms told AFP she fears will only accelerate the problem.

  ## Broader fossil fuel reversal

  The behind-the-meter surge is part of a wider reversal in US fossil fuel generation. New announced fossil fuel capacity for the public grid hit a low in 2022 before rebounding in 2023 as AI-driven electricity demand grew, according to data compiled by Atlas Public Policy.

  "In the first half of 2026 alone, we're already seeing that the announced new fossil capacity is higher than it's been in the last 10 years, so that's a pretty significant sign," Rachel Reolfi, a senior policy analyst at Atlas Public Policy, told AFP.
body_zh: |
  美國科技公司正以足以媲美國家電網規模的方式，大規模興建私有天然氣發電廠，繞過公共事業電網以加速AI資料中心的部署，同時製造出分析師估計每年恐超過2億公噸二氧化碳的排放量。

  研究機構Cleanview識別出約60座計畫於未來數年內建設的資料中心，合計擁有97吉瓦（GW）的「表後發電」容量——亦即設置於設施內部或附近、不接入公共電網的發電廠——建設時程延伸至2030年代初期。Cleanview將此分析結果提供給法新社（AFP）。97吉瓦的總量，大致相當於墨西哥全國裝置發電容量。

  目前最新浮出檯面的專案，是德克薩斯州一座與亞馬遜（Amazon）資料中心相連、容量達7.5吉瓦的電廠。該設施的空氣污染許可證已於今年1月核發；亞馬遜上週取得建設許可；衛星影像亦確認，空氣許可紀錄所載地點已開始進行地面清整作業。Cleanview隨後直接向亞馬遜方面確認了兩者之間的關聯。

  ## 規模與參與業者

  Cleanview的調查發現，幾乎每一家主要的超大型雲端業者都在推動表後發電專案，其中包括Meta、微軟（Microsoft）和甲骨文（Oracle）。OpenAI與Anthropic等AI實驗室所簽署的此類容量租約，合計已逾10吉瓦。

  馬斯克（Elon Musk）旗下的AI公司xAI，實際上在2024年奠定了這套模式的先例——該公司以卡車將可移動式燃氣渦輪機運至位於田納西州孟菲斯的資料中心。在目前已運行的約2吉瓦表後發電容量中，xAI占了其中大部分。

  由於企業通常不會公開宣布這類專案，分析師必須拼湊空氣許可申請文件、施工紀錄與衛星影像，才能將設施與特定業者掛鉤。

  ## 設備與地理分布

  這些電廠並非採用傳統公用事業等級的設備，而是使用各式非標準器械：拖車掛載式發電機、原為飛機與軍艦設計的渦輪機、汽車等級的活塞引擎，以及來自工業設施的翻新渦輪機。

  在地理分布上，開發案多集中於傾向共和黨的州別。德克薩斯州以超過40吉瓦的已宣告表後發電容量，高居各州之首。

  ## 排放與公共衛生

  即便以保守假設——亦即實際建造的電廠數量較少、渦輪效率較高、運轉時數較短——來估算，這批計畫中的電廠每年仍將排放超過2億公噸的二氧化碳。根據Cleanview的分析，此一數字相當於逾4,600萬輛燃油轎車的年排放總量。

  除溫室氣體之外，天然氣發電廠還會排放與心臟及肺部疾病相關的細懸浮微粒，以及會加劇呼吸道症狀的氮氧化物，和包含汞、甲醛、苯在內的有害空氣污染物。

  「以仰賴骯髒天然氣或柴油渦輪機的表後發電為資料中心供電，無異於無謂地將各種有害污染物泵入在地社區的空氣中，同時加劇氣候變遷。」憂思科學家聯盟（Union of Concerned Scientists）能源分析副主任約翰·羅傑斯（John Rogers）向法新社表示。

  「這些骯髒的電廠本就較少受到公共課責的約束；在創紀錄的高溫與野火不斷提醒我們必須改弦易轍之際，這樣的作法完全無法被接受。」自然資源保護協會（Natural Resources Defense Council）資深政策分析師朱玉琦（Yuqi Zhu）向法新社說道。

  ## 法律挑戰與聯邦政策

  這股趨勢已引發訴訟。美國全國有色人種促進協會（NAACP）已對xAI提起訴訟，指控該公司非法在密西西比州操作渦輪機，為其孟菲斯資料中心供電。

  「我們擔心，若此案判決對我們不利，全國各地的資料中心都將因此受到鼓舞，更大膽地向前推進。」共同代理NAACP的地球正義組織（Earthjustice）執法主任蘿拉·湯姆斯（Laura Thoms）表示。

  川普政府設立了一項名為「電費保護承諾」（Ratepayer Protection Pledge）的自願性計畫，簽署方承諾不將資料中心的相關成本轉嫁給住宅用電戶。湯姆斯向法新社表示，她擔憂這項措施只會加速問題惡化。

  ## 化石燃料使用的更廣泛逆轉

  表後發電的熱潮，是美國化石燃料發電更廣泛逆轉趨勢的一部分。根據Atlas Public Policy彙整的資料，新宣告進入公共電網的化石燃料裝置容量曾於2022年觸底，隨後在AI驅動的用電需求成長下，於2023年起開始回升。

  「光是2026年上半年，我們就已看到新宣告的化石燃料容量高於過去10年的水準，這是一個相當顯著的信號。」Atlas Public Policy資深政策分析師瑞秋·雷歐菲（Rachel Reolfi）向法新社表示。
