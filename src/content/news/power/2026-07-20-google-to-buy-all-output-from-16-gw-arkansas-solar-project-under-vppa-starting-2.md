body_en: |
  Google has agreed to purchase the entire initial electricity output of the Steel River solar project in Arkansas — a 1.6-gigawatt facility under development by Cypress Creek Energy — through a virtual power purchase agreement, with commercial deliveries set to begin in 2029.

  The deal also covers approximately 2 gigawatts of battery energy storage co-located with the project, a capacity Cypress Creek says is equivalent to the electricity needs of roughly 315,000 homes.

  ## How a VPPA works

  The agreement uses a Virtual Power Purchase Agreement, or VPPA — a contract structure in which electricity generated by the project flows into the public grid rather than directly to Google's facilities. Under this model, Google commits to long-term purchasing obligations that help Cypress Creek secure project financing. Google's data centers continue to draw power from the regional grid, which supplies a mix of coal, natural gas, nuclear, and renewable generation depending on what is available at any given time. Google neither owns nor operates the generating assets.

  ## Project scale

  The full Steel River project is designed to reach up to 2.46 gigawatts of solar generating capacity and 2.94 gigawatt-hours of battery storage. Google's VPPA covers the project's initial phase: the 1.6 GW solar tranche and its associated battery capacity. Construction is scheduled for completion in 2029.

  ## Demand driven by AI expansion

  Google's electricity consumption and grid-based emissions rose approximately 37 percent during 2025, according to reports cited by Kompas.com, driven by the expansion of artificial intelligence services and data center infrastructure. The company has previously signed power purchase agreements for hydroelectric output and other clean energy projects as part of broader carbon reduction commitments.

  ## Sector context

  Google's Arkansas deal is part of a broader pattern of large technology companies contracting for renewable capacity. Meta has signed a power purchase agreement for output from the Waterloo Solar project in Texas. Amazon took a different approach, acquiring the Sunstone solar project in Oregon outright through a subsidiary rather than contracting for its output. Google's Steel River agreement follows the contract model, keeping ownership of the generating asset with the developer.

  The full Steel River project, encompassing 2.46 GW of solar capacity and 2.94 GWh of battery storage, is scheduled to reach completion in 2029.

  ## VPPA 的運作方式

  此協議採用虛擬購電協議架構——在這種合約模式下，太陽能場址所發的電力直接饋入公共電網，而非直送 Google 旗下設施。Google 承諾長期購電義務，藉此協助 Cypress Creek 取得專案融資。Google 的資料中心仍持續從區域電網取電，該電網依實際供需狀況，混合供應來自煤炭、天然氣、核能及各類再生能源的電力。Google 本身並不持有也不運營這些發電資產。

  Steel River 完整專案的設計容量最高可達 2.46 GW 太陽能發電量，以及 2.94 GWh 電池儲能容量。Google 的 VPPA 涵蓋專案的初期階段：1.6 GW 的太陽能部分及其配套儲能容量。建設工程預計於 2029 年完工。

  ## AI 擴張帶動電力需求

  根據 Kompas.com 引述的報告，受人工智慧服務擴張及資料中心基礎設施建置推動，Google 的用電量與電網排放量在 2025 年間成長了約 37%。為履行廣泛的減碳承諾，該公司此前已陸續就水力發電及其他潔淨能源專案簽署購電協議。

  ## 產業背景

  Google 在阿肯色州的這項協議，是大型科技公司大規模採購再生能源容量的普遍趨勢之一。Meta 已就德克薩斯州 Waterloo Solar 專案的發電量簽署購電協議；Amazon 則採取不同策略，透過子公司直接收購奧勒岡州的 Sunstone 太陽能案，而非採契約購買其發電量。Google 的 Steel River 協議沿用合約模式，將發電資產的所有權保留在開發商手中。
