body_en: |
  Malaysian electricity demand will more than double by 2050, driven by a rapid expansion of data centres, the Ministry of Energy Transition and Water Transformation said Thursday.

  Total power demand is forecast to climb to 49.8 gigawatts (GW) by mid-century from 22.5 GW in 2027, the ministry said. Data centres alone will account for 21% of that load by 2050, up from 11% in 2027.

  ## Scale of the data centre buildout

  Malaysia has drawn billions of dollars in investment from major technology companies as it pursues a strategy to become a regional hub for artificial intelligence. Oracle, Amazon, and Google — a subsidiary of Alphabet — have all committed capital to the country. Google plans to spend $15 billion expanding data centre and cloud operations in Malaysia over the next two years, according to the ministry's statement.

  ## Power supply challenges

  Meeting demand that large will require substantial changes to Malaysia's generation mix. The country is considering replacing part of its coal fleet with nuclear power, though the ministry said that transition is not expected until 2040.

  This month, Malaysia announced plans to establish a separate wholesale electricity market specifically for large power consumers, including data centres. The move is designed to safeguard electricity supply for households and factories as industrial demand scales up.

  ## US export restrictions and smuggling concerns

  Tighter United States restrictions on access to advanced technology pose a risk to Malaysia's data centre ambitions. Washington has blacklisted some companies in the region over concerns that Nvidia chips are being smuggled to China via Southeast Asia. Malaysia has pledged to instruct data centre operators not to host, lease space to, or provide services to the blacklisted firms.

  ## What this means

  The forecast underscores how data centre investment, concentrated over a relatively short window, can fundamentally reshape a country's long-term energy planning. Malaysia's grid will need to absorb a near-doubling of total demand while simultaneously managing a shift away from coal, standing up new market structures for industrial consumers, and navigating geopolitical constraints on the technology supply chain that underpins the investment in the first place.

  Malaysia plans to launch the separate wholesale electricity market for large power consumers, including data centres, as the next near-term step in managing that transition.

  馬來西亞能源轉型暨水務轉型部週四表示，受資料中心快速擴張驅動，全國電力需求預計至2050年將較現今倍增以上。

  部方指出，總電力需求預測將從2027年的22.5吉瓦（GW）攀升至本世紀中葉的49.8吉瓦。其中資料中心單項負載到2050年將佔總需求的21%，高於2027年的11%。

  ## 資料中心建設規模

  馬來西亞積極爭取成為區域人工智慧樞紐，已吸引多家科技巨頭投入數十億美元資金。甲骨文（Oracle）、亞馬遜（Amazon）及谷歌（Google，字母控股旗下子公司）均已宣布在馬來西亞投資設廠。根據能源轉型暨水務轉型部發布的聲明，谷歌計畫在未來兩年內斥資150億美元，擴充馬來西亞的資料中心與雲端運算業務。

  ## 電力供應挑戰

  要因應如此龐大的電力需求，馬來西亞的發電組合勢必面臨重大調整。目前，該國正研議以核能取代部分燃煤機組，但能源部表示，這項轉型預計最快要到2040年才能落實。

  本月稍早，馬來西亞宣布計畫針對資料中心等大型用電戶，另行建立獨立的電力批發市場。此舉旨在工業用電規模持續擴大之際，確保一般家庭與工廠的電力供應不受衝擊。

  ## 美國出口管制與走私疑慮

  美國持續收緊對先進科技的出口管制，對馬來西亞發展資料中心的雄心構成風險。由於外界擔憂輝達（Nvidia）晶片透過東南亞走私流入中國大陸，華盛頓已將該地區部分企業列入黑名單。對此，馬來西亞已承諾，將要求資料中心業者不得為遭列入黑名單的公司提供主機代管、租賃空間或任何相關服務。

  ## 未來展望

  上述預測凸顯出，當資料中心投資在相對短暫的時間內密集湧入，將如何從根本上重塑一個國家的長期能源規劃。馬來西亞電網須在消化近乎倍增的總需求同時，兼顧推動去煤轉型、為工業大用戶建立新的市場機制，以及應對地緣政治因素對支撐相關投資之科技供應鏈所帶來的制約——三重挑戰環環相扣，缺一不可。

  馬來西亞計畫在近期推出針對資料中心等大型用電戶的獨立電力批發市場，作為因應這場能源轉型的下一個關鍵步驟。
