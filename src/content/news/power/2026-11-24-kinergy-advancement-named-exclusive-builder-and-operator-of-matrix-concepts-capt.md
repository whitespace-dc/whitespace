body_en: |
  Malaysian electrical contractor Kinergy Advancement Bhd has signed a binding exclusive term sheet appointing it as the sole builder and long-term operator of captive power plants at industrial sites owned by property developer Matrix Concepts Holdings Bhd, Kinergy disclosed in a Kuala Lumpur stock exchange filing on Monday.

  The plants are intended to supply dedicated electricity to data centres, semiconductor manufacturers, advanced manufacturers, and other power-intensive operations at Matrix Concepts' industrial developments in Malaysia.

  ## Scope and division of responsibilities

  Under the term sheet, Kinergy will serve as both the engineering, procurement, construction and commissioning (EPCC) contractor and the operations and maintenance (O&M) provider for the proposed plants. As EPCC contractor, Kinergy takes responsibility for delivering the facilities from design through start-up; as O&M provider, it will run them after commissioning.

  Matrix Concepts will supply the land, carry out site preparation and infrastructure provision, coordinate with stakeholders, and obtain planning approvals. Kinergy will conduct feasibility studies, secure regulatory approvals and licences, construct the plants, and operate them thereafter.

  The companies did not disclose the estimated investment value, total generation capacity, number of plants, or a development timeline. Definitive agreements arising from the binding term sheet would be announced in due course, Kinergy said. The filing did not specify which of Matrix Concepts' industrial sites would be included.

  ## Rationale for dedicated on-site generation

  Captive power plants generate electricity on-site for a designated industrial development rather than drawing supply from the shared national electricity grid. Kinergy's executive deputy chairman and group managing director, Datuk Dr Lai Keng Onn, said the approach could help industrial developments reduce their exposure to grid supply constraints and disruptions.

  "While grid connectivity remains fundamental, large-scale industrial developments and energy-intensive sites require a more comprehensive approach to power planning, particularly in ensuring sufficient and reliable electricity to support future tenant requirements and long-term operations," Lai said. He added that bringing generation closer to the point of consumption could "help mitigate exposure to grid supply constraints and interruptions, while providing greater certainty over electricity availability and continuity."

  Kinergy said the arrangement would expand its involvement in power generation and utility infrastructure beyond conventional electrical construction.

  ## Kinergy's power project experience

  Kinergy has served as Petroliam Nasional Bhd's (PETRONAS, Malaysia's national oil company) local technical partner for the 72-megawatt (MW) Sipitang power plant and as main EPCC contractor for the 120MW Labuan power plant. It has also secured electrical works for a hyperscale data centre, a large-scale computing facility, as part of its expansion into energy-intensive infrastructure.

  ## Matrix Concepts' industrial portfolio

  Matrix Concepts' industrial development portfolio includes Sendayan TechValley, a 1,100-acre park in Negeri Sembilan that has been fully developed, and MVV TechValley, a 1,000-acre site within the Malaysia Vision Valley 2.0 development corridor that is currently under development.

  The developer is also expanding its industrial footprint. It has announced plans for an RM8 billion industrial and logistics park at MVV TechValley in partnership with Golog Holdings, alongside a proposed RM2.1 billion industrial-led mixed development in Kulai, Johor.

  ## Market response

  Kinergy shares fell 0.5 sen, or 1.35%, to 36.5 sen at Monday's noon market break, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM797.55 million. Matrix Concepts shares declined one sen, or 1.11%, to 89 sen, valuing the developer at RM1.67 billion.

  ## Outlook

  The exclusive arrangement expands Kinergy's power generation mandate beyond previous standalone plant contracts into the role of long-term infrastructure partner tied to Matrix Concepts' active industrial land programme. The financial and technical parameters of the deal remain undisclosed, with Kinergy saying definitive agreements would be announced in due course.

  ## Kinergy獲Matrix Concepts獨家授權　建置並營運專屬電廠

  馬來西亞電氣承包商Kinergy Advancement Bhd週一在吉隆坡交易所公告，宣布已與房地產開發商Matrix Concepts Holdings Bhd簽署具約束力的獨家條款書，取得在Matrix Concepts旗下工業用地內，擔任唯一建置方及長期營運商的資格，以供應專屬電力給資料中心、半導體製造商、先進製造業者及其他高耗能設施使用。

  ## 合作範疇與職責劃分

  根據條款書，Kinergy將同時扮演工程、採購、施工及試運轉（EPCC）承包商，以及營運與維護（O&M）服務提供商兩種角色。身為EPCC承包商，Kinergy負責從設計到啟用的全流程交付；進入營運階段後，則持續提供O&M服務。

  Matrix Concepts負責提供土地、進行場地整備與基礎設施建置、協調各方利害關係人，並取得規劃許可。Kinergy則負責進行可行性研究、取得法規審批與相關執照、完成電廠建設，並於完工後承接後續營運工作。

  雙方並未揭露預估投資金額、總發電容量、電廠數量或開發時程。Kinergy表示，由具約束力條款書衍生的正式協議，將於適當時機另行公告。此次交易所涉及的具體工業用地亦未在公告中指明。

  ## 採用專屬現地發電的考量

  專屬電廠（captive power plant）直接在工業園區現地發電，專供特定開發案使用，不依賴共用的國家電網供電。Kinergy執行副董事長兼集團總裁拿督賴慶安博士（Datuk Dr Lai Keng Onn）表示，此一做法有助於工業開發案降低對電網供電限制與中斷的曝險。

  「電網連接固然是基礎，但大型工業開發案與高耗能用地，在電力規劃上需要更全面的策略，尤其是確保電力充足且穩定，以支撐未來進駐業者的需求及長期營運，」賴慶安說。他補充，將發電端移近用電端，「有助於降低電網供電受限與中斷的風險，同時提供更高的電力可用性與持續供電保障。」

  Kinergy表示，此合作安排將使公司在電力生產與公用事業基礎設施領域的業務範疇，超越傳統電氣工程建設。

  ## Kinergy的電力專案經驗

  Kinergy曾擔任馬來西亞國家石油公司（Petroliam Nasional Bhd，PETRONAS）的本地技術合作夥伴，參與72百萬瓦（MW）的詩比丹（Sipitang）電廠案，並擔任120MW拉布安（Labuan）電廠的主要EPCC承包商。此外，公司亦已取得一座超大規模資料中心及大型運算設施的電氣工程合約，持續拓展在高耗能基礎設施領域的業務版圖。

  ## Matrix Concepts的工業不動產組合

  Matrix Concepts旗下的工業開發組合，包括位於森美蘭州（Negeri Sembilan）、佔地1,100英畝且已全數開發完成的仙德延科技谷（Sendayan TechValley），以及位於馬來西亞願景谷2.0（Malaysia Vision Valley 2.0）開發走廊內、目前仍在開發中的1,000英畝MVV科技谷（MVV TechValley）。

  Matrix Concepts也積極擴展工業不動產版圖。公司宣布計畫與Golog Holdings合作，在MVV科技谷打造一座規模達80億令吉的工業暨物流園區，同時提議在柔佛州古來（Kulai, Johor）推動一項規模21億令吉、以工業為主軸的綜合開發案。

  ## 市場反應

  Kinergy股價週一午盤收跌0.5分，跌幅1.35%，報36.5分，市值約為7億9,755萬令吉。Matrix Concepts股價同步下滑1分，跌幅1.11%，收89分，市值約為16億7,000萬令吉。

  ## 展望

  此次獨家合作安排，使Kinergy的電力業務定位從過去的單一電廠承包，擴展為與Matrix Concepts活躍工業土地開發計畫緊密結合的長期基礎設施夥伴。目前，這筆交易的財務與技術細節仍未對外披露，Kinergy表示，正式協議將於適當時機公告。
