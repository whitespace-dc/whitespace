body_en: |
  Arizona Public Service (APS) plans to convert two retired coal-fired units at its Cholla Power Plant in Navajo County, Arizona, to natural gas generation, adding up to 380 megawatts (MW) of capacity, the utility announced Thursday.

  The 380 MW target represents roughly 38% of Cholla's peak coal output, which APS said reached approximately 1 gigawatt (GW, or 1,000 MW). The plant's coal units were retired over the last decade under federal environmental mandates, with the final retirement occurring in March 2025.

  ## Why APS reversed course

  APS said federal regulations had long included an option to convert Cholla to natural gas, but earlier feasibility analyses found no economic benefit for customers. The utility said its position changed because of "Arizona's increasing demand for around-the-clock energy and the long timelines for adding new generation."

  The conversion will reuse the plant's existing transmission lines and site infrastructure, APS said — a factor the utility cited in making a project viable that was previously uneconomic.

  ## Demand driving the decision

  APS said last year it expects electric sales to grow 4% to 6% annually through 2027, driven primarily by large commercial and industrial customers being added to its system. The Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC), the state's utility regulator, said APS estimates peak demand from large customers alone will reach approximately 13.1 GW this year.

  ## Timeline and next steps

  Construction on the gas conversion is expected to begin in 2028, with a target in-service date of 2029, APS said. The project still requires formal permitting and planning processes, and APS said it will accept public comment through open houses and via phone and email.

  "Repurposing the Cholla Power Plant for natural gas allows us to build on that legacy," said Johnny Penrod, APS vice president of generation, referring to the plant's decades-long role serving the communities of Joseph City, Holbrook, and Navajo County.

  APS said the new gas facility will join a generation portfolio that includes nuclear power from the Palo Verde Generating Station, natural gas, coal, solar, wind, and battery energy storage.

  ## What it means

  APS framed the Cholla decision around two factors explicitly: accelerating load growth driven by large customers, and the difficulty of bringing entirely new generation online on the timelines that demand requires. By reusing existing transmission and site infrastructure, the utility said the conversion delivers customer value that earlier studies found lacking.

  The project's earliest possible in-service date of 2029 — three years from announcement — remains contingent on the outcome of permitting processes APS has not yet completed.

  ## APS擬將廢棄Cholla燃煤機組改建為380MW天然氣發電廠

  亞利桑那公共服務公司（APS）週四宣布，計畫將位於亞利桑那州納瓦霍郡Cholla發電廠的兩座已除役燃煤機組改建為天然氣發電設施，最多可新增380百萬瓦（MW）的發電容量。

  380 MW的目標容量約為Cholla燃煤高峰發電量的38%。APS表示，該廠燃煤機組的高峰輸出約為1百萬千瓦（GW，即1,000 MW）。過去十年間，Cholla各燃煤機組陸續依聯邦環保規定強制除役，最後一批機組於2025年3月停機退場。

  ## APS為何改變立場

  APS表示，聯邦法規長期以來本就包含將Cholla廠改燒天然氣的選項，但早期可行性評估認為對用戶並無經濟效益，因此未予推進。如今立場丕變，該公司指出，關鍵在於「亞利桑那州對全天候穩定供電的需求與日俱增，加上新建電廠所需的漫長時程」。

  此次改建計畫可沿用現有的輸電線路與廠區基礎設施，APS表示，這正是讓原本不具經濟效益的專案得以落地的重要因素。

  ## 需求成長是決策關鍵

  APS去年預估，到2027年電力銷售量每年將成長4%至6%，成長動能主要來自大型商業與工業用戶陸續加入其供電系統。亞利桑那州公用事業主管機關「亞利桑那企業委員會」（ACC）指出，APS估計光是大型用戶今年的尖峰用電需求就將達到約13.1 GW。

  ## 時程與後續步驟

  APS表示，天然氣改建工程預計於2028年動工，目標於2029年正式商轉。本專案仍須完成正式的許可申請與規劃程序，APS表示將透過公開說明會及電話、電子郵件等管道接受民眾意見。

  APS發電部門副總裁強尼・潘洛德（Johnny Penrod）在提及Cholla廠數十年來服務約瑟夫市（Joseph City）、霍爾布魯克（Holbrook）及納瓦霍郡地區居民的歷史時表示：「將Cholla發電廠改建為天然氣用途，讓我們得以延續這份傳承。」

  APS表示，新的天然氣發電設施將納入其多元化的發電組合，與帕洛維德發電站（Palo Verde Generating Station）的核能發電，以及天然氣、燃煤、太陽能、風力及電池儲能等能源並肩運作。

  ## 本案的意義

  APS在詮釋Cholla決策時，明確點出兩大關鍵因素：大型用戶帶動的負載快速成長，以及在需求所需的時程內從頭建設全新電廠所面臨的重重困難。透過沿用現有輸電線路與廠區基礎設施，該公司表示此次改建可為用戶創造早期研究未能發現的實質效益。

  本專案最快可能於2029年商轉，距今宣布尚需三年，且最終仍取決於APS尚未完成的許可申請程序結果。
