body_en: |
  NEXTDC, one of Australia's largest data centre operators, requested a trading halt on its shares on Oct. 19, pending a market update on a delay to power supply at its M3 data centre in Melbourne, saying the announcement would follow within two days.

  The company said its board had not yet met to assess the delay or its implications, and that the halt would allow it to meet continuous disclosure obligations while evaluating any potential effect on its fiscal year 2027 financial guidance.

  ## What analysts say is behind the delay

  Analysts at Citi said the delay likely relates to Jemena, an electricity distributor, pausing work on the power lines intended to deliver electricity to M3. Citi did not attribute that characterisation to NEXTDC directly.

  M3 represents approximately 70 megawatts (MW) of the 155 MW of incremental billing — new contracted capacity coming online during the year — that Citi had assumed for NEXTDC across the state of Victoria in fiscal 2027. Citi said it is likely the entire billing capacity for M3 is not affected, but the delay could put around A$25 million (approximately US$17 million) of NEXTDC's operating earnings for fiscal 2027 at risk.

  ## Financial guidance at stake

  In August, NEXTDC forecast underlying earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation — a standard measure of operating profitability for capital-intensive infrastructure businesses — for fiscal 2027 in a range of A$385 million to A$410 million (US$268 million to US$286 million). The company said the trading halt was sought specifically to allow it to assess whether and how the power delay affects that guidance.

  ## Why this matters

  For data centre operators, grid connection timelines are a primary constraint on revenue growth. A facility that is built but lacks utility power cannot bill customers for capacity. A delay affecting even a portion of a large campus like M3 — which at 70 MW represents a significant share of a planned 155 MW of new Victorian billing — can shift revenue from one fiscal year to the next and force guidance revisions. NEXTDC said it expects to make a formal market announcement within two days of the trading halt.

  澳洲最大資料中心業者之一NEXTDC於10月19日申請股票停牌，等待公告其墨爾本M3資料中心供電延誤的相關訊息，並表示正式公告將於兩日內發出。

  該公司表示，董事會尚未召開會議評估此次延誤及其影響。申請停牌的目的，是讓公司在評估潛在影響期間仍能履行持續揭露義務，以確認此事是否影響2027財年的財務指引。

  ## 分析師解讀：延誤背後的原因

  花旗銀行分析師表示，延誤原因可能與配電業者Jemena暫停為M3設施施作輸電線路工程有關。花旗並未將此說法直接歸咎於NEXTDC。

  M3約占70百萬瓦（MW）的容量，是花旗原先預估NEXTDC在維多利亞州2027財年新增計費容量155 MW——即年內上線的新簽約容量——中的重要組成。花旗表示，M3的全部計費容量未必全數受到影響，但此次延誤可能使NEXTDC 2027財年約2,500萬澳元（約1,700萬美元）的營業利益面臨風險。

  ## 財務指引面臨壓力

  NEXTDC於今年8月預測，2027財年未調整息稅折舊攤銷前利潤（EBITDA，資本密集型基礎設施業常用的營業獲利衡量指標）將落在3億8,500萬至4億1,000萬澳元（約2億6,800萬至2億8,600萬美元）之間。公司表示，此次申請停牌，正是為了評估供電延誤是否及如何影響上述財務指引。

  ## 為何此事至關重要

  對資料中心業者而言，電網接入時程是營收成長的首要制約因素。一座已完工但尚未接通市電的設施，無法向客戶收取容量費用。即使只是大型園區的部分容量遭遇延誤——M3的70 MW占維多利亞州新增計費容量155 MW的相當大比例——也可能導致營收遞延至下一財年，並迫使業者修正財務指引。NEXTDC表示，預計在申請停牌後兩日內正式發出市場公告。
