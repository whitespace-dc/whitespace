body_en: |
  Malaysia's state utility Tenaga Nasional Bhd (TNB) has committed to connecting approved data centres to the national electricity grid within one year, responding to what its chief distribution network officer described as "pressure" from the sector.

  Ahmad Sazree Abd Aziz made the pledge on Tuesday at the Singapore International Energy Week (SIEW) 2026, during a panel titled "Strengthening Electricity Security through the Asean Power Grid (APG)."

  ## The one-year commitment

  The timeline starts once a data centre project has received approval from a committee chaired by Malaysia's relevant ministry. "Once it's been approved by the ministry-chaired committee, we pledge to get data centres connected to the grid in one year's time," Ahmad Sazree said.

  ## Domestic network as the foundation for regional benefits

  Ahmad Sazree positioned a strong domestic transmission and distribution network as the prerequisite for Malaysia to fully benefit from the APG. "APG does help to strengthen the regional electricity security, but, at the same time, the real benefit from APG came about from how strong is the domestic network, in particular, transmission and distribution," he said.

  He identified three measures as central to preparing Malaysia's network for rising demand: demand forecasting, network capacity planning, and targeted infrastructure development.

  TNB also needs to modernise and digitalise its distribution networks to support renewable energy interconnections at the distribution level, Ahmad Sazree said. Integrated planning across demand and supply, coordinating forecasts, infrastructure schedules, and investment priorities across the transmission and distribution networks, was also identified as necessary to accommodate rising loads from data centres and renewable energy integration simultaneously.

  ## Significance

  Ahmad Sazree said TNB must keep pace with electricity demand rising from economic growth, residential and commercial consumption, and energy-intensive data centres, while maintaining network reliability and resilience. The one-year connection pledge provides data centre operators a defined grid-connection timeline anchored to the ministerial approval stage.

  He said aligning demand forecasts, infrastructure planning, and investment priorities would help ensure that network development strengthened the electricity system as a whole.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞國家電力公司（Tenaga Nasional Bhd，TNB）承諾，凡經核准的資料中心，將於一年內完成電網接入，此舉回應了其配電網路長所形容來自業界的「壓力」。

  TNB配電網路長Ahmad Sazree Abd Aziz於週二在2026年新加坡國際能源週（SIEW）上發表上述承諾，出席場合為一場題為「透過東協電力網（APG）強化電力安全」的小組討論。

  ## 一年併網承諾

  此時程自資料中心專案獲得由馬來西亞相關部會主持之委員會核准後起算。Ahmad Sazree表示：「一旦取得部會主持委員會的核准，我們承諾將在一年內完成資料中心的電網接入。」

  ## 強健國內電網是接軌區域效益的基礎

  Ahmad Sazree將健全的國內輸配電網路定位為馬來西亞充分受惠於東協電力網的前提條件。他說：「東協電力網確實有助於強化區域電力安全，但與此同時，真正從APG所獲得的效益，取決於國內電網的強健程度，尤其是輸電與配電網路。」

  他點出三項為馬來西亞電網備戰需求成長的核心措施：需求預測、電網容量規劃，以及有針對性的基礎設施建設。

  Ahmad Sazree也指出，TNB須將配電網路現代化與數位化，以支援配電層級的再生能源互聯。他同時強調，跨需求與供給的整合規劃——協調輸配電網路在需求預測、基礎設施時程與投資優先順序上的部署——是同步因應資料中心負載增長與再生能源整合所不可或缺的條件。

  ## 重要意義

  Ahmad Sazree表示，TNB必須在維持電網可靠性與韌性的同時，跟上來自經濟成長、住宅與商業用電，以及高耗能資料中心所共同帶動的電力需求增長。一年內完成接入的承諾，為資料中心業者提供了一個明確的電網接入時程，並以取得部會核准作為計時起點。

  他說，將需求預測、基礎設施規劃與投資優先順序相互對齊，將有助於確保電網建設整體強化整個電力系統的功能。
