  Petroliam Nasional Bhd's (Petronas) clean energy subsidiary Gentari Sdn Bhd has warned that Malaysia must plan electricity supply alongside data centre expansion rather than allow energy provision to catch up after the fact, as peak power demand in Peninsular Malaysia reached a record 21.47 gigawatts (GW) in April, surpassing last year's 21.05GW peak, with much of the increase attributed to data centre consumption.

  Shah Yang Razalli, Gentari's group chief operating officer and Malaysia country head, said digital and energy infrastructure can no longer be planned separately. "While land availability, digital connectivity and government incentives remain important, investors are increasingly looking for confidence that reliable, scalable and lower-carbon electricity will be available over the long term," he told StarBiz.

  ## Record demand and rising grid load

  The April peak figure was reported by TA Research, which attributed much of the rise to data centre load. Load utilisation reached 23% at end-March 2026, TA Research said, indicating that existing data centres are steadily increasing their electricity consumption even as new capacity continues to come online. The figures suggest electricity demand will continue rising as more approved data centre projects reach operation.

  ## Energy as a strategic factor in siting decisions

  AI-driven data centres have fundamentally different energy requirements from traditional industrial customers, Shah Yang said, requiring large volumes of reliable, round-the-clock power rather than intermittent or interruptible supply. Long-term energy availability and certainty are therefore becoming key factors in investment decisions, he said.

  "As competition for AI investments intensifies, electricity is no longer simply a utility but is becoming a strategic advantage," he told StarBiz. Countries that can provide reliable, scalable and increasingly lower-carbon power will be better placed to attract the next wave of digital and industrial investment, he said.

  The continued appetite for data centre development in Malaysia and the broader South-East Asia region partly reflects a structural shift: hyperscalers, the large operators that build and run global data centre networks at scale, are diversifying their infrastructure footprint amid geopolitical and supply chain uncertainties, with the region emerging as an increasingly attractive destination.

  Shah Yang stressed that Malaysia's data centre expansion must be pursued in a "safe, responsible and sustainable manner," and cautioned that countries treating energy as something that can simply catch up later risk undermining their digital ambitions. Energy infrastructure requires years of planning and investment, he said.

  ## Renewable supply and storage

  While renewable energy can be deployed relatively quickly, Shah Yang said Malaysia still requires a balanced energy mix. Battery energy storage systems (BESS) and flexible generation will be needed to maintain grid reliability throughout the energy transition, he said.

  Gentari is expanding its renewable energy portfolio in Malaysia through larger-scale projects, including a 150-megawatt (MW) large-scale solar installation in Kuala Muda, Kedah. The company has also partnered Gamuda Bhd's energy unit, Gamuda Energy, to develop up to 1.5GW of solar photovoltaic (PV) and battery energy storage capacity under Malaysia's Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme (CRESS), targeting hyperscale data centre customers. The 1.5GW target is ten times the scale of Gentari's Kedah solar project and is aimed directly at large-scale data centre power procurement.

  Globally, Gentari has more than 9.1GW of renewable energy and battery storage capacity either installed or under construction. The company also operates businesses in hydrogen and green mobility.

  ## Regional electricity trade

  Shah Yang said cross-border electricity trade could play an important role in strengthening Malaysia's energy security. He cited two initiatives under development: a proposed Vietnam-Malaysia-Singapore renewable electricity project and a separate Indonesia-Singapore cross-border renewable energy development, both of which would support greater regional collaboration on clean power.

  No single company can deliver the energy transition, Shah Yang said, with success depending on collaboration among customers, utilities, technology providers, developers, financiers and the government.

  ## What the data centre build-out means for the grid

  Malaysia's record April demand peak and rising load utilisation together indicate electricity consumption will continue to grow as more approved data centre capacity moves from approval to operation. Shah Yang's remarks signal that the gap between energy infrastructure planning timelines and the pace of data centre development is a recognised risk, with energy reliability now a competitive factor among countries vying for AI infrastructure investment.

  The Gentari and Gamuda Energy partnership targeting up to 1.5GW of solar and battery storage capacity under CRESS for hyperscale customers is among the larger clean-energy supply commitments disclosed for Malaysia's data centre sector to date.

  ## 馬來西亞資料中心急速擴張，國油旗下Gentari警告電網建設必須同步跟上

  國家石油公司（Petroliam Nasional Bhd，Petronas）旗下潔淨能源子公司Gentari Sdn Bhd發出警告：馬來西亞的電力供應規劃，必須與資料中心的擴張腳步同步推進，而非等到供電出現缺口後才急起直追。今年四月，馬來西亞半島的電力尖峰需求達到創紀錄的21.47吉瓦（GW），超越去年21.05GW的峰值，增幅主要來自資料中心的龐大用電需求。

  Gentari集團營運長暨馬來西亞區負責人Shah Yang Razalli表示，數位基礎設施與能源基礎設施已不能再分開規劃。他在接受《StarBiz》採訪時指出：「雖然土地取得、數位連結能力與政府優惠措施仍是重要條件，但投資人愈來愈在意的，是能否長期確保取得可靠、可擴展且低碳的電力。」

  ## 創紀錄的需求與攀升的電網負載

  四月份的尖峰需求數據由TA Research發布，該機構指出需求大幅攀升的主因正是資料中心的用電負載。TA Research的報告顯示，截至2026年3月底，現有連線的負載使用率已達23%，說明現有資料中心的用電量正持續增加，同時間新增產能仍不斷上線。這些數字顯示，隨著更多已核准的資料中心專案陸續完工營運，電力需求將持續走高。

  ## 能源成為選址的策略性關鍵因素

  Shah Yang表示，以人工智慧為核心的資料中心，其能源需求與傳統工業用戶截然不同——需要大量、全天候不間斷的穩定電力，而非間歇性或可中斷的供電方式。因此，長期能源的可用性與確定性，已成為投資決策的核心考量。

  他告訴《StarBiz》：「隨著爭奪AI投資的競爭日趨激烈，電力已不再只是一種公用事業，而是正在演變為一項策略優勢。」他說，能夠提供可靠、可擴展、且日趨低碳化電力的國家，將在吸引下一波數位與工業投資方面占據更有利的位置。

  馬來西亞乃至整個東南亞地區資料中心建設的持續熱潮，部分反映了一個結構性轉變：超大規模業者（hyperscaler）——即那些在全球大規模建設與營運資料中心網路的巨頭——正因地緣政治與供應鏈的不確定性而積極分散基礎設施布局，東南亞地區因此成為愈發具有吸引力的目的地。

  Shah Yang強調，馬來西亞的資料中心擴張必須以「安全、負責任且永續的方式」推進，並警告，若有國家把能源建設視為可以「事後補齊」的配套，將會損害其數位發展的長遠願景。能源基礎設施需要多年的規劃與投資，他如此說道。

  ## 再生能源供給與儲能

  Shah Yang表示，儘管再生能源的部署速度相對較快，但馬來西亞仍需要均衡的能源組合。他說，在能源轉型的過程中，電池儲能系統（Battery Energy Storage Systems，BESS）與具有彈性的調峰發電能力，對維持電網穩定性而言不可或缺。

  Gentari正透過更大規模的專案，在馬來西亞擴展其再生能源投資組合，包括位於吉打州瓜拉姆達（Kuala Muda）、裝置容量達150百萬瓦（MW）的大型太陽能電站。此外，Gentari已與建設公司Gamuda Bhd旗下的能源部門Gamuda Energy合作，在馬來西亞「企業再生能源供應計畫」（Corporate Renewable Energy Supply Scheme，CRESS）框架下，共同開發最多達1.5GW的太陽能光伏（PV）及電池儲能容量，鎖定超大規模資料中心客戶。這1.5GW的目標規模，是Gentari吉打州太陽能專案的十倍，直接針對大型資料中心的購電需求。

  在全球布局方面，Gentari目前已安裝或正在興建中的再生能源與電池儲能容量合計超過9.1GW。該公司亦在氫能與綠色移動業務領域積極布局。

  ## 跨境電力貿易

  Shah Yang表示，跨境電力交易在強化馬來西亞能源安全方面可扮演重要角色。他提及兩項正在推動的計畫：一是越南—馬來西亞—新加坡跨境再生電力輸送計畫，另一是印尼—新加坡跨境再生能源開發計畫，兩者均有助於推動區域潔淨電力的合作。

  Shah Yang表示，能源轉型不是任何一家公司能單獨完成的使命，成功有賴於客戶、公用事業者、技術供應商、開發商、金融機構與政府之間的通力合作。

  ## 資料中心擴建對電網的深遠影響

  馬來西亞今年四月的電力尖峰需求創歷史新高，加上負載使用率持續攀升，共同顯示隨著更多已核准的資料中心產能從核准走向實際營運，電力消耗量將持續增長。Shah Yang的發言清楚傳達出一個訊息：能源基礎設施的規劃時程與資料中心的擴張速度之間存在落差，這是一項已被業界正視的風險，而能源供電的穩定性，如今已成為各國競逐AI基礎設施投資時的重要競爭籌碼。

  Gentari與Gamuda Energy在CRESS框架下合作、鎖定超大規模資料中心客戶、目標裝置容量最多達1.5GW太陽能及電池儲能的合作案，是迄今為止馬來西亞資料中心領域中，規模最大的潔淨能源供應承諾之一。
