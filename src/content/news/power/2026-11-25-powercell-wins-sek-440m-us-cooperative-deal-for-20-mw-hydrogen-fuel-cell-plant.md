body_en: |
  Swedish fuel cell developer PowerCell Group has signed a SEK 440 million (roughly $40 million at current exchange rates) contract with a US electric cooperative to supply and integrate a utility-scale hydrogen fuel cell installation in the southern United States, the company said. The system will deliver 20 MW of rated generating capacity.

  The order was placed through PowerCell's US subsidiary, PowerCell Inc, and is based on the company's containerized PowerPod 3000 platform. The installation will incorporate 27 MW of installed fuel cell capacity — a figure higher than the 20 MW rated output because the system is sized to balance load conditions while consistently delivering the stated power level to the grid.

  ## How the system works

  The project is designed to integrate with the cooperative's existing renewable energy infrastructure. Surplus electricity generated by solar panels during peak production periods will be used to produce green hydrogen — hydrogen made without fossil fuels — through electrolysis. That hydrogen will be stored on site and later fed into proton exchange membrane (PEM) fuel cells, which generate electricity through an electrochemical reaction rather than combustion, when solar output falls short of demand.

  Fuel cells produce water as a byproduct of the hydrogen-to-electricity conversion. PowerCell said the water could potentially be recovered and reused within the broader system, though it noted that the actual volume recovered and its end use would depend on the final design, operating conditions, and any water treatment requirements. The company said this dual output — electricity and potentially recoverable water — could be relevant in parts of the southern United States where water supply is a planning consideration for energy projects.

  The PowerPod 3000 platform bundles the fuel cell stacks with power conversion equipment, cooling infrastructure, digital monitoring and control systems, and energy orchestration software in a modular configuration. The parties intend to enter into a dedicated service-level agreement covering ongoing operations before the site is commissioned.

  ## Delivery timeline and customer

  PowerCell expects to deliver the system within 18 to 24 months of the order signing. The cooperative has not been named. The company described it as an established electric cooperative, a structure common in rural and semi-rural US markets where member-owned utilities serve areas not covered by investor-owned utilities. PowerCell said such cooperatives have been exploring alternative storage and distributed generation technologies as they add renewable generation capacity and seek more flexible tools for balancing supply and demand.

  ## Strategic significance for PowerCell

  For PowerCell, the deal is the latest step in building stationary power generation into a second major business segment alongside its marine division. Chief Executive Richard Berkling said the order demonstrated the company's ability to operate at utility scale.

  "For PowerCell, this order represents an important step in establishing Power Generation as our second major segment alongside Marine. It demonstrates how our industrialized PEM technology, system integration and Energy Orchestration capabilities can create value at utility scale," Berkling said in a statement.

  A 20 MW hydrogen-to-power project is a significant scale for this category of technology, which has more commonly been deployed in smaller pilot or backup-power configurations. The contract value of SEK 440 million reflects that shift toward larger grid-linked installations.

  The order adds to PowerCell's US portfolio as the company seeks to extend its fuel cell business beyond transport and marine applications. The installation will begin the delivery process within the next 18 to 24 months, with a service agreement to follow before the system enters commercial operation.
body_zh: |
  瑞典燃料電池開發商PowerCell Group宣布，與一家美國電力合作社簽署價值4.4億瑞典克朗（按目前匯率換算約4,000萬美元）的合約，將在美國南部提供並整合一套公用事業規模的氫燃料電池系統，總額定發電容量為20MW。

  此訂單由PowerCell旗下美國子公司PowerCell Inc承接，採用該公司的貨櫃化PowerPod 3000平台。整體安裝將配備27MW的燃料電池裝機容量——高於20MW的額定輸出，原因在於系統設計須平衡負載條件，同時持續穩定地向電網輸送既定電力。

  ## 系統運作方式

  本專案設計上將與該合作社現有的再生能源基礎設施整合。太陽能板在發電高峰期產生的多餘電力，將透過電解程序用來製造綠氫——即不使用化石燃料製成的氫氣。這些氫氣將儲存於現場，並在太陽能發電量不足以因應需求時，送入質子交換膜（PEM）燃料電池，透過電化學反應（而非燃燒）發電。

  燃料電池在氫轉電的過程中，水是其副產品。PowerCell表示，這些水有機會回收並在整體系統中再利用，但同時指出實際可回收量及其用途，將取決於最終設計、運行條件及水處理需求。該公司表示，電力與潛在可回收水這雙重產出，在美國南部部分地區具有特別意義——當地水資源供應向來是能源專案規劃的考量因素。

  PowerPod 3000平台採模組化配置，將燃料電池堆疊與電力轉換設備、冷卻基礎設施、數位監控與控制系統，以及能源調度軟體整合於一體。雙方計畫在系統正式投入運行前，另行簽訂一份涵蓋後續營運的服務水準協議。

  ## 交付時程與客戶

  PowerCell預計在簽約後18至24個月內完成系統交付。該合作社目前未對外公開名稱。PowerCell將其描述為一家具有一定規模的電力合作社——此類組織架構在美國農村與半農村市場十分普遍，由會員共同持有，服務範圍涵蓋投資者所有的電力公司所未覆蓋的地區。PowerCell表示，隨著再生能源裝機容量持續增加，此類合作社一直在積極探索替代儲能與分散式發電技術，以尋求更靈活的供需平衡工具。

  ## 對PowerCell的策略意義

  對PowerCell而言，這筆合約是將固定式發電業務建立為第二大事業部門——與船舶部門並駕齊驅——的最新進展。執行長Richard Berkling表示，此訂單充分展現了公司在公用事業規模上的執行能力。

  Berkling在聲明中表示：「對PowerCell而言，這筆訂單代表我們在將發電業務建立為繼船舶之後第二大事業部門方面，邁出了重要的一步。它展示了我們的工業化PEM技術、系統整合能力與能源調度功能，如何在公用事業規模上創造價值。」

  在此類技術領域中，20MW的氫能發電專案規模相當可觀——過去此類技術多以較小型的試驗性或備用電源配置部署。4.4億瑞典克朗的合約金額，正反映出業界正逐步轉向規模更大的電網連結型安裝。

  這筆訂單進一步充實了PowerCell的美國業務版圖，公司正積極將燃料電池業務拓展至運輸與船舶應用以外的領域。系統將在未來18至24個月內啟動交付程序，服務協議則將在系統進入商業運轉前完成簽署。
