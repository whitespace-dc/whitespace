body_en: |
  Cloudleap Energy, a Taiwan-based low-carbon energy integration group, approved a NT$2-per-share cash dividend and ratified its fiscal year 2025 business report and financial statements at its annual shareholder meeting on Tuesday, while announcing the formation of a new AI data center unit as it moves to capture power demand driven by artificial intelligence infrastructure.

  ## New AI Data Center Subsidiary

  The company said it established a unit called Cloudleap Supercomputing this year to enter the AI data center (AIDC) market, integrating the group's energy and computing resources into AI infrastructure supply chains. The subsidiary is expected to begin contributing revenue in Taiwan this year, with operating scale projected to double in the following year, the company said. Cloudleap targets the AIDC business reaching a double-digit share of group revenue by 2030.

  General Manager Chao Shu-min said demand for stable, low-carbon, and resilient power continues to increase alongside rapid growth in high-performance computing, data centers, and advanced manufacturing. "AI brings not just a computing revolution, but a power revolution," Chao said.

  ## Subsidiary Listings and Storage Milestones

  Two Cloudleap subsidiaries reached capital market milestones in May. Tianneng Green Power, the group's power retail arm, completed a listing on Taiwan's over-the-counter securities market. The subsidiary reported accumulated green power transfer contracts exceeding 34.1 billion kilowatt-hours — a measure of the total electricity volume committed for delivery under existing agreements — which the company described as the leading position among private power retailers in Taiwan's domestic market.

  Tapwei Energy, the group's system-level energy storage subsidiary, registered on Taiwan's emerging stock market in the same month. Tapwei reported cumulative installed energy storage capacity of more than 412 megawatts (MW) of power output and 1,055 megawatt-hours (MWh) of total stored energy across domestic and international projects. At 412 MW, the portfolio is roughly comparable in discharge capacity to a mid-sized conventional power plant. Tapwei is already operating in Japan and said it formally entered the Australian market to expand its overseas footprint.

  ## Strategic Direction

  Cloudleap said it will continue to build out green power trading, energy storage, energy management, and overseas market operations, describing AI energy infrastructure, international expansion, and new business units as the group's next growth engines. The company said its aim is to raise overall operating scale and shareholder value by combining its existing advantages across those segments.

  The most concrete near-term milestone the company identified is the AIDC unit beginning Taiwan revenue contributions within the current fiscal year, with an international scale-up targeted for 2025 through 2030.

  ## 雲躍能源股東會通過每股2元現金股利　宣布成立AI資料中心子公司

  台灣低碳能源整合集團雲躍能源於週二召開股東常會，通過每股新台幣2元現金股利，並核准2025會計年度營業報告書及財務報表，同時宣布成立全新AI資料中心事業單位，積極搶進人工智慧基礎建設所帶動的龐大電力需求商機。

  ## 成立AI資料中心子公司

  雲躍能源表示，集團今年已設立「雲躍超算」子公司，正式跨入AI資料中心（AIDC）市場，整合集團旗下能源與算力資源，打入AI基礎建設供應鏈。該子公司預計今年內即可在台灣開始貢獻營收，並於次年將營運規模翻倍擴大。雲躍能源設定，AI資料中心業務於2030年前佔集團整體營收比重達到兩位數的目標。

  總經理趙淑敏表示，隨著高效能運算、資料中心及先進製造快速發展，對穩定、低碳且具韌性的電力需求持續攀升。她指出：「AI帶來的不僅是算力革命，更是一場電力革命。」

  ## 子公司掛牌及儲能里程碑

  集團旗下兩家子公司於5月分別達成資本市場重要里程碑。負責電力零售業務的天能綠電，完成在台灣證券櫃檯買賣中心（OTC市場）掛牌上市。該子公司累計綠電轉供合約電量已突破341億度（千瓦時），此數字代表現有合約中承諾交付之電力總量，雲躍能源表示，這在台灣本土市場的民營電力零售業者中居於領先地位。

  集團系統級儲能子公司台威能源，同月登錄台灣證券交易所興櫃市場。台威能源在國內外各項目中，累計已建置完成的儲能系統裝置容量超過412百萬瓦（MW）的電力輸出能力，以及1,055百萬瓦時（MWh）的總儲電量。以412 MW的規模而言，其放電能力大致相當於一座中型傳統電廠。台威能源目前已在日本展開業務，並宣布正式進軍澳洲市場，持續拓展海外版圖。

  ## 策略發展方向

  雲躍能源表示，集團將持續深耕綠電交易、儲能、能源管理及海外市場等業務，並將AI能源基礎建設、國際擴張與新興事業單位定位為集團下一階段的成長引擎。公司表示，目標是透過整合現有各業務板塊的既有優勢，全面提升集團整體營運規模與股東價值。

  公司目前最明確的近期里程碑，是AI資料中心事業單位在本會計年度內於台灣開始貢獻營收，並預計在2025年至2030年間逐步推動國際規模擴張。
