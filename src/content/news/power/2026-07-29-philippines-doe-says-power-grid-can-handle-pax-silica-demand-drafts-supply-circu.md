body_en: |
  The Philippine Department of Energy (DoE) has said it expects adequate power supply to meet the electricity needs of Pax Silica technology hub projects, and is preparing a formal circular to set out how that supply will be structured, Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin announced at a briefing.

  The DoE's assessment comes as the US-led Pax Silica initiative — which aims to build technology supply chains and counter China's growing manufacturing position — is proposing a major industrial and innovation district in the Philippines. The Philippine component spans 1,620 hectares and carries an initial investment target of $10 billion, covering semiconductor design, fabrication and advanced packaging, artificial intelligence computing, critical mineral processing, energy and data infrastructure, research and development, logistics, housing, and commercial facilities within the Luzon Economic Corridor.

  ## Grid capacity and demand planning

  Garin said the power system can accommodate up to 5,000 megawatts (MW) of additional electricity demand — a threshold that would cover projected Pax Silica requirements. For context, 5,000 MW is roughly the output of five large-scale power plants and represents substantial headroom beyond routine industrial load.

  Energy Undersecretary Rowena Cristina L. Guevara said the DoE is working to build precise demand projections tied to locator timelines. "If we [could get a] timeline for the additional capacity needed per year then we can also project the needed transmission, distribution, and generation and put that into the Philippine energy plan," Guevara said. She added that the department is confident it can secure generators to supply electricity to Pax Silica.

  ## 20-year commitment requirement

  The DoE is conditioning infrastructure planning on long-term locator commitments. Guevara said 20-year agreements are necessary to allow project developers to recover the cost of building generation and grid facilities. Without those commitments, the economic case for dedicated new capacity does not close.

  ## Consumer protection constraint

  Garin set an explicit boundary on how power arrangements for Pax Silica can be structured. "We just need to make sure that anything we do to power a data center or hub will not put the rest of the country at a disadvantage. Consumers have protections in place," she said. The forthcoming circular is intended to define supply options within those constraints.

  ## Significance

  The DoE's position resolves a key uncertainty that had surrounded Pax Silica's Philippine component: whether the grid could absorb the power-intensive load of large-scale semiconductor and artificial intelligence computing facilities. By affirming a 5,000-MW ceiling and committing to a policy circular, the DoE is establishing the regulatory and capacity framework investors will need before finalizing site and infrastructure decisions. The next concrete step is publication of that circular, which will determine the specific mechanisms — whether dedicated generation, grid offtake agreements, or other structures — through which Pax Silica locators can secure electricity supply.

  菲律賓能源部（DoE）表示，現有電力供應足以滿足Pax Silica科技樞紐各項專案的用電需求，並正準備發布一份正式通告，以明確規範供電架構。能源部長Sharon S. Garin在一場簡報會上宣布了上述消息。

  能源部此次評估，正值由美國主導的Pax Silica倡議積極推進之際——該倡議旨在建構科技供應鏈，並抗衡中國日益擴張的製造地位。Pax Silica計劃在菲律賓設立一處大型產業與創新特區，菲律賓方面的規劃範圍達1,620公頃，初期投資目標為100億美元，涵蓋半導體設計、晶圓製造與先進封裝、人工智慧運算、關鍵礦物加工、能源與資料基礎設施、研究開發、物流、住宅及商業設施，全部納入呂宋經濟走廊的整體框架之中。

  ## 電網容量與需求規劃

  Garin表示，現有電力系統可再承載最多5,000百萬瓦（MW）的額外用電需求，此一上限足以涵蓋Pax Silica的預估用電量。作為參考，5,000 MW大約相當於五座大型發電廠的裝置容量，遠超一般工業用電的需求水準，仍有相當充裕的餘裕空間。

  能源部次長Rowena Cristina L. Guevara表示，能源部正致力於依照進駐業者的建設時程，建立精確的需求預測。她說：「如果我們能取得每年所需額外容量的時間表，就能進一步推算出輸電、配電與發電的所需規模，並將其納入菲律賓能源計畫之中。」她並補充，能源部有信心能為Pax Silica確保充足的發電供應來源。

  ## 20年承諾要求

  能源部將基礎設施規劃與長期進駐承諾相互掛鉤。Guevara指出，20年協議是讓專案開發商得以回收發電及電網設施建設成本的必要條件。若缺乏這類長期承諾，興建專屬新供電設施在經濟上將難以成立。

  ## 消費者保護限制

  Garin為Pax Silica的供電安排設下了明確界線。她表示：「我們必須確保，任何為資料中心或科技樞紐所做的供電安排，都不能讓菲律賓其他地區的民眾處於不利地位。消費者的保護機制必須維持。」即將發布的通告，將在這些限制條件下，明確界定可行的供電選項。

  ## 重要意義

  能源部的立場，化解了外界對Pax Silica菲律賓方案的一項關鍵疑慮：現有電網能否承受大規模半導體廠房與人工智慧運算設施所帶來的高密度用電需求。透過確認5,000 MW的容量上限，並承諾發布相關政策通告，能源部正在為投資者確定廠址及基礎設施決策之前，奠定所需的法規與容量框架。下一個具體步驟，便是正式發布上述通告——通告內容將決定Pax Silica進駐業者確保電力供應的具體機制，包括專屬發電、電網購電協議或其他供電結構。
