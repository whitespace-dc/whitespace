body_en: |
  The U.S. Department of Energy ordered PJM Interconnection, the country's largest electric grid operator, to maximize fossil fuel generation output as a heat wave drove demand toward a 20-year record, with PJM forecasting Thursday's peak at 166.3 gigawatts (GW), above the 2006 summer high of 165.5 GW.

  Demand on the PJM grid topped 160 GW on Wednesday afternoon. Spot wholesale electricity prices in PJM's Virginia zone, home to the world's largest concentration of data centers, surged to more than $600 per megawatt hour (MWh), up from roughly $40/MWh earlier in the day before temperatures climbed toward 37.8 degrees Celsius. Prices were expected to exceed $1,000/MWh on Wednesday evening as operators managed congested transmission lines around Virginia's data center hub.

  PJM serves 67 million people across the Mid-Atlantic, South, and Washington, D.C. areas.

  ## Federal emergency order

  Energy Secretary Chris Wright issued the emergency order citing "the emergency caused by the expected load stress," writing that "additional dispatch of the Specified Resources is necessary to best meet the emergency and serve the public interest." The specified resources are all fossil fuel plants, including many dual natural gas and fuel oil plants. Wright acknowledged the additional generation "may result in conflict with environmental standards and requirements" but authorized it under conditions in the order.

  The order also approved PJM's request to direct transmission owners to curtail data centers and other large loads as a last resort before implementing voltage reduction or load shedding — deliberate, rolling power cuts used to prevent a wider grid collapse. The DOE order noted "tens of gigawatts of readily available backup generation that have remained largely untapped," particularly at data center facilities, including hyperscaler sites.

  ## Generation and reserve response

  Coal plant generation this week produced up to 22% of PJM's electricity supply, with output approaching 28 GW, approximately 75% higher than the 2026 average to date, according to PJM generation data. PJM reported 18 GW of reserve power resources available within 30 minutes to cover unexpected shortfalls.

  PJM on June 25 issued a recall requiring all generators on maintenance outages to return to service to increase available capacity. On Wednesday afternoon, the grid operator issued an additional alert asking power plants to maximize output and assess whether any offline units could be restarted.

  PJM also issued a low-voltage alert, signaling elevated risk of rotating outages as voltage levels on transmission lines weakened. Rotating outages are brief, targeted power cuts used to stabilize the grid before a broader failure occurs.

  ## Price spikes and congestion

  Georg Rute, chief executive officer of grid analytics firm Gridraven, said extreme heat, low wind, and surging demand are coinciding at a time when transmission lines carry the least margin for safety, contributing to price spikes as the cost of moving power rises with network congestion.

  The Virginia zone is particularly exposed because of its dense concentration of hyperscale data center campuses, which have driven sustained load growth across PJM's northern Virginia footprint.

  ## Regional grid strain

  The heat wave strained grids beyond PJM. The Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO), the regional grid operator for 15 U.S. states in the Midwest and South, issued a conservative operations declaration on Tuesday, and forecasts indicated its demand record of 127.1 GW could fall as early as Wednesday evening.

  The New York Independent System Operator (NYISO), which operates the New York state grid, projected a Thursday peak of 32,410 megawatts (MW) and asked customers to moderate air conditioning and major appliance use. NYISO's all-time record stands at 34 GW, set in 2013.

  ISO New England projected a Thursday peak of 25,850 MW arriving around 7 p.m. and said it was "expecting exceptionally tight operating conditions on the regional electric grid" at that time.

  Con Edison, the utility serving New York City, asked customers to limit use of multiple air conditioners and large appliances between 2 and 10 p.m. AccuWeather reported 167,000 outages across the U.S. on Tuesday night, and AccuWeather Senior Meteorologist Alan Reppert said "some companies could see possible power cuts to stabilize the grid if it becomes unstable."

  The National Weather Service forecast "dangerous, record-breaking heat" continuing across most of the central and eastern U.S. through Friday, with peak heat indices up to 115 degrees Fahrenheit possible and numerous daily temperature records at risk.

  ## Structural pressures

  Even before this week's heat event, PJM had been straining to overhaul a system pushed by surging energy consumption from data centers and electric vehicles. The mounting strain across multiple U.S. grids reflects how extreme weather, electrification, and rapid data center growth are converging faster than transmission infrastructure upgrades can keep pace.

  PJM and other operators have urged faster permitting for new transmission lines, more flexible market rules, and expanded reserve capacity to prevent future bottlenecks. Regulators and grid planners have warned that this summer's conditions are a preview of deeper structural challenges ahead as electrification accelerates and data center clusters multiply faster than new transmission can be built.

  Energy analysts said the coming weeks will serve as a critical stress test for operators already managing aging lines, volatile generation patterns, and unprecedented peak loads.

  ## PJM電網需求逼近20年新高，熱浪引發聯邦緊急命令

  美國能源部緊急下令全美最大電網營運商PJM Interconnection全力開動化石燃料發電機組，因熱浪驟至，電力需求正逼近20年來最高紀錄。PJM預估，週四用電尖峰將達166.3百萬瓩（GW），超越2006年夏季創下的165.5 GW歷史高點。

  週三下午，PJM電網用電量已突破160 GW。在全球資料中心密度最高的維吉尼亞州，PJM維吉尼亞區域的即時批發電價飆升至每百萬瓦時（MWh）逾600美元，而當天稍早氣溫尚未攀升至攝氏37.8度前，電價僅約40美元／MWh。由於維吉尼亞資料中心核心區周邊輸電線路壅塞，週三傍晚電價預計將進一步突破每MWh 1,000美元。

  PJM電網服務涵蓋大西洋中岸、南部及華盛頓特區一帶，共計6,700萬名用戶。

  ## 聯邦緊急命令

  能源部長克里斯·萊特（Chris Wright）以「預期負載壓力所造成的緊急情況」為由發布緊急命令，並表示「額外調度指定資源，是因應此緊急情況、維護公共利益的必要措施。」所謂「指定資源」，係指所有化石燃料發電廠，其中包括許多天然氣與燃油雙燃料機組。萊特承認，追加發電「可能與環境標準及相關規定產生衝突」，但依命令所列條件授權執行。

  該命令亦批准PJM的請求，授權在採取降壓措施或輪流限電——即為防止電網全面崩潰而進行有計畫性、輪流式的強制斷電——等手段之前，先指示輸電業者對資料中心及其他大型用電戶實施用電削減，作為最後手段。能源部命令中特別提及，資料中心設施——尤其是超大規模業者（hyperscaler）的園區——擁有「數十GW隨時可用的備用發電能力，但迄今大多閒置未用」。

  ## 發電與備用容量應對

  本週煤電發電量最高曾占PJM總電力供應的22%，輸出接近28 GW，較2026年迄今的平均值高出約75%（據PJM發電數據）。PJM表示，目前備有18 GW的備用電力資源，可在30分鐘內調度，以因應突發缺電。

  6月25日，PJM已發出召回令，要求所有正在進行計畫性停機維護的發電機組立即恢復運轉，以提升可用容量。週三下午，電網營運商進一步發出警示，要求所有電廠全力運轉，並評估是否有任何離線機組能夠重新啟動。

  PJM同時發布低電壓警示，示警輸電線路電壓下滑、輪流限電風險升高。輪流限電係指短暫、定點式的強制斷電，用以在電網發生更大規模故障前穩定電力系統。

  ## 電價飆升與輸電壅塞

  電網分析公司Gridraven執行長喬治·魯特（Georg Rute）表示，極端高溫、風力不足與需求急升三者同時交疊，而此時輸電線路的安全餘裕也降至最低，隨著電網壅塞導致電力傳輸成本上升，電價因此大幅飆漲。

  維吉尼亞區域的用電負荷特別脆弱，原因在於當地密集聚集了超大規模資料中心園區，多年來持續推升PJM北維吉尼亞足跡範圍內的整體用電成長。

  ## 區域電網普遍吃緊

  這波熱浪的衝擊不只限於PJM。負責美國中西部及南部15州電力調度的中陸獨立系統運營商（MISO），週二即發布保守運轉聲明，且預報指出，其127.1 GW的需求歷史紀錄，最快可能在週三晚間就告破。

  負責紐約州電網的紐約獨立系統運營商（NYISO），預估週四尖峰需求為32,410百萬瓦（MW），並呼籲民眾節約使用冷氣及大型家電。NYISO的歷史最高紀錄為34 GW，創下於2013年。

  新英格蘭獨立系統運營商（ISO New England）預估週四尖峰約在晚間7時出現，峰值為25,850 MW，並表示預期屆時「區域電網將面臨極度緊繃的運轉條件」。

  服務紐約市的公用事業公司Con Edison，呼籲用戶在下午2時至晚間10時之間，盡量減少同時使用多台冷氣及大型家電。AccuWeather統計，週二夜間全美已有167,000戶停電；AccuWeather資深氣象師艾倫·雷普特（Alan Reppert）表示，「若電網趨於不穩，部分企業可能面臨電力削減以穩定電網的情況。」

  美國國家氣象局預報，「危險的破紀錄高溫」將持續籠罩美國中部及東部大多數地區，直至週五，最高熱指數可能達到攝氏約46度（華氏115度），多個地區的單日氣溫紀錄面臨挑戰。

  ## 結構性壓力

  早在本週熱浪事件發生之前，PJM就已在努力應對資料中心與電動車快速成長所帶來的用電激增壓力，並亟需對整個電力系統進行大規模升級改造。眼下多個美國電網同時告急，正是極端天氣、電氣化浪潮與資料中心爆炸性成長三股力量匯聚之下的縮影，其速度遠超輸電基礎設施更新改建的腳步。

  PJM與其他電網運營商已多次呼籲加快新建輸電線路的許可審批程序、制定更具彈性的市場規則，並擴大備用容量，以防止日後再度出現供電瓶頸。監管機關與電網規劃人員也已警告，今夏的情況不過是更深層結構性挑戰的預演——隨著電氣化持續加速，資料中心群的擴張速度一再超前於新輸電線路的建設進度。

  能源分析師指出，未來數週將是對各電網運營商的重大壓力測試，他們面對的是老化的輸電線路、波動劇烈的發電模式，以及前所未有的尖峰負載。
