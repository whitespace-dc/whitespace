body_en: |
  PT Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk. (PGEO) confirmed that PT Barito Renewables Energy Tbk. (BREN) operates as a partner under a Joint Operating Contract (JOC) arrangement, with PGEO collecting royalties from BREN's management of its geothermal working areas, as the company pursues a target of 1 gigawatt (GW) of installed generating capacity.

  PGEO Finance Director Fransetya Hasudungan Hutabarat made the clarification at the Surabaya Investor Meeting and Connectivity 2026 on August 10, responding to investor questions about the two companies' positions in the geothermal business. "BREN is PGEO's partner in the form of a JOC, where the company receives royalties from BREN's management of PGEO's working area," Fransetya said, as quoted in a company disclosure filing.

  ## Path to 1 GW

  PGEO is developing several priority projects to reach 1 GW of installed capacity. These include a cogeneration project, a method of jointly producing heat and electricity from the same geothermal resource, which the company said can add output at lower capital expenditure and lower risk than conventional greenfield development. The Hululais Unit 1 and Unit 2 projects are also among the priority developments.

  Beyond organic growth, PGEO said it is open to acquisitions as an inorganic route to accelerate the capacity target, contingent on identifying suitable, quality assets.

  PGEO also sees room to increase utilization of its existing working areas, saying utilization could reach 100% if demand from PLN, the state electricity utility and PGEO's sole power buyer under current regulations, increases.

  ## Operations and pricing

  PGEO's plants currently achieve a capacity factor of up to 90%, meaning they sustain output at close to that share of their maximum rated output on an ongoing basis. The company described geothermal as a baseload energy source, with output that is relatively stable compared with variable sources such as solar power, which depends on sunlight availability.

  The average selling price of PGEO's electricity and steam is approximately US$0.082 per kilowatt-hour (kWh). Most current projects carry pricing set under agreements signed before Presidential Decree No. 112 of 2022, known as Perpres 112/2022, the regulation governing geothermal power pricing, because the relevant power purchase agreements (PPAs) were executed before that decree took effect.

  PGEO is also running a production excellence program aimed at optimizing steam and electricity output from existing fields.

  ## Regulatory advocacy

  PGEO is lobbying the Indonesian government to revise Perpres 112/2022. The company said a regulatory revision could improve the economics of geothermal development and potentially serve as a catalyst for higher profitability for PGEO and other geothermal developers.

  ## Emerging revenue streams

  Through June 2026, PGEO recorded US$42,000 in carbon credit transactions, marking an initial entry into carbon trading as a supplementary line alongside electricity and steam sales.

  The company is also evaluating green hydrogen development, though that work remains at the pilot project and economic feasibility study stage. PGEO said it anticipates future growth in demand for green hydrogen and green ammonia, with potential applications across sectors including vehicle fuel, refining, and terminals.

  ## Investor and regulatory outlook

  The PGEO disclosure draws a clear distinction between an ownership stake and a contractual operating arrangement, a difference material to investors assessing each company's exposure to Indonesia's geothermal sector. PGEO's 1 GW target combines organic project development, potential acquisitions, and higher PLN offtake. The outcome of the Perpres 112/2022 revision process will determine whether improved pricing terms apply to future capacity additions beyond PGEO's existing pre-regulation PPAs.
body_zh: |
  PT Pertamina Geothermal Energy Tbk.（PGEO）正式確認，PT Barito Renewables Energy Tbk.（BREN）以聯合營運合約（Joint Operating Contract，JOC）的形式成為其合作夥伴——BREN負責管理PGEO的地熱工作區域，並向PGEO支付權利金。與此同時，PGEO正積極朝裝置發電容量達1吉瓦（GW）的目標邁進。

  PGEO財務長Fransetya Hasudungan Hutabarat於8月10日在「泗水投資人會議暨連結2026」（Surabaya Investor Meeting and Connectivity 2026）上就此作出說明，回應投資人對兩家公司在地熱業務中各自定位的提問。「BREN是PGEO以JOC形式合作的夥伴，本公司從BREN管理PGEO工作區域的業務中收取權利金，」Fransetya於公司揭露申報文件中如此表示。

  ## 通往1 GW之路

  PGEO正推進數項優先專案，以達成1 GW裝置容量的目標。其中包括一項汽電共生（cogeneration）專案——此一方式能從同一地熱資源同時產生熱能與電力，公司表示，相較於傳統的全新建置（greenfield development），汽電共生可在較低資本支出與較低風險的條件下增加產出。Hululais一號機組與二號機組亦同屬優先開發項目之列。

  除有機成長外，PGEO表示也開放以收購作為加速達標的非有機途徑，惟須以找到合適且優質的資產為前提。

  PGEO同時認為現有工作區域的利用率仍有提升空間，並指出，若現行法規下作為唯一購電方的國有電力公司PLN需求增加，利用率最高可達100%。

  ## 運營與電價

  PGEO的電廠目前容量因子高達90%，意即在持續運轉的情況下，實際輸出約維持在額定最大容量的九成左右。公司將地熱能定位為基載能源（baseload energy），其輸出相較於仰賴日照的太陽能等間歇性能源，具有明顯較高的穩定性。

  PGEO電力與蒸汽的平均售價約為每度電（kWh）0.082美元。目前大多數現行專案的定價，均依據2022年第112號總統令（Perpres 112/2022）正式生效前所簽訂的協議而定——相關購電協議（PPA）均在該總統令頒布之前即已執行完畢，Perpres 112/2022為印尼規範地熱發電定價的法規。

  PGEO同時也在執行一項卓越生產計畫，目標是優化現有地熱田的蒸汽與電力產出。

  ## 法規倡議

  PGEO正積極遊說印尼政府修訂Perpres 112/2022。公司表示，法規修訂有望改善地熱開發的整體經濟效益，並有潛力成為提升PGEO及其他地熱開發商獲利能力的催化劑。

  ## 新興收益來源

  截至2026年6月，PGEO已累計記錄4.2萬美元的碳權交易，標誌著公司正式踏入碳交易領域，作為電力與蒸汽銷售以外的輔助收益來源。

  公司也在評估綠氫開發的可行性，惟相關工作目前仍處於試驗專案與經濟可行性研究階段。PGEO表示，預期未來綠氫與綠氨的需求將持續成長，潛在應用範疇涵蓋車輛燃料、煉製及儲運碼頭等多個領域。

  ## 投資人與法規展望

  PGEO此次揭露明確區分了持股權益與合約營運安排之間的差異——這對於評估兩家公司在印尼地熱產業曝險程度的投資人而言，是一項至關重要的區別。PGEO的1 GW目標融合了有機專案開發、潛在收購，以及擴大PLN購電量等多元路徑。Perpres 112/2022修訂程序的最終結果，將決定改良後的定價條款能否適用於PGEO現有法規前購電協議以外的未來新增裝置容量。
