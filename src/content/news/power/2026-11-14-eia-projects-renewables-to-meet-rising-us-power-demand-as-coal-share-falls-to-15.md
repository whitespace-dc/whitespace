body_en: |
  The U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) projects that rising electricity demand through 2027 will be met primarily by renewable energy, with utility-scale solar's share of national generation expected to grow from 7% in 2025 to nearly 10% in 2027, according to the agency's November Short-Term Energy Outlook, published November 10, 2026.

  U.S. electric power generation will reach almost 4,390 billion kilowatt-hours (kWh) this year, a 2.7% increase over 2025 and a new production record. EIA attributed the increase to "rising demand from the commercial sector and increased cooling needs from warmer-than-average summer temperatures."

  ## Renewables absorbing demand growth

  Solar and wind are expected to add approximately 72 gigawatts (GW) and 20 GW of new capacity, respectively, across 2026 and 2027 combined, EIA said. Wind's share of the generation mix is forecast to rise from 11% in 2025 to 12% by 2027. "Generation from other energy sources is likely to remain relatively flat or decrease," the agency said.

  The rate of generation growth is expected to slow in 2027. Commercial and industrial demand is forecast to continue expanding, but that growth could be "offset somewhat by lower residential electricity demand resulting from forecast milder summer weather next year," according to EIA.

  ## Battery storage buildout

  The electric power sector is expected to install more than 25 GW of new battery storage capacity in 2027 alone, bringing total installed battery capacity to 91.7 GW by the end of that year, an increase of nearly 40% over expected 2026 totals. The figures cover systems rated at 1 megawatt (MW) or greater.

  Battery capacity stood at 28.9 GW in the first quarter of 2025, rising to an expected 66 GW by the end of 2026 before the projected 2027 additions.

  ## Coal contracting, gas and nuclear steady

  Coal's share of U.S. generation is expected to fall from 17% in 2025 to 15% by 2027, as 13 GW of coal-fired capacity is scheduled to retire by the end of next year. The electric power sector's coal consumption declined relative to 2025 levels in eight of the first 10 months of this year. EIA attributed those decreases to "significant increases in wind and solar capacity as well as a 9% decline in the average natural gas price in the electric power sector during the second and third quarters of this year."

  EIA projects coal consumption will fall an additional 5% in 2027, driven by a forecast 19% decrease in natural gas prices for electric generating plants alongside further renewable capacity additions. Despite coal's contracting share, total absolute generation from coal and gas combined is expected to remain "in line with the combined generation from 2025," EIA said.

  Natural gas is forecast to hold approximately 40% of the generation mix in both 2026 and 2027. Nuclear is expected to average an 18% share over the same period.

  Battery storage capacity is on track to reach 91.7 GW by end-2027, more than three times the 28.9 GW recorded in the first quarter of 2025. EIA expects utility-scale solar to supply nearly 10% of U.S. electricity generation in 2027.

  ## EIA預測再生能源將承接美國用電成長，煤炭占比2027年降至15%

  美國能源資訊署（EIA）在2026年11月10日發布的十一月《短期能源展望》報告中預測，2027年以前持續上揚的電力需求將主要由再生能源消化吸收。其中，公用規模太陽能的全國發電占比預計將從2025年的7%，成長至2027年的近10%。

  美國電力部門今年的總發電量預計將達到近4兆3,900億度（kWh），較2025年成長2.7%，刷新歷史紀錄。EIA將此增幅歸因於「商業部門需求持續擴張，以及夏季氣溫高於均值所帶動的冷氣用電需求增加」。

  ## 再生能源承擔需求增量

  EIA表示，太陽能與風力發電預計將分別在2026年至2027年的合計期間新增約72 GW與20 GW的裝置容量。風力發電的發電占比預計將從2025年的11%上升至2027年的12%。該署指出，「其他能源的發電量可能維持相對持平或呈現下降趨勢」。

  發電成長速度預計將於2027年趨緩。商業與工業需求雖預測仍將持續擴張，但EIA指出，此一成長動能可能因「明年夏季氣候預報趨於溫和，導致住宅用電需求下滑」而有所抵銷。

  ## 儲能電池大規模建置

  電力部門預計僅2027年一年便將新增逾25 GW的儲能電池容量，使總裝置容量於該年底達到91.7 GW，較預期2026年底的總量增加近40%。上述數據涵蓋裝置容量達1 MW（百萬瓦）以上的系統。

  儲能電池裝置容量在2025年第一季時為28.9 GW，預計到2026年底將增至66 GW，2027年的新增量將在此基礎上進一步堆疊。

  ## 煤炭持續萎縮，天然氣與核能維持穩定

  煤炭在美國發電結構中的占比預計將從2025年的17%下滑至2027年的15%，原因之一是共計13 GW的燃煤裝置容量預定於明年底前除役。電力部門今年前十個月中，有八個月的燃煤消耗量低於2025年同期水準。EIA將此趨勢歸因於「風力與太陽能裝置容量大幅增加，以及今年第二、三季電力部門天然氣平均價格下跌9%」。

  EIA預測，2027年的燃煤消耗量將再進一步下滑5%，主要驅動力來自電廠天然氣價格預期下跌19%，以及再生能源裝置容量的持續擴增。儘管煤炭占比持續萎縮，煤炭與天然氣合計的絕對發電量預計仍將「與2025年兩者合計的發電量相當」。

  天然氣預計將在2026年及2027年皆維持約40%的發電占比。核能則預計在同期平均維持18%的占比。

  儲能電池裝置容量預計在2027年底達到91.7 GW，超過2025年第一季所記錄的28.9 GW的三倍以上。EIA預估，公用規模太陽能將於2027年供應近10%的美國電力。
