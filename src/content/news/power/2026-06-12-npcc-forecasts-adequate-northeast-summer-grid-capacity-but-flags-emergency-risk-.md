body_en: |
  The Northeast Power Coordinating Council (NPCC) said Wednesday that New York, New England, and four Canadian provinces have enough electricity resources to meet typical summer demand, but warned that New York and New England could face emergency operating conditions if load rises above forecast levels.

  NPCC published its 2026 Summer Reliability Assessment on June 11. The region it covers — New York, New England, Ontario, Québec, New Brunswick, and Nova Scotia — is expected to have adequate supplies under typical weather patterns, the council said.

  ## Emergency risk under higher demand

  The assessment identifies a scenario in which peak loads exceed typical levels; the NPCC assigned that scenario roughly a 7% probability of occurring. Under those conditions, the Maritimes, New England, and New York "will need to rely on operating procedures in addition to imports to address resource shortages," the council said. A probabilistic analysis goes further, concluding that operators in New York and New England "will likely need to implement emergency operating procedures" and possibly issue energy emergency alerts during periods of higher-than-expected demand combined with low-probability reductions in available resources. The greatest risks are concentrated in July and August, the report said, "demonstrating concerns under extreme conditions."

  ## Capacity outlook

  The region's summer peak demand is forecast at approximately 105,000 megawatts (MW) — enough electricity to power tens of millions of homes simultaneously — some 400 MW above last summer's projection. Available installed capacity is projected at roughly 158,000 MW, about 1,700 MW more than a year ago. The NPCC attributed part of that increase to additional natural gas generation and energy storage facilities expected to become available in Ontario.

  Québec, whose electricity demand peaks in winter rather than summer, "is expected to meet forecast electricity demand by a wide margin enabling transfers of surplus electricity supplies to other areas of the region," the council said.

  ## New transmission reinforcement

  Transmission projects recently completed in New York and New England "have reinforced overall regional reliability for this summer," Phil Fedora, NPCC chief engineer and senior vice president of external affairs, said in a June 10 statement.

  Among the new infrastructure is the 1,250 MW Champlain Hudson Power Express, a high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission line designed to carry hydropower from Québec directly to New York City. As of mid-May the project was in its final testing phase. NPCC President and CEO Charles Dickerson said the assessment "shows that the region has sufficient transmission capability and the resources needed to reliably meet its summer peak demand for a wide range of system conditions," and that "established operating procedures are available to maintain reliability and keep supply and demand in balance" if needed.

  NPCC's probabilistic analysis assumed the Champlain Hudson Power Express would be available for the full summer assessment period.
body_zh: |
  東北電力協調委員會（NPCC）週三表示，紐約、新英格蘭及加拿大四個省份在典型夏季需求條件下，電力資源供應充足，但警告若負載高於預測水準，紐約與新英格蘭恐將面臨緊急運轉情境。

  NPCC於6月11日發布2026年夏季可靠度評估報告。報告涵蓋範圍包括紐約、新英格蘭、安大略省、魁北克省、新伯倫瑞克省及新斯科細亞省，委員會表示，在典型天氣型態下，上述地區的電力供應預計將維持充足。

  ## 高需求情境下的緊急風險

  此評估報告點出一種尖峰負載超越典型水準的情境，NPCC賦予該情境約7%的發生機率。在此條件下，濱海省份、新英格蘭及紐約「將需要借助運轉程序及進口電力以彌補資源短缺」，委員會表示。機率分析結果更進一步指出，紐約及新英格蘭的電力調度人員「極可能需要啟動緊急運轉程序」，在超出預期的高需求與低機率的可用資源縮減同時發生的情況下，甚至可能發出能源緊急警報。報告指出，最大風險集中於7月及8月，「顯示出在極端條件下令人憂慮的隱患」。

  ## 容量展望

  本區域今夏的尖峰電力需求預測約為10萬5,000百萬瓦（MW）——相當於同時供應數千萬戶家庭所需的電力——較去年夏季預測值高出約400 MW。可用裝置容量預估約為15萬8,000 MW，較一年前增加約1,700 MW。NPCC將部分增幅歸因於安大略省預計新投入運轉的天然氣發電廠及儲能設施。

  魁北克省的電力需求高峰出現在冬季而非夏季，委員會表示，該省「預計將以大幅領先的差距達成電力供應目標，並得以將盈餘電力輸往區域內其他地區」。

  ## 新輸電強化工程

  NPCC首席工程師暨對外事務資深副總裁菲爾·費多拉（Phil Fedora）於6月10日發表聲明指出，近期在紐約及新英格蘭竣工的輸電工程「已強化今夏整體區域電網的可靠度」。

  新增基礎設施中，裝置容量達1,250 MW的尚普蘭哈德遜電力快線（Champlain Hudson Power Express）尤為矚目，這條高壓直流（HVDC）輸電線路專為將魁北克省的水力電力直接輸送至紐約市而設計。截至5月中旬，該工程仍處於最終測試階段。NPCC主席暨執行長查爾斯·迪克森（Charles Dickerson）表示，此評估報告「顯示該區域擁有充足的輸電能力及資源，能在廣泛的系統條件下穩定滿足夏季尖峰需求」，並強調「必要時，既有的運轉程序可維護電網可靠度，確保供需維持平衡」。

  NPCC的機率分析假設尚普蘭哈德遜電力快線在整個夏季評估期間均可正常運作。
