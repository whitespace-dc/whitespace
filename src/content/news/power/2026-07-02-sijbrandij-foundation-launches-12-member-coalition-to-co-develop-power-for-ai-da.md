body_en: |
  The Sijbrandij Foundation has launched the Data Centre Power Coalition, a network of 12 founding energy partners intended to help AI data centre developers plan and procure power supply as an integrated part of site development rather than a separate, sequential procurement exercise.

  The coalition's founding members are Amperesand, DG Matrix, Emerald AI, florrent, GridCARE, Hammerhead AI, Hanwha Data Centres, Hitachi, NeuralWatt, Planted Solar, Skeleton Technologies, and Voltus. The foundation said it structured membership across three functional areas: on-site power generation, load flexibility — the ability to modulate computing demand in response to grid conditions — and faster grid interconnection, the process by which a new facility is connected to the public electricity network.

  ## What the coalition is designed to solve

  Data centre developers building large AI computing clusters currently piece together separate arrangements with different vendors for generation, grid access, and demand management, according to the foundation. The coalition's premise is that this fragmented approach is the primary obstacle to faster deployment, not the underlying economics.

  "The AI industry runs on procurement, but power is infrastructure — it has to be co-developed, planned alongside compute from the start, not bought piecemeal after the fact," said Aric Li, Director of Data Centre Energy at the Sijbrandij Foundation. "That complexity, not the economics, is why the commercially superior path hasn't been adopted."

  The foundation described the coalition as a vetted supplier network, meaning member companies have been assessed before inclusion, with the intention of reducing the due diligence burden on infrastructure teams evaluating power options for new sites. The foundation said several partners are expected to contribute across more than one area of the framework rather than being confined to a single role.

  ## Relationship to the Power Playbook

  The coalition extends work the foundation published earlier this year in its Data Centre Power Playbook, an open framework for integrating energy and computing planning. The foundation said the coalition is intended to move that guidance from a reference document to an operational supplier network that infrastructure teams can draw on when planning new sites.

  Under the framework's model, power planning runs alongside compute planning — decisions about on-site solar capacity, battery storage, flexible demand management, and grid connection are made at the same time as data centre design decisions, rather than after the building and cooling layout are largely fixed.

  ## Grid pressure driving demand for new approaches

  The launch comes as a rapid build-out of AI infrastructure has intensified pressure on electricity networks in multiple markets. Developers have faced delays linked to grid connection queues, limited transmission capacity, and concerns about local system reliability, according to the foundation's account of current conditions. Those constraints have pushed operators to examine on-site generation and storage and to consider making large computing loads more responsive to grid signals — approaches the coalition's structure treats as complementary rather than separate fixes.

  Hitachi, one of the 12 founding members, framed its participation in terms of specialisation across a complex supply chain. "No single company can solve data center power alone," said KJ Joshi, Chief Business Officer and Head of Data Centre Business at Hitachi. "Hitachi focuses on what we do best as an integrator and partners with best-in-class companies for the rest. That's what makes this coalition compelling — convening partners across the energy ecosystem to deliver reliable, clean power at the speed and scale AI demands."

  ## Significance for the industry

  For AI infrastructure teams under pressure to bring computing capacity online quickly — particularly where utility interconnection timelines remain uncertain — a coordinated supplier group working within a common framework could reduce the time and complexity of assembling a project-specific energy stack. A shared framework may also make it easier to compare options across sites with different local power conditions.

  The Sijbrandij Foundation said its broader activities span cancer care, education, philanthropy, and art, and that it builds and incubates projects internally before spinning successful initiatives into independent organisations. The data centre energy coalition is one of its sector-focused efforts.

  The foundation said its goal is for power co-development — integrating on-site generation, flexible demand, and faster grid access into a single coordinated process — to become a standard approach across the AI data centre industry, with the 12-member coalition serving as the initial operational vehicle for that model.

  ## Sijbrandij基金會組建12成員聯盟，協力為AI資料中心共同開發電力

  Sijbrandij基金會正式成立「資料中心電力聯盟」（Data Centre Power Coalition），集結12家創始能源夥伴，協助AI資料中心開發商將電力規劃與採購整合進場址開發流程，而非另立門戶、依序推進的獨立採購程序。

  聯盟創始成員包括：Amperesand、DG Matrix、Emerald AI、florrent、GridCARE、Hammerhead AI、Hanwha Data Centres、日立（Hitachi）、NeuralWatt、Planted Solar、Skeleton Technologies，以及Voltus。基金會表示，成員結構橫跨三大功能領域：現地電力供應、負載彈性調度（即依電網狀況動態調整運算需求的能力），以及加速電網併網——亦即新設施接入公共電力網路的程序。

  ## 聯盟成立所要解決的問題

  根據基金會的說明，目前建置大型AI運算叢集的資料中心開發商，往往需要分別與不同廠商就發電、電網接入及需量管理各自洽談合作，拼湊出一套運作方案。聯盟的核心立論在於：正是這種碎片化的方式，而非底層的經濟邏輯，才是拖慢部署速度的主要障礙。

  「AI產業靠採購驅動，但電力是基礎設施——必須與算力從頭共同規劃、協同開發，而不是事後零散購置，」Sijbrandij基金會資料中心能源總監黎艾瑞克（Aric Li）表示，「正是這種複雜性，而不是經濟面的因素，才是商業上更優越的路徑至今未能普及的原因。」

  基金會將此聯盟定位為經過審核的供應商網絡，意即成員企業在加入前均已完成評估，旨在降低基礎設施團隊在為新場址評估電力方案時的盡職調查負擔。基金會亦指出，預計有多家夥伴將跨足框架中一個以上的領域，而非侷限於單一角色。

  ## 與《電力手冊》的關係

  此聯盟是基金會對其稍早發布的《資料中心電力手冊》（Data Centre Power Playbook）所做的延伸落實。該手冊是一套整合能源與算力規劃的開放框架。基金會表示，聯盟的目的在於將手冊從參考文件轉化為可供基礎設施團隊規劃新場址時實際取用的運營供應商網絡。

  依據此框架的運作模式，電力規劃與算力規劃同步推進——現地太陽能裝置容量、電池儲能、彈性需量管理及電網連接等決策，均與資料中心設計決策同步制訂，而非等到建築與冷卻配置大致定案後才啟動。

  ## 電網壓力催生新方法需求

  此次聯盟的成立，恰逢AI基礎設施快速擴建對多個市場電力網路形成強烈壓力之際。據基金會對當前形勢的描述，開發商已面臨與電網併網排隊等候、輸電容量不足，以及地區電力系統可靠性疑慮等問題相關的延誤。這些限制促使業者積極評估現地發電與儲能方案，並考慮讓大型運算負載對電網訊號做出更靈敏的回應——這些做法在聯盟的框架結構中，被視為相輔相成的配套措施，而非各自獨立的解方。

  創始成員之一日立，以複雜供應鏈中的專業分工角度詮釋其加入聯盟的動機。「沒有任何一家公司能單獨解決資料中心的電力問題，」日立首席業務長暨資料中心事業負責人喬希（KJ Joshi）表示，「日立專注於我們身為整合商最擅長的事，其餘部分則與各領域頂尖夥伴合作。這正是此聯盟令人信服之處——匯聚能源生態系中的各方夥伴，以AI所需的速度與規模，提供可靠的潔淨電力。」

  ## 對產業的意義

  對於急需快速投入算力、尤其是在電力公司併網時程仍不明朗的地區運營的AI基礎設施團隊而言，一個在共同框架下協作的供應商群組，有望大幅縮減為特定專案拼湊能源方案所需的時間與複雜度。共用框架也可能使跨場址、在不同在地電力條件下的方案比較更為便捷。

  Sijbrandij基金會表示，其廣泛業務範疇涵蓋癌症照護、教育、慈善公益及藝術等領域，並習慣在內部孵化各類專案，待成熟後再將成功的計畫獨立為自主組織。資料中心能源聯盟即為其旗下的產業聚焦型專案之一。

  基金會表示，其目標是讓電力共同開發——將現地發電、彈性需量調度與加速電網接入整合為單一協調流程——成為AI資料中心產業的標準做法，而這個由12家成員組成的聯盟，正是推動此模式落地的初始運營載體。
