body_en: |
  Taiwan's Delta Electronics, Gaoli, and a growing cluster of component makers, end users, and integrators are pursuing three distinct tracks in the solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) market for AI data center power, according to research firm DIGITIMES, as rising electricity demand and lengthening grid connection timelines push data center operators toward on-site, behind-the-meter generation.

  ## Why SOFC is gaining ground

  Data centers currently rely mainly on gas turbines for behind-the-meter (BTM) generation, meaning electricity produced at the facility rather than drawn from the public grid. Gas turbines face constraints including long equipment delivery lead times, as well as efficiency, noise, and carbon-emission limits, DIGITIMES said. SOFC, by contrast, offers modular deployment, higher efficiency, lower carbon output, and compatibility with the high-voltage direct-current power architectures used in AI data centers, DIGITIMES said. SOFC has begun replacing gas turbines in some installations.

  ## Market size and BTM penetration

  Global data center BTM penetration currently sits below 10%, according to DIGITIMES. The firm projects that by 2030, between 25% and 33% of new global data center power demand will be met through hybrid BTM arrangements that combine grid supply with on-site sources including gas turbines, SOFC, and battery storage.

  The global SOFC market is expected to exceed $10 billion by 2030, DIGITIMES said.

  ## Two global supply ecosystems

  Two major SOFC supply structures are taking shape globally, DIGITIMES analyst Yu Pei-ru said. U.S.-based Bloom Energy holds the leading supplier position and is expanding capacity; DIGITIMES expects Bloom Energy's revenue to grow more than 80% in 2026.

  A second ecosystem is coalescing around technology licensing from U.K.-based Ceres. Ceres's partners include Delta Electronics of Taiwan, South Korea's Doosan Fuel Cell, and China's Weichai Power. Yu said this Ceres-centered ecosystem is expected to gain market influence after 2027, establishing a second major force alongside Bloom Energy's established network.

  ## Taiwan's three participation tracks

  Taiwan companies have participated in Bloom Energy's supply chain since 2008, according to DIGITIMES. Since 2023, electronics manufacturers began adopting SOFC generation equipment directly, and the market has since extended into system integration. DIGITIMES identifies three pathways through which Taiwan firms now engage with the SOFC sector.

  Component suppliers form the first track: Gaoli, Baolaite, Chicony Power, and Hongjin Metal.

  End-use adopters form the second track. Electronics companies including Unimicron were among early adopters; state-owned enterprises CPC Corporation and Taiwan Power Company have since followed, as has Creative's data center, which incorporates SOFC as a distributed, low-carbon power source.

  System integration and services form the third track. Delta Electronics is developing the role of SOFC system integrator. Xunxun Technology, Leopard Energy, and the Chongye Group participate through distribution agreements and licensed installation services.

  ## Industry significance

  The convergence of component supply, end-use adoption, and system integration marks a broadening of Taiwan's SOFC industry presence beyond its original role as a Bloom Energy component supplier. DIGITIMES expects the Ceres-licensing ecosystem, which includes Delta Electronics, to become a significant competitive force after 2027. The firm projects the global SOFC market will exceed $10 billion by 2030.

  ## SOFC為何逐漸崛起

  根據研究機構DIGITIMES，台灣台達電、高力及一批持續擴大的零組件業者、終端用戶與系統整合商，正沿三條截然不同的路徑深耕固態氧化物燃料電池（SOFC）市場，鎖定AI資料中心供電需求。隨著電力需求攀升、電網接入時程不斷拉長，資料中心業者加速尋求現地表後（behind-the-meter，BTM）發電替代方案，帶動SOFC商機浮現。

  資料中心目前主要仰賴燃氣渦輪機進行表後發電，亦即在廠區自行產電，而非直接從公共電網取電。DIGITIMES指出，燃氣渦輪機面臨設備交期漫長、效率受限、噪音擾鄰及碳排放限制等多重挑戰。相較之下，SOFC具備模組化部署彈性、更高能源效率、更低碳排，以及與AI資料中心高壓直流電架構高度相容等優勢；在部分裝置中，SOFC已開始取代燃氣渦輪機。

  ## 市場規模與表後滲透率

  DIGITIMES資料顯示，全球資料中心表後發電滲透率目前仍低於10%。該機構預測，至2030年，全球新增資料中心電力需求中，將有25%至33%透過結合電網供電與現地電源（涵蓋燃氣渦輪機、SOFC及電池儲能）的混合式表後架構來滿足。

  DIGITIMES表示，全球SOFC市場預計於2030年前突破100億美元。

  ## 全球兩大供應生態系

  DIGITIMES分析師余培如指出，全球目前正形成兩大主要SOFC供應體系。美國Bloom Energy占據領先供應商地位，並持續擴充產能；DIGITIMES預期Bloom Energy 2026年營收將成長逾80%。

  第二個生態系則以英國Ceres的技術授權為核心，逐步凝聚成形，合作夥伴涵蓋台灣台達電、韓國斗山燃料電池（Doosan Fuel Cell）及中國濰柴動力（Weichai Power）。余培如表示，以Ceres為核心的生態系預計在2027年後逐步擴大市場影響力，與Bloom Energy既有供應網絡並列，成為另一股不容忽視的主要力量。

  ## 台灣的三條參與路徑

  根據DIGITIMES，台灣企業自2008年起便已參與Bloom Energy的供應鏈。自2023年起，電子製造商開始直接採購SOFC發電設備，市場隨後進一步延伸至系統整合領域。DIGITIMES歸納出台灣業者目前切入SOFC產業的三條路徑。

  第一條路徑為**零組件供應商**，代表業者包括高力、寶萊特、群光電能及宏進金屬。

  第二條路徑為**終端導入用戶**。電子業者欣興電子率先採用，國營事業台灣中油（CPC Corporation）與台灣電力公司隨後跟進；Creative 資料中心亦將SOFC納入分散式低碳電源配置。

  第三條路徑為**系統整合與服務**。台達電正積極發展SOFC系統整合商角色；迅迅科技、豹能源及崇業集團則透過代理協議與授權安裝服務共同參與市場。

  ## 產業意義

  零組件供應、終端導入與系統整合三大面向的匯聚，標誌著台灣SOFC產業的存在感已超越最初單純作為Bloom Energy供應商的定位，朝全方位布局邁進。DIGITIMES預期，涵蓋台達電在內的Ceres授權生態系，將在2027年後成為重要競爭力量。該機構同時預測，全球SOFC市場將於2030年前突破100億美元規模。
