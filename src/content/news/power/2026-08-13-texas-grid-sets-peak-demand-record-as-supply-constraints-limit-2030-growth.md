body_en: |
  Texas' grid hit an unofficial all-time peak demand record on July 22, but a market analysis from Ascend Analytics warns that severe supply-side constraints could prevent the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) — the state's grid operator — from meeting the surge in large-load requests driven by data centers and industrial growth.

  Ascend forecasts that ERCOT peak demand could reach 120 gigawatts (GW) by 2030, representing growth of more than 30% above the new record. That figure falls notably below ERCOT's own adjusted forecast of 138 GW and well below the 208 GW in total 2030 load reported by transmission service providers based on contracts and letters of intent, a figure ERCOT disclosed in April.

  Ascend's lower estimate reflects an assumed attrition rate. The firm projects that only 55.4% of proposed large loads will successfully interconnect, with delays in energization further complicating the forecast. "Even though that appetite is enormous, if it can't get met, it's not coming online," Brent Nelson, senior managing director of market intelligence at Ascend, told Utility Dive.

  ERCOT's large-load interconnection queue has grown by more than 200 GW since 2024, driven by data centers, manufacturing facilities, cryptocurrency operations, and industrial oil and gas development. That volume reflects the scale of demand pressure facing the grid, but Ascend says the grid's capacity to absorb it is far more limited.

  ## Generation supply cannot keep pace

  Robert LaFaso, Ascend's director of market intelligence, identified generation availability as the primary constraint. "The ability of the grid to add new generation is much, much smaller than the demand of queued large-load facilities," LaFaso said.

  Ascend cites a limited number of tier-one gas turbine manufacturers as a key factor in project attrition among Texas Energy Fund applicants. The firm also identifies engineering, procurement and construction capacity, high-voltage equipment, and permitting as growing bottlenecks to building new generation. Multi-year development timelines mean that supply additions cannot quickly respond to accelerating demand, both executives said.

  Nelson noted that co-locating generation and load — placing a power plant on the same site as a large customer — can partially mitigate some transmission challenges, but supply chain hurdles for dispatchable plants limit how widely that approach can be deployed.

  ## Market design under pressure

  Ascend says ERCOT's energy-only market — which relies on scarcity pricing, rather than a capacity market, to incentivize new generation investment — no longer provides sufficient revenue certainty to finance the dispatchable generation needed to meet future demand. Persistent gaps between forward market prices and actual realized outcomes further weaken the investment signals developers depend on, the firm said.

  Nelson identified what Ascend characterizes as a structural problem with scarcity pricing: it produces only two outcomes. Either scarcity pricing fails to generate enough revenue to support new entry, leaving the system short of dispatchable capacity, or it succeeds but creates an affordability crisis by forcing consumers to pay high, volatile prices across the entire supply stack during scarcity events.

  A changing generation mix could make scarcity revenues more weather- and outage-dependent, adding further volatility for generators and complicating financing, Ascend said.

  Storage faces a parallel financing problem. LaFaso said lenders are increasingly requiring contracted revenues rather than relying on merchant market returns. "We've seen pullbacks from a lot of the lenders expressing drastic concerns over the past several years, where [storage projects] have not met revenue expectations, and the lending community is starting to question whether or not merchant storage is investable without firmer revenue," he said. Those financing challenges are compounded by ERCOT's energy-only market design.

  ## Reform concepts and emerging risk windows

  The tightening supply-demand outlook is prompting discussion of potential market reforms, including a concept Nelson described as a "bring-your-own-new-generation" requirement for large loads — a structure that would tie large-load interconnections to commitments to bring matching new generation online. Nelson said such a framework could help align new demand with new supply but warned it could undermine the economics of existing merchant generators and increase policy uncertainty for investors.

  Ascend expects ERCOT's reserve margins to remain healthy through 2026 before tightening as load growth outpaces supply additions. The firm has also identified September as an emerging risk period: reduced solar output from earlier sunsets and lower evening wind generation compared with August decrease the grid's ability to meet demand during that month.

  Ascend expects wholesale electricity prices to rise in the near term as demand grows, before stabilizing over the longer term as additional renewable generation comes online. Natural gas plants are still expected to set wholesale prices during evening ramping periods when solar output declines.
body_zh: |
  德州電網於7月22日創下非官方歷史最高尖峰需求紀錄，然而市場分析機構Ascend Analytics的最新報告卻發出警示：嚴峻的供給面制約，恐令德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）——該州的電網調度機構——難以因應資料中心與工業成長所帶動的大型用電申請浪潮。

  Ascend預測，ERCOT的尖峰需求至2030年可能達到120吉瓦（GW），較這次新紀錄成長逾30%。這一數字明顯低於ERCOT自身調整後預測的138 GW，更遠低於輸電服務業者依合約及意向書所呈報的2030年總負載208 GW——此數字係ERCOT於今年4月所揭露。

  Ascend的較低估計反映了一套假定的淘汰率。該機構預計，在所有提出申請的大型用電案例中，僅有55.4%能順利完成併網，而通電時程的延誤更使預測愈加複雜。Ascend市場情報資深董事總經理布倫特·尼爾森（Brent Nelson）向《Utility Dive》表示：「即便需求胃納量十分龐大，若無法被滿足，這些負載就不會上線。」

  ERCOT的大型用電併網申請佇列自2024年以來已累積超過200 GW，來源涵蓋資料中心、製造業設施、加密貨幣運算及石油天然氣工業開發。這一體量充分反映出電網所承受的需求壓力之巨，但Ascend指出，電網真正能夠吸納的容量遠遠有限。

  ## 新增發電量跟不上需求

  Ascend市場情報總監羅伯特·拉法索（Robert LaFaso）將發電可用量列為首要制約因素。「電網新增發電能力，遠遠小於申請排隊中大型用電設施的需求，」拉法索說。

  Ascend指出，一線燃氣渦輪機製造商家數有限，是德州能源基金申請案出現淘汰的關鍵原因之一。此外，工程採購與施工（EPC）產能、高壓電氣設備，以及許可審批程序，也正成為新增發電日益嚴峻的瓶頸。兩位主管均表示，動輒數年的開發時程，意味著供給端無法迅速因應加速成長的需求。

  尼爾森指出，將發電設施與用電大戶設置於同一廠址——即「共址」模式——在一定程度上可緩解部分輸電挑戰，但可調度電廠的供應鏈障礙，使這種做法難以廣泛推廣。

  ## 市場設計承受壓力

  Ascend表示，ERCOT以稀缺定價而非容量市場來刺激新發電投資的「純電能市場」設計，已無法為可調度發電所需的融資提供足夠的收益確定性。遠期市場價格與實際結算結果之間長期存在的落差，更進一步削弱了開發商所仰賴的投資訊號。

  尼爾森點出Ascend所稱的稀缺定價結構性問題：它只會導致兩種結果。若稀缺定價無法產生足夠收益支撐新進入者，系統便會缺乏可調度容量；若稀缺定價奏效，又會在稀缺事件期間強迫消費者對整個供給組合支付高額且波動劇烈的電價，從而引發可負擔性危機。

  Ascend表示，發電結構轉變可能使稀缺收益更依賴天氣與停機狀況，進一步加劇發電業者的收益波動，並使融資難度升高。

  儲能亦面臨類似的融資困境。拉法索表示，貸款機構日益要求儲能項目提供有合約保障的收益來源，而非依賴純粹的電力市場收益。「過去幾年，許多貸款機構已大幅縮手，對（儲能項目）未能達到收益預期表達嚴重憂慮；借貸圈開始質疑，在缺乏更穩定收益保障的情況下，商業化儲能是否具備可投資性，」他說。ERCOT的純電能市場設計，更進一步加劇了上述融資挑戰。

  ## 市場改革構想與浮現中的風險窗口

  供需吃緊的前景正推動各界討論潛在的市場改革方案，其中包括尼爾森所描述的「自備新發電」要求——這是一種將大型用電併網與承諾同步引入對應新發電容量相掛鉤的機制。尼爾森表示，此一框架或有助於使新需求與新供給保持同步，但也警告，這可能削弱現有商業化發電業者的經濟效益，並增加投資者面對的政策不確定性。

  Ascend預期，ERCOT的備轉容量率至2026年前將維持健康水準，但隨後將隨負載成長超越供給增速而逐步趨緊。此外，Ascend已將9月識別為一個浮現中的高風險時段：日落提早導致太陽能輸出減少，加上相比8月夜間風力發電量偏低，使電網在該月滿足需求的能力下滑。

  Ascend預期，短期內批發電價將隨需求成長而上揚，但隨著額外再生能源持續上線，中長期電價將趨於穩定。在太陽能輸出下滑的傍晚電力爬坡時段，天然氣機組仍預計將作為批發電價的邊際定價機組。
