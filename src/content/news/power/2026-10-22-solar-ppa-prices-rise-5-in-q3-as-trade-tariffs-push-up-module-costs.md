body_en: |
  The average price of a solar power purchase agreement (PPA) — a long-term contract under which a buyer agrees to purchase electricity at a fixed rate from a developer — rose 4.9% to $64.23 per megawatt-hour (MWh) in the third quarter of 2026, according to clean energy marketplace LevelTen Energy. The increase puts solar PPA prices 6% above their year-ago level and 12.7% higher than in the third quarter of 2024.

  Wind PPAs remained relatively flat in Q3 but have increased 13.8% year-over-year and 27.5% since 2024, LevelTen said.

  Tariffs are the primary driver of the solar price increase and are likely to push costs higher before the end of the year, said Sarah Wolf, LevelTen's director of North America transactions. The Section 232 tariffs announced in early August have not yet fully taken effect, but Wolf said they could add between $3/MWh and $7/MWh to solar PPA prices once they do.

  ## Tariff effects already moving through the supply chain

  A significant portion of the Q3 increase is anticipatory: buyers have been moving to lock in contracts before the new tariff schedule applies, Wolf said. But the tariffs are already affecting equipment costs upstream. Current rules prohibit solar developers from stockpiling modules in advance of tariff changes, and pricing is already shifting.

  "We're already seeing more than half of solar modules repriced, and we know that price is being passed down to sellers and ultimately buyers," Wolf said.

  ## Wind flat overall, but ERCOT skews the average

  Wind PPA prices rose across most of the United States during the third quarter, but a decline in prices within the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) — the grid operator covering most of Texas — pulled the North American average down, LevelTen said. ERCOT has seen a larger number of wind projects reach commercial operation in recent months.

  At the same time, demand for wind power has softened as buyers increasingly view solar as the lower-risk option in the current policy environment, Wolf said. New-build wind contracts are particularly scarce. "We have much less wind than we have ever seen getting onto the platform because of the challenges with permitting," she said, referring to ongoing federal-level permitting constraints facing wind developers.

  ## Hyperscalers dominate new-build PPA market

  Demand from hyperscalers — large technology companies with extensive data center and computing infrastructure — has come to dominate the new-build segment of the PPA market. Wolf said 99% of new-build PPAs signed in the first quarter of 2026 went to hyperscalers. The number of first-time PPA buyers has fallen to its lowest level since 2016, according to CEBA.

  Other corporate buyers, unable to compete in the new-build segment, have turned to PPAs tied to existing power projects whose original contracts have expired. An aging renewable energy fleet has opened this channel: as projects built in earlier development cycles reach the end of their initial contract terms, new buyers can step in. Developers are also repowering or expanding existing sites, creating additional supply outside the new-build pipeline.

  Wolf said prices for PPAs on existing projects are currently comparable to those for new-build contracts, even as availability differs.

  ## What comes next

  Prices for all PPA categories appear set to continue rising, given the combination of tariff uncertainty and the approaching expiration of renewable energy tax credits, Wolf said. Developers and buyers are under pressure to finalize projects that still qualify for those credits before the deadline.

  "It's still a matter of racing against the clock to lock in projects" that still qualify for tax credits, she said.

  ## 太陽能PPA價格第三季漲近5%，貿易關稅推升模組成本

  根據清潔能源市場平台LevelTen Energy發布的最新數據，2026年第三季太陽能購電協議（PPA）——即買方與開發商簽訂的長期合約，以固定費率購買電力——平均價格上漲4.9%至每兆瓦時（MWh）64.23美元。此一漲幅使太陽能PPA價格較一年前高出6%，較2024年第三季則高出12.7%。

  風電PPA價格在第三季相對持平，但LevelTen指出，其年增幅已達13.8%，相較2024年更累計上漲27.5%。

  LevelTen北美交易業務總監莎拉·沃爾夫（Sarah Wolf）表示，關稅是推動太陽能PPA價格上漲的主要因素，且在今年底前成本可能進一步攀升。今年8月初公告的第232條款關稅尚未全面生效，但沃爾夫指出，一旦落地，每兆瓦時太陽能PPA價格恐將再增加3至7美元。

  ## 關稅衝擊已在供應鏈中蔓延

  沃爾夫說明，第三季漲幅有相當一部分屬於「預期性」上漲：買方搶在新關稅時程適用前加速鎖定合約。然而，關稅對上游設備成本的影響早已開始顯現。現行規定禁止太陽能開發商在關稅調整前預先囤積模組，而模組定價也已出現波動。

  「我們已看到逾半數太陽能模組重新議價，而且我們知道這個價格正一路往下傳遞，影響到賣方，最終也影響到買方，」沃爾夫說。

  ## 風電整體持平，但ERCOT拉低平均值

  第三季美國大多數地區風電PPA價格均有所上漲，但德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）——負責覆蓋德州大部分地區的電網營運機構——轄區內價格下滑，拉低了北美整體平均值。LevelTen指出，近幾個月ERCOT有更多風電項目陸續完工投產。

  與此同時，沃爾夫表示，在當前的政策環境下，買方愈來愈傾向將太陽能視為風險較低的選項，對風電的需求因而有所降溫。新建風電合約的供給尤為稀缺。「由於聯邦層級的許可核准持續面臨重重障礙，我們看到進入平台的風電量比任何時候都少，」她說，指的是風電開發商目前面臨的聯邦許可限制。

  ## 超大規模業者主導新建PPA市場

  超大規模業者——即擁有大規模資料中心與運算基礎設施的大型科技公司——已主導PPA市場的新建項目板塊。沃爾夫指出，2026年第一季簽署的新建PPA中，99%均由超大規模業者包攬。根據企業清潔能源聯盟（CEBA）的數據，首次購買PPA的企業買家數量已跌至2016年以來的最低點。

  其他企業買家在無力競爭新建項目的情況下，紛紛轉向與現有電力項目掛鉤的PPA——即原始合約已到期的既有案場。不斷老化的再生能源機組為這條管道創造了機會：隨著早期開發週期所建的項目陸續到達初始合約期限，新買方得以接手。開發商也正對既有場址進行設備更新或擴建，在新建管道之外增添額外供給。

  沃爾夫指出，現有項目PPA的價格目前與新建合約相當，儘管兩者在供給量上存在差異。

  ## 後市展望

  沃爾夫表示，考量關稅不確定性以及再生能源稅收抵減即將到期的雙重壓力，各類PPA的價格看來將持續走高。開發商與買方都面臨壓力，必須趕在截止期限前完成仍可享有稅收抵減資格的項目。

  「對於那些還符合稅收抵減資格的項目，現在仍是一場與時間賽跑、搶先鎖定的競局，」她說。
