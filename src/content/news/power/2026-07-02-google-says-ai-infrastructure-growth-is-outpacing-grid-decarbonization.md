body_en: |
  Google disclosed in its 2026 Environmental Report that its AI infrastructure buildout is "currently accelerating faster than the grid is decarbonizing," as the company's electricity consumption rose 37% in 2025 — the largest single-year increase in its history.

  The report arrives as utilities, grid operators and federal regulators contend with surging electricity demand from AI data centers. Recent proceedings at the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC), along with load forecast revisions from ERCOT and other grid operators, have increasingly focused on large-load interconnections, transmission constraints and the availability of dispatchable generation — power that can be called on at any time, unlike intermittent wind and solar.

  ## Demand growth and emissions

  Google's total electricity consumption has grown more than 250% since 2019, driven by expanding AI products and cloud services, according to the report. Despite that trajectory, the company said it matched 100% of its annual global electricity consumption with renewable energy purchases for the ninth consecutive year and reduced operational emissions 2% through additional clean-energy procurement and efficiency improvements.

  That annual renewable matching is distinct from Google's longer-term goal of supplying its operations with carbon-free electricity every hour of every day — a 24/7 standard that annual procurement figures do not satisfy. Fleet-wide power usage effectiveness (PUE) — a ratio measuring how efficiently a data center uses energy, where 1.0 represents perfect efficiency and higher values indicate more overhead for cooling and other systems — averaged 1.09 across Google's facilities in 2025.

  The company also reported that the median Gemini Apps text prompt required 33 times less energy and produced a 44-fold reduction in carbon footprint over 12 months through hardware, software and model improvements. Those efficiency gains have not offset the overall rise in electricity demand, the company said.

  ## Barriers to decarbonization

  Google attributed the widening gap to structural barriers rather than procurement shortfalls: interconnection queues, permitting delays, transmission constraints and shortages of firm carbon-free generation.

  In a May policy paper, the company called for faster transmission permitting, wider deployment of grid-enhancing technologies, voluntary data center participation in demand response, and accelerated investment in advanced nuclear, geothermal and other firm energy resources. Google argued that meeting AI-driven electricity demand requires an "all-of-the-above" approach that includes natural gas with carbon capture, energy storage and demand response alongside renewables.

  Ihab Osman, an independent strategist focused on data center and mission-critical infrastructure, told Data Center Knowledge that Google's assessment is "directionally right," but said the industry's challenge extends beyond annual procurement totals.

  "The gap is not between 'clean energy ambition' and 'no clean energy.' It is between annual clean-energy procurement and firm clean power delivered at the right node, in the right hour, under grid stress," Osman said.

  ## Clean energy commitments

  Google signed agreements for more than 12 gigawatts (GW) of net-new clean generation during 2025, more than the combined total from the previous two years. One gigawatt is roughly equivalent to the output of a large conventional power plant. The company is expanding beyond traditional wind and solar procurement to support advanced geothermal, nuclear, hydropower and fusion projects, and has separately advocated for permitting reform and grid modernization.

  ## Flexible load as a grid resource

  Google is also positioning its flexible computing capacity as a tool for grid operators. The company said it can shift machine-learning workloads away from periods of peak grid stress without affecting customer-facing services, and as of early 2026 had integrated approximately 1 GW of demand-response capacity — the ability to reduce consumption on request from a utility — into long-term utility agreements in the United States. Google said that flexibility can improve grid utilization and support faster interconnection of new data center capacity.

  The company did not disclose what share of its overall computing load the 1 GW represents or how that flexibility is operationally achieved.

  Ahmad Faruqui, an economist specializing in electricity markets and demand response, said those details are essential to evaluating the significance of the announcement.

  "I need to know what percent of the grid's peak demand that 1 GW represents. I also need to know what percent of the data center's peak demand that represents. I also need to know how that 1 GW is being achieved," Faruqui told Data Center Knowledge. He added that empirical data on data center load flexibility remains limited.

  Faruqui also cautioned utilities and regulators against drawing broad conclusions from early hyperscale demand-response claims. "Utilities, regulators, and governments need to consider multiple scenarios when evaluating the economics of adding hyperscalers to the grid, not just base them on the assertion of these new customers," he said.

  ## Industry context

  The dynamic Google describes is not unique to the company. Microsoft has acknowledged that AI infrastructure is making its climate goals harder to achieve while continuing to expand carbon-free electricity procurement. Amazon and Meta have likewise announced major investments in nuclear energy and other firm power resources as they build out AI infrastructure, according to the report.

  Google Chief Sustainability Officer Kate Brandt wrote in a LinkedIn post accompanying the report that the company is navigating "the tension between hyper-growth and environmental stewardship."

  Google reaffirmed its long-term goal of operating on 24/7 carbon-free energy while acknowledging in the report that AI infrastructure is expanding faster than the underlying energy system can decarbonize. Its May policy agenda argued that closing that gap will require faster transmission permitting, accelerated deployment of firm clean generation, and broader use of demand response — steps the company said are not yet occurring at the pace needed to keep up with AI-driven load growth.
body_zh: |
  Google在2026年環境報告中揭露，其AI基礎建設的擴張速度「目前已超越電網去碳化的腳步」，2025年用電量較前一年增加37%，創下公司史上單年最大漲幅。

  此份報告發布之際，正值公用事業業者、電網營運商及聯邦監管機構齊力應對AI資料中心用電需求激增的關鍵時刻。聯邦能源監管委員會（FERC）近期的審議程序，以及德州電網（ERCOT）等電網營運商的負載預測修正，已愈來愈聚焦於大型負載併網、輸電壅塞，以及可調度發電——即不同於間歇性風力與太陽能、可隨時應需啟動的電力——的供應狀況。

  ## 需求成長與排放

  根據報告，受AI產品與雲端服務持續擴張所驅動，自2019年以來Google的總用電量已成長逾250%。儘管如此，該公司表示連續第九年以再生能源採購量抵銷其全球全年度總用電，並透過增購潔淨能源及提升能源效率，將營運排放量減少2%。

  上述年度再生能源抵銷，與Google更長遠的目標有所不同——後者是要讓每一天每一小時的營運都由零碳電力供應，亦即業界所稱「全天候無碳電力（24/7 CFE）」的標準，而年度採購量無法滿足此一要求。2025年，Google旗下設施的電力使用效率（PUE）平均為1.09——PUE是衡量資料中心能源使用效率的指標，1.0代表完美效率，數值愈高則表示冷卻及其他系統消耗的額外能源愈多。

  該公司另指出，透過硬體、軟體與模型改進，Gemini Apps文字提示的中位數能耗在12個月內降低了33倍，碳足跡也縮減了44倍。然而Google也坦承，這些效率提升並未能抵銷整體用電需求的上升趨勢。

  ## 去碳化的結構性障礙

  Google將這道日益擴大的落差，歸因於結構性障礙而非採購不足：包括電網併網排程壅塞、許可審查延誤、輸電線路限制，以及穩定零碳發電來源的短缺。

  今年5月，Google發布政策白皮書，呼籲加快輸電許可審查、更廣泛部署電網強化技術、資料中心自願參與需量反應計畫，以及加速投資先進核能、地熱發電及其他穩定能源資源。Google主張，要滿足AI帶動的用電需求，必須採取「全方位並進」的策略，在再生能源之外，也納入搭配碳捕捉技術的天然氣、儲能系統及需量反應。

  專注於資料中心與關鍵任務基礎建設的獨立策略顧問Ihab Osman向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，Google的評估「方向正確」，但他指出，業界面臨的挑戰已超越年度採購總量的層次。

  「這道落差並非存在於『潔淨能源雄心』與『毫無潔淨能源』之間，而是存在於年度潔淨能源採購量，與在正確節點、正確時段、在電網承壓情況下實際輸送的穩定潔淨電力之間，」Osman說。

  ## 潔淨能源承諾

  Google在2025年簽署協議，新增逾12吉瓦（GW）的淨增潔淨發電裝置容量，超過前兩年的合計總量。1吉瓦大致相當於一座大型傳統電廠的輸出功率。該公司正擴大採購範疇，超越傳統風能與太陽能，納入先進地熱、核能、水力發電及核融合等項目的支持，並另行倡議推動許可法規改革與電網現代化。

  ## 彈性負載作為電網資源

  Google也正將其具備彈性的運算產能，定位為電網營運商的調節工具。該公司表示，可在電網壅塞尖峰時段，於不影響面向客戶服務的前提下，將機器學習工作負載移轉至其他時段；截至2026年初，Google已在美國的長期公用事業合約中，納入約1吉瓦的需量反應容量——即在公用事業要求下可隨時削減用電的能力。Google表示，這種彈性有助於提升電網使用效率，並支援新增資料中心容量更快取得電網併網資格。

  該公司未揭露這1吉瓦佔其整體運算負載的比例，也未說明此彈性在作業層面如何實現。

  專攻電力市場與需量反應的經濟學家Ahmad Faruqui指出，這些細節對於評估此公告的實質意義至關重要。

  「我需要知道這1吉瓦佔電網尖峰需求的比例，也需要知道這佔該資料中心尖峰需求的比例，更需要知道這1吉瓦究竟是如何達成的，」Faruqui向《Data Center Knowledge》表示。他並補充，目前學界對資料中心負載彈性的實證資料仍相當有限。

  Faruqui也警告公用事業業者與監管機關，不應僅憑超大規模業者早期的需量反應宣稱就遽下廣泛結論。「公用事業業者、監管機關與政府在評估將超大規模業者納入電網的經濟效益時，應考量多種情境，而非僅憑這些新客戶的自我聲稱作為依據，」他說。

  ## 產業背景

  Google所描述的這一態勢，並非該公司所獨有。微軟已承認AI基礎建設使其氣候目標更加難以實現，同時仍持續擴大零碳電力採購。據報告指出，亞馬遜與Meta在建設AI基礎建設之際，也相繼宣布對核能及其他穩定電力來源進行重大投資。

  Google首席永續長Kate Brandt在隨報告發布的LinkedIn貼文中寫道，該公司正設法駕馭「超高速成長與環境永續之間的張力」。

  Google重申了以全天候零碳電力供應營運的長期目標，同時在報告中坦承，AI基礎建設的擴張速度已超越基礎能源系統去碳化的步伐。其5月政策議程主張，縮小這道落差有賴於加快輸電許可審查、加速部署穩定潔淨發電來源，以及更廣泛應用需量反應——而這些措施目前的推進速度，仍不足以跟上AI帶動的負載成長。
