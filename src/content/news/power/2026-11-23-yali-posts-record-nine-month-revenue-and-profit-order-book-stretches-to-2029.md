  ## Record results through September

  Taiwanese electrical equipment maker Yali (亞力) reported record nine-month consolidated revenue and earnings for 2026, the company said at an online investor conference on November 20, with orders already booked through 2029.

  For the first three quarters of 2026, Yali posted consolidated revenue of NT$7.994 billion (New Taiwan dollars), operating profit of NT$809 million, and net profit after tax of NT$674 million — all record highs for the January-September period. Earnings per share (EPS, net profit divided by shares outstanding) for the first three quarters reached NT$2.44, up from NT$2.14 in the same period of the prior year and a new record for that period.

  ## Third quarter: slight EPS dip on currency headwind

  On a standalone basis, Yali's third quarter was the second-strongest Q3 on record by EPS. Q3 consolidated revenue came in at NT$2.649 billion, with operating profit of NT$283 million and net profit after tax of NT$229 million. Q3 EPS was NT$0.83, down marginally from NT$0.88 in Q3 of the prior year.

  Yali attributed the slight year-on-year EPS decline in the third quarter to foreign exchange: the company said gross margin and operating profit both rose compared with the prior-year quarter, but foreign exchange gains were NT$30 million lower, which it described as the primary reason for the dip. The nine-month gross margin improvement more than offset that headwind at the cumulative level, allowing full first-three-quarter EPS to set a new record.

  ## Three demand drivers behind the backlog

  Yali said three distinct demand sources are filling its order book.

  First, the semiconductor industry's ongoing construction of new fabs and capacity expansions is generating a sustained flow of electrical equipment orders. Second, a subsidiary of NVIDIA (輝達) operating in Latin America has placed orders for AI data center (AIDC) infrastructure. Third, Taiwan Power Company (台電, also known as Taipower) is releasing procurement under its grid resilience program, which is designed to strengthen the national power network.

  The company said its current order backlog stands at more than NT$13 billion, with visibility — meaning confirmed or highly visible order flow — extending to 2029.

  ## What it means for the sector

  Yali's results illustrate how demand from AI infrastructure and domestic grid investment is adding a durable layer of orders on top of the semiconductor-driven work that has historically anchored its business. Order visibility stretching three years out reduces near-term revenue uncertainty and provides a base for production planning.

  The company is scheduled to discuss its full third-quarter financial results and operating outlook in detail at its November 20 investor conference.

  ## 前三季績效寫下歷史紀錄

  台灣電力設備廠商亞力於11月20日舉行線上法人說明會，公布2026年前三季合併營收與獲利均創下歷史新高，且訂單能見度已延伸至2029年。

  2026年前三季，亞力合併營收達新台幣79.94億元，營業利益新台幣8.09億元，稅後淨利新台幣6.74億元，三項指標均為1至9月同期歷史最高。前三季每股盈餘（EPS，即稅後淨利除以流通在外股數）達新台幣2.44元，較上年同期的2.14元成長，同樣創下同期新高。

  ## 第三季：匯率逆風拖累每股盈餘小幅下滑

  單季表現方面，亞力第三季每股盈餘為同期歷史次高。第三季合併營收為新台幣26.49億元，營業利益新台幣2.83億元，稅後淨利新台幣2.29億元。第三季每股盈餘為新台幣0.83元，較上年同期的0.88元略有下降。

  對於第三季每股盈餘年比小幅下滑，亞力將主因歸結於外匯因素：公司表示，第三季毛利率與營業利益均較去年同期成長，但匯兌利益減少新台幣3,000萬元，是此次每股盈餘小幅下滑的主要原因。從前三季累計角度來看，毛利率的整體改善已足以抵消上述逆風，使前三季每股盈餘得以刷新紀錄。

  ## 三大需求動能支撐訂單簿

  亞力表示，目前訂單來源可區分為三個面向。

  其一，半導體產業持續推動新晶圓廠興建與產能擴充，穩定帶來電力設備訂單流。其二，輝達（NVIDIA）旗下在拉丁美洲運營的一家子公司已下訂AI資料中心（AIDC）基礎設施相關訂單。其三，台灣電力公司（台電）依照電網韌性計畫陸續釋出採購案，目的在於強化全國電力網路的穩定性。

  亞力表示，目前在手訂單逾新台幣130億元，訂單能見度——即已確認或高度可見的訂單量——已延伸至2029年。

  ## 對產業的意涵

  亞力的成績單顯示，AI基礎設施需求與國內電網投資，正在原有半導體業務之上為公司疊加出一層具持續性的訂單基礎，而半導體業務向來是亞力營運的核心支柱。訂單能見度長達三年，大幅降低近期營收的不確定性，也為生產排程提供了穩固的規劃基礎。

  亞力預計於11月20日的法說會上，就第三季完整財務成果與營運展望進行詳細說明。
