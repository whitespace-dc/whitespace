body_en: |
  The European Commission and the European Investment Bank have approved €2.5bn in grants covering 51 energy projects across 11 lower-income EU member states, with heat pump installations in Czechia and Croatia among the leading recipients, according to the Cooling Post. The money flows through the EU Modernisation Fund, a mechanism financed by revenues from the EU Emissions Trading System (ETS), the bloc's carbon market.

  The latest round brings total Modernisation Fund disbursements to €23.2bn since the programme began in January 2021.

  ## Country allocations in this round

  Romania received the largest share at €636.9m, followed by Hungary at €552.3m and Czechia at €516.8m. Other allocations:

  - Poland: €180m
  - Lithuania: €169m
  - Greece: €233.9m
  - Croatia: €109m
  - Portugal: €81.4m
  - Estonia: €44.8m
  - Latvia: €40m
  - Slovenia: €20.2m

  ## Czechia: coal-to-heat-pump conversions

  Several of Czechia's funded projects target direct replacement of coal-fired district heating infrastructure with industrial heat pumps.

  The Mělník power plant in Hodonin received the largest individual Czech allocation in this round: two separate grants totalling more than €28m. Nearly €23.7m will fund partial replacement of the coal-fired plant with an industrial heat pump or a cascade of multiple units delivering approximately 25 megawatts of thermal output (MWt). A further €4.6m will fund a separate 5 MWt industrial heat pump to supply the district heating (DH) network.

  The Mařatice district heating plant in Uherské Hradiště was awarded nearly €4.3m to install two large-capacity industrial air-to-water heat pumps and heat storage, replacing its existing coal-burning plant.

  In the city of Litovel, a €4.26m grant will fund an energy centre supplying the Unicovská and Vítězná district heating systems. That project plans to use heat pumps drawing energy from the municipal wastewater treatment plant, combined with heat storage, replacing existing gas boilers.

  ## Croatia: parks modernisation and refrigerant transition

  Croatia's €109m allocation spans two distinct heat pump-related workstreams.

  An €18m grant covers the modernisation of energy and transportation systems at public institutions managing Croatia's national and nature parks. The scope includes heating and cooling solutions based on renewable sources — notably heat pumps — alongside rooftop and car-park photovoltaic installations and associated energy storage equipment.

  A separate €35m grant targets the replacement of inefficient refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat pump systems to support Croatia's implementation of the European Green Deal. The investment covers a 2026-2028 programme to retire systems typically older than ten years and replace them with modern, energy-efficient equipment using so-called natural refrigerants — substances with lower global-warming potential (GWP) than the high-GWP synthetic refrigerants prevalent in older installations.

  ## Significance

  The disbursement illustrates how ETS carbon revenues are being recycled into decarbonisation capital for the EU's less-wealthy member states, with industrial heat pumps emerging as a primary technology for retiring coal and gas from district heating networks. The Czechia projects in particular represent a concrete pipeline of coal-to-heat-pump conversions at the municipal scale, while Croatia's refrigerant-replacement scheme signals a parallel push to clean up the existing installed base of cooling and HVAC equipment ahead of tightening EU refrigerant regulations.

  The 2026-2028 timeframe specified for Croatia's refrigerant transition programme marks the nearest forward date established by this round of funding.

  ## 歐盟現代化基金撥付25億歐元，助11國推動熱泵與潔淨能源

  據《Cooling Post》報導，歐盟執委會與歐洲投資銀行已批准25億歐元補助款，涵蓋11個中低收入歐盟成員國的51項能源計畫，其中捷克與克羅埃西亞的熱泵安裝項目尤為突出。資金透過歐盟現代化基金撥付，該機制以歐盟排放交易體系（ETS）碳市場收益作為財源。

  本輪撥付完成後，現代化基金自2021年1月啟動以來的累計撥付總額已達232億歐元。

  ## 本輪各國分配金額

  羅馬尼亞獲得本輪最高分配額，達6億3,690萬歐元；匈牙利以5億5,230萬歐元居次；捷克獲得5億1,680萬歐元排名第三。其他成員國分配金額如下：

  - 波蘭：1億8,000萬歐元
  - 立陶宛：1億6,900萬歐元
  - 希臘：2億3,390萬歐元
  - 克羅埃西亞：1億900萬歐元
  - 葡萄牙：8,140萬歐元
  - 愛沙尼亞：4,480萬歐元
  - 拉脫維亞：4,000萬歐元
  - 斯洛維尼亞：2,020萬歐元

  ## 捷克：燃煤轉型熱泵計畫

  捷克獲資助的多個項目，目標直接以工業熱泵取代燃煤區域供熱基礎設施。

  位於霍多寧的梅爾尼克電廠（Mělník power plant）獲得本輪捷克單項最高補助，兩筆補助款合計逾2,800萬歐元。其中近2,370萬歐元用於以工業熱泵或多組串聯機組，局部取代現有燃煤電廠，預計可提供約25百萬瓦熱功率（MWt）的輸出；另有460萬歐元用於另設一組5 MWt工業熱泵，以供應區域供熱（DH）網路。

  位於烏赫爾斯凱赫拉季什捷（Uherské Hradiště）的馬拉提采（Mařatice）區域供熱廠，獲批近430萬歐元，用於安裝兩台大容量工業空氣源熱水熱泵及熱能儲存設備，取代現有燃煤設施。

  利托維爾市（Litovel）則獲得426萬歐元補助，用於建設一座能源中心，為烏尼可夫斯卡（Unicovská）及維捷茲納（Vítězná）兩個區域供熱系統供能。該計畫擬利用市政污水處理廠的熱能驅動熱泵，並配合熱能儲存設備，取代現有燃氣鍋爐。

  ## 克羅埃西亞：國家公園現代化與冷媒轉型

  克羅埃西亞獲得的1億900萬歐元，涵蓋兩條不同的熱泵相關工作主軸。

  其中1,800萬歐元用於克羅埃西亞各國家公園與自然公園管理機構的能源及交通系統現代化。計畫範疇包括以再生能源為基礎的冷暖氣解決方案——尤以熱泵為主——並同時安裝屋頂及停車場太陽能光電系統及相應的能源儲存設備。

  另一筆3,500萬歐元的補助，目標是汰換低效率的冷凍、空調及熱泵系統，以支持克羅埃西亞落實歐洲綠色政綱。此投資計畫涵蓋2026至2028年的執行期程，重點汰換通常已超過十年的老舊設備，改裝採用「天然冷媒」的現代高效設備——所謂天然冷媒，是指全球暖化潛勢（GWP）遠低於舊式裝置中常見高GWP合成冷媒的物質。

  ## 意義

  本輪撥付清楚呈現出ETS碳市場收益如何被循環再投入，為歐盟較低收入成員國提供去碳化所需資本，而工業熱泵正逐漸成為區域供熱系統汰除燃煤與燃氣的核心技術。捷克各項計畫尤其代表了市政層級燃煤轉型熱泵的具體建設管線，克羅埃西亞的冷媒汰換計畫則顯示出另一條平行推進的路徑——在歐盟冷媒法規日益收緊之前，積極整頓現有冷氣及暖通空調設備的既有裝機基礎。

  克羅埃西亞冷媒轉型計畫所訂定的2026至2028年執行期程，是本輪資金核定中最具體的近期執行時間表。
