body_en: |
  Carbon dioxide emissions from the U.S. electric power sector rose 4%, or 58 million metric tons (MMmt), in 2025, driven by a combination of record electricity generation and a significant increase in coal-fired power, the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) said Tuesday.

  Total energy-related CO2 emissions across all sectors climbed 2%, or roughly 115 MMmt, over the same period compared with 2024.

  ## Generation hits a new record

  Net generation in the electric power sector grew 3%, adding 12 terawatt-hours (TWh) — enough to supply millions of homes — and surpassed 2024's record annual output, the EIA said. Two forces drove that growth: an unusually hot summer that pushed space-cooling demand to record summer peak levels, and sustained increases in electricity consumption from data centers and manufacturing facilities.

  ## Coal rebounds sharply

  Coal-fired generation rose 13% in 2025, adding 78 MMmt of CO2 emissions — the single largest contributor to the sector's emissions increase. Natural gas-fired generation, by contrast, fell 4%, reducing emissions by 23 MMmt.

  Wind generation grew 3% and solar generation expanded 34%, both of which helped offset some of the gains from coal, the EIA said, but not enough to prevent an overall increase in power-sector emissions.

  ## Broader energy picture

  Outside the power sector, emissions growth was limited. The EIA said emissions increases in the industrial and transportation sectors were small, with energy efficiency improvements and shifts to less carbon-intensive fuels curbing expansion in those areas.

  ## What it means

  The 2025 data show that demand growth — from weather and from large electricity consumers such as data centers — is placing upward pressure on emissions even as renewable generation expands. The rebound in coal use, which more than offset natural gas reductions, underscores that the fuel mix responding to peak demand still carries significant carbon intensity. The EIA's findings come as grid planners and policymakers weigh how to meet rising electricity demand while managing emissions trajectories.

  Solar generation's 34% year-over-year increase was the fastest growth rate among any generation source tracked in the report.
body_zh: |
  美國能源資訊局（EIA）週二表示，2025年美國電力部門二氧化碳排放量增加4%，亦即5,800萬公噸（MMmt），主要驅動因素為創紀錄的發電量，以及燃煤發電量的大幅成長。

  同期間，涵蓋所有部門的整體能源相關二氧化碳排放量，相較於2024年攀升2%，約達1億1,500萬公噸。

  ## 發電量創歷史新高

  EIA表示，電力部門淨發電量成長3%，新增12兆瓦時（TWh）——足以供應數百萬戶家庭用電——並超越2024年的年度發電量紀錄。推動此一成長的力量來自兩方面：異常酷熱的夏季將空調降溫需求推升至歷史夏季尖峰水準，以及資料中心與製造業設施的用電量持續擴增。

  ## 燃煤發電大幅反彈

  2025年燃煤發電量增加13%，新增7,800萬公噸二氧化碳排放，是電力部門排放量增加的最大單一來源。相形之下，天然氣發電量下滑4%，排放量因此減少2,300萬公噸。

  EIA指出，風力發電量成長3%，太陽能發電量擴張34%，兩者雖部分抵銷了燃煤增排的影響，但仍不足以阻止電力部門整體排放量的上升。

  ## 整體能源排放概況

  電力部門以外，排放量的成長相對有限。EIA表示，工業與交通運輸部門的排放量增幅不大，能源效率提升以及轉向碳排強度較低的燃料，抑制了這些領域的排放擴張。

  ## 數據意涵

  2025年的數據顯示，來自氣候因素與資料中心等大型用電戶的需求成長，正對排放量形成上行壓力，即便再生能源發電量持續擴張亦然。燃煤用量的反彈幅度超過天然氣的減量，凸顯出因應尖峰需求的燃料組合仍具相當高的碳排強度。EIA調查結果發布之際，正值電網規劃者與政策制定者積極思考如何在滿足日益攀升的電力需求的同時，有效管控排放軌跡。

  在報告追蹤的所有發電來源中，太陽能發電量年增34%，是成長速度最快的能源。
