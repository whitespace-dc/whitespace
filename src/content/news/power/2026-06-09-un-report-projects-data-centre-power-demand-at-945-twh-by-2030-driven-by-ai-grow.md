body_en: |
  A United Nations research institute projects that global data centre electricity consumption will reach 945 terawatt-hours (TWh) annually by 2030 — nearly 3% of projected global electricity demand — with artificial intelligence identified as a primary driver, while separate developments this week signal mounting cybersecurity risks from contactless payment fraud and advancing technology activity across energy planning, healthcare and regional corporate appointments.

  ## UN Report: AI's Growing Energy and Environmental Footprint

  The United Nations University Institute for Water, Environment and Health (UNU-INWEH), marking its 30th anniversary, published a report warning that data centres could consume 945 TWh by 2030. The institute described that figure as nearly triple the combined annual electricity use of Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria, countries together home to more than 650 million people. Associated carbon emissions would reach 399 million tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) — a volume requiring 6.7 billion trees grown over 10 years to offset, roughly twice the number of trees in the United Kingdom, the report states.

  AI is identified as the most significant growth driver. In 2025, AI workloads accounted for approximately 20% of total data centre electricity use, according to the report. If that share rises to 40% by 2030 as projected, AI's electricity demand alone could reach around 378 TWh — enough to meet the residential electricity needs of the entire population of Sub-Saharan Africa for more than two years, the institute said.

  The report extends its analysis beyond power consumption and carbon. It estimates that data centres could consume 9.3 trillion litres of water annually, require more than 14,000 square kilometres of land for electricity generation infrastructure, and generate up to 2.5 million tonnes of electronic waste — equivalent in mass to 250 Eiffel Towers — each year by 2030.

  The authors warn that measuring sustainability through a single metric such as carbon output can obscure trade-offs in water and land use, shifting environmental burdens onto regions already under resource pressure. The report calls for mandatory environmental reporting, resource management standards and international cooperation to address the unequal distribution of AI's benefits and environmental costs.

  ## NFC Payment Scams Rise 188%

  Cybersecurity company Kaspersky reported that attacks targeting Android users through near-field communication (NFC) technology — the radio standard that enables contactless payments — rose 188% in the first four months of 2026 compared with the same period a year earlier. Its security products blocked 35,600 NFC-related malware attacks between January and April 2026, up from more than 12,300 in the same window of 2025.

  Kaspersky said fraudsters are deploying malicious applications to steal payment card data or manipulate victims into transferring money through contactless payment systems. Attacks typically begin when users are persuaded via messaging platforms to install fake applications. A newer variant — in which victims are directed to make deposits at automated teller machines (ATMs) using infected smartphones — is growing in prevalence and is harder to detect, the company said. Kaspersky advised users to avoid installing applications from unofficial sources and to treat unsolicited banking or payment instructions with caution.

  ## Google Adds Fake Call Detection to Android

  Google is rolling out a feature called fake call detection to Android devices globally starting in June, beginning with Pixel smartphones and extending to Android 12 and later devices running the Phone by Google application. The feature is designed to counter scams that combine caller identification spoofing — displaying a false number to the recipient — with AI-generated voice cloning to impersonate people in a victim's contact list.

  The tool works by verifying whether a call claiming to originate from a saved contact is actually being placed from that person's device. If verification fails, the user receives an on-screen warning. Google said the feature uses encrypted Rich Communication Services (RCS) — a messaging protocol designed to replace traditional SMS — and operates automatically in the background without user intervention. It can be disabled through the Phone by Google application settings.

  ## TransitionZero Extends Scenario Builder Across ASEAN

  Climate technology non-profit TransitionZero has expanded its cloud-based energy modelling platform, Scenario Builder, to cover multi-country analysis across all 10 members of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN): Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, Brunei, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia and the Philippines. The updated platform integrates 25 nodes in a single model, allowing users to analyse cross-border electricity generation, transmission and trade flows simultaneously.

  TransitionZero said the expansion is intended to support planning for the region's growing cross-border transmission projects and electricity trading arrangements. Scenario Builder is a long-term capacity expansion and dispatch modelling tool aimed at energy planners, policymakers and analysts assessing regional and national energy scenarios.

  ## MedPlanner Launches AI Scribe and Patient Assistant

  Malaysian healthcare technology company MedPlanner Sdn Bhd launched two AI-powered products: HEMI Health, an AI medical scribe for clinicians, and AskHEMI, a multilingual AI health assistant for patients and carers. Both are built on the company's proprietary Health Medical Intelligence (HEMI) engine and join the existing clinical coordination platform HEMI Teams.

  HEMI Health assists clinicians with documentation tasks including recording clinical reasoning, handling complaint responses and maintaining medicolegal records. It is available to healthcare professionals in Malaysia and the United Kingdom. AskHEMI, available on iOS and Android, provides health information and guidance outside of clinical appointments.

  To support its UK and European expansion, MedPlanner has incorporated a subsidiary, HEMI Ltd, in London to manage commercial and partnership activities in those markets. The company is scheduled to present its products at NHS ConfedExpo — a conference for National Health Service leaders — in Manchester on June 10 and 11.

  ## 聯合國報告：AI的能源與環境足跡日益擴大

  聯合國大學水、環境與健康研究所（UNU-INWEH）在迎接創立30週年之際，發布報告警告：全球資料中心用電量可能於2030年達到945太瓦時。研究所指出，這個數字幾乎是巴基斯坦、孟加拉和奈及利亞三國年用電量總和的三倍，而這三國人口合計逾6億5,000萬人。報告指出，相關碳排放量將達到3億9,900萬公噸二氧化碳當量（CO₂e）——若要抵銷這些排放，需要67億棵樹木生長10年，相當於英國境內樹木總數的約兩倍。

  人工智慧被認定為最主要的成長驅動因素。報告指出，2025年AI工作負載約佔資料中心總用電量的20%。若該比例如預測般於2030年上升至40%，AI的用電需求單項即可達到約378 TWh——足以供應撒哈拉以南非洲全體居民的住宅用電逾兩年，研究所表示。

  報告的分析範疇不僅限於電力消耗與碳排放，更延伸至其他資源面向。報告估計，到2030年，資料中心每年可能消耗9.3兆公升的水，發電基礎設施所需土地面積將超過1萬4,000平方公里，並可能產生多達250萬公噸的電子廢棄物——以重量計，相當於250座艾菲爾鐵塔。

  報告作者警告，若僅以碳排放等單一指標衡量永續性，可能掩蓋水資源與土地利用之間的取捨矛盾，將環境負擔轉嫁至原本已承受資源壓力的地區。報告呼籲強制推行環境資訊揭露、建立資源管理標準，並透過國際合作解決AI效益與環境成本分配不均的問題。

  ## NFC支付詐騙攻擊暴增188%

  網路安全公司卡巴斯基（Kaspersky）報告指出，2026年前四個月，針對Android用戶、透過近場通訊（NFC）技術發動的攻擊——NFC即支援非接觸式支付的無線電標準——較前一年同期暴增188%。其安全產品在2026年1月至4月間攔截了3萬5,600起NFC相關惡意軟體攻擊，高於2025年同期逾1萬2,300起。

  卡巴斯基表示，詐騙者正部署惡意應用程式，竊取支付卡資料，或透過非接觸式支付系統誘騙受害者轉帳。攻擊通常始於用戶被說服透過通訊平台安裝假冒應用程式。該公司指出，一種較新的變體手法正日益盛行且更難被察覺——受害者被引導使用遭植入惡意程式的智慧型手機，前往自動提款機（ATM）存款。卡巴斯基建議用戶避免從非官方來源安裝應用程式，並對任何主動傳送的銀行或支付操作指示保持高度警覺。

  ## Google為Android推出偽造來電偵測功能

  Google即將於6月起在全球Android裝置上陸續推出「偽造來電偵測」功能，首先從Pixel智慧型手機起步，並擴展至搭載Android 12及後續版本、且執行「Google電話」應用程式的裝置。這項功能旨在對抗結合來電號碼偽冒（即向受話方顯示虛假號碼）與AI語音複製技術、假冒受害者通訊錄中聯絡人的詐騙手法。

  此工具的運作方式是驗證一通聲稱來自已儲存聯絡人的電話，是否確實從該人的裝置撥出。若驗證失敗，用戶將在螢幕上收到警告提示。Google表示，這項功能採用加密的RCS（一種旨在取代傳統SMS的訊息通訊協定）運作，並在背景自動執行，無需用戶手動介入。用戶可透過「Google電話」應用程式的設定關閉此功能。

  ## TransitionZero將情境建置工具擴展至全體東協成員國

  氣候科技非營利組織TransitionZero已將其雲端能源建模平台「情境建置工具」（Scenario Builder）的分析範圍，擴展至涵蓋東南亞國家協會（東協）全體10個成員國，包括印尼、新加坡、馬來西亞、汶萊、緬甸、泰國、越南、寮國、柬埔寨及菲律賓，支援跨國分析。更新後的平台在單一模型中整合25個節點，讓用戶能夠同步分析跨境電力發電、輸電與電力交易流量。

  TransitionZero表示，此次擴展旨在支援該地區日益增多的跨境輸電計畫與電力交易安排的規劃工作。情境建置工具是一套長期容量擴張與調度建模工具，目標使用對象為評估區域及國家能源情境的能源規劃者、政策制定者與分析師。

  ## MedPlanner推出AI抄寫員與病患健康助理

  馬來西亞醫療科技公司MedPlanner私人有限公司（MedPlanner Sdn Bhd）正式推出兩款AI驅動產品：供臨床醫師使用的AI醫療抄寫員HEMI Health，以及面向病患與照護者的多語言AI健康助理AskHEMI。兩款產品均建構於該公司自有的健康醫療智慧（HEMI）引擎之上，並與現有的臨床協調平台HEMI Teams整合。

  HEMI Health協助臨床醫師處理文書工作，包括記錄臨床推理過程、處理申訴回覆及維護醫療法律紀錄，目前已開放馬來西亞與英國的醫療專業人員使用。AskHEMI在iOS與Android平台上架，於正式醫療預約以外的時間提供健康資訊與指引。

  為支援在英國與歐洲的業務拓展，MedPlanner已在倫敦設立子公司HEMI Ltd，負責管理當地市場的商務與夥伴合作事宜。該公司預計於6月10日至11日出席在曼徹斯特舉辦的NHS ConfedExpo——一場面向英國國家醫療服務體系（NHS）領導人的研討會——展示其產品。
