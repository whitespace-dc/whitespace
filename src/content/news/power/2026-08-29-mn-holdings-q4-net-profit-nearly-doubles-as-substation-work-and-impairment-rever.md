body_en: |
  MN Holdings Bhd, a Malaysian infrastructure utilities construction and engineering company, reported fourth-quarter net profit of RM23.98 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, a 98.8% increase from RM12.06 million in the same period a year earlier, according to a bourse filing released Wednesday.

  Quarterly revenue rose 17.9% year-on-year to RM211.47 million from RM179.42 million. The substation engineering segment — which designs and builds the high-voltage switching stations that connect power generation to transmission grids — posted a 24% rise in revenue contribution, which the company attributed to higher work progress and increased project activity.

  The bottom line also benefited from a net reversal of impairment losses on financial assets and contract assets of RM2.02 million, compared with net impairment losses of RM9.72 million in the fourth quarter of the prior financial year, a swing of roughly RM11.74 million that amplified the profit gain.

  No dividend was declared with the quarterly results.

  ## Full-year results

  For the full financial year ended June 30, 2026, MN Holdings recorded net profit of RM92.14 million, nearly double the prior year's figure and described by the company as a record high. Full-year revenue reached RM869.19 million, a 60.8% increase year-on-year.

  ## Data centre demand cited as demand driver

  MN Holdings pointed to the continued expansion of data centre developments in Malaysia as a major demand catalyst for the country's power infrastructure outlook. Data centres require large, reliable power connections and are among the primary customers for new substation and grid capacity.

  "The expected electricity demand growth is expected to support capital expenditures related to transmission reinforcement, substation expansion, grid interconnection and related power engineering works," the company said in its filing.

  The group's order book stood at RM1.64 billion as of the filing date, spanning power, renewable energy, data centre, and water infrastructure segments. MN Holdings said it remains cautiously optimistic and will focus on timely project execution, selective tender participation, cost discipline, and order book replenishment.

  ## Market reaction

  Shares of MN Holdings closed nine sen, or 2.47%, lower at RM3.56 on Wednesday, giving the company a market capitalisation of RM2.39 billion.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞基礎建設公用事業營建與工程公司MN Holdings Bhd，根據週三發布的交易所申報文件，截至2026年6月30日止三個月的第四季淨利為2,398萬馬幣，較去年同期的1,206萬馬幣大幅成長98.8%。

  單季營收年增17.9%，由1億7,942萬馬幣成長至2億1,147萬馬幣。變電站工程業務——負責設計與建造連結發電端與輸電網路的高壓開關站——營收貢獻年增24%，公司將此歸因於工程進度加快及專案活動增加。

  在獲利面上，金融資產及合約資產的減損損失淨迴轉金額為202萬馬幣，而上一財年第四季則認列972萬馬幣的淨減損損失，兩者之間約1,174萬馬幣的顯著轉變，進一步放大了整體獲利成長幅度。

  本季業績未宣告配發任何股利。

  ## 全年業績

  截至2026年6月30日止完整財政年度，MN Holdings淨利達9,214萬馬幣，幾乎較前一年翻倍，公司形容此為歷史新高。全年營收達8億6,919萬馬幣，年增60.8%。

  ## 資料中心需求成為主要市場動能

  MN Holdings將馬來西亞資料中心建設的持續擴張，列為帶動該國電力基礎建設前景的主要需求催化劑。資料中心需要大量且穩定可靠的電力供應，是新建變電站及電網容量的主要需求客群之一。

  公司在申報文件中表示：「預期電力需求的增長，將支撐與輸電強化、變電站擴建、電網互聯及相關電力工程作業相關的資本支出。」

  截至申報日，集團在手訂單達16.4億馬幣，涵蓋電力、再生能源、資料中心及水利基礎建設等業務板塊。MN Holdings表示，公司態度審慎樂觀，將聚焦於如期執行專案、選擇性參與投標、落實成本紀律，以及持續補充在手訂單。

  ## 市場反應

  MN Holdings股票週三收盤下跌9仙，跌幅2.47%，收於每股3.56馬幣，公司市值約23.9億馬幣。
