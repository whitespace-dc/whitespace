body_en: |
  The U.S. Department of Energy released a draft National Transmission Needs Study on July 9, 2026, finding that transmission congestion added $12 billion in wholesale electricity costs in 2024 and that new interregional transfer capacity — links connecting separate grid regions — represents the highest-potential solution to easing U.S. grid bottlenecks.

  ## Congestion costs and their trajectory

  Total U.S. transmission congestion costs rose to $12 billion in 2024 from $11 billion in 2023, according to the DOE draft report. The 2024 figure remains below the $21 billion recorded in 2022, a year the department attributed to high natural gas prices and severe weather. DOE said it expects congestion costs to continue increasing.

  Congestion costs represent the premium wholesale electricity buyers pay when physical constraints on transmission lines prevent power from flowing from lower-cost generators to load centers, forcing grid operators to dispatch more expensive local generation instead.

  ## Where new capacity would produce the most value

  Locational marginal prices — the real-time cost of electricity at specific grid nodes, used to identify where transmission constraints are most economically damaging — indicate that new links between the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) and its neighbors and between the Eastern and Western interconnections could produce the largest reductions in congestion costs, according to analysis DOE cited from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.

  ERCOT operates an electrically isolated grid covering most of Texas, with only limited connections to neighboring systems. The Eastern and Western interconnections are the two large synchronized power grids that span the contiguous U.S. outside Texas; they currently have limited transfer capacity between them.

  In the West, additional capacity between NorthernGrid and WestConnect — two transmission planning regions — could also yield significant savings, the draft report said. In the Northeast, new transfer capacity between ISO New England (ISO-NE) and the New York Independent System Operator (NYISO) was identified as another high-value opportunity.

  ## Southeast identified as the lagging region

  The Southeast is the only U.S. region that failed to address at least one of the four key transmission needs DOE assessed. Those categories are: improved reliability and resilience; reduced grid congestion within regions; improved transfer capacity with neighboring regions; and resource adequacy through interregional transfer capacity.

  DOE's analysis found the Southeast would benefit from increased transmission capacity with neighboring regions by roughly $10 per megawatt-hour (MWh) on average. A megawatt-hour is the standard unit of wholesale electricity volume — enough to power roughly 1,000 homes for one hour — and the $10/MWh figure represents the average price differential that additional transmission connections could eliminate. The region lacks an organized wholesale power market or publicly available locational marginal price data, which complicated the analysis, DOE noted.

  The three interregional corridors with the highest estimated congestion value in the Southeast in 2022 and 2023 were: the link between Southern Company and Florida; the connection between Duke Energy and PJM Interconnection, the large mid-Atlantic and Midwest grid operator; and the path between the Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) and the Midcontinent Independent System Operator's (MISO) southern region.

  ## Nine years of transmission investment

  For the period 2016 through 2024, the DOE found that roughly 150 miles of interregional transmission was built annually across the U.S. Incumbent utilities — established local grid owners — developed 98% of all transmission during that period.

  By region, ERCOT built the most transmission at 2,400 circuit miles over the nine years. PJM's eastern region spent the most on transmission investment at $3.5 billion per year. ISO-NE carried the highest load-weighted cost for transmission investment at $9 per MWh.

  ## Industry significance and next steps

  The findings carry direct consequences for grid planners, developers, and large power consumers. The DOE's identification of specific interregional corridors with high congestion value is intended to inform regional and interregional transmission planning processes, which determine which projects receive regulatory approval and cost allocation. The Southeast's failure to meet all four transmission need categories — while also lacking the market structures that generate LMP data — may intensify pressure on utilities and state regulators in that region to expand both market participation and cross-border grid investment.

  The National Transmission Needs Study is produced every three years. DOE is accepting public comments on the draft through September 7, 2026.
body_zh: |
  美國能源部（DOE）2026年7月9日公布《國家輸電需求研究》草案，發現2024年輸電壅塞使躉售電力成本增加120億美元，並指出新增跨區輸電容量——即連結不同電網區域的輸電線路——是紓解美國電網瓶頸最具潛力的解方。東南部則是全美唯一被認定未能解決至少一項關鍵輸電需求的地區。

  ## 壅塞成本與趨勢走向

  根據能源部草案報告，全美輸電壅塞總成本從2023年的110億美元上升至2024年的120億美元。2024年的數字仍低於2022年所創的210億美元高峰——能源部將當年的異常高點歸因於天然氣價格飆漲及嚴酷天氣。能源部預期壅塞成本將持續攀升。

  壅塞成本反映的是，當輸電線路出現物理限制、電力無法從低成本發電廠順暢輸往用電中心時，電網調度業者被迫啟用更昂貴的在地發電機組，而躉售電力買方因此須額外支付的溢價。

  ## 新增容量最具效益之處

  根據能源部引用勞倫斯柏克萊國家實驗室（Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory）的分析，地點邊際電價（LMP）——即電網各節點的即時電力成本，用以識別輸電限制在經濟面最具殺傷力之處——顯示，在德克薩斯電力可靠性委員會（ERCOT）與其鄰近系統之間，以及在東部與西部互聯電網之間新增輸電連結，可帶來最大幅度的壅塞成本下降效果。

  ERCOT營運著涵蓋德克薩斯大部分地區的電力孤立電網，僅透過極為有限的連線與鄰近系統相接。東部與西部互聯電網是涵蓋德克薩斯以外美國本土的兩大同步電網，目前兩者之間的轉輸容量相當有限。

  在西部，北方電網（NorthernGrid）與西聯電網（WestConnect）這兩個輸電規劃區域之間若增加容量，亦可帶來顯著節省，草案報告指出。在東北部，新英格蘭獨立系統營運商（ISO-NE）與紐約獨立系統營運商（NYISO）之間的新增轉輸容量，也被列為另一個高效益機會。

  ## 東南部：進度落後的地區

  東南部是全美唯一未能解決能源部評估之四大關鍵輸電需求中至少一項的地區。這四大類別分別為：提升可靠性與韌性、降低區域內電網壅塞、改善與鄰近地區的轉輸容量，以及透過跨區轉輸容量確保電源充裕度。

  能源部分析發現，東南部若能增加與鄰近地區的輸電容量，平均可受益約每百萬瓦時（MWh）10美元。MWh是躉售電力的標準計量單位——足以供約1,000戶家庭使用一小時——而每MWh 10美元的數字，代表額外輸電連線可望消弭的平均電價差距。能源部指出，該地區缺乏有組織的躉售電力市場，也沒有公開的地點邊際電價資料，增加了分析難度。

  2022年與2023年東南部壅塞價值最高的三條跨區走廊分別為：南方電力公司（Southern Company）與佛羅里達之間的連線；杜克能源（Duke Energy）與PJM互聯電網（PJM Interconnection，即橫跨大西洋中部及中西部的大型電網營運商）之間的通路；以及田納西河谷管理局（TVA）與中部大陸獨立系統營運商（MISO）南部區域之間的輸電路徑。

  ## 九年輸電投資概況

  能源部發現，2016年至2024年間，全美每年平均新建約150英里的跨區輸電線路。在此期間，既有公用事業——即地方已有的電網業者——包辦了全美98%的輸電建設。

  以地區論，ERCOT在這九年間建設了2,400電路英里的輸電線路，居全美之冠。PJM東部地區每年輸電投資金額最高，達35億美元。ISO-NE的負載加權輸電投資成本最高，達每MWh 9美元。

  ## 產業意涵與後續步驟

  上述研究結果對電網規劃業者、開發商及大型電力用戶均具有直接影響。能源部點名特定跨區走廊的高壅塞成本，旨在為區域及跨區輸電規劃程序提供參考依據——這些程序決定哪些項目得以取得監管核准及成本分攤資格。東南部在未能達到全部四大輸電需求類別之際，又欠缺能產生地點邊際電價資料的市場結構，可能進一步加大外界對該地區公用事業者及州級監管機關的壓力，要求其擴大市場參與程度並強化跨境電網投資。
