body_en: |
  PLN, Indonesia's state electricity utility, had advanced 9.2 gigawatts (GW) of hydropower projects into active execution as of July 2026, representing 79 percent of the 11.7 GW of large-scale and mini hydroelectric capacity the company is targeting under its 2025–2034 electricity supply business plan, known as the RUPTL.

  The 9.2 GW encompasses 315 projects developed by PLN alongside independent power producers (IPPs). Of that total, 6.8 GW is in the procurement stage, 1.4 GW is under construction, and 570 megawatts (MW) has entered commercial operation.

  Suroso Isnandar, PLN's Director of Project Management and New and Renewable Energy, disclosed the figures at the Indonesia Hydropower Summit 2026, held at PLN's Jakarta headquarters on August 5, 2026, according to a written statement received by Kompas.com on August 14.

  ## Stakeholder coordination

  Suroso identified multi-party collaboration as the central requirement for accelerating development across projects that vary widely in scale and complexity. The parties involved include the government, PLN, IPPs, and institutions such as METI (Masyarakat Energi Baru Terbarukan Indonesia, Indonesia's New and Renewable Energy Society). "PLN is not alone in carrying out this mandate. Collaboration is the key to making this program a success," he said. "We need strong synergy, strong commitment, and active contribution from all stakeholders."

  Suroso described hydropower as "one of the important pillars in strengthening resilience and supporting Indonesia's energy transition."

  ## Pumped storage program

  Beyond conventional hydro and mini hydro, PLN's RUPTL 2025–2034 also targets approximately 4.3 GW of pumped storage hydropower. Pumped storage uses surplus electricity to pump water into an elevated reservoir, then releases it through turbines when electricity demand rises, functioning as large-scale grid storage. PLN said the technology is intended to maintain flexibility and reliability in the electricity system, and the company is developing several pumped storage projects to support a renewable energy-based grid.

  ## Scale and pipeline status

  The combined water-based generation and storage program in the RUPTL — 11.7 GW of conventional and mini hydro plus 4.3 GW of pumped storage — totals approximately 16 GW over the 10-year plan period. With 9.2 GW of the hydro portion in execution, the 6.8 GW currently in procurement represents the largest share yet to advance to construction.

  ## PLN推動92億瓦水力發電專案納入執行，完成十年計畫目標79%

  印尼國家電力公司PLN截至2026年7月，已將9.2吉瓦（GW）的水力發電專案納入積極執行階段，相當於該公司依據2025至2034年電力供應業務計畫（RUPTL）所設定的大型與迷你水力發電容量目標11.7吉瓦的79%。

  這9.2吉瓦涵蓋PLN與獨立電力業者（IPP）共同開發的315個專案。其中，6.8吉瓦處於採購階段，1.4吉瓦正在施工建設，另有570百萬瓦（MW）已進入商業運轉。

  PLN專案管理暨新能源與再生能源董事蘇羅索·伊斯南達爾（Suroso Isnandar）於2026年8月5日在PLN雅加達總部舉辦的「2026年印尼水力發電高峰會」上公布上述數據，相關資訊根據《Kompas.com》於8月14日收到的書面聲明報導。

  ## 利害關係人協調

  蘇羅索表示，多方協作是加速推進各規模與複雜程度不一之專案的核心要件。涉及各方包括政府、PLN、獨立電力業者，以及印尼新能源與再生能源學會（METI，Masyarakat Energi Baru Terbarukan Indonesia）等機構。「PLN不是單打獨鬥地執行這項任務。協作才是讓計畫成功的關鍵，」他表示，「我們需要強而有力的協同合作、堅定的承諾，以及所有利害關係人的積極貢獻。」

  蘇羅索將水力發電定位為「強化韌性、支持印尼能源轉型的重要支柱之一」。

  ## 抽蓄水力計畫

  除傳統水力與迷你水力之外，PLN的RUPTL 2025至2034年計畫亦目標新增約4.3吉瓦的抽蓄水力發電容量。抽蓄水力利用電力供過於求時將水抽至高位水庫，待電力需求上升時再透過渦輪機放水發電，功能相當於大規模的電網儲能系統。PLN表示，引入此技術是為了維持電力系統的靈活性與可靠性，並正積極開發多個抽蓄水力專案，以支持以再生能源為基礎的電網建設。

  ## 規模與專案進度

  RUPTL計畫中以水資源為基礎的發電與儲能方案——包含11.7吉瓦的傳統與迷你水力，加上4.3吉瓦的抽蓄水力——在這十年計畫期間合計約達16吉瓦。目前，水力部分已有9.2吉瓦進入執行階段，而仍處於採購程序的6.8吉瓦則是尚未推進至施工建設階段的最大宗項目。
