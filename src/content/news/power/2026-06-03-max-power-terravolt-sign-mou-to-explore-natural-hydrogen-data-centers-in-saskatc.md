  ## Max Power and Terravolt Sign MoU to Explore Natural Hydrogen-Powered Data Centers in Saskatchewan

  Data center developer Max Power has signed a memorandum of understanding with Terravolt to investigate building natural hydrogen-powered data centers in Saskatchewan, Canada. The partnership is anchored to the Lawson Natural Hydrogen Complex, placing it at one of North America's more active natural-hydrogen exploration sites.

  ### What Is Natural Hydrogen?

  Natural hydrogen — sometimes called geologic or "gold" hydrogen — seeps from underground rock formations without any industrial production process. It is distinct from:

  - **Green hydrogen**, which is manufactured by electrolyzing water using renewable electricity
  - **Blue/grey hydrogen**, which is derived from natural gas reforming

  Because it requires no energy-intensive synthesis step, natural hydrogen carries the potential for significantly lower production costs and lifecycle emissions than manufactured alternatives, provided it can be extracted and conditioned at commercial scale. Saskatchewan has attracted growing attention as a prospective natural-hydrogen jurisdiction, and the Lawson complex is a focal point of that activity.

  ### The Partnership Structure

  An MoU is a non-binding letter of intent: it commits both parties to a structured exploration phase but stops short of a development or offtake agreement. In practice this stage typically covers joint feasibility studies, resource characterization, power conversion pathway selection (fuel cells, hydrogen-fired turbines, or hybrid systems), and preliminary site planning. A confirmed data center build would require subsequent binding agreements and regulatory approvals.

  ### What Data Center Planners Should Watch

  **Potential advantages if the model proves out:**
  - A dispatchable, low-carbon power source decoupled from constrained grid infrastructure — relevant in a province where transmission access for large industrial loads can be a limiting factor
  - Near-zero Scope 2 emissions for compute workloads, depending on the hydrogen-to-power conversion pathway chosen
  - On-site fuel supply that reduces exposure to wholesale electricity price volatility

  **Key risks to track:**
  - Natural hydrogen extraction at commercial scale remains largely unproven globally; the resource base at Lawson is still being characterized
  - Saskatchewan's regulatory framework for natural hydrogen production is in early development, adding permitting timeline uncertainty
  - Power conversion infrastructure for hydrogen at data center scale carries its own cost and engineering unknowns

  ### Bottom Line

  The Max Power–Terravolt MoU is an exploratory signal, not a capacity commitment. For planners watching alternative-power strategies on the Canadian prairies, the project is worth tracking: if feasibility studies yield positive results and binding agreements follow, Saskatchewan's natural hydrogen resource could support a novel class of low-carbon compute infrastructure. The next meaningful milestone will be any announcement of a formal feasibility study outcome or a move toward a development agreement.

  ## Max Power與Terravolt簽署備忘錄　探索薩斯喀徹溫省天然氫資料中心

  資料中心開發商Max Power已與Terravolt簽署合作備忘錄，研究在加拿大薩斯喀徹溫省興建以天然氫為動力的資料中心的可行性。此合作以勞森天然氫複合體（Lawson Natural Hydrogen Complex）為核心，使其成為北美天然氫探勘最活躍的地點之一。

  ### 何謂天然氫？

  天然氫（又稱地質氫或「黃金氫」）從地下岩層自然滲出，無需任何工業生產程序，有別於：

  - **綠氫**：以再生能源電力電解水製成
  - **藍氫／灰氫**：以天然氣重組製成

  由於省去耗能的合成步驟，若能在商業規模下完成開採與純化，天然氫的生產成本和全生命週期碳排放均有望顯著低於人工製氫。薩斯喀徹溫省近年已成為天然氫前景備受關注的地區，勞森複合體是當地探勘活動的焦點。

  ### 合作架構

  備忘錄屬非約束性的意向書：雙方承諾進入結構化探索階段，但尚未達成開發或供應協議。實務上，此階段通常涵蓋聯合可行性研究、資源特性評估、電力轉換路徑選擇（燃料電池、氫燃燒渦輪機或混合系統），以及初步選址規劃。資料中心的確認興建，仍有待後續簽訂具約束力的協議並完成監管審批。

  ### 資料中心規劃者的關注重點

  **若模式可行，潛在優勢包括：**
  - 提供可調度的低碳電力，不受電網傳輸容量限制——在大型工業用電接取受限的省份尤具價值
  - 視氫轉電的路徑而定，計算工作負載的範疇二碳排放可接近零
  - 自備燃料供應，降低對躉售電價波動的暴露風險

  **需持續追蹤的主要風險：**
  - 天然氫的商業規模開採在全球範圍內仍屬未成熟技術，勞森地區的資源量尚在評估中
  - 薩斯喀徹溫省天然氫生產的監管框架仍在早期發展階段，許可時程存在不確定性
  - 資料中心規模的氫能發電基礎設施在成本與工程上亦有待解決的挑戰

  ### 結語

  Max Power與Terravolt的備忘錄代表探索意向，而非容量承諾。對於關注加拿大大草原替代電力策略的規劃者而言，此案值得持續追蹤：若可行性研究得出正面結果並順利推進至開發協議，薩斯喀徹溫省的天然氫資源有望支撐一類嶄新的低碳算力基礎設施。下一個具指標意義的里程碑，將是可行性研究結果的公布，或邁向開發協議的宣示。
