body_en: |
  PT PLN Batam has formally denied that data centre activity caused a widespread power outage across Batam and Bintan islands on October 12, 2026, while national and regional police investigate unexplained anomalies at the substation where the fault began.

  Kwin Fo, director of PT PLN Batam, said reports that data centre testing or commissioning, which purportedly required very large amounts of power, had triggered the outage were "completely untrue," according to a statement cited by news agency Antara on October 18. "The widespread blackout that occurred on Monday on Batam and Bintan islands has absolutely nothing to do with data centres," Kwin said.

  ## Data centre ecosystem not yet fully operational

  Kwin said Batam's data centre ecosystem has not yet reached full operation. When it does, data centre electricity consumption is projected to remain below 10 percent of total system capacity, he said.

  Batam's power grid currently carries a supply capacity of 930 megawatts (MW, a measure of instantaneous generating power) against an active load of approximately 780 MW, leaving a reserve margin of close to 20 percent. PLN Batam said it plans to raise supply capacity to 1,300 MW by 2027 through construction of gas turbine power plants (PLTG, from the Indonesian *Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Gas*) and solar plants (PLTS), alongside Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), which store surplus electricity for release during periods of high demand or supply shortfall.

  ## Anomalies at Sei Harapan substation under police investigation

  The fault that triggered the outage originated at the Sei Harapan substation. Wild animal intrusion was initially suspected, but PLN Batam said investigators found several irregularities at the site after the electrical sparks occurred.

  Responders who arrived at the substation at 07:00 WIB (Western Indonesia Time) found no animal carcass and no sign of wildlife. An injured animal was only discovered at 10:30 WIB, more than three hours later, beneath a transformer. Separately, the electrified security wire running along the substation fence was found to be de-energised at the time of the incident.

  A third anomaly involved the site's CCTV surveillance cameras. Footage showed the cameras covering the faulted transformer had shifted position the previous evening. "Based on the recording, the camera had shifted on Sunday, at around 18:43 WIB, the day before the disruption occurred," Kwin said. "So the transformer that experienced the sparking was no longer clearly visible in the CCTV footage."

  Bareskrim Polri, Indonesia's national police criminal investigation bureau, and Polda Kepri, the regional police command for the Riau Islands, are jointly examining all evidence and CCTV recordings to establish the cause of the fault.

  ## Grid expansion planned as data centre load grows

  PLN Batam's data centre ecosystem remains below full operational capacity. The utility's planned expansion from 930 MW to 1,300 MW by 2027, using gas turbines, solar generation, and battery storage, is intended to support projected load growth, including from the data centre sector. The criminal investigation into the Sei Harapan fault remains open.

  PT PLN Batam董事長Kwin Fo表示，有關資料中心測試或試運轉因需耗用龐大電力而引發停電的說法「完全不實」，此一聲明於10月18日由印尼國家通訊社安塔拉（Antara）引述報導。Kwin表示：「週一峇淡島與民丹島發生的大規模停電，與資料中心毫無關聯。」

  ## 資料中心生態系尚未全面投入運轉

  Kwin指出，峇淡的資料中心生態系目前尚未達到全面運轉的狀態。他說，即便將來正式全面運轉，資料中心的用電量預計仍將低於整體系統容量的10%。

  峇淡電網目前的供電容量為930百萬瓦（MW，衡量瞬時發電功率的單位），實際負載約為780MW，備用容量接近20%。PLN Batam表示，計畫於2027年前透過興建燃氣渦輪發電廠（PLTG，取自印尼語Pembangkit Listrik Tenaga Gas）及太陽能發電廠（PLTS），並搭配電池儲能系統（BESS，可儲存多餘電力，於尖峰用電或供電短缺時釋出），將供電容量提升至1,300MW。

  ## 錫哈拉潘變電站異常情況列入警方調查

  此次停電的故障源頭位於錫哈拉潘（Sei Harapan）變電站。事件初期，野生動物入侵被列為首要懷疑原因，但PLN Batam表示，調查人員在電弧事故發生後進入現場，發現多項異常。

  首批於印尼西部標準時間（WIB）早上07:00趕抵變電站的人員，未發現任何動物屍體，也沒有野生動物出沒的跡象；直至10:30，才在一台變壓器下方發現一隻受傷動物，距最初抵達已逾三小時。此外，沿變電站圍籬架設的帶電安全電線，在事發當時被發現處於斷電狀態。

  第三項異常涉及現場的閉路電視攝影機。錄影畫面顯示，對準故障變壓器的攝影機，在事故前一晚已發生位移。Kwin表示：「根據錄影，攝影機在停電發生的前一天，也就是週日晚上約18:43 WIB時發生了位移，導致發生電弧的變壓器在閉路電視畫面中已不再清晰可見。」

  印尼國家警察刑事調查局（Bareskrim Polri）與廖內群島地方警察（Polda Kepri）目前正聯手檢視所有物證及閉路電視錄影，以釐清故障原因。

  ## 電網擴容計畫因應資料中心負載成長

  PLN Batam的資料中心生態系目前仍低於全面運轉容量。該電力公司計畫於2027年前，藉由燃氣渦輪機組、太陽能發電及電池儲能系統，將供電容量從930MW提升至1,300MW，以因應包括資料中心產業在內的預期負載成長。錫哈拉潘變電站故障的刑事調查目前仍在進行中。
