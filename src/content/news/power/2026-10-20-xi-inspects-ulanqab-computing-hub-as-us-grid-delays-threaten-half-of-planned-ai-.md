body_en: |
  Chinese President Xi Jinping traveled to Ulanqab, Inner Mongolia, on October 16 to inspect the Chahar High-Tech Development Zone, reviewing construction of a national "East Data West Computing" (东数西算) hub node and the region's green computing industry. The official Xinhua News Agency dispatch was brief; a detailed analysis published October 19 by the Kunlun Policy Research Institute, a Beijing-based policy body, argued the visit signals a strategic inflection in the US-China AI competition—away from a contest over advanced semiconductors and toward a contest over who can most cheaply and reliably convert electricity into delivered computing capacity.

  ## US grid constraints on AI data centers

  The analysis cites Goldman Sachs data projecting that US data center electricity demand will rise from 31 gigawatts (GW, a measure of continuous power draw) in 2025 to 66 GW by 2027, more than doubling in two years. The US Department of Energy warned in July that most grid regions face what it called "unacceptable reliability risks" within five years. The North American Electric Reliability Corporation, the body overseeing grid standards, issued what the analysis describes as its highest-ever power system reliability alert.

  Capacity auction prices on the PJM Interconnection grid rose nearly ninefold within one year, hitting federal regulatory caps. New York State announced a one-year moratorium on new hyperscale data center construction.

  The binding near-term constraint is not electricity pricing but transformer availability, the analysis argues. AI data centers can typically be constructed in 18 months, but delivery times for high-power transformers in the United States have stretched to as long as five years; approximately 80 percent of key raw materials for large transformers are imported. The analysis cites US media reporting that nearly half of US data center projects planned for this year face delay or cancellation, with power equipment—not computing hardware—identified as the bottleneck.

  ## China's computing capacity

  China's National Data Administration standardized the Chinese-language term for a "token"—the minimum billing unit for AI computing services—in March 2026. The analysis reports that China's daily token consumption has exceeded 500 trillion, a roughly 5,000-fold increase from two years earlier.

  National intelligent computing capacity reached 2,185 EFLOPS (exaFLOPS; floating-point computing throughput at the scale of one quintillion operations per second) as of June 2026, up 177 percent year-over-year. China's eight designated national hub nodes account for more than 80 percent of that total.

  ## Ulanqab's three structural advantages

  The analysis identifies three overlapping advantages in Ulanqab that together form a combination it argues cannot be replicated elsewhere.

  The first is climate. Ulanqab's annual average temperature is 4.3 degrees Celsius, enabling data centers to rely on natural air cooling for nearly 10 months of the year. The analysis states that facilities there have achieved power usage effectiveness (PUE) ratios—total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy, where 1.0 represents theoretical perfection—as low as 1.1, cutting total energy consumption by 20 to 30 percent relative to warmer-climate facilities.

  The second is green power supply. Ulanqab's installed renewable energy capacity exceeds 20,288 megawatts (MW), with green electricity accounting for 67 percent of the regional total. New computing projects are required to source at least 80 percent of their power from renewables. CICC Data's zero-carbon computing base receives electricity through a dedicated transmission line from a renewable generation site 16 kilometers away, with 70 percent of its total power drawn from the operator's own renewable energy projects.

  The third is network proximity to Beijing. Two high-capacity fiber optic cables connect Ulanqab directly to the capital, with a data latency of 4.2 milliseconds.

  ## State-directed model versus market-driven buildout

  The analysis contrasts the two countries' infrastructure approaches. The US grid is privately owned, fragmented, and has not undergone large-scale upgrades in decades, the analysis states; technology companies building data centers must independently negotiate grid connections, source power, and wait years for transformer delivery. China's "East Data West Computing" program uses state designation to match western renewable electricity supply with eastern computing demand.

  The policy framework "computing-electricity coordination" (算電協同)—directing grid investment to expand in tandem with computing capacity—was included in China's 2026 government work report and named in the 15th Five-Year Plan covering 2026 to 2030 as one of 109 major national engineering projects. China's National Data Administration defines the framework as a mutual-reinforcement cycle: computing demand drives grid upgrades, and upgraded grids enable larger computing deployments.

  The analysis attributes to Elon Musk the view that China will ultimately prevail in the AI competition, citing China's electricity infrastructure: total installed generation capacity at nearly three times that of the United States, power output at more than twice US levels, and installed capacity redundancy of 2.5 times peak demand versus 1.5 times in the United States. The analysis accepts electricity scale as a necessary but insufficient condition, arguing that organizational capacity—the ability to plan and coordinate power and computing infrastructure at a national level—determines whether that advantage translates into delivered computing services.

  ## Ulanqab's current output

  As of August 2026, Ulanqab's deployed computing capacity stood at 190,000 petaFLOPS (P; one petaFLOPS equals one quadrillion floating-point operations per second), with intelligent computing workloads—AI model training and inference, as distinct from cold data storage—accounting for more than 90 percent of capacity, the analysis states. The region has signed agreements covering 109 data center projects. Huawei, Alibaba, Baidu, and ByteDance have all established operations in the zone; workloads include autonomous vehicle model training, large-language-model inference, and film and television rendering.

  Computing industry revenue in Ulanqab for the first eight months of 2026 totaled 5.68 billion yuan, up 40.3 percent year-over-year. The region's 15th Five-Year Plan designates a target of becoming a "Green Computing Power Capital."
body_zh: |
  中國國家主席習近平於10月16日赴內蒙古烏蘭察布，視察察哈爾高新技術開發區，考察全國「東數西算」算力樞紐節點的建設進展及當地綠色算力產業。官方新華社的相關報導篇幅簡短；北京政策研究機構崑崙政策研究院隨後於10月19日發布詳細分析，認為此次視察標誌著中美AI競爭的戰略轉折——競爭焦點已從先進半導體之爭，轉向誰能以更低成本、更穩定的方式將電力轉換為可交付算力服務的較量。

  ## 美國電網對AI資料中心的制約

  該分析援引高盛數據預測，美國資料中心的電力需求將從2025年的31吉瓦（GW，持續功率單位）攀升至2027年的66吉瓦，兩年內增幅逾一倍。美國能源部於今年7月警告，多數電網區域將在五年內面臨其所稱的「不可接受的可靠性風險」。負責監管電網標準的北美電力可靠性公司（NERC），亦發布了該分析所描述的史上最高級別電力系統可靠性警報。

  PJM互聯電網的容量競標價格在一年內暴漲近九倍，觸及聯邦監管上限。紐約州宣布對新建超大規模資料中心實施為期一年的暫停令。

  該分析指出，近期最根本的制約因素並非電費，而是變壓器的供貨問題。AI資料中心的建設週期通常約為18個月，但美國高功率變壓器的交貨期已延長至最長五年；大型變壓器關鍵原材料約有八成依賴進口。分析援引美國媒體報導，今年原計畫推進的資料中心項目中，近半數面臨延誤或取消，瓶頸在於電力設備，而非算力硬體。

  ## 中國算力現況

  中國國家數據局於2026年3月正式統一了AI算力服務最小計費單位「詞元（Token）」的中文術語。分析指出，中國每日詞元消耗量已突破500兆個，較兩年前暴增約5,000倍。

  截至2026年6月，全國智算力已達2,185 EFLOPS（exaFLOPS；即每秒執行10的18次方次浮點運算），年增177%。其中，全國八大國家算力樞紐節點合計佔全國總量逾八成。

  ## 烏蘭察布的三大結構性優勢

  分析指出，烏蘭察布具備三項相互疊加的優勢，共同構成一種難以在他處複製的條件組合。

  第一項是氣候優勢。烏蘭察布年均氣溫僅4.3攝氏度，資料中心每年可有近10個月依賴自然氣冷運作。分析指出，當地設施的電能使用效率（PUE，即設施總耗電量除以IT設備耗電量，理論最佳值為1.0）最低已達1.1，相較氣候較暖地區的設施可降低總能耗20%至30%。

  第二項是綠能供電優勢。烏蘭察布再生能源裝機容量逾20,288百萬瓦（MW），綠電佔當地電力總量的67%。新建算力項目須確保至少80%的用電來自再生能源。中金數據的零碳算力基地透過一條專用輸電線路，從16公里外的再生能源發電廠直接取電，其總用電量的70%來自運營商自有的再生能源項目。

  第三項是緊鄰北京的網路優勢。兩條大容量光纖直連烏蘭察布與首都北京，資料傳輸延遲僅4.2毫秒。

  ## 國家主導模式對比市場驅動模式

  分析對兩國的基礎建設路徑進行了對比。分析指出，美國電網以私有為主、結構分散，數十年來未曾大規模升級；科技企業興建資料中心時，須自行談判電網接入、自行籌措電力，並等待數年的變壓器交貨期。中國的「東數西算」計畫則透過國家統籌部署，將西部再生能源供給與東部算力需求相互匹配。

  「算電協同」政策框架——即引導電網投資與算力建設同步擴張——已被納入中國2026年政府工作報告，並在涵蓋2026至2030年的第十五個五年計畫中，被列為109項重大國家工程之一。中國國家數據局將這一框架定義為相互強化的循環：算力需求帶動電網升級，升級後的電網再支撐更大規模的算力部署。

  分析援引伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）的觀點，認為中國最終將在AI競爭中勝出，並列舉中國電力基礎設施數據為佐證：發電裝機總量約為美國的三倍、發電量逾美國兩倍、裝機備用率為尖峰需求的2.5倍（美國僅為1.5倍）。分析接受「電力規模是必要條件但非充分條件」的論點，主張組織能力——即在國家層面統籌規劃電力與算力基礎設施的能力——才是決定這項優勢能否轉化為可交付算力服務的關鍵。

  ## 烏蘭察布的當前產出

  分析指出，截至2026年8月，烏蘭察布已部署算力達190,000 PFLOPS（petaFLOPS；即每秒執行10的15次方次浮點運算），其中智算工作負載——包括AI模型訓練與推論，有別於冷資料儲存——佔算力總量逾九成。當地已簽約的資料中心項目達109個。華為、阿里巴巴、百度及字節跳動均已在此布局；工作負載涵蓋自動駕駛模型訓練、大型語言模型推論，以及影視渲染等應用。

  2026年前八個月，烏蘭察布算力產業營收合計56.8億元人民幣，年增40.3%。當地第十五個五年計畫訂定的目標，是將烏蘭察布打造成「綠色算力之都」。
