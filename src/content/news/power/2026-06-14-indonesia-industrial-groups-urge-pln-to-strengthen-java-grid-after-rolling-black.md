  ## Industrial groups demand grid reform after Java blackouts

  Indonesia's industrial estate association has formally written to the Energy and Mineral Resources Minister and the chief of state utility PT PLN (Persero) demanding a comprehensive review of the national power system after rolling blackouts struck parts of Java, including Bandung, Jakarta, and Cileungsi.

  The Himpunan Kawasan Industri (HKI), or Indonesian Industrial Estate Association, sent the letter to Minister Bahlil Lahadalia with copies to PLN's president director and all provincial governors, HKI chairman Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana said in a press statement on Friday, June 12, 2026.

  Residents in affected areas reported outages lasting one to two hours per day, with some locations experiencing cuts of up to five hours, according to the report.

  ## What the letter asks

  HKI's letter makes five substantive requests. It calls for a full diagnostic of the blackout's causes across generation, transmission, distribution, and reserve capacity. It asks for infrastructure reinforcement at strategic industrial estates to raise reliability standards. It requests that industrial zones receive priority restoration when system faults occur. It urges faster coordination and accurate restoration timelines between PLN and estate operators. And it pushes for accelerated development of backup systems, network capacity expansion, and new substations at rapidly growing industrial areas.

  Ma'ruf said outages of even limited duration can disrupt production processes, goods distribution, and customer delivery schedules. "Reliable electricity is a fundamental need for industrial estates," he said.

  ## Investor confidence and the broader economic stakes

  The association framed reliable power supply as a precondition for Indonesia's industrial policy goals, citing the government's downstream processing agenda, export growth targets, data center construction, and inbound investment as activities that all depend on stable energy infrastructure.

  "Power reliability is one of the main considerations investors use when choosing an investment location. Energy supply certainty gives businesses confidence in planning expansions and long-term development," Ma'ruf said.

  Industrial estates concentrate manufacturing, export activity, investment, and employment, making them significant contributors to both national and regional economies, HKI said in justifying its request for priority treatment during grid disruptions.

  ## Longer-term structural proposals

  Beyond immediate fixes, HKI said it wants broader policy space for three longer-term measures: expansion of captive power plants — generation assets owned and operated by industrial users rather than PLN — growth of new and renewable energy sources, and development of Wilayah Usaha Ketenagalistrikan (Wilus), or licensed electricity supply areas, within certain industrial estates. The association described these as elements of a strategy to strengthen national energy resilience.

  HKI also called for the adoption of more modern grid-monitoring technology as part of the remediation effort.

  The letter puts formal pressure on PLN and the Energy Ministry to respond with concrete remediation plans at a moment when Indonesia is actively courting new industrial investment and data center operators, both of which treat power quality as a site-selection criterion.

  ## 工業團體要求電網改革　爪哇停電觸發正式陳情

  印尼工業園區協會（Himpunan Kawasan Industri，簡稱HKI）已正式致函能源暨礦產資源部長及國營電力公司PT PLN（Persero）總裁，要求對爪哇部分地區發生的輪流停電事件展開全面檢討，受波及地區涵蓋萬隆、雅加達及芝勒昂西。

  HKI主席阿赫馬德・馬魯夫・毛拉納（Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana）於2026年6月12日（週五）發表新聞稿表示，協會已將信件送交能源部長巴赫利・拉哈達利亞（Bahlil Lahadalia），並副知PLN總裁及各省省長。

  據報導，受影響地區居民反映每天停電一至兩小時，部分地點甚至停電長達五小時。

  ## 信件提出五項具體要求

  HKI的信件提出五項實質請求：一、針對停電原因展開全面診斷，涵蓋發電、輸電、配電及備用容量各環節；二、強化策略性工業園區的基礎設施，以提升供電可靠度標準；三、要求在系統發生故障時，工業區應享有優先恢復供電的待遇；四、督促PLN與工業園區業者加強協調，提供準確的復電時程；五、推動加快建設備用系統、擴充電網容量，並在成長快速的工業區新設變電站。

  馬魯夫表示，即便只是短暫停電，也可能打亂生產流程、貨物調配及客戶交貨時程。他說：「穩定的電力供應是工業園區的基本需求。」

  ## 投資人信心與更廣泛的經濟影響

  HKI將可靠的電力供應定位為印尼工業政策目標的前提條件，並指出政府的下游加工議程、出口成長目標、資料中心建設，以及吸引外資等各項工作，全都有賴穩定的能源基礎設施支撐。

  馬魯夫說：「電力可靠性是投資人選擇投資地點時的主要考量之一。能源供應的穩定性，讓企業在規劃擴張與長期發展時更具信心。」

  HKI表示，工業園區匯聚了製造業、出口活動、投資及就業機會，對國家及地方經濟均有舉足輕重的貢獻，此亦為其要求在電網中斷時享有優先待遇的依據。

  ## 長期結構性改革建議

  除了立即性的補救措施外，HKI亦呼籲在政策層面為三項長期措施創造更大空間：擴大自備電廠（即由工業用戶自行擁有並運營的發電設施，而非依賴PLN供電）的規模、發展新能源及再生能源，以及在特定工業園區內推動「Wilayah Usaha Ketenagalistrikan」（Wilus，即特許電力供應區）的建設。HKI將上述措施描述為強化全國能源韌性策略的重要組成部分。

  HKI亦呼籲採用更現代化的電網監控技術，作為整體補救行動的一環。

  在印尼積極爭取新工業投資及資料中心業者進駐的關鍵時刻，這封信件對PLN及能源部形成正式壓力，要求兩者拿出具體的補救計畫——而電力品質，正是上述兩類業者選擇落腳地點時的重要評估指標。
