body_en: |
  GE Vernova will invest $34.5 million to expand transformer manufacturing capacity at its existing Shreveport, Louisiana facility, the company announced Tuesday, citing rising demand for electrical grid equipment.

  The expansion is expected to grow the Shreveport workforce by nearly 30%, with the facility reaching full production capacity by 2028, according to the company.

  ## State incentives

  Louisiana offered GE Vernova what it described as a competitive incentive package to support the investment. That package includes a $300,000 performance-based grant for site and infrastructure improvements. The company also said it expects to participate in Louisiana's Quality Jobs and Industrial Tax Exemption programs.

  ## Context

  Philippe Piron, CEO of GE Vernova's electrification segment, attributed the investment to growing electricity demand. "Transformers are part of the critical grid equipment essential to delivering the power our customers and modern economies depend on," Piron said in a press release. He added that the investment builds on more than five decades of manufacturing operations at the Shreveport site.

  Transformers are devices that step electrical voltage up or down as power moves through the grid, and are considered essential infrastructure for transmitting and distributing electricity.

  ## Significance

  The investment reflects continued pressure on transformer supply chains as utilities move to expand and modernize electrical infrastructure. GE Vernova's commitment to an existing domestic facility, backed by state incentives, positions the Shreveport plant for increased output as demand for grid equipment grows.

  The company said production at the expanded facility is expected to reach full capacity by 2028.

  ## 州政府優惠措施

  路易斯安那州為支持這項投資，向GE Vernova提供了一套具競爭力的獎勵方案。方案內容包括一筆30萬美元的績效補助款，專用於廠址與基礎設施的改善工程。此外，公司表示也預計參與路易斯安那州的「優質就業計畫」（Quality Jobs）及「工業稅賦豁免計畫」（Industrial Tax Exemption Program）。

  ## 背景

  GE Vernova電氣化業務部門執行長菲利普．皮龍（Philippe Piron）將此次投資歸因於不斷成長的電力需求。皮龍在新聞稿中表示：「變壓器是電網關鍵設備的一部分，對於滿足客戶與現代經濟所仰賴的電力供應不可或缺。」他補充指出，此次投資是奠基於什里夫波特廠區逾五十年製造營運的深厚根基之上。

  變壓器是電力在電網中傳輸時，負責升壓或降壓的裝置，被視為電力輸配不可或缺的重要基礎設施。

  ## 重要性

  這項投資反映出在電力公司積極擴建與現代化電力基礎設施之際，變壓器供應鏈所承受的持續壓力。GE Vernova選擇投資一座現有的國內廠房，並獲州政府獎勵方案的支持，使什里夫波特廠在電網設備需求持續成長的背景下，得以進一步擴大產出。

  公司表示，擴建後的廠房預計於2028年達到完整產能。
