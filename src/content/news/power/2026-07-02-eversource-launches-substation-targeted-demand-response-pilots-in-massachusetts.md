body_en: |
  Eversource Energy launched two geographically targeted demand response pilots in Massachusetts on June 25, enrolling batteries, electric vehicles, and smart thermostats at named substations to manage summer peak loads and midday solar surpluses as the region braces for what grid forecasters expect to be one of its highest-demand days in years.

  The Greater Boston pilot, called ConnectedSolutions+, and two programs in southeastern Massachusetts — ConnectedSolutions+ and Managed Charging+ — are expansions of Eversource's existing ConnectedSolutions demand response program. All three were live as of late June, an Eversource spokesperson told Utility Dive, coinciding with an intense heat dome affecting southern New England. ISO New England, the regional grid operator, has forecast 478,450 MWh of net load on July 2, among the highest daily readings this decade.

  ## What the pilots do and where

  The Greater Boston ConnectedSolutions+ pilot targets residential and commercial customers in parts of Cambridge, Milton, and south Boston served by three Eversource substations: Alewife, Hyde Park, and Dewar. Eversource plans to enroll approximately 2,800 devices across its Greater Boston pilots, with most in the ConnectedSolutions+ program, the spokesperson said.

  Eligible devices include stationary batteries, electric vehicles, building management systems, and smart thermostats. When Eversource calls an event — between 2 p.m. and 10 p.m. from June through September — enrolled devices curtail consumption or discharge stored energy to reduce stress on local distribution circuits. Eversource anticipates calling 40 to 60 events per year for residential battery customers and 10 to 20 events per year for residential thermostat customers.

  The southeastern Massachusetts programs cover customers in parts of Freetown, Lakeville, New Bedford, Dartmouth, and Acushnet served by Eversource's Industrial Park substation. Enrollment targets are smaller there: 30 Managed Charging+ and 14 ConnectedSolutions+ customers, according to the company. Southeastern ConnectedSolutions+ events can be called across a wider seasonal window — April through November — and up to 150 times per year, a frequency that reflects the area's exposure to distributed solar fluctuations.

  Managed Charging+ addresses a distinct grid condition: midday excess solar generation, a phenomenon sometimes called the duck curve, in which midday solar output exceeds local demand and the grid operator must manage over-supply. Enrolled residential EV customers are encouraged to schedule charging sessions between 11 a.m. and 3 p.m., when local solar production is highest. Charging outside that window does not qualify for the monthly incentive.

  ## Incentive structures

  Eversource is using tiered financial incentives to recruit participants across both regions. In Greater Boston, residential battery owners can earn a $400 per kilowatt (kW) performance incentive — kW here represents the amount of load reduced during an event. Residential thermostat customers receive $50 to $150 upfront plus a $20 annual performance incentive. On the commercial side, battery participants earn an average $250/kW, and smart thermostat participants receive $50 to $150 upfront plus $200 annually.

  In southeastern Massachusetts, ConnectedSolutions+ performance incentives are $275/kW for events called from June through September and $100/kW for events in April, May, October, and November. Managed Charging+ enrollees receive a $50 enrollment payment and $30 each month they complete at least five 15-minute charging sessions during the designated 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. solar-peak window.

  ## The grid pressures driving the pilots

  Eversource cited two structural trends reshaping New England's power system. The first is rising electricity demand driven by the electrification of buildings and transportation. The second is rapid growth in small-scale, behind-the-meter solar installations — panels on rooftops and in backyards that feed power into local circuits but are not directly controlled by the grid operator. ISO New England has projected that wintertime behind-the-meter solar production across the region could reach 6.5 gigawatts (GW) by the late 2030s, roughly equivalent to the combined output of several large natural gas power plants.

  Both trends can create localized stress on distribution infrastructure even when the broader regional grid appears well-supplied. A neighborhood with heavy EV adoption can overload a substation during evening charging peaks, while a circuit dense with rooftop solar can see voltage swings during midday hours. The pilots are designed to test whether utility-directed customer flexibility can relieve that stress at the substation level before it requires conventional infrastructure additions such as new transformers or upgraded feeders.

  "It is an important step in testing how customer-side flexibility can support the grid while easing congestion, keeping supply costs down, and mitigating the need for certain infrastructure investments as electricity demand continues to grow," said Tilak Subrahmanian, Eversource's vice president for energy efficiency and electric mobility, in a statement. Subrahmanian described ConnectedSolutions+ as a "practical way to target demand response where it can deliver the most value for local reliability" at the substation level.

  ## Substation-level targeting as a design choice

  Conventional demand response programs typically aggregate load reductions across a wide service territory, giving the utility a broad reduction in consumption without directing it to specific circuits. By restricting eligibility to customers served by named substations, Eversource is attempting to steer flexibility to the locations where distribution constraints are most likely to bind during peak events. The Utility Dive report noted that other U.S. utilities and electricity retailers have used time-of-use pricing and managed charging incentives to shift EV load toward periods of high solar penetration, citing programs from Pacific Gas & Electric and Texas retailer TXU, though program designs vary.

  ## What comes next

  Eversource intends to collect data from the pilots through the current year and anticipates continuing and potentially expanding them through 2029, the company's spokesperson said. The programs launched during one of southern New England's most intense early-summer heat events, providing an early high-stress test of the enrollment, dispatch, and incentive systems before the pilots enter their planned multi-year data-gathering phase.

  ## Eversource在麻州啟動以變電站為目標的需量反應試驗計畫

  Eversource Energy於6月25日在麻薩諸塞州正式啟動兩項以地理位置為核心的需量反應試驗計畫，納管蓄電池、電動車與智慧溫控器，對應特定變電站的饋線，以因應夏季尖峰負載與午間太陽能電力過剩問題。此時正值區域電網調度機構預測，今年將迎來近十年來用電需求最高的一天。

  大波士頓試驗計畫「ConnectedSolutions+」，以及東南部麻薩諸塞州的「ConnectedSolutions+」與「Managed Charging+」兩項方案，均為Eversource現行ConnectedSolutions需量反應計畫的擴充版本。三項計畫均已於6月下旬上線，恰逢新英格蘭南部遭受強烈熱穹效應侵襲。Eversource發言人向《Utility Dive》表示，新英格蘭獨立系統運營商（ISO New England）預測7月2日的淨負載將達到478,450百萬瓦時（MWh），是本十年來日用電量最高紀錄之一。

  ## 試驗計畫內容與涵蓋範圍

  大波士頓ConnectedSolutions+試驗計畫的目標客群，為劍橋、彌爾頓與南波士頓部分地區的住宅及商業用戶，由Eversource旗下的Alewife、Hyde Park與Dewar三座變電站供電。Eversource計畫在大波士頓試驗計畫中共招募約2,800台裝置，其中大多數將納入ConnectedSolutions+方案，發言人表示。

  符合資格的裝置包括固定式蓄電池、電動車、樓宇管理系統及智慧溫控器。每逢Eversource啟動需量反應事件——時間範圍為6月至9月間的下午2時至晚間10時——已登記的裝置將減少用電或釋放儲存電能，以舒緩地區配電線路的壓力。Eversource預計每年為住宅蓄電池客戶啟動40至60次事件，為住宅溫控器客戶啟動10至20次事件。

  東南部麻薩諸塞州方案的涵蓋範圍，為弗里鎮、萊克維爾、新貝德福德、達特茅斯與阿庫什內特部分地區的客戶，由Eversource的Industrial Park變電站供電。該區域的招募目標規模較小：Managed Charging+招募30位客戶，ConnectedSolutions+招募14位客戶。東南部ConnectedSolutions+的事件啟動窗口更寬，橫跨4月至11月，每年最多可啟動150次，這樣的頻率反映出當地分散式太陽能發電的波動程度較高。

  Managed Charging+針對的是另一種電網狀況：午間太陽能發電過剩，此現象有時稱為「鴨子曲線」——午間太陽能輸出超過本地需求，迫使電網調度機構設法消化過剩電力。已登記的住宅電動車客戶被鼓勵將充電時段安排在上午11時至下午3時之間，也就是本地太陽能發電量最高的時段。在此窗口以外進行充電，則不符合每月獎勵資格。

  ## 獎勵機制

  Eversource針對兩個地區採用分級財務獎勵方式招募參與者。在大波士頓，住宅蓄電池用戶每千瓦（kW，代表事件期間的減少負載量）可獲得400美元的績效獎勵。住宅溫控器用戶可獲得50至150美元的前期獎勵，加上每年20美元的績效獎勵。商業用戶方面，蓄電池參與者平均每千瓦可獲得250美元，智慧溫控器參與者則可獲得50至150美元的前期獎勵，加上每年200美元的獎勵。

  東南部麻薩諸塞州的ConnectedSolutions+績效獎勵，6月至9月啟動事件為每千瓦275美元，4月、5月、10月及11月啟動事件則為每千瓦100美元。Managed Charging+參與者可獲得50美元的登記獎勵，並在每個完成至少5次、每次至少15分鐘、且在指定的上午11時至下午3時太陽能尖峰窗口內充電的月份，另獲得30美元月度獎勵。

  ## 驅動試驗計畫的電網壓力

  Eversource指出兩項重塑新英格蘭電力系統的結構性趨勢。其一是建築與交通電氣化所帶動的用電需求上升；其二是小型、表後太陽能設施的快速成長——屋頂與後院的太陽能板將電力饋入本地線路，但電網調度機構無法直接控制這些電源。ISO New England預測，至2030年代末，整個區域的冬季表後太陽能發電量可能達到6.5吉瓦（GW），相當於數座大型天然氣發電廠的合計裝置容量。

  上述兩項趨勢，即便在整體區域電網看似供應充裕的情況下，仍可能在配電基礎設施上造成局部壓力。電動車普及程度高的社區，在傍晚充電尖峰時段可能使變電站過載；而屋頂太陽能密度高的線路，則可能在午間出現電壓波動。這些試驗計畫旨在測試，由電力公司主導的用戶端彈性調度，能否在變電站層級舒緩壓力，從而避免動用傳統基礎設施擴充方案，例如新增變壓器或升級饋線。

  Eversource能源效率與電動運輸事業副總裁Tilak Subrahmanian在聲明中表示：「這是測試用戶端彈性如何支撐電網、同時舒緩壅塞、壓低供應成本，並在用電需求持續成長之際減少特定基礎設施投資需求的重要一步。」Subrahmanian將ConnectedSolutions+描述為「在變電站層級，將需量反應導向最能發揮本地可靠性價值之處的實際可行方式」。

  ## 以變電站為目標的設計邏輯

  傳統需量反應計畫通常將整個服務區域的負載削減加以彙總，為電力公司帶來廣泛的用電減少效果，卻無法將其導向特定線路。透過將資格限定在特定變電站供電的客戶，Eversource試圖將彈性調度資源集中於在尖峰事件中最可能面臨配電瓶頸的位置。《Utility Dive》的報導指出，美國其他電力公司與電力零售商也已採用時間電價與託管充電獎勵，將電動車充電需求導向太陽能高滲透率時段，例如太平洋瓦斯電力公司（Pacific Gas & Electric）及德州電力零售商TXU的相關計畫，惟各計畫設計細節不盡相同。

  ## 後續展望

  Eversource計畫在本年度蒐集試驗計畫的數據，並預計將計畫延續至2029年，屆時可能進一步擴大規模，公司發言人表示。這些計畫恰好在新英格蘭南部其中一場最猛烈的初夏熱浪期間啟動，為登記、調度與獎勵系統提供了一次早期高壓力測試，為計畫正式進入多年期數據蒐集階段奠定基礎。
