body_en: |
  Arizona Public Service (APS), the state's largest electric utility, filed an integrated resource plan (IRP) — a long-range supply-and-demand blueprint submitted every three years to state regulators — projecting that retail electricity sales will more than double and peak demand will grow roughly 60% over the next 15 years, driven largely by data centers and large industrial customers. To meet that load, APS said it will extend the life of its Four Corners coal plant by up to seven years, add significant natural gas capacity, and accelerate utility-scale solar and battery storage procurement.

  The IRP, filed Friday with the Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC), projects peak demand will exceed 14.5 GW (gigawatts) by 2041 — up from a current record peak of approximately 9.2 GW set in August — and that annual retail sales will grow to about 80,600 GWh (gigawatt-hours) in 2041 from about 34,200 GWh in 2025. Peak demand is expected to grow at 3.4% annually after accounting for customer resources such as demand-side management programs.

  ## What is driving demand

  APS attributed the growth primarily to large-load industrial and data center customers. The utility said it has experienced "sustained record levels of interest" from large customers, which it acknowledged poses "a risk in increased potential for unrealized loads" if those customers do not materialize. To mitigate that risk, APS said it has adopted strategies including requiring customers to commit to "minimum demand and energy-based contract terms, providing collateral in case of default, and instituting early exit fees."

  Population growth is also a factor. APS said it expects to add up to 29,000 residential customers annually in the near term, with annual customer growth projected in the range of 1.5% to 2.5% through 2030.

  ## The Four Corners coal extension

  The most consequential near-term decision in the plan is the extension of the Four Corners Power Plant in northwestern New Mexico, a coal-fired facility that APS had previously planned to retire in 2031. Under the preferred plan, APS will keep the plant operating through as late as 2038 — a seven-year extension.

  "The company will continue to evaluate the appropriate time to cease operations between 2031 and 2038, with the decision depending on customer needs, economics and other factors evaluated through future planning processes," APS said in a statement. The utility said the extension could save customers close to $1 billion compared with earlier retirement.

  "Reliability is nonnegotiable," APS director of resource planning Mike Eugenis said in a statement.

  ## Gas, solar, and storage additions

  Alongside the coal extension, APS outlined a substantial natural gas buildout. The plan calls for approximately 4,000 MW (megawatts) of new gas generation through 2036, broken into several projects:

  - Repowering the Cholla Power Plant as a 387-MW facility by 2030
  - Adding 1,107 MW through new combustion turbines at existing APS sites and uprates to tolling units during 2026–2030
  - Building the 1,238-MW Desert Sun combined-cycle facility in 2032, designated to serve large-customer subscription load

  On the clean-energy side, APS said it will bring approximately 2,000 MW of utility-scale solar and 2,500 MW of battery storage online by 2029. By 2030, the utility said it expects to have approximately 6 GW of battery storage on its system.

  ## Revised customer-sited resource forecasts

  APS revised its outlook for customer-owned resources in both directions. The utility said it now expects electric vehicle (EV) adoption in its territory to come in approximately 18% lower than its prior 2023 forecast, and behind-the-meter rooftop solar adoption to run about 30% below that earlier projection.

  By contrast, APS raised its forecast for customer-sited battery storage significantly. The utility now projects behind-the-meter storage will reach 1.2 GW by 2045 — a figure it said is roughly double the 2023 forecast. For 2032 specifically, the storage forecast is approximately 95% higher than the 2023 estimate. Behind-the-meter solar, meanwhile, is expected to grow modestly from 2.1 GW today to 3.7 GW by 2045, though at a slower pace than the 2020–2023 period.

  APS projected the EV fleet in its service territory will grow from 65,000 vehicles today to 1.1 million in 2045, generating 5.2 TWh (terawatt-hours) of additional charging load.

  ## Critic response

  Clean energy advocates criticized the plan at filing. Sandy Bahr, director of the Sierra Club's Grand Canyon Chapter, said the proposal "further delays our move from gas and coal to solar and wind" and accused the utility of protecting its "own bottom lines, instead of making the most economical decisions for ratepayers."

  Kate Bowman, Vote Solar's West senior regulatory director, said APS "failed to consider how alternative technologies like rooftop solar, battery storage, and other distributed resources could help meet energy needs" and called on the ACC to push for "a more comprehensive approach that puts affordability, reliability, and the interests of customers at the center of long-term planning."

  ## What comes next

  The IRP now goes before the Arizona Corporation Commission for review. APS said it will continue to evaluate the Four Corners retirement date through future planning cycles, with any final decision on a date between 2031 and 2038 contingent on customer demand, economics, and other factors. The next IRP filing is expected in three years.
body_zh: |
  亞利桑那公共服務公司（APS）——全州規模最大的電力公用事業業者——日前向州政府監管機關提交一份綜合資源規劃（IRP），預測未來15年內，零售電力銷售量將增長逾一倍，尖峰需求將成長約六成，主要驅動力來自資料中心與大型工業客戶。為因應此一需求，APS表示將把旗下四角燃煤電廠的運營壽命最多延長七年，原定2031年除役的計畫因此推遲；與此同時，公司也宣布將大幅擴充天然氣發電容量，並加速採購公用規模太陽能發電與電池儲能設備。

  此份IRP於週五提交亞利桑那公司委員會（ACC），預測2041年尖峰需求將突破14.5 GW（十億瓦），相較於今年8月剛創下的約9.2 GW歷史紀錄，增幅相當可觀；年度零售電力銷售量則將從2025年的約34,200 GWh（十億瓦時）成長至2041年的約80,600 GWh。若將需量管理等用戶端資源納入計算，尖峰需求的年均成長率預估為3.4%。

  ## 需求成長的驅動因素

  APS指出，此波需求成長主要來自大型工業客戶與資料中心。公司表示，大型客戶的加入詢問一直維持「持續創紀錄的高度熱絡」，但也坦承，若這些客戶最終未能如期落地，將帶來「已承諾負荷無法實現的潛在風險」。為降低此一風險，APS已採取相應策略，包括要求客戶履行「最低需量與用電量的合約承諾」、在違約時提供擔保，以及設立提前解約違約金。

  人口成長同樣是需求增加的因素之一。APS預計近期每年最多新增2萬9,000戶住宅客戶，2030年前的年用戶成長率預估介於1.5%至2.5%之間。

  ## 四角電廠延役決策

  此次規劃中最具影響力的近期決策，是延長位於新墨西哥州西北部四角電廠（Four Corners Power Plant）壽命的方案。這座燃煤電廠原定2031年除役，在APS的優選方案下，將最晚延役至2038年——相當於延長七年。

  「公司將持續評估2031年至2038年間最適宜的停止運營時間，最終決定將視用戶需求、經濟因素，以及未來規劃流程中評估的其他條件而定，」APS在聲明中表示。公司並指出，延役方案與提前除役相比，可為客戶節省接近10億美元（約新台幣330億元）的費用。

  「可靠供電是不容妥協的底線，」APS資源規劃總監麥克‧尤金尼斯（Mike Eugenis）在聲明中說道。

  ## 天然氣、太陽能與儲能設備擴充

  在延長四角電廠使用壽命的同時，APS也規劃了規模可觀的天然氣發電擴建計畫。根據規劃，公司將於2036年前新增約4,000 MW（百萬瓦）的天然氣發電容量，分為數個項目推進：

  - 將丘拉電廠（Cholla Power Plant）改建為387 MW的新設施，預計於2030年完工
  - 在2026年至2030年間，透過在APS既有廠址新建燃氣渦輪機，以及提升既有容量租賃機組出力，新增1,107 MW
  - 興建1,238 MW的沙漠太陽聯合循環電廠（Desert Sun），預計2032年完工，專門服務大型客戶訂閱用電需求

  在潔淨能源方面，APS表示將於2029年前建置約2,000 MW的公用規模太陽能發電，以及2,500 MW的電池儲能設備。至2030年，公司預計系統內的電池儲能總量將達到約6 GW。

  ## 用戶端資源預測調整

  APS對客戶自有資源的前景預測進行了雙向修正。在電動車普及率方面，公司預估其服務區域內的採用進度將比2023年的前次預測低約18%；用戶自設屋頂太陽能的裝置速度，也將較先前預測落後約30%。

  相對地，APS大幅上調了用戶端電池儲能的預測數字。公司現在預估，用戶端後端儲能至2045年將達到1.2 GW——約為2023年預測值的兩倍。就2032年單年而言，儲能預測數字更較2023年的估算高出約95%。用戶端太陽能方面，預計將從目前的2.1 GW小幅成長至2045年的3.7 GW，但成長速度將較2020年至2023年間明顯趨緩。

  APS預估，其服務區域內的電動車數量將從目前的6萬5,000輛，成長至2045年的110萬輛，屆時將帶來5.2 TWh（兆瓦時）的額外充電用電需求。

  ## 環保團體批評

  此次規劃提交後隨即遭到潔淨能源倡議者的抨擊。塞拉俱樂部（Sierra Club）大峽谷分會主任桑迪‧巴爾（Sandy Bahr）表示，此方案「進一步拖延了我們從天然氣和煤炭轉向太陽能與風能的進程」，並指控該電力公司「保護自身的商業利益，而非為用電戶做出最具經濟效益的決策」。

  太陽能倡議組織Vote Solar西部高級監管總監凱特‧鮑曼（Kate Bowman）則表示，APS「未能考量屋頂太陽能、電池儲能及其他分散式資源如何協助滿足能源需求」，並呼籲ACC要求APS提出「以可負擔性、供電可靠性及客戶利益為核心的更全面長期規劃方案」。

  ## 後續進程

  此份IRP目前已提交亞利桑那公司委員會進行審查。APS表示，將透過未來的規劃週期持續評估四角電廠的除役時間，2031年至2038年之間的最終決定將取決於客戶需求、經濟因素及其他相關條件。下一次IRP提交預計在三年後進行。
