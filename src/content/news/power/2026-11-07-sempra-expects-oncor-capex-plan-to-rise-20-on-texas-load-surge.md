body_en: |
  Sempra expects the five-year capital spending plan at its Texas transmission and distribution subsidiary Oncor to increase by roughly 20% above the existing $47.5 billion base, driven by accelerating customer and load growth across the state, executives said Thursday during the company's third-quarter earnings call.

  Oncor CEO Allen Nye said the revised figure would appear in the utility's upcoming annual capital plan update. The increase reflects "continued strong customer and load growth from a variety of sectors, all over our system," Nye said.

  Sempra CEO and chairman Jeffrey Martin said Oncor — which serves more than 14 million customers in Texas — recently recorded a new peak demand of 33 gigawatts (GW), a unit equal to roughly 33 large nuclear power plants running simultaneously. That figure represented a 6% increase over the prior record set last year. "This increase underscores the robust economic growth that's occurring in Texas," Martin said.

  ## Texas load drivers

  Martin described Texas demand growth as "broad-based." Data centers represent "the lion's share" of incremental load, he said, but "there is a significant amount of non-data center growth" as well. He did not specify which other sectors are driving the remaining demand.

  Analysts on the call asked about the potential effect on Texas data center expansion following the November midterm elections. Martin said the company looks forward to working with the incoming Texas administration. Nye struck a similar tone, saying it was "too soon to tell exactly what it all means" but that the utility provides an essential service regardless of which party holds power. "We continue to believe that both parties want a strong and growing Texas," Nye said.

  ## California headwinds

  While Oncor's growth picture was the headline item, Martin also raised concerns about Sempra's California operations — specifically San Diego Gas & Electric (SDG&E) and Southern California Gas Co. (SoCalGas) — where wildfire liability uncertainty poses financial risk.

  The California legislature failed in its last session to pass a bill that would have reduced the effect of wildfire liabilities on SDG&E and other utilities. Fitch Ratings called that outcome a "significant adverse credit development" in a September report. Martin joined the CEOs of Pacific Gas and Electric Co. and Southern California Edison in calling for the legislature to pass wildfire liability reform. Credit downgrades resulting from inaction will "constrain future utility service," he said, adding that "the situation clearly underscores that inaction doesn't work, and that there's a real need for a timely solution."

  Sempra's third-quarter filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission noted that the scope of potential damages from the January 2025 Eaton fire "could materially reduce or exhaust" California's Wildfire Fund, a state backstop for utility wildfire costs. The filing stated that Southern California Edison is currently unable to estimate a range of potential losses from the Eaton fire, and that SDG&E is therefore also unable to estimate what reduction in Wildfire Fund coverage it might face.

  ## Rate case dispute

  Sempra also disclosed that the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) last week issued a proposed decision denying the company's petition to modify a 2024 general rate case ruling. Sempra contends the CPUC made a calculation error that would prevent its California utilities from collecting $188 million in associated revenue in 2027.

  CFO Karen Sedgwick described the matter as a one-year issue. "It's a one-year issue. We think it's a mistake. We think it gets fixed. If for any reason it couldn't, we're going to try to mitigate that," she said, noting that a new general rate case takes effect in January 2028, which would supersede the disputed figure.

  Martin emphasized that the CPUC's decision remains a proposal, not a final order. "There's a series of regulatory avenues that we will appeal to try to get the right answer," he said.

  ## Significance

  A 20% increase on a $47.5 billion base would add roughly $9.5 billion to Oncor's five-year capital program, a scale of investment that reflects how rapidly electricity demand in Texas is outpacing existing grid infrastructure. For grid equipment suppliers, contractors, and financing markets, the revised figure signals sustained, large-volume procurement activity from one of the country's largest transmission and distribution utilities.

  Oncor's next annual capital plan update, in which the revised figures are expected to be formally presented, will be the next concrete milestone for the program.

  ## Sempra預期Oncor五年資本計畫上修逾兩成，德州用電需求創歷史新高

  Sempra旗下德州輸配電子公司Oncor現行的五年資本支出計畫金額為475億美元，高階主管週四在集團第三季法說會上表示，受德州各地客戶數量與用電負載快速成長推動，預期下一版計畫將在此基礎上再增加約20%。

  Oncor執行長Allen Nye表示，修訂後的數字將在即將公布的年度資本計畫更新中正式揭露。他指出，這波增幅反映的是「來自各行各業、遍及整個系統的持續強勁客戶與負載成長」。

  Sempra董事長兼執行長Jeffrey Martin表示，Oncor目前在德州服務超過1,400萬名客戶，日前剛創下33GW（百萬瓩）的新高峰需求紀錄——相當於約33座大型核電廠同時滿載運轉的發電量——較前一年創下的歷史紀錄成長6%。Martin說：「這一增幅充分彰顯了德州正在發生的強勁經濟擴張。」

  ## 德州用電需求驅動因素

  Martin形容德州的用電需求成長「具有廣泛基礎」。他表示，資料中心是新增用電負載中「占比最大的部分」，但同時也存在「相當可觀的非資料中心成長」，惟他未具體說明其餘需求來自哪些產業。

  法說會上，分析師詢問11月期中選舉後對德州資料中心擴張的潛在影響。Martin表示，公司期待與即將上任的德州新政府展開合作。Nye也持相近立場，表示現在「要確切判斷這一切代表什麼意義還為時過早」，但強調無論哪個政黨執政，電力公司所提供的都是不可或缺的基礎服務。Nye說：「我們持續相信，兩黨都希望德州強勁成長。」

  ## 加州業務面臨逆風

  儘管Oncor的成長前景是本次法說會的焦點，Martin同時也對Sempra加州業務表達憂慮——具體而言，是旗下聖地牙哥瓦斯電力公司（SDG&E）與南加州瓦斯公司（SoCalGas）——因野火責任不確定性而面臨財務風險。

  加州州議會在上一個會期未能通過一項原可降低野火責任對SDG&E等電力公司衝擊的法案。惠譽信評（Fitch Ratings）在9月的報告中將此一結果定性為「重大不利信用發展」。Martin聯同太平洋瓦斯電力公司（PG&E）及南加州愛迪生公司（Southern California Edison）的執行長，共同呼籲州議會盡速推動野火責任改革立法。他表示，若持續不作為，信用評等遭到調降將「限制未來的電力公用服務供給」，並強調「這一局面清楚說明，消極不作為行不通，眼下亟需及時解方。」

  Sempra向美國證券交易委員會（SEC）提交的第三季申報文件指出，2025年1月伊頓（Eaton）野火案的潛在損害規模「可能大幅削減甚至耗盡」加州野火基金——即加州為電力公司野火成本所設立的國家後備機制。申報文件並指出，南加州愛迪生公司目前仍無法估算伊頓野火的潛在損失範圍，因此SDG&E也同樣無法估計其可能面臨的野火基金賠付縮減幅度。

  ## 費率案爭議

  Sempra另外披露，加州公用事業委員會（CPUC）上週發布了一項擬議決定，駁回公司要求修改2024年一般費率案裁決的申請。Sempra主張，CPUC在計算上出現錯誤，將導致其加州子公司在2027年無法收取1.88億美元的相關收入。

  財務長Karen Sedgwick將此事定性為單一年度問題。她說：「這是一個單年度的問題，我們認為是個錯誤，我們認為會獲得更正。若因任何原因無法更正，我們也會設法降低影響。」她並指出，新一輪一般費率案將於2028年1月生效，屆時將取代這筆存疑的數字。

  Martin強調，CPUC的決定目前仍僅屬擬議，尚非最終裁定。他說：「我們將循一系列監管途徑提出申訴，以期獲得正確結果。」

  ## 市場意義

  在475億美元的基礎上上調20%，意味著Oncor五年資本計畫將新增約95億美元，這一投資規模清楚反映出德州電力需求超越現有電網基礎建設的速度之快。對電網設備供應商、工程承包商以及融資市場而言，修訂後的數字預示著來自全美最大輸配電公用事業之一的持續大量採購需求。

  Oncor下一次年度資本計畫更新將是正式揭示修訂數字的具體里程碑，市場各方正密切關注其公布時程。
