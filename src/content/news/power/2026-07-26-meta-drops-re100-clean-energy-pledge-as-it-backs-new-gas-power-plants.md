body_en: |
  Meta has withdrawn from RE100, a global corporate pledge to source all electricity from renewable energy, the company confirmed on July 24, after the initiative's administrator ruled that Meta's investments in new natural gas power plants made it ineligible for continued membership.

  The Climate Group, the Britain-based non-profit that runs RE100, removed Meta from its member list following what it described as several in-depth conversations. "After several in-depth conversations between Meta and Climate Group, Meta has withdrawn from the RE100 initiative, as it is no longer able to meet the technical criteria due to investments made in new gas power," a Climate Group spokesperson told Recharge News, a renewable energy industry outlet that first reported the departure.

  Meta joined RE100 in 2016, according to a Wayback Machine archive of the initiative's website. The group counts 444 members, including Apple, Google, and Microsoft.

  ## Why Meta was disqualified

  RE100 requires members to source all electricity from renewable supplies. Meta has said it has matched its total electricity consumption with 100 percent clean energy on an annualised basis since 2020, doing so through long-term power purchase agreements with renewable energy providers. The Climate Group told Recharge News that arrangement is no longer sufficient given Meta's direct involvement in bringing new fossil fuel generation online.

  Meta has signed contracts with utilities to build new natural gas power plants, including 10 plants dedicated to powering its Hyperion data center in Louisiana alone. Annualised renewable matching — a common accounting method where companies offset their total annual consumption with renewable energy certificates rather than matching supply hour by hour — does not neutralise the construction of net-new gas capacity under RE100's criteria.

  A Meta spokesperson told AFP the company continues to scale up renewable energy investments and that leaving RE100 does not change its goal of matching electricity use with 100 percent clean energy.

  ## Gas as a data center power source

  Natural gas has been described in energy policy discussions as a bridge fuel between higher-emitting fossil fuels and renewables such as wind and solar. Its combustion produces lower carbon dioxide emissions per unit of energy than coal or oil, but drilling and extraction cause methane leakage — methane is the primary component of natural gas and a potent greenhouse gas. Burning natural gas also produces nitric oxides, sulfur, mercury, and particulate emissions, and hydraulic fracturing used in its extraction can affect groundwater.

  Meta is not alone among major technology companies in turning to gas to meet surging data center power demand. Microsoft signed a deal with Chevron in June to supply natural gas electricity to a data center in West Texas. Google has also been linked to similar arrangements, according to press reports cited by AFP.

  ## What the exit signals

  Meta's removal from RE100 is a concrete marker of how the rapid expansion of data center capacity is straining corporate clean energy commitments made during a period of slower power demand growth. RE100 membership has functioned as a public signal of environmental intent; losing that designation reflects a direct conflict between the membership criteria and the operational decisions large technology companies are making to secure reliable power at scale.

  Meta's confirmed investments in new gas generation — ten plants for a single Louisiana facility — indicate the company has concluded that renewable procurement alone cannot meet the timeline and reliability requirements of its current buildout. The company maintains it will continue purchasing renewable energy and matching its consumption on an annualised basis even outside the RE100 framework.

  Meta於7月24日正式確認，已退出RE100這項要求企業百分之百使用再生能源的全球倡議。主辦單位氣候組織（Climate Group）裁定，Meta投資興建新天然氣電廠的舉動，使其不再符合持續會員資格的技術條件，此舉也標誌著這家全球最大資料中心營運業者之一，正式告別純再生能源用電承諾。

  RE100由總部位於英國的非營利組織氣候組織負責管理。氣候組織將Meta從會員名單中除名，並表示雙方已進行多次深入溝通。氣候組織發言人向再生能源業界媒體《Recharge News》（最早報導此事的媒體）表示：「Meta與氣候組織經過多次深入溝通後，Meta已退出RE100倡議，原因是其投資興建新天然氣電廠，已無法再符合相關技術條件。」

  根據網際網路檔案館（Wayback Machine）留存的RE100官網紀錄，Meta於2016年加入RE100。目前RE100共有444個成員，包括蘋果（Apple）、Google及微軟（Microsoft）。

  ## Meta為何遭到取消資格

  RE100要求成員必須完全仰賴再生能源供電。Meta表示，自2020年起，透過與再生能源業者簽訂長期購電協議，已在年度計算基礎上實現電力使用百分之百潔淨能源的目標。然而，氣候組織告知《Recharge News》，鑑於Meta直接參與讓新化石燃料發電設施上線運轉，此種抵銷方式已不再足夠。

  Meta已與電力公司簽約，委託興建多座新天然氣電廠，光是為路易斯安那州Hyperion資料中心供電，就已簽訂了10座電廠的合約。所謂「年度再生能源抵銷」，是一種常見的企業會計方式——業者以再生能源憑證抵銷全年總用電量，而非按小時對應實際電力供需。依據RE100的條件，這種方式無法抵消新建天然氣發電產能所帶來的淨增排放。

  Meta發言人向法新社（AFP）表示，公司將持續擴大對再生能源的投資，退出RE100並不影響其達成電力使用百分之百潔淨能源的目標。

  ## 天然氣作為資料中心的電力來源

  在能源政策討論中，天然氣常被定位為從高碳排放化石燃料過渡到風能、太陽能等再生能源的「橋接燃料」。天然氣燃燒的單位能源二氧化碳排放量低於煤炭或石油，但鑽探與開採過程中會造成甲烷洩漏——甲烷是天然氣的主要成分，也是威力強勁的溫室氣體。燃燒天然氣還會產生氮氧化物、硫化物、汞及懸浮微粒等污染物；其開採所使用的水力壓裂技術，也可能對地下水源造成影響。

  面對資料中心用電需求暴增的壓力，Meta並非唯一轉向天然氣的大型科技公司。微軟今年6月與雪佛龍（Chevron）簽署協議，由後者為其位於德克薩斯州西部的資料中心提供天然氣電力。根據法新社引述的媒體報導，Google也傳出有類似安排。

  ## 此次退出所傳遞的訊號

  Meta遭移出RE100，具體呈現了資料中心容量快速擴張如何讓企業的潔淨能源承諾陷入困境——而這些承諾都是在用電需求成長較為緩慢的時期做出的。RE100會員資格一直是企業展現環保意圖的公開信號；失去這項標誌，反映出成員資格條件與大型科技公司為確保大規模可靠供電所做出的實際營運決策之間，存在直接衝突。

  Meta已確認投資興建新天然氣電廠——光是路易斯安那州一處設施就簽訂了10座電廠的合約——顯示該公司已研判：單靠採購再生能源，在時程與可靠性要求上已無法滿足目前的建設規模。即便退出RE100框架，Meta表示仍將持續購買再生能源，並在年度計算基礎上維持電力使用量的抵銷目標。
