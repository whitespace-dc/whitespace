  ## INA takes stake in Vena Group's 2.8 GW renewable portfolio

  Indonesia Investment Authority (INA), Indonesia's sovereign wealth fund, has invested in a 2.8-gigawatt (GW) portfolio of renewable energy projects owned by Singapore-based Vena Global Group Pte. Ltd., the companies announced Wednesday. The portfolio spans solar and wind assets in the Philippines and Indonesia, covering projects that are operational, under construction, contracted, or in earlier stages of development.

  Neither company disclosed the investment amount, INA's ownership stake, or the share of the portfolio attributable to each country.

  ## Investment rationale and structure

  INA Chief Executive Officer Oki Ramadhana said the fund seeks investments that meet commercial criteria while maintaining strategic relevance for Indonesia. He described the partnership as providing exposure to a diversified Southeast Asian renewable energy portfolio, including operating assets and future development opportunities within Indonesia.

  The transaction falls under Vena Group's Green Fund Partnership strategy, through which institutional investors participate in its renewable energy portfolio. Vena Group said in August 2025 it had announced plans to expand the scale and geographic reach of these partnerships across Japan, North Asia-Pacific, and Southeast Asia to support its construction program.

  ## Vena Group's footprint

  Vena Group Chief Executive Officer Nitin Apte said the partnership combines INA's long-term investment perspective with Vena Group's development, construction, and operating capabilities across two markets he described as important to the region's energy transition.

  In the Philippines, Vena Group operates through its subsidiary Vena Energy, which runs six power plants with a combined capacity of 330.80 megawatts (MW) — enough to supply a mid-sized city — across the provinces of Negros Occidental, Rizal, Leyte, Ilocos Norte, and Bukidnon.

  Vena Group's total renewable energy portfolio is approximately 38 GW and includes solar, onshore wind, offshore wind, battery energy storage, and green hydrogen and ammonia developments.

  ## Significance

  The deal illustrates a broader pattern of sovereign and institutional capital flowing into Southeast Asian clean energy infrastructure. By channeling a national wealth fund into a cross-border developer's pipeline, the transaction links Indonesia's state investment priorities directly to regional decarbonization and energy security goals. For Vena Group, securing INA as an institutional partner advances its stated strategy of attracting long-term capital to fund its construction program across Asia-Pacific.

  Vena Group said it plans to continue expanding its Green Fund Partnership model into additional markets including Japan and North Asia-Pacific.

  ## INA入股Vena集團2.8 GW再生能源投資組合

  印尼主權財富基金「印尼投資管理局」（Indonesia Investment Authority，INA）已投資新加坡商Vena Global Group Pte. Ltd.旗下一批規模達2.8吉瓦（GW）的再生能源專案，雙方於週三聯合宣布。此一投資組合橫跨菲律賓與印尼的太陽能及風力資產，涵蓋已投入運營、施工中、已簽約，以及處於早期開發階段的各類專案。

  兩家公司均未披露投資金額、INA的持股比例，亦未說明兩國資產在整體投資組合中各占多少份額。

  ## 投資邏輯與交易架構

  INA執行長奧基·拉馬達那（Oki Ramadhana）表示，該基金致力於尋求同時符合商業標準與印尼戰略利益的投資標的。他將此次合作定位為取得多元化東南亞再生能源投資組合曝險的機會，涵蓋現有運營資產，以及未來在印尼境內的開發機會。

  本交易屬於Vena集團「綠色基金合作夥伴策略」（Green Fund Partnership）的一環，機構投資人透過此架構參與其再生能源投資組合。Vena集團於2025年8月宣布，計畫擴大此類合作夥伴關係的規模與地理涵蓋範圍，延伸至日本、北亞太及東南亞各地，以支撐旗下建設計畫的推進。

  ## Vena集團的業務版圖

  Vena集團執行長尼廷·阿普特（Nitin Apte）表示，此次合作融合了INA的長期投資視野，以及Vena集團在兩大市場的開發、建設與營運能力——他形容這兩個市場對於區域能源轉型具有舉足輕重的地位。

  在菲律賓，Vena集團透過子公司Vena Energy進行運營，目前在內格羅斯西方省、黎剎省、萊特省、依羅戈北省及布基農省共設有六座電廠，總裝置容量達330.80百萬瓦（MW），足以供應一座中型城市所需的電力。

  Vena集團的再生能源投資組合總規模約達38吉瓦，涵蓋太陽能、陸域風力、離岸風力、電池儲能，以及綠氫與綠氨開發項目。

  ## 交易意義

  此筆交易呈現出主權資金與機構資本持續流入東南亞潔淨能源基礎建設的大趨勢。藉由將國家財富基金引入跨境開發商的專案管線，本交易將印尼的國家投資優先方向，與區域去碳化及能源安全目標直接掛鉤。對Vena集團而言，將INA納為機構合作夥伴，推進了其在亞太地區廣募長期資本、挹注建設計畫的既定策略。

  Vena集團表示，未來將持續把「綠色基金合作夥伴」模式拓展至日本及北亞太等更多市場。
