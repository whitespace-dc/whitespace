  ## Three-way MOU

  GE Vernova, the U.S. power equipment manufacturer; SK On, the battery unit of South Korea's SK Group; and Taiwan-listed Seetel New Energy (stock code 7740, listed on the emerging market board) signed a memorandum of understanding establishing a Taiwan-U.S.-South Korea strategic partnership, the companies announced November 16.

  The agreement assigns each party a distinct technical role. GE Vernova will supply high-power power conversion systems (PCS) — the equipment that converts stored battery energy into grid-ready alternating current — with the stated aim of simplifying system configurations, reducing energy losses, and lowering installation costs. SK On will provide lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery cells and modules; LFP chemistry uses iron and phosphate rather than nickel or cobalt, and is favored in stationary storage for its thermal stability and long cycle life. Seetel's subsidiary Aurosi, based in Taichung, will handle localized integration manufacturing, assembling the components from both international partners into complete energy storage systems.

  ## Site visit and executive participation

  To formalize the partnership, GE Vernova's North Asia and Southeast Asia PCS business head Shaopeng Ji and SK On's energy storage business operations vice president Minsuk Kwak traveled to Taiwan to visit the Aurosi battery module production facility in Taichung, according to the announcement.

  Minsuk Kwak said SK On's LFP cells offer high safety, long cycle life, and stable consistent performance suited to the long-term reliability requirements of large-scale storage systems, and that combining GE Vernova's power conversion technology with Seetel's system integration capability is expected to strengthen competitiveness in global markets.

  Shaopeng Ji said the global energy transition has reached a point where advanced technology alone is insufficient and that stable, trusted partnerships are required, adding that the three parties will work together to build high-resilience energy infrastructure for customers.

  Seetel chairman Lin Shengze said AI data centers' rising requirements for stable power supply and energy resilience make cross-border technology collaboration a key factor in capturing market opportunity, and that the combined strengths of the three parties will produce synergies greater than the sum of their parts.

  ## AI data center demand as the stated driver

  All three parties cited accelerating global AI data center construction as the primary demand signal behind the agreement. Data centers running large AI workloads require continuous, high-quality power; large-scale battery storage systems can provide backup power, peak shaving, and grid stabilization services that conventional grid connections alone may not guarantee.

  Seetel described itself as the leading shipper of battery modules in Taiwan and cited a track record in large-scale storage project development as the basis for being selected as the integration partner.

  ## Supply chain positioning

  The announcement explicitly frames the partnership within the context of non-Chinese, or what the industry terms "non-red," supply chains. The statement notes that the United States, Japan, and other major markets are tightening cybersecurity and supply chain resilience requirements, increasing the strategic value of supply chains that exclude Chinese-origin components.

  The three companies said they intend to use Taiwan as the starting point for the collaboration and will target Japan, the United States, and Canada as priority expansion markets.

  ## 三方MOU合作架構

  美國電力設備製造商GE Vernova、韓國SK集團旗下電池事業SK On，以及台灣興櫃上市公司晰特新能源（股票代號7740）於11月16日宣布簽署備忘錄，建立台美韓三方策略合作夥伴關係。

  三方於協議中各司其職，分工明確。GE Vernova負責提供高功率電力轉換系統（PCS）——即將電池儲存的能量轉換為電網所需交流電的核心設備——目標在於簡化系統架構、降低能量損耗並壓低安裝成本。SK On則供應磷酸鐵鋰（LFP）電池芯與模組；LFP化學體系以鐵和磷酸鹽取代鎳或鈷，在固定式儲能應用上以熱穩定性佳、循環壽命長著稱。晰特旗下子公司Aurosi位於台中，將承擔在地化整合製造任務，負責將兩家國際夥伴的元件組裝為完整的儲能系統。

  ## 高層親赴台灣，正式見證合作

  為正式確立合作關係，GE Vernova北亞暨東南亞PCS業務負責人紀少鵬（Shaopeng Ji）與SK On儲能事業營運副總裁郭敏碩（Minsuk Kwak）專程赴台，前往台中參觀Aurosi電池模組生產設施。

  郭敏碩表示，SK On的LFP電池芯具備高安全性、長循環壽命及穩定一致的性能，能夠滿足大規模儲能系統對長期可靠度的嚴格要求；他指出，GE Vernova的電力轉換技術與晰特的系統整合能力相互結合，預計將大幅提升三方在全球市場上的競爭力。

  紀少鵬則表示，全球能源轉型已走到單憑先進技術仍嫌不足的階段，穩固且值得信賴的夥伴關係不可或缺，三方將攜手為客戶打造高韌性的能源基礎設施。

  晰特董事長林聖澤表示，AI資料中心對穩定供電與能源韌性的要求與日俱增，跨境技術合作已成掌握市場機會的關鍵因素，三方結合各自優勢，將產生遠超過個別加總的協同效益。

  ## AI資料中心需求為核心驅動力

  三方均將全球AI資料中心建設加速，列為促成此次合作的首要市場訊號。執行大規模AI工作負載的資料中心，需要持續、高品質的電力供應；大型電池儲能系統可提供備用電力、削峰填谷及電網穩定化服務，彌補傳統電網連線可能無法單獨保障的供電品質。

  晰特自述為台灣電池模組出貨量最大的業者，並以大規模儲能專案的開發實績，作為獲選整合夥伴的依據。

  ## 供應鏈布局：非紅色供應鏈的戰略價值

  此次公告明確將三方合作定位於非中國、亦即業界所稱「非紅色」供應鏈的框架之下。聲明指出，美國、日本及其他主要市場正持續強化網路安全與供應鏈韌性要求，不含中國原產元件的供應鏈戰略價值隨之水漲船高。

  三家公司表示，計畫以台灣為合作起點，優先向日本、美國及加拿大拓展市場。
