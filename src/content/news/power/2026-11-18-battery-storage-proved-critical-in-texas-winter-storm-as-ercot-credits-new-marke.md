body_en: |
  Battery energy storage systems were indispensable in covering peak-demand reserves during Winter Storm Fern, which struck Texas in January 2026, while the grid remained stable without Energy Emergency Alerts, public conservation calls or systemwide outages, according to ERCOT's post-event report as cited by Xpansiv senior vice president Brian Fellon. Unlike Winter Storm Uri in 2021, Fellon writes, the lights stayed on.

  ERCOT (the Electric Reliability Council of Texas) implemented RTC+B (Real-Time Co-optimization Plus Batteries) in December 2025, one month before the storm. The rule co-optimizes energy and reserves in real time while accounting for what battery resources can actually deliver. When a localized transmission emergency occurred during Fern, ERCOT credited RTC+B with redistributing ancillary services across the system, Fellon writes in a Utility Dive analysis.

  ## Why load growth is making storage essential

  The U.S. electricity sector is undergoing what Fellon calls its most abrupt transformation since deregulation, driven by accelerating load growth from data centers, transportation electrification and industrial demand. After nearly two decades of flat demand, the forward demand curve is sharply steepening.

  ERCOT's long-range planning assumptions project peak load reaching more than four times today's record levels. Across the PJM Interconnection footprint, the grid operator covering much of the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest, forecasted load growth is raising expectations that tens of gigawatts of new supply capacity will be needed over the coming decade to maintain system reliability, Fellon writes.

  That growth is colliding with aging grid infrastructure, constrained transmission capacity, record-long interconnection queues and a shifting policy landscape, Fellon writes. The result is a reconfiguration of wholesale power markets and what Fellon describes as the elevation of battery storage to essential grid infrastructure.

  ## How organized markets dispatch batteries

  In organized wholesale markets, dispatch is not a single daily event. It is a sequence of day-ahead scheduling, intraday adjustments and real-time dispatch executed in 5-minute price intervals, Fellon explains. For batteries, every dispatch decision is inseparable from state of charge (SoC), which measures how much energy a battery currently holds relative to its full capacity.

  Every megawatt discharged at one moment reduces what the battery can deliver later. That physical constraint makes SoC management central to both reliability and revenue, and it means dispatch decisions cannot be made without accounting for current and forecast battery conditions.

  FERC (the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission) addressed storage market access through Order No. 841, which requires ISOs (Independent System Operators) and RTOs (Regional Transmission Organizations) to remove barriers to storage participation and adopt participation models that recognize storage's physical and operational characteristics. The order expanded the range of products storage can provide and the revenue opportunities available to storage owners. ERCOT, which is not governed by FERC, went further by implementing RTC+B, Fellon notes.

  ## What reliable storage operations require

  Unreliable data or inconsistent operations translate directly into weaker project economics after commercial operations begin, Fellon writes. Market submissions only generate value if they are timely, accurate and compliant. Fellon outlines four operational requirements for battery owners and operators:

  - **Data and telemetry as mission-critical infrastructure.** Validate data quality and latency end-to-end, not just at the device level.
  - **Multi-market playbooks over single-product strategies.** Markets change; operators need adaptive capability to shift with them.
  - **Market submission as a core operating capability.** Whether built in-house or outsourced, deploy resilient controls, versioned workflows and exception handling.
  - **SoC discipline.** Establish state-of-charge guardrails tied to existing obligations, revenue targets and forecast uncertainty, and review performance after unforeseen events.

  Wholesale markets do not pause after business hours, Fellon notes. Scarcity pricing, forced outages and weather disruptions often occur overnight or on weekends, requiring defined escalation pathways, telemetry monitoring and situational awareness tools for around-the-clock readiness.

  ## The case for faster market reform

  Fellon argues that other grid operators should match ERCOT's pace of market reform, though not necessarily its specific design. ERCOT treats market design as ongoing work: when storage became a core part of the Texas fleet, the rules were updated accordingly; when large-load requests began reshaping interconnection, regulators moved promptly to revise how those loads are studied, prioritized and approved. Some changes have been contentious, Fellon acknowledges, but the gap between identifying a problem and implementing a fix has been relatively short.

  Most of the country operates on a slower reform cycle, with changes moving through multi-year stakeholder processes, compliance filings and phased implementations. Operators running assets across multiple markets effectively manage separate businesses under divergent rules governing state-of-charge accounting, telemetry requirements and bid formats, Fellon writes. Closer alignment on those standards would lower the cost of entry for smaller operators and reduce operational complexity across the industry.

  The RTC+B change also altered how batteries earn revenue in the Texas market: the same rule that enabled ancillary-service redistribution during Fern shifted the mix of market products through which storage operators generate returns, Fellon writes. As market rules open new ways for storage to participate, the revenue-generating mix will keep shifting.
body_zh: |
  2026年1月，冬季風暴「蕨」（Winter Storm Fern）侵襲德州，電池儲能系統（Battery Energy Storage Systems）在滿足尖峰需求備用容量方面扮演了不可或缺的角色。整個期間，德州電網始終保持穩定，未發出能源緊急警報（Energy Emergency Alerts）、未呼籲民眾節電，也未發生全系統停電。上述結論來自ERCOT（德州電力可靠度委員會）的事後報告，由Xpansiv公司資深副總裁布萊恩·費倫（Brian Fellon）加以引述。費倫在能源媒體《Utility Dive》的分析文章中指出，與2021年的冬季風暴「烏里」（Winter Storm Uri）不同，這一次德州的電燈沒有熄滅。

  ERCOT於2025年12月——即風暴發生前一個月——正式啟用「即時共同最佳化加電池規則」（Real-Time Co-optimization Plus Batteries，簡稱RTC+B）。該規則在即時調度中同步最佳化電力供應與備用服務，並充分考量電池資源實際可交付的能力。費倫寫道，當風暴「蕨」期間發生局部輸電緊急事件時，ERCOT正是藉助RTC+B在全系統重新分配輔助服務，順利化解危機。

  ## 負載成長為何使儲能成為必需品

  費倫指出，美國電力部門正經歷自電力市場解除管制以來最劇烈的一次轉型，驅動力來自資料中心擴張、交通電氣化及工業需求激增所帶動的負載高速成長。近二十年的需求平穩期已告終結，前瞻需求曲線正急速攀升。

  ERCOT的長期規劃預測顯示，未來尖峰負載可能超過當前紀錄水準的四倍。在覆蓋美國中大西洋地區及中西部大部分地區的PJM互聯電網（PJM Interconnection）服務範圍內，費倫寫道，負載成長預測已引發外界預期：未來十年需要新增數十GW（十億瓦）的供應容量，才能維持系統可靠度。

  費倫指出，這波成長浪潮正與老化的電網基礎設施、受限的輸電容量、創歷史紀錄的待並網排隊量，以及快速變動的政策環境相互碰撞。其結果是躉售電力市場的全面重組，而電池儲能也因此被費倫形容為「電網不可或缺的基礎設施」。

  ## 有組織的電力市場如何調度電池

  費倫解釋，在有組織的躉售電力市場中，調度並非每天只發生一次，而是以五分鐘電價區間執行的日前排程（day-ahead scheduling）、日內調整（intraday adjustments）與即時調度（real-time dispatch）連續組合而成的流程。對電池而言，每一個調度決策都與荷電狀態（State of Charge，簡稱SoC）密不可分——SoC衡量的是電池目前的電量相對於滿容量的比例。

  當下每放出一MW（百萬瓦），就會減少電池稍後的可供電量。這一物理限制使SoC管理成為可靠度與收益的核心所在，也意味著任何調度決策都必須同時考量當前及預測的電池狀態。

  美國聯邦能源監管委員會（FERC）透過第841號命令（Order No. 841）處理了儲能的市場准入問題。該命令要求各獨立系統運營商（ISO）與區域輸電組織（RTO）消除儲能參與市場的障礙，並建立能夠反映儲能物理特性與運行特性的參與模型，藉此擴大儲能可提供的服務種類，以及業主可獲得的收益來源。費倫指出，不受FERC管轄的ERCOT更進一步，直接推行了RTC+B規則。

  ## 可靠儲能運行所需的條件

  費倫寫道，資料品質不佳或運行不穩定，在商業運轉開始後會直接拖累專案的經濟效益。市場申報只有在即時、準確且符合規範的前提下才能產生價值。費倫為電池業主與運營商列出四項運行要求：

  - **資料與遙測視為關鍵任務基礎設施。** 須從端到端——而非僅在裝置層面——驗證資料品質與延遲表現。
  - **多市場操作手冊取代單一產品策略。** 市場規則持續演變；運營商需要具備隨之調整的應變能力。
  - **市場申報視為核心運行能力。** 無論自建或外包，均須部署具備韌性的控制系統、版本化工作流程及例外處理機制。
  - **荷電狀態紀律。** 建立與既有義務、收益目標及預測不確定性相對應的SoC防護邊界，並在發生意外事件後檢討績效表現。

  費倫指出，躉售電力市場不會在下班後暫停運作。稀缺性電價、強制停機與天氣干擾往往在夜間或週末發生，因此業者必須建立明確的升級應變路徑、遙測監控機制，以及全天候備戰的態勢感知工具。

  ## 加快市場改革的論據

  費倫主張，其他電網運營商應效法ERCOT的改革步調，但不必複製其具體設計。ERCOT將市場設計視為持續進行的工作：當儲能成為德州電力機組的核心組成，規則隨之更新；當大型負載申請開始重塑並網流程，監管機構便迅速修訂相關負載的審查、優先排序與核准方式。費倫承認，部分改革確有爭議，但從發現問題到落地解決，其間的時間差相對較短。

  全美大多數地區的改革節奏較為緩慢，各項變革須歷經多年的利害關係人程序、合規申報與分階段實施。費倫寫道，跨市場運營資產的業者，實際上等於在各自適用不同規則的多個市場中分別經營獨立業務，因為各地在SoC計算方式、遙測要求與報價格式上均存在差異。費倫指出，若能在上述標準上加強對齊，將可降低小型業者的進入門檻，並減少整個產業的運營複雜度。

  RTC+B規則的推行也改變了電池在德州市場的收益方式：同一條規則——在風暴「蕨」期間促成輔助服務重新分配的那條規則——同時也改變了儲能業者透過不同市場產品賺取報酬的組合。費倫寫道，隨著市場規則持續開放儲能的參與空間，收益來源的組合將持續演變。
