body_en: |
  NextEra Energy has secured $5.7 billion in funding to develop three federally backed energy hubs in Texas and Pennsylvania, Chairman, President and CEO John Ketchum said Wednesday during the company's third-quarter earnings call, with sovereign backing from Japan and South Korea alongside the U.S. Department of Commerce.

  ## Three hubs, up to 16.5 GW

  Two of the hubs, in Texas and Pennsylvania, are backed by the Government of Japan and the U.S. Department of Commerce. An initial $3.3 billion released in August will fund procurement of turbines and other long-lead equipment. NextEra plans to build up to 10 gigawatts (GW) of gas-fired generation across the two sites, targeting completion in 2032.

  A third hub, backed by the Republic of Korea and the U.S. Department of Commerce, will pair 6.5 GW of natural gas generation with a 5 GW data center campus in Encinal, Texas, with the first phase potentially online as early as 2029.

  Ketchum said NextEra has a pipeline of 30 to 40 additional hub projects beyond those already under agreement.

  ## Capital-light earnings model

  NextEra will not invest its own capital in any of the three hubs, Ketchum said, but expects the projects to generate at least half the earnings the company would realize by owning and operating the facilities outright. Adding co-located battery storage at the hubs could increase that return, he said.

  The company has also joined a coalition of energy and infrastructure companies to develop a privately owned hub in western Kentucky, combining 2 GW of natural gas generation with 2.6 GW of battery storage.

  ## Customer demand shifting toward storage

  A Goldman Sachs analyst on the call flagged that NextEra Energy Resources — the company's competitive power development arm — has been trimming its solar backlog, with projects slated for 2028 to 2029 down half a gigawatt from the prior-quarter report. Brian Bolster, president and CEO of NextEra Energy Resources, attributed the change to shifting customer priorities, not rising solar costs.

  "As we're out with customers right now, we've seen a focus on capacity. That's why we've seen a big pickup in storage. We are starting to see our customers look for more energy products," Bolster said.

  The company added 2.4 GW of battery storage contracts in the third quarter, following 2 GW signed in the second quarter. NextEra Energy Resources carries a total development backlog of 36.7 GW. Since January, the company has also signed new power purchase agreements — long-term contracts committing a buyer to purchase a generator's output — covering 2.4 GW of existing projects, out of a 7.5 GW recontracting opportunity it identified last year.

  ## Dominion merger faces Virginia opposition

  Ketchum said the company is prepared to defend its planned merger with Dominion Energy before Virginia regulators and officials. The deal has drawn opposition from Lieutenant Governor Ghazala Hashmi and Governor Abigail Spanberger, a Democrat, who has proposed a six-year rate freeze as a condition of approval. NextEra plans to extend bill credits for Virginia customers from two to four years and expand support for bill assistance programs and workforce development; the merger would also create 1,000 jobs in the state, Ketchum said.

  "If we cannot get it done, I do not know who else can in terms of meeting the requirements of the Virginia Clean Economy Act," he said.

  The Institute for Energy Economics and Financial Analysis (IEEFA) issued a report this month questioning the merged company's clean energy commitment, noting that the merger plans do not mention offshore wind and that NextEra has been dismissive of it. "By overlooking offshore wind and paying lip service to coal facilities, it is clear that NextEra is looking out for its shareholders first and foremost, and not Virginia ratepayers," said Dennis Wamsted, an energy finance analyst at IEEFA.

  ## Florida operations, litigation

  Florida Power & Light (FP&L), NextEra's regulated utility subsidiary, restored service to 97% of customers within three days of Hurricane Isaias, which struck Florida's panhandle earlier this month. The restoration effort required $450 million to $500 million, according to the company's quarterly filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. FP&L's large-load pipeline — commitments to serve high-consumption industrial and commercial customers — grew to 8 GW through 2032, up from a 6 GW target set last year.

  Company executives declined to discuss specifics of large-load development projects in Florida, citing the complexity and scale of the negotiations.

  "These are complicated deals, multibillion-dollar deals that take some time, and given the outside environment we're working with the local communities to build the consensus and support so we can move forward before we announce these deals," FP&L President and CEO Scott Bores said.

  NextEra faces multiple class action securities lawsuits, including one alleging the company made false or misleading statements about its campaign finance and political activities in Florida. A court in August denied without prejudice a motion to settle that suit for $150 million. The settling parties have since provided additional information to the court and again requested approval.

  FP&L executives said the company expects to announce a large-load development project in Florida before the end of the year.

  ## 三座樞紐，最高16.5 GW裝置容量

  NextEra Energy宣布取得57億美元資金，將在德州與賓州開發三座由聯邦政府背書的能源樞紐。董事長兼總裁暨執行長約翰·凱奇姆（John Ketchum）於週三公司第三季財報法說會上公布這項消息，三座樞紐分別獲日本政府、南韓政府及美國商務部的主權支持。

  其中兩座位於德州與賓州的樞紐，由日本政府與美國商務部共同支持。今年八月首批核撥的33億美元，將用於採購燃氣渦輪機及其他前置採購設備。NextEra計畫在這兩個場址合計興建最高10吉瓦（GW）的燃氣發電機組，預定於2032年完工。

  第三座樞紐由韓國政府與美國商務部聯合支持，地點選在德州恩西納爾（Encinal），規劃搭配6.5 GW天然氣發電廠及一座5 GW規模的資料中心園區，最快2029年即可讓第一期上線運轉。

  凱奇姆表示，除已簽約的三座樞紐外，NextEra手中還有30至40座樞紐專案正在洽談推進。

  ## 輕資本獲利模式

  凱奇姆表示，NextEra不會在這三座樞紐投入自有資本，但預計這些專案所帶來的獲利，至少相當於公司直接持有並自營設施所能取得收益的一半。他並指出，若能在樞紐內加裝共置電池儲能系統，整體回報還有進一步提升空間。

  此外，NextEra已加入一個由能源與基礎建設業者組成的聯盟，共同在肯塔基州西部開發一座私人持有的能源樞紐，結合2 GW天然氣發電與2.6 GW電池儲能容量。

  ## 客戶需求轉向儲能

  電話會議中，高盛（Goldman Sachs）一位分析師指出，NextEra Energy Resources——集團負責競爭性電力開發的事業部門——已縮減太陽能待建訂單，2028至2029年間預定完工的專案較上一季財報減少了0.5 GW。NextEra Energy Resources總裁暨執行長布萊恩·博爾斯特（Brian Bolster）表示，此一變化源於客戶優先考量的轉移，並非太陽能成本攀升所致。

  「目前我們與客戶接觸時，明顯感受到他們更重視裝置容量。這也是為什麼儲能訂單大幅增加的原因。我們也開始看到客戶轉而尋求更多電能型產品，」博爾斯特說。

  公司在第三季新簽2.4 GW電池儲能合約，延續第二季簽署2 GW的動能。NextEra Energy Resources目前總開發待建訂單達36.7 GW。今年一月以來，公司亦針對現有專案新簽涵蓋2.4 GW的購電協議（Power Purchase Agreement，即買方承諾長期購買發電商電力輸出的合約），是去年識別出的7.5 GW重新簽約機會的一部分。

  ## 併購Dominion案遭維吉尼亞州反對

  凱奇姆表示，公司已準備好向維吉尼亞州監管機構與官員捍衛計畫中的Dominion Energy併購案。此案遭到副州長賈扎拉·哈什米（Ghazala Hashmi）以及民主黨籍州長艾比蓋爾·斯潘伯格（Abigail Spanberger）的反對，斯潘伯格並提出以凍結電價六年作為批准條件。NextEra計畫將維吉尼亞州用電戶的電費帳單折抵期限從兩年延長至四年，並擴大電費補助計畫與人才培訓方面的支援；凱奇姆表示，本次合併還將在維吉尼亞州創造1,000個工作機會。

  「若我們無法完成這件事，我不知道還有誰能在符合維吉尼亞州清潔能源經濟法案要求的前提下達成目標，」他說。

  能源經濟與金融分析研究所（IEEFA）本月發布報告，質疑合併後公司對潔淨能源的承諾，指出合併計畫對離岸風電隻字未提，且NextEra對此議題態度消極。「由於忽視離岸風電、對煤電設施口惠而實不至，NextEra顯然把股東利益擺在首位，而非維吉尼亞州用電消費者，」IEEFA能源財務分析師丹尼斯·瓦姆斯特德（Dennis Wamsted）如是說。

  ## 佛羅里達業務與訴訟

  NextEra旗下受管制電力子公司佛羅里達電力與照明（Florida Power & Light，FP&L），在伊薩亞斯颶風（Hurricane Isaias）侵襲佛羅里達州狹長地帶後三天內，即完成了97%用戶的電力恢復。根據公司提交給美國證券交易委員會（SEC）的季報，此次搶修作業耗資約4.5億至5億美元。FP&L的大型負載管線——亦即對高用電量工業與商業客戶的供電承諾——已成長至2032年前合計8 GW，高於去年訂定的6 GW目標。

  公司高層對佛羅里達州大型負載開發專案的細節未置可否，理由是相關談判規模龐大、結構複雜。

  「這些都是複雜的多億美元大案，需要時間推進。加上外部環境的因素，我們正積極與當地社區合作，建立共識與支持，務求在宣布之前穩固基礎、順利推進，」FP&L總裁暨執行長史考特·博雷斯（Scott Bores）說。

  NextEra目前面臨多起集體訴訟，其中一起指控公司就其在佛羅里達州的競選財務與政治活動發表不實或誤導性陳述。今年八月，法院雖駁回了以1.5億美元達成和解的聲請，但保留了重新聲請的空間。和解雙方此後向法院提交補充資料，並再度請求批准和解。

  FP&L高層表示，公司預計在今年底前宣布一項佛羅里達州大型負載開發專案。
