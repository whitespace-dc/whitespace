body_en: |
  Taiwan's two leading power supply manufacturers, Delta Electronics and Lite-On Technology, are set to face investor questions about the commercial timeline for Nvidia's 800-volt direct current (800VDC) AI server power architecture after research firm SemiAnalysis reported the rollout may slip past 2028. The companies hold separate analyst briefings on July 30 and July 31.

  ## SemiAnalysis report

  SemiAnalysis said that Nvidia, which has been driving the industry shift to 800VDC high-voltage direct current (HVDC) power architecture, may not deploy the technology at meaningful scale until after 2028. The architecture is designed to handle the power requirements of next-generation AI server cabinets, each of which can draw up to 600 kilowatts (kW) — a demand that existing server power infrastructure was not built to sustain at that level. Delta Electronics and Lite-On Technology are identified as primary beneficiaries of the transition.

  ## Delta Electronics' prior guidance

  Delta Electronics addressed the 800VDC schedule at its most recent earnings briefing. The company said products using a ±400VDC intermediate configuration, whose installation requirements are similar to existing setups, are on track for some shipments this year, though volumes will be limited. For the 800VDC standard that Nvidia is leading, Delta said meaningful shipments are not expected until 2027. A post-2028 delay, if the SemiAnalysis report proves accurate, would push that window out further.

  ## Revenue and margins

  Both companies reported strong results heading into the briefings. Delta Electronics posted second-quarter revenue of NT$183.256 billion, up 47.7% year-on-year and 15% from the first quarter, a quarterly record. First-half revenue reached NT$342.609 billion, up 41% year-on-year.

  Lite-On Technology reported second-quarter revenue of NT$52.704 billion, up 30.4% year-on-year and 21.4% from the prior quarter. First-half revenue was NT$96.112 billion, up 25.08% year-on-year.

  Delta's first-quarter gross margin reached 37%, a gain of 5.2 percentage points from the same period a year earlier. Delta's chairman said at the previous briefing that the high-margin profile is expected to hold as the share of AI products in total shipments continues to rise.

  Lite-On's president said at the company's most recent analyst meeting that the first quarter represented the year's operating low point and that the AI product share of total shipments is expected to increase each quarter through the rest of the year, supporting growth in both revenue and profit.

  ## What the delay would mean

  A post-2028 delay in 800VDC adoption, if confirmed, would push back a significant revenue catalyst for both companies. Delta and Lite-On have been positioned as the main beneficiaries of the shift to HVDC power delivery that Nvidia is driving across its AI server ecosystem. The July 30 and July 31 analyst briefings will be the first occasions for management at each company to publicly address the SemiAnalysis findings and provide updated shipment guidance.

  台灣兩大電源供應器廠商台達電子與光寶科技，即將在7月30日與31日分別召開法說會，屆時預料將面對投資人針對輝達800伏特直流電（800VDC）AI伺服器電源架構商業化時程的提問——研究機構SemiAnalysis日前發布報告，指出該架構的大規模導入時程恐將延至2028年後。

  ## SemiAnalysis報告

  SemiAnalysis指出，一直主導業界轉向800VDC高壓直流電（HVDC）電源架構的輝達，可能要到2028年之後才能大規模部署這項技術。該架構是為了因應下一代AI伺服器機櫃的用電需求而設計，每個機櫃的耗電量最高可達600千瓦（kW）——已遠超現有伺服器電源基礎設施所能持續承受的水準。台達電子與光寶科技均被點名為此次架構轉型的主要受益者。

  ## 台達電子先前的展望

  台達電子在最近一次法說會上已就800VDC時程提出說明。公司表示，採用±400VDC中間配置的產品，其安裝需求與現行設備相近，預計今年將有部分出貨，但出貨量仍屬有限。至於輝達所主導的800VDC標準，台達電子表示，預計要到2027年才會有大規模出貨。若SemiAnalysis報告屬實，導致時程延至2028年後，上述出貨窗口將進一步推遲。

  ## 營收與毛利率

  兩家公司在法說會前公布的業績均相當亮眼。台達電子第二季營收為新台幣1,832億5,600萬元，年增47.7%、季增15%，創單季歷史新高；上半年累計營收達新台幣3,426億900萬元，年增41%。

  光寶科技第二季營收為新台幣527億400萬元，年增30.4%、季增21.4%；上半年累計營收為新台幣961億1,200萬元，年增25.08%。

  台達電子第一季毛利率達37%，較去年同期提升5.2個百分點。董事長在前次法說會上表示，隨著AI產品佔整體出貨比重持續提升，高毛利的走勢預計將延續。

  光寶科技總裁在最近一次法說會上表示，第一季為今年的營運低點，AI產品佔整體出貨比重預計將逐季攀升，支撐全年營收與獲利的持續成長。

  ## 延遲的潛在影響

  若800VDC導入時程確認延至2028年後，對兩家公司而言，將推遲一項重要的營收催化劑。台達電子與光寶科技一直被市場視為輝達在AI伺服器生態系中推動HVDC電源傳輸轉型的主要受益者。7月30日與31日的法說會，將是兩家公司管理層首度在公開場合回應SemiAnalysis研究報告、並提供最新出貨展望的機會。
