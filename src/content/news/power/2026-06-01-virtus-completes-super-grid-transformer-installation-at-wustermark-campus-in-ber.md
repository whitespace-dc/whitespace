# Virtus Completes Super-Grid Transformer Installation at Wustermark Campus in Berlin

Virtus has completed the installation of two super-grid transformers at its Wustermark Campus in Berlin, Germany. The transformers will support 300MW power delivery capacity, enhancing the campus's electrical infrastructure.

# Virtus 於柏林 Wustermark 園區完成超級電網變壓器安裝
