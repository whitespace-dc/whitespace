body_en: |
  The Philippine Department of Energy (DoE) said the country is on course to bring 10 gigawatts (GW) of new power capacity online by 2028, with 60 completed projects accounting for 4,167 megawatts (MW) — or 4.17 GW — already operational.

  DoE Undersecretary Mario C. Marasigan made the announcement at a briefing Monday. "We remain on track to meet our target of completing 10,000 MW by 2028," he said.

  ## Capacity completed and pipeline

  The 60 projects now in service include 46 conventional power plants and renewable energy installations, as well as 14 battery energy storage systems (BESS). BESS units store surplus electrical energy from the grid and discharge it when demand exceeds supply or when grid stability requires support; Marasigan said the installed BESS capacity is designated as a power reserve for the Luzon and Visayas grids.

  Marasigan said a further 200 power projects are under DoE monitoring and are due for completion within the next two years — the remaining window before the current administration leaves office. That pipeline, combined with projects already finished, is expected to push cumulative new capacity to 6,587 MW by the end of 2026. The regional breakdown of that year-end figure: 5,341 MW on the main Luzon grid, 733 MW in the Visayas island group, and 513 MW in Mindanao.

  ## Renewable energy outlook

  Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin said the Philippines expects to add 2,000 MW of renewable energy capacity in 2027 alone. "We will continue adding more capacity, particularly from renewable energy sources that are not dependent on oil," Garin said, adding that the additions are aimed at addressing both seasonal demand pressures — including the effects of the El Niño weather pattern and the summer peak — and longer-term energy requirements.

  ## What the numbers mean

  One gigawatt is roughly the output of a large conventional power station and is sufficient to supply several hundred thousand homes under typical load conditions. Reaching 6.587 GW of new capacity by year-end would represent about 66 percent of the 10-GW goal with two years remaining on the timeline.

  The DoE's public progress update signals that the government views the 2028 deadline as achievable given the volume of projects currently in its monitoring pipeline. The 2,000 MW of renewable additions projected for 2027 would, if realized, further close the gap toward the final target.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓能源部（DoE）表示，該國正穩步推進2028年前新增10吉瓦（GW）電力裝置容量的計畫，目前已有60項竣工專案合計4,167百萬瓦（MW），即4.17GW的容量正式投入運轉。

  能源部次長馬里歐·C·馬拉西根（Mario C. Marasigan）於週一的記者會上宣布上述進展。他表示：「我們仍在既定軌道上，預計在2028年前完成10,000MW的目標。」

  ## 已完成容量與待建管線

  目前投入運轉的60項專案涵蓋46座傳統發電廠與再生能源設施，以及14套電池儲能系統（BESS）。電池儲能系統可儲存電網中的多餘電能，並在用電需求超出供應量或電網穩定度需要支援時放電。馬拉西根表示，已安裝的BESS容量被指定作為呂宋（Luzon）及維薩亞斯（Visayas）電網的電力備用。

  馬拉西根進一步指出，另有200項電力專案正在能源部監管之下，預計將在未來兩年內竣工——這也是現任政府任期屆滿前的最後期限。這批待建專案加上已完成的案場，預計將使新增裝置容量於2026年底前累計達到6,587MW。就地區分布而言，主要的呂宋電網將佔5,341MW，維薩亞斯群島為733MW，民答那峨（Mindanao）則為513MW。

  ## 再生能源展望

  能源部長莎朗·S·加林（Sharon S. Garin）表示，菲律賓預計僅2027年一年便可新增2,000MW的再生能源裝置容量。「我們將持續增加容量，特別是不依賴石油的再生能源。」加林說，這些新增容量旨在因應季節性用電需求壓力——包括聖嬰現象（El Niño）氣候型態與夏季用電尖峰的衝擊——以及中長期的電力需求。

  ## 數字的意涵

  一吉瓦大致相當於一座大型傳統發電廠的輸出功率，在一般負載條件下足以供應數十萬戶家庭用電。若2026年底前累計新增容量能達到6.587GW，將代表已完成10GW目標的約66%，距2028年的期限尚餘兩年。

  能源部此次主動對外公布進度，顯示政府認為以目前監管管線中的專案規模而言，2028年的期限是可以如期實現的。若2027年預計新增的2,000MW再生能源容量如期到位，將進一步縮短與最終目標之間的差距。
