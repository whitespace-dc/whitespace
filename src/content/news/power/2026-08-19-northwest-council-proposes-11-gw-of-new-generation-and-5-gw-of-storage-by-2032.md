body_en: |
  The Northwest Power and Conservation Council (NWPCC) released a draft of its Ninth Power Plan on Aug. 12, 2026, calling for roughly 11 gigawatts of new generation capacity and 5.2 gigawatts of energy storage by 2032 to meet accelerating electricity demand across a four-state region of the Pacific Northwest.

  The council unanimously approved the draft for public comment. The portfolio includes approximately 9 gigawatts of renewables, 2.1 gigawatts of natural gas, and 5.2 gigawatts of energy storage. The NWPCC estimates fixed costs for the portfolio will reach $2.3 billion by 2032, equivalent to about 0.15% of the four-state region's $1.46 trillion gross domestic product as of 2025.

  Regional electricity use is projected to grow by about 50% over the next six years. Over the plan's full 20-year outlook, demand could nearly double, according to the council.

  ## What is driving demand

  Data centers are expected to be the primary driver of electricity demand growth in the near term, the plan states. Over the longer term, electrification of transportation, buildings, and industrial processes is expected to add further load across Oregon, Washington, Idaho, and Montana.

  The draft plan also proposes energy-efficiency standards for new data centers and calls for working with data center operators on flexible electricity consumption, including demand response programs and use of backup generation.

  ## How the resource mix is structured

  The plan does not set state-by-state capacity targets; siting decisions are left to individual utilities. However, the modeling underlying the plan shows most of the roughly 2,100 megawatts of new natural gas capacity being built in Idaho and Montana. Oregon state restrictions preclude new gas plants there, and Washington limits new gas development as well.

  Jennifer Light, the council's power division director, described the portfolio as calibrated to perform across a wide range of outcomes. "If the loads came in only on the low end, it would be a bit overbuilt. If it came in only on the high end, it wouldn't be fully adequate. But when you look at that range and uncertainty, it is adequate across that risk," Light said in a media briefing.

  The council said its planning process was tested against variability in load growth, extreme weather, resource and transmission availability, and operations of the Columbia Basin hydrosystem.

  ## Bonneville Power Administration's role

  The Bonneville Power Administration (BPA), the federal agency that markets electricity generated by the Columbia River dam system, is central to the plan. BPA is legally required to acquire resources consistent with the council's strategy. The draft anticipates that BPA may need to procure new generating capacity, not just energy efficiency measures. The council's draft urges BPA to favor renewables for energy needs and to weigh battery storage against new natural gas plants when evaluating capacity options.

  ## Transmission assumptions

  The plan's resource projections rest on assumptions about future transmission infrastructure. Studies by the Western Transmission Expansion Coalition (WestTEC), an industry-led transmission planning effort, underpin the plan's assumptions about how much new generation the grid can accommodate. Light noted that the strategy assumes some of that transmission gets built and said the council will track transmission progress through midterm assessments.

  ## What comes next

  The NWPCC will hold public hearings in Oregon, Washington, Idaho, and Montana in September and October 2026. The council is targeting final adoption of the Ninth Power Plan by the end of 2026 or early 2027. The council is required by law to review its plan at least every five years; it adopted its previous power plan in February 2022.

  ## 西北電力委員會提案：2032年前新增11 GW發電與5 GW儲能

  西北電力暨保育委員會（NWPCC）於2026年8月12日發布第九期電力計畫草案，計畫在太平洋西北四州加速成長的電力需求驅動下，於2032年前新增約11吉瓦新發電容量及5.2吉瓦儲能設施。

  委員會以全票通過，將草案送交公眾意見徵詢。此一資源組合包含約9吉瓦的再生能源、2.1吉瓦的天然氣，以及5.2吉瓦的儲能設施。NWPCC估計，至2032年，這套組合的固定成本將達23億美元，約相當於2025年四州合計1.46兆美元國內生產毛額的0.15%。

  區域電力需求預估將在未來六年內成長約五成；在計畫完整的二十年展望期內，需求量更可能近乎翻倍。

  ## 需求成長的驅動因素

  計畫草案指出，資料中心預計將是近期電力需求成長的主要推手。就較長期而言，奧勒岡、華盛頓、愛達荷及蒙大拿四州的交通運輸、建築物及工業製程電氣化，預計將進一步推升整體用電負荷。

  草案亦提出針對新建資料中心的能源效率標準，並呼籲與資料中心業者合作，推動彈性用電機制，包括需量反應方案及備用發電設備的活用。

  ## 資源組合結構

  此計畫並未設定各州的容量分配目標，選址決策交由各地電力公司自行決定。然而，計畫所依據的模型顯示，約2,100百萬瓦的新增天然氣容量，大部分將建置於愛達荷州及蒙大拿州。奧勒岡州法規限制禁止新建天然氣電廠，華盛頓州亦對新增天然氣發展設有管制。

  委員會電力部門主任珍妮佛．萊特（Jennifer Light）表示，這套資源組合的設計旨在因應各種可能情境。她在媒體說明會上指出：「若負荷成長偏向低估，建置容量會稍微過剩；若只看高估情境，則不夠充裕。但綜觀整體區間與不確定性，在風險範圍內是足夠的。」

  委員會表示，其規劃流程已針對負荷成長變動、極端氣候、資源與輸電可用性，以及哥倫比亞盆地水力發電系統的運作等情境逐一進行壓力測試。

  ## 邦納維爾電力局的角色

  邦納維爾電力局（BPA）是負責銷售哥倫比亞河水壩系統電力的聯邦機構，在此計畫中居於核心地位。BPA依法須依委員會策略採購資源。草案預期BPA可能需要採購新的發電容量，而不僅止於能源效率措施。委員會草案敦促BPA在能源需求方面優先考量再生能源，並在評估容量選項時，將電池儲能與新建天然氣電廠進行比較衡量。

  ## 輸電基礎設施假設

  計畫的資源預測建立在未來輸電基礎設施的假設之上。業界主導的輸電規劃組織「西部輸電擴張聯盟」（WestTEC）所進行的研究，為計畫對電網可容納新增發電量的假設提供依據。萊特指出，此一策略假設部分輸電線路確實得以興建，並表示委員會將透過期中評估持續追蹤輸電進展。

  ## 後續計畫

  NWPCC將於2026年9月至10月間，分別在奧勒岡、華盛頓、愛達荷及蒙大拿四州舉辦公聽會。委員會的目標是在2026年底或2027年初完成第九期電力計畫的最終核定。依法規定，委員會至少須每五年審視一次計畫；上一版電力計畫於2022年2月通過。
