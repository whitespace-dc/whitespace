body_en: |
  PJM Interconnection, the regional grid operator serving 13 U.S. states and the District of Columbia, is forecasting a peak demand of 147.5 gigawatts (GW) for winter 2026–27 under expected conditions, which would set a winter record, while a Keysight-commissioned survey found that only 45% of U.S. utility and network operators rate their regions as well prepared — a gap that coincides with a wave of federal reliability deadlines arriving in 2027.

  A gigawatt is roughly the output of a large nuclear or coal power plant. PJM's 147.5 GW projection comes after the grid operator kept power flowing when demand topped 130 GW for eight consecutive days during a prolonged cold spell in early 2026. In a more severe scenario, PJM found it could serve demand as high as 152.2 GW by calling on large customers to voluntarily reduce electricity use, a practice known as demand response.

  The North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC), the nonprofit body that sets and enforces grid reliability standards in the U.S. and Canada, projects that combined peak demand across all of its assessment areas will reach approximately 836 GW this winter, an increase of 21 GW, or 2.6%, compared with the prior year. NERC expects increases in 21 of its 23 assessment areas and says adequate resources exist for normal winter peaks across all areas, though widespread extreme cold could still cause supply shortfalls.

  ## Data centers and electrification driving load growth

  Data centers, large industrial customers, and broader electrification are all contributing to the demand increase, according to NERC. The International Energy Agency (IEA) projects that global data center electricity consumption will nearly double, from 485 terawatt-hours (TWh) in 2025 to 950 TWh in 2030 — a terawatt-hour is a billion kilowatt-hours, roughly the annual electricity use of 90,000 U.S. homes. The IEA projects that consumption at artificial-intelligence-focused facilities will triple over the same period. The IEA's November 2025 World Energy Outlook points to a similar trajectory under current policies.

  PJM lowered several of its near-term peak demand projections in its 2026 long-term forecast after applying closer scrutiny to large-load interconnection requests and updating its economic and electric vehicle assumptions. The operator still expects substantial long-term load growth. The timing of individual large-load connections, such as data center campuses, creates planning complications: a campus that connects earlier than expected can strain local substation capacity ahead of schedule, while a project that is delayed or canceled may leave a utility holding grid upgrades sized for a load that has not yet materialized.

  ## The readiness gap

  Frost & Sullivan, a market research firm, surveyed 120 U.S. grid professionals on behalf of test-and-measurement company Keysight Technologies. Eighty percent reported making progress integrating renewable energy sources and distributed energy resources — smaller, decentralized sources such as rooftop solar, batteries, and backup generators — over the previous five years. Yet only 45% of utility and network operator respondents rated the regions they serve as well prepared. Among respondents involved in integration testing, only 49% rated their ability to validate compliance and interoperability as above average or excellent.

  Farah Saeed, industry director of energy and environment growth advisory at Frost & Sullivan, called for "a more coordinated way to validate complex, connected, software systems before they reach the field." Thomas Goetzl, vice president and general manager of Keysight's Automotive and Energy Solutions business, said that as power equipment, communications systems, and cybersecurity controls become more interconnected, teams need to verify that they work together reliably under real operating conditions.

  ## Regulatory milestones arriving in 2027

  Several compliance deadlines give the readiness question near-term urgency.

  On January 1, 2027, a phased compliance deadline takes effect under NERC standard PRC-030-1, which covers inverter-based resources — equipment that converts the direct current output of solar panels and batteries into the alternating current used on the grid — located outside the Bulk Electric System. Covered facilities must identify, analyze, and address qualifying unexpected changes in output.

  On April 1, 2027, NERC standard BAL-007-1.1 takes effect, requiring grid operators to assess, report on, and prepare for forecast energy emergencies.

  On December 11, 2027, the main product-security requirements of the European Union's Cyber Resilience Act begin to apply to covered products with digital elements sold on the EU market. Reporting obligations for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents began in September 2026.

  Looking further out, NERC's internal network security monitoring standard, CIP-015-1, takes effect on October 1, 2028, in jurisdictions under the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC). A subsequent version, CIP-015-2, follows on October 1, 2029, with additional phased compliance milestones in 2030 and 2031.

  ## What 2027 demands of grid operators

  The combination of record-level demand forecasts and persistent validation gaps means that the question of whether operating assumptions have been tested is now a compliance question as well as an operational one. The source article highlights a scenario that connects the two: a large industrial customer that has agreed to curtail electricity use during a cold-spell emergency may be unable to respond if the communications link to the utility fails at the moment the curtailment request is sent. Under the new NERC standards, operators will need documented evidence that such arrangements work.

  Keysight is convening a Grid Modernization Summit on January 28, 2027, in Mountain View, California, to address which large-load projects are sufficiently committed to include in winter forecasts, which operating assumptions should be tested before the next cold spell, and what test evidence manufacturers, system integrators, and grid operators can share before equipment is energized.

  PJM's forecast that it may need demand-response commitments from customers to handle a severe winter scenario — a scenario it places at 152.2 GW — means that the reliability of those commitments is not a hypothetical concern but a live variable in its operating plan for the season beginning this month.
body_zh: |
  PJM Interconnection管轄美國13個州及哥倫比亞特區，是該區域的電網調度業者，預測在正常情況下，2026至27年冬季尖峰需求將達147.5 GW（十億瓦），有望創下冬季歷史紀錄。然而，受是德科技（Keysight Technologies）委託進行的產業調查卻顯示，僅有45%的美國電力事業及電網業者認為其所服務的區域準備充分——這一整備缺口，恰好與2027年即將到來的一波聯邦可靠性法規截止期限不謀而合。

  1 GW相當於一座大型核電廠或燃煤電廠的發電量。PJM的147.5 GW預測，是在2026年初一次持續低溫天氣中成功維持供電後所作出的——彼時需求連續八天超過130 GW。在更嚴峻的情境下，PJM估計可透過需量反應（demand response）機制——亦即邀請大型用戶自願削減用電——將供電能力提升至最高152.2 GW。

  北美電力可靠度公司（NERC）是在美加兩國設定並執行電網可靠性標準的非營利機構。該機構預測，今年冬季其全部評估區域的合計尖峰需求約為836 GW，較前一年增加21 GW，年增幅達2.6%。NERC預計，23個評估區域中有21個需求將提升，並表示在正常冬季尖峰情況下，各區域資源均屬充足；但若發生大規模極端寒冷天氣，仍可能出現供電短缺。

  ## 資料中心與電氣化帶動用電成長

  根據NERC的分析，資料中心、大型工業用戶以及整體電氣化趨勢均是帶動用電需求增加的推手。國際能源總署（IEA）預測，全球資料中心用電量將從2025年的485太瓦時（TWh）近乎翻倍成長，至2030年達950 TWh——1太瓦時等於10億度電，約相當於美國9萬戶家庭的全年用電量。IEA預計，同期以人工智慧為主的設施用電量將成長至三倍。IEA在2025年11月發布的《世界能源展望》亦指出，在現行政策軌跡下，未來走勢相近。

  PJM在其2026年長期預測中，已對幾項近期尖峰需求預測值有所下修，原因是針對大型用電接網申請進行更嚴格審查，並更新了經濟及電動車相關假設。不過，PJM仍預期長期用電需求將有顯著成長。大型用電設施的接網時間點——例如資料中心園區——對規劃帶來複雜挑戰：若一座園區比預期提早接網，可能在計畫外的時間點提前壓垮當地變電站的容量；而若計畫延遲或取消，電力公司則可能留下一批已按未實現用電規模升級的電網設施。

  ## 整備缺口

  市調公司Frost & Sullivan受是德科技（Keysight Technologies）委託，對120位美國電網專業人士進行調查。八成受訪者表示，過去五年間在整合再生能源及分散式能源資源方面已有所進展——所謂分散式能源資源，係指規模較小、去中心化的供電來源，例如屋頂太陽能、蓄電池及備用發電機等。然而，僅有45%的電力事業及網路業者受訪者認為其所服務的區域整備程度良好。在參與整合測試的受訪者中，僅49%認為自身驗證合規性與互通性的能力達到中上水準或優秀。

  Frost & Sullivan能源與環境成長顧問業務部產業總監法拉·薩伊德（Farah Saeed）呼籲，應建立「更具協調性的方式，在複雜、聯網的軟體系統正式投入現場前加以驗證」。是德科技汽車與能源解決方案業務部副總裁兼總經理湯瑪斯·格茲爾（Thomas Goetzl）表示，隨著電力設備、通訊系統與網路安全控制系統日趨相互整合，各團隊必須在真實運作條件下驗證，確保這些系統能夠可靠地協同運作。

  ## 2027年的法規里程碑

  多項合規截止期限使整備問題具備了近在眼前的迫切性。

  2027年1月1日，NERC標準PRC-030-1的分階段合規截止期限正式生效。該標準涵蓋「變流器型資源」——亦即將太陽能板與蓄電池的直流電輸出，轉換為電網所使用交流電的設備——且適用範圍延伸至大型電力系統（Bulk Electric System）以外的設施。受規範設施必須識別、分析並處理符合條件的輸出突發變動。

  2027年4月1日，NERC標準BAL-007-1.1正式生效，要求電網業者評估、報告並因應預測中的能源緊急狀況。

  2027年12月11日，歐盟《網路韌性法》（Cyber Resilience Act）的主要產品安全規定，將開始適用於在歐盟市場銷售之具備數位元件的相關產品。針對被積極利用漏洞及重大事件的通報義務，則已於2026年9月起生效。

  放眼更長遠，NERC的內部網路安全監控標準CIP-015-1，將於2028年10月1日在聯邦能源管理委員會（FERC）管轄範圍內正式生效；後續版本CIP-015-2則於2029年10月1日跟進，並分別在2030年及2031年設有額外的分階段合規里程碑。

  ## 2027年對電網業者的要求

  記錄性需求預測與持續存在的驗證缺口相互疊加，意味著「運作假設是否已經過測試」這一問題，如今不只是作業層面的問題，更已成為合規層面的課題。業界對此特別舉出一個將兩者串聯的具體情境：一位已同意在寒流緊急事件期間削減用電的大型工業用戶，若在電力公司發出削減請求的那一刻，通訊連線恰好中斷，將完全無法回應。在新NERC標準下，業者必須提供有憑有據的書面證明，確認此類需量反應機制確實能夠正常運作。

  是德科技預計於2027年1月28日在加州山景市召開「電網現代化高峰會」（Grid Modernization Summit），議題涵蓋：哪些大型用電計畫的承諾程度足以納入冬季預測；哪些運作假設應在下一波寒流來襲前完成測試；以及製造商、系統整合商與電網業者在設備通電之前，可共享哪些測試證據。

  PJM預測，在嚴峻冬季情境下——PJM將此情境設定為152.2 GW——可能需要動用用戶的需量反應承諾。這意味著這些承諾的可靠性並非假設性的顧慮，而是本季運作計畫（自本月起算）中一個不折不扣的活性變數。
