  ## US data centres on track to consume one-fifth of national electricity by 2035

  Data centres in the United States will account for approximately 20% of the country's total electricity consumption by 2035, up from 5.9% today, according to a report published Tuesday by BloombergNEF (BNEF), the research arm of Bloomberg. The primary driver is accelerating demand from artificial intelligence infrastructure.

  BNEF projects US data centre power demand will reach 194 gigawatts (GW) by 2035. One gigawatt is roughly equivalent to the output of a single traditional nuclear reactor. The 194 GW figure represents an 83% increase from the firm's own December forecast, a revision BNEF attributes to a growing pipeline of AI facilities expected to be built over the next decade.

  By 2030, data centres are expected to represent approximately 12% of US electricity use, before climbing to the 20% level by 2035, according to the report.

  ## Concentration in Virginia and Texas

  The national average will mask sharper impacts in states where data centres cluster. BNEF singles out Virginia and Texas as markets where data centres' share of electricity consumption will exceed the national figure. Facilities in areas served by PJM Interconnection LLC, the grid operator covering 13 states that includes the Northern Virginia corridor known as Data Center Alley, and those within the territory of the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) are expected to account for an above-average share of annual electricity use by 2035.

  ## Grid capacity falling short

  The pace of new data centre connections is already testing grid infrastructure. The record for new data centre capacity connected to the US grid in a single year stands at 7.1 GW, according to BNEF. Even if that record pace is sustained every year through 2035, the analysts project a shortfall of 19 GW under their base-case scenario. That projection assumes demand beyond what connects to the grid is served through on-site gas generation drawing from the natural gas pipeline network.

  "We're already seeing the grid struggling to keep up and that's driving a change in how assets connect to the grid," said Lloyd Arnold, a BNEF analyst and one of the report's authors.

  ## AI demand reversing two decades of flat load growth

  US electric grids have experienced roughly two decades of stagnant electricity demand growth. The surge in AI data centre construction has reversed that trend sharply, forcing grid operators and utilities to accelerate capacity additions while data centre operators seek alternative power sources for their campuses.

  Developers are also encountering non-grid obstacles. BNEF notes that local governments have imposed permitting freezes and that projects face political opposition tied to their growing energy footprints and environmental effects.

  ## What the scale means

  "Every coal plant, every gas plant, every solar farm in the US — one unit of energy out of five generated by them is going to data centres," Arnold said. "So that's the same energy that's going to be going into powering electric vehicles, powering cities, et cetera."

  The projected 19 GW grid-connection shortfall by 2035, even under a sustained record buildout, signals that on-site generation will remain a structural feature of large data centre campuses rather than a temporary measure during grid interconnection queues.

  ## 美國資料中心用電量預計2035年占全國五分之一

  根據彭博旗下研究機構彭博新能源財經（BloombergNEF，下稱BNEF）週二發布的報告，美國資料中心到2035年將消耗全國約20%的總電力，較目前5.9%的占比大幅提升，背後的主要推力正是人工智慧基礎建設的加速擴張。

  BNEF預測，美國資料中心的電力需求到2035年將達到194吉瓦（GW）。一吉瓦的發電量大致相當於一座傳統核電機組的輸出功率。194吉瓦這個數字，比BNEF去年12月的預測足足高出83%——BNEF將此次大幅上修歸因於未來十年內預計將陸續興建的AI設施規模持續擴大。

  報告指出，到2030年，資料中心的用電量預計將占美國總電力消耗的約12%，此後繼續攀升，在2035年前後達到20%的水準。

  ## 維吉尼亞州與德克薩斯州高度集中

  全國平均值的背後，掩蓋的是部分州份更為顯著的衝擊。BNEF特別點名維吉尼亞州與德克薩斯州，指出這兩個市場的資料中心用電占比將超越全國平均水準。隸屬於PJM電力調度公司（PJM Interconnection LLC）管轄範圍的設施——該公司負責統籌涵蓋13州的電網，其中包括素有「資料中心大道」之稱的北維吉尼亞走廊——以及位於德克薩斯州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）管轄範圍內的設施，預計到2035年均將占據高於全國均值的年度用電份額。

  ## 電網容量供不應求

  資料中心新增併網的速度已在考驗電網基礎建設的承載極限。據BNEF統計，美國單一年度資料中心新增併網容量的歷史紀錄為7.1吉瓦。即便以這個破紀錄的速度持續擴建至2035年，分析師仍預測在基本情境下將出現19吉瓦的缺口。這項預測假設超出電網供應能力的電力需求，將由廠區內的天然氣自發電機組從天然氣管網取氣補足。

  BNEF分析師、報告共同執筆人洛伊德·阿諾德（Lloyd Arnold）表示：「我們已經看到電網疲於應對的跡象，這正在促使資產接入電網的方式發生改變。」

  ## AI需求逆轉二十年電力需求停滯局面

  美國各大電網在過去約二十年間，電力需求幾乎陷入停滯成長的困境。AI資料中心建設的浪潮急速扭轉了這一趨勢，迫使電網營運商與電力公司加快擴充容量，而資料中心業者也同步尋求為園區引入替代電源。

  開發商還面臨電網以外的重重障礙。BNEF指出，地方政府已對部分案件祭出許可凍結措施，加上資料中心日益龐大的能源足跡與環境影響引發輿論反彈，相關開發案正面臨政治阻力。

  ## 規模意味著什麼

  阿諾德表示：「美國所有的燃煤電廠、燃氣電廠、太陽能電場——這些機組每發出五度電，就有一度電流向資料中心。這些電力本來可以用來替電動車充電、供城市運轉，諸如此類。」

  即便在維持破紀錄擴建步調的前提下，2035年仍將出現19吉瓦的電網併網缺口，這預示著廠區自建發電設施將成為大型資料中心園區的長期結構性配置，而非等待電網排隊接入期間的臨時應急之舉。
