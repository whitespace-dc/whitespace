body_en: |
  PJM Interconnection stakeholders on Tuesday approved a two-part reliability backstop procurement plan aimed at closing an expected power supply gap across the grid operator's 13-state footprint and meeting rising electricity demand driven by data center growth.

  Under the plan, utilities and other load-serving entities — companies responsible for supplying electricity to end customers — and potentially data centers themselves would request that PJM purchase a specified amount of generating capacity through a one-time auction held outside PJM's regular capacity market process. Load-serving entities would then bill large electricity consumers, including data centers, for the cost of that procurement. The average cost across the entire procurement is capped at $555 per megawatt-day.

  The PJM board will use the stakeholder-approved plan as it drafts a final backstop procurement proposal, which the grid operator aims to submit to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC), the U.S. agency overseeing wholesale electricity markets, this month.

  ## Who developed the plan

  The proposal was developed jointly by the Data Center Coalition (DCC) and a group of utilities including Buckeye Power, Dominion Energy, Duke Energy Ohio, Duke Energy Kentucky, Duquesne Light Co., East Kentucky Power Cooperative, Exelon, Old Dominion Electric Cooperative, PPL Electric Utilities, and Southern Maryland Electric Cooperative.

  The plan is built around what the coalition described as a "registry-based subscription model" intended to address concerns about over-procurement, how costs are allocated, and whether costs might shift unfairly among customer groups. It also includes a bilateral contracting phase — direct power purchase agreements between buyers and sellers — which PJM staff had proposed as a complement to the central auction. "A strong bilateral contracting phase will improve the likelihood of success of the central procurement proposed by the Coalition," the DCC and utilities said.

  ## Why PJM is acting

  PJM, which coordinates electricity transmission across 13 Mid-Atlantic and Midwestern states and the District of Columbia, has warned of potential electricity supply shortfalls later this decade. The grid operator attributes the risk to a combination of retiring power plants, insufficient new generation coming online, and accelerating load growth driven by data center development.

  Earlier this year, PJM created "critical issue fast path" stakeholder groups — an expedited process for urgent reliability matters — to evaluate both the one-time backstop procurement and a separate framework for managing how data centers connect to the grid.

  ## Cost allocation and clean energy provisions

  The Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC), an environmental advocacy organization, said it supported the approved plan. "The approved proposal for the Reliability Backstop Procurement ensures that the public will not be stuck with the bill to build new power plants for data centers," said Tom Rutigliano, senior advocate for climate and energy at NRDC.

  Rutigliano also noted the plan directs PJM to prioritize new supply located near data centers rather than building transmission lines to move power from more distant fossil-fueled generation. "This is good news for clean energy, and avoids an expensive boondoggle that benefits fossil fuel companies at public expense," he said.

  ## Connect-and-manage framework stalls

  A separate "connect and manage" framework for data centers — which would govern how large new loads integrate with the grid — did not reach consensus among stakeholders and was not approved. However, most stakeholders appeared to support a revised proposal from PJM staff, according to ClearView Energy Partners, a research firm.

  The revised PJM staff proposal shifted away from a direct curtailment process for data centers. Instead, it proposed creating a large load registry that would give state regulators the data they need to set their own load-reduction priorities for retail customers during grid stress events. "Ultimately, PJM will partner with these state-led programs by using the registry as an informational and operational tool in managing the grid during stressed system conditions," PJM staff said. PJM plans to file a connect-and-manage proposal at FERC alongside the backstop procurement filing.

  ## Next steps

  PJM plans to hold the backstop procurement auction in September. The grid operator said it expects to finalize capacity commitments from that procurement before it holds a capacity auction for the 2029/30 delivery year on December 9. The PJM board also plans to issue a letter to its members detailing its final plans before submitting the proposals to FERC.

  ## PJM利害關係人批准資料中心備援容量採購計畫

  PJM Interconnection的利害關係人週二批准了一項兩階段電力可靠度備援採購計畫，目標是填補這家橫跨13州電網業者預期出現的電力供應缺口，並滿足因資料中心蓬勃發展而持續攀升的用電需求。

  根據計畫內容，電力公司及其他供電實體——即負責向終端用戶供電的企業——乃至資料中心本身，均可向PJM申請透過一次性拍賣方式採購特定數量的發電容量，且此拍賣程序獨立於PJM常規容量市場之外。供電實體事後將把採購成本轉嫁給包括資料中心在內的大型用電戶。整體採購的平均成本上限設定為每百萬瓦日555美元。

  PJM董事會將以利害關係人批准的計畫為基礎，擬定最終備援採購提案，並計畫本月向美國負責監管躉售電力市場的主管機關——聯邦能源管理委員會（FERC）——提出申請。

  ## 計畫起草單位

  此提案由資料中心聯盟（Data Center Coalition，DCC）與多家電力公司聯合研擬，參與業者包括：Buckeye Power、Dominion Energy、Duke Energy Ohio、Duke Energy Kentucky、Duquesne Light Co.、East Kentucky Power Cooperative、Exelon、Old Dominion Electric Cooperative、PPL Electric Utilities，以及Southern Maryland Electric Cooperative。

  計畫的核心設計是聯盟所稱的「登記制訂閱模式」，旨在解決各界對過度採購、成本分攤方式，以及成本是否可能不公平轉嫁至特定用戶群體等疑慮。此外，計畫也納入了雙邊合約階段——即買賣雙方直接簽訂的購電協議——此為PJM工作人員所提出、作為集中拍賣補充機制的方案。DCC與相關電力公司表示：「強健的雙邊合約階段，將提升聯盟所提集中採購計畫的成功可能性。」

  ## PJM採取行動的背景

  PJM負責協調美國13個中大西洋及中西部州以及哥倫比亞特區的電力輸送，已警告本十年稍後可能出現電力供應短缺。該電網業者將此風險歸因於多重因素：老舊電廠相繼除役、新增發電量不足，以及資料中心建設帶動的用電需求加速增長。

  今年稍早，PJM設立了「關鍵議題快速通道」利害關係人工作小組——一種專門處理緊急電網可靠度事務的加速程序——以評估此次一次性備援採購計畫，以及另一套管理資料中心電網接入方式的獨立框架。

  ## 成本分攤與潔淨能源條款

  環保倡議組織自然資源保護委員會（Natural Resources Defense Council，NRDC）表示支持獲批計畫。NRDC氣候與能源高級倡導員Tom Rutigliano指出：「獲批的可靠度備援採購提案確保大眾不必為資料中心新建電廠買單。」

  Rutigliano進一步說明，計畫要求PJM優先考量在資料中心附近新增電力供應，而非興建輸電線路將電力從更遠處的化石燃料發電廠輸送過來。他表示：「這對潔淨能源而言是好消息，也避免了一場讓化石燃料公司以公費獲益的昂貴浪費。」

  ## 電網接入管理框架擱置

  另一套針對資料中心的「接入與管理」框架——旨在規範大型新用電戶如何整合進電網——未能在利害關係人之間達成共識，因此未獲批准。然而，研究機構ClearView Energy Partners指出，多數利害關係人似乎支持PJM工作人員提出的修訂提案。

  PJM工作人員的修訂提案改變了原先對資料中心直接限電的做法，轉而提議建立大型用電登記冊，讓州監管機關取得所需資料，以便在電網壓力事件發生時，自行訂定零售用戶的減載優先順序。PJM工作人員表示：「最終，PJM將與各州主導的計畫合作，在電網壓力狀況下，以登記冊作為電網管理的資訊與操作工具。」PJM計畫在提交備援採購申請的同時，一併向FERC申報接入與管理提案。

  ## 後續步驟

  PJM計畫於九月舉行備援採購拍賣，並表示預計在十二月九日舉行2029至2030年度輸電容量拍賣之前，完成該次採購的容量承諾確認作業。PJM董事會也計畫在向FERC提交提案前，向成員發出說明信函，詳述最終計畫內容。
