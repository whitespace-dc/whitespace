body_en: |
  Peninsular Malaysia's data centers have seen a rise in power sag incidents — brief voltage drops triggered by faults at power lines and generation plants — as the grid's effective reserve margin has narrowed to around 13%, well below what installed-capacity figures imply, while data center electricity consumption is projected to climb from 7% of the peninsula's load today to 31% by 2035, The Edge Malaysia reported.

  ## Reserve margin narrower than headline figures

  Peninsular Malaysia recorded a peak grid demand of 21,583 megawatts (MW) on June 8. Against grid-connected installed generation capacity of 28,192MW in 2025, the arithmetic yields a reserve margin of 23.4% — a margin that would typically be considered adequate by power-sector benchmarks. However, available generation currently stands at just under 25,000MW, cutting the effective margin to roughly 13%. Part of the gap is explained by planned maintenance outages, including at the Edra Melaka Power Plant, which at 2,242MW is the country's largest thermal facility.

  Grid System Operator (GSO) data shows Peninsular Malaysia imported 300MW of electricity from Thailand, mainly between September and November 2025 and throughout June 2026, supplementing domestic supply during periods of tighter generation availability.

  ## Data center load accelerating

  Approved data center capacity in the country exceeds what is currently in service: actual DC demand represents just 54% of total approved load, with the remaining 948MW expected to come online largely by 2028. That increment alone is equivalent to roughly 3% of total grid-connected installed generation capacity, excluding other approved but not yet commissioned projects.

  The pipeline extends further. Tenaga Nasional Bhd (TNB), Malaysia's national utility, has said 1,800MW of additional DC capacity is under construction and electricity supply agreements cover a further 2,000MW. Additional projects remain under evaluation by the Data Centre Task Force.

  The rate of demand growth has been climbing. Actual DC maximum power demand rose by more than 100MW each quarter through the second half of 2025, then jumped by a record 200MW in the first quarter of 2026 alone, according to The Edge's reporting.

  ## Government restricts non-AI projects, orders new gas plants

  Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim has said Malaysia is limiting new DC approvals to projects linked to artificial intelligence (AI), in order to control water and electricity consumption. As a medium-term supply measure, the government has conducted two tenders for new gas-fired power plants, in 2025 and 2026.

  A global gas turbine supply bottleneck, however, means new plants are likely to take longer than the standard three-year commissioning timeline. A plant awarded today may not enter service until 2029 at the earliest — one year after the next significant wave of DC demand is expected to arrive in 2028. In the near term, the government has allowed several existing gas plants to operate beyond their original commissioning end dates to preserve available capacity.

  ## Coal retirement in 2029 compounds the timing problem

  Energy Minister Datuk Seri Fadillah Yusof stated in 2024 that Malaysia would not build new coal-fired power plants, consistent with the country's net-zero emissions target. Coal currently provides the grid's base load — the continuous, round-the-clock generation that underpins supply regardless of time of day. Coal plants totaling 1,474MW are scheduled for decommissioning in 2029, the same year that gas turbine delivery constraints make it unlikely that newly awarded replacement capacity will be ready.

  ## Renewables provide partial, intermittent cover

  Renewable energy — primarily solar and hydro — accounts for approximately 12% of peak demand. On July 9, large-scale solar (LSS) supplied around 1,500MW during daylight hours, while hydro generation exceeded 1,100MW overnight and provided between 300MW and 600MW at other times of day. As of July 2025, LSS totaled 2,237MW of installed capacity against 2,526MW of installed hydro generation capacity, according to Energy Commission and GSO data.

  An additional 2,400MW of solar capacity sits at the distribution network level and is not counted in grid demand figures. Oil- and diesel-fired plants continue to supply between 360MW and 450MW.

  ## Power quality incidents at data centers

  As DC load has grown and generation headroom has tightened, data centers in Malaysia have experienced more power sag incidents, The Edge reported, citing channel checks. Power sags are temporary voltage drops that can last as little as tens of milliseconds, but top-tier data centers — built for extremely high reliability — typically flag anomalies lasting more than 20 milliseconds. In some incidents, backup power systems activated automatically. In others, operators weighed whether to transfer loads from the grid to backup diesel generators to eliminate the risk of server shutdowns.

  ## Outlook

  Malaysia's 2029 window concentrates several pressures simultaneously: DC demand from approved but not yet operational projects is expected to materialize largely by 2028; 1,474MW of coal base-load capacity comes offline in 2029; and gas turbine supply constraints mean replacement generation awarded today is unlikely to be commissioned before that same year. The government's two gas tender rounds represent the primary medium-term supply response, though whether those plants can be built and connected to the grid fast enough to offset the coal retirements while absorbing continued DC load growth remains the central supply-adequacy question facing Malaysia's grid planners, according to The Edge's analysis.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞半島的資料中心近來頻繁出現電力驟降事件——這是由輸電線路或發電機組故障所引發的短暫電壓下降現象——背後原因在於電網實際備用容量已收窄至約13%，遠低於裝置容量數字所呈現的水準。與此同時，半島資料中心用電量預計將從目前占整體負載的7%，攀升至2035年的31%。以上訊息出自《The Edge Malaysia》的報導。

  ## 備用容量比帳面數字更為緊縮

  馬來西亞半島於6月8日錄得21,583兆瓦（MW）的電網尖峰需求。對比2025年已接入電網的裝置發電容量28,192MW，計算所得備用容量比率為23.4%——以電力產業標準而言，這一數字通常被視為充裕。然而，目前實際可用發電量僅略低於25,000MW，使有效備用容量降至約13%。差距的部分原因來自計畫性維護停機，其中包括裝置容量達2,242MW、為全國規模最大火力發電廠的埃德拉馬六甲電廠（Edra Melaka Power Plant）。

  電網系統運營商（GSO）的數據顯示，馬來西亞半島在2025年9月至11月間及2026年整個6月主要向泰國進口300MW電力，以補充國內供電在發電容量較為吃緊期間的不足。

  ## 資料中心負載加速成長

  全國已核准的資料中心容量超過現有在運行規模：實際資料中心需求僅占總核准負載的54%，其餘948MW預計大致將於2028年前陸續上線。光是這部分增量，就相當於已接入電網裝置發電容量（不含其他已核准但尚未投入運營的項目）的約3%。

  待建案件量更為可觀。馬來西亞國家電力公司（Tenaga Nasional Bhd，簡稱TNB）表示，另有1,800MW的資料中心容量正在建設中，供電協議亦已涵蓋額外的2,000MW。更多項目則仍在資料中心工作小組的評估審查之中。

  需求成長速度持續加快。根據《The Edge》的報導，2025年下半年，實際資料中心最大電力需求每季增幅均超過100MW，進入2026年首季更創下單季增加200MW的歷史紀錄。

  ## 政府限制非AI項目、下令新建燃氣電廠

  總理拿督斯里安華·易卜拉欣（Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim）表示，馬來西亞正將新資料中心核准範圍限定於與人工智慧（AI）相關的項目，以期有效管控用水及用電需求。作為中期供電應對措施，政府分別於2025年及2026年舉行了兩輪新燃氣電廠招標。

  然而，全球燃氣輪機供應出現瓶頸，意味著新電廠的建設週期很可能超過標準的三年完工時程。今日獲標的電廠，最快也要到2029年才能投入運營——比預計於2028年湧現的下一波大規模資料中心需求整整晚了一年。在近期應對上，政府已允許數座現有燃氣電廠在原定運營到期後繼續延役，以維持可用發電容量。

  ## 2029年燃煤電廠退役加劇時間壓力

  能源部長拿督斯里法迪拉·尤索夫（Datuk Seri Fadillah Yusof）於2024年表示，馬來西亞不會新建燃煤電廠，此舉符合該國的淨零碳排目標。燃煤發電目前承擔電網的基載供電——即不分時段、全天候持續提供電力的電源基礎，是整體供電不可或缺的骨幹。總計1,474MW的燃煤機組預計將於2029年除役，而這一年恰好也是燃氣輪機供應限制導致新核准替代容量不太可能完工投產的時間節點，雙重壓力在此交會。

  ## 再生能源提供部分但不穩定的補充

  再生能源——主要為太陽能與水力發電——約占尖峰需求的12%。7月9日，大型太陽能（LSS）在日間提供約1,500MW的發電量，水力發電在夜間則超過1,100MW，其餘時段約提供300MW至600MW不等。根據能源委員會與GSO的數據，截至2025年7月，大型太陽能裝置容量合計達2,237MW，水力發電裝置容量則為2,526MW。

  另有2,400MW的太陽能容量位於配電網層級，未計入電網需求數字之中。石油及柴油發電廠持續提供360MW至450MW的電力供應。

  ## 資料中心電能品質事故頻傳

  隨著資料中心負載持續成長、電力系統備用空間不斷收窄，馬來西亞各資料中心的電壓驟降事故也愈加頻繁——《The Edge》援引相關業界查核結果如此報導。電壓驟降為短暫性電壓下降現象，持續時間有時僅數十毫秒；但頂級資料中心以極高可靠性為設計核心，通常對持續超過20毫秒的異常狀況便會予以標記記錄。部分事故中，備用電源系統自動啟動；另有部分事故中，營運商則評估是否需要將負載從市電切換至備用柴油發電機，以杜絕伺服器因意外停機所帶來的風險。

  ## 展望

  馬來西亞2029年這個時間窗口集中了多重壓力：已核准但尚未投入運營的資料中心項目所帶來的需求，預計大致將於2028年集中實現；1,474MW的燃煤基載發電容量將於2029年正式退場；而燃氣輪機供應限制意味著今日獲批的替代電源，同樣不太可能在此之前完工並接入電網。政府兩輪燃氣招標是目前最主要的中期供電應對方案，然而這些電廠能否及時完工、連接電網，在持續吸納資料中心不斷增加的用電負載之餘，同步填補燃煤電廠退役後留下的缺口——仍是馬來西亞電網規劃者所面臨的核心供電充裕性課題。《The Edge》的分析如此總結。
