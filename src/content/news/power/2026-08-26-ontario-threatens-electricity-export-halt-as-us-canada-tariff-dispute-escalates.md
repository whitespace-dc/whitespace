body_en: |
  Ontario Premier Doug Ford said Monday that Canada could halt electricity exports to the United States entirely, as the countries' escalating trade dispute moves into the power sector and threatens higher wholesale electricity prices for American consumers.

  Ford told the Associated Press that "everything is on the table," including stopping exports of critical minerals and electricity. Ford and Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney discussed imposing a 25% surcharge on electricity shipped to the United States, the BBC reported. Canada was expected to announce retaliatory tariffs on U.S. goods Tuesday, following the entry into force over the weekend of 50% U.S. tariffs on approximately $20 billion in Canadian goods, with additional U.S. measures threatened.

  ## Grid reliability and market prices

  The primary near-term risk from reduced Canadian power exports is financial rather than physical, according to ISO New England, the regional grid operator for the northeastern United States. If Canada reduced or eliminated electricity trade, the operator said it "would not anticipate reliability issues tied to reduced imports, at least under typical weather conditions."

  Under extreme temperatures, supplies in New England "could become tight, but that would hinge on many factors that are difficult to project," ISO New England said. The operator said the main effect would be "higher wholesale market prices" and increased emissions across the region. ISO New England also noted that electricity flows in both directions across the Canada-New England border.

  ## Scale of electricity trade

  The value of electricity trade between the United States and Canada totaled $3.2 billion in 2025, with 67% of that representing Canadian exports to the United States, according to the U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), the federal government's primary energy statistics agency. The EIA said electricity trade between the countries "is relatively small compared to trade in other energy sources."

  ## New infrastructure ties the two grids more closely

  Cross-border power infrastructure has grown more substantial in recent months. In June, the Champlain-Hudson Power Express, described as North America's longest fully-buried transmission line, began delivering Canadian hydropower to New York City. The $6 billion line is expected to meet up to 20% of the city's electric needs.

  The New York Independent System Operator (NYISO), which manages New York's bulk power grid, said it is in "close and regular contact" with Hydro-Quebec and Ontario's Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO). Kevin Lanahan, NYISO's senior vice president of external affairs and corporate communications, said the operator "anticipates having adequate supplies to meet expected demand on the system."

  ## A dispute the sector has seen before

  The trade conflict has reached the electricity sector before. In 2025, Ontario enacted a 25% tariff on power exports to the United States during a previous round of tensions. That tariff was eliminated one day after taking effect, as the two sides reached a temporary accommodation.

  Canada's announcement of retaliatory tariffs, expected Tuesday, will determine whether electricity trade again becomes a formal instrument in the broader trade dispute.

  安大略省長道格·福特週一警告，加拿大有可能全面中止對美電力出口。隨著兩國貿易爭端不斷升級，衝突已延燒至電力部門，並恐將推高美國消費者的批發電價。

  福特接受美聯社採訪時表示，「一切選項均在考量之列」，包括停止出口關鍵礦物與電力。據BBC報導，福特已與加拿大總理馬克·卡尼討論對輸往美國的電力課徵25%附加費。繼本週末美國對加拿大約200億美元商品課徵50%關稅正式生效、並揚言祭出更多制裁措施之後，加拿大預計週二宣布對美商品的報復性關稅。

  ## 電網穩定性與市場電價

  負責美國東北部地區電網調度的「新英格蘭獨立系統營運商」（ISO New England）表示，加拿大減少電力出口的短期主要風險屬財務面，而非實體供電問題。該機構指出，若加拿大減少或終止電力貿易，「至少在正常氣候條件下，預期不會出現與進口減少相關的電力供應可靠性問題」。

  不過，ISO New England表示，在極端氣溫下，新英格蘭地區的電力供應「可能趨於吃緊，但這取決於許多難以預測的因素」。該機構認為，主要影響將是「批發市場電價走高」，以及整個地區碳排放量增加。ISO New England並指出，加拿大與新英格蘭邊境的電力流向為雙向。

  ## 電力貿易規模

  根據美國聯邦政府主要能源統計機構「美國能源資訊署」（EIA）的數據，2025年美加兩國電力貿易總額達32億美元，其中67%為加拿大出口至美國的電力。EIA表示，兩國之間的電力貿易「相較於其他能源貿易，規模仍屬有限」。

  ## 新基礎設施使兩國電網聯繫更趨緊密

  近幾個月來，跨境電力基礎設施已大幅擴建。今年6月，被譽為北美最長全地下輸電線路的「尚普蘭—哈德遜電力快線」（Champlain-Hudson Power Express）正式投運，開始向紐約市輸送加拿大水力發電。這條耗資60億美元的輸電線路，預計可滿足紐約市最高達20%的用電需求。

  負責管理紐約州整體電力系統的「紐約獨立系統營運商」（NYISO）表示，正與魁北克水電公司（Hydro-Québec）及安大略省獨立電力系統營運商（IESO）「保持密切且定期的聯繫」。NYISO對外事務與企業傳播部門資深副總裁凱文·拉納漢（Kevin Lanahan）表示，該機構「預期將有充足的電力供應，以應對系統的預期需求」。

  ## 電力部門並非首次捲入貿易爭端

  這並非貿易衝突首次波及電力部門。2025年，安大略省在上一輪緊張局勢期間，曾對輸往美國的電力課徵25%關稅，但該關稅在生效僅一天後即遭取消，因雙方達成了臨時協議。

  加拿大預計週二宣布的報復性關稅，將決定電力貿易是否再度成為這場更廣泛貿易爭端中的正式籌碼。
