body_en: |
  Utilities are deploying virtual power plants, which aggregate customer batteries, electric vehicles, and thermostats into a single dispatchable resource, as tools to reduce system costs and improve grid resilience beyond their traditional reliability role, industry experts said Tuesday at a webinar organized by the Regulatory Assistance Project.

  ## VPPs as affordability tools

  Kevin Brehm, who manages the carbon-free electricity practice at the Rocky Mountain Institute, said utilities increasingly treat VPPs as cost levers at two grid levels. On the bulk power system, aggregated distributed resources can reduce customer demand when wholesale electricity prices spike, cutting both capacity and transmission costs. On distribution networks, VPPs can open up headroom for new customer load connections and help utilities reduce, defer, or avoid incremental infrastructure spending, Brehm said.

  ## Customer participation requirements

  Bronte Payne, senior manager for policy at residential solar and battery company Sunrun, said scaling customer enrollment and improving dispatchability — the ability to call on resources predictably and on demand — requires reliable and predictable compensation, revenue-grade metering to document actual performance, and customer protections such as penalty-free opt-out provisions.

  ## Program models and resource trends

  Brehm, whose team helped compile RMI's catalog of active U.S. VPP programs, said programs anchored by residential batteries and actively managed electric vehicle charging are growing particularly fast, while thermostat-based programs have seen slower growth. Three operational models have emerged: utility-run VPPs that compensate consumers directly or use third-party aggregators as intermediaries; market-participant VPPs, in which aggregations bid directly into energy, capacity, or ancillary services markets; and an emerging "bring your own capacity" model that blends elements of both.

  ## Reliability and resilience applications

  Consolidated Edison Co. of New York's demand-response VPP illustrates the reliability use case, managing power flows on constrained distribution circuits, Brehm said. Luma Energy's Customer Battery Energy Sharing program in Puerto Rico illustrates the resilience case, providing backup capacity in areas subject to weather-related outages or load shedding.

  ## National Grid: 19 non-wires projects in Massachusetts

  Nick Watson, director of flexible resource engineering at National Grid, said the utility is using distributed resource aggregations to address 19 non-wires alternatives projects across its Massachusetts service territory. Non-wires alternatives replace or defer traditional grid hardware upgrades — such as new substations or transformers — with demand-side resources. National Grid has 7.2 megawatts of active or committed capacity across two programs.

  ConnectedSolutions+ is a residential and small-business program drawing on stationary batteries, EVs, and other customer-sited devices. It is an outgrowth of New England's long-running ConnectedSolutions VPP, offering higher payments than standard ConnectedSolutions in exchange for allowing National Grid to dispatch resources more frequently and at different times of day, including weekend evenings that may reflect local demand peaks. Eversource, National Grid's main investor-owned utility competitor in Massachusetts, has also enrolled customers in ConnectedSolutions+.

  A separate front-of-meter, market-based framework targets commercial and industrial customers and requires a competitive request-for-proposals process. Watson said National Grid weighs factors including customer type and existing ConnectedSolutions enrollment levels at constrained nodes when deciding which framework to apply; limited enrollment at a given location may indicate that an RFP is needed to attract sufficient capacity.

  ## Xcel Energy addresses solar-driven grid stress in Colorado

  Xcel Energy's Colorado system, which totals approximately 7 gigawatts in capacity, now carries more than 1 gigawatt of solar — much of it on rooftops — and is experiencing voltage swings and reverse power flow as a result, said Zach Pollock, the company's director of grid strategy and emerging technologies. With midday solar generating less incremental system value, Xcel is shifting toward a "direct-participant model" that places greater weight on the capacity value of distributed energy resources, or DERs, the umbrella term for customer-sited assets such as solar panels, batteries, and EV chargers.

  Xcel issued a competitive solicitation this summer for the state-mandated Dispatchable Distributed Generation program, which will enroll megawatt-scale batteries operating on a set schedule to reduce load in distribution-constrained areas. Separately, Xcel's Active VPP program, called AVPP, targets 25 megawatts of behind-the-meter resources per year for five years; third-party aggregators will manage those resources for now, though Xcel said it reserves the right to serve as its own aggregator. Xcel has enrolled "a few megawatts" so far. The company's stated goal for AVPP is to operate the aggregated portfolio with the performance characteristics of a four-hour battery regardless of the mix of underlying resources.

  ## Vehicle-to-grid identified as next growth driver

  Bidirectional electric vehicle charging — in which EVs export stored power back to the grid, a model called vehicle-to-grid or V2G — represents the next significant resource category for VPPs, Brehm said. An analysis commissioned by General Motors last month found V2G integration can deliver up to 15 times more value per vehicle than one-way managed charging. Brehm said some automakers are already pressing for greater V2G participation in utility VPP programs.

  虛擬電廠藉由整合客戶端的電池、電動車與智慧溫控器，形成單一可調度資源，正逐步成為電力公司降低系統成本、提升電網韌性的關鍵工具，而不再僅限於傳統的可靠度應用。業界專家週二在監管協助計畫（Regulatory Assistance Project）主辦的網路研討會上指出，上述趨勢正在加速成形。

  ## 虛擬電廠作為降費工具

  落磯山研究所（Rocky Mountain Institute）碳零電力業務主管凱文．布雷姆（Kevin Brehm）表示，電力公司愈來愈傾向將虛擬電廠視為兩個電網層級的成本調節槓桿。在大型電力系統層面，聚合的分散式資源可在躉售電價飆漲時壓低客戶需求，從而削減容量與輸電成本。在配電網路層面，虛擬電廠則可釋放線路餘裕，讓新增客戶負載得以接入，並協助電力公司減少、遞延乃至完全迴避額外的基礎設施支出，布雷姆說。

  ## 客戶參與的前提條件

  住宅太陽能與電池公司Sunrun的政策資深經理布朗特．佩恩（Bronte Payne）表示，若要擴大客戶加入規模並提升可調度性——亦即能夠按需、可預期地調度資源的能力——必須具備三項要素：可靠且可預期的補償機制、用以記錄實際績效的商業級電表，以及保障客戶權益的措施，例如免罰款的退出條款。

  ## 方案模式與資源發展趨勢

  布雷姆的團隊協助彙整了落磯山研究所的美國現行虛擬電廠方案目錄。他指出，以住宅電池為核心、以及主動管理電動車充電的方案成長最為快速，而以溫控器為主的方案成長則相對遲緩。目前市場上已出現三種運營模式：由電力公司主導、直接補償消費者或透過第三方聚合商居中協調的「公用事業主導型虛擬電廠」；由聚合資源直接參與能源、容量或輔助服務市場的「市場參與型虛擬電廠」；以及融合兩者特色、正在興起的「自備容量」（bring your own capacity）模式。

  ## 可靠度與韌性應用

  布雷姆指出，紐約聯合愛迪生公司（Consolidated Edison Co. of New York）的需量反應虛擬電廠體現了可靠度應用案例，專門管理受壅塞配電線路上的電力潮流。波多黎各Luma Energy的「客戶電池能源共享」（Customer Battery Energy Sharing）方案則展現了韌性應用案例，為容易遭受天氣相關停電或限電的地區提供備用容量。

  ## 國家電網：麻薩諸塞州19項非線路替代計畫

  國家電網（National Grid）彈性資源工程總監尼克．華生（Nick Watson）表示，該公司正運用分散式資源聚合，在麻薩諸塞州服務區域內推動19項「非線路替代方案」（non-wires alternatives）計畫。非線路替代方案以需求側資源取代或遞延傳統電網硬體升級，例如興建新變電站或更換變壓器。國家電網在兩個方案中合計已有7.2 MW的有效或承諾容量。

  「ConnectedSolutions+」是一項面向住宅及小型企業的方案，整合固定式電池、電動車及其他客戶端裝置。它源自新英格蘭長期推行的ConnectedSolutions虛擬電廠，提供比標準ConnectedSolutions更高的補償報酬，換取允許國家電網更頻繁、在一天中不同時段調度資源，包括可能反映地方需量高峰的週末傍晚。麻薩諸塞州另一主要民間電力業者Eversource也已將客戶納入ConnectedSolutions+。

  另一個表前計量、以市場為導向的框架則針對商業及工業客戶，須經過競爭性徵求建議書（RFP）流程。華生表示，國家電網在決定採用哪種框架時，會考量客戶類型，以及特定受壅塞節點的現有ConnectedSolutions加入率；若某地點的加入率偏低，可能意味著需透過徵求建議書吸引足夠容量。

  ## 科羅拉多州：Xcel Energy應對太陽能引發的電網壓力

  Xcel Energy科羅拉多州系統的總裝置容量約為7 GW，目前太陽能裝置已逾1 GW——其中大部分為屋頂型太陽能——導致電壓波動與逆向潮流問題，公司電網策略與新興技術總監查克．波洛克（Zach Pollock）說。隨著正午太陽能發電的邊際系統價值下滑，Xcel正轉向「直接參與模式」，對分散式能源資源（DER）的容量價值賦予更高權重；DER為客戶端資產的統稱，涵蓋太陽能板、電池及電動車充電樁。

  Xcel今夏為州政府強制推動的「可調度分散式發電」（Dispatchable Distributed Generation）方案發出競爭性徵求，該方案將招募百萬瓦（MW）級電池，依照固定時程運作，以舒緩配電壅塞地區的負載壓力。另外，Xcel的主動型虛擬電廠（Active VPP，簡稱AVPP）方案目標是每年招募25 MW的表後資源，為期五年；第三方聚合商目前將負責管理這些資源，但Xcel表示保留自行擔任聚合商的權利。目前Xcel已招募「數個MW」的容量。公司對AVPP的既定目標，是讓聚合後的資源組合在整體表現上，能夠展現四小時電池的性能特徵，無論底層資源的組成為何。

  ## 雙向充電被視為下一波成長動能

  布雷姆表示，雙向電動車充電——電動車將儲存電力回饋至電網，即所謂的「車輛回饋電網」（vehicle-to-grid，V2G）模式——是虛擬電廠下一個重要資源類別。通用汽車（General Motors）上個月委託進行的一項分析顯示，V2G整合每輛車可帶來的價值，最高可達單向受控充電的15倍。布雷姆說，部分汽車製造商已積極推動，要求在電力公司的虛擬電廠方案中擴大V2G參與比重。
