body_en: |
  Malaysia's electricity reserve margin is projected at 24 to 27 percent from 2027 through 2035, Deputy Energy Transition and Water Transformation Minister Datuk Seri Abdul Rahman Mohamad told parliament, as the government adjusts data centre tariffs and capacity charges to prevent infrastructure costs from falling on residential consumers.

  Abdul Rahman made the disclosure while concluding committee-stage debate on the Supply Bill 2027 in the Dewan Rakyat. He attributed the forecast to the Electricity Supply Planning and Implementation and Tariff Committee (JPPPET), which met on November 11. A reserve margin measures the share of spare generation capacity a power system holds above its peak demand; the projected 24-to-27 percent range is intended to maintain grid reliability while supporting data centre development.

  ## Curbing data centre cost pass-through

  The government has adjusted connected load charge (CLC) rates — fees levied on large consumers who reserve grid capacity — to curb what Abdul Rahman described as excessive capacity reservations by data centre operators. The stated purpose is to prevent data centre development costs from being passed on to domestic electricity consumers.

  Demand-side management measures for high-intensity energy users, including data centres, have also been identified as mitigation strategies, Abdul Rahman said.

  An ultra-high voltage (UHV) tariff structure applying to data centre operators that connect to the grid at voltage levels exceeding 50 kilovolts took effect on July 1, 2025.

  ## Parliamentary proceedings

  Yeo Bee Yin (PH-Puchong) proposed during the debate that the JPPPET report be made publicly available; the ministry noted the proposal without indicating a decision.

  The Dewan Rakyat approved RM5.33 billion in operating and development expenditure for the Energy Transition and Water Transformation Ministry (PETRA) under Budget 2027, following debate by 32 members of parliament.

  ## Water demand

  Under guidelines from the National Water Services Commission (SPAN), data centres are required to maintain at least a two-day on-site water reserve, prioritise alternative water sources, and bear the cost of any necessary infrastructure upgrades. Abdul Rahman said ensuring supply to existing customers remains the priority.

  To reduce demand on the treated water supply, 19.5 million litres per day of reclaimed water is currently being sourced from four sewage treatment plants in Selangor and Johor. Data centre water consumption across Selangor, Johor, and Negeri Sembilan is projected to reach 445.04 million litres per day by 2030.

  ## 馬來西亞預估2035年前電力備用容量達24至27%，資料中心負載持續成長

  馬來西亞副能源轉型暨水務轉型部長拿督斯里阿都拉曼莫哈末在國會表示，2027至2035年間馬來西亞的電力備用容量預計將維持在24至27%之間。政府同步調整資料中心電費與容量收費結構，目的是防止相關基礎建設成本轉嫁給一般住宅用電戶。

  阿都拉曼是在下議院（Dewan Rakyat）審議2027年度撥款法案委員會階段辯論總結時作出上述說明。他表示，此一預測數據來自電力供應規劃、執行與關稅委員會（JPPPET），該委員會於11月11日召開會議。電力備用容量係指電力系統在尖峰負載之上所保有的額外發電能力比例；當局設定24至27%的目標區間，旨在確保電網穩定可靠，同時支持資料中心產業的持續發展。

  ## 抑制資料中心成本轉嫁

  政府已調整「接線容量收費」（Connected Load Charge，CLC）費率——此費用係向預約電網容量的大型用電戶收取——以遏制資料中心業者過度預留電網容量的情形。阿都拉曼表示，此舉的目的是防止資料中心發展成本轉嫁至一般家庭用電戶。

  此外，他也提到針對高耗能用戶（包括資料中心）所擬定的需求面管理措施，作為緩解電力壓力的配套策略之一。

  適用於以超過50千伏特電壓接入電網之資料中心業者的「超高壓」（UHV）電費結構，已於2025年7月1日正式生效。

  ## 國會審議進程

  辯論期間，葉冰盈議員（希望聯盟，蒲種選區）提議公開JPPPET報告；部方對此項建議表示知悉，但未表明最終決定。

  下議院通過2027年度預算案中能源轉型暨水務轉型部（PETRA）共53億3,000萬馬幣的營運及發展經費，本次辯論共有32位國會議員參與。

  ## 用水需求

  依據國家水務服務委員會（SPAN）的相關規範，資料中心須在廠區內儲備至少兩天的用水量，優先採用替代水源，並自行承擔所需基礎建設升級費用。阿都拉曼表示，確保既有用水戶的供水需求仍是優先要務。

  為降低對自來水的依賴，當局目前透過雪蘭莪州及柔佛州四座污水處理廠，每日回收再利用1,950萬公升的再生水。預計至2030年，雪蘭莪、柔佛及森美蘭等州的資料中心每日用水量將達4億4,504萬公升。
