body_en: |
  ## Outlook shift

  Fitch Ratings downgraded its outlook for the utility and power sector to deteriorating on June 12, 2026, saying that affordability concerns are accelerating political and regulatory risks that threaten utilities' ability to recover costs from ratepayers. As recently as December 2025, Fitch had rated the sector's outlook as neutral while flagging potential resistance to rate increases; the agency now says that risk is materializing "faster and more broadly" than it anticipated.

  The change carries credit implications for an industry that Fitch says plans record capital expenditures of approximately $240 billion in 2026, with annual spending expected to rise at a low- to mid-teens percentage rate through 2030. While Fitch said those investments support credit quality over the longer term, it warned that rising bills may make timely rate recovery more difficult.

  ## The affordability pressure

  The average residential electricity price in the United States rose 10.2% year over year to 18.8 cents per kilowatt-hour in March 2026, up from 17.1 cents a year earlier, Fitch said, citing U.S. Energy Information Administration data. A kilowatt-hour is the unit used to measure household electricity consumption; an increase of that magnitude translates directly into higher monthly bills for residential customers.

  That price pressure is already influencing legislative action. Lawmakers in Indiana, Maine, and Maryland passed bills this year aimed at reducing electric bills, according to Fitch. In Pennsylvania, PECO Energy, a subsidiary of Exelon, withdrew a combined $510 million electric and gas rate-hike request from the Pennsylvania Public Utilities Commission in mid-April, citing affordability concerns.

  ## Political risk ahead of elections

  Fitch identified the November 2026 election cycle as a near-term amplifier of the risk. With 36 states holding gubernatorial elections, the agency said utility bills are "emerging as a front-and-center campaign issue." Electricity costs were already a major factor in the New Jersey and Virginia governor races in 2025, both states served by the PJM Interconnection — a regional transmission organization covering all or parts of 13 states and the District of Columbia — where capacity prices have soared as forecast data center load has increased and power supplies have remained flat.

  ## Data center demand: a partial offset

  Fitch acknowledged that the surge in data center demand presents a potential counterweight to bill pressure. Large new loads allow utilities to spread fixed infrastructure costs across a broader customer base, and utilities have been developing specialized tariffs for data centers specifically to prevent cost shifts to existing retail customers. However, Fitch said those benefits "are likely to emerge only over time and may not fully offset near-term bill pressure from elevated capital spending."

  ## What it means for the sector

  The outlook change signals that investors and analysts should expect more frequent and more contentious rate cases, potential legislative intervention in rate-setting processes, and the possibility that some utilities will delay or modify capital plans if regulatory recovery becomes uncertain. For utilities already executing large spending programs tied to load growth and grid resilience, the inability to recover costs on schedule would erode the financial returns that underpin their credit ratings.

  Fitch expects utility sector capital expenditures to continue rising at a low- to mid-teens annual rate from 2026 through 2030.

  ## 展望調降

  惠譽信評（Fitch Ratings）於2026年6月12日將電力公用事業展望調降為「惡化」，指出電費可負擔性疑慮正在加速政治與監管風險，威脅公用事業向用電戶回收成本的能力。就在2025年12月，惠譽對該產業的展望評級仍為「中立」，僅提示費率調漲可能遭遇阻力；惠譽現在表示，這一風險正以「比預期更快、更廣泛」的速度成真。

  此次展望調整對整個產業具有重大信用影響。惠譽指出，該產業計劃於2026年創下約2,400億美元的資本支出紀錄，且預計年均支出將以低至中雙位數百分比的速度持續成長至2030年。惠譽表示，雖然這些投資長期而言有助於支撐信用品質，但電費攀升可能使費率的及時回收更加困難。

  ## 電費可負擔性壓力

  惠譽援引美國能源資訊署（EIA）數據指出，2026年3月美國住宅平均電價年增10.2%，從一年前的每度17.1美分上漲至18.8美分。每度電（kilowatt-hour）是衡量家庭用電量的基本單位，如此幅度的漲價直接反映在住宅用電戶每月更高的電費帳單上。

  此一電費壓力已開始影響立法行動。惠譽指出，印第安納州、緬因州與馬里蘭州今年均已通過旨在降低電費的相關法案。在賓夕法尼亞州，Exelon旗下子公司PECO Energy於4月中旬撤回向賓州公用事業委員會（Pennsylvania Public Utilities Commission）提出的電費與天然氣費率合計5.1億美元調漲申請，理由正是電費可負擔性疑慮。

  ## 選舉前夕的政治風險

  惠譽將2026年11月選舉周期列為近期風險的主要放大因素。由於全美36個州將舉行州長選舉，惠譽表示，電費議題正「成為競選的核心焦點」。電費成本已在2025年的紐澤西州與維吉尼亞州州長選舉中成為關鍵議題，這兩個州均由PJM互聯（PJM Interconnection）提供電力服務。PJM互聯是一個涵蓋13個州及哥倫比亞特區（全部或部分地區）的區域輸電組織，隨著預測資料中心負載持續增加、電力供應卻維持平穩，其容量電價已大幅飆升。

  ## 資料中心需求：潛在的抵銷力量

  惠譽承認，資料中心需求的急速成長為電費壓力提供了潛在的抵銷力量。大量新增負載使公用事業得以將固定基礎設施成本分攤至更廣泛的用戶群，公用事業業者也針對資料中心開發了專屬費率方案，以防止成本轉嫁給現有零售用電戶。然而，惠譽表示，這些效益「可能只會隨時間逐步顯現，且未必能完全抵銷高額資本支出所帶來的近期電費壓力」。

  ## 對產業的影響

  此次展望調降意味著，投資人與分析師應預期費率審查案將更加頻繁且爭議更多，立法機關可能介入費率制定程序，部分公用事業若監管回收前景不明朗，也可能延遲或修改資本計畫。對於已在推動與負載成長及電網韌性相關龐大支出計畫的公用事業而言，若無法按期回收成本，將侵蝕支撐其信用評級的財務報酬。

  惠譽預計，公用事業產業資本支出將從2026年至2030年持續以低至中雙位數的年增率攀升。
