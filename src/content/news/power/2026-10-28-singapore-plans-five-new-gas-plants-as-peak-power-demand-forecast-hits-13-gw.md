body_en: |
  Singapore's Energy Market Authority (EMA) plans to commission five new gas-fired power plants between 2026 and 2031 as the city-state prepares for peak electricity demand that could reach 13 gigawatts (GW) by the end of that period — roughly 51 percent above the 8.6 GW peak recorded in August 2026.

  EMA announced the five-year energy infrastructure plan on Monday, October 26, 2026. Natural gas currently supplies approximately 95 percent of Singapore's electricity generation, the authority said. The five new plants, added to existing installed capacity of around 12 GW, would increase total generation capacity by about 26 percent, according to EMA.

  ## Scale of demand growth

  EMA projected peak demand would rise from 8.6 GW to between 9.9 GW and 13 GW by 2031. The authority said the range reflects uncertainty over the timing of new large-scale energy consumers entering service. Semiconductor manufacturing, data centers, and broad adoption of electric vehicles are the primary demand drivers identified by EMA.

  ## Infrastructure plan scope

  The five-year plan spans six areas: power generation, natural gas supply, electricity transmission and distribution networks, solar energy, energy storage, and low-carbon electricity imports. On the gas supply side, EMA said the plan includes expanding capacity at Singapore's liquefied natural gas (LNG) terminal. LNG is natural gas cooled to liquid form for transport by sea and regasified on arrival before being fed into the grid.

  ## Pipeline for additional capacity

  Beyond the five plants already in the plan, EMA said it is evaluating proposals for further gas-fired generation capacity submitted before a September 2026 deadline. That tranche targets units intended to operate by 2032. The authority added that additional tenders for new generation could be launched within five years if electricity demand growth remains high.

  ## Pulau Tekong energy cluster

  EMA said it is also studying the feasibility of a new energy cluster on Pulau Tekong island. The authority said the site could integrate hydrogen-ready gas plants — units designed to eventually run on hydrogen rather than natural gas — alongside an LNG terminal and other low-carbon energy technologies on a single site.

  ## Significance for the sector

  The plan confirms natural gas will remain Singapore's dominant generation fuel through at least the early 2030s, with the country simultaneously expanding both its gas-fired fleet and its LNG import infrastructure to absorb load growth driven largely by data centers and semiconductor manufacturing. The Pulau Tekong feasibility study indicates the government is beginning to position gas infrastructure to accommodate a longer-term transition toward lower-carbon fuels.

  EMA said further capacity tenders could be opened within five years if demand growth remains strong, leaving the plan open to expansion beyond its current scope.
body_zh: |
  新加坡能源市場管理局（EMA）計畫在2026年至2031年間陸續啟用五座新燃氣電廠，以因應這座城市國家屆時可能達到13吉瓦（GW）的電力尖峰需求——較2026年8月所創下的8.6吉瓦歷史高點高出約51%。

  EMA於2026年10月26日（週一）正式公布這份五年能源基礎建設計畫。當局表示，天然氣目前供應新加坡約95%的電力。五座新電廠建成後，加計現有約12吉瓦的裝置容量，整體發電容量將提升約26%。

  ## 需求成長規模

  EMA預測，尖峰需求將從8.6吉瓦攀升至2031年的9.9吉瓦至13吉瓦區間。當局表示，此一預測區間反映的是大型新增用電戶正式投入運轉時程的不確定性。EMA指出，半導體製造、資料中心，以及電動車的全面普及，是三大主要需求驅動力。

  ## 基礎建設計畫範疇

  這份五年計畫涵蓋六大面向：發電、天然氣供應、輸配電網路、太陽能、儲能，以及低碳電力進口。在天然氣供應端，EMA表示計畫內容包括擴充新加坡液化天然氣（LNG）接收站的處理容量。液化天然氣是將天然氣冷卻至液態後以船舶運輸，抵達目的地後再氣化，並饋入電力系統。

  ## 後續容量建置管線

  除計畫內的五座電廠之外，EMA表示目前正在評估於2026年9月截止日期前所收到的其他燃氣發電容量提案，該批次的目標是建置預計在2032年前投入運轉的機組。當局並補充，若電力需求成長持續強勁，五年內可能再次啟動新發電容量的招標作業。

  ## 德光島能源園區

  EMA表示，目前亦在研究在德光島（Pulau Tekong）建置新能源園區的可行性。當局指出，該基地可整合具備氫能轉換準備的燃氣電廠——即設計上未來可改以氫氣取代天然氣作為燃料的機組——並在同一地點配置LNG接收終端及其他低碳能源技術。

  ## 產業意涵

  此計畫確認，天然氣至少在2030年代初期將持續作為新加坡的主力發電燃料，而新加坡也將同步擴大燃氣發電機組規模及LNG進口基礎建設，以吸納主要由資料中心與半導體製造所帶動的負載成長。德光島可行性研究則顯示，政府已開始前瞻布局，為天然氣基礎建設預留向低碳燃料長期轉型的空間。

  EMA表示，若需求成長維持強勁態勢，五年內可望再開放新容量招標，使本計畫保有超越現有規模的擴展彈性。
