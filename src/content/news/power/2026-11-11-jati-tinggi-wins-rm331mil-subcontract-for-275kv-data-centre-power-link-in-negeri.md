body_en: |
  Jati Tinggi Holding Sdn Bhd, a wholly-owned subsidiary of listed Malaysian group Jati Tinggi Group Bhd, has secured a RM33.1 million subcontract to install 275kV (kilovolt) power cable infrastructure for a data centre in Gerbil, Negeri Sembilan, the company said.

  The contract, titled "proposed 275KV bulk supply to a Gerbil data centre - interim supply," runs for nine months from the notice to start work.

  ## Contract Scope

  The work covers the supply, design, installation, testing and commissioning of two circuits of 275kV cables connecting a transition yard to the data centre's onsite substation. Jati Tinggi Holding will also construct the transition yard itself, complete overhead line T-off works (a junction that branches power from an existing overhead line into the facility), and carry out associated secondary system modifications.

  Jati Tinggi said the project is undertaken to support the interim power supply infrastructure requirements of the Gerbil data centre.

  ## Parties

  Jati Tinggi Holding is performing the work as a subcontractor. The main contractor and direct customer, which the company did not name, has more than 20 years of experience in Malaysia's construction sector and has completed infrastructure, construction and cable installation projects across the country, Jati Tinggi said.

  The nine-month engagement from the notice to start work sets the near-term delivery timeline for the interim power supply infrastructure.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞上市集團Jati Tinggi Group Bhd旗下全資子公司Jati Tinggi Holding Sdn Bhd，宣布取得一份價值3,310萬馬幣的分包合約，負責為森美蘭州Gerbil一座資料中心建置275千伏（kV）高壓電纜基礎設施。

  該合約正式名稱為「Gerbil資料中心275kV大容量供電方案——過渡期供電工程」，工期自開工通知起算共九個月。

  ## 合約範疇

  本專案工作範圍涵蓋兩迴路275kV電纜的供應、設計、安裝、測試及試運轉，負責連接過渡站場與資料中心園區內的變電站。Jati Tinggi Holding亦將負責興建過渡站場本體、完成架空線T型分接工程（即從現有架空線路分支引電進入設施的接頭工程），以及進行相關二次系統的配套修改作業。

  Jati Tinggi表示，本專案旨在支援Gerbil資料中心過渡期間的電力供應基礎設施需求。

  ## 合約各方

  Jati Tinggi Holding在本專案中擔任分包商角色。主合約商及直接發包方的名稱並未對外揭露，惟Jati Tinggi指出，該主合約商在馬來西亞建築業擁有逾20年經驗，過去曾在全國各地完成基礎設施、土木建設及電纜安裝等多項工程。

  本合約自開工通知起算九個月的工期，確立了此一過渡期電力供應基礎設施工程的近期交付時程。
