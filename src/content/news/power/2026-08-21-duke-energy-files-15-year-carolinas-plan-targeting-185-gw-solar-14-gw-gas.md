body_en: |
  Duke Energy filed its 2026 Carolinas Resource Plan with South Carolina regulators on Aug. 17, 2026, outlining a recommended portfolio of 18.5 gigawatts of new solar capacity, 14 GW of gas generation, 13 GW of energy storage, and 4.5 GW of new nuclear power to be added over the next 15 years across its North and South Carolina service territory.

  The plan, submitted to the Public Service Commission of South Carolina, projects that Duke's winter peak demand will rise by more than 10 GW — representing cumulative growth of approximately 30% — by 2041. Duke attributed the increase to rapid population and economic expansion, telling regulators that both South Carolina and North Carolina rank among the fastest-growing states in the nation.

  ## Scale of the planned build-out

  The 18.5 GW of solar called for in the plan is the largest single resource category by capacity. To put the figure in context, one gigawatt is roughly enough to power several hundred thousand average homes. Duke said solar procurement and construction are already active, with completed facilities, projects under construction, and open requests for proposals for solar and solar paired with storage.

  The 13 GW of energy storage — battery systems that can absorb excess generation and discharge it when demand peaks — accompanies the solar additions. Duke said it is scaling battery storage execution, with projects already in service, equipment secured, and interconnection activity underway. The utility has also issued a separate request for proposals for 400 megawatts of standalone storage in South Carolina.

  The 14 GW of gas capacity is divided between 8.2 GW of combined-cycle generators, which run more efficiently at sustained output, and 5.8 GW of combustion turbines, which are better suited to rapid startup and peaking duty. Duke described gas as a "major near-term reliability resource." The utility said it has already received regulatory approval for a 1.4 GW combined-cycle facility in Anderson County, South Carolina, and has executed turbine supply agreements with GE Vernova.

  ## Nuclear as a long-term reliability anchor

  The recommended portfolio includes approximately 4.5 GW of new nuclear capacity, which Duke called an "important resource to support long-term reliability and system needs." The utility is also working to extend the operational licenses of its existing nuclear fleet to run up to 80 years. Duke operates six nuclear sites with 11 total generating units. Two of those sites, both in South Carolina and representing four units, have already received subsequent renewed operating licenses, the company told regulators.

  ## Demand-side programs

  Alongside supply additions, Duke's plan relies on what it calls grid edge programs — energy efficiency measures, demand-side management, load curtailment, customer programs, and storage demand response — to moderate growth in overall energy consumption. The plan maintains a minimum annual energy efficiency savings target of 1% of Duke's load forecast and accounts for expanding demand response capabilities, the utility said.

  ## Regulatory timeline

  The South Carolina Public Service Commission is expected to hold a hearing on the resource plan in April and issue an order by June. The plan covers Duke's dual-state Carolinas service territory, meaning the outcome will shape generation and grid investment across both states through 2041.
body_zh: |
  杜克能源（Duke Energy）於2026年8月17日向南卡羅萊納州監管機關提交「2026年卡羅萊納資源計畫」，規劃在未來15年內，於北卡羅萊納州與南卡羅萊納州的供電區域新增18.5 GW太陽能、14 GW天然氣發電、13 GW儲能，以及4.5 GW核能裝置容量，以因應預計成長約30%的用電負載需求。

  該計畫提交至南卡羅萊納州公用服務委員會。根據計畫推估，杜克能源的冬季尖峰需求至2041年將增加逾10 GW，累計成長幅度約達30%。杜克能源向監管機關表示，南、北卡羅萊納州均屬全美成長速度最快的州份之列，人口與經濟的快速擴張是推升用電需求的主要因素。

  ## 建設規模一覽

  計畫中規劃的18.5 GW太陽能，是所有資源類別中裝置容量最大的單一項目。就規模而言，1 GW的發電量約足以供應數十萬戶一般家庭的用電需求。杜克能源指出，太陽能採購與建設工程已積極展開，目前已有完工設施與進行中的建設案，並已就太陽能及太陽能搭配儲能項目公開招標。

  13 GW的儲能容量——即可吸收多餘電力、並在用電尖峰時放電的電池系統——是配合太陽能新增容量的重要配套。杜克能源表示，電池儲能的建設推進已見成效，目前已有案場上線運轉、設備已完成鎖定，且併網作業正積極推動中。此外，該公司亦已針對南卡羅萊納州400 MW的獨立儲能項目另行發出招標邀請。

  14 GW天然氣裝置容量中，包含8.2 GW的聯合循環機組（持續穩定出力時效率較高）與5.8 GW的燃氣渦輪機組（啟動速度快，適合調峰需求）。杜克能源將天然氣定位為「近期重要的可靠度資源」。目前，該公司已取得南卡羅萊納州安德森郡一座1.4 GW聯合循環電廠的監管核准，並已與GE Vernova簽訂渦輪機供應協議。

  ## 核能：長期可靠度的壓艙石

  建議方案納入約4.5 GW的新核能容量，杜克能源稱其為「支撐長期可靠度與系統需求的重要資源」。與此同時，該公司也積極推動現有核電機組的運轉執照展延，目標是讓機組的運轉年限最多延長至80年。杜克能源目前共操作6座核電廠、合計11部發電機組，其中位於南卡羅萊納州的兩座電廠共4部機組，已完成後續運轉執照的更新並獲核准，公司已就此向監管機關說明。

  ## 需求面管理計畫

  在新增供電容量之外，杜克能源的計畫亦仰賴其所謂「電網邊緣計畫」來抑制整體用電需求的成長，措施涵蓋能源效率、需求面管理、負載削減、客戶端計畫，以及儲能需量反應等多種機制。公司表示，計畫設定每年至少達成相當於其負載預測1%的能源效率節約目標，並持續擴大需量反應能力的布建規模。

  ## 監管審查時程

  南卡羅萊納州公用服務委員會預計於4月就資源計畫舉行聽證會，並於6月發出裁示。本計畫涵蓋杜克能源橫跨南、北卡羅萊納州的雙州供電區域，審查結果將決定兩州直至2041年的發電建設方向與電網投資布局。
