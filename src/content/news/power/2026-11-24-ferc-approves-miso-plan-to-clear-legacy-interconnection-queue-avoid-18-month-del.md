body_en: |
  The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission approved rule changes on Nov. 20, 2026, allowing the Midcontinent Independent System Operator to accelerate review of its "Definitive Planning Phase–2022" interconnection queue and avoid delays that would have rippled across the grid operator's entire project pipeline.

  FERC approved the changes with an Oct. 19 effective date. The 2022 study cycle holds about 35 gigawatts of active generation and storage projects. Without the approved revisions, that cycle would have been delayed by roughly 18 months, which MISO said would have set back other pending study cycles as well.

  ## The scope of MISO's interconnection backlog

  MISO's total interconnection queue spans four study cycles and contains nearly 600 projects representing close to 125 gigawatts of proposed capacity. The queue breaks down as follows:

  - 44 GW of solar
  - 25 GW of batteries
  - About 20 GW of wind
  - 19 GW of gas
  - 10 GW of hybrid projects

  One gigawatt is roughly equivalent to the output of a large conventional power plant and enough to supply several hundred thousand homes under typical conditions.

  ## What the rule changes do

  The core problem MISO identified in its Sept. 21 proposal was that the rules governing its 2022 study cycle — which predated more recent interconnection process reforms — created incentives for developers of nonviable projects to stay in the queue rather than withdraw. Under the old rules, withdrawing triggered financial penalties, so developers held their positions even when projects were unlikely to proceed. When those developers eventually did exit, later in the process, they triggered restudies that delayed every remaining project in the queue.

  To address this, MISO's approved plan allowed projects to exit the 2022 queue before "Decision Point II" — which occurred Oct. 20 — without paying withdrawal penalties. The plan also caps the number of restudies in Definitive Planning Phase III to a single restudy, applying that limit to the 2022 cycle and all future DPP cycles.

  FERC also approved MISO's proposal to raise the threshold that qualifies a project for a penalty-free withdrawal later in the review process. The previous threshold allowed a penalty-free exit if MISO determined a project faced a 35% increase in network upgrade costs. The approved rule raises that figure to 50%.

  ## Challenges from developers rejected

  Two renewable energy developers challenged elements of the proposal. Ranger Power argued that allowing penalty-free withdrawals rewards unwanted behavior and is discriminatory. FERC rejected that argument, saying MISO had demonstrated a need to process its queue quickly and reduce the risk of cascading withdrawals and restudies that have delayed previous cycles.

  Pathway Power, a clean energy developer, contended that MISO had not justified raising the penalty-free withdrawal threshold to 50%. FERC dismissed that argument as well, noting the 50% threshold is consistent with its prior approval of the same threshold for MISO's queue process beginning with the 2023 study cycle, and finding it does not unfairly burden small project developers.

  ## Significance for the region's generation pipeline

  FERC said MISO's tariff revisions fulfill the purposes of the commission's final rules on generator interconnection by helping ensure that projects can connect to the transmission system "in a reliable, efficient, transparent, and timely manner."

  Some details of the approved plan remain unresolved pending FERC decisions in two separate dockets at the agency.
body_zh: |
  ## FERC批准MISO方案，化解舊積壓併網審查恐延宕18個月危機

  美國聯邦能源監管委員會（FERC）於2026年11月20日批准規則修訂，允許中大陸獨立系統運營商（MISO）加速審查其「確定性規劃階段—2022年」併網申請佇列，同時避免延誤效應蔓延至整個電網運營商的專案管線。

  FERC批准本次修訂，溯及生效日期為10月19日。2022年研究週期目前約持有350億瓦的有效發電與儲能專案。若無此次修訂，該週期將延誤約18個月，MISO表示此一延誤亦將拖累其他待審研究週期。

  ## MISO併網積壓案件的規模

  MISO的併網申請佇列橫跨四個研究週期，共收納近600個專案，代表約1,250億瓦（125 GW）的擬建容量。佇列組成如下：

  - 太陽能：440億瓦（44 GW）
  - 電池儲能：250億瓦（25 GW）
  - 風電：約200億瓦（20 GW）
  - 天然氣：190億瓦（19 GW）
  - 複合型專案：100億瓦（10 GW）

  10億瓦（1 GW）大致相當於一座大型傳統電廠的發電量，在一般用電條件下可供應數十萬戶家庭用電。

  ## 規則修訂的內容

  MISO於9月21日提出的修訂方案中，指出核心問題在於：規範2022年研究週期的舊有規則——早於近期併網流程改革——為不具可行性的專案開發商提供了留守佇列而非退出的誘因。在舊規則下，退出申請將觸發財務罰款，因此即便專案不太可能推進，開發商仍占住資格。當這些開發商最終在流程較後階段退出時，便引發重新評估（restudy），拖延佇列中所有剩餘專案的進度。

  為解決上述問題，FERC批准的MISO方案允許專案在「決策點II」（Decision Point II，發生於10月20日）之前退出2022年佇列，無需繳納退出罰款。方案亦將確定性規劃階段第三期（DPP Phase III）的重新評估次數上限設為一次，並將此限制適用於2022年週期及所有未來的DPP週期。

  FERC同時批准MISO提案，調高在審查流程較後階段仍可申請免罰退出的門檻。舊規則允許當MISO認定專案面臨電網升級費用增加35%時，可免罰退出；修訂後的規則將此門檻提高至50%。

  ## 開發商的挑戰遭到駁回

  兩家再生能源開發商對方案部分內容提出異議。Ranger Power主張，允許免罰退出是在獎勵不當行為，且具有歧視性。FERC駁回此一主張，表示MISO已充分說明迅速處理佇列的必要性，並指出此舉旨在降低連鎖退出與重新評估的風險——正是這些情形拖累了過去數個週期的進度。

  潔淨能源開發商Pathway Power則主張，MISO未能充分說明將免罰退出門檻調高至50%的理由。FERC同樣駁回此一主張，指出50%的門檻與委員會先前批准自2023年研究週期起適用相同門檻的決定一致，並認定此規定並未對小型專案開發商造成不公平負擔。

  ## 對該地區發電管線的重要意義

  FERC表示，MISO的費率修訂履行了委員會關於發電機併網最終規則的目的，有助於確保專案能以「可靠、有效率、透明且及時的方式」接入輸電系統。

  獲批方案中部分細節，仍有待FERC就委員會兩個獨立案號所作的裁決後方能確定。
