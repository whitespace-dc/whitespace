body_en: |
  A fire at a three-storey shophouse in Kanchanaburi province, Thailand, has uncovered a suspected illegal Bitcoin mining operation, the second such discovery near Bangkok in less than a week, according to Thai PBS and local police reports.

  Firefighters responded to the blaze in Mueang district on July 15. The fire, which broke out on the third floor, was contained within approximately 30 minutes and did not spread to neighboring buildings. After extinguishing the fire, officials discovered more than 20 computers and associated devices believed to have been used to mine Bitcoin, all damaged in the fire. The room also contained large air-conditioning units and soundproofing material.

  Officials from Thailand's Provincial Electricity Authority (PEA), the state body that oversees regional power distribution, were called to inspect the property. They reportedly found modified wiring designed to bypass the electricity meter. Authorities estimated the operation consumed between 100,000 and 200,000 baht ($3,000 to $6,000) per month in electricity, while the building's recorded monthly bill was approximately 1,000 baht ($30). Investigators believe an electrical short circuit in the property's control panel caused the fire. Police are seeking the building's owner for questioning.

  ## Pathum Thani raid one week earlier

  The Kanchanaburi discovery followed a raid on July 9 in Pathum Thani province, north of Bangkok, where police and PEA officials searched an apparently abandoned three-storey commercial building in Khlong Luang district. Inside a soundproofed room on the third floor, they found mining equipment in operation.

  Investigators alleged that concealed wiring had been installed to bypass the electricity meter, leaving the property with a recorded monthly bill of just 35.62 baht ($1). The illegal connection was believed to have been active for more than two years, with authorities estimating losses of several million baht. Police found 18 mining machines at the site and seized 11 rigs, four internet receiver boxes, cables, and other equipment. No arrests were announced at the time of the raid.

  Police are investigating who installed and operated the Pathum Thani mine and whether it is connected to other sites using similar meter-bypass methods.

  ## Legal status and enforcement context

  Crypto mining is legal in Thailand, where miners are classified as manufacturers and subject to taxation. However, Thai authorities have uncovered a series of unregistered mining operations in recent years that are accused of bypassing electricity meters and stealing power from the grid.

  The two incidents illustrate a recurring enforcement challenge: operations in both cases were housed in ordinary commercial or mixed-use buildings, used soundproofing to conceal equipment noise, and relied on tampered metering to suppress electricity costs to a fraction of actual consumption. The Pathum Thani site's recorded bill of less than one dollar per month against an estimated actual consumption of several million baht over two years underscores the scale of potential losses to the electricity authority per site.

  Police investigations into both sites remain open, with authorities seeking to identify operators and determine whether the operations are linked to a broader network.

  泰國北碧府（Kanchanaburi）一棟三層樓店屋日前發生火警，事後查出現場疑似藏有非法比特幣採礦設備，成為不到一週內曼谷近郊第二起同類案件，消息來源為泰國PBS及當地警方報告。

  消防人員於7月15日趕赴孟縣（Mueang District）搶救。火勢從三樓竄起，約30分鐘內獲得控制，未延燒至鄰近建築。火勢撲滅後，官員在現場發現逾20台電腦及相關設備，研判均曾用於挖掘比特幣，且已在火災中燒毀受損。房間內另設有大型空調機組及隔音材料。

  主管地區電力配送的泰國國營機構——省電力局（Provincial Electricity Authority，PEA）官員隨後奉召前往勘查。據報，調查人員發現屋內電路遭改裝，刻意繞過電表計算用電量。當局估計，該採礦設施每月實際耗電費用介於10萬至20萬泰銖（約3,000至6,000美元），然而建築物正式登錄的月電費帳單僅約1,000泰銖（約30美元）。調查人員研判，火災起因為建築物配電箱發生電線短路。警方目前正追緝建築物所有人到案說明。

  ## 一週前的巴吞他尼府突搜行動

  北碧府案的發現，發生於7月9日巴吞他尼府（Pathum Thani）搜查行動後不到一週。巴吞他尼府位於曼谷以北，警方與省電力局官員當日搜索了空朗縣（Khlong Luang District）一棟外觀廢棄的三層樓商業建築，並在三樓一間隔音房間內，發現仍在運作的採礦設備。

  調查人員指稱，屋內藏有隱蔽電線，刻意繞過電表，致使建築物正式登錄的月電費帳單僅35.62泰銖（不足1美元）。當局研判，這條非法接線已持續使用逾兩年，估計造成損失達數百萬泰銖。警方在現場查獲18台採礦機，並扣押11台礦機、4台網路接收器、電纜及其他設備。搜查行動當時未宣布任何逮捕。

  警方正就是誰安裝並操控巴吞他尼府礦場展開調查，並釐清此案是否與其他使用類似電表破解手法的案件相互關聯。

  ## 法律地位與執法背景

  加密貨幣採礦在泰國屬合法行為，礦工在法律上被歸類為製造業者，須依規定申報納稅。然而，泰國當局近年已陸續揭發一系列未登記採礦業者，這些業者被指控繞過電表、從電網竊取電力。

  上述兩起事件凸顯出一種屢見不鮮的執法挑戰：兩處據點均設於普通商業或混合用途建築，皆藉隔音材料掩蓋設備噪音，並依賴竄改電表，將所記錄的電費壓低至實際耗電量的極小比例。以巴吞他尼府案為例，該址正式登錄的月電費帳單不足1美元，而當局估計兩年來的實際耗電成本高達數百萬泰銖，充分說明每處據點可能對電力局造成的損失規模之鉅。

  兩起案件的警方調查仍在進行中，當局持續追查業者身分，並研判這些採礦設施是否隸屬更大規模的非法網絡。
