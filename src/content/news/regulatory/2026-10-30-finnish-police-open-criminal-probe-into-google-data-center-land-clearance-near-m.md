body_en: |
  Finnish police launched a criminal investigation Thursday into suspected breaches of environmental law at a planned Google data center construction site near Muhos, in northern Finland, after the Alphabet subsidiary responsible for the project cleared forest and conducted drainage work before completing legally required environmental impact assessments.

  The investigation centers on Tuike Finland, the Alphabet unit overseeing the construction. Detective Superintendent Kari Pakanen, who is leading the probe, told Reuters that police are examining whether the company's operations violated the water act and caused environmental damage. A guilty finding could result in a corporate fine. Pakanen declined to say whether individuals could also become investigation targets.

  Preliminary findings showed "ambiguities and shortcomings" in drainage and water management at the site, police said in a statement. The investigation will extend into 2027, with a progress update due in December.

  ## Halt order and permitting timeline

  Finland's Supervisory Agency — known by its Finnish abbreviation LVV — which oversees environmental permits and assessments, issued a stop order on October 6 directing Google to halt preparatory measures that alter the environment at Muhos and at a second, unnamed site. Pakanen said LVV holds the authority to decide whether and when work may resume. An LVV spokesperson said it was unclear how long the permitting process might take.

  Google said it would cooperate with the investigation. "We've partnered closely with the government and communities in Finland on infrastructure for over a decade, and are actively responding to enquiries from LVV and other authorities," a company spokesperson said. The company added that it would continue to focus on maintaining compliance at its sites.

  ## The $15 billion investment at stake

  The Muhos development is part of a $15 billion commitment by Google to build artificial intelligence infrastructure across Finland, announced last month and described by the company as its largest investment in Europe. The program includes data centers, electricity grid improvements, and clean energy and battery projects to support services such as Gemini, Search, Maps, and YouTube.

  Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo backed the plan at the time of the announcement, calling it an economic boost and playing down concerns that it would draw heavily on Finland's energy supply and push up electricity prices. The prime minister's office did not respond to a request for comment on the criminal probe.

  ## Finland's data center market

  Finland has become one of Europe's primary data center investment destinations, supported by its cool climate, low-carbon electricity, and government endorsement of AI projects. The country is roughly the size of Germany but has a population of fewer than 6 million people, and has seen less vocal opposition to large-scale data center construction than other markets, including in the United States.

  AI data centers are among the main drivers of a projected rise in Finnish electricity demand. Grid operator Fingrid has said consumption could increase by up to 40 percent in coming years if all agreed grid connection projects proceed.

  The criminal investigation and the unresolved permitting question at LVV now create uncertainty over the timeline for resuming construction at Muhos. LVV has not indicated when it expects to rule on the permits needed to restart the work.

  芬蘭警方週四就 Google 在芬蘭北部穆霍斯（Muhos）附近計畫興建的資料中心工地疑似違反環保法規一事，正式展開刑事調查。起因是 Alphabet 旗下負責該案的子公司 Tuike Finland，在尚未完成法定環境影響評估的情況下，已先行砍伐林地並施作排水工程。

  此次調查的核心對象為負責監督工程的 Alphabet 旗下單位 Tuike Finland。主導調查的偵查督察卡里·帕卡南（Kari Pakanen）向路透社表示，警方正在調查該公司的相關作業是否違反《水法》，以及是否造成環境損害。若經認定有罪，公司將面臨罰款。帕卡南拒絕透露是否有個人可能同樣成為調查對象。

  警方在聲明中指出，初步調查結果顯示，工地的排水與水資源管理存在「模糊之處與缺失」。調查預計延續至 2027 年，並將於今年 12 月提交一份進度更新報告。

  ## 停工令與許可時程

  芬蘭環境監管機構——以芬蘭文縮寫「LVV」為人所知，負責監督環境許可與評估事務——已於 10 月 6 日發出停工令，要求 Google 立即停止在穆霍斯及另一處未具名工地進行任何會改變環境的前置作業。帕卡南表示，LVV 擁有決定工程能否以及何時恢復的權限。LVV 一名發言人則表示，目前尚不清楚許可審核程序需要多長時間。

  Google 表示將配合調查。公司發言人說：「我們在芬蘭與政府及各地社區攜手推動基礎建設已逾十年，目前正積極回應 LVV 及其他主管機關的詢問。」Google 並補充，公司將持續致力於確保旗下各工地符合法規要求。

  ## 攸關 150 億美元的龐大投資

  穆霍斯開發計畫是 Google 在芬蘭投資建設人工智慧基礎設施承諾的一部分，投資總額高達 150 億美元。這項計畫於上月宣布，Google 將其定位為在歐洲規模最大的一筆投資，涵蓋資料中心、電網升級，以及用於支援 Gemini、搜尋、地圖與 YouTube 等服務的潔淨能源與電池項目。

  芬蘭總理佩泰里·奧爾波（Petteri Orpo）在計畫宣布時表示支持，稱此舉將為經濟帶來動能，並淡化外界對芬蘭能源供應可能因此承壓、電價恐將上漲的疑慮。總理府對於此次刑事調查的置評請求，截至發稿前未予回應。

  ## 芬蘭資料中心市場

  芬蘭已躋身歐洲主要的資料中心投資目的地之列，背後有賴於涼爽的氣候、低碳電力供應，以及政府對人工智慧相關計畫的積極支持。芬蘭國土面積與德國相當，人口卻不足 600 萬，與包括美國在內的其他市場相比，當地民眾對於大型資料中心建設的反彈聲浪相對較小。

  AI 資料中心是推動芬蘭電力需求預期增長的主要驅動力之一。電網業者 Fingrid 表示，若所有已簽約的電網接入計畫均如期推進，芬蘭電力消耗在未來幾年內可能增加多達四成。
