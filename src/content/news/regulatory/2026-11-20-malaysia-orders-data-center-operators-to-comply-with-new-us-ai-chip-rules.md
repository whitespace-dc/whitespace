  ## Malaysia directs data centers to follow US chip restrictions

  Malaysia's government has told data center operators in the country to comply with new US restrictions on advanced artificial intelligence chips, Trade Minister Johari Abdul Ghani said Wednesday, a day after Washington announced tighter controls on the export of high-performance semiconductors.

  Johari said Malaysia's government will seek clarification from US authorities on the scope of the new restrictions before communicating compliance requirements to the 117 data center projects that have been approved to operate in the country. The minister said the government had not yet determined precisely how the new rules would affect the financing needs of individual companies, but said operators must follow US chip regulations because the chips are classified as strategic assets.

  "Malaysia remains open to investment. The new US rules and regulations will provide clear direction for investors and partners when carrying out business activities in Malaysia," Johari said, as quoted by Reuters.

  ## Stricter due diligence for non-US customers

  Johari said companies that transact with customers based in countries outside the United States may face tighter due diligence checks and stricter know-your-customer (KYC) requirements — a process by which firms verify the identity of their clients — from US authorities. KYC is a standard compliance procedure used to prevent fraud and sanctions violations.

  The minister said Malaysia had not received any evidence that data center tenants in the country had traded or re-exported chips in violation of US rules. He indicated the government would need documentary evidence, including shipping manifests, before taking enforcement action against any party.

  "If they are re-exporting, we want to know who is doing it. They must show the manifest documents that have been submitted. After that, we can take further steps. We cannot simply make accusations," Johari said, as quoted by the Malaysian national news agency Bernama.

  ## Malaysia's data center expansion at stake

  Malaysia has grown into a significant regional data center hub, attracting nearly $100 billion in investment since 2021, according to the source. Analysts have warned, however, that increasing scrutiny over the use and distribution of AI chips could hamper growth in the country's data center industry.

  ## Megaspeed probe

  Separately, Malaysia's government said it is investigating the local unit of Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud computing company, over a US probe into allegations that Megaspeed's services were used to circumvent AI chip export restrictions. Megaspeed is a customer of Nvidia, the dominant maker of AI processors. The company denied the allegations and said it takes its compliance obligations under US regulations seriously.

  ## What comes next

  Malaysia's government said it will communicate specific compliance requirements to all 117 approved data center projects once it has received clarification from US authorities on the rules' scope, particularly regarding licensing conditions and the provision of data center services to third-party customers.

  ## 馬來西亞指示資料中心遵守美國晶片限制

  馬來西亞政府已要求該國各資料中心業者，遵守美國針對先進人工智慧晶片頒布的最新出口管制規定。貿易部長祖哈里·阿都加尼（Johari Abdul Ghani）周三宣布此一指示，距離華盛頓宣布收緊高性能半導體出口管制僅一天。

  祖哈里表示，政府將先向美國當局尋求新規適用範圍的釐清，再向目前在馬來西亞獲批准運營的117個資料中心專案溝通具體合規要求。他說，政府尚未確定新規將如何影響個別企業的融資需求，但強調業者必須遵守美國的晶片法規，因為這些晶片被列為戰略資產。

  「馬來西亞對投資保持開放。美國的新規將為投資者和合作夥伴在馬來西亞開展商業活動提供明確方向，」祖哈里在接受路透社引述時表示。

  ## 非美國客戶面臨更嚴格盡職調查

  祖哈里表示，與非美國客戶進行交易的企業，可能面臨美國當局更嚴格的盡職調查及「認識你的客戶」（KYC）要求——這是企業核實客戶身分的標準程序，也是防範詐欺與違反制裁的常見合規措施。

  部長表示，馬來西亞目前尚未收到任何證據，顯示該國資料中心租戶有交易或轉出口晶片並違反美國法規的情形。他指出，政府在對任何一方採取執法行動之前，需要包括貨運清單在內的書面憑證。

  「如果有人在轉出口，我們想知道是誰在做。他們必須出示已提交的貨運清單文件。之後，我們才能採取進一步措施。我們不能憑空指控，」祖哈里在接受馬來西亞國家通訊社馬新社（Bernama）引述時表示。

  ## 馬來西亞資料中心擴張前景受矚

  馬來西亞近年已躍升為重要的區域資料中心樞紐，自2021年以來累計吸引近1,000億美元投資。然而，分析師警告，隨著外界對人工智慧晶片使用與分銷的審查日趨嚴格，馬來西亞資料中心產業的成長動能恐因此受阻。

  ## Megaspeed遭調查

  另一方面，馬來西亞政府表示，正針對新加坡雲端運算公司Megaspeed的馬來西亞子公司展開調查，起因是美國方面就「Megaspeed服務被用於規避人工智慧晶片出口限制」的指控展開調查。Megaspeed是人工智慧處理器市場龍頭製造商輝達（Nvidia）的客戶。該公司否認相關指控，並表示認真看待其在美國法規下的合規義務。

  ## 後續發展

  馬來西亞政府表示，待向美國當局釐清新規的適用範圍——尤其是授權條件及向第三方客戶提供資料中心服務等相關細節——後，將向所有117個已核准資料中心專案溝通具體合規要求。
