body_en: |
  The European Union's supply of hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), greenhouse gases widely used in refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat pumps, fell 15% in climate-impact terms in 2025 and finished 2% below the ceiling set by the EU F-gas regulation, the European Environment Agency (EEA) has reported.

  ## Supply volume holds steady while climate impact falls

  Total supply of fluorinated gases (F-gases) to the EU in 2025 was 12% lower than in 2024 when measured in CO2 equivalents (CO2e), a unit that expresses a gas's warming impact relative to carbon dioxide. In raw tonnes, however, supply remained almost unchanged year-on-year.

  The EEA calculates F-gas supply primarily from reported production, imports, and exports, treating it as an indicator of actual industry consumption. Against 2015 levels, the CO2e figure in 2025 was approximately 53% lower.

  ## Refrigeration and heat pumps dominate F-gas use

  Refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat pumps (RACHP) accounted for more than 70% of F-gas applications in 2025 by weight, or approximately 40% when measured in CO2e. In 2025, 47,394 tonnes of fluorinated gases were supplied for RACHP applications, a rise of 0.59% from 2024. Measured in CO2e, RACHP supply fell approximately 18%, to 40.5 million tonnes CO2 equivalent (MtCO2e).

  ## Equipment imports rebound, drawing on banked quota

  Imports of HFCs pre-charged inside RACHP equipment rose approximately 12% in CO2e terms in 2025 compared to 2024, reversing two consecutive years of decline that followed increases between 2020 and 2022. The EEA attributed the increase to higher overall imports of stationary cooling and heating equipment, including heat pumps.

  Under the F-gas regulation, importers of HFC-containing equipment must hold quota authorisations covering the refrigerant inside their products. Consumption of authorisations for equipment imports again exceeded newly issued authorisations in 2025, with importers drawing on reserves accumulated in earlier years. That reserve has fallen 33% since 2020 but still stood at more than four times the annual HFC volume imported in RACHP equipment in 2025.

  ## Reclaim activity edges lower

  The volume of HFCs reclaimed, meaning recovered and reprocessed for reuse, was 5.3 MtCO2e in 2025, approximately 2% below the 2024 level.

  ## Illegal trade a persistent data gap

  The EEA compliance figures cover only the legitimate market. The agency noted that illegal trade in HFCs has been estimated to account for between 20% and 40% of the legitimate market volume, a quantity not reflected in the official supply totals.

  ## What the figures mean

  The 2025 results confirm that EU HFC supply remained within the limits of the F-gas regulation's phase-down schedule, with the CO2e metric declining faster than physical volume. The quota authorisation reserve held by RACHP equipment importers declined by one-third between 2020 and 2025 and stood at more than four times the annual HFC volume imported in RACHP equipment in 2025, according to the EEA.
body_zh: |
  歐洲環境署（EEA）最新報告指出，歐盟氫氟碳化物（HFC）供應量於2025年以氣候衝擊計算下滑15%，低於歐盟含氟氣體法規所設上限2%。HFC廣泛應用於冷凍、空調及熱泵系統，屬於溫室氣體。報告同時顯示，含氟氣體（F-gas）總供應量較2015年水準已下降約53%。

  ## 供應重量持平，氣候衝擊指標下滑

  以二氧化碳當量（CO2e）衡量，2025年歐盟含氟氣體總供應量較2024年減少12%。CO2e是一種將氣體暖化效應換算為相對於二氧化碳的單位。然而，若以實際公噸計算，供應量較前一年幾乎持平。

  歐洲環境署主要依據業者申報的生產量、進口量及出口量推算含氟氣體供應量，並將其視為實際產業消費的指標。與2015年相比，2025年的CO2e數值已降低約53%。

  ## 冷凍空調與熱泵主導含氟氣體用途

  2025年，冷凍、空調及熱泵（RACHP）應用以重量計占含氟氣體用途逾70%，若以CO2e衡量則約占40%。2025年供應至RACHP用途的含氟氣體為47,394公噸，較2024年微增0.59%。以CO2e計算，RACHP供應量下滑約18%，降至4,050萬公噸CO2當量（MtCO2e）。

  ## 設備進口回升，業者動用配額儲備

  2025年，預充HFC冷媒的RACHP設備進口量以CO2e計算較2024年增加約12%，扭轉了2020至2022年持續成長後連續兩年下滑的趨勢。歐洲環境署將此增幅歸因於定置式冷暖設備（包含熱泵）整體進口量的回升。

  依據含氟氣體法規，進口含HFC設備的業者須持有涵蓋產品內冷媒用量的配額授權。2025年，設備進口商消耗的配額授權再度超過當年新核發量，業者持續動用前幾年累積的儲備配額。該儲備量自2020年以來已縮減33%，但截至2025年底，仍相當於當年RACHP設備進口HFC年量的四倍以上。

  ## 回收再利用量略降

  2025年HFC回收量（即回收後再處理以供重複使用的量）為530萬公噸CO2當量（5.3 MtCO2e），較2024年減少約2%。

  ## 非法貿易仍是數據缺口

  歐洲環境署的合規數據僅涵蓋合法市場。該機構指出，HFC非法貿易量估計約占合法市場量的20%至40%，此部分並未反映在官方供應量統計中。

  ## 數據的意涵

  2025年的數據確認，歐盟HFC供應量維持在含氟氣體法規削減時程限額之內，CO2e指標的降幅快於實際重量的降幅。歐洲環境署表示，RACHP設備進口商持有的配額授權儲備量在2020年至2025年間縮減了三分之一，但截至2025年底，仍超過當年RACHP設備進口HFC年量的四倍。
