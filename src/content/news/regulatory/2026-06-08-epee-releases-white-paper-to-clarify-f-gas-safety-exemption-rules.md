body_en: |
  The European Partnership for Energy and the Environment (EPEE), a Brussels-based industry association representing refrigeration, air conditioning, and heat pump manufacturers, has released a white paper clarifying how safety exemptions under the European Union's revised F-gas regulation (EU 2024/573) should be interpreted and applied in practice.

  The paper, titled *The Safety requirements exemption under the 2024 revised F-gas Regulation*, is available for public download and is the product of approximately two years of work with EPEE members and industry stakeholders.

  ## What the regulation requires

  EU 2024/573 — the updated EU regulation on fluorinated greenhouse gases, commonly called F-gases — tightens restrictions on the sale and use of refrigerants with high global warming potential (GWP), a measure of how much heat a substance traps in the atmosphere relative to carbon dioxide. The regulation introduces stricter prohibitions on placing certain products on the market once they exceed defined GWP thresholds.

  The regulation does not apply those bans absolutely. Article 3 of EU 2024/573 and the associated Implementing Act 2024/2174 together define conditions under which a "safety exemption" may be invoked — that is, circumstances where a higher-GWP refrigerant may still be used because switching to a lower-GWP alternative would conflict with safety requirements at a specific site of operation.

  ## Where ambiguity remained

  Despite those legal provisions, EPEE said questions persist about how the exemptions work in practice and how they will be enforced. The white paper is designed to support a common understanding across the industry of when the exemption applies and what steps stakeholders must take to implement it correctly.

  ## Significance for the industry

  The publication matters because the gap between a regulation's text and its practical enforcement can create significant compliance uncertainty for equipment manufacturers, installers, and building operators across the EU. By establishing a shared interpretation developed with member companies and other stakeholders, EPEE is attempting to reduce that uncertainty before enforcement questions arise at the national level.

  The white paper is now available for download from EPEE.

  ## EPEE發布白皮書，釐清F-gas安全豁免規則

  總部設於布魯塞爾的產業協會「歐洲能源與環境夥伴關係」（EPEE）代表冷凍、空調及熱泵製造商，日前發布一份白皮書，說明歐盟修訂版氟化溫室氣體法規（EU 2024/573）下的安全豁免條款應如何解讀與實務操作。

  這份白皮書題為《2024年修訂版F-gas法規下的安全要求豁免》，目前已開放公開下載，是EPEE與旗下會員及產業利害關係人歷時約兩年共同研究的成果。

  ## 法規要求內容

  EU 2024/573是歐盟針對氟化溫室氣體（通稱F-gas）的最新修訂法規，進一步收緊對高全球暖化潛勢（GWP）冷媒的銷售與使用限制。GWP是衡量某物質相對於二氧化碳在大氣中捕獲熱量能力的指標。該法規對超過特定GWP門檻的產品，訂有更嚴格的上市禁令。

  然而，這些禁令並非毫無例外。EU 2024/573第3條及相關執行法令2024/2174共同規定了可援引「安全豁免」的條件——亦即在特定作業場所，若改用低GWP替代冷媒將與安全要求相牴觸，業者仍可繼續使用較高GWP冷媒的情形。

  ## 現存的模糊地帶

  儘管上述法律條文已有明文規定，EPEE指出，業界對豁免條款的實務運作方式及執法標準仍存有諸多疑問。這份白皮書旨在協助產業各方建立共同理解，釐清豁免條款的適用時機，以及利害關係人在正確執行時應採取哪些步驟。

  ## 對產業的重要意義

  這份文件之所以受到重視，在於法規條文與實務執法之間的落差，往往會在整個歐盟境內為設備製造商、安裝業者及建築管理者帶來相當程度的法規遵循不確定性。EPEE藉由與會員企業及各方利害關係人共同確立一套統一的解讀框架，試圖在各國主管機關正式執法前，預先降低這種不確定性。

  該白皮書目前已可從EPEE官方網站下載取得。
