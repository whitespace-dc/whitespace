# Police seize £1.2m of equipment from Farnborough data center used for illegal streaming

UK police seized approximately £1.2m worth of equipment during a raid on a Farnborough data center. The facility had been used for illegal streaming activities. The operation represents enforcement action against unauthorized content distribution.

# 英警查獲資料中心非法串流案，沒收120萬英鎊設備
