  ## Malaysia moves to legislate data centre industry as energy share heads toward one-third of national demand

  Malaysia's government is drafting dedicated legislation to regulate data centre development, the Economy Ministry announced Monday, citing mounting pressure on the country's energy and water resources as the sector grows rapidly toward consuming a third of national power by 2035.

  The Digital Infrastructure (Data Centre) Bill is expected to be presented to the cabinet next year, the ministry said in a statement. The bill aims to address environmental imbalances, ensure data centre investments benefit Malaysia's broader digital ecosystem, and support the country's artificial intelligence ambitions.

  Data centres currently account for a tenth of Malaysia's total energy consumption. The government projects that share could climb to one-third by 2035 — a figure that Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim cited on Monday in a social media post calling for closer scrutiny of infrastructure readiness, energy supply, and resource-use efficiency.

  ## Investors to fund grid upgrades; power to be unsubsidised

  The government intends to require data centre operators to pay for the electricity grid upgrades their facilities demand. Minister of Investment, Trade and Industry Datuk Seri Johari Abdul Ghani told Parliament last week that power supplied to data centres would be unsubsidised and ring-fenced from the energy infrastructure serving domestic households and other industries — a measure the government said is intended to protect generation capacity for the broader economy.

  A government task force on data centres has set a local content requirement: at least 30% of a data centre's components must be sourced domestically, with mandated participation across construction, engineering services, cooling systems, and electrical equipment.

  ## Scale of approved investment

  Up to July, the government had approved 27 data centre projects with a combined estimated value of RM225.97 billion (Malaysian ringgit). The Economy Ministry said 89.21% of high-skilled positions at those projects are to be filled by Malaysians.

  ## Circular economy and economic resilience also under review

  The data centre bill was one of three strategic priorities reviewed by MTEN, a government economic council, alongside a Circular Economy Framework and the National Economic Resilience Framework (NERF).

  The Circular Economy Framework is designed to move Malaysia away from a linear "take-make-dispose" model toward one that extends the useful life of materials and reduces waste. The Economy Ministry said the Housing and Local Government; Investment, Trade and Industry; Science, Technology and Innovation; and Human Resources Ministries will serve as lead agencies implementing the framework, which is also tied to Malaysia's target of achieving net-zero emissions by 2050. The framework is also intended to address what the ministry described as fragmented implementation across government agencies and prevent economic losses from resource wastage.

  MTEN reviewed the NERF — designed to strengthen Malaysia's ability to absorb external economic shocks — and agreed that further deliberations are needed before any decisions are made, the ministry said.

  ## What comes next

  The legislation, if enacted, would mark Malaysia's first statutory framework specifically governing data centre development, establishing binding rules on energy sourcing, local procurement, and grid cost allocation. The bill is expected to reach the cabinet in 2025.

  ## 馬來西亞擬立法規範資料中心產業　能源占比恐達全國三分之一

  馬來西亞政府正式宣布著手草擬專項法規，規範資料中心產業發展。經濟部週一發表聲明，指出隨著產業快速擴張，全國電力與水資源所承受的壓力與日俱增，預計至2035年，資料中心用電量將達全國總用電量的三分之一。

  《數位基礎建設（資料中心）法案》預計明年提交內閣審議。該法案旨在解決環境失衡問題，確保資料中心投資惠及馬來西亞整體數位生態系統，同時支持該國的人工智慧發展目標。

  目前，資料中心約占馬來西亞總能源消耗的十分之一。政府預測，此一比例至2035年可能攀升至三分之一。首相拿督斯里安華伊布拉欣週一於社群媒體發文，引用上述數據，呼籲各界更密切關注基礎建設準備程度、電力供應及資源使用效率。

  ## 投資方須自行負擔電網升級費用；供電取消補貼

  政府擬要求資料中心業者自行承擔其設施所需的電網升級費用。投資、貿易及工業部長拿督斯里佐哈里阿都加尼上週在國會表示，供應給資料中心的電力將不享有補貼，並與服務一般家庭及其他產業的能源基礎建設分開管理。政府表示，此舉旨在保護整體經濟的發電容量。

  政府資料中心工作小組已訂定在地採購要求：資料中心至少30%的設備元件須從國內採購，建設工程、工程服務、冷卻系統及電氣設備等領域均須有本地業者參與。

  ## 已核准投資規模

  截至今年7月，政府已核准27項資料中心建設計畫，合計估計投資總額達2,259.7億馬來西亞令吉。經濟部表示，上述計畫中，89.21%的高技能職位將由馬來西亞本國人才擔任。

  ## 循環經濟與經濟韌性亦納入審議

  資料中心法案是政府經濟理事會MTEN此次審議的三大戰略優先項目之一，另外兩項分別為《循環經濟框架》及《國家經濟韌性框架》（NERF）。

  《循環經濟框架》旨在引導馬來西亞跳脫「取用—製造—棄置」的線性生產模式，轉型為延長材料使用壽命、減少廢棄物的循環型經濟模式。經濟部表示，房屋及地方政府部、投資貿易及工業部、科學技術及創新部，以及人力資源部將擔任落實該框架的主責機關。此框架亦與馬來西亞2050年達成淨零排放的目標相互扣連，同時也旨在解決各政府機關施政分散的問題，防止因資源浪費造成的經濟損失。

  MTEN亦就《國家經濟韌性框架》進行審議——該框架旨在強化馬來西亞抵禦外部經濟衝擊的能力——並同意在作出任何決定之前，仍需進一步深入討論。

  ## 後續進展

  一旦完成立法，這將是馬來西亞首部專門規範資料中心產業發展的法定框架，就能源取得、本地採購及電網成本分擔訂立具約束力的規定。該法案預計於2025年提交內閣審議。
