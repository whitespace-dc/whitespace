body_en: |
  AI governance company Veranthios has partnered with PT Digivla Indonesia, headquartered in South Jakarta, to bring continuous AI risk monitoring technology to the Indonesian market, targeting businesses that need to demonstrate regulatory compliance on demand as Indonesia's data protection regime matures.

  PT Digivla Indonesia President Director Reza Maulana said in an official statement on July 28 that Indonesia's regulatory direction has become clearer, shifting the compliance burden from understanding rules to proving adherence at any moment. "The challenge is no longer understanding what the rules require, but proving, whenever asked, that a company's AI systems continuously operate within those boundaries," Maulana said. "Many organizations still lack comprehensive visibility over their AI use, especially as agentic AI — systems that act autonomously to complete multi-step tasks — gains wider adoption."

  PT Digivla Indonesia said the partnership enables Indonesian companies to implement AI governance with verifiable evidence, strengthening compliance, reducing operational risk, and accelerating responsible AI adoption.

  ## Indonesia's 72-hour reporting deadline

  The partnership targets compliance with Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law, known by its Indonesian abbreviation UU PDP (Undang-Undang Perlindungan Data Pribadi), which requires companies to report certain incidents within 72 hours. Veranthios's platform allows businesses to assemble compliance evidence packages well before that deadline rather than reconstruct system behavior after a regulatory inquiry begins.

  ## How the monitoring platform works

  Veranthios's platform monitors AI risk on a continuous basis, detecting behavioral drift — changes in how an AI system behaves over time — in real time. The system tracks activity while AI systems run, monitors data access, and records connections made by AI agents. When behavior, data access, or agent connections move outside predefined limits, the platform automatically flags and documents the deviation.

  Automated guardrails apply risk assessments continuously, the company said, so compliance documentation accumulates through normal system operations rather than being assembled separately only when regulators request it.

  ## Single evidence package across Asia-Pacific

  Veranthios said its platform produces a single compliance evidence package designed to satisfy regulatory requirements across multiple Asia-Pacific jurisdictions. Companies operating across the region would otherwise need to prepare separate compliance documentation for each country's regulator, the company said.

  The agreement with Digivla is intended to give Indonesian companies a documented, auditable record of AI system behavior available on demand if regulators request it, with Maulana framing the arrangement as a way to accelerate responsible AI adoption in Indonesia as the country's AI regulatory requirements become more defined.

  ## Veranthios與Digivla攜手 布局印尼AI合規監控市場

  AI治理公司Veranthios與總部位於南雅加達的PT Digivla Indonesia達成合作協議，計畫將持續性AI風險監控技術推向印尼市場。隨著印尼個人資料保護法規逐步完善，雙方合作鎖定的目標客群，正是那些需要隨時向主管機關證明合規狀態的企業。

  PT Digivla Indonesia總裁Reza Maulana於7月28日在官方聲明中指出，印尼的法規方向已愈趨明朗，合規的挑戰已從「理解法規要求」轉變為「隨時舉證符合規定」。「現在的難題不再是搞清楚法規要求什麼，而是無論何時被問到，企業都必須能夠證明其AI系統持續在規範範圍內運行，」Maulana表示。「許多組織對自身的AI使用狀況仍缺乏全面的掌握，尤其是在自主式AI——也就是能夠自主完成多步驟任務的系統——日益普及的情況下，這個問題更加突出。」

  PT Digivla Indonesia表示，這項合作讓印尼企業得以透過可查核的證據落實AI治理，進而強化合規能力、降低營運風險，並加速負責任的AI應用推展。

  ## 72小時通報期限的合規壓力

  此次合作的核心目標，在於協助企業遵循印尼《個人資料保護法》（印尼文縮寫為UU PDP，即Undang-Undang Perlindungan Data Pribadi）的相關規定。該法要求企業須在特定事件發生後72小時內完成通報。Veranthios的平台能讓企業在期限屆至之前，事先備妥完整的合規佐證文件，而非等到主管機關展開調查後，才倉促重建系統行為紀錄。

  ## 監控平台運作方式

  Veranthios的平台以持續監控的方式追蹤AI風險，能夠即時偵測「行為飄移」——也就是AI系統行為隨時間演變所產生的偏差。系統在AI運行期間全程追蹤操作活動、監控資料存取情形，並記錄AI代理程式所建立的各項連線。一旦行為模式、資料存取或代理連線超出預設範圍，平台便會自動標記並記錄該偏差事件。

  該公司表示，自動化防護機制會持續套用風險評估，因此合規文件會在系統日常運作過程中自然累積，而不必等到主管機關要求時才另行整理。

  ## 單一證據包涵蓋亞太多個司法管轄區

  Veranthios表示，其平台可產出單一合規佐證文件包，設計上可同時滿足亞太地區多個司法管轄區的法規要求。若沒有這樣的解決方案，跨國營運的企業原本必須針對每個國家的主管機關，分別備妥一套合規文件。

  與Digivla達成的這項協議，旨在讓印尼企業擁有一份有據可查、可供稽核的AI系統行為紀錄，隨時可在主管機關要求時提出。Maulana將此次合作定位為一種方式，藉此在印尼AI法規要求日趨明確之際，加速推動負責任的AI應用發展。
