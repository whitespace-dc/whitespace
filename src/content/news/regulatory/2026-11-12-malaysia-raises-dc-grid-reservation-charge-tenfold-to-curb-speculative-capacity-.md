body_en: |
  Malaysia has increased its connected load charge for data centres more than tenfold — from RM8.50 to RM89.64 per kilowatt — as the government moves to end what it calls speculative grid reservations that threaten household electricity supplies and national energy security.

  Investment, Trade and Industry Minister Datuk Seri Johari Abdul Ghani announced the measure at the Bursa Malaysia Sectoral Series conference in Kuala Lumpur on Tuesday, an event organised in collaboration with Macquarie and themed "Powering the AI Era – Watt Next?"

  The connected load charge is a fee levied on the maximum power capacity a data centre operator reserves from the national grid, regardless of whether that capacity is actually used. By raising it more than tenfold, the government is imposing a direct financial penalty on operators who declare large power requirements but leave grid capacity idle — a practice Johari described as creating "phantom demand."

  "Tenaga Nasional Bhd [Malaysia's national utility] would have already planned its infrastructure and allocated capacity to accommodate full declarations, leaving unused megawatt capacity that could otherwise support other users," Johari said at the conference. "The revised fee ensures operators face strict financial accountability for contracted capacity."

  ## Scale of the data centre build-out

  The policy tightening comes against a backdrop of explosive growth. Data centre investments have risen from 0.4 percent of total approved investments — RM1.25 billion — in 2021 to 43.9 percent, or RM95.8 billion, in the first half of financial year 2026 alone, according to Johari.

  Malaysia has approved 117 data centres with a combined maximum energy demand of approximately 23,500 megawatts (MW) — enough to power a mid-sized country. Of these, 55 are operational, 19 are under construction, 16 are approved but have not yet broken ground, and 27 have been cleared to operate on off-grid energy supply. A further 24 applications remain stalled in the approvals pipeline pending checks on power and water availability.

  To put the grid pressure in context, Bursa Malaysia chairman Tan Sri Abdul Farid Alias noted that data centres currently account for around 7 percent of Malaysia's electricity consumption and are projected to reach 31 percent by 2035 — meaning close to a third of the country's electricity could flow to a single segment of the economy within a decade.

  ## Ring-fencing households and legacy industry

  Beyond the charge increase, the government has separated data centre electricity planning and management from that of households and traditional industrial users. Johari said ordinary citizens and established businesses will not directly or indirectly subsidise data centre infrastructure costs.

  A dedicated ultra-high-voltage tariff category has been created for data centre operators, requiring them to fully bear the cost of transmission upgrades needed to connect their facilities to the grid. Johari noted that governments dealing with large data centre influxes in the United States, the European Union, and Ireland have adopted comparable stances to protect household and small-business ratepayers.

  ## Off-grid push and Petronas opportunity

  With global turbine shortages slowing conventional power generation projects, the Energy Commission is fast-tracking a framework for self-generation facilities, Johari said. Data centre developers are being encouraged to pursue dedicated off-grid options, including gas-fired generation, solar, hydropower, and battery energy storage systems (BESS — systems that store electricity when supply exceeds demand and release it during shortfalls).

  Johari identified a commercial opportunity for Petroliam Nasional Bhd (Petronas), the national oil and gas company, in serving dedicated data centre demand. Malaysia currently exports roughly 30 million tonnes of liquefied natural gas (LNG) per year, a volume he said translates in theory to roughly 31,000 MW of electricity generation capacity. Dedicated data centre demand could create a commercially attractive domestic market for that gas, he said, provided it is priced on market terms without subsidy.

  On water and carbon efficiency, Johari said the Data Centre Task Force is tightening performance metrics covering power usage effectiveness (PUE — the ratio of total facility energy to IT equipment energy, where lower is better), water usage effectiveness (WUE), and carbon usage effectiveness (CUE). He cited advanced air-cooling systems adopted by EdgeConneX as an example of engineering approaches that eliminate continuous water consumption, arguing that resource constraints should push investors toward higher efficiency rather than heavier extraction.

  ## Significance for the region

  Malaysia's measures represent one of the most explicit policy frameworks in Southeast Asia for managing the intersection of a data centre investment boom with domestic energy security. The government is simultaneously trying to maintain Malaysia's attractiveness to hyperscale and AI infrastructure investment — the RM95.8 billion in first-half 2026 approvals signals continued demand — while preventing grid strain from translating into higher costs or supply risk for the broader economy.

  The forward-looking constraint is power supply itself. Twenty-four data centre applications remain in the pipeline, held up by power and water availability reviews, and the government has indicated that project timelines will be governed by realistic generation schedules rather than capacity commitments that cannot be delivered.
body_zh: |
  馬來西亞將資料中心的連結負載費（connected load charge）調漲逾十倍——從每千瓦8.50令吉提高至89.64令吉——政府此舉旨在終結所謂「投機性電網容量預留」行為。當局表示，這類行為已威脅到民眾供電穩定與國家能源安全。

  投資、貿易及工業部長拿督斯里佐哈里·阿都加尼（Datuk Seri Johari Abdul Ghani）於週二在吉隆坡舉辦的馬來西亞交易所（Bursa Malaysia）行業系列論壇上宣布這項措施，該論壇由麥格理（Macquarie）協辦，主題為「為AI時代供電——下一步是什麼？（Powering the AI Era – Watt Next?）」。

  連結負載費是對資料中心業者向國家電網預留的最大供電容量所徵收的費用，無論該容量是否實際使用皆須繳納。費率調漲逾十倍後，政府對那些宣告大量用電需求、卻讓電網容量閒置的業者施加直接財務懲罰——佐哈里將這種行為稱為製造「幽靈需求」（phantom demand）。

  「馬來西亞國家能源公司（Tenaga Nasional Bhd）早已依據業者的完整申報進行基礎設施規劃並分配容量，結果卻留下本可供其他用戶使用的閒置百萬瓦容量，」佐哈里在論壇上說。「修訂後的費率確保業者對已簽約容量承擔嚴格的財務責任。」

  ## 資料中心建設規模

  這項政策緊縮措施的背景，是馬來西亞資料中心投資的爆炸性成長。根據佐哈里的數據，資料中心投資占總核准投資的比例，已從2021年的0.4%（約12.5億令吉）暴增至2026財年上半年的43.9%（約958億令吉）。

  馬來西亞目前已核准117座資料中心，合計最大能源需求約23,500 MW——足以為一個中型國家供電。其中55座已投入營運，19座正在興建，16座已獲核准但尚未動工，27座已獲准採用離網能源供電。另有24件申請仍因電力與水源供應審查，卡關在審批程序中。

  就電網壓力的規模而言，馬來西亞交易所主席丹斯里阿都法立·阿里亞斯（Tan Sri Abdul Farid Alias）指出，資料中心目前約佔馬來西亞電力消耗的7%，預計到2035年將達31%——意即在未來十年內，近三分之一的國家電力可能流向單一產業。

  ## 保護民眾與傳統產業用電

  除費率調漲外，政府已將資料中心的電力規劃與管理，與住宅及傳統工業用電分離，分開處理。佐哈里表示，一般民眾與傳統企業，不會直接或間接補貼資料中心的基礎設施成本。

  政府已為資料中心業者設立專屬的超高壓電費類別，要求業者全額承擔將設施連接至電網所需的輸電升級費用。佐哈里指出，面對大量資料中心湧入，美國、歐盟與愛爾蘭等政府均已採取類似立場，以保護住宅用戶與中小企業業主的用電權益。

  ## 推動離網發電與國油（Petronas）商機

  由於全球渦輪機供應短缺，傳統發電計畫進展遲緩，能源委員會正在加速推動自發電設施框架，佐哈里說。資料中心開發商被鼓勵採用專屬離網方案，包括天然氣發電、太陽能、水力發電，以及電池儲能系統（BESS——在供電超出需求時儲存電力、於供電不足時釋放的系統）。

  佐哈里點出馬來西亞國家石油公司（Petroliam Nasional Bhd，Petronas，以下簡稱國油）在服務資料中心專屬需求方面的商業機遇。馬來西亞目前每年約出口3,000萬噸液化天然氣（LNG），他表示這個數量理論上相當於約31,000 MW的發電容量。只要以市場價格而非補貼方式定價，資料中心的專屬需求可為這些天然氣創造具吸引力的國內市場，他說。

  在水資源與碳效率方面，佐哈里表示，資料中心特別工作小組正在收緊績效指標，涵蓋電源使用效率（PUE——設施總用電量與IT設備用電量之比，數值越低越好）、用水效率（WUE）及碳排效率（CUE）。他以EdgeConneX採用的先進氣冷系統為例，說明可完全消除持續用水需求的工程解決方案，並主張資源限制應促使投資者追求更高效率，而非更大量的資源消耗。

  ## 對區域的政策意義

  馬來西亞這套措施，是東南亞最明確的政策框架之一，專門處理資料中心投資熱潮與國內能源安全交叉的議題。政府一方面試圖維持馬來西亞對超大規模（hyperscale）及AI基礎設施投資的吸引力——2026年上半年高達958億令吉的核准投資額，顯示需求仍然強勁——同時也致力防止電網壓力轉化為更高的電費負擔或供電風險，殃及整體經濟。

  當前最關鍵的制約因素是電力供應本身。仍有24件資料中心申請滯留在審批管線，等候電力與水源供應審查，政府已表明，專案時程將以實際可行的發電計畫為準，而非以無法兌現的容量承諾為基礎。
