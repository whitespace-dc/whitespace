  ## DoE refers non-compliant diesel plants to regulator

  The Philippine Department of Energy (DoE) is preparing to recommend the suspension of approximately 40 diesel power plants to the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC), the country's independent power sector regulator, after those facilities failed to act on official show-cause orders.

  "We've given them a warning. And we are now in the process of recommending suspension to the ERC for proper action," Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin said on the sidelines of a site visit last week.

  Garin noted that around 10 of the affected diesel plants are not currently operational.

  ## Background: show-cause campaign and response rate

  The suspension referrals stem from a broader DoE enforcement campaign launched last month, when the department sent show-cause orders — formal demands requiring companies to explain why regulatory action should not be taken against them — to more than 200 generation companies operating across both on-grid and off-grid areas of the country. Slightly more than half of those companies responded. The roughly 40 plants now facing suspension referrals are among those that did not.

  "We've been meeting since last week on what to do. So, the decision is to send it to ERC for proper action," Garin said.

  ## New rules would set legal timelines and penalty tiers

  The enforcement push accompanies a DoE circular and a separate rulemaking effort designed to strengthen the regulatory framework governing generation companies. The DoE said the circular "seeks to ensure the timely submission of complete, accurate, and transparent reports, promote due process and regulatory consistency in enforcing reporting obligations, and support the DoE's mandate to uphold the reliability, adequacy, security, and resilience of the electric power industry."

  Under the proposed rules currently being drafted, the DoE would impose a 72-hour email response window for show-cause orders and establish a tiered, three-strikes system of administrative sanctions.

  The penalty structure differs depending on the category of violation. For reporting violations, a first offense results in a warning and strict compliance monitoring. For more serious technical and generation standard failures, however, a first offense immediately triggers a referral to the ERC and an order to submit a corrective action plan — bypassing the initial warning stage.

  A second offense of any kind would result in an ERC referral and suspension of the firm's certificate of endorsement (CoE), a credential that determines a generator's eligibility to participate in key programs. A third offense would bring permanent CoE revocation, blacklisting from those programs, and a direct DoE endorsement to the ERC to suspend or revoke the plant's certificate of compliance, the operating authorization required to generate and sell power.

  ## What comes next

  The DoE's formal recommendation to the ERC will place the suspension decision in the hands of the commission, which has statutory authority over generation company operating certificates. The department is still finalizing the draft rules that would codify the 72-hour response window and three-strikes framework into binding regulation.

  ## 能源部將不合規柴油電廠移送監管機構

  菲律賓能源部（DoE）正準備向獨立電力監管機構——能源監管委員會（ERC）——建議暫停約40座柴油發電廠的營運，原因是這些設施未依規定回應官方發出的限期說明令。

  能源部長Sharon S. Garin上週在一場現場視察場合受訪時表示：「我們已給予警告，目前正在進行程序，準備向ERC建議暫停，以便採取適當行動。」

  Garin指出，在受影響的柴油電廠中，約有10座目前並未實際運轉。

  ## 背景：限期說明令推進情形與業者回應率

  此次移送暫停的動作，源自能源部上個月啟動的一項大規模執法行動。當時，能源部向全國逾200家分布於離網與並網地區的發電公司發出限期說明令——即要求業者正式說明為何不應對其採取監管行動的文件。最終約有略超過一半的業者提出回應，而目前面臨暫停移送的約40座電廠，正是其中未做任何回應的業者。

  Garin表示：「我們從上週就開始開會討論如何處置，最後決定移送ERC採取適當行動。」

  ## 新規將訂定法定時限與分級懲處制度

  此波執法行動同步配合能源部的一項行政通函，以及一項旨在強化發電公司監管框架的獨立立規程序。能源部表示，該行政通函「旨在確保相關報告能及時、完整、正確且透明地提交，並在執行申報義務時促進正當程序與監管一致性，進而支持能源部維護電力工業可靠性、充裕性、安全性與韌性的職責。」

  依據目前草擬中的擬議規則，能源部將要求業者在收到限期說明令後72小時內以電子郵件回應，並建立三振分級行政制裁制度。

  懲處結構依違規類別而有所不同。就申報違規而言，初犯結果為書面警告並施以嚴格的合規監控；然而，對於較嚴重的技術與發電標準違規，初犯即直接移送ERC，並命令業者提交矯正行動計畫，跳過初期警告階段。

  任何類別的二犯，均須移送ERC，並暫停業者的背書證書（CoE）——此證書決定發電業者是否具備參與關鍵計畫的資格。三犯則將面臨永久撤銷CoE、從相關計畫中列入黑名單，以及由能源部直接向ERC背書，要求暫停或撤銷其合規證書（即從事發電及售電所需的營運授權）。

  ## 後續發展

  能源部正式向ERC提出建議後，暫停與否的決定權將移交至依法擁有發電公司營運證書管轄權的ERC手中。能源部目前仍在完善草案規則，待定案後，72小時回應時限及三振制度將正式納入具約束力的法規之中。
