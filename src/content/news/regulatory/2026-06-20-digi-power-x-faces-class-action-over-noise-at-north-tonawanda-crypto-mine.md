body_en: |
  ## Lawsuit targets North Tonawanda crypto facility

  Digi Power X, a data center and energy infrastructure developer, has been hit with a proposed class action lawsuit alleging that its cryptocurrency mining facility in North Tonawanda, New York has subjected nearby residents to persistent industrial noise and low-frequency vibrations. The complaint was filed in the US District Court for the Western District of New York on behalf of named plaintiffs Sharon Demers, Karen Hance, and Mark Polito, along with other area residents.

  The suit centers on Digi Power X's operations at 1070 Erie Avenue, where cooling fans and other mining equipment run continuously. According to the complaint, that uninterrupted operation has caused residents "substantial annoyance, emotional distress, loss of comfort and enjoyment, diminution in the value of their property and an increased risk of adverse health effects."

  Plaintiffs are seeking monetary damages, injunctive relief — a court order requiring the company to abate the nuisance — punitive damages, and the establishment of a medical monitoring program for residents allegedly exposed to chronic noise.

  ## Four years of complaints

  Residents near the Erie Avenue facility have been raising noise concerns for roughly four years, according to broadcaster WKBW. Neighbor John Helmer told the outlet: "It's very loud, and it wakes my wife up at night. It's every day. It's every night. It doesn't stop."

  Digi Power X installed a 26-foot acoustic wall around the site in 2023. Plaintiffs allege the barrier has not resolved the noise problem.

  ## Site history and current operations

  The North Tonawanda facility is a former Fortistar gas-fired peaker plant — a type of power station designed to run only during periods of peak electricity demand. The complaint alleges that before Digi Power X acquired the property, the Fortistar plant operated only intermittently. Following the acquisition, the company used the site to support around-the-clock cryptocurrency mining, with industrial fans running continuously to cool the mining hardware.

  Digi Power X, formerly known as Digihost Technology, describes the North Tonawanda site in its most recent annual filing as a 60-megawatt (MW) gas-fired power plant that continues to function as a peaker plant, supplying electricity to the grid during demand peaks. The company reports 196.7 MW of total available power capacity across its infrastructure, with 141.7 MW located across its New York sites. It also operates facilities in Buffalo, New York, and Columbiana, Alabama.

  ## Regulatory and legal backdrop

  The class action adds to existing legal and regulatory scrutiny of the facility. In May 2026, the New York State Appellate Division ruled that the state Public Service Commission (PSC) — the body that regulates utilities and energy infrastructure — must examine the emissions impacts of the 2022 sale of the Fortistar plant to Digihost under New York's Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA), the state's sweeping climate law.

  ## Pivot plans clouded by moratorium

  Digi Power X has stated plans to transition the North Tonawanda site away from cryptocurrency mining toward high-performance computing (HPC) — the dense server infrastructure used for artificial intelligence workloads — and has reportedly already removed some mining hardware from the site. Those plans have been complicated by a local data center moratorium, which limits new or expanded data center development in the area.

  The combination of the class action lawsuit, the appellate court's emissions directive, and the local moratorium leaves the company's strategy for the North Tonawanda site facing simultaneous legal, regulatory, and municipal constraints.

  ## 訴訟矛頭指向北托納萬達加密礦場

  資料中心暨能源基礎設施開發商Digi Power X，遭到一宗集體訴訟纏身。訴狀主張該公司設於紐約州北托納萬達的加密貨幣挖礦設施，使附近居民長期承受持續不斷的工業噪音與低頻振動。此案由Sharon Demers、Karen Hance及Mark Polito三名具名原告代表周邊居民，向美國聯邦紐約西區地方法院提出訴訟。

  訴訟核心指向Digi Power X位於Erie大道1070號的廠區——冷卻風扇及其他挖礦設備在此全天候不間斷運轉。訴狀指稱，這種連續運作已使居民承受「嚴重的困擾、精神痛苦、舒適與生活享受之損失、財產價值減損，以及健康受到不良影響的風險上升」。

  原告要求法院判予損害賠償、禁制令救濟（即命令該公司排除妨害）、懲罰性賠償，並要求為長期暴露於噪音中的居民設立醫療監測計畫。

  ## 四年來的投訴不斷

  據廣播媒體WKBW報導，Erie大道廠區附近的居民就噪音問題提出抗議，至今已長達約四年。居民John Helmer向媒體表示：「噪音非常大，害我太太半夜睡不著。這是每天、每晚都在發生的事，從來沒有停過。」

  Digi Power X曾於2023年在廠區四周架設一道約7.9公尺（26英尺）高的隔音牆。然而原告主張，這道隔音屏障並未解決噪音問題。

  ## 廠址沿革與現有營運

  北托納萬達這座廠址，前身為Fortistar天然氣調峰電廠——這類電廠的設計初衷，是僅在電力需求高峰期間短暫投入運轉。訴狀指稱，在Digi Power X收購該廠址之前，Fortistar電廠只是間歇性運作。收購後，該公司將廠址用於全天候加密貨幣挖礦，工業風扇持續運轉以冷卻挖礦硬體設備。

  Digi Power X前身為Digihost Technology，在其最新年度報告中，將北托納萬達廠址描述為一座60百萬瓦（MW）的天然氣發電廠，持續以調峰電廠形式運作，在電力需求高峰期間向電網供電。該公司旗下基礎設施的總可用電力容量為196.7 MW，其中141.7 MW分布於其紐約州各廠址。此外，該公司亦在紐約州水牛城及阿拉巴馬州科倫比亞納設有廠區。

  ## 法規與法律背景

  這起集體訴訟，是該廠址現有法律與監管壓力的最新添加。2026年5月，紐約州上訴法院裁定，負責監管公用事業與能源基礎設施的州公共服務委員會（PSC），必須依據紐約州《氣候領導與社區保護法》（CLCPA）——即該州影響深遠的氣候法——審查2022年Fortistar電廠出售予Digihost一事所產生的排放影響。

  ## 轉型計畫蒙上陰影

  Digi Power X已表明計畫將北托納萬達廠址從加密貨幣挖礦轉型為高效能運算（HPC）用途——即用於人工智慧工作負載的高密度伺服器基礎設施，據悉已陸續將部分挖礦硬體設備自廠區移除。然而，當地針對資料中心的暫停令限制了該地區新建或擴建資料中心的發展，使上述轉型計畫蒙上陰影。

  集體訴訟、上訴法院的排放審查指令，以及當地暫停令三管齊下，令Digi Power X針對北托納萬達廠址的策略，同時面臨法律、監管與地方政府的三重制約。
