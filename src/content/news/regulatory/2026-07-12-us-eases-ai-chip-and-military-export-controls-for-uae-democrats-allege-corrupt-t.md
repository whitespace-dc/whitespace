body_en: |
  The US Commerce Department has formally eased export controls on the United Arab Emirates, enabling Nvidia artificial intelligence chips, military equipment, commercial satellites, and spacecraft to reach the Gulf state more freely under rules set to take effect July 14, 2026. The decision is intended to deepen bilateral ties and reflects the UAE's expanding role in Washington's strategy toward Iran, but it has drawn sharp domestic criticism from Democratic lawmakers who allege the policy is inseparable from former President Donald Trump's cryptocurrency earnings.

  ## What the New Rules Allow

  A 17-page rule published in the Federal Register commits the Commerce Department to give "favorable review" to export license applications involving MGX, a UAE state-linked investment fund. The rules also extend specific license exemptions for advanced computing equipment to UAE technology company G42 and its cloud subsidiary Core42. US technology companies including Amazon, Apple, Alphabet, Meta, Microsoft, Oracle, and xAI will receive simplified export review treatment for controlled equipment when participating in UAE data center projects.

  ## Warren's Corruption Allegations

  Senator Elizabeth Warren issued a formal statement condemning the policy as a "corrupt" deal, pointing to MGX's prior financial relationship with the Trump family. MGX used USD1, a stablecoin linked to the Trump family, in a $2 billion investment in cryptocurrency exchange Binance. Warren stated that Trump earned $263 million through that arrangement, which she described as part of $1.4 billion in total cryptocurrency-related earnings he received in 2025. Warren has formally requested Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and Undersecretary Jeffrey Kessler to testify before the Senate Banking Committee to explain the policy's decision-making process.

  ## National Security Concerns Over G42

  The relaxation of restrictions on G42 has revived unresolved national security concerns. In June 2026, Senate Democrats warned that despite G42's prior commitments to cut ties with China, US intelligence officials had found the company transferred US chip technology in ways that aided the development of Chinese missile systems. The new rules extend license exemptions to G42 and its subsidiary Core42 despite those findings.

  ## Broader US-UAE Financial Ties

  The export easing accompanies a parallel expansion of financial relations between Washington and Abu Dhabi. As of May 2026, the UAE was seeking a currency swap line — an agreement allowing two central banks to exchange currencies to support trade and financial stability — with the United States as part of a broader bilateral financial cooperation initiative.

  The export policy is expected to face sustained congressional scrutiny, with Warren's requests for testimony from Lutnick and Kessler before the Senate Banking Committee representing the most immediate formal oversight action on record.
body_zh: |
  美國商務部正式放寬對阿拉伯聯合大公國（阿聯酋）的出口管制，依據2026年7月14日起生效的新規定，輝達人工智慧晶片、軍事設備、商業衛星及太空載具將能更順暢地進入這個波斯灣國家。此決定旨在深化雙邊關係，也反映出阿聯酋在華盛頓對伊朗戰略中日益吃重的角色；然而，這項政策已遭民主黨籍國會議員強烈批評，他們指控此舉與前總統唐納·川普的加密貨幣收益密不可分。

  ## 新規定開放的範圍

  刊登於《聯邦公報》的一份長達17頁的規定，要求商務部對涉及MGX——一個與阿聯酋政府有關聯的投資基金——的出口許可申請給予「優先審查」。新規亦將先進運算設備的特定許可豁免，延伸至阿聯酋科技公司G42及其雲端子公司Core42。亞馬遜、蘋果、Alphabet、Meta、微軟、甲骨文及xAI等美國科技公司，在參與阿聯酋資料中心建設專案時，將享有受管制設備的簡化出口審查待遇。

  ## 華倫的貪腐指控

  參議員伊麗莎白·華倫發表正式聲明，痛批此政策是一樁「腐敗」交易，並點出MGX與川普家族先前的財務往來。MGX曾使用與川普家族相關的穩定幣USD1，對加密貨幣交易所幣安（Binance）進行一筆20億美元的投資。華倫指出，川普透過這項安排獲得了2億6,300萬美元，並稱這是他2025年合計收取的14億美元加密貨幣相關收益的一部分。華倫已正式要求商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克（Howard Lutnick）與副部長傑弗里·凱斯勒（Jeffrey Kessler）出席參議院銀行委員會聽證，就政策決策過程作出說明。

  ## G42引發的國家安全疑慮

  放寬對G42限制的決定，使懸而未決的國家安全疑慮再度浮上檯面。2026年6月，民主黨籍參議員曾警告，儘管G42此前承諾切斷與中國的業務往來，美國情報官員仍發現該公司以有助於中國飛彈系統研發的方式，轉移了美國晶片技術。新規定在上述發現仍未獲解決的情況下，依然將許可豁免延伸至G42及其子公司Core42。

  ## 美阿更廣泛的金融往來

  此次出口管制鬆綁，伴隨著華盛頓與阿布達比之間金融關係的同步擴展。截至2026年5月，阿聯酋正就建立貨幣互換額度——一種允許兩國央行互相交換貨幣、以支撐貿易與金融穩定的協議——與美國展開協商，作為雙邊更廣泛金融合作計畫的一環。

  這項出口政策預料將持續面臨國會的嚴格審查，華倫要求盧特尼克與凱斯勒出席參議院銀行委員會作證的請求，目前是有據可查的最即時正式監督行動。
