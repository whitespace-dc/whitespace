body_en: |
  Texas Gov. Greg Abbott on Monday ordered state utility regulators to conduct a comprehensive audit of all data center projects seeking connection to the Texas grid, directing that any project failing to meet state law or regulatory requirements be denied interconnection — a move that immediately halted a key transmission planning study and raised the evidentiary bar for developers across one of the nation's largest AI infrastructure markets.

  Abbott's directive, addressed to the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) — the state body that regulates electric utilities — and the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT), the independent grid operator serving most of the state, orders a "comprehensive verification and audit" of every data center interconnection request in ERCOT's queue. ERCOT said it will work with the PUCT to implement the order, including an immediate postponement of its Batch Zero transmission planning study, a process that evaluates the largest proposed loads before incorporating them into long-term grid planning.

  "Our top priority is to protect Texans' safety and quality of life," Abbott said in a statement. "Any project that fails to comply with the requirements set forth by the PUCT and ERCOT, and by state law, must be denied connection to the Texas grid. Simply put, Texans must come first."

  PUCT Chairman Thomas Gleeson endorsed the directive, saying the commission would continue working with ERCOT to carry out Abbott's order alongside recommendations the agency outlined in a July 17 letter to the governor. "We appreciate Gov. Abbott's directive and will continue to work closely with ERCOT to ensure these requirements, along with those laid out in our July 17, 2026 letter to Gov. Abbott, are fully carried out on behalf of the people of Texas," Gleeson said.

  ## Why Texas acted

  Texas has attracted hyperscale data center projects — facilities run by the largest cloud and technology companies — that together represent hundreds of gigawatts of proposed new electric demand, a figure that far exceeds the state's current peak load. State leaders have expressed concern that speculative proposals are distorting long-term grid planning and could drive unnecessary and costly infrastructure investment if grid operators are forced to plan transmission and generation capacity around projects that may never be built or operated at projected scale.

  Even before Monday's directive, ERCOT's Batch Zero process applied maturity criteria to the largest proposed loads before folding them into long-term studies. Abbott's order expands that logic across the full interconnection queue and makes broader verification a prerequisite for continued advancement through the process.

  ## What developers may be required to prove

  Energy analyst Neil Osnato, founder of Persistence Analytics Group, said the directive signals a fundamental shift in how Texas evaluates proposed demand, not simply an effort to trim an overfilled queue. "Texas is not simply cleaning up an oversized queue. It is redefining what counts as credible demand," Osnato said.

  According to Osnato, the order moves Texas away from a conventional interconnection model — in which submitted requests advance through sequential engineering studies — toward an evidence-based approach that requires developers to demonstrate project maturity before grid planners commit resources around them. That would mean showing not just that a project has requested service, but that it has secured financing, site control, permitting, equipment, construction plans, power and water availability, and a realistic path to long-term commercial operation.

  "A place in the queue is not proof that the grid should build around the request," Osnato said.

  For developers, that translates into a higher evidentiary burden before grid planners commit transmission and generation resources on their behalf. Projects with advanced financing and site control may benefit if the audit removes speculative competitors from contention for grid capacity. Less mature projects face additional scrutiny before moving forward.

  ## A regulatory shift taking shape nationally

  Texas is not acting in isolation. PJM, the grid operator covering 13 Mid-Atlantic and Midwest states, has approved reforms that separate physical interconnection from firm resource adequacy for large new loads, while also tightening project viability requirements elsewhere in its interconnection process. In Virginia, regulators are debating whether hyperscale customers should bear a greater share of the transmission costs associated with serving their facilities.

  Osnato described these as different responses to the same underlying challenge facing grid operators across the country. "Large-load planning can no longer rely on forecast magnitude alone. It must test probability, timing, deliverability, durability and cost responsibility," he said.

  ## What remains unresolved

  Abbott's directive leaves several operational questions unanswered. It does not specify how long the audit will take, how the PUCT and ERCOT will divide responsibility for carrying out the review, what specific information developers will be required to submit, or exactly how projects already moving through the interconnection process will be affected during the audit period.

  Those details will determine whether the initiative functions as a targeted validation of the state's largest proposed loads or becomes a broader procedural checkpoint affecting AI development timelines across the queue. ERCOT's postponement of the Batch Zero transmission planning study indicates that at least some projects already in the pipeline face delays while the review is conducted.
body_zh: |
  德州州長葛雷格·艾伯特（Greg Abbott）週一下令州公用事業監管機構對所有尋求接入德州電網的資料中心專案進行全面稽核，指示任何未能符合州法規或監管要求的專案均應被拒絕併網——此舉立即中止了一項關鍵輸電規劃研究，並在全美最大的AI基礎設施市場之一，大幅提高了開發商的舉證門檻。

  艾伯特的指令送達德州公用事業委員會（PUCT）——負責監管電力公用事業的州機構——以及德克薩斯電力可靠性委員會（ERCOT），即服務德州大部分地區的獨立電網營運商，要求對ERCOT佇列中每一筆資料中心併網申請進行「全面驗證與稽核」。ERCOT表示將與PUCT合作落實此命令，包括立即暫停第零批次（Batch Zero）輸電規劃研究——該程序在將最大規模的擬議用電負載納入長期電網規劃之前，會先對其進行評估。

  「我們的首要任務是保護德州人的安全與生活品質，」艾伯特在聲明中表示。「任何未能遵守PUCT、ERCOT及州法所設條件的專案，均必須被拒絕接入德州電網。簡而言之，德州人必須優先。」

  PUCT主席湯瑪斯·葛里森（Thomas Gleeson）表示支持該指令，稱委員會將持續與ERCOT合作，執行艾伯特的命令，同時落實該機構在7月17日致州長函件中所提出的建議。「我們感謝艾伯特州長的指令，並將持續與ERCOT密切合作，確保這些要求以及我們2026年7月17日致艾伯特州長函件中所列各項要求，代表德州人民得到全面落實，」葛里森說。

  ## 德州為何出手

  德州吸引了大批超大規模資料中心專案——由規模最大的雲端與科技業者所營運的設施——這些專案合計提出的新增用電需求高達數百吉瓦（GW），遠超該州目前的尖峰負載。州政府官員憂慮，投機性提案正扭曲長期電網規劃，若電網營運商被迫依照那些可能永遠無法建成、或無法依計畫規模運行的專案來規劃輸電與發電容量，將導致不必要且代價高昂的基礎設施投資。

  即便在週一指令發布之前，ERCOT的第零批次程序已對最大規模的擬議用電負載設定了成熟度標準，再將其納入長期研究。艾伯特的命令將這一邏輯擴展至整個併網申請佇列，並將更廣泛的驗證作為繼續推進流程的前提條件。

  ## 開發商可能需要證明什麼

  能源分析師、Persistence Analytics Group創辦人尼爾·奧斯納托（Neil Osnato）表示，此指令標誌著德州評估擬議需求方式的根本性轉變，而非單純為了縮減過量的申請佇列。「德州不只是在清理超量的佇列，而是在重新定義什麼才算是可信的需求，」奧斯納托說。

  奧斯納托指出，此命令使德州從傳統的併網模式——在該模式下，已提交的申請依序進入工程研究流程——轉向以證據為基礎的方法，要求開發商在電網規劃人員投入資源之前，先證明專案的成熟度。這意味著不僅要表明某專案已提出服務申請，還須證明其已取得融資、完成場址控制、獲得許可審批、備妥設備、制定施工計畫、確保電力與水源供應，並具備通往長期商業運營的可行路徑。

  「佇列中的位置並不能證明電網應當圍繞這項申請進行建設，」奧斯納托說。

  對開發商而言，這意味著在電網規劃人員代其承諾輸電和發電資源之前，需承擔更高的舉證責任。融資狀況較為成熟且已取得場址控制權的專案，若稽核程序淘汰了投機性競爭者，或許能從中受益；成熟度較低的專案，則在推進前將面臨額外審查。

  ## 正在成形的全國性監管轉向

  德州並非孤立行動。PJM——服務大西洋中部與中西部13州的電網營運商——已批准改革措施，將實體併網與大型新增用電負載的確定性資源適足性分離，同時收緊其併網流程其他環節的專案可行性要求。在維吉尼亞州，監管機構正在討論超大規模用電客戶是否應承擔更大比例、與其設施服務相關的輸電成本。

  奧斯納托將這些舉措描述為全國各地電網營運商面對相同底層挑戰所做出的不同回應。「大型用電負載規劃不能再單靠預測規模，而必須檢驗其概率、時程、可交付性、持續性與成本責任，」他說。

  ## 懸而未決的問題

  艾伯特的指令留下了若干操作層面的問題尚待釐清：稽核將持續多久、PUCT與ERCOT如何分工執行審查、開發商須提交哪些具體資訊，以及在稽核期間，已在推進中的併網流程專案將受到何種影響。

  這些細節將決定此次行動究竟是對該州最大規模擬議用電負載的定向驗證，還是一個影響整個申請佇列AI開發時程的更廣泛程序性關卡。ERCOT暫停第零批次輸電規劃研究，表明至少部分已進入審批流程的專案，將在審查進行期間面臨延誤。
