body_en: |
  Taiwan's Bureau of Energy, an agency of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, released the country's fiscal year 2025 national power supply and demand report on June 18, listing AI data center electricity consumption as a standalone forecast variable for the first time — a formal break from the practice of bundling AI-related load inside broader semiconductor and technology categories.

  The report projects average annual electricity demand growth of 2.5 percent over the 10-year period from 2026 to 2035, the bureau said. That rate is described as the second-highest growth level recorded in the report's history and exceeds comparable near-term forecasts for Japan and South Korea.

  ## Night-time load emerges as distinct concern

  The bureau linked AI data center expansion directly to rising nighttime grid pressure. Night peak load — the highest power draw recorded during late-evening hours, when traditional industrial demand has typically subsided — is forecast to reach 38,840 megawatts (MW) in 2026, up from 36,888 MW in 2025, an increase of roughly 1,952 MW in a single year. By 2035, the bureau projects night peak load will climb to 47,948 MW.

  The operating profile of AI infrastructure drives that distinction. Unlike conventional factories, which concentrate consumption during daytime production shifts, large AI computing clusters run graphics processing units (GPUs) and servers continuously around the clock, maintaining heavy load well after nightfall. The bureau's report states that this 24-hour operating pattern imposes a qualitatively different burden on the power system than conventional industrial loads.

  ## What changed from last year's report

  The prior year's report grouped AI technology development, high-performance computing (HPC — processing-intensive workloads requiring large clusters of specialized chips), and semiconductor industry demand together as a combined set of growth drivers. The fiscal year 2025 report isolates AI data centers as a separate variable, distinct from semiconductor manufacturing expansion and general technology demand. The bureau said its 10-year demand model now incorporates five discrete factors: economic growth forecasts from Taiwan's Directorate-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics; semiconductor plant expansions; AI data center electricity use; temperature variation; and deep energy-efficiency programs.

  ## Supply additions planned through 2035

  To cover the projected load growth, the bureau said the government plans to add approximately 25,980 MW of gas-fired generation capacity by 2035. Some generation units previously scheduled for retirement will instead be retained as backup reserve capacity to increase scheduling flexibility for grid operators. The report also calls for continued expansion of renewable energy, battery and other energy storage systems, and demand-side management programs designed to shift or reduce consumption during peak periods.

  ## Significance

  The decision to break AI data centers out of a broader technology-demand category marks a structural shift in how Taiwan's energy planners treat AI infrastructure: as a long-lived, predictable load class that warrants its own modeling assumptions, rather than a transient or derivative effect of chip manufacturing investment. The bureau said the new framework reflects the accelerating spread of generative AI applications and rising demand for cloud computing services.

  The government's 10-year outlook projects night peak load rising from 36,888 MW in 2025 to 47,948 MW in 2035, a figure that will serve as the planning baseline against which new generation, storage, and grid investment decisions are measured.

  ## 台灣能源報告首度將AI資料中心列為獨立需求變數

  經濟部能源署於6月18日發布114年度全國電力供需報告，首次將AI資料中心用電量列為獨立預測變數，正式告別過去將AI相關負載併入半導體及科技產業大類的做法，標誌著台灣電力規劃方式的結構性轉變。

  報告預估，2026年至2035年這10年間，全國年均用電需求成長率為2.5%。能源署指出，此一成長幅度在該報告歷史紀錄中排名第二高，亦高於日本、南韓等鄰近國家的近期預測。

  ## 夜間負載成為新興隱憂

  能源署在報告中將AI資料中心的擴張，直接點名為夜間電網壓力上升的主因。所謂「夜間尖峰負載」，係指深夜時段的最高用電量——傳統上，此時工業需求通常已大幅回落——預估2026年將達38,840百萬瓦（MW），較2025年的36,888 MW增加約1,952 MW，單年增幅相當顯著。到2035年，夜間尖峰負載更預計攀升至47,948 MW。

  造成此一現象的關鍵，在於AI基礎建設的運作特性。不同於傳統工廠集中在白天生產班次耗電，大型AI運算叢集須讓繪圖處理器（GPU）與伺服器全天候持續運行，深夜仍維持龐大負載。能源署報告指出，這種全天24小時不間斷的運作模式，對電力系統所造成的質性衝擊，與傳統工業負載截然不同。

  ## 今年報告與去年的差異

  去年度報告將AI技術發展、高效能運算（HPC——需要大規模專用晶片叢集處理的密集運算工作負載）及半導體產業需求，合併為一組成長驅動因素。114年度報告則將AI資料中心獨立出來，與半導體製造擴產及一般科技需求明確區隔。能源署表示，更新後的10年需求模型納入五項獨立變數：行政院主計總處的經濟成長預測、半導體廠擴產計畫、AI資料中心用電、氣溫變化，以及深度節能方案。

  ## 2035年前的供電擴充計畫

  為因應預估的負載成長，能源署表示政府計畫在2035年前新增約25,980 MW的燃氣發電裝置容量。部分原定除役的發電機組將改列為備用容量保留，以提升電網調度的彈性空間。報告同時要求持續擴充再生能源、電池及其他儲能系統，並推動需量反應等需求面管理措施，以達到移轉或削減尖峰用電的效果。

  ## 意義與影響

  將AI資料中心從科技需求大類中單獨拆出，標誌著台灣能源規劃部門對AI基礎建設定性的根本轉變：不再將其視為晶片製造投資的短暫效應或衍生影響，而是視為具有長期性、可預測性的獨立負載類別，值得建立專屬的模型假設。能源署指出，新框架的建立，反映了生成式AI應用加速普及、雲端運算服務需求持續攀升的現實。

  政府10年展望預估，夜間尖峰負載將從2025年的36,888 MW成長至2035年的47,948 MW，這項數字將成為未來新增發電、儲能及電網投資決策的規劃基準。
