body_en: |
  The National Grid Corp. of the Philippines (NGCP) has formally demanded the Department of Energy (DoE) withdraw two department circulars that allow power generation companies and the state-owned National Transmission Corp. (TransCo) to finance and construct transmission infrastructure, saying the measures violate its exclusive mandate as the country's sole grid operator.

  NGCP filed a consolidated position paper addressed to Energy Secretary Sharon S. Garin calling for the immediate withdrawal of both circulars. The company argued they conflict with the Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001 (EPIRA), the foundational law restructuring the Philippine power sector, as well as its concession agreement with the Power Sector Assets and Liabilities Management Corp. (PSALM) and TransCo.

  ## What NGCP says the law requires

  NGCP's central argument is that EPIRA and its concession agreement grant it the exclusive right and obligation to construct, finance, maintain, expand, and operate the nationwide transmission grid. The company also holds the obligation to prepare the annual Transmission Development Plan (TDP), a formal roadmap for grid investment.

  The grid operator cited Section 9 of EPIRA, which it said limits generation companies to dedicated point-to-point transmission facilities that connect their own power plants to the grid — a narrow carve-out that does not extend to broader transmission development.

  NGCP argued that the DoE, by issuing circulars that expand the scope of projects other entities may undertake, altered the statutory framework without amending the law itself. "By altering the statutory framework and substantially modifying what is allowed under the EPIRA, the DoE has exceeded its delegated executive authority and has violated the constitutional principle of Separation of Powers," the company said in the position paper.

  ## TransCo planning authority at issue

  Beyond the generation company provisions, NGCP raised a separate concern about clauses in the circulars that permit TransCo — the government entity from which NGCP holds its operating concession — to implement transmission projects outside the TDP. NGCP argued that allowing parallel planning and construction arrangements outside its TDP process could disrupt coordinated development of the interconnected grid.

  To reinforce its planning authority, NGCP cited a February 19, 2025 arbitral award that it said affirmed its exclusive responsibility to prepare the TDP for the duration of its concession period.

  ## Background: NGCP's role and the concession structure

  NGCP took over operation and maintenance of the Philippine transmission system in 2009 under a concession agreement with TransCo, which retains ownership of the grid assets. The arrangement was created under the EPIRA restructuring framework, which separated generation, transmission, and distribution into distinct regulated segments.

  ## What comes next

  NGCP said any gaps in transmission development should be resolved through coordination with itself and the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) within the existing legal and concession framework, rather than through new executive circulars. The company called on the DoE to act immediately, leaving the question of whether Garin's department will comply or defend the circulars as the key pending development.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓國家電網公司（NGCP）正式要求能源部（DoE）撤回兩項部門通令，這兩項通令允許發電公司及國有的國家輸電公司（TransCo）自行融資興建輸電基礎設施。NGCP主張，上述措施違反其身為全國唯一電網營運商的獨家授權。

  NGCP提交了一份彙整立場書，呈遞給能源部長Sharon S. Garin，要求立即撤回兩項通令。該公司主張，這些通令與2001年頒布的《電力產業改革法》（EPIRA）——菲律賓電力部門重組的根本性法律——以及其與電力資產與負債管理公司（PSALM）及TransCo簽訂的特許經營協議相牴觸。

  ## 法律明定的獨家授權

  NGCP的核心論點在於，EPIRA及其特許經營協議賦予其興建、融資、維護、擴充及營運全國輸電網路的獨家權利與義務。該公司同時負有編製年度輸電發展計畫（TDP）的義務，TDP是電網投資的正式路線圖。

  這家電網營運商援引EPIRA第9條，指出該條文將發電公司的業務範圍限定於從自有電廠接入電網的專用點對點輸電設施——此為一項狹窄的例外規定，並不延伸至更廣泛的輸電發展業務。

  NGCP主張，能源部透過發布通令擴大其他業者可承攬的業務範圍，實際上是在未修法的情況下改變了法定框架。「能源部透過改變法定框架，並實質修改EPIRA所允許的內容，已逾越其被授予的行政職權，並違反了憲法上的權力分立原則，」NGCP在立場書中如此表示。

  ## 輸電規劃權限之爭

  除了涉及發電公司的條款之外，NGCP還對通令中允許TransCo——即NGCP持有特許經營權的政府機關——在TDP框架以外自行推動輸電計畫的相關條款提出另一項質疑。NGCP主張，若允許在其TDP流程以外進行平行規劃與建設，可能破壞互聯電網的協調發展。

  為強化其規劃主導地位，NGCP援引2025年2月19日的一項仲裁裁決，表示該裁決確認了NGCP在整個特許經營期間對TDP編製負有獨家責任。

  ## 背景：NGCP的角色與特許經營架構

  NGCP於2009年依據與TransCo的特許經營協議，接手菲律賓輸電系統的營運與維護業務；TransCo則保留電網資產的所有權。這項安排是在EPIRA重組框架下建立的，該框架將發電、輸電與配電劃分為各自獨立的受管制部門。

  ## 後續發展

  NGCP表示，輸電發展上的任何缺口，應在現行法律及特許經營框架內，透過與其本身及能源管制委員會（ERC）協調的方式加以解決，而非透過新頒行政通令來處理。NGCP呼籲能源部立即採取行動，而Garin領導的能源部究竟將遵從要求撤回通令、還是出面捍衛現行規定，則是目前懸而未決的關鍵發展。
