body_en: |
  Indonesia's industrial zone association is urging the government to grant industrial parks the right to build and operate their own power plants, citing recent blackouts across Java and Sumatra as evidence that the centralized national grid cannot adequately support the country's manufacturing ambitions.

  The Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia (HKI), or Indonesian Industrial Zone Association, made the call on June 22, 2026, asking the government to allow industrial zones to hold Wilayah Usaha Ketenagalistrikan (WILUS) licenses — the regulatory designation for electricity business areas — and to construct generation capacity integrated with the national grid.

  HKI chairman Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana said the recent outages should be treated as a signal that the national system requires structural change. "The blackouts that have occurred recently must be seen as an important alarm that the national electricity system requires stronger safety layers. National energy resilience cannot rely solely on one centralized system. More reliability nodes are needed that can mutually support each other when disruptions occur," Ma'ruf said in a written statement.

  HKI oversees more than 170 industrial zones spread across Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, and eastern Indonesia. Those parks collectively house thousands of domestic and multinational companies operating in sectors including manufacturing, petrochemicals, logistics, food and beverage, electronics, automotive, minerals, data centers, and high-technology industries.

  ## Why even brief outages carry large costs

  In modern industry, production processes run in an integrated and continuous manner, meaning even a brief interruption triggers cascading losses, the association said. A single outage can damage raw materials, cause products to fail mid-process, delay delivery schedules, disrupt supply chains, and force operators to incur additional costs running backup systems.

  For certain sectors — petrochemicals, steel, semiconductors, data centers, pharmaceuticals, and industries based on continuous processes — the damage is compounded because restarting operations after an outage takes an extended period of time, HKI said.

  ## Alignment with Indonesia's industrial push

  Ma'ruf framed the reliability issue as inseparable from Indonesia's broader economic goals. The country is currently promoting mineral downstreaming, electric vehicle development, data center construction, artificial intelligence industries, basic chemicals, and high-technology manufacturing — all sectors that require stable, layered energy supply meeting international reliability standards, he said.

  "The challenges will grow larger as new investments arrive that require very high levels of electricity reliability," Ma'ruf said.

  HKI's position is that the national electricity system needs to enter a new phase that is more adaptive and collaborative, with industrial zones serving as additional reliability nodes rather than passive consumers of grid power. The association's proposal — permitting industrial parks to hold WILUS licenses and build integrated generation — represents a structural shift in how Indonesia organizes electricity supply to its manufacturing base, particularly as higher-reliability sectors such as semiconductors and data centers expand across the archipelago.
body_zh: |
  印尼工業區協會敦促政府授權工業園區自建電廠並自主營運，並援引近期爪哇島與蘇門答臘大停電事件，指出中央集中式國家電網無法充分支撐該國製造業擴張的野心。

  印尼工業區聯合會（Himpunan Kawasan Industri Indonesia，以下簡稱HKI）於2026年6月22日提出上述訴求，要求政府允許工業區申請取得「電力事業經營區」（Wilayah Usaha Ketenagalistrikan，WILUS）執照，並興建與國家電網整合的自有發電容量。

  HKI主席阿赫馬德·馬魯夫·毛拉納（Akhmad Ma'ruf Maulana）表示，近期的停電事故應被視為一個重要警示，說明國家電力系統需要進行結構性變革。他在書面聲明中指出：「近期發生的停電事件，必須被視為一記重要警鐘——國家電力系統需要更強的安全防護層。國家能源韌性不能僅僅依賴單一的集中式系統，還需要更多可在發生故障時相互支援的可靠節點。」

  HKI監管逾170個工業區，分布於蘇門答臘、爪哇、加里曼丹、蘇拉威西及印尼東部各地。這些工業區共容納了數千家國內外企業，涵蓋製造業、石化、物流、食品與飲料、電子、汽車、礦業、資料中心及高科技產業等多個領域。

  ## 短暫停電何以帶來巨大損失

  HKI指出，在現代工業中，生產流程高度整合且持續不間斷，即便是短暫的中斷，也會引發一連串的損失。單一停電事故便可能造成原材料損毀、產品半途報廢、交貨時程延誤、供應鏈中斷，並迫使業者承擔額外的備用系統運轉成本。

  對於石化、鋼鐵、半導體、資料中心、製藥及其他需要連續製程的產業而言，停電造成的損害更為嚴峻，因為停電後重啟作業往往需要耗費相當長的時間，HKI表示。

  ## 與印尼產業政策的高度契合

  馬魯夫將電力可靠性議題與印尼更宏觀的經濟目標緊密連結。他表示，印尼目前正積極推動礦物下游加工、電動車發展、資料中心建設、人工智慧產業、基礎化學品及高科技製造業，這些領域全都需要穩定且多層次的能源供應，並須符合國際可靠性標準。

  「隨著新投資陸續進駐，對電力可靠度要求極高的需求將與日俱增，挑戰也將持續擴大，」馬魯夫說。

  HKI的立場是，國家電力系統需要進入一個更具適應性與協作性的新階段，讓工業區扮演額外可靠性節點的角色，而非僅是電網電力的被動消費者。HKI提出的方案——允許工業園區持有WILUS執照並興建整合式發電設施——代表著印尼在組織製造業電力供應方式上的一項結構性轉變。隨著半導體與資料中心等高可靠度需求產業在群島各地持續擴張，這項轉變的重要性將愈發凸顯。
