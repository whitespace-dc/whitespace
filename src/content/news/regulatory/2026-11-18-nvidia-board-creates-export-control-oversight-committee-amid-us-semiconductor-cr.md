body_en: |
  Nvidia's board of directors has established a standing committee dedicated to overseeing the company's export control compliance and national security policies, the chip designer announced, one day after the US Department of Commerce tightened licensing requirements for advanced semiconductor exports to five countries.

  ## The new committee

  The National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee will be chaired by board director Suzanne Nora Johnson and will also include directors Stephen Neal and John Dabiri, according to a company press release. The committee will meet at least quarterly, with management and the executive team present.

  The panel will oversee Nvidia's National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team, both of which will report to the committee and to interim CEO Colette Kress on day-to-day operations. The committee will also engage independent third-party experts in law, national security, and global trade compliance, the company said.

  "NVIDIA recognizes that advanced silicon technologies and the emergence of artificial and superintelligence present unprecedented challenges for national security. As the world's leading advanced computing company, NVIDIA has both a responsibility and an opportunity to lead the industry in addressing these challenges," Harvey Jones, Chairman of Nvidia's Board of Directors, said in the announcement.

  Jones said the committee reflects the board's commitment to engaging with government stakeholders. "We are committed to working constructively with government and industry partners to help establish and advance a robust framework that uses every tool at our disposal to safeguard national security while supporting continued technological innovation," he said.

  ## BIS licensing rule

  The announcement came one day after the US Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) — the federal agency that administers export controls on sensitive technologies — released an interim final rule requiring licenses for exports of advanced semiconductors to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil.

  The BIS rule is designed to prevent chips from being diverted through those countries to destinations where US exports are restricted, and to curb remote access to advanced computing power built on US technology, according to the press release.

  ## Significance

  The formation of a dedicated board-level committee represents a formal escalation of Nvidia's governance response to tightening US export controls. By elevating compliance oversight to the board and committing to independent third-party expert engagement, Nvidia is establishing a more structured accountability chain at a time when US enforcement pressure on chip exports is increasing. The quarterly meeting cadence, with executive team participation, makes compliance reporting a standing item at the company's highest governance level.

  輝達（NVIDIA）宣布，董事會已成立一個專責委員會，負責監督公司的出口管制合規事宜及國家安全政策。消息公布的前一天，美國商務部甫宣布收緊對五個國家出口先進半導體的授權要求。

  ## 新設委員會

  依據公司新聞稿，「國家安全與出口管制監督委員會」將由董事會成員蘇珊・諾拉・強森（Suzanne Nora Johnson）擔任主席，另有董事史蒂芬・尼爾（Stephen Neal）與約翰・達比里（John Dabiri）共同出任委員。委員會每季至少召開一次會議，管理層與執行團隊均須出席。

  該委員會將監管輝達的「國家安全團隊」與「全球貿易合規團隊」，兩個團隊均向委員會及代理執行長科萊特・克雷斯（Colette Kress）匯報日常業務。委員會亦將延攬法律、國家安全及全球貿易合規領域的獨立第三方專家參與運作。

  輝達董事長哈維・瓊斯（Harvey Jones）在聲明中表示：「輝達認知到，先進矽晶片技術的發展以及人工智慧與超級智慧的崛起，為國家安全帶來前所未有的挑戰。作為全球領先的先進運算公司，輝達在應對這些挑戰上既有責任，也有機會引領業界。」

  瓊斯並指出，此委員會的成立彰顯了董事會積極與政府利害關係人溝通的決心。他說：「我們致力於與政府及產業夥伴建設性地合作，協助建立並推進一套健全的框架，善用一切可用工具，在維護國家安全的同時，持續支持科技創新。」

  ## 美國工業暨安全局授權規定

  此次公告在美國商務部工業暨安全局（Bureau of Industry and Security，BIS）——負責管理敏感科技出口管制的聯邦機構——發布臨時最終規定的隔天正式對外宣布。該規定要求，出口先進半導體至印尼、馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國及巴西前，必須取得授權許可。

  依據相關新聞稿，BIS此項規定旨在防止晶片透過上述國家輾轉流向美國出口受限地區，並遏制外界透過遠端存取使用以美國技術為基礎的先進運算資源。

  ## 重要意義

  專責董事會層級委員會的成立，代表輝達在美國出口管制持續收緊之際，正式提升了公司治理層面的回應規格。將合規監督提升至董事會層級，並承諾引入獨立第三方專家，使輝達建立起一條更具架構性的責任鏈。每季召開、並要求執行團隊出席的例會機制，更讓合規回報成為公司最高治理層級的常態議程。
