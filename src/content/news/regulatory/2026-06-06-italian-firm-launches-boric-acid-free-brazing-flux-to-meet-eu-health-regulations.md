body_en: |
  Italian manufacturer Pietro Galliani has released Galflo Eco Green, a brazing flux line engineered to remove reprotoxic hazard classifications from its formulation while preserving the technical performance of traditional products.

  The range eliminates hazard statements H360 and H361, the European Union designations for substances suspected or known to damage fertility or an unborn child — classifications that have come under increasing regulatory scrutiny under REACH, the EU framework governing the Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals. The product is also formulated without boric acid, a compound subject to REACH restrictions due to its reprotoxic properties.

  ## What the product replaces

  Brazing flux is a chemical compound applied during the brazing process — a metal-joining technique that uses a filler metal melted above 450°C — to prevent oxidation and help the filler flow cleanly into a joint. Conventional brazing fluxes have historically relied on boron-based compounds, including boric acid and borates, which provide effective oxide removal but carry reprotoxic classifications under EU chemical regulations.

  Pietro Galliani said Galflo Eco Green retains the core functional properties of those conventional formulations: oxide removal capability, process stability, and consistent brazing results.

  ## Regulatory driver

  REACH compliance is the explicit regulatory basis for the new formulation. Under REACH, substances of very high concern — including reprotoxic compounds — can face use restrictions or require authorisation, creating compliance risk for manufacturers and end users across the supply chain. By removing H360- and H361-classified substances from the product, Pietro Galliani is positioning the range to remain usable as regulatory pressure on boron-containing chemicals continues.

  ## Format availability

  Galflo Eco Green is offered in three physical forms: powder, paste, and coated rods, covering the application methods most commonly used in industrial brazing operations.

  The product's availability across multiple formats means it can be adopted without changing application equipment or processes, which Pietro Galliani said supports a direct substitution for existing flux products.

  ## 此產品所取代的舊有配方

  硬焊助焊劑是一種在硬焊過程中施用的化學化合物。硬焊是一種金屬接合技術，使用熔點高於450°C的填充金屬，而助焊劑的作用在於防止氧化，並協助填充金屬順暢地流入接合處。傳統硬焊助焊劑長期以來依賴硼基化合物，包括硼酸與硼酸鹽，這些化合物雖能有效去除氧化物，但在歐盟化學法規下帶有生殖毒性分類。

  Pietro Galliani表示，Galflo Eco Green保留了傳統配方的核心功能特性：去除氧化物的能力、製程穩定性，以及一致的硬焊效果。

  ## 法規驅動因素

  符合REACH法規是此次新配方明確的法規依據。根據REACH，高度關注物質——包括具生殖毒性的化合物——可能面臨使用限制或須取得授權許可，這對整個供應鏈中的製造商與終端用戶均構成合規風險。透過從產品中移除標有H360與H361分類的物質，Pietro Galliani正將此系列定位為在含硼化學品持續承受監管壓力的情況下，仍可合規使用的解決方案。

  ## 供應形式

  Galflo Eco Green提供三種物理形式：粉末、膏狀及包覆焊棒，涵蓋工業硬焊作業中最常見的施用方式。

  多種形式的供應意味著使用者無需更換施用設備或調整製程即可直接導入。Pietro Galliani表示，這有助於將其作為現有助焊劑產品的直接替代品，讓轉換過程更加順暢。
