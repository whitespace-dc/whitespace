body_en: |
  Japan's Fair Trade Commission (FTC), the country's antimonopoly regulator, raided the offices of three refrigerated display case manufacturers on suspicion of bid-rigging tied to supply contracts with Seven-Eleven Japan, the country's largest convenience store chain.

  The companies subject to the raids are Fuji Electric and Nakano Refrigerators, both headquartered in Tokyo, and SDRS, headquartered in Isesaki, approximately 100 kilometres northwest of the capital. All three are manufacturers of refrigerated and frozen display cases.

  The FTC is investigating whether the three firms violated Japan's Antimonopoly Act by colluding to pre-arrange which company would win Seven-Eleven Japan's supplier selection contracts across different Japanese districts. Seven-Eleven Japan uses a regional selection process to award contracts for both new stores and refurbished locations, and the three companies are reported to have won most of those orders in recent years.

  Seven-Eleven Japan operates more than 22,000 stores, making it the dominant convenience store operator in Japan. The scale of the chain means procurement contracts for refrigeration equipment across its network represent significant commercial volume.

  ## Company responses

  All three companies said they are taking the FTC's allegations seriously and will cooperate fully with the investigation.

  ## What the case means for the industry

  If the FTC's allegations are substantiated, the case would represent a significant enforcement action in the commercial refrigeration supply sector in Japan. Bid-rigging in public and private procurement undermines competitive pricing and can result in substantial fines and disqualification from future contracts under Japan's Antimonopoly Act. The outcome of the investigation will determine what formal charges, if any, follow the raids.

  日本公平交易委員會（以下簡稱「公取委」）突擊搜查三家冷藏展示櫃製造商，懷疑其涉嫌在與日本最大便利商店龍頭7-Eleven Japan的供應合約中進行串標。

  遭搜查的三家公司分別為：總部同設於東京的富士電機（Fuji Electric）與中野冷機（Nakano Refrigerators），以及總部位於伊勢崎市的SDRS——伊勢崎市距首都東京西北方約100公里。三家公司均為冷藏及冷凍展示櫃製造商。

  公取委正在調查上述三家業者是否違反《獨占禁止法》，指控核心在於：三方相互勾結，預先協議在日本各地區的7-Eleven Japan供應商遴選標案中輪流安排得標廠商。7-Eleven Japan採分區遴選制度，針對新開門市及翻新店面分別辦理採購，據報導，三家業者近年囊括了絕大多數訂單。

  7-Eleven Japan在全日本擁有逾2萬2,000家門市，是日本規模最龐大的便利商店業者。因門市數量龐大，其冷藏設備採購合約所涵蓋的商業規模相當可觀。

  ## 各公司回應

  三家公司均表示，對公取委提出的指控態度嚴肅，並將全力配合調查。

  ## 此案對業界的意義

  若公取委的指控獲得確認，本案將成為日本商用冷藏供應領域一起重大的反壟斷執法行動。串標行為破壞公私部門採購的公平競爭機制，依《獨占禁止法》，相關業者可能面臨鉅額罰款，並遭禁止參與未來投標。調查結果將決定此次突擊搜查後是否進一步提起正式指控。
