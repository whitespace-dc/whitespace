body_en: |
  The European Commission has opened an infringement procedure against Greece for failing to allow fluorinated greenhouse gas (F-gas) technicians certified in other EU member states to work in the country on the basis of those certificates alone, in breach of EU F-gas regulation 2024/573.

  ## The breach

  Article 10(10) of regulation 2024/573 requires all EU member states to mutually recognise training attestations and certificates issued by other member states. Under that provision, a technician certified in one EU country must be able to carry out the relevant activities in any other member state without further qualification checks.

  Greece does not currently comply. F-gas technicians holding certificates issued by another EU country cannot access work as refrigeration technicians in Greece on the basis of those certificates. Greek national legislation instead requires them to undergo additional assessment procedures before they are permitted to pursue the relevant activities.

  ## The Commission's reasoning

  The Commission argues that the transition away from F-gases towards climate-friendly alternatives requires a sufficient number of appropriately trained and certified technicians across the EU. Effective mutual recognition of nationally issued certificates is, in the Commission's view, essential to achieving that objective.

  ## Procedure and next steps

  The Commission has sent Greece a letter of formal notice, which is the first official step in a legal investigation against a member state. Greece now has two months to respond and address the shortcomings identified. If the Commission finds the response unsatisfactory, it may issue a reasoned opinion — a formal document detailing precisely why it believes EU legislation is being breached and demanding corrective measures within a specified period.

  The two-month response window represents Greece's first formal opportunity to either demonstrate compliance or propose remedial action before the Commission escalates the procedure.

  ## 違規情形

  第2024/573號法規第10條第10項要求所有歐盟成員國相互承認其他成員國核發的培訓證明與執業證書。依據該條款，凡在某一歐盟成員國取得認證的技術人員，應能在任何其他成員國從事相關作業，無需再接受額外的資格審查。

  ## 執委會的立場

  歐盟執行委員會指出，推動從氟化氣體轉型至對氣候更友善之替代物質，有賴於歐盟各地擁有足夠數量、且經過適當培訓與認證的技術人員。執委會認為，有效落實各成員國所核發證書的相互承認制度，對於實現此一目標至關重要。

  執委會已向希臘發出正式通知函，這是對成員國展開法律調查程序的第一個官方步驟。希臘現有兩個月時間提出回應，並說明如何改正相關缺失。若執委會認為希臘的回應不盡令人滿意，可進一步發出有理由的意見書——此為一份正式文件，詳細闡明執委會認定歐盟法規遭到違反的具體理由，並要求希臘在指定期限內採取改正措施。
