body_en: |
  Thailand's data centre committee announced on Oct. 27 that it will impose a set of minimum requirements on data centre operators, including standards for site location and land use, fuel storage, water management infrastructure, and an environmental code of practice, as the country moves to regulate an industry that has attracted more than 100 prospective operators.

  The requirements will divide data centres into seven tiers based on power demand. The criteria are to be submitted to Cabinet next week, after which new data centre applications are expected to open in early 2027, said Danucha Pichayanan, secretary-general of the National Economic and Social Development Council. Existing data centres will have one year to meet the new minimum standards, Danucha said.

  ## Scale of investment driving regulatory action

  As of February, Thailand had 42 data centres representing 3,400 megawatts (MW) of information technology load — roughly equivalent to the output of several large power stations — and approximately 750 billion baht (S$28.5 billion) in total investment. The volume of incoming applications prompted the government to scrutinise the sector's land, energy, and water footprint before granting further approvals.

  Among the largest recent commitments, TikTok invested US$24.61 billion (S$31.5 billion) in Thailand in 2026. Google announced a US$1 billion investment in data centre and cloud infrastructure in 2024.

  ## Regional positioning

  Thailand, Southeast Asia's second-largest economy, is seeking to establish itself as a regional data infrastructure hub alongside Singapore and Malaysia. The new regulatory framework is designed to provide a structured pathway for that expansion while setting baseline operational and environmental standards.

  The submission of criteria to Cabinet next week marks the next formal step before applications reopen in early 2027.

  ## 泰國資料中心訂定最低標準，2027年起開放新申請

  泰國資料中心委員會於10月27日宣布，將對資料中心業者實施一套最低要求，內容涵蓋場址選址與土地使用標準、燃料儲存規範、水資源管理基礎設施，以及環境行為準則。隨著泰國吸引逾百家意向業者進駐，政府正式啟動對這一產業的法規管理程序。

  依新規定，資料中心將依用電需求分為七個等級。國家經濟與社會發展委員會秘書長達努查·皮差亞南表示，相關標準將於下週提交內閣審議，完成後預計於2027年初開放新資料中心的申請作業。現有資料中心業者須在一年內符合新訂的最低標準。

  ## 龐大投資規模促使監管出手

  截至今年2月，泰國境內共有42座資料中心，資訊技術負載合計達3,400百萬瓦（MW），相當於數座大型發電廠的總輸出功率，累計總投資額約為7,500億泰銖（約新台幣8,550億元）。大量申請案湧入，促使政府在批准更多案件之前，先行審視整體產業對土地、能源及水資源的影響。

  在近期規模最大的投資承諾中，TikTok於2026年在泰國投資達246.1億美元；Google則於2024年宣布投入10億美元，用於資料中心與雲端基礎設施建設。

  ## 區域定位

  泰國是東南亞第二大經濟體，正積極爭取與新加坡、馬來西亞並駕齊驅，成為區域資料基礎設施樞紐。此次新監管框架旨在為產業擴張提供有序的推進路徑，同時建立基本的營運與環保標準。

  標準草案下週提交內閣，是2027年初重新開放申請前的下一個正式程序步驟。
