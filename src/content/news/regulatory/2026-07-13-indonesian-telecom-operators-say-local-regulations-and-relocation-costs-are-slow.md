  ## Regulatory and cost barriers slowing Indonesia's fixed-network buildout

  Indonesia's fixed-network telecommunications operators face a dual drag on investment: the cost of relocating already-installed infrastructure and a patchwork of local-government regulations that industry groups say add financial burden and legal uncertainty, according to statements published July 10–11, 2026.

  The Indonesian government has set targets for fiber-optic network coverage to reach 90 percent of sub-districts (kecamatan) and fixed broadband household penetration to reach 50 percent by 2029, according to Kontan. Industry representatives say those goals are at risk without regulatory reform.

  ## Relocation costs divert capital from expansion

  Raymond Hubertus, chair of Jartatel — the Association of Fixed Telecommunications Network Operators — said relocation of network infrastructure is one of the primary factors reducing investment efficiency. Funds that operators would otherwise use to extend networks are instead spent moving infrastructure that has already been installed, he said.

  Raymond said governance of public-space use and shared infrastructure needs improvement to provide legal certainty and equal treatment for all fixed-network operators. He called for more intensive dialogue between industry and government to address regulations affecting fixed-network construction, including policies set at the regional level.

  "Jartatel seeks to be a partner of the government in accelerating national telecommunications infrastructure development through collaboration, standardization, investment efficiency, and equitable connectivity across all regions of Indonesia," Raymond said on July 10, as quoted by Kontan.

  Jartatel said it will push for discussions on investment efficiency, alignment of technical regulations, and equitable fixed-network development across regions, and will raise issues including network relocation and shared-infrastructure utilization.

  ## Overlapping rules at national and regional levels

  Kamilov Sagala, chair of Peratin — the Indonesian Telecommunications Experts Association — said overlapping regulations remain a challenge for the industry. He said telecommunications infrastructure should be treated as a national strategic project to give it stronger legal protection and certainty. Without regulatory harmonization, he warned, digital infrastructure investment faces growing pressure.

  Tagor H. Sihombing, executive director of Aspimtel — the Indonesian Telecommunications Infrastructure Providers Association — said the integration of telecommunications licensing into an online system is a positive step because it provides certainty and speeds up the investment process. However, he noted that the industry still faces rising land-lease costs while tower-lease rates tend to decline, making collaboration and innovation increasingly important.

  ## What the barriers mean for Indonesia's connectivity goals

  The combined effect of relocation expenditure and fragmented regional regulation is that operators cannot deploy capital as efficiently as the government's 2029 coverage targets require. Raymond said resolving these issues would improve operator investment efficiency and allow fixed-network development to reach more of Indonesia's territory.

  Jartatel also said it is targeting improvements in technical standards and transparent organizational governance as part of its broader effort to support telecommunications infrastructure development. The association indicated it will continue to press those issues in discussions with government.

  ## 法規與成本雙重壓力，拖慢印尼固網建設

  印尼固網電信業者正面臨雙重投資阻力：已建設完成的基礎設施遷移費用，以及各地政府法規紊亂所造成的財務負擔與法律不確定性。上述情況根據2026年7月10日至11日發布的聲明指出。

  印尼政府已訂定目標，要在2029年前使光纖網路覆蓋率達到全國九成的鄉鎮（kecamatan），並使固網寬頻家庭普及率達到50%，據《Kontan》報導。業界代表表示，若無法推動法規改革，上述目標恐有落空之虞。

  ## 搬遷費用侵蝕擴網資本

  固網電信業者協會（Jartatel）主席Raymond Hubertus表示，網路基礎設施的遷移費用是降低投資效率的主要因素之一。他指出，業者原本可用於擴建網路的資金，卻被迫花在搬移已建設完成的基礎設施上。

  Raymond表示，公共空間使用及共用基礎設施的治理需要改善，以提供法律確定性，並確保所有固網業者獲得平等對待。他呼籲業界與政府展開更密集的對話，以解決影響固網建設的相關法規問題，包括地方層級所制定的政策。

  Raymond於7月10日表示：「Jartatel致力成為政府的夥伴，透過協作、標準化、投資效率及區域均衡連網，共同加速印尼全國電信基礎設施的發展。」（引自《Kontan》）

  Jartatel表示，將積極推動投資效率、技術法規整合及全國固網均衡發展等議題的討論，並將提出網路遷移及共用基礎設施利用等問題。

  ## 中央與地方法規疊床架屋

  印尼電信專家協會（Peratin）主席Kamilov Sagala表示，法規重疊仍是業界面臨的重大挑戰。他認為，電信基礎設施應被列為國家戰略計畫，以取得更強力的法律保障與確定性。他警告，若法規無法統一整合，數位基礎設施投資將面臨日益沉重的壓力。

  印尼電信基礎設施業者協會（Aspimtel）執行董事Tagor H. Sihombing表示，將電信執照申請整合至線上系統是一項正面進展，因為此舉能提供確定性並加快投資流程。然而，他也指出，業界仍面臨土地租賃成本上升、而鐵塔租賃費率卻趨於下滑的困境，使得協作與創新愈發重要。

  ## 雙重障礙對印尼連網目標的衝擊

  遷移支出與地方法規零碎化的共同效應，使業者無法以政府2029年覆蓋目標所需的效率部署資本。Raymond表示，解決上述問題將有助於提升業者的投資效率，並使固網建設得以延伸至印尼更廣泛的地區。

  Jartatel也表示，正以改善技術標準及提升組織治理透明度為目標，作為其支持電信基礎設施發展整體努力的一部分。該協會表示，將持續在與政府的對話中推動上述議題。
