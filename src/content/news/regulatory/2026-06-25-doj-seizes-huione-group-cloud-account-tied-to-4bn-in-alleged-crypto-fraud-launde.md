body_en: |
  The US Department of Justice has seized the cloud computing account used by subsidiaries of Huione Group, a Cambodian corporation that federal prosecutors say helped move billions of dollars in cryptocurrency fraud proceeds through a network of illicit online marketplaces and Telegram-based services.

  The seized account allegedly hosted infrastructure for a Huione subsidiary that, according to the DOJ, "assisted individuals and organizations in transferring proceeds of cryptocurrency investment frauds, cyber scams, and other criminal activities on cryptocurrency blockchains and allowing for the conversion of the proceeds of these schemes to the legitimate banking sector undetected."

  FinCEN, the US Treasury's financial crimes enforcement network, has previously estimated that Huione Group laundered more than $4 billion in illegal funds between August 2021 and January 2025.

  ## What the account hosted

  The DOJ said the cloud account was used to operate Huione Guarantee, a service that ran Telegram channels containing discussions of illicit products and services, including stolen credit card and identity information, proceeds of malware-enabled theft, recruitment for human trafficking schemes, and assistance laundering the proceeds of romance and investment scams. The agency said the service facilitated "movement of considerable funds stolen by Southeast Asian scam centers."

  Huione's websites have since been disabled following the seizure.

  ## Cloud provider not identified; Google's crime unit among those thanked

  The DOJ did not disclose which cloud provider hosted the seized account. In its announcement, however, the department thanked the intelligence teams at Chainalysis, a blockchain data platform, and Elliptic, a blockchain analytics firm specializing in detecting cryptocurrency crime, as well as Google's CyberCrime Investigation Group — a specialized internal division that investigates and disrupts illicit activity on Google's infrastructure — for "voluntarily providing valuable information for this investigation."

  ## Official statements

  "Today's seizure strikes a blow against one of the world's most prolific criminal marketplaces," said Assistant Attorney General A. Tysen Duva of the DOJ's Criminal Division. "The Huione Group used this cloud computing account as part of a technological backbone that allowed billions in fraud proceeds to be transferred, moved, and concealed — much of it stolen through Southeast Asian scam centers."

  FBI Assistant Director Brett Leatherman of the Cyber Division said the action "targets a key enabler of cyber-enabled fraud and money laundering schemes, demonstrating that the FBI will pursue not only the perpetrators, but also the services that support their criminal operations."

  ## Background and prior enforcement actions

  Huione Group was first publicly linked to money laundering services in July 2024. In March 2025, the National Bank of Cambodia revoked the banking license of Huione Pay, a subsidiary of the group. In April 2026, Li Xiong, the company's former chairman, was extradited to China to face money laundering charges.

  ## Operation Riptide

  The seizure was conducted under Operation Riptide, an ongoing FBI campaign directed at the criminal actors, infrastructure, and financial networks behind cybercrime, cyber-enabled crime, and fraud targeting Americans. The Huione action illustrates the operation's focus on the service providers and technical infrastructure that enable fraud at scale, rather than individual perpetrators alone.

  The case signals that cloud infrastructure used to facilitate cryptocurrency-based financial crime is now a direct target of US federal enforcement, and that blockchain analytics firms and major cloud providers are active partners in identifying and disrupting such services.

  ## 美國司法部查扣匯旺集團雲端帳戶，涉嫌洗白逾400億元加密貨幣詐騙資金

  美國司法部（DOJ）已查扣柬埔寨企業匯旺集團（Huione Group）旗下子公司所使用的雲端運算帳戶。聯邦檢察官指控，該集團透過一套由非法網路市集與Telegram頻道所構成的地下網絡，協助轉移數十億美元的加密貨幣詐騙收益。

  據司法部表示，遭查扣的帳戶涉嫌為匯旺旗下子公司提供基礎設施，該子公司「協助個人及組織在加密貨幣區塊鏈上轉移加密貨幣投資詐騙、網路詐騙及其他犯罪活動所得，並在不引起察覺的情況下，將這些犯罪所得轉入合法銀行體系。」

  美國財政部金融犯罪執法網絡（FinCEN）先前估計，匯旺集團在2021年8月至2025年1月間，累計洗白非法資金逾40億美元（約合新台幣1,300億元）。

  ## 帳戶所承載的服務內容

  司法部指出，該雲端帳戶用於運營「匯旺擔保」（Huione Guarantee）服務，該服務透過Telegram頻道提供非法商品與服務的討論管道，內容涵蓋竊取的信用卡資訊及個人身分資料、以惡意軟體進行盜竊所得、人口販賣招募，以及協助洗白「殺豬盤」與投資詐騙收益等。司法部表示，該服務「協助東南亞詐騙中心轉移大量被竊資金」。

  帳戶遭查扣後，匯旺集團旗下相關網站已全面停止運作。

  ## 雲端供應商身分未公開；Google打擊犯罪小組列名致謝

  司法部並未透露被查扣帳戶所屬的雲端服務供應商。然而，在官方聲明中，司法部特別感謝區塊鏈資料平台Chainalysis、專精加密貨幣犯罪偵測的區塊鏈分析公司Elliptic，以及Google內部專責調查並打擊旗下基礎設施上非法活動的「網路犯罪調查小組」（CyberCrime Investigation Group），感謝上述單位「自願提供本案調查所需的寶貴情資」。

  ## 官方聲明

  司法部刑事司助理司法部長A·泰森·杜瓦（A. Tysen Duva）表示：「今日的查扣行動重創了全球最猖獗的犯罪市集之一。匯旺集團以此雲端運算帳戶作為技術骨幹的一環，使數十億美元的詐騙收益得以轉移、流通並隱匿——其中大部分是東南亞詐騙中心竊取所得。」

  聯邦調查局網路部門助理局長布雷特·萊澤曼（Brett Leatherman）則表示，此次行動「針對的是網路詐騙與洗錢犯罪的關鍵幫兇，充分展現聯邦調查局不僅追緝犯罪者本身，更會追查支撐其犯罪行動的各類服務。」

  ## 背景與過往執法行動

  匯旺集團最早於2024年7月被公開點名與洗錢服務掛鉤。2025年3月，柬埔寨國家銀行吊銷該集團子公司「匯旺支付」（Huione Pay）的銀行執照。2026年4月，該公司前董事長李雄遭引渡至中國，面臨洗錢相關指控。

  ## 激流行動

  此次查扣行動隸屬聯邦調查局持續推進的「激流行動」（Operation Riptide）。該行動專門針對網路犯罪、網路詐騙及以美國民眾為目標的詐欺案件背後的犯罪者、基礎設施與金融網絡。匯旺案清楚呈現了激流行動的核心策略——不僅追緝個別犯罪者，更將矛頭指向那些讓詐騙得以大規模運作的服務供應商與技術基礎設施。

  此案釋出明確訊號：用於協助加密貨幣金融犯罪的雲端基礎設施，已成為美國聯邦執法的直接打擊目標；區塊鏈分析公司與主要雲端服務供應商，也已是識別並瓦解此類犯罪服務的積極合作夥伴。
