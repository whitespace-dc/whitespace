body_en: |
  The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) on Thursday issued a notice of proposed rulemaking (NOPR) it says would "permanently end home appliance and equipment mandates," targeting the agency's existing program that sets minimum energy efficiency standards for a wide range of consumer products including light bulbs, washing machines, and furnaces.

  The NOPR is formally framed as an update to DOE's methodologies under its Appliance and Equipment Standards Program, which Congress has mandated to set and periodically update efficiency standards. Whether the proposal would fully disband the program remains unclear, according to Utility Dive's reporting.

  ## What the proposed rule would change

  The rule would alter DOE's testing procedures and the process by which energy conservation standards are developed. It would add a definition of "significant energy savings" as a threshold requirement for setting standards and include certain economic thresholds that rules would need to meet before taking effect.

  DOE said the changes would "safeguard the American people's freedom to choose from a variety of goods and appliances" and would "promote market competition and innovation within the manufacturing and appliance industries," according to the proposal.

  Assistant Secretary of Energy Audrey Robertson said in a statement that the proposal "will ensure that new regulations promote affordability, preserve consumer choice, and meet the highest standards for transparency and due diligence."

  ## Efficiency advocates warn of consumer harm

  Andrew deLaski, executive director of the Appliance Standards Awareness Project, said the proposed rule would "create hurdles" to updating standards and described the package as an "obstacle course of restrictions" that would prevent DOE from fulfilling its congressional mandate.

  "We have products that keep getting more efficient and we need to embrace these technology advances, not reject them, especially as data centers strain our electric grid," deLaski said in a statement.

  ## Industry backs the proposal

  The Association of Home Appliance Manufacturers (AHAM) said it supports DOE's proposed changes to the process rule, which governs how efficiency standards are set and updated. AHAM President and CEO Kelly Mariotti said the existing process rule, because it is administered by DOE rather than enshrined in statute, has shifted from administration to administration, creating uncertainty that complicates manufacturers' planning and investment decisions.

  Mariotti called on Congress to go further: "The natural next step is for Congress to act — to amend the Energy Policy and Conservation Act and lock these reforms into statute, so that the progress made here endures."

  ## Broader regulatory context

  The NOPR is part of a broader set of actions by the Trump administration targeting appliance efficiency policy. The administration has also moved to close the Energy Star program, a voluntary labeling initiative that helps consumers identify energy-efficient products, and recently ended a rebate program that encouraged consumers to switch from gas-powered to electric appliances, according to Utility Dive.

  Efficiency advocates characterize these actions as measures that harm consumers while being presented as expanding consumer choice.

  ## What comes next

  DOE will accept public comments on the proposed rule for 30 days after it is published in the Federal Register. The agency also issued a separate request for information seeking comment on the methodologies used in developing energy conservation standards, with a 60-day comment window after Federal Register publication.

  ## 美能源部提案永久廢除家電能效標準強制規定

  美國能源部（DOE）週四發布「擬議規則制定通知」（NOPR），聲稱此舉將「永久終止家用電器與設備的強制規定」，矛頭直指該機構現行的「家電與設備標準計畫」。該計畫依國會授權，為燈泡、洗衣機、暖氣爐等各類消費性產品設定最低能源效率標準，並定期更新。據《Utility Dive》報導，這項提案是否會全面廢止該計畫，目前仍不明朗。

  ## 擬議規則將改變哪些內容

  此規則將修改能源部的測試程序，以及能源節約標準的制定流程。規則新增「顯著節能」的定義，作為設定標準的門檻要求，並納入若干經濟門檻條件，規定新法規須符合這些條件方能生效。

  能源部表示，這些變更將「保障美國人民自由選擇各式商品與家電的權利」，並將「促進製造業與家電產業的市場競爭與創新」。

  能源部助理部長奧德莉．羅伯森（Audrey Robertson）在聲明中指出，這項提案「將確保新法規促進負擔能力、維護消費者選擇，並符合最高標準的透明度與盡職調查要求」。

  ## 能效倡議人士警告將損害消費者利益

  「家電標準意識計畫」（Appliance Standards Awareness Project）執行長安德魯．德拉斯基（Andrew deLaski）表示，這項擬議規則將在更新標準的過程中「設置重重障礙」，並將整套方案形容為「限制重重的障礙賽」，恐使能源部無法履行國會授予的職責。

  德拉斯基在聲明中說：「我們的產品持續變得更加節能，我們應該擁抱這些技術進步，而非加以排斥——尤其是在資料中心正為電網帶來沉重壓力的當下。」

  ## 產業界支持此提案

  「家用電器製造商協會」（AHAM）表示，支持能源部對程序規則所提出的修改方向，該程序規則規範了效率標準的制定與更新方式。AHAM總裁暨執行長凱莉．馬里奧蒂（Kelly Mariotti）指出，現行程序規則由能源部管理而非明文入法，導致其內容隨政府更迭而改變，形成不確定性，進而使製造商在規劃與投資決策上倍感困難。

  馬里奧蒂呼籲國會進一步採取行動：「下一步理所當然是由國會出手——修訂《能源政策與節約法》，將這些改革明文入法，使此次進展得以長久延續。」

  ## 更廣泛的監管背景

  此次NOPR是川普政府針對家電能效政策一系列行動的一部分。據《Utility Dive》報導，川普政府還著手關閉「能源之星」（Energy Star）計畫——這項自願性標章計畫協助消費者辨別節能產品——並於近期終止了一項鼓勵消費者從燃氣設備換用電氣設備的退稅補助計畫。

  能效倡議人士將上述舉措定性為以「擴大消費者選擇」為名、實則損害消費者利益的措施。

  ## 後續進展

  能源部將在擬議規則刊登於《聯邦公報》後，開放為期三十天的公眾意見徵集。此外，能源部另行發布一份「資訊徵詢」（Request for Information），就制定能源節約標準所採用的方法論徵求意見，徵集期限為刊登《聯邦公報》後六十天。
