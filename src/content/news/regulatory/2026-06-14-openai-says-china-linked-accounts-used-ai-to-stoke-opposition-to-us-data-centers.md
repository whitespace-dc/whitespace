body_en: |
  OpenAI said Wednesday that a cluster of ChatGPT accounts with ties to China attempted to manufacture domestic opposition to data center construction in the United States, in what the company characterized as an effort to undermine American competitiveness in artificial intelligence.

  The company published a report describing the campaign, which it named "Data Center Bandwagon." According to OpenAI, the accounts used AI to generate English-language social media posts asserting that data centers drive up electricity costs for American residents.

  ## What OpenAI found

  OpenAI said the accounts are believed to be connected to an unnamed Chinese private technology company that serves provincial government clients in China. The company did not disclose how many accounts were involved or which social media platforms were targeted.

  The posts were designed to impersonate ordinary American residents and inject contested, AI-generated content into public debate, OpenAI said. The company acknowledged the operation's reach was limited but said the activity nonetheless warrants concern about foreign attempts to weaken a strategically important US industry.

  ## Context: legitimate debate versus foreign manipulation

  OpenAI drew a distinction between authentic domestic discussion and coordinated foreign interference. The company said there is "legitimate debate" in the United States over AI infrastructure and data centers, but argued these accounts sought to manipulate that conversation by disguising their origin and using synthetic content.

  Data center construction has become a focus of US policy as demand for AI computing capacity grows. Concerns about the electricity consumption of large-scale data centers are a real and active policy discussion in the United States, which the campaign appears designed to exploit.

  OpenAI said the limited spread of the posts means the operation had little measurable impact, but framed its disclosure as a warning about the broader pattern of foreign actors attempting to influence American infrastructure policy through coordinated inauthentic behavior.

  OpenAI發布了一份報告，詳細描述這場被命名為「資料中心搭便車」（Data Center Bandwagon）的行動。根據報告內容，這批帳號利用AI生成英文社群媒體貼文，聲稱資料中心導致美國居民電費上漲。

  ## OpenAI的調查發現

  OpenAI表示，這批帳號被認為與一家服務中國地方省級政府客戶的中國民營科技公司有所關聯，但該公司並未公開其名稱。OpenAI亦未透露涉及的帳號數量，也未說明具體針對哪些社群媒體平台。

  這些貼文的設計意圖，是假冒普通美國居民的身分，將具有爭議性的AI生成內容注入公共輿論之中。OpenAI承認，此次行動的傳播範圍有限，但表示這些活動仍足以引發外界對境外勢力試圖削弱美國戰略性重要產業的疑慮。

  ## 正當辯論與境外操弄的界線

  OpenAI區分了真實的國內討論與有組織的境外干預之間的差異。該公司表示，美國社會對AI基礎設施與資料中心確實存在「正當的辯論」空間，但認為這批帳號試圖透過隱瞞帳號來源、使用合成內容的方式操弄輿論走向。

  隨著AI運算需求持續攀升，資料中心的建設已成為美國政策關注的焦點。大規模資料中心的用電量問題，在美國政策圈本就是真實且持續進行中的討論議題，而此次行動似乎正是刻意利用了這一社會背景。

  OpenAI表示，由於貼文擴散範圍有限，此次行動幾乎未造成可量測的實際影響，但該公司將此次揭露定位為一項警示，提醒各界留意境外行為者試圖透過協同造假行為來影響美國基礎設施政策的更廣泛模式。
