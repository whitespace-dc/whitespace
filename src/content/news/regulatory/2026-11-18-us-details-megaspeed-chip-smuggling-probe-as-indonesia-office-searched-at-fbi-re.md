body_en: |
  The US Departments of Commerce and Justice issued a joint statement Monday, November 16, detailing their investigation into Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud computing company accused of channeling at least $7.5 billion worth of restricted Nvidia artificial-intelligence chips to China, as Indonesian police separately searched the firm's local office at FBI request and found unopened server racks but no graphics processing units (GPUs).

  The two agencies said they released the timeline in the public interest to correct inaccurate information circulating in news coverage, without identifying which specific reports they considered erroneous. Both agencies acknowledged they would not ordinarily comment on a continuing investigation.

  ## Alleged diversion route

  Megaspeed is accused of routing restricted Nvidia chips through its Malaysian subsidiary, Speedmatrix, to supply data centers operated by Shanghai Century Litong in Shanghai and Shenzhen. The chips involved include Nvidia's Blackwell and Rubin series, which the US government bars from export to China.

  ## Personal connection to Nvidia's co-founder

  The case has drawn wider attention because of reported ties between Megaspeed and Jensen Huang, co-founder of Nvidia. US media outlets have reported that Jensen Huang had a personal relationship with Alice Huang, Megaspeed's former chief executive. Some US authorities are reported to suspect Alice Huang has connections to Chinese intelligence, an allegation that has not been verified.

  ## How the investigation developed

  The US Commerce Department began investigating Megaspeed in 2024 after detecting suspicious activity through monitoring for potential semiconductor diversion, according to the joint statement. Singapore police opened a parallel inquiry in February 2025.

  In May 2025, investigators from the Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS), the Commerce Department unit responsible for export controls, requested records from Nvidia covering both direct and indirect sales to Megaspeed. The following month, Nvidia placed Megaspeed on a "no sell" list, barring further chip sales to the firm. BIS agents visited Megaspeed in June 2025 alongside Singapore law enforcement to conduct an end-use check, a physical inspection verifying that exported goods are being used as declared, and interviewed Alice Huang, who was then serving as chief executive.

  Following that inspection, the Justice Department assumed lead responsibility for the criminal investigation; the joint statement does not specify when the handover occurred. Megaspeed said Alice Huang formally left the company on August 30, 2025.

  ## Indonesia search yields no GPUs

  Indonesian police searched Megaspeed's Indonesian office at FBI request, Kontan reported. Officers found a number of unopened server racks on the premises but did not locate any GPUs.

  ## Multi-jurisdictional scope

  The investigation has drawn in agencies from three countries alongside BIS, the Justice Department, and the FBI. The alleged scheme routed chips through Speedmatrix in Malaysia before reaching Chinese data centers, spanning at least four jurisdictions in total. The governments of the United States and Singapore have agreed to continue discussions on the case, Kontan reported.

  美國商務部與司法部於11月16日（週一）聯合發布聲明，詳述對總部位於新加坡的雲端運算業者Megaspeed的調查——該公司被指控透過旗下馬來西亞子公司，將至少75億美元的受限輝達人工智慧晶片轉運至中國。與此同時，印尼警方也應FBI要求搜查該公司當地辦公室，現場發現多個未拆封的伺服器機架，但未查獲任何顯示卡（GPU）。

  兩部門表示，發布此一時間軸係基於公眾利益，旨在更正新聞報導中流傳的不實資訊，但未指明具體認定有誤的報導為何。兩機構均承認，在正常情況下，不會就進行中的調查發表評論。

  ## 涉嫌轉運路線

  Megaspeed被指控透過其馬來西亞子公司Speedmatrix，將受限輝達晶片轉運至上海世紀立通在上海及深圳營運的資料中心。涉案晶片包括輝達的Blackwell及Rubin系列，美國政府明令禁止將上述晶片出口至中國。

  ## 與輝達共同創辦人的私人淵源

  此案引發廣泛關注，部分原因在於Megaspeed據報與輝達共同創辦人黃仁勳之間存在關聯。美國媒體報導，黃仁勳與Megaspeed前執行長Alice Huang有私人關係。部分美國當局據報懷疑Alice Huang與中國情報機構有所往來，惟此一指控尚未獲得證實。

  ## 調查進展始末

  根據聯合聲明，美國商務部於2024年在監控半導體潛在轉運活動時偵測到可疑動態，隨即對Megaspeed展開調查。新加坡警方則於2025年2月展開平行調查。

  2025年5月，負責出口管制的商務部下設機構——工業暨安全局（BIS）的調查人員，向輝達調取涵蓋直接及間接對Megaspeed銷售的相關紀錄。同年6月，輝達將Megaspeed列入「禁售」名單，禁止向該公司進一步出售晶片。BIS人員亦於同月偕同新加坡執法人員前往Megaspeed，進行最終用途查核——即實地核查出口貨物是否依申報用途使用——並約談時任執行長Alice Huang。

  完成前述查核後，司法部接手主導刑事調查，惟聯合聲明未說明移交的確切時間點。Megaspeed表示，Alice Huang已於2025年8月30日正式離職。

  ## 印尼搜查未發現顯示卡

  據印尼財經媒體《Kontan》報導，印尼警方應FBI要求搜查Megaspeed的印尼辦公室，現場發現多個未拆封的伺服器機架，但未查獲任何顯示卡。

  ## 跨國調查規模

  此次調查除涉及BIS、司法部及FBI外，更牽動三國執法機關共同參與。據稱，涉案晶片先經由馬來西亞的Speedmatrix轉運，再流入中國資料中心，整體涉及至少四個司法管轄區。據《Kontan》報導，美國與新加坡政府已同意就本案持續進行溝通討論。
