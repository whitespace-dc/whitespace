body_en: |
  Taiwan Power Company said July 15 it may conditionally end a nearly three-year freeze on electricity connections for large data centers in northern Taiwan, potentially reopening the region to AI infrastructure investment that has been effectively blocked since September 2023.

  Taipower Chairman Tseng Wen-sheng made the announcement while appearing before the Legislative Yuan's Economics Committee during budget hearings. He said the cities of Taoyuan and Keelung could have the restriction lifted if their municipal governments designate AI data center zones close to existing power plants, enabling Taipower to supply power to those facilities directly from adjacent generation assets.

  The freeze, imposed in September 2023, blocked new electricity applications from data centers consuming 5 megawatts (MW) or more north of Taoyuan. Tseng offered a scale reference: Taipower's own two-building headquarters consumes roughly 4 MW, and the entire Shin Kong Mitsukoshi department store tower adjacent to Taipei Main Station consumes approximately 5 MW. At that level of demand, a single large cloud or AI computing facility represents a substantial draw on the regional transmission network.

  ## Why Taipower imposed the ban

  Tseng said a 2023 Taipower supply planning meeting resolved the restriction and notified industry operators at the time. The northern grid faced two compounding problems: large-scale cloud service providers had concentrated north of Taoyuan, and the region's planned additional generation capacity had not yet come online. Datan power plant units 7, 8, and 9 were still under construction, and the planned reconstruction of the Hoping power plant had not passed its environmental impact assessment. Taipower imposed the freeze to prevent large operators from reserving grid capacity ahead of actual use — a practice officials described as "virtual grid occupation" — and to avoid aggravating congestion on northern transmission infrastructure.

  Tseng drew a distinction between two categories of user. Corporations running AI computing internally present a manageable load, he said. Aggregated, leasable AI data centers — such as those operated by cloud service providers — present a larger grid-management challenge because demand is harder to forecast and control.

  ## What changed to make a lifting possible

  Two developments have altered the northern supply picture, according to Tseng. Datan units 7, 8, and 9 are now operational and accepted into the dispatch system, adding generation capacity to the region. The Hoping power plant reconstruction, which had been pending regulatory clearance, has since passed its environmental review and begun construction, giving planners greater confidence in the northern supply trajectory.

  Economy Minister Kung Ming-hsin visited Taoyuan and met with Mayor Chang Shan-cheng before the July 15 announcement. Following that exchange, Kung said the relaxation of the supply restriction was "basically not a problem," Commercial Times reported.

  ## Conditions Taipower has set

  Taipower has attached a siting condition to any relief from the freeze. Taoyuan and Keelung must propose formal plans to establish AI data center zones in proximity to power plants. If municipal governments submit such plans, Taipower said it would cooperate with them to develop the zones and provide power supply. Tseng noted the requirement reflects a standard that has emerged internationally: new data centers abroad are now generally expected to locate near generation assets or to arrange their own power supply.

  Tseng also noted that Taipower, as a state-owned enterprise, carries a legal obligation to supply electricity, which constrains its ability to refuse large-scale users on an indefinite basis.

  ## What it means for northern Taiwan's AI buildout

  The northern corridor — encompassing Taipei, New Taipei, Taoyuan, and Keelung — holds the country's densest concentration of technology industry and cloud infrastructure. The three-year ban has effectively directed large-scale data center development away from that region. A conditional reopening tied to proximity to generation assets would mark a structural shift in how Taipower manages large industrial power requests, coupling supply commitments to specific geographic and infrastructure conditions rather than issuing broad restrictions.

  The next formal step rests with the Taoyuan and Keelung city governments, whose AI data center zone proposals would trigger Taipower's stated cooperation framework.

  ## 台電擬有條件解除北部大型資料中心限電令

  台灣電力公司15日表示，可能有條件終止實施近三年、針對台灣北部大型資料中心的電力申請凍結，為自2023年9月起實質上遭到封鎖的AI基礎建設投資重新打開大門。

  台電董事長曾文生在出席立法院經濟委員會預算審查時作出上述宣示。他表示，若桃園市與基隆市政府能在現有電廠附近正式劃設AI資料中心專區，台電即可配合解除限制，並由鄰近的發電機組直接供電給這些設施。

  此一凍結令自2023年9月起實施，禁止桃園以北地區、用電量達5百萬瓦（MW）以上的資料中心提出新的供電申請。曾文生以具體數字說明這個用電規模：台電自家兩棟辦公大樓的用電量約為4MW，而緊鄰台北車站的新光三越百貨大樓用電量則約為5MW。在這樣的需求量級下，單一大型雲端或AI運算機房對北部區域輸電網路的衝擊相當可觀。

  ## 台電當初為何祭出禁令

  曾文生表示，2023年台電供電規劃會議決議實施此一限制，並已在當時知會業界。北部電網面臨兩項相互疊加的問題：大型雲端服務業者大量集中於桃園以北，而原訂補充該區域供電能力的新增機組卻尚未上線。大潭電廠7、8、9號機仍在興建中，和平電廠的重建計畫也尚未通過環境影響評估。台電祭出凍結令，目的在於防止大型業者搶先預占備而不用的電網容量——官員稱此種行為為「虛占電網容量」——並避免進一步加劇北部輸電網路的壅塞情形。

  曾文生也區分了兩類用電主體的差異。他表示，企業自用的AI運算負載尚屬可控；然而可對外出租的聚合型AI資料中心——例如雲端服務業者所營運的機房——對電網管理構成更大挑戰，因為這類需求難以預測與調度。

  ## 哪些變化讓解禁成為可能

  曾文生指出，北部供電情勢已因兩項新發展而出現轉機。大潭電廠7、8、9號機目前已完工並納入調度系統，為北部地區新增了發電容量。原本等待主管機關核准的和平電廠重建計畫，也已通過環境影響評估並動工興建，讓供電規劃人員對北部供電前景更具信心。

  在15日宣示前，經濟部長郭智輝已赴桃園拜會市長張善政。《工商時報》報導，郭智輝在會面後表示，鬆綁供電限制「基本上沒有問題」。

  ## 台電所附加的條件

  台電對此次解禁設有選址條件。桃園市與基隆市須提出正式計畫，在鄰近電廠的地區劃設AI資料中心專區。若市政府提出此類計畫，台電表示將與地方合作共同發展園區並提供電力供應。曾文生指出，這項要求呼應了國際間已逐漸形成的標準：海外新建資料中心如今普遍被期待設置於發電設施附近，或自行安排電源。

  曾文生也提到，台電身為國營事業，依法負有供電義務，這也在一定程度上限制了台電無限期拒絕大型用電戶的空間。

  ## 對北台灣AI建設的意義

  北部廊道涵蓋台北市、新北市、桃園市與基隆市，集中了全台密度最高的科技產業與雲端基礎設施。三年來的禁令已實質上將大型資料中心的開發需求導向其他地區。此次有條件的解禁若與鄰近發電設施的選址要求掛鉤，將標誌著台電在管理大型工業用電申請方式上的結構性轉變——從過去的全面限制，轉向以具體地理條件與基礎設施條件作為供電承諾的前提。

  下一步的關鍵，在於桃園市與基隆市政府。兩市若提出AI資料中心專區計畫，即可啟動台電所宣示的合作機制。
