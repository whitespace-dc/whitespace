  ## Indonesia drafts presidential regulation to push data center growth into eastern regions

  Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital (Komdigi) is finalizing a presidential regulation — known locally as a Perpres — that would govern data center development and submarine cable systems, with the explicit aim of distributing new facilities into eastern Indonesia rather than concentrating them in the country's established western hubs.

  Wayan Toni Supriyanto, Director General of Digital Infrastructure at Komdigi, said the draft regulation has completed a public consultation round and continues to collect input from industry, regional governments, and private companies.

  "We need to know how to push data centers in the eastern region, not only in the western region like Batam and Java," Wayan said, according to Bisnis.com. "We prioritize certain areas by combining them with other zones, such as government, industrial, education, and public service areas."

  ## Connectivity as a condition for viability

  Wayan identified isolation as the central risk for any eastern data center strategy. Komdigi intends to guarantee connectivity links between facilities built outside Java and Batam and both national and international activity centers, he said, so that those facilities do not operate as stranded assets.

  "We also provide connectivity to national and international activity centers for data centers outside Java and Batam so that the facility does not become an isolated facility," Wayan said. "Thus, we can provide redundant paths and build a resilient data center network in Indonesia."

  Specific provinces named as candidates for expansion include Sulawesi and Kupang, a city on the island of Timor in East Nusa Tenggara province. Wayan noted that each location carries distinct infrastructure characteristics that require separate planning for power density and water supply.

  ## Energy and permitting for AI workloads

  Komdigi is coordinating across government agencies on power supply, land availability, and permitting to support artificial intelligence computing, which the ministry characterized as power-intensive. Wayan said scalable, lower-emission electricity is a prerequisite.

  "This depends on power supply that can be scaled up and is increasingly low-emission. That power supply is critical for AI's power-intensive needs," he said.

  The ministry also flagged backbone and access network capacity as an area requiring urgent attention as both global and domestic demand for AI computing rises. "In the digital sector, we can no longer experience delays. Connectivity capacity also must not decrease," Wayan said.

  ## Regulatory process and stakeholders

  The submarine cable regulation is being developed alongside the data center Perpres as a paired instrument. Wayan said the drafting process requires input from industry operators, regional authorities, state-owned enterprises, network operators, and investors, and that infrastructure development must be planned collaboratively rather than in isolation.

  The regulation, once finalized, would represent a formal policy instrument directing where and under what conditions large-scale digital infrastructure can be built in Indonesia. Public consultation has concluded, but the timeline for presidential signature was not disclosed.

  ## 印尼起草總統令，推動資料中心向東部地區擴展

  印尼通訊暨數位部（Komdigi）正在敲定一項總統令（印尼稱為Perpres），規範資料中心開發及海底電纜系統，明確目標是將新設施引導至印尼東部地區，而非繼續集中於西部既有樞紐。

  Komdigi數位基礎建設總司長瓦揚·托尼·蘇普里揚托（Wayan Toni Supriyanto）表示，該草案已完成一輪公開徵詢，目前仍持續收集來自產業界、地方政府及私人企業的意見。

  「我們需要思考如何將資料中心推向東部地區，而不是只集中在巴淡島（Batam）和爪哇（Java）等西部地區，」瓦揚向Bisnis.com表示。「我們結合政府、工業、教育與公共服務等區域，優先推動特定地區的發展。」

  ## 連網能力是東部布局能否成功的關鍵

  瓦揚指出，與外界隔絕是推動東部資料中心戰略最核心的風險。Komdigi計劃確保在爪哇和巴淡島以外興建的設施，都能與國內外重要活動中心維持連線，避免這些設施淪為孤立的閒置資產。

  「我們也為爪哇和巴淡島以外的資料中心提供連接至國內外活動中心的網路，讓這些設施不致成為孤立設施，」瓦揚說。「如此一來，我們便能提供備援路徑，在印尼建立具韌性的資料中心網路。」

  規劃中的擴展地點包括蘇拉威西（Sulawesi），以及位於東努沙登加拉省（East Nusa Tenggara）帝汶島（Timor）上的古邦（Kupang）市。瓦揚指出，各地點的基礎建設條件各有差異，必須針對電力密度與水源供應分別規劃。

  ## 人工智慧工作負載的能源供應與法規許可

  Komdigi正跨部會協調電力供應、土地取得及法規許可等事宜，以支援人工智慧運算需求——該部將其定性為高耗電的應用場景。瓦揚表示，可彈性擴充且低排放的電力是不可或缺的前提條件。

  「這有賴於能夠持續擴容、且排放量日益降低的電力供應。這樣的電力供應對人工智慧的高耗電需求至關重要，」他說。

  隨著全球及國內對人工智慧運算的需求持續攀升，該部也將骨幹網路及接取網路容量列為迫切需要關注的課題。「在數位領域，我們已無法承受任何延誤。連網容量也絕不能縮減，」瓦揚說。

  ## 法規制定程序與利害關係人

  海底電纜法規正與資料中心總統令同步研擬，作為配套規範。瓦揚表示，草擬過程需要產業業者、地方主管機關、國營企業、網路業者及投資人共同參與，基礎建設的發展必須以協作方式規劃，而非各自為政。

  這項法規一旦完成，將成為一份正式政策文件，規範印尼大規模數位基礎建設的建設地點與許可條件。目前公開徵詢已告一段落，但總統簽署的時間表尚未對外公布。
