body_en: |
  Australia's federal government will introduce legislation requiring data centers to meet mandatory national standards on electricity and water consumption, Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced Tuesday, framing the move as necessary to attract large-scale technology investment without driving up household energy bills or straining water supplies.

  Albanese was set to outline the proposed obligations at a meeting of all state and territory leaders on Wednesday, according to excerpts of his speech released by his office. The centre-left government is also planning minimum standards covering copyright, security, and safety for the training of artificial intelligence models.

  ## What the standards would require

  The government has not yet detailed the specific metrics the legislation would impose, but Albanese said the framework would ensure "energy bills do not rise, water security is maintained and locations are appropriate." The announcement follows remarks earlier in August by Energy Minister Chris Bowen, who said the government would not allow "a race to the bottom" in which states and territories compete to attract data center investment at the cost of household electricity bills, grid reliability, or emissions targets.

  Bowen, speaking Sunday, dismissed opposition from the state of Queensland, saying that "with the right rules in place, you can actually get the investment but actually get to a good outcome where those data centres need to bring their own renewable energy and can back it up with gas peaking."

  ## Social licence and federal-state tension

  Albanese told the business community that Australia is "in a strong position to attract investment in data centres, but maintaining social licence will be essential" to achieving that goal. The phrase social licence refers to public acceptance of an industry's operations — a concern that arises as data centers, which consume large volumes of electricity and water for cooling, expand rapidly alongside AI workloads.

  Bowen's public pushback against Queensland signals potential friction between the federal government, which is setting the national floor, and individual states that have discretion over land use and utility policy. By legislating a national standard, Canberra aims to prevent states from undercutting each other on environmental and grid-impact requirements to win investment.

  ## Significance for the industry

  Mandatory national standards would represent a significant shift for Australia's data center sector, replacing a patchwork of voluntary commitments and state-level rules with enforceable federal requirements. Operators seeking to build or expand facilities in Australia would face a uniform baseline on energy sourcing and water use, with Bowen's comments indicating the government expects large facilities to procure their own renewable energy and use gas-fired generation only as backup capacity.

  Albanese framed the regulatory push as compatible with economic growth, saying "Australia can do more to unlock frontier AI development in Australia" while shaping the transition to "harness the positive opportunities while minimising the downsides."

  The government's plans were scheduled to be formally presented to state and territory leaders at Wednesday's meeting.

  ## 澳洲擬立法規範資料中心用電與用水標準

  澳洲聯邦政府宣布將推動立法，要求資料中心必須符合全國強制性電力與用水標準。總理艾班尼斯（Anthony Albanese）週二發表這項聲明，將此舉定調為吸引大規模科技投資、同時防止民眾電費上漲或水資源供應吃緊的必要措施。

  根據總理辦公室提前公開的演講摘錄，艾班尼斯預定在週三召開的全國各州及領地領袖峰會上，正式說明相關義務框架。這個中間偏左的執政政府同時計畫針對人工智慧模型的訓練，訂定涵蓋著作權、資安與安全性的最低標準。

  ## 標準的具體要求

  政府目前尚未公布立法的具體量化指標，但艾班尼斯表示，這套框架將確保「電費不會上漲、水資源安全獲得維護，且選址地點適當」。此次宣示呼應了能源部長鮑文（Chris Bowen）在八月稍早的談話——他表示政府不會允許各州及領地為搶奪資料中心投資而競相壓低標準，犧牲家庭電費、電網穩定性或減碳目標。

  鮑文週日駁斥昆士蘭州的反對立場，指出「只要有適當規則，確實可以兼顧吸引投資與達成良好結果——讓那些資料中心自行帶來再生能源，並以天然氣調峰機組作為備援。」

  ## 社會認可度與聯邦和州之間的張力

  艾班尼斯向商界表示，澳洲「在吸引資料中心投資方面處於有利地位，但要達成這個目標，維持社會認可度至關重要」。所謂「社會認可度」，指的是社會大眾對某一產業運作的接受程度。隨著人工智慧工作負載快速擴張，大量消耗電力與冷卻用水的資料中心急速增長，這正成為一項不容忽視的議題。

  鮑文公開點名批評昆士蘭州，顯示聯邦政府與各州之間潛在的摩擦——聯邦負責訂定全國底線，各州則在土地利用與公用事業政策上保有自主裁量空間。透過聯邦立法確立全國統一標準，坎培拉意在防止各州在環境與電網衝擊要求上相互競相壓低，以此搶奪投資。

  ## 對產業的重要意義

  強制性全國標準將為澳洲資料中心產業帶來重大轉變，以可執行的聯邦規範取代目前拼湊而成的自願性承諾與各州規定。計畫在澳洲興建或擴建設施的業者，將面臨統一的能源來源與用水基準；鮑文的談話也顯示，政府期望大型設施自行採購再生能源，僅以燃氣發電作為備援容量。

  艾班尼斯將此波監管推進定調為與經濟成長並行不悖，表示「澳洲可以採取更多行動，在境內解鎖前沿人工智慧的發展」，同時引導這場轉型「善用正面機遇，同時將負面影響降至最低」。

  政府的相關計畫預定在週三的州及領地領袖峰會上正式提出。
