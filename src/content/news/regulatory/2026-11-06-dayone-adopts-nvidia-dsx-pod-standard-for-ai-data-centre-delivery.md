body_en: |
  DayOne, the Singapore-based data centre operator, has committed to supporting NVIDIA's DSX POD reference design, a technical standard intended to align compute, power and cooling requirements for artificial intelligence infrastructure deployments.

  The DSX POD reference design is an NVIDIA-developed framework meant to bring facility and computing specifications into closer coordination so customers can deploy AI systems more quickly. DayOne said it will deploy the standard alongside its own integrated delivery model, which centres on the modular prefabrication of power, cooling and base-build systems.

  ## DayOne's delivery model

  DayOne said its construction method relocates a portion of mechanical, electrical and plumbing work from construction sites into factory settings before components are shipped to a project location. The company said this reduces development timelines and improves consistency across projects in different markets. It said the approach enables data centre builds to be completed in eight to 12 months in Southeast Asia, which it described as shorter than conventional construction cycles.

  Jamie Khoo, Chief Executive Officer of DayOne, said the adoption of the NVIDIA standard was a natural fit with its existing construction approach. "At DayOne, we believe that prefabrication and modularization are key to delivering infrastructure at the pace required by the AI era," Khoo said. "Our teams have spent years developing integrated platforms, products, and modules that enable us to deliver large-scale campuses at speed and scale, with the consistency and quality our customers trust. Supporting the new NVIDIA DSX POD reference design is a natural extension of that work."

  ## NVIDIA's position

  NVIDIA framed the reference design as a response to the growing complexity of AI infrastructure requirements. AI facilities typically require more tightly integrated electricity supply, cooling, networking and computing hardware than traditional data centres, placing greater demands on coordination between facility engineers and equipment suppliers.

  Charlie Boyle, Vice President of DGX Systems at NVIDIA, said the arrangement was designed to accelerate deployments for end customers. "Meeting growing demand for accelerated computing requires integrated infrastructure solutions, and the NVIDIA DSX POD reference design helps align compute, power, and cooling for faster deployment," Boyle said. "DayOne's support for this reference design, combined with its modular infrastructure and integrated delivery capabilities, can help customers bring capacity online sooner and shorten the time to first token."

  ## DayOne's footprint

  DayOne was established in 2022 and operates across 10 markets in Asia Pacific and Europe. Its locations include Singapore; Johor and Greater Kuala Lumpur in Malaysia; Indonesia; Thailand; Tokyo and Kyushu in Japan; Hong Kong; Finland; and Spain. The company develops and operates high-capacity campuses for hyperscalers and large enterprises through a common delivery framework applied across jurisdictions.

  ## Industry significance

  The announcement reflects a broader shift in how data centre operators are responding to AI workloads. As AI deployments have grown larger and more complex, operators have faced increased pressure to deliver power, cooling and physical space on compressed timelines while maintaining consistent technical standards across multiple sites. Repeatable designs and standardised construction methods have become a more significant competitive factor in the market.

  DayOne said modular infrastructure will play a key role in delivering what it described as next-generation data centre capacity as AI requirements intensify. The company's adoption of the NVIDIA DSX POD standard extends its standardised delivery framework across its 10-market footprint.

  新加坡資料中心業者DayOne宣布，將全面支援輝達（NVIDIA）所推出的DSX POD參考設計——這套技術標準旨在整合人工智慧（AI）基礎設施部署所需的運算、電力與冷卻規格，使各項要求得以緊密協調配合。

  DSX POD參考設計是輝達所研發的技術框架，目的在於讓設施規格與運算規格達到更高度的協調，以便客戶更迅速地部署AI系統。DayOne表示，將在旗下既有的整合交付模式框架下導入這套標準，整合交付模式以電力、冷卻與基礎建設系統的模組化預製為核心。

  ## DayOne的交付模式

  DayOne表示，其施工方式是在元件運抵工地之前，先將部分機電管路工程移至工廠環境中完成。該公司表示，這種做法能縮短專案開發時程，並提升不同市場專案之間的一致性。在東南亞地區，這套方式可將資料中心的建置工期壓縮至八至十二個月，DayOne形容這比傳統施工週期更短。

  DayOne執行長Jamie Khoo表示，採用輝達這套標準與公司既有的施工方式不謀而合。「在DayOne，我們相信預製化與模組化是以AI時代所需速度交付基礎設施的關鍵，」Khoo說。「我們的團隊多年來持續打造整合平台、產品與模組，使我們得以高速且大規模地交付大型園區，並以客戶所信賴的一致性與品質完成任務。支援輝達全新的DSX POD參考設計，是這項工作的自然延伸。」

  ## 輝達的立場

  輝達將這套參考設計定位為因應AI基礎設施需求日益複雜所提出的解決方案。AI設施通常需要比傳統資料中心更緊密整合的供電、冷卻、網路與運算硬體，對設施工程師與設備供應商之間的協調要求也更為嚴苛。

  輝達DGX系統事業副總裁Charlie Boyle表示，這項合作安排旨在加速終端客戶的部署進程。「滿足加速運算日益增長的需求，有賴於整合性的基礎設施解決方案，而輝達DSX POD參考設計有助於整合運算、電力與冷卻，實現更迅速的部署，」Boyle說。「DayOne對這套參考設計的支持，加上其模組化基礎設施與整合交付能力，可協助客戶提早讓容量上線，並縮短首次生成推論（time to first token）的等待時間。」

  ## DayOne的市場版圖

  DayOne成立於2022年，業務橫跨亞太地區與歐洲共十個市場，據點涵蓋新加坡、馬來西亞柔佛及大吉隆坡地區、印尼、泰國、日本東京及九州、香港、芬蘭，以及西班牙。該公司透過一套跨轄區通用的交付框架，為超大規模雲端業者及大型企業開發並營運高容量園區。

  ## 產業意義

  此次宣布反映出資料中心業者在應對AI工作負載方面更廣泛的轉型趨勢。隨著AI部署規模不斷擴大、複雜度持續提升，業者面臨更大的壓力，必須在壓縮的時程內交付電力、冷卻與實體空間，同時維持多個站點之間一致的技術標準。可重複使用的設計與標準化施工方式，已成為市場競爭中愈來愈重要的差異化因素。

  DayOne表示，隨著AI需求持續強化，模組化基礎設施將在交付該公司所稱的次世代資料中心容量方面扮演關鍵角色。採用輝達DSX POD標準，也讓DayOne的標準化交付框架得以延伸覆蓋至旗下十個市場的整體版圖。
