body_en: |
  The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) on July 2, 2026, denied a waiver request from Advanced Power Services that would have allowed the company to downsize its roughly $2 billion Chestnut Run natural gas plant in Carroll County, Ohio, ruling that granting the exemption would harm other developers seeking grid connection in PJM Interconnection's queue.

  The Chestnut Run project is enrolled in PJM's Reliability Resource Initiative (RRI), a fast-track interconnection review process reserved for shovel-ready projects intended to address near-term grid reliability needs. The RRI prohibits changes to a project's size and capacity interconnection rights — the rules from which Advanced Power sought relief.

  Advanced Power, a Boston-based independent power producer owned by ArcLight Capital Partners, said it could not acquire the turbines it had originally planned to use. Alternate turbines were available, but substituting them would alter the plant's configuration, requiring the company to reduce its maximum output by 55 megawatts (MW) to 1.245 gigawatts (GW) — enough to power roughly one million homes — and to reduce its capacity interconnection rights accordingly.

  ## Why FERC refused

  FERC denied the waiver on one of its standard criteria: that exemptions from commission-approved tariff rules must not harm third parties. The agency sided with PJM's argument that permitting the reconfiguration would trigger new interconnection studies whose effects would cascade across the review schedule.

  "Studies resulting from the equipment changes would introduce substantial delays to Transition Cycle #2 and have a ripple effect on the remainder of PJM's [interconnection study] cycle schedule," FERC said in its decision.

  Transition Cycle 2 is the final phase of PJM's interconnection reform transition, in which RRI projects are currently under review. It is scheduled to conclude early next year with the execution of interconnection agreements. PJM spokesman Jeffrey Shields said the cycle faces a decision point this week, when some project developers may choose to withdraw their projects from the process.

  Advanced Power did not respond to a request for comment.

  ## What remains in the RRI

  The RRI initially covered 51 projects totaling 9.3 GW of capacity, according to PJM. As of now, 41 projects representing 7.9 GW of nameplate capacity remain in the process, Shields said.

  ## Broader interconnection context

  The ruling comes as PJM manages a reformed interconnection queue of substantial scale. After clearing a backlog of prior requests, PJM in April 2026 began reviewing applications under its revamped queue process for the first time. That first cycle encompasses 811 generating projects totaling approximately 220 GW — a workload that illustrates why both the grid operator and federal regulators are guarding the integrity of study schedules.

  The decision signals that PJM's reformed interconnection framework will be enforced strictly even when individual projects face genuine supply-chain obstacles. Developers in the RRI and in PJM's broader interconnection queue have limited recourse to seek rule modifications once project configurations are locked in at the time of application.

  The next scheduled milestone for the RRI cohort is the Transition Cycle 2 decision point this week, at which some developers may opt to withdraw before the cycle advances toward interconnection agreements expected early next year.
body_zh: |
  美國聯邦能源監管委員會（FERC）於2026年7月2日，裁定駁回Advanced Power Services提出的豁免申請——該公司原希望獲准縮減其位於俄亥俄州卡羅爾郡、造價約20億美元的Chestnut Run天然氣電廠規模。FERC指出，批准此豁免將損害其他正在PJM電網佇列中申請併網的開發商權益。

  Chestnut Run專案已納入PJM的「可靠性資源倡議」（Reliability Resource Initiative，RRI）——這是一套專為應對短期電網可靠性需求所設、面向已具備動工條件之專案的快速併網審查流程。RRI明確禁止申請人變更專案規模及併網容量權利，而Advanced Power正是尋求豁免於此規定。

  Advanced Power是一家總部位於波士頓的獨立發電業者，隸屬ArcLight Capital Partners旗下。該公司表示，原定採用的渦輪機無法取得；雖有替代機型可供選購，但替換將改變電廠配置，迫使公司將最大輸出容量削減55百萬瓦（MW），降至1.245吉瓦（GW）——相當於約百萬戶家庭的用電需求——並相應壓縮其併網容量權利。

  ## FERC拒絕豁免的理由

  FERC依據其標準審查準則之一裁定駁回：委員會批准之費率規則的豁免，不得損害第三方。委員會支持PJM的立場，認定允許電廠重新配置將觸發新一輪併網研究，進而對整體審查排程產生連鎖影響。

  FERC在裁決中表示：「設備變更所引發的研究，將對過渡循環第二期造成重大延誤，並對PJM其餘〔併網研究〕循環排程產生漣漪效應。」

  過渡循環第二期（Transition Cycle 2）是PJM併網改革過渡階段的最終一環，RRI專案目前正在接受本期審查。依計畫，本循環將於明年初完成，屆時將簽署正式的併網協議。PJM發言人Jeffrey Shields表示，本循環本週即將面臨一個關鍵決策點，屆時部分專案開發商可能選擇退出流程。

  Advanced Power截至截稿前未回應置評請求。

  ## RRI剩餘專案概況

  據PJM統計，RRI最初納入51個專案，總裝置容量達9.3吉瓦（GW）。Shields指出，目前仍有41個專案留在流程中，合計銘牌容量為7.9吉瓦（GW）。

  ## 更廣泛的併網背景

  本裁決發布之際，PJM正在管理一套規模龐大的改革後併網佇列。在清理積壓已久的舊有申請案之後，PJM於2026年4月首度啟動修訂後佇列流程的審查作業；第一個周期涵蓋811個發電專案，總計約220吉瓦（GW）——如此龐大的工作量，正說明了電網調度機構與聯邦監管機關為何對研究排程的嚴謹性高度警惕。

  此次裁決釋出明確訊號：即便個別專案確實面臨供應鏈障礙，PJM改革後的併網框架仍將嚴格執行。在RRI及PJM廣泛併網佇列中的開發商，一旦於申請時確立專案配置，事後尋求修改規則的空間極為有限。

  RRI群組的下一個重要里程碑，便是本週即將到來的過渡循環第二期決策點，部分開發商可能在此時選擇退出；之後，本循環將推進至預計明年初完成的併網協議簽署階段。
