body_en: |
  The Philippines' Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) has proposed maximum bid prices of up to P7.16 per kilowatt-hour (kWh) for the country's first mid-merit natural gas power auction, with the caps varying by plant type and whether the facility already exists or is yet to be built.

  The ERC published a draft resolution setting the gas auction threshold (GAT) prices — the maximum allowable offer a bidder may submit — ahead of a government auction targeting 3,840 megawatts (MW) of mid-merit gas-fired capacity across the Luzon and Visayas grids.

  ## Price caps by plant type

  The proposed thresholds, drawn from the Department of Energy's (DoE) reference parameters and preliminary price calculations, differ significantly depending on the age and configuration of the generating asset:

  - Existing combined-cycle gas turbine (CCGT) facility, 1,400-MW scale, Luzon: **P2.76/kWh**
  - Existing CCGT facility, 500-MW scale: **P2.66/kWh**
  - New-build CCGT plant: **P7.16/kWh**
  - New open-cycle gas turbine (OCGT) plant: **P4.45/kWh**
  - New gas-reciprocating engine facility: **P6.79/kWh**

  A combined-cycle gas turbine captures waste heat from a primary combustion turbine to generate additional electricity, making it more efficient than an open-cycle plant, which uses only the combustion turbine. A gas-reciprocating engine uses pistons rather than turbines and is typically suited to smaller or more flexible capacity.

  The GAT price is designed to cover capital recovery and operations and maintenance costs for each facility type. Fuel commodity costs and capped landed logistics costs are excluded from the threshold and will be recovered separately through a fuel cost pass-through mechanism, the ERC said.

  ## Dispatch assumptions

  The DoE's calculations assume a reference net capacity factor of 42%, which is equivalent to roughly 14 hours of operation per day, for the purpose of determining cost recovery. The ERC noted that this assumption applies only to calculating the auction threshold and does not constitute a guaranteed minimum dispatch or a take-or-pay obligation on the part of off-takers.

  ## Auction scope and timeline

  The government plans to procure a total of 3,840 MW of mid-merit capacity through the auction, split between two grids. Mid-merit generation refers to plants that run more than peak-load units but less than baseload plants, filling demand gaps as electricity use rises and falls through the day.

  Of the total, 2,900 MW is targeted for gas-fired capacity in Luzon, with delivery scheduled to begin as early as this year and extending through 2031. The remaining 940 MW covers the Visayas, specifically the islands of Cebu and Panay, with delivery expected between 2028 and 2031.

  The DoE last month published its list of pre-qualified bidders, comprising 10 companies including LNGPH and Prime Infrastructure Capital, Inc. The department aims to award contracts before the end of this year.

  ## What it means

  The ERC's draft resolution establishes the pricing guardrails within which competition in the auction will occur. By setting separate thresholds for existing and new-build plants, the framework allows lower-cost established assets to compete on tighter margins while providing sufficient headroom for developers committing capital to new construction. The exclusion of fuel costs from the cap preserves a direct pass-through for commodity price risk rather than embedding it in a fixed bid price, a structure that shifts fuel exposure to off-takers rather than generators.

  With the DoE targeting contract awards before year-end and pre-qualification already complete, the release of the draft GAT resolution marks one of the final regulatory steps before bidding opens.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓能源監管委員會（ERC）針對全國首次中間負載天然氣發電競標，提出每千瓦時最高7.16披索的出價上限，依電廠類型及是否為新建設施而有所不同。

  ERC發布了一份草案決議，訂定天然氣競標門檻（GAT）價格——即投標者可提交的最高出價——為政府即將舉行的競標做準備。本次競標目標採購呂宋島與維薩亞斯電網合計3,840百萬瓦（MW）的中間負載燃氣發電容量。

  ## 各類電廠價格上限

  上述門檻依據能源部（DoE）的參考參數及初步價格計算結果而定，依發電資產的建設年限與配置型態而有顯著差異：

  - 現有複循環燃氣渦輪機（CCGT）設施，1,400 MW規模，呂宋島：**每千瓦時2.76披索**
  - 現有CCGT設施，500 MW規模：**每千瓦時2.66披索**
  - 新建CCGT電廠：**每千瓦時7.16披索**
  - 新建開放循環燃氣渦輪機（OCGT）電廠：**每千瓦時4.45披索**
  - 新建燃氣往復式引擎設施：**每千瓦時6.79披索**

  複循環燃氣渦輪機會回收主燃燒渦輪機的廢熱以額外發電，發電效率優於僅使用燃燒渦輪機的開放循環電廠。燃氣往復式引擎採用活塞而非渦輪機驅動，通常適用於規模較小或調度彈性較高的發電容量。

  GAT價格旨在涵蓋各類設施的資本回收及營運維護成本。ERC表示，燃料商品成本及有上限的到岸物流成本均不計入門檻，將透過燃料成本轉嫁機制另行回收。

  ## 調度假設條件

  能源部的計算以42%的參考淨容量因子（相當於每日約14小時的運轉時數）作為確定成本回收的基準。ERC強調，此假設僅用於計算競標門檻，並不代表保證最低調度承諾，亦不構成購電方的照付不議義務。

  ## 競標範圍與時程

  政府計畫透過本次競標採購合計3,840 MW的中間負載容量，分布於兩大電網。中間負載發電是指運行時數多於尖峰負載機組、但少於基載電廠的發電設施，負責填補電力需求隨日間起伏所產生的供電缺口。

  其中，呂宋島目標取得2,900 MW的燃氣發電容量，最快今年即可開始供電，並延續至2031年。其餘940 MW涵蓋維薩亞斯群島，具體範圍為宿霧及班乃島，預計於2028年至2031年間陸續供電。

  能源部上個月公布預先資格審查合格的投標商名單，共10家企業，包括LNGPH及Prime Infrastructure Capital, Inc.，目標在今年底前完成合約授予。

  ## 市場意涵

  ERC草案決議為競標中的競爭設定了定價護欄。藉由為既有電廠與新建電廠分別訂定門檻，此框架一方面讓低成本的現有資產得以在更緊縮的利潤空間內競標，另一方面也為承諾投入新建工程資本的開發商保留足夠的報酬空間。將燃料成本排除在上限之外，保留了商品價格風險的直接轉嫁機制，而非將其嵌入固定出價之中——此結構將燃料價格風險轉移至購電方而非發電商承擔。

  隨著能源部目標在年底前完成合約授予、預先資格審查業已完成，GAT草案決議的發布標誌著競標正式開放前最後幾項法規程序之一。
