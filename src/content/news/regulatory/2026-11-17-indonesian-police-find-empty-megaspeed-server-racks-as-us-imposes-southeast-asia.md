body_en: |
  Indonesian police searching the Jakarta premises of PT Megaspeed International Indonesia found server racks containing no graphics processing units (GPUs), according to a senior Indonesian government official, in an enforcement action requested by the US Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) to trace Nvidia chips acquired by the Singapore-based cloud company at the centre of a US national security probe. The search came as the US Department of Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) announced it would require export licences for advanced semiconductors shipped to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil.

  ## The Jakarta raid

  Officers from the Indonesian National Police's (INP) Criminal Investigation Department, known as Bareskrim, carried out the search earlier in 2026 at PT Megaspeed's listed address on the fifth floor of the Cyber 1 building in South Jakarta, the official said, speaking on condition of anonymity because he was not authorised to speak to the media. The racks appeared never to have been unpacked or used, he said, suggesting the chips were never installed at the site.

  The official alleged that most of the Nvidia hardware bought by Megaspeed entities was likely shipped to Indonesia and that investigators believe much of it was subsequently sent to China, possibly by private jet.

  The Straits Times confirmed on November 13 that PT Megaspeed is not listed among the Cyber 1 building's tenants; the company instead operates from the office of PT MettaDC Teknologi Indonesia, a carrier-neutral data centre and sovereign AI infrastructure provider on the same floor. Megaspeed also rents space in the Jababeka Industrial Estate in Cikarang, Bekasi, West Java, 45 kilometres east of Jakarta, according to a MettaDC officer.

  Commissioner-General Asep Safrudin, head of Bareskrim, did not respond to a request for comment. Inspector-General Untung Widyatmoko, head of INP's International Relations Division, declined on November 13 to comment on how many GPUs may have been shipped through Indonesia to China or whether FBI agents are present in Indonesia.

  ## The $4.8 billion chip trail

  Megaspeed's Malaysian sister company bought at least $4.8 billion worth of advanced AI equipment containing Nvidia chips between June 2024 and February 2026, according to trade records. US officials had already investigated whether the company helped China obtain restricted chips before the broader scandal became public.

  Alice Huang, Megaspeed's former managing director, is the individual US officials believe was passing information to Beijing. Megaspeed says she left the company in August 2025 and denies any wrongdoing. Beijing has dismissed the allegations as "groundless." Alice Huang is not related to Jensen Huang.

  ## Jensen Huang's resignation

  Nvidia's board announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately, ending 33 years at the helm of the company he co-founded in 1993. Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress has agreed to serve as interim chief executive while the board searches for a permanent successor from internal and external candidates.

  Harvey Jones was named Nvidia's first-ever board chair in the same announcement. "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team," Jones said, adding that operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap would continue without interruption.

  The board said at the outset of its probe that it had no reason to believe Nvidia secrets or national security had been compromised. The board hired law firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to run its investigation, and no official finding has been published.

  Nvidia shares fell more than 12% in early trading on the day of the resignation before recovering to about 5% down. The stock had already fallen 4.3% on November 9 and 5.5% on November 10 as the investigation became public. Reuters has since reported a third affair, involving a second unnamed Nvidia employee. Three House committees sent Huang a joint letter summoning him to a closed-door interview. The Financial Times reported that Huang has discussed a return to the company with allies.

  ## New US export licensing requirements

  The BIS announced an interim final rule requiring a licence to export advanced semiconductors to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil. The rule also places controls on data centres that serve Chinese companies.

  "The rule is designed to ensure that existing restrictions on access to sensitive American technology cannot be circumvented through remote computing arrangements involving data centres in third countries," the Commerce Department said in a statement.

  Exporters, foreign foundries and chip data centres are required to perform due diligence on customers, including checking for direct or indirect ownership or control by entities in Country Group D:5, the US government's designation for countries of particular national security concern, or in Macau. The licence requirement does not apply to commercial recommender systems, such as those used by online retailers or social media companies, which the department said do not pose the same national security threat.

  Companies headquartered in the United States will not need a licence but must submit monthly reports to the BIS detailing parties involved, parent companies, the total processing performance of shipped products and quantities.

  The Wall Street Journal reported on November 15, citing sources, that the Commerce Department is working on rules that would require licences for exports of advanced chips including Nvidia's Blackwell series to those countries, with the specific aim of prohibiting Chinese customers from remotely accessing the resulting computing power.

  ## Regional diplomatic response

  Singapore and the United States discussed the Megaspeed case at a senior-level meeting in Washington, with both countries committing to continue discussions, The Straits Times reported on November 14. Malaysia's Ministry of Investment, Trade and Industry said on November 13 that it is working closely with Singapore and the US to strengthen safeguards against chip smuggling.

  ## Implications for Southeast Asia's data centre pipeline

  The enforcement and regulatory developments place significant pressure on AI data centre projects planned or under construction across the region. In Indonesia, Nvidia-linked facilities clustered in West Java and Batam, with Jakarta as the main hub, could face greater scrutiny; much of the capacity for those centres is still being planned or built.

  In Purwakarta, West Java, Raia Grid, a joint digital infrastructure venture of Indonesian investment conglomerate Arsari Group and telecommunications provider Indosat, plans to begin assembling 140 to 150 Nvidia GPUs in December. The company did not respond to a November 12 inquiry about its procurement plans.

  In Batam, Australian AI start-up Firmus and Singapore-based data centre operator DayOne are planning a 360-megawatt Nvidia AI Factory campus, with a megawatt figure equivalent to the electricity draw of roughly 360,000 average households, set to receive up to 170,000 GPUs in 2027 and 2028, with initial operations targeted for 2027. Nvidia is also reportedly exploring a $4.5 billion investment in Batam; talks are ongoing and nothing has been confirmed.

  ## Industry-wide effects

  Investors have treated the leadership upheaval as a risk to the broader GPU-financed data centre build-out, not only a problem for one chip vendor. Rival AMD, which reported a 97% increase in data centre revenue to $8.5 billion this month, is a potential alternative source for buyers seeking to reduce Nvidia exposure.

  The Wall Street Journal reported on November 15 that interim CEO Kress is expected to rein in Nvidia's programme of financing AI industry ventures. The programme had backed 129 venture deals, and start-ups that relied on it expect a pullback.

  Huang had been the AI infrastructure sector's most effective advocate in Washington, including with the Trump administration. His departure removes that access at a time when Congress has grown more hostile toward chip exporters. Huang himself had called US export controls "a failure" that benefits China's semiconductor development.

  Nvidia is scheduled to report third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on November 17, its first earnings release since Huang's exit, against revenue guidance of approximately $108 billion.

  ## 雅加達搜索行動

  前述官員以未獲授權對外發言為由要求匿名，並透露印尼國家警察（INP）刑事調查部（Bareskrim）已於2026年稍早突擊搜查PT Megaspeed在南雅加達Cyber 1大樓五樓的登記地址。他表示，機架看起來從未開箱或使用過，顯示晶片從未在該址安裝。

  該官員指稱，Megaspeed旗下公司所購買的輝達硬體，大部分很可能已運抵印尼，調查人員研判其中大量設備隨後被轉送至中國，或許是藉由私人飛機完成的。

  《海峽時報》已於11月13日確認，PT Megaspeed並未列於Cyber 1大樓的租戶名單中；該公司實際上是在同一樓層、由PT MettaDC Teknologi Indonesia所提供的辦公室內運作，後者是一家載波中立的資料中心及主權AI基礎設施業者。根據MettaDC一名員工的說法，Megaspeed另外也在距雅加達東方約45公里、西爪哇省勿加西市嘉巴貝卡工業區租有空間。

  Bareskrim局長Asep Safrudin警察上將並未回覆置評請求。INP國際關係局局長Untung Widyatmoko警察督察長則於11月13日拒絕就有多少GPU可能已透過印尼轉運至中國、以及FBI探員是否身在印尼等問題發表評論。

  ## 48億美元的晶片流向

  根據貿易紀錄，Megaspeed的馬來西亞姐妹公司在2024年6月至2026年2月間，採購了至少48億美元、內含輝達晶片的先進AI設備。早在此事件鬧大之前，美方官員便已著手調查該公司是否協助中國取得受管制晶片。

  美方官員認定，前Megaspeed執行董事Alice Huang就是向北京傳遞資訊的當事人。Megaspeed表示，她已於2025年8月離職，並否認有任何不當行為。北京方面則將相關指控斥為「毫無根據」。Alice Huang與輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）並無親屬關係。

  ## 黃仁勳辭職

  輝達董事會於11月12日宣布，黃仁勳已辭去總裁、執行長及董事職務，即日起生效，結束了他在這家他於1993年共同創辦的公司長達33年的領導生涯。財務長柯蕾特·克雷絲（Colette Kress）同意出任臨時執行長，同時董事會將從內部及外部候選人中尋覓正式接班人。

  在同一份公告中，哈維·瓊斯（Harvey Jones）獲任命為輝達史上首位董事會主席。瓊斯表示：「董事會對柯蕾特及領導團隊深具信心」，並強調業務營運、客戶承諾及產品藍圖均將不受影響、持續推進。

  董事會在啟動調查之初即表示，沒有理由相信輝達商業機密或國家安全遭到洩露。董事會委請律師事務所Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz主導調查，目前尚未公布任何正式調查結論。

  輝達股價在辭職消息公布當日盤前一度重挫逾12%，隨後收窄跌幅至約5%。在此之前，隨著調查消息逐漸曝光，股價已於11月9日下跌4.3%、11月10日再跌5.5%。路透社其後報導，另有第三段外遇事件，涉及另一名未具名的輝達員工。眾議院三個委員會聯名致函黃仁勳，要求他出席一場不公開的聽證會。《金融時報》則報導，黃仁勳已與親近盟友討論重返公司的可能性。

  ## 美國祭出新出口許可規定

  BIS宣布一項暫行最終規定，要求向印尼、馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國及巴西出口先進半導體均須取得許可。此規定亦對服務中國企業的資料中心實施管制。

  美國商務部在聲明中表示：「此規定旨在確保，現行對取得敏感美國技術的限制，不會透過涉及第三國資料中心的遠端運算安排而遭到規避。」

  出口商、境外晶圓廠及晶片資料中心均須對客戶進行盡職調查，包括核查是否有來自美國政府所定義的D:5國家群組（特別國家安全顧慮國）或澳門的實體，對其進行直接或間接持股或控制。此許可要求不適用於商業推薦系統，例如網路零售商或社群媒體平台所使用的系統；商務部指出，此類系統並不構成同等級別的國家安全威脅。

  總部設於美國的企業無須申請許可，但必須每月向BIS提交報告，詳載相關各方、母公司、出貨產品的總運算效能及數量。

  《華爾街日報》11月15日援引消息人士報導，商務部正研擬規定，要求出口包含輝達Blackwell系列在內的先進晶片至上述國家時須取得許可，核心目標在於禁止中國客戶遠端取用由此產生的運算能力。

  ## 區域外交回應

  《海峽時報》11月14日報導，新加坡與美國已就Megaspeed案在華盛頓舉行高層會議，兩國均承諾持續就此進行磋商。馬來西亞投資、貿易及工業部則於11月13日表示，正與新加坡及美國密切合作，強化防制晶片走私的相關保障措施。

  ## 對東南亞資料中心建設管道的衝擊

  此波執法及法規動態，對該地區規劃中或正在興建的AI資料中心專案帶來龐大壓力。在印尼，聚集於西爪哇及巴淡島、以雅加達為主要樞紐的輝達相關設施，將面臨更嚴格的審查；這些中心的大部分容量仍處於規劃或建設階段。

  在西爪哇省普哇加達，由印尼投資集團Arsari Group與電信業者Indosat合資成立的數位基礎設施公司Raia Grid，計畫於12月開始組裝140至150顆輝達GPU。該公司並未回覆於11月12日就其採購計畫所發出的詢問。

  在巴淡島，澳洲AI新創公司Firmus與新加坡資料中心業者DayOne正規劃興建一座360百萬瓦（MW）的輝達AI Factory園區，其用電量相當於約36萬戶普通家庭的用電量，預計於2027年至2028年間接收多達17萬顆GPU，初期營運目標訂於2027年。外界亦報導輝達正評估在巴淡島投資45億美元，但相關談判仍在進行中，目前尚未有任何正式確認。

  ## 對整體產業的影響

  投資人將此次領導層動盪視為對整體GPU驅動資料中心建設浪潮的潛在風險，而不僅僅是單一晶片廠商的問題。競爭對手超微（AMD）本月公布資料中心營收大增97%至85億美元，可望成為有意降低輝達曝險的買家的替代選項。

  《華爾街日報》11月15日報導，臨時執行長克雷絲預計將縮減輝達資助AI產業創投的計畫，該計畫已支持了129筆創投交易，仰賴此一計畫的新創公司預料將受到衝擊。

  黃仁勳長期以來是AI基礎設施產業在華盛頓最具影響力的倡議者，包括在川普政府任內亦然。他的離去，在國會對晶片出口業者態度日趨強硬之際，讓業界失去了這條重要的遊說管道。黃仁勳本人曾直言，美國的出口管制是「一項失敗的政策」，只會有利於中國半導體產業的發展。

  輝達預定於11月17日公布2027財年第三季財報，這將是黃仁勳離職後的首次財報發布，市場對當季營收的預期指引約為1,080億美元。
