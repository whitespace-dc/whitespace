body_en: |
  Democratic victories in US midterm elections — the party secured 253 House seats and 54 Senate seats — have triggered a roughly $2.5 trillion market selloff, as investors priced in the risk that newly elected governors and legislators will follow through on campaign pledges to curb or halt data center construction across the country.

  ## Market reaction

  Wall Street recorded its worst session since April 2025, when President Donald Trump's sweeping tariffs rattled markets, in the days following the vote. The tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite fell 5.52 percent, CNN reported. The S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 4.36 percent and 3.44 percent respectively, each posting their worst day since those tariffs were unveiled.

  ## Governors target data centers

  The political risk is concentrated in states that hold a large share of planned US capacity. Around 40 percent of pending data center capacity in the United States now sits in states where newly elected governors promised voters they would stop or pause construction, according to data from Cleanview.

  Texas represents the most prominent case. Concerns are most prominent there, where Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa has called for an immediate moratorium on data center projects and criticized outgoing Governor Greg Abbott's approach to oversight. "We will have laws to protect Texans, and we need to stop what we are doing with data centers until we have those protections," Hinojosa said on the campaign trail. Hinojosa takes office on January 19, 2027, and her moratorium would require action by the Texas Legislature, which convenes the same month. Abbott had already paused data center approvals at two state agencies in August pending an audit expected to conclude by year-end. Hinojosa's other stated priorities include requiring union labor on projects, protecting water supplies, ending nondisclosure agreements that limit public tracking of developments, and eliminating tax subsidies that cost the state billions in sales tax revenue.

  In Ohio, Governor-elect Amy Acton has said she supports a conditional moratorium requiring developers to cover utility costs, use union labor, and operate with full transparency. "Ohio is open for business, but we are not up for sale. My message as governor will be if you do not meet these conditions, we do not want you in Ohio," Acton said ahead of the election. Acton has also indicated that new data centers in the state should use brownfields — previously developed land — rather than farmland or areas within residential communities.

  ## Ballot measures reflect broad public opposition

  The shift extends beyond individual governors. Data tracked by Redbud Consulting shows that US voters overwhelmingly backed measures to restrict data center development in their states and communities. As of publication, only the city of Harwood in North Dakota voted to allow construction to proceed without independent review. Restrictive sentiment was recorded across New Jersey, Illinois, Kansas, Maine, and Wisconsin, among other states. Citizens cited higher energy prices and concerns that artificial intelligence poses a threat to the workforce as driving their opposition.

  Dan Diorio of the Data Center Coalition told ABC News, via the Associated Press, that moratoriums could cause communities to forfeit tax revenue and payments from technology companies.

  ## Investor response

  Melissa Brown, Global Head of Investment Decision Research at Simcorp, told Reuters that markets are recalibrating the AI infrastructure thesis. "Investors appear to be recognizing that some newly elected members of Congress and governors are looking to slow the pace of AI infrastructure development. While it seems unlikely that they will be able to stop it altogether, any slowdown would raise concerns because the AI growth story has largely depended on the rapid buildout of supporting infrastructure. That could create uncertainty for many companies exposed to the sector, at least in the near to medium term," Brown said.

  ## Contagion spreads to Asia

  The selloff moved into Asian markets the following day, Bloomberg reported. The MSCI Asia Pacific Index — a broad gauge of equity markets across the region — fell 5.3 percent. Japan's Nikkei 225 and Taiwan's Taiex each fell to levels 20 percent below their recent peaks. In South Korea, the Korea Exchange imposed a two-minute cooling-off period on shares of Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix under heavy selling pressure; when those measures were lifted, the Kospi index fell further, triggering a 20-minute trading halt, Bloomberg reported.

  ## Capital shifts toward India

  As losses spread through developed markets, foreign institutional investors moved capital into India. Foreign institutions purchased more than $3 billion in Indian shares over the 10 days preceding the selloff, with purchases topping $1.9 billion for the week through Wednesday — the highest weekly inflow in more than a year — Bloomberg reported, as investors sought to limit losses from AI-exposed positions elsewhere.

  Anindya Banerjee, head of research for commodity and currency derivatives at Kotak Neo, told Bloomberg that the rupee could strengthen further. "Unless the war in West Asia re-escalates, this Goldilocks situation could continue for the rupee. In that situation, once 94 per dollar breaks, we will see a breakout from a range that has held since April and then a lot of panic dollar-selling could happen from exporters," Banerjee said.

  ## China context

  The US selloff follows a parallel contraction in China's AI and semiconductor sector. The STAR50 index — which tracks Chinese AI and semiconductor companies listed on Shanghai's science and technology board — fell 40 percent over less than four months before this week's events, wiping out approximately $480 billion in market value, Reuters reported last week. Traders attributed the decline to high prices, tax liabilities, and the threat of Chinese data center equipment being banned by the United States and other countries. Robot-maker Unitree saw its value cut in half since August; chipmaker CXMT lost more than a quarter of its market value over the same period. While the causes differed from the US selloff, the episode illustrated how quickly AI sector valuations can contract under sustained pressure.

  ## What comes next

  With roughly 40 percent of pending US data center capacity facing potential regulatory disruption, the sector faces an extended period of uncertainty. Industry groups and developers are expected to engage with incoming governors and legislators in an attempt to advance projects before new rules take effect. Texas's situation will be the most closely watched: Hinojosa takes office in January 2027, the same month the Texas Legislature convenes, and any moratorium legislation would need to clear that body before it could take legal effect.

  民主黨在美國期中選舉大獲全勝——於眾議院拿下253席、參議院取得54席——隨即引發約2.5兆美元的股市拋售潮。投資人憂慮新當選的州長與立法機構將兌現競選承諾，限制乃至叫停全美各地的資料中心建設，紛紛調降相關持股。

  ## 市場反應

  華爾街在選後數日錄得2025年4月以來最慘烈的單日跌幅——彼時正是川普總統祭出全面性關稅、令市場大幅震盪之時。科技股重鎮那斯達克綜合指數暴跌5.52%，CNN報導指出。標普500指數與道瓊工業平均指數則分別下挫4.36%與3.44%，同創關稅政策宣布以來的單日最大跌幅。

  ## 州長矛頭指向資料中心

  政治風險集中在擁有大量美國規劃產能的州份。根據Cleanview的數據，全美約40%的待建資料中心產能，位於新任候選州長已承諾暫停或中止建設的州內。

  其中以德州的情勢最為引人關注。候任州長吉娜·伊諾霍薩（Gina Hinojosa）呼籲立即對資料中心專案實施暫停令，並批評即將卸任的州長葛瑞格·艾伯特（Greg Abbott）的監管方式失當。她在競選期間表示：「我們將立法保護德州人，在相關保護措施到位之前，必須先暫停資料中心的一切建設。」伊諾霍薩將於2027年1月19日就職，而其擬推動的暫停令須經德州州議會立法通過，州議會恰好同月召開。事實上，艾伯特已於8月以稽核為由，暫停兩個州立機構對資料中心的審批，預計稽核將於年底前完成。伊諾霍薩的其他施政優先項目，包括要求工程採用工會勞工、保護水資源供應、廢除限制外界追蹤開發動態的保密協議，以及取消使該州損失數十億美元銷售稅收入的稅收補貼。

  在俄亥俄州，候任州長艾美·阿克頓（Amy Acton）表示支持有條件的暫停令，要求開發商負擔公用事業費用、採用工會勞工，並落實完全透明化的經營原則。她在選前表示：「俄亥俄州歡迎企業投資，但我們不出賣自己。我擔任州長時的立場就是：如果你達不到這些條件，我們不歡迎你來俄亥俄州。」阿克頓也指出，該州新建的資料中心應優先選用棕地——即已開發過的土地——而非農地或住宅社區周邊地帶。

  ## 選票提案反映廣泛民意反彈

  轉變並不僅止於個別州長的態度。Redbud Consulting匯整的數據顯示，美國選民普遍支持限制各州及社區資料中心開發的相關提案。截至本文發稿，僅有北達科他州哈伍德市（Harwood）投票通過，同意在不進行獨立審查的情況下繼續推進建設。新澤西、伊利諾、堪薩斯、緬因、威斯康辛等多州均出現限制性民意。民眾反對的主要理由，包括能源價格上漲，以及憂慮人工智慧對勞動市場帶來的威脅。

  資料中心聯盟（Data Center Coalition）的丹·狄奧里歐（Dan Diorio）透過美聯社接受ABC新聞採訪時表示，暫停令可能導致地方社區損失科技業者帶來的稅收與相關款項。

  ## 投資人反應

  Simcorp全球投資決策研究主管梅麗莎·布朗（Melissa Brown）向路透社表示，市場正在重新評估AI基礎建設的投資邏輯。「投資人似乎逐漸意識到，部分新當選的國會議員與州長正在設法放慢AI基礎建設的推進步伐。儘管他們似乎不太可能完全阻止這股趨勢，但任何程度的放緩都令人憂慮，因為AI的成長故事在很大程度上有賴基礎建設的快速擴張。這至少在短中期內，可能對所有涉及該產業的企業形成不確定因素，」布朗說。

  ## 賣壓蔓延至亞洲

  彭博社報導，拋售潮於次日延燒至亞洲市場。涵蓋整個亞太地區的MSCI亞太指數下跌5.3%。日本日經225指數與台灣加權指數均較近期高點回落逾20%，雙雙跌入技術性熊市。在南韓，韓國交易所對三星電子與SK海力士股票在沉重賣壓下啟動兩分鐘冷靜機制；待措施解除後，韓國綜合股價指數（KOSPI）進一步下挫，觸發長達20分鐘的交易暫停，彭博社如此報導。

  ## 資金轉進印度

  隨著跌勢蔓延至已開發市場，境外法人機構將資金移往印度。彭博社報導，外資在拋售潮前10個交易日累計買入逾30億美元的印度股票，其中截至週三當週的淨流入達19億美元以上，創一年多來最高單週流入紀錄，顯示投資人積極尋求降低在其他市場AI相關部位的損失。

  Kotak Neo大宗商品與外匯衍生品研究主管阿南德雅·班納吉（Anindya Banerjee）告訴彭博社，印度盧比有望進一步走強。「除非西亞戰事再度升溫，否則這種『恰到好處』的局面將有利於盧比持續走強。一旦每美元兌換的盧比突破94關口，我們將看到盧比自4月以來守住的區間出現突破，屆時出口商可能爆發大規模的恐慌性拋售美元潮，」班納吉說。

  ## 中國市場背景

  美股賣壓的出現，恰好是在中國AI與半導體類股同步收縮之際。追蹤上海科創板AI與半導體企業的科創50指數，在本週事件發生前的不到四個月內已重挫40%，蒸發約4,800億美元市值，路透社上週對此有所報導。市場人士將跌勢歸因於高估值、稅務負擔，以及中國資料中心設備遭美國與其他國家禁用的威脅。機器人製造商宇樹科技（Unitree）自8月以來市值腰斬；晶片製造商長鑫存儲（CXMT）同期亦損失逾四分之一的市值。儘管成因與美股賣壓不同，但這一插曲清楚說明，AI類股估值在持續壓力下可以收縮得多麼迅速。

  ## 後續展望

  隨著全美約40%的待建資料中心產能面臨潛在的監管衝擊，整個產業正迎來一段充滿不確定性的時期。業界團體與開發商預計將積極與候任州長及立法機構溝通，力圖在新法規生效前推進相關專案。德州的動向將是外界最密切關注的焦點：伊諾霍薩將於2027年1月就職，而德州州議會亦於同月召開，任何暫停令立法都必須通過州議會方能具有法律效力。
