body_en: |
  Memphis City Council rejected a proposed 18-month moratorium on new data center projects Tuesday, voting instead for a 135-day pause on applications while Mayor Paul Young's independent review of the industry's impact on the city runs its course.

  The council deadlocked 6-6 on the 18-month ban — with one member absent — after a debate that lasted five and a half hours. Councilors then approved the shorter pause as an alternative, timed to overlap with the 90-day review Mayor Young commissioned at the start of October. That review is charged with developing what the city described as "clear, actionable recommendations for regulating large and hyperscale data centers in Memphis," according to Data Center Dynamics.

  ## Background to the vote

  The 18-month moratorium was put forward by councilor JB Smiley Jr. and had been under debate for several weeks before Tuesday's vote. A council meeting on October 6 drew more than 100 members of the public — most of them opposed to data centers — and reportedly ended in protests and physical altercations outside the chamber.

  That meeting's fallout extended to Tuesday's session: six attendees had been banned by the council for violating its speech and decorum policy at the October 6 meeting, but a judge lifted those bans before the vote, ruling the council's actions were unreasonable. A total of 407 residents were able to fit inside the council chamber for Tuesday's meeting, with dozens more watching on screens outside, according to local outlet Action News 5.

  ## xAI facilities at the center of the dispute

  Memphis does not have a large data center market, but it is home to several facilities operated by Elon Musk's xAI, now rebranded as SpaceXAI, or SpaceXSI. Built over the last two years, those facilities power xAI's Grok artificial intelligence chatbot and provide cloud services to clients including generative AI company Anthropic.

  The xAI sites have been the subject of multiple lawsuits. Environmental and civil rights groups have alleged the company violated its permits through the use of gas turbines, which xAI used to bring its data centers online quickly. One of Musk's facilities is already located across the state border in Mississippi.

  ## Economic arguments against a moratorium

  Some councilors argued that a longer ban would damage economic development. Opponents of the moratorium warned that data center companies could redirect investment — and the associated tax revenue — to other jurisdictions if Memphis imposed an extended prohibition.

  ## Significance

  The outcome illustrates the tension playing out in communities across the United States between residents concerned about the environmental and civic impact of large digital infrastructure projects and local governments seeking to balance regulation with economic development. Data center moratoriums have become increasingly common in US communities as opposition to artificial intelligence-related infrastructure grows, with some bans enacted at the state level.

  The 135-day pause in Memphis will now run against the clock of Mayor Young's review, the findings of which are expected to shape whatever permanent regulatory framework the city adopts for large and hyperscale data center development.

  ## 孟菲斯市議會否決18個月資料中心暫停令，改通過135天禁令

  孟菲斯市議會週二否決了一項針對新資料中心開發案的18個月暫停令，改以135天的申請暫停取代，並與市長保羅·楊（Paul Young）委託啟動的獨立評估調查並行推進，共同審視資料中心產業對這座城市的整體衝擊。

  議會就18個月禁令進行表決，結果以6比6僵局告終（一名議員缺席），此前雙方歷經長達五個半小時的激烈辯論。議員隨後批准了時程較短的暫停方案作為替代，其設計與楊市長於十月初委託啟動的90天評估審查相互配合、同步進行。根據《Data Center Dynamics》的報導，該評估任務的職責是為孟菲斯市制訂「針對大型及超大規模資料中心的明確、可執行監管建議」。

  ## 投票背景

  這項18個月暫停令由議員JB·斯邁利二世（JB Smiley Jr.）提出，在週二投票前已歷經數週討論。10月6日的議會聽證會吸引逾百名市民到場，其中大多數反對資料中心的設立，據報導，散會後在議事廳外更引發抗議活動及肢體衝突。

  當日事件的餘波延燒至週二的會議：六名出席者因在10月6日會議上違反發言與秩序規定，遭議會禁止出席後續會議，但一名法官在表決前裁定議會的處置措施不合理，解除了禁令。根據當地媒體《Action News 5》的報導，週二會議共有407名居民進入議事廳旁聽，另有數十人在廳外螢幕前收看直播。

  ## 爭議核心：xAI的設施

  孟菲斯並非主要的資料中心市場，但境內設有數座由伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）旗下xAI營運的設施——該公司現已更名為SpaceXAI或SpaceXSI。這些設施在過去兩年間陸續建成，主要為xAI的Grok人工智慧聊天機器人提供運算支援，並向包括生成式AI公司Anthropic在內的客戶提供雲端服務。

  xAI的相關設施已面臨多起訴訟。環保及公民權利團體指控該公司透過使用燃氣渦輪機違反許可規定——xAI正是藉此快速讓資料中心上線運作。馬斯克旗下另有一座設施已跨越田納西州州界，坐落於密西西比州境內。

  ## 反對暫停令的經濟論點

  部分議員主張，較長期的禁令將損害城市的經濟發展。反對暫停令的人士警告，若孟菲斯實施長期禁令，資料中心業者可能將投資及相關稅收轉移至其他地區。

  ## 事件意義

  此次投票結果，折射出美國各地社區正在上演的深層張力——憂慮大型數位基礎設施對環境與公共生活造成衝擊的居民，與試圖在監管和經濟發展之間尋求平衡的地方政府，雙方之間的角力持續升溫。隨著外界對人工智慧相關基礎設施的反彈情緒不斷擴大，資料中心暫停令在美國各地社區已愈來愈普遍，部分州甚至已在州層級頒布禁令。

  孟菲斯135天的暫停令目前正與楊市長的評估審查同步倒數計時，評估結果預計將左右這座城市最終針對大型及超大規模資料中心發展所採用的永久性監管框架。
