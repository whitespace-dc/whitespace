body_en: |
  The Trump administration is considering expanded semiconductor tariffs that could reach beyond chips themselves to cover goods manufactured with them, including data center servers, Politico reported, as cited by Data Center Dynamics on August 28, 2026.

  ## What is under consideration

  The tariff discussions are still in an early stage and could change over the coming weeks or months, according to the report. The administration is considering a phase-in period for any new levies. Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick supports using the tariffs as a mechanism to force investment in US chip manufacturing, with companies that commit to such investment potentially exempt from the charges. Tariffs are paid by the importer, not the exporting country.

  ## White House position

  White House spokesperson Kush Desai framed the discussions in terms of industrial policy. "Reshoring semiconductor manufacturing is a top priority for President Trump, whose policies have already secured hundreds of billions of dollars of investments in this key sector," Desai said. "The Trump administration remains focused on delivering more investments and economic relief for the American people while safeguarding our national security."

  ## Industry cost projections

  The Computer and Communications Industry Association (CCIA) claimed in June that tariffs of this kind could cost the United States approximately $90 billion in gross domestic product (GDP) annually. The CCIA also projected that such tariffs could delay or cancel around 20 percent of data center projects planned through 2030.

  ## What comes next

  With discussions described as early-stage and both a phase-in period and investment-based exemptions still under consideration, the final structure of any tariffs has yet to be determined. The administration's timeline for any formal action was not specified in the report.

  ## 川普政府研議擴大半導體關稅　資料中心伺服器恐在列

  據《Politico》報導，川普政府正考慮擴大半導體關稅的課徵範圍，除晶片本身外，亦可能延伸至以晶片製造的成品，其中包括資料中心伺服器。此消息由《Data Center Dynamics》於2026年8月28日引述報導。

  ## 目前研議方向

  據報導，相關關稅討論仍處於初期階段，政策內容可能在未來數週至數月內持續調整。政府方面正研議對任何新關稅設置分階段實施期。商務部長霍華德·盧特尼克（Howard Lutnick）支持以關稅作為槓桿，迫使企業投資美國本土晶片製造業；凡承諾進行此類投資的企業，有望獲得豁免。值得注意的是，關稅由進口商承擔，而非出口國。

  ## 白宮立場

  白宮發言人庫什·德賽（Kush Desai）將此次討論定位於產業政策的框架之下。德賽表示：「將半導體製造業遷回美國本土，是川普總統的施政首要之務。川普政府的政策已在這一關鍵產業吸引數千億美元的投資承諾。川普政府將持續致力於為美國人民帶來更多投資機會與經濟紅利，同時守護國家安全。」

  ## 產業界的成本估算

  電腦與通訊產業協會（Computer and Communications Industry Association，CCIA）於今年六月指出，此類關稅每年恐使美國損失約900億美元的國內生產毛額（GDP）。CCIA同時預估，相關關稅可能導致2030年前規劃中約20%的資料中心建設計畫遭到延遲或取消。

  ## 後續發展

  由於討論仍屬初期，分階段實施期與以投資換豁免等機制均尚在研議之中，任何關稅的最終架構尚未確定。報導亦未說明政府計劃採取正式行動的時程。
