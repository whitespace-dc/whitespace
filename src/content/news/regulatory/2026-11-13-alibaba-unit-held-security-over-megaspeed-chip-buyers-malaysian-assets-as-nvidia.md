body_en: |
  Corporate records show that an Alibaba-owned Singapore company held a security interest over assets of Megaspeed International's Malaysian subsidiary as recently as November 11, 2026, establishing a documented financial link between the Chinese technology conglomerate and the chip distributor at the centre of US and Singapore investigations into the possible diversion of advanced Nvidia artificial intelligence processors to China.

  The disclosure came on the same day Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang resigned, following his November 8 admission of a personal relationship with Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le, who served as Megaspeed's director and managing director. US authorities had raised concerns about Jensen Huang's close ties to Alice Huang, news of which emerged in late October 2026.

  ## The Alibaba-Megaspeed financial link

  Under an agreement dated June 18, 2024, Speedmatrix, Megaspeed's Malaysian subsidiary, pledged specified IT and network equipment, intellectual property, and related rights to Apex Enterprise Solutions (Singapore) as security against Speedmatrix failing to meet certain contractual obligations, according to Malaysian corporate records obtained by The Straits Times. The arrangement gave Apex a priority claim over those assets in the event of default. Malaysian records showed the arrangement remained active as of November 11, 2026.

  Apex is wholly owned, through Cloud Intelligence (Cayman) Holding, by Alibaba Group Holding, the Cayman Islands-incorporated entity that holds contractual rights to Alibaba China's profits, according to Singapore's Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) records. Apex was incorporated in Singapore in July 2023 and is registered at Lazada One in Bras Basah Road, a building leased by Alibaba and its South-east Asian e-commerce unit, Lazada Group.

  In its audited financial statement for the year ended March 31, 2025, Apex described its business activity as "providing procurement activities." Its auditor is PwC, which declined to comment, citing it as a client matter.

  A May report by US short-seller Culper Research, which had taken a position that would profit from a decline in Nvidia's share price, alleged that Alibaba may have channelled billions of dollars through Apex to finance Megaspeed's Nvidia chip purchases. Culper suggested Alibaba provided funds to Apex, which may then have passed a substantial portion to Megaspeed for distribution to its overseas subsidiaries. Alibaba and Megaspeed did not respond to The Straits Times' queries on the nature of their commercial relationships or whether those relationships remained intact amid the investigations.

  The Financial Times reported in November that Alibaba was one of Megaspeed's major customers for its South-east Asian cloud service operation.

  ## Director links

  Corporate records show that Yuan Qian served as an Apex director from August 2023 to August 2025. The name matches the Chinese name of Selina Yuan, a former Alibaba executive who headed Alibaba Cloud's international business in Singapore, though The Straits Times said it had not independently confirmed the director named in Apex's records is the same person.

  Apex's current directors are Gong Zhengyuan, Zhu Bofu, and Madelyn Kwang Yeit Lam, a Singaporean who also serves as company secretary. Kwang has been listed as a director and secretary of Alibaba Cloud (Singapore) since 2016. She did not respond to queries.

  ## Megaspeed's subsidiary network

  Megaspeed owns seven subsidiaries across five overseas markets, according to its 2024 financial statements, its latest publicly available records. Three are in Malaysia: Speedmatrix and Megaspeed International Sdn Bhd, both described as cloud-hosting service providers, and MSFusion. MSFusion was classified as dormant at end-2024 but entered a RM263.2 million (S$82.8 million) agreement in August 2025 to acquire a site in Johor for a proposed data centre, according to a stock exchange filing by the seller.

  Megaspeed also holds 99.99% of Megaspeed (Thailand) and 99% of PT Megaspeed International Indonesia, both cloud-hosting businesses, with wholly owned subsidiaries in Australia and the United States.

  The most recent Megaspeed shipment on record with corporate intelligence platform Sayari was an Nvidia baseboard for high-performance graphics processing units (GPUs) delivered to Speedmatrix on November 18, 2025. Speedmatrix and PT Megaspeed International Indonesia were the top receivers of Megaspeed's shipments, according to Sayari.

  ## Investigations

  Megaspeed attracted scrutiny through its large purchases of advanced Nvidia AI chips, some of which are subject to US export restrictions intended to limit China's access to high-performance computing hardware. US authorities began investigating the company over suspected breaches of those restrictions in late 2024. Singapore police confirmed in October 2025 that they were also investigating Megaspeed over "suspected breaches of our domestic laws."

  The investigations broadened in late October 2026 when reports emerged that US authorities were concerned about Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang's close ties to Alice Huang, Megaspeed's former director. Jensen Huang admitted to an affair on November 8 and resigned from Nvidia on November 12.

  ## Megaspeed on the ground

  Megaspeed's office at Ocean Financial Centre in Singapore has shown no signs of activity. When The Straits Times visited in October 2025, a tenant said the office had been empty "for as long as it has been" there.

  On November 11, The Straits Times visited the registered address of Swiftdata, the Singapore company that owns Megaspeed, and found it apparently occupied by an interior design firm whose co-founder said they had no knowledge of Swiftdata, Megaspeed, or any of their affiliates. The Straits Times was unable to reach Swiftdata or its sole director Tan Yong Pong, who was appointed Megaspeed's director in 2023 on the day Alice Huang left the board.

  The arrangement between Apex and Speedmatrix, confirmed active as of November 11, 2026, establishes a documented financial tie between an Alibaba subsidiary and Megaspeed's Malaysian unit at a time when Megaspeed faces simultaneous investigations in the United States and Singapore over the suspected breach of chip export restrictions.

  On November 12, Megaspeed posted a statement on its website saying it had engaged a US law firm to "lead a comprehensive census of its graphics processing units and independently verify its export control compliance practices." The statement was attributed to Tan Yong Pong, who identified himself as the company's chairman and chief executive.

  公司登記資料顯示，一家隸屬阿里巴巴的新加坡公司截至2026年11月11日，仍持有Megaspeed International馬來西亞子公司資產的擔保利益，確立了這家中國科技集團與晶片經銷商之間有據可查的財務關聯——後者正是美國與新加坡就先進輝達人工智慧處理器疑遭轉運至中國一事展開調查的核心對象。

  上述資訊的揭露，恰與輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）辭職同日。黃仁勳此前於11月8日承認與Alice Huang存在私人關係，Alice Huang中文名黃樂，曾任Megaspeed董事及董事總經理。美國當局對黃仁勳與黃樂的密切關係深感憂慮，相關消息於2026年10月下旬曝光。

  ## 阿里巴巴與Megaspeed的財務關聯

  根據《海峽時報》取得的馬來西亞公司登記資料，依據一份簽訂於2024年6月18日的協議，Megaspeed在馬來西亞的子公司Speedmatrix，將特定IT及網路設備、智慧財產權及相關權利，質押給Apex Enterprise Solutions（新加坡）作為擔保，以防Speedmatrix未能履行特定合約義務。該安排賦予Apex在違約情況下對上述資產享有優先求償權。馬來西亞公司登記資料顯示，這項安排截至2026年11月11日仍然有效。

  Apex透過Cloud Intelligence（開曼）控股公司，全資隸屬於阿里巴巴集團控股（Alibaba Group Holding），後者是在開曼群島註冊成立的實體，持有阿里巴巴中國業務的合約利潤權利——此為新加坡會計與企業管制局（ACRA）登記資料所載明的內容。Apex於2023年7月在新加坡成立，登記地址位於勿拉士峇沙路「Lazada One」大樓，該大樓由阿里巴巴及其東南亞電商子公司Lazada集團租用。

  Apex在截至2025年3月31日財政年度的審計財務報表中，將其業務活動描述為「提供採購活動」。其審計師為普華永道（PwC），後者以客戶事宜為由，拒絕置評。

  美國放空機構Culper Research今年5月發布報告——該機構已建立在輝達股價下跌時可獲利的部位——指稱阿里巴巴可能透過Apex將數十億美元資金注入Megaspeed，藉以資助其採購輝達晶片。Culper指出，阿里巴巴向Apex提供資金，Apex可能再將其中一大部分轉給Megaspeed，由其分配至旗下海外子公司。對於《海峽時報》就雙方商業關係性質以及在調查期間這些關係是否仍然維持的詢問，阿里巴巴與Megaspeed均未予回應。

  《金融時報》11月報導，阿里巴巴是Megaspeed東南亞雲端服務業務的主要客戶之一。

  ## 董事關聯

  公司登記資料顯示，袁茜（Yuan Qian）於2023年8月至2025年8月間擔任Apex董事。此名字與阿里巴巴前高管Selina Yuan的中文名相符——Selina Yuan曾在新加坡主掌阿里雲國際業務，但《海峽時報》表示，尚未獨立確認Apex登記資料中的董事與其確為同一人。

  Apex現任董事為龔正元（Gong Zhengyuan）、朱博富（Zhu Bofu），以及兼任公司秘書的新加坡籍人士廣藝琳（Madelyn Kwang Yeit Lam）。廣藝琳自2016年起即擔任阿里雲（新加坡）的董事及秘書，她未回應詢問。

  ## Megaspeed的子公司網絡

  根據Megaspeed最近公開的財務資料——2024年財務報告——該公司在五個海外市場共設有七家子公司。其中三家位於馬來西亞：Speedmatrix、Megaspeed International Sdn Bhd——兩者均定位為雲端主機服務供應商——以及MSFusion。MSFusion在2024年底被列為休眠狀態，但根據賣方向股票交易所申報的文件，該公司於2025年8月簽訂一份價值2億6,320萬令吉（約新幣8,280萬元）的協議，擬在柔佛收購一處地點，用於興建資料中心。

  Megaspeed亦持有Megaspeed（泰國）99.99%股份及PT Megaspeed International Indonesia 99%股份，兩者均從事雲端主機業務，並分別在澳洲及美國設有全資子公司。

  根據企業情報平台Sayari的資料，Megaspeed最近一筆有紀錄的出貨，是2025年11月18日運交Speedmatrix的一批輝達高效能繪圖處理器（GPU）底板。Speedmatrix與PT Megaspeed International Indonesia是Megaspeed出貨量最大的兩個收貨方。

  ## 調查進展

  Megaspeed因大量採購先進輝達AI晶片而受到外界關注，其中部分晶片受美國出口管制限制，旨在防止高效能運算硬體流入中國。美國當局於2024年底開始調查該公司涉嫌違反上述限制的情事。新加坡警方於2025年10月確認，也正就「涉嫌違反本地法律」對Megaspeed展開調查。

  調查範圍於2026年10月下旬進一步擴大，有報導指出美國當局對輝達執行長黃仁勳與Megaspeed前董事黃樂的密切關係深感憂慮。黃仁勳於11月8日承認外遇，並於11月12日辭去輝達執行長職務。

  ## Megaspeed現場實況

  Megaspeed位於新加坡海洋金融中心（Ocean Financial Centre）的辦公室毫無動靜。《海峽時報》記者於2025年10月前往查訪時，一名租戶表示，自其入駐以來該辦公室就一直是空的。

  11月11日，《海峽時報》記者前往Swiftdata的登記地址——Swiftdata是持有Megaspeed的新加坡公司——卻發現該地址顯然由一家室內設計公司進駐，該公司共同創辦人表示，對Swiftdata、Megaspeed或其任何關聯方毫不知情。《海峽時報》未能聯繫上Swiftdata或其唯一董事陳永邦（Tan Yong Pong），後者於2023年在黃樂離開董事會當日，被任命為Megaspeed董事。

  Apex與Speedmatrix之間的安排截至2026年11月11日仍屬有效，在Megaspeed同時面臨美國和新加坡就涉嫌違反晶片出口管制展開調查之際，這一安排確立了阿里巴巴子公司與Megaspeed馬來西亞子公司之間有據可查的財務關聯。

  11月12日，Megaspeed在其官方網站發表聲明，表示已委聘一家美國律師事務所「主導全面清查其繪圖處理器，並獨立核實其出口管制合規作業」。聲明由陳永邦署名，他自稱為該公司董事長兼執行長。
