body_en: |
  Palm Beach County, Florida commissioners voted 5-1 on Wednesday to reject Project Tango, a proposed 600-megawatt (MW) artificial intelligence data center campus, despite a planning staff recommendation for approval and months of developer revisions — a decision that analysts at investment bank Jefferies say challenges a core assumption underlying utility capital plans and investor forecasts for AI-driven electricity demand.

  The application was denied without prejudice, meaning the developer may submit a revised proposal, but faces material delay in bringing the project online.

  ## What the decision means for Florida Power & Light

  Jefferies estimates that Florida Power & Light's capital investment plan through 2032 includes approximately 6 gigawatts (GW) — or 6,000 MW, enough to power several million homes — of prospective data center load. That prospective load underpins roughly $12 billion in planned capital spending, equal to about 12% of the utility's total planned investment. The Palm Beach outcome raises the question of how much of that prospective load will actually materialize into operating demand.

  Neil Osnato, founder of Persistence Analytics Group, told Data Center Knowledge that utilities and investors should distinguish between load at different stages — announced, permitted, financed, under construction, and operating — rather than treating all proposed projects as equally likely to reach service. "Local permitting risk is now load risk. Community acceptance is now forecast risk," Osnato said.

  Data Center Knowledge reached out to NextEra Energy, which owns Florida Power & Light, and to the utility directly for comment; neither had responded at the time of publication.

  ## What the developer submitted and why commissioners still refused

  Project Tango entered the July hearing after an unusually extensive pre-approval process. The developer revised the proposal, reduced the data center portion of the site, expanded warehouse space, met with residents and county officials, and submitted updated studies covering noise, traffic, and concurrency — the regulatory term for whether existing infrastructure has sufficient capacity to serve new development.

  County planning staff ultimately recommended approval, finding the application compliant with land development regulations subject to additional conditions, including noise mitigation, operational limits, and utility service requirements.

  The developer also addressed concerns raised by the Palm Beach County School Board. An independent assessment by specialists in acoustics, occupational medicine, and environmental health concluded the project would not create adverse health effects for nearby students or residents under the proposed design. The filing described Project Tango as a 600 MW campus supporting AI and cloud workloads that would rely on battery energy storage rather than routine diesel generator operation.

  Commissioners were not persuaded. According to Jefferies, they cited unresolved concerns about land-use compatibility, low-frequency noise, water consumption, traffic, and the limited operating history of hyperscale — meaning large, industrial-scale — AI campuses.

  ## Local opposition is not confined to Palm Beach

  Palm Beach is not the first community to push back on large data center proposals. Communities in Northern Virginia, metro Phoenix, and parts of Georgia have raised similar objections over noise, water use, and land compatibility, according to Data Center Knowledge reporting. Many projects in those areas have ultimately moved forward after redesigns or negotiated conditions.

  Whether Palm Beach represents an outlier or an early signal of tightening local resistance to AI infrastructure remains unclear.

  ## On-site generation as a timing workaround

  The denial is also accelerating a separate trend. Michael Webber, professor of energy resources at the University of Texas at Austin, told Data Center Knowledge that large AI projects facing long utility interconnection timelines — the formal process of connecting a new facility to the electric grid — are turning to on-site generation as a temporary measure to begin operations sooner. "On-site generation is a 'bridge to power' service," Webber said. "It provides them power to bridge the time delay between when the data center turns on and a utility connection can be made."

  Webber expects most of those generators will eventually revert to backup power and operation during periods of peak electricity demand once permanent utility service is established.

  ## The forecasting problem for utilities and investors

  The broader significance of the Palm Beach decision, according to Jefferies, lies in how proposed AI demand should be reflected in utility long-term load forecasts before projects have secured local approval. In this case, the binding constraint on AI infrastructure buildout was not grid transmission capacity but local land-use authority — a variable that does not appear in most utility interconnection queues or investor load models.

  As Osnato put it: "Submitted load is not realizable load. Forecast demand is not durable demand."

  The developer retains the option to submit a revised application to Palm Beach County under the without-prejudice denial.
body_zh: |
  佛羅里達州棕櫚灘縣（Palm Beach County）縣務委員週三以5比1投票，否決一座擬建的600百萬瓦（MW）人工智慧資料中心園區提案「Project Tango」，儘管規劃幕僚建議核准，且開發商已歷經數月修改。投資銀行傑富瑞（Jefferies）的分析師指出，這項決定挑戰了電力公司資本計畫與投資人AI用電需求預測的核心假設。

  此次申請案雖遭駁回，但保留開發商後續申請的權利，意味開發商可提交修正提案，惟專案上線時程將面臨實質延誤。

  ## 此決定對佛羅里達電力與照明公司的影響

  傑富瑞估計，佛羅里達電力與照明公司（Florida Power & Light）至2032年的資本投資計畫中，包含約6吉瓦（GW，即6,000 MW，足以供應數百萬戶家庭用電）的預期資料中心負載。這批預期負載支撐了約120億美元的計畫資本支出，約占該電力公司整體計畫投資的12%。棕櫚灘的結果引發外界質疑：這批預期負載究竟有多少能真正轉化為實際運作需求。

  Persistence Analytics Group創辦人尼爾·奧斯納托（Neil Osnato）向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，電力公司與投資人應區分不同開發階段的負載——「已宣布」、「已取得許可」、「已取得融資」、「建設中」與「已運作」——而非將所有提案一律視為同樣可能上線的項目。「地方許可審查風險，現在就是負載風險；社區接受度，現在就是預測風險，」奧斯納托說。

  《Data Center Knowledge》就此事聯繫佛羅里達電力與照明公司母公司NextEra Energy及該電力公司本身尋求回應，截至發稿時均未獲回覆。

  ## 開發商的提案內容與委員仍拒絕的原因

  Project Tango進入七月聽證程序之前，已歷經異常漫長的預審核流程。開發商修改提案、縮減資料中心佔地比例、擴大倉儲空間、與居民及縣府官員舉行多次會議，並提交涵蓋噪音、交通及「同步配套審查」（concurrency，即現有基礎設施是否具備足夠容量服務新開發項目的法規用語）的最新評估報告。

  縣規劃幕僚最終建議核准，認定申請案符合土地開發法規，惟附帶額外條件，包括噪音緩解措施、營運限制及電力服務要求。

  開發商也回應了棕櫚灘縣學校委員會提出的疑慮。聲學、職業醫學與環境衛生專家進行的獨立評估結論指出，在現行設計方案下，該專案不會對附近學生或居民造成不良健康影響。申請文件將Project Tango描述為一座600 MW園區，支援AI與雲端運算工作負載，並以電池儲能系統取代例行性柴油發電機運作。

  然而委員仍未被說服。傑富瑞指出，委員援引土地使用相容性、低頻噪音、用水量、交通疑慮，以及超大規模（hyperscale，即大型工業規模）AI園區有限運作紀錄等未解問題為由，拒絕核准。

  ## 地方反對聲浪不限於棕櫚灘

  棕櫚灘並非首個反對大型資料中心提案的社區。根據《Data Center Knowledge》的報導，北維吉尼亞（Northern Virginia）、大鳳凰城（Metro Phoenix）都會區及喬治亞州（Georgia）部分地區，均曾對噪音、用水與土地相容性提出類似反對意見。這些地區的許多專案最終在重新設計或協商附帶條件後獲准推進。

  棕櫚灘究竟只是個案，還是地方對AI基礎設施抵制趨於嚴格的早期訊號，目前仍不明朗。

  ## 場地自建發電作為時程因應方案

  此次否決也加速了另一項趨勢的發展。德克薩斯大學奧斯汀分校（University of Texas at Austin）能源資源學教授麥可·韋伯（Michael Webber）向《Data Center Knowledge》表示，面臨漫長電網併網時程（即新設施正式接入電網的審核流程）的大型AI專案，正轉向場地自建發電作為暫時措施，以便儘早開始運作。「場地自建發電是一種『電力橋接』服務，」韋伯說，「能在資料中心上線與電力公司正式併網之間的時間差內，為其提供電力支撐。」

  韋伯預期，一旦永久性電力公司服務確立，大多數這類發電機最終將退回備用電源的角色，或僅在電力需求尖峰期間啟用。

  ## 電力公司與投資人面臨的預測難題

  根據傑富瑞的分析，棕櫚灘決定的更深層意義，在於AI需求應如何在尚未取得地方核准前，反映於電力公司的長期負載預測之中。在此案例中，制約AI基礎設施建設的關鍵障礙並非電網輸配容量，而是地方土地使用審核權——這是一個幾乎不見於電力公司併網申請佇列或投資人負載模型的變數。

  正如奧斯納托所言：「已申請的負載，不等於可實現的負載；預測的需求，不等於可持續的需求。」

  開發商仍可選擇在保留後續申請資格的駁回條件下，向棕櫚灘縣提交修正申請。
