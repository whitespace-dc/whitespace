body_en: |
  Exelon and Invenergy Transmission filed separate complaints at the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission this week, each seeking changes to how regional grid operators run their capacity markets — Exelon targeting the auction schedule at PJM Interconnection and Invenergy challenging the treatment of high-voltage, direct-current merchant transmission lines at MISO, the Midcontinent Independent System Operator.

  ## Exelon seeks prompt capacity auctions at PJM

  Exelon filed its complaint Tuesday, asking FERC to order PJM to replace its current forward capacity auction structure — which secures power supplies three years in advance — with "prompt" auctions held two months before each delivery year begins.

  The proposal would take effect starting with the 2030/31 delivery year. Under the timeline Exelon envisions, PJM would hold its next auction in December as already planned, covering the 2029/30 delivery year. The subsequent auction for the 2030/31 delivery year would then shift to early 2030 — roughly two months before that period begins — rather than following the current three-year-forward schedule.

  Exelon, which serves about 10.7 million customers across PJM's Mid-Atlantic and Midwest footprint, argued that a shorter horizon would yield more accurate load forecasts, resource availability data, and capacity accreditation levels. The company also contended that prompt auctions would sharpen market signals for resource entry and exit.

  The underlying argument is that PJM's capacity market "is no longer working properly," according to the complaint. The push to delay the 2030/31 auction is also tactical: it would give PJM time to overhaul the market design before the next auction cycle, Exelon said.

  Exelon asked FERC to rule by Jan. 31 — the day before PJM is scheduled to release planning parameters for the 2030/31 base auction, which is currently set for May.

  PJM said it is reviewing the complaint and will reply "at the appropriate time," according to spokesman Jeffrey Shields. Shields noted that PJM is already soliciting stakeholder input on capacity market reform through its Power Reliability Through Market Design initiative, which has drawn more than 30 responses. Those responses are scheduled for discussion at a Nov. 13 workshop.

  PJM laid out three broad reform pathways when it launched the initiative in May: stabilizing the existing capacity market, rationing grid reliability by customer class or region, or shifting focus to energy and ancillary services markets and away from the capacity market.

  ## Invenergy challenges MISO's treatment of HVDC lines

  Invenergy Transmission filed its complaint Wednesday, arguing that MISO improperly excludes high-voltage, direct-current (HVDC) merchant transmission lines from its capacity market while still counting their output when calculating how much capacity it needs to buy.

  The complaint centers on a structural asymmetry: MISO accounts for HVDC line capacity value when setting its procurement volume — a calculation that reduces how much capacity it ultimately buys in auctions — but its rules do not allow HVDC line owners to be accredited as capacity resources or receive payment for that contribution, Invenergy said.

  "MISO cannot rely on that capacity, to the benefit of other customers, while refusing to provide any avenue for the entity that provides that capacity to receive commensurate compensation," the Chicago-based company said in the filing.

  The issue is particularly acute for merchant transmission lines because, unlike regulated utilities, they cannot recover costs through FERC-approved cost-of-service rate structures. Instead, merchant lines depend on rates negotiated directly with customers. Without a capacity product to transact, Invenergy argued, "there is no product or service that can be the basis of a merchant-based transaction in MISO."

  Invenergy cited its Grain Belt Express project as a direct example. Using a "direct loss of load" accreditation methodology, the company said the Grain Belt HVDC line could provide more than 1,800 megawatts of accredited capacity — enough to supply power to a large metropolitan area.

  The Grain Belt Express is a 5-gigawatt, 600-kilovolt HVDC line planned to run approximately 800 miles from Kansas to Indiana at a projected cost of about $11 billion. Invenergy plans to build the project in two phases; the first phase would connect southwest Kansas to central Missouri.

  ## What comes next

  The two complaints, filed days apart by Chicago-based companies, reflect mounting pressure on both PJM and MISO from market participants who argue that existing capacity market rules fail to send accurate price signals or fairly compensate all resources that contribute to grid reliability. FERC must decide whether to accept, reject, or modify each proposed remedy. The nearest scheduled milestone is PJM's Nov. 13 stakeholder workshop on its own capacity market reform initiative; Exelon's deadline for a FERC ruling is Jan. 31, the day before PJM is set to release planning parameters for its next base auction.
body_zh: |
  Exelon與Invenergy Transmission本週分別向聯邦能源管理委員會（FERC）提出申訴，各自要求區域電網運營商改革容量市場規則——Exelon鎖定PJM互聯電網（PJM Interconnection）的拍賣排程，Invenergy則挑戰中大陸獨立系統運營商（MISO）對高壓直流商業輸電線路的處理方式。

  ## Exelon要求PJM改採即時容量拍賣

  Exelon於週二提出申訴，要求FERC命令PJM廢除現行預售容量拍賣制度——該制度提前三年鎖定電力供應——改為在每個交付年度開始前兩個月舉行「即時」拍賣。

  此方案預計從2030／31交付年度起生效。依照Exelon規劃的時程，PJM將依原定計畫於今年12月舉行下一場拍賣，涵蓋2029／30交付年度；而2030／31交付年度的後續拍賣，則將移至2030年初舉行，大約在該年度開始前兩個月，不再依循現行的三年預售排程。

  Exelon在PJM的中大西洋與中西部服務範圍內共服務約1,070萬名客戶。該公司主張，縮短預售期限將能產出更精確的負載預測、資源可用性資料及容量認證水準，並強調即時拍賣將能強化資源進出市場的價格訊號。

  申訴的核心論點是：PJM的容量市場「已不再正常運作」。推動延後2030／31拍賣的背後也有戰術考量——Exelon指出，此舉可為PJM爭取時間，在下一個拍賣週期前全面改革市場設計。

  Exelon要求FERC在1月31日前作出裁決，也就是PJM預定公布2030／31基礎拍賣規劃參數的前一天，該拍賣目前排定於5月舉行。

  PJM發言人Jeffrey Shields表示，PJM正在審閱申訴，將在「適當時機」作出回應。Shields指出，PJM已透過「以市場設計實現電力可靠性」（Power Reliability Through Market Design）倡議向利害關係人徵詢容量市場改革意見，目前已收到逾30份回應，預計於11月13日的研討會上討論。

  PJM在今年5月啟動該倡議時，提出三大改革方向：穩定現有容量市場、依客戶類別或地區分配電網可靠度，或將重心從容量市場轉移至電能與輔助服務市場。

  ## Invenergy挑戰MISO對HVDC線路的處理方式

  Invenergy Transmission於週三提出申訴，主張MISO不當地將高壓直流（HVDC）商業輸電線路排除在容量市場之外，同時在計算需採購的容量總量時，卻仍將這些線路的輸出量納入計算。

  此申訴聚焦於一項結構性不對等：MISO在設定採購量時計入了HVDC線路的容量價值——此舉實際上壓低了拍賣的最終採購量——但其規則卻不允許HVDC線路業者取得容量資源的認證資格，也無法就其貢獻獲得報酬，Invenergy指出。

  「MISO不能一方面仰賴這些容量、讓其他客戶從中受益，同時又拒絕提供任何途徑，讓提供該容量的業者獲得相應補償，」這家總部位於芝加哥的公司在申訴文件中表示。

  此問題對商業輸電線路而言尤為嚴峻，因為與受管制的公用事業不同，商業線路無法透過FERC核准的成本服務費率結構回收成本，只能依賴與客戶直接議定的費率。Invenergy主張，若沒有容量產品可供交易，「在MISO中便不存在任何可作為商業交易基礎的產品或服務」。

  Invenergy以旗下的Grain Belt Express計畫為具體案例。該公司援引「直接失負載」認證方法，指出Grain Belt高壓直流輸電線路可提供逾1,800百萬瓦（MW）的認證容量，足以供應一座大型都會區所需的電力。

  Grain Belt Express是一條規劃中的高壓直流輸電線路，容量達50億瓦（5GW）、電壓600千伏（600kV），預計從堪薩斯州延伸至印第安納州，全長約1,290公里（約800英里），預估建設成本約110億美元。Invenergy計畫分兩期建設，第一期將連接堪薩斯州西南部與密蘇里州中部。

  ## 後續發展

  這兩起申訴由兩家同樣總部位於芝加哥的公司相隔數日提出，反映出市場參與者對PJM與MISO所累積的壓力——他們認為現行容量市場規則無法傳遞準確的價格訊號，也無法公平補償所有對電網可靠度有所貢獻的資源。FERC須就每項提案裁決接受、拒絕或修改。最近的一個關鍵時程節點，是PJM將於11月13日舉行的容量市場改革利害關係人研討會；Exelon則要求FERC在1月31日前作出裁決，也就是PJM預定公布下一場基礎拍賣規劃參數的前一天。
