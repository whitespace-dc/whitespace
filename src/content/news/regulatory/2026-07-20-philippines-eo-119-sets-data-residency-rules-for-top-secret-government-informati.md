body_en: |
  The Philippines has enacted a new executive order requiring top-secret and secret government data to be stored within the country's borders, while allowing less sensitive information to move to cloud platforms under defined security conditions.

  President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. signed Executive Order No. 119 on July 13, creating an updated government data classification system and introducing data residency requirements tied to those classifications. The order draws a line between the country's most sensitive information, which must remain within Philippine jurisdiction, and other data categories, which may be stored using cloud technologies subject to prescribed safeguards.

  ## What EO 119 requires

  The order establishes a tiered structure. Data classified as top secret or secret is subject to strict residency controls keeping it on Philippine soil. Data in lower classifications may be held in cloud environments, provided agencies meet the security controls the order specifies. The framework is intended to let government agencies continue adopting cloud and digital tools while protecting information the government deems critical to national security and essential public services.

  ## Reaction from the private sector

  Globe Telecom, Inc., one of the Philippines' major telecommunications and digital services providers, said the order offers a workable framework for both government agencies and their private sector partners. Vicente Froilan M. Castelo, Globe's General Counsel, described EO 119 as "a significant milestone in the country's digital transformation journey" that provides "a clear, risk-based framework" protecting sensitive government information while allowing responsible use of modern digital technologies.

  Globe President and Chief Executive Officer Carl Raymond R. Cruz said the order addresses the relationship between security and public trust in digital government services. "Digital transformation succeeds only when people trust that government systems are secure," Cruz said, adding that the order allows agencies to modernize while ensuring sensitive information receives the highest level of protection.

  ## Broader policy context

  Globe said governments globally have moved to strengthen cybersecurity frameworks as digital public services, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence have expanded. The company noted that clearer policies on the storage, processing, and protection of sensitive government data have grown more important as cyber threats continue to target critical infrastructure and public institutions.

  According to Globe, the Philippines joins Australia, Singapore, India, and European Union member states in adopting data governance frameworks designed to balance digital innovation with data protection requirements.

  ## Significance

  EO 119 gives government agencies and their technology partners a defined legal basis for cloud adoption decisions, specifying which data categories can move off-premise and which cannot. For the data center and cloud services industry operating in or targeting the Philippine government market, the order sets the compliance boundaries within which contracts and infrastructure investments will need to be structured. The residency requirement for top-secret and secret data creates demand for sovereign or on-shore infrastructure capable of meeting the order's security controls.

  總統費迪南德·馬科斯二世（Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr.）於7月13日簽署第119號行政令，建立更新後的政府資料分類制度，並針對各分類設定相應的資料在地化要求。此令明確劃定界線：最敏感的資訊須保留在菲律賓境內，其他類別的資料則可在符合特定防護措施的前提下，透過雲端技術儲存。

  ## 第119號行政令的規範內容

  此令建立了分層架構。列為最高機密或機密等級的資料須受嚴格的在地化管制，必須存放於菲律賓境內。較低分類的資料則可存放於雲端環境，前提是相關機關須符合本令所規定的安全管控要求。此框架旨在讓政府機關持續採納雲端與數位工具，同時保護政府認定攸關國家安全及基本公共服務的機敏資訊。

  ## 私部門的回應

  菲律賓主要電信暨數位服務供應商之一的環球電信（Globe Telecom, Inc.）表示，此令為政府機關及其民間合作夥伴提供了一套可行的框架。環球電信法務長卡斯特洛（Vicente Froilan M. Castelo）將第119號行政令形容為「菲律賓數位轉型歷程中的重要里程碑」，並指出該令提供了「清晰且以風險為本的框架」，在保護政府機敏資訊的同時，也允許負責任地運用現代數位技術。

  環球電信總裁暨執行長克魯茲（Carl Raymond R. Cruz）表示，此令正視了數位政府服務中安全性與公眾信任之間的關係。「數位轉型唯有在民眾信任政府系統安全無虞的前提下，方能成功，」克魯茲說，並補充指出，此令允許各機關推動現代化轉型，同時確保機敏資訊受到最高等級的保護。

  ## 更廣泛的政策背景

  環球電信表示，隨著數位公共服務、雲端運算及人工智慧的持續擴展，全球各國政府紛紛強化網路安全框架。該公司指出，隨著網路威脅持續以關鍵基礎設施及公共機構為攻擊目標，政府機敏資料在儲存、處理及保護方面的政策更加清晰已日形重要。

  根據環球電信的說法，菲律賓已加入澳洲、新加坡、印度及歐盟成員國的行列，共同採納旨在兼顧數位創新與資料保護要求的資料治理框架。

  ## 重要意義

  第119號行政令為政府機關及其科技合作夥伴提供了明確的法律依據，供其做出雲端採用決策，具體規範哪些類別的資料可遷移至場外、哪些則不得如此。對於在菲律賓經營或以菲律賓政府市場為目標的資料中心及雲端服務業者而言，此令設定了合約與基礎建設投資所需符合的法規邊界。最高機密與機密資料的在地化要求，將帶動對主權或境內基礎設施的需求，使其具備符合本令安全管控標準的能力。
