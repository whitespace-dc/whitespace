body_en: |
  Spain has enacted a binding regulation requiring data centres with electrical capacity above 10 megawatts (MW) to meet renewable-energy sourcing, power-efficiency, and water-efficiency standards before obtaining grid connection rights, under Royal Decreto 1032/2026 published by the Ministry for Ecological Transition and Demographic Challenge (MITECO).

  The decree sets an 80 percent renewable energy threshold and requires those renewables to be matched on an hourly basis against actual consumption — a method known as hourly correlation — rather than offset through annual renewable energy certificate purchases. The energy sources must also demonstrate additionality, meaning operators must support the creation of new renewable capacity rather than simply buying credits from existing installations.

  ## Efficiency ceilings and reporting obligations

  During the transition period, the regulation establishes a Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) ceiling of 1.25. PUE is calculated by dividing total facility energy consumption by the energy consumed by IT equipment alone; a PUE of 1.25 means that for every watt used by servers, the facility consumes an additional 0.25 watts on cooling, power distribution, lighting, and other overhead. The rule also sets a Water Usage Effectiveness (WUE) ceiling of 0.1, a metric that measures water consumed per unit of IT energy and reflects the efficiency of water-based cooling systems.

  Both metrics must align with energy and water efficiency labelling standards being developed at the European Union level for data centres, according to MITECO's published materials.

  Data centres with capacity above 500 kilowatts (kW) — a lower threshold than the 10 MW grid-access rule — are required to submit energy efficiency and sustainability data to the government for eventual public disclosure.

  ## Digital sovereignty provisions

  The decree introduces data localisation requirements for facilities hosting high-security government systems. Sensitive data in sectors including healthcare, energy, transportation, and taxation must remain within the European Union or European Economic Area. Third-country access to management and maintenance functions for such systems is restricted under the new framework.

  ## Policy rationale

  Spain's government stated that data centre growth without regulatory constraints could increase costs across the national electricity system. The country has attracted substantial data centre investment, which the government attributed to its high share of renewable generation and available land resources. The stated concern is that large, high-load facilities connecting to the grid in volume could place disproportionate pressure on the energy system. The regulation is designed to align the expansion of digital infrastructure with Spain's energy transition by making grid access eligibility contingent on verified sustainability performance.

  ## What the rules mean for operators

  For data centres above 10 MW seeking new grid connections, compliance with the 80 percent hourly-matched renewable threshold and the PUE and WUE ceilings is a prerequisite under the new framework. Operators of facilities above 500 kW will separately need to begin compiling efficiency and sustainability data for submission and public reporting.
body_zh: |
  西班牙已頒布具法律約束力的法規，規定電力容量超過10百萬瓦（MW）的資料中心，必須在取得電網連接權之前，符合再生能源採購、電力效率及用水效率標準。此一法規依據生態轉型與人口挑戰部（MITECO）所發布的《皇家法令1032/2026》施行。

  該法令設定80%的再生能源門檻，並要求業者以每小時為單位，將再生能源供應量與實際用電量進行配對——此方法稱為「每小時相關性配對」——而非透過購買年度再生能源憑證來抵消用電。能源來源同時須符合「額外性」原則，意即業者必須支持新增再生能源裝置容量的建設，而非僅購買現有設施所產生的憑證。

  ## 效率上限與申報義務

  在過渡期間，法規設定電源使用效率（PUE）上限為1.25。PUE的計算方式是以設施總耗電量除以IT設備本身的耗電量；PUE為1.25意味著，伺服器每消耗1瓦電力，整體設施便另外消耗0.25瓦用於冷卻、電力配送、照明及其他附屬設備。法規同時設定用水效率（WUE）上限為0.1，此指標衡量每單位IT能源所消耗的水量，反映水冷系統的效率表現。

  根據MITECO發布的相關文件，上述兩項指標均須與歐盟層級正在制定中的資料中心能源與用水效率標章標準接軌。

  電力容量超過500千瓦（kW）的資料中心——其門檻低於電網接入規定的10 MW——須向主管機關提交能源效率與永續性資料，以供日後公開揭露。

  ## 數位主權條款

  該法令針對託管高安全等級政府系統的設施，引入資料在地化要求。包括醫療保健、能源、交通及稅務等敏感領域的資料，必須留存於歐盟或歐洲經濟區境內。在新規框架下，第三國實體存取此類系統之管理與維護功能的權限受到明確限制。

  ## 政策背景說明

  西班牙政府表示，若資料中心在缺乏監管限制的情況下持續擴張，可能推高全國電力系統的整體成本。西班牙近年吸引了大量資料中心投資，政府將此歸因於該國高比例的再生能源發電量及豐沛的可用土地資源。政府擔憂的是，大量高負載設施集中接入電網，可能對能源系統造成不成比例的壓力。此一法規旨在將數位基礎設施的擴張與西班牙的能源轉型接軌，以經驗證的永續性表現作為取得電網接入資格的先決條件。

  ## 對業者的實際影響

  對於尋求新電網連接的10 MW以上資料中心，在新規框架下，符合80%每小時配對再生能源門檻及PUE、WUE上限，是取得資格的前提條件。電力容量超過500 kW的設施業者，則須另行著手彙整效率與永續性資料，以備提交及公開申報之用。
