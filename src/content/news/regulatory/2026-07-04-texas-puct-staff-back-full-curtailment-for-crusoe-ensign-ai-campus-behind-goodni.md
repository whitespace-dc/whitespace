body_en: |
  Staff at the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) have recommended approving a 260 megawatt (MW) AI data center expansion by Crusoe and Ensign Infrastructure on the condition that the facility remain capable of full emergency curtailment, endorsing a position from grid operator ERCOT that the developer says exceeds what state law authorizes and potentially establishing precedent for how reliability obligations scale at colocated AI campuses across Texas.

  The PUCT staff recommendation, filed June 26, 2026, comes in a proceeding that is among the first to test Texas's new framework for placing large electric loads behind the interconnection point of an existing generator, an arrangement known as co-location that can shorten the timeline to grid power but raises contested questions about who bears reliability obligations during grid emergencies.

  ## The Goodnight campus

  The application centers on a proposal to add "Crusoe Load 2," a 260 MW AI data center, behind the 265.5 MW Goodnight 1 wind farm in Armstrong County, Texas. Crusoe Load 2 would share Goodnight 1's existing point of interconnection (POI) with a previously approved facility, "Crusoe Load 1," rated at 265.5 MW. The POI is the electrical tie point where the wind farm connects to the broader ERCOT grid. Together, the two data centers would create 525.5 MW of behind-the-meter load at the same POI.

  "Behind-the-meter" in this context means the data centers are electrically located on the generator's side of the grid connection, drawing power before it reaches the ERCOT market and therefore outside standard grid-side metering and scheduling requirements.

  ## What PUCT staff and ERCOT are asking

  PUCT staff recommended approving the application subject to the same six operating conditions the Commission imposed earlier this year on Crusoe Load 1. The most consequential of those conditions requires Crusoe Load 2 to curtail — reduce or halt consumption — within 30 minutes whenever ERCOT determines that Goodnight 1's generation is needed to address a transmission emergency or an Energy Emergency Alert (EEA), a formal declaration that grid supply is at risk. If Crusoe Load 2 later demonstrates the capability, that window would tighten to 10 minutes.

  PUCT staff's rationale is that both facilities share Goodnight 1's POI and should therefore be governed by the same reliability framework, regardless of the order in which they were approved. ERCOT made a parallel argument, adding that Ensign and Crusoe could have avoided the review requirement by constructing a separate POI for the expansion; by choosing the existing interconnection, ERCOT argued, the project became subject to the review process established under Senate Bill 6 (SB 6).

  The review process operates under PUCT Rule 25.205, which the Commission adopted to implement SB 6's provisions governing net-metering arrangements between existing generators and large colocated loads.

  ## Ensign's objection

  Ensign does not dispute the Commission's authority to impose reliability conditions but argues the scale of what ERCOT is requesting exceeds what the underlying statute authorizes for this project.

  Ensign's argument rests on sequencing. Crusoe Load 1, approved earlier, already carries an obligation to curtail up to 265.5 MW during emergency conditions, which Ensign says restores Goodnight 1's full generating capacity to the ERCOT market. Because that obligation already covers the wind farm's output, Ensign contends there is no remaining reliability deficit requiring Crusoe Load 2 to curtail as well.

  Ensign also asked regulators to evaluate net system effects rather than total behind-the-meter load, citing ERCOT studies that found no thermal or voltage violations and no need for new transmission facilities as a result of the proposed expansion.

  PUCT staff rejected that interpretation, asserting that the shared POI governs the analysis rather than the sequence of project approvals, and that both data centers must remain capable of curtailing so Goodnight 1's available capacity is accessible to ERCOT during emergencies. Staff also declined to credit the planned Goodnight 2 wind project as a mitigating factor, arguing it falls outside the scope of the current application. ERCOT made the same point, noting that Crusoe Load 2 would still interconnect behind Goodnight 1 regardless of any future development at Goodnight 2.

  ## Industry implications

  Chris Talley, co-founder of grid data firm Interconnection.fyi, said the Commission's decision could reshape how developers design future AI campuses in Texas.

  "The precedent is really about proportionality," Talley said. "If ERCOT wins, colocating any load with any qualifying standalone generator locks in 100% curtailment risk on the entirety of that load, plus exclusion from all demand response programs, regardless of how small the generator is relative to the load."

  Talley pointed to a hypothetical raised in Ensign's brief: whether a 1 gigawatt (GW, or 1,000 MW) data center colocated with a 50 MW generator could be required to curtail the full 1 GW facility during emergencies. Under ERCOT's reading of the rules, Talley said, the answer would be yes.

  The practical consequence, Talley said, is that co-location would become viable primarily for developers who can build on-site backup generation large enough to serve their entire facility load, because ERCOT's condition requires the full load to curtail during emergencies rather than only enough to offset the colocated generator's contribution. He cited Amazon Data Services' pending "Project Spectrum" application involving the Comanche Peak nuclear plant as a contrasting design approach, under which the data center would transition to customer-owned backup generation during emergencies while returning the nuclear plant's output to the grid.

  Talley cautioned against reading ERCOT's stance as opposition to AI development. "They're navigating a really hard tradeoff to figure out how to preserve grid reliability as data center load grows without shutting out the developers that Texas wants to attract," he said. Co-location can reduce the need for new transmission and improve grid resilience if operators can return the generator's output to ERCOT during emergencies, Talley noted. He also said that many inference-focused facilities and hyperscale data centers with strict uptime commitments already plan for full backup capacity regardless of regulatory requirements, which could blunt the deterrent effect of a broad curtailment rule.

  A PUCT decision is expected later this summer, and the ruling will join several other pending SB 6 proceedings as an early indicator of how Texas intends to apply its co-location framework as AI developers continue to seek power for new campuses.
body_zh: |
  德州公用事業委員會（PUCT）幕僚已建議核准Crusoe與Ensign Infrastructure提出的260百萬瓦（MW）AI資料中心擴建案，但附帶條件是該設施必須具備全面緊急削峰的能力。此舉支持了電網營運商ERCOT的立場——而開發商認為此要求超出州法授權範圍——同時也可能為德州各地共址AI園區的可靠度義務如何計算樹立判例。

  PUCT幕僚建議書於2026年6月26日提交，所涉程序是德州測試新框架的首批案例之一。該框架規範如何將大型電力負載設置於既有發電機組的併網接入點（POI）後方，此種安排稱為「共址」，雖可縮短接入電網的時程，卻也引發爭議：在電網緊急狀況下，可靠度義務究竟由誰承擔？

  ## 古德奈特園區

  本申請案的核心，是在德州阿姆斯壯郡265.5 MW的古德奈特1號風電場後方，新增一座名為「Crusoe負載2號」的260 MW AI資料中心。Crusoe負載2號將與先前已獲核准的「Crusoe負載1號」（額定容量265.5 MW）共用古德奈特1號的現有POI。POI是風電場連接至廣大ERCOT電網的電氣接入節點。兩座資料中心合計，將在同一POI後方形成525.5 MW的表後負載。

  此處所稱「表後」，意指資料中心在電氣上位於發電機組的電網連接點的電廠側，在電力進入ERCOT市場之前即直接取用，因此不受標準電網側電表計量與排程規定約束。

  ## PUCT幕僚與ERCOT的要求

  PUCT幕僚建議在六項相同的營運條件下核准本申請——這六項條件正是委員會年初對Crusoe負載1號所設定的。其中影響最為深遠的一項，要求Crusoe負載2號在ERCOT認定古德奈特1號的發電量有必要用於處理輸電緊急狀況，或發出「電力緊急警報」（Energy Emergency Alert，EEA，一種正式聲明，代表電網供電面臨風險）時，須在30分鐘內削峰——亦即削減或停止用電。若Crusoe負載2號日後證明具備相應能力，這一時限將縮短至10分鐘。

  PUCT幕僚的理由是：兩座設施共用古德奈特1號的POI，因此不論核准順序為何，均應適用相同的可靠度框架。ERCOT也提出類似論點，並補充指出，Ensign與Crusoe本可為擴建部分另建一個獨立的POI，從而迴避審查要求；既然選擇沿用既有的互聯接入點，ERCOT認為，該案自然應受《參議院第6號法案》（Senate Bill 6，SB 6）所確立的審查程序規範。

  此審查程序依據PUCT第25.205號規則運作，該規則由委員會依照SB 6的規定制定，專門管理既有發電機組與大型共址負載之間的淨計量安排。

  ## Ensign的異議

  Ensign並不質疑委員會的監管權限，但主張ERCOT的要求規模超出了基礎法規對本案所授予的範圍。

  Ensign的論點立基於審批順序。先前獲准的Crusoe負載1號，已承擔在緊急狀況下削減最多265.5 MW的義務，Ensign表示，這已足以將古德奈特1號的完整發電容量恢復供應ERCOT市場。既然此義務已涵蓋風電場的全部輸出，Ensign認為並不存在需要Crusoe負載2號同樣削峰的可靠度缺口。

  Ensign也要求主管機關評估整體系統的淨影響，而非僅看錶後負載的總量，並引用ERCOT的研究結果——該研究顯示，擬議的擴建計畫並未造成任何熱能或電壓違規，也無需新建輸電設施。

  PUCT幕僚拒絕採納這一解釋，主張分析基準應以共用的POI為準，而非以專案核准順序為據，且兩座資料中心均必須保有削峰能力，以確保古德奈特1號的可用容量在緊急時可供ERCOT調用。幕僚亦拒絕將計畫中的古德奈特2號風電項目視為緩解因素，認為其超出本申請案的審查範疇。ERCOT也持相同立場，指出無論古德奈特2號未來如何發展，Crusoe負載2號仍將接在古德奈特1號後方進行互聯。

  ## 產業影響

  電網數據公司Interconnection.fyi共同創辦人克里斯·塔利（Chris Talley）表示，委員會的裁決可能重塑開發商在德州規劃未來AI園區的方式。

  「這個判例真正的核心是比例原則，」塔利說。「如果ERCOT勝訴，任何負載只要與任何符合資格的獨立發電機組共址，就等於鎖定了對該負載全部容量的百分之百削峰風險，且不論發電機組相對於負載有多小，同時還將被排除在所有需求回應計畫之外。」

  塔利指出Ensign意見書中提出的一個假設情境：若一座1 GW（即1,000 MW）的資料中心與一座50 MW的發電機組共址，在緊急狀況下是否可要求其削減整個1 GW設施的用電？依照ERCOT對規則的解讀，塔利表示，答案將是肯定的。

  塔利說，這帶來的實際影響，是共址模式將主要對那些能夠在場址內建設足以供應整座設施負載的備用發電設施的開發商才具可行性——因為ERCOT的條件要求的是全部負載削峰，而非僅削減足以抵銷共址發電機組貢獻的部分。他以亞馬遜資料服務（Amazon Data Services）待審的「Spectrum計畫」為例作為對照，該案涉及科曼奇峰核電廠，在此設計方案下，資料中心將在緊急情況下切換至客戶自備的備用發電設施，同時將核電廠的輸出電力釋回電網。

  塔利也提醒不宜將ERCOT的立場解讀為反對AI發展。「他們正在應對一個非常困難的權衡取捨，試圖在資料中心負載持續增長的情況下維護電網可靠度，同時又不排擠德州希望吸引的開發商，」他說。塔利指出，若業者能在緊急狀況下將發電機組的輸出電力釋回ERCOT，共址安排其實可以減少新建輸電設施的需求，並提升電網韌性。他也提到，許多以推論工作負載為主的設施，以及對正常運作時間有嚴格要求的超大規模資料中心，無論法規要求為何，本已計畫備有完整的備用容量，因此廣泛削峰規則的嚇阻效果可能相當有限。

  PUCT預計在今年夏天稍晚作出裁決，此裁決將與其他數件待審的SB 6程序案件並列，成為德州在AI開發商持續為新園區尋求電力之際，如何落實其共址框架的早期指標。
