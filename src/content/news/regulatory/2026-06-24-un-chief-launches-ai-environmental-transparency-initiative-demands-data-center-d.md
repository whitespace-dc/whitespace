body_en: |
  UN Secretary-General António Guterres on Tuesday called on major artificial intelligence companies to publicly disclose the full environmental costs of their data centers and commit to running them entirely on renewable energy by 2030, launching what the United Nations is calling the AI Environmental Transparency Initiative during London Climate Action Week.

  The initiative targets a sector that has so far relied on voluntary commitments rather than standardized public reporting. Guterres framed the disclosure demand as a precondition for AI's credibility as a tool for societal benefit. "If AI is to help build a better future, it must be honest about what it costs us now," he said in his address.

  ## Scale of projected resource consumption

  Guterres cited figures that underscore the urgency behind the initiative. By 2030, global data centers built to support AI development could consume more electricity than all but five countries, he said. On water use, he said the same infrastructure could draw enough water annually to meet the basic needs of all 1.3 billion residents of sub-Saharan Africa for an entire year.

  The rapid global expansion of data centers to support AI workloads has drawn scrutiny from environmental groups, who have pointed to the sector's high energy and water consumption and its limited transparency on those impacts.

  ## What the initiative asks of AI firms

  The UN's transparency initiative calls on AI companies to measure and publicly disclose three categories of impact: water use, carbon emissions, and land use. It also calls on those companies to commit to sourcing all data center power from renewable energy by 2030.

  Currently, AI companies manage their environmental footprint through voluntary net-zero pledges and renewable electricity procurement targets. Many are also turning to natural gas or promoting nuclear power as energy sources for new data center projects, according to the source.

  ## Broader climate remarks

  Guterres used the same address to state that the world remains off track to meet global climate goals and to criticize calls for expanded fossil fuel use. He said deploying more renewable energy projects and using that power to electrify transport, buildings, and industry is among the fastest available paths to cutting emissions and reducing dependence on imported fossil fuels.

  He separately launched a call to action on methane, the greenhouse gas responsible for approximately one-third of current global warming, according to Guterres. That call targets the fossil fuel industry and asks companies to fix pipeline and facility leaks, end routine flaring of gas at oil wells, and adopt a science-based global standard for methane management.

  ## What comes next

  Guterres also announced plans to convene world leaders in September, ahead of COP31, the United Nations Climate Conference scheduled to take place in Turkey. The September gathering is intended to advance a "just transition" away from fossil fuels in preparation for the formal climate negotiations.
body_zh: |
  聯合國秘書長安東尼歐·古特瑞斯（António Guterres）週二呼籲各大人工智慧企業，公開揭露其資料中心的完整環境成本，並承諾於2030年前全面改用再生能源運作。相關倡議在倫敦氣候行動週期間正式啟動，聯合國將其命名為「AI環境透明度倡議」（AI Environmental Transparency Initiative）。

  這項倡議鎖定的是一個迄今仍依賴自願性承諾、缺乏標準化公開報告的產業。古特瑞斯將揭露要求定位為AI作為社會公益工具之公信力的先決條件。他在致詞中表示：「若AI要協助建構更美好的未來，它必須誠實面對當下所付出的代價。」

  ## 預估資源消耗的規模

  古特瑞斯引述一系列數據，凸顯這項倡議背後的迫切性。他表示，到2030年，全球為支援AI發展而興建的資料中心，其電力消耗量可能超越全球絕大多數國家，僅有五個國家的用電量超過它。在用水方面，他指出同樣的基礎設施每年耗水量之大，足以滿足撒哈拉以南非洲地區全體13億居民整整一年的基本用水需求。

  全球資料中心為支援AI工作負載而快速擴張，已引發環保團體的高度關注。這些團體指出，該產業的能源與用水消耗量相當龐大，且在相關影響方面的透明度極為有限。

  ## 倡議對AI企業的要求

  聯合國這項透明度倡議，要求AI企業針對三大類影響進行量測並公開揭露：用水量、碳排放量及土地使用情況。倡議同時要求各企業承諾，於2030年前將所有資料中心的電力來源全面轉換為再生能源。

  目前，各AI企業是透過自願性的淨零承諾與再生電力採購目標，來管理自身的環境足跡。據報導，許多企業也正轉向天然氣，或積極倡導以核能作為新資料中心的電力來源。

  ## 更廣泛的氣候議題談話

  古特瑞斯在同一場致詞中指出，全球在實現氣候目標方面仍嚴重偏離正軌，並對擴大化石燃料使用的相關主張提出批評。他表示，加速部署再生能源專案，並以此電力推動交通、建築與工業電氣化，是目前削減碳排放、降低對進口化石燃料依賴最快速可行的路徑之一。

  他另外就甲烷問題單獨發起行動呼籲。據古特瑞斯表示，甲烷這種溫室氣體目前約占全球暖化成因的三分之一。這項呼籲針對化石燃料產業，要求企業修復管線與生產設施的洩漏問題、終止油井的例行性廢氣燃除作業，並採用以科學為基礎的全球甲烷管理標準。

  ## 後續展望

  古特瑞斯同時宣布，計畫於9月召集各國領袖舉行會議，作為聯合國氣候大會COP31的前置準備——COP31預定在土耳其舉行。這場9月峰會旨在推進化石燃料的「公正轉型」（just transition），為正式氣候談判奠定基礎。
