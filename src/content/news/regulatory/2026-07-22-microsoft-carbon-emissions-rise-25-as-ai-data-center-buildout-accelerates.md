body_en: |
  Microsoft's total greenhouse gas emissions rose 25% in fiscal year 2025, reaching 20.29 million metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent, up from 16.22 million metric tons the prior year, according to the company's latest Environmental Sustainability Report. The increase continues a multiyear trend tied to the company's expanding cloud computing and AI infrastructure.

  The report shows cumulative emissions are now 23% above Microsoft's fiscal year 2020 baseline, reflecting sustained growth in data center construction and hardware procurement.

  ## What is driving the increase

  The largest emissions category for Microsoft is Scope 3, which covers pollution generated indirectly through the purchase of goods, services, and heavy equipment — including the steel, concrete, and servers required to build and equip data centers. Scope 3 emissions have been the primary driver of Microsoft's rising totals as the company has accelerated facility construction to meet demand for AI computing capacity.

  Fiscal year 2025 also brought a sharp shift in Scope 2 emissions, which measure pollution associated with purchased electricity. Scope 2 rose to 13% of Microsoft's total emissions in fiscal year 2025, compared with roughly 2% in fiscal year 2024. Microsoft attributed the jump to a deliberate change in how it accounts for renewable energy procurement.

  ## A shift in renewable energy accounting

  Microsoft said it has stopped relying on inexpensive renewable energy certificates — paper instruments that allow companies to claim credit for clean power generation without necessarily funding new capacity — in favor of directly funding the construction of new clean power projects. The company acknowledged that this accounting change makes its reported emissions figures appear higher in the near term, but said the approach is intended to support the development of additional generating capacity rather than purchasing credits tied to existing projects.

  In fiscal year 2025, Microsoft said it matched 100% of its annual global electricity consumption with renewable energy. The company has also signed agreements to bring 40 gigawatts of new clean power capacity online across 26 countries, of which 19 gigawatts are already operational. For context, 40 gigawatts is roughly equivalent to the output of 40 large conventional power plants, or enough to power tens of millions of average homes.

  ## Carbon removal purchasing slows sharply

  Microsoft has historically been among the largest buyers of carbon removal credits — contracts that fund projects designed to pull carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere — but its purchasing activity slowed significantly in the first half of 2026. According to data from carbon credit rating firm Sylvera, Microsoft's forward carbon removal contracts fell 65% year over year in the first half of 2026, to 21.52 million tons from 61 million tons in the same period of 2025. The total announced contract value dropped 70%, from $7.46 billion to $2.25 billion.

  Microsoft's scale in this market has made its pullback consequential for the broader carbon removal industry. In the first half of 2025, Microsoft accounted for 54.08 million tons of carbon removal purchases, representing 88.7% of the entire global market for that period, according to Sylvera.

  ## What it means for the industry

  Microsoft's emissions trajectory illustrates the tension facing large technology companies that have committed to carbon reduction targets while simultaneously scaling AI infrastructure at a rapid pace. The company's shift away from renewable energy certificates toward funding new generation capacity raises the bar for how corporate clean energy claims are substantiated, but it also makes near-term emissions figures larger and harder to offset.

  The sharp contraction in Microsoft's carbon removal purchasing represents a significant demand shock to a nascent market that had been heavily reliant on a single buyer.   ## 微軟碳排放量年增25%，AI資料中心擴建加速為主因

  根據微軟最新發布的《環境永續報告》，微軟2025財年的溫室氣體排放總量達到2,029萬公噸二氧化碳當量，較前一年的1,622萬公噸增加25%。這一趨勢延續了該公司近年來因持續擴張雲端運算與AI基礎設施所帶來的排放成長軌跡。

  報告顯示，以微軟2020財年為基準，累計排放量目前已超出基準值23%，反映出資料中心興建及硬體採購的持續擴大。

  ## 排放量攀升的主要原因

  微軟最大的排放類別是範疇三（Scope 3），涵蓋企業在採購商品、服務及大型設備過程中間接產生的污染，包括興建資料中心及相關設備所需的鋼鐵、混凝土與伺服器。隨著微軟加快設施興建步伐以因應AI運算需求，範疇三排放一直是其排放總量持續攀升的主要驅動力。

  2025財年，範疇二（Scope 2）排放也出現明顯轉變。範疇二衡量企業因購電所產生的間接排放，在2025財年佔微軟總排放量的13%，而2024財年僅約2%。微軟表示，這一大幅躍升源於該公司在再生能源採購計算方式上所做的主動調整。

  ## 再生能源計算方式的轉變

  微軟表示，該公司已停止使用廉價的再生能源憑證——這種書面工具讓企業得以宣稱使用潔淨電力，但實際上未必有資助新發電設施的建設——轉而直接投資興建新的潔淨能源設施。微軟坦承，此一計算方式的調整將使短期內的申報排放數字顯得較高，但該公司強調，這樣的做法旨在支持新增發電容量的開發，而非購買與既有設施掛鉤的憑證。

  2025財年，微軟表示已以再生能源抵換其全球100%的年度用電量。該公司也已在全球26個國家簽署協議，計畫新增40吉瓦（GW）的潔淨能源裝置容量，其中19吉瓦已投入運轉。以規模換算，40吉瓦大約相當於40座大型傳統發電廠的總發電量，足以供應數千萬戶一般家庭用電。

  ## 碳移除採購量大幅萎縮

  微軟長期以來是全球最大的碳移除信用額度買家之一，購買的合約用於資助專門從大氣中吸收二氧化碳的專案，然而其採購活動在2026年上半年明顯降溫。根據碳信用評等機構Sylvera的資料，微軟在2026年上半年的遠期碳移除合約較去年同期大幅下降65%，從2025年同期的6,100萬公噸降至2,152萬公噸，合約總公告金額更從74.6億美元驟降至22.5億美元，跌幅達70%。

  微軟在這個市場舉足輕重，因此其採購力道的收縮對整個碳移除產業影響深遠。Sylvera資料顯示，2025年上半年，微軟的碳移除採購量達5,408萬公噸，佔同期全球市場總量的88.7%。

  ## 對產業的意義

  微軟的碳排放走勢，清楚呈現出大型科技公司面臨的兩難困境——一邊承諾達成減碳目標，另一邊卻同時快速擴建AI基礎設施。微軟從再生能源憑證轉向直接投資新發電設施，提高了企業宣稱使用潔淨能源的實質標準，但也使短期排放數字更加難以壓低及抵銷。

  微軟大幅縮減碳移除採購，對這個原本高度仰賴單一買家的新興市場造成顯著的需求衝擊。目前，微軟已簽署合約，計畫在26個國家開發合計40吉瓦的新潔淨能源裝置容量，其中19吉瓦已正式上線運轉。
