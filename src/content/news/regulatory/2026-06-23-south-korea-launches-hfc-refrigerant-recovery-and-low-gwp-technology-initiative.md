body_en: |
  South Korea's Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment and the Korea Environmental Industry & Technology Institute (KEITI) have launched a national initiative to overhaul the country's management of hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants — synthetic gases widely used in cooling and heating systems that carry high global warming potential (GWP, a measure of how much heat a gas traps relative to carbon dioxide).

  The project began this month and covers four areas: refrigerant recovery technology, regeneration and destruction technology for mixed waste refrigerants, development of low-GWP refrigerant materials, and the introduction of low-charge heat pump systems that use propane as a refrigerant.

  ## Expanding recovery requirements and improving traceability

  Refrigerant recovery — the process of capturing refrigerant from equipment before servicing or disposal to prevent atmospheric release — is already legally required in South Korea for equipment with a refrigeration capacity of 20 RT (refrigeration tons, a unit of cooling capacity) or more. The new initiative seeks to extend that requirement and develop high-performance recovery equipment with significantly faster recovery speeds, according to the ministry.

  To improve transparency, recovered quantities will be linked to the Refrigerant Information Management System (RIMS), an online regulatory platform operated by the Korea Environment Corporation. RIMS tracks the full lifecycle of fluorinated refrigerants — from sale through usage, recovery, and disposal — under the requirements of South Korea's Clean Air Conservation Act.

  ## Regeneration, destruction, and next-generation refrigerants

  Beyond recovery, the project will develop regeneration technology to return recovered refrigerants to usable condition, as well as high-efficiency, low-cost destruction technology for mixed waste refrigerants that cannot be reused.

  In parallel, the initiative targets the development of low-charge, high-efficiency heat pump technology using propane — a natural refrigerant with a GWP near zero — for use in multi-unit residential buildings such as apartments. It will also develop refrigerant leak detection and control systems to enable safe propane use in those settings, the ministry said.

  ## Alignment with international and national climate targets

  The ministry framed the initiative as a direct response to tightening international regulations on refrigerants and as a contribution to South Korea's National Greenhouse Gas Reduction Target (NDC), the country's binding climate commitment under the Paris Agreement.

  "We will do our utmost to reduce the burden on our industries and establish a robust refrigerant supply chain by proactively responding to the international community's tightening regulations on refrigerants and actively supporting the introduction of next-generation eco-friendly and recycled refrigerants," said Kim Jin-sik, director general of the Air Environment Bureau at the Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment.

  ## What this means for the sector

  The initiative signals a shift from compliance monitoring toward active circular-economy infrastructure for refrigerants — recovering, regenerating, and reusing materials rather than replacing them with new stock. For equipment manufacturers and service providers operating in South Korea, the expansion of mandatory recovery requirements beyond the current 20 RT threshold is the most immediate regulatory development to watch as the project's research phase progresses.

  ## 南韓啟動氫氟碳化物冷媒回收與低暖化潛勢技術計畫

  南韓環境部與韓國環境產業技術院（KEITI）共同發起一項全國性計畫，全面翻新該國對氫氟碳化物（HFC）冷媒的管理方式。HFC 是廣泛應用於冷暖氣系統的合成氣體，具有極高的全球暖化潛勢（GWP，衡量某種氣體相較於二氧化碳所能吸收熱量的指標）。

  該計畫本月正式啟動，涵蓋四大面向：冷媒回收技術、混合廢冷媒的再生與銷毀技術、低 GWP 冷媒材料的研發，以及導入以丙烷為冷媒的低充填量熱泵系統。

  ## 擴大回收義務與強化追蹤透明度

  冷媒回收是指在設備維修或報廢前，將冷媒捕集回收以防止其釋入大氣的程序。在南韓，製冷容量達 20 冷凍噸（RT）以上的設備，依法已須強制回收冷媒。此次計畫旨在進一步擴大該義務的適用範圍，並研發回收速度顯著提升的高效能回收設備，環境部表示。

  為提升管理透明度，回收量數據將與「冷媒資訊管理系統」（RIMS）介接。RIMS 是由韓國環境公團營運的線上法規平台，依據南韓《大氣環境保全法》的規定，全程追蹤含氟冷媒從銷售、使用、回收乃至廢棄處理的完整生命週期。

  ## 再生、銷毀與次世代冷媒

  除回收技術外，本計畫亦將開發再生技術，使已回收的冷媒得以恢復至可再利用的狀態；同時針對無法再利用的混合廢冷媒，研發高效率、低成本的銷毀技術。

  與此並行，計畫目標之一是開發以丙烷為冷媒的低充填量、高效率熱泵技術，應用於公寓等集合住宅建築。丙烷屬於天然冷媒，GWP 值接近零。計畫亦將同步開發冷媒洩漏偵測與管控系統，以確保丙烷在上述場所使用時的安全性，環境部補充說明。

  ## 與國際及國內氣候目標接軌

  環境部將此計畫定位為因應國際冷媒法規日益收緊的主動回應，同時也是對南韓依《巴黎協定》所承諾的「國家自定貢獻」（NDC）溫室氣體減量目標的具體貢獻。

  環境部大氣環境局局長金進植表示：「我們將竭盡全力，主動因應國際社會對冷媒日趨嚴格的管制，積極支持導入次世代環保及再生冷媒，從而減輕產業負擔，並建立穩健的冷媒供應鏈。」

  ## 對產業的意義

  此計畫標誌著南韓的冷媒管理方向，正從合規監控轉向積極建構冷媒的循環經濟基礎設施——著重於冷媒的回收、再生與再利用，而非一味以新品替換。對於在南韓營運的設備製造商與服務業者而言，隨著計畫研究階段持續推進，強制回收義務從現行 20 冷凍噸門檻進一步擴大的動向，是當下最值得密切關注的法規發展。
