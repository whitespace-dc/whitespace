body_en: |
  The US Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) issued an interim final rule (IFR) on November 16, 2026, requiring export licenses for shipments of advanced AI semiconductors to data centers in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil. The rule is intended to cut off channels through which Chinese companies and others in restricted jurisdictions have been accessing US-made chips by routing purchases through third countries and, in some cases, accessing US computing technology remotely.

  ## What the rule requires

  Under the IFR, exporters of advanced US semiconductors must obtain a BIS license before shipping to the five designated countries. Data centers, foreign foundries, and chip exporters operating in those markets are additionally required to conduct due diligence on their customers, including identifying any direct or indirect ownership or control by persons in restricted countries or Macau. License applications will be evaluated individually on the basis of US national security and foreign policy considerations, according to Indonesian media reporting.

  The rule also restricts remote access to advanced US semiconductor technology, closing a pathway that did not require the physical import of chips.

  ## Government responses

  Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information (MDDI) and Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry (METI) issued a joint statement saying they were "studying the details of this new measure carefully," were engaging affected companies, and had been in contact with US authorities "on the scope and implementation of the measure, as well as exploring how to facilitate trusted access for Singapore companies." The two ministries stated that Singapore "does not condone companies using their association with it to circumvent or violate laws of other countries" and that it would act against those who violate its laws.

  Indonesia's Government Communication Agency told Bloomberg that government officials were reviewing the measure and gathering industry input on its potential impact on operations and investment. The agency said Indonesia was developing a strategic trade management framework aimed at enabling legitimate trade while reducing the risk of technology diversion and misuse.

  Thailand indicated its readiness before the rule was published. Vice Minister for Commerce Kirida Bhaopichitr told CNBC ahead of the announcement that Thailand was prepared for chip export restrictions and would more carefully scrutinize chip importers to ensure products were not being shipped onward to restricted countries.

  Brazil's Ministry of Development, Industry, and Services said it was monitoring possible developments from the measure. The ministry clarified that the IFR is not a ban on advanced semiconductor exports to Brazil, but rather an authorization requirement, and said it was not yet possible to evaluate the financial impact on US chip imports.

  ## Supply chain implications

  TrendForce, a Taiwan-based market research firm, said the rule will raise the procurement threshold for AI chips at Southeast Asian data centers. The direct impact, TrendForce said, will fall on non-US companies and on compute rental operators whose customers are predominantly Chinese. Some orders may be delayed or reduced as license reviews are processed, and compliance costs for non-US cloud operators will rise.

  For Taiwan's AI server supply chain, TrendForce projected that near-term Southeast Asian shipments will concentrate among US cloud service providers (CSPs), while orders from non-US clients will require additional lead time to accommodate license processing.

  Graphics processing unit (GPU) rental operators that lose Chinese customers may face declining utilization rates. With new GPUs continuously reducing per-unit computing costs, existing clusters could face lower rental prices before recovering their capital investment, in turn delaying future equipment purchases, TrendForce said.

  Over the longer term, TrendForce said the controls could limit the total computing capacity available to China for AI model training, with potential consequences for China's competitiveness in that field. In the near term, Chinese CSPs are expected to shift demand toward domestic AI chips, custom application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), and Nvidia chips that remain legally importable, such as the RTX PRO 5500, though supply-side constraints will limit the quantities available. TrendForce noted that US-China negotiations on the new rule remain possible, and that the full impact would require further observation.

  ## The Megaspeed investigation

  The rule was announced against the backdrop of an active investigation into Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud computing company and Nvidia regional partner in Asia-Pacific. The US Department of Justice and the Department of Commerce have stated they intend to pursue any criminal conduct uncovered during the investigation. Neither agency disclosed specific allegations against Megaspeed, identified a suspected destination for any potentially diverted semiconductors, or indicated whether charges had been filed.

  Indonesian national police (Polri), acting on a request from the US Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), previously raided the Indonesian office of a Megaspeed subsidiary in early 2026, before the company became publicly prominent. Officers from the Criminal Investigation Agency (Bareskrim) found only server racks at the location, with no Nvidia GPUs present; the racks appeared to have never been unpacked from their packaging or put into use, according to an Indonesian government official who spoke to Singapore's Straits Times. The same official said investigators believed most Nvidia devices purchased by companies affiliated with Megaspeed were likely shipped to Indonesia and subsequently transported to China, possibly by private aircraft. The search was described as the first known enforcement action in Indonesia connected to the Megaspeed case.

  Megaspeed's name later surfaced in connection with a personal scandal involving Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang, who stepped down from that role.

  ## What comes next

  Singapore, Indonesia, and Thailand have each indicated they are engaging with US authorities or developing domestic frameworks to demonstrate that their markets can serve as trusted destinations for advanced semiconductors. Brazil said it cannot yet assess the financial impact of the new licensing requirement. TrendForce said the actual market impact will depend in part on whether the US and China enter negotiations over modifications to the IFR.
body_zh: |
  美國商務部工業暨安全局（BIS）於2026年11月16日頒布臨時最終規則（IFR），要求出口先進AI半導體至印尼、馬來西亞、新加坡、泰國及巴西資料中心時，須事先取得出口許可證。此舉旨在切斷中國企業等受限司法管轄區買家透過第三國繞道購買，乃至遠端存取美國晶片技術的管道。

  ## 規則要求內容

  根據IFR，出口商在向上述五個指定國家出貨先進美國半導體之前，必須先取得BIS許可證。在這些市場營運的資料中心、境外晶圓廠及晶片出口商，另須對客戶進行盡職調查，包括查明是否有來自受限國家或澳門的人士直接或間接持有股權或掌握控制權。據印尼媒體報導，許可證申請將依美國國家安全與外交政策考量逐案審核。

  此外，該規則亦限制遠端存取先進美國半導體技術，封堵了無須實際進口晶片即可取得相關算力的管道。

  ## 各國政府回應

  新加坡數位發展與資訊部（MDDI）與能源、貿易及工業部（METI）發表聯合聲明，表示正「仔細研究這項新措施的細節」，已與受影響企業溝通，並就該措施的範圍與執行方式與美方保持聯繫，同時積極探討如何為新加坡企業創造可信賴的取得管道。兩部會表示，新加坡「不容許企業借用其名義規避或違反其他國家的法律」，並將依法追究違規者的責任。

  印尼政府通訊局告訴彭博社，政府官員正在審查此措施，並向業界徵詢對營運和投資潛在衝擊的意見。該局表示，印尼正在制定戰略性貿易管理框架，目標是在促進合法貿易的同時，降低技術轉移與濫用的風險。

  泰國在規則正式發布前便已表明準備就緒。商業部副部長基里達·鮑皮奇特（Kirida Bhaopichitr）在消息公布前接受CNBC採訪時表示，泰國已為晶片出口管制做好準備，將更嚴格審查晶片進口商，確保相關產品不會被轉運至受限國家。

  巴西發展、工業及服務部表示，正在密切關注此措施可能帶來的後續發展。該部澄清，IFR並非禁止先進半導體出口至巴西，而是一項授權要求，並表示目前尚無法評估對美國晶片進口所造成的財務衝擊。

  ## 供應鏈影響

  台灣市場研究機構集邦科技（TrendForce）表示，此規則將提高東南亞資料中心採購AI晶片的門檻。集邦科技指出，直接衝擊將落在非美國企業，以及客戶群以中國業者為主的算力租賃業者身上。隨著許可證審核程序的推進，部分訂單可能延遲或縮減，非美系雲端業者的合規成本也將隨之上升。

  就台灣AI伺服器供應鏈而言，集邦科技預測，短期內東南亞出貨量將集中於美系雲端服務供應商（CSP），而非美系客戶的訂單則需要額外的前置時間，以配合許可證審核作業。

  失去中國客戶的繪圖處理器（GPU）租賃業者可能面臨使用率下滑的困境。集邦科技表示，由於新款GPU持續壓低單位算力成本，現有機群在回收資本投資之前，可能就要承受租金下調的壓力，進而延遲未來的設備採購計畫。

  從較長遠的角度來看，集邦科技認為，管制措施可能限制中國用於AI模型訓練的總算力，進而影響其在該領域的競爭力。短期內，中國雲端服務供應商預計將需求轉向國產AI晶片、客製化特殊應用積體電路（ASIC），以及仍可合法進口的輝達晶片（如RTX PRO 5500），但供應端的限制將制約可取得的數量。集邦科技也指出，美中雙方就新規展開談判的可能性仍然存在，全面影響有待進一步觀察。

  ## Megaspeed調查案

  此規則的宣布，發生在美國當局正積極調查Megaspeed案的背景之下。Megaspeed是一家總部位於新加坡的雲端運算公司，也是輝達（NVIDIA）在亞太地區的區域合作夥伴。美國司法部與商務部均表示，將追究調查過程中所發現的任何刑事不法行為。兩個機構均未披露針對Megaspeed的具體指控、指出任何可能遭轉運半導體的疑似目的地，也未說明是否已提出起訴。

  印尼國家警察（Polri）此前應美國聯邦調查局（FBI）的請求，於2026年初——彼時Megaspeed在公眾視野中尚未廣為人知——突擊搜查了Megaspeed一家子公司的印尼辦公室。刑事調查局（Bareskrim）人員在現場僅發現伺服器機架，並無輝達GPU；據一名向新加坡《海峽時報》透露消息的印尼政府官員表示，這些機架看似從未被拆封或投入使用。同一官員表示，調查人員認為，與Megaspeed相關企業所購買的大多數輝達設備，很可能已被運抵印尼後轉運至中國，轉運工具可能是私人飛機。此次搜查被描述為印尼境內首次已知的、與Megaspeed案相關的執法行動。

  Megaspeed的名字其後又出現在一宗涉及輝達執行長黃仁勳（Jensen Huang）的個人醜聞之中，黃仁勳事後辭去了該職務。

  ## 後續發展

  新加坡、印尼與泰國均已表示，正與美國當局接觸或制定國內框架，以證明本國市場可成為先進半導體的可信賴目的地。巴西則表示，目前尚無法評估新許可證要求所帶來的財務衝擊。集邦科技指出，實際市場影響在一定程度上將取決於美中雙方是否就IFR的修訂展開談判。
