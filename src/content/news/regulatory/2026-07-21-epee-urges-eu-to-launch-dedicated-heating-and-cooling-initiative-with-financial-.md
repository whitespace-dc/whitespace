body_en: |
  The European Partnership for Energy and the Environment (EPEE), a Brussels-based industry association representing the heating, cooling, and refrigeration sector, has called on the European Union to create a dedicated heating and cooling initiative supported by financial incentives, responding directly to the European Commission's newly published Electrification Action Plan.

  EPEE director general Russell Patten welcomed the Action Plan but said follow-through from member states and co-legislators is now essential. "This is the moment our industry has been waiting for," Patten said. "Heat pumps are the single most effective technology that helps Europe to cut oil and gas dependence, lower household energy bills and keep manufacturing jobs in Europe."

  ## What EPEE is asking for

  Beyond welcoming the Commission's document, EPEE is pressing for concrete measures that go further. Patten said the initiative must be accompanied by fiscal incentives and financial support mechanisms to bring efficient heating and cooling technologies within reach of households. "Europe cannot accelerate electrification without making efficient technologies genuinely affordable," he said.

  The association also broadened its argument beyond heating to include cooling, citing increasingly frequent periods of extreme heat as a reason access to cooling has become a public protection issue, not only a comfort or energy-efficiency matter.

  ## Context: the Electrification Action Plan

  The European Commission published its Electrification Action Plan the week before EPEE's statement, with an stated objective of accelerating electrification across Europe. EPEE acknowledged that the plan recognises efficient electrification as both a climate priority and a question of economic sovereignty — the latter a framing that has gained prominence in EU policy discussions around reducing dependence on imported oil and gas.

  Patten described the Action Plan as "a strong starting point" while making clear the association views it as insufficient on its own without sector-specific follow-up measures covering the full heating and cooling supply chain.

  ## What comes next

  EPEE's call places pressure on member state governments and the European Parliament, as co-legislators, to translate the Commission's Action Plan into binding or funded measures targeting the heating and cooling sector. The association has not specified a timeline or a target funding figure in its statement. The next steps depend on how member states and co-legislators respond to the Action Plan in the legislative process.

  ## EPEE呼籲歐盟推出專屬暖冷氣倡議並提供財政支持

  總部位於布魯塞爾、代表暖氣、冷氣及製冷產業的歐洲能源與環境夥伴關係組織（EPEE），在歐盟執行委員會甫發布《電氣化行動計畫》後，正式呼籲歐盟建立一項專屬的暖氣與冷氣倡議，並搭配相應的財政激勵措施。

  EPEE執行長羅素．帕頓（Russell Patten）對該行動計畫表示歡迎，但同時強調，成員國與共同立法機構的後續配合至關重要。「這正是我們產業長久以來期待的時機，」帕頓表示，「熱泵是歐洲擺脫石油與天然氣依賴、降低家庭能源帳單、並留住製造業就業機會的最有效單一技術。」

  ## EPEE的訴求

  EPEE不僅對執行委員會的文件表示歡迎，更積極推動更具體、更進一步的配套措施。帕頓表示，這項倡議必須輔以稅務優惠與財政支持機制，讓高效暖冷氣技術真正進入一般家庭的可負擔範圍。「若不讓高效技術真正具備可負擔性，歐洲就無法加速推動電氣化，」他說。

  該組織也將論述範疇從暖氣延伸至冷氣，指出極端高溫天氣日益頻繁，使得冷氣的普及已不再只是舒適性或節能效率的問題，而是攸關公眾保護的議題。

  ## 背景：《電氣化行動計畫》

  歐盟執行委員會在EPEE發表聲明前一週公布了《電氣化行動計畫》，明確以加速全歐洲電氣化進程為目標。EPEE認可該計畫將高效電氣化定位為氣候優先議題，同時也視其為「經濟主權」問題，後者這一論述框架在歐盟政策討論中日益受到重視，尤其是在降低對進口石油與天然氣依賴的脈絡下。

  帕頓形容《電氣化行動計畫》是「一個強而有力的起點」，但也明確表示，若缺乏針對整條暖冷氣供應鏈的特定產業後續配套措施，該計畫本身仍嫌不足。

  ## 後續動向

  EPEE的呼籲向成員國政府及歐洲議會（作為共同立法機構）施加壓力，要求將執行委員會的行動計畫轉化為具約束力或有資金支撐、專門針對暖冷氣產業的措施。EPEE在其聲明中並未明訂時間表或具體目標資金金額。後續進展將取決於成員國與共同立法機構在立法程序中如何回應該行動計畫。
