body_en: |
  Thailand's Cabinet approved noise limits and water-use requirements for data centres on Nov 10, responding to public concern over an AI-driven construction boom that had led the government to pause nearly 50 projects in September.

  Finance Minister Ekniti Nitithanprapas announced the decision at a news conference. "The use of national resources must not adversely affect the public," he said.

  ## New environmental standards

  Proposed standards include capping noise at site boundaries at 70 A-weighted decibels — slightly quieter than a vacuum cleaner, according to a comparison chart published by Yale University.

  The government also proposed limiting nearby temperature increases to between 1 and 2 degrees Celsius, and said cooling water must be recirculated and tested before discharge.

  New zoning rules were approved in principle: facilities consuming more than 20 megawatts (MW) of energy must be located in industrial zones and will face stricter oversight.

  ## Project pipeline

  Narit Therdsteerasukdi of Thailand's Board of Investment said 57 data centre projects have been approved nationally. Of those, 17 are operating, 20 have received construction permits, and 20 are preparing to invest.

  ## Speculative applications

  The government said it received a large number of speculative applications. More than 110 applicants have requested a combined 30,000 MW of electricity, Finance Minister Ekniti said, but only about a quarter or fewer had genuine investment plans.

  Eligible projects approved before Aug 14 will have one year to meet the new environmental guidelines.
body_zh: |
  泰國內閣於11月10日通過資料中心噪音限制與用水規定，回應人工智慧建設熱潮所引發的公眾疑慮——此前政府已於9月暫停近50個建設案。

  財政部長艾克尼提‧尼提坦普拉帕斯（Ekniti Nitithanprapas）在記者會上宣布這項決定。他表示：「使用國家資源不得對公眾造成不利影響。」

  ## 新環境標準

  擬定標準包括：在基地邊界處將噪音上限訂為70 A加權分貝——依耶魯大學發布的對照表，這略低於吸塵器的音量。

  政府亦提議將周邊溫度升幅限制在攝氏1至2度之間，並規定冷卻水必須循環使用，且須經檢測後方可排放。

  新分區規定已獲原則通過：能源消耗超過20百萬瓦（MW）的設施，必須設置於工業區，並將面臨更嚴格的監管。

  ## 建設案管線

  泰國投資促進委員會的那瑞特‧特德斯提拉蘇克迪（Narit Therdsteerasukdi）表示，全國共有57個資料中心建設案獲得核准。其中17個已在運營，20個已取得建設許可，另有20個正準備投資。

  ## 投機性申請

  政府表示，收到大量投機性申請。財政部長艾克尼提指出，逾110名申請者合計要求3萬MW的電力供應，但其中僅約四分之一或更少的申請者具有真實的投資計畫。

  在8月14日前獲准的合格建設案，將有一年時間符合新環境規範。
