body_en: |
  Indonesia is accelerating AI-driven data center construction and designating new hub locations while projections from a nonprofit waste-tracking organization warn the sector could generate hundreds of millions of metric tons of electronic waste by mid-century — a liability that investors and technology companies have largely omitted from their cost calculations.

  Coordinating Minister for Downstream and Energy Transition Bahlil Lahadalia announced at the Bali Tech Week 2026 forum in Sanur, Bali on October 19 that the government will formally designate Batam, Bintan, and Karimun — a cluster of islands in the Riau archipelago near Singapore — as new data center hubs. The government is pursuing data center development across multiple regions to attract investment while drawing on the country's water resources and renewable energy potential, according to Bisnis.com.

  ## Scale of projected e-waste

  The Basel Action Network (BAN), a U.S.-based nonprofit that monitors hazardous waste flows, estimates that data center construction could produce between 395 million and 617 million metric tons of electronic waste globally between 2025 and 2050. Server components and graphics chips — the processing units central to AI workloads — account for 13 percent of the total weight of data center equipment, BAN said.

  When electrical distribution systems and network infrastructure are added to the calculation, BAN's waste estimates rise by a factor of 40 to 60. That multiplier means hardware often cited in environmental reporting — servers and chips — represents only a small share of the eventual disposal challenge.

  BAN said no government, technology company, or international organization has yet developed mechanisms capable of handling waste at that volume.

  ## Gap in industry financial models

  BAN said public debate over the environmental footprint of artificial intelligence has concentrated on electricity consumption, water use, and carbon emissions while overlooking the management of physical hardware at end of life. Electronic waste variables are rarely included in the financial projections or operational cost models of technology companies, according to the report.

  BAN said its findings could become a factor in how investors and the industry calculate total cost of ownership — the full lifetime cost of building, operating, and decommissioning a facility — for data center projects in Indonesia.

  ## UNEP procurement guidelines

  The United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) has published a framework — the Sustainable Procurement Guidelines for Data Centres and Servers — aimed at reducing the negative environmental impact of data center development. The guidelines identify seven areas for government action:

  1. **Data center energy efficiency**: Measure electricity consumption across servers, cooling systems, and power distribution infrastructure, with attention to impacts on communities near facilities.
  2. **Cooling systems**: Select efficient cooling technologies, optimize airflow, and minimize the energy required to dissipate heat generated by servers.
  3. **Water use efficiency**: Assess water requirements for cooling, measure water usage effectiveness, and account for local climate conditions and potential conflicts over water supply.
  4. **Server and storage efficiency**: Choose servers that deliver higher computing performance per unit of electricity consumed, optimizing processors and memory.
  5. **Renewable energy use**: Promote electricity sourced from renewables, whether from on-site generation, the grid, or qualified renewable energy procurement.
  6. **Durability, repair, and waste management**: Prioritize equipment that is maintainable and repairable, use serviceable components, and manage hardware waste responsibly.
  7. **Infrastructure capability and resilience**: Ensure power supply, backup systems, power distribution, and cooling are matched to actual operational requirements.

  ## What the designation means for Indonesia

  The government's move to concentrate new data center capacity in Batam, Bintan, and Karimun comes as Indonesia is simultaneously developing facilities in other regions, framing the expansion partly around access to water and renewable energy. BAN's projection — that waste estimates rise 40 to 60 times once electrical and network systems are counted — suggests that the physical infrastructure required to power and connect AI data centers may ultimately generate far more waste than the computing hardware itself.

  BAN's finding that no government or company currently has waste management mechanisms equal to the projected volumes indicates that Indonesia's regulatory framework for data center decommissioning remains an open question as the Batam-Bintan-Karimun hub designations move forward.

  印尼正加速推動以人工智慧為核心的資料中心建設並指定多處新樞紐，但一家非營利廢棄物監測機構的最新預測警告，整個產業可能在本世紀中葉以前產生數億公噸的電子廢棄物——這筆龐大的責任成本，至今仍普遍被投資者與科技業者排除在試算之外。

  統籌下游產業與能源轉型的協調部長巴赫里勒·拉哈達利亞（Bahlil Lahadalia），於10月19日在峇里島薩努爾（Sanur）舉辦的「峇里島科技週2026」論壇上宣布，政府將正式將巴淡島（Batam）、民丹島（Bintan）與卡里蒙（Karimun）——位於廖內群島、緊鄰新加坡的三座島嶼——指定為新的資料中心樞紐。根據印尼《商業日報》（Bisnis.com）報導，印尼政府正積極在多個地區推動資料中心開發，除吸引外來投資外，也將善加利用該國豐沛的水資源與再生能源潛力。

  ## 電子廢棄物的預估規模

  總部設於美國、專門監測危險廢棄物流向的非營利機構「巴塞爾行動網絡」（Basel Action Network，BAN）估計，全球資料中心建設在2025年至2050年間，可能產生3.95億至6.17億公噸的電子廢棄物。BAN指出，伺服器元件與繪圖晶片——AI運算工作負載的核心處理單元——佔資料中心設備總重量的13%。

  若將配電系統與網路基礎設施納入計算，BAN的廢棄物估算將提高40至60倍。這個倍數意味著，環境報告中頻繁被點名的硬體——伺服器與晶片——實際上只佔最終廢棄物處置挑戰的一小部分。

  BAN表示，目前尚無任何政府、科技業者或國際組織建立了足以應對如此龐大廢棄物量的處理機制。

  ## 產業財務模型的缺口

  BAN指出，關於人工智慧環境足跡的公共討論，目前大多集中在電力消耗、用水量與碳排放，卻忽略了硬體設備在生命週期終止後的管理問題。根據BAN的報告，電子廢棄物相關的變數，鮮少被納入科技公司的財務預測或營運成本模型。

  BAN表示，其研究發現未來可能成為投資者與業界評估印尼資料中心專案「總持有成本」（Total Cost of Ownership）的關鍵參考依據——亦即涵蓋設施建設、營運乃至除役的全生命週期成本。

  ## 聯合國環境規劃署採購指引

  聯合國環境規劃署（UNEP）已發布一套框架——《資料中心與伺服器永續採購指引》（Sustainable Procurement Guidelines for Data Centres and Servers），旨在降低資料中心開發對環境造成的負面衝擊。該指引為政府行動列出七大面向：

  1. **資料中心能源效率**：全面衡量伺服器、冷卻系統及配電基礎設施的電力消耗，並關注設施對周邊社區的影響。
  2. **冷卻系統**：選用高效冷卻技術、優化氣流配置，並降低散逸伺服器廢熱所需的能源消耗。
  3. **用水效率**：評估冷卻所需用水量、衡量用水效能，並考量當地氣候條件及潛在的水資源競用問題。
  4. **伺服器與儲存效率**：選擇在單位電力消耗下能提供更高運算效能的伺服器，優化處理器與記憶體的使用配置。
  5. **再生能源使用**：推動使用再生能源供電，來源可涵蓋場域自建電源、公共電網或符合資格的再生能源採購合約。
  6. **耐用性、維修與廢棄物管理**：優先選用可維護、可修繕的設備，採用可替換零組件，並妥善管理硬體廢棄物。
  7. **基礎設施能力與韌性**：確保電力供應、備用系統、配電架構與冷卻設施符合實際營運需求。

  ## 此次指定對印尼的意義

  印尼政府此次將資料中心新增容量集中部署於巴淡島、民丹島與卡里蒙，同時也在其他地區持續推進設施建設，並將這波擴張的部分論述建立在取得水資源與再生能源的基礎上。BAN的預測顯示，一旦將電力與網路系統納入計算，廢棄物估算將放大40至60倍，這意味著用於供電與連結AI資料中心的實體基礎設施，最終所產生的廢棄物量，可能遠遠超過運算硬體本身。

  BAN指出，目前沒有任何政府或企業具備足以應對預估廢棄物規模的管理機制，這說明隨著巴淡島—民丹島—卡里蒙樞紐指定計畫的推進，印尼在資料中心除役管理上的法規框架，仍是一項懸而未決的開放議題。
