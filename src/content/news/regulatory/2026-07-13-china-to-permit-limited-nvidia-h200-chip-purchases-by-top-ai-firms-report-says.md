body_en: |
  China plans to allow top artificial intelligence companies, including Alibaba Group, ByteDance, and DeepSeek, to purchase a limited number of Nvidia H200 processors, signaling an easing of restrictions on American AI chip imports after U.S. President Donald Trump granted Nvidia permission to sell the processors to China in December, The Information reported July 8.

  Chinese officials have told those companies they will receive permission to buy some H200 chips, which are used to develop AI models, The Information said. Companies must submit requests specifying how many processors they need and explaining why before receiving approval.

  ## Scale and approval process

  The Chinese government is still determining the total volume to permit, but the overall number may be fewer than 200,000 processors, The Information reported. A single data center can use more than 400,000 of Nvidia's newer Blackwell-generation processors, the report noted, placing the planned H200 allowance in context as a modest allocation by current AI infrastructure standards.

  ## Why China delayed imports

  Chinese authorities had been slow to permit H200 processors into the country even after U.S. clearance. The delays stemmed in part from Chinese concerns that an influx of American-designed AI chips would undermine the government's long-held goal of building a domestic chip industry, according to the report. Beijing also cited fears that American-made chips in the local market could pose a cybersecurity risk.

  Soaring demand for AI processors ultimately drove Chinese authorities toward permitting some H200 purchases, The Information reported, citing two unidentified people familiar with the matter. Labs in China, like their U.S. counterparts, have been struggling to find enough computing power to train and run new models.

  ## About the H200

  The H200 belongs to Nvidia's Hopper line of processors, designed for training and running AI models. It was the most powerful AI chip on the market until Nvidia introduced its next-generation Blackwell models in late 2024, according to the report.

  ## Market reaction

  Nvidia shares climbed 3.7 percent to $204.12 on July 8, their largest single-day gain in more than a month, as part of a broader chip rally. The stock is up 9.4 percent in 2026, though that trails the gains of most major semiconductor peers, according to the report. A Nvidia representative did not respond to a request for comment.

  ## China's stated position

  Chinese embassy spokesperson Liu Chang said China's position on U.S. chip exports has been consistent. "We advocate that China and the United States achieve mutual benefit and win-win outcomes through cooperation, and we oppose the politicisation, instrumentalisation, and weaponisation of technological and economic issues," Liu said in a statement. "We stand ready to work with all parties to jointly safeguard the stability of global industrial and supply chains."

  ## Nvidia's financial exposure

  As of May, Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress said H200 processors had yet to generate any revenue in China. "We are uncertain whether any imports will be allowed into the country," she said. Nvidia excluded China revenue from that market in its financial outlook as a result.

  ## What comes next

  Washington has continued to restrict sales of more capable AI chips to China on national security grounds. Nvidia is working to release a more powerful chip lineup called Rubin in the second half of 2026.

  中國計畫允許包括阿里巴巴集團、字節跳動及DeepSeek在內的頂尖人工智慧（AI）企業，有限度採購輝達（Nvidia）H200處理器，顯示美國總統唐納·川普去年12月授權輝達向中國銷售該款處理器後，中方已出現鬆綁跡象。《The Information》於7月8日刊發上述報導。

  據《The Information》指出，中國官員已通知上述企業，將獲准購買部分H200晶片——該晶片主要用於開發AI模型。各企業必須提出申請，具體說明所需數量及採購理由，方可獲得核准。

  ## 規模與審批流程

  《The Information》報導，中國政府仍在評估整體允許進口的數量，但總量可能不超過20萬顆。報導並指出，單一資料中心就可能使用超過40萬顆輝達新一代Blackwell架構處理器，藉此對照，外界可看出此次H200的核准配額，以當前AI基礎設施的標準衡量，實屬有限。

  ## 中國延遲進口的原因

  即使美方已放行，中國當局遲遲未批准H200處理器入境。據報導，延誤部分源於中方的疑慮——大量美系AI晶片湧入，恐將動搖北京長期致力推動本土晶片產業發展的既定目標。北京方面亦援引國家資安考量，擔憂美系晶片在境內流通可能帶來網路安全風險。

  《The Information》援引兩名知情人士（均未具名）的說法指出，AI處理器需求急速攀升，最終促使中國當局決定有條件開放部分H200採購。中國各大實驗室與美國同業一樣，在訓練和運行新模型時，長期面臨算力不足的困境。

  ## 關於H200

  H200隸屬輝達的Hopper系列處理器，專為訓練及運行AI模型而設計。據報導，在輝達於2024年底推出下一代Blackwell系列之前，H200曾是市場上運算能力最強的AI晶片。

  ## 市場反應

  受整體晶片股強勢帶動，輝達股價於7月8日上漲3.7%，收報每股204.12美元，創逾一個月來單日最大漲幅。輝達股價今年（2026年）迄今累計上漲9.4%，但漲幅仍落後於多數主要半導體同業。輝達發言人未就此事回覆置評請求。

  ## 中國官方立場

  中國駐美大使館發言人劉暢表示，中方在美國晶片出口問題上的立場始終一貫。劉暢發表聲明稱：「我們主張中美雙方透過合作實現互利共贏，反對將科技與經濟問題政治化、工具化、武器化。我們願與各方攜手合作，共同維護全球產業鏈與供應鏈的穩定。」

  ## 輝達的財務風險

  截至今年5月，輝達財務長柯蕾特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）表示，H200處理器在中國至今尚未產生任何營收。「我們尚不確定是否有任何進口將獲准入境，」她說。有鑑於此，輝達已將中國市場的營收排除在財測展望之外。

  ## 後續發展

  華盛頓方面以國家安全為由，持續限制對中國出售更高效能的AI晶片。輝達目前正積極備戰，預計在2026年下半年推出更強大的新一代晶片產品線——Rubin系列。
