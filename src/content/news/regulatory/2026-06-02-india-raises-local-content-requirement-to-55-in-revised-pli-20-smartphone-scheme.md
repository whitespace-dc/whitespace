# India Raises Local Content Requirement to 55% in Revised PLI 2.0 Smartphone Scheme

India is revising its smartphone production incentive scheme (PLI 2.0) to require manufacturers to source at least 55% of value locally. The shift aims to transform India from an electronics assembly hub into a destination for deeper manufacturing. The move reflects government concerns that the current system has failed to build sufficient domestic manufacturing capacity.

## 中文

# 印度修訂PLI 2.0，本地化目標提升至55%

印度正修訂智慧型手機激勵方案（PLI 2.0），要求本地化價值新增比率達55%以上。此舉旨在將印度從組裝中心轉變為深度電子製造基地。該策略轉變反映政府擔憂現有制度未能建立充分的國內製造業基礎。
