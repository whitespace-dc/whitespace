body_en: |
  PJM Interconnection's most recent capacity auction, the market mechanism the grid operator uses to secure committed electricity supply years in advance, cleared nearly 7 gigawatts below its reliability target and drew only about 500 megawatts of new generation, Federal Energy Regulatory Commission Chairman Laura Swett said Thursday. Swett called the results a compounding of "alarm bells" requiring coordinated action at federal, market, and state levels.

  ## PJM shortfall and FERC's reaction

  Swett made the statements at FERC's monthly meeting. "These numbers compound the alarm bells for a call to action in PJM," she said. At a subsequent media briefing, she said she was not surprised by the outcome: "Am I surprised that PJM failed to deliver? No, I am not."

  PJM is the nation's largest grid operator, serving 67 million people across the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest. A capacity auction clearing below its reliability target means the grid operator secured fewer megawatts of committed supply than its planning criteria require to ensure sufficient generation is available during high-demand periods.

  Swett framed the problem as systemic rather than solely regulatory: "This is a problem that involves people at the federal level, at the market level, the state level, the registered entities, the market participants … all the utilities, the companies there. This is a very complex issue that everyone has to coalesce around, coming up with a solution." She said FERC is not trying to "target" PJM.

  FERC Commissioner Lindsay See echoed the call for structural change. "PJM has to be able to get reforms across the finish line in a timely and transparent way," she said. "Part of that also includes the need for a governance structure that can not only deliver concrete results but that can give parties the type of confidence in those reforms that's necessary to drive investment where and when it's needed."

  Last week, Commissioner David LaCerte described the status quo at PJM as "untenable."

  ## Governance overhaul targeted at July 23 conference

  FERC will hold a technical conference on July 23 focused on PJM's governance structure. Swett characterized the existing stakeholder process as "slow where it must be fast, opaque where it must be transparent, and vulnerable to vetoes and agenda control exactly when the region needs immediate action."

  She said FERC expects the conference to produce "ideas on paper, on a record," and indicated that leading proposals were already taking shape: "I am very optimistic that certain proposals will be front runners that are grounded in the record that we collect next week, so that there should be a more clear path forward for PJM after that."

  ## Data center reliability standards get firm deadlines

  FERC set a December 31 deadline for the North American Electric Reliability Corp. (NERC), the federally designated body that develops and enforces grid reliability standards, to complete reliability standards covering computational loads, a category that includes data centers and cryptocurrency mining operations, along with registration rules for those loads. FERC also ordered NERC to file by March 1 a plan detailing its next steps in the standards development process.

  "I applaud NERC's proactive efforts on these matters," Swett said. She said the deadlines serve as "a great mechanism for producing results."

  NERC said Thursday that it expects to issue proposed reliability standards and draft registry criteria for public comment in August as part of its Large Loads Action Plan.

  ## Western grid seams under scrutiny

  FERC ordered the California Independent System Operator (CAISO) and the Southwest Power Pool (SPP) to file a joint report by September 30 on how they plan to manage seams, the operational and market interfaces between their respective markets and neighboring balancing authority areas in the West. CAISO launched its Extended Day-Ahead Market in May; SPP expanded its footprint into the Western Interconnection in April, and its Markets+ initiative is expected to go live in October 2027.

  "While the increased deployment of organized markets is intended to bring substantial reliability and economic benefits to the West, the resulting seams create reliability, operational, and market efficiency hurdles that warrant proactive attention," FERC said. Earlier this month, CAISO President and CEO Elliot Mainzer said the two operators were working to develop a joint operating agreement before Markets+ begins operating.

  ## PSE&G cost recovery hearing ordered

  FERC directed an administrative law judge to hold hearings on a January complaint by Public Citizen alleging that Public Service Electric and Gas Co. (PSE&G) imprudently incurred costs on a $546 million transmission project in New Jersey. PSE&G agreed in December 2024 to pay a $6.6 million fine to settle a FERC enforcement investigation into the utility's justifications to PJM for building the Roseland-Pleasant Valley transmission project.

  ## Duke transmission rate complaint dismissed

  FERC rejected a complaint by North Carolina Electric Membership Corp. that sought to prevent Duke Energy Progress from rolling the costs of four transmission lines, lines that could benefit solar developers, into its overall transmission rates. The commission said rolled-in rate treatment was "consistent with longstanding Commission precedent that favors rolled-in rate treatment for integrated transmission facilities."

  ## Capital structure incentive under review

  FERC approved a 50/50 hypothetical debt-to-equity capital structure for two transmission projects that Basin Electric Power Cooperative plans to build in North Dakota, worth approximately $469.3 million. The hypothetical capital structure incentive allows developers to secure project financing based on an assumed debt-to-equity ratio that reflects a project's long-term financial profile, even when the developer's actual balance sheet does not yet match it.

  Swett said FERC is considering changes to the incentive, which she acknowledged increases consumer costs. "This is a very complex topic with significant implications for financing, project development, regional planning, and customer affordability. Even small changes to utilities' return can have significant impacts," she said.

  The July 23 technical conference on PJM governance is the next immediate milestone, with FERC expecting the record it builds there to define a clearer path forward for the grid operator.

  ## PJM供應缺口與FERC的回應

  PJM Interconnection最新一輪容量拍賣——此為電網業者用以提前數年鎖定承諾電力供應的市場機制——清算量較可靠度目標少了將近7 GW，新增發電量僅約500 MW。聯邦能源監管委員會（Federal Energy Regulatory Commission，FERC）主席蘿拉·史薇特（Laura Swett）周四表示，此一結果是「警報聲的持續疊加」，需要聯邦、市場及州層級協調行動。

  史薇特在FERC月度例會上做出上述陳述。「這些數字讓PJM的警報聲更加響亮，呼籲各界採取行動，」她說。在隨後的媒體簡報會上，她表示對結果並不感到意外：「PJM沒能達標，我意外嗎？不，我不意外。」

  PJM是全美最大的電網業者，服務範圍涵蓋中大西洋及中西部地區，共6,700萬人。容量拍賣清算量低於可靠度目標，意味著電網業者所鎖定的承諾供電量，未能達到其規劃標準所要求的水準——而這些標準正是為了確保用電高峰期間有充足電力可用。

  史薇特將這個問題定性為系統性問題，而非單純的監管問題：「這涉及聯邦層級、市場層級、州層級的相關人員、登記實體、市場參與者……所有公用事業、所有相關企業，這是一個極為複雜的課題，所有人必須凝聚共識，共同找出解方。」她強調，FERC並非要「針對」PJM。

  FERC委員林賽·西（Lindsay See）也附和了對結構性改革的呼籲。「PJM必須能夠及時且透明地完成改革，」她說，「這其中也包括需要一個不僅能夠交出具體成果，還能讓各方對改革建立足夠信心的治理架構——唯有如此，才能在正確的時間、正確的地點吸引所需投資。」

  上週，委員大衛·拉瑟特（David LaCerte）將PJM目前的現狀形容為「難以為繼」。

  ## 7月23日技術會議鎖定治理架構改革

  FERC將於7月23日召開技術會議，聚焦PJM的治理架構。史薇特將現行的利害關係人程序形容為「在必須快速的地方遲緩拖沓、在必須透明的地方暗箱操作，並在該地區最需要立即行動之際，偏偏容易遭到否決權與議程操控的干擾」。

  她表示，FERC期望此次會議能夠產出「以書面形式留存於紀錄中的具體構想」，並指出領先方案已初見雛形：「我非常樂觀，認為某些方案將成為前沿選項——這些方案將以我們下週蒐集的紀錄為基礎，因此在那之後，PJM的前進路徑應該會更加清晰。」

  ## 資料中心可靠度標準獲設硬性期限

  FERC為北美電力可靠性公司（North American Electric Reliability Corp.，NERC）——聯邦指定負責制定並執行電網可靠度標準的機構——設定了12月31日的截止期限，要求其完成涵蓋運算負載的可靠度標準，此類別包含資料中心及加密貨幣挖礦作業，以及相應的登記規則。FERC並要求NERC於3月1日前提交計畫，詳述其在標準制定流程中的後續步驟。

  「我讚揚NERC在這些議題上的積極主動，」史薇特說。她表示，這些截止期限是「催生成果的絕佳機制」。

  NERC周四表示，預計將於8月發布擬議中的可靠度標準及登記標準草案，供公眾意見徵詢，此舉是其「大型負載行動計畫」（Large Loads Action Plan）的一部分。

  ## 西部電網邊界銜接問題受到審視

  FERC命令加州獨立系統運營商（California Independent System Operator，CAISO）與西南電力池（Southwest Power Pool，SPP）於9月30日前聯合提交一份報告，說明雙方計畫如何處理其各自市場與西部地區鄰近平衡責任區域之間的邊界銜接問題——亦即彼此的運營與市場介面。CAISO於5月啟動延伸日前市場；SPP則於4月將服務範圍擴展至西部互聯電網，其「Markets+」計畫預計於2027年10月正式上線。

  「儘管有組織市場的擴大部署旨在為西部地區帶來顯著的可靠度與經濟效益，由此形成的邊界銜接問題卻在可靠度、運營及市場效率方面製造了障礙，值得積極應對，」FERC表示。本月稍早，CAISO總裁兼執行長艾略特·梅因澤爾（Elliot Mainzer）表示，兩家業者正致力於在Markets+正式運營前簽訂聯合運營協議。

  ## PSE&G成本回收案裁定聽證

  FERC指示行政法法官就公共公民組織（Public Citizen）於1月提出的申訴召開聽證，該申訴主張公共服務電力及天然氣公司（Public Service Electric and Gas Co.，PSE&G）在新澤西州一項造價5.46億美元的輸電工程上，存在不當成本支出。PSE&G已於2024年12月同意繳納660萬美元罰款，以和解FERC對該公司為PJM興建羅斯蘭—普萊森特谷（Roseland-Pleasant Valley）輸電工程所提出理由的執法調查。

  ## 杜克能源輸電費率申訴遭駁回

  FERC駁回了北卡羅來納州電力會員公司（North Carolina Electric Membership Corp.）的申訴，該申訴旨在阻止杜克能源進展公司（Duke Energy Progress）將四條可能使太陽能開發商受益的輸電線路成本，納入其整體輸電費率。委員會表示，統一費率處理方式「符合委員會長期以來偏向對整合輸電設施採用統一費率處理的慣例」。

  ## 資本結構獎勵機制面臨審查

  FERC批准了盆地電力合作社（Basin Electric Power Cooperative）計畫在北達科他州興建的兩項輸電工程採用50/50假設負債股權比的資本結構，這兩項工程合計價值約4.693億美元。假設資本結構獎勵機制允許開發商依據反映專案長期財務狀況的假設負債股權比取得融資，即便開發商實際的資產負債表尚未達到該比例亦然。

  史薇特表示，FERC正在考慮修改這項獎勵機制，她坦承此機制會推高消費者成本。「這是一個高度複雜的議題，對融資、專案開發、區域規劃及消費者負擔能力都有重大影響。公用事業報酬率即便是微小的變動，也可能產生顯著衝擊，」她說。

  7月23日PJM治理架構技術會議是眼前最迫切的里程碑，FERC期望屆時建立的紀錄，能為這家電網業者勾勒出更清晰的前進路徑。
