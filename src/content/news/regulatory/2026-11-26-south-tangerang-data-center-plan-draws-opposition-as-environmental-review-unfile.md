body_en: |
  A planned data center in the BSD district of South Tangerang City, Indonesia has drawn public opposition over water scarcity, air pollution, and flooding risks, with the city's environmental agency confirming on November 18, 2026, that no environmental impact assessment has been submitted by the developer.

  ## Environmental Review Outstanding

  The Head of South Tangerang's Environmental Agency (Dinas Lingkungan Hidup, or DLH), Bani Khosyatullah, said the agency had received only a company profile document from the developer — not the Amdal (Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan), the environmental impact assessment required under Indonesian regulations before major development can proceed. "Belum ada Amdal, yang masuk baru cuma CV," Khosyatullah said, meaning no Amdal had been received and only the company profile had come in.

  The DLH said it has been monitoring public complaints and is coordinating with the city's spatial planning division and other relevant government bodies to oversee the environmental permitting process.

  ## Public Concerns

  The project entered public debate after a post on the Threads social media platform by user @elisaantl on November 1, 2026, asking BSD-area residents whether they were aware of the planned facility and its potential ecological consequences. The post cited several concerns: reduced clean water availability, higher electricity demand, increased ambient temperatures, noise from cooling equipment and generators, air pollution from generator exhaust, and loss of water-absorbing land that could worsen flooding and surface runoff.

  Another Threads user, @audreykath, wrote that water and air quality in South Tangerang were already poor before any data center was built, raising the question of cumulative environmental impact on a city where baseline conditions are already stressed.

  ## Zoning Compliance, Technical Permits Pending

  The city's spatial planning authority said the project site is zoned for trade and services use and is consistent with existing land-use rules. The Head of Spatial Planning at the Department of Civil Works and Spatial Planning (Dinas Cipta Karya dan Tata Ruang, or DCKTR), Yulia Rahmawati, explained that Mayor Regulation No. 118, which governs South Tangerang's Detailed Spatial Plan (Rencana Detail Tata Ruang, or RDTR) for the period 2022 to 2042, permits data processing facilities in four land-use categories: industrial zones, trade and services zones, office zones, and mixed-use zones.

  The planned site is at the Smartfren office complex, which falls within the trade and services zone, Rahmawati said. Technical permits, however, remain under review.

  ## Significance

  The gap between zoning approval and environmental permitting illustrates a recurring tension in Indonesia's fast-expanding data center market: land-use compatibility can be established quickly under existing spatial plans, while the Amdal process — which assesses water consumption, emissions, heat output, and stormwater impact — operates on a separate, slower track. For South Tangerang residents already contending with degraded air and water quality, the absence of a filed Amdal means those impacts have not yet been formally evaluated or mitigated.

  The DLH said it will continue coordinating with spatial planning authorities and other agencies as the permitting process moves forward, with the Amdal submission remaining the next required step before environmental review can formally begin.
body_zh: |
  印尼南唐格朗市BSD地區一項資料中心開發計畫，因外界擔憂其對水資源、空氣品質及淹水風險的潛在衝擊，已引發當地居民強烈反對。市府環境局於2026年11月18日確認，開發業者迄今尚未提交印尼法規所要求的環境影響評估報告。

  ## 環評報告尚未送審

  南唐格朗市環境局（Dinas Lingkungan Hidup，簡稱DLH）局長Bani Khosyatullah表示，該局至今僅收到業者提交的公司簡介文件，並未收到依印尼法規規定、重大開發案動工前必須提交的環境影響評估報告（Amdal，即Analisis Mengenai Dampak Lingkungan）。Khosyatullah以印尼文說道：「Belum ada Amdal，yang masuk baru cuma CV」，意即環評報告尚未送達，目前收到的只有公司簡介。

  環境局表示，已持續關注民間陳情，並正與市府城市空間規劃部門及其他相關政府機關協調，共同監督環境許可申請程序的推進。

  ## 民眾疑慮

  此案進入公共討論的契機，源於2026年11月1日一篇發布在Threads社群平台上的貼文。用戶@elisaantl在文中詢問BSD地區居民，是否知悉這項開發計畫及其可能帶來的生態影響。該貼文點出數項隱憂：乾淨水源供應減少、電力需求大幅攀升、環境溫度升高、冷卻設備與發電機噪音擾民、發電機廢氣造成空氣污染，以及透水地表流失恐使淹水與地表逕流情形惡化。

  另一位Threads用戶@audreykath則寫道，早在任何資料中心動工之前，南唐格朗市的水質與空氣品質就已相當惡劣，進而提出一個問題：在基準環境條件已然嚴峻的城市中，累積性的環境衝擊該如何評估？

  ## 土地使用分區合規，技術許可審查中

  市府城市空間規劃主管機關表示，開發案所在地依現行法規被劃定為商業與服務用地，符合現有土地使用規則。負責城市建設與空間規劃局（Dinas Cipta Karya dan Tata Ruang，簡稱DCKTR）空間規劃業務的局長Yulia Rahmawati說明，市長第118號規定所訂定的南唐格朗市2022年至2042年詳細空間計畫（Rencana Detail Tata Ruang，簡稱RDTR），允許資料處理設施設置於四類土地使用分區：工業區、商業與服務區、辦公區，以及混合使用區。

  Rahmawati表示，開發案預定地位於Smartfren辦公園區，屬於商業與服務區範疇。然而，相關技術許可目前仍在審查程序之中。

  ## 重要意涵

  土地使用分區取得批准與環境許可尚付闕如之間的落差，恰好反映出印尼快速擴張的資料中心市場中一再浮現的結構性矛盾：在現有空間計畫架構下，土地使用相容性往往能迅速確立；然而Amdal程序——涵蓋用水量、廢氣排放、熱能輸出及雨水逕流衝擊等面向的評估——卻在另一條速度更慢的軌道上獨立運行。對於那些早已飽受空氣和水質惡化之苦的南唐格朗居民而言，環評報告遲未送審，意味著上述各項衝擊至今既未經正式評估，也尚無相應的減緩措施。

  環境局表示，將持續與空間規劃主管機關及其他相關部門協調，推動許可審查程序向前推進；而Amdal報告的提交，仍是環境審查程序正式啟動前不可或缺的下一步。
