  Singapore Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Trade and Industry Gan Kim Yong said the government is engaging businesses affected by new US semiconductor export restrictions and will work with Washington to preserve their access to high-end chips, remarks that follow the US Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) placing three Singapore-based companies on its Entity List.

  Gan made the comments at the SPH Asia Future Summit in response to questions about restrictions the United States has placed on exports of advanced semiconductors to countries including Singapore. The BIS, which sits within the US Department of Commerce and administers export controls, this week added Swiftdata Pte. Ltd., Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd., and Apex Enterprise Solutions (Singapore) Private Limited — a subsidiary of Alibaba — to its Entity List, a designation that restricts US suppliers from selling to those companies without a licence.

  "We are in the process of engaging our businesses, to reach out to them, to see what is the impact on their businesses," Gan said. "I think the businesses are also organising themselves and they will work with their different partners to continue to have access."

  ## Singapore's response to the restrictions

  Gan framed the government's position around both diplomatic engagement and Singapore's stated national priority in artificial intelligence. "It is important for Singapore to continue to focus on our AI mission. We are determined to realise our AI mission, our AI vision, and our AI ambition for our economy," he said.

  The deputy prime minister noted that many US hyperscalers — large-scale cloud infrastructure providers such as Amazon, Microsoft, and Google — operate in Singapore, and argued that supporting those businesses' ability to serve regional customers is in the United States' own interest. "So we will have to work with the US to see how we can address their concerns and to continue to allow Singapore companies access to this high-end compute power," he said.

  ## Megaspeed investigation and Singapore's regulatory stance

  Running alongside the export-control question is a separate law-enforcement matter. Singapore authorities are investigating Megaspeed International Chief Executive Officer Alice Huang and others for breaches of Singapore law, and have asked US authorities for information and cooperation in that probe. Earlier this month the US Department of Justice took the lead in its own investigation into the Singapore-based company.

  Gan said Singapore's decision to pursue legal action against those who break its laws reflects, rather than undermines, the country's reputation as a trusted international business hub. "The fact that we launch an investigation and the fact that we take legal action against those who flout our laws and we are very stringent on that, I think that reflects our determination to safeguard our reputation," he said.

  He acknowledged that some observers might view such enforcement as damaging to Singapore's standing, but argued the opposite: that rigorous enforcement demonstrates the country's commitment to the trust it has built with international partners.

  ## Structural constraints and market context

  Gan said the broader wave of US restrictions underscores the need for Singapore to build resilience against external shocks. He pointed to the country's size as simultaneously a constraint — in land, energy, and manpower — and a potential advantage in terms of agility. "Our smallness is also our strength, and we must find ways to leverage on this strength of being small, being agile, to move and respond very quickly," he said.

  Those structural constraints are set against a backdrop of significant market growth. Mordor Intelligence projects Singapore's data center market will expand from US$4.56 billion in 2026 to US$5.88 billion in 2031. Installed capacity — the total computing and power infrastructure available — is forecast to grow from 2.95 gigawatts to 3.78 gigawatts over the same period.

  ## What is at stake

  The Entity List additions and the broader tightening of US semiconductor export controls represent a material threat to Singapore's ability to source the high-performance chips needed to support both its own AI ambitions and the cloud infrastructure its data center sector provides to regional customers. Singapore's ability to negotiate access arrangements with the United States while simultaneously cooperating on enforcement against firms that may have violated export rules will determine how much of that projected market growth the country can sustain.

  Singapore's next steps hinge on the outcome of its diplomatic engagement with Washington, which Gan described as ongoing.

  ## 新加坡與美國就晶片取得問題展開協商，三家本地企業遭列入實體清單

  新加坡副總理兼貿易及工業部長顏金勇表示，政府正積極接觸受美國半導體出口管制影響的企業，並將與華府合作，確保相關企業持續取得高端晶片的管道。此前，美國商務部工業暨安全局（BIS）已將三家在新加坡設立的企業列入實體清單。

  顏金勇在出席「SPH亞洲未來峰會」時，針對美國對新加坡等國實施先進半導體出口管制的相關問題作出回應。BIS隸屬美國商務部，負責管理出口管制業務，本週已將Swiftdata Pte. Ltd.、Megaspeed International Pte. Ltd.，以及阿里巴巴旗下子公司Apex Enterprise Solutions（Singapore）Private Limited列入實體清單。一旦被列入實體清單，美國供應商若未取得出口許可證，即不得向這些企業銷售產品。

  「我們正在逐步接觸受影響的企業，主動聯繫他們，瞭解相關措施對其業務的衝擊，」顏金勇說。「我相信這些企業也正在自行組織因應，並將與各方合作夥伴協力，確保持續取得所需資源。」

  ## 新加坡的回應立場

  顏金勇從外交接觸與人工智慧國家優先戰略兩個層面，闡明政府的立場。他表示：「新加坡持續專注於人工智慧使命至關重要。我們決心實現人工智慧的使命、願景，以及對本國經濟的抱負。」

  顏金勇指出，亞馬遜、微軟、Google等眾多美國大型超大規模雲端業者均在新加坡設有據點，並主張支持這些業者服務區域客戶的能力，本身也符合美國的利益。「因此，我們必須與美方合作，研究如何回應他們的關切，同時讓新加坡企業持續取得這些高端運算資源，」他說。

  ## Megaspeed調查案與新加坡的監管立場

  在出口管制議題之外，另有一起獨立的執法案件同步進行。新加坡當局正調查Megaspeed International執行長Alice Huang及其他相關人員是否違反新加坡法律，並已向美方請求提供資訊與司法協助。本月稍早，美國司法部也主導展開針對這家新加坡企業的調查程序。

  顏金勇表示，新加坡選擇對違法者採取法律行動，恰恰彰顯、而非損害了新加坡作為值得信賴的國際商業樞紐的聲譽。「我們展開調查，並對違法者採取法律行動、嚴格執法，我認為這正反映了我們維護本國聲譽的決心，」他說。

  他也承認，部分觀察人士或許認為這類執法行動有損新加坡的地位，但他持相反看法：嚴格執法恰恰向國際夥伴展示了新加坡捍衛互信基礎的承諾。

  ## 結構性限制與市場背景

  顏金勇表示，美國這一波接連不斷的出口管制，凸顯了新加坡強化抵禦外部衝擊韌性的必要性。他以國土面積為例，指出新加坡在土地、能源與人力資源方面均受到先天限制，但在靈活應變方面卻具有潛在優勢。「我們的小，也是我們的強項，我們必須善用這個『小而靈活』的優勢，迅速回應、快速調整，」他說。

  這些結構性限制的背後，是一個正蓬勃成長的市場。市場調研機構Mordor Intelligence預測，新加坡資料中心市場規模將從2026年的45.6億美元，擴大至2031年的58.8億美元。同期，代表可用運算與電力基礎設施總量的裝機容量，也預計從2.95吉瓦（GW）增至3.78吉瓦。

  ## 關鍵利益與後續走向

  此次實體清單新增名單，以及美國整體半導體出口管制的持續收緊，對新加坡取得高效能晶片的能力構成實質威脅，而這些晶片正是支撐新加坡本身人工智慧發展雄心、以及其資料中心產業為區域客戶提供雲端基礎設施服務的關鍵所在。新加坡能否在與美國談判取得晶片管道的同時，積極配合對潛在違反出口規定企業的執法調查，將決定這個國家能否維繫上述市場預測所描繪的成長動能。

  新加坡的下一步，取決於與華府外交協商的結果。顏金勇表示，相關溝通仍在持續進行中。
