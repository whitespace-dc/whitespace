body_en: |
  The 403 hyperscale data centers operating across the United States emitted 25.7 million tonnes of carbon dioxide in the 12 months from May 2024 through April 2025, equaling Denmark's total annual greenhouse gas output, according to a peer-reviewed study published Monday in the journal *Nature Sustainability* by Harvard University researchers.

  The figure is three times higher than the emissions recorded in 2018, the last time a peer-reviewed measurement of US data center greenhouse gas output was conducted, the study found.

  ## Regional concentration and fuel sources

  Virginia, Oregon, Ohio, and Iowa together accounted for more than half of all electricity consumption measured across the 403 facilities, according to the study. Most of the data centers were powered by natural gas.

  ## Authors' aim

  Gianluca Guidi, a Harvard researcher on artificial intelligence and public health and a lead author of the study, said the aim of the report is to bring transparency and data to the debate over data centers and artificial intelligence. "We are highlighting importance of transparency," Guidi said. "We tried and managed to build a methodology that is scientifically accepted to build public trust."

  ## Political backdrop

  The report's publication coincides with what Reuters described as heavy backlash against hyperscale data center construction in the United States. In the Nov. 3 midterm elections, candidates who ran on policies including moratoria on new data center construction or strengthened regulatory oversight were among the biggest winners of the Democrats' landslide victories, Reuters reported.

  The study is the first peer-reviewed accounting of US data center emissions since 2018, a gap of roughly seven years, and its lead author said establishing a scientifically accepted methodology is intended to build the public trust needed to ground that debate in verifiable data.
body_zh: |
  哈佛大學研究人員週一在《自然永續》（*Nature Sustainability*）期刊上發表同儕審查研究指出，在美國全境運作的403座超大規模資料中心，在2024年5月至2025年4月的12個月間，共排放2,570萬公噸二氧化碳，相當於丹麥全年溫室氣體總排放量。

  研究指出，這一數字是2018年同類紀錄的三倍。2018年是美國資料中心溫室氣體排放量上一次接受同儕審查測量的年份。

  ## 區域分布與燃料來源

  研究發現，維吉尼亞州、奧勒岡州、俄亥俄州與愛荷華州，合計占403座設施用電總量的逾半數。大多數資料中心以天然氣作為主要能源。

  ## 研究目的

  哈佛大學人工智慧與公共衛生研究員、本研究共同主要作者吉安盧卡・圭迪（Gianluca Guidi）表示，這份報告的目的是為資料中心與人工智慧的相關辯論帶來透明度與具體數據。「我們強調透明度的重要性，」圭迪說，「我們嘗試並成功建立了一套獲科學認可的方法論，以建立公眾信任。」

  ## 政治背景

  路透社報導，這份報告發表的時間點，恰逢美國超大規模資料中心建設遭遇強烈反彈。在11月3日的期中選舉中，主張暫停新建資料中心或強化監管力道的候選人，成為民主黨大勝浪潮中最大的贏家之一。

  這是美國自2018年以來首次針對資料中心排放進行同儕審查核算，時間差距約達七年。主要作者表示，建立一套獲科學認可的方法論，旨在建立公眾信任，讓這場辯論得以奠基於可驗證的數據之上。
