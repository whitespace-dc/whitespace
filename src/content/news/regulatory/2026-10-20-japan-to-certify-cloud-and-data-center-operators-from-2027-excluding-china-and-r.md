body_en: |
  Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications and Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry plan to introduce a security certification system for data centers and cloud services starting in fiscal year 2027, with review criteria designed to screen out operators with ties to China, Russia, or other countries the government regards as security risks, the Nikkei reported.

  The system will cover data center operators located in Japan and cloud service providers that run servers on Japanese soil. Reviewers will evaluate three main categories: an operator's corporate background, risk management systems designed to prevent data leaks, and the countries of origin for hardware and equipment procurement.

  ## Steering key industries toward certified providers

  Financial institutions, medical facilities, and research organizations handling sensitive personal information will be encouraged to contract only with certified data centers and cloud providers. The government also plans a pre-reporting requirement under which companies that store or provide important personal information must notify authorities in advance.

  Participation in the certification scheme will remain formally voluntary. However, companies handling sensitive data will be subject to government oversight and supervision, a condition the report says is expected to make it practically unlikely that regulated organizations would choose non-certified services.

  ## Legislation and enforcement powers

  Japan plans to submit amendments to the Economic Security Promotion Act — a law governing national economic security measures — to the regular Diet session scheduled to convene in January 2027. Once the amendments take effect, the government will gain authority to order a company to halt data storage operations if it identifies a security risk, or to issue formal recommendations. The certification system will operate on the legal foundation those amendments create.

  ## Modeled on an existing IoT certification framework

  The government plans to design the system along the lines of JC-STAR, a tiered security certification program for internet-of-things (IoT) products, including network equipment, administered by the Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA), a body affiliated with METI. JC-STAR assigns ratings by level of security protection and does not explicitly bar products from any country by name. As of the report's publication, no Chinese-made product had obtained JC-STAR certification.

  From April 2027, grid-connected equipment including solar power generation devices and storage batteries will be required to meet JC-STAR Level 1 standards, a requirement the report says is expected to effectively exclude Chinese-made products from that category.

  ## Market scale and an existing regulatory gap

  Japan's cloud services market exceeded 1.7 trillion yen in 2024, according to Cabinet Secretariat data cited in the article. Global providers operating in the market include U.S. companies Amazon and Microsoft, alongside China's Alibaba Group, whose market influence the report describes as continuing to grow.

  Japan currently has no regulatory framework specifically governing data centers and cloud service operators as critical information infrastructure. The report notes that critics have argued this gap leaves Japan limited ability to prevent data theft through cyberattacks, or service disruptions in an emergency, if infrastructure linked to countries it views with concern were to become widely embedded.

  ## Chinese law cited as a structural risk

  The report identifies concerns that companies with Chinese capital may be subject to Chinese domestic law. China's National Intelligence Law requires organizations and citizens to cooperate with state intelligence activities. The National Defense Mobilization Law requires organizations and citizens to accept the requisition of civilian resources. Both obligations are cited as reasons for concern about Chinese-linked cloud infrastructure handling sensitive Japanese data.

  ## International precedents

  Other jurisdictions have moved in similar directions. The European Union requires data center and cloud service providers to register with authorities and manage risks, including those arising from third-country influence. South Korea requires information and communications technology service providers to obtain information security management system certification and to report cybersecurity incidents.

  ## What comes next

  Japan's two ministries plan to submit the Economic Security Promotion Act amendments to the January 2027 Diet session. The certification system is set to begin operations in fiscal year 2027, which starts in April of that year.

  ## 日本2027年起認證雲端與資料中心業者，中俄關聯企業將被排除在外

  據《日本經濟新聞》報導，日本總務省與經濟產業省計畫自2027財年起，針對資料中心與雲端服務導入安全認證制度，審查標準旨在篩除與中國、俄羅斯或其他政府認定具有安全疑慮之國家存在關聯的業者。

  此制度適用對象涵蓋位於日本境內的資料中心業者，以及在日本國內架設伺服器的雲端服務供應商。審查委員會將就三大類別進行評估：業者的企業背景、為防範資料外洩所建立的風險管理體系，以及硬體與設備採購的來源國。

  ## 引導關鍵產業優先採用認證業者

  處理敏感個人資訊的金融機構、醫療設施及研究機構，將被鼓勵僅與通過認證的資料中心及雲端供應商簽約。政府亦計畫建立事前通報機制，要求儲存或提供重要個人資訊的企業須預先向主管機關申報。

  參與認證制度在形式上維持自願性質。然而，報導指出，由於處理敏感資料的企業將受到政府監督與管理，受規範機構實際上幾乎不可能選擇未通過認證的服務。

  ## 立法授權與執法權力

  日本計畫於2027年1月召開的例行國會會期中，提交《經濟安全保障推進法》修正案。此法為規範國家經濟安全保障措施的基本法律。修正案一旦生效，政府將取得授權，得對存在安全疑慮的企業命令其停止資料儲存業務，或發出正式建議。認證制度將以該修正案所建立的法律基礎作為運作依據。

  ## 參考現行物聯網認證架構

  政府計畫以JC-STAR為藍本設計此制度。JC-STAR是針對物聯網（IoT）產品（包含網路設備）所設立的分級安全認證計畫，由隸屬於經濟產業省的獨立行政法人資訊處理推進機構（IPA）負責管理。JC-STAR依安全防護等級評定認證級別，並未明文禁止任何特定國家的產品。截至報導發布時，尚無任何中國製產品取得JC-STAR認證。

  自2027年4月起，包含太陽能發電設備及儲能電池在內的併網設備，將須符合JC-STAR第一級標準。報導指出，此要求預計將實質排除中國製產品進入該類別市場。

  ## 市場規模與現行監管缺口

  根據報導引用的內閣府資料，日本雲端服務市場規模於2024年已突破1.7兆日圓。目前在日本市場營運的全球供應商，包括美國的亞馬遜與微軟，以及中國的阿里巴巴集團，報導形容後者的市場影響力持續擴大。

  日本目前並無專門針對資料中心與雲端服務業者作為關鍵資訊基礎設施進行規範的法律框架。報導指出，批評人士認為此一缺口導致日本對於防範資料竊取的網路攻擊能力有限；一旦與其所關切國家相關聯的基礎設施廣泛嵌入日本體系，緊急情況下也難以因應服務中斷問題。

  ## 中國法律被點名為結構性風險

  報導點出外界對具有中資背景的企業可能受中國國內法律約束的疑慮。中國《國家情報法》要求組織與公民配合國家情報活動；《國防動員法》則要求組織與公民接受民間資源徵用。上述兩項法律義務，均被列為中資雲端基礎設施處理日本敏感資料時令人憂慮的理由。

  ## 國際先例

  其他司法管轄區已先後朝類似方向推進。歐盟要求資料中心與雲端服務供應商向主管機關辦理登記，並管理包括第三國影響在內的相關風險。南韓則要求資訊通訊技術服務供應商取得資訊安全管理系統認證，並申報資安事件。

  ## 後續進程

  日本總務省與經濟產業省計畫於2027年1月的國會例行會期提交《經濟安全保障推進法》修正案。認證制度預定於2027財年（即同年4月起）正式啟動運作。
