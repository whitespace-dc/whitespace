body_en: |
  The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission approved a cost allocation framework on Aug. 14, 2026, for transmission facilities planned by the Midcontinent Independent System Operator but physically located in the PJM Interconnection's service territory, clearing a regulatory path for cross-regional grid investment totaling approximately $909 million.

  Under the approved framework, Exelon's Commonwealth Edison is set to build one group of projects valued at about $904 million, while Duke Energy Ohio is assigned projects totaling $5.3 million. FERC rejected arguments from the Electricity Transmission Competition Coalition and the Industrial Energy Consumers of America that those projects should be subject to MISO's competitive bidding process before being assigned to specific utilities.

  ## Why competitive solicitation was rejected

  FERC determined that MISO does not have the legal authority to require or facilitate the development of transmission facilities located in another region's footprint. The commission said it was "not persuaded" by arguments that bypassing competitive developer selection reduces transparency, compounds cost causation concerns, undermines market solutions, or forfeits potential cost savings. WPPI Energy had joined the Competition Coalition and IECA in pressing for competitive solicitations.

  ## Background: MISO's Tranche 2.1 plan

  The cost recovery and funding agreements at issue grew out of MISO's Tranche 2.1 transmission expansion plan, which calls for building a 765-kilovolt backbone across its Midwest region. MISO's board approved that plan in December 2024. Because the optimal routing for some of those facilities crosses into PJM's adjacent territory, the plan required transmission construction outside MISO's own footprint, giving rise to the cross-regional cost allocation question FERC resolved this week.

  ## Commissioners call for broader interregional planning

  Two FERC commissioners used the decision to argue for a structural shift in how grid operators approach interregional transmission. In a joint concurring statement, commissioners Judy Chang and David Rosner said that "in some cases, the most beneficial and cost-effective solutions for one region's system needs may include transmission upgrades in another region's footprint."

  Chang and Rosner acknowledged that existing tariff definitions presented limitations on identifying interregional projects, but said the steps MISO took to ensure the facilities could be built demonstrate the value of cross-regional coordination. The commissioners argued that transmission planners should make interregional transmission a "more regular and intentional feature" of their work, citing benefits including improved reliability, reduced congestion costs, more efficient resource integration, and lower collective infrastructure costs.

  ## Significance for interregional transmission policy

  The decision establishes that a grid operator can seek and recover costs for transmission built in a neighboring region's footprint when that infrastructure serves the requesting region's system needs, even without applying the requesting region's competitive solicitation rules to facilities it cannot directly control. Chang and Rosner stated that greater interregional connectivity would allow regions to share resources more efficiently, mitigate extreme weather impacts, and accelerate the connection of new demand and generation resources.
body_zh: |
  聯邦能源管理委員會（FERC）於2026年8月14日批准了一套成本分攤框架，適用於由中部大陸獨立系統業者（MISO）規劃、但實際位於PJM互聯（PJM Interconnection）服務轄區內的輸電設施，為跨區域電網投資掃除監管障礙，相關建設金額合計約9.09億美元。

  依據獲批框架，艾克瑟倫（Exelon）旗下的聯邦愛迪生（Commonwealth Edison，ComEd）將負責建設其中一批項目，合計金額約9.04億美元；杜克能源俄亥俄公司（Duke Energy Ohio）則獲分配金額約530萬美元的項目。FERC駁回了輸電競爭聯盟（Electricity Transmission Competition Coalition）與美國工業能源消費者協會（Industrial Energy Consumers of America，IECA）的主張——兩個組織均認為，上述項目在指定給特定電力公司前，應先經過MISO的競爭性招標程序。

  ## 為何拒絕競爭性招標

  FERC認定，MISO在法律上並無權限要求或推動位於其他區域轄區內輸電設施的開發。委員會表示，對於「繞過競爭性開發商遴選程序將降低透明度、加劇成本歸因疑慮、削弱市場機制，或喪失潛在節省空間」等主張，「不予採信」。WPPI能源（WPPI Energy）曾加入競爭聯盟與IECA，共同力主推行競爭性招標程序。

  ## 背景：MISO第2.1批次輸電擴建計畫

  此次成本回收與融資協議爭議，源自MISO的第2.1批次（Tranche 2.1）輸電擴建計畫。該計畫旨在其中西部服務區域建設一條765千伏骨幹輸電線路，MISO董事會已於2024年12月核准此計畫。由於部分設施的最佳路線走廊延伸至PJM相鄰轄區，計畫因此涉及在MISO自身服務範圍以外的輸電建設，進而引發了FERC本週裁決所處理的跨區域成本分攤問題。

  ## 委員呼籲建立更廣泛的跨區域規劃機制

  兩位FERC委員藉此裁決，力主電網業者在處理跨區域輸電問題上應進行結構性轉變。委員張茱蒂（Judy Chang）與大衛·羅斯納（David Rosner）在聯合贊同意見書中指出，「在某些情況下，針對一個區域系統需求最具效益、最符合成本效益的解決方案，可能包含位於另一區域轄區內的輸電升級工程。」

  張茱蒂與羅斯納承認，現行費率定義在識別跨區域項目方面存在侷限，但表示MISO為確保這些設施得以順利建設所採取的步驟，充分展現了跨區域協調的價值。兩位委員主張，輸電規劃者應將跨區域輸電納為日常工作中「更加常態化、更具目的性」的一環，並援引其潛在效益，包括提升供電可靠度、降低壅塞成本、更有效整合各類資源，以及降低整體基礎設施成本。

  ## 此裁決對跨區域輸電政策的意義

  此次裁決確立了一項原則：即便電網業者無法直接將本身的競爭性招標規則適用於其無法直接掌控的設施，仍可針對建設於鄰近區域轄區內、且服務於其本身系統需求的輸電設施，尋求建設並回收相關費用。張茱蒂與羅斯納表示，強化跨區域互聯將使各區域更有效率地共享資源、減緩極端天氣衝擊，並加速新增需求與發電資源的接入。
