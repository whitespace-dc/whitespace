body_en: |
  The Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) on July 24, 2026, approved a net metering arrangement for a 260-megawatt (MW) artificial intelligence data center co-located with a wind farm of approximately equal capacity, establishing curtailment and demand response conditions that commissioners said could serve as a template for future large behind-the-meter loads in the state.

  The data center, developed by Crusoe, is the second planned for the site and will be operated by Ensign, the designated large load customer. The wind farm has a generation capacity of 265.5 MW. Together, the two data centers planned for the site represent a combined load of roughly 525 MW — nearly double what the generation resource can supply.

  ## Curtailment and Demand Response Conditions

  The commission's July 23 order requires the data center to curtail its full load within 30 minutes during grid emergencies, using physical breaker disconnection if necessary. The facility is also barred from participating in paid demand response programs tied to the co-location arrangement — a distinction that separates mandatory emergency curtailment from voluntary grid services that receive compensation.

  The Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT), the grid operator for most of the state, must provide 60 minutes' advance notice of curtailment orders "when practicable," according to the order. The operator may voluntarily commit to a faster 10-minute response.

  ## Why the Commission Rejected the Developer's Argument

  Crusoe and Ensign argued that the second data center should not face the same curtailment requirements as the first because the combined load of both facilities already exceeds the wind farm's 265.5 MW output. They contended that forcing the entire site offline was disproportionate given that the additional curtailment would not free up generation capacity the grid could use.

  Commissioners rejected that position, largely adopting the administrative law judge's recommendation as revised by Chairman Thomas Gleeson. The order concluded that allowing the second data center to remain online while the first is curtailed would undermine the goal of ensuring generation capacity is available to the broader grid during emergencies.

  ## SB 6 and Regulatory Precedent

  The case is one of the first significant tests of Texas Senate Bill 6 (SB 6), which became law in 2025 and established new rules for large loads within ERCOT's territory, including giving ERCOT the authority to disconnect data centers during grid emergencies. Rather than creating a project-specific exception, commissioners adopted ERCOT's proposed reliability conditions wholesale — a decision that establishes operating precedent for future co-location applications rather than resolving each on its own terms.

  ## Industry Reaction

  Chris Talley, co-founder of the grid-monitoring firm GridTracker, wrote in a LinkedIn post following the decision that the order is not a "death blow" to co-location in ERCOT. In a follow-up message to Utility Dive, Talley said the ruling "essentially requires full backup capacity" — meaning resources capable of carrying the load off-grid for a meaningful period, not simply riding through a brief interruption. He described that interpretation as "an untested assumption."

  Texas Competitive Power Advocates and natural gas producer BKV both filed in the proceeding, arguing the framework could discourage future co-location projects. PUCT staff contended those parties lacked standing under the governing statute, and the commission's final order did not address their arguments.

  ## What Comes Next

  Talley identified a pending co-location application from Amazon and Vistra — proposing a data center campus adjacent to the Comanche Peak nuclear plant, southwest of Fort Worth — as the next major case to watch. "How ERCOT and the Commission treat that arrangement will be very telling," he said.
body_zh: |
  德州公用事業委員會（PUCT）於2026年7月24日批准一座260百萬瓦（MW）人工智慧資料中心與一座裝置容量相近的風力發電廠共址的淨計量用電安排，並確立限電與需量反應條件。委員們表示，此裁決可作為德州未來大型表後負載的管制範本。

  該資料中心由Crusoe開發，是規劃中該場址的第二座，將由指定大用電客戶Ensign營運。風場裝置容量為265.5 MW。兩座資料中心合計負載約達525 MW，幾乎是發電資源供應量的兩倍。

  ## 限電與需量反應條件

  委員會7月23日的命令要求，資料中心必須在電網緊急情況下於30分鐘內完成全負載限電，必要時須以實體斷路器強制切離。該設施亦被禁止參與與共址安排掛鉤、具償付性質的需量反應計畫——這項區分將強制性緊急限電與自願性有償電網服務明確切割。

  依據命令規定，德州電力可靠度委員會（ERCOT）——也就是負責德州絕大部分地區的電網調度機構——須於「可行情況下」提前60分鐘通知限電指令。電廠營運方亦可自願承諾縮短至10分鐘內響應。

  ## 委員會拒絕開發商論點的理由

  Crusoe與Ensign主張，第二座資料中心不應承受與第一座相同的限電要求，理由是兩座設施的合計負載已超過風場265.5 MW的輸出上限。兩家業者認為，強制整個場址全線停機並不相稱，因為額外的限電並不會釋出電網可以調度使用的發電容量。

  委員們拒絕了這項立場，大體上採納了行政法法官的建議——經委員會主席Thomas Gleeson修訂後的版本。命令結論認為，若允許第二座資料中心在第一座限電期間繼續運作，將損害在緊急狀況下確保發電容量可供更廣大電網使用的核心目標。

  ## SB 6與監管先例

  本案是德州參議院第6號法案（SB 6）的首批重大考驗之一。SB 6於2025年正式生效，針對ERCOT管轄範圍內的大型用電負載確立了新規，包括賦予ERCOT在電網緊急狀況下切離資料中心的權力。委員們並未為本案設立個案例外，而是全盤採用ERCOT提出的可靠度條件——此舉為日後的共址申請建立了營運先例，而非逐案個別裁量。

  ## 業界反應

  電網監測公司GridTracker共同創辦人Chris Talley在裁決公布後於LinkedIn發文指出，本次命令並非共址發展在ERCOT的「致命打擊」。他隨後在接受《Utility Dive》採訪時表示，此裁決「實質上要求具備完整的備援容量」——意指資料中心必須具備能在離網狀態下持續支撐負載一段有意義時間的資源，而非僅能撐過短暫中斷。他將這種詮釋方式形容為「尚未經過驗證的假設」。

  德州競爭電力倡議組織（Texas Competitive Power Advocates）與天然氣業者BKV均就本案提出意見，主張此框架可能打擊未來的共址投資意願。PUCT工作人員則主張，上述兩方在主管法規下不具聲請當事人資格，委員會最終命令亦未回應其論點。

  ## 後續發展

  Talley點名亞馬遜（Amazon）與Vistra提出的一件待審共址申請，將是下一個值得關注的重大案例——提案內容為在沃斯堡西南方的科曼奇峰（Comanche Peak）核能電廠旁建設資料中心園區。「ERCOT與委員會如何處理那項安排，將非常能說明問題，」他說。
