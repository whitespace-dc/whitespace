  ## Climate Power launches public emissions tracker for AI data centers

  A climate communications organization called Climate Power has released a tool that publicly scores eight major technology companies on the air pollution potential of their AI data centers, with Elon Musk's xAI ranking last at 6 out of 100 and Amazon ranking highest at 68. The tool, called the Data Center Air Pollution Tracker, is updated daily and draws on data from research firm Cleanview.

  The tracker comes as newly built AI-dedicated data centers drive a surge in natural gas power plant use, which Climate Power says has caused carbon emissions to spike by double-digit percentages at large companies including Amazon, Google, and Microsoft.

  ## How the scoring works

  Each company receives a score between 1 and 100 based on two factors: the share of its projects that use or plan to use standalone natural gas power generation, and the carbon intensity of the electricity grid in the regions where its data centers operate.

  Grid carbon intensity is measured in pounds of carbon dioxide per megawatt-hour (CO2/MWh). A megawatt-hour is the amount of electricity a one-megawatt generator produces in one hour — enough to power roughly 330 average US homes for an hour. Under the tracker's scale, a grid producing 200 lbs CO2/MWh is rated the cleanest; one producing 1,000 lbs CO2/MWh is rated the dirtiest. A score of 100 means a company relies entirely on electricity from the world's cleanest grids.

  The tracker applies standards set by the US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the United Nations, and reporting frameworks from The Financial Times and The Guardian, according to the Kompas.com report citing trade publication Trellis.

  ## Company rankings as of July 24

  Amazon scored 68, the highest among the eight companies assessed, with average emissions of 716 lbs CO2/MWh. Climate Power attributed Amazon's relative standing to the lowest ratio of standalone gas power projects among the group.

  Microsoft ranked second at 64. Google and Meta each scored 61; Google's average emissions were 857 lbs CO2/MWh and Meta's were 802 lbs CO2/MWh.

  AI model developers Anthropic and OpenAI scored 59 and 56, respectively. Oracle scored 51, despite recording average carbon emission rates lower than those of Google or Meta — a result the scoring methodology attributes to other factors such as grid cleanliness in its operating regions.

  xAI scored 6, by far the lowest in the group. Climate Power cited xAI's Colossus data center in Memphis, which uses gas turbines that the organization says are largely unregulated for air pollution limits.

  ## Who the tool is designed for

  Climate Power says the tracker is aimed at companies that are increasing their use of AI and cloud computing services and want to monitor how those procurement decisions affect their own carbon-reduction commitments. The scores are refreshed daily.

  ## Community opposition and policy context

  The construction of large-scale AI data centers has generated resistance from neighboring communities, according to the report, driven both by their electricity consumption and by government support for new coal and natural gas capacity to power them rather than renewable energy sources.

  A Climate Power survey conducted in June found that voters expressed substantially stronger support for data center projects that commit to clean energy than for those powered by coal or natural gas.

  ## Climate Power公開發布AI資料中心排放追蹤工具

  氣候傳播組織Climate Power近日發布一項工具，公開對八家主要科技公司的AI資料中心空氣污染潛力進行評分。結果顯示，伊隆·馬斯克旗下的xAI以滿分100分中僅得6分排名墊底，亞馬遜則以68分居於首位。這項名為「資料中心空氣污染追蹤器」（Data Center Air Pollution Tracker）的工具每日更新，資料來源為研究機構Cleanview。

  此追蹤器的推出，正值專為AI打造的新建資料中心帶動天然氣發電廠使用量急遽攀升之際。Climate Power指出，這波趨勢已導致亞馬遜、Google、微軟等大型企業的碳排放量出現兩位數百分比的大幅成長。

  ## 評分機制說明

  每家公司的得分介於1至100分之間，評分依據兩項因素：其一，該公司旗下有多少比例的專案使用或計畫使用獨立天然氣發電；其二，其資料中心所在地區的電網碳排放強度。

  電網碳排放強度以每百萬瓦小時（MWh）所排放的二氧化碳磅數（lbs CO₂/MWh）計算。一個百萬瓦小時，相當於一部1百萬瓦發電機運轉一小時所產生的電量，大約可供330個美國普通家庭使用一小時。依照此追蹤器的評分標準，電網碳排放強度達200磅CO₂/MWh者被評為最清潔；達1,000磅CO₂/MWh者則被評為最高污染。若一家公司完全仰賴全球最清潔電網的電力，則可獲得滿分100分。

  根據Kompas.com援引業界刊物Trellis的報導，此追蹤器採用美國環境保護署（EPA）、聯合國，以及《金融時報》與《衛報》報告框架所制定的相關標準。

  ## 截至7月24日的公司評分排名

  亞馬遜以68分拿下八家受評公司的最高分，平均碳排放量為716磅CO₂/MWh。Climate Power認為，亞馬遜能相對領先，在於其獨立天然氣發電專案的占比在所有受評公司中最低。

  微軟以64分排名第二。Google與Meta各得61分；Google的平均碳排放量為857磅CO₂/MWh，Meta則為802磅CO₂/MWh。

  AI模型開發商Anthropic與OpenAI分別獲得59分與56分。甲骨文（Oracle）得51分，儘管其平均碳排放量實際上低於Google和Meta，但在評分方法論中，其資料中心所在地區的電網清潔度等其他因素仍拖累了整體得分。

  xAI以6分遠遠墊底，與其他公司差距懸殊。Climate Power特別點名xAI位於美國田納西州曼菲斯市的Colossus資料中心，指出該中心使用的燃氣渦輪機在空氣污染排放限制方面幾乎處於監管空白。

  ## 工具的目標使用族群

  Climate Power表示，此追蹤器的目標對象是那些正在擴大使用AI與雲端運算服務的企業，協助它們監控採購決策對自身減碳承諾所造成的影響。評分每日刷新更新。

  ## 社區反彈與政策背景

  根據報導，大規模AI資料中心的興建在周邊社區引發強烈反彈，原因不僅在於其龐大的用電需求，也包括政府為支應資料中心電力需求，轉而支持新建燃煤與天然氣電廠，而非優先採用再生能源。

  Climate Power於今年6月進行的民調顯示，選民對於承諾使用清潔能源的資料中心建設案，明顯比使用燃煤或天然氣的同類案件抱持更強烈的支持態度。
