body_en: |
  China's Ministry of Commerce (MofCom) is consulting leading domestic artificial intelligence and semiconductor companies on possible export controls that would limit the transfer of advanced Chinese technologies to foreign buyers, including restrictions on AI model weights and overseas chip production based on Chinese designs, the Financial Times reported on July 21.

  The consultations involve major Chinese technology groups including Alibaba and ByteDance, according to the FT. MofCom has reportedly sought views on limiting the transfer of key data used to train AI models overseas, as well as restricting foreign users from downloading model weights — the numerical parameters that encode a trained AI model's capabilities and represent significant intellectual and commercial value.

  ## What the proposed controls would cover

  Beyond data and model weight restrictions, MofCom has also sought industry views on preventing overseas chipmakers such as Qualcomm and TSMC from manufacturing advanced semiconductors based on designs developed by Chinese companies, including Huawei, the FT reported. Agentic AI — systems capable of taking sequences of actions with limited human oversight — was identified as one area where potential restrictions could apply.

  The proposals are still under discussion. Technology companies consulted by regulators have advised that tighter controls would hinder China's ability to advance in the AI technology race, sources told the FT.

  ## Escalating US-China technology tensions

  The consultations follow a period of intensifying friction between Washington and Beijing over semiconductor supply chains and AI capabilities. The United States has restricted sales of some advanced processors to China, a move that has contributed to broader strains in global semiconductor supply at a time when the industry is also contending with conflict in the Middle East and tariffs imposed by US President Donald Trump.

  China has closed the gap with the United States in frontier AI development in recent years, a shift that gained wider international attention after the launch of the DeepSeek AI chatbot in 2025.

  ## China's parallel push for AI cooperation

  The reported export control deliberations come alongside public statements by Chinese leadership emphasising global AI collaboration. President Xi Jinping, speaking at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) in Shanghai last week, called for greater international cooperation on AI and pledged training support for developing nations. Xi said countries should take a "people-centred approach" to AI development and urged partners to build what he described as a fair and equitable system for global AI governance to "bridge digital divides, promote sustainable development and create new historical injustice in AI."

  China has also committed to significant domestic buildout of data centres to support further AI development, underscoring its intent to maintain sovereign control over critical AI infrastructure.

  ## What it means for the industry

  If enacted, the proposed controls would introduce a new layer of restriction on how Chinese AI technology flows to foreign companies and governments, mirroring — in reverse — the chip export restrictions the United States has applied to China. For global chipmakers and AI developers with supply relationships or licensing arrangements tied to Chinese designs, restrictions on overseas fabrication of those designs would add compliance complexity and potentially disrupt existing production arrangements.

  The outcome of MofCom's consultations, and whether the proposals advance to formal regulation, has not yet been determined, according to the FT's sources.

  ## 中國研擬管制AI模型、晶片及模型權重出口

  據《金融時報》7月21日報導，中國商務部正向國內主要人工智慧與半導體企業徵詢意見，研議是否對先進中國技術的對外移轉實施出口管制，範圍涵蓋AI模型權重，以及境外依中國設計生產晶片等項目。

  據《金融時報》報導，此次徵詢涉及阿里巴巴、字節跳動等中國科技巨頭。商務部據悉已就限制將用於訓練AI模型的關鍵資料移轉至境外，以及限制境外使用者下載模型權重等議題廣徵業界意見。模型權重是指編碼已訓練AI模型能力的數值參數，具有重大的智慧財產與商業價值。

  ## 擬管制範圍

  除資料與模型權重外，商務部亦就如何防止高通、台積電等境外晶片製造商，依華為等中國企業所開發的設計製造先進半導體一事，向業界徵集看法。報導指出，能在幾乎不需人工監督的情況下自主執行一連串動作的「代理型AI」（Agentic AI），也被列為可能受到管制的領域之一。

  相關提案目前仍在討論中。知情人士告訴《金融時報》，接受政府徵詢的科技企業已提出警告，認為若管制趨嚴，將阻礙中國在AI技術競賽中持續進步的能力。

  ## 中美科技緊張局勢持續升溫

  上述徵詢行動，發生在華盛頓與北京因半導體供應鏈及AI能力問題而摩擦不斷加劇之際。美國已限制部分先進處理器對中國的銷售，此舉加劇了全球半導體供應鏈的緊張態勢——而整個產業同時還面臨中東地區衝突，以及美國總統唐納·川普所加徵關稅的雙重壓力。

  近年來，中國在前沿AI開發領域已大幅縮小與美國的差距。2025年DeepSeek AI聊天機器人問世後，這一轉變在國際間引發了更廣泛的關注。

  ## 中國同步推動AI國際合作

  在外界報導中國研擬出口管制的同時，中國領導層也公開強調全球AI合作的重要性。國家主席習近平上週在上海出席世界人工智慧大會（WAIC）時，呼籲加強人工智慧領域的國際合作，並承諾為開發中國家提供培訓支援。習近平表示，各國應以「以人為本」的方式推動AI發展，並敦促合作夥伴共同構建他所描述的公平公正的全球AI治理體系，以「弭平數位落差、促進永續發展，避免在AI領域製造新的歷史不公」。

  中國亦已承諾大力推動國內資料中心建設，以支撐AI進一步發展，凸顯其維護關鍵AI基礎設施主權的決心。

  ## 對產業的潛在影響

  若上述管制措施付諸實施，將為中國AI技術流向境外企業與政府的方式增添一道新的限制，其效果恰與美國對中國實施晶片出口管制相互對應，可謂鏡像翻轉。對於在供應關係或授權協議上與中國設計有所連結的全球晶片製造商及AI開發商而言，限制在境外依該等設計進行代工生產，將帶來更複雜的法規遵循負擔，並可能擾亂現有的生產安排。

  據《金融時報》消息人士透露，商務部此次徵詢的結果，以及相關提案是否將推進為正式法規，目前仍尚未確定。
