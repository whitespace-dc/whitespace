body_en: |
  The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission has exempted a major subset of data center asset-backed securities from post-2008 financial crisis investor-protection rules, including risk retention requirements, in a clarification that removes compliance burdens that had kept some firms from entering the market.

  The SEC's staff issued the guidance in a letter late last month responding to a query from law firm Latham & Watkins. "We agree that the fixed-income or other securities issued in data center securitizations of the type described in your letter are not asset-backed securities," the regulator said. Because data centers are physical infrastructure rather than financial assets that liquidate over time — such as car loans or home mortgages — the SEC concluded that bonds backed by them fall outside the statutory definition that triggers the rules.

  ## What the exemption covers and what it does not

  The rules the SEC said do not apply were written after the 2008 global financial crisis to protect investors from the kind of excesses in securitization markets that contributed to the near collapse of the banking system. Chief among them is risk retention, which requires issuers of asset-backed securities (ABS) — investments created by pooling together financial assets that generate regular cash flows — to hold a portion of those securities, aligning their interests with outside investors.

  The exemption is not universal. Commercial mortgage-backed securities (CMBS) backed by data centers must still comply with the rules because their collateral is a mortgage rather than the physical assets of the data center itself.

  The SEC's guidance is not a formal rule change. Nevertheless, it carries practical weight: firms had been applying the rules as a precaution even when their applicability was unclear, according to The Edge Malaysia.

  ## Industry argument for relief

  Latham & Watkins had argued that the post-crisis rules do not fit the structure of data center securitizations and that compliance had proven costly and unnecessary. Kevin Fingeret, a partner at Latham, said data center operators that issue ABS already retain a substantial amount of risk in their deals by necessity — without doing so, they would not achieve strong credit ratings. Requiring formal compliance with risk retention rules had forced "ownership structures that weren't necessarily in line with their ultimate objectives," Fingeret said. "There was a growing need for this relief," he added.

  ## A fast-growing market

  The clarification arrives as Wall Street works to channel increasing volumes of debt into data center and digital infrastructure construction. Data center ABS issuance rose from $2.4 billion in annual new issuance in 2020 to $15.5 billion last year, according to data compiled by Bloomberg News cited by The Edge Malaysia. The market is on pace to set a new annual record in 2026, the data show.

  Soaring demand for AI computing has pushed companies to seek new ways to raise capital for expansion, according to Reuters. The Trump administration has backed the buildout; last year the president signed executive orders aimed at accelerating AI development by loosening regulations and bolstering data center energy supplies, according to The Edge Malaysia.

  ## Significance

  The exemption broadens the pool of potential issuers by reducing compliance costs and removing structural constraints that had deterred some firms from tapping ABS markets. With data center ABS already on track to surpass last year's $15.5 billion record, the SEC's guidance is positioned to accelerate debt issuance in a sector where capital demand remains large and growing.

  ## SEC裁定資料中心債券豁免金融危機後的證券化法規

  美國證券交易委員會（SEC）已將資料中心資產擔保證券（ABS）的一大類別，從2008年全球金融危機後為保護投資人所訂立的法規中豁免，這些規定包括風險自留要求。此次釐清移除了部分業者因法規合規負擔而遲遲不敢進場的障礙。

  SEC幕僚於上月底回應法律事務所Latham & Watkins的詢問，在回函中發布了上述指引。監管機構表示：「我們同意，依照您來函所描述的資料中心證券化架構所發行的固定收益或其他有價證券，並不屬於資產擔保證券。」由於資料中心是實體基礎設施，而非汽車貸款或房屋抵押貸款等隨時間逐步清償的金融資產，SEC因此認定，以資料中心為擔保所發行的債券，不落入觸發相關法規的法定定義範疇之內。

  ## 豁免範圍及其限制

  SEC所認定不適用的法規，是2008年全球金融危機後為防止證券化市場過度擴張而制定的——正是那些失控的行為幾乎導致銀行體系崩潰。其中最核心的規定是風險自留要求：ABS發行人——亦即透過匯集可產生定期現金流的金融資產來創設投資商品的機構——必須持有一定比例的證券，以使其利益與外部投資人保持一致。

  然而，此次豁免並非全面適用。以資料中心為擔保的商業不動產抵押貸款證券（CMBS）仍須遵守相關規定，因為其擔保品是抵押貸款本身，而非資料中心的實體資產。

  SEC此次指引並非正式的法規修訂。儘管如此，其實際影響不容忽視：據《The Edge Malaysia》報導，業者過去即便對法規是否適用存有疑慮，仍傾向於謹慎遵循相關規定。

  ## 業界爭取豁免的理由

  Latham & Watkins曾主張，金融危機後訂立的法規並不符合資料中心證券化的結構特性，強制合規不僅代價高昂，也屬多此一舉。該事務所合夥人Kevin Fingeret表示，發行ABS的資料中心營運商在交易中本就必須保留相當大比例的風險——若不如此，便無法取得強健的信用評等。強制要求正式遵守風險自留規定，反而迫使業者採取「與其最終目標未必契合的持股架構」。Fingeret補充說：「業界對此一豁免的需求日益迫切。」

  ## 快速擴張的市場

  此次釐清正值華爾街積極將大規模債務資金引入資料中心及數位基礎設施建設之際。根據彭博資訊匯編、並由《The Edge Malaysia》引述的數據，資料中心ABS年度新發行規模已從2020年的24億美元攀升至去年的155億美元。數據顯示，2026年的年度發行量有望再創歷史新高。

  據路透社報導，人工智慧運算需求急遽升溫，促使企業積極尋求新的擴張融資管道。川普政府也力挺相關建設；據《The Edge Malaysia》報導，川普去年簽署多項行政命令，旨在透過鬆綁法規、強化資料中心能源供應，加速推動人工智慧發展。

  ## 重要意義

  此次豁免藉由降低合規成本、消除部分業者卻步於ABS市場的結構性障礙，擴大了潛在發行人的範疇。在資料中心ABS已然有望突破去年155億美元紀錄的背景下，SEC此次指引料將進一步加速這一資本需求龐大且持續成長的產業之債務發行。
