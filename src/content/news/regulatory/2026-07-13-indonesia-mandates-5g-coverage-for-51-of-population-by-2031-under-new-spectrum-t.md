body_en: |
  Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital (Komdigi) has ordered Telekomunikasi Selular (Telkomsel), PT Indosat Tbk. (ISAT), and PT XLSMART Telecom Sejahtera Tbk. to deploy fifth-generation (5G) mobile networks covering at least 51 percent of Indonesia's national population by 2031, attaching the obligation to new spectrum licenses in the 700 megahertz (MHz) and 2.6 gigahertz (GHz) bands, Bisnis.com reported July 10, 2026. The three operators have a maximum of five years to meet the coverage threshold.

  ## Two tracks of rollout obligation

  The new spectrum awards carry two parallel sets of commitments. On the first track, all three operators must extend fourth-generation (4G) Long Term Evolution (LTE) mobile broadband — the current mainstream standard for mobile internet — to rural villages and sub-districts selected from a pool of 538 target localities managed by Komdigi.

  On the second, each winning operator must accelerate commercial deployment of International Mobile Telecommunications-2020 (IMT-2020) networks — the formal technical standard underpinning 5G — across designated cities and regencies, continuing until the network reaches the minimum 51-percent national population threshold.

  Komdigi described the policy as targeting more than signal reach. "This strategic step in selecting radio frequency band users is not merely to expand signal coverage, but rather the government's effort to improve the quality of life of Indonesians comprehensively," the ministry said in an official statement. The government characterized the approach as having broad impact on the socio-economic resilience of Indonesian society.

  ## Spectrum allocations and bid values

  Telkomsel secured the largest total spectrum allocation, winning 100 MHz across both bands: 80 MHz of time-division duplex (TDD) spectrum in the 2.6 GHz band at a bid value of Rp 545.84 billion, and 2x10 MHz in the 700 MHz band at Rp 642.5 billion.

  Indosat and XLSMART each secured 80 MHz in total. Indosat won 2x10 MHz at 700 MHz for Rp 507.48 billion and 60 MHz at 2.6 GHz for Rp 372 billion. XLSMART — the entity formed from the merger of XL Axiata and Smartfren — captured the largest single block in the 700 MHz band at 2x15 MHz for Rp 1.06 trillion, and added 50 MHz at 2.6 GHz for Rp 231.6 billion.

  Across both bands combined, Telkomsel is the only operator to exceed 80 MHz. XLSMART holds the largest individual block in the 700 MHz band, while Telkomsel leads at 2.6 GHz.

  ## Regulatory context

  Komdigi established the 5G coverage target as a condition of the spectrum licenses, requiring each operator to activate service in committed cities and regencies until the 51-percent national population level is reached, with 2031 as the hard deadline. The ministry said it views the spectrum selection framework as a vehicle for improving quality of life across Indonesia, describing the combined 4G rural expansion and 5G urban rollout obligations as part of a broader effort to strengthen social and economic resilience nationwide. Each of the three operators must meet the population threshold within the five-year window tied to their respective frequency allocations.

  ## 印尼強制三大電信商2031年前部署5G，覆蓋逾半數人口

  據Bisnis.com於2026年7月10日報導，印尼通訊暨數位部（Komdigi）已正式下令電信業者Telekomunikasi Selular（Telkomsel）、PT Indosat Tbk.（ISAT）及PT XLSMART Telecom Sejahtera Tbk.，在2031年前完成第五代（5G）行動網路的商業部署，覆蓋範圍須達印尼全國人口的至少51%。此項義務已直接附加於7月10日核發的700 MHz與2.6 GHz兩個頻段新頻譜執照之中。三家業者最多擁有五年時間達成上述覆蓋門檻。

  ## 兩條並行的建網義務

  此次新頻譜核發附帶兩套並行義務。第一軌方面，三家業者均須將第四代（4G）長期演進技術（LTE）行動寬頻——即目前行動網際網路的主流標準——延伸至農村村落與次區域，服務對象來自Komdigi所管理的538個目標地點名單。

  第二軌方面，每家得標業者須加速在指定城市與縣份商業部署IMT-2020網路——即5G的正式國際技術標準——並持續推進，直至網路覆蓋達到全國人口至少51%的最低門檻為止。

  Komdigi表示，此政策的目標不僅止於擴大訊號涵蓋範圍。該部在官方聲明中指出：「這項遴選無線電頻率使用者的策略性舉措，不只是為了擴大訊號覆蓋，更是政府全面提升印尼人民生活品質的努力。」政府並將此做法定位為對印尼社會社會經濟韌性具有廣泛影響的重要施政。

  ## 頻譜分配與競標金額

  Telkomsel取得了三家業者中最大的頻譜總量，在兩個頻段合計贏得100 MHz：在2.6 GHz頻段取得80 MHz時分雙工（TDD）頻譜，競標金額為5,458.4億印尼盾；在700 MHz頻段取得2×10 MHz，競標金額為6,425億印尼盾。

  Indosat與XLSMART各取得80 MHz的頻譜總量。Indosat在700 MHz頻段贏得2×10 MHz，競標金額為5,074.8億印尼盾；在2.6 GHz頻段取得60 MHz，競標金額為3,720億印尼盾。XLSMART——由XL Axiata與Smartfren合併而成的實體——在700 MHz頻段奪得單一最大頻譜區塊，以2×15 MHz、1.06兆印尼盾的競標金額勝出，另在2.6 GHz頻段取得50 MHz，競標金額為2,316億印尼盾。

  合計兩個頻段，Telkomsel是唯一超過80 MHz的業者。在700 MHz頻段的最大單一頻譜區塊由XLSMART持有，而2.6 GHz頻段則由Telkomsel居首。

  ## 主管機關的法規背景

  Komdigi將5G覆蓋目標列為頻譜執照的核發條件，要求每家業者在承諾的城市與縣份持續開通服務，直至全國人口覆蓋率達到51%，並以2031年為最終期限。該部表示，視頻譜遴選框架為提升印尼全國人民生活品質的施政工具，並將4G農村擴網與5G城市建網兩項義務並列，描述為全面強化全國社會與經濟韌性之整體努力的一部分。三家業者均須在各自頻率分配所附帶的五年期限內達成人口覆蓋門檻。
