body_en: |
  A Texas border crossing smuggling case has resulted in a $1,400 fine and three-year probation term for a man who attempted to bring six 25-pound cylinders of R410A refrigerant into the United States from Mexico.

  Victor Guzman-Soto was apprehended at the Brownsville, Texas, port of entry on the US-Mexico border in May 2024, according to Cooling Post. He subsequently pleaded guilty to the offence.

  ## The case

  Guzman-Soto was caught carrying six cylinders of R410A, a hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) blend refrigerant widely used in air conditioning systems. Each cylinder weighed 25 pounds, placing the total seized quantity at 150 pounds of refrigerant.

  The court imposed a $1,400 fine and three years of probation following his guilty plea.

  ## Significance

  R410A is subject to import controls in the United States under federal regulations governing HFC refrigerants, which are potent greenhouse gases. Unauthorised importation bypasses those controls. The Brownsville crossing, situated on the Texas-Mexico border, is a documented point of entry for smuggled goods between the two countries.

  The case illustrates ongoing enforcement activity targeting illegal refrigerant imports at US land borders.
