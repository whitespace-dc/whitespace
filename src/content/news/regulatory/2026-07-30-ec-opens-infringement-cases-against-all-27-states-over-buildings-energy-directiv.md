body_en: |
  The European Commission on July 15 opened infringement procedures against all 27 EU member states for failing to fully transpose the recast Energy Performance of Buildings Directive — formally Directive 2024/1275, known as the EPBD — into national law, sending letters of formal notice to each government. The directive was adopted in 2024 and member states were required to notify their transposition by May 29, 2026.

  ## Industry coalition calls for effective implementation

  The enforcement action has prompted a coordinated response from across the buildings and energy sector. Twenty-seven organisations, including the European contractors' group AREA, the European Heat Pump Association, EPEE, and Eurovent, have issued a joint statement calling for the EPBD to be fully transposed and effectively implemented, and for citizens, businesses, building owners, professionals, and public authorities to receive "the clarity and certainty they need to act."

  The signatories argue that workable implementation requires clear guidance, compliance and verification mechanisms, sufficient administrative capacity, investment in skills, and strong coordination among national, regional, and local authorities and market actors.

  ## Why the directive matters

  The joint statement underscores the scale of the sector the EPBD governs. Buildings account for around 40% of the EU's final energy consumption and roughly half of its gas consumption, while heating, cooling, and hot water together represent around 80% of household energy use.

  "The EPBD is one of Europe's most important tools to reduce energy demand and optimise energy use across building services, including heating, cooling, ventilation, hot water and lighting; lower energy bills; strengthen energy security; improve indoor environmental quality and comfort; and support Europe's clean technology market," the joint statement says. Those benefits, the signatories add, will materialise only if the directive is fully transposed, effectively implemented, and properly enforced.

  ## Implementation remains uneven across the bloc

  Despite describing the May 29, 2026 transposition deadline as "a key milestone," the signatories report that the situation across the bloc is inconsistent. Based on exchanges with national authorities, market actors, and stakeholders, "the current picture remains mixed," they write. Some member states are moving in the right direction and developing practical implementation tools, but many remain delayed, address requirements only partially, or have focused on legal transposition without putting in place the necessary guidance, verification, and compliance checks.

  "We are therefore concerned that several EPBD provisions risk being weakened, delayed or left unenforced in practice," the joint statement says.

  ## Market certainty at stake

  The industry coalition frames incomplete implementation as a direct threat to investment planning across the sector. Companies, investors, installers, engineers, building owners, and public authorities need confidence that EU-level rules will be applied in practice, the statement argues. Many have already made, or are preparing, investments based on the EPBD framework, and delays at this stage "could undermine confidence and send the wrong signal to the market."

  "Without clear and consistent implementation, it becomes harder to plan investments, develop solutions, train professionals and scale up delivery across member states," according to the statement.

  ## What the signatories want from the Commission

  The 27-organisation coalition calls on the Commission to follow up where member states have not fully transposed the directive or where national frameworks are incomplete or unenforceable. They explicitly endorse continuation of the newly opened infringement proceedings as a necessary tool to ensure full transposition of the EPBD across all member states.
body_zh: |
  歐盟執委會於7月15日對全體27個成員國啟動違規程序，原因是各國未能在期限前全面將重訂版《建築能效指令》——正式名稱為第2024/1275號指令，通稱EPBD——轉化為國內法，並已向各國政府發出正式通知函。該指令於2024年通過，成員國應於2026年5月29日前完成轉化並通報。

  ## 產業聯盟呼籲有效落實

  執法行動已促使建築與能源部門跨界聯合回應。包括歐洲承包商協會AREA、歐洲熱泵協會、EPEE及Eurovent在內的27個組織發表聯合聲明，呼籲全面轉化並有效落實EPBD，並要求確保公民、企業、建築業主、專業人士與公共主管機關獲得「採取行動所需的明確方向與確定性」。

  聯署組織主張，可行的落實方案需具備明確指引、合規與驗證機制、充足的行政能量、技能投資，以及中央、地區與地方主管機關及市場參與者之間的有力協調。

  ## 指令的重要性

  聯合聲明強調了EPBD所涵蓋領域的規模。建築約占歐盟最終能源消耗的40%、天然氣消耗量的約一半，而暖氣、冷氣與熱水合計約占家庭能源使用量的80%。

  聲明指出：「EPBD是歐洲降低能源需求、優化建築服務（涵蓋暖氣、冷氣、通風、熱水及照明）能源使用，進而降低能源帳單、強化能源安全、改善室內環境品質與舒適度，以及支持歐洲清潔科技市場的最重要工具之一。」聯署組織補充，唯有在指令獲得全面轉化、有效落實並切實執行的前提下，上述效益才能真正實現。

  ## 各成員國落實進度參差不齊

  儘管聯署組織將2026年5月29日的轉化期限描述為「關鍵里程碑」，但他們指出，各成員國的現況仍然不一致。根據與各國主管機關、市場參與者及利害關係人的交流，「目前局面依然複雜」。部分成員國正朝正確方向推進，並著手開發實用的落實工具；但許多國家仍有所延遲，僅局部處理相關要求，或雖完成法律層面的轉化，卻未建立必要的指引、驗證與合規查核機制。

  聲明表示：「因此，我們憂慮EPBD的多項規定可能在實務上遭到削弱、延遲或形同虛設。」

  ## 市場確定性岌岌可危

  產業聯盟將不完整的落實定性為對整個部門投資規劃的直接威脅。聲明指出，企業、投資人、安裝業者、工程師、建築業主與公共主管機關，都需要確信歐盟層級的規定將在實務上獲得落實。許多人已基於EPBD框架進行投資或正在籌備投資，此時出現的延誤「可能打擊市場信心，並向市場傳遞錯誤訊號」。

  聲明進一步指出：「若缺乏明確一致的落實，各成員國的投資規劃、解決方案開發、專業人才培訓及規模化交付都將面臨更大困難。」

  ## 聯署組織對執委會的訴求

  這個由27個組織組成的聯盟呼籲執委會，對尚未完整轉化指令、或國內法律框架不完整或無從執行的成員國採取後續行動。他們明確表態支持繼續推進新啟動的違規程序，視之為確保所有成員國全面轉化EPBD的必要手段。
