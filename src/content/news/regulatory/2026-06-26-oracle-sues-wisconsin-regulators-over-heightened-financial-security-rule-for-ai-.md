body_en: |
  Oracle America Cloud Services has filed suit against Wisconsin utility regulators, asking a state court to overturn a Public Service Commission order that raised the financial security standards hyperscale data center customers must meet before Wisconsin Electric Power Co. will build dedicated infrastructure to serve them.

  The lawsuit targets one element of Wisconsin Electric's new tariffs for very large electricity customers — specifically, the credit thresholds regulators imposed after the tariff proposal had already been negotiated among the utility, its customers, and other parties. Oracle says the change could force it to provide more than $100 million annually in letters of credit or cash deposits to proceed with its planned Lighthouse data center campus in Port Washington, Wisconsin.

  ## The disputed financial security terms

  Wisconsin Electric developed the tariffs to accommodate rapidly growing electricity demand from hyperscale data centers while shielding existing ratepayers from costs of infrastructure built exclusively for those projects. Customers served under the tariffs must subscribe to dedicated generating resources.

  In its original proposal, Wisconsin Electric would have exempted customers from posting financial security if their parent company held an investment-grade credit rating of BBB from S&P Global Ratings or Baa2 from Moody's Investors Service and met specified financial tests. The utility also proposed retaining discretion to waive the requirement case by case.

  The Public Service Commission instead raised the qualifying threshold to A- from S&P or A3 from Moody's — a meaningfully higher bar on both agencies' scales — and eliminated the utility's authority to grant waivers. Oracle argues the commission had no evidentiary basis for those changes and asks the court to restore Wisconsin Electric's original proposal. Oracle is not challenging the tariffs themselves.

  "The overwhelming weight of the record evidence supports approving the tariffs' Financial Support Requirements as proposed by Wisconsin Electric," Oracle argued in the filing, noting that commission staff, Microsoft, Wisconsin Electric, and other parties had all supported the original framework.

  ## The Lighthouse campus at stake

  The lawsuit centers on Oracle's Lighthouse development, a nearly 1-gigawatt (GW) campus in Port Washington being built with Vantage Data Centers and OpenAI. A gigawatt is a billion watts of capacity, roughly equivalent to the output of a large nuclear power plant. Oracle describes the project as representing approximately $15 billion in planned investment, intended to support AI operations including large language model training and inference.

  Under the commission's revised standards, Oracle says it faces an annual financial security obligation exceeding $100 million in letters of credit or cash deposits — costs Oracle argues are not supported by the record before the commission.

  ## Why regulators tightened the requirements

  Clean Wisconsin, an environmental advocacy group that opposed a separate rehearing request in the proceeding, argued the commission acted correctly in strengthening the tariff. The group said hyperscale data centers represent a "fundamental change" for Wisconsin Electric's system and that new very large customers could add load equal to "half or more" of the utility's existing system within five years, warranting stronger financial protections for existing customers and shareholders.

  Clean Wisconsin disputed Oracle's claim that regulators acted without adequate evidentiary support, arguing the commission reasonably concluded that stronger credit standards were warranted and that broad utility discretion to waive collateral requirements was inappropriate. The group also opposed a separate proposal that would allow Oracle to satisfy most of its collateral obligation through a parent guarantee rather than cash or letters of credit.

  ## Regulators' preference for cash over parent guarantees

  Kent Chandler, a former chairman of the Kentucky Public Service Commission and now a senior fellow at the R Street Institute, said financial assurances become more consequential as utilities commit larger sums to serve individual customers.

  "Regulators are often more comfortable with cash-like assurances than corporate parent guarantees," Chandler told Data Center Knowledge, explaining that cash deposits and letters of credit are easier for utilities to recover than parent guarantees when hundreds of millions or billions of dollars in infrastructure have been committed to a single project.

  Chandler noted that utilities can generally recover their capital investments through rates regardless of what happens to the customer. "So it's not surprising to see regulators stepping in to look out for other captive customers," he said.

  ## A compromise proposal still pending

  The Wisconsin Public Service Commission has not yet ruled on a rehearing petition filed by Wisconsin Electric, Vantage, and Cloverleaf Infrastructure. That petition proposes a graduated security framework for customers that fall below the A-/A3 credit threshold. It would also allow Oracle to satisfy 90% of its required financial security through a parent guaranty, with the remaining 10% met through a letter of credit or cash deposit — a structure that Clean Wisconsin has opposed.

  ## What the case could establish

  Utilities nationwide are reassessing how to allocate the financial risks of building power infrastructure for AI data centers as grid operators have raised questions about whether all announced hyperscale projects will ultimately be built. Oracle's suit may become one of the first judicial rulings on how far state utility regulators can go in requiring hyperscale customers to demonstrate financial strength before utilities commit large amounts of capital to new infrastructure on their behalf.

  The Wisconsin Public Service Commission's ruling on the pending rehearing petition has not been issued.
body_zh: |
  Oracle America Cloud Services已就威斯康辛州電力監管機關提起訴訟，要求州法院撤銷公共服務委員會（Public Service Commission）的一項命令。該命令提高了超大規模資料中心客戶在Wisconsin Electric Power Co.（威斯康辛電力公司）為其建設專用基礎設施之前，所需符合的財務擔保標準。

  本次訴訟針對的是Wisconsin Electric為超大型用電客戶制定的新費率中的特定條款——即監管機關在費率提案已由電力公司、客戶及其他各方完成協商後，額外加諸的信用門檻。Oracle表示，此變更可能迫使其每年提供逾1億美元的信用狀或現金保證金，方可推進其在威斯康辛州波特華盛頓（Port Washington）計畫興建的Lighthouse資料中心園區。

  ## 爭議中的財務擔保條款

  Wisconsin Electric制定此費率，旨在因應超大規模資料中心急速攀升的用電需求，同時保護現有用戶免於承擔專為這些大型專案建設基礎設施所生的成本。依費率規定，受其服務的客戶須認購專屬發電資源。

  在原始提案中，若客戶母公司持有標準普爾全球評級（S&P Global Ratings）BBB或穆迪投資者服務（Moody's Investors Service）Baa2的投資等級信用評等，並符合特定財務指標，Wisconsin Electric可豁免其提交財務擔保的義務。電力公司亦提議保留逐案豁免要求的裁量權。

  然而，公共服務委員會將符合豁免資格的評級門檻調高至標準普爾A-或穆迪A3——在兩家評級機構的評等體系中，這均屬明顯更高的標準——並取消了電力公司的豁免裁量權。Oracle主張委員會作出此等變更缺乏充分的事證依據，要求法院恢復Wisconsin Electric的原始提案。Oracle並未就費率本身提出異議。

  Oracle在訴狀中指出：「壓倒性的記錄證據支持依Wisconsin Electric所提版本核准費率中的財務擔保規定，」並指出委員會工作人員、微軟、Wisconsin Electric及其他各方均支持原始框架。

  ## 攸關Lighthouse園區

  本訴訟的核心在於Oracle的Lighthouse開發案——一座位於波特華盛頓、容量近1吉瓦（GW）的大型園區，正與Vantage Data Centers及OpenAI聯手興建。1吉瓦相當於10億瓦的容量，約等同於一座大型核電廠的發電量。Oracle表示，此專案計畫投資金額約達150億美元，旨在支持AI運算，涵蓋大型語言模型的訓練與推論作業。

  依委員會修訂後的標準，Oracle表示其每年將面臨逾1億美元的信用狀或現金保證金財務擔保義務——Oracle主張此等費用並無委員會記錄在案的依據可循。

  ## 監管機關為何強化財務要求

  Clean Wisconsin是一個環保倡議團體，曾反對本案程序中一項個別的再審聽證申請，主張委員會此舉正確無誤。該團體表示，超大規模資料中心代表Wisconsin Electric系統的「根本性變革」，新增的超大型客戶在五年內可能增加相當於電力公司現有系統「一半或以上」的負載，因此有必要為現有客戶和股東提供更強有力的財務保障。

  Clean Wisconsin反駁Oracle所稱監管機關在缺乏充分事證下採取行動的說法，主張委員會有充分理由認定更嚴格的信用標準是必要的，且賦予電力公司豁免擔保要求的廣泛裁量權並不妥當。該團體亦反對另一項提案——即允許Oracle以母公司擔保方式履行其大部分的擔保義務，而非使用現金或信用狀。

  ## 監管機關傾向現金勝於母公司擔保

  肯特．錢德勒（Kent Chandler）曾任肯塔基州公共服務委員會主席，現為R Street Institute資深研究員。他指出，隨著電力公司為個別客戶承諾投入更大規模資金，財務保障的重要性也隨之提升。

  「監管機關通常更青睞現金性質的保障，而非企業母公司擔保，」錢德勒接受Data Center Knowledge採訪時解釋，當數億乃至數十億美元的基礎設施已為單一專案投入，現金保證金和信用狀比母公司擔保更易於電力公司追索。

  錢德勒指出，無論客戶後續情況如何，電力公司通常可透過電費回收其資本投資。「因此，看到監管機關出手保護其他被動客戶，並不令人意外，」他說。

  ## 折衷方案仍待裁決

  Wisconsin公共服務委員會尚未就Wisconsin Electric、Vantage及Cloverleaf Infrastructure提出的再審請願作出裁決。該請願提議對信用評等未達A-/A3門檻的客戶採用漸進式擔保框架，同時允許Oracle以母公司擔保滿足其90%的財務擔保義務，其餘10%則以信用狀或現金保證金支應——此架構遭到Clean Wisconsin的反對。

  ## 本案的指標意義

  由於電網業者已就已宣布的超大規模專案能否全數落地提出質疑，全美各地電力公司正重新審視如何分配為AI資料中心建設電力基礎設施的財務風險。Oracle此次訴訟，或將成為首批就州級電力監管機關在電力公司為其承諾大規模資本投入前，要求超大規模客戶展現財務實力的裁量範圍作出司法裁決的案例之一。

  Wisconsin公共服務委員會對該再審請願的裁決，迄今尚未公布。
