body_en: |
  The Philippine Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC) reported Wednesday that processing times for three categories of energy permits have fallen well below mandated timelines since the regulator integrated with the Energy Virtual One-Stop Shop (EVOSS), a web-based government platform for power project applications.

  EVOSS, operated by the Department of Energy, is designed to consolidate and accelerate the processing of applications for power generation, transmission, and distribution projects. The ERC began connecting to the system in late 2023.

  ## Permit timelines now running at half the mandated ceiling

  Under the ERC's 2020 Citizen's Charter — the Philippine government's published service standard — three permit types previously carried a 270-day processing ceiling. The ERC said all three are now being resolved in roughly half that time through EVOSS:

  - Authority to develop and operate point-to-point (P2P) limited transmission facilities: averaging 144.46 days against the 270-day standard
  - Approval of capital expenditures (capex) for regulated entities: averaging 140.53 days against the same 270-day standard
  - Issuance of certificates of compliance (CoCs), which confirm that facilities meet regulatory requirements: averaging 29.65 days against a 60-day standard

  ## Volume processed through the platform

  As of July, 646 applications have been filed through EVOSS and resolved — either approved or disapproved — according to the ERC. The breakdown by permit type: 331 provisional authority to operate requests, 143 CoC applications, 104 P2P applications, and 68 capex filings.

  ## Regulator's position

  ERC Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Francis Saturnino C. Juan attributed the gains to the centralized digital process. "Through EVOSS, we are able to speed up our processes without compromising thorough review," Juan said in the ERC's statement. "Our goal is to make project approvals more efficient and predictable to support a reliable and adequate power supply."

  ## Significance for energy project developers

  Permit processing speed is a practical constraint on how quickly power generation, transmission, and distribution projects can move from application to construction. Faster and more predictable timelines reduce schedule risk for developers and, in the view of the ERC, support broader power supply adequacy goals. The EVOSS platform's rollout represents a structural change in how Philippine energy regulators receive and process project applications, with the ERC's late-2023 integration still relatively recent. The 646 applications resolved to date provide an early data set on the system's performance at scale.

  菲律賓能源監管委員會（ERC）週三公布，自該機構接入「能源虛擬單一窗口」（EVOSS）這個網路化政府平台以來，三大類能源許可的審查時程已大幅低於法定上限。EVOSS專門受理電力開發專案的申請，涵蓋發電、輸電及配電等項目。

  EVOSS由能源部負責營運，目的是整合並加速電力發電、輸電與配電計畫的申請作業。ERC於2023年底正式接入該系統。

  ## 三類許可審查時程已縮短至法定上限的一半

  依據ERC於2020年頒布的《公民憲章》——即菲律賓政府公開發布的服務標準——三類許可過去的審查時程上限均為270天。ERC表示，透過EVOSS，這三類許可目前的實際處理時間均已縮短至約一半：

  - 點對點（P2P）有限輸電設施的開發與營運授權：平均144.46天，法定標準為270天
  - 受監管事業資本支出（capex）的核准：平均140.53天，同樣對應270天的法定標準
  - 合規證書（CoC）的核發（用以確認設施符合監管要求）：平均29.65天，法定標準為60天

  ## 平台受理申請數量

  截至7月，共有646件申請透過EVOSS提出並完成審結——無論核准或駁回。依許可類型拆分：暫時營運授權331件、CoC申請143件、P2P申請104件、資本支出申請68件。

  ## 監管機關立場

  ERC主席兼執行長Francis Saturnino C. Juan將上述成效歸功於集中化的數位流程。「透過EVOSS，我們得以在不犧牲審查嚴謹度的前提下加快作業速度，」Juan在ERC聲明中表示。「我們的目標是讓專案核准更有效率、更具可預期性，以支持穩定且充足的電力供應。」

  ## 對能源專案開發商的意義

  許可審查速度是電力發電、輸電與配電計畫從申請到動工之間的實質限制因素。更快速、更可預期的時程能降低開發商的工期風險，也符合ERC所揭示的擴大電力供應充裕度目標。EVOSS平台的推行代表菲律賓能源主管機關在受理與處理專案申請方式上的結構性變革，而ERC於2023年底才完成接入，至今仍屬相對初期階段。迄今完成審結的646件申請，提供了該系統在一定規模下實際運作表現的初步數據。
