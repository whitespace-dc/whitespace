body_en: |
  Indonesia signed the founding declaration of the World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO) at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) 2026 in Shanghai on July 17, securing a seat as a founding member of a new intergovernmental body established within the United Nations framework to shape the global governance of artificial intelligence.

  Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto announced the signing at a virtual press conference from Shanghai, saying the founding-member status gives Indonesia "first access to all discussions on the development of AI itself."

  ## What WAICO is and how it works

  WAICO is constituted as an independent intergovernmental international organization with international legal personality, according to the founding document signed in Shanghai. All countries that sign the founding document hold founding-member status. The organization operates within the UN framework, and Airlangga said AI development under WAICO is expected to align with UN principles: inclusive, safe, trustworthy, and human-centered.

  Airlangga attended a high-level meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and UN Secretary-General António Guterres in Shanghai ahead of the founding declaration signing.

  ## Indonesia's stated rationale

  Airlangga said Indonesia's participation follows a directive from President Prabowo Subianto for Indonesia to play an active role in managing global AI governance. He said Indonesia's core concern is that AI development remain inclusive, safe, ethical, and not monopolized by any single power.

  "AI is expected to be a bridge, not a digital divide, but a bridge for togetherness," Airlangga said.

  ## Digital economy projections

  Airlangga linked the WAICO membership to Indonesia's broader digital economy ambitions. Indonesia's digital economy is currently estimated at $130 billion and is projected to reach $366 billion by 2030, he said. He added that implementation of the ASEAN Digital Economic Framework Agreement (DEFA) — a regional digital trade and cooperation pact — could push Indonesia's digital economy value to approximately $600 billion.

  For the ASEAN region as a whole, DEFA is projected to grow the bloc's digital economy from approximately $1 trillion to $2 trillion, according to Airlangga. DEFA is targeted for signing during the Philippines' chairmanship of ASEAN this year.

  ## Significance

  Founding-member status in an intergovernmental organization typically confers influence over institutional rules, governance structures, and agenda-setting that later-joining members do not hold. For Indonesia, participation in WAICO positions the country to represent developing-economy perspectives in global AI policy at an early stage, before governance norms are established. Airlangga described this as an opportunity to bridge the global technology gap and prevent AI from becoming a source of division between technologically advanced and developing nations.

  The next concrete milestone tied to Indonesia's AI governance positioning is the expected signing of ASEAN DEFA under the Philippines' 2026 chairmanship.
body_zh: |
  印尼於7月17日在上海舉行的2026年世界人工智慧大會（WAIC）上，簽署《世界人工智慧合作組織》（WAICO）創始宣言，在這個依聯合國框架設立、旨在塑造全球人工智慧治理格局的新跨政府機構中，取得創始成員席位。

  統籌經濟事務部長艾爾朗卡·哈爾塔托在上海以視訊方式召開記者會，正式宣布完成簽署，並表示創始成員地位讓印尼得以「優先參與一切有關AI發展的討論」。

  ## WAICO的組織性質與運作方式

  依上海所簽創始文件，WAICO設立為具有國際法律人格的獨立跨政府國際組織，所有簽署創始文件的國家均享有創始成員地位。該組織在聯合國框架下運作；艾爾朗卡表示，WAICO框架下的AI發展預期將遵循聯合國原則，即具包容性、安全、可信賴，且以人為本。

  艾爾朗卡在簽署創始宣言前，出席了在上海舉行的高層會議，與中國國家主席習近平及聯合國秘書長安東尼奧·古特瑞斯進行會面。

  ## 印尼加入的政策考量

  艾爾朗卡表示，印尼的加入係依循總統普拉伯沃·蘇比安托的指示，要求印尼積極參與全球AI治理的管理工作。他說，印尼最核心的關切在於確保AI發展具有包容性、安全、合乎倫理，且不被任何單一強權所壟斷。

  「AI應成為橋梁，而非數位鴻溝，要成為凝聚彼此的橋梁，」艾爾朗卡說。

  ## 數位經濟前景預測

  艾爾朗卡將加入WAICO與印尼更宏觀的數位經濟發展目標相連結。他表示，印尼數位經濟規模目前估計達1,300億美元，預計至2030年將成長至3,660億美元。他進一步補充，若能落實《東協數位經濟框架協議》（DEFA）——一份促進區域數位貿易與合作的協定——可望將印尼數位經濟總值進一步推升至約6,000億美元。

  就整個東協地區而言，DEFA預計可將東協數位經濟規模從約1兆美元擴大至2兆美元。DEFA預定在菲律賓擔任今年東協主席國期間完成簽署。

  ## 創始成員地位的戰略意涵

  跨政府組織的創始成員地位，通常使成員對機構規則、治理架構及議題設定享有影響力，而這是後加入的成員所無法獲得的。對印尼而言，加入WAICO讓這個國家得以在治理規範尚未確立之前，便能在全球AI政策舞台上代表發展中經濟體的立場。艾爾朗卡將此形容為彌合全球科技落差、防止AI淪為科技先進國與開發中國家之間分裂根源的重要契機。

  與印尼AI治理布局相關的下一個具體里程碑，是預期在菲律賓2026年東協主席國任期內完成簽署的《東協數位經濟框架協議》（DEFA）。
