body_en: |
  Amazon has settled its six-year civil lawsuit against two former AWS managers, Carl Nelson and Casey Kirschner, who acknowledged accepting kickbacks tied to hundreds of millions of dollars in data center leasing deals, the company confirmed on October 27. The case against their alleged co-conspirator, Denver-based real estate firm Northstar Commercial Partners, remains active and is set for trial on November 2.

  In a joint statement, Nelson and Kirschner said they "regret their actions" and "apologize for failing to meet Amazon's high ethical standards," stating that the payments broke the company's code of conduct and put its reputation at risk. Terms of the settlement were not disclosed.

  ## What the allegations covered

  Amazon filed the original lawsuit in 2020, alleging that Nelson and Kirschner, while employed as managers at AWS, steered nine build-to-suit leasing projects in Virginia to Northstar. Build-to-suit leases are arrangements in which a developer constructs a facility to a tenant's specifications before handing it over under a long-term lease; the nine projects at issue were collectively worth hundreds of millions of dollars, according to court documents.

  In return, Nelson and Kirschner received kickbacks structured as "referral fees" paid through a trust owned by Casey Kirschner's brother, Christian Kirschner. The fees were calculated as a percentage of the fees Northstar earned on its Amazon contracts. Over the course of Northstar's business with Amazon, more than $5 million passed into the trust, court documents show.

  The trust then transferred money to Christian Kirschner and to other entities that funneled funds to Nelson and Casey Kirschner, according to the same documents.

  Amazon alleged that its own project costs were inflated to finance the kickbacks, with Northstar CEO Brian Watson embedding fees into project budgets and overstating Amazon's rent.

  ## A six-year procedural arc

  The case navigated substantial legal reversals before reaching settlement. In 2023, a federal judge ruled that Amazon's internal code of conduct did not constitute an enforceable contract and dismissed most claims against Nelson and Kirschner. Amazon appealed. Last year, the US Court of Appeals for the Fourth Circuit revived several of those dismissed claims and sent the case back for trial — a ruling that preceded the settlement now announced.

  ## What remains and what it signals

  The settlement closes the dispute between Amazon and the two individuals. Northstar and its CEO Brian Watson remain defendants; trial begins November 2.

  The case illustrates the procurement risk embedded in large-scale, build-to-suit data center contracting, where individual managers can exercise substantial discretion over vendor selection and deal terms. Amazon's allegation that inflated rents and project budgets were used to fund the payments points to cost controls and conflict-of-interest oversight as areas of material concern in hyperscale data center development programs.

  Amazon於10月27日確認，已就一起長達六年的民事訴訟與前AWS主管Carl Nelson及Casey Kirschner達成和解。兩人承認曾收受與數億美元資料中心租賃交易掛鉤的回扣。涉嫌共謀的丹佛房地產公司Northstar Commercial Partners案件仍維持開庭，預定11月2日進行審判。

  Nelson與Kirschner在聯合聲明中表示，他們「對自身的行為深感遺憾」，並「為未能符合Amazon嚴格的道德標準致歉」，指出相關款項違反了公司行為準則，並危及公司聲譽。和解條款未予公開。

  ## 指控內容涵蓋範圍

  Amazon於2020年提起原訴，指控Nelson與Kirschner在擔任AWS主管期間，將九件位於維吉尼亞州的客製化建置租賃案引導給Northstar。所謂客製化建置租賃，是指開發商依承租方規格興建設施後，再以長期租約移交的安排；根據法庭文件，這九件案件的合計價值達數億美元。

  作為回報，Nelson與Kirschner以「介紹費」名義收受回扣，款項透過Casey Kirschner之兄Christian Kirschner名下的信託支付，金額按Northstar從亞馬遜合約中所得費用的一定比例計算。在Northstar與Amazon的業務往來期間，流入該信託的款項逾500萬美元，法庭文件如此顯示。

  該信託隨後將款項轉至Christian Kirschner及其他導管實體，再輾轉流向Nelson與Casey Kirschner，相關文件均有記載。

  Amazon指控，公司自身的專案成本遭到哄抬以支應回扣——Northstar執行長Brian Watson將費用埋入專案預算，並虛報Amazon應支付的租金。

  ## 長達六年的訴訟歷程

  本案在達成和解前，歷經多次重大法律逆轉。2023年，一名聯邦法官裁定Amazon內部行為準則不構成具有法律效力的合約，駁回針對Nelson與Kirschner的大部分訴請。Amazon隨即提起上訴。去年，美國聯邦第四巡迴上訴法院撤銷部分駁回裁定，將多項訴請發回審理——此一裁決為此次和解奠定了基礎。

  ## 後續動向與案件意涵

  和解使Amazon與兩名當事人之間的爭議畫下句點。Northstar及其執行長Brian Watson仍列為被告，審判將於11月2日展開。

  本案凸顯了大規模客製化建置資料中心採購合約所潛藏的採購風險——個別主管在廠商遴選與交易條件方面握有相當大的自由裁量空間。Amazon指控租金與專案預算遭虛報以挹注回扣款項，這進一步凸顯出在超大規模資料中心開發計畫中，成本管控與利益衝突監督機制均屬重大關切事項。
