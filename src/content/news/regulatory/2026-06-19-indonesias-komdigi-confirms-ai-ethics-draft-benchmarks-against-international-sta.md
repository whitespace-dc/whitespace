body_en: |
  Indonesia's Ministry of Communication and Digital (Komdigi) has confirmed that a draft presidential regulation on artificial intelligence ethics is benchmarked against international standards, responding to formal inquiries from US business associations and technology providers that questioned whether the framework aligns with global norms.

  ## US industry raises formal questions

  The US-ASEAN Business Council, the US Chamber, and US technology providers submitted formal consultations to Komdigi asking about the direction of Indonesia's digital innovation governance policy. Aju Widyasari, Director of Artificial Intelligence and New Technology Ecosystem (AIKITA), Komdigi's directorate for AI policy, disclosed the exchanges at the Indonesia Ethical AI Summit in Jakarta on June 18, 2026.

  The companies sought detailed explanations of implementation mechanisms, oversight instruments, and the reference indicators guiding national regulation. A central concern was synchronization between the domestic draft and global standards.

  ## International frameworks cited

  The draft Presidential Regulation, known in Indonesian as a Peraturan Presiden or Perpres, references ASEAN AI ethics guidelines, UNESCO frameworks, IEEE standards, and agreements from other multilateral forums, Aju said.

  "They needed clarity on whether the formulation of these ethics had already benchmarked against international standards. Of course it has, because we are quite active in those international forums," Aju said, as quoted by Bisnis.com.

  Aju described the adoption of those international references as critical to maintaining compatibility between Indonesia's domestic technology industry and the global ecosystem.

  ## Incident reporting scope disputed

  A contested point in the consultations was the scope of mandatory incident reporting to regulators. US industry participants proposed limiting reporting obligations to high-risk incidents only. Komdigi rejected that threshold, choosing to require operators to report all AI operational incidents regardless of assessed risk level.

  Aju framed the decision as appropriate for Indonesia's early stage of AI governance development. "According to us, all of them, because we are still in the learning stage. Everything is reported, because even small incidents, if left unchecked, will become large ones," she said.

  The ministry said every incident record, however minor, would serve as evaluation material to strengthen future oversight systems. Komdigi said the US side ultimately accepted the legal arguments and substance contained in the draft Perpres, according to the government's account.

  ## Draft revised to balance innovation and national interests

  The formal consultations also covered the division of roles among digital ecosystem actors, oversight mechanisms, and reporting provisions beyond the standardization questions, according to Bisnis.com.

  Communications and Digital Minister Meutya Hafid said separately that inputs from global business actors had been accommodated through balanced adjustments to the policy draft. Those revisions aim to find an optimal balance between space for technological innovation and protection of national interests, the minister said.

  The government's decision to retain all-incident reporting requirements, rather than accept the high-risk-only threshold proposed by US industry, was the principal documented point of regulatory difference to emerge from the consultations. Aju cited Indonesia's position as still building AI governance experience as the rationale, with each incident record serving as data for improving the oversight system over time.

  ## 美國業界提出正式質詢

  美國—東協商業理事會、美國商會及多家美國科技業者，就印尼數位創新治理政策的走向，向Komdigi提出正式諮詢，詢問該框架是否與全球規範接軌。Komdigi人工智慧政策局——人工智慧暨新科技生態系局（AIKITA）局長Aju Widyasari，於2026年6月18日在雅加達舉辦的「印尼AI倫理高峰會」上公開披露了上述往來內容。

  相關企業要求詳細說明實施機制、監督工具，以及指導國家法規制定的參考指標，核心關切在於國內草案與全球標準之間的同步程度。

  ## 草案援引之國際框架

  Aju表示，這份被稱為「Perpres」（Peraturan Presiden，總統令）的人工智慧倫理法規草案，參照了東協人工智慧倫理準則、聯合國教科文組織相關框架、IEEE標準，以及其他多邊論壇的協議。

  「他們需要確認，這些倫理條文的制定是否已對標國際標準。當然有，因為我們在這些國際論壇中相當活躍，」Aju接受《Bisnis.com》引述時如此表示。

  Aju指出，援引上述國際參考依據，對於維持印尼國內科技產業與全球生態系的相容性至關重要。

  ## 事故通報範圍引發爭議

  此次諮詢中，強制通報主管機關的事故範圍成為一大爭議焦點。美國業界參與者提議，僅對高風險事故設定通報義務；Komdigi否決了這項門檻設定，選擇要求業者無論風險等級高低，均須通報所有AI營運事故。

  Aju將這項決定定位為符合印尼AI治理發展初期階段的適當做法。「依我們的判斷，全部都要通報，因為我們仍處於學習階段。所有事故都必須呈報，因為即使是小事故，若放任不管，終將演變為大問題，」她說。

  通訊數位部表示，每一筆事故紀錄，無論多麼細微，都將作為強化未來監督體系的評估材料。據印尼政府方面的說法，美方最終接受了Perpres草案中的法律論據與實質內容。

  ## 草案修訂以平衡創新與國家利益

  根據《Bisnis.com》的報導，此次正式諮詢還涵蓋數位生態系各方角色的職責劃分、監督機制，以及標準化問題以外的通報規定。

  通訊暨數位部長Meutya Hafid另行表示，全球商業行為者的意見已透過對政策草案的均衡調整而獲得吸納。這些修訂旨在於科技創新空間與保護國家利益之間，尋求最佳平衡點。

  政府堅持保留「所有事故均須通報」的要求，而非接受美國業界所提「僅限高風險事件」的門檻，成為此次諮詢中浮現的主要監管分歧點。Aju以印尼仍在累積AI治理經驗為由，強調每一筆事故紀錄都是隨時間改善監督體系的寶貴數據。
