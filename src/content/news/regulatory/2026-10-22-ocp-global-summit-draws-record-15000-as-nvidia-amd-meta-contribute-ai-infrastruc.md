body_en: |
  The 2026 Open Compute Project (OCP) Global Summit drew 15,000 attendees to San Jose, California, October 12 through 15, a 38 percent increase over the 10,835 who attended in 2025 and the largest gathering in the consortium's 15-year history. The sold-out event produced contributions from NVIDIA, AMD, Meta, Microsoft, and Intel covering rack designs, networking blueprints, power architecture, and security specifications, and research firm IDC raised its forecast for OCP-aligned infrastructure spending to $800 billion annually by 2029.

  ## IDC forecast and participation value

  The most significant market-scale data point from the event came from an IDC study released at the summit, which projected that annual spending on OCP-influenced infrastructure will reach $800 billion by 2029, approximately 2.7 times IDC's previous forecast for the same year. IDC further projected the figure would rise to $892 billion in 2030, representing approximately 51 percent of spending across the infrastructure categories measured. These are forecasts, not realized spending figures.

  OCP also released a separate study quantifying the value of consortium membership, reporting approximately 5 percent lower average total cost of ownership, deployment timelines up to 15 percent faster, and qualification periods up to 30 percent shorter for participating organizations. Some hyperscale operators surveyed reported return on investment of 150 times their participation costs; OCP noted that figure should not be read as a typical outcome.

  ## NVIDIA and AMD contribute rack designs

  NVIDIA contributed its Vera Rubin rack and compute-tray designs to OCP through its MGX architecture, a framework intended to give hardware manufacturers a common foundation for next-generation AI server deployments. The announcements also included open-source DSX components, networking collaborations, and power infrastructure work, according to OCP.

  AMD introduced Helion, an open rack blueprint for agentic AI workloads, in which AI models autonomously execute tasks. Helion supports CPU (central processing unit) configurations for agent execution as well as configurations combining CPUs with GPU (graphics processing unit) inference accelerators. AMD said it plans to contribute the Helion specification to OCP, with partner configurations planned for November 2026.

  ## Meta opens AI networking portfolio

  Meta released what OCP described as a substantial new open AI networking portfolio at the summit, comprising five elements. IcePack is Meta's first liquid-cooled networking rack. IceCube is a 102.4-terabit-per-second (Tbps) switch platform, with Tbps measuring aggregate data-transfer capacity across all ports. Meta also released specifications for Flattened Planar Fabric, a network topology design; MetaRoCE, a network protocol for AI cluster communication; and the Open Switch Platform Architecture, known as OSPA. Meta announced OSPA on October 8, the week before the summit opened. Meta said the portfolio provides shared building blocks for scaling AI networks across hardware and software.

  ## Megawatt racks and 800V DC power

  OCP reported progress on facility designs for megawatt-class racks using 800-volt direct current (DC) power delivery, an area the consortium said had advanced significantly since a related initiative launched in 2025. OCP reported advances in power conversion, grid interaction, energy storage, and cooling for high-density deployments, which the consortium described as addressing facility constraints that increasingly determine how quickly AI capacity can be deployed. Microsoft contributed its Mt. Diablo power architecture and its Mt. Atlas heat exchanger reference architecture to the effort.

  ## Open Ethernet networking

  Microsoft highlighted Multipath Reliable Connection, a protocol designed to support networks connecting more than 100,000 GPUs across two network tiers. Microsoft also reported that the Ethernet for Scale-Up Networking community had released specification version 1.0. OCP described both as open Ethernet approaches intended as alternatives for connecting large AI accelerator clusters.

  ## Hardware security

  Microsoft, Marvell, and Oracle announced a joint effort to establish open specifications for server-integrated hardware security modules (HSMs), components that perform cryptographic operations. Microsoft introduced an associated open-source initiative called Tessera Integrated HSM. The partners said the effort is intended to bring protected cryptographic operations closer to workloads through a shared architecture. The specification remained under development at the time of the announcement.

  ## Intel

  Intel disclosed additional details about its GPU D-series, the inference accelerator formerly known under the development name Crescent Island, alongside new rack and software developments. OCP described the announcements as providing additional options for heterogeneous infrastructure combining multiple accelerator types for inference and agentic AI workloads.

  ## Research awards

  The summit's Future Technologies Symposium recognized three research contributions. The University of Cambridge received the Best FTS Paper award and a $10,000 prize for work on copper sintering ball-grid-array (BGA) interconnects for mitigating electromigration in high-current AI power delivery. The University of Florida and University of Maryland at College Park jointly won the Best Sustainability Paper for research on direct waste heat recovery using membrane-assisted heat sink technology in AI data centers. Shenzhen Litratech Co. Ltd. and Pixora LLC won the Best AI/HPC Paper for work on wavelength-granular optical circuit switching (OCS) using solid-state optical phased arrays (OPA) for AI interconnect fabrics.

  ## OCP Nexus knowledge assistant

  OCP launched Nexus, an AI knowledge assistant providing conversational access to the consortium's specifications, reference designs, white papers, and summit materials, with citations to supporting source documents. OCP described the tool as designed to make its accumulated technical library easier to discover and apply.

  ## Attendance and program scale

  Attendees at the 2026 summit represented more than 3,400 organizations from 64 countries, according to OCP. More than half, 53 percent, were attending the Global Summit for the first time, and 141 came from academic institutions. By region, North America accounted for 73 percent of attendees, Asia Pacific for 20 percent, Europe, the Middle East and Africa for 6 percent, and the Caribbean and Latin America for less than 1 percent. Within the United States, 55.5 percent of domestic attendees were based in California; Texas represented the next largest state contingent at 9.5 percent.

  The program comprised 22 content tracks, 578 sessions, and 948 speakers. One hundred seventy-eight sponsors occupied 158 booths across two exhibit halls, and more than 200 media and analyst representatives from over 115 organizations attended, including Reuters, Bloomberg, Gartner, and IDC.

  ## Industry significance

  The range of contributions at the 2026 summit, spanning racks, power delivery, networking, cooling, and security, reflects what OCP characterized as a shift toward open, complete AI infrastructure designed to work as an integrated whole. OCP said its model of collaboration to drive openness, efficiency, sustainability, scalability, and impact will be central to meeting the demands of massively parallel and distributed AI computation at scales that, the consortium said, have never before been attempted. The IDC forecast, if realized, would place OCP-influenced procurement at roughly half of total infrastructure spending in the measured categories by 2030.

  AMD said partner configurations for the Helion rack specification are planned for November 2026; the hardware security specification from Microsoft, Marvell, and Oracle remained under development at the time of the summit.
body_zh: |
  2026年開放運算計畫（OCP）全球峰會於10月12日至15日在加州聖荷西登場，吸引一萬五千名與會者，較2025年出席的一萬零八百三十五人成長38%，創下這個聯盟15年歷史以來規模最大的年度盛會。這場一票難求的活動，匯聚了輝達（NVIDIA）、AMD、Meta、微軟（Microsoft）及英特爾（Intel）的共同貢獻，涵蓋機架設計、網路藍圖、電源架構與安全規格，研究機構IDC亦同步上調OCP相關基礎架構支出預測至2029年每年8,000億美元。

  ## IDC預測與參與效益

  本次峰會最受矚目的市場數據，來自IDC在會中發布的研究報告。該報告預測，受OCP影響的基礎架構年度支出將於2029年達到8,000億美元，約為IDC先前針對同年所作預測的2.7倍。IDC並進一步預測，此數字將於2030年增至8,920億美元，約佔所測量基礎架構類別總支出的51%。以上均為預測數字，並非已實現的實際支出。

  OCP另發布一份獨立研究，量化聯盟成員資格所帶來的效益，指出參與組織的平均整體擁有成本（TCO）約降低5%、部署時程最多縮短15%、認證週期最多縮短30%。部分接受調查的超大規模業者表示，投資報酬率達參與成本的150倍；OCP特別說明，該數字不應被視為典型結果。

  ## 輝達與AMD貢獻機架設計

  輝達透過其MGX架構，將Vera Rubin機架及運算托盤設計貢獻給OCP。MGX是一套旨在為硬體製造商提供共通基礎的框架，供下一代AI伺服器部署使用。相關公告還涵蓋開源DSX元件、網路合作項目及電源基礎架構工作。

  AMD推出Helion，這是一套專為代理式AI（agentic AI）工作負載設計的開放機架藍圖——在此類工作負載中，AI模型能自主執行任務。Helion支援純CPU（中央處理器）的代理執行配置，以及CPU搭配GPU（圖形處理器）推論加速器的混合配置。AMD表示計畫將Helion規格貢獻給OCP，合作夥伴配置預計於2026年11月推出。

  ## Meta開放AI網路產品組合

  Meta在峰會上發布了OCP所形容為大規模的全新開放AI網路產品組合，共包含五項元素。IcePack是Meta首款液冷網路機架；IceCube是一款每秒傳輸速率達102.4兆位元（Tbps）的交換器平台，Tbps衡量所有連接埠的整體資料傳輸能力。Meta還發布了「扁平化平面網路拓撲」（Flattened Planar Fabric）規格、專為AI叢集通訊設計的網路協定MetaRoCE，以及開放交換器平台架構（Open Switch Platform Architecture，OSPA）。OSPA於峰會開幕前一週的10月8日正式對外公布。Meta表示，這套產品組合為跨硬體與軟體擴展AI網路提供了共用基礎模組。

  ## 百萬瓦機架與800V直流電源

  OCP報告了採用800伏特直流（DC）電源輸送技術的百萬瓦（megawatt）級機架設施設計進展，並表示自2025年相關計畫啟動以來，此領域已取得顯著進步。OCP在電源轉換、電網互動、儲能及高密度部署冷卻方面均有所突破，並指出這些進展正在解決日益制約AI算力部署速度的設施瓶頸。微軟為此貢獻了Mt. Diablo電源架構及Mt. Atlas熱交換器參考架構。

  ## 開放乙太網路

  微軟重點介紹了Multipath Reliable Connection，這是一款旨在支援跨兩層網路、連接超過十萬顆GPU的通訊協定。微軟同時宣布，Ethernet for Scale-Up Networking社群已發布1.0版規格。OCP將上述兩者定位為連接大型AI加速器叢集的開放乙太網路替代方案。

  ## 硬體安全

  微軟、Marvell與甲骨文（Oracle）宣布合作，共同為伺服器整合式硬體安全模組（HSM）制定開放規格——HSM是執行密碼學運算的硬體元件。微軟同時推出相關開源計畫Tessera Integrated HSM。各方表示，此計畫旨在透過共用架構，讓受保護的密碼學運算更貼近應用工作負載。截至公告時，該規格仍在開發中。

  ## 英特爾

  英特爾披露了GPU D系列的更多細節，即前開發代號為「Crescent Island」的推論加速器，並同步公布新的機架與軟體發展動態。OCP表示，相關公告為結合多種加速器類型的異質基礎架構提供了更多選擇，適用於推論及代理式AI工作負載。

  ## 研究獎項

  峰會「未來技術研討會」（Future Technologies Symposium）共表彰三項研究貢獻。英國劍橋大學榮獲「最佳FTS論文獎」及1萬美元獎金，獲獎研究聚焦於採用銅燒結球格陣列（BGA）互連技術，以緩解高電流AI電源輸送中的電遷移問題。美國佛羅里達大學與馬里蘭大學帕克分校聯合獲得「最佳永續論文獎」，研究主題為AI資料中心採用薄膜輔助散熱片技術進行廢熱直接回收。深圳利創科技有限公司與Pixora LLC則榮獲「最佳AI／HPC論文獎」，研究主題為利用固態光學相控陣（OPA）實現波長粒度光學電路交換（OCS），應用於AI互連架構。

  ## OCP Nexus知識助理

  OCP推出Nexus，這是一款AI知識助理，可讓使用者以對話方式存取聯盟的規格文件、參考設計、白皮書及峰會資料，並附有原始來源文件的引用。OCP表示，此工具旨在讓其累積的技術文獻庫更易於被發掘與應用。

  ## 出席人數與議程規模

  根據OCP資料，2026年峰會的與會代表來自64個國家、逾3,400個組織。超過半數（53%）為首次出席全球峰會，另有141名代表來自學術機構。就地區分布而言，北美佔73%、亞太地區佔20%、歐洲、中東及非洲佔6%、加勒比海及拉丁美洲不足1%。美國境內方面，55.5%的國內出席者來自加州，德州則以9.5%位居次席。

  大會共設22個議題軌道、578場議程及948位講者。178家贊助商分佈於兩個展覽廳的158個攤位，逾200名來自115個以上組織的媒體與分析師代表出席，包括路透社（Reuters）、彭博社（Bloomberg）、顧能（Gartner）及IDC。

  ## 產業意義

  2026年峰會的貢獻涵蓋機架、電源輸送、網路、冷卻與安全等多個面向，OCP表示，這反映出業界正朝向開放、完整且能作為整合整體運作的AI基礎架構轉型。OCP表示，其透過協作推動開放性、效率、永續性、可擴展性與影響力的模式，將是應對大規模平行與分散式AI運算需求的核心——OCP指出，此類運算規模前所未有。若IDC預測成真，OCP影響的採購額將於2030年約佔所測量類別基礎架構總支出的一半。

  AMD表示，Helion機架規格的合作夥伴配置預計於2026年11月推出；微軟、Marvell與甲骨文聯合提出的硬體安全規格截至峰會召開時仍在開發中。
