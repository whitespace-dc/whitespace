body_en: |
  The European Heat Pump Association (EHPA) is pressing for heat pumps to receive a dedicated European Partnership — a co-funded, multi-year research track — within Horizon Europe, the EU's €175 billion research and innovation framework programme scheduled to run from 2028 to 2034.

  Also referred to as FP10, Horizon Europe is the EU's tenth framework programme for research and innovation and is designed to succeed the current cycle, which runs from 2021 to 2027. Legislative negotiations between EU member states and the European Parliament are scheduled to run through 2026 and 2027, with the full programme targeted to launch in January 2028.

  ## What EHPA is asking for

  The association is calling on the European Commission to recognise heat pumps as a dedicated thematic priority covering the full technology and application spectrum. It wants heat pumps named as a priority area in the programme and given their own European Partnership — an instrument within the programme through which the EU and industry, research organisations, and national funders commit to a shared research agenda and co-fund it over several years.

  The EHPA argues that such a designation would deliver long-term, predictable funding for work including better efficiency, new refrigerants, lower costs, grid integration, and high-temperature industrial applications. It would also, the association said, signal that the EU treats heat pumps as a strategic technology.

  The proposed partnership would structure its research and innovation priorities around four areas: cost competitiveness, environmental contribution, energy system integration, and market uptake.

  ## Broader policy framework sought

  Beyond the dedicated partnership, the EHPA proposes that heat pump R&I be embedded in a wider set of EU policy mechanisms. These include a Strategic Energy Technology (SET) plan implementation working group focused on heat pumps, a strong focus on heat pumps within the inter-working group (IWG) on buildings and industry, a continued heat pump accelerator platform, and a net-zero industry academy for heat pumps.

  The association also calls for strong links between any heat pump R&I priority and the European Competitiveness Fund, as well as deployment-focused funding instruments such as the Industrial Decarbonisation Bank.

  ## Significance and next steps

  If adopted, a dedicated European Partnership would give the heat pump sector a guaranteed seat in the EU's primary research funding architecture for at least six years, providing the kind of policy visibility and financial predictability that industry groups often cite as necessary to attract private co-investment in long-cycle hardware development.

  The outcome will depend on how the Commission and member states shape programme priorities during negotiations running through 2027. The full Horizon Europe FP10 programme is targeted to launch in January 2028.

  ## 歐洲熱泵協會籲「地平線歐洲」設立專屬夥伴關係

  歐洲熱泵協會（EHPA）正積極推動，要求熱泵在歐盟預算達1,750億歐元的「地平線歐洲」研究與創新框架計畫中，取得專屬「歐洲夥伴關係」地位——即一種由歐盟與產業界、研究機構及各國資助方共同出資、執行期橫跨數年的研究軌道。此一框架計畫預計於2028年至2034年間運作。

  「地平線歐洲」又稱第十框架計畫（FP10），是歐盟第十期研究與創新框架計畫，旨在接續現行的2021至2027年週期。歐盟各成員國與歐洲議會之間的立法協商預計將在2026年至2027年間進行，整個計畫的目標啟動時間為2028年1月。

  ## EHPA的訴求

  協會呼籲歐盟執委會將熱泵認定為一項專屬主題優先領域，涵蓋完整的技術類型與應用範疇。EHPA希望熱泵能被明確列為優先領域，並在計畫架構內獲得專屬的「歐洲夥伴關係」——這是框架計畫中的一種機制，透過此機制，歐盟與產業界、研究機構及各國資助方共同確立研究議程，並聯合出資推動多年期研究。

  EHPA主張，一旦取得此一地位，將為相關研究工作帶來長期且可預期的資金挹注，涵蓋範圍包括效率提升、新型冷媒開發、降低成本、電網整合，以及高溫工業應用等領域。協會並指出，此舉也將傳遞出一個明確訊號：歐盟將熱泵視為具戰略意義的技術。

  這項擬議中的夥伴關係，將圍繞四大面向來建構其研究與創新優先方向：成本競爭力、環境貢獻、能源系統整合，以及市場普及。

  ## 更廣泛的政策框架

  除了設立專屬夥伴關係之外，EHPA還建議將熱泵的研究與創新工作納入更廣泛的歐盟政策機制。這些機制包括：以熱泵為焦點的「策略能源技術計畫」（SET Plan）執行工作小組、在建築與工業跨工作小組（IWG）中強化熱泵的相關工作、持續推動熱泵加速平台，以及建立熱泵領域的「淨零工業學院」。

  協會同時呼籲，任何熱泵研發優先政策都應與「歐洲競爭力基金」建立緊密連結，並與以推廣部署為導向的資金工具掛鉤，例如「工業去碳銀行」。

  ## 重要性與後續進程

  若此一提案獲得採納，專屬「歐洲夥伴關係」將使熱泵產業在歐盟主要研究資金架構中至少享有六年的保障席次，提供業界所需的政策能見度與財務可預期性——這正是業界團體經常援引、用以吸引私人資金共同投入長週期硬體開發的關鍵條件。

  最終結果將取決於執委會與各成員國如何在2027年前的協商過程中形塑計畫優先方向。「地平線歐洲」第十框架計畫的完整版本，目標啟動時間為2028年1月。
