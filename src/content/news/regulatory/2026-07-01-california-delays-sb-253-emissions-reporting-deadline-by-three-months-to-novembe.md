body_en: |
  The California Air Resources Board (CARB) has extended the compliance deadline for the state's corporate greenhouse gas disclosure law by three months, moving the reporting date from August 10 to November 10, 2026, according to a June 24 agency bulletin.

  The delay applies to Senate Bill 253, the Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act, which requires companies doing business in California with more than $1 billion in annual revenue to disclose their scope 1 and scope 2 greenhouse gas emissions. Scope 1 refers to direct emissions from operations a company controls; scope 2 covers indirect emissions from purchased electricity and heat.

  CARB said it also intends to propose "limited changes" to the regulation to "clarify certain requirements" for reporting entities. Those proposed amendments will be opened for a 15-day public comment period once released.

  ## Why the deadline was moved

  The agency adopted the formal regulation for SB 253 in February 2026, but the rule has not yet received approval from California's Office of Administrative Law (OAL), which must sign off before the regulation goes into effect. CARB said that proposing amendments to the regulation could delay the OAL approval timeline, and that the three-month deferral is designed to ensure that reporting entities have "additional clarity following approval of the final regulation before reporting is due."

  CARB has previously issued enforcement notices urging covered companies to make "good faith efforts" to comply with the disclosure requirements, but the agency said it does not plan to take enforcement action during the first year.

  ## The broader disclosure framework

  California's corporate climate disclosure laws were enacted in 2023 and cover two statutes. SB 253 governs greenhouse gas emissions reporting; SB 261, the Climate-Related Financial Risk Act, requires certain entities to make climate-related financial disclosures. CARB is responsible for implementing both laws.

  SB 253's scope expands over time. While companies subject to the law are initially required to report only scope 1 and scope 2 emissions, scope 3 reporting — covering indirect emissions across a company's full value chain — is required beginning in 2027.

  ## Legal challenge halts companion law

  SB 261 is currently on hold. The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit has issued a preliminary injunction blocking its implementation following a legal challenge by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce and other business groups. The same groups challenged SB 253, but the federal court allowed that law's implementation to continue.

  Despite the injunction, more than 170 companies had voluntarily submitted climate-related financial risk reports to the public docket as of June 26, according to ESG Dive's review of the database. CARB has made compliance with SB 261 voluntary while the court case proceeds.

  ## What comes next

  The November 10 reporting deadline for scope 1 and scope 2 emissions under SB 253 now stands as the next major compliance milestone for covered companies. CARB's forthcoming proposed amendments and the OAL's final approval of the regulation will determine the precise requirements companies face before that date.

  ## 加州SB 253溫室氣體申報期限延後三個月至11月

  根據加州空氣資源委員會（CARB）6月24日發布的公告，該機構已將加州企業溫室氣體資訊揭露法的申報截止日期延後三個月，從2026年8月10日順延至11月10日。

  此次延期適用於《第253號參議院法案》（SB 253），即《氣候企業資料問責法》。該法要求在加州從事業務、年營收超過10億美元的企業，必須揭露其第一類與第二類溫室氣體排放量。第一類排放是指企業直接控制的營運所產生的排放；第二類排放則涵蓋購買電力與熱能所產生的間接排放。

  CARB表示，該機構亦打算就相關法規提出「有限度的修正」，以「釐清申報主體的特定申報要求」。上述修正草案一經公布，將開啟為期15天的公眾意見徵詢期。

  ## 期限延後的原因

  CARB於2026年2月正式通過SB 253的相關法規，然而該法規迄今尚未獲得加州行政法辦公室（OAL）的核准。在法規正式生效之前，必須取得OAL的批准。CARB指出，提出法規修正可能拖延OAL的核准時程，而此次三個月的延期，旨在確保申報主體能在最終法規核准後，在申報截止日前擁有「更充分的資訊與明確指引」。

  CARB此前曾多次發出執法通知，敦促受規範企業以「善意誠信」的態度遵循揭露要求，但該機構表示，在法規實施第一年內，不計畫採取執法行動。

  ## 更廣泛的揭露法規架構

  加州的企業氣候資訊揭露法規於2023年完成立法，涵蓋兩部法律。SB 253規範溫室氣體排放資訊的申報；《第261號參議院法案》（SB 261）即《氣候相關財務風險法》，則要求特定實體揭露氣候相關的財務風險資訊。CARB負責執行這兩部法律。

  SB 253的申報範圍將逐步擴大。受規範企業目前僅須申報第一類與第二類排放量，但自2027年起，將須進一步申報第三類排放——即涵蓋企業整體價值鏈的間接排放。

  ## 配套法律因法律挑戰而暫停實施

  SB 261目前處於停擺狀態。美國聯邦第九巡迴上訴法院已發布初步禁令，在美國商會及其他商業團體提出法律挑戰後，暫緩其施行。這些團體同樣對SB 253提出挑戰，但聯邦法院允許該法繼續實施。

  儘管禁令已發布，據ESG Dive對相關資料庫的檢視，截至6月26日，已有逾170家企業自願向公眾檔案提交了氣候相關財務風險報告。在訴訟程序進行期間，CARB已將SB 261的遵循改為自願性質。

  ## 後續發展

  SB 253項下第一類與第二類排放的申報截止日期——11月10日——如今成為受規範企業下一個重要的合規里程碑。CARB即將提出的修正草案，以及OAL對相關法規的最終核准結果，將決定企業在此日期前所須面對的確切申報要求。
