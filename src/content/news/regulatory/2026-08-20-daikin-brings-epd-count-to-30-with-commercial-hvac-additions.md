body_en: |
  Daikin has extended its portfolio of Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) to cover commercial air conditioning, chillers, and commercial heat pump ranges, bringing its total catalogue to 30 declarations now accessible to design teams, the company said.

  The expansion follows the earlier release of EPDs for Daikin's residential heat pump and air conditioning lines. Together, the two tranches mean the company's commercial and residential HVAC ranges are now backed by third-party verified lifecycle carbon data.

  ## What an EPD contains

  An EPD is a standardised document that details verified environmental data across a product's full lifecycle — from manufacturing through construction-phase installation to end of life, including the potential for reuse, recovery, or recycling. Third-party verification means the figures can be entered directly into building assessments without further validation by the design team.

  Embodied carbon — the carbon emitted in manufacturing, transporting, and installing building products, as distinct from the carbon generated by operating a building once in use — is a critical but often overlooked contributor to the built environment's carbon footprint, Daikin said. EPDs provide the granular, product-level data that whole-life carbon assessments require to quantify that contribution accurately.

  ## Regulatory and certification applications

  Design teams can use the data to meet legislative requirements and pursue certification under several schemes. These include BREEAM, the widely used UK building sustainability rating system; NABERS, an energy and sustainability rating framework; and the UK Net Zero Carbon Building Standard (UKNZCBS), an emerging national framework for net-zero-aligned building performance.

  The declarations are also formatted for use in BIM (Building Information Modelling) workflows, allowing carbon data to be integrated into design software directly.

  All 30 EPDs are available through Daikin's website, ready for use in whole-life carbon assessments and BREEAM submissions.
body_zh: |
  大金（Daikin）宣布，其環境產品聲明（EPD）組合已延伸至商用空調、冷水機組及商用熱泵等產品線。公司表示，目前設計團隊可取用的聲明文件總數已達 30 份。

  此次擴充係接續先前發布的大金住宅熱泵與住宅空調產品線 EPD。兩批次合計，代表大金旗下商用與住宅暖通空調（HVAC）產品線，現已全數取得第三方驗證的生命週期碳排放數據背書。

  ## EPD 的內容

  EPD 是一份標準化文件，詳載經過驗證的環境數據，涵蓋產品完整生命週期——從製造、施工階段安裝，直至產品壽命終止，包括再利用、回收或循環再生的潛力。由於數據已通過第三方驗證，設計團隊可直接將相關數字納入建築評估作業，無需另行核實。

  大金指出，隱含碳——即製造、運輸及安裝建築產品過程中所排放的碳，有別於建築啟用後營運所產生的碳——是建築環境碳足跡中一項關鍵卻常遭忽略的貢獻來源。EPD 提供了產品層級的細粒度數據，使全生命週期碳評估得以精確量化此一貢獻。

  ## 法規與認證應用

  設計團隊可運用這些數據以符合法規要求，並申請多項認證計畫，包括：BREEAM（英國廣泛採用的建築永續評等系統）、NABERS（能源與永續評等框架），以及英國淨零碳建築標準（UKNZCBS，英國新興的淨零建築性能國家框架）。

  此外，這些聲明文件亦已格式化以相容於 BIM（建築資訊模型）工作流程，讓碳排放數據得以直接整合至設計軟體。

  全部 30 份 EPD 均可透過大金官方網站取得，可直接用於全生命週期碳評估及 BREEAM 申報作業。
