body_en: |
  SpaceXAI is facing a potential $6.1 million fine from Mississippi environmental regulators after starting construction on three data centers without first obtaining required stormwater permits, according to a Securities and Exchange Commission filing disclosed October 23.

  The Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ), the state's environmental regulator, issued the notice to MZX Tech LLC, a SpaceXAI subsidiary. MDEQ alleges that "disturbing construction activities at sites occurred prior to coverage under Mississippi's Large Construction Stormwater General Permit," the filing states. The agency is seeking civil penalties of up to $25,000 per day, with preliminary claimed exposure totaling approximately $6.1 million across three sites. The company said it is cooperating with MDEQ and in discussions to resolve the matters through a single agreed order.

  ## The Mississippi data center cluster

  The three sites are part of a cluster SpaceXAI, now operating under the brand SpaceXSI, has built near Memphis, Tennessee, though the facilities lie across the state line in Mississippi. Two of the centers — named Macrohard and Macroharder — are operational; a third, Minihard, remains under construction. The company also operates a fourth site within Memphis proper.

  ## NAACP lawsuit survives DOJ dismissal bid

  SpaceXAI faces a separate and concurrent legal challenge at the same complex. The National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP) has sued the company, alleging it violated federal law and state regulations by constructing and operating a gas-fired power plant in Southaven, Mississippi, without first obtaining the required permits. The Department of Justice (DOJ) sought to block that lawsuit, but US District Judge Debra Brown rejected the effort this week.

  The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), acting in its role as primary enforcer of the Clean Air Act — the federal air quality statute underlying the NAACP's allegations — had moved to shut the case down. Judge Brown ruled that the EPA's enforcement position does not grant the federal government authority to unilaterally dismiss private litigation. "The EPA's role as a primary enforcer of the Clean Air Act does not mean that the federal government retains complete control over all Clean Air Act litigation," Brown wrote.

  The DOJ had argued that the remedies the NAACP is seeking — civil penalties and an injunction to halt turbine operations — would threaten national security and economic interests. The precise reasoning for that argument was not made clear in the proceeding, according to Data Center Dynamics, which first reported the fine notice. SpaceXAI CEO Elon Musk has donated approximately $445 million to support Republican party candidates in the upcoming midterm elections, the publication reported.

  "The DOJ tried to bypass the District Court with their efforts to dismiss our case, and it did not work in their favor," said Abre' Conner, NAACP Director of Environmental and Climate Justice. "Our case has always been about holding polluters accountable and making sure that entities follow the law. The court made clear today that the law applies to everyone, including the federal government." Data Center Dynamics said it had contacted SpaceXAI for comment.

  ## Financial backdrop

  The regulatory and legal actions arrive as SpaceXAI's data center operations are driving substantial growth for the parent company. SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.58 billion for the three months ending September 30, up 151 percent year-on-year and above analysts' estimates. AI revenue contributed $7.6 billion of that total.

  SpaceXAI initially built its Mississippi-area data centers to train and run Grok, its artificial intelligence chatbot, but has since signed cloud GPU rental agreements with Anthropic and Google. SpaceXAI capital expenditure accounted for $16.4 billion of SpaceX's total $19.6 billion capex outlay in the third quarter. The company said spending will remain at similar levels going forward.

  "We are in an inflection moment for SpaceX across launch, connectivity and superintelligence," Musk told investors on the earnings call.

  ## Significance and next steps

  The concurrent actions represent the most direct regulatory pressure yet on SpaceXAI's Memphis-area data center hub, with stormwater permit violations and Clean Air Act allegations now proceeding on separate tracks. The MDEQ stormwater matter is subject to ongoing settlement negotiations aimed at a single consolidated order covering all three Mississippi sites. The NAACP's Clean Air Act case over the Southaven gas turbine plant will now proceed in federal district court following Judge Brown's ruling. SpaceX said capital expenditure on the AI business is expected to remain near third-quarter levels in coming quarters.

  SpaceXAI 因未取得必要的暴雨逕流許可證便擅自動工興建三座資料中心，根據10月23日揭露的一份美國證券交易委員會（SEC）申報文件，該公司面臨密西西比州環境主管機關最高610萬美元的潛在罰款。

  密西西比州環境品質局（MDEQ）已向SpaceXAI子公司MZX Tech LLC發出通知，指控該公司「在未取得密西西比州大型建設暴雨逕流一般許可證的情況下，便於工地進行擾動性施工活動」。MDEQ要求每日最高2萬5,000美元的民事罰款，初步認定三個工地的潛在罰款合計約610萬美元。SpaceXAI表示，公司正與MDEQ積極合作，並就以單一和解令解決所有問題進行磋商。

  ## 密西西比州資料中心群

  這三個工地是SpaceXAI（現以SpaceXSI品牌運營）在田納西州曼菲斯附近建設的資料中心群的一部分，但相關設施實際位於密西西比州境內。其中兩座資料中心——分別命名為「Macrohard」與「Macroharder」——已投入運營；第三座「Minihard」仍在興建中。該公司另在曼菲斯市區內設有第四個據點。

  ## NAACP訴訟案成功抵禦司法部終止申請

  SpaceXAI在同一園區同時面臨另一項法律挑戰。全國有色人種促進協會（NAACP）對該公司提起訴訟，指控其未先取得必要許可證，便在密西西比州索萬（Southaven）興建並運營一座燃氣發電廠，違反聯邦法律及州政府法規。司法部（DOJ）尋求終止該訴訟，但美國聯邦地區法院法官德布拉·布朗（Debra Brown）本週駁回了這一申請。

  美國環境保護署（EPA）以《清潔空氣法》主要執法機構的身份要求終止此案；《清潔空氣法》正是NAACP訴訟所依據的聯邦空氣品質法規。布朗法官裁定，EPA的執法立場並不賦予聯邦政府單方面終止私人訴訟的權力。布朗在裁定書中寫道：「EPA身為《清潔空氣法》的主要執法機構，並不意味著聯邦政府對所有《清潔空氣法》相關訴訟擁有完全的主導權。」

  司法部曾主張，NAACP所尋求的救濟——民事罰款及要求停止渦輪機運行的禁制令——將威脅國家安全與經濟利益。據首先報導此罰款通知的《資料中心動態》（Data Center Dynamics）表示，該主張的確切理由在訴訟程序中並未得到充分說明。該媒體另報導，SpaceXAI執行長伊隆·馬斯克（Elon Musk）已捐款約4億4,500萬美元，支持共和黨候選人參加即將到來的期中選舉。

  NAACP環境與氣候正義總監阿布雷·康納（Abre' Conner）表示：「司法部試圖繞過地區法院來終止我們的案件，但並未如願。我們的案件始終致力於讓污染者承擔責任，確保各方遵守法律。法院今天清楚表明，法律適用於所有人，包括聯邦政府。」《資料中心動態》表示，已就此事向SpaceXAI發出採訪請求。

  ## 財務背景

  上述監管與法律行動，正值SpaceXAI資料中心業務帶動母公司強勁成長之際。SpaceX公布截至9月30日的第三季營收為145.8億美元，年增151%，高於分析師預期，其中AI業務貢獻76億美元。

  SpaceXAI最初興建密西西比州一帶的資料中心，是為了訓練及運行其人工智慧聊天機器人Grok，但此後已與Anthropic及Google簽署雲端GPU租用協議。第三季SpaceXAI的資本支出達164億美元，占SpaceX集團196億美元資本支出總額的絕大部分。公司表示，資本支出水準未來將維持在相近水位。

  馬斯克在法說會上向投資人表示：「SpaceX在發射、連結與超級智慧三大領域，正處於拐點時刻。」

  ## 後續意義與發展

  上述同步推進的行動，是迄今針對SpaceXAI曼菲斯地區資料中心樞紐最直接的監管壓力——暴雨逕流許可違規與《清潔空氣法》指控，現正分別在各自軌道上推進。MDEQ暴雨逕流違規案仍處於和解協商階段，目標是就密西西比州三個工地達成單一合併和解令。NAACP就索萬燃氣渦輪電廠提起的《清潔空氣法》訴訟，則將在布朗法官裁定後於聯邦地區法院繼續審理。SpaceX表示，AI業務的資本支出預計在未來幾季將維持在第三季水準附近。
