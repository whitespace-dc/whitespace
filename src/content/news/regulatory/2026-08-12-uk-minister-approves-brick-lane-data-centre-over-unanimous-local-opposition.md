body_en: |
  UK Housing Secretary Angela Rayner has approved a £500 million redevelopment of the Truman Brewery estate on Brick Lane in east London, including a data centre, overriding unanimous opposition from Tower Hamlets Council, the local elected mayor, and nine resident groups. The decision applies the UK's Critical National Infrastructure (CNI) designation — conferred on the data centre sector in September 2024 — to clear a scheme that local authorities had rejected in favour of housing.

  ## The approved scheme

  The masterplan, submitted by developers Truman Estates Ltd and Zeloof LLP, covers five new office buildings, a 5,200 sq ft data centre on Grey Eagle Street, a cinema, a supermarket, market space, and 44 residential units. Six of those homes are earmarked for social rent.

  At 5,200 sq ft, the data centre is small by industry standards. Capacity Media describes it as bearing no resemblance to the hyperscale campuses driving planning contests in Texas, Virginia, and Lagos — large-scale facilities that consume substantial power and water resources.

  ## How approval was granted

  The decision came through ministerial recovery of planning appeals, a mechanism by which the Secretary of State takes the final planning decision rather than leaving it with the local authority. According to reporting on the decision letter, the government gave the local housing case no more than limited weight and gave the data centre significant weight on grounds of meeting the need for data centre capacity. The decision letter did not specify how many jobs the facility would create.

  ## Opposition and competing priorities

  Tower Hamlets Council had argued the site should deliver a housing-led scheme. The borough has more than 30,000 households on its social housing waiting list. The Save Brick Lane campaign, a coalition of nine resident groups, also opposed the development.

  Tom Larsson, chief executive of development manager Grow Places, called the approval a significant milestone. Save Brick Lane described the outcome as deeply disappointing.

  ## What the CNI designation means in practice

  Data centres were designated Critical National Infrastructure in the UK in September 2024, a status that gives data centre planning applications additional regulatory weight when set against competing local priorities. Capacity Media describes the Brick Lane decision as the clearest domestic test yet of what that designation means in practice.

  The significance lies in scale. A single building within a mixed-use development, opposed unanimously by local and elected representatives, cleared planning on CNI grounds. A ministerial override was required even for a 5,200 sq ft facility, indicating the designation carries real weight across the size spectrum — not only on large sites where power and water consumption dominate the planning argument.

  Capacity Media has previously reported that data centre development in Central Texas has faced sustained community pushback over water use, with projections that the sector could account for 2.7% of the state's water consumption by 2030. The Brick Lane case involves none of those resource pressures at scale. Its significance is that the planning dynamic — infrastructure need outweighing local objection — now operates at the level of a modest urban development in an inner-London borough, applied through a designation that has been in force for less than two years.
body_zh: |
  英國住房大臣安琪拉·雷納（Angela Rayner）已核准倫敦東區磚巷（Brick Lane）釀酒廠舊址（Truman Brewery estate）5億英鎊的混合用途重建計畫，其中包含一座資料中心，此舉推翻了塔村哈姆萊茨區議會（Tower Hamlets Council）、當地民選區長及九個居民團體的一致反對立場。本案援引英國於2024年9月正式賦予資料中心行業的「關鍵國家基礎設施」（Critical National Infrastructure，CNI）地位，強行放行了一個地方政府原本以住宅需求為由駁回的開發案。

  ## 核准方案內容

  開發商Truman Estates Ltd與Zeloof LLP提交的整體規劃方案，涵蓋五棟全新辦公大樓、一座位於灰鷹街（Grey Eagle Street）面積約483平方公尺（5,200平方英尺）的資料中心、一間電影院、一間超市、市集空間，以及44個住宅單元，其中六戶規劃為社會租屋。

  就業界標準而言，這座資料中心的規模相當小。產業媒體《Capacity Media》指出，它與德克薩斯州、維吉尼亞州及拉哥斯（Lagos）等地引發規劃爭議的超大規模園區截然不同——那些大型設施耗費大量電力與水資源，規模不可同日而語。

  ## 核准程序

  此案是透過「部長收回規劃上訴」機制獲得批准的。這一機制允許住房大臣取代地方政府，直接作出最終規劃決定。根據相關決定函的報導，政府對地方住宅需求的主張僅給予「有限比重」，而以滿足資料中心容量需求為由，給予資料中心「重大比重」。決定函並未說明該設施預計創造多少就業機會。

  ## 反對意見與優先順序之爭

  塔村哈姆萊茨區議會主張，該地點應優先推動以住宅為主的開發方案。該區目前有逾3萬戶家庭在社會住宅輪候名單上苦候。由九個居民團體組成的「拯救磚巷」（Save Brick Lane）運動同樣反對此開發案。

  開發管理公司Grow Places執行長湯姆·拉森（Tom Larsson）稱本次核准是「重要的里程碑」；「拯救磚巷」則形容這一結果「令人深感失望」。

  ## CNI指定地位在實務上的意義

  英國於2024年9月將資料中心列為關鍵國家基礎設施，此一地位使資料中心規劃申請在面對地方優先需求時，得以獲得額外的法規比重。《Capacity Media》將磚巷案形容為迄今最能清楚顯示這項指定地位實際意義的本土案例。

  本案的意義關鍵在於規模。一棟位於混合用途開發案中的單棟建築，在遭地方民選代表一致反對的情況下，仍以CNI為由通過規劃許可。即便只是一座5,200平方英尺的設施，仍須部長親自介入方能過關，顯示這項指定地位在各種規模的開發案上均具有實質效力——而非僅限於電力與用水消耗主導規劃論辯的大型場址。

  《Capacity Media》此前曾報導，德克薩斯州中部的資料中心開發案因用水議題持續遭到社區反彈，有預測指出，該行業到2030年可能佔據德州全州用水量的2.7%。磚巷案在規模上完全不涉及這類資源壓力，其意義在於：基礎設施需求凌駕地方反對的規劃邏輯，如今已延伸至倫敦內城一個規模適中的都市開發案，而這項指定地位生效迄今不過不到兩年。
