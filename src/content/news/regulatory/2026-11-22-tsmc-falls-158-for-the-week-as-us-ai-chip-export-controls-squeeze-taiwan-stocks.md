  ## TSMC Posts 15.8% Weekly Drop Amid US Export Control Tightening

  Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), the world's largest contract chipmaker, fell 15.8% over the course of the week, according to Taiwanese financial news outlet Anue cnyes. The decline contributed to what the outlet described as Taiwan's equity market posting its worst single-week record in 36 years.

  The Taiwan stock exchange index dropped 7,641 points during the week, leaving analysts watching whether the benchmark can hold the 35,000-point level in the following week, Anue cnyes reported.

  ## US Tightens AI Chip Export Controls

  Tightening US restrictions on exports of artificial-intelligence chips — semiconductors designed to accelerate machine-learning workloads — added pressure to the market, the outlet reported. TSMC, as the primary manufacturer of advanced chips for companies including Nvidia and Apple, sits at the center of any trade restrictions affecting AI semiconductor supply chains.

  ## National Security Fund Intervenes

  Taiwan's National Security Fund, a government-backed stabilization mechanism, moved quickly to reassure investors, issuing statements that cited four specific market-support highlights, according to Anue cnyes. The fund did not detail those four points in the content captured by the outlet.

  ## Market Significance

  A weekly decline of 15.8% in TSMC shares — a stock that anchors Taiwan's equity index and represents a significant share of global semiconductor capacity — signals the degree to which US export policy has become a direct market variable for the island's technology sector. The 36-year record for single-week losses underscores the breadth of the selloff beyond TSMC alone.

  Investors were watching whether the TAIEX (Taiwan Stock Exchange Capitalization Weighted Stock Index) would hold the 35,000 threshold in the week ahead, with the National Security Fund's market-support posture remaining the main near-term policy variable, the outlet reported.

  ## 美出口管制持續收緊，台積電單週重挫15.8%

  全球最大晶圓代工廠台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）本週股價大跌15.8%，據台灣財經媒體鉅亨網（Anue cnyes）報導，此跌幅拖累台股創下36年來最慘單週紀錄。

  台股加權指數（TAIEX）本週重挫7,641點，分析師和法人正密切關注，下週指數能否守住35,000點關卡，鉅亨網報導。

  ## 美國收緊AI晶片出口管制

  美國進一步收緊人工智慧晶片出口管制——這類半導體專為加速機器學習運算而設計——對市場造成額外壓力，鉅亨網報導。台積電身為輝達（Nvidia）、蘋果（Apple）等公司先進晶片的主要製造商，在任何影響AI半導體供應鏈的貿易限制中，都處於核心位置。

  ## 國安基金出手護盤

  台灣國家金融安定基金（國安基金）——一個由政府支持的市場穩定機制——迅速出手安撫投資人，發表聲明列舉四項具體護盤亮點，鉅亨網報導。惟根據該媒體所取得的內容，國安基金並未進一步說明此四項的具體細節。

  ## 市場影響與觀察

  台積電單週重挫15.8%，其影響意義深遠——台積電不僅是台股加權指數的核心權值股，更代表全球半導體產能中舉足輕重的一席之地——充分凸顯美國出口政策已直接成為台灣科技業的市場變數。36年來最慘的單週跌幅，更說明這波賣壓之廣，遠不止台積電一家。

  投資人持續緊盯台股加權指數（TAIEX）下週能否守穩35,000點大關，而國安基金的護盤立場，將是近期最關鍵的政策觀察指標，鉅亨網報導。
