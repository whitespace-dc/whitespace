body_en: |
  Bulgarian authorities have arrested four people, including the leader of an organised criminal group, on charges of illegally importing and distributing hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants, money laundering, and tax crimes, the Bulgarian Anti-Corruption Bureau (Gdbop) announced.

  Two of the four individuals detained during the operation have been formally charged. One faces a money laundering charge involving €1,548,052 (approximately $1.67 million).

  ## What the searches uncovered

  Investigators searched 10 addresses, including private residences and commercial office premises, as part of the operation. In a warehouse, authorities found more than 1,500 bottles of various HFC refrigerants. Seized assets included a car, more than €70,000 in cash, investment gold coins, and expensive jewellery and watches.

  HFCs are a class of synthetic refrigerant gases widely used in air conditioning, refrigeration, and heat pump equipment. Their trade is subject to customs duties and, in many jurisdictions, quotas and import licensing requirements tied to international climate agreements.

  ## How the scheme operated

  According to Gdbop, part of the criminal activity involved evading customs duties on refrigerant imported from China. The group used false documents to conceal the origin, quantity, and quality of the goods, the bureau said.

  Gdbop noted that other individuals beyond those arrested are also believed to have participated in the group. The investigation is ongoing.

  ## Significance for the industry

  Illegal HFC imports, often underdeclared in quantity or misrepresented in type, undercut legitimate distributors who pay applicable duties and comply with import controls. The use of falsified documentation to mask Chinese origin suggests the scheme was structured to avoid both customs duties and any regulatory scrutiny of the refrigerant's specifications. The scale of the seizure — more than 1,500 bottles in a single warehouse — points to a distribution operation of significant size rather than opportunistic smuggling.

  ## 保加利亞逮捕四人　查獲非法走私HFC冷媒大案

  保加利亞當局逮捕四名涉嫌非法進口及販售氫氟碳化物（HFC）冷媒的人士，其中包括一個有組織犯罪集團的首腦，並以洗錢及稅務犯罪等罪名提起訴追。保加利亞反腐敗局（Gdbop）正式對外宣布此案。

  此次行動中被拘留的四人裡，已有兩人遭正式起訴。其中一人面臨洗錢指控，涉案金額高達1,548,052歐元（約合167萬美元）。

  ## 搜索行動的查獲成果

  調查人員針對10處地址展開搜索，對象涵蓋私人住宅及商業辦公場所。在一處倉庫中，當局起出各類HFC冷媒逾1,500瓶。遭查扣的資產還包括一輛汽車、逾7萬歐元現金、投資用金幣，以及名貴珠寶與名錶。

  HFC屬於一類合成冷媒氣體，廣泛應用於冷氣機、冷凍設備及熱泵裝置。此類產品的貿易受到關稅管制，在許多國家和地區，依據國際氣候協議，還須符合配額限制及進口許可要求。

  ## 犯罪手法揭秘

  根據Gdbop的說法，此犯罪集團的部分非法活動在於逃漏自中國進口冷媒所應繳納的關稅。該集團持偽造文件，掩蓋貨物的原產地、數量與品質。

  Gdbop指出，除已逮捕的人員外，相信仍有其他人士涉案參與其中，調查工作目前仍在持續進行。

  ## 此案對業界的意義

  非法進口的HFC冷媒往往在數量上低報申報，或在品項上虛假申報，此舉嚴重打壓了依法繳納關稅、遵守進口管制的合法經銷商。以偽造文件掩蓋中國原產地的做法，顯示此一犯罪計畫的設計意在同時規避關稅義務及監管機關對冷媒規格的審查。單一倉庫即查扣逾1,500瓶冷媒的規模，更說明此案涉及一個具相當規模的配銷網絡，而非偶發性的零星走私行為。
