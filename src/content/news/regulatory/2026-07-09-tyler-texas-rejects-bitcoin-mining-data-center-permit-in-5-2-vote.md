body_en: |
  Tyler, Texas, has rejected a proposal to build a Bitcoin mining data center after the city's planning and zoning commission voted 5-2 to deny a special use permit for the project.

  The permit, sought by Vulcan Core LLC, would have allowed the company to build an 8,090 square foot (750 square meter) data center on a 1.92-acre parcel at 1101 and 1105 W. Erwin Street. The facility would have contracted up to 12 megawatts (MW) of electrical capacity, with any demand beyond that threshold requiring additional regulatory approval under the conditions of the permit.

  ## Community opposition

  Residents voiced strong opposition to the project, raising concerns about potential water use, noise levels, and the facility's proximity to surrounding properties. The commission's 5-2 vote against the permit reflects that community pressure.

  The permit's conditions specified that the facility would have used a closed-loop cooling system and outdoor dry-cooler heat rejection, eliminating the need for diesel generators or any on-site combustion equipment. Despite those design constraints, the commission denied the application.

  Data Center Dynamics reported it reached out to Vulcan Core LLC for comment.

  ## Local data center context

  Tyler is situated in eastern Texas, approximately 100 miles west of Dallas, and is not an established data center hub. The city does host at least one existing facility: Barrio Energy operates a 12MW data center in downtown Tyler, which it acquired in August 2024.

  The rejection illustrates the regulatory friction Bitcoin mining operations face in residential and semi-urban communities, where concerns about noise and resource consumption can override proposed technical mitigations. Vulcan Core LLC's next steps have not been disclosed.
body_zh: |
  ## 德州泰勒市以5比2否決比特幣挖礦資料中心許可申請

  德州泰勒市規劃與分區委員會以5比2的投票結果，否決了一項興建比特幣挖礦資料中心的提案，令該計畫宣告終止。

  此次許可申請由Vulcan Core LLC提出，該公司原計畫在西厄文街1101號及1105號、占地約0.78公頃的土地上，興建一棟面積約750平方公尺（8,090平方英尺）的資料中心。該設施預計合約用電容量上限為12百萬瓦（MW），若實際需求超出此門檻，依許可條件規定須另行取得主管機關核准。

  ## 社區居民強力反對

  當地居民對這項計畫展現強烈反對立場，提出的疑慮涵蓋潛在用水量、噪音程度，以及設施與周邊住宅過於鄰近等問題。委員會最終以5比2否決許可申請，充分反映了社區的壓力。

  依照許可條件，該設施原本規劃採用密閉循環冷卻系統及戶外乾式冷卻器散熱，因此不需要柴油發電機或任何現場燃燒設備。儘管提案方已在設計上加入上述限制條件，委員會仍決定駁回申請。

  《Data Center Dynamics》表示已就此事向Vulcan Core LLC尋求回應。

  ## 泰勒市資料中心現況

  泰勒市位於德州東部，距達拉斯約160公里（100英里），並非公認的資料中心重鎮。目前該市至少有一座既有設施：Barrio Energy於2024年8月收購了位於泰勒市中心的一座12MW資料中心，目前仍在營運中。

  此次許可申請遭拒，凸顯出比特幣挖礦業者在住宅區或半都市化社區所面臨的法規阻力——居民對於噪音與資源消耗的疑慮，往往足以推翻業者所提出的技術減緩措施。目前Vulcan Core LLC尚未對外披露後續計畫。
