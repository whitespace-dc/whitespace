  ## ComEd's battery VPP wins ICC approval

  The Illinois Commerce Commission has approved Commonwealth Edison's proposal for a battery-based "scheduled dispatch virtual power plant" (SDVPP), the Chicago-area utility announced last week, marking the first significant program to take shape under Illinois's landmark Clean and Reliable Grid Affordability Act (CRGA).

  The approval comes as the region faces intensifying grid pressure. PJM Interconnection — the wholesale electricity market operator whose territory stretches from northern Illinois to New Jersey — recorded an instantaneous load of 162.7 GW on July 2, less than 3 GW below its all-time demand peak set in 2006. PJM said it would likely have broken that record were it not for robust demand response participation.

  ## How the program works

  Under the SDVPP, ComEd will dispatch power from enrolled customer-sited batteries during periods of peak demand to reduce strain on the distribution grid serving the densely populated Chicago area. Participants must commit to five consecutive seasons, each running from June 1 through September 30. They receive seasonal performance payments based on the amount of energy they inject into ComEd's distribution grid during peak events.

  ComEd said it has already connected approximately 1.8 GW of distributed energy resources — residential and small commercial batteries, electric vehicle chargers, HVAC controls, and similar assets — to its grid.

  ## Context: the law behind the program

  The SDVPP replaces a narrower VPP proposal that ComEd withdrew in November following the passage of the CRGA. Illinois Governor J.B. Pritzker signed the CRGA in January, shortly after a U.S. Congressional report found Illinois retail electricity prices rose more than 15% in 2025.

  The CRGA directs Illinois electric utilities to deploy 3 GW of energy storage by 2030 and to develop virtual power plants — programs that aggregate distributed customer assets to act collectively as a grid resource — as one tool for meeting that target. The law also requires expanded energy efficiency programs and mandates that utilities offer residential time-of-use pricing plans, which charge customers more during high-demand hours to encourage load shifting.

  Illinois's grid is split between PJM and the Midcontinent Independent System Operator (MISO), both of which have seen sharp increases in capacity prices — the charges that load-serving utilities pay to ensure enough generation is available during peak periods.

  Andrew Plenge, ComEd's vice president of strategy and energy policy, said in a statement that the SDVPP "is an important step in bolstering the potential of customer-sited energy resources to make the grid more resilient during periods of peak demand while helping customers receive additional value for their support at a time when supply costs are rising."

  Will Kenworthy, Midwest regulatory director for Vote Solar, called the program "exactly what Illinois lawmakers intended" and "a model for how the clean energy transition can save people money."

  ## What it means

  The ICC's approval demonstrates how state clean energy legislation can accelerate the commercialization of distributed battery resources as a grid management tool. The program gives ComEd a dispatchable demand-side resource in a service territory that contributes to one of the most congested portions of the PJM footprint. Whether the SDVPP scales quickly enough to make a material dent in the CRGA's 3 GW storage mandate by 2030 will depend in large part on customer enrollment rates over the program's initial five-season commitment periods.

  ## ComEd電池虛擬電廠獲伊利諾州商業委員會批准

  伊利諾州商業委員會（ICC）已批准聯邦愛迪生公司（ComEd）提出的電池式「排程調度虛擬電廠」（SDVPP）方案。這家服務芝加哥地區的電力公司上週宣布此消息，標誌著伊利諾州指標性立法《清潔可靠電網可負擔法》（CRGA）施行以來，首個具重大意義的配套計畫正式成形。

  此次批准恰逢該地區電網壓力持續升高之際。負責批發電力市場的PJM電力互聯公司（PJM）服務範圍橫跨伊利諾州北部至新澤西州，於7月2日錄得162.7 GW的瞬間負載，距2006年創下的歷史需求峰值僅差不到3 GW。PJM表示，若非需求響應機制的積極參與，當日極可能打破歷史紀錄。

  ## 計畫運作方式

  依據SDVPP機制，ComEd將在尖峰需求期間調度客戶端電池所儲存的電力，以舒緩服務人口稠密之芝加哥地區配電網的壓力。參與用戶須承諾連續參與五個季度，每個季度從6月1日至9月30日。用戶將依據在尖峰事件期間向ComEd配電網注入的電量，獲得季節性績效電費補貼。

  ComEd表示，目前已有約1.8 GW的分散式能源資源連接至其電網，包括住宅及小型商業電池、電動車充電樁、暖通空調控制系統及其他類似設備。

  ## 背景：計畫所依據的法律

  SDVPP取代了ComEd於去年11月撤回的一項較為狹窄的虛擬電廠提案，撤案時間正值CRGA甫完成立法之後。伊利諾州州長J.B. Pritzker於今年1月簽署CRGA，此前美國國會一份報告發現，伊利諾州零售電價在2025年漲幅超過15%。

  CRGA要求伊利諾州各電力公司於2030年前建置3 GW的儲能設施，並發展虛擬電廠——即整合分散式客戶資產、使其集體作為電網資源運作的計畫——以作為達成此目標的手段之一。該法律同時要求擴大能源效率方案，並強制要求電力公司為住宅用戶提供分時電價方案，在需求高峰時段收取較高費率，藉此鼓勵用戶移轉用電時間。

  伊利諾州電網分由PJM及中大陸獨立系統運營商（MISO）管轄，兩者的容量電價均大幅攀升——所謂容量電價，即負責供電的電力公司為確保尖峰時段有足夠發電容量所須支付的費用。

  ComEd負責策略與能源政策的副總裁Andrew Plenge在聲明中表示，SDVPP「是強化客戶端能源資源潛力的重要一步，有助於提升電網在尖峰需求期間的韌性，同時在供電成本上升之際，協助客戶從自身的貢獻中獲得額外價值」。

  倡議組織Vote Solar的中西部法規事務總監Will Kenworthy則表示，這項計畫「正是伊利諾州立法者的初衷」，並稱其為「清潔能源轉型如何為民眾省錢的典範」。

  ## 意義與展望

  ICC此次批准展現了州級清潔能源立法如何加速分散式電池資源商業化、使其成為電網管理工具。此計畫讓ComEd在其服務範圍內掌握一項可調度的需求端資源，而該服務範圍正是PJM電網中壅塞最嚴重的區域之一。SDVPP能否以足夠快的速度擴大規模，在2030年前對CRGA所設3 GW儲能目標產生實質貢獻，在很大程度上將取決於計畫初始五個季度承諾期間的用戶參與率。
