body_en: |
  Greenpeace activists interrupted then-Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's keynote at GTC Berlin, unfurling a banner and projecting the message "Clean Up Your Dirty Business" onto the stage while shouting questions about Nvidia technology reportedly found in Russian drones and about the environmental effects of data center expansion.

  At least two protesters staged the disruption midway through Huang's presentation, according to Data Center Knowledge, which reported from the event. Security escorted them from the hall. Huang, who resigned as Nvidia's chief executive on Nov. 12, remained calm and asked security not to overreact. "Everybody has a chance to say something," he said from the stage. "I'll talk to you later. But let me get through my keynote."

  Benjamin Stephan, Greenpeace International's Big Tech campaigner and the action's on-site spokesperson, said Huang did not follow through on that offer. Stephan said he waited outside by Huang's car and informed the security detail that Huang had offered to speak afterward. "They did not enable this; instead, they had his car drive off empty," Stephan told Data Center Knowledge.

  ## Two Grievances Behind the Action

  Stephan described the protest's criticism as twofold. First, Nvidia chips have reportedly been found in autonomous Russian drones deployed in Ukraine. Greenpeace is calling on Nvidia to implement independent auditing across all global distributors, subsidiaries, and third-party vendors to ensure its technology is not diverted into military equipment or autonomous weapons.

  Second, Stephan said, Nvidia's supply chain runs largely on fossil fuels, which he said creates hardship for communities in Taiwan, where Nvidia relies on TSMC to manufacture its chips. Greenpeace is demanding that Nvidia commit to 100% renewable energy across its supply chain and data center investments by 2030 and invest in new renewable capacity in key supplier regions including Taiwan and Southeast Asia.

  Stephan also cited Nvidia's role in organizing financing models to help its large technology customers fund new data center construction and chip purchases, arguing this accelerates data center expansion that itself relies heavily on fossil fuels.

  ## Nvidia's Sustainability Position

  Nvidia disputes that its operations are environmentally unaccountable. In the company's 2026 Sustainability Report, Huang wrote that Nvidia matched 100% of its global electricity consumption with clean energy for the second consecutive year and continues to work to reduce emissions and improve efficiency. The company said it designs platforms such as Vera Rubin so that "powerful computing and sustainability advance together."

  The Greenpeace statement, however, draws a distinction between Nvidia's own operational electricity use and the broader supply chain and the data centers it helps finance and equip. "While Nvidia is building an unchecked AI empire no one consented to, people are paying the price," Stephan said in the organization's public statement following the protest. "Around the world, communities face damage to water supplies and soil, alongside rising electricity bills because of unchecked expansion of data centers."

  ## A Pattern of Direct Actions

  The Berlin protest was not an isolated incident. Greenpeace East Asia activists previously confronted Huang at GTC Taipei, presenting him with a five-layered cake topped with wind turbines — a reference to Huang's own metaphor describing AI as a five-layered stack — to illustrate what they said was a gap in renewable energy commitment across Nvidia's supply chain. Stephan said Huang responded positively at Taipei when asked whether Nvidia would invest in renewable energy together with its suppliers there, and that the Berlin action was intended to hold him accountable because "nothing had happened yet."

  Days after the Berlin protest, Greenpeace activists joined the Stop Dirty Data Centres coalition for two days of action with protests across Europe and elsewhere. Separately, Greenpeace activists unfurled a large banner at a STACK Infrastructure data center site in Switzerland. STACK said in a statement that it is "a committed, long-term partner in the communities where we operate" and designs projects with "careful stewardship of local resources in mind."

  ## Industry Significance

  The Berlin protest occurred as Nvidia and the broader technology sector confront what Data Center Knowledge described as the "key-man risk" long associated with Huang, who built the company's public profile around his own persona. His resignation on Nov. 12 adds a new dimension to that risk calculus. Data center operators, Nvidia's primary customers, are under rising pressure from regulators, communities, and campaign groups over environmental impact and water use — scrutiny that Greenpeace's campaign is designed to deepen and sustain.

  Greenpeace said the Berlin action is part of a new global campaign on Big Tech and indicated that further direct actions targeting the data center sector are planned.
body_zh: |
  綠色和平（Greenpeace）活動人士闖入前輝達（Nvidia）執行長黃仁勳在GTC柏林大會的主題演講現場，展開抗議橫幅，並在舞台上投影「清理你的骯髒生意」（Clean Up Your Dirty Business）等字樣，同時高聲追問輝達技術是否出現在俄羅斯無人機上，以及資料中心持續擴張對環境造成的衝擊。

  根據《資料中心知識》（Data Center Knowledge）的現場報導，至少有兩名抗議者在黃仁勳演講進行至一半時發動行動，隨後被維安人員護送離場。黃仁勳已於11月12日辭去輝達執行長一職，當時他保持冷靜，要求維安人員不要反應過度。「每個人都有機會發聲，」他在台上說道，「我等一下再跟你們談。讓我先把主題演講講完。」

  然而，此次行動的現場發言人、綠色和平國際大型科技倡議員班傑明．史蒂芬（Benjamin Stephan）表示，黃仁勳並未兌現這項承諾。史蒂芬說，他在外頭黃仁勳座車旁等候，並告知維安人員黃仁勳曾表示事後願意交談。「他們並未促成此事；反而讓他的座車空車開走。」史蒂芬如此告訴《資料中心知識》。

  ## 行動背後的兩項訴求

  史蒂芬表示，此次抗議針對兩大問題。其一，據報導輝達晶片已出現在俄羅斯部署於烏克蘭的自主無人機上。綠色和平呼籲輝達對全球所有經銷商、子公司及第三方廠商實施獨立稽核，確保其技術不會被挪作軍事設備或自主武器之用。

  其二，史蒂芬指出，輝達的供應鏈大多依賴化石燃料，他表示這對臺灣社區造成困擾——輝達倚重台積電（TSMC）在臺灣製造晶片。綠色和平要求輝達承諾於2030年前，在整個供應鏈與資料中心投資中達到100%再生能源，並在包括臺灣與東南亞在內的主要供應商地區投資新建再生能源容量。

  史蒂芬也提及輝達的另一角色：協助組織融資模式，幫助大型科技客戶為新建資料中心及採購晶片提供資金。他認為此舉加速了資料中心的擴張，而這些資料中心本身又高度依賴化石燃料。

  ## 輝達的永續立場

  輝達否認其營運對環境缺乏問責。在公司2026年永續報告中，黃仁勳寫道，輝達連續第二年以再生能源抵銷全球100%的電力消耗，並持續致力於降低排放、提升效率。公司表示，其在設計Vera Rubin等平台時，致力使「強大的運算能力與永續發展並駕齊驅」。

  然而，綠色和平的聲明在輝達自身的用電營運，與其協助融資及裝備的更廣泛供應鏈和資料中心之間，做出了明確區隔。「輝達正在建立一個無人同意的失控AI帝國，而人們正在為此付出代價，」史蒂芬在抗議結束後發表的組織公開聲明中說道。「世界各地的社區因資料中心的無節制擴張，面臨水源與土壤受損，以及電費高漲的問題。」

  ## 一連串直接行動

  柏林的抗議並非個案。綠色和平東亞分部的活動人士此前曾在GTC臺北大會上與黃仁勳正面交鋒，向他呈上一個五層蛋糕，蛋糕頂端插有風力發電機——藉用黃仁勳本人將AI比喻為五層堆疊架構的說法——凸顯他們所指出的輝達供應鏈再生能源承諾落差。史蒂芬表示，黃仁勳在臺北被問及輝達是否願意與當地供應商共同投資再生能源時，給出了正面回應；而柏林的行動，正是為了追究責任，因為「至今毫無進展」。

  柏林抗議結束後數日，綠色和平活動人士加入「停止骯髒資料中心」（Stop Dirty Data Centres）聯盟，在歐洲各地及其他地區展開為期兩天的抗議行動。另外，綠色和平活動人士也在瑞士的STACK基礎設施（STACK Infrastructure）資料中心工地展開橫幅。STACK在聲明中表示，公司是「在我們營運所在社區中堅定的長期夥伴」，並以「對當地資源的審慎管理」為核心進行專案設計。

  ## 產業意義

  此次柏林抗議發生之際，輝達及整體科技業正面臨《資料中心知識》所稱長期與黃仁勳相關的「關鍵人風險」（key-man risk）——黃仁勳多年來以個人形象建立起公司的公眾品牌。他於11月12日辭職一事，為這一風險的評估增添了新的變數。資料中心業者作為輝達的主要客戶，正面臨監管機構、在地社區及倡議團體對環境衝擊與用水問題的日益嚴格審視——而綠色和平的倡議行動，正是為了深化並延續這種施壓力道。

  綠色和平表示，柏林行動是其針對大型科技業新一波全球倡議的一部分，並表示計劃針對資料中心產業採取更多直接行動。
