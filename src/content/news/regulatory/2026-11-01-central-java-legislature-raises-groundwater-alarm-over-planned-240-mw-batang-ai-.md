body_en: |
  Central Java's provincial legislature has raised groundwater and land subsidence concerns over a planned 240-megawatt (MW) AI hyperscale data center at Batang's Special Economic Zone, with the legislature's oversight commission saying it has not been briefed on the project despite the environmental stakes involved.

  ## Groundwater and subsidence concerns

  Ida Nur Saadah, chair of Commission D within the Central Java Regional People's Representative Council (DPRD Jawa Tengah — the provincial legislature), said land subsidence, the gradual sinking of the ground surface, in several Central Java coastal areas has already reached alarming levels. She made the remarks at a public forum in Semarang focused on Central Java's Regional Energy Plan (RUED) and equitable energy transition.

  Saadah called on the provincial Energy and Mineral Resources Agency (Dinas ESDM) to enforce stricter oversight of groundwater extraction, warning that the data center project must not deepen the subsidence already affecting coastal zones.

  "We have pushed ESDM to carry out strict supervision. Groundwater extraction must not further worsen land subsidence, especially since the impact in coastal areas is already extraordinary," Saadah was quoted as saying by Tribun Jateng on October 29, 2026.

  ## Moratorium push predates the data center proposal

  Commission D said it has been pressing Dinas ESDM to impose a moratorium — a temporary halt — on issuing new groundwater extraction permits for the past three years. Saadah said oversight cannot be limited to screening new permit applications: existing permit holders must also be audited to confirm they are not over-extracting. She described that dual focus as essential to preventing the kind of groundwater exploitation that accelerates surface sinking in coastal areas.

  ## Legislature has not been formally informed

  Despite these standing concerns, Commission D acknowledged it has not received any official communication or presentation about the 240 MW data center planned for Kawasan Ekonomi Khusus (KEK) Industropolis Batang — the Batang Special Economic Zone in Central Java. The 240 MW figure represents the facility's planned electrical power demand.

  Saadah said the commission does not intend to wait for problems to emerge before acting. "We have not been invited to communicate and have not monitored directly. But we must find out. We don't have to wait for a problem to emerge — we will find out and request a presentation from the relevant parties," she said.

  ## Planned oversight actions

  Commission D said it plans to formally summon the relevant regional government agencies — known as Organisasi Perangkat Daerah (OPD), the operational units within the provincial administration — to obtain information about the project. The commission also intends to conduct a direct review, evaluation, and field inspection of the site.

  Saadah cited the Gunung Ungaran geothermal project as an illustration of the social conflict that large energy-adjacent infrastructure projects can generate in the region, and said anticipating such friction early was part of the commission's rationale for seeking a formal briefing.

  ## Significance

  The legislature's intervention places the 240 MW Batang project under regulatory scrutiny that extends beyond standard power supply and land-use approvals to encompass water resource management — a dimension amplified by the coastal site's existing and documented subsidence risk. The episode also signals that Indonesian provincial legislatures are beginning to engage with data center infrastructure as a distinct category of environmental concern before construction commences rather than after.

  Commission D's next concrete steps are to formally summon the relevant OPDs and carry out a direct field inspection of the Batang site.
body_zh: |
  中爪哇省省議會針對計畫在巴坦特別經濟區（KEK Industropolis Batang）興建的240百萬瓦（MW）AI超大規模資料中心，提出地下水與地層下陷方面的疑慮。省議會監督委員會表示，儘管此案涉及重大環境議題，卻從未收到任何相關簡報。

  ## 地下水與地層下陷疑慮

  中爪哇省地方人民代表委員會（DPRD Jawa Tengah，即省議會）D委員會主席Ida Nur Saadah指出，中爪哇省沿海地區多處的地層下陷——即地表逐漸沉降的現象——早已達到令人憂心的程度。她在三寶瓏（Semarang）一場以中爪哇省地區能源計畫（RUED）及公平能源轉型為主題的公開論壇上作出上述表示。

  Saadah呼籲省能源暨礦產資源局（Dinas ESDM）加強地下水開採的監督管理，並警告此資料中心計畫不得進一步加劇沿海地區已然存在的地層下陷問題。

  她在2026年10月29日接受《Tribun Jateng》引述時表示：「我們已敦促能源礦產局實施嚴格監督。地下水開採絕不能使地層下陷情況雪上加霜，尤其是沿海地區的衝擊已相當驚人。」

  ## 暫停令的推動早於資料中心提案

  D委員會表示，三年來一直積極推動能源礦產局對新地下水開採許可的發放實施暫停令——即暫時停止核發新許可。Saadah指出，監管工作不能僅限於審查新的許可申請，還必須對現有許可持有者進行稽核，確認其是否存在超量開採的情況。她形容這種雙軌並進的方式，對於防範地下水過度開發、進而加速沿海地區地表下沉，至關重要。

  ## 省議會迄今未獲正式告知

  儘管存在上述既有的關切，D委員會坦承，對於計畫在中爪哇省巴坦工業極特別經濟區（KEK Industropolis Batang）興建的這座240MW資料中心，委員會至今未收到任何正式的溝通或簡報。240MW代表該設施計畫中的電力需求規模。

  Saadah表示，委員會並不打算等問題出現後才採取行動。「我們尚未受邀溝通，也未曾直接進行監督，但我們必須查明情況。我們不必等到問題出現才行動——我們會主動了解，並要求相關單位提供簡報，」她說。

  ## 計畫中的監督行動

  D委員會表示，計畫正式傳喚相關地方政府機關——即省級行政運作單位「地區機關組織」（Organisasi Perangkat Daerah，OPD）——以取得與此計畫相關的資訊。委員會亦計畫對現場進行直接審查、評估及實地視察。

  Saadah以Gunung Ungaran地熱計畫為例，說明大型能源相關基礎設施計畫在該地區可能引發的社會衝突，並表示及早預見此類摩擦，正是委員會尋求正式簡報的動機之一。

  ## 重要意義

  省議會的介入，使巴坦240MW計畫面臨的法規審查，已超出一般電力供應與土地利用審批的範疇，延伸至水資源管理層面——而沿海基地現有且有據可查的地層下陷風險，更放大了這一面向的重要性。此事件也顯示，印尼省級議會正開始在資料中心動工之前、而非之後，將資料中心基礎設施視為獨立的環境關注類別主動介入。

  D委員會下一步的具體行動，是正式傳喚相關地區機關組織，並赴巴坦基地進行直接的現場視察。
