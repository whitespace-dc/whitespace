# Environmental activist Erin Brockovich launches data center tracking website

Environmental activist Erin Brockovich, known for her legal battle against PG&E over the Hinkley contamination case, has launched a website to track data centers across the United States. She is now directing her environmental advocacy toward the growing data center industry.

## 中文

# 環保人士推出美國資料中心追蹤網站

環保人士艾琳布洛克維奇推出新網站，追蹤美國全境的資料中心。這位因對抗太平洋電氣公司（PG&E）欣克利污染案而聞名的人士，如今將關注焦點轉向資料中心產業的環境影響。
