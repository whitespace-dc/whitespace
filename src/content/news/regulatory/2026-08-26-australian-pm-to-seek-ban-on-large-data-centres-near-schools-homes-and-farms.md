body_en: |
  Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese will ask state and territory leaders on August 26 to adopt nationwide rules barring large data centres from being built near schools, homes, and prime farmland, according to plans seen by AFP ahead of the meeting.

  The proposal would establish national minimum standards covering water and energy use by data centres, as well as siting rules designed to limit disruption to communities, potential housing sites, and agricultural land, AFP reported. Albanese is also expected to argue at a business dinner on August 25 that sustaining community support is a prerequisite for the data centre boom to continue.

  ## Scale of the buildout

  The policy push comes as New South Wales, Australia's most populous state, said it is assessing 26 new data centre projects with a combined value of A$100 billion (approximately US$71.7 billion). The state already hosts 90 operating data centres, following construction growth of 65 percent annually over the past three years, according to AFP.

  Community resistance has already derailed at least one project. A proposal to build a data centre in the World Heritage-listed Blue Mountains area, in close proximity to a school, was withdrawn in June after public opposition.

  ## National AI legislation

  Albanese's government has pledged to move quickly on a national artificial intelligence law that would address AI training rules, covering copyright, security, and safety, AFP reported. Officials likened the government's urgency to Australia's earlier move to ban minors from social media, which was described as a world-first measure.

  In remarks prepared for the August 25 dinner, Albanese is quoted as saying that hosting large-scale AI training data centres would help Australia "capture a larger share of the economic value, technical capability and strategic influence of AI." He is also expected to call for conditions that ensure AI is "developed safely, securely, and in ways that respect copyright and provide control and payment to the creative sector."

  ## Copyright pressure

  The copyright element of the proposed law carries a political dimension. US startup Anthropic has lobbied Australian officials to amend copyright rules to facilitate AI model training, according to AFP. That lobbying has drawn a backlash from media companies, musicians, and other creators.

  ## What the meeting means for the industry

  If state leaders agree to the framework at the August 26 meeting, Australia would have a single national baseline governing where large data centres can be located and how much water and energy they can consume, replacing the patchwork of state-level assessments currently in place. The outcome of the meeting will determine whether the proposed siting restrictions and AI training rules advance toward legislation.
body_zh: |
  澳洲總理安東尼·艾班尼斯將於8月26日邀集各州及領地領導人，要求採納全國統一規範，禁止大型資料中心建設於學校、住宅區及優質農地附近。根據法新社在會議召開前所取得的相關計畫，此舉旨在對人工智慧帶動的建設熱潮進行系統性管制。

  這項提案將建立涵蓋資料中心用水量與能源消耗的全國最低標準，並訂定選址規則，以減少對社區、潛在住宅用地及農業用地的衝擊。法新社報導，艾班尼斯預計在8月25日的商業晚宴上力陳，維繫社區支持是資料中心建設熱潮能否持續的先決條件。

  ## 建設規模

  這波政策推進的背景，是澳洲人口最多的州——新南威爾斯州——宣布正在評估26項新資料中心計畫，合計總值高達1,000億澳元（約717億美元）。法新社報導，該州目前已有90座資料中心在運營，過去三年來建設量每年成長65%。

  社區的反對聲浪已讓至少一項計畫告吹。一項擬在列入世界遺產名錄的藍山地區興建資料中心的方案，因緊鄰一所學校且遭民眾強烈抵制，於6月宣告撤案。

  ## 全國人工智慧立法

  艾班尼斯政府已承諾加速推動全國人工智慧法規，內容涵蓋人工智慧訓練規則，包括著作權、資安與安全性等面向。據法新社報導，官員將政府此次的積極態度比擬為澳洲早前率先立法禁止未成年人使用社群媒體的舉措，稱那是「全球首例」。

  在為8月25日晚宴所準備的發言稿中，艾班尼斯表示，在澳洲設立大規模人工智慧訓練資料中心，有助於澳洲「在人工智慧的經濟價值、技術能力及戰略影響力上爭取更大份額」。他預計也將呼籲確立相關條件，使人工智慧「在安全、有保障的環境下發展，以尊重著作權的方式運作，同時讓創意產業獲得合理的掌控權與報酬」。

  ## 著作權爭議

  擬議立法中的著作權條款具有一定的政治敏感性。法新社報導，美國新創公司 Anthropic 已向澳洲官員遊說，要求修改著作權規則，以利人工智慧模型訓練。這一遊說行動已引發媒體業者、音樂人及其他創作者的強烈反彈。

  ## 會議對產業的意義

  若各州領導人於8月26日的會議上同意這套框架，澳洲將擁有一套統一的全國基準，規範大型資料中心的選址條件及用水量、能耗上限，取代目前各州各自評估的分散做法。這次會議的結果，將決定選址限制規定及人工智慧訓練相關法規能否進一步推進至立法程序。
