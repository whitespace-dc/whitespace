  ## Crown Castle flags spectrum pipeline and SpaceX terrestrial ambitions as tower demand drivers

  Crown Castle chief executive Chris Hillabrant said on the company's third-quarter earnings call this week that a wave of carrier spectrum acquisitions — including SpaceX's $17 billion purchase of AWS4, H Block, and 800MHz licenses — represents a meaningful long-term catalyst for tower infrastructure demand, and confirmed Crown Castle is in active discussions with parties including SpaceX about master lease agreements.

  The comments come as the US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) has scheduled an auction of 160 megahertz of upper C-band spectrum by July 2027, a band expected to underpin the next phase of 5G network buildout. C-band spectrum sits in the 3.7–4.2 GHz range and is valued for its balance of coverage and capacity in wireless networks.

  "Historically, new spectrum deployment has been an important driver of network investment, and we believe the industry spectrum pipeline remains supportive of long-term infrastructure demand," Hillabrant said.

  ## Recent spectrum transactions cited as bullish signals

  Hillabrant pointed to three recent deals as evidence that carriers are committed to network investment: AT&T's $23 billion acquisition of 3.45 GHz and 600 MHz spectrum from EchoStar, Verizon's acquisition of AWS3 licenses (AWS3 refers to Advanced Wireless Services spectrum in the 1.7/2.1 GHz band), and SpaceX's $17 billion purchase of AWS4, H Block, and 800 MHz spectrum, the last of which came most recently from Grain Management.

  "These transactions demonstrate the commitments of the wireless industry to aggressively invest and to secure spectrum resources needed to support future terrestrial wireless network growth," Hillabrant said. "The recent spectrum transactions, albeit somewhat delayed in the AT&T acquisition of 600MHz, Verizon's acquisition of AWS3, and now SpaceX with the 800MHz — these are all positive catalysts for towers."

  On AT&T's deployment timeline for its EchoStar spectrum, Hillabrant said he expects the carrier to move faster than it did with its 600 MHz block. "The customer just paid $23 billion for the spectrum. It's highly valuable. I would think that they would look to deploy this as quickly as possible, given the value in the overall spectrum portfolio of what they've just acquired," he said.

  ## SpaceX as a potential fourth US carrier

  SpaceX's name featured prominently in the earnings call, reflecting its stated ambition to challenge AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile — the three dominant US mobile carriers — with a terrestrial wireless network to complement its Starlink satellite service. During SpaceX's own earnings call this week, founder Elon Musk said the company's satellite launches and spectrum acquisitions will "pave the way for Starlink Mobile to become a major mobile carrier in the US."

  Hillabrant said SpaceX's entry could benefit tower companies in two ways: directly, through tower leases to build out a terrestrial network, and indirectly, by pressuring incumbent carriers to accelerate their own network investment in response to a new competitor.

  When asked whether Crown Castle has entered master lease agreement talks with SpaceX or Amazon, Hillabrant confirmed discussions are underway. "I don't think I want to get into customer-specific detail, but you can assume that we're talking to all parties," he said.

  Hillabrant acknowledged the complexity of building a terrestrial mobile network — a fundamentally different undertaking from operating a satellite constellation — but said Crown Castle is positioned to help. "If SpaceX is serious, as it sounds like they are, in building a terrestrial network, I think we are a great partner for them in being able to help take a concept and turning it into reality of how to navigate through building a terrestrial network," he said.

  ## Edge compute trials cross 100 sites

  Crown Castle also updated investors on its edge computing initiative. The company has been working with Available Infrastructure, which is building a nationwide network of edge data centers, to deploy small-scale compute installations at existing tower sites. More than 100 sites have been deployed as part of a trial, with several concurrent trials also underway, Hillabrant said.

  Each installation runs between 0.2 and 0.5 megawatts (MW) — roughly the power draw of 200 to 500 average US homes — using existing power and broadband infrastructure at tower locations. Crown Castle said it has identified approximately 5,000 existing structures as candidates for such deployments.

  Hillabrant said Crown Castle has also identified 25 recently completed sites that together could support up to 100 MW of aggregate capacity. "While initial results appear promising, we continue to evaluate whether these Edge solutions will scale," he said.

  ## Q3 financials: Dish losses weigh on site rental revenue

  For the third quarter, Crown Castle reported site rental revenue of $966 million, down five percent from $1.01 billion in the same period a year earlier. The company attributed the decline to $61 million in lost revenue from Dish terminations and $5 million from Sprint cancellations.

  Dish had equipment on more than 12,000 Crown Castle sites, with contractual capacity to expand to 20,000. Crown Castle's legal action against Dish's parent company has been paused while the parties attempt to negotiate a settlement through court-ordered mediation, said Sunit Patel, executive vice president and chief financial officer. "There's no specific update to report on the progress of that yet," Patel told investors.

  Net income fell 12 percent to $283 million from $323 million year-on-year. Despite the revenue pressure, Crown Castle raised its full-year site rental revenue outlook by $20 million, to $3.835 billion.

  ## What it means

  Crown Castle's framing of SpaceX as both a direct tower customer and a competitive spur to incumbent carriers reflects a structural shift underway in US wireless. If SpaceX follows through on a terrestrial buildout, tower companies stand to gain a fourth major customer for the first time in years, replacing the revenue lost when Dish's network effort collapsed. The FCC's planned C-band auction in the first half of 2027 will be the next concrete test of whether carriers convert spectrum purchases into tower leases at the pace Crown Castle is projecting.

  ## Crown Castle點名頻譜資產管線與SpaceX地面野心，視為基地台需求驅動力

  Crown Castle執行長Chris Hillabrant本週在第三季法說會上表示，一波電信業者頻譜收購浪潮——包括SpaceX以170億美元購入AWS4、H Block及800MHz執照——將成為基地台基礎建設需求的重要長期催化劑，並確認Crown Castle正積極與包括SpaceX在內的多方洽談主租賃協議。

  此番言論正值美國聯邦通信委員會（FCC）計劃於2027年7月前完成上段C頻段160 MHz頻譜拍賣，該頻段被視為下一階段5G網路建設的核心基礎。C頻段涵蓋3.7至4.2 GHz範圍，因在覆蓋率與容量之間取得良好平衡，而備受無線網路業者青睞。

  Hillabrant表示：「歷史上，新頻譜的部署一直是網路投資的重要驅動力，我們認為產業頻譜資產管線對長期基礎建設需求仍具支撐。」

  ## 近期頻譜交易被視為多頭訊號

  Hillabrant引用三筆近期交易作為電信業者積極投入網路建設的佐證：AT&T以230億美元向EchoStar收購3.45 GHz及600 MHz頻譜、Verizon取得AWS3執照（AWS3指1.7／2.1 GHz頻段的先進無線服務頻譜），以及SpaceX以170億美元購入AWS4、H Block及800 MHz頻譜——最後一筆近期完成，來源為Grain Management。

  Hillabrant說：「這些交易展現了無線產業積極投資、確保未來地面無線網路成長所需頻譜資源的決心。近期頻譜交易——儘管AT&T收購600 MHz、Verizon收購AWS3有所延遲，如今SpaceX取得800 MHz——這些都是基地台的正面催化劑。」

  談及AT&T部署EchoStar頻譜的時程，Hillabrant預期AT&T的動作將比當年部署600 MHz頻段時更快。「這位客戶剛花了230億美元買下頻譜，極具價值。考量到他們剛剛取得的整體頻譜組合的價值，我認為他們會盡快著手部署，」他說。

  ## SpaceX：潛在的美國第四大電信業者

  SpaceX在此次法說會上被頻繁提及，反映出其宣稱要以地面無線網路挑戰AT&T、Verizon及T-Mobile——美國三大主流行動電信業者——作為Starlink衛星服務補充的野心。SpaceX本週同步舉行的法說會上，創辦人伊隆·馬斯克表示，公司的衛星發射與頻譜收購將「為Starlink Mobile成為美國主要行動電信業者鋪路」。

  Hillabrant表示，SpaceX的加入可從兩個面向為基地台公司帶來利益：直接面向是透過租用基地台建設地面網路；間接面向則是迫使現有業者為因應新競爭者而加快自身的網路投資。

  被問及Crown Castle是否已與SpaceX或亞馬遜展開主租賃協議談判，Hillabrant確認磋商正在進行。「我不想透露特定客戶的細節，但你可以假設我們正在與所有相關方洽談，」他說。

  Hillabrant承認建設地面行動網路極為複雜——本質上與營運衛星星座截然不同——但表示Crown Castle有能力提供協助。「如果SpaceX真如外界所聽到的那樣認真考慮建設地面網路，我認為我們將是他們的絕佳夥伴，能夠協助將概念化為現實，引導他們走過建設地面網路的每一個環節，」他說。

  ## 邊緣運算試點突破百站

  Crown Castle亦就其邊緣運算計畫向投資人進行了說明。公司與正在建設全國邊緣資料中心網路的Available Infrastructure合作，在既有基地台站點部署小型運算設施。Hillabrant表示，試點部署已超過100個站點，同時還有數個平行試驗正在進行。

  每個安裝設施的用電量介於0.2至0.5百萬瓦（MW）之間——大約相當於200至500戶美國普通家庭的用電量——並利用基地台站點既有的電力與寬頻基礎建設。Crown Castle表示，已從現有設施中篩選出約5,000個候選站點供部署之用。

  Hillabrant表示，Crown Castle還識別出25個近期完工站點，合計可支援高達100 MW的總容量。「雖然初步結果看來前景可期，我們仍在持續評估這些邊緣方案是否具備規模化潛力，」他說。

  ## 第三季財務：Dish損失拖累站點租賃營收

  Crown Castle第三季站點租賃營收報9.66億美元，較去年同期的10.1億美元下滑5%。公司將這一降幅歸因於Dish終止合約造成6,100萬美元的營收損失，以及Sprint取消合約帶來的500萬美元損失。

  Dish在Crown Castle逾12,000個站點設有設備，合約容量可擴展至20,000個。Crown Castle執行副總裁兼財務長Sunit Patel表示，公司對Dish母公司提起的法律訴訟已暫停，雙方正透過法院指定的調解程序嘗試達成和解。「目前尚無進展的具體更新可以報告，」Patel告訴投資人。

  淨利年減12%，由3.23億美元降至2.83億美元。儘管面臨營收壓力，Crown Castle仍將全年站點租賃營收預測上調2,000萬美元，至38.35億美元。

  ## 市場意涵

  Crown Castle將SpaceX定位為既是直接基地台客戶、又是刺激現有業者的競爭壓力來源，反映出美國無線產業正在發生的結構性轉變。若SpaceX真的推進地面網路建設，基地台公司將有望多年來首度迎來第四大主要客戶，以填補Dish網路計畫破局所留下的營收缺口。FCC計劃於2027年上半年舉行的C頻段拍賣，將是驗證電信業者能否按Crown Castle預期的步調，將頻譜收購轉化為基地台租賃的下一個具體試金石。
