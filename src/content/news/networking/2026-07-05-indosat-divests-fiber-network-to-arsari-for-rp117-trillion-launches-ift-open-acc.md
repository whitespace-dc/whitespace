body_en: |
  PT Indosat Tbk. has completed the divestiture of its fiber-optic network assets to Arsari Group, raising gross proceeds of approximately Rp11.7 trillion and simultaneously launching PT Infra Fiber Teknologi (IFT) as an independent open-access wholesale carrier managing more than 86,000 kilometers of cable across Indonesia.

  The transaction closes an investment agreement signed in December 2025. Under the deal structure, Indosat and its subsidiary PT Aplikanusa Lintasarta transferred their shareholdings in IFT to PT Nusantara Fiber Teknologi (NFT), Arsari Group's investment platform. Indosat and Lintasarta collectively retain a 49.9% stake in NFT, preserving strategic exposure to the fiber infrastructure while converting the assets to cash.

  ## Proceeds earmarked for 5G and AI services

  Indosat said the Rp11.7 trillion in gross proceeds will be allocated to its core mobile business, 5G network expansion, and digital services oriented toward artificial intelligence applications. President Director and Chief Executive Officer Vikram Sinha said the company's goal of empowering Indonesian society through technology had not changed, and that IFT's structure is intended to ensure the benefits of AI, cloud computing, and next-generation digital services reach a broader population.

  ## IFT's network and open-access model

  IFT will operate more than 86,000 kilometers of fiber covering backbone routes, domestic subsea cables — optical cable linking Indonesia's islands — and last-mile access networks. The company's model is open-access wholesale: it sells transmission capacity to telecom operators, enterprises, hyperscalers (large cloud and internet platform companies), and digital service providers, with no direct retail consumer business. Hendry Syam serves as IFT's president director.

  Approximately 45% of IFT's network lies within Java, Indonesia's most densely populated island; 55% extends across Sumatra, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, and eastern Indonesia. Syam said the company's mandate extends beyond established commercial zones to reach areas that have historically been underserved, serving communities and businesses that drive the national economy.

  IFT's wholesale service portfolio includes submarine fiber capacity suited to hyperscalers and international corporations, and inland terrestrial fiber covering both dark fiber — raw cable capacity leased to operators — and overland backbone routes connecting cities.

  ## Competition with Telkom's Infranexia

  IFT's launch places it alongside Infranexia, the wholesale fiber entity spun out of state-owned Telkom Indonesia through a gradual asset separation process. Infranexia controls more than 170,000 kilometers of fiber — roughly twice IFT's network — and is 99.9% owned by Telkom Indonesia. Infranexia's coverage extends to metropolitan centers, dense residential areas, regional backbone corridors, and remote 3T areas.

  IFT focuses its network on high-traffic urban corridors, business district connections, data-center zones, and domestic subsea routes linking emerging digital-economy hubs, according to the companies.

  The two platforms' service portfolios overlap but diverge at the margins. Infranexia offers virtual unbundled local access (VULA), which allows third-party internet service providers (ISPs) to extend coverage to end users within Telkom's metro Ethernet footprint without building new cable; bitstream services providing layer-2 and layer-3 bandwidth capacity; and fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) last-mile infrastructure for lease to partner ISPs. IFT's portfolio includes comparable wholesale products alongside subsea capacity and dark-fiber terrestrial offerings targeting hyperscalers and international operators.

  The two companies also differ in ownership structure and governance. Infranexia operates as a direct Telkom Indonesia subsidiary under the AKHLAK values framework applied to Indonesian state-owned enterprises. IFT is a private joint venture aligned to an RSSP framework — Reliability, Scalability, Security, Partnership — reflecting its origins as a private-sector partnership.

  ## Market implications

  The simultaneous emergence of two large independent wholesale fiber platforms is projected to alter capital expenditure patterns for mobile operators and ISPs in Indonesia, according to the companies, by offering low-latency transmission capacity without requiring operators to build and own physical cable infrastructure.

  Deputy CEO and COO of Arsari Group Aryo P.S. Djojohadikusumo said IFT's leadership brings both operational expertise in large-scale infrastructure and an understanding of why that infrastructure matters to connected communities and empowered businesses, as well as to Indonesia's position in the future digital economy.

  With a majority of its network outside Java and a wholesale open-access model, IFT is positioned to expand partnerships with operators, enterprises, and hyperscalers seeking high-capacity connectivity in regions where investment has historically lagged the metropolitan core.
body_zh: |
  PT印多薩特公司（PT Indosat Tbk.）已完成將旗下光纖網路資產出售予阿薩里集團（Arsari Group）的交易，獲得約11.7兆印尼盾的總收益，並同步成立PT Infra Fiber Teknologi（IFT）作為獨立的開放式批發電信業者，在印尼境內管理逾8萬6千公里的光纖纜線。

  此次交易完成了一份於2025年12月簽署的投資協議。依照交易架構，印多薩特及其子公司PT Aplikanusa Lintasarta將各自持有的IFT股份，轉讓予阿薩里集團的投資平台——PT Nusantara Fiber Teknologi（NFT）。印多薩特與Lintasarta合計保留NFT 49.9%的股份，在將資產轉換為現金的同時，仍維持對光纖基礎設施的策略性參與。

  ## 收益用於5G與AI服務

  印多薩特表示，這筆11.7兆印尼盾的總收益將挹注於核心行動通訊業務、5G網路擴建，以及面向人工智慧應用的數位服務。總裁暨執行長Vikram Sinha表示，公司透過科技賦能印尼社會的目標始終如一，IFT的架構旨在確保AI、雲端運算及次世代數位服務的紅利，能夠惠及更廣大的民眾。

  ## IFT網路與開放接取模式

  IFT將運營逾8萬6千公里的光纖，涵蓋骨幹路由、國內海底纜線——即連接印尼各島嶼的光纜——以及最後一哩接取網路。該公司採取開放式批發接取模式：向電信業者、企業、超大型雲端服務商（即大型雲端與網際網路平台業者），以及數位服務提供商出售傳輸容量，不直接從事零售消費者業務。IFT總裁董事由Hendry Syam擔任。

  IFT約45%的網路位於爪哇島——印尼人口最稠密的島嶼；另55%則延伸至蘇門答臘、加里曼丹、蘇拉威西及印尼東部地區。Syam表示，公司的使命不僅限於已建立的商業區域，更要延伸至歷來服務不足的地區，服務推動國家經濟的社區與企業。

  IFT的批發服務組合包含適合超大型雲端服務商與國際企業的海底光纖容量，以及涵蓋暗光纖——即出租給業者的原始纜線容量——與連接各城市的陸上骨幹路由在內的內陸地面光纖。

  ## 與Telkom旗下Infranexia的競爭

  IFT的成立，使其與Infranexia並肩競爭——後者是國有電信公司印尼電信（Telkom Indonesia）透過逐步資產分離程序所分拆出的批發光纖事業體。Infranexia掌管逾17萬公里的光纖——約為IFT網路的兩倍——且99.9%股份由印尼電信持有。Infranexia的覆蓋範圍延伸至大都會中心、高密度住宅區、區域骨幹廊道，以及偏遠的3T地區。

  依據兩家公司的說明，IFT將網路重心置於高流量城市廊道、商業區連線、資料中心區域，以及連接新興數位經濟樞紐的國內海底路由。

  兩大平台的服務組合有所重疊，但在邊際範圍有所差異。Infranexia提供虛擬解綁本地接取（VULA）服務，讓第三方網際網路服務提供商（ISP）得以在不新建纜線的情況下，於印尼電信都會乙太網路覆蓋範圍內延伸服務至終端用戶；另提供位元流服務（bitstream services），供應第二層與第三層頻寬容量；以及供合作ISP租用的光纖到府（FTTH）最後一哩基礎設施。IFT的服務組合涵蓋類似的批發產品，並附加面向超大型雲端服務商及國際業者的海纜容量，以及以暗光纖與陸上地面路由為主的地面服務。

  兩家公司在所有權結構與治理模式上亦有所不同。Infranexia作為印尼電信的直屬子公司，依循適用於印尼國有企業的AKHLAK價值體系運作。IFT則是一家私人合資企業，遵循RSSP框架——即可靠性（Reliability）、擴展性（Scalability）、安全性（Security）、夥伴關係（Partnership）——反映其作為民間部門合作關係的起源。

  ## 市場影響

  依據兩家公司的說明，兩大獨立批發光纖平台同步崛起，預計將改變印尼行動業者與ISP的資本支出模式——透過提供低延遲傳輸容量，讓業者無需自行建設並持有實體纜線基礎設施。

  阿薩里集團副執行長暨營運長Aryo P.S. Djojohadikusumo表示，IFT的領導層兼具大型基礎設施的運營專業，以及對這些基礎設施為互聯社區和賦權企業所具意義的深刻理解，亦著眼於印尼在未來數位經濟中的定位。

  IFT逾半數網路位於爪哇島以外，並採行批發開放接取模式，使其具備條件，在歷來投資落後於大都會核心的地區，與尋求高容量連線的業者、企業及超大型雲端服務商拓展合作夥伴關係。
