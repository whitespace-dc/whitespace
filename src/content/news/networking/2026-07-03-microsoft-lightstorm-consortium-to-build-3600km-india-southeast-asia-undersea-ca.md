body_en: |
  A six-member consortium led by Singapore-based telecom startup Lightstorm and including Microsoft will build a 3,600-kilometre submarine cable connecting India, Malaysia and Singapore, the companies said Thursday, targeting a fourth-quarter 2029 launch to serve artificial intelligence, cloud and hyperscale workloads.

  The cable, named I-2SEA, also includes Tata Communications, Singapore Telecommunications, Singapore's ASEAN Cableship and Japan's NEC Corporation. No investment figure was disclosed.

  ## Landing site and project scope

  Landing stations will be built in Machilipatnam, in the southern Indian state of Andhra Pradesh — a location where Meta and Alphabet have separately announced data centres. Lightstorm Group chief executive and managing director Amajit Gupta told Reuters the cable is expected to be operational in the fourth quarter of 2029.

  ## Lightstorm's network expansion

  Lightstorm, backed by private equity firm I Squared Capital, currently connects 19 AI and cloud zones across India via terrestrial fibre networks. Gupta said the I-2SEA cable is expected to expand that count to 29 zones, extending the company's reach from inland Indian connectivity into international submarine infrastructure.

  Separately, Gupta confirmed Lightstorm plans to list in India in mid-2027, without disclosing further details. The company was reportedly seeking a valuation of up to $1.5 billion as of March.

  ## India's submarine cable landscape

  India currently has 17 active submarine cables with a maximum potential capacity of 960 terabits per second — a measure of total data-carrying ability — with at least 10 more cables publicly announced, according to data provider TeleGeography. Submarine cables carry roughly 95% of the world's internet traffic.

  ## Data centre demand as backdrop

  India's data centre capacity is expanding rapidly, providing the demand that new cable infrastructure is built to serve. Macquarie Equity Research estimated last October that the country's operational capacity of 1.4 gigawatts — roughly the continuous output of a large power plant — could double by 2027 based on projects already under construction, and increase five-fold by 2030 if planned developments are fast-tracked.

  ## What this means for the region

  The I-2SEA announcement adds to a growing pipeline of submarine cable projects targeting India as AI-driven investment in cloud infrastructure accelerates across the country. The choice of Machilipatnam as a landing point — already selected by Meta and Alphabet for data centre facilities — reinforces the site's emergence as a node for international connectivity on India's southern coast.

  Lightstorm's planned mid-2027 stock market listing in India is expected to follow the cable's construction phase, with an operational target date of the fourth quarter of 2029.
body_zh: |
  以新加坡電信新創公司Lightstorm為首、微軟參與其中的六成員聯盟，將建設一條全長3,600公里的海底電纜，連結印度、馬來西亞與新加坡。各公司週四宣布這項計畫，目標於2029年第四季正式啟用，以承載人工智慧、雲端及超大規模工作負載。

  這條電纜命名為I-2SEA，成員還包括塔塔通訊（Tata Communications）、新加坡電信（Singapore Telecommunications）、新加坡ASEAN Cableship及日本NEC公司。各方均未透露投資金額。

  ## 登陸站與專案範疇

  登陸站將設於印度南部安得拉邦的馬基利帕特南（Machilipatnam）——Meta與Alphabet也分別在此宣布設立資料中心。Lightstorm集團執行長暨董事總經理阿馬吉特·古普塔（Amajit Gupta）向路透社表示，這條電纜預計於2029年第四季正式投入運作。

  ## Lightstorm的網路擴張

  受私募股權公司I Squared Capital支持的Lightstorm，目前透過陸上光纖網路連結印度境內19個人工智慧與雲端區域。古普塔表示，I-2SEA電纜預計可將這一數字擴展至29個區域，使該公司的業務版圖從印度內陸連線延伸至國際海底基礎設施。

  古普塔另外確認，Lightstorm計畫於2027年中在印度掛牌上市，但未透露進一步細節。據報導，該公司截至今年3月尋求的估值最高可達15億美元。

  ## 印度海底電纜概況

  根據資料供應商TeleGeography的數據，印度目前有17條運作中的海底電纜，最大潛在容量達每秒960兆位元（Terabit），反映其總傳輸能力；此外至少還有10條電纜已公開宣布建設計畫。海底電纜承載了全球約95%的網際網路流量。

  ## 資料中心需求為背景

  印度的資料中心容量正快速擴張，為新建的電纜基礎設施提供了源源不絕的需求動能。麥格理股票研究（Macquarie Equity Research）去年10月估計，印度目前1.4吉瓦（GW）的運作容量——大致相當於一座大型電廠的持續輸出——在已動工建設的專案帶動下，有望於2027年翻倍；若規劃中的建設案加速推進，更可能在2030年前擴大至五倍。

  ## 對區域的意義

  I-2SEA的宣布，為日益壯大的海底電纜專案陣容再添一員，這些專案均以印度為目標，背景正是人工智慧帶動的雲端基礎設施投資在全印度加速升溫。以馬基利帕特南作為登陸地點——Meta與Alphabet均已選定此地設立資料中心設施——更進一步確立了印度南岸這處據點作為國際連線節點的地位。

  Lightstorm計畫於2027年中在印度掛牌上市，預計緊接電纜建設階段之後，而電纜正式啟用的目標日期則訂於2029年第四季。
