  ## Wi-Fi 8 reframes the upgrade proposition

  Qualcomm says Wi-Fi 8 marks a departure from the speed-first framing of Wi-Fi 6 and Wi-Fi 7, positioning the new generation around reliability, coverage, and low latency as agentic AI — systems in which multiple AI agents operate simultaneously to complete tasks — begins to reshape what wireless networks must deliver. The company's senior vice president and general manager of connectivity, broadband and networking, Gautam Sheoran, and vice president and general manager of wireless infrastructure and networking, Ganesh Swaminathan, outlined that view in an interview with TechNews.

  Swaminathan said Wi-Fi 8's core technical identity is "ultra-high reliability," a design target that encompasses wider coverage range, extremely low latency under AI workloads, consistent performance across all locations, and reduced power consumption. The industry is now framing network requirements in terms of AI workload management and traffic prioritization rather than raw throughput figures, he said, a shift that makes headline speed specifications less meaningful than they were in previous cycles.

  Wi-Fi generations have followed an upgrade cadence of roughly three to four years, and the first Wi-Fi 8 products are beginning to reach the market in 2026.

  ## New technical features in the standard

  Wi-Fi 8 introduces several technologies aimed at the reliability and coverage goals Qualcomm described:

  - **ELR (Enhanced Long Range):** extends transmission distance
  - **dRU (Distributed Resource Units):** distributes spectrum resource allocation across the network
  - **SMD Roaming (Single Mobility Domain Roaming):** enables seamless handoff across access points within a unified mobility domain

  Swaminathan said these features are intended to benefit not only new Wi-Fi 8 devices but also legacy equipment on existing infrastructure, which he described as a deliberate design priority for the generation.

  ## Qualcomm's hardware positioning

  Sheoran said most devices on the market today, including smartphones, continue to ship with 2x2 Wi-Fi antenna configurations. Qualcomm said it is the only terminal chip supplier currently offering 4x4 Wi-Fi capability at the device level, which it says translates to better performance at the edge of coverage and fuller use of 4x4 MIMO (multiple-input, multiple-output) technology — a technique in which multiple antennas transmit and receive simultaneously to increase throughput and link quality.

  On the access point side, Qualcomm has introduced a 5x5 architecture that adds one additional radio-frequency chain and antenna beyond a standard 4x4 configuration. Sheoran said this is intended to improve performance for older-generation connected devices while moving the overall Wi-Fi experience closer to what cellular networks deliver in terms of consistency and mobility.

  The company said both its device chips and its access point chips incorporate AI processing, applied through what it calls Connectivity AI — technology it says improves signal quality and connection performance between endpoints and infrastructure.

  ## Agentic AI changes traffic patterns

  Swaminathan said the arrival of agentic AI is expected to produce network traffic patterns that differ substantially from current norms. Multiple agents running in parallel will generate burst connection events and high-frequency interactive sessions, and will place heavier demand on uplink capacity — traffic flowing from the device to the network — than current applications do. Meeting those demands will require more efficient use of available spectrum, improvements to air link interface efficiency, and lower latency, he said.

  To address those requirements, Qualcomm said it has developed its own DSO Pro software, which it says resolves the uplink efficiency and latency challenges the agentic AI traffic model creates. The company said DSO Pro is compatible with older-generation devices as well as new ones.

  ## Significance for network planners

  The shift Qualcomm is describing has direct implications for how enterprises and infrastructure operators should evaluate Wi-Fi 8 deployments. If AI workload traffic prioritization and uplink performance replace peak download speed as the primary evaluation criteria, procurement decisions and network design will need to account for parameters that prior-generation assessments largely ignored, including per-flow latency guarantees and behavior under concurrent AI agent sessions.

  Swaminathan also said Wi-Fi is moving toward cellular-grade infrastructure capability, and that the goal is for users to receive consistent, uninterrupted connectivity regardless of whether they are on a cellular or Wi-Fi link.

  ## Looking toward Wi-Fi 9

  Sheoran said Qualcomm's engineering teams are already working on Wi-Fi 9. He said the next generation is expected to focus on increasing total system capacity and is examining the potential incorporation of the 60 GHz frequency band, which offers very high bandwidth over short distances, to support the growing volume of device-to-device communication that agentic AI applications will require. Swaminathan added that millimeter wave technology — a category of very high-frequency radio transmission capable of carrying large amounts of data — is another direction the industry may pursue for future Wi-Fi generations.

  Both executives said Qualcomm is working toward what they called "AI Native Wi-Fi," a framework that would embed AI-driven optimization throughout the wireless stack, requiring greater bandwidth and more flexible spectrum use to move data rapidly between network nodes. Sheoran said the priority for now is the commercial rollout of Wi-Fi 8 products.

  ## Wi-Fi 8重新定義升級的意義

  高通表示，Wi-Fi 8標誌著與Wi-Fi 6、Wi-Fi 7「速度優先」訴求的決定性轉變，將這一新世代定位在可靠性、覆蓋範圍與低延遲上——尤其是隨著代理式AI（由多個AI代理同時協作、完成任務的系統）逐漸改變無線網路所必須承擔的工作，這一定位更顯關鍵。高通連接、寬頻與網路部門資深副總裁暨總經理Gautam Sheoran，以及無線基礎架構與網路部門副總裁暨總經理Ganesh Swaminathan，在接受《TechNews》專訪時共同闡述了這一觀點。

  Swaminathan表示，Wi-Fi 8的核心技術特色是「超高可靠性」，這一設計目標涵蓋更廣的覆蓋範圍、在AI工作負載下的極低延遲、全場域的一致性效能，以及更低的耗電量。他說，業界現在正以AI工作負載管理與流量優先順序作為網路需求的衡量框架，而非原始傳輸速率數字——這一轉變使得標題速度規格的意義，遠不如前幾個世代那般重要。

  Wi-Fi世代的升級週期約為三至四年，首批Wi-Fi 8產品已於2026年陸續問世。

  ## 標準引入的新技術功能

  Wi-Fi 8引入了數項技術，以實現高通所描述的可靠性與覆蓋目標：

  - **ELR（增強長距離）：** 延伸無線傳輸距離
  - **dRU（分散式資源單元）：** 將頻譜資源配置分散至整個網路
  - **SMD漫遊（單一行動網域漫遊）：** 在統一行動網域內的存取點之間實現無縫切換

  Swaminathan表示，這些功能不僅針對新的Wi-Fi 8裝置，也讓現有基礎架構上的舊世代設備一併受惠——他說這是這個世代刻意優先考量的設計方向。

  ## 高通的硬體定位

  Sheoran表示，目前市面上絕大多數裝置，包括智慧型手機，仍搭載2x2 Wi-Fi天線配置。高通表示，目前在終端晶片廠商中，它是唯一能在裝置端提供4x4 Wi-Fi能力的業者，並說明這代表在覆蓋邊緣有更佳的效能，以及對4x4 MIMO技術的更充分運用——MIMO（多輸入多輸出）是指多支天線同時收發資料，藉以提升傳輸量與連線品質的技術。

  在存取點方面，高通推出了5x5架構，在標準4x4配置的基礎上，額外增加一條射頻鏈路與一支天線。Sheoran說，此設計旨在改善舊世代連線裝置的效能，同時讓整體Wi-Fi體驗在一致性與移動性上，更接近行動通訊網路所能提供的水準。

  高通表示，其裝置晶片與存取點晶片均內建AI處理能力，並透過其所稱的「Connectivity AI」技術加以實現——該公司表示，這項技術能改善終端裝置與網路基礎架構之間的訊號品質與連線效能。

  ## 代理式AI改變流量型態

  Swaminathan表示，代理式AI的來臨，預計將產生與現行規範大相逕庭的網路流量型態。多個代理同時並行運作，將帶來突發性連線事件與高頻率的互動工作階段，對上行容量——即從裝置端流向網路的流量——所產生的需求，也將遠高於現有應用。他說，要因應這些需求，必須更有效率地運用可用頻譜、提升無線鏈路介面效率，並進一步降低延遲。

  為此，高通表示已自行研發DSO Pro軟體，並說這款軟體能解決代理式AI流量模型所帶來的上行效率與延遲挑戰。高通表示，DSO Pro與舊世代及新世代裝置皆相容。

  ## 對網路規劃者的意涵

  高通所描述的這一轉變，對企業與基礎架構業者評估Wi-Fi 8部署方式具有直接影響。若AI工作負載流量優先順序與上行效能取代峰值下載速度，成為主要評估標準，採購決策與網路設計便必須納入前幾世代評估時幾乎未曾考量的參數，包括單一流量的延遲保證，以及在多個AI代理工作階段並行時的網路行為表現。

  Swaminathan也表示，Wi-Fi正朝向電信等級的基礎架構能力演進，目標是讓用戶無論身處行動通訊或Wi-Fi網路，都能獲得一致且不中斷的連線體驗。

  ## 展望Wi-Fi 9

  Sheoran表示，高通的工程團隊已著手研發Wi-Fi 9。他說，下一世代預計將聚焦於提升整體系統容量，並正在研究納入60 GHz頻段的可能性——該頻段能在短距離內提供極高頻寬，以支援代理式AI應用所帶來的大量裝置間通訊需求。Swaminathan補充，毫米波技術——一種能承載大量資料的超高頻無線傳輸技術——也是業界在未來Wi-Fi世代可能探索的另一方向。

  兩位主管均表示，高通正致力於實現他們所稱的「AI原生Wi-Fi」（AI Native Wi-Fi）框架——這一框架將把AI驅動的最佳化機制嵌入無線技術棧的各個層面，需要更大的頻寬與更靈活的頻譜運用，以在網路節點之間快速傳輸資料。Sheoran表示，目前的優先要務，是推動Wi-Fi 8產品的商業化上市。
