body_en: |
  NVIDIA's Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics switch, which integrates optical transceivers directly into the chip package using co-packaged optics (CPO) technology, has entered mass production, with Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), ASE Technology Holding's Siliconware Precision Industries subsidiary, and Foxconn taking the primary manufacturing roles, Commercial Times reported.

  NVIDIA presented the production milestone at Hot Chips 2026, where it detailed performance characteristics and a new architecture designed for large AI cluster deployments.

  ## Performance claims

  NVIDIA said Spectrum-X Ethernet Photonics delivers five times the power efficiency of networks built with conventional pluggable optical modules (removable transceivers that insert at the board edge), five times longer AI application continuous uptime, and 1.3 times faster data center deployment, according to Commercial Times.

  A companion variant, Spectrum-X Multiplane, splits each server's network connection into multiple independent planes, each operating its own two-tier network fabric. The design allows an AI factory to scale to 512,000 GPUs without adding a third network tier. Under an eight-plane configuration, a single plane failure preserves approximately 90 percent of total bandwidth, and hardware-level failure recovery runs 11 times faster than software-based load balancing, the paper reported.

  ## Taiwan supply chain division

  TSMC manufactures the silicon photonics components, integrating the photonic integrated circuit (PIC, which routes optical signals on-chip), the electronic integrated circuit (EIC, which drives the PIC), and TSMC's advanced packaging platform, Commercial Times said. Siliconware handles chip-level packaging, assembly, and testing. Foxconn assembles the switch into a rack-mountable network system for data center installation.

  As NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform ramps to volume, AI server manufacturers Quanta Cloud Technology (QCT), Wistron, Wiwynn, and Inventec are positioned to take on silicon photonics switch integration, SuperNIC (a high-performance network adapter), and rack-level system work, the paper said.

  ## Optical components and test equipment

  CPO concentrates critical optical functions in on-package components: fiber array units (FAUs, which align fiber arrays to the photonic chip), external lasers, microlenses, fiber-coupling elements, and active alignment systems. Analysts cited by Commercial Times identified Taiwanese suppliers active in fiber array, FAU, and optical assembly technologies, including Largan Precision, as companies likely to draw investor attention.

  Testing is a central constraint on CPO production yields. Inspection must shift from the finished-product stage upstream to wafer and bare-die stages, covering optical power, wavelength, insertion loss (signal attenuation through the component), coupling efficiency, signal integrity, bit error rate, and high-low temperature and long-duration reliability verification, the paper reported. Companies active in high-speed connectivity testing and test equipment were also named by analysts as expected beneficiaries.

  ## Industry significance

  Analysts quoted by Commercial Times described the Spectrum-X silicon photonics switch entering full mass production as the point at which CPO technology transitions from demonstration to commercial deployment. Taiwan's electronics supply chain value, they said, is extending beyond component manufacturing into silicon photonics fabrication, precision optical assembly, advanced packaging and test, and rack-level system integration.
body_zh: |
  輝達（NVIDIA）Spectrum-X乙太網路光子交換器採用共封裝光學（CPO）技術，將光學收發器直接整合至晶片封裝內，如今已正式進入量產。《工商時報》報導，台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）、日月光投資控股旗下矽品精密工業，以及鴻海，分別在此次量產中擔任主要製造角色。

  輝達在Hot Chips 2026研討會上正式揭示這項量產里程碑，並詳細說明效能特性，以及專為大型AI叢集部署所設計的全新架構。

  ## 效能優勢

  《工商時報》報導，輝達表示，Spectrum-X乙太網路光子交換器的用電效率，是採用傳統可插拔光模組（即插入電路板邊緣的可拆卸收發器）網路的五倍，AI應用的連續正常運作時間延長五倍，資料中心建置速度則加快1.3倍。

  另一款衍生產品Spectrum-X多平面（Multiplane）版本，將每台伺服器的網路連線分割成多個獨立平面，每個平面各自運行兩層網路架構。此設計讓AI工廠無需增加第三層網路架構，即可擴充至512,000顆GPU。在八平面配置下，單一平面故障仍可保留約九成總頻寬；硬體層級的故障恢復速度比軟體式負載平衡快11倍，同報導指出。

  ## 台灣供應鏈分工

  《工商時報》報導，台積電負責製造矽光子元件，整合光子積體電路（PIC，負責在晶片上傳導光訊號）、電子積體電路（EIC，負責驅動PIC），以及台積電的先進封裝平台。矽品精密負責晶片級封裝、組裝與測試；鴻海則將交換器組裝成可機架安裝的網路系統，供資料中心安裝使用。

  隨著輝達Vera Rubin平台進入量產，AI伺服器製造商廣達雲端科技（QCT）、緯創、緯穎及英業達，皆已就位，準備承接矽光子交換器整合、SuperNIC（高效能網路介面卡）及機架級系統業務，同報導指出。

  ## 光學元件與測試設備

  CPO技術將關鍵光學功能集中於封裝內元件，涵蓋光纖陣列單元（FAU，負責將光纖陣列對準光子晶片）、外部雷射光源、微透鏡、光纖耦合元件及主動對準系統。《工商時報》引述分析師指出，台灣供應商在光纖陣列、FAU及光學組裝技術上表現活躍，包括大立光（Largan Precision）在內的企業，預計將引發投資人高度關注。

  測試是影響CPO生產良率的核心限制環節。檢測作業必須從成品階段上移至晶圓與裸晶階段，涵蓋光功率、波長、插入損耗（訊號通過元件的衰減程度）、耦合效率、訊號完整性、位元錯誤率，以及高低溫與長時間可靠性驗證，同報導指出。分析師亦點名從事高速連接測試及測試設備業務的企業，預期同樣是受惠族群。

  ## 產業意義

  《工商時報》引述分析師表示，Spectrum-X矽光子交換器正式進入全面量產，標誌著CPO技術從展示驗證跨入商業部署的關鍵轉折點。他們指出，台灣電子供應鏈創造的附加價值，已從元件製造進一步延伸至矽光子製程、精密光學組裝、先進封裝與測試，以及機架級系統整合等多個高價值環節。
