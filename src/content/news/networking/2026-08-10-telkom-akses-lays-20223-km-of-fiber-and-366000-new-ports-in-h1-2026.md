body_en: |
  PT Telkom Akses, the network access infrastructure subsidiary of state-owned PT Telkom Indonesia (Persero), deployed 20,223 km of fiber optic cable and added 366,000 new access ports across Indonesia in the first half of 2026, the company announced on August 7. The company also completed 3,417 km of tower fiberization — the process of replacing or supplementing wireless backhaul links to mobile towers with fiber optic connections — during the same period.

  The figures represent Telkom Akses's contribution to the broader TelkomGroup network, which provides fixed broadband, business, and public-sector connectivity across the Indonesian archipelago.

  ## Service performance against targets

  Alongside the infrastructure build-out, Telkom Akses reported that three operational service metrics exceeded their internal targets in H1 2026:

  - **Time to Install (TTI) within 3x24 hours** — the share of new service activations completed within a 72-hour window — came in at 110.4% of target.
  - **Fulfillment Guarantee (FFG)**, a measure of order completion reliability, reached 100.1% of target.
  - **Time to Repair (TTR)**, tracking fault resolution speed, reached 127.9% of target.

  The company attributed the performance gains to what it described as four transformation pillars: strengthening business processes and governance, accelerating operational digitalization through data-driven technology, optimizing resource and materials management, and applying operational excellence standards across all processes.

  ## Strategic alignment

  CEO Irphan Wijaya said the H1 results reflect consistent execution of the company's ongoing transformation program. "This H1 2026 achievement is the result of the consistent transformation carried out by all Telkom Akses Warriors to deliver increasingly reliable and quality services," Wijaya said in a press release received by Kompas.com.

  Telkom Akses said its expansion program is aligned with the parent group's TLKM 30 transformation initiative, specifically the operational and service excellence pillar, which targets improved work culture, business process efficiency, and service quality across all TelkomGroup entities.

  The company also stated that its infrastructure buildout supports commitments made by Danantara Indonesia — the state-owned holding entity — to strengthen the foundation of the national digital economy.

  ## What comes next

  Entering the second half of 2026, Telkom Akses said it plans to continue network access expansion, accelerate fiberization, improve service quality, and deepen collaboration within TelkomGroup. The company also said it is preparing for future connectivity demand through network capacity increases, workforce development, and strengthening occupational safety governance across its operations.

  PT Telkom Akses 是印尼國營企業 PT Telkom Indonesia（Persero）旗下負責網路接取基礎建設的子公司，該公司於8月7日宣布，2026年上半年在印尼全境共鋪設20,223公里光纖電纜，並新增36萬6,000個接取埠。同期間，公司亦完成了3,417公里的基地台光纖化工程——即以光纖連線取代或補強行動基地台原有的無線傳輸回程連結。

  上述成果代表 Telkom Akses 對 TelkomGroup 整體網路建設的貢獻。TelkomGroup 的網路服務涵蓋印尼群島各地的固定寬頻、企業及公共部門連線服務。

  ## 服務績效與達標情形

  在基礎建設擴張的同時，Telkom Akses 也報告了三項運營服務指標均超越內部目標：

  - **3×24小時安裝時效（TTI）**——即在72小時內完成新服務開通的比例——達成率為目標的110.4%。
  - **履單保證（FFG）**——衡量訂單完成可靠度的指標——達成率為目標的100.1%。
  - **故障維修時效（TTR）**——追蹤故障排除速度的指標——達成率為目標的127.9%。

  公司將上述績效提升歸功於四大轉型支柱：強化業務流程與公司治理、透過數據驅動技術加速運營數位化、優化資源與物料管理，以及在所有流程中落實卓越運營標準。

  ## 策略方向

  執行長 Irphan Wijaya 表示，上半年成果反映了公司持續推進轉型計畫的執行力。Wijaya 在一份由 Kompas.com 取得的新聞稿中表示：「2026年上半年的成就，是全體 Telkom Akses Warriors 落實一致轉型、提供日益可靠且優質服務的成果。」

  Telkom Akses 指出，公司的擴張計畫與母集團 TLKM 30 轉型計畫高度契合，尤其著重於運營與服務卓越支柱，目標在於提升 TelkomGroup 旗下所有實體的工作文化、業務流程效率及服務品質。

  公司同時聲明，其基礎建設擴張亦支持國家控股機構 Danantara Indonesia 的相關承諾，旨在強化印尼國家數位經濟的根基。

  ## 下半年展望

  進入2026年下半年，Telkom Akses 表示將持續推進網路接取擴張、加速光纖化進程、提升服務品質，並深化與 TelkomGroup 內部各單位的協作。公司也表示，正透過擴充網路容量、培育工作人才，以及強化各項業務的職業安全治理，積極為未來連線需求的持續成長做好準備。
