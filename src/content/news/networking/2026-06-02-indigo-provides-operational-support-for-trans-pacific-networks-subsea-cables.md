# Indigo Provides Operational Support for Trans Pacific Networks' Subsea Cables

Indigo will provide operational support to Trans Pacific Networks for subsea cables serving the Asia-Pacific to US route. The partnership enhances operational reliability and efficiency for trans-Pacific cable infrastructure.

## 中文

# Indigo 為 Trans Pacific Networks 提供海底電纜營運支援

Indigo 將為 Trans Pacific Networks 提供海底電纜營運支援，服務亞太至美國的跨太平洋路線。此合作將提升跨太平洋海底電纜基礎設施的營運可靠性與效率。
