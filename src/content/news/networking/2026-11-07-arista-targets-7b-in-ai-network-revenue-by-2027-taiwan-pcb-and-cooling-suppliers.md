body_en: |
  Arista Networks has set a target to double its AI networking revenue to $7 billion by 2027, a goal that, according to Commercial Times, positions a group of Taiwanese makers of printed circuit boards, copper-clad laminates, liquid cooling modules, and mechanical components to benefit from the resulting supply chain demand.

  Arista posted third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, up 20.5% from the prior quarter and 58.5% year on year, beating market expectations, the report said. The company attributed the performance to demand from both AI and enterprise customers. Arista has also set a full-year 2027 revenue target of $18.5 billion, an increase of roughly 39% over the prior year.

  ## Network architecture shift drives switch demand

  Arista's AI network business spans two segments. Scale-out networking — which connects servers within a single data center — accounts for approximately 70% of AI network revenue, with Scale-across — which links geographically separate data centers — making up the remainder, according to Commercial Times. Arista expects the Scale-across proportion to grow by 2027 as AI workloads shift from centralized training to distributed inference, raising requirements for low-latency, high-bandwidth transmission and lifting demand for high-speed switches.

  Arista's Etherlink AI networking product line has surpassed 100 customers. By 2027, the company expects Etherlink deployments to span more than 10 gigawatts (GW) of computing infrastructure — a measure of the total electrical capacity of the data centers running the network — and to support more than 10 types of compute accelerators.

  ## High-end PCB and CCL suppliers positioned for growth

  As switch specifications rise, demand for high-end printed circuit boards (PCBs) — the boards that carry and interconnect electronic components inside switches — and for copper-clad laminate (CCL), the base substrate from which PCBs are fabricated, is expected to increase, the report said. Commercial Times identified Gold Circuit Electronics (TSE: 2368) as a PCB-side beneficiary, and noted that CCL producers Elite Material (TSE: 2383) and Iteq (TSE: 6274) also stand to gain.

  Arista's third-quarter inventory climbed to approximately $3.2 billion, while purchase commitments rose to $11.4 billion from $9.7 billion the prior quarter. The company has entered long-term supply agreements to secure chips, memory, and PCB capacity.

  ## Rack-level liquid cooling architecture in development

  Separately, Arista is working with AMD and Meta to develop a Scale-up architecture — a high-speed interconnect designed to link accelerators both within and across server racks. Under this design, a single rack can hold up to 144 accelerators, with configurations expandable across racks to 1,024 units, which the company refers to as XPUs. The architecture uses liquid cooling, which circulates coolant directly through or around components to remove heat from densely packed hardware more efficiently than air alone.

  Liquid cooling module and mechanical component suppliers Auras Technology (TSE: 3017) and Fusconn Technology (TSE: 6805) were identified by Commercial Times as companies with potential exposure to this architecture's commercial rollout. However, Arista said it does not expect Scale-up to generate meaningful revenue in 2027. Customer testing for the rack architecture is scheduled to begin in 2027, with mass production targeted for 2028.

  ## Supply chain outlook

  Arista said not all 2027 orders have been placed and that further purchase commitments remain possible, leaving room for additional supply chain volume beyond what current figures reflect. The combination of growing procurement commitments, increasing switch density, and the liquid-cooling road map extending into 2028 represents a multi-year demand runway for the Taiwanese component suppliers the company has contracted in its supply chain.

  ## Arista 瞄準2027年AI網路營收70億美元，台灣PCB與散熱供應商受矚

  Arista Networks 宣布，計畫於2027年將AI網路營收倍增至70億美元。據《工商時報》報導，這項目標預計將帶動台灣印刷電路板（PCB）、銅箔基板（CCL）、液冷模組及機構件廠商受惠於供應鏈需求擴張。

  Arista 公布第三季營收為36.6億美元，較上一季成長20.5%，年增率達58.5%，優於市場預期。公司表示，此一表現主要來自AI客戶與企業客戶的雙重需求驅動。Arista 同時訂出2027年全年營收目標185億美元，較前一年度約成長39%。

  ## 網路架構轉型帶動交換器需求

  據《工商時報》報導，Arista 的AI網路業務分為兩大區塊：「Scale-out」（橫向擴展網路）負責連結單一資料中心內的伺服器，占AI網路營收約七成；「Scale-across」（跨域連結網路）則用於串聯地理位置分散的不同資料中心，占其餘三成。Arista 預期，隨著AI工作負載由集中式訓練逐步轉向分散式推論，對低延遲、高頻寬傳輸的需求將提升，Scale-across 的占比將在2027年前持續成長，進而拉升高速交換器需求。

  Arista 旗下 Etherlink AI 網路產品線的客戶數已突破100家。該公司預計，至2027年，Etherlink 的部署規模將跨越超過10 GW（百億瓦）的運算基礎設施——此數字代表所承載資料中心的總用電容量——並支援超過10種運算加速器類型。

  ## 高階PCB與銅箔基板廠商具備成長動能

  隨著交換器規格不斷提升，高階印刷電路板及銅箔基板的需求預料將隨之增加。印刷電路板是交換器內部承載並連接各電子元件的基板，銅箔基板則是製造PCB的核心原料基材。《工商時報》點名金像電（TSE: 2368）為PCB端的潛在受惠廠商，並指出銅箔基板廠聯茂（TSE: 2383）與台耀（TSE: 6274）同樣有望受益。

  Arista 第三季庫存攀升至約32億美元，採購承諾金額則由上一季的97億美元增至114億美元。公司已簽訂長期供貨協議，以確保晶片、記憶體及PCB產能供應無虞。

  ## 機架級液冷架構進入開發階段

  另一方面，Arista 正與 AMD 及 Meta 合作，共同開發「Scale-up」架構——一種高速互連設計，旨在連結同一機架內及跨機架的加速器。在此架構下，單一機架最多可容納144顆加速器，並可跨機架擴充至1,024顆，公司將這些運算單元稱為 XPU。該架構採用液冷技術，透過讓冷卻液直接流經或環繞元件，對高密度硬體進行散熱，效率優於單純仰賴空氣冷卻的傳統方案。

  《工商時報》點名液冷模組與機構件廠商奇鋐科技（TSE: 3017）及富士康（TSE: 6805）為具備潛在曝險的業者，可望受惠於此架構的商業化推進。不過，Arista 表示，預計 Scale-up 架構在2027年前尚不會貢獻顯著營收。機架架構的客戶測試預計於2027年啟動，量產則訂於2028年。

  ## 供應鏈展望

  Arista 指出，2027年的訂單尚未全數到位，後續採購承諾仍有增加空間，意味著實際供應鏈出貨量可能超越現行數字所反映的水準。採購承諾持續擴大、交換器密度不斷提升，加上液冷技術路線圖延伸至2028年，對於已進入 Arista 供應鏈的台灣元件廠商而言，這一切共同構成了跨越多年的長期需求能見度。
