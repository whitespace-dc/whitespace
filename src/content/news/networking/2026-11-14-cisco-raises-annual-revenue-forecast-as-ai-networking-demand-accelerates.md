body_en: |
  Cisco Systems raised its full-year revenue forecast and reported first-quarter results above analyst expectations on Nov. 12, as demand for the networking hardware required to connect large-scale artificial intelligence data centers continued to outpace earlier projections.

  The San Jose, California-based company now expects fiscal 2027 revenue of $74.8 billion to $75.8 billion, up from its prior forecast of $72.2 billion to $73.4 billion. It also raised its fiscal 2027 forecast for AI infrastructure revenue from hyperscalers — the large cloud and technology companies building out AI capacity at scale — to $9 billion, from a previous target of $7.5 billion. That segment generated $4 billion in revenue a year earlier.

  "We are in the very early stages of a multi-year networking supercycle," chief executive Chuck Robbins said in a statement.

  ## First-quarter results

  For the quarter ended Oct. 24, Cisco reported revenue of $18.5 billion, above the analyst consensus estimate of $18.14 billion compiled by financial data provider LSEG. Adjusted earnings per share came in at $1.40, ahead of the $1.33 estimate.

  Shares were flat in after-hours trading following the results. The stock has risen approximately 32% so far in 2026 through the Nov. 12 close.

  ## Gross margin pressure

  One area of investor concern was gross margin — the percentage of revenue remaining after the direct cost of goods sold. Cisco reported a first-quarter adjusted gross margin of 65.6%, in line with estimates but down from 68.1% a year earlier.

  The decline reflects higher component costs driven by a shortage of memory chips. Hardware makers have faced increased input costs as chip suppliers prioritize fulfilling orders from AI data-center customers, squeezing margins for networking equipment vendors.

  Bob O'Donnell, president and chief analyst at TECHnalysis Research, said the dip was not a major concern. "A small dip in gross margin is not a major concern given higher memory and component costs," he said, adding that the raised forecast and above-estimate results were what the market wanted to see from a company positioned as an AI infrastructure play.

  ## The shift from processors to networking

  Cisco's upgraded outlook reflects a broader shift in where AI capital spending is flowing. Big Technology companies initially concentrated investment in graphics processing units (GPUs) and other AI chips used to train and run AI models. That spending is now extending to the switches and routers — the physical networking hardware — needed to move data between servers at the speed and volume AI workloads require.

  Cisco has pursued this opportunity through strategic investments in AI, the company said, positioning its hardware and software portfolio to serve data centers being built or expanded to support AI workloads.

  The company is also developing software that businesses can use to deploy AI agents — automated software bots that operate with limited human oversight — to defend IT infrastructure against cybersecurity threats.

  ## Investor concerns

  Investors had been weighing the risk of a slowdown in data-center construction following Democrats' sweeping win in the U.S. midterm elections, as well as rising borrowing costs and their potential effect on the debt-financed building programs that underpin much of the current infrastructure expansion.

  ## Significance

  Cisco's upgraded hyperscaler AI revenue forecast — from $7.5 billion to $9 billion for fiscal 2027, more than double the $4 billion recorded the prior year — signals that demand for AI networking infrastructure remains strong enough to materially outpace the company's own earlier projections. For data-center planners and network equipment buyers, the results suggest the buildout phase driving procurement of high-capacity switches and routers has not peaked.

  Cisco's next scheduled financial update will cover results for the quarter ending in late January 2027.

  ## 思科上調年度營收預測，AI網路需求持續加速

  思科系統（Cisco Systems）於11月12日公布第一季財報，各項數據均優於分析師預期，同時上調全年營收預測。驅動因素在於，連結大型AI資料中心所需的網路硬體需求，持續超出公司先前的估算。

  總部位於加州聖荷西的思科，現預期2027財年營收落在748億至758億美元之間，高於先前預測的722億至734億美元。思科同步上調2027財年來自超大規模業者（即大規模建置AI產能的大型雲端與科技公司）的AI基礎設施營收預測，從原先的75億美元提高至90億美元。同一業務在上個財年的營收為40億美元。

  執行長查克·羅賓斯（Chuck Robbins）在聲明中表示：「我們正處於一個延續多年的網路超級週期的極早期階段。」

  ## 第一季財報表現

  截至10月24日的本季，思科公布營收185億美元，高於金融數據供應商LSEG彙整的分析師共識預估值181.4億美元。調整後每股盈餘為1.40美元，亦優於預估的1.33美元。

  財報公布後，思科股價在盤後交易中大致持平。截至11月12日收盤，該股今年以來累計漲幅約達32%。

  ## 毛利率面臨壓力

  投資人關注的一個面向是毛利率，即扣除銷貨成本後的營收佔比。思科第一季調整後毛利率為65.6%，符合市場預估，但較一年前的68.1%有所下滑。

  毛利率下降，反映出記憶體晶片短缺所推升的零組件成本上漲。由於晶片供應商優先履行AI資料中心客戶的訂單，硬體製造商的投入成本隨之攀升，進而壓縮網路設備廠商的利潤空間。

  TECHnalysis Research總裁暨首席分析師鮑伯·歐唐納（Bob O'Donnell）表示，毛利率的小幅下滑並不令人過度憂慮。「考量到記憶體與零組件成本偏高，毛利率小幅下滑並非主要隱憂，」他說，並補充指出，上調預測與優於預期的財報表現，才是市場希望從一家定位為AI基礎設施概念股的公司身上看到的結果。

  ## AI資本支出從處理器轉向網路設備

  思科上調展望，反映出AI資本支出流向的結構性轉變。大型科技公司起初將投資集中於繪圖處理器（GPU）及其他用於訓練和執行AI模型的AI晶片。如今，支出正延伸至交換器與路由器等實體網路硬體——這些設備是以AI工作負載所需的速度與流量，在伺服器之間傳輸資料的關鍵基礎設施。

  思科表示，公司已透過對AI的策略性投資把握此一商機，調整硬體與軟體產品組合，以服務正在建置或擴充以支援AI工作負載的資料中心。

  此外，思科也正在開發軟體，讓企業得以部署AI代理（Agent）——即在有限人工監督下自主運作的自動化軟體機器人——以抵禦資訊基礎設施所面臨的資安威脅。

  ## 投資人的顧慮

  部分投資人此前正評估以下潛在風險：美國期中選舉民主黨大獲全勝後資料中心建設可能趨緩的疑慮、借貸成本上揚，以及其對目前大規模基礎設施擴張背後所仰賴的舉債建設計畫可能造成的影響。

  ## 重要意義

  思科將2027財年超大規模業者AI營收預測從75億美元上調至90億美元，較前一年實現的40億美元超過翻倍，釋出一個明確訊號：AI網路基礎設施的需求依然強勁，足以實質性地超越公司自身先前的預測。對資料中心規劃者與網路設備採購商而言，本次財報顯示，帶動高容量交換器與路由器採購的基礎設施建置階段尚未觸頂。

  思科下一次預定公布的財報，將涵蓋截至2027年1月底的季度業績。
