  ## Revenue Surge

  Taiwan-listed photonics component maker Lianyi (3081) reported June combined revenue of NT$421 million (New Taiwan dollars), up 3.75% from May and up 128.14% from June 2025, marking a sixth consecutive month of all-time highs, the company disclosed on July 4.

  Second-quarter revenue reached NT$1.217 billion, rising approximately 35% from the first quarter and 120.8% year-on-year. First-half 2026 revenue totaled NT$2.126 billion, up 110.95% from the same period in 2025.

  The company attributed the gains to increased shipments of data center products compared with the prior-year period, saying AI data center construction continues to drive demand for high-speed optical communications.

  ## Products and Customers

  Lianyi's primary revenue drivers are CW Laser — continuous-wave laser chips used as light sources inside optical transceivers — and CW Epi, the epitaxial wafer substrates from which those chips are fabricated. The company said shipments of both product lines have climbed steadily as cloud service providers (CSPs) including Google, AWS, Microsoft, and Meta continue expanding AI data center infrastructure.

  Demand has been further lifted by the volume ramp of new accelerator platforms — NVIDIA Blackwell graphics processing units, Google tensor processing units (TPUs), and AWS Trainium chips — all of which require high-density optical interconnects within and between servers. That has accelerated adoption of 800G optical modules (capable of transmitting data at 800 gigabits per second) and begun pulling forward demand for 1.6T modules (1.6 terabits per second), according to the company.

  ## Margin Improvement

  Analysts cited in the Commercial Times report noted that end-market demand for AI optical transceiver modules has exceeded expectations. They also pointed to a process upgrade — Lianyi's transition of its epitaxial growth line from 2-inch to 3-inch wafers — as a factor improving gross margins through economies of scale. Following those developments, analysts revised upward their second-quarter revenue and full-year operating estimates for the company.

  ## Capital Expenditure and Capacity Expansion

  Lianyi's board approved NT$3.009 billion in capital expenditure in June, earmarked for MOCVD (metal-organic chemical vapor deposition) reactors — the equipment used to grow compound semiconductor crystal layers — and next-generation lithography systems. The company said it had already placed orders for MOCVD equipment from a German supplier in the first half of 2026.

  The investment is expected to double the company's production capacity by the end of 2027 relative to the end of 2026, the company said, to meet long-term AI optical communications demand.

  ## Supply Chain

  Global supply of indium phosphide (InP) substrates — the base material on which Lianyi grows its epitaxial layers — remains tight industrywide, the company said. To secure its material pipeline, Lianyi signed a five-year supply agreement with Japan's Sumitomo Electric for InP substrates.

  ## Outlook

  Analysts described AI high-speed optical communications as being in a long-term growth cycle. They said both Scale-Up architectures, which link processors within a single server rack, and Scale-Out architectures, which connect racks and clusters across a data center, are sustaining fiber interconnect demand. CW Laser supply remains constrained relative to demand, according to the company. After 800G products reach broad adoption, 1.6T modules are expected to become the next primary growth category, with Lianyi estimating its doubled capacity at end-2027 will position it to meet that wave of demand.

  ## 營收大幅成長

  台股上市光電元件廠聯益（3081）於7月4日公告，6月合併營收達新台幣4.21億元，較5月成長3.75%，較2025年6月大增128.14%，連續第六個月寫下歷史新高。

  第二季營收達新台幣12.17億元，較第一季成長約35%，年增幅達120.8%。2026年上半年累計營收為新台幣21.26億元，較2025年同期成長110.95%。

  聯益表示，營收成長主要來自資料中心產品出貨量較去年同期大幅提升，並指出AI資料中心持續擴建，帶動高速光通訊需求不斷升溫。

  ## 產品與客戶

  聯益主要營收來源為連續波雷射（CW Laser）晶片與CW磊晶晶圓（CW Epi）兩大產品線。CW Laser晶片作為光收發模組內部的光源，CW磊晶晶圓則是製造該晶片的磊晶基板材料。該公司表示，隨著Google、AWS、微軟、Meta等雲端服務供應商（CSP）持續擴建AI資料中心基礎設施，兩大產品線出貨量均呈穩定成長態勢。

  新一代加速器平台的量產拉貨，更進一步推升需求，包括輝達Blackwell繪圖處理器、Google張量處理器（TPU）及AWS Trainium晶片，這些平台均需在伺服器內部及伺服器間部署高密度光學互連。此一趨勢加速了800G光學模組（每秒傳輸800Gb）的導入速度，並已開始提前拉抬1.6T模組（每秒傳輸1.6Tb）的需求。

  ## 毛利率改善

  工商時報引述分析師指出，AI光收發模組終端市場需求超乎預期。分析師同時點出製程升級為毛利率改善的重要因素——聯益將磊晶成長製程由2吋晶圓升級至3吋晶圓，透過規模效益有效提升毛利空間。在上述利多發酵後，分析師已上修聯益第二季營收及全年營業獲利預估。

  ## 資本支出與產能擴充

  聯益董事會於6月核准新台幣30.09億元資本支出計畫，用於添購有機金屬化學氣相沉積（MOCVD）反應爐——即用於成長化合物半導體晶體層的關鍵設備——以及新一代微影系統。公司表示，已於2026年上半年向德國供應商下訂MOCVD設備訂單。

  此次投資預計將使聯益於2027年底的生產產能較2026年底倍增，以因應AI光通訊長期需求。

  ## 供應鏈

  聯益表示，磷化銦（InP）基板作為磊晶成長的基礎材料，目前全球供應在業界普遍偏緊。為確保材料供應無虞，聯益已與日本住友電工（Sumitomo Electric）簽訂五年期InP基板供應協議，以穩固原料來源。

  ## 展望

  分析師認為，AI高速光通訊正處於長期成長週期。無論是Scale-Up架構（連結單一機架內處理器）或Scale-Out架構（跨機架與叢集的資料中心互連），均持續支撐光纖互連需求。就供需面而言，CW Laser的供給相對需求仍顯吃緊。待800G產品普及後，1.6T模組預計將成為下一波主要成長品項；聯益估計，2027年底完成的倍增產能，將使其具備充分條件迎接這波需求浪潮。
