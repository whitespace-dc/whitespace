body_en: |
  Google's Nuvem and Sol subsea cable systems have landed in Bermuda, the island's government announced on 11 June 2026, adding Bermuda as a node on a transatlantic network linking the United States with the Azores, Spain, and Portugal. The two systems now interconnect on the island, a configuration Google says will bolster capacity, reduce latency, and increase resilience across its international network, which spans 42 Google Cloud regions worldwide.

  Premier David Burt attended a landing ceremony and said the project would create employment opportunities, support innovation, and attract investment. "For centuries, Bermuda's location in the Atlantic has been one of our greatest strengths," Burt said. "Today, that advantage continues to position our island at the centre of the connections that drive the global economy."

  ## The two cable systems

  Google first unveiled Sol last year, describing it as a cable system designed to connect the US with Bermuda, the Azores, Spain, and Portugal. It followed Google's investment in the Nuvem cable. The two systems are now linked terrestrially on Bermuda, forming what Google describes as an interconnected transatlantic backbone.

  The combined arrangement is intended to significantly increase capacity, reduce latency, and raise resiliency across Google's international network, the government said.

  ## Bermuda's infrastructure strategy

  The Bermuda government framed the landings as part of a deliberate effort to attract critical digital infrastructure investment. Kendaree Burgess, managing director of the Bermuda Business Development Agency, said the milestone followed sustained market engagement. "Major infrastructure investment does not happen by accident. It follows sustained work to engage the market, build confidence and show global investors that Bermuda has the ambition, credibility and operating environment to support projects of this scale," Burgess said.

  The government said it views the cables as vital to sectors including international business, fintech, and digital assets, and described reliable connectivity as no longer optional in a digital economy. "In an increasingly digital world, resilience is no longer optional," the government said in a statement. "Reliable connectivity is critical for business continuity, economic security, disaster preparedness and maintaining Bermuda's competitiveness on the global stage."

  Minister of Home Affairs Alexa Lightbourne said connectivity should be treated on a par with physical infrastructure. "Reliable connectivity is essential infrastructure, as vital to a modern island economy as our roads and our power. The Nuvem and Sol systems strengthen the digital foundation our businesses, households, and youth depend on, and they bring the opportunities of the global economy closer to home," Lightbourne said.

  ## Significance

  The dual landing increases Bermuda's route diversity for transatlantic data traffic, reducing dependence on any single cable path. For Google, the terrestrial interconnection of Sol and Nuvem on the island creates a mid-Atlantic aggregation point that the company says strengthens the capacity, latency, and resiliency profile of its global cloud network.

  The Bermuda government said it intends to continue pursuing global investment opportunities in digital infrastructure to support the island's long-term competitiveness.

  ## Google旗下Nuvem與Sol海底電纜正式登陸百慕達

  Google旗下的Nuvem與Sol海底電纜系統已正式登陸百慕達，百慕達政府於2026年6月11日宣布此消息。此次登陸使百慕達成為跨大西洋網路上的重要節點，串聯起美國、亞速群島、西班牙與葡萄牙。兩套系統目前已在島上完成陸地互聯，Google表示這一配置將提升其國際網路的容量、降低延遲並增強韌性——該網路橫跨全球42個Google Cloud區域。

  百慕達首席部長大衛·伯特（David Burt）出席了電纜登陸儀式，並表示此項目將創造就業機會、支持創新並吸引投資。「數百年來，百慕達在大西洋上的地理位置一直是我們最大的優勢之一，」伯特說。「如今，這項優勢持續讓我們的島嶼站在驅動全球經濟的互聯樞紐中心。」

  ## 兩套電纜系統介紹

  Google去年首次公布Sol電纜系統，將其定位為連結美國、百慕達、亞速群島、西班牙及葡萄牙的海底電纜。此後，Google又投資了Nuvem電纜。如今，兩套系統已在百慕達完成陸地互聯，形成Google所稱的跨大西洋互聯骨幹網路。

  百慕達政府表示，這一組合配置旨在大幅提升Google國際網路的容量、降低延遲並提高韌性。

  ## 百慕達的基礎建設戰略

  百慕達政府將此次電纜登陸定位為積極吸引關鍵數位基礎建設投資的成果之一。百慕達商業發展署署長肯達里·伯吉斯（Kendaree Burgess）表示，這一里程碑是長期市場耕耘的結晶。「重大基礎建設投資不會憑空發生，它需要持續的市場溝通、建立信心，並向全球投資者展示百慕達具備承接此規模項目的企圖心、公信力與營運環境，」伯吉斯說。

  百慕達政府表示，視這兩條電纜為國際商業、金融科技及數位資產等領域的重要命脈，並強調在數位經濟時代，穩定的連線能力已不再是可選項。「在日益數位化的世界中，韌性不再是選擇，而是必要條件，」政府在聲明中指出。「可靠的連線能力對於業務持續運作、經濟安全、災害應變及維持百慕達全球競爭力至關重要。」

  內政部長艾萊克薩·萊特本（Alexa Lightbourne）表示，連線能力應與實體基礎建設同等看待。「可靠的連線能力是不可或缺的基礎建設，對現代島嶼經濟的重要性不亞於道路和電力。Nuvem與Sol系統強化了我們的企業、家庭和年輕世代所仰賴的數位基礎，也讓全球經濟的機遇離我們更近，」萊特本說。

  ## 重要意義

  這兩條電纜同時登陸，提升了百慕達跨大西洋資料流量的路由多元性，降低了對單一電纜路徑的依賴。對Google而言，Sol與Nuvem在島上的陸地互聯，打造出一個大西洋中繼匯聚節點，Google表示此配置將全面強化其全球雲端網路在容量、延遲與韌性方面的表現。

  百慕達政府表示，未來將持續尋求全球數位基礎建設的投資機會，以支撐該島長遠的競爭力。
