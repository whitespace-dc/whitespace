body_en: |
  A potential US ban on Chinese optical transceiver modules would drive up costs for American hyperscalers and slow the deployment of artificial intelligence infrastructure, research firm Counterpoint warned Wednesday, as no Western supplier can replace Chinese production at scale in the near term.

  Chinese firms hold nearly two-thirds of the global supply of optical transceivers — the hardware components that convert electrical signals to light pulses and back, enabling high-speed data transmission within and across server racks. A ban would restrict components that are central to the hundreds of billions of dollars' worth of AI infrastructure US companies are currently building out, according to Counterpoint analyst Neil Shah.

  "The belief that the optical transceiver market can be neatly divided geographically misinterprets how the hardware ecosystem operates," Shah wrote in a research note published Wednesday. "The global AI ecosystem remains heavily reliant on Chinese optical module vendors for scale execution."

  ## What the ban would target

  The proposed restriction focuses on one of the few segments of global AI infrastructure where Chinese companies hold a dominant position. Chinese firms Zhongji Innolight Co and Eoptolink Technology Inc have an advantage over Western competitors in manufacturing the high-precision modules at the volumes AI data center buildouts require, Shah said.

  Chinese optical modules also integrate components from Western and allied suppliers. According to Counterpoint, those include chips from Broadcom Inc and Marvell Technology Inc, as well as lasers and optical chips from Lumentum Holdings Inc and Japan's Mitsubishi Electric Corp. A ban would therefore affect the broader Western AI supply chain, not only Chinese vendors, Shah said.

  ## Market reaction

  The initial report of a potential trade restriction triggered an immediate market response. Zhongji Innolight's stock fell as much as 14% on Wednesday. Western competitors — including Applied Optoelectronic Inc, Coherent Corp, and Nokia Oyj — rallied on the prospect of reduced Chinese competition.

  ## Supply gap Western firms cannot close

  Counterpoint's analysis identifies a timing problem at the center of the proposed policy: Western suppliers do not have the production capacity to absorb Innolight and Eoptolink's combined volume within one to two years, Shah said. Hyperscalers — the term for large-scale cloud and AI infrastructure operators such as Amazon.com Inc and Microsoft Corp — would face higher component costs as a result, and those cost pressures would translate into lower utilization of expensive AI accelerators, the processors that power AI model training and inference.

  "While policy initiatives seek to insulate critical AI supply chains, sudden regulatory shifts risk creating hardware bottlenecks that could slow down deployment schedules for the world's largest cloud operators," Shah said.

  ## Industry significance

  The warning illustrates a structural tension in US technology policy: efforts to reduce dependence on Chinese hardware in critical infrastructure can themselves create supply disruptions when the domestic and allied industrial base has not yet scaled to replace the targeted suppliers. For data center planners and cloud operators, a ban would introduce procurement risk and schedule uncertainty at a moment when AI infrastructure investment is accelerating. Western optical component makers may benefit commercially in the medium term, but Counterpoint's assessment suggests the transition period carries material risk for operators dependent on current Chinese supply volumes.
body_zh: |
  市場研究機構 Counterpoint 週三警告，若美國禁止進口中國光學收發器模組，將推高美國超大規模雲端業者的採購成本，並拖慢人工智慧基礎建設的部署腳步——因為短期內根本沒有任何西方供應商能在產量上取代中國廠商。

  中國企業掌握全球近三分之二的光學收發器供應量。這類硬體元件負責將電訊號轉換為光脈衝、再將光脈衝還原為電訊號，使伺服器機架內部及機架之間得以實現高速資料傳輸。Counterpoint 分析師尼爾．沙（Neil Shah）表示，美國企業目前正斥資數千億美元打造 AI 基礎建設，光學收發器正是其中不可或缺的核心元件，禁令一旦落地，必將衝擊這場建設浪潮。

  「認為光學收發器市場可以依地理疆界整齊切割，這種想法根本誤解了硬體生態系的運作方式，」沙在週三發布的研究報告中寫道。「全球 AI 生態系在大規模量產執行上，至今仍高度依賴中國光學模組廠商。」

  ## 禁令的打擊目標

  此次擬議中的管制措施，瞄準的是全球 AI 基礎建設中少數幾個由中國企業占據主導地位的市場區塊之一。沙指出，中際旭創（Zhongji Innolight）與易飛揚光電（Eoptolink Technology）在高精密模組的大批量製造上優於西方競爭對手，而這正是 AI 資料中心建設所需要的規模能力。

  中國光學模組同時整合了來自西方及盟友廠商的元件。根據 Counterpoint 的分析，這些元件包括博通（Broadcom）與邁威爾科技（Marvell Technology）提供的晶片，以及盧門坦（Lumentum Holdings）與日本三菱電機（Mitsubishi Electric）供應的雷射器與光學晶片。沙表示，禁令的衝擊因此不僅限於中國廠商，而是會波及整個西方 AI 供應鏈。

  ## 市場反應

  潛在貿易管制的消息一出，市場隨即出現劇烈反應。中際旭創股價週三盤中一度重挫逾 14%。與此同時，應用光電（Applied Optoelectronic）、科欣特（Coherent Corp）與諾基亞（Nokia Oyj）等西方競爭對手的股價，則因中國競爭壓力預期減輕而顯著上揚。

  ## 西方廠商無法填補的供給缺口

  Counterpoint 的分析點出了這項擬議政策的核心癥結——時程問題：沙表示，西方供應商在一至兩年內根本沒有足夠產能，可吸收中際旭創與易飛揚光電合計的出貨規模。超大規模業者——即亞馬遜（Amazon.com）、微軟（Microsoft）等大規模雲端與 AI 基礎建設的營運商——將因此面臨更高的元件採購成本，而此一成本壓力最終將轉化為昂貴 AI 加速器使用率的下滑。所謂 AI 加速器，即驅動 AI 模型訓練與推論的處理器。

  「政策措施固然致力於保護關鍵 AI 供應鏈的安全，但驟然的法規轉向，恐將造成硬體瓶頸，進而拖慢全球最大雲端業者的部署時程，」沙說道。

  ## 產業意義

  這項警告揭示了美國科技政策中一個結構性的矛盾：當境內及盟友的產業基礎尚未具備足以取代目標供應商的規模時，試圖降低關鍵基礎建設對中國硬體依賴的政策措施，本身反而可能引發供應中斷。對資料中心規劃者與雲端業者而言，在 AI 基礎建設投資正加速推進的此刻，禁令將帶來採購風險與時程上的不確定性。西方光學元件製造商或許能在中期受益，但 Counterpoint 的評估顯示，對於目前仰賴中國現有供應量的業者而言，過渡期將帶來實質性的風險。
