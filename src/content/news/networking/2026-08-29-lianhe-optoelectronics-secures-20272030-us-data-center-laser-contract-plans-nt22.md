  ## Lianhe Optoelectronics secures US data center laser contract, commits NT$2.27B to capacity

  Lianhe Optoelectronics (Taiwan Stock Exchange: 3081) has signed a long-term supply agreement with a US customer to deliver CW Lasers — continuous-wave lasers, the light-source components used in high-speed optical data center interconnects — from 2027 through 2030, the company said. Alongside the revenue-side commitment, Lianhe will invest up to NT$2.27 billion in new production equipment to support capacity growth from the second half of 2026 through 2027.

  The announcement drove the stock to its daily limit-up close of NT$3,255 on August 26 and to an intraday high of NT$3,460 — a gain of approximately 6.3% — on August 27, according to Commercial Times.

  ## Supply chain lock-in on both sides

  The US customer was not identified in Lianhe's disclosures. In parallel with the supply contract, the company signed separate long-term procurement agreements with suppliers in Japan and the United States for indium phosphide (InP) substrates — the specialized compound semiconductor wafers from which the lasers are fabricated — Commercial Times reported. The company said the dual agreements are designed to stabilize raw-material supply and reduce sourcing risk as demand from artificial intelligence (AI), high-performance computing (HPC), and data center construction accelerates.

  ## Capacity expansion and product demand

  The capital expenditure of up to NT$2.27 billion is directed at purchasing production equipment to handle anticipated order growth, the company said. Lianhe also produces EPI wafers — epitaxial wafers — and said demand for both CW Laser chips and EPI wafers remains strong as the unnamed US customer accelerates its AI data center buildout. Downstream chip foundries that process Lianhe's wafers are also expanding capacity, which the company said should further increase its own shipment momentum.

  ## Financial outlook

  Institutional investors cited by Commercial Times project Lianhe's third-quarter revenue at NT$1.554 billion, a quarter-on-quarter increase of 27.2%. They forecast gross margin rising to 58.5%, driven by a more favorable product mix and higher capacity utilization, with earnings per share (EPS) for the quarter reaching NT$6.13 — levels the publication described as potential records for both revenue and profit.

  ## Significance for the optical component supply chain

  The deal illustrates how Taiwanese compound semiconductor suppliers are pursuing multi-year commitments from hyperscale data center operators building AI and HPC infrastructure. By securing a customer contract on the revenue side and InP substrate supply on the cost side simultaneously, Lianhe is positioning to reduce the supply-chain volatility that has historically constrained compound semiconductor manufacturers during demand surges. The company said the combined agreements increase order and capacity visibility over the medium-to-long term.

  Lianhe said AI data center expansion, rising CW Laser chip shipments, and growing EPI wafer volumes will remain its primary growth drivers through the second half of 2026 and into 2027.

  ## 聯合光電取得美國資料中心雷射訂單，投入22.7億元擴充產能

  聯合光電（台灣證券交易所代號：3081）宣布，已與一家美國客戶簽訂長期供應合約，將於2027年至2030年間持續交付CW Laser（連續波雷射）——即高速光學資料中心互連所採用的光源元件。與此同時，聯合光電也宣示將投入最高新台幣22.7億元採購新生產設備，以支應自2026年下半年至2027年間的產能擴充需求。

  此一消息帶動公司股價於8月26日以漲停板收盤，收報新台幣3,255元；8月27日盤中更一度攀至3,460元高點，較前日上漲約6.3%，據《工商時報》報導。

  ## 供應鏈兩端同步鎖定

  聯合光電公告中並未揭露該美國客戶的身份。在簽署供應合約的同時，公司也分別與日本及美國供應商簽訂長期採購協議，鎖定磷化銦（InP）基板的供應來源——磷化銦基板為製造上述雷射元件所使用的特殊化合物半導體晶圓，《工商時報》報導指出。公司表示，這兩份採購協議的目的，在於隨著人工智慧（AI）、高效能運算（HPC）及資料中心建設需求加速升溫，進一步穩固原料供應並降低採購風險。

  ## 產能擴充與產品需求

  最高新台幣22.7億元的資本支出，將全數用於採購生產設備，以因應預期訂單的持續成長。聯合光電亦生產磊晶晶圓（EPI wafer），並表示，隨著前述美國不具名客戶加速擴建AI資料中心，CW Laser晶片及磊晶晶圓的需求依然強勁。負責後段加工聯合光電晶圓的下游晶片代工廠也正積極擴充產能，公司表示，此舉將進一步帶動自身出貨動能的成長。

  ## 財務展望

  據《工商時報》引述法人機構預估，聯合光電第三季營收將達新台幣15.54億元，較上季成長27.2%；在產品組合改善及產能利用率提升的帶動下，毛利率預計上揚至58.5%；每股盈餘（EPS）則預估可達6.13元。上述報導指出，這些數字有望雙雙創下公司營收與獲利的歷史新高。

  ## 對光學元件供應鏈的意義

  此次合作清楚說明，台灣化合物半導體供應商正積極爭取超大規模資料中心營運商的多年期長約，而這些業者正大力擴建AI與HPC基礎設施。聯合光電藉由在營收端鎖定客戶訂單、在成本端同步確保磷化銦基板供應，致力降低化合物半導體製造商長久以來在需求急速攀升時深受其苦的供應鏈波動風險。公司表示，上述雙邊協議大幅提升了中長期的訂單能見度與產能規劃的確定性。

  聯合光電表示，AI資料中心持續擴建、CW Laser晶片出貨量增加，以及磊晶晶圓出貨量成長，將是驅動2026年下半年至2027年業績的主要成長動能。
