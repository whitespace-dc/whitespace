body_en: |
  UMC has signed a licensing agreement with imec, the Belgian semiconductor research institute, to transfer imec's iSiPP300 silicon photonics process technology to the foundry's 12-inch wafer manufacturing lines, as data centre and artificial intelligence workloads push demand for optical interconnects beyond what conventional copper can deliver.

  The licensed platform, iSiPP300, is compatible with co-packaged optics (CPO) — a design approach that places optical and compute elements in closer physical proximity to reduce the bottlenecks and power demands that arise when data moves between processors and network equipment. UMC said it is already working with several new customers to produce photonic integrated circuits (PICs), the chips that perform optical signal processing, for optical transceiver applications, and has scheduled risk production — early-stage manufacturing runs used to qualify a process before full commercial ramp — for 2026 and 2027.

  GC Hung, Senior Vice President at UMC, said the agreement is intended to accelerate the foundry's 12-inch photonic platform readiness. "Combined with our diverse advanced packaging technologies, UMC is positioned to extend our offerings going forward as system architectures evolve toward greater integration, such as co-packaged optics and optical I/O, to achieve high-bandwidth and highly scalable optical interconnects for both intra- and inter-data center communications," Hung said.

  ## The iSiPP300 platform

  Imec said its iSiPP300 platform has been developed over the past decade on 12-inch wafers using advanced CMOS (complementary metal-oxide-semiconductor) processing, the same core technique used to manufacture mainstream logic chips. The platform includes microring-based filters and modulators — compact devices that control light on-chip — as well as GeSi electro-absorption modulators (EAMs), which use a germanium-silicon material to switch optical signals electrically. It also incorporates low-loss fibre interfaces for connecting chips to optical fibre, and 3D packaging modules for stacking components.

  Philippe Absil, Vice-President of IC-Link at imec, the institute's technology transfer and commercialisation arm, said the agreement demonstrates imec's strategy of working with foundries to move advanced research into commercial production. "This agreement with UMC is a demonstration of our collaborative approach, enabling us to bring cutting-edge silicon photonics solutions to a broader market and accelerate adoption in next-generation compute systems," Absil said.

  ## UMC's manufacturing position

  Silicon photonics uses light rather than electrical signals to transmit data, a distinction that matters as data centre and AI workloads increase bandwidth demand and tighten latency requirements on networking and computing systems. Moving to 12-inch wafers from smaller 8-inch formats can reduce per-unit production costs by spreading fixed manufacturing expenses across a larger substrate area, a factor cited in the foundry industry's broader push to migrate speciality processes onto more mainstream wafer sizes.

  UMC said it plans to combine imec's 12-inch silicon photonics process with its own silicon-on-insulator (SOI) wafer manufacturing experience — SOI being a wafer structure commonly used in silicon photonics because of its optical properties — and it pointed to existing 8-inch silicon photonics production as a prior foundation for the expanded platform.

  The company operates 12 fabrication plants with combined monthly capacity exceeding 400,000 wafers on a 12-inch-equivalent basis, and employs approximately 20,000 people worldwide. Its manufacturing base is concentrated in Taiwan, where most of its 12-inch and 8-inch plants and core research and development operations are located. It provides fabrication services across logic, mixed-signal and multiple speciality process technologies.

  ## Industry significance

  The deal follows a well-established model in the semiconductor industry in which research institutes develop process building blocks and then license them to foundries for commercial adaptation. For UMC, adding a 12-inch silicon photonics platform extends its speciality manufacturing portfolio at a time when foundries are seeking differentiation beyond standard logic processes in areas tied to communications, AI infrastructure and high-performance computing.

  Optical transceivers — the components referenced in UMC's customer plans — are key links between servers, switches and storage systems inside data centres and across inter-facility networks. The mention of optical I/O alongside CPO indicates UMC is positioning the platform for system architectures in which optical connections replace or supplement electrical ones at multiple points in the signal chain.

  UMC's risk production runs for the new platform are scheduled to begin in 2026, with further production in 2027.

  ## 聯電授權imec矽光子平台　12吋晶圓量產計畫啟動

  聯華電子（UMC）日前與比利時半導體研究機構imec簽署授權協議，將imec的iSiPP300矽光子製程技術移轉至聯電12吋晶圓製造產線。這項合作的背景，是資料中心與人工智慧工作負載持續推升光互連需求，傳統銅線傳輸已難以應付高頻寬及低延遲的要求。

  此次授權的iSiPP300平台，相容於共封裝光學（co-packaged optics，CPO）設計架構——這種設計將光學元件與運算元件整合於更近的實體空間，藉此降低處理器與網路設備之間資料傳輸所產生的瓶頸與耗電。聯電表示，目前已與多家新客戶合作，為光收發器應用生產光子積體電路（PIC，即執行光訊號處理的晶片），並規劃於2026年及2027年進行風險試產——此為正式商業量產前用以驗證製程的早期試製階段。

  聯電資深副總裁洪啟德（GC Hung）表示，這項協議旨在加速聯電12吋光子平台的量產就緒進度。他說：「結合聯電多元的先進封裝技術，隨著系統架構朝向更高度整合演進，包括共封裝光學與光學I/O，聯電具備延伸產品服務的條件，以實現資料中心內部及跨資料中心通訊所需的高頻寬、高擴展性光互連。」

  ## iSiPP300平台簡介

  imec表示，iSiPP300平台歷經過去十年於12吋晶圓上、採用先進CMOS（互補式金屬氧化物半導體）製程開發而成——CMOS正是主流邏輯晶片製造所使用的核心技術。該平台涵蓋多項關鍵元件：以微環為基礎的濾波器與調製器（用於晶片上的光訊號控制）、鍺矽電吸收調製器（GeSi EAM，利用鍺矽材料以電氣方式切換光訊號），以及低損耗光纖介面（用於晶片與光纖的連接）和三維封裝模組（用於元件堆疊）。

  imec負責技術移轉與商業化業務的IC-Link部門副總裁Philippe Absil表示，這項協議展示了imec與晶圓廠合作、將先進研究成果推向商業量產的策略方向。他說：「此次與聯電的合作，正是我們協作模式的具體實踐，讓我們得以將頂尖的矽光子解決方案推廣至更廣泛的市場，並加速其在次世代運算系統中的應用普及。」

  ## 聯電的製造優勢

  矽光子技術以光取代電訊號傳輸資料，在資料中心與AI工作負載持續推升頻寬需求、對網路與運算系統的延遲要求日益嚴苛的背景下，這項差異至關重要。相較於舊有的8吋晶圓，改用12吋晶圓可將固定製造成本分攤至更大的基板面積，有效降低每單位生產成本，這也是整個晶圓代工產業積極將特殊製程遷移至主流晶圓尺寸的重要動因。

  聯電表示，計畫將imec的12吋矽光子製程與自身的矽覆絕緣體（SOI）晶圓製造經驗相結合——SOI是矽光子領域常用的晶圓結構，因其優異的光學特性而廣受採用。聯電並指出，現有的8吋矽光子生產線將成為擴展後新平台的重要基礎。

  聯電目前營運12座晶圓廠，每月合計產能以12吋約當基準換算超過40萬片，全球員工人數約2萬人。其製造基地主要集中於台灣，12吋及8吋廠區與核心研發營運均設於此地。聯電提供涵蓋邏輯、混合訊號及多種特殊製程的晶圓代工服務。

  ## 產業意義

  這項合作遵循半導體產業行之有年的模式：由研究機構開發製程建構模組，再授權予晶圓廠進行商業化適配。對聯電而言，導入12吋矽光子平台，在通訊、AI基礎設施及高效能運算等領域拓展了特殊製造的產品版圖，有助於在標準邏輯製程之外尋求差異化定位，這對當前晶圓代工廠普遍面臨的競爭格局而言尤具戰略意義。

  光收發器——即聯電客戶計畫中所提及的元件——是資料中心內部伺服器、交換器與儲存系統之間，以及跨機房網路之間的關鍵連接環節。聯電在CPO之外同時提及光學I/O，顯示該公司正積極將這套平台定位於光學連接在訊號鏈多個節點取代或補充電氣連接的系統架構，以因應未來高整合度系統設計的需求。

  聯電針對新平台的風險試產預計於2026年啟動，並將於2027年持續推進後續生產。
