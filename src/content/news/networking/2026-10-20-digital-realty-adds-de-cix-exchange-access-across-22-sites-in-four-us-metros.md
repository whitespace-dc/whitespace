body_en: |
  Digital Realty and DE-CIX have extended their US interconnection partnership to 22 additional data centers across Chicago, Dallas, New York, and Phoenix, giving customers at every Digital Realty facility in those four metropolitan markets direct access to DE-CIX Internet Exchange services.

  The expansion, delivered through Digital Realty's Metro Connect service — which links customers between locations within a single metropolitan area — nearly doubles the two companies' joint footprint in the United States and increases their combined global footprint by 25%, according to the companies.

  DE-CIX operates Internet Exchanges, neutral facilities where networks, internet service providers, cloud platforms, and enterprises can interconnect directly with one another rather than routing traffic through third-party transit providers. Digital Realty provides data center and colocation space through a global estate of more than 300 facilities in more than 55 metropolitan areas across six continents.

  ## Scale and scope of the agreement

  The four metros added under this agreement are among the most active interconnection hubs in the US. New York and Chicago are established exchange points for financial services, media, and network operators. Dallas and Phoenix have drawn sustained data center investment as lower-cost, high-growth markets.

  By covering all Digital Realty locations within each metro rather than a selected subset, the arrangement gives tenants with workloads distributed across multiple sites in the same market a consistent path into the DE-CIX ecosystem. Services available through the connection include peering, cloud connectivity, AI connectivity, and private interconnection, the companies said.

  ## Two-decade partnership extended

  The expansion builds on a relationship the companies say has run for more than two decades. Their existing partnership already spans 16 metropolitan markets across 11 countries in Europe, the Americas, and Asia. The US addition represents the largest single extension of that arrangement.

  DE-CIX was founded in 1995 and now operates interconnection services at 60 locations worldwide. Its platforms are accessible from data centers in more than 600 cities and connect thousands of networks from more than 100 countries.

  ## What the companies said

  Ivo Ivanov, Chief Executive Officer of DE-CIX, framed the expansion around the demands placed on interconnection infrastructure by AI inference workloads and cloud-based services. "Digital infrastructure is becoming increasingly distributed, making simple and reliable connectivity between data centers, networks, clouds, and applications more important than ever," Ivanov said. "The DE-CIX ecosystem is now within easy reach for customers at dozens of facilities across the most important markets in North America."

  Chris Sharp, Chief Technology Officer of Digital Realty, said the rollout would give customers a standard route into the DE-CIX environment across the four markets. "Customers at every Digital Realty data center in those metros can reach the DE-CIX ecosystem through Metro Connect — with the flexibility, choice and neutrality they expect from our platform," Sharp said.

  ## Industry significance

  The agreement reflects a broader trend of data center operators and exchange providers pursuing closer commercial ties as customers seek consolidated access to networks, cloud platforms, and private interconnection within a single campus or metro footprint. For Digital Realty, uniform DE-CIX coverage across four major US markets strengthens the interconnection proposition it can offer multi-site tenants. For DE-CIX, the deal opens its platform to a wider pool of networks, enterprises, and cloud providers in markets where interconnection demand is growing.

  The expanded partnership now covers all Digital Realty facilities in Chicago, Dallas, New York, and Phoenix, with the two companies' combined global collaboration reaching across 11 countries on multiple continents.

  Digital Realty與DE-CIX宣布，將兩家公司的美國互連合作延伸至芝加哥、達拉斯、紐約及鳳凰城四大都會市場共22座資料中心，讓這四個都會區內每一座Digital Realty機房的客戶，均可直接取用DE-CIX網際網路交換所服務。

  本次擴展透過Digital Realty的Metro Connect服務完成——該服務可在單一都會區內串連各個據點的客戶。根據兩家公司說法，此次合作使雙方在美國的聯合版圖規模近乎翻倍，全球合作規模則同步增加25%。

  DE-CIX營運的是網際網路交換所，一種讓網路業者、網際網路服務供應商、雲端平台及企業能夠彼此直接互連的中立設施，無需透過第三方轉接業者繞路傳輸流量。Digital Realty則透過遍布六大洲、超過55個都會區、逾300座設施的全球機房網絡，提供資料中心及共置空間服務。

  ## 合作規模與涵蓋範圍

  此次協議新增的四個都會區，均是美國互連需求最旺盛的樞紐。紐約與芝加哥是金融服務、媒體及網路業者長期倚重的流量交換節點；達拉斯與鳳凰城則因營運成本相對較低、成長動能強勁，持續吸引大量資料中心投資進駐。

  由於協議涵蓋各都會區內Digital Realty的所有站點，而非僅限於部分機房，在同一市場中跨多個據點部署工作負載的租戶，均可透過一致的路徑接入DE-CIX生態系。兩家公司表示，可用服務涵蓋對等互連（peering）、雲端連線、AI連線及私有互連等項目。

  ## 逾二十年夥伴關係再度延伸

  此次擴展建立在兩家公司自稱已延續逾二十年的合作基礎之上。現有夥伴關係已涵蓋歐洲、美洲及亞洲11個國家、16個都會市場。此次美國新增的布局，是雙方有史以來單次規模最大的合作延伸。

  DE-CIX成立於1995年，目前在全球60個地點營運互連服務，其平台可從超過600座城市的資料中心存取，並串連來自逾100個國家的數千個網路。

  ## 雙方回應

  DE-CIX執行長Ivo Ivanov從AI推論工作負載及雲端服務對互連基礎架構的需求角度，詮釋本次擴展的意義。「數位基礎設施日益走向分散部署，資料中心、網路、雲端與應用程式之間簡單可靠的連線因此愈形重要，」Ivanov表示，「如今北美最重要市場數十座機房的客戶，都能輕鬆觸及DE-CIX生態系。」

  Digital Realty技術長Chris Sharp則表示，此次上線將為四個市場的客戶提供一條進入DE-CIX環境的標準路徑。「這四個都會區內每一座Digital Realty資料中心的客戶，都能透過Metro Connect連上DE-CIX生態系——享有他們期待從我們平台獲得的彈性、選擇與中立性，」Sharp說。

  ## 產業意義

  此次協議反映出一個更宏觀的趨勢：隨著客戶尋求在單一園區或都會區版圖內整合取用網路、雲端平台及私有互連服務，資料中心業者與交換所業者正積極深化商業合作關係。對Digital Realty而言，在美國四大主要市場實現DE-CIX的全面覆蓋，強化了其向多據點租戶提供的互連價值主張；對DE-CIX來說，此次合作則讓其平台在互連需求持續成長的市場中，向更多網路業者、企業及雲端供應商敞開大門。

  擴展後的合作夥伴關係現已涵蓋Digital Realty在芝加哥、達拉斯、紐約及鳳凰城的所有機房，兩家公司的全球合作版圖橫跨多個大洲、延伸至11個國家。
