  ## Formation and membership

  Lightmatter announced on August 14 that its "Open Silicon Photonics for AI Systems" initiative has been formally constituted as a working group under the Open Compute Project (OCP), an industry consortium that develops open hardware standards for data center infrastructure. Nineteen companies are participating in the working group, which has released an approximately 300-page architecture white paper intended to serve as the foundation for a shared system architecture blueprint for co-packaged optics (CPO) — a technology that integrates optical components directly with compute chips to replace copper interconnects at short distances.

  Participating members include Celestica, Dell Technologies, Flex, Foxconn Interconnect Technology, GUC (Global Unichip Corp.), Qualcomm, and Wiwynn, according to the announcement.

  ## Technical scope

  The working group's stated goal is to establish an open CPO rack architecture compatible with MHS (Modular Hardware System) and Open Rack v3 specifications — existing OCP rack and hardware standards — enabling AI clusters to scale from 72 nodes to more than 1,024 nodes while preserving multi-vendor interoperability.

  The architecture is designed to be technology-neutral, meaning it is intended to support multiple underlying optical technologies including silicon photonics, VCSEL (vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser), and Micro LED. The group said the architecture targets future AI clusters at the scale of 100,000 XPUs (accelerator processing units).

  ## Why optical interconnects are gaining urgency

  The initiative reflects a broader industry shift driven by the limitations of copper cabling at high data rates. As SerDes (serializer/deserializer) interface speeds advance toward 448 gigabits per second, the effective transmission distance of copper wiring shrinks to a few tens of centimeters, according to the white paper's framing. That physical constraint is accelerating industry interest in optical interconnects, which can carry signals over greater distances at those speeds without equivalent signal degradation.

  ## Next steps

  The first set of specifications from the working group is expected to be submitted in the fourth quarter of 2026.

  ## 成立背景與成員組成

  Lightmatter 於 8 月 14 日宣布，其「AI 系統開放矽光子」（Open Silicon Photonics for AI Systems）計畫已正式在開放運算專案（Open Compute Project，OCP）旗下成立為工作小組。OCP 是一個致力於為資料中心基礎設施制定開放硬體標準的產業聯盟。此工作小組共有 19 家企業參與，並發布一份約 300 頁的架構白皮書，旨在作為共封裝光學（co-packaged optics，CPO）共通系統架構藍圖的奠基文件。CPO 是一種將光學元件直接與運算晶片整合的技術，用以在短距離內取代銅材質的互連線路。

  根據公告，參與成員包括 Celestica、戴爾科技（Dell Technologies）、Flex、鴻騰精密（Foxconn Interconnect Technology）、創意電子（GUC，Global Unichip Corp.）、高通（Qualcomm）及緯穎科技（Wiwynn）等。

  ## 技術範疇

  此工作小組的既定目標，是建立一套相容於 MHS（模組化硬體系統）及 Open Rack v3 規格的開放 CPO 機架架構——這兩者均為現行的 OCP 機架與硬體標準——使 AI 叢集得以從 72 個節點擴展至逾 1,024 個節點，同時確保多廠商之間的互通性。

  此架構設計採取技術中立立場，意即旨在支援多種底層光學技術，涵蓋矽光子（silicon photonics）、垂直共振腔面射型雷射（VCSEL）及微型 LED（Micro LED）。工作小組表示，此架構的目標是支援未來達 10 萬顆 XPU（加速處理器）規模的 AI 叢集。

  ## 光學互連受到高度重視的原因

  此計畫反映了產業界因銅纜在高速資料傳輸上的局限性而產生的更廣泛轉型趨勢。根據白皮書的論述，隨著 SerDes（序列化／反序列化）介面速度邁向每秒 448 Gbps，銅線的有效傳輸距離已縮短至僅剩數十公分。這一物理上的硬限制，正加速業界轉向光學互連的興趣——光學互連在上述傳輸速率下，能夠跨越更長距離傳送訊號，且不會產生同等程度的訊號衰減。

  ## 後續規劃

  工作小組的第一批規格預計將於 2026 年第四季正式提交。
