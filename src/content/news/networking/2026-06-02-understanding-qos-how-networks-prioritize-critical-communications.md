# Understanding QoS: How Networks Prioritize Critical Communications

QoS (Quality of Service) is a technology that ensures the quality of critical network communications. It prioritizes transmission based on application type and reserves necessary bandwidth to maintain performance standards. This approach helps organizations guarantee reliable service delivery for their most important applications.

## 中文

# 認識 QoS：網路優先傳輸重要通信的方法

QoS（服務品質）是確保重要通信品質的網路技術。它根據應用程式類型優先安排資料傳輸，並預留必要的頻寬以維持效能標準。這種方法幫助組織保障關鍵應用程式的可靠服務傳遞。
