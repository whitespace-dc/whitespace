body_en: |
  Nvidia has moved into first place in the data center Ethernet switching market, capturing a 21.5% share in the first quarter of 2026 after its revenue in the category grew 193% year over year to $2.1 billion, according to market research firm IDC. The result marks a sharp rise from less than 4% share two years ago and places Nvidia ahead of established networking vendors Arista Networks and Cisco Systems.

  Arista held a 20.7% share of the data center Ethernet switching market during the quarter, and Cisco accounted for 17.8%, IDC said. White-box manufacturers, which sell commodity switch hardware without a vendor brand, also maintained a significant presence.

  The broader Ethernet switching market grew 39.8% year over year to $15.4 billion in the quarter. Data center switching, the equipment used inside large facilities to interconnect servers and storage, accounted for $10 billion of that total, up 61% from a year earlier. By speed tier, 800G switches, capable of moving 800 gigabits of data per second per port, represented 35.8% of data center switching revenue, while 200G and 400G platforms combined for another 34.1%, IDC said.

  ## Integrated AI platform drives purchasing shift

  Nvidia's rise in switching is tied to how AI infrastructure is now purchased rather than to networking specifications alone. The company sells its Spectrum-X Ethernet switching platform alongside BlueField data processing units (DPUs), LinkX cables and optics, and its CUDA software stack, the programming environment used to run workloads on Nvidia graphics processing units (GPUs). That combination allows buyers to acquire a pre-integrated architecture for building AI clusters rather than sourcing compute and networking separately.

  "Spectrum-X has gained significant share in the data center Ethernet switch market because it is sold as part of a tightly integrated GPU-plus-networking package optimized for AI factory workloads, not as a standalone switching architecture," Brandon Butler, senior research manager for network infrastructure and services at IDC, told Data Center Knowledge.

  Ron Westfall, vice president and practice lead for networking and infrastructure at HyperFrame Research, said the shift reflects a change in how operators approach data center procurement. "Enterprises are no longer purchasing networking as an isolated infrastructure silo, but rather as an essential, pre-optimized component of their broader GPU cluster investments," he said.

  Spectrum-X carries technical attributes suited to AI workloads, including congestion management, reduced packet loss, and low-latency data movement, but Westfall said Nvidia's broader platform position is the larger competitive advantage. "Nvidia's surge to a 21.5% share in data center Ethernet switching is driven by the evolution of AI infrastructure rather than traditional networking dynamics," he said.

  ## Nvidia networking revenue reaches $14.8 billion

  IDC's market-share findings align with Nvidia's own reported results. The company recorded data center networking revenue of $14.8 billion in the first quarter of its fiscal year 2027, up 199% from the same period a year earlier. Total data center revenue reached $75.2 billion for the quarter, up 92%.

  Nvidia's networking business extends beyond Ethernet switches to include InfiniBand high-speed interconnects, network interface cards, DPUs, optics, cables, and related hardware, the company said.

  "The buildout of AI factories — the largest infrastructure expansion in human history — is accelerating at extraordinary speed," Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang said in the company's earnings release.

  ## Enterprise market seen as next competitive front

  Established switching vendors have pursued varied strategies in response to Nvidia's gains. Cisco works with Nvidia in some areas while simultaneously developing its own networking silicon and AI infrastructure approach. HPE and Juniper have aligned with semiconductor manufacturer AMD on certain initiatives while maintaining partnerships with Nvidia. Those overlapping relationships illustrate how vendors are attempting to balance participation in Nvidia's ecosystem against the risk of dependence on it, Butler said.

  The prior competitive model, in which companies such as Cisco, Arista, and HPE/Juniper built large market positions by selling networking platforms into enterprise and cloud environments, differs from Nvidia's approach of selling Ethernet as one component of a broader AI factory architecture. Winning AI infrastructure deals may now require strategies that span compute, networking, and software rather than competitive switch specifications or pricing alone, Butler said.

  Hyperscale cloud providers and AI-focused operators have driven the bulk of recent spending. Butler said the next competitive phase is likely to unfold in the enterprise segment, where incumbents carry decades of customer relationships, operational expertise, and installed infrastructure. "That is the next battleground," he said.

  Whether Nvidia can extend its AI factory momentum into enterprise networking, and whether incumbents can defend that ground, will determine how durable its current market lead proves to be, Butler said.
body_zh: |
  根據市場研究機構IDC報告，輝達（Nvidia）於2026年第一季在資料中心乙太網路交換器市場拿下21.5%市占率，該季交換器營收年增193%至21億美元，正式躍居市場龍頭。這一成績標誌著輝達從兩年前不足4%的市占大幅攀升，並超越網路設備老牌廠商Arista Networks與思科系統（Cisco Systems）。

  IDC指出，同季Arista在資料中心乙太網路交換器市場的市占率為20.7%，思科則佔17.8%。以商品化交換器硬體銷售、不掛品牌廠商名稱的白牌製造商，同樣在市場中維持相當份額。

  整體乙太網路交換器市場於該季年增39.8%，規模達154億美元。其中資料中心交換器——即大型設施內部用於互連伺服器與儲存設備的設備——佔了100億美元，年增幅達61%。按傳輸速率區分，每埠可達每秒800千兆位元的800G交換器，佔資料中心交換器營收的35.8%；200G與400G平台合計則佔另外34.1%，IDC表示。

  ## 整合式AI平台帶動採購模式轉變

  輝達在交換器市場的崛起，與AI基礎建設的採購方式改變息息相關，而非單純取決於網路規格。輝達將其Spectrum-X乙太網路交換平台與BlueField資料處理單元（DPU）、LinkX纜線與光模組，以及CUDA軟體堆疊——即用於在輝達繪圖處理器（GPU）上執行工作負載的程式設計環境——整合銷售。這樣的組合讓買家能直接取得預先整合的架構來建置AI叢集，而無需分別採購運算與網路設備。

  「Spectrum-X之所以能在資料中心乙太網路交換器市場大幅搶占市占，是因為它是以GPU與網路設備緊密整合套件的形式銷售，針對AI工廠工作負載進行最佳化，而非以獨立的交換架構出售，」IDC網路基礎建設與服務資深研究經理Brandon Butler在接受Data Center Knowledge採訪時表示。

  HyperFrame Research網路與基礎建設副總裁暨業務主管Ron Westfall則指出，這一轉變反映業者在資料中心採購方式上的根本改變。「企業不再將網路採購視為孤立的基礎建設孤島，而是將其視為更廣泛GPU叢集投資中不可或缺、預先最佳化的組成部分，」他說。

  Spectrum-X在技術面具備適合AI工作負載的特性，包括壅塞管理、降低封包遺失以及低延遲資料傳輸，但Westfall表示，輝達更廣泛的平台地位才是更大的競爭優勢。「輝達在資料中心乙太網路交換器市場飆升至21.5%市占率的驅動力，在於AI基礎建設的演變，而非傳統的網路競爭邏輯，」他說。

  ## 輝達網路營收達148億美元

  IDC的市占率調查結果與輝達自身公布的財報相互印證。輝達在其2027財年第一季的資料中心網路營收達148億美元，年增199%。該季資料中心總營收達752億美元，年增92%。

  輝達的網路業務範疇遠不止乙太網路交換器，還涵蓋InfiniBand高速互連、網路介面卡、DPU、光模組、纜線及相關硬體設備，該公司表示。

  「AI工廠的建設——人類歷史上規模最大的基礎建設擴張——正以驚人的速度加速推進，」輝達執行長黃仁勳在公司財報新聞稿中表示。

  ## 企業市場被視為下一個競爭戰場

  面對輝達的強勢崛起，傳統交換器廠商各自採取了不同的應對策略。思科在部分領域與輝達合作，同時也在自行開發網路晶片與AI基礎建設方案。惠普企業（HPE）與瞻博網路（Juniper）在特定項目上與半導體廠商AMD結盟，但同時也維持與輝達的合作關係。這些交錯的合作關係說明各廠商如何在參與輝達生態系統與規避過度依賴風險之間尋求平衡，Butler說。

  過去的競爭模式，是思科、Arista、HPE／Juniper等廠商靠著將網路平台銷售至企業與雲端環境來建立龐大的市場地位，這與輝達將乙太網路作為更廣泛AI工廠架構中一項元件來銷售的方式截然不同。如今要贏得AI基礎建設訂單，或許需要橫跨運算、網路與軟體的全方位策略，而非單靠具競爭力的交換器規格或定價，Butler說。

  超大規模雲端服務業者與以AI為核心的業者，帶動了近期支出成長的主體。Butler表示，下一個競爭階段可能在企業市場展開，而現有廠商在此握有數十年的客戶關係、運營專業知識與已建置的基礎建設。「那才是下一個戰場，」他說。

  輝達能否將其AI工廠的氣勢延伸至企業網路市場，而既有廠商又能否守住這塊陣地，將決定輝達目前市場領先地位究竟能維持多久，Butler說。
