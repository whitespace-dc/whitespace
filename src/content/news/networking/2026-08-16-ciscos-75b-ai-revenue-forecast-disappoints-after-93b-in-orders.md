body_en: |
  Cisco Systems projected $7.5 billion in sales tied to the artificial intelligence data-center build-out for fiscal year 2027, a figure that disappointed investors after the world's largest networking equipment supplier had accumulated $9.3 billion in AI-related orders over the past year.

  The AI revenue target, disclosed Wednesday alongside the company's fourth-quarter earnings, represents roughly 10% of Cisco's projected total revenue of $72.2 billion to $73.4 billion for the fiscal year. It was the first time the San Jose, California-based company had issued a full-year AI revenue forecast.

  Analysts immediately questioned the relationship between the order total and the revenue projection. In the quarter ended July 25 alone, Cisco recorded $4 billion in AI-related orders — more than half the annual revenue figure it forecast for all of fiscal 2027.

  "That just strikes me as very, very conservative," David Vogt, an analyst at UBS, said on a conference call with Cisco executives.

  Cisco shares fell about 4% in extended trading after closing at $123.88 in New York. The stock had gained nearly 25% over the prior three months as investors bet that Cisco's AI focus would translate into higher sales.

  ## CEO Explains the Order-to-Revenue Gap

  Chief Executive Officer Chuck Robbins said the company generated approximately $4 billion in AI revenue in fiscal 2026 even as orders totaled more than $9 billion over the same period. He attributed the gap to the nature of large AI procurement deals.

  "These are non-linear orders that are massive in scale and are usually placed well ahead of time," Robbins said, describing the AI sales guidance as "a good, prudent guide for the year."

  The dynamic he described — customers placing large forward orders that convert to revenue over extended delivery schedules — means order volumes and near-term revenue can diverge significantly, particularly when deals are measured in the billions of dollars.

  ## Quarterly Results Beat Estimates

  The reaction to the AI guidance overshadowed quarterly results that came in ahead of analyst expectations across the board.

  Fourth-quarter revenue rose 18% from a year earlier to $17.3 billion, compared with an average analyst projection of $16.8 billion, according to Bloomberg data. Profit, excluding certain items, was $1.22 per share against an average estimate of $1.17.

  For the first quarter, which runs through October, Cisco forecast revenue of $18 billion to $18.2 billion, well above the $16.8 billion analyst consensus. Adjusted earnings per share guidance of $1.32 to $1.34 also exceeded estimates.

  ## Restructuring and Competitive Pressure

  Cisco has been reorganizing its business to pursue more contracts tied to the AI market. Earlier this year the company announced a reorganization focused more closely on AI, with associated job cuts expected to generate as much as $1 billion in severance costs and other one-time expenses.

  The shift comes as Cisco faces rising competition for AI infrastructure contracts from Broadcom Inc. and Hewlett Packard Enterprise Co., the company acknowledged.

  ## Security Business Shows Growth

  Cisco's security business recorded $2.23 billion in fourth-quarter sales, up 14% from a year earlier. Chief Financial Officer Mark Patterson said he expected AI to accelerate further growth in that segment, noting that increasingly capable AI models are proving able to identify and exploit cybersecurity vulnerabilities.

  "From that perspective, that's creating a shift of dollars from other areas in the organisations to information technology to actually do that work," Patterson said.

  Despite its AI pivot, Cisco continues to rely on traditional networking and security businesses for the majority of its revenue, with AI sales projected to account for roughly one in ten dollars of fiscal 2027 turnover.

  For the quarter ending in October, Cisco guided for revenue of $18 billion to $18.2 billion, implying continued double-digit growth as the company attempts to convert its $9.3 billion AI order backlog into recognized sales.

  ## 思科AI營收預測75億美元令市場失望，訂單已達93億

  全球最大網路設備供應商思科系統（Cisco Systems）預測，2027財年與人工智慧資料中心建置相關的營收將達75億美元。然而，該公司過去一年已累積93億美元的AI相關訂單，這份預測數字令投資人大感失望。

  AI營收目標於週三隨第四季財報一同揭露，約佔思科預估2027財年總營收722億至734億美元的10%。這也是這家總部位於加州聖荷西的公司首度公布全年AI營收預測。

  分析師隨即對訂單總額與營收預測之間的落差提出質疑。僅在截至7月25日的單一季度，思科便錄得40億美元的AI相關訂單——超過該公司預測整個2027財年AI營收的一半以上。

  「這讓我覺得非常、非常保守，」瑞銀（UBS）分析師大衛·沃格特（David Vogt）在與思科高層的法說會電話中表示。

  思科股價在紐約收盤於123.88美元後，盤後交易下跌約4%。此前三個月，該股已上漲近25%，反映投資人押注思科的AI布局將轉化為更高營收。

  ## 執行長說明訂單與營收的落差

  執行長查克·羅賓斯（Chuck Robbins）表示，儘管同期訂單總額逾90億美元，思科在2026財年仍創下約40億美元的AI營收。他將這項落差歸因於大型AI採購案的特性。

  「這些訂單規模龐大且非線性，通常都是提前很長時間下單，」羅賓斯說道，並形容此次AI營收指引為「一份穩健審慎的全年預測」。

  他所描述的現象——客戶下達大量預先訂單，再依延長的交貨排程逐步轉化為營收——意味著訂單量與近期營收可能出現顯著差距，尤其是當交易金額動輒達數十億美元時更為明顯。

  ## 單季財報全面超越預期

  市場對AI指引的反應，掩蓋了思科這份全面優於分析師預期的季度財報。

  第四季營收較去年同期成長18%，達173億美元，高於彭博資訊統計的分析師平均預測168億美元。排除特定項目後，每股獲利為1.22美元，優於平均預期的1.17美元。

  針對截至10月底的第一季，思科預測營收介於180億至182億美元之間，遠高於分析師共識預期的168億美元。調整後每股盈餘指引為1.32至1.34美元，同樣超越市場預估。

  ## 組織重整與競爭壓力

  思科正重整業務架構，以爭取更多與AI市場相關的合約。今年稍早，該公司宣布以更聚焦AI為核心的組織重組計畫，相關裁員預計將產生最高達10億美元的資遣費及其他一次性支出。

  思科坦承，此一轉型正值博通（Broadcom Inc.）與慧與科技（Hewlett Packard Enterprise Co.）在AI基礎設施合約市場上競爭日益激烈之際。

  ## 資安業務展現成長動能

  思科資安業務第四季營收達22.3億美元，較去年同期成長14%。財務長馬克·帕特森（Mark Patterson）表示，他預期AI將進一步加速這個部門的成長，並指出能力日益增強的AI模型正被證明能夠識別並利用資安漏洞。

  「從這個角度來看，這正在推動各組織將資金從其他領域轉移到資訊技術，以實際應對這項工作，」帕特森說。

  儘管積極轉型AI，思科的營收大宗仍來自傳統網路與資安業務，AI營收預計僅佔2027財年總營收的約十分之一。

  在截至10月的當季，思科預測營收為180億至182億美元，意味著在持續轉換93億美元AI訂單積壓為已認列營收的過程中，該公司將維持兩位數的成長態勢。
