  ## CPO displaces copper as AI bandwidth demands hit 1.6T

  Co-packaged optics (CPO) — a technology that bonds optical engines directly onto the same substrate as processor chips — has emerged as the central focus of optical communications discussions at COMPUTEX 2026, as conventional copper-based interconnects approach fundamental physical limits that block the expansion of large-scale AI computing clusters, according to analysis by DIGITIMES.

  AI compute infrastructure is rapidly transitioning from 800-gigabit to 1.6-terabit per second transmission speeds. At those bandwidths, copper solutions including direct attach cables (DAC) — short, passive cables used to link hardware within a rack — encounter what the DIGITIMES analysis describes as a "one-meter limit": beyond approximately one meter, signal attenuation and power draw increase sharply. That constraint confines copper-based connectivity to a single server rack and makes it unable to support the coordinated computation required by cross-rack, hyperscale GPU clusters.

  ## What CPO changes, and by how much

  CPO addresses those constraints by collapsing the physical gap between an optical engine and its paired processing chip. In a conventional arrangement, that gap exceeds 100 millimeters; CPO reduces it to 0.1 millimeters by co-locating both components on a single substrate. According to DIGITIMES, the architectural change cuts per-bit energy consumption by up to five times and reduces network latency by 20 to 30 times compared with conventional optical module designs.

  Silicon photonics — the manufacturing process that enables light-based signaling on standard chip substrates and forms the core of CPO — underpins the technology. Global silicon photonics production capacity is currently limited, and that constraint is shaping near-term competitive dynamics.

  ## Market forecast and capacity race

  DIGITIMES forecasts the global data center CPO market will grow at a compound annual growth rate of 140% from 2026 through 2030. The firm characterizes the competitive focus among hyperscalers — the large cloud and AI infrastructure operators that deploy data centers at scale — as having shifted from GPU procurement volume to overall network transmission efficiency.

  In response to limited silicon photonics foundry output, hyperscalers have begun reserving wafer fabrication allocations at chip manufacturers through 2028, according to the DIGITIMES analysis, to ensure their infrastructure upgrades are not blocked by copper-era bottlenecks.

  ## Anthropic contract illustrates operational pressure

  Transmission bottlenecks have already affected at least one major AI operator's expansion plans, the analysis notes. With Anthropic's quarterly revenue approaching $10.9 billion, the company's operational expansion has nonetheless been constrained by a lack of optimized network architecture, according to the Commercial Times report citing DIGITIMES observations.

  To secure access to high-density interconnect infrastructure and hedge against hardware transmission constraints, Anthropic has signed a contract to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion per month through May 2029 for access to the dense internal interconnect infrastructure of the Colossus 1 supercomputer, the report states.

  ## What the shift means for the industry

  Hyperscalers that secure CPO production allocations in the near term stand to gain durable advantages in operating efficiency and cost control, while those that delay a transition away from copper-based architectures face the prospect of a material performance ceiling before 2030, the DIGITIMES analysis concludes.

  Data center power consumption and transmission constraints are expected to tighten further as AI workloads scale, and the current race to reserve silicon photonics wafer capacity through 2028 will in large part determine which operators are positioned to run the next generation of GPU clusters without architectural bottlenecks.

  ## CPO取代銅纜，AI頻寬需求挑戰1.6T極限

  共封裝光學（Co-Packaged Optics，CPO）——一種將光學引擎直接與處理器晶片封裝在同一基板上的技術——已成為2026年COMPUTEX光通訊議題的核心焦點。根據DIGITIMES的分析，傳統銅纜互連方案正逼近物理極限，已無法支撐大規模AI運算叢集的持續擴張。

  AI運算基礎架構正快速從800Gbps向每秒1.6Tb的傳輸速率過渡。在如此高的頻寬需求下，包括直接連接電纜（DAC）在內的銅纜方案——這類短程被動式電纜通常用於機架內的硬體連接——面臨DIGITIMES分析所描述的「一公尺限制」：訊號衰減與功耗在超過約一公尺後將急劇上升。這項物理限制將銅纜連接侷限於單一伺服器機架之內，使其無力支援跨機架、超大規模GPU叢集所需的協同運算。

  ## CPO的改變，以及改變的幅度

  CPO透過大幅縮短光學引擎與配對處理晶片之間的實體距離，解決了上述瓶頸。在傳統架構中，兩者之間的距離超過100毫米；CPO則將雙方整合於同一基板，使距離縮短至0.1毫米。根據DIGITIMES的數據，這項架構變革可將每位元能耗降低最多五倍，並使網路延遲比傳統光學模組設計減少20至30倍。

  矽光子技術——一種在標準晶片基板上實現光訊號傳輸的製造工藝，也是CPO的技術核心——是支撐這項技術的根基。目前全球矽光子產能有限，這一供給限制正在重塑近期的市場競爭格局。

  ## 市場預測與產能爭奪戰

  DIGITIMES預測，全球資料中心CPO市場從2026年至2030年的年複合成長率將達140%。該機構指出，超大規模業者——即大規模部署資料中心的雲端與AI基礎架構營運商——之間的競爭焦點，已從GPU採購量轉向整體網路傳輸效率。

  為應對矽光子晶圓廠產能有限的現況，超大規模業者已開始向晶片製造商預訂晶圓製造配額，並鎖定產能至2028年，以確保其基礎架構升級不因銅纜時代的瓶頸而受阻，DIGITIMES分析指出。

  ## Anthropic合約揭示營運壓力

  傳輸瓶頸已對至少一家主要AI業者的擴張計畫造成影響。報告指出，儘管Anthropic單季營收接近109億美元，該公司的業務擴張仍因缺乏最佳化的網路架構而受到制約，此為《工商時報》引述DIGITIMES觀察所呈現的結果。

  為確保能夠取得高密度互連基礎架構，並規避硬體傳輸限制帶來的風險，Anthropic已簽署合約，每月支付SpaceX 12.5億美元，以換取使用Colossus 1超級電腦密集內部互連基礎架構的權利，合約期限至2029年5月，報告如此說明。

  ## 這場轉變對產業意味著什麼

  DIGITIMES分析的結論指出，在近期率先取得CPO產能配額的超大規模業者，將在營運效率與成本控制上獲得長期的競爭優勢；反之，遲遲未能從銅纜架構轉型的業者，則可能在2030年前就碰觸到嚴峻的效能天花板。

  隨著AI工作負載持續擴大，資料中心的電力消耗與傳輸瓶頸預計將進一步吃緊。眼前這場搶訂矽光子晶圓產能至2028年的競賽，將在很大程度上決定哪些營運商有能力在不受架構瓶頸制約的情況下，順利運行下一世代的GPU叢集。
