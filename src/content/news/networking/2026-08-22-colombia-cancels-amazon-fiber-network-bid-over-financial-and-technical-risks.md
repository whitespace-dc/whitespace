body_en: |
  Colombia's government has canceled the bidding process for a fiber optic network stretching more than 1,600 kilometers through the Amazon basin, citing financial and technical risks that rendered the original procurement unworkable, the Ministry of Information and Communications Technology (ICT) announced August 21.

  The cancellation comes roughly three months after the government approved CONPES 4195 — a national economic and social policy document that had formally committed state resources to the project's implementation, operation, and maintenance through 2035. At the time, the Ministry of ICT described the initiative as one of its flagship digital infrastructure programs.

  The project carried a projected investment of approximately 1.14 trillion Colombian pesos and was structured over a financing term of 10.5 years. It was designed to connect close to 100,000 households and 227,000 people across the departments of Amazonas and Putumayo, two of the country's most geographically isolated regions.

  ## What the network was designed to do

  Beyond bridging the local connectivity gap, the project had an explicit international dimension. Engineers planned to route fiber optic cables under the Putumayo and Amazon rivers — a sub-river deployment technique — as well as overland, linking towns including Puerto Asís, Puerto Leguízamo, Leticia, and Puerto Nariño to Colombia's national backbone.

  The design called for cross-border connections to Brazil's InfoVías network and, through that link, to the South American Crossing (SAC) submarine cable, which lands at the Pacific port of Buenaventura. That configuration would have created alternative routing paths for data traffic between Colombia's Atlantic and Pacific coasts, adding resilience to the country's long-haul telecommunications infrastructure.

  ## Why the bid collapsed

  The Ministry of ICT attributed the withdrawal to the technical and economic complexity of deploying fiber through a vast region characterized by low population density, limited physical infrastructure, and waterways that require specialized sub-river cable-laying methods. Those conditions, the ministry said, increased both construction costs and the operational and maintenance risks associated with running a network in such terrain.

  The government did not specify which particular cost estimates or risk assessments triggered the decision, nor did it indicate whether a revised version of the project would be put back out to tender.

  ## What comes next

  The cancellation does not formally close the question of Amazonian fiber connectivity. According to the Ministry of ICT, the government must now determine how to restructure the project and identify which deployment model — whether a different financing structure, phased construction, satellite augmentation, or another approach — could be technically and financially viable.

  The strategic rationale for the corridor remains intact: the infrastructure was intended both to serve communities with limited digital access and to integrate Colombia's Amazon into national and international backbone networks. The government has not announced a timeline for a revised procurement or an alternative connectivity plan for the affected region.

  哥倫比亞政府宣布取消一項穿越亞馬遜盆地、延伸逾一千六百公里的光纖網路招標程序，理由是財務與技術風險使原有採購模式窒礙難行。資訊與通訊科技部（ICT）於八月二十一日正式公告此一決定。

  此次取消距政府批准CONPES 4195約三個月。CONPES 4195是一份國家經濟社會政策文件，正式承諾國家資源投入該計畫的執行、營運及維護工作，期限延伸至二○三五年。當時，資通訊部將此計畫定位為旗艦數位基礎建設項目之一。

  該計畫預估投資金額約一兆一千四百億哥倫比亞披索，融資期限為十年半。計畫服務範圍涵蓋亞馬遜省與普圖馬約省——哥倫比亞地理位置最偏遠的兩個省分——預計連結近十萬戶家庭、二十二萬七千名居民。

  ## 網路的設計目標

  除彌補當地的數位落差外，此計畫另具明確的國際連結意義。工程師規劃採用河床穿越技術，將光纖電纜鋪設於普圖馬約河與亞馬遜河河底，同時搭配陸上線路，將阿西斯港、萊吉薩莫港、萊蒂西亞及納里尼奧港等城鎮接入哥倫比亞國家骨幹網路。

  設計方案規劃跨境連接至巴西的InfoVías網路，並透過該鏈路進一步接入在太平洋港口布埃納文圖拉登陸的「南美橫越」（SAC）海底電纜。此一架構將為哥倫比亞大西洋沿岸與太平洋沿岸之間的資料流量建立備援路由，強化國家長途電信基礎設施的整體韌性。

  ## 標案為何告吹

  資通訊部將撤案歸因於在一片人口稀疏、基礎設施匱乏的廣大地區部署光纖所衍生的技術與經濟複雜性——該地區河道縱橫，須採用特殊的水下電纜鋪設工法。部方指出，上述條件不僅大幅推高建設成本，也加重了在此類地形長期維運網路的風險。

  政府並未具體說明是哪項成本估算或風險評估促成最終決定，亦未透露是否有意以修訂版本重新招標。

  ## 後續發展

  取消標案並未正式終結亞馬遜地區光纖連網的議題。據資通訊部說明，政府現須研議如何重新架構計畫，並評估哪種部署模式——無論是調整融資結構、分階段施工、引入衛星輔助，抑或其他方案——在技術與財務層面切實可行。

  這條廊道的戰略意義依然不變：相關基礎設施肩負雙重目標，既在服務數位近用受限的偏遠社區，也在將哥倫比亞亞馬遜地區整合入國家與國際骨幹網路。目前，政府尚未公布修訂採購方案的時程，也尚未宣布針對受影響地區的替代連網計畫。
