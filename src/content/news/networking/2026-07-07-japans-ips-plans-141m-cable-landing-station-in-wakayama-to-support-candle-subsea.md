  ## IPS announces Wakayama cable landing station

  Japanese telecommunications company IPS is planning to build a cable landing station (CLS) in the city of Wakayama, near Osaka, at an initial estimated cost of 23 billion yen ($141 million), the company announced Thursday, July 2. The figure covers both the CLS facility and the cost of developing a branch of the Candle subsea cable project.

  IPS said it is commencing a feasibility study on the project with the "intention of commercializing" it.

  ## The Candle cable system

  Candle is an approximately 8,000-kilometer (4,971-mile) subsea cable system announced in 2025. It is led by SoftBank and Meta, with participation from IPS, Telkom Malaysia, and Indonesia's XL Smart. The cable is designed to connect Japan, Taiwan, the Philippines, Indonesia, Malaysia, and Singapore, and is scheduled to begin operations in 2028.

  In Japan, Candle is also set to land at SoftBank's Maruyama landing station in Minamiboso City, at the southern edge of Chiba Prefecture, giving the system two Japanese landing points.

  ## IPS's role

  IPS is a participant in the Candle consortium and also owns Infinivan, a Philippines-based consumer network business. The Wakayama CLS, if developed, would serve as IPS's own landing infrastructure for the cable branch it is contributing to the project.

  ## What comes next

  With the feasibility study now underway, the next formal milestone will be IPS's decision on whether to proceed to commercialization of the Wakayama facility ahead of Candle's targeted 2028 service launch.

  ## IPS宣布在和歌山興建海纜登陸站

  日本電信業者IPS宣布，計畫在大阪近郊的和歌山市興建一座海纜登陸站（Cable Landing Station，CLS），初期估計總費用為230億日圓（約1.41億美元），涵蓋登陸站設施本體，以及開發Candle海底電纜專案支線的相關費用。IPS於7月2日（週四）正式對外公布此消息。

  IPS表示，目前已啟動該專案的可行性評估，並以「推動商業化」為明確目標。

  ## Candle海底電纜系統

  Candle是一條全長約8,000公里（4,971英里）的海底電纜系統，於2025年正式宣布啟動，由軟銀（SoftBank）與Meta共同主導，參與方還包括IPS、馬來西亞電信（Telkom Malaysia）及印尼的XL Smart。這條電纜規劃連接日本、台灣、菲律賓、印尼、馬來西亞與新加坡，預計2028年正式投入服務。

  在日本境內，Candle除和歌山外，亦預計在千葉縣南端的南房總市，於軟銀的丸山登陸站登岸，使整個系統在日本擁有兩個登陸點。

  ## IPS在專案中的角色

  IPS是Candle聯盟的成員之一，同時也持有在菲律賓經營消費者網路業務的Infinivan。若和歌山登陸站順利落成，將作為IPS為該專案所貢獻之電纜支線的自有登陸基礎設施。

  ## 後續進展

  隨著可行性評估正式展開，IPS下一個重要里程碑，將是決定是否推進和歌山設施的商業化進程，以趕上Candle原定2028年的服務啟動目標。
