body_en: |
  Oracle has made Oracle Interconnect for AWS generally available, offering companies a private managed connection between Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) and Amazon Web Services without routing traffic over the public internet.

  The service launches first in Oracle's Ashburn region paired with AWS US East (N. Virginia), one of the largest cloud infrastructure hubs in the United States. Oracle said additional region pairs will follow, with multinational customers requiring low-latency links in other geographies the stated target for that expansion.

  The product was previously available on a limited basis to selected customers before the wider launch. General availability positions it for broader production use.

  ## What the service does

  Oracle Interconnect for AWS allows customers to run applications and move data between OCI and AWS through a private connection managed through each platform's standard cloud interfaces. Oracle described it as "an on-demand private connection that network and security leaders create and manage through familiar cloud interfaces, while the service itself handles the underlying connectivity complexity."

  Target use cases identified by Oracle include distributed application architectures, phased cloud migrations, and workloads requiring private traffic flows between the two providers. Oracle also cited data-heavy and AI-related workloads as intended applications, reflecting demand for moving large volumes of data between cloud services without relying on the public internet.

  Customers can provision capacity on demand and increase connection capacity over time without downtime or the need to rearchitect systems, Oracle said.

  ## Pricing and security

  The service uses OCI's globally uniform connection pricing and does not impose egress fees. Customers pay for the connection capacity they provision rather than incurring separate data transfer charges — a structure Oracle positioned as easier to forecast than conventional multicloud networking costs, where egress fees can be difficult to predict.

  Always-on encryption is available for traffic traversing the link. The connection supports private application-to-application traffic and data movement over a private network.

  On support, Oracle said customers use standard support channels, with Oracle and AWS coordinating directly when an issue involves traffic moving between the two providers.

  ## Why the multicloud networking market is growing

  Networking between separate cloud environments has increasingly become a distinct operational challenge. When businesses run different parts of an application stack across multiple providers — keeping databases, analytics, applications, and AI workloads in separate environments for cost, technical, or regulatory reasons — connecting those environments typically requires third-party contracts, additional security controls, and bespoke support arrangements.

  Oracle framed the interconnect as a way to reduce that operational burden, allowing customers to use existing cloud interfaces rather than standing up custom connectivity solutions.

  The move reflects a broader shift among large cloud providers toward accepting that major customers frequently do not standardise on a single platform. Oracle said it has increasingly focused on making OCI part of mixed cloud environments rather than requiring customers to pursue an all-in migration.

  ## What comes next

  Oracle's immediate commercial challenge is attracting customers who currently rely on third-party connectivity arrangements or public internet-based links between OCI and AWS. The northern Virginia launch gives the service access to customers already operating substantial deployments with both providers in that region. Expansion to additional region pairs is expected to broaden the addressable market further.

  ## 甲骨文與AWS私有互連正式上線，首站落地維吉尼亞州

  甲骨文宣布「Oracle Interconnect for AWS」正式全面上市，讓企業能夠在甲骨文雲端基礎架構（OCI）與Amazon Web Services（AWS）之間，透過私有託管連線傳輸資料與應用程式流量，完全無需繞經公開網路。

  此服務首先在甲骨文的阿什本區域，搭配AWS美東（北維吉尼亞）區域推出，這一組合正是美國規模最大的雲端基礎架構核心之一。甲骨文表示，後續將陸續新增更多區域配對，主要目標客群為需要在不同地理位置建立低延遲連線的跨國企業。

  此產品先前已在限定客戶中提供試用，正式全面上市後，將開放更廣泛的生產環境部署。

  ## 服務功能說明

  「Oracle Interconnect for AWS」讓客戶能夠透過私有連線，在OCI與AWS之間執行應用程式、移動資料，並透過各平台的標準雲端介面進行管理。甲骨文將其描述為「一種按需建立的私有連線，由網路與資安人員透過熟悉的雲端介面進行建立與管理，而服務本身則負責處理底層的連線複雜性。」

  甲骨文指出的目標使用情境包括：分散式應用程式架構、分階段雲端遷移，以及需要在兩大供應商之間保持私有流量的工作負載。甲骨文亦特別提及資料密集型工作負載與AI相關應用，反映出市場對於在雲端服務之間大量傳輸資料、同時避免走公開網路的需求正持續增長。

  甲骨文表示，客戶可按需佈建連線容量，並可隨時間增加容量，無需停機或重新架構系統。

  ## 定價與資安

  此服務採用OCI全球統一的連線定價，不另外收取出口費用。客戶僅需為所佈建的連線容量付費，而非另行承擔資料傳輸費用。甲骨文將此定價結構定位為比傳統多雲端網路成本更易於預測，因為傳統方案的出口費用往往難以估算。

  此連線支援永遠啟用的加密機制，可對所有流經此連線的流量進行加密保護，並支援應用程式對應用程式的私有流量，以及透過私有網路進行的資料移動。

  在支援服務方面，甲骨文表示客戶可透過標準支援管道提出申請，若問題涉及在兩大供應商之間流通的流量，甲骨文與AWS將直接協調處理。

  ## 多雲端網路市場的成長背景

  跨雲端環境的網路連線，已日益成為一項獨立的營運挑戰。當企業將應用程式堆疊的不同部分，分散在多個供應商上運行——例如因成本、技術或法規因素，將資料庫、資料分析、應用程式與AI工作負載分置於不同環境——連結這些環境通常需要額外簽訂第三方合約、建立更多資安控管措施，以及量身打造的支援安排。

  甲骨文將此互連服務定位為降低上述營運負擔的解方，讓客戶能直接沿用現有雲端介面，而非另起爐灶建立客製化的連線解決方案。

  此舉也反映出大型雲端供應商之間一個更廣泛的轉變：業者逐漸接受主要客戶往往不會只選擇單一平台。甲骨文表示，公司近年來越來越著重於讓OCI融入混合雲端環境，而不再要求客戶全面遷移至甲骨文生態系。

  ## 後續發展

  甲骨文當前面臨的商業挑戰，在於吸引那些目前仰賴第三方連線方案，或透過公開網路連接OCI與AWS的客戶。北維吉尼亞州的首站上線，讓此服務得以接觸在該區域同時擁有OCI與AWS大規模部署的客戶群。預計後續擴展至更多區域配對後，將進一步擴大可服務的市場規模。
