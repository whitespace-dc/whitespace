body_en: |
  More than three-quarters of UK data center operators are hitting critical bottlenecks in sourcing cables for AI workloads, with 41% saying cabling always or frequently delays AI capacity projects, according to research by data center infrastructure specialist Onnec, as reported by TechRadar Pro.

  The findings coincide with a warning from a senior executive at Tratos, a British cable manufacturer founded in 1966 with seven factories and customers in 36 countries, that data center developers routinely treat physical connectivity procurement as a secondary concern, ordering fibre and power cables too late to avoid schedule slippage. Physical connectivity is foundational: the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) estimates that submarine cables carry approximately 99% of the world's internet traffic, and the International Cable Protection Committee (ICPC) recorded more than 170 cable repairs worldwide in 2025, close to four per week.

  ## Investment scale amplifies supply risk

  McKinsey estimates that data centers worldwide will require $6.7 trillion in investment by 2030. Of that total, approximately $1.3 trillion is directed at power generation, transmission, cooling and electrical equipment, the physical infrastructure layer that cable manufacturers supply. The Tratos executive, writing in DataCenterNews Asia Pacific, said that unless developers elevate supply chain and connectivity planning to match the urgency applied to compute procurement, disruption looks inevitable.

  ## Manufacturing constraints already affecting established suppliers

  Tratos itself, an established cable group, has experienced difficulty securing optical fibre deliveries at around US$14 per fibre-kilometre. Supply is constrained by several factors simultaneously: raw material availability, energy prices, factory capacity and competition from grid expansion, renewable energy and telecoms projects drawing on the same production lines. When a supply problem is identified, resolution can take weeks or months, and each week lands directly on the project completion date.

  Britain faces a structural disadvantage: the country retains very few cable manufacturers, the Tratos executive noted, which extends lead times and makes delivery date verification harder.

  ## Late procurement as the core problem

  The root cause identified by Tratos is that procurement runs separately from technical design and the construction programme. Cables and electrical components carry long lead times. Without confirmed factory capacity allocation and secured raw materials, quoted lead times remain estimates. A new submarine cable is a project measured in years from design to operation; data center cabling is a smaller undertaking, but the same procurement principle applies.

  Tratos advises integrating procurement from the first design meeting and specifying cables to recognised industry standards rather than to any single manufacturer's proprietary design, a practice that widens the pool of factories capable of fulfilling an order. For medium-voltage power applications, Tratos said it is currently proposing aluminium conductors as an alternative to copper to British network operators. The substitution can ease pressure on copper supply and shorten lead times where engineering specifications permit.

  McKinsey's $6.7 trillion investment projection implies that pressure on cable supply chains will intensify through the end of the decade. The Tratos executive's position is that chips and cables should be ordered on the same schedule: securing compute hardware without confirming cable supply, factory capacity allocation and firm delivery dates leaves projects exposed to the delays already documented across the UK market.

  根據資料中心基礎設施專家Onnec的研究（由TechRadar Pro報導），逾四分之三的英國資料中心業者在採購AI工作負載所需纜線時遭遇嚴重瓶頸，41%表示纜線問題始終或頻繁導致AI容量擴建計畫延誤。

  這項調查結果發布之際，英國纜線製造商Tratos一名高階主管同步發出警告。Tratos創立於1966年，旗下設有七座工廠，客戶遍及36個國家。該主管指出，資料中心開發商習慣將實體連線採購列為次要事項，往往太晚才訂購光纖與電力纜線，導致工程進度延誤。實體連線是整體架構的根基：國際電信聯盟（ITU）估計，海底纜線承載全球約99%的網際網路流量；國際纜線保護委員會（ICPC）的紀錄顯示，2025年全球共進行逾170次纜線修復作業，平均每週接近四次。

  ## 投資規模擴大供應風險

  麥肯錫估計，全球資料中心在2030年前將需要6.7兆美元的投資。其中約1.3兆美元將投入發電、輸電、冷卻及電氣設備等實體基礎設施層——也就是纜線製造商所供應的領域。Tratos這位主管在《DataCenterNews亞太版》撰文指出，除非開發商將供應鏈與連線規劃提升至與運算採購同等的優先層級，否則供應中斷幾乎已是必然。

  ## 製造產能限制已影響成熟供應商

  即便是Tratos這樣的成熟纜線集團，目前也面臨光纖交貨困難，每光纖公里的採購價格約為14美元。供應吃緊同時受多重因素制約：原材料供應、能源價格、工廠產能，以及電網擴建、再生能源與電信工程爭搶同一批生產線所帶來的競爭壓力。一旦發現供應問題，往往需要數週乃至數月才能解決，而每耽擱一週，就直接反映在專案完工日期上。

  英國還面臨結構性劣勢：該主管指出，英國境內留存的纜線製造商寥寥無幾，這不僅拉長交貨前置時間，也使交期核實更加困難。

  ## 採購過晚才是核心問題

  Tratos點出的根本原因在於，採購流程與技術設計及施工計畫各自為政。纜線與電氣零組件均有漫長的前置時間。若未事先確認工廠產能配置並鎖定原材料，所報的前置時間不過是估算，並非保證。一條新海底纜線從設計到正式啟用需要數年；資料中心內部纜線工程規模雖小，但採購邏輯如出一轍。

  Tratos建議，從第一次設計會議起就將採購納入整合流程，並依據業界公認標準規格選用纜線，而非綁定特定製造商的專有設計——此舉可擴大有能力承接訂單的工廠範圍。針對中壓電力應用，Tratos表示目前正向英國電網業者推薦以鋁導體替代銅導體，在工程規格允許的情況下，此一替換方案可舒緩銅料供應壓力並縮短前置時間。

  麥肯錫6.7兆美元的投資預測意味著，纜線供應鏈所承受的壓力將在本十年結束前持續升溫。Tratos主管的立場是：晶片與纜線應該按照同一時程訂購——在未確認纜線供應、工廠產能配置及明確交期的情況下就搶先採購運算硬體，只會讓專案暴露於英國市場已有前車之鑑的延誤風險之中。
