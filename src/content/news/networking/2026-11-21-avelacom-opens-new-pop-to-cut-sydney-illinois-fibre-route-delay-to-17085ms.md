body_en: |
  Avelacom has launched a fibre connection between the Australian Securities Exchange's (ASX) Australian Liquidity Centre in Sydney and CME Group's Aurora data centre in Illinois, achieving a round-trip delay of 170.85 milliseconds, a reduction of approximately one millisecond from the previous path and what the company says is the fastest fibre route available between the two facilities.

  The connection, announced on 19 November 2026, uses a new North American point of presence (PoP) that provides a more direct optical path. Avelacom has not disclosed the PoP's location.

  The route targets financial trading firms that operate across Australian and US derivatives markets. Lorenz Voss, managing director for the US at Avelacom, told Capacity that firms making markets in ASX SPI 200 futures — contracts that track Australia's benchmark S&P/ASX 200 share index — must respond quickly to price movements in US equity futures during Sydney's night session.

  "If US equity futures move sharply, the firm may need to update its Australian quotes before someone trades against a stale price," Voss said. Similar requirements apply to interest rate trading, where movements in US Treasury futures can prompt desks trading US-Australia yield spreads to adjust positions while both markets are open. "That's where low latency matters. It shortens the time between seeing the move in one market and acting on it in the other," Voss added.

  ## Route resilience and backup paths

  Lower latency is one consideration for trading firms; the performance of backup connections is another. Avelacom supports the 170.85ms primary route with alternative paths through Guam and Tokyo, alongside US connectivity options via Los Angeles, Seattle, Hillsboro and San Jose.

  Voss said customers want to verify that primary and backup connections are physically separate along the full route. "Two circuits can still share a subsea cable, a landing station, or a stretch of terrestrial fibre," he said. "Customers want to understand where those paths overlap and how much latency the backup adds."

  A September 2026 report by the China Strategic Risks Institute, prepared for the US-China Economic and Security Review Commission and titled *China's Gray Zone Threats to Trans-Pacific Undersea Cables*, assessed how disruptions to transpacific cable infrastructure could affect available capacity and latency. In one scenario focused on Taiwan-US connectivity, estimated round-trip delay rose from 231.5ms to 279.2ms when direct routes were overwhelmed. The report also modelled simultaneous disruptions to multiple cables, finding that traffic could be forced onto substantially longer alternative routes with consequences for latency-sensitive financial services. The modelling focused on US-Asia connections rather than Avelacom's Sydney-Illinois route, but it illustrates the importance of evaluating backup performance alongside primary-path speed.

  Avelacom said available backup bandwidth depends on individual customer configurations and cannot be expressed as a single figure. The company did not provide round-trip latency figures for its backup connections.

  ## Transpacific cable investment

  The launch comes during a period of rising investment in transpacific submarine cable infrastructure. According to TeleGeography's April 2026 analysis, *Building Tomorrow's Internet: A 2026 Update on Cable Investment*, more than US$3 billion in construction costs are forecast for new transpacific subsea cables expected to enter service between 2026 and 2029, compared with approximately US$1.7 billion invested over the preceding three years. TeleGeography noted the forecast includes announced projects that may not all reach completion.

  By mid-2026, global international internet bandwidth had reached 2,259 terabits per second (Tbps), up 23% year-on-year, according to TeleGeography data published in September 2026. Oceania recorded a compound annual growth rate of 25% between 2022 and 2026. Content providers dominate overall transpacific usage: they accounted for 80.2% of used transpacific bandwidth in 2024, according to TeleGeography data cited in the OECD's *Digital Economy and Society Outlook 2026*, published in November 2026.

  Voss pointed to Google's Tabua and Honomoana subsea cable projects as examples of how new infrastructure is expanding capacity and route options across the Pacific. Tabua connects Australia with California, with branches to Hawaii and Fiji; Honomoana links Australia and the US through the South Pacific, including French Polynesia and New Zealand. Both form part of Google's South Pacific Connect initiative, announced in October 2023.

  Among forthcoming systems, SUBCO's APX East is a planned 16-fibre-pair subsea cable connecting Sydney with San Diego, California. Unveiled in January 2026, the system is designed to provide a continuous optical path without intermediate landing points or regeneration facilities and is targeting service in the fourth quarter of 2028. In September 2026, Australian artificial intelligence infrastructure company Firmus agreed to invest approximately US$300 million to secure up to 150Tbps of dedicated transpacific capacity on APX East over 25 years.

  For trading firms, Voss said the evaluation comes down to two questions: how fast is the exchange-to-exchange connection, and what performance is available if the primary path goes down.

  Avelacom已建立一條連接澳洲證券交易所（ASX）澳洲流動性中心（位於雪梨）與CME集團伊利諾州Aurora資料中心的光纖連線，往返延遲達170.85毫秒，較原有路徑縮短約一毫秒，公司表示這是目前兩地間最快的光纖路由。

  此連線於2026年11月19日宣布，透過新設的北美接入點（PoP）提供更直接的光路。Avelacom並未公開該接入點的具體位置。

  這條路由的目標客群，是同時在澳洲與美國衍生性金融商品市場布局的金融交易業者。Avelacom美國區董事總經理洛倫茲·沃斯（Lorenz Voss）接受《Capacity》採訪時表示，在ASX SPI 200期貨市場——即追蹤澳洲基準S&P/ASX 200股價指數之合約——從事造市業務的業者，必須在雪梨夜盤期間對美國股票期貨的價格變動做出迅速回應。

  「若美國股票期貨出現急劇波動，業者可能需要在有人以過時報價成交之前，搶先更新澳洲報價，」沃斯說。類似需求同樣適用於利率交易——當美國公債期貨出現波動，負責操作美澳殖利率價差的交易台，便需在兩地市場同時開盤期間及時調整部位。「這正是低延遲發揮關鍵的地方。它縮短了在某一市場看見行情變動、到在另一市場採取行動之間的時間，」沃斯補充道。

  ## 路由韌性與備援路徑

  對金融交易業者而言，低延遲是一項考量，備援連線的表現則是另一項。Avelacom以途經關島與東京的替代路徑，以及經由洛杉磯、西雅圖、希爾斯伯勒與聖荷西的美國端連線選項，為170.85毫秒的主要路由提供備援支援。

  沃斯表示，客戶希望確認主線與備援線路在全程路由中實體分離。「兩條線路仍可能共用同一條海底電纜、登陸站或一段陸地光纖，」他說，「客戶希望了解這些路徑在哪些路段重疊，以及備援線路會增加多少延遲。」

  2026年9月，中國戰略風險研究所受美中經濟暨安全審查委員會委託，發布《中國對跨太平洋海底電纜的灰色地帶威脅》報告，評估跨太平洋電纜基礎設施遭受干擾後，對可用頻寬與延遲的潛在影響。在一個聚焦於台美連線的情境中，當直接路由因流量超載而不堪負荷，往返延遲估計從231.5毫秒上升至279.2毫秒。報告亦模擬多條電纜同時中斷的情境，發現流量可能被迫轉往明顯更長的替代路徑，對延遲敏感的金融服務造成重大衝擊。該建模雖聚焦於美亞連線，而非Avelacom的雪梨至伊利諾路由，但仍凸顯在評估主要路徑速度的同時，審視備援效能的重要性。

  Avelacom表示，可用的備援頻寬取決於個別客戶的組態設定，無法以單一數字呈現。公司亦未提供備援連線的往返延遲數據。

  ## 跨太平洋電纜投資

  此次發布恰逢跨太平洋海底電纜基礎設施投資持續熱絡之際。根據TeleGeography於2026年4月發布的分析報告《建構明日網路：2026年電纜投資最新動態》，預計於2026至2029年間投入服務的新跨太平洋海底電纜，建設成本合計預估逾30億美元，對比前三年約17億美元的投資規模大幅成長。TeleGeography並指出，此預測涵蓋已宣布但未必全數完工的計畫案。

  根據TeleGeography於2026年9月發布的數據，截至2026年中，全球國際網路頻寬已達2,259 Tbps（兆位元每秒），年增23%。大洋洲在2022至2026年間的複合年均成長率達25%。在整體跨太平洋用量方面，內容業者占有主導地位：根據經濟合作暨發展組織（OECD）於2026年11月發布的《數位經濟與社會展望2026》所引用的TeleGeography數據，2024年內容業者占跨太平洋已用頻寬的80.2%。

  沃斯以Google的Tabua與Honomoana海底電纜計畫為例，說明新興基礎設施如何為太平洋地區帶來更充裕的容量與更多元的路由選項。Tabua連接澳洲與加州，並設有延伸至夏威夷與斐濟的支線；Honomoana則途經南太平洋——包括法屬玻里尼西亞與紐西蘭——連結澳洲與美國。兩者均屬於Google於2023年10月宣布的南太平洋連接計畫（South Pacific Connect）的一部分。

  在即將落成的系統中，SUBCO的APX East是一條規劃中的16光纖對海底電纜，計畫連接雪梨與加州聖地牙哥，於2026年1月公開宣布。該系統設計採用連續光路，不設中間登陸點或中繼再生設施，目標於2028年第四季正式投入服務。2026年9月，澳洲人工智慧基礎設施公司Firmus同意投資約3億美元，取得APX East未來25年最高達150 Tbps的跨太平洋專屬頻寬。

  對金融交易業者而言，沃斯表示，評估最終回歸兩個核心問題：交易所之間的連線速度有多快，以及主要路徑中斷時能獲得什麼樣的效能保障。
