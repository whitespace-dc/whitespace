  Chunghwa Leading Photonics is moving into short-wave infrared (SWIR) testing, positioning the company to serve demand generated by advanced semiconductor packaging processes, DigiTimes Asia reported.

  ## What the source supports

  The DigiTimes Asia report identifies Chunghwa Leading Photonics as targeting SWIR testing demand specifically tied to advanced packaging — a segment of chip manufacturing that stacks or interconnects multiple dies in a single module, requiring precise inspection techniques that standard visible-light systems cannot perform.

  SWIR refers to light in the wavelength range beyond what the human eye can see, roughly 1,000 to 2,500 nanometers. Imaging systems operating in this band can see through silicon and certain packaging materials, making them useful for inspecting hidden features in advanced packages such as 2.5D and 3D chip configurations.
