body_en: |
  ServeTheHome has purchased and reviewed a ZX530S-24S2CQ switch built on Centec's CTC7132 chip, marking what the publication described as the first thorough independent test of the chip after finding no prior published review of it online. The unit was bought on AliExpress for just under $575, with comparable units from multiple vendors available in the $480 to $600 range.

  The switch provides twenty-four SFP+ (Small Form-factor Pluggable) ports rated for 10 Gigabit Ethernet or 1 Gigabit Ethernet and two QSFP28 uplink ports rated for 100GbE or 40GbE, all in a 1U rack chassis. The publication benchmarked the two uplink ports as 100GbE links using RS-FEC (Reed-Solomon Forward Error Correction, a technique for reducing bit errors on high-speed optical links). ServeTheHome also purchased a second switch carrying the same chip to extend its testing.

  ## The chip

  The CTC7132 is allegedly Centec's sixth-generation switch chip, according to the review, which used that qualifier in relaying the company's claim. ServeTheHome said it had not previously encountered the chip in its lab. The publication characterized Centec as primarily focused on sales within the domestic Chinese market, which it cited as the reason the chip had seen limited independent attention outside China.

  ## Pricing and availability

  The specific AliExpress seller used for the review unit no longer lists the switch, but units with model names matching the PCB silkscreen remain available from other sellers. The hardware also appears under the Sodola brand on Amazon. The video portion of the review was sponsored by Micro Center.

  ## Hardware design

  The front panel separates the management cluster from the data-plane port field. The management cluster at the left edge holds stacked RJ45 jacks for console and out-of-band Ethernet management, a USB Type-A port, and status LEDs for power, system, ID, and Ethernet. The out-of-band Ethernet jack provides management access independent of the data plane.

  Twenty-four SFP+ cages run across the panel in vertical pairs, numbered 1 through 24. CentecOS, the switch operating system, labels port speeds directly on the faceplate. Two larger QSFP28 cages for ports 25 and 26 sit at the right edge of the panel, marked 100G/40G. A dedicated LED indicator panel covers all twenty-six ports plus a Breakout group, using color coding to distinguish speed classes.

  The rear panel carries four cooling fans and an IEC C14 power inlet rated 100-240V AC, with a chassis bonding point adjacent to the inlet. Included accessories are two L-shaped rack brackets for 19-inch rack mounting, a D-sub console cable, and an Ethernet patch cord.

  ## Significance

  The review places 100GbE uplink capacity at a price point — under $600 — that the mainstream enterprise networking market has not traditionally reached. For planners evaluating low-cost switching for lab, edge, or small-scale production use, the availability of a chip from a supplier largely absent from English-language trade coverage is itself a data point. ServeTheHome indicated its testing produced notable findings — "the answer is yes… yes we did," the publication wrote in its introduction — without disclosing them in the hardware overview portion of the review.   ## ServeTheHome評測AliExpress不到575美元入手的Centec CTC7132交換器

  ServeTheHome購入並評測了一台搭載Centec CTC7132晶片的ZX530S-24S2CQ交換器。該媒體在網路上找不到任何已發表的相關評測，因此將本次測試定位為業界首篇對這顆晶片的完整獨立評估。這台交換器在AliExpress上的購入價格不到575美元，同類產品在各家賣家之間的售價介於480至600美元之間。

  這台交換器提供24個SFP+（小型可插拔）埠，支援10Gbps或1Gbps乙太網路，並配有兩個QSFP28上行埠，支援100GbE或40GbE，整機封裝於1U機架機箱內。ServeTheHome以RS-FEC（Reed-Solomon前向錯誤更正，一種用於降低高速光纖連結位元錯誤率的技術）模式對兩個上行埠進行了100GbE連線的基準測試。為了擴大測試範圍，該媒體另外購入了第二台搭載相同晶片的交換器。

  ## 關於這顆晶片

  根據評測報告所轉述，CTC7132據稱是Centec的第六代交換器晶片；該媒體在引用這項說法時特別加上了「據稱」的限定語。ServeTheHome表示，此前從未在自家實驗室接觸過這顆晶片。該媒體將Centec描述為一家主要專注於中國國內市場的企業，並認為這正是該晶片在中國以外幾乎沒有引起獨立關注的原因。

  ## 售價與購買管道

  提供評測樣品的AliExpress賣家目前已下架這款交換器，但型號與電路板絲印相符的產品在其他賣家處仍可購得。這款硬體也以Sodola品牌在Amazon上架販售。本次評測的影片部分由Micro Center贊助。

  ## 硬體設計

  前面板將管理區塊與資料平面埠區分隔開來。位於左側邊緣的管理區塊設有堆疊式RJ45插孔，分別用於主控台連線與帶外乙太網路管理，另配有一個USB Type-A埠，以及電源、系統、機器識別（ID）與乙太網路的狀態指示燈。帶外乙太網路插孔可提供獨立於資料平面之外的管理存取路徑。

  24個SFP+籠座以垂直兩兩一組的方式橫排於面板上，編號1至24。交換器作業系統CentecOS直接在面板上標示各埠速率。面板右側設有兩個較大的QSFP28籠座，對應第25及第26埠，標示為100G/40G。獨立的LED指示燈面板涵蓋全部26個埠及一個Breakout群組，以顏色區分不同速率等級。

  後面板設有四個散熱風扇及一個IEC C14電源入口，額定電壓為100至240V交流電，入口旁設有機箱接地點。隨附配件包括兩個L型19吋機架安裝托架、一條D-sub主控台線材，以及一條乙太網路跳線。

  ## 市場意義

  本次評測將100GbE上行頻寬帶入了600美元以下的價格帶，而這是主流企業級網路設備市場傳統上從未觸及的區間。對於正在評估低成本交換器以用於實驗室、邊緣運算或小規模正式生產環境的規劃人員而言，一家幾乎在英語科技媒體中銷聲匿跡的供應商推出的晶片進入市場，本身就是一個值得關注的數據點。ServeTheHome在評測導言中暗示測試結果相當出人意料——「答案是肯定的……我們確實做到了」——但在硬體概覽部分並未透露具體細節。完整的基準測試結果詳載於正式發表文章的後續章節中。
