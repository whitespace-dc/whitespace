body_en: |
  Globe Telecom is expanding a network of community-based broadband centers across the Visayas region of the Philippines, with new openings planned this month in Aklan, Capiz, Antique, Samar, Leyte, Bohol, and Cebu, the company said Sunday.

  The centers, operated under Globe's home broadband unit Globe At Home, are designed to give residents in areas far from traditional service hubs a place to get application assistance, learn about fiber plans, and access connectivity services without traveling long distances.

  ## What the centers do

  Globe said the facilities are positioned at the barangay, or village, level to make information about home internet services more visible and accessible in communities that would otherwise have limited contact with the company's service infrastructure. Staff at the centers provide guidance on available fiber plans and help residents apply for services.

  Jimbo B. Cordero, Globe's general manager for the Visayas, said in a statement that reliable connectivity has become essential for education, work, business, and everyday life, and that bringing broadband services closer to communities makes it easier for families to access opportunities tied to a strong internet connection.

  ## Existing footprint

  Globe said it has already established community-based broadband access points in Negros Occidental, Iloilo, Guimaras, Negros Oriental, Samar, Leyte, Bohol, and Cebu. The planned openings this month would deepen coverage in several of those provinces while adding new locations in Aklan, Capiz, and Antique.

  ## Regional push beyond Metro Manila

  The Visayas expansion is part of a broader effort by Globe to strengthen fiber connectivity outside Metro Manila as internet demand grows in provincial areas, the company said. The approach of deploying localized service points reflects a strategy of reducing the friction involved in acquiring home broadband in communities where service awareness and access to support have historically been lower.

  ## Financial backdrop

  Globe reported attributable net income of 5.55 billion Philippine pesos (P5.55 billion) for the first quarter of 2026, a 20 percent decline from the same period a year earlier. The company attributed the drop to higher financing costs and the absence of one-off gains that had boosted the prior-year figure, even as revenue and core earnings grew on the strength of data demand.

  The company said it plans to continue opening additional broadband centers in the Visayas through the remainder of the month.

  菲律賓Globe電信正將社區型寬頻服務中心網絡擴展至維薩亞斯地區，該公司周日宣布，本月將在阿克蘭（Aklan）、卡皮斯（Capiz）、安蒂克（Antique）、薩馬爾（Samar）、萊特（Leyte）、保和（Bohol）及宿霧（Cebu）等省份陸續開設新據點。

  這些服務中心由Globe旗下住宅寬頻部門Globe At Home營運，旨在讓距離傳統服務據點較遠的居民，能有一個就近諮詢申辦流程、了解光纖方案及取得連網服務的地方，免去長途奔波之苦。

  ## 服務中心的功能

  Globe表示，這些設施深入至社區基層的村里（barangay）層級，讓偏遠社區居民能更便利地接觸住宅網路服務的相關資訊。中心工作人員將為居民說明各項可選光纖方案，並協助完成申辦手續。

  Globe維薩亞斯區總經理Jimbo B. Cordero在聲明中指出，穩定的網路連線已成為教育、工作、商業及日常生活不可或缺的基礎，將寬頻服務推進至各個社區，能讓更多家庭更容易把握優質網路所帶來的各種機會。

  ## 現有佈點

  Globe表示，目前已在西內格羅斯（Negros Occidental）、伊洛伊洛（Iloilo）、吉馬拉斯（Guimaras）、東內格羅斯（Negros Oriental）、薩馬爾、萊特、保和及宿霧等省份建立社區型寬頻服務據點。本月計畫新增的據點，將進一步深化上述部分省份的覆蓋密度，並在阿克蘭、卡皮斯及安蒂克等省份新設服務點。

  ## 向大馬尼拉以外擴張

  Globe表示，維薩亞斯的擴張行動是該公司更廣泛戰略布局的一環，目的是在各省網路需求持續攀升之際，強化大馬尼拉以外地區的光纖連線基礎建設。部署在地化服務據點的做法，反映出Globe致力降低民眾申辦住宅寬頻門檻的核心策略——尤其針對服務認知度與支援可及性歷來相對不足的社區。

  ## 財務背景

  Globe公布2026年第一季歸屬母公司淨利為55.5億菲律賓披索，較去年同期下滑20%。該公司將獲利縮水歸因於融資成本上升，以及去年同期曾有一次性收益挹注、今年不再重現；但該公司同時指出，在數據需求強勁帶動下，整體營收與核心獲利均呈現成長。
