body_en: |
  ServeTheHome has reviewed the Fortinet FortiGate FG-60F, a fanless desktop firewall the publication tested back-to-back with the smaller FG-40F model using Keysight CyPerf network performance workloads.

  The FG-60F ships in a white desktop chassis with no active cooling — the same form factor used by the FG-40F — and is positioned for edge deployments. ServeTheHome noted the two units share significant design similarities.

  ## Port layout

  The unit carries ten Gigabit Ethernet RJ-45 ports in total, pre-labeled across several functional roles on the chassis:

  - WAN1 and WAN2 (wide-area network uplinks)
  - DMZ (demilitarized zone, a network segment typically used to host externally accessible services)
  - Two FortiLink ports (Fortinet's proprietary fabric management interface for downstream switches)
  - Five LAN (local area network) ports

  ServeTheHome noted that ports and status indicators are physically located on opposite sides of the unit — front indicators, rear ports — an arrangement the publication said users tend to have strong opinions about.

  ## Chassis and physical security

  The front panel carries power, status, and an HA (high-availability, meaning failover-cluster) status LED indicator, along with per-port status LEDs. The left side houses a reset button, a 12V DC power input with a mechanical latch to prevent accidental disconnection — a design ServeTheHome said is shared with brands such as SonicWall — along with a USB port and a console port.

  A Kensington lock slot is present on one side, which ServeTheHome flagged as relevant for edge deployments where physical security of the appliance itself is a consideration. Passive ventilation is handled through side and bottom vents.

  ## Testing note

  During the review process, ServeTheHome encountered a firmware update failure on the unit and used the console port to interrupt the boot sequence and complete recovery. The publication ran the FG-60F under Keysight CyPerf workloads — a commercial network and application simulation platform — and said the results were consistent with expectations, without providing specific figures in the portion of the review covering the external hardware overview.

  The FG-60F is available for purchase through retail channels including Amazon, and ServeTheHome noted that buyers should confirm the appropriate license and subscription term bundle at the time of purchase.

  ## 連接埠配置

  該機器共配備十個 Gigabit 乙太網路 RJ-45 連接埠，機箱上各埠均已預先標示功能分類：

  - WAN1 與 WAN2（廣域網路上行連線）
  - DMZ（非軍事區，即隔離區，通常用於部署對外開放的服務）
  - 兩個 FortiLink 埠（Fortinet 專屬的 Fabric 管理介面，用於管理下游交換器）
  - 五個 LAN（區域網路）埠

  ServeTheHome 指出，連接埠與狀態指示燈分別位於機器兩側——指示燈在前面板，連接埠在後方——並表示使用者對於這種配置安排，通常都有相當強烈的個人意見。

  ## 機箱與實體安全

  前面板設有電源、狀態及 HA（高可用性，即故障切換叢集）狀態 LED 指示燈，以及各連接埠的狀態 LED。左側則設有重置按鈕、附機械鎖扣的 12V 直流電源輸入——可防止意外拔除，ServeTheHome 指出此設計與 SonicWall 等品牌採用相同做法——另外還有一個 USB 埠及一個主控台埠。

  機器一側設有 Kensington 鎖槽，ServeTheHome 指出此功能在邊緣部署場景中格外值得關注，因為在此類環境中，設備本身的實體安全性是必須納入考量的要素。被動式散熱則透過側面與底部的通風孔來實現。

  ## 測試說明

  在評測過程中，ServeTheHome 遭遇韌體更新失敗的問題，並透過主控台埠中斷開機程序，完成了系統還原作業。該媒體以 Keysight CyPerf 工作負載對 FG-60F 進行測試——這是一套商業網路與應用程式模擬平台——並表示測試結果符合預期，但在涵蓋外部硬體概觀的評測章節中，並未提供具體數據。

  FG-60F 可透過 Amazon 等零售通路購買。ServeTheHome 提醒購買者，應在購買時確認所需的授權類型及訂閱期限組合。
