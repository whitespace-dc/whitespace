body_en: |
  Globe Telecom, PLDT Inc., and Converge ICT Solutions have jointly proposed a $500-million (about P30.63 billion) nationwide undersea fiber-optic network that would extend telecommunications coverage across the Philippine archipelago, the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) said last week.

  ## Proposal details

  DICT Secretary Henry Rhoel R. Aguda said the three carriers, which he described as a consortium, proposed to "fiberize the whole country using undersea cable." The plan calls for laying cables from Batanes — the northernmost Philippine province — to Palawan in the southwest, with links extending to the Visayas and Mindanao island groups.

  The DICT is evaluating the proposal, which remains subject to government approval before implementation can begin. If approved, the project is expected to be completed within two years, Aguda said.

  ## Seismic sensing and defense capability

  The proposed cables would incorporate a digital acoustic sensing system capable of detecting seismic movements associated with earthquakes and tsunamis, enabling real-time data collection, Aguda said. He added that the technology would also have national defense applications, though he did not elaborate.

  ## Complement to the national backbone

  The proposed network is designed to complement — not replace — the government's National Fiber Backbone project, one of the DICT's flagship connectivity programs. That program is intended to improve internet speeds nationwide through high-speed fiber-optic infrastructure and is projected to benefit about 70 million Filipinos. The DICT said in May it was fast-tracking Phases 4 and 5 of the National Fiber Backbone project, with both phases scheduled for completion within the year.

  Aguda said the domestic undersea project would require interagency coordination because it is intended as a domestic network. It is also designed to link all 14 of the country's international points of presence — the cable landing stations that connect the Philippines to global internet systems — according to Aguda.

  ## Carriers' existing international cable positions

  Each of the three proposing carriers already holds positions in international submarine cable systems. Globe joined the Candle Cable System consortium in April to connect the Philippines to submarine cable networks across Asia. PLDT has invested in the Apricot cable system, a 12,000-kilometer undersea network providing a direct link between Singapore and Japan. Converge participates in the Bifrost cable system and the Southeast Asia Hainan-Hong Kong Express Cable System (SEA-H2X). The DICT said the proposed domestic undersea fiber network would connect to these international systems.

  ## What the project would mean

  The undersea routing is intended to serve islands and areas that currently lack telecommunications service and that land-based cables cannot easily reach, according to the DICT. The seismic sensing component would add an early-warning and national defense function alongside the network's commercial connectivity purpose.

  The proposal remains under DICT evaluation with no government decision announced, and a final implementation timeline depends on that approval.
body_zh: |
  Globe電信（Globe Telecom）、菲律賓長途電話公司（PLDT Inc.）與Converge ICT Solutions三家菲律賓最大電信業者，日前聯合提案，擬斥資5億美元（約306.3億披索）鋪設全國性海底光纖網路，將電信覆蓋範圍延伸至菲律賓群島各地。菲律賓資訊通訊技術部（DICT）上週表示，目前正評估該計畫，須待政府核准後方能動工。

  ## 提案內容

  DICT部長Henry Rhoel R. Aguda指出，三家業者組成財團，提案「以海底電纜為全國各地鋪設光纖」。計畫規劃從菲律賓最北端省份巴丹省（Batanes）向南延伸至西南方的巴拉望（Palawan），並連接米沙鄢（Visayas）與民答那峨（Mindanao）兩大島群。

  DICT正評估該提案，仍待政府審核後始可執行。Aguda表示，若獲批准，預計可於兩年內竣工。

  ## 地震感測與國防功能

  Aguda表示，擬建電纜將整合數位聲學感測系統，能夠偵測與地震及海嘯相關的地震活動，實現即時資料蒐集。他補充說，這項技術亦具備國家國防應用潛力，但未進一步說明細節。

  ## 與國家骨幹網路互補

  擬議中的網路旨在補充——而非取代——政府的「國家光纖骨幹」（National Fiber Backbone）計畫，此為DICT重點推動的寬頻連結旗艦項目。該計畫旨在透過高速光纖基礎設施提升全國網路速度，預估將惠及約7,000萬名菲律賓民眾。DICT於今年5月表示，正加速推進國家光纖骨幹計畫第四期與第五期工程，兩期均預計於年內完工。

  Aguda表示，由於此次提案定位為國內網路，推動過程中將需要跨部會協調。此外，該網路亦規劃連結菲律賓全國14個國際接入點——即菲律賓與全球網際網路系統相連的海纜登陸站。

  ## 三大業者現有國際海纜布局

  提案三方業者均已在國際海底電纜系統中佈局。Globe今年4月加入Candle Cable System財團，藉此將菲律賓接入亞洲各地的海底電纜網路。PLDT則投資了Apricot海纜系統，該系統全長1萬2,000公里，提供新加坡至日本的直連線路。Converge參與了Bifrost海纜系統，以及東南亞海南—香港快捷海纜系統（SEA-H2X）。DICT表示，擬議中的國內海底光纖網路將與上述國際系統相互連接。

  ## 計畫的意義

  DICT指出，採用海底路由的目的，在於服務目前欠缺電信服務、陸基電纜難以到達的島嶼與偏遠地區。地震感測功能則將在商業連網用途之外，額外提供早期預警及國防應用價值。

  目前，該提案仍在DICT評估階段，政府尚未宣布任何決定，最終施工時程亦有賴核准結果而定。
