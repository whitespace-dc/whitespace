  ## Upgrade and target price revision

  UOB Kay Hian (UOBKH) Research upgraded Malaysia-listed NationGate Holdings Bhd to a "buy" rating and raised its 12-month target price to RM1.88 from RM0.82, more than doubling its previous valuation, as the broker forecast a sharp earnings turnaround beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026. NationGate's shares closed at RM1.49 on Monday.

  The revised target is based on 24 times UOBKH's forecast 2027 earnings per share, according to the research house's report. The firm more than doubled its 2027 net profit estimate to RM178.8 million from RM98.1 million, projecting what it described as an S-curve earnings inflection — a period of accelerating growth following a slow initial ramp — as new optical module programmes move into volume production and the company shifts toward higher-value manufacturing.

  ## First-half results below expectations

  The upgrade comes despite NationGate reporting first-half 2026 results that fell short of analyst forecasts. Adjusted net profit for the period reached RM29.5 million, representing 38 percent of UOBKH's full-year forecast and 51 percent of consensus estimates. UOBKH attributed the shortfall to a slower-than-expected recovery in revenue and margins, and cut its full-year 2026 net profit forecast to RM39.2 million from RM78.2 million. The broker cited a longer-than-expected gestation period for NationGate's new optical module programmes as the primary reason.

  ## AI networking demand as the growth driver

  The broker's medium-term thesis rests on growing demand for high-speed optical connectivity as artificial intelligence infrastructure expands. UOBKH said AI deployments are broadening beyond graphics processing units (GPUs) — the chips that perform AI calculations — into high-speed networking, as communication between GPUs is becoming the critical bottleneck in large-scale AI clusters.

  This shift, the broker said, is accelerating an industry transition from 800 gigabits-per-second (Gbps) to 1.6 terabits-per-second (Tbps) optical interconnects — connections that carry data between servers at speeds roughly twice the current standard. NationGate's networking and telecommunications segment is positioned to benefit as hyperscalers — large cloud and data centre operators — accelerate AI data centre buildouts and adopt higher-speed connectivity, according to UOBKH.

  ## US restrictions on Chinese transceivers

  UOBKH also flagged a potential regulatory tailwind. Reuters reported earlier this month that the US Federal Communications Commission (FCC) was preparing a measure that would bar imports of new Chinese-made optical transceiver models, devices that convert electrical signals to light for fibre-optic transmission. UOBKH said such a restriction, if enacted, could benefit optical component suppliers outside China, including NationGate. The broker noted the proposal could still be modified or withdrawn.

  ## Value-chain advancement into optical sub-assemblies

  Separately, UOBKH expects NationGate to move up the optical networking value chain, progressing from conventional printed circuit board-level assembly for optical transceivers into more specialised component and sub-assembly manufacturing. The broker said the company's capabilities now extend to chip-on-carrier (CoC) and chip-on-submount (CoS) processes — techniques for attaching semiconductor chips directly to packaging substrates — as well as transmitter optical sub-assembly (Tosa), receiver optical sub-assembly (Rosa), and transceiver optical sub-assembly (Trosa) manufacturing.

  These higher-value processes require longer customer qualification periods and closer integration, UOBKH said, which it expects to strengthen customer relationships and support more stable revenue as volumes grow. The broker described the shift as "potentially game-changing," contingent on successful yield optimisation and large-scale rollout from 2027 onwards. Margins are expected to improve progressively as volumes ramp beyond the initial gestation phase.

  ## What the outlook means

  For industry planners and investors, the upgrade signals that component manufacturers serving AI data centre optical networking are experiencing uneven near-term demand as new programme qualifications take longer than anticipated, even as the structural demand case remains intact. The transition to 1.6 Tbps interconnects and the potential redirection of US procurement away from Chinese-made transceivers represent demand tailwinds that UOBKH expects to materialise most clearly in 2027. The broker projects NationGate's full-year 2027 net profit at RM178.8 million, compared with RM39.2 million forecast for 2026.

  ## 評等調升與目標價修訂

  大華繼顯（UOBKH）研究部將在馬來西亞掛牌的NationGate Holdings Bhd調升至「買進」評等，並將12個月目標價從RM0.82大幅上調逾倍至RM1.88，因為該券商預測NationGate的獲利將於2026年第四季起出現急速反轉。NationGate股價週一收盤報RM1.49。

  修訂後的目標價以大華繼顯預測2027年每股盈餘的24倍為基礎，研究報告如此說明。大華繼顯將2027年淨利預測從RM9,810萬大幅上調逾倍至RM1億7,880萬，預計公司將呈現所謂的「S型曲線獲利拐點」——即在初期緩慢爬升後進入加速成長的階段——隨著新光學模組計畫進入量產，公司也逐步轉向更高附加價值的製造業務。

  ## 上半年業績不如預期

  此次調升評等，是在NationGate公布遜於分析師預測的2026年上半年業績後做出的。上半年調整後淨利達RM2,950萬，僅佔大華繼顯全年預測的38%、市場共識預估的51%。大華繼顯將此差距歸因於營收及毛利率的復甦速度慢於預期，並將2026年全年淨利預測從RM7,820萬下修至RM3,920萬。券商指出，NationGate新光學模組計畫的培育期比預期更長，是主要原因所在。

  ## 人工智慧網路需求為主要成長動能

  大華繼顯的中期投資論點，建立在人工智慧基礎設施擴張所帶動的高速光學連接需求之上。大華繼顯表示，AI應用的部署範疇正從繪圖處理器（GPU）——即負責執行AI運算的晶片——擴展至高速網路領域，因為GPU之間的通訊已成為大規模AI叢集的關鍵瓶頸。

  大華繼顯指出，這一轉變正加速業界從每秒800 Gbps向每秒1.6 Tbps光學互連的升級過渡——此類連線以約現行標準兩倍的速度在伺服器間傳輸資料。隨著超大規模業者——即大型雲端及資料中心營運商——加速興建AI資料中心並採用更高速的連線方案，NationGate的網路及電信部門預計將因此受益，大華繼顯如此說明。

  ## 美國限制中國製收發器

  大華繼顯亦點出一項潛在的法規利多因素。路透社本月稍早報導，美國聯邦通訊委員會（FCC）正準備推出一項措施，禁止進口新款中國製光學收發器——這是一種將電訊號轉換為光訊號以進行光纖傳輸的裝置。大華繼顯表示，若此限制正式生效，將使中國以外的光學元件供應商受益，NationGate即為其中之一。不過，大華繼顯亦指出，該提案仍有可能被修改或撤回。

  ## 向光學次組件升級的價值鏈躍進

  另一方面，大華繼顯預期NationGate將在光學網路價值鏈中進一步升級，從傳統光學收發器的印刷電路板組裝，邁向更專業的元件與次組件製造。大華繼顯指出，NationGate的製造能力目前已延伸至晶片覆載板（CoC）及晶片覆次基板（CoS）製程——這是將半導體晶片直接固定於封裝基板的技術——以及發射光學次組件（Tosa）、接收光學次組件（Rosa）和收發光學次組件（Trosa）的製造。

  大華繼顯表示，這些更高附加價值的製程需要較長的客戶認證期，並要求更緊密的整合，預計將強化客戶關係，並在出貨量成長時支撐更穩定的營收。大華繼顯將此轉型描述為「潛在的遊戲規則改變者」，惟前提是NationGate能於2027年起成功優化良率並完成大規模量產。隨著出貨量突破初期培育階段，毛利率預計將逐步改善。

  ## 前景展望的意涵

  對於產業規劃者及投資人而言，此次評等調升顯示，服務AI資料中心光學網路的元件製造商，在新計畫認證期拉長的情況下，正面臨近期需求表現不均的挑戰，儘管中長期需求基本面依然穩固。向1.6 Tbps互連的過渡，以及美國採購可能轉離中國製收發器，代表大華繼顯預期將於2027年最為明顯體現的需求順風。大華繼顯預測NationGate 2027年全年淨利將達RM1億7,880萬，相較之下，2026年的預測值為RM3,920萬。
