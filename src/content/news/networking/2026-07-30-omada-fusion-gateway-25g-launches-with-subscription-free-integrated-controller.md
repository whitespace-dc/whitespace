body_en: |
  Omada has released the Fusion Gateway 2.5G, a business-oriented network gateway built around an integrated, subscription-free local controller that manages connected switches, wireless access points, VLANs (virtual local area networks, which segment traffic between groups of devices), and VPNs (virtual private networks) from a single interface, according to ServeTheHome, which received the device as part of a sponsored review package.

  The gateway carries five 2.5-gigabit Ethernet (GbE) ports on its rear panel alongside a USB Type-C port. All five ports run at 2.5 Gbps, a standard that sits above standard gigabit Ethernet and below 10 GbE, making the device suited for internet service provider connections in the one- to two-gigabit-per-second range, the review said. A color touchscreen on the front panel displays diagnostic data.

  ## Integrated controller is the central design choice

  The device's defining feature is its local controller, which differs from conventional gateway products managed at the individual-device level. The controller is subscription-free and, once configured, distributes wireless settings to Wi-Fi access points across the network. ServeTheHome said this allows centralized management of VLANs, WLANs (wireless LANs), and guest networks from a single point, describing that capability as a significant operational advantage for small- to medium-sized businesses that have relied on ISP-provided router-and-Wi-Fi combinations or standalone access points.

  ## Setup and management interface

  The device can be configured through a Bluetooth-paired mobile application or by connecting to the default gateway IP address, 192.168.188.1, through a web browser. ServeTheHome described the resulting graphical dashboard as comparable in ease of use to Ubiquiti UniFi, a competing network management platform, while noting that each has its own distinct features and workflows.

  The review noted the absence of a serial console interface, characterizing the device as oriented toward ease of use rather than command-line or serial-automation workflows.

  ## Feature set

  The gateway supports multiple simultaneous WAN connections, allowing operators to combine a wired ISP connection with a backup link such as a 5G cellular or Starlink connection, or to route a VPN through a dedicated WAN link. VPN server and client configuration, intrusion detection and prevention systems (IDS/IPS, which monitor and block malicious network activity), and content blocking are accessible through the management interface. ServeTheHome said IDS/IPS testing had not been completed at the time of publication.

  Additional tools include a LAN-side network topology chart, a connected-client list with per-device connection details, standard network diagnostics including ping and packet capture, and a migration assistant for transferring configuration when replacing a unit. The review noted the migration assistant as an uncommon feature but said it was not tested during the evaluation.

  ## Ecosystem dependency

  Omada shipped ServeTheHome a package that included the gateway alongside a PoE+ (Power over Ethernet Plus) switch capable of powering connected devices, several Wi-Fi access points, and mounting hardware, with the intent that the review assess the products as a combined solution. ServeTheHome said the gateway's value proposition depends substantially on pairing it with other Omada devices that the integrated controller can manage centrally, noting that a gateway alone still requires access points and a PoE+ switch to power them.

  ## Significance

  Centrally managed business networking has historically required either expensive enterprise platforms or cloud-dependent subscriptions. The Fusion Gateway 2.5G's subscription-free local controller positions the device as an accessible option for smaller organizations seeking unified management of wired and wireless infrastructure without recurring software costs, according to the review.

  ServeTheHome said separate articles covering the individual Omada devices included in the evaluation bundle, the PoE+ switch, wall-mounted and outdoor access points, and rackmount and magnetic mounting options, are planned for future publication.
body_zh: |
  Omada 發布了商用網路閘道器 Fusion Gateway 2.5G，搭載內建免訂閱費本地控制器，可從單一介面集中管理已連接的交換器、無線存取點、VLAN（虛擬區域網路，用於隔離不同裝置群組之間的流量）及 VPN（虛擬私人網路）。科技媒體 ServeTheHome 以贊助評測的形式收到這款裝置，並將其作為完整 Omada 生態系評鑑套件的一部分進行審測。

  該閘道器後面板配備五個 2.5GbE（2.5 Gigabit 乙太網路）連接埠，另附一個 USB Type-C 連接埠。五個連接埠均以 2.5 Gbps 速率運行，介於標準千兆乙太網路與 10GbE 之間，適合應用於頻寬介於每秒一至兩 Gigabit 區間的 ISP 連線環境，評測文章如此描述。前面板則搭載彩色觸控螢幕，可顯示診斷資料。

  ## 整合式控制器為核心設計特色

  這款裝置最具代表性的功能在於其本地控制器，有別於傳統閘道器逐台個別管理的方式。這套控制器無需訂閱費用，設定完成後即可將無線設定統一推送至網路中各個 Wi-Fi 存取點。ServeTheHome 表示，這讓使用者能從單一管理介面集中控制 VLAN、WLAN（無線區域網路）及訪客網路，並指出這項能力對於長期仰賴 ISP 提供的路由器加 Wi-Fi 組合、或獨立存取點的中小型企業而言，在維運上具有相當顯著的優勢。

  ## 設定與管理介面

  裝置可透過藍牙配對行動應用程式進行設定，或以瀏覽器連線至預設閘道器 IP 位址 192.168.188.1 進行操作。ServeTheHome 指出，其圖形化管理儀表板在使用便利性上與競爭平台 Ubiquiti UniFi 相當，但兩者各有其特定功能與操作流程。

  評測亦指出，該裝置未提供序列主控台介面，顯示其設計導向著重使用便利性，而非命令列或序列自動化操作流程。

  ## 功能規格

  該閘道器支援多條同時運作的 WAN 連線，讓管理者得以將有線 ISP 連線搭配 5G 行動網路或 Starlink 等備援鏈路，或透過專用 WAN 連線路由 VPN 流量。管理介面中亦可設定 VPN 伺服器與用戶端、入侵偵測與防禦系統（IDS/IPS，用於監控並阻擋惡意網路活動），以及內容封鎖功能。ServeTheHome 表示，IDS/IPS 測試在文章發布時尚未完成。

  其他工具包括 LAN 端網路拓樸圖、顯示各裝置連線詳情的已連線用戶端清單、標準網路診斷工具（含 ping 及封包擷取），以及在更換設備時用於轉移設定的遷移助理。評測指出遷移助理並不多見，但此次評估期間未對其進行實際測試。

  ## 生態系依存性

  Omada 向 ServeTheHome 提供了一套完整評測組合，內含該閘道器、一台可為連接裝置供電的 PoE+（增強型乙太網路供電）交換器、數個 Wi-Fi 存取點及安裝配件，旨在讓評測以完整解決方案的角度進行。ServeTheHome 表示，這款閘道器的價值主張在很大程度上有賴於搭配其他能由整合式控制器集中管理的 Omada 裝置，並指出僅有閘道器本身，仍需額外搭配存取點及 PoE+ 交換器為存取點供電，方能構成完整部署。

  ## 市場意義

  集中管理的商用網路環境，歷來需要昂貴的企業級平台，或依賴雲端服務的訂閱方案。Fusion Gateway 2.5G 免訂閱費的本地控制器，讓這款裝置得以成為規模較小的組織尋求統一管理有線與無線基礎架構時，無需承擔持續性軟體費用的可行選項。

  ServeTheHome 表示，後續將陸續發表針對此次評測組合中各個 Omada 裝置的獨立評測文章，涵蓋 PoE+ 交換器、壁掛式及戶外型存取點，以及機架式與磁吸式安裝配件等產品。
