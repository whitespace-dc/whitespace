body_en: |
  AU Optronics (AUO), a Taiwanese display panel manufacturer, is moving its micro light-emitting diode (Micro LED) technology into the optical communications market, targeting short-range data transmission inside AI data center racks as a potential replacement for laser-based light sources in conventional transceivers, according to TrendForce.

  TrendForce analyst Xie Zongcun said Micro LED co-packaged optics (CPO), a module design that integrates light emitters directly with switching hardware, can cut power consumption by 95% compared with conventional 1.6-terabit-per-second (Tbps) pluggable optical transceivers, reducing per-module draw from 30 watts to 1.6 watts. The same modules can achieve data transmission densities of up to 2 terabits per second per square millimeter (Tbps/mm²), Xie said.

  ## Limits of current rack interconnect options

  Xie said AI's escalating demand for computing power has caused data exchange volumes to grow exponentially, straining existing short-range interconnect technologies inside data centers. Current 1.6 Tbps pluggable optical transceivers consume 30 watts each, creating substantial heat and energy costs at data center scale. Active electrical cables (AECs), an alternative short-reach electrical interconnect, approach 10 picojoules per bit (pJ/bit) in energy consumption at 1.6 Tbps and above, and cable-level heat buildup further constrains server-to-server transmission capacity, according to TrendForce.

  ## Hyperscaler targets for next-generation interconnects

  NVIDIA, Google, Meta, and Microsoft have set mandatory specifications for next-generation optical communications, according to TrendForce. The targets are:

  - Cost: below $0.10 per gigabit per second (Gbps)
  - Transmission density: above 0.5 Tbps/mm²
  - Energy efficiency: below 1.5 pJ/bit

  Xie said Micro LED CPO meets all three thresholds through a wide-channel, lower-frequency architecture that deploys hundreds to thousands of micron-scale channels in parallel to reach 1.6 Tbps and higher aggregate bandwidth. He said the technology's combination of ultra-low power and high physical transmission density represents a performance envelope that competing technologies cannot reach within their physical limits.

  ## AUO's supply chain and delivery model

  AUO has built a vertically integrated supply chain around the technology, drawing on what TrendForce describes as deep optical field control experience among Taiwan's established manufacturers. The company combines Micro LED epitaxial wafer production from affiliate Lextar (富采) with silicon photodiode (Si PD) array receivers from Dingene (鼎元), and has partnered with fiber optic manufacturers and automated optical coupling equipment suppliers.

  AUO integrates the completed Micro LED CPO modules directly onto glass redistribution-layer (RDL) interposers, substrates used to reroute connections between chips, and delivers them ready for customer use, Xie said. That approach eliminates the need for downstream customers to install expensive mass-transfer equipment, which would otherwise be required to handle Micro LED chips at production scale.

  ## Market outlook

  TrendForce projects Micro LED CPO shipments will reach 5.3 million units by 2030, generating $848 million in output value. Xie attributed the projected growth to strong demand for short-range, high-speed transmission inside AI computing racks. TrendForce expects Micro LED CPO to hold an indispensable position in short-range transmission within AI computing networks by 2030.
body_zh: |
  友達光電（AUO）正將微型發光二極體（Micro LED）技術跨入光通訊市場，鎖定AI資料中心機架內部的短距資料傳輸應用，有望取代傳統收發器中的雷射光源。此為集邦科技（TrendForce）最新研究報告所揭露的動向。

  集邦科技分析師謝宗勳表示，Micro LED共封裝光學（CPO）——一種將發光元件直接整合於交換硬體上的模組設計——相較於傳統1.6 Tbps可插拔光學收發器，可將耗電量削減95%，每模組功耗從30瓦大幅降至1.6瓦。謝宗勳指出，同款模組的資料傳輸密度可達每平方毫米2 Tbps（Tbps/mm²）。

  ## 現有機架互連方案的侷限

  謝宗勳表示，AI對運算能力的需求急速攀升，導致資料交換量呈指數級成長，嚴重壓迫資料中心內部現有的短距互連技術。目前1.6 Tbps可插拔光學收發器每顆耗電30瓦，在資料中心規模下形成可觀的熱量與能源成本。集邦科技指出，主動電氣纜線（AEC）作為另一種短距電氣互連方案，在1.6 Tbps及以上速率時能耗趨近每位元10皮焦耳（pJ/bit），且纜線層級的熱積累進一步限制了伺服器間的傳輸容量。

  ## 超大規模雲端業者對下世代互連的規格要求

  集邦科技指出，輝達（NVIDIA）、Google、Meta與微軟（Microsoft）已針對下世代光通訊制定強制性規格，目標如下：

  - 成本：每 Gbps 低於 0.10 美元
  - 傳輸密度：高於 0.5 Tbps/mm²
  - 能源效率：低於 1.5 pJ/bit

  謝宗勳表示，Micro LED CPO 透過寬通道、低頻架構達到上述三項門檻——此架構以數百至數千條微米尺度通道並行運作，達成 1.6 Tbps 及更高的聚合頻寬。他指出，該技術兼具超低耗電與高物理傳輸密度的特性，形成競爭技術在物理限制下無法企及的效能邊界。

  ## 友達光電的供應鏈與交付模式

  友達光電圍繞這項技術建立了垂直整合供應鏈，集邦科技形容台灣既有廠商在光學場控制領域擁有深厚底蘊。友達結合旗下關係企業富采（Lextar）的 Micro LED 磊晶晶圓，以及鼎元（Dingene）的矽光二極體（Si PD）陣列接收器，並與光纖廠商及自動化光學耦合設備供應商締結合作關係。

  謝宗勳表示，友達光電將完成的 Micro LED CPO 模組直接整合至玻璃重佈線層（RDL）中介層上——這是一種用於重新佈線晶片間連接的基板——並以客戶可直接使用的形式交付。此一做法省去下游客戶安裝昂貴巨量轉移設備的必要，否則這類設備在量產規模下處理 Micro LED 晶片時不可或缺。

  ## 市場展望

  集邦科技預測，Micro LED CPO 出貨量至 2030 年將達 530 萬套，產值達 8.48 億美元。謝宗勳將預期成長歸因於 AI 運算機架內部對短距高速傳輸的強勁需求。集邦科技預期，Micro LED CPO 至 2030 年將在 AI 運算網路的短距傳輸領域占據不可或缺的地位。
