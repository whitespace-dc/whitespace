body_en: |
  PT Ketrosden Triasmitra (IDX: KETR) and PT Ekamas Mora Republik, known as MoraRepublic (IDX: MORA), declared the Rising 8 submarine cable system ready for service on June 17, 2026, activating a new path between Jakarta and Batam on what the companies described as one of Southeast Asia's busiest digital corridors.

  The system was built in May 2026 and completed testing and commissioning before the operational ceremony held that Wednesday. The currently active segment runs from Tanjung Pakis in Jakarta to Tanjung Bemban in Batam. A Batam-to-Singapore extension remains a separate planned project, and the full system is described as measuring 1,128.5 kilometers in total length.

  ## Cable specifications

  Rising 8 uses a repeatered system — technology that places signal amplifiers at intervals along the cable to sustain transmission quality across long distances. The system is designed to support a minimum capacity of 400 terabits per second (Tbps). The submarine cable was manufactured by Norddeutsche Seekabelwerke GmbH (NSW), a German facility of Prysmian Group that the companies said has more than 125 years of submarine cable manufacturing experience.

  ## Landing station and data center infrastructure

  At the Jakarta landing point, Triasmitra operates a Cable Landing Station and data center at Tanjung Pakis built to a Tier-3 concurrently maintainable specification — a standard requiring redundant infrastructure paths capable of carrying a full load while individual components are maintained or repaired, allowing servicing without interrupting live traffic.

  At the Batam end, Rising 8 is set to interconnect with MoraRepublic's Nusantara Data Center (NDC) at Tanjung Bemban, which holds Tier-3 certification from the American National Standards Institute and the Telecommunications Industry Association (ANSI/TIA).

  ## First independently executed deployment for Triasmitra

  Titus Dondi Patria, President Director and CEO of Triasmitra, said Rising 8 marks the first submarine cable project the company has executed entirely on its own — managing permits, vessel operations, and technical personnel without relying on external contractors for those functions. Patria said this positions Triasmitra to pursue further cable deployment projects with greater confidence.

  ## Target market and commercial rationale

  The companies said Rising 8 is aimed at telecom operators, internet service providers (ISPs), content delivery providers, and enterprises with high bandwidth requirements — specifically naming financial services firms, cloud providers, and data center operators as target customers. The Indonesia-Singapore corridor is characterized by the companies as a strategically important route because Singapore concentrates data centers and cloud infrastructure serving the region.

  Route diversity is the central commercial proposition. For operators already using existing cables on the corridor, Rising 8 can function as backup capacity or for load distribution. For enterprises, a second independent cable path reduces the risk of service interruption from a single cable fault.

  Timotius Max Sulaiman, President Director and CEO of MoraRepublic, said completion of the Jakarta-Batam segment is an important step toward digital infrastructure that can support Indonesia's future connectivity needs, and that strong telecommunications infrastructure acts as a catalyst for digital economic growth and broader digital access across the country's regions.

  ## What comes next

  The Batam-to-Singapore segment remains the outstanding step before Rising 8 completes its full planned route. Once built, the system will provide a continuous connection from Tanjung Pakis in Jakarta to Singapore, with Tier-3 data center facilities anchoring both ends of the route.

  ## 印尼Rising 8海底電纜雅加達至巴淡島航段正式啟用

  PT Ketrosden Triasmitra（印尼交易所代號：KETR）與PT Ekamas Mora Republik（俗稱MoraRepublic，印尼交易所代號：MORA）於2026年6月17日宣布Rising 8海底電纜系統正式就緒，在兩家公司所形容的東南亞最繁忙數位走廊之一上，開通了雅加達至巴淡島之間的全新傳輸路徑。

  該系統於2026年5月竣工，並在當週三舉行的啟用典禮前完成測試與驗收作業。目前已啟用的航段從雅加達的丹戎帕基斯（Tanjung Pakis）延伸至巴淡島的丹戎本班（Tanjung Bemban）。巴淡島至新加坡的延伸段則是另一個待建項目，整套系統全長規劃為1,128.5公里。

  ## 電纜規格

  Rising 8採用中繼器系統，即沿電纜線路每隔一定距離設置訊號放大器，以維持長距離傳輸的訊號品質。該系統設計最低容量為每秒400兆位元（400 Tbps）。海底電纜由德國製造商諾德多伊奇海底電纜廠（Norddeutsche Seekabelwerke GmbH，NSW）生產，該廠隸屬義大利普睿司曼集團（Prysmian Group），兩家公司表示NSW擁有逾125年的海底電纜製造經驗。

  ## 登陸站與資料中心基礎設施

  在雅加達登陸端，Triasmitra於丹戎帕基斯設有一座海底電纜登陸站（Cable Landing Station）及附屬資料中心，符合Tier-3同步維護（Concurrently Maintainable）規範——此標準要求具備備援基礎設施路徑，在個別元件進行維護或修繕時仍能承擔全負載運作，實現不中斷現網流量的帶電維護。

  在巴淡島端，Rising 8將與MoraRepublic位於丹戎本班的努山塔拉資料中心（Nusantara Data Center，NDC）互聯，該資料中心持有美國國家標準協會暨電信工業協會（ANSI/TIA）頒發的Tier-3認證。

  ## Triasmitra首次獨立執行的海纜部署

  Triasmitra總裁暨執行長Titus Dondi Patria表示，Rising 8是該公司史上首個完全由自身獨立執行的海底電纜專案——包括許可申請、船舶作業及技術人員調度，均未仰賴外部承包商。Patria指出，這一經歷讓Triasmitra日後推進更多電纜建設案時更具信心。

  ## 目標市場與商業邏輯

  兩家公司表示，Rising 8的目標客群涵蓋電信業者、網路服務供應商（ISP）、內容傳遞供應商，以及具高頻寬需求的企業用戶，並特別點名金融服務業者、雲端供應商與資料中心營運商為主要潛在客戶。兩家公司將印尼至新加坡走廊定位為戰略要道，原因在於新加坡集中了服務整個區域的資料中心與雲端基礎設施。

  路由多元化是Rising 8的核心商業價值主張。對於已使用該走廊現有電纜的電信業者而言，Rising 8可作為備援容量或用於流量分散；對企業用戶而言，第二條獨立電纜路徑能有效降低單一電纜故障所導致的服務中斷風險。

  MoraRepublic總裁暨執行長Timotius Max Sulaiman表示，雅加達至巴淡島航段的完工是邁向數位基礎設施的重要一步，有助於支撐印尼未來的連網需求；他並指出，健全的電信基礎設施是推動數位經濟成長、擴大全國各地區數位普及的重要催化劑。

  ## 後續展望

  巴淡島至新加坡航段仍是Rising 8完成全線規劃路由前的最後一塊拼圖。一旦建成，該系統將提供從雅加達丹戎帕基斯至新加坡的完整連續連線，並以Tier-3資料中心設施作為路由兩端的核心節點。
