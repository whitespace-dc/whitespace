body_en: |
  VodafoneThree has partnered with Nokia and Nvidia to trial artificial intelligence applied to its radio access network (RAN) — the part of a mobile network that connects devices to the core infrastructure via radio signals — on its UK network, the carrier announced November 18, 2026.

  The trial will deploy Nokia's AI-native anyRAN software running on Nvidia's Aerial RAN computer. VodafoneThree said the program will begin in a controlled lab environment and then progress to a selected-site field trial.

  ## Scope of the trial

  VodafoneThree identified eight categories of use cases it intends to test: radio optimization, mobility management, energy efficiency, anomaly detection, coverage and capacity optimization, Quality of Service (QoS) assurance, and the sharing of accelerated compute resources between RAN workloads and AI workloads. The carrier said it will benchmark trial results against its existing network performance to determine where AI delivers measurable improvements.

  The trials will also examine whether AI-RAN can support VodafoneThree's SuperMobile service, which the company announced in September 2026. SuperMobile uses network slicing — a technique that partitions a physical network into dedicated virtual channels — to deliver speeds up to four times faster than standard plans, with guaranteed minimum speeds of up to 15 Mbps.

  ## UK operators align around Nokia and Nvidia

  VodafoneThree is the latest British operator to enter an AI-RAN trial agreement with Nokia. Virgin Media O2 tested the technology at its Giga Site in Paddington, London, last month. EE has also confirmed it will trial AI-RAN on its live network next year with Nokia's support. Nokia has pursued the AI-RAN strategy broadly in partnership with Nvidia, working with multiple mobile carriers across markets.

  ## What VodafoneThree said

  "Working with Nokia, we'll test these capabilities to understand how AI can improve network performance and, ultimately, the experience we deliver for our customers," said Iain Milligan, network development and infrastructure director at VodafoneThree. "As our 5G Standalone network evolves, we see significant potential for AI-RAN to help us respond more dynamically to different customer needs and unlock new, differentiated connectivity experiences, while laying the foundations for the next generation of intelligent, AI-native networks."

  ## Industry significance

  Three of the UK's major mobile operators — VodafoneThree, Virgin Media O2, and EE — are now either actively testing or publicly committed to testing the Nokia and Nvidia AI-RAN stack, indicating consolidation around that vendor pairing for this stage of AI-RAN evaluation in the British market. VodafoneThree's planned two-phase structure, moving from lab to field trial, represents the path toward potential commercial deployment on its 5G Standalone network.

  VodafoneThree於2026年11月18日宣布，將與諾基亞（Nokia）及輝達（Nvidia）合作，在英國網路上試行人工智慧無線電存取網路（AI-RAN）技術。所謂無線電存取網路（RAN），是行動網路中負責透過無線電訊號將裝置連接至核心基礎設施的部分。此舉讓VodafoneThree成為英國最新一家測試諾基亞與輝達AI-RAN組合的行動電信業者。

  本次試驗將部署諾基亞的AI原生anyRAN軟體，搭配輝達的Aerial RAN運算平台運行。VodafoneThree表示，計畫將先從受控的實驗室環境起步，再逐步推進至特定選定場址的現場試驗。

  ## 試驗範疇

  VodafoneThree確立了八大測試用例類別，分別為：無線電優化、移動性管理、能源效率、異常偵測、覆蓋率與容量優化、服務品質（QoS）保障，以及在RAN工作負載與AI工作負載之間共享加速運算資源。該公司表示，將以現有網路效能作為基準，對比試驗結果，藉此判斷人工智慧在哪些面向能帶來可量化的改善。

  此次試驗也將評估AI-RAN能否支援VodafoneThree於2026年9月發布的SuperMobile服務。SuperMobile採用網路切片技術——一種將實體網路分割為專屬虛擬通道的技術——為用戶提供較標準方案最高快四倍的速度，並保證最低下載速率達15 Mbps。

  ## 英國電信業者齊向諾基亞與輝達靠攏

  VodafoneThree是英國最新與諾基亞簽署AI-RAN試驗協議的電信業者。Virgin Media O2上個月已在倫敦帕丁頓的Giga Site完成這項技術的測試；EE也已公開確認，將於明年在其商用網路上展開AI-RAN試驗，同樣獲得諾基亞的支援。諾基亞長期與輝達攜手合作，在全球多個市場與多家行動電信業者共同推動AI-RAN戰略布局。

  ## VodafoneThree的表態

  VodafoneThree網路開發與基礎設施總監Iain Milligan表示：「與諾基亞攜手合作，我們將測試這些能力，以了解人工智慧如何提升網路效能，並最終改善我們為客戶帶來的使用體驗。隨著我們的5G獨立組網（5G Standalone）持續演進，我們認為AI-RAN具有相當大的潛力，能協助我們更動態地回應不同客戶需求，解鎖更多差異化的連線體驗，同時為下一代智慧、AI原生網路奠定基礎。」

  ## 產業意涵

  英國三大主要行動電信業者——VodafoneThree、Virgin Media O2與EE——如今都已積極投入或公開承諾測試諾基亞與輝達的AI-RAN技術堆疊，顯示英國市場在這一階段的AI-RAN評估上，已逐漸向這組廠商配對集中靠攏。VodafoneThree規劃的兩階段架構——由實驗室環境逐步推進至現場試驗——代表著其在5G獨立組網上邁向潛在商業部署的具體路徑。
