body_en: |
  Indium phosphide (InP), the compound semiconductor that generates the laser light inside high-speed optical modules used in artificial intelligence data centers, is running more than 30 percent below customer demand, Lumentum Chief Executive Michael Hurlston warned, as Nvidia committed $4 billion to two key optical component manufacturers to secure supply for next-generation AI infrastructure.

  In March 2026, Nvidia announced $2 billion investments each in Lumentum and Coherent, totaling $4 billion, to fund expansion of manufacturing capacity in the United States, with multi-year purchase agreements and priority rights over future production included in the deals. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang said next-generation AI factories require more efficient, lower-power optical interconnects and that optical technology will be a critical foundation for continued AI infrastructure expansion.

  Supply pressure has extended to upstream material suppliers. AXT, a Nasdaq-listed InP wafer manufacturer, reported second-quarter revenue up 165 percent year over year, with cash holdings rising sharply. Lumentum agreed to pay AXT $87 million in prepayments to lock in a six-year InP wafer supply agreement, a financing structure previously associated with high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced semiconductor packaging supply chains and now appearing for the first time in the InP sector.

  Hurlston said that even combining Lumentum and Coherent's total output, the two companies cannot fully satisfy market demand.

  ## What InP is and why silicon cannot replace it

  Indium phosphide is a III-V compound semiconductor, a class of materials made from elements in the third and fifth groups of the periodic table. Its defining characteristic is a direct bandgap, a quantum property that enables efficient conversion of electrical energy into laser light. That property makes InP the dominant material for laser sources inside 800G (800 gigabit-per-second) and 1.6T (1.6 terabit-per-second) optical modules deployed in AI data centers, including electro-absorption modulated lasers (EML), distributed feedback lasers (DFB), and pump lasers.

  Silicon photonics, which uses silicon chips to transmit, modulate, and process optical signals, is often characterized as a competitive threat to InP. In practice the two are complementary. Silicon has an indirect bandgap and cannot efficiently emit light; silicon photonic chips therefore still require InP-based lasers, integrated via heterogeneous or hybrid manufacturing techniques, to provide a stable light source. Silicon carries light; InP generates it.

  Co-packaged optics (CPO) is a packaging architecture that places switch application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) and optical engines in the same module, shortening electrical signal paths and reducing power consumption. Most current CPO designs use an external laser source in which a continuous-wave (CW) laser made from InP feeds a silicon photonic optical engine through fiber. CPO deployment does not reduce demand for InP; market research firm LightCounting projects that the architecture will drive demand for higher-power, more reliable InP lasers as it is more widely adopted.

  LightCounting also forecasts that while silicon photonics penetration will increase with broader adoption of 800G and 1.6T optical modules, demand for InP lasers will grow in parallel.

  ## Why InP supply cannot be quickly expanded

  Three structural constraints limit how fast InP production can be scaled, and the shortage may prove more difficult to close than earlier AI supply shortfalls in GPU and memory chips.

  The first constraint is wafer size. Silicon semiconductor manufacturing has largely standardized on 12-inch diameter wafers, enabling large chip volumes per wafer. InP wafers are produced primarily in three- to four-inch formats; the industry is gradually introducing six-inch production, but adoption remains limited. InP crystal is brittle, grows slowly, and is difficult to produce without defects, reducing the number of laser chips cut from each wafer and depressing yields. Even with capital investment in place, new InP capacity typically requires two to three years or more to move from crystal growth through wafer manufacturing, customer qualification, and volume production.

  The second constraint is indium availability. InP is a compound of indium and phosphorus. Indium is a byproduct of zinc ore smelting, meaning its output depends on zinc production rather than on AI demand. Rapid growth in AI capital spending cannot directly pull more indium into the market.

  The third constraint is geopolitical exposure. The U.S. Geological Survey's Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 estimates global refined indium production in 2025 at approximately 1,100 metric tons, with China accounting for around 70 percent of the total. China's Ministry of Commerce and General Administration of Customs announced on February 4, 2025 export controls covering tungsten, tellurium, bismuth, molybdenum, and indium-related products, with InP chips included in the regulated categories. AXT, though Nasdaq-listed, conducts InP crystal growth and wafer manufacturing through its subsidiary Beijing Tongmei; exports from that subsidiary require approval from Chinese regulators.

  ## Taiwan's position in the downstream supply chain

  Taiwanese companies hold established positions in the InP supply chain below the wafer level, with firms active in InP epitaxial growth, high-speed optoelectronic components, optical transceivers, active optical cables (AOC), fiber array units (FAU) and MPO fiber connectors for CPO applications, and high-speed fiber interconnects. The source identifies several Taiwanese manufacturers as positioned to benefit from rising AI optical demand; one firm with long-standing InP epitaxy expertise is described as among the few domestic companies with III-V compound semiconductor manufacturing capability. None of the Taiwanese companies named in the source produces InP wafers.

  Analysts cited in the source caution that strong orders do not guarantee sustained benefit for all participants. If upstream InP wafer supply remains constrained, downstream manufacturers could face material shortfalls even with full order books. If global InP supply gradually improves, the elevated margins and valuations associated with the current shortage could face downward revision.

  LightCounting warned that as supply conditions ease, some customers who placed duplicate orders to secure material, a practice known as double ordering, may cancel excess volume, causing order visibility across the supply chain to contract.

  ## What comes next

  1.6T optical modules are expected to begin entering large AI data centers from 2026, with adoption accelerating through 2027 and 2028. Nvidia's active promotion of CPO deployment in future AI facilities is projected to increase the number of high-speed optical modules, laser components, transceivers, and fiber connectors required at each facility, sustaining demand for InP across the supply chain.

  磷化銦（InP）是一種化合物半導體，負責在人工智慧資料中心高速光學模組內產生雷射光。Lumentum執行長麥可·赫爾斯頓警告，目前磷化銦供應量比客戶需求低逾三成，而輝達已宣布對兩家關鍵光學元件製造商合計投入40億美元，以確保次世代AI基礎設施的供應來源。

  赫爾斯頓表示，即便合計Lumentum與Coherent的總產量，兩家公司仍無法完全滿足市場需求。

  ## 何謂磷化銦，為何矽無法取代它

  磷化銦屬於III-V族化合物半導體，這類材料由元素週期表第三族與第五族元素組成。其核心特性在於具備直接能隙（direct bandgap），這是一種量子特性，能夠高效地將電能轉換為雷射光。正因如此，磷化銦成為AI資料中心所部署的800G（每秒800吉位元）及1.6T（每秒1.6太位元）光學模組內雷射光源的主流材料，涵蓋電吸收調變雷射（EML）、分散式回授雷射（DFB）及泵浦雷射。

  矽光子技術利用矽晶片傳輸、調變及處理光學訊號，外界常將其視為磷化銦的競爭威脅。然而實際上，兩者是互補關係。矽具有間接能隙，無法有效發光；因此矽光子晶片仍須借助磷化銦基礎的雷射，透過異質或混合製造技術加以整合，以提供穩定的光源。矽負責傳輸光線，磷化銦負責產生光線。

  共封裝光學（CPO）是一種封裝架構，將交換器專用積體電路（ASIC）與光學引擎置於同一模組中，縮短電氣訊號路徑並降低功耗。目前多數CPO設計採用外部雷射光源，由磷化銦製成的連續波（CW）雷射透過光纖驅動矽光子光學引擎。CPO的部署並不會降低對磷化銦的需求；市調機構LightCounting預測，隨著CPO架構更廣泛普及，將推動對更高功率、更高可靠度磷化銦雷射的需求。

  LightCounting同時預測，隨著800G及1.6T光學模組進一步普及，矽光子的滲透率雖將提升，但對磷化銦雷射的需求也將同步成長。

  ## 為何磷化銦供應難以快速擴充

  三大結構性限制制約著磷化銦產能的擴張速度，這場短缺恐怕比AI供應鏈過去在GPU與記憶體晶片所面臨的缺貨更難彌補。

  第一項制約是晶圓尺寸。矽半導體製造業已大致標準化於12吋直徑晶圓，每片晶圓可產出大量晶片。磷化銦晶圓主要以三至四吋規格生產；業界雖逐漸導入六吋生產，但普及程度仍然有限。磷化銦晶體質地脆、生長緩慢，且難以在無缺陷的情況下生產，導致每片晶圓可切出的雷射晶片數量減少，良率也因此受壓。即便資本投資已到位，新增的磷化銦產能通常仍需兩年至三年以上，才能從晶體生長歷經晶圓製造、客戶認證，走到量產階段。

  第二項制約是銦的供應。磷化銦由銦與磷化合而成。銦是鋅礦冶煉的副產品，其產量取決於鋅的生產規模，而非AI的需求。AI資本支出的快速增長，無法直接帶動更多銦進入市場。

  第三項制約是地緣政治風險。美國地質調查局2026年《礦產商品摘要》估計，2025年全球精煉銦產量約為1,100公噸，其中中國約佔總量的70%。中國商務部與海關總署於2025年2月4日宣布，對鎢、碲、鉍、鉬及銦相關產品實施出口管制，磷化銦晶片亦列入受管制類別。AXT雖在那斯達克掛牌，但其磷化銦晶體生長與晶圓製造業務係透過旗下子公司北京通美（Beijing Tongmei）進行；該子公司的出口須獲中國監管機構核准。

  ## 台灣在下游供應鏈的地位

  台灣廠商在磷化銦供應鏈的晶圓層級以下已建立穩固地位，相關業者涉及磷化銦磊晶成長、高速光電元件、光學收發器、主動光纜（AOC）、用於CPO應用的光纖陣列單元（FAU）及MPO光纖連接器，以及高速光纖互連等領域。根據報導，數家台灣製造商被認為有望受益於AI光學需求的攀升；其中一家擁有長期磷化銦磊晶專業的廠商，被描述為少數具備III-V族化合物半導體製造能力的本土業者之一。報導中點名的台灣廠商均不生產磷化銦晶圓。

  分析師提醒，訂單強勁並不保證所有參與者都能持續受益。若上游磷化銦晶圓供應持續吃緊，下游製造商即便訂單滿載，仍可能面臨原料短缺的困境。若全球磷化銦供應逐步改善，當前短缺所帶來的高毛利及高估值，也可能面臨向下修正的壓力。

  LightCounting警告，隨著供應狀況趨緩，部分為確保料源而重複下單的客戶，可能取消多餘訂單——此一做法業界稱為「重複訂購（double ordering）」——進而導致整個供應鏈的訂單能見度收縮。

  ## 後續展望

  1.6T光學模組預計將於2026年起陸續進入大型AI資料中心，並在2027至2028年間加速普及。輝達積極推動CPO在未來AI設施中的部署，預計將增加每座設施所需的高速光學模組、雷射元件、收發器及光纖連接器的數量，使磷化銦需求在整個供應鏈中得以持續延伸。
