body_en: |
  McKay Brothers has partnered with Florida-based Relativity Networks to deploy hollow core fiber networks targeting financial services locations in New Jersey, with plans to expand into additional markets following the initial rollout.

  Under the agreement, McKay will deploy Relativity's ChronoCore hollow core fiber (HCF), a cable design featuring an air-filled central channel surrounded by a ring of glass tubes in a honeycomb arrangement. The design allows light to travel faster than it does through the solid glass of conventional fiber. Relativity claims ChronoCore enables data to travel roughly 47 percent faster than through conventional glass fiber, reducing network latency.

  McKay intends to use the technology to offer dedicated dark-fiber-pair indefeasible rights of use (IRUs), which are long-term contractual grants of capacity on a fiber route, alongside managed connectivity services ranging from 10 Gbps to 400 Gbps.

  ## Why hollow core fiber

  HCF is not expected to replace conventional single-mode fiber across most routes, but rather be deployed on the most performance-critical, latency-sensitive links. The companies said those applications include connections between hyperscaler and neoscaler AI infrastructure, between data centers, between AI training and inference clusters, between cloud regions, at financial trading locations, at major network hubs, and for ultra-low-latency enterprise applications.

  The design offers higher capacity and minimized chromatic dispersion compared with conventional fiber, Relativity said. Chromatic dispersion is the tendency of different wavelengths of light to travel at slightly different speeds through a medium, an effect that degrades signal quality over distance.

  ## New Jersey launch

  New Jersey is the initial target market, with the focus on supporting key financial market locations in the state. The companies said it will serve as the launch point before the network expands into other, as yet unnamed, markets.

  Tim Boyle, McKay Brothers' chief operating officer, said the company has worked with successive generations of HCF for nearly a decade. "Relativity offers the best technical solution we have evaluated for the networks we want to build," Boyle said. He cited Relativity's U.S. patent portfolio, its production relationship with Prysmian, and what he described as IP provenance and security of supply as factors in the investment decision. "New Jersey is just the start," he added.

  Jason Eichenholz, founder and chief executive of Relativity Networks, described McKay's operational experience across data centers, outside plant, towers, and integrated fiber-wireless networks as making it "the right partner to develop this opportunity." He called the agreement "an important commercial milestone for Relativity."

  ## Manufacturing and supply chain

  ChronoCore HCF is produced at Prysmian's facility in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, and cabled at Prysmian's plant in Claremont, North Carolina. Couplers, fiber characterization, and installation training are carried out in Orlando, Florida.

  Relativity has worked closely with Italian optical cable manufacturer Prysmian on HCF development. In July, the two companies said they had produced their highest-density HCF cable to date. Colt Technology Services is among other companies that have already deployed Relativity's ChronoCore product.

  ## Industry significance

  The agreement positions HCF as commercial infrastructure for both the financial services and AI computing sectors in the United States. McKay and Relativity cited growing AI inference demand alongside financial services as drivers for the build-out, with Eichenholz describing the partnership's aim as advancing "the network infrastructure of the AI economy."

  McKay said the New Jersey deployment will serve as the foundation for expanding financial-market networks into additional markets, with no specific locations or timelines disclosed.
body_zh: |
  McKay Brothers 與佛羅里達州 Relativity Networks 達成合作，將在紐澤西州的金融服務重點位置部署空芯光纖網路，並計畫在初期建置完成後進軍其他市場。

  根據協議，McKay 將部署 Relativity 的 ChronoCore 空芯光纖（HCF）。這款光纜的設計特色是以充滿空氣的中空管道為核心，外圍以蜂巢狀排列的玻璃管環繞。光線在空氣中的傳播速度快於在傳統實心玻璃中，Relativity 宣稱 ChronoCore 能讓資料的傳輸速度比傳統玻璃光纖快約 47%，有效降低網路延遲。

  McKay 計畫利用這項技術，提供專屬暗光纖對的不可撤銷使用權（IRU）——即在特定光纖路由上長期授予容量的合約權利——以及涵蓋 10 Gbps 至 400 Gbps 的託管連線服務。

  ## 為何選擇空芯光纖

  空芯光纖預計不會在大多數路由上取代傳統單模光纖，而是優先部署於對效能要求最高、對延遲最敏感的連線。兩家公司表示，適用場景包含：超大規模雲端業者（hyperscaler）與新型大規模算力業者（neoscaler）AI 基礎設施之間的連接、資料中心之間的互聯、AI 訓練與推論叢集之間的連結、雲端區域之間的串接、金融交易場所，以及主要網路交換節點和超低延遲企業應用。

  Relativity 表示，這種設計與傳統光纖相比，具備更高的傳輸容量，並能有效降低色散（chromatic dispersion）。色散是指不同波長的光在介質中傳播速度略有差異的現象，此效應會隨距離增加而劣化訊號品質。

  ## 紐澤西州啟動

  紐澤西州是初步進駐的目標市場，重點在於支援該州的關鍵金融市場位置。兩家公司表示，紐澤西將作為網路擴展的起點，後續將進入其他尚未公布的市場。

  McKay Brothers 營運長 Tim Boyle 表示，公司與歷代空芯光纖技術合作已近十年。「Relativity 提供了我們在所有評估方案中技術最佳的解決方案，」Boyle 說。他援引 Relativity 在美國的專利組合、與 Prysmian 的量產合作關係，以及他所形容的智慧財產權來源清晰性與供應鏈安全性，作為投資決策的考量因素。「紐澤西只是個起點，」他補充說。

  Relativity Networks 創辦人暨執行長 Jason Eichenholz 形容，McKay 在資料中心、戶外管線、鐵塔，以及光纖與無線整合網路方面的豐富營運經驗，使其成為「開發這個機會的最佳夥伴」。他稱此次協議是「Relativity 的重要商業里程碑」。

  ## 製造與供應鏈

  ChronoCore 空芯光纖在 Prysmian 位於荷蘭埃因霍芬（Eindhoven）的工廠生產，並在 Prysmian 位於北卡羅來納州克萊爾蒙特（Claremont）的廠房進行成纜作業。耦合器製作、光纖特性量測及安裝培訓則在佛羅里達州奧蘭多進行。

  Relativity 長期與義大利光纖電纜製造商 Prysmian 密切合作，共同推進空芯光纖技術的開發。今年七月，兩家公司宣布已生產出迄今密度最高的空芯光纜。Colt Technology Services 是已部署 Relativity ChronoCore 產品的企業之一。

  ## 產業意義

  這份協議將空芯光纖定位為美國金融服務業與 AI 運算領域的商業基礎設施。McKay 與 Relativity 均指出，AI 推論需求的快速成長與金融服務需求同為此次建設的驅動力，Eichenholz 將這次合作的目標描述為推進「AI 經濟的網路基礎設施」。

  McKay 表示，紐澤西的部署將作為擴展金融市場網路至其他市場的基礎，但目前尚未透露具體地點或時程。
