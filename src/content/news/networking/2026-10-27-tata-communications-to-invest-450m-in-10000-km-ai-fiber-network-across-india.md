body_en: |
  Tata Communications, which provides data connections and cybersecurity services to enterprises, said Monday it will invest close to $450 million to build high-speed fiber cables linking India's AI data-center corridors, connecting cloud services companies, banks, and global capability centers — organizations that consolidate business functions such as IT, finance, and operations — to data-center clusters.

  The company said it plans to lay approximately 10,000 kilometers of new long-haul fiber cable across India.

  ## Three-phase expansion

  The build-out will proceed in three phases. The first covers the Mumbai-Pune-Hyderabad-Chennai corridor. The second targets the east coast, including Vizag — the port city of Visakhapatnam in Andhra Pradesh — and Bhubaneswar, the capital of Odisha. The third addresses western India data-center ecosystems, including Jamnagar in Gujarat and GIFT City, the Gujarat International Finance Tec-City financial services hub near Gandhinagar.

  ## Significance

  The investment represents a direct response to growing demand for dedicated, high-capacity connectivity between the AI computing infrastructure being built across India and the enterprises that consume those services. By spanning multiple regional corridors in a single program, Tata Communications is positioning its network to serve the emerging geography of Indian AI infrastructure rather than extending existing routes incrementally.

  ## 三階段擴建

  整個建設工程將分三個階段推進。第一階段涵蓋孟買—浦那—海德拉巴—清奈廊道。第二階段聚焦東岸，包括安得拉邦的港口城市維薩卡帕特南（Vizag）以及奧里薩邦首府布巴內什瓦爾。第三階段則深入西印度資料中心生態系，範圍含括古吉拉特邦的賈姆訥格爾，以及位於甘地那迦爾附近、定位為金融服務樞紐的「古吉拉特國際金融科技城」（GIFT City）。

  ## 戰略意義

  此次投資直接回應了市場對專屬高容量連線的殷切需求——這類連線旨在橋接印度各地正如火如荼建設的AI運算基礎設施，以及消費這些服務的各類企業用戶。透過一個整合計畫同步跨越多個區域廊道，塔塔通訊正在將自家網路預先佈局於印度AI基礎設施的新興地理版圖之上，而非僅在既有路線上零星延伸。
