body_en: |
  Accton Technology (2345.TW) said its 2026–2027 global investment commitments exceed NT$32 billion across five countries, with 68% of the total directed to overseas facilities, as the Taiwanese networking equipment maker repositions from component manufacturing toward systems integration to serve surging AI infrastructure demand. The company disclosed the investment plan at an investor conference in Taipei on November 27, 2026.

  ## Financial performance

  Accton's first-10-month consolidated revenue for 2026 reached NT$331.07 billion, a 65.81% year-on-year increase that already exceeds full-year 2025 revenue of NT$248.32 billion, the company said. After-tax net profit for the first three quarters of 2026 reached NT$32.45 billion, up 80.7% year-on-year, also surpassing the company's full-year 2025 profit figure. The company said revenue growth for both 2025 and 2026 has been sustained at high speed.

  By product mix, network application equipment accounted for 49.9% of first-three-quarter 2026 revenue, with network switches at 47.8%.

  ## Investment scope and geography

  The investment commitments cover sites in Taiwan, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, and the United States. Overseas locations absorb 68% of the total. Accton said the program is designed to support scaled delivery and strengthen supply-chain resilience by building out manufacturing capacity ahead of anticipated demand.

  The company stated the expansion is not purely a pursuit of manufacturing scale. It said a portion of the investment will go toward building optical communications development, testing, and validation laboratories, as well as research and development facilities for new products. Accton said it aims to accelerate technology innovation and product development by integrating resources across its global sites, characterizing the emphasis as being on "capability" as much as "capacity."

  ## Technology and product focus

  Accton identified five strategic areas for the investment program: high-speed networking, network computing equipment, optical communications solutions, AI systems and cabinets, and open infrastructure.

  On high-speed networking, the company said it will push the ramp-up of 800G — a network interface speed of 800 gigabits per second, the current leading tier for data-center interconnects — and prepare for the transition to 1.6T, or 1.6 terabits per second, the next-generation tier. The company also said it will pursue opportunities in GPU, FPGA (field-programmable gate array), and ASIC (application-specific integrated circuit) accelerator cards under its network computing equipment category.

  For optical communications, Accton said it will explore optical engines, optical transceiver modules, co-packaged optics (CPO) — a technique that integrates optical components directly onto the same package as a chip to reduce power consumption and signal loss — near-packaged optics (NPO), and silicon photonics.

  Accton said its product lineup will extend from individual components to systems-level solutions, including AI systems integration, high-density cabinet design, power systems, and thermal management.

  ## Market context

  Accton cited research forecasts that eight major cloud and emerging AI cloud providers — including Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, SpaceX, and Meta — will spend a combined US$1.66 trillion in cash capital expenditure in 2027, a 75% year-on-year increase. The company said that level of spending will drive demand for greater network bandwidth, upgrades to 800G and 1.6T high-speed networking, AI network architectures, optical communications, and systems integration.

  ## Significance

  The scale and geographic spread of Accton's investment program reflects a strategic shift from pure contract manufacturing toward higher-value systems integration and optical technologies, areas where product complexity and customer switching costs are typically greater than in component supply. The five-country footprint also reflects the broader industry movement to diversify manufacturing beyond Taiwan as supply-chain resilience has become a procurement criterion for large cloud customers.

  Accton's most forward-looking disclosed figure is the forecast that hyperscaler capital expenditure will reach US$1.66 trillion in 2027, which the company said forms the demand basis for its current 2026–2027 investment commitments.
body_zh: |
  智邦科技（股票代號：2345）宣布2026至2027年全球投資承諾逾新台幣320億元，橫跨五個國家，其中68%資金流向海外廠區。這家台灣網路設備製造商正積極從零組件製造轉型為系統整合，以因應AI基礎建設需求的強勁成長。公司於2026年11月27日在台北舉行的法人說明會上正式揭露此一投資計畫。

  ## 財務表現

  智邦科技表示，2026年前十個月合併營收達新台幣3,310.7億元，年增65.81%，已超越2025年全年營收新台幣2,483.2億元。2026年前三季稅後淨利達新台幣324.5億元，年增80.7%，同樣超過公司2025年全年獲利數字。公司表示，2025年及2026年的營收成長均維持高速成長態勢。

  就產品組合而言，2026年前三季營收中，網路應用設備占49.9%，網路交換器占47.8%。

  ## 投資範疇與地理布局

  投資承諾涵蓋台灣、越南、新加坡、馬來西亞及美國等據點，海外地點吸收總金額的68%。智邦表示，此計畫旨在因應預期需求，提前擴建製造產能，以支援大規模交貨並強化供應鏈韌性。

  公司強調，此次擴張並非單純追求製造規模。部分投資將用於建置光通訊研發、測試與驗證實驗室，以及新產品研發設施。智邦表示，將透過整合全球各據點資源來加速技術創新與產品開發，並指出這次擴張同等重視「能力」與「產能」。

  ## 技術與產品重點

  智邦為此投資計畫確立五大策略方向：高速網路、網路運算設備、光通訊解決方案、AI系統與機櫃，以及開放基礎架構。

  高速網路方面，公司表示將加速推進800G（每秒800Gbps，目前資料中心互連的主流速率規格）的量產進程，並備妥向1.6T（每秒1.6Tbps，次世代規格）過渡的技術能力。網路運算設備類別方面，公司亦表示將積極布局GPU、FPGA（現場可程式化閘陣列）及ASIC（特殊應用積體電路）加速器卡的相關商機。

  光通訊方面，智邦表示將探索光引擎、光收發模組、共封裝光學（CPO，一種將光學元件直接整合至晶片同一封裝體內的技術，可降低功耗並減少訊號損耗）、近封裝光學（NPO）及矽光子等技術。

  智邦表示，產品線將從單一零組件延伸至系統級解決方案，涵蓋AI系統整合、高密度機櫃設計、電源系統及散熱管理。

  ## 市場背景

  智邦引用研究機構預測，包含Alphabet、Amazon、Microsoft、SpaceX及Meta在內的八大主要雲端及新興AI雲端業者，2027年合計現金資本支出將達1.66兆美元，年增75%。公司表示，如此規模的支出將帶動更大網路頻寬需求、800G與1.6T高速網路升級、AI網路架構、光通訊及系統整合等需求成長。

  ## 意義

  智邦投資計畫的規模與地理多元性，反映出公司從純代工製造轉向高附加價值系統整合與光學技術的策略轉型，這些領域的產品複雜度與客戶轉換成本通常遠高於零組件供應。五國製造布局亦呼應業界更廣泛的趨勢——在供應鏈韌性已成為大型雲端客戶採購標準的背景下，製造基地正逐步分散至台灣以外。

  智邦目前公開揭露的最具前瞻性數字，是超大規模雲端業者2027年資本支出將達1.66兆美元的預測。公司表示，這一預期需求正是其當前2026至2027年投資承諾的立論基礎。
