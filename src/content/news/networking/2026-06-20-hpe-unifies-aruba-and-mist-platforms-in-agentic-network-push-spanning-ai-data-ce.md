body_en: |
  Hewlett Packard Enterprise on June 16, 2026, announced an expansion of its autonomous network strategy across AI factories, data centers, and the enterprise edge, centering on a unification of the HPE Aruba Central and HPE Mist AI operations platforms, new switching hardware for AI workloads, and a new integrated security platform.

  The announcement covers three areas: AI data center networking and routing, Agentic AIOps (agent-driven, AI-automated IT operations in which software acts on the network without continuous human instruction), and security. HPE said the initiative aims to reduce operational complexity in distributed AI environments through intelligent automation, targeting large-scale IT operations that require less manual intervention.

  ## Aruba Central and Mist platform unification

  The central operational change is the alignment of HPE Aruba Central and HPE Mist into a shared capability set. The two platforms will adopt common agent functions, hardware, and AI-native operations methods to advance the integration of HPE Aruba Networking and HPE Juniper Networking product lines, the company said.

  HPE Networking CX wired access switches will gain support for the HPE Mist platform. That addition brings AI-based visibility, automated initial configuration, Layer 2 access assurance, dynamic packet analysis, service-level analysis, and automatic remediation actions through HPE Marvis, according to the announcement.

  HPE Marvis capabilities will also extend to HPE Aruba Central, including automatic recovery of wired ports, expanding the scope of autonomous operation across the broader product portfolio, the company said.

  ## AI data center switching hardware

  HPE introduced two new products: the HPE Juniper Networking QFX5140 Switch, designed for inference clusters and edge AI workloads, and the HPE Juniper Networking QFX5252 Switch tray, a scale-up module for rack-scale AI infrastructure. Both are intended to deliver low-latency, high-bandwidth connectivity for large AI deployments.

  HPE said it has added the QFX series, suited to inference processing and scale-up configurations, to its HPE AI Data Center Solution, managed through HPE Networking Data Center Director. The integrated solution spans compute, networking, storage, software, and services. HPE said the combination is designed to accelerate the transition from experimental to production environments for AI infrastructure platforms including AMD Helios, and to improve interoperability.

  HPE stated the new switching additions aim to reduce GPU idle time and eliminate communication bottlenecks in AI infrastructure, improving operational efficiency and lowering total cost of ownership.

  ## Predictive analytics and reasoning agents for the data center

  For data center operations, HPE announced predictive analytics capabilities using AI and machine learning to detect anomaly indicators in systems and optical components before failures occur. Multi-dimensional visibility functions are intended to help operators avoid network outages and improve application resilience, the company said.

  HPE also said it will introduce advanced reasoning agents for the data center. These agents draw on millions of Technical Assistance Center (TAC) case records and the context graph database within HPE Networking Data Center Director to automate root-cause analysis and generate remediation recommendations.

  ## Compute and network management convergence

  HPE is linking HPE Mist Networking Data Center Assurance with HPE Compute Ops Management to provide unified visibility and analytics across compute and network infrastructure, reducing the number of separate management tools required. The company also said it has integrated these capabilities with HPE GreenLake under a single user interface for overall IT infrastructure management.

  ## Unified SASE security platform

  In security, HPE announced a unified SASE (Secure Access Service Edge — a framework that combines wide-area networking and network security functions in a single cloud-delivered service) platform built on HPE Networking EdgeConnect. The platform consolidates SD-WAN (software-defined wide-area networking) and cloud-delivered security into a single AI-native management interface, which HPE said is intended to simplify zero-trust deployments and strengthen protection for users, devices, and applications.

  The platform includes an SSE (Security Service Edge) connector that operates without a separate ZTNA (Zero Trust Network Access) infrastructure layer, Secure Web Gateway (SWG) tunnels that extend protection to IoT (Internet of Things) devices, and support for a Private Edge configuration that keeps traffic within enterprise boundaries. A SASE copilot feature with natural language operations and analysis capabilities is also included, the company said.

  ## Migration financing

  HPE Financial Services will launch a Network Migration Program to support transitions to AI-ready networking. The program combines hardware financing, zero-interest loans for software, and mechanisms to extract value from existing IT assets, with the stated aim of reducing customer adoption costs and migration risk.

  ## What the announcement means

  The unification of HPE Aruba Central and HPE Mist into a common operational model consolidates what had been distinct AI operations stacks within HPE's networking portfolio, integrating HPE Aruba Networking and HPE Juniper Networking product lines under shared agent capabilities and hardware. The extension of Marvis-driven automation to wired infrastructure and the data center broadens the scope of autonomous operation across the full networking stack.

  HPE Financial Services' zero-interest software financing and asset-value utilization programs indicate the company expects cost and transition risk to affect enterprise decisions on AI-ready network infrastructure refresh.

  HPE said the Network Migration Program from HPE Financial Services is set to launch alongside the broader strategy rollout announced on June 16.
body_zh: |
  惠普企業（HPE）於2026年6月16日宣布，將全面擴大其自主網路策略，涵蓋AI工廠、資料中心與企業邊緣三大領域。此次公告的核心在於整合HPE Aruba Central與HPE Mist AI運營平台，同時推出適用於AI工作負載的新型交換器硬體，以及全新的整合式安全平台。

  此次公告涵蓋三大面向：AI資料中心網路與路由、代理式AIOps（由代理程式驅動、AI自動化的IT運營，軟體無需人工持續介入即可自主管理網路），以及資安防護。HPE表示，這項計畫旨在透過智慧自動化，降低分散式AI環境的運營複雜度，目標是讓大規模IT運營減少對人工操作的依賴。

  ## Aruba Central與Mist平台整合

  此次最核心的運營變革，在於將HPE Aruba Central與HPE Mist整合為共同的能力集合。HPE表示，兩套平台將採用共通的代理功能、硬體及AI原生運營方法，以推進HPE Aruba網路與HPE Juniper網路產品線的深度整合。

  HPE Networking CX有線存取交換器將新增對HPE Mist平台的支援。根據公告，此舉將透過HPE Marvis引入以AI為基礎的可視性、自動化初始配置、第二層存取保障、動態封包分析、服務等級分析，以及自動修復功能。

  HPE Marvis的能力也將延伸至HPE Aruba Central，包括有線連接埠的自動復原功能，進一步擴大整個產品組合中自主運營的適用範圍。

  ## AI資料中心交換器硬體

  HPE推出兩款新產品：HPE Juniper Networking QFX5140交換器，專為推論叢集與邊緣AI工作負載而設計；以及HPE Juniper Networking QFX5252交換器托盤，作為機架規模AI基礎架構的擴充模組。兩款產品均以為大型AI部署提供低延遲、高頻寬連線能力為目標。

  HPE表示，已將適合推論處理與擴充配置的QFX系列納入其HPE AI資料中心解決方案，並透過HPE Networking Data Center Director進行管理。整合解決方案涵蓋運算、網路、儲存、軟體與服務。HPE指出，此組合旨在加速AI基礎架構平台（包括AMD Helios）從實驗環境過渡至正式生產環境，並提升互通性。

  HPE表示，新增的交換器產品旨在降低GPU閒置時間、消除AI基礎架構中的通訊瓶頸，進而提升運營效率並降低總持有成本。

  ## 資料中心預測分析與推論代理

  在資料中心運營方面，HPE宣布推出預測分析功能，利用AI與機器學習技術，在故障發生之前提前偵測系統與光學元件中的異常徵兆。多維度可視性功能旨在協助運營人員預防網路中斷，提升應用程式的韌性。

  HPE同時宣布，將為資料中心引入進階推論代理。這些代理程式能夠調用數百萬筆技術協助中心（TAC）案例記錄，以及HPE Networking Data Center Director內建的情境圖形資料庫，以自動化根本原因分析並產生修復建議。

  ## 運算與網路管理整合

  HPE正將HPE Mist Networking Data Center Assurance與HPE Compute Ops Management相互串聯，以提供橫跨運算與網路基礎架構的統一可視性與分析能力，減少所需的獨立管理工具數量。HPE也表示，已透過單一使用者介面將這些功能整合至HPE GreenLake，實現整體IT基礎架構的統一管理。

  ## 統一SASE安全平台

  在資安領域，HPE宣布推出以HPE Networking EdgeConnect為基礎的統一SASE（安全存取服務邊緣，一種將廣域網路與網路安全功能整合為單一雲端交付服務的架構）平台。該平台將SD-WAN（軟體定義廣域網路）與雲端交付的資安功能整合至單一AI原生管理介面，HPE表示此舉旨在簡化零信任部署，強化對使用者、裝置及應用程式的防護。

  該平台包含一個無需獨立ZTNA（零信任網路存取）基礎架構層即可運作的SSE（安全服務邊緣）連接器、可將防護延伸至IoT（物聯網）裝置的安全網頁閘道（SWG）通道，以及支援將流量保留在企業邊界內的私有邊緣（Private Edge）配置。此外，平台也內建具備自然語言操作與分析能力的SASE協作助理（Copilot）功能。

  ## 遷移融資方案

  HPE Financial Services將推出「網路遷移計畫」，協助客戶過渡至AI就緒網路。此計畫結合硬體融資、軟體零利率貸款，以及從現有IT資產中釋放價值的機制，目標是降低客戶的採用成本與遷移風險。

  ## 公告的意義

  將HPE Aruba Central與HPE Mist整合為共同運營模式，統合了HPE網路產品組合中原本各自獨立的AI運營技術堆疊，讓HPE Aruba網路與HPE Juniper網路產品線共享代理功能與硬體。將Marvis驅動的自動化延伸至有線基礎架構與資料中心，進一步拓展了自主運營在完整網路堆疊中的適用範圍。

  HPE Financial Services推出的軟體零利率融資及資產價值利用計畫，顯示HPE預期成本與過渡風險將是企業評估AI就緒網路基礎架構汰換時的關鍵考量因素。

  HPE表示，HPE Financial Services的網路遷移計畫將與6月16日公告的整體策略同步推出。
