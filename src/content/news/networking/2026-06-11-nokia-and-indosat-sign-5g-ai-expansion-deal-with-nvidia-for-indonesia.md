body_en: |
  Nokia and Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison (IOH) have announced a network expansion partnership under which Nokia will serve as primary technology supplier for a 5G rollout across Indonesia, with artificial intelligence built into the radio access layer through a three-way collaboration that also includes NVIDIA.

  Under the agreement, Nokia will deploy its latest Habrok and Pandion radio units, Levante baseband hardware, Centralized RAN (radio access network) architecture, and network management and automation platforms. Low-band 5G — which uses lower radio frequencies to deliver wide geographic coverage — will be deployed across the entire network. Mid-band 5G, which delivers higher data speeds over shorter distances, is targeted to reach approximately 80 percent of the network within 3.5 years, the companies said.

  The three companies have also been jointly developing AI-RAN — an architecture that runs wireless connectivity and AI processing on shared infrastructure at the network edge — and completed the first AI-RAN call together at Mobile World Congress 2026. Field trials in Indonesia are scheduled for the end of 2026, the companies said.

  ## Equipment deployment

  Nokia's hardware scope covers Habrok and Pandion radio units, the Levante baseband platform, Centralized RAN, and network management and automation systems. The company said those systems will support more efficient operations, improved energy performance, and faster service delivery. The agreement covers both retail and enterprise customers across sectors including public services, industry, and digital services, Indosat said.

  ## AI-RAN development and field trials

  AI-RAN provides a shared architecture that places AI workloads alongside connectivity functions on the same radio access infrastructure. Nokia and NVIDIA have been developing the technology jointly and have brought Indosat into that work.

  As part of the partnership, Nokia will develop new AI algorithms designed to improve spectrum efficiency — the amount of data that can be transmitted over a given slice of radio frequency — on NVIDIA's AI-RAN platform. Those capabilities are expected to be included in the late-2026 field trials, the companies said.

  Indosat is simultaneously building what it calls an AI Grid, which combines centralized AI factories with distributed AI-RAN infrastructure. The company said the AI Grid is intended to bring AI capabilities and connectivity to millions of Indonesians and has already accelerated real-world deployments in public services, healthcare, education, and agriculture. The initiative is supported by an AI-RAN Innovation Center in Surabaya and the NVIDIA AI Technology Center ecosystem.

  ## Executive statements

  Vikram Sinha, President Director and CEO of Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison, said the partnership is aimed at building an AI-integrated network foundation that will improve connectivity quality and deliver a smoother digital experience for customers.

  Justin Hotard, President and CEO of Nokia, said the next phase of network evolution will be determined by operators' ability to combine connectivity, intelligence, and operational scale. "Together with Indosat and NVIDIA, Nokia is helping build the next evolution of networks — networks capable of expanding 5G coverage, supporting more AI-based services, and creating long-term value," Hotard said. He described the agreement as reflecting a broader industry shift as operators invest in high-performance networks that also support new business models and improved efficiency.

  Ronnie Vasishta, SVP Telecoms at NVIDIA, said Indosat and Nokia are demonstrating how 5G networks can evolve into intelligence platforms. "By jointly developing AI-RAN and moving toward field trials in Indonesia, we are helping build an architecture where AI and connectivity can run side by side to improve efficiency, deliver new applications, and support digital transformation at scale," Vasishta said.

  ## What the deal means

  The agreement positions Nokia as the lead RAN supplier for a major Indonesian mobile operator's next network cycle, with mid-band 5G coverage targeted at roughly 80 percent of the network over a 3.5-year horizon. The AI-RAN component places Indonesia among the first markets where the Nokia-NVIDIA joint architecture will advance from a laboratory demonstration — the MWC 2026 call — to a live network trial.

  The late-2026 field trial in Indonesia is the next scheduled milestone for the three-party AI-RAN collaboration.

  Nokia與Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison（IOH）宣布網路擴展合作協議，Nokia將擔任印尼5G建網的主要技術供應商，並透過同時納入輝達（NVIDIA）的三方協作，在無線接取層導入人工智慧，共同推進這項建設計畫。

  根據協議，Nokia將部署旗下最新的Habrok與Pandion無線電單元、Levante基頻硬體、集中式無線接取網路（Centralized RAN）架構，以及網路管理與自動化平台。低頻段5G——採用較低無線電頻率以提供廣泛地理覆蓋——將遍及整個網路全面建設。中頻段5G以較短距離傳輸更高速率的資料，三家公司表示，目標是在3.5年內覆蓋約80%的網路。

  三家公司也共同開發AI-RAN，這是一種在網路邊緣以共享基礎設施，同時運行無線連線與AI運算的架構，並已於2026年世界行動通訊大會（MWC 2026）共同完成首次AI-RAN通話。印尼的現地測試預計於2026年底展開，三家公司表示。

  ## 設備部署

  Nokia的硬體範疇涵蓋Habrok與Pandion無線電單元、Levante基頻平台、集中式RAN，以及網路管理與自動化系統。該公司表示，這些系統將支援更高效的運營、改善能源效能，並加速服務交付。Indosat表示，此協議涵蓋零售與企業客戶，涉及公共服務、工業及數位服務等多個領域。

  ## AI-RAN開發與現地測試

  AI-RAN提供共享架構，將AI工作負載與連線功能置於同一無線接取基礎設施上並行運作。Nokia與輝達持續共同開發這項技術，並已將Indosat納入合作。

  作為此次合作的一部分，Nokia將為輝達的AI-RAN平台開發全新AI演算法，目標在於提升頻譜效率——即在特定無線電頻段內可傳輸的資料量。三家公司表示，這些功能預計將納入2026年底的現地測試。

  Indosat同時正在建構其所稱的「AI Grid」，結合集中式AI工廠與分散式AI-RAN基礎設施。該公司表示，AI Grid旨在為數百萬印尼人帶來AI能力與連線服務，並已在公共服務、醫療、教育及農業等領域加速真實場景的落地部署。此計畫獲得位於泗水（Surabaya）的AI-RAN創新中心，以及輝達AI技術中心生態系的支持。

  ## 高層聲明

  Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison總裁暨執行長Vikram Sinha表示，此次合作旨在建立整合AI的網路基礎，以提升連線品質，為用戶帶來更順暢的數位體驗。

  Nokia總裁暨執行長Justin Hotard表示，網路演進的下一階段，將取決於電信業者整合連線能力、智慧與運營規模的能力。「我們與Indosat及輝達攜手，協助打造下一世代的網路演進——這些網路能夠擴展5G覆蓋範圍、支援更多AI服務，並創造長期價值，」Hotard說道。他形容此協議反映了業界更廣泛的轉型趨勢——電信業者正積極投資高效能網路，同時開拓新商業模式、提升整體運營效率。

  輝達電信業務資深副總裁Ronnie Vasishta表示，Indosat與Nokia正在示範5G網路如何演進為智慧平台。「透過共同開發AI-RAN並邁向印尼的現地測試，我們正在協助建立一個讓AI與連線能力並肩運作的架構，以提升效率、催生新應用，並大規模支持數位轉型，」Vasishta說道。

  ## 協議意義

  此協議將Nokia定位為印尼主要行動電信業者下一個網路建設週期的主要RAN供應商，中頻段5G覆蓋目標是在3.5年內達到約80%的網路。AI-RAN部分使印尼成為Nokia與輝達聯合架構從實驗室示範——即MWC 2026通話——推進至真實網路測試的首批市場之一。

  2026年底在印尼進行的現地測試，是三方AI-RAN合作下一個預定的重要里程碑。
