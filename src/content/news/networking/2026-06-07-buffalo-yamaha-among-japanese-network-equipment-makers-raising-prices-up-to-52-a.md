  ## Japanese Network Equipment Vendors Announce Sweeping Price Increases

  Japanese network equipment manufacturers including Buffalo and Yamaha have raised product prices by as much as 52%, adding to cost pressure on corporate network budgets already strained by rising labor and transportation expenses, according to Nikkei xTECH reporting published June 5, 2026.

  Buffalo, a major vendor of routers, switches, and wireless access points, announced increases of 5% to 52% on select products in March 2026. Yamaha, whose lineup includes enterprise-grade routers and layer-2 switches, announced increases of approximately 12% to 26%, effective May 28, 2026. The publication reported that other major manufacturers have also successively raised prices, though it did not specify figures for those vendors.

  The increases are not limited to hardware acquisition costs. Equipment price hikes also flow through to maintenance and operations budgets, while labor and logistics costs are rising in parallel, the report said.

  ## Three Practitioner Strategies

  Nikkei xTECH consulted practitioners from three categories — a network integrator, a network equipment manufacturer, and a corporate information-systems department — and identified three cost-control principles: eliminate excess wired local area network (LAN) infrastructure, standardize on a smaller set of equipment models, and buy ahead of announced price increases.

  ## Cutting Wired LAN Infrastructure

  The first principle centers on removing wired LAN gear and cabling that exceeds current requirements. Kota Sasaki, of Mitsui Information's Enterprise First Technology Division, said reviewing which network equipment remains necessary is among the most effective cost-control steps available, with wired LAN components offering particular room for reduction.

  Most corporate offices now rely primarily on wireless LAN — commonly called Wi-Fi — rather than wired connections, the report noted. Sasaki said that when companies refresh existing networks or build new ones, minimizing the number of wired LAN cables and wall-mounted data outlets reduces both construction labor and materials costs.

  Yu Nishiura, also of Mitsui Information's Enterprise First Technology Division, said reducing installed wired equipment further yields a secondary benefit by freeing physical office space, adding an indirect cost saving on top of the direct infrastructure reduction.

  ## Addressing Redundancy Concerns

  Some corporate network managers retain wired LAN specifically as a fallback when Wi-Fi connectivity fails, and may be reluctant to scale it back. Sasaki said redundancy can still be achieved through a different wireless method: the tethering function on company-issued smartphones, which allows a device to share its mobile data connection with a laptop or other equipment.

  He noted this approach requires either that business applications have been migrated to cloud services, or that the corporate environment supports access to internal systems over a tethered mobile connection.

  ## Significance for Enterprise IT Planning

  The simultaneous price increases across multiple vendors — Buffalo, Yamaha, and others named without detail — indicate that cost pressure is industry-wide rather than confined to individual manufacturers. For enterprise IT planners and network engineers, the compounding effect of higher equipment prices alongside rising labor and logistics costs means network refresh project budgets may need upward revision. The practitioners' recommendation to buy ahead of announced increases suggests that procurement timing has become a cost variable in its own way alongside product selection and infrastructure design.

  ## 日本網路設備商宣布大幅調漲售價

  據《Nikkei xTECH》於2026年6月5日發布的報導，日本網路設備製造商Buffalo與Yamaha等廠商已將部分產品售價調漲最高達52%，在人事與運輸成本雙雙走高的背景下，進一步加重了企業網路建置預算的負擔。

  Buffalo是路由器、交換器及無線存取點的主要供應商，該公司於2026年3月宣布針對特定產品調漲5%至52%不等。Yamaha的產品線涵蓋企業級路由器與第二層交換器，該公司宣布自2026年5月28日起，調漲幅度約為12%至26%。報導指出，其他主要製造商也相繼跟進調漲，但未具體說明各家廠商的漲幅數字。

  此波漲價影響並不侷限於硬體採購成本。設備價格上漲的效應同樣會傳遞至維護與營運預算，與此同時，人事成本與物流費用也在同步攀升，報導如此指出。

  ## 業界人士提出三大因應策略

  《Nikkei xTECH》訪問了來自三類機構的實務工作者——包括網路整合商、網路設備製造商，以及企業資訊系統部門——歸納出三項控制成本的原則：精簡多餘的有線區域網路（LAN）基礎設施、統一採用較少的設備型號，以及在廠商公告漲價前提前採購。

  ## 精簡有線LAN基礎設施

  第一項原則的核心在於汰除超出現行需求的有線LAN設備與佈線。三井資訊企業第一技術部門的佐佐木航太表示，重新檢視哪些網路設備確實必要，是目前最有效的成本控制手段之一，而有線LAN元件尤其具有縮減的空間。

  報導指出，多數企業辦公室如今主要仰賴無線LAN（俗稱Wi-Fi），而非有線連線。佐佐木表示，當企業進行既有網路的汰換更新或建置新網路時，盡量減少有線LAN纜線數量與牆面資料插座的設置，可同時降低施工人力與建材費用。

  同屬三井資訊企業第一技術部門的西浦悠表示，減少已安裝的有線設備還能帶來額外效益——騰出辦公室的實體空間，在直接削減基礎設施成本之外，進一步創造間接的節費效果。

  ## 因應備援需求的考量

  部分企業的網路管理人員會將有線LAN保留為Wi-Fi連線中斷時的備援方案，因此可能對縮減有線基礎設施有所顧慮。佐佐木指出，備援需求仍可透過另一種無線方式實現：善用公司配發智慧型手機的網路共享（數據機熱點）功能，讓裝置透過行動數據網路為筆記型電腦或其他設備提供連線。

  他補充說明，採用此方案的前提是企業的業務應用程式已遷移至雲端服務，或企業環境支援透過行動網路熱點連線存取內部系統。

  ## 對企業IT規劃的重要意涵

  Buffalo、Yamaha及其他未具名廠商同步調漲售價，顯示這股成本壓力是整個產業的普遍現象，並非個別廠商的特殊情況。對於企業IT規劃人員與網路工程師而言，設備價格上漲疊加人事與物流成本走高的複合效應，意味著網路汰換更新專案的預算可能需要向上修正。業界人士建議在公告漲價前提前採購，也說明採購時機本身已成為一項成本變數，與產品選型和基礎設施設計同等重要。
