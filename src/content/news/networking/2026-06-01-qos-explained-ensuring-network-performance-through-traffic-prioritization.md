# QoS Explained: Ensuring Network Performance Through Traffic Prioritization

Quality of Service (QoS) is a network technology that ensures critical communications maintain performance standards. By prioritizing traffic according to application type and allocating necessary bandwidth, QoS enables networks to deliver consistent quality during peak usage.

## 中文

# QoS 技術：透過流量優先權確保網路效能

服務品質（QoS）是一項網路技術，用以確保關鍵通訊維持效能標準。透過根據應用程式類型優先排序流量並分配必要頻寬，QoS 使網路即使在尖峰使用時段也能提供穩定的服務品質。
