body_en: |
  Taiwanese optical component maker Shangquan (上詮) said its fiber array unit products will enter volume production for Nvidia's Vera Rubin AI platform in the third quarter of 2026, with fourth-quarter shipments expected to exceed third-quarter volumes and revenue and profit improvement anticipated starting in Q3.

  Shangquan chairman Lin Songfu told Commercial Times in an exclusive interview published July 3 that shipments would grow sequentially thereafter. Lin described AI optical communications as a "once-in-a-century industry opportunity" and said the company would commit fully to it.

  ## Vera Rubin supply chain context

  The Vera Rubin platform, described in the article as Nvidia's next-generation AI server architecture, has begun mass production and is being delivered to customers. The platform draws on several Taiwanese suppliers: TSMC handles advanced manufacturing processes and advanced packaging; silicon photonics component maker 奇景光電 supplies key optical components; and Foxconn Industrial Internet (工業富聯), a unit of Hon Hai (鴻海), covers AI server switch business and has confirmed that Vera Rubin production and delivery are underway, according to the article.

  Shangquan's role in the supply chain focuses on FAUs — fiber array units, components that align multiple optical fibers into precise arrays to couple light signals between chips and fibers — and optical coupling.

  ## Technical differentiation

  Lin said Shangquan's competitive advantage spans the full production chain: component development, precision alignment and coupling between FAUs and COUPE units, co-development of automated FAU production and packaging equipment with equipment suppliers, and iterative yield improvement through to stable mass production. He described each stage as carrying a high technical barrier that the company and its partners have cleared.

  For Q3 and Q4 products, Shangquan is targeting compliance with customer verification standards. Alongside yield improvement, the company is optimizing processes and equipment calibration to shorten production cycles while maintaining quality, Lin said.

  ## CPO timeline and demand signals

  On CPO — co-packaged optics, an architecture that integrates optical transceivers directly alongside switching or compute chips to reduce signal loss and power consumption — Lin said commercialization timing is not within any single company's control. CPO requires coordinated development across chip, packaging, optics, testing, and system integration suppliers, and its commercial schedule will depend on the overall progress of leading industry vendors and system customers, he said.

  Shangquan has not observed any demand slowdown, Lin said. Customer delivery schedules remain tight, and the company is continuing to invest in building capacity for 2027 and 2028 demand.

  ## Architecture coexistence

  Lin said LPO (linear-drive pluggable optics), NPO (near-packaged optics), and CPO will coexist in the market for a period. Continuing upgrades to AI data center scale-up architectures are driving rapid growth in demand for high-speed optical interconnects, raising the strategic importance of high-bandwidth FAUs, he said.

  Shangquan co-develops products with each customer and follows whichever architecture the customer adopts. "The ones who truly decide the direction are the customers," Lin said, as quoted in the article.

  ## Three-to-five year roadmap

  Over the next three to five years, Shangquan plans to continue developing FAUs capable of 12.8 terabits per second and above, and to advance WDM — wavelength division multiplexing, a technique that carries multiple wavelengths of light simultaneously through a single fiber to multiply transmission capacity — aligned with customer technology roadmaps, positioning the company for the next wave of AI optical interconnect demand, Lin said.

  ## 上詮第三季起出貨Vera Rubin光纖陣列元件，預期逐季成長

  台灣光學元件廠上詮表示，其供應輝達Vera Rubin AI平台所需的光纖陣列單元（FAU）產品，將於2026年第三季進入量產階段，第四季出貨量預計超越第三季，營收與獲利也可望自第三季起逐步好轉。

  上詮董事長林松富在7月3日刊出的《工商時報》獨家專訪中透露，後續出貨量將呈現逐季成長的態勢。林松富將AI光通訊形容為「百年一遇的產業機遇」，強調公司將全力投入其中。

  ## Vera Rubin供應鏈背景

  Vera Rubin平台是輝達的新世代AI伺服器架構，目前已進入量產並陸續交付客戶。該平台匯聚多家台灣供應商的力量：台積電負責先進製程與先進封裝；矽光子元件廠奇景光電提供關鍵光學元件；鴻海旗下子公司工業富聯則承接AI伺服器交換器業務，並確認Vera Rubin的生產與交貨作業已全面啟動。

  上詮在此供應鏈中的定位，聚焦於FAU（光纖陣列單元）與光學耦合領域。FAU是將多根光纖精確排列成陣列、以利光訊號在晶片與光纖之間耦合傳輸的關鍵元件。

  ## 技術差異化優勢

  林松富表示，上詮的競爭優勢貫穿整條生產鏈，涵蓋元件開發、FAU與COUPE單元之間的精密對準與耦合、與設備廠商共同開發自動化FAU生產與封裝設備，以及持續迭代良率、直至穩定量產等各個環節。他強調，每個階段都存在極高的技術門檻，而公司與合作夥伴已逐一突破。

  針對第三季與第四季的產品，上詮以符合客戶驗證標準為目標。林松富說，除持續提升良率外，公司也在優化製程與設備校準，以縮短生產週期，同時確保品質不打折。

  ## CPO時程與需求訊號

  談及CPO（共封裝光學，一種將光學收發器直接整合於交換或運算晶片旁側、以降低訊號損耗與功耗的架構），林松富表示，商業化的時間點並非任何單一企業所能掌控。CPO需要晶片、封裝、光學、測試及系統整合等各方供應商協同開發，其商業化進程將取決於領頭業者與系統客戶的整體推進速度。

  林松富指出，上詮目前並未觀察到任何需求降溫的跡象，客戶的交貨排程依然緊湊，公司也持續投入布局，為2027年與2028年的需求預先建立產能。

  ## 多種架構並存格局

  林松富表示，LPO（線性驅動可插拔光學模組）、NPO（近封裝光學）與CPO三種架構在市場上將並存一段時間。隨著AI資料中心擴展架構持續升級，高速光學互連的需求快速增長，高頻寬FAU的戰略地位也隨之大幅提升。

  上詮與每位客戶共同開發產品，並跟隨客戶所採用的架構方向走。林松富在專訪中表示：「真正決定方向的，還是客戶。」

  ## 三至五年技術藍圖

  展望未來三至五年，上詮計畫持續開發每秒12.8兆位元（Tbps）及以上傳輸規格的FAU，並推進WDM（波長分割多工，一種在單根光纖中同時傳輸多個波長光訊號、藉此成倍提升傳輸容量的技術）的技術發展，對齊客戶的技術藍圖，為迎接下一波AI光學互連需求預先卡位，林松富說。
