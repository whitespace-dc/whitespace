body_en: |
  PT Inti Bangun Sejahtera Tbk (IBST), the Djarum Group's publicly listed telecommunications-infrastructure company, is shifting its strategic emphasis toward squeezing more revenue from assets it already owns — more than 3,200 telecommunication towers and nearly 19,500 kilometers of fiber-optic cable — rather than adding new infrastructure, Director Doni Wilaga Kusuma said on June 5, 2026.

  ## Tower Strategy: More Tenants Per Site

  On the tower side, IBST's priority is colocation — signing additional tenants onto existing structures rather than building new ones. Colocation improves the revenue generated per tower without adding proportionate cost, since the physical structure, land, and power connections are already in place. "Our main focus right now is increasing utilization and productivity of assets we already have. For the tower business, we continue to push for colocation increases and the addition of tenants at existing sites," Doni said in a virtual statement.

  ## Fiber Revenue: Backhaul, Metro-E, and Leased Lines

  For its fiber network — nearly 19,500 kilometers, a scale roughly equivalent to the distance from Jakarta to London — IBST is expanding the range of commercial services it sells over existing cable. The company is targeting connectivity, backhaul (the links that carry traffic from cell sites to the core network), metro Ethernet (metro-E, high-speed local-area data links used by enterprises and carriers), leased lines, and other fiber-based solutions, Doni said.

  ## Group Synergies With Protelindo and iForte

  IBST's ability to execute on both fronts is tied to its integration into Protelindo Group, which also controls iForte Group. Doni said the group structure creates opportunities to optimize asset use in an integrated way, widen the customer base, and improve infrastructure-management efficiency. "With the synergies alongside Protelindo Group and iForte Group and their subsidiaries, the company has greater opportunities to optimize asset utilization in an integrated manner, expand the customer base, and improve the efficiency of infrastructure management," he said.

  ## Financial Impact of Group Integration

  IBST recorded improved profitability through 2025, according to Director Suciratin, who attributed a portion of the EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization — a standard measure of operating cash generation) and margin improvement to joining the Protelindo Group. "Joining the Protelindo Group has provided many strategic benefits, both operationally, financially, and for business development," Suciratin said. "The company has gained access to greater business scale, the application of wider best practices, and various synergy opportunities that can increase efficiency and competitiveness."

  ## Significance

  The strategy reflects a broader pattern in Southeast Asian tower and fiber markets, where infrastructure owners that built out aggressively in prior years are now under pressure to demonstrate that existing assets generate adequate returns. By concentrating on tenancy ratios and fiber service diversification rather than capital-intensive construction, IBST is positioning its balance sheet to benefit from revenue gains with comparatively limited incremental spending. The company's forward focus, as stated, is on monetizing the connectivity, backhaul, and enterprise-service opportunities it says its current fiber footprint makes available.

  ## IBST聚焦提升租戶率與光纖營收，優化逾3,200座鐵塔及19,500公里纜線

  Djarum集團旗下上市電信基礎設施公司PT Inti Bangun Sejahtera Tbk（IBST）正逐步將策略重心從新建基礎設施轉向深度挖掘既有資產的價值。該公司旗下擁有逾3,200座電信鐵塔及近19,500公里光纖電纜，董事Doni Wilaga Kusuma於2026年6月5日表示，公司目前的優先要務是從已持有的資產中爭取更高的營收，而非持續追加新建設。

  ## 鐵塔策略：提高每座站點的租戶數量

  在鐵塔業務方面，IBST的首要目標是推動共址（colocation）——即在現有鐵塔結構上增加租用的電信業者，而非新建鐵塔。由於實體塔架、土地及電力設施均已到位，共址模式能在不顯著增加成本的前提下提升每座鐵塔的營收貢獻。「我們目前最核心的任務，就是提升既有資產的使用率與生產效益。在鐵塔業務上，我們持續推動共址成長，並增加現有站點的租戶數量，」Doni在一份線上聲明中表示。

  ## 光纖營收：回傳鏈路、都會乙太網路與專線服務

  在光纖網路方面，IBST的近19,500公里纜線規模相當於從雅加達到倫敦的直線距離，公司正積極擴展透過既有纜線所能提供的商業服務項目。Doni表示，公司的目標市場涵蓋一般連網服務、回傳鏈路（backhaul，即將基地台流量傳輸至核心網路的傳輸段）、都會乙太網路（Metro-E，供企業與電信業者使用的高速區域資料連線）、專線，以及其他以光纖為基礎的解決方案。

  ## 與Protelindo及iForte的集團綜效

  IBST在上述兩大業務的執行能力，與其整合進Protelindo集團密不可分，而Protelindo集團旗下亦控股iForte集團。Doni表示，集團架構為IBST創造了以整合方式優化資產運用的機會，同時有助於擴大客戶群、提升基礎設施管理效率。「憑藉與Protelindo集團及iForte集團及其子公司之間的綜效，公司擁有更大的機會，能以整合方式優化資產利用效率、擴展客戶基礎，並提升基礎設施管理的整體效能，」他說。

  ## 集團整合帶來的財務效益

  董事Suciratin指出，IBST在2025年全年的獲利能力呈現改善態勢，並將其中部分EBITDA（息稅折舊攤銷前利潤，即衡量營運現金創造能力的標準指標）及利潤率的提升歸因於加入Protelindo集團所帶來的效益。「加入Protelindo集團帶來了許多策略性利益，涵蓋營運、財務及業務拓展等各個層面，」Suciratin表示。「公司得以獲取更大的業務規模、應用更廣泛的最佳實務，並把握各種可提升效率與競爭力的綜效機會。」

  ## 策略意義

  這一策略轉向，反映出東南亞鐵塔與光纖市場的普遍趨勢——在過去數年積極建設的基礎設施業者，如今承受著更大壓力，必須證明既有資產能創造足夠的投資回報。IBST選擇聚焦於提升租戶率與光纖服務多元化，而非投入資本密集的新建工程，此舉有助於在資本支出相對有限的情況下，從營收成長中提升資產負債表的整體效益。就公司對外宣示的前進方向而言，IBST的策略核心在於將現有光纖足跡所能提供的連網服務、回傳鏈路及企業服務商機，切實轉化為具體的商業收益。
