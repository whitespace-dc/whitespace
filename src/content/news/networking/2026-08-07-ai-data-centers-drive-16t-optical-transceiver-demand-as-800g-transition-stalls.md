  ## Demand Rises Even as AI Capital Spending Faces Scrutiny

  ALT Group has introduced a 1.6T optical transceiver module through its subsidiary, targeting AI data centers that require high-bandwidth, high-speed data transmission, as the global market for AI-dedicated optical transceivers is forecast to grow more than 57% year-over-year — from $16.5 billion in 2025 to $26 billion in 2026, according to TrendForce research published in April 2026.

  The announcement comes as AI-related equities have grown more volatile and markets have begun re-examining the return on AI capital expenditure. The underlying upgrade cycle in data center infrastructure, however, has not paused, according to Commercial Times reporting.

  ## What 800G and 1.6T Mean

  Optical transceiver modules convert electrical signals into optical signals and back, enabling high-speed data transfer between AI servers, network switches, and computing nodes. The numbers describe port speed: 800G indicates a transmission rate of 800 gigabits per second (Gb/s); 1.6T indicates 1.6 terabits per second (Tb/s), nominally double the throughput of 800G.

  As GPU clusters grow in scale, the volume of data moving between servers and switches increases proportionally. If network bandwidth does not keep pace, data bottlenecks between servers and switches prevent computing resources from being fully utilized.

  ## NVIDIA's GB300 Illustrates Accelerating Bandwidth Requirements

  NVIDIA's GB300 NVL72 system uses the ConnectX-8 SuperNIC, which provides up to 800 Gb/s of network connectivity per GPU. The configuration reflects the rising demand for high-speed, low-latency networking in next-generation AI servers. As switch capacity and port density increase further, 1.6T transceivers are becoming the next reference specification for AI data center high-speed interconnects, according to the report.

  ## The 800G-to-1.6T Transition Is Not a Simple Swap

  For data center operators and server system integrators, moving from 800G to 1.6T involves more than replacing transceivers. Operators must also assess switch capacity, fiber cabling, connector interfaces, device power draw, thermal management, and system-level compatibility. A significant installed base of 800G equipment remains in service and is still being depreciated, but new AI server projects are already incorporating 1.6T network requirements into their planning.

  TrendForce identified 2026 and 2027 as the critical window for vendors to enter the next-generation supply chain as 1.6T modules move toward volume production.

  ## ALT Group's Two-Track Product Strategy

  ALT Group is addressing the transition through two parallel product lines. The 1.6T transceiver is designed for deployment compatibility across different AI server configurations and network architectures. The company's second offering is a modular containerized data center (CDC) product, designed to address the gap between the rapid growth in AI server demand and the longer build times associated with conventional data hall construction; ALT Group says the CDC can be deployed in as little as 12 weeks.

  ALT Group chief executive Liang Jianguo said the company sees bandwidth and deployment speed as simultaneous constraints on AI infrastructure. "Our 1.6T optical transceiver improves data transmission capability while emphasizing deployment compatibility in different application environments," Liang said, as quoted by Commercial Times. "The CDC shortens data center construction timelines through modular design, allowing enterprises to respond more quickly to capacity demands as AI server counts increase."

  ## Industry Outlook

  The report concluded that 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers will coexist for the foreseeable future, with neither standard displacing the other immediately. At the same time, the rapid growth in AI server deployments is expected to push data center construction toward modular and accelerated-deployment models. TrendForce's forecast treats 2026 and 2027 as the period when vendors in the 1.6T supply chain can establish competitive positions before the specification becomes standard across major AI data center builds.

  ## AI資本支出受到檢視，需求依然攀升

  ALT集團透過旗下子公司，推出1.6T光收發器模組，鎖定需要高頻寬、高速資料傳輸的AI資料中心市場。根據TrendForce於2026年4月發布的研究報告，全球專用於AI的光收發器市場預計將年增逾57%，從2025年的165億美元成長至2026年的260億美元。

  此次發布恰逢AI相關股票波動加劇、市場開始重新審視AI資本支出投資回報之際。然而，根據《工商時報》報導，資料中心基礎設施的升級週期並未因此停歇。

  ## 800G與1.6T代表什麼意義

  光收發器模組負責將電訊號轉換為光訊號、再將光訊號還原為電訊號，實現AI伺服器、網路交換器與運算節點之間的高速資料傳輸。數字代表的是埠口速率：800G指每秒傳輸800Gb（Gb/s）；1.6T則指每秒1.6Tb（Tb/s），名義上是800G吞吐量的兩倍。

  隨著GPU叢集規模持續擴大，伺服器與交換器之間流動的資料量也等比例增加。若網路頻寬無法跟上需求，伺服器與交換器之間便會出現資料瓶頸，導致運算資源無法充分發揮效能。

  ## 輝達GB300彰顯頻寬需求急速攀升

  輝達的GB300 NVL72系統搭載ConnectX-8 SuperNIC，每顆GPU可提供高達每秒800Gb的網路連線能力。此一配置充分體現了下一代AI伺服器對高速、低延遲網路的殷切需求。報告指出，隨著交換器容量與埠口密度持續提升，1.6T光收發器正逐步成為AI資料中心高速互連的下一個參考規格。

  ## 從800G升級至1.6T並非單純的替換作業

  對資料中心營運商與伺服器系統整合商而言，從800G升級至1.6T絕不只是更換光收發器這麼簡單。業者還必須全面評估交換器容量、光纖佈線、連接器介面、設備耗電量、散熱管理，以及系統層級的相容性。目前仍有大量800G設備持續服役且尚在折舊期間，但新的AI伺服器建置案已將1.6T網路需求納入初期規劃之中。

  TrendForce將2026年與2027年定為供應商切入下一代供應鏈的關鍵時間窗口，屆時1.6T模組將邁向量產階段。

  ## ALT集團的雙軌產品策略

  ALT集團以兩條平行產品線因應這波世代交替。1.6T光收發器專為在不同AI伺服器配置與網路架構下皆能相容部署而設計；另一項產品則是模組化貨櫃式資料中心（CDC），旨在填補AI伺服器需求快速成長與傳統機房建設工期冗長之間的落差，ALT集團表示，CDC最快可在12週內完成部署。

  ALT集團執行長梁建國表示，公司認為頻寬與部署速度是AI基礎設施同時面臨的雙重制約。他接受《工商時報》採訪時指出：「我們的1.6T光收發器在提升資料傳輸能力的同時，也強調在不同應用環境下的部署相容性。CDC則透過模組化設計縮短資料中心建設時程，讓企業能夠在AI伺服器數量持續增加的情況下，更迅速地回應容量需求。」

  ## 產業展望

  報告結論指出，800G與1.6T光收發器在可預見的未來將長期並存，兩種規格不會立即相互取代。與此同時，AI伺服器部署的快速成長預計將推動資料中心建設朝向模組化與快速部署模式轉型。TrendForce的預測將2026年與2027年視為1.6T供應鏈廠商建立競爭優勢的關鍵時期——在此規格成為各大AI資料中心標準配備之前，各家業者仍有搶先卡位的空間。
