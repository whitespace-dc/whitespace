body_en: |
  Zayo Group has secured reserved manufacturing capacity from fiber maker Corning to cover a material portion of the fiber it needs through 2030, protecting a buildout of 15,000 route miles of new and overbuilt network infrastructure as artificial intelligence drives demand for high-capacity data center connectivity.

  The agreement, disclosed after an interview with Zayo Chief Network Officer Troy Lupe, covers part of the telco's broader funded expansion comprising 28 routes — 10 new builds and 18 overbuilds — totaling 15,000 new or overbuilt route miles and 20 million new or overbuilt fiber miles.

  Lupe said Zayo has not yet experienced material fiber shortages but is seeing early signals of supply pressure. "I think we've seen some concerning signals," Lupe said. "If you look at the amount of demand coming, the amount of historical demand needed, it takes time to build factories across the stack."

  Under the expanded agreement, the two companies will plan manufacturing capacity around Zayo's projected requirements. "We're reserving capacity with them," Lupe said. "We're planning with them so that they're opening the manufacturing lines and buying the supplies they need to deliver all of this fiber."

  Corning did not disclose financial terms. A company spokesperson said Corning maintains a policy of manufacturing close to customers to support reliable supply and service. The company operates two of the world's largest optical fiber and cable manufacturing facilities in North Carolina.

  ## Nvidia partnership anchors 8,000 route miles

  The largest single driver of Zayo's expansion is a separate agreement with Nvidia, which accounts for 24 of the 28 routes in the program and covers 8,000 of the 15,000 planned route miles. That portion includes six new routes, 10 overbuilds, and eight existing fiber routes, representing 8,000 new or overbuilt route miles and 15 million new or overbuilt fiber miles.

  Zayo delivered four Nvidia routes in 2026 and expects to complete four more before year-end, Lupe said. Remaining projects span various stages of engineering, environmental review, and permitting. Lupe said permitting and construction across multiple jurisdictions represent the largest remaining execution risks, with requirements varying by right-of-way and permitting authority.

  Jimmy Yu, vice president at research firm Dell'Oro Group, said the Nvidia agreement gives Zayo visibility into the demand the expansion is meant to serve. "I think Zayo is being proactive in building the fiber network that would be needed for the future AI infrastructure needs in the US," Yu said. "I'm sure the agreement with Nvidia for 8,000 of that 15,000 route miles gives the company added confidence in the future demand for capacity."

  ## Fiber emerges as a structural constraint

  Historically, Zayo ordered fiber as specific projects required it, even though long-haul construction involves years of planning. The Corning agreement extends supply visibility through at least 2030 and allows both companies to align factory production schedules with Zayo's projected requirements.

  Ron Westfall, vice president and practice lead for networking and infrastructure at HyperFrame Research, described high-count fiber optic cabling as having "rapidly emerged as a secondary, structural constraint" alongside power in AI infrastructure buildouts, driven by the multi-terabit connectivity required between distributed AI facilities and data centers.

  Corning has become an increasingly prominent supplier to AI network builders. The company has signed major optical supply agreements with Meta and Amazon and partnered with Nvidia to expand US optical manufacturing capacity. Its Optical Communications business grew 36 percent year over year in the first quarter of 2026.

  ## AI network design: volume and diversity over novelty

  AI traffic does not require a fundamentally different type of fiber, Yu said, but the scale and design of the infrastructure are changing. "The main difference is that the amount of fiber per conduit will need to be in the hundreds, if not thousands," Yu said.

  Lupe described AI demand as driven by machine-to-machine communication at volumes that exceed human-driven traffic. "It's machines talking to machines, and they're transferring a lot more data than humans talking to humans or humans talking to machines," he said.

  AI operators are also seeking greater physical route diversity to protect against outages, Lupe said. He described demand for what he called "triversity" and "quadversity" — three or four physically separate routes between the same network endpoints. New compute facilities are pulling Zayo into markets outside its historical footprint as those facilities follow the availability of power, creating demand for north-south and east-west long-haul routes where existing corridors have limited capacity or physical diversity.

  Yu said AI networks also require more direct routes to reduce latency between data centers and fewer add/drop points — locations where traffic is inserted into or removed from a network — along routes.

  ## Broader optical market reflects AI demand

  Optical transport equipment revenue grew 15 percent year over year in the second quarter of 2026, according to Dell'Oro. Data center interconnect revenue from wavelength-division multiplexing (WDM) systems and IP-over-WDM optics — technology that combines internet protocol traffic with high-capacity optical transmission — grew 45 percent in the same period. Yu said demand is strong between neighboring data centers and across long-distance backbones, particularly in North America.

  Yu said Zayo's announcement reflects a broader industry pattern. "As more new data centers get built, more new fiber routes will also be deployed," he said.

  ## Inference shift could reshape metro requirements

  Most current AI infrastructure investment is associated with model training, Lupe said. Zayo is beginning to see inference-related activity — the use of trained AI models to generate responses for end users — inside metropolitan areas. Lupe said personalized AI-generated content could eventually require more connections between end users and the compute facilities producing that content.

  Unlike conventional digital content, which can be cached close to users, AI-generated content may require live connections between users and wherever the relevant computing is located. "If that potentially starts to change, that could materially change the demands inside the metros," Lupe said.

  Zayo expects to deliver additional Nvidia project routes over the following several years as the broader 15,000-mile buildout continues toward its 2030 planning horizon.
body_zh: |
  Zayo集團已向光纖製造商康寧（Corning）預訂製造產能，鎖定至2030年所需光纖的相當大一部分，在人工智慧推動資料中心高容量連線需求的背景下，為其1.5萬路線英里新建及擴建網路基礎設施的計畫提供保障。

  上述協議係在Zayo首席網路長（CNO）特洛伊．盧普（Troy Lupe）接受媒體採訪後披露。協議涵蓋該電信公司更廣泛有資金支持之擴建計畫的部分需求，整體計畫共涉及28條路線，其中包括10條新建路線及18條擴建路線，合計達1.5萬路線英里及2,000萬光纖英里的新建或擴建規模。

  盧普表示，Zayo目前尚未遭遇重大光纖短缺問題，但已觀察到供應壓力的早期訊號。「我們確實看到了一些令人擔憂的訊號，」盧普說，「如果你看看即將到來的需求量、以及歷史上所需的需求量，就會明白在整個供應鏈中建廠都需要時間。」

  依據擴大後的協議，雙方將依Zayo的預測需求共同規劃製造產能。「我們正在與他們預訂產能，」盧普說，「我們與他們一起規劃，讓他們能夠開啟生產線、採購所需原料，以交付所有這些光纖。」

  康寧未披露財務條款。該公司發言人表示，康寧堅持在接近客戶的地點設廠的政策，以確保穩定的供應與服務。康寧在北卡羅來納州設有全球最大的兩座光纖光纜製造廠。

  ## 輝達合作協議支撐8,000路線英里建設

  Zayo擴建計畫的最大單一驅動力，是與輝達（Nvidia）另行簽署的合作協議。該協議涵蓋28條路線中的24條，涉及1.5萬規劃路線英里中的8,000英里，具體包含6條新建路線、10條擴建路線及8條現有光纖路線，合計達8,000路線英里及1,500萬光纖英里的新建或擴建規模。

  盧普表示，Zayo已於2026年完成4條輝達路線的交付，並預計在年底前再交付4條。其餘項目目前分別處於工程設計、環境審查及許可審批等不同階段。盧普指出，跨多個司法管轄區的許可審批及工程施工是目前最主要的執行風險，各地路權與許可主管機關的要求各不相同。

  研究機構戴爾奧羅集團（Dell'Oro Group）副總裁吉米．余（Jimmy Yu）表示，輝達協議使Zayo得以掌握此次擴建所服務的需求前景。「我認為Zayo正積極建設美國未來AI基礎設施所需的光纖網路，」余說，「與輝達就1.5萬路線英里中的8,000英里達成協議，無疑讓公司對未來容量需求更具信心。」

  ## 光纖成為結構性制約因素

  過去，Zayo慣於在特定項目有需求時才訂購光纖，即使長途網路建設往往需要多年規劃。此次與康寧的協議將供應能見度延伸至至少2030年，並讓雙方得以將工廠生產排程與Zayo的預測需求相互對齊。

  HyperFrame Research網路與基礎設施業務副總裁暨實務主持人朗．韋斯特福（Ron Westfall）表示，在AI基礎設施建設中，高芯數光纖光纜已「迅速成為繼電力之後的第二大結構性制約因素」，背後的驅動力正是分散式AI設施與資料中心之間所需的多太位元連線頻寬。

  康寧近年來已成為AI網路建設領域日益重要的供應商。該公司已分別與Meta及亞馬遜（Amazon）簽署大型光學供應協議，並與輝達合作擴大美國境內的光學製造產能。其光通訊事業部在2026年第一季的營收較去年同期成長36%。

  ## AI網路設計：規模與多樣性優先，而非技術創新

  余指出，AI流量並不需要截然不同類型的光纖，但基礎設施的規模與設計正在發生改變。「主要差異在於，每條管道所需的光纖芯數將達到數百芯，甚至數千芯，」余說。

  盧普形容，AI需求來自機器對機器之間的通訊，且傳輸量遠超人類產生的流量。「這是機器在和機器對話，它們所傳輸的資料量，遠比人與人之間，或人與機器之間的對話多得多，」他說。

  AI營運商也在尋求更高的實體路由多樣性，以防範網路中斷，盧普指出。他描述了業界對其所稱「三重多樣性」（triversity）與「四重多樣性」（quadversity）的需求——即在相同網路端點之間建立三條或四條實體分隔路線。新興運算設施正帶動Zayo進入其原有業務版圖以外的市場，因為這些設施的選址跟隨電力供應的可及性，進而在現有走廊容量或實體多樣性不足之處，催生對南北向與東西向長途路由的需求。余補充，AI網路還需要更直接的路由以降低資料中心之間的延遲，並減少路線沿途的「分插復用點」（add/drop points）——即流量被插入或從網路中擷取的位置。

  ## 更廣泛的光學市場反映AI需求

  根據戴爾奧羅集團的數據，2026年第二季光學傳輸設備營收較去年同期成長15%。資料中心互連（DCI）領域的波長分割多工（WDM）系統及IP-over-WDM光學設備——即結合網際網路協定流量與高容量光學傳輸的技術——在同期的營收成長幅度達45%。余表示，無論是相鄰資料中心之間，還是長距離骨幹網路上，需求均相當強勁，尤以北美地區為甚。

  余認為，Zayo的宣告反映了業界的普遍趨勢。「隨著越來越多新資料中心落成，也將有越來越多新光纖路線被部署，」他說。

  ## 推論業務轉型或將重塑都會網路需求

  盧普表示，目前大多數AI基礎設施投資仍集中在模型訓練領域。Zayo已開始在都會地區觀察到推論相關的業務活動——即利用已訓練完成的AI模型為終端用戶生成回應。盧普指出，個人化AI生成內容最終可能需要在終端用戶與生成該內容的運算設施之間建立更多連線。

  與可快取至用戶附近的傳統數位內容不同，AI生成的內容可能需要用戶與相關運算設施所在地之間建立即時連線。「若這一情況開始改變，都會地區內部的需求可能將出現重大變化，」盧普說。

  Zayo預計將在未來數年內陸續完成更多輝達項目路線的交付，整體1.5萬英里建設計畫將持續推進至2030年規劃期限。
