body_en: |
  Verizon has signed a deal with Google valued at more than $1 billion to provide dark fiber connectivity — dedicated, unlit fiber-optic cables leased to a customer who operates them independently — for Google's data centers, Verizon CEO Dan Schulman said Friday.

  Schulman disclosed the agreement on Verizon's post-earnings call, describing it as one of several large network infrastructure transactions the carrier is pursuing with hyperscale customers.

  ## Additional deals expected by year-end

  Beyond the Google contract, Schulman said Verizon expects to announce further deals before the end of 2026 that, taken together with the Google agreement, are projected to be worth multiple billions of dollars in revenue over the next several years. He did not identify the other counterparties or specify the services involved.

  ## What the deal signals

  The agreement positions Verizon as a supplier of the physical fiber infrastructure that large cloud and internet companies require to interconnect their data center campuses. Dark fiber arrangements give the buyer direct control over the transmission equipment and capacity, typically in exchange for a long-term lease with the fiber owner. For Verizon, such contracts convert its existing fiber assets into a recurring revenue stream tied to data center growth.

  舒爾曼在Verizon法說會後的電話會議上揭露這項合約，並將其定位為公司正積極與超大規模雲端業者洽談的多筆大型網路基礎設施交易之一。

  ## 年底前預計公告更多交易

  除了這份Google合約之外，舒爾曼表示，Verizon預計在2026年底前陸續公告其他交易，這些合約與Google合約合計，預估將在未來數年內為公司創造數十億美元的營收。他並未透露其他交易對象的身份，也未說明所涉及的具體服務項目。

  ## 這筆交易透露的意義

  這份合約確立了Verizon作為大型雲端與網路公司所需實體光纖基礎設施供應商的地位，專門服務需要互連資料中心園區的超大規模業者。暗光纖合約讓買方能直接掌控傳輸設備與頻寬容量，通常以長期租約的形式與光纖所有方簽訂。對Verizon而言，此類合約能將公司現有的光纖資產轉化為與資料中心成長緊密掛鉤的定期收入來源。
