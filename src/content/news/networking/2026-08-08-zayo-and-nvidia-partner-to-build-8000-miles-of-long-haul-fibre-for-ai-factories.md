body_en: |
  Zayo is building more than 8,000 miles of new long-haul fibre across the United States in a collaboration with Nvidia aimed at closing what the company describes as a widening gap between AI compute demand and available network capacity.

  The buildout will add six net-new long-haul routes across what Zayo calls emerging AI corridors — regions where AI infrastructure is growing beyond established data centre hubs — alongside overbuilds of existing fibre in ten high-demand markets. The routes are intended to connect AI factories (large, purpose-built facilities for training and running AI models), graphics processing unit (GPU) clusters, and hyperscaler deployments — large-scale cloud computing facilities — as they expand beyond traditional data centre locations.

  ## Why Zayo is routing beyond established hubs

  Steve Smith, chief executive of Zayo, said the company had invested significantly in modelling where AI-driven demand will emerge and was building ahead of that demand, rather than concentrating on existing routes. Smith said the work with Nvidia was intended to extend high-capacity connectivity to neoclouds — AI-focused cloud providers — and enterprise AI users, not only the largest hyperscalers.

  Vladimir Troy, vice president of engineering for AI infrastructure at Nvidia, said network capacity was becoming as critical to AI progress as compute itself.

  Dylan Patel, chief executive of research firm SemiAnalysis, said connectivity was becoming as significant a constraint on AI scaling as compute, with long-haul fibre needed to link distributed infrastructure in corridors where capacity is currently scarce.

  ## Crown Castle acquisition and prior buildouts

  The expansion follows Zayo's acquisition of Crown Castle's Fiber Solutions business, which Zayo said added 90,000 metro route miles and 40,000 on-net enterprise locations to its network, strengthening its density for AI inference workloads — the process of running trained AI models to generate outputs.

  Over the past 18 months, Zayo said its AI-focused build and overbuild projects have spanned more than 15,000 route miles across North America.

  ## Network connectivity as an AI scaling constraint

  The Zayo-Nvidia partnership reflects growing recognition within the AI infrastructure sector that long-haul network capacity — not just compute hardware — is a binding constraint on scaling AI workloads, a position expressed by both Nvidia's Troy and SemiAnalysis's Patel.

  Zayo has also launched an AI Infrastructure Blueprint, a framework intended to connect training, inference, and interconnection environments across the AI ecosystem. Smith said the blueprint is designed to serve neoclouds and enterprise AI users as well as the largest hyperscalers, as AI deployments spread into corridors where long-haul fibre capacity is currently limited.

  ## Zayo與輝達攜手，斥資打造逾1.3萬公里AI長途光纖幹線

  Zayo宣布與輝達（NVIDIA）展開合作，在美國境內新建逾8,000英里長途光纖骨幹網路。這項建設計畫旨在填補該公司所描述的AI運算需求與現有網路容量之間日益擴大的落差。

  此次擴建將新增六條長途骨幹路由，貫穿Zayo所稱的「新興AI走廊」——即AI基礎建設正迅速擴張、卻尚未形成成熟資料中心聚落的地區——並同步在十個高需求市場對既有光纖線路進行超量建設。這些路由的目標，是將AI工廠（專為訓練與執行AI模型而建造的大型專用設施）、GPU叢集，以及超大規模雲端部署設施串連起來，支援它們向傳統資料中心據點以外的區域拓展。

  ## Zayo為何選擇布局既有樞紐之外

  Zayo執行長史蒂夫·史密斯（Steve Smith）表示，公司已投入大量資源建立預測模型，掌握AI需求的潛在浮現地點，並選擇超前於需求進行建設，而非僅集中在現有熱門路由上。史密斯指出，與輝達的合作旨在將高容量網路連線延伸至新雲端業者（neocloud）——即以AI為核心業務的雲端服務供應商——以及企業AI用戶，而不僅限於規模最大的超大規模業者。

  輝達AI基礎建設工程副總裁弗拉基米爾·特洛伊（Vladimir Troy）表示，網路容量對AI進展的重要性，已與運算力本身不相上下。

  研究機構SemiAnalysis執行長狄倫·帕特爾（Dylan Patel）則指出，連線能力對AI規模化的制約程度，已不亞於運算力本身，而長途光纖在當前容量匱乏的走廊地帶，正是串接分散式基礎建設的關鍵。

  ## 收購Crown Castle業務與既有建設成果

  此次擴建緊接在Zayo收購Crown Castle光纖解決方案（Fiber Solutions）業務之後。Zayo表示，這項收購為其網路新增了9萬英里的都會路由里程，以及4萬個直接連線的企業據點，進一步強化了其在AI推論工作負載方面的網路密度。所謂AI推論，係指執行已完成訓練的AI模型以產生輸出結果的運算過程。

  Zayo表示，過去18個月來，其以AI為導向的新建與超量建設專案，已橫跨北美超過15,000條路由英里。

  ## 網路連線能力成為AI規模化的關鍵瓶頸

  Zayo與輝達的合作，反映出AI基礎建設產業對長途網路容量的日益重視——其所扮演的角色不亞於運算硬體，已成為AI工作負載規模化的核心制約因素。這一觀點同樣獲得輝達的特洛伊與SemiAnalysis的帕特爾所認同。

  Zayo同時推出「AI基礎建設藍圖」（AI Infrastructure Blueprint），這是一套旨在將訓練、推論與互聯環境整合串通、貫穿整個AI生態系的建設框架。史密斯表示，該藍圖的設計涵蓋新雲端業者與企業AI用戶，不只服務最大型的超大規模業者，尤其針對長途光纖容量目前仍相對匱乏的新興走廊地區。
