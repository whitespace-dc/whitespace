body_en: |
  Philippine property developers that fail to build fiber-optic connectivity into projects from the start risk compounding an already severe vacancy problem, analysts at Colliers Philippines and Savills Philippines said Wednesday, as Globe Telecom pushes for national building standards to mandate the infrastructure.

  Metro Manila currently carries an office vacancy rate of nearly 20% and more than 30,000 unsold ready-for-occupancy condominium units, according to Colliers Philippines Director for Research Joey Roi Bondoc. Against that backdrop, he said, connectivity has moved from a differentiator to a baseline requirement.

  "If you want to actively tap demand in the market, you really have to be fiber-ready," Bondoc said by phone. "It's part of the checklist of a lot of companies now."

  ## Connectivity as a leasing prerequisite

  Hybrid work arrangements have made reliable, high-speed internet a standard expectation for both office tenants and residential buyers, Bondoc said. Business process outsourcing companies — a major driver of office demand in the Philippines — typically require redundant internet connections, meaning access to more than one provider simultaneously. Buildings that cannot support multiple fiber carriers may be unable to meet that requirement and, by extension, unable to close leases with that tenant segment.

  Savills Philippines Research Manager Kathrina Angelica Bautista said offices with fiber-ready infrastructure are more attractive because tenants can begin operations more quickly after move-in. She noted that older properties built before fiber internet became widespread are often constrained by outdated cabling and utility risers with insufficient capacity.

  ## Cost of retrofitting versus building in

  Bautista said installing conduits, vertical cable runs known as risers, and dedicated telecommunications rooms during original construction is materially less expensive than adding them after a building is complete. Retrofitting finished buildings can require structural modifications and disrupt existing occupants, she said in a written comment.

  Bondoc framed the calculus in terms of the existing inventory overhang. Developers who do not meet connectivity standards face the prospect of adding to an already large pool of vacant or unsold space. "You really have to be proactive," he said.

  ## Globe's push for regulatory change

  The analysts' remarks follow public statements by Globe Telecom, one of the Philippines' two dominant carriers, calling for closer coordination between telecoms and property developers at the project design stage. Globe President and Chief Executive Officer Carl R. Cruz said early-stage coordination can prevent costly redesigns and surface infrastructure requirements before construction begins.

  Globe has also proposed that fiber-ready building standards and in-building connectivity requirements be incorporated into the ongoing review of the National Building Code of the Philippines, which governs construction standards nationwide. Embedding such requirements in the code would make connectivity infrastructure a legal construction standard rather than a market-driven option.

  ## What this means for the sector

  The convergence of analyst warnings and a telecom operator's regulatory proposal signals that fiber infrastructure is becoming a material factor in the Philippines' commercial and residential real estate market. Developers entering a market with elevated supply and cautious demand face a narrowing margin for error on tenant requirements. Buildings that cannot support multi-provider fiber connectivity may find their competitive position deteriorating further as connectivity expectations continue to rise.

  Globe's National Building Code proposal, if adopted, would shift the decision from individual developer judgment to a mandatory standard applied across all new construction in the country.
body_zh: |
  菲律賓開發商若未能在建案初期就導入光纖連網基礎設施，恐將使原本已相當嚴峻的空置問題更加惡化。高力國際（Colliers Philippines）與第一太平戴維斯（Savills Philippines）菲律賓部門的分析師於週三表示，與此同時，環球電信（Globe Telecom）也積極推動將此類基礎設施標準納入國家建築規範之中。

  根據高力國際菲律賓研究部總監喬伊·羅伊·邦多克（Joey Roi Bondoc）提供的數據，馬尼拉都會區目前辦公室空置率接近20%，逾3萬戶可即刻入住的共管公寓至今仍未售出。在此背景下，邦多克指出，網路連線能力已從錦上添花的加分項目，轉變為市場進入的基本門檻。

  「如果你想主動挖掘市場需求，就真的必須具備光纖就緒條件，」邦多克透過電話表示，「這已經是許多企業選房清單上的標準項目。」

  ## 光纖連網成租賃必備條件

  邦多克說，混合辦公模式的普及，使穩定高速的網路連線成為辦公室租戶與住宅買家的基本期待。企業流程外包公司（BPO）是菲律賓辦公室需求的主要推動力，這類業者通常要求具備備援網路連線，亦即能同時接入多家電信業者的服務。無法支援多家光纖業者同時進駐的大樓，將難以滿足此一需求，進而無從與這類租戶完成簽約。

  第一太平戴維斯菲律賓研究部經理卡崔娜·安潔莉卡·包蒂斯塔（Kathrina Angelica Bautista）表示，已具備光纖就緒基礎設施的辦公室對租戶更具吸引力，因為租戶在進駐後能更快速展開營運。她指出，在光纖網路尚未普及的年代興建的老舊物業，往往受限於過時的配線系統，以及容量不足的垂直線路管道。

  ## 事後改裝與一次到位的成本比較

  包蒂斯塔表示，在原始建設階段就完成管道配置、垂直線路幹管（riser）鋪設，以及專用電信機房的施工，費用遠低於建築完工後才進行的改裝工程。她在書面回覆中指出，對已完工大樓進行光纖改裝，往往需要結構修繕，並對現有住戶造成干擾。

  邦多克則從現有庫存積壓的角度評估這項抉擇。未能符合連網標準的開發商，將面臨進一步推高空置率與滯銷數量的風險。「你真的必須主動超前部署，」他說。

  ## 環球電信推動法規修訂

  上述分析師的表態，呼應了菲律賓兩大主要電信業者之一環球電信的公開立場。環球電信呼籲電信業者與房地產開發商在建案設計階段展開更緊密的協作。環球電信總裁兼執行長卡爾·R·克魯茲（Carl R. Cruz）表示，在早期階段就啟動協調，可以避免日後代價高昂的設計變更，並在施工開始前及時釐清基礎設施需求。

  環球電信也提議，將光纖就緒建築標準與室內連網要求，納入目前正在進行審查的《菲律賓國家建築法規》之中。該法規規範全國建築施工標準。若相關要求獲得採納，連網基礎設施將從市場自由選擇的項目，轉變為具有法律約束力的建築標準。

  ## 對產業的意義

  分析師警告與電信業者推動法規修訂的訴求同步浮現，顯示光纖基礎設施正逐漸成為菲律賓商辦與住宅房地產市場的重要考量因素。在供給過剩、需求審慎的市場環境中，開發商面對租戶需求時所能容忍的誤差空間愈來愈窄。無法支援多家業者光纖連網的大樓，隨著連網期待持續提升，恐將面臨競爭地位進一步惡化的處境。

  環球電信提出的《國家建築法規》修訂案一旦獲得採納，相關決策將從各開發商的個別判斷，轉變為適用於全國所有新建工程的強制標準。
