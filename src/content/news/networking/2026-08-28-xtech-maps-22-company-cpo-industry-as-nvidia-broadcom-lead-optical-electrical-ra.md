body_en: |
  XTECH, a Japanese technology publication, has published an industry landscape analysis mapping at least 22 companies across the co-packaged optics supply chain, placing NVIDIA and Broadcom at the front of a sector now extending from optical components into AI accelerators, network switches, and data center systems, according to reporting by TechNews.

  Co-packaged optics (CPO) is a hardware approach that integrates optical communication components with electronic chips across multiple system layers, including AI accelerators, network switches, optical engines, chip packaging, and data center infrastructure, rather than operating as a single optical component category. The analysis and an accompanying diagram, also cited and extended by a second Japanese media outlet, reflect the growing breadth of competitive activity as generative AI workloads drive demand for higher-capacity data center interconnects.

  ## Front-runners and new entrants

  NVIDIA and Broadcom hold the leading positions in the XTECH mapping, each approaching CPO from the AI computing and data center demand side. XTECH newly added NTT, Japan's national telecom operator, to the top tier of the diagram alongside the two U.S. chipmakers. The analysis notes that NTT's orientation comes from the telecom network and optical-electrical architecture side, and that NTT's commercialization pace in CPO is slower than that of its American counterparts. The analysis characterizes NTT's long-term positioning in optical technology and network architecture as a potentially significant asset for Japan in the competition.

  XTECH also noted four acquisitions within the CPO sector, citing them as a signal of overheating.

  ## Supply chain layers

  The XTECH mapping organizes the supply chain into distinct functional layers. XTECH identifies TSMC, GlobalFoundries, and Tower Semiconductor as the wafer foundries handling integration of optical components with electronic chips.

  In laser light sources, a core CPO input, the landscape includes Coherent and Lumentum from the United States, and Furukawa Electric and Sumitomo Electric from Japan. XTECH newly added Corning and AGC (formerly Asahi Glass) to the diagram in the waveguide segment, which involves glass materials. CPO modules assembled by NVIDIA and Broadcom flow to large U.S. data center operators: AWS, Microsoft, and Meta.

  A second Japanese media report cited by TechNews extends the participant list to include Marvell, AMD, Samsung, MediaTek, Mitsubishi, IBM, Intel, and Fujitsu, categorizing them as companies working on how to integrate optical engines into switches, GPUs, or AI accelerator interconnects. That report also cites ASE, Amkor, Shinko Electric Industries, and JCET as advanced packaging suppliers.

  ## Japan's component position

  The XTECH analysis identifies a structural division between U.S. and Japanese participation. U.S. companies hold advantages in AI chips, network chips, and system design. Japanese companies concentrate in laser light sources, optical components, and advanced materials, areas where Japan has built deep technical expertise, the analysis notes. XTECH titled one of its articles on the topic to highlight Japan's current position in laser sources, pointing to Furukawa Electric and Sumitomo Electric as examples.

  The analysis suggests Japanese firms may enter the global CPO market through supply chain positions in components and materials even without leading in overall system architecture.

  ## What the analysis signals

  XTECH's mapping of a 22-company baseline, combined with noting four acquisitions as a sign of sector overheating, indicates CPO is moving from a niche photonics technology toward a mainstream infrastructure layer for AI data centers. The expansion of CPO's competitive scope to include AI accelerators, network switches, optical engines, and packaging means the field now draws in semiconductor, materials, and systems companies not previously central to optical networking.

  The second Japanese media report's expanded roster, adding Marvell, AMD, Samsung, MediaTek, Mitsubishi, IBM, Intel, Fujitsu, and advanced packaging firms alongside the XTECH baseline, indicates the CPO participant map continues to grow as the technology moves toward commercial deployment at scale.
body_zh: |
  日本科技媒體 XTECH 發布一份產業全景分析，點名至少22家業者分布於共封裝光學（CPO）供應鏈各環節，並將輝達（NVIDIA）與博通（Broadcom）列為產業前列，所覆蓋的範疇已從光學元件延伸至 AI 加速器、網路交換器及資料中心系統。此份分析據 TechNews 報導。

  共封裝光學（CPO）是一種硬體整合方式，將光通訊元件與電子晶片整合於多個系統層次之中，包括 AI 加速器、網路交換器、光學引擎、晶片封裝及資料中心基礎設施，而非單純作為一個獨立的光學元件類別。這份分析及其附帶的示意圖，亦獲第二家日本媒體引用並加以延伸，反映出生成式 AI 工作負載帶動更高容量資料中心互連需求之際，業界競爭活動的廣度持續擴展。

  ## 領跑者與新進業者

  在 XTECH 的產業圖中，輝達與博通占據領先地位，兩家公司均從 AI 運算與資料中心需求端切入 CPO 市場。XTECH 此次將日本國家電信業者 NTT 新列入頂層，與這兩家美國晶片大廠並列。分析指出，NTT 的切入角度來自電信網路與光電架構面，其在 CPO 領域的商業化步調慢於美國同業。該分析認為，NTT 在光學技術與網路架構方面的長期布局，可能成為日本在這場競賽中不可忽視的潛在資產。

  XTECH 亦點出 CPO 領域內已發生四起併購案，並將此視為市場過熱的訊號。

  ## 供應鏈各層

  XTECH 的圖譜將供應鏈組織為各具功能的分層結構。在晶圓代工方面，XTECH 點名台積電（TSMC）、格芯（GlobalFoundries）及 Tower 半導體（Tower Semiconductor），負責光學元件與電子晶片的整合製造。

  在 CPO 的核心投入要素——雷射光源方面，產業版圖涵蓋來自美國的 Coherent 與 Lumentum，以及來自日本的古河電工（Furukawa Electric）與住友電工（Sumitomo Electric）。在波導（waveguide）環節——即涉及玻璃材料的部分，XTECH 此次新增了康寧（Corning）與 AGC（原旭硝子）。由輝達與博通組裝完成的 CPO 模組，流向美國大型資料中心業者：AWS、微軟（Microsoft）及 Meta。

  TechNews 所引述的另一份日本媒體報告，則將參與者名單進一步延伸，納入邁威爾（Marvell）、AMD、三星（Samsung）、聯發科（MediaTek）、三菱（Mitsubishi）、IBM、英特爾（Intel）及富士通（Fujitsu），將其歸類為研究如何將光學引擎整合至交換器、GPU 或 AI 加速器互連的業者。該報告亦將日月光（ASE）、安靠（Amkor）、新光電工（Shinko Electric Industries）及長電科技（JCET）列為先進封裝供應商。

  ## 日本的元件定位

  XTECH 的分析點出美日兩國參與者之間的結構性分工。美國企業在 AI 晶片、網路晶片及系統設計方面具有優勢；日本企業則集中於雷射光源、光學元件及先進材料——這些領域正是日本長期深耕、積累深厚技術專業之處。XTECH 以日本目前在雷射光源的定位為題，撰寫其中一篇相關文章，特別點名古河電工與住友電工作為典型案例。

  分析認為，即便日本廠商無法在整體系統架構上取得領先，仍可透過在元件與材料端的供應鏈定位，切入全球 CPO 市場。

  ## 分析所傳遞的訊號

  XTECH 以22家業者為基準繪製產業圖譜，並將四起併購視為產業過熱訊號，顯示 CPO 正從一項小眾光子技術，邁向 AI 資料中心的主流基礎設施層。CPO 競爭版圖已擴及 AI 加速器、網路交換器、光學引擎與封裝，意味著這個領域如今已吸引原本並非以光學網路為核心業務的半導體、材料及系統業者紛紛投入。

  另一份日本媒體報告進一步擴充名單，在 XTECH 基準之上新增邁威爾、AMD、三星、聯發科、三菱、IBM、英特爾、富士通及先進封裝業者，顯示隨著 CPO 技術朝大規模商業部署邁進，參與者版圖仍持續擴張之中。
