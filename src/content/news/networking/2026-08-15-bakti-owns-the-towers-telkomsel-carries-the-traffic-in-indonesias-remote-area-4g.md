  ## Bakti Owns the Towers, Telkomsel Carries the Traffic in Indonesia's Remote-Area 4G Push

  Indonesia's government telecommunications body Bakti and mobile operator PT Telekomunikasi Selular (Telkomsel) have formalized a split-role model for delivering 4G internet to the country's most remote communities, with Bakti building and owning physical infrastructure while Telkomsel routes traffic across its national backbone — a division that now covers approximately 6,747 villages, officials said at the Bakti Media Connect 2026 event in Jakarta on Wednesday.

  Bakti — formally Badan Aksesibilitas Telekomunikasi dan Informasi, the government agency responsible for telecommunications access — has taken full ownership of passive and active site infrastructure in what Indonesia officially classifies as 3T areas (tertinggal, terdepan, dan terluar: underdeveloped, frontier, and outer regions). That covers towers, on-site equipment, and base transceiver stations, or BTS, the radio hardware that connects handsets to a mobile network. Telkomsel's role is to interconnect those Bakti sites with its own core network and carry the resulting traffic to its central switching infrastructure in Java.

  "Most of the sites here, in Parigi Moutong, are indeed Bakti sites," Deni Kurniawan, Telkomsel's VP of Network Strategic Collaboration and Settlement, said at the event. In Parigi Moutong Regency, Central Sulawesi, approximately 81 Bakti sites operate under this arrangement. In East Nusa Tenggara (NTT) province — which includes Flores — the network is a mixed deployment of Bakti-owned sites and infrastructure built commercially by operators.

  ## How the arrangement works

  Under the current model, Bakti procures and operates the physical site, then contracts operators through a tender process to provide connectivity services at those locations. Telkomsel has won a large share of those tenders, according to Deni. Once connected, traffic originating at a remote Bakti point-of-interest node travels across Telkomsel's transport network to the company's core network on Java.

  This model differs from earlier arrangements, in which the operator relationship with 3T areas predated Bakti's establishment. After Bakti was created, cooperation was restructured into the current service-contract framework.

  ## Scale and program scope

  Bakti Director Fadhilah Mathar said the agency relies on commercial operators to deliver 4G access, with Telkomsel serving as the dominant provider. "There are around 6,747 locations or villages using that service," Fadhilah said, referring to Telkomsel's footprint within the program. She added that the Palapa Ring — Indonesia's national fiber-optic backbone — reaches 131 locations under the program, with around 57 active service participants.

  Palapa Ring is the government-built submarine and terrestrial fiber network intended to link Indonesia's archipelago of more than 17,000 islands. The gap between 131 Palapa Ring locations and 57 active service players indicates that not all ring-connected points are currently served by an active operator.

  ## Commercial limits shape coverage decisions

  Telkomsel's participation in the program is bounded by commercial viability. Deni said the company is evaluating fiber piloting in select areas but that any rollout would depend on field conditions and both technical and business feasibility. He was direct about the constraint: "At the end of the day, we are a commercial company, so the numbers have to work out first. If they don't, we will face difficulties too — our stakeholders will. But I am confident our board also sees how important connectivity is."

  The comment reflects a structural tension in remote-area coverage globally: operators require sufficient subscriber density to justify infrastructure investment, while the communities most in need of connectivity are typically the least commercially attractive. The Bakti model addresses this by having the government absorb site capital expenditure, reducing the financial threshold operators must clear to participate.

  ## What this means for Indonesia's connectivity gap

  The Bakti-Telkomsel framework functions as a public-private subsidy mechanism: government funds infrastructure in markets that cannot support commercial deployment, and operators provide the network operations and backhaul that connect those sites to the national internet. The 6,747-village figure represents the current extent of that reach under Telkomsel's portion of the program.

  Fadhilah said demand from communities for expanded coverage continues to exceed current supply, suggesting further tender rounds and site deployments are expected as Bakti's program progresses.

  ## Bakti建塔、Telkomsel傳輸：印尼偏鄉4G推進

  印尼政府電信機構Bakti與電信業者PT Telekomunikasi Selular（Telkomsel），已就向偏遠社區提供4G網路一事正式確立分工合作模式——Bakti負責建設與持有實體基礎設施，Telkomsel則透過其全國骨幹網路傳輸流量。官員於週三在雅加達舉行的「Bakti Media Connect 2026」活動中宣布，這套分工模式目前已涵蓋約6,747個村落。

  Bakti，全名Badan Aksesibilitas Telekomunikasi dan Informasi，是印尼負責電信普及的政府機構，已全面接管印尼官方所稱「3T地區」（tertinggal、terdepan、terluar，即落後、邊境及外島地區）的被動式與主動式基地設備所有權。涵蓋項目包括鐵塔、現場設備，以及基地收發站（BTS）——即負責連結手機與行動網路的無線電硬體。Telkomsel的角色則是將Bakti各站點接入自身核心網路，並將產生的流量傳輸至位於爪哇島的中央交換基礎設施。

  Telkomsel網路策略合作與結算副總裁Deni Kurniawan在活動中表示：「這裡（帕里吉莫烏東）的大多數站點，確實都是Bakti的站點。」在中蘇拉威西省帕里吉莫烏東縣，約有81個Bakti站點在此框架下運作。在包括弗洛勒斯島在內的東努沙登加拉省（NTT），網路則為Bakti自建站點與業者自行商業部署基礎設施的混合建置。

  ## 合作安排的運作方式

  依據目前模式，Bakti負責採購並運營實體站點，再透過公開招標程序委託業者在當地提供連線服務。Deni表示，Telkomsel已在多項標案中勝出，取得相當大的份額。一旦完成接通，偏遠地區Bakti興趣點節點所產生的流量，即透過Telkomsel的傳輸網路送往該公司位於爪哇的核心網路。

  此模式與早期安排有所不同。過去業者與3T地區的合作關係早於Bakti的成立；Bakti設立後，雙方合作已重組為現行的服務契約框架。

  ## 規模與計畫範疇

  Bakti局長Fadhilah Mathar表示，該機構倚重商業業者提供4G接取服務，其中Telkomsel為主要供應商。「目前使用該服務的地點或村落約有6,747個，」Fadhilah說，此數字指的是Telkomsel在計畫中的覆蓋足跡。她並補充，印尼國家光纖骨幹網路「帕拉帕環」（Palapa Ring）在計畫中已連接131個地點，目前約有57個活躍服務參與者。

  帕拉帕環是印尼政府興建的海底與陸上光纖網路，旨在串聯這個擁有逾1萬7,000座島嶼的群島國家。131個帕拉帕環連接點與57個活躍服務業者之間的落差，說明並非所有已接入骨幹環的節點，目前都有業者在當地提供服務。

  ## 商業考量限制覆蓋決策

  Telkomsel參與計畫的範疇，受商業可行性所制約。Deni表示，公司正在特定地區評估光纖試點的可能性，但任何推展都將取決於現場條件及技術與商業可行性。他對此限制直言不諱：「說到底，我們是一家商業公司，數字必須站得住腳。若不行，我們也將面臨困難——我們的利害關係人也是。但我相信，我們的董事會同樣看到了連線的重要性。」

  這番話折射出全球偏遠地區網路覆蓋所面臨的結構性矛盾：業者需要足夠的用戶密度，才能合理化基礎設施投資；而最需要網路的社區，往往在商業上最缺乏吸引力。Bakti模式的解方是由政府承擔站點資本支出，藉此降低業者參與所需跨越的財務門檻。

  ## 對印尼數位落差的意義

  Bakti與Telkomsel的合作框架，實質上是一套公私合作補貼機制：政府出資在無法支撐商業部署的市場建設基礎設施，業者則提供網路營運與回程傳輸，將這些站點接入全國網際網路。6,747個村落的數字，代表Telkomsel在計畫中目前所實現的覆蓋規模。

  Fadhilah表示，各社區對擴大覆蓋的需求持續超出現有供給，顯示隨著Bakti計畫推進，預計將有更多招標回合與站點部署陸續展開。
