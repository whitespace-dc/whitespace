body_en: |
  Telin, BW Digital, and Nongsa Digital Park completed the landing of the Nongsa Changi Cable (NCC), a submarine cable linking Batam, Indonesia to Singapore, at Nongsa Digital Park on July 20, 2026, establishing a 50-kilometer connection with capacity exceeding 1.6 petabits per second and latency below 2 milliseconds.

  Indonesia's Coordinating Minister for Economic Affairs Airlangga Hartarto and Singapore's Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Trade and Industry Gan Kim Yong attended the landing ceremony. Also present were Indonesia's Ambassador to Singapore Hotmangaraja Panjaitan, Riau Islands Deputy Governor Nyanyang Haris Pratamura, Danantara Indonesia Managing Director for Business-2 Setyanto Hantoro, and senior executives from Telkom, Telin, BW Digital, and Nongsa Digital Park.

  Airlangga described NCC as "one of the fastest and closest digital connections in Southeast Asia between Indonesia and Singapore" and said it would support advanced cloud computing, AI inference, high-frequency financial transactions, and next-generation digital services.

  ## Technical specifications

  NCC spans approximately 50 kilometers and carries 24 fiber pairs. Its capacity exceeds 1.6 petabits per second (1,600 terabits per second), with end-to-end latency below 2 milliseconds, according to figures Airlangga cited at the ceremony.

  ## NCC in TelkomGroup's cable expansion

  NCC is one of two Batam-to-Singapore submarine cables TelkomGroup is developing. The second is the Indonesia Singapore Cable System (INSICA). Both form part of what TelkomGroup calls the Indonesia Cable Express (ICE) ecosystem.

  Telkom President Director Dian Siswarini said the cable "not only strengthens Indonesia's digital connectivity and resilience, but also provides greater path diversity and connectivity for Singapore as a regional digital hub, creating benefits for both countries." Director for Wholesale and International Service Budi Satria Dharma Purba said TelkomGroup plans to continue expanding the ICE ecosystem through additional phases to address demand from global customers and digital industry players across Southeast Asia.

  ## BW Digital's Batam infrastructure ecosystem

  BW Digital CEO Ludovic Hutier described NCC as part of BW Digital's integrated infrastructure ecosystem in Batam, which also includes the Citra Connect terrestrial fiber network and the NDP1 data center campus at Nongsa Digital Park. Hutier cited NDP1's capacity at 144 megawatts (MW); a separate government readout from the same event placed NDP1's IT capacity at 120 MW. The two sources did not clarify whether the figures refer to different measures of the same facility.

  Hutier said the platform is designed to serve growing regional demand for cloud services, AI, and data center capacity.

  The government readout from the ceremony cited additional hyperscale investment activity in Batam: DayOne and INA are developing a new hyperscale campus with contracted power supply on a path toward 450 MW, and Oracle has designated Batam as its Indonesia North Cloud Region.

  ## Political and diplomatic context

  The cable's landing follows a Leaders' Retreat between Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto and Singapore's Prime Minister in early July 2026, making NCC one of the concrete outcomes of that meeting, according to Indonesia's economic coordination ministry. The project is also tied to the Indonesia-Singapore 6 Working Group (6WG), a bilateral cooperation framework covering the Batam-Bintan-Karimun (BBK) region that has been working to strengthen connectivity and digital investment in that area.

  ## Indonesia's digital market position

  Airlangga cited figures on Indonesia's scale as a digital market: 235.26 million internet users, a penetration rate of 81.72 percent, and 99.5 million fixed broadband subscribers. He placed Indonesia as the fourth-largest potential AI market in Asia after China, India, and Japan, with an AI market valued at $70 billion, representing about 6.4 percent of Asia's total AI market.

  Danantara's Setyanto said digital infrastructure of this type acts as a catalyst for innovation and competitiveness and that NCC should produce a wider economic multiplier effect across Batam and Indonesia.

  ## Regulatory and trade backdrop

  Airlangga cited two policy tracks he said strengthen the investment climate for digital infrastructure: Indonesia entered Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership (CPTPP) accession discussions on June 26, 2026, and an OECD accession process is running concurrently. The government is also targeting expansion of renewable and mixed-energy capacity to more than 100 gigawatts (GW) within this decade to support data center growth.

  TelkomGroup said it will continue expanding the ICE cable ecosystem through subsequent phases to serve growing regional demand for cloud, AI, hyperscaler connectivity, and data center interconnection.
body_zh: |
  Telin、BW Digital與農薩數位園區於2026年7月20日，在印尼峇淡島農薩數位園區完成農薩樟宜電纜（Nongsa Changi Cable，NCC）登陸作業，建立一條長達50公里、連接印尼峇淡島至新加坡的海底電纜，容量超過每秒1.6拍位元，端對端延遲低於2毫秒。

  印尼統籌經濟事務部長艾爾朗加・哈爾塔托（Airlangga Hartarto）與新加坡副總理兼貿易及工業部長顏金勇（Gan Kim Yong）出席登陸典禮。同場出席者還包括印尼駐新加坡大使霍特曼加拉賈・潘賈伊坦（Hotmangaraja Panjaitan）、廖內群島副省長尼揚揚・哈里斯・普拉塔穆拉（Nyanyang Haris Pratamura）、丹安塔拉印尼商業事務二部常務董事塞提揚托・漢托羅（Setyanto Hantoro），以及Telkom、Telin、BW Digital與農薩數位園區的高階主管。

  艾爾朗加形容NCC是「東南亞印尼與新加坡之間速度最快、距離最近的數位連線之一」，並表示這條電纜將支援進階雲端運算、AI推論、高頻金融交易及次世代數位服務的發展。

  ## 技術規格

  NCC全長約50公里，配備24對光纖，容量超過每秒1.6拍位元（即每秒1,600兆位元），端對端延遲低於2毫秒，以上數據均由艾爾朗加於典禮上引述公布。

  ## NCC在TelkomGroup海纜擴張計畫中的定位

  NCC是TelkomGroup正在建設的兩條峇淡島至新加坡海底電纜之一，另一條為印尼新加坡電纜系統（Indonesia Singapore Cable System，INSICA）。兩條電纜均屬TelkomGroup所稱「印尼電纜快線」（Indonesia Cable Express，ICE）生態系的一部分。

  Telkom總裁暨執行長蒂安・西斯瓦里尼（Dian Siswarini）表示，這條電纜「不僅強化了印尼的數位連結能力與韌性，同時也為新加坡作為區域數位樞紐提供更完整的路徑多元化與連線能力，讓兩國共同受益。」批發暨國際服務董事布迪・薩特里亞・達爾馬・普爾巴（Budi Satria Dharma Purba）則表示，TelkomGroup計畫持續透過後續階段擴展ICE生態系，以因應來自全球客戶及東南亞數位產業業者的需求。

  ## BW Digital在峇淡島的基礎設施生態系

  BW Digital執行長盧多維克・于蒂埃（Ludovic Hutier）表示，NCC是BW Digital在峇淡島整合型基礎設施生態系的一部分，該生態系還涵蓋Citra Connect陸地光纖網路，以及位於農薩數位園區的NDP1資料中心園區。于蒂埃引述NDP1容量為144 MW；而同場典禮的另一份政府新聞稿則將NDP1的IT容量標示為120 MW，兩份資料並未說明這兩項數字是否指同一設施的不同計量方式。

  于蒂埃表示，這套平台的設計旨在服務區域對雲端服務、AI及資料中心容量日益增長的需求。

  典禮的政府新聞稿另引述了峇淡島的超大規模投資動態：DayOne與INA正在開發一座新的超大規模園區，已簽約電力供應並朝450 MW目標推進；甲骨文（Oracle）亦已將峇淡島指定為其印尼北部雲端區域（Indonesia North Cloud Region）。

  ## 政治與外交背景

  此次電纜登陸作業，發生於印尼總統普拉伯沃・蘇比安托（Prabowo Subianto）與新加坡總理在2026年7月初舉行領導人閉門會議後不久，印尼統籌經濟事務部指出，NCC是該次會議的具體成果之一。這項計畫同時與印尼—新加坡第六工作小組（Indonesia-Singapore 6 Working Group，6WG）掛鉤，後者是涵蓋峇淡—賓旦—卡里蒙（Batam-Bintan-Karimun，BBK）地區的雙邊合作框架，長期致力於強化該地區的連結能力與數位投資。

  ## 印尼數位市場定位

  艾爾朗加引述了印尼數位市場的規模數據：網路用戶達2億3,526萬人，普及率為81.72%，固定寬頻用戶達9,950萬人。他並指出，印尼是亞洲繼中國、印度、日本之後第四大潛力AI市場，AI市場規模達700億美元，約占亞洲AI市場總量的6.4%。

  丹安塔拉的塞提揚托表示，此類數位基礎設施是創新與競爭力的催化劑，NCC預計將為峇淡島及整個印尼帶來更廣泛的經濟乘數效應。

  ## 法規與貿易背景

  艾爾朗加提及兩項他認為有助於改善數位基礎設施投資環境的政策動向：印尼於2026年6月26日展開《跨太平洋夥伴全面進步協定》（CPTPP）入會討論，並同步推進加入經濟合作暨發展組織（OECD）的程序。印尼政府亦以本十年內將再生能源與混合能源裝置容量擴充至超過100吉瓦（GW）為目標，以支撐資料中心的持續發展。

  TelkomGroup表示，將持續透過後續階段擴展ICE海纜生態系，以服務雲端、AI、超大規模業者連線及資料中心互連等方面日益增長的區域需求。
