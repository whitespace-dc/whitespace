body_en: |
  Amphenol demonstrated its XPO (eXtra-dense Pluggable Optics) interconnect architecture at COMPUTEX 2026, claiming the platform delivers eight times the bandwidth and four times the front-panel density of the industry-standard OSFP form factor. The company said the performance advantage directly addresses transmission bottlenecks in high-density AI computing environments.

  XPO is designed to work alongside Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) and Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO) architectures. Amphenol said the combination sets a new benchmark for next-generation AI data centers, providing high bandwidth, low latency, and energy efficiency for hyperscale clusters.

  ## Thermal load and silicon photonics

  XPO integrates a liquid cooling cold plate. Amphenol said a single module can manage thermal loads of up to 400 watts, addressing what the company described as critical challenges around power consumption, bandwidth, and high-density installation facing AI servers and switches.

  The architecture is built on Amphenol's existing optical technology and incorporates silicon photonics, the company said. Amphenol said XPO can reduce the need for customers to substantially overhaul existing technology and supply chains, a factor it positioned as an accelerant for cloud service providers and system integrators adopting next-generation AI infrastructure.

  ## Fiber connector targets hyperscale maintenance burden

  Also on show at COMPUTEX 2026 was FastSelfClean, a fiber connector developed by Amphenol's CommScope unit. The company said the product attracted significant attention from global cloud providers during the exhibition.

  Amphenol said that as fiber density increases in hyperscale AI environments, even minor dust, contamination, or fiber end-face misalignment can cause signal loss and connection faults. FastSelfClean uses a proprietary Matching Joint architecture that performs active cleaning the moment a connector is inserted, removing the need for repeated manual inspection and cleaning by field personnel.

  The connector's typical insertion loss is approximately 0.1 dB, which the company said approaches fiber fusion splicing quality while retaining the flexibility of a pluggable connection. A stackable design supports different fiber counts and enables high-density deployment in constrained space, Amphenol said. The company said FastSelfClean converts labor-intensive fiber maintenance into a more streamlined operation, reducing the time data center operators spend on upkeep and fault isolation.

  ## Backplane and PCIe connectivity

  Amphenol also exhibited its Sentry HD and ZettaMAX high-speed backplane and PCIe solutions at COMPUTEX. The company described them as essential for intra-rack connectivity, providing short-distance, high-density, low-latency transmission inside AI servers.

  At COMPUTEX, Amphenol framed AI data center competition as shifting from individual compute chips toward high-speed interconnect architectures capable of sustaining large-scale deployment. The company's exhibited portfolio spans fiber connectors, optical modules, backplanes, and PCIe connectivity, which it positioned as full-stack support for AI data center interconnect.

  Amphenol said XPO's compatibility with existing supply chains is intended to help cloud providers and system integrators accelerate adoption of next-generation AI infrastructure.

  ## 熱負載與矽光子技術

  XPO整合了液冷冷板，安費諾表示單一模組可管理高達400瓦的熱負載，解決AI伺服器與交換器在功耗、頻寬及高密度安裝方面所面臨的關鍵挑戰。

  該架構建立在安費諾現有的光學技術之上，並導入矽光子技術。安費諾表示，XPO可降低客戶大幅改造現有技術與供應鏈的需求，並將此定位為雲端服務供應商與系統整合商導入下一代AI基礎建設的加速因素。

  ## 光纖連接器針對超大規模維護負擔

  在COMPUTEX 2026同場亮相的還有FastSelfClean，這是安費諾旗下CommScope事業單位開發的光纖連接器。安費諾表示，該產品在展覽期間吸引了全球雲端供應商的高度關注。

  安費諾指出，隨著超大規模AI環境中光纖密度持續提升，即使是微小的灰塵、污染或光纖端面對準偏差，都可能造成訊號損失與連接故障。FastSelfClean採用專利Matching Joint架構，在連接器插入的瞬間即主動進行清潔，免除現場人員反覆手動檢查與清潔的必要。

  該連接器的典型插入損耗約為0.1分貝，安費諾表示這已接近光纖熔接的品質水準，同時保留了可插拔連接的靈活性。可堆疊設計支援不同光纖芯數，可在受限空間內實現高密度部署。安費諾表示，FastSelfClean將耗費大量人力的光纖維護工作轉化為更流暢的作業流程，縮短資料中心業者在日常維護與故障排查上所花費的時間。

  ## 背板與PCIe連接方案

  安費諾在COMPUTEX也展出了Sentry HD與ZettaMAX高速背板及PCIe解決方案，並將其定位為機架內連接的核心，可在AI伺服器內部提供短距離、高密度、低延遲的傳輸能力。

  在COMPUTEX期間，安費諾將AI資料中心的競爭重心描述為正從個別運算晶片轉向能夠支撐大規模部署的高速互連架構。此次展出的產品線涵蓋光纖連接器、光學模組、背板與PCIe連接方案，安費諾將其定位為AI資料中心互連的全方位支援組合。

  安費諾表示，XPO與現有供應鏈的相容性，旨在協助雲端供應商與系統整合商加速導入下一代AI基礎建設。
