body_en: |
  PLDT Inc. has signed a memorandum of agreement (MoA) with the Tagbanua Indigenous Cultural Communities (ICC) for its Palawan Resiliency Submarine Cable Project, completing the permitting process with the Tagbanua Central ICC for the undersea cable development, the company said Sunday.

  The agreement marks the latest development in the project and, according to PLDT, reflects a shared commitment to recognize and protect the rights of Indigenous Peoples while advancing the network build.

  ## What the cable project does

  The submarine cable is intended to improve network reliability and expand internet capacity in Palawan, the long western island province that extends toward the South China Sea. PLDT Vice-President for Network Design and Engineering Arvin L. Siena described the project's purpose in practical terms: connecting Palawan residents to voice calls, messaging services, online learning resources, and digital commerce platforms.

  "The cables that we are laying here connect Palawan to the rest of the Philippines and the world," Siena said in a company media release.

  ## Indigenous consent and permitting

  The MoA with the Tagbanua ICC represents the conclusion of a permitting process that engaged the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP), the Philippine government agency responsible for protecting the rights of indigenous communities. PLDT did not disclose the specific terms of the agreement or the timeline for construction.

  Siena acknowledged the environmental dimension of the project. "As we continue to endeavor to finish this project without doing harm to the environment, trust that we do not take lightly our role as responsible co-stewards of our natural resources," he said.

  ## Significance for network development

  For Philippine telecommunications infrastructure, the agreement clears a regulatory and community-relations milestone that had been outstanding for the project. Obtaining free, prior, and informed consent from indigenous communities is a legal requirement under Philippine law for projects affecting ancestral domains. Completing this step allows PLDT to advance construction of a cable it says is central to its plans to enhance network infrastructure in line with the government's broader digitalization agenda.

  PLDT said it continues to expand and enhance its network infrastructure as part of those digitalization efforts. The company did not provide a target completion date for the Palawan Resiliency Submarine Cable Project.
body_zh: |
  PLDT Inc. 已與塔格巴努瓦原住民文化社群（Tagbanua Indigenous Cultural Communities，ICC）就其巴拉望韌性海底電纜計畫簽署合作備忘錄（MoA），完成與塔格巴努瓦中央ICC針對這條海底電纜開發所需的許可程序，公司週日宣布。

  這份協議標誌著計畫推進的最新進展。PLDT表示，此舉體現了雙方在推動網路建設的同時，共同承諾認可並保障原住民族的各項權利。

  ## 電纜計畫的功能

  這條海底電纜旨在提升巴拉望的網路穩定性並擴大網際網路容量。巴拉望是菲律賓西側一座細長的島嶼省份，向南海方向延伸。PLDT網路設計與工程副總裁Arvin L. Siena以具體實際的角度描述計畫目的：讓巴拉望居民能夠使用語音通話、即時通訊服務、線上學習資源與數位商務平台。

  「我們在這裡鋪設的電纜，將巴拉望與菲律賓其他地區及全世界相連，」Siena在公司媒體新聞稿中表示。

  ## 原住民同意與許可程序

  與塔格巴努瓦ICC簽署的備忘錄，代表整個許可程序的最終完成。這一程序涉及與菲律賓原住民族國家委員會（NCIP）的協調合作——NCIP是負責保障原住民社群權利的菲律賓政府機關。PLDT並未披露協議的具體條款或施工時程。

  Siena亦表達了對環境層面的重視。「我們持續努力在不傷害環境的前提下完成這項計畫，請相信我們絕不輕忽自身作為自然資源共同守護者的責任，」他說。

  ## 對網路發展的意義

  就菲律賓電信基礎建設而言，這份協議排除了計畫長期懸而未決的法規與社區關係障礙。依據菲律賓法律，對於影響原住民祖傳領域的建設計畫，取得原住民的自由意志、事先且知情的同意（FPIC）是法定要求。完成這一步驟，使PLDT得以推進電纜的實體建設；該公司表示，這條電纜是其配合政府整體數位化政策、強化網路基礎建設計畫的核心所在。

  PLDT表示，將持續擴展並強化其網路基礎建設，作為數位化推進工作的一環。公司並未提供巴拉望韌性海底電纜計畫的預計完工日期。
