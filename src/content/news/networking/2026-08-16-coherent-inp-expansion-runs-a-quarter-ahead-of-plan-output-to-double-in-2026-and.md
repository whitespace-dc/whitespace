body_en: |
  Coherent has accelerated its expansion of indium phosphide (InP) production capacity, with its 6-inch InP production line running approximately one quarter ahead of the original plan, institutional analysts said in reports cited by Taiwan's Commercial Times on August 15. The faster-than-expected ramp is set to increase supply of 800G and 1.6T optical transceiver modules as AI data centers drive demand for higher-bandwidth interconnects.

  ## Capacity trajectory

  Coherent's InP capacity is on track to roughly double in 2026 compared to 2025 levels, and to double again in 2027, according to analysts cited by the paper. InP is a compound semiconductor used to fabricate the high-speed lasers inside optical transceiver modules.

  Coherent said its InP capacity remains the company's primary internal constraint, while bottlenecks elsewhere in its supply chain are relatively limited. Multiple product lines are entering simultaneous expansion phases, the company said.

  ## Demand environment

  Analysts cited by the paper said 800G optical interconnects have become the market mainstream, while 1.6T is entering an accelerated adoption phase. Switch bandwidth inside AI server clusters is evolving from 800G toward 1.6T and eventually 3.2T, driving increased demand for high-speed lasers, fiber array units (FAUs), fiber connectors, and passive optical components inside transceivers.

  ## Co-packaged optics

  Co-packaged optics (CPO) — a design that integrates optical engines directly alongside switch application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs), rather than in separate pluggable modules — is creating new demand for silicon photonics components, external laser sources, and high-precision fiber coupling hardware, analysts said. Optical circuit switching (OCS) and silicon photonics architectures are also expected to gain penetration, sustaining longer-term growth in the supply chain, the analysts added.

  ## Taiwan supply chain beneficiaries

  Several Taiwan Stock Exchange-listed optical communications companies are seen as beneficiaries of the demand upturn: Lian Ya (3081), Hua Xing Guang (4979), Shang Quan (3363), Guang Sheng (6442), and Lian Jun (3450). The Taiwan optical communications supply chain is positioned for continued high growth as CPO, OCS, and silicon photonics architectures increase their market penetration, analysts said.

  Coherent expects CPO to begin contributing to its revenue from the second quarter of fiscal year 2027.
body_zh: |
  法人機構研究報告指出，Coherent正加速擴充磷化銦（InP）產能，其6吋InP產線目前較原定計畫超前約一季，此一消息由台灣《工商時報》8月15日引述法人報告報導。提前達標的擴產進度，將加速800G及1.6T光收發模組的供貨量，以因應AI資料中心對更高頻寬互連的旺盛需求。

  ## 產能成長軌跡

  法人預估，Coherent的InP產能2026年將較2025年大幅翻倍，2027年再次翻倍。磷化銦（InP）是一種化合物半導體，用於製造光收發模組內部的高速雷射元件。

  Coherent表示，InP產能目前仍是公司最主要的內部瓶頸，但供應鏈其他環節的瓶頸相對有限。目前多條產品線正同步進入擴產階段。

  ## 需求市場概況

  法人指出，800G光互連已成為市場主流，1.6T則正進入加速導入期。AI伺服器叢集內部的交換機頻寬，正從800G朝1.6T演進，最終將邁向3.2T，帶動高速雷射、光纖陣列單元（FAU）、光纖連接器，以及光收發器內部被動光學元件的需求持續攀升。

  ## 共封裝光學

  法人表示，共封裝光學（CPO）——一種將光學引擎直接與交換機特殊應用積體電路（ASIC）整合封裝、而非採用獨立可插拔模組設計的架構——正為矽光子元件、外部雷射光源及高精度光纖耦合硬體創造新需求。分析師補充，光路交換（OCS）與矽光子架構的市占率亦有望持續提升，為整體供應鏈奠定長期成長動能。

  ## 台灣供應鏈受惠股

  多家在台灣證券交易所上市的光通訊廠商，被視為此波需求回升的受惠族群，包括聯亞（3081）、華星光（4979）、上詮（3363）、光聖（6442）及聯鈞（3450）。法人表示，隨著CPO、OCS及矽光子架構持續拓展市占率，台灣光通訊供應鏈可望維持高速成長態勢。

  Coherent預計，CPO業務將從2027會計年度第二季起開始對公司營收作出貢獻。
