body_en: |
  HPE this week disclosed a phased timeline to bring its three cloud network management platforms — Mist, Aruba Central, and EdgeConnect — under a single agentic artificial intelligence framework, setting the first integration milestone for the end of 2026.

  Under the published schedule, HPE Mist will support HPE Networking EdgeConnect by the end of 2026; HPE Aruba Central will add EdgeConnect support in the first half of 2027. The integration covers wired access networking, wireless local area networking, and SASE — Secure Access Service Edge, a framework that combines wide-area networking with cloud-delivered security functions — the company said.

  ## The three platforms and their origins

  The three platforms reflect a decade of acquisitions. HPE acquired Aruba Networks in 2015, gaining the cloud-based Aruba Central network management platform. Its 2020 acquisition of Silver Peak added the EdgeConnect SD-WAN (Software-Defined Wide Area Network) and SASE portfolio. HPE's 2024 acquisition of Juniper Networks brought Mist, an AI-native network platform built around an AI engine called Marvis.

  ## Self-driving networking strategy

  HPE said the integration advances what it calls its "self-driving networking" strategy — the goal of automating network operations so the network can detect and resolve problems without human intervention. The company said the merged system will deliver a single operational experience, enhanced visibility from edge to cloud, and simplified agentic AI — AI capable of taking actions autonomously rather than only answering queries — for both network operations (NetOps) and security operations (SecOps), enabling automated protection and faster problem resolution.

  The announcement extends security investment HPE outlined at a September investor conference on networking, where the company said it is embedding security functions across its entire network stack by integrating SASE, network access control, firewalls, identity-based policy enforcement, and AI-driven operations.

  ## Agentic AI steps taken earlier in 2026

  Ahead of the timeline disclosure, HPE had already moved toward the unified platform. In May, the company said agentic AI was available across both Aruba Central and Mist to support autonomous operations, with new AI agents described as capable of optimizing network capacity and wireless signal configuration, performing self-protective actions, and resolving user roaming issues.

  In June, HPE said EdgeConnect had merged SD-WAN and SSE — Security Service Edge, the cloud-delivered security component of a SASE architecture — into a single SASE platform managed through one AI-native console.

  ## Security functions built into EdgeConnect

  HPE described several security capabilities now integrated directly into EdgeConnect. The platform embeds SSE connectors natively, which the company said allows enterprises to access private applications over the SD-WAN fabric without adding dedicated connectors in data centers or cloud environments, accelerating adoption of ZTNA — Zero Trust Network Access, a model that treats every connection request as untrusted until verified.

  For organizations with sovereignty or data-residency requirements, a Private Edge configuration enables a sovereign SASE architecture that enforces zero-trust access within the enterprise perimeter without routing traffic through external network service points of presence (PoPs).

  EdgeConnect also integrates a network firewall directly into the platform, adding intrusion detection and prevention (IDS/IPS), adaptive distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) protection, URL filtering, and role-based network segmentation. A dedicated Secure Web Gateway (SWG) tunnel extends cloud-delivered security functions to all connected devices, including IoT (Internet of Things) devices, without requiring an SSE agent installed on each device individually.

  ## Mist and Aruba Central convergence already under way

  In parallel, Mist and Aruba Central have begun integrating with each other. HPE said Mist now supports HPE Networking CX series switches, giving those switch users access to AI-native network visibility analytics, Layer 2 wired network access assurance — Layer 2 being the data-link layer that handles device-to-device communication on the same network segment — service-level insights, dynamic packet capture, and operations driven by the Marvis AI engine.

  Aruba Central has gained support for Marvis AI-driven insights and automated network port remediation, extending autonomous operations capabilities across HPE's broader networking product lineup, the company said.

  ## What the timeline means for enterprise planners

  The two-stage schedule gives enterprise network planners a concrete window for when unified management across all three platforms will be available. Organizations running HPE networking environments across wired, wireless, and WAN layers currently administer those environments through separate platforms; the integration is designed to consolidate that into a single agentic AI-driven interface.

  HPE Mist's support for HPE Networking EdgeConnect is targeted for completion by the end of 2026, with HPE Aruba Central's support for EdgeConnect to follow in the first half of 2027.
body_zh: |
  ## HPE訂2026至2027年路線圖　Mist、Aruba Central與EdgeConnect整合為單一AI框架

  HPE本週公布分階段時程，計畫將旗下三大雲端網路管理平台——Mist、Aruba Central與EdgeConnect——整合至單一自主式人工智慧框架之下，並將首個整合里程碑訂於2026年底達成。

  根據已公開的時程表，HPE Mist將於2026年底前支援HPE Networking EdgeConnect；HPE Aruba Central則將於2027年上半年新增EdgeConnect支援。HPE表示，此次整合涵蓋有線存取網路、無線區域網路，以及SASE（Secure Access Service Edge，安全存取服務邊緣）——一套將廣域網路與雲端交付安全功能整合為一體的架構。

  ## 三大平台的由來

  這三大平台折射出HPE近十年來的併購軌跡。HPE於2015年收購Aruba Networks，取得基於雲端的Aruba Central網路管理平台。2020年收購Silver Peak，納入EdgeConnect SD-WAN（軟體定義廣域網路）與SASE產品組合。2024年收購Juniper Networks，則帶來了Mist——一個以名為Marvis的AI引擎為核心、原生支援AI的網路平台。

  ## 「自駕網路」策略

  HPE表示，此次整合推進了其所稱的「自駕網路」（self-driving networking）策略，目標是將網路運維自動化，使網路能夠在無需人工介入的情況下自行偵測並解決問題。HPE表示，整合後的系統將提供統一的運維體驗、從邊緣到雲端的增強可視性，以及適用於網路運維（NetOps）與資安運維（SecOps）的簡化自主AI——即能夠自主採取行動、而不僅是回應查詢的AI——從而實現自動化防護與更快速的問題解決。

  此次公告延續了HPE在今年9月一場網路業務投資人說明會上揭示的資安投資方向。在該場說明會上，HPE表示正透過整合SASE、網路存取控制、防火牆、基於身分的政策執行，以及AI驅動的運維，將資安功能嵌入其整個網路堆疊。

  ## 2026年先行布局自主式AI

  在正式公布時程表之前，HPE已先行朝統一平台的目標邁進。今年5月，HPE宣布自主式AI已可在Aruba Central與Mist兩大平台上支援自主運維，新推出的AI代理人據稱能夠優化網路容量與無線訊號配置、執行自我防護動作，並解決使用者漫遊問題。

  6月，HPE宣布EdgeConnect已將SD-WAN與SSE（Security Service Edge，安全服務邊緣——即SASE架構中由雲端交付的資安元件）合併為單一SASE平台，透過一個原生AI控制台統一管理。

  ## EdgeConnect內建的資安功能

  HPE說明了目前已直接整合至EdgeConnect的多項資安能力。該平台原生嵌入SSE連接器，HPE表示此舉讓企業無需在資料中心或雲端環境中另行部署專用連接器，即可透過SD-WAN架構存取私有應用程式，從而加速導入ZTNA（Zero Trust Network Access，零信任網路存取）——一種將每個連線請求視為不可信、必須驗證方可放行的模型。

  針對有資料主權或資料落地需求的組織，Private Edge配置可在企業邊界內部實施符合主權要求的SASE架構，強制執行零信任存取，而無需將流量導至外部網路服務業者的存在點（PoP）。

  EdgeConnect亦將網路防火牆直接整合至平台之中，新增入侵偵測與防禦（IDS/IPS）、自適應分散式阻斷服務（DDoS）防護、URL過濾，以及基於角色的網路分段功能。此外，專用的安全網路閘道（SWG）通道可將雲端交付的資安功能延伸至所有連接裝置，包括物聯網（IoT）裝置，且無需在每台裝置上個別安裝SSE代理程式。

  ## Mist與Aruba Central的融合已在進行中

  與此同時，Mist與Aruba Central之間的整合也已同步展開。HPE表示，Mist現已支援HPE Networking CX系列交換器，讓這些交換器的用戶得以使用原生AI的網路可視性分析、第二層（Layer 2）有線網路存取保證——第二層即資料連結層，負責處理同一網路區段內裝置對裝置的通訊——服務等級洞察、動態封包擷取，以及由Marvis AI引擎驅動的運維功能。

  Aruba Central則已取得Marvis AI驅動洞察與網路埠自動修復的支援，將自主運維能力延伸至HPE更廣泛的網路產品陣容，HPE表示。

  ## 此時程對企業規劃的意義

  這份兩階段時程表為企業網路規劃人員提供了明確的整合窗口，讓他們得以掌握三大平台何時能實現統一管理。目前，在有線、無線與廣域網路等各層面部署HPE網路環境的組織，仍須透過各自獨立的平台進行管理；此次整合的目標，正是將上述管理整合至單一的自主AI驅動介面。
