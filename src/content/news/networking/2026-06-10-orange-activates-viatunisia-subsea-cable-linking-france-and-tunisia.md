body_en: |
  ## Cable enters commercial service

  Orange, the French multinational telecommunications operator, has activated the ViaTunisia subsea cable connecting Marseille, France, to Bizerte, Tunisia, marking the start of commercial operations on a new direct telecommunications route between southern Europe and North Africa.

  The cable was built as an open, point-to-point system with a projected operational lifespan of 25 years. Michaël Trabbia, CEO of Orange Wholesale, announced the activation in a LinkedIn post, describing the link as "a direct, secure, and resilient route between Europe and North Africa, with enhanced access to the digital hub of Marseille and its interconnection capabilities to Europe."

  ## System design and construction

  Alcatel Submarine Networks (ASN) supplied the system design and transmission equipment. Marine installation was carried out by two cable ships: Orange Marine's Sophie Germain and Elettra TLC's Teliri, with Elettra TLC coordinating marine operations overall.

  Construction work encompassed marine surveys, equipment testing, cable loading, seabed installation, shore landings, and final network integration, according to the project announcement.

  On the European end, the cable connects to Orange's network infrastructure in Marseille through a redundant metropolitan fibre ring — a looped cable architecture that provides backup paths in case of a single-point failure — linking the city's data centers. That connection provides onward distribution of international capacity into European networks.

  ## EU funding and strategic rationale

  ViaTunisia received partial public funding from the European Union through the Connecting Europe Facility (CEF) Digital programme, which supports cross-border digital infrastructure projects across EU member states and partner countries. Under a grant agreement signed in December 2022, the EU covered 30 percent of the cable's construction and management costs.

  Project stakeholders said the cable addresses growing demand for international data capacity driven by digital services, cloud computing, and artificial intelligence applications, while also increasing routing options in a region where subsea cable disruptions can affect connectivity across multiple countries.

  ## Part of the Medusa system

  ViaTunisia is described as a segment of the broader Medusa cable system. Trabbia's announcement identified the Marseille-to-Bizerte link specifically as the ViaTunisia segment of that larger system, providing an additional path for traffic between North Africa and Europe in the event that existing subsea infrastructure is disrupted.

  The cable's entry into service adds a new redundant international route to a corridor where digital traffic is growing, and extends the reach of Marseille's position as a major submarine cable landing and interconnection hub for European networks.

  ## 電纜正式投入商業服務

  法國跨國電信業者 Orange 正式啟用 ViaTunisia 海底電纜，連結法國馬賽與突尼西亞比塞大（Bizerte），開通南歐與北非之間一條全新的直接電信路由，標誌著商業營運的正式啟動。

  這條電纜採開放式點對點架構建造，預計使用壽命 25 年。Orange Wholesale 執行長 Michaël Trabbia 在 LinkedIn 發文宣布啟用消息，將這條電纜描述為「歐洲與北非之間一條直接、安全且具韌性的路由，並強化了進入馬賽數位樞紐及其通往歐洲互聯能力的管道。」

  ## 系統設計與建設

  系統設計及傳輸設備由阿爾卡特海底網路（Alcatel Submarine Networks，ASN）提供。海底鋪設工程由兩艘纜船分工完成：Orange Marine 旗下的 Sophie Germain 號，以及 Elettra TLC 旗下的 Teliri 號，整體海事作業由 Elettra TLC 統籌協調。

  根據專案公告，建設工程涵蓋海洋勘測、設備測試、纜線裝船、海床鋪設、陸地登陸，以及最終的網路整合等各項作業。

  在歐洲端，電纜透過一條冗餘都會光纖環接入 Orange 在馬賽的網路基礎設施——所謂冗餘都會光纖環，是一種環型纜線架構，可在單點故障發生時提供備援路徑——並串聯該市各資料中心，再進一步將國際頻寬向外分配至歐洲各大網路。

  ## 歐盟資金挹注與策略考量

  ViaTunisia 獲得歐盟透過「連結歐洲設施」（Connecting Europe Facility，CEF）數位計畫提供的部分公共資金；該計畫專門支持歐盟成員國及合作夥伴國之間的跨境數位基礎設施建設。依據 2022 年 12 月簽署的補助協議，歐盟承擔了這條電纜建設與管理成本的 30％。

  專案各利害關係人表示，這條電纜旨在回應因數位服務、雲端運算及人工智慧應用所帶動的國際資料頻寬需求成長，同時也增加了該地區的路由選擇——在現有海底電纜遭到中斷、可能波及多國連線的情況下，這一點尤為關鍵。

  ## Medusa 系統的組成路段

  ViaTunisia 被定位為更大規模 Medusa 海纜系統的一個組成路段。Trabbia 在公告中特別將馬賽至比塞大這段線路標示為該大型系統的 ViaTunisia 路段，在現有海底基礎設施遭到中斷時，為北非與歐洲之間的流量提供額外的傳輸路徑。

  這條電纜正式投入服務，為這條數位流量持續成長的走廊新增了一條冗餘國際路由，並進一步鞏固馬賽作為歐洲網路重要海底電纜登陸與互聯樞紐的地位。
