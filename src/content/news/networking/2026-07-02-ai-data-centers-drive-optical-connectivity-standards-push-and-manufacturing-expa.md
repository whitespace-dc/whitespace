body_en: |
  A wave of announcements spanning standardization, manufacturing investment, and early-stage technology development is positioning optical connectivity as a strategic layer in AI data center infrastructure, with a 44-member industry coalition establishing specifications for expanded beam optical connectors, Corning committing to a tenfold U.S. manufacturing expansion under a partnership with NVIDIA, and a University of Arizona-linked startup reporting a prototype optical switch its developers claim operates on one-thousandth the energy of conventional approaches.

  The convergence of these moves, reported by Data Center Frontier, reflects a shift in where the AI infrastructure buildout is concentrating: from compute and power systems toward the network fabric that ties accelerated computing clusters together.

  ## Standards effort targets interoperability for expanded beam optical connectors

  3M announced it has joined a coalition forming a multi-source agreement — a shared technical framework enabling multiple vendors to build compatible products — focused on expanded beam optical, or EBO, connectivity for AI infrastructure. As of June 29, 2026, the EBO MSA (Expanded Beam Optical Multi-Source Agreement) group counted 44 member companies drawn from the networking, cloud, semiconductor, connector, and optical ecosystem, according to the group's website.

  EBO technology is designed to improve the reliability and maintainability of optical links in high-density environments. Traditional multi-fiber physical contact connectors can be sensitive to contamination, inspection requirements, and handling practices. In large AI clusters, where optical link counts run high, even modest operational friction can multiply into significant deployment and maintenance overhead, according to Data Center Frontier.

  The MSA group is working to create shared specifications for multiple connector configurations, establishing a framework for interoperable products across hyperscale, cloud, and enterprise AI deployments. For data center operators, standardized EBO connectivity could reduce procurement complexity and improve supplier diversity. For enterprises building AI infrastructure, it could reduce the risk of locking into a narrow vendor ecosystem before the market has matured, the publication said.

  ## University of Arizona research backs early-stage all-optical switch

  Research developed by Pierre-Alexandre Blanche, a research professor at the University of Arizona, is powering Post Quantum Tek's High-Speed Optical Switch, designated PQT-HOS. The company describes the technology as using light diffraction to keep data in optical format throughout the switching process — an approach it calls optical-optical-optical switching.

  In many conventional data center network architectures, traffic may travel optically across fiber but be converted into electrical signals for switching before being converted back into optical form. Post Quantum Tek's design is intended to eliminate that conversion step.

  According to Post Quantum Tek and the University of Arizona announcement, the PQT-HOS is capable of operating up to 1,000 times faster than conventional switching while consuming approximately one-thousandth the energy. Those figures are developer-reported benchmarks and will require validation through commercial deployment and independent testing.

  The PQT-HOS is described as patented and bench-proven but is not yet a commercialized data center product. The announcement says it is ready to be developed into a commercial prototype through the University of Arizona's Tech Launch Arizona Institute, placing it earlier in the adoption cycle than the EBO MSA effort or the major manufacturing commitments elsewhere in the industry.

  ## Corning and NVIDIA announce tenfold U.S. optical manufacturing expansion

  Corning and NVIDIA announced a partnership to expand U.S. manufacturing for AI infrastructure. Under the agreement, Corning is expanding its U.S.-based optical connectivity manufacturing capacity by 10 times and its U.S. fiber production capacity by more than 50%. The expansion includes three new advanced manufacturing facilities in North Carolina and Texas and will create more than 3,000 jobs.

  Data Center Frontier identified three dimensions of value in the partnership. First, supply: AI infrastructure buildouts have grown large enough that a shortage of optical connectivity components can delay deployment as surely as a shortage of power equipment or GPUs. Second, domestic manufacturing: as hyperscale AI campuses become strategic assets, operators and chip companies are placing greater emphasis on resilient U.S.-based supply chains. Third, optics are becoming more tightly aligned with accelerated computing systems, according to the publication's analysis.

  Data Center Frontier also noted a separate Corning partnership with Amazon, though specific terms of that agreement were not detailed in the source material.

  ## Broader ecosystem activity spans co-packaged optics, acquisitions, and domestic investment

  Beyond the headline announcements, Data Center Frontier identified a series of related moves across the optical connectivity ecosystem. GlobalFoundries is pushing into co-packaged optics; Sivers is collaborating with GlobalFoundries on laser arrays; server maker Wiwynn demonstrated co-packaged optics at Computex; Credo has acquired DustPhotonics; and LightSpeed Photonics is developing near-packaged optical interconnect designs.

  STL is planning to invest up to $100 million in U.S. manufacturing capacity to support AI data center and telecom customers with optical connectivity products.

  Data Center Frontier described these collective moves as indicating a maturing market around the optical layer of AI infrastructure, where the value proposition extends beyond faster data movement to include reduced deployment complexity, lower operating overhead, higher-density cluster support, improved energy efficiency, and a more resilient domestic supply chain.

  ## Implications for AI infrastructure planning

  Data centers are assembled from components supplied by multiple vendors — servers, GPUs, switches, transceivers, fiber assemblies, connectors, cabinets, power distribution systems, and software-defined network layers. As the number of optical links per cluster grows, the absence of shared specifications creates integration and procurement risk. The EBO MSA effort addresses that gap directly.

  On the energy side, network switching represents an increasingly visible cost center as AI cluster power demands grow. Post Quantum Tek's PQT-HOS — if its developer-reported performance figures hold under production conditions — would represent a significant reduction in energy consumed by the switching layer, though commercial validation remains ahead.

  Corning's planned manufacturing expansion and STL's announced $100 million U.S. investment point to supply chain capacity as a near-term constraint on AI infrastructure growth. Corning's three new facilities in North Carolina and Texas, tied to the NVIDIA partnership, represent a concrete step toward closing that capacity gap.
body_zh: |
  一波涵蓋標準制定、製造投資及早期技術開發的重大宣布，正將光纖連接確立為AI資料中心基礎設施的戰略核心。由44家企業組成的產業聯盟正為擴束光纖連接器制定共同規格；康寧宣布在與輝達的合作框架下，將美國製造產能擴增十倍；而一家源自亞利桑那大學的新創公司也宣稱，其研發的光學交換器原型機耗能僅為傳統方案的千分之一。

  上述動向交集於同一時點，反映出AI基礎設施建設重心的轉移——從運算與電力系統，逐步移向連結加速運算叢集的網路架構。以上資訊由Data Center Frontier率先報導。

  ## 標準化工作鎖定擴束光纖連接器的互通性

  3M宣布加入一個正在推動多來源協議（multi-source agreement，MSA）的聯盟——此框架可讓多家廠商生產相互相容的產品——焦點在於AI基礎設施的擴束光纖（Expanded Beam Optical，EBO）連接技術。截至2026年6月29日，EBO MSA聯盟成員已達44家，涵蓋網路、雲端、半導體、連接器及光纖等生態系領域，據該聯盟官網資訊顯示。

  EBO技術旨在提升高密度環境中光纖連接的可靠性與可維護性。傳統多纖實體接觸式連接器對污染、檢測要求及操作手法較為敏感。在大型AI叢集中，光纖連接數量龐大，即便是輕微的操作摩擦，也可能成倍放大部署與維護的負擔，Data Center Frontier如此指出。

  MSA聯盟正致力於為多種連接器配置制定共同規格，為超大規模、雲端及企業AI部署建立可互通產品的技術框架。對於資料中心營運商而言，標準化的EBO連接有助於降低採購複雜度並提升供應商多元性；對於正在建置AI基礎設施的企業來說，在市場尚未成熟之前，則可降低鎖定單一供應商生態系的風險，該媒體表示。

  ## 亞利桑那大學研究支撐早期全光學交換器技術

  由亞利桑那大學研究教授Pierre-Alexandre Blanche主導的研究成果，正為Post Quantum Tek公司的高速光學交換器（High-Speed Optical Switch，PQT-HOS）提供技術基礎。該公司描述這項技術利用光繞射原理，使資料在整個交換過程中全程保持光學格式，並將此方案稱為「光－光－光交換」（optical-optical-optical switching）。

  在許多傳統資料中心網路架構中，流量可能透過光纖以光學形式傳輸，但進行封包交換時，須先轉換為電訊號，再轉換回光學形式。Post Quantum Tek的設計旨在省去這道轉換步驟。

  根據Post Quantum Tek與亞利桑那大學的聯合公告，PQT-HOS的運作速度最高可達傳統交換器的1,000倍，耗能則僅約千分之一。上述數字為開發商自行公布的基準測試數據，仍需透過商業部署與獨立測試加以驗證。

  PQT-HOS目前已取得專利並通過實驗台驗證，但尚未商業化為資料中心產品。公告指出，該技術已準備透過亞利桑那大學技術移轉機構Tech Launch Arizona進一步開發為商業原型，在整個產業的採用週期中，其發展階段仍早於EBO MSA的標準化進程及業界各項大型製造承諾。

  ## 康寧與輝達宣布美國光纖製造產能擴增十倍

  康寧與輝達宣布建立合作夥伴關係，共同擴大AI基礎設施所需的美國製造產能。根據協議，康寧將美國光纖連接製造產能擴增十倍，美國光纖生產產能也將提升逾50%。此次擴產計畫包括在北卡羅來納州與德克薩斯州新設三座先進製造設施，並將創造逾3,000個就業機會。

  Data Center Frontier將這項合作夥伴關係的價值歸納為三個面向：第一是供應面——AI基礎設施的建置規模已大到光纖連接元件短缺可能延誤部署進程，其影響不亞於電力設備或GPU短缺；第二是本土製造——隨著超大規模AI園區成為戰略資產，營運商與晶片公司愈來愈重視具韌性的美國本土供應鏈；第三，光纖技術正與加速運算系統愈趨緊密整合，該媒體的分析如此指出。

  Data Center Frontier另外提及康寧與亞馬遜（Amazon）的另一項合作夥伴關係，但原始資料中未詳述該協議的具體條款。

  ## 更廣泛的生態系動態：共封裝光學、併購與本土投資

  除上述主要宣布之外，Data Center Frontier還梳理出光纖連接生態系的一系列相關動向：格芯（GlobalFoundries）正積極布局共封裝光學（co-packaged optics）；Sivers正與格芯合作開發雷射陣列；伺服器製造商緯穎（Wiwynn）在Computex展示了共封裝光學技術；Credo已完成對DustPhotonics的收購；LightSpeed Photonics則正在開發近封裝光學互連設計。

  STL計劃在美國製造產能上投資最多1億美元，以光纖連接產品服務AI資料中心及電信客戶。

  Data Center Frontier形容上述種種動向顯示，AI基礎設施光纖層相關市場正邁向成熟，其價值主張已超越「更快的資料傳輸」，進一步延伸至降低部署複雜度、減少日常維運負擔、支援更高密度叢集、提升能源效率，以及強化本土供應鏈韌性等多個面向。

  ## AI基礎設施規劃的意涵

  資料中心由多家廠商供應的元件共同組成，涵蓋伺服器、GPU、交換器、收發器、光纖組件、連接器、機櫃、配電系統及軟體定義網路層。隨著每個叢集的光纖連接數量持續攀升，缺乏共同規格將帶來整合與採購風險，EBO MSA的標準化工作正是直接回應這一缺口。

  在能耗方面，隨著AI叢集對電力的需求不斷增加，網路交換已成為日益顯著的成本來源。Post Quantum Tek的PQT-HOS——若其開發商公布的效能數據在量產條件下獲得驗證——將大幅降低交換層的能源消耗，但商業驗證工作仍有待完成。

  康寧的製造擴產計畫與STL宣布的1億美元美國投資，均指向供應鏈產能是AI基礎設施近期成長的瓶頸所在。康寧在北卡羅來納州與德克薩斯州新建的三座設施，與輝達合作關係密切相連，是填補這一產能缺口的具體行動。
