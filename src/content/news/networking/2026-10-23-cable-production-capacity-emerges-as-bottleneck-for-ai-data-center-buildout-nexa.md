body_en: |
  Cable manufacturing capacity is becoming a constraint on AI-driven data center construction, Nexans Chief Financial Officer Vincent Piquet told Reuters, as demand from operators outpaces the cable industry's ability to add production output.

  Global cable capacity is tight, Piquet said, and building a new cross-linked polyethylene, or XLPE, cable production line — known in the industry as a CV line — takes around three years. That lead time limits how quickly suppliers can respond to rising demand. "Things can't move quickly and the people building these data centres are in a great hurry," Piquet said.

  Data centers require large volumes of low- and medium-voltage cable to connect power infrastructure. Low-voltage cable typically handles distribution within a facility, while medium-voltage cable connects the site to the grid or to large on-site transformers. Larger facilities consume proportionately more medium-voltage cable, Piquet said.

  ## Power constraints compounding pressure

  The strain on power infrastructure is already delaying some projects. In September, Oracle issued a force majeure notice — a legal declaration that unforeseen circumstances prevent fulfilling a contract — to a Blue Owl unit developing a large data center in New Mexico, citing potential difficulties securing sufficient power for the site.

  Piquet said grid constraints, while slowing some projects, were simultaneously supporting demand for Nexans' products and allowing the company to be more selective when allocating its capacity.

  The supply tightness is also pushing buyers toward longer contract terms to secure access. French distribution network operator Enedis has moved from annual procurement agreements to contracts lasting six or seven years in order to lock in cable supply, according to Piquet.

  ## Europe versus the United States

  Europe generally faces fewer grid-connection challenges than the United States and benefits from stronger electricity networks in some northern countries, Piquet said. However, lengthy regulatory processes are delaying certain European projects, he added.

  Europe's data center expansion is likely to be less pronounced than the United States because private investment capacity is smaller and public funding plays a larger role, Piquet said. He added that he does not expect demand linked to artificial intelligence to slow.

  ## Industry implications

  The three-year timeline to bring a new CV production line online means cable supply cannot be scaled quickly enough to match accelerating construction schedules, leaving data center developers and grid operators competing for limited output. Utility-scale buyers such as Enedis are responding by locking in multiyear supply agreements, a shift that could further reduce spot availability for new entrants or projects without long-term procurement strategies.

  Piquet said Nexans does not expect AI-driven demand to moderate, suggesting the capacity gap is likely to persist into the latter half of the decade as new production lines commissioned today would not come online until around 2028 or 2029.
body_zh: |
  全球電纜大廠耐克森（Nexans）財務長文森·皮凱（Vincent Piquet）接受路透社專訪時表示，隨著資料中心業者的需求持續超出電纜產業新增產能的速度，電纜製造產能已成為AI驅動資料中心建設的一大瓶頸。

  皮凱指出，全球電纜產能相當吃緊，而要建設一條新的交聯聚乙烯（XLPE）電纜生產線——業界稱之為CV線——大約需要三年時間。這樣的前置期限制了供應商回應需求成長的能力。皮凱表示：「事情無法快速推進，而那些建造資料中心的人卻急得很。」

  資料中心需要大量低壓與中壓電纜，以連接各項電力基礎設施。低壓電纜通常負責設施內部的配電，中壓電纜則負責將場址接入電網，或連接現場的大型變壓器。皮凱表示，規模愈大的設施，所消耗的中壓電纜比例也相對更高。

  ## 電力限制加劇壓力

  電力基礎設施的壓力已導致部分專案延宕。九月間，甲骨文（Oracle）向開發新墨西哥州大型資料中心的藍貓頭鷹（Blue Owl）旗下單位發出不可抗力通知——即一項宣告不可預見情況導致合約無法履行的法律聲明——理由是該場址可能難以取得充足電力。

  皮凱表示，電網限制雖然放緩了部分專案的進度，但同時也支撐了耐克森產品的需求，並讓公司在分配產能時得以更具選擇性。

  供應緊張也促使買方轉向簽訂更長期的合約，以確保穩定取得供貨。據皮凱表示，法國配電網路業者Enedis已從年度採購協議轉為簽訂六至七年的長約，以鎖定電纜供應來源。

  ## 歐洲與美國的差異

  皮凱表示，歐洲在電網連接方面普遍較美國面臨較少挑戰，部分北歐國家的電力網路尤為強健，提供了一定優勢。不過，他補充說，冗長的法規審查程序正在拖慢歐洲部分專案的腳步。

  皮凱認為，歐洲資料中心的擴張力道可能不如美國，原因在於私人投資規模較小，且公共資金扮演更吃重的角色。他同時表示，預期與人工智慧相關的需求不會趨緩。

  ## 產業影響

  一條新CV生產線需耗時三年才能投產，意味著電纜供應無法及時跟上不斷加速的建設時程，使資料中心開發商與電網業者相互競逐有限的供貨。Enedis等大型公用事業買家紛紛轉向鎖定多年期供應協議，這一趨勢可能進一步壓縮現貨市場的可用量，令缺乏長期採購策略的新進業者或新興專案更難取得所需電纜。

  皮凱表示，耐克森並不預期AI帶動的需求將趨於平緩，這意味著產能缺口可能持續至本十年代後半段——因為今日啟動建設的新產線，要到2028年或2029年前後才能正式投產。
