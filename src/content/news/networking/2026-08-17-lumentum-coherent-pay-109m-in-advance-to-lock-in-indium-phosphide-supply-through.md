body_en: |
  Lumentum and Coherent have committed more than $109 million in advance payments to AXT, the world's leading maker of indium phosphide (InP) substrates, to lock in supply through at least the end of 2031, as AI data center demand for optical interconnects drives a surge in InP prices and raises industry warnings of a supply shortfall more severe than the memory chip shortage.

  ## Supply deals with AXT

  Lumentum will pay $87 million in prepayments under a capacity-reservation and supply agreement with AXT running through the end of 2031. Coherent has prepaid more than $22 million to AXT to secure separate production capacity. AXT is identified by industry participants as the global leader in InP substrate manufacturing.

  Both companies produce InP-based lasers used in optical communications links inside data centers and have moved to lock in substrate supply as AI-driven demand accelerates.

  ## The optical-for-copper shift in AI data centers

  Industry analysts attribute the demand surge to a structural shift in AI data center connectivity described as "optical in, copper out" — the replacement of copper interconnects with high-speed optical fiber links inside large-scale facilities. InP-based lasers are central components in those optical links.

  Lumentum has warned that AI data center demand for InP lasers has grown at a multiplied rate. The company has cautioned that the resulting supply shortage could prove more severe than the memory chip shortage that followed earlier rounds of AI infrastructure expansion.

  ## The memory shortage parallel

  The memory comparison is specific. During the earlier wave of AI data center construction, the industry underestimated how rapidly memory demand would outpace supply, an episode that contributed to what industry observers describe as technology cost inflation. Analysts now predict InP could follow the same pattern, becoming the next commodity subject to industry-wide scrambles during the AI investment cycle.

  ## Tight supply expected through at least 2027

  The long-term contracts do not eliminate the shortage risk. Industry participants note the agreements cover only part of each company's supply requirements, and a significant share of the reserved capacity depends on AXT bringing new production online in coming years rather than drawing on existing output.

  Supply is expected to remain tight in 2026 and 2027, with prices forecast to continue rising. The contracts lock in partial supply, but the shortfall risk persists until AXT's future capacity expansion comes online.

  Lumentum與Coherent已向全球磷化銦（InP）基板龍頭製造商AXT承諾逾1.09億美元的預付款，以鎖定至少至2031年底的供應；AI資料中心對光互連的需求急速攀升，不僅推動磷化銦價格大幅上漲，更引發業界警告：此次供應缺口的嚴重程度，恐將超越記憶體晶片短缺危機。

  ## 與AXT達成供應協議

  Lumentum將依據一份涵蓋至2031年底的產能預留暨供應協議，向AXT支付8,700萬美元的預付款。Coherent則已預付逾2,200萬美元給AXT，以確保獨立的生產產能。業界人士普遍認定AXT為全球磷化銦基板製造的龍頭企業。

  兩家公司均生產用於資料中心內部光通訊連結的磷化銦雷射元件，並隨著AI驅動需求加速升溫，率先採取行動鎖定基板供應。

  ## AI資料中心的「光進銅退」浪潮

  產業分析師將此波需求激增歸因於AI資料中心連接架構的結構性轉變——業界將此趨勢稱為「光進銅退」（optical in, copper out）——亦即大型設施內部以高速光纖連結取代銅線互連。磷化銦雷射是上述光互連的核心元件。

  Lumentum已警告，AI資料中心對磷化銦雷射的需求呈倍數成長。該公司表示，由此引發的供應短缺，恐怕將比前一輪AI基礎建設擴張熱潮後爆發的記憶體晶片短缺更為嚴重。

  ## 磷化銦與記憶體短缺的歷史對照

  這項與記憶體的類比相當具體。在上一波AI資料中心建設熱潮中，業界嚴重低估記憶體需求超越供給的速度，此一事件被業界觀察人士形容為推升科技成本通膨的重要因素之一。分析師如今預測，磷化銦可能重蹈覆轍，成為AI投資週期中下一個引發全行業搶購的關鍵原物料。

  ## 供應緊張局面預計至少延續至2027年

  長期合約並不能消除短缺風險。業界人士指出，上述協議僅涵蓋兩家公司各自部分的供應需求，且預留產能中有相當比例仰賴AXT未來幾年新建產能的投產，而非依托現有產出。

  供應情況預計在2026年及2027年仍將持續吃緊，價格亦預測將繼續攀升。這些合約所鎖定的僅是部分供應，在AXT未來的產能擴充正式上線之前，短缺風險依然揮之不去。
