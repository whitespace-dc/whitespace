body_en: |
  OGX Networks Sdn Bhd (OGXN), the wholly-owned subsidiary of Malaysian information technology infrastructure solutions group OGX Group Bhd, has won a RM96.64 million (approximately US$20.6 million) contract to supply fibre optic cables, patch panels, and related materials for data centre applications.

  OGX Group disclosed the award in a filing with Bursa Malaysia, the country's stock exchange. OGXN accepted a letter of award (LoA) — a formal document confirming a contract has been granted — from a buyer identified only as Customer E, a Malaysian private limited company whose principal business is the supply and installation of cables and computer equipment.

  ## Contract terms

  Under the agreement, OGXN is required to deliver the data centre products within six months of the LoA date. The filing did not disclose the specific data centre project or end-customer the materials will serve.

  The products are intended to support high-capacity fibre optic connectivity and network infrastructure for data centre operations, OGX said.

  ## Significance for OGX

  OGX said the contract is expected to strengthen the group's presence in the data centre market and enhance its participation in the sector's supply chain. The award positions OGXN as a materials supplier within a multi-tier contracting structure, with Customer E sitting between OGXN and the ultimate data centre operator or developer.

  The six-month delivery window indicates near-term execution pressure and suggests the underlying data centre project is already in an active build-out phase.
body_zh: |
  ## OGX Networks獲逾9600萬令吉合約　供應資料中心光纖材料

  OGX Group Bhd旗下全資子公司OGX Networks Sdn Bhd（OGXN），日前取得一紙總值9,664萬令吉（約2,060萬美元）的合約，供應項目涵蓋光纖電纜、配線盤及各類資料中心應用相關材料。

  OGX Group已透過向馬來西亞證券交易所「馬交所」（Bursa Malaysia）申報揭露此一合約。OGXN接受的授標函（Letter of Award，LoA）係一份正式確認合約獲批的文件，發函方在公告中僅以「客戶E」代稱，該客戶為馬來西亞一家私人有限公司，主要業務為電纜及電腦設備的供應與安裝。

  ## 合約條款

  依據本合約，OGXN須於授標函簽署日起六個月內完成所有資料中心產品的交付。申報文件未揭露上述材料最終將用於哪一具體資料中心專案，亦未說明終端客戶為何。

  OGX表示，相關產品旨在支援資料中心營運所需的大容量光纖連線能力與網路基礎建設。

  ## 對OGX集團的意義

  OGX表示，此合約預期將強化集團在資料中心市場的布局，並深化其在該產業供應鏈中的參與程度。本次授標使OGXN在多層次承包架構中扮演材料供應商的角色，「客戶E」則居於OGXN與最終資料中心營運商或開發商之間的中間層。

  六個月的交付期限顯示本合約的執行時程相當緊湊，也意味著背後的資料中心專案已正式進入積極建設階段。
