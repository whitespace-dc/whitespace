body_en: |
  euNetworks has launched a 1,057-kilometre long-haul fibre route between Paris and Milan, the pan-European bandwidth infrastructure provider announced on 9 July 2026, describing the path as the most direct connectivity option available between the two cities.

  ## New Alpine route bypasses longer coastal alternative

  The route runs through the Alps on a new, diverse path, providing a shorter alternative to established connections that follow the coast via Lyon and Marseille. euNetworks said the development creates opportunities to further shorten and diversify interconnecting routes across its wider European network.

  The Paris-Milan launch follows euNetworks' delivery of a Frankfurt-to-Milan connection via Zurich in October 2025, spanning 771 kilometres. Combined, euNetworks said, the two route additions provide the shortest cumulative connections between Frankfurt and Milan, at 771km and 1,000km respectively.

  ## Data centre reach at each endpoint

  Customers on the new route can connect into euNetworks' metro networks in both cities. The Paris network includes 38 on-net data centres and the Milan network covers 18, with onward access to more than 600 connected data centres the company operates across Europe.

  euNetworks runs 18 metropolitan city networks across 53 cities in 17 countries, linked by a high-capacity intercity backbone, and directly connects to all major cloud platforms.

  ## Growing demand for shorter, diverse routes

  Marisa Trisolino, CEO of euNetworks, said the route reflects the company's focus on providing direct and diverse connectivity options across Europe. "As demand for AI, cloud and data driven connectivity rises, shorter routes and diversity become increasingly crucial," Trisolino said. "Building a diverse route through the Alps takes a lot of persistence and collaboration with an extensive supplier network."

  euNetworks said the Paris-Milan launch, combined with the October 2025 Frankfurt-Milan addition, now gives the company what it describes as the shortest cumulative routes between Frankfurt and Milan on its network.
body_zh: |
  ## euNetworks推出巴黎至米蘭1,057公里阿爾卑斯山光纖路由

  泛歐頻寬基礎設施供應商euNetworks於2026年7月9日宣布，正式啟用一條連結巴黎與米蘭的長途光纖路由，全長1,057公里，並將其定位為目前兩城之間最直接的連線選項。

  ## 全新阿爾卑斯山路由繞開較長的沿海替代路徑

  這條路由穿越阿爾卑斯山，採用全新的多元化路徑，提供比既有沿海連線更短的替代方案——後者需途經里昂與馬賽。euNetworks表示，此路由的建成，為進一步縮短並多元化其歐洲整體網路中各互連路由創造了更多機會。

  巴黎至米蘭路由的啟用，緊接於euNetworks在2025年10月完成的法蘭克福至米蘭（經蘇黎世）連線之後，後者全長771公里。euNetworks指出，兩條路由合計，為法蘭克福與米蘭之間提供了目前最短的累計連線距離，分別為771公里與1,000公里。

  ## 兩端點的資料中心覆蓋範圍

  使用這條新路由的客戶，可接入euNetworks在兩座城市的都會網路。巴黎網路涵蓋38座直連資料中心，米蘭網路則涵蓋18座，並可進一步連接該公司在歐洲各地運營的逾600座資料中心。

  euNetworks目前在17個國家的53座城市運營18個都會城市網路，並透過高容量的城際骨幹網路相互串連，同時直接連接所有主要雲端平台。

  ## 對更短、更多元路由的需求持續成長

  euNetworks執行長Marisa Trisolino表示，這條路由體現了公司致力於在歐洲各地提供直接且多元化連線選項的核心策略。「隨著AI、雲端及資料驅動連線需求持續攀升，更短的路由與路徑多元性變得愈來愈關鍵，」Trisolino說道，「在阿爾卑斯山建設一條多元化路由，需要極大的毅力，以及與龐大供應商網絡的緊密協作。」

  euNetworks表示，巴黎至米蘭路由的啟用，結合2025年10月新增的法蘭克福至米蘭連線，使公司在其網路上擁有法蘭克福與米蘭之間目前最短的累計路由。
