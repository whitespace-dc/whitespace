body_en: |
  The Emerging Africa & Asia Infrastructure Fund (EAAIF), a blended-finance vehicle managed by investment firm Ninety One under the Private Infrastructure Development Group (PIDG), has committed $50 million in private debt to Liquid Intelligent Technologies, the pan-African connectivity provider, to support the restructuring and expansion of its terrestrial fiber network across the continent.

  The $50 million loan is one component of a larger $450 million restructuring and expansion package for Liquid, which operates a 110,000-kilometer terrestrial fiber network spanning 25 African countries, including Kenya, Zimbabwe, and South Africa. EAAIF said the funds will be used to optimize Liquid's capital structure and sustain its cross-border fiber infrastructure.

  ## Where the money fits in Liquid's financing picture

  Earlier this year, Liquid closed a $660 million debt refinancing round that included a $300 million Eurobond. That bond was oversubscribed 2.5 times, according to the company, which said the refinancing was intended to give it additional capacity to invest in connectivity, cloud services, and cybersecurity. The $450 million restructuring package, of which the EAAIF loan is a part, is separate from that earlier round.

  Liquid Group Chief Executive Hardy Pemhiwa described the EAAIF commitment as both a financial and strategic milestone, saying it would strengthen the company's balance sheet and support its cross-border fiber operations.

  ## A second commitment in the same package

  Alongside the Liquid deal, EAAIF simultaneously extended a $32.8 million secured loan to Eastcastle Infrastructure DRC to fund the construction of 728 new telecom towers in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Together, the two commitments total $82.8 million. EAAIF said both investments are intended to de-risk and scale the physical and digital networks underpinning Africa's digital economy.

  Martijn Proos, Co-Head of Emerging Market Alternative Credit at Ninety One, said the investments reflect a view that localized access infrastructure and pan-African connectivity corridors must advance together to support economic development across the continent.

  ## Why Liquid's network matters to the broader market

  According to EAAIF, Liquid's fiber network serves major telecommunications operators, local enterprises, and hyperscalers — large-scale cloud computing providers — with cross-border connectivity. The network has also become critical for African data storage and processing, the fund said.

  The combination of the prior $660 million refinancing and the current $450 million restructuring package signals continued institutional appetite for large-scale African digital infrastructure debt, with the EAAIF commitment the latest tranche to close under that restructuring program.
body_zh: |
  新興非洲與亞洲基礎設施基金（Emerging Africa & Asia Infrastructure Fund，EAAIF）——一個由投資公司Ninety One代表私人基礎設施發展集團（Private Infrastructure Development Group，PIDG）管理的混合融資工具——已承諾向泛非洲連網業者Liquid Intelligent Technologies提供5000萬美元私人債務，用以支援該公司遍及整個非洲大陸的陸上光纖網路重組與擴張。

  這筆5000萬美元的貸款，是Liquid一項規模達4.5億美元的重組與擴張整體融資方案中的一部分。Liquid目前運營一條全長11萬公里的陸上光纖網路，橫跨肯亞、辛巴威、南非等25個非洲國家。EAAIF表示，這筆資金將用於優化Liquid的資本結構，並維持其跨境光纖基礎設施的穩定運行。

  ## 這筆資金在Liquid融資藍圖中的定位

  今年稍早，Liquid已完成一輪規模達6.6億美元的債務再融資，其中包括一筆3億美元的歐元債券。據該公司表示，這檔債券的認購量超出發行量2.5倍，再融資的目的是為公司提供額外資本，以持續投入連網服務、雲端服務及資安領域。此次4.5億美元的重組方案（EAAIF的貸款即屬其中一環），與先前那輪再融資屬於獨立的融資安排。

  Liquid集團執行長Hardy Pemhiwa將EAAIF的這項承諾定性為財務與戰略上的雙重里程碑，表示此舉將強化公司的資產負債表，並為其跨境光纖業務提供支撐。

  ## 同一方案中的第二筆承諾

  與Liquid這筆交易同步，EAAIF也向Eastcastle Infrastructure DRC延伸了一筆3280萬美元的擔保貸款，用於在剛果民主共和國建設728座新電信鐵塔。兩筆承諾合計達8280萬美元。EAAIF表示，這兩項投資旨在降低風險並擴大支撐非洲數位經濟的實體與數位網路規模。

  Ninety One新興市場另類信貸聯席主管Martijn Proos表示，這兩項投資反映了一種觀點：在地接取基礎設施與泛非洲連網廊道必須同步推進，才能支撐整個非洲大陸的經濟發展。

  ## Liquid網路對更廣泛市場的重要性

  根據EAAIF的說法，Liquid的光纖網路為主要電信業者、本地企業及大型雲端運算服務商（即超大規模業者）提供跨境連網服務。該基金表示，這條網路對非洲的資料儲存與運算處理同樣至關重要。

  先前6.6億美元的再融資與此次4.5億美元的重組方案相互疊加，顯示大型機構投資人對非洲大規模數位基礎設施債務的持續胃納，而EAAIF此次承諾則是該重組方案中最新完成交割的一筆資金。
