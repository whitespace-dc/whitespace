# Indigo to Provide Operational Support for Trans Pacific Networks' Subsea Cables

Indigo will provide expanded operational support for Trans Pacific Networks' subsea cables connecting Asia-Pacific and the US. The partnership strengthens critical trans-Pacific data infrastructure.
