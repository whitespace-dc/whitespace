body_en: |
  1FINITY, a networking subsidiary of Fujitsu, on November 17, 2026 announced the "1Finity Ultra Optical System N100," a network interface card (NIC, hardware that connects a server to a network) capable of linking servers in separate data centers up to approximately 100 kilometers apart over optical fiber with low latency and high throughput. The company said the product targets operators building distributed AI computing infrastructure.

  ## Specifications and technology

  The N100 carries two optical interface ports each rated at 400 gigabits per second (Gbps), giving a combined maximum throughput of 800 Gbps. The card incorporates 1FINITY's proprietary xRDMA (Extend Remote Direct Memory Access) data-transfer protocol, which the company says reduces the communication latency and packet loss that degrade performance over long-distance inter-site fiber links.

  ## AI infrastructure demand

  Demand for AI training and inference is driving operators to distribute compute resources across multiple data centers and run them as a coordinated pool, 1FINITY said. With conventional products, the company said, greater inter-facility distances bring higher latency and more packet loss, reducing data transfer efficiency. The N100 is designed to maintain high-efficiency transfers at the distances where existing interconnect hardware degrades.

  ## Commercial rollout and customer pipeline

  1FINITY began accepting advance orders in November 2026 and plans to begin global shipments in April 2027. Five customers in the healthcare and financial sectors have already been confirmed under the advance-order program, the company said. Negotiations are under way with more than 50 companies worldwide.

  ## Revenue target

  1FINITY said it aims for 100 billion yen in annual sales from the AI data center networking segment, including the N100, by fiscal year 2030.

  富士通旗下網路子公司1FINITY於2026年11月17日宣布推出「1Finity Ultra Optical System N100」網路介面卡（NIC，即連接伺服器與網路的硬體），可透過光纖以低延遲、高吞吐量的方式，連結距離最遠約100公里的異地資料中心伺服器。該公司表示，此產品專為建構分散式AI運算基礎設施的業者所設計。

  ## 規格與技術

  N100配備兩個光學介面埠，每個埠的傳輸速率均達每秒400千兆位元（Gbps），合計最高吞吐量為800 Gbps。這張網卡內建1FINITY自研的xRDMA（延伸遠端直接記憶體存取）資料傳輸協定，該公司表示，此協定可有效降低長距離跨站點光纖連線中影響效能的通訊延遲與封包遺失問題。

  ## AI基礎設施需求

  1FINITY指出，AI訓練與推論需求持續攀升，促使業者將運算資源分散部署至多個資料中心，並以協同方式統一調度運用。然而以現有產品而言，設施間距離愈遠，延遲愈高、封包遺失率愈大，導致資料傳輸效率下降。N100的設計目標，正是在現有互連硬體開始出現效能劣化的距離範圍內，仍能維持高效率的資料傳輸。

  ## 商業推進與客戶管線

  1FINITY已於2026年11月開始接受預訂，並計畫於2027年4月起展開全球出貨。該公司表示，目前已有來自醫療保健與金融兩大產業的五家客戶完成預訂確認，另有逾50家全球企業正在洽談中。

  ## 營收目標

  1FINITY表示，包含N100在內的AI資料中心網路業務，目標在2030財年達到年營收1,000億日圓。
