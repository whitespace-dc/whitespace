body_en: |
  Industry executives warned at the Johor Interconnect World Forum 2026 that Malaysia faces a serious deficit in network infrastructure that could undermine Johor's emergence as Southeast Asia's largest data center hub, as construction of data centers outpaces the underlying fiber and subsea connectivity needed to support them.

  ## Malaysia's three connectivity gaps

  Johor needs to close shortfalls in three areas: diverse inter-data center connectivity, backhaul fiber across the country, and cable landing stations for subsea cables, the forum heard. Speakers said the problem extends beyond Johor to Malaysia as a whole.

  "Malaysia has a 'serious deficit' in networking as operators rush to build data centers without enough underlying network infrastructure," said Sylvester Wong, Executive Director of Global Telecom. Wong was speaking on a panel titled "Interconnection Architecture: Building the Fiber & Carrier Ecosystem for Johor" at the forum, which was organized by w.media as part of its annual SIJORI Week and held this week in Johor.

  ## Why subsea cables are the most time-sensitive bottleneck

  Wong identified subsea connectivity as the most critical challenge. While power has alternatives, including gas, solar, hydro, and fuel cells, and power plants can be built relatively quickly, a subsea cable can take between six and seven years just to obtain permission and complete a landing, he said.

  "Subsea connectivity is hence more critical and more time-sensitive than power," Wong said. He added that Malaysia is not planning capacity against real projected demand in the way that Singapore has.

  Data center developers and operators need to anticipate upcoming demand rather than provision only for current needs, said Paul Mah, Executive Editor of w.media, who moderated the panel.

  ## Johor's rise and its gradual separation from Singapore

  Johor has moved beyond its original role as a spillover destination for Singapore's data center overflow demand, the forum heard. The state has surpassed Singapore to become the next largest data center hub in Southeast Asia and is projected to host 5 gigawatts of capacity by 2030.

  Currently, customers connect first into Singapore's established connectivity ecosystem and then backhaul — routing traffic over long-haul network links — to Johor data centers for compute and storage. That pattern may not persist. "As capacity shifts, the appeal of landing in Singapore will decrease, and as more subsea cables land directly in Johor, the two markets will gradually detach," said William Heng, Chief Solutions Engineering Officer of Empyrion Digital.

  ## AI reshapes traffic patterns and investment metrics

  Artificial intelligence workloads are driving a new pattern of inter-data center traffic. Data centers will increasingly "talk to each other in clusters," reflecting AI's distinct connectivity and latency demands, the forum heard.

  AI is also changing how investors evaluate data center assets. Instead of traditional real estate criteria such as long leases, stable tenants, and predictable power, investors are now focused on megawatts secured, GPU-readiness, cooling density, and power access, said Prof Edward Tay of Infracrowd Capital, speaking in his opening keynote at the SIJORI Cloud and Datacenter Convention 2026.

  ## Three distinct markets within SIJORI

  The SIJORI region comprises Singapore, Johor in Malaysia, and the Riau Islands in Indonesia. Each submarket occupies a distinct position: Johor leads in land and power scale; Singapore holds advantages in trust, capital, and interconnection; Batam, in the Riau Islands, offers regulatory diversity and resilience.

  Batam is also seeing surging demand. In the past two months alone, two companies signed data center deals of 500 megawatts each in the territory, said Dendi Gustinandar of BP Batam.

  ## What the connectivity deficit means for Johor's 2030 targets

  The forum's warnings carry practical urgency given the lead times involved. If permitting and landing a subsea cable requires six to seven years, network projects needed to support Johor's projected 2030 capacity targets would need to begin now. The central message from the forum was that the pace of data center construction in Malaysia has outrun the fiber and subsea infrastructure required for those facilities to reach their full potential.

  SIJORI Week 2026, running June 21 to 27, drew a record 3,000 digital infrastructure leaders from across Asia Pacific to Singapore, Johor, and Batam. The 2026 edition added two new events: DCIS Summit Asia 2026 and HPC Summit Southeast Asia 2026.
body_zh: |
  業界高層在本週舉行的2026年柔佛互聯世界論壇（Johor Interconnect World Forum 2026）上示警，馬來西亞面臨嚴峻的網路基礎建設缺口，恐將動搖柔佛州躍升為東南亞最大資料中心樞紐的發展軌跡。資料中心的建設速度，已大幅超前於支撐其運作所需的光纖與海底電纜連網基礎設施，與會者對此表達高度憂慮。

  ## 馬來西亞的三大連網缺口

  論壇指出，柔佛必須填補三個面向的不足：資料中心之間多元化的互聯能力、全國性的回程光纖骨幹，以及供海底電纜登陸的電纜登陸站。與會者強調，問題不僅限於柔佛，而是擴及整個馬來西亞。

  「馬來西亞的網路存在嚴重缺口，業者爭相建設資料中心，底層網路基礎設施卻嚴重不足，」Global Telecom執行董事Sylvester Wong說。Wong是在名為「互聯架構：為柔佛打造光纖與電信生態系」的專題座談中發表上述言論。此次論壇由w.media主辦，是其年度SIJORI Week活動的一部分，本週於柔佛舉行。

  ## 海底電纜為何是最迫切的瓶頸

  Wong點名海底連網為最關鍵的挑戰。他指出，電力供應尚有替代方案，涵蓋天然氣、太陽能、水力及燃料電池，且發電廠建設相對快速；然而，一條海底電纜光是取得許可並完成登陸，就需耗費六至七年時間。

  「因此，海底連網比電力更為關鍵、也更加刻不容緩，」Wong說。他補充，馬來西亞並未像新加坡那樣，依據真實的預測需求來規劃容量。

  論壇主持人、w.media執行編輯Paul Mah表示，資料中心開發商與運營商必須預先因應未來需求，而非僅針對當前需求進行布建。

  ## 柔佛的崛起與逐漸脫離新加坡

  與會者表示，柔佛已超越其最初作為新加坡資料中心溢出需求承接地的定位。該州已超越新加坡，成為東南亞第二大資料中心樞紐，預計至2030年將承載5吉瓦（GW）的容量。

  目前，客戶先接入新加坡成熟的連網生態系，再透過回程路由，將流量導向柔佛的資料中心進行運算與儲存。然而這種模式未必會持續。「隨著容量重心轉移，在新加坡登陸的吸引力將逐漸降低；隨著更多海底電纜直接在柔佛登陸，兩個市場將逐步脫鉤，」Empyrion Digital首席解決方案工程長William Heng說。

  ## 人工智慧重塑流量模式與投資指標

  人工智慧（AI）工作負載正在催生全新的資料中心間流量模式。論壇指出，資料中心將越來越傾向「以叢集方式相互通訊」，反映出AI對連網能力與低延遲的獨特需求。

  AI也正在改變投資人評估資料中心資產的標準。Infracrowd Capital的Edward Tay教授在SIJORI雲端與資料中心大會2026（SIJORI Cloud and Datacenter Convention 2026）的開幕主題演講中指出，投資人已不再側重長期租約、穩定租戶與可預期電力等傳統房地產指標，轉而聚焦於已確保的百萬瓦容量（megawatts secured）、GPU就緒程度、冷卻密度以及電力取得能力。

  ## SIJORI的三個截然不同的子市場

  SIJORI區域涵蓋新加坡、馬來西亞柔佛州，以及印尼廖內群島。每個子市場各有其明確定位：柔佛在土地與電力規模上居於領先；新加坡在信任度、資本市場與互聯能力方面具有優勢；廖內群島的巴淡島（Batam）則提供監管多元性與系統韌性。

  巴淡島的需求也正急速攀升。BP Batam的Dendi Gustinandar透露，僅在過去兩個月內，就有兩家企業分別在當地簽署了各達500百萬瓦（MW）的資料中心合約。

  ## 連網缺口對柔佛2030目標的意涵

  考量到相關建設的漫長前置時間，論壇的示警具有切實的迫切性。若海底電纜的申請許可與完成登陸需耗時六至七年，那麼為支撐柔佛預計2030年容量目標所需的網路建設，現在就必須啟動。本次論壇傳遞的核心訊息是：馬來西亞資料中心的建設步伐，已遠遠超過讓這些設施充分發揮潛力所需的光纖與海底基礎設施的建設速度。
