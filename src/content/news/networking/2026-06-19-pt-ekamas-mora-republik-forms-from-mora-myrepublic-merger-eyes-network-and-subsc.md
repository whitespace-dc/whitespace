body_en: |
  PT Ekamas Mora Republik Tbk (MORA), formed through the April 2026 merger of PT Mora Telematika Indonesia Tbk and PT Eka Mas Republik (MyRepublic), is moving immediately to set strategic priorities aimed at expanding its network and growing its subscriber count, the company's chief executive told Kontan on Wednesday.

  The combined entity positions itself as an integrated telecommunications infrastructure and fiber-to-the-home (FTTH) services company. FTTH refers to broadband connections delivered via fiber-optic cable run directly to individual residences, as distinct from connections that rely on legacy copper or coaxial cable for the final segment to the home.

  ## Post-merger strategy

  Timotius Max Sulaiman, President Director and Chief Executive Officer of PT Ekamas Mora Republik Tbk, said the company would move directly to establish a set of strategic focuses following the April 2026 merger. The stated aim is to reinforce business growth and improve the company's competitive position in the period after the combination, according to the Kontan report.

  Beyond those broad objectives, the article's substantive detail on specific network expansion targets, subscriber figures, investment plans, and financial guidance was not available in the portion of the report accessible for this synthesis.

  ## Significance

  The formation of MORA consolidates two previously separate publicly listed and private telecommunications operators into a single integrated FTTH vehicle, which could affect competitive dynamics in Indonesia's fixed-broadband market. The combined company's next steps on network rollout and subscriber acquisition will be the primary indicators of whether the merger delivers the competitive gains the chief executive described.
