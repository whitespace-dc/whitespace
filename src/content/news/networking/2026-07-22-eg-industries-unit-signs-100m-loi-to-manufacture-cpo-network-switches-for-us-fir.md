body_en: |
  EG Industries Bhd said its Malaysian subsidiary Genetronic (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd has signed a letter of intent with an unnamed US-based company to manufacture and sell high-performance open networking switches incorporating co-packaged optics (CPO) technology, in a collaboration expected to generate approximately US$100 million in revenue.

  ## The deal

  EG Industries did not name the US partner, describing it as a global provider of open networking solutions that specialises in high-performance Ethernet switches for hyperscale cloud and AI data centres.

  Under the collaboration, Genetronic will manufacture CPO-based network switches designed to deliver higher bandwidth, lower power consumption, and improved efficiency for next-generation AI infrastructure, EG Industries said.

  ## Technology scope and executive commentary

  EG Industries group chief executive Datuk Alex Kang said the letter of intent validates the group's progress in developing expertise and manufacturing capabilities to support both co-packaged optics and a related technology, linear pluggable optics (LPO). Kang added that he expects additional purchase orders for CPO products to follow as customer programmes progress.

  The company described the deal as marking its expansion into high-growth data centre infrastructure and advanced AI networking solutions.

  ## Significance

  The collaboration places a Malaysian contract electronics manufacturer in the supply chain for AI networking hardware used by hyperscale cloud and data centre operators. Kang said EG Industries remains confident in its long-term growth prospects as global demand for AI-driven networking infrastructure, hyperscale data centres, and next-generation connectivity solutions continues to expand.

  ## 交易內容

  EG Industries Bhd表示，旗下馬來西亞子公司Genetronic（馬來西亞）有限公司已與一家未具名的美國企業簽署意向書，雙方將合作製造並銷售搭載共封裝光學（CPO）技術的高效能開放式網路交換器，此次合作預計創造約1億美元的營收。

  EG Industries並未透露美方合作夥伴的名稱，僅說明對方是一家全球性的開放網路解決方案供應商，專精於為超大規模雲端與AI資料中心提供高效能乙太網路交換器。

  根據合作內容，Genetronic將負責製造採用CPO技術的網路交換器，這類產品設計用於提供更高頻寬、更低功耗，並提升次世代AI基礎設施的整體效率。

  ## 技術範疇與高層評述

  EG Industries集團執行長拿督Alex Kang表示，此次意向書的簽署，印證了集團在共封裝光學及相關技術——線性可插拔光學（LPO）——領域累積的專業能力與製造實力。Kang進一步表示，隨著客戶端計畫持續推進，預期後續將有更多CPO產品採購訂單陸續到位。

  公司將此次合作定位為集團正式跨入高成長性資料中心基礎設施及先進AI網路解決方案領域的重要里程碑。

  ## 產業意義

  此次合作使一家馬來西亞電子專業代工製造商正式進入AI網路硬體的供應鏈，成為超大規模雲端與資料中心業者的上游夥伴。Kang表示，隨著全球對AI驅動的網路基礎設施、超大規模資料中心及次世代連接解決方案的需求持續擴張，EG Industries對集團長期成長前景深具信心。
