body_en: |
  Amazon has signed a multi-billion-dollar, multi-year agreement with Corning to expand U.S. manufacturing of optical fiber and connectivity products used in data centers, the companies announced Monday. Amazon did not disclose the financial terms of the deal.

  Shares of Corning rose approximately 7% in early trading following the announcement, according to Reuters. The gain came as a boost to Corning's fiber optics business at a time when weak consumer electronics demand has weighed on its separate Gorilla Glass segment.

  ## Jobs and workforce development

  Amazon said the partnership will create 1,000 jobs at Corning's facilities in North Carolina. As part of the agreement, the two companies will jointly expand Corning's fiber optic technician training program in partnership with Catawba Valley Community College in North Carolina, preparing students for roles in those facilities.

  ## Role of fiber optics in AI data centers

  Optical fiber transmits data using pulses of light through glass or plastic strands, and Corning's optical fiber products are used to move data between thousands of processors inside artificial intelligence data centers, according to Amazon. The scale and density of AI computing infrastructure has increased demand for high-speed, high-capacity interconnects within data center campuses.

  ## Corning's broader U.S. manufacturing expansion

  Corning has already announced plans to increase its U.S.-based optical connectivity manufacturing capacity tenfold and expand domestic fiber production capacity by more than 50%, the companies said. Last month, Corning signed a separate partnership with Nvidia to expand U.S. manufacturing of fiber optics, making the Amazon agreement the second major technology-sector deal Corning has announced in recent weeks.

  ## Industry significance

  The deal signals that major cloud and AI infrastructure operators are moving to secure domestic supply chains for fiber optic components, a category that has seen rising demand alongside the rapid build-out of AI data centers. For Corning, the back-to-back partnerships with Nvidia and Amazon represent a sustained commitment from two of the largest buyers of data center equipment to anchor their fiber supply in U.S. production.

  Corning's planned tenfold increase in U.S. optical connectivity manufacturing capacity is set to proceed alongside the workforce training initiative at Catawba Valley Community College in North Carolina.

  ## 就業機會與人才培訓

  亞馬遜表示，此次合作將在康寧位於北卡羅來納州的廠房創造一千個就業機會。根據協議內容，兩家公司將攜手與北卡羅來納州的卡托巴谷社區學院（Catawba Valley Community College）合作，共同擴大康寧的光纖技術人員培訓計畫，協助學員做好進入相關廠房工作的準備。

  ## 光纖在人工智慧資料中心的角色

  亞馬遜指出，光纖透過玻璃或塑膠纖維絲以光脈衝的形式傳輸資料，康寧的光纖產品正是用於人工智慧資料中心內數千個處理器之間的資料傳遞。隨著人工智慧運算基礎設施的規模與密度持續擴大，資料中心園區內對高速、高容量互連技術的需求也隨之大幅攀升。

  ## 康寧在美國擴大製造產能的布局

  兩家公司表示，康寧已宣布計畫，將其美國境內光纖連接產品製造產能擴大至目前的十倍，並將國內光纖生產產能提升逾50%。上個月，康寧甫與輝達簽署一項單獨的合作協議，同樣著眼於擴大光纖產品在美國的製造規模，使此次與亞馬遜達成的協議，成為康寧在近幾週內宣布的第二項重大科技業合作。

  ## 產業意義

  此次協議顯示，主要雲端與人工智慧基礎設施業者正積極確保光纖元件的國內供應鏈。隨著人工智慧資料中心快速建置擴張，光纖需求持續升溫，這一品類的戰略地位愈發凸顯。對康寧而言，接連獲得輝達與亞馬遜兩大夥伴的合作背書，代表這兩家全球最大資料中心設備採購商已明確表態，要將光纖供應鏈根植於美國本土生產。
