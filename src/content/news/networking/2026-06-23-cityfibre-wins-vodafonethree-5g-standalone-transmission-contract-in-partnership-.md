body_en: |
  CityFibre has secured a mobile network transmission contract with VodafoneThree, broadening an existing supplier relationship to cover 5G Standalone — the next-generation mobile architecture that runs core network functions on software rather than dedicated hardware — at selected sites across the United Kingdom.

  The new agreement adds a mobile transmission dimension to a partnership in which CityFibre already serves as a preferred supplier of full fibre infrastructure for VodafoneThree's retail broadband services. CityFibre's network currently passes more than 4.7 million premises and the company has plans to extend that to more than 8 million; coverage added as the rollout progresses will fall within the scope of the expanded deal, the company said.

  ## Scale of the VodafoneThree investment programme

  The partnership sits within VodafoneThree's £11 billion investment programme, which the combined operator — formed from the merger of Vodafone UK and Three UK — has described as intended to build the UK's best network, with 5G Standalone at its centre.

  More than 330,000 Vodafone residential broadband customers are already connected through CityFibre's infrastructure, including customers on Vodafone's 2.2 Gbps symmetrical home broadband service. That service runs exclusively over CityFibre's 10Gb XGS-PON network. XGS-PON (10-Gigabit Symmetric Passive Optical Network) is a fibre access standard capable of delivering up to 10 Gbps in both upload and download directions.

  ## What each party said

  Simon Holden, CEO of CityFibre, said the company had grown alongside Vodafone and Three and was "delighted to be a key part of VodafoneThree's vision as it continues to invest and innovate."

  Max Taylor, CEO of VodafoneThree, said the partnership supports its ambition to take 5G Standalone "further than any other operator" while continuing to grow its broadband footprint across the UK.

  ## What the deal signals

  The contract extends CityFibre's role from a fixed broadband infrastructure supplier into mobile backhaul — the transmission links that carry traffic between cell sites and the wider network — marking a broadening of the commercial relationship between the two companies. For CityFibre, whose network expansion target of more than 8 million premises is still in progress, the deal ties future coverage gains directly to a major operator's 5G build schedule.

  VodafoneThree's deployment of 5G Standalone, rather than the more common Non-Standalone architecture that relies on existing 4G core infrastructure, represents a more capital-intensive but architecturally distinct approach. The £11 billion investment programme cited by VodafoneThree will determine the pace and reach of that build, with CityFibre's fibre footprint serving as part of the underlying transmission layer as the rollout progresses across selected sites.

  CityFibre已取得與VodafoneThree的行動網路傳輸合約，將既有的供應商合作關係擴展至5G獨立組網（5G Standalone）——這是次世代行動網路架構，其核心網路功能以軟體運行，不再依賴專用硬體——涵蓋英國境內的多個選定據點。

  這份新協議在既有夥伴關係的基礎上新增行動傳輸業務向度。在現有合作中，CityFibre已是VodafoneThree零售寬頻服務的全光纖基礎設施優先供應商。CityFibre的網路目前覆蓋逾470萬個用戶端，並計畫擴展至逾800萬個；隨著佈建進展所新增的覆蓋範圍，皆納入擴大後合約的適用範圍，該公司表示。

  ## VodafoneThree投資計畫的規模

  這項夥伴關係隸屬於VodafoneThree規模達110億英鎊的投資計畫。這家由Vodafone英國與Three UK合併而成的業者，將該計畫定位為打造英國最佳網路，以5G獨立組網為核心目標。

  目前已有逾33萬名Vodafone住宅寬頻用戶透過CityFibre的基礎設施連線，包括使用Vodafone 2.2 Gbps對稱式家用寬頻服務的用戶。該服務專門透過CityFibre的10Gb XGS-PON網路提供。XGS-PON（10Gbps對稱式被動光纖網路）是一種光纖接取標準，上下行均可提供最高達10 Gbps的傳輸速率。

  ## 雙方表態

  CityFibre執行長Simon Holden表示，公司與Vodafone及Three共同成長，「非常高興能夠成為VodafoneThree願景的關鍵一環，與其持續投資與創新同行。」

  VodafoneThree執行長Max Taylor表示，這項夥伴關係支持其將5G獨立組網推進得「比任何其他業者都更遠」的企圖心，同時持續拓展在英國的寬頻覆蓋版圖。

  ## 合約所釋放的訊號

  這份合約將CityFibre的角色從固定寬頻基礎設施供應商延伸至行動回程——即負責在基地台與更廣泛網路之間承載流量的傳輸連結——標誌著兩家公司商業關係的進一步拓展。對於網路擴建目標逾800萬用戶端仍在推進中的CityFibre而言，這份合約將未來的覆蓋成長直接與一家主要電信商的5G建設時程緊密連結。

  VodafoneThree選擇部署5G獨立組網，而非更普遍、依賴現有4G核心基礎設施的非獨立組網（Non-Standalone）架構，代表一種資本投入更高、但在架構上截然不同的發展路徑。VodafoneThree所揭示的110億英鎊投資計畫，將決定這項建設的速度與涵蓋範圍，而CityFibre的光纖覆蓋版圖則在佈建持續推進至各選定據點的過程中，擔任底層傳輸層的一部分。
