body_en: |
  The Philippine Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) has opened bidding for a P473.14 million (approximately $8.4 million) contract to provide internet connectivity at public locations nationwide through fiber optic networks, funded from the agency's 2026 budget.

  The contract covers 1,413 locations spread across three regional groupings. North Luzon accounts for 560 sites at a cost of P187.51 million, South Luzon covers 599 locations for P200.57 million, and the Visayas and Mindanao regions combined account for 254 sites at P85.05 million, according to the DICT bid notice.

  Interested bidders have until July 29 to submit proposals. To qualify, bidders must have completed a project of a similar nature within the past five years.

  ## A parallel procurement for satellite connectivity

  The fiber optic tender follows a separate procurement the DICT opened the prior week for a P179.49 million contract to provide low-earth orbit (LEO) satellite internet services. LEO satellites orbit much closer to Earth than traditional geostationary satellites, which reduces signal latency and can make broadband viable in areas where laying ground infrastructure is impractical.

  That LEO contract targets geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas (GIDAs), defined as communities difficult or impossible to reach with conventional wired networks. It covers 396 locations on Luzon and 191 sites in the Visayas and Mindanao, the DICT said.

  ## Two-track approach to connectivity gaps

  Taken together, the two procurements reflect a dual strategy: fiber optic cable for accessible public sites where fixed infrastructure is feasible, and satellite links for remote communities where it is not. The combined value of the two contracts reaches P652.63 million.

  The DICT has set July 29 as the proposal deadline for the fiber optic contract.

  ## 菲律賓DICT招標逾4.7億披索光纖網路計畫，涵蓋全國1,413處公共場所

  菲律賓資訊通信技術部（DICT）已就一項總金額4億7,314萬披索（約840萬美元）的合約展開公開招標，計畫透過光纖網路在全國各地公共場所提供網路連線服務，所需經費來自該機構2026年度預算。

  此合約涵蓋分佈於三大區域的1,413處場所。其中，北呂宋地區涵蓋560個站點，預算為1億8,751萬披索；南呂宋地區涵蓋599個站點，預算為2億57萬披索；維薩亞斯與民答那峨地區合計涵蓋254個站點，預算為8,505萬披索，以上數據均依DICT招標公告所示。

  有意投標的廠商須在7月29日前提交提案。資格要求方面，投標廠商必須在過去五年內完成過性質相近的專案。

  ## 平行採購：衛星網路連線

  光纖招標公告的前一週，DICT已另行展開一項金額達1億7,949萬披索的採購案，擬提供低地球軌道（LEO）衛星網路服務。LEO衛星的運行軌道遠比傳統地球同步衛星更接近地表，因此可大幅降低訊號延遲，並使寬頻服務得以延伸至鋪設地面基礎建設不切實際的偏遠地區。

  這份LEO合約的服務對象為地理上孤立且弱勢的地區（GIDAs），意即傳統有線網路難以或根本無法到達的社區。依DICT說明，此合約涵蓋呂宋地區396處場所，以及維薩亞斯與民答那峨地區191個站點。

  ## 雙軌並進，弭平連線落差

  綜合來看，這兩項採購案體現了一套雙軌策略：對於基礎建設可行的可及性公共場所，採用光纖電纜；對於有線網路無法企及的偏遠社區，則改以衛星連線補足。兩項合約的總金額合計達6億5,263萬披索。

  DICT已將光纖合約的提案截止日期訂於7月29日。
