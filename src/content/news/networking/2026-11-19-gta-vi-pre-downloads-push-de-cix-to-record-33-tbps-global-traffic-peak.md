body_en: |
  Pre-downloads of Grand Theft Auto VI drove internet exchange operator DE-CIX to its highest-ever single-day traffic volume on November 10, 2026, pushing its global network past 33 terabits per second for the first time, the company said.

  Across DE-CIX's worldwide network of internet exchanges (IXs), neutral interconnection points where independent networks hand traffic to one another, throughput peaked at 33.01 terabits per second. DE-CIX Frankfurt, Europe's largest IX, hit 22.30 terabits per second, a 19.06 percent increase over the record set in January 2026. DE-CIX's major US exchanges in Chicago, Dallas, and Phoenix also reached new highs on the same day.

  DE-CIX CEO Ivo Ivanov said the GTA VI-linked traffic increase was larger than the spike generated by the 2026 FIFA World Cup final in July.

  ## Why the load was so concentrated

  Rockstar Games released GTA VI with no physical edition, requiring all players to download the game — a 212-gigabyte file. Analysts estimated approximately 16 million copies were pre-ordered. Rockstar opened a pre-installation window the week before the game's November 18 launch date, allowing players to pull the file ahead of release, which concentrated network demand into a narrow window.

  In the United Kingdom, carrier Virgin Media O2 reported both its largest-ever single traffic peak and its highest-ever daily data volume on November 10, the day pre-downloads began.

  ## How DE-CIX absorbed the surge

  Ivanov said DE-CIX prepares for large traffic events by building redundancy into every node. "You have to be able to serve the peaks because otherwise you will get packet loss and disruption of service," he told Data Center Dynamics. The company targets dual redundancy in routers and switches at each data center, and sometimes triple redundancy, he said.

  DE-CIX also operates under a utilization ceiling: its infrastructure is never allowed to exceed 75 percent of capacity, preserving a 25 percent headroom buffer. "We try to plan in a way that we can serve all events, known or unknown," Ivanov said. "The only way to do this is to overinstall capacity."

  The company cited the Covid-19 pandemic, when traffic surged as work and education moved online, as a prior test of that design philosophy.

  ## DE-CIX's infrastructure footprint

  DE-CIX was founded in 1995 and now operates exchanges in 65 countries, with equipment installed in data centers across more than 600 cities. The company follows a data-center-neutral model, placing its switching equipment inside third-party facilities rather than operating its own. Ivanov said the approach enables dense presence in major metropolitan markets: 50 data centers in Frankfurt and 25 in the New York area. That density allows a network operator to reach hundreds of peers through a single cross-connect, he said, reducing the need for multiple bilateral connections.

  ## What comes next

  Ivanov said DE-CIX expects a second traffic wave on November 19, when the game becomes fully playable. "So far we're very happy with the results we've seen from our network," he said.
body_zh: |
  《俠盜獵車手 VI》（Grand Theft Auto VI）的預下載於 2026 年 11 月 10 日帶動網路交換點（IX）營運商 DE-CIX 創下全球有史以來的單日流量最高紀錄，全球網路吞吐量首度突破每秒 33 Tb 大關，DE-CIX 官方宣布。

  在 DE-CIX 遍布全球的網路交換點節點網絡中——這些節點是各獨立網路相互傳遞流量的中立互連設施——全球尖峰吞吐量達到每秒 33.01 Tb。歐洲最大網路交換點 DE-CIX 法蘭克福節點的峰值流量達每秒 22.30 Tb，較 2026 年 1 月所創下的前次紀錄大幅提升 19.06%。DE-CIX 在美國的主要交換點，包括芝加哥、達拉斯及鳳凰城，也在同一天同步刷新各自的歷史紀錄。

  DE-CIX 執行長 Ivo Ivanov 表示，GTA VI 所帶動的流量增幅，甚至超越了 2026 年 7 月 FIFA 世界盃決賽期間所引發的流量高峰。

  ## 流量高度集中的原因

  Rockstar Games 此次發售 GTA VI 並未推出任何實體版本，所有玩家均須下載遊戲——一個高達 212 GB 的超大檔案。分析師估計預購量約達 1,600 萬套。Rockstar 在遊戲正式上市前一週、亦即 11 月 18 日發售日之前，開放了預先安裝的時間窗口，讓玩家得以提前下載遊戲檔案，使得龐大的網路需求集中爆發於一段極為短暫的時間內。

  在英國，電信業者 Virgin Media O2 表示，11 月 10 日——亦即預下載開始當天——同時創下了該公司有史以來的單一時刻最高流量峰值，以及單日最高資料總量兩項歷史紀錄。

  ## DE-CIX 如何應對流量衝擊

  Ivanov 表示，DE-CIX 透過在每個節點建置備援機制，預先為大型流量事件做好準備。「你必須有能力應付尖峰需求，否則就會出現封包遺失和服務中斷，」他接受《Data Center Dynamics》採訪時如此說道。他指出，公司的目標是在每個資料中心的路由器與交換器上達到雙重備援，有時甚至採三重備援。

  DE-CIX 同時設有嚴格的基礎設施使用率上限：所有基礎設施的使用率不得超過總容量的 75%，以確保保留 25% 的餘裕空間。「我們盡力以能夠因應所有事件的方式來規劃，不論是已知還是未知的，」Ivanov 說。「唯一的辦法就是超量建置容量。」

  公司也以 COVID-19 疫情期間的考驗為例——當時工作與教育活動大規模轉移至線上，流量急遽攀升——視之為這套設計理念的前一次實戰驗證。

  ## DE-CIX 的基礎設施版圖

  DE-CIX 於 1995 年創立，目前已在全球 65 個國家設有交換點，設備部署於逾 600 個城市的資料中心內。公司採取資料中心中立模式，將交換設備置於第三方機房之內，而非自行建設和營運資料中心。Ivanov 表示，這種做法有助於在各大都會市場建立高密度的部署據點：法蘭克福擁有 50 個資料中心節點，紐約地區則有 25 個。他說，如此高的密度讓網路業者只需透過單一跨接（cross-connect），便能連接到數百個對等方，大幅減少了建立多條雙邊連線的需求。

  ## 後續展望

  Ivanov 表示，DE-CIX 預期在 11 月 19 日遊戲正式開放遊玩後，將迎來第二波流量高峰。「到目前為止，我們對自家網路所呈現出的表現非常滿意，」他說。
